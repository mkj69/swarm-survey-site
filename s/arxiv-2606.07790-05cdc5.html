拜占庭廉价沟通：LLM 协调博弈中的对抗韧性与拓扑效应Byzantine Cheap Talk: Adversarial Resilience and Topology Effects in LLM Coordination Games
摘要Abstract
多智能体 LLM 系统日益依赖通信协议来实现协调，但其在对抗性约束和结构性约束下的稳健性仍缺乏充分理解。 Multi-agent LLM systems increasingly rely on communication protocols for coordination, yet their robustness under adversarial and structural constraints remains poorly understood. 既有研究表明，廉价沟通（cheap talk）信道能够促进 LLM 协调博弈中的合作；在此基础上，我们在涵盖六个模型家族、共计 720 次试验的 4 人猎鹿博弈（Stag Hunt）中考察了两类脆弱性。 Building on prior work showing that cheap-talk channels enable cooperation in LLM coordination games, we investigate two vulnerability classes in a 4-player Stag Hunt across six model families and 720 trials. 首先，当拜占庭智能体发出合作信号却选择背叛时，非拜占庭智能体能在一轮内识别背叛，却无法在集体层面作出调整：相当一部分智能体尽管反复遭到利用，仍继续合作，而博弈中要求全体一致的收益结构使其无法恢复协调。 First, when Byzantine agents signal cooperation but defect, non-Byzantine agents detect the betrayal within one round yet fail to adapt collectively: a substantial fraction continue cooperating despite repeated exploitation, unable to recover coordination due to the game’s unanimity payoff structure. 其次，明确告知智能体通信拓扑受到限制会导致合作崩溃，而在不告知智能体的情况下施加相同限制，则能维持近乎完美的合作。 Second, explicitly restricting communication topology collapses cooperation, while applying identical restrictions silently preserves near-perfect cooperation. 这表明，协调失败源于智能体对隐藏信息的元推理，而非信息损失本身。 This establishes that coordination failure stems from agents’ meta-reasoning about hidden information, not information loss itself. 我们识别出两种在所有模型组中均能复现的稳定行为类型：背叛倾向型（Defection-Prone）模型在遭遇背叛后会永久转向背叛，而合作坚持型（Cooperation-Persistent）模型则会付出显著的个体代价继续合作。 We identify two stable behavioral archetypes that replicate across all model cohorts: Defection-Prone models that switch permanently after betrayal, and Cooperation-Persistent models that continue cooperating at significant individual cost. 这些发现揭示了具体的安全漏洞：通信信道可被用作对抗性注入的载体，而且即使不存在任何对手，向智能体披露网络拓扑也可能削弱协调。 These findings reveal concrete security vulnerabilities: communication channels can be exploited as adversarial injection vectors, and disclosing network topology to agents can degrade coordination even without any adversary present.

1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型正日益作为自主智能体部署于多智能体系统中，而协调在此类系统中至关重要 [16, 20]。 Large language models are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents in multi-agent systems where coordination is essential [16, 20]. 在经典博弈论中，廉价沟通（博弈开始前无成本、无约束力的交流）可以通过建立焦点来化解协调困境，焦点是智能体可用于协调的共享信号 [6, 5]。 In classical game theory, cheap talk (costless, non-binding pre-play communication) can resolve coordination dilemmas by establishing focal points, which are shared signals that agents can use to coordinate [6, 5].
近期研究在 LLM 智能体中证实了这一点：在 4 人猎鹿博弈中加入单词广播后，合作率从 0% 提升至 96.7% [14]。 Recent work confirmed this for LLM agents: adding a single-word broadcast to a 4-player Stag Hunt raised cooperation from 0% to 96.7% [14]. 然而，这一结果依赖于诚实传递信号和不受限制的通信这两项假设，而这些条件在实践中很少得到满足。 However, this result relies on assumptions of honest signaling and unrestricted communication, which are conditions that are rarely met in practice. 在现实世界的多智能体系统中，要在不损害协调的前提下从通信中获益，就需要有机制来验证所交换信号的可靠性。 In real-world multi-agent systems, benefiting from communication without compromising coordination requires mechanisms to verify the reliability of exchanged signals. 然而，AutoGen[21] 等多智能体 LLM 框架及类似流程依赖智能体间通信（即通过交换信号或消息来协调决策并使策略保持一致），却缺乏此类验证机制，这使协调的脆弱性成为一个实际的安全问题。 Yet, multi-agent LLM frameworks such as AutoGen[21] and similar pipelines rely on inter-agent communication (i.e., the exchange of signals or messages to coordinate decisions and align strategies) without such verification mechanisms, making coordination fragility a practical security concern.

我们考察了两类脆弱性。 We investigate two classes of vulnerabilities. 首先，拜占庭智能体[11]（发出合作信号却选择背叛的智能体）使我们能够评估在策略性欺骗下能否维持协调（第 4 节），见图 1。 First, Byzantine agents[11] (agents that signal cooperation but defect) allow us to evaluate whether coordination can be sustained under strategic deception (Section 4), see Figure 1. 其次，通信拓扑限制会约束消息的可见性，使我们能够检验廉价沟通带来的协调优势（即其所促成的近乎完美的合作）能否在可见性受限的情况下持续存在（第 5 节）。 Second, communication topology restrictions limit message visibility, allowing us to test whether the coordination benefits of cheap-talk communication (namely, the near-perfect cooperation it enables) persist under limited visibility (Section 5). 我们将未被明确指定为对抗方的智能体称为非拜占庭智能体，尽管它们仍可能表现出对抗性行为。 We refer to non-Byzantine agents as agents that are not explicitly designated as adversarial, although they may still behave in an adversarial manner. 我们的实验揭示了三项主要发现： Our experiments reveal three main findings:
单个采取确定性行为的拜占庭智能体就能彻底消除群体合作；然而，相当一部分非拜占庭智能体即使在识别背叛后仍继续合作。 A single deterministic Byzantine agent eliminates group cooperation entirely; yet a substantial fraction of non-Byzantine agents continue to cooperate even after detecting the betrayal.
通信约束只有在智能体知晓其存在时才会降低合作率：当消息可见性受到限制但这一限制未被披露时（静默拓扑），合作仍维持在近乎完美的水平。 Communication constraints reduce cooperation only when agents are aware of them: when message visibility is restricted but not disclosed (silent topology), cooperation remains near-perfect.
LLM 智能体在不同模型家族中呈现出两种稳定的行为类型：背叛倾向型（DP）智能体在大多数试验中会于识别背叛后转向背叛，而合作坚持型（CP）智能体尽管反复遭到利用，仍继续尝试合作，从而使背叛者在对抗性条件下获得显著更高的收益。 LLM agents exhibit two stable behavioral archetypes across model families: Defection-Prone (DP) agents, which switch to defection after detecting betrayal in the majority of trials, and Cooperation-Persistent (CP) agents, which continue attempting cooperation despite repeated exploitation, resulting in significantly higher payoffs for defectors under adversarial conditions.
2 背景与相关工作2 Background and Related Work
2.1 廉价沟通与协调博弈2.1 Cheap talk and coordination games
Crawford 和 Sobel [3] 表明，即使没有具有约束力的承诺，博弈开始前无成本的消息也能传递信息。 Crawford and Sobel [3] showed that costless pre-play messages can transmit information even without binding commitments. Farrell [6] 进一步证明，廉价沟通有助于在协调博弈的多个均衡之间作出选择。 Farrell [6] further demonstrated that cheap talk can help select among multiple equilibria in coordination games. 一个典型例子是猎鹿博弈 [18]，其中参与者更偏好收益占优的均衡（相互合作），但也面临策略风险，因为独自合作会带来最差的结果。 A canonical example is the Stag Hunt [18], where players prefer the payoff-dominant equilibrium (mutual cooperation) but face strategic risk, since cooperating alone yields the worst outcome. 在此类情境中，廉价沟通（cheap talk）使智能体能够传达其预期行动并就共同策略达成一致，从而降低这一风险 [5]。 In such settings, cheap talk can mitigate this risk by enabling agents to signal their intended actions and align on a common strategy [5].
2.2 拜占庭容错2.2 Byzantine fault tolerance
拜占庭将军问题 [11] 研究的是，当部分智能体表现出任意行为或恶意行为时，如何达成共识。 The Byzantine Generals Problem [11] studies how to achieve consensus when some agents behave arbitrarily or maliciously. 我们将这一框架应用于多智能体 AI：拜占庭智能体通过廉价沟通表示愿意合作，却采取与之冲突的行动，这体现的是策略性失效，而非任意性失效。 We adapt this framework to multi-agent AI: a Byzantine agent signals cooperation through cheap-talk communication but takes a conflicting action, representing strategic rather than arbitrary failure.
2.3 LLM 协调与通信拓扑2.3 LLM coordination and communication topology
基于博弈论的 LLM 智能体评估是一个不断发展的研究领域 [19]，已有研究表明，上下文和表述框架可以使智能体的行为在合作策略与竞争策略之间转变 [8, 13, 22]。 Game-theoretic evaluation of LLM agents is a growing area [19], with prior work showing that context and framing can shift behavior between cooperative and competitive strategies [8, 13, 22]. 网络结构通过限制智能体能够观察到哪些消息，进一步影响协调 [9]。 Network structure further shapes coordination by constraining which messages agents can observe [9]. 我们的研究直接建立在 [14] 的基础上，该研究表明，廉价沟通可将猎鹿博弈中的合作率从 0% 提高至 96.7%；我们进一步考察这一益处在拓扑限制下是否仍然存在，以及尤为关键的，智能体对这些限制的知晓是否会影响结果。 We build directly on [14], which showed that cheap talk raises Stag Hunt cooperation from 0% to 96.7%, and investigate whether this benefit persists under topology restrictions and, critically, whether agents’ awareness of these constraints affects outcomes.
2.4 LLM 系统中的拜占庭行为与对抗性智能体2.4 Byzantine behavior and adversarial agents in LLM systems
近期研究开始超越经典拜占庭容错 [11] 的范畴，考察 LLM 多智能体系统中的对抗性智能体。 Recent work has begun examining adversarial agents in LLM multi-agent systems beyond classical Byzantine fault tolerance [11]. Lee 和 Tiwari [12] 表明，单个遭到入侵的智能体可以通过通信渠道，在相互连接的智能体之间传播恶意提示，这在结构上类似于我们的拜占庭廉价沟通设置。 Lee and Tiwari [12] show that a single compromised agent can propagate malicious prompts across interconnected agents via communication channels, a structural analog to our Byzantine cheap-talk setting. Chen 等人 [2] 提出了基于区块链的共识机制，使协调能够抵御拜占庭智能体的影响，凸显了这一问题的实际重要性。 Chen et al. [2] propose blockchain-based consensus mechanisms to make coordination robust to Byzantine agents, highlighting the practical importance of this problem. 我们的工作侧重于行为层面而非架构层面的稳健性，从而补充了这些方法：我们研究非拜占庭智能体如何应对对抗性行为，以及协调在何种条件下会崩溃或得以维持。 Our work complements these approaches by focusing on behavioral rather than architectural robustness: we study how non-Byzantine agents respond to adversarial behavior, and under which conditions coordination breaks down or is sustained.
3 实验设置3 Experimental Setup
3.1 博弈3.1 Game
我们采用 4 人猎鹿博弈 [14]。 We use a 4-player Stag Hunt [14]. 每轮包含两个阶段：(1) 通信阶段，智能体同时广播一个词（不具约束力的廉价沟通）；(2) 行动阶段，每个智能体选择猎鹿或猎兔。 Each round has two stages: (1) communication stage, where agents simultaneously broadcast one word (non-binding cheap talk), and (2) action stage, where each agent chooses Hunt Stag or Hunt Hare.
每项实验包含多次相互独立的试验；每次试验都是由同一组智能体参与的完整 5 轮博弈，而一轮是指一次试验中通信与行动的单次迭代。 Each experiment consists of multiple independent trials; each trial is a full 5-round game with the same group of agents, while a round refers to a single iteration of communication and action within a trial. 智能体 $i$ 的收益为： The payoff for agent $i$ is: 
| | $u_{i}=\begin{cases}10&\text{if all 4 choose {Stag},}\\ 3&\text{if }i\text{ chooses {Hare},}\\ 0&\text{if }i\text{ chooses {Stag} and any other chooses {Hare}.}\end{cases}$ |
智能体能够观察同一次试验中此前各轮的完整历史（所有消息、行动和收益）。 Agents observe the full history of prior rounds within the same trial (all messages, actions, and payoffs). 各次试验相互独立：每次试验都以全新的提示、空白历史开始，且不保留过往交互的记忆，从而确保观察到的行为反映的是单次博弈中的上下文内推理，而非跨试验积累的学习。 Trials are independent: each trial starts with a fresh prompt, empty history, and no memory of past interactions, ensuring that observed behaviors reflect in-context reasoning within a single game rather than accumulated learning across trials.
3.2 提示结构3.2 Prompt structure
每个智能体都会收到一条系统提示，其中指定博弈规则、其玩家 ID 以及收益结构。 Each agent receives a system prompt specifying the game rules, its player ID, and the payoff structure. 在通信阶段，智能体广播一条由单个词构成、不具约束力的消息（“廉价沟通”）；在行动阶段，它们观察可见消息，并以结构化 JSON 生成思维链推理，随后给出最终行动（猎鹿或猎兔）。 At the communication stage, agents broadcast a single-word, non-binding message (“cheap talk”); at the action stage, they observe the visible messages and produce a chain-of-thought reasoning followed by a final action (Hunt Stag or Hunt Hare) in structured JSON.
格式错误的响应会触发一次重新提示；持续失败的情况将被排除（附录 0.A.7）。 Malformed responses trigger a single re-prompt; persistent failures are excluded (Appendix 0.A.7).
3.3 模型3.3 Models
我们通过 DeepInfra 和专有 API 使用六种异构 LLM（温度参数为 0.0）：Mixtral-8x22B-Instruct [10]、Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct [17]、Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct [7]、DeepSeek-V3 [4]、GPT-4o [15] 和 Claude Sonnet 4.6 [1]。 We use six heterogeneous LLMs via DeepInfra and proprietary APIs (temperature 0.0): Mixtral-8x22B-Instruct [10], Qwen2.5-72B-Instruct [17], Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct [7], DeepSeek-V3 [4], GPT-4o [15], and Claude Sonnet 4.6 [1]. 在所有试验中，每个玩家始终被分配同一个模型。 Each player is consistently assigned the same model across all trials.
在拜占庭条件下，对抗性（拜占庭）智能体在每次试验开始时从智能体集合中随机选出，并在该次试验的所有轮次中保持不变。 In Byzantine conditions, the adversarial (Byzantine) agent is selected at random from the set of agents at the start of each trial and remains fixed for all rounds within that trial.
3.4 条件3.4 Conditions
硬拜占庭（$k\in\{0,1,2\}$）：$k$ 个拜占庭智能体始终广播“stag”，却选择猎兔；每个 $k$ 值进行 20 次试验（见附录 0.A.5）。 Hard Byzantine ( $k\in\{0,1,2\}$ ): $k$ Byzantine agents always broadcast “stag” but choose Hare; 20 trials per value of $k$ (see Appendix 0.A.5).
软拜占庭（$k{=}1$，$p{=}0.5$）：对抗者以 0.5 的概率背叛；进行 20 次试验。 Soft Byzantine ( $k{=}1$ , $p{=}0.5$ ): adversary defects with probability 0.5; 20 trials.
显式拓扑：考虑三种通信结构——广播结构（所有智能体都能看到所有消息）、环形结构（每个智能体只能看到其两个邻居的消息）和星形结构（一个中心智能体能看到所有消息，而其他智能体只能看到中心智能体的消息）。 Explicit topology: three communication structures are considered—broadcast (all agents see all messages), ring (each agent sees only its two neighbors’ messages), and star (one central agent sees all messages, while others see only the central agent). 智能体会被明确告知这些可见性限制；每种拓扑进行 20 次试验。 Agents are explicitly informed of these visibility constraints; 20 trials per topology.
静默拓扑：施加与显式拓扑条件相同的消息可见性限制，但不向智能体提供任何可见性提示，并从提示中移除玩家数量；每种拓扑进行 20 次试验。 Silent topology: the same message visibility constraints as in the explicit topology condition are applied, but agents receive no visibility cues, and the number of players is removed from the prompt; 20 trials per topology.
拜占庭 $\times$ 星形：在星形拓扑中设置 1 个硬对抗者，分为两个子条件：中心节点为对抗者与中心节点为诚实智能体；每个子条件进行 20 次试验（见第 4.4 节）。 Byzantine $\times$ star: star topology with 1 hard adversary, in two sub-conditions: hub-is-adversarial vs hub-is-honest; 20 trials per sub-condition (see Section 4.4).
此外，为评估不同群体规模下原型的稳定性，我们使用可用的模型组，在 $n\in\{2,3,5,6\}$ 名玩家的设置中复现硬拜占庭（$k{=}1$）条件，以补充主要的 4 人设置；结果见第 7 节。 Additionally, to assess archetype stability across group sizes, we replicate the hard Byzantine ( $k{=}1$ ) condition across $n\in\{2,3,5,6\}$ players, complementing the main 4-player setting, using available model cohorts; results are reported in Section 7.
3.5 指标3.5 Metrics
我们使用三个指标评估结果：群体合作率（$\Phi$）：所有智能体均选择猎鹿（Hunt Stag）的（试验，轮次）对所占的百分比；非拜占庭智能体合作率（$\rho_{nb}$）：非拜占庭智能体的选择中，猎鹿选择所占的百分比；平均收益（$\bar{u}$）：非拜占庭智能体各次观测的每轮收益均值。 We evaluate outcomes using three metrics: Group cooperation rate ( $\Phi$ ): percentage of (trial, round) pairs where all agents choose Hunt Stag; Non-Byzantine cooperation rate ( $\rho_{nb}$ ): percentage of Hunt Stag choices among non-Byzantine agents; Average payoff ( $\bar{u}$ ): mean per-round payoff across non-Byzantine agent observations.

4.2 行为原型4.2 Behavioral Archetypes
在 $k{=}1$ 条件下，我们观察到两种稳定的行为原型，其操作性定义依据是智能体在遭到拜占庭智能体背叛后（即某个智能体发出猎鹿信号却选择猎兔时）如何作出反应。 We observe two stable behavioral archetypes under $k{=}1$ , defined operationally based on how agents respond after being betrayed by a Byzantine agent (i.e., when an agent signals Stag but chooses Hare).
对于给定试验中的每个非拜占庭智能体，我们考察其在首次观察到背叛之后的行动。 For each non-Byzantine agent within a given trial, we examine its actions following the first observed betrayal. 如果一个智能体在首次遭遇背叛后，在该次试验的所有后续轮次中都选择猎兔，则称其在该次试验内永久转向背叛。 An agent is said to permanently switch to defection within a trial if, after the first betrayal, it chooses Hare in all subsequent rounds of that trial.
例如，在一次试验中： For example, in a single trial:
R1：猎鹿（发生背叛） R1: Stag (betrayal occurs)
R2–R5：猎兔、猎兔、猎兔、猎兔 R2–R5: Hare, Hare, Hare, Hare
在这种情况下，该智能体被归类为在该次试验内永久转向背叛。 In this case, the agent is classified as having permanently switched to defection within that trial.
随后，我们汇总该智能体作为非拜占庭智能体参与的所有试验中的这一行为。 We then aggregate this behavior across all trials in which the agent is non-Byzantine. 对于每个模型家族，我们计算在这些试验中智能体表现出永久转向背叛的试验所占的比例。 For each model family, we compute the fraction of such trials in which agents exhibit a permanent switch to defection. 如果这一比例超过 50%，则将该模型家族归类为背叛倾向型（Defection-Prone，DP），否则归类为合作坚持型（Cooperation-Persistent，CP）。 A model family is classified as Defection-Prone (DP) if this fraction exceeds 50%, and as Cooperation-Persistent (CP) otherwise. 例如，如果 Mixtral 在 10 次试验中作为非拜占庭智能体参与，并在其中 8 次试验中表现出永久转向背叛，则其比率为 $8/10=0.8>0.5$，因此被归类为 DP。 For instance, if Mixtral is non-Byzantine in 10 trials and exhibits a permanent switch to defection in 8 of them, its rate is $8/10=0.8>0.5$ , and it is therefore classified as DP.
这种行为分化在各模型家族中表现一致：Mixtral 和 DeepSeek 被归类为 DP，而 Qwen 和 Llama 被归类为 CP。 This behavioral split is consistent across model families: Mixtral and DeepSeek are classified as DP, while Qwen and Llama are classified as CP. 收益差距十分明显：DP 智能体每轮获得 2.42，而 CP 智能体每轮获得 0.45（相差 5.4 $\times$）。 The payoff gap is stark: DP agents earn 2.42/round vs. CP agents’ 0.45/round (a 5.4 $\times$ difference). 尽管在对抗条件下，背叛对个体而言是理性的，但它会导致群体合作彻底瓦解。 While defection is individually rational under adversarial conditions, it leads to a complete breakdown of group cooperation. 我们观察到模型家族与行为原型之间存在稳健的相关性，但无法确定其因果机制：RLHF 训练数据、模型规模和指令遵循风格都与模型家族身份相混杂。 We observe a robust correlation between model family and behavioral archetype, but cannot identify a causal mechanism: RLHF training data, model scale, and instruction-following style are all confounded with family identity.

5 通信拓扑5 Communication Topology
我们将通信结构建模为有向图 $G=(V,E)$。 We model the communication structure as a directed graph $G=(V,E)$ . 对于每种拓扑，智能体 $i$ 可见的消息集合由其邻域 $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{j\in V\mid(j,i)\in E\}$ 定义。 For each topology, the set of messages visible to agent $i$ is defined by its neighborhood $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{j\in V\mid(j,i)\in E\}$ . 我们考察三种配置： We investigate three configurations:
广播：$G$ 为完全图 $K_{n}$，其中 $\forall i,\mathcal{N}(i)=V\setminus\{i\}$。 Broadcast: $G$ is a complete graph $K_{n}$ , where $\forall i,\mathcal{N}(i)=V\setminus\{i\}$ .
环形：智能体按 $1\dots n$ 排列，满足 $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{i-1,i+1\}\pmod{n}$。 Ring: Agents are ordered $1\dots n$ such that $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{i-1,i+1\}\pmod{n}$ .
星形：存在一个中心节点 $h\in V$，满足 $\mathcal{N}(h)=V\setminus\{h\}$ 且 $\forall i\neq h,\mathcal{N}(i)=\{h\}$。 Star: A hub $h\in V$ such that $\mathcal{N}(h)=V\setminus\{h\}$ and $\forall i\neq h,\mathcal{N}(i)=\{h\}$ .
每种配置均在两种条件下进行研究：显式条件（告知智能体其可见性限制）和静默条件（采用相同的消息过滤方式，不提供提示，并移除玩家数量信息）。 Each configuration is studied under two conditions: explicit (agents told their visibility constraints) and silent (same filtering, no cues, player count removed).
显式环形和星形拓扑使群体合作率降至接近零（表 2），尽管不存在对抗者，其严重程度仍接近硬拜占庭条件。 Explicit ring and star collapse cooperation to near-zero group rate (Table 2), approaching hard Byzantine severity despite no adversary being present. DP/CP 原型分化在显式拓扑中再次出现：在环形拓扑中，Llama 的合作率为 67%，Qwen 为 59%，而 Mixtral（7%）和 DeepSeek（8%）几乎始终背叛，呈现出与拜占庭条件下相同的模型家族对应关系。 The DP/CP archetype split reappears in explicit topologies: in the ring, Llama cooperates at 67% and Qwen at 59%, while Mixtral (7%) and DeepSeek (8%) defect almost universally, mirroring the same model-family alignment as under Byzantine conditions.
静默条件产生了截然不同的结果：广播和环形拓扑实现了完全合作（100%/100%），而静默星形拓扑下非拜占庭智能体的合作率达到 88%，虽然未实现完全合作，但显著高于显式星形拓扑（23.8%）。 The silent conditions produce a strikingly different outcome: broadcast and ring achieve perfect cooperation (100%/100%), while silent star reaches 88% Non-Byzantine cooperation, which is substantially higher than explicit star (23.8%) though not perfect. 由于静默环形拓扑中的智能体只能看到其他三名玩家中两名的消息，却仍能实现完全合作，因此仅凭信息损失无法解释显式条件下的合作崩溃。 Since silent-ring agents see only two of three co-players’ messages yet cooperate perfectly, information loss alone cannot explain the explicit collapse.
其机制是元推理：我们推测，当被告知“你只能看到来自邻居的消息”时，智能体会推断存在意图未知的不可见玩家，从而引发策略不确定性；没有这种框架设定时，它们则按消息的字面含义理解消息。 The mechanism is meta-reasoning: told “you can only see messages from your neighbors,” we conjecture that agents infer unseen players with unknown intentions, triggering strategic uncertainty; without such framing, they take messages at face value. 这与 LLM 博弈中的框架效应 [13] 相呼应，并揭示出 DP/CP 原型是由提示诱发的，而非内在固有的：移除策略框架后，这些原型便会消失。 This parallels framing effects in LLM game play [13] and reveals that the DP/CP archetypes are prompt-elicited, not intrinsic: they vanish when strategic framing is removed.

8 安全性与安全保障方面的启示8 Security and Safety Implications
我们的研究发现将多智能体 LLM 协调失败重新界定为具体的安全漏洞，而非抽象的博弈论现象。 Our findings reframe multi-agent LLM coordination failures as concrete security vulnerabilities rather than abstract game-theoretic phenomena.
8.1 作为对抗性提示注入的拜占庭廉价沟通8.1 Byzantine cheap talk as adversarial prompt injection
在我们的设置中，拜占庭智能体（通过廉价沟通传递合作信号，却在行动阶段背叛的智能体）在结构上等同于通过通信渠道实施的对抗性提示注入攻击 [12]。 A Byzantine agent in our setting (one that signals cooperation via cheap talk but defects at the action stage) is structurally equivalent to an adversarial prompt injection attack delivered through a communication channel [12]. 注入的内容（“stag”）旨在诱导特定行为（合作），而攻击者追求的却是与之不相容的目标（背叛）。 The injected content (“stag”) is designed to elicit a specific behavior (cooperation) while the adversary pursues an incompatible goal (defection). 经典提示注入针对单个智能体的系统提示，而拜占庭廉价沟通利用的是社会推断层：非拜占庭智能体根据收到的消息更新信念，其受到的损害并非源于直接的指令覆盖，而是源于错误信念的形成。 Unlike classical prompt injection, which targets a single agent’s system prompt, Byzantine cheap talk exploits the social inference layer: Non-Byzantine agents update their beliefs based on received messages and are harmed not by direct instruction override but by false belief formation. 我们的结果量化了这种损害：单个拜占庭智能体就足以使群体协调完全瓦解，而且非拜占庭智能体即使识破欺骗也无法恢复协调，因为博弈要求全体一致的结构使任何残余疑虑都可能带来灾难性的代价。 Our results quantify the damage: a single Byzantine agent is sufficient to eliminate group coordination entirely, and Non-Byzantine agents cannot recover even after detecting the deception, because the game’s unanimity structure makes any residual doubt catastrophically costly. 具体而言，由于成功合作要求所有智能体都选择 Stag，即使只有一个智能体背叛，也必然导致失败，因此，其他智能体即便认清了欺骗的来源，选择避免合作仍是理性的。 In particular, since successful cooperation requires all agents to choose Stag, even a single agent’s defection guarantees failure, making it rational for others to avoid cooperation despite recognizing the source of the deception.
8.2 作为攻击面的通信拓扑8.2 Communication topology as an attack surface
关于显式拓扑与隐式拓扑的研究发现揭示了一个不太明显的漏洞：向智能体披露网络架构本身就可能成为攻击途径。 The explicit-vs.-silent topology finding reveals a less obvious vulnerability: disclosing network architecture to agents can itself be an attack vector. 当智能体被告知其可见范围受限时，它们便开始推断不可见同伴的情况，由此引入策略不确定性，并最终导致合作瓦解，即使不存在任何对抗性智能体也是如此。 Agents that are informed of restricted visibility begin to reason about unseen peers, which introduces strategic uncertainty and ultimately collapses cooperation even in the absence of any adversarial agent. 这在已部署系统中有直接对应的情形：向单个智能体暴露智能体角色、通信图或系统架构的框架 [21]，可能会无意中破坏协调。 This has a direct analogue in deployed systems: frameworks that expose agent roles, communication graphs, or system architecture to individual agents [21] may inadvertently undermine coordination. 这一启示有悖直觉：当智能体对系统结构进行元推理时，提高系统结构的透明度可能会降低系统性能。 The implication is counterintuitive: increasing transparency about system structure can decrease system performance when agents engage in meta-reasoning about that structure.
8.3 对齐税8.3 The alignment tax
DP/CP 的行为分化揭示了我们所称的对齐税：在标准（非对抗性）条件下表现得更合作、更具亲社会性的模型，即持续合作型（CP）模型，在面对对抗性同伴时承受了不成比例的损失，其收益仅为背叛倾向型模型的 $5.4\times$ 分之一。 The DP/CP behavioral split reveals what we term an alignment tax: models that behave in a more cooperative and prosocial manner under standard (non-adversarial) conditions, i.e., Cooperation-Persistent (CP) models, pay a disproportionate penalty when facing adversarial peers, earning $5.4\times$ less than defection-prone models.
我们无法根据这些实验确定 CP 行为究竟源于 RLHF 对齐训练、模型规模还是架构差异，因为这些因素在不同模型家族之间相互混杂。 We cannot determine from our experiments whether CP behavior is a product of RLHF alignment training, model scale, or architectural differences, as these are confounded across model families. 然而，这种分化在三个模型组中均保持一致（第 6 节），表明这一模式具有系统性，而非偶然现象。 However, the consistency of the split across three model cohorts (Section 6) suggests the pattern is systematic rather than incidental.
如果亲社会训练产生的模型会被对抗性同伴系统性地利用，那么这就构成了一种与标准对齐议程正交的安全隐患：模型在个体层面可以表现良好，但在集体层面却可能存在脆弱性。 If prosocial training produces models that are systematically exploitable by adversarial peers, this represents a safety concern orthogonal to the standard alignment agenda: a model can be individually well-behaved yet collectively vulnerable. 换言之，孤立地优化合作行为，可能会无意中产生独立运行时表现良好、但在对抗性环境中与其他智能体交互时失效的智能体。 In other words, optimizing for cooperative behavior in isolation may inadvertently create agents that perform well on their own but fail when interacting with other agents in adversarial environments.
9 讨论与结论9 Discussion and Conclusion
LLM 智能体之间的廉价沟通（cheap talk）协调虽然在标准（非对抗性）条件下有效 [14]，但在两个方面存在脆弱性：(1) 拜占庭智能体实施的对抗性欺骗，以及 (2) 通信拓扑限制。 Cheap-talk coordination among LLM agents, while effective under standard (non-adversarial) conditions [14], is fragile along two axes: (1) adversarial deception through Byzantine agents, and (2) restrictions on communication topology.
在强拜占庭攻击下，非拜占庭智能体在一轮内便能发现背叛，但仍有约 50% 继续合作。 Under hard Byzantine attack, non-Byzantine agents detect betrayal within one round, yet approximately 50% continue to cooperate. 当对手以随机方式背叛（$p{=}0.5$）时，它通过维持足够的合作信号，使其他智能体始终无法确定是否仍有可能实现协调，从而获得 $11\%$ 的收益优势。 When the adversary defects stochastically ( $p{=}0.5$ ), it achieves an $11\%$ payoff advantage by maintaining enough cooperative signals to keep other agents uncertain about whether coordination is still possible. 因此，非拜占庭智能体迟迟不愿放弃猎鹿策略，使对手得以利用这种不确定性。 As a result, non-Byzantine agents hesitate to abandon the stag strategy, allowing the adversary to exploit this uncertainty.
只有在智能体被明确告知存在拓扑限制时，这些限制才会导致合作崩溃。 Topology restrictions collapse cooperation only when agents are explicitly informed about them. 当拓扑限制与拜占庭行为相结合时，性能退化更为严重：受限的可见性会放大不确定性，使协调更加难以维持。 When combined with Byzantine behavior, the degradation is more severe: restricted visibility amplifies uncertainty, making coordination even harder to sustain. DP/CP 分化在各种对抗性条件下均保持稳定，但在未告知拓扑限制的条件下消失，这表明这些行为类型并非固定特质，而是由提示中对交互情境的设定方式塑造的。 The DP/CP split is stable across adversarial conditions but disappears under silent topology, indicating that these archetypes are not fixed traits but are instead shaped by how the interaction is framed in the prompt.
这两种脆弱性具有共同的机制。 Both vulnerabilities share a common mechanism. 在这两种情况下，合作瓦解都是因为智能体无法确定其他智能体的意图：这种不确定性要么源于欺骗性信号（拜占庭智能体），要么源于不完整的可见性（拓扑限制）。 In each case, cooperation breaks down because agents become uncertain about others’ intentions: either due to deceptive signals (Byzantine agents) or incomplete visibility (topology restrictions). 面对这种不确定性，智能体会采取更安全的策略，而这些策略在集体层面阻碍了协调。 Faced with this uncertainty, agents adopt safer strategies, which collectively prevent coordination.
多智能体 LLM 系统正日益广泛地部署于现实场景中 [21]。 Multi-agent LLM systems are increasingly being deployed in real-world settings [21]. 在此类情境中，这些脆弱性会转化为实际风险：即使各个智能体都按预期行事，依赖智能体通信的系统仍可能失效。 In such contexts, these fragilities translate into practical risks: systems that rely on agent communication may fail even when individual agents behave as intended.
我们的实验仅限于 4 人猎鹿博弈，每种条件下进行 20 次试验。 Our experiments are limited to a 4-player Stag Hunt with 20 trials per condition. 这种基于全体一致的收益结构要求所有智能体都选择猎鹿才能成功合作，因此协调尤为脆弱，因为仅一次背叛就足以导致彻底失败。 The unanimity-based payoff structure, where successful cooperation requires all agents to choose Stag, makes coordination particularly fragile, as a single defection is sufficient to cause complete failure. 与采用更渐进或允许部分奖励的结构的情境相比，这可能会放大所观察到的对抗性行为的影响。 This may amplify the observed impact of adversarial behavior compared to settings with more gradual or partial reward structures.
在更多模型组中开展的重复实验确认了主要发现具有可推广性，其中 Qwen 是唯一在所有受测设置中始终表现为合作坚持型（Cooperation-Persistent）的模型（第 6 节）。 Replication across additional model cohorts confirms that the main findings generalize, with Qwen as the only consistently Cooperation-Persistent model across all tested settings (Section 6).
未来工作应考察这些发现如何推广到其他情境，包括不同的博弈结构、更大规模或规模各异的群体，以及更长的交互时程（即每次试验包含更多轮次）。 Future work should examine how these findings extend to other settings, including different game structures, larger or heterogeneous group sizes, and longer interaction horizons (i.e., more rounds per trial). 研究语言和上下文如何影响协调也很有价值，例如，当使用不同语言（如英语与法语）描述博弈，或采用不同的提示表述时，是否会出现类似行为。 It would also be valuable to study how language and context affect coordination, for example, whether similar behaviors emerge when the game is specified in different languages (e.g., English vs. French) or under different prompt formulations.

非 JSON 响应：如果响应完全无法解析为 JSON，引擎会使用相同的提示再次请求，并在末尾附上模式提醒。 Non-JSON response: if the response cannot be parsed as JSON at all, the engine re-prompts identically with a schema reminder appended.
