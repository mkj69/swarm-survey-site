Abstract摘要
LLM-based multi-agent systems (MAS) have shown promise in tackling complex tasks. However, existing solutions often suffer from limited agent coordination and heavy reliance on predefined Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), which demand extensive human input. To address these limitations, we propose MegaAgent, a large-scale autonomous LLM-based multi-agent system. MegaAgent generates agents based on task complexity and enables dynamic task decomposition, parallel execution, efficient communication, and comprehensive system monitoring of agents. In evaluations, MegaAgent demonstrates exceptional performance, successfully developing a Gobang game within 800 seconds and scaling up to 590 agents in a national policy simulation to generate multi-domain policies. It significantly outperforms existing systems, such as MetaGPT, in both task completion efficiency and scalability. By eliminating the need for predefined SOPs, MegaAgent demonstrates exceptional scalability and autonomy, setting a foundation for advancing true autonomy in MAS.[fn1]
基于 LLM 的多智能体系统（MAS）在处理复杂任务方面展现出了潜力。然而，现有方案往往存在智能体协调能力有限、严重依赖预定义标准操作程序（SOP）的问题，而这些程序需要大量人工投入。为解决这些局限，我们提出了 MegaAgent，一种基于 LLM 的大规模自主多智能体系统。MegaAgent 根据任务复杂度生成智能体，并支持动态任务分解、并行执行、高效通信以及对智能体的全面系统监控。在评估中，MegaAgent 表现出色，成功在 800 秒内开发出一款五子棋游戏，并在国家政策模拟中扩展至 590 个智能体，以生成多个领域的政策。在任务完成效率和可扩展性方面，它均显著优于 MetaGPT 等现有系统。通过消除对预定义标准操作程序（SOP）的需求，MegaAgent 展现出卓越的可扩展性和自主性，为推动多智能体系统（MAS）实现真正的自主性奠定了基础。[fn1]

(2023)，这类系统通过协调多个 LLM 智能体来处理复杂任务。例如，MetaGPT 引入元编程框架来模拟软件开发过程 Hong et al. (2023)，而 Simulacra Park et al. However, existing MAS have two limitations. (1) They fail to achieve adaptive task coordination when the task is big and complex e.g. generating hundreds of agents for a social simulation; and do not consider the coordination between a large scale of agents. (2) Most systems heavily depend on user-defined configurations, including predefined agent roles, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and static communication graphs Hong et al. (2023); Chen et al. (2023b); Wu et al. (2023b). This approach limits flexibility and requires significant human effort when deploying numerous agents to complete a task.
然而，现有 MAS 存在两个局限。(1) 当任务规模庞大且复杂时，例如为社会模拟生成数百个智能体，它们无法实现自适应任务协调；同时也未考虑大规模智能体之间的协调。(2) 大多数系统严重依赖用户定义的配置，包括预定义的智能体角色、标准操作程序（SOP）以及静态通信图 Hong et al. (2023); Chen et al. (2023b); Wu et al. (2023b)。这种方法限制了灵活性，而且在部署大量智能体来完成任务时需要投入大量人力。
Addressing the above limitations presents the following key challenges: (1) Facilitating adaptive and effective communication among agents and with external file systems. As tasks grow in complexity and scale, managing communication becomes increasingly difficult, especially when incorporating parallelism and coordinating multiple agents across different rounds of communication Zhang et al. (2024c). (2) Ensuring that each agent completes its task accurately without relying on predefined SOPs. LLM agents often generate hallucinated outputs Huang et al. (2023b) or fail to complete tasks correctly within a single round Liu et al. (2023a); Andriushchenko et al. (2024), necessitating robust mechanisms to ensure reliability and correctness. This is particularly critical in multi-agent systems, where hallucinations can propagate and compromise the entire system’s performance Zhang et al. (2024c); Lee and Tiwari (2024).
解决上述局限面临以下关键挑战：(1) 促进智能体之间以及智能体与外部文件系统之间的自适应、高效通信。随着任务复杂度和规模的增加，通信管理变得愈发困难，尤其是在引入并行机制并协调多个智能体跨不同轮次进行通信时 Zhang et al. (2024c)。(2) 确保每个智能体在不依赖预定义 SOP 的情况下准确完成任务。LLM 智能体经常生成含有幻觉的输出 Huang et al. (2023b)，或无法在单轮内正确完成任务 Liu et al. (2023a); Andriushchenko et al. (2024)，因此需要稳健的机制来确保可靠性和正确性。这在多智能体系统中尤为关键，因为幻觉可能传播并损害整个系统的性能 Zhang et al. (2024c); Lee and Tiwari (2024)。

MegaAgent decomposes large tasks into multiple hierarchical subtasks (analogous to processes), with each subtask completed by a dedicated group of agents (similar to threads). Communication occurs either within agent groups or between them as needed, resembling inter-process communication in an OS. 2.2 Hierachical Task Management2.2 层级化任务管理
2.2.1 Multi-level Task Splitting2.2.1 多级任务拆分
To efficiently manage complex tasks in large-scale MAS, we implement a multi-level task management framework. Boss Agent is responsible for decomposing the main task into manageable subtasks upon receiving the meta-prompt from a user. Each subtask is delegated to a specialized admin agent with a well-defined role by Boss Agent. If a subtask is too complex for an admin agent to complete independently, it can recruit additional agents to handle specific components. These newly created agents can, in turn, recruit more agents if needed, assuming the role of admin agent themselves, as depicted in Level 2 and Level 3 in Figure 1. This recursive task-splitting mechanism enables the system to adapt dynamically as task complexity increases.
为高效管理大规模多智能体系统（MAS）中的复杂任务，我们实现了一个多级任务管理框架。主管智能体（Boss Agent）在收到用户的元提示（meta-prompt）后，负责将主任务分解为可管理的子任务。主管智能体将每个子任务委派给一个具有明确角色的专门管理智能体（admin agent）。如果某个子任务过于复杂，管理智能体无法独立完成，则可以招募更多智能体来处理特定部分。这些新创建的智能体又可以根据需要招募更多智能体，并由此承担管理智能体的角色，如图 1 中的第 2 层和第 3 层所示。这种递归任务拆分机制使系统能够随着任务复杂度的增加而动态调整。
To enhance efficiency, we implement a parallel mechanism for agent groups operating at the same level. For instance, the two agent groups in Level 2 of Figure 1 can work in parallel, with one generating economic policies and the other developing health policies. This parallelization reduces overall task completion time.
为提高效率，我们为处于同一层级的智能体组实现了并行机制。例如，图 1 第 2 层中的两个智能体组可以并行工作，一个生成经济政策，另一个制定卫生政策。这种并行化缩短了任务的总体完成时间。
2.2.2 Hierarchical Coordination Mechanism2.2.2 层级化协调机制
Effective task execution in MegaAgent is driven by a two-layer hierarchical coordination structure: (1) Intra-group Chat, where agents within the same task group collaborate by sharing updates through prompt-based communication, ensuring smooth progress and effective task execution when interaction is required, as indicated by the black double-arrow line in Figure 1; and (2) Inter-group Chat, where admin agents from different groups communicate to resolve task dependencies and coordinate cross-group efforts, as represented by the yellow double-arrow line in Figure 1. For instance, in the software development experiment discussed in subsection 3.2, the software implementation must adhere to the game logic designer’s requirements. Ordinary agents are restricted from directly communicating with agents outside their group to enhance efficiency.

2.2.3 Message Queue Mechanism2.2.3 消息队列机制
For each agent, we implement a message queue system based on the producer-consumer paradigm to manage asynchronous communications. External agents act as producers that generate messages through function calls, while the target agent acts as the consumer that periodically retrieves batched messages via a polling mechanism. This architecture introduces three operational states: 1. Idle State, where the consumer agent has no message, and will not have computational cost; 2. Processing State: Upon message acquisition, the agent enters an atomic execution phase where LLM inference engines process the message batch. The message received during this state will stay in the message queue until the next processing state; 3. Response State: Processed outputs undergo validity verification before being dispatched through designated function call. After that, the agent will check whether its message queue is empty. If so, it will enter the Idle state; if not, it will enter the Processing state. This approach can merge multiple messages to process at one time while sufficiently utilize the throughput of the LLM, thus balancing the cost and efficiency.
我们为每个智能体实现了一个基于生产者—消费者范式的消息队列系统，以管理异步通信。外部智能体充当生产者，通过函数调用生成消息；目标智能体则充当消费者，通过轮询机制定期批量获取消息。该架构引入了三种运行状态：1.空闲状态：消费者智能体没有消息，不会产生计算开销；2.处理状态：获取消息后，智能体进入原子执行阶段，由大语言模型（LLM）推理引擎处理该批消息。在此状态下收到的消息将保留在消息队列中，直到下一次进入处理状态；3.响应状态：处理后的输出经过有效性验证，再通过指定的函数调用发送。随后，智能体将检查其消息队列是否为空。如果为空，则进入空闲状态；否则，进入处理状态。这种方法可以将多条消息合并后一次性处理，同时充分利用 LLM 的吞吐能力，从而平衡成本与效率。
2.2.4 File Management2.2.4 文件管理
To enable effective interaction between LLM agents and external files, we introduce an external storage module that manages all file-related tasks. This module includes components such as agent execution logs, a memory database, task monitoring tools, Python code execution support, shared files, and individualized agent checklists. To ensure consistent and accurate file management, we propose the following two designs:
为实现 LLM 智能体与外部文件之间的有效交互，我们引入了一个外部存储模块，用于管理所有与文件相关的任务。该模块包含智能体执行日志、记忆数据库、任务监控工具、Python 代码执行支持、共享文件以及各智能体专属的检查清单等组件。为确保文件管理的一致性和准确性，我们提出以下两项设计：
(1) Git-Based Version Control. To maintain file consistency, we integrate a Git-based version control mechanism. Since agents may spend considerable time editing files after reading them, concurrent modifications by other agents could cause conflicts. To prevent this, an agent retrieves the file’s current Git commit hash upon reading it. Before making changes, the agent submits this hash to the file management system, which commits the updates, merges them into the latest HEAD, and prompts the agent to resolve any merge conflicts if necessary. All Git operations are serialized using a global mutex lock to ensure synchronization and prevent race conditions.
(1) 基于 Git 的版本控制。为保持文件一致性，我们集成了基于 Git 的版本控制机制。由于智能体在读取文件后可能花费较长时间进行编辑，其他智能体的并发修改可能引发冲突。为避免这种情况，智能体在读取文件时会获取该文件当前的 Git 提交哈希值。在进行修改之前，智能体会将该哈希值提交给文件管理系统；系统随后提交更新，将其合并到最新的 HEAD，并在必要时提示智能体解决合并冲突。所有 Git 操作均通过全局互斥锁串行执行，以确保同步并防止竞态条件。
(2) Long-Term Memory Management with a Vector Database. Many studies show that LLM agents would forget the conversation history after several rounds due to the token length limit Becker (2024); Xue et al. (2024). To address this, we implement a vector database to store the outputs of agents. Each output is encoded into embeddings using language models and stored in a vector database. Therefore, agents can retrieve relevant memory entries, enabling them to recall past interactions and maintain contextual awareness.
(2) 使用向量数据库进行长期记忆管理。许多研究表明，由于词元（token）长度限制，大语言模型（LLM）智能体会在若干轮交互后遗忘对话历史 Becker (2024); Xue et al. (2024)。为解决这一问题，我们采用向量数据库来存储智能体的输出。每条输出均使用语言模型编码为嵌入向量，并存储在向量数据库中。因此，智能体可以检索相关记忆条目，从而回忆过去的交互并保持对上下文的感知。

2.3 Hierarchical Monitoring2.3 分层监控
To ensure accurate task execution and minimize the propagation of hallucinations Huang et al. (2023b); Hao et al. (2024) in an MAS, we implement a hierarchical monitoring mechanism that facilitates real-time oversight, error correction, and progress validation through a structured process.

2.3.1 Multi-level Monitoring2.3.1 多级监控
The monitoring system in MegaAgent follows a structured, multi-level hierarchy to ensure accurate task completion and prevent error propagation. Then, MegaAgent employs a hierarchical monitoring and coordination framework for each agent as follows:
MegaAgent 的监控系统采用结构化的多级层次体系，以确保任务准确完成并防止错误传播。随后，MegaAgent 为每个智能体采用如下分层监控与协调框架：
Agent-Level Monitoring: Each agent maintains an checklist upon its being generated by its admin agent to document its actions and verify progress. This monitoring ensures accountability and allows agents to independently validate their work before proceeding to the next step.
智能体级监控：每个智能体在由其管理智能体创建后，都会维护一份检查清单，用于记录自身行动并核验进度。这种监控确保责任可追溯，并使智能体能够在进入下一步之前独立核验自身工作。
Group-Level Monitoring: Each agent group is supervised by an admin agent, which tracks the progress of individual agents, ensures smooth execution, and coordinates tasks within the group.
群组级监控：每个智能体群组均由一个管理智能体监督，后者跟踪各个智能体的进度，确保执行顺利，并协调组内任务。
System-Level Monitoring: At the highest level, Boss Agent oversees the outputs of all agent groups upon task completion, ensuring adherence to the correct format and minimizing hallucinated results. This process enhances system-wide consistency, reliability, and correctness.
系统级监控：在最高层级，Boss Agent 会在任务完成后审查所有智能体群组的输出，确保其符合正确格式，并尽量减少幻觉结果。这一过程增强了整个系统的一致性、可靠性和正确性。
2.3.2 Failure Scenarios and Solutions2.3.2 失败场景与解决方案
Monitoring focuses on two key aspects: output format verification and result validation, detailed as follows:
监控重点关注两个关键方面：输出格式检查和结果验证，具体如下：
(1) Output Format Verification. First, the monitoring would focus on the output format of an agent. For example, if an agent generates a Python file that fails to execute, its admin agent would flag the issue, log the error, and prompt a retry. By enforcing consistent output formats, this step prevents downstream agents from misinterpreting data, reducing potential hallucinations.
(1) 输出格式检查。首先，监控会关注智能体的输出格式。例如，如果智能体生成的 Python 文件无法执行，其管理智能体就会标记该问题、记录错误并提示重试。通过强制要求输出格式保持一致，这一步骤可防止下游智能体误解数据，从而减少潜在幻觉。
(2) Result Validation. Once a group completes its tasks, the admin agent reviews the generated outputs and compares them against the initial task requirements. If discrepancies are detected, the admin agent would detail error messages, outline missing or incorrect aspects, prompt the responsible agents to revise their work. This validation process ensures that final outputs align with intended objectives while minimizing task failures.
(2) 结果验证。一个群组完成任务后，管理智能体会审查生成的输出，并将其与最初的任务要求进行比较。如果发现不一致之处，管理智能体会提供详细的错误信息，指出缺失或错误的部分，并提示负责的智能体修改其工作。这一验证过程确保最终输出符合预期目标，同时尽量减少任务失败。
To clarify the monitoring process, we outline common failure scenarios and solutions as follows:

任务重复：有限的上下文记忆可能导致智能体忘记已完成的任务，从而产生冗余操作或任务循环。管理智能体会通过交叉核对智能体的检查清单来识别不一致之处，并根据需要提示采取纠正措施。
Secure Alignment Interruptions: Agents may become unresponsive or repeatedly return alignment-related constraint messages, such as "Sorry, I can’t help with that." In such cases, an admin agent attempts to recruit other agents to finish the task.
安全对齐中断：智能体可能变得无响应，或反复返回与对齐相关的约束提示，例如“抱歉，我无法提供这方面的帮助。”在这种情况下，管理智能体会尝试招募其他智能体来完成任务。

4.2 SOPs in MAS4.2 MAS 中的标准操作规程（SOPs）
Allocating SOPs is a common approach in designing agent profiles and tasks within MAS Hong et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2023a); Park et al. (2023); Zhuge et al. (2024); Shi et al. (2024). These systems define SOPs for both individual agents and their communication protocols. While this method has proven effective in previous works, it has two major limitations: (1) Agents may possess unforeseen capabilities that cannot be anticipated during the human design stage but become relevant during task execution Rivera et al. (2024); Sypherd and Belle (2024); Piatti et al. (2024); (2) As the scale of MAS grows—potentially involving thousands or even billions of agents—designing SOPs manually for each agent becomes infeasible Mou et al. (2024); Pan et al. (2024). To address this, the design mechanism must evolve, leveraging LLMs themselves, as in the LLM-as-the-Judge concept Huang et al. (2024); Chen et al. (2024a), allowing LLMs to autonomously generate SOPs for large-scale MAS.
在 MAS 中，为智能体配置文件和任务分配标准操作程序（SOPs）是一种常见的设计方法 Hong et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2023a); Park et al. (2023); Zhuge et al. (2024); Shi et al. (2024)。这些系统为单个智能体及其通信协议均定义了 SOPs。尽管这种方法在以往研究中已被证明有效，但它存在两大局限：(1) 智能体可能具备人类在设计阶段无法预见、但在任务执行过程中变得相关的能力 Rivera et al. (2024); Sypherd and Belle (2024); Piatti et al. (2024)；(2) 随着 MAS 规模扩大，可能涉及数千乃至数十亿个智能体，为每个智能体手动设计 SOPs 将变得不可行 Mou et al. (2024); Pan et al. (2024)。为解决这一问题，设计机制必须演进，借助 LLMs 自身，如同 LLM-as-the-Judge 概念 Huang et al. (2024); Chen et al. (2024a) 所体现的那样，使 LLMs 能够自主为大规模 MAS 生成 SOPs。

We present MegaAgent, a large-scale autonomous LLM-based multi-agent system enabling dynamic and autonomous coordination, where users only need to provide a meta prompt at the start of the process. MegaAgent demonstrates strong foundational capabilities by outperforming existing baselines on standard benchmarks. Furthermore, through a Gobang game software development experiment, we demonstrate MegaAgent’s superior autonomy and coordination compared to baseline models. Our social simulation on national policy generation highlights MegaAgent’s scalability to hundreds of agents while ensuring effective coordination. With its hierarchical and adaptive design, MegaAgent has the potential to serve as the foundational OS for future MAS. We encourage the research community to further explore enhancing agent coordination to address the increasing demands of large-scale MAS.
我们提出 MegaAgent，这是一种基于 LLM 的大规模自主多智能体系统，能够实现动态、自主的协调，用户只需在流程开始时提供一个元提示。MegaAgent 在标准基准测试中超越了现有基线，展现出强大的基础能力。此外，通过一项五子棋游戏软件开发实验，我们证明了 MegaAgent 相较于基线模型具有更出色的自主性和协调能力。我们围绕国家政策生成开展的社会模拟表明，MegaAgent 能够扩展至数百个智能体，同时确保有效协调。凭借其分层且自适应的设计，MegaAgent 有望成为未来 MAS 的基础操作系统（OS）。我们鼓励研究界进一步探索如何增强智能体协调能力，以满足大规模 MAS 日益增长的需求。
Limitations局限性
Planning and Communication Overhead. The primary bottleneck lies in the planning and communication processes among LLM agents, particularly in translating code into prompts, managing task checklists, maintaining the framework, and debugging. As the number of agents and communication rounds increases, input-output token consumption grows substantially, affecting both efficiency and cost. Future work should explore advanced token summarization, semantic compression, and efficient dialogue storage methods.
规划与通信开销。主要瓶颈在于 LLM 智能体之间的规划与通信过程，尤其体现在将代码转换为提示、管理任务检查清单、维护框架以及调试等方面。随着智能体数量和通信轮次增加，输入输出词元（token）的消耗显著增长，进而影响效率与成本。未来工作应探索先进的词元摘要、语义压缩及高效对话存储方法。
Hallucination in Agent Outputs. Despite using task-specific checklists to monitor agent actions, occasional hallucinations persist, with output formats sometimes deviating from expected requirements. Since the checklists themselves are generated by LLMs, errors may propagate. Addressing this requires more robust verification mechanisms, potentially involving external expert knowledge bases before, during, or after agent response generation.
智能体输出中的幻觉。尽管使用了针对特定任务的检查清单来监控智能体行为，仍偶尔会出现幻觉，且输出格式有时会偏离预期要求。由于检查清单本身也是由 LLMs 生成的，错误可能会传播。解决这一问题需要更稳健的验证机制，可考虑在智能体生成响应之前、期间或之后引入外部专家知识库。
API Cost and Model Integration. MegaAgent’s reliance on GPT-4 incurs high API costs. While cheaper alternatives exist, they may lack generalizability. A promising direction would involve integrating specialized LLMs for specific tasks, leveraging models that excel in certain domains while maintaining efficient communication and data sharing across the LLMs.
API 成本与模型集成。MegaAgent 对 GPT-4 的依赖带来了高昂的 API 成本。虽然存在更便宜的替代方案，但它们可能缺乏泛化能力。一个有前景的方向是针对特定任务集成专用 LLMs，利用在某些领域表现出色的模型，同时维持各 LLMs 之间高效的通信与数据共享。

C.2 Reasoning and PlanningC.2 推理与规划
Reasoning. The Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning framework illustrates that detailed reasoning conducted in multiple stages is notably beneficial for the effectiveness of LLMs, particularly when compared to the limitations of single-step reasoning (Wei et al., 2022). Multi-stage reasoning significantly enhances LLM performance by decomposing problems into sequential steps (Wei et al., 2022), mirroring human cognition. Single-step approaches often neglect critical intermediate phases, reducing solution accuracy (Wei et al., 2022).
推理。思维链（Chain-of-Thought，CoT）推理框架表明，分多个阶段开展细致推理对于提升 LLMs 的效能具有显著益处，尤其是在与单步推理的局限性进行比较时 (Wei et al., 2022)。多阶段推理通过将问题分解为顺序步骤来显著提升 LLM 的性能 (Wei et al., 2022)，这一过程与人类认知相似。单步方法往往忽略关键的中间阶段，从而降低求解准确率 (Wei et al., 2022)。
While CoT (Wei et al., 2022) employs one LLM invocation, newer techniques integrate search algorithms like MCTS (Leblond et al., 2021), Q-star search (Chakraborty et al., 2024) and recursive validation (Fu et al., 2024) during token generation to refine outputs. Parallel LLM invocations (Brown et al., 2024) and answer selection via CoT-SC (Wang et al., 2022) improve reliability. Structured approaches like ToT (Yao et al., 2024) and GoT (Besta et al., 2024) organize reasoning paths graphically, while collaborative agent simulations enable decentralized problem-solving (Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2024). And some research indicates that integrating knowledge graphs (Luo et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023c) and prompt structuring (Jiang et al., 2023) can empower LLMs reasoning ability. Multiple agents is another form of using parallel LLM to enhance reasoning performance and solve problems (Hu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023).
CoT (Wei et al., 2022) 仅调用一次 LLM，而较新的技术则在词元生成过程中集成 MCTS (Leblond et al., 2021)、Q-star 搜索 (Chakraborty et al., 2024) 和递归验证 (Fu et al., 2024) 等搜索算法，以改进输出。并行调用 LLM (Brown et al., 2024) 以及通过 CoT-SC (Wang et al., 2022) 选择答案，可以提高可靠性。ToT (Yao et al., 2024) 和 GoT (Besta et al., 2024) 等结构化方法以图结构组织推理路径，而协作式智能体模拟则支持去中心化的问题求解 (Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2024)。此外，一些研究表明，整合知识图谱 (Luo et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023c) 和提示结构化方法 (Jiang et al., 2023) 可以增强 LLM 的推理能力。多智能体是利用并行 LLM 提升推理性能和解决问题的另一种形式 (Hu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023)。
Planning. Complex task resolution and reasoning requires divide-and-conquer strategies, where LLMs act as coordinators partitioning problems and delegating subtasks (Hong et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023). Hybrid systems combining neural and symbolic reasoning further enhance planning efficiency (Zhang et al., 2023b). Some recent research begins utilizing LLMs to independently orchestrate planning and scheduling (Hong et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023b).
规划。复杂任务的解决与推理需要采用分而治之的策略，其中 LLM 充当协调者，负责分解问题并分派子任务 (Hong et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023)。结合神经推理与符号推理的混合系统进一步提高了规划效率 (Zhang et al., 2023b)。近期的一些研究开始利用 LLM 自主统筹规划与调度 (Hong et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023b)。

C.4 Operational Protocols in Multi-Agent SystemsC.4 多智能体系统中的操作规程
Allocating SOPs is a common approach in designing agent profiles and tasks within MAS (Hong et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2023a; Park et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024). These systems define SOPs for both individual agents and their communication protocols. While this method has proven effective in previous works, it has two major limitations: (1) Agents may possess unforeseen capabilities that cannot be anticipated during the human design stage but become relevant during task execution (Rivera et al., 2024; Sypherd and Belle, 2024; Piatti et al., 2024); (2) As the scale of MAS grows—potentially involving thousands or even billions of agents—designing SOPs manually for each agent becomes infeasible (Mou et al., 2024; Pan et al., 2024).
在 MAS 中设计智能体配置与任务时，分配标准操作规程（SOPs）是一种常见方法 (Hong et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2023a; Park et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2024; Shi et al., 2024)。这些系统为单个智能体及其通信协议均定义了 SOPs。尽管这种方法已在以往研究中被证明有效，但它存在两个主要局限：(1) 智能体可能具有人类在设计阶段无法预见、但在任务执行过程中变得相关的能力 (Rivera et al., 2024; Sypherd and Belle, 2024; Piatti et al., 2024)；(2) 随着 MAS 规模扩大，可能涉及数千乃至数十亿个智能体，为每个智能体手动设计 SOPs 将变得不可行 (Mou et al., 2024; Pan et al., 2024)。
C.5 LLM Inference and ServingC.5 LLM 推理与服务
Prefilling and Decoding. Modern language model deployment relies on two distinct operational phases. During context initialization, the system processes input prompts to establish attention key-value states – a memory-saving process that prevents redundant recomputation of historical token embeddings (Pope et al., 2023). This phase is particularly critical for applications requiring sequential coherence, such as interactive chatbots.
预填充与解码。现代语言模型的部署依赖于两个不同的运行阶段。在上下文初始化期间，系统处理输入提示以建立注意力键值状态——这一节省内存的过程可避免对历史词元嵌入进行冗余的重复计算 (Pope et al., 2023)。对于交互式聊天机器人等需要序列连贯性的应用，这一阶段尤为关键。
The subsequent token generation phase produces output sequences using strategies like deterministic beam search (Pryzant et al., 2023) or stochastic sampling (Brown et al., 2024). Beam search balances quality and computational cost, while temperature-controlled sampling introduces controlled randomness for creative outputs.
随后的词元生成阶段采用确定性束搜索 (Pryzant et al., 2023) 或随机采样 (Brown et al., 2024) 等策略生成输出序列。束搜索在质量与计算成本之间取得平衡，而温度控制采样则引入可控的随机性，以生成富有创造性的输出。
Memory Optimization Techniques. Efficient KV cache management has become pivotal for high-throughput LLM serving. The PagedAttention methodology (Kwon et al., 2023a) revolutionized this domain by organizing cached attention states into non-contiguous memory blocks, enabling dynamic allocation proportional to sequence lengths. Leading inference engines like vLLM (Kwon et al., 2023a), TensorRT-LLM (ten, 2023), and HuggingFace TGI (tgi, 2023) have implemented this approach with varying architectural adaptations.
内存优化技术。高效的 KV 缓存管理已成为实现高吞吐量 LLM 服务的关键。PagedAttention 方法 (Kwon et al., 2023a) 将缓存的注意力状态组织到非连续的内存块中，从而支持按序列长度成比例地动态分配内存，革新了这一领域。vLLM (Kwon et al., 2023a)、TensorRT-LLM (ten, 2023) 和 HuggingFace TGI (tgi, 2023) 等主流推理引擎均已实现这一方法，并根据各自的架构进行了不同的适配。
LLM serving has seen a surge of research activity in recent years, with many systems developed to address the different challenges. The systems include TensorFlow Serving (Olston et al., 2017), Clockwork (Gujarati et al., 2020), AlpaServe (Li et al., 2023b), Orca (Yu et al., 2022), vllm (Kwon et al., 2023b), SGLang (Zheng et al., 2023b) and others. These serving system explore many aspects including batching, caching, placement, scheduling, model parallelism for the serving of single or multiple models.
近年来，LLM 服务领域的研究活动激增，研究人员开发了众多系统来应对不同的挑战。这些系统包括 TensorFlow Serving (Olston et al., 2017)、Clockwork (Gujarati et al., 2020)、AlpaServe (Li et al., 2023b)、Orca (Yu et al., 2022)、vllm (Kwon et al., 2023b)、SGLang (Zheng et al., 2023b) 等。这些服务系统探索了为单个或多个模型提供服务的诸多方面，包括批处理、缓存、放置、调度和模型并行。
Serving Metrics. System designers evaluate LLM serving efficiency through multiple quantitative measures: (1) TTFT (Time To First Token) that is critical for user-perceived responsiveness; (2) TPOT (Time Per Output Token)that impacts streaming experience quality; (3) Throughput that measures total tokens processed per unit time; (4) SLO Compliance Rate which is the percentage of requests meeting latency guarantees. Different serving frameworks utilize different serving strategies to optimize them.
服务指标。系统设计者通过多种量化指标评估 LLM 服务效率：(1) TTFT（首个词元生成时间，Time To First Token），对用户感知的响应速度至关重要；(2) TPOT（每个输出词元的生成时间，Time Per Output Token），影响流式输出的体验质量；(3) 吞吐量，衡量单位时间内处理的词元总数；(4) SLO 达标率，即满足延迟保证的请求所占的百分比。不同的服务框架采用不同的服务策略来优化这些指标。
Scheduling Requests. Effective scheduling requires balancing conflicting objectives: maximizing GPU utilization through batching while maintaining strict latency constraints (Ali et al., 2020). Advanced systems employ: (1) Dynamic Batching: Groups requests by similar context lengths; (2) Iterative Scheduling: Interleaves processing of new and ongoing generations; (3) Predictive Scaling: Anticipates resource needs via request length estimation (Zheng et al., 2024). Modern schedulers like FastServe (Wu et al., 2023a) implement priority queues based on input complexity, while response-aware systems (Jin et al., 2023) optimize for tail latency reduction. The industry-wide adoption of continuous batching allows incremental addition of requests to active computation batches, dramatically improving hardware utilization compared to static batching approaches.
请求调度。有效的调度需要平衡相互冲突的目标：通过批处理最大化 GPU 利用率，同时满足严格的延迟约束 (Ali et al., 2020)。先进的系统采用以下方法：(1) 动态批处理：将上下文长度相近的请求分组；(2) 迭代式调度：交错处理新的生成任务和正在进行的生成任务；(3) 预测式扩缩容：通过估计请求长度来预测资源需求 (Zheng et al., 2024)。FastServe (Wu et al., 2023a) 等现代调度器实现了基于输入复杂度的优先级队列，而响应感知系统 (Jin et al., 2023) 则以降低尾延迟为优化目标。业界广泛采用的连续批处理允许向正在计算的批次中逐步加入请求，与静态批处理方法相比，显著提高了硬件利用率。
C.6 System Optimization of MASC.6 MAS 的系统优化
Workflow Composition. Agent-based applications typically involve interconnected LLM calls organized as computational graphs. These directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) represent data dependencies between model invocations, where nodes correspond to individual agents and edges define execution prerequisites (lan, 2024). Tools like Microsoft’s PromptFlow (pro, 2023) provide visual editors for constructing such workflows, enabling latency prediction and parallel execution planning.
工作流构建。基于智能体的应用通常涉及相互关联的 LLM 调用，这些调用以计算图的形式组织。这些有向无环图（DAG）表示模型调用之间的数据依赖关系，其中节点对应各个智能体，边定义执行的先决条件 (lan, 2024)。Microsoft 的 PromptFlow (pro, 2023) 等工具提供了用于构建此类工作流的可视化编辑器，支持延迟预测和并行执行规划。
Legacy Graph Processing. Previous generation DAG schedulers (Apache, 2019; Isard et al., 2007; Mahgoub et al., 2021) focused on general data processing optimizations: (1) Pipeline parallelism for multi-stage workloads; (2) Data locality-aware task placement; (3) Inter-node communication minimization. While effective for traditional dataflows, these systems lack semantic understanding of LLM-specific patterns like prompt reuse opportunities or attention cache sharing potential across requests in MAS.
传统图处理。上一代 DAG 调度器 (Apache, 2019; Isard et al., 2007; Mahgoub et al., 2021) 侧重于通用数据处理优化：(1) 面向多阶段工作负载的流水线并行；(2) 感知数据局部性的任务放置；(3) 最小化节点间通信。尽管这些系统对传统数据流有效，但它们缺乏对 LLM 特有模式的语义理解，例如 MAS 中跨请求复用提示词的机会或共享注意力缓存的潜力。
DAG-aware LLM Serving. Recent innovations address LLM workflow peculiarities through two complementary approaches.
感知 DAG 的 LLM 服务。近期的创新通过两种互补的方法应对 LLM 工作流的特殊性。
Semantic Task Chaining. Parrot (Lin et al., 2024) introduces reusable context containers (semantic variables) that explicitly track dependencies between LLM calls. This enables cross-request prompt deduplication and context-aware scheduling, reducing redundant computation by up to 40% in benchmark tests.
语义任务串联。Parrot (Lin et al., 2024) 引入了可复用的上下文容器（语义变量），用于显式跟踪 LLM 调用之间的依赖关系。这实现了跨请求的提示词去重和上下文感知调度，在基准测试中将冗余计算最多减少了 40%。
Cross-Request Cache Optimization. SGLang (Zheng et al., 2023b) implements RadixAttention – a prefix-aware KV cache management system. By maintaining an LRU-cached radix tree of attention states, the framework identifies and reuses common prompt prefixes across concurrent requests. When combined with cache-aware scheduling, this technique demonstrates 3.8× throughput improvement on workflows with shared context segments.
跨请求缓存优化。SGLang (Zheng et al., 2023b) 实现了 RadixAttention，这是一种前缀感知的 KV 缓存管理系统。该框架通过维护一棵采用 LRU 缓存策略的注意力状态基数树，识别并复用并发请求中的公共提示词前缀。与缓存感知调度相结合时，该技术在具有共享上下文片段的工作流上实现了 3.8× 的吞吐量提升。
These advancements highlight the necessity of specialized scheduling mechanisms for LLM agent systems, contrasting with the one-size-fits-all approaches of conventional DAG processors.
这些进展凸显了为 LLM 智能体系统设计专用调度机制的必要性，与传统 DAG 处理器采用的通用化方法形成对比。
C.7 LLM-based Agents CoordinationC.7 基于 LLM 的智能体协调
The coordination between LLM-based agents is critical infrastructure for supporting MAS Guo et al. (2024). There are three main coordination paradigms: cooperative, debate, and competitive. MegaAgent focuses on the coordination paradigm, aiming to have agents work together toward a shared goal. Within the cooperative paradigm are three main structures: layered, decentralized, and centralized. Layered communication is organized hierarchically, with agents at each level having distinct roles and each layer interacting with adjacent layers Liu et al. (2023b). Decentralized communication operates on a peer-to-peer basis among agents. Centralized communication involves a central agent or a group of central agents coordinating the system’s communication, with other agents primarily connecting to the central agent. A shared message pool, as proposed in MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023), maintains a shared message pool where agents publish and subscribe to relevant messages, boosting communication efficiency.
基于 LLM 的智能体之间的协调是支撑 MAS 的关键基础设施 Guo et al. (2024)。主要有三种协调范式：合作、辩论和竞争。MegaAgent 专注于协调范式，旨在使智能体围绕共同目标协同工作。合作范式主要包含三种结构：分层式、去中心化式和中心化式。分层通信按层级组织，每个层级的智能体具有不同的角色，且每一层都与相邻层交互 Liu et al. (2023b)。去中心化通信以智能体之间点对点的方式运行。中心化通信由一个中心智能体或一组中心智能体协调系统通信，其他智能体主要与中心智能体连接。MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023) 提出的共享消息池机制维护一个共享消息池，智能体在其中发布和订阅相关消息，从而提高通信效率。
C.8 LLM-based Agents ManagementC.8 基于 LLM 的智能体管理
Research on the management of LLM-based agents is limited. Popular LLM-based multi-agent systems, such as MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023), AgentVerse Chen et al. (2023b), and AutoGen Wu et al. (2023b), typically divide tasks into smaller sub-tasks and allocate multiple agents to complete them. However, their approaches to planning are sequential, lacking strategic management. In contrast, AIOS Mei et al. (2024b) introduces an LLM agent operating system that provides module isolation and integrates LLM and OS functions. It employs various managers, including Agent Scheduler, Context Manager, Memory Manager, Storage Manager, Tool Manager, and Access Manager, to effectively handle numerous agents. However, AIOS manually organizes different applications, such as a math problem-solving agent and a travel planning agent, rather than multiple agents within the same application. This approach represents a different type of SOP and is not applicable to large-scale MAS, as it is impractical for humans to write every SOP and prompt for each agent when the scale reaches thousands or even millions.
关于基于 LLM 的智能体管理的研究较为有限。流行的基于 LLM 的多智能体系统，如 MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023)、AgentVerse Chen et al. (2023b) 和 AutoGen Wu et al. (2023b)，通常将任务划分为更小的子任务，并分配多个智能体来完成这些子任务。然而，它们采用顺序式规划方法，缺乏战略性管理。相比之下，AIOS Mei et al. (2024b) 引入了一种 LLM 智能体操作系统，提供模块隔离，并集成 LLM 与操作系统功能。它采用多种管理器，包括智能体调度器、上下文管理器、内存管理器、存储管理器、工具管理器和访问管理器，以有效管理大量智能体。然而，AIOS 通过人工组织不同的应用，例如数学解题智能体和旅行规划智能体，而非同一应用内的多个智能体。这种方法代表了另一类标准操作流程（SOP），不适用于大规模多智能体系统（MAS），因为当规模达到数千甚至数百万个智能体时，由人工为每个智能体编写所有 SOP 和提示词并不现实。
C.9 Hallucinations in MASC.9 MAS 中的幻觉
Hallucination refers to the phenomenon where a model generates factually incorrect text Zhao et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2023b). Hallucinations are considered inevitable in LLMs Banerjee et al. (2024). This issue becomes more severe in MAS due to the multi-agent nature: one agent can send information to others. If an agent generates a hallucinated message, it may propagate to other agents, causing a cascading effect Lee and Tiwari (2024); Ju et al. (2024). Self-refinement through feedback and reasoning has proven effective, such as using self-reflection and prompting the LLM again to verify its outputs Ji et al. (2023); Tonmoy et al. (2024). Inspired by this, we equip MegaAgent with a self-correction mechanism, enabling agents to review their outputs based on a to-do list generated at initialization. To enhance monitoring efficiency, we introduce a hierarchical monitoring mechanism: first, agents check their own outputs; second, an admin agent reviews the group’s outputs; and third, a boss agent oversees the outputs of all groups.
幻觉是指模型生成与事实不符的文本的现象 Zhao et al. (2023); Huang et al. (2023b)。幻觉被认为在 LLM 中不可避免 Banerjee et al. (2024)。由于 MAS 具有多智能体特性，即一个智能体可以向其他智能体发送信息，这一问题在 MAS 中变得更加严重。如果某个智能体生成了包含幻觉的消息，该消息可能传播到其他智能体，从而引发级联效应 Lee and Tiwari (2024); Ju et al. (2024)。通过反馈和推理进行自我改进已被证明是有效的，例如采用自我反思，并再次提示 LLM 核验其输出 Ji et al. (2023); Tonmoy et al. (2024)。受此启发，我们为 MegaAgent 配备了自我纠错机制，使智能体能够根据初始化时生成的待办事项列表审查自身输出。为提高监控效率，我们引入了一种分层监控机制：首先，智能体检查自身输出；其次，管理员智能体审查所在组的输出；最后，主管智能体监督所有组的输出。

G.4 Evaluation MetricsG.4 评估指标
To assess the effectiveness of the selected LLMs in evaluating national policies generated by MegaAgent, we use four standard evaluation metrics: Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and Accuracy Huang et al. (2024). These metrics provide a comprehensive overview of the LLMs’ classification performance.
为评估所选 LLM 在评价 MegaAgent 生成的国家政策方面的有效性，我们采用四项标准评估指标：精确率（Precision）、召回率（Recall）、F1 分数（F1-Score）和准确率（Accuracy）Huang et al. (2024)。这些指标全面反映了 LLM 的分类性能。
Precision: Precision measures the proportion of correctly predicted positive samples out of all samples predicted as positive. It indicates how accurate the model is when it predicts a policy as reasonable.
精确率：精确率衡量在所有被预测为正类的样本中，正确预测的正类样本所占的比例。它表明模型将一项政策预测为合理时的准确程度。
$$\text{Precision}=\frac{\text{True Positives}}{\text{True Positives}+\text{False Positives}}$$
Recall: Recall, also known as sensitivity, measures the proportion of actual positive samples correctly identified by the model. It reflects how well the model can detect reasonable policies.
召回率：召回率也称为敏感度，衡量实际正类样本中被模型正确识别的比例。它反映了模型识别合理政策的能力。
$$\text{Recall}=\frac{\text{True Positives}}{\text{True Positives}+\text{False Negatives}}$$
F1-Score: The F1-Score is the harmonic mean of Precision and Recall, providing a balanced evaluation of the model’s performance. It is useful when there is an uneven class distribution.
F1 分数：F1 分数是精确率与召回率的调和平均值，可对模型性能作出均衡的评估。当类别分布不均衡时，该指标很有用。
$$\text{F1-Score}=2\cdot\frac{\text{Precision}\cdot\text{Recall}}{\text{Precision}+\text{Recall}}$$
Accuracy: Accuracy represents the proportion of correct predictions out of all samples evaluated. While straightforward, accuracy alone may be less informative if the dataset is imbalanced.
准确率：准确率表示所有受评估样本中预测正确的样本所占的比例。尽管准确率直观易懂，但在数据集不均衡的情况下，仅凭准确率可能无法提供充分的信息。
$$\text{Accuracy}=\frac{\text{Correct Predictions}}{\text{Total Samples}}$$
