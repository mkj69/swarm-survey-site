在计算机科学和运筹学中，蚁群优化算法（ant colony optimization，ACO）是一种概率技术，用于解决可归结为在图中寻找良好路径的计算问题。 In computer science and operations research, the ant colony optimization algorithm (ACO) is a probabilistic technique for solving computational problems that can be reduced to finding good paths through graphs. 人工蚂蚁代表了一类受真实蚂蚁行为启发的多智能体方法。 Artificial ants represent multi-agent methods inspired by the behavior of real ants.
生物蚂蚁基于信息素的通信方式通常是所采用的主要范式。 The pheromone-based communication of biological ants is often the predominant paradigm used. 将人工蚂蚁与局部搜索算法相结合，已成为许多涉及某种图结构的优化任务的优选方法，例如车辆路径规划和互联网路由。 Combinations of artificial ants and local search algorithms have become a preferred method for numerous optimization tasks involving some sort of graph, e.g., vehicle routing and internet routing.
例如，蚁群优化是一类以蚁群行为为模型的优化算法。 As an example, ant colony optimization is a class of optimization algorithms modeled on the actions of an ant colony. 人工“蚂蚁”（例如仿真智能体）通过在表示所有可能解的参数空间中移动来寻找最优解。 Artificial 'ants' (e.g. simulation agents) locate optimal solutions by moving through a parameter space representing all possible solutions. 真实蚂蚁在探索环境时会释放信息素，引导彼此前往资源所在之处。 Real ants lay down pheromones to direct each other to resources while exploring their environment. 类似地，仿真“蚂蚁”会记录自身的位置及其解的质量，以便在后续仿真迭代中让更多蚂蚁找到更好的解。 The simulated 'ants' similarly, record their positions and the quality of their solutions so that in later simulation iterations more ants locate better solutions. 这种方法的一种变体是蜜蜂算法，它更类似于另一种社会性昆虫——蜜蜂的觅食模式。 One variation on this approach is the bees algorithm, which is more analogous to the foraging patterns of the honey bee, another social insect.
该算法属于群体智能方法中的蚁群算法家族，并构成了一类元启发式优化方法。 This algorithm is a member of the ant colony algorithms family, in swarm intelligence methods, and it constitutes some metaheuristic optimizations. Marco Dorigo 最初于 1992 年在其博士论文中提出了这一算法，最早的算法以蚂蚁寻找巢穴与食物源之间路径的行为为基础，旨在搜索图中的最优路径。 Initially proposed by Marco Dorigo in 1992 in his PhD thesis, the first algorithm was aiming to search for an optimal path in a graph, based on the behavior of ants seeking a path between their colony and a source of food. 此后，最初的思想逐渐发展出多种形式，用于解决更广泛的数值问题，由此也出现了若干借鉴蚂蚁行为不同方面的问题。 The original idea has since diversified to solve a wider class of numerical problems, and as a result, several problems have emerged, drawing on various aspects of the behavior of ants. 从更广泛的角度来看，ACO 执行的是基于模型的搜索，与分布估计算法有一些相似之处。 From a broader perspective, ACO performs a model-based search and shares some similarities with estimation of distribution algorithms.
概述Overview
在自然界中，某些种类的蚂蚁最初会随机游走，找到食物后便返回巢穴，同时沿途留下信息素踪迹。 In the natural world, ants of some species (initially) wander randomly, and upon finding food return to their colony while laying down pheromone trails. 如果其他蚂蚁发现了这样的路径，它们很可能会停止随机游走，转而沿着踪迹前进；如果最终找到了食物，它们便会沿路返回并强化这一踪迹（参见蚂蚁通信）。 If other ants find such a path, they are likely to stop travelling at random and instead follow the trail, returning and reinforcing it if they eventually find food (see Ant communication).
然而，随着时间推移，信息素踪迹会开始挥发，从而降低其吸引力。 Over time, however, the pheromone trail starts to evaporate, thus reducing its attractive strength. 蚂蚁沿路径往返所需的时间越长，信息素可供挥发的时间就越长。 The more time it takes for an ant to travel down the path and back again, the more time the pheromones have to evaporate. 相比之下，较短的路径会被更频繁地经过，因此短路径上的信息素浓度会高于长路径。 A short path, by comparison, is marched over more frequently, and thus the pheromone density becomes higher on shorter paths than longer ones. 信息素挥发还具有避免收敛到局部最优解的优点。 Pheromone evaporation also has the advantage of avoiding the convergence to a locally optimal solution. 如果完全没有挥发，最初几只蚂蚁选择的路径往往会对后续蚂蚁产生过强的吸引力。 If there were no evaporation at all, the paths chosen by the first ants would tend to be excessively attractive to the following ones. 在这种情况下，对解空间的探索就会受到限制。 In that case, the exploration of the solution space would be constrained. 信息素挥发在真实蚂蚁系统中的影响尚不明确，但它在人工系统中非常重要。 The influence of pheromone evaporation in real ant systems is unclear, but it is very important in artificial systems.
总体结果是，当一只蚂蚁找到一条从巢穴通往食物源的良好（即较短）路径时，其他蚂蚁更有可能沿着这条路径前进，而正反馈最终会使许多蚂蚁沿着同一条路径行进。 The overall result is that when one ant finds a good (i.e., short) path from the colony to a food source, other ants are more likely to follow that path, and positive feedback eventually leads to many ants following a single path. 蚁群算法的思想是让“仿真蚂蚁”在表示待解决问题的图中行走，以模拟这种行为。 The idea of the ant colony algorithm is to mimic this behavior with "simulated ants" walking around the graph representing the problem to be solved.
智能物体的环境网络Ambient networks of intelligent objects
由于“智能”不再集中于一处，而是遍布所有微小物体之中，因此需要新的概念。 New concepts are required since "intelligence" is no longer centralized but can be found throughout all minuscule objects. 众所周知，以人为中心的概念促成了数据处理、控制单元和计算能力集中化的信息技术系统的产生。 Anthropocentric concepts have been known to lead to the production of IT systems in which data processing, control units and calculating power are centralized. 这些集中式单元的性能不断提高，可以与人脑相类比。 These centralized units have continually increased their performance and can be compared to the human brain. 大脑模型已成为计算机发展的终极愿景。 The model of the brain has become the ultimate vision of computers. 智能物体的环境网络，以及迟早会出现的、分布更加广泛且基于纳米技术的新一代信息系统，将深刻改变这一概念。 Ambient networks of intelligent objects and, sooner or later, a new generation of information systems that are even more diffused and based on nanotechnology, will profoundly change this concept. 可与昆虫相类比的小型设备，其自身并不具备很高的智能。 Small devices that can be compared to insects do not possess a high intelligence on their own. 事实上，它们的智能可被归为相当有限的水平。 Indeed, their intelligence can be classed as fairly limited. 例如，不可能将一台能够解决任何类型数学问题的高性能计算器，集成到植入人体的生物芯片中，或集成到用于追踪商品的智能标签中。 It is, for example, impossible to integrate a high performance calculator with the power to solve any kind of mathematical problem into a biochip that is implanted into the human body or integrated in an intelligent tag designed to trace commercial articles. 然而，一旦这些物体相互连接，它们就会形成一种可与蚁群或蜂群相类比的智能。 However, once those objects are interconnected they develop a form of intelligence that can be compared to a colony of ants or bees. 对于某些问题，这种智能可能优于类似大脑的集中式系统的推理能力。 In the case of certain problems, this type of intelligence can be superior to the reasoning of a centralized system similar to the brain.
自然界提供了多个例子，展示了微小生物如何在全体遵循同一基本规则的情况下，在宏观层面形成一种集体智能。 Nature offers several examples of how minuscule organisms, if they all follow the same basic rule, can create a form of collective intelligence on the macroscopic level. 社会性昆虫的群体完美地展示了这一模型，而这一模型与人类社会有很大不同。 Colonies of social insects perfectly illustrate this model which greatly differs from human societies. 这一模型以独立单元之间的协作为基础，这些单元的行为简单且不可预测。 This model is based on the cooperation of independent units with simple and unpredictable behavior. 它们在周围区域内移动以执行某些任务，而执行这些任务所能利用的信息非常有限。 They move through their surrounding area to carry out certain tasks and only possess a very limited amount of information to do so. 例如，蚁群具有许多同样适用于环境物体网络的特性。 A colony of ants, for example, represents numerous qualities that can also be applied to a network of ambient objects. 蚁群具有极强的环境变化适应能力，而且在某个个体未能完成指定任务时，也具有很强的应对能力。 Colonies of ants have a very high capacity to adapt themselves to changes in the environment, as well as great strength in dealing with situations where one individual fails to carry out a given task. 这种灵活性对于不断发展的移动物体网络也非常有用。 This kind of flexibility would also be very useful for mobile networks of objects which are perpetually developing. 从计算机传输到数字对象的信息包，其行为方式与蚂蚁相同。 Parcels of information that move from a computer to a digital object behave in the same way as ants would do. 它们在网络中移动，从一个节点传递到下一个节点，目标是尽快到达最终目的地。 They move through the network and pass from one node to the next with the objective of arriving at their final destination as quickly as possible.

常见扩展Common extensions
以下是 ACO 算法中一些最流行的变体。 Here are some of the most popular variations of ACO algorithms.
蚂蚁系统（AS）Ant system (AS)
蚂蚁系统是第一个 ACO 算法。 The ant system is the first ACO algorithm. 该算法对应于上文介绍的算法。 This algorithm corresponds to the one presented above. 它由 Dorigo 开发。 It was developed by Dorigo.
蚁群系统（ACS）Ant colony system (ACS)
蚁群系统算法对原始蚂蚁系统进行了三个方面的修改： In the ant colony system algorithm, the original ant system was modified in three aspects:
边的选择偏向于利用（即提高选择信息素含量较高的最短边的概率）； The edge selection is biased towards exploitation (i.e. favoring the probability of selecting the shortest edges with a large amount of pheromone);
在构建解的过程中，蚂蚁通过应用局部信息素更新规则，改变其所选边上的信息素水平； While building a solution, ants change the pheromone level of the edges they are selecting by applying a local pheromone updating rule;
在每次迭代结束时，仅允许最优蚂蚁通过应用修改后的全局信息素更新规则来更新路径上的信息素。 At the end of each iteration, only the best ant is allowed to update the trails by applying a modified global pheromone updating rule.
精英蚂蚁系统Elitist ant system
在该算法中，每次迭代后，全局最优解都会与其他所有蚂蚁一起，在其路径上沉积信息素（即使该路径未被再次访问）。 In this algorithm, the global best solution deposits pheromone on its trail after every iteration (even if this trail has not been revisited), along with all the other ants. 精英策略旨在引导所有蚂蚁的搜索，使其构建的解包含当前最优路径中的连接边。 The elitist strategy has as its objective directing the search of all ants to construct a solution to contain links of the current best route.
最大最小蚂蚁系统（MMAS）Max-min ant system (MMAS)
该算法控制每条路径上的最大和最小信息素量。 This algorithm controls the maximum and minimum pheromone amounts on each trail. 仅允许全局最优巡回路径或当前迭代的最优巡回路径在其路径上添加信息素。 Only the global best tour or the iteration best tour are allowed to add pheromone to its trail. 为避免搜索算法停滞，每条路径上可能的信息素量被限制在区间 [τmax,τmin] 内。 To avoid stagnation of the search algorithm, the range of possible pheromone amounts on each trail is limited to an interval [τmax,τmin]. 所有边上的信息素量均初始化为 τmax，以促使算法更充分地探索解。 All edges are initialized to τmax to force a higher exploration of solutions. 当搜索接近停滞时，各路径上的信息素量会重新初始化为 τmax。 The trails are reinitialized to τmax when nearing stagnation.
基于排序的蚂蚁系统（ASrank）Rank-based ant system (ASrank)
所有解均按其路径长度排序。 All solutions are ranked according to their length. 仅允许本次迭代中固定数量的最优蚂蚁更新其路径上的信息素。 Only a fixed number of the best ants in this iteration are allowed to update their trials. 每个解所沉积的信息素量均经过加权，使得路径较短的解比路径较长的解沉积更多信息素。 The amount of pheromone deposited is weighted for each solution, such that solutions with shorter paths deposit more pheromone than the solutions with longer paths.
并行蚁群优化（PACO）Parallel ant colony optimization (PACO)
开发了一种具有通信策略的蚁群系统（ACS）。 An ant colony system (ACS) with communication strategies is developed. 人工蚂蚁被划分为若干组。 The artificial ants are partitioned into several groups. 七种通信 Seven communication
方法被提出，用于更新 ACS 中组间的信息素水平，并应用于旅行商问题。 methods for updating the pheromone level between groups in ACS are proposed and work on the traveling salesman problem.
连续正交蚁群（COAC）Continuous orthogonal ant colony (COAC)
COAC 的信息素沉积机制旨在使蚂蚁能够协同、高效地搜索解。 The pheromone deposit mechanism of COAC is to enable ants to search for solutions collaboratively and effectively. 通过采用正交设计方法，可行域中的蚂蚁能够快速、高效地探索其选定的区域，从而提高全局搜索能力和精度。 By using an orthogonal design method, ants in the feasible domain can explore their chosen regions rapidly and efficiently, with enhanced global search capability and accuracy. 正交设计方法和自适应半径调整方法也可以扩展到其他优化算法，以在解决实际问题时发挥更广泛的优势。 The orthogonal design method and the adaptive radius adjustment method can also be extended to other optimization algorithms for delivering wider advantages in solving practical problems.
递归蚁群优化Recursive ant colony optimization
它是蚂蚁系统的一种递归形式，将整个搜索域划分为若干子域，并在这些子域上求解目标问题。 It is a recursive form of ant system which divides the whole search domain into several sub-domains and solves the objective on these subdomains. 对所有子域的结果进行比较，并将其中最优的若干结果选入下一层。 The results from all the subdomains are compared and the best few of them are promoted for the next level. 进一步细分所选结果对应的子域，并重复这一过程，直到获得达到所需精度的输出。 The subdomains corresponding to the selected results are further subdivided and the process is repeated until an output of desired precision is obtained. 该方法已在不适定地球物理反演问题上进行测试，效果良好。 This method has been tested on ill-posed geophysical inversion problems and works well.
收敛性Convergence
对于该算法的某些版本，可以证明其具有收敛性（即能够在有限时间内找到全局最优解）。 For some versions of the algorithm, it is possible to prove that it is convergent (i.e., it is able to find the global optimum in finite time). 2000 年，首次针对一种蚁群算法——基于图的蚂蚁系统算法——给出了收敛性证明，随后又证明了 ACS 和 MMAS 算法的收敛性。 The first evidence of convergence for an ant colony algorithm was made in 2000, the graph-based ant system algorithm, and later on for the ACS and MMAS algorithms. 与大多数元启发式算法一样，其理论收敛速度很难估计。 Like most metaheuristics, it is very difficult to estimate the theoretical speed of convergence. 一项针对连续蚁群算法各项参数（边选择策略、距离度量和信息素蒸发率）的性能分析表明，其性能和收敛速度对所选参数值十分敏感，尤其是信息素蒸发率的取值。 A performance analysis of a continuous ant colony algorithm with respect to its various parameters (edge selection strategy, distance measure metric, and pheromone evaporation rate) showed that its performance and rate of convergence are sensitive to the chosen parameter values, and especially to the value of the pheromone evaporation rate. 2004 年，Zlochin 及其同事表明，ACO 类算法与随机梯度下降法、交叉熵方法和分布估计算法密切相关。 In 2004, Zlochin and his colleagues showed that ACO-type algorithms are closely related to stochastic gradient descent, Cross-entropy method and estimation of distribution algorithm. 他们提出了“基于模型的搜索”（Model-based search）这一统称，用于描述这类元启发式算法。 They proposed an umbrella term "Model-based search" to describe this class of metaheuristics.

它还被用于求取旅行商问题的近似最优解。 It has also been used to produce near-optimal solutions to the travelling salesman problem. 当图可能发生动态变化时，与用于类似问题的模拟退火法和遗传算法相比，蚁群算法具有优势；它可以持续运行，并实时适应变化。 They have an advantage over simulated annealing and genetic algorithm approaches of similar problems when the graph may change dynamically; the ant colony algorithm can be run continuously and adapt to changes in real time. 这一特性在网络路由和城市交通系统中具有应用价值。 This is of interest in network routing and urban transportation systems.
第一个 ACO 算法称为蚂蚁系统，旨在解决旅行商问题，其目标是找到连接一系列城市的最短闭合巡回路线。 The first ACO algorithm was called the ant system and it was aimed to solve the travelling salesman problem, in which the goal is to find the shortest round-trip to link a series of cities. 该算法的总体流程相对简单，以一组蚂蚁为基础，每只蚂蚁沿城市间的一条可能的闭合巡回路线行进。 The general algorithm is relatively simple and based on a set of ants, each making one of the possible round-trips along the cities. 在每个阶段，蚂蚁根据以下规则选择从一个城市移动到另一个城市： At each stage, the ant chooses to move from one city to another according to some rules:
它必须恰好访问每个城市一次； It must visit each city exactly once;
距离较远的城市被选中的概率较低（可见度）； A distant city has less chance of being chosen (the visibility);
两个城市之间的边上所留下的信息素轨迹越浓，该边被选中的概率就越大； The more intense the pheromone trail laid out on an edge between two cities, the greater the probability that that edge will be chosen;
完成行程后，如果行程较短，蚂蚁就会在其经过的所有边上留下更多信息素； Having completed its journey, the ant deposits more pheromones on all edges it traversed, if the journey is short;
每次迭代后，信息素轨迹都会蒸发。 After each iteration, trails of pheromones evaporate.

定义的难点Definition difficulty
使用 ACO 算法时，图中 A、B 两点之间的最短路径由多条路径组合构建而成。 With an ACO algorithm, the shortest path in a graph, between two points A and B, is built from a combination of several paths. 要精确定义哪些算法属于蚁群算法、哪些不属于，并非易事，因为定义可能因作者和用途而异。 It is not easy to give a precise definition of what algorithm is or is not an ant colony, because the definition may vary according to the authors and uses. 广义而言，蚁群算法被视为基于种群的元启发式算法，其中每个解由一只在搜索空间中移动的蚂蚁表示。 Broadly speaking, ant colony algorithms are regarded as populated metaheuristics with each solution represented by an ant moving in the search space. 蚂蚁标记最优解，并利用先前的标记来优化搜索。 Ants mark the best solutions and take account of previous markings to optimize their search. 它们可被视为概率型多智能体算法，利用概率分布实现各次迭代之间的状态转移。 They can be seen as probabilistic multi-agent algorithms using a probability distribution to make the transition between each iteration. 在面向组合问题的版本中，它们以迭代方式构建解。 In their versions for combinatorial problems, they use an iterative construction of solutions. 一些作者认为，ACO 算法与其他相关算法（如分布估计算法或粒子群优化算法）的区别恰恰在于其构造性。 According to some authors, the thing which distinguishes ACO algorithms from other relatives (such as algorithms to estimate the distribution or particle swarm optimization) is precisely their constructive aspect. 在组合问题中，即使没有任何一只蚂蚁表现出良好的求解效果，也仍有可能最终找到最优解。 In combinatorial problems, it is possible that the best solution eventually be found, even though no ant would prove effective. 因此，以旅行商问题为例，并不需要某只蚂蚁实际走过最短路线：最短路线可以由最优解中信息素最强的路段构建而成。 Thus, in the example of the travelling salesman problem, it is not necessary that an ant actually travels the shortest route: the shortest route can be built from the strongest segments of the best solutions. 然而，对于实变量问题，这一定义可能存在问题，因为此类问题中不存在“邻居”结构。 However, this definition can be problematic in the case of problems in real variables, where no structure of 'neighbours' exists. 社会性昆虫的集体行为一直是研究人员的灵感来源。 The collective behaviour of social insects remains a source of inspiration for researchers. 大量从生物系统中探寻自组织机制的算法（无论是否用于优化）促成了“群体智能”（swarm intelligence）这一概念的形成；它是一个非常宽泛的框架，蚁群算法便属于其中。 The wide variety of algorithms (for optimization or not) seeking self-organization in biological systems has led to the concept of "swarm intelligence", which is a very general framework in which ant colony algorithms fit.
间接协同算法（Stigmergy algorithms）Stigmergy algorithms
实践中，有大量算法自称为“蚁群”算法，却并不总是遵循标准蚁群优化的一般框架。 There is in practice a large number of algorithms claiming to be "ant colonies", without always sharing the general framework of optimization by canonical ant colonies. 实践中，只要利用环境在蚂蚁之间交换信息（这一原理称为“间接协同”），就被认为足以使一种算法归入蚁群算法这一类别。 In practice, the use of an exchange of information between ants via the environment (a principle called "stigmergy") is deemed enough for an algorithm to belong to the class of ant colony algorithms. 这一原理促使一些作者创造了“价值”（value）一词，用于组织基于觅食、幼虫分类、劳动分工和协作搬运的方法与行为。 This principle has led some authors to create the term "value" to organize methods and behavior based on search of food, sorting larvae, division of labour and cooperative transportation.

禁忌搜索（TS） Tabu search (TS)
与模拟退火类似，两者都通过测试单个解的变异结果来遍历解空间。 Similar to simulated annealing in that both traverse the solution space by testing mutations of an individual solution. 模拟退火只生成一个变异解，而禁忌搜索会生成多个变异解，并转移到其中适应度最低的解。 While simulated annealing generates only one mutated solution, tabu search generates many mutated solutions and moves to the solution with the lowest fitness of those generated. 为防止循环并鼓励在解空间中进行更大范围的移动，算法会维护一个记录部分解或完整解的禁忌表。 To prevent cycling and encourage greater movement through the solution space, a tabu list is maintained of partial or complete solutions. 禁止转移到包含禁忌表中元素的解；随着搜索在解空间中推进，禁忌表也会更新。 It is forbidden to move to a solution that contains elements of the tabu list, which is updated as the solution traverses the solution space.
