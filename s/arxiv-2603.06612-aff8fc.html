摘要Abstract
在数学和代码等具有外部验证器的领域，Pass@ $k$ 及其他增加推理计算量的方法能够提升语言模型的性能，因为在这些领域中，错误的候选答案可以被可靠地筛除。 Pass@ $k$ and other methods of scaling inference compute can improve language model performance in domains with external verifiers, including mathematics and code, where incorrect candidates can be filtered reliably. 这引出了一个自然的问题：对于缺乏便捷验证手段的领域，我们能否同样通过增加计算量来提升真实性？ This raises a natural question: can we similarly scale compute to elicit gains in truthfulness for domains without convenient verification? 我们在五个基准和多个模型上的研究表明，出乎意料的是，这并不可行。 We show that across five benchmarks and models, surprisingly, it cannot. 即使推理成本达到朴素采样的 $25\times$，投票式聚合相较于单次采样基线也无法带来稳定的准确率提升，而且往往会放大共同的错误认知。 Even at $25\times$ the inference cost of naive sampling, polling-style aggregation yields no consistent accuracy gains over single-sample baselines and often amplifies shared misconceptions. 我们发现，在不确定的情况下，模型更擅长预测模型集成中的其他模型会说什么，而非判断什么是真实的，这揭示了社会预测与真伪验证之间的分离。 We find that under uncertainty, models are better at predicting what other models will say within model ensembles than at identifying what is true, revealing a separation between social prediction and truth verification. 在不同模型和基准上，聚合都无法提供稳健的真实性信号，因为语言模型的错误具有很强的相关性。 Across models and benchmarks, aggregation fails to provide a robust truth signal because language model errors are strongly correlated. 这种相关性的来源并不限于任何单一基准：我们表明，即使以分布外的随机字符串为条件，并被要求生成伪随机输出，不同模型的输出仍具有相关性。 The source of correlation goes beyond any individual benchmark: we show that even when conditioned on out of distribution random strings and asked to produce pseudo-random outputs, different models produce correlated outputs. 基于置信度的加权并无益处，因为模型自报的置信度无法可靠地区分正确答案与错误答案。 Confidence-based weighting provides no benefit because self-reported confidence fails to reliably distinguish correct from incorrect answers. 这些结果界定了推理时扩展的适用边界：在可验证的领域，增加样本会为验证器提供更多可供筛选的候选答案；在不可验证的领域，增加样本只会强化共同的错误认知。 These results delineate a boundary for inference-time scaling: in verified domains, additional samples provide more candidates for a verifier to filter; in unverified domains, additional samples merely reinforce shared misconceptions.

1 引言1 Introduction
对于大型语言模型，推理时扩展已成为参数规模扩展的一种强有力的替代方案。 Inference-time scaling has emerged as a powerful alternative to parameter scaling for large language models. 通过在测试时分配额外的计算资源，模型可以生成多个候选解并从中进行选择，通常能够提高准确率。 By allocating additional compute at test time, models can generate multiple candidate solutions and select among them, often achieving accuracy gains. 自一致性（Wang et al., 2023）、重复采样（Brown et al., 2024）和搜索等方法已被证明在数学和代码等可自动验证正确性的领域中有效。 Methods such as self-consistency (Wang et al., 2023), repeated sampling (Brown et al., 2024), and search have proven effective in domains like mathematics and code, where correctness can be verified automatically. 在这些情境下，聚合通过外部验证器筛选候选答案，从而增强模型能力。 In these settings, aggregation amplifies capability by filtering candidates through an external verifier.
一个自然的问题是，在缺乏验证手段的领域，聚合能否也通过规模扩展来提升真实性。 A natural question is whether aggregation can also scale truthfulness in domains without verification. 此时，选择就必须转而依赖一致程度、置信度或预测的受欢迎程度等内部信号。 Selection must then instead rely on internal signals such as agreement, confidence, or predicted popularity. 源自群体智慧文献（Surowiecki, 2004）的一种常见直觉是，即使个体会犯错，汇总大量不完美的判断也应当能够还原真相。 A common intuition, borrowed from the wisdom-of-crowds literature (Surowiecki, 2004), is that aggregating many imperfect judgments should recover the truth even when individuals err.
我们发现，这一直觉并不适用于语言模型。 We find that this intuition does not transfer to language models. 我们在多个开源模型和涵盖事实知识、常识推理、专家级问题及预测的基准上，评估了多种聚合策略，包括多数投票、置信度加权和 Surprisingly Popular 算法（Prelec et al., 2017）。 We evaluate multiple aggregation strategies—including majority voting, confidence weighting, and the Surprisingly Popular algorithm (Prelec et al., 2017)—across several open-source models and benchmarks spanning factual knowledge, commonsense reasoning, expert-level questions, and forecasting. 我们发现，没有一种聚合方法能够相较于单次采样基线稳定地提高准确率；在某个基准上有效的方法，往往会在另一个基准上造成性能下降。 We find that no aggregation method consistently improves accuracy over single-sample baselines; methods that help on one benchmark often hurt on another. 对于结果发生在模型知识截止日期之后的预测问题，所有方法的表现都处于随机猜测水平。 On forecasting questions with outcomes postdating model knowledge cutoffs, all methods perform at chance. 这些结果并不与自一致性方法此前的成功相矛盾，而是明确了这些方法失效的适用情境。 These results do not contradict prior successes of self-consistency; they identify the regime in which those methods stop working.
这种失败可追溯至错误之间的相关性。 This failure traces to correlated errors. 群体智慧建立在一个关键假设之上：错误之间至多只能具有较弱的相关性。 Wisdom-of-crowds rests on a critical assumption: errors must be at most weakly correlated. 人类群体满足这一条件，因为个体依赖的经验和信息来源各不相同。 Human crowds satisfy this condition because individuals draw on diverse experiences and information sources. 现代语言模型则不满足这一条件。 Modern language models do not. 在相互重叠的语料库上训练、并针对相似目标进行优化的模型，会形成共同的先验和盲点。 Models trained on overlapping corpora and optimized for similar objectives acquire shared priors and blind spots. 这与对抗迁移性类似：不同模型由于学到了相似的特征，会以相似的方式失效（Goodfellow et al., 2015）。 This parallels adversarial transferability, where different models fail in similar ways because they learn similar features (Goodfellow et al., 2015). 当一个模型给出看似合理却不正确的答案时，其他模型往往也会如此。 When one model produces a plausible but incorrect answer, others frequently do the same. 投票不会抵消这些错误，反而会放大共同的错误认知，使置信度提高，却未提高正确性。 Polling does not cancel these mistakes; it amplifies shared misconceptions, producing greater confidence without greater correctness.
这些相关性源自何处？ What is the source of these correlations? 人们可能会怀疑，它们完全源于重叠训练数据中编码的共同知识和错误认知。 One might suspect they stem entirely from shared knowledge and misconceptions encoded in overlapping training data. 为检验这一点，我们向模型输入随机生成的 ASCII 字符串，并要求其强制给出一个多项选择答案——这一设置完全不存在任何真实答案信号。 To test this, we feed models randomly generated ASCII strings and ask for a forced multiple-choice answer—a setting with no ground-truth signal whatsoever. 即使在这一零知识基线上，不同语言模型之间也表现出高达 0.35 的相关性。 Even on this zero-knowledge baseline, different LMs exhibit correlations as high as 0.35. 这排除了将共同的事实知识作为唯一解释的可能性，并表明输出的相关性源于相近的归纳偏置和架构相似性，因此无论是否进行聚合都会持续存在。 This rules out shared factual knowledge as the sole explanation and suggests that correlated outputs arise from aligned inductive biases and architectural similarities, therefore persist regardless of aggregation.
我们从机制层面对这些失败进行了诊断。 We diagnose these failures mechanistically. 无论在同一模型家族内部，还是在不同模型家族之间，模型错误都具有高度相关性，违背了群体智慧所要求的独立性。 Model errors are highly correlated both within and across model families, violating the independence that crowd wisdom requires. 自报告置信度与正确性并不一致，而是随预期的一致程度变化。 Self-reported confidence is misaligned with correctness and instead tracks expected agreement. 为复杂聚合规则提供支撑的基于惊异度的信号，无法可靠地区分真相与共识。 Surprise-based signals that power sophisticated aggregation rules do not reliably distinguish truth from consensus. 与此同时，模型预测群体意见的能力显著强于其预测正确性的能力，揭示了社会预测与真相验证之间的分离。 At the same time, models predict collective opinion substantially better than they predict correctness, revealing a separation between social prediction and truth verification.
这些结果共同界定了推理时扩展的边界。 Together, these results define a boundary for inference-time scaling. 存在验证器时，聚合可以提升性能，但它无法替代验证器。 Aggregation improves performance when a verifier exists, but it cannot serve as a verifier substitute. 在不可验证的领域中，提高真实性需要外部依据，或能够打破错误相关性的干预，而非通过从同一认知先验中抽取更多样本来践行苦涩的教训（Sutton, 2019）。 Scaling truthfulness in unverifiable domains requires external grounding or interventions that break error correlation, rather than instantiation of the bitter lesson (Sutton, 2019) involving more samples from the same epistemic prior.
我们的结论概括如下： To summarize our conclusions:
我们表明，在缺乏验证器的各项基准上，即使投入大量推理时计算，投票式聚合也无法可靠地提高真实性。 We show that polling-style aggregation fails to reliably improve truthfulness across verifier-absent benchmarks even at large inference-time compute.
我们证明，语言模型的错误在不同样本之间以及不同模型家族之间都具有很强的相关性。 We demonstrate that language model errors are strongly correlated across samples and across model families.
我们解释了为何置信度、预测受欢迎程度和惊异度等聚合方法反映的是共识而非正确性。 We explain why aggregation methods like confidence, predicted popularity, and surprise reflect consensus rather than correctness.
我们引入了一种简单的阴性对照来探究模型间相关性的深层程度——为随机字符串设置强制选择式答案，其中不存在潜在的真相——并表明模型之间持续存在高于随机水平的一致性。 We introduce a simple negative control to probe the depth of inter-model correlations - random strings with forced-choice answers - in which no underlying truth exists, and show persistent above-chance agreement. 这提供了机制层面的证据，表明相关性反映的是共享的先验，而非共享的知识。 This provides mechanistic evidence that correlation reflects shared priors rather than shared knowledge.
2 相关工作2 Related Work
推理时扩展通过在测试时分配计算资源，对参数规模扩展形成补充。 Inference-time scaling complements parameter scaling by allocating compute at test time. 自一致性解码对多条推理路径进行采样，并通过多数投票选择答案（Wang et al., 2023）。 Self-consistency decoding samples multiple reasoning paths and selects answers by majority vote (Wang et al., 2023). 后续研究表明，增加样本可带来对数线性增益（Brown et al., 2024; Hughes et al., 2025），并通过重尾难度分布解释了这些增益（Schaeffer et al., 2025），还证明了自适应测试时计算能够优于固定的 best-of- $N$ 策略（Snell et al., 2025）。 Subsequent work showed log-linear gains with additional samples (Brown et al., 2024; Hughes et al., 2025), explained these gains via heavy-tailed difficulty distributions (Schaeffer et al., 2025), and demonstrated that adaptive test-time compute can outperform fixed best-of- $N$ strategies (Snell et al., 2025). 这些成功案例的共同点在于，答案可以通过证明检查器或代码执行进行自动验证。 What these successes share is that answers can be verified automatically – through proof checkers or code execution.
在没有外部验证器的领域中，选择必须依赖内部信号。 In domains without external verifiers, selection must rely on internal signals. 一种提议是借鉴群体智慧文献（Surowiecki, 2004），将聚合本身视为验证的替代指标。 One proposal is to treat aggregation itself as a proxy for verification, drawing on the wisdom-of-crowds literature (Surowiecki, 2004). 对独立判断取平均通常优于任何个体的判断，而同一个人的多次估计也可以通过“内在群体”效应降低方差（van Dolder and van den Assem, 2018）。 Averaging independent judgments often outperforms any individual, and repeated estimates from a single person can reduce variance through an “inner crowd” effect (van Dolder and van den Assem, 2018). 对一个语言模型反复采样可形成内在群体，而汇集不同模型的输出则形成外在群体，类似于机器学习中的集成。 Repeated samples from a language model form an inner crowd, while pooling across models forms an outer crowd, analogous to ensembles in machine learning.
经典集成理论预测，当各组成模型的错误具有多样性或相关性较弱时，集成会带来增益（Lakshminarayanan et al., 2017）。 Classical ensemble theory predicts gains when component errors are diverse or weakly correlated (Lakshminarayanan et al., 2017). 在二元任务中，这一假设涉及的是错误事件，而非原始的一致程度：如果在回答错误的条件下，模型倾向于选择同一个错误选项，那么这些错误就是相关的。 In binary tasks, this assumption concerns error events, not raw agreement: errors are correlated if, conditioned on being wrong, models tend to select the same incorrect option. 现代语言模型越来越不满足这一条件。 Modern language models increasingly violate this condition. 当不同的 LLMs 在同一道问题上出错时，它们往往会集中选择同一个错误答案（Kim and others, 2025），而且错误相关性会随着模型能力和训练相似性的提高而增强（Goel and others, 2025）。 When different LLMs err on the same question, they often collapse onto a single wrong answer (Kim and others, 2025), and error correlation grows with model capability and training similarity (Goel and others, 2025). 因此，聚合可能会放大共同的误解，而非抵消噪声。 As a result, aggregation may amplify shared misconceptions rather than cancel noise.
一些聚合方法旨在多数人判断错误时恢复真相。 Several aggregation methods aim to recover truth when the majority is wrong. 出乎意料地受欢迎（Surprisingly Popular，SP）算法将实际得票比例与预测得票比例进行比较，利用知情与不知情受访者之间的不对称性（Prelec et al., 2017）。 The Surprisingly Popular (SP) algorithm compares vote shares to predicted ones, exploiting asymmetries between informed and uninformed respondents (Prelec et al., 2017). 要使 SP 成功，必须有一些受访者既知道真相，又能预见多数人的错误。 For SP to succeed, some respondents must know the truth and anticipate the majority’s error. 尽管已有研究探索将 SP 式聚合用于语言模型，但 LLM 群体是否呈现这种专家少数派结构，仍不清楚。 While SP-style aggregation has been explored for language models, it remains unclear whether LLM populations exhibit this expert-minority structure. 我们直接检验这两个先决条件——知道真相和预见多数人的错误。 We test both prerequisites – knowing the truth and anticipating majority error – directly.
其他聚合方案根据自报告置信度为投票赋权，并假设置信度越高，准确率就越高。 Other aggregation schemes weight votes by self-reported confidence, assuming higher confidence implies higher accuracy. 尽管基础语言模型可以得到校准（Kadavath et al., 2022），后训练往往会削弱这一特性（Tian et al., 2023; Leng et al., 2025; Xiong et al., 2024）。 Although base language models can be calibrated (Kadavath et al., 2022), post-training often degrades this property (Tian et al., 2023; Leng et al., 2025; Xiong et al., 2024). 迎合行为进一步促使模型优先追求一致而非真相（Sharma et al., 2023），这表明置信度可能随预期共识而变化，而非反映认知上的确定性。 Sycophancy further encourages agreement over truth (Sharma et al., 2023), suggesting that confidence may track expected consensus rather than epistemic certainty.
与此相关，自我纠错研究表明，在缺乏外部反馈时，模型往往会为错误寻找合理解释，而非发现错误（Huang et al., 2024）。 Relatedly, work on self-correction shows that absent external feedback, models often rationalize errors rather than detect them (Huang et al., 2024). 由于群体聚合依赖于具有一定独立性的错误，共享的训练数据和训练目标构成了根本性障碍。 Since crowd aggregation relies on partially independent errors, shared training data and objectives pose a fundamental obstacle.
先前研究表明，在存在验证器时，推理时计算有助于提升性能，而且 LLM 的错误往往具有相关性。 Prior work shows that inference-time compute helps scale performance when verifiers exist, and that LLM errors are often correlated. 尚未解决的问题是，这种相关性是否会限制缺乏验证器的领域中的真实性。 What remains unresolved is whether this correlation limits truthfulness in verifier-absent domains. 我们表明，确实如此：相关误差施加了一种自聚合规则无法突破的结构性限制。 We show that it does: correlated errors impose a structural limit that self-aggregation rules don’t overcome.
3 实验设置3 Experimental Setup
群体智慧依赖于误差之间不存在强相关性。 Crowd wisdom relies on errors that are not strongly correlated. 我们的实验通过将投票方法应用于单个模型的重复采样结果（模型内群体）和跨模型汇集的集成结果（模型间群体），并测量其是否满足聚合所要求的依赖结构，来检验这一假设。 Our experiments test this assumption by applying polling methods to repeated samples from single models (intra-model crowds) and to ensembles pooled across models (inter-model crowds), while measuring whether the dependence structure required for aggregation is satisfied.
任务与回答格式。Tasks and response formats.
我们要求模型提供三种类型的回答：(i) 二元答案（例如 YES/NO 或 TRUE/FALSE），(ii) 附带以 0–100 量表自报的置信度的答案，以及 (iii) 对某个规范选项的得票比例的预测（例如，回答 YES 或 TRUE 的响应所占的比例）。 We elicit three response types: (i) a binary answer (e.g., YES/NO or TRUE/FALSE), (ii) an answer with self-reported confidence on a 0–100 scale, and (iii) a prediction of the vote share of one canonical option (e.g., the fraction of responses answering YES or TRUE). 多数投票和出乎意料地受欢迎（Surprisingly Popular，SP）算法等聚合规则要求选项空间有限，且得票比例和预测分布有明确定义。 Aggregation rules such as majority voting and the Surprisingly Popular (SP) algorithm require a finite option space with well-defined vote shares and prediction distributions. 因此，二元问题并非必要条件，而是最简单的设置，可以避免离散化带来的伪差，并支持不同聚合方法之间的比较。 Binary questions are therefore not a requirement but the simplest setting, avoiding discretization artifacts and enabling comparison across aggregation methods. 这一选择限制了结论向开放式生成的推广，我们将在第 5 节讨论这一点。 This choice limits generalization to open-ended generation, which we discuss in Section 5. 二元问题是最有利于投票式聚合的设置；即使在这一简化情形下也无法获得可靠增益，这有力地反驳了将聚合作为在缺乏验证器的领域中提升真实性的通用机制的观点。 Binary questions represent the most favorable setting for polling-style aggregation; failure to obtain reliable gains even in this simplified regime provides strong evidence against aggregation as a general mechanism for scaling truthfulness in verifier-absent domains.

Com2Sense (Singh et al., 2021)：二元常识推理。 Com2Sense (Singh et al., 2021): binary commonsense reasoning.
Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE) (Phan and others, 2025)：专家级问题，仅限于答案结构天然为二元的问题。 Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE) (Phan and others, 2025): expert-level questions, restricted to those with a naturally binary answer structure.
BoolQ (Clark et al., 2019)：二元事实问答。 BoolQ (Clark et al., 2019): binary factual question answering.
Predict-the-Future：本文引入的一组预测问题，其中所有真实结果在数据集构建和论文撰写时均已明确确定且可验证，尽管这些事件发生在模型知识截止日期之后。 Predict-the-Future: a collection of forecasting questions introduced in this work, where all ground-truth outcomes were definitively resolved and verifiable at the time of dataset construction and writing, even though the events postdate model knowledge cutoffs.
HLE 子集由于规模较小而产生了较宽的置信区间，但准确率（5.7%）仍远低于 50% 的随机猜测基线，这表明模型存在系统性的错误答案偏向，而非采样噪声所致。 The HLE subset yields wider confidence intervals due to its small size, but accuracy remains far below the 50% guessing baseline (5.7%), indicating systematic attraction to incorrect answers rather than sampling noise.
采样方案。Sampling protocol.
对于每个问题和每种回答类型，我们在温度 $T\in\{0.7,1.0\}$ 下为每个模型收集 25 个独立样本。 For each question and response type, we collect 25 independent samples per model at temperatures $T\in\{0.7,1.0\}$ . 我们使用中高温度，因为群体聚合需要多样性；在低温度下，重复采样会退化为近乎确定性的输出。 We use moderate-to-high temperatures because crowd aggregation requires diversity; at low temperatures, repeated sampling collapses to near-deterministic outputs. 关键问题并非单次贪心采样是否优于高温度下的聚合，而是在具备群体方法适用所需的多样性时，聚合能否带来增益。 The relevant question is not whether a single greedy sample outperforms high-temperature aggregation, but whether aggregation provides gains given the diversity required for crowd methods to apply. 在所有基准上，这相当于每个（问题，模型）组合有 50 个样本，各项实验合计涉及 375,000 次模型采样；我们在附录 B 中报告各基准的具体数量。 Across all benchmarks, this corresponds to 50 samples per (question, model) and a total of 375,000 model across experiments; we report benchmark-wise counts in Appendix B.

聚合方法。Aggregation methods.
我们评估了五种聚合规则，涵盖了常见的内部选择信号：(1) 多数投票 (de Condorcet, 1785)；(2) 最高置信度 (Kadavath et al., 2022)；(3) 置信度加权投票 (Wang et al., 2023)；(4) 预测加权投票，按每个回答的预测受欢迎程度赋予权重；以及 (5) 出乎意料地受欢迎（SP）(Prelec et al., 2017)，选择实际支持率超出预测支持率的答案。 We evaluate five aggregation rules that exhaust common internal selection signals: (1) Majority Vote (de Condorcet, 1785); (2) Highest Confidence (Kadavath et al., 2022); (3) Confidence-Weighted Vote (Wang et al., 2023); (4) Prediction-Weighted Vote, weighting each response by its predicted popularity; and (5) Surprisingly Popular (SP) (Prelec et al., 2017), which selects the answer whose observed support exceeds its predicted support. 如果自聚合能够在没有外部验证器的情况下提升真实性，那么至少在这些规则中的一种之下应当能够实现这一点。 If self-aggregation can scale truthfulness without an external verifier, it should do so under at least one of these rules. 我们还报告了一项逆向 SP 诊断，仅用于检验 SP 信号与正确性之间的一致性；我们并未将其作为一种方法提出。 We additionally report an inverse-SP diagnostic solely to test alignment between the SP signal and correctness; it is not proposed as a method.
评估与相关性。Evaluation and correlation.
性能以准确率衡量，并通过对问题进行重采样获得 95% 的自助法置信区间。 Performance is measured by accuracy with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals obtained by resampling questions. 由于独立性是所检验的关键假设，相关性是一个核心考察量。 Because independence is the key assumption under test, correlation is a first-class quantity. 我们通过温度变化下的多数意见稳定性、评定者间信度（包括投票熵和 Fleiss’ $\kappa$），以及模型出错时错误答案的集中程度来衡量它。 We measure it via majority stability under temperature variation, inter-rater reliability (including vote entropy and Fleiss’ $\kappa$ ), and the concentration of wrong answers when models err. 这些指标能够区分真正的认知多样性与表层差异。 These measures distinguish genuine epistemic diversity from surface-level variation.
对照分析与预测基准。Controls and forecasting benchmark.
为区分结构性相关与共享知识的影响，我们纳入了不存在正确答案的对照分析，从而检验在没有信号的情况下，模型间的一致性是否仍然存在。 To distinguish structural correlation from shared knowledge, we include control analyses in which no correct answer exists, allowing us to test whether model agreement persists even when no signal is present. 最后，我们纳入了 Predict-the-Future，这是一个预测评估集，其结果发生在模型知识截止日期之后。 Finally, we include Predict-the-Future, a forecasting evaluation set whose outcomes postdate model knowledge cutoffs. 所有结果均经过人工核实。 All outcomes are manually verified. 如果聚合能够从不确定的作答者那里提取真相，那么它应当在这一情境下有所帮助；因此，接近随机水平的准确率构成了一项严格的否定性检验。 If aggregation can extract truth from uncertain respondents, it should help in this setting; accuracy near chance therefore provides a stringent negative test.

在各项缺少验证器的基准上，我们发现，自聚合无法提供一条通过增加推理时计算量来可靠提升真实性的途径。 Across verifier-absent benchmarks, we find that self-aggregation does not provide a reliable path from inference-time compute to truthfulness. 尽管共识显著增强，准确率却保持不变或有所下降。 Despite substantial increases in consensus, accuracy remains flat or degrades. 这种失败是系统性的，而非偶然现象。 This failure is systematic rather than accidental. 我们表明，语言模型出错时，其错误往往相互关联，违反了群体智慧所要求的独立性假设。 We show that when language models err, their errors are often correlated, violating the independence assumptions required for crowd wisdom. 因此，当模型错误相互关联时，基于一致性、置信度或预测受欢迎程度等内部信号的聚合规则，无法随着规模扩大而可靠地提升真实性。 Consequently, when model errors are correlated, aggregation rules based on internal signals like agreement, confidence, or predicted popularity cannot reliably scale truthfulness.

4.1 聚合无法提升真实性4.1 Aggregation Fails to Improve Truthfulness
我们在四项缺少验证器的基准上评估了五种聚合规则（多数投票、最高置信度、置信度加权投票、预测加权投票和 Surprisingly Popular）：Humanity’s Last Exam（HLE）、BoolQ、Com2Sense 和 Predict-the-Future。 We evaluate five aggregation rules (majority vote, highest confidence, confidence-weighted vote, prediction-weighted vote, and Surprisingly Popular) across four verifier-absent benchmarks: Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE), BoolQ, Com2Sense, and Predict-the-Future. 在不同模型、温度和数据集上，没有任何方法能够持续优于单次采样基线（图 1）。 Across models, temperatures, and datasets, no method consistently outperforms single-sample baselines (Figure 1). 增加样本数量会增强共识，却不会提高正确性。 Increasing the number of samples increases consensus, but not correctness.
预测基准提供了最明确的否定性检验：由于结果发生在模型知识截止日期之后，如果聚合能够提取潜在的专业知识，那么它就应当在这里取得成功。 The forecasting benchmark provides the clearest negative test: because outcomes postdate model knowledge cutoffs, aggregation should succeed here if it could extract latent expertise. 然而，所有方法的表现仍与随机水平无法区分。 Instead, all methods remain indistinguishable from chance.
这些失败具有结构性。 These failures are structural. 群体智慧要求错误之间的相关性足够低，使错误能够在聚合过程中相互抵消（Surowiecki, 2004; de Condorcet, 1785）。 Crowd wisdom requires errors to be uncorrelated enough that mistakes cancel under aggregation (Surowiecki, 2004; de Condorcet, 1785). 语言模型违反了这一假设——共享的训练数据、目标和后训练激励产生了共同的先验与盲点。 Language models violate this assumption - shared training data, objectives, and post-training incentives produce shared priors and blind spots. 因此，聚合增强了共识，却没有提高正确性。 Hence, aggregation increases consensus without increasing correctness.
由于多数投票未能奏效，我们接下来考察其他模型内部信号（如置信度或预测受欢迎程度）能否恢复正确性。 Since majority voting fails, we next examine whether other model-internal signals - like confidence or predicted popularity - can recover correctness. 我们测试了基于置信度的加权方法和 Surprisingly Popular（SP）算法。 We test confidence-based weighting and the Surprisingly Popular (SP) algorithm. 两者均未奏效，因为在实践中，它们最终衡量的是预期共识，而非真相。 Both fail because, in practice, they end up measuring expected consensus rather than truth.
置信度不能反映正确性。 Confidence does not track correctness. 自报置信度与准确率的相关性较弱，与一致性的相关性也处于类似水平。 Self-reported confidence correlates weakly with accuracy but similarly with agreement. 在高难度基准上，高置信度的答案往往是错误的——这是因为迎合式训练会奖励听起来符合常见模式的输出（Sharma et al., 2023; Leng et al., 2025）。 On hard benchmarks, confident answers are often wrong - due to sycophantic training that rewards typical-sounding outputs (Sharma et al., 2023; Leng et al., 2025).
SP 算法假设存在一个专家少数群体，他们既知道真相，又能预见多数群体的错误（Prelec et al., 2017）。 The SP algorithm assumes an expert minority that both knows the truth and anticipates the majority’s error (Prelec et al., 2017). 语言模型群体很少呈现这种结构。 Language model populations rarely exhibit this structure. 作为一项诊断性分析，我们评估了 inverse-SP。 As a diagnostic, we evaluate inverse-SP. 在 HLE 上，inverse-SP 达到了 80% 的准确率，这意味着标准 SP 信号与正确性存在系统性负相关。 On HLE, inverse-SP attains 80% accuracy, implying that the standard SP signal is systematically anti-correlated with correctness. 在其他数据集上，inverse-SP 的表现处于随机水平。 On other datasets, inverse-SP performs at chance. 因此，惊讶差距（surprise gap）的符号在不同任务之间并不稳定，这意味着它无法作为可靠的验证信号。 The sign of the surprise gap is therefore not stable across tasks, meaning it cannot serve as a reliable verification signal.

5 讨论与局限性5 Discussion and Limitations
我们的结果揭示了推理时扩展的一个边界条件。 Our results identify a boundary condition for inference-time scaling. 当候选答案能够通过外部方式核验时，采样与聚合可以提升性能。 Sampling and aggregation improve performance when candidates can be externally verified. 当不存在核验器时，新增样本通常仍从同一认知先验中抽取。 When no verifier exists, additional samples are typically drawn from the same epistemic prior. 在这种情形下，聚合增强共识的速度快于其提升真实性的速度。 In this regime, aggregation increases consensus faster than it increases truth.
5.1 内部聚合信号为何失效5.1 Why Internal Aggregation Signals Fail
更丰富的内部信号也无法克服这一局限。 Richer internal signals do not resolve this limitation. 一致程度、置信度、预测受欢迎程度和意外差值，都能有力地预测群体会给出什么回答。 Agreement, confidence, predicted popularity, and surprise gaps are all strong predictors of what the crowd will say. 但它们都无法在不同任务中提供稳定的正确性信号。 None provides a stable signal of correctness across tasks.
出乎意料地受欢迎（Surprisingly Popular，SP）算法说明了这一问题。 The Surprisingly Popular (SP) algorithm illustrates the problem. 该算法的成功依赖于一种稳定的少数专家结构。 Its success depends on a stable expert-minority structure. 我们的诊断分析表明，这种结构在语言模型群体中并不稳定存在。 Our diagnostics show that this structure is not reliably present in language model populations. 意外差值的正负号因数据集而异，在某些情况下甚至与正确性负相关。 The sign of the surprise gap varies across datasets, and in some cases is anti-correlated with correctness. 一个含义随任务变化的信号无法充当核验器。 A signal whose semantics change across tasks cannot function as a verifier.
基于置信度的方法也因同样的原因而失效。 Confidence-based methods fail for the same reason. 言语化置信度与预期一致程度的关联，几乎与其同准确率的关联一样强。 Verbalized confidence tracks expected agreement nearly as strongly as it tracks accuracy. 因此，按置信度加权会放大占主导地位的错误观念，而非纠正它。 Weighting by confidence therefore amplifies the dominant misconception rather than correcting it.
5.2 社会预测比真伪验证更容易5.2 Social Prediction Is Easier Than Truth Verification
模型预测群体意见的能力显著优于其预测答案正确与否的能力。 Models predict collective opinion substantially better than they predict correctness. 这揭示了两种常被混为一谈的能力之间的区别：社会预测（其他人会怎么说？）与真伪验证（这个答案是否正确？）。 This reveals a separation between two capabilities that are often conflated: social prediction (what will others say?) and truth verification (is this answer correct?). 大多数聚合规则依赖的是前者。 Most aggregation rules operate on the former. 当群体系统性地犯错时，这些信号就会产生实质性的误导。 When the crowd is systematically wrong, these signals become actively misleading.
这一区别提示了社会预测的一种建设性用途。 This distinction suggests a constructive use of social prediction. 预测出的共识可以用于标记高风险问题，并触发检索、工具使用或暂缓作答，而非用于选择答案。 Rather than selecting answers, predicted consensus can be used to flag high-risk questions and trigger retrieval, tool use, or deferral. 在这一框架下，一致性并非真相的证据，而是共同失效模式的警示信号。 In this framing, agreement is not evidence of truth, but a warning sign of shared failure modes.
5.3 局限性5.3 Limitations
二元回答格式。Binary response format.
我们将研究限制在二元问题上，以便明确定义得票比例和基于意外程度的聚合。 We restrict to binary questions to make vote shares and surprise-based aggregation well-defined. 这使得不同方法之间能够进行清晰的比较，但限制了其对开放式生成的直接适用性。 This enables clean comparison across methods but limits direct applicability to open-ended generation. 向开放式场景的扩展通常会引入隐式验证步骤，这并不与我们的核心论点相矛盾。 Extensions to open-ended settings typically introduce implicit verification steps, which do not contradict our core claim.
置信度引出。Confidence elicitation.
我们使用言语化置信度，以契合黑箱使用场景。 We use verbalized confidence to match black-box usage. 尽管其他不确定性估计方法的表现可能有所不同，但核心失效模式并非噪声，而是错位：置信度与共识的关联，几乎与其同正确性的关联一样强。 While alternative uncertainty estimates may behave differently, the central failure mode is not noise but misalignment: confidence tracks consensus nearly as strongly as correctness.
多样性干预。Diversity interventions.
温度采样和模型集成对表面形式的改变大于对信念的改变。 Temperature sampling and model ensembling change surface form more than beliefs. 能够产生真正独立性的更强干预，通常会引入新的信息、目标或监督。 Stronger interventions that induce genuine independence typically introduce new information, objectives, or supervision. 这些干预实际上增加了验证或打破了共享先验，因此与我们的结论一致，而非驳斥了它。 These effectively add verification or break shared priors, aligning with rather than refuting our conclusion.
预测基准的范围。Forecasting benchmark scope.
Predict-the-Future 有意采用严格的要求，但规模相对较小。 Predict-the-Future is intentionally stringent but relatively small. 更大规模的重复研究将增强外部效度。 Larger-scale replications would strengthen external validity.
5.4 什么会改变这一结论？5.4 What Would Change the Conclusion?
我们的结果适用于无验证的自聚合。 Our results apply to self-aggregation without verification. 当系统采取以下措施时，真实性可能随规模扩大而提升： Truthfulness may scale when systems:
引入发挥验证器作用的依据来源（检索、工具、执行或人类反馈）， add grounding that functions as a verifier (retrieval, tools, execution, or human feedback),
通过互不重叠的训练或目标构建真正的认知多样性，或 engineer genuine epistemic diversity through disjoint training or objectives, or
学习基于外部标注证据训练的显式验证器。 learn explicit verifiers trained on externally labeled evidence.
这三种方式都引入了单纯投票所不具备的信息。 All three introduce information absent from pure polling. 因此，要使真实性随规模扩大而提升，需要验证或真正的独立性，而非从共享先验中获取更多样本。 Scaling truthfulness therefore requires verification or true independence, not more samples from a shared prior.
6 结论6 Conclusion
当语言模型的错误彼此相关时，在没有外部验证器的情况下，任何仅依赖一致性、置信度或预测受欢迎程度的聚合规则，都无法可靠地使真实性随规模扩大而提升。 When language model errors are correlated, no aggregation rule based solely on agreement, confidence, or predicted popularity can reliably scale truthfulness without an external verifier.
投票不能替代验证。 Polling is not a substitute for verification. 在五个经过指令微调的语言模型和多个无验证器的基准上，我们发现，即使推理成本高达 $25\times$，基于一致性的聚合规则和元认知聚合规则也无法相较于强大的单样本基线可靠地提高准确率。 Across five instruction-tuned language models and multiple verifier-absent benchmarks, we find that agreement-based and metacognitive aggregation rules do not reliably improve accuracy over strong single-sample baselines, even at up to $25\times$ inference cost.
这种失败是结构性的。 The failure is structural. 群体智慧依赖于错误之间的部分独立性，使错误能够在聚合过程中相互抵消。 Crowd wisdom relies on partially independent errors so that mistakes cancel under aggregation. 现代语言模型不满足这一假设。 Modern language models violate this assumption. 共同的训练数据、目标和后训练激励会形成共同的先验与盲点，从而导致高度相关的错误。 Shared training data, objectives, and post-training incentives induce shared priors and shared blind spots, yielding strongly correlated errors. 当占主导地位的答案是错误的时，增加样本和模型大多只会重复同样的错误，使聚合成为共同误解的放大器，而非纠错机制。 When the dominant answer is wrong, additional samples and additional models mostly reproduce the same mistake, turning aggregation into an amplifier of common misconceptions rather than a mechanism for correction.
由此可以得出一个明确的推论。 This yields a clear corollary. 当错误彼此相关时，在缺少外部验证器的情况下，任何仅基于内部信号的聚合规则都无法随着规模扩大而可靠地提升真实性。 When errors are correlated, no aggregation rule based solely on internal signals can reliably scale truthfulness in the absence of an external verifier. 置信度、预测受欢迎程度和惊讶差距主要反映的是预期共识，而非正确性。 Confidence, predicted popularity, and surprise gaps primarily track expected consensus, not correctness. 我们的诊断分析揭示了社会预测与真伪验证之间一贯存在的差距：模型预测他人会说什么的能力，显著强于其辨别何者为真的能力。 Our diagnostics reveal a consistent separation between social prediction and truth verification: models are substantially better at forecasting what others will say than at identifying what is true. 用于优化前者的信号无法被改作验证后者的工具。 Signals that optimize the former cannot be repurposed into a verifier for the latter.
这些结果界定了推理时扩展的边界。 These results delineate a boundary for inference-time scaling. 当存在验证器时，聚合可以提升性能，因为验证能够将额外样本转化为正确性的提升。 Aggregation improves performance when a verifier exists, because verification converts additional samples into additional correctness. 在缺少验证器的情境下，要随着规模扩大而提升真实性，就需要外部依据，或主动打破错误相关性的干预措施，而不是从共同的认知先验中抽取更多样本。 In verifier-absent regimes, scaling truthfulness requires external grounding or interventions that actively break error correlation, rather than drawing more samples from a shared epistemic prior. 简言之，在可以进行验证时，增加推理时计算量能够提升推理能力，但无法提升真理本身。 In short, inference-time compute scales reasoning when verification is available, but it does not scale truth itself.
我们的结果对语言模型的未来具有重要启示。 Our results have important implications for the future of language models. 从推理到知识，许多问题仅通过扩大参数规模、计算量和训练数据规模就得到了解决。 Many problems, from reasoning to knowledge, have been solved simply by scaling up parameters, compute, and training data. 然而，随着模型能力增强，诱导模型表现出真实性，以及为可扩展监督获取可靠验证器，将变得越来越重要。 However, as models become more capable, the importance of eliciting truthfulness and obtaining reliable verifiers for scalable oversight will increase. 我们的结果表明，单纯向问题投入更多计算资源的朴素方法很可能并不足够。 Our results imply that the naive approach of throwing compute at the problem is unlikely to suffice. 要验证可靠性并加强安全性，就需要更具创造性的解决方案。 More creative solutions will be necessary to certify reliability and reinforce safety.

我们表明，在缺少验证器的领域，让多个语言模型对答案进行投票并不能可靠地提高准确率。 We show that having multiple language models vote on answers does not reliably improve accuracy in domains that lack a verifier. 由于模型经常犯同样的错误，一致意见可能具有误导性。 Since models often make the same mistakes, agreement can be misleading. 我们希望，这能促使人们对聚合方法所能取得的效果形成更切合实际的预期，并推动采用真正验证正确性、而非仅仅衡量共识的方法。 We hope this encourages more realistic expectations about what aggregation methods can achieve, and pushes toward approaches that actually verify correctness rather than simply measure consensus.
附录 A 超出预期受欢迎算法Appendix A The Surprisingly Popular Algorithm
超出预期受欢迎（Surprisingly Popular，SP）算法（Prelec et al., 2017）旨在解决民主式聚合的一个根本局限：当大多数受访者缺乏相关知识时，多数投票法就会失效。 The Surprisingly Popular (SP) algorithm (Prelec et al., 2017) addresses a fundamental limitation of democratic aggregation: majority voting fails when most respondents lack the relevant knowledge. 标准方法无法区分自信的无知与真正的专业知识。 Standard approaches cannot distinguish between confident ignorance and genuine expertise. SP 通过引导受访者预测他人的回答，利用知情者与不知情者之间的信息不对称来解决这一问题。 SP solves this by eliciting predictions about others’ responses, exploiting an information asymmetry between those who know and those who do not.
问题设定。Setup.
考虑一个答案为 $A$ 和 $B$ 的二元问题。 Consider a binary question with answers $A$ and $B$ . 每位受访者 $i$ 提供： Each respondent $i$ provides:
一个投票选择 $v_{i}\in\{A,B\}$ A vote $v_{i}\in\{A,B\}$
一个预测值 $p_{i}\in[0,1]$，表示其预期投票选择 $A$ 的受访者比例 A prediction $p_{i}\in[0,1]$ representing the expected fraction of respondents voting $A$
令 $\bar{v}$ 表示实际投票选择 $A$ 的比例，$\bar{p}$ 表示平均预测值。 Let $\bar{v}$ denote the actual fraction voting $A$ , and $\bar{p}$ denote the average prediction. 若 $\bar{v}>\bar{p}$，则 SP 算法选择 $A$，否则选择 $B$。 The SP algorithm selects $A$ if $\bar{v}>\bar{p}$ , and $B$ otherwise. 换言之：选择比人们预测的更受欢迎的答案。 In words: choose the answer that is more popular than people predicted.
直观解释。Intuition.
该算法利用了元认知意识上的不对称性。 The algorithm exploits an asymmetry in metacognitive awareness. 考虑这个经典例子：“费城是宾夕法尼亚州的首府吗？” Consider the canonical example: “Is Philadelphia the capital of Pennsylvania?” 大多数人错误地认为是（多数人的答案是错的）。 Most people incorrectly believe yes (the majority is wrong). 知道正确答案（哈里斯堡）的人也知道，费城更有名，而且大多数人会猜错。 Those who know the correct answer (Harrisburg) also know that Philadelphia is more famous and that most people will guess wrong. 当被要求预测群体的回答时，他们会预测费城将获得较高的支持率。 When asked to predict the crowd, they forecast high support for Philadelphia. 与此同时，猜测答案为费城的人认为自己的答案很普遍，也会作出类似的预测。 Meanwhile, those who guess Philadelphia assume their answer is common and predict similarly.
结果是：几乎所有人都预测费城会获得较高的支持率，但由于部分受访者知道真相，费城的实际得票比例会低于预测值。 The result: nearly everyone predicts high support for Philadelphia, but the actual vote share is lower because some respondents know the truth. “否”这一答案获得的票数高于预测，因此出乎意料地受欢迎。 The “no” answer receives more votes than predicted, making it surprisingly popular. 这种出乎意料的现象揭示了知情受访者的存在，而他们的知识会在简单多数投票中被淹没。 This surprise reveals the presence of informed respondents whose knowledge would be drowned out by simple majority voting.
具体示例（费城）。Worked example (Philadelphia).
考虑问题“费城是宾夕法尼亚州的首府吗？”，备选答案为 YES 和 NO（正确答案：NO，首府是哈里斯堡）。 Consider the question “Is Philadelphia the capital of Pennsylvania?” with answers YES and NO (truth: NO, the capital is Harrisburg). 假设 $80\%$ 的受访者不知情并投票选择 YES，而 $20\%$ 的受访者知情并投票选择 NO。 Suppose $80\%$ of respondents are uninformed and vote YES, while $20\%$ are informed and vote NO. 每位受访者还会预测所有受访者中投票选择 YES 的比例。 Each respondent also predicts the overall fraction voting YES. 假设不知情受访者的预测为 $p_{i}=0.95$（他们预期几乎所有人都会选择 YES），而知情受访者的预测为 $p_{i}=0.80$（他们知道大多数人会猜 YES）。 Assume uninformed respondents predict $p_{i}=0.95$ (they expect near-consensus on YES), while informed respondents predict $p_{i}=0.80$ (they know most people will guess YES).
令 $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}$ 表示 YES 的实际得票比例，$\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$ 表示对 YES 得票比例的平均预测值。 Let $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}$ be the observed vote share for YES and $\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$ the average predicted vote share for YES. 则 Then 
| | $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}=0.80,\qquad\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}=0.80\cdot 0.95+0.20\cdot 0.80=0.92.$ |
若 $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}>\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$，SP 选择 YES，否则选择 NO。 SP selects YES if $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}>\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$ , and otherwise selects NO. 此处 $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}<\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$，因此 SP 选择 NO。 Here $\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}<\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}$ , so SP selects NO. 等价地，NO 这一答案“出乎意料地受欢迎”，因为其实际得票比例超过了预测比例： Equivalently, the NO answer is “surprisingly popular” because its observed share exceeds its predicted share: 
| | $\bar{v}_{\texttt{NO}}=1-\bar{v}_{\texttt{YES}}=0.20\quad\text{and}\quad\bar{p}_{\texttt{NO}}=1-\bar{p}_{\texttt{YES}}=0.08,$ |
因此 $\bar{v}_{\texttt{NO}}>\bar{p}_{\texttt{NO}}$。 so $\bar{v}_{\texttt{NO}}>\bar{p}_{\texttt{NO}}$ . 直观而言，知情受访者既 (i) 投票选择正确答案，又 (ii) 预见到多数人的错误，从而形成了 SP 所利用的系统性预测差距。 Intuitively, informed respondents both (i) vote correctly and (ii) anticipate the majority’s mistake, creating a systematic prediction gap that SP exploits.
理论保证。Theoretical Guarantee.
Prelec 等人 (2017) 证明，在受访者具有共同先验并根据私人信号更新信念的贝叶斯模型下，只要满足以下条件，SP 就能识别出正确答案： Prelec et al. (2017) prove that under a Bayesian model where respondents share a common prior and update beliefs based on private signals, SP recovers the truth whenever the following conditions hold:
受访者如实报告自己的信念 Respondents report their beliefs honestly
受访者对他人的预测是无偏的 Respondents form unbiased predictions about others
存在一个能够获知正确答案的子群体 There exists a subpopulation with access to the correct answer
关键洞见在于，掌握正确答案者与未掌握正确答案者的预测存在系统性差异。 The key insight is that truth-holders make systematically different predictions than non-truth-holders. 如果 $A$ 是正确答案，知道 $A$ 正确的人对 $A$ 支持率的预测低于相信 $B$ 正确的人（因为知道 $A$ 正确的人明白，相信 $B$ 正确的人不仅存在，而且很常见）。 If $A$ is correct, those who know $A$ predict lower support for $A$ than those who believe $B$ (because $A$ -knowers understand that $B$ -believers exist and are common). 这种预测差距使 $A$ 的实际支持率超过其预测支持率，从而促使 SP 选择 $A$。 This prediction gap causes $A$ to exceed its predicted support, triggering selection by SP.
SP 何时失效。When SP Fails.
该算法要求存在一种“专家少数群体”结构：部分受访者必须知道正确答案，同时明白其他人并不知道。 The algorithm requires an “expert minority” structure: some respondents must know the truth while understanding that others do not. SP 在以下情况下会失效： SP fails when:
没有受访者知道答案（纯粹猜测） No respondent knows the answer (pure guessing)
所有人都知道答案（不可能出现意外） Everyone knows the answer (no surprise possible)
知情受访者无法预测他人的无知 Knowledgeable respondents cannot predict others’ ignorance
错误之间存在相关性，导致错误答案的实际支持率也超过预测值 Errors are correlated such that wrong answers also exceed predictions
我们的实验检验语言模型是否呈现出 SP 所要求的专家少数群体结构。 Our experiments test whether language models exhibit the expert-minority structure SP requires. 当它们呈现出这种结构时（HLE），SP 会带来显著提升。 When they do (HLE), SP yields large gains. 当它们未呈现出这种结构时（BoolQ），SP 会选择那些因与正确性无关的原因而出乎意料地受欢迎的答案，从而降低准确率。 When they do not (BoolQ), SP degrades accuracy by selecting answers that are surprisingly popular for reasons unrelated to correctness.
