Agentic AI and the next intelligence explosion智能体式 AI 与下一次智能爆炸
Abstract摘要
For decades, the artificial intelligence (AI) “singularity” [21] has been heralded as a single, titanic mind bootstrapping itself to godlike intelligence [9], consolidating all cognition into a cold silicon point. But this vision is almost certainly wrong in its most fundamental assumption. If AI development follows the path of previous major evolutionary transitions [19] or “intelligence explosions” [29], our current step-change in computational intelligence will be plural, social, and deeply entangled with its forebears (us!).
几十年来，人工智能（AI）“奇点”[21] 一直被描绘为一个单一而庞大的心智，通过自我提升达到神一般的智能水平 [9]，将一切认知汇聚于一个冰冷的硅基点。但这一愿景最根本的假设几乎肯定是错误的。如果 AI 的发展遵循以往重大演化转变 [19] 或“智能爆炸”[29] 的路径，那么我们当前所经历的计算智能跃迁将是多元的、社会性的，并与其前身（也就是我们！）深度交织。
By its nature, intelligence is high-dimensional and relational, not a single quantity that must be unambiguously less or greater than human scale. In fact, it is unclear what we even mean by “human scale,” given that our intelligence is already a collective property, not an individual one. Recent advances in agentic AI show us once again that intelligence has always fundamentally involved the interaction of distinctive, distributed perspectives [23], and it is from social organization [11] that transformative intelligence has and will continue to emerge.
从本质上说，智能是高维的、关系性的，而不是一个必然能够被明确判定为低于或高于人类水平的单一量。事实上，鉴于我们的智能本就具有集体属性，而非个体属性，我们甚至不清楚所谓的“人类水平”究竟意味着什么。智能体式 AI 的近期进展再次向我们表明，智能从根本上始终涉及独特且分布式的视角之间的互动 [23]，而变革性智能过去诞生于、未来也将继续诞生于社会组织 [11]。
We can observe this in at least two ways: In the orchestration of societies of AI agents [28] by and with human users in new “centaur” configurations, and in the microsocieties that flourish inside and between reasoning models themselves. Let’s start with the latter.
我们至少可以从两个方面观察到这一点：一是人类用户通过新的“半人马”式配置来组织 AI 智能体社会 [28]，并参与其中；二是在推理模型自身内部及模型之间蓬勃发展的微型社会。让我们先从后者谈起。
What happens inside an ostensibly singular reasoning model? A community conversation, as it turns out. In a recent study, we demonstrated that frontier reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1 and QwQ-32B do not improve simply by “thinking longer.” Instead, they simulate complex, multi-agent-like interactions within their own chain of thought—what we term a “society of thought” [8]. These models spontaneously generate internal debates among distinct cognitive perspectives that argue, question, verify, and reconcile. This conversational structure causally accounts for the models’ accuracy advantage on hard reasoning tasks, which we demonstrated by explicitly priming and amplifying multi-party conversation.
一个表面上单一的推理模型内部会发生什么？事实证明，其中进行的是一场群体对话。在最近的一项研究中，我们证明，DeepSeek-R1 和 QwQ-32B 等前沿推理模型并非仅仅通过“思考更久”来提升表现。相反，它们在自身的思维链中模拟复杂的、类似多智能体的互动——我们将其称为“思维社会”（society of thought）[8]。这些模型会自发生成不同认知视角之间的内部辩论，让这些视角相互争辩、质疑、核验并协调分歧。这种对话结构从因果上解释了这些模型在高难度推理任务中的准确率优势，我们通过显式诱导和强化多方对话证明了这一点。
The finding is striking because it reveals an emergent behavior. None of these models were trained to produce societies of thought. When reinforcement learning is used to reward base models solely for reasoning accuracy, they spontaneously increase conversational, multi-perspective behaviors [27]. Models are rediscovering, through optimization pressure alone, what centuries of epistemology and decades of cognitive science [12] have suggested: that robust reasoning is a social process [13], even when it occurs within a single mind [10]. The exact nature of that emergent behavior is, of course, to be further discovered (and invented) as cooperative human-agent social dynamics become more grounded, complex, and durable. What proves fundamental about socially-mediated reasoning in general, and what is specific to fine-tuned and reinforced contexts, is likely to inspire considerable research in the coming years.
这一发现引人注目，因为它揭示了一种涌现行为。这些模型都未曾接受过生成思维社会的训练。当使用强化学习、仅依据推理准确率来奖励基础模型时，它们会自发增加对话式、多视角的行为 [27]。仅凭优化压力，模型正在重新发现数百年的认识论和数十年的认知科学 [12] 所提示的道理：稳健的推理是一种社会过程 [13]，即使它发生在单一心智内部也是如此 [10]。当然，随着人类与智能体之间的合作性社会动态变得更加扎根于现实、更加复杂且持久，这种涌现行为的确切性质仍有待进一步发现（和创造）。社会中介推理的一般性根本特征是什么，以及哪些特征是微调和强化学习情境所特有的，这些问题很可能在未来几年激发大量研究。
This opens a vast—yet familiar—design space. The social and organizational sciences have spent a century studying how team size [24], composition, hierarchy [26], role differentiation, conflict norms, institutions, and network structures shape collective performance. Almost none of this research has been brought to bear on AI reasoning [25]. Today’s reasoning models produce a single conversation—an AI town hall transcript. But effective groups exhibit hierarchy, specialization, division of labor, and structured disagreement. To explore this, we will need systems that support multiple parallel, converging, and diverging streams of deliberation—architectures in which brainstorming, devil’s advocacy, and constructive conflict are not accidental emergent properties but designed features. The toolkits of team science, small-group sociology, and social psychology become blueprints for next-generation AI development.
这开辟了一个广阔而又熟悉的设计空间。社会科学和组织科学已用一个世纪的时间研究团队规模 [24]、成员构成、层级结构 [26]、角色分化、冲突规范、制度以及网络结构如何塑造集体表现。这些研究几乎都尚未被应用于 AI 推理 [25]。当今的推理模型生成的是一场单一的对话——一份 AI 市民大会的文字记录。然而，高效的群体具有层级结构、专业化、劳动分工和有组织的意见分歧。为了探索这一点，我们需要能够支持多条并行、汇聚和分岔的思考讨论流的系统——在这样的架构中，头脑风暴、反方质辩和建设性冲突不是偶然涌现的属性，而是有意设计的功能。团队科学、小群体社会学和社会心理学的工具体系由此成为下一代 AI 开发的蓝图。
In addition to its practical applications, these insights may clarify the entire history of intelligence. Each prior “intelligence explosion” was not an upgrade to individual cognitive hardware, but the emergence of a new, socially aggregated unit of cognition [17]. Primate intelligence scaled with social group size [4], not habitat difficulty. Human language created what Michael Tomasello calls the “cultural ratchet” [20]: knowledge accumulating across generations without any individual requirement to reconstruct the whole. Writing, law, and bureaucracy externalized social intelligence into infrastructure [6], institutions that coordinate across longer time horizons than any participant within them. A Sumerian scribe running a grain accounting system did not comprehend its macroeconomic function; the system was functionally more intelligent than he was.
除了实际应用之外，这些见解还可能帮助我们厘清智能的整个历史。此前的每一次“智能爆炸”都不是个体认知硬件的升级，而是一个新的、通过社会聚合形成的认知单元的涌现 [17]。灵长类动物的智能随社会群体规模的扩大而提升 [4]，而非随栖息环境挑战程度的增加而提升。人类语言创造了 Michael Tomasello 所称的“文化棘轮”（cultural ratchet）[20]：知识能够跨代积累，而无须任何个体重建其全部内容。文字、法律和官僚体系将社会智能外化为基础设施 [6]，形成了能够在比其中任何参与者所能涵盖的时间跨度更长的范围内开展协调的制度。一位操作粮食核算系统的苏美尔书吏并不理解该系统的宏观经济功能；从功能上看，这个系统比他更具智能。
AI extends this sequence. Large language models are trained on the accumulated output of human social cognition [16]—the cultural ratchet made computationally active, every parameter a compressed residue of communicative exchange. What migrates into silicon is not abstract reasoning but social intelligence in externalized form [5], encountering itself on a new substrate.
AI 延续了这一演进序列。大型语言模型以人类社会认知积累的产出为训练材料 [16]——文化棘轮由此在计算中运转起来，每一个参数都是交往交流所留下的压缩痕迹。迁入硅基载体的并非抽象推理，而是以外化形式存在的社会智能 [5]，它在一种新的基底上与自身相遇。
If intelligence is inherently social, then the path to more powerful AI runs not through building a single colossal oracle but through composing richer social systems—and these systems will be hybrid. We have entered the era of human-AI centaurs: composite actors that are neither purely human nor purely machine. Centaur actors can take many forms and inhabit many different roles. Each one of us may move in and out of diverse ensembles many times a day: one human directing many AI agents; one AI serving many humans; many humans and many AIs collaborating in shifting configurations [3].
如果智能本质上具有社会性，那么通往更强大 AI 的道路就不是构建一个庞大的单一神谕系统，而是组建更丰富的社会系统——而这些系统将是混合式的。我们已经进入人类与 AI 组成的半人马时代：这类复合行动者既非纯粹的人类，也非纯粹的机器。半人马行动者可以呈现多种形式，并承担多种不同的角色。我们每个人每天都可能多次进入和退出不同的协作组合：一个人指挥多个 AI 智能体；一个 AI 服务多个人；多个人与多个 AI 以不断变化的组合方式开展协作 [3]。
A corporation or state comprising myriad of humans already holds singular legal standing and acts with collective agency that no individual member can fully control. The recent explosion of agentic AI suggests the possibility of something similar at the scale of billions of interacting minds, human and non-human alike. Platforms like OpenClaw, an open source platform for building multi-purpose AI agents that persist within a computer, and Moltbook, a popular social network for AI agents to interact, offer embryonic glimpses [28] of this future. But the deeper structural shift goes beyond any single platform. Agents can now renew and fork themselves, splitting into two versions, and interact with one another; an agent facing a complex task can initiate new copies, differentiate and assign them subtasks, then recombine the results. Imagine an agent confronting a dauntingly complex problem spawns an internal society of thought. One emergent perspective, encountering a sub-problem beyond its reach, spawns its own subordinate society, a recursive descent into collective deliberation that expands when complexity demands and collapses when the problem resolves. Conflict is not a bug but a resource, flexibly instantiated and dissolved at every level of the folding and unfolding hypergraph of conversations.
由众多人组成的公司或国家，已经拥有单一的法律主体地位，并以任何个体成员都无法完全控制的集体能动性采取行动。智能体式 AI 近期的爆发式发展表明，类似的情形也可能出现在数十亿个相互作用的心智所构成的规模上，其中既包括人类心智，也包括非人类心智。OpenClaw 是一个用于构建可持续驻留于计算机中的多用途 AI 智能体的开源平台，Moltbook 则是一个供 AI 智能体互动的热门社交网络；这类平台让我们得以初步窥见这一未来的雏形 [28]。但更深层的结构性转变超越了任何单一平台。如今，智能体可以更新自身，也可以通过分叉分裂成两个版本，并彼此互动；面对复杂任务的智能体可以创建新的副本，使其分化并为其分配子任务，然后重新整合结果。试想，一个智能体在面对极其复杂的问题时，生成了一个内部的思维社会。其中涌现的某个视角在遇到超出自身能力范围的子问题时，又生成了自己的下属社会，由此递归地深入集体审议；这一过程在复杂性提出要求时扩展，在问题解决时收拢。冲突并非缺陷，而是一种资源，在不断折叠与展开的对话超图的每个层级上，都可以灵活地被实例化和消解。
This implies a very different approach to scaling. It is not only about scaling the raw computational capacity of an agent, but about building systems that can operate at the scale and within the context of a real society. This means putting as much effort into building agent institutions as building agents themselves. The dominant paradigm for AI alignment—Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback [2]—resembles a parent-child model of correction, fundamentally dyadic and unable to scale to billions of agents. The social intelligence perspective suggests an alternative: institutional alignment [15]. Just as human societies rely not on individual virtue but on persistent institutional templates [14]—courtrooms, markets, bureaucracies—defined by roles and norms, scalable AI ecosystems will require digital equivalents [1]. The identity of any agent matters less than its ability to fulfill a role protocol, just as a courtroom functions because “judge,” “attorney,” and “jury” are well-defined slots, independent of who occupies them.
这意味着一种截然不同的规模扩展路径。这不仅涉及扩展智能体的原始计算能力，还涉及构建能够在真实社会的规模和情境中运行的系统。这意味着，在构建智能体制度方面，需要投入与构建智能体本身同样多的努力。AI 对齐的主导范式——基于人类反馈的强化学习（Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback）[2]——类似于亲子式的纠正模式，本质上是一种二元关系，无法扩展到数十亿个智能体。社会智能视角提出了另一条路径：制度性对齐 [15]。正如人类社会依靠的并非个体美德，而是由角色和规范界定的持久性制度模板 [14]——法庭、市场、官僚机构——可扩展的 AI 生态系统也将需要与之对应的数字化制度 [1]。任何智能体的身份都不如其履行角色协议的能力重要，正如法庭之所以能够运作，是因为“法官”“律师”和“陪审团”都是定义明确的角色位置，而不取决于由谁占据这些位置。
Nowhere is this more urgent than in governance itself. When AI systems are deployed in high-stakes decisions—hiring, sentencing, benefits allocation, regulatory enforcement—the question of who audits the auditors becomes unavoidable. The answer may be constitutional in structure. Governments will need AI systems with distinct, explicitly invested values—transparency, equity, due process—whose function is to check and balance AI systems deployed by the private sector and other branches of government, and vice versa. For example, a labor department AI may audit a corporation’s hiring algorithm for disparate impact; a judicial branch AI may evaluate whether an executive branch AI’s risk assessments meet constitutional standards. The alternative is, for example, for the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission to ineffectively hire business school graduates armed with Excel spreadsheets to combat high-dimensional collusion of AI-augmented trading platforms.
这一需求在治理本身这一领域尤为迫切。当 AI 系统被用于招聘、量刑、福利分配和监管执法等重大决策时，谁来审计审计者的问题便无法回避。答案在结构上或许具有宪制性质。政府将需要被明确赋予不同价值取向——透明、公平、正当程序——的 AI 系统，其职能是对私营部门及政府其他分支部署的 AI 系统进行制约与平衡，而后者也应反过来对前者进行制衡。例如，劳动部门的 AI 可以审计某家公司的招聘算法是否造成差别影响；司法部门的 AI 可以评估行政部门 AI 的风险评估是否符合宪法标准。另一种做法则是，例如，让美国证券交易委员会雇用配备 Excel 电子表格的商学院毕业生，低效地对抗 AI 增强型交易平台之间的高维合谋。
“Governance,” however, does not only mean what governments do. Governance systems, in the cybernetic sense of the term, need to be built into human-agent and agent-to-agent systems as they grow and complexify. This will likely entail means to ensure and verify outcomes and decisions of multiple-stakeholder deliberation, procedural delegation of tasks and sub-tasks and reliable scaffolds for automating delicate inter-agent collaborations. Such protocols may have as much real-world effect for “agent governance” as any laws will.
然而，“治理”并不只意味着政府所做的事情。随着人类与智能体系统以及智能体之间的系统不断发展并变得复杂，需要将控制论意义上的治理系统内嵌其中。这很可能需要建立相应机制，以确保并验证多利益相关方审议的结果与决策、按程序委派任务和子任务，并提供可靠的支撑框架，以实现精细的智能体间协作的自动化。此类协议对“智能体治理”产生的现实影响，可能与任何法律同样深远。
Crucially, humans remain in the loop. Agent institutions are populated by both humans and AI agents in different roles and configurations. It’s not “either/or”, but “both/and”. The U.S. Founders would have recognized the logic [7]: no single concentration of intelligence, human or artificial, should regulate itself. Power must check power, and in a world of artificial agents, this means building conflict and oversight into the institutional architecture.
关键在于，人类仍然参与其中。在智能体制度中，人类和 AI 智能体以不同的角色和组合方式共同参与。这不是“非此即彼”，而是“二者兼具”。美国的建国者们应当会认同这一逻辑 [7]：任何单一的智能集中体，无论是人类的还是人工的，都不应自我监管。权力必须制衡权力，而在一个由人工智能体构成的世界中，这意味着要将冲突与监督纳入制度架构。
The vision we describe is neither utopian nor dystopian; it is evolutionary. Any emergent intelligence explosion will be seeded by eight billion humans interacting with hundreds of billions, eventually trillions, of AI agents. The scaffold is not a single mind ascending but a combinatorial society complexifying: intelligence growing like a city [18], not a single meta-mind.
我们所描绘的愿景既非乌托邦式，也非反乌托邦式；它是演化式的。任何涌现的智能爆炸，都将孕育于八十亿人类与数千亿、最终数万亿个 AI 智能体的互动之中。其支撑框架并非一个不断跃升的单一心智，而是一个通过组合不断复杂化的社会：智能像城市一样生长 [18]，而非形成一个单一的元心智。
A “monolithic singularity” framework leads to policies aimed at preventing a technology that may never exist. Instead, we should be looking for the next intelligence explosion in the same place from which the previous ones emerged: in cooperative, competitive and creative interaction between multitudes of socially intelligent minds. The difference this time is that most of those minds will be non-biological. This plurality model [22] focuses attention where it belongs: on the design of mixed human-AI social systems, the norms that govern them, and the institutions and protocols through which they conflict and coordinate.
“单体奇点”（monolithic singularity）框架会导向旨在阻止一种可能永远不会存在的技术的政策。相反，我们应当在历次智能爆炸的发源之处寻找下一次智能爆炸：众多具备社会智能的心智之间合作、竞争与创造性的互动。这一次的不同之处在于，这些心智中的大多数将是非生物的。这种多元性模型（plurality model）[22] 将注意力引向其应当关注之处：人类与 AI 混合社会系统的设计、支配这些系统的规范，以及它们借以发生冲突并开展协调的制度与协议。
In a very real sense, the intelligence explosion is already here [22]—in the society of thought debating inside every reasoning model, in the centaur workflows reshaping every knowledge profession, in the recursive agent ecologies beginning to fork and collaborate at scale, and in the constitutional questions we must now begin to ask. The question is not whether intelligence will become radically more powerful, but whether we will build the social infrastructure worthy of what it is becoming. No mind is an island.
从非常切实的意义上说，智能爆炸已经到来 [22]——它存在于每个推理模型内部展开辩论的思维社会之中，存在于重塑各类知识型职业的人机协作工作流之中，存在于开始大规模分叉与协作的递归智能体生态之中，也存在于我们如今必须开始追问的宪制问题之中。问题不在于智能是否会变得强大得多，而在于我们是否会构建起与其演变相匹配的社会基础设施。没有任何心智是一座孤岛。
