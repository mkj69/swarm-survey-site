邓巴数（Dunbar's number）是指一种假定的认知上限，即一个人能够与之维持稳定社会关系的人数上限；在这种关系中，个体知道每个人是谁，也知道每个人与其他所有人的关系。 Dunbar's number is a suggested cognitive limit to the number of people with whom one can maintain stable social relationships—relationships in which an individual knows who each person is and how each person relates to every other person. 这一数字最早由英国人类学家 Robin Dunbar 于 1990 年代提出，他发现灵长类动物的脑大小与平均社会群体规模之间存在相关性。 This number was first proposed in the 1990s by Robin Dunbar, a British anthropologist who found a correlation between primate brain size and average social group size. 他采用人类的平均脑大小，并根据灵长类动物的研究结果进行外推，提出人类可以轻松维持 150 段稳定关系。 By using the average human brain size and extrapolating from the results of primates, he proposed that humans can comfortably maintain 150 stable relationships. 有一些证据表明，大脑结构能够预测一个人拥有的朋友数量，但其中的因果关系尚待确定。 There is some evidence that brain structure predicts the number of friends one has, though causality remains to be seen.
Dunbar 曾通俗地将这一原理解释为：“假如你在酒吧偶然遇到某些人，即使未经邀请就加入他们一起喝一杯，你也不会觉得尴尬；这样的人有多少，这个数字就是多少。” Dunbar explained the principle informally as "the number of people you would not feel embarrassed about joining uninvited for a drink if you happened to bump into them in a bar." Dunbar 从理论上提出：“这一上限直接取决于新皮质的相对大小，而这又限制了群体规模……新皮质处理能力所施加的限制，仅仅是对能够与之维持稳定人际关系的个体数量的限制。” Dunbar theorised that "this limit is a direct function of relative neocortex size, and that this, in turn, limits group size ... the limit imposed by neocortical processing capacity is simply on the number of individuals with whom a stable inter-personal relationship can be maintained". 在这一关系圈的外围，该数字还包括过去的同伴，例如高中时期的朋友；如果再次相遇，个体会希望与他们重新建立联系。 On the periphery, the number also includes past colleagues, such as high school friends, with whom a person would want to reacquaint themselves if they met again. 支持者认为，人数超过这一数字时，通常需要约束更严格的规则、法律以及强制执行的规范，才能维持一个稳定且有凝聚力的群体。 Proponents assert that numbers larger than this generally require more restrictive rules, laws, and enforced norms to maintain a stable, cohesive group. 有人提出，这一数字介于 100 至 250 之间，通常采用的数值为 150。 It has been proposed to lie between 100 and 250, with a commonly used value of 150.
研究背景Research background
灵长类动物学家指出，由于灵长类动物具有高度社会性，它们必须与所属社会群体的其他成员保持个体间的接触，通常通过社交梳理（social grooming）来实现。 Primatologists have noted that, owing to their highly social nature, primates must maintain personal contact with the other members of their social group, usually through social grooming. 这类社会群体在灵长类动物共同生活的实体群体内部发挥着保护性小团体的作用。 Such social groups function as protective cliques within the physical groups in which the primates live. 一只灵长类动物能够持续掌握其情况的社会群体成员数量，似乎受到新皮质体积的限制。 The number of social group members a primate can track appears to be limited by the volume of the neocortex. 这表明，社会群体规模存在一个物种特异性指标，可以根据该物种的平均新皮质体积计算得出。 This suggests that there is a species-specific index of the social group size, computable from the species' mean neocortical volume.
1992 年，Dunbar 利用在非人灵长类动物中观察到的相关性，预测了人类的社会群体规模。 In 1992, Dunbar used the correlation observed for non-human primates to predict a social group size for humans. Dunbar 利用 38 个灵长类动物属的数据建立回归方程，预测人类的“平均群体规模”为 148（粗略取整为 150）；由于误差较大（95% 置信区间为 100 至 230），他认为这一结果具有探索性质。 Using a regression equation on data for 38 primate genera, Dunbar predicted a human "mean group size" of 148 (casually rounded to 150), a result he considered exploratory because of the large error measure (a 95% confidence interval of 100 to 230).
随后，Dunbar 将这一预测与可观察到的人类群体规模进行了比较。 Dunbar then compared this prediction with observable group sizes for humans. Dunbar 首先假定，人类新皮质目前的平均大小形成于约 250,000 年前的更新世；在此基础上，他查阅人类学和民族志文献，寻找各种狩猎采集社会中类似人口普查的群体规模资料，因为这些社会是现存社会中最接近人类学所重建的更新世社会形态的实例。 Beginning with the assumption that the current mean size of the human neocortex had developed about 250,000 years ago, during the Pleistocene, Dunbar searched the anthropological and ethnographical literature for census-like group size information for various hunter–gatherer societies, the closest existing approximations to how anthropology reconstructs the Pleistocene societies. Dunbar 指出，这些群体可分为小型、中型和大型三类，分别对应游群、文化世系群体和部落，其成员数量范围分别为 30–50、100–200 和 500–2500。 Dunbar noted that the groups fell into three categories—small, medium and large, equivalent to bands, cultural lineage groups and tribes—with respective size ranges of 30–50, 100–200 and 500–2500 members each.
Dunbar 对村落和部落规模的调查结果似乎也接近这一预测值，其中包括：新石器时代农业村落的估计规模为 150 人；哈特派聚居点在达到 150 人时分裂；某学科下一个细分专业领域的学者人数上限为 200 人；古罗马时期以及自 16 世纪以来的近现代职业军队的基本单位规模为 150 人；此外还有关于公司适宜规模的看法。 Dunbar's surveys of village and tribe sizes also appeared to approximate this predicted value, including 150 as the estimated size of a Neolithic farming village; 150 as the splitting point of Hutterite settlements; 200 as the upper bound on the number of academics in a discipline's sub-specialisation; 150 as the basic unit size of professional armies in Roman antiquity and in modern times since the 16th century, as well as notions of appropriate company size.
Dunbar 认为，只有对于那些有极强动力维系在一起的社群，150 才会是其平均群体规模。 Dunbar has argued that 150 would be the mean group size only for communities with a very high incentive to remain together. Dunbar 推测，要使这种规模的群体保持凝聚力，就必须将群体多达 42% 的时间用于社交梳理。 For a group of this size to remain cohesive, Dunbar speculated that as much as 42% of the group's time would have to be devoted to social grooming. 相应地，只有承受巨大生存压力的群体，例如自给自足的村落、游牧部落和历史上的军事群体，其平均规模才达到了 150 人。 Correspondingly, only groups under intense survival pressure, such as subsistence villages, nomadic tribes, and historical military groupings, have, on average, achieved the 150-member mark. 此外，Dunbar 指出，这类群体的成员在空间上几乎总是彼此邻近：“[...] 我们可以预期，群体规模的上限取决于社会成员的分散程度。 Moreover, Dunbar noted that such groups are almost always physically close: "[...] we might expect the upper limit on group size to depend on the degree of social dispersal. 在成员分散的社会中，个体见面的频率较低，因此彼此也较不熟悉，群体规模因而应当更小。” In dispersed societies, individuals will meet less often and will thus be less familiar with each other, so group sizes should be smaller in consequence." 因此，150 人的群体只有在绝对必要时才会出现，也就是在环境和经济压力极大的情况下。 Thus, the 150-member group would occur only because of absolute necessity—because of intense environmental and economic pressures.
Dunbar 在《梳理、闲谈与语言的演化》（Grooming, Gossip, and the Evolution of Language）中进一步提出，语言可能是作为一种“低成本”的社交梳理手段而产生的，使早期人类能够高效地维持社会凝聚力。 Dunbar, in Grooming, Gossip, and the Evolution of Language, proposes furthermore that language may have arisen as a "cheap" means of social grooming, allowing early humans to maintain social cohesion efficiently. Dunbar 推测，如果没有语言，人类就必须将近一半的时间花在社交梳理上，这将使富有成效的合作活动几乎无法开展。 Without language, Dunbar speculates, humans would have to expend nearly half their time on social grooming, which would have made productive, cooperative effort nearly impossible. 语言可能使社会得以保持凝聚力，同时减少了对身体接触和社交亲密关系的需求。 Language may have allowed societies to remain cohesive, while reducing the need for physical and social intimacy. 社会脑假说的数学表述支持这一结果，该表述显示，如果没有只有语言才能实现的那种复杂交流，脑容量的增加就不太可能促成大型群体。 This result is supported by the mathematical formulation of the social brain hypothesis, that showed that it is unlikely that increased brain size would have led to large groups without the kind of complex communication that only language allows.
应用Applications
邓巴数（Dunbar's number）已引起人类学、进化心理学、统计学和企业管理领域的关注。 Dunbar's number has become of interest in anthropology, evolutionary psychology, statistics, and business management. 例如，社交软件开发者对此感兴趣，因为他们需要了解其软件必须考虑的社交网络规模；在现代军队中，作战心理学家则寻求此类数据，以支持或反驳与维持或提高部队凝聚力和士气有关的政策。 For example, developers of social software are interested in it, as they need to know the size of social networks their software needs to take into account; and in the modern military, operational psychologists seek such data to support or refute policies related to maintaining or improving unit cohesion and morale. 最近一项研究表明，邓巴数适用于在线社交网络和通信网络（移动电话）。 A recent study has suggested that Dunbar's number is applicable to online social networks and communication networks (mobile phone). 在欧洲面向职业发展的在线社交网络 XING 上，拥有约 157 位联系人的用户报告了最高的求职录用成功率，这也支持了约为 150 的邓巴数。 Participants of the European career-oriented online social network XING who have about 157 contacts reported the highest level of job offer success, which also supports Dunbar's number of about 150. 澳大利亚旅行社 Flight Centre 在将公司重组为“家庭”（门店）、“村庄”（门店集群）和“部落”（由多个村庄组成、人数最多为 150 的集合体）时，应用了邓巴数。 Flight Centre, an Australian travel agency, applied Dunbar's number when reorganising the firm into "families" (stores), "villages" (clusters of stores) and "tribes" (aggregates of villages up to a maximum of 150 people).
一些文章和书籍讨论了利用邓巴数分析分布式动态恐怖主义网络、网络犯罪网络或宣扬犯罪意识形态的网络的可能应用。 There are discussions in articles and books, of the possible application of using Dunbar's number for analyzing distributed, dynamic terrorist networks, cybercrime networks, or networks preaching criminal ideology.

其他数值Alternative numbers
人类学家 H. Russell Bernard、Peter Killworth 及其同事在美国开展了多项实地研究，估计每个人的关系数量均值为 290，约为邓巴估计值的两倍。 Anthropologist H. Russell Bernard, Peter Killworth and associates have done a variety of field studies in the United States that came up with an estimated mean number of ties, 290, which is roughly double Dunbar's estimate. 由于分布呈右偏，Bernard–Killworth 得出的中位数 231 较低，但仍明显高于邓巴的估计值。 The Bernard–Killworth median of 231 is lower, because of an upward skew in the distribution, but still appreciably larger than Dunbar's estimate. Bernard–Killworth 对个人社交网络规模的最大似然估计，基于在不同人群中采用不同方法开展的多项实地研究。 The Bernard–Killworth estimate of the maximum likelihood of the size of a person's social network is based on a number of field studies using different methods in various populations. 它并非各项研究均值的平均值，而是反复得到的研究结果。 It is not an average of study averages but a repeated finding. 尽管如此，Bernard–Killworth 数并未像邓巴数那样得到广泛传播。 Nevertheless, the Bernard–Killworth number has not been popularized as widely as Dunbar's.
批评Criticism
一项使用不同系统发育比较方法、基于更新后的互补数据集对邓巴分析进行的重复研究，得出了差异极大的数值。 A replication of Dunbar's analysis on updated complementary datasets using different comparative phylogenetic methods yielded wildly different numbers. 贝叶斯系统发育方法和广义最小二乘系统发育方法得出的平均群体规模估计值分别为 69–109 和 16–42。 Bayesian and generalized least-squares phylogenetic methods generated approximations of average group sizes between 69–109 and 16–42, respectively. 然而，极宽的 95% 置信区间（分别为 4–520 和 2–336）意味着，指定任何单一数值的价值都很有限。 However, enormous 95% confidence intervals (4–520 and 2–336, respectively) implied that specifying any one number is of limited value. 研究人员得出结论，无法以这种方式推导出人类群体规模的认知上限。 The researchers drew the conclusion that a cognitive limit on human group size cannot be derived in this manner. 研究人员还批评了邓巴数背后的理论，因为其他灵长类动物的大脑处理信息的方式与人脑并不完全相同，灵长类动物的社会性主要由大脑以外的其他因素解释，例如它们吃什么以及天敌是谁，而且人类社交网络的规模存在很大差异。 The researchers also criticised the theory behind Dunbar's number because other primates' brains do not handle information exactly as human brains do, because primate sociality is primarily explained by other factors than the brain, such as what they eat and who their predators are, and because humans have a large variation in the size of their social networks. 不过，邓巴对这项研究的数据选择发表了评论，转而表示，他的数值不应像原始研究中那样根据灵长类或类人猿类的数据计算，而应根据猿类的数据计算。 Dunbar commented on the choice of data for this study, however, now stating that his number should not be calculated from data on primates or anthropoids, as in his original study, but on apes. 这意味着，他提出的认知上限将以 16 种成对生活的长臂猿、三种独居的猩猩，以及仅四种群居的大猿（黑猩猩、倭黑猩猩和两种大猩猩）为依据，而这不足以进行统计分析。 This would mean that his cognitive limit would be based on 16 pair-living gibbon species, three solitary orangutans, and only four group living great apes (chimpanzees, bonobos and two gorilla species), which would not be sufficient for statistical analyses.
Philip Lieberman 认为，约 30–50 人的游群社会受到营养供给的限制：在缺乏哪怕是初步农业的情况下，能够养活的群体规模有限，而人类较大的大脑又比猿类大脑消耗更多营养，因此，约 150 人的群体规模不可能在旧石器时代的人类中受到自然选择的青睐。 Philip Lieberman argues that since band societies of approximately 30–50 people are bounded by nutritional limitations to what group sizes can be fed without at least rudimentary agriculture, big human brains consuming more nutrients than ape brains, group sizes of approximately 150 cannot have been selected for in Paleolithic humans. 人们还发现，远小于人脑甚至哺乳动物大脑的脑也能支持社会关系，例如具有等级结构、每个个体都“知道”自身位置的社会性昆虫（如群体约有 80 个个体的纸蜂），以及通过简单反应程序模拟灵长类动物学中所谓“猿类政治”的计算机模拟虚拟自主智能体。 Brains much smaller than human or even mammalian brains are also known to be able to support social relationships, including social insects with hierarchies where each individual "knows" its place (such as the paper wasp with its societies of approximately 80 individuals) and computer-simulated virtual autonomous agents with simple reaction programming emulating what is referred to in primatology as "ape politics".
对灵长类物种的比较表明，群体规模与脑容量之间看似存在的联系，以及哪些物种不符合这种相关关系，都可以通过饮食来解释。 Comparisons of primate species show that what appears to be a link between group size and brain size, and also what species do not fit such a correlation, is explainable by diet. 许多依赖稀缺食物、食性特化的灵长类动物进化出了较小的大脑以节省营养，并且只能以小群体生活，甚至独居；它们拉低了独居或小群体生活的灵长类动物的平均脑容量。 Many primates that eat specialized diets that rely on scarce food have evolved small brains to conserve nutrients and are limited to living in small groups or even alone, and they lower average brain size for solitary or small group primates. 饮食理论成功预测到，生活在大群体中的小脑灵长类物种，正是那些以数量丰富但营养价值不高的食物为食的物种。 Small-brained species of primate that are living in large groups are successfully predicted by diet theory to be the species that eat food that is abundant but not very nutritious. 这一点，加上生活在大群体中且有机会实施复杂欺骗的小脑灵长类动物确实存在此类行为（既包括在自然环境中以丰富食物为食的物种，也包括原本独居、但在人工提供充足食物的条件下转而采用群居生活方式的物种），被援引为反对社会群体通过选择作用促进大脑增大和/或智力提高这一模型的证据。 Along with the existence of complex deception in small-brained primates in large groups with the opportunity (both abundant food eaters in their natural environments and originally solitary species that adopted social lifestyles under artificial food abundances), this is cited as evidence against the model of social groups selecting for large brains and/or intelligence.
普及传播Popularization
加拿大作家兼记者 Malcolm Gladwell 在其 2000 年出版的著作《引爆点》中讨论了邓巴数。 Canadian author and journalist Malcolm Gladwell discusses the Dunbar number in his 2000 book The Tipping Point. Gladwell 介绍了如今以 Gore-Tex 品牌闻名的 W. L. Gore and Associates 公司。 Gladwell describes the company W. L. Gore and Associates, now known for the Gore-Tex brand. 通过反复试验，该公司管理层发现，如果超过 150 名员工在同一栋楼里共同工作，就可能出现各种社会关系问题。 By trial and error, the leadership in the company discovered that if more than 150 employees were working together in one building, different social problems could occur. 该公司开始建造每栋最多容纳 150 名员工、仅配备 150 个停车位的办公楼。 The company started building company buildings with a limit of 150 employees and only 150 parking spaces. 当停车位全部占满时，公司就会再建一栋可容纳 150 名员工的办公楼。 When the parking spaces were filled, the company would build another 150-employee building. 有时，这些办公楼之间的距离很近。 Sometimes these buildings would be placed only short distances apart.
这一数值已被用于虚拟社区研究，尤其是对 Ultima Online 等大型多人在线角色扮演游戏（MMORPGs），以及 Facebook（邓巴本人于 2010 年对 Facebook 进行过一项研究）和 MySpace 等社交网站的研究。 The number has been used in the study of virtual communities, especially MMORPGs, such as Ultima Online, and social networking websites, such as Facebook (Dunbar himself did a study on Facebook in 2010) and MySpace.
瑞典税务局参考邓巴的研究，计划于 2007 年重组其职能，将每个办事处的员工人数限制在最多 150 人。 The Swedish Tax Agency planned to reorganise its functions in 2007 with a maximum 150 employees per office, referring to Dunbar's research.
2007 年，Cracked.com 编辑 David Wong 撰写了一篇题为《什么是猴圈？》的幽默文章，解释邓巴数及其含义。 In 2007, Cracked.com editor David Wong wrote a humour piece titled "What is the Monkeysphere?" explaining Dunbar's number and its implications. 在他 2012 年出版的小说《这本书里全是蜘蛛》中，人物 Marconi 向 David 解释了邓巴数对人类社会的影响。 In his 2012 novel This Book Is Full of Spiders, the character Marconi explains to David the effect Dunbar's number has on human society. 按照 Marconi 的解释，邓巴数对个体施加的限制，可以解释种族主义和仇外心理等现象，以及人们对自身所属社群之外的人所遭受苦难的漠视。 In Marconi's explanation, the limit Dunbar's number imposes on the individual explains phenomena such as racism and xenophobia, as well as apathy towards the suffering of peoples outside of an individual's community.
在发表于《金融时报》（2018 年 8 月 10 日）、题为《为何饮酒是人类成功的秘诀》的文章中，邓巴还提到了另外两个数字：一个由约 5 人组成的核心圈，我们将约 40% 的可用社交时间投入其中；此外还有 10 人，我们将另外 20% 的时间投入到他们身上。 In a piece for the Financial Times (10 August 2018) titled "Why drink is the secret to humanity's success", Dunbar mentioned two more numbers: an inner core of about 5 people to whom we devote about 40 percent of our available social time and 10 more people to whom we devote another 20 percent. 总体而言，我们将约三分之二的时间仅投入到 15 个人身上。 All in all, we devote about two-thirds of our time to just 15 people.
在播客 Hello Internet 的第 103 期（2018 年 5 月 31 日）中，Brady Haran 和 CGP Grey 讨论了这一人数可能被限制在 150 的原因，包括人们掌握大群体内部政治关系的能力，以及人们能够投入到建立和维持友谊上的时间。 In episode 103 of the podcast Hello Internet (31 May 2018) Brady Haran and CGP Grey discuss the reasons the number may be limited to 150, including the ability to keep track of political relationships in large groups of people and the amount of time that people have to devote towards developing and maintaining friendships.
