Abstract摘要
Multi-agent systems (MAS) are increasingly used for open-ended idea generation, driven by the expectation that collective interaction will broaden the exploration diversity. However, when and why such collaboration truly expands the solution space remains unclear. We present a systematic empirical study of diversity in MAS-based ideation across three bottom-up levels: model intelligence, agent cognition, and system dynamics. At the model level, we identify a compute efficiency paradox, where stronger, highly aligned models yield diminishing marginal diversity despite higher per-sample quality. At the cognition level, authority-driven dynamics suppress semantic diversity compared to junior-dominated groups. At the system level, group-size scaling yields diminishing returns and dense communication topologies accelerate premature convergence. We characterize these outcomes as collective failures emerging from structural coupling, a process where interaction inadvertently contracts agent exploration and triggers diversity collapse. Our analysis shows that this collapse arises primarily from the interaction structure rather than inherent model insufficiency, highlighting the importance of preserving independence and disagreement when designing MAS for creative tasks. Our code is available at https://github.com/Xtra-Computing/MAS_Diversity.
多智能体系统（MAS）正日益广泛地用于开放式创意生成，这源于人们预期集体交互能够拓展探索的多样性。然而，这种协作何时以及为何能够真正拓展解空间，仍不明确。我们从模型智能、智能体认知和系统动力学这三个自下而上的层次，对基于 MAS 的创意构思中的多样性开展了系统性实证研究。在模型层面，我们发现了计算效率悖论：能力更强、对齐程度更高的模型尽管单个样本的质量更高，但带来的边际多样性却递减。在认知层面，与初级成员主导的群体相比，权威驱动的动态过程会抑制语义多样性。在系统层面，扩大群体规模带来的收益递减，而稠密的通信拓扑会加速过早收敛。我们将这些结果刻画为结构性耦合所引发的集体失效；结构性耦合是指交互无意中收缩智能体的探索范围并触发多样性坍缩的过程。我们的分析表明，这种坍缩主要源于交互结构，而非模型固有的能力不足，这凸显了在为创造性任务设计 MAS 时保留独立性与分歧的重要性。我们的代码可在 https://github.com/Xtra-Computing/MAS_Diversity 获取。

Large language models (LLMs) have evolved from static text generators to dynamic engines for open-ended idea generation, supporting tasks ranging from scientific hypothesis formulation Zhou et al. (2024); Alkan et al. (2025) to strategic planning Cao et al. (2025) and creative design Hong et al. (2024); Gottweis et al. (2025). In these exploratory domains, the utility of a system is not defined by its ability to converge on a single "ground truth," but rather by its capacity to explore a diverse space of plausible ideas that reflect alternative assumptions and solution paths Boden (2004); Liang et al. (2024); Moon et al. (2025). Diversity, is not merely a qualitative preference; it is a functional requirement for effective decision-making. A lack of diversity risks trapping users in a narrow region of the solution space, inflating confidence in suboptimal solutions while suppressing unconventional but high-potential hypotheses Wright et al. (2025).
大语言模型（LLMs）已从静态文本生成器发展为开放式创意生成的动态引擎，支持的任务涵盖科学假设提出 Zhou et al. (2024); Alkan et al. (2025)、战略规划 Cao et al. (2025) 以及创意设计 Hong et al. (2024); Gottweis et al. (2025)。在这些探索性领域中，系统的效用并不取决于其收敛到单一“真实答案”的能力，而取决于其探索多样化合理创意空间的能力，这些创意体现了不同的假设和求解路径 Boden (2004); Liang et al. (2024); Moon et al. (2025)。多样性不仅是一种定性偏好，更是有效决策的一项功能性要求。缺乏多样性可能使用户困于解空间中的狭窄区域，夸大其对次优解的信心，同时压制非传统但潜力巨大的假设 Wright et al. (2025)。
To transcend the limitations of single-model generation, recent research has increasingly pivoted toward Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) Du et al. (2024); Ye et al. (2025). The prevailing intuition is that, by enabling multiple agents to interact while adopting distinct roles or perspectives, MAS can achieve broader coverage of the idea space than a solitary model Su et al. (2025). However, this assumption remains largely unexamined. In practice, MAS frameworks are often built on homogeneous underlying models that share the same pre-training distributions and alignment objectives Jiang et al. (2025); Wenger and Kenett (2025). Consequently, multi-agent interaction can end up amplifying shared priors rather than introducing genuine variety, causing the system to repeatedly search the same narrow manifold at a higher computational cost Wynn et al. (2025). It remains unclear when, why, and under what structural conditions such collaboration actually expands the semantic solution space, rather than reaching a premature consensus that we characterize as diversity collapse.
为突破单模型生成的局限，近期研究日益转向多智能体系统（MAS）Du et al. (2024); Ye et al. (2025)。普遍的直觉是，通过让多个智能体以不同角色或视角进行交互，MAS 能够比单个模型更广泛地覆盖创意空间 Su et al. (2025)。然而，这一假设在很大程度上仍未经检验。在实践中，MAS 框架通常建立在同质的底层模型之上，这些模型具有相同的预训练分布和对齐目标 Jiang et al. (2025); Wenger and Kenett (2025)。因此，多智能体交互最终可能放大共同的先验，而非引入真正的多样性，导致系统以更高的计算成本反复搜索同一个狭窄流形 Wynn et al. (2025)。这种协作何时、为何以及在何种结构条件下能够真正拓展语义解空间，而非达成我们称之为多样性坍缩的过早共识，仍不明确。
To investigate this, we conduct a systematic empirical analysis evaluating over 10,000 research proposals spanning 20 topics. By using these proposals as a proxy for diversity in MAS-based idea generation, we dissect the trade-offs within mechanisms of collective interaction across three hierarchical levels:
为研究这一问题，我们开展了系统性实证分析，评估了涵盖 20 个主题的 10,000 多份研究提案。我们将这些提案作为基于 MAS 的创意生成中多样性的代理指标，从三个层级剖析集体交互机制中的权衡：
First, at the level of Model Intelligence (Section 3), we identify the Compute Efficiency Paradox: as foundation models scale in capability, their outputs often become more fluent and score better on correctness-oriented metrics, yet converge toward increasingly similar semantic content. From an information-theoretic perspective Coveney and Succi (2025), this points to a decoupling in which greater intelligence does not necessarily translate into a more informative expansion of the idea space, producing little marginal information gain.
首先，在模型智能层面（第 3 节），我们发现了计算效率悖论：随着基础模型能力的提升，其输出往往变得更加流畅，在以正确性为导向的指标上得分更高，但语义内容却趋于愈发相似。从信息论视角来看 Coveney and Succi (2025)，这表明存在一种脱钩现象：更高的智能未必能转化为包含更多信息的创意空间拓展，因此产生的边际信息增益甚微。
Building on this foundation, we examine Agent Cognition (Section 4), finding that interaction often triggers a false consensus trap. Although agents are prompted with distinct personas or roles to elicit diverse viewpoints, they remain grounded in shared inductive biases. Our results reveal that authority-driven dynamics further suppress semantic diversity compared to junior-dominated horizontal groups. In these settings, interaction devolves into an Echo-Chamber Effect" Wang et al. (2025a); Liu et al. (2024); Wynn et al. (2025), where agents prioritize agreement over independent critique.
在此基础上，我们考察了智能体认知（第 4 节），发现交互往往会触发虚假共识陷阱。尽管通过提示为智能体设定了不同的人设或角色，以引出多样化的观点，但它们仍以共同的归纳偏置为基础。我们的结果表明，与初级成员主导的横向群体相比，权威驱动的动态过程会进一步抑制语义多样性。在这些情境中，交互退化为“回音室效应（Echo-Chamber Effect）” Wang et al. (2025a); Liu et al. (2024); Wynn et al. (2025)，智能体优先追求一致，而非独立批判。
Further, we analyze System Dynamics (Section 5), where increased group size or dense communication topologies exacerbate Premature Convergence. If we view idea generation as search over a high-dimensional landscape, parallel interactions are expected to explore a broader region. However, by tracing evolutionary trajectories, protocols that implicitly reward fast agreement often push the group to collapse early onto local optima, much like the Ringelmann Effect Ringelmann (1913) observed on humans. Under this condition, additional system complexity Moon et al. (2025); Shen et al. (2025) tends to generate redundant trajectories rather than truly divergent exploration.
此外，我们分析了系统动力学（第 5 节），其中群体规模的增大或通信拓扑的稠密化会加剧过早收敛。如果将创意生成视为在高维景观中的搜索，那么并行交互理应能够探索更广阔的区域。然而，通过追踪演化轨迹可以发现，隐式奖励快速达成一致的协议往往会促使群体过早收敛于局部最优解，这与 Ringelmann (1913) 在人类身上观察到的林格尔曼效应（Ringelmann Effect）十分相似。在这种条件下，额外增加的系统复杂性 Moon et al. (2025); Shen et al. (2025) 往往会产生冗余轨迹，而非真正具有发散性的探索。
Finally, we synthesize these takeaways in our Discussion (Section 6), showing that these outcomes represent collective failures emerging from structural coupling. Our analysis reveals that diversity collapse arises primarily from the interaction structure (how agents are connected and how they influence one another), rather than any inherent model insufficiency. The more we force agents to coordinate, the more their individual trajectories become synchronized, effectively "locking" the group into a single path. Crucially, we show that this effect is most pronounced in complex tasks that demand both rigid logical rigor and open-ended imagination; in such cases, the pressure to be "correct" and "collaborative" inadvertently forces the system to prematurely abandon novel but unverified ideas.
最后，我们在讨论（第 6 节）中综合这些发现，表明这些结果体现了由结构耦合引发的集体失效。我们的分析表明，多样性坍缩主要源于交互结构（智能体如何连接以及如何相互影响），而非模型本身的任何固有不足。我们越是强制智能体进行协调，它们各自的轨迹就越趋于同步，实际上将整个群体“锁定”在单一路径上。关键在于，我们表明，这种效应在同时要求严密逻辑和开放式想象力的复杂任务中最为显著；在这些情况下，追求“正确”和“协作”的压力会无意间迫使系统过早放弃新颖但尚未验证的想法。
In summary, achieving effective and diverse ideation in MAS requires more than simply assembling a larger or more connected group. The orchestration of interaction structures, carefully balancing collaboration with independence, is essential for unlocking the full creative potential of multi-agent systems in open-ended domains.
总之，要在 MAS 中实现有效且多样的创意生成，仅仅组建规模更大或连接更紧密的群体是不够的。编排交互结构，审慎平衡协作与独立性，对于充分释放多智能体系统在开放式领域中的创造潜力至关重要。
2 Methodology2 方法
Unlike deep research and other goal-directed agentic tasks Zhang et al. (2025b); Zhang et al. (2025a), which optimize planning, retrieval, and synthesis toward an evidence-grounded objective, ideation is inherently open-ended: it requires navigating a complex, high-dimensional search space to uncover distinct, plausible solutions Boden (2009); Chen et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2025c). In this section, we formalize the task into scientific proposal generation, discuss the pitfalls of agent collaboration, and introduce the means for quantifying diversity.
深度研究和其他目标导向的智能体任务 Zhang et al. (2025b); Zhang et al. (2025a) 围绕有证据支撑的目标优化规划、检索与综合，而创意构思与之不同，本质上是开放式的：它要求在复杂的高维搜索空间中探索，以发现彼此不同且合理可行的解决方案 Boden (2009); Chen et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2025c)。在本节中，我们将该任务形式化为科研提案生成，讨论智能体协作的潜在问题，并介绍量化多样性的方法。
2.1 Task Formulation: Research Proposals as Units of Ideation2.1 任务形式化：以研究提案作为创意构思的单元
To rigorously evaluate diversity, we require a unit of analysis that is both structured and open-ended. We adopt the generation of scientific research proposals as our unit of analysis. Unlike generic open-ended generation Jiang et al. (2025), a research proposal is a semi-structured artifact that demands both divergence and internal convergence.
为了严谨地评估多样性，我们需要一种兼具结构性与开放性的分析单元。我们采用生成的科学研究提案作为分析单元。与一般的开放式生成 Jiang et al. (2025) 不同，研究提案是一种半结构化产物，既要求发散，也要求内部收敛。
Formally, given a research domain context $\mathcal{C}$ , the system aims to generate a set of proposals $X=\{x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\}$ . Each proposal $x_{i}$ is not an independent sample, but the emergent outcome of a collaborative history $H$ among a group of agents. We detail the formal schema of valid proposals (e.g., Title, Hypothesis, Method) in Appendix A.
形式化地，给定研究领域背景 $\mathcal{C}$，系统旨在生成一组提案 $X=\{x_{1},\dots,x_{n}\}$。每个提案 $x_{i}$ 都不是独立样本，而是一组智能体之间的协作历史 $H$ 所产生的涌现结果。我们在附录 A 中详细说明了有效提案的形式化模式（例如，标题、假设、方法）。
2.2 The Multi-Agent Ideation Pipeline2.2 多智能体创意构思流程
To systematically analyze diversity, we construct a generic multi-agent interaction framework consisting of three phases (illustrated in Figure 1).
为了系统地分析多样性，我们构建了一个由三个阶段组成的通用多智能体交互框架（如图 1 所示）。
Role Instantiation. The system initializes a set of agents $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},\dots,a_{k}\}$ . To simulate diverse cognitive sources, agents are assigned distinct "personas" or expert roles (e.g., "The Skeptic," "The Interdisciplinarian") via system prompts. This heterogeneity is designed to mimic a scientific committee.
角色实例化。系统初始化一组智能体 $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},\dots,a_{k}\}$。为了模拟多样化的认知来源，系统通过系统提示词为智能体分配不同的“人格设定”或专家角色（例如，“怀疑者”“跨学科专家”）。这种异质性旨在模拟科学委员会。
Iterative Deliberation. Agents engage in a multi-turn dialogue governed by a specific topology (e.g., Round-robin Debate). In each turn $t$ , an agent observes the context $\mathcal{C}$ and the discussion history $H_{t-1}$ to formulate a contribution. This phase allows for the collision of perspectives, critique of premises, and refinement of concepts.
迭代研讨。智能体按照特定拓扑（例如，轮流辩论）开展多轮对话。在每一轮 $t$ 中，智能体根据背景 $\mathcal{C}$ 和讨论历史 $H_{t-1}$ 形成自己的发言内容。这一阶段使不同观点得以碰撞、前提得以受到批判、概念得以完善。
Proposal Synthesis. Upon reaching the interaction horizon $\mathcal{T}$ , a designated "Editor" agent (or the collective group) synthesizes the discussion history into a finalized, structured research proposal $x_{i}$ . This step forces the convergence of unstructured debate into a concrete scientific artifact. For each experimental setting, we conduct 50 independent discussion sessions per topic across the 20 topics listed in Table 26, using temperature 0.7, giving 1,000 proposals per setting.
提案综合。当达到交互时域 $\mathcal{T}$ 时，指定的“编辑”智能体（或整个群体）将讨论历史综合为最终的结构化研究提案 $x_{i}$。这一步强制将非结构化辩论收敛为具体的科研产物。对于每种实验设置，我们针对表 26 所列的 20 个主题，使用温度 0.7，为每个主题开展 50 场独立讨论，从而在每种设置下生成 1,000 份提案。
Specific experimental setups, including agent prompts and topologies, are detailed in Appendix N.2; the full prompt templates for every collaboration mode appear in Appendix O.
具体实验设置，包括智能体提示词和拓扑结构，详见附录 N.2；各协作模式的完整提示词模板见附录 O。

Evaluating diversity in collaborative systems requires distinguishing between true conceptual variety and trivial surface-level variation. We apply metrics covering four complementary dimensions for the analysis. Mathematical definitions are provided in Appendix C and sensitivity analysis in Appendix F.
评估协作系统的多样性，需要区分真正的概念多样性与微不足道的表层变化。我们采用涵盖四个互补维度的指标进行分析。数学定义见附录 C，敏感性分析见附录 F。
Effective Diversity (Vendi Score Friedman and Dieng (2023)): Measures the effective number of unique semantic modes in the set $X$ based on the spectral entropy of the kernel matrix. Unlike simple counting, it is robust to cluster imbalances, indicating whether the system is exploring the semantic space efficiently.
有效多样性（Effective Diversity，Vendi Score Friedman and Dieng (2023)）：基于核矩阵的谱熵，衡量集合 $X$ 中不同语义模态的有效数量。与简单计数不同，该指标对簇大小不均衡具有稳健性，能够表明系统是否在高效探索语义空间。
Structural Disorder: Adapted from the order parameter $\phi$ Landau and others (1937); Vicsek et al. (1995) as the average cosine similarity between individual proposals and the group’s mean embedding, this metric diagnoses the group’s dynamic state. Low values of $1-\phi$ indicate collapse toward a single centroid (Echo Chamber Wang et al. (2025a) state), while high values indicate that the system maintains pluralistic perspectives despite interaction.
结构无序度（Structural Disorder）：改编自序参量 $\phi$ Landau and others (1937); Vicsek et al. (1995)，定义为各个提案与群体平均嵌入之间的平均余弦相似度，用于诊断群体的动态状态。$1-\phi$ 的值较低，表明系统向单一质心坍缩（回音室状态，Echo Chamber Wang et al. (2025a)）；其值较高，则表明系统在交互过程中仍保持多元视角。
Semantic Dispersion (PCD): Computes the average pairwise cosine distance between proposals. While Vendi Score counts the modes, Dispersion measures the magnitude of the spread.
语义离散度（Semantic Dispersion，PCD）：计算提案之间的平均成对余弦距离。Vendi Score 统计模态数量，而离散度衡量分布的分散程度。
Lexical Uniqueness: Utilizes IDF-weighted n-gram statistics to measure surface-level redundancy. This serves as a sanity check: high semantic diversity scores should not be driven merely by verbose rephrasing of identical ideas.
词汇独特性（Lexical Uniqueness）：利用 IDF 加权的 n-gram 统计量衡量表层冗余。这是一项合理性检查：较高的语义多样性得分不应仅仅来自对相同想法的冗长改述。
We validated these metrics via human evaluation (see Appendix B) using pairwise comparisons by five expert annotators: the Vendi Score matched expert diversity judgments in 87% of cases, with all three embedding-based metrics exceeding 80% agreement (Table 1).
我们通过人工评估验证了这些指标（见附录 B），由五位专家标注员进行成对比较：Vendi Score 在 87% 的案例中与专家对多样性的判断一致，三个基于嵌入的指标的一致率均超过 80%（表 1）。

Model-level quality is no longer the limiting factor for idea generation. Across the full diversity–quality frontier, including high-diversity regimes, models maintain consistently strong average quality, and qualitative inspection confirms semantic coherence. 模型层面的质量已不再是创意生成的限制因素。在整个多样性–质量前沿上，包括高多样性区间，模型始终保持较高的平均质量，定性检查也证实了其语义连贯性。这些发现共同表明，多智能体系统的核心挑战并非生成多样性或在多样性与质量之间进行权衡，而是保留、组织和协调单模型生成中已经存在的潜在多样性。

7.1 Social Psychology of Group Ideation7.1 群体创意生成的社会心理学
The study of group failures in ideation has a rich history in social psychology. The brainstorming hypothesis (Osborn, 1963): groups generate more ideas than individuals, was famously refuted by subsequent research showing the opposite (Mullen et al., 1991). Janis (1972) introduced the concept of groupthink to explain how cohesive groups suppress dissent. The Ringelmann effect (Ringelmann, 1913), later reinterpreted as social loafing (Latané et al., 1979), demonstrated that per-capita contribution declines with group size. Diehl and Stroebe (1987) identified production blocking, the inability to generate ideas while listening to others, as a primary cause of brainstorming loss. Nominal Group Technique (NGT) (Delbecq et al., 1986) was developed as a structural intervention to counter these failures by enforcing independent generation before group discussion. Status Characteristics Theory (Berger et al., 1977) predicts that high-status individuals dominate group output regardless of actual competence. The same pattern appears in human opinion dynamics, where social influence undermines the “wisdom of crowds” (Surowiecki, 2005) by coupling independent judgments (Lorenz et al., 2011), and diversity in problem-solving groups outperforms individual ability only when independence is preserved (Hong and Page, 2004). Our work tests whether these phenomena survive in agents that lack explicit psychological substrate, and whether structural coupling alone is sufficient to reproduce them.
社会心理学对群体创意生成失败的研究有着悠久而丰富的历史。头脑风暴假说（Osborn, 1963）认为，群体比个体能产生更多想法；后续研究却得出了相反的结果，从而对这一假说作出了著名的反驳（Mullen et al., 1991）。Janis (1972) 提出了群体思维（groupthink）的概念，用以解释凝聚力强的群体如何压制异议。林格尔曼效应（Ringelmann, 1913）表明，人均贡献会随着群体规模增大而下降，这一效应后来被重新解释为社会惰化（social loafing）（Latané et al., 1979）。Diehl and Stroebe (1987) 指出，产出阻滞（production blocking），即在倾听他人时无法生成想法，是头脑风暴效能损失的主要原因。名义群体技术（Nominal Group Technique，NGT）（Delbecq et al., 1986）作为一种结构性干预措施被提出，通过要求成员在群体讨论前独立生成想法来应对这些失败。地位特征理论（Status Characteristics Theory）（Berger et al., 1977）预测，无论实际能力如何，地位较高的个体都会主导群体产出。同样的模式也出现在人类意见动态中：社会影响通过耦合原本独立的判断（Lorenz et al., 2011），削弱了“群体智慧”（Surowiecki, 2005）；而在问题解决群体中，只有保持独立性，多样性的作用才会超过个体能力（Hong and Page, 2004）。我们的工作检验了这些现象是否仍会出现在缺乏明确心理基础的智能体中，以及仅靠结构耦合是否足以重现这些现象。
7.2 Multi-Agent Systems and Collective Intelligence7.2 多智能体系统与集体智能
Beyond simple model ensembles (Ye et al., 2025), recent frameworks leverage heterogeneity through social-attribute modulation (Zhang et al., 2026) or diverse thinking prompts (He and Feng, 2025). Multi-agent debate has been employed to enhance reasoning (Du et al., 2024), and heterogeneous teaming to boost scientific ideation (Su et al., 2025; Shi et al., 2025). However, interaction dynamics introduce structural vulnerabilities: Wynn et al. (2025) identify that debate frequently suffers from sycophancy and “disagreement collapse,” and empirical studies reveal a homogenizing effect where AI collaboration reduces collective diversity (Moon et al., 2025). An “Artificial Hivemind” phenomenon has been documented where LLMs converge on identical semantic distributions regardless of prompting strategies (Jiang et al., 2025; Wenger and Kenett, 2025). Related efforts use LLM agents as proxies for human social behavior (YANG et al., 2025; Anthis et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b); our study complements this line by providing a structural explanation for the observed collapse, showing that the effect is driven by interaction topology rather than persona fidelity.
除简单的模型集成（Ye et al., 2025）外，近期框架还通过社会属性调节（Zhang et al., 2026）或多样化思维提示（He and Feng, 2025）来利用异质性。多智能体辩论已被用于增强推理能力（Du et al., 2024），异质团队组建则被用于促进科学创意生成（Su et al., 2025; Shi et al., 2025）。然而，交互动态也引入了结构性弱点：Wynn et al. (2025) 指出，辩论经常受到谄媚迎合与“分歧坍缩”的困扰，而实证研究揭示了一种同质化效应，即 AI 协作会降低集体多样性（Moon et al., 2025）。已有研究记录了一种“人工蜂群思维”（Artificial Hivemind）现象，即无论采用何种提示策略，LLM 都会收敛到相同的语义分布（Jiang et al., 2025; Wenger and Kenett, 2025）。相关研究使用 LLM 智能体作为人类社会行为的替代模型（YANG et al., 2025; Anthis et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025b）；我们的研究通过为所观察到的坍缩提供结构性解释，补充了这一研究方向，表明该效应由交互拓扑而非人格设定的保真度所驱动。

8 Conclusion8 结论
We systematically evaluated diversity in multi-agent systems for open-ended idea generation, using scientific proposal tasks as a testbed. Simply increasing agent count does not guarantee greater idea diversity. Rather, diversity collapse arises from structural coupling across three levels: alignment at the model level, hierarchical or role-differentiated coordination at the cognition level, and dense communication at the system level. These factors jointly promote premature consensus. Interaction designs that preserve independence, such as the blind-writing phase of NGT and subgroup isolation, consistently yield higher diversity with only modest differences in judged quality.
我们以科学研究方案任务为测试平台，系统评估了多智能体系统在开放式创意生成中的多样性。仅仅增加智能体数量并不能保证创意多样性提高。相反，多样性坍缩源于三个层面的结构耦合：模型层面的对齐、认知层面的层级式或角色差异化协调，以及系统层面的密集通信。这些因素共同促使共识过早形成。保持独立性的交互设计，例如 NGT 的盲写阶段和子群隔离，始终能够带来更高的多样性，而评定质量仅存在小幅差异。
Limitations局限性
This work focuses on evaluating diversity in multi-agent idea generation under a controlled experimental setting, and several limitations follow from this scope.
本研究聚焦于在受控实验环境下评估多智能体创意生成的多样性，这一研究范围带来了若干局限。
First, our analysis is centered on scientific proposal generation as a representative ideation task. While this domain offers a structured yet open-ended testbed with high intrinsic entropy, the observed dynamics may not directly transfer to tasks with stronger ground-truth constraints (e.g., mathematical problem solving) or to unconstrained creative writing. We view our setting as a stress test for diversity under hybrid constraints rather than a universal proxy for all generative tasks.
首先，我们的分析以科学研究方案生成这一具有代表性的构思任务为中心。尽管该领域提供了一个兼具结构性与开放性、且具有较高内在熵的测试平台，但观察到的动态规律可能无法直接迁移到受真实答案更强约束的任务（例如数学问题求解），或不受约束的创意写作中。我们将这一设置视为对混合约束下多样性的压力测试，而非所有生成任务的通用替代指标。
Second, our primary analyses use DeepSeek-V3 as the backbone to isolate the effects of interaction and structure. Cross-model replication on GPT-5.1 and o1-mini (Figure 11; Appendix M) and genuinely heterogeneous-model ensembles that mix DeepSeek-V3, GPT-4o, and Claude-Sonnet-4 (Appendix J) confirm that the structural findings generalise across backbones, but a broader sweep over additional architectures and pretraining families remains future work.
其次，我们的主要分析采用 DeepSeek-V3 作为骨干模型，以分离交互与结构的影响。在 GPT-5.1 和 o1-mini 上开展的跨模型复现实验（图 11；附录 M），以及混合 DeepSeek-V3、GPT-4o 和 Claude-Sonnet-4 的真正异构模型集成（附录 J），均证实了有关结构的发现可推广至不同骨干模型，但对更多架构和预训练模型家族进行更广泛的考察仍有待未来研究。
Third, our diversity evaluation relies on embedding-based and lexical metrics, supplemented by human validation on a limited scale. Although agreement with expert judgments is high, no single metric can fully capture the nuanced notion of creativity or novelty in ideation. Quality scores are obtained via an LLM-as-Judge protocol (DeepSeek-V3, temperature 0) and therefore inherit the standard biases of automatic judges; our human evaluation (Appendix B) validates the diversity axis only. Our metrics are intended to diagnose relative differences between collaboration modes rather than to provide absolute measures of creativity.
第三，我们的多样性评估依赖于基于嵌入的指标和词汇指标，并辅以有限规模的人工验证。尽管这些指标与专家判断高度一致，但没有任何单一指标能够充分捕捉构思过程中创造力或新颖性这一内涵细致的概念。质量分数通过大语言模型评判（LLM-as-Judge）协议（DeepSeek-V3，温度为 0）获得，因此也继承了自动评判器的常见偏差；我们的人工评估（附录 B）仅验证了多样性维度。我们的指标旨在诊断不同协作模式之间的相对差异，而非提供创造力的绝对度量。
Finally, we analyze interaction protocols with a fixed number of rounds and a default sampling temperature. Although we sweep group size from $N=3$ to $N=7$ (Section 5) and verify robustness to temperature across $T\in\{0.3,0.7,1.0\}$ (Appendix L), adaptive or dynamically optimized interaction strategies may exhibit different behaviors that are not captured in this study.
最后，我们分析的交互协议采用固定轮数和默认采样温度。尽管我们考察了从 $N=3$ 到 $N=7$ 的群体规模（第 5 节），并在 $T\in\{0.3,0.7,1.0\}$ 范围内验证了结果对温度变化的稳健性（附录 L），但自适应或动态优化的交互策略可能表现出本研究未能涵盖的不同行为。
Ethical Statement and Potential risks伦理声明与潜在风险
This paper studies the structural properties of multi-agent language model systems for idea generation, focusing on diversity rather than task correctness or decision-making authority. As such, the work does not introduce new model capabilities, training data, or deployment mechanisms.
本文研究用于创意生成的多智能体语言模型系统的结构属性，关注多样性，而非任务正确性或决策权。因此，本研究未引入新的模型能力、训练数据或部署机制。
A potential risk of multi-agent ideation systems is that increased fluency or consensus may create a false sense of confidence in generated ideas, particularly in high-stakes or expert domains. Our findings explicitly highlight this risk by identifying premature convergence and false consensus as failure modes, and thus aim to inform safer system design rather than to promote uncritical adoption.
多智能体构思系统的一项潜在风险是，更高的表达流畅度或更强的共识可能使人们对生成的创意产生虚假的信心，尤其是在高风险领域或专业领域中。我们的研究结果通过将过早收敛和虚假共识识别为失效模式，明确凸显了这一风险，因而旨在为更安全的系统设计提供依据，而非推动不加审视的采用。

本节介绍随机分组的子组协作流程的纯文本版本。在此设置中，每个智能体生成可见的自然语言发言，各次调用之间不传递任何潜在状态。随后，一名指定的领导智能体阅读部分讨论内容，并综合形成最终答案。
High-level overview总体概述
Given a question or topic, a fixed set of agents participate in a multi-round discussion. In each round, the full set of agents is randomly partitioned into disjoint subgroups of a specified size. Within every subgroup, agents speak in sequence, with each utterance visible only to members of the same subgroup. After a pre-defined number of rounds, a leader agent reads a transcript of the most recent subgroup discussions together with a short summary of the corresponding round structure, and produces the final response.
给定一个问题或主题，一组固定的智能体参与多轮讨论。在每一轮中，全部智能体被随机划分为互不重叠、大小指定的子组。在每个子组内，智能体依次发言，每条发言仅对同一子组的成员可见。经过预先设定的轮数后，领导智能体阅读最近一轮子组讨论的文字记录，以及对相应轮次结构的简短概述，并生成最终回复。
Agent-side text generation智能体端的文本生成
All agents, including the leader, are implemented by the same underlying language model with shared decoding hyperparameters (e.g., sampling temperature, nucleus sampling threshold, and maximum number of generated tokens per turn). For each non-leader agent, the model is queried with a prompt that includes:
所有智能体（包括领导智能体）均由同一个底层语言模型实现，并共享解码超参数（例如采样温度、核采样阈值以及每轮生成的最大词元数）。对于每个非领导智能体，我们使用包含以下内容的提示词调用模型：
a natural language description of the overall task or topic;
对整体任务或主题的自然语言描述；
a description of the current discussion phase (e.g., brainstorming, critique, synthesis), indexed by round;
按轮次索引的当前讨论阶段描述（例如头脑风暴、批评、综合）；
a short description of the agent’s role (e.g., “optimistic critic”, “domain expert”);
对该智能体角色的简短描述（例如“乐观的批评者”“领域专家”）；
a personalized memory consisting of all previous utterances that this agent is allowed to see (defined below);
由该智能体获准查看的所有先前发言构成的个性化记忆（定义见下文）；
the sequence of speakers that have already contributed in the current subgroup and the round-specific instructions for how to respond to them.
当前子组中已经发言的发言者序列，以及针对本轮如何回应这些发言者的指令。
The language model then generates a single textual utterance for that agent, up to a preset maximum number of tokens. No latent representations or cached internal states are shared across calls: each utterance is produced from scratch, conditioned only on the textual prompt.
随后，语言模型为该智能体生成一条文本发言，长度不超过预设的最大词元数。各次调用之间不共享潜在表示或缓存的内部状态：每条发言均从头生成，仅以文本提示词为条件。

a global list of utterance records, where each record stores the agent identity, the round index or name, and the generated text;
一个全局发言记录列表，其中每条记录存储智能体身份、轮次索引或名称，以及生成的文本；

为每个智能体维护一个有序列表，包含该智能体可见的所有发言，构成其个性化讨论记忆；

Whenever an agent in a subgroup produces an utterance, a corresponding record is appended to the global list. The same record is then appended to the personalized memory of every member of that subgroup. As a result, all members of a subgroup share the same local view of the subgroup-level discussion, but agents in different subgroups do not see each other’s utterances from that round.
每当子组中的某个智能体生成一条发言时，就会向全局列表追加一条相应的记录。随后，同一条记录也会追加到该子组每个成员的个性化记忆中。因此，同一子组的所有成员对该子组内的讨论具有相同的局部视图，但不同子组中的智能体无法看到彼此在该轮中的发言。

The resulting text is stored as a new utterance record (agent identity, round label, text content) and added to the personalized memory of all agents in the current subgroup. Thus, within a round, only subgroup members see each other’s contributions.

最后一轮子组交互结束后，系统为领导智能体准备输入。为在保留最相关内容的同时控制上下文长度，领导智能体不会读取完整的讨论历史。相反，仅考虑最近几轮（例如，最后两轮）：

将属于这些近期轮次的所有发言记录拼接为一份供领导智能体阅读的文字实录。实录中的每个条目都包含轮次标签、智能体身份及相应文本，并采用简单的格式（如标题和空行）以保持可读性。

与此同时，对讨论期间创建的轮次级日志进行筛选，仅保留所选近期轮次的日志。由此得到一份简明摘要，说明在讨论的近期部分，哪些智能体在哪些子组中进行了交互。

Appendix F Sensitivity Analysis附录 F 敏感性分析
This appendix examines the robustness of our conclusions to reasonable variations in metric design choices. Rather than emphasizing absolute metric magnitudes, we focus on whether the relative ordering across collaboration modes remains stable under such variations. All analyses reported here are conducted on the same set of proposals as in the main paper.
本附录考察当指标设计选择发生合理变化时，我们的结论是否仍然稳健。我们关注的是不同协作模式之间的相对排序在这些变化下是否保持稳定，而非强调指标的绝对数值。此处报告的所有分析均基于与正文相同的一组提案进行。
F.1 OverviewF.1 概述
We consider four orthogonal sources of potential sensitivity: (i) the choice of semantic embedding model, (ii) the choice of structural diversity metric, (iii) the definition of lexical uniqueness, including $n$ -gram order, and (iv) content-only versus raw lexical tokenization. Across all settings, we observe that qualitative trends and relative comparisons across collaboration modes remain invariant.
我们考虑四个相互正交的潜在敏感性来源：(i) 语义嵌入模型的选择，(ii) 结构多样性指标的选择，(iii) 词汇独特性的定义，包括 $n$ 元语法的阶数，以及 (iv) 仅基于内容的词汇分词与原始词汇分词。在所有设置下，我们均观察到，定性趋势以及不同协作模式之间的相对比较结果保持不变。
F.2 Embedding Model RobustnessF.2 嵌入模型的稳健性
All embedding-based metrics in the main paper use text-embedding-3-large. To assess whether our conclusions depend on this choice, we recompute Vendi score, $1-\phi$ , and PCD using an open-source, retrieval-oriented embedding model (BGE-large). Due to differing inductive biases, absolute values differ across embeddings. However, the induced relative ordering across the five collaboration modes is identical for all three metrics. This suggests that our conclusions are not driven by a specific choice of semantic representation.
正文中所有基于嵌入的指标均使用 text-embedding-3-large。为评估我们的结论是否依赖于这一选择，我们使用一个面向检索的开源嵌入模型（BGE-large）重新计算 Vendi 分数、$1-\phi$ 和 PCD。由于归纳偏置不同，使用不同嵌入得到的绝对数值有所差异。然而，对于这三个指标，五种协作模式的相对排序均完全一致。这表明，我们的结论并非由某一种特定的语义表示选择所驱动。
F.3 Consistency Across Structural MetricsF.3 结构指标之间的一致性
We next examine consistency among three embedding-based structural metrics: Vendi score, $1-\phi$ , and PCD. Given the limited number of collaboration modes ( $n=5$ ), rank correlations trivially reach $1.0$ whenever orderings coincide. We therefore report ordering consistency rather than correlation magnitudes. All three metrics induce identical relative orderings across collaboration modes under both embedding models, suggesting that they capture related but non-redundant aspects of structural diversity.
接下来，我们考察三个基于嵌入的结构指标之间的一致性：Vendi 分数、$1-\phi$ 和 PCD。鉴于协作模式的数量有限（$n=5$），只要排序一致，秩相关系数就自然达到 $1.0$。因此，我们报告排序一致性，而非相关系数的大小。在两种嵌入模型下，这三个指标对不同协作模式给出的相对排序均完全一致，这表明它们捕捉了结构多样性中相互关联但并不冗余的不同方面。

We examine the relationship among the four metrics reported in the main paper (Table 2). Three of them are structural metrics computed in embedding space (Vendi, $1-\phi$ , and PCD using the OpenAI embedding), while the fourth captures lexical uniqueness (content-only weighted distinct-3, W-D-3).
我们考察正文所报告的四个指标之间的关系（表 2）。其中三个是在嵌入空间中计算的结构指标（使用 OpenAI 嵌入的 Vendi、$1-\phi$ 和 PCD），第四个则衡量词汇独特性（仅基于内容的加权 distinct-3，W-D-3）。

In contrast, W-D-3 does not necessarily match the embedding-based ordering, which is expected: it measures surface-level lexical novelty that can vary independently from semantic dispersion. We therefore treat W-D-3 as a complementary signal rather than a redundant proxy for structural diversity.
对于不同协作模式，基于嵌入的结构指标给出的相对排序高度一致（仅存在少量局部次序互换），这表明我们的主要结构性结论并不依赖于某一种特定的形式化定义。相比之下，W-D-3 给出的排序不一定与基于嵌入的排序一致，这是符合预期的：它衡量的是表层词汇新颖性，其变化可以独立于语义离散程度。因此，我们将 W-D-3 视为一种补充信号，而非结构多样性的冗余替代指标。
Overall, the absence of systematic contradictions between the structural and lexical views supports the interpretation that observed differences across collaboration modes reflect robust changes in diversity and consensus, rather than artifacts of a specific metric choice.
总体而言，结构视角与词汇视角之间不存在系统性矛盾，这支持了如下解释：不同协作模式下观察到的差异反映了多样性与共识的稳健变化，而非特定指标选择所造成的假象。
F.5 Lexical Uniqueness and $n$ gram OrderF.5 词汇独特性与 $n$ 元语法阶数

为评估仅针对内容的分词的影响，我们将原始 distinct- $3$ 与仅基于内容的加权 distinct- $3$ 进行比较。由于普遍存在的模板化表达，原始词汇计数呈现出较高的方差。仅针对内容的分词降低了这一方差，同时保留了各协作模式之间的相对排序。这表明，仅保留内容的过滤主要起到降噪作用，而非驱动所观察结果的因素。

G.1 Inspection Rule DefinitionsG.1 检查规则定义
Polite Consensus Collapse.礼貌性共识坍缩。
We operationalize “polite consensus collapse” as a purely behavioral pattern satisfying all of the following criteria within a single session:
我们将“礼貌性共识坍缩”操作化定义为一种纯粹的行为模式，即在单次会话中满足以下所有标准：
Absence of counter-claims: no turn contains an explicit critique, disagreement marker, or alternative proposal branch (defined as an explicit proposal of a different research direction from the one assigned by the Leader).
缺乏反对主张：任何一轮发言均不包含明确的批评、异议标记或替代提案分支（定义为明确提出与领导者所分配方向不同的研究方向）。
Absence of independent sub-problems: no Collaborator turn introduces a research question or sub-problem not already present in the Leader’s framing.
缺乏独立子问题：协作者的任何一轮发言均未提出领导者既有框架中尚未出现的研究问题或子问题。
Final-proposal alignment: the final proposal title and abstract recombine keywords and directions from the Leader’s Round 1 assignment without introducing new thematic anchors.
最终提案的一致性：最终提案的标题与摘要重新组合了领导者在第 1 轮任务分配中的关键词和方向，未引入新的主题锚点。
Deference and Pushback Markers.顺从与反驳标记。
We define two complementary marker vocabularies applied to the opening sentence of each Collaborator turn:
我们定义了两套互补的标记词表，用于识别协作者每轮发言的开场句：
Deference markers (agreement-first phrases): “Building on…,” “Following…,” “As you noted…,” “Excellent point…,” “I fully agree…,” “I’d like to add to what…,” “Great insight…,” “That’s a fascinating point…,” and paraphrase-then-extend patterns (restating a prior speaker’s claim before adding content).
顺从标记（先表同意的措辞）：“在……的基础上”“沿着……”“正如你所指出的……”“这个观点非常好……”“我完全同意……”“我想对……的说法作些补充”“很有见地……”“这是一个引人入胜的观点……”，以及先转述再扩展的模式（先重述前一位发言者的主张，再补充内容）。
Pushback markers: “I disagree,” “however,” “counterpoint,” “I’m not convinced,” “I would challenge,” “alternatively,” “a different view,” “I’d push back,” “I’m skeptical,” “correct me if I’m wrong.”
反驳标记：“我不同意”“然而”“相反的观点是”“我尚未被说服”“我想质疑”“另一种选择是”“一种不同的看法”“我想提出反对意见”“我持怀疑态度”“如果我说错了，请纠正我”。
Under these definitions, deference-marker openings appear in approximately 61% of Leader-Led sessions; pushback markers appear in fewer than 1%.
按照这些定义，约 61% 的领导者主导会话中出现了以顺从标记开场的发言；出现反驳标记的会话则少于 1%。

This appendix provides a per-topic decomposition of the group-size scaling analysis in Section 5 to address whether the observed diversity saturation is driven by limited ideation capacity of individual research topics.
本附录对第 5 节的群体规模扩展分析进行逐主题分解，以考察所观察到的多样性饱和现象是否由单个研究主题有限的构思能力所驱动。
H.1 MotivationH.1 动机
The aggregate analysis in Figure 7 shows that the Diversity Utilization Ratio (Vendi/ $N$ ) declines from 1.03 at $N{=}3$ to 0.47 at $N{=}7$ . A natural alternative hypothesis is that this saturation reflects the finite complexity of the 20 ICLR research topics used as our testbed: perhaps each topic supports only ${\sim}3$ genuinely distinct ideas, and groups larger than $N{=}3$ simply exhaust the available ideation space. To test this hypothesis, we decompose the scaling analysis to the individual topic level.
图 7 中的汇总分析表明，多样性利用率（Diversity Utilization Ratio，Vendi/ $N$）从 $N{=}3$ 时的 1.03 下降到 $N{=}7$ 时的 0.47。一个自然的替代假设是，这种饱和反映了我们用作测试平台的 20 个 ICLR 研究主题的复杂性有限：或许每个主题只能支持 ${\sim}3$ 个真正不同的想法，而规模超过 $N{=}3$ 的群体只不过是耗尽了可用的构思空间。为检验这一假设，我们将扩展分析分解到单个主题层面。
H.2 MethodH.2 方法
For each of the 20 ICLR topics and each group size $N\in\{3,4,5,6,7\}$ , we have 50 proposals generated by independent $N$ -agent MAS runs (no cross-run interaction). We compute the per-topic Vendi Score using the same OpenAI text-embedding-3-large embeddings and cosine-similarity kernel as in the main paper. We then analyze: (1) whether the absolute Vendi Score increases or plateaus with $N$ , (2) whether topic-level intrinsic diversity capacity (measured at $N{=}3$ ) predicts the scaling behavior, and (3) the cross-topic variance in utilization ratio.
对于 20 个 ICLR 主题中的每个主题，以及每种群体规模 $N\in\{3,4,5,6,7\}$，我们均有 50 份提案，分别由包含 $N$ 个智能体的多智能体系统（MAS）独立运行生成（各次运行之间没有交互）。我们使用与正文相同的 OpenAI text-embedding-3-large 嵌入和余弦相似度核，计算每个主题的 Vendi Score。随后，我们分析：(1) Vendi Score 的绝对值是随 $N$ 增大而上升，还是趋于平台；(2) 主题层面的内在多样性容量（在 $N{=}3$ 时测量）能否预测扩展行为；以及 (3) 利用率的跨主题方差。

H.4 InterpretationH.4 解释
The declining Utilization Ratio (Vendi/ $N$ ) reported in Section 5 reflects diminishing marginal returns per additional agent: each new agent contributes some diversity to the proposal pool, but less than the theoretical maximum of one fully orthogonal perspective. This is analogous to diminishing returns in team scaling (Brooks, 1975), not evidence that topics “run out” of ideas. The bottleneck is the shared alignment priors and consensus dynamics inherent in LLM-based multi-agent systems, which our structural interventions address.
第 5 节报告的利用率（Vendi/ $N$）下降，反映了每增加一个智能体所带来的边际收益递减：每个新智能体都会为提案池贡献一定的多样性，但低于一个完全正交的视角所能带来的理论最大贡献。这类似于团队规模扩展中的收益递减（Brooks, 1975），并不能证明主题的想法会“耗尽”。瓶颈在于基于 LLM 的多智能体系统所固有的共享对齐先验和共识动态，而我们的结构性干预正是针对这些问题。
