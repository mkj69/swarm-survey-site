Abstract摘要
As language models are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents, they must coordinate with others over long horizons in open-ended interactive tasks. Yet existing evaluations rarely test these demands together, instead emphasising single-agent tasks, short interactions, or highly structured multi-agent settings. We introduce alem, a JAX-based benchmark for open-ended multi-agent coordination built on Craftax-like dynamics. alem embeds procedurally generated coordination tasks, soft specialisation, communication, and controllable coordination difficulty into a long-horizon survival world with exploration, crafting, trading, and combat. We evaluate $13$ modern LLMs zero-shot within homogeneous teams, with trained MARL agents as reference points. Current LLM agents remain far from solving alem, averaging only $\sim 6\%$ normalised return, but their failures are not uniform. On the hardest coordination setting, zero-shot Gemini-3.1-Pro-High approaches MARL agents trained for one billion steps, while GPT-5.4-High achieves strong base-task reward but much lower coordination reward. This contrast shows that individual task competence does not imply coordination competence. Ablations show that communication is the largest contributor to coordination, while memory and reasoning help when used to maintain multi-step plans. Overall, our results identify coordination as a distinct bottleneck for frontier LLM agents, separate from single-agent capabilities. alem makes this bottleneck measurable and provides a controlled testbed for developing agents that communicate, allocate roles, and execute shared plans. Code is available at https://github.com/alem-world/alem-env.

Large Language Models (LLMs) have evolved rapidly, from systems primarily used for text generation [8, 57, 13] to agentic systems capable of completing long-horizon, open-ended tasks [31, 62, 11, 35, 39, 59]. As these systems are deployed as autonomous agents, they will increasingly operate in multi-agent settings where success depends on coordinating with others [25]. Yet the mechanisms required for sustained multi-agent coordination remain poorly understood, especially when agents must communicate intent, allocate roles, maintain shared plans, and align actions over long horizons [23]. This creates a need to evaluate whether LLM agents can coordinate effectively in long-horizon, open-ended multi-agent environments, and to characterise the failures that arise when they cannot.
大语言模型（LLM）发展迅速，已从主要用于文本生成的系统 [8, 57, 13] 演变为能够完成长时程开放式任务的智能体系统 [31, 62, 11, 35, 39, 59]。随着这些系统被部署为自主智能体，它们将越来越多地在多智能体场景中运行，而在这些场景中，成功取决于与其他智能体的协调 [25]。然而，人们对持续开展多智能体协调所需的机制仍缺乏充分理解，尤其是在智能体必须传达意图、分配角色、维持共同计划并在长时程中使行动保持一致的情况下 [23]。因此，有必要评估 LLM 智能体能否在长时程、开放式多智能体环境中有效协调，并刻画它们无法做到这一点时出现的失败模式。
Existing benchmarks cover only part of this space. MultiAgentBench, Collab-Overcooked, and related multi-agent LLM benchmarks evaluate collaboration, but typically in shorter-horizon and more narrowly specified tasks [69, 53, 3, 60, 22, 38]. Others focus on long-horizon task completion in primarily single-agent settings [42, 67, 49, 61, 68, 27, 20], or include multiple agents but lack explicit coordination tasks or controllable coordination difficulty [40, 65, 63]. The community therefore still lacks evaluations that combine long-horizon interaction and open-ended task structure with explicit, procedurally generated, and controllable coordination demands. Games are established testbeds for sequential decision-making [5, 7, 47, 58, 9], and procedural generation is especially useful because it reduces memorisation and contamination while enabling controlled variation across tasks and difficulty levels [42, 48].
现有基准仅覆盖这一问题空间的一部分。MultiAgentBench、Collab-Overcooked 及相关的多智能体 LLM 基准会评估协作能力，但通常采用时程较短、任务规定更为狭窄的任务 [69, 53, 3, 60, 22, 38]。其他基准主要关注单智能体场景中的长时程任务完成情况 [42, 67, 49, 61, 68, 27, 20]，或虽包含多个智能体，却缺乏明确的协调任务或可控的协调难度 [40, 65, 63]。因此，研究界仍然缺乏将长时程交互和开放式任务结构与明确、程序化生成且可控的协调需求相结合的评测。游戏是序贯决策研究中成熟的测试平台 [5, 7, 47, 58, 9]，而程序化生成尤其有用，因为它能够减少记忆和数据污染，同时支持对不同任务和难度级别进行可控变化 [42, 48]。
To address these gaps, we introduce alem[fn1](Fig. 1), a benchmark for open-ended[fn2] multi-agent coordination built on Craftax-like dynamics [37, 40]. alem procedurally generates complex environments where coordination difficulty is modulated by a single parameter, ranging from loose temporal dependencies and handovers to tightly coupled joint actions (Fig. 2). Information-theoretic diagnostics show that these mechanics induce genuine inter-agent dependence, not just added single-agent difficulty (App. B.1). Implemented in JAX [6], the benchmark facilitates scalable and reproducible evaluation of how multi-agent failure modes emerge as coordination demands increase.
为填补这些空白，我们提出 alem[fn1]（图 1），这是一个基于类 Craftax 动态机制 [37, 40] 构建的开放式[fn2]多智能体协调基准。alem 通过程序化方式生成复杂环境，其中的协调难度由单一参数调节，涵盖从宽松的时间依赖和交接到紧密耦合的联合行动等不同情况（图 2）。信息论诊断表明，这些机制确实引入了智能体间的依赖关系，而不仅仅是增加了单智能体任务的难度（附录B.1）。该基准使用 JAX [6] 实现，便于以可扩展、可复现的方式评估多智能体失败模式如何随着协调需求的增加而出现。

Using alem, we evaluate 13 modern LLMs in zero-shot homogeneous teams, with trained MARL agents as reference points. Current LLM agents remain far from solving the benchmark, averaging only $\sim 6\%$ normalised return. Gemini-3.1-Pro-High performs comparably to MARL agents trained for one billion environment steps on the hardest coordination setting ( $17.5\%$ vs. $17.6\%$ Coord.%), while GPT-5.4-High achieves strong base-task reward but much lower coordination reward. Ablations further show that communication has the largest effect on coordination performance, while memory and reasoning help when they support multi-step planning. Finally, in initial heterogeneous-team experiments, mixed teams perform near the average of their constituent homogeneous baselines rather than matching the strongest member’s performance. 我们使用 alem，在零样本同质团队中评估了 13 个现代 LLM，并以经过训练的多智能体强化学习（MARL）智能体作为参照。当前的 LLM 智能体距离解决该基准仍相去甚远，平均归一化回报仅为 $\sim 6\%$。在最具挑战性的协调设置下，Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 的表现与经过十亿个环境步训练的 MARL 智能体相当（协调得分 Coord.% 分别为 $17.5\%$ 和 $17.6\%$），而 GPT-5.4-High 虽然获得了较高的基础任务奖励，但协调奖励却低得多。这一对比表明，个体的任务能力不足以保证协同推进。消融实验进一步表明，通信对协调表现的影响最大，而记忆和推理在能够支持多步规划时会有所帮助。最后，在初步的异质团队实验中，混合团队的表现接近其组成成员各自对应的同质团队基线的平均水平，而未能达到最强成员的表现水平。这些结果共同表明，长时程、开放式协调是当前 LLM 智能体面临的一个独立瓶颈。
Our contributions are: i) alem, a JAX-based benchmark for long-horizon, open-ended multi-agent coordination with procedural tasks, soft specialisation, communication, and controllable difficulty; ii) pixel, symbolic, and text interfaces for evaluating MARL and zero-shot LLM agents under matched coordination demands; and iii) a systematic evaluation of 13 modern LLMs and 4 MARL baselines, showing that coordination remains a distinct bottleneck from base-task competence and exposing failure modes across communication, memory, reasoning, and team composition.
我们的贡献包括：i) alem，一个基于 JAX 的长时程、开放式多智能体协调基准，具有程序化任务、软专业化、通信和可控难度；ii) 像素、符号和文本接口，用于在相同的协调要求下评估 MARL 智能体和零样本 LLM 智能体；以及 iii) 对 13 个现代 LLM 和 4 个 MARL 基线进行的系统性评估，表明协调仍是独立于基础任务能力的瓶颈，并揭示了通信、记忆、推理和团队组成方面的失败模式。
2 Background2 背景
We model the environment as a partially observable stochastic game (POSG) [26], denoted as the tuple $\langle I,S,A,O,\Omega,T,R\rangle$ . Here, $I=\{1,\dots,n\}$ is the set of agents, $S$ is the set of states, $A=A^{1}\times\dots\times A^{n}$ is the joint action space, and $O=O^{1}\times\dots\times O^{n}$ is the joint observation space. The observation function $\Omega=(\Omega^{1},\dots,\Omega^{n})$ maps states to per-agent observations: at each state $s\in S$ , agent $i$ receives an individual observation $o^{i}=\Omega^{i}(s)\in O^{i}$ and selects an action $a^{i}\in A^{i}$ . The environment transitions according to $T:S\times A\to\Delta(S)$ , where $\Delta(\cdot)$ is the set of probability distributions over a set. Each agent receives an individual reward given by $R^{i}:S\times A\times S\to\mathbb{R}$ .
我们将环境建模为部分可观测随机博弈（POSG）[26]，记为元组 $\langle I,S,A,O,\Omega,T,R\rangle$。其中，$I=\{1,\dots,n\}$ 是智能体集合，$S$ 是状态集合，$A=A^{1}\times\dots\times A^{n}$ 是联合动作空间，$O=O^{1}\times\dots\times O^{n}$ 是联合观测空间。观测函数 $\Omega=(\Omega^{1},\dots,\Omega^{n})$ 将状态映射为各智能体的观测：在每个状态 $s\in S$ 下，智能体 $i$ 接收个体观测 $o^{i}=\Omega^{i}(s)\in O^{i}$，并选择动作 $a^{i}\in A^{i}$。环境依据 $T:S\times A\to\Delta(S)$ 进行状态转移，其中 $\Delta(\cdot)$ 表示某一集合上的概率分布集合。每个智能体获得由 $R^{i}:S\times A\times S\to\mathbb{R}$ 给出的个体奖励。

3 alem: An Open-Ended World for Multi-Agent Coordination3 alem：用于多智能体协调的开放式世界
We introduce alem, a benchmark for studying coordination in open-ended multi-agent worlds. Built on Craftax-Coop [40], alem extends prior work along four axes: diverse coordination types, procedural generation of coordination tasks, soft specialisation, and an explicit communication channel. Across nine procedurally generated levels, agents explore, trade resources, craft tools, build structures, and fight mobs under controllable coordination demands. By scaling these demands while preserving the underlying world structure, alem enables controlled evaluation of how coordination failures emerge as coordination demands increase. Information-theoretic diagnostics verify that these mechanics require genuine inter-agent dependence rather than added single-agent difficulty [55] (App. B.1), while our JAX implementation supports billion-step MARL training on a single GPU in under two days (App. C.3).
我们提出 alem，这是一个用于研究开放式多智能体世界中协调问题的基准。alem 基于 Craftax-Coop [40] 构建，在四个方面拓展了先前的工作：多样化的协调类型、协调任务的程序化生成、软专业化，以及显式通信通道。在九个程序化生成的关卡中，智能体在可控的协调要求下进行探索、交换资源、制作工具、建造结构并与怪物战斗。通过在保留底层世界结构的同时调整这些要求，alem 能够以受控方式评估协调失败如何随着协调要求的提高而出现。信息论诊断验证了这些机制要求智能体之间存在真正的相互依赖，而非仅仅增加单个智能体的任务难度 [55]（附录B.1），同时，我们的 JAX 实现支持在单个 GPU 上用不到两天的时间完成十亿步的 MARL 训练（附录C.3）。
3.1 Coordination Types3.1 协调类型
Following Malone and Crowston [36], we view coordination as interdependence between activities. In multi-agent systems, these activities correspond to agents’ actions. We characterise the dependencies between coupled actions $A_{t}^{i}$ and $A_{t^{\prime}}^{j}$ by their temporal separation, $\Delta t=|t-t^{\prime}|$ , resulting in a coordination spectrum from long-range dependencies to simultaneous joint action (Fig. 2).
遵循 Malone 和 Crowston [36] 的观点，我们将协调视为活动之间的相互依赖。在多智能体系统中，这些活动对应于智能体的动作。我们通过时间间隔 $\Delta t=|t-t^{\prime}|$ 来刻画耦合动作 $A_{t}^{i}$ 与 $A_{t^{\prime}}^{j}$ 之间的依赖关系，由此形成了从长程依赖到同时联合行动的协调谱（图 2）。
Existing MARL benchmarks typically isolate specific regions of this spectrum: SMAC [45] emphasises synchronous focus-fire ( $\Delta t\approx 0$ ), Overcooked [10] tests short-window handovers (small $\Delta t$ ), and Melting Pot’s Clean Up [1] targets long-range resource interdependence (large $\Delta t$ ). alem instantiates this entire spectrum within a single open-ended environment, using procedural generation to vary both the temporal structure of coordination and the number of agents involved (details App. B.2).
现有的多智能体强化学习（MARL）基准通常只覆盖这一谱系中的特定区段：SMAC [45] 强调同步集火（$\Delta t\approx 0$），Overcooked [10] 测试短时间窗口内的交接（较小的 $\Delta t$），而 Melting Pot 的 Clean Up [1] 则侧重于长时间跨度的资源相互依赖（较大的 $\Delta t$）。alem 在单一开放式环境中实现了这一完整谱系，利用程序化生成来改变协调的时间结构及参与智能体的数量（详见附录B.2）。
3.2 Procedural Coordination Generation3.2 程序化协调生成
Existing environments procedurally generate spatial layouts to reduce memorisation and encourage generalisation [40, 37, 65, 24]. However, if coordination requirements are fixed, agents can still exploit recurring interaction patterns. alem therefore procedurally generates the coordination structure itself.
现有环境通过程序化生成空间布局来减少记忆化并促进泛化 [40, 37, 65, 24]。然而，如果协调要求固定不变，智能体仍可利用反复出现的交互模式。因此，alem 对协调结构本身进行程序化生成。
Each episode, eligible entities across tasks such as mining, construction, combat, and crafting (Fig. 1) are independently assigned a coordination type (synchronous or handover) and a required agent count ( $2$ to $N$ ). Synchronous tasks also adopt game-theoretic incentives: hard tasks act as pure coordination problems requiring joint action for any reward [46], while soft tasks function as assurance games, balancing a safe solo payoff against a larger joint-action bonus [50] (details App. B.2).
在每个回合中，采矿、建造、战斗和制作等任务（图 1）中符合条件的实体都会被独立分配一种协调类型（同步或交接）以及所需的智能体数量（$2$ 至 $N$）。同步任务还采用了博弈论激励机制：硬性任务构成纯协调问题，必须采取联合行动才能获得任何奖励 [46]；而软性任务则构成保证博弈，在稳妥的单独行动收益与更高的联合行动奖励之间进行权衡 [50]（详见附录B.2）。
3.3 Soft Specialisation and Difficulty Scaling3.3 软性专业分工与难度调节
Instead of enforcing rigid division of labour via hard role gates [1, 40], alem uses soft specialisation. Any agent can attempt any role-gated action, but specialists succeed deterministically ( $\eta_{\text{spec}}=1$ ) while non-specialists succeed with probability $\eta_{\text{non}}=1-\alpha$ . This rewards effective role allocation while preserving the capacity for online adaptation.
alem 采用软性专业分工，而不是通过严格的角色准入限制来强制实施固定的劳动分工 [1, 40]。任何智能体都可以尝试受角色限制的任何动作，但专业智能体必定成功（$\eta_{\text{spec}}=1$），而非专业智能体以概率 $\eta_{\text{non}}=1-\alpha$ 成功。这既能奖励有效的角色分配，又能保留在线适应的能力。
We scale overall coordination difficulty with the single scalar $\alpha\in[0,1]$ . Because world generation in JAX is deterministic, varying $\alpha$ preserves the spatial layout and frequency of coordination opportunities (Fig. 3), cleanly isolating execution difficulty from world variation. Higher $\alpha$ tightens execution constraints: it increases the probability of $N$ -agent requirements, shortens handover windows, increases solo failure rates on soft tasks, and lowers non-specialist efficiency. We evaluate on Easy ( $\alpha=0.3$ ), Medium ( $\alpha=0.6$ ), and Hard ( $\alpha=0.9$ ) settings (parameters detailed in App. B.3).

3.4 Environment Interface3.4 环境接口
3.4.1 RL Interface3.4.1 强化学习接口
Observation space.观测空间。
alem exposes both pixel (see Fig. 6, App B) and symbolic observations of the underlying state. Each egocentric observation includes a local map, inventory, and a teammate dashboard (health, specialisation, requested resources, off-screen direction, and recent communications). Beyond Craftax-Coop, alem explicitly embeds communication messages, coordination states, including soft/hard requirements, handover countdowns, and multi-agent mob targets.
alem 同时提供底层状态的像素观测（见附录 B 中的图 6）和符号观测。每个以自身为中心的观测都包含局部地图、物品栏以及队友信息面板（生命值、专业分工、请求的资源、屏幕外方位和近期通信）。在 Craftax-Coop 的基础上，alem 显式嵌入了通信消息和协调状态，包括软性／硬性要求、交接倒计时以及需要多个智能体共同应对的怪物目标。
Action space.动作空间。
Agents act in a 60-dimensional discrete action space encompassing environment manipulation, crafting, exploration, and combat. To facilitate teamwork, agents can issue persistent resource requests (active for up to 10 timesteps), execute teammate-targeted giving, and broadcast discrete messages. The primary Do action is contextual, it supports both environment interaction and direct agent-to-agent mechanics, such as reviving downed teammates or triggering friendly fire. We apply conservative action masking to filter strictly infeasible choices.
智能体在一个 60 维离散动作空间中行动，该空间涵盖环境操作、制作、探索和战斗。为促进团队合作，智能体可以发出持续性的资源请求（最多持续 10 个时间步）、向指定队友赠送物品，以及广播离散消息。主要的 Do 动作依上下文而定，既支持与环境交互，也支持智能体之间的直接交互机制，例如救起倒地的队友或触发友军伤害。我们采用保守的动作掩码来过滤严格不可行的选择。
Achievements and rewards.成就与奖励。
Following prior environments [37, 40], alem pairs a dense health reward with sparse, first-time rewards for completing a hierarchy of achievements. We introduce 27 novel coordination achievements spanning synchronised mining, temporal handovers, cooperative construction, and coordinated combat. Rewards are individual by default; coordination bonuses are awarded only to participating agents by default (details in App. C.6).
沿用先前环境的做法 [37, 40]，alem 将稠密的生命值奖励与首次完成层级化成就时获得的稀疏奖励相结合。我们引入了 27 项新的协调成就，涵盖同步采矿、时序交接、合作建造和协同战斗。默认情况下，奖励按个体发放；协调奖励默认仅发放给参与的智能体（详见附录C.6）。
3.4.2 Text-based Language Interface3.4.2 基于文本的语言接口
To evaluate language agents, we expose a structured text interface mapped directly to the underlying environment (details in App. C.5). At each timestep, models receive a textual state containing recent observation-action history, teammate messages, a private scratchpad, and visible coordination constraints, together with explicit action affordances. Models must return a structured response containing exactly one valid action and, optionally, reasoning, communication, and scratchpad fields. This parsed action is then mapped to the corresponding discrete environment step. A sample prompt is provided in App. C.7.
为评估语言智能体，我们提供了一个直接映射到底层环境的结构化文本接口（详见附录C.5）。在每个时间步，模型都会接收一个文本形式的状态，其中包含近期的观测—动作历史、队友消息、私有草稿区和可见的协调约束，以及明确列出的可执行动作。模型必须返回结构化响应，其中恰好包含一个有效动作，并可选择包含推理、通信和草稿区字段。随后，解析得到的动作被映射为相应的离散环境步进。提示示例见附录。C.7。
3.5 MARL Baselines3.5 多智能体强化学习（MARL）基线
We train IPPO [12], MAPPO [66], PQN-VDN [16], and HyperMARL-IPPO [56] to provide reference points for zero-shot LLM performance and to verify that the difficulty settings meaningfully affect learned policies. Figure 3 reports 1B-step performance (hyperparameter sweeps detailed in App. C.1). Performance sharply degrades as coordination difficulty increases; even after 3B environment steps, the strongest baseline reaches only 18% of the maximum total reward on the Hard setting (App. C.4). These results show that alem remains an unsaturated challenge for current MARL methods.
我们训练了 IPPO [12]、MAPPO [66]、PQN-VDN [16] 和 HyperMARL-IPPO [56]，为大语言模型（LLM）的零样本性能提供参照，并验证难度设置是否会对学习得到的策略产生实质性影响。图 3 报告了训练 1B 步后的性能（超参数搜索详见附录。C.1）。随着协调难度增加，性能急剧下降；即使经过 3B 个环境步，最强基线在 Hard 设置下也仅达到最大总奖励的 18%（附录。C.4）。这些结果表明，对当前的 MARL 方法而言，alem 仍是一项性能尚未饱和的挑战。
4 Experiments4 实验
We evaluate 13 modern LLMs in zero-shot homogeneous teams, with 4 trained MARL baselines as reference points. Our goal is to measure coordination in long-horizon, open-ended environments, while separating coordination difficulty from the difficulty of the underlying world. To calibrate this distinction, we compare alem against a single-agent baseline and find that its multi-agent dynamics result in a drop in performance, showing that coordination introduces a distinct layer of difficulty (App. E.1).
我们在零样本同质团队中评估了 13 个现代 LLM，并以 4 个经过训练的 MARL 基线作为参照。我们的目标是衡量长时程、开放式环境中的协调能力，同时将协调难度与底层世界本身的难度区分开来。为校准这一区分，我们将 alem 与单智能体基线进行了比较，发现其多智能体动态会导致性能下降，表明协调引入了一个独立的难度层次（附录。E.1）。
Our experiments ask three questions: Q1: How well do LLM agents coordinate zero-shot within homogeneous teams? Q2: How do communication, memory, and reasoning affect coordination? Q3: How well do agents coordinate in heterogeneous teams composed of different models?
我们的实验探讨三个问题：Q1：LLM 智能体在同质团队中的零样本协调表现如何？Q2：通信、记忆和推理如何影响协调？Q3：在由不同模型组成的异质团队中，智能体的协调表现如何？
4.1 Experimental Setup4.1 实验设置
Evaluation protocol.评估协议。
We follow a zero-shot evaluation protocol similar to BALROG [42]. At each timestep, each agent receives the game rules, environment details, local observation, and legal actions in text form through the interface described in Sec. 3.4.2. By default, agents have scratchpad memory, broadcast communication, and a text history containing the last eight observations and actions. Success in alem requires spatial reasoning, long-horizon planning, tracking crafting dependencies, memory, effective communication, and coordination under partial observability.
我们采用了与 BALROG [42] 类似的零样本评估协议。在每个时间步，每个智能体都通过第 3.4.2 节所述的接口，以文本形式接收游戏规则、环境详情、局部观测和合法动作。默认情况下，智能体具备草稿区记忆、广播通信，以及包含最近八次观测和动作的文本历史记录。要在 alem 中取得成功，需要空间推理、长时程规划、追踪合成依赖关系、记忆、有效通信，以及在部分可观测条件下进行协调的能力。
Metrics.评估指标。
Our primary metric is normalised episode return, reported as a percentage of the maximum achievable reward. Following previous Craftax-based environments [40, 37], each agent’s cumulative reward is normalised by the maximum reward for the relevant category and averaged across agents. We report Base%, Coord.%, and Total%, each normalised independently, so Total% is not the sum of Base% and Coord.%. Reward bounds are given in App. C.2.
我们的主要指标是归一化的回合回报，以可获得的最大奖励的百分比表示。沿用此前基于 Craftax 的环境 [40, 37] 的做法，每个智能体的累积奖励先按相应类别的最大奖励进行归一化，再对所有智能体取平均。我们报告 Base%、Coord.% 和 Total%，三者分别独立归一化，因此 Total% 并非 Base% 与 Coord.% 之和。奖励上下界见附录。C.2。
Models.模型。
We evaluate 13 modern language models. Closed-source models are evaluated via their respective APIs (GPT-5.4 [41] and Gemini 3.1 Pro [18]). Open-weight models are served with vLLM [32] and include Gemma-4-E4B-it/26B-A4B-it/31B-it [19], Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct [21], Qwen-3.5-9B/27B/35B-A3B/122B-A10B [54], and Qwen-3.6-27B/35B-A3B [43]. Where applicable, reasoning is enabled, with proprietary models configured to high-reasoning settings. We run 20 seeds per difficulty for open-weight models and 10 for closed-source models (due to API costs), reporting cross-seed means with 95% stratified bootstrap confidence intervals using the rliable library [2].
我们评估了 13 个现代语言模型。闭源模型通过各自的 API 进行评估（GPT-5.4 [41] 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro [18]）。开放权重模型通过 vLLM [32] 提供服务，包括 Gemma-4-E4B-it/26B-A4B-it/31B-it [19]、Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 和 Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct [21]、Qwen-3.5-9B/27B/35B-A3B/122B-A10B [54]，以及 Qwen-3.6-27B/35B-A3B [43]。在适用的情况下启用推理，并将专有模型配置为高推理强度设置。对于每个难度级别，我们为开放权重模型运行 20 个随机种子，为闭源模型运行 10 个随机种子（受 API 成本限制），并使用 rliable 库 [2] 报告跨随机种子的均值及通过分层自助法计算的 95% 置信区间。
Agent harness.智能体运行框架。
Language models are evaluated with the agent harness described in Sec. 3.4.2 and App. C.5. The harness follows a reason-then-act paradigm similar to ReAct [64], but generates a new prompt at each step focusing the evaluation on the reasoning and coordination capabilities of the model instead of confounding factors such as context length or compaction strategy.
语言模型使用第 3.4.2 节和附录中所述的智能体运行框架进行评估。C.5.该评测框架采用类似于 ReAct [64] 的先推理后行动范式，但在每一步都会生成一个新提示，使评估聚焦于模型的推理和协调能力，而不受上下文长度或压缩策略等混杂因素的干扰。

Conversely, Qwen3.5-122B-A10B and Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it achieve higher Coord.% than Base%, indicating that coordination can be exploited even when general competence is limited.
对表现进行分解，可以揭示被汇总分数掩盖的失败。GPT-5.4-High 体现了这种分离：尽管其在各个难度下的 Base% 均位居第二（$14.1\%,10.0\%,11.8\%$），但其 Coord.%（$7.0\%,4.0\%,4.2\%$）仍低得多，在困难难度下落后于 Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it 等较小模型，且双方的 $95\%$ 置信区间互不重叠。图 4(b) 显示，GPT-5.4-High 的交易次数最多（$48.9$），但复活次数相对较少（$3.4$），而 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 则更为均衡（$29.3$ 次交易，$6.9$ 次复活）。因此，局部合作行动和基础任务进展不一定能转化为持续协调。相反，Qwen3.5-122B-A10B 和 Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it 的 Coord.% 高于 Base%，表明即使一般能力有限，也可以利用协调能力。
Most LLM agents do not exploit the difficulty axis.大多数 LLM 智能体未能利用难度这一维度。
Trained MARL agents degrade as coordination difficulty increases (Sec. 3.3), but aggregate LLM Total% remains nearly flat at $\approx 6\%$ across Easy, Medium, and Hard (Tbl. 1). This suggests that most LLM agents do not coordinate reliably enough for the difficulty parameter to strongly affect aggregate performance. Achievement-tier coverage supports this interpretation (Fig. 9), as most LLMs fail to progress beyond Basic achievements regardless of difficulty, whereas stronger agents, such as MARL 1B and Gemini-3.1-Pro-High, show clearer degradation at intermediate and advanced tiers.
随着协调难度增加，经过训练的 MARL 智能体的表现会下降（第 3.3 节），但 LLM 的总体 Total% 在简单、中等和困难难度下几乎保持不变，均为 $\approx 6\%$（表1）。这表明，大多数 LLM 智能体的协调可靠性不足，因而难度参数未能显著影响总体表现。成就层级覆盖情况支持这一解释（图 9）：无论难度如何，大多数 LLM 都无法超越基础成就层级，而 MARL 1B 和 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 等更强的智能体在中级和高级层级上表现出更明显的性能下降。

Coordination failures differ across task structure.协调失败因任务结构而异。
The task-level breakdown in Fig. 4(a) shows that coordination is not a single axis. Handover tasks are comparatively more accessible because they allow temporal slack, as one agent can initiate an opportunity and another can complete it within a bounded time window. By contrast, Synchronous-Hard tasks require agents to identify a shared target, navigate to compatible positions, communicate intent, and act in the same timestep. This strict synchronisation compounds the spatial reasoning challenges already observed in single-agent LLMs [42]. The results reflect this difficulty, as Gemini-3.1-Pro-High leads LLMs on synchronous-hard coordination with only $18\%$ coverage, while the next-best tested LLMs, Gemma-4-31B-it and Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, reach $9\%$ .
图 4(a) 中按任务进行的分解表明，协调并非单一维度。交接任务相对更容易完成，因为它们允许一定的时间余裕：一个智能体可以创造机会，另一个智能体则可在有限的时间窗口内完成交接。相比之下，Synchronous-Hard 任务要求智能体确定共同目标，移动到彼此兼容的位置，交流行动意图，并在同一时间步采取行动。这种严格的同步要求进一步加剧了已在单智能体 LLM 中观察到的空间推理难题 [42]。结果反映了这一困难：Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 在同步困难任务的协调表现上领先于其他 LLM，但覆盖率仅为 $18\%$，而受测 LLM 中表现次佳的 Gemma-4-31B-it 和 Qwen3.6-35B-A3B 的覆盖率为 $9\%$。
Pretrained priors offer advantages in sparse, multi-stage coordination.预训练先验在稀疏、多阶段协调中具有优势。
Construction tasks require long-horizon planning in addition to coordinated execution. Agents must collect resources, progress through a technology chain, converge on a shared site, and execute a synchronised build. As shown in Fig. 4(a), construction coverage is at most $2\%$ for every LLM except Gemini-3.1-Pro-High ( $7\%$ ), and $0\%$ for the MARL 1B reference. This suggests that sparse, long-horizon coordination is difficult to discover through trial-and-error at this MARL compute budget, while pretrained priors may give stronger LLMs a limited advantage.
建造任务除要求协调执行外，还要求长时域规划。智能体必须收集资源，沿技术链逐步推进，汇聚到共同地点，并同步执行建造。如图 4(a) 所示，除 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High（$7\%$）外，所有 LLM 的建造覆盖率均不超过 $2\%$，而 MARL 1B 参考基线的覆盖率为 $0\%$。这表明，在该 MARL 计算预算下，难以通过试错发现稀疏、长时域的协调策略，而预训练先验可能为较强的 LLM 带来有限的优势。
Scale alone does not explain coordination performance.仅靠规模无法解释协调表现。
Across open-weight models, performance is not monotonic in parameter count. Even within model families such as Gemma-4 and Qwen-3.5/3.6, coordination performance does not scale linearly across dense and mixture-of-experts variants (Figs. 4(a), 4(c)). This suggests that coordination depends on factors beyond raw scale, including possibly post-training, reasoning, and how effectively agents use communication.
在开放权重模型中，性能并不随参数量单调变化。即使在 Gemma-4 和 Qwen-3.5/3.6 等模型家族内部，稠密变体与混合专家变体的协调表现也不随规模线性增长（图 4(a)、4(c)）。这表明，协调还取决于单纯规模以外的因素，可能包括后训练、推理以及智能体利用通信的有效程度。

4.2.2 Q2: How do communication, memory, and reasoning affect coordination?4.2.2 Q2：通信、记忆和推理如何影响协调？
We next ablate three components of the LLM agent harness: scratchpad memory, communication, and reasoning (Sec. 4.1). We run these ablations on the strongest closed-source and open-weight models from Sec. 4.2.1, Gemini-3.1-Pro-High and Gemma-4-31B-it, on the Hard setting.
接下来，我们对 LLM 智能体运行框架的三个组成部分进行消融：草稿板记忆、通信和推理（第 4.1 节）。我们在 Hard 设置下，对第 4.2.1 节中表现最强的闭源模型和开放权重模型 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 和 Gemma-4-31B-it 开展这些消融实验。
Communication is critical for coordination.通信对协调至关重要。
Removing communication produces the largest drop in coordination performance for both models (Fig. 5). Gemini-3.1-Pro-High falls from $17.5$ to $5.3$ Coord.%, while Gemma-4-31B-it falls from $8.8$ to $3.8$ Coord.%. The corresponding changes in Base% are smaller and less consistent (Gemini $13.8\to 11.6$ , Gemma $7.3\to 8.3$ ), suggesting that communication primarily supports coordination rather than general environment progress. Message statistics support this interpretation: agents frequently address specific teammates (Gemini $43.4\%$ , Gemma $44.1\%$ of messages) and often use imperative verbs (Gemini $73.7\%$ , Gemma $52.2\%$ ). Agents therefore appear to use communication to broadcast intent, assign actions, and align timing. Without communication, they can still make base progress, but struggle to coordinate effectively (qualitative analysis in App. I).
对于这两个模型，移除通信均导致协调表现出现最大幅度的下降（图 5）。Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 的 Coord.% 从 $17.5$ 降至 $5.3$，而 Gemma-4-31B-it 的 Coord.% 从 $8.8$ 降至 $3.8$。Base% 的相应变化较小，且方向不一致（Gemini $13.8\to 11.6$，Gemma $7.3\to 8.3$），这表明通信主要支持协调，而非一般性的环境进展。消息统计支持这一解释：智能体经常向特定队友发送消息（Gemini 的消息中占 $43.4\%$，Gemma 占 $44.1\%$），并且经常使用祈使动词（Gemini 占 $73.7\%$，Gemma 占 $52.2\%$）。因此，智能体似乎通过通信来广播意图、分配行动并协调时机。没有通信时，它们仍能取得基础进展，但难以有效协调（定性分析见附录I）。
Scratchpad memory helps when used for planning.草稿板记忆在用于规划时有所帮助。
The memory ablation is model-dependent. Removing scratchpad memory lowers Gemini-3.1-Pro-High’s mean Coord.% from $17.5$ to $13.1$ , while leaving Base% nearly unchanged ( $13.8\to 13.6$ ). For Gemma-4-31B-it, the mean changes are small in Coord.% ( $8.8\to 8.6$ ) and Base% ( $7.3\to 8.6$ ), with overlapping confidence intervals. This suggests that scratchpad memory is not uniformly useful; its effect depends on how models use it. Qualitative analysis provides a possible explanation (App. G). Gemini uses the scratchpad as a forward-looking planner: $71.5\%$ of entries are multi-line, $75.3\%$ contain future-oriented terms such as will, next, then, or plan, and $12.4\%$ contain explicit turn-indexed action sequences such as T9:Do, T10:Move West, and T11:Give wood to Agent 1. In contrast, Gemma’s scratchpads are mostly one-line state summaries (average $1.08$ lines), with frequent coordinate references ( $98.8\%$ ) and status information that is often already present in the next observation. These patterns suggest that scratchpad memory appears more helpful when models use it to preserve plans or commitments across turns, rather than to restate the current state.
记忆消融的效果因模型而异。移除草稿板记忆使 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 的平均 Coord.% 从 $17.5$ 降至 $13.1$，而 Base% 几乎不变（$13.8\to 13.6$）。对于 Gemma-4-31B-it，平均 Coord.%（$8.8\to 8.6$）和 Base%（$7.3\to 8.6$）的变化均较小，且置信区间相互重叠。这表明，草稿板记忆并非总是有用；其效果取决于模型如何使用它。定性分析提供了一种可能的解释（附录G）。Gemini 将草稿板用作前瞻性的规划工具：$71.5\%$ 的条目包含多行内容，$75.3\%$ 包含 will、next、then 或 plan 等面向未来的词语，$12.4\%$ 包含明确按回合编号标记的行动序列，例如 T9:Do、T10:Move West 和 T11:Give wood to Agent 1。相比之下，Gemma 的草稿区大多是单行状态摘要（平均 $1.08$ 行），频繁提及坐标（$98.8\%$），且其中的状态信息往往已包含在下一次观测中。这些模式表明，当模型使用草稿区记忆来跨轮次保留计划或承诺，而非重述当前状态时，它似乎更有帮助。
Reasoning supports both coordination and base progress.推理既支持协调，也支持基础任务的推进。
Reasoning is the only ablation that lowers both Base% and Coord.% for both models (Fig. 5). For Gemini-3.1-Pro-High, the ablation is partial because the API only allows us to reduce reasoning, not disable it. Even so, Coord.% falls from $17.5$ to $9.7$ and Base% from $13.8$ to $10.3$ . For Gemma-4-31B-it, disabling reasoning reduces Coord.% from $8.8$ to $5.5$ and Base% from $7.3$ to $4.6$ .
在所有消融实验中，只有推理消融会使两个模型的 Base% 和 Coord.% 均下降（图 5）。对于 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High，消融是部分性的，因为 API 只允许我们减少推理，而不能将其禁用。即便如此，Coord.% 仍从 $17.5$ 降至 $9.7$，Base% 从 $13.8$ 降至 $10.3$。对于 Gemma-4-31B-it，禁用推理使 Coord.% 从 $8.8$ 降至 $5.5$，Base% 从 $7.3$ 降至 $4.6$。
Qualitative traces suggest different failure modes (App. H). For Gemini, reduced reasoning removes much of the planner-like behaviour: scratchpad length falls from $207$ to $69$ characters, turn-indexed plans nearly disappear ( $12.4\%\to 0.1\%$ ), and messages become shorter ( $132\to 53$ characters). Gemma instead writes longer scratchpads without reasoning ( $153\to 228$ characters), often near the $\sim\!1000$ -character cap, suggesting that the scratchpad partly substitutes for the removed reasoning. This compensation is incomplete, as both Base% and Coord.% still decrease.
定性轨迹表明存在不同的失败模式（附录H）。对于 Gemini，减少推理会使其大部分规划者式行为消失：草稿区长度从 $207$ 个字符降至 $69$ 个字符，按轮次索引的计划几乎消失（$12.4\%\to 0.1\%$），消息也变得更短（$132\to 53$ 个字符）。相反，Gemma 在没有推理的情况下会撰写更长的草稿区内容（$153\to 228$ 个字符），且往往接近 $\sim\!1000$ 个字符的上限，这表明草稿区在一定程度上替代了被移除的推理。这种补偿并不充分，因为 Base% 和 Coord.% 仍然都在下降。
4.2.3 Q3: How well do agents coordinate in teams composed of different models?4.2.3 Q3：在由不同模型组成的团队中，智能体的协调表现如何？
We evaluate heterogeneous teams on Hard in two settings: a same-family Gemma team composed of Gemma-4-E4B-it, Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it, and Gemma-4-31B-it, and a cross-family team composed of the three strongest LLM agents: Gemma-4-31B-it, GPT-5.4-High, and Gemini-3.1-Pro-High.
我们在 Hard 难度下，针对两种设置评估异构团队：一种是由 Gemma-4-E4B-it、Gemma-4-26B-A4B-it 和 Gemma-4-31B-it 组成的同系列 Gemma 团队，另一种是由三个最强的 LLM 智能体 Gemma-4-31B-it、GPT-5.4-High 和 Gemini-3.1-Pro-High 组成的跨系列团队。
Heterogeneous teams perform near the homogeneous-team average.异构团队的表现接近同构团队的平均水平。
Fig. 5 shows that heterogeneous teams perform close to the average of their corresponding homogeneous teams. The same-family Gemma team is slightly above this average ( $+0.1$ Total%), while the cross-family team is slightly below it ( $-0.1$ Total%). Thus, in the tested settings, heterogeneous teams neither collapse to the weakest member nor inherit the performance of the strongest one. This suggests that adding a stronger model to a team is not sufficient to lift team performance. Mixed teams may face additional coordination problems, as agents must align their communication conventions, planning horizons, and actions with teammates that reason and communicate differently.
图 5 显示，异构团队的表现接近其对应同构团队的平均水平。同系列 Gemma 团队略高于这一平均水平（Total% 为 $+0.1$），而跨系列团队则略低于这一平均水平（Total% 为 $-0.1$）。因此，在所测试的设置中，异构团队既没有退化到最弱成员的水平，也没有达到最强成员的表现。这表明，仅向团队中加入一个更强的模型，并不足以提升团队表现。混合团队可能面临额外的协调问题，因为智能体必须与推理和沟通方式不同的队友，在沟通惯例、规划跨度和行动上达成一致。
Summary.小结。
Overall, alem reveals coordination as a bottleneck distinct from base-task performance. Communication matters most among the ablated harness components, and initial heterogeneous-team experiments suggest that mixed teams do not automatically match their strongest member’s performance.
总体而言，alem 揭示了协调是一个独立于基础任务表现的瓶颈。在接受消融的智能体运行框架组件中，通信的影响最大，而初步的异构团队实验表明，混合团队不会自动达到其最强成员的表现。
5 Related Work5 相关工作
Long-horizon and agentic evaluation.长时程与智能体评估。
LLMs are increasingly evaluated as agents in long-horizon, open-ended tasks [14, 31, 62, 11]. Recent benchmarks test models in complex environments requiring spatial reasoning, exploration, planning, and tool use [42, 67, 68, 27]. Procedural generation is especially useful because it reduces memorisation, mitigates contamination, and supports scalable variation across tasks [42, 48]. alem brings these principles to multi-agent evaluation, where agents must also infer and satisfy diverse coordination demands.
越来越多的研究将 LLM 作为智能体，在长时程、开放式任务中进行评估 [14, 31, 62, 11]。近期的基准在需要空间推理、探索、规划和工具使用的复杂环境中测试模型 [42, 67, 68, 27]。程序化生成尤其有用，因为它能减少记忆效应、缓解数据污染，并支持以可扩展的方式生成多样化任务 [42, 48]。alem 将这些原则引入多智能体评估，在这种评估中，智能体还必须推断并满足多样化的协调需求。
Multi-Agent LLM collaboration.多智能体 LLM 协作。
Multi-agent LLMs have shown promise in code generation, debate, and collaborative reasoning [28, 14]. Recent benchmarks evaluate LLM agents in collaborative settings [69, 53, 22], but typically use shorter-horizon tasks where roles, goals, or interaction structure are specified in advance. In contrast, alem tests whether LLM agents can infer and execute changing coordination requirements in a long-horizon open-ended world.
多智能体 LLM 已在代码生成、辩论和协作推理方面展现出潜力 [28, 14]。近期的基准在协作场景中评估 LLM 智能体 [69, 53, 22]，但通常采用时程较短的任务，其中的角色、目标或交互结构均预先指定。相比之下，alem 测试 LLM 智能体能否在长时程、开放式世界中推断并落实不断变化的协调要求。
MARL and coordination.多智能体强化学习（MARL）与协调。
MARL benchmarks often focus on particular forms of coordination: SMAC [45] emphasises synchronous focus-fire, Overcooked [10] tests short-window handovers, and Melting Pot [1] includes long-range resource interdependence. alem builds on Craftax-Coop [40], but turns coordination into an explicit, procedural, and controllable part of the environment: tasks are sampled across a coordination spectrum (Fig. 2), agents use soft rather than hard specialisation, communication is explicit, and difficulty can be scaled. We further use information-theoretic diagnostics [55] to verify that our environment demands genuine multi-agent interdependence (App. B.1).
MARL 基准通常侧重于特定形式的协调：SMAC [45] 强调同步集火，Overcooked [10] 测试短时间窗口内的交接，而 Melting Pot [1] 则包含长时程的资源相互依赖。alem 基于 Craftax-Coop [40] 构建，但将协调变成环境中显式、程序化且可控的组成部分：任务从一个协调需求谱系中采样（图 2），智能体采用软性而非硬性专业分工，通信是显式的，且难度可以调节。我们进一步使用信息论诊断方法 [55]，验证我们的环境确实要求多智能体之间存在相互依赖（附录B.1）。
6 Conclusions6 结论
We introduce alem, a JAX-based benchmark for long-horizon, open-ended multi-agent coordination. Our zero-shot evaluations of 13 LLMs alongside trained MARL baselines show that coordination is a distinct bottleneck for current agents, base-task competence does not guarantee coordinated progress, and communication has the largest effect on coordinated behaviour. By exposing failure modes across communication, memory, reasoning, and team composition, alem makes coordination measurable and provides a controlled testbed for developing agents that coordinate effectively.
我们提出 alem，这是一个基于 JAX 的基准，用于评估长时程、开放式多智能体协调。我们对 13 个 LLM 进行了零样本评估，并与经过训练的 MARL 基线进行比较，结果表明：协调是当前智能体面临的一个独立瓶颈，基础任务能力并不保证协调推进，而通信对协调行为的影响最大。通过揭示通信、记忆、推理和团队构成方面的失效模式，alem 使协调变得可衡量，并为开发能够有效协调的智能体提供了受控测试平台。
Limitations and Future Work.局限性与未来工作。
Our evaluation focuses on text-based LLM agents, leaving Vision-Language Models (VLMs) and Claude-family models for future work. API costs limit evaluations of proprietary models (GPT and Gemini) to 10 seeds per difficulty. Future work can use alem to study stronger agent harnesses, cross-episode memory, and lifelong learning in interactive multi-agent worlds.

B.2 Coordination TasksB.2 协调任务
B.2.1 Coordination Task CategoriesB.2.1 协调任务类别
We instantiate coordination across four activity categories:
我们在四类活动中实现协调机制：
Mining. Resource blocks (including trees, ores, gems) are sampled for coordination during world generation. Synchronous mining requires the assigned number of agents to act on the same block in the same step. Handover mining requires a second agent to mine the block within $\Delta t\in[\Delta t_{\min},\Delta t_{\max}]$ steps of the first agent.
采矿。在世界生成过程中，抽样选取资源方块（包括树木、矿石、宝石）并为其设置协调要求。同步采矿要求指定数量的智能体在同一步对同一个方块采取行动。交接采矿要求第二个智能体在第一个智能体采矿后的 $\Delta t\in[\Delta t_{\min},\Delta t_{\max}]$ 步内开采该方块。
Construction. Construction sites for shelters, forges, and beacons are placed on valid overworld and mining-floor tiles, independently of mining blocks. Each site receives the same synchronous or handover structure as mining, together with a soft or hard synchronous variant. Building also requires the appropriate resources for the chosen structure. In handover construction, one agent initiates a specific structure, and a second agent must complete that structure type before the deadline, otherwise the site reverts and materials are refunded to the initiator.
建造。庇护所、熔炉和信标的建造地点被放置在地表世界和采矿层的有效地块上，其位置独立于采矿方块。每个地点都采用与采矿相同的同步或交接结构，其中同步结构分为软同步和硬同步两种变体。建造还需要具备所选建筑对应的资源。在交接建造中，一个智能体发起某种特定建筑的建造，第二个智能体必须在截止时间前完成该类型建筑，否则该地点将恢复原状，材料也将退还给发起者。
Combat. Mob slots are pre-labelled during world generation as standard or coordinated mobs. Coordinated mobs appear as either elite melee mobs, elite ranged mobs, or large passive mobs, and each is marked as soft or hard and assigned the required agent count.
战斗。在世界生成过程中，生物生成槽位被预先标记为普通生物槽位或需协调应对的生物槽位。需协调应对的生物以精英近战生物、精英远程生物或大型被动生物的形式出现，每个生物都被标记为软协调或硬协调，并被指定所需的智能体数量。
Crafting. Diamond pickaxes, swords, and armour require synchronous coordination, i.e. the craft succeeds only when the required number of agents attempt it at the same Epic Forge in the same step. This is a deliberately restricted variant, it is hard coordination only and tied to a specific tool station, and tests whether agents can converge on a shared location for a one-shot joint action.
制作。钻石镐、钻石剑和钻石护甲的制作需要同步协调，即只有当所需数量的智能体在同一步于同一个 Epic Forge 尝试制作时，制作才会成功。这是一个刻意受限的变体，仅采用硬协调，并绑定于特定工具站，用于检验智能体能否汇聚到同一地点以执行一次性的联合行动。

C.5 Text-based Language Interface DetailsC.5 基于文本的语言接口细节
Prompt Structure.提示词结构。
At each timestep $t$ , the prompt for agent $i$ is assembled from three components: a system prompt, a history of recent observations and actions, and the current observation with instructions.
在每个时间步 $t$，智能体 $i$ 的提示词由三个部分组成：系统提示词、近期观测与动作的历史记录，以及附带指令的当前观测。
System prompt: The system prompt is shared across all time steps for an agent and defines the agent identity and role, the team size, the objective, and the rules needed to act in the environment. By default it also optionally includes the full action catalogue with natural-language descriptions, game and coordination mechanics.
系统提示词：对于一个智能体，系统提示词在所有时间步中保持一致，定义了智能体的身份和角色、团队规模、目标，以及在环境中行动所需的规则。默认情况下，它还可选择包含附有自然语言描述的完整动作目录，以及游戏机制和协调机制。
Observation and Action History: The observation and action history is a rolling sequence of recent messages from the environment and the agent itself. The observation messages contain previous observations, labelled as “Observation from $k$ step(s) ago”, and the action messages contain the action previously taken by the agent. When memory and communication is enabled, the observation history also includes scratchpad notes and teammate messages, each labelled with the corresponding timestep.
观测与动作历史：观测与动作历史是由环境和智能体自身的近期消息构成的滚动序列。观测消息包含先前的观测，并标记为“$k$ 步前的观测”；动作消息则包含智能体先前采取的动作。启用记忆和通信后，观测历史还会包含暂存笔记和队友消息，每条内容均标有对应的时间步。
Current Observation and Action Space: The current observation message contains textual descriptions of: achievement progress, current level, nearby terrain, items and enemies, visible coordination opportunities and requirements, teammate status, agent stats, vitals and inventory. The agent is then prompted to respond with an action. By default, the call to action prompt also includes current legal action affordances, instructions to think step-by-step before acting, to optionally broadcast a message, and to optionally write a private scratchpad note.
当前观测与动作空间：当前观测消息包含以下内容的文本描述：成就进度、当前层级、附近的地形、物品和敌人、可见的协调机会与要求、队友状态、智能体属性、生存指标及物品栏。随后，提示词会要求智能体以一个动作作答。默认情况下，要求采取行动的提示词还包括当前可执行的合法动作选项，以及在行动前逐步思考、可选择广播一条消息和可选择撰写一条私有暂存笔记的指令。
The length of observation, communication, and memory history is independently configurable, and the system prompt can be ablated to control how much information about the game and coordination mechanics is disclosed to the model. The default setting includes all information in the system prompt and current observation with communication and scratchpad history enabled, but we ablate these components to probe their effects on LLM performance in 4.2.2.
观测、通信和记忆历史的长度可独立配置，还可对系统提示词进行消融，以控制向模型披露多少有关游戏机制和协调机制的信息。默认设置在系统提示和当前观测中包含所有信息，并启用通信和暂存区历史记录，但我们在 4.2.2 中对这些组成部分进行消融，以探究它们对 LLM 性能的影响。
Prompt Disclosure Levels.提示信息披露级别。
In order to reduce the amount of information given to the model, we only reveal specific gameplay information as the team reaches the relevant level.
为减少提供给模型的信息量，我们仅在团队达到相应关卡时披露特定的游戏信息。
Coordination Information.协调信息。
The current observation includes explicit natural language descriptions of the current coordination targets and requirements. For synchronous targets, the prompt states whether the target works solo with a bonus or requires $N$ agents simultaneously, what the required action is, and which side of the target the acting agent currently occupies. The location and vitals of teammates are included as well as their previous messages.
当前观测包含对当前协调目标及要求的明确自然语言描述。对于同步目标，提示会说明该目标是否可由单个智能体完成并获得额外奖励，或是否需要 $N$ 个智能体同时参与，以及所需的动作和执行动作的智能体当前位于目标的哪一侧。其中还包含队友的位置、生命体征及其先前的消息。
Action Parsing and Execution.动作解析与执行。
The model response is instructed to respond in a structured format containing exactly one action tag, optionally preceded by a think tag for reasoning, communication tags for teammate messages, and scratchpad tags for private notes. The required action is parsed from the response and must match one of the legal actions. If parsing fails, the agent is re-prompted with a format error. If the model still fails to produce a valid action after the configured retry budget is exhausted, the action defaults to Noop. The final parsed action is mapped back to the underlying discrete action index before stepping the environment.
模型被要求以结构化格式作答，其中恰好包含一个 action 标签，其前可选择性地添加用于推理的 think 标签、用于向队友发送消息的 communication 标签，以及用于记录私人笔记的 scratchpad 标签。从回复中解析出所需动作，且该动作必须与某个合法动作匹配。如果解析失败，则会向智能体重新发送提示，告知其存在格式错误。如果配置的重试次数耗尽后，模型仍未能生成有效动作，则该动作默认为 Noop。在推进环境一步之前，将最终解析出的动作映射回底层离散动作索引。

Three patterns emerge.
呈现出三种模式。
Gemini communicates more like a planner.Gemini 的沟通方式更像规划者。
Gemini sends longer and more directive messages than Gemma. Its messages contain about $27\%$ more characters, about $24\%$ more sentences, and more imperative verbs per message ( $1.80$ vs. $1.06$ ). They also contain more self-references ( $1.65$ vs. $0.92$ per message). In the trajectories, this often looks like a model maintaining a team plan: Gemini states what it is doing, assigns actions to particular teammates, and marks when the next coordinated step should happen. Gemma’s messages are shorter and more often read as local status updates.
与 Gemma 相比，Gemini 发送的消息更长，指令性也更强。其消息的字符数约多 $27\%$，句子数约多 $24\%$，每条消息中的祈使动词也更多（$1.80$ 对 $1.06$）。其中还包含更多自我指涉（每条消息 $1.65$ 次，对比 $0.92$ 次）。在运行轨迹中，这往往表现为模型在维护一份团队计划：Gemini 说明自己正在做什么，为特定队友分配行动，并标明下一步协同行动应在何时进行。Gemma 的消息更短，读起来也更常像是局部状态更新。
The channel is mostly used to broadcast intent, not to deliberate.该通道主要用于广播意图，而非商议。
The two models differ in tone. Gemini uses direct commands and exclamation marks more often. Gemma more often uses polite or urgent language: please/thanks appears in $20.9\%$ of Gemma messages compared with $14.0\%$ for Gemini, and urgency words appear in $21.2\%$ compared with $14.1\%$ . The shared pattern is equally important. Both models address a specific teammate at similar rates ( $43.4\%$ vs. $44.1\%$ ), while questions are rare for both ( $1.6\%$ and $1.7\%$ ). Thus, agents mainly use communication to announce intent, request help, and assign actions, rather than to conduct back-and-forth discussion.
这两个模型的语气有所不同。Gemini 更常使用直接命令和感叹号。Gemma 更常使用礼貌或表达紧迫性的语言：please/thanks 出现在 $20.9\%$ 的 Gemma 消息中，而 Gemini 的这一比例为 $14.0\%$；表达紧迫性的词语出现的比例为 $21.2\%$，相比之下 Gemini 为 $14.1\%$。两者共有的模式同样重要。两个模型向特定队友发话的比例相近（$43.4\%$ 对 $44.1\%$），而两者都很少提问（分别为 $1.6\%$ 和 $1.7\%$）。因此，智能体主要通过沟通来宣布意图、请求帮助和分配行动，而非进行来回讨论。
Communication style mirrors scratchpad style.沟通风格与草稿区风格相呼应。
The message channel reflects the same model-specific tendencies seen in the scratchpads. Planning words appear in $59.1\%$ of Gemini messages but only $27.0\%$ of Gemma messages; in scratchpads, the corresponding rates are $75.3\%$ and $36.6\%$ . The channels are not redundant: Gemma’s messages are more coordinate-heavy than its scratchpads, while Gemini’s messages are less coordinate-heavy than its scratchpads. Still, the overall pattern is consistent. Gemini uses its scratchpad as a private plan and its messages as outward-facing instructions. Gemma uses its scratchpad more like a state snapshot and its messages more like status reports or requests for help.
消息通道反映出与草稿区中相同的模型特有倾向。规划类词语出现在 $59.1\%$ 的 Gemini 消息中，而在 Gemma 消息中仅为 $27.0\%$；在草稿区中，相应比例为 $75.3\%$ 和 $36.6\%$。这些通道并非相互冗余：Gemma 的消息比其草稿区更侧重坐标信息，而 Gemini 的消息比其草稿区更少侧重坐标信息。不过，总体模式是一致的。Gemini 将草稿区用作私有计划，将消息用作对外指令。Gemma 使用草稿区的方式更像是记录状态快照，而使用消息的方式更像是报告状态或请求帮助。
