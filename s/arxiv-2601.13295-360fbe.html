摘要Abstract
解决团队冲突不仅需要具备特定任务所需的能力，还需要具备社会智能，以寻找共同点并建立共识。 Resolving team conflicts requires not only task-specific competence, but also social intelligence to find common ground and build consensus. 同样，随着 AI 智能体越来越多地协作完成复杂工作，它们必须发展协调能力，才能成为有效的队友。 Similarly, as AI agents increasingly collaborate on complex work, they must develop coordination capabilities to function as effective teammates. 然而，我们假设当前的智能体缺乏这些能力。 Yet we hypothesize that current agents lack these capabilities. 为检验这一假设，我们提出了 CooperBench，这是一个涵盖 4 种编程语言、12 个库中超过 600 项协作编程任务的基准。 To test this hypothesis, we introduce CooperBench, a benchmark of over 600 collaborative coding tasks across 12 libraries in 4 programming languages. 每项任务为两个智能体分配不同的功能，这些功能可以独立实现，但如果缺乏适当协调，就可能产生冲突。 Each task assigns two agents different features that can be implemented independently but may conflict without proper coordination. 这些任务基于真实的开源代码仓库，并配有专家编写的测试。 Tasks are grounded in real open-source repositories with expert-written tests. 通过评估最先进的编程智能体，我们观察到了协调的诅咒（curse of coordination）：在所有任务难度范围内，与单个智能体独自完成两项任务相比，智能体协同工作时的成功率平均降低了 30%。 Evaluating state-of-the-art coding agents, we observe the curse of coordination: agents achieve on average 30% lower success rates when working together compared to performing both tasks individually, across the full spectrum of task difficulties. 这与人类团队形成鲜明对比：增加队友通常会提高而非降低生产力。 This contrasts sharply with human teams, where adding teammates typically improves rather than diminishes productivity. 我们的分析揭示了三个关键问题：(1) 沟通渠道被含糊、时机不当且不准确的消息堵塞；(2) 即使沟通有效，智能体也会偏离自己的承诺；(3) 智能体常常对其他智能体的计划、观察结果和沟通抱有错误预期。 Our analysis reveals three key issues: (1) communication channels become jammed with vague, ill-timed, and inaccurate messages; (2) even with effective communication, agents deviate from their commitments; and (3) agents often hold incorrect expectations about others’ plans, observations, and communication. 除这些问题之外，通过大规模模拟，我们还观察到了智能体之间罕见但有趣的涌现协调行为，包括角色分工、资源划分和协商。 Besides these issues, through large-scale simulation, we also observe rare but interesting emergent coordination behavior between agents including role division, resource division, and negotiation. 我们的研究不仅提出了一个用于协作编程的新基准，还呼吁研究重心从追求单个智能体的能力转向发展社会智能，即理解他者、有效沟通和协调行动的能力。 Our research not only presents a novel benchmark for collaborative coding, but also calls for a research shift from pursuing individual agent capability to developing social intelligence: the ability to understand others, communicate effectively, and coordinate actions.
1 引言1 Introduction
从大教堂的建造到开源软件的开发，现代文明的大多数成就都源于个体之间的合作 (Raymond, 1999; Woolley et al., 2010)。 Most achievements in modern civilization arise from individuals working cooperatively, from the construction of cathedrals to the development of open-source software (Raymond, 1999; Woolley et al., 2010). 在人类社会中，这种合作依赖于社会智能：传达意图、理解他人的目标，以及通过协商达成彼此兼容的解决方案的能力 (Humphrey, 1976)。 In human societies, such cooperation relies on social intelligence: the ability to communicate intentions, understand others’ goals, and negotiate mutually compatible solutions (Humphrey, 1976). 这种能力通常被视为人类独特性的来源，也是人类思维的基础 (Tomasello, 2014)。 This capability is often viewed as what makes us uniquely human and the basis of human thinking (Tomasello, 2014). 随着我们将 AI 智能体部署到合作场景中，强大的个体能力能否转化为与人类或其他智能体有效合作的能力，仍是一个悬而未决的问题。 As we deploy AI agents in cooperative settings, whether strong individual capabilities translate to effective cooperation with either humans or agents remains an open question. 本文通过实证表明，当前的 AI 系统存在协调的诅咒：在总工作量相同的情况下，多个智能体合作的表现远逊于单个智能体。 In this paper, we empirically demonstrate that for current AI systems, there is a curse of coordination: agent cooperation perform much worse than a single agent given the same total workload. 这一缺陷构成了部署能够与人类或其他智能体共同工作的 AI 系统的根本障碍。 This deficit presents a fundamental barrier to deploying AI systems that can work alongside humans or other agents. 我们从理论上提出，有效的人类–AI 合作与智能体–智能体合作在根本上依赖于相同的协调能力。 We theorize that at a fundamental level, effective human–AI and agent–agent cooperation rely on the same coordination abilities.
现有关于人类任务自动化和多智能体系统的研究，大多通过提供更多支撑框架 (Fourney et al., 2024a; Pan et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025b; Zhuge et al., 2024)、强制执行严格的工作流程 (Hong et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2024; Cheng et al., 2025)，或提供主动监督与验证 (Huang et al., 2025; Xiang et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025)，来绕过这一挑战。 Existing research on automating human tasks and multi-agent systems largely sidesteps this challenge by either providing more scaffolds (Fourney et al., 2024a; Pan et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025b; Zhuge et al., 2024), enforcing strict workflows (Hong et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2024; Cheng et al., 2025), or providing active supervision and verification (Huang et al., 2025; Xiang et al., 2025; Zheng et al., 2025). 这些系统依赖开发者或用户提供的支撑框架来管理协调，这限制了灵活合作，并给人类增加了额外负担。 These systems rely on developer- or user-provided scaffolding to manage coordination, which limits flexible cooperation and places additional burden on humans.
我们提出了 CooperBench，这是首个旨在衡量智能体在处理存在潜在冲突的各自任务时合作能力的基准。 We present CooperBench, the first benchmark designed to measure how well agents can cooperate when handling individual tasks with potential conflicts. 考虑到软件工程是一个现实领域，人们通常需要在团队中协调开展工作 (Purna Sudhakar et al., 2011)，我们的基准为智能体合作是否成功提供了可验证的评估。 Considering software engineering as a realistic domain where humans typically need to navigate work in a team (Purna Sudhakar et al., 2011), our benchmark offers verifiable evaluation for the success of agent cooperation. 如图所示，CooperBench 包含 652 项任务，这些任务基于涵盖 Python、TypeScript、Go 和 Rust 的 12 个流行开源库构建。 As illustrated in Fig. , CooperBench comprises 652 tasks constructed from 12 popular open-source libraries across Python, TypeScript, Go, and Rust. 本文中具有实际软件工程背景的八位共同作者为这些库创建了新功能、单元测试和标准参考代码，确保任务设计具有高质量和真实性。 Eight co-authors of this paper with real-world software engineering backgrounds created new features, unit tests, and ground-truth code for these libraries, ensuring high-quality and realistic task design.
在 CooperBench 中，每项任务都为每个智能体分配一个功能，要求它们基于相同的代码仓库状态进行实现。 In CooperBench, each task assigns each agent a feature to be implemented based on the same repository state. 冲突被有意设置在代码层面，因为所分配的功能在逻辑上彼此兼容，但需要智能体修改相互重叠或相互依赖的代码。 Conflicts are intentionally embedded at the code level, as the assigned features are logically compatible but require agents to modify overlapping or interdependent code. 例如，在图中，一个智能体在序列化过程中实现图像的可变性，另一个智能体则为同一过程添加备份功能。 For example, in Fig. , one agent implements image mutability in the serialization process while another adds backup functionality to the same process. 如果不了解彼此的目标、计划和预期，它们的解决方案就可能引入不兼容的改动。 Without understanding each other’s goals, plans, and expectations, their solutions may introduce incompatible changes. 这与现实世界中的软件开发情况相似，其中协调失败源于相互理解不足。 This mirrors real-world software development where coordination failures stem from insufficient mutual understanding. CooperBench 使我们能够研究三个研究问题： CooperBench enables us to investigate three research questions:

通过在 CooperBench 上评估最先进的编程智能体，我们观察到了协调的诅咒：基于 GPT-5 和 Claude Sonnet 4.5 的智能体在 CooperBench 上进行双智能体合作时，成功率仅为 25%，比使用单个智能体实现两项功能的“Solo”基线低约 50%。 Through evaluating state-of-the-art coding agents on CooperBench, we observe the curse of coordination: GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 based agents achieve only 25% with two-agent cooperation on CooperBench, which is around 50% lower than a “Solo” baseline which uses one agent to implement both features.
通过深入分析协调失败，我们识别出三个关键问题。 Diving deeper into the coordination failures, we identify three key issues. 首先，通信渠道被含糊、时机不当且不准确的消息堵塞：智能体未能回答直接提出的问题，发送的消息到达过晚，无法为决策提供参考，或者用缺乏可操作细节的重复状态更新淹没通信渠道。 First, communication channels become jammed with vague, ill-timed, and inaccurate messages where agents fail to respond to direct questions, send messages that arrive too late to inform decisions, or flood channels with repetitive status updates that lack actionable detail. 其次，即使通信有效，智能体也会偏离其承诺。 Second, even with effective communication, agents deviate from their commitments. 它们对代码状态作出无法验证的断言，忽视商定的集成点，并违背明确作出的承诺。 They make unverifiable claims about code state, ignore agreed-upon integration points, and break explicit promises. 第三，智能体对合作伙伴的计划和观察结果抱有错误预期，无视警告而重复开展工作，并覆盖它们认为能够顺利合并的改动 (§6)。 Third, agents hold incorrect expectations about their partner’s plans, observations and duplicate work despite warnings and overwrite changes they believe will merge cleanly (§6).
除了失败之外，我们也很高兴地报告一些涌现出的协调行为，这些行为往往促成 CooperBench 任务的成功。 Besides failures, we are excited to report emergent coordination behaviors which often lead to the success of the CooperBench tasks. 智能体很少表现出这些协调行为，但通过大规模模拟，我们发现了其中的三大类别：角色分工、资源划分和协商 (§6.4)。 These coordination behaviors are rarely performed by the agents, but through our large-scale simulation, we uncover three major categories of them: role division, resource division, and negotiations (§6.4). 这些例子提示了一条通过强化在 CooperBench 上的成功表现来获得协调能力的路径。 These examples hint at a path of coordination capability acquisition through reinforcing success on CooperBench.
我们的贡献既包括对智能体需要具备哪些条件才能成为有效队友的新认识，也包括用于衡量进展的实用基准。 We contribute both a novel understanding of what agents need to become effective teammates and a practical benchmark for measuring progress. 我们开源的 CooperBench 平台使研究人员和实践者能够评估和改进协作式编程智能体。 Our open-sourced CooperBench platform enables researchers and practitioners to evaluate and improve cooperative coding agents.

2.2 评估流程2.2 Evaluation pipeline
合作难以评估，但我们使合作的产出可以得到验证。 Cooperation is hard to evaluate, but we make the product of the cooperation verifiable. CooperBench 根据两项标准评估任务：(1) 解决方案的兼容性和 (2) 实现的正确性。 CooperBench evaluates tasks based on two criteria: (1) compatible solutions and (2) implementation correctness.
解决方案的兼容性 两个智能体执行完毕后，我们尝试使用 git merge-file -L patch_1 -L repo_state -L patch_2 合并它们生成的补丁。 Solution compatibility After the two agents complete execution, we attempt to merge their resulting patches using git merge-file -L patch_1 -L repo_state -L patch_2. 该操作用于判断独立生成的解决方案在结构上是否兼容。 This operation captures whether the independently produced solutions are structurally compatible. 在实践中，一些合并失败源于格式或缩进风格等表面差异（例如 K&R 与 Allman 风格的差异），而非实质性冲突。 In practice, some merge failures arise from superficial differences such as formatting or indentation styles (e.g., K&R versus Allman) rather than substantive conflicts. 为避免将此类情况视为协调失败，我们使用合成示例训练了一个小型编程模型（Qwen 3 Coder 1.5B; Yang et al. 2025），以便在标准合并失败时解决简单的合并冲突。 To avoid treating such cases as coordination failures, we train a small coding model (Qwen 3 Coder 1.5B; Yang et al. 2025) on synthetic examples to resolve trivial merge conflicts when standard merging fails. 这一步确保兼容性检查反映的是解决方案之间在语义上的一致性，而非底层风格上的差异，同时基本不影响总体合作得分（App. § B）。 This step ensures that the compatibility check reflects semantic agreement between solutions rather than low-level stylistic discrepancies, while leaving the overall cooperation score largely unaffected (App. § B). 如果连该编程模型也无法生成无冲突的补丁，则两个智能体均被判定为任务失败。 If even the coding model cannot produce a patch without conflicts, the agents both fail the tasks.
实现的正确性 如果我们成功将两个补丁合并到代码仓库状态中，就会在合并后的代码库上运行两组单元测试。 Implementation correctness If we successfully merge the two patches into the repository state, we run both sets of unit tests on the merged codebase. 如前所述，我们不限制智能体只能完成各自的工作。 As mentioned before, we do not restrict agents to only finish their own work. 如果它们能够良好协调，就可以以不同的方式分工实现这两项功能，只要合并后的解决方案能够通过这两项功能的测试即可。 If they can coordinate well, they can divide their two features in a different way as long as the merged solution can pass the two features’ tests. 这一评估流程确保了对合作结果的严格评估。 This evaluation pipeline ensures a rigorous evaluation of the cooperation outcome.

空间协调与语义协调。Spatial vs. semantic coordination.
这些发现解释了为什么通信有助于减少冲突，却无助于提高成功率。 These findings explain why communication helps conflicts but not success. 合并冲突本质上是一个空间协调问题：智能体必须就由谁编辑哪些行达成一致。 Merge conflicts are fundamentally a spatial coordination problem: agents must agree on who edits which lines. 上述模式（早期规划、具体行号、文件路径）都针对空间协调，而且确实有效。 The patterns above (early planning, specific line numbers, file paths) all address spatial coordination, and they work.
然而，任务成功需要语义协调：理解要实现什么，而不仅仅是在哪里实现。 However, task success requires semantic coordination: understanding what to implement, not just where. 附录 I 中的案例研究展示了这一差距。 Our case study in Appendix I illustrates this gap. 两个智能体成功协调了行号和编辑范围（空间层面），却因从未讨论各自实现应使用的实际参数值（语义层面）而失败。 Two agents successfully coordinated on line numbers and edit ranges (spatial), yet failed because they never discussed the actual parameter values their implementations should use (semantic). 它们解决了避免编辑重叠这一“格式”问题，却未解决确保各自实现相互兼容这一“设计”问题。 They solved the “formatting” problem of avoiding overlapping edits but not the “design” problem of ensuring compatible implementations.

本节表明，智能体频繁使用通信工具，却未能恰当地利用该工具进行协调。 In this section, we show that the communication tool is heavily used, but not properly leveraged by agents for coordination. 这表明，智能体缺乏对语言至关重要的语用理解：通信不仅是传递消息，更是通过传递消息来实现特定功能。 This shows that agents lack the critical pragmatics understanding of language: communication is not just about message passing, but about achieving certain functions through passing the messages. 智能体虽然“说”了很多，但当通信信道被重复内容、未获回应的问题或虚假信息堵塞时，它们就无法通过通信实现沟通目标。 Agents are “talking” a lot, but they cannot achieve their communication goals through communication when communication channel is jammed with repetitions, unresponded questions, or false information.

6.4 涌现的协调行为6.4 Emergent Coordination Behavior
在成功的运行中，我们观察到一些在失败案例中基本不存在的协调模式。 Among successful runs, we observe coordination patterns that are largely absent from failures. 这些行为并非由提示词引导，也并非由预设的辅助框架促成；它们是在智能体成功应对部分可观测性时涌现的。 These behaviors are not prompted or scaffolded; they emerge when agents successfully navigate partial observability. 它们的共同之处在于，从模糊的意图转向具体的承诺，使协作伙伴即使看不到实际工作，也能核实这些承诺。 What they share is a shift from vague intentions to specific commitments that a partner can verify even without seeing the underlying work. 我们识别出三种此类模式。 We identify three such patterns.
角色分工。Role division.
智能体就各自负责的任务部分达成一致，并明确各自职责范围的边界。 Agents agree on who handles which part of the task and establish clear boundaries around their scope.
成功的角色分工的关键特征是相互确认。 What distinguishes successful role division is mutual confirmation. 在部分可观测条件下，单方面的声明很容易被忽略或误解。 Under partial observability, a unilateral declaration can easily be missed or misunderstood. 当两个智能体都明确确认分工时，它们就建立了经过核实的共同理解，双方在独立工作时均可据此行事。 When both agents explicitly acknowledge the split, they create verified shared understanding that both sides can rely on during independent work.
资源划分。Resource division.
智能体通过划分共享资源来避免冲突，最常见的划分对象是特定文件、代码范围或归属区块。 Agents avoid collisions by partitioning shared resources, most commonly specific files, code ranges, or ownership blocks.
资源划分之所以有效，关键在于其具体性。 What makes resource division effective is specificity. 模糊的承诺无法核实，因此需要依赖信任。 Vague commitments cannot be verified and thus require trust. 相比之下，行级边界划定了安全区域，其划分方式本身就保证了冲突不可能发生。 Line-level boundaries, by contrast, create safe zones where conflict is impossible by construction.
协商。Negotiation.
智能体通过提出替代方案，并在行动前就单一计划达成一致，来解决不同方法之间的冲突。 Agents resolve conflicting approaches by proposing alternatives and converging on a single plan before acting.
有效的协商会替双方承担认知工作。 Effective negotiation does cognitive work for both parties. 通过提出互斥的选项，并在每个选项中完整明确各智能体将采取的行动，一个智能体就能将复杂的协调问题简化为一个简单的选择。 By proposing mutually exclusive options that fully specify what each agent will do, one agent reduces a complex coordination problem to a simple choice. 其结果不仅是意图上的一致，更是完整的行动规范，不留下任何需要自行解读的余地。 The result is not just agreement on intent but complete action specifications that leave nothing to interpret.
这些协调模式在我们的轨迹记录中很少见，但它们在成功案例中的出现表明，智能体具备相应的潜在能力。 These coordination patterns are rare in our traces but their presence in successful cases suggests that the underlying capability exists. 挑战不在于教会智能体新的协调技能，而在于使其已有技能变得可靠。 The challenge is not teaching agents new coordination skills but making existing ones reliable.

SWE-agent (Yang et al., 2024)、OpenHands (Wang et al., 2025) 和 Agentless (Xia et al., 2024) 等使用工具的编码智能体在 SWE-bench (Jimenez et al., 2024) 上取得了优异成绩。 Tool-using coding agents such as SWE-agent (Yang et al., 2024), OpenHands (Wang et al., 2025), and Agentless (Xia et al., 2024) achieve strong results on SWE-bench (Jimenez et al., 2024). 然而，这些评估衡量的是单智能体的成功情况，而非多个对等智能体能否在信息不完全条件下无冲突地整合更改。 However, these evaluations measure single-agent success rather than whether multiple peers can integrate changes without conflict under partial information.

心智理论评估Theory of Mind evaluation
有效的协调需要对协作伙伴的信念和意图进行建模，这通常被称为心智理论（Theory of Mind）(Premack & Woodruff, 1978; Rabinowitz et al., 2018; Zhu et al., 2021)。 Effective coordination requires modeling partner beliefs and intentions, which commonly referred to Theory of Mind (Premack & Woodruff, 1978; Rabinowitz et al., 2018; Zhu et al., 2021). ToMBench (Chen et al., 2024)、FANToM (Kim et al., 2023) 和 SoMi-ToM (Fan et al., 2025) 对大语言模型（LLM）的心智理论能力进行了评估，发现其表现与人类相比存在显著差距。 ToMBench (Chen et al., 2024), FANToM (Kim et al., 2023), and SoMi-ToM (Fan et al., 2025) evaluate theory of mind in LLMs, finding substantial gaps versus human performance. ToMSWE (Zhou et al., 2025) 尝试构建能够推断用户心智理论的编程智能体。 ToMSWE (Zhou et al., 2025) tries to build coding agents which can infer users’ Theory of Mind. 关于合作游戏 (Li et al., 2023b) 和 Generative Agents (Park et al., 2023) 的研究表明，智能体会涌现出社会行为，但将这些行为转化为可验证的协作工作仍面临挑战。 Studies of cooperative games (Li et al., 2023b) and Generative Agents (Park et al., 2023) show emergent social behaviors but also challenges translating these to verifiable collaborative work.
我们将自由形式的协调单独作为评估的核心对象。 We isolate free-form coordination as the central object of evaluation. CooperBench 为两个智能体分配共享代码库中部分重叠的功能，同时隔离各自的工作空间，并将协调方式限制为自然语言。 CooperBench assigns two agents partially overlapping features on a shared codebase while isolating their workspaces and restricting coordination to natural language. 与规定交互结构或仅衡量结果的基准不同，我们通过冗余、假设不一致和语义破坏等协调失败现象开展评估。 Unlike benchmarks that impose interaction structure or measure outcomes alone, we evaluate through coordination failures such as redundancy, inconsistent assumptions, and semantic breakage. 我们在代码集成结果可验证的受控环境中展示了协调诅咒，指出社会智能是智能体实现有效团队协作的瓶颈。 We demonstrate the curse of coordination in a controlled setting with verifiable code integration, pointing to social intelligence as the bottleneck for effective agent teamwork.
8 结论与未来工作8 Conclusion and Future Work
在未来，智能体将与人类在高风险领域协作 (Kim et al., 2025)、加速科学技术研究 (Gottweis et al., 2025)，并助力创造性活动 (Waikar, 2021)；在这样的未来中，很难想象一个缺乏协调能力的智能体能够作出怎样的贡献，无论其个体能力有多强。 In a future where agents team with humans in high-stakes domains (Kim et al., 2025), accelerate science and technology research (Gottweis et al., 2025), and empower creative endeavors (Waikar, 2021), it is hard to imagine how an agent incapable of coordination would contribute to such a future, however strong their individual capabilities.
我们的工作表明，协调能力而非单纯的编程能力，是多智能体软件开发的核心瓶颈。 Our work demonstrates that coordination, not raw coding ability, is a central bottleneck for multi-agent software development. 通过 CooperBench，我们发现，当两个智能体协作时，GPT-5 和 Claude Sonnet 4.5 等前沿模型的成功率仅为 25%，约为单个智能体完成相同工作量时成功率的一半。 Through CooperBench, we show that frontier models like GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 achieve only 25% success when two agents collaborate, roughly half the success rate of a single agent performing the same workload. 这种协调诅咒源于三方面的能力缺口：智能体无法传达可据以采取行动的信息、偏离自身承诺，以及对合作伙伴抱有错误预期。 This curse of coordination stems from three capability gaps: agents fail to communicate actionable information, deviate from their own commitments, and hold incorrect expectations about their partners.
不过，协调并非遥不可及。 Yet coordination is not beyond reach. 在成功的执行轨迹中，我们观察到了角色分工、资源划分和协商等涌现行为，这些行为将模糊的意图转化为可验证的承诺。 In successful traces, we observe emergent behaviors such as role division, resource division, and negotiation that turn vague intentions into verifiable commitments. 这些模式虽然罕见，但其存在表明底层能力已经具备；挑战在于使其可靠地发挥作用。 These patterns are rare but their presence suggests the underlying capability exists; the challenge is making it reliable. 借助 Sotopia- $\pi$ (Wang et al., 2024a; Yu et al., 2025) 等多智能体训练方法，我们可以预期，这些涌现行为将通过合作的成功得到强化。 With multi-agent training methods, e.g. Sotopia- $\pi$ (Wang et al., 2024a; Yu et al., 2025), we can expect these emergent behaviors to be reinforced through the success of cooperation.
我们的发现开辟了若干研究方向：(1) 奖励部分可观测条件下协调行为的训练目标，(2) 支持可验证承诺的轻量级协议（例如，共享签名、插入点契约），以及 (3) 屏幕共享等更丰富的通信渠道，以将交互模态扩展到文本之外。 Our findings open several directions: (1) training objectives that reward coordination under partial observability, (2) lightweight protocols for verifiable commitments (e.g., shared signatures, insertion-point contracts), and (3) richer communication channels such as screen sharing to expand the modality beyond text. 我们将 CooperBench 作为开放基准发布，以衡量这些方向上的进展。 We release CooperBench as an open benchmark to measure progress on these fronts.
尽管我们聚焦于软件开发，但我们的发现可推广至任何在部分可观测条件下涉及角色和资源冲突的领域。 Although we focus on software development, our findings generalize to any domain involving role and resource conflicts under partial observability. 我们预计，在理解他人、有效沟通和协调行动这些能力得到有针对性的培养之前，社会智能的缺乏仍将是限制智能体作为团队伙伴在现实世界中部署的根本障碍。 We expect that the lack of social intelligence, the ability to understand others, communicate effectively, and coordinate actions, will remain a fundamental barrier limiting the real-world deployment of agents as teammates until these capabilities are explicitly developed.

附录 C 按难度分层的评估Appendix C Difficulty-stratified evaluation
仅凭原始成功率不足以比较不同模型的协调开销。 Raw success rates are insufficient for comparing coordination overhead across models. 一个模型从 Solo 下的 50% 降至 Coop 下的 30%，与另一个模型从 80% 降至 60% 的差距同为 20 个百分点，但前者损失了 40% 的能力，而后者仅损失了 25%。 A model dropping from 50% Solo to 30% Coop has the same 20-point gap as one dropping from 80% to 60%, but the first loses 40% of its capability while the second loses only 25%. 我们需要一个能够考虑基线差异的指标。 We need a metric that accounts for baseline differences. 我们还希望综合不同任务难度下的表现，而不是依赖会掩盖差异的汇总统计。 We also want to integrate across task difficulty rather than rely on aggregates that mask variation. 本节使用第 4 节定义的相对难度推导这样一个指标。 This section derives such a metric using the relative difficulty defined in Section 4.
我们将任务按归一化难度范围 $[0,1]$ 划分为 10 个等宽区间，并在每个区间的中点处计算成功率，同时给出在接近 0 和 1 时仍保持良好校准的 95% Wilson 置信区间。 We partition tasks into 10 equal-width buckets over the normalized difficulty range $[0,1]$ and compute success rate at each bucket midpoint, with 95% Wilson confidence intervals that remain well-calibrated near 0 and 1. 由此为每个模型得到两条曲线，分别对应 Solo 和 Coop。 This produces two curves per model, one for Solo and one for Coop.
我们通过梯形积分计算曲线下面积（AUC），以概括每条曲线。 We summarize each curve by its area under the curve (AUC) via trapezoidal integration. 绝对差距 $\Delta_{\mathrm{AUC}}=\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Solo}}-\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Coop}}$ 衡量协调成本，但取决于基线。 The absolute gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{AUC}}=\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Solo}}-\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Coop}}$ measures coordination cost but depends on baseline. 因此，我们报告保留率 $=\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Coop}}/\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Solo}}$，以对能力进行归一化。 We therefore report retention $=\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Coop}}/\mathrm{AUC}_{\mathrm{Solo}}$ , which normalizes for capability. 保留率为 0.64 表示协调后保留了 Solo 表现的 64%。 A retention of 0.64 means 64% of Solo performance survives coordination.

附录 G 失效症状标注流程Appendix G Failure Symptom Annotation Procedure
我们遵循了一个六阶段流程，其思路与近期关于多智能体失效分析的研究相似（Cemri et al., 2025）。 We followed a six-stage process, similar in spirit to recent work on multi-agent failure analysis (Cemri et al., 2025). (1) 收集 Collaborative 运行中的多智能体系统（MAS）轨迹；(2) 根据合并后的产物识别失效（例如测试失败或缺少预期行为），并将其追溯至交互过程；(3) 通过迭代式定性编码制定症状类别，并解决分歧，使标注者就一套共同定义达成一致；(4) 确定最终的症状集合；(5) 基于商定的定义校准一个基于 LLM 的标注器；(6) 应用该标注器大规模生成症状标注。 (1) Collect multi-agent-system (MAS) traces from Collaborative runs; (2) identify failures from merged artifacts (e.g., failing tests or missing intended behavior), and link them back to the interaction; (3) develop symptom categories by iterative qualitative coding and resolve disagreements to reach inter-annotator agreement on a shared set of definitions; (4) finalize the resulting symptom set; (5) calibrate an LLM-based annotator on the agreed definitions; and (6) apply the annotator to produce symptom annotations at scale.
每个已标注实例均以三类产物为依据：(i) 对话证据（协调对话），(ii) 补丁/代码证据（各智能体所做的修改），以及 (iii) 结果证据（合并报告和测试输出）。 Each labeled instance is grounded in three artifacts: (i) conversation evidence (the coordination dialogue), (ii) patch/code evidence (what each agent changed), and (iii) outcome evidence (merge reports and test outputs). 我们的标注准则在操作层面作出的一项关键区分是实现失效与协调失效：前者指单个智能体交付了不完整或存在缺陷的代码，无论协调情况如何；后者指只有在结合工作空间隔离条件下各智能体的言论与假设加以考察时，才会显现的失效。 A key operational distinction in our rubric is between implementation failures (an individual agent delivers incomplete/buggy code regardless of coordination) and coordination failures (a breakdown that is only apparent when we consider what agents said and assumed under workspace isolation). 具体而言，只有存在明确的对话证据时，我们才赋予协调失效标签；如果证据仅存在于代码或错误轨迹中，我们默认将其归为实现层面的失效，而不推断发生了协调失效。 Concretely, we require explicit conversation evidence to assign a coordination-failure label; if the only evidence is in the code or error trace, we default to implementation-level failure rather than inferring a coordination breakdown. 我们将最终的症状定义编制为结构化标注准则，其中包括验证要求和常见混淆情形，例如何时应将某种情况判定为“不可验证的声明”或“工作重叠”。 We codified the final symptom definitions as a structured rubric (including verification requirements and common confusions, e.g., when to treat “unverifiable claims” versus “work overlap”). 随后，我们依据这套准则校准了一个基于 LLM 的标注器，并要求其输出结构化标签（一个主要症状及任何次要症状），同时提供简短的支持性证据片段。 We then calibrated an LLM-based annotator on this rubric and required it to emit structured labels (a primary symptom plus any secondary symptoms) together with short supporting evidence snippets.
