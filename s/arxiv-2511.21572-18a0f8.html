摘要Abstract
基于大语言模型（LLM）的多智能体系统已成为一种强大的范式，使自主智能体能够解决复杂任务。 Large language model (LLM)-based multi-agent systems have emerged as a powerful paradigm for enabling autonomous agents to solve complex tasks. 随着这些系统的复杂性不断增加，成本成为实际部署中的重要考量。 As these systems scale in complexity, cost becomes an important consideration for practical deployment. 然而，现有研究很少探讨如何在明确的预算约束下构建多智能体系统。 However, existing work rarely addresses how to structure multi-agent systems under explicit budget constraints. 本文提出 BAMAS，这是一种构建预算感知多智能体系统的新方法。 In this paper, we propose BAMAS, a novel approach for building multi-agent systems with budget awareness. BAMAS 首先通过构建并求解一个兼顾性能与成本的整数线性规划（Integer Linear Programming）问题，选择最优的 LLM 集合。 BAMAS first selects an optimal set of LLMs by formulating and solving an Integer Linear Programming problem that balances performance and cost. 随后，它利用基于强化学习的方法选择交互拓扑，以确定这些 LLM 应如何协作。 It then determines how these LLMs should collaborate by leveraging a reinforcement learning-based method to select the interaction topology. 最后，根据选定的智能体及其协作拓扑，对系统进行实例化并执行。 Finally, the system is instantiated and executed based on the selected agents and their collaboration topology. 我们在三项具有代表性的任务上评估 BAMAS，并将其与当前最先进的智能体构建方法进行比较。 We evaluate BAMAS on three representative tasks and compare it with state-of-the-art agent construction methods. 结果表明，BAMAS 在取得相当性能的同时，可将成本降低高达 86%。 Results show that BAMAS achieves comparable performance while reducing cost by up to 86%.

引言Introduction
在基于 LLM 的多智能体系统中，LLM 充当核心推理引擎，执行感知、通信与协作；此类系统在解决复杂任务方面已展现出卓越能力 (Wu et al. 2024; Hong et al. 2024)。 LLM-based multi-agent systems, in which LLMs serve as core reasoning engines that perceive, communicate, and collaborate, have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in solving complex tasks (Wu et al. 2024; Hong et al. 2024). 通过支持多样化的交互模式，这些系统能够分解问题、生成备选解决方案，并以迭代方式改进输出，从而拓展自动化问题求解的能力边界。 By supporting diverse interaction patterns, these systems can decompose problems, generate alternative solutions, and iteratively refine outputs, thereby pushing the boundaries of automated problem solving.
然而，随着这些系统的复杂性和智能体数量不断增加，成本成为实际部署中的关键考量 (Chen et al. 2025)。 However, as these systems increase in complexity and agent count, cost becomes a critical consideration for practical deployment (Chen et al. 2025). 具体而言，成本主要由 LLM 调用过程中的词元（token）消耗决定。 In particular, the cost is primarily driven by token consumption during LLM calls. 单项任务可能需要多个智能体合计进行数十次 LLM 调用，而成本会随协作拓扑和推理深度的不同而发生难以预测的增长。 A single task may require dozens of LLM calls across multiple agents, with costs scaling unpredictably depending on the collaboration topology and reasoning depth. 这种成本的不可预测性使这些系统难以在生产环境中可靠地扩展规模。 Such unpredictability in cost makes these systems difficult to scale reliably in production.
尽管预算管理至关重要，现有研究仍主要关注性能最大化，往往事后才考虑成本。 Despite the critical role of budget management, existing research has predominantly focused on maximizing performance, often treating cost as an afterthought. AutoGen (Wu et al. 2024) 和 MetaGPT (Hong et al. 2024) 等领先框架通常采用被动响应策略，对成本与性能之间权衡的控制能力有限。 Leading frameworks such as AutoGen (Wu et al. 2024) and MetaGPT (Hong et al. 2024) typically employ reactive strategies that provide limited control over the cost-performance trade-off. 据我们所知，此前尚无研究直接探讨这一根本问题：如何设计一个既能实现出色任务性能，又能遵守预设成本预算的多智能体系统？ To our knowledge, no prior work directly addresses the fundamental question: how can we design a multi-agent system that delivers strong task performance while adhering to a predefined cost budget?
为应对这一挑战，我们提出 BAMAS，这是一种构建预算感知多智能体系统的新方法。 To address this challenge, we propose BAMAS, a novel approach for constructing budget-aware multi-agent systems. 我们的核心洞见是，由于 LLM 是成本的主要来源，有效的预算管理应首先将 LLM 分配视为一个约束优化问题。 Our key insight is that since LLMs are the primary drivers of cost, effective budget management should start by treating LLM allocation as a constrained optimization problem. 因此，BAMAS 首先通过构建并求解一个整数线性规划问题，选择最优的 LLM 集合，以同时保证性能并严格遵守给定预算。 Accordingly, BAMAS begins with selecting an optimal set of LLMs by formulating and solving an Integer Linear Programming problem that ensures both performance and strict adherence to the given budget. 接下来，BAMAS 通过强化学习训练拓扑选择策略，学习如何组织选定 LLM 之间的协作。 Next, BAMAS learns how to orchestrate collaboration among the selected LLMs by training a topology selection policy via reinforcement learning. 该策略识别最有效的协作拓扑，例如线性结构或星形结构，以在已分配的资源范围内最大化任务性能。 This policy identifies the most effective collaboration topology, such as linear or star structures, that maximizes task performance within the allocated resources. 最后，BAMAS 根据选定的 LLM 池和协作拓扑实例化多智能体系统，有策略地权衡成本与性能。 Finally, BAMAS instantiates the multi-agent system based on the chosen LLM pool and collaboration topology, strategically balancing the cost-performance trade-off.
我们在涵盖代码生成和数学推理的三个广泛使用的基准数据集上评估 BAMAS，并将其与三种当前最先进的多智能体构建方法进行比较。 We evaluate BAMAS on three widely-used benchmark datasets spanning code generation and mathematical reasoning, and compare it against three state-of-the-art multi-agent construction approaches. 结果表明，BAMAS 在取得相当准确率的同时，可将成本降低高达 86%，并遵守预设预算，展现出良好的成本与性能权衡。 Results show that BAMAS achieves comparable accuracy while reducing costs by up to 86% and adhering to predefined budgets, demonstrating a strong cost-performance trade-off. 消融研究进一步证实，我们的联合优化策略能够找到兼顾成本与性能的更优解，从而显著优于贪心基线方法。 An ablation study further confirms that our joint optimization strategy significantly outperforms greedy baselines by identifying superior solutions that balance cost and performance. 我们的分析还表明，BAMAS 学会了自适应地选择协作拓扑：预算紧张时倾向于采用更简单的拓扑，而资源更充裕时则针对特定任务领域调整结构，兼具适应性与可解释性。 Our analysis also reveals that BAMAS learns to adaptively select collaboration topologies: it favors simpler topologies under tight budgets, while tailoring its structure to specific task domains when resources are more ample, offering both adaptability and interpretability.

方法Methodology
我们将核心问题表述如下：给定任务 $T$、可用 LLM 集合 $\mathcal{A}$ 和成本预算 $B$，目标是在预算约束下设计一个多智能体系统的结构，实现良好的性能与成本权衡。 We formulate the core problem as follows: given a task $T$ , a set of available LLMs $\mathcal{A}$ , and a cost budget $B$ , the goal is to structure a multi-agent system that achieves a favorable performance–cost trade-off under the budget constraint. 具体而言，我们旨在确定一个最优的 LLM 子集 $\mathcal{P}\subseteq\mathcal{A}$，以及一个用于规定其交互方式的协作拓扑 $t$。 Specifically, we aim to determine an optimal subset of LLMs $\mathcal{P}\subseteq\mathcal{A}$ and a collaboration topology $t$ that governs their interactions. $\mathcal{P}$ 中每个被选中的 LLM 都作为系统中的一个独立智能体。 Each selected LLM in $\mathcal{P}$ functions as a distinct agent in the system.
为解决这一问题，我们提出了 BAMAS，这是一种用于设计预算感知多智能体系统结构的新方法。 To address this problem, we propose BAMAS, a novel approach for structuring budget-aware multi-agent systems. 如图 1 所示，BAMAS 包含三个关键组件：(1) 预算约束下的 LLM 配置，将最优 LLM 池 $\mathcal{P}$ 的选择建模为整数线性规划（ILP）问题，并通过求解来满足给定的成本预算；(2) 智能体协作拓扑选择，设计一种基于强化学习的方法，以确定有效的智能体交互拓扑 $t$；(3) 智能体实例化，根据选定的 $\mathcal{P}$ 和 $t$ 对多智能体系统进行实例化和编排，以执行任务。 As illustrated in Figure 1, BAMAS consists of three key components: (1) budget-constrained LLM provisioning, which formulates the selection of an optimal LLM pool $\mathcal{P}$ as an Integer Linear Programming (ILP) problem and solves it to satisfy the given cost budget; (2) agent collaboration topology selection, which designs a reinforcement learning-based method to identify an effective agent interaction topology $t$ ; and (3) agent instantiation, where the multi-agent system is instantiated and orchestrated based on the selected $\mathcal{P}$ and $t$ to perform the task.
预算约束下的 LLM 配置Budget-Constrained LLM Provisioning
该组件从可用池 $\mathcal{A}$ 中选择一个 LLM 子集 $\mathcal{P}$，使总成本不超过给定预算 $B$。 This component selects a subset of LLMs $\mathcal{P}$ from the available pool $\mathcal{A}$ such that the total cost does not exceed a given budget $B$ . 实证研究表明，对于复杂推理任务，单个高性能模型的表现通常优于多个较弱模型组成的集成 (Wang et al. 2024)。 Empirical studies have shown that, for complex reasoning tasks, a single high-performance model often outperforms ensembles of weaker models (Wang et al. 2024). 受这一发现启发，我们采用性能优先的选择策略，只要预算允许，就优先选择能力更强的 LLM。 Motivated by this insight, we adopt a performance-first selection strategy that prioritizes stronger LLMs whenever they can be afforded within the budget.
我们将这一选择过程形式化为一个 ILP 问题，旨在成本预算内最大化整体性能。 We formalize the selection as an ILP problem, aiming to maximize overall performance within the cost budget.
为将 LLM 性能转化为可操作的指标，我们将所有可用的 LLM（注意，同一 LLM 可能存在多个实例）按性能划分为不同层级，其中 $\mathcal{A}_{1}$ 表示性能最高的层级，索引越大表示层级越低，直至性能最低的层级 $\mathcal{A}_{L}$。 To operationalize LLM performance, we rank all available LLMs (note that multiple instances of the same LLM may exist) into tiers, with $\mathcal{A}_{1}$ representing the highest-performance tier and higher indices indicating lower tiers, down to $\mathcal{A}_{L}$ as the lowest-performance tier. 我们采用被广泛引用的 LMSys Chatbot Arena Leaderboard (Chiang et al. 2024) 作为性能的代理指标，其中榜单排名越靠前，表示性能越好。 We use the widely referenced LMSys Chatbot Arena Leaderboard (Chiang et al. 2024) as a performance proxy, where a higher leaderboard rank indicates better performance.
第 $i$ 个 LLM $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ 的成本计算如下： The cost of the $i$ -th LLM $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ is computed as: 
| | $c_{i}=T_{in}\cdot P_{in}+T_{out}\cdot P_{out},$ | | (1)
其中，$T_{\text{in}}$ 和 $T_{\text{out}}$ 分别表示任务 $T$ 每次 API 调用的输入和输出词元（token）数量，$P_{\text{in}}$、$P_{\text{out}}$ 为 LLM 供应商提供的相应单词元价格。 where $T_{\text{in}}$ and $T_{\text{out}}$ denote the input and output token counts per API call for task $T$ , and $P_{\text{in}}$ , $P_{\text{out}}$ are the corresponding per-token prices provided by the LLM vendor. 由于执行前无法获知确切的词元用量，我们采用具有代表性的估计值。 Since exact token usage is unknown before execution, we adopt representative estimates. 具体而言，我们将 $T_{\text{in}}=500$，其依据是先前的测量表明，典型的 LLM 输入长度在 128 $\sim$ 256 个词元之间 (Li et al. 2025a)，并将上限大致翻倍（256 $\rightarrow$ 500），以容纳多智能体场景中智能体可能从其他智能体接收的额外上下文；我们通过从目标数据集中抽取 50 个训练样本，并将观测到的最大输出长度作为上限，来确定 $T_{\text{out}}$。 Specifically, we set $T_{\text{in}}=500$ , based on prior measurements showing that typical LLM input lengths fall within 128 $\sim$ 256 tokens (Li et al. 2025a), and we roughly double the upper bound (256 $\rightarrow$ 500) to accommodate additional context an agent may ingest from others in multi-agent settings; we determine $T_{\text{out}}$ by sampling 50 training instances from the target dataset and using the maximum observed output length as an upper bound.
随后，我们为每个 LLM 层级构建决策权重 $W_{i}$，以引导整数线性规划（ILP）求解器优先选择较高层级的 LLM。 We then construct a decision weight $W_{i}$ for each LLM tier to guide the ILP solver in prioritizing higher-tier LLMs. 这些权重以自底向上的方式递归定义，以确保选择较高层级 LLM 的权重始终超过预算范围内任何可行的较低层级 LLM 组合。 These weights are defined recursively in a bottom-up manner to ensure that selecting a higher-tier LLM always outweighs any budget-feasible combination of lower-tier LLMs. 具体而言，我们将最低层级的权重设为 $W_{L}=1$，并按如下方式计算任意较高层级 $i$ 的权重： Specifically, we set the weight for the lowest tier as $W_{L}=1$ , and compute the weight for any higher tier $i$ as: 
| | $W_{i}=1+\sum_{j=i+1}^{L}\left(W_{j}\cdot\left\lfloor\frac{B}{c_{j}}\right\rfloor\right).$ | | (2)
这种构造保证了层级 $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ 中任意单个 LLM 的权重都高于预算 $B$ 内可负担的任意一组较低层级 LLM 的总权重。 This construction guarantees that any single LLM from tier $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ has a higher total weight than any group of lower-tier LLMs that can be afforded under the budget $B$ .
设 $x_{ij}$ 为二元决策变量，其中 $x_{ij}=1$ 表示选择第 $i$ 层级 LLM $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ 的第 $j$ 个实例。 Let $x_{ij}$ be a binary decision variable, where $x_{ij}=1$ indicates selecting the $j$ -th instance of the $i$ -th tier LLM $\mathcal{A}_{i}$ . 这允许选择同一 LLM 的多个实例。 This allows that multiple instances of the same LLM are selected. 目标是在满足预算约束 $B$ 和配置要求的条件下，最大化所选 LLM 的总权重；具体而言，为确保多智能体配置具有实际意义，必须至少选择两个 LLM。 The objective is to maximize the total weight of the selected LLMs, subject to the budget constraint $B$ and configuration requirement—specifically, to ensure a meaningful multi-agent setup, at least two LLMs must be selected. 由此得到的 ILP 表述如下： The resulting ILP formulation is: 
| | | $\displaystyle\underset{x}{\text{maximize}}$ | | $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{L}\sum_{j}W_{i}\cdot x_{ij},$ | | (3)
| | | $\displaystyle\text{subject to}$ | | $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{L}\sum_{j}c_{i}\cdot x_{ij}\leq B,$ | $\displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{L}\sum_{j}x_{ij}\geq 2.$ |
求解该 ILP 即可得到满足预算约束的候选 LLM 池 $\mathcal{P}$。 Solving this ILP yields the budget-feasible candidate LLM pool $\mathcal{P}$ . 由于 $W_{i}$ 的结构，所选配置可保证在字典序意义下最优，从而在预算范围内最大化整体性能。 Due to the structure of $W_{i}$ , the selected configuration is guaranteed to be lexicographically optimal, maximizing overall performance within the budget. 这为下游组件奠定了坚实基础，确保后续智能体构建基于当前可获得的最佳 LLM 组合进行。 This forms a strong foundation for the downstream components, ensuring that subsequent agent construction operates on the best-available LLM composition.
智能体协作拓扑选择Agent Collaboration Topology Selection
配置组件确定使用哪些 LLM，而本组件决定这些 LLM 应如何协作以解决给定任务。 While the provisioning component determines which LLMs to use, this component decides how these LLMs should collaborate to solve the given task. 固定的协作拓扑难以在多种不同任务上都取得良好表现。 A fixed collaboration topology is unlikely to perform well across a wide variety of tasks. 为此，我们引入拓扑选择策略 $\pi_{\theta}$，以任务描述 $T$ 和总预算 $B$ 为条件，选择合适的智能体协作拓扑 $t\in\mathcal{T}$。 To address this, we introduce a topology selection policy, $\pi_{\theta}$ , that selects an appropriate agent collaboration topology $t\in\mathcal{T}$ , conditioned on the task specification $T$ and the total budget $B$ .
$\pi_{\theta}$ 的动作空间是一个精心筛选的常用智能体工作流拓扑库 $\mathcal{T}$，其中每种拓扑都体现了一种不同的认知或推理策略： The action space of $\pi_{\theta}$ is a curated library $\mathcal{T}$ of widely-used agent workflow topologies, each reflecting a distinct cognitive or reasoning strategy:
线性拓扑：实现顺序推理，每个智能体都在前一个智能体的输出基础上继续处理。 Linear Topology: Implements sequential reasoning, where each agent builds upon the output of the previous one. 这种拓扑非常适合多步推理任务 (Hong et al. 2024; Qian et al. 2024)。 This topology is well-suited for multi-step reasoning tasks (Hong et al. 2024; Qian et al. 2024).
星形拓扑：支持并行生成和评估假设，体现了分而治之的方法，非常适合可分解或需要多视角分析的问题 (Du et al. 2024; Zheng et al. 2023)。 Star Topology: Supports parallel hypothesis generation and evaluation, embodying a divide-and-conquer approach ideal for decomposable or multi-perspective problems (Du et al. 2024; Zheng et al. 2023).
反馈拓扑：通过生成与评判的循环实现迭代改进。 Feedback Topology: Enables iterative refinement through generate-and-critique cycles. 这对于需要自我纠正或提升质量的任务尤为有效 (Madaan et al. 2023; Shinn et al. 2023)。 This is particularly effective for tasks requiring self-correction or quality enhancement (Madaan et al. 2023; Shinn et al. 2023).
规划器驱动拓扑：引入一个中央规划智能体，动态协调其他智能体，从而提供最大的灵活性。 Planner-Driven Topology: Incorporates a central planner agent to dynamically coordinate other agents, offering maximum flexibility. 它尤其适用于没有预定义求解路径的开放式或非结构化任务 (Yao et al. 2023b; Yao et al. 2023a)。 It is especially useful for open-ended or unstructured tasks with no predefined solution path (Yao et al. 2023b; Yao et al. 2023a).
我们采用离线强化学习范式训练拓扑选择策略 $\pi_{\theta}$。 We train the topology selection policy $\pi_{\theta}$ using an offline reinforcement learning paradigm. 之所以采用这一设计，是因为在基于 LLM 的多智能体环境中收集在线轨迹会带来难以承受的成本和延迟。 This design choice is driven by the prohibitive cost and latency associated with collecting online trajectories in LLM-based multi-agent environments. 学习目标是优化策略参数 $\theta$，以最大化执行完整任务轨迹 $\tau$ 所获得的期望最终奖励。 The learning objective is to optimize the policy parameters $\theta$ to maximize the expected final reward derived from executing a complete task trajectory $\tau$ . 形式化地，该目标表示为： Formally, the objective is given by: 
| | $\begin{split}\theta^{*}&=\arg\max_{\theta}J(\theta)\\ &=\arg\max_{\theta}\mathbb{E}_{t\sim\pi_{\theta},\tau\sim\text{Exec}(t,\mathcal{P})}\left[R_{\text{final}}(\tau)\right].\end{split}$ | | (4)
这里，Exec $(t,\mathcal{P})$ 表示智能体执行引擎，它使用先前配置的 LLM 池 $\mathcal{P}$ 实例化并运行所选拓扑 $t$，而 $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ 是一个同时反映任务成功程度和成本效率的复合奖励函数。 Here, Exec $(t,\mathcal{P})$ denotes the agent execution engine that instantiates and runs the chosen topology $t$ using the previously provisioned LLM pool $\mathcal{P}$ , and $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ is a composite reward function reflecting both task success and cost efficiency. 下面，我们将详细介绍复合奖励函数和学习过程。 In the following, we describe the composite reward function and the learning process in detail.
复合奖励函数Composite Reward Function
最终奖励 $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ 被设计为两个不同分量的加权组合。 The final reward $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ is designed as a weighted combination of two distinct components. 权重 $(w_{\text{perf}},w_{\text{cost}})$ 是可调超参数，用于控制最大化任务性能与最小化计算成本之间的权衡： The weights $(w_{\text{perf}},w_{\text{cost}})$ are tunable hyperparameters that govern the trade-off between maximizing task performance and minimizing computational cost: 
| | $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)=w_{\text{perf}}\cdot R_{\text{perf}}+w_{\text{cost}}\cdot R_{\text{cost}}.$ | | (5)
每个奖励分量都发挥着不同但互补的作用： Each reward component serves a different yet complementary role:
任务成功奖励（$R_{\text{perf}}$）：这是一种稀疏的二元奖励，用于评估任务是否得到正确解决。 Task Success Reward ( $R_{\text{perf}}$ ): This is a sparse, binary reward that evaluates whether the task was solved correctly. 它为实现主要目标提供了明确的学习信号：如果任务成功，则 $R_{\text{perf}}=+C_{\text{succ}}$，否则为 $-C_{\text{fail}}$。 It provides a clear learning signal for achieving the primary goal: $R_{\text{perf}}=+C_{\text{succ}}$ if the task succeeds, and $-C_{\text{fail}}$ otherwise.
成本效率奖励（$R_{\text{cost}}$）：该分量旨在促进预算遵守和具有成本效率的解决方案。 Cost Efficiency Reward ( $R_{\text{cost}}$ ): This component promotes budget adherence and cost-efficient solutions. 如果实际成本 $C_{\text{actual}}(\tau)$ 超过预算 $B$，则会施加较重的惩罚 $-C_{\text{overflow}}$。 A heavy penalty $-C_{\text{overflow}}$ is incurred if the actual cost $C_{\text{actual}}(\tau)$ exceeds the budget $B$ . 如果任务成功，还会给予额外奖励 $g(1-C_{\text{actual}}(\tau)/B)$，其大小随节省的预算金额而变化。 If the task is successful, an additional bonus $g(1-C_{\text{actual}}(\tau)/B)$ is granted, which scales with the amount of budget saved. 需要强调的是，只有执行成功时才会获得这一效率奖励。 Importantly, this efficiency bonus is awarded only for successful executions. 这鼓励策略寻找既正确又高效的解决方案，同时抑制以牺牲任务性能为代价来降低成本的退化策略。 This encourages the policy to find solutions that are not only correct but also efficient, while discouraging degenerate strategies that reduce cost at the expense of task performance.
学习算法与损失函数Learning Algorithm and Loss Function
我们的学习算法是 REINFORCE（Williams 1992）的一种形式，针对离线、单次决策场景进行了定制；在该场景中，策略仅选择一次拓扑 $t$，并在整个轨迹完成后获得最终奖励 $R_{\text{final}}$。 Our learning algorithm is a form of REINFORCE (Williams 1992), tailored for an offline, one-shot decision context where the policy makes a single choice of topology, $t$ , and receives a final reward, $R_{\text{final}}$ , after the entire trajectory completes. 我们在由预先收集的经验组成的静态数据集 $\mathcal{D}$ 上，通过最小化以下损失函数来训练策略： We train the policy by minimizing the following loss function on a static dataset, $\mathcal{D}$ , of pre-collected experiences: 
| | $\begin{split}\mathcal{L}(\theta)&=-\hat{\mathbb{E}}_{(T,B,t,\tau)\sim\mathcal{D}}\left[\log\pi_{\theta}(t|T,B)\cdot R_{\text{final}}(\tau)\right]\\ &\quad-\beta\cdot H(\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|T,B)).\end{split}$ | | (6)
该损失函数包含两个指导优化的关键项： This loss function consists of two key terms that guide the optimization:
策略梯度项：$-\log\pi_{\theta}(t|T,B)\cdot R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ 是学习算法的核心。 Policy Gradient Term: The term $-\log\pi_{\theta}(t|T,B)\cdot R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ is the core of the learning algorithm. 这里，最终轨迹奖励 $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ 用作选择拓扑 $t$ 的优势估计。 Here, the final trajectory reward, $R_{\text{final}}(\tau)$ , serves as an estimate of the advantage of selecting topology $t$ . 通过最小化这一由奖励加权的负对数似然，优化过程会更新 $\theta$，以提高能够带来高奖励的动作的概率。 By minimizing this negative log-likelihood weighted by the reward, the optimization process updates $\theta$ to increase the probability of actions that lead to high rewards. 这种方法将策略决策与其最终结果直接关联起来，提供了明确而有力的学习信号，同时避免了离线场景中重要性采样通常会引入的高方差（Thomas and Brunskill 2016; Xie et al. 2019）。 This approach directly connects the policy’s decision to its ultimate outcome, providing a clear and powerful learning signal without the high variance often introduced by importance sampling in offline settings (Thomas and Brunskill 2016; Xie et al. 2019).
熵正则化项：$-\beta\cdot H(\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|T,B))$ 是熵奖励项，其中 $H(\cdot)$ 为香农熵，$\beta$ 为超参数。 Entropy Regularization Term: The term $-\beta\cdot H(\pi_{\theta}(\cdot|T,B))$ is an entropy bonus, where $H(\cdot)$ is the Shannon entropy and $\beta$ is a hyperparameter. 该项鼓励策略保持一定程度的随机性，防止其过早收敛到单一的次优拓扑。 This term encourages the policy to maintain a degree of stochasticity, preventing it from prematurely converging to a single, suboptimal topology. 通过促进策略空间内的探索，该项增强了训练稳定性，并提高了发现更接近全局最优策略的机会。 By promoting exploration within the policy space, it enhances training stability and improves the chances of discovering a more globally optimal strategy.
基于上述奖励函数、学习算法和损失函数，我们对拓扑选择策略进行训练。 Given the reward function, learning algorithm, and loss function, we perform training of the topology selection policy. 在训练过程中，我们保留在整个离线数据集 $\mathcal{D}$ 上取得最高平均综合奖励的模型参数 $\theta^{*}$，作为最终优化后的策略。 During training, the model parameters $\theta^{*}$ that achieve the highest average composite reward across the entire offline dataset $\mathcal{D}$ are preserved as the final optimized policy. 完整的训练流程见附录 A。 The complete training procedure is presented in Appendix A.
智能体实例化Agent Instantiation
最后一个组件通过在所选工作流拓扑 $t$ 中实例化选定的 LLM 集合 $\mathcal{P}$，构建多智能体系统。 The final component constructs the multi-agent system by instantiating the selected LLMs $\mathcal{P}$ within the chosen workflow topology $t$ . 其主要功能是将 $\mathcal{P}$ 中的具体 LLM 实例分配给拓扑所定义的不同智能体角色。 Its primary function is to assign specific LLM instances from $\mathcal{P}$ to the distinct agent roles defined by the topology. 在 Linear 和 Star 中，所有智能体均充当执行者。 In Linear and Star, all agents act as executors. 在 Feedback 中，权重最高的 LLM 充当评审者，其余模型充当执行者。 In Feedback, the highest-weight LLMs serve as critics and the rest as executors. 在 Planner-driven 中，权重最高和次高的 LLM 分别充当规划者和评审者，其余模型充当执行者。 In Planner-driven, the highest- and second-highest-weight LLMs serve as the planner and critics, respectively, and the remaining models act as executors. 由于较高的权重反映了更强的能力，这种分配方式确保将能力最强的 LLM 分配到最关键的角色。 Since higher weight reflects stronger capability, this assignment ensures that the most capable LLMs are allocated to the most critical roles.
实例化完成后，执行引擎会根据所选拓扑管理智能体之间的信息流与协调。 Once instantiated, the execution engine manages information flow and coordination among agents based on the selected topology. 对于 Linear 和 Star，执行引擎使用基于模板的调度器按顺序调用智能体，从而最大限度地减少协调开销。 For Linear and Star, the engine uses a template-based scheduler to invoke agents sequentially, minimizing coordination overhead. 对于 Feedback，执行引擎将一名专门的评审者与一名或多名执行者配对，以运行生成—评审—修订循环：评审者仅审核输出，不执行任务侧的计算；如果未发现问题，则将执行者的最新输出作为最终答案返回，否则将评审意见传回以进行修订，直至输出被接受或达到预设预算上限。 For Feedback, the engine pairs a dedicated critic with one or more executors to run a generate–critique–revise loop: the critic only audits outputs and does not perform task-side computation; if no issues are found, the executor’s latest output is returned as the final answer, otherwise the critique is routed back for revision until acceptance or a preset budget limit. 对于 Planner-driven，规划者会根据不断变化的需求，逐步动态编排智能体之间的交互。 For Planner-driven, the planner dynamically orchestrates agent interactions step by step according to evolving requirements.

针对任务的拓扑选择。Task-specific topology selection.
BAMAS 并不依赖一种适用于所有情况的拓扑，而是根据任务领域调整其策略。 BAMAS does not rely on a one-size-fits-all topology; rather, it adapts its strategy to the task domain. 对于受益于迭代改进的数学推理任务（即 GSM8K 和 MATH 数据集中的任务），该策略的选择以压倒性优势收敛于反馈（Feedback）拓扑。 For mathematical reasoning tasks (i.e., tasks in the GSM8K and MATH datasets), which benefit from iterative refinement, the policy overwhelmingly converges on the Feedback topology. 具体而言，BAMAS 为 GSM8K 中 40.1% 的任务和 MATH 中 69.8% 的任务选择了反馈拓扑。 Specifically, BAMAS selects the Feedback topology for 40.1% of tasks in GSM8K and 69.8% of tasks in MATH. MATH 中的这一比例更高是符合预期的，因为与 GSM8K 相比，它包含更具挑战性的高阶问题。 The higher proportion in MATH is expected, as it contains more challenging advanced problems compared to GSM8K. 相比之下，对于代码生成任务（即 MBPP 数据集），BAMAS 主要倾向于线性（Linear）拓扑，该拓扑与渐进式改进或多步生成过程相契合 (Liu et al. 2025; Rahardja et al. 2025)，因此是处理此类任务更有效、更可靠的策略。 In contrast, for code generation tasks (i.e., the MBPP dataset), BAMAS predominantly favors the Linear topology, which aligns with a progressive refinement or multi-step generation process (Liu et al. 2025; Rahardja et al. 2025), making it a more effective and reliable strategy for such tasks.
风险规避且依赖预算的拓扑选择。Risk-averse and budget-dependent topology selection.
在低预算条件下，BAMAS 的拓扑选择策略表现出风险规避倾向。 The topology selection policy of BAMAS is risk-averse under low budgets. 具体而言，在所有数据集上，当预算紧张时，该策略都明显偏好线性拓扑和星形（Star）拓扑。 Specifically, across all datasets, it heavily favors Linear and Star topologies when the budget is tight. 这种行为是合理的：这些较简单的拓扑不涉及评议智能体，因此本身就降低了复杂度以及超出预算的可能性。 This behavior is reasonable: these simpler topologies do not involve a critic agent, inherently reducing complexity and the likelihood of exceeding the budget. 随着预算增加、灵活性提高，BAMAS 更愿意选择反馈等复杂拓扑；这些拓扑虽然可能具有更强的能力，但其基础成本也更高，超出预算的风险也更大。 As the budget increases, allowing more flexibility, BAMAS becomes more willing to select complex topologies such as Feedback, which, while potentially more powerful, also carry higher baseline costs and greater risk of budget overrun. 这表明，该策略能够根据可用预算有效调整其策略，在潜在收益与财务风险之间取得平衡。 This demonstrates that the policy effectively adapts its strategy according to the available budget, balancing potential gains against financial risk.
拓扑选择的多样性。Topology selection diversity.
在所有数据集和预算条件下，规划器驱动（Planner-Driven）拓扑从未被选中，这为理解 BAMAS 的拓扑选择策略提供了关键认识。 Across all datasets and budgets, the Planner-Driven topology is never selected, a crucial insight into BAMAS’ topology selection policy. 尽管这种拓扑提供了最大的灵活性，但强化学习组件学到，其高成本和不稳定性（因为一个不佳的计划就可能使整个过程偏离正轨）使其成为一种次优选择。 Although this topology offers the most flexibility, the reinforcement learning component learns that its high cost and instability (since a poor plan can derail the entire process) make it a suboptimal choice. 相反，该策略通过依赖其他结构更明确的模式，获得了更高且更可靠的最终奖励。 Instead, the policy achieves higher and more reliable final rewards by relying on other, more structured patterns. 此外，我们观察到，拓扑选择的多样性与数据集特征相关。 Furthermore, we observe that the diversity of topology selection correlates with dataset characteristics. 对于 GSM8K 数据集，在给定预算下，拓扑选择高度一致。 For the GSM8K dataset, topology choices for a given budget are highly uniform. 相比之下，对于包含更多样化问题类型的 MATH 和 MBPP 数据集，该策略在某些预算水平下呈现出更为混合的拓扑分布。 In contrast, for the MATH and MBPP datasets, which contain more diverse problem types, the policy exhibits a more mixed distribution of topologies at certain budget levels. 这些发现表明，BAMAS 已学会一种能够细致区分不同情况的选择策略，对问题实例的具体特征具有敏感性。 These findings suggest that BAMAS has learned a nuanced selection strategy, sensitive to the specific characteristics of problem instances.
结论Conclusion
本文提出了 BAMAS，一种用于构建多智能体系统的预算感知方法。 This paper presents BAMAS, a budget-aware approach for constructing multi-agent systems. BAMAS (1) 使用整数线性规划（Integer Linear Programming）在预算约束内分配 LLM，(2) 应用离线强化学习选择有效的协作拓扑，并且 (3) 使用所选的 LLM 和拓扑实例化系统。 BAMAS (1) uses Integer Linear Programming to allocate LLMs within budget constraints, (2) applies offline reinforcement learning to choose an effective collaboration topology, and (3) instantiates the system with the selected LLMs and topology. 评估结果表明，与现有的智能体构建方法相比，BAMAS 实现了当前最先进的成本与性能权衡，以显著更低的成本达到相当或更高的准确率。 The evaluation results show that BAMAS achieves a state-of-the-art cost–performance trade-off compared to existing agent construction approaches, attaining comparable or superior accuracy with substantially lower cost.

拓扑选择策略（在算法 1 中称为 Topo-Selection）决定已配置的 LLM 智能体应如何针对给定任务和预算开展协作。 The topology selection policy (referred to as Topo-Selection in Algorithm 1) determines how provisioned LLM agents should collaborate for a given task and budget. 我们并未将单一工作流程硬编码，而是基于常用的协作模式定义了一个离散动作空间，包括线性、星形、反馈和规划器驱动拓扑。 Rather than hard-coding a single workflow, we define a discrete action space over commonly used collaboration patterns: Linear, Star, Feedback, and Planner-Driven topologies.
该策略以任务的紧凑表示和用户指定的预算为条件，选择一种兼顾性能与成本的拓扑。 Conditioned on a compact representation of the task and the user-specified budget, the policy selects a topology that balances performance and cost. 我们使用离线强化学习目标来训练这一选择器，以避免通过调用 LLM 进行在线试错所带来的延迟和费用。 We train this selector using an offline reinforcement learning objective to avoid the latency and expense of online trial-and-error with LLM calls. 具体而言，该学习目标将任务成功情况、对成本超支的强惩罚以及对未超出预算的适度奖励结合起来。 Specifically, the learning objective combines task success with strong penalties for cost overruns and modest rewards for remaining under budget. 优化通过带有熵正则化的奖励加权策略梯度更新（REINFORCE）进行 (Williams 1992)。 Optimization is performed via a reward-weighted policy-gradient update with entropy regularization (REINFORCE) (Williams 1992).

策略网络架构Policy Network Architecture
策略网络处理任务嵌入和预算，生成可用协作拓扑上的概率分布。 The policy network processes a task embedding and the budget to produce a probability distribution over the available collaboration topologies.
任务编码器：我们使用 ‘all-MiniLM-L6-v2’ 模型，为输入的任务描述生成 384 维嵌入（Reimers and Gurevych 2019; Wang et al. 2020）。 Task Encoder: We use the ‘all-MiniLM-L6-v2’ model to generate a 384-dimensional embedding for the input task description (Reimers and Gurevych 2019; Wang et al. 2020).
输入层： Input Layers:
嵌入层将 384 维任务嵌入投影为 64 维特征向量。 An Embedding Layer projects the 384-dimensional task embedding to a 64-dimensional feature vector.
预算层将标量预算值升维投影为 64 维特征向量。 A Budget Layer projects the scalar budget value up to a 64-dimensional feature vector.
核心网络：将两个 64 维特征向量拼接后，输入一个具有单个含 128 个神经元的隐藏层的多层感知机（MLP）（128 -> 128）。 Core Network: The two 64-dimensional feature vectors are concatenated and fed into a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) with one hidden layer of 128 neurons (128 -> 128).
输出层：最后一个线性层将 128 维隐藏状态映射到与可用拓扑模式数量相同的维度。 Output Layer: A final linear layer maps the 128-dimensional hidden state to the number of available topology patterns.
激活函数：除最终输出层外，整个网络均使用 ReLU 激活函数。 Activation Function: The ReLU activation function is used throughout the network, except for the final output layer.
