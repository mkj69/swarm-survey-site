Abstract摘要
Large language models (LLMs) are growing increasingly capable, prompting recent interest in LLM teams. Yet, despite increased deployment of LLM teams at scale, we lack a principled framework for addressing key questions such as when a team is helpful, how many agents to use, how structure impacts performance — and whether a team is better than a single agent. Rather than designing and testing these possibilities through trial-and-error, we propose using distributed systems as a principled foundation for creating and evaluating LLM teams. We find that many of the fundamental advantages and challenges studied in distributed computing also arise in LLM teams, highlighting the rich practical insights that can come from the cross-talk of these two fields of study.
大语言模型（LLMs）的能力日益增强，近期引发了人们对 LLM 团队的关注。然而，尽管 LLM 团队的大规模部署日益增多，我们仍缺乏一个系统性的理论框架，来回答团队何时有用、应使用多少个智能体、结构如何影响性能，以及团队是否优于单个智能体等关键问题。我们提出，以分布式系统作为创建和评估 LLM 团队的理论基础，而非通过反复试错来设计和测试这些可能的方案。我们发现，分布式计算研究中的许多基本优势与挑战也出现在 LLM 团队中，这表明这两个研究领域的交流能够带来丰富的实践启示。

1 Introduction1 引言
“Adding manpower to a late software project, makes it later.” – Frederick P. Brooks, The Mythical Man Month [1]
“向一个已经延期的软件项目增加人手，只会使其进一步延期。”——Frederick P. Brooks，《人月神话》[1]
The quest for artificial intelligence (AI) has long captivated human minds [2], and the remarkable capabilities of large language models (LLMs) have sparked widespread excitement that we are approaching this goal. Yet, single models remain fundamentally limited in memory, context, and ability. A natural response is to compose them into teams, or collections of interacting agents that can divide work, communicate, and pool their individual resources. The promise is compelling: just as people achieve far more together than alone [3, 4], teams of LLM agents could extend capabilities beyond those of any single monolithic model.
对人工智能（AI）的追求长期以来一直吸引着人类 [2]，而大语言模型（LLMs）的卓越能力引发了广泛的兴奋之情，使人们相信我们正在接近这一目标。然而，单个模型在记忆、上下文和能力方面仍存在根本性限制。一种自然的应对方式是将它们组成团队，即由相互交互的智能体构成的集合，这些智能体能够分工、通信并汇集各自的资源。这一前景令人期待：正如人们共同合作所能取得的成就远超独自行动 [3, 4]，LLM 智能体团队也可能将能力拓展至任何单一整体式模型所能达到的范围之外。
However, achieving this promise requires addressing several questions: when do teams help, when do they hurt, and how should they be designed and deployed to maintain performance and efficiency? Failing to appropriately address these questions can have substantial implications. Individual LLM calls are already resource-intensive, demanding substantial compute and energy to query, and racking up hefty monetary costs to users. These resource demands grow rapidly as agents exchange messages and iterate on shared work. Without careful orchestration to address coordination challenges raised by teams [5], agents may overwrite one another, produce redundant outputs, conflict in decisions, propagate errors through chains of reasoning, and reinforce incorrect conclusions through mutually sycophantic exchanges.
然而，要实现这一前景，就需要回答几个问题：团队何时有益，何时有害，以及应如何设计和部署团队以维持性能与效率？未能妥善回答这些问题可能产生重大影响。单次 LLM 调用本就需要消耗大量资源，查询需要大量计算和能源，并给用户带来高昂的费用。随着智能体交换消息并围绕共同任务反复迭代，这些资源需求会迅速增长。如果不进行精心编排以应对团队带来的协调挑战 [5]，智能体可能会相互覆盖彼此的工作、产生冗余输出、在决策上发生冲突、沿推理链传播错误，并通过相互迎合的交流强化错误结论。
The journey from single- to multi-agent LLM systems mirrors the historical evolution of computing itself [6, 7]. Early computing systems relied on single processors, but as demands for scale grew, engineers turned to distributed architectures that combined many machines to achieve greater capacity and robustness (Figure 1A). However, this also brought about new challenges that have defined core research topics in distributed computing for decades, such as issues of coordination, consistency, and task assignment (Figure 1B) . LLM teams appear to be following a similar path; as single models approach limits in context and reliability, there is increasing interest in assembling teams of interacting agents to overcome their individual constraints. However, the design and deployment of these teams have revealed many additional complexities, such as limited scaling depending on the task [8, 9], reduced collective problem-solving abilities [10], and amplifications of errors, false confidence, and confusion [11]. Viewing LLM teams through the lens of distributed systems (Figure 1C) both explains observed inefficiencies and generates actionable solutions, providing principled design guidelines, testable hypotheses, and normative benchmarks for evaluating team behavior across varying tasks and conditions. Rather than optimizing LLM teams through trial and error, this framework offers a rigorous foundation for understanding where coordination breaks down and how to improve it.
从单智能体 LLM 系统向多智能体 LLM 系统的发展历程，与计算本身的历史演进相呼应 [6, 7]。早期计算系统依赖单个处理器，但随着对规模的需求不断增长，工程师转向了分布式架构，通过组合多台机器来实现更大的容量和更强的鲁棒性（图 1A）。然而，这也带来了新的挑战，例如协调、一致性和任务分配等问题，数十年来，这些挑战一直是分布式计算的核心研究课题（图 1B）。LLM 团队似乎正沿着类似的路径发展；随着单个模型在上下文和可靠性方面逐渐接近极限，人们越来越关注组建由相互交互的智能体构成的团队，以克服各个智能体自身的限制。然而，这些团队的设计和部署也暴露出许多额外的复杂问题，例如扩展能力受任务影响而受到限制 [8, 9]、集体问题求解能力下降 [10]，以及错误、虚假信心和混乱的放大 [11]。从分布式系统的视角审视 LLM 团队（图 1C），既能解释已观察到的低效现象，也能提出可付诸实施的解决方案，为评估不同任务和条件下的团队行为提供有理论依据的设计准则、可检验的假设以及规范性基准。这一框架为理解协调在哪些环节失效以及如何改进协调提供了严谨的基础，无须依靠反复试错来优化 LLM 团队。
In this paper, we outline concrete guidelines from distributed computing for designing and evaluating efficient LLM teams, backed by initial empirical demonstrations. We begin by reviewing existing approaches to LLM team design and their limitations (Section 2), then establish the formal correspondence between LLM teams and distributed systems (Section 3). Section 4 provides an empirical test of this analogy. We first show that LLM teams collaborating in simplified settings with pre-assigned tasks exhibit efficiency gains that generally mirror classic scaling laws in distributed computing. We then extend this evaluation to self-coordinating decentralized teams, finding that LLM teams exhibit the same challenges, such as consistency conflicts, architectural tradeoffs, communication overhead, and stragglers, that distributed systems theory anticipates. Finally, Section 5 outlines future directions and limitations of this framework.
本文概述了源自分布式计算、用于设计和评估高效 LLM 团队的具体准则，并以初步的实证演示提供支持。我们首先回顾现有的 LLM 团队设计方法及其局限性（第 2 节），随后建立 LLM 团队与分布式系统之间的形式化对应关系（第 3 节）。第 4 节对这一类比进行了实证检验。我们首先表明，在预先分配任务的简化环境中开展协作的 LLM 团队，其效率增益总体上符合分布式计算中的经典扩展定律。随后，我们将这一评估扩展至自主协调的去中心化团队，发现 LLM 团队呈现出了分布式系统理论所预期的同类挑战，例如一致性冲突、架构权衡、通信开销和拖后腿的智能体（stragglers）。最后，第 5 节概述了这一框架的未来研究方向与局限性。
These connections position distributed computing as a principled foundation for designing and evaluating LLM teams. As these teams are deployed, failure to address these scalability and coordination concerns risks more than inefficient computation. Poorly designed systems may waste enormous computational resources while producing unreliable outputs. Because these failures emerge from interactions among agents, they can be difficult to diagnose or correct without principled design frameworks. A formal foundation for LLM team architectures – and deciding whether to implement a team in the first place – therefore offers a path toward deploying these systems efficiently, robustly, and responsibly at scale.
这些联系使分布式计算成为设计和评估 LLM 团队的理论基础。随着这些团队投入部署，若不解决这些可扩展性与协调问题，所带来的风险将不仅仅是计算效率低下。设计不当的系统可能浪费大量计算资源，同时产生不可靠的输出。由于这些故障源于智能体之间的交互，如果缺乏具有理论依据的设计框架，就可能难以诊断或纠正。因此，为 LLM 团队架构建立形式化基础，并据此决定是否应当采用团队，为高效、稳健且负责任地大规模部署这些系统提供了一条路径。
2 Background2 背景
2.1 The rise of LLM teams2.1 LLM 团队的兴起
LLM teams, or multi-agent systems in which multiple language model agents coordinate through task decomposition, role specialization, and inter-agent communication, are increasingly being deployed in both research and production. Experimental studies show that sufficiently capable LLM agents can successfully cooperate in simple economic settings like common-pool resource problems [12]. Researchers have argued that such teams may be particularly valuable for scientific discovery, where distributing subtasks across agents can increase effective capacity, enable parallel exploration, and reduce individual errors through cross-checking [13]. Several frontier labs are deploying agentic coding teams that allow users to coordinate multiple LLM instances working together on shared coding tasks [14, 15]. Despite this enthusiasm, there is a growing need to evaluate these systems rigorously, both in terms of individual models [16] and their emergent collective behaviors [17].
LLM 团队，即多个语言模型智能体通过任务分解、角色专业化和智能体间通信进行协调的多智能体系统，正日益广泛地应用于研究和生产环境。实验研究表明，能力足够强的 LLM 智能体能够在公共池资源问题（common-pool resource problems）等简单经济情境中成功合作 [12]。研究人员认为，这类团队在科学发现中可能尤为有价值，因为将子任务分配给不同智能体可以提升有效处理能力、支持并行探索，并通过交叉核查减少个体错误 [13]。多家前沿实验室正在部署智能体编程团队，使用户能够协调多个 LLM 实例，共同完成共享的编程任务 [14, 15]。尽管人们对此充满热情，但无论是针对单个模型 [16]，还是针对其涌现出的集体行为 [17]，都越来越需要对这些系统进行严格评估。
2.2 Benefits, limitations, and risks of LLM teams2.2 LLM 团队的优势、局限与风险
There is growing empirical evidence that the performance of multi-agent LLM teams relative to single agents is mixed and highly task-dependent.
越来越多的实证证据表明，多智能体 LLM 团队相较于单个智能体的性能表现有好有坏，并且高度依赖具体任务。
On one hand, teams can outperform individual agents by aggregating complementary reasoning paths, enabling cross-checking, and distributing long-horizon context across multiple agents. LLM teams have demonstrated impressive scientific discovery capabilities, including the design of novel nanobodies [18] and autonomous hypothesis-generation pipelines for chemistry [19]. Controlled studies further show that ensembles of interacting LLM agents can improve accuracy by enabling diversity and debate [20, 21], and by decomposing contexts that would exceed a single agent’s limits across multiple agents [22]. Early benchmarks reinforced this optimistic view: increasing the number of agents answering a question and asking them to vote upon the correct output often increased accuracy [23].
一方面，团队可以通过汇集互补的推理路径、开展交叉核查，以及将长时程任务的上下文分散到多个智能体中，取得优于单个智能体的表现。LLM 团队已展现出令人瞩目的科学发现能力，包括设计新型纳米抗体 [18]，以及构建用于化学研究的自主假设生成流程 [19]。受控研究进一步表明，由相互交互的 LLM 智能体组成的集成系统，可以通过促进多样性和辩论 [20, 21]，以及将超出单个智能体处理上限的上下文拆分给多个智能体 [22]，提高准确率。早期基准测试强化了这一乐观看法：增加回答同一问题的智能体数量，并要求它们投票选出正确输出，往往能够提高准确率 [23]。
However, subsequent work has identified important limitations and failure modes. Performance gains from adding agents are neither linear nor guaranteed; instead, they depend strongly on factors such as agent heterogeneity and coordination structure [10, 8]. Multi-agent systems tend to benefit on parallelizable or decomposable tasks but can degrade on sequential or tightly coupled workflows due to communication overhead and coordination errors [9]. Moreover, LLM agents are often biased towards being helpful and agreeable rather than honest [24], which may suppress productive disagreement and reduce collective problem-solving quality [10]. Interacting agents can also amplify misinformation and adversarial behavior, uncontrollably consuming resources and reporting task completion despite failures [11].
然而，后续研究发现了重要的局限和失效模式。增加智能体带来的性能提升既不是线性的，也并非必然发生；相反，它高度依赖智能体异质性和协调结构等因素 [10, 8]。多智能体系统通常在可并行化或可分解的任务上获益，但在顺序执行或紧密耦合的工作流程中，可能因通信开销和协调错误而出现性能下降 [9]。此外，LLM 智能体往往更倾向于提供帮助和迎合他人，而非保持诚实 [24]，这可能抑制有益的分歧，降低集体解决问题的质量 [10]。相互交互的智能体还可能放大错误信息和对抗性行为，不受控制地消耗资源，并在任务失败的情况下仍报告任务已完成 [11]。
Together, these findings suggest that the advantages of LLM teams are conditional rather than universal, motivating a deeper analysis of when and why teams outperform single agents.
综合来看，这些发现表明，LLM 团队的优势是有条件的，并非普遍存在，因此有必要更深入地分析团队何时以及为何能优于单个智能体。
2.3 Existing design approaches2.3 现有设计方法
A major line of work seeks to improve LLM teams by drawing inspiration from human collaboration and organizational design. The challenges faced by LLM teams resemble many that we regularly face when collaborating with people (e.g., dividing tasks, coordinating actions, communicating). Frameworks such as MetaGPT, AgentVerse, and AutoGen explicitly structure agents into familiar roles (e.g., planner, engineer, reviewer) [25, 26, 27], and hierarchical coordination patterns resembling managerial workflows can improve multi-agent performance [28, 29]. Beyond role-based hierarchies, other work explores alternative coordination structures. Some approaches emphasize deliberative collectives, in which multiple agents debate or critique one another to improve reasoning accuracy [20, 21], though the gains from debate are sensitive to team design and communication [30]. Separate lines of work treat the structure of multi-agent communication as its own design variable, finding that topology substantially affects scaling and performance [31]. More recent work aims to automate topology selection by learning task-adaptive communication graphs [32].
一条主要研究路线从人类协作和组织设计中汲取灵感，以改进 LLM 团队。LLM 团队面临的挑战，与我们在人际协作中经常遇到的许多挑战相似，例如划分任务、协调行动和沟通。MetaGPT、AgentVerse 和 AutoGen 等框架明确地将智能体组织为人们熟悉的角色，例如规划者、工程师和评审者 [25, 26, 27]，而类似管理工作流程的层级协调模式能够提升多智能体性能 [28, 29]。除基于角色的层级结构外，其他研究还探索了不同的协调结构。一些方法强调审议式集体，即让多个智能体相互辩论或批评，以提高推理准确率 [20, 21]，但辩论带来的收益对团队设计和通信方式较为敏感 [30]。另一些研究路线将多智能体通信结构本身视为一个设计变量，并发现拓扑结构会显著影响系统的扩展能力和性能 [31]。较新的研究旨在通过学习能够适应任务的通信图，实现拓扑结构的自动选择 [32]。
2.4 The need for a formal framework2.4 对形式化框架的需求
Despite rapid progress in building multi-agent LLM systems, there remains limited systematic understanding of when and why teams outperform individual models, or how their structure should be designed for a given task. Existing design approaches largely propose coordination patterns heuristically, often by analogy to human organizations or empirical benchmarking, rather than deriving them from formal properties of tasks or workflows. Evaluations also focus predominantly on accuracy, leaving questions of efficiency, cost, and robustness largely unaddressed. This gap makes it difficult to anticipate when LLM teams will improve performance versus introduce coordination overhead, redundancy, or failure modes. A formal framework transforms this problem. Rather than discovering failure modes empirically after deployment, such a framework allows us to derive conditions under which teams are expected to succeed or struggle, turning design decisions that are currently made by intuition into ones that can be reasoned about, compared, and tested.
尽管多智能体 LLM 系统的构建已取得快速进展，但对于团队何时以及为何优于单个模型，以及应如何针对特定任务设计其结构，仍缺乏系统性理解。现有设计方法大多以启发式方式提出协调模式，往往借鉴人类组织或依赖实证基准评测，而非从任务或工作流的形式化属性推导这些模式。评估也主要关注准确性，而效率、成本和鲁棒性问题在很大程度上仍未得到探讨。这一缺口使我们难以预判 LLM 团队何时能够提升性能，何时又会引入协调开销、冗余或失效模式。形式化框架改变了这一问题的处理方式。这样的框架使我们能够推导出团队预期会成功或遇到困难的条件，而不必等到部署后才通过实证发现失效模式，从而将目前凭直觉做出的设计决策转化为可以推理、比较和检验的决策。

3 A framework for evaluating LLM teams as distributed systems3 将 LLM 团队作为分布式系统进行评估的框架
Single language model agents have limited capacity: context windows bound how much information they can access at once, memory limits what can be retained, and reasoning, execution, or tool use all require time. Additionally, single agents are prone to faults such as hallucinations, missing relevant context, or failing to respond, all of which disrupt the task they are responsible for. As a result, no single agent can do everything at once, and we might not want it to.
单个语言模型智能体的能力有限：上下文窗口限制了它一次能够访问的信息量，记忆限制了它能够保留的内容，而推理、执行或工具使用都需要时间。此外，单个智能体容易出现幻觉、遗漏相关上下文或未能响应等故障，这些都会干扰它所负责的任务。因此，任何单个智能体都无法同时完成所有事情，而我们也未必希望它这样做。
These challenges are not unique to LLM agents. Individual machines suffer from similar constraints; they are bounded by memory, time, and unpredictable failures or crashes. These exact problems have driven decades of research in distributed systems, suggesting that its theories offer a natural and rigorous foundation for understanding LLM teams.
这些挑战并非 LLM 智能体所独有。单台机器也面临类似约束；它们受到内存、时间以及不可预测的故障或崩溃的限制。正是这些问题推动了分布式系统领域数十年的研究，这表明该领域的理论为理解 LLM 团队提供了自然而严谨的基础。
3.1 Shared properties3.1 共同属性
To begin, let us make this analogy precise. An LLM team shares four key properties of the core structure of a distributed system (Figure 1C).
首先，让我们精确阐明这一类比。LLM 团队具有分布式系统核心结构的四项关键属性（图 1C）。
Independence: LLM agents are independent, maintaining their own local contexts with only partial observability of the state of the broader task and team. Similarly, nodes in a distributed system operate with local memory and have no global clock or state. In both cases, agents or nodes might be heterogeneous, having different capabilities due to distinct prompts, base models, or processing times.
独立性：LLM 智能体彼此独立，各自维护自身的局部上下文，只能部分观测整体任务和团队的状态。类似地，分布式系统中的节点依靠本地内存运行，不具备全局时钟或全局状态。在这两种情况下，智能体或节点都可能具有异构性，因提示词、基础模型或处理时间不同而具备不同的能力。
Communication: LLM teams coordinate through communication. Rather than sharing state directly, they exchange messages such as prompts to divide and integrate work. This mirrors how nodes in a distributed system exchange packets to coordinate computation.
通信：LLM 团队通过通信进行协调。它们通过交换提示词等消息来分配和整合工作，而非直接共享状态。这与分布式系统中的节点通过交换数据包来协调计算的方式相对应。
Concurrency: In an LLM team, multiple agents are working on tasks simultaneously. This parallelism can increase speed and coverage, but also introduces major coordination problems: agents may act using stale information, produce conflicting outputs, or overwrite each other’s progress. Distributed systems face the same problems when operating in parallel on shared data. Ensuring that all nodes maintain consistency requires synchronization protocols that determine how and when nodes exchange updates and commit results.
并发性：在 LLM 团队中，多个智能体同时处理任务。这种并行性可以提高速度并扩大覆盖范围，但也会带来重大的协调问题：智能体可能依据过时信息采取行动、产生相互冲突的输出，或覆盖彼此已完成的工作。分布式系统在对共享数据进行并行操作时也面临同样的问题。要确保所有节点保持一致性，就需要同步协议来规定节点如何以及何时交换更新并提交结果。
Fallibility: LLM agents can hallucinate, stall, or produce incorrect outputs that propagate through a team. Nodes in a distributed system can crash, fall out of sync, or return corrupted results. In both cases, the system must be designed to tolerate these faults gracefully.
易错性：LLM 智能体可能产生幻觉、陷入停滞，或生成会在团队中传播的错误输出。分布式系统中的节点可能崩溃、失去同步，或返回损坏的结果。在这两种情况下，系统设计都必须能够妥善容忍这些故障。
These four properties establish a structural correspondence between LLM teams and distributed systems, offering a new analytical scaffold. This is not to say that LLM teams conform to every assumption made in distributed computing. For example, communication in LLM teams occurs in natural language rather than fixed, formally specified protocols, making it ambiguous or shaped by pragmatic interpretation. Similarly, traditional distributed systems models often assume well-defined failure modes, whereas LLM failures can be semantic and probabilistic. We believe that these mismatches sharpen the usefulness of this analogy rather than undermine it. Where the analogy holds, we inherit decades of existing theories by which we can generate concrete predictions about LLM team behavior. Where it breaks down, the gap itself becomes informative, revealing where new theory is needed and providing a baseline against which deviations become visible and measurable.
这四项属性确立了 LLM 团队与分布式系统之间的结构对应关系，提供了一种新的分析框架。这并不意味着 LLM 团队符合分布式计算中的所有假设。例如，LLM 团队使用自然语言进行通信，而非采用固定且经过形式化规定的协议，因此通信可能存在歧义，或受到语用解读的影响。类似地，传统分布式系统模型通常假定故障模式有明确的定义，而 LLM 的故障可能具有语义性和概率性。我们认为，这些不匹配之处非但没有削弱这一类比，反而使其用途更加鲜明。在类比成立之处，我们可以借助数十年来积累的现有理论，对 LLM 团队的行为作出具体预测。在类比失效之处，差距本身也能提供信息，揭示哪些方面需要新的理论，并提供一个基准，使偏离情况得以显现和测量。
3.2 Predicting LLM team performance using this framework3.2 使用这一框架预测 LLM 团队的表现
Applying this framework to LLMs yields specific predictions about how behavior should change as a result of task and team structure. Here, we test two specific predictions: (1) how task structure affects the efficiency gains achievable through division of labor, and (2) how team architecture shapes coordination costs.
将这一框架应用于 LLM，可以对任务结构和团队结构如何引起行为变化作出具体预测。在此，我们检验两个具体预测：(1) 任务结构如何影响通过分工所能实现的效率提升，以及 (2) 团队架构如何影响协调成本。
First, this framework yields specific predictions about how performance should change with team size depending on the task. Work in distributed systems has formally characterized how efficiency should scale depending on how much of a task can be executed simultaneously by multiple nodes [33, 34, 35]. We predict that the same efficiency limits should apply to LLMs, such that teams will exhibit stronger efficiency gains when tasks are highly parallel compared to ones with serial dependencies.
首先，这一框架可以根据任务的不同，对表现如何随团队规模变化作出具体预测。分布式系统领域的研究已经形式化地刻画了效率如何随任务中可由多个节点同时执行的部分所占比例而扩展 [33, 34, 35]。我们预测，同样的效率限制也适用于 LLM，因此，与具有串行依赖关系的任务相比，团队在高度并行的任务中将表现出更大的效率提升。
This framework also yields predictions about the tradeoffs between different team architectures. Centralized architectures, in which one node delegates tasks and integrates results, reduce overhead by routing communication through fewer channels. They also improve consistency by enforcing that only one agent updates shared resources or dependencies at a time. However, because task assignments are fixed, these systems are also vulnerable to variability within nodes; one slow agent (or “straggler”) can delay the team as a whole. Decentralized architectures mitigate this risk by allowing tasks to be assigned dynamically, but at the cost of greater coordination overhead and elevated risk of conflicts when multiple agents concurrently modify shared resources. Together, these principles motivate the prediction that centralized and decentralized teams have distinct strengths and vulnerabilities: decentralized teams should exhibit greater coordination overhead (e.g., number of messages, idle rounds) and conflict rates (e.g., file conflicts, rewrites, intermediate test failures), while centralized teams should show greater vulnerability to stragglers.
这一框架还可以对不同团队架构之间的权衡作出预测。在集中式架构中，由一个节点分派任务并整合结果，通过更少的通道传递通信，从而降低开销。这类架构还通过强制规定同一时刻只能由一个智能体更新共享资源或依赖项来提高一致性。然而，由于任务分配是固定的，这些系统也容易受到节点内部变异性的影响；一个速度缓慢的智能体（或“拖后者”（straggler））就可能拖延整个团队。去中心化架构通过允许动态分配任务来缓解这一风险，但代价是更高的协调开销，以及多个智能体并发修改共享资源时更高的冲突风险。这些原则共同促成了如下预测：集中式团队和去中心化团队各有不同的优势和弱点：去中心化团队应表现出更高的协调开销（例如，消息数量、空闲轮次）和冲突率（例如，文件冲突、重写、中间测试失败），而集中式团队应更容易受到拖后者的影响。
We tested these correspondences by assigning teams of LLM agents to three collaborative coding tasks: implementing a math utilities library, analyzing simulated data, and rendering an SVG file (for more details, see Appendix A.1). Teams were composed of 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 homogeneous agents drawn from a single base model (Claude-Sonnet-4-6, Gemini 3-Flash, or GPT-5.2). In each of these tasks, agents were presented with twenty programming subtasks, each specifying a description, required output, and corresponding test file. There were three task structures per domain: a highly parallel task in which eighteen subtasks were mutually independent, a mixed task with ten subtasks forming a sequential dependency chain and remaining ten independent, and a highly serial task with sixteen interdependent subtasks. We tested two assignment schemes. In Experiment 1, coordination challenges were minimized by pre-assigning tasks to different agents to isolate the effects of task structure on scalability. In Experiment 2, this was relaxed by prompting agents to both select which tasks they would perform and then complete them.
我们通过让 LLM 智能体团队执行三项协作编程任务来检验这些对应关系：实现一个数学工具库、分析模拟数据，以及渲染一个 SVG 文件（更多细节见附录 A.1）。团队由来自同一基础模型（Claude-Sonnet-4-6、Gemini 3-Flash 或 GPT-5.2）的 1、2、3、4 或 5 个同质智能体组成。在每项任务中，智能体都会获得二十个编程子任务，每个子任务均明确给出描述、所需输出和对应的测试文件。每个领域均设有三种任务结构：高度并行任务，其中十八个子任务相互独立；混合任务，其中十个子任务构成顺序依赖链，其余十个相互独立；以及高度串行任务，其中十六个子任务相互依赖。我们测试了两种分配方案。在实验 1 中，我们通过将任务预先分配给不同智能体，尽量减少协调方面的挑战，以单独考察任务结构对可扩展性的影响。在实验 2 中，我们放宽了这一限制，通过提示要求智能体自行选择要执行的任务，然后完成这些任务。

4.1 Amdahl’s Law predicts scalability in LLM teams4.1 阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl’s Law）预测 LLM 团队的可扩展性
A central motivation for distributed systems is scalable performance: if large-scale computing tasks are decomposed across many nodes, increasing system size can improve efficiency in terms of completion times or throughput. However, decades of research demonstrate that scalability is often neither linear nor guaranteed [33, 34, 35]. Even before accounting for coordination overhead, classical scalability laws demonstrate that performance gains depend primarily on parallelizability, or the extent to which a task can be executed concurrently.
分布式系统的一个核心动机是实现性能的可扩展性：如果将大规模计算任务分解并分配到多个节点上，增加系统规模就可以在完成时间或吞吐量方面提高效率。然而，数十年的研究表明，扩展通常既不是线性的，也不一定能带来性能提升 [33, 34, 35]。即便尚未计入协调开销，经典的可扩展性定律也已表明，性能提升主要取决于可并行性，即任务能够被并发执行的程度。
The ability for multiple nodes to execute a task in parallel is constrained by the inherent structure of the task itself. Bottlenecks due to locking, sequential dependencies between subtasks, shared memory accesses, or resource contention force nodes to wait, limiting parallelism [33, 36]. Amdahl’s Law formalizes how these constraints limit speedup $S$ with $s$ available processors under fixed workloads:
多个节点并行执行一项任务的能力受到任务自身固有结构的约束。锁定、子任务之间的顺序依赖、共享内存访问或资源争用所造成的瓶颈会迫使节点等待，从而限制并行性 [33, 36]。阿姆达尔定律形式化地描述了在固定工作负载下，这些约束如何限制使用 $s$ 个可用处理器时的加速比 $S$：
$$S_{\text{latency}}(s)=\frac{1}{(1-p)+\frac{p}{s}},$$
where $p$ is the parallelizable fraction of the workload and $1-p$ is inherently serial. For example, when 95% of a task is parallelizable, a speedup of 20 $\times$ is achievable, and even thousands of nodes can contribute before efficiency plateaus. In contrast, if only 50% of a task can be parallelized, then even with infinitely many processors and no coordination overhead, the maximum speedup is only 2 $\times$ , and that plateau is reached with far fewer nodes.
其中，$p$ 是工作负载中可并行化的部分所占的比例，而 $1-p$ 是固有的串行部分。例如，当一项任务的 95% 可以并行化时，可以实现 20 $\times$ 的加速，而且在效率进入平台期之前，甚至数千个节点都仍能作出贡献。相比之下，如果一项任务中只有 50% 可以并行化，那么即使拥有无限多个处理器且没有协调开销，最大加速比也仅为 2 $\times$，而且使用少得多的节点就会达到这一平台期。
The same efficiency limits should apply to LLM teams. Distributing work across multiple agents can improve performance only to the extent that the underlying task is parallelizable: highly decomposable tasks with independent components should exhibit larger speedups than those with tight interdependencies or contention. Emerging evidence supports this prediction, showing that LLM teams perform better on decomposable than sequential tasks [9]. This raises a natural question: can classical scalability laws such as Amdahl’s Law mechanistically explain when scaling LLM teams yields genuine efficiency gains?
同样的效率限制也应适用于 LLM 团队。将工作分配给多个智能体所能带来的性能提升，受底层任务可并行化程度的限制：由相互独立的部分组成、可高度分解的任务，其加速比应大于具有紧密相互依赖关系或资源争用的任务。新近出现的证据支持这一预测，表明 LLM 团队在可分解任务上的表现优于顺序任务 [9]。这引出了一个自然的问题：Amdahl 定律等经典可扩展性定律，能否从机制上解释扩大 LLM 团队规模何时会带来真正的效率提升？

We tested this question directly in Experiment 1 using our collaborative coding platform. Parallelizability was manipulated by varying prespecified dependencies between subtasks, resulting in a set of highly parallel, mixed, and sequential tasks. 我们在实验 1 中使用协作编程平台直接检验了这一问题。我们通过改变子任务之间预先指定的依赖关系来操纵可并行化程度，从而得到一组高度并行、混合和顺序任务。为简化协调，我们预先将任务分配给智能体，以单独考察任务结构对可扩展性的影响。我们测量了端到端实际完成时间随团队规模的变化，并将加速比计算为单智能体与 N 个智能体完成时间之比，即 $S(N)=T(1)/T(N)$，从而能够直接比较观测到的加速比与 Amdahl 定律预测的极限。

4.2 Tradeoffs arise with architectural choices4.2 架构选择带来的权衡
Distributed systems also offer insight into the coordination challenges that arise when tasks and information are divided between nodes. Architectural choices introduce tradeoffs between efficiency, consistency, and robustness. System architectures range from centralized, where a single coordinator manages shared state and task assignment, to decentralized, where components coordinate through local decisions and communication [7]. Each approach has well-known trade-offs. Centralized coordination simplifies consistency challenges, but introduces communication bottlenecks and single points of failure. Decentralized coordination improves robustness and parallelism, but increases negotiation overhead, redundancy, and the risk of conflicting decisions.
分布式系统也有助于理解任务和信息被分配到不同节点时出现的协调挑战。架构选择会在效率、一致性和鲁棒性之间引入权衡。系统架构涵盖从中心化到去中心化的多种形式：在中心化架构中，单一协调者管理共享状态和任务分配；在去中心化架构中，各组件通过局部决策和通信进行协调 [7]。每种方法都有众所周知的权衡。中心化协调简化了一致性方面的挑战，但会引入通信瓶颈和单点故障。去中心化协调提高了鲁棒性和并行性，但也增加了协商开销、冗余以及决策冲突的风险。
In LLM teams, similar decisions must be made about whether a central leader (whether human or LLM) should assign tasks to agents, or whether agents will autonomously decide how to allocate work among themselves. In Experiment 2, we tested the performance of decentralized LLM teams in our suite of collaborative coding tasks. Rather than pre-assigning tasks to agents, agents were given the ability to self-claim them while communicating with their teammates. We again tested teams of 1, 2, 3, 4, or 5 agents to directly compare performance of these self-coordinating teams to the centralized ones in Experiment 1.
在 LLM 团队中，同样需要决定：应由一位中央领导者（人类或 LLM）向智能体分配任务，还是由智能体自主决定如何在彼此之间分配工作。在实验 2 中，我们在一套协作编程任务上测试了去中心化 LLM 团队的表现。我们没有预先向智能体分配任务，而是赋予智能体在与队友沟通的同时自行认领任务的能力。我们再次测试了由 1、2、3、4 或 5 个智能体组成的团队，以直接比较这些自主协调团队与实验 1 中的中心化团队的表现。

Across all runs, preassigned teams achieved significantly higher speedup than decentralized teams (Mann–Whitney $U=155523$ , $p<0.001$ ), with median speedups of $1.36\times$ versus $0.88\times$ respectively. This pattern held within each model individually (all $p\leq 0.01$ ). 图 3A 表明，相较于图 2 中的中心化团队，去中心化协调往往会降低效率。在所有运行中，预先分配任务的团队取得的加速比显著高于去中心化团队（Mann–Whitney $U=155523$，$p<0.001$），两者的加速比中位数分别为 $1.36\times$ 和 $0.88\times$。对每个模型分别进行分析时，这一模式均成立（所有 $p\leq 0.01$）。在接下来的各节中，我们将考察去中心化所带来的预期成本——具体而言，一致性冲突和通信开销——是否促成了这些效率差异。
4.3 Coordination leads to consistency conflicts4.3 协调导致一致性冲突
One of the most fundamental challenges that arises from parallel coordination is maintaining consistency: the degree to which different nodes observe a coherent view of shared state despite concurrent updates and communication delays [7]. In order for multiple nodes to make simultaneous progress on a task, distributed systems commonly replicate a shared state (e.g., data, files, memory, or intermediate results) across them. However, once multiple replicas of the same information exist, they may be updated independently and at different times. As a result, nodes can temporarily hold different versions of what is intended to be the same underlying information, which must be reconciled to execute the task correctly [37, 38]. When one node modifies information that another node is still using, their views of the task can diverge, producing conflicts and downstream errors.
并行协调带来的最根本挑战之一是维持一致性：即在存在并发更新和通信延迟的情况下，不同节点对共享状态所观察到的视图保持一致的程度 [7]。为了使多个节点能够同时推进一项任务，分布式系统通常会在这些节点之间复制共享状态（例如数据、文件、内存或中间结果）。然而，一旦同一信息存在多个副本，这些副本就可能被独立地在不同时间更新。因此，对于本应相同的底层信息，各节点可能暂时持有不同版本，而要正确执行任务，就必须协调这些版本，使其一致 [37, 38]。当一个节点修改了另一个节点仍在使用的信息时，它们对任务的视图可能出现分歧，从而产生冲突和下游错误。
LLM teams similarly operate over a shared state that must be constantly updated as agents progress, such as task plans, code repositories, analysis outputs, design documents, and evolving context representations. Because multiple agents may read, modify, and use these shared states concurrently, maintaining adequate consistency becomes a central challenge of LLM teams. Otherwise, agents may overwrite each other’s work, make changes that break downstream tasks, or make progress with an outdated version of results.
LLM 团队同样基于共享状态开展工作，而这些状态必须随着智能体的推进而不断更新，例如任务计划、代码仓库、分析输出、设计文档以及不断演变的上下文表示。由于多个智能体可能并发读取、修改和使用这些共享状态，维持足够的一致性成为 LLM 团队面临的一项核心挑战。否则，智能体可能覆盖彼此的工作，做出破坏下游任务的更改，或基于过时版本的结果继续推进。
Without clear protocols for assignment, we observed three types of consistency violations directly in the decentralized teams in Experiment 2 (Figure 3B). Decentralized teams exhibited a substantial number of concurrent writes, in which two or more agents edit the same file simultaneously and silently overwrite each other’s work. We also observed a significant number of rewrites, in which an agent would completely overwrite a file written by a teammate in a previous round. Finally, we observed temporal consistency violations, in which an agent would attempt to implement a task out of order without its predecessor being implemented yet. Because of these consistency conflicts, decentralized teams produced substantially more failed tests (Figure 3C). Across all runs, decentralized teams generated significantly more test failures than preassigned teams (Mann-Whitney $U=287013$ , $p<0.001$ ). Median failures were 19 for decentralized teams compared to 4 for preassigned teams, and this pattern held within each model individually (all $p<0.001$ ). These findings are consistent with the distributed systems prediction that decentralized teams will pose a greater risk of consistency conflicts.
在缺乏明确任务分配协议的情况下，我们在实验 2 的去中心化团队中直接观察到了三类一致性违规（图 3B）。去中心化团队出现了大量并发写入，即两个或更多智能体同时编辑同一文件，并在没有任何提示的情况下覆盖彼此的工作。我们还观察到了大量重写，即一个智能体完全覆盖队友在上一轮编写的文件。最后，我们还观察到了时序一致性违规，即在前置任务尚未实现时，智能体便试图不按顺序实现后续任务。由于这些一致性冲突，去中心化团队出现了多得多的测试失败（图 3C）。在所有运行中，去中心化团队产生的测试失败次数显著多于预先分配任务的团队（Mann-Whitney $U=287013$，$p<0.001$）。去中心化团队的失败次数中位数为 19，而预先分配任务的团队为 4；对每个模型分别进行分析时，这一模式均成立（所有 $p<0.001$）。这些发现与分布式系统理论的预测一致，即去中心化团队面临更高的一致性冲突风险。
4.4 Larger and decentralized teams incur compounding overhead4.4 更大规模和去中心化的团队会产生不断叠加的开销
In order for interconnected nodes to coordinate effectively, they must send packets of information (or messages) back and forth to update each other. Sending these messages is not instantaneous, thus incurring substantial costs and overhead. In distributed systems, these communication costs typically depend on how nodes are structured [39, 7]. Decentralized architectures require agents to communicate directly with each other, increasing coordination overhead. In the worst case, $n$ agents can induce $O(n^{2})$ communication channels, resulting in both delays in processing and a polynomial increase in messages exchanged. In contrast, centralized architectures mediate interactions through a single coordinator, reducing coordination complexity to $O(n)$ as each agent communicates with the central leader.
为了有效协调，相互连接的节点必须来回发送信息包（或消息），以向彼此提供最新信息。发送这些消息并非瞬间完成，因此会产生可观的成本和开销。在分布式系统中，这些通信成本通常取决于节点的组织结构 [39, 7]。去中心化架构要求智能体彼此直接通信，从而增加协调开销。在最坏情况下，$n$ 个智能体可能产生 $O(n^{2})$ 条通信通道，既导致处理延迟，也使交换的消息数量呈多项式增长。相比之下，中心化架构通过单一协调者来协调交互，由于每个智能体都与中央领导者通信，协调复杂度降至 $O(n)$。
Similarly, LLM teams must exchange messages with each other as tasks progress, which should thus theoretically worsen in decentralized teams. To test this correspondence directly, we measured coordination overhead in two complementary ways (Figure 4). First, decentralized teams exchanged substantially more messages than ones with preassigned tasks (Mann-Whitney $U=311551$ , $p<0.001$ ), and these message counts increased with team size ( $r=0.483$ , $p<0.001$ ). Second, self-coordinating teams suffered from more idle rounds, or interaction steps in which agents communicated but did not complete any task progress (Mann-Whitney $U=289672$ , $p<0.001$ ). These idle rounds reflected delays and contention introduced by peer-to-peer coordination, such as agents trying to work on the same tasks but being blocked or sending messages back and forth about what to do next. Thus, as predicted by prior findings in distributed computing, decentralized LLM teams accumulate substantially more communication and coordination overhead than preassigned teams, which often worsen with more agents.
同样，LLM 团队在任务推进过程中也必须彼此交换消息，因此，从理论上讲，去中心化团队的通信开销应当更高。为了直接检验这种对应关系，我们以两种互补的方式测量了协调开销（图 4）。首先，去中心化团队交换的消息数量显著多于预先分配任务的团队（Mann-Whitney $U=311551$，$p<0.001$），且消息数量随团队规模增大而增加（$r=0.483$，$p<0.001$）。其次，自协调团队出现了更多空闲轮次，即智能体进行了通信但未取得任何任务进展的交互步骤（Mann-Whitney $U=289672$，$p<0.001$）。这些空闲轮次反映了点对点协调带来的延迟和争用，例如多个智能体试图处理相同任务却受到阻碍，或就下一步该做什么反复发送消息。因此，正如分布式计算领域的既有研究所预测的那样，去中心化 LLM 团队累积的通信和协调开销显著多于预先分配任务的团队，而且这些开销往往会随着智能体数量增加而加剧。

4.5 Decentralized teams mitigate straggler delays4.5 去中心化团队缓解掉队者延迟
Another well-documented source of inefficiency in distributed systems is the presence of stragglers: processors that take an unusually long or unpredictable amount of time to complete their assigned task [40, 41]. Because many distributed computations synchronize after set phases, overall progress is frequently determined by the slowest workers. In LLM teams, agents must similarly synchronize their outputs (e.g., implementing subtasks before testing, merging results), so downstream progress depends on all agents completing their assigned work. Variability in reasoning time, tool latency, or context complexity can therefore produce LLM stragglers whose delays stall the entire team.
分布式系统中另一个已有充分研究的低效来源是掉队者（stragglers）的存在：这些处理器完成所分配任务所需的时间异常漫长或难以预测 [40, 41]。由于许多分布式计算会在既定阶段结束后进行同步，因此整体进度往往由最慢的工作节点决定。在 LLM 团队中，智能体同样必须同步其输出（例如，在测试前实现各个子任务、合并结果），因此后续进展取决于所有智能体完成各自分配到的工作。因此，推理时间、工具延迟或上下文复杂性的差异可能导致 LLM 掉队者的出现，其延迟会使整个团队停滞。
Distributed systems mitigate this problem through replication [42]. Algorithms like MapReduce duplicate slow or late-stage tasks across multiple workers and accept the earliest completion [40, 41]. A similar advantage emerged in self-coordinating teams in Experiment 2 (Figure 5). When agents were not locked into fixed assignments, they could flexibly pick up unfinished tasks, allowing faster agents to compensate for slower ones. To measure straggler impact directly, we computed a straggler gap, or the difference between the slowest agent’s completion time and the mean completion time of the other agents. This gap was significantly larger in preassigned than decentralized teams (Mann-Whitney $U=8889359$ , $p<0.001$ ), with a median delay of 2.64 seconds compared to 1.42 seconds. The effect was consistent across models (all $p<0.001$ ), and increased with team size in both coordination modes (preassign: $r=0.23$ , $p<0.001$ ; decentralized: $r=0.35$ $p<0.001$ ). Finally, this gap was significantly larger for mixed and serial tasks than for parallel ones within the preassign condition (KW $H=137.5$ , $p<0.001$ ; mixed vs. parallel: median $3.91$ s vs. $1.73$ s, MWU $p<0.001$ ), consistent with the intuition that a single straggler on the sequential dependency chain forces all downstream work to wait.
分布式系统通过复制来缓解这一问题 [42]。MapReduce 等算法会将执行缓慢或处于后期阶段的任务复制到多个工作节点上，并采纳最先完成的结果 [40, 41]。实验 2 中的自协调团队也表现出了类似优势（图 5）。当智能体不受固定任务分配的约束时，它们可以灵活接手尚未完成的任务，使较快的智能体能够弥补较慢智能体的不足。为直接衡量掉队者的影响，我们计算了掉队者差距，即最慢智能体的完成时间与其他智能体平均完成时间之差。预先分配任务团队的这一差距显著大于去中心化团队（Mann-Whitney $U=8889359$，$p<0.001$），前者的延迟中位数为 2.64 秒，后者为 1.42 秒。这一效应在各模型中均一致存在（均为 $p<0.001$），且在两种协调模式下均随团队规模增大而增强（preassign：$r=0.23$，$p<0.001$；decentralized：$r=0.35$ $p<0.001$）。最后，在预先分配任务条件下，混合任务和串行任务的这一差距显著大于并行任务（KW $H=137.5$，$p<0.001$；混合任务与并行任务：中位数分别为 $3.91$ s 和 $1.73$ s，MWU $p<0.001$），这与以下直觉一致：顺序依赖链上的单个掉队者就会迫使所有下游工作等待。
This finding provides further evidence that core tradeoffs in distributed systems also emerge in LLM teams. While fixed assignments and centralized coordination can reduce overhead and improve consistency, they are more vulnerable to variability across agents, a disadvantage which becomes particularly pronounced in tasks with greater dependencies. Taken together, these results suggest that LLM teams are vulnerable to coordination challenges that are similar to those faced by distributed systems. In this way, distributed systems offer basic design principles that can be used to understand the tradeoffs faced by centralized and decentralized teams, and to select the team architecture that is best suited for a particular task.
这一发现进一步表明，分布式系统中的核心权衡也会出现在 LLM 团队中。尽管固定任务分配和中心化协调能够减少开销并提高一致性，但它们更容易受到智能体间差异的影响，而且在依赖关系更多的任务中，这一劣势尤为明显。综合来看，这些结果表明，LLM 团队容易受到与分布式系统类似的协调挑战的影响。由此，分布式系统提供了基本设计原则，可用于理解中心化和去中心化团队面临的权衡，并选择最适合特定任务的团队架构。

4.6 Cost-efficiency tradeoffs4.6 成本与效率的权衡
Finally, a long-recognized challenge in distributed systems is that splitting work across machines incurs additional computational costs. Scaling a system requires increased energy consumption and operational resources, resulting in an inevitable tradeoff between performance and energy or budget [43].
最后，分布式系统中一个早已得到认识的挑战是，将工作分配到多台机器上会产生额外的计算成本。扩大系统规模需要增加能源消耗和运维资源，因此不可避免地需要在性能与能耗或预算之间进行权衡 [43]。
This tradeoff is certainly arising in LLM teams. While certain tasks benefit substantially from LLM teams in terms of wall-clock time, distributing work across multiple agents introduces additional computational costs, including token usage, budget, and energy consumption. Because teams of agents communicate, synchronize, manage consistency, and occasionally cheer each other on (see Appendix 6.2), token consumption often outpaces speedup depending on task structure and model (Table 1).
这种权衡无疑也正在 LLM 团队中出现。尽管某些任务在实际运行时间（wall-clock time）方面能从 LLM 团队中显著受益，但将工作分配给多个智能体会带来额外的计算成本，包括 token 用量、预算和能源消耗。由于智能体团队需要通信、同步、管理一致性，有时还会相互鼓劲（见附录 6.2），因此，视任务结构和模型而定，token 消耗的增长倍数往往超过加速比（表 1）。
We computed the per-run difference between token multiplier and speedup, where positive values indicate costs outpacing speedup. Preassigned teams showed a small but significant excess overall (Wilcoxon signed-rank $p=0.013$ , median $=0.02$ ), driven primarily by the inefficiency of teams performing serial tasks (serial: mean token multiplier $5.83\times$ vs. speedup $1.13\times$ , $p<0.001$ ). Decentralized teams showed a consistently larger gap across all parallelizability conditions (Wilcoxon $p<0.001$ , median $=1.17$ ), with token costs scaling strongly with team size (Spearman $\rho=0.40$ , $p<0.001$ ) while speedup did not ( $\rho=-0.07$ , $p=0.15$ ).
我们计算了每次运行中 token 用量倍数与加速比之差，其中正值表示成本的增长超过了加速收益。预先分配的团队总体上呈现出幅度较小但显著的超出（Wilcoxon 符号秩检验 $p=0.013$，中位数 $=0.02$），这主要源于团队执行串行任务时的低效率（串行任务：平均 token 用量倍数为 $5.83\times$，而加速比为 $1.13\times$，$p<0.001$）。去中心化团队在所有可并行性条件下均表现出更大的差距（Wilcoxon 检验 $p<0.001$，中位数 $=1.17$），其中 token 成本随团队规模增大而显著增长（Spearman $\rho=0.40$，$p<0.001$），而加速比则没有呈现这种关系（$\rho=-0.07$，$p=0.15$）。
These findings highlight that deciding to deploy LLM teams is not purely a performance question in terms of speed or accuracy, but an efficiency-cost optimization problem as well, one that can have huge implications for energy consumption and budgets. Extensions of scaling laws have previously incorporated resource costs when evaluating parallel systems [44]. Analogous formulations may therefore provide a useful framework for evaluating LLM team architectures under token or compute budgets.
这些发现强调，决定是否部署 LLM 团队不仅是一个涉及速度或准确率的性能问题，也是一个效率与成本的优化问题，可能对能源消耗和预算产生巨大影响。此前，扩展后的规模定律已在评估并行系统时纳入资源成本 [44]。因此，类似的数学表述或许能为在 token 或计算预算约束下评估 LLM 团队架构提供一个有用的框架。

5 Discussion5 讨论
LLM teams are being increasingly deployed in practice, yet their design remains largely ad-hoc. Teams are assembled, tested, and adjusted through trial and error, with little principled guidance for when coordination will succeed or fail. We argue that distributed systems theory can give us this foundation. The challenges that arise when agents collaborate, such as maintaining consistency, managing synchronization, reducing communication overhead, and absorbing failures, are not novel or unique to LLM teams. They are well-characterized problems that the distributed systems community has spent decades investigating.
LLM 团队在实践中的部署日益增多，但其设计仍在很大程度上依赖临时性的经验做法。团队通过试错来组建、测试和调整，对于协调何时会成功或失败，几乎缺乏基于原理的指导。我们认为，分布式系统理论可以为我们提供这一基础。智能体协作时出现的挑战，例如维持一致性、管理同步、减少通信开销以及应对故障，既不是新问题，也不是 LLM 团队所独有的问题。这些问题已得到充分刻画，分布式系统研究界已为此投入了数十年的研究。
We propose that distributed systems provide a conceptual framework for designing LLM teams, anticipating their limits, and diagnosing how they fail. We demonstrate this framework’s utility across two domains. First, we show that the scalability limits predicted by Amdahl’s Law arise in LLM teams just as they do in distributed systems (Experiment 1). Second, we show that coordination challenges inherent to distributed systems emerge in LLM teams as well (Experiment 2). Across both, we characterize a fundamental architectural tension between centralized and decentralized team structures, a tradeoff inherited from distributed computing that anyone designing or deploying LLM teams must navigate. Finally, we show that beyond raw speedup, LLM teams introduce meaningful computational overhead in token usage.
我们提出，分布式系统为设计 LLM 团队、预判其局限以及诊断其失效方式提供了一个概念框架。我们从两个方面展示了这一框架的实用性。首先，我们表明，阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl’s Law）所预测的可扩展性限制同样会出现在 LLM 团队中，正如其在分布式系统中的表现一样（实验 1）。其次，我们表明，分布式系统固有的协调挑战也会在 LLM 团队中出现（实验 2）。在这两方面，我们刻画了集中式与去中心化团队结构之间的一种根本性架构矛盾；这种权衡继承自分布式计算，任何设计或部署 LLM 团队的人都必须加以应对。最后，我们表明，除了单纯的加速收益之外，LLM 团队还会在 token 用量方面引入不可忽视的计算开销。
5.1 Limitations and future directions5.1 局限性与未来方向
Our experiments provide evidence that supports parallels between LLM teams and distributed systems, but there are many potential avenues for extending this framework in the future.
我们的实验提供了支持 LLM 团队与分布式系统之间存在相似性的证据，但未来仍有许多可能的途径来扩展这一框架。
Our tasks relied on prespecified dependency structures, which depart from real-world settings where dependencies must be inferred or discovered dynamically. Generalizing to tasks such as text analysis, research synthesis, or open-ended reasoning would test whether the theoretical framework extends beyond structured programming. Future work should also examine complementary scaling laws, including Gustafson’s Law, which models performance under scalable workloads, and Gunther’s Universal Scalability Law, which captures non-monotonic scaling due to coordination and contention overhead [34, 35].
我们的任务依赖于预先指定的依赖结构，这与现实场景有所不同，因为现实中的依赖关系必须动态推断或发现。将其推广至文本分析、研究综合或开放式推理等任务，将能够检验该理论框架是否适用于结构化编程以外的领域。未来工作还应考察互补的规模定律，包括对可扩展工作负载下的性能进行建模的古斯塔夫森定律（Gustafson’s Law），以及刻画由协调和争用开销导致的非单调扩展行为的冈瑟通用可扩展性定律（Gunther’s Universal Scalability Law）[34, 35]。
Additionally, our teams consist entirely of homogeneous agents drawn from the same base model. Prior work suggests that heterogeneous teams, composed of agents with different strengths or base models, can yield accuracy benefits [8]. Formalizing how diversity interacts with coordination and scalability, and exploring if these teams can be optimized with algorithms from heterogeneous load balancing, is a natural extension [45].
此外，我们的团队完全由来自同一基础模型的同质智能体组成。已有研究表明，由具有不同优势或采用不同基础模型的智能体组成的异质团队，可以带来准确率收益 [8]。将多样性与协调及可扩展性之间的相互作用形式化，并探索能否利用异质负载均衡算法优化这些团队，是一个自然的扩展方向 [45]。
Beyond overcoming individual constraints on memory and computation, distributed systems provide an additional benefit: fault tolerance, or the ability to gracefully withstand failures that impact individual nodes [46, 47]. Systems are designed so that local failures like node crashes, unresponsiveness, or corrupted outputs do not halt overall progress. LLM agents are similarly susceptible to unpredictable faults, including task abandonment, errors, and hallucinations that produce arbitrary or misleading outputs [48, 49]. Distributed systems address faults through redundancy, verification, and consensus mechanisms, which should be explored in future work to improve LLM team robustness.
除了克服单个节点在内存和计算方面的限制之外，分布式系统还提供了一项额外优势：容错性，即在个别节点发生故障时仍能平稳维持运行的能力 [46, 47]。系统经过专门设计，使节点崩溃、无响应或输出损坏等局部故障不会阻碍整体进展。LLM 智能体同样容易受到不可预测故障的影响，包括放弃任务、出错，以及产生任意或误导性输出的幻觉 [48, 49]。分布式系统通过冗余、验证和共识机制来应对故障，未来工作应探索这些机制，以提高 LLM 团队的鲁棒性。
Finally, a related direction concerns the potential application of scheduling and load balancing protocols to improving task assignment [50, 51, 52, 53]. As LLM teams are deployed in large-scale settings, they will encounter not only well-defined “static” problems but also dynamic and partially observable workloads that emerge as tasks are being executed. Ad-hoc negotiation among agents could worsen the coordination overhead and consistency conflicts documented in this paper. Load balancing algorithms from distributed systems offer a principled alternative, potentially improving efficiency by reducing coordination overhead while maintaining utilization across available agents.
最后，一个相关方向是探索应用调度和负载均衡协议来改进任务分配的可能性 [50, 51, 52, 53]。随着 LLM 团队被部署于大规模场景，它们遇到的将不仅是定义明确的“静态”问题，还包括在任务执行过程中出现的动态且部分可观测的工作负载。智能体之间的临时协商可能会加剧本文所记录的协调开销和一致性冲突。分布式系统中的负载均衡算法提供了一种具有理论依据的替代方案，有望通过减少协调开销，同时维持可用智能体的利用率，来提高效率。
5.2 Conclusion5.2 结论
Distributed computing provides a rigorous and practical framework for understanding LLM team behavior, generating testable predictions, explaining observed inefficiencies, and pointing to concrete design principles. The stakes are high in developing such a principled framework for evaluating these multi-agent systems as they are deployed at scale. LLM teams that are poorly coordinated will not merely underperform: they will propagate errors, generate conflicting outputs, and incur substantial costs in terms of compute, energy, and tokens. As these systems scale, these inefficiencies will further compound. We hope that grounding the design of these teams in a formal framework will offer a path towards systems that are not only more capable, but more predictable, efficient, and responsible.
分布式计算提供了一个严谨且实用的框架，可用于理解 LLM 团队的行为、提出可检验的预测、解释观察到的低效现象，并指明具体的设计原则。随着这些多智能体系统被大规模部署，开发这样一个具有理论依据的评估框架至关重要。协调不良的 LLM 团队不仅会表现不佳，还会传播错误、生成相互冲突的输出，并在计算、能源和 token 方面产生高昂成本。随着这些系统规模的扩大，这些低效问题还会进一步累积。我们希望，将这些团队的设计建立在形式化框架之上，能够为构建能力更强、行为更可预测、效率更高且更负责任的系统提供一条路径。
