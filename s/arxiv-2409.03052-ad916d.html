An Introduction to Centralized Training for Decentralized Execution in Cooperative Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning合作式多智能体强化学习中的集中式训练与分散式执行入门
Multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) has exploded in popularity in recent years. Many approaches have been developed but they can be divided into three main types: centralized training and execution (CTE), centralized training for decentralized execution (CTDE), and Decentralized training and execution (DTE).
近年来，多智能体强化学习（multi-agent reinforcement learning，MARL）的热度迅速攀升。目前已提出许多方法，但它们可以分为三大类：集中式训练与执行（CTE）、集中式训练与分散式执行（CTDE），以及分散式训练与执行（DTE）。
CTE methods assume centralization during training and execution (e.g., with fast, free and perfect communication) and have the most information during execution. That is, the actions of each agent can depend on the information from all agents. As a result, a simple form of CTE can be achieved by using a single-agent RL method with centralized action and observation spaces (maintaining a centralized action-observation history for the partially observable case). CTE methods can potentially outperform the decentralized execution methods (since they allow centralized control) but are less scalable as the (centralized) action and observation spaces scale exponentially with the number of agents. CTE is typically only used in the cooperative MARL case since centralized control implies coordination on what actions will be selected by each agent. CTDE methods are the most common as they can use centralized information during training but execute in a decentralized manner—using only information available to that agent during execution. CTDE is the only paradigm that requires a separate training phase where any available information (e.g., other agent policies, underlying states) can be used. As a result, they can be more scalable than CTE methods, do not require communication during execution, and can often perform well. CTDE fits most naturally with the cooperative case, but can be potentially applied in competitive or mixed settings depending on what information is assumed to be observed. Decentralized training and execution methods make the fewest assumptions and are often simple to implement. In fact, any single-agent RL method can be used for DTE by just letting each agent learn separately. Of course, there are pros and cons to such approaches (Amato, 2024). It is worth noting that DTE is required if no centralized training phase is available (e.g., though a centralized simulator), requiring all agents to learn during online interactions without prior coordination. DTE methods can be applied in cooperative, competitive, or mixed cases.
CTE 方法假设训练和执行期间均可采用集中式机制（例如，通信快速、无成本且完全可靠），因此在执行期间能够获取最充分的信息。也就是说，每个智能体的动作都可以依赖于所有智能体的信息。因此，通过在集中式动作空间和观测空间上使用单智能体强化学习（RL）方法，即可实现一种简单的 CTE 形式（在部分可观测的情况下，维护集中式的动作—观测历史）。CTE 方法的性能有可能优于采用分散式执行的方法（因为它们允许集中式控制），但其可扩展性较差，因为（集中式）动作空间和观测空间的规模会随智能体数量呈指数增长。CTE 通常仅用于合作式 MARL 场景，因为集中式控制意味着需要协调各个智能体将选择的动作。CTDE 方法最为常见，因为它们可以在训练期间使用集中式信息，而在执行期间以分散式方式运行——仅使用该智能体在执行期间可获得的信息。CTDE 是唯一要求设置独立训练阶段的范式，在这一阶段可以使用任何可获得的信息（例如，其他智能体的策略、底层状态）。因此，与 CTE 方法相比，这类方法可以具有更好的可扩展性，在执行期间不需要通信，而且通常能够取得良好的性能。CTDE 最自然地适用于合作场景，但根据对可观测信息所作的假设，也有可能应用于竞争或混合场景。分散式训练与执行方法所需的假设最少，而且通常易于实现。事实上，只需让每个智能体分别学习，任何单智能体 RL 方法都可以用于 DTE。当然，这类方法各有优缺点 (Amato, 2024)。值得注意的是，如果无法开展集中式训练阶段（例如，无法通过集中式模拟器进行训练），就必须采用 DTE，这要求所有智能体在没有事先协调的情况下，通过在线交互进行学习。DTE 方法可以应用于合作、竞争或混合场景。
MARL methods can be further broken up into value-based and policy gradient methods. Value-based methods (e.g., Q-learning) learn a value function and then choose actions based on those values. Policy gradient methods learn an explicit policy representation and attempt to improve the policy in the direction of the gradient. Both classes of methods are widely used in MARL.
MARL 方法还可以进一步分为基于价值的方法和策略梯度方法。基于价值的方法（例如，Q-learning）学习一个价值函数，然后根据这些价值选择动作。策略梯度方法学习显式的策略表示，并尝试沿梯度方向改进策略。这两类方法都在 MARL 中得到广泛应用。
This text is an introduction to CTDE MARL. It is meant to explain the setting, basic concepts, and common methods. It does not cover all work in CTDE MARL as the subarea is quite extensive. I have included work that I believe is important for understanding the main concepts in the subarea and apologize to those that I have omitted.
本文是对 CTDE MARL 的入门介绍。本文旨在阐明其问题设定、基本概念和常用方法。由于这一子领域涉及的研究相当广泛，本文并未涵盖 CTDE MARL 的所有研究工作。我收录了自己认为对理解这一子领域主要概念至关重要的研究工作，并向那些研究工作未被收录的作者致歉。
I will first give a brief description of the cooperative MARL problem in the form of the Dec-POMDP. Then, I present an overview of CTDE and the two main classes of CTDE methods: value function factorization methods and centralized critic actor-critic methods. Value function factorization methods include the well-known VDN (Sunehag et al., 2017), QMIX (Rashid et al., 2018), and QPLEX (Wang et al., 2021a) approaches, while centralized critic methods include MADDPG (Lowe et al., 2017), COMA (Foerster et al., 2018b), and MAPPO (Yu et al., 2022). Finally, I discuss other forms of CTDE such as adding centralized information to decentralized (i.e., independent) learners (such as parameter sharing) and decentralizing centralized solutions.
我将首先以 Dec-POMDP 的形式简要描述合作式 MARL 问题。然后，我将概述 CTDE 及其两类主要方法：价值函数分解方法和采用集中式评价器的行动者—评价器方法。价值函数分解方法包括广为人知的 VDN (Sunehag et al., 2017)、QMIX (Rashid et al., 2018) 和 QPLEX (Wang et al., 2021a)，而集中式评价器方法包括 MADDPG (Lowe et al., 2017)、COMA (Foerster et al., 2018b) 和 MAPPO (Yu et al., 2022)。最后，我将讨论其他形式的 CTDE，例如向分散式（即独立）学习器中引入集中式信息（如参数共享），以及将集中式解转化为分散式解。
The basics of reinforcement learning (in the single-agent setting) are not presented in this text. Anyone interested in RL should read the book by Sutton and Barto (2018). Similarly, for a broader overview of MARL, the recent book by Albrecht, Christianos and Schäfer is recommended (Albrecht et al., 2024).
本文不介绍（单智能体设定下的）强化学习基础知识。任何对 RL 感兴趣的人都应阅读 Sutton and Barto (2018) 的著作。同样，若希望更全面地了解 MARL，推荐阅读 Albrecht、Christianos 和 Schäfer 近期出版的著作 (Albrecht et al., 2024)。
1 The cooperative MARL problem: The Dec-POMDP1 合作式 MARL 问题：Dec-POMDP
Figure 1: A depiction of cooperative MARL—a Dec-POMDP.
图 1：合作式 MARL 的示意图——一个 Dec-POMDP。
The cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) problem can be represented as a Dec-POMDP (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016; Bernstein et al., 2002). Dec-POMDPs generalize POMDPs (Kaelbling et al., 1998) (and MDPs (Puterman, 1994)) to the multi-agent, decentralized setting. As depicted in Figure 1, multiple agents operate under uncertainty based on partial views of the world, with execution unfolding over time. At each step, every agent chooses an action (in parallel) based purely on locally observable information, resulting in each agent obtaining an observation and the team obtaining a joint reward. The shared reward function makes the problem cooperative, but their local views mean that execution is decentralized.
合作式多智能体强化学习（MARL）问题可以表示为一个分散式部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（Dec-POMDP）(Oliehoek and Amato, 2016; Bernstein et al., 2002)。Dec-POMDP 将 POMDP (Kaelbling et al., 1998)（以及 MDP (Puterman, 1994)）推广到多智能体、分散式设定。如图 1 所示，多个智能体基于对世界的局部观测，在不确定性下行动，执行过程随时间展开。在每一步，每个智能体仅依据局部可观测信息（并行地）选择一个动作，随后每个智能体获得一个观测，整个团队获得一个联合奖励。共享奖励函数使该问题具有合作性质，但各智能体只能获得局部信息，因此执行是去中心化的。
Formally, a Dec-POMDP is defined by tuple $\langle\mathbb{I},\mathbb{S},\{{\mathbb{A}}_{i}\},T,R,\{{\mathbb{O}}_{i}\},O,\mathcal{H},\gamma\rangle$ . For simplicity, I define the finite version but it easily extends to the continuous case:

$\mathbb{I}$ is a finite set of agents of size $|\mathbb{I}|=n$ ;
$\mathbb{I}$ 是一个有限智能体集合，其大小为 $|\mathbb{I}|=n$；
$\mathbb{S}$ is a finite set of states with designated initial state distribution ${{{b}}_{0}}$ ;[fn1]
$\mathbb{S}$ 是一个有限状态集合，其指定的初始状态分布为 ${{{b}}_{0}}$；[fn1]

${\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ is a finite set of actions for each agent $i$ with $\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}=\times_{i}{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ the set of joint actions;
${\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ 是每个智能体 $i$ 的有限动作集合，其中 $\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}=\times_{i}{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ 为联合动作集合；

$R$ 是奖励函数：$R:\mathbb{S}\times\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}\to\mathbb{R}$，表示处于状态 $s\in\mathbb{S}$ 并执行动作 $\mathbf{a}\in\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ 时获得的即时奖励；

${\mathbb{O}}_{i}$ 是每个智能体 $i$ 的有限观测集合，其中 $\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}=\times_{i}{\mathbb{O}}_{i}$ 为联合观测集合；

$O$ 是观测概率函数：$O:\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}\times\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}\times\mathbb{S}\to[0,1]$，表示在执行动作 $\mathbf{a}\in\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ 并转移到状态 $s^{\prime}\in\mathbb{S}$ 后，获得观测 $\mathbf{o}\in\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}$ 的概率（即 $O(\mathbf{a},s^{\prime},\mathbf{o})=\Pr(\mathbf{o}|\mathbf{a},s^{\prime})$）；

$\mathcal{H}$ 是终止前的步数，称为时域（horizon）；

A solution to a Dec-POMDP is a joint policy, denoted $\mathbf{\pi}$ —a set of policies, one for each agent, each of which is denoted ${\pi}_{i}$ . Because the state is not directly observed, it is typically beneficial for each agent to remember a history of its observations (and potentially actions). Then, a local policy, ${\pi}_{i}$ , for an agent is a mapping from local action-observation histories to actions or probability distributions over actions. A local deterministic policy for agent $i$ , ${\pi}_{i}({h}_{i})={a}_{i}$ , maps ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ , where ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}$ is the set of local observation histories, ${h}_{i}=\{{a}_{i,0},{o}_{i,0},\ldots,{a}_{i,t-1},{o}_{i,t-1}\}$ [fn3], by agent $i$ up to the current time step, $t$ . Note that histories have implicit time steps due to their length which we do not include in the notation (i.e., we always assume a history starts on the first time step and the last time step is defined by the number of action-observation pairs). We can denote the joint histories for all agents at a given time step as $\mathbf{h}{}=\langle{h}_{1},\ldots,{h}_{n}\rangle$ . A joint deterministic policy is denoted $\pi(\mathbf{h}{})=\mathbf{a}=\langle{\pi}_{1}({h}_{1}),\dots,{\pi}_{n}({h}_{n})\rangle=\langle{a}_{1},\ldots,{a}_{n}\rangle$ . A stochastic local policy for agent $i$ is ${\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$ , representing the probability of choosing action ${a}_{i}$ in history ${h}_{i}$ . A joint stochastic policy is denoted $\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$ . Because one policy is generated for each agent and these policies depend only on local observations, they operate in a decentralized manner.
Dec-POMDP 的解是一个联合策略，记为 $\mathbf{\pi}$，即一组策略，每个智能体各有一个策略，分别记为 ${\pi}_{i}$。由于无法直接观测状态，每个智能体记住自身的观测历史（也可能包括动作历史）通常是有益的。于是，智能体的局部策略 ${\pi}_{i}$ 是从局部动作—观测历史到动作或动作概率分布的映射。智能体 $i$ 的局部确定性策略 ${\pi}_{i}({h}_{i})={a}_{i}$ 定义了映射 ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$，其中 ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}$ 是智能体 $i$ 截至当前时间步 $t$ 的局部观测历史 ${h}_{i}=\{{a}_{i,0},{o}_{i,0},\ldots,{a}_{i,t-1},{o}_{i,t-1}\}$ [fn3] 的集合。请注意，历史的长度隐含了时间步，我们未在记号中将其写出（即我们始终假设历史从第一个时间步开始，最后一个时间步由动作—观测对的数量确定）。我们可以将给定时间步所有智能体的联合历史记为 $\mathbf{h}{}=\langle{h}_{1},\ldots,{h}_{n}\rangle$。联合确定性策略记为 $\pi(\mathbf{h}{})=\mathbf{a}=\langle{\pi}_{1}({h}_{1}),\dots,{\pi}_{n}({h}_{n})\rangle=\langle{a}_{1},\ldots,{a}_{n}\rangle$。智能体 $i$ 的随机局部策略为 ${\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$，表示在历史 ${h}_{i}$ 下选择动作 ${a}_{i}$ 的概率。联合随机策略记为 $\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$。由于为每个智能体分别生成一个策略，且这些策略仅依赖局部观测，因此它们以去中心化方式运行。
Footnote: 3 Sometimes, ${o}_{i,0}$ is also included as an observation generated by the initial state distribution
脚注：3 有时也会将 ${o}_{i,0}$ 作为由初始状态分布生成的观测纳入其中。
Many researchers just use observation histories (without including actions), which is sufficient for deterministic policies but may not be for stochastic policies (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016). Deterministic policies will be used in the value-based methods in Section 3 while stochastic policies will be used in the policy gradient gradient methods in Section 4. There always exists an optimal deterministic joint policy in Dec-POMDPs (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016), but stochastic (or continuous) policies are needed for policy gradient methods.
许多研究者仅使用观测历史（不包含动作），这对于确定性策略而言已经足够，但对于随机策略而言可能并不充分（Oliehoek and Amato, 2016）。第 3 节的基于价值的方法将使用确定性策略，而第 4 节的策略梯度方法将使用随机策略。Dec-POMDP 中总是存在最优的确定性联合策略（Oliehoek and Amato, 2016），但策略梯度方法需要随机（或连续）策略。
The value of a joint policy, $\pi$ , at joint history $\mathbf{h}{}$ can be defined for the case of discrete states and observations as
对于离散状态和观测的情形，联合策略 $\pi$ 在联合历史 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 处的价值可定义为
$$\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{})=\sum_{s}P(s|\mathbf{h}{},{{{b}}_{0}})\Big[R(s,\mathbf{a})+\gamma\sum_{s^{\prime}}P(s^{\prime}|\mathbf{a},s)\sum_{\mathbf{o}}P(\mathbf{o}|\mathbf{a},s^{\prime})\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{}\mathbf{a}\mathbf{o})\Big]\Big|_{\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{h}{})}$$
where $P(s|\mathbf{h}{},{{{b}}_{0}})$ is the probability of state $s$ after observing joint history $\mathbf{h}{}$ starting from state distribution ${{{b}}_{0}}$ and $\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{h}{})$ is the joint action taken at the joint history. Also, $\mathbf{h}{}\mathbf{a}\mathbf{o}$ represents $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}$ , the joint history after taking joint action $\mathbf{a}$ in joint history $\mathbf{h}{}$ and observing joint observation $\mathbf{o}$ . In the RL context, algorithms do not iterate over states and observations to explicitly calculate this expectation but approximate it through sampling. For the finite-horizon case, $\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{})=0$ when the length of $\mathbf{h}{}$ equals the horizon $\mathcal{H}$ , showing the value function includes the time step from the history.
其中，$P(s|\mathbf{h}{},{{{b}}_{0}})$ 表示从状态分布 ${{{b}}_{0}}$ 出发，在观测到联合历史 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 后处于状态 $s$ 的概率，而 $\mathbf{a}=\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{h}{})$ 是在该联合历史下采取的联合动作。此外，$\mathbf{h}{}\mathbf{a}\mathbf{o}$ 表示 $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}$，即在联合历史 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 下采取联合动作 $\mathbf{a}$ 并获得联合观测 $\mathbf{o}$ 后的联合历史。在强化学习（RL）背景下，算法不会遍历状态和观测来显式计算这一期望，而是通过采样来近似它。对于有限时域情形，当 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 的长度等于时域 $\mathcal{H}$ 时，$\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{})=0$，这表明价值函数通过历史包含了时间步信息。
We will often want to evaluate policies starting from the beginning—starting at the initial state distribution before any action or observation. Starting from this initial, null history we denote the value of a policy as $\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{0})$ . An optimal joint policy beginning at $\mathbf{h}{}_{0}$ is $\argmax_{\mathbf{\pi}}\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{0})$ , where the $\argmax$ here denotes enumeration over decentralized policies. The optimal joint policy is then the set of local policies for each agent that provides the highest value, which is denoted $\mathbf{V}^{*}$ .
我们常常希望从最开始评估策略，即从尚未采取任何动作或获得任何观测时的初始状态分布出发。从这一初始的空历史出发，我们将策略的价值记为 $\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{0})$。从 $\mathbf{h}{}_{0}$ 开始的一个最优联合策略为 $\argmax_{\mathbf{\pi}}\mathbf{V}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{0})$，其中，这里的 $\argmax$ 表示对去中心化策略进行枚举。因此，最优联合策略是由各智能体的局部策略组成、能够取得最高价值的策略集合，该最高价值记为 $\mathbf{V}^{*}$。
Reinforcement learning methods often use history-action values, $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ , rather than just history values $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ . $\mathbf{Q}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ is the value of choosing joint action $\mathbf{a}$ at joint history $\mathbf{h}{}$ and then continuing with policy $\mathbf{\pi}$ ,

$$\mathbf{Q}^{\mathbf{\pi}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\sum_{s}P(s|\mathbf{h}{},{{{b}}_{0}})\Big[R(\mathbf{a},s)+\gamma\sum_{s^{\prime}}P(s^{\prime}|\mathbf{a},s)\sum_{\mathbf{o}}P(\mathbf{o}|\mathbf{a},s^{\prime})\mathbf{Q}^{\mathbf{\pi}}\big(\mathbf{h}{}\mathbf{a}\mathbf{o},\mathbf{a}^{\prime})|_{\mathbf{a}^{\prime}=\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{h}{}\mathbf{a}\mathbf{o})}\big)\Big],$$
while $\mathbf{Q}^{*}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ is the value of choosing action $\mathbf{a}$ at history $\mathbf{h}{}$ and then continuing with the optimal policy, $\mathbf{\pi}^{*}$ .
而 $\mathbf{Q}^{*}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ 是在历史 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 下选择动作 $\mathbf{a}$，随后继续遵循最优策略 $\mathbf{\pi}^{*}$ 的价值。
Policies that depend only on a single observation仅依赖单次观测的策略
It is somewhat popular to define policies that depend only on a single observation rather than the whole observation history. That is, ${\pi}_{i}:{\mathbb{O}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ , rather than ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ . This type of policy is often referred to as reactive or Markov since it just depends on (or reacts from) the past observation. These reactive policies are typically not desirable since they can be arbitrarily worse than policies that consider history information (Murphy, 2000) but can perform well or even be optimal in simpler subclasses of Dec-POMDPs (Goldman and Zilberstein, 2004). In general, reactive policies reduce the complexity of finding a solution (since many fewer policies need to be considered) but only perform well in limited cases such as when the problem is not really partially observable or when the observation history isn’t helpful for decision-making.
将策略定义为仅依赖单次观测而非整个观测历史，是一种较为常见的做法。也就是说，${\pi}_{i}:{\mathbb{O}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$，而非 ${\mathbb{H}}_{i}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$。这类策略通常称为反应式策略或马尔可夫策略，因为它仅依赖于上一次观测（或对其作出反应）。这些反应式策略通常并不理想，因为它们相较于考虑历史信息的策略，性能差距可以任意大 (Murphy, 2000)，但在较简单的 Dec-POMDPs 子类中，它们可能表现良好，甚至达到最优 (Goldman and Zilberstein, 2004)。一般而言，反应式策略降低了求解的复杂度（因为需要考虑的策略少得多），但仅在有限的情形下表现良好，例如问题实际上并非部分可观测，或观测历史对决策没有帮助时。
The fully observable case完全可观测情形
It is worth noting that if the cooperative problem is fully observable, it becomes much simpler. Specifically, a multi-agent MDP (MMDP) can be defined by the tuple $\langle\mathbb{I},\mathbb{S},\{{\mathbb{A}}_{i}\},T,R,\mathcal{H},\gamma\rangle$ (Boutilier, 1996). Each agent can observe the full state in this case. In the CTDE context, solving an MMDP can be done by standard MDP methods where the action space is the joint action space, $\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}=\times_{i}{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ . The resulting policy, $\mathbb{S}\to\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ , can be trivially decentralized as $\mathbb{S}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$ for each agent $i$ . A number of methods have been developed for learning in MMDPs (and other multi-agent models) (Buşoniu et al., 2008).
值得注意的是，如果协作问题是完全可观测的，它就会变得简单得多。具体而言，多智能体 MDP（MMDP）可由元组 $\langle\mathbb{I},\mathbb{S},\{{\mathbb{A}}_{i}\},T,R,\mathcal{H},\gamma\rangle$ 定义 (Boutilier, 1996)。在这种情况下，每个智能体都能观测到完整状态。在集中训练、去中心化执行（CTDE）背景下，可以使用标准 MDP 方法求解 MMDP，其中动作空间为联合动作空间 $\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}=\times_{i}{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$。得到的策略 $\mathbb{S}\to\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ 可以很容易地去中心化，成为每个智能体 $i$ 的策略 $\mathbb{S}\to{\mathbb{A}}_{i}$。针对 MMDPs（以及其他多智能体模型）中的学习问题，已有多种方法被提出 (Buşoniu et al., 2008)。
Finding solutions in Dec-POMDPs is much more challenging. This can even be seen by the (finite-horizon) complexity for finite problems—optimally solving an MDP is P-complete (polynomial in the size of the system) (Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis, 1987; Littman et al., 1995) and optimally solving a POMDP is PSPACE (essentially exponential in the actions and observations), (Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis, 1987), while optimally solving a Dec-POMDP is NEXP-complete (essentially doubly exponential) (Bernstein et al., 2002). This can be intuitively understood by considering the simplest possible solution method for each class of problems. For an MDP, you could search all policies mapping states to (joint) actions: $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{|\mathbb{S}|}$ of them. For a POMDP, you need to consider histories so centralized control (mapping joint histories to joint actions) would result in searching over $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{|\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}|^{\mathcal{H}}}$ possibilities.[fn4] In the Dec-POMDP case, each agent would have $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}}}$ possible policies, so the possible number of possible joint policies would be $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}^{|\mathbb{I}|}}}$ if all agents have the same number of actions and observations as agent $i$ . Of course, state-of-the-art methods are more sophisticated than this and the complexity is for the worst case. Most real-world problems are intractable to solve optimally anyway. Regardless, these numbers make it clear that searching the joint space of history-based policies is enormous and the resulting partially observable problem is much more challenging than the fully observable case.
在 Dec-POMDPs 中寻找解要困难得多。这一点甚至可以从有限问题的（有限时域）复杂度中看出：最优求解 MDP 是 P-complete（相对于系统规模为多项式复杂度）(Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis, 1987; Littman et al., 1995)，最优求解 POMDP 属于 PSPACE（本质上相对于动作和观测呈指数复杂度）(Papadimitriou and Tsitsiklis, 1987)，而最优求解 Dec-POMDP 是 NEXP-complete（本质上为双重指数复杂度）(Bernstein et al., 2002)。考虑每类问题最简单的求解方法，就可以直观地理解这一点。对于 MDP，可以搜索所有将状态映射到（联合）动作的策略：共有 $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{|\mathbb{S}|}$ 个。对于 POMDP，需要考虑历史，因此集中式控制（将联合历史映射为联合动作）需要在 $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{|\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}|^{\mathcal{H}}}$ 种可能性中进行搜索。[fn4] 在 Dec-POMDP 情形下，每个智能体都有 $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}}}$ 种可能的策略，因此，如果所有智能体的动作数量和观测数量都与智能体 $i$ 相同，则可能的联合策略数量为 $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}^{|\mathbb{I}|}}}$。当然，最先进的方法比这更为复杂，而且这里的复杂度针对的是最坏情况。无论如何，大多数现实世界问题都难以求得最优解。尽管如此，这些数字清楚地表明，基于历史的策略所构成的联合搜索空间极其庞大，由此产生的部分可观测问题比完全可观测情形更具挑战性。
Footnote: 4 Since we are assuming deterministic policies, the actions are fixed but we would still have to search over all possible observation histories up to the given horizon, resulting in $\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}^{\mathcal{H}}$ possible histories.
脚注：4 由于我们假设策略是确定性的，动作是固定的，但仍然必须搜索给定时域内所有可能的观测历史，因此共有 $\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}^{\mathcal{H}}$ 种可能的历史。
2 CTDE overview2 CTDE 概述
The idea of centralized training for decentralized execution is quite vague. The general idea of CTDE the Dec-POMDP case originated (with the Dec-POMDP itself) in the planning literature (Bernstein et al., 2002), where planning would be centralized but execution was decentralized. This idea goes back further to team decision making more generally since it is natural to think of deriving a solution for the team as a whole and then assigning corresponding parts to the team members (Marschak, 1955; Radner, 1962; Ho, 1980). This concept was carried over to the reinforcement learning case and has come to mean that there is an ‘offline’[fn5] training phase where some amount of centralization is allowed (such as in (Kraemer and Banerjee, 2016)). After this centralized training phase is complete, agents must then act in a decentralized manner based on their own histories (as given by the Dec-POMDP definition above). Any information that would not be available during decentralized execution can be used in the centralized training phase ranging from neural network parameters, policies of the other agents, a joint value estimate, etc. Nevertheless, CTDE has never been formally defined (to the best of my knowledge) and I will not do so here. I will take a wide view of CTDE and consider it to include any centralized information or coordination during learning—anything that is not available during decentralized execution.
集中式训练、分散式执行这一理念相当模糊。Dec-POMDP 情形下 CTDE 的总体理念与 Dec-POMDP 本身一同起源于规划文献 (Bernstein et al., 2002)，其中规划是集中式的，而执行是分散式的。这一理念还可以进一步追溯到更一般的团队决策，因为先为整个团队推导出一个解决方案，再将相应部分分配给团队成员，是一种很自然的思路 (Marschak, 1955; Radner, 1962; Ho, 1980)。这一概念被沿用到强化学习情形中，其含义逐渐演变为：存在一个“离线”[fn5]训练阶段，在该阶段允许一定程度的集中化（例如（Kraemer and Banerjee, 2016）中的做法）。这一集中式训练阶段完成后，智能体必须基于各自的历史，以分散式方式行动（如上文 Dec-POMDP 的定义所规定的那样）。在集中式训练阶段，可以使用分散式执行期间无法获得的任何信息，包括神经网络参数、其他智能体的策略、联合价值估计等。不过，据我所知，CTDE 从未被正式定义，本文也不会给出正式定义。我将对 CTDE 采取宽泛的理解，认为它涵盖学习过程中的任何集中式信息或协调，即分散式执行期间无法获得的任何信息或协调。
Footnote: 5 Offline does not refer to having a fixed training set (Levine et al., 2020) but rather in a different setting than what is used for execution (which would be considered online).
脚注：5 此处的“离线”并非指使用固定的训练集（Levine et al., 2020），而是指所处环境不同于执行时使用的环境（执行时的环境则被视为在线环境）。
CTDE methods for Dec-POMDPs vary widely. Some methods add centralized information to DTE methods. These approaches should be scalable but use limited centralized information. Other methods are at the other extreme, where they learn a centralized policy and then attempt to decentralize it so it can be executed without the centralized information. Most CTDE methods fall somewhere in between these two ideas. The main class of value-based CTDE methods learns a set of decentralized Q-functions (one for each agent) by factoring a joint value function, as in value function factorization methods. The main class of policy gradient CTDE methods learns a centralized critic that approximates the joint value function for all agents and that centralized critic is used to update a set of decentralized actors (one per agent). I discuss these ideas in more detail below.
用于 Dec-POMDP 的 CTDE 方法差异很大。一些方法在 DTE 方法中加入集中式信息。这些方法应当具有可扩展性，但使用的集中式信息有限。另一些方法则处于另一个极端：它们先学习一个集中式策略，再尝试将其分散化，使其能够在没有集中式信息的情况下执行。大多数 CTDE 方法介于这两种思路之间。基于价值的 CTDE 方法中，主要的一类通过分解联合价值函数来学习一组分散式 Q 函数（每个智能体一个），价值函数分解方法便属于这一类。基于策略梯度的 CTDE 方法中，主要的一类学习一个集中式评价器（critic），用于近似所有智能体的联合价值函数，并利用该集中式评价器更新一组分散式行动器（actor，每个智能体一个）。下文将更详细地讨论这些思路。
3 Value function factorization methods3 价值函数分解方法
There is a long history of learning factored value functions in multi-agent reinforcement learning, but many previous methods were focused on more efficiently learning a joint value function (e.g., (Kok and Vlassis, 2006)). Other methods have also been developed that share Q-functions during training to improve coordination (Schneider et al., 1999). Modern value function factorization methods learn Q-functions for each agent by combining them into a joint Q-function and calculating a loss based on the joint Q-function. In this way, the joint Q-function is factored into local, decentralized Q-values that can be used during decentralized execution. This idea is appealing because a joint Q-function can be learned to approximate the one in Equation 2 but agents can choose actions based on their decentralized Q-values. Additional details and the most popular methods are below.
在多智能体强化学习中，学习分解形式的价值函数已有很长的历史，但以往许多方法侧重于更高效地学习联合价值函数（例如 (Kok and Vlassis, 2006)）。还有一些方法通过在训练期间共享 Q 函数来改善协调 (Schneider et al., 1999)。现代价值函数分解方法将各智能体的 Q 函数组合成一个联合 Q 函数，并基于该联合 Q 函数计算损失，从而学习各智能体的 Q 函数。通过这种方式，联合 Q 函数被分解为局部的、分散式的 Q 值，这些 Q 值可用于分散式执行。这一思路很有吸引力，因为可以学习一个联合 Q 函数来近似公式 2 中的 Q 函数，同时智能体又能基于各自的分散式 Q 值选择动作。下文将介绍更多细节以及最常用的方法。

具体而言，它假设 Q 函数具有如下近似分解形式：
$$\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\approx\sum_{i\in\mathbb{I}}^{n}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$$
That is, the joint Q-function is approximated by a sum over each agent’s individual Q-function. This is a form of factorization—the joint Q-function is factored as a sum of the local ${Q}_{i}$ functions (Guestrin et al., 2001). It is worth noting that each ${Q}_{i}$ is technically a utility and not a value function since it is not estimating a particular expected return but can be any arbitrary value in the sum.
也就是说，联合 Q 函数由各个智能体自身的 Q 函数之和来近似。这是一种因子分解形式——联合 Q 函数被分解为局部 ${Q}_{i}$ 函数之和 (Guestrin et al., 2001)。值得注意的是，严格来说，每个 ${Q}_{i}$ 都是效用函数，而非价值函数，因为它并不估计某个特定的期望回报，而可以是求和中的任意数值。
The architecture diagram is given in Figure 3. Each agent learns an individual set of Q-values that depend only on local information. These Q-networks take the current observation as input to a recurrent network, which updates the history representation and then outputs Q-values for that agent. During training, these individual Q-values are summed to generate the joint Q-value which can then be used to calculate the loss like (a centralized form of) DRQN:
架构图如图 3 所示。每个智能体学习一组仅依赖于局部信息的 Q 值。这些 Q 网络将当前观测作为循环网络的输入，循环网络更新历史表示，然后输出该智能体的 Q 值。在训练期间，将这些单独的 Q 值相加以生成联合 Q 值，随后可像（集中式形式的）DRQN 那样利用该联合 Q 值计算损失：
$$\mathcal{L}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{<\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a},r,\mathbf{o}>\sim\mathcal{D}}\Big[\big(y-\sum_{i}^{n}{Q}_{i}^{\theta}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})\big)^{2}\Big]\text{, where\,\, }y=r+\gamma\sum_{i}^{n}\max_{{a}_{i}^{\prime}}{Q}_{i}^{\theta^{-}}({h}_{i}^{\prime},{a}_{i}^{\prime})$$
where ${h}_{i}^{\prime}$ is ${h}_{i}{a}_{i}{o}_{i}$ for ${h}_{i}$ taken from $\mathbf{h}{}$ , ${a}_{i}$ from $\mathbf{a}$ , and ${o}_{i}$ from $\mathbf{o}$ .
其中，${h}_{i}^{\prime}$ 为 ${h}_{i}{a}_{i}{o}_{i}$，${h}_{i}$ 取自 $\mathbf{h}{}$，${a}_{i}$ 取自 $\mathbf{a}$，${o}_{i}$ 取自 $\mathbf{o}$。
Since the loss uses the sum of the agent’s Q-functions, the result is a set of Q-values whose sum approximates the joint Q-function. This approximation will be lossless in extreme cases (e.g., full independence) but it may be close or still permit the agents to select the best actions even if the approximation is poor. The approach is also scalable since the max used to create the target value $y$ is not over all agent actions as in the centralized case ( $\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ ), but done separately for each agent in Equation 6.
由于损失使用了各智能体 Q 函数之和，因此最终得到一组 Q 值，其总和近似于联合 Q 函数。这种近似在极端情况下（例如完全独立时）是无损的；在其他情况下，它可能接近真实值，或者即使近似效果较差，仍能使智能体选择最优动作。该方法还具有可扩展性，因为用于构造目标值 $y$ 的最大化操作并不像集中式情形那样遍历所有智能体的联合动作（$\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$），而是在公式 6 中针对每个智能体分别进行。

3.3 QMIX3.3 QMIX
QMIX (Rashid et al., 2018; Rashid et al., 2020b) extends the value factorization idea of VDN to allow more general decompositions. In particular, instead of assuming the joint Q-function, $Q(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ , factors into a sum of local Q-values, ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ , QMIX assumes the joint Q-function is a monotonic function of the individual Q-functions. The sum used in VDN is also a monotonic function but QMIX allows more general (possibly nonlinear) monotonic functions to be learned.
QMIX（Rashid et al., 2018; Rashid et al., 2020b）扩展了 VDN 的价值分解思想，允许采用更一般的分解形式。具体而言，QMIX 不再假设联合 Q 函数 $Q(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ 可以分解为局部 Q 值 ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ 之和，而是假设联合 Q 函数是各个体 Q 函数的单调函数。VDN 使用的求和运算也是一种单调函数，但 QMIX 允许学习更一般的（可能是非线性的）单调函数。
Specifically, QMIX assumes the following approximate factorization of the Q-function:
具体来说，QMIX 假设 Q 函数具有如下近似分解形式：
$$\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\approx f_{mono}({Q}_{i}({h}_{1},{a}_{1}),\ldots,Q_{n}({h}_{n},{a}_{n}))$$
This monotonic assumption means the argmax over the local Q-functions is also an argmax over the joint Q-function, This property allows agents to choose actions from their local Q-functions instead of having to choose them from a joint Q-function—ensuring the choice would be the same in both cases. Like in VDN, it also makes the training more efficient since calculating the argmax in the update Equation 9 is linear in the number of agents rather than exponential.
这一单调性假设意味着，使各局部 Q 函数最大化的动作组合也能使联合 Q 函数最大化；该性质允许智能体根据各自的局部 Q 函数选择动作，而无需根据联合 Q 函数选择，并确保这两种方式得到的选择相同。与 VDN 一样，这也提高了训练效率，因为在更新公式 9 中计算 argmax 的复杂度随智能体数量呈线性增长，而非指数增长。
Son et al. (2019) formalized this property as the Individual-Global-Max (IGM) principal. IGM states that the argmax over the joint Q-function is the same as argmaxing over each agent’s individual Q-function. The definition below is for a particular history but it should ideally hold for all histories (or at least the ones visited by the policy).
Son et al. (2019) 将这一性质形式化为个体—全局最大化（Individual-Global-Max，IGM）原则。IGM 指出，对联合 Q 函数执行 argmax，等价于对每个智能体的个体 Q 函数分别执行 argmax。下面的定义针对某个特定历史，但理想情况下，它应对所有历史（或至少对策略访问到的历史）成立。
Definition 1 (Individual-Global-Max (IGM) Son et al. (2019))定义 1（个体—全局最大化（IGM），Son et al. (2019)）
For a joint action-value function $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ , where $\mathbf{h}{}=\langle{h}_{1},\ldots,{h}_{n}\rangle$ is a joint action-observation history, if there exist individual functions [ ${Q}_{i}$ ], such that:
对于联合动作价值函数 $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$，其中 $\mathbf{h}{}=\langle{h}_{1},\ldots,{h}_{n}\rangle$ 为联合动作—观测历史，若存在个体函数 [ ${Q}_{i}$ ]，使得：
$$\argmax_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\left(\begin{array}[]{ll}\argmax_{{a}_{1}}Q_{1}({h}_{1},{a}_{1})\\ \hfill\vdots\hfill\\ \argmax_{{a}_{n}}Q_{n}({h}_{n},{a}_{n})\end{array}\right),$$
then, [ ${Q}_{i}$ ] satisfy IGM for $Q$ at $\mathbf{h}{}$ .
则 [ ${Q}_{i}$ ] 在 $\mathbf{h}{}$ 处对 $Q$ 满足 IGM。

A simplified version of the QMIX architecture is shown in Figure 4. Like VDN, each agent has an RNN that takes in the current observation and can output Q-values for the updated history over all actions.[fn7] A particular action is then chosen (e.g., using $\epsilon$ -greedy action selection) using each ${Q}_{i}$ and each ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ is fed into the mixing network. The mixing network is made to be monotonic by restricting the weights (but not the bias terms) to be non-negative. The mixing network can also receive the state to potentially improve performance. In practice, the state is given as input to hypernetworks (Ha et al., 2017) which generate the weights for the layers of the mixing network. Because the output depends on the state, it is $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$ , and is often called $\mathbf{Q}_{tot}$ .
图 4 展示了 QMIX 架构的一个简化版本。与 VDN 类似，每个智能体都有一个 RNN，它接收当前观测，并可针对更新后的历史输出所有动作的 Q 值。[fn7] 随后，利用各自的 ${Q}_{i}$ 选择一个具体动作（例如，使用 $\epsilon$-贪心动作选择），并将每个 ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ 输入混合网络。通过将权重（但不包括偏置项）限制为非负值，使混合网络具有单调性。混合网络还可以接收状态作为输入，从而有望提升性能。在实践中，状态被作为输入提供给超网络（hypernetworks）（Ha et al., 2017），由超网络生成混合网络各层的权重。由于输出依赖于状态，因此将其记为 $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$，通常也称为 $\mathbf{Q}_{tot}$。

The network is trained end-to-end, like VDN by calculating a loss between $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$ and the joint return. The corresponding loss is:
与 VDN 类似，该网络通过计算 $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$ 与联合回报之间的损失进行端到端训练。相应的损失为：
$$\mathcal{L}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{<\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a},r,\mathbf{o},s^{\prime}>\sim\mathcal{D}}\Big[\big(y-\mathbf{Q}^{\theta}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})\big)^{2}\Big],\text{where\, }y=r+\gamma\mathbf{Q}^{\theta^{-}}(\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime},s^{\prime},\tilde{\mathbf{a}}^{\prime}),\\ \text{ and\, }\tilde{\mathbf{a}}^{\prime}=\langle\argmax_{{a}_{1}^{\prime}}Q_{1}({h}_{1}^{\prime},{a}_{1}^{\prime}),\ldots,\argmax_{{a}_{n}^{\prime}}Q_{n}({h}_{n}^{\prime},{a}_{n}^{\prime})\rangle$$
where the value on the next time step, $\mathbf{Q}^{\theta^{-}}(\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime},s^{\prime},\tilde{\mathbf{a}}^{\prime})$ , is gotten by inputting the argmax over each agent’s local Q-value using $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}=\langle{h}_{1}^{\prime},\ldots,{h}_{n}^{\prime}\rangle$ as well as the corresponding state $s^{\prime}$ that is sampled from the buffer at $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}$ .
其中，下一时间步的值 $\mathbf{Q}^{\theta^{-}}(\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime},s^{\prime},\tilde{\mathbf{a}}^{\prime})$ 的获取方式是：使用 $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}=\langle{h}_{1}^{\prime},\ldots,{h}_{n}^{\prime}\rangle$，分别对每个智能体的局部 Q 值取 argmax，并将所得动作以及从缓冲区中采样得到的、与 $\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}$ 对应的状态 $s^{\prime}$ 一同作为输入。
The mixing network is only needed during training. Agents can retain their RNNs (which are just DRQNs) for selecting actions during decentralized execution just like VDN.
混合网络仅在训练期间需要使用。与 VDN 一样，智能体可以保留各自的 RNN（实际上就是 DRQN），用于在分散执行期间选择动作。
Again, such a factorization may not be possible in all problems so QMIX may not be able to approximate the true joint Q-function in all problems accurately. In particular, as discussed in the paper, this factorization should fail when agents’ action choices depend on (at least some) other agents’ actions at the same time step.
同样，并非所有问题都能进行这种分解，因此 QMIX 可能无法在所有问题中准确逼近真实的联合 Q 函数。具体而言，正如论文中所讨论的，当智能体的动作选择依赖于同一时间步中（至少某些）其他智能体的动作时，这种分解应当会失效。
Weighted QMIX加权 QMIX
extends QMIX in an attempt to improve its expressiveness (Rashid et al., 2020a). The main idea is to weigh the actions at different histories differently so the Q-values for some actions can be accurately represented but other action values can be less accurate. For instance, if you knew the optimal policy, you could set the weight highest for the optimal action in each history. You don’t need to accurately represent the other action values but you do need to make sure they are lower than the optimal action (so the policy will choose the optimal action!).
对 QMIX 进行了扩展，试图提升其表达能力（Rashid et al., 2020a）。其主要思想是对不同历史下的动作赋予不同权重，使某些动作的 Q 值能够得到准确表示，而允许其他动作的价值不那么准确。例如，如果已知最优策略，就可以为每个历史下的最优动作设置最高权重。你不需要准确表示其他动作的价值，但必须确保这些价值低于最优动作的价值（这样策略才会选择最优动作！）。
Specifically, weighted QMIX uses weights $w(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\in(0,1]$ to adjust the importance of joint history-action pairs in the loss (as formalized below).[fn8] It turns out that there always exists some weighing that will allow the optimal policy to be recovered by independently argmaxing over each agent’s ${Q}_{i}$ . An idealized form of the algorithm could also converge in the fully observable case but this proof doesn’t extend to the partially observable case or when approximations are used (i.e., the deep RL case).
具体而言，加权 QMIX 使用权重 $w(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\in(0,1]$ 来调整联合历史与动作对在损失中的重要性（下文将给出形式化定义）。[fn8] 结果表明，总存在某种加权方式，使得通过分别对各智能体的 ${Q}_{i}$ 独立取 argmax，就能恢复最优策略。该算法的一种理想化形式在完全可观测情形下也能够收敛，但这一证明无法推广到部分可观测情形或使用近似的情形（即深度强化学习情形）。

The method has two main components, a QMIX network where the output is weighted, and an approximation of the optimal Q-value called $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}^{*}$ . The first network takes the output of QMIX (as seen in Figure 4) and weighs it as seen in Equation 10. For easier differentiation with the other Q network, we call the output of the first (QMIX-style) network $\mathbf{Q}_{tot}$ . This network is exactly the same as the one in QMIX but the output is weighted in the loss to prioritize different actions at different histories. The $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}^{*}$ network is similar to the one in QMIX but it does not limit the weights to be non-negative and doesn’t use a hypernetwork (just directly inputting the state into the mixing network). These networks don’t share parameters and are trained using the losses given in Equation 10. The same target ( $y$ ) is used in both cases, which now uses the unconstrained output $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}^{*}$ . The argmax is done the same way as in QMIX, where the actions are chosen using each agent’s ${Q}_{i}$ . As a result, the argmax is tractable but a potentially more accurate value of those actions can be provided from $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}^{*}$ rather than $\mathbf{Q}_{tot}$ .

3.5 QPLEX3.5 QPLEX
QPLEX (Wang et al., 2021a) further extends the value factorization idea to (provably) include more general decentralized policies. In particular, QPLEX can potentially learn any set of Q-functions that satisfy the IGM principle.
QPLEX (Wang et al., 2021a) 进一步扩展了价值分解的思路，使其能够涵盖更一般的分散式策略（并给出了证明）。具体而言，QPLEX 有潜力学习任何一组满足 IGM 原则的 Q 函数。
First, QPLEX extends the IGM principle to an advantage-based case. They define joint values and advantages from the joint Q-function: $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})=\max_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ , and $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})-\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ as well as individual values and advantages from each agent’s individual Q-function: ${V}_{i}({h}_{i})=\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ , and ${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})={Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})-{V}_{i}({h}_{i})$ . Note that this notion of advantage is a bit different than the typical notion (such as (Wang et al., 2016)) since the values and advantages are generated from the Q-values, rather than the other way around. As a result, the advantages will have the property that they will be 0 for optimal actions and negative otherwise. That is, ${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{*})=0$ (since ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{*})-\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})=0$ ) and for some non-optimal action ${a}_{i}^{\dagger}$ , ${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{\dagger})<0$ (since ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{\dagger})<\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ ). The same holds true for joint advantages.
首先，QPLEX 将 IGM 原则扩展到基于优势的情形。他们根据联合 Q 函数定义联合价值和联合优势：$\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})=\max_{\mathbf{a}}\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ 和 $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})-\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$，并根据每个智能体的个体 Q 函数定义个体价值和个体优势：${V}_{i}({h}_{i})=\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$ 和 ${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})={Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})-{V}_{i}({h}_{i})$。请注意，这里的优势概念与通常的概念（例如 (Wang et al., 2016) 中的概念）略有不同，因为价值和优势是由 Q 值推导而来的，而非相反。因此，优势具有如下性质：对于最优动作，其值为 0；对于其他动作，其值为负。也就是说，${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{*})=0$（因为 ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{*})-\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})=0$），而对于某个非最优动作 ${a}_{i}^{\dagger}$，有 ${A}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{\dagger})<0$（因为 ${Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i}^{\dagger})<\max_{{a}_{i}}{Q}_{i}({h}_{i},{a}_{i})$）。联合优势也具有同样的性质。

3.6 The use of state in factorization methods3.6 分解方法中状态的使用
Many of the current methods use inconsistent notation about the inclusion of state (except QMIX). As a result, the theory is often developed without considering the state input. It turns out that, unlike the policy gradient case discussed in Section 4.6, the using the state doesn’t introduce bias and is thus theoretically sound (Marchesini et al., 2024). The intuition is that the state is additional information in these cases. It doesn’t replace the history, but potentially augments it (similar to a history-state critic in Section 4.6). Furthermore, actions are not chosen based on the state but only the local, history-dependent Q-values, ${Q}_{i}$ . Nevertheless, in implementations of algorithms such as QPLEX and weighted QMIX, the weights only take the state as input and not the history. This replacement of history with state in these contexts limits the representational power of the weights in partially observable problems. As a result, using the state as input in QPLEX (instead of the joint history) prevents it from being able to represent the full IGM class of functions. It still may be beneficial to use the state instead of the joint history but exploring what additional information to use and in what way is an interesting open question.
当前许多方法在是否包含状态方面使用的记号并不一致（QMIX 除外）。因此，理论推导往往没有考虑状态输入。事实表明，与第 4.6 节讨论的策略梯度情形不同，使用状态不会引入偏差，因此在理论上是合理的（Marchesini et al., 2024）。其直观解释是，在这些情形中，状态提供了额外的信息。它并不取代历史，而是可能对历史进行补充（类似于第 4.6 节中的历史—状态评论家）。此外，动作的选择并不基于状态，而仅基于依赖局部历史的 Q 值 ${Q}_{i}$。然而，在 QPLEX 和加权 QMIX 等算法的实现中，权重仅以状态为输入，而不以历史为输入。在这些情形中，用状态取代历史会限制权重在部分可观测问题中的表示能力。因此，在 QPLEX 中使用状态作为输入（而非联合历史），会使其无法表示完整的 IGM 函数类。使用状态代替联合历史仍可能有益，但探索应使用哪些额外信息以及如何使用这些信息，是一个有趣的开放问题。

In the multi-agent case, there is one actor per agent and either one critic per agent (as shown in 6 and discussed in (Amato, 2024)) or a shared, centralized critic (as shown in 6 and discussed below). The basic motivation is to leverage the centralized information that is available during training and potentially combat nonstationarity that can happen in decentralized training. This is in contrast to the value-based methods in Section 3 where deterministic policies were generated based on the learned value functions. 4.2 A basic centralized critic approach4.2 一种基本的集中式评价器方法
In the simplest form, we can consider a class of centralized critic methods where the joint history critic, which we denote as $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ , estimates $\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ with parameters $\theta$ . $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ is then used to update each decentralized policy ${\pi}_{i}$ . We call this approach independent actor with centralized critic (IACC) and it is described in Algorithm 2. Learning is over a set of (on-policy) episodes and the histories are initialized to be empty. Agent $i$ ’s history at time step $t$ is denoted ${h}_{i,t}$ , while the set of histories for all agents is denoted $\mathbf{h}{}_{t}$ . All agents choose an initial action from their policies ${a}_{i,0}\sim{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i,0})$ and then the learning takes place over a fixed horizon of $\mathcal{H}$ . At each step, the agents take the corresponding current action ${\mathbf{a}}_{t}$ , receive the joint reward ${r}_{t}$ and see observations ${\mathbf{o}}_{t}$ . The histories for the next step are updated to include the action taken and observation seen, ${h}_{i,t}{a}_{i,t}{o}_{i,t}$ , and actions are sampled for the next step, ${a}_{i,t+1}\sim{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i,t+1})$ . Then, the TD error can be computed with the current Q-values, and then is used to update the actors and critic. Each agent’s actor is updated using the TD error from the joint Q-function (moving in the direction that improves the joint Q-value). The centralized critic is updated using a standard on-policy gradient update. The action for the next step, $t+1$ , becomes the action for the current step, $t$ , and the process continues until the end of the horizon and for each episode. The algorithm is written for the finite-horizon case but it can be adapted to the episodic or infinite-horizon case by including terminal states or removing the loop over episodes. Note that the histories make the value functions based on a particular time step as the history length provides the time step.
在最简单的形式下，我们可以考虑一类集中式评价器方法，其中，基于联合历史的评价器记为 $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$，使用参数 $\theta$ 来估计 $\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$。随后，使用 $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ 更新每个分散式策略 ${\pi}_{i}$。我们将这种方法称为采用集中式评价器的独立行动器（independent actor with centralized critic，IACC），其具体过程见算法 2。学习在一组（同策略）回合中进行，历史初始化为空。智能体 $i$ 在时间步 $t$ 的历史记为 ${h}_{i,t}$，而所有智能体的历史集合记为 $\mathbf{h}{}_{t}$。所有智能体都从各自的策略中选择初始动作 ${a}_{i,0}\sim{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i,0})$，随后在固定时域 $\mathcal{H}$ 内进行学习。在每一步，智能体执行相应的当前动作 ${\mathbf{a}}_{t}$，接收联合奖励 ${r}_{t}$，并获得观测 ${\mathbf{o}}_{t}$。下一步的历史通过纳入已执行的动作和已获得的观测进行更新，即 ${h}_{i,t}{a}_{i,t}{o}_{i,t}$，并为下一步采样动作 ${a}_{i,t+1}\sim{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i,t+1})$。随后，可以使用当前的 Q 值计算时序差分（TD）误差，再用该误差更新行动器和评价器。每个智能体的行动器都使用联合 Q 函数产生的 TD 误差进行更新（沿着提高联合 Q 值的方向更新）。集中式评价器使用标准的同策略梯度更新进行更新。下一步 $t+1$ 的动作成为当前步 $t$ 的动作，该过程在每个回合中持续进行，直至时域结束。该算法针对有限时域情形编写，但可以通过纳入终止状态或移除对回合的循环，使其适用于回合式或无限时域情形。注意，历史使价值函数依赖于特定的时间步，因为历史长度提供了时间步信息。

The advantage is defined as $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})-\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ , representing the difference between the Q-value for a given action and the V-value at that history. Because $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ doesn’t depend on the action, it is called a baseline and becomes a constant from the perspective of the policy gradient. As a result using $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})-\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ in place of $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ doesn’t change the convergence properties of the method. Nevertheless, using a baseline (e.g., subtracting $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ ) can reduce the variance of the estimate and improve performance in practice. In the algorithm, $\mathbf{A}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ isn’t explicitly stored since the TD error is used as an approximation for the advantage— $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ is approximated with $r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t+1})$ , which is true in expectation. $\delta_{t}$ can be thought of as a sample of $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t},\mathbf{a}_{t})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t})=\hat{\mathbf{A}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t},\mathbf{a}_{t})$ .

While the critic is called centralized because it uses centralized information, it estimates the joint value function of the decentralized policies. That is, the centralized critic estimates the value of the current set of decentralized policies (not a centralized policy) (Lyu et al., 2023). This is the correct thing to do since the critic’s job is to evaluate the policies, which are decentralized in this case.
虽然该评论家因使用集中式信息而被称为集中式评论家，但它估计的是分散式策略的联合价值函数。也就是说，集中式评论家估计的是当前这组分散式策略的价值（而非某个集中式策略的价值）（Lyu et al., 2023）。这种做法是正确的，因为评论家的职责是评估策略，而在这里，这些策略是分散式的。

在适当的假设下（例如关于探索、学习率和函数近似的假设），IACC（以及 IA2CC）将收敛到局部最优解（Peshkin et al., 2000; Lyu et al., 2023; Lyu et al., 2024）。这些结果为策略梯度方法奠定了坚实的理论基础，我将讨论各种方法的细节以及一些理论上的不足。
This idea of learning a centralized critic to update decentralized actors is very general. It can be (and has been) used with different types of critics, actors, and updates. I present some of the most popular methods below.
通过学习集中式评论家来更新分散式演员这一思想具有很强的通用性。它可以与不同类型的评论家、演员和更新方式结合使用，而且已有这样的应用。下面我将介绍其中一些最流行的方法。
4.3 MADDPG4.3 MADDPG
Multi-Agent DDPG (MADDPG) (Lowe et al., 2017) considers the more general case of possibly competitive agents as well as continuous actions. To deal with the different reward functions of different agents in the competitive case, separate centralized critics are learned for each agent. As we are only concerned with the cooperative case, we assume a single shared critic among agents, do not have to learn policy models of the other agents (since these are assumed to be accessible in a cooperative CTDE setting), and do not consider ensembles of other agent policies to improve robustness. MADDPG is also an off-policy method, unlike the previous algorithms discussed, so it makes use of replay buffer similar to DQN-based approaches. Nevertheless, MADDPG for the cooperative case is very similar to Algorithm 2 with the main changes noted below.
多智能体 DDPG（MADDPG）（Lowe et al., 2017）考虑了更一般的情形，包括智能体之间可能存在竞争以及动作连续的情况。为了处理竞争情形下不同智能体具有不同奖励函数的问题，为每个智能体分别学习一个集中式评论家。由于我们只关注合作情形，因此假设所有智能体共享一个评论家，无须学习其他智能体的策略模型（因为在合作式 CTDE 设置下，假定这些模型可以直接获取），也不考虑通过其他智能体策略的集成来提高鲁棒性。与前面讨论的算法不同，MADDPG 还是一种离策略方法，因此它使用了与基于 DQN 的方法类似的经验回放缓冲区。尽管如此，合作情形下的 MADDPG 与算法 2 非常相似，主要变化如下所述。
To deal with continuous actions, MADDPG extends the continuous action single-agent deep actor-critic method DDPG (Lillicrap et al., 2016) to the multi-agent case. I denote the deterministic continuous-action policies for each agent as $\mu_{i}$ . In the cooperative case, MADDPG uses the following policy gradient:
为了处理连续动作，MADDPG 将连续动作的单智能体深度演员—评论家方法 DDPG（Lillicrap et al., 2016）扩展到了多智能体情形。我将每个智能体的确定性连续动作策略记为 $\mu_{i}$。在合作情形下，MADDPG 使用以下策略梯度：
$$\nabla_{\!{\psi}_{i}}J\doteq(1-\gamma)\mathbb{E}_{x,\mathbf{a}\sim\rho(x,\mathbf{a})}\left[\nabla_{\!{\psi}_{i}}\mu_{i}({o}_{i})\nabla_{\!{\mathbf{a}}}\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(x,\mathbf{a})\mid_{a_{i}=\mu_{i}({o}_{i})}\right]\,,$$
where $x$ and $\mathbf{a}$ are sampled according to the (discounted) visitation probability with $x$ discussed below and $\mathbf{a}=\langle{a}_{1},\ldots,{a}_{n}\rangle$ . Since we can can no longer sum over actions and weigh their value by their probability as is done in the stochastic policy case, we have to consider the change in the Q-value function evaluated at the chosen action $a_{i}$ for the agent (Silver et al., 2014).
其中，$x$ 和 $\mathbf{a}$ 按照（折扣）访问概率进行采样，$x$ 将在下文讨论，且 $\mathbf{a}=\langle{a}_{1},\ldots,{a}_{n}\rangle$。由于我们无法再像随机策略情形那样，对动作求和并按其概率对动作价值加权，因此必须考虑 Q 价值函数在该智能体所选动作 $a_{i}$ 处的变化（Silver et al., 2014）。
Note that MADDPG considers reactive policies that only map from the last observation to an action (as seen in $\mu_{i}$ ). As noted in Section 1, the policy, $\mu_{i}$ , should choose actions based on histories, ${h}_{i}$ , rather than single observations, ${o}_{i}$ . The Q-value is from a centralized critic so the paper states $x$ “could consist of the observations of all agents […] however, we could also include additional state information if available.” More generally, $x$ should be history representations for each agent and we discuss the use of states in more detail in 4.6. These issues are easily fixable and result in the following policy gradient (we can incorporate the state information later):
请注意，MADDPG 考虑的是反应式策略，它仅将最近一次观测映射为动作（如 $\mu_{i}$ 所示）。如第 1 节所述，策略 $\mu_{i}$ 应根据历史 ${h}_{i}$ 而非单次观测 ${o}_{i}$ 来选择动作。Q 值来自集中式评论家，因此论文指出，$x$“可以由所有智能体的观测组成 […] 不过，如果有额外的状态信息可用，我们也可以将其纳入。”更一般地，$x$ 应为各个智能体的历史表示，我们将在 4.6 中更详细地讨论状态的使用。这些问题很容易修正，并可得到以下策略梯度（我们可以稍后再纳入状态信息）：
$$\nabla_{\!{\psi}_{i}}J\doteq(1-\gamma)\mathbb{E}_{x,\mathbf{a}\sim\rho(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})}\left[\nabla_{\!{\psi}_{i}}\mu_{i}({h}_{i})\nabla_{\!{\mathbf{a}}}\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\mid_{a_{i}=\mu_{i}({h}_{i})}\right]\,.$$

由于 MADDPG 是离策略方法，因此它像基于 DQN 的方法一样维护一个经验回放缓冲区，同时还维护演员网络和评论家网络的“目标”副本，分别为 $\mu_{{\psi}_{i}^{-}}$ 和 $Q_{\theta^{-}}$。于是，Q 更新（对于基于历史的情形）为：
$$\mathcal{L}(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{<\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a},r,\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime}>\sim\mathcal{D}}\Big[\big(y-Q_{\theta}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})\big)^{2}\Big]\text{, where\,\, }y=r+\gamma Q_{\theta^{-}}(\mathbf{h}{}^{\prime},\mathbf{a}^{\prime})\mid_{{a}_{i}=\mu^{-}({h}_{i})\ \forall i\in\mathbb{I}}$$
MADDPG executes a stochastic exploration policy (e.g., by adding Gaussian noise to the current deterministic policy) while estimating (and optimizing) the value of the deterministic policy. This can be seen because while the action, $\mathbf{a}$ , is sampled from the (behavior policy) dataset, the next action, $\mathbf{a}^{\prime}$ is selected by the target policy, $\mu^{-}$ .
MADDPG 在估计（并优化）确定性策略的价值时，执行的是随机探索策略（例如，在当前确定性策略上添加高斯噪声）。这一点可以从以下事实看出：动作 $\mathbf{a}$ 从（行为策略）数据集中采样，而下一个动作 $\mathbf{a}^{\prime}$ 则由目标策略 $\mu^{-}$ 选择。
Algorithm 2 can be updated to incorporate these changes. Since the continuous-action policy is deterministic, (Guassian) noise is added when selecting actions to aid in exploration. The approach is off-policy, so the experiences are first stored in a replay buffer (like DQN and other value-based methods in Section 3). Similarly, episodes are sampled from the replay buffer and target networks used (again, like DRQN-based approaches) for the actor and the critic.
可以对算法 2 进行更新，以纳入这些变化。由于连续动作策略是确定性的，因此在选择动作时添加（高斯）噪声以促进探索。该方法是离策略方法，因此首先将经验存入经验回放缓冲区（与第 3 节中的 DQN 及其他基于价值的方法一样）。类似地，从经验回放缓冲区中采样回合，并为演员和评论家使用目标网络（同样类似于基于 DRQN 的方法）。
MADDPG is no longer widely used but the ideas (such as the centralized critic) have been adopted extensively.
MADDPG 已不再被广泛使用，但其中的思想（例如集中式评价器）已被广泛采用。
4.4 COMA4.4 COMA
The main contributions of Counterfactual Multi-Agent Policy Gradients (COMA) were the introduction of the centralized critic along with a counterfactual baseline (Foerster et al., 2018b). As mentioned above, baselines are common in policy gradient methods since they are high variance. The baseline value is subtracted from the Q-value and it can be anything that is not dependent on the agent’s action.
反事实多智能体策略梯度（Counterfactual Multi-Agent Policy Gradients，COMA）的主要贡献是引入了集中式评价器以及反事实基线（Foerster et al., 2018b）。如上所述，策略梯度方法的方差较高，因此通常会使用基线。基线值会从 Q 值中减去，只要不依赖于该智能体的动作，任何量都可以作为基线。
In the case of COMA, the motivation for the baseline is not only variance reduction but also better credit assignment by subtracting off the perceived contribution to the Q-value from the other agents. Specifically, it marginalizes out the agent actions from the Q-function to get an estimate of what the (counterfactual) Q-function would be while holding the other agent actions fixed. The result is an agent-specific advantage function that subtracts the baseline for agent $i$ from the joint Q-function:
对于 COMA，使用基线的动机不仅是降低方差，还包括通过减去其他智能体对 Q 值的估计贡献，改善信用分配。具体而言，它通过对 Q 函数中的该智能体动作进行边缘化，在保持其他智能体动作不变的情况下，估计（反事实）Q 函数的值。由此得到一个针对特定智能体的优势函数，它从联合 Q 函数中减去智能体 $i$ 的基线：
$${A}_{i}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})-\sum_{{a}_{i}^{\prime}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}^{\prime}|{h}_{i})\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},{a}_{i}^{\prime},\mathbf{a}_{-i})$$
This baseline no longer depends on agent $i$ ’s action (in expectation) so it will not bias the gradient. Note that the COMA paper uses a state-based critic and advantage, $\mathbf{Q}(s,\mathbf{a})$ and ${A}_{i}(s,\mathbf{a})$ , but this is incorrect as I’ll discuss in Section 4.6.
该基线不再依赖于智能体 $i$ 的动作（在期望意义上），因此不会使梯度产生偏差。请注意，COMA 论文使用了基于状态的评价器和优势函数，即 $\mathbf{Q}(s,\mathbf{a})$ 和 ${A}_{i}(s,\mathbf{a})$，但这种做法并不正确，我将在第 4.6 节讨论这一点。
Rather than have separate baseline networks for each agent, there is a single centralized critic that takes in the other agent actions, $\mathbf{a}_{-i}$ , the joint observation, $\mathbf{o}$ , an agent id, $i$ , the proposed action ${a}_{i}$ , and policy probabilities ${\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}^{\prime}|{h}_{i})$ , and outputs the advantage for that agent ${A}_{i}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ using the equation above. This network can also output the joint Q-values for all agent $i$ ’s actions, $Q(\mathbf{h}{},\cdot,\mathbf{a}_{-i})$ , given the other agent actions, $\mathbf{a}_{-i}$ , the joint observation, $\mathbf{o}$ , and an agent id, $i$ . Both of these values are needed for the algorithm.
该方法没有为每个智能体分别设置基线网络，而是使用一个集中式评价器，接收其他智能体的动作 $\mathbf{a}_{-i}$、联合观测 $\mathbf{o}$、智能体标识 $i$、拟采取的动作 ${a}_{i}$ 以及策略概率 ${\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}^{\prime}|{h}_{i})$，并利用上述公式输出该智能体的优势 ${A}_{i}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$。给定其他智能体的动作 $\mathbf{a}_{-i}$、联合观测 $\mathbf{o}$ 和智能体标识 $i$，该网络也可以输出智能体 $i$ 所有动作对应的联合 Q 值 $Q(\mathbf{h}{},\cdot,\mathbf{a}_{-i})$。该算法需要上述两种值。
Then, the COMA algorithm can be an extension of Algorithm 2. The critic update can be calculated in the same way (using the Q-values from the network described above) but the agent-specific advantage is incorporated in the actor update. That is, during the actor update (starting on line 16), the agent-advantage ${A}_{i}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$ is calculated for the given agent and ${A}_{i}$ is used in the actor gradient estimate instead of $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}$ .
这样，COMA 算法就可以作为算法 2 的扩展。评价器的更新可以用相同的方式计算（使用上述网络给出的 Q 值），但在行动器更新中会引入针对特定智能体的优势。也就是说，在行动器更新期间（从第 16 行开始），会计算给定智能体的优势 ${A}_{i}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})$，并在行动器的梯度估计中使用 ${A}_{i}$ 替代 $\hat{\mathbf{Q}}$。
While COMA has been very influential, it isn’t widely used since newer methods tend to outperform it.
尽管 COMA 的影响很大，但由于较新的方法往往表现更好，它并未得到广泛使用。
4.5 MAPPO4.5 MAPPO
Multi-Agent PPO (MAPPO) extends PPO (Schulman et al., 2017) to the centralized critic MARL case (Yu et al., 2022). The motivation behind PPO is to adjust the magnitude of the policy update to learn quickly without becoming unstable. PPO does this using a simple clipped loss that approximates the more complex trust region update (Schulman et al., 2015).
多智能体 PPO（Multi-Agent PPO，MAPPO）将 PPO（Schulman et al., 2017）扩展到采用集中式评价器的多智能体强化学习（MARL）情形（Yu et al., 2022）。PPO 的动机是调整策略更新的幅度，从而在保持稳定的同时快速学习。PPO 使用简单的裁剪损失来近似更复杂的信赖域更新，以实现这一目标（Schulman et al., 2015）。
Like IA2CC (Algorithm 3), MAPPO uses an advantage-based update (but not the agent-specific counterfactual one used in COMA). In particular, instead of the loss being $\gamma^{t}\delta_{t}\log{\pi}_{i}(a_{i,t}|h_{i,t})$ as reflected on line 17, which is an approximation of $\gamma^{t}\mathbf{A}_{t}\log{\pi}_{i}(a_{i,t}|h_{i,t})$ , the loss becomes:
与 IA2CC（算法 3）一样，MAPPO 使用基于优势的更新（但并非 COMA 中针对特定智能体的反事实优势）。具体而言，损失不再是第 17 行所体现的 $\gamma^{t}\delta_{t}\log{\pi}_{i}(a_{i,t}|h_{i,t})$（它是 $\gamma^{t}\mathbf{A}_{t}\log{\pi}_{i}(a_{i,t}|h_{i,t})$ 的近似），而变为：
$$\mathcal{L}^{MAPPO}_{clip}({\psi}_{i})=\min\Big(r_{{\psi}_{i},i}\mathbf{A},\text{clip}(r_{{\psi}_{i},i},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon)\mathbf{A}\Big),$$
where $r_{{\psi}_{i},i}=\frac{\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}{\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}$ .[fn13] Maximizing $\frac{\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}{\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}\mathbf{A}$ seeks to maximally improve the new policy, $\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}$ , compared to the old policy, $\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}$ , by reweighing the advantage using an importance sampling ratio (considering the advantage was calculated using the old policy). It can be difficult to estimate this value from a small number of samples and it may result in too large of an update (resulting in parameters that perform worse). As a result, PPO also considered a term using a clipped ratio, limiting $r_{{\psi}_{i},i}$ so it can’t be too far above or below 1 (which would be the value when the new policy is equal to the old one). By minimizing over the unclipped and clipped values, PPO limits changes in the policy. Note the ratio is always positive but the advantage could be positive or negative. When the advantage is positive, the loss will be clipped if the ratio is too large, limiting how much more likely the action can be in the policy. Similarly, when the advantage is negative, the loss will be clipped when the ratio is near 0, limiting how much less likely the action can be in the policy. Like in IA2CC, $\mathbf{A}$ can still be approximated as $r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t})$ .
其中 $r_{{\psi}_{i},i}=\frac{\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}{\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}$。[fn13] 最大化 $\frac{\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}{\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})}\mathbf{A}$，旨在通过重要性采样比率对优势重新加权（考虑到优势是使用旧策略计算的），使新策略 $\pi_{{\psi}_{i}}$ 相对于旧策略 $\pi_{\psi_{i,old}}$ 得到最大程度的改进。仅凭少量样本可能难以估计该值，而且这可能导致更新幅度过大（从而使得到的参数表现更差）。因此，PPO 还考虑了一个使用裁剪比率的项，对 $r_{{\psi}_{i},i}$ 加以限制，使其不能高于或低于 1 太多（当新策略与旧策略相同时，该值为 1）。通过取未裁剪值与裁剪值中的较小者，PPO 限制了策略的变化幅度。请注意，该比率始终为正，但优势可以为正，也可以为负。当优势为正时，如果比率过大，损失就会被裁剪，从而限制策略中该动作被选择的概率能够增加的幅度。类似地，当优势为负时，如果比率接近 0，损失就会被裁剪，从而限制策略中该动作被选择的概率能够降低的幅度。与 IA2CC 中一样，$\mathbf{A}$ 仍可近似为 $r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t})$。
Footnote: 13 Here, I consider the stochastic (not continuous) action case and for simplicity do not include GAE, mini-batching, or a policy entropy term (which is common in policy gradient methods to improve exploration). Also, the paper uses a local advantage loss, ${A}_{i}$ , but this value would not be different than $\mathbf{A}$ in a general Dec-POMDP if it is updated using $\mathbf{A}$ and $r$ since these quantities would be the same for all agents. Finally, policies over single observations rather than histories are used, which as noted before is typically not sufficient for partially observable problems (general Dec-POMDPs) and state-based critics are used, which is incorrect as discussed below.
脚注：13 这里，我考虑的是随机动作（而非连续动作）的情形，并且为简单起见，不纳入 GAE、小批量处理或策略熵项（策略梯度方法通常使用策略熵项来改善探索）。此外，该论文使用了局部优势损失 ${A}_{i}$，但在一般的 Dec-POMDP 中，如果使用 $\mathbf{A}$ 和 $r$ 来更新该值，那么它就不会与 $\mathbf{A}$ 有所不同，因为这些量对所有智能体都相同。最后，该方法使用基于单次观测而非历史的策略；如前所述，这通常不足以处理部分可观测问题（一般的 Dec-POMDP）；此外，它还使用基于状态的评价器，而如下文所述，这种做法并不正确。
The critic loss for MAPPO also includes clipping and is given by:
MAPPO 的评价器损失也包含裁剪，其表达式为：
$$\mathcal{L}^{MAPPO}(\theta)=\max\left[(\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{}_{t})-\hat{R}_{t})^{2},\left(\text{clip}(\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{}),\mathbf{V}_{old}(\mathbf{h}{})-\epsilon,\mathbf{V}_{old}(\mathbf{h}{})+\epsilon)-\hat{R}_{t}\right)^{2}\right]$$
where $\hat{R}$ are Monte Carlo returns starting from the current history (i.e., $\hat{R}_{t}=\sum_{j=t}^{\mathcal{H}}\gamma r_{j}$ ) and $\mathbf{V}_{old}$ represents the value function from the previous time step but TD can also be used and value clipping does not have to be used.
其中，$\hat{R}$ 是从当前历史开始的蒙特卡洛回报（即 $\hat{R}_{t}=\sum_{j=t}^{\mathcal{H}}\gamma r_{j}$），$\mathbf{V}_{old}$ 表示上一时间步的价值函数，但也可以使用 TD，并且不一定要使用价值裁剪。
All agents use the same centralized critic and parameter sharing can also used so all agents share the same actor network.
所有智能体使用同一个集中式评价器，也可以使用参数共享，使所有智能体共享同一个行动者网络。

是 MAPPO 的一种版本，其中每个智能体使用自身的局部优势，而非联合优势。因此，IPPO 的行动者损失变为：
$$\mathcal{L}^{IPPO}_{clip}({\psi}_{i})=\min\Big(r_{{\psi}_{i},i}{A}_{i},\text{clip}(r_{{\psi}_{i},i},1-\epsilon,1+\epsilon){A}_{i}\Big),$$
with $\hat{A}_{i}=r_{t}+\gamma\hat{V}_{i}({h}_{i,t+1})-\hat{V}_{i}({h}_{i,t})$ . The IPPO critic loss becomes:

$$\mathcal{L}^{IPPO}(\theta)=\max\left[({V}_{i}(h_{i,t}))-\hat{R}_{t})^{2},\left(\text{clip}({V}_{i}(h_{i,t})),V_{i,old}(h_{i,t}))-\epsilon,V_{i,old}(h_{i,t}))+\epsilon)-\hat{R}_{t}\right)^{2}\right]$$
where $\hat{R}$ are Monte Carlo returns starting from the current local history, ${V}_{i}(h_{i,t})$ . As a result, there is only one actor and one critic, but it is updated using the data from all agents.
其中，$\hat{R}$ 是从当前局部历史 ${V}_{i}(h_{i,t})$ 开始的蒙特卡洛回报。IPPO 使用参数共享，因此所有智能体共享行动者网络和评价器网络，使其成为一种集中式训练、分散式执行（CTDE）方法。因此，只有一个行动者和一个评价器，但更新时使用来自所有智能体的数据。
MAPPO and IPPO perform very well on the standard benchmark domains. Both algorithms typically perform similarly, but a version of MAPPO where the information in the critic was hand designed to only include features that are relevant to the task could outperform the standard versions of MAPPO and IPPO. This makes sense as the agents did not have to learn what information was necessary, which is difficult in high-dimensional settings.
MAPPO 和 IPPO 在标准基准任务领域中表现都非常出色。这两种算法的表现通常相近，但一种通过人工设计使评价器中的信息仅包含与任务相关特征的 MAPPO 版本，能够优于标准版本的 MAPPO 和 IPPO。这是合理的，因为智能体不必学习哪些信息是必要的，而在高维情境下，学习这一点十分困难。
4.6 State-based critics4.6 基于状态的评价器
Critics that use state (and not history) are unsound in partially observable environments (i.e., general Dec-POMDPs). That is, they are incorrect and could be arbitrarily bad. They can work well in domains that are nearly fully observable (such as SMAC (Samvelyan et al., 2019)) and domains in which remembering history information is not helpful (observations are not very informative or the task is too hard to solve). This result has been shown theoretically and empirically (Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023).
在部分可观测环境（即一般的 Dec-POMDP）中，使用状态（而非历史）的评价器在理论上并不成立。也就是说，它们是不正确的，而且可能差到任意程度。它们可以在近乎完全可观测的任务领域（如 SMAC (Samvelyan et al., 2019)）以及记忆历史信息无助于解决问题的任务领域（观测所含信息很少，或任务过难而无法解决）中表现良好。这一结果已在理论和实证上得到证明 (Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023)。
In particular, MADDPG (Lowe et al., 2017) and COMA (Foerster et al., 2018b) popularized the idea of using state-based critics using the intuition that this ground truth information that is available during training can help address partial observability. In truth, it is typically a bad idea to eliminate partial observability in the critic. A Dec-POMDP typically is partially observable and the actor is using partially observable information (i.e., histories). The state-based critic can be biased (i.e., incorrect) because there is a mismatch between the actor and the critic. The actor needs to use history information to choose actions. The state-based critic uses state values instead. As a result, the state values are averaged over the histories that are visited and can’t possibly have the correct history values unless there is a one-to-one mapping from states to histories (which would happen in the fully observable case). That is, the critic loses the information about partial observability, which is necessary for the actor to make good choices.
具体而言，MADDPG (Lowe et al., 2017) 和 COMA (Foerster et al., 2018b) 推广了使用基于状态的评价器这一思路，其直觉是，训练期间可获得的这些真实信息有助于应对部分可观测性。事实上，在评价器中消除部分可观测性通常是一个糟糕的主意。Dec-POMDP 通常是部分可观测的，而行动者使用的是部分可观测信息（即历史）。基于状态的评价器可能存在偏差（即不正确），因为行动者与评价器之间存在不匹配。行动者需要使用历史信息来选择动作。而基于状态的评价器使用的是状态价值。因此，状态价值是对访问过的历史取平均得到的，除非状态与历史之间存在一一映射（完全可观测的情形就是如此），否则它们不可能具有正确的历史价值。也就是说，评价器丢失了有关部分可观测性的信息，而这些信息是行动者做出良好选择所必需的。
For example, in the class Dec-Tiger problem (Nair et al., 2003; Oliehoek and Amato, 2016), there are only 2 states (the tiger being on the left or on the right) but noisy observations are received when an agent listens (an agent can also choose to open one of the two doors). A state-based critic would only have two values $\mathbf{V}({s}^{1})$ and $\mathbf{V}({s}^{2})$ but there are an exponential number of histories that depends on the horizon. As more information is gathered (i.e., the same observation about the tiger’s location is heard multiple times) the value of the history, $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ , should increase (so $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{}_{tiger-left-10-times-in-a-row})>\mathbf{V}({\mathbf{h}{}}_{0})$ , the initial history). This can’t be reflected in the state-based critic. Furthermore, information-gathering actions (i.e., listening in this tiger problem) won’t improve the value according to the critic so they shouldn’t be taken. As a result, the state-based critic values will be incorrect in the Dec-Tiger problem and the agents would learn a policy to open one of the doors and hope for a good outcome (assuming the tiger is randomly initialized).
例如，在经典的 Dec-Tiger 问题 (Nair et al., 2003; Oliehoek and Amato, 2016) 中，只有 2 个状态（老虎在左侧或右侧），但智能体在倾听时会接收到带噪声的观测（智能体也可以选择打开两扇门中的一扇）。基于状态的评价器只会有两个价值 $\mathbf{V}({s}^{1})$ 和 $\mathbf{V}({s}^{2})$，但历史的数量随规划时域呈指数增长。随着收集到的信息增多（即多次听到关于老虎位置的相同观测），历史的价值 $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{})$ 应当增加（因此，$\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{}_{tiger-left-10-times-in-a-row})>\mathbf{V}({\mathbf{h}{}}_{0})$，其中后者对应初始历史）。这一点无法在基于状态的评价器中体现。此外，根据评价器的评估，收集信息的动作（即这个老虎问题中的倾听动作）不会提高价值，因此不应采取这些动作。因此，基于状态的评价器在 Dec-Tiger 问题中给出的价值将是不正确的，智能体会学到一种打开其中一扇门并寄希望于好结果的策略（假设老虎的位置是随机初始化的）。
The updates to Algorithm 3 for the state-based critic case are straightforward— $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s)$ is used in place of $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{})$ . Then, the TD error calculation becomes $\delta_{t}\leftarrow r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s_{t})$ , which is used in the actor and critic updates.
针对基于状态的评价器情形，对 Algorithm 3 的修改很直接：用 $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s)$ 替代 $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{})$。此时，时序差分（TD）误差的计算变为 $\delta_{t}\leftarrow r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(s_{t})$，该误差用于演员和评论家的更新。
It turns out that history-state critics, those that take both the state and history as input ( $\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$ or $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{},s)$ are unbiased and often perform the best (Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2022). This result has been theoretically and empirically shown for the general single and multi-agent cases (Baisero and Amato, 2022; Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023) as well as empirically with variants of MAPPO (Yu et al., 2022). The intuition for correctness of the history-state critic is that no history information is lost (as it is in the simpler state-based critic). That is, the correct history value function can be (and is) recovered from the history-state value: $\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbb{E}_{s\mid\mathbf{h}{}}\left[\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})\right]$ . The MAPPO paper also showed that if you can handcraft the features of the history-state critic to retain only the relevant information, the performance can be the highest since the agent doesn’t need to learn which information is relevant and which is not. Unfortunately, determining which information is relevant and properly separating it can be difficult to do. The algorithmic updates for the history-state critic case are also straightforward— $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s)$ is used in place of $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{})$ . The TD error calculation becomes $\delta_{t}\leftarrow r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s_{t})$ , which is used in the actor and critic updates.
研究表明，历史-状态评论家，即同时以状态和历史为输入的评论家（$\mathbf{Q}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})$ 或 $\mathbf{V}(\mathbf{h}{},s)$），是无偏的，而且通常表现最佳（Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2022）。这一结果已在一般的单智能体和多智能体情形下得到理论和实证验证（Baisero and Amato, 2022; Lyu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023），也通过 MAPPO 的变体得到了实证验证（Yu et al., 2022）。历史-状态评论家之所以正确，直观原因在于它不会丢失任何历史信息（而更简单的基于状态的评论家则会丢失历史信息）。也就是说，可以从历史-状态价值中恢复出正确的历史价值函数（而且确实如此）：$\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},\mathbf{a})=\mathbb{E}_{s\mid\mathbf{h}{}}\left[\mathbf{Q^{\mathbf{\pi}}}(\mathbf{h}{},s,\mathbf{a})\right]$。MAPPO 论文还表明，如果能够手工设计历史-状态评论家的特征，使其仅保留相关信息，就可能获得最佳性能，因为智能体无需再学习哪些信息相关、哪些信息不相关。遗憾的是，确定哪些信息相关并将其恰当地分离出来，可能很难做到。历史-状态评论家情形下的算法更新也很直接——用 $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s)$ 替代 $\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{})$。TD 误差的计算变为 $\delta_{t}\leftarrow r_{t}+\gamma\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s_{t+1})-\hat{\mathbf{V}}(\mathbf{h}{},s_{t})$，该误差用于演员和评论家的更新。
4.7 Choosing different types of decentralized and centralized critics4.7 选择不同类型的去中心化与中心化评论家
While CTDE methods are (by far) the most popular form of MARL, they do not always perform the best. In fact, decentralized training and execution methods (DTE) (Amato, 2024) are actually quite close to CTDE methods. This is because DTE methods typically make the concurrent learning assumption where all the agents learn using the same algorithm, making updates at the same time on the joint data. This equivalence is seen explicitly in the policy gradient case as the gradient of the joint update is the same as the decentralized gradient (Peshkin et al., 2000). This phenomenon has been also been studied theoretically and empirically with modern actor-critic methods (Lyu et al., 2021; Lyu et al., 2023). It turns out that while centralized critic actor-critic methods are often assumed to be better than DTE actor-critic methods, they are theoretically the same (with mild assumptions) and often empirically similar (and sometimes worse). Too much centralized information can sometimes be overwhelming, harming scalability of centralized-critic methods and leading to higher variance (Yu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023).
尽管 CTDE 方法是（迄今为止）最流行的多智能体强化学习（MARL）形式，但它们并不总是表现最佳。事实上，去中心化训练与执行方法（DTE）（Amato, 2024）实际上与 CTDE 方法非常接近。这是因为 DTE 方法通常采用并发学习假设，即所有智能体都使用同一种算法学习，并同时基于联合数据进行更新。这种等价性在策略梯度情形下表现得尤为明确，因为联合更新的梯度与去中心化梯度相同（Peshkin et al., 2000）。研究者也使用现代演员-评论家方法，从理论和实证两方面研究了这一现象（Lyu et al., 2021; Lyu et al., 2023）。研究表明，虽然通常认为采用中心化评论家的演员-评论家方法优于 DTE 演员-评论家方法，但在温和的假设下，两者在理论上相同，在实证中也往往表现相近（前者有时甚至更差）。过多的中心化信息有时会造成信息负担，损害中心化评论家方法的可扩展性，并导致更高的方差（Yu et al., 2022; Lyu et al., 2023）。
The theory assumes learned critics (or sufficient Monte Carlo estimates) but different types of critics may be easier to learn in different cases. As a result, it is not straightforward to determine when to use each critic in practice. Centralized critics often cause higher variance actor updates since information from the other agents needs to be marginalized out (e.g., through sampling) while information from the other agents is already removed from the decentralized critics. Centralized critics can also be more difficult to learn in problems with large numbers of agents or with large action or observation sets, making decentralized critics more scalable. Decentralized critics may be difficult to learn due to agents changing their policies (i.e., nonstationarity), making critic estimates stale. State-based critics are often the easiest to learn (because no history representation needs to be learned) but are biased in partially observable settings and will have high variance (again, because of the mismatch between the actor and the critic). In general, learning a history representation is difficult. Agents need to figure out what history information is relevant and what is not from a noisy and often sparse RL signal. Using a recurrent network (or even a transformer) is usually not sufficient for this task. History-state critics further increase the variance in the policy update (because the state information needs to also be marginalized out) but can often be easier to learn than centralized critics with only history information.[fn14] As a result the choice of critic is often a bias-variance tradeoff since, in practice, a centralized critic should have a lower bias than decentralized critics with more stable values that are more easily updated when policies change but higher variance because the policy updates need to be averaged over other agents.
该理论假设评论家已被充分学习（或已获得足够的蒙特卡洛估计），但在不同情形下，不同类型的评论家的学习难度可能不同。因此，在实践中确定何时使用哪种评论家并非易事。中心化评论家往往会使演员更新具有更高的方差，因为需要将来自其他智能体的信息边缘化消去（例如通过采样），而去中心化评论家中已经去除了来自其他智能体的信息。在智能体数量众多或动作集、观测集很大的问题中，中心化评论家也可能更难学习，因此去中心化评论家具有更好的可扩展性。由于智能体会改变其策略（即非平稳性），导致评论家的估计过时，去中心化评论家可能难以学习。基于状态的评论家通常最容易学习（因为无需学习历史表示），但在部分可观测环境中存在偏差，而且方差较高（同样是由于演员与评论家之间的不匹配）。一般而言，学习历史表示是困难的。智能体需要从含有噪声且往往稀疏的强化学习（RL）信号中，辨别哪些历史信息相关、哪些不相关。使用循环网络（甚至 transformer）通常不足以完成这项任务。历史-状态评价器会进一步增大策略更新的方差（因为还需要将状态信息边缘化掉），但往往比仅使用历史信息的集中式评价器更容易学习。[fn14] 因此，评价器的选择往往是在偏差与方差之间进行权衡，因为在实践中，与分散式评价器相比，集中式评价器的偏差应当更低，其价值估计更稳定，在策略发生变化时也更容易更新，但方差更高，因为策略更新需要对其他智能体取平均。
Footnote: 14 The exact reason why this is the case is unclear and a great topic for future research!
脚注：14 出现这种情况的确切原因尚不清楚，这是一个很值得未来研究的课题！
There are many different ways of performing CTDE, and current work has only scratched the surface. Studying the differences and similarities between DTE and CTDE variants of algorithms seems like a promising direction for understanding current methods and developing improved approaches for both cases. Determining what centralized information to use and how to best use it is another key question. Lastly, to the best of my knowledge, there are no globally optimal model-free MARL methods for Dec-POMDPs. This is surprising since there are optimal planning methods where the model is assumed known (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016) and many globally optimal model-free methods for single-agent RL (Sutton and Barto, 2018). Developing such methods (even for the tabular case) would be interesting and may lead to new, better methods that could be approximated in the deep case.
实现 CTDE 的方式多种多样，而现有研究仅触及了其中的皮毛。研究算法的 DTE 变体与 CTDE 变体之间的异同，似乎是理解现有方法并为这两种情形开发改进方法的一个有前景的方向。确定使用哪些集中式信息以及如何最有效地利用这些信息，是另一个关键问题。最后，据我所知，目前还没有针对 Dec-POMDPs 的全局最优无模型 MARL 方法。这令人意外，因为在假定模型已知的情况下，已有最优规划方法 (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016)，而针对单智能体 RL，也已有许多全局最优的无模型方法 (Sutton and Barto, 2018)。开发此类方法（即使仅针对表格型情形）也很有意义，并可能催生新的、更好的方法，而这些方法在深度学习情形下可以通过近似来实现。
4.8 Methods that combine policy gradient and value factorization4.8 结合策略梯度与价值分解的方法
Several methods combine using a centralized critic with value factorization. One notable method is FACtored Multi-Agent Centralized policy gradients (FACMAC) (Peng et al., 2021) which extends MADDPG to include a QMIX-style factored centralized critic, but since the local Q-values don’t need to be used for action selection (the actor does that), the factorization can be nonmonotonic. Also, rather than sampling the actions of other agents from the off-policy dataset, as MADDPG does, actions for other agents are sampled from the current policies during the actor updates. Decomposed Off-Policy policy gradient (DOP) (Wang et al., 2021b) uses a factored centralized critic that is a weighted sum of the local Q-values and a multi-agent extension of tree backup (Precup et al., 2000) to more efficiently calculate off-policy updates. While a centralized critic is learned, each agent’s local Q-value estimate is used to update its actor and with mild assumptions the method is shown to converge to a local optimum even with the simple critic.
一些方法将集中式评价器与价值分解结合使用。其中一个值得关注的方法是因子化多智能体集中式策略梯度（FACtored Multi-Agent Centralized policy gradients，FACMAC）(Peng et al., 2021)，它扩展了 MADDPG，引入了 QMIX 风格的因子化集中式评价器；但由于局部 Q 值无需用于动作选择（动作选择由行动器完成），这种分解可以是非单调的。此外，在更新行动器时，其他智能体的动作从其当前策略中采样，而不像 MADDPG 那样从离策略数据集中采样。分解式离策略策略梯度（Decomposed Off-Policy policy gradient，DOP）(Wang et al., 2021b) 使用因子化集中式评价器，该评价器是局部 Q 值的加权和，并采用树回溯（tree backup）(Precup et al., 2000) 的多智能体扩展来更高效地计算离策略更新。虽然学习的是集中式评价器，但每个智能体都使用自身的局部 Q 值估计来更新其行动器；已有证明表明，在较弱的假设下，即使使用这种简单的评价器，该方法也能收敛到局部最优解。
4.9 Other centralized critic methods4.9 其他集中式评价器方法
Many other methods use centralized critics. For instance, Iqbal and Sha (2019) use attention for determining which other agent information to include in the centralized critic. Qatten (Yang et al., 2020) extends QMIX (and weighted QMIX) to add structure to the mixing network, where the structure is inspired by a linear approximation of the Q-function and one set of weights is leaned using attention. A wide range of other approaches have been developed but are not included in this introduction to the area.
许多其他方法也使用集中式评价器。例如，Iqbal and Sha (2019) 使用注意力机制来确定应将哪些其他智能体的信息纳入集中式评价器。Qatten (Yang et al., 2020) 扩展了 QMIX（以及加权 QMIX），为混合网络引入结构；该结构的灵感来自 Q 函数的线性近似，其中一组权重通过注意力机制学习。研究者还开发了各种其他方法，但这篇领域入门介绍并未涵盖它们。
5 Other forms of CTDE5 CTDE 的其他形式
While the methods above are the ones that are most widely used, there are several other forms of CTDE. I discuss common approaches and include a note on the many other topics that are not in this text in order to focus on core issues of CTDE.
虽然上述方法应用最为广泛，但 CTDE 还有其他几种形式。我将讨论常见方法，并简要说明本文未涵盖的其他诸多主题，以便聚焦于 CTDE 的核心问题。
5.1 Adding centralized information to decentralized methods5.1 向分散式方法中添加集中式信息
As noted in the introduction, decentralized training and execution (DTE) methods are independent learning methods (Claus and Boutilier, 1998) where each agent learns on its own using only its own information. More details about decentralized training methods can be found in a companion text (Amato, 2024). DTE fit well when there is no offline training phase or when scalability is the key factor. DTE methods can be augmented with centralized information to potentially improve performance. These extensions are discussed below.
如引言所述，分散式训练与执行（decentralized training and execution，DTE）方法属于独立学习方法 (Claus and Boutilier, 1998)，其中每个智能体仅使用自身的信息独立学习。有关分散式训练方法的更多细节，可参阅配套文献 (Amato, 2024)。当不存在离线训练阶段，或可扩展性是关键因素时，DTE 很适用。可以利用集中式信息增强 DTE 方法，从而有望提升性能。下文将讨论这些扩展。
Parameter sharing参数共享
Many cooperative MARL methods use parameter sharing. As noted in Section 4.5, the idea behind parameter (or weight) sharing is instead of each agent using a different network to estimate the value function or policy, all agents share the same networks. The data from each agent can be used to update a single value network for an algorithm such as DRQN, speeding up learning. Agents can still perform differently due to observing different histories and agent indices can be added to increase specialization (Gupta et al., 2017; Foerster et al., 2016). While parameter sharing is typically used with homogeneous agents (i.e., those with the same action and observation spaces), it can also be used with heterogeneous agents (Terry et al., 2020). Since parameter sharing requires agents to share networks during training, it couldn’t be used for online training in a decentralized fashion. Nevertheless, decentralized algorithms can be extended to use parameter sharing (such as the case with IPPO). Parameter-sharing implementations may be more scalable than other forms of CTDE, are often simple to implement, and can perform well (Gupta et al., 2017; Foerster et al., 2018b; Yu et al., 2022).
许多合作式 MARL 方法使用参数共享。如第 4.5 节所述，参数（或权重）共享的思路是让所有智能体共享相同的网络，而非由每个智能体使用不同的网络来估计价值函数或策略。对于 DRQN 等算法，可以使用各个智能体的数据来更新同一个价值网络，从而加快学习速度。由于观测到的历史不同，智能体仍然可以表现出不同的行为，而且可以加入智能体索引以增强其专门化程度 (Gupta et al., 2017; Foerster et al., 2016)。虽然参数共享通常用于同构智能体（即具有相同动作空间和观测空间的智能体），但它也可用于异构智能体 (Terry et al., 2020)。由于参数共享要求智能体在训练期间共享网络，因此无法将其用于分散式在线训练。尽管如此，仍可扩展分散式算法以使用参数共享（IPPO 就是这样的例子）。采用参数共享的实现可能比其他形式的 CTDE 更具可扩展性，通常也易于实现，并且能够取得良好的性能 (Gupta et al., 2017; Foerster et al., 2018b; Yu et al., 2022)。
Alternating learning交替学习
A number of methods allow agents to take turns learning. For instance, decentralized learning methods (Amato, 2024) can be used but all agents are fixed (not learning) except for one. The learning agent learns until convergence, generating a best-response to the other agent policies. If this process continues until no agents can further improve their polices, the result is a Nash equilibrium (Banerjee et al., 2012; Su et al., 2024). In limited settings with additional strong assumptions (e.g., deterministic environments, full observability, additional coordination mechanisms to ensure coordinated policies), such methods can potentially converge to an optimal solutions (Lauer and Riedmiller, 2000; Jiang and Lu, 2023; Jiang and Lu, 2022). While these methods are sometimes called ‘decentralized’ since coordination and communication is need during learning that is not available during execution, I consider them to be CTDE.
有一些方法允许智能体轮流学习。例如，可以使用分散式学习方法 (Amato, 2024)，但除一个智能体之外，其余所有智能体均保持固定（不进行学习）。进行学习的智能体持续学习直至收敛，从而得到针对其他智能体策略的最佳响应。如果这一过程持续进行，直到所有智能体都无法进一步改进其策略，所得结果就是纳什均衡（Nash equilibrium）(Banerjee et al., 2012; Su et al., 2024)。在某些受限的情形下，若再加上较强的假设（例如，环境是确定性的、完全可观测，以及存在额外的协调机制来确保策略相互协调），此类方法有可能收敛到最优解 (Lauer and Riedmiller, 2000; Jiang and Lu, 2023; Jiang and Lu, 2022)。尽管这些方法有时被称为“去中心化”方法，但由于它们在学习过程中需要执行阶段无法获得的协调与通信，我将其归为 CTDE。
Kuba et al. (2022) use sequential agent updates (i.e., one agent updates at a time while holding the others fixed) in Heterogeneous-Agent Trust Region Policy Optimisation (HATRPO) and Heterogeneous-Agent Proximal Policy Optimisation (HAPPO). HATRPO and HAPPO build off of TRPO (Schulman et al., 2015) and PPO (Schulman et al., 2017) and remove parameter sharing used in MAPPO (Yu et al., 2022) (leading to the heterogeneous-agent name). Using this sequential update scheme, they can theoretically prove monotonic improvement for the fully observable case and the approaches can work well in practice.
Kuba et al. (2022) 在异构智能体信赖域策略优化（Heterogeneous-Agent Trust Region Policy Optimisation，HATRPO）和异构智能体近端策略优化（Heterogeneous-Agent Proximal Policy Optimisation，HAPPO）中采用了智能体顺序更新的方式（即每次只更新一个智能体，同时保持其他智能体不变）。HATRPO 和 HAPPO 分别以 TRPO (Schulman et al., 2015) 和 PPO (Schulman et al., 2017) 为基础，并取消了 MAPPO (Yu et al., 2022) 所采用的参数共享机制（“异构智能体”这一名称由此而来）。采用这种顺序更新方案，他们能够在理论上证明完全可观测情形下的性能单调改进，而且这些方法在实践中也能取得良好表现。
Addressing nonstationarity应对非平稳性
Decentralized learning methods face a nonstationary problem due to changing policies of other agents. Some methods try to address this challenge by directly modeling these changes. For example, Foerster et al. (2017) propose using importance sampling to correct for the probability differences and decay old data or simpler ‘fingerprints’ (e.g., episode number) to mark the data’s age. Other methods try to model the other agent learning updates as well (Foerster et al., 2018a; Willi et al., 2022).
由于其他智能体的策略不断变化，去中心化学习方法面临非平稳性问题。一些方法试图通过直接建模这些变化来应对这一挑战。例如，Foerster et al. (2017) 提出使用重要性采样来校正概率差异并降低旧数据的权重，或者使用更简单的“指纹”（fingerprints，例如回合编号）来标记数据的新旧程度。另一些方法还试图对其他智能体的学习更新进行建模 (Foerster et al., 2018a; Willi et al., 2022)。
5.2 Decentralizing centralized solutions5.2 将中心化解去中心化
Another potential approach is to learn a centralized solution during training and then (attempt to) decentralize it before execution. This is much harder than it seems. A centralized policy can map $\mathbf{\mathbb{H}}{}\to\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$ without the constraint (in the stochastic case) that $\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$ . That is, each agent’s policy can depend on other agent histories: $\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|\mathbf{h}{})$ . The centralized policy class is much larger and richer than the decentralized policy class.
另一种可能的方法是在训练阶段学习一个中心化解，然后在执行之前（尝试）将其去中心化。这比看起来要困难得多。中心化策略可以实现映射 $\mathbf{\mathbb{H}}{}\to\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}$，而不受（随机情形下的）约束 $\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|{h}_{i})$ 限制。也就是说，每个智能体的策略都可以依赖其他智能体的历史：$\mathbf{\pi}(\mathbf{a}|\mathbf{h}{})=\prod_{i\in\mathbb{I}}{\pi}_{i}({a}_{i}|\mathbf{h}{})$。中心化策略类比去中心化策略类大得多，也丰富得多。
For example, just considering deterministic policies of horizon $\mathcal{H}$ , there would be $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}^{|\mathbb{I}|}}}$ possible decentralized policies (assuming all agents have the same size action and observation sets) vs. $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}^{\mathcal{H}}}$ possible centralized policies. Plugging in 4 actions per agent, 5 observations, a horizon (history-length) of 10 and 4 agents gives ${{4^{5}}^{10}}^{4}=1.024\times 10^{43}$ vs. ${(4^{4})^{(5^{4})}}^{10}=2.56\times 10^{62520}$ policies. This is a massive difference.
例如，仅考虑时域长度为 $\mathcal{H}$ 的确定性策略，就有 $|{\mathbb{A}}_{i}|^{|{\mathbb{O}}_{i}|^{\mathcal{H}^{|\mathbb{I}|}}}$ 种可能的去中心化策略（假设所有智能体的动作集和观测集大小分别相同），而可能的中心化策略则有 $|\mathbf{\mathbb{A}}|^{\mathbf{\mathbb{O}}^{\mathcal{H}}}$ 种。代入每个智能体有 4 个动作、5 种观测、时域长度（历史长度）为 10，以及共有 4 个智能体，可得两类策略的数量分别为 ${{4^{5}}^{10}}^{4}=1.024\times 10^{43}$ 和 ${(4^{4})^{(5^{4})}}^{10}=2.56\times 10^{62520}$。这是一个巨大的差距。
As a result, most centralized policies will not be directly decentralizeable in the sense that they will be equivalent.[fn15] Policies can only be decentralized when they don’t depend on other agent information but centralized agents that make joint action choices based on the information of all agent can often perform much better. In fact, this centralized partially observable case is called a multi-agent POMDP and can be solved using single-agent methods (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016).
因此，大多数集中式策略无法直接转化为与之等价的分散式策略。[fn15] 只有当策略不依赖其他智能体的信息时，才能将其分散化；然而，根据所有智能体的信息选择联合动作的集中式智能体往往能够取得好得多的表现。事实上，这种中心化的部分可观测情形被称为多智能体 POMDP，可以使用单智能体方法求解 (Oliehoek and Amato, 2016)。
Footnote: 15 Note that this is not true in the fully observable case as discussed in Section 1.
脚注：15 请注意，如第 1 节所述，这一点在完全可观测的情形下并不成立。
As a result of these issues, approaches that decentralize centralized solutions are not common. Nonetheless, there are some instances. For example, Li et al. (2023) attempt to reconstruct the global information using a reconstruction loss, other methods attempt to mimic a centralized controller (Lin et al., 2022), and others allow communication during training but reduce or remove it during execution (Foerster et al., 2016; Wang et al., 2020). FOP (for Factorizes the Optimal joint Policy) (Zhang et al., 2021) makes the assumption that centralized solutions are decentralizeable and then seeks to learn solutions using a max entropy formulation along with a value factorization scheme that is similar to methods in Section 3. While FOP (and other methods) could be applied in general Dec-POMDP settings, they are likely to perform poorly when the centralized solution is sufficiently different from the decentralized solution.
由于存在这些问题，将中心化解去中心化的方法并不常见。尽管如此，仍然有一些实例。例如，Li et al. (2023) 尝试利用重构损失来重构全局信息，另一些方法尝试模仿中心化控制器 (Lin et al., 2022)，还有一些方法允许在训练阶段进行通信，但在执行阶段减少或取消通信 (Foerster et al., 2016; Wang et al., 2020)。FOP（Factorizes the Optimal joint Policy，即分解最优联合策略）(Zhang et al., 2021) 假设中心化解可以被去中心化，然后采用最大熵形式化方法，并结合类似于第 3 节中相关方法的价值分解方案来学习解。虽然 FOP（以及其他方法）可以应用于一般的 Dec-POMDP 场景，但当中心化解与去中心化解之间存在足够大的差异时，它们的表现很可能较差。
