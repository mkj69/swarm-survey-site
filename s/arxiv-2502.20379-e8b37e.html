Abstract摘要
By utilizing more computational resources at test-time, large language models (LLMs) can improve without additional training. One common strategy uses verifiers to evaluate candidate outputs. In this work, we propose a novel scaling dimension for test-time compute: scaling the number of verifiers. We introduce Multi-Agent Verification (MAV) as a test-time compute paradigm that combines multiple verifiers to improve performance. We propose using Aspect Verifiers (AVs), off-the-shelf LLMs prompted to verify different aspects of outputs, as one possible choice for the verifiers in a MAV system. AVs are a convenient building block for MAV since they can be easily combined without additional training. Moreover, we introduce BoN-MAV, a simple multi-agent verification algorithm that combines best-of- $n$ sampling with multiple verifiers. BoN-MAV demonstrates stronger scaling patterns than self-consistency and reward model verification, and we demonstrate both weak-to-strong generalization, where combining weak verifiers improves even stronger LLMs, and self-improvement, where the same base model is used to both generate and verify outputs. Our results establish scaling the number of verifiers as a promising new dimension for improving language model performance at test-time.
通过在测试时利用更多计算资源，大语言模型（LLMs）无需额外训练即可提升性能。一种常见策略是使用验证器来评估候选输出。在本研究中，我们提出了测试时计算的一个新扩展维度：增加验证器的数量。我们引入多智能体验证（Multi-Agent Verification，MAV），这是一种结合多个验证器来提升性能的测试时计算范式。我们提出使用方面验证器（Aspect Verifiers，AVs）作为 MAV 系统中验证器的一种可选实现，即通过提示让现成的 LLMs 验证输出的不同方面。AVs 是构建 MAV 的便捷组件，因为它们无需额外训练即可轻松组合。此外，我们提出了 BoN-MAV，这是一种将 best-of- $n$ 采样与多个验证器相结合的简单多智能体验证算法。BoN-MAV 展现出比自一致性和奖励模型验证更强的扩展趋势；我们还展示了从弱到强的泛化，即组合较弱的验证器能够提升能力更强的 LLMs 的性能，以及自我改进，即使用同一个基础模型来生成和验证输出。我们的结果表明，增加验证器数量是一个有前景的新维度，可用于在测试时提升语言模型的性能。

1 Introduction1 引言
Scaling the size of large language models (LLMs) and their training datasets has driven remarkable progress in artificial intelligence (Brown et al., 2020; Chowdhery et al., 2023; Hoffmann et al., 2022). However, the growing cost of scaling model size and obtaining unseen high-quality pretraining data has sparked growing interest in methods that improve LLM performance without simply scaling parameters or data. Among these, a promising new direction has emerged: scaling test-time compute, where models spend more computational resources during inference—much like humans spend more time thinking through harder problems.
扩大大语言模型（LLMs）及其训练数据集的规模，推动了人工智能领域的显著进步（Brown et al., 2020; Chowdhery et al., 2023; Hoffmann et al., 2022）。然而，扩大模型规模以及获取模型尚未见过的高质量预训练数据的成本不断上升，这使得人们日益关注那些无需单纯增加参数量或数据量就能提升 LLM 性能的方法。在这些方法中，一个有前景的新方向已经出现：扩展测试时计算，即让模型在推理过程中投入更多计算资源——这与人类花更多时间思考较难问题的方式非常相似。
A common strategy for scaling test-time compute is best-of- $n$ sampling (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021), where $n$ candidate outputs are sampled from a generator LLM and a verifier model scores each candidate output based on its quality or correctness. The highest-scoring output is then selected. Under this strategy, the amount of test-time compute can be scaled up by increasing the number of sampled outputs. However, in this work, we propose a new orthogonal scaling dimension: scaling the number of verifiers. We introduce Multi-Agent Verification (MAV), a test-time compute paradigm that combines multiple verifiers to improve performance.
扩展测试时计算的一种常见策略是 best-of- $n$ 采样（Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021），即从生成器 LLM 中采样 $n$ 个候选输出，再由验证器模型根据每个候选输出的质量或正确性进行评分。随后选择得分最高的输出。在这一策略下，可以通过增加采样输出的数量来扩大测试时计算量。然而，在本研究中，我们提出了一个与之正交的新扩展维度：增加验证器的数量。我们引入多智能体验证（MAV），这是一种结合多个验证器来提升性能的测试时计算范式。
Typically, verifiers are implemented as reward models which are trained using reinforcement learning from human feedback (Christiano et al., 2017; Stiennon et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022a). However, relying on reward models as verifiers introduces two crucial limitations for multi-agent verification: (1) each reward model has to be trained on expensive curated preference data, and (2) there is no straightforward way to combine scores generated by heterogeneous reward models trained on different datasets (they produce uncalibrated scores). These limitations make reward models poorly suited for multi-agent verification and restrict our ability to simply scale up the number and type of verifiers at test-time.
通常，验证器被实现为使用人类反馈强化学习训练的奖励模型（Christiano et al., 2017; Stiennon et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022; Bai et al., 2022a）。然而，依赖奖励模型作为验证器会给多智能体验证带来两个关键局限：(1) 每个奖励模型都必须使用成本高昂、经过精心整理的偏好数据进行训练；(2) 对于在不同数据集上训练的异构奖励模型，并没有直接的方法来组合它们生成的分数（这些分数未经校准）。这些局限使奖励模型不太适合多智能体验证，并限制了我们在测试时直接增加验证器数量和种类的能力。
To address these limitations and enable scalable multi-agent verification, we propose using Aspect Verifiers (AVs) — off-the-shelf LLMs prompted to verify specific aspects of candidate outputs through binary True/False approvals. This approach is motivated by the observation that internet data contains abundant examples of humans providing binary evaluations with feedback (e.g., educational assessments, academic peer reviews, online forums, and automated code tests), which suggests that language models may be naturally suited for binary verification. Unlike reward models, AVs do not require additional training since producing binary approvals falls naturally within the training distribution of their base LLMs, and their binary outputs can be easily combined across multiple models through simple voting mechanisms. Thus, the number and type of aspect verifiers can be easily scaled up without additional training. We note that aspect verifiers are just one possible implementation choice for the verifiers in a MAV system, which address the two key limitations of typical reward model verifiers.
为了解决这些局限并实现可扩展的多智能体验证，我们提出使用方面验证器（AVs）——即通过提示，让现成的 LLMs 以二元 True/False 认可判定来验证候选输出的特定方面。这一方法的动机来自以下观察：互联网数据中包含大量人类提供附带反馈的二元评价的示例（例如教育评估、学术同行评审、在线论坛和自动化代码测试），这表明语言模型可能天然适合二元验证。与奖励模型不同，AVs 无需额外训练，因为生成二元认可判定自然地属于其基础 LLMs 的训练分布范围，而且可以通过简单的投票机制，轻松组合多个模型的二元输出。因此，无需额外训练即可轻松增加方面验证器的数量和种类。我们指出，方面验证器只是 MAV 系统中验证器的一种可选实现，它解决了典型奖励模型验证器的两个关键局限。
By aggregating binary signals across a diverse set of aspect verifiers, we can leverage the growing ecosystem of language models to produce a more robust verification signal. Each verifier can focus on different aspects of outputs like mathematical correctness or logical soundness, and employ different verification strategies such as direct yes/no approval, step-by-step analysis, solution rephrasing, or edge case checking. Thus, a diverse set of aspect verifiers can be obtained by varying three key axes: the base LLM, the aspect to verify, and the verification strategy.
通过汇总一组多样化的方面验证器提供的二元信号，我们可以利用日益壮大的语言模型生态系统，生成更稳健的验证信号。每个验证器可以关注输出的不同方面，例如数学正确性或逻辑严密性，并采用不同的验证策略，例如直接给出是/否判断、逐步分析、重述解答或检查边界情况。因此，通过改变三个关键维度，即基础 LLM、待验证的方面和验证策略，可以获得一组多样化的方面验证器。
To investigate scaling multi-agent verification, we introduce BoN-MAV as a specific algorithm which combines best-of- $n$ sampling with aspect verifiers. This is one implementation of a MAV algorithm, combining traditional best-of- $n$ sampling with multiple verifiers. Given an input, BoN-MAV (1) samples $n$ outputs from a generator LLM, (2) collects binary approvals from a set of $m$ aspect verifiers, and (3) selects the output with the most approvals. We investigate scaling test-time compute with this approach along two orthogonal dimensions: the traditional dimension of increasing the number of sampled candidate outputs $n$ , and our novel test-time scaling dimension of increasing the number of verifiers $m$ . We find that using multiple diverse verifiers to select between candidate outputs is an effective strategy, and that performance improves as we use more verifiers.
为研究多智能体验证的规模扩展，我们引入了 BoN-MAV，这是一种将 best-of- $n$ 采样与方面验证器相结合的具体算法。这是 MAV 算法的一种实现，将传统的 best-of- $n$ 采样与多个验证器相结合。给定一个输入，BoN-MAV 会 (1) 从生成器 LLM 中采样 $n$ 个输出，(2) 收集一组 $m$ 个方面验证器给出的二元认可判断，以及 (3) 选择获得认可最多的输出。我们利用这种方法，沿两个正交维度研究测试时计算量的扩展：传统维度是增加采样候选输出的数量 $n$，而我们提出的新测试时扩展维度是增加验证器的数量 $m$。我们发现，使用多个不同的验证器在候选输出之间进行选择是一种有效策略，而且使用的验证器越多，性能就越好。
More specifically, across multiple domains and LLMs, BoN-MAV demonstrates more effective scaling patterns when we increase the number of sampled outputs, compared to best-of- $n$ with reward model verification (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021) and self-consistency (Wang et al., 2022; Li et al., 2022b; Thoppilan et al., 2022; Lewkowycz et al., 2022). We also demonstrate weak-to-strong generalization (Burns et al., 2023), whereby combining many small aspect verifiers can improve the performance of even stronger generator LLMs, and we show that BoN-MAV enables self-improvement by using the same base LLM for both the generator and set of aspect verifiers. Since BoN-MAV is just one simple approach to multi-agent verification, we expect that substantial improvements can be achieved using alternative methods.
更具体地说，在多个领域和多种 LLM 上，与使用奖励模型进行验证的 best-of- $n$ 方法 (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021) 和自一致性方法 (Wang et al., 2022; Li et al., 2022b; Thoppilan et al., 2022; Lewkowycz et al., 2022) 相比，BoN-MAV 在增加采样输出数量时表现出更有效的扩展规律。我们还展示了从弱到强的泛化（weak-to-strong generalization）(Burns et al., 2023)，即组合多个小型方面验证器能够提升更强的生成器 LLM 的性能；同时，我们表明，BoN-MAV 通过让生成器和方面验证器集合使用同一个基础 LLM，能够实现自我改进。由于 BoN-MAV 只是多智能体验证的一种简单方法，我们预计采用其他方法可以实现显著改进。

Multi-Agent Verification (MAV) is a test-time compute paradigm where multiple verifiers are combined to evaluate outputs from a generator LLM. To implement a MAV algorithm, we must address two questions: (1) What type of verifiers can be easily combined and scaled up in number without additional training? (2) How should we aggregate verification signals from multiple verifiers? In this section, we propose answers to these questions and describe one simple implementation of a multi-agent verification algorithm called BoN-MAV. We discuss future directions for alternative multi-agent verification algorithms in Section 4.

2.1 Aspect Verifiers2.1 方面验证器
In the context of test-time computation with LLMs, a verifier typically refers to a model that evaluates the quality or correctness of an output sampled from a generator LLM. Here, we ask: What type of verifiers can be easily combined and scaled up in number without additional training?
在 LLM 的测试时计算背景下，验证器通常指评估从生成器 LLM 中采样得到的输出的质量或正确性的模型。这里，我们提出一个问题：什么类型的验证器能够在无需额外训练的情况下，轻松组合并扩展其数量？
Prior works have largely focused on using neural reward models as verifiers (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Snell et al., 2024). However, these models present key challenges for scaling multi-agent verification. First, each reward model requires training on expensive curated preference data to produce reliable reward scores (Stiennon et al., 2020). Second, while ensembles of homogeneous reward models (identical model initializations trained on the same data but with different random seeds) have been proposed as a way to mitigate overoptimization (Coste et al., 2023; Eisenstein et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023b), there is no straightforward way to combine scores from heterogeneous reward models trained on different datasets. This second limitation arises because scores from different reward models are uncalibrated—–they operate on different numerical scales based on their distinct training setups. These limitations make reward models poorly suited for multi-agent verification, where we wish to simply scale up the number and type of verifiers without additional training.
以往的工作主要侧重于使用神经奖励模型作为验证器 (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Snell et al., 2024)。然而，这些模型给多智能体验证的规模扩展带来了关键挑战。首先，每个奖励模型都需要使用经过精心整理、成本高昂的偏好数据进行训练，才能产生可靠的奖励分数 (Stiennon et al., 2020)。其次，尽管已有研究提出通过集成同质奖励模型（采用相同的模型初始化，在相同数据上使用不同随机种子进行训练）来缓解过度优化 (Coste et al., 2023; Eisenstein et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023b)，但对于在不同数据集上训练的异质奖励模型，并没有直接的方法来合并其分数。第二个局限源于不同奖励模型的分数未经校准——由于各自的训练设置不同，它们采用不同的数值尺度。这些局限使奖励模型不太适合用于多智能体验证，因为在这种场景中，我们希望无需额外训练就能轻松扩展验证器的数量和类型。
We propose Aspect Verifiers (AVs) as one possible implementation choice for the verifiers in a MAV system, which address the two key limitations of typical reward model verifiers. AVs are off-the-shelf LLMs prompted to evaluate specific aspects of candidate outputs and produce binary True/False approvals. Unlike reward models, they require no additional training since binary evaluation is a natural task for LLMs (the internet contains abundant examples of humans providing binary approvals with explanation, such as educational assessments, academic peer reviews, online forums, and automated code tests), and their binary approvals can be easily combined through simple voting mechanisms, even when AVs are based on completely different models or training data. Moreover, since AVs are based on LLMs, they can produce Chain-of-Thought reasoning (Wei et al., 2021) to analyze outputs step-by-step before producing an approval, similar to recent work on generative reward models (Zhang et al., 2024b; Mahan et al., 2024). Using aspect verifiers, we can easily scale up the number and type of verifiers which may be based on different LLMs, training algorithms, architectures, data, or prompts.
我们提出方面验证器（AVs），作为 MAV 系统中验证器的一种可行实现方案，以解决典型奖励模型验证器的上述两个关键局限。AVs 是现成的 LLM，通过提示使其评估候选输出的特定方面，并给出二元的 True/False 认可判定。与奖励模型不同，它们无需额外训练，因为二元评估对 LLM 而言是一项自然的任务（互联网上有大量人类给出二元认可判定并附带解释的示例，例如教育评估、学术同行评审、在线论坛和自动化代码测试），而且，即使 AVs 基于完全不同的模型或训练数据，也可以通过简单的投票机制轻松合并它们的二元认可判定。此外，由于 AVs 基于 LLM，它们可以在给出认可判定之前生成思维链（Chain-of-Thought）推理 (Wei et al., 2021)，逐步分析输出，这与近期关于生成式奖励模型的工作类似 (Zhang et al., 2024b; Mahan et al., 2024)。使用方面验证器，我们可以轻松扩展验证器的数量和类型，这些验证器可以基于不同的 LLM、训练算法、架构、数据或提示。

The verification strategy—how the verifier reaches its decision (e.g., direct approval, going over the output step-by-step, rephrasing, checking edge cases, etc.)

2.2 Combining Aspect Verifiers2.2 组合方面验证器
With aspect verifiers as our building block, we ask: How can we effectively aggregate verification signals across multiple AVs? We take the simplest possible approach in our experiments: each binary True/False approval is a single vote, and the aggregated score for a candidate output is the sum of the positive votes from all AVs. That is, the aggregated verification score is the sum of the individual binary scores from each verifier:
以方面验证器为基本构件，我们提出一个问题：如何有效汇总多个 AVs 的验证信号？在实验中，我们采用尽可能简单的方法：每个二元 True/False 认可判断均计为一票，候选输出的聚合得分为所有 AV 投出的赞成票之和。也就是说，聚合验证得分是各个验证器给出的二元得分之和：
$$\text{AggScore}(o^{(i)})=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{M}|}\sum_{v\in\mathcal{M}}\text{BinaryScore}_{v}(o^{(i)}),$$
where $o^{(i)}\in\mathcal{O}$ is the $i$ th candidate output from the set of sampled outputs $\mathcal{O}$ , $\mathcal{M}$ is the set of aspect verifiers, and $\text{BinaryScore}_{v}:\mathcal{O}\to\{0,1\}$ maps a candidate output from $\mathcal{O}$ to the binary approval produced by verifier $v\in\mathcal{M}$ for that output. This voting strategy gives equal weight to all verifiers in the final aggregated score, and it proves remarkably effective in our experiments (see Section 3.1). However, future works could investigate more sophisticated aggregation strategies such as grouping verifiers by aspect and then voting across aspects, or having aspect verifiers debate with each other (Du et al., 2023) before producing an approval. We discuss these and other potential directions for future work in Section 4.
其中，$o^{(i)}\in\mathcal{O}$ 是采样输出集合 $\mathcal{O}$ 中的第 $i$ 个候选输出，$\mathcal{M}$ 是方面验证器的集合，$\text{BinaryScore}_{v}:\mathcal{O}\to\{0,1\}$ 将 $\mathcal{O}$ 中的候选输出映射为验证器 $v\in\mathcal{M}$ 对该输出给出的二元认可判断。这种投票策略在最终聚合得分中赋予所有验证器相同的权重，并且在我们的实验中表现出显著的有效性（见第 3.1 节）。不过，未来的工作可以研究更复杂的聚合策略，例如按方面对验证器进行分组，然后在各方面之间进行投票，或者让方面验证器在给出认可判断之前相互辩论（Du et al., 2023）。我们将在第 4 节讨论这些以及其他潜在的未来研究方向。
2.3 BoN-MAV2.3 BoN-MAV
Best-of- $n$ (BoN) sampling is a test-time optimization technique (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021) where $n$ candidate outputs are sampled from a generator LLM, each candidate is scored by a verifier model, and the highest-scoring output is selected. We introduce BoN-MAV as a simple multi-agent verification algorithm that combines best-of- $n$ sampling with aspect verifiers. It uses the simple aggregation strategy from Equation 1 and consists of three steps: (1) sampling $n$ candidate outputs from a generator LLM, (2) collecting binary approvals from a set of $m$ aspect verifiers, and (3) selecting the output with the most approvals. That is,
Best-of- $n$（BoN）采样是一种测试时优化技术（Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021），其过程是从生成器 LLM 中采样 $n$ 个候选输出，由验证器模型对每个候选输出评分，然后选择得分最高的输出。我们提出 BoN-MAV，这是一种将 best-of- $n$ 采样与方面验证器相结合的简单多智能体验证算法。它采用公式 1 中的简单聚合策略，包括三个步骤：(1) 从生成器 LLM 中采样 $n$ 个候选输出，(2) 收集一组 $m$ 个方面验证器给出的二元认可判断，以及 (3) 选择获得认可最多的输出。即，
$$\hat{i}=\argmax_{0<i<n}\left(\text{AggScore}(o^{(i)})\right)$$
where $o^{(i)}$ is the $i$ th candidate output, $n$ is the total number of sampled candidate outputs, and $\hat{i}$ is the index of the output with the highest aggregated score (the selected output). Figure 3 illustrates how BoN-MAV can be used to select between a set of candidates.
其中，$o^{(i)}$ 是第 $i$ 个候选输出，$n$ 是采样所得候选输出的总数，$\hat{i}$ 是聚合得分最高的输出（即所选输出）的索引。图 3 展示了如何使用 BoN-MAV 从一组候选输出中进行选择。
Using BoN-MAV, we can increase test-time computation by sampling more candidate outputs (increasing $n$ ) and by querying more verifiers (increasing $m=|\mathcal{M}|$ ), where test-time computation can be easily parallelized during generation as well as verification. In addition, BoN-MAV represents just one specific approach to multi-agent verification, and more nuanced aggregation algorithms or alternatives to aspect verifiers could further enhance performance.
使用 BoN-MAV，我们可以通过采样更多候选输出（增大 $n$）以及查询更多验证器（增大 $m=|\mathcal{M}|$）来增加测试时计算量，而且生成和验证阶段的测试时计算都可以轻松并行化。此外，BoN-MAV 仅代表多智能体验证的一种具体方法，更精细的聚合算法或方面验证器的替代方案可能进一步提升性能。
2.4 Verifier Engineering2.4 验证器工程
Using aspect verifiers, we can create a diverse pool of verifiers with different capabilities. However, not all verifiers are equally relevant for every domain (e.g., math, coding, general knowledge). Thus, we propose verifier engineering as a process to select a subset of verifiers most effective for a particular domain (similar to prompt engineering, where prompts are engineered for specific domains or tasks).
使用方面验证器，我们可以构建一个由具备不同能力的验证器组成的多样化验证器池。然而，并非所有验证器对每个领域（例如数学、编程、通用知识）都具有同等的相关性。因此，我们提出验证器工程（verifier engineering），将其作为选择对特定领域最有效的验证器子集的过程（类似于提示工程，即针对特定领域或任务设计提示）。
We engineer domain-specific sets of verifiers by first creating a diverse initial set $\mathcal{M}$ and then selecting the subset $\mathcal{M}^{d}\subseteq\mathcal{M}$ which contains the most relevant verifiers for domain $d$ . Specifically, for each domain $d$ , we select the subset $\mathcal{M}^{d}\subseteq\mathcal{M}$ which maximizes the average performance across all generator LLMs evaluated on a validation set. Our current approach keeps the engineered set of verifiers fixed for all questions in a domain, but future works could explore dynamically customizing verifiers for particular questions, as we discuss in Section 4.
我们通过以下方式构建领域专用的验证器集合：首先创建一个多样化的初始集合 $\mathcal{M}$，然后选择子集 $\mathcal{M}^{d}\subseteq\mathcal{M}$，其中包含与领域 $d$ 最相关的验证器。具体而言，对于每个领域 $d$，我们选择能够使所有生成器 LLM 在验证集上的平均性能达到最高的子集 $\mathcal{M}^{d}\subseteq\mathcal{M}$。我们当前的方法对一个领域中的所有问题均使用同一组经过工程设计的固定验证器，但未来的工作可以探索针对特定问题动态定制验证器，正如我们在第 4 节中所讨论的那样。

For reward model verification, we use the current top-performing open-source 8B reward model on RewardBench (Lambert et al., 2024). See Section A.3 for more details.
我们将结合多智能体验证的 Best-of- $n$ 采样（BoN-MAV）与两种成熟的测试时计算方法进行比较：(1) 结合奖励模型验证的 best-of- $n$ 采样 (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021)，其中使用训练好的神经奖励模型作为外部验证器，选择得分最高的候选输出；(2) 自一致性 (Wang et al., 2022; Li et al., 2022b; Thoppilan et al., 2022; Lewkowycz et al., 2022)，从候选输出集合中选择出现次数最多的答案。对于奖励模型验证，我们使用目前在 RewardBench (Lambert et al., 2024) 上表现最好的开源 8B 奖励模型。更多细节见第 A.3 节。

3.2 MAV Enables Weak-to-Strong Generalization and Self-Improvement3.2 MAV 实现弱到强泛化与自我改进
We now explore two important capabilities of multi-agent verification: improving strong models using only weaker verifiers (weak-to-strong generalization) and improving models using only self-verification (self-improvement).
我们现在探讨多智能体验证的两项重要能力：仅使用较弱的验证器来改进强模型（弱到强泛化），以及仅通过自我验证来改进模型（自我改进）。
Weak-to-Strong Generalization.弱到强泛化。
Prior work has shown that weak supervisors can improve the performance of strong pretrained models (Burns et al., 2023). Here, we show that multi-agent verification can be used to enhance the performance of strong generator LLMs by combining weaker verifiers. As shown in Table 2, our strongest generators (Gemini-1.5-Pro and GPT-4o) show substantial improvements over their base pass@1 accuracy when using verifiers based on weaker models (Gemini-1.5-Flash and GPT-4o-mini), and Figure 5 shows how the performance of Gemini-1.5-Pro and GPT-4o changes as we scale the number of verifiers. These results suggest that the diverse perspectives of multiple smaller models can collectively produce a verification signal robust enough to improve even state-of-the-art generators. It also shows that effective verification can be achieved using computationally cheaper and faster models, rather than requiring large verifiers which match the performance of the generator—a promising result for deploying multi-agent verification at scale.
先前研究表明，弱监督者能够提升强预训练模型的性能 (Burns et al., 2023)。在此，我们表明，多智能体验证可以通过组合较弱的验证器来提升强生成器 LLM 的性能。如表 2 所示，当使用基于较弱模型（Gemini-1.5-Flash 和 GPT-4o-mini）的验证器时，我们最强的生成器（Gemini-1.5-Pro 和 GPT-4o）的准确率相较于其基础 pass@1 准确率均有大幅提升，图 5 则展示了 Gemini-1.5-Pro 和 GPT-4o 的性能如何随验证器数量的增加而变化。这些结果表明，多个较小模型的多样化视角能够共同产生足够稳健的验证信号，甚至可以改进最先进的生成器。这也表明，使用计算成本更低、速度更快的模型即可实现有效验证，无须使用性能与生成器相当的大型验证器——这一结果为大规模部署多智能体验证展现了良好前景。
Self-Improvement.自我改进。
Multi-agent verification can also enable models to improve their own performance through self-verification. To demonstrate, we configure BoN-MAV to use the same base LLM for both generation and verification. That is, we sample candidate outputs from a generator LLM (Gemini-1.5-Flash or GPT-4o-mini) and create multiple aspect verifiers derived from the same LLM. Following the verifier engineering procedure from Section 3.1, we select the best subset of self-verifiers based on validation performance. As shown in Table 2, this self-verification approach yields substantial improvements over base pass@1 accuracy across all domains except HumanEval. For instance, GPT-4o-mini shows particularly strong self-improvement on MATH (+7%) and GPQA diamond (+8%).
多智能体验证还能够使模型通过自我验证来提升自身性能。为展示这一点，我们将 BoN-MAV 配置为在生成和验证时使用同一个基础 LLM。也就是说，我们从生成器 LLM（Gemini-1.5-Flash 或 GPT-4o-mini）中采样候选输出，并基于同一个 LLM 创建多个针对不同方面的验证器。按照第 3.1 节中的验证器工程流程，我们根据验证集上的表现选择最佳自我验证器子集。如表 2 所示，除 HumanEval 外，这种自我验证方法在所有领域中均使准确率相较于基础 pass@1 准确率有大幅提升。例如，GPT-4o-mini 在 MATH (+7%) 和 GPQA diamond (+8%) 上表现出尤为显著的自我改进。

4 Discussion4 讨论
Multi-Agent Verification (MAV) introduces a promising dimension for scaling test-time compute: scaling the number of verifiers. In Section 3, we demonstrated that combining multiple verifiers enables more effective evaluation of candidate outputs, facilitates weak-to-strong generalization, and allows for self-improvement. However, our approach has important limitations and there are several opportunities for future work to explore.
多智能体验证（MAV）为扩展测试时计算提供了一个前景可期的维度：增加验证器的数量。在第 3 节中，我们证明了结合多个验证器能够更有效地评估候选输出，促进由弱到强的泛化，并实现自我改进。然而，我们的方法存在重要局限，未来的工作也有若干值得探索的方向。
First, our investigation is limited to a pool of 20 aspect verifiers based on just two base LLMs, and the design of our verifiers is constrained by our ability to come up with diverse verification strategies and relevant aspects. Future work could explore scaling to many more verifiers and try a more systematic exploration of the space of verifiers, potentially using LLMs themselves to generate diverse verification strategies and identify relevant aspects to verify. With better-engineered verifiers and more systematic exploration, we expect to observe stronger scaling patterns.
首先，我们的研究仅限于由两个基础 LLM 构建的 20 个方面验证器，且验证器的设计受限于我们提出多样化验证策略和相关验证方面的能力。未来的工作可以探索扩展到更多验证器，并尝试更系统地探索验证器空间，或许可以利用 LLM 本身来生成多样化的验证策略，并确定需要验证的相关方面。随着验证器设计的改进和探索的系统化，我们预计将观察到更强的规模扩展规律。
Second, our aggregation technique described in Section 2.2 uses a simple voting mechanism that directly sums the individual binary approvals from each verifier. This approach does not account for the confidence or relevance of each verifier, and verifiers do not observe each other’s decisions or feedback. Future works could explore more sophisticated aggregation methods such as confidence-weighted voting or allowing verifiers to engage in debate (Du et al., 2023) before producing an approval. Moreover, our current approach uses a static engineered set of verifiers $\mathcal{M}^{d}$ for all questions in a domain $d$ , even though it may be best to use fewer or different verifiers for specific questions. Future works could investigate dynamically selecting the best set of verifiers for particular problems or adaptively choosing additional verifiers based on the results of the first few verification queries. Additionally, the field of social choice theory (Arrow, 2012; Fishburn, 2015; Kelly, 2013; Brandt et al., 2016) is concerned with procedures for collective decision-making and might offer insights for aggregating the perspectives of diverse verifiers. Although, our setting differs in that we care more about verifier capabilities than preferences.
其次，第 2.2 节中介绍的聚合技术采用了一种简单的投票机制，直接对各验证器给出的二元认可结果求和。这种方法没有考虑各验证器的置信度或相关性，而且验证器无法观察彼此的决策或反馈。未来的工作可以探索更复杂的聚合方法，例如按置信度加权的投票，或允许验证器在给出认可结果之前进行辩论 (Du et al., 2023)。此外，我们目前的方法对领域 $d$ 中的所有问题都使用一组预先设计的固定验证器 $\mathcal{M}^{d}$，尽管对于特定问题，使用更少或不同的验证器可能更合适。未来的工作可以研究如何针对特定问题动态选择最佳验证器集合，或根据最初几次验证查询的结果自适应地选择额外的验证器。此外，社会选择理论领域 (Arrow, 2012; Fishburn, 2015; Kelly, 2013; Brandt et al., 2016) 关注集体决策程序，可能为聚合不同验证器的观点提供启示。不过，我们的设定有所不同，因为我们更关心验证器的能力，而非其偏好。
Next, our implementation of BoN-MAV is limited to only a single generator LLM. Thus, an interesting direction would be to explore sampling from multiple generators in addition to evaluating with multiple verifiers. Since different models may excel at solving different types of problems, this approach could make even better use of the growing ecosystem of LLMs and their diverse capabilities.
接下来，我们的 BoN-MAV 实现仅使用了单个生成器 LLM。因此，一个值得探索的方向是，在使用多个验证器进行评估的同时，也从多个生成器中采样。由于不同模型可能擅长解决不同类型的问题，这种方法可以更充分地利用不断发展的 LLM 生态系统及其多样化能力。
Furthermore, while our results show that BoN-MAV can improve language model performance at test-time, we did not investigate finetuning the generator LLM on the outputs selected by our verifiers. Similar to how prior works have finetuned on outputs selected through self-consistency (Huang et al., 2022) or reward models (Dong et al., 2023), training on outputs selected by MAV systems could be explored as a method to improve the generator LLM and also each of the LLM-based verifiers. Moreover, an interesting direction for future work is to directly use reinforcement learning to train both the generator and verifier models. That is, generator LLMs can be trained to maximize the scores across multiple verifiers, and the verifiers can simultaneously be trained to accurately verify individual aspects of responses.
此外，尽管我们的结果表明 BoN-MAV 可以在测试时提升语言模型的性能，但我们并未研究利用验证器选出的输出来微调生成器 LLM。以往的工作曾利用通过自一致性 (Huang et al., 2022) 或奖励模型 (Dong et al., 2023) 选出的输出进行微调；类似地，可以探索利用 MAV 系统选出的输出进行训练，作为改进生成器 LLM 以及各个基于 LLM 的验证器的方法。此外，未来一个值得探索的方向是直接使用强化学习来训练生成器模型和验证器模型。也就是说，可以训练生成器 LLM，使其在多个验证器上的得分最大化，同时训练验证器，使其能够准确验证回答的各个方面。
Finally, multi-agent verification offers interesting opportunities for AI safety and oversight. The ability to combine multiple verifiers checking different aspects of model outputs aligns with recent efforts towards safety checking the outputs of language models. That is, different verifiers can be engineered to check various safety and alignment properties, from basic constraints like avoiding harmful content to more nuanced properties like reasoning transparency. Our results on weak-to-strong generalization also align with recent work on scalable oversight, where weaker systems supervise stronger ones (Amodei et al., 2016; Saunders et al., 2022; Burns et al., 2023). In general, our work connects to broader ideas in AI alignment about using multiple models to improve safety (Irving et al., 2018).
最后，多智能体验证为 AI 安全与监督提供了值得探索的机会。将多个检查模型输出不同方面的验证器结合起来的能力，与近期针对语言模型输出开展安全检查的研究方向一致。也就是说，可以设计不同的验证器来检查各种安全性与对齐属性，从避免有害内容等基本约束，到推理透明性等更细致的属性。我们关于由弱到强泛化的结果也与近期可扩展监督方面的研究一致，在这类研究中，较弱的系统监督较强的系统 (Amodei et al., 2016; Saunders et al., 2022; Burns et al., 2023)。总体而言，我们的工作与 AI 对齐领域中利用多个模型提高安全性的更广泛理念相联系 (Irving et al., 2018)。
An underlying thread throughout our work and discussion is the vision of a growing ecosystem of diverse language models that generate, verify, and learn from each other. Our work on multi-agent verification represents one step in this direction, and each of the future directions we have discussed offers a potential avenue for additional progress. We look forward to seeing how the research community advances these ideas.
贯穿我们整个工作与讨论的一条主线，是构建一个不断发展的多样化语言模型生态系统的愿景，其中的模型能够相互生成内容、验证内容并相互学习。我们关于多智能体验证的工作是朝这一方向迈出的一步，而我们讨论的每一个未来方向都为进一步取得进展提供了一条潜在路径。我们期待看到研究界如何推进这些理念。
5 Related Works5 相关工作
Scaling Test-Time Compute.扩展测试时计算。
Recent work has demonstrated that increasing computational resources during inference can significantly improve LLM performance (e.g., Wei et al. 2022; Snell et al. 2024). One line of research focuses on techniques where a single generator LLM produces additional output tokens during inference. These include scratchpads or Chain-of-Thought prompting (Nye et al., 2021; Wei et al., 2022), self-consistency or majority voting techniques (Wang et al., 2022; Li et al., 2022b; Thoppilan et al., 2022; Lewkowycz et al., 2022), and various self-reflection methods (e.g., Shinn et al. 2024; Qu et al. 2024; Madaan et al. 2024; Saunders et al. 2022; Bai et al. 2022b). Other works have explored training LLMs to generate special tokens which enhance reasoning ability at test-time (e.g., Goyal et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023a; Herel & Mikolov 2024) or augmenting language models with tool-use abilities (e.g., Schick et al. 2023; Gao et al. 2023a; Qin et al. 2023; Qu et al. 2025).
近期研究表明，在推理过程中增加计算资源可以显著提升 LLM 的性能（例如，Wei et al. 2022; Snell et al. 2024）。其中一类研究聚焦于让单个生成器 LLM 在推理过程中生成额外输出 token 的技术。这些方法包括草稿区（scratchpad）或思维链（Chain-of-Thought）提示（Nye et al., 2021; Wei et al., 2022）、自一致性或多数投票技术（Wang et al., 2022; Li et al., 2022b; Thoppilan et al., 2022; Lewkowycz et al., 2022），以及各种自我反思方法（例如，Shinn et al. 2024; Qu et al. 2024; Madaan et al. 2024; Saunders et al. 2022; Bai et al. 2022b）。其他研究探索了训练大语言模型（LLM）生成特殊词元，以增强测试时的推理能力（例如，Goyal et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023a; Herel & Mikolov 2024），或为语言模型增添工具使用能力（例如，Schick et al. 2023; Gao et al. 2023a; Qin et al. 2023; Qu et al. 2025）。
Another line of research focuses on using a verifier model to evaluate the quality or correctness of outputs sampled from generator models (Cobbe et al., 2021; Zheng et al., 2023; Snell et al., 2024). Typically, this is done through best-of- $n$ sampling (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021), where $n$ candidate outputs are generated and the highest-scoring output is selected based on some verifier. This verification can be performed at the outcome-level (Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021) or process-level (Lightman et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a). Recent works (Coste et al., 2023; Eisenstein et al., 2023) have also explored using ensembles of homogeneous reward models (identical model initializations trained on the same data but with different random seeds) to mitigate reward model overoptimization (Gao et al., 2023b). Additionally, some approaches allow reward models to produce their own Chain-of-Thought reasoning before scoring (Zhang et al., 2024b; Mahan et al., 2024). Various papers have combined language with search techniques at test-time, using verifiers to provide a heuristic signal. These verifiers may use LLMs as prompted value functions (e.g., Yu et al. 2023; Yao et al. 2024; Xie et al. 2024), incorporate real environment feedback (e.g., Zhou et al. 2023; Koh et al. 2024; Putta et al. 2024; Long 2023; Besta et al. 2024), or use trained value functions (e.g., Feng et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2024a; Chen et al. 2024). Unlike prior works which typically rely on a single reward model verifier or homogeneous reward model ensembles trained on the same data, we propose a framework for combining multiple heterogeneous verifiers without additional training, and investigate scaling the number and type of verifiers as a novel test-time scaling dimension.
另一类研究侧重于使用验证器模型来评估从生成器模型中采样得到的输出的质量或正确性（Cobbe et al., 2021; Zheng et al., 2023; Snell et al., 2024）。这通常通过 best-of- $n$ 采样来实现（Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021; Nakano et al., 2021），即生成 $n$ 个候选输出，并根据某个验证器选择得分最高的输出。这种验证可以在结果层面（Stiennon et al., 2020; Cobbe et al., 2021）或过程层面（Lightman et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a）进行。近期研究（Coste et al., 2023; Eisenstein et al., 2023）还探索了使用同质奖励模型集成（模型初始化相同，使用相同数据但以不同随机种子进行训练）来缓解奖励模型过度优化问题（Gao et al., 2023b）。此外，一些方法允许奖励模型在评分前生成自身的思维链推理（Zhang et al., 2024b; Mahan et al., 2024）。多篇论文在测试时将语言与搜索技术相结合，使用验证器提供启发式信号。这些验证器可以通过提示将 LLM 用作价值函数（例如，Yu et al. 2023; Yao et al. 2024; Xie et al. 2024），纳入真实环境反馈（例如，Zhou et al. 2023; Koh et al. 2024; Putta et al. 2024; Long 2023; Besta et al. 2024），或使用经过训练的价值函数（例如，Feng et al. 2023; Zhang et al. 2024a; Chen et al. 2024）。以往研究通常依赖单个奖励模型验证器，或在相同数据上训练的同质奖励模型集成；与之不同，我们提出了一个无需额外训练即可组合多个异质验证器的框架，并研究将验证器数量和类型的扩展作为一种新的测试时扩展维度。
Multi-Agent Reasoning with Language Models.使用语言模型进行多智能体推理。
Recent works have investigated several approaches to multi-agent interaction for improving language model reasoning. Language model debate (e.g., Du et al. 2023; Chan et al. 2023; Pham et al. 2023; Liang et al. 2023; Subramaniam et al. 2025; Li et al. 2023; Cohen et al. 2023) and multi-agent discourse (e.g., Chen et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023b; Wang et al. 2024b; Xu et al. 2023) have been studied as ways to enhance reasoning, and also as a direction for scalable oversight research (Irving et al., 2018). Prior works have also explored performing search with language models, which typically combines a generator LLM and a value model to guide exploration (see the previous paragraph). Moreover, some works have explored multi-modal reasoning through agent collaboration (e.g., Zeng et al. 2022; Li et al. 2022a; Ajay et al. 2023; Jiang et al. 2024). Unlike prior work on multi-agent reasoning which focuses on collaborative problem-solving, we introduce a framework specifically for scaling test-time verification by combining multiple verifiers without training.
近期研究探索了多种多智能体交互方法，以改进语言模型的推理能力。语言模型辩论（例如，Du et al. 2023; Chan et al. 2023; Pham et al. 2023; Liang et al. 2023; Subramaniam et al. 2025; Li et al. 2023; Cohen et al. 2023）和多智能体讨论（例如，Chen et al. 2023; Wang et al. 2023b; Wang et al. 2024b; Xu et al. 2023）已被作为增强推理的方法加以研究，同时也是可扩展监督研究的一个方向（Irving et al., 2018）。以往研究还探索了使用语言模型进行搜索，通常将生成器 LLM 与价值模型相结合，以指导探索（见上一段）。此外，一些研究探索了通过智能体协作进行多模态推理（例如，Zeng et al. 2022; Li et al. 2022a; Ajay et al. 2023; Jiang et al. 2024）。以往的多智能体推理研究侧重于协作解决问题；与之不同，我们提出了一个专门用于扩展测试时验证的框架，通过组合多个验证器来实现，且无需训练。
6 Conclusion6 结论
We have introduced Multi-Agent Verification (MAV), a test-time compute paradigm that combines multiple verifiers to improve performance. MAV enables test-time scaling along two orthogonal dimensions: (1) the traditional dimension of increasing the number of candidate outputs sampled from a generator LLM, and (2) our novel test-time scaling dimension of increasing the number of verifiers evaluating each output. We propose Aspect Verifiers (AVs) as one possible implementation choice for the verifiers in a MAV system. AVs are off-the-shelf LLMs that require no additional training and naturally support combining verification signals from models based on different LLMs, training algorithms, architectures, data, or prompts. Thus, AVs are a convenient building block for multi-agent verification, allowing us to leverage the growing ecosystem of language models and their diverse capabilities. We introduce BoN-MAV as a simple multi-agent verification algorithm and our results indicate that increasing the number of diverse verifiers is a promising dimension for scaling test-time compute. Specifically, we demonstrate that this approach improves test-time performance across multiple domains and generator LLMs, enables weak-to-strong generalization by combining multiple weak verifiers to improve stronger generators, and facilitates self-improvement when the generator LLM is also used as the base LLM for each of the aspect verifiers. Moreover, BoN-MAV represents just one approach to multi-agent verification and we expect better-engineered verifiers and more nuanced aggregation strategies to unlock even stronger scaling patterns. We hope that our work inspires future research into multi-agent verification algorithms and further exploration of scaling the number of verifiers as a powerful new dimension for test-time compute.
我们提出了多智能体验证（Multi-Agent Verification，MAV），这是一种通过组合多个验证器来提升性能的测试时计算范式。MAV 支持沿两个正交维度进行测试时扩展：(1) 增加从生成器 LLM 中采样的候选输出数量这一传统维度，以及 (2) 增加评估每个输出的验证器数量这一我们提出的新测试时扩展维度。我们提出方面验证器（Aspect Verifiers，AVs），作为 MAV 系统中验证器的一种可选实现方案。AVs 是无需额外训练的现成 LLM，能够自然地支持整合来自基于不同 LLM、训练算法、架构、数据或提示的模型的验证信号。因此，AVs 是构建多智能体验证系统的便捷组件，使我们能够利用日益壮大的语言模型生态系统及其多样化能力。我们提出了 BoN-MAV 这一简单的多智能体验证算法，结果表明，增加多样化验证器的数量是扩展测试时计算的一个有前景的维度。具体而言，我们证明了该方法能够提升多个领域和多种生成器 LLM 的测试时性能，通过组合多个弱验证器来改进更强的生成器，从而实现弱到强泛化，并且当生成器 LLM 同时被用作各个方面验证器的基础 LLM 时，该方法还能促进自我改进。此外，BoN-MAV 只是多智能体验证的一种方法，我们预计，设计得更完善的验证器和更精细的聚合策略将带来更强的扩展趋势。我们希望这项工作能够启发未来对多智能体验证算法的研究，并推动进一步探索将验证器数量的扩展作为测试时计算的一个强大新维度。
