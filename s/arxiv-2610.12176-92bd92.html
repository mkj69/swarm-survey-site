通过多智能体自监督实现递归自我改进Recursive Self-Improvement through Multi-Agent Self-Supervision
摘要Abstract
对于开放式研究等不可验证任务，当模型的输出超出人类专家也能可靠评估的范围时，模型的递归自我改进（RSI）就会面临监督瓶颈，使得模型自身（被优化对象）成为当前可用的最佳优化器和评估器。 Recursive self-improvement (RSI) of a model on non-verifiable tasks, such as open-ended research, faces a supervision bottleneck when its outputs exceed what even human experts can reliably assess, leaving the model itself (optimizee) as the best available optimizer and evaluator. 然而，在这种同质化循环中，单个模型实例难以批判性审视并改进自身的复杂推理。 However, a single model instance struggles to critique and improve its own complex reasoning under this homogeneous loop. 为此，我们提出多智能体自监督（Multi-Agent Self-Supervision，MASS），这是一种在进化式工作流优化与基于自生成轨迹的监督微调之间交替进行的 RSI 方法。 To address this, we propose Multi-Agent Self-Supervision (MASS), an RSI method that alternates between evolutionary workflow optimization and supervised fine-tuning on self-generated trajectories. 早期研究发现，多智能体拓扑结构擅长复杂推理；受此启发，MASS 通过提示引导单个基础模型迭代地提出、执行并自我评估多智能体工作流。 Guided by early findings that multi-agent topologies excel at complex reasoning, MASS prompts a single base model to iteratively propose, execute, and self-evaluate multi-agent workflows. 通过受结构性约束限制的进化搜索，模型优化这些类似计算图的编排方式，为给定任务发现最有效的差异化角色分工和信息路由方式。 Through an evolutionary search constrained by structural guardrails, the model optimizes these computational-graph-like orchestrations, discovering the most effective distinct roles and information routing for a given task. 在使用 Qwen3.6-27B 进行两个 MASS 周期后，模型在四个开放式公开基准上，每单位输出 token 的性能达到原来的 1.2–1.6×。 Over two MASS cycles with Qwen3.6-27B, the model achieves 1.2–1.6× higher performance per output tokens on four open-ended public benchmarks. 由于改进后的模型随后能够充当更好的优化器和评估器，这一交替框架使模型能力得以持续地递归自举提升。 Because the improved model subsequently acts as a better optimizer and evaluator, this alternating framework enables a continuous, recursive bootstrapping of the model’s capabilities. 此外，多智能体轨迹的训练效率也更高：基于这些轨迹训练的学生模型，其表现优于使用 1.4× 训练 token 训练的单智能体学生模型。 Moreover, multi-agent traces are also more training-efficient: a student trained on them outperforms a single-agent student trained on 1.4× more training tokens. 这些发现表明，从多智能体轨迹中联合学习编排与受限子智能体执行，可以为 RSI 提供有效的信号。 These findings suggest that jointly learning orchestration and bounded subagent execution from multi-agent trajectories can provide an effective signal for RSI.

1 引言1 Introduction
前沿 AI 系统如今已为原创性科学成果作出贡献，包括改进球堆积界限、提出新的胶子散射公式，以及设计经实验验证的纳米抗体（OpenAI, 2026; Guevara et al., 2026; Swanson et al., 2025）。 Frontier AI systems now contribute to original scientific results, including improved sphere-packing bounds, new gluon-scattering formulas, and experimentally validated nanobody designs (OpenAI, 2026; Guevara et al., 2026; Swanson et al., 2025). 为了维持这一进展的规模和速度，一个自然的方向是递归自我改进（RSI），这一框架从根本上由评估和改进这两个核心组成部分驱动。 To sustain the scale and pace of this progress, a natural direction is recursive self-improvement (RSI), a framework fundamentally driven by two core components, evaluation and improvement. 然而，在前沿能力水平上实现这两个组成部分的自动化，各自面临不同的瓶颈。 However, automating both components at the frontier presents distinct bottlenecks. 在评估方面，当工作超出可用人类评估者的专业能力范围（Burns et al., 2023），或者反馈来得过于缓慢时，就很难获得可靠的监督。 On the evaluation side, obtaining reliable supervision is difficult when the work exceeds the expertise of available human evaluators (Burns et al., 2023), or when feedback arrives too slowly. 例如，在核聚变研究中，单项托卡马克等离子体研究可能需要超过 120 百万 CPU 小时（Maheswaran et al., 2026）。 For example, in nuclear fusion research, a single tokamak plasma study may require more than 120 million CPU-hours (Maheswaran et al., 2026). 在改进方面，提升模型能力需要使用高质量的执行数据进行训练，而这又依赖于发现更好的问题求解工作流来生成这些数据。 On the improvement side, advancing the model’s capabilities requires training it on high-quality execution data, which in turn depends on discovering better problem-solving workflows to generate that data. 为了优化这些工作流，手动修改任务分解、工具使用和验证步骤，在跨任务、跨领域的情形下需要付出难以承受的工作量（Hu et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025）。 Manually revising task decompositions, tool use, and verification steps to optimize these workflows requires prohibitive effort across tasks and domains (Hu et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025). 我们研究开放式研究任务中 RSI 面临的这一双重挑战，所考察的设定是：当前可用的最强模型交替充当任务求解器、工作流优化器和评估器。 We study this dual challenge of RSI on open-ended research tasks, in a setting where the strongest available model serves interchangeably as the task solver, workflow optimizer, and evaluator. 尽管这种以模型作为评估器的方法缓解了评估方面的问题，但核心挑战在于：当被改进的模型同时也是其当前可用的最佳监督者时，如何获得有用的自监督信号。 Although this model-as-evaluator method alleviates the problem on the evaluation side, the central challenge is obtaining useful self-supervision when the model being improved is also its best available supervisor.
这一挑战源于生成、评估和修订共享同一套学习得到的先验，这意味着自我反馈可能会强化错误，而非纠正错误（Huang et al., 2024）。 This challenge arises because generation, evaluation, and revision share the same learned prior, meaning that self-feedback can reinforce rather than correct errors (Huang et al., 2024). 为了打破这种同质化约束，我们通过重组模型的工作方式来寻求更好的监督。 To break this homogeneous constraint, we seek better supervision by reorganizing the model’s working style. 我们借鉴了 Minsky 的心智社会（Society of Mind）理论，该理论认为，智能涌现于较简单组成部分之间有组织的交互（Minsky and Lee, 1988）。 We draw on Minsky’s Society of Mind, which views intelligence as emerging from organized interactions among simpler components (Minsky and Lee, 1988). 这一观点启发我们协调模型的多个副本，以激发孤立实例可能无法可靠展现的能力。 This perspective motivates coordinating copies of the model to elicit capabilities that an isolated instance may not reliably exhibit. 例如，在自动化量化金融研究时，可以通过提示让一个模型实例进行数据预处理，让另一个生成交易信号，再让第三个运行回测。 For example, when automating quantitative finance research, one model instance might be prompted to do data preprocessing, another to generate trading signals, and a third to run backtests. 该工作流如同一个具有明确结构的团队来运作。 The workflow acts as a structured team rather than a single, monolithic generator. 通过编排这些交互，我们可以将复杂任务分解为更简单的部分，克服个体的盲点，然后将其集体经验蒸馏到共享权重中。 By orchestrating these interactions, we can break down a complex task into simpler pieces, overcome individual blind spots, and then distill their collective experience into shared weights.
我们以两个假设为指导，设计一种能够激发这些增益并实现高效学习的解决方案。 Two hypotheses guide us in designing a solution that may elicit these gains and learn efficiently.
生成更高质量的数据。 Generating higher-quality data. 优化智能体间的信息流，可以激发集体能力，从引入更强推理链之外的角度提供有效的自监督。 Optimizing inter-agent information flow can elicit collective capabilities that provide effective self-supervision from a perspective other than incorporating stronger reasoning chains. 已有证据表明，优化通信结构可以提升集体表现 (Zhuge et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026)，在此基础上，我们着重优化智能体交流什么、与谁交流以及何时交流，而不只是参与的智能体数量。 Building on evidence that optimizing communication structure can improve collective performance (Zhuge et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026), we focus on optimizing what agents communicate, to whom, and when—not simply how many agents participate.
最大限度地提高微调期间的学习效率。 Maximize learning efficiency during fine-tuning. 对涉及子智能体执行与编排的轨迹进行联合训练，比基于单个智能体的长时程轨迹进行训练具有更高的学习效率。 Jointly training on subagent execution and orchestration enjoys greater learning efficiency than training on a single-agent’s long-horizon trajectories. 这一假设受到分层模仿学习的启发，在分层模仿学习中，任务分解可以提高学习效率 (Le et al., 2018)。 This hypothesis draws inspiration from hierarchical imitation learning, where task decomposition can improve learning efficiency (Le et al., 2018). 在我们的设定中，子智能体轨迹展示了范围受限的局部执行，而编排器轨迹则展示了任务委派、验证与整合。 In our setting, subagent trajectories demonstrate bounded local execution, while orchestrator trajectories demonstrate delegation, verification, and integration. 我们推测，在这些互补行为各自对应的抽象层次上学习如何解决长时程任务，比从一条交织了这两个层次的长轨迹中学习更容易。 We conjecture that learning to solve long-horizon tasks from these complementary behaviors at their respective levels of abstraction is easier than learning from one extended trajectory that interleaves both levels.
为此，我们提出了用于 RSI 的多智能体自监督（Multi-Agent Self-Supervision，MASS），该方法在工作流优化与模型后训练之间交替进行（图 1）。 To this end, we propose Multi-Agent Self-Supervision (MASS) for RSI, a method that alternates between workflow optimization and model post-training (Figure 1). 研究发现，多智能体工作流在开放式研究任务上的表现优于单智能体执行 (Lee et al., 2026)，受此启发，MASS 的内循环通过迭代反馈过程改进工作流：智能体执行多智能体工作流，自评估器评估产生的成果，优化器则修订工作流。 Motivated by findings that multi-agent workflows outperform single-agent execution on open-ended research tasks (Lee et al., 2026), MASS’s inner loop refines workflows through an iterative feedback process where the agent executes a multi-agent workflow, the self-evaluator assesses the resulting artifacts, and the optimizer revises the workflow. 外循环在优化后的工作流下生成轨迹，然后基于编排器与子智能体的对话对共享模型进行联合微调。 The outer loop generates trajectories under the optimized workflows, then jointly fine-tunes the shared model on orchestrator and subagent conversations. 更新后的模型在下一轮循环中继续承担全部三种角色。 The updated model resumes all three roles in the next cycle. 我们在受控设定下评估 MASS，其中智能体、优化器和评估器共享同一个基础模型。 We evaluate MASS in a controlled setting where the agent, optimizer, and evaluator share the same base model. 最终性能通过外部强模型的评判进行独立评估，这些评判不向智能体和工作流优化器提供（第 4.1 节）。 Final performance is assessed separately using external strong model judgments withheld from the agents and workflow optimizer (Section 4.1).

我们以 Qwen3.6-27B 为基础模型，使用来自八个合成研究任务的多智能体任务求解轨迹进行训练，这些任务涵盖金融、机器人学和药学，并在三个测试用合成研究任务及六个公开基准上评估 MASS：MLR-Bench (Chen et al., 2026)、ScienceAgentBench (Chen et al., 2025b)、DSBench (Jing et al., 2025)、AstaBench (Bragg et al., 2026)、Terminal-Bench 2.0 (Merrill et al., 2026) 和 SWE-bench Verified (Jimenez et al., 2024)。 We train Qwen3.6-27B as the base model on multi-agent task-solving trajectories from eight synthetic research tasks spanning finance, robotics, and pharmacy, and evaluate MASS on three test synthetic research tasks, and six public benchmarks: MLR-Bench (Chen et al., 2026), ScienceAgentBench (Chen et al., 2025b), DSBench (Jing et al., 2025), AstaBench (Bragg et al., 2026), Terminal-Bench 2.0 (Merrill et al., 2026), and SWE-bench Verified (Jimenez et al., 2024). 经过两轮 MASS 循环，训练后模型在三个测试任务上相对于基础模型的胜率从 $53.9\%$ 提高到 $69.9\%$（第 4.2 节，图 1(a)），在四个研究基准（MLR-Bench、ScienceAgentBench、DSBench 和 AstaBench）上，其每个输出 token 所得分数为基础模型的 $1.2$ – $1.6\times$（图 2）。 Across two MASS cycles, the trained model’s win rate against the base model on the three test tasks increases from $53.9\%$ to $69.9\%$ (Section 4.2, Figure 1(a)), and on the four research benchmarks (MLR-Bench, ScienceAgentBench, DSBench, and AstaBench) its score per output token is $1.2$ – $1.6\times$ that of the base model (Figure 2). 值得注意的是，仅基于任务求解轨迹进行后训练，就能提升 RSI 循环中全部三种角色的表现：任务求解、工作流优化和评估（第 4.2 节，图 3）。 Notably, post-training only on task-solving trajectories improves all three roles in the RSI loop: task solving, workflow optimization, and evaluation (Section 4.2, Figure 3). 训练后的模型能够更快地发现高质量工作流，评估器的准确率也从 $73\%$ 提高到 $93\%$。 The trained model discovers high-quality workflows faster, and the evaluator accuracy increases from $73\%$ to $93\%$ .
我们进一步发现，优化后的工作流形成了更具任务针对性的信息交接与路由方式，这与我们的信息流假设一致（第 4.3 节）。 We further find that optimized workflows develop more task-specific information handoffs and routing, consistent with our information-flow hypothesis (Section 4.3). 第一轮循环得到的多智能体学生模型相对于基础模型的胜率为 $68.3\%$，而使用约 $1.4\times$ 数量的已处理监督 token 训练的单智能体学生模型的胜率为 $64.0\%$（第 4.4 节，图 6）。 The first-cycle multi-agent student achieves a $68.3\%$ win rate against the base model, compared with $64.0\%$ for a single-agent student trained on approximately $1.4\times$ more processed supervised tokens (Section 4.4, Figure 6). 这表明，联合学习编排与范围受限的子智能体执行，可以为 RSI 提供更有效的监督。 This suggests that jointly learning orchestration and bounded subagent execution can provide more effective supervision for RSI. 我们的消融实验进一步确定，工作流优化而非评估，是同质 RSI 循环中的一个主要瓶颈（第 5.1 节）。 Our ablations further identify workflow optimization, rather than evaluation, as a major bottleneck in the homogeneous RSI loop (Section 5.1). 最后，轨迹分析提供的证据表明，后训练使模型内化了信息交接行为（第 5.2 节）。 Finally, trajectory analysis provides evidence that post-training internalizes information-handoff behaviors (Section 5.2).
总体而言，我们的工作提出了一种利用多智能体协调为 RSI 生成自监督信号的算法。 Overall, our work introduces an algorithm that uses multi-agent coordination to generate self-supervision for RSI.

概述。Overview.
MASS 交替进行多智能体工作流优化（算法 2）和将工作流内化的模型微调（算法 3）。 MASS alternates between multi-agent workflow optimization (Algorithms 2) and model fine-tuning that internalizes the workflows (Algorithms 3). 在第 $g$ 代，当前模型 ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g)}$ 同时担任任务求解智能体、工作流优化器和评估器。 At generation $g$ , the current model ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g)}$ serves as the task-solving agent, workflow optimizer, and evaluator. 算法 2 首先在模型权重固定的情况下，生成针对各任务的保留工作流。 Algorithm 2 first produces task-specific retained workflows using fixed model weights. 随后，算法 3 在这些工作流下生成轨迹并进行自我筛选，再对同一模型进行微调，得到 ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g+1)}$。 Algorithm 3 then generates and self-selects trajectories under these workflows and fine-tunes the same model to obtain ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g+1)}$ . 更新后的模型在下一代中承担全部三种角色。 The updated model assumes all three roles in the next generation. 因此，MASS 形成了一个递归循环：当前模型先改进其各个副本之间的协作方式，再从由此产生的协作轨迹中学习。 Thus, MASS forms a recursive loop in which the current model first improves how copies of itself coordinate, then learns from the resulting coordinated trajectories. 下一代使用更新后的权重重复相同的过程。 The next generation repeats the same process with the updated weights. 经过 $R$ 次迭代后，我们使用 ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ 在 ${\mathcal{T}}_{\text{test}}$ 上评估所有 $g$ 对应的 ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g)}$，并报告测试得分。 After $R$ iterations, we evaluate ${\mathcal{L}}^{(g)}$ on ${\mathcal{T}}_{\text{test}}$ with ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{ext}}$ for all $g$ and report the test score.

算法 2。 Algorithm 2. 多智能体工作流优化。 Multi-agent workflow optimization. 已有研究表明，在提示中定义多智能体工作流，是使 LLM 遵循组织指令并据此行动的一种有效方式 (Nielsen et al., 2026; Tang et al., 2026)。 Defining multi-agent workflow in the prompt is demonstrated to be an effective way for LLMs to follow the organization instruction and act accordingly (Nielsen et al., 2026; Tang et al., 2026). 据此，我们将工作流定义为对编排器—子智能体架构的文本规范，该规范附加在任务提示之后，用于指导编排器如何分派工作、交换信息以及调用子智能体。 Following this, we define a workflow as a textual specification of an orchestrator–subagent architecture that is appended to the task prompt and guides how the orchestrator delegates work, exchanges information, and invokes subagents. 具体而言，对于任务 $t$，我们将工作流表示为 $w_{t}:=\{\mathbf{h}_{t},{(\mathbf{r}_{t,j},\mathbf{u}_{t,j},\mathbf{c}_{t,j})}_{j=1}^{m_{t}}\}$，其中 $m_{t}$ 是子智能体的数量。 Specifically, for task $t$ , we represent the workflow as $w_{t}:=\{\mathbf{h}_{t},{(\mathbf{r}_{t,j},\mathbf{u}_{t,j},\mathbf{c}_{t,j})}_{j=1}^{m_{t}}\}$ , where $m_{t}$ is the number of subagents. 这里，$\mathbf{r}_{t,j}$ 指定子智能体 $j$ 的角色，$\mathbf{u}_{t,j}$ 指定其指令，$\mathbf{c}_{t,j}$ 指定其输出契约（output contract）。 Here, $\mathbf{r}_{t,j}$ specifies subagent $j$ ’s role, $\mathbf{u}_{t,j}$ its instructions, and $\mathbf{c}_{t,j}$ its output contract. 该契约规定子智能体向编排器返回哪些信息，而 $\mathbf{h}_{t}$ 则规定子智能体的调用顺序。 The contract specifies what information the subagent returns to the orchestrator, while $\mathbf{h}_{t}$ specifies the order of subagent calls. 我们将 $w_{t}$ 拼接到任务提示 $x_{t}$ 之后，并以 ${\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t};{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{agent}})$ 的形式执行编程智能体。 We concatenate $w_{t}$ to the task prompt $x_{t}$ , and execute the coding agent as ${\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t};{\mathcal{L}}_{\text{agent}})$ . 编排器和所有子智能体共享同一个 ${\mathcal{L}}_{\text{agent}}$。 The orchestrator and all subagents share the same ${\mathcal{L}}_{\text{agent}}$ . 下方展示了简化的工作流格式；完整示例见附录 I.1 和 I.2。 A simplified workflow format is shown below; complete examples appear in Appendices I.1 and I.2. 
$w$ • orchestrator: $\mathbf{h}=\{\texttt{subagent}_{2}\rightarrow\texttt{subagent}_{m}\rightarrow\cdots\rightarrow\texttt{subagent}_{1}\}$ • $\texttt{subagent}_{1}:\{\text{role}:\mathbf{r}_{1},\;\text{instruction}:\mathbf{u}_{1},\;\text{output contract}:\mathbf{c}_{1}\}$ • $\cdots$ • $\texttt{subagent}_{m}:\{\text{role}:\mathbf{r}_{m},\;\text{instruction}:\mathbf{u}_{m},\;\text{output contract}:\mathbf{c}_{m}\}$
对于每个训练任务 $t\in\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{train}}$，工作流优化过程维护一个独立序列 $\{w_{t}^{(i)}\}_{i\geq 0}$，以及由 ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ 判定的迄今最佳工作流 $w_{t}^{*}$。 For each training task $t\in\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{train}}$ , workflow optimization maintains an independent sequence $\{w_{t}^{(i)}\}_{i\geq 0}$ and a best so-far workflow $w_{t}^{*}$ , as determined by ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ . 执行工作流 $w_{t}^{(i)}$ 会产生 $(\tau_{t}^{(i)},y_{t}^{(i)})\sim{\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t}^{(i)};{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{agent}})$。 Executing workflow $w_{t}^{(i)}$ produces $(\tau_{t}^{(i)},y_{t}^{(i)})\sim{\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t}^{(i)};{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{agent}})$ . 在第 $i$ 次迭代中，${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ 将当前输出 $y_{t}^{(i)}$ 与迄今为止最佳工作流 $w_{t}^{*}$ 生成的输出 $y_{t}^{*}$ 进行比较，并返回反馈 $d_{t,i}={\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}(y_{t}^{(i)},y_{t}^{*};x_{\mathrm{eval}})$。 At iteration $i$ , ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ compares the current output $y_{t}^{(i)}$ with the output $y_{t}^{*}$ produced by the best so-far workflow $w_{t}^{*}$ and returns feedback $d_{t,i}={\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}(y_{t}^{(i)},y_{t}^{*};x_{\mathrm{eval}})$ . 若 $y_{t}^{(i)}\succ y_{t}^{*}$，则更新 $w_{t}^{*}\leftarrow w_{t}^{(i)}$ 和 $y_{t}^{*}\leftarrow y_{t}^{(i)}$。 If $y_{t}^{(i)}\succ y_{t}^{*}$ , we update $w_{t}^{*}\leftarrow w_{t}^{(i)}$ and $y_{t}^{*}\leftarrow y_{t}^{(i)}$ . 随后，将反馈 $d_{t,i}$ 添加到该任务的专属历史记录中，${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{opt}}$ 利用这些记录以及优化提示 $x_{\mathrm{opt}}$，提出下一个工作流 $w_{t}^{(i+1)}$。 The feedback $d_{t,i}$ is then added to the task-specific history, which ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{opt}}$ uses, together with the optimization prompt $x_{\mathrm{opt}}$ , to propose the next workflow $w_{t}^{(i+1)}$ . 这类似于精英保留数量为 1 的演化过程 (Risi et al., 2026)。 This resembles an evolution process with an elite size one (Risi et al., 2026).
为优先改进多智能体协调，$x_{\mathrm{opt}}$ 明确强调通信结构 $(\mathbf{h}_{t},\{\mathbf{c}_{t,j}\}_{j=1}^{m_{t}})$，其优先级高于角色和指令组成部分 $\{(\mathbf{r}_{t,j},\mathbf{u}_{t,j})\}_{j=1}^{m_{t}}$。 To prioritize improvements in multi-agent coordination, $x_{\mathrm{opt}}$ explicitly emphasizes the communication structure $(\mathbf{h}_{t},\{\mathbf{c}_{t,j}\}_{j=1}^{m_{t}})$ over the role and instruction components $\{(\mathbf{r}_{t,j},\mathbf{u}_{t,j})\}_{j=1}^{m_{t}}$ . 因此，$w_{t}^{*}$ 表示在循环内依次进行的成对评估中，经自我评判得到的最佳工作流，而非经认证的全局最优解。 Thus, $w_{t}^{*}$ denotes the best self-judged workflow under sequential in-loop pairwise evaluation, rather than a certified global optimum. 下文给出 $x_{\mathrm{opt}}$ 的概述，附录 A.1 中的算法 2 总结了该过程。 We show the overview of $x_{\mathrm{opt}}$ below, Algorithm 2 in Appendix A.1 summarizes the procedure.

算法 3. 通过监督微调实现工作流内化。Algorithm 3. Workflow internalization by supervised fine-tuning.
接下来，我们通过监督微调（SFT），将算法 2 所得工作流 $w_{t}^{*}$ 诱导的行为内化。 We next internalize the behavior induced by the workflows $w_{t}^{*}$ obtained from Algorithm 2 through supervised fine-tuning (SFT). 对于每个训练任务 $t\in\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{train}}$，我们独立执行 ${\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t}^{*};{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{agent}})$ 共 $M$ 次，生成候选轨迹–工作区对 $\{(\tau_{t,j},y_{t,j})\}_{j=1}^{M}$。 For each training task $t\in\mathcal{T}_{\mathrm{train}}$ , we execute ${\mathcal{A}}(x_{t}\oplus w_{t}^{*};{\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{agent}})$ independently $M$ times, yielding candidate trajectory–workspace pairs $\{(\tau_{t,j},y_{t,j})\}_{j=1}^{M}$ . 在每个任务内，${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ 通过成对锦标赛比较可交付工作区 $y_{t,{j_{1}}}$ 和 $y_{t,{j_{2}}}$，其中 $j_{1}\neq j_{2}\in[M]$。 Within each task, ${\mathcal{L}}_{\mathrm{eval}}$ compares pairs of deliverable workspaces $y_{t,{j_{1}}}$ and $y_{t,{j_{2}}}$ , where $j_{1}\neq j_{2}\in[M]$ , in a pairwise tournament. Bradley–Terry 模型将这些成对评判转换为工作区级别的分数。 A Bradley–Terry model converts these pairwise judgments into workspace-level scores. 我们按分数对轨迹–工作区对进行排序，将每个任务中排名前 $K-1$ 的轨迹 $\{\tau_{t,j}\}_{j=1}^{K-1}$ 用作 SFT 训练集 ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{train}}$，并将排名第 $K$ 的轨迹留作验证集 ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{val}}$。 We rank the trajectory–workspace pairs by score, use the top $K-1$ trajectories $\{\tau_{t,j}\}_{j=1}^{K-1}$ from each task as the SFT training set ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{train}}$ , and reserve the $K$ th trajectory for the validation set ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{val}}$ . 在构建数据集时，我们从提示中移除 $w^{*}_{t}$。 We remove $w^{*}_{t}$ from the prompt for dataset construction.
${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{train}}$ 和 ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{val}}$ 中的轨迹被划分为最多包含 $L_{\max}$ 个词元的窗口，可选择保留前文上下文，但在损失计算中将其屏蔽。 Trajectories in ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{train}}$ and ${\mathcal{D}}_{\mathrm{val}}$ are partitioned into windows of at most $L_{\max}$ tokens, with preceding context optionally retained but masked from the loss. 令 $\mathcal{D}$ 表示由此得到的 SFT 窗口集合。 Let $\mathcal{D}$ denote the resulting set of SFT windows. 对于窗口 $z=(z_{1},\ldots,z_{|z|})\in\mathcal{D}$，若 $z_{\ell}$ 是受监督的助手目标词元，则令 $\mu_{\ell}=1$；对于提示词元、工具输出和被屏蔽的上下文，则令 $\mu_{\ell}=0$。 For a window $z=(z_{1},\ldots,z_{|z|})\in\mathcal{D}$ , let $\mu_{\ell}=1$ if $z_{\ell}$ is a supervised assistant target and $\mu_{\ell}=0$ for prompt tokens, tool outputs, and masked context. 令 $q$ 表示已存储窗口上的采样分布。 Let $q$ denote the sampling distribution over stored windows. SFT 目标为 $\ell_{\mathrm{SFT}}(\theta)=-\mathbb{E}_{z\sim q}\left[\frac{\sum_{\ell=1}^{|z|}\mu_{\ell}\log p_{\theta}(z_{\ell}\mid z_{<\ell})}{\sum_{\ell=1}^{|z|}\mu_{\ell}}\right]$。 The SFT objective is $\ell_{\mathrm{SFT}}(\theta)=-\mathbb{E}_{z\sim q}\left[\frac{\sum_{\ell=1}^{|z|}\mu_{\ell}\log p_{\theta}(z_{\ell}\mid z_{<\ell})}{\sum_{\ell=1}^{|z|}\mu_{\ell}}\right]$ . 附录 A.2 中的算法 3 总结了该过程。 Algorithm 3 in Appendix A.2 summarizes the procedure.

7 结论7 Conclusion
我们研究了同一个 LLM 同时充当智能体、优化器和评估器时的递归自我改进（RSI）。 We study recursive self-improvement (RSI) when the same LLM serves as the agent, optimizer, and evaluator. 由于这一同质循环共享同一个学得的先验，它可能会强化自身的错误，而非纠正这些错误。 Because this homogeneous loop shares a learned prior, it risks reinforcing its own errors rather than correcting them. 借鉴心智社会（Society of Mind）理论，我们通过使用模型的多个副本构建多智能体系统，并将其集体取得的改进蒸馏为自监督信号来应对这一问题；我们表明，这能为 RSI 提供有用的自监督信号。 Drawing on the Society of Mind, we address this by forming a multi-agent system from multiple copies of the model and distilling their collective gains as self-supervision, and we show this provides useful self-supervision in RSI. 加快每个循环中这些性能提升的速度，仍是未来工作的重要方向。 Accelerating the speed of these performance gains in each cycle remains an important direction for future works.
