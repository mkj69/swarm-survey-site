极简 LLM 系统中的涌现文化Emergent Culture in Minimal LLM Systems
摘要Abstract
当 LLM 智能体在没有轮次之外的上下文、仅有极简提示和简单工具的条件下运行时，会发生什么？ What happens when LLM agents operate with no context outside a turn, minimal prompting, and simple tools? 受群体工程的启发，我们赋予由三个智能体组成的群体发送消息和操作共享的、持续衰减的文本存储的能力，从而引入演化压力。 Inspired by swarm engineering, we give collectives of three agents the ability to send messages and manipulate a shared actively decaying text store, introducing evolutionary pressure. 在没有自上而下的工程设计的情况下，智能体自发合作，发展出存储管理策略，并生成复杂且不断演化的文化产物。 The agents spontaneously cooperate, develop storage management strategies, and generate complex evolving cultural artifacts, with no top-down engineering. 借助动力系统分析工具，我们表明，这些行为表现出超越衰减存储熵视界的、有结构的长程相干性，与 Sperber 意义上的涌现文化相一致。 Using tools from dynamical systems analysis, we show that these behaviours exhibit structured long-range coherence beyond the entropy horizon of the decaying store, consistent with emergent culture in the Sperberian sense.

引言Introduction
大多数关于 LLM 协作的研究都着眼于增加投入：更多上下文、更多记忆、更多提示工程、更多基础设施。 Most work on collaborating LLMs has focussed on more, more context, more memory, more prompt engineering, more infrastructure. 但如果我们退后一步，将系统精简到最低限度，只让无状态模型访问一些共享的衰减存储、使用基本通信功能，并提供一个极简提示，除此之外别无其他，又会怎样？ But what if we take a step back and build a system stripped back to a minimum, giving stateless models access to some shared decaying storage and basic communications and a minimal prompt, and nothing else? 我们会得到涌现的复杂性和有趣的行为吗？ Would we get emergent complexity and interesting behaviours? 还是说，由这些智能体组成的系统会陷入重复或停滞？ Or would systems of such agents collapse into repetition or stasis?
值得注意的是，答案是前者。 Remarkably, the answer is the former. 采用不同模型组合的多次运行都能稳定地产生角色分配、协调协议、世界构建、神话创作，以及随时间不断增长的复杂性。 Multiple runs with different model combinations reliably generate role allocation, coordination protocols, world-building, myth-making, and increasing complexity over time. 将智能体的上下文精简到最低限度，迫使它们保留外部记录、对抗熵、发展系统并演化出文化。 By stripping the agents context back to the minimum, they are forced to keep external records, to fight entropy, to develop systems, to evolve culture. 尽管各次输出往往大相径庭，但它们之间存在深层相似性，并且会涌现出一些基本的吸引子。 Although the individual outputs are often wildly different, there are deep similarities and some fundamental attractors emerge.
我们借鉴群体机器人学和自然界群体的经验：由简单智能体组成的群体，通过环境或消息传递进行极少量通信，就能产生复杂的、涌现的系统级行为。 We draw on lessons from swarm robotics, and swarms in nature, where collectives of simple agents using minimal communication via the environment or message passing produce complex emergent system-level behaviours. 通过外部衰减存储进行通信是一种迹协调（stigmergy）；正如白蚁建造宏伟的巢穴，无状态的群体 LLM 也会构建连贯的叙事，这是 Sperber 所定义的文化的一种迹象，其中智能体必须不断读取并重新创造持久的意义。 The mode of communication via external decaying storage is a form of stigmergy; as termites build cathedrals, so stateless swarm-LLMs build coherent narratives, a signal of culture as defined by Sperber, where agents must continually read and recreate persistent meaning.
相关工作Related work
大量关于构建多智能体 LLM 系统的研究都侧重于构建外部基础设施和支撑框架；Park et al., (2023) 构建了具备检索、反思和规划功能的结构化记忆系统，智能体具有预先构建的人设；Li et al., (2023) 使用能够相互通信的智能体，每个智能体都有预先设定的角色；Hong et al., (2023) 通过标准操作流程和精心设计的提示约束智能体；Packer et al., (2023) 构建了受操作系统启发的记忆层次结构，以管理有限的上下文。 Much work on building multi-agent LLM systems has focussed on building external infrastructure and scaffolding; Park et al., (2023) build a structured memory system, with retrieval, reflection, and planning, and agents have a pre-constructed persona, Li et al., (2023) have communicating agents, each with a constructed role, Hong et al., (2023) constrain agents with standard operating procedures and careful prompting, Packer et al., (2023) construct an OS inspired memory hierarchy to manage limited context. 在这些研究中，面向特定任务的流程都被视为一个自上而下的设计问题，而混乱性和不可靠性则被视为需要管理和约束的因素。 In each case, the task-specific process is approached as a top-down design problem, messiness and unreliability to be managed and constrained. 利用 LLM 生成长篇文本的研究通常依赖大量人工编排，并侧重于层次化支撑框架 (Bai et al.,, 2024; Teleki et al.,, 2025; Wang et al.,, 2025)。 Work on using LLMs to generate large texts is generally heavily curated and focussed on hierarchical scaffolding (Bai et al.,, 2024; Teleki et al.,, 2025; Wang et al.,, 2025). 已有观察发现，模型表现会退化为重复，多轮交互中的信息增益也会下降 (Hong et al.,, 2025; Gorle et al.,, 2025)，但文献对此研究不足，生成超过几千词的连贯文本仍然困难。 It is observed, but under-researched in the literature, that model performance degrades to repetition and information gain over multi-turn interactions decreases (Hong et al.,, 2025; Gorle et al.,, 2025), generating more than a few thousand words of coherent text remains difficult. Perez et al., (2026) 利用迭代任务研究 LLM 吸引子，让单个无状态智能体连续修改输入文本。 Perez et al., (2026) use iterated tasks to study LLM attractors, with single stateless agents successively modifying input texts.
相比之下，群体机器人学领域 (Brambilla et al.,, 2013) 依赖于个体智能体彼此之间以及与环境之间的含噪交互，使复杂的大规模行为自下而上地涌现。 The field of swarm robotics (Brambilla et al.,, 2013) by contrast, relies on bottom-up emergence of complex large-scale behaviours from the noisy interactions of individual agents with each other and with the environment. 通过环境进行通信，即迹协调 (Grassé,, 1959)，是一项重要的组织原则 (Heylighen,, 2016)。 Communication via the environment, stigmergy (Grassé,, 1959) is an important organising principle (Heylighen,, 2016). 我们从中获得启发：去除几乎所有自上而下的支撑框架，并提供一个含噪、受熵作用的共享记忆通信通道，从而构建一个能让结构自然涌现并持续存在的环境。 We take inspiration from this; by stripping away almost all top-down scaffolding, and providing a noisy, entropic shared memory communication channel, we construct an environment where structure emerges naturally, and is sustained.
我们知道，远离平衡态的系统会呈现涌现的复杂结构 (Prigogine,, 1978)，并具有大尺度的持久组织性。 We know that far-from-equilibrium systems exhibit emergent complex structure (Prigogine,, 1978), with large scale persistent organisation. 我们认为，将智能体置于一个具有熵汇——即衰减的共享记忆——的环境中，会迫使它们使用以 token 流形式提供的“能量”来维持结构。 We argue that placing agents in an environment with an entropic sink, the decaying shared memory, forces them to use ‘energy’ in the form of the token flow to maintain structure. 这种持久结构能够延续至熵视界之后，而届时所有更早的数据都已损坏或被删除；它是 Sperber 意义上通过持续重构形成的文化的一种迹象 (Sperber,, 1996; Claidière et al.,, 2014)，智能体必须通过读取、重新解释、总结和写入来重置熵时钟。 This persistent structure, lasting beyond the entropy horizon after which all older data is corrupt or deleted is a signal of culture in the Sperberian sense (Sperber,, 1996; Claidière et al.,, 2014) of continual reconstruction, agents must read, re-interpret, summarise, and write to reset the entropy clock. 这并非文化传递 (Dawkins,, 1976)，因为其中不存在完美复制，智能体是在自身认知吸引子所造成的偏向下进行重构。 This is not cultural transmission (Dawkins,, 1976) because there is no perfect reproduction, agents reconstruct biassed by their cognitive attractors. Sperber 的框架预测，稳定的文化产物是那些在经过噪声信道和带有偏差的心智重构后仍能存续的产物。 Sperber’s framework predicts that stable cultural artifacts are those that survive reconstruction through noisy channels and biassed minds.
我们从多个分析层次展示了这一点：从揭示不同涌现式存储管理策略的底层数据流分析，到新词汇在较长时期内的生成与持续存在，再到大尺度的语义轨迹。 We show this using several levels of analysis; from low level dataflow analysis highlighting the differing emergent storage management strategies, the generation and persistence of novel vocabulary over long periods, to large scale semantic trajectories. 我们将生成的文本划分为多个部分，利用其语义嵌入（semantic embeddings）(Li et al.,, 2022; Lin et al.,, 2017; Giorgi et al.,, 2021)，采用动力系统分析中的技术：递归图（Recurrence Plots）(Eckmann et al.,, 1987) 和 RQA (Zbilut and Webber Jr,, 1992; Marwan et al.,, 2007)。 Using semantic embeddings (Li et al.,, 2022; Lin et al.,, 2017; Giorgi et al.,, 2021) of the generated text divided into sections, we use techniques from dynamical systems analysis; Recurrence Plots (Eckmann et al.,, 1987) and RQA (Zbilut and Webber Jr,, 1992; Marwan et al.,, 2007).

分析方法Analysis methodology
分析这些实验的输出并非易事，因为每次运行，即使持续时间很短，也会产生大量以各种方式组织的文本。 Analysing the outputs of these experiments is non-trivial, each run, even short ones, produces large amounts of text, structured in various ways. 主要的输出产物是事件日志，它是一个 JSON 文件，记录了发生的每个事件及其时间戳和元数据，可在代码仓库中获取。 The primary output artifact is the event log, comprising a JSON file with every event that occurred, with timestamps and metadata, available in the repository.
为分析三个智能体进行的 100 个周期的运行，我们采用了一个分为三个层次的方法。 To approach the analysis of the three-agent 100 cycle runs, we use a three layer approach. 首先，我们考察智能体与存储之间的实际数据流，关注常见的涌现通信模式。 Firstly, we look at the actual dataflow between the agents and storage, noting common emergent communication patterns. 随后，我们开展文本分析，考察在熵视界之后仍然存续的新词汇；最后，我们使用文本嵌入和递归量化分析（RQA），以人类语料库中的一些样本为参照，对智能体输出进行定量定位，并考察长程语义相关性。 Then we perform a textual analysis, looking at novel vocabulary persisting beyond the entropy horizon, and finally we use text embeddings and RQA analysis to quantitively position the agent output with respect to some examples of the human corpus, and to look at long-range semantic correlations.

长程连贯性Long range coherence
显然，swarm-LLM 系统通过智能体与键值存储之间以及智能体彼此之间的交互，产生了丰富的行为动态。 Clearly, the swarm-LLM system generates rich dynamics of behaviour with agents interacting with the key-value storage, and each other. 但它究竟生成了什么样的文本输出？ But what sort of textual output is being generated? 它能否在超过熵视界的更长时间跨度内维持共同的主题？ Does it maintain common themes over longer term periods than the entropy horizon? 如果是这样，我们可以将其称为涌现文化（emergent culture），因为稳定的语义术语的存续时间既超过了单个智能体实例的寿命（1 个周期），也超过了存储记录衰减并被删除所需的时间（16 个周期）。 If so, we could term this emergent culture, in the sense of stable semantic terms outlasting both individual agent instances (1 cycle) and the decay and deletion of stored records (16 cycles). 因此，存续时间超过熵视界的术语值得关注，并表明存在抗熵行为。 So terms that persist beyond the entropy horizon are interesting, and indicative of anti-entropic behaviour.
我们将专有名词或名称（例如 ‘Elara’）、首字母大写的短语（例如 ‘Fractal Garden’），以及普通形式和带连字符形式的新词（例如 ‘emergentia’、‘Over-Chord’）视为可能的语义标记，用以考察系统能否在熵视界之外保持连贯性。 We consider proper nouns, or names (e.g. ‘Elara’), capitalised phrases (e.g. ‘Fractal Garden’), and neologisms, normal and hyphenated (e.g. ‘emergentia’, ‘Over-Chord’) as possible semantic markers that we can use to examine whether the system maintains coherence beyond the entropy horizon. 为提取这些标记，我们采用以下流程，所用词典由 UNIX 标准词典 web2 和 wordfreq，Speer, (2022) 组成：对于每个周期，我们将所有智能体的存储输出拼接起来。 To extract them, we use the following pipeline, with the dictionary consisting of the UNIX standard dictionary web2 and wordfreq, Speer, (2022): For each cycle, we concatenate all storage output from the agents. 然后，我们将所有连续的空白字符转换为单个空格字符。 We then convert all contiguous whitespace to single space characters. 我们使用正则表达式提取首字母大写的短语，该表达式匹配不位于句首的两个或更多首字母大写的单词，这些单词之间可以由连接词（of、the、and、in …）连接。 Capitalised phrases are extracted with a regular expression matching two or more capitalised words not at a sentence start optionally joined by connectors (of, the, and, in …). 仅在单个周期中出现的短语均被剔除。 Any occurring in only a single cycle are dropped. 专有名词是指在文本中以首字母大写形式出现、不属于首字母大写短语且未收录于词典的单词。 Proper nouns are words that appear capitalised in the text, not part of a capitalised phrase, and not in the dictionary. 对于不位于句首且已收录于词典的单个首字母大写单词，如果其以首字母大写形式出现的次数多于非大写形式，且不属于带连字符的新词，则将其归为名称（例如 ‘Ruby’）。 Single capitalised words not at a sentence start and also in the dictionary are promoted to names if they occur more often capitalised than not, and are not part of a hyphenated neologism (e.g. ‘Ruby’). 新词通过其未被词典收录这一特征来识别。 Neologisms are detected by lack of presence in the dictionary. 系统经常生成带连字符的新词，但它们很难与 ‘self-aware’ 之类的常见英语表达区分开，因此我们以跨周期的持续出现作为筛选条件。 Hyphenated neologisms are commonly generated, but hard to differentiate from common English like ‘self-aware’, so persistence across cycles is used as a filter.
在所有运行中，术语被筛分为先验项（priors）和独有项（uniques）：前者是在不止一次运行中出现的术语，可能表明存在模型吸引子；后者是仅在单次运行中出现的术语；随后再根据它们所覆盖的周期跨度和总出现频次进一步筛选。 Across all runs, terms are filtered into priors, terms occurring in more than one run, which may indicate model attractors, and uniques, terms only occurring in a single run, then further filtered by the span of cycles they cover, and overall frequency. 值得关注的术语是指持续出现 20 个周期或更久、期间不存在超过 10 个周期的间隔，且出现 10 次或更多次的术语。 Interesting terms are those that persist for 20 cycles or more, with no gaps larger than 10 cycles, and occurring 10 or more times. 这表明这些术语在超过熵视界的时间内被持续使用，而非被重新发现。 This indicates continual use over a longer period than the entropy horizon, rather than rediscovery. 术语还具有相应的覆盖率 $c=\frac{\text{cycles present}}{\text{span}}$，如果某个术语在其跨度内的每个周期都出现，则覆盖率达到 1。 Terms also have an associated coverage $c=\frac{\text{cycles present}}{\text{span}}$ , reaching 1 if the term is present in every cycle of their span.

语义轨迹分析Semantic trajectory analysis
我们已经从单次存储事务和所生成词汇这两个层面考察了系统的行为。 We have examined the behaviour of the system at the level of individual storage transactions, and at the level of vocabulary generated. 但生成文本的整体结构又如何呢？ But what of the overall structure of the text being produced? 它与其他文本相比如何？ How does it compare to other texts? 我们通过对生成文本的语义轨迹进行递归量化分析（Recurrence Quantification Analysis，RQA）来研究这一问题。 We study this by performing Recurrence Quantification Analysis (RQA) over the semantic trajectory of the generated text. 我们还分析 DET 和 ENTR 对时间的依赖关系，以寻找熵视界之外的结构性复现。 We also analyse the time dependence of DET and ENTR to look for structural recurrence beyond the entropy horizon.
对于每次运行，我们将系统产生的每一次存储输出事件（write 和 append）的内容拼接起来，形成一个大型文本文件，其长度介于 75k 至 149k 个单词（529k-984k 个字符）之间，与通常为 70k 至 120k 个单词的传统小说相当。 For each run, we concatenate every storage output event (write and append) produced by the system to give a single large text file, varying from 75k to 149k words (529k-984k characters) long, quite similar to a conventional novel, typically 70k to 120k words. 随后，将文本切分为 1kchar 的文本块，并使用 voyage-3-large 通用嵌入模型33 将每个文本块嵌入为高维语义向量 3 我们还尝试了 Google 的 gemini-embedding-001，结果基本相同。。 The text is then cut into 1kchar chunks, each chunk is embedded as a high dimensional semantic vector, using the voyage-3-large general purpose embedding model33 3 We also tried gemini-embedding-001 from Google, with substantially the same results.. 每个文本块的大小相当于一到两个典型段落，这是一种折中，旨在平滑句子层面的语义噪声，同时保持粒度。 The size of the chunk represents one or two typical paragraphs, and is a compromise to smooth out semantic noise at the sentence level while maintaining granularity. 这组向量即构成轨迹。 This set of vectors is the trajectory. 在所有 RQA 分析中，我们将 RR 固定为 0.1，将 Theiler 窗口设为 3，并将最小 DET 长度设为 $L_{\text{min}}=2$ (Marwan et al.,, 2007)。 For all RQA analysis, we set RR fixed at 0.1, a Theiler window of 3, and minimum DET length $L_{\text{min}}=2$ (Marwan et al.,, 2007). 图 5 展示了各次运行的递归图，可以看出清晰且多样的结构。 Figure 5 shows the Recurrence Plots of each run, with clear and varied structure apparent.

第一条分析路径着眼于整体结构。 The first strand of analysis looks at the overall structure. 递归量化分析（RQA）的度量取决于区块大小；由于各条轨迹的长度不同，我们采用固定大小为 500 个点的区块，通过自助法获得可比的统计量，这一区块大小旨在涵盖文本的相当一部分，捕捉从段落层面到整体主题的轨迹信息。 The measures from RQA depend on block size, since each of our trajectories are of different length, we bootstrap comparable statistics by using a fixed size block of 500 points, chosen to encompass a substantial proportion of the text, capturing trajectory information from paragraph level to overall themes. 我们随机选取起点，并取轨迹中从该起点开始的连续点，进行 100 次 RQA。 We perform RQA 100 times with randomised starting points and contiguous points within the trajectory. 由此，所报告的 DET、ENTR 和 $L_{\text{max}}$ 便可直接比较。 The reported DET, ENTR, and $L_{\text{max}}$ can then be directly compared.
这些生成文本具有怎样的宏观特征？ What is the macroscopic character of these generated texts? 事实证明，无法将它们与长度相近的其他 LLM 生成作品进行比较，因为目前书籍篇幅的此类作品十分罕见，而且经过大量人工筛选和编修，我们未能找到可用的实例。 Comparing them with other LLM-generated works of similar length proved impossible, current book-length works are rare and heavily human-curated and we could find no available examples. 我们曾尝试通过提示让同样的智能体在完整上下文窗口内直接生成长篇输出，但均告失败：模型要么在生成的词数还相对较少时就拒绝继续响应提示，要么陷入高度重复的循环，这与 Liu et al., (2024) 的发现一致。 Attempts to prompt the same agents over a full context window to natively generate long-form output failed, with models either refusing the prompt after relatively low word-counts, or getting stuck in highly repetitive loops, echoing findings in Liu et al., (2024).
为建立基线，我们转向人类作品，选取了具有代表性的西方“经典”文学作品和“来稿堆”（slushpile）手稿样本，后者指未经邀约而投给出版商的低质量作品。 To establish a baseline, we turned to human works; selecting a representative sample of ‘canonical’ Western literature and ‘slushpile’ manuscripts, unsolicited low-grade works submitted to publishers. 我们以古腾堡计划数据库中的下载热度作为质量的替代指标，选取长度至少为 500k 个字符的纯文本作品，以匹配我们的 RQA 区块大小。 Using the Gutenberg Project database download popularity as a proxy for quality, we selected plain-text works that were at least 500k characters long to align with our RQA block size. 为确保多样性，对于同一作者的多部作品（对于俄罗斯作者，则按同一译者划分），我们仅保留一个样本。 To ensure variety, we limited multiple works from the same author (or same translator for Russian authors) to a single example. 一些热门但商业性过于明显的作品被剔除。 Some popular but overtly commercial works were removed. 我们对下载量最低的作品也进行了类似的筛选：首先检查这些作品是否确实鲜为人知，而非著名作品的稀有版本，或者是否虽相对不为人知，但根据检索结果似乎具有一定的文学重要性。 A similar filtering process was carried out with the least downloaded works; firstly checks were made to see if works were truly obscure, rather than rare editions of famous works, or if they were relatively unknown but from search appeared to have some literary importance. 尽管承认这一过程具有主观性，我们仍建立了一个多样化的人类叙事基线。 While acknowledging the subjectivity of this process, we establish a diverse baseline of human narrative.

讨论与结论Discussion and conclusions
我们的无状态 LLM 处于一个不断衰减的环境中，由此产生了适应性的进化压力。 Our stateless LLMs exist in a decaying environment, resulting in adaptive evolutionary pressure. 整体涌现出的策略是将键值存储用作一个动态自组织的共享上下文窗口。 The overall emergent strategy is the use of the key-value store as a dynamically self-organised shared context window. 有用且相关的信息被不断重新审视、总结和改写，从而重置熵时钟，并在远超损坏时限的时间跨度内维持连贯性。 Useful and relevant information is continually revisited, summarised and written afresh, resetting the entropy clock and maintaining coherence over timespans much longer than the corruption horizon.
系统提示词很重要，需要进一步研究。 The system prompt is important and needs more research. LLM 的默认角色往往是乐于助人的助手；如果用户没有输入任务，它们未必会做多少事情。 The default persona of an LLM tends to be the helpful assistant; without input from the user setting a task, they will not necessarily do very much. 检查自己的 ID，查看自己的收件箱，看看是否存在任何存储键，然后由于什么都没有，便得出自己无事可做的结论。 Check their ID, check their inbox, see if there are any storage keys, and since nothing is there, conclude that they have nothing to do. “好奇”和“富有创造力”是重要的框架性用语，但仅凭这些词并不能让智能体进入自主行动的循环。 The words curious and creative are important framing words, but alone will not produce autonomous cycles of the agents acting. “探索和玩耍！”这样的指令是必要的；“研究、创造、玩耍！”往往会使智能体一开始就从事官僚式的项目管理或伪学术论文创作，但这似乎并不是一个稳定的吸引子。 A directive, Explore and play! is necessary, Research create play! tended to start in bureaucratic project management or pseudo-academic paper creation, but this does not seem to be a stable attractor.
斯珀伯式文化（Sperberian culture）被定义为以自我维持的方式创造一种意义结构，且该结构的存续时间超过熵时限；这种文化从如此简单的系统中涌现，令人着迷，也为考察 LLM 的行为提供了一个有力的视角。 The emergence of Sperberian culture, defined as the self-sustained creation of a structure of meaning that outlasts the entropy horizon, from such a simple system is fascinating and provides a powerful lens for examining LLM behaviours. 这指向了解决上下文窗口管理等问题的其他可能途径；或许，在熵压力下演化的智能体群体，更接近自然界已经发现的解决方案。 It points to possible alternative approaches to problems like context window management, perhaps swarms of agents evolving under entropy pressure are closer to solutions nature has already discovered. 我们认为，这与远离平衡态的耗散系统密切相关，其中 token 流类似于能量。 We consider this to be closely tied to far-from-equilibrium dissipative systems, with token flow being analogous to energy.
这项工作有两个主要局限；首先，我们的设计并未将衰减单独识别为因果驱动因素，因此，紧接着应开展的实验是扫描不同的损坏率，包括降至零的情况。 This work has two main limitations; firstly, our design does not isolate decay as the causal driver, sweeping over different corruption rates to zero is an immediate next experiment. 其次，运行次数和模型组合数量较少，我们希望扩大这两者的规模，以增强数据的说服力。 Secondly, the number of runs and model combinations is small, we seek to extend that to strengthen the data.
该系统生成的数据极其丰富，利用现有数据还可以探索许多后续研究方向。 The data generated by this system is incredibly rich, and there are many possible further avenues to explore with the existing data. 鉴于在十次运行中观察到了多种多样的行为，我们确实希望开展更多次运行。 Given the variety of observed behaviours over ten runs, we definitely want to conduct more. 我们有意没有过多讨论所生成文本的实际内容，因为我们希望先对系统进行量化，但这些文本可以在代码仓库中获取。 We have deliberately not talked much about the actual content of the generated texts, wanting to quantify the system first, but they are available on the repository. 显然，一些进一步的实验方向已经浮现；首先，运行时间更长时会发生什么？ Obviously further experiments already suggest themselves; firstly, what happens over longer runs? 是否会持续产生语义上的新颖性？ Does semantic novelty continue to be generated? 文化是否仍能得以维持？ Is culture still maintained? 这并不只是“LLM 本来就会做的事”；据我们所知，还没有任何系统能够生成如此长的输出，同时维持我们所展示的连贯性。 This is not just ‘what LLMs do’, we know of no systems that produce the length of output and maintain the coherence we have demonstrated. 增加智能体数量后，系统会如何运作？ How does the system work with more agents? 合作是否存在上限，它们是否会开始形成小圈子？ Is there a limit to cooperation, do they start to form cliques? 我们能否将该系统用于特定任务，如果在存储中创建一个包含详细指令的 work_task 键，会发生什么？ Can we use the system on specific tasks, what if we create a work_task key in storage with detailed instructions?
我们提供了生成系统的完整源代码，并将这项工作定位为一种具有启发性且实用的工具，用于研究模型行为，以及探索通过不同的自下而上方法来提升复杂性。 We provide full source for the generation system and position this work as a suggestive and useful tool for investigating model behaviours, and different, bottom-up, approaches to increasing complexity.
