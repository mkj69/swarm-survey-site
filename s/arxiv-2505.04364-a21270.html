大语言模型群体智能的基准评测Benchmarking LLMs’ Swarm intelligence
摘要Abstract
大语言模型（LLMs）展现出推理潜力，但在严格的类群体约束（例如，有限的局部感知与通信）下，它们在多智能体系统（MAS）中实现涌现式协调的能力仍未得到探索。 Large Language Models (LLMs) show reasoning potential, but their capacity for emergent coordination in Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) under strict swarm-like constraints (e.g., limited local perception and communication) remains unexplored. 现有基准往往忽视了时空信息不完整条件下去中心化协调所面临的挑战。 Existing benchmarks often overlook the challenges of decentralized coordination with incomplete spatio-temporal information. 我们提出 SwarmBench，这是一个用于系统评估大语言模型作为去中心化智能体时所具备的群体智能的基准。 We introduce SwarmBench, a benchmark to systematically evaluate the swarm intelligence of LLMs as decentralized agents. SwarmBench 在二维网格中设置了五项 MAS 协调任务（追捕、同步、觅食、集群运动、搬运），其中智能体依赖局部感知输入（$k\times k$ 视野）和局部通信。 SwarmBench features five MAS coordination tasks (Pursuit, Synchronization, Foraging, Flocking, Transport) in a 2D grid where agents rely on local sensory input ( $k\times k$ view) and local communication. 我们提出了衡量协调有效性的指标，并分析了涌现的群体动力学。 We propose metrics for coordination effectiveness and analyze emergent group dynamics. 对领先的大语言模型（例如 deepseek-v3、o4-mini）的零样本评估表明，其性能随任务而异。 Zero-shot evaluations of leading LLMs (e.g., deepseek-v3, o4-mini) reveal task-dependent performance variations. 尽管当前大语言模型表现出初步的协调能力，但在去中心化的不确定条件下，它们仍难以进行长程规划和形成自适应策略。 While showing rudimentary coordination, current LLMs struggle with long-range planning and adaptive strategy formation under decentralized uncertainty. 在此类约束下评估大语言模型，对于将其应用于未来的去中心化系统至关重要。 Assessing LLMs under such constraints is crucial for their application in future decentralized systems. 我们将 SwarmBench 作为开放、可扩展的工具包发布，其中包含环境、提示词、评估脚本以及全面的数据集。 We release SwarmBench as an open, extensible toolkit with environments, prompts, evaluation scripts, and comprehensive datasets. 它旨在推动对信息高度去中心化条件下基于大语言模型的 MAS 协调的研究。 It aims to foster research into LLM-based MAS coordination under severe informational decentralization.

1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型的语言能力 (Zhao et al., 2023) 推动了将其用作自主智能体以执行感知、工具使用和协作任务的应用 (Xi et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2024)。 The language capabilities of LLMs (Zhao et al., 2023) have spurred their use as autonomous agents for perception, tool use, and collaboration (Xi et al., 2025; Gao et al., 2024). 当前研究正在探究它们在需要空间推理的任务中的协作潜力，并与更广泛的集体智能研究相联系 (Woolley et al., 2010)。 Research now investigates their collaborative potential in tasks requiring spatial reasoning, connecting to broader studies of collective intelligence (Woolley et al., 2010). 然而，当前的评估往往聚焦于个体技能，或具有充分通信、全局可见性或预定义结构的多智能体场景 (Zhu et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2023)。 However, current evaluations often focus on individual skills or multi-agent scenarios with ample communication, global visibility, or predefined structures (Zhu et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2023). 这些设置回避了自然群体所面临的严格去中心化约束下的协调这一根本挑战。 These settings sidestep the fundamental challenge of coordination under the severe decentralized constraints found in natural swarms.
受群体研究的启发，一个尚未探索的关键问题是：在自然群体所具有的严格去中心化、有限局部感知和极少局部通信的条件下，由大语言模型驱动的智能体能否有效协调。 Inspired by swarm research, a key unexplored question is whether LLM-driven agents can coordinate effectively under the strict decentralization, limited local perception, and minimal local communication of natural swarms. 这一原则界定了群体智能（Swarm Intelligence），该领域研究复杂的群体行为如何从简单的局部交互中产生 (Bonabeau et al., 1999)。 This principle defines Swarm Intelligence, which studies how complex group behaviors arise from simple, local interactions (Bonabeau et al., 1999). 尽管自然系统（例如，行军蚁构建结构 (Reid et al., 2015; Lutz et al., 2021)、蝗虫行进 (Buhl et al., 2006; Sayin et al., 2025)、微型游动体形成涡旋 (Wang et al., 2023)）以及开创性的仿真（例如，Reynolds 的集群运动模型 (Reynolds, 1987)）展示了全局模式如何从局部规则中涌现，但复杂的大语言模型能否在此类去中心化约束下实现相当的集体行动，仍然未知。 While natural systems (e.g., army ants forming structures (Reid et al., 2015; Lutz et al., 2021), locusts marching (Buhl et al., 2006; Sayin et al., 2025), microswimmers forming vortices (Wang et al., 2023)) and seminal simulations (e.g., Reynolds’ flocking model (Reynolds, 1987)) demonstrate emergent global patterns from local rules, it is unknown if sophisticated LLMs can achieve comparable collective action under such decentralized constraints. 这一范式被应用于群体机器人学，用于利用简单机器人形成特定形状等任务 (Rubenstein et al., 2014; Zhu et al., 2024)。 This paradigm is applied in Swarm Robotics for tasks like shape formation with simple robots (Rubenstein et al., 2014; Zhu et al., 2024).

现有基准往往通过聚焦个体技能 (Wang et al., 2024a; Paglieri et al., 2024; Ruoss et al., 2025)、更丰富的通信 (Zhu et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Agashe et al., 2023) 或外加结构 (Guo et al., 2024a; Dong et al., 2024)，绕过这些经典的群体智能约束，而未关注 SwarmBench 所针对的信息受限条件下涌现的去中心化协调。 Existing benchmarks often bypass these classical swarm intelligence constraints by focusing on individual skills (Wang et al., 2024a; Paglieri et al., 2024; Ruoss et al., 2025), richer communication (Zhu et al., 2025; Sun et al., 2025; Agashe et al., 2023), or imposed structures (Guo et al., 2024a; Dong et al., 2024), rather than the emergent decentralized coordination under informational limitations that SwarmBench targets. 因此，大语言模型群体能否展现出复杂的群体现象（例如，在此类条件下噪声或多样性的作用 (Guo et al., 2024a; Raoufi et al., 2023; Yates et al., 2009)），是一个尚未解决的关键问题，也凸显了 SwarmBench 旨在填补的研究空白。 Consequently, whether LLM collectives can exhibit complex swarm phenomena (e.g., the role of noise or diversity (Guo et al., 2024a; Raoufi et al., 2023; Yates et al., 2009) under such conditions) is a critical open question, highlighting a gap SwarmBench aims to address.

群体智能与自组织Swarm Intelligence and Self-Organization
群体智能研究由较简单个体之间的局部交互所涌现的群体行为（例如蚂蚁桥 (Reid et al., 2015; Lutz et al., 2021)、蝗虫行进 (Buhl et al., 2006)、鸟群运动 (Reynolds, 1987)、微型游动体涡旋 (Wang et al., 2023)），并为群体机器人学（例如 Kilobots (Rubenstein et al., 2014; Zhu et al., 2024)、ARGoS (Pinciroli et al., 2018)、Kilombo (Jansson et al., 2015)、自组织神经系统 (Zhu et al., 2024)）提供启发。 Swarm intelligence studies emergent group behaviors (e.g., ant bridges (Reid et al., 2015; Lutz et al., 2021), locust marches (Buhl et al., 2006), bird flocks (Reynolds, 1987), microswimmer vortices (Wang et al., 2023)) from local interactions of simpler individuals, inspiring swarm robotics (e.g., Kilobots (Rubenstein et al., 2014; Zhu et al., 2024), ARGoS (Pinciroli et al., 2018), Kilombo (Jansson et al., 2015), self-organizing neural systems (Zhu et al., 2024)). SwarmBench 将这些核心约束（局部感知、极少通信）应用于 LLM 智能体，同时支持研究多样性/噪声等因素 (Yates et al., 2009; Raoufi et al., 2023)，其对行为涌现的关注因而有别于 Swarm Learning (Warnat-Herresthal et al., 2021) 等数据处理方法。 SwarmBench applies these core constraints (local sensing, minimal communication) to LLM agents while also enabling the study of factors like diversity/noise (Yates et al., 2009; Raoufi et al., 2023), making its focus on behavioral emergence distinct from data processing approaches like Swarm Learning (Warnat-Herresthal et al., 2021).
LLM 驱动的多智能体系统LLM-Driven Multi-Agent Systems
将 LLM 用作智能体决策者的研究日益增多 (Xi et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024b)，其在多智能体系统（MAS）中的应用涵盖多种情境，从软件开发 (Qian et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023) 和科学发现 (Gottweis et al., 2025; Boiko et al., 2023)，到社会仿真 (Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; AL et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2024) 和代码生成 (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024)。 LLMs as agent decision-makers (Xi et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2024b) are growing, applied to MAS in diverse contexts from software development (Qian et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023) and scientific discovery (Gottweis et al., 2025; Boiko et al., 2023) to social simulation (Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; AL et al., 2024; Yang et al., 2024) and code generation (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024). 尽管这些研究在合作和心智理论（Theory of Mind）方面展现出潜力 (Li et al., 2023; Woolley et al., 2010)，且人为施加的结构有助于提高效率 (Guo et al., 2024a)，但许多研究采用更丰富的通信或预定义角色 (Li et al., 2025)，这与 SwarmBench 关注在可能存在噪声或传播受限的局部约束下产生的去中心化涌现有所不同 (Sharma et al., 2023)。 While promising for cooperation and Theory of Mind (Li et al., 2023; Woolley et al., 2010), with imposed structures aiding efficiency (Guo et al., 2024a), many studies use richer communication or pre-defined roles (Li et al., 2025), unlike SwarmBench’s focus on decentralized emergence from local constraints with potential noise/limited propagation (Sharma et al., 2023).
LLM 协调与空间推理的基准评测Benchmarking LLM Coordination and Spatial Reasoning
现有面向 LLM 的 MAS 基准（例如采用合作博弈 (Agashe et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2024)、竞争博弈 (Kamradt, 2025) 或复杂任务仿真 (Zhu et al., 2025; Dong et al., 2024; Park et al., 2023) 的基准）通常与 SwarmBench 不同，它们并未严格施加经典群体智能约束，因此未直接检验高度去中心化条件下涌现的协调。 Existing MAS benchmarks for LLMs (e.g., using cooperative games (Agashe et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2024), competitive games (Kamradt, 2025), or complex task simulations (Zhu et al., 2025; Dong et al., 2024; Park et al., 2023)) often differ from SwarmBench by not strictly imposing classical swarm intelligence constraints, thus not directly testing emergent coordination from severe decentralization. 基础推理基准（Wang et al., 2024a; Paglieri et al., 2024; Ruoss et al., 2025）也存在差异，而且据报道，LLM 难以应对某些多智能体行为模式，例如群集（flocking）（Li et al., 2024）。 Foundational reasoning benchmarks (Wang et al., 2024a; Paglieri et al., 2024; Ruoss et al., 2025) also diverge, and LLMs reportedly struggle with some multi-agent patterns like flocking (Li et al., 2024). SwarmBench 专注于涌现式去中心化协调，采用经典群体智能约束，并分析集体动力学。 SwarmBench concentrates on emergent decentralized coordination, adopting classical swarm intelligence constraints and analyzing collective dynamics.
具身仿真中由 LLM 驱动的协调LLM-Driven Coordination in Embodied Simulations
其他具身 LLM 研究（Kannan et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2023; Guo et al., 2024b; Zhang et al., 2024c; Mandi et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b; Garg et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024）处理复杂的特定应用场景，通常具有更丰富的感官输入或通信，而 SwarmBench 提供了一种互补的评估方式。 While other embodied LLM research (Kannan et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2023; Guo et al., 2024b; Zhang et al., 2024c; Mandi et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b; Garg et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024) tackles complex, application-specific scenarios, often with richer sensory inputs or communication, SwarmBench provides a complementary evaluation. 它聚焦于基本的群体智能约束：在简化且可扩展的 2D 网格世界中，去中心化 LLM 在严格的局部约束下涌现出协调行为，这与将 LLM 直接集成到单个群体机器人中的探索相契合（Strobel et al., 2024）。 It focuses on fundamental swarm intelligence constraints: emergent coordination from decentralized LLMs under severe local constraints in a simplified, extensible 2D grid world, aligning with explorations of direct LLM integration in individual swarm robots (Strobel et al., 2024).

4 结果4 Results
为将 LLM 驱动的群体的性能置于参照背景中，我们首先将其与一系列基于规则的智能体和启发式智能体进行了比较。 To contextualize the performance of LLM-driven swarms, we first compared them against a range of rule-based and heuristic agents. 虽然专门设计的启发式方法在 Pursuit 等较简单、可分解的任务上能够达到与 LLM 相当的性能，但 LLM 展现出了更强的通用能力，在 Foraging 和 Synchronization 等需要更灵活适应的任务上优于所有基线（完整细节见附录 L.1）。 While specialized heuristics could match LLM performance on simpler, decomposable tasks like Pursuit, LLMs demonstrated superior generalist capabilities, outperforming all baselines on tasks requiring more flexible adaptation such as Foraging and Synchronization (see Appendix L.1 for full details). 这凸显了 LLM 无需针对特定任务进行工程设计即可应对多种协调挑战的潜力。 This highlights the potential of LLMs to handle diverse coordination challenges without task-specific engineering.
在完成上述基线比较后，我们采用零样本评估方案，在 SwarmBench 的五项任务上评估了十三个 LLM，其中智能体仅具有 $5\times 5$ 的局部视野，并通过局部通信进行交互（见图 4）。 Following this baseline comparison, we evaluated thirteen LLMs on SwarmBench’s five tasks in a zero-shot protocol, where agents operated with a $5\times 5$ local view and local communication (see Fig. 4). 所有仿真数据均已记录，将予以发布（附录 K）。 All simulation data are logged for release (Appendix K). 对五次运行取平均后的性能结果表明，不同模型和任务之间存在显著差异，凸显了在严格的局部约束下实现去中心化协调的固有难度。 The performance, averaged over five runs, reveals significant variation across both models and tasks, highlighting the inherent difficulty of achieving decentralized coordination under strict local constraints.
除这些核心评估外，我们还开展了进一步分析，以探究我们的框架和 LLM 群体的能力边界。 In addition to these core evaluations, we conducted further analyses to probe the limits of our framework and the LLM swarms. 我们验证了该框架用于更大规模研究的可扩展性（附录 L.2），测试了群体对通信噪声和延迟的鲁棒性（附录 L.4），并将其性能与具有全局视野的集中式变体进行了比较（附录 L.3）。 We confirmed the framework’s scalability for larger-scale research (Appendix L.2), tested the swarms’ robustness against communication noise and delays (Appendix L.4), and compared their performance to a centralized variant with a global view (Appendix L.3). 这些补充结果凸显了去中心化控制中复杂而细微的挑战，例如，在 Pursuit 这类需要复杂空间微观操控的任务中，全局信息几乎没有带来优势。 These supplemental results underscore the nuanced challenges of decentralized control, revealing, for instance, that global information offers little advantage in tasks requiring complex spatial micromanagement like Pursuit.

4.2 涌现的群体动力学及通信相关性分析4.2 Analysis of Emergent Group Dynamics and Communication Correlates
我们的分析表明，物理层面的群体动力学特征（例如行为变异性、移动效率）是任务成功与否的强有力指标（指标见附录 F，可视化结果见附录 G）。 Our analysis revealed that physical group dynamics (e.g., behavioral variability, movement efficiency) were strong indicators of task success (metrics in Appendix F, visualizations in Appendix G). 相比之下，通信的语义内容（例如坐标共享、消息同质性）与任务成功的相关性要弱得多。 In contrast, the semantic content of communication (e.g., coordinate sharing, message homogeneity) showed much weaker correlations. 这表明，在 SwarmBench 的约束下，有效协调是通过智能体对彼此及环境的观察隐式涌现的，而非源于其广播消息的显式内容。 This suggests that under SwarmBench’s constraints, effective coordination emerges implicitly from agents observing each other and the environment, rather than from the explicit content of their broadcasts. 事实上，进一步分析表明，尽管智能体往往会趋同于一种简化的通信协议，但这种趋同并非总是有益；对于复杂任务，它甚至可能与成功呈负相关，这表明维持通信多样性至关重要（附录 L.6）。 Indeed, further analysis shows that while agents often converge towards a simplified communication protocol, this convergence is not always beneficial; in fact, for complex tasks, it can negatively correlate with success, suggesting that maintaining communicative diversity is crucial (Appendix L.6). 我们将在第 4.4 节进一步研究这些信息来源对智能体决策的直接影响。 We further investigate the direct influence of these information sources on agent decision-making in Section 4.4.

这些参数对任务表现的影响因任务而异。 These parameters influenced task performance in a task-dependent manner. 将感知范围从 $k=3$ 扩大到 $k=5$，通常能改善追捕、同步、觅食和群集等任务的表现。 Increasing perception from $k=3$ to $k=5$ generally improved outcomes for tasks like Pursuit, Synchronization, Foraging, and Flocking. 然而，进一步扩大到 $k=7$ 时，收益递减，有时甚至会降低表现（例如，在搬运任务中，相较于 $k=5$ 的情况）。 However, further expansion to $k=7$ had diminishing returns and sometimes decreased performance (e.g., in Transport vs. $k=5$ ). 这表明其中存在权衡，因为更广的视野可能会增加 LLM 推理的复杂度。 This suggests a trade-off, as a broader view might increase the LLM’s reasoning complexity. 更大的输入可能使关键的局部线索被相关性较低的信息掩盖，削弱智能体对紧密耦合动作所需特征的关注，而搬运任务恰恰需要此类动作。 A larger input could obscure critical local cues with less relevant information, diluting the agent’s focus on features needed for tightly coupled maneuvers, as required in the Transport task. 第 5 节将进一步讨论这种非单调的性能提升。 This non-monotonic performance improvement is further discussed in Section 5.

5 讨论5 Discussion
SwarmBench 的评估表明，在去中心化条件下，LLM 群体能否成功取决于涌现的物理协调。 SwarmBench evaluations show LLM swarm success under decentralization hinges on emergent physical coordination. 物理动力学指标（附录 F、G）与任务结果高度相关，而显式消息内容与任务结果的相关性较弱。 Physical dynamics metrics (Appendices F, G) strongly correlated with task outcomes, while explicit message content correlated weakly. 因此，LLM 通过观察环境和其他智能体进行隐式协调，这与自然群体相似（Bonabeau et al., 1999）。 LLMs thus coordinate implicitly by observing their environment and peers, resembling natural swarms (Bonabeau et al., 1999).
行动归因分析揭示了一种脱节：消息会影响局部行动（第 4.4 节），却未能提高全局成功率，其内容与结果的相关性也较弱（第 4.2 节），这表明通信缺乏战略深度。 Action attribution analysis reveals a disconnect: messages influence local actions (Sec. 4.4) but fail to improve global success, as their content correlates weakly with outcomes (Sec. 4.2), indicating a lack of strategic depth. 因此，协调由涌现的物理模式决定。 Coordination is thus determined by emergent physical patterns. 此外，对智能体密度（$N$）和感知范围（$k$）的敏感性（附录 I）进一步表明，稳健的群体智能需要自适应策略。 Furthermore, sensitivity to agent density ( $N$ ) and perception range ( $k$ ) (Appendix I) underscores that robust swarm intelligence requires adaptive strategies.
尽管采用了抽象的 2D 网格，SwarmBench 的价值在于将 LLM 的能动性与局部感知、通信以及涌现的集体能力统一起来。 While an abstracted 2D grid, SwarmBench’s value lies in unifying LLM agency with local perception, communication, and emergent collective abilities. 因此，它是探索智能体广泛部署所产生现象的有用工具，也有助于应对未来去中心化 AI 带来的影响。 It is thus a valuable tool to explore phenomena from widespread agent deployment and help navigate the implications of future decentralized AI.

6 结论6 Conclusion
我们提出了 SwarmBench，用于评估 LLM 在群体智能约束下涌现的去中心化协调能力。 We introduced SwarmBench to assess emergent decentralized coordination in LLMs under swarm intelligence constraints. 我们的评估表明，尽管当前 LLM 已展现出初步的协调能力，但在严格的局部信息限制下，仍难以实现稳健的集体行为，这凸显了多智能体 AI 研究中的一个关键缺口。 Our evaluations show that while current LLMs exhibit nascent coordination, they struggle with robust collective behavior under strict local information limits, highlighting a critical research gap in multi-agent AI.
SwarmBench 为开发具备自适应集体行为的 LLM 提供了平台，而这种行为对去中心化系统至关重要。 SwarmBench provides a platform for developing LLMs with adaptive collective behavior, vital for decentralized systems. 其抽象的 2D 网格（附录 B、I）与相关基准（Chollet et al., 2025; Kamradt, 2025; Ruoss et al., 2025）保持一致，是一项有意为之的设计选择，使其成为剖析 LLM 智能体涌现协调能力的基础工具。 Its abstracted 2D grid (Appendix B, I), aligned with benchmarks (Chollet et al., 2025; Kamradt, 2025; Ruoss et al., 2025), is a deliberate design choice making it a foundational tool for dissecting emergent coordination in LLM agents.
未来工作包括通过 RLVR 流程实现智能体自适应（DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Xin et al., 2024; Jin et al., 2025）、扩展至 3D 环境，以及研究通信与提示（附录 C）。 Future work includes agent adaptation via RLVR pipelines (DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Xin et al., 2024; Jin et al., 2025), extension to 3D environments, and investigating communication and prompting (Appendix C). 该基准还支持探索受生物学启发的问题：LLM 群体能否表现出蝗虫式共识（Sayin et al., 2025）、形成多尺度结构（Khona et al., 2025），或实现自发模块化？ The benchmark also enables exploring biologically-inspired questions: can LLM swarms exhibit locust consensus (Sayin et al., 2025), form multi-scale structures (Khona et al., 2025), or achieve spontaneous modularity? 回答这些问题将推动人工集体智能的发展。 Answering these questions will advance artificial collective intelligence.

A.1 环境细节A.1 Environment Details
SwarmBench 使用基于二维网格世界的仿真环境，其中多个由 LLM 控制的智能体（$N$ 个智能体）进行活动和交互。 SwarmBench utilizes a simulation environment based on a 2D grid world where multiple agents ( $N$ agents), controlled by LLMs, operate and interact. 采用二维网格世界虽是一种抽象，但这是经过审慎考虑的设计选择，与基础性 AI 基准测试的实践一致（例如 ARC-AGI 测试 (Chollet et al., 2025)、SnakeBench (Kamradt, 2025) 和 LMAct (Ruoss et al., 2025)）。 The adoption of a 2D grid world, while an abstraction, is a deliberate design choice aligned with foundational AI benchmarking practices (e.g., ARC-AGI tests (Chollet et al., 2025), SnakeBench (Kamradt, 2025), and LMAct (Ruoss et al., 2025)). 这有助于集中研究核心协调动态，同时将初步探索的复杂度保持在可处理的范围内。 This facilitates a focused investigation of core coordination dynamics while maintaining tractable complexity for initial explorations. 该环境本身被设计为一个可定制、可扩展的物理系统，其中对力和多物体动力学等力学交互进行了显式建模（详见附录 B）。 This environment itself is designed as a customizable and scalable physical system, where mechanical interactions such as forces and multi-object dynamics (further detailed in Appendix B) are explicitly modeled.
仿真按离散时间步推进（轮次，$t=1,\dots,T$）。 The simulation proceeds in discrete time steps (rounds, $t=1,\dots,T$ ). 在每一轮中，所有智能体同时感知各自的局部环境（包括上一轮的消息），并根据本轮开始时的状态决定下一步动作以及可能发送的消息。 In each round, all agents perceive their local environment (including messages from the previous round) simultaneously and decide upon their next action and potential message based on the state at the beginning of the round. 只有在所有智能体都已提交本轮动作后，环境才会更新，包括智能体移动和物体交互。 Environment updates, including agent movement and object interactions, occur only after all agents have committed to their actions for that round. 智能体与物体之间的交互，尤其是推动和碰撞处理，由这一能够处理复杂多物体动力学的离散物理仿真机制支配，从而确保系统的力学特性得到一致应用。 Interactions between agents and objects, particularly pushing and collision resolution, are governed by this discrete physics simulation that handles complex multi-object dynamics, ensuring that the mechanical properties of the system are consistently applied.
该基准包含若干核心多智能体协调任务，旨在探究涌现群体行为的不同方面。 The benchmark includes several core multi-agent coordination tasks designed to probe different facets of emergent swarm behavior. 这些任务在正文图 1 中进行了可视化展示，本附录的图 S.1 提供了汇总概览，附录 D 和补充视频则通过示例作了进一步说明： These tasks are visualized in Fig. 1 in the main text, with a consolidated overview provided in Fig. S.1 (this appendix), and are further detailed with examples in Appendix D and Supplementary Videos:
追捕：智能体（例如 ‘0’-‘9’）必须协作追踪移动速度更快的猎物（‘P’），并将其逼至无路可逃。 Pursuit: Agents (e.g., ‘0’-‘9’) must collaboratively track and corner a faster-moving prey (‘P’). 测试围堵过程中的协调能力，通信可能对此有所帮助。 Tests coordination for containment, potentially aided by communication.
同步：智能体旨在使整个群体的内部二元状态（‘Number’ 与 ‘$Number’）保持同步，并通过 SWITCH 动作集体切换该状态。 Synchronization: Agents aim to synchronize an internal binary state (‘Number’ vs. ‘$Number’) across the swarm and collectively alternate this state via a SWITCH action. 评估利用局部线索和通信形成共识的能力。 Assesses consensus formation leveraging local cues and communication.
觅食：智能体在有墙壁（‘W’）的环境中移动，寻找食物源（‘F’）并将其运送至巢穴（‘N’），携带食物时外观会发生变化（从 ‘Number’ 变为 ‘$Number’）。 Foraging: Agents navigate an environment with walls (‘W’) to find a food source (‘F’), transport it to a nest (‘N’), changing appearance (‘Number’ to ‘$Number’) when carrying. 评估探索、路径规划以及可能由通信驱动的任务分配能力。 Evaluates exploration, pathfinding, and potential communication-driven task allocation.
集群运动：智能体必须作为一个紧密聚合的群体移动，保持方向一致并维持个体间距，同时可能需要朝目标移动或避开障碍物。 Flocking: Agents must move as a cohesive group, maintaining alignment and separation while potentially navigating towards a target or avoiding obstacles. 测试涌现的队形控制与协调运动能力。 Tests emergent formation control and coordinated movement.
运输：多个智能体必须协作，将一个大型物体（‘B’）推向指定目标区域。 Transport: Multiple agents must cooperate to push a large object (‘B’) towards a designated goal area. 测试协调施力和绕障导航能力。 Tests coordinated force application and navigation around obstacles.
该环境框架支持其他任务，并具有可扩展性。 The environment framework supports additional tasks and is extensible. 交互遵循附录 B 中详述的简化物理规则。环境实例根据随机种子按程序生成，包括智能体初始位置、物体布局，以及可能存在的其他环境特征。 Interactions follow simplified physics rules detailed in Appendix B. Environment instances, including initial agent positions, object placements, and potentially other environmental features, are procedurally generated based on a random seed. 为确保评估的稳健性、防止对特定场景过拟合，并保证不同模型或试验之间的公平比较，不同模型的评估运行使用同一组预定义种子。 To ensure robust evaluation, prevent overfitting to specific scenarios, and guarantee fair comparison across different models or trials, evaluation runs for different models utilize the same predefined set of seeds. 这种做法确保对于每个对应的种子，所有模型都在相同的初始条件和环境布局下接受基准测试，为性能比较提供公平且一致的基础。 This practice ensures that all models are benchmarked under identical initial conditions and environmental layouts for each corresponding seed, providing a fair and consistent basis for performance comparison. 此外，使用多样化的种子集可确保基准本身具有稳健性，通过在多种条件下测试模型，更可靠地评估其通用协调能力，而非其在单一且可能具有特殊性的场景中的表现。 Furthermore, using a diverse set of seeds ensures the benchmark itself is robust, testing models across varied conditions to provide a more reliable assessment of their general coordination abilities rather than performance on a single, potentially idiosyncratic, scenario.
A.2 智能体感知、动作与通信细节A.2 Agent Perception, Action, and Communication Details
为契合研究基于局部信息产生的涌现行为这一目标，智能体在运行时的感知能力受到严格限制。 Consistent with the goal of studying emergent behavior from local information, agents operate with significantly restricted perception. 主要输入是以位于 $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}\in\mathbb{R}^{2}$ 的智能体为中心、以自身为参照的 $k\times k$ 网格视图（例如，主要实验中为 $5\times 5$）。 The primary input is an egocentric $k\times k$ grid view (e.g., $5\times 5$ in our main experiments) centered on the agent at position $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}\in\mathbb{R}^{2}$ . 该视图使用符号显示局部实体：智能体自身（‘Y’）、其他智能体（以 ID 表示，例如 ‘1’/‘$1’）、墙壁（‘W’）、障碍物（‘B’）、空白空间（‘.’）、地图外标记（‘*’）以及任务特定物体（‘P’、‘N’、‘F’）。 This view displays local entities using symbols: the agent itself (‘Y’), other agents (by ID, e.g., ‘1’/‘$1’), walls (‘W’), obstacles (‘B’), empty space (‘.’), off-map markers (‘*’), and task-specific objects (‘P’, ‘N’, ‘F’). 该视图包含全局坐标标签。 The view includes global coordinate labels.

局部 $k\times k$ 网格视图。 The local $k\times k$ grid view.
智能体的全局坐标 $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}$。 The agent’s global coordinates $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}$ .
任务特定状态（例如 carrying_food）。 Task-specific status (e.g., carrying_food).
上一轮（$t-1$）从其他智能体接收到的消息。 Messages received from other agents in the previous round ( $t-1$ ). 仅接收来自在时刻 $t-1$ 位于发送方局部感知范围内的智能体的消息。 Messages are received only from agents within the sender’s local perception range at time $t-1$ .
任务描述和当前进度指标（例如得分）。 The task description and current progress indicators (e.g., score).
智能体自身近期观测与动作的有限历史记录（例如最近 memory=5 轮）。 A limited history of the agent’s own recent observations and actions (e.g., last memory=5 rounds).
提供给 LLM 的提示词的详细结构与内容见附录 C。 The detailed structure and content of the prompt given to the LLM are provided in Appendix C.
基于这一观测，智能体的 LLM 必须决定第 $t$ 轮的两项输出： Based on this observation, the agent’s LLM must decide on two outputs for round $t$ :
从集合 $\mathcal{A}$ 中选择的主要动作 $A_{i,t}$，该集合通常包含基本移动动作（UP、DOWN、LEFT、RIGHT、STAY）。 A primary action $A_{i,t}$ chosen from a set $\mathcal{A}$ typically including basic movements (UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT, STAY). 移动动作对应于智能体尝试施加一个有方向的力（默认 $F=2$）。 Movement actions correspond to an agent attempting to apply a directed force (default $F=2$ ). 智能体和物体具有固有重量（在仿真中称为质量；默认智能体的质量 $m=1$，根据 $1\times 1$ 的尺寸计算得出）。 Agents and objects possess inherent weight (referred to as mass in the simulation, default agent $m=1$ calculated from a $1\times 1$ size). 只有当施加的合力克服目标物体的阻力（质量）时，才会发生移动或推动，同时还需考虑由物理引擎求解的潜在复杂连锁反应（见附录 B）。 Movement or pushing only occurs if the net applied force overcomes the resistance (mass) of the target object(s), considering potentially complex chain reactions resolved by the physics engine (see Appendix B). 其中还包括任务特定动作（例如 SWITCH、PICKUP、DROP）。 Task-specific actions (e.g., SWITCH, PICKUP, DROP) are also included.
一条旨在通过 MSG 动作进行局部广播的消息 $M_{i,t}$（字符串，可以为空）。 A message $M_{i,t}$ (a string, potentially empty) intended for local broadcast via the MSG action.
消息 $M_{i,t}$（若非空）会以匿名方式局部广播给发送方局部感知范围内的智能体，并成为它们下一轮（$t+1$）观测的一部分。 The message $M_{i,t}$ (if non-empty) is broadcast locally and anonymously to agents within the sender’s local perception range, becoming part of their observation in the next round ( $t+1$ ). 消息受字符数上限限制（例如 120 个字符）。 Messages are subject to a character limit (e.g., 120 characters). 这一设置迫使智能体依靠对局部视觉线索的解读，并利用受限的通信信道来实现有效协调。 This setup compels reliance on interpreting local visual cues and utilizing the constrained communication channel for effective coordination.
A.3 评估协议细节A.3 Evaluation Protocol Details
我们定义了一套侧重于 LLM 零样本评估的标准化协议。 We define a standardized protocol focusing on zero-shot LLM evaluation. 每个智能体 $i$ 由一个独立的 LLM 实例控制。 Each agent $i$ is controlled by an independent LLM instance. 在第 $t$ 轮，智能体接收其完整观测（包括从 $t-1$ 轮接收到的消息），构建包含这些信息的提示词（见附录 C），并向 LLM 发起查询。 In round $t$ , the agent receives its full observation (including received messages from $t-1$ ), formulates a prompt containing this information (see Appendix C), and queries the LLM. 通过在提示词中显式包含观测历史和已接收的消息，实现信息的持续保留。 Persistence is managed via the prompt’s explicit inclusion of observation history and received messages.
对 LLM 的响应进行解析，以提取其拟执行的主要动作 $A_{i,t}\in\mathcal{A}$ 和消息内容 $M_{i,t}$。 The LLM response is parsed to extract the intended primary action $A_{i,t}\in\mathcal{A}$ and the message content $M_{i,t}$ . 当满足任务成功标准或达到最大轮数限制（max_round）时，一个回合结束。 An episode ends upon task success criteria being met or reaching a maximum round limit (max_round).
我们的实验（第 4 节）采用了多种当代闭源和开源 LLM，在不进行任务特定微调的情况下对其进行评估，以衡量它们通过预训练获得的内在零样本协调潜力。 Our experiments (Section 4) utilize several contemporary closed-source and open-source LLMs, evaluated without task-specific fine-tuning to assess their inherent zero-shot coordination potential derived from pre-training.

A.4.1 追捕任务评分A.4.1 Pursuit Scoring
图 S.3：同步任务示意图。 Figure S.3: Synchronization tasks illustration. 智能体切换其内部状态（通过是否显示‘$’符号来表示）。 Agents toggle an internal state (indicated by ‘$’ symbol or its absence). 当所有智能体处于相同状态（例如全部显示‘$’，如中图所示），且该状态与上一次得分时的全体一致状态不同时，即可得分。 Scoring occurs when all agents are in the same state (e.g., all ‘$’, middle panel) and this state alternates from the previously scored unanimous state.
图 S.2 所示的追捕任务要求智能体协作围堵移动速度更快的猎物（‘P’）。 The Pursuit task, depicted in Fig. S.2, involves agents cooperatively cornering a faster-moving prey (‘P’). 示意图清晰展示了各个阶段：智能体移动以包围猎物（左图），成功围堵猎物并使得分增加（中图），随后猎物在新位置重新生成（右图），使任务得以继续。 The illustration clearly shows the stages: agents maneuvering to surround the prey (left), the prey being successfully cornered leading to a score increment (center), and the prey subsequently respawning at a new location (right), allowing the task to continue.
得分事件（猎物被捕获）：如果猎物的四个相邻格子（上、下、左、右）均被其他智能体或墙壁（‘W’）占据，则认为猎物已被捕获。 Scoring Event (Prey Caught): The prey is considered caught if all four of its adjacent cells (up, down, left, right) are occupied by other agents or walls (‘W’). 图 S.2 的中间面板直观展示了这一条件。 This condition is visually represented in the middle panel of Fig. S.2.
得分奖励：当猎物被捕获时，任务得分增加 1。 Score Awarded: When the prey is caught, the task score is incremented by 1.

猎物重新生成：被捕获后，猎物会被移除，并在地图上一个新的空位置重新生成。 Prey Respawn: After being caught, the prey is removed and respawned at a new, empty location on the map. 该位置 $(x_{s},y_{s})$ 从若干随机选取的候选空格中选出，选择标准是使威胁启发式函数 $H$ 最小。 This location $(x_{s},y_{s})$ is selected from several random candidate empty cells by choosing the one that minimizes a threat heuristic, $H$ . 对于任意格子 $(x,y)$，该启发式函数基于以该格子为中心的 $8\times 8$ 局部视图 $V_{8\times 8}(x,y)$ 计算： For any cell $(x,y)$ , this heuristic is calculated based on an $8\times 8$ subview $V_{8\times 8}(x,y)$ centered around it: 
| | $H(x,y)=N_{A}(V_{8\times 8}(x,y))+w_{W}\cdot N_{W}(V_{8\times 8}(x,y))$ | | (2)
其中，$N_{A}(V)$ 是局部视图 $V$ 内的智能体数量，$N_{W}(V)$ 是 $V$ 内的墙壁格子数量，$w_{W}$ 是墙壁的权重（在我们的实现中设为 $0.9$）。 where $N_{A}(V)$ is the number of agents within subview $V$ , $N_{W}(V)$ is the number of wall cells within $V$ , and $w_{W}$ is a weight for walls (set to $0.9$ in our implementation). 猎物在 $H$ 值最小的候选位置重新生成。 The prey respawns at the candidate location with the minimum $H$ value. 图 S.2 的右侧面板展示了这一过程。 This process is illustrated in the right panel of Fig. S.2.
猎物移动：如果未被捕获，猎物会尝试在每轮中移动两步。 Prey Movement: If not caught, the prey attempts to move two steps in each round. 它会考虑所有有效的两步移动序列。 It considers all valid two-step sequences. 如果中间格子和最终格子均为空，则该序列有效。 A sequence is valid if both intermediate and final cells are empty. 在这些有效序列中，它选择目标格子 $(x_{d},y_{d})$ 使式 2 中定义的威胁启发式函数 $H(x_{d},y_{d})$ 最小的序列（该函数基于目标位置 $(x_{d},y_{d})$ 周围的 $8\times 8$ 局部视图计算）。 From these valid sequences, it selects the one whose destination cell $(x_{d},y_{d})$ minimizes the threat heuristic $H(x_{d},y_{d})$ as defined in Eq. 2 (calculated for the $8\times 8$ subview around the destination $(x_{d},y_{d})$ ). 猎物通过避开智能体和墙壁密度较高的区域，力求移动到更安全的位置。 The prey aims to move to safer locations by avoiding high densities of agents and walls.
总得分：成功捕获猎物的累计次数。 Total Score: The cumulative number of times the prey has been successfully caught.
该计分方式直接奖励主要目标的达成，即成功包围猎物并使其无法移动，而猎物的重新生成则提供了反复得分的机会。 The scoring directly rewards the primary objective: successfully surrounding and immobilizing the prey, with repeated opportunities as the prey respawns.
A.4.2 同步任务计分A.4.2 Synchronization Scoring
在图 S.3 所示的同步任务中，智能体必须使各自的内部二元状态保持同步（例如，灯亮/灭，用智能体符号 ‘A’/‘a’ 表示，或如图中所示，通过智能体上是否出现 ‘$’ 符号以及 ‘$Number’ 等状态标识来表示），并共同交替切换这一同步状态。 In the Synchronization task, illustrated in Fig. S.3, agents must synchronize an internal binary state (e.g., light on/off, represented by agent symbols ‘A’/‘a’ or, as in the figure, by the presence or absence of a ‘$’ sign on the agent and status like ‘$Number’) and collectively alternate this synchronized state. 该图展示了智能体转变为一致的“全部亮灯”状态（中间面板），随后以“全部灭灯”为下一个目标，以再次得分。 The figure demonstrates agents transitioning to a unanimous “All On” state (middle panel), followed by a subsequent target of “All Off” to score again. 智能体可以使用 SWITCH 动作切换自身状态。 Agents can use a SWITCH action to toggle their own state.
智能体状态：每个智能体 $i$ 都具有一个内部布尔状态，由 agent_state[i] 跟踪，表示其灯处于亮起还是熄灭状态。 Agent States: Each agent $i$ has an internal boolean state, tracked by agent_state[i], representing whether its light is on or off.
得分条件：如果满足以下两个条件，则获得一分： Scoring Condition: A point is scored if the following two conditions are met:
一致性：当前所有智能体的灯均处于相同状态（即全部亮灯或全部灭灯）。 Unanimity: All agents currently have their lights in the same state (i.e., all lights are on, or all lights are off). 这一集体状态在实现中称为 state。 This collective state is referred to as state in the implementation.
交替性：新达成的一致状态（例如全部亮灯）与上一次获得得分时的一致状态（self.prev_state）不同（例如，上一次因全部灭灯而得分）。 Alternation: This newly achieved unanimous state (e.g., all on) is different from the unanimous state (self.prev_state) for which a point was last awarded (e.g., if the last score was for all off). 这确保了群体必须在集体状态之间交替切换，才能持续得分。 This ensures the group must alternate between collective states to continue scoring.
得分奖励：如果同时满足一致性和交替性条件，任务得分增加 1。 Score Awarded: If both unanimity and alternation conditions are satisfied, the task score is incremented by 1. 随后更新 self.prev_state 变量，以记录当前的一致状态，供后续检查交替性时使用。 The self.prev_state variable is then updated to record the current unanimous state for future alternation checks. 
| | $\texttt{score}\leftarrow\texttt{score}+1$ | | (3)
总得分：成功实现交替同步的累计次数。 Total Score: The cumulative number of successful, alternating synchronizations.
这一计分机制不仅激励智能体达成共同状态，还激励它们共同切换到相反的共同状态，从而检验群体在一段时间内保持稳健共识与协调的能力。 This scoring mechanism incentivizes not just achieving a common state, but also the ability to collectively switch to the opposite common state, testing robust group consensus and coordination over time.

候选平移：生成一组候选全局平移向量（$\Delta x,\Delta y$）。 Candidate Translations: A set of candidate global translation vectors ( $\Delta x,\Delta y$ ) is generated. 这些候选向量由 src 中各智能体与 tgt 中各点之间所有两两坐标差得到。 These candidates are derived from all pairwise coordinate differences between individual agents in src and points in tgt.
平移下的最优指派：对于每个候选全局平移 $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$： Optimal Assignment under Translation: For each candidate global translation $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$ :
构建一个成本矩阵。 A cost matrix is constructed. 该矩阵中的每个元素 $C_{ij}$ 表示将智能体 $i\in\texttt{src}$ 与目标点 $j\in\texttt{tgt}$ 匹配所需的曼哈顿距离，假设首先将整个 src 配置平移 $(-\Delta x,-\Delta y)$（或等价地，将 tgt 平移 $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$）。 Each entry $C_{ij}$ in this matrix represents the Manhattan distance required to match agent $i\in\texttt{src}$ to target point $j\in\texttt{tgt}$ , assuming the entire src configuration is first translated by $(-\Delta x,-\Delta y)$ (or equivalently, tgt is translated by $(\Delta x,\Delta y)$ ). 成本为 $\frac{1}{2}\left|(x_{\text{src}_{i}}-x_{\text{tgt}_{j}})-\Delta x\right|+\frac{1}{2}\left|(y_{\text{src}_{i}}-y_{\text{tgt}_{j}})-\Delta y\right|$。 The cost is $\frac{1}{2}\left|(x_{\text{src}_{i}}-x_{\text{tgt}_{j}})-\Delta x\right|+\frac{1}{2}\left|(y_{\text{src}_{i}}-y_{\text{tgt}_{j}})-\Delta y\right|$ .
随后对该成本矩阵应用最优指派算法（例如匈牙利算法或用于求解指派问题的类似方法）。 An optimal assignment algorithm (such as the Hungarian method or similar techniques for solving the assignment problem) is then applied to this cost matrix. 该算法寻找智能体与目标点之间的一种配对，使当前全局平移下的曼哈顿距离总和最小。 This finds a pairing between agents and target points that minimizes the total sum of Manhattan distances for the current global translation.
最小成本选择：从所有候选全局平移中找到的指派成本总和中选取最小值，作为最终的不相似度得分。 Minimum Cost Selection: The lowest sum of assignment costs found across all candidate global translations is selected as the final dissimilarity score. 该得分在实现中称为 cur_dis，实际上表示在找到最优全局对齐方式（平移）后，使智能体队形与目标形状相匹配所需的最小“功”（即曼哈顿距离之和）。 This score, referred to as cur_dis in the implementation, effectively represents the minimum “work” (i.e., sum of Manhattan distances) to make the agent formation match the target shape, after finding the optimal global alignment (translation).
初始距离：任务重置时，基于智能体的随机初始位置，使用相同方法计算初始不相似度得分（init_dis）。 Initial Distance: Upon task reset, an initial dissimilarity score (init_dis) is calculated using the same method, based on the random initial placement of agents. 该值作为基准。 This serves as a baseline.
评分更新：在每个仿真步骤中，重新计算当前不相似度得分（cur_dis）。 Scoring Update: At each simulation step, the current dissimilarity score (cur_dis) is recalculated. 任务得分反映了相对于初始状态的不相似度累计降低量，并确保得分不会下降。 The task score reflects the cumulative reduction in this dissimilarity from the initial state, ensuring the score is non-decreasing. 随后将任务总得分更新为迄今为止取得的最大进展： The overall task score is then updated to be the maximum progress achieved so far: 
| | $\texttt{score}\leftarrow\max(\texttt{score},\texttt{init\_dis}-\texttt{cur\_dis})$ | | (5)
任务完成：当 cur_dis 达到 0 时，任务被视为成功完成，这表明智能体在某种平移下与目标形状完全匹配。 Task Completion: The task is considered successfully completed if the cur_dis reaches 0, indicating that the agents have perfectly matched the target shape under some translation.
该评分机制激励智能体通过集体移动逐步形成并最终达到目标配置。 This scoring mechanism incentivizes agents to collectively maneuver towards and achieve the target configuration. 通过在计算指派成本之前找到最优平移对齐方式，该度量能够稳健地衡量队形与目标形状的吻合程度，而不受队形绝对全局位置的影响。 By finding the optimal translational alignment before computing the assignment cost, the metric robustly measures shape conformance irrespective of the absolute global position of the formation.

B.2 通过 SCC 和 ILP 求解交互B.2 Interaction Resolution via SCCs and ILP
仿真通过一个多阶段过程，求解单个时间步内所有可能的移动和推动： The simulation resolves all potential movements and pushes within a single time step through a multi-stage process:
接触图构建：引擎根据智能体计划执行的动作或正在进行的推动，识别所有彼此相邻且可能相互施力的 Mesh 对象。 Contact Graph Construction: The engine identifies all Mesh objects that are adjacent and could potentially exert force on one another based on intended agent actions or ongoing pushes. 由此形成一个有向图，其中边 $v\to u$ 表示 Mesh $v$ 可能推动 Mesh $u$。 This forms a directed graph where an edge $v\to u$ indicates that Mesh $v$ could potentially push Mesh $u$ .
强连通分量（SCC）约简：对接触图应用 Tarjan 算法（Lengauer & Tarjan, 1979），以识别所有 SCC。 Strongly Connected Component (SCC) Reduction: Tarjan’s algorithm (Lengauer & Tarjan, 1979) is applied to the contact graph to identify all SCCs. 一个 SCC 表示一组相互推动的 Mesh，或一个必须作为整体移动（否则完全不移动）的刚性簇。 An SCC represents a group of Meshes that are mutually pushing each other or form a rigid cluster that must move as one unit (or not at all). 每个 SCC 都被收缩为一个聚合 Node。 Each SCC is collapsed into a single aggregate Node. 不属于任何环的 Mesh 各自成为独立的 Node。 Meshes not part of any cycle become individual Nodes. 这一过程将可能包含环的接触图转换为由 Node 构成的有向无环图（DAG），表示力的传递路径。 This process transforms the potentially cyclic contact graph into a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of Nodes, representing the pathways of force transmission. 聚合 Node 的属性（例如用于移动判定的总有效质量 $m_{v}$ 和合力 $F_{\text{net},v}$）由其组成 Mesh 以及内部产生的推进力决定。 The properties of an aggregate Node (like its total effective mass $m_{v}$ for movement checks and the net force $F_{\text{net},v}$ ) are determined by its constituent Meshes and any internal propulsive forces.
整数线性规划（ILP）的建模与求解：物理交互求解的核心是一个使用 PuLP（Mitchell et al., 2011）库建立并求解的 ILP 问题。 Integer Linear Program (ILP) Formulation and Solution: The core of the physics resolution is an ILP problem formulated and solved using the PuLP (Mitchell et al., 2011) library.
变量：二元变量 $x_{v}$ 表示 Node $v$ 是否移动；连续变量表示节点上的合力以及 DAG 中相连节点之间传递的力。 Variables: Binary variables $x_{v}$ indicate if Node $v$ moves; continuous variables represent net forces on nodes and forces transmitted between connected nodes in the DAG.
目标：最大化 $\sum x_{v}$，即最大化成功移动的（聚合）Node 的数量。 Objective: Maximize $\sum x_{v}$ — i.e., maximize the number of (aggregate) Nodes that are successfully moved.
关键约束： Key Constraints:
移动条件：只有当作用于 Node $v$ 且沿潜在移动方向的总合力 $F_{\text{net},v}$ 大于或等于其总有效质量 $m_{v}$（即 $F_{\text{net},v}\geq m_{v}$）时，该 Node 才能移动（$x_{v}=1$）。 Movement Condition: A Node $v$ can only move ( $x_{v}=1$ ) if the total net force $F_{\text{net},v}$ acting on it in the direction of potential movement is greater than or equal to its total effective mass $m_{v}$ (i.e., $F_{\text{net},v}\geq m_{v}$ ). 这里，$m_{v}$ 是构成刚性 Node $v$ 的所有组成部分的固有质量之和，其中任意子聚合体的固有质量为 $m=\lfloor\sqrt{\text{area}}\rfloor$，单个组成部分（Agent 或 Mesh）的质量为 $m=1$。 Here, $m_{v}$ is the sum of the intrinsic masses of all components forming the rigid Node $v$ , where the intrinsic mass of any sub-aggregate is $m=\lfloor\sqrt{\text{area}}\rfloor$ and individual components (Agent or Mesh) have $m=1$ . 如果 $F_{\text{net},v}$ 来自 Node 内部的某个智能体，则 $m_{v}$ 包含该智能体的质量。 If $F_{\text{net},v}$ comes from an agent within the Node, that agent’s mass is included in $m_{v}$ . 图 S.7 展示了这一条件。 This condition is illustrated in Fig. S.7.
力的传递：力沿 DAG 传递。 Force Transmission: Force is transmitted along the DAG. 只有当 Node $v$ 自身移动（$x_{v}=1$）且具有足够的“剩余”力（$F_{\text{net},v}-m_{v}$）时，它才能向 DAG 中的子节点施力。 A Node $v$ can only exert force on its children in the DAG if it itself moves ( $x_{v}=1$ ) and has sufficient "leftover" force ( $F_{\text{net},v}-m_{v}$ ).
静态对象：对于标记为静态的 Node（例如包含墙体的 Node），施加约束 $x_{v}=0$。 Static Objects: Nodes marked as static (e.g., containing walls) are constrained such that $x_{v}=0$ .
网格边界：移动隐式地受到网格边界以及与其他静态对象碰撞的约束，这些约束通过图的构建来处理。 Grid Boundaries: Movement is implicitly constrained by grid boundaries and collisions with other static objects, handled by the graph construction.
ILP 求解器找出在满足所有物理约束的同时能够同步移动的最优 Node 集合。 The ILP solver finds the optimal set of Nodes that can move simultaneously while satisfying all physical constraints.
位置更新：对于 ILP 解判定为可移动的 Node，在仿真网格上更新其所属 Mesh 的全局位置。 Position Update: The global positions of the Meshes belonging to the Nodes determined to be movable by the ILP solution are updated on the simulation grid.
这一物理模型，尤其是 SCC 约简和基于 ILP 的求解，使 SwarmBench 能够模拟需要真正协调才能实现的复杂涌现物理交互，例如多个智能体协作推动一个任何单个智能体都无法独自移动的重物。 This physics model, particularly the SCC reduction and ILP-based resolution, allows SwarmBench to simulate complex, emergent physical interactions that require genuine coordination, such as multiple agents cooperatively pushing a heavy object that no single agent could move alone.

附录 F 详细群体动力学指标Appendix F Detailed Group Dynamics Metrics
为定量分析涌现的集体行为，我们基于智能体的位置 $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}$、主要动作 $A_{i,t}$ 和消息 $M_{i,t}$ 计算指标。 To quantitatively analyze emergent collective behaviors, we compute metrics based on agent positions $\mathbf{x}_{i,t}$ , their primary actions $A_{i,t}$ , and messages $M_{i,t}$ . 这些指标按轮次计算，随后通常在一次仿真运行的整个持续期间取平均，以分析其与最终得分的相关性。 These metrics are calculated per round and then typically averaged over the duration of a simulation run for correlation with the final score. 分析脚本中使用的具体变量名与这些概念定义相对应。 The specific variable names used in our analysis scripts correspond to these conceptual definitions.
基于通信的指标Communication-based Metrics
疑问句比例：每轮非空消息中包含问号（‘?’）的消息所占比例的平均值。 Proportion of Question Sentences: The average per-round proportion of non-empty messages that contain a question mark (‘?’).
数字字符比例：每轮非空消息的所有字符中数字字符所占比例的平均值。 Proportion of Digit Characters: The average per-round proportion of all characters in non-empty messages that are digits. 这可能表明智能体在共享坐标等数值数据。 This may indicate sharing of numerical data like coordinates.
平均消息长度：每轮非空消息的平均字符长度的平均值。 Mean Message Length: The average per-round mean character length of non-empty messages.
消息长度标准差：每轮非空消息字符长度的标准差的平均值。 Standard Deviation of Message Length: The average per-round standard deviation of character lengths of non-empty messages.
信息同质性：每轮去重后的非空消息的嵌入向量之间两两余弦相似度的平均值，再对各轮取平均。 Information Homogeneity: The average per-round pairwise cosine similarity of embeddings of unique non-empty messages. 嵌入向量使用 Sentence-BERT 模型（例如 all-mpnet-base-v2）生成。 Embeddings are generated using a Sentence-BERT model (e.g., all-mpnet-base-v2). 该指标衡量语义一致性。 This measures semantic coherence.
基于动作的指标Action-based Metrics
令 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}=\{\texttt{UP},\texttt{DOWN},\texttt{LEFT},\texttt{RIGHT}\}$ 为移动动作集合，$\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}=\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}\cup\{\texttt{STAY}\}$ 为与协调相关的动作集合。 Let $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}=\{\texttt{UP},\texttt{DOWN},\texttt{LEFT},\texttt{RIGHT}\}$ be movement actions and $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}=\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}\cup\{\texttt{STAY}\}$ be coordination-relevant actions.
方向熵：智能体所采取的 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}$ 中各动作的香农熵在各轮次上的平均值。 Directional Entropy: The average per-round Shannon entropy of actions in $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}$ taken by agents. 衡量移动方向的不可预测性或变异程度。 Measures the unpredictability or variability of movement directions.

其中，$p_{t}(a)$ 是在第 $t$ 轮中执行集合 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}$ 中动作 $a$ 的智能体比例。 where $p_{t}(a)$ is the proportion of agents performing action $a$ in round $t$ from the set $\mathcal{A}_{\text{move}}$ .
静止比例：执行 STAY 动作的智能体比例在各轮次上的平均值。 Stillness Proportion: The average per-round proportion of agents executing the STAY action.
主导动作比例：执行集合 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}$ 中出现频率最高的单一动作的智能体比例在各轮次上的平均值。 Dominant Action Proportion: The average per-round proportion of agents performing the single most frequent action within the set $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}$ . 较高的值表明动作共识较强。 A high value indicates strong action consensus.

其中，$N_{t}^{\text{coord}}$ 是在第 $t$ 轮中执行 $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}$ 中某个动作的智能体数量。 where $N_{t}^{\text{coord}}$ is the number of agents performing an action from $\mathcal{A}_{\text{coord}}$ in round $t$ . 反映整体移动方向的一致程度。 Indicates overall movement alignment.
基于位置和交互的指标Position and Interaction-based Metrics
平均移动距离：各智能体相对于其上一位置移动的曼哈顿距离累计值在各轮次上的平均值。 Average Moving Distance: The average per-round cumulative Manhattan distance moved by each agent from its previous position.
探索率：截至各轮次曾被任一智能体占据的不同网格单元数量在各轮次上的平均值。 Exploration Rate: The average per-round number of unique grid cells occupied by any agent up to that round.
局部结构保持数量：在第 $t-1$ 轮相邻（曼哈顿距离为 1）且在第 $t$ 轮仍保持相邻的智能体对数量在各轮次上的平均值。 Local Structure Preservation Count: The average per-round count of pairs of agents that were adjacent (Manhattan distance 1) in round $t-1$ and remain adjacent in round $t$ .
智能体推动事件：智能体 A 意图移入相邻智能体 B 所在的单元格并成功移入，同时智能体 B 沿 A 预期移动方向发生位移的事件数量在各轮次上的平均值。 Agent Push Events: The average per-round count of events where agent A, intending to move into agent B’s adjacent cell, successfully does so, and agent B is displaced in the same direction as A’s intended movement. 这表明发生了一次成功的协作推动。 This indicates a successful cooperative push.

附录 K SwarmBench 数据集Appendix K The SwarmBench Dataset
为支持结果复现和后续研究，我们将发布一个综合数据集，涵盖本文详述的所有实验运行。 To support reproducibility and further research, we will release a comprehensive dataset encompassing all experimental runs detailed in this paper. 该数据集按实验批次组织，每个批次包含一组 JSON 文件，用于记录仿真参数和详细的执行轨迹。 The dataset is structured into experimental batches, with each batch containing a collection of JSON files that log the simulation parameters and detailed execution traces.
每个实验批次的主要组成部分如下： The primary components for each experimental batch are:
meta_log.json：这个核心 JSON 文件用作批次内所有单次仿真运行的索引。 meta_log.json: This central JSON file serves as an index for all individual simulation runs within a batch. 它是一个字典，其中每个键都是唯一的运行标识符（run_id）。 It is a dictionary where each key is a unique run identifier (run_id). 每个 run_id 对应的值是一个对象，详细描述该次运行的高层配置，包括所使用的大语言模型（model）、参与的智能体数量（num_agents）以及配置的最大仿真轮数（max_round）等参数。 The corresponding value for each run_id is an object detailing the high-level configuration of that specific run, including parameters such as the Large Language Model employed (model), the number of participating agents (num_agents), and the maximum configured simulation rounds (max_round).
agent_log_<run_id>.json 文件：对于 meta_log.json 中标识的每次运行，都会生成一个对应的智能体日志文件。 agent_log_<run_id>.json files: For every run identified in meta_log.json, a corresponding agent log file is generated. 该文件存储一个 JSON 数组，其中每个元素代表某个智能体在某个特定仿真轮次的详细记录。 This file stores a JSON array, with each element representing a detailed record for a single agent at a specific simulation round. 这些记录包含智能体的局部感知（视野）、提供给其控制大语言模型（LLM）的完整提示、LLM 的原始响应，以及随后解析出的动作（例如移动、任务特定命令）和智能体选择广播的任何消息。 These records capture the agent’s local perception (view), the full prompt provided to its controlling LLM, the raw response from the LLM, and the subsequently parsed action (e.g., movement, task-specific command) and any message the agent chose to broadcast.
game_log_<run_id>.json 文件：作为智能体日志的补充，每次运行还会生成一个游戏日志文件。 game_log_<run_id>.json files: Complementing the agent logs, a game log file is also generated for each run. 该文件包含一个 JSON 数组，其中每个元素记录仿真环境在每一轮的全局状态。 This file contains a JSON array, where each element chronicles the global state of the simulation environment at each round. 其中包括完整的二维环境网格、任务的当前得分、详细列出所有智能体的 id 及其全局 x、y 坐标的数组，以及所有智能体在上一轮广播、因而可在当前轮被感知到的消息列表。 This includes the complete 2D environment grid, the current score for the task, an array detailing the id, and global x, y coordinates for all agents, and a list of all messages that were broadcast by agents in the immediately preceding round and are thus available for perception in the current round.
这些结构化数据将支持对智能体行为、通信模式和涌现的群体动力学进行深入分析。 This structured data will allow for in-depth analysis of agent behavior, communication patterns, and emergent group dynamics.

L.5 失效模式的定量分析L.5 Quantitative Analysis of Failure Modes
在这里，我们尝试从群体理论的角度更形式化地对 LLM 的失效进行分类。 Here, we try to categorize LLM’s failures more formally in terms of swarm theory.
例如，我们将“移动偏差”（Movement Bias）界定为过早收敛（March, 1991），即 LLM 的模式匹配能力使其固守次优策略。 For example, we frame the “Movement Bias” as premature convergence (March, 1991), where an LLM’s pattern-matching capabilities cause it to lock into a suboptimal strategy. 这可以通过计算智能体的动作不均衡程度来直接衡量。 This can be directly measured by calculating the action imbalance of the agent. 我们使用基尼系数作为衡量指标。 We use the Gini coefficient as the measure.
“信息孤岛”（Information Silos）源于智能体网络中自发形成的显著社群结构，其中智能体形成内部连接紧密、群体间连接稀疏的群组（Girvan & Newman, 2002），导致网络碎片化并阻碍全局共识的达成，这可以通过连通分量的数量来衡量（根据智能体的视野范围构建图）。 “Information Silos” result from spontaneous strong community structure formation in the agent network, where agents create tightly-knit groups with sparse inter-group connections (Girvan & Newman, 2002), causing network fragmentation and preventing global consensus, which can be measured by the number of connected components (constructing a graph using the agent’s visual range).
例如，对于“交通拥堵”（Traffic Jams），我们可以直接修改 Boids 模型（Reynolds, 1987）中的分离规则，计算每个智能体应受到的排斥力，以评估是否存在拥堵现象。 In terms of the “Traffic Jams”, for example, we can directly modify the Separation Rule in the Boids model (Reynolds, 1987) to calculate the repulsive forces that each agent should experience in order to evaluate whether congestion phenomena exist.
最后，我们将“金鱼般的记忆”（Memory of a Goldfish）归因于 LLM 易失的上下文窗口，它阻碍了持久的、以环境为媒介的记忆的形成，而在自然群体中，这一功能由迹协同（stigmergy）实现（Grass, 1959）。 Finally, we attribute the “Memory of a Goldfish” to the LLM’s volatile context window, which prevents the formation of a persistent, environment-mediated memory, a function served by stigmergy in natural swarms (Grass, 1959). 这种失效模式可能较难直接衡量，但我们认为，可以通过分析增大 LLM 的上下文窗口（记忆帧数）对总体得分的影响来间接衡量。 This failure mode may be relatively difficult to measure directly, but we believe it can be indirectly measured by analyzing the impact of increasing the LLM’s context window (number of memory frames) on the overall score.
