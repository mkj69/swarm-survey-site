摘要Abstract
关于团队多样性是否能提高输出质量，多智能体 LLM 流水线给出了相互矛盾的证据：异构 Mixture-of-Agents 团队优于单个模型，但在基于综合的聚合方式下，同构 Self-MoA 团队却始终胜出。 Multi-agent LLM pipelines produce contradictory evidence on whether team diversity improves output quality: heterogeneous Mixture-of-Agents teams outperform single models, yet homogeneous Self-MoA teams consistently win under synthesis-based aggregation. 我们通过识别选择瓶颈来解释这一矛盾：聚合质量存在一个交叉阈值，决定了多样性究竟有益还是有害。 We propose a resolution by identifying the selection bottleneck—a crossover threshold in aggregation quality that determines whether diversity helps or hurts. 在该模型下，我们得到了交叉阈值 $s^{*}$ 的闭式表达式（命题 3.3），它划分了多样性有益与有害的两种情形。 Under this model, we obtain a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ (Proposition 3.3) that separates the regimes where diversity helps and hurts. 在一项涵盖 7 个类别、42 项任务的针对性实验中（$N=210$），采用基于评判者的选择方式时，多样化团队相对于单模型基线的胜率达到 0.810，而同构团队的胜率为 0.512，接近随机水平（Glass’s $\Delta=2.07$）。 In a targeted experiment spanning 42 tasks across 7 categories ( $N=210$ ), a diverse team with judge-based selection achieves a win rate of 0.810 against a single-model baseline, while a homogeneous team scores 0.512—near chance (Glass’s $\Delta=2.07$ ). 基于评判者的选择方式优于 MoA 式综合，优势为 $\Delta_{\mathrm{WR}}=+0.631$；在 42 项任务中，评判小组没有在任何一项任务上认为综合方法优于基线。 Judge-based selection outperforms MoA-style synthesis by $\Delta_{\mathrm{WR}}=+0.631$ —the synthesis approach is preferred over the baseline in zero of 42 tasks by the judge panel. 采用完全独立评判者的解耦评估确认了所有结果的方向（Spearman $\rho=0.90$）；相较于主要估计值，独立评估下的胜率衰减了 53–67%，这与基于评判者的实验单元中存在部分测量循环性的情况一致。 A decoupled evaluation with fully independent judges confirms all directional findings (Spearman $\rho=0.90$ ); win rates attenuate by 53–67% under independent evaluation relative to the primary estimates, consistent with partial measurement circularity in the judge-based cells. 探索性证据表明，纳入一个较弱模型可在降低成本的同时提高性能（$p<10^{-4}$，未预注册）。 Exploratory evidence suggests that including a weaker model improves performance while reducing cost ( $p<10^{-4}$ , not pre-registered). 我们的结果表明，在以一个特定的、仅使用 Opus 的单模型配置为基线的单轮先生成后选择流水线中，选择器质量可能是比生成器多样性更具影响力的设计调节因素。 Our results suggest that selector quality may be a more impactful design lever than generator diversity in single-round generate-then-select pipelines, with a specific Opus-only singleton as the baseline.

1 引言1 Introduction
“一组多样化的问题解决者可以胜过一组能力出众的问题解决者”——Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) 在集体智能领域被引用最多的研究结果之一中得出了这一结论。 “A group of diverse problem solvers can outperform a group of high-ability problem solvers”—so concluded Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) in one of the most cited results in collective intelligence. 二十年后，多智能体 LLM 研究界正在稳定地复现这一论断正反两面的结果。 Two decades later, the multi-agent LLM community is reliably reproducing both sides of this claim. Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) 证明，将不同系列的模型混合构成 Mixture-of-Agents 架构，能够使回答质量优于任何单个模型，并宣称其方法“在 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench 和 FLASK 上达到最先进水平”。 Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) demonstrated that mixing models from different families into a Mixture-of-Agents architecture improves response quality over any single model, declaring their approach “achieve[s] state-of-the-art on AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench, and FLASK.” Li et al. Li et al. (2025) 在受控条件下检验了同一前提，却得出了相反的结论：Self-MoA——由相同模型组成的同构团队——“在所有基准测试中均始终优于 Mixed-MoA”。 Li et al. Li et al. (2025) tested the same premise under controlled conditions and reached the opposite conclusion: Self-MoA—a homogeneous team of identical models—“consistently outperforms Mixed-MoA across all benchmarks.” 两篇论文在方法论上都严谨可靠。 Both papers are methodologically sound. 两者都声称其结论具有普适性，但它们的结论似乎相互矛盾。 Both claim generality, yet their conclusions appear contradictory.
我们识别出了一种能够调和这一矛盾的机制：选择瓶颈。 We identify a mechanism that reconciles this tension: the selection bottleneck. 多样化团队产生的高方差候选池究竟是优势还是负担，完全取决于如何聚合这些候选结果。 Whether a diverse team’s high-variance candidate pool is an asset or a liability depends entirely on how those candidates are aggregated. 基于综合的聚合器——此前两项研究均采用的方法——将所有候选结果压缩为一个融合后的回答，从而丧失了生成出某个突出候选结果所带来的优势。 A synthesis-based aggregator—the method both prior works employed—compresses all candidates into one blended response, forfeiting the advantage of having generated a standout candidate. 基于选择的聚合器逐一评估候选结果并选出最佳结果，恰好利用了综合方式所浪费的方差。 A selection-based aggregator evaluates candidates individually and picks the best, exploiting exactly the variance that synthesis wastes. 这一区别并非细枝末节，而是决定多样性是否有益的主导因素。 The distinction is not a nuance; it is the dominant factor determining whether diversity helps.
在一项涵盖七个类别、42 项任务，并将团队组成与聚合机制进行交叉组合的针对性实验中，我们观察到了交叉现象。 In a targeted experiment crossing team composition with aggregation mechanism across 42 tasks spanning seven categories, we observe a crossover. 在基于评判者的选择方式下，多样化团队显著优于同构团队，但在多数投票方式下，同一个多样化团队却没有带来任何优势。 A diverse team dramatically outperforms a homogeneous team under judge-based selection, but the same diverse team provides no advantage under majority voting. 我们数据中最大的效应来自实践者最需要了解的比较：基于评判者的选择与 MoA 式综合之间的比较。 The largest effect in our data is the comparison practitioners most need: judge-based selection versus MoA-style synthesis. 在全部 7 个类别的 42 项任务中，选择方式在每一项任务上都胜出。 Selection wins in every single one of the 42 tasks across all 7 categories. 综合方法远未做到兼取多个智能体之长，其输出在超过 80% 的情况下输给了单模型基线。 The synthesis approach, far from combining the best of multiple agents, produces outputs that lose to a single-model baseline over 80% of the time. 本文各处报告的胜率均经过 Bradley–Terry 校正（BT-WR；正式定义见第 3.11 节）。 Win rates reported throughout this paper are Bradley–Terry-corrected (BT-WR; formally defined in Section 3.11). 这一表面上的悖论或许可以消解：Li et al. 和 Wang et al. 各自描述了由聚合器质量决定的交叉现象的一侧。 The apparent paradox may dissolve: Li et al. and Wang et al. each describe one side of a crossover governed by aggregator quality.
一个简单的线性模型使这一点得到了精确表述。 A simple linear model makes this precise. 我们定义了选择器质量参数 $s\in[0,1]$，并得到了交叉阈值 $s^{*}$：低于该阈值时，多样性有害；高于该阈值时，多样性有益（命题 3.3）。 We define a selector quality parameter $s\in[0,1]$ and obtain a crossover threshold $s^{*}$ below which diversity hurts and above which it helps (Proposition 3.3). 该模型预测，选择器质量应当对同构团队没有影响，这与我们数据中同构实验单元表现接近随机水平的观察结果一致。 The model predicts that selector quality should have no effect for homogeneous teams, consistent with the observed near-chance performance of the homogeneous cell in our data. 当所有候选结果都来自同一分布时，即使是完美的选择器也无从利用任何优势。 When all candidates are drawn from the same distribution, there is nothing for even a perfect selector to exploit. 图 1 展示了交叉现象的几何结构。 Figure 1 illustrates the crossover geometry.
有一项发现尤其值得关注，不过，由于它未经预注册，我们将其标记为探索性发现。 One finding warrants particular attention, though we label it exploratory because it was not pre-registered. 在一个实力强且具有多样性的团队中加入一个明显较弱的模型（Claude Haiku），与显著更高的胜率和更低的成本相关。 Adding a substantially weaker model (Claude Haiku) to a strong diverse team is associated with a significantly higher win rate and lower cost. 这一机制是合理的：来自不同能力层级的模型会引入正交的错误模式，在降低团队平均表现的同时，提高理想选择器所能达到的表现上限。 The mechanism is plausible: a model from a different capability tier introduces orthogonal error patterns, raising the oracle ceiling even as it lowers the team mean. 强大的选择器能够利用有利的一面，并忽略不利的一面。 A strong selector captures the upside and ignores the downside.
本文作出三项贡献。 This paper makes three contributions. 首先，我们提出选择瓶颈模型——一种分析性的解释视角，由此得到闭式交叉阈值 $s^{*}$，将支持多样性与反对多样性的研究发现统一起来（命题 3.3）。 First, we introduce the selection bottleneck model—an analytical explanatory lens that yields a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ unifying pro-diversity and anti-diversity findings (Proposition 3.3). 该阈值为 Hong 和 Page 提出的“有效聚合机制”这一抽象概念提供了一种操作化方式。 The threshold offers one operationalization of Hong and Page’s abstract notion of an “effective aggregation mechanism.” 其次，我们提供实证证据，表明选择远优于综合——Wang et al. 和 Li et al. 均采用的聚合机制——且其效应量在本研究中最大，这说明在我们的研究设定下，选择的表现大幅优于融合候选结果的 MoA 范式。 Second, we present empirical evidence that selection dramatically outperforms synthesis—the aggregation mechanism used by both Wang et al. and Li et al.—with the largest effect size in our study, demonstrating that the MoA paradigm of blending candidates is substantially outperformed by selection in our setting. 第三，我们报告了有关弱模型悖论的探索性证据：纳入一个更便宜、更弱的模型，可能同时提高质量并降低成本；如果这一发现得到重复验证，它将颠覆关于团队构成的传统认识。 Third, we report exploratory evidence of a weak-model paradox: including a cheaper, weaker model may simultaneously improve quality and reduce cost, a finding that, if replicated, inverts conventional wisdom about team composition.

Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) 提出了 Mixture-of-Agents（MoA），这是一种分层架构，其中多样化的“提议者”模型生成候选结果，再由“聚合器”将其综合为最终输出。 Wang et al. Wang et al. 3.7 团队构成3.7 Team Compositions
三种团队构成涵盖两个维度：模型多样性（同质与异质）和模型能力（全部为强模型与能力混合）。 Three team compositions span two dimensions: model diversity (homogeneous vs. heterogeneous) and model capability (all-strong vs. mixed-capability). 所有团队均由三个智能体组成。 All teams consist of three agents.
homo_opus：Claude Opus $\times$ 3。 homo_opus: Claude Opus $\times$ 3. 全部由强模型组成的同质基线。 All-strong homogeneous baseline.
diverse_strong：Claude Opus + GPT-5.4 + Gemini 2.5 Pro。 diverse_strong: Claude Opus + GPT-5.4 + Gemini 2.5 Pro. 来自不同系列的三个前沿模型。 Three frontier models from different families.
diverse_mixed：Claude Opus + Gemini 2.5 Pro + Claude Haiku。 diverse_mixed: Claude Opus + Gemini 2.5 Pro + Claude Haiku. 两个强模型加上一个来自不同能力层级的较弱模型。 Two strong models plus one weaker model from a different capability tier.
3.8 选择器机制3.8 Selector Mechanisms
每个团队中各智能体的输出通过以下三种机制之一进行聚合： Each team’s individual outputs are aggregated via one of three mechanisms:
基于评审的选择：外部评审组——Claude Sonnet、GPT-5-mini 和 DeepSeek-V3p2——阅读所有候选输出，并通过采用 Bradley–Terry 评分的成对比较选出最佳输出（第 3.11 节）。 Judge-based selection: An external judge panel—Claude Sonnet, GPT-5-mini, and DeepSeek-V3p2—reads all candidate outputs and selects the best via pairwise comparison with Bradley–Terry scoring (Section 3.11). 每次评审成对比较时，候选输出的顺序均随机排列，以控制位置偏差 Stureborg et al. (2024)。 Candidate order was randomized for each pairwise judge comparison to control for position bias Stureborg et al. (2024).
多数投票：每个智能体独立地从候选池中选出最佳候选输出。 Majority vote: Each agent independently selects the best candidate from the pool. 得票最多的候选输出被选中；平票时随机决定。 The candidate receiving the most votes is chosen; ties are broken randomly.
MoA 综合：Claude Sonnet 阅读所有候选输出，并遵循 Mixture-of-Agents 协议 Wang et al. (2024)，生成一条融合各候选输出中元素的综合回答。 MoA synthesis: Claude Sonnet reads all candidate outputs and produces a single synthesized response that blends elements from each candidate, following the Mixture-of-Agents protocol Wang et al. (2024).
3.9 智能体与评审分离3.9 Agent–Judge Separation
我们严格确保每次运行中的智能体池与评审池完全不重叠： We enforce strict zero-overlap between agent and judge pools within each run:
智能体模型（生成候选输出）：Claude Opus、GPT-5.4、Gemini 2.5 Pro、Claude Haiku。 Agent models (generate candidates): Claude Opus, GPT-5.4, Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude Haiku.
评审模型（评估与选择）：Claude Sonnet、GPT-5-mini、DeepSeek-V3p2。 Judge models (evaluate and select): Claude Sonnet, GPT-5-mini, DeepSeek-V3p2.
在同一实验条件下，没有任何模型同时出现在两个池中。 No model appears in both pools within the same cell. 尽管 Sonnet（评审／综合器）和 Opus（智能体）同属 Anthropic 系列——这一残留的同系列问题将在第 3.13 节中讨论——但它们是不同的模型检查点，具有不同的训练运行过程和能力特征。 While Sonnet (judge/synthesizer) and Opus (agent) belong to the Anthropic family—a residual same-family concern we address in Section 3.13—they are distinct model checkpoints with different training runs and capability profiles. 我们报告了按评审模型细分的结果（第 4.1 节），确认与所有智能体均无共同谱系的 DeepSeek-V3p2 也呈现出相同的定性模式。 We report per-judge breakdowns (Section 4.1) confirming that DeepSeek-V3p2, which shares no lineage with any agent, reproduces the same qualitative patterns.

3.12 预注册与分析计划3.12 Pre-Registration and Analysis Plan
我们的分析计划在执行任何 V4 实验运行之前就已确定。 Our analysis plan was specified before any V4 experimental runs were executed. 我们区分验证性分析（已预注册，并控制族错误率）与探索性分析（透明报告，但不作强推断性主张）。 We distinguish confirmatory analyses (pre-registered, with family-wise error control) from exploratory analyses (reported transparently but without strong inferential claims).
验证性对比（对包含 $K=3$ 项计划比较的比较族进行 Holm–Bonferroni 校正）：(1) 多样性效应：diverse_strong+judge 对比 homo_opus+judge；(2) 评判与投票：diverse_strong+judge 对比 diverse_strong+vote；(3) 选择与综合：diverse_strong+judge 对比 diverse_strong+synthesis。 Confirmatory contrasts (Holm–Bonferroni corrected across the family of $K=3$ planned comparisons): (1) diversity effect: diverse_strong+judge vs. homo_opus+judge; (2) judge vs. vote: diverse_strong+judge vs. diverse_strong+vote; (3) selection vs. synthesis: diverse_strong+judge vs. diverse_strong+synthesis.
验证性模型：采用 HC3 稳健标准误的普通最小二乘（OLS）回归；以任务为随机截距的混合效应模型（MixedLM）Barr et al. (2013)。 Confirmatory models: OLS regression with HC3 robust standard errors; mixed-effects model (MixedLM) with task as a random intercept Barr et al. (2013).
统计功效分析。 Power analysis. 每个实验条件有 $N=42$ 个观测值时，我们的设计以 80% 的统计功效检测到 Hedges’ $g\geq 0.62$ 的效应（$\alpha=0.05$，双样本 $t$ 检验，双侧）。 With $N=42$ observations per cell, our design detects effects of Hedges’ $g\geq 0.62$ at 80% power ( $\alpha=0.05$ , two-sample $t$ -test, two-sided). 全部三项验证性对比（$g$ 的范围为 1.61 至 3.86）均具有极高的统计功效。 All three confirmatory contrasts ( $g$ ranging from 1.61 to 3.86) are extremely well-powered. 在此样本量下，diverse_mixed 与 diverse_strong 的比较（$g=0.87$）达到约 98% 的统计功效。 The diverse_mixed vs. diverse_strong comparison ( $g=0.87$ ) achieves approximately 98% power at this sample size. 全文所有效应量均采用 Hedges’ $g$。 All effect sizes are Hedges’ $g$ throughout.
探索性分析：diverse_mixed 与 diverse_strong 的比较属于探索性分析（未预注册）。 Exploratory analyses: The diverse_mixed vs. diverse_strong comparison is exploratory (not pre-registered). 探索性分析部分的所有 $p$ 值均未进行多重比较校正。 All $p$ -values in the exploratory section are uncorrected for multiple comparisons.
3.13 效度威胁3.13 Threats to Validity
同系列偏差。 Same-family bias. Claude Sonnet（评判模型）与 Claude Opus（智能体）同属 Anthropic 系列。 Claude Sonnet (judge) and Claude Opus (agent) share the Anthropic family. 如果 Sonnet 对 Opus 生成的文本存在潜在偏好，这可能会抬高包含 Opus 的团队的胜率。 If Sonnet harbors a latent preference for Opus-generated text, this could inflate win rates for Opus-containing teams. 我们通过报告各评判模型的结果，并确认与任何智能体均不属于同一系列的 DeepSeek-V3p2 也呈现相同的定性模式，来缓解这一问题。 We mitigate this by reporting per-judge results and confirming that DeepSeek-V3p2, which shares no family with any agent, shows the same qualitative pattern. 综合与评判的角色重叠。 Synthesis–judge overlap. 在综合实验条件中，Claude Sonnet 综合候选输出，同时还作为三个评判模型之一，评价综合输出相对于基线的表现。 In the synthesis cell, Claude Sonnet synthesizes candidates and also serves as one of three judges evaluating the synthesis output against the baseline. 这带来了潜在的自我评价问题，不过，极低的综合输出胜率（0.179）表明自我美化偏差并未占据主导地位。 This creates a potential self-evaluation concern, though the extremely low synthesis win rate (0.179) argues against self-enhancement bias dominating. 选择与评价的角色重叠。 Selection–evaluation overlap. 在基于评判模型的实验条件中，选出获胜输出的评判模型也负责给出评价偏好。 In judge-based cells, the same judges that select the winning output also provide evaluation preferences. 使用完全独立的评判模型进行的一轮解耦评价确认了所有对比的效应方向，但效应量有所减小（表 2；详情见第 3.9 节）。 A decoupled evaluation pass with fully independent judges confirms all directional contrasts with attenuated effect sizes (Table 2; see Section 3.9 for details). 任务代表性。 Task representativeness. 七个类别中的四十二项任务提供了广泛的覆盖，但无法代表所有用例。 Forty-two tasks across seven categories provide broad coverage but cannot represent all use cases. 我们的研究结果证明了组成 $\times$ 选择器交互作用的存在，而非其在所有可能任务中的精确大小。 Our findings demonstrate the existence of a composition $\times$ selector interaction, not its precise magnitude across all possible tasks. API 变异性。 API variability. 我们在生成时使用 $T=0.7$，以平衡输出的多样性与连贯性；在评判模型评价时使用 $T=0.1$，以提高一致性并减少成对比较偏好中的噪声。 We use $T=0.7$ for generation to balance output diversity with coherence, and $T=0.1$ for judge evaluation to promote consistency and reduce noise in pairwise preferences. 所有运行均在 72 小时的时间窗口内完成。 All runs were completed within a 72-hour window.

5.2 综合为何失败5.2 Why Synthesis Fails
选择与综合之间的比较（$g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]）呈现了我们数据中最大的效应，我们认为这也是实践意义最重大的发现。 The selection-vs-synthesis comparison ( $g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]) is the largest effect in our data and, we believe, the most practically important finding. 其机制很直观：综合求平均，选择作挑选。 The mechanism is straightforward: synthesis averages, selection picks.
多样化团队的价值在于其候选输出池的方差，具体而言，在于至少有一个候选输出表现优异的概率。 A diverse team’s value lies in the variance of its candidate pool—specifically, the probability that at least one candidate is excellent. 基于选择的聚合器通过识别并保留最佳候选输出来获取这一价值。 A selection-based aggregator captures this value by identifying and preserving the best candidate. 基于综合的聚合器则将所有候选输出混合为一个“折中”输出，从而破坏了这一价值。 A synthesis-based aggregator destroys it by blending all candidates into a single “compromise” output. 由此得到的综合输出并非候选输出质量的平均水平，而可能更差，因为混合可能引入任何单个候选输出都未曾出现的语义不连贯、观点冲突和论证力度削弱等问题。 The resulting synthesis is not the average of the candidates’ qualities but something potentially worse, because blending can introduce incoherence, conflicting perspectives, and diluted arguments that no individual candidate exhibited.
综合实验单元的 $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.179$ 意味着，综合输出在 82% 的情况下会输给单个模型的输出。 The synthesis cell’s $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.179$ means the synthesized output loses to a single model’s output 82% of the time. 在选择瓶颈模型中，这正是假设 3.6 所预测的情况：当 $s\approx 0$（综合不具备选择能力）时，多样性没有能够发挥益处的机制。 In the selection bottleneck model, this is exactly what Assumption 3.6 predicts: when $s\approx 0$ (synthesis has no selection capacity), diversity has no mechanism to help. 其实际启示十分明确：使用 MoA 式综合的实践者应考虑改用基于评判者的选择，这不需要额外训练或基础设施，只需一个结构化的评估提示词。 The practical implication is stark: practitioners using MoA-style synthesis should consider switching to judge-based selection, which requires no additional training or infrastructure—only a structured evaluation prompt.
我们指出，我们的综合实现采用 Claude Sonnet 进行单轮聚合。 We note that our synthesis implementation uses single-round aggregation by Claude Sonnet. Wang 等人 (2024) 在原始 MoA 架构中提出的多轮迭代综合可能产生不同的结果。 Multi-round iterative synthesis, as proposed in the original MoA architecture Wang et al. 5.4 实用决策框架5.4 Practical Decision Framework
我们的研究发现为多智能体流水线设计提供了三条规则： Our findings yield three rules for multi-agent pipeline design:
规则 1：使团队多样化。 Rule 1: Diversify the team. 使用来自不同家族的模型。 Use models from different families. 无论选择哪种前沿模型，重复使用同一模型都不会带来收益。 Replicating the same model provides no benefit regardless of which frontier model is chosen.
规则 2：选择，而非综合。 Rule 2: Select, don’t synthesize. 在每项任务和每个类别中，基于评判者的选择都优于 MoA 综合。 Judge-based selection outperforms MoA synthesis in every task and category. 即使是抵消了多样性作用的多数投票，也优于会直接降低质量的综合。 Even majority voting—which negates diversity—is preferable to synthesis, which actively degrades quality. 基于评判者的选择器只需要结构化的评估提示，无需额外训练。 A judge-based selector requires only a structured evaluation prompt, no additional training.

5.5 局限性5.5 Limitations
针对性设计。 Targeted design. 我们的五实验单元设计最大化了特定对比的统计功效，但并未估计所有可能的交互作用（例如 homogeneous+vote、diverse_mixed+synthesis）。 Our five-cell design maximizes power for specific contrasts but does not estimate all possible interactions (e.g., homogeneous+vote, diverse_mixed+synthesis). 全因子设计将支持更丰富的交互作用分析。 A full factorial would enable richer interaction analyses.
模型特异性与基线范围。 Model specificity and baseline scope. 这些结果是在一组特定的前沿模型上得到的，其中以仅使用 Opus 的同质运行作为单模型基线。 Results are demonstrated for a specific set of frontier models using an Opus-only homogeneous run as the single-model baseline. 若与整个多样化候选池中最强的单个模型进行比较，可能会得到不同的定量估计；在这一更严格的比较下，选择器的优势是否依然存在，有待未来研究。 Comparing against the strongest individual model across the full diverse candidate pool might yield different quantitative estimates; whether the selector advantage survives that stricter comparison is left to future work. 相同的模式是否适用于其他模型家族或未来世代的模型，也仍是一个悬而未决的问题。 Whether the same patterns hold for other model families or future generations is also an open question.
固定的生成温度。 Fixed generation temperature. 所有生成运行均使用 $T=0.7$。 All generation runs use $T=0.7$ . 改变温度可能会改变同一模型内部的输出方差，并可能影响同质团队与多样化团队的相对性能。 Varying temperature could alter within-model output variance and potentially affect the relative performance of homogeneous versus diverse teams. 因此，同质采样变异可忽略不计这一结论，仅适用于固定在适中温度的设置。 The conclusion that homogeneous sampling variation is negligible is therefore specific to moderate fixed-temperature settings.
以大语言模型为评判者与主观任务。 LLM-as-judge and subjective tasks. 基于大语言模型的评估仍然只是人类判断的替代指标。 LLM-based evaluation remains a proxy for human judgment. 对于开放式的主观类别——在我们的任务集中即创意写作和伦理/政策——已有研究表明，大语言模型评判者存在风格偏好，而且与分析类任务相比，它们在这些任务上与人类评分者的一致性可能更低 Zheng et al. (2023)。 For open-ended subjective categories—creative writing and ethics/policy in our task battery—LLM judges are known to exhibit stylistic preferences and may agree less with human raters than on analytical tasks Zheng et al. (2023). 我们没有这些类别的人类评估数据，因此尚不清楚我们的结果在多大程度上能够推广到主观领域中的人类偏好。 We do not have human evaluation data for these categories, and the extent to which our results generalize to human preferences in subjective domains is unknown. 未来研究的一项优先工作，是对具有代表性的任务子集开展人类评估，尤其是创意和政策任务。 Human evaluation on a representative subset of tasks, particularly creative and policy tasks, is a priority for future work. 我们的解耦评估轮次（表 2）部分解决了选择与评估之间的循环性问题，在独立评判者下确认了所有对比的方向，但独立评审组本身也存在局限：三名评判者中的一名（GPT-4o-mini）表现出退化行为（平局率为 99.6%），使有效的独立评审组缩减为两名评判者。 Our decoupled evaluation pass (Table 2) partially addresses selection–evaluation circularity, confirming all directional contrasts under independent judges, but the independent panel itself exhibited limitations: one of three judges (GPT-4o-mini) proved degenerate (99.6% tie rate), reducing the effective independent panel to two judges. 各评判者的平局率差异显著（GPT-4o-mini：99.6%，Gemini Flash：76.7%，GLM-5：50.3%），这表明较弱的模型可能缺乏进行可靠成对评估所需的区分能力。 Per-judge tie rates varied substantially (GPT-4o-mini: 99.6%, Gemini Flash: 76.7%, GLM-5: 50.3%), suggesting that weaker models may lack the discriminative capacity for reliable pairwise evaluation. 综合器与评判者的角色重叠（Claude Sonnet 同时担任综合器和三名评判者之一）仍是一个具体的隐忧，不过，综合结果极低的胜率表明，自我增强偏差并非主要驱动因素。 The synthesis–judge overlap (Claude Sonnet serving as both synthesizer and one of three judges) remains a specific concern, though the very low synthesis win rate argues against self-enhancement bias as a primary driver.
有界因变量。 Bounded dependent variable. 胜率的取值范围为 $[0,1]$，但我们采用线性模型对其建模。 Win rates are bounded in $[0,1]$ , yet we model them linearly. 我们观测到的数值（0.13–0.96）避免了极端的地板效应或天花板效应，且我们已验证，经 logit 变换后的结果在定性上完全一致。 Our observed values (0.13–0.96) avoid extreme floor/ceiling effects, and we verified that logit-transformed results are qualitatively identical.
静态拓扑。 Static topology. 所有实验均采用单轮先生成后选择的流程。 All experiments use a single-round generate-then-select pipeline. 迭代式拓扑（多轮辩论、递归改进）可能表现出不同的动态特征。 Iterative topologies (multi-round debate, recursive refinement) may exhibit different dynamics.
未测量可区分性。 Distinguishability not measured. 我们的框架将输出可区分性（$s$）作为关键中介变量，但并未直接测量它。 Our framework invokes output distinguishability ( $s$ ) as the key mediator, but we do not measure it directly. 未来工作应通过嵌入空间距离将可区分性操作化。 Future work should operationalize distinguishability via embedding-space distances.
Diverse_mixed 的混杂因素。 Diverse_mixed confound. diverse_mixed 实验条件同时改变了能力（将 GPT-5.4 替换为 Claude Haiku）和模型家族多样性（使用两个 Anthropic 模型，而非一个）。 The diverse_mixed cell simultaneously changes capability (replacing GPT-5.4 with Claude Haiku) and family diversity (two Anthropic models instead of one). 我们无法分离这些效应，并将其明确列为该探索性比较的设计局限。 We cannot isolate these effects and flag this as a design limitation of the exploratory comparison.
5.6 未来工作5.6 Future Work
由此引出三个研究方向。 Three directions follow. 首先，采用预注册设计复现关于较弱模型的发现，可使其从探索性发现转变为验证性发现。 First, replicating the weak-model finding with a pre-registered design would move it from exploratory to confirmatory. 其次，大规模人工评估可验证以 LLM 为评判者这一代理指标的有效性，并量化其偏差结构。 Second, human evaluation at scale would validate the LLM-as-judge proxy and quantify its bias structure. 第三，连续改变选择器的质量（例如，逐步削弱评判模型的能力），可确定基于评判者的选择失效时的能力阈值，从而检验我们在先导研究中观察到的“架构 $>$ 能力”这一发现是否存在下限。 Third, varying selector quality continuously (e.g., by progressively weakening the judge model) would map the capability threshold below which judge-based selection fails, testing whether the “architecture $>$ capability” finding observed in our pilot has a lower bound.
6 结论6 Conclusions
本文作出三项贡献。 This paper makes three contributions. 首先，选择瓶颈模型作为一种分析性解释视角，给出了闭式交叉阈值 $s^{*}$（命题 3.3），统一了支持多样性与反对多样性的研究发现：当聚合机制的运行水平低于 $s^{*}$ 时，多样性有害；高于该阈值时，多样性有益。 First, the selection bottleneck model—an analytical explanatory lens—provides a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ (Proposition 3.3) unifying pro-diversity and anti-diversity findings: when aggregation operates below $s^{*}$ , diversity hurts; above it, diversity helps. 其次，我们的针对性实验表明，基于选择的聚合显著优于 MoA 式综合（$g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]），且综合方法在全部 42 个任务中均不及单模型基线——这一发现对占主导地位的流程范式提出了挑战。 Second, our targeted experiment demonstrates that selection-based aggregation dramatically outperforms MoA-style synthesis ( $g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]), with the synthesis approach losing to a single-model baseline in all 42 tasks—a finding that challenges the dominant pipeline paradigm. 第三，我们报告了探索性证据，表明纳入一个能力较弱、成本较低的模型反而会提升性能（$g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]），这提示最优团队组成可能并不要求全部采用前沿模型。 Third, we report exploratory evidence that including a weaker, cheaper model paradoxically improves performance ( $g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]), suggesting that optimal team composition may not require all-frontier models.
我们推测，选择瓶颈是更广泛的先生成后选择流程的一种结构性属性：候选池中方差的价值完全取决于利用该方差的机制。 The selection bottleneck is, we conjecture, a structural property of generate-then-select pipelines more broadly: the value of variance in the candidate pool depends entirely on the mechanism that exploits it. 该领域已投入大量精力来构建更好的单体生成器。 The field has spent considerable effort building better individual generators. 我们的结果表明，通过构建更好的选择器，可以获得显著收益。 Our results suggest that substantial gains are available by building better selectors. 使用完全独立的评判者进行的一轮解耦评估确认了所有方向性发现，但效应量有所减小（表 2；Spearman $\rho=0.90$），这表明即使消除了选择与评估之间的循环性，核心结果依然成立。 A decoupled evaluation pass using fully independent judges confirms all directional findings with attenuated effect sizes (Table 2; Spearman $\rho=0.90$ ), indicating that the core results survive even when selection–evaluation circularity is removed.
我们的结果表明，在多智能体流程中，选择器设计可能比生成器改进带来更大的质量提升；我们鼓励未来工作在更广泛的情境中检验这一假设。 Our results suggest that selector design may offer larger quality gains than generator improvement in multi-agent pipelines, a hypothesis we encourage future work to test across broader settings.
