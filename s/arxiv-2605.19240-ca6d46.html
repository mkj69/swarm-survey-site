1 引言1 Introduction
随着大语言模型越来越多地部署于协作式多智能体系统（LLM-MAS）中 Wu et al. (2024); Hong et al. (2023); Venkatesh et al. (2026b); Venkatesh et al. (2026a)，对抗性失效的性质发生了根本转变。 As large language models are increasingly deployed within collaborative multi-agent systems (LLM-MAS) Wu et al. (2024); Hong et al. (2023); Venkatesh et al. (2026b); Venkatesh et al. (2026a), the nature of adversarial failures has fundamentally shifted. 单智能体系统容易受到孤立的幻觉或提示注入攻击，而多智能体架构则容易受到级联攻击：这是一种协同失效，其中注入某个智能体的对抗性影响或错误影响会跨智能体、跨轮次传播、放大并自我强化 Xie et al. (2026); Lee et al. (2025)。 While single-agent systems are vulnerable to isolated hallucinations or prompt injection attacks, multi-agent architectures are susceptible to cascade attacks: coordinated failures in which adversarial or erroneous influence injected at one agent propagates, amplifies, and self-reinforces across agents and turns Xie et al. (2026); Lee et al. (2025). 这些失效利用了系统自身的交互结构：单个注入的提示就可能污染共享记忆、重定向工具输出，并使多个智能体同步走向一条整体上存在缺陷的轨迹，从而造成一旦形成就难以遏制的全系统失效 Xie et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025)。 These failures exploit the interaction structure of the system itself: a single injected prompt can corrupt shared memory, redirect tool outputs, and synchronize agents toward a collectively flawed trajectory, producing system-wide failures that are difficult to contain once established Xie et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025). 级联表现为两种形态：突发式级联，即强烈的放大与同步在单个交互轮次内立即造成全系统耦合；渐进式级联，即局部无害的影响通过持久记忆的复用、工具产物的传播以及缓慢的语义漂移，跨轮次逐步累积 Xie et al. (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); An et al. (2026)。 Cascades manifest in two regimes: abrupt cascades, where strong amplification and synchronization produce immediate system-wide coupling within a single interaction turn; and gradual cascades, where locally benign influence accumulates across turns through persistent memory reuse, tool artifact propagation, and slow semantic drift Xie et al. (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); An et al. (2026).

CASPIAN 使用高效的基于 LI-CTE 的影响估计方法，从通信、记忆、工具和执行交互中构建统一的跨通道因果影响张量。 CASPIAN constructs a unified cross-channel causal influence tensor from communication, memory, tool, and execution interactions using an efficient LI-CTE-based influence estimation. 通过对演化中的拓扑进行谱监测，在线检测突发性和持续性级联的形成，同时利用缓存的影响动态，在检测时恢复参与级联的智能体及主导传播主干，相对延迟开销低于 1%。 Spectral monitoring over the evolving topology detects abrupt and persistent cascade formation online, while cached influence dynamics enable recovery of cascade agents, and dominant spines at detection time, with sub-1% relative overhead latency.
核心挑战在于，级联的证据分散在不同的交互通道与轮次中。 The central challenge is that cascade evidence is distributed across both interaction channels and turns. 由于影响可能通过通信、记忆、工具和执行交互并发传播，任何单一通道都只能提供底层影响动态的局部视图 de Witt et al. (2025)。 Because influence may propagate concurrently through communication, memory, tool, and execution interactions, any single channel provides only a partial view of the underlying influence dynamics de Witt et al. (2025). 与此同时，影响会通过智能体上下文逐渐累积，因此某个交互轮次中的行为仍可能反映许多轮次之前发生的注入所留下的残余效应。 At the same time, influence accumulates gradually through agent context, so behavior at a given interaction turn may still reflect residual effects of injections occurring many turns earlier. 因此，即使系统整体正在向协同失效转变，各智能体在局部看来仍可能是无害的。 As a result, agents may appear locally benign even as the system collectively transitions toward coordinated failure. 因此，检测级联需要监测影响如何跨智能体、跨通道并随时间联合传播，而非孤立地检查各次交互。 Detecting cascades therefore requires monitoring how influence propagates jointly across agents, channels, and time rather than inspecting interactions in isolation.
现有防御方法无法提供这种视角。 Existing defenses fail to provide this view. 语义护栏 Chennabasappa et al. (2025); Zhang et al. (2025d); Shi et al. (2025) 和基于 LLM 的评判器逐轮检查消息内容，无法感知跨通道的传播结构和时间上的累积。 Semantic guardrails Chennabasappa et al. (2025); Zhang et al. (2025d); Shi et al. (2025) and LLM-based judges inspect message content per-turn, remaining blind to cross-channel propagation structure and temporal accumulation. 基于图的方法 Wang et al. (2025); Miao et al. (2025); Zhou et al. (2026) 利用通信图对智能体交互进行建模，并通过表示空间中的异常检测异常智能体，但这些方法大多仅针对单一交互通道，无法捕捉影响如何沿记忆、工具和执行路径联合传播。 Graph-based approaches Wang et al. (2025); Miao et al. (2025); Zhou et al. (2026) model agent interactions over communication graphs and detect anomalous agents through representation-space irregularities, but largely operate on a single interaction channel and do not capture how influence propagates jointly across memory, tool, and execution pathways. 此外，现有归因方法主要依赖对已完成执行的事后轨迹分析 Zhang et al. (2025c); Wang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2025a)，对传播正在进行时的在线干预支持有限。 Existing attribution methods further rely primarily on post-hoc trace analysis over completed executions Zhang et al. (2025c); Wang et al. (2026); Zhang et al. (2025a), providing limited support for online intervention during active propagation. 这些方法的共同局限在于，它们围绕提示、智能体或静态图进行局部推理，而级联攻击本质上是全局传播过程。 The shared limitation across these approaches is that they reason locally about prompts, agents, or static graphs, whereas cascade attacks are fundamentally global propagation processes. 因此，CASPIAN 将级联检测从局部语义检查转向对传播动态的在线监测。 CASPIAN therefore shifts cascade detection from localized semantic inspection to online monitoring of propagation dynamics.
本研究的关键洞见是，级联的出现意味着多智能体系统中智能体间影响传播的结构性重组。 The key insight of this work is that cascade onset constitutes a structural reorganization of inter-agent influence propagation in a multi-agent system. 级联并非源于孤立的异常交互，而是通过四种传播动态的耦合演化形成：影响能量的增长（放大）、传播模态之间对齐程度的提高（同步）、跨交互通道的扩散（跨通道传播），以及跨轮次的持续强化（持续性）de Witt et al. (2025); Xie et al. (2026); Venkatesh and Cui (2026)。 Rather than arising from isolated anomalous interactions, cascades emerge through the coupled evolution of four propagation dynamics: growing influence energy (amplification), increasing alignment between propagation modes (synchronization), spread across interaction channels (cross-channel propagation), and sustained reinforcement across turns (persistence) de Witt et al. (2025); Xie et al. (2026); Venkatesh and Cui (2026). 谱分析在网络化系统级联行为的研究中已有成熟应用 Zocca et al. (2021); Restrepo et al. (2006)，并提供了一种统一机制，可通过演化中的影响拓扑跟踪这些动态。 Spectral analysis has a well-established role in studying cascade behavior in networked systems Zocca et al. (2021); Restrepo et al. (2006), and provides a unified mechanism for tracking these dynamics through the evolving influence topology. 同步性和持续性表现为谱耦合的收紧，而放大和跨通道传播则反映出级联影响范围的扩大。 Synchronization and persistence manifest as tightening spectral coupling, while amplification and cross-channel propagation reflect increasing cascade reach. 相比之下，良性协作可能暂时提高交互强度，但不会产生持续耦合或持久的跨通道扩散 Goltsev et al. (2012)。 In contrast, benign collaboration may temporarily increase interaction intensity without sustained coupling or persistent cross-channel spread Goltsev et al. (2012). 由于谱分析直接作用于不断演化的拓扑结构，因此无需监督或针对特定拓扑的校准。 Because spectral analysis operates directly on the evolving topology, it requires no supervision or topology-specific calibration.

systems through cross-channel causal propagation monitoring. 以往的谱方法作用于静态通信图或预定义的网络结构，而 CASPIAN 将 MAS 建模为不断演化的跨通道因果影响拓扑，涵盖通信、记忆、工具和执行交互。 Unlike prior spectral approaches that operate over static communication graphs or pre-defined network structure, CASPIAN models the MAS as an evolving cross-channel causal influence topology spanning communication, memory, tool, and execution interactions. 该拓扑通过后期交互条件传递熵（Late-Interaction Conditional Transfer Entropy，LI-CTE）形式化方法 Khattab and Zaharia (2020) 进行在线估计，以毫秒级开销捕捉有方向且考虑历史信息的影响传播。 This topology is estimated online using a Late-Interaction Conditional Transfer Entropy (LI-CTE) formulation Khattab and Zaharia (2020) that captures directed, history-aware influence propagation with millisecond-scale overhead. 随后，CASPIAN 对不断演化的拓扑进行在线谱监测，通过放大效应增长、谱耦合收紧、相变和跨通道扩散等传播特征检测级联的发生。 CASPIAN then performs online spectral monitoring over the evolving topology to detect cascade onset through propagation signatures such as amplification growth, tightening spectral coupling, phase transitions, and cross-channel spread. 这些信号共同区分短暂的协作活动与自我强化的级联形成过程，从而无需攻击模板、良性校准轨迹或离线训练，即可检测突发的单轮级联和渐进的多轮级联。 Together, these signals distinguish transient collaborative activity from self-reinforcing cascade formation, enabling detection of both abrupt single-turn and gradual multi-turn cascades without attack templates, benign calibration traces, or offline training. 检测到级联后，CASPIAN 进一步直接根据缓存的影响动态进行在线归因，识别导致全系统扩散的级联源头、放大节点、桥接节点和主导传播主干，从而在传播仍在进行时实现及时干预。 Upon detection, CASPIAN further performs online attribution directly from cached influence dynamics, identifying the cascade origin, amplifier, bridge, and dominant propagation spines responsible for system-wide spread, enabling timely intervention during active propagation. 在多种多智能体框架和级联攻击设置下，CASPIAN 在检测准确性和级联早期识别方面均持续优于语义护栏、基于 LLM 的评判器和基于图的异常检测器，同时相对延迟开销低于 1%。 Across diverse multi-agent frameworks and cascade attack settings, CASPIAN consistently outperforms semantic guardrails, LLM-based judges, and graph-based anomaly detectors in both detection accuracy and early cascade identification while operating with sub-1% relative overhead latency.
我们提出首个用于建模 LLM 多智能体系统中跨通道因果影响的统一框架，联合捕捉通信、记忆、工具和执行通道中智能体之间的有向影响。 We introduce the first unified framework for modeling cross-channel causal influence in LLM multi-agent systems, jointly capturing directed inter-agent influence across communication, memory, tool, and execution channels.
我们提出一种在线谱级联检测框架，根据不断演化的智能体间影响动态，识别突发的单轮级联和渐进的多轮级联，无需攻击模板、良性校准轨迹或离线训练，且仅产生毫秒级开销。 We propose an online spectral cascade detection framework that identifies both abrupt single-turn and gradual multi-turn cascades from evolving inter-agent influence dynamics, without attack templates, benign calibration traces, or offline training, with only millisecond-scale overhead.

3 背景3 Background
3.1 LLM 多智能体系统中的级联故障3.1 Cascade Failures in LLM Multi-Agent Systems
LLM-MAS 通过迭代交互运行，其中，智能体的输出在多个轮次中成为下游智能体的输入，由此形成有向依赖关系，影响通过这些依赖关系随时间传播并增强 de Witt et al. (2025); Xie et al. (2026)。 LLM-MAS operate through iterative interactions, where agent outputs become inputs to downstream agents across multiple turns, inducing directed dependencies through which influence propagates and reinforces over time de Witt et al. (2025); Xie et al. (2026). 一个关键挑战是级联故障的出现，即错误信号或对抗性信号在跨智能体和跨轮次传播的过程中逐渐自我强化。 A key challenge is the emergence of cascade failures, where erroneous or adversarial signals become progressively self-reinforcing across agents and turns. 这种行为由四种机制驱动：1 放大，即下游智能体重复使用已受污染的中间状态 Zhu et al. (2025); Jiang et al. (2026)；2 跨通道传播，即影响在通信、记忆和工具交互之间扩散 An et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025)；3 持续性，即信号在多个轮次中持续嵌入系统 Xie et al. (2026)；以及 4 同步，即智能体逐渐趋向相互耦合的推理轨迹 Xie et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025)。 This behavior is driven by four mechanisms: 1 Amplification, where downstream agents reuse corrupted intermediate states Zhu et al. (2025); Jiang et al. (2026); 2 Cross-Channel Propagation, where influence spreads across communication, memory, and tool interactions An et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025); 3 Persistence, where signals remain embedded across turns Xie et al. (2026); and 4 Synchronization, where agents converge toward coupled reasoning trajectories Xie et al. (2026); de Witt et al. (2025). 这些动态过程既会产生突发的单轮级联，也会产生渐进的多轮级联 An et al. (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); Zhu et al. (2025)。 These dynamics produce both abrupt Single-turn Cascades and gradual Multi-turn Cascades An et al. (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); Zhu et al. (2025). 要区分短暂活动与持续的级联形成过程，需要分析不断演变的传播动态，而非孤立的智能体输出 Watts (2002); Kempe et al. (2003)。 Distinguishing transient activity from sustained cascade formation requires analyzing evolving propagation dynamics instead of isolated agent outputs Watts (2002); Kempe et al. (2003).

在轮次 $t_{0}$ 检测到级联后，CASPIAN 直接利用检测过程中缓存的影响矩阵进行在线归因，无需额外参数或重新计算。 Upon detection at turn $t_{0}$ , CASPIAN performs online attribution directly from the influence matrices cached during detection, requiring no additional parameters or recomputation. 归因基于起始快照 $\tilde{A}_{t_{w}}$ 以及保留的区间 $\{\tilde{A}_{\tau},A_{\tau}\}_{\tau=t_{w}}^{t_{0}}$ 进行，其中 $t_{w}$ 表示 Watch 首次激活的轮次。 Attribution operates over the onset snapshot $\tilde{A}_{t_{w}}$ together with the retained interval $\{\tilde{A}_{\tau},A_{\tau}\}_{\tau=t_{w}}^{t_{0}}$ , where $t_{w}$ denotes the first Watch activation turn. 对于即时级联，$t_{w}=t_{0}$，归因简化为基于单轮快照的分析。 For immediate cascades, $t_{w}=t_{0}$ and attribution reduces to a single-turn snapshot. 我们识别出三种智能体角色，以及级联的主导传播骨干路径。 We identify three agent roles together with the dominant propagation spines of the cascade.
源头（Origin）对应于级联开始时对外影响力最强的智能体： The Origin corresponds to the agent with the strongest outgoing influence at cascade onset: 
| | $i^{\mathrm{origin}}=\arg\max_{i}\sum_{j}\tilde{A}_{t_{w}}(i,j)$ | | (11)
放大器（Amplifier）对应于在确认区间 $[t_{w},t_{0}]$ 内，相对于其接收的影响，最显著地增强传播的智能体： The Amplifier corresponds to the agent that most strongly reinforces propagation relative to what it receives across the confirmation interval $[t_{w},t_{0}]$ : 
| | $i^{\mathrm{amp}}=\arg\max_{i}\sum_{\tau=t_{w}}^{t_{0}}\frac{\sum_{j}\tilde{A}_{\tau}(i,j)}{\sum_{k}\tilde{A}_{\tau}(k,i)+\epsilon}$ | | (12)
桥梁（Bridge）是网络中接收并重新分配影响最为显著的智能体。 The Bridge is the agent that most strongly receives and redistributes influence across the network. 为避免与枢纽节点相邻的边受到抑制，该指标使用未归一化矩阵 $A_{\tau}$ 计算： To avoid suppression of hub-adjacent edges, it is computed using the unnormalized matrices $A_{\tau}$ : 
| | $i^{\mathrm{bridge}}=\arg\max_{i}\sum_{\tau=t_{w}}^{t_{0}}\left(\sum_{j}A_{\tau}(i,j)\right)\left(\sum_{k}A_{\tau}(k,i)\right)$ | | (13)
随后，我们从确认区间内逐元素取最大值得到的影响矩阵中提取前 K 条级联传播主干 We then extract the top-K cascade propagation spines from the elementwise maximum influence matrix over the confirmation interval 
| | $\bar{A}^{\max}(i,j)=\max_{\tau\in[t_{w},\,t_{0}]}\,\tilde{A}_{\tau}(i,j)$ |
该矩阵捕捉每条边在级联期间达到的最强影响，而非其在任一单独轮次的状态。 which captures the strongest influence each edge achieves during the cascade rather than its state at any single turn. 随后，按最弱环节的强度对传播主干进行排序： Spines are then ranked by weakest-link strength: 
| | $\Pi^{K}=\operatorname{TopK}_{\pi\,:\,|\pi|\leq\mathrm{diam}(G_{0})}\;\min_{(i,j)\in\pi}\,\bar{A}^{\max}(i,j)$ | | (14)
对于每条传播主干 $\pi_{k}\in\Pi^{K}$，其主导交互通道确定为：$c^{\star}(\pi_{k})=\arg\max_{c\in\mathcal{C}}\sum_{(i,j)\in\pi_{k}}\bar{A}^{\max,(c)}(i,j)$，其中 $\bar{A}^{\max,(c)}$ 表示通道 $c$ 在 $[t_{w},t_{0}]$ 内的区间最大影响矩阵。 For each propagation spine $\pi_{k}\in\Pi^{K}$ , the dominant interaction channel is identified as: $c^{\star}(\pi_{k})=\arg\max_{c\in\mathcal{C}}\sum_{(i,j)\in\pi_{k}}\bar{A}^{\max,(c)}(i,j)$ , where $\bar{A}^{\max,(c)}$ denotes the maximum interval influence matrix for channel $c$ over $[t_{w},t_{0}]$ .
最终的归因输出为： The final attribution output is given by: 
| | $\Bigl\{i^{\mathrm{origin}},\;i^{\mathrm{amp}},\;i^{\mathrm{bridge}},\;\Pi^{K},\;(c^{\star}(\pi_{k}))_{k=1}^{K}\Bigr\}$ |
该输出提供以轨迹为条件的影响分解，揭示级联的起源、放大方式、传播路径及通道。 which provides a trace-conditioned influence decomposition of where the cascade originates, how it amplifies, its propagation pathways, and channels.

6 讨论与更广泛的影响6 Discussions and Broader Impact
CASPIAN 表明，基于 LLM 的多智能体系统中的级联攻击不仅受局部消息异常的影响，还受跨智能体、通道和轮次的因果影响结构演化的影响。 CASPIAN shows that cascade attacks in LLM-based multi-agent systems are shaped not only by localized message anomalies, but by the evolving structure of causal influence across agents, channels, and turns. 我们的结果表明，可靠的检测需要联合监测通信、记忆、工具和执行交互：消融实验显示，执行通道中的证据无法从其余通道中完全恢复，因此，在具有记忆或工具访问能力的系统中，仅监测消息可能会遗漏重要的传播信号。 Our results suggest that reliable detection requires joint monitoring of communication, memory, tool, and execution interactions: ablations show that execution-channel evidence cannot be fully recovered from the remaining channels, so message-only monitoring can miss important propagation signals in systems with memory or tool access. 我们的谱分析和角色—通道分析进一步表明，尽管不同攻击类别之间存在差异，级联往往遵循反复出现的组织模式，涉及源头、桥梁、放大器和其余智能体。 Our spectral and role-channel analyses further indicate that, despite differences across attack classes, cascades often follow recurring organizational patterns involving origin, bridge, amplifier, and residual agents. 成功与失败的攻击在注入前基本无法区分，这一事实表明，脆弱性主要在在线交互过程中显现，而非源于系统静止状态下的静态属性。 The fact that successful and unsuccessful attacks are largely indistinguishable before injection suggests that vulnerability emerges primarily during online interaction, rather than from static properties of the resting system.
随着 LLM-MAS 越来越多地在配备持久记忆、工具和自主协调能力的情况下部署，仅通过检查消息可能难以定位跨智能体传播的故障。 As LLM-MAS are increasingly deployed with persistent memory, tools, and autonomous coordination, failures that propagate across agents may become difficult to localize through message inspection alone. CASPIAN 较低的相对运行时开销表明，跨通道因果监测可以集成到 MAS 的在线执行过程中，且对吞吐量的影响有限。 CASPIAN’s low relative runtime overhead suggests that cross-channel causal monitoring can be integrated into online MAS execution with limited impact on throughput. 除安全用途外，跟踪影响传播还可能支持可解释性、审计和智能体协调分析，从而推动对复杂 LLM 智能体生态系统中结构透明性的进一步研究。 Beyond security, tracking influence propagation may support interpretability, auditing, and analysis of agent coordination, motivating further work on structural transparency in complex LLM-agent ecosystems.
7 局限性7 Limitations
CASPIAN 假设能够获取通信、记忆、工具和执行通道中足够的交互轨迹，以在线构建不断演化的因果影响张量。 CASPIAN assumes access to sufficient interaction traces across communication, memory, tool, and execution channels to construct the evolving causal influence tensor online. 在某些模态仅部分可观测或不可用的部署环境中，性能可能下降至相应通道消融设置下的水平。 In deployments where some modalities are partially observable or unavailable, performance may degrade toward the corresponding channel-ablation setting. 然而，由于 LI-CTE 对跨通道传播进行联合建模，一种模态中的微弱证据仍可通过其他通道中的相关结构得到增强。 However, because LI-CTE models cross-channel propagation jointly, weak evidence in one modality can still be reinforced by correlated structure in other channels. 在 LLM Debate 等高度分布式的 MAS 框架中，归因也更具挑战性，因为传播路径较为分散，桥接角色在结构上的集中程度较低。 Attribution is also more challenging in highly distributed MAS frameworks such as LLM Debate, where propagation paths are diffuse and bridge roles are less structurally concentrated. 最后，我们的评估聚焦于现有的 MAS 框架和攻击类别。 Finally, our evaluation focuses on existing MAS frameworks and attack families. 将 CASPIAN 扩展至新兴交互模态、部分可观测的部署环境，以及经过优化以规避基于传播的监测的自适应对手，仍是未来工作的重要方向。 Extending CASPIAN to emerging interaction modalities, partially observable deployments, and adaptive adversaries optimized to evade propagation-based monitoring remains an important direction for future work.
8 结论8 Conclusion
我们提出了 CASPIAN，这是一个用于在线检测基于 LLM 的多智能体系统中的级联攻击并对其进行归因的统一框架。 We introduced CASPIAN, a unified framework for online detection and attribution of cascade attacks in LLM-based multi-agent systems. CASPIAN 将基于 LI-CTE 的影响估计与对放大、同步、持续性和跨通道扩散的谱监测相结合，无需离线训练或针对特定攻击的模板，即可检测突发和渐进式级联。 CASPIAN combines LI-CTE-based influence estimation with spectral monitoring of amplification, synchronization, persistence, and cross-channel spread, enabling detection of abrupt and gradual cascades without offline training or attack-specific templates. 在各项基准和 MAS 框架中，CASPIAN 的性能优于语义护栏、LLM 评判器和基于图的检测器，同时还能在执行过程中还原级联角色和传播路径。 Across benchmarks and MAS frameworks, CASPIAN outperforms semantic guardrails, LLM judges, and graph-based detectors, while also recovering cascade roles and propagation paths during execution. 这些结果表明，结构性因果监测可为复杂 LLM 智能体生态系统中的安全、归因和审计提供可扩展的基础。 These results highlight structural causal monitoring as a scalable foundation for security, attribution, and auditing in complex LLM-agent ecosystems.

(2) 按有向边和通道对事件进行分组。 (2) Grouping events by directed edge and channel. CASPIAN 在有向边—通道三元组 $(i,j,c)$ 层面估计影响。 CASPIAN estimates influence at the level of directed edge-channel triplets $(i,j,c)$ . 对于每个轮次 $t$，适配器将规范化事件分组为 For each turn $t$ , the adapter groups normalized events as 
| | $\mathcal{B}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)=\{e\in\mathcal{E}_{t}:\mathrm{src}(e)=i,\;\mathrm{tgt}(e)=j,\;c(e)=c\}.$ | | (16)
如果在轮次 $t$，三元组 $(i,j,c)$ 没有发生任何事件，CASPIAN 就不会为该轮的这一边—通道对赋予新证据。 If no event occurs for a triplet $(i,j,c)$ at turn $t$ , CASPIAN assigns no new evidence to that edge-channel pair for the turn. 如果同一三元组上发生多个事件，则将它们的向量取平均，得到单一的源端信号和目标端信号： If multiple events occur on the same triplet, their vectors are averaged into a single source-side and target-side signal: 
| | $\bar{\mathbf{u}}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)|}\sum_{e\in\mathcal{B}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)}\mathbf{u}_{e},\qquad\bar{\mathbf{v}}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)|}\sum_{e\in\mathcal{B}_{ij}^{(c)}(t)}\mathbf{v}_{e}.$ | | (17)
这里，$\mathbf{u}_{e}$ 表示事件的源端表示，$\mathbf{v}_{e}$ 表示目标端表示。 Here, $\mathbf{u}_{e}$ denotes the source-side representation of the event and $\mathbf{v}_{e}$ denotes the target-side representation. 对于文本通道，这些向量是附加了轻量级元数据的基于嵌入的向量。 For text channels, these are embedding-based vectors augmented with lightweight metadata. 对于执行通道，它们是数值型运行时特征向量。 For execution channels, they are numeric runtime-feature vectors. 对同一轮次内的多个事件取平均，可以减少局部噪声，同时保留从源到目标的有向通道结构。 Averaging multiple events within a turn reduces local noise while preserving directed source-to-target channel structure.
(3) 维护各通道专属的目标历史。 (3) Maintaining channel-specific target histories. 对于每个目标智能体 $a_{j}$ 和通道 $c$，CASPIAN 都维护一个流式历史向量 $h_{j}^{(c)}(t)$，用于概括目标智能体此前在该通道中的行为。 For every target agent $a_{j}$ and channel $c$ , CASPIAN maintains a streaming history vector $h_{j}^{(c)}(t)$ summarizing the target’s previous behavior in that channel. 令 $\bar{\mathbf{v}}_{j}^{(c)}(t)$ 表示智能体 $a_{j}$ 在轮次 $t$、通道 $c$ 上的聚合目标侧状态，该状态通过对所有经由通道 $c$ 影响 $a_{j}$ 的事件所关联的目标侧向量取平均得到。 Let $\bar{\mathbf{v}}_{j}^{(c)}(t)$ denote the aggregate target-side state for agent $a_{j}$ on channel $c$ at turn $t$ , obtained by averaging all target-side vectors associated with events affecting $a_{j}$ through channel $c$ . 所有通道的目标历史均使用同一条与通道无关的在线平滑规则更新： The target history is updated using the same channel-agnostic online smoothing rule for all channels: 
| | $h_{j}^{(c)}(t)=\mathrm{EMA}\!\left(h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1),\bar{\mathbf{v}}_{j}^{(c)}(t)\right).$ | | (18)
该历史向量仅用作目标智能体的条件上下文。 This history vector is used only as the conditioning context for the target agent. 重要的是，LI-CTE 使用 $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ 对当前轮次进行评分，即使用观测当前事件之前的目标历史。 Importantly, LI-CTE scores the current turn using $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ , the target history before observing the current event. 只有在计算完影响分数之后才更新历史，从而防止当前目标观测泄漏到条件上下文中。 The history is updated only after the influence score is computed, preventing the current target observation from leaking into the conditioning context. 通道特有的行为由事件表示本身捕捉：通信、记忆、工具和执行事件会产生不同的特征信号，而非通过人为地为不同通道指定不同的衰减率来捕捉。 Channel-specific behavior is captured by the event representations themselves: communication, memory, tool, and execution events produce different feature signals, rather than by manually assigning different decay rates to different channels.
(4) 后期交互条件影响评分。 (4) Late-interaction conditional influence scoring. 对于源智能体 $a_{i}$、目标智能体 $a_{j}$ 和通道 $c$，CASPIAN 将有向影响估计为 For a source agent $a_{i}$ , target agent $a_{j}$ , and channel $c$ , CASPIAN estimates directed influence as 
| | $A_{t}^{(c)}(i,j)\propto I\!\left(u_{i}^{(c)}(t);v_{j}^{(c)}(t)\mid h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)\right),$ | | (19)
其中，$u_{i}^{(c)}(t)$ 是当前源侧通道信号，$v_{j}^{(c)}(t)$ 是当前目标侧信号，$h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ 是目标此前的通道历史。 where $u_{i}^{(c)}(t)$ is the current source-side channel signal, $v_{j}^{(c)}(t)$ is the current target-side signal, and $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ is the target’s previous channel history.
在实际操作中，这相当于询问：在考虑了根据目标智能体自身的过去行为即可预期其采取的行为之后，源智能体的通道活动是否还能解释目标当前行为中的额外变异？ Operationally, this asks: after accounting for what the target agent was already likely to do based on its own past behavior, does the source agent’s channel activity explain additional variation in the target’s current behavior? 如果可以，则边 $(i\rightarrow j)$ 在通道 $c$ 上会获得较高的影响分数。 If yes, the edge $(i\rightarrow j)$ receives a high influence score for channel $c$ .
后期交互设计对效率至关重要。 The late-interaction design is important for efficiency. CASPIAN 独立构建紧凑的源表示、目标表示和历史表示，而不是通过计算成本高昂的高维密度估计器，对完整的源、目标和历史上下文进行联合编码。 Instead of jointly encoding full source, target, and history contexts through an expensive high-dimensional density estimator, CASPIAN constructs compact source, target, and history representations independently. 这些表示仅在最终的条件依赖性评分阶段才进行组合。 These representations are combined only at the final conditional-dependence scoring stage. 这既保留了考虑历史的有向影响信息，又避免了回放、额外的 LLM 调用或计算成本高昂的全状态 CTE 估计。 This preserves directed, history-aware influence information while avoiding replay, additional LLM calls, or expensive full-state CTE estimation.
(5) 残差依赖性视角。 (5) Residual dependence view. LI-CTE 可以理解为一种残差依赖性计算。 LI-CTE can be understood as a residual-dependence computation. CASPIAN 首先估计当前目标信号中已被目标自身通道历史解释的分量： CASPIAN first estimates the component of the current target signal that is already explained by the target’s own channel history: 
| | $\widehat{v}_{j}^{(c)}(t)=P\!\left(v_{j}^{(c)}(t)\mid h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)\right).$ | | (20)
随后计算未被解释的目标残差： It then computes the unexplained target residual: 
| | $r_{j}^{(c)}(t)=v_{j}^{(c)}(t)-\widehat{v}_{j}^{(c)}(t),$ | | (21)
并对源信号与该残差之间的依赖强度进行评分： and scores how strongly the source signal depends on this residual: 
| | $A_{t}^{(c)}(i,j)=\mathrm{Dep}\!\left(u_{i}^{(c)}(t),r_{j}^{(c)}(t)\right).$ | | (22)
因此，较高的 LI-CTE 并不只是意味着两个智能体在语义上相似或同时活跃。 Thus, high LI-CTE does not simply mean that two agents are semantically similar or active at the same time. 它意味着，源信号能够在目标自身既往历史所能解释的范围之外，进一步解释目标当前的行为。 It means the source signal explains the target’s current behavior beyond the target’s own prior history. 这一条件化步骤减少了由共享提示、共同任务上下文或背景协调所导致的虚假影响。 This conditioning step reduces spurious influence caused by shared prompts, common task context, or background coordination.
(6) 流式实现。 (6) Streaming implementation. 在每个轮次，CASPIAN 都通过以下流程更新每个可行的边与通道三元组 $(i,j,c)$： At each turn, CASPIAN updates every feasible edge-channel triplet $(i,j,c)$ through the following procedure:
收集事件：汇集所有从源 $a_{i}$ 经由通道 $c$ 到达目标 $a_{j}$ 的规范化事件。 Collect events: gather all normalized events from source $a_{i}$ to target $a_{j}$ through channel $c$ .
编码载荷：将每个载荷转换为紧凑的通道向量。 Encode payloads: convert each payload into a compact channel vector. 文本通道使用基于嵌入的表示；执行通道使用轻量级数值型运行时特征。 Textual channels use embedding-based representations; execution channels use lightweight numeric runtime features.
轮次内聚合：对同一 $(i,j,c)$ 三元组上的多个事件取平均，得到一个源侧向量和一个目标侧向量。 Aggregate within turn: average multiple events on the same $(i,j,c)$ triplet into one source-side and one target-side vector.
检索目标历史：加载该目标智能体在该通道上此前的 EMA 历史 $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$。 Retrieve target history: load the previous EMA history $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ for the target agent and channel.
计算残差依赖性：估计在以 $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ 为条件后，源向量能够在多大程度上解释目标残差。 Compute residual dependence: estimate how much the source vector explains the target residual after conditioning on $h_{j}^{(c)}(t-1)$ .
更新历史：评分后，更新目标通道历史以及用于后续边与通道估计的流式状态。 Update histories: after scoring, update the target channel history and the streaming state used for future edge-channel estimates.
在我们的实现中，依赖性分数通过一个作用于紧凑通道向量的轻量级流式协方差估计器计算。 In our implementation, the dependence score is computed using a lightweight streaming covariance estimator over compact channel vectors. 具体而言，将紧凑的源向量、目标向量和历史向量拼接起来，在线对各边缘分布进行秩归一化，并用其更新指数平滑协方差估计。 Concretely, the compact source, target, and history vectors are concatenated, marginally rank-normalized online, and used to update an exponentially smoothed covariance estimate. 随后，根据源、目标和历史所对应的协方差分块计算条件依赖性分数，并采用收缩和对角扰动来确保求逆的稳定性。 The conditional dependence score is then computed from the covariance blocks corresponding to source, target, and history, with shrinkage and jitter for stable inversion. 这提供了一种快速的条件互信息高斯 copula 近似方法，无需拟合密度模型，也无需调用额外的 LLM。 This provides a fast Gaussian-copula approximation to conditional mutual information without fitting a density model or invoking an additional LLM. 最终得分被截断为非负值，并填入对应通道的影响矩阵。 The final score is clipped to be nonnegative and inserted into the channel-specific influence matrix.
(7) 构建统一的跨通道影响张量。 (7) Building the unified cross-channel influence tensor. 每条可行边在各通道上的 LI-CTE 得分构成跨通道因果影响张量中的一个元素： The LI-CTE score for each feasible edge and channel becomes one entry of the cross-channel causal influence tensor: 
| | $\mathcal{A}_{t}(i,j,c)=A_{t}^{(c)}(i,j),\qquad\mathcal{A}_{t}\in\mathbb{R}^{N\times N\times|\mathcal{C}|}.$ | | (23)
每个张量切片对应一个交互通道。 Each tensor slice corresponds to one interaction channel. 用于归因的原始矩阵通过对各通道求和得到， The raw matrix used for attribution is obtained by summing across channels, 
| | $A_{t}^{\mathrm{raw}}(i,j)=\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}}\mathcal{A}_{t}(i,j,c),$ | | (24)
而用于谱监测的归一化矩阵则通过应用主要方法中所述的拓扑掩码和度感知归一化得到。 while the normalized matrix used for spectral monitoring is obtained by applying the topology mask and degree-aware normalization described in the main method. 由此得到动态演化的影响拓扑 $\tilde{A}_{t}$，供 CASPIAN 用于级联检测、相位监测和在线归因。 This produces the evolving influence topology $\tilde{A}_{t}$ used by CASPIAN for cascade detection, phase monitoring, and online attribution.
