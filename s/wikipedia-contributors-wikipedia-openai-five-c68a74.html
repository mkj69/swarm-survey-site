OpenAI Five is a computer program by OpenAI that plays the five-on-five video game Dota 2. Its first public appearance occurred in 2017, where it was demonstrated in a live one-on-one game against the professional player Dendi, who lost to it. The following year, the system had advanced to the point of performing as a full team of five, and began playing against and showing the capability to defeat professional teams.
OpenAI Five 是 OpenAI 开发的一款计算机程序，能够玩五对五电子游戏 Dota 2。它于 2017 年首次公开亮相，在现场演示的一对一比赛中击败了职业选手 Dendi。次年，该系统已发展到能够组成完整的五人战队参赛的程度，并开始与职业战队交手，展现出击败职业战队的能力。
By choosing a game as complex as Dota 2 to study machine learning, OpenAI thought they could more accurately capture the unpredictability and continuity seen in the real world, thus constructing more general problem-solving systems. The algorithms and code used by OpenAI Five were eventually borrowed by another neural network in development by the company, one which controlled a physical robotic hand. OpenAI Five has been compared to other similar cases of artificial intelligence (AI) playing against and defeating humans, such as AlphaStar in the video game StarCraft II, AlphaGo in the board game Go, Deep Blue in chess, and Watson on the television game show Jeopardy!.
OpenAI 认为，选择 Dota 2 这样复杂的游戏来研究机器学习，可以更准确地捕捉现实世界中的不可预测性和连续性，从而构建更通用的问题求解系统。OpenAI Five 所使用的算法和代码，最终被该公司正在开发的另一个用于控制实体机械手的神经网络借鉴。OpenAI Five 常被拿来与其他人工智能（AI）对抗并击败人类的类似案例相比较，例如电子游戏 StarCraft II 中的 AlphaStar、棋盘游戏围棋中的 AlphaGo、国际象棋中的 Deep Blue，以及电视智力竞赛节目 Jeopardy! 中的 Watson。
History历史
Development on the algorithms used for the bots began in November 2016. OpenAI decided to use Dota 2, a competitive five-on-five video game, as a base due to it being popular on the live streaming platform Twitch, having native support for Linux, and had an application programming interface (API) available. Before becoming a team of five, the first public demonstration occurred at The International 2017 in August, the annual premiere championship tournament for the game, where Dendi, a Ukrainian professional player, lost against an OpenAI bot in a live one-on-one matchup. After the match, CTO Greg Brockman explained that the bot had learned by playing against itself for two weeks of real time, and that the learning software was a step in the direction of creating software that can handle complex tasks "like being a surgeon". OpenAI used a methodology called reinforcement learning, as the bots learn over time by playing against itself hundreds of times a day for months, in which they are rewarded for actions such as killing an enemy and destroying towers.
这些游戏智能体所用算法的开发始于 2016 年 11 月。OpenAI 决定以五对五竞技电子游戏 Dota 2 为基础，因为该游戏在直播平台 Twitch 上很受欢迎，原生支持 Linux，并且提供了应用程序编程接口（API）。在组成五人战队之前，该系统于 8 月举行的 The International 2017 上进行了首次公开演示；这是该游戏一年一度的顶级锦标赛，乌克兰职业选手 Dendi 在现场一对一比赛中输给了 OpenAI 的一个游戏智能体。赛后，首席技术官 Greg Brockman 解释说，该智能体通过现实时间为两周的自我对弈完成了学习，而这款学习软件是朝着开发能够处理“像担任外科医生这样”复杂任务的软件迈出的一步。OpenAI 采用了一种称为强化学习（reinforcement learning）的方法，让这些智能体在数月内每天进行数百次自我对弈，逐步学习，并对击杀敌人、摧毁防御塔等行为给予奖励。
By June 2018, the ability of the bots expanded to play together as a full team of five and were able to defeat teams of amateur and semi-professional players. At The International 2018, OpenAI Five played in two games against professional teams, one against the Brazilian-based paiN Gaming and the other against an all-star team of former Chinese players. Although the bots lost both matches, OpenAI still considered it a successful venture, stating that playing against some of the best players in Dota 2 allowed them to analyze and adjust their algorithms for future games. The bots' final public demonstration occurred in April 2019, where they won a best-of-three series against The International 2018 champions OG at a live event in San Francisco. A four-day online event to play against the bots, open to the public, occurred the same month. There, the bots played in 42,729 public games, winning 99.4% of those games.
到 2018 年 6 月，这些智能体的能力已扩展到能够组成完整的五人战队协同比赛，并能够击败由业余和半职业选手组成的战队。在 The International 2018 上，OpenAI Five 与职业战队进行了两场比赛，一场对阵来自巴西的 paiN Gaming，另一场对阵由中国前职业选手组成的全明星战队。尽管这些智能体在两场比赛中均告负，OpenAI 仍认为这是一次成功的尝试，并表示，与 Dota 2 中一些最优秀的选手交手，使他们能够分析并调整算法，为未来的比赛做准备。这些智能体的最后一次公开演示于 2019 年 4 月举行，它们在旧金山的一场现场活动中，以三局两胜制击败了 The International 2018 冠军 OG。同月，还举行了一场面向公众、为期四天的线上活动，让公众与这些智能体对战。在该活动中，这些智能体共参加了 42,729 场公开比赛，胜率为 99.4%。
Architecture架构
Each OpenAI Five bot is a neural network containing a single layer with a 4096-unit LSTM that observes the current game state extracted from the Dota developer's API. The neural network conducts actions via numerous possible action heads (no human data involved), and every head has meaning. For instance, the number of ticks to delay an action, what action to select – the X or Y coordinate of this action in a grid around the unit. In addition, action heads are computed independently. The AI system observes the world as a list of 20,000 numbers and takes an action by conducting a list of eight enumeration values. Also, it selects different actions and targets to understand how to encode every action and observe the world.
每个 OpenAI Five 智能体都是一个包含单层、4096 个单元的长短期记忆网络（LSTM）的神经网络，用于观测通过 Dota 开发者 API 提取的当前游戏状态。该神经网络通过多个可能的动作头（action heads）执行动作（不涉及人类数据），每个动作头都有其含义。例如，执行某个动作前延迟的时钟步数、选择何种动作，以及该动作在单位周围网格中的 X 或 Y 坐标。此外，各个动作头均独立计算。该 AI 系统将世界观测为一个包含 20,000 个数字的列表，并通过输出一个包含八个枚举值的列表来执行动作。此外，它还会选择不同的动作和目标，以理解如何对每个动作进行编码以及如何观测世界。
OpenAI Five has been developed as a general-purpose reinforcement learning training system on the "Rapid" infrastructure. Rapid consists of two layers: it spins up thousands of machines and helps them 'talk' to each other and a second layer runs software. By 2018, OpenAI Five had played around 180 years worth of games in reinforcement learning running on 256 GPUs and 128,000 CPU cores, using Proximal Policy Optimization, a policy gradient method.
OpenAI Five 是基于“Rapid”基础设施开发的通用强化学习训练系统。Rapid 由两层组成：第一层启动数千台机器，并帮助它们相互“通信”；第二层运行软件。截至 2018 年，OpenAI Five 使用策略梯度方法近端策略优化（Proximal Policy Optimization），在 256 个 GPU 和 128,000 个 CPU 核心上运行强化学习，累计进行了相当于约 180 年的游戏对局。
Comparisons with other game AI systems与其他游戏 AI 系统的比较
Prior to OpenAI Five, other AI versus human experiments and systems have been successfully used before, such as Jeopardy! with Watson, chess with Deep Blue, and Go with AlphaGo. In comparison with other games that have used AI systems to play against human players, Dota 2 differs as explained below:
在 OpenAI Five 之前，其他 AI 与人类对抗的实验和系统已有成功应用，例如参加 Jeopardy! 的 Watson、下国际象棋的 Deep Blue，以及下围棋的 AlphaGo。与其他使用 AI 系统对抗人类玩家的游戏相比，Dota 2 的不同之处如下：
Long run view: The bots run at 30 frames per second for an average match time of 45 minutes, which results in 80,000 ticks per game. OpenAI Five observes every fourth frame, generating 20,000 moves. By comparison, chess usually ends before 40 moves, while Go ends before 150 moves.
长期视野：这些智能体以每秒 30 帧的速率运行，每场比赛平均持续 45 分钟，因此每局游戏包含 80,000 个时钟步。OpenAI Five 每隔四帧观测一次，生成 20,000 次行动。相比之下，国际象棋通常在 40 步以内结束，而围棋在 150 步以内结束。
Partially observed state of the game: Players and their allies can only see the map directly around them. The rest of it is covered in a fog of war which hides enemies units and their movements. Thus, playing Dota 2 requires making inferences based on this incomplete data, as well as predicting what their opponent could be doing at the same time. By comparison, Chess and Go are "full-information games", as they do not hide elements from the opposing player.
部分可观测的游戏状态：玩家及其盟友只能看到各自周围的地图区域。其余区域被战争迷雾覆盖，敌方单位及其行动因此被隐藏。因此，玩 Dota 2 需要根据这些不完整的信息进行推断，同时预测对手可能正在做什么。相比之下，国际象棋和围棋属于“完全信息博弈”，因为它们不会向对手隐藏任何要素。
Continuous action space: Each playable character in a Dota 2 game, known as a hero, can take dozens of actions that target either another unit or a position. The OpenAI Five developers allow the space into 170,000 possible actions per hero. Without counting the perpetual aspects of the game, there are an average of ~1,000 valid actions each tick. By comparison, the average number of actions in chess is 35 and 250 in Go.
连续动作空间：Dota 2 游戏中的每个可操控角色称为英雄，可以执行数十种以其他单位或某个位置为目标的动作。OpenAI Five 的开发者将这一空间设定为每个英雄有 170,000 种可能的动作。不计游戏中持续变化的因素，每个时间步平均有 ~1,000 个有效动作。相比之下，国际象棋的平均可选动作数为 35，围棋则为 250。
Continuous observation space: Dota 2 is played on a large map with ten heroes, five on each team, along with dozens of buildings and non-player character (NPC) units. The OpenAI system observes the state of a game through developers' bot API, as 20,000 numbers that constitute all information a human is allowed to get access to. A chess board is represented as about 70 lists, whereas a Go board has about 400 enumerations.
连续观测空间：Dota 2 在一张大型地图上进行，其中有十名英雄，每队五名，以及数十座建筑和非玩家角色（NPC）单位。OpenAI 系统通过开发者提供的机器人 API 观测游戏状态，获取 20,000 个数值，这些数值涵盖了人类玩家获准访问的全部信息。国际象棋棋盘用约 70 个列表表示，而围棋棋盘则有约 400 个枚举项。
Reception反响
OpenAI Five have received acknowledgement from the AI, tech, and video game community at large. Microsoft founder Bill Gates called it a "big deal", as their victories "required teamwork and collaboration". Chess champion Garry Kasparov, who lost against the Deep Blue AI in 1997, stated that despite their losing performance at The International 2018, the bots would eventually "get there, and sooner than expected".
OpenAI Five 获得了人工智能、科技和电子游戏领域的广泛认可。微软创始人比尔·盖茨称其为“一件大事”，因为它们的胜利“需要团队配合与协作”。曾于 1997 年输给 Deep Blue 人工智能的国际象棋冠军加里·卡斯帕罗夫表示，尽管这些机器人在 2018 年国际邀请赛上落败，但它们最终会“达到目标，而且会比预期更早”。
In a conversation with MIT Technology Review, AI experts also considered OpenAI Five system as a significant achievement, as they noted that Dota 2 was an "extremely complicated game", so even beating non-professional players was impressive. PC Gamer wrote that their wins against professional players was a significant event in machine learning. In contrast, Motherboard wrote that the victory was "basically cheating" due to the simplified hero pools on both sides, as well as the fact that bots were given direct access to the API, as opposed to using computer vision to interpret pixels on the screen. The Verge wrote that the bots were evidence that the company's approach to reinforcement learning and its general philosophy about AI was "yielding milestones".
在与《麻省理工科技评论》的交谈中，人工智能专家也认为 OpenAI Five 系统是一项重大成就；他们指出，Dota 2 是一款“极其复杂的游戏”，因此即便击败非职业玩家也令人印象深刻。PC Gamer 写道，它们战胜职业玩家是机器学习领域的一件大事。相比之下，Motherboard 写道，这场胜利“基本上就是作弊”，因为双方可选的英雄池都经过了简化，而且机器人可以直接访问 API，而无须使用计算机视觉来解读屏幕上的像素。The Verge 写道，这些机器人证明，该公司的强化学习方法及其人工智能总体理念正在“取得里程碑式进展”。
In 2019, DeepMind unveiled a similar bot for StarCraft II, AlphaStar. Like OpenAI Five, AlphaStar used reinforcement learning and self-play. The Verge reported that "the goal with this type of AI research is not just to crush humans in various games just to prove it can be done. Instead, it's to prove that — with enough time, effort, and resources — sophisticated AI software can best humans at virtually any competitive cognitive challenge, be it a board game or a modern video game." They added that the DeepMind and OpenAI victories were also a testament to the power of certain uses of reinforcement learning.
2019 年，DeepMind 发布了用于《星际争霸 II》的类似机器人 AlphaStar。与 OpenAI Five 一样，AlphaStar 也采用了强化学习和自我对弈。The Verge 报道称：“这类人工智能研究的目标，并不只是为了证明人工智能能够在各种游戏中碾压人类。相反，其目标是证明，只要投入足够的时间、精力和资源，复杂的人工智能软件就能在几乎任何竞争性的认知挑战中胜过人类，无论是棋盘游戏还是现代电子游戏。”该媒体还补充道，DeepMind 和 OpenAI 的胜利也证明了强化学习在某些应用方式下的强大能力。
It was OpenAI's hope that the technology could have applications outside of the digital realm. In 2018, they were able to reuse the same reinforcement learning algorithms and training code from OpenAI Five for Dactyl, a human-like robot hand with a neural network built to manipulate physical objects. In 2019, Dactyl solved the Rubik's Cube.
OpenAI 希望这项技术能够应用于数字领域之外。2018 年，他们成功将 OpenAI Five 所用的强化学习算法和训练代码复用于 Dactyl；这是一只配备神经网络、旨在操纵实体物体的仿人机械手。2019 年，Dactyl 还原了魔方。
