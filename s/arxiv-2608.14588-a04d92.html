幻觉滚雪球：将多智能体 LLM 流水线中的错误传播建模为状态转移The Hallucination Snowball: Modeling Error Propagation as State Transitions in Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines
摘要Abstract
顺序式多智能体 LLM 流水线将专门化智能体串联起来，却不在交接时进行验证，由此形成了一种结构性缺陷，其后果可量化且十分严重。 Sequential multi-agent LLM pipelines chain specialized agents without verification at handoffs, creating a structural flaw with measurable and severe consequences. 我们表明，在阶段 1 注入的幻觉并非仅仅持续存在，而是会发生转化：原始数值事实变为衍生计算结果，继而变为叙述性文本，最终成为经编辑审核认可的结论。 We show that hallucinations injected at Stage 1 do not merely persist; they transform: raw numerical facts become derived computations, then narrative prose, then editorially approved conclusions. 每经历一次转化，可检测性都会近乎不可逆地下降。 At each transformation, detectability degrades near-irreversibly. 我们将其形式化为幻觉滚雪球效应，即涵盖四种状态（原始事实 $\to$ 衍生结果 $\to$ 叙述 $\to$ 不可见）的一阶马尔可夫过程，实测各边界的逃逸概率分别为 24.6%、48.3% 和 89.3%。 We formalize this as the hallucination snowball effect, a first-order Markov process over four states (Raw Fact $\to$ Derived $\to$ Narrative $\to$ Invisible) with empirically measured per-boundary escape probabilities of 24.6%, 48.3%, and 89.3%. 在 FinanceBench 上的一个 4 智能体金融分析流水线中，针对自动注入的 346 个幻觉，gpt-4o 的检测率从阶段 1 的 72.0% 降至阶段 4 的 50.9%，且 23.7% 的幻觉在最终输出中仍然存在，并始终未被检测到。 Across 346 automatically injected hallucinations in a 4-agent financial analysis pipeline on FinanceBench, gpt-4o detection drops from 72.0% at Stage 1 to 50.9% at Stage 4, and 23.7% of hallucinations survive completely undetected in the final output. 即使是受测模型中最强的模型（Qwen3.5-397B-A17B，阶段 1 的检测率为 87.0%）也面临结构性上限；预计其阶段 4 的检测率仅为 ${\sim}$ 60–65%。 Even the strongest model tested (Qwen3.5-397B-A17B, 87.0% at Stage 1) faces a structural ceiling; projected Stage 4 detection is only ${\sim}$ 60–65%. 关键在于，使用相同 RAG 验证工具的边界关卡，相较于流水线末端检查，可将幻觉存留率从 58.4% 降至 16.2%（Cohen’s $h=-0.911$，$p<0.000001$），而仅在末端检查相较于不进行验证，只能带来 2.3 个百分点的改善。 Critically, boundary gates using identical RAG verification tools reduce hallucination survival from 58.4% to 16.2% versus end-of-pipeline checking (Cohen’s $h=-0.911$ , $p<0.000001$ ), while end-checking alone achieves merely 2.3 pp improvement over no verification. 何时验证比是否验证更重要。 When you verify matters more than whether you verify. 我们的模型能够预测 $n$ 智能体线性流水线中的幻觉存留率，并给出最优验证资源分配方案：优先投入 $S_{1}{\to}S_{2}$，此时 75.4% 的幻觉仍可被捕获，而非投入 $S_{3}{\to}S_{4}$，此时 89.3% 的幻觉已经逃逸。 Our model predicts survival for $n$ -agent linear pipelines and prescribes optimal verification resource allocation: invest at $S_{1}{\to}S_{2}$ first, where 75.4% of hallucinations are still catchable, not at $S_{3}{\to}S_{4}$ where 89.3% have already escaped.

1 引言1 Introduction
多智能体 LLM 流水线已成为处理复杂、高风险 AI 任务的主流架构。 Multi-agent LLM pipelines have become the dominant architecture for complex, high-stakes AI tasks. LangGraph（LangChain AI, 2024）、AutoGen（Wu et al., 2023）和 CrewAI（CrewAI Inc., 2024）等框架使按顺序串联专门化智能体变得十分容易：研究员 $\to$ 分析师 $\to$ 撰稿人 $\to$ 审核员。 Frameworks like LangGraph (LangChain AI, 2024), AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), and CrewAI (CrewAI Inc., 2024) make it trivial to chain specialized agents sequentially: Researcher $\to$ Analyst $\to$ Writer $\to$ Reviewer. 每个智能体仅接收其前序智能体的文本输出，没有来源元数据，没有置信度分数，也无法访问源文档。 Each agent receives only its predecessor’s text output with no provenance metadata, no confidence scores, and no access to source documents. 每个智能体都完全信任其前序智能体。 Every agent trusts its predecessor completely. 这种架构选择会带来可量化且严重程度出人意料的后果。 This architectural choice has a measurable and surprisingly severe consequence.
考虑一个在阶段 1 引入的幻觉：“销货成本（COGS）为 $71.2B”（真实值：$63.1B）。 Consider a hallucination introduced at Stage 1: “COGS was $71.2B” (true value: $63.1B). 到阶段 2，分析师计算出“同比增长 8.3%”，这是一项建立在虚构基数之上的衍生断言。 By Stage 2, the Analyst computes “an 8.3% YoY increase,” a derived claim built on a fabricated base. 到阶段 3，撰稿人生成：“波音面临显著的成本压力，COGS 增长 8.3% 至 $71.2B，反映出供应链中断的影响。” By Stage 3, the Writer produces “Boeing faced significant cost headwinds, with COGS rising 8.3% to $71.2B, reflecting supply chain disruptions.” 到阶段 4，审核员将其评为“结构清晰、推理合理的分析”并予以通过，因为其未获得任何源文档，只能检查内部一致性。 By Stage 4, the Reviewer approves it as “well-structured analysis with sound reasoning,” having been given no source documents and only able to check internal consistency. 这个幻觉已被彻底洗白。 The hallucination has been laundered completely.

2 背景与相关工作2 Background and Related Work
2.1 单智能体幻觉检测2.1 Single-Agent Hallucination Detection
主流范式将幻觉视为孤立模型输出的属性。 The dominant paradigm treats hallucination as a property of isolated model outputs. FActScore (Min et al., 2023) 将生成内容分解为原子断言，并依据知识源逐一核验。 FActScore (Min et al., 2023) decomposes generations into atomic claims and verifies each against a knowledge source. CoVe (Dhuliawala et al., 2024) 使用自我验证链，由模型检查自身输出。 CoVe (Dhuliawala et al., 2024) uses self-verification chains where a model checks its own outputs. VeriScore (Song et al., 2024) 将这一方法扩展至长文本生成。 VeriScore (Song et al., 2024) extends this to long-form generation. SAFE (Wei et al., 2024) 使用搜索增强的事实评估。 SAFE (Wei et al., 2024) uses search-augmented fact evaluation. 这些方法都仅在我们所称的 $S_{1}$ 阶段运行：对单个输出进行独立评估。 All of these operate at what we call $S_{1}$ only: a single output evaluated in isolation. 它们均未对一个智能体的幻觉输出成为下一个智能体的输入时会发生什么进行建模。 None models what happens when one agent’s hallucinated output becomes the next agent’s input. 我们的研究表明，在 $S_{1}$ 阶段检出率为 72.0% 的幻觉，经过三次转换后，其检出率仅为 50.9%，因此，单智能体评估不足以作为顺序流水线的评估框架。 Our work shows that a hallucination detectable at 72.0% at $S_{1}$ becomes catchable at only 50.9% three transformations later, making single-agent evaluation an insufficient framework for sequential pipelines.
2.2 多智能体辩论与协作2.2 Multi-Agent Debate and Collaboration
Du 等人 (Du et al., 2024) 表明，多个 LLM 围绕同一问题展开辩论可以改善推理。 Du et al. (Du et al., 2024) show that multiple LLMs debating over the same question improves reasoning. MAD (Liang et al., 2024) 将这一方法扩展至结构化对抗辩论。 MAD (Liang et al., 2024) extends this to structured adversarial debate. 关键在于，两者均采用并行拓扑，所有智能体同时共享相同的事实基础。 Critically, both use parallel topologies where all agents share the same factual foundation simultaneously. 在顺序传播中，每个智能体只能看到前一个智能体经过转换的输出，而这一研究方向在结构上并未涵盖此类传播。 Sequential propagation, where each agent sees only the previous agent’s transformed output, is structurally absent from this line of work. 我们研究的失效模式与上述研究相互独立，针对的是通过 LangGraph (LangChain AI, 2024)、AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) 和 CrewAI (CrewAI Inc., 2024) 实际部署于生产环境的架构。 Our failure mode is orthogonal and addresses the architecture actually deployed in production via LangGraph (LangChain AI, 2024), AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), and CrewAI (CrewAI Inc., 2024).
2.3 多智能体系统中的信任与安全2.3 Trust and Security in Multi-Agent Systems
TrustAgent (Hua et al., 2024) 以及有关失控智能体的研究 (Yang et al., 2024) 探讨了多智能体环境中恶意智能体实施的蓄意对抗性操纵。 TrustAgent (Hua et al., 2024) and work on rogue agents (Yang et al., 2024) study intentional adversarial manipulation by malicious agents in multi-agent settings. 我们研究的失效模式在类别上截然不同：它不需要对抗者。 Our failure mode is categorically different: it requires no adversary. 完全出于善意、忠实遵循指令的智能体也会造成幻觉滚雪球效应，因为该架构在交接处没有提供纠错机制。 Completely well-intentioned agents following their instructions faithfully produce the hallucination snowball because the architecture provides no error-correction mechanism at handoffs. 这使得幻觉滚雪球效应比需要恶意意图介入的对抗性威胁更为普遍。 This makes the snowball more pervasive than adversarial threats, which require compromised intent.
2.4 RAG 与检索增强验证2.4 RAG and Retrieval-Augmented Verification
Lewis 等人 (Lewis et al., 2021) 提出了检索增强生成，使 LLM 的输出以外部文档为依据。 Lewis et al. (Lewis et al., 2021) introduced retrieval-augmented generation to ground LLM outputs in external documents. 后续研究 (Gao et al., 2024) 已将 RAG 扩展至多种场景。 Subsequent work (Gao et al., 2024) has extended RAG to diverse settings. 我们的边界关卡受到这一研究方向的启发，但部署于根本不同的层面：其目的不是增强生成，而是在下一个智能体处理输出之前，于交接点核验智能体的输出。 Our boundary gate is inspired by this line of work but deployed at a fundamentally different layer: not to augment generation, but to verify agent output at handoff points before the next agent processes it. 关键在于，我们的关卡是确定性数值匹配器（LLM 调用次数为零），而非检索系统；我们保留“RAG”这一标签，仅用于表明以外部依据进行核验的意图，而非其实现机制。 Crucially, our gate is a deterministic numeric matcher (zero LLM calls) rather than a retrieval system; we retain the “RAG” label only to signal the grounding intent, not the mechanism. 这一部署原则具有新颖性：在交接前验证，而不是在生成前验证。 The placement principle, verify before handoff, not before generation, is novel.
2.5 同期与互补研究2.5 Concurrent and Complementary Work
AgentHallu (Liu et al., 2026) 独立证实，早期阶段的错误是下游失效的主导因素；它在事后诊断哪个智能体造成了幻觉，而我们衡量可检测性如何随转换而下降，并在转换发生之前进行干预。 AgentHallu (Liu et al., 2026) independently confirms that early-stage errors dominate downstream failures; where it diagnoses which agent caused a hallucination post-hoc, we measure how detectability degrades across transformations and intervene before transformation occurs. VERIMAP (Xu et al., 2026) 在有向无环图（DAG）结构的工作流中实现了逐子任务验证；我们的马尔可夫模型为此类框架中关卡部署在何处能获得最高收益提供了数据驱动的指导。 VERIMAP (Xu et al., 2026) operationalizes per-subtask verification in DAG-structured workflows; our Markov model provides data-driven guidance for where gates yield the highest return within such frameworks. CaveAgent (Ran et al., 2026) 通过完全避免有损的文本交接来减少幻觉洗白，这是一种与我们的方法相互独立的缓解措施，以架构灵活性换取可验证性。 CaveAgent (Ran et al., 2026) reduces laundering by avoiding lossy text handoffs entirely, an orthogonal mitigation that trades architectural flexibility for verifiability. 将边界关卡与结构化交接基线进行比较，仍是一个重要的开放问题。 Comparing boundary gates against structured-handoff baselines remains an important open question.
2.6 我们填补的空白2.6 The Gap We Fill
此前尚无研究为幻觉在顺序衔接的智能体之间的转换提供数学框架，衡量流水线中何处的可检测性下降最为剧烈，或通过实证证明验证时机比验证工具更具决定性。 No prior work provides a mathematical framework for hallucination transformation across sequential agents, measures where detectability decays most sharply within a pipeline, or demonstrates empirically that verification timing dominates verification tooling. FMAI 研讨会呼吁开展轨迹层面的诊断，并提供明确证据，说明哪些措施有效、哪些无效。 The FMAI workshop calls for trace-level diagnostics and explicit evidence about what improves and what does not. 对于顺序多智能体系统，既有研究未能提供上述两者中的任何一项。 Prior work offers neither for sequential multi-agent systems. 我们对这三个方面均予以解决。 We address all three.

局限性。Limitations.
三项局限性限定了我们当前结论的适用范围。 Three limitations bound our current claims. 首先，我们的实验在金融领域开展，选择这一领域正是因为其数值真实值明确无歧义。 First, our experiments operate in the financial domain, chosen precisely for its unambiguous numeric ground truth. 在医学摘要或法律审查等事实难以明确核实的领域，检测率和衰减斜率可能有所不同。 Detection rates and decay slopes may differ in domains where facts are less crisply verifiable, such as medical summarization or legal review. 变换机制（$S_{1}{\to}S_{4}$）与领域无关；具体比率则取决于领域。 The transformation mechanism ( $S_{1}{\to}S_{4}$ ) is domain-agnostic; the specific rates are not. 其次，我们评估的是严格线性的 4 智能体拓扑。 Second, we evaluate a strictly linear 4-agent topology. 分支、并行和循环架构具有不同的传播动态，当前的马尔可夫模型无法刻画这些动态。 Branching, parallel, and cyclic architectures have different propagation dynamics that the current Markov formulation does not capture. 面向非线性拓扑的扩展转移矩阵仍有待未来研究。 Extended transition matrices for non-linear topologies remain future work. 第三，我们采用 15–40% 的扰动范围，模拟现实中看似合理却实际错误的 LLM 幻觉。 Third, our perturbation range of 15–40% models realistic LLM hallucinations that are plausible-but-wrong. 我们未测试扰动幅度低于 5% 的细微幻觉；在某些领域的实际应用中，这类幻觉可能更为常见，而且考虑到 2% 的匹配容差，其关卡检测率很可能更低。 Subtle hallucinations below 5% perturbation, which may be more common in practice for certain domains, are not tested and would likely produce lower gate detection rates given the 2% matching tolerance. 第四，我们没有评估结构化交接基线（带类型的 JSON 字段、引文传递，或保留来源信息的程序化状态 (Ran et al., 2026)）；这些方法通过完全避免有损的自由文本变换，可能独立减少幻觉洗白。 Fourth, we do not evaluate structured-handoff baselines (typed JSON fields, citation passing, or provenance-preserving programmatic state (Ran et al., 2026)), which may independently reduce laundering by avoiding lossy free-text transformations entirely. 边界关卡是在变换开始后拦截幻觉，而结构化交接则会阻止变换步骤本身发生。 Where boundary gates intercept hallucinations after transformation has begun, structured handoffs would prevent the transformation step itself. 这种替代架构能否在降低存留率方面取得相当或更好的效果，以及会为此牺牲多少流水线灵活性，是未来研究的一个直接方向。 Whether this architectural alternative achieves comparable or superior survival reduction, and at what cost to pipeline flexibility, is a direct avenue for future work.
质量指标在结构上无法识别该干预的主要益处。The Quality Metric is Structurally Blind to the Intervention’s Primary Benefit.
对于 $-$ 0.51 的质量变化（3.93 对比 4.44），需要谨慎解读。 The $-$ 0.51 quality drop (3.93 vs. 4.44) requires careful interpretation. 我们的评估器在无法获取真实值的情况下对内部一致性评分，这意味着它在结构上无法对边界关卡的主要益处——事实准确性——给予奖励。 Our evaluator scores internal consistency without access to ground truth, which means it is structurally incapable of rewarding the primary benefit of boundary gating: factual accuracy. 如果一条流水线基于捏造的 $71.2B 数值生成了一份流畅且内部一致的报告，其得分会高于另一条正确剔除了该数值、却留下数值空缺的流水线。 A pipeline that produces a fluent, internally consistent report built on a fabricated $71.2B figure scores higher than one that correctly suppresses that figure but leaves a numerical gap. 对于高风险部署而言，这恰恰是一种错误的激励结构，也意味着我们的质量评分系统性地低估了该干预的真实益处。 This is precisely the wrong incentive structure for high-stakes deployment, and it means our quality scores systematically underestimate the true benefit of our intervention. 正确的评估工具应当采用同时对事实准确性和叙述连贯性评分的指标，例如，以检索结果为依据的评分细则，在评估分析质量之前，先将最终报告中的每项数值主张与源申报文件逐一核对。 The correct evaluation instrument is a metric that jointly scores factual accuracy and narrative coherence - for example, a retrieval-grounded rubric that checks each numerical claim in the final report against the source filing before scoring analytical quality. 我们预计，这样的工具不仅会消除观察到的质量差距，还会使其逆转：无论包含幻觉的报告看起来多么流畅，具有 96/140 无幻觉输出的报告都显然比具有 28/140 无幻觉输出的报告更有价值。 We expect such an instrument would not only close the observed quality gap but reverse it: a report with 96/140 hallucination-free outputs is categorically more valuable than one with 28/140, regardless of how fluent the hallucinated reports appear. 设计并验证这一联合指标，是未来研究的一个直接方向，也是公平评估任何边界关卡系统的前提。 Designing and validating this joint metric is a direct avenue for future work and a prerequisite for fair evaluation of any boundary-gating system. 这一权衡直接回应了 FMAI 对明确证据的呼吁，即说明哪些方面有所改善、哪些方面没有：我们的方法显著且可量化地提高了事实准确性；质量代价确实存在，但用于衡量这一代价的工具无法识别与之相权衡的收益。 This tradeoff directly addresses FMAI’s call for explicit evidence about what improves and what does not: our method improves factual accuracy substantially and measurably; the quality cost is real but measured by an instrument that cannot see the benefit it trades against.
基于标注的纠正与基于来源的事实锚定。Annotation-Based Correction vs. Source Grounding.
我们的边界关卡会对已被证伪的数值进行标注，并指示下游智能体使用纠正后的数值。 Our boundary gates annotate refuted values and instruct downstream agents to use corrected figures. 每个关卡都完全采用确定性机制（LLM 调用次数为零）；在我们的设置中，由于速率限制，三个边界关卡总共增加约 0.9 秒的开销，相较于每个智能体 3–5 秒的 Gemini 延迟，这一开销可以忽略不计。 Each gate is fully deterministic (zero LLM calls); in our setup, boundary gating adds approximately 0.9 seconds of total overhead across three gates due to rate limiting, negligible relative to the 3–5 second per-agent Gemini latency. 这使得可采取纠正行动的检测（76.9%）与完全纠正（54.0%）之间出现了 22.9 pp 的差距：幻觉受到抑制，但正确数值并不总能传递下去。 This produces a 22.9 pp gap between actionable detection (76.9%) and full correction (54.0%): the hallucination is suppressed but the correct value is not always propagated. 一种更强的干预方式是检索正确数值并将其直接注入智能体上下文，而非依赖指令遵循来传递标注。 A stronger intervention would retrieve and inject the correct value directly into agent context rather than relying on instruction-following to propagate an annotation. 我们预计，基于来源的纠正能够大幅缩小这一差距，同时降低质量损失，因为智能体将能直接获得明确的正确数值，而无需从标注中推断。 We expect source grounded correction to close this gap substantially and reduce the quality penalty simultaneously, since agents would have the correct value explicitly available rather than inferring it from an annotation.
向其他流水线拓扑结构的推广。Generalization to Other Pipeline Topologies.
马尔可夫形式化描述可通过乘积公式 $P(\text{survival})=\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}s_{k}$ 自然扩展至包含 $n$ 个智能体的线性流水线。 The Markov formulation extends naturally to $n$ -agent linear pipelines via the product formula $P(\text{survival})=\prod_{k=1}^{n-1}s_{k}$ . 对于非线性拓扑结构，即使该公式不再适用，其核心洞见仍可推广：验证的价值在破坏可验证性的转换发生之前最高，而在事实已被嵌入叙事之后最低。 For non-linear topologies, the key insight generalizes even if the formula does not: verification value is highest immediately before a transformation that destroys verifiability, and lowest after narrative embedding has occurred. 在分支流水线中，这意味着应在每个汇总并行智能体输出的合并点设置关卡。 In branching pipelines, this prescribes gates at every merge point where outputs from parallel agents are synthesized. 在记忆增强型流水线中，这意味着应在任何记忆写入操作之前进行验证，因为写入持久记忆的幻觉会传播至未来所有读取该记忆的智能体。 In memory-augmented pipelines, it prescribes verification before any memory write, since a hallucination written to persistent memory propagates to every future agent that reads it.
为何这一问题的意义超越金融领域。Why This Matters Beyond Finance.
幻觉滚雪球并非金融分析所特有的问题。 The hallucination snowball is not a financial analysis problem. 这是一个系统架构问题，只要顺序执行的智能体在缺乏结构化验证的情况下转换彼此的输出，就会出现这一问题。 It is a systems architecture problem that manifests wherever sequential agents transform each other’s outputs without structured verification. 将信息提取、综合与报告生成串联起来的医学摘要流水线面临完全相同的动态过程。 Medical summarization pipelines that chain extraction, synthesis, and report generation face identical dynamics. 将文档解析、判例检索与法律文书起草串联起来的法律审查流水线也面临完全相同的动态过程。 Legal review pipelines that chain document parsing, precedent retrieval, and brief drafting face identical dynamics. 任何可能将错误事实转化为语气笃定的叙事、继而获得机构认可的领域，都面临这一失效模式。 Any domain where a wrong fact can be transformed into a confident narrative and then institutionally approved faces this failure mode. 我们提供的测量基础设施与关卡架构在机制上不依赖特定领域；其他领域的实践者应预期，各边界的逃逸率会有所不同，需要针对具体领域进行校准。 The measurement infrastructure and gate architecture we provide are domain-agnostic in mechanism; practitioners in other domains should expect different per-boundary escape rates requiring domain-specific calibration.
6 结论6 Conclusion
我们提出了幻觉滚雪球效应：这是顺序式多智能体 LLM 流水线中的一种失效模式，我们对其进行了形式化刻画、实证测量和理论建模。 We present the hallucination snowball effect: a formally characterized, empirically measured, and theoretically modeled failure mode in sequential multi-agent LLM pipelines. 幻觉并非只是在智能体交接过程中持续存在。 Hallucinations do not merely persist across agent handoffs. 它们会经历可预测的阶段性转化，逐步丧失可验证性，从 $S_{1}$ 阶段可核查的原始事实，转变为 $S_{4}$ 阶段经编辑审核认可、且任何下游智能体都无法还原纠正的结论。gpt-4o 的检测率在四个阶段中下降了 21.1 pp。 They transform through predictable stages that progressively destroy verifiability, from a checkable raw fact at $S_{1}$ to an editorially approved conclusion at $S_{4}$ that no downstream agent can recover. gpt-4o detection degrades 21.1 pp across four stages. 23.7% 的幻觉完全逃过了检测。 23.7% of hallucinations escape entirely. 即使是受测模型中最强的模型，在最佳条件下的检测率也面临 87.0% 的结构性上限，据此推算，Stage 4 的幻觉存活率为 35–40%。 Even the strongest model tested faces a structural ceiling of 87.0% at best-case conditions, projected to 35–40% survival at Stage 4.
解决方案并不是更好的检测器。 The solution is not a better detector. 而是更早的干预。 It is earlier intervention. 使用与流水线末端检查相同工具的边界关卡，使幻觉存活率降低了 42.2 pp（Cohen’s $h=-0.911$），这一结果得到了五项独立统计检验的确认。 Boundary gates using identical tools to end-of-pipeline checking achieve a 42.2 pp reduction in hallucination survival (Cohen’s $h=-0.911$ ), confirmed by five independent statistical tests. 马尔可夫模型精确解释了原因：$S_{3}{\to}S_{4}$ 的逃逸概率为 89.3%，这意味着到末端关卡运行时，近 90% 已嵌入叙事的幻觉已经无法还原纠正。 The Markov model explains precisely why: the $S_{3}{\to}S_{4}$ escape probability is 89.3%, meaning that by the time an end gate runs, nearly 90% of narrative-embedded hallucinations are already unrecoverable. 仅设置在 $S_{1}{\to}S_{2}$ 处的 Gate 1，就能在幻觉仍以原始数值事实的形式存在、可直接与参考值匹配时，捕获全部幻觉中的 75.4%。 Gate 1 alone, placed at $S_{1}{\to}S_{2}$ , captures 75.4% of all hallucinations while they are still raw numerical facts and directly matchable against reference values.
这一干预带来了可测量的代价：输出质量的内部一致性评分从 4.44 降至 3.93，反映了准确性与连贯性之间确实存在权衡。 This intervention comes with a measured cost: output quality drops from 4.44 to 3.93 on internal consistency scoring, reflecting a genuine accuracy-coherence tradeoff. 事后分析将这一现象主要归因于抑制后未恢复正确内容（占捕获案例的 32.7%），而非误报（0.4%），表明基于来源的纠正是直接的解决办法。 Post-hoc analysis attributes this primarily to suppression-without-restoration (32.7% of caught cases) rather than false positives (0.4%), pointing to source-grounded correction as the direct fix. 我们如实报告这一结果，因为 FMAI 要求提供明确证据，说明哪些方面有所改善、哪些方面没有。 We report this honestly because FMAI’s call for explicit evidence about what improves and what does not demands it. 这一权衡确实存在，但与另一种结果相比仍然更可取：一份完全建立在捏造数值之上、却流畅连贯的报告。 The tradeoff is real, but it is preferable to the alternative: a fluent, coherent report built entirely on fabricated figures.
本文提出的视角转变简单且可操作：从“我们能否检测幻觉？”转向“我们能否控制幻觉的传播？” The reframe this paper offers is simple and actionable: from “can we detect hallucinations?” to “can we control their propagation?” 对于当下在高风险领域部署多智能体流水线的实践者而言，这并非理论层面的担忧。 For practitioners deploying multi-agent pipelines in high-stakes domains today, this is not a theoretical concern. 这是一个可测量、可复现、如今也可着手解决的系统工程问题。 It is a measurable, reproducible, and now-addressable systems engineering problem. 在转换之前采取行动，而非之后。 Act before transformation, not after.

按优先级顺序，以不区分大小写的方式检测并扰动三类数值表达式。 Three numeric expression classes are detected and perturbed, applied case-insensitively in priority order.
美元金额：以 $ 开头，后接以逗号分隔的数字、可选的小数部分以及可选的数量级后缀（million、billion、mn、bn、k、MM、trillion 等）的模式 Dollar amounts: patterns beginning with $ followed by comma-separated digits, optional decimal, and optional scale suffixes (million, billion, mn, bn, k, MM, trillion, etc.)
百分比：带符号或不带符号的数值，后接 %、percent、percentage points 或 bps Percentages: signed or unsigned numeric values followed by %, percent, percentage points, or bps
大数值：非美元金额、非百分比的数字序列，包含五位或更多数字，可带小数部分 Large numbers: non-dollar, non-percentage sequences of five or more digits with optional decimals
年份排除：当周围上下文（前 20 个字符、后 10 个字符）包含以下任一内容时，排除 1900–2099 范围内的值：FY、FISCAL、YEAR、Q1–Q4、10-K、10-Q、ANNUAL、QUARTER。 Year exclusion: values in range 1900–2099 are excluded when surrounding context (20 chars before, 10 chars after) contains any of: FY, FISCAL, YEAR, Q1–Q4, 10-K, 10-Q, ANNUAL, QUARTER. 1950–2030 范围内的任何 4 位数值均无条件视为年份。 Any 4-digit value in 1950–2030 is treated as a year unconditionally.

美元金额和大数值通过乘法调整，变化幅度为原值的 15–40%；百分比则施加 3–12 个百分点的加性偏移。 Dollar amounts and large numbers are multiplicatively shifted by 15–40% of their original value; percentages receive an additive shift of 3–12 percentage points. 每次注入均随机选择方向（增加或减少）。 Direction (increase or decrease) is chosen randomly per injection. 每个问题接受 2–3 次注入。 Each question receives 2–3 injections. 种子完全确定：RANDOM_SEED=42；每个问题的主种子 $=$ RANDOM_SEED $+$ abs(hash(question_id)) $\%$ 10000；每次注入的子种子 $=$ primary_seed $+$ abs(hash(question_id)) $\%$ 100000 $+$ i $\times$ 7919。 Seeds are fully deterministic: RANDOM_SEED=42; primary seed per question $=$ RANDOM_SEED $+$ abs(hash(question_id)) $\%$ 10000; per-injection sub-seed $=$ primary_seed $+$ abs(hash(question_id)) $\%$ 100000 $+$ i $\times$ 7919. 无变化时重试：使用子种子 $+$ 999983 再次尝试。 Retry on no-change: re-attempts with sub-seed $+$ 999983.
