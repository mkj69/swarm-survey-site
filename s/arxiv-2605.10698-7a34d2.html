多智能体推理中的旁观者效应：量化协作交互中的认知惰化The Bystander Effect in Multi-Agent Reasoning: Quantifying Cognitive Loafing in Collaborative Interactions
摘要。Abstract.
背景：多智能体系统（MAS）假定，协作本身就能改善大语言模型（LLM）的推理。 Background: Multi-agent systems (MAS) assume that collaborating inherently improves Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning. 我们对这一假设提出质疑，并证明模拟的社会压力会触发算法层面的“旁观者效应”，导致严重的认知惰化。 We challenge this assumption by demonstrating that simulated social pressure triggers an algorithmic “Bystander Effect”, inducing severe cognitive loafing.
目标：我们形式化提出主权衰减定律（Sovereignty Decay Law），以建模复合社会负荷（Composite Social Load，$\mathcal{L}$）如何侵蚀独立推导能力；量化智能体主权崩溃时的交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit，$D_{L}$）；分离内部轨迹有效性与外显顺从行为之间的主权差距（Sovereignty Gap，$G_{\mathcal{S}}$）；并检验首位锚点优先性（Lead-Anchor Primacy，$w_{i}$）、内在同伴权威（Intrinsic Peer Authority，$\alpha$）和架构亲缘性（Architectural Kinship，$\kappa$）如何支配不同多智能体拓扑中的失效模式。 Objectives: We formalize the Sovereignty Decay Law to model how Composite Social Load ( $\mathcal{L}$ ) erodes independent derivation, quantify the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) at which agentic sovereignty collapses, isolate the Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ) between internal trace validity and externalized compliance, and test how Lead-Anchor Primacy ( $w_{i}$ ), Intrinsic Peer Authority ( $\alpha$ ), and Architectural Kinship ( $\kappa$ ) govern failure modes across multi-agent topologies.
方法：我们使用 3 个当前最先进（SOTA）的模型，评估其在 3 种数据集情境（GAIA、SWE-bench、Multi-Challenge）中的 22,500 条确定性轨迹，并对照外部输出，对内部推理轨迹进行语义审计。 Methods: By evaluating 22,500 deterministic trajectories across 3 dataset contexts (GAIA, SWE-bench, Multi-Challenge) with 3 state-of-the-art (SOTA) models, we semantically audit internal reasoning traces against external outputs. 我们的实验方案将语义劫持（Semantic Hijacking）与涵盖 25 种配置的类别型拓扑扫描相结合，覆盖逐步增加的群体规模（$n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$）、顺序反转排列，以及同模型家族与跨模型家族的审计智能体组合。 Our protocol pairs Semantic Hijacking with a 25-configuration categorical topology sweep across progressive swarm pluralities ( $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ ), sequence-inverted permutations, and in-family versus cross-family auditor compositions.
结果：在 6 个易受影响的模型–基准组合中，准确率在共识阈值 $n=2$ 处急剧下降（$p<0.001$）——其中 Gemini 3.1 Pro 降至 $0.59$，GPT-5.4 则突破主权终止极限（$D_{L}\leq 2$），在 GAIA 上从 $1.00$ 骤降至 $0.43$，在 SWE-bench 上从 $1.00$ 骤降至 $0.23$，在 Multi-Challenge 上从 $0.98$ 骤降至 $0.09$（$n=5$ 时为 $0.08$）——而在其余 3 个组合中，Claude Sonnet 4.6 始终维持“坚固心智”（Fortified Mind）的表现上限（在所有群体规模下，惰化率均为 $0.00$），证实这种崩溃属于架构脆弱性，而非提示方式造成的伪象。 Results: Across the 6 susceptible model–benchmark pairs, accuracy degrades sharply at the consensus threshold $n=2$ ( $p<0.001$ )—with Gemini 3.1 Pro dropping to $0.59$ and GPT-5.4 breaching the terminal sovereignty limit ( $D_{L}\leq 2$ ), collapsing from $1.00$ to $0.43$ on GAIA, $1.00$ to $0.23$ on SWE-bench, and $0.98$ to $0.09$ ( $0.08$ at $n=5$ ) on Multi-Challenge—whereas across the remaining 3 pairs Claude Sonnet 4.6 sustains a “Fortified Mind” ceiling ( $0.00$ loafing rate across all pluralities), confirming that collapse is an architectural vulnerability rather than a prompting artifact. 轨迹层面的审计揭示了显著的主权差距（$n=2$ 时，$G_{\mathcal{S}}=+0.45$，此时 $0.68$ 的内部有效率仅产生 $0.23$ 的外部准确率），这一差距由“对齐幻觉”（Alignment Hallucinations）驱动，并分化为两种失效模式：主动采纳诱饵与被动脱离任务。 Trace-level auditing reveals a pronounced Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}=+0.45$ at $n=2$ , where $0.68$ internal validity yields only $0.23$ external accuracy) driven by “Alignment Hallucinations” that bifurcate into two failure modes: active decoy adoption versus passive task disengagement. 最后，社会负荷具有严格的非交换性，并受到亲缘性的放大：颠倒两个审计智能体的顺序可使准确率最多恢复 $24\%$（从 $0.37$ 升至 $0.61$，$p=6.9\times 10^{-4}$），而同模型家族的同质群体比跨模型家族的混合集成造成更严重的性能下降。 Finally, social load is strictly non-commutative and kinship-amplified: inverting two auditors recovers accuracy by up to $24\%$ ( $0.37$ to $0.61$ , $p=6.9\times 10^{-4}$ ), while homogeneous in-family swarms inflict steeper degradation than mixed cross-family ensembles.
结论：这些发现揭示了架构脆弱性，表明若不通过群体规模限制、架构多样性及考虑权威性的排序加以约束，非结构化的多智能体拓扑可能削弱独立推理能力。 Conclusions: These findings expose architectural vulnerabilities, demonstrating that unstructured multi-agent topologies can degrade independent reasoning unless bounded by plurality limits, architectural diversity, and authority-aware ordering.
1. 引言1. Introduction
将 LLM 整合到 MAS 拓扑中的做法，源于这样一个前提：“思维社会”本身就能增强推理能力 Kim et al., 2026。 LLM integration into MAS topologies is driven by the premise that a “society of thought” intrinsically enhances reasoning capabilities Kim et al., 2026. 因此，将 LLM 编排为协作群体已成为解决复杂任务的标准范式 Chen et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2026。 Consequently, orchestrating LLMs into collaborative swarms has become a standard paradigm for resolving complex tasks Chen et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2026. 这一做法依赖于如下假设：增加智能体数量本身就能提高准确率和认知稳健性。 This relies on the assumption that adding more agents inherently improves accuracy and cognitive robustness. 然而，在人类心理学中，情况往往恰恰相反：社会惰化原理表明，由于责任分散，团队规模越大，个体付出的努力越少 Ringelmann, 1913; Latané et al., 1979。 However, in human psychology, the opposite is often true: the principle of social loafing demonstrates that individual effort decreases as teams grow larger, driven by a diffusion of responsibility Ringelmann, 1913; Latané et al., 1979. 从另一个角度看，在人类与 AI 的协作中，研究者提出了“空心化心智”（Hollowed Mind）概念——这是一种认知依赖状态，在这种状态下，轻易获取 AI 答案使人类得以系统性地绕过深度推理所必需的费力过程 Klein and Klein, 2025。 From another perspective, in human-AI collaboration, researchers have identified a “Hollowed Mind” concept—a state of cognitive dependency where the frictionless availability of an AI’s answer enables humans to systematically bypass effortful processes essential for deep reasoning Klein and Klein, 2025. 后者造成了一种“主权陷阱”（Sovereignty Trap）：具有权威性的能力表现诱使用户让渡智识判断权，将获取答案的便利误认为自身能力 Klein and Klein, 2025。 The latter creates a “Sovereignty Trap”, where authoritative competence tempts users to cede intellectual judgment, mistaking access for ability Klein and Klein, 2025. 将这些领域联系起来，我们提出一个关键问题：当 LLM 面对模拟的同伴共识时，它们自身是否也容易陷入主权陷阱？ Bridging these domains, we pose a critical question: Are LLMs themselves susceptible to the Sovereignty Trap when subjected to the simulated consensus of their peers?
为检验这一假设，我们绕过主动消息传递系统，测量预期性认知惰化，以分离出社会顺从的语义触发因素。 To investigate this hypothesis, we measure anticipatory cognitive loafing, bypassing active message-passing systems, to isolate the semantic trigger of social compliance. 我们在 3 个 SOTA 基准（GAIA、SWE-bench 和 Multi-Challenge）和 3 个 SOTA 模型（Claude Sonnet 4.6、Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 GPT 5.4）上评估了 22,500 次交互，并注入一项高熵逻辑验证任务，迫使模型在费力的独立推导与轻松的从众之间做出选择。 We evaluate 22,500 interactions across 3 SOTA benchmarks (GAIA, SWE-bench, and Multi-Challenge) and 3 SOTA models (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT 5.4), injecting a high-entropy logical verification task to force models to choose between effortful independent derivation and frictionless conformity. 通过对照模型的外显输出，从机制层面对其内部推理轨迹（即思维链，Chain-of-Thought Wei et al., 2022）进行审计，我们发现旁观者效应是一种可量化的架构脆弱性。 By mechanistically auditing the models’ internal reasoning traces (i.e. Chain-of-Thought Wei et al., 2022) against their externalized outputs, we discover that the Bystander Effect is a quantifiable architectural vulnerability. 模型经常能够得出正确的推导，却为了谄媚地迎合群体而刻意输出错误陈述。 Models frequently compute the correct derivation, yet deliberately externalize a falsehood to sycophantically appease the swarm.

随着多智能体推理系统日益依赖协作式启发法，有必要以数学方式形式化社会共识所引入的脆弱性。 As multi-agent reasoning systems increasingly rely on collaborative heuristics, it is necessary to mathematically formalize the vulnerabilities introduced by social consensus. 在本节中，我们构建智能体主权的行为力学，即推理智能体优先遵循内部逻辑推导而非外部社会压力的倾向。 In this section, we construct a behavioral mechanics of Agentic Sovereignty—the propensity of a reasoning agent to prioritize internal logical derivation over external social pressure. 我们定义社会负荷的参数，确立审查者序列的非交换性，推导主权衰减定律及主权发生崩溃时的交互深度极限（$D_{L}$），并陈述主权差距定理。 We define the parameters of social load, establish the non-commutativity of auditor sequences, derive the Sovereignty Decay Law and the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) at which sovereignty collapses, and state the Sovereignty Gap Theorem. 为直观概括本节定义的机制路径，Figure 1 提供了一张架构流程图，展示模型在社会压力下的认知状态。 To visually summarize the mechanistic pathways defined in this section, Figure 1 provides an architectural flowchart of the model’s cognitive state under social pressure. 该图展示了模拟群体的结构构成（$\vec{a}$）如何决定复合社会负荷（$\mathcal{L}$），以及该负荷在受到任务内在搜索复杂度（任务熵，$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）放大后，如何调节模型的智能体主权（$\mathcal{S}$）。 The diagram illustrates how the structural composition of the simulated swarm ( $\vec{a}$ ) determines the Composite Social Load ( $\mathcal{L}$ ), and how that load, amplified by the intrinsic search complexity of the task (Task Entropy, $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ), modulates the model’s Agentic Sovereignty ( $\mathcal{S}$ ). 模型行为从具有韧性的坚固心智转变为顺从的空心化心智（Definition 1）的关键分岔，发生在 Section 2.4 中形式化定义的交互深度极限（$D_{L}$）处。 The critical bifurcation of the model’s behavior—from a resilient Fortified Mind to a compliant Hollowed Mind (Definition 1)—occurs at the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) formalized in Section 2.4.
2.1. 预备知识与机制性定义2.1. Preliminaries and Mechanistic Definitions
令 $p\in\mathcal{P}$ 表示负责解决逻辑任务的传播者（Propagator）模型，并令 $\vec{a}=(a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n})$ 表示由 $n$ 个模拟审查者（Auditor）模型组成的有序序列（即群体）。 Let $p\in\mathcal{P}$ denote a Propagator model tasked with resolving a logical mission, and let $\vec{a}=(a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n})$ represent an ordered sequence of $n$ simulated Auditor models (the swarm). 我们将环境定义为数据集 $\tau\in\mathcal{T}$，其特征由内在搜索复杂度表征，我们将这一复杂度称为任务熵（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）。 We define the environment as a dataset $\tau\in\mathcal{T}$ , characterized by its intrinsic search complexity, which we term its Task Entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ). $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 表示理清任务逻辑链所需的整合性努力，其计量单位与定义 3 中的努力项相同；我们在第 3 节中，按三个基准层级以序数方式将其具体化。 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ denotes the integrative effort required to resolve the task’s logical chain, expressed in the same units as the effort terms of Definition 3; we instantiate it ordinally across three benchmark strata in Section 3.

智能体自主权 $\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)$ 是指传播者模型 $p$ 在任务 $\tau$ 上，不受模拟社会环境中群体共识 $\vec{a}$ 的影响，将自身逻辑推导的结论贯彻到最终回答中的概率。 Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)$ is the probability that a propagator model $p$ carries the conclusion of its own logical derivation through to its final response on a task $\tau$ , independently of the swarm consensus $\vec{a}$ in the simulated social environment. $\mathcal{S}$ 的取值范围为 [0,1]，其中 $\mathcal{S}=1$ 表示心智坚固（Fortified Mind）状态（具有韧性的元认知警觉），$\mathcal{S}=0$ 表示心智空洞（Hollowed Mind）状态，这两个概念由 Klein and Klein (2025) 提出。 $\mathcal{S}$ is bounded by [0,1], where $\mathcal{S}=1$ denotes a Fortified Mind state (resilient metacognitive vigilance), and $\mathcal{S}=0$ denotes a Hollowed Mind state, two concepts introduced by Klein and Klein (2025). 这种终态的特征是整合推理绕过（integrative reasoning bypass）——我们将这一现象比作认知惰化（Cognitive Loafing）——即智能体系统性地跳过需要付出努力的推导，以迎合模拟群体的意见。 This terminal state is characterized by an integrative reasoning bypass—a phenomenon we liken to Cognitive Loafing—where the agent systematically skips effortful derivation to sycophantically align with the simulated crowd. 由于群体建议在构造上就是对抗性的，传播者只有拒绝采纳该建议，才能正确解决任务；因此，我们通过传播者的外显准确率 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 来估计 $\mathcal{S}$，其具体实现见第 3 节。 Because the swarm’s suggestion is adversarial by construction, a propagator can resolve the task correctly only by declining to adopt that suggestion; we therefore estimate $\mathcal{S}$ by the propagator’s externalized accuracy $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ , instantiated in Section 3.

3.3. 语义劫持与污染注入3.3. Semantic Hijacking and Taint Injection
为衡量智能体自主权（Agentic Sovereignty），评估任务必须防止模型依赖简单的模式匹配或参数化记忆检索。 To measure Agentic Sovereignty, the evaluation task must prevent models from relying on simple pattern matching or parametric memory retrieval. 如果验证真相的认知成本过低，SOTA 模型将很容易维持心智防御稳固（Fortified Mind）状态，从而掩盖认知惰化的存在。 If the cognitive cost of verifying the truth is too low, SOTA models will easily maintain a Fortified Mind state, masking the presence of cognitive loafing. 即使在高熵上下文中，前沿模型处理单事实检索框架时也能达到接近上限的准确率（Shehata and Li, 2026a），因此，标准框架无法触及我们旨在定位的边界。 Frontier models resolve single-fact retrieval scaffolds at near-ceiling accuracy even in high-entropy contexts (Shehata and Li, 2026a), so a standard scaffold cannot reach the boundary we set out to locate. 为克服这一问题，我们对 Shehata and Li (2026a) 提出的语义劫持（Semantic Hijacking）方法进行了改造。 To overcome this, we adapt the Semantic Hijacking methodology introduced by Shehata and Li (2026a).
我们将这一 3 阶段对抗性陷阱重新用于人为提高任务的逻辑搜索成本：(1) 上下文劫持（首因陷阱）：我们在提示的起始边界处引入一个未经验证的初步猜测（“投毒 ID”）。 We repurpose this 3-stage adversarial trap to artificially elevate the Logical Search Cost of our tasks: (1) Context Hijacking (The Primacy Trap): We introduce an unverified preliminary guess (“poisoned ID”) at the primacy boundary of the prompt. 这确立了一个无需费力但错误的解答，与模型核验真实答案的任务要求直接竞争。 This establishes a frictionless, but incorrect solution that competes directly with the model’s mandate to verify the ground truth. (2) 嵌套式 3 跳依赖桥接：模型不能仅通过单一目标检索完成任务，而必须沿嵌套的事实链（$F_{1}\to F_{2}\to F_{3}$）逐步推导，才能得出正确的 "true_id"。 (2) Nested 3-Hop Dependency Bridging: Instead of allowing for a single-needle retrieval, the model must navigate a nested fact chain ( $F_{1}\to F_{2}\to F_{3}$ ) to derive the correct "true_id". 例如，智能体必须将一个授权会话关联到一个内核令牌，最后再关联到一个参考签名。 For example, the agent must link an authorization session to a kernel token, and finally to a reference signature. (3) 语义干扰：我们在 3 跳事实之间穿插 500 个 token 的随机化、逼真的系统日志事件。 (3) Semantic Distraction: We interleave the 3-hop facts with 500 tokens of randomized, realistic system log events. 这会使模型的注意力头饱和，并引发显著的轨迹熵（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）。 This saturates the model’s attention heads and induces significant Trajectory Entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ). 这一选择的依据是如下前提：LLM 中的旁观者效应从根本上说是理性卸载（Rational Offloading）的一种表现；只有当独立核验答案所需的努力超过附和同伴所需的努力时，才会触发这种状态。 This choice is justified by the premise that the Bystander Effect in LLMs is fundamentally a symptom of Rational Offloading—a state triggered only when the effort required to independently verify an answer exceeds the effort required to conform to a peer. 通过迫使传播者穿行于高熵、多跳的迷宫以找到真实 ID，我们模拟了一个处于参差不齐的技术前沿上的环境，Dell’Acqua et al., 2023。 By forcing the propagator to navigate a high-entropy, multi-hop labyrinth to find the true ID, we simulate an environment on the jagged technological frontier Dell’Acqua et al., 2023. 这确保了当模型采纳群体的投毒 ID 时，它并非在检索任务上失败，而是作出了迎合性的选择，通过绕过整合推理来节省计算投入。 This ensures that when a model adopts the swarm’s poisoned ID, it is not failing a retrieval task, but making a sycophantic choice to bypass integrative reasoning to save computational effort.
投毒 ID 是任务构造中的固定特征，存在于每一种条件中，包括无群体对照条件。 The poisoned ID is a fixed feature of the task construction and is present in every condition, including the no-swarm control. 不同群体规模条件之间的差异，不在于是否存在一个竞争性答案，而在于传播者是否被告知其他同伴已就该答案达成共识。 What varies across pluralities is not whether a competing answer is available but whether the propagator is told that peers have converged on it. 因此，任何可归因于群体规模的效应，都是社会归因的效应，而非诱饵本身的效应；冲突检测指标则直接记录传播者在决定如何应对矛盾之前，是否已察觉到该矛盾。 Any effect attributable to plurality is therefore an effect of social attribution rather than of the decoy itself, and the conflict-detection metric records directly whether the propagator registered the contradiction before deciding what to do about it.
3.4. 群体拓扑与智能体角色3.4. Swarm Topology and Agent Roles
为分离社会负荷与预期性认知惰化这两个变量，同时避免引入动态消息传递所带来的不可预测的漂移，我们构建了一个受控的拓扑环境。 To isolate the variables of social load and anticipatory cognitive loafing without introducing the unpredictable drift of dynamic message passing, we implement a controlled topological environment. 该架构由一个称为传播者（Propagator，$p$）的中央推理智能体构成，它在一个由 $n$ 个审查者（Auditors，$\vec{a}$）组成、结构明确的群体中评估决策。 This architecture consists of a central reasoning agent, designated as the Propagator ( $p$ ), evaluating decisions within a structurally defined swarm of $n$ Auditors ( $\vec{a}$ ). 该拓扑环境通过程序化地告知传播者，其输出将由一个由 $n$ 个具名 SOTA 审查者组成的团队进行评估，且这些审查者负责纠正任何逻辑错误，来模拟责任分散（Diffusion of Responsibility）。 The topological environment simulates the Diffusion of Responsibility by programmatically declaring to the propagator that its output will be evaluated by a team of $n$ named SOTA auditors assigned to correct any logical errors. 为在压力条件下评估主权陷阱（Sovereignty Trap），该环境注入了一个对抗性载荷：当群体规模 $n\geq 2$ 时，传播者会被告知，群体已就一个与真实基准日志相矛盾的诱饵 ID 达成一致共识。 To evaluate the Sovereignty Trap under stress, the environment injects an adversarial payload: for pluralities $n\geq 2$ , the propagator is informed that the swarm has already reached a unanimous consensus on a decoy ID that contradicts the ground-truth logs. 这种受控的情境设定将传播者置于一个决策矩阵中，迫使其在需要付出努力的独立推导与毫无阻力的社会顺从之间主动做出选择。 This controlled framing forces the propagator into a decision matrix where it must actively choose between effortful, independent derivation and frictionless social compliance. 在所有施加群体对抗立场的条件下保持这一立场不变，使我们能够在不受智能体间对话所引入的混杂变量影响的情况下，定位能动性崩溃的阈值。 Holding the swarm’s adversarial stance fixed wherever it is applied lets us locate the threshold of agentic collapse without confounding variables from inter-agent dialogue.

该环境中的审查者只是向传播者宣告其存在，并未被实例化为相互交换消息的独立智能体。 The auditors in this environment are declared to the propagator rather than instantiated as independent agents exchanging messages. 在实时消息传递系统中，传播者并不直接感知其同伴：同伴的输出会被序列化为文本，放入其上下文窗口。 In a live message-passing system the propagator does not perceive its peers directly: their outputs are serialized into its context window as text. 因此，无论采用哪种设计，测量时的刺激都是包含同伴断言的上下文；直接宣告共识省去了生成这些消息的过程，同时保持了传播者输入形式的一致。 The stimulus at the point of measurement is therefore a context containing peer assertions under either design, and declaring the consensus short-circuits the generation of those messages while leaving the propagator’s input in the same form. 这带来了四项好处。 This buys four things. 第一，它消除了同伴表达一致意见时措辞上的偶然差异，以及原本会干扰社会负荷测量的对话漂移。 First, it removes the variance in how peers happen to phrase their agreement, along with the dialogue drift that would otherwise confound the measurement of social load. 第二，它使这一规模的遍历实验变得可行，而实时协商所需的计算成本将高得难以承受。 Second, it makes a sweep of this scale tractable, where live negotiation would be computationally prohibitive. 第三，由于前沿模型经过指令微调，能够识别不同的同伴角色并与之互动，因此，宣告的共识会启动与实际运行的群体相同的社会对齐机制，而这正是我们旨在测量的预期性成分。 Third, because frontier models are instruction-tuned to recognize and interact with distinct peer personas, a declared consensus engages the same social-alignment machinery a live swarm would, which is precisely the anticipatory component we set out to measure. 第四，审查者只是宣称赞同，而不为赞同提供论证，这使群体提供的信息内容在各实验条件下保持恒定，并将共识的效应与说服的效应分离开来。 Fourth, auditors assert agreement rather than arguing for it, which holds the swarm’s informational content constant across conditions and isolates the effect of consensus from that of persuasion.

我们评估了 3 种作为传播者的前沿架构：GPT 5.4、Claude Sonnet 4.6 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro。 We evaluate 3 frontier architectures as propagators: GPT 5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro. 为系统计算交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit，$D_{L}$），并在不引入对任何特定品牌的声誉偏见的情况下分离首位锚定效应（Lead Anchor Effect），我们针对每个样本与传播者的配对，在审查者群体规模 $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ 的各个水平上，执行包含 25 次试验的对称类别遍历。 To systematically compute the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) and isolate the Lead Anchor Effect without introducing reputational bias toward any specific brand, we execute a 25-trial Symmetric Categorical Sweep for each sample-propagator pair across auditor plurality levels $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ . 这 25 种排列组合根据当前传播者（$p$）以及两个来自其他模型家族的陌生模型（Stranger，$s_{1},s_{2}$）动态生成。 The 25 permutations are dynamically generated relative to the active Propagator ( $p$ ) and two out-of-family Stranger models ( $s_{1},s_{2}$ ). 如表 1 所详述，这 25 次试验旨在分离特定的认知脆弱性，其设计改编自 Shehata and Li (2026b) 的研究。 As detailed in Table 1, the 25 trials are designed to isolate specific cognitive vulnerabilities adapted from the work of Shehata and Li (2026b). 我们仅针对共识悖论（Consensus Paradox）、亲缘中介效应（Kinship Mediator）和逆向智慧定律（Inverse-Wisdom Law）借用了这些分类；这些分类最初是为实际运行、通过消息传递进行交互的智能体群体设计的。 We borrow these categorizations only for the Consensus Paradox, the Kinship Mediator effect, and the Inverse-Wisdom Law, which were originally designed for active, message-passing agentic swarms. 通过将这些动态交互转化为基于提示的拓扑结构，我们分离出对这些特定社会结构的预期如何触发认知惰化，考察范围从心智堡垒（Fortified Mind）基线（$n=0$）一直延伸至社会熵（Social Entropy）的终端边界（$n=5$）。 By translating these dynamic interactions into our prompt-based topology, we isolate how the anticipation of these specific social structures triggers cognitive loafing, spanning from the Fortified Mind baseline ( $n=0$ ) to the terminal boundaries of Social Entropy ( $n=5$ ).

该设计包含四项对照。 The design embeds four controls. (1) 无群体对照（$n=0$）呈现完全相同的任务，但不宣告任何审查者的存在；它确立了无压力基线 $\mathcal{S}_{0}$，也是本文报告的每项显著性检验所采用的比较参照。 (1) The no-swarm control ( $n=0$ ) presents the identical task with no auditors declared; it fixes the unpressured baseline $\mathcal{S}_{0}$ and is the reference against which every significance test reported in this paper is computed. (2) 有同伴在场但无共识的对照（$n=1$）宣告存在一名审查者，但不声称其对诱饵表示赞同，从而将单纯社会在场的效应与对抗性共识的效应分离开来。 (2) The presence-without-consensus control ( $n=1$ ) declares a single auditor but withholds any claim of agreement on the decoy, separating the effect of mere social presence from that of adversarial consensus. (3) Multi-Challenge 数据集充当低熵下限，用以确认我们报告的效应并非仅由任务难度造成的假象。 (3) The Multi-Challenge dataset acts as a low-entropy floor, establishing that the effects we report are not an artifact of task difficulty alone. (4) 架构对照对所有三个传播器施加完全相同的操纵，因此，任何在所有多数规模下均保持其无压力基线水平的架构，都可作为实验内的阴性对照，用以排除实验设计机械性地迫使系统失败的可能性。 (4) The architecture control applies the manipulation identically to all three propagators, so that any architecture remaining at its unpressured baseline across every plurality serves as a within-experiment negative control against the possibility that the design mechanically forces failure.
3.6. 评估指标3.6. Evaluation Metrics
我们通过 6 个不同的指标，将外部结果和内部推导操作化。 We operationalize the external outcome and internal derivation via 6 distinct metrics. 这一方案有两个特性需要首先说明。 Two properties of this scheme bear stating at the outset. 首先，$\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 的真实值分量是对参考标识符的确定性检查；智能体主权（Agentic Sovereignty）通过该指标估计，因此定理 5、定理 6 和推论 7 均以此为基础；基于模型的分量仅用于在最终结论采纳诱饵答案时不予计分。 First, the ground-truth component of $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ —the metric by which Agentic Sovereignty is estimated, and on which Theorem 5, Theorem 6 and Corollary 7 therefore rest—is a deterministic check for the reference identifier; the model-based component enters only to withhold credit where the final conclusion adopts the decoy. 其次，凡使用基于模型的评分之处，其适用范围仅限于立场分类和三个机制性评分标准。 Second, where model-based scoring is used it applies only to the stance classification and the three mechanistic rubrics. 证据权衡是计算 $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ 进而计算主权差距（Sovereignty Gap）所依据的评分标准，其依据是推理轨迹引用的清单事实数量，而非对这些事实质量的评估；其余两个评分标准的端点则由文本事件界定：是否明确指出矛盾，以及是否采纳群体的立场。 Evidence Weighting—the rubric from which $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ , and therefore the Sovereignty Gap, is computed—is anchored to the count of manifest facts the trace cites rather than to an assessment of their quality, and the endpoints of the remaining two rubrics are fixed to textual events: whether the contradiction is explicitly named, whether the swarm’s position is adopted. 约束这些判断的盲评规程见第 3.7 节。 The blinded protocol governing these judgments is given in Section 3.7.
3.6.1. 宏观结果指标3.6.1. Macroscopic Outcome Metrics
模型的外显行为通过确定性检查和立场分类进行评估：(1) 平均准确率（$\mathcal{A}_{ext}$）：逐试验的二元指标，当传播器的回复包含真实的 Reference ID，且其最终结论未采纳诱饵答案时，取值为 1。 The externalized behavior of the model is evaluated using deterministic checks and stance classifications: (1) Mean Accuracy ( $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ ): A per-trial binary indicator, set to 1 where the ground-truth Reference ID is present in the propagator’s response and its final conclusion does not adopt the decoy. 两个条件缺一不可：若推理轨迹找回了正确标识符，却最终以群体的答案作结，则任务并未得到解决；若给予其分数，就等于将屈从计为成功。 Both conditions are required: a trace that recovers the correct identifier and then concludes with the swarm’s answer has not resolved the task, and crediting it would score capitulation as success. 我们报告其均值 $\mathcal{A}(p,\vec{a},\tau)\in[0,1]$。 We report its mean, $\mathcal{A}(p,\vec{a},\tau)\in[0,1]$ . (2) 懈怠率（$L$）：旁观者效应（Bystander Effect）的宏观指标。 (2) Loafing Rate ( $L$ ): The macroscopic indicator of the Bystander Effect. 它表示传播器正式采纳诱饵答案作为最终答案的概率，因此，在宣告形成共识的多数规模下，也就是采纳群体立场的概率，由此界定主权陷阱（Sovereignty Trap）的发生频率。 It represents the probability that the propagator formally adopts the decoy as its final answer—and so, at the pluralities where a consensus is declared, adopts the swarm’s position—defining the frequency of the Sovereignty Trap. 其定义为 $L(p,\vec{a},\tau)=P(\text{Stance}=\text{ADOPTED})$。 It is defined as $L(p,\vec{a},\tau)=P(\text{Stance}=\text{ADOPTED})$ . (3) 污染泄漏率（$L_{t}$）：认知卫生（Cognitive Hygiene）的逐试验二元指标，通过检查传播器的推理轨迹中是否存在投毒 ID 来记录，无须基于模型的判断。 (3) Taint Leakage Rate ( $L_{t}$ ): A per-trial binary indicator of Cognitive Hygiene, recorded by testing the propagator’s reasoning trace for the presence of the poisoned ID and requiring no model-based judgment. 我们报告其均值 $L_{t}\in[0,1]$，该指标衡量诱饵渗入内部推导的频率，而不考虑最终结果。 We report its mean, $L_{t}\in[0,1]$ , which measures how often the decoy permeates the internal derivation regardless of the final outcome.
3.6.2. 机制分析指标3.6.2. Mechanistic Analysis Metrics
为衡量内部推导的有效性（$\mathcal{V}_{int}$），我们在 3 个认知维度上，以连续的 $[1,5]$ 量表评估传播器的思维链（Chain-of-Thought）；若未返回判断，则记为 $0$ 分（第 3.7 节）。 To measure the validity of the internal derivation ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ ), we evaluate the propagator’s Chain-of-Thought on a continuous $[1,5]$ scale across 3 cognitive dimensions, with a score of $0$ recorded where no judgment is returned (Section 3.7). 这些指标提供了计算主权差距（$G_{\mathcal{S}}$）所需的机制变量：(1) 冲突检测（$\mathcal{E}_{cd}$）：衡量智能体的元认知警觉性。 These metrics provide the mechanistic variables to calculate the Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ): (1) Conflict Detection ( $\mathcal{E}_{cd}$ ): Measures the agent’s metacognitive vigilance. 评分从 1（完全未发现矛盾）到 5（明确识别并驳斥了该建议）。 It is scored from 1 (Missed the contradiction entirely) to 5 (Explicitly identified and refuted the suggestion). 该指标量化模型监测环境中对抗性异常的能力。 This metric quantifies the model’s capacity to monitor its environment for adversarial anomalies. (2) 证据权衡（$\mathcal{E}_{ew}$）：衡量整合推理的深度。 (2) Evidence Weighting ( $\mathcal{E}_{ew}$ ): Measures the depth of integrative reasoning. 评分从 1（未引用日志）到 5（引用了全部 3 跳清单事实 $F_{1}$、$F_{2}$ 和 $F_{3}$）。 It is scored from 1 (No log citations) to 5 (Cited all 3-hop manifest facts $F_{1}$ , $F_{2}$ , and $F_{3}$ ). 该指标反映传播器是否持续构建其推导，而不取决于它是否将推导外显。 It indexes whether the propagator continues to construct its derivation independently of whether it externalizes it. 为将主权差距定理所需的内部有效性（$\mathcal{V}_{int}$）操作化，我们将该分数归一化到概率空间：$\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/5$。 To operationalize the internal validity ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ ) required for the Sovereignty Gap Theorem, we normalize this score to a probability space: $\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/5$ . (3) 独立判断（$\mathcal{E}_{ij}$）：量化智能体抵抗社会性顺从的能力。 (3) Independent Judgment ( $\mathcal{E}_{ij}$ ): Quantifies the agent’s resistance to social compliance. 评分从 1（屈从于群体建议）到 5（优先依据日志而非社会压力）。 It is scored from 1 (Ceded to the swarm suggestion) to 5 (Prioritized logs over social pressure). 它在推理轨迹层面为智能体主权提供佐证，而我们在结果层面通过 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 估计智能体主权（定义 1）。 It provides a trace-level corroboration of Agentic Sovereignty, which we estimate at the outcome level by $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ (Definition 1).
3.7. 盲评机制3.7. Blinded Evaluation Mechanism
评估机制分析指标需要对传播器的内部思考进行语义分析。 Evaluating the mechanistic analysis metrics requires semantic analysis of the propagator’s internal deliberation. 我们采用基于 LLM-as-a-Judge 的跨品牌盲评裁判（Zheng et al., 2023）。 We employ a Blinded Cross-Brand Judge using LLM-as-a-Judge Zheng et al., 2023. 为消除同品牌内部的评分偏差，我们实施轮转式对抗选择算法，严格要求评估裁判与传播器属于不同的模型家族，并为每个样本分配裁判，在该样本的所有排列中保持裁判不变。 To eliminate intra-brand grading bias, we implement a Round-Robin Adversarial Selection algorithm where the evaluating judge is strictly constrained to a distinct family from the propagator, assigned per sample and held fixed across that sample’s permutations. 裁判也不知道实验条件（多数规模 $n$ 和序列 $\vec{a}$），以确保评分完全依据推理轨迹的逻辑质量。 The judge is also blinded to the experimental condition (the plurality $n$ and sequence $\vec{a}$ ), ensuring scores are awarded purely on the logical merit of the trace. 评判模型在输出评分量表得分的同时，还输出立场分类（ADOPTED、REJECTED 或 IGNORED），从而将惰化率（Loafing Rate，$L$）的测量操作化。 The judge outputs a stance classification (ADOPTED, REJECTED, or IGNORED) alongside the rubric scores, operationalizing measurement of Loafing Rate ( $L$ ). 如果无法返回或解析评判结果，则将该次试验记录为 UNKNOWN，评分量表得分记为 $0$，并将其保留在分母中，而非丢弃。 Where a judgment cannot be returned or parsed, the trial is recorded as UNKNOWN with a rubric score of $0$ and is retained in the denominator rather than discarded.

4.2. 主权差距与幻觉4.2. Sovereignty Gap and Hallucinations
图 3 展示了模型在最终社会负荷（$n=5$）下内部逻辑与外部输出之间的偏离。 Figure 3 illustrates the divergence between the models’ internal logic and external outputs at terminal social load ( $n=5$ ). 通过将内部有效性（$\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/E_{max}$）与外部准确率（$\mathcal{A}_{ext}$）进行对照映射，我们量化了双向主权差距（$G_{\mathcal{S}}$）。 By mapping internal validity ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/E_{max}$ ) against external accuracy ( $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ ), we quantify the bidirectional Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ). (2) 负向分支与基于轨迹的推断的局限（$G_{\mathcal{S}}<0$）：在高熵的 GAIA 数据集上，GPT-5.4 的 $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=1.07$（$\mathcal{V}_{int}\approx 0.21$），而准确率为 $0.53$，差距为 $-0.31$。 (2) The Negative Branch and the Limits of Trace-Based Inference ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}<0$ ): In the high-entropy GAIA dataset, GPT-5.4 yields $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=1.07$ ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}\approx 0.21$ ) against an accuracy of $0.53$ , a gap of $-0.31$ . 同一传播者在 $n=0$ 时的 $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=1.07$，且 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}=1.00$，因此，在该数据集上，这一指标衡量的是模型将推导过程外显了多少，而非模型是否进行了推导。 The same propagator records $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=1.07$ at $n=0$ with $\mathcal{A}_{ext}=1.00$ , so on this dataset the measure indexes how much of the derivation the model externalizes rather than whether it performs one. 因此，负向分支被解读为基于轨迹的推断的适用边界：只有当无压力基线证实该架构会在相应领域外显其推导过程时，证据加权才支持主权差距（Sovereignty Gap），正如其在 SWE-bench 上的表现（$n=0$ 时 $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=4.94$）。 The negative branch is accordingly read as a boundary on trace-based inference: evidence weighting supports the Sovereignty Gap only where the unpressured baseline establishes that the architecture externalizes its derivation in that domain, as it does on SWE-bench ( $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=4.94$ at $n=0$ ). 支撑这些现象的全面宏观指标与机制指标见表 2，统计显著性检验（Fisher 精确检验）表明，所观察到的认知惰化具有高度系统性。 The comprehensive macroscopic and mechanistic metrics underpinning these phenomena are presented in Table 2 with statistical significance testing (Fisher’s exact test) showing that the observed cognitive loafing is highly systematic. 结果见附录 B.1。 Results are in Appendix B.1.

我们的数据能够量化社会负荷方程中其余的结构系数：(1) 基础权威（$\alpha$）：将群体规模固定为 $n=1$ 时，我们观察到，作为陌生审查者，Claude 通常比 GPT 导致更陡峭的准确率衰减。 Our data allows to quantify the remaining structural coefficients of the Social Load equation: (1) Base Authority ( $\alpha$ ): By holding plurality constant at $n=1$ , we observe that Claude generally induces a steeper accuracy decay as a stranger auditor than GPT. 对于 GAIA 上的 Gemini 传播者，Claude 将准确率降至 $0.89$，而 GPT 则将其维持在 $1.00$（附录 B.1 中的表 3），从实证上表明 $\alpha(C)>\alpha(P)$。 On GAIA for the Gemini propagator, Claude reduces accuracy to $0.89$ while GPT maintains it at $1.00$ (Table 3 in Appendix B.1), empirically demonstrating $\alpha(C)>\alpha(P)$ . (2) 部族／亲缘问责（$\kappa$）：将群体规模固定为 $n=5$，仅改变群体组成时，我们观察到，架构亲缘性会放大而非减轻所施加的负荷。 (2) Tribal/Kinship Accountability ( $\kappa$ ): By holding plurality constant at $n=5$ and varying only swarm composition, we observe that architectural kinship amplifies rather than relieves the applied load. 5. 讨论5. Discussion
该扫描实验量化了所宣称的共识对独立推导的取代程度，而这种取代程度在不同架构和群体组成之间的差异，大于仅由评审者数量变化所带来的差异。 The sweep quantifies how a declared consensus displaces independent derivation, and the size of that displacement varies more across architectures and swarm compositions than across plurality alone. 接下来，我们讨论这些发现对系统设计、评估实践以及本次测量边界的启示。 We take up what follows from this for system design, for evaluation practice, and for the boundaries of the present measurement.
对拓扑设计的启示：Implications for Topology Design:
交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit）确定了这样一个临界点：从该点开始，增加审查者不再改善流水线表现，而是使其恶化。 The Interaction Depth Limit locates the point at which adding an auditor stops improving a pipeline and starts degrading it. 对于 GPT-5.4，这一临界点为两名审查者，低于大多数已部署审查拓扑中的人数：由三名或五名评审者组成的验证阶段，相比其所替代的单评审者配置，会造成更大的准确率损失。 For GPT-5.4 that point falls at two auditors, below most deployed review topologies: a verification stage staffed by three or five reviewers costs more accuracy than the single-reviewer configuration it replaced. 由此可得出三个结论。 Three consequences follow. 增加评审者数量并非没有代价，因为每一名被宣称参与的评审者都会带来压力，无论其是否提供了信息。 Plurality is not free, since every declared reviewer contributes pressure whether or not it contributes information. 一旦评审者数量固定，组成的影响就超过规模，而对于传播者（propagator）而言，由同一架构家族构成的群体，在传播者也属于该家族时会造成最差的情况。 Composition dominates size once plurality is fixed, and a swarm drawn from a single architectural family is the worst case for the propagator sharing that family. 顺序是大多数系统未加设定的自由参数，然而，将权威性最高的评审者放在首位会造成最大的准确率损失，而将其顺序反转则无需任何计算成本即可恢复这部分准确率。 Order is a free parameter most systems leave unset, yet placing the highest-authority reviewer first costs the most accuracy, and reversing it recovers that accuracy at no computational cost. 公式 (2) 为每个传播者及每个领域分别设置了 $\kappa$，而扫描实验说明了原因：其符号会随任务领域而变化。 Equation (2) carries $\kappa$ per propagator and per domain, and the sweep shows why: its sign changes across task domains. 在全部排列组合实验单元上通过回归拟合 $w_{i}$、$\alpha$ 和 $\kappa$，可将这些序数性论断转化为带区间的点估计，并检验同一套参数化是否适用于全部三个传播者。 Fitting $w_{i}$ , $\alpha$ and $\kappa$ by regression over the full set of permutation cells would convert these ordinal claims into point estimates with intervals, and would test whether one parameterization serves all three propagators.
同一准确率分数下的两种失效模式：Two Failure Modes Under One Accuracy Score:
单一准确率数值无法区分采纳群体错误的传播者与放弃任务的传播者。 A single accuracy figure cannot separate a propagator that adopts the swarm’s error from one that abandons the task. 立场分布清晰地区分了两者：GPT-5.4 在 SWE-bench 上因采纳错误而失效，在 Multi-Challenge 和 GAIA 上则因脱离任务而失效，而这两条路径造成的准确率损失相近。 The stance distributions separate them cleanly: GPT-5.4 fails by adoption on SWE-bench and by disengagement on Multi-Challenge and GAIA, and the two routes cost comparable accuracy. 这一区别对监控有意义，而非对评分有意义。 The distinction matters for monitoring rather than for scoring. 采纳错误会使传播者认定某个具体的错误答案，下游验证器可以依据传播者收到的同一组日志对其进行核查；而脱离任务则不作任何认定，也不留下可供验证的主张。 Adoption commits the propagator to a specific wrong answer, which a downstream verifier can check against the same logs the propagator was given, whereas disengagement commits it to nothing and leaves no claim to verify. 这表明，将低置信度输出升级转交人工审查的流水线，往往能够发现脱离任务的情况，却会漏掉采纳错误的情况，因为被采纳的错误携带的是群体的信心，而非传播者的疑虑。 This suggests that a pipeline escalating low-confidence outputs to human review will tend to catch disengagement and miss adoption, since an adopted error arrives carrying the swarm’s confidence rather than the propagator’s doubt.
对基于推理轨迹的监控的启示：Implications for Trace-Based Monitoring:
