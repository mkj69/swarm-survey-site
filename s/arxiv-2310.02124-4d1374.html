摘要Abstract
随着自然语言处理（NLP）系统日益广泛地应用于复杂的社会环境，一个亟待回答的问题随之浮现：在由多个大语言模型（LLMs）组成的多智能体社会中，这些 NLP 系统能否展现出类似人类的协作智能？ As Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems are increasingly employed in intricate social environments, a pressing query emerges: Can these NLP systems mirror human-esque collaborative intelligence, in a multi-agent society consisting of multiple large language models (LLMs)? 本文结合实践实验与理论洞见，探究当代 NLP 系统之间的协作机制。 This paper probes the collaboration mechanisms among contemporary NLP systems by melding practical experiments with theoretical insights. 我们构建了四个由 LLM 智能体组成的独特“社会”，其中每个智能体都具有特定的“特质”（随和或过度自信），并以不同的“思维模式”（辩论或反思）参与协作。 We fabricate four unique ‘societies’ comprised of LLM agents, where each agent is characterized by a specific ‘trait’ (easy-going or overconfident) and engages in collaboration with a distinct ‘thinking pattern’ (debate or reflection). 通过在三个基准数据集上评估这些多智能体社会，我们发现，某些协作策略不仅优于以往的顶尖方法，还能提升效率（使用更少的 API token）。 Through evaluating these multi-agent societies on three benchmark datasets, we discern that certain collaborative strategies not only outshine previous top-tier approaches but also optimize efficiency (using fewer API tokens). 此外，我们的结果进一步表明，LLM 智能体表现出从众和达成共识等类似人类的社会行为，与社会心理学的基础理论相呼应。 Moreover, our results further illustrate that LLM agents manifest human-like social behaviors, such as conformity and consensus reaching, mirroring foundational social psychology theories. 最后，我们融入社会心理学的洞见，以理解 LLM 智能体的协作，为进一步研究 LLM 的协作机制提供启发。 In conclusion, we integrate insights from social psychology to contextualize the collaboration of LLM agents, inspiring further investigations into the collaboration mechanism for LLMs. 我们已分享代码和数据集11 1 https://github.com/zjunlp/MachineSoM.，希望推动对这一前景广阔的方向的进一步研究。 We have shared our code and datasets11 1 https://github.com/zjunlp/MachineSoM., hoping to catalyze further research in this promising avenue.
1 引言1 Introduction
随着 LLMs (Zhao et al., 2023; Yin et al., 2023; Zhu et al., 2023) 日益普及并成为日常社会协作的重要组成部分，构建具备社会智能的 AI 系统变得愈发迫切。 With the prevalence of LLMs (Zhao et al., 2023; Yin et al., 2023; Zhu et al., 2023) integral to daily social collaboration, there is a growing imperative to cultivate AI systems embodied with social intelligence. 这也与心智社会（Society of Mind，SoM）概念 (Li et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023) 相呼应，该概念认为，当计算模块相互交互，实现超越单个模块能力的集体目标时，智能便会涌现 (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003)。 This also resonates with the Society of Mind (SoM) concept (Li et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023), which suggests that intelligence emerges when computational modules interact with each other, achieving collective objectives that surpass the capabilities of individual modules (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003). 先前的研究 (Park et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Akata et al., 2023) 已深入探索了让称为智能体的 LLM 实例 (Wang et al., 2024c; Xi et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023a; Cheng et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2024b) 通过协同合作（例如辩论和反思）完成任务的策略 (Du et al., 2023a; Pezeshkpour et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024)。 Previous studies (Park et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Akata et al., 2023) have delved into strategies where LLM instances, termed agents (Wang et al., 2024c; Xi et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023a; Cheng et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2024b), cooperate synergistically (e.g., debate and reflect) to accomplish tasks (Du et al., 2023a; Pezeshkpour et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024). 如图 1 所示，这种协作促进了 LLMs 的发散性思维过程，使其在需要深入反思的任务中尤为有效。 As illustrated in Figure 1, such collaboration fosters divergent thinking processes in LLMs, making them particularly effective for tasks demanding profound reflection.

直观而言，参照众多具有不同目标和角色的个体共存的人类社会 (Siegal and Varley, 2002; Leslie et al., 2004; Sap et al., 2022; Shapira et al., 2024)，SoM 框架倡导和谐的交互 (Singh, 2003)。 Intuitively, reflecting on human societies (Siegal and Varley, 2002; Leslie et al., 2004; Sap et al., 2022; Shapira et al., 2024), where a myriad of individuals with distinct goals and roles coexist, the SoM framework champions harmonious interactions (Singh, 2003). 有趣的是，尽管 SoM 将社会心理学 (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009) 与人类群体动力学 (Woolley et al., 2010; Alderfer, 1987) 相融合，揭示了社会群体内部的心理模式，但在 LLMs 领域中对其的阐释仍相对缺乏探索 (Ke et al., 2024)。 Intriguingly, despite the fusion of social psychology (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009) in SoM with human group dynamics (Woolley et al., 2010; Alderfer, 1987), which illuminates psychological patterns within social groups, its interpretation in the realm of LLMs is relatively uncharted (Ke et al., 2024). 此外，我们对社会行为如何影响 LLMs 的理解仍处于初步阶段。 Besides, our grasp of how social behaviors influence LLMs is still in its nascent stages.
为解决这些问题，我们深入研究机器社会，探究 LLMs 在多大程度上以及以何种方式展现社会智能与协作能力 (Mei et al., 2024)。 To address these issues, we delve into the machine society, probing the extent and ways that LLMs manifest social intelligence and collaboration capabilities (Mei et al., 2024). 我们利用 GPT-3.5 (OpenAI, 2022) 等强大的 LLMs，基于三个数据集构建测试平台：MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b)、MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021a) 和 Chess Move Validity (Srivastava et al., 2022)。 Utilizing powerful LLMs like GPT-3.5 (OpenAI, 2022), we build a test-bed across three datasets: MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b), MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021a) and Chess Move Validity (Srivastava et al., 2022). 我们的方法包含四个社会，每个社会由三个智能体组成，并依据两种个体特质（随和与过度自信）划分为：全员随和、大多数随和、全员过度自信、大多数过度自信。 Our approach incorporates four societies characterized by two individual traits (easy-going and overconfident) with three agents: totally/mostly easy-going; totally/mostly overconfident. 这些特质用于模拟人类社会中细微复杂的动态 (Soni et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2024a; Li et al., 2023b; Kong et al., 2023)。 These traits are employed to emulate nuanced human society dynamics (Soni et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2024a; Li et al., 2023b; Kong et al., 2023).
此外，我们深入探究多轮协作中的两种不同思维模式：辩论 (Perelman, 1971; Sunstein, 2005; Amgoud and Prade, 2009; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) 与反思 (Bogumil, 1985; Mezirow, 2003; Bolton, 2010)。 Moreover, we delve into two distinct thinking patterns under multi-round collaboration: debate (Perelman, 1971; Sunstein, 2005; Amgoud and Prade, 2009; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) and reflection (Bogumil, 1985; Mezirow, 2003; Bolton, 2010). 通过排列组合思维模式，我们可以构建多种协作策略。 With the permutation of thinking patterns, we can constitute various collaborative strategies. 为此，我们在协作策略中实现了两种协作模式：$(i)$ 每一轮中，所有智能体采用相同的思维模式；$(ii)$ 每一轮中，一个智能体采用与其他智能体不同的思维模式。 To this end, we implement two patterns of collaboration in the collaborative strategies: $(i)$ All agents adopt the same thinking pattern at each round; $(ii)$ One agents adopts the different thinking patterns from others at each round. 随后，我们在不同社会中执行这些多轮协作策略。 We then execute these multi-round collaborative strategies within different societies. 通过实证分析，我们主要得出以下见解（更多结论见 §3、§4 和附录 A）： Through our empirical analysis, we primarily discern the following insights (Further takeaways are in §3, §4 & Appendix A):
(1) 采用不同思维模式排列组合的协作策略在性能上存在显著差异，而开展实质性辩论能够提升协作性能。 (1) Collaborative strategies with various permutations of thinking patterns vary significantly in performance, and engaging in substantive debates enhances collaboration performance. 有趣的是，由具有不同特质的智能体组成的多智能体社会，在性能上并未表现出明显差异。 Intriguingly, multi-agent societies composed of agents with different traits do not clearly differ in performance.

(3) LLM 智能体表现出类似于人类社会倾向的行为，例如从众（Allen and Levine, 1969; Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004）或群体思维中的多数决原则（Seal et al., 1998），这些行为与社会心理学中的若干基本理论相呼应（Castro and Liskov, 1999; Tajfel and Turner, 2004）。 (3) LLM agents manifest behaviors reminiscent of human social tendencies, such as conformity (Allen and Levine, 1969; Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004) or the principle of majority rule in group thinking (Seal et al., 1998), which resonate with several fundamental theories in social psychology (Castro and Liskov, 1999; Tajfel and Turner, 2004).

2.1 协作的基础概念2.1 Preliminary Concepts in Collaboration
个体特质。Individual Trait.
受到智能源于众多规模较小、相对简单且各具不同特质的智能体共同努力这一观点的启发（Minsky, 1988），我们设置了两类表现出典型对立特质的智能体：随和型和过度自信型，如图 2(a) 所示。 Inspired by intelligence emerging from the collective efforts of numerous smaller and relatively simple agents (Minsky, 1988), each characterized by diverse traits, we set two types of agents exhibiting typically contrasting traits: easy-going and overconfident, as shown in Figure 2(a). 随和型智能体能够客观看待事物，善于适应不同情境，并能与各类智能体融洽相处（Friedman and Schustack, 1999），从而形成一种和谐、民主的社会结构（Mutz, 2006; Held, 2006）。 Easy-going agents keep things in perspective, adapt well to different situations, and are compatible with various types of agents (Friedman and Schustack, 1999), which results in a harmonious societal structure with democracy (Mutz, 2006; Held, 2006). 相反，过度自信型智能体往往高估自身能力、忽视潜在风险，并抵触他人的意见（Moore and Healy, 2008）。 Conversely, overconfident agents tend to overestimate their competence, ignore potential risks, and resist others’ opinions (Moore and Healy, 2008).
思维模式。Thinking Pattern.
鉴于 SoM 概念（Minsky, 1988）认为，当社会中各有所长的个体通过思考开展合作时，智能便会涌现，我们旨在研究哪些思维模式最有助于产生这种涌现智能。 Considering the SoM concept (Minsky, 1988) states that intelligence emerges when specialized individuals within a society cooperate through thinking, we aim to study what thinking patterns are most successful in producing such emerging intelligence. 因此，我们探索了两种思维模式：辩论（Sunstein, 2005; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023）和反思（Bogumil, 1985; Bolton, 2010; Shinn et al., 2023），如图 2(c) 所示。 Thus we explore two thinking patterns: debate (Sunstein, 2005; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) and reflection (Bogumil, 1985; Bolton, 2010; Shinn et al., 2023), as illustrated in Figure 2(c). $(i)$ 在辩论模式中，多个智能体提出想法、交流回应、开展集体论辩，并最终达成共识。 $(i)$ In the debate pattern, several agents propose ideas, exchange responses, engage in collective argumentation, and ultimately reach a consensus. 这有助于社会中所有智能体共享知识、开展学习并增强适应能力（Weiß, 1995; Stone and Veloso, 2000; Vidal, 2006; Wooldridge, 2009）。 This fosters knowledge sharing, facilitates learning, and promotes adaptation among all agents within the society (Weiß, 1995; Stone and Veloso, 2000; Vidal, 2006; Wooldridge, 2009). $(ii)$ 在反思模式中，智能体回顾先前的回应，从自身经验中汲取教训，并据此完善答案。 $(ii)$ In the reflection pattern, agents review their prior responses, extract lessons from their experiences, and refine their answers accordingly. 这两种模式都可以开展多轮。 These two patterns can unfold over several rounds.
协作策略。Collaborative Strategy.
通过批判性反思和积极参与辩论，智能体能够质疑自身已有的假设、获得新的视角，并最终完善自身观点。 Through both critical reflection and active participation in debate, agents are poised to challenge their existing assumptions, acquire fresh perspectives, and ultimately refine their viewpoints. 采用基于这两种思维模式的协作机制，可以促进更具洞察力的决策（Wooldridge, 2009; Amgoud and Prade, 2009），并改善推理结果（Mezirow, 2018）。 Employing a collaboration mechanism built on these two thinking patterns can foster more insightful decision-making (Wooldridge, 2009; Amgoud and Prade, 2009) and improve reasoning outcomes (Mezirow, 2018). 在社会环境中，智能体通常通过多轮协作来解决问题。 In societal settings, agents typically engage in multiple rounds of collaboration for problem-solving. 在本文中，我们将协作策略刻画为多轮协作过程中思维模式的排列，如图 2(d) 所示，并在 §2.2 中进一步阐述。 In this paper, we characterize the collaborative strategy as a permutation of thinking patterns throughout multi-round collaboration, as illustrated in Figure 2(d) and further elaborated in §2.2.

4 从众与共识达成现象4 Phenomena of Conformity and Consensus Reaching
为回答 RQ3，我们从社会心理学视角展开进一步分析 (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009)，以辨明机器社会协作与人类社会动态之间的契合之处 (Woolley et al., 2010)。 To address RQ3, we embark on further analysis from a social psychology view (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009), to discern alignment between machine society collaboration and human societal dynamics (Woolley et al., 2010). 我们的研究结果表明，机器社会协作呼应了某些特定的人类社会现象或理论，例如从众（conformity）(Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015) 和共识达成（consensus reaching）(Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018)（更多分析见附录 G.1）。 Our findings indicate that machine society collaboration echoes specific human societal phenomena or theories, such as conformity (Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015) and consensus reaching (Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018) (more analysis are in Appendix G.1). 受篇幅限制，我们还在附录 G.2 中分析了多智能体协作中的群体动力学（group dynamics）(Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018)。 We also analyze group dynamics (Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018) in multi-agent collaboration at Appendix G.2 as page limits.
我们展开详细分析，以辨明协作中的从众与共识达成现象。 We embark on a detailed analysis, to discern the conformity and consensus-reaching phenomena in collaboration. 例如，如附录 D.3 中的图 8(a) 所示，一个智能体最初正确回答了问题。 For instance, as depicted in Figure 8(a) at Appendix D.3, an agent initially responds correctly to a question. 然而，受到另外两个智能体的错误答案和解释的影响，三个智能体最终因从众而接受了一个错误答案。 However, swayed by the misguided answers and explanations from the other two agents, eventually, the three agents conform to an incorrect answer. 这一现象反映了“群体思维”（groupthink）的弊端 (Janis, 1972; Jehn, 1995)，表明关系紧密的群体成员往往更重视和谐与共识，而非对不同观点进行客观评判，这可能导致有缺陷的决策。 This phenomenon mirrors detriments in “groupthink” (Janis, 1972; Jehn, 1995), suggesting that members of tight-knit groups tend to value harmony and consensus over objective critique of divergent views, potentially leading to flawed decisions. 相比之下，在附录 D.3 的图 8(b) 所示的另一种情境中，三个智能体在参与整个社会范围内的辩论后，都收敛到了正确答案。 Contrastingly, in another scenario illustrated in Figure 8(b) at Appendix D.3, all three agents converge on the right answer after engaging in a society-wide debate. 这反映了“群体思维” (Jehn, 1995) 和“SoM” (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003) 的益处，即多个智能体通过协作产生智能。 This mirrors benefits in “groupthink” (Jehn, 1995) and “SoM” (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003), where a multitude of agents collaboratively yield intelligence. 在此类辩论中，智能体提供不同的观点和信息。 Within such debates, agents furnish varied viewpoints and information. 通过这些交流，冲突得以解决，想法得到完善，群体逐渐趋向于形成基于充分信息的共识 (Fisher et al., 2011; Forsyth, 2018)。 Through these exchanges, conflicts are resolved, ideas are honed, and the group gravitates toward an informed consensus (Fisher et al., 2011; Forsyth, 2018).

5 结论与未来工作5 Conclusion and Future Work
本研究凸显了基于 LLM 的协作机制的潜力。 This study has highlighted the potential of collaboration mechanisms with LLMs. 我们的研究结果表明，具有不同个体特征、思维模式和协作策略的 LLM 智能体展现出了出色的协作能力。 Our findings reveal the impressive collaboration capabilities of LLM agents, with different individual traits, thinking patterns, and collaborative strategies. 这些智能体中涌现出的类人行为与社会心理学理论相呼应，进一步凸显了人类与 AI 交互的潜力。 The emergence of human-like behaviors in these agents, resonating with social psychology theories, further emphasizes the potential of human-AI interaction. 未来，有必要更深入地探索多智能体社会，重点改进协作行为；进一步融入社会心理学的见解，也有助于指导具备社会感知能力的 NLP 系统的开发。 Moving forward, a deeper exploration into the multi-agent society is warranted, focusing on collaboration behavior refinement; integrating further insights from social psychology could also guide the development of socially aware NLP systems.
局限性Limitations
尽管我们探索了多种社会和协作策略，但本研究仍存在局限性。 Although we explored various societies and collaborative strategies, our study still has its limitations. 首先，受成本限制，我们没有探索分别基于不同 LLM 的多个智能体所产生的影响，而由于这些模型使用了分布不同的预训练数据以及与人类意图对齐的策略，这种探索可能会在社会层面带来更有趣的发现。 Firstly, limited by expense, we don’t explore the impact of multiple agents respectively based on different LLMs, which may lead to more interesting findings at the social level due to the usage of differently distributed pre-trained data and strategies aligned with human intentions. 此外，我们仅通过搜索遍历了所有可能的情境，缺乏一种让智能体在特定情境中自适应地自主决定协作策略的方法。 Furthermore, we traversed all possible scenarios by search alone, lacking a way to let the agents adaptively make autonomous decisions on collaborative strategies in specific scenarios. 尽管辩论能够尽可能接近性能上限，但这种方法的消耗较大，而且存在一些能够以更低开销取得更好性能的策略。 Although debate can be as close as possible to the upper limit, this approach entails a larger consumption and there exist some strategies that can achieve better performance with less overhead. 此外，我们的实验设置相对简单，尚未考虑更复杂的配置，例如更广泛的特质或更大规模的社会。 Additionally, our experimental setup is relatively straightforward, as we have not considered more intricate configurations, such as a broader range of traits or a larger-scale society. 最后，我们通过人工验证和基于规则的匹配来评估性能，这也限制了对更贴近现实且更具创造性的任务（如文学创作）的验证能力。 Finally, we evaluate performance through manual validation and rule-based matching, which also limits the ability to validate more realistic and creative tasks, such as literary creation.

当预训练 LLM（例如，由 LLM 驱动的智能体）实现社会对齐时 (Duéñez-Guzmán et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2023b)，它们可能表现出类人智能 (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003; Zhuge et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Xu et al., 2024; Talebirad and Nadiri, 2023)。 When the pre-trained LLMs (e.g., LLM-empowered agents) are socially aligned (Duéñez-Guzmán et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2023b), they could exhibit human-like intelligence (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003; Zhuge et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Xu et al., 2024; Talebirad and Nadiri, 2023). 具体而言，智能体可以模拟类人行为 (Mei et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024a; Xiao et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023a; Chuang et al., 2023; Chuang and Rogers, 2023; Crouse et al., 2023; Xie et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024)，像人类一样扮演角色 (Shanahan et al., 2023; Hou et al., 2023; He et al., 2023)，甚至与人类协作 (Fuchs et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2024; Alberts et al., 2024)。 Specifically, agents can simulate human-like behaviors (Mei et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024a; Xiao et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023a; Chuang et al., 2023; Chuang and Rogers, 2023; Crouse et al., 2023; Xie et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024), play roles like humans (Shanahan et al., 2023; Hou et al., 2023; He et al., 2023), and even collaborate with humans (Fuchs et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2024; Alberts et al., 2024).
值得注意的是，从社会心理学视角来看，多智能体协作可以与人类社会中的现象或理论相呼应 (Binz and Schulz, 2023; Demszky et al., 2023; Hagendorff, 2023; Kuribayashi et al., 2024)，例如从众 (Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015)、共识达成 (Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018)、群体动力学 (Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Seal et al., 1998; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018) 和社会科学 (Gilbert and Terna, 2000; Epstein, 2012; Flache et al., 2017; Lorenz et al., 2021; Smaldino, 2023; Lanctot et al., 2023)。 Notably, multi-agent collaboration can echo human society phenomena or theories in a social psychology view (Binz and Schulz, 2023; Demszky et al., 2023; Hagendorff, 2023; Kuribayashi et al., 2024), such as conformity (Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015), consensus reaching (Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018), group dynamics (Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Seal et al., 1998; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018) and social science (Gilbert and Terna, 2000; Epstein, 2012; Flache et al., 2017; Lorenz et al., 2021; Smaldino, 2023; Lanctot et al., 2023).

G.1 从众与共识达成G.1 Conformity and Consensus Reaching
图 6, 28, 37, 65, 51 展示了从众情况。 Figures 6, 28, 37, 65, and 51 illustrate the conformity. 图 7, 28, 37, 65, 51 展示了共识情况。 Figures 7, 28, 37, 65, and 51 illustrate the consensus. 本节详细说明了用于计算从众和共识的方法。 This section provides a detailed explanation of the methodologies used to calculate both conformity and consensus.
对于从众，我们仅关注在给定轮次中积极参与辩论的智能体，不考虑处于反思阶段的智能体。 For conformity, we solely focus on agents actively engaging in debate, disregarding those in reflection during a given round. 将第 $i$ 个智能体在第 $j$ 轮的答案记为 $a_{i,j}$。 Let the answer of the $i$ -th agent at $j$ -th round be denoted as $a_{i,j}$ . 对于第 $j$ 轮的第 $k$ 个智能体，如果“ $\text{Frequency}\big(\{a_{i,j-1}|i\in[1,n]\}\big)=a_{k,j}$ ”，我们就认定智能体 $k$ 在第 $j$ 轮发生了从众行为，其中 $\text{Frequency}(\cdot)$ 表示出现频率最高的答案（排除所有答案均仅出现一次的情况，因为此类情况被视为未发生从众）。 For the $k$ -th agent at $j$ -th round, if “ $\text{Frequency}\big(\{a_{i,j-1}|i\in[1,n]\}\big)=a_{k,j}$ ”, we identify this as the occurrence of conformity by agent $k$ at $j$ -th round, where $\text{Frequency}(\cdot)$ represents the most frequently given answer (excluding instances where all answers occur only once, as such cases are considered as non-conformity). 此外，我们根据从众前后答案的正确性将其分为四种情况，其中 ‘True’ 表示正确，‘False’ 表示错误。 Additionally, we categorize the correctness of answers both before and after conformity into four cases, with ‘True’ denoting correct and ‘False’ denoting incorrect.
对于共识，我们考察不同答案的数量（即共识簇的数量）随协作轮数增加而发生的变化。 For consensus, we examine the evolution of the number of distinct answers (i.e., consensus clusters) with increasing rounds of collaboration. 将第 $i$ 个智能体在时刻 $j$ 的答案记为 $a_{i,j}$。 Let the answer of the $i$ -th agent at time $j$ be denoted as $a_{i,j}$ . 对于第 $j$ 轮，共识簇的数量定义为 $\left\|\text{Set}(\{a_{i,j}|i\in[1,n]\})\right\|$，其中 $\left\|\text{Set}(\cdot)\right\|$ 表示不同答案的数量。 For the $j$ -th round, consensus clusters is defined as $\left\|\text{Set}(\{a_{i,j}|i\in[1,n]\})\right\|$ , where $\left\|\text{Set}(\cdot)\right\|$ represents the count of different answers. 图 17, 16, 60, 59, 46, 45 中的分析采用了这一计算方法。 This computational approach has been utilized in the analysis presented in Figures 17, 16, 60, 59, 46, 45.
