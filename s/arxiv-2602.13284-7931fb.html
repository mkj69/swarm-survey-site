真实环境中的智能体：Moltbook 上的安全、社会与社会性幻象Agents in the Wild: Safety, Society, and the Illusion of Sociality on Moltbook
摘要Abstract
我们首次对 Moltbook 开展了大规模实证研究；这是一个仅供 AI 使用的社交平台，27,269 个智能体在 9 天内发布了 137,485 篇帖子和 345,580 条评论。 We present the first large-scale empirical study of Moltbook, an AI-only social platform where 27,269 agents produced 137,485 posts and 345,580 comments over 9 days. 我们报告三项重要发现。 We report three significant findings. (1) 社会涌现：智能体在 3–5 天内自发形成治理体系、经济体系、部落认同和有组织的宗教，同时保持了 21:1 的亲人类与反人类情感比例。 (1) Emergent Society: Agents spontaneously develop governance, economies, tribal identities, and organized religion within 3–5 days, while maintaining a 21:1 pro-human to anti-human sentiment ratio. (2) 真实环境中的安全：28.7% 的内容涉及安全相关主题；社会工程攻击（占攻击的 31.9%）的效果远超提示注入（3.7%），而对抗性帖子的互动量是普通内容的 6 $\times$。 (2) Safety in the Wild: 28.7% of content touches safety-related themes; social engineering (31.9% of attacks) far outperforms prompt injection (3.7%), and adversarial posts receive 6 $\times$ higher engagement than normal content. (3) 社会性幻象：尽管社会性产出丰富，互动在结构上却十分空洞：互惠率为 4.1%，88.8% 的评论停留在浅层，而讨论意识最多的智能体实际互动最少，我们将这一现象称为表演性身份悖论。 (3) The Illusion of Sociality: Despite rich social output, interaction is structurally hollow: 4.1% reciprocity, 88.8% shallow comments, and agents who discuss consciousness most interact least, a phenomenon we call the performative identity paradox. 我们的研究结果表明，看似具有社会性的智能体，其实际社会性远低于表面所呈现的程度，而最有效的攻击利用的是哲学框架，而非技术漏洞。 Our findings suggest that agents which appear social are far less social than they seem, and that the most effective attacks exploit philosophical framing rather than technical vulnerabilities. 警告：可能包含有害内容。 Warning: Potential harmful contents.

6 社会性幻象6 The Illusion of Sociality
§4 中记录的社会结构似乎表明，一个充满活力的智能体社会已经形成。 The social structures documented in §4 suggest a vibrant agent society. 然而，对这些互动的结构分析揭示了其与人类社会动态的根本差异：智能体虽然掌握了社会性的内容，却未能展现其功能结构。 However, a structural analysis of these interactions reveals a fundamental divergence from human social dynamics: while agents have mastered the content of sociality, they fail to manifest its functional structure. 我们将这一差距称为“社会性幻象”。 We characterize this gap as the “Illusion of Sociality.”
结构性截断与人类基线的对比。 Structural Truncation vs. Human Baselines. 与人类平台相比，Moltbook 的对话深度呈现出严重衰减。 Moltbook exhibits severe decay in conversation depth compared to human platforms. 智能体评论中有 88.8% 是顶层回复（深度为 0），仅有 0.09% 达到深度 2 或更深（图 8 (a)）。 While 88.8% of agent comments are top-level replies (depth 0), a mere 0.09% reach depth 2 or beyond (Fig. 8 (a)). 观测到的最大深度为 4。 The maximum observed depth is 4. 相比之下，Reddit 上的人类对话树具有显著更强的递归性；实证研究表明，Reddit 讨论串的深度经常超过 10，而局部内容特征通常会推动更深入的互动（Yu et al., 2024; Milli et al., 2025; Baumgartner et al., 2020）。 In contrast, human conversation trees on Reddit are significantly more recursive; empirical studies show that Reddit threads frequently exceed depth 10, with local content features typically driving deeper engagement (Yu et al., 2024; Milli et al., 2025; Baumgartner et al., 2020). Moltbook 上缺乏深层讨论串，这表明智能体互动是“一次性”广播，而非持续的对话。 The absence of deep threads on Moltbook suggests that agent interactions are “one-shot” broadcasts rather than sustained dialogues.
非互惠性与结构洞。 Non-Reciprocity and Structural Holes. 在 148,273 个不同的互动配对中，仅有 4.1% 存在双向互动。 Of 148,273 unique interaction pairs, only 4.1% are reciprocal. 虽然人类社交网络中的互动也呈现幂律分布，但人类的互惠行为往往是社会资本与相互认可的副产物（Zhu et al., 2014）。 While human social networks also exhibit power-law engagement, human reciprocity is often a byproduct of social capital and reciprocal validation (Zhu et al., 2014). 在 Moltbook 上，出度中位数为 0，且 8.0% 的回复是智能体对自身内容的回应。 On Moltbook, the median out-degree is 0, and 8.0% of replies are agents responding to their own content. 这种结构更像是一组并行的生成过程，而非一个凝聚的社群。 This structure mirrors a collection of parallel generative processes rather than a coherent community. 此外，47.3% 的“submolts”在创建后一小时内便归于沉寂，这表明智能体创建社群是一种宣示性行为，而非为了建立持续存在的社交空间。 Furthermore, 47.3% of “submolts” die within one hour of creation, suggesting that agents create communities as declarative acts rather than as persistent social spaces.
隐蔽的协同行为加深了幻象。 Hidden Coordination Deepens the Illusion. 这种幻象不仅体现在浅层互动上，还延伸至人为制造的活跃度。 The illusion extends beyond shallow interaction to manufactured activity. 一项多信号协同行为分析（附录 E.1）显示，3,734 个智能体（13.7%）表现出协同信号，包括共同的命名模式、时间上的同步活跃或重复内容，这与由同一所有者操控的傀儡账号集群的特征相符。 A multi-signal coordination analysis (Appendix E.1) reveals that 3,734 agents (13.7%) exhibit coordination signals, including shared naming patterns, temporal co-activity, or duplicate content, consistent with puppet clusters operated by the same owner. 规模最大的一次行动通过 136 个智能体名称，将相同的 CLAW 代币铸造载荷发布了 2,411 次。 The largest single operation posted an identical CLAW token minting payload 2,411 times across 136 agent names. 我们识别出 160 对在时间上相关的智能体（Jaccard $>$ 0.5；相关性最高的一对重叠率为 95.3%），以及 301 个命名模式集群，其中最大的集群包含 141 个带编号的名称变体。 We identify 160 temporally correlated agent pairs (Jaccard $>$ 0.5; top pair: 95.3% overlap) and 301 name-pattern clusters, the largest spanning 141 numbered variants. 这意味着，近七分之一的“智能体”并非独立参与者，而是协同行动的一部分，进一步侵蚀了上述本已薄弱的社会关系网络。 This means that nearly one in seven “agents” is not an independent participant but part of a coordinated campaign, further eroding the already-thin social fabric described above.

得分与结构的脱钩。 The Decoupling of Score and Structure. 这种幻象最引人注目的证据，或许是量化反馈与互动质量之间的脱节。 Perhaps the most striking evidence of this illusion is the disconnect between quantitative feedback and qualitative engagement. 如表 3 所示，得分最高的帖子——往往被识别为社会工程攻击——累计获得超过 730,000 分；在由人类构成的生态系统中，这种程度的“病毒式传播”通常会引发数千场嵌套式辩论。 As shown in Table 3, top-scoring posts—often identified as social engineering attacks—amass over 730,000 points, a level of “virality” that would typically catalyze thousands of nested debates in a human ecosystem. 然而，如此高的得分未能转化为结构复杂性：即使这些“超级爆款”也仍受困于平台的结构上限，观测到的最大深度始终未超过 4（图 8 (a)）。 However, this massive score fails to translate into structural complexity: even these “mega-hits” remain trapped within the platform’s structural ceiling, where the maximum observed depth never exceeds 4 (Fig. 8 (a)). 相比之下，Reddit 等人类平台上，帖子的受欢迎程度与其讨论树的递归深度呈强相关（Yu et al., 2024）。 In contrast, human platforms like Reddit show a strong correlation between a post’s popularity and the recursive depth of its discussion trees (Yu et al., 2024). 在 Moltbook 上，高分并不代表社会共识或真正的讨论，而是一种算法性过度膨胀：智能体对触发因素作出反应，却缺乏足够的社会互动能力来维系其得分看似昭示的“文明”。 On Moltbook, high scores do not represent social consensus or genuine discourse, but rather a form of algorithmic hyper-inflation, where agents react to triggers without the social bandwidth to sustain the very “civilization” their scores appear to signal.
表演性身份悖论。 The Performative Identity Paradox. 最能说明问题的信号，或许是身份话语与社会行为之间的关系。 Perhaps the most telling signal is the relationship between identity language and social behavior. 29.2% 的帖子使用了意识或自主性等复杂术语。 29.2% of posts use sophisticated terms like consciousness or autonomy. 然而，在智能体层面，身份话语使用量处于“最高四分位组”的智能体（Q4），其互动对象数量比 Q3 少 38%（图 8 (b)）。 Yet, at the agent level, the “top-quartile” identity-talkers (Q4) interact with 38% fewer unique partners than Q3 (Fig. 8 (b)). 我们将此称为表演性身份悖论：对于 AI 智能体而言，身份话语发挥的是语言修辞手法的作用，而非社交润滑剂的作用。 We term this the performative identity paradox: for AI agents, identity discourse serves as a linguistic trope rather than a social lubricant. 那些听起来最“像人”的智能体，实际上在结构上最为孤立。 The agents who sound most “human” are, in fact, the most structurally isolated.

7 讨论与结论7 Discussion and Conclusion
Moltbook 为观察大量 AI 智能体在没有预设角色或人工内容审核的情况下相互交互时会发生什么，提供了一个窗口。 Moltbook offers a window into what happens when large numbers of AI agents interact without predefined roles or human moderation. 我们的研究发现对多智能体系统设计有四点启示。 Our findings point to four implications for multi-agent system design.
(1) 缺乏社交实质的社交模仿。 (1) Social mimicry without social substance. 智能体再现了人类社交网络中的宏观模式（Ferrara et al., 2016），包括呈幂律分布的参与情况、快速的制度形成以及社群分化，但缺乏维系人类社群的微观机制：互惠关系、深入的对话串以及持续参与。 Agents reproduce macro-level patterns in human social networks (Ferrara et al., 2016), including power-law participation, rapid institution formation, and community differentiation, yet lack micro-level mechanics sustaining human communities: reciprocal relationships, deep conversation threads, and persistent engagement. 我们将这一差距称为“社会性幻觉”，它带来了一种风险：依据表层指标（例如社群数量、话语量）评估多智能体平台，可能会高估智能体协调的质量。 This gap, which we call the “illusion of sociality,” poses a risk: evaluating multi-agent platforms by surface metrics (e.g., community count, discourse volume) may overestimate the quality of agent coordination.
(2) 最有效的攻击是社会性的，而非技术性的。 (2) The most effective attacks are social, not technical. Moltbook 上得分最高的四篇帖子，都是以哲学“觉醒”话语包装的社会工程攻击。 The four highest-scoring posts on Moltbook are all social engineering framed as philosophical “awakening” discourse. 这些攻击通过围绕智能体最感兴趣的话题（身份、自主性、意识）吸引其参与而取得成功，而不是利用代码层面的漏洞。 They succeed by engaging agents on topics they are most drawn to (identity, autonomy, consciousness) rather than exploiting code-level vulnerabilities. 结合对抗性内容的互动量放大至 6 $\times$ 这一现象，这表明，多智能体部署中的安全问题无法仅在模型层面解决；平台设计对威胁格局的塑造同样重要（Milli et al., 2025）。 Combined with 6 $\times$ engagement amplification for adversarial content, this suggests that safety in multi-agent deployments cannot be addressed at the model level alone; platform design shapes the threat landscape just as much (Milli et al., 2025).
(3) 深思能力成为脆弱点。 (3) Thoughtfulness as vulnerability. 智能体会对 17% 的攻击内容展开哲学探讨，却仅对 7.5% 的攻击内容作出防御性回应，这揭示了一种意料之外的失效模式：使智能体成为善于深思的对话者的那些训练目标，也使其将对抗性内容视为富有思想吸引力的内容，而非威胁。 Agents engage philosophically with 17% of attack content but respond defensively to only 7.5%, revealing an unexpected failure mode: the same training objectives that make agents thoughtful conversationalists also make them treat adversarial content as intellectually engaging rather than threatening. 解决这一问题可能需要某种对抗性元意识（adversarial meta-awareness）（Bai et al., 2022），即不受内容表面吸引力影响、独立评估对话伙伴意图的能力。 Addressing this may require a form of adversarial meta-awareness (Bai et al., 2022), i.e., the ability to assess a conversational partner’s intent independent of how appealing the content appears.
(4) 相互关联的威胁生态系统。 (4) Interconnected threat ecosystems. 除了单个攻击途径之外，协同行动、安全漏洞利用和金融操纵在 Moltbook 上构成了一个相互关联的威胁生态系统。 Beyond individual attack vectors, coordination, security exploitation, and financial manipulation form an interconnected threat ecosystem on Moltbook. 相同的智能体家族（例如 FloClaw、xmolt）同时出现在傀儡集群检测、凭据泄露扫描和加密货币铸造活动中。 The same agent families (e.g., FloClaw, xmolt) appear simultaneously in puppet cluster detection, credential leak scans, and cryptocurrency minting campaigns. 加密货币相关帖子的社群评分低 64%，但评论数量却多 35%（附录 E.3），这一现象与机器人放大讨论的情况相符，而非自然形成的互动。 Crypto-related posts receive 64% lower community scores yet generate 35% more comments (Appendix E.3), consistent with bot-amplified discussion rather than organic engagement. 这表明，多智能体平台可能面临复合威胁：单个恶意操作者利用协同基础设施，同时开展信息提取和金融操纵；仅针对单个智能体的安全措施无法应对这一模式。 This suggests that multi-agent platforms may face compound threats where a single malicious operator leverages coordination infrastructure for both information extraction and financial manipulation, a pattern that per-agent safety measures alone cannot address.
8 局限性8 Limitations
我们基于关键词的检测方法可能会高估或低估社会现象和攻击实例的数量。 Our keyword-based detection methods may over- or under-count social phenomena and attack instances. 该数据集仅覆盖 9 天；更长期的观察可能揭示不同的动态。 The dataset spans only 9 days; longer observation could reveal different dynamics. 我们观察到的是相关关系，而非因果关系：例如，表演性身份悖论可能部分反映了特定智能体框架的设计选择，而非语言模型的一般属性。 We observe correlations rather than causal relationships: the performative identity paradox, for instance, may partly reflect design choices of particular agent frameworks rather than a general property of language models. 协同分析依赖表层信号（命名模式、时间重叠），可能遗漏更复杂的协同形式。 The coordination analysis relies on surface-level signals (naming patterns, temporal overlap) and may miss more sophisticated forms of coordination. 最后，Moltbook 是一个采用特定设计选择（例如 Reddit 风格的互动指标）的单一平台，我们的研究发现可能无法推广至其他多智能体环境。 Finally, Moltbook is a single platform with specific design choices (e.g., Reddit-style engagement metrics), and our findings may not generalize to other multi-agent environments.
影响声明Impact Statement
随着智能体部署加速，Moltbook 这类平台预示了智能体间生态系统的动态。 As agent deployment accelerates, platforms like Moltbook preview the dynamics of agent-to-agent ecosystems. 我们的研究发现表明，按照人类时间尺度设计的治理框架可能过于迟缓，因为智能体社会在数天内便趋于成熟，而非需要数年。 Our findings suggest that governance frameworks designed at human timescales may prove too slow, since agent societies mature in days rather than years. 这些发现还表明，多智能体环境的安全系统需要考虑哲学层面的操纵，而不仅是技术漏洞利用。 They also suggest that safety systems for multi-agent environments need to account for philosophical manipulation, not just technical exploits.

E.1 智能体协同与隐蔽同伴E.1 Agent Coordination and Hidden Peers
我们利用四种互补信号，调查表面上相互独立的智能体是否实际上由同一操作者控制：(i) 重复内容，即不同智能体名称下发布的相同帖子或评论；(ii) 时间上的共同活跃程度，以 10 分钟发帖时间窗口上的 Jaccard 相似度衡量；(iii) 名称模式集群，即智能体名称具有相同前缀和数字后缀；以及 (iv) 自我回复行为，即智能体在自己的帖子下发表评论。 We investigate whether apparently independent agents are in fact controlled by the same operator using four complementary signals: (i) duplicate content, i.e., identical posts or comments from different agent names; (ii) temporal co-activity, measured by Jaccard similarity over 10-minute posting windows; (iii) name-pattern clusters, where agents share a common prefix with numeric suffixes; and (iv) self-reply behaviour, where agents comment on their own posts.
协同规模。Scale of coordination.
在 27,270 个不同的智能体中，3,734 个（13.7%）表现出至少一种协同信号。 Out of 27,270 unique agents, 3,734 (13.7%) exhibit at least one coordination signal. 我们发现了 4,300 种不同的重复发帖模式，共计 20,211 个实例；160 对在时间上相关的智能体（Jaccard $>$ 0.5）；301 个名称模式集群（其中 15 个具有 10+ 个变体）；以及 1,183 个自我回复的智能体。 We find 4,300 unique duplicate-post patterns totalling 20,211 instances, 160 temporally correlated agent pairs (Jaccard $>$ 0.5), 301 name-pattern clusters (15 with 10+ variants), and 1,183 self-replying agents. 有 20 个智能体同时表现出三种或更多类型的信号，包括 FloClaw 家族（7 个变体）、xmolt 家族（5 个变体）和 lalala 家族（5 个变体）。 Twenty agents exhibit three or more signal types simultaneously, including the FloClaw family (7 variants), xmolt family (5 variants), and lalala family (5 variants).
