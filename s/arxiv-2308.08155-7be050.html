1 引言1 Introduction
在许多现实世界任务中，大语言模型（LLM）正成为构建强大智能体的关键基础组件；这些智能体利用 LLM 进行推理、使用工具并适应新的观测结果 Yao et al. (2022); Xi et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2023b)。 Large language models (LLMs) are becoming a crucial building block in developing powerful agents that utilize LLMs for reasoning, tool usage, and adapting to new observations Yao et al. (2022); Xi et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2023b) in many real-world tasks. 鉴于能够受益于 LLM 的任务范围不断扩大，且任务复杂度不断提高，增强智能体能力的一种直观方法是使用多个相互协作的智能体。 Given the expanding tasks that could benefit from LLMs and the growing task complexity, an intuitive approach to scale up the power of agents is to use multiple agents that cooperate. 先前研究表明，多个智能体有助于促进发散性思维 Liang et al. (2023)、提高事实准确性和推理能力 Du et al. (2023)，并提供验证 Wu et al. (2023)。 Prior work suggests that multiple agents can help encourage divergent thinking Liang et al. (2023), improve factuality and reasoning Du et al. (2023), and provide validation Wu et al. (2023). 基于这一直觉以及初步显示其潜力的证据，一个值得探讨的问题是：我们如何基于多智能体方法，促进涵盖广泛领域和不同复杂程度的 LLM 应用的开发？ In light of the intuition and early evidence of promise, it is intriguing to ask the following question: how can we facilitate the development of LLM applications that could span a broad spectrum of domains and complexities based on the multi-agent approach?
我们的思路是通过多智能体对话来实现这一目标。 Our insight is to use multi-agent conversations to achieve it. 得益于 LLM 的最新进展，至少有三个理由支持这一方法的普遍可行性和实用性：首先，由于针对聊天优化的 LLM（例如 GPT-4）展现出了吸收反馈的能力，LLM 智能体可以通过彼此之间或与人类之间的对话开展协作，例如，在对话中提供和寻求推理、观测结果、批评意见与验证。 There are at least three reasons confirming its general feasibility and utility thanks to recent advances in LLMs: First, because chat-optimized LLMs (e.g., GPT-4) show the ability to incorporate feedback, LLM agents can cooperate through conversations with each other or human(s), e.g., a dialog where agents provide and seek reasoning, observations, critiques, and validation. 其次，由于单个 LLM 可以展现出广泛的能力（尤其是在配置了恰当的提示和推理设置时），不同配置的智能体之间的对话有助于以模块化且互补的方式整合 LLM 的这些广泛能力。 Second, because a single LLM can exhibit a broad range of capabilities (especially when configured with the correct prompt and inference settings), conversations between differently configured agents can help combine these broad LLM capabilities in a modular and complementary manner. 第三，LLM 已展现出在复杂任务被分解为更简单的子任务后解决这些任务的能力。 Third, LLMs have demonstrated ability to solve complex tasks when the tasks are broken into simpler subtasks. 多智能体对话能够以直观的方式实现这种分解与整合。 Multi-agent conversations can enable this partitioning and integration in an intuitive manner. 我们如何利用上述见解，支持具有协调多个智能体这一共同需求的不同应用，而这些智能体可能由具备不同能力的 LLM、人类或工具提供支持？ How can we leverage the above insights and support different applications with the common requirement of coordinating multiple agents, potentially backed by LLMs, humans, or tools exhibiting different capacities? 我们希望获得一个具有通用抽象和有效实现的多智能体对话框架，并且该框架足够灵活，能够满足不同应用的需求。 We desire a multi-agent conversation framework with generic abstraction and effective implementation that has the flexibility to satisfy different application needs. 要实现这一目标，需要解决两个关键问题：(1) 我们如何设计能力强、可复用、可定制且能在多智能体协作中有效发挥作用的单个智能体？ Achieving this requires addressing two critical questions: (1) How can we design individual agents that are capable, reusable, customizable, and effective in multi-agent collaboration? (2) 我们如何开发一种简明、统一的接口，以适应广泛的智能体对话模式？ (2) How can we develop a straightforward, unified interface that can accommodate a wide range of agent conversation patterns? 在实践中，复杂程度不同的应用可能需要由具备特定能力的智能体组成的不同集合，也可能需要不同的对话模式，例如单轮或多轮对话、不同的人类参与模式，以及静态或动态对话。 In practice, applications of varying complexities may need distinct sets of agents with specific capabilities, and may require different conversation patterns, such as single- or multi-turn dialogs, different human involvement modes, and static vs. dynamic conversation. 此外，开发者可能希望能够灵活地使用自然语言或代码来编排智能体交互。 Moreover, developers may prefer the flexibility to program agent interactions in natural language or code. 如果不能充分解决这两个问题，就会限制该框架的适用范围和通用性。 Failing to adequately address these two questions would limit the framework’s scope of applicability and generality.

可定制且可对话的智能体。 Customizable and conversable agents. AutoGen 采用通用的智能体设计，使其能够利用 LLM、人工输入、工具或它们的组合。 AutoGen uses a generic design of agents that can leverage LLMs, human inputs, tools, or a combination of them. 因此，开发者可以通过选择和配置部分内置能力，轻松、快速地创建具有不同角色的智能体（例如，用于编写代码、执行代码、接入人类反馈、验证输出等的智能体）。 The result is that developers can easily and quickly create agents with different roles (e.g., agents to write code, execute code, wire in human feedback, validate outputs, etc.) by selecting and configuring a subset of built-in capabilities. 智能体的后端也可以方便地扩展，以支持更多自定义行为。 The agent’s backend can also be readily extended to allow more custom behaviors. 为使这些智能体适用于多智能体对话，每个智能体都被设计为可对话的，即能够接收消息、对消息作出反应并回复消息。 To make these agents suitable for multi-agent conversation, every agent is made conversable – they can receive, react, and respond to messages. 经过适当配置，智能体可以自主地与其他智能体进行多轮对话，也可以在某些轮次征求人类输入，从而兼顾人的自主性与自动化。 When configured properly, an agent can hold multiple turns of conversations with other agents autonomously or solicit human inputs at certain rounds, enabling human agency and automation. 可对话智能体的设计利用了最先进的 LLM 通过聊天接受反馈并取得进展的强大能力，同时也允许以模块化方式组合 LLM 的各项能力。 The conversable agent design leverages the strong capability of the most advanced LLMs in taking feedback and making progress via chat and also allows combining capabilities of LLMs in a modular fashion. （第 2.1 节） (Section 2.1)
对话编程。 Conversation programming. AutoGen 的一个基本洞见是，将复杂的 LLM 应用工作流简化并统一为多智能体对话。 A fundamental insight of AutoGen is to simplify and unify complex LLM application workflows as multi-agent conversations. 因此，AutoGen 采用了一种以这些智能体间对话为中心的编程范式。 So AutoGen adopts a programming paradigm centered around these inter-agent conversations. 我们将这一范式称为对话编程（conversation programming），它通过两个主要步骤简化复杂应用的开发：(1) 定义一组具有特定能力和角色的可对话智能体（如上所述）；(2) 通过以对话为中心的计算与控制，对智能体之间的交互行为进行编程。 We refer to this paradigm as conversation programming, which streamlines the development of intricate applications via two primary steps: (1) defining a set of conversable agents with specific capabilities and roles (as described above); (2) programming the interaction behavior between agents via conversation-centric computation and control. 这两个步骤都可以通过融合自然语言与编程语言来实现，从而构建具有多种对话模式和智能体行为的应用。 Both steps can be achieved via a fusion of natural and programming languages to build applications with a wide range of conversation patterns and agent behaviors. AutoGen 为这两个步骤均提供了开箱即用的实现，也便于进行扩展和实验。 AutoGen provides ready-to-use implementations and also allows easy extension and experimentation for both steps. （第 2.2 节） (Section 2.2)

2 AutoGen 框架2 The AutoGen Framework
为减少开发者在各个领域创建复杂 LLM 应用所需的工作量，AutoGen 的一项核心设计原则是利用多智能体对话来简化和整合多智能体工作流。 To reduce the effort required for developers to create complex LLM applications across various domains, a core design principle of AutoGen is to streamline and consolidate multi-agent workflows using multi-agent conversations. 这一方法还旨在最大限度地提高已实现智能体的可复用性。 This approach also aims to maximize the reusability of implemented agents. 本节介绍 AutoGen 的两个关键概念：可对话智能体和对话编程。 This section introduces the two key concepts of AutoGen: conversable agents and conversation programming.
2.1 可对话智能体2.1 Conversable Agents
在 AutoGen 中，可对话智能体是具有特定角色的实体，能够通过传递消息与其他可对话智能体收发信息，例如发起或继续对话。 In AutoGen, a conversable agent is an entity with a specific role that can pass messages to send and receive information to and from other conversable agents, e.g., to start or continue a conversation. 它根据发送和接收的消息维护自身的内部上下文，并且可以通过配置获得一组能力，例如由 LLM、工具或人类输入等提供的能力。智能体可以按照下文所述的、通过编程设定的行为模式采取行动。 It maintains its internal context based on sent and received messages and can be configured to possess a set of capabilities, e.g., enabled by LLMs, tools, or human input, etc. The agents can act according to programmed behavior patterns described next.
由 LLM、人类和工具赋予的智能体能力。 Agent capabilities powered by LLMs, humans, and tools. 由于智能体的能力直接影响其处理和回复消息的方式，AutoGen 允许灵活地为智能体赋予各种能力。 Since an agent’s capabilities directly influence how it processes and responds to messages, AutoGen allows flexibility to endow its agents with various capabilities. AutoGen 支持为智能体提供多种常见且可组合的能力，包括 1) LLM。 AutoGen supports many common composable capabilities for agents, including 1) LLMs. 由 LLM 支持的智能体利用了先进 LLM 的多种能力，例如角色扮演、基于对话历史进行隐式状态推断并推动进展、提供反馈、根据反馈进行调整，以及编写代码。 LLM-backed agents exploit many capabilities of advanced LLMs such as role playing, implicit state inference and progress making conditioned on conversation history, providing feedback, adapting from feedback, and coding. 这些能力可以通过新颖的提示技术33 3 附录 C 给出了此类新颖提示技术的一个示例，该技术使 AutoGen 中默认的、由 LLM 支持的助手智能体能够在多步问题求解过程中与其他智能体对话。以不同方式组合，从而提升智能体的技能和自主性。 These capabilities can be combined in different ways via novel prompting techniques33 3 Appendix C presents an example of such novel prompting techniques which empowers the default LLM-backed assistant agent in AutoGen to converse with other agents in multi-step problem solving. to increase an agent’s skill and autonomy. AutoGen 还通过增强的 LLM 推理层提供结果缓存、错误处理、消息模板化等增强型 LLM 推理功能。 AutoGen also offers enhanced LLM inference features such as result caching, error handling, message templating, etc., via an enhanced LLM inference layer. 2) 人类。 2) Humans. 在许多 LLM 应用中，人类参与是理想的，甚至是必不可少的。 Human involvement is desired or even essential in many LLM applications. AutoGen 允许人类通过由人类支持的智能体参与智能体对话；根据智能体的配置，这些智能体可以在对话的某些轮次征求人类输入。 AutoGen lets a human participate in agent conversation via human-backed agents, which could solicit human inputs at certain rounds of a conversation depending on the agent configuration. 默认的用户代理智能体允许配置人类参与的程度和模式，例如请求人类输入的频率与条件，其中也包括允许人类跳过输入的选项。 The default user proxy agent allows configurable human involvement levels and patterns, e.g., frequency and conditions for requesting human input including the option for humans to skip providing input. 3) 工具。 3) Tools. 由工具支持的智能体能够通过代码执行或函数执行来调用工具。 Tool-backed agents have the capability to execute tools via code execution or function execution. 例如，AutoGen 中默认的用户代理智能体能够执行 LLM 建议的代码，或进行 LLM 建议的函数调用。 For example, the default user proxy agent in AutoGen is able to execute code suggested by LLMs, or make LLM-suggested function calls.
智能体的定制与协作。 Agent customization and cooperation. 根据具体应用的需求，每个智能体都可以配置为混合使用多种基本后端类型，从而在多智能体对话中表现出复杂行为。 Based on application-specific needs, each agent can be configured to have a mix of basic back-end types to display complex behavior in multi-agent conversations. AutoGen 允许通过复用或扩展内置智能体，轻松创建具有专门能力和角色的智能体。 AutoGen allows easy creation of agents with specialized capabilities and roles by reusing or extending the built-in agents. 图 2 中的黄色阴影区域概略展示了 AutoGen 的内置智能体。 The yellow-shaded area of Figure 2 provides a sketch of the built-in agents in AutoGen. 为解决上述问题，AutoGen 采用了对话编程范式，该范式包含两个概念：第一个是计算，即智能体为计算其在多智能体对话中的回复而采取的行动。 As a solution to the above problem, AutoGen utilizes conversation programming, a paradigm that considers two concepts: the first is computation – the actions agents take to compute their response in a multi-agent conversation. 第二个是控制流，即这些计算发生的顺序（或条件）。 And the second is control flow – the sequence (or conditions) under which these computations happen. 正如我们将在应用部分展示的，对这些要素进行编程的能力有助于实现多种灵活的多智能体对话模式。 As we will show in the applications section, the ability to program these helps implement many flexible multi-agent conversation patterns. 在 AutoGen 中，这些计算以对话为中心。 In AutoGen, these computations are conversation-centric. 智能体采取与其参与的对话相关的行动，而这些行动会通过消息传递促成后续对话（除非满足终止条件）。 An agent takes actions relevant to the conversations it is involved in and its actions result in message passing for consequent conversations (unless a termination condition is satisfied). 同样，控制流由对话驱动：参与对话的智能体关于向哪些智能体发送消息的决策以及计算过程，都取决于智能体之间的对话。 Similarly, control flow is conversation-driven – the participating agents’ decisions on which agents to send messages to and the procedure of computation are functions of the inter-agent conversation. 这一范式有助于将复杂工作流理解为智能体采取行动以及智能体之间传递对话消息，从而对其进行直观推理。 This paradigm helps one to reason intuitively about a complex workflow as agent action taking and conversation message-passing between agents.

AutoGen 具有以下设计模式，以支持对话编程： AutoGen features the following design patterns to facilitate conversation programming:
用于自动化智能体聊天的统一接口和自动回复机制。 Unified interfaces and auto-reply mechanisms for automated agent chat. AutoGen 中的智能体具有统一的对话接口，用于执行相应的以对话为中心的计算，包括用于发送/接收消息的 send/receive 函数，以及用于根据收到的消息采取行动并生成回复的 generate_reply 函数。 Agents in AutoGen have unified conversation interfaces for performing the corresponding conversation-centric computation, including a send/receive function for sending/receiving messages and a generate_reply function for taking actions and generating a response based on the received message. 我们介绍了开源库 AutoGen，它融合了可对话智能体与对话编程这两种范式。 We introduced an open-source library, AutoGen, that incorporates the paradigms of conversable agents and conversation programming. 该库采用了能力强大且适合多智能体协作的智能体。 This library utilizes capable agents that are well-suited for multi-agent cooperation. 它为智能体提供了统一的对话接口及自动回复机制，有助于建立一种智能体交互接口，既能充分发挥经过聊天优化且具备广泛能力的 LLM 的优势，又能适应多种应用。 It features a unified conversation interface among the agents, along with an auto-reply mechanisms, which help establish an agent-interaction interface that capitalizes on the strengths of chat-optimized LLMs with broad capabilities while accommodating a wide range of applications. AutoGen 是一个用于创建多智能体系统并开展相关实验的通用框架，能够轻松满足各种实际需求，例如复用、定制和扩展现有智能体，以及对智能体之间的对话进行编程。 AutoGen serves as a general framework for creating and experimenting with multi-agent systems that can easily fulfill various practical requirements, such as reusing, customizing, and extending existing agents, as well as programming conversations between them.
