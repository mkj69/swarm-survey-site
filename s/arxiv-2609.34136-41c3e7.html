Abstract摘要
As LLM agents increasingly collaborate on complex tasks, how to organize their interactions becomes a central design question. Existing multi-agent systems typically learn or adapt explicit roles, hierarchies, routing policies, or communication topologies. We shift the learning target to a reusable local law that can be shared across interchangeable agents and adapt coordination as populations or interaction conditions change, without redefining a global organization. We introduce Waggle, a shared anonymous policy over bounded local views that jointly selects task actions, semantic communication, and local commitment updates. Repeated execution of the same law allows coordination to form, persist, and reorganize online without explicit roles or global topology. To learn this law across interchangeable agents and evolving coordination, we develop Swarm-Consistent Distillation (SCD), combining anonymous-orbit consistency with rollout-grounded prediction of the next local coordination field, with no added inference-time components. Across diverse coordination settings, the same learned law remains effective as populations and interaction budgets change, retains over 96% of substrate-specific oracle quality, and transfers without retraining; SCD further improves reorganization after counterevidence. Together, these results show that LLM-agent organization can emerge and adapt through repeated execution of a learned local law.
随着 LLM 智能体越来越多地协作处理复杂任务，如何组织它们之间的交互成为一个核心设计问题。现有多智能体系统通常学习或调整显式的角色、层级结构、路由策略或通信拓扑。我们将学习目标转向一条可复用的局部法则，使其能够在可互换的智能体之间共享，并在群体规模或交互条件变化时调整协调方式，而无需重新定义全局组织结构。我们提出 Waggle，这是一种基于有界局部视图的共享匿名策略，可联合选择任务动作、语义通信和局部承诺更新。通过反复执行同一条法则，协调能够在线形成、持续存在并重新组织，而无需显式角色或全局拓扑。为了在可互换的智能体和不断演化的协调过程中学习这条法则，我们开发了群体一致性蒸馏（Swarm-Consistent Distillation，SCD），将匿名轨道一致性与基于展开轨迹的下一局部协调场预测相结合，且不增加任何推理时组件。在多种协调场景中，同一条学得的法则在群体规模和交互预算变化时仍然有效，保留了特定底层环境下预言机性能的 96% 以上，并且无需重新训练即可迁移；SCD 还进一步改善了出现反证后的重组能力。这些结果共同表明，LLM 智能体的组织结构可以通过反复执行一条学得的局部法则而涌现并适应变化。
1 Introduction1 引言
LLM agents can reason, use tools, and revise plans from feedback, enabling flexible collaborative systems (Yao et al., 2023). Multi-agent systems organize these capabilities through roles, managers, routes, and conversation graphs (Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2024). Recent methods further design task-dependent communication graphs, jointly generate agent compositions and topologies, or adapt connectivity and agent activation online (Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b; Yang et al., 2025b; Wu et al., 2026). Despite this flexibility, the learned object remains an explicit organization—the graph, routing policy, or agent composition.
LLM 智能体能够进行推理、使用工具并根据反馈修订计划，从而支持灵活的协作系统（Yao et al., 2023）。多智能体系统通过角色、管理者、路由和对话图来组织这些能力（Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2024）。近期方法进一步设计了依赖任务的通信图，联合生成智能体组成与拓扑，或在线调整连接关系和智能体激活状态（Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b; Yang et al., 2025b; Wu et al., 2026）。尽管具有这种灵活性，所学习的对象仍然是显式的组织结构，即图、路由策略或智能体组成。
Swarm intelligence suggests a different organizing principle: persistent yet revisable population-level coordination can emerge from repeated local reactions to local signals, without requiring a population-wide plan (Bonabeau et al., 1999; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Seeley et al., 2012). Related decentralized and swarm-inspired learning systems provide computational precedents for this local-rule view (Hüttenrauch et al., 2019; Shaw et al., 2022; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025). Inspired by this local-rule view, we shift the learning target from explicit organization to the reusable local law that generates and revises it. This law can be shared across interchangeable agents and reused as populations or interaction conditions change.
群体智能提出了另一种组织原则：通过对局部信号反复作出局部反应，可以涌现出持久但可修订的群体层面协调，而无需覆盖整个群体的计划（Bonabeau et al., 1999; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Seeley et al., 2012）。相关的去中心化学习系统和受群体智能启发的学习系统，为这种局部规则视角提供了计算方面的先例（Hüttenrauch et al., 2019; Shaw et al., 2022; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025）。受这种局部规则视角的启发，我们将学习目标从显式组织结构转向生成并修订该结构的可复用局部法则。这条法则可以在可互换的智能体之间共享，并在群体规模或交互条件变化时复用。
Yet effective local laws require more than individually reasonable actions. Proposals based on private observations can become redundant, conflicting, or stale when composed across a population. This failure is pronounced in Silo-Bench: at $N=64$ , transient-exchange and fixed-rule baselines reach only about 15% success, compared with 58.2% for our method (Table 1), and additional interaction alone does not close the gap (Figure 2). The bottleneck is therefore not merely local competence or message availability, but how a shared local rule composes proposals into evolving collective state.
然而，有效的局部法则所需的不仅仅是单独看来合理的动作。基于私有观测提出的提案，在整个群体中组合时可能变得冗余、相互冲突或过时。这一失效现象在 Silo-Bench 中尤为明显：当 $N=64$ 时，瞬时交换基线和固定规则基线的成功率仅约为 15%，而我们的方法达到 58.2%（表 1），且仅增加交互并不能弥合这一差距（图 2）。因此，瓶颈不仅在于局部能力或消息可用性，还在于共享的局部规则如何将提案组合为不断演化的集体状态。
We therefore treat proposals as inputs to coordination and learn the shared policy that determines each local decision. This leads to our central question: can a shared anonymous local law generate, maintain, and revise organization online through repeated execution? 在方法上，我们提出群体一致性蒸馏（Swarm-Consistent Distillation，SCD），训练该法则，使其在匿名局部视图之间作出一致的决策，并学习能够预测不断演变的协调状态的表征，而无需在推理时引入额外机制。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
LLM multi-agent organization.LLM 多智能体组织。
LLM multi-agent systems encode organization through roles, identities, protocols, or programmable interaction patterns (Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a). DyLAN and AgentVerse adapt team composition and collaboration (Liu et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024b). Other methods optimize workflows or communication topology, or jointly generate task-conditioned agent compositions and graphs, as in ARG-Designer (Zhang et al., 2025c; Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025a; Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b). Others route or activate specialists through adaptive connectivity, publish–subscribe structures, or specialized agent classes (Wu et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026a; Li et al., 2025). LLawCo derives natural-language cooperation principles from failures for embodied planners, while multi-agent distillation compresses interaction graphs or dynamics into a student (Zhou et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2024a; Luo et al., 2026). Agent Distillation trains smaller agents on teacher-generated interaction trajectories (Kang et al., 2025). Across these approaches, organization remains represented, adapted, or compressed through system-level structures or agents; Waggle instead learns one shared anonymous local law whose repeated execution generates and revises organization online.
LLM 多智能体系统通过角色、身份、协议或可编程的交互模式来编码组织 (Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a)。DyLAN 和 AgentVerse 对团队组成与协作进行自适应调整 (Liu et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024b)。其他方法优化工作流或通信拓扑，或像 ARG-Designer 那样，联合生成以任务为条件的智能体组成和图结构 (Zhang et al., 2025c; Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025a; Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b)。还有一些方法通过自适应连接、发布–订阅结构或专门化的智能体类别，将任务路由至专家智能体或激活这些智能体 (Wu et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026a; Li et al., 2025)。LLawCo 从失败中提炼面向具身规划器的自然语言合作原则，而多智能体蒸馏则将交互图或交互动力学压缩到学生模型中 (Zhou et al., 2026; Chen et al., 2024a; Luo et al., 2026)。Agent Distillation 使用教师生成的交互轨迹来训练较小的智能体 (Kang et al., 2025)。在这些方法中，组织仍然通过系统级结构或智能体来表示、调整或压缩；Waggle 则学习一条共享的匿名局部法则，通过反复执行该法则，在线生成并修订组织。
Learned communication and swarm coordination.学习式通信与群体协调。
Learned-communication methods jointly optimize control with message content, broadcast gates, or recipient selection (Sukhbaatar et al., 2016; Foerster et al., 2016; Singh et al., 2019; Das et al., 2019), while shared-policy swarm RL uses interchangeable controllers with local, variable-size observations (Hüttenrauch et al., 2019). LLM2Swarm explores LLM-based controller synthesis and per-robot reasoning, whereas GenSwarm generates and deploys task-specific multi-robot code policies from natural-language instructions (Strobel et al., 2024; Ji et al., 2026). Population protocols repeatedly apply a shared local transition rule among anonymous finite-state agents, whereas stigmergic systems coordinate through persistent local state, from ant-colony reinforcement and evaporation to learned deposition and sensing in ForMIC (Angluin et al., 2006; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Dorigo & Gambardella, 1997; Shaw et al., 2022). Building on shared control, learned communication, local rules, and persistent state, Waggle learns the organization-generating local law itself.
学习式通信方法将控制与消息内容、广播门控或接收者选择进行联合优化 (Sukhbaatar et al., 2016; Foerster et al., 2016; Singh et al., 2019; Das et al., 2019)，而共享策略的群体强化学习使用可互换的控制器，并以大小可变的局部观测作为输入 (Hüttenrauch et al., 2019)。LLM2Swarm 探索基于 LLM 的控制器合成和逐机器人推理，而 GenSwarm 则根据自然语言指令生成并部署面向特定任务的多机器人代码策略 (Strobel et al., 2024; Ji et al., 2026)。种群协议（population protocols）在匿名有限状态智能体之间反复应用共享的局部转移规则，而迹协调（stigmergic）系统则通过持久化的局部状态进行协调，其形式涵盖蚁群中的强化与蒸发，以及 ForMIC 中通过学习获得的沉积与感知 (Angluin et al., 2006; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Dorigo & Gambardella, 1997; Shaw et al., 2022)。在共享控制、学习式通信、局部规则和持久化状态的基础上，Waggle 学习的是生成组织的局部法则本身。
Symmetry-consistent learning.对称一致性学习。
Anonymous-network theory characterizes when topology and local information permit symmetry-sensitive tasks such as leader election (Yamashita & Kameda, 1996). Permutation structure can be built in through parameter sharing or set architectures, or promoted through transformed examples and consistency objectives; related work also regularizes task-equivalent prompts and models transition regularities in world and local MARL models (Ravanbakhsh et al., 2017; Zaheer et al., 2017; Hounie et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2022; Park et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2023b). SCD brings these ideas into a deployed anonymous local policy by promoting consistent decisions across equivalent local views and representations that track evolving coordination state.
匿名网络理论刻画了拓扑和局部信息在何种条件下允许完成领导者选举等对对称性敏感的任务 (Yamashita & Kameda, 1996)。置换结构可以通过参数共享或集合架构内置于模型中，也可以通过变换后的样例和一致性目标加以促进；相关工作还对任务等价的提示施加正则化，并在世界模型和局部多智能体强化学习（MARL）模型中对转移规律进行建模 (Ravanbakhsh et al., 2017; Zaheer et al., 2017; Hounie et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2022; Park et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2023b)。SCD 通过促进等价局部视图之间的决策一致性，以及学习跟踪不断演变的协调状态的表征，将这些思想引入部署后的匿名局部策略。
3 Waggle: The Deployed Anonymous Local Law3 Waggle：部署后的匿名局部法则
Waggle separates a learned semantic reaction law from a fixed local runtime. We first define what each anonymous node observes and decides, then show how repeated decisions generate reversible coordination, and finally state the boundary between learned semantics and substrate realization.
Waggle 将学得的语义反应法则与固定的局部运行时分离。我们首先定义每个匿名节点观测什么、作出什么决策，随后展示反复决策如何生成可逆的协调，最后明确学得的语义与底层基底实现之间的边界。
3.1 Anonymous local view3.1 匿名局部视图
In the primary synchronous runtime, $t$ indexes local-update rounds on a contact graph $G_{t}=(V,E_{t})$ , with all active-node views formed from the same pre-update state. Node $i$ has private task state and evidence $p_{i}^{t}$ , a set $\mathcal{H}_{i}^{t}$ of currently incident channel handles, and an optional local commitment $c_{i}^{t}$ . Handles are opaque, sampled independently for each node from an episode-local pool, and fixed only within that episode; they are routing references rather than designated agent identities. Every active node receives the same bounded interface,
在主要的同步运行时中，$t$ 表示接触图 $G_{t}=(V,E_{t})$ 上的局部更新轮次，所有活跃节点的视图均由同一更新前状态构建。节点 $i$ 具有私有任务状态与证据 $p_{i}^{t}$、当前与其关联的通道句柄集合 $\mathcal{H}_{i}^{t}$，以及可选的局部承诺 $c_{i}^{t}$。句柄（handle）是不透明的，从单个回合内的句柄池中为每个节点独立采样，且仅在该回合内保持固定；它们是路由引用，而非指定的智能体身份。每个活跃节点都接收相同的有界接口，
$$\ell_{i}^{t}=\operatorname{Canon}\!\left(q,\;p_{i}^{t},\;w_{i}^{t},\;\{\bm{\phi}_{ih}^{t}\}_{h\in\mathcal{H}_{i}^{t}},\;c_{i}^{t},\;b_{i}^{t}\right),$$
where $q$ is the task contract, $w_{i}^{t}$ is an adapter-provided candidate native action derived from private evidence $p_{i}^{t}$ , and $b_{i}^{t}$ is the remaining local budget. The private proposal is an input to the joint local decision, not a separate decision or language-model call. $\operatorname{Canon}$ standardizes field structure rather than substrate semantics: adapters place native task contracts, private observations, and proposals in the common $q$ , $p_{i}^{t}$ , and $w_{i}^{t}$ slots, while incident communication uses a shared bounded trace schema. Native action names accompany the same controller instruction (Appendix D). Serialization exposes neither persistent identities nor a global node ordering.
其中，$q$ 是任务契约，$w_{i}^{t}$ 是适配器根据私有证据 $p_{i}^{t}$ 提供的候选原生动作，$b_{i}^{t}$ 是剩余的局部预算。私有提案是联合局部决策的输入，而非独立的决策或语言模型调用。$\operatorname{Canon}$ 统一的是字段结构，而非底层环境的语义：适配器将原生任务契约、私有观测和提案放入共用的 $q$、$p_{i}^{t}$ 和 $w_{i}^{t}$ 槽位，而通过关联通道进行的通信则使用共享的有界迹记录模式。原生动作名称随同一套控制器指令一并提供（附录 D）。序列化既不暴露持久身份，也不暴露全局节点排序。
A policy is anonymous-local when all nodes share $\theta$ , every social input arrives through an incident channel, and neither its view nor output schema contains a designated identity, role, roster, population-size field, non-neighbor state, or global transcript. The interface enforces anonymity; Section 4.2 trains consistency across equivalent views obtained by permuting incident records and consistently relabeling opaque local references.
当所有节点共享 $\theta$、所有社会交互输入均通过关联通道到达，且策略的视图和输出模式均不包含指定身份、角色、成员名册、群体规模字段、非邻居状态或全局交互记录时，该策略就是匿名局部策略。该接口强制保证匿名性；第 4.2 节通过对关联记录进行排列置换，并一致地重新标记不透明的局部引用，获得等价视图，进而训练这些视图之间的一致性。
3.2 One joint local decision3.2 一次联合局部决策
Each node invokes the same controller once per local update,
每个节点在每次局部更新时调用同一个控制器一次，
$$\tilde{d}_{i}^{t}=\mathrm{Decode}_{\theta}(\ell_{i}^{t}),\qquad d_{i}^{t}=\mathrm{Valid}_{s}(\tilde{d}_{i}^{t},\ell_{i}^{t})=(a_{i}^{t},m_{i}^{t},\Delta c_{i}^{t}),$$
where $s$ denotes the substrate, $a_{i}^{t}$ is a task-level action, $m_{i}^{t}$ is an optional bounded semantic update addressed through incident handles, and $\Delta c_{i}^{t}$ is an optional local commitment update. The decoder proposes all three components jointly, and the substrate validator admits only locally legal fields and trace relations. The decision type defines the available local effects, while $\pi_{\theta}$ learns their state-dependent selection and semantic content. All three components are produced by one decode, so acting and communicating are chosen jointly rather than by separate procedures.
其中，$s$ 表示底层环境，$a_{i}^{t}$ 是任务级动作，$m_{i}^{t}$ 是通过关联句柄寻址的可选有界语义更新，$\Delta c_{i}^{t}$ 是可选的局部承诺更新。解码器联合提出这三个分量，而底层环境验证器仅接纳局部合法的字段和迹记录关系。决策类型定义可用的局部作用，而 $\pi_{\theta}$ 学习如何根据状态选择这些作用及其语义内容。三个分量均由一次解码生成，因此行动与通信是联合选择的，而非由不同程序分别选择。

4 Learning the Law with Swarm-Consistent Distillation4 通过群体一致性蒸馏学习法则
Decision distillation teaches the policy to emit valid local records, but leaves two swarm-specific ambiguities unresolved. First, equivalent anonymous views may induce different semantic decisions when incident order and opaque names change. Second, imitation alone does not require the controller representation to capture how the local coordination field evolves during collective execution. Swarm-Consistent Distillation (SCD) addresses these ambiguities with consistency across anonymous views and rollout-grounded next-field prediction.
决策蒸馏教会策略输出有效的局部记录，但仍有两种群体特有的歧义尚未解决。首先，当关联记录的顺序和不透明名称发生变化时，等价的匿名视图可能诱发不同的语义决策。其次，仅靠模仿并不要求控制器表征捕捉局部协调场在集体执行过程中如何演变。群体一致性蒸馏（Swarm-Consistent Distillation，SCD）通过匿名视图之间的一致性以及以执行轨迹为依据的下一时刻场预测来消除这些歧义。

4.2 Consistency across anonymous views4.2 匿名视图之间的一致性
For a local view $\ell$ , let $\mathcal{G}(\ell)$ be the family generated by incident-record permutations and view-local bijections over opaque channel and claim references. For each training pair, we sample $g\sim\mathcal{G}(\ell)$ and construct $(g\ell,gd^{\star})$ by applying $g$ consistently to the view and record, while non-identifier task content remains unchanged. Thus the pair encodes one anonymous local situation under two surface representations. Let $\rho(d)$ be an identifier-free summary of the task action, communication act, trace attributes, and commitment; by construction, $\rho(gd)=\rho(d)$ .
对于局部视图 $\ell$，令 $\mathcal{G}(\ell)$ 为由关联记录的排列以及视图局部范围内不透明通道引用和声明引用上的双射所生成的变换族。对于每个训练对，我们采样 $g\sim\mathcal{G}(\ell)$，并通过将 $g$ 一致地应用于视图和记录来构造 $(g\ell,gd^{\star})$，同时保持非标识符的任务内容不变。因此，该配对以两种表面表示编码同一个匿名局部情境。令 $\rho(d)$ 为任务动作、通信行为、轨迹属性和承诺的不含标识符的摘要；根据构造，有 $\rho(gd)=\rho(d)$。
Orbit supervision has two complementary roles. Transformed token supervision preserves the correct opaque references in the emitted record, while identifier-free alignment requires the two views to represent the same semantic decision. A factorized training head $Q_{\theta,\psi}$ predicts $\rho(d)$ from the controller representation, giving the compact objective
轨道监督具有两种互补作用。变换后的词元监督保留输出记录中正确的不透明引用，而不含标识符的对齐则要求两个视图表示相同的语义决策。因子化训练头 $Q_{\theta,\psi}$ 根据控制器表示预测 $\rho(d)$，得到如下紧凑目标
$$\displaystyle\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{orbit}}=\mathbb{E}_{(\ell,d^{\star}),g}\Big[ \\ \displaystyle-\log p_{\theta}(gd^{\star}\mid g\ell)+\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sup}}\!\left(Q_{\theta,\psi}(\ell),Q_{\theta,\psi}(g\ell);\rho(d^{\star})\right) \\ \displaystyle+\beta D_{\mathrm{SKL}}\!\left(Q_{\theta,\psi}(\ell),Q_{\theta,\psi}(g\ell)\right)\Big].$$
Here $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sup}}$ is factorized supervised cross-entropy on both views and $D_{\mathrm{SKL}}$ is symmetric KL divergence. Supervision prevents content-free agreement, while transformation-consistent augmentation preserves executable references (Hounie et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2022); Appendix F.2 gives the complete factorization.
这里，$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{sup}}$ 是两个视图上的因子化监督交叉熵，$D_{\mathrm{SKL}}$ 是对称 KL 散度。监督可防止不包含实质内容的一致，而与变换保持一致的数据增强则保留可执行引用 (Hounie et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2022)；附录 F.2 给出了完整的因子分解。
Exact local equivariance lifts to the swarm level under an equivariant runtime.在等变运行时下，精确的局部等变性可提升至群体层面。
Let a legal $\Gamma$ have node-permutation component $\sigma$ and consistently transport every schema-designated opaque reference. It induces $g_{\Gamma,i}^{t}$ on local views and records, including incident-order permutations, with $(\Gamma D^{t})_{\sigma(i)}=g_{\Gamma,i}^{t}d_{i}^{t}$ ; below we suppress the $\Gamma,t$ indices on $g$ . Write $F$ for one synchronous update and let $\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$ denote the complete local decode-and-admit pipeline, including permitted repair and conservative projection.
设合法变换 $\Gamma$ 的节点置换分量为 $\sigma$，并一致地变换模式所指定的每个不透明引用。它在局部视图和记录上诱导出 $g_{\Gamma,i}^{t}$，其中包括关联顺序的排列，且满足 $(\Gamma D^{t})_{\sigma(i)}=g_{\Gamma,i}^{t}d_{i}^{t}$；下文省略 $g$ 上的 $\Gamma,t$ 下标。用 $F$ 表示一次同步更新，并令 $\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$ 表示完整的局部解码与准入流程，其中包括允许的修复和保守投影。
Proposition 1 (Conditional local-to-global relabeling equivariance).命题 1（有条件的从局部到全局的重标记等变性）。
Suppose every node uses the same $\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$ . Suppose also that view construction, $\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$ , and $F$ commute with every legal relabeling:
假设每个节点都使用相同的 $\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$。还假设视图构造、$\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}$ 和 $F$ 均与每一种合法重标记可交换：
$$\displaystyle\operatorname{View}_{\sigma(i)}(\Gamma S)=g_{i}\operatorname{View}_{i}(S),\qquad\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}(g_{i}\ell)=g_{i}\pi_{\theta}^{\mathrm{dep}}(\ell),\qquad F(\Gamma S,\Gamma D;\Gamma\xi)=\Gamma F(S,D;\xi).$$
If $S_{\Gamma}^{0}=\Gamma S^{0}$ and exogenous randomness is coupled by the same relabeling, then $(S_{\Gamma}^{t},D_{\Gamma}^{t})=(\Gamma S^{t},\Gamma D^{t})$ for every $t$ . Consequently every trajectory functional $J$ satisfying $J(\Gamma\tau)=J(\tau)$ is unchanged.
若 $S_{\Gamma}^{0}=\Gamma S^{0}$，且外生随机性通过同一重标记进行耦合，则对每个 $t$ 都有 $(S_{\Gamma}^{t},D_{\Gamma}^{t})=(\Gamma S^{t},\Gamma D^{t})$。因此，满足 $J(\Gamma\tau)=J(\tau)$ 的每个轨迹泛函 $J$ 均保持不变。
Appendices E.1–E.2 give the proof and its event-driven extension under equivariant scheduling.
附录 E.1–E.2 给出了证明及其在等变调度下的事件驱动扩展。
Corollary 1 (Symmetry preservation).推论 1（对称性保持）。
Under Proposition 1, consider a deterministic execution and a nonidentity legal relabeling $\Gamma$ satisfying $\Gamma S^{0}=S^{0}$ and $\Gamma\xi^{t}=\xi^{t}$ . Then $\Gamma S^{t}=S^{t}$ and $\Gamma D^{t}=D^{t}$ for every $t$ ; hence a uniquely selected node must be fixed by $\Gamma$ .
在命题 1 的条件下，考虑一次确定性执行，以及满足 $\Gamma S^{0}=S^{0}$ 和 $\Gamma\xi^{t}=\xi^{t}$ 的非恒等合法重标记 $\Gamma$。则对每个 $t$ 都有 $\Gamma S^{t}=S^{t}$ 和 $\Gamma D^{t}=D^{t}$；因此，唯一选出的节点必须是 $\Gamma$ 的不动点。
Thus anonymity preserves genuine symmetries until task state, topology, interaction history, or episode-local randomness distinguishes the alternatives. SCD promotes the proposition’s local premise, while the AgentsNet-RP diagnostic in Appendix I tests the corollary’s symmetry-breaking prediction.
因此，匿名性会保持真正的对称性，直到任务状态、拓扑、交互历史或单次运行内的局部随机性将各个备选对象区分开来。SCD 促进该命题的局部前提成立，而附录 I 中的 AgentsNet-RP 诊断则检验该推论关于对称性破缺的预测。
4.3 Anticipating the local coordination field4.3 预测局部协调场
Anonymous consistency constrains how a decision is represented but does not directly teach how its local coordination field evolves. After fitting a decision-supervised warm start, we execute complete training-seed episodes through the fixed runtime and collect genuine adjacent transitions $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{tr}}=\{(\ell_{i}^{t},d_{i}^{t},\ell_{i}^{t+1})\}$ , where $d_{i}^{t}$ is the admitted focal decision and $\ell_{i}^{t+1}$ is the next view of the same node after the synchronous update. The bounded, identifier-free target is

$$m(\ell)=[\operatorname{Hist}_{n}(\ell),\operatorname{Hist}_{s}(\ell),\operatorname{Hist}_{x}(\ell),\operatorname{Hist}_{T}(\ell),r(\ell),\kappa(\ell)],$$
where the four histograms summarize incident novelty, support, conflict, and remaining lifetime, while $r$ and $\kappa$ denote local readiness and commitment. Writing $h_{\theta}(\ell_{i}^{t})$ for the controller’s final current-view hidden state, a training-only head $f_{\omega}$ receives this representation together with the admitted-decision summary $\rho(d_{i}^{t})$ and predicts $\hat{m}_{i}^{t+1}=f_{\omega}(h_{\theta}(\ell_{i}^{t}),\rho(d_{i}^{t}))$ . We group the prediction loss by target family,
其中，四个直方图概括关联记录的新颖性、支持度、冲突和剩余生存时间，而 $r$ 和 $\kappa$ 分别表示局部就绪度和承诺。用 $h_{\theta}(\ell_{i}^{t})$ 表示控制器对当前视图的最终隐藏状态，仅在训练时使用的预测头 $f_{\omega}$ 接收该表示及获准执行的决策摘要 $\rho(d_{i}^{t})$，并预测 $\hat{m}_{i}^{t+1}=f_{\omega}(h_{\theta}(\ell_{i}^{t}),\rho(d_{i}^{t}))$。我们按目标类别对预测损失进行分组，
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{field}}=\mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{tr}}}\left[\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{hist}}(\hat{m}_{i}^{t+1},m(\ell_{i}^{t+1}))+\lambda_{\mathrm{state}}\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{state}}(\hat{m}_{i}^{t+1},m(\ell_{i}^{t+1}))\right].$$
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{hist}}$ averages cross-entropy over the four trace histograms, whereas $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{state}}$ averages over readiness and commitment. We use equal group weight, $\lambda_{\mathrm{state}}=1$ , so the four histogram targets do not dominate the two state targets by count alone; the componentwise expression appears in Appendix F.3.
$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{hist}}$ 对四个痕迹直方图的交叉熵取平均，而 $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{state}}$ 则对就绪度和承诺状态的交叉熵取平均。我们为各组使用相同的权重，即 $\lambda_{\mathrm{state}}=1$，使四个直方图目标不会仅因数量较多而主导两个状态目标；逐分量表达式见附录 F.3。
Because neighboring nodes act simultaneously and the substrate and runtime also determine transport, expiry, and commitment updates, this objective implements rollout-conditioned next-field prediction rather than an isolated causal model of the focal decision. The objective therefore encourages the shared representation to retain local regularities involving support accumulation, readiness, commitment, conflict-triggered reopening, and expiry—local changes relevant to subsequent reorganization—rather than reconstruct a global world state (Park et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2023b).
由于相邻节点同时行动，且底层交互环境与运行时也会决定传输、过期及承诺状态的更新，因此该目标实现的是以展开轨迹为条件的下一步场预测，而非针对当前决策的独立因果模型。因此，该目标鼓励共享表示保留涉及支持累积、就绪度、承诺状态、冲突触发的重新开启以及过期的局部规律，即与后续重组相关的局部变化，而非重建全局世界状态 (Park et al., 2022; Wu et al., 2023b)。

The training-head columns verify that each auxiliary objective is optimized, while the deployment columns measure what remains in the decoder and frozen controller representation after those heads are removed (Appendix G.1).

5.5 SCD Improves Reorganization after Counterevidence5.5 SCD 改善反证出现后的重组
The late-counterevidence intervention carries the same four-arm factorial into closed loop. With the runtime fixed, the two single-term arms yield intermediate behavioral gains, while complete SCD has the strongest joint profile. Recovery, requiring conflict-triggered reopening followed by evidence-supported replacement synthesis, rises from 80.0% to 88.5%, while stale-field burden and post-conflict traffic both fall (Table 3; paired effects in Appendix G.1). Stale-field AUC integrates obsolete semantic-trace mass after conflict, so lower values indicate faster clearance. A representative trajectory shows verified conflict dissolving the old commitment, synthesis building a replacement, and both activities subsiding after the new commitment stabilizes (Appendix P.2).
后期反证干预将同一四组析因实验引入闭环。在运行时固定的情况下，两个仅含单项的实验组取得了中等程度的行为改善，而完整的 SCD 表现出最强的综合性能。恢复要求先由冲突触发重新开启，再在证据支持下合成替代方案，其比例从 80.0% 提高到 88.5%，同时陈旧场负担和冲突后通信量均有所下降（表 3；配对效应见附录 G.1）。陈旧场 AUC 对冲突后过时语义痕迹的总量进行积分，因此数值越低，表示清除速度越快。一条具有代表性的轨迹显示，经验证的冲突消解了旧承诺，合成过程构建了替代方案，而在新承诺稳定后，这两种活动均逐渐减弱（附录 P.2）。
The same structural and behavioral pattern replicates with Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct under the same runtime (Appendix G.2). This motivates the attribution question addressed next: whether the gain comes from learned semantic decisions or from the fixed runtime affordances that realize them.
在相同运行时下，Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 也复现了相同的结构与行为模式（附录 G.2）。这引出了接下来要研究的归因问题：增益究竟来自学得的语义决策，还是来自实现这些决策的固定运行时所提供的功能。

6 Conclusion and Limitations6 结论与局限性
Waggle shows that adaptive LLM-agent organization can be learned as a reusable anonymous local law, without representing organization as an explicit system-level structure. Across the tested substrates and scales, repeated local reactions form, sustain, and revise coordination, while SCD improves anonymous stability and reorganization after counterevidence. More broadly, these results suggest a complementary design principle for multi-agent systems: organization can arise repeatedly from a learned local rule instead of being specified directly.
Waggle 表明，自适应的 LLM 智能体组织方式可以被学习为一种可复用的匿名局部法则，而无需将组织表示为显式的系统级结构。在所测试的各类底层环境与规模下，反复的局部反应能够形成、维持并修订协调，而 SCD 则提高了匿名条件下的稳定性以及出现反证后的重组能力。更广泛地说，这些结果提示了多智能体系统的一项互补设计原则：组织可以从习得的局部规则中反复涌现，而无需直接加以规定。
The present study deliberately isolates this organizing law behind a bounded canonical interface and a fixed runtime. Deterministic substrate adapters expose private state, locally checkable evidence, and legal actions, while validation, bounded transport, expiry, and native realization remain fixed runtime mechanisms and are not learned end to end. The theoretical guarantee requires equivariant local decisions, view construction, and runtime updates; the empirical evidence establishes reuse across the tested local-interaction substrates and transfer settings. Appendices Q and R detail these scope boundaries and extensions.
本研究有意通过一个有界的规范接口和固定的运行时，将这一组织法则隔离出来。确定性的底层环境适配器暴露私有状态、可在局部核验的证据和合法动作，而验证、有界传输、过期处理以及原生实现仍是固定的运行时机制，并非通过端到端学习获得。理论保证要求局部决策、视图构建和运行时更新具有等变性；实证证据则确立了在所测试的局部交互底层环境与迁移设置中的可复用性。附录 Q 和 R 详细说明了这些适用范围边界与扩展。

Ethics Statement伦理声明
We study coordination in benchmark environments using teacher-generated training records. The experiments contain no human-subject data. Anonymous handles remove identity cues from the controller interface but are not a privacy guarantee. In open-world deployments, unreliable observations or malicious messages could propagate through local interactions and influence collective actions. Bounded transport, finite trace lifetimes, and reversible commitments constrain coordination dynamics but do not establish a complete security boundary. Applications involving consequential external actions should authenticate evidence provenance, sandbox tools, protect sensitive inputs, and retain appropriate human approval. The relabeling $\Gamma$ has node-bijection component $\sigma$ and transports the graph and all node-indexed state, consistently renaming every schema-designated opaque reference, including reference-bearing action arguments; it does not change topology, population size, reference-free task semantics, directions, or trace values. Delays, loss, churn, and symmetry.延迟、丢失、成员变动与对称性。

For each held-out local view $\ell$ , we sample a non-identity anonymous transformation $g$ whenever the orbit is nontrivial. Appendix Q Limitations and Deployment Boundaries附录 Q 局限性与部署边界
Interface and substrate scope.接口与底层环境的适用范围。
Waggle assumes a deterministic adapter that exposes bounded private task state, locally checkable incident evidence, and legal task actions through the shared anonymous interface (Section 3; Appendix D). We evaluate this contract on spatial and distributed-language substrates, through frozen transfer to graph-structured tasks with a new action vocabulary, and under population extrapolation beyond the primary matrix (Tables 1 and 2; Appendix C.2). These results support reuse across the tested local-interaction settings; environments in which the observation interface or transport mechanism must itself be learned remain outside the present scope.
Waggle 假设存在一个确定性适配器，通过共享的匿名接口暴露有界的私有任务状态、可在局部核验的事件证据以及合法的任务动作（第 3 节；附录 D）。我们在空间环境和分布式语言环境中，通过冻结模型后迁移至具有新动作词汇的图结构任务，以及在主要评估矩阵之外进行群体规模外推，对这一契约进行评估（表 1 和表 2；附录 C.2）。这些结果支持在所测试的局部交互设置中复用该方法；需要学习观测接口或传输机制本身的环境仍不在当前研究范围内。
Anonymity and identity-dependent coordination.匿名性与依赖身份的协调。
The present interface excludes persistent identity-indexed reputation and assigned global roles, so it does not maintain reliability histories for named agents across episodes. Task-relevant capability differences could instead enter through bounded local descriptors while arbitrary identities remain hidden (Appendix R).
当前接口不包含按身份索引的持久声誉和预先分配的全局角色，因此不会跨回合维护具名智能体的可靠性历史。与任务相关的能力差异可以通过有界的局部描述符引入，同时仍隐藏任意身份标识（附录 R）。
Learned semantics and fixed runtime.学习得到的语义与固定运行时。
Waggle learns state-dependent task, trace, and commitment decisions, while the runtime supplies validation, bounded transport, expiry, and native realization (Section 3). This leaves low-level coordination affordances in the deployment contract rather than learning them end to end. Identical-runtime decision-source comparisons, controller-fixed component interventions, and nearby spatial-runtime sweeps isolate the two sides of this boundary (Section 5.6; Appendices M.1, P.1, and P.4). Across the instrumented evaluation matrix, 99.75% of first decodes are executable as emitted and the no-communication fallback is never invoked. Remaining cases require envelope normalization, at most one validator-code-conditioned controller regeneration, or conservative projection; wrapper operations cannot originate social content (Appendix C.3). Thus the runtime constrains and realizes controller decisions rather than supplying their social semantics.
Waggle 学习依赖状态的任务、痕迹和承诺决策，而运行时提供验证、有界传输、到期失效和原生实现机制（第 3 节）。这使得底层协调支持机制仍属于部署契约的一部分，而非通过端到端学习获得。在相同运行时下对决策来源进行比较、在固定控制器的条件下进行组件干预，以及对空间运行时进行邻近参数扫描，分别考察了这一边界的两侧（第 5.6 节；附录 M.1、P.1 和 P.4）。在整个配备监测机制的评估矩阵中，99.75% 的首次解码结果在输出时即可直接执行，且从未调用无通信回退机制。其余情况需要进行封装规范化、以验证器代码为条件至多重新调用控制器生成一次，或进行保守投影；包装层操作不能自行产生社会性内容（附录 C.3）。因此，运行时负责约束并实现控制器决策，而非提供这些决策的社会语义。
Conditional symmetry guarantee.有条件的对称性保证。
Proposition 1 states the synchronous case; Appendix E.2 extends the structural guarantee to paired event-driven executions, with a distributional result under an equivariant marked-event kernel. Both require equivariant view construction, local decisions, and runtime operations, including completion-time admission in the asynchronous contract. SCD promotes the local premise but does not enforce it architecturally. We measure the learned approximation through held-out summary-, decoded-record-, and admitted-record agreement and the symmetry-breaking diagnostic (Table 3; Figure 6). Our primary benchmark executions and next-field training targets use synchronous updates. Appendix P.5 measures degradation under finite delivery delays, message loss, and temporary churn. More general scheduling and membership changes can reduce evidence freshness, alter temporal reachability, or remove task-relevant information, affecting both progress and feasibility beyond these tested regimes. Structural relabeling consistency is distinct from equal task performance across execution regimes, schedule independence, or task convergence.
命题 1 陈述了同步情形；附录 E.2 将这一结构性保证扩展至配对的事件驱动执行，并在等变的带标记事件核下给出分布层面的结果。二者都要求视图构建、局部决策和运行时操作具有等变性，其中包括异步契约中在完成时进行的准入。SCD 促进这一局部前提的成立，但并未从架构上强制保证它。我们通过留出数据上的摘要一致性、解码记录一致性、准入记录一致性以及对称性破缺诊断，衡量学习所得的近似程度（表 3；图 6）。我们的主要基准执行和下一时刻场的训练目标均采用同步更新。附录 P.5 衡量了有限交付延迟、消息丢失和临时节点流失条件下的性能下降。更一般的调度与成员变动可能降低证据的新鲜度、改变时间可达性，或移除与任务相关的信息，从而在这些已测试情形之外影响任务进展与可行性。结构重标记一致性不同于各执行模式下任务表现相同、调度无关性或任务收敛性。
Supervision and transfer.监督与迁移。
The law is trained from teacher-labeled single local views and rollout transitions on the training substrates, so its coverage is bounded by those local situations (Section 4; Appendix F). Training collection and formal evaluation use disjoint cases and seeds; the factorial arms share teacher records and optimization budgets, and the same structural and behavioral pattern replicates with a second student backbone (Appendices G.1 and G.2). In AgentsNet-RP, the controller is frozen before any benchmark instance is accessed, and no AgentsNet teacher record, rollout transition, or evaluation answer enters training or model selection (Appendix I). Continual adaptation in open-ended domains is not evaluated here.
该法则使用训练底层环境中由教师标注的单个局部视图和 rollout 转移进行训练，因此其覆盖范围受限于这些局部情境（第 4 节；附录 F）。训练数据收集与正式评估使用互不重叠的案例和随机种子；各析因实验组共享教师记录和优化预算，并且相同的结构与行为模式在第二种学生骨干模型上也得到了复现（附录 G.1 和 G.2）。在 AgentsNet-RP 中，控制器在访问任何基准实例之前即被冻结，且任何 AgentsNet 教师记录、rollout 转移或评估答案均未用于训练或模型选择（附录 I）。本文未评估开放式领域中的持续适应。
Open-world deployment.开放世界部署。
The evaluation assumes faithful benchmark observations and non-adversarial agents. Local admission checks record legality and consistency with available evidence, not the truthfulness of arbitrary external observations. In particular, the spatial readiness gate counts incident channels rather than independent evidence origins (Appendix H). Duplicate suppression and finite trace lifetime limit repetition and persistence; they do not authenticate sources or establish Byzantine fault tolerance. In open tool environments, untrusted observations could imitate a verified-conflict marker or evade canonicalization. Deployments with consequential external actions should authenticate evidence provenance, sandbox tools, and retain appropriate human approval. The experiments contain no human-subject data.
评估假定基准观测忠实可靠，且智能体不具有对抗性。局部准入检查核验记录的合法性及其与可用证据的一致性，而不核验任意外部观测的真实性。具体而言，空间就绪门控统计的是关联通道的数量，而非独立证据来源的数量（附录 H）。重复抑制与有限的轨迹存续时间限制了重复和持久留存；它们并不验证来源，也不构成拜占庭容错。在开放工具环境中，不可信观测可能仿冒已验证冲突标记，或规避规范化处理。对于涉及具有重大影响的外部操作的部署，应验证证据来源、在沙箱中运行工具，并保留适当的人工审批。实验不包含人类受试者数据。
Appendix R Future Directions附录 R 未来方向
Adaptive interfaces and broader substrates.自适应接口与更广泛的底层环境。
The local interface in Waggle separates substrate mechanics from the law that generates organization (Section 3; Appendix D). Cross-substrate evaluation and frozen AgentsNet-RP transfer provide a starting point for extending this separation to multimodal observations, tool-using agents, embodied interaction, partially specified action spaces, and environments whose local interface changes over time (Tables 1 and 2). Parts of the adapter could also be learned or continually refined while retaining provenance, locality, boundedness, and legal actuation. The central design question is how much interface plasticity can be introduced without relocating organizational semantics from the shared law into the substrate layer.
Waggle 中的局部接口将底层环境的运行机制与生成组织的法则分离（第 3 节；附录 D）。跨底层环境评估和冻结模型的 AgentsNet-RP 迁移，为将这种分离扩展至多模态观测、使用工具的智能体、具身交互、部分指定的动作空间，以及局部接口随时间变化的环境提供了起点（表 1 和表 2）。适配器的部分组件也可以通过学习获得或持续改进，同时保留来源可追溯性、局部性、有界性和动作执行的合法性。核心设计问题在于：在不将组织语义从共享法则转移到底层环境层的前提下，能够引入多大程度的接口可塑性。
Asynchronous and changing populations.异步与动态群体。
Appendix E.2 establishes conditional relabeling consistency for event-driven execution, and Appendix P.5 tests bounded delays, message loss, and temporary churn. Further evaluation could examine irregular clocks, longer delays, ongoing membership turnover, and asynchronous model-call completion. It should distinguish matched call budgets from matched time deadlines and account for whether departing nodes remove information needed to solve the task.
附录 E.2 确立了事件驱动执行下的条件重标记一致性，附录 P.5 则测试了有界延迟、消息丢失和暂时性的成员进出。进一步评估可以考察不规则时钟、更长延迟、持续的成员更替，以及模型调用的异步完成。评估应区分调用预算相同与时间截止期限相同的情形，并考虑离开节点是否会移除解决任务所需的信息。
Capability-conditioned local laws.以能力为条件的局部法则。
Parameter sharing constrains the coordination rule rather than requiring identical task-relevant capabilities. A heterogeneous extension could expose bounded local descriptors of available tools, action constraints, and context or compute budgets while retaining one shared controller and the joint decision schema. The adapter would validate actions against the focal node’s capabilities. Budget-dependent truncation and summarization would remain local and satisfy the same equivariance requirements, including insensitivity to arbitrary handle ordering. Capability attributes would travel with their nodes under relabeling, while information about other nodes would arrive through incident communication. Under equivariant view construction, the complete deployed map, and runtime operations, the local-to-global argument (Proposition 1; Appendix E.2) applies to this augmented state. Anonymity removes dependence on arbitrary names while preserving genuine capability differences as a source of task-relevant asymmetry, without assigning organizational roles. This extension retains a shared coordination policy; mixtures of independently parameterized controllers require separate analysis. Generalization of the current checkpoint to unseen capability mixtures remains to be evaluated.
参数共享约束的是协调规则，而非要求与任务相关的能力完全相同。一种异构扩展可以提供关于可用工具、动作约束以及上下文或计算预算的有界局部描述，同时保留单一共享控制器和联合决策模式。适配器将根据当前节点的能力验证动作。依赖预算的截断与摘要仍将在局部进行，并满足相同的等变性要求，包括不受任意句柄排序的影响。重标记时，能力属性将随其所属节点一同变换，而关于其他节点的信息将通过关联通信传入。当视图构建、完整部署映射和运行时操作均满足等变性时，从局部到全局的论证（命题 1；附录 E.2）适用于这一增广状态。匿名性消除了对任意名称的依赖，同时保留真实的能力差异，使其成为与任务相关的不对称性的来源，而不分配组织角色。这一扩展保留了共享协调策略；对于由独立参数化的控制器组成的混合系统，需要另行分析。当前检查点对未见过的能力组合的泛化能力仍有待评估。
More autonomous and continual learning.更自主的学习与持续学习。
SCD currently combines teacher-labeled local decisions with rollout-grounded transition supervision (Section 4; Appendix F). Disjoint evaluation and frozen target-substrate transfer establish a clean basis for reducing external supervision further (Appendices F.1 and I). Candidate signals include self-supervised local prediction, preference or outcome feedback, self-play, offline or online reinforcement learning, and locally generated counterfactual trajectories. Continual updates introduce a sharper problem: new local experience should improve the law without erasing anonymous-view consistency or destabilizing previously useful coordination reactions. Local replay, constrained policy updates, and decentralized parameter adaptation offer possible routes to combining plasticity with the invariances promoted by SCD.
SCD 目前将教师标注的局部决策与基于 rollout 的转移监督相结合（第 4 节；附录 F）。互不重叠的评估与冻结模型的目标底层环境迁移，为进一步减少外部监督奠定了清晰的基础（附录 F.1 和 I）。候选信号包括自监督局部预测、偏好或结果反馈、自博弈、离线或在线强化学习，以及局部生成的反事实轨迹。持续更新带来了一个更为尖锐的问题：新的局部经验应当改进局部法则，同时不破坏匿名视图一致性，也不使先前有用的协调反应失稳。局部经验回放、受约束的策略更新和去中心化参数自适应，为将可塑性与 SCD 所促进的不变性相结合提供了可能的途径。
Learned transport and multi-timescale coordination state.学习得到的传输机制与多时间尺度协调状态。
The current runtime fixes validity, transport, expiry, and native realization so that semantic decisions can be attributed to the policy. Controller-fixed component interventions and runtime sensitivity sweeps provide an empirical base for constrained co-design of these mechanics (Section 5.6; Appendices P.1 and P.4). Future systems could learn when to allocate communication bandwidth, how long locally useful information should persist, which traces to compress or forget, and how readiness thresholds should respond to local conditions. Longer tasks may also benefit from several timescales of bounded coordination state. Such adaptation should preserve hard local constraints on provenance, bounded communication, legal actuation, and reversibility while learning the operating policy around them.
当前运行时将有效性、传输、失效和原生实现机制固定下来，以便将语义决策归因于策略。在控制器保持固定时进行的组件干预，以及运行时敏感性扫描，为这些机制的受约束协同设计提供了实证基础（第 5.6 节；附录 P.1 和 P.4）。未来系统可以学习何时分配通信带宽、局部有用的信息应保留多久、应压缩或遗忘哪些痕迹，以及就绪阈值应如何响应局部条件。持续时间更长的任务也可能受益于多个时间尺度上的有界协调状态。这种自适应应在学习围绕这些约束运行的策略时，保留对来源、有界通信、合法执行和可逆性的硬性局部约束。
Theory beyond exact relabeling equivariance.超越精确重标记等变性的理论。
Propositions 1 and 2 provide a structural basis for a quantitative theory of learned local laws. Approximate extensions could bound finite-horizon trajectory deviation under uniform local error controls over reachable histories. Beyond symmetry, open questions include conditions for convergence or recurrent organization under asynchronous updates, communication complexity, recovery time after counterevidence, bounds on stale coordination mass, and the relation between local memory horizon and population-scale stability. Progress and convergence results would additionally need conditions on event scheduling, information reachability, and the local reaction dynamics. These questions connect learned LLM swarms to population protocols, distributed algorithms, and stochastic interacting systems.
命题 1 和 2 为学习得到的局部法则的定量理论提供了结构性基础。近似扩展可以在对可达历史施加一致的局部误差控制的条件下，为有限时域内的轨迹偏差给出界限。除对称性之外，尚待解决的问题还包括异步更新下收敛或形成反复出现的组织的条件、通信复杂度、出现反证后的恢复时间、陈旧协调量的界限，以及局部记忆时域与群体尺度稳定性之间的关系。关于进展性和收敛性的结果还需要对事件调度、信息可达性和局部反应动力学施加条件。这些问题将学习得到的 LLM 群体与群体协议、分布式算法和随机交互系统联系起来。
Robustness, trust, and partially aligned participants.鲁棒性、信任与目标部分一致的参与者。
The bounded interface makes trust assumptions explicit and creates a natural place to attach stronger safeguards. Open systems may contain corrupted observations, misleading messages, prompt injection, compromised tools, or participants whose objectives are only partially aligned. Research directions include authenticated provenance, robust local evidence aggregation, privacy-preserving coordination, and trust mechanisms that do not depend on stable global identities. Formal fault models could measure graceful degradation under Byzantine, unavailable, or colluding nodes, while mixed human–agent and mixed-incentive populations would test whether local reversibility remains effective when disagreement is strategic rather than incidental. These extensions build on the deployment boundary stated in Appendix Q.
有界接口使信任假设显式化，并为引入更强的防护措施提供了一个自然的切入点。开放系统可能包含受损观测、误导性消息、提示注入、遭到入侵的工具，或目标仅部分一致的参与者。研究方向包括经过认证的来源、鲁棒的局部证据聚合、隐私保护协调，以及不依赖稳定全局身份的信任机制。形式化故障模型可以衡量系统在存在拜占庭节点、不可用节点或串谋节点时的性能平缓退化情况，而人类与智能体混合的群体以及具有不同激励的群体，则可用于检验当分歧出于策略考量而非偶然因素时，局部可逆性是否仍然有效。这些扩展以附录 Q 所述的部署边界为基础。
Longer-horizon evaluation and measurement of emergent organization.更长时域的评估与涌现组织的测量。
The current evaluation separates population and interaction scaling, frozen transfer, anonymous stability, and reorganization after counterevidence (Sections 5.2–5.6). Future benchmarks could combine these stresses through long-lived objectives, multiple competing commitments, changing goals, node churn, heterogeneous capabilities, and repeated environmental shocks. Richer diagnostics could measure formation time, turnover of coordination state, complete recovery trajectories, communication concentration, and the diversity and persistence of emergent organizational patterns. Causal interventions on local evidence or topology could further distinguish communication patterns that merely correlate with success from organization that mediates collective behavior. Broader backbone families, larger deployments, and system-level measurements of latency, memory, energy, and model-call cost would complement the algorithmic evidence reported here.
当前评估分别考察群体规模扩展与交互规模扩展、冻结迁移、匿名条件下的稳定性，以及出现反证后的重组（第 5.2–5.6 节）。未来的基准可以通过长期持续的目标、多个相互竞争的承诺、不断变化的目标、节点更替、异构能力和反复发生的环境冲击，将这些压力因素结合起来。更丰富的诊断指标可以衡量形成时间、协调状态的更替、完整的恢复轨迹、通信集中度，以及涌现组织模式的多样性和持久性。对局部证据或拓扑结构进行因果干预，可以进一步区分仅与成功相关的通信模式，以及在集体行为中发挥中介作用的组织。更广泛的骨干模型家族、更大规模的部署，以及对延迟、内存、能耗和模型调用成本的系统级测量，将补充本文报告的算法层面证据。
Across these directions, the broader question is how far adaptive organization can emerge from repeated execution of a reusable local law, and when, if ever, explicit system-level structure provides additional value.
贯穿这些方向的更广泛问题是：通过反复执行可复用的局部法则，自适应组织能够涌现到何种程度，以及显式的系统级结构究竟是否会带来额外价值，若会，又是在何种情况下。
