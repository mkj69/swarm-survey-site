Abstract摘要
Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) has emerged as a promising paradigm for improving the performance of large language models through collaborative reasoning. Despite recent advances, the key factors driving MAD’s effectiveness remain unclear. In this work, we disentangle MAD into two key components–Majority Voting and inter-agent Debate–and assess their respective contributions. Through extensive experiments across seven NLP benchmarks, we find that Majority Voting alone accounts for most of the performance gains typically attributed to MAD. To explain this, we propose a theoretical framework that models debate as a stochastic process. We prove that it induces a martingale over agents’ belief trajectories, implying that debate alone does not improve expected correctness. Guided by these insights, we demonstrate that targeted interventions, by biasing the belief update toward correction, can meaningfully enhance debate effectiveness. Overall, our findings suggest that while MAD has potential, simple ensembling methods remain strong and more reliable alternatives in many practical settings. Code is released in https://github.com/deeplearning-wisc/debate-or-vote.
多智能体辩论（Multi-Agent Debate，MAD）已成为一种颇具潜力的范式，通过协作推理提升大语言模型的性能。尽管近期取得了一些进展，但决定 MAD 有效性的关键因素仍不清楚。在本研究中，我们将 MAD 分解为多数投票和智能体间辩论两个关键组成部分，并评估它们各自的贡献。通过在七个自然语言处理（NLP）基准上开展广泛实验，我们发现，通常归因于 MAD 的性能提升，大部分仅靠多数投票就能实现。为解释这一现象，我们提出了一个将辩论建模为随机过程的理论框架。我们证明，辩论使智能体的信念轨迹构成鞅（martingale），这意味着仅靠辩论并不能提高期望正确率。在这些认识的指导下，我们表明，通过使信念更新偏向纠错，有针对性的干预能够显著增强辩论的有效性。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，尽管 MAD 具有潜力，但在许多实际场景中，简单的集成方法仍是性能强劲且更可靠的替代方案。代码已发布于 https://github.com/deeplearning-wisc/debate-or-vote。

Throughout history, humans have relied on deliberation to resolve ambiguity, challenge assumptions, and seek better answers. From courtrooms and panels to scientific collaborations, group reasoning plays a central role in decision-making. This process—where individuals reflect, revise, and converge through interaction—has long been seen as a hallmark of intelligent behavior. Inspired by this, recent work has explored whether large language models (LLMs) might similarly benefit from structured interaction. Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) has emerged as a popular framework: multiple LLM agents are prompted to discuss a shared question, each updating their answer based on the responses of their peers [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]. The hope is that, like human deliberation, such interaction will improve reasoning and lead to better outcomes.
纵观历史，人类一直依靠商议来消除歧义、质疑假设并寻求更好的答案。从法庭、专家小组到科学合作，群体推理在决策中发挥着核心作用。在这一过程中，个体通过互动进行反思、修正并逐渐达成共识；长期以来，这一直被视为智能行为的标志。受此启发，近期研究开始探索大语言模型（LLMs）是否也能从结构化互动中获益。多智能体辩论（Multi-Agent Debate，MAD）已成为一种流行框架：通过提示引导多个 LLM 智能体讨论同一个问题，每个智能体都根据其他智能体的回答更新自己的答案 [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]。人们期望，这种互动能够像人类商议一样，改善推理并带来更好的结果。
At its core, MAD integrates two key ingredients: the use of multiple agents (“Multi-Agent”) and their interaction through iterative discussions (“Debate”). Recent work has introduced increasingly sophisticated variants—ranging from diverse communication protocols [3, 7, 8], designing efficient and effective system architectures [1, 2, 9, 10], and assigning varied roles or personas to agents [11, 12, 13]. Despite these advances, the underlying mechanisms behind MAD’s effectiveness remain unclear. A natural step toward understanding MAD’s performance is to disentangle the contribution of each component—are the gains primarily due to meaningful communication between agents, or simply the result of aggregating multiple outputs? Answering this question is important because it informs whether the growing complexity of MAD design is justified by tangible benefits. If most of the performance gain stems from ensembling—i.e., aggregating diverse outputs from independent agents—then simpler methods like majority voting may suffice, avoiding additional computational and architectural overhead (see Figure 2 for visual comparison).
从本质上说，MAD 融合了两个关键要素：使用多个智能体（“多智能体”），以及让它们通过迭代讨论进行互动（“辩论”）。近期研究提出了日益复杂的变体，涉及多样化的通信协议 [3, 7, 8]、高效且有效的系统架构设计 [1, 2, 9, 10]，以及为智能体分配不同的角色或人格设定 [11, 12, 13]。尽管取得了这些进展，MAD 有效性背后的内在机制仍不清楚。要理解 MAD 的性能，一个自然的步骤是厘清各个组成部分的贡献：性能提升主要源于智能体之间有意义的交流，还是仅仅源于对多个输出的聚合？回答这一问题十分重要，因为它有助于判断 MAD 设计日益增加的复杂性是否带来了切实的收益。如果大部分性能提升源于集成，即聚合独立智能体的多样化输出，那么多数投票等更简单的方法或许就已足够，并能避免额外的计算和架构开销（直观比较见图 2）。

To better understand the relative contributions of ensembling vs. interaction, we conduct an extensive empirical study quantifying each component’s effect. Specifically, we measure the contribution of the “Multi-Agent” component using the performance achieved through Majority Voting, i.e., the aggregated output of agents before any debate rounds occur. We then compare this baseline to the final performance after multiple rounds of “Debate”, allowing us to isolate the additional benefit introduced by inter-agent communications. Surprisingly, we find that Majority Voting accounts for most of the performance gains in MAD. In fact, in most cases, majority voting without any debate performs on par with MAD, as seen in Figure 2. To ensure the broad applicability of our findings, our evaluation spans seven diverse benchmarks across multiple tasks and models.
为了更好地理解集成与互动的相对贡献，我们开展了广泛的实证研究，量化各个组成部分的作用。具体而言，我们通过多数投票所达到的性能来衡量“多智能体”这一组成部分的贡献，即在任何辩论轮次开始之前，对智能体的输出进行聚合所获得的性能。随后，我们将这一基线与多轮“辩论”后的最终性能进行比较，从而分离出智能体间交流带来的额外收益。令人意外的是，我们发现 MAD 中的大部分性能提升都来自多数投票。事实上，在大多数情况下，不经过任何辩论的多数投票都能取得与 MAD 相当的性能，如图 2 所示。为确保研究结果具有广泛适用性，我们的评估涵盖了跨多个任务和模型的七个多样化基准。
Beyond empirical observations, we introduce a theoretical framework in Section 4 that rigorously explains how agents’ uncertainty and belief updates shape collective decision-making in both voting and debate. At its core, the framework models each agent as a stochastic process governed by a Dirichlet-Compound-Multinomial (DCM) distribution, capturing internal uncertainty through a Dirichlet belief prior and output randomness via Multinomial sampling. This closely mirrors the behavior of real-world LLMs, which produce different outputs for the same question due to uncertainty and stochastic generation process (e.g., via temperature or nucleus sampling). Within this framework, we characterize MAD as a Bayesian posterior belief update process and prove that it induces a martingale over agents’ belief in the correct answer—meaning the expected belief remains unchanged over debate rounds. This implies that debate itself does not systematically improve or degrade beliefs on average; rather, belief evolution is driven by stochastic peer influence. In other words, we prove formally that majority vote does essentially all the work, which explains our empirical findings.
除实证观察外，我们还在第 4 节引入了一个理论框架，严格解释智能体的不确定性和信念更新如何在投票与辩论中塑造集体决策。该框架的核心是将每个智能体建模为一个由狄利克雷复合多项分布（Dirichlet-Compound-Multinomial，DCM）支配的随机过程，通过狄利克雷信念先验刻画内部不确定性，并通过多项分布采样刻画输出的随机性。这与实际 LLMs 的行为高度一致：由于不确定性和随机生成过程（例如温度采样或核采样），它们会对同一个问题生成不同的输出。在这一框架下，我们将 MAD 刻画为贝叶斯后验信念更新过程，并证明该过程使智能体对正确答案的信念构成鞅（martingale）——这意味着，信念的期望值在各轮辩论中保持不变。这表明，平均而言，辩论本身不会系统性地改善或削弱信念；相反，信念的演变由随机的同伴影响所驱动。换言之，我们从形式上证明，多数投票实际上承担了几乎全部作用，这解释了我们的实证发现。
Our theoretical framework further sheds light on the new design principles to improve MAD (Section 5). In particular, it highlights the importance of controlling the martingale by biasing belief updates toward correct signals during debate. We operationalize this insight through several interventions, where correct responses exert more influence than misleading ones, leading to improvements over standard MAD. 我们的理论框架还为改进 MAD 的新设计原则提供了启示（第 5 节）。具体而言，该框架强调了在辩论过程中通过使信念更新偏向正确信号来控制鞅过程的重要性。我们通过若干干预措施将这一见解付诸实践，使正确回答比误导性回答发挥更大的影响，从而取得优于标准 MAD 的效果。我们将本研究的贡献及其意义概括如下：
We comprehensively demonstrate that Majority Voting is as effective as Multi-Agent Debate, when evaluated across seven representative benchmark datasets. We further expand our investigation to more general MAD settings, including configurations with larger and more capable agents, heterogeneous agent populations, and open-ended natural language tasks.
我们在七个具有代表性的基准数据集上进行评估，全面证明了多数投票与多智能体辩论同样有效。我们进一步将研究扩展到更一般的 MAD 设置，包括采用规模更大、能力更强的智能体、异质智能体群体以及开放式自然语言任务的配置。
We develop a new theoretical framework that reveals majority voting’s success probability, and rigorously characterizes multi-agent debate as a martingale process. This framework lays a principled foundation for future work to better understand MAD systems.
我们提出了一个新的理论框架，揭示了多数投票的成功概率，并将多智能体辩论严格刻画为鞅过程。该框架为未来更好地理解 MAD 系统的研究奠定了坚实的理论基础。
Our theoretical analysis informs that debate alone does not improve beyond majority voting. By designing strategies that help preserve correct responses across debate rounds, we achieve notable improvements in multi-agent debate performance. This sheds light on future research to effectively improve MAD systems.
我们的理论分析表明，仅靠辩论无法取得超越多数投票的效果。通过设计有助于在各轮辩论中保留正确回答的策略，我们显著提升了多智能体辩论的性能。这为未来有效改进 MAD 系统的研究提供了启示。
2 Preliminaries2 预备知识
Multi-Agent Debate多智能体辩论
is a collaborative framework in which multiple language model agents engage in structured interaction—typically in the form of iterative exchanges or discussions—to solve a task such as question answering or text generation [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]. In a typical MAD protocol, each agent independently generates an initial response and then engages in a series of debate rounds. At round $t$ , an agent receives the original question along with responses from its peers at round $t-1$ , prompting the model to update its response accordingly. This iterative process is designed to leverage diverse reasoning paths and peer wisdom, potentially enhancing the overall decision quality. After all rounds of debate, the final answer is typically derived through an aggregation mechanism, such as majority voting. Specific prompts are provided in Appendix B.1.
是一种协作框架，其中多个语言模型智能体通过结构化交互——通常采用迭代交流或讨论的形式——来完成问答或文本生成等任务 [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]。在典型的 MAD 协议中，每个智能体先独立生成初始回答，然后参与一系列辩论轮次。在第 $t$ 轮，智能体接收原始问题及其同伴在第 $t-1$ 轮的回答，促使模型据此更新自身的回答。这一迭代过程旨在利用多样化的推理路径和同伴智慧，从而有望提升整体决策质量。所有辩论轮次结束后，通常通过多数投票等聚合机制得出最终答案。具体提示词见附录 B.1。

Let $\mathcal{X}$ denote the input space (e.g., natural language questions), and $\mathcal{Y}$ the output space (e.g., free-form or multiple-choice answers). We consider a set of $N$ language model agents, denoted by $\{a_{1},\dots,a_{N}\}$ , where each agent defines a stochastic function $f_{i}:\mathcal{X}\rightarrow\mathcal{Y}$ that produces an initial response ${y}_{i,0}\sim f_{i}(x)$ for input $x\in\mathcal{X}$ .
令 $\mathcal{X}$ 表示输入空间（例如自然语言问题），$\mathcal{Y}$ 表示输出空间（例如自由形式答案或选择题答案）。我们考虑由 $N$ 个语言模型智能体组成的集合，记为 $\{a_{1},\dots,a_{N}\}$，其中每个智能体定义一个随机函数 $f_{i}:\mathcal{X}\rightarrow\mathcal{Y}$，针对输入 $x\in\mathcal{X}$ 生成初始回答 ${y}_{i,0}\sim f_{i}(x)$。
In the Majority Voting setting, the initial responses $\{{y}_{i,0}\}_{i=1}^{N}$ are directly aggregated using a voting function $\mathcal{V}:\mathcal{Y}^{N}\rightarrow\mathcal{Y}$ to obtain the final prediction, typically returning the most frequent answer:
在多数投票设置中，使用投票函数 $\mathcal{V}:\mathcal{Y}^{N}\rightarrow\mathcal{Y}$ 直接聚合初始回答 $\{{y}_{i,0}\}_{i=1}^{N}$，以获得最终预测，通常返回出现频率最高的答案：
$${y}_{0}=\mathcal{V}({y}_{1,0},\dots,{y}_{N,0}).$$

At round $t\geq 1$ , each agent $a_{i}$ observes responses from a set of neighboring agents at the previous round and updates its answer accordingly. We adopt the simultaneous-talk protocol [3], where all agents update in parallel based on the previous round’s responses. Following the common setup in prior works, we focus on homogeneous agent settings, i.e., all agents share the same underlying model architecture or behavior. This allows us to isolate the effect of inter-agent communication and contrast MAD directly with simple majority voting. Multi-agent debate is often regarded as a promising mechanism for enhancing LLM performance via collaborative deliberation. But how much of its effectiveness truly comes from the debate itself—and how much is simply due to aggregating multiple answers? To address this question, we dissect MAD into two components—multi-agent ensembling and inter-agent communication—and present empirical evidence revealing that simple majority voting accounts for most of the observed gains. 3.1 Experimental Setup3.1 实验设置
Baselines.基线方法。
The key distinction among multi-agent debate methods typically lies in the design of the debate function $\mathcal{D}$ , particularly in how agents communicate and the roles they assume. To comprehensively evaluate these variations, we consider the following representative approaches: (1) Decentralized MAD [2], where each agent observes all other agents’ responses from the previous round. (2) Sparse MAD [10], a variant of Decentralized MAD with a sparse communication topology to enhance efficiency. (3) Centralized MAD [14], where a central agent aggregates peer responses and generates the updated response at each round. (4) Majority Voting, which selects the final answer by aggregating initial responses from multiple agents without any debate. This can be viewed as a special case with $T=0$ . For all the multi-agent approaches, we adopt $N=5$ in our main comparison and will ablate on the effect of $N$ . For single-agent baselines, we average across 5 independent runs.
多智能体辩论方法之间的主要区别通常在于辩论函数 $\mathcal{D}$ 的设计，尤其是智能体的通信方式及其所承担的角色。为全面评估这些差异，我们考虑以下具有代表性的方法：(1) Decentralized MAD [2]，其中每个智能体都能观察到上一轮所有其他智能体的回答。(2) Sparse MAD [10]，它是 Decentralized MAD 的一种变体，采用稀疏通信拓扑以提高效率。(3) Centralized MAD [14]，其中由一个中央智能体在每轮聚合其他智能体的回答，并生成更新后的回答。(4) 多数投票（Majority Voting），通过聚合多个智能体的初始回答来选出最终答案，不进行任何辩论。这可视为 $T=0$ 的特例。对于所有多智能体方法，我们在主要比较中采用 $N=5$，并将通过消融实验考察 $N$ 的影响。对于单智能体基线，我们取 5 次独立运行结果的平均值。

4 Theoretical Analysis4 理论分析
To better understand the dynamics underlying our empirical findings in Section 3, we now turn to a formal analysis of multi-agent debate and majority voting. Our theoretical framework allows us to capture how agent uncertainty and belief updates shape the collective decision-making in both debate and voting, grounded in Bayesian principles. Specifically, for a given input question, we consider a population of $N$ agents, each generating a response from a finite set $\mathcal{A}$ , which may represent multiple-choice question options or a set of plausible completions for open-ended tasks. We model each agent as an idealized generative model governed by a Dirichlet-Compound-Multinomial (DCM) distribution. This closely mirrors how practical LLM systems produce different outputs for the same question due to uncertainty and sampling variability. In particular, the Dirichlet prior captures the agent’s internal belief over possible answers, while the Multinomial models the stochastic generation process (e.g., via temperature or nucleus sampling). This distribution is thus a natural choice because it encapsulates both internal uncertainty and output randomness, while also providing a principled Bayesian framework for belief updates across debate rounds—enabling analytical study of dynamics during the debate process. Below we provide the mathematical details of the DCM model.
为更好地理解第 3 节实证发现背后的动态机制，我们现在转向对多智能体辩论和多数投票的形式化分析。我们的理论框架以贝叶斯原理为基础，能够刻画智能体的不确定性和信念更新如何塑造辩论与投票中的集体决策。具体而言，对于给定的输入问题，我们考虑一个由 $N$ 个智能体组成的群体，每个智能体从有限集合 $\mathcal{A}$ 中生成一个回答，该集合可以表示多项选择题的选项，也可以表示开放式任务的一组合理补全。我们将每个智能体建模为一个由狄利克雷复合多项式（Dirichlet-Compound-Multinomial，DCM）分布支配的理想化生成模型。这与实际 LLM 系统因不确定性和采样变异性而对同一问题产生不同输出的方式高度吻合。具体来说，狄利克雷先验刻画智能体对可能答案的内部信念，而多项分布则对随机生成过程（例如，通过温度采样或核采样）进行建模。因此，该分布是一个自然的选择，因为它既涵盖了内部不确定性和输出随机性，又为跨辩论轮次的信念更新提供了一个具有理论依据的贝叶斯框架，从而能够对辩论过程中的动态变化进行解析研究。下面给出 DCM 模型的数学细节。
Definition 1. (Agent Response Generation via DCM)定义 1.（通过 DCM 生成智能体回答）
At round $t$ , each agent $i$ is associated with a belief vector $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=(\alpha_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t}^{(K)})\in\mathbb{R}^{K}_{+}$ , where each entry $\alpha_{i,t}^{(k)}$ reflects the agent’s belief in response option $k\in\mathcal{A}$ . To generate a response $y_{i,t}$ , the agent follows a two-step process:
在第 $t$ 轮，每个智能体 $i$ 都对应一个信念向量 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=(\alpha_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t}^{(K)})\in\mathbb{R}^{K}_{+}$，其中每个分量 $\alpha_{i,t}^{(k)}$ 反映该智能体对回答选项 $k\in\mathcal{A}$ 的信念。为生成回答 $y_{i,t}$，智能体遵循以下两步过程：
$$\displaystyle\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}), \\ \displaystyle y_{i,t}\sim\mathrm{Categorical}(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}).$$
The marginal probability of generating any particular response $y_{i,t}\in\mathcal{A}$ —after integrating out the randomness in $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ —is given by $P(y_{i,t}=k\mid\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t})=\alpha_{i,t}^{(k)}/\sum_{j\in\mathcal{A}}\alpha_{i,t}^{(j)}.$
将 $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 中的随机性积分消去后，生成任意特定回答 $y_{i,t}\in\mathcal{A}$ 的边缘概率为 $P(y_{i,t}=k\mid\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t})=\alpha_{i,t}^{(k)}/\sum_{j\in\mathcal{A}}\alpha_{i,t}^{(j)}.$
Before analyzing the dynamics of debate, we first consider the base case where agents respond independently without interaction. The following characterizes the success probability of majority voting under this condition, based solely on the agents’ homogeneous initial beliefs, $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,0}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}=(\alpha^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha^{(K)}$ ). Without loss of generality, let answer index 1 be the correct option. We assume that the correct answer has the largest belief $\alpha^{(1)}$ , and all the other beliefs are ordered such that $\alpha^{(1)}>\alpha^{(2)}\geq\cdots\geq\alpha^{(K)}$ .
在分析辩论的动态变化之前，我们首先考虑智能体在没有交互的情况下独立作答这一基本情形。下面仅基于智能体相同的初始信念 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,0}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}=(\alpha^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha^{(K)}$ )，刻画在此条件下多数投票的成功概率。不失一般性，设索引为 1 的答案是正确选项。我们假设正确答案对应的信念 $\alpha^{(1)}$ 最大，且所有其他信念按如下方式排列：$\alpha^{(1)}>\alpha^{(2)}\geq\cdots\geq\alpha^{(K)}$。
Theorem 1. (Majority Voting Success Probability)定理 1.（多数投票的成功概率）
Let $\bar{\boldsymbol{\theta}}=(\bar{\theta}^{(1)},\ldots,\bar{\theta}^{(K)})=\boldsymbol{\alpha}/\sum_{j=1}^{K}\alpha_{j}$ denote the mean of the Dirichlet distribution, $\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\alpha)$ , and define the margin $\Delta:=\bar{\theta_{1}}-\bar{\theta_{2}}$ . If $N>K/\Delta^{2}$ , then the probability that majority voting selects the answer 1 is lower bounded as:
令 $\bar{\boldsymbol{\theta}}=(\bar{\theta}^{(1)},\ldots,\bar{\theta}^{(K)})=\boldsymbol{\alpha}/\sum_{j=1}^{K}\alpha_{j}$ 表示狄利克雷分布 $\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\alpha)$ 的均值，并定义间隔 $\Delta:=\bar{\theta_{1}}-\bar{\theta_{2}}$。若 $N>K/\Delta^{2}$，则多数投票选出答案 1 的概率具有如下下界：
$$\mathbb{P}(y_{\mathrm{mv}}=1)\geq 1-\exp\left(-N\left(\frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{K}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\right)^{2}\right).$$
Remark 1.注 1.
This result highlights the magnifying effect of majority voting: even when the correct answer is only marginally more likely than the alternative answers, the lower bound on the success probability asymptotically approaches 1 as $N$ scales. Notably, this holds even when $\bar{\theta}_{1}\ll\frac{1}{2}$ , as long as it remains the most probable choice option. In practice, we recognize that MAD systems often operate with a small number of agents due to computational constraints. We provide a sharper analysis specialized to this realistic regime that applies to arbitrary $N$ in Theorem 1.A (Appendix C), without constraining its size. This complementary result provides insights into the reliability of majority voting in more practical, resource-constrained settings.
这一结果凸显了多数投票的放大效应：即使正确答案的概率仅略高于其他答案，随着 $N$ 增大，成功概率的下界也会渐近趋于 1。值得注意的是，即使 $\bar{\theta}_{1}\ll\frac{1}{2}$，只要它仍然是概率最高的选项，这一结论就成立。我们认识到，在实践中，由于计算资源的限制，MAD 系统通常仅使用少量智能体运行。我们在定理 1.A（附录 C）中针对这一现实情形给出了更精确的分析，该分析适用于任意 $N$，不对其大小施加限制。这一补充结果有助于理解多数投票在更贴近实际、资源受限的场景中的可靠性。
Next, we analyze the multi-agent debate performance by formalizing how each agent’s belief $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}$ evolves through debate. Specifically, each agent observes its neighbors’ responses and performs a Bayesian posterior update of its belief accordingly.
接下来，我们通过形式化描述每个智能体的信念 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}$ 如何在辩论中演化，分析多智能体辩论的性能。具体而言，每个智能体观察其邻居的回答，并据此对自身信念进行贝叶斯后验更新。
Definition 2. (Bayesian Belief Update from Neighbor Responses)定义 2.（基于邻居回答的贝叶斯信念更新）
Let $\{y_{j,t-1}\mid j\in\mathcal{N}(i)\}$ be the set of responses observed by agent $i$ from its neighbors $\mathcal{N}(i)$ at round $t$ . These responses induce a count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}=(c_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,c_{i,t}^{(K)})\in\mathbb{N}^{K}$ , where $c_{i,t}^{(k)}$ denotes the number of neighbors who selected response $k$ . Then, the agent updates its Dirichlet parameter as: $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}.$
令 $\{y_{j,t-1}\mid j\in\mathcal{N}(i)\}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 在第 $t$ 轮从其邻居 $\mathcal{N}(i)$ 处观察到的回答集合。这些回答形成一个计数向量 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}=(c_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,c_{i,t}^{(K)})\in\mathbb{N}^{K}$，其中 $c_{i,t}^{(k)}$ 表示选择回答 $k$ 的邻居数量。随后，智能体按如下方式更新其狄利克雷参数：$\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}.$
Under this formulation, each round of multi-agent debate corresponds to a Bayesian update step under the conjugacy of the Dirichlet-Multinomial model.
在这一形式化描述下，每一轮多智能体辩论都对应于利用狄利克雷–多项式模型的共轭性进行的一步贝叶斯更新。
Lemma 1. (Bayesian Conjugacy in Multi-Agent Debate)引理 1.（多智能体辩论中的贝叶斯共轭性）
At round $t$ , after observing responses from its neighbors $\mathcal{N}(i)$ , the agent $i$ aggregates these into a count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ as in Definition 2. Then, by Bayesian conjugacy, the posterior distribution over $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ remains Dirichlet:
在第 $t$ 轮，智能体 $i$ 观察其邻居 $\mathcal{N}(i)$ 的回答后，按照定义 2 将这些回答汇总为计数向量 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$。于是，根据贝叶斯共轭性，$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 的后验分布仍为狄利克雷分布：
$$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}).$$
Then, for an agent $i$ with neighbors $\mathcal{N}(i)$ , let $p_{i,t}:=\bar{{\theta}}_{i,t}^{(1)}$ denote its belief in the correct answer at debate round $t$ . Under Bayesian conjugacy,
随后，对于邻居集合为 $\mathcal{N}(i)$ 的智能体 $i$，令 $p_{i,t}:=\bar{{\theta}}_{i,t}^{(1)}$ 表示其在第 $t$ 轮辩论中对正确答案的信念。根据贝叶斯共轭性，
$$p_{i,t}:=\bar{\theta}_{i,t}^{(1)}=\frac{\alpha_{i,t}^{(1)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}\alpha_{i,t}^{(j)}}=\frac{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)}+c_{i,t}^{(1)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}(\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(j)}+c_{i,t}^{(j)})}.$$
This agent belief’s evolution throughout debate forms a martingale process (Theorem 2), with full proof in Appendix D.
该智能体的信念在整个辩论过程中的演化构成一个鞅过程（定理 2），完整证明见附录 D。

Theorem 2. (Martingale Behavior of Multi-Agent Debate)定理 2.（多智能体辩论的鞅行为）
For any agent $i$ at round $t>0$ , if
对于轮次 $t>0$ 的任意智能体 $i$，如果
$$\frac{1}{|\mathcal{N}(i)|}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}p_{j,t-1}=p_{i,t-1},$$
then sequence $\{p_{i,t}\}_{t\geq 0}$ forms a martingale. That is, the expected belief at the next round equals the current belief:
则序列 $\{p_{i,t}\}_{t\geq 0}$ 构成一个鞅。也就是说，下一轮的期望信念等于当前信念：
$$\mathbb{E}[p_{i,t}\mid\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{t-1}]=p_{i,t-1}.$$
Theoretical insights: Majority vote does essentially all the work.理论洞见：多数投票实际上完成了几乎全部工作。
This theorem highlights a fundamental property of multi-agent debate under Bayesian update of the DCM model: the agent’s belief in the correct answer behaves like a martingale—that is, its expected value remains unchanged across rounds. This result is closely related to the classical Pólya Urn scheme [24]. Intuitively, this means that debate itself does not systematically improve or degrade an agent’s belief on average; instead, belief updates are driven entirely by the stochastic influence of peer responses. While some debate trajectories may lead to stronger belief in the correct answer (i.e., correction), others may lead to weaker belief (i.e., subversion). While these local fluctuations affect the posterior counts, the expected belief in the correct answer remains equal to the initial $p_{i,0}=\bar{\theta}_{i,0}^{(1)}$ , without any debate, when agents are homogeneous and fully-connected (Appendix D.3.1). This implies that, under our theoretical model, debate alone does not necessarily improve the initial accuracy–majority voting accounts for the primary performance gains. Our theory thus aligns well with our empirical findings in Section 3. Furthermore, in Appendix D.4, we extend our analysis to a more general setting in which each agent’s expected belief evolves over the course of the debate, such that the condition in Theorem 2 may no longer hold. In this context, we introduce the notion of “collective intelligence" and demonstrate that it forms a martingale process even with heterogeneous agents.
该定理揭示了 DCM 模型中采用贝叶斯更新的多智能体辩论的一项基本性质：智能体对正确答案的信念表现为一个鞅，即其期望值在各轮之间保持不变。这一结果与经典的 Pólya 瓮模型 [24] 密切相关。直观而言，这意味着从平均意义上看，辩论本身不会系统性地增强或削弱智能体的信念；相反，信念更新完全由其他智能体回应的随机影响所驱动。一些辩论轨迹可能增强对正确答案的信念（即纠正），而另一些轨迹则可能削弱这种信念（即误导）。尽管这些局部波动会影响后验计数，但当智能体同质且全连接时，对正确答案的信念的期望仍等于未进行任何辩论时的初始值 $p_{i,0}=\bar{\theta}_{i,0}^{(1)}$（附录 D.3.1）。这意味着，在我们的理论模型下，仅靠辩论并不一定能提高初始准确率，多数投票才是性能提升的主要来源。因此，我们的理论与第 3 节中的实证发现高度一致。此外，在附录 D.4 中，我们将分析扩展到一种更一般的情形，其中每个智能体的信念期望会在辩论过程中发生变化，因此定理 2 中的条件可能不再成立。在这一情境下，我们引入了“集体智能”的概念，并证明即使智能体具有异质性，它也构成一个鞅过程。

In Lemma 1 and Theorem 2, we define the update dynamics in terms of the count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ . Our framework can be generalized to open-ended tasks and capture the heterogeneous influence of each agent’s response, with minor interpretational adjustments. For open-ended tasks, although responses are not strictly countable in categorical form, they can be represented in distributional or similarity-based spaces. Here, the count vector can be viewed more broadly, e.g., as a soft histogram over clustered response types, an embedding-based semantic agreement measure, or a weighted similarity score between textual outputs. In such settings, “consensus” is better defined semantically rather than symbolically: if agents independently produce explanations or rationales that are semantically aligned, this can be considered consensus even when surface forms differ. This notion can be operationalized using thresholds on embedding similarity, Levenshtein distance, or overlap in reasoning chains, among other metrics.
在引理 1 和定理 2 中，我们用计数向量 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ 定义更新动态。只需对解释作少量调整，我们的框架就可以推广到开放式任务，并刻画每个智能体回应所产生的异质性影响。对于开放式任务，尽管回应无法严格按类别进行计数，但可以在基于分布或相似度的空间中表示。在这里，可以对计数向量作更广义的理解，例如，将其视为聚类后各回应类型的软直方图、基于嵌入的语义一致性度量，或文本输出之间的加权相似度得分。在此类情形下，从语义层面而非符号层面定义“共识”更为合适：如果智能体独立生成的解释或推理依据在语义上一致，那么即使表面形式不同，也可以视为达成共识。这一概念可以通过对嵌入相似度、Levenshtein 距离或推理链重叠程度等指标设置阈值来操作化。
5 How Does Theory Inform Improved Design of MAD?5 理论如何指导 MAD 的设计改进？
The martingale property in our theory reflects a neutral expectation over time, underscoring that without additional bias towards correct signals, debate alone does not guarantee convergence to the truth. Any additional benefit arises from local asymmetries in the observed stochastic process $\{p_{t}\}_{t\geq 0}$ . Hence, to improve the effectiveness of MAD, we explore alternative designs that facilitate correction and/or suppress subversion in the debate process.
我们理论中的鞅性质反映了随时间保持中性的期望，这凸显出：如果没有额外偏向正确信号的机制，仅靠辩论无法保证收敛到真相。任何额外收益都源于所观测随机过程 $\{p_{t}\}_{t\geq 0}$ 中的局部不对称性。因此，为提高 MAD 的有效性，我们探索了能够在辩论过程中促进纠正和/或抑制误导的其他设计。
5.1 Belief Update by Biasing Towards Correct Signal5.1 通过偏向正确信号更新信念
To investigate how targeted intervention in the belief update process can promote convergence to the correct answer, we first consider an oracle-style method that explicitly biases updates toward the correct signal. In this variant, once an agent produces the correct answer in any debate round, it becomes “locked” in that state; that is, its belief vector is no longer updated by subsequent peer responses. Formally, if agent $i$ outputs the correct answer at round $t$ , we use this response in all subsequent rounds $t^{\prime}>t$ . This update mechanism amplifies the asymmetry in favor of correction: correct signals persist and accumulate over rounds, while incorrect signals can still be revised. Consequently, the system’s dynamics depart from the neutral martingale behavior discussed in Section 4 and instead exhibit a directional drift toward the correct answer.
为研究对信念更新过程进行针对性干预如何促进向正确答案收敛，我们首先考虑一种预言机式方法，显式地使更新偏向正确信号。在这一变体中，一旦智能体在任意一轮辩论中生成正确答案，它就会被“锁定”在该状态；也就是说，其信念向量不再根据后续其他智能体的回应进行更新。形式上，如果智能体 $i$ 在轮次 $t$ 输出正确答案，我们就在所有后续轮次 $t^{\prime}>t$ 中使用该回应。这一更新机制强化了有利于纠正的不对称性：正确信号会在各轮中持续保留并累积，而错误信号仍可被修正。因此，系统的动态变化偏离了第 4 节讨论的中性鞅行为，转而表现出朝向正确答案的定向漂移。
We refer to this method as MAD-oracle, and report its performance in Table 2. This variant yields substantial improvements over standard MAD, and always surpasses the Majority Voting baseline by a large margin. For instance, in Decentralized MAD with $T=5$ rounds, accuracy on MMLU (Form. Log.) increases from 0.5000 to 0.6825. Although this approach is not feasible in practice—since the true answer is not available—it reveals an upper bound on the benefit achievable by incorporating bias toward correct signals in the belief update process. In the next subsection, we investigate a more realistic alternative that aims to suppress subversion without direct access to ground truth.

5.2 Belief Update Guided by the Majority Vote5.2 多数投票引导的信念更新
In practice, one would not have access to the oracle setting where correct answers are preserved by assumption. To approximate this behavior, we introduce simple modifications to the MAD update rule that leverage the positive signals from majority voting. Our design rationale is guided by our theoretical analysis—which shows that majority voting provides a more reliable estimate of the correct answer than any single agent, as it aggregates marginal advantages across the population. This suggests that using the majority response as a proxy for the ground truth can help steer belief updates in the right direction—effectively biasing the system toward correction without needing oracle access. To explore this idea, we propose two lightweight interventions that incorporate the majority vote into agents’ belief dynamics. Specifically, we evaluate two strategies: (1) MAD-Conformist: if an agent’s response matches the majority vote in the previous round, it retains that response; (2) MAD-Follower: with 30% probability, the agent adopts the majority response from the previous round, and otherwise samples a new one. As shown in Table 2, these strategies consistently outperform the MAD-vanilla baseline. While they do not reach the oracle’s upper bound performance, they demonstrate that simple, theory-informed modifications can yield meaningful improvements—pointing to a promising direction for future work to close the gap.
在实践中，无法采用通过假设来保证正确答案得以保留的预言机设定。为近似实现这一行为，我们对 MAD 更新规则进行了简单修改，以利用多数投票提供的正向信号。我们的设计思路以理论分析为指导——该分析表明，多数投票通过汇聚群体中各个个体的微弱优势，能够比任何单个智能体更可靠地估计正确答案。这表明，将多数答案作为真实答案的替代，可以帮助引导信念朝正确方向更新——从而在无需获取预言机信息的情况下，有效地使系统偏向纠错。为探索这一思路，我们提出了两种轻量级干预措施，将多数投票融入智能体的信念动态变化过程。具体而言，我们评估了两种策略：(1) MAD-Conformist：如果某个智能体的回答与上一轮的多数投票结果一致，则保留该回答；(2) MAD-Follower：智能体以 30% 的概率采用上一轮的多数答案，否则重新采样一个答案。如表 2 所示，这些策略始终优于 MAD-vanilla 基线。尽管这些策略未达到预言机的性能上限，但它们表明，基于理论指导的简单修改能够带来有意义的改进，为未来缩小这一差距的研究指明了一个有前景的方向。

7 Related Works7 相关工作
Recently, there has been growing interest in multi-agent systems (MAS). Several survey papers have reviewed state-of-the-art LLM-based MAS approaches [14, 26, 27, 28]. Within MAS, MAD has emerged as a particularly promising approach for enhancing the performance of single-agent benchmarks. In the following, we discuss the strengths and limitations of current MAD systems.
近年来，多智能体系统（MAS）受到越来越多的关注。多篇综述论文回顾了基于 LLM 的 MAS 的前沿方法 [14, 26, 27, 28]。在 MAS 中，MAD 已成为一种尤其有望提升单智能体基准测试表现的方法。下文将讨论当前 MAD 系统的优势与局限。
Pros of Multi-Agent Debate.多智能体辩论的优势。
A key strength of MAD lies in its iterative discussion process, which has the potential to enhance both factual accuracy and reasoning quality. Building on this paradigm, several works have proposed MAD-based approaches for a variety of tasks [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]. To further advance MAD systems, [7] introduced enhancements grounded in debate theory, while [29] developed the Peer Rank and Peer Discussion mechanisms to select appropriate agent pairs for debate. Many studies have focused on designing effective communication architectures and protocols to improve efficiency and effectiveness [3, 8, 9, 10, 30, 31]. Other works have emphasized the importance of diversity in MAD systems, leveraging heterogeneous LLM agents [11], injecting distinct personas into each agent [9, 12, 13], or enabling text generation with controlled diversity [32, 33]. Additionally, learning-based methods have been explored to optimize MAD dynamics [9, 34, 35].
MAD 的一项关键优势在于其迭代讨论过程，该过程有望同时提高事实准确性和推理质量。基于这一范式，多项工作针对各类任务提出了基于 MAD 的方法 [1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6]。为进一步推动 MAD 系统的发展，[7] 引入了基于辩论理论的改进，而 [29] 开发了同伴排序（Peer Rank）和同伴讨论（Peer Discussion）机制，用于选择合适的智能体配对进行辩论。许多研究致力于设计有效的通信架构和协议，以提高效率和效果 [3, 8, 9, 10, 30, 31]。另一些工作强调 MAD 系统中多样性的重要性，采用了异构 LLM 智能体 [11]、为每个智能体注入不同的人设 [9, 12, 13]，或实现多样性可控的文本生成 [32, 33]。此外，也有研究探索基于学习的方法，以优化 MAD 的动态过程 [9, 34, 35]。
Cons of Multi-Agent Debate.多智能体辩论的局限。
While MAD systems are widely used as effective tools for solving various tasks, recent studies have raised concerns about their actual effectiveness. For instance, [36] conducted an in-depth analysis identifying 14 distinct failure modes in MAD systems, and [37] found that MAD does not consistently outperform single-agent approaches. Similarly, [38] showed that LLM agents are not self-corrective enough for MAD to be successful, and [39] reported that MAD performs no better than advanced single-agent reasoning methods and highlighted its sensitivity to hyperparameters. [40] echoed these concerns, showing that well-prompted single agents can sometimes outperform MAD. More specifically, [7] and [37] observed occurrences of subverted or incorrect answers in MAD debates, while [41] showed that MAD systems often converge to the majority opinion, even when that opinion reflects common misconceptions. On a slightly different gear, [42] compared various decision protocols and showed that multiple rounds of MAD actually decreases performance. In this work, we perform a systematic comparison between MAD and simple majority vote, and provide theoretical foundation to understand how the success probability evolves throughout debate, shedding light on future design of improved MAD.
尽管 MAD 系统被广泛用作解决各类任务的有效工具，近期研究对其实际有效性提出了质疑。例如，[36] 通过深入分析识别出 MAD 系统中 14 种不同的失效模式，而 [37] 发现 MAD 并不总是优于单智能体方法。类似地，[38] 表明，LLM 智能体的自我纠错能力不足以使 MAD 取得成功；[39] 则报告称，MAD 的表现并不优于先进的单智能体推理方法，并强调了其对超参数的敏感性。[40] 也表达了类似的担忧，表明经过精心提示的单智能体有时能够优于 MAD。更具体地说，[7] 和 [37] 观察到 MAD 辩论中存在正确答案被推翻或产生错误答案的情况，而 [41] 表明，MAD 系统往往会收敛到多数意见，即使该意见反映的是常见误解。从略有不同的角度出发，[42] 比较了各种决策协议，并表明多轮 MAD 实际上会降低性能。在本研究中，我们系统比较了 MAD 与简单多数投票，并提供了理论基础，以理解成功概率在整个辩论过程中的演变，从而为未来改进 MAD 的设计提供启示。
8 Conclusion8 结论
In this study, we provided comprehensive analysis of MAD and its core components. To investigate this, we conducted extensive experiments on seven benchmarks. Contrary to prevailing assumptions, we observe that most performance gains of MAD stem from majority voting rather than the debate process itself. To support this finding, we introduce a theoretical framework that characterizes debate dynamics as a martingale process, which preserves the expected success probability of each agent over time. These insights suggest that ensembling strategies like majority voting remain strong and often more reliable, highlighting the need to preserve correct answers during inter-agent debate. Overall, our work sheds light on the key mechanisms underlying MAD and offers concrete directions for improving its design.

Appendix C Special Case of Theorem 1附录 C 定理 1 的特殊情形
In this section, we analyze a special case of Theorem 1, where the probability of the correct answer, denoted by $\theta_{1}$ , exceeds $\frac{1}{2}$ . In other words, each agent independently makes a correct decision with probability at least 0.5. It naturally follows that $\frac{1}{2}\geq 1-\theta_{1}=\sum_{i=2}^{k}\theta_{i}\geq\max_{i\in\{2,\ldots,k\}}\theta_{i}$ , indicating that the margin term $\Delta$ in Theorem 1 requires $\Delta\geq 0$ , which does not rely on the number of agents $N$ or the set size of possible answers $k$ .
在本节中，我们分析定理 1 的一种特殊情形，其中，记为 $\theta_{1}$ 的正确答案的概率超过 $\frac{1}{2}$。换言之，每个智能体独立地以至少 0.5 的概率作出正确决策。由此自然可得 $\frac{1}{2}\geq 1-\theta_{1}=\sum_{i=2}^{k}\theta_{i}\geq\max_{i\in\{2,\ldots,k\}}\theta_{i}$，这表明定理 1 中的间隔项 $\Delta$ 需满足 $\Delta\geq 0$，该条件不依赖于智能体数量 $N$ 或可能答案集合的大小 $k$。
To formalize this, we define $p_{0}:=\theta_{1}$ to represent the initial probability that an individual agent selects the correct answer. Also, for simplicity, let $X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{N}$ be independent Bernoulli random variables, where $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p_{0})$ . The average correctness across agents is then given by the empirical mean $\bar{X}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}X_{i}$ , which corresponds to the fraction of agents who vote correctly. Under this setup, we analyze the lower bound of the Majority Voting success probability as follows:
为对此进行形式化描述，我们定义 $p_{0}:=\theta_{1}$，表示单个智能体选择正确答案的初始概率。此外，为简便起见，令 $X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{N}$ 为相互独立的伯努利随机变量，其中 $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p_{0})$。于是，所有智能体的平均正确率由经验均值 $\bar{X}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}X_{i}$ 给出，它对应于投票正确的智能体所占的比例。在此设定下，我们对多数投票成功概率的下界进行如下分析：
Theorem 1.A (Majority Voting Success Probability). Let $X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}$ be independent Bernoulli random variables with $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p_{0})$ , where $p_{0}\in(0,1)$ is the probability that each agent is correct. Let $\bar{X}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}X_{i}$ be the fraction of correct agents among $N$ independent agents. If $p_{0}>\frac{1}{2}$ , the probability that a majority vote is successful is lower bounded as follows:
定理 1.A（多数投票成功概率）。设 $X_{1},X_{2},\ldots,X_{n}$ 为相互独立的伯努利随机变量，满足 $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p_{0})$，其中 $p_{0}\in(0,1)$ 为每个智能体回答正确的概率。令 $\bar{X}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}X_{i}$ 表示 $N$ 个独立智能体中回答正确的智能体所占的比例。若 $p_{0}>\frac{1}{2}$，则多数投票成功概率的下界如下：
$$P\left(\bar{X}>\frac{1}{2}\right)\geq 1-\exp\left(-2N\left(p_{0}-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}\right).$$
Proof of Theorem 1.A. Since $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p)$ , we have $E[X_{i}]=p$ , and the variables are independent and bounded: $0\leq X_{i}\leq 1$ . Define $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$ , so $E[S_{n}]=np$ , and $\bar{X}=\frac{S_{n}}{n}$ , so $E[\bar{X}]=p$ .
定理 1.A 的证明。由于 $X_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p)$，有 $E[X_{i}]=p$，且这些变量相互独立并且有界：$0\leq X_{i}\leq 1$。定义 $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$，则 $E[S_{n}]=np$，且 $\bar{X}=\frac{S_{n}}{n}$，因此 $E[\bar{X}]=p$。
We apply Hoeffding’s Inequality to bound the deviation of $S_{n}$ from its mean. Hoeffding’s Inequality states that for independent random variables $X_{i}$ with $a_{i}\leq X_{i}\leq b_{i}$ , and $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$ :
我们应用 Hoeffding 不等式来界定 $S_{n}$ 偏离其均值的程度。Hoeffding 不等式指出，对于满足 $a_{i}\leq X_{i}\leq b_{i}$ 的相互独立的随机变量 $X_{i}$，以及 $S_{n}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}$，有：
$$P(S_{n}-E[S_{n}]\geq t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{2t^{2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}\right)$$
$$P(S_{n}-E[S_{n}]\leq-t)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{2t^{2}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}}\right)$$
Here, $a_{i}=0$ , $b_{i}=1$ , so $(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}=1$ , and $\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}=n$ . Let $t=\epsilon n$ :
此处，$a_{i}=0$，$b_{i}=1$，因此 $(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}=1$，且 $\sum_{i=1}^{n}(b_{i}-a_{i})^{2}=n$。令 $t=\epsilon n$：
$$P(S_{n}-np\leq-\epsilon n)\leq\exp\left(-\frac{2(\epsilon n)^{2}}{n}\right)=\exp(-2n\epsilon^{2})$$
Rewrite in terms of $\bar{X}$ :
用 $\bar{X}$ 改写为：
$$P(S_{n}-np\leq-\epsilon n)=P\left(\frac{S_{n}}{n}-p\leq-\epsilon\right)=P(\bar{X}\leq p-\epsilon)$$
$$P(\bar{X}\leq p-\epsilon)\leq\exp(-2n\epsilon^{2})$$
We want the majority vote to be successful, i.e., $\bar{X}>\frac{1}{2}$ .
我们希望多数投票成功，即 $\bar{X}>\frac{1}{2}$。
First, consider the complementary event:
首先，考虑其补事件：
$$P\left(\bar{X}\leq\frac{1}{2}\right)=P\left(\bar{X}\leq p-\left(p-\frac{1}{2}\right)\right)$$
and set $\epsilon=p-\frac{1}{2}$ :
并令 $\epsilon=p-\frac{1}{2}$：
$$p-\epsilon=\frac{1}{2}\implies\epsilon=p-\frac{1}{2}$$

证明完毕。$\hfill\blacksquare$

Then, note that the dominant term in the resulting lower bound remains consistent with that of the orignal Theorem 1, scaling as $\mathcal{O}(N\cdot\Delta^{2})$ .

This lemma implies:

$$\mathbb{P}\left(\|\hat{\mathbf{p}}-\mathbf{p}\|_{1}<\Delta\right)\geq 1-\exp\left(-N\left(\frac{\Delta}{\sqrt{K}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}\right)^{2}\right).$$

Lemma 1. (Bayesian Conjugacy in Multi-Agent Debate) At round $t$ , after observing responses from its neighbors $\mathcal{N}(i)$ , the agent $i$ aggregates these into a count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ as in Definition 2. $$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}).$$
Proof of Lemma 1. We aim to show that the posterior distribution of $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ , the agent $i$ ’s belief over the answer space at round $t$ , given the observed responses $\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}$ from its neighbors, is a Dirichlet distribution with updated parameters $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ .

First, consider the prior belief of agent $i$ at the start of round $t$ , which is based on its belief from the previous round $t-1$ . The prior distribution over the parameter $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ is:
首先，考虑智能体 $i$ 在第 $t$ 轮开始时的先验信念，该信念基于其在上一轮 $t-1$ 的信念。参数 $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 的先验分布为：
$$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(K)}),$$
where $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}=(\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(K)})$ are positive real numbers representing the prior parameters inherited from the previous round, and the density is proportional to:
其中，$\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}=(\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(K)})$ 的各分量均为正实数，表示从上一轮继承的先验参数，其概率密度正比于：
$$p(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t})\propto\prod_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)^{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(m)}-1}},$$
with $\sum_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)}=1$ .
其中 $\sum_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)}=1$。
At round $t$ , agent $i$ observes the responses $\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}$ from its neighbors, where $y_{j,t-1}\in\{1,\ldots,K\}$ represents the answer chosen by neighbor $j$ at round $t-1$ . According to Definition 2, these responses are aggregated into a count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}=(c_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,c_{i,t}^{(k)})$ , where:
在第 $t$ 轮，智能体 $i$ 观测到其邻居的回答 $\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}$，其中 $y_{j,t-1}\in\{1,\ldots,K\}$ 表示邻居 $j$ 在第 $t-1$ 轮选择的答案。根据定义 2，这些回答被汇总为计数向量 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}=(c_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,c_{i,t}^{(k)})$，其中：
$$c_{i,t}^{(m)}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}\mathbf{1}[y_{j,t-1}=m],$$
and $\sum_{m=1}^{k}c_{i,t}^{(m)}=|\mathcal{N}(i)|$ , the number of neighbors. Assuming each neighbor’s response $y_{j,t-1}$ is drawn independently from a categorical distribution parameterized by $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ , the likelihood of observing the count vector $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ given $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ follows a multinomial distribution:
并且 $\sum_{m=1}^{k}c_{i,t}^{(m)}=|\mathcal{N}(i)|$，即邻居的数量。假设每个邻居的回答 $y_{j,t-1}$ 都独立地采样自以 $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 为参数的类别分布，则给定 $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 时，观测到计数向量 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ 的似然由多项分布给出：
$$\mathbf{c}_{i,t}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\sim\text{Multinomial}(|\mathcal{N}(i)|,\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}),$$
with the likelihood proportional to:
其似然正比于：
$$p(\mathbf{c}_{i,t}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t})\propto\prod_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)^{c_{i,t}^{(m)}}}.$$
By Bayesian conjugacy, the posterior distribution of $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ given the observed counts is proportional to the product of the prior and the likelihood:
根据贝叶斯共轭性，给定观测计数时，$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}$ 的后验分布正比于先验与似然的乘积：
$$p(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\mathbf{c}_{i,t})\propto p(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t})\cdot p(\mathbf{c}_{i,t}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}).$$
Substituting the expressions:

$$p(\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\mathbf{c}_{i,t})\propto\left(\prod_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)^{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(m)}-1}}\right)\cdot\left(\prod_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)^{c_{i,t}^{(m)}}}\right)=\prod_{m=1}^{K}\theta_{i,t}^{(m)^{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(m)}+c_{i,t}^{(m)}-1}}.$$

该形式具有狄利克雷分布的特征。因此，后验分布为：
$$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)}+c_{i,t}^{(1)},\ldots,\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(K)}+c_{i,t}^{(K)}),$$
or equivalently:
或等价地：
$$\boldsymbol{\theta}_{i,t}\mid\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}\sim\mathrm{Dirichlet}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{c}_{i,t}).$$
Since $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ is derived from the neighbor responses $\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}$ as specified, and the update follows from the conjugacy property of the Dirichlet and multinomial distributions, the lemma holds. Proof completed. $\hfill\blacksquare$
由于 $\mathbf{c}_{i,t}$ 按照规定由邻居回答 $\{y_{j,t-1}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}(i)}$ 得到，且该更新由狄利克雷分布与多项分布的共轭性质导出，因此引理成立。证明完毕。$\hfill\blacksquare$

$$\displaystyle p_{i,t-1} \\ \displaystyle=\frac{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(j)}} \\ \displaystyle p_{i,t} \\ \displaystyle=\frac{\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(1)}+c_{i,t}^{(1)}}{\sum_{j=1}^{K}\alpha_{i,t-1}^{(j)}+|\mathcal{N}(i)|},$$

D.3.1 When does (1) in Theorem 2 Hold?D.3.1 定理 2 中的 (1) 何时成立？
In order for a multi-agent debate to be a martingale process, the condition in Theorem 2, equation (1), which states the relation between the belief of an agent with its peers’ beliefs, should hold. Here, we expand our discussion to exploring debate topologies that satisfy (1), therefore the martingale property.
要使多智能体辩论成为鞅过程，定理 2 的公式 (1) 所给出的条件应当成立，该条件描述了一个智能体的信念与其同伴信念之间的关系。这里，我们进一步探讨满足 (1) 从而具有鞅性质的辩论拓扑。
Proposition 1. (Sufficient Conditions for (1))命题 1.（(1) 成立的充分条件）
If the debate topology consists of agents that either (i) are homogeneous and fully-connected, or (ii) are fully-isolated, or (iii) form isolated cliques whose agents in each clique are homogeneous, then the debate process is a martingale.
如果辩论拓扑中的智能体满足以下任一情形：(i) 同质且全连接，或 (ii) 完全孤立，或 (iii) 构成彼此孤立的团，且每个团内的智能体均为同质智能体，则辩论过程是一个鞅。
proof of Proposition 1. We prove one by one that each case satisfies (1).
命题 1 的证明。我们逐一证明每种情形都满足 (1)。
(i) Homogeneous agents with fully-connected communication topology
(i) 具有全连接通信拓扑的同质智能体
This setting best represents our core experimental setting: Decentralized MAD [2]. In this setup, since the agents are homogeneous, their initial beliefs are identical: $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1,0}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2,0}=\cdots=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{n,0}$ . Also, since we are assuming a fully-connected communication topology, the count vector $\boldsymbol{c}_{i,t}$ that counts the neighboring agents’ answers (including itself), is identical across agents. Therefore, $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\boldsymbol{c}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i^{\prime},t}$ holds for all $i,i^{\prime}$ . By dividing the equation with $||\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{\cdot,t}||_{1}$ , we get $p_{i,t}=p_{i^{\prime},t}\;\forall_{i,i^{\prime}}$ . Then, (1) is satisfied. $\hfill\square$
这一设置最能代表我们的核心实验设置：去中心化 MAD [2]。在此设置中，由于智能体是同质的，它们的初始信念相同：$\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{1,0}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{2,0}=\cdots=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{n,0}$。此外，由于我们假设通信拓扑为全连接，用于统计邻居智能体（包括其自身）答案的计数向量 $\boldsymbol{c}_{i,t}$ 对所有智能体均相同。因此，对于所有 $i,i^{\prime}$，均有 $\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i,t-1}+\boldsymbol{c}_{i,t}=\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{i^{\prime},t}$。将等式两边除以 $||\boldsymbol{\alpha}_{\cdot,t}||_{1}$，得到 $p_{i,t}=p_{i^{\prime},t}\;\forall_{i,i^{\prime}}$。于是，(1) 成立。$\hfill\square$
(ii) Fully-isolated structure with $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{i\}$ for all $i$
(ii) 对所有 $i$ 均有 $\mathcal{N}(i)=\{i\}$ 的完全孤立结构
This is an extreme case where none of the agent is connected to a peer. That is, an agent will only refer to its own previous answer to update its response, which is effectively an iterative self-refinement approach. In this case, each agent’s belief parameter evolves independently through $\alpha_{i,t}=\alpha_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{1}\{y_{i,t-1}=1\}$ , where without loss of generality, answer 1 is the correct answer. Hence, it can be easily shown that each agent follows a martingale process, satisfying (1). Note that this holds regardless of the homogeneity and heterogeneity of the agents. $\hfill\square$
这是一种极端情形，其中没有任何智能体与同伴相连。也就是说，智能体只会参考自身上一轮的答案来更新其回答，这实际上是一种迭代式自我改进方法。在这种情况下，每个智能体的信念参数独立地按照 $\alpha_{i,t}=\alpha_{i,t-1}+\mathbf{1}\{y_{i,t-1}=1\}$ 演化，其中不失一般性，答案 1 为正确答案。因此，很容易证明每个智能体都遵循鞅过程，满足 (1)。注意，无论智能体是同质的还是异质的，这一结论都成立。$\hfill\square$
(iii) Isolated cliques, where agents in a clique are homogeneous
(iii) 彼此孤立的团，其中同一团内的智能体是同质的
This scenario is a mixture of the above two cases. Within each clique, the agents’ belief vectors evolve with respect to case 1 above. Then, the entire topology of multiple cliques will follow case 2, where each clique’s collective belief evolves independently of other cliques. Hence, as a whole, (1) is satisfied. $\hfill\square$
这一情形是上述两种情况的混合。在每个团内，智能体的信念向量按照上述情况 1 演化。随后，由多个团构成的整体拓扑将遵循情况 2，即每个团的集体信念独立于其他团演化。因此，从整体上看，(1) 成立。$\hfill\square$
In all three cases (i)-(iii), (1) is satisfied. Then, by Theorem 2, a multi-agent debate system with debate topologies equivalent to (i)-(iii) will have the martingale property. $\hfill\blacksquare$
在 (i)-(iii) 这三种情况下，(1) 均成立。因此，根据定理 2，辩论拓扑与 (i)-(iii) 等价的多智能体辩论系统将具有鞅性质（martingale property）。$\hfill\blacksquare$

Another key takeaway from our study is that careful evaluation is critical for accurately assessing the utility of MAD. We find that the method used to extract final answers from free-form model responses can significantly affect measured performance—sometimes even reversing conclusions. While prior works have reported consistent gains from MAD over majority voting [2, 3, 10], we find these results may be partially driven by error-prone answer extraction, where rule-based parsing can fail even when the model’s response is correct. For example, a model’s output may be correct, but incorrectly marked as incorrect purely due to failures in parsing, rather than actual reasoning mistakes.

Appendix H Broader Impact, Limitations and Future Works附录 H 更广泛的影响、局限性与未来工作
Broader Impact.更广泛的影响。
Our findings highlight an important perspective on Multi-Agent Debate, showing that much of its effectiveness can be achieved through simpler, more accessible methods like Majority Voting. This opens the door to building more efficient and scalable collaborative AI systems without sacrificing performance. Moreover, by identifying the MAD as a martingale process, we offer actionable insights that can help make future MAD systems more robust and trustworthy. We believe our work contributes to a new perspective on debate-based AI frameworks that are both principled and practical. Ultimately, this supports the broader goal of making AI systems more reliable, collaborative, and aligned with human reasoning.
我们的研究发现为多智能体辩论（Multi-Agent Debate）提供了一个重要视角，表明通过多数投票（Majority Voting）等更简单、更易用的方法，就能实现其大部分效果。这为在不牺牲性能的前提下构建更高效、可扩展性更强的协作式 AI 系统开辟了道路。此外，通过将 MAD 识别为鞅过程（martingale process），我们提供了可付诸实践的见解，有助于使未来的 MAD 系统更加稳健、可信。我们认为，我们的工作为兼具理论依据与实用性的基于辩论的 AI 框架提供了新的视角。最终，这有助于实现更广泛的目标，即使 AI 系统更加可靠、更善于协作，并与人类推理保持一致。
Limitations and Future Works.局限性与未来工作。
As discussed in Section 2, our study primarily focuses on the Simultaneous Talk protocol [3], where all agents generate and share their responses concurrently in each debate round. While this setting is widely adopted in prior work, it does not capture the full spectrum of possible communication strategies within multi-agent systems. Alternative protocols, such as One-by-One, where agents respond sequentially, or Simultaneous-Talk-with-Summarizer, where a summarizer agent oversees and summarizes the state of the debate, introduce different dynamics that may influence the rates of subversion and correction. Investigating these alternative protocols in depth remains an important direction for future work. Furthermore, our theoretical framework relies on the assumption of agent homogeneity and may not directly generalize to heterogeneous settings. A promising direction for future work is to extend the martingale analysis to account for heterogeneous agents with differing prior beliefs or reasoning capabilities.
如第 2 节所述，我们的研究主要关注同时发言（Simultaneous Talk）协议 [3]，在该协议中，所有智能体在每轮辩论中同时生成并共享各自的回答。尽管这一设置在既有研究中被广泛采用，但它并未涵盖多智能体系统中所有可能的通信策略。其他协议，例如智能体依次作答的逐一发言（One-by-One）协议，或由一个总结智能体监督并总结辩论状态的带总结者的同时发言（Simultaneous-Talk-with-Summarizer）协议，会引入不同的动态机制，可能影响颠覆率和纠正率。深入研究这些替代协议仍是未来工作的一个重要方向。此外，我们的理论框架依赖于智能体同质性假设，可能无法直接推广至异质性设置。未来工作的一个有前景的方向是扩展鞅分析，以纳入先验信念或推理能力各不相同的异质智能体。
