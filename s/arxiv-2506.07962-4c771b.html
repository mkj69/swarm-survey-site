Abstract摘要
Diversity in training data, architecture, and providers is assumed to mitigate homogeneity in LLMs. However, we lack empirical evidence on whether different LLMs differ meaningfully. We conduct a large-scale empirical evaluation on over 350 LLMs overall, using two popular leaderboards and a resume-screening task. We find substantial correlation in model errors—on one leaderboard dataset, models agree 60% of the time when both models err. We identify factors driving model correlation, including shared architectures and providers. Crucially, however, larger and more accurate models have highly correlated errors, even with distinct architectures and providers. Finally, we show the effects of correlation in two downstream tasks: LLM-as-judge evaluation and hiring—the latter reflecting theoretical predictions regarding algorithmic monoculture.
人们通常认为，训练数据、架构和提供商的多样性能够缓解大语言模型（LLM）的同质化。然而，对于不同 LLM 是否存在实质性差异，我们缺乏实证证据。我们利用两个热门排行榜和一项简历筛选任务，对总计超过 350 个 LLM 开展了大规模实证评估。我们发现，模型错误之间存在显著的相关性——在其中一个排行榜数据集上，当两个模型都出错时，它们有 60% 的情况会给出相同答案。我们识别出了导致模型相关性的因素，包括采用相同架构和来自相同提供商。然而，关键的是，规模更大、准确率更高的模型即使采用不同架构且来自不同提供商，其错误也具有高度相关性。最后，我们展示了相关性在两项下游任务中的影响：以 LLM 为评判者的评估和招聘——后者印证了有关算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）的理论预测。

1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly involved in high-stakes multi-agent settings. A key characteristic of the emerging ecosystem is the large number of models made available by various providers. One HuggingFace leaderboard currently hosts over 2000 models. Diversity potentially supports a healthy ecosystem. In labor markets, different firms using different models could reduce systemic exclusion compared to universal adoption of a single model (Bommasani et al., 2022; Toups et al., 2023; Creel & Hellman, 2022). Systems with multiple models may generally be more robust, allowing “wisdom of crowds” effects and avoiding correlated failure (Verga et al., 2024; Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Tekin et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025).
大语言模型（LLM）正越来越多地参与高风险的多智能体场景。这一新兴生态系统的一个关键特征是，各类提供商推出了大量模型。HuggingFace 的一个排行榜目前收录了超过 2000 个模型。多样性有望促进生态系统的健康发展。在劳动力市场中，与普遍采用单一模型相比，不同企业使用不同模型可能减少系统性排斥 (Bommasani et al., 2022; Toups et al., 2023; Creel & Hellman, 2022)。包含多个模型的系统通常可能更稳健，能够产生“群体智慧”效应，并避免相关性失效 (Verga et al., 2024; Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Tekin et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2025; Yang et al., 2025)。

However, we lack large-scale empirical evidence on whether different available models differ in a meaningful way. How correlated are LLM errors—i.e., how often do models converge on the same wrong answer? What features of models predict high correlation? Are newer models more or less homogeneous? What are the downstream effects of evolving levels of model correlation in high-stakes settings where LLMs are used? Answering these questions is necessary both to understand the current limits of ecosystem diversity, and to more effectively engineer multi-agent systems.
然而，对于现有的不同模型是否存在实质性差异，我们缺乏大规模实证证据。LLM 错误的相关性有多强——即模型有多大概率会给出相同的错误答案？模型的哪些特征能够预测高度相关性？较新的模型是更加同质化，还是更少同质化？在使用 LLM 的高风险场景中，模型相关性水平的变化会产生哪些下游影响？回答这些问题，既是理解当前生态系统多样性局限的必要条件，也是更有效地构建多智能体系统的必要条件。
Here, we answer these questions, using three large datasets of LLM responses: Responses of 349 LLMs on 12,032 multiple choice questions on a HuggingFace leaderboard, 71 LLMs on 14,042 multiple choice questions on the Helm leaderboard (Liang et al., 2023), and 20 LLMs on 1,800 resume-job description pairs.
在本文中，我们利用三个大规模 LLM 回答数据集来回答这些问题：349 个 LLM 对 HuggingFace 排行榜上 12,032 道选择题的回答、71 个 LLM 对 Helm 排行榜 (Liang et al., 2023) 上 14,042 道选择题的回答，以及 20 个 LLM 对 1,800 个简历与职位描述配对的回答。
(1) How correlated are LLM errors?(1) LLM 错误的相关性有多强？
To assess correlation on multiple choice questions, we evaluate the agreement rate of model pairs—conditional on both models being wrong. On the resume evaluations, we evaluate the correlation in residuals in comparison to human labels. In all three datasets, we find that errors correlate highly across LLMs. For example, on Helm, pairs of models agree on average about 60% of the time when both models are incorrect (choosing between incorrect answers uniformly at random would lead to an agreement rate of $1/3$ ).
为了评估选择题上的相关性，我们在两个模型均回答错误的条件下，评估模型对的答案一致率。对于简历评估，我们以人工标签为参照，评估残差之间的相关性。在全部三个数据集中，我们都发现不同 LLM 的错误具有高度相关性。例如，在 Helm 上，当两个模型均回答错误时，模型对平均约有 60% 的情况会给出相同答案（若在错误答案中进行均匀随机选择，答案一致率将为 $1/3$）。
(2) What explains model correlation?(2) 哪些因素能够解释模型相关性？
Through a regression analysis on the datasets above, we find that models with the same provider (company), with the same base architecture, or with similar sizes have more correlated errors. Importantly, even after conditioning on these factors, pairs of models that are more accurate individually also have more correlated errors. This means that newer models that differ on the surface may be converging in their outputs.
通过对上述数据集进行回归分析，我们发现，来自相同提供商（公司）、采用相同基础架构或规模相近的模型，其错误具有更强的相关性。重要的是，即使控制这些因素，各自准确率更高的模型所组成的模型对，其错误也具有更强的相关性。这意味着，表面上有所不同的较新模型，其输出可能正趋于一致。
(3) What are the downstream effects of model correlation?(3) 模型相关性会产生哪些下游影响？
We then study how these patterns in correlation translate to downstream outcomes in two settings: LLM-as-judge evaluation (Zheng et al., 2023) and hiring applications. In the LLM-as-judge setting, we show that model correlations substantially affect LLM model evaluations when another model is used to proxy ground truth labels: judges overinflate the accuracy of models that are less accurate than it—especially for models of the same provider or architecture. Furthermore, motivated by concerns regarding algorithmic monoculture and systemic exclusion in hiring markets (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a), we quantify the implications of correlations across firms when making hiring decisions—for example, how model correlation affects who is hired (compared to hand labels of resume-job fit) and worker welfare (do they match with their most desired firms).
随后，我们研究这些相关性模式如何在两种场景中转化为下游结果：以 LLM 为评判者的评估 (Zheng et al., 2023) 和招聘应用。在以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）的设置中，我们表明，当使用另一个模型作为真实标签的替代时，模型间的相关性会显著影响对 LLM 模型的评估：评判模型会过度高估准确率低于自身的模型的准确率，尤其是来自同一提供商或采用相同架构的模型。此外，出于对招聘市场中算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）和系统性排斥（systemic exclusion）的担忧（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a），我们量化了企业间相关性对招聘决策的影响，例如，模型相关性如何影响哪些人被录用（以简历与岗位匹配程度的人工标签为参照），以及劳动者福利（他们是否与自己最心仪的企业达成匹配）。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Multi-agent and multi-model use多智能体与多模型的使用
A large literature, either implicitly or explicitly, assumes that using different LLMs provides benefits over using the same model. For example, to avoid self-preferencing (Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024), many papers use a separate LLM model to evaluate outputs from one or more models (Zhou et al., 2024; Verga et al., 2024). Raghavan (2024) models a game where it is beneficial to both be accurate and be different than other players; he finds that increasing noise (e.g., via increasing temperature) and choosing a different LLM than competitors may be preferable over using the most individually accurate agent. Our work provides an empirical foundation for this literature: how correlated are different models, and what explains correlation? Our results thus aid in choosing uncorrelated models and analyzing whether there exists sufficient diversity to receive its benefits.
大量文献或隐含或明确地假设，使用不同的 LLM 比使用同一个模型更有益。例如，为避免自我偏好（self-preferencing）（Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024），许多论文使用一个独立的 LLM 模型来评估一个或多个模型的输出（Zhou et al., 2024; Verga et al., 2024）。Raghavan (2024) 构建了一个博弈模型，其中，保持准确以及与其他参与者有所不同都能带来收益；他发现，增加噪声（例如，通过提高温度参数）并选择与竞争对手不同的 LLM，可能比使用单独来看准确率最高的智能体更可取。我们的工作为这类文献提供了实证基础：不同模型之间的相关性有多强，又有哪些因素能够解释这种相关性？因此，我们的结果有助于选择不相关的模型，并分析是否存在足够的多样性来获得其益处。
Algorithmic monoculture and systemic exclusion算法单一化与系统性排斥
A burgeoning literature is concerned with the effects of algorithmic monoculture, when many decision-makers use the same model (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Toups et al., 2023; Baek et al., 2025). For example, in hiring, monoculture can negatively affect correlated hiring firms (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a). There are further concerns about systemic exclusion, when one worker is rejected from all jobs because they all use the same algorithm (Bommasani et al., 2022; Creel & Hellman, 2022). However, Peng & Garg (2024a) argue that monoculture can benefit applicants on average because those given offers have more power to choose where to work and, in equilibrium, a similar number of workers will be hired. Motivated by this literature, in Section 5 we simulate a labor market in which firms use either the same or different large language models to screen applicant resumes.
越来越多的文献关注算法单一化的影响，即许多决策者使用同一个模型时所产生的影响（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Toups et al., 2023; Baek et al., 2025）。例如，在招聘中，算法单一化可能对招聘决策相互关联的企业产生负面影响（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a）。另一个令人担忧的问题是系统性排斥，即由于所有岗位都使用同一种算法，一名劳动者被所有岗位拒绝（Bommasani et al., 2022; Creel & Hellman, 2022）。然而，Peng & Garg (2024a) 认为，算法单一化平均而言可能有利于求职者，因为获得录用邀请的人在选择工作单位时拥有更大的自主权，而且在均衡状态下，被录用的劳动者人数相近。受这类文献启发，我们在第 5 节中模拟了一个劳动力市场，其中企业使用相同或不同的大语言模型来筛选求职者简历。
Ecosystem evaluation生态系统评估
A closely related literature studies the ecosystem of large language models and foundation models more broadly. This literature focuses on the degree to which models use shared components. This is motivated, for example, by the component sharing hypothesis studied by Bommasani et al. (2022); Toups et al. (2023)—i.e., that models with the same components are more likely to have correlated outputs. Further work documents how different models share components (Bommasani et al., 2023). Our paper can be viewed partially as studying the component sharing hypothesis at a large scale.
一类密切相关的文献研究了大语言模型以及更广泛的基础模型的生态系统。这类文献关注模型共享组件的程度。这类研究的动机之一是 Bommasani et al. (2022); Toups et al. (2023) 所研究的组件共享假说，即具有相同组件的模型更可能产生相关的输出。进一步的研究记录了不同模型如何共享组件（Bommasani et al., 2023）。我们的论文在一定程度上可以被视为对组件共享假说的大规模研究。
Generative diversity and monoculture in large language models大语言模型中的生成多样性与单一化
Generative diversity and mode collapse is a common worry regarding large language models (Zhang et al., 2025; Senthilkumar et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024; Alvero et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025). Wu et al. (2024) find severe monoculture within an LLM in instances of a task; for example, they find that the distribution of code written by an LLM has less variance than the distribution of code in its training set. They further find that changing inference parameters (such as temperature, top-p, and prompts) does not mitigate this monoculture, and that it is worse for models with RLHF fine-tuning. Our work complements this analysis by measuring monoculture across LLMs: are different LLMs more correlated with each other than they are with ground truth? We find that they are, and that this is worse among models within the same model company and larger models—while within-model generative diversity is a concern, using multiple different models is not a panacea.
生成多样性和模式坍塌（mode collapse）是围绕大语言模型的普遍担忧（Zhang et al., 2025; Senthilkumar et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024; Alvero et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025）。Wu et al. (2024) 发现，同一个 LLM 在处理某项任务的不同实例时存在严重的单一化现象；例如，他们发现，LLM 编写的代码的分布方差小于其训练集中代码的分布方差。他们进一步发现，改变推理参数（如温度参数、top-p 和提示词）并不能缓解这种单一化现象，而且经过 RLHF 微调的模型中，这种现象更为严重。我们的工作通过衡量不同 LLM 之间的单一化程度，补充了这一分析：不同 LLM 相互之间的相关性是否高于它们与真实标签之间的相关性？我们发现，答案是肯定的，而且这种现象在同一家模型公司的模型之间以及规模更大的模型之间更为严重；虽然模型内部的生成多样性令人担忧，但使用多个不同的模型也并非万能之策。
In complementary and concurrent work, Wenger & Kenett (2025) and Goel et al. (2025) also study model correlation. Wenger & Kenett (2025) show that, on creative tasks, “LLM responses are much more similar to other LLM responses than human responses are to each other.” Goel et al. (2025) build on the observation that error consistency metrics (Geirhos et al., 2020) do not capture differing predictions on errors. They develop a novel metric for measuring similarity that adjusts for model accuracy, considers different wrong predictions as a disagreement, and uses the probability distribution over answer choices; we likewise use a metric (do models agree on an answer when both err) that satisfies the first two criteria but does not incorporate model probabilities. Goel et al. (2025) then study implications of model correlation, such as affinity bias in LLM-as-judge and weak-to-strong training, and find that more capable models make more similar mistakes. We similarly find—across three datasets—that more accurate models make similar mistakes, and that this affects LLM-as-judge setups. However, we also broadly quantify other correlates of similarity (shared developer, base architecture, model size). We then focus on different downstream outcomes, especially how correlation affects multi-agent systems like in hiring.
在同期开展且与本研究互补的工作中，Wenger & Kenett (2025) 和 Goel et al. (2025) 也研究了模型相关性。Wenger & Kenett (2025) 表明，在创造性任务中，“LLM 回答之间的相似程度远高于人类回答之间的相似程度。”Goel et al. (2025) 的研究基于以下观察：错误一致性指标（Geirhos et al., 2020）无法反映模型出错时所作预测之间的差异。他们提出了一种衡量相似性的新指标，该指标对模型准确率进行校正，将不同的错误预测视为不一致，并利用答案选项上的概率分布；我们同样使用了一种指标（两个模型都出错时，它们是否给出相同答案），满足前两项标准，但不纳入模型概率。随后，Goel et al. (2025) 研究了模型相关性的影响，例如以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）时的亲和偏差以及从弱到强的训练，并发现能力越强的模型所犯的错误越相似。我们同样在三个数据集上发现，准确率越高的模型所犯的错误越相似，而且这会影响以 LLM 为评判者的设置。不过，我们还广泛量化了与相似性相关的其他因素（相同的开发者、基础架构、模型规模）。随后，我们重点研究不同的下游结果，尤其是相关性如何影响招聘等场景中的多智能体系统。
Finally, recent work explores underlying homogeneity in language model representations, e.g., demonstrating that embeddings across models can be translated (Jha et al., 2025) and that activations are consistent at similar depths across multiple networks (Wolfram & Schein, 2025). These findings suggest a mechanistic explanation for our results.
最后，近期研究探索了语言模型表征中潜在的同质性，例如证明了不同模型之间的嵌入可以相互转换（Jha et al., 2025），以及多个网络在相近深度处的激活具有一致性（Wolfram & Schein, 2025）。这些发现为我们的结果提供了一种可能的机制解释。
3 Correlated Errors3 相关性错误
We start by establishing that LLMs broadly have correlated errors, and that this correlation is both substantial and is higher for more individually accurate models.
我们首先证实，LLM 普遍存在错误相关性，而且这种相关性既相当显著，又在单个模型准确率越高时越强。
3.1 Data and methods3.1 数据与方法
We use three datasets of LLM responses: Helm, HuggingFace, and Resumes. Helm and HuggingFace are large, existing datasets of LLM answers to multiple-choice questions. Resumes is a dataset of LLM ratings of resumes (given a job description) that we generate. Details, including prompts and full lists of models, are in the appendix.
我们使用三个 LLM 响应数据集：Helm、HuggingFace 和 Resumes。Helm 和 HuggingFace 是现有的大型数据集，包含 LLM 对选择题给出的答案。Resumes 是我们生成的一个数据集，包含 LLM 根据给定职位描述对简历作出的评分。包括提示词和完整模型列表在内的详细信息见附录。

Both use questions from the Massive Multitask Language Understanding (MMLU) question-answering dataset (Hendrycks et al., 2020), with multiple choice questions and labeled correct answers. Starting from large datasets of job postings (Asaniczka, 2024) and resumes (Bhawal, 2022; Jiechieu & Tsopze, 2021), we select a subset of 30 job descriptions and 60 resumes chosen via a cluster analysis (to find related job descriptions and resumes). This provides 1,800 resume-job description pairs, which we evaluate using 20 LLMs (from Meta, Mistral AI, Amazon, Anthropic, and OpenAI). Again, we extract available features describing these models. We hand-label 450 resume-job pairs (30 unique resumes and 15 job descriptions) using the same criteria as our prompts.
我们以大型招聘信息数据集（Asaniczka, 2024）和简历数据集（Bhawal, 2022; Jiechieu & Tsopze, 2021）为基础，通过聚类分析（以找到相关的职位描述和简历）选出一个包含 30 份职位描述和 60 份简历的子集。由此得到 1,800 个简历与职位描述配对，我们使用 20 个 LLM（来自 Meta、Mistral AI、Amazon、Anthropic 和 OpenAI）对其进行评估。同样，我们提取了描述这些模型的可用特征。我们按照与提示词相同的标准，手动标注了 450 个简历与职位配对（涉及 30 份不同的简历和 15 份职位描述）。
Measuring correlated error.衡量错误相关性。
On HuggingFace and Helm, where responses are multiple choice and where we have ground truth, we measure pairwise error correlation through agreement rate when both models are wrong: when two models are wrong, how often do their wrong answers coincide? On Resumes, we compare the correlation between residuals: the difference between a model’s rating and the human rating for each job-resume pair. Here, we treat the human rating as “ground truth,” but note that job-resume evaluations are subjective. These measures of error correlation aim to reduce confounding based on model accuracy: two independently accurate models will agree on a large fraction of questions in which they both give the correct answer. We, in contrast, are primarily interested in correlated errors. Appendices B and C contain results for alternate correlation measures (such as overall agreement).
对于 HuggingFace 和 Helm，模型回答均为选择题答案，且我们掌握真实答案，因此我们通过两个模型都答错时的一致率来衡量两两模型之间的错误相关性：当两个模型都答错时，它们的错误答案有多大比例相同？对于 Resumes，我们比较残差之间的相关性：残差是指模型对每个职位与简历配对的评分与人工评分之差。这里，我们将人工评分视为“真值”，但需要指出，职位与简历的匹配评估具有主观性。这些错误相关性度量旨在减少模型准确率带来的混杂影响：两个准确率较高且彼此独立的模型，会在大量题目上因均给出正确答案而达成一致。相比之下，我们主要关注的是错误之间的相关性。附录 B 和 C 包含采用其他相关性度量（例如总体一致率）得到的结果。

Then, we evaluate the accuracy of other models according to the judge’s answers. We consider judges chosen to be the most accurate model within a shared model provider (on Helm) or architecture (on HuggingFace). 在本节中，我们考察 LLM 之间的相关性在 LLM 作为评判者的设置中的影响；在这种设置中，使用一个评判模型来评估其他模型的准确率。然后，我们根据评判模型的答案来评估其他模型的准确率。我们选取同一模型提供商旗下（在 Helm 上）或同一架构内（在 HuggingFace 上）准确率最高的模型作为评判者。图 2 展示了 Helm 上的结果，横轴表示模型的真实准确率，纵轴表示准确率的虚增幅度（评判准确率 - 真实准确率）。虚增幅度大于 0 表示评判准确率高于模型的真实准确率。我们用红色绘制来自同一提供商或采用同一架构的模型，用蓝色绘制其他模型。
A stark pattern emerges: each judge systematically inflates the accuracy of models that are less accurate than itself, due to correlated errors (the judge marks incorrect answers as correct if both models agree on the incorrect answer). On the other hand, each judge underinflates the accuracy of models that are more accurate than itself (the judge cannot reward a model for answering correctly on a question it itself answers incorrectly). We also see examples in which judges significantly inflate the accuracy of models from the same provider (shown in red). This reflects concerns of self-preferencing (Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024), further suggesting that relative self-preferencing can occur across different models from the same family; it further connects to other limitations of the LLM-as-judge paradigm (Gu et al., 2024; Kamoi et al., 2024b; Kamoi et al., 2024a; Stureborg et al., 2024). More generally, the results suggest that, when comparing models or establishing error rates using LLM-as-judge, it is important to calibrate error metrics for each model-judge pair using ground-truth data.
一个鲜明的模式显现出来：由于错误之间存在相关性，每个评判模型都会系统性地高估准确率低于自身的模型的准确率（如果两个模型给出了相同的错误答案，评判模型就会将该错误答案判为正确）。另一方面，每个评判模型都会低估准确率高于自身的模型的准确率（如果某个问题评判模型自身回答错误，它就无法对另一模型在该问题上的正确回答给予肯定）。我们还观察到一些例子，其中评判模型显著高估了来自同一提供商的模型的准确率（以红色显示）。这反映了对自我偏好（self-preferencing）的担忧 (Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024)，并进一步表明，相对的自我偏好可能出现在同一模型家族的不同模型之间；这也与 LLM 作为评判者这一范式的其他局限性有关 (Gu et al., 2024; Kamoi et al., 2024b; Kamoi et al., 2024a; Stureborg et al., 2024)。更一般地说，结果表明，在使用 LLM 作为评判者来比较模型或确定错误率时，必须使用真实标注数据，对每个模型与评判者组合的误差指标进行校准。

LLM-use in hiring decisions has been identified as a particularly important setting in which homogeneity—i.e., algorithmic monoculture—may have negative effects (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a), on top of general concerns regarding biased decisionmaking (Wilson & Caliskan, 2024; Gaebler et al., 2024). We study several downstream outcomes studied in this prior literature. First, we study the systemic exclusion rate (Bommasani et al., 2022), the proportion of applicants who are screened out of all jobs. Second, we study downstream outcomes of applicants in a matching market setting (Peng & Garg, 2024a). A primary aim of this analysis is to test theoretical predictions on the impact of homogeneity. In contrast to existing empirical work, our experiments use real resumes and job descriptions. We also use LLMs as opposed to more classical tabular machine learning models, more closely resembling potential deployments of LLMs as hiring tools.
在本节中，我们研究 LLM 之间的相关性在招聘场景中的影响。除了对决策偏见的一般性担忧 (Wilson & Caliskan, 2024; Gaebler et al., 2024) 之外，招聘决策中使用 LLM 已被认为是同质性（即算法单一化）可能产生负面影响的一个尤为重要的场景 (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a)。我们研究了这些既有文献所考察的若干下游结果。首先，我们研究系统性排除率 (Bommasani et al., 2022)，即在所有岗位的筛选中均被淘汰的求职者所占的比例。其次，我们研究匹配市场环境中求职者的下游结果 (Peng & Garg, 2024a)。这项分析的一个主要目的是检验关于同质性影响的理论预测。与现有实证研究不同，我们的实验使用真实的简历和职位描述。我们还使用 LLM，而非更传统的表格数据机器学习模型，从而更贴近 LLM 作为招聘工具的潜在部署方式。

A key concern in algorithmic monoculture is systemic exclusion, in which an applicant is screened out of all opportunities (Creel & Hellman, 2022; Bommasani et al., 2022).
算法单一化中的一个关键问题是系统性排斥，即申请者在所有机会的筛选中都被淘汰（Creel & Hellman, 2022; Bommasani et al., 2022）。
More formally, for a set of firms $F$ and a set of applicants $A$ , we let $s_{f}(a)$ denote the percentile ranking of applicant $a\in A$ in the preference list of firm $f\in F$ . Then an applicant $a$ receives an interview from firm $f$ if and only if $s_{f}(a)\geq 1-p$ . $$r(F)=\frac{|{a\in A\mid s_{f}(a)<1-p\text{ for all }f\in F}|}{|A|},$$

When firms each use a random LLM, however, we see that even with 20 firms, around 20 percent of applicants continue to be systemically excluded—a consequence of general correlation across LLMs. (Some level of systemic exclusion may be acceptable if some resumes are definitely poor fits for a position.)

5.2 Results: Matching markets effects5.2 结果：匹配市场效应
Above, we study systemic exclusion in a setting where each firm offers interviews to a fixed number of top applicants. However, theoretical work on monoculture in matching markets suggests that it is also important to consider market-level effects, since firms may adjust offers to fill capacity (Peng & Garg, 2024a). Under a stable matching framework, Peng & Garg (2024a) make three sets of predictions under algorithmic monoculture, corresponding to firm outcomes (do firms collectively hire the best-fit applicants), applicant outcomes (do they match with their most preferred firms), and outcomes under differential access (some applicants apply to more jobs than do other applicants).
上文中，我们在每家公司向固定数量的排名靠前的申请者提供面试机会的情境下研究了系统性排斥。然而，关于匹配市场中单一化的理论研究表明，考虑市场层面的影响同样重要，因为公司可能会调整录用通知的发放，以填满招聘名额（Peng & Garg, 2024a）。在稳定匹配（stable matching）框架下，Peng & Garg (2024a) 针对算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）提出了三组预测，分别涉及企业结果（企业整体上是否雇用了最匹配的求职者）、求职者结果（求职者是否与自己最偏好的企业匹配），以及申请机会存在差异时的结果（一些求职者申请的职位数量多于其他求职者）。
In this section, we test these predictions under a stable matching framework and LLM correlation. We consider markets with a set of 60 applicants $A$ and a set of 30 firms $F$ (or the hand-labeled subset). Each firm has capacity of 1: each applicant accepts at most one job offer, and each firm can hire at most one applicant. For all experiments in this section, each applicant $a\in A$ has uniformly random preferences over firms. (Appendix B.2 reproduces experiments where applicant preferences are determined by an LLM.) To understand how markets behave when firms use LLMs to rank applicants, we consider markets in which firms form their preferences according to the five methods outlined above, spanning full monoculture to full polyculture.
本节在稳定匹配框架下，结合 LLM 的相关性检验这些预测。我们考虑的市场包含一个由 60 名求职者构成的集合 $A$ 和一个由 30 家企业构成的集合 $F$（或其人工标注子集）。每家企业的容量为 1：每名求职者最多接受一份工作录用，每家企业最多雇用一名求职者。在本节的所有实验中，每名求职者 $a\in A$ 对企业的偏好均从所有可能的偏好排序中均匀随机抽取。（附录 B.2 复现了求职者偏好由 LLM 决定的实验。）为了解企业使用 LLM 对求职者排序时市场如何运作，我们考虑了企业按照上述五种方法形成偏好的市场，涵盖从完全单一化到完全多样化（polyculture）的情形。

Firm Outcomes: match probability conditional on applicant rating企业结果：以求职者评分为条件的匹配概率
First, we study how an applicant’s true rating affects match probability across different firm preference methods. The theoretical literature establishes that monoculture worsens firm outcomes, i.e., applicants with the highest true ratings are less likely to match with firms (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a); in contrast, more diverse models can result in a “wisdom of crowds” effect, with firms collectively matching with the highest rated applicants (Peng & Garg, 2024b).
首先，我们研究在不同企业偏好形成方法下，求职者的真实评分如何影响匹配概率。理论文献表明，单一化会使企业结果恶化，即真实评分最高的求职者与企业匹配的可能性更低（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a）；相比之下，更多样化的模型可能产生“群体智慧”效应，使企业整体上与评分最高的求职者匹配（Peng & Garg, 2024b）。
We use our human hand labels as a ground truth for applicant quality. In each market, we track which applicants are successfully matched and their corresponding human-labeled scores. To analyze the effect of applicant rating, we group scores into discrete buckets (e.g., $[1,2)$ , $[2,3)$ , etc.). The match probability for a given bucket $b$ is then defined as the fraction of applicants in that bucket who are matched.
我们以人工标注作为求职者质量的真实基准。在每个市场中，我们记录哪些求职者成功匹配，以及他们对应的人工标注评分。为分析求职者评分的影响，我们将评分划分到离散区间中（例如 $[1,2)$、$[2,3)$ 等）。给定区间 $b$ 的匹配概率定义为该区间中成功匹配的求职者所占的比例。
Figure 4 shows how match probability changes with true applicant rating, in each market. As expected from the theoretical literature—among the LLM-based markets due to monoculture—using the same (randomly chosen) single LLM leads to the worst firm welfare, with relatively smaller match probabilities for the highest-ranked applicants. On the other hand, using the latest LLMs—which are the most accurate individually but also more correlated than random LLMs—maximizes firm welfare. These results suggest that, as in Raghavan (2024), there are two competing effects for firm welfare: individual model accuracy, and diversity. LLM diversity may not yield wisdom-of-crowds effects that outperform choosing the best LLMs.
图 4 展示了各个市场中匹配概率如何随求职者真实评分变化。与理论文献的预期一致，在基于 LLM 的市场中，由于单一化，使用同一个（随机选择的）LLM 会导致最低的企业福利，排名最高的求职者的匹配概率相对较低。另一方面，使用最新的 LLM 可使企业福利最大化；这些模型单独使用时准确率最高，但彼此之间的相关性也高于随机选择的 LLM。这些结果表明，与 Raghavan (2024) 中的发现一致，企业福利受到两种相互竞争的效应影响：单个模型的准确性与模型多样性。LLM 的多样性所产生的群体智慧效应，未必能优于选择最佳 LLM 所带来的效果。
Applicant Outcomes求职者结果
Another basic metric is applicant welfare: do applicants match to their top choices? Peng & Garg (2024a) suggest that monoculture (increased correlation in firm decisions) yields higher overall applicant welfare (intuitively, the applicants that receive offers do so from more firms, and get to choose which firm to accept an offer from). Correspondingly, we expect applicants in markets with more LLM monoculture to match with more-preferred firms than in markets with less correlation.
另一个基本指标是求职者福利：求职者是否匹配到了自己最偏好的企业？Peng & Garg (2024a) 指出，单一化（企业决策之间的相关性提高）会带来更高的求职者整体福利（直观而言，获得录用的求职者会收到更多企业的录用，并可选择接受哪家企业的录用）。相应地，我们预期，与相关性较低的市场相比，LLM 单一化程度更高的市场中的求职者会匹配到自己更偏好的企业。
Define $\text{rank}(x,y)$ as the rank of the firm $y$ in the preference list of applicant $x$ —a ranking of 1 indicates an applicant’s most preferred firm. The output of the stable matching algorithm results in a matching $M:A\rightarrow F\cup\{\emptyset\}$ . For a set of applicants $A$ , and firm-applicant pairs $M$ , average applicant ranking of match of a labor market is equal to

$$\text{AvgRank}(M)=\frac{\sum_{a:M(a)\neq\emptyset}\text{rank}(a,(M(a))}{|a\in A:M(a)\neq\emptyset|}$$
Figure 5(a) shows that markets using a single LLM (full monoculture) yield the best average applicant rank; as market settings shift towards using uniformly random firm preferences (full polyculture), applicant welfare worsens.
图 5(a) 表明，使用单一 LLM 的市场（完全单一化）能使申请者获得最佳的平均匹配排名；随着市场设置向采用均匀随机企业偏好（完全多样化）转变，申请者福利随之下降。
These market-level results show an opposite trend from the systemic exclusion analysis in Section 5.1, as predicted by Peng & Garg (2024a). Markets with the highest systemic exclusion rates produce the highest-quality matches for applicants, suggesting a trade-off between systemic exclusion (without considering market effects) and applicant welfare when market effects are considered. As above, markets with correlated models (either by the same company or the latest model from each company) have effects in between those of markets with firms using the same LLM or one at random.
正如 Peng & Garg (2024a) 所预测的，这些市场层面的结果呈现出与第 5.1 节系统性排斥分析相反的趋势。系统性排斥率最高的市场为申请者带来了质量最高的匹配，这表明，不考虑市场效应时的系统性排斥与考虑市场效应时的申请者福利之间存在权衡。与上述结果一致，采用相关模型（来自同一公司，或各公司的最新模型）的市场，其影响介于所有企业使用同一 LLM 的市场与企业随机选择 LLM 的市场之间。
Differential Application Access申请机会差异
Finally, we study differential application access, in which applicants may differ in the number of firms to which they apply. Instead of submitting an application to every firm, each applicant applies to a random subset of $t$ firms, where $t\sim\text{Uniform}(1,|F|)$ , where $t$ is drawn for each applicant independently. This mimics a job searching process in which various factors (time, effort, environment, etc.) may affect the number of applications an applicant can submit. Peng & Garg (2024a) predict theoretically that monoculture (using the same algorithm) is more robust to differential access. We explore the effect of the number of applications submitted on relative match probability of applicants.
最后，我们研究申请机会差异，即不同申请者申请的企业数量可能不同。每位申请者并非向所有企业提交申请，而是向随机选取的 $t$ 家企业组成的子集提交申请，其中 $t\sim\text{Uniform}(1,|F|)$，且每位申请者的 $t$ 均独立抽取。这模拟了一种求职过程，其中各种因素（时间、精力、环境等）可能影响申请者能够提交的申请数量。Peng & Garg (2024a) 从理论上预测，单一化（使用相同算法）对机会差异具有更强的稳健性。我们探究提交申请的数量对申请者相对匹配概率的影响。

$$P_{\text{match}}(t)=\frac{|\{a\in A_{t}\mid a\text{ is matched}\}|}{|A_{t}|}$$

其中，$A_{t}$ 是恰好申请了 $t$ 家企业的申请者集合。相对匹配概率 $P_{\text{relative}}(t)$ 则为：
$$P_{\text{relative}}(t)=\frac{P_{\text{match}}(t)}{P_{\text{match}}(1)},$$
where $P_{\text{match}}(1)$ represents the match probability when applicants submit only one application. This normalized metric measures the advantage of submitting more applications.
其中，$P_{\text{match}}(1)$ 表示申请者仅提交一份申请时的匹配概率。这一归一化指标衡量了提交更多申请所带来的优势。
We find that overall, the relative probability of applicants matching increases based on the number of applications submitted, but at different rates depending on the marketplace structure. As shown in Figure 5(b), similar to trends from Figure 5(a), as market settings shift from full monoculture to full polyculture, relative match probability is consistently higher for any $t$ . An applicant is approximately 7 times more likely to match when submitting 30 applications than 1 application in markets under uniformly random preferences, and conversely, an applicant is approximately just 2 times more likely to match when submitting 30 applications versus 1 application when firms use the same LLM. These results align with the hypothesis in Peng & Garg (2024a) markets under full monoculture are most robust to differential application access, since those with more applications receive fewer independent “lottery tickets.”
我们发现，总体而言，申请者匹配成功的相对概率随提交申请数量的增加而上升，但上升速度因市场结构而异。如图 5(b) 所示，与图 5(a) 中的趋势类似，随着市场设置从完全单一化向完全多样化转变，对于任意 $t$，相对匹配概率都更高。在采用均匀随机偏好的市场中，申请者提交 30 份申请时的匹配概率约为提交 1 份申请时的 7 倍；相反，当企业使用同一 LLM 时，申请者提交 30 份申请时的匹配概率仅约为提交 1 份申请时的 2 倍。这些结果与 Peng & Garg (2024a) 的假设一致：完全单一化的市场对申请机会差异最为稳健，因为提交更多申请的人获得的独立“彩票”更少。
6 Conclusion6 结论
We show that LLMs have correlated errors, and that this correlation is substantially higher for individually accurate models and those by the same developer or using the same base architecture. These findings suggest that as model performance increases, models are also converging in the errors that they make. This error correlation has implications for the effectiveness of the LLM-as-judge paradigm, the use of LLMs in hiring, and of multi-agent systems broadly.
我们表明，LLM 的错误具有相关性，而且对于单个模型准确率较高的模型，以及由同一开发者开发或采用相同基础架构的模型，这种相关性显著更高。这些发现表明，随着模型性能提升，模型所犯的错误也在趋同。这种错误相关性会影响以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）范式的有效性、LLM 在招聘中的应用，以及更广泛的多智能体系统应用。
Measure of correlation相关性度量
For our multiple choice analysis in the main text, we use “agreement rate when both models err” as our measure of correlation. Like in the work of Goel et al. (2025), such a metric is designed to correct for model accuracy: two models will not be deemed to be more correlated simply because they both get a question correct. The metric of Goel et al. (2025) also leverages agreements on correct answers and model output probabilities, and so may be preferable when such probabilities are available. In the appendix, we also show results using alternate metrics (overall agreement rate, and agreement rate when either is wrong), and find similar results. Finally, note that one limitation of current metrics (including ours and that of Goel et al. (2025)) is that they treat incorrect answers identically; in practice, some incorrect answers may be “closer” to correct, and so preferred by more accurate models; some questions may also be harder than others. Future work should consider developing a metric that is robust to these characteristics.
在正文的选择题分析中，我们使用“两个模型均出错时的答案一致率”作为相关性度量。与 Goel et al. (2025) 的研究一样，这类指标旨在校正模型准确率的影响：不会仅仅因为两个模型都答对了一道题，就认为它们具有更高的相关性。Goel et al. (2025) 的指标还利用了正确答案上的一致性和模型输出概率，因此，在能够获得这些概率时，该指标可能更为合适。在附录中，我们还展示了使用其他指标（总体答案一致率，以及至少一个模型出错时的答案一致率）得到的结果，并发现结果相似。最后，需要指出，现有指标（包括我们的指标和 Goel et al. (2025) 的指标）的一个局限是，它们对所有错误答案一视同仁；在实际中，某些错误答案可能“更接近”正确答案，因此更容易被准确率较高的模型选中；某些问题也可能比其他问题更难。未来研究应考虑开发一种对这些特征具有稳健性的指标。
Multi-agent performance measurement and limitations.多智能体性能衡量及其局限性。
How to properly evaluate LLM output is a highly contested, complex question (Wallach et al., 2024; Guerdan et al., 2025; Perlitz et al., 2024; Weidinger et al., 2025), and this is especially true for multi-agent systems. Here, we leverage leaderboard metrics based on multiple-choice evaluations, and further have LLMs score resumes numerically, in an offline manner. While these measurements provide only a limited view of LLM capabilities, their standardized nature and broad coverage across models enable us to conduct a large-scale empirical analysis of the correlation between model errors. In comparison to work evaluating open-ended model output diversity (Wu et al., 2024), we are able to evaluate many more (over 350) models, across over 20,000 questions with ground truth labels. Richer evaluation of open-ended generation and complex reasoning tasks remains an important direction for future work. Recent methods may allow analysis over the open-ended types of questions models are correlated on and the ways in open-ended responses correlate (Movva et al., 2025). Our evaluations reflect many proposed use cases of LLMs in high-stakes settings: for example, natural language parsers of resumes already in use produce single-dimensional numeric scores of resumes, such as to shortlist resumes for human hiring managers. Our work does not however directly measure the implications of correlations induced by LLMs helping write job descriptions or resumes (Wiles et al., 2025; Wiles & Horton, 2025).
如何恰当地评估 LLM 输出是一个争议很大且复杂的问题（Wallach et al., 2024; Guerdan et al., 2025; Perlitz et al., 2024; Weidinger et al., 2025），对于多智能体系统尤其如此。在此，我们采用基于选择题评测的排行榜指标，并进一步让 LLM 以离线方式对简历进行数值评分。尽管这些测量只能有限地反映 LLM 的能力，但其标准化的性质以及对众多模型的广泛覆盖，使我们能够对模型错误之间的相关性开展大规模实证分析。与评估模型开放式输出多样性的研究（Wu et al., 2024）相比，我们能够在超过 20,000 道带有真实标签的问题上评估数量多得多的模型（超过 350 个）。对开放式生成和复杂推理任务进行更丰富的评估，仍是未来研究的重要方向。近期的方法或可用于分析模型在哪些类型的开放式问题上表现出相关性，以及开放式回答以何种方式相互关联（Movva et al., 2025）。我们的评估反映了 LLM 在高风险场景中的许多拟议用途：例如，已经投入使用的简历自然语言解析器会为简历生成单维度数值评分，用于为人类招聘经理筛选入围简历等目的。不过，我们的研究并未直接衡量由 LLM 辅助撰写职位描述或简历所引发的相关性的影响（Wiles et al., 2025; Wiles & Horton, 2025）。
Implications for ecosystem monitoring.对生态系统监测的启示。
While work evaluating models individually is prevalent, analysis of correlations across models has been surprisingly rare. This is true even though, as we leverage, such analyses can be done using the same data as is used to evaluate models individually, since benchmarks often repeat questions across models. We thus encourage leaderboard developers to continuously track model correlation—as we show, doing so reveals surprising patterns between individual pairs of models and provides insights on which models might be most effective to use together. Similarly, in our application to study LLMs in labor markets, there exist laws (such as NYC Local Law 144) mandating that companies audit hiring algorithms (Groves et al., 2024; Wright et al., 2024; Terzis et al., 2024); however, empirical analyses of correlations across models or of multiple firms sharing algorithms are relatively unexplored.
尽管单独评估模型的研究十分普遍，但对模型间相关性的分析却出人意料地少见。即便此类分析可以使用单独评估模型时所用的相同数据来开展，情况仍是如此；这是因为基准测试通常会对不同模型重复使用相同的问题，而我们正是利用了这一点。因此，我们鼓励排行榜开发者持续跟踪模型之间的相关性——正如我们所展示的，这样做能够揭示模型两两之间令人意外的模式，并帮助判断哪些模型搭配使用可能最为有效。同样，在我们将研究应用于劳动力市场中的 LLM 时，已有法律（如 NYC Local Law 144）要求企业审计招聘算法（Groves et al., 2024; Wright et al., 2024; Terzis et al., 2024）；然而，对模型间相关性或多家企业共用算法的实证分析仍相对缺乏。
Impact Statement影响声明
We demonstrated that LLMs exhibit large amounts of correlation, even in how they err. This has implications for high-stakes settings such as hiring, where there is risk that applicants are systematically screened out of opportunities, and in LLM-as-judge settings, where models may inflate the estimated performance of other models. While our analysis is limited to a subset of tasks on which homogeneity may be a concern, it serves as a foundation for further evaluation and monitoring of the LLM ecosystem.
我们证明，LLM 表现出很强的相关性，甚至连出错方式也存在相关性。这对招聘等高风险场景具有影响，因为求职者可能面临被系统性地排除在机会之外的风险；这也对以 LLM 作为评判者的场景具有影响，因为模型可能会高估其他模型的表现。尽管我们的分析仅限于同质性可能引发担忧的一部分任务，但它为进一步评估和监测 LLM 生态系统奠定了基础。

For models it evaluates, HuggingFace further provides a range of model features (all known due to the open-source nature of the models), including the number of parameters and the base architecture. Such features are not consistently available for the Helm models, given the proprietary nature of the models. For each model in both data sources, we further extract the model company and performance features (such as accuracy across the leaderboard questions).
由于我们关注的是解释模型之间的相关性，因此还进一步收集了每个模型的特征。对于其评估的模型，HuggingFace 还提供了一系列模型特征（由于这些模型具有开源性质，这些特征均为已知），包括参数数量和基础架构。鉴于 Helm 中的模型具有专有性质，这些特征并非始终可得。对于两个数据来源中的每个模型，我们还进一步提取了模型所属公司及性能特征（例如在排行榜各道问题上的准确率）。
