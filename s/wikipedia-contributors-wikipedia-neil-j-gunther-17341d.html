Neil Gunther（生于1950年8月15日）是一位计算机信息系统研究者，在国际上以开发开源性能建模软件 Pretty Damn Quick (PDQ)，以及提出计算机容量规划与性能分析的游击式方法（Guerrilla approach）而闻名。 Neil Gunther (born 15 August 1950) is a computer information systems researcher best known internationally for developing the open-source performance modeling software Pretty Damn Quick (PDQ) along with the Guerrilla approach to computer capacity planning and performance analysis. 他还因对计算机系统和分组网络中的大幅瞬态现象理论的贡献，以及提出计算可扩展性通用定律而受到引用。 He has also been cited for his contributions to the theory of large transients in computer systems and packet networks, and his universal law of computational scalability.
Gunther 是美国计算机学会（ACM）和电气电子工程师学会（IEEE）的高级会员，同时也是美国数学学会（AMS）、美国物理学会（APS）、计算机测量组织（CMG）及 ACM SIGMETRICS 的会员。 Gunther is a Senior Member of both the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), as well as a member of the American Mathematical Society (AMS), American Physical Society (APS), Computer Measurement Group (CMG) and ACM SIGMETRICS.
教育经历Education
Gunther 是一位具有德国和苏格兰血统的澳大利亚人，于1950年8月15日出生于墨尔本。 Gunther is an Australian of German and Scots ancestry, born in Melbourne on 15 August 1950. 他在北巴尔温郊区长大，于1968年从巴尔温高中毕业并取得大学入学资格。 He grew up in the suburb of North Balwyn, matriculating from Balwyn High School in 1968.
他于1971年获得化学理学学士学位，于1974年在 Tomas M. Kalotas 博士指导下获得物理学荣誉理学学士学位，并于1976年在 Christie J. Eliezer 教授指导下获得应用数学理学硕士学位；这些学位均由拉筹伯大学授予。 He obtained a B.Sc in Chemistry in 1971, as well as a B.Sc.(Hons) in Physics, under Dr. Tomas M. Kalotas in 1974, and an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics, under Prof. Christie J. Eliezer) in 1976; all from LaTrobe University. 随后，他于1980年获得南安普顿大学理论物理学博士学位。 He then obtained a Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics from Southampton University in 1980.
研究工作Research
Gunther 于1980年至1981年在圣何塞州立大学教授物理学。 Gunther taught physics at San Jose State University from 1980 to 1981. 随后，他加入 Syncal Corporation，担任科学家，受 NASA 和 JPL 委托，为其深空任务开发热电材料。 He then joined Syncal Corporation as a scientist contracted by NASA and JPL to develop thermoelectric materials for their deep-space missions. 在那里，他发现硅锗（Si-Ge）热电合金的稳定性受一种基于孤子的析出机制控制。 There, he discovered that the stability of the silicon–germanium (Si-Ge) thermoelectric alloy was controlled by a soliton-based precipitation mechanism.
JPL 利用他的研究成果，为1989年发射的伽利略号任务选择了下一代热电材料。 JPL used his work to select the next generation thermoelectric materials for the Galileo mission launched in 1989.
1982年，Gunther 加入施乐 PARC 研究中心，为 PARC 的小规模超大规模集成电路（VLSI）设计制造生产线开发参数测试和功能测试软件。 In 1982, Gunther joined the Xerox PARC research center to develop parametric and functional test software for PARC's small-scale VLSI design fabrication line. 最终，他被招募加入 Dragon 多处理器工作站项目，并在该项目中开发了 PARCbench 多处理器基准测试。 Ultimately, he was recruited onto the Dragon multiprocessor workstation project where he also developed the PARCbench multiprocessor benchmark.
1989年，他开发了费曼路径积分形式体系的一种变体，用于分析大型计算机系统和分组网络中的性能下降。 In 1989, he developed a version of Feynman's path integral formalism for analyzing performance degradation in large-scale computer systems and packet networks.
1990年，Gunther 加入 Pyramid Technology（该公司后来通过与 Fujitsu Siemens Computers 合并，被并入 Fujitsu Technology Solutions），先后担任高级科学家及性能分析组经理；该小组负责使公司的 Unix 多处理器系统在 TPC 基准测试中达到业界领先水平。 In 1990 Gunther joined Pyramid Technology (later absorbed into Fujitsu Technology Solutions via a merger with Fujitsu Siemens Computers) where he held positions as senior scientist and manager of the Performance Analysis Group that was responsible for attaining industry-high TPC benchmarks on their Unix multiprocessors.
通用可扩展性定律（Universal Scalability Law），即 USL 模型的前身，是为评估和预测内部 TPC 基准测试的可扩展性而开发的。 The progenitor of the Universal Scalability Law, or USL model, was developed to assess and predict in-house TPC benchmark scalability.
这项工作最终在以下书籍的相关章节中得到了进一步阐述。 That work was eventually elaborated upon in Chaps. 《Guerrilla》一书的第4-6章 4-6 of the Guerrilla book
他还为 Reliant RM1000 并行数据库服务器的设计进行了基于事件的排队仿真。 He also performed event-based queueing simulations for the design of the Reliant RM1000 parallel database server.
1994年，Gunther 在加利福尼亚州注册成立了独资企业 Performance Dynamics Company，为高性能计算机系统的管理提供咨询和教育服务，重点关注性能分析与企业范围的容量规划。 In 1994 Gunther founded Performance Dynamics Company as a sole proprietorship, registered in California, to provide consulting and educational services for the management of high performance computer systems with an emphasis on performance analysis and enterprise-wide capacity planning.
基于其游击式容量规划（Guerrilla Capacity Planning）技术的培训课程，已在全球范围内的德意志银行、FedEx、Vodafone 和 Walmart 等公司开设。 Training classes based on his Guerrilla Capacity Planning techniques have been presented worldwide at companies like Deutsche Bank, FedEx, Vodafone, and Walmart.
此后，他于1998年前后发布并持续开发了自己的开源性能建模软件，名为“PDQ (Pretty Damn Quick)”。 He went on to release and develop his own open-source performance modeling software called "PDQ (Pretty Damn Quick)" around 1998. 该软件也随他的第一部性能分析教科书《实用性能分析师》（The Practical Performance Analyst）一同提供。 That software also accompanied his first textbook on performance analysis entitled The Practical Performance Analyst. 此后，他又出版了数部著作。 Several additional books followed. 此外，最初的通用可扩展性定律（USL）在两个方面得到了扩展 Additionally, the original Universal Scalability Law (USL) was extended in two ways
2005年至2008年，Gunther 是洛桑联邦理工学院（EPFL）量子工程团队的成员，该团队开发了一种基于 CMOS 的单光子阵列成像器（量子相机），能够探测具有强度关联的光子。 From 2005 to 2008, Gunther was a member of the Quantum Engineering team at EPFL that developed a CMOS-based single-photon array imager (a quantum camera) capable of detecting intensity correlated photons.
目前的研究兴趣包括将通用可扩展性定律应用于 GenAI 平台。 Current research interests include applying the Universal Scalability Law to GenAI platforms.

IEEE 高级会员（2009年2月当选）。 Senior Member IEEE (elected February 2009).
2008年12月获 A. A. Michelson 奖。 Recipient of the A. A. Michelson Award, December 2008.
2006年和2007年任 EPFL 夏季研究所访问研究人员。 Summer Research Institute visitor, EPFL 2006 and 2007.
1997–2000年任斯坦福大学西部计算机科学研究所讲师。 Lecturer, Western Institute of Computer Science, Stanford University, 1997–2000.
1996年获 CMG 会议最佳论文奖。 Best paper award, CMG conference 1996.
1981–1982年任斯坦福大学材料科学访问学者。 Visiting Scholar in Materials Science, Stanford University, 1981–1982.
1976–1980年获英国科学研究理事会研究生奖学金。 Science Research Council Studentship, U.K. 1976–1980.
1975–1976年获澳大利亚联邦研究生奖学金。 Commonwealth Postgraduate Scholarship, Australia 1975–1976.
