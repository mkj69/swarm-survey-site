林格尔曼效应（Ringelmann effect）是指随着群体规模扩大，群体成员个人的生产效率逐渐降低的倾向。 The Ringelmann effect is the tendency for individual members of a group to become increasingly less productive as the size of their group increases. 这一效应由法国农业工程师 Maximilien Ringelmann（1861–1931）发现，揭示了群体规模与群体成员个人对任务完成所作贡献的大小之间存在反向关系。 This effect, discovered by French agricultural engineer Maximilien Ringelmann (1861–1931), illustrates the inverse relationship that exists between the size of a group and the magnitude of group members’ individual contribution to the completion of a task.
在研究过程损失（process loss，即绩效有效性或效率的降低）与群体生产效率之间的关系时，Ringelmann (1913) 发现，让群体成员共同完成一项任务（例如拉绳子），实际上会使他们付出的努力显著少于各自单独行动时。 While studying the relationship between process loss (i.e., reductions in performance effectiveness or efficiency) and group productivity, Ringelmann (1913) found that having group members work together on a task (e.g., pulling a rope) actually results in significantly less effort than when individual members are acting alone. Ringelmann 发现，随着越来越多的人加入群体，群体往往会变得越来越低效，最终与群体协作和团队参与必然会促使成员付出更多努力这一观念相悖。 Ringelmann discovered that as more and more people are added to a group, the group often becomes increasingly inefficient, ultimately violating the notion that group effort and team participation reliably leads to increased effort on behalf of the members.
原因Causes
根据 Ringelmann (1913) 的观点，群体无法充分发挥其潜力，是因为各种人际过程削弱了群体的整体效能。 According to Ringelmann (1913), groups fail to reach their full potential because various interpersonal processes detract from the group’s overall proficiency. 具体而言，已有研究确定了两种不同的过程，它们可能是群体生产效率降低的来源：动机损失和协调问题。 Namely, two distinct processes have been identified as potential sources for the reduced productivity of groups: loss of motivation, and coordination problems.
动机损失Loss of motivation
动机损失（motivation loss），也称社会惰化（social loafing），是指与独自工作相比，人们在群体中工作时个人付出的努力有所减少的现象 (Williams, Harkin, & Latané, 1981)。 Motivation loss, or social loafing as it is otherwise known, is the reduction of exerted individual effort observed when people work in groups compared to when they work alone (Williams, Harkin, & Latané, 1981). 根据 Ringelmann (1913) 的观点，群体成员往往依赖同事或其他成员来付出完成共同任务所需的努力。 According to Ringelmann (1913), group members tend to rely on their co-workers or co-members to furnish the desired effort required for a communal task. 尽管群体成员在被问及时通常认为自己已尽最大能力作出贡献，但证据表明，即使成员没有意识到自己在偷懒，他们仍会表现出社会惰化 (Karau & Williams, 1993)。 Although group members generally believe that they are contributing at maximum potential when asked, evidence has indicated that members exhibit loafing even when they are unaware that they are doing so (Karau & Williams, 1993). 为了降低群体中的社会惰化程度，关于社会促进（social facilitation）的文献提出了若干“解决办法”。 In order to reduce the level of social loafing in a group, several “solutions” have appeared in the literature on social facilitation. 其中一些解决办法如下： A selection of these solutions is as follows:
提高可识别性：当人们觉得自己的个人想法或产出可以被识别出来（例如会受到评价）时，他们就会更有动力为群体任务付出更多努力 (Harkins & Jackson, 1985)。 Increase identifiability: When people feel as though their individual ideas or outputs are identifiable (e.g., subject to evaluation), they are motivated to exert greater effort towards a group task (Harkins & Jackson, 1985). 这是因为，当任务简单且以个人为单位进行时，人们会开始担忧受到他人评价（评价焦虑），进而通过社会促进提高生产效率。 This is because people become concerned about being evaluated by others (evaluation apprehension) when a task is simple and individualistic, in turn increasing productivity through social facilitation. 同理，如果一项任务允许群体成员保持匿名（即在群体互动中处于幕后，并以不引人注意的方式作出贡献），他们因受到他人评价而感到的压力就会减轻，从而导致社会惰化，并降低完成群体任务的生产效率 (Forsyth, 2006)。 By the same token, should a task allow group members to be anonymous (that is, stay in the background of group interactions and contribute in non-salient ways), they feel less pressure about being evaluated by others, leading to social loafing and reduced productivity on the group task (Forsyth, 2006).
尽量减少搭便车行为：表现出社会惰化的个体通常未能按标准作出贡献，因为他们相信其他人会弥补自己少付出的努力。 Minimize free-riding: Individuals who exhibit social loafing typically fail to contribute to standard because they believe others will make up for their slack. 因此，应使每位成员感到自己是群体中不可或缺的一分子。 Therefore, individual members should be made to feel like they are an indispensable asset of the group. 通过增强成员对自身在群体中所扮演角色重要性的认识，可以促使他们更加努力地实现群体目标 (Kerr & Bruun, 1983)。 By increasing the perceived importance of their personal roles within the group, members tend to work harder towards achieving group goals (Kerr & Bruun, 1983). 缩小群体规模也可以产生类似效果，因为随着群体规模缩小，每位成员在群体中的作用会变得越来越不可或缺，因此偷懒的机会也就更少 (Forsyth, 2006)。 A similar effect can also be achieved by reducing the size of the group, because as group size shrinks, the role of each member in that group becomes increasingly integral, so there is less opportunity to loaf (Forsyth, 2006).
设定目标：根据 Harkins & Szymanski (1989) 的研究，设立清晰、明确目标的群体，其表现往往优于那些已忽视自身目标的群体。 Set goals: According to Harkins & Szymanski (1989), groups that establish clear, explicit goals tend to outperform groups that have lost sight of their objectives. 人们认为，设定明确无歧义的目标能够激发一系列提高生产效率的过程，包括增强对群体的投入、对群体工作进行周密规划和质量监控，以及增加努力的付出 (Weldon, Jehn, & Pradhan, 1991)。 Setting unambiguous goals is believed to stimulate an array of production-enhancing processes, including increased commitment to the group, thorough planning and quality-monitoring of group work, and improved effort exertion (Weldon, Jehn, & Pradhan, 1991). 除了清晰明确之外，群体目标还必须具有挑战性。 Aside from clarity, it is important that group goals be challenging. 这是因为，简单任务无需由群体完成，因此会给成员提供偷懒的机会；而实现具有挑战性的目标则需要所有群体成员充分协作 (Forsyth, 2006)。 This is because easy tasks do not necessitate a group to complete them and thus provide an opportunity for members to loaf, whereas reaching challenging goals requires full collaboration from all groups members (Forsyth, 2006). 例如，回答“2+2 等于多少”这一问题并不需要一个群体；如果为此组建一个群体，也只需要其中一位成员工作。 For example, a group is unnecessary to answer the question “what is 2+2”, and if a group were created to do this, only one member would have to work. 相比之下，完成积分数学作业可能需要一个群体，因为这一目标显然更具挑战性，需要所有成员作出贡献 (Forsyth, 2006)。 In contrast, a group might be necessary to complete integral mathematics assignments, because this goal is obviously more challenging and requires input from all members (Forsyth, 2006).
提高参与程度：减少社会惰化的另一种办法，就是提高群体成员对当前任务或目标的参与程度。 Increase involvement: Another option to reduce social loafing is to simply increase how involved group members are with the task or goal at hand. 这可以通过将任务转化为群体成员之间的友好竞赛来实现，也可以根据群体的整体表现，对任务设置相应的奖励或惩罚 (Forsyth, 2006)。 This can be achieved by turning the task into a friendly competition between group members, or attach rewards or punishment to the task, contingent on the performance of the group as a whole (Forsyth, 2006). 类似地，还可以通过使群体中的个体成员相信当前目标很重要、但其他成员缺乏实现该目标的动机，来防止社会惰化，这一过程称为社会补偿（social compensation）（Forsyth, 2006）。 In a similar vein, loafing can also be prevented by convincing individual group members that the goal at hand is important, but that their colleagues are unmotivated to reach this goal, in a process called social compensation (Forsyth, 2006).
协调损失Loss of coordination
当个体组成群体执行任务时，其表现取决于各自的资源（例如天赋、技能、努力）以及群体内部发挥作用的各种人际过程。 When individuals come together in groups to perform a task, their performance depends on their individual resources (e.g., talents, skills, effort) and the various interpersonal processes at work within the group. 即使群体成员具备完成指定任务所需的能力和专业知识，他们也可能无法以富有成效的方式协调彼此的努力。 Even if group members possess the abilities and expertise required to complete an assigned task, they may fail to coordinate their efforts in a productive way. 例如，冰球球迷可能仅仅因为某支球队全部由明星球员组成，就认为它最有可能获胜。 For example, hockey fans may feel that a particular team has the best chances of winning simply because the team is composed of all-star players. 然而，实际上，如果队员在比赛过程中无法有效地协调彼此的行动，球队的整体表现就很可能受到影响。 However, in reality, if the members of the team are not able to effectively synchronize their actions during game play, the team’s overall performance will likely suffer. 根据 Steiner (1972) 的观点，群体成员之间的协调问题取决于所执行任务的要求。 According to Steiner (1972), coordination problems between group members are a function of the demands of the tasks to be performed. 如果一项任务是单一性任务（即无法拆分为供个别成员执行的子任务），需要使产出最大化才能成功（即达到较高的产出量），并且需要成员相互依赖才能形成群体成果，那么群体的潜在绩效就取决于成员相互协调的能力。 If a task is unitary (i.e., cannot be broken into subtasks for individual members), requires output maximization to be successful (i.e., a high rate of production quantity), and requires interdependence among members to yield a group product, the potential performance of a group relies on members’ abilities to coordinate with one another.
实证支持Empirical support
后续研究推动了林格曼效应（Ringelmann effect）理论的进一步发展。 Subsequent research has aided the further development of the Ringelmann effect theory. 其中尤为值得注意的是，Ingham, Levinger, Graves, and Peckham (1974) 发现，即使被安排在伪群体（即由实验同谋和一名真正的参与者组成的群体）中，群体成员仍然表现出拉绳力量下降的现象。 Most notably, Ingham, Levinger, Graves, and Peckham (1974) discovered that group members continue to exhibit reductions in rope-pulling force even after being placed in pseudo-groups (i.e., groups composed of confederates and one, true participant). 在研究中，Ingham et al. (1974) 指示实验同谋装作用力拉绳，使真正的参与者以为所有成员都在共同用力。 In their study, Ingham et al. (1974) directed confederates to pretend to pull on a rope by faking exertion, suggesting to the real participant that all members were working together. 这里值得关注的是，由于参与者与实验同谋之间实际上不存在协调（后者并未真正参与拉绳），因此沟通不畅无法解释努力程度的下降。 What proves of interest here is that because there was virtually no coordination between the participant and the confederates (they were not physically taking part in the actions), poor communication cannot account for the decrease in effort. 因此，Ingham et al. (1974) 的研究支持了这样一种观点：个体作为群体成员行动时，其表现下降在很大程度上是由动机损失决定的。 Therefore, Ingham et al. (1974) support the assertion that motivational losses largely determine an individual’s decline in performance when acting as a member of a group.
此外，研究表明，曾有团队运动经历的参与者可能不会表现出林格曼效应。 In addition, research has shown that participants who have previous experience in a team sport may not show the Ringelmann effect.
蚂蚁中的反林格曼效应实例Anti-Ringelmann effect in ant examples
蚂蚁中出现了几个明显与林格曼效应相反的实例。 There have been several apparent counter examples to the Ringelmann effect coming from the ants. 早期针对新世界行军蚁的一种 Eciton burchellii 所开展的反林格曼效应研究，将这一现象称为“超效率”（superefficiency）。 Early studies of this anti-Ringelmann effect in Eciton burchellii, a species of New World army ants, referred to the phenomenon as "superefficiency." 在该实例中，行军蚁团队能够合作搬运很大的负载，即使将负载切成大小相等的块并分配给各个个体，个体也无法独自搬运。 In that case, army ant teams could cooperatively transport loads so large that if they were cut into equally sized pieces to distribute to individuals, the individuals could not carry them. 较近的研究表明，织叶蚁（Oecophylla smaragdina）也表现出这种超效率，但情境有所不同：它们以团队形式共同组成牵拉链。 More recently Weaver ants (Oecophylla smaragdina) were also shown to demonstrate such superefficiency but in the different context of teams collectively forming pulling chains. 参与这些链条的蚂蚁分为主动牵拉者和被动抵抗者，使整个团队能够发挥“力棘轮”的作用。 Ants taking part in these chains divide into active pullers and passive resisters that let them work as a team "force ratchet". 主动牵拉者产生拉力，随后这些拉力被储存在由被动抵抗者组成的链条中。 The active pullers generate a pulling force that then get stored in chains of passive resisters. 这使它们能够利用织叶蚁附着器官的摩擦力。 This lets them exploit the frictional strength of weaver ant attachment organs. 其结果是，每只蚂蚁的平均作用力增加一倍，从而使较大的群体能够施加比各个个体独立用力之和更大的力量。 The effect of this is to double average force of each individual ant so that larger groups can exert greater strength than their individual efforts.
