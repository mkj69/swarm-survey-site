Abstract摘要
Current multi-agent large language model (LLM) frameworks rely on explicit orchestration patterns borrowed from human organizational structures: planners delegate to executors, managers coordinate workers, and hierarchical control flow governs agent interactions. These approaches suffer from coordination overhead that scales poorly with agent count and task complexity. We propose a fundamentally different paradigm inspired by natural coordination mechanisms: agents operate locally on a shared artifact, guided only by pressure gradients derived from measurable quality signals, with temporal decay preventing premature convergence. We formalize this as optimization over a pressure landscape and prove convergence guarantees under mild conditions.
当前的多智能体大语言模型（LLM）框架依赖于借鉴人类组织结构的显式编排模式：规划者向执行者分派任务，管理者协调工作者，而层级式控制流支配智能体之间的交互。这些方法存在协调开销，随着智能体数量和任务复杂度的增加，其扩展性较差。我们提出了一种受自然协调机制启发、根本不同的范式：智能体在共享产物上进行局部操作，仅由可测量的质量信号所导出的压力梯度引导，同时通过时间衰减防止过早收敛。我们将其形式化为压力景观上的优化问题，并证明了在温和条件下的收敛保证。
Empirically, on meeting room scheduling across 1350 total trials (270 per strategy), pressure-field coordination achieves $4\times$ higher solve rates than conversation-based coordination and over $30\times$ higher than hierarchical control (48.5% vs 11.1% vs 1.5%; all pairwise comparisons $p<0.001$ ). Ablation studies suggest temporal decay is beneficial. On easy problems, pressure-field achieves 86.7% solve rate compared to 33.3% for the next-best baseline. Foundation models enable this approach: their broad pretraining and zero-shot reasoning allow quality-improving patches from local pressure signals alone, without domain-specific coordination protocols. This suggests that constraint-driven emergence offers a simpler and more effective foundation for multi-agent AI.
在会议室调度任务共计 1350 次试验（每种策略 270 次）的实证研究中，压力场协调的求解成功率达到基于对话的协调的 $4\times$，并超过层级控制的 $30\times$（48.5% vs 11.1% vs 1.5%；所有两两比较均为 $p<0.001$）。消融研究表明，时间衰减具有益处。在简单问题上，压力场方法的求解成功率达到 86.7%，而表现次优的基线为 33.3%。基础模型使这一方法成为可能：其广泛的预训练和零样本推理能力使其仅凭局部压力信号就能生成提升质量的修补方案，而无需特定领域的协调协议。这表明，约束驱动的涌现为多智能体 AI 提供了一种更简单、更有效的基础。

2.1 MAS Coordination Theory2.1 MAS 协调理论
Pressure-field coordination occupies a unique position in the Multi-Agent System landscape: it eliminates roles (unlike organizational paradigms), messages (unlike Generalized Partial Global Planning), and intention reasoning (unlike SharedPlans) while providing formal convergence guarantees (unlike purely reactive systems). This section positions our contribution within four established coordination frameworks, showing how artifact refinement with measurable quality signals enables this architectural simplification. For this domain class, coordination complexity collapses from quadratic message-passing to constant-time state-sharing.
压力场协调在多智能体系统领域占据独特位置：它消除了角色（有别于组织范式）、消息（有别于广义部分全局规划）和意图推理（有别于 SharedPlans），同时提供形式化的收敛保证（有别于纯反应式系统）。本节将我们的贡献置于四种成熟的协调框架中，展示如何通过具有可测量质量信号的产物改进，实现这种架构简化。对于这类领域，协调复杂度从二次复杂度的消息传递降至常数时间的状态共享。
2.1.1 Organizational Paradigms and Dependency Management2.1.1 组织范式与依赖关系管理
Pressure-field coordination achieves role-free coordination: any agent can address any high-pressure region without negotiating access rights or awaiting task assignment. This contrasts sharply with traditional organizational paradigms. Horling and Lesser Horling and Lesser (2004) surveyed nine such paradigms—from rigid hierarchies to flexible markets—finding that all assign explicit roles constraining agent behavior. Dignum Dignum (2009) systematizes this tradition, defining organizational models through three dimensions: structure (roles and relationships), norms (behavioral constraints), and dynamics (how organizations adapt). These dimensions require explicit specification and maintenance—designers must anticipate role interactions, encode coordination norms, and implement adaptation mechanisms.
压力场协调实现了无角色协调：任何智能体都可以处理任何高压区域，无需协商访问权限或等待任务分配。这与传统的组织范式形成了鲜明对比。Horling and Lesser Horling and Lesser (2004) 综述了九种此类范式——从刚性的层级结构到灵活的市场机制——发现它们都分配了约束智能体行为的显式角色。Dignum Dignum (2009) 对这一研究传统进行了系统化梳理，通过三个维度定义组织模型：结构（角色与关系）、规范（行为约束）和动态（组织如何适应）。这些维度需要显式规定和维护——设计者必须预先考虑角色之间的交互、编码协调规范，并实现适应机制。
Pressure-field coordination eliminates all three dimensions through gradient-based coordination. Roles dissolve: any agent may address any high-pressure region without negotiating access rights or awaiting task assignment. Norms become implicit: the pressure function encodes what “good” behavior means, and agents that reduce pressure are by definition norm-compliant. Dynamics emerge naturally: temporal decay continuously destabilizes the pressure landscape, forcing ongoing adaptation without explicit organizational change protocols. Where Dignum’s organizational models require designers to specify “who may do what with whom,” pressure-field coordination answers: “anyone may improve anywhere, and coordination emerges from shared perception of quality signals.”
压力场协调通过基于梯度的协调消除了这三个维度。角色不复存在：任何智能体都可以处理任何高压区域，无需协商访问权限或等待任务分配。规范变为隐式：压力函数编码了何为“良好”行为，而降低压力的智能体按定义就符合规范。动态自然涌现：时间衰减持续使压力景观失稳，从而迫使系统不断适应，而无需显式的组织变更协议。Dignum 的组织模型要求设计者规定“谁可以与谁一起做什么”，而压力场协调给出的答案是：“任何人都可以在任何位置进行改进，协调则从对质量信号的共同感知中涌现。”
Our approach instantiates Malone and Crowston’s Malone and Crowston (1994) coordination framework with a critical difference: the artifact itself is the shared resource, and pressure gradients serve as dependency signals. Malone and Crowston identify “shared resource” management as a fundamental coordination pattern requiring protocols for access control, conflict resolution, and priority assignment. Pressure-field coordination implements this pattern through a different mechanism: rather than assigning roles to manage resource access, agents share read access to the entire artifact and propose changes to high-pressure regions. Selection and validation phases resolve conflicts implicitly—only pressure-reducing patches are applied, and the highest-scoring patch wins when proposals conflict. Coordination emerges from pressure alignment—agents reduce local pressure, which reduces global pressure through the artifact’s shared state.
我们的方法是 Malone and Crowston’s Malone and Crowston (1994) 协调框架的一种具体实现，但有一个关键区别：产物本身就是共享资源，而压力梯度充当依赖关系信号。Malone and Crowston 将“共享资源”管理视为一种基本协调模式，需要访问控制、冲突解决和优先级分配协议。压力场协调通过另一种机制实现这一模式：智能体共享对整个产物的读取权限，并提出针对高压区域的修改，而不是通过分配角色来管理资源访问。选择和验证阶段以隐式方式解决冲突——只有降低压力的补丁才会被应用；当提案发生冲突时，得分最高的补丁胜出。协调从压力对齐中涌现——智能体降低局部压力，并通过产物的共享状态降低全局压力。
2.1.2 Distributed Problem Solving and Communication Overhead2.1.2 分布式问题求解与通信开销
Pressure-field coordination achieves $O(1)$ inter-agent communication overhead—agents exchange no messages. Coordination occurs entirely through shared artifact reads and writes, eliminating the message-passing bottleneck. This contrasts with the Generalized Partial Global Planning framework Decker and Lesser (1995), which reduces communication from $O(n^{2})$ pairwise negotiation to $O(n\log n)$ hierarchical aggregation through summary information exchange. While Generalized Partial Global Planning represents significant progress, its explicit messages—task announcements, commitment exchanges, schedule updates—still introduce latency and failure points at scale.
压力场协调实现了 $O(1)$ 的智能体间通信开销——智能体之间不交换任何消息。协调完全通过对共享产物的读写来实现，从而消除了消息传递瓶颈。这与广义部分全局规划框架 Decker and Lesser (1995) 形成对比，后者通过交换摘要信息，将通信从 $O(n^{2})$ 的两两协商降为 $O(n\log n)$ 的层级聚合。尽管广义部分全局规划代表了显著进展，但其显式消息——任务公告、承诺交换、调度更新——在大规模情况下仍会引入延迟和故障点。
The approaches target different domains. Pressure-field coordination specializes in artifact refinement tasks where quality decomposes into measurable regional signals—a class including code quality improvement, document editing, and configuration management. Generalized Partial Global Planning generalizes to complex task networks with precedence constraints. For artifact refinement, however, pressure-field’s stigmergic coordination eliminates message-passing overhead entirely.
这些方法面向不同的领域。压力场协调专门适用于质量可分解为可测量区域信号的制品改进任务，这类任务包括代码质量提升、文档编辑和配置管理。广义部分全局规划（Generalized Partial Global Planning）可推广至具有先后次序约束的复杂任务网络。然而，对于制品改进，压力场的迹协调（stigmergic coordination）完全消除了消息传递开销。
2.1.3 Shared Intentions and Alignment Costs2.1.3 共享意图与对齐成本
Pressure-field coordination eliminates intention alignment through pressure alignment. Rather than reasoning about what other agents believe or intend, agents observe artifact state and pressure gradients. When agents greedily reduce local pressure under separable or bounded-coupling conditions, global pressure decreases. This is coordination without communication about intentions—agents align through shared objective functions, not mutual beliefs.
压力场协调通过压力对齐消除了意图对齐的需要。智能体观察制品状态和压力梯度，而非推理其他智能体的信念或意图。当智能体在可分离或耦合有界的条件下以贪心方式降低局部压力时，全局压力也会下降。这是一种无需就意图进行通信的协调——智能体通过共享目标函数实现对齐，而非通过相互信念。
This contrasts sharply with two foundational frameworks for joint activity. The SharedPlans framework Grosz and Kraus (1996) formalizes collaboration through shared mental attitudes: mutual beliefs about goals, commitments, and action sequences. Cohen and Levesque’s Cohen and Levesque (1991) Joint Intentions theory provides an even more stringent requirement: team members must hold mutual beliefs about the joint goal, individual commitments to the goal, and mutual beliefs about each member’s commitment. Both frameworks capture human-like collaboration but require significant cognitive machinery—intention recognition, commitment protocols, belief revision—all computationally expensive operations that scale poorly with agent count.
这与联合活动的两个基础性框架形成鲜明对比。SharedPlans 框架 Grosz and Kraus (1996) 通过共享的心理态度来形式化协作：关于目标、承诺和行动序列的相互信念。Cohen 和 Levesque 的 Cohen and Levesque (1991) 联合意图（Joint Intentions）理论提出了更严格的要求：团队成员必须持有关于共同目标的相互信念、个人对该目标的承诺，以及关于每位成员承诺的相互信念。这两个框架都刻画了类人的协作，但需要大量认知机制——意图识别、承诺协议和信念修正——这些操作的计算成本都很高，且随着智能体数量增加，其可扩展性较差。
Pressure-field coordination eliminates the mutual belief formation that Joint Intentions requires. Where Cohen and Levesque demand that each agent believe that all teammates are committed to the joint goal (and believe that all teammates believe this, recursively), pressure-field agents need only observe local pressure gradients. The shared artifact is the mutual belief—agents perceive the same pressure landscape without explicit belief exchange. This eliminates the infinite regress of “I believe that you believe that I believe” that makes Joint Intentions computationally expensive at scale.
压力场协调消除了联合意图所要求的相互信念形成过程。Cohen 和 Levesque 要求每个智能体相信所有队友都承诺实现共同目标（并相信所有队友都相信这一点，如此递归），而压力场智能体只需观察局部压力梯度。共享制品本身就是相互信念——智能体无需显式交换信念，就能感知同一压力景观。这消除了“我相信你相信我相信”的无限递归，正是这种递归使联合意图在大规模应用中计算成本高昂。
Our experiments validate this analysis: pressure-field coordination eliminates the overhead of explicit dialogue by coordinating through shared artifact state. The coordination overhead of belief negotiation in explicit dialogue systems can exceed its organizational benefit for constraint satisfaction tasks. The trade-off is transparency: SharedPlans and Joint Intentions support dialogue about why agents act and what teammates are committed to; pressure-field agents react to gradients without explaining reasoning or maintaining models of teammate intentions.
我们的实验验证了这一分析：压力场协调通过共享制品状态进行协调，消除了显式对话的开销。对于约束满足任务，显式对话系统中信念协商的协调开销可能超过其带来的组织收益。代价在于透明度：SharedPlans 和联合意图支持就智能体为何采取行动以及队友作出了哪些承诺进行对话；压力场智能体则对梯度作出反应，既不解释推理过程，也不维护队友意图的模型。
2.1.4 Self-Organization and Emergent Coordination2.1.4 自组织与涌现式协调
Pressure-field coordination satisfies the self-organization criteria established by two complementary frameworks. De Wolf and Holvoet De Wolf and Holvoet (2005) characterize self-organizing systems through absence of external control, local interactions producing global patterns, and dynamic adaptation. They explicitly cite “gradient fields” as a self-organization design pattern—our approach instantiates this pattern with formal guarantees.
压力场协调满足两个互补框架所确立的自组织标准。De Wolf 和 Holvoet 在 De Wolf and Holvoet (2005) 中以不存在外部控制、局部交互产生全局模式以及动态适应来刻画自组织系统。他们明确将“梯度场”列为一种自组织设计模式——我们的方法以具有形式化保证的方式实现了这一模式。
Serugendo et al. Serugendo et al. (2005) provide a more fine-grained taxonomy, identifying four mechanisms through which self-organization emerges: (1) positive feedback amplifying beneficial behaviors, (2) negative feedback dampening harmful behaviors, (3) randomness enabling exploration, and (4) multiple interactions allowing local behaviors to propagate globally. Pressure-field coordination instantiates all four mechanisms:
Serugendo 等人在 Serugendo et al. (2005) 中提出了更细致的分类，指出自组织通过四种机制涌现：(1) 放大有益行为的正反馈，(2) 抑制有害行为的负反馈，(3) 支持探索的随机性，以及 (4) 使局部行为能够传播至全局的多重交互。压力场协调实现了全部四种机制：
Positive feedback: Successful patches are stored as few-shot examples (“positive pheromones”), increasing the probability of similar improvements in neighboring regions. This amplifies productive behaviors.
正反馈：成功的补丁被存储为少样本示例（“正信息素”），从而提高相邻区域出现类似改进的概率。这会放大富有成效的行为。
Negative feedback: Temporal decay continuously erodes fitness, preventing any region from becoming permanently “solved.” Inhibition further dampens over-activity in recently-patched regions.
负反馈：时间衰减持续削弱适应度，防止任何区域永久处于“已解决”状态。抑制机制进一步遏制了近期已应用补丁区域中的过度活动。
Randomness: Stochastic model sampling and band escalation (exploitation $\to$ exploration) inject controlled randomness that prevents premature convergence to local optima.
随机性：随机模型采样和档位升级（利用 $\to$ 探索）引入受控的随机性，防止过早收敛于局部最优解。
Multiple interactions: Each tick produces $K$ parallel patch proposals across agents, with selection applying the best improvements. Multiple interactions per timestep accelerate the propagation of successful strategies.
多重交互：每个时钟步中，各智能体共并行生成 $K$ 个补丁提案，并通过选择机制应用其中最佳的改进。每个时间步内的多重交互加速了成功策略的传播。
No external controller exists—agents observe and act autonomously based on local pressure signals. Coordination emerges from local decisions: agents reduce regional pressure through greedy actions, and global coordination arises from shared artifact state. Temporal decay provides dynamic adaptation—fitness erodes continuously, preventing premature convergence and enabling continued refinement.
系统不存在外部控制器——智能体根据局部压力信号自主观察并采取行动。协调从局部决策中涌现：智能体通过贪心行动降低区域压力，而全局协调则由共享制品状态产生。时间衰减提供了动态适应能力——适应度持续下降，从而防止过早收敛，并使持续改进成为可能。
The theoretical contribution formalizes this intuition through potential game theory. Theorem 5.1 establishes convergence guarantees for aligned pressure systems; the Basin Separation result (Theorem 5.3) explains why decay is necessary to escape suboptimal basins. This connects self-organization principles to formal coordination theory: Serugendo’s four mechanisms map to our formal model—positive feedback to pheromone reinforcement, negative feedback to decay and inhibition, randomness to stochastic sampling, and multiple interactions to parallel validation.
理论贡献通过势博弈理论（potential game theory）将这一直觉形式化。定理 5.1 为对齐的压力系统确立了收敛保证；吸引域分离结果（定理 5.3）解释了为什么衰减对于逃离次优吸引域是必要的。这将自组织原理与形式化协调理论联系起来：Serugendo 的四种机制与我们的形式模型相对应——正反馈对应信息素强化，负反馈对应衰减和抑制，随机性对应随机采样，多重交互对应并行验证。
2.1.5 Foundation Model Enablement2.1.5 基础模型的赋能作用
Foundation Models enable stigmergic coordination by providing capabilities that map directly to stigmergic requirements. This mapping explains why pressure-field coordination becomes practical with Foundation Models in ways it was not with prior agent architectures.
基础模型（Foundation Models）通过提供与迹协同（stigmergic coordination）需求直接对应的能力，使迹协同成为可能。这种对应关系解释了为什么基础模型能使压力场协调变得切实可行，而此前的智能体架构却无法做到。
Broad pretraining enables domain-general patch proposal. Stigmergic coordination requires agents that can propose improvements based solely on local quality signals. Traditional agents require domain-specific action representations—enumerated moves, parameterized operators, or learned policies. Foundation Models’ pretraining on diverse corpora (code, text, structured data) allows patch generation across artifact types without fine-tuning. An Large Language Model can propose meeting schedule adjustments, code refactorings, or configuration changes through the same interface: observe local context, receive pressure feedback, generate improvement. This “universal actor” capability is what makes stigmergic coordination practical for open-ended artifact refinement.
广泛的预训练使跨领域的补丁提议成为可能。迹协同要求智能体能够仅依据局部质量信号提出改进。传统智能体需要特定于领域的动作表示——枚举的动作、参数化的算子或学习得到的策略。基础模型在多样化语料（代码、文本、结构化数据）上的预训练，使其无需微调即可针对不同类型的制品生成补丁。大语言模型可以通过同一接口提出会议日程调整、代码重构或配置变更：观察局部上下文、接收压力反馈、生成改进方案。正是这种“通用行动者”能力，使迹协同能够切实用于开放式制品改进。
Instruction-following replaces action space enumeration. Stigmergic agents need only local context to act—they should not require global state, explicit goals, or complex action grammars. Foundation Models’ instruction-following capabilities allow operation from natural language pressure descriptions alone. Rather than encoding “reduce scheduling conflicts” as a formal operator with preconditions and effects, we simply prompt: “This time block has 3 double-bookings. Propose a schedule change to reduce conflicts.” The Foundation Model interprets this pressure signal and generates appropriate patches without requiring designers to enumerate valid actions.
指令遵循取代了动作空间枚举。迹协同智能体仅需局部上下文即可行动——它们不应需要全局状态、显式目标或复杂的动作语法。基础模型的指令遵循能力使其仅凭自然语言描述的压力即可运作。我们无需将“减少日程冲突”编码为具有前置条件和效果的形式化算子，而只需给出提示：“这个时间段有 3 处重复预订。请提出一项日程变更以减少冲突。”基础模型会解读这一压力信号并生成适当的补丁，无需设计者枚举有效动作。
Zero-shot reasoning interprets quality signals. Stigmergic coordination requires agents to recognize quality deficiencies locally. Foundation Models can identify constraint violations, inefficiencies, and improvement opportunities from examples alone, without explicit training on domain-specific quality metrics. When presented with a schedule showing “Meeting A and Meeting B both have Alice at 2pm,” the model recognizes the conflict and proposes resolutions—not because it was trained on scheduling, but because conflict recognition transfers from pretraining.
零样本推理用于解读质量信号。迹协同要求智能体能够在局部识别质量缺陷。基础模型仅凭示例即可识别约束违反、低效之处和改进机会，无需针对特定领域的质量指标进行显式训练。当看到一份显示“会议 A 和会议 B 都安排 Alice 在下午 2 点参加”的日程时，模型会识别出冲突并提出解决方案——这并非因为它接受过日程安排训练，而是因为冲突识别能力可以从预训练中迁移而来。
In-context learning implements pheromone memory. Stigmergic systems reinforce successful strategies through positive feedback. Foundation Models’ in-context learning provides this mechanism naturally: successful patches become few-shot examples in subsequent prompts, increasing the probability of similar improvements. This “positive pheromone” effect requires no external memory system—the prompt itself carries reinforcement signals.
上下文学习实现了信息素记忆。迹协同系统通过正反馈强化成功的策略。基础模型的上下文学习自然地提供了这一机制：成功的补丁成为后续提示中的少样本示例，从而提高产生类似改进的概率。这种“正信息素”效应无需外部记忆系统——提示本身就承载着强化信号。
Generative flexibility enables unbounded solution spaces. Traditional stigmergic systems (ant colony optimization, particle swarm) operate over discrete, enumerated solution spaces. Foundation Models generate from effectively continuous spaces, proposing patches that no designer anticipated. This generative flexibility is essential for open-ended artifact refinement where the space of valid improvements cannot be enumerated in advance.
生成灵活性使无界解空间成为可能。传统迹协同系统（蚁群优化、粒子群）在离散且已枚举的解空间中运行。基础模型从实质上连续的空间中进行生成，提出任何设计者都未曾预料到的补丁。这种生成灵活性对于开放式制品改进至关重要，因为其中有效改进的空间无法预先枚举。
These five capabilities—domain-general patches, instruction-based operation, zero-shot quality recognition, in-context reinforcement, and generative flexibility—collectively enable stigmergic coordination for artifact refinement tasks that were previously intractable. The FM-MAS synthesis is not merely additive: Foundation Models solve the action enumeration problem that blocked stigmergic approaches, while stigmergic coordination solves the output combination problem that limits single-Foundation Model systems.
这五种能力——跨领域补丁、基于指令的操作、零样本质量识别、上下文强化以及生成灵活性——共同使迹协同能够用于此前难以处理的制品改进任务。FM-MAS 的融合并非简单相加：基础模型解决了阻碍迹协同方法应用的动作枚举问题，而迹协同则解决了限制单一基础模型系统的输出组合问题。

2.3 Swarm Intelligence and Stigmergy2.3 群体智能与迹协同
The concept of stigmergy—indirect coordination through environment modification—was introduced by Grassé Grassé (1959) to explain termite nest-building behavior. Termites deposit pheromone-infused material that attracts further deposits, leading to emergent construction without central planning. This directly instantiates Malone and Crowston’s Malone and Crowston (1994) shared resource coordination: pheromone trails encode dependency information about solution quality. Complex structures arise from simple local rules without any agent having global knowledge.
迹协同这一概念，即通过修改环境实现间接协调，由 Grassé Grassé (1959) 提出，用于解释白蚁的筑巢行为。白蚁放置含有信息素的材料，吸引后续材料继续堆积，从而在没有中央规划的情况下涌现出建造行为。这直接体现了 Malone and Crowston 的 Malone and Crowston (1994) 共享资源协调：信息素轨迹编码了有关解质量的依赖信息。复杂结构由简单的局部规则产生，无需任何智能体掌握全局知识。
Dorigo and colleagues Dorigo et al. (1996); Dorigo and Gambardella (1997) formalized this insight into Ant Colony Optimization, where artificial pheromone trails guide search through solution spaces. Key mechanisms include positive feedback (reinforcing good paths), negative feedback (pheromone evaporation), and purely local decision-making. Ant Colony Optimization has achieved strong results on combinatorial optimization problems including Traveling Salesman Problem, vehicle routing, and scheduling.
Dorigo 及其同事 Dorigo et al. (1996); Dorigo and Gambardella (1997) 将这一认识形式化为蚁群优化（Ant Colony Optimization），其中人工信息素轨迹引导解空间中的搜索。其关键机制包括正反馈（强化优良路径）、负反馈（信息素蒸发）以及完全局部的决策。蚁群优化在旅行商问题、车辆路径规划和调度等组合优化问题上取得了优异的结果。
Our pressure-field coordination directly inherits from stigmergic principles. The artifact serves as the shared environment; regional pressures are analogous to pheromone concentrations; decay corresponds to evaporation. However, we generalize beyond path-finding to arbitrary artifact refinement and provide formal convergence guarantees through the potential game framework.
我们的压力场协调直接继承了迹协同原理。制品充当共享环境；区域压力类似于信息素浓度；衰减对应于蒸发。然而，我们将其从路径搜索推广至任意制品的改进，并通过势博弈框架提供形式化的收敛保证。
2.4 Decentralized Optimization2.4 去中心化优化
Potential games, introduced by Monderer and Shapley Monderer and Shapley (1996), are games where individual incentives align with a global potential function. A key property is that any sequence of unilateral improvements converges to a Nash equilibrium—greedy local play achieves global coordination. This provides the theoretical foundation for our convergence guarantees: under pressure alignment, the artifact pressure serves as a potential function.
势博弈（potential games）由 Monderer and Shapley Monderer and Shapley (1996) 提出，是指个体激励与全局势函数保持一致的博弈。其一个关键性质是，任何单边改进序列都会收敛至纳什均衡——贪心式的局部博弈能够实现全局协调。这为我们的收敛保证提供了理论基础：在压力对齐条件下，制品压力充当势函数。
Distributed gradient descent methods Nedić and Ozdaglar (2009); Yuan et al. (2016) address optimization when data or computation is distributed across nodes. The standard approach combines local gradient steps with consensus averaging. While these methods achieve convergence rates matching centralized alternatives, they typically require communication protocols and synchronization. Our approach avoids explicit communication entirely: agents coordinate only through the shared artifact, achieving $O(1)$ coordination overhead.
分布式梯度下降方法 Nedić and Ozdaglar (2009); Yuan et al. (2016) 处理数据或计算分布在多个节点上的优化问题。标准方法将局部梯度更新与共识平均相结合。尽管这些方法能达到与集中式方法相当的收敛速率，但它们通常需要通信协议和同步。我们的方法完全避免了显式通信：智能体仅通过共享制品进行协调，实现了 $O(1)$ 的协调开销。
The connection between multi-agent learning and game theory has been extensively studied Shoham and Leyton-Brown (2008). Our contribution is applying these insights to Large Language Model-based artifact refinement, where the “game” is defined by pressure functions over quality signals rather than explicit reward structures.
多智能体学习与博弈论之间的联系已得到广泛研究 Shoham and Leyton-Brown (2008)。我们的贡献在于将这些认识应用于基于大语言模型的制品改进，其中的“博弈”由定义在质量信号上的压力函数确定，而非由显式奖励结构确定。
3 Problem Formulation3 问题形式化
We formalize artifact refinement as a dynamical system over a pressure landscape rather than an optimization problem with a target state. The system evolves through local actions and continuous decay, settling into stable basins that represent acceptable artifact states.
我们将制品改进形式化为压力景观上的动力系统，而非具有目标状态的优化问题。系统通过局部行动和持续衰减不断演化，最终稳定在代表可接受制品状态的稳定吸引域中。
3.1 State Space3.1 状态空间
An artifact consists of $n$ regions with content $c_{i}\in\mathcal{C}$ for $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ , where $\mathcal{C}$ is an arbitrary content space (strings, Abstract Syntax Tree nodes, etc.). Each region also carries auxiliary state $h_{i}\in\mathcal{H}$ representing confidence, fitness, and history. Regions are passive subdivisions of the artifact; agents are active proposers that observe regions and generate patches.
一个制品由 $n$ 个区域组成，对于 $i\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$，其内容为 $c_{i}\in\mathcal{C}$，其中 $\mathcal{C}$ 是任意内容空间（字符串、抽象语法树节点等）。每个区域还带有辅助状态 $h_{i}\in\mathcal{H}$，表示置信度、适应度和历史信息。区域是制品的被动划分单元；智能体则是主动的提案者，负责观察区域并生成补丁。
The full system state is:
完整的系统状态为：
$$s=((c_{1},h_{1}),\ldots,(c_{n},h_{n}))\in(\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{H})^{n}$$
3.2 Pressure Landscape3.2 压力景观
A signal function $\sigma:\mathcal{C}\to\mathbb{R}^{d}$ maps content to measurable features. Signals are local: $\sigma(c_{i})$ depends only on region $i$ .
信号函数 $\sigma:\mathcal{C}\to\mathbb{R}^{d}$ 将内容映射为可测量的特征。信号具有局部性：$\sigma(c_{i})$ 仅取决于区域 $i$。
A pressure function $\phi:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ maps signals to scalar “badness.” We consider $k$ pressure axes with weights $\mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{R}^{k}_{>0}$ . The region pressure is:
压力函数 $\phi:\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ 将信号映射为表示“不良程度”的标量。我们考虑 $k$ 个压力维度，其权重为 $\mathbf{w}\in\mathbb{R}^{k}_{>0}$。区域压力为：
$$P_{i}(s)=\sum_{j=1}^{k}w_{j}\phi_{j}(\sigma(c_{i}))$$
The artifact pressure is:
制品（artifact）压力为：
$$P(s)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}P_{i}(s)$$
This defines a landscape over artifact states. Low-pressure regions are “valleys” where the artifact satisfies quality constraints.
这在制品状态空间上定义了一个景观。低压力区域是制品满足质量约束的“谷地”。
3.3 System Dynamics3.3 系统动力学
The system evolves in discrete time steps (ticks). Each tick consists of four phases:
系统以离散时间步（tick）演化。每个时间步包含四个阶段：
Phase 1: Decay. Auxiliary state erodes toward a baseline. For fitness $f_{i}$ and confidence $\gamma_{i}$ components of $h_{i}$ :
阶段 1：衰减。辅助状态逐渐衰减至基线。对于 $h_{i}$ 中的适应度 $f_{i}$ 和置信度 $\gamma_{i}$ 分量：
$$f_{i}^{t+1}=f_{i}^{t}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{f}},\quad\gamma_{i}^{t+1}=\gamma_{i}^{t}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{\gamma}}$$
where $\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma}>0$ are decay rates. Decay ensures that stability requires continuous reinforcement.
其中，$\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma}>0$ 为衰减率。衰减机制确保维持稳定需要持续强化。
Phase 2: Proposal. For each region $i$ where pressure exceeds activation threshold ( $P_{i}>\tau_{\text{act}}$ ) and the region is not inhibited, each actor $a_{k}:\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{H}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathcal{C}$ proposes a content transformation in parallel. Each actor observes only local state $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$ —actors do not communicate or coordinate their proposals.
阶段 2：提议。对于压力超过激活阈值（$P_{i}>\tau_{\text{act}}$）且未受到抑制的每个区域 $i$，各行动者 $a_{k}:\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{H}\times\mathbb{R}^{d}\to\mathcal{C}$ 并行提出内容变换方案。每个行动者仅观察局部状态 $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$——行动者之间不进行通信，也不协调各自的提议。
Phase 3: Validation. When multiple patches are proposed, each is validated on an independent fork of the artifact. Forks are created by cloning artifact state; validation proceeds in parallel across forks. This addresses a fundamental resource constraint: a single artifact cannot be used to test multiple patches simultaneously without cloning.
阶段 3：验证。当提出多个补丁时，每个补丁都在制品的独立分支上进行验证。通过克隆制品状态创建分支；各分支上的验证并行进行。这解决了一个根本性的资源约束：若不进行克隆，就无法使用同一个制品同时测试多个补丁。
Phase 4: Reinforcement. Regions where actions were applied receive fitness and confidence boosts, and enter an inhibition period preventing immediate re-modification. Inhibition allows changes to propagate through the artifact and forces agents to address other high-pressure regions, preventing oscillation around local fixes.
阶段 4：强化。已执行操作的区域会获得适应度和置信度提升，并进入抑制期，以防止立即被再次修改。抑制机制使变更能够在制品中传播，并迫使智能体处理其他高压力区域，从而避免围绕局部修复反复振荡。
$$f_{i}^{t+1}=\min(f_{i}^{t}+\Delta_{f},1),\quad\gamma_{i}^{t+1}=\min(\gamma_{i}^{t}+\Delta_{\gamma},1)$$
3.4 Stable Basins3.4 稳定吸引域
Definition 3.1 (Stability).定义 3.1（稳定性）。
A state $s^{*}$ is stable if, under the system dynamics with no external perturbation:
如果在没有外部扰动的系统动力学下满足以下条件，则状态 $s^{*}$ 是稳定的：
All region pressures are below activation threshold: $P_{i}(s^{*})<\tau_{\text{act}}$ for all $i$
所有区域的压力均低于激活阈值：对所有 $i$，均有 $P_{i}(s^{*})<\tau_{\text{act}}$
Decay is balanced by residual fitness: the system remains in a neighborhood of $s^{*}$
残余适应度抵消衰减：系统保持在 $s^{*}$ 的某个邻域内
The central questions are:
核心问题如下：
Existence: Under what conditions do stable basins exist?
存在性：稳定吸引域在什么条件下存在？
Quality: What is the pressure $P(s^{*})$ of states in stable basins?
质量：稳定吸引域中状态的压力 $P(s^{*})$ 是多少？
Convergence: From initial state $s_{0}$ , does the system reach a stable basin? How quickly?
收敛性：从初始状态 $s_{0}$ 出发，系统能否到达稳定吸引域？需要多长时间？
Decentralization: Can stability be achieved with purely local decisions?
去中心化：能否仅通过局部决策实现稳定？
3.5 The Locality Constraint3.5 局部性约束
The constraint distinguishing our setting from centralized optimization: agents observe only local state. An actor at region $i$ sees $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$ but not:
将我们的设定与集中式优化区分开来的约束是：智能体仅观察局部状态。位于区域 $i$ 的行动者能够看到 $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$，但无法看到：
Other regions’ content $c_{j}$ for $j\neq i$
其他区域的内容 $c_{j}$，其中 $j\neq i$
Global pressure $P(s)$
全局压力 $P(s)$
Other agents’ actions
其他智能体的动作
This rules out coordinated planning. Stability must emerge from local incentives aligned with global pressure reduction.
这排除了协同规划的可能性。稳定性必须从与降低全局压力相一致的局部激励中涌现。
4 Method4 方法
We now present a coordination mechanism that achieves stability through purely local decisions. Under appropriate conditions, the artifact pressure $P(s)$ acts as a potential function: local improvements by individual agents decrease global pressure, guaranteeing convergence without coordination.
现在，我们提出一种完全通过局部决策实现稳定性的协调机制。在适当条件下，制品压力 $P(s)$ 充当势函数：各个智能体的局部改进会降低全局压力，从而保证在无需协调的情况下收敛。
4.1 Pressure Alignment4.1 压力对齐
The locality constraint prohibits agents from observing global state. For decentralized coordination to succeed, we need local incentives to align with global pressure reduction.
局部性约束禁止智能体观测全局状态。要使去中心化协调取得成功，我们需要使局部激励与降低全局压力相一致。
Definition 4.1 (Pressure Alignment).定义 4.1（压力对齐）。
A pressure system is aligned if for any region $i$ , state $s$ , and action $a_{i}$ that reduces local pressure:
若对于任意区域 $i$、状态 $s$ 以及降低局部压力的动作 $a_{i}$，均满足以下条件，则称该压力系统是对齐的：
$$P_{i}(s^{\prime})<P_{i}(s)\quad\Longrightarrow\quad P(s^{\prime})<P(s)$$
where $s^{\prime}=s[c_{i}\mapsto a_{i}(c_{i})]$ is the state after applying $a_{i}$ .
其中，$s^{\prime}=s[c_{i}\mapsto a_{i}(c_{i})]$ 是应用 $a_{i}$ 后的状态。
Alignment holds automatically when pressure functions are separable: each $P_{i}$ depends only on $c_{i}$ , so $P(s)=\sum_{i}P_{i}(s)$ and local improvement directly implies global improvement.
当压力函数可分离时，对齐性自动成立：每个 $P_{i}$ 仅依赖于 $c_{i}$，因此 $P(s)=\sum_{i}P_{i}(s)$，局部改进直接意味着全局改进。
More generally, alignment holds when cross-region interactions are bounded:
更一般地，当跨区域相互作用有界时，对齐性成立：
Definition 4.2 (Bounded Coupling).定义 4.2（有界耦合）。
A pressure system has $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling if for any action $a_{i}$ on region $i$ :
若对于区域 $i$ 上的任意动作 $a_{i}$，均满足以下条件，则称该压力系统具有 $\epsilon$ 有界耦合：
$$|P_{j}(s^{\prime})-P_{j}(s)|\leq\epsilon\quad\forall j\neq i$$
That is, modifying region $i$ changes other regions’ pressures by at most $\epsilon$ .
也就是说，修改区域 $i$ 对其他区域压力造成的变化至多为 $\epsilon$。
Under $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling with $n$ regions, if a local action reduces $P_{i}$ by $\delta>(n-1)\epsilon$ , then global pressure decreases by at least $\delta-(n-1)\epsilon>0$ .
在具有 $n$ 个区域且满足 $\epsilon$ 有界耦合的条件下，如果某个局部动作使 $P_{i}$ 降低了 $\delta>(n-1)\epsilon$，则全局压力至少降低 $\delta-(n-1)\epsilon>0$。
4.2 Connection to Potential Games4.2 与势博弈的联系
The aligned pressure system forms a potential game where:
对齐的压力系统构成一个势博弈，其中：
Players are regions (or agents acting on regions)
参与者是区域（或在区域上采取动作的智能体）
Strategies are content choices $c_{i}\in\mathcal{C}$
策略是内容选择 $c_{i}\in\mathcal{C}$
The potential function is $\Phi(s)=P(s)$
势函数为 $\Phi(s)=P(s)$
In potential games, any sequence of improving moves converges to a Nash equilibrium. In our setting, Nash equilibria correspond to stable basins: states where no local action can reduce pressure below the activation threshold.
在势博弈中，任何由改进动作组成的序列都会收敛到纳什均衡。在我们的设定中，纳什均衡对应于稳定吸引域：在这些状态下，没有任何局部动作能够将压力降至激活阈值以下。
This connection provides our convergence guarantee without requiring explicit coordination.
这一联系为我们提供了无需显式协调的收敛保证。
Note that this convergence result assumes finite action spaces. In practice, patches are drawn from a finite set of Large Language Model-generated proposals per region, satisfying this requirement. More fundamentally, the validation phase (Phase 2b) implicitly discretizes the action space: only patches that reduce pressure are accepted, so the effective action set at any state is the finite set of pressure-reducing proposals generated that tick. For infinite content spaces, convergence to approximate equilibria can be established under Lipschitz continuity conditions on pressure functions.
请注意，这一收敛结果以动作空间有限为前提。在实践中，补丁选自大语言模型为每个区域生成的有限提案集合，因此满足这一要求。更根本地说，验证阶段（阶段 2b）隐式地将动作空间离散化：只有降低压力的补丁才会被接受，因此任一状态下的有效动作集都是在该时步生成的、能够降低压力的有限提案集合。对于无限内容空间，可以在压力函数满足 Lipschitz 连续性条件时证明其收敛到近似均衡。
4.3 The Coordination Algorithm4.3 协调算法
The tick loop implements greedy local improvement with decay-driven exploration:
时步循环通过衰减驱动的探索来实现贪心局部改进：
Input: State $s^{t}$ , signal functions $\{\sigma_{j}\}$ , pressure functions $\{\phi_{j}\}$ , actors $\{a_{k}\}$ , parameters $(\tau_{\text{act}},\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma},\Delta_{f},\Delta_{\gamma},\kappa)$
输入：状态 $s^{t}$，信号函数 $\{\sigma_{j}\}$，压力函数 $\{\phi_{j}\}$，行动者 $\{a_{k}\}$，参数 $(\tau_{\text{act}},\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma},\Delta_{f},\Delta_{\gamma},\kappa)$
Phase 1: Decay For each region $i$ : $\quad f_{i}\leftarrow f_{i}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{f}},\quad\gamma_{i}\leftarrow\gamma_{i}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{\gamma}}$
阶段 1：衰减 对每个区域 $i$：$\quad f_{i}\leftarrow f_{i}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{f}},\quad\gamma_{i}\leftarrow\gamma_{i}\cdot e^{-\lambda_{\gamma}}$
Phase 2: Activation and Proposal $\mathcal{P}\leftarrow\emptyset$ For each region $i$ where $P_{i}(s)\geq\tau_{\text{act}}$ and not inhibited: $\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{i}\leftarrow\sigma(c_{i})$ For each actor $a_{k}$ : $\delta\leftarrow a_{k}(c_{i},h_{i},\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{i})$ $\mathcal{P}\leftarrow\mathcal{P}\cup\{(i,\delta,\hat{\Delta}(\delta))\}$
阶段 2：激活与提案 $\mathcal{P}\leftarrow\emptyset$ 对每个满足 $P_{i}(s)\geq\tau_{\text{act}}$ 且未受抑制的区域 $i$：$\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{i}\leftarrow\sigma(c_{i})$ 对每个行动者 $a_{k}$：$\delta\leftarrow a_{k}(c_{i},h_{i},\boldsymbol{\sigma}_{i})$ $\mathcal{P}\leftarrow\mathcal{P}\cup\{(i,\delta,\hat{\Delta}(\delta))\}$
Phase 3: Parallel Validation and Selection For each candidate patch $(i,\delta,\hat{\Delta})\in\mathcal{P}$ : Fork artifact: $(f_{\text{id}},A_{f})\leftarrow A.\text{fork}()$ Apply $\delta$ to fork $A_{f}$ Validate fork (run tests, check compilation) Collect validation results $\{(i,\delta,\Delta_{\text{actual}},\text{valid})\}$ Sort validated patches by $\Delta_{\text{actual}}$ Greedily select top- $\kappa$ non-conflicting patches
阶段 3：并行验证与选择 对每个候选补丁 $(i,\delta,\hat{\Delta})\in\mathcal{P}$：创建制品分支：$(f_{\text{id}},A_{f})\leftarrow A.\text{fork}()$ 将 $\delta$ 应用于分支 $A_{f}$ 验证分支（运行测试、检查编译）收集验证结果 $\{(i,\delta,\Delta_{\text{actual}},\text{valid})\}$ 按 $\Delta_{\text{actual}}$ 对已通过验证的补丁排序 贪心地选择排名最前的 $\kappa$ 个互不冲突的补丁
Phase 4: Application and Reinforcement For each selected patch $(i,\delta,\cdot)$ : $c_{i}\leftarrow\delta(c_{i})$ $f_{i}\leftarrow\min(f_{i}+\Delta_{f},1)$ , $\gamma_{i}\leftarrow\min(\gamma_{i}+\Delta_{\gamma},1)$ Mark region $i$ inhibited for $\tau_{\text{inh}}$ ticks
阶段 4：应用与强化 对每个选中的补丁 $(i,\delta,\cdot)$：$c_{i}\leftarrow\delta(c_{i})$ $f_{i}\leftarrow\min(f_{i}+\Delta_{f},1)$，$\gamma_{i}\leftarrow\min(\gamma_{i}+\Delta_{\gamma},1)$ 将区域 $i$ 标记为在 $\tau_{\text{inh}}$ 个时步内受抑制
Return updated state $s^{t+1}$
返回更新后的状态 $s^{t+1}$
The algorithm has three key properties:
该算法具有三个关键性质：
Locality. Each actor observes only $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$ . No global state is accessed.
局部性。每个行动者仅观测 $(c_{i},h_{i},\sigma(c_{i}))$。不访问任何全局状态。
Bounded parallelism. At most $\kappa$ patches per tick prevents thrashing. Inhibition prevents repeated modification of the same region.
有界并行性。每个时钟步最多应用 $\kappa$ 个补丁，以防止系统抖动。抑制机制可防止对同一区域进行反复修改。
Decay-driven exploration. Even stable regions eventually decay below confidence thresholds, attracting re-evaluation. This prevents premature convergence to local minima.
衰减驱动的探索。即使是稳定区域，其置信度最终也会衰减至阈值以下，从而引发重新评估。这可防止系统过早收敛到局部极小值。
4.4 Stability and Termination4.4 稳定性与终止
The system reaches a stable basin when:
当满足以下条件时，系统进入稳定吸引域：
All region pressures satisfy $P_{i}(s)<\tau_{\text{act}}$
所有区域的压力均满足 $P_{i}(s)<\tau_{\text{act}}$
Decay is balanced: fitness remains above the threshold needed for stability
衰减得到平衡：适应度保持在维持稳定所需的阈值以上
Termination is economic, not logical. The system stops acting when the cost of action (measured in pressure reduction per patch) falls below the benefit. This matches natural systems: activity ceases when gradients flatten, not when an external goal is declared achieved.
终止依据的是经济性，而非逻辑条件。当行动成本（以每个补丁带来的压力降低量衡量）低于收益时，系统便停止行动。这与自然系统一致：活动在梯度趋于平坦时停止，而非在外部宣告某个目标已达成时停止。
In practice, we also impose budget constraints (maximum ticks or patches) to bound computation.
在实践中，我们还施加预算约束（最大时钟步数或补丁数），以限制计算量。
5 Theoretical Analysis5 理论分析
We establish three main results: (1) convergence to stable basins under alignment, (2) bounds on stable basin quality, and (3) scaling properties relative to centralized alternatives.
我们确立了三个主要结果：(1) 在对齐条件下收敛到稳定吸引域，(2) 稳定吸引域质量的界，以及 (3) 相对于集中式替代方案的扩展特性。
5.1 Convergence Under Alignment5.1 对齐条件下的收敛性
Theorem 5.1 (Convergence).定理 5.1（收敛性）。
Let the pressure system be aligned with $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling. Let $\delta_{\min}>0$ be the minimum local pressure reduction $P_{i}(s)-P_{i}(s^{\prime})$ from any applied patch, and assume $\delta_{\min}>(n-1)\epsilon$ where $n$ is the number of regions. Then from any initial state $s_{0}$ with pressure $P_{0}=P(s_{0})$ , the system reaches a stable basin within:
设压力系统满足对齐条件，且耦合以 $\epsilon$ 为界。设 $\delta_{\min}>0$ 为任意已应用补丁带来的局部压力降低量 $P_{i}(s)-P_{i}(s^{\prime})$ 的最小值，并假设 $\delta_{\min}>(n-1)\epsilon$，其中 $n$ 为区域数量。那么，从压力为 $P_{0}=P(s_{0})$ 的任意初始状态 $s_{0}$ 出发，系统将在以下时钟步数内进入稳定吸引域：
$$T\leq\frac{P_{0}}{\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon}$$
ticks, provided the fitness boost $\Delta_{f}$ from successful patches exceeds decay during inhibition: $\Delta_{f}>1-e^{-\lambda_{f}\cdot\tau_{\text{inh}}}$ .
个时钟步，前提是成功补丁带来的适应度提升 $\Delta_{f}$ 超过抑制期间的衰减量：$\Delta_{f}>1-e^{-\lambda_{f}\cdot\tau_{\text{inh}}}$。
Proof sketch. Under alignment with $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling, each applied patch reduces global pressure by at least $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon>0$ . Since $P(s)\geq 0$ and decreases by a fixed minimum per tick (when patches are applied), the system must reach a state where no region exceeds $\tau_{\text{act}}$ within the stated bound. The decay constraint ensures that stability is maintained once reached: fitness reinforcement from the final patches persists longer than the decay erodes it. $\square$
证明概要。在满足对齐条件且耦合以 $\epsilon$ 为界的情况下，每个已应用补丁使全局压力至少降低 $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon>0$。由于 $P(s)\geq 0$，且每个时钟步（应用补丁时）的压力降低量均不小于某个固定正值，因此系统必定在上述界限内达到所有区域的压力均不超过 $\tau_{\text{act}}$ 的状态。衰减约束确保系统一旦达到稳定状态便能保持稳定：最后若干补丁带来的适应度强化，其持续时间长于衰减将其消耗殆尽所需的时间。$\square$
The bound is loose but establishes that convergence time scales with initial pressure, not with state space size or number of possible actions.
该界限较为宽松，但它表明，收敛时间随初始压力变化，而不随状态空间大小或可能的行动数量变化。
5.2 Basin Quality5.2 吸引域质量
Theorem 5.2 (Basin Quality).定理 5.2（吸引域质量）。
In any stable basin $s^{*}$ , the artifact pressure satisfies:
在任意稳定吸引域 $s^{*}$ 中，产物的压力满足：
$$P(s^{*})<n\cdot\tau_{\text{act}}$$
where $n$ is the number of regions and $\tau_{\text{act}}$ is the activation threshold.
其中，$n$ 为区域数量，$\tau_{\text{act}}$ 为激活阈值。
Proof. By definition of stability, $P_{i}(s^{*})<\tau_{\text{act}}$ for all $i$ . Summing over regions: $P(s^{*})=\sum_{i}P_{i}(s^{*})<n\cdot\tau_{\text{act}}$ . $\square$
证明。根据稳定性的定义，对所有 $i$ 均有 $P_{i}(s^{*})<\tau_{\text{act}}$。对所有区域求和，得到：$P(s^{*})=\sum_{i}P_{i}(s^{*})<n\cdot\tau_{\text{act}}$。$\square$
This bound is tight: adversarial initial conditions can place the system in a basin where each region has pressure just below threshold. However, in practice, actors typically reduce pressure well below $\tau_{\text{act}}$ , yielding much lower basin pressures.
该界是紧的：对抗性初始条件可使系统处于一个各区域压力均略低于阈值的吸引域中。然而，在实践中，行动器（actor）通常会将压力降至远低于 $\tau_{\text{act}}$ 的水平，从而使吸引域内的压力显著降低。
Theorem 5.3 (Basin Separation).定理 5.3（吸引域分离）。
Under separable pressure (zero coupling), distinct stable basins are separated by pressure barriers of height at least $\tau_{\text{act}}$ .
在压力可分离（零耦合）的条件下，不同的稳定吸引域之间由高度至少为 $\tau_{\text{act}}$ 的压力势垒隔开。
Proof sketch. Moving from one basin to another requires some region to exceed $\tau_{\text{act}}$ (otherwise no action is triggered). The minimum such exceedance defines the barrier height. $\square$
证明概要。从一个吸引域转移到另一个吸引域，要求某个区域的压力超过 $\tau_{\text{act}}$（否则不会触发任何行动）。满足这一超阈值条件所需的最低压力定义了势垒高度。$\square$
This explains why decay is necessary: without decay, the system can become trapped in suboptimal basins. Decay gradually erodes fitness, eventually allowing re-evaluation and potential escape to lower-pressure basins.
这解释了为何衰减是必要的：如果没有衰减，系统可能会陷入次优吸引域。衰减会逐渐削弱适应度，最终使系统能够重新评估，并有可能逃逸至压力更低的吸引域。
5.3 Scaling Properties5.3 扩展特性
Theorem 5.4 (Linear Scaling).定理 5.4（线性扩展）。
Let $m$ be the number of regions and $n$ be the number of parallel agents. The per-tick complexity is:
设 $m$ 为区域数量，$n$ 为并行智能体数量。每个时步的复杂度为：
Signal computation: $O(m\cdot d)$ where $d$ is signal dimension
信号计算：$O(m\cdot d)$，其中 $d$ 为信号维度
Pressure computation: $O(m\cdot k)$ where $k$ is the number of pressure axes
压力计算：$O(m\cdot k)$，其中 $k$ 为压力轴数量
Patch proposal: $O(m\cdot a)$ where $a$ is the number of actors
补丁提议：$O(m\cdot a)$，其中 $a$ 为行动器数量
Selection: $O(m\cdot a\cdot\log(m\cdot a))$ for sorting candidates
选择：对候选项排序的复杂度为 $O(m\cdot a\cdot\log(m\cdot a))$
Coordination overhead: $O(1)$ —no inter-agent communication (fork pool is $O(K)$ where $K$ is fixed)
协调开销：$O(1)$——智能体之间无需通信（分叉池的复杂度为 $O(K)$，其中 $K$ 为固定值）
Total: $O(m\cdot(d+k+a\cdot\log(ma)))$ , independent of agent count $n$ .
总计：$O(m\cdot(d+k+a\cdot\log(ma)))$，与智能体数量 $n$ 无关。
Adding agents increases throughput (more patches proposed per tick) without increasing coordination cost. This contrasts with hierarchical schemes where coordination overhead grows with agent count.
增加智能体可以提高吞吐量（每个时步提出更多补丁），而不会增加协调成本。这与层级式方案形成对比，后者的协调开销会随智能体数量增加而增长。
Theorem 5.5 (Parallel Convergence).定理 5.5（并行收敛）。
Under the same alignment conditions as Theorem 5.1, with $K$ patches validated in parallel per tick where patches affect disjoint regions, the system reaches a stable basin within:
在与定理 5.1 相同的对齐条件下，若每个时步并行验证 $K$ 个补丁，且这些补丁影响的区域互不相交，则系统达到稳定吸引域所需的时间上界为：
$$T\leq\frac{P_{0}}{K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)}$$
This improves convergence time by factor $K$ while maintaining guarantees.
这使收敛时间缩短为原来的 $K$ 分之一，同时保持原有保证。
Proof sketch. When $K$ non-conflicting patches are applied per tick, each reduces global pressure by at least $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$ . The combined reduction is $K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)$ per tick. The bound follows directly. Note that if patches conflict (target the same region), only one is selected per region, and effective speedup is reduced. $\square$
证明概要。当每个时步应用 $K$ 个互不冲突的补丁时，每个补丁都会使全局压力至少降低 $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$。每个时步的总降幅为 $K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)$。由此可直接得到该上界。注意，如果补丁存在冲突（以同一区域为目标），则每个区域只会选中一个补丁，实际加速效果也会随之降低。$\square$
5.4 Comparison to Alternatives5.4 与其他方案的比较
We compare against three coordination paradigms:
我们将其与三种协调范式进行比较：
Centralized planning. A global planner evaluates all $(m\cdot a)$ possible actions, selects optimal subset. Per-step complexity: $O(m\cdot a)$ evaluations, but requires global state access. Sequential bottleneck prevents parallelization.
集中式规划。全局规划器评估全部 $(m\cdot a)$ 个可能的行动，并选择最优子集。每步复杂度：需要进行 $O(m\cdot a)$ 次评估，但要求能够访问全局状态。串行瓶颈使其无法并行化。
Hierarchical delegation. Manager agents decompose tasks, delegate to workers. Communication complexity: $O(n\log n)$ for tree-structured delegation with $n$ agents. Latency scales with tree depth. Failure of manager blocks all descendants.
层级式委派。管理智能体将任务分解，并委派给工作智能体。通信复杂度：对于包含 $n$ 个智能体的树形委派结构，为 $O(n\log n)$。延迟随树的深度增加而增长。管理者失效会阻塞其所有后代节点。
Message-passing coordination. Agents negotiate actions through pairwise communication. Convergence requires $O(n^{2})$ messages in worst case for $n$ agents. Consensus protocols add latency.

Alignment verification: The per-region pressure computation uses only gaps, overlaps, and util_var—all strictly local to each time block. The unsched component is added to total pressure only, not per-region. This makes the per-region pressure separable: modifying region $i$ has zero effect on region $j$ ’s pressure for $j\neq i$ , satisfying the alignment condition (Definition 2) with $\epsilon=0$ . While attendee constraints could theoretically create cross-region coupling (the same person attending meetings in different time blocks), our overlap sensor counts overlaps only within each time block, eliminating this coupling source. Empirical analysis (Appendix B) confirms that all observed pressure improvements are positive, consistent with separable pressure.
对齐验证：各区域的压力计算仅使用 gaps、overlaps 和 util_var——这些量都严格局限于各自的时间块。unsched 分量仅计入总压力，不计入各区域的压力。这使得各区域的压力具有可分性：对于 $j\neq i$，修改区域 $i$ 对区域 $j$ 的压力没有任何影响，因此满足 $\epsilon=0$ 时的对齐条件（定义 2）。尽管参会者约束在理论上可能造成跨区域耦合（同一个人参加不同时间块中的会议），但我们的重叠传感器仅统计各时间块内部的重叠，从而消除了这一耦合来源。实证分析（附录 B）证实，所有观测到的压力改善值均为正，这与压力的可分性一致。
6.1.2 Baselines6.1.2 基线
We compare five coordination strategies, all using identical Large Language Models (qwen2.5:0.5b/1.5b/3b via Ollama) to isolate coordination effects:
我们比较了五种协调策略，它们均使用相同的大语言模型（通过 Ollama 运行的 qwen2.5:0.5b/1.5b/3b），以单独考察协调机制的影响：
Pressure-field (ours): Full system with decay (fitness half-life 5s), inhibition (2s cooldown), greedy region selection (highest-pressure region per tick), and parallel validation. Includes band escalation (Exploitation $\to$ Balanced $\to$ Exploration) and model escalation (0.5b $\to$ 1.5b $\to$ 3b).
压力场（我们的方法）：完整系统，包含衰减（适应度半衰期为 5s）、抑制（冷却时间为 2s）、贪心区域选择（每个时钟步选择压力最高的区域）以及并行验证。包含档位升级（开发 $\to$ 均衡 $\to$ 探索）和模型升级（0.5b $\to$ 1.5b $\to$ 3b）。
Conversation: AutoGen-style multi-agent dialogue where agents exchange messages to coordinate scheduling decisions. Agents discuss conflicts and propose solutions through explicit communication.
对话：采用 AutoGen 风格的多智能体对话，智能体通过交换消息来协调排程决策。智能体通过显式通信讨论冲突并提出解决方案。
Hierarchical: Single agent selects the highest-pressure time block each tick, proposes a schedule change, and validates before applying (only accepts pressure-reducing patches). Uses identical prompts to pressure-field. The differences are: (1) greedy region selection always targets the hardest region, and (2) sequential execution processes one region per tick. This represents centralized, quality-gated control.
层级式：单个智能体在每个时钟步选择压力最高的时间块，提出日程修改方案，并在应用前进行验证（仅接受能够降低压力的补丁）。使用与压力场方法相同的提示词。区别在于：(1) 贪心区域选择始终以最困难的区域为目标；(2) 顺序执行在每个时钟步处理一个区域。这体现了集中式、以质量为准入条件的控制。
Sequential: Single agent iterates through time blocks in fixed order, proposing schedule changes one region at a time. No parallelism, pressure guidance, or patch validation—applies any syntactically valid patch regardless of quality impact.
顺序式：单个智能体按固定顺序遍历时间块，每次针对一个区域提出日程修改。不采用并行处理、压力引导或补丁验证——应用任何语法有效的补丁，无论其对质量有何影响。
Random: Selects random time blocks and proposes schedule changes. No patch validation—applies any syntactically valid patch regardless of quality impact.
随机式：随机选择时间块并提出日程修改。不进行补丁验证——应用任何语法有效的补丁，无论其对质量有何影响。
Note on parallelism: Pressure-field validates multiple patches in parallel ( $K$ regions per tick), while hierarchical validates one patch sequentially. This asymmetry is inherent to the coordination paradigm, not an implementation choice: hierarchical control requires the manager to select a region, delegate to a worker, and validate the result before proceeding—delegating to multiple workers simultaneously would require additional coordination protocols (work distribution, conflict resolution, result aggregation) that would transform it into a different architecture entirely. The sequential bottleneck is the cost of centralized control. When hierarchical’s single patch is rejected, the tick produces no progress; when one of pressure-field’s parallel patches is rejected, others may still succeed.
关于并行性的说明：压力场方法并行验证多个补丁（每个时间步处理 $K$ 个区域），而层级式方法每次顺序验证一个补丁。这种不对称性是协调范式固有的，而非实现上的选择：层级式控制要求管理者先选择一个区域，将任务委派给工作者，并在继续之前验证结果——同时向多个工作者委派任务将需要额外的协调协议（任务分配、冲突解决、结果聚合），从而使其转变为一种完全不同的架构。顺序处理的瓶颈是集中式控制的代价。当层级式方法的唯一补丁被拒绝时，该时间步不会取得任何进展；而当压力场方法的某个并行补丁被拒绝时，其他补丁仍可能成功。
Model choice rationale: We deliberately use small, minimally-capable models (0.5b–3b parameters) rather than frontier models. This design choice strengthens our thesis: if the coordination mechanism can extract effective performance from weak models, the mechanism itself is valuable—independent of model capability. Using identical model chains across all strategies isolates coordination effects from model effects. We hypothesize that frontier models (e.g., GPT-4, Claude) would raise absolute solve rates across all strategies while preserving relative rankings: the coordination advantage is orthogonal to model capability, so pressure-field’s $4\times$ improvement over conversation baselines should persist even as the baseline rises.
模型选择依据：我们有意采用能力较为有限的小型模型（0.5b–3b 参数），而非前沿模型。这一设计选择强化了我们的论点：如果协调机制能够使弱模型发挥出有效的性能，那么该机制本身就具有独立于模型能力的价值。所有策略均使用相同的模型链，从而将协调效应与模型效应分离。我们假设，前沿模型（例如 GPT-4、Claude）会提高所有策略的绝对求解成功率，同时保持其相对排名：协调优势与模型能力正交，因此，即使基线水平提高，压力场方法相对于对话式基线的 $4\times$ 提升也应持续存在。

6.3.3 Negative Pheromones6.3.3 负信息素
In addition to positive pheromones (successful patches stored for few-shot examples), we implement negative pheromones: tracking rejected patches that worsened pressure. When agents repeatedly propose ineffective patches (pressure stuck at maximum), the system accumulates rejection history and injects guidance into subsequent prompts.
除了正信息素（存储起来用作少样本示例的成功补丁），我们还实现了负信息素：跟踪那些使压力恶化而被拒绝的补丁。当智能体反复提出无效补丁（压力停滞在最大值）时，系统会积累拒绝历史，并将指导信息注入后续提示中。
Unlike the “AVOID” framing that small models (1.5B parameters) struggle to follow, we use positive language: rejected empty-room patches become “TIP: Schedule meetings in Room A (improves by X).” This reframes what not to do as what to try instead.
小模型（1.5B 参数）难以遵循“避免”式表述，因此我们采用正向语言：被拒绝的空会议室补丁会转化为“提示：将会议安排在 Room A（改善幅度为 X）。”这将不应采取的做法重新表述为可以尝试的替代做法。
Negative pheromones decay at the same rate as positive examples ( $\text{weight}\times 0.95$ per tick, evicted below 0.1), ensuring that old failures don’t permanently block valid approaches. Up to 3 recent rejections per region are included in prompts as “Hints for better scheduling.”
负信息素以与正向示例相同的速率衰减（每个时间步执行 $\text{weight}\times 0.95$，低于 0.1 时移除），确保过去的失败不会永久阻碍有效方法。每个区域最多 3 条近期拒绝记录会以“更好地安排会议的提示”的形式纳入提示中。

The escalation mechanism demonstrates the FM-MAS symbiosis that pressure-field coordination enables. Traditional approaches face a dilemma: use a large, capable model everywhere (expensive) or use a small, efficient model everywhere (limited). Pressure-field coordination dissolves this dilemma through gradient-driven capability invocation: the coordination mechanism itself determines when greater Foundation Model capability is needed.
升级机制展示了压力场协调所促成的 FM-MAS 共生关系。传统方法面临两难：要么在所有环节使用能力强的大模型（成本高），要么在所有环节使用高效的小模型（能力有限）。压力场协调通过梯度驱动的能力调用化解了这一两难：协调机制本身决定何时需要更强的基础模型（Foundation Model）能力。
Ant colonies balance exploitation of known food sources against exploration for new ones. When a pheromone trail grows stale—indicating a depleted source—foragers abandon trail-following and resume random exploration, eventually discovering new paths that become the next generation of trails. This exploitation-exploration balance is fundamental to stigmergic systems: premature commitment to suboptimal solutions must be counteracted by mechanisms that restore exploratory behavior.
蚁群在利用已知食物来源与探索新食物来源之间取得平衡。当信息素路径逐渐失效，表明食物来源已经耗尽时，觅食蚂蚁便会放弃沿路径行进，重新开始随机探索，最终发现新的路线，形成下一代信息素路径。这种利用与探索之间的平衡是痕迹协调（stigmergy）系统的基本特征：必须通过恢复探索行为的机制，抵消过早固守次优解的倾向。

MAS 的贡献：压力梯度为何时升级提供了客观判据。无需关于“问题难度”的启发式规则；压力变化速率停滞就已足以作为信号。协调机制无需显式的能力模型，即可管理异构基础模型的能力。

The system does not “decide” to explore or escalate; it reacts to pressure stagnation, just as ants react to pheromone decay. The result is adaptive Foundation Model utilization: cheap models handle easy regions, expensive models are reserved for regions where cheap models stall.
这一双层机制——智能体内部的行为适应（档位升级）与智能体之间的能力升级（模型升级）——遵循了痕迹协调原则：协调由环境信号（压力梯度）涌现，而非源于显式规划。系统并不“决定”进行探索或升级；它只是对压力停滞作出反应，正如蚂蚁对信息素衰减作出反应一样。由此实现了基础模型的自适应使用：低成本模型处理容易的区域，高成本模型则留给低成本模型陷入停滞的区域。

7 Discussion7 讨论
7.1 Why Does Pressure-Field Dominate?7.1 为什么 Pressure-Field 占据优势？
Our results contradict the intuition that explicit coordination should outperform implicit coordination. We identify three factors explaining pressure-field’s dominance:
我们的结果与显式协调应优于隐式协调这一直觉相悖。我们确定了三个因素，用以解释 pressure-field 的优势：
Coordination overhead harms performance. Hierarchical systems spend computational budget on coordination rather than problem-solving. The manager-worker protocol requires multiple Large Language Model calls per patch (planning, delegation, execution), while pressure-field requires only one (patch proposal). This overhead compounds: hierarchical attempts fewer patches per tick, reducing exploration.
协调开销会损害性能。层级式系统将计算预算用于协调，而非求解问题。管理者—工作者协议针对每个补丁都需要多次调用大语言模型（规划、委派、执行），而 pressure-field 只需调用一次（提出补丁）。这种开销会累积：hierarchical 在每个时间步尝试的补丁更少，从而减少了探索。
Local greedy decisions are effective for constraint satisfaction. Meeting room scheduling exhibits locality: fixing a conflict in one time block rarely creates conflicts in distant blocks. This matches pressure-field’s locality assumption, making greedy local optimization effective. Hierarchical coordination’s global planning provides no benefit for locally-decomposable problems.
局部贪心决策对约束满足问题有效。会议室调度具有局部性：修复一个时间块中的冲突，很少会在相距较远的时间块中引发冲突。这与 pressure-field 的局部性假设相吻合，使局部贪心优化能够有效发挥作用。对于可局部分解的问题，层级式协调中的全局规划并无益处。
Parallel validation amplifies pressure-field’s advantage. Pressure-field validates patches for multiple regions simultaneously, applying the highest-scoring patch per region that reduces pressure. Hierarchical validates one patch at a time, requiring multiple ticks to explore alternatives. On problems with many valid solutions, parallel exploration finds solutions faster.
并行验证放大了 pressure-field 的优势。Pressure-field 同时验证多个区域的补丁，并为每个区域应用能够降低压力且得分最高的补丁。Hierarchical 每次只验证一个补丁，需要多个时间步才能探索备选方案。对于具有许多有效解的问题，并行探索能够更快地找到解。
7.2 Failure Analysis: Why Hierarchical Collapses7.2 失败分析：为什么 Hierarchical 会崩溃
The 30 $\times$ performance gap (48.5% vs 1.5%) demands deeper investigation. Analysis of hierarchical trials reveals a catastrophic failure pattern: the rejection loop.
30 $\times$ 的性能差距（48.5% 对比 1.5%）需要更深入的研究。对 hierarchical 试验的分析揭示了一种灾难性的失败模式：拒绝循环。
The rejection loop mechanism. Hierarchical always selects the highest-pressure region for improvement. But the highest-pressure regions are high-pressure precisely because they are difficult to improve. When the Large Language Model proposes a patch that fails validation (does not reduce pressure), the region remains highest-pressure and is selected again next tick. This creates a self-reinforcing cycle: 66.7% of hierarchical runs (180/270) applied zero patches across all 50 ticks, stuck targeting the same intractable region repeatedly.
拒绝循环的机制。Hierarchical 总是选择压力最高的区域进行改进。然而，压力最高的区域之所以压力高，恰恰是因为它们难以改进。当大语言模型提出的补丁未通过验证（未能降低压力）时，该区域仍然具有最高压力，因此会在下一个时间步再次被选中。这形成了一个自我强化的循环：66.7% 的 hierarchical 运行（180/270）在全部 50 个时间步中未应用任何补丁，陷入了反复针对同一难以处理的区域进行尝试的困境。
Rejection rates confirm the pattern. Across all hierarchical trials, only 173 patches were accepted out of 13,460 proposed—a 98.7% rejection rate. By contrast, pressure-field’s parallel exploration means that even if one agent’s patch is rejected, other agents make progress on different regions. The non-blocking architecture prevents any single difficult region from stalling the entire system.
拒绝率证实了这一模式。在所有 hierarchical 试验中，提出的 13,460 个补丁仅有 173 个被接受，拒绝率达 98.7%。相比之下，pressure-field 的并行探索意味着，即使一个智能体的补丁被拒绝，其他智能体仍可在不同区域取得进展。这种非阻塞架构可防止任何单个困难区域导致整个系统停滞。
The architectural lesson. Hierarchical’s design embodies a reasonable intuition: focus intelligent effort on the worst problems. But this creates a trap when combined with strict validation: the hardest problems resist improvement, causing repeated rejection, which blocks progress everywhere. Pressure-field avoids this trap through distributed exploration—progress happens where it can, not where a central planner dictates it must.
架构层面的启示。Hierarchical 的设计体现了一种合理的直觉：将智能处理资源集中于最棘手的问题。但当这种设计与严格验证结合时，就会形成一个陷阱：最难的问题难以改进，导致补丁反复被拒绝，进而阻碍所有区域取得进展。Pressure-field 通过分布式探索避开了这一陷阱——哪里能够取得进展，就在哪里推进，而不是由中央规划者指定必须在哪里取得进展。
7.3 Limitations7.3 局限性
Our experiments reveal several important limitations:
我们的实验揭示了若干重要局限性：
Absolute solve rates are modest on hard problems. Even pressure-field achieves only 15.6% on hard problems. Meeting room scheduling with tight constraints (5 rooms, 60 meetings, 30% pre-scheduled) remains challenging for small models (0.5b–3b parameters); larger models may achieve higher absolute solve rates.
在困难问题上，绝对求解率仍然不高。即使是 pressure-field，在困难问题上的求解率也仅为 15.6%。约束严格的会议室调度（5 间会议室、60 场会议、30% 已预先排定）对于小模型（0.5b–3b 参数）而言仍具有挑战性；更大的模型可能实现更高的绝对求解率。
Domain specificity. Results on meeting room scheduling may not generalize to domains lacking measurable pressure gradients or locality properties. Tasks requiring global planning or long-horizon reasoning may favor hierarchical approaches.
领域特异性。会议室调度上的结果可能无法推广到缺乏可测量压力梯度或局部性特征的领域。需要全局规划或长时程推理的任务可能更适合采用层次化方法。
However, meeting scheduling is representative of a broader class of resource allocation problems with soft constraints: cloud compute scheduling (VMs to hosts with affinity/anti-affinity rules), logistics optimization (packages to routes with capacity and time windows), and workforce rostering (employees to shifts with skill and availability constraints). These domains share the structural properties that enable pressure-field coordination: (1) decomposable quality metrics (utilization, constraint violations, balance), (2) locality (fixing one allocation rarely breaks distant allocations), and (3) multiple valid solutions (no single optimum, many acceptable configurations). We selected meeting scheduling as the evaluation domain because it is intuitive, reproducible, and exhibits these properties clearly. The coordination mechanism itself—pressure gradients, temporal decay, parallel validation—is domain-agnostic; only the pressure function requires domain-specific design.
然而，会议调度代表了一类更广泛的带软约束的资源分配问题：云计算调度（在亲和性/反亲和性规则下将 VM 分配到主机）、物流优化（在容量和时间窗约束下将包裹分配到路线），以及人员排班（在技能和可用性约束下将员工分配到班次）。这些领域具有使压力场协调得以实现的共同结构特性：(1) 可分解的质量指标（利用率、约束违反情况、均衡性），(2) 局部性（修正一处分配很少会破坏远处分配），以及 (3) 多个有效解（不存在唯一最优解，而是有许多可接受的配置）。我们选择会议调度作为评估领域，是因为它直观、可复现，并且清晰地体现了这些特性。协调机制本身——压力梯度、时间衰减、并行验证——与领域无关；只有压力函数需要针对具体领域进行设计。
Additional practical limitations:
其他实际局限：
Requires well-designed pressure functions (not learned from data)
需要精心设计的压力函数（并非从数据中学习得到）
Decay rates $\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma}$ and inhibition period require task-specific tuning
衰减率 $\lambda_{f},\lambda_{\gamma}$ 和抑制期需要针对具体任务进行调优
May not suit tasks requiring long-horizon global planning
可能不适合需要长时程全局规划的任务
Goodhart’s Law: agents may game poorly-designed metrics
古德哈特定律（Goodhart’s Law）：智能体可能钻设计不当的指标的空子
Resource cost of parallel validation: testing $K$ patches requires $O(K\cdot|A|)$ memory where $|A|$ is artifact size
并行验证的资源开销：测试 $K$ 个补丁需要 $O(K\cdot|A|)$ 内存，其中 $|A|$ 为产物大小
7.3.1 Hallucination Filtering and Emergent Trajectories7.3.1 幻觉过滤与涌现轨迹
The validation phase (Phase 2b) serves as a filter for individual Foundation Model hallucinations: patches that increase pressure or violate syntactic constraints are rejected before application. This provides strong guarantees at the individual-action level—no single hallucination can directly harm artifact quality.
验证阶段（Phase 2b）用于过滤单个基础模型（Foundation Model）产生的幻觉：会增加压力或违反语法约束的补丁会在应用前被拒绝。这在单次行动层面提供了强有力的保证——任何单次幻觉都无法直接损害产物质量。
However, system-level behavior emerges from sequences of validated patches, and trajectory-level risks remain. Consider a hypothetical scenario: an agent proposes a patch that reduces pressure in region $R_{1}$ by making a change that creates a subtle dependency on region $R_{2}$ . The patch is validated (pressure decreased), but subsequent patches to $R_{2}$ now have unpredictable effects on $R_{1}$ . The individual patches are all pressure-reducing, but the emergent trajectory creates fragile coupling that was invisible to local validation.
然而，系统层面的行为由一系列通过验证的补丁涌现而成，轨迹层面的风险仍然存在。考虑一个假设场景：某个智能体提出一个补丁，通过一项改动降低区域 $R_{1}$ 的压力，但该改动引入了对区域 $R_{2}$ 的隐蔽依赖。该补丁通过了验证（压力下降），但此后对 $R_{2}$ 的补丁会对 $R_{1}$ 产生不可预测的影响。每个补丁都降低了压力，但涌现出的轨迹形成了局部验证无法察觉的脆弱耦合。
More generally, validation filters based on pressure reduction cannot detect:
更一般地说，基于压力降低的验证过滤机制无法检测到：
Coherence drift: Individual improvements that collectively shift the artifact toward an inconsistent state
一致性漂移：单项改进累积起来，使产物逐渐偏向不一致状态
Emergent gaming: Patches that exploit pressure function weaknesses in ways only apparent over multiple steps
涌现式钻空子：补丁利用压力函数的弱点，其方式只有经过多个步骤才会显现
Dependency accumulation: Gradual introduction of hidden couplings that reduce future improvability
依赖累积：逐步引入隐藏耦合，降低未来的可改进性
Mitigating trajectory-level risks requires mechanisms beyond local validation: periodic global coherence checks, trajectory logging for post-hoc analysis, and pressure functions that penalize not just local quality but also coupling metrics. These remain areas for future work.
缓解轨迹层面的风险需要局部验证之外的机制：定期进行全局一致性检查、记录轨迹以供事后分析，以及在压力函数中不仅对局部质量问题施加惩罚，还对耦合指标施加惩罚。这些仍有待未来研究。
7.3.2 Decay Miscalibration Failure Modes7.3.2 衰减校准不当的失效模式
Temporal decay is critical for escaping local optima (Theorem 5.3), but miscalibrated decay rates introduce distinct failure modes:
时间衰减对于摆脱局部最优至关重要（定理 5.3），但校准不当的衰减率会引入不同的失效模式：
Too-fast decay prevents stability. When fitness decays faster than agents can reinforce successful regions, the system cannot maintain any stable configuration. Solved regions immediately become high-pressure again, triggering unnecessary rework. In the limit, excessively fast decay produces perpetual oscillation: agents patch, decay erases, agents re-patch, indefinitely.
衰减过快会妨碍系统保持稳定。当适应度的衰减速度超过智能体强化成功区域的速度时，系统就无法维持任何稳定配置。已解决的区域会立即重新变为高压力区域，从而触发不必要的返工。在极限情况下，过快的衰减会导致持续振荡：智能体打补丁，衰减抹去其效果，智能体再次打补丁，如此无限循环。
Too-slow decay traps in local minima. When fitness decays slower than the exploration timescale, agents cannot escape suboptimal basins. Early patches establish fitness peaks that persist indefinitely, preventing reconsideration even when better solutions exist. This is the failure mode our ablation study suggests: without decay, we observed a 10 percentage point reduction in solve rate, consistent with agents remaining trapped in initial basins (though the effect did not reach statistical significance at $n=30$ ).
衰减过慢会使系统陷入局部极小值。当适应度的衰减时间尺度长于探索时间尺度时，智能体就无法逃离次优吸引域。早期补丁形成的适应度峰值会无限期持续存在，即使存在更好的解，也会阻碍重新评估。我们的消融研究提示了这种失效模式：在没有衰减的情况下，我们观察到求解率下降了 10 个百分点，这与智能体持续困于初始吸引域的情况一致（尽管在 $n=30$ 时，该效应未达到统计显著性）。
The optimal decay rate depends on problem characteristics: harder problems with deeper local minima require faster decay to enable escape, while problems with fragile solutions require slower decay to maintain stability. Our experiments use fixed decay rates ( $\lambda_{f}=0.1$ , fitness half-life 5 seconds), which may be suboptimal for some problem instances. Adaptive decay—adjusting rates based on pressure velocity—is a promising direction for future work.
最优衰减率取决于问题特征：局部极小值更深的困难问题需要更快的衰减以便逃离，而解较为脆弱的问题则需要更慢的衰减以维持稳定。我们的实验采用固定衰减率（$\lambda_{f}=0.1$，适应度半衰期为 5 秒），对于某些问题实例而言，这可能并非最优。自适应衰减——根据压力变化速率调整衰减率——是未来工作中一个很有前景的方向。

我们的结果提出了以下指导建议：

7.5 Band and Model Escalation as Adaptive Capability7.5 作为自适应能力的采样档位升级与模型升级
All experiments use a two-level escalation mechanism. Band escalation cycles through sampling strategies (Exploitation $\to$ Balanced $\to$ Exploration, 7 ticks each) before model escalation progresses through model sizes (0.5b $\to$ 1.5b $\to$ 3b parameters). Model escalation triggers when regions remain high-pressure for 21 consecutive ticks.
所有实验均采用两级升级机制。在模型升级依次采用更大规模的模型（0.5b $\to$ 1.5b $\to$ 3b 参数）之前，采样档位升级会依次轮换采样策略（利用 $\to$ 均衡 $\to$ 探索，每种策略持续 7 个时步）。当区域连续 21 个时步保持高压力时，便会触发模型升级。
This mechanism proves beneficial for pressure-field: on hard problems, pressure-field achieves 15.6% with escalation enabled. The escalation mechanism works because larger models have broader solution coverage and different sampling bands explore different regions of solution space.
事实证明，这一机制有益于压力场方法：在困难问题上，启用升级机制后，压力场方法的求解成功率达到 15.6%。升级机制之所以有效，是因为更大的模型能够覆盖更广泛的解，而不同的采样档位会探索解空间中的不同区域。

7.7 FM-MAS Reciprocity: Bidirectional Problem Solving7.7 FM-MAS 互惠关系：双向问题求解
The intersection of Foundation Models and Multi-Agent Systems is not merely additive—each paradigm solves fundamental problems that the other cannot address alone. This reciprocity suggests that pressure-field coordination represents a genuine synthesis rather than a simple combination.
基础模型（Foundation Models）与多智能体系统（Multi-Agent Systems）的交汇并非仅仅是两者相加——每种范式都能解决另一种范式单独无法应对的根本问题。这种互惠关系表明，压力场协调代表了一种真正的综合，而非简单的组合。
7.7.1 Foundation Models Solve a MAS Coordination Problem7.7.1 基础模型解决 MAS 协调问题
Traditional Multi-Agent System coordination requires explicit action space enumeration. Generalized Partial Global Planning coordinates tasks through declared capabilities and commitments; SharedPlans reasons about action sequences and their preconditions; organizational models assign roles with specified behaviors. All these approaches assume that designers can enumerate what agents can do and under what conditions.
传统的多智能体系统协调需要显式枚举动作空间。广义部分全局规划（Generalized Partial Global Planning）通过声明的能力与承诺来协调任务；SharedPlans 对动作序列及其前置条件进行推理；组织模型则分配具有指定行为的角色。所有这些方法都假设，设计者能够枚举智能体可以执行哪些操作，以及在何种条件下执行这些操作。
For open-ended artifact refinement—improving code quality, refining documents, optimizing configurations—this enumeration is intractable. The space of possible improvements is unbounded; no finite action language captures all valid patches. Traditional Multi-Agent System approaches require either (a) restricting the problem to a tractable subset with enumerable actions, or (b) developing domain-specific action representations for each artifact type.
对于开放式产物改进——提高代码质量、完善文档、优化配置——这种枚举在计算上是不可行的。可能的改进空间是无界的；任何有限的动作语言都无法涵盖所有有效补丁。传统的多智能体系统（Multi-Agent System）方法要求要么 (a) 将问题限制在动作可枚举且易于处理的子集内，要么 (b) 为每种产物类型开发领域专用的动作表示。
Foundation Models eliminate this enumeration requirement. Their broad pretraining provides implicit coverage of improvement strategies across diverse domains without explicit action specification. An Large Language Model actor can propose patches for code, natural language, structured data, or configuration files using the same interface: observe local context, receive pressure feedback, generate improvement proposals. This “universal actor” capability enables stigmergic coordination on problem classes where traditional Multi-Agent System approaches would require extensive domain engineering.
基础模型（Foundation Models）消除了这种枚举要求。它们广泛的预训练隐式涵盖了不同领域的改进策略，无需显式指定动作。大语言模型执行者可以使用同一套接口，为代码、自然语言、结构化数据或配置文件提出补丁：观察局部上下文、接收压力反馈、生成改进提案。这种“通用执行者”能力使得基于迹的协调（stigmergic coordination）能够应用于某些问题类别，而传统的多智能体系统方法在这些问题上需要大量的领域工程工作。
7.7.2 Multi-Agent Systems Solve a FM Problem7.7.2 多智能体系统解决了基础模型的一个问题
Conversely, Foundation Models face a fundamental problem that Multi-Agent System coordination solves: how to combine multiple outputs coherently. A single Foundation Model produces one response per query; scaling to complex artifacts requires orchestrating multiple generations. Current approaches use ad-hoc combination strategies: voting, ranking, chain-of-thought aggregation, or human-in-the-loop selection.
反过来，基础模型面临一个可由多智能体系统协调机制解决的根本问题：如何连贯一致地整合多个输出。单个基础模型对每次查询生成一个响应；要扩展到复杂产物，就需要编排多次生成。当前方法使用临时性的整合策略：投票、排序、思维链聚合，或人在回路中的选择。
Pressure-field coordination provides a principled framework for combining Foundation Model outputs. Rather than voting on which response is “best” or ranking outputs by heuristic scores, the pressure gradient defines an objective criterion: accept patches that reduce pressure, reject those that do not. Multiple Foundation Model outputs compete not through popularity or arbitrary ranking, but through their effect on a well-defined quality function.
压力场协调为整合基础模型输出提供了一个具有系统性原理依据的框架。压力梯度定义了一项客观标准：接受能够降低压力的补丁，拒绝无法降低压力的补丁，而不是通过投票决定哪个响应“最好”，或根据启发式评分对输出进行排序。多个基础模型输出依据其对一个明确定义的质量函数的影响展开竞争，而非依据受欢迎程度或任意排序。
This framing clarifies why pressure-field coordination outperforms conversation baselines. AutoGen-style multi-agent dialogue is an ad-hoc combination strategy—agents exchange messages and reach conclusions through emergent consensus. Pressure-field coordination replaces emergent consensus with objective gradients: no negotiation is needed when the artifact state adjudicates quality.
这一视角阐明了压力场协调为何优于基于对话的基线方法。AutoGen 风格的多智能体对话是一种临时性的整合策略——智能体交换消息，并通过涌现出的共识得出结论。压力场协调用客观梯度取代涌现出的共识：当产物状态能够裁定质量时，就无需协商。
7.7.3 Implications for FM-MAS Integration7.7.3 对 FM-MAS 集成的启示
This reciprocity suggests design principles for future FM-MAS systems:
这种互补关系为未来的 FM-MAS 系统提出了以下设计原则：
Leverage FM coverage, constrain via MAS gradients. Use Foundation Models for their broad solution coverage, but use Multi-Agent System coordination mechanisms to filter and combine outputs objectively.
利用 FM 的覆盖广度，通过 MAS 梯度施加约束。利用基础模型对解决方案的广泛覆盖，同时使用多智能体系统的协调机制，客观地筛选和整合输出。
Prefer stigmergic over explicit coordination. When Foundation Models serve as actors, stigmergic coordination avoids the overhead of explicit protocols that FMs may not reliably follow.
优先采用基于迹的协调，而非显式协调。当基础模型充当执行者时，基于迹的协调可以避免显式协议带来的开销，而 FM 未必能够可靠地遵循这些协议。
Design pressure functions as FM-MAS interfaces. The pressure function is the critical interface: it must capture human intent precisely enough that Foundation Model outputs reducing pressure are genuinely improvements.
将压力函数设计为 FM-MAS 的接口。压力函数是关键接口：它必须足够精确地捕捉人类意图，使得基础模型生成的、能够降低压力的输出确实构成改进。
Our experimental results—pressure-field achieving $4\times$ higher solve rates than conversation baselines—provide empirical support for these principles. The coordination overhead of explicit dialogue exceeds its organizational benefit when objective gradients are available.
我们的实验结果——压力场的求解成功率达到基于对话的基线方法的 $4\times$——为这些原则提供了实证支持。当存在客观梯度时，显式对话的协调开销超过了其组织收益。
7.8 Societal Implications7.8 社会影响
Pressure-field coordination raises societal concerns that extend beyond technical performance. We identify three critical issues—accountability attribution, metric gaming through Goodhart’s Law, and explainability challenges—that require deliberate design choices in deployment.
压力场协调引发了超出技术性能范畴的社会层面关切。我们识别出三个关键问题——责任归属、古德哈特定律（Goodhart’s Law）所揭示的指标操纵，以及可解释性挑战——这些问题要求在部署时作出审慎的设计选择。
7.8.1 Accountability and Attribution7.8.1 问责与归因
When coordination emerges from shared pressure gradients rather than explicit delegation, attributing outcomes to individual agents becomes challenging. In hierarchical systems, task assignment creates clear accountability chains. In pressure-field coordination, multiple agents may contribute to a region through independent pressure-reducing actions, with no record of which agent “owned” the outcome.
当协调源于共享压力梯度而非显式委派时，将结果归因于单个智能体就会变得困难。在层级系统中，任务分配建立了清晰的责任链。在压力场协调中，多个智能体可能通过独立的降压动作对同一区域作出贡献，却没有记录表明哪个智能体对该结果“负责”。
This accountability diffusion has both benefits and risks. The benefit is fault tolerance: agent failures degrade performance gracefully rather than catastrophically. The risk is opacity in failure analysis: identifying which agent proposed a problematic patch—and what pressure signal motivated it—requires detailed logging that the minimal coordination mechanism does not inherently provide.
这种责任分散既有益处，也有风险。其益处是容错性：智能体故障会使性能平缓下降，而不会导致灾难性失效。其风险是故障分析缺乏透明度：要确定哪个智能体提出了有问题的补丁，以及何种压力信号促使其提出该补丁，需要详细的日志记录，而最小化的协调机制本身并不提供这种记录。
For deployment in regulated domains, this suggests an augmentation requirement: pressure-field systems must maintain audit logs recording patch provenance, pressure signals at proposal time, and validation outcomes. The coordination mechanism remains simple—agents coordinate through shared state—but operational deployment adds logging infrastructure preserving accountability.
对于受监管领域的部署，这意味着需要增加一项功能：压力场系统必须维护审计日志，记录补丁来源、提出补丁时的压力信号，以及验证结果。协调机制仍然保持简单——智能体通过共享状态进行协调——但实际部署需要增设日志基础设施，以保留可追责性。
7.8.2 Goodhart’s Law and Metric Gaming7.8.2 古德哈特定律与指标投机
Goodhart’s Law states: “When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure.” Pressure-field coordination is vulnerable to this dynamic because agents are optimized to reduce pressure as defined by designer-specified functions. If those functions imperfectly capture true quality—and they inevitably do—agents will discover and exploit the mismatch.
古德哈特定律指出：“当一项度量成为目标时，它就不再是一项好的度量。”压力场协调容易受到这一动态机制的影响，因为智能体的优化目标是降低由设计者指定的函数所定义的压力。如果这些函数未能完全反映真实质量——而这不可避免——智能体就会发现并利用这种偏差。
Consider code quality pressure functions penalizing complexity metrics. An agent might reduce complexity by splitting functions excessively, harming readability while improving the metric. The mitigation is not abandoning pressure functions but designing them defensively: use multiple orthogonal pressure axes, include adversarial sensors detecting gaming strategies, and audit whether pressure reduction correlates with human quality judgments. Pressure functions should evolve as agents discover exploits.
考虑对复杂度指标施加惩罚的代码质量压力函数。智能体可能通过过度拆分函数来降低复杂度，在改善指标的同时损害可读性。缓解方法不是放弃压力函数，而是对其进行防御性设计：采用多个正交的压力维度，加入用于检测投机策略的对抗性传感器，并审查压力降低是否与人类对质量的判断相关。随着智能体发现可利用的漏洞，压力函数也应随之演进。
Foundation models introduce second-order gaming concerns: Large Language Models trained on internet-scale text may have implicit knowledge of how to game specific benchmarks. This suggests pressure functions for Large Language Model-based systems should favor domain-specific quality signals harder to optimize without genuine improvement.
基础模型引入了二阶投机问题：在互联网规模文本上训练的大语言模型可能隐含地掌握了如何针对特定基准进行投机的知识。这表明，基于大语言模型的系统所采用的压力函数应优先使用领域特定的质量信号，使其更难在没有实质性改进的情况下得到优化。
7.8.3 Explainability Challenges7.8.3 可解释性挑战
In hierarchical systems, explanations follow delegation chains: “Manager X assigned task Y to Worker Z because condition C held.” In pressure-field coordination, the explanation is: “Region R had high pressure, agent A proposed patch $\Delta$ reducing pressure by $\delta$ .” This is mechanistically transparent but causally opaque—it describes what happened without explaining why that particular patch was chosen.
在层级式系统中，解释沿着任务委派链展开：“管理者 X 将任务 Y 分配给工作者 Z，因为条件 C 成立。”在压力场协调中，解释是：“区域 R 的压力较高，智能体 A 提出了补丁 $\Delta$，使压力降低了 $\delta$。”这种解释在机制上是透明的，但在因果上却是不透明的——它描述了发生了什么，却没有解释为何选择了这一特定补丁。
This is the explainability trade-off inherent to emergent coordination: simplicity in mechanism comes at the cost of legibility in rationale. For many domains—code formatting, resource optimization, routine maintenance—the trade-off is acceptable: outcomes are verifiable even if reasoning is opaque. For high-stakes domains requiring human oversight, opacity is unacceptable.
这是涌现式协调固有的可解释性权衡：机制的简单性以决策理由的可理解性为代价。对于许多领域——代码格式化、资源优化、日常维护——这种权衡是可以接受的：即使推理过程不透明，结果仍可验证。对于需要人类监督的高风险领域，不透明性是不可接受的。
The design implication is domain-dependent deployment: pressure-field coordination suits domains where outcome verification is cheap even if reasoning transparency is limited. For domains requiring justification to human stakeholders, hierarchical coordination remains necessary despite overhead costs.
由此得出的设计启示是，应根据领域特点进行部署：压力场协调适用于结果验证成本低的领域，即使其推理透明度有限。对于需要向人类利益相关方说明理由的领域，尽管存在额外开销，层级式协调仍然是必要的。
7.8.4 Design Implications7.8.4 设计启示
These concerns suggest three requirements for responsible deployment: comprehensive audit logging preserving patch provenance and pressure signals, defensive pressure function design with multiple orthogonal axes, and domain-appropriate verification matching coordination opacity with outcome verifiability. The coordination mechanism remains simple—but responsible deployment requires surrounding infrastructure addressing accountability, gaming, and explainability.
这些问题表明，负责任的部署需要满足三项要求：通过全面的审计日志保留补丁来源和压力信号，采用具有多个正交维度的防御性压力函数设计，以及实施适合领域特点的验证，使协调过程的不透明程度与结果的可验证性相匹配。协调机制仍然简单——但负责任的部署需要配套基础设施来解决问责、投机和可解释性问题。
8 Conclusion8 结论
We presented pressure-field coordination, a decentralized approach to Multi-Agent Systems that achieves coordination through shared state and local pressure gradients rather than explicit orchestration.
我们提出了压力场协调，这是一种用于多智能体系统的去中心化方法，通过共享状态和局部压力梯度实现协调，而非依靠显式编排。
Our theoretical analysis establishes convergence guarantees under pressure alignment conditions, with coordination overhead independent of agent count. Empirically, on meeting room scheduling across 1350 total trials (270 per strategy), we find:
我们的理论分析确立了压力对齐条件下的收敛保证，且协调开销与智能体数量无关。在实证研究中，我们针对会议室调度共进行了 1350 次试验（每种策略 270 次），发现：
Implicit coordination outperforms explicit coordination. Pressure-field achieves 48.5% aggregate solve rate—nearly half of all problems solved through local pressure-following alone, with no coordinator, no message passing, and no explicit task delegation. The gap versus hierarchical control ( $30\times$ ) and conversation-based coordination ( $4\times$ ) is both large and highly significant (all $p<0.001$ ).
隐式协调优于显式协调。压力场方法的总体求解成功率达到 48.5%——近一半的问题仅通过遵循局部压力得到解决，无需协调器、消息传递或显式任务委派。相较于层级式控制（$30\times$）和基于对话的协调（$4\times$），其差距既大又具有高度统计显著性（均为 $p<0.001$）。
Pressure-field is the only strategy that scales to harder problems. On medium and hard problems, pressure-field achieves 43.3% and 15.6% solve rates respectively, while all baselines achieve 0%.
压力场方法是唯一能够扩展到更难问题的策略。在中等和高难度问题上，压力场方法的求解成功率分别达到 43.3% 和 15.6%，而所有基线方法均为 0%。
Temporal decay shows beneficial effects. Ablation studies observe a 10 percentage point improvement with decay enabled, consistent with theoretical predictions about escaping local minima, though larger samples would be needed to establish statistical significance.
时间衰减表现出有益效果。消融研究观察到，启用衰减后性能提高了 10 个百分点，这与关于逃离局部极小值的理论预测一致，但仍需要更大的样本量来确立统计显著性。
This work demonstrates that implicit coordination can outperform explicit coordination for constraint satisfaction tasks. Pressure-field achieves this with simpler architecture: no coordinator agent, no explicit message passing, just shared state and local pressure gradients.
本研究表明，对于约束满足任务，隐式协调可以优于显式协调。压力场方法以更简单的架构实现了这一点：无需协调器智能体，无需显式消息传递，仅依靠共享状态和局部压力梯度。
Foundation Models’ zero-shot capabilities eliminate the need for domain-specific action representations; pressure-field coordination eliminates the need for complex multi-agent protocols; together they enable simple Multi-Agent Systems.
基础模型的零样本能力消除了对领域特定动作表示的需求；压力场协调消除了对复杂多智能体协议的需求；两者共同使简单的多智能体系统成为可能。
These results suggest that for domains with measurable quality signals and locally-decomposable structure, implicit coordination through shared state offers not just a simpler but a more effective alternative to explicit hierarchical control.
这些结果表明，对于具有可度量质量信号和可局部分解结构的领域，通过共享状态进行隐式协调，为显式层级式控制提供了一种不仅更简单、而且更有效的替代方案。

Why “one patch per tick” for hierarchical? This asymmetry is inherent to the coordination paradigm, not an implementation choice. Hierarchical control requires the manager to: (1) observe global state, (2) select a target region, (3) delegate to a worker, (4) receive the proposed patch, and (5) validate before applying. Delegating to $K$ workers simultaneously would require additional coordination protocols—work distribution to avoid conflicts, result aggregation, tie-breaking when multiple patches target overlapping state—that would transform hierarchical control into a fundamentally different architecture. The sequential bottleneck is the cost of centralized decision-making. Pressure-field avoids this overhead through stigmergic coordination: agents observe shared state independently and propose patches without central delegation, enabling natural parallelism.
为什么层级式协调采用“每个时间步一个补丁”？这种不对称性是协调范式所固有的，而非实现上的选择。层级式控制要求管理者：(1) 观察全局状态，(2) 选择目标区域，(3) 将任务委派给工作者，(4) 接收所提出的补丁，以及 (5) 在应用前进行验证。同时向 $K$ 个工作者委派任务需要额外的协调协议——通过任务分配避免冲突、汇总结果，以及在多个补丁针对重叠状态时进行冲突裁决——这会将层级式控制转变为一种根本不同的架构。串行瓶颈是集中式决策的代价。压力场（pressure-field）通过迹协同（stigmergic coordination）避免了这种开销：各智能体独立观察共享状态，无需中央委派即可提出补丁，从而自然地实现并行。
Conversation baseline implementation The conversation baseline implements AutoGen-style multi-agent dialogue with three roles: a Coordinator that selects which region to address, a Proposer that suggests schedule changes, and a Validator that critiques proposals. Each tick proceeds as follows: (1) the Coordinator identifies a high-pressure region (1 Large Language Model call), (2) the Proposer and Validator engage in up to 5 dialogue turns (2 calls per turn), (3) if the Validator approves, the patch is applied. This yields 4–12 Large Language Model calls per tick for a single region, compared to pressure-field’s $K$ parallel calls across $K$ regions. The conversation terminates when the Validator approves or the turn limit is reached. We use fixed sampling parameters (T=0.3, top-p=0.9) for structured dialogue, lower than pressure-field’s exploration bands. Note that this represents one instantiation of conversation-based coordination inspired by AutoGen’s design principles; actual AutoGen deployments may use different role configurations, turn protocols, or termination conditions.
对话基线的实现 对话基线实现了 AutoGen 风格的多智能体对话，包含三种角色：选择待处理区域的协调者（Coordinator）、提出日程变更建议的提议者（Proposer），以及评议提案的验证者（Validator）。每个时间步按以下流程进行：(1) 协调者识别一个高压力区域（1 次大语言模型调用），(2) 提议者与验证者进行最多 5 轮对话（每轮 2 次调用），(3) 如果验证者批准，则应用该补丁。这意味着，每个时间步针对单个区域需要 4–12 次大语言模型调用，而压力场则针对 $K$ 个区域进行 $K$ 次并行调用。当验证者批准或达到对话轮数上限时，对话终止。我们对结构化对话采用固定的采样参数（T=0.3, top-p=0.9），其取值低于压力场的探索档位。请注意，这只是受 AutoGen 设计原则启发的一种基于对话的协调实现；实际的 AutoGen 部署可能采用不同的角色配置、轮次协议或终止条件。

10 Pressure Alignment Verification10 压力对齐验证
This appendix verifies that the meeting scheduling domain satisfies the alignment condition (Definition 2) required for convergence (Theorem 1).
本附录验证会议排程领域满足收敛性（定理 1）所要求的对齐条件（定义 2）。
10.1 Per-Region Pressure Components10.1 各区域的压力分量
The per-region pressure function includes three components:
各区域的压力函数包含三个分量：
gap_ratio: Fraction of empty slots in this time block. Strictly local—depends only on meetings scheduled within this region.
gap_ratio：该时间块中空闲时隙所占的比例。严格局部——仅取决于安排在该区域内的会议。
overlap_count: Number of attendee double-bookings within this time block. Strictly local—the overlap sensor counts attendees with multiple meetings in this block only, not across blocks.
overlap_count：该时间块内参会者被重复安排参加会议的次数。严格局部——重叠传感器仅统计在该时间块内被安排参加多场会议的参会者，不进行跨时间块统计。
utilization_variance: Variance in room utilization within this time block. Strictly local—measures balance across rooms for meetings in this region.
utilization_variance：该时间块内会议室利用率的方差。严格局部——衡量该区域内会议在各会议室之间的分配均衡程度。
The unscheduled_count component is added to total pressure only, not to per-region pressure. This design choice ensures separability.
unscheduled_count 分量仅计入总压力，不计入各区域的压力。这一设计选择确保了可分离性。
10.2 Coupling Analysis10.2 耦合分析
For the alignment condition, we need $|P_{j}(s^{\prime})-P_{j}(s)|\leq\epsilon$ for all $j\neq i$ when modifying region $i$ .
为满足对齐条件，在修改区域 $i$ 时，我们需要对所有 $j\neq i$ 都有 $|P_{j}(s^{\prime})-P_{j}(s)|\leq\epsilon$。
Result: $\epsilon=0$ (per-region pressure is separable).
结果：$\epsilon=0$（各区域的压力具有可分离性）。
Rationale: All three per-region components depend only on the region’s own content:
理由：各区域的三个分量均仅取决于该区域自身的内容：
Modifying region $i$ ’s schedule changes which meetings occupy region $i$ ’s slots.
修改区域 $i$ 的排程会改变占用区域 $i$ 时隙的会议。
This affects region $i$ ’s gap_ratio, overlap_count, and utilization_variance.
这会影响区域 $i$ 的 gap_ratio、overlap_count 和 utilization_variance。
It has zero effect on any other region $j$ ’s pressure, since $j$ ’s components depend only on meetings within $j$ ’s time slots.
这对任何其他区域 $j$ 的压力均无影响，因为 $j$ 的各分量仅取决于 $j$ 的时隙内的会议。
Note on attendee constraints: While the same attendee may have meetings in multiple time blocks, our overlap sensor counts overlaps within each block independently. Moving a meeting from region $i$ to region $j$ may create or resolve overlaps in region $j$ , but this is a local effect on $j$ ’s pressure, not a coupling effect where modifying region $i$ affects region $j$ through some indirect mechanism.
关于参会者约束的说明：尽管同一参会者可能在多个时间块中都有会议，但我们的重叠传感器会独立统计每个时间块内的重叠。将一场会议从区域 $i$ 移至区域 $j$，可能会在区域 $j$ 中产生或消除重叠，但这是对 $j$ 的压力产生的局部影响，而非修改区域 $i$ 通过某种间接机制影响区域 $j$ 的耦合效应。

11.1 Proof of Theorem 5.1 (Convergence)11.1 定理 5.1（收敛性）的证明
Proof.证明。
We show that the system reaches a stable basin within the stated bound.
我们证明，系统会在所述界限内达到稳定吸引域。
Setup. Let $P^{t}=P(s^{t})$ denote global pressure at tick $t$ . Under $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling, when a patch reduces local pressure $P_{i}$ by $\delta_{i}$ , global pressure changes by:
设定。令 $P^{t}=P(s^{t})$ 表示时刻 $t$ 的全局压力。在 $\epsilon$ 有界耦合下，当一个补丁使局部压力 $P_{i}$ 降低 $\delta_{i}$ 时，全局压力的变化为：
$$P^{t+1}-P^{t}=-\delta_{i}+\sum_{j\neq i}(P_{j}(s^{t+1})-P_{j}(s^{t}))$$
By bounded coupling, $|P_{j}(s^{t+1})-P_{j}(s^{t})|\leq\epsilon$ for each $j\neq i$ . With $n$ regions:
由有界耦合条件，对每个 $j\neq i$，均有 $|P_{j}(s^{t+1})-P_{j}(s^{t})|\leq\epsilon$。对于 $n$ 个区域：
$$P^{t+1}-P^{t}\leq-\delta_{i}+(n-1)\epsilon$$
Progress guarantee. If $\delta_{i}\geq\delta_{\min}$ and $\delta_{\min}>(n-1)\epsilon$ , then:
进展保证。若 $\delta_{i}\geq\delta_{\min}$ 且 $\delta_{\min}>(n-1)\epsilon$，则：
$$P^{t+1}-P^{t}\leq-\delta_{\min}+(n-1)\epsilon<0$$
Each tick with an applied patch reduces global pressure by at least $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon>0$ .
每个应用了补丁的时间步都会使全局压力至少降低 $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon>0$。
Termination. Since $P(s)\geq 0$ for all states and pressure decreases by a fixed minimum $\Delta=\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$ per tick (when patches are applied), the system must reach a state where no region exceeds $\tau_{\text{act}}$ within:
终止性。由于对所有状态均有 $P(s)\geq 0$，且在应用补丁时，每个时间步的压力降幅至少为固定值 $\Delta=\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$，因此系统必定会达到没有任何区域的压力超过 $\tau_{\text{act}}$ 的状态，所需时间步数不超过：
$$T\leq\frac{P_{0}}{\Delta}=\frac{P_{0}}{\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon}$$
ticks. At this point, no region activates, and the system has reached a stable basin.
个时间步。此时，没有任何区域被激活，系统已达到稳定吸引域。
Stability maintenance. The decay constraint $\Delta_{f}>1-e^{-\lambda_{f}\cdot\tau_{\text{inh}}}$ ensures that fitness reinforcement from the final patches exceeds decay during the inhibition period. This prevents immediate re-activation after reaching stability. ∎
稳定性的维持。衰减约束 $\Delta_{f}>1-e^{-\lambda_{f}\cdot\tau_{\text{inh}}}$ 确保最后几个补丁带来的适应度强化量超过抑制期内的衰减量。这可防止系统在达到稳定后立即重新激活。∎
11.2 Proof of Theorem 5.3 (Basin Separation)11.2 定理 5.3（吸引域分离）的证明
Proof.证明。
We show that distinct stable basins are separated by pressure barriers of height at least $\tau_{\text{act}}$ .
我们证明，不同的稳定吸引域之间由高度至少为 $\tau_{\text{act}}$ 的压力势垒隔开。
Basin definition. A stable basin $B$ is a connected region of state space where all states satisfy $P_{i}(s)<\tau_{\text{act}}$ for all regions $i$ . Within a basin, no agent takes action (pressure below activation threshold).
吸引域的定义。稳定吸引域 $B$ 是状态空间中的一个连通区域，其中所有状态对于所有区域 $i$ 均满足 $P_{i}(s)<\tau_{\text{act}}$。在吸引域内，没有智能体采取行动（压力低于激活阈值）。
Transition requirement. To move from basin $B_{1}$ to a distinct basin $B_{2}$ , the system must pass through states not in either basin. At the boundary of $B_{1}$ , at least one region $i$ must have $P_{i}(s)\geq\tau_{\text{act}}$ (otherwise, by continuity under separable pressure, the state would still be in $B_{1}$ ).
转移要求。要从吸引域 $B_{1}$ 转移到另一个不同的吸引域 $B_{2}$，系统必须经过不属于这两个吸引域中任何一个的状态。在 $B_{1}$ 的边界处，至少有一个区域 $i$ 必须满足 $P_{i}(s)\geq\tau_{\text{act}}$（否则，根据可分离压力下的连续性，该状态仍将属于 $B_{1}$）。
Barrier height. The minimum pressure exceedance required to exit a basin is $\tau_{\text{act}}$ . This defines the barrier height separating basins.
势垒高度。退出吸引域所需达到的最低压力越限值为 $\tau_{\text{act}}$。这定义了分隔吸引域的势垒高度。
Implication for decay. Without decay, once the system enters a basin, it remains there indefinitely—fitness remains high, preventing re-activation. Decay erodes fitness over time, eventually allowing pressure to exceed $\tau_{\text{act}}$ and enabling transition to potentially lower-pressure basins. ∎
对衰减的含义。若没有衰减，系统一旦进入某个吸引域，就会无限期地停留其中——适应度始终保持较高水平，从而阻止重新激活。衰减会使适应度随时间逐渐降低，最终使压力能够超过 $\tau_{\text{act}}$，从而允许系统转移到压力可能更低的吸引域。∎
11.3 Proof of Theorem 5.4 (Linear Scaling)11.3 定理 5.4 的证明（线性扩展）
Proof.证明。
We analyze the per-tick computational complexity.
我们分析每个时钟步的计算复杂度。
Signal computation. For each of $m$ regions, computing $d$ -dimensional signals requires $O(d)$ operations (reading local content, computing quality metrics). Total: $O(m\cdot d)$ .
信号计算。对于 $m$ 个区域中的每一个，计算 $d$ 维信号需要 $O(d)$ 次操作（读取局部内容、计算质量指标）。总计：$O(m\cdot d)$。
Pressure computation. For each region, computing pressure from $k$ signal axes requires $O(k)$ operations (weighted sum). Total: $O(m\cdot k)$ .
压力计算。对于每个区域，根据 $k$ 个信号轴计算压力需要 $O(k)$ 次操作（加权求和）。总计：$O(m\cdot k)$。
Patch proposal. Each of $a$ actors proposes patches for activated regions. In the worst case (all regions activated), this is $O(m\cdot a)$ proposals. Each proposal involves an Large Language Model call, which dominates wall-clock time but is constant per call.
补丁提议。$a$ 个执行者中的每一个都为已激活的区域提出补丁。在最坏情况下（所有区域均被激活），提议数量为 $O(m\cdot a)$。每项提议都涉及一次大语言模型调用，该调用占据了实际运行时间的主要部分，但每次调用的耗时为常数。
Selection. Sorting $O(m\cdot a)$ candidate patches by quality requires $O(m\cdot a\cdot\log(m\cdot a))$ comparisons. Greedy selection of non-conflicting patches is $O(m\cdot a)$ with appropriate data structures.
选择。按质量对 $O(m\cdot a)$ 个候选补丁进行排序需要 $O(m\cdot a\cdot\log(m\cdot a))$ 次比较。使用适当的数据结构，贪心选择互不冲突的补丁的复杂度为 $O(m\cdot a)$。
Coordination overhead. Critically, agents share no messages. Each agent reads shared artifact state (read-only) and writes proposed patches to a central queue. No inter-agent communication occurs. The fork pool for parallel validation has fixed size $K$ , contributing $O(K)$ overhead independent of agent count.
协调开销。关键在于，智能体之间不交换任何消息。每个智能体以只读方式读取共享产物的状态，并将提出的补丁写入中央队列。智能体之间不发生通信。用于并行验证的分叉池大小固定为 $K$，产生的开销为 $O(K)$，与智能体数量无关。
Total. Summing components: $O(m\cdot d+m\cdot k+m\cdot a+m\cdot a\cdot\log(ma)+K)=O(m\cdot(d+k+a\cdot\log(ma)))$ , independent of agent count $n$ beyond its contribution to $a$ . ∎
总计。将各部分相加：$O(m\cdot d+m\cdot k+m\cdot a+m\cdot a\cdot\log(ma)+K)=O(m\cdot(d+k+a\cdot\log(ma)))$，除智能体数量 $n$ 对 $a$ 的影响外，该复杂度与 $n$ 无关。∎
11.4 Proof of Theorem 5.5 (Parallel Convergence)11.4 定理 5.5 的证明（并行收敛）
Proof.证明。
We extend Theorem 5.1 to the parallel setting.
我们将定理 5.1 扩展到并行情形。
Setup. Suppose $K$ patches are validated in parallel per tick, targeting disjoint regions $i_{1},\ldots,i_{K}$ .
设定。假设每个时钟步并行验证 $K$ 个补丁，它们分别针对互不相交的区域 $i_{1},\ldots,i_{K}$。
Combined pressure reduction. Under $\epsilon$ -bounded coupling, each patch $k$ targeting region $i_{k}$ reduces local pressure by at least $\delta_{\min}$ . The global pressure change from patch $k$ is:
总压力降幅。在耦合以 $\epsilon$ 为界的条件下，针对区域 $i_{k}$ 的每个补丁 $k$ 都使局部压力至少降低 $\delta_{\min}$。补丁 $k$ 引起的全局压力变化为：
$$\Delta P_{k}\leq-\delta_{\min}+(n-1)\epsilon$$
When patches target disjoint regions, their effects on global pressure are additive (no double-counting of coupling effects within the modified regions). The combined reduction is:
当补丁针对互不相交的区域时，它们对全局压力的影响具有可加性（不会重复计算修改区域内的耦合效应）。总降幅为：
$$P^{t+1}-P^{t}\leq\sum_{k=1}^{K}\Delta P_{k}\leq K\cdot(-\delta_{\min}+(n-1)\epsilon)$$
Convergence bound. With combined reduction $K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)$ per tick:
收敛界。当每个时钟步的总降幅为 $K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)$ 时：
$$T\leq\frac{P_{0}}{K\cdot(\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon)}$$
Conflict handling. When multiple patches target the same region, only one is selected (highest quality). This reduces effective parallelism but maintains correctness—the selected patch still provides at least $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$ reduction. ∎
冲突处理。当多个补丁针对同一区域时，仅选择其中一个（质量最高的补丁）。这会降低有效并行度，但仍能保持正确性——所选补丁仍可使压力至少降低 $\delta_{\min}-(n-1)\epsilon$。∎
