摘要Abstract
基于大语言模型（LLM）的智能体的最新进展表明，集体智能可以显著超越单个智能体的能力，这主要归功于精心设计的智能体间通信拓扑。 Recent advancements in large language model (LLM)-based agents have demonstrated that collective intelligence can significantly surpass the capabilities of individual agents, primarily due to well-crafted inter-agent communication topologies. 尽管已有多种性能出色的设计，实践者在为其具体任务选择最有效的流程时，仍常常感到困惑：哪种拓扑最适合我的任务，既能避免不必要的通信 token 开销，又能确保高质量的解决方案？ Despite the diverse and high-performing designs available, practitioners often face confusion when selecting the most effective pipeline for their specific task: Which topology is the best choice for my task, avoiding unnecessary communication token overhead while ensuring high-quality solution? 为应对这一困境，我们提出了 G-Designer，这是一种用于多智能体部署的自适应、高效且鲁棒的解决方案，能够动态设计感知任务的定制化通信拓扑。 In response to this dilemma, we introduce G-Designer, an adaptive, efficient, and robust solution for multi-agent deployment, which dynamically designs task-aware, customized communication topologies. 具体而言，G-Designer 将多智能体系统建模为多智能体网络，利用变分图自编码器对节点（智能体）和一个任务专属虚拟节点进行编码，并解码生成能够适应任务且性能出色的通信拓扑。 Specifically, G-Designer models the multi-agent system as a multi-agent network, leveraging a variational graph auto-encoder to encode both the nodes (agents) and a task-specific virtual node, and decodes a task-adaptive and high-performing communication topology. 在六个基准上的大量实验表明，G-Designer 具备以下特点：(1) 性能出色，在 MMLU 上取得 $84.50\%$ 的准确率，在 HumanEval 上取得 $89.90\%$ 的 pass@1，均表现优异；(2) 能够适应任务，根据任务难度构建相应的通信协议，在 HumanEval 上将 token 消耗最多降低 $95.33\%$；(3) 具有对抗鲁棒性，能够抵御针对智能体的对抗攻击，准确率仅下降 $0.3\%$。 Extensive experiments on six benchmarks showcase that G-Designer is: (1) high-performing, achieving superior results on MMLU with accuracy at $84.50\%$ and on HumanEval with pass@1 at $89.90\%$ ; (2) task-adaptive, architecting communication protocols tailored to task difficulty, reducing token consumption by up to $95.33\%$ on HumanEval; and (3) adversarially robust, defending against agent adversarial attacks with merely $0.3\%$ accuracy drop. 代码可在 https://github.com/yanweiyue/GDesigner 获取。 The code is available at https://github.com/yanweiyue/GDesigner.

1 引言1 Introduction
基于 LLM 的智能体将 LLM 的语言生成能力与决策和动作执行功能相结合 (Richards & et al., 2023; Nakajima, 2023; Reworkd, 2023)，在广泛的任务中展现了令人瞩目的性能，从推理 (Yao et al., 2023b) 和代码生成 (Shinn et al., 2023)，到电子游戏 (Wang et al., 2023) 和自动驾驶 (Jin et al., 2023) 等更复杂的应用。 An LLM-based agent, which integrates the language generation capabilities of LLMs with decision-making and action-execution functionalities (Richards & et al., 2023; Nakajima, 2023; Reworkd, 2023), has exhibited impressive performance across a wide range of tasks, from reasoning (Yao et al., 2023b) and code generation (Shinn et al., 2023) to even more complex applications like video gaming (Wang et al., 2023) and autonomous driving (Jin et al., 2023). 更令人振奋的是，研究人员发现，无论以隐式还是显式的方式将多个基于 LLM 的智能体组成团队，在处理复杂任务时都能超越单个智能体 (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023b; Jiang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023)，在多智能体系统中展现出一种类似人类团队协作的协同智能 (Zhang et al., 2023b)。 Even more exciting, researchers have discovered that combining multiple LLM-based agents–whether implicitly or explicitly–into a team can outperform individual agents when tackling complex tasks (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023b; Jiang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023), demonstrating a form of collaborative intelligence reminiscent of human teamwork in multi-agent systems (Zhang et al., 2023b). 这种类人集体智能的涌现，从根本上源于其拓扑设计，即多个智能体如何连接，以及它们如何相互传递、交换和吸收信息。 This emergence of human-esque collective intelligence is fundamentally driven by the design of their topology, i.e., how multi-agents are connected, and how they transmit, exchange, and assimilate information reciprocally.

针对这一困境，我们提出基于 LLM 的多智能体通信协议（Multi-agent Communication Protocol，MACP），为 LLM-MA 拓扑设计确立标准化指导原则： In light of this dilemma, we propose the LLM-based Multi-agent Communication Protocol (MACP), establishing standardized guidance for LLM-MA topology design:
多智能体通信协议（MACP）：给定任务/查询 $q$，针对 $q$ 的最优 LLM-MA 通信拓扑应满足以下协议逻辑：(1) 有效性：通信结构必须能够有效地产生符合要求的 $q$ 的解答；(2) 复杂度自适应性：拓扑应根据任务复杂度动态调整，尽可能降低通信开销；(3) 对抗鲁棒性：拓扑应在遭受对抗攻击时保持可靠。 Multi-agent Communication Protocol (MACP): Given a task/query $q$ , an optimal LLM-MA communication topology for $q$ should satisfy the following protocol logics: (1) Effectiveness: The communication structure must effectively produce the qualified solution for $q$ ; (2) Complexity-adaptiveness: The topology should dynamically adjust to the complexity of the task, minimizing communication overhead; (3) Adversarial robustness: The topology should maintain reliable under adversarial attacks.
MACP 的形式化定义见第 3.3 节。 The formal definition of MACP is provided in Section 3.3. 为设计出理想地遵循 MACP 原则的通信拓扑，我们提出了一种由 LLM 驱动、兼具有效性、自适应性和鲁棒性的多智能体通信图设计器，称为 G-Designer。 To design a communication topology that ideally adheres to the MACP principles, we propose an effective, adaptive, and robust LLM-powered multi-agent communication graph designer, termed G-Designer. 在技术上，G-Designer 首先构建一个多智能体图，其中每个智能体及其特定属性（例如，角色设定 (Li et al., 2023a)、外部 API 工具 (Zhuang et al., 2023) 或知识库 (Chen et al., 2024a)）被表示为一个节点，而智能体之间的通信构成边。 Technically, G-Designer first architects a multi-agent graph, where each agent, along with its specific properties (e.g., profile (Li et al., 2023a), external API tools (Zhuang et al., 2023), or knowledge base (Chen et al., 2024a)), is represented as a node, and communication between agents forms the edges. G-Designer 使用变分图自编码器（variational graph auto-encoder）对节点（智能体）及任务特定信息进行编码，并解码得到智能体之间的协作网络。 G-Designer employs a variational graph auto-encoder to encode the nodes (agents) along with task-specific information, and to decode the resulting collaboration network between agents. 这种依赖输入的范式使 G-Designer 能够设计适应任务且性能优异的通信拓扑，同时通过稀疏性正则化保障其效率和鲁棒性。 This input-dependent paradigm allows G-Designer to design task-adaptive, high-performing communication topology, which is, at the same time, assured of efficiency and robustness with sparsity regularization. 以往基于 LLM 的多智能体拓扑设计对所有查询/任务均采用静态结构，而 G-Designer 则能够针对不同领域和任务自适应地构建定制拓扑，充当用于多智能体系统构建和部署的完全自主且灵活的助手。 Unlike previous LLM-based multi-agent topology designs, which rely on a static structure for all queries/tasks, G-Designer adaptively crafts customized topologies for different domains and tasks, serving as a fully autonomous and flexible assistant for multi-agent system establishment and deployments.

2 相关工作2 Related Works
LLM 智能体协作LLM-agent Collaboration
近期研究探索了多种多智能体通信拓扑，包括：(1) 非交互式，各智能体独立运行，彼此之间不进行通信，采用这一拓扑的系统包括 LATM (Zhang et al., 2023a) 和 LLM-Debate (Du et al., 2023)；(2) 链式，各智能体按顺序排列，每个智能体接收其前驱的输出，并向其后继传递信息，ChatDev (Qian et al., 2023)、MetaGPT (Hong et al., 2023) 和 L2MAC (Holt et al., 2024) 采用了这一拓扑；(3) 星形，由一个中央管理智能体（通常称为指挥官、教师等）指挥下属智能体，见于 AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023)、SecurityBot (Yan et al., 2024) 和 MiniGrid (Zhou et al., 2023)；(4) 树形，由一个根智能体以层级方式管理多个子智能体，例如 SoA (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024)；(5) 图式，包括完全图 (Qian et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024)、随机图 (Qian et al., 2024) 等。 Recent research has explored various multi-agent communication topologies, including: (1) Non-interactive, where agents operate independently without inter-agent communication, as employed in systems like LATM (Zhang et al., 2023a) and LLM-Debate (Du et al., 2023); (2) Chain, where agents are arranged in a sequential structure, each receiving the output from its predecessor and passing information to its successor, utilized by ChatDev (Qian et al., 2023), MetaGPT (Hong et al., 2023), and L2MAC (Holt et al., 2024); (3) Star, where a central administrative agent (often referred to as a commander, teacher, etc.) directs subordinate agents, seen in AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), SecurityBot (Yan et al., 2024), and MiniGrid (Zhou et al., 2023); (4) Tree, where a root agent hierarchically manages multiple child agents, as in SoA (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024); and (5) Graph, encompassing complete graphs (Qian et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024) and random graphs (Qian et al., 2024), among others.
将多智能体表示为图Multi-agents as Graphs
图作为组织和表示实体间关系的基本数据结构 (Zhang & Chartrand, 2006)，在 LLM 出现之前就已被广泛用作促进多智能体强化学习（multi-agent reinforcement learning，MARL）中有效通信的强大工具 (Pesce & Montana, 2023; Hu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2022)。 Graphs, as a fundamental data structure for organizing and representing relationships between entities (Zhang & Chartrand, 2006), are widely adopted in the pre-LLM era as a powerful tool to facilitate effective communication in multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) (Pesce & Montana, 2023; Hu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2022). 随着大语言模型（LLM）的兴起以及基于 LLM 的智能体的广泛涌现 (Chen et al., 2023a; Cohen et al., 2023; Hua et al., 2023)，研究人员也认识到，可以自然地从图的视角对多个智能体之间的交互进行建模 (Chen et al., 2023b; Zhuge et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2023)。 With the rise of LLMs and the proliferation of LLM-based agents (Chen et al., 2023a; Cohen et al., 2023; Hua et al., 2023), researchers have similarly recognized that interactions among multiple agents can naturally be modeled from a graph-based perspective (Chen et al., 2023b; Zhuge et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2023). 早期尝试采用隐式的图建模方式，例如 ChatEval (Chan et al., 2023)、AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) 和 DSPy (Khattab et al., 2023)。 Early attempts are implicit, like ChatEval (Chan et al., 2023), AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), and DSPy (Khattab et al., 2023). 较近期的实践，包括 ChatLLM (Hao et al., 2023)、DyLAN (Liu et al., 2023)、GPTSwarm (Zhuge et al., 2024) 和 MacNet (Qian et al., 2024)，已将多个智能体的组织结构显式表示为图。 More recent practices including ChatLLM (Hao et al., 2023), DyLAN (Liu et al., 2023), GPTSwarm (Zhuge et al., 2024), and MacNet (Qian et al., 2024), have explicitly represented the organization of multiple agents as a graph. 然而，所有这些尝试，无论是预先定义的还是通过迭代优化的，都仍然独立于输入。 However, all these attempts, whether predefined or iteratively optimized, remain input-independent. 因此，它们无法感知任务，也无法自适应地设计出适合具体任务复杂度的拓扑结构。 Consequently, they fail to be task-aware and adaptively design topologies that suit the complexity of the specific task.
3 形式化3 Formalization
本节确立符号体系，从拓扑视角对关键概念进行形式化，并正式定义我们提出的多智能体通信协议。 This section establishes the notation, formalizes key concepts from a topology perspective, and formally defines our proposed multi-agent communication protocol.
3.1 拓扑结构3.1 Topology Structure
我们将多智能体系统建模为有向图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$，其中 $\mathcal{V}=\{v_{1},\dots,v_{N}\}$ 表示节点集合（$N=|\mathcal{V}|$），$\mathcal{E}$ 表示边集合。 We model the multi-agent system as a directed graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ , where $\mathcal{V}=\{v_{1},\dots,v_{N}\}$ represents the set of nodes (with $N=|\mathcal{V}|$ ) and $\mathcal{E}$ denotes the set of edges. 每个节点 $v_{i}\in\mathcal{V}$ 对应一个智能体，其形式化表示为： Each node $v_{i}\in\mathcal{V}$ corresponds to an agent, formalized as: 
| | $v_{i}=\{\texttt{Base}_{i},\texttt{Role}_{i},\texttt{State}_{i},\texttt{Plugin}_{i}\},$ | | (1)
其中，每个智能体 $v_{i}$ 由四个关键要素组成：(1) $\texttt{Base}_{i}$，为 $v_{i}$ 提供支持的语言模型实例；(2) $\texttt{Role}_{i}$，预先分配给该智能体的角色或功能；(3) $\texttt{State}_{i}$，表示该智能体积累的知识和交互历史；以及 (4) $\texttt{Plugin}_{i}$，可供 $v_{i}$ 使用的一组外部工具或插件，例如网络搜索工具 (Ma et al., 2023)、代码编译器 (Richards & et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023; Bouzenia et al., 2024; Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024) 或文件读取工具 (Zhuge et al., 2024; Richards & et al., 2023)。 where each agent $v_{i}$ is composed of four key elements: (1) $\texttt{Base}_{i}$ , the language model instance powering $v_{i}$ ; (2) $\texttt{Role}_{i}$ , the agent’s pre-assigned role or function; (3) $\texttt{State}_{i}$ , representing the agent’s accumulated knowledge and interaction history; and (4) $\texttt{Plugin}_{i}$ , a set of external tools or plugins available to $v_{i}$ , such as web searchers (Ma et al., 2023), code compilers (Richards & et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023; Bouzenia et al., 2024; Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024), or file readers (Zhuge et al., 2024; Richards & et al., 2023). 每个基于 LLM 的智能体 $v_{i}$ 接收提示 $\mathcal{P}$ 并生成响应 $\mathcal{R}_{i}$： Each LLM-based agent $v_{i}$ receives prompt $\mathcal{P}$ and generates response $\mathcal{R}_{i}$ : 
| | $\mathcal{R}_{i}=v_{i}(\mathcal{P})=v_{i}(\mathcal{P}_{\text{sys}},\mathcal{P}_{\text{usr}}),$ | | (2)
其中，$\mathcal{P}_{\text{sys}}=\{\texttt{Role}_{i},\texttt{State}_{i}\}$ 表示包含其角色和状态的系统提示，$\mathcal{P}_{\text{usr}}$ 表示用户提示，其中可能包含给定任务、其他智能体的响应/指令以及从外部检索到的知识。 where $\mathcal{P}_{\text{sys}}=\{\texttt{Role}_{i},\texttt{State}_{i}\}$ represents the system prompt encompassing its role and state, and $\mathcal{P}_{\text{usr}}$ denotes the user prompt, which possibly includes the given tasks, responses/instructions from other agents and externally retrieved knowledge.

3.2 通信流程3.2 Communication Pipeline
给定一个查询/问题 $\mathcal{Q}$，多智能体系统开展 $K$ 轮交互式对话，通过协作推动各智能体基于累积的对话交流生成最终解答 $a^{(K)}$。 Given a query/problem $\mathcal{Q}$ , the multi-agent system engages in $K$ rounds of interactive utterances, which collaboratively drive the agents toward producing the final solution $a^{(K)}$ based on their cumulative dialogue exchanges. 在第 $t$ 轮对话开始时，应用映射函数 $\phi$ 来确定每个智能体的执行序号： At the beginning of the $t$ -th dialogue round, a mapping function $\phi$ is applied to determine the execution index for each agent: 
| | $\displaystyle\phi:\mathcal{G}\longmapsto\sigma,\;\sigma=[v_{\sigma_{1}},v_{\sigma_{2}},\cdots,v_{\sigma_{N}}],$ | | (3)
| | $\displaystyle\operatorname{s.t.}\forall i>j,\;\;v_{\sigma_{i}}\notin\mathcal{N}_{\text{in}}(v_{\sigma_{j}}),$ |
其中，$\sigma$ 为智能体的执行顺序，$\mathcal{N}_{\text{in}}(v_{\sigma(j)})$ 表示 $v_{\sigma(j)}$ 的入邻域，该约束确保智能体 $v_{\sigma(i)}$ 只能在向其传递信息的任一智能体 $v_{\sigma(j)}$ 执行之后执行。 where $\sigma$ is the execution sequence of agents, $\mathcal{N}_{\text{in}}(v_{\sigma(j)})$ denotes the in-neighborhood of $v_{\sigma(j)}$ , and the constraint ensures that an agent $v_{\sigma(i)}$ can only execute after any agent $v_{\sigma(j)}$ from which it receives information. 执行顺序确定后，各智能体依次进行输入输出操作： Once the execution order is determined, each agent proceeds to perform input-output operations sequentially: 
| | $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}=v_{i}(\mathcal{P}^{(t)}_{\text{sys}},\mathcal{P}^{(t)}_{\text{usr}}),\;\mathcal{P}^{(t)}_{\text{usr}}=\{\mathcal{Q},\cup_{v_{j}\in\mathcal{N}_{\text{in}}(v_{i})}\mathcal{R}_{j}^{(t)}\}$ | | (4)
其中，$\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ 表示 $v_{i}$ 的输出，根据具体上下文，该输出可以是推理依据、答案或部分解答。 where $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ represents the output of $v_{i}$ , which could be a rationale, an answer, or a partial solution, depending on the specific context. 输出 $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ 基于系统提示 $\mathcal{P}_{\text{sys}}^{(t)}$ 和上下文提示生成，后者由查询 $\mathcal{Q}$ 以及来自其他智能体的消息组成。 The output $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ is generated based on the system prompt $\mathcal{P}_{\text{sys}}^{(t)}$ and the context prompt, consisting of the query $\mathcal{Q}$ and messages from other agents. 在每轮对话结束时，采用聚合函数生成答案/解答 $a^{(t)}$： At the end of each dialogue, an aggregation function is adopted to generate the answer/solution $a^{(t)}$ : 
| | $a^{(t)}\leftarrow\operatorname{Aggregate}(\mathcal{R}_{1}^{(t)},\mathcal{R}_{2}^{(t)},\cdots,\mathcal{R}_{N}^{(t)}).$ | | (5)
$\operatorname{Aggregate}$ 函数的实现方式十分灵活，可选方案包括多数投票 (Chen et al., 2024b; Zhuge et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024)、汇总所有智能体的响应并委派一个智能体给出最终答案 (Wu et al., 2023; Jiang et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024)，或直接使用最后一个智能体的输出 $\mathcal{R}^{(t)}_{\sigma_{N}}$ (Qian et al., 2024)。 The implementation of the $\operatorname{Aggregate}$ function is flexible, with possible options including majority voting (Chen et al., 2024b; Zhuge et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024), aggregating all agents’ responses and delegating one agent to provide the final answer (Wu et al., 2023; Jiang et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024), or simply using the output of the last agent $\mathcal{R}^{(t)}_{\sigma_{N}}$ (Qian et al., 2024). 经过 $K$ 轮对话，整个系统 $\mathcal{G}$ 为 $\mathcal{Q}$ 生成最终答案 $a^{(K)}$，其中轮数可以预先设定 (Qian et al., 2024)，也可以由提前停止机制确定 (Liu et al., 2023)。 Through $K$ rounds of utterances, either predefined (Qian et al., 2024) or determined by an early-stopping mechanism (Liu et al., 2023), the overall system $\mathcal{G}$ produces the final answer $a^{(K)}$ for $\mathcal{Q}$ .

4.2 通信拓扑设计4.2 Designing Communication Topology
在任务专用的多智能体网络 $\tilde{\mathcal{G}}$ 的基础上，G-Designer 旨在构建更细粒度、更精确的通信拓扑 $\mathcal{G}_{com}$。 Building upon the task-specific multi-agent network $\tilde{\mathcal{G}}$ , G-Designer seeks to establish a more fine-grained and precise communication topology $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ . 受变分图自编码器（VGAE）框架（Kipf & Welling, 2016; Zhao & Zhang, 2024）的启发，G-Designer 采用基于 VGAE 的编码器—解码器 $f_{v}$ 来生成多智能体交互拓扑： Drawing inspiration from the variational graph auto-encoder (VGAE) framework (Kipf & Welling, 2016; Zhao & Zhang, 2024), G-Designer employs a VGAE-based encoder-decoder $f_{v}$ to generate the multi-agent interaction topology: 
| | $\small\mathcal{G}_{com}=f_{v}(\tilde{\mathcal{G}};\Theta_{v})=p(\mathcal{G}_{com}\;|\;\mathbf{H})q(\mathbf{H}\;|\;\tilde{\mathbf{X}},\tilde{\mathbf{A}}_{anchor}),$ | | (9)

| | $\displaystyle q(\mathbf{H}_{agent}\;|\;\tilde{\mathbf{X}},\tilde{\mathbf{A}}_{anchor})=\prod_{i=1}^{N}q(\mathbf{h}_{i}\;|\;\tilde{\mathbf{X}},\tilde{\mathbf{A}}_{anchor}),$ | | (10)

| | $\small p_{s}(\mathbf{S}\;|\;\mathbf{H}_{agent})=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\prod_{j=1}^{N}p_{s}(\mathbf{S}_{ij}\;|\;\mathbf{h}_{i},\mathbf{h}_{j},\mathbf{h}_{task};\Theta_{d}),$ | | (12)

| | $\displaystyle p_{s}(\mathbf{S}_{ij}=1\;|\;\mathbf{h}_{i},\mathbf{h}_{j},\mathbf{h}_{task})=g(\mathbf{h}_{i},\mathbf{h}_{j},\mathbf{h}_{task}),$ | | (13)

4.3 优化 G-Designer4.3 Optimizing G-Designer
获得 $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ 后，多智能体便可使用 $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ 照常进行发言和对话，具体见第 3.2 节。 Upon obtaining $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ , the multi-agent utterances and dialogues can proceed as usual using $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ , as detailed in Section 3.2. 经过 $K$ 轮交互后，智能体收敛至最终解答 $a^{(K)}=\mathcal{G}_{com}(\mathcal{Q})$。 After $K$ rounds of interaction, the agents converge to a final solution $a^{(K)}=\mathcal{G}_{com}(\mathcal{Q})$ . 随后，我们给出如下优化目标： We then give the following optimization objective: 
| | $\underset{\Theta_{e},\Theta_{d}}{\arg\min}\;\mathbb{E}_{\Theta_{e},\Theta_{d}\sim\Omega}\Bigl[u\bigl(\mathcal{G}_{com}(\mathcal{Q})\bigl)\Bigl],$ | | (16)
其中，$\Theta_{e}$ 和 $\Theta_{d}$ 分别是编码器 $q(\cdot)$ 和解码器 $p(\cdot)$ 的参数，$\Omega$ 为参数空间，$\mathbb{E}(\cdot)$ 表示数学期望。 where $\Theta_{e}$ and $\Theta_{d}$ are the parameters of the encoder $q(\cdot)$ and decoder $p(\cdot)$ , respectively, $\Omega$ is the parameter space and $\mathbb{E}(\cdot)$ denotes the mathematical expectation. 公式 16 旨在最大化所生成解答的效用，但由于 $u(\cdot)$ 通常依赖外部 API 调用 (Li et al., 2023b; Hendrycks et al., 2021)，该目标本质上难以求解且不可微。 Equation 16 aims to maximize the utility of the generated solution, but it is inherently intractable and non-differentiable, as $u(\cdot)$ often depends on external API calls (Li et al., 2023b; Hendrycks et al., 2021). 为解决这一问题，我们遵循多智能体结构设计中的标准方法 (Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024)，采用策略梯度（policy gradient）(Williams, 1992) 来近似并优化公式 16： To address this, following standard approaches in multi-agent structure design (Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024), we apply policy gradient (Williams, 1992) to approximate and optimize Equation 16: 
| | $\small\nabla_{\Theta}\mathbb{E}_{\Theta\sim\Omega}\Bigl[u\bigl(\mathcal{G}_{com}(\mathcal{Q})\bigl)\Bigl]\approx\frac{1}{M}\sum_{k=1}^{M}u(a_{m}^{(K)})\nabla_{\Theta}(P(\mathcal{G}_{k})),$ | | (17)
其中，$\mathbf{\Theta}=\{\Theta_{e},\Theta_{d}\}$，$\{\mathcal{G}_{k}\}_{m=1}^{M}$ 是从 $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ 中独立采样得到的样本，$\{a_{m}^{(K)}\}_{m=1}^{M}$ 是相应的输出。 where $\mathbf{\Theta}=\{\Theta_{e},\Theta_{d}\}$ , $\{\mathcal{G}_{k}\}_{m=1}^{M}$ are indepently samples from $\mathcal{G}_{com}$ , and $\{a_{m}^{(K)}\}_{m=1}^{M}$ are the corresponding output. $P(\mathcal{G}_{k})$ 计算 $\mathcal{G}_{k}$ 被采样的概率，可表示为 $P(\mathcal{G}_{k})=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\tilde{\mathbf{S}}_{ij}$。 $P(\mathcal{G}_{k})$ calculates the probability of $\mathcal{G}_{k}$ being sampled, which can be expressed as $P(\mathcal{G}_{k})=\prod_{i=1}^{N}\prod_{j=1}^{N}\tilde{\mathbf{S}}_{ij}$ . 通过将有限的查询集作为“训练集”，并在公式 14 和 16 的指导下进行迭代优化，G-Designer 能够高效地形成任务感知能力以及策略性设计智能体网络的能力，实现真正按任务定制的多智能体拓扑设计。 Through iterative optimization guided by Equations 14 and 16 over a limited set of queries as the “training set”, G-Designer efficiently develops task-awareness and the capability to strategically design the agent network, achieving truly task-customized multi-agent topology design.
