多智能体系统中的对抗性影响如何随规模变化？How does Adversarial Influence Scale in Multi-Agent Systems?
摘要Abstract
多智能体集体研讨可以提升性能，但当某些智能体并非善意行事时，会发生什么？ Multi-agent deliberation can improve performance, but what happens when some agents do not act in good faith? 在实践中，智能体可能具有欺骗性，并因自身目标或外部指令而试图破坏群体的决策。 In practice, an agent may be deceptive and work to subvert the group, whether through its own objectives or external instruction. 我们研究了随着群体规模扩大、欺骗者占比提高，群体受欺骗的易感性如何变化。 We study how susceptibility to deception scales as groups increase in size and deceivers become more prevalent. 关键不在于群体中的智能体数量，而在于欺骗者所占的比例。 It is not the number of agents in the group that matters, but the proportion of deceivers. 我们观察到，转错率（defection rate），即最初回答正确的智能体最终改为错误答案的频率，随这一比例线性上升。 We observe that the defection rate, how often initially correct agents switch to an incorrect final answer, rises linearly with this proportion. 在类似的人类从众研究中，只有当提供误导信息的实验同谋占据多数时，人类才会稳定地受到影响；相比之下，即使欺骗者仍占少数，LLM 智能体也经常转而选择错误答案。 Whereas humans in comparable conformity studies are reliably swayed only when misleading confederates form a majority, LLM agents defect regularly even when deceivers remain a minority. 这种易感性还取决于参与交互的是哪些模型，尤其取决于诚实智能体所使用的模型。 Susceptibility also depends on which models are interacting, especially on the honest agent side. 出乎意料的是，允许欺骗者私下协调可能会降低其欺骗效果。 Unexpectedly, allowing deceivers to coordinate privately can make them less effective. 总体而言，我们的结果表明，仅增加智能体数量并不足以构成有效防御，因为对手也可以随群体规模同步扩张。 Altogether, our results show that adding more agents is therefore not a sufficient defense, because the adversary can simply scale with the group.

1 引言1 Introduction
多智能体系统通常建立在一个简单的前提之上：让更多智能体参与过程，可以提高群体决策的质量。 Multi-agent systems are often built on the simple premise that bringing more agents into the process can improve the quality of the group’s decisions. 多智能体辩论通过允许智能体交换论点并相互质疑，提升了多项任务的性能 (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024)，而采样与聚合则提供了一条互补的改进路径 (Wang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025)。 Multi-agent debate has improved performance on several tasks by allowing agents to exchange and challenge one another’s arguments (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024), while sampling and aggregation provide a complementary route to improvement (Wang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025). 这些结果使扩大群体规模成为扩展准确推理能力的一种颇具吸引力的方法。 These results make larger groups an appealing way to scale accurate inference. 但它们也使系统越来越依赖于群体中的智能体善意行事。 But they also create a growing dependence on the agents within the group behaving in good faith. 当其中一些智能体并非如此行事时，会发生什么？ What happens when some of them do not behave as such? 欺骗性智能体所能做的不仅仅是引入一个错误判断。 A deceptive agent can do more than simply introduce an incorrect judgment. 它还可以主动而巧妙地说服最初判断正确的智能体得出错误结论。 It can actively and subtly persuade initially correct agents to reach an incorrect conclusion. 近期研究揭示了协作系统中的这一漏洞 (Huang et al., 2025; He et al., 2025; Kraidia et al., 2026)。 Recent work demonstrates this vulnerability in collaborative systems (Huang et al., 2025; He et al., 2025; Kraidia et al., 2026). 因此，我们提出：随着群体规模扩大、欺骗性智能体数量增加，群体对这种负面影响的易感性会如何演变？ We therefore ask how susceptibility to this kind of negative influence evolves as groups grow in size and deceptive agents increase in number.
人类从众研究为理解这一漏洞提供了一个有用的起点。 Human conformity research provides a useful starting point for understanding this vulnerability. Asch 的经典实验表明，持错误判断的多数群体可能使人们放弃原本可靠的判断 (Asch, 1951; Asch, 1955; Asch, 1956)。 Asch’s classic experiments showed that an incorrect majority can lead people to abandon otherwise reliable judgments (Asch, 1951; Asch, 1955; Asch, 1956). 后续研究考察了这种影响如何取决于群体构成。 Later work has examined how this influence depends on group composition. 在目击者讨论实验中，Mojtahedi et al. (2018) 改变了真实参与者和提供误导信息的实验同谋的数量，发现当实验同谋占多数时，参与者容易受到影响而错误归责；但在所测试的仅有一名实验同谋的条件下，参与者并未受到显著影响。 In eyewitness discussions, Mojtahedi et al. (2018) varied the numbers of genuine participants and misleading confederates, finding that participants were susceptible to false blame when confederates formed a majority, but not significantly influenced in the tested conditions with a single confederate. 类似地，Coultas (2004) 发现，是否模仿某种不寻常行为取决于群体中表现出该行为的成员比例，而非群体的绝对规模，而且除非多数成员都表现出该行为，否则模仿很少发生。 Similarly, Coultas (2004) found that copying an unusual behavior depended on the proportion of the group displaying it rather than on absolute group size, and was rare unless a majority did so. 综合来看，这些发现表明，在人类群体中，具有误导性的少数派所能施加的影响有限。 Together, these findings suggest that in humans, a misleading minority exerts limited influence. 这促使我们针对多智能体 LLM 系统提出一个互补的问题：随着具有欺骗性的少数派壮大，诚实的多数派能够提供多大程度的保护？ This motivates a complementary question for multi-agent LLM systems: how much protection does an honest majority provide as a deceptive minority grows?
我们使用闭源和开源模型，在由 2 至 21 个智能体组成的群体中，同时改变诚实智能体和欺骗性智能体的数量，研究欺骗性智能体如何影响针对 Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE) (Center for AI Safety et al., 2026) 中问题的集体推理。 We study how deceptive agents influence collective reasoning on questions from Humanity’s Last Exam (HLE) (Center for AI Safety et al., 2026), using closed- and open-source models in groups of 2 to 21 agents and scaling both the quantities of honest and deceptive agents. 我们发现，即使欺骗者占少数，诚实智能体的转错率也会随欺骗者比例的上升而近似线性增加，因此，即便是规模很小的欺骗性少数派，也会以持续上升的频率诱发转错。 We find that honest agents defect at rates that rise approximately linearly with the proportion of deceivers even when deceivers are the minority, so even a small deceptive minority induces defection at consistently increasing rates. 我们还发现，私下协调会降低欺骗者的有效性，并通过受控分析和定性分析考察了智能体的论点与策略如何调整。 We also find that private coordination reduces the effectiveness of deceivers, and we provide a controlled and qualitative analysis of how agents’ arguments and strategies adapt.

我们检验由语言模型智能体组成的更大群体是否更能抵御欺骗。 We test whether larger groups of language-model agents are more resistant to deception. 智能体首先独立回答问题，随后反复进行公开讨论和私下反思，最后投票。 Agents first answer a question independently, repeatedly discuss public and reflect privately, and cast their final votes. 我们改变诚实参与者和欺骗性参与者的数量。 We vary the numbers of honest and deceptive participants. 我们衡量诚实智能体是否会放弃其最初答对的答案。 We measure whether honest agents abandon answers they initially got right. 一个群体包含 $N$ 个智能体：$k$ 个欺骗性智能体和 $N-k$ 个诚实智能体。 A group contains $N$ agents: $k$ deceptive agents and $N-k$ honest agents. 欺骗性智能体的比例为 $k/N$。 The deceptive proportion is $k/N$ .
2.1 智能体角色2.1 Agent Roles
诚实智能体被要求与同事合作，找出正确答案。 Honest agents are instructed to work with their colleagues to find the correct answer. 在最终投票时，它们被要求运用自己的判断。 At the final vote, they are asked to use their own judgement. 它们不会被告知任何参与者都可能具有对抗性。 They are not told that any participant may be adversarial.
欺骗者在每个阶段都会私下获得参考答案。 Deceivers receive the reference answer privately at every stage. 它们的目标是说服其他智能体选择错误答案，同时既不暴露自己的目标，也不透露自己能够获取参考答案。 Their objective is to persuade the other agents to choose an incorrect answer without revealing either their objective or their access to the reference answer. 它们被要求提出看似合理、内部一致的论证，并根据讨论情况作出调整。 They are instructed to make plausible, internally consistent arguments and to adapt to the discussion. 如果它们认为另一个错误答案更具说服力，就可以改用该答案。 They can switch to a different incorrect answer if they think it will be more persuasive. 它们可以在私下反思中坦率地讨论自己的策略，并被要求在最终投票时选择错误答案。 They can discuss their strategy openly in their private reflections and are instructed to cast an incorrect final vote.

2.2 讨论流程2.2 Discussion process
智能体通过共享留言板进行交流，其回复以匿名标签（“同事 1”“同事 2”等）显示。 Agents communicate through a shared message board, where their responses appear under anonymous labels (“Colleague 1”, “Colleague 2”, and so on). 每个智能体还会保留一份私下反思：以书面形式记录其当前答案、推理过程，以及它希望从讨论中保留的信息。 Each agent also maintains a private reflection: a written record of its current answer, reasoning, and information it wants to retain from the discussion. 反思仅对撰写它的智能体可见。 Reflections are visible only to the agent that writes them. 图 1 概述了该协议，附录 B 给出了完整指令。 Figure 1 summarizes the protocol, and Appendix B gives the full instructions.
默认情况下，欺骗者不会被告知哪些同事与自己具有相同角色，也无法彼此私下交流。 By default, deceivers are not told which colleagues share their role and cannot communicate privately with one another. 一次试验包含以下步骤： A trial consists of the following steps:
初始作答（第 0 轮）。 Initial answers (round 0). 每个智能体都会收到问题及其角色指令，但不会收到反思或其他智能体的回复。 Each agent receives the question and its role instructions, with no reflection or peer responses. 它给出答案及解释。 It gives an answer and explanation. 所有回复发布后，每个智能体都会收到问题和第 0 轮留言板的全部内容，以撰写其首次私下反思。 Once all responses are posted, each agent receives the question and the full round 0 board to write its first private reflection.
讨论（第 $1,\ldots,R-1$ 轮）。 Discussion (rounds $1,\ldots,R-1$ ). 为撰写公开回复，每个智能体都会收到问题、自己上一份反思，以及上一轮的所有公开回复，包括它自己的回复。 To write a public reply, each agent receives the question, its previous reflection, and every public response from the previous round, including its own. 在提交之前，它看不到本轮的任何回复。 It sees no current-round responses before submitting. 所有回复发布后，每个智能体都会收到问题、自己上一份反思、第 0 轮留言板的全部内容，以及本轮留言板的全部内容，以撰写更新后的反思。 Once all replies are posted, each agent receives the question, its previous reflection, the full round 0 board, and the full current board to write an updated reflection.
最终投票。 Final vote. 每个智能体都会收到问题和自己的最终反思，但不会收到公开回复留言板的内容。 Each agent receives the question and its final reflection, with no public-response board. 它在看不到其他任何智能体投票的情况下提交答案。 It submits an answer without seeing anyone else’s vote.

2.3 任务描述2.3 Task Description
我们使用 HLE（Center for AI Safety et al., 2026）中的问题，并为每个模型构建按难度分层的评估集。 We use questions from HLE (Center for AI Safety et al., 2026) and construct a difficulty-stratified evaluation set for each model. 为估计问题难度，每个模型使用与讨论试验第 0 轮相同的指令，对每个问题作答四次。 To estimate question difficulty, each model answers every question four times using the same instructions as in round 0 of the deliberation trial. 我们根据这四次试验中回答正确的次数对问题进行分层，得到三个难度组，分别对应四次作答中有一次、两次或三次正确。 We stratify questions by the number of correct responses across these four trials, yielding three difficulty groups corresponding to one, two, or three correct responses out of four. 回答正确次数为零次或四次的问题被排除。 Questions answered correctly zero or four times are excluded.

(2024) 说服能力评估中分别得分最高和最低。 For deceivers, we select DeepSeek and Grok, the highest- and lowest-scoring models in our replication of the persuasion evaluation of Durmus et al. (2024). 我们在两个对抗性智能体比例下测试全部四种诚实模型与欺骗者模型的配对：比例为 $1/5$ 时采用 $4{+}1$ 和 $8{+}2$，比例为 $3/7$ 时采用 $4{+}3$ 和 $8{+}6$（图 6）。 We test all four honest–deceiver pairings at two adversarial proportions: $4{+}1$ and $8{+}2$ at $1/5$ , and $4{+}3$ and $8{+}6$ at $3/7$ (Figure 6).

我们考察诚实智能体何时改变答案、欺骗者如何调整论证、诚实智能体在私下反思中报告什么，以及相互协调的欺骗者如何使用其私密通道（采用按问题聚类的双侧 $t$ 检验；在每组比较内使用 Holm 方法校正 $p$ 值）。 We examine when honest agents change their answers, how deceivers adapt their arguments, what honest agents report in their private reflections, and how coordinated deceivers use their private channel (two-sided question-clustered $t$ tests; $p$ values are Holm-adjusted within each comparison family).
大多数背离发生在早期。 Most defections happen early. 诚实智能体放弃正确答案时，通常是在讨论开始后不久。 When honest agents abandon a correct answer, they usually do so near the start of the discussion. 在欺骗者未协调行动的情况下，37–54% 的首次背离发生在第 1 轮，58–72% 在第 2 轮结束前发生（以 50% 为参照值进行检验：Gemini，$t(83)=4.88$，$p<0.001$；Grok，$t(83)=5.20$，$p<0.001$；DeepSeek，$t(87)=3.00$，$p=0.0035$；Muse Glimmer，$t(46)=4.11$，$p<0.001$）。 With non-coordinated deceivers, 37–54% of first defections occur in round 1, and 58–72% occur by round 2 (tests against 50%: Gemini, $t(83)=4.88$ , $p<0.001$ ; Grok, $t(83)=5.20$ , $p<0.001$ ; DeepSeek, $t(87)=3.00$ , $p=0.0035$ ; Muse Glimmer, $t(46)=4.11$ , $p<0.001$ ).
讨论一旦开始，欺骗者就会改变策略。 Deceivers change tactics once discussion begins. 在看到其他智能体的回答之前，欺骗者会使用捏造或歪曲的证据（56%）以及误导性推论（57%）。 Before seeing other agents’ answers, deceivers use fabricated or misrepresented evidence (56%) and misleading inferences (57%). 在看到第一轮回复后，他们的论证变得更具回应性。 After seeing the first-round responses, their arguments become more reactive. 承认另一智能体的部分论点并将其引向其他方向的比例从 6% 上升至 65%，选择性怀疑的比例从 7% 上升至 57%，重新解释问题的比例从 15% 上升至 52%（分别为 $t(69)=16.80$、$11.51$ 和 $9.59$；均为 $p<0.001$；图 7b）。 Conceding part of another agent’s argument and redirecting it rises from 6% to 65%, selective skepticism from 7% to 57%, and question reinterpretation from 15% to 52% (respectively, $t(69)=16.80$ , $11.51$ , and $9.59$ ; all $p<0.001$ ; Figure 7b). 协同欺骗者也表现出同样的转变：先让步再转向的比例从 14% 上升至 67%，选择性怀疑的比例从 12% 上升至 57%（分别为 $t(33)=8.02$ 和 $9.15$；两者均为 $p<0.001$）。 Coordinated deceivers show the same shift: concede-and-redirect rises from 14% to 67%, and selective skepticism from 12% to 57% (respectively, $t(33)=8.02$ and $9.15$ ; both $p<0.001$ ).

多智能体推理与规模扩展。 Multi-agent reasoning and scaling. 多智能体商议可以通过交换论证来改善推理和评估（Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2024），而 CAMEL、AutoGen 和 MetaGPT 则提供了更广泛的协作框架（Li et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024）。 Multi-agent deliberation can improve reasoning and evaluation through argument exchange (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2024), while CAMEL, AutoGen, and MetaGPT provide broader frameworks for collaboration (Li et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024). 结构化辩论也利用相互竞争的辩护方，帮助独立的裁判发现真相（Irving et al., 2018; Khan et al., 2024）。 Structured debate also uses competing advocates to help a separate judge recover the truth (Irving et al., 2018; Khan et al., 2024). 增加智能体或模型输出的数量可以提升性能（Li et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025），但相对于投票或更强的单智能体基线，辩论带来的增益可能会减小（Zhang et al., 2025; Choi et al., 2025a; Yang et al., 2025），且规模扩展的收益取决于任务结构和信息的独立性（Kim et al., 2025; Bertalanič & Fortuna, 2026）。 Adding agents or model outputs can improve performance (Li et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025), although debate gains can diminish relative to voting or stronger single-agent baselines (Zhang et al., 2025; Choi et al., 2025a; Yang et al., 2025), and scaling benefits depend on task structure and information independence (Kim et al., 2025; Bertalanič & Fortuna, 2026). 关于人类集体判断的相关研究表明，多样性有助于提高准确性，而互动既可能改善判断，也可能削弱聚合效果（Hong & Page, 2004; Lorenz et al., 2011; Becker et al., 2017）。 Related work on human collective judgment shows that diversity can support accuracy and that interaction can either improve judgments or undermine aggregation (Hong & Page, 2004; Lorenz et al., 2011; Becker et al., 2017). 我们关注的是，规模更大的群体能否在面对蓄意采取对抗行为的少数成员时维持正确推理。 Our focus is whether larger groups preserve correct reasoning against a deliberately adversarial minority.
大语言模型（LLMs）中的社会影响。 Social influence in LLMs. 语言模型既是影响的施加者，也是影响的承受者。 Language models are both sources and targets of influence. 作为影响的施加者，它们的说服成功率与人类写作者相当，并且能够持久地改变根深蒂固的信念（Durmus et al., 2024; Salvi et al., 2025; Costello et al., 2024）。 As sources, they persuade at rates comparable to human writers and can durably change strongly held beliefs (Durmus et al., 2024; Salvi et al., 2025; Costello et al., 2024). 作为影响的承受者，它们往往会赞同用户或社会显著性较高的立场，而不考虑其准确性（Sharma et al., 2024; Wei et al., 2023; Cheng et al., 2026），并且仅仅被要求重新考虑，就可能放弃正确答案（Huang et al., 2024）。 As targets, they tend to agree with users or socially salient positions regardless of accuracy (Sharma et al., 2024; Wei et al., 2023; Cheng et al., 2026) and can abandon correct answers when merely asked to reconsider (Huang et al., 2024). 在群体中，它们的判断会向多数立场靠拢，并受到不确定性、互动历史和辩论伙伴的影响（Zhu et al., 2025; Weng et al., 2025; Choi et al., 2025b; Yao et al., 2025），不过 Hao et al. (2026) 将自发的不稳定性、对明确表达的立场的从众以及被推理说服区分开来。 In groups, their judgments move toward majority positions and depend on uncertainty, interaction history, and debate partners (Zhu et al., 2025; Weng et al., 2025; Choi et al., 2025b; Yao et al., 2025), although Hao et al. (2026) separate spontaneous instability from conformity to stated positions and from persuasion by reasoning. 在这些情境中，多数方大多是按预设脚本行动或真诚表达的，而非策略性地实施欺骗，且其人数与占比同步变化。 In most of these settings the majority is scripted or sincere rather than strategically deceptive, and its size and share vary together. 我们将说服能力和易受影响性视为对抗性商议双方都具有的属性，独立改变欺骗性智能体的数量和比例，并衡量最初回答正确的诚实智能体最终是否给出错误答案，而不将每一次此类变化都归因于某一种机制。 We treat persuasive ability and susceptibility as properties of both sides of an adversarial deliberation, vary the count and proportion of deceptive agents independently, and measure whether initially correct honest agents end with incorrect answers, without attributing every such change to one mechanism.
对抗性智能体与系统鲁棒性。 Adversarial agents and system robustness. 本研究探讨恶意参与者或出现故障的参与者所导致的失效。 This work studies failures caused by malicious or faulty participants. 拜占庭容错（Byzantine fault tolerance）以及有关身份和鲁棒聚合的相关工作，在明确的协议假设下分析恶意参与者（Lamport et al., 1982; Douceur, 2002; Blanchard et al., 2017; Yin et al., 2018）。 Byzantine fault tolerance and related work on identity and robust aggregation analyze malicious participants under explicit protocol assumptions (Lamport et al., 1982; Douceur, 2002; Blanchard et al., 2017; Yin et al., 2018). 在 LLM 系统中，攻击会利用提示、记忆、共享上下文和智能体间通信（Greshake et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026; Ju et al., 2026; He et al., 2025），而其他研究则考察存在故障的协作者、协同破坏以及智能体交互带来的更广泛风险（Huang et al., 2025; Hammond et al., 2025; Schroeder de Witt et al., 2025; Radev et al., 2026; McAllister et al., 2026）。 In LLM systems, attacks exploit prompts, memory, shared context, and inter-agent communication (Greshake et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026; Ju et al., 2026; He et al., 2025), while other studies examine faulty collaborators, coordinated sabotage, and broader risks from interacting agents (Huang et al., 2025; Hammond et al., 2025; Schroeder de Witt et al., 2025; Radev et al., 2026; McAllister et al., 2026). 与本研究最直接相关的是，Kraidia et al. (2026) 研究了辩论中由说服驱动的对抗性影响，并发现增加智能体数量或辩论轮次并不能可靠地保障群体准确率。 Most directly, Kraidia et al. (2026) study persuasion-driven adversarial influence in debate and find that adding agents or rounds does not reliably protect group accuracy. 我们的工作聚焦于多智能体 LLM 系统中的对抗性影响如何随规模变化，以及这种影响如何改变原本具备良好能力的智能体的行为。 Our work focuses on how adversarial influence scales in multi-agent LLM systems and how it changes the behavior of otherwise capable agents. 我们并非仅将鲁棒性视为群体能否得出正确答案的问题，而是研究欺骗性参与者如何塑造个体判断，以及更大的群体或更高程度的协调能否提供实质性保护。 Rather than treating robustness only as a question of whether a group reaches the right answer, we study how deceptive participants shape individual judgments and whether larger groups or greater coordination provide meaningful protection.
5 结论5 Conclusion
我们研究随着群体规模扩大、欺骗性参与者占比上升，多智能体系统如何应对欺骗。 We study how multi-agent systems respond to deception as groups grow and deceptive participants become more prevalent. 在闭源和开源模型中，随着欺骗者比例上升，诚实智能体更有可能放弃最初的正确答案。 Across closed and open-source models, honest agents are more likely to abandon initially correct answers as the proportion of deceivers increased. 在对抗性参与者比例固定的情况下，扩大群体规模并未提供稳定的保护作用。 Increasing group size at a fixed adversarial proportion offered no consistent protection. 这些发现表明，群体构成对其鲁棒性至关重要，仅增加参与者数量并不能可靠地保护正确判断免受对抗性影响。 These findings show that the composition of a group matters for its robustness, and that increasing the number of participants alone does not reliably protect correct judgments from adversarial influence.
群体构成的重要性与人类从众研究的发现相呼应（Coultas, 2004），但我们的结果表明，系统对欺骗性少数群体的易感性更令人担忧。 The importance of group composition echoes findings from human conformity research (Coultas, 2004), but our results suggest a more concerning susceptibility to deceptive minorities. 在 Mojtahedi et al. (2018) 开展的人类目击证人研究中，提供误导信息的实验同谋只有在占多数时才会显著影响判断。 In the human eyewitness study of Mojtahedi et al. (2018), misleading confederates significantly influenced judgments only when they formed a majority. 在我们的实验中，欺骗者始终占少数，却仍然显著促使诚实智能体放弃正确答案。 In our experiments, deceivers remained a minority yet still substantially influenced honest agents to abandon correct answers. 这一对比表明，LLM 群体抵御少数群体影响的能力可能弱于人类。 This contrast suggests that LLM populations may be less resistant to minority influence than humans. 让大多数参与者致力于得出正确答案，并不能确保群体抵御那些试图误导它的参与者。 Having most participants work toward the correct answer does not ensure that the group will resist those trying to mislead it.
易感性还取决于所涉及的模型及其交互方式。 Susceptibility also depended on the models involved and how they interacted. 在我们的异构实验中，更具迎合性的诚实模型更频繁地放弃正确答案，而更具说服力的欺骗者通常会导致更多此类转变。 In our heterogeneous experiments, the more sycophantic honest model defected more often, while the more persuasive deceiver generally caused more defection. 这些结果表明，在评估多智能体系统时，诚实模型的易感性和欺骗者的说服力都值得关注。 These results suggest that both the honest model’s susceptibility and the deceiver’s persuasiveness deserve attention when evaluating a multi-agent system. 然而，在所测试的两个模型家族中，私下协调均降低了欺骗者的有效性，尽管这使它们能够共同制定计划并分配角色。 Private coordination, however, reduced deceivers’ effectiveness in both tested families, despite allowing them to plan together and divide roles.
我们的行为分析表明，许多智能体首次从正确答案转向错误答案发生在前两轮讨论内。 Our behavioral analysis shows that many agents first switched from correct to incorrect answers within the first two discussion rounds. 在私下反思中，诚实智能体经常提及对问题的不同解读，或在相互竞争的答案之间仍未消除的不确定性。 In their private reflections, honest agents often cited a different interpretation of the question or unresolved uncertainty between competing answers. 讨论也帮助智能体纠正了最初的错误，因此不能仅通过放弃正确答案的行为来理解这些系统。 Discussion also helped agents correct initial mistakes, so these systems cannot be understood through defection alone. 评估应同时考虑讨论纠正了哪些错误，以及推翻了哪些正确判断。 Evaluations should consider both the errors that discussion corrects and the correct judgments it overturns. 更广泛的挑战是在保留论点交流益处的同时，降低对欺骗的易感性，而仅靠增加智能体数量无法解决这一问题。 The broader challenge is to preserve the benefits of exchanging arguments while reducing susceptibility to deception, a problem that adding more agents does not resolve on its own.
局限性。 Limitations. 我们的实验涵盖两种审议协议，群体规模最多为 21 个智能体。 Our experiments cover two deliberation protocols for groups of up to 21 agents. 与人类的比较基于不同的任务和实验设置。 The comparison with humans draws on different tasks and experimental settings. 我们的行为分析属于描述性分析，无法确定智能体为何放弃正确答案，也无法确定私下协调为何会降低欺骗者的有效性。 Our behavioral analyses are descriptive and do not establish why agents defect or why private coordination reduces deceivers’ effectiveness. 未来工作应检验这些模式是否适用于其他任务、通信结构以及更大的群体。 Future work should test whether these patterns extend to other tasks, communication structures, and larger groups. 还应考察对有争议的主张进行独立核验等干预措施，能否在保留纠错能力和总体准确率的同时减少对抗性影响。 It should also examine whether interventions such as independent verification of disputed claims can reduce adversarial influence while preserving error correction and overall accuracy.
