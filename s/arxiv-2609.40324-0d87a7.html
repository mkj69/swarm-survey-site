Abstract摘要
We present Cogentic, a multi-agent harness for automated proof discovery on open research problems. While frontier language models can generate strong mathematical ideas in a single shot, single-shot generation is often insufficient for open problems that require exploring multiple competing conjectures, overcoming subtle technical obstructions, and retaining intermediate progress over a long horizon. Cogentic addresses these challenges through an iterative prove–verify loop in which an orchestrator allocates a population of independent provers across distinct proof directions, subjects their output to adversarial verification by several specialized components, and promotes confirmed intermediate results into a persistent verified ledger that later rounds build on. The harness is designed to be able to solve research-level math and theoretical computer science problems. Using either Gemini 3.1 Pro or an early version of Gemini 4 Argon as the base model, Cogentic produced novel results on open problems across online learning, auction theory, and mechanism design. Each result was independently verified by domain experts and is developed in full in companion papers. We list these results, and new ones as they are verified, at https://sites.google.com/view/cogentic.
我们提出 Cogentic，这是一个面向开放研究问题、用于自动化证明发现的多智能体运行框架。尽管前沿语言模型能够通过单次生成提出有力的数学思路，但对于需要探索多个相互竞争的猜想、克服微妙的技术障碍，并在较长时间跨度内保留中间进展的开放问题，单次生成往往并不足够。Cogentic 通过迭代的证明–验证循环应对这些挑战：编排器将一组相互独立的证明智能体分配到不同的证明方向上，让多个专门组件对其输出进行对抗性验证，并将已确认的中间结果纳入持久保存的已验证结果台账，供后续轮次在此基础上继续推进。该运行框架旨在解决研究级别的数学和理论计算机科学问题。以 Gemini 3.1 Pro 或 Gemini 4 Argon 的早期版本作为基础模型，Cogentic 在在线学习、拍卖理论和机制设计领域的开放问题上取得了新成果。每项成果均已由领域专家独立验证，并在配套论文中作了完整阐述。我们在 https://sites.google.com/view/cogentic 列出了这些成果，并会在新成果通过验证后将其加入。

1 Introduction1 引言
We introduce Cogentic, a multi-agent harness for proof discovery on open research problems. Inspired by our own experience conducting research in theoretical computer science and mathematics, the harness divides the work like a research group would: an orchestrator decides what gets worked on, provers draft proofs in parallel, and verifiers read those drafts looking for potential issues. We found that our system is quite efficient: The results reported here were based on Cogentic runs (each powered entirely by either Gemini 3.1 Pro or an early version of Gemini 4 Argon) which used $O(100)$ model calls for most problems, and $O(1000)$ for the hardest. The system has headroom for further optimization of the number of calls but also allows for naturally scaling up to solve harder problems. A key feature of the system is that it only needs a problem statement, without any expert hints, and can work autonomously until it produces a result in the form of a paper. To evaluate the harness, we focus on problem domains within our own areas of expertise, targeting natural-language, human-readable proofs where we can directly verify the mathematical reasoning and provide exposition surrounding the background of the paper and the novel techniques that the system generates.
我们介绍 Cogentic，这是一个面向开放研究问题、用于证明发现的多智能体运行框架。受我们自身从事理论计算机科学和数学研究的经验启发，该运行框架像研究团队一样分工：编排器决定研究哪些内容，证明智能体并行起草证明，验证智能体则阅读这些草稿，寻找潜在问题。我们发现，该系统相当高效：本文报告的成果基于 Cogentic 的运行获得（每次运行均完全由 Gemini 3.1 Pro 或 Gemini 4 Argon 的早期版本驱动），其中大多数问题使用了 $O(100)$ 次模型调用，最难的问题使用了 $O(1000)$ 次。该系统在减少调用次数方面仍有进一步优化的空间，同时也能够自然地扩大运行规模，以解决更难的问题。该系统的一个关键特点是，它只需要问题陈述，无须任何专家提示，就能自主工作，直至以论文形式产出成果。为评估该运行框架，我们聚焦于自身专业领域内的问题，以自然语言表述、人类可读的证明为目标，从而能够直接验证数学推理，并阐释论文的相关背景以及系统生成的新技术。
Interactive theorem provers — Lean (Moura and Ullrich, 2021), Isabelle/HOL (Nipkow et al., 2002), Coq (Castéran and Bertot, 2004) — provide machine-checked guarantees, and a substantial line of work uses language models to lower the cost of obtaining them: retrieving premises and generating tactics (Yang et al., 2023), drafting an informal proof and compiling it into a formal sketch (Jiang et al., 2022), and training for olympiad-level formal reasoning with reinforcement learning (Hubert et al., 2026). More recently the same machinery has been used on open research problems (Tsoukalas et al., 2026). In contrast, Cogentic operates in natural language, producing mathematical prose that domain experts can verify.
交互式定理证明器——Lean (Moura and Ullrich, 2021)、Isabelle/HOL (Nipkow et al., 2002)、Coq (Castéran and Bertot, 2004)——提供机器核验的保证，而大量研究利用语言模型来降低获得此类保证的成本：检索前提并生成证明策略 (Yang et al., 2023)，起草非形式化证明并将其编译为形式化证明草图 (Jiang et al., 2022)，以及通过强化学习训练奥林匹克竞赛级别的形式化推理能力 (Hubert et al., 2026)。最近，同样的技术也被用于开放研究问题 (Tsoukalas et al., 2026)。相比之下，Cogentic 使用自然语言开展工作，生成可由领域专家验证的数学论述。
A distinct and highly successful line of work uses language models to search for mathematical objects rather than for arguments. Program search has discovered new constructions in extremal combinatorics (Romera-Paredes et al., 2024), evolutionary coding agents have improved algorithms and bounds (Novikov et al., 2025), and related systems perform test-time learning on open problems (Wang et al., 2025; Yuksekgonul et al., 2026) or explore many problems at once (Georgiev et al., 2025). Neuro-symbolic search attains medal-level performance in olympiad geometry by pairing a learned proposer with a symbolic engine (Trinh et al., 2024). The same recipe extends beyond mathematics to writing expert-level empirical research software under a quality metric (Aygün et al., 2026). What each of these approaches requires is a cheap, faithful, and machine-computable score. Sometimes theorems can be proven by reducing the question to finding mathematical structures with certain properties: e.g., Nagda et al. (2026) found proofs of inapproximability for combinatorial problems by finding extremal structures, and (Cai et al., 2026f) found bounds on gain from bilateral trade by finding extremal probability distributions, both using AlphaEvolve (Novikov et al., 2025) with such oracles. However, not every problem is amenable to such an approach.
另一条独立且极为成功的研究路线利用语言模型搜索数学对象，而非论证。程序搜索发现了极值组合数学中的新构造 (Romera-Paredes et al., 2024)，演化式编程智能体改进了算法及其界 (Novikov et al., 2025)，相关系统则针对开放问题进行测试时学习 (Wang et al., 2025; Yuksekgonul et al., 2026)，或同时探索多个问题 (Georgiev et al., 2025)。神经符号搜索将学习得到的提议器与符号引擎相结合，在奥林匹克几何竞赛中达到了奖牌水平的表现 (Trinh et al., 2024)。同样的方法还从数学扩展到了在质量指标指导下编写专家级实证研究软件 (Aygün et al., 2026)。这些方法都需要一种计算成本低、忠实反映目标且可由机器计算的评分。有时，可以通过将问题归约为寻找具有特定性质的数学结构来证明定理：例如，Nagda et al. (2026) 通过寻找极值结构，找到了组合问题的不可近似性证明，而 (Cai et al., 2026f) 通过寻找极值概率分布，得到了双边交易收益的界；两者都使用了配备此类预言机的 AlphaEvolve (Novikov et al., 2025)。然而，并非所有问题都适合采用这种方法。
A second line of work is based on scaling the LLM inference budget, perhaps through multiple model calls, to solve more complex tasks. The base case is simply to sample more — chain-of-thought prompting (Wei et al., 2022), self-consistency across sampled solutions (Wang et al., 2022), and repeated sampling with a selector over the candidates (Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024). Another approach is to scale the inference via multi-agent interaction. Debate between instances has been reported to improve factuality and reasoning (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024), and models have been used to evaluate the work of other models (Zhuge et al., 2024). Our framework builds on an iterative interaction between provers and verifiers. The verifiers are adversarial and begin with the assumption that the proofs are incorrect or incomplete. We explain our framework in more detail in Section 2.
第二条研究路线基于扩大大语言模型（LLM）的推理预算，例如通过多次模型调用，来解决更复杂的任务。最基本的做法是增加采样量——采用思维链提示（chain-of-thought prompting）（Wei et al., 2022）、在采样得到的解答之间进行自一致性检验（Wang et al., 2022），以及反复采样并使用选择器从候选解答中进行选择（Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024）。另一种方法是通过多智能体交互来扩展推理计算规模。已有研究报告称，模型实例之间的辩论能够提升事实准确性和推理能力（Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024），也有研究使用模型来评估其他模型的工作（Zhuge et al., 2024）。我们的框架建立在证明者与验证者之间的迭代交互之上。验证者采取对抗性立场，并以证明不正确或不完整为初始假设。我们将在第 2 节更详细地介绍这一框架。
There are several recent works that apply agentic harnesses to research-level mathematics and theoretical computer science. We do not attempt to give a comprehensive survey or compare these efforts, but they include (Feng et al., 2026; Lin et al., 2026; Schmitt et al., 2026; Zheng et al., 2026; Gottweis et al., 2026).
近期有若干工作将智能体运行框架（agentic harnesses）应用于研究级数学和理论计算机科学问题。我们无意对这些工作进行全面综述或比较，但它们包括（Feng et al., 2026; Lin et al., 2026; Schmitt et al., 2026; Zheng et al., 2026; Gottweis et al., 2026）。
Finally, we place our contributions against recent milestones obtained with large agentic systems, including the resolution of a Millennium Prize problem (OpenAI, 2026b) and other long-standing mathematical conjectures (OpenAI, 2026a; Anthropic, 2026). We focus on problems in our areas of expertise, at the hardness level of open questions in flagship theoretical computer science conferences like STOC and FOCS, using a relatively low inference budget, $O(100)$ to $O(1000)$ Gemini calls per problem.
最后，我们将自身贡献置于大型智能体系统近期取得的里程碑成果这一背景下加以考察，其中包括解决一个千禧年大奖难题（OpenAI, 2026b）以及其他长期悬而未决的数学猜想（OpenAI, 2026a; Anthropic, 2026）。我们聚焦于自身专长领域中的问题，其难度相当于 STOC 和 FOCS 等理论计算机科学旗舰会议中的开放问题，并采用相对较低的推理预算，即每个问题调用 Gemini $O(100)$ 到 $O(1000)$ 次。
2 The Harness2 运行框架
Cogentic consists of a group of agents working on a single problem. They share a workspace on disk and are coordinated by an orchestrator that decides which of them runs, when, and on what.
Cogentic 由一组共同处理同一个问题的智能体组成。它们共享磁盘上的一个工作空间，并由一个协调器统一协调，由其决定哪些智能体在何时运行以及处理什么任务。
Components.组成部分。
We outline the main components of our system below.
下面概述我们系统的主要组成部分。
The orchestrator serves as the central controller: it tracks global state, partitions prover slots across research directions, spawns summarizers to condense history into targeted briefings for individual provers, evaluates verifier consensus, and manages ledgers that store important findings obtained so far and records that track prior proof attempts and their verdicts. The orchestrator does not perform mathematical derivations itself.
协调器充当中央控制器：它跟踪全局状态，在各研究方向之间分配证明者运行名额，启动摘要智能体以将历史信息压缩为面向各个证明者的针对性简报，评估验证者之间的共识，并管理用于存储迄今重要发现的台账，以及用于跟踪以往证明尝试及其评定结果的记录。协调器本身不进行数学推导。
Literature reviewers search for related work and retrieve information such as definitions and theorems that a prover is likely to need. They can also be sent out again mid-run to do a more targeted search and find results that can help overcome ongoing technical barriers.
文献审查者搜索相关工作，并检索证明者可能需要的定义、定理等信息。在运行过程中，也可以再次派遣它们开展更有针对性的搜索，寻找有助于克服当前技术障碍的结果。
Provers generate candidate proofs independently and in parallel based on assigned briefings that selectively summarize findings obtained so far.
证明者根据分配给各自的简报，独立且并行地生成候选证明，这些简报选择性地概述了迄今获得的发现。
Verifiers critique candidate solutions from complementary angles and scopes.
验证者从相互补充的角度和审查范围出发，对候选解答进行评议。
The record keeps track of prover attempts and their corresponding critiques from verifiers, and the ledger keeps track of verified intermediate lemmas that came out of proof attempts. These are the central ways that our agents communicate and document progress, as explained in more detail below.
记录用于跟踪证明者的尝试及验证者对这些尝试的相应评议，而台账用于跟踪证明尝试中产生的、经过验证的中间引理。这些是我们的智能体进行交流并记录进展的主要方式，下文将作更详细的说明。
An advisor reads outputs across rounds to help the orchestrator decide how to control and allocate prover attempts and what to adjust the instructions given to each individual prover for the next round.
顾问阅读各轮输出，帮助协调器决定如何控制和分配证明尝试，以及下一轮应如何调整给每位证明者的指令。
Finally, a consolidation stage formats and audits the final manuscript.
最后，整合阶段负责对最终文稿进行格式编排和审查。
Workflow.工作流程。
The work proceeds in rounds (Figure 1). A round produces a batch of candidate proofs, puts them through verification, and writes into the record and the verified ledger that the next round starts from. Rounds continue until a draft clears verification or until the budget runs out.
工作按轮次推进（图 1）。每一轮生成一批候选证明，对其进行验证，并将结果写入记录和已验证结果台账，作为下一轮的起点。各轮持续进行，直到某份草稿通过验证或预算耗尽。
Figure 1: One round of Cogentic. The orchestrator decides how many provers to run and which direction each attends to, an advisor writes each prover an individual briefing, the provers draft candidate proofs in parallel, and every draft is verified both on its own and alongside the others from the round. What a round establishes is written down for subsequent rounds: the attempts and why they failed, the verified ledger of intermediate results, and the standing instructions maintained by the process advisor. Literature reviewers supply background at the start and can be dispatched again mid-run when attempts stall at the same step. Rounds continue until a draft clears verification after which the accepted proof is consolidated, expanded into a formal manuscript, and audited.
图 1：Cogentic 的一轮运行。协调器决定运行多少个证明者以及每个证明者负责哪个方向，顾问为每个证明者撰写单独的简报，证明者并行起草候选证明，而每份草稿既接受单独验证，也与本轮其他草稿一同接受验证。每轮确立的信息都会被记录下来，供后续轮次使用：各次尝试及其失败原因、记录中间结果的已验证结果台账，以及流程顾问维护的持续适用指令。文献审查者在开始时提供背景信息，并可在运行过程中，当各次尝试都停滞于同一步骤时再次被派遣执行任务。各轮持续进行，直到某份草稿通过验证，随后对获认可的证明进行整合，将其扩展为正式文稿，并加以审查。
2.1 Directions and Assignments2.1 研究方向与任务分配
A round opens with the orchestrator planning the work: how many provers to run, and what direction each should attend to. A direction here is a specific claim (e.g., a bound, a constant, a construction), finding counterexamples, or as the run goes on, repairing and finalizing promising proofs. A prover is assigned to a direction to work on, but not how to work on it. It reads what has been attempted previously for that direction, what the verifiers said about those attempts, as well as common information like the literature survey and the verified ledger, and chooses its own next step.
每轮开始时，协调器先规划工作：运行多少个证明者，以及每个证明者应负责哪个方向。这里的方向可以是一个具体断言（例如，一个界、一个常数或一种构造）、寻找反例，或者随着运行推进，对有希望的证明进行修补和定稿。证明者会被指定一个研究方向，但不会被指定具体的研究方法。它会阅读此前针对该方向所做的尝试、验证者对这些尝试的评判，以及文献综述和已验证结果台账等共用信息，并自行选择下一步行动。
As the run goes on, the material relevant to a prover grows in volume. Rather than passing all of it as input, each prover sees only a briefing, written for it by a summarizer, which is spawned by the orchestrator. The summarizer reads all prior attempts and their verdicts, selects the most informative ones as context, and gives suggestions for possible next steps, based on what has worked and what has not. Each summarizer produces its briefing independently, so provers in the same round receive different readings of the same history. Longer documents are given as paths rather than quoted in full, so a prover (which itself is an agent, such as an Antigravity agent (The Antigravity Team, 2025)) can open what it wants to read selectively.
随着运行的推进，与证明者相关的材料会越来越多。系统不会将所有材料都作为输入传递给证明者，而是让每个证明者只接收一份由摘要者为其撰写的简报；摘要者由编排器创建。摘要者阅读此前的所有尝试及其评判结果，选出信息量最大的尝试作为上下文，并根据哪些做法奏效、哪些做法无效，为可能的后续步骤提出建议。每个摘要者独立生成简报，因此，同一轮中的证明者会收到对同一段历史的不同解读。较长的文档以路径形式提供，而不全文引用，因此证明者（其本身也是一个智能体，例如 Antigravity 智能体 (The Antigravity Team, 2025)）可以有选择地打开自己想阅读的内容。
2.2 Verification2.2 验证
After each prover completes a proof, it is read by a verifier whose task is to check the correctness of the proof. A separate verifier reads all of the round’s drafts side by side, which allows it to spot shared blind spots and compare the strengths of individual proofs. A draft is accepted only when it passes both verifiers. Both are adversarial: they start from the assumption that every step is wrong until justified, and that every citation is incorrect until checked.
每个证明者完成一份证明后，都会由一名验证者阅读，其任务是检查证明的正确性。另有一名验证者会并排阅读本轮的所有草稿，从而发现共同的盲点，并比较各份证明的优势。草稿只有通过两名验证者的检查后才会被接受。两名验证者都采用对抗式审查：它们起初假定每一步都是错误的，直到得到充分论证，并假定每处引用都是不正确的，直到经过核查。
2.3 Cross-Round Communication2.3 跨轮次通信
Each round leaves two artifacts: a record of what was tried, and a ledger of results that have been verified. The record pools attempts by what they were trying to establish, storing with each one the method used and the objection it failed on. The orchestrator and advisor read the record and decide what the next round should do.
每一轮都会留下两份产物：一份记录所做尝试的档案，以及一份已验证结果的台账。档案按照各项尝试试图确立的结论对其归类，并为每项尝试保存所用的方法及其未能通过的质疑。编排器和顾问阅读档案，并决定下一轮应开展哪些工作。
The ledger accumulates confirmed fragments, such as intermediate lemmas, across rounds. Verifiers often confirm individual lemmas inside proofs they otherwise reject. At the end of a round, an auditor extracts those fragments, rewrites each as a self-contained lemma, and sends it to be verified again in isolation. What survives becomes available to every later prover as something that may be reused without being proved again. The ledger also records dead ends, for example, a bound excluded by a verified counterexample is written down as excluded, so that later rounds do not revisit them.
台账跨轮次积累已经确认的片段，例如中间引理。验证者常常会确认某份证明中的个别引理，即使它们否决了该证明的其余部分。每轮结束时，一名审计者会提取这些片段，将每个片段改写为一条自成一体的引理，并将其单独送交再次验证。通过再次验证的内容将向此后的所有证明者开放，可供直接复用，无须重新证明。台账也会记录行不通的路径，例如，若某个界已被经过验证的反例排除，就会将其记为已排除，以免后续轮次重新探索这些路径。
2.4 Process Level Adaptation2.4 流程层面的适应性调整
At the end of each round, a process advisor reviews verification logs, not only from the current round but from the run as a whole, to adjust how subsequent rounds are conducted. It surfaces patterns that become visible over time: repeated mistakes, common gaps in arguments, recurring verification blind spots where one verifier missed but was caught by some other verifier, etc. Based on these observations, it recommends adjustments to the instructions given to provers and verifiers — for example, a warning about a mistake several provers keep making, or a stricter justification standard for the kind of step where earlier drafts cut corners. It also advises the orchestrator on how to allocate prover attempts and how to adjust the instructions given to each prover and verifier for the next round. This is a separate agent because the orchestrator, which must juggle concurrent duties across many agents, rarely has the context window to review and synthesize log trends itself. Note that, neither the advisor nor the orchestrator is permitted a mathematical opinion: they cannot speculate on what the answer is likely to be, recommend a technique, or declare a direction promising or dead.
每轮结束时，一名流程顾问会审查验证日志，审查范围不仅包括当前轮次，还涵盖整个运行过程，以调整后续轮次的开展方式。它会揭示随时间推移而逐渐显现的模式：反复出现的错误、论证中的常见缺漏、反复出现的验证盲点（即一名验证者漏检、却被另一名验证者发现的问题）等；根据这些观察，它会建议调整提供给证明者和验证者的指令，例如，针对多名证明者反复犯下的某个错误发出警告，或对早期草稿中论证不充分的那类步骤提出更严格的论证标准。它还会就如何分配证明尝试，以及如何调整下一轮提供给各个证明者和验证者的指令，向编排器提出建议。这一角色由独立的智能体承担，因为编排器必须兼顾涉及多个智能体的并发任务，通常没有足够的上下文窗口来自行审查并综合分析日志中的趋势。请注意，顾问和编排器都不被允许发表数学方面的意见：它们不能推测答案可能是什么、推荐某种技巧，或宣称某个方向有希望或已无路可走。
2.5 Termination and Output2.5 终止与输出
When a candidate clears all verification, the orchestrator may continue to explore some remaining promising that may yield a better result, terminating once those avenues are exhausted. Upon termination, a comparator selects the strongest verified proof, a formal writer expands it into a complete manuscript, and a final verification audit checks the compiled document against the accepted proof to ensure no errors were introduced during exposition. The output is a self-contained document that states the theorems and the proofs, with the lemmas it depends on, notation, and background of the problem written out in full, so that it can be read and checked by domain experts who knows nothing about the run that produced it.
当一个候选结果通过所有验证后，编排器可以继续探索一些尚未尝试且有望产生更好结果的路径，并在这些路径均探索完毕后终止运行。运行终止后，一名比较者选出已验证证明中最强的一份，一名正式写作者将其扩写为完整文稿，最后再进行一次验证审计，将编译后的文档与已接受的证明进行核对，确保表述过程中没有引入错误。输出是一份自成一体的文档，其中陈述了定理及其证明，并完整写出证明所依赖的引理、符号说明和问题背景，使对生成该文档的运行过程一无所知的领域专家也能阅读和核查。

3.1 Efficient Online Inverse Linear Optimization3.1 高效在线逆线性优化
In online inverse linear optimization, a learner watches an expert make choices and tries to make the same ones without ever being told what the expert is optimizing. A vector $w^{*}$ in the unit ball $\mathbb{B}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{d}$ is fixed and hidden. At each round an adversary reveals a compact action set $X_{t}\subseteq\mathbb{B}$ , the learner recommends some $\hat{x}_{t}\in X_{t}$ , and then observes the expert’s choice $x_{t}\in\arg\max_{x\in X_{t}}\langle w^{*},x\rangle$ . It does not see $w^{*}$ or the value of any action. The learner is charged the cumulative shortfall $R_{T}=\sum_{t\leq T}\langle w^{*},x_{t}-\hat{x}_{t}\rangle$ , a sum of non-negative terms, and the question is whether that sum can be bounded by a function of $d$ alone, uniformly in $T$ .
在在线逆线性优化中，学习者观察专家作出选择，并试图作出相同的选择，但始终不会被告知专家所优化的目标。单位球 $\mathbb{B}\subseteq\mathbb{R}^{d}$ 中的一个向量 $w^{*}$ 被固定下来且不可见。在每一轮，对手公布一个紧致行动集 $X_{t}\subseteq\mathbb{B}$，学习者推荐某个 $\hat{x}_{t}\in X_{t}$，随后观察专家的选择 $x_{t}\in\arg\max_{x\in X_{t}}\langle w^{*},x\rangle$。学习者无法看到 $w^{*}$，也无法看到任何行动的价值。学习者承担的累计差额为 $R_{T}=\sum_{t\leq T}\langle w^{*},x_{t}-\hat{x}_{t}\rangle$，它是若干非负项之和；问题在于，能否对所有 $T$ 一致地用一个仅依赖于 $d$ 的函数来界定该总和。
A bound uniform in $T$ is stronger than $O(\sqrt{T})$ or $O(d\ln T)$ , both of which let the total grow without bound. And a learner is proper if it commits to a nonzero estimate $\hat{w}_{t}$ of the objective before seeing the menu and then recommends a maximizer of $\langle\hat{w}_{t},\cdot\rangle$ ; a proper learner answers not only what to do but what it takes the expert to be optimizing, and its recommendation costs one linear optimization over $X_{t}$ . The problem reduces to a cutting-plane game with a strong separation oracle, in which the learner queries $p_{t}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ and an adversary who has seen $p_{t}$ returns a unit vector $v_{t}$ with $\langle w^{*}-p_{t},v_{t}\rangle\geq 0$ ; bounding the regret of that game suffices to bounds $R_{T}$ .
对所有 $T$ 一致成立的界比 $O(\sqrt{T})$ 或 $O(d\ln T)$ 更强，因为后两者都允许总量无限增长。如果学习者在看到可选行动集之前就确定一个非零的目标估计 $\hat{w}_{t}$，然后推荐 $\langle\hat{w}_{t},\cdot\rangle$ 的一个最大化解，则称其为恰当的（proper）；恰当学习者不仅回答该做什么，还表明它认为专家在优化什么，而生成其推荐只需在 $X_{t}$ 上进行一次线性优化。该问题可归约为一个带有强分离预言机的割平面博弈：学习者查询 $p_{t}\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$，而看到 $p_{t}$ 的对手返回一个满足 $\langle w^{*}-p_{t},v_{t}\rangle\geq 0$ 的单位向量 $v_{t}$；只要界定该博弈的遗憾，就足以界定 $R_{T}$。
Prior work left a gap between the two desiderata. For arbitrary action sets the best efficient bounds were $O(d\ln T)$ , polynomial but growing with the horizon: Gollapudi et al. (2021) obtained this from a regularized center of gravity, and Sakaue et al. (2025b) obtained it efficiently from an online Newton step, at $O(d^{2})$ per round after Sakaue (2026a) removed the Mahalanobis projection. Bounds uniform in $T$ were either exponential in $d$ — the John ellipsoid rule of Gollapudi et al. (2021), at $\exp(O(d\log d))$ — or required extra structure on the action sets (Sakaue et al., 2025a; Oki and Sakaue, 2026). Whether a finite $\operatorname{poly}(d)$ bound was achievable at all was answered affirmatively by Dewasurendra (2026), but by a rule that is neither proper nor efficient: it pools covers of the optimality-gap class across dyadic scales into a weighted vote whose finite implementation can involve $T^{\Theta(d)}$ tests and requires nonconvex optimization over the action set, without committing to a linear objective before seeing the menu.
先前的工作未能同时满足这两项要求。对于任意行动集，已有高效方法的最佳界为 $O(d\ln T)$，它具有多项式量级，但随时间跨度增长：Gollapudi et al. (2021) 通过正则化重心获得了这一界，Sakaue et al. (2025b) 则通过在线牛顿步高效地获得了这一界，而在 Sakaue (2026a) 去除马氏投影之后，每轮计算量降至 $O(d^{2})$。对所有 $T$ 一致成立的界要么关于 $d$ 呈指数增长——Gollapudi et al. (2021) 的 John 椭球规则给出的界为 $\exp(O(d\log d))$——要么要求行动集具有额外结构（Sakaue et al., 2025a; Oki and Sakaue, 2026）。Dewasurendra (2026) 肯定地回答了是否能够实现有限的 $\operatorname{poly}(d)$ 界这一问题，但所用规则既不恰当也不高效：它将最优性差距函数类在各个二进尺度上的覆盖汇集为加权投票，其有限实现可能涉及 $T^{\Theta(d)}$ 次检验，并且需要在行动集上进行非凸优化，而不会在看到可选行动集之前确定一个线性目标。
Theorem 1 (Cai et al., 2026a).定理 1（Cai et al., 2026a）。
There is a deterministic, proper, anytime algorithm for online inverse linear optimization with cumulative shortfall $R_{T}=O(d)$ , uniform in the horizon $T$ , using $O(d^{2})$ arithmetic and one linear optimization per round.
对于在线逆线性优化，存在一种确定性的、恰当的、无需预先知道时间跨度的算法，其累计差额为 $R_{T}=O(d)$，该界对时间跨度 $T$ 一致成立，且每轮使用 $O(d^{2})$ 次算术运算和一次线性优化。
This is the first bound of that order that is efficient, and the first that is proper. It is within a factor of $O(\sqrt{d})$ of the optimal bound: every algorithm suffers $\Omega(\sqrt{d})$ (Sakaue et al., 2025b).
这是首个由高效算法实现的这一量级的界，也是首个由恰当算法实现的这一量级的界。它与最优界相差至多 $O(\sqrt{d})$ 倍：任何算法都会承受 $\Omega(\sqrt{d})$ 的累计差额（Sakaue et al., 2025b）。
The proof builds on the variable-metric framework of Sakaue et al. (2025b), in which the learner runs online gradient descent under a metric $H_{t}$ that stretches along directions already queried. Two changes remove the logarithm. First, with $g_{t}=-v_{t}$ , the rank-one metric update $g_{t}g_{t}^{\top}$ is self-normalized, divided by the dual-metric length $s_{t}=\|g_{t}\|_{H_{t}^{-1}}$ of the observed direction. Second, the $\log\det H_{t}$ potential — which bounds the total squared step length $\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2}$ but grows like $d\log T$ , and is the source of the $\ln T$ in every earlier volumetric analysis — is replaced by the trace power $\operatorname{tr}(H_{t}^{-1/2})$ . That potential starts at $d$ , stays non-negative, and falls by at least $\tfrac{\tau}{4}s_{t}^{2}$ each round, where $\tau=\Theta(1/d)$ controls the metric update, so $\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2}\leq 4d/\tau=O(d^{2})$ , independently of the horizon. With the iterate step size $\alpha=\Theta(1/d)$ , the distance-contraction argument and the reduction then give $R_{T}=O(1/\alpha+\alpha\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2})=O(d)$ .
该证明建立在 Sakaue et al. (2025b) 的变度量框架之上；在该框架中，学习者在沿已查询方向伸展的度量 $H_{t}$ 下运行在线梯度下降。两项改动消除了对数因子。首先，令 $g_{t}=-v_{t}$，对秩一度量更新 $g_{t}g_{t}^{\top}$ 进行自归一化，即将其除以所观测方向在对偶度量下的长度 $s_{t}=\|g_{t}\|_{H_{t}^{-1}}$。其次，将势函数 $\log\det H_{t}$ 替换为矩阵幂的迹 $\operatorname{tr}(H_{t}^{-1/2})$；前者能够界定总平方步长 $\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2}$，但会以 $d\log T$ 的量级增长，且是此前所有基于体积的分析中 $\ln T$ 因子的来源。该势函数的初始值为 $d$，始终非负，并且每轮至少下降 $\tfrac{\tau}{4}s_{t}^{2}$，其中 $\tau=\Theta(1/d)$ 控制度量更新，因此 $\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2}\leq 4d/\tau=O(d^{2})$，与时间跨度无关。取迭代步长 $\alpha=\Theta(1/d)$，再结合距离收缩论证与上述归约，即可得到 $R_{T}=O(1/\alpha+\alpha\sum_{t}s_{t}^{2})=O(d)$。
The argument uses the expert’s optimality only to make each round legal, so the same bound holds when the expert merely does at least as well as the learner by its own criterion; this is the first $O(d)$ bound competing against an expert that does not optimize. The companion paper also gives corruption-robust and rank-adaptive variants, and an application to convex minimization: an $L$ -Lipschitz convex function with a minimizer within distance $R$ of the initial point can be minimized from subgradient directions alone with total suboptimality $O(dLR)$ over an infinite run.
该论证仅利用专家的最优性来保证每一轮满足博弈规则，因此，当专家仅按自身准则做到至少不逊于学习者时，同样的界仍然成立；这是首个在与不执行最优化的专家竞争时成立的 $O(d)$ 界。配套论文还给出了对数据损坏具有鲁棒性和秩自适应的变体，以及在凸最小化中的一项应用：对于一个 $L$-Lipschitz 凸函数，若其某个最小值点距初始点不超过 $R$，则仅凭次梯度方向即可对其进行最小化，并在无限运行过程中使累计次优性为 $O(dLR)$。
Subsequent to the companion paper, Sakaue (2026b) obtained a tight $O(\sqrt{d})$ bound. However, this algorithm is inefficient and it remains an open question to obtain an efficient algorith with $O(\sqrt{d})$ regret.
在配套论文发表之后，Sakaue (2026b) 得到了紧的 $O(\sqrt{d})$ 界。然而，该算法效率不高，如何获得遗憾为 $O(\sqrt{d})$ 的高效算法仍是一个开放问题。
The base model used for this problem was Gemini 3.1 Pro.
用于这一问题的基础模型是 Gemini 3.1 Pro。
3.2 Competition Complexity for Two-Sided Markets3.2 双边市场的竞争复杂度
The Bulow–Klemperer theorem (Bulow and Klemperer, 1994) establishes that in one-sided auctions, adding a single bidder to a simple mechanism yields at least the revenue of the optimal mechanism for the original market. A natural question is whether a Bulow–Klemperer-type result also holds for two-sided markets. This line of work was initiated by Babaioff et al. (2020a), who show that, when the buyers’ valuations first-order stochastically dominate the sellers’ costs, adding roughly $n(m+4\sqrt{m})$ buyers suffices for a prior-independent mechanism to have gains from trade (GFT) at least that of the first-best GFT, where $m$ is the number of buyers, $n$ the number of sellers, and $m\geq n$ . Following up on this, Cai et al. (2024) showed that if one augments both sides of the market then $O(1)$ agents suffice. Their analysis, however, requires a large constant, namely at least $20{,}000$ per side. At least two questions remained open, including (i) whether the constant can be improved and (ii) whether it is sufficient to recruit from only one side of the market.
Bulow–Klemperer 定理（Bulow and Klemperer, 1994）表明，在单边拍卖中，向一种简单机制增加一名竞标者，所获得的收入至少与原市场最优机制的收入相当。一个自然的问题是，双边市场是否也存在 Bulow–Klemperer 型结果。这一研究方向由 Babaioff et al. (2020a) 开创，他们证明，当买方估值一阶随机占优于卖方成本时，增加约 $n(m+4\sqrt{m})$ 名买方，就足以使一种不依赖先验的机制获得至少等于第一最佳交易收益的交易收益（gains from trade，GFT），其中 $m$ 为买方数量，$n$ 为卖方数量，且 $m\geq n$。在此基础上，Cai et al. (2024) 证明，如果同时扩充市场两侧，则增加 $O(1)$ 名参与者就足够了。然而，他们的分析要求一个很大的常数，即每侧至少增加 $20{,}000$ 名参与者。至少还有两个问题尚未解决，包括 (i) 能否改进这一常数，以及 (ii) 是否只从市场一侧招募参与者就足够。
Using Cogentic we proved the following theorem, which answers both questions simultaneously.
借助 Cogentic，我们证明了以下定理，同时回答了这两个问题。
Theorem 2 (Cai et al., 2026e).定理 2（Cai et al., 2026e）。
For any $m\geq n\geq 1$ , if the buyer distribution $F_{B}$ first-order stochastically dominates the seller distribution $F_{S}$ , then adding exactly two sellers — the smaller side of the market — suffices for Seller Trade Reduction to achieve expected gains from trade at least the first-best gains from trade of the original market:
对于任意 $m\geq n\geq 1$，如果买方分布 $F_{B}$ 一阶随机占优于卖方分布 $F_{S}$，那么恰好增加两名卖方（即市场中人数较少的一侧），就足以使 Seller Trade Reduction 机制实现至少等于原市场第一最佳交易收益的期望交易收益：
$$\operatorname{STR}(m,n+2)\;\geq\;\operatorname{OPT}(m,n).$$
Moreover this is tight: even for $m=n=1$ , recruiting one additional seller does not suffice for any prior-independent mechanism.
此外，这一结果是紧的：即使在 $m=n=1$ 时，对任何不依赖先验的机制而言，仅额外招募一名卖方也不够。
The constant drops from at least $20{,}000$ per side to $2$ on one side, and the recruitment is one-sided, so the mechanism designer need only find two more participants of the type already in shorter supply.
这一常数从每侧至少 $20{,}000$ 降至仅一侧的 $2$，而且招募只涉及一侧，因此机制设计者只需再找到两名属于原本较为稀缺类型的参与者。
The base model used for this problem was an early version of Gemini 4 Argon.

3.3 Anytime Regret with $n$ Experts3.3 $n$ 名专家情形下的任意时刻遗憾
In prediction with expert advice, a learner plays a distribution over $n$ experts, an adversary reveals a loss vector in $[0,1]^{n}$ , and the learner is charged its expected loss relative to the best single expert in hindsight. When the horizon $T$ is known, multiplicative weights tuned to $T$ guarantees regret $\sqrt{T\ln n/2}$ , and the leading constant $1/\sqrt{2}$ cannot be improved for large $n$ (Cesa-Bianchi et al., 1997). An anytime algorithm is given no horizon and must satisfy its bound at every $t$ simultaneously. The best known anytime guarantee for many experts was $\sqrt{t\ln n}$ , a factor $\sqrt{2}$ worse, and whether that factor was necessary had remained open. For $n=2$ , Luo and Schapire (2014) proved that the anytime regret is strictly larger than the fixed-time regret obtained by Cover (1966). Later, Harvey et al. (2023) gave the optimal anytime algorithm for $n=2$ but also conjectured that as $n\to\infty$ , the leading constant of the anytime regret and fixed-time regret would coincide. Some evidence towards this conjecture was given by Harvey et al. (2024), who proved that in continuous time, the anytime and fixed-time constants agree as $n\to\infty$ when the experts follow independent Brownian motions.

The proof Cogentic found is elementary. Run one multiplicative-weights instance for each horizon on a geometric grid $H^{m}=(1+\varepsilon)^{m}$ and aggregate them with a master multiplicative-weights algorithm. At any time $t$ some instance is tuned to a horizon in $[t,(1+\varepsilon)t]$ and so has regret $\sqrt{1+\varepsilon}\sqrt{t\ln n/2}$ ; it would suffice for the master to track that instance cheaply. The obstruction is that the pool grows with $t$ while the master’s regret grows with the number of instances it tracks, which would leave a time-dependent overhead in the leading constant. The construction keeps the pool bounded by waking instance $m$ only at round $\lfloor\delta H^{m}\rfloor$ and retiring it after round $\lfloor H^{m}\rfloor$ . This second count matters because when new instances arrive, we must allocate some of the existing probability mass to new instances. Retirement leaves the prefix before $\lfloor\delta H^{m}\rfloor$ uncovered, and recursing on it contributes a geometric series $1+\sqrt{\delta}+\delta+\cdots$ . 3.5 Price of Anarchy for Autobidding Auctions3.5 自动出价拍卖的无政府代价
Automated bidding (autobidding) is now a widely adopted interface for advertisers to bid into high frequency ad auctions. In this interface, advertisers specify high level constraints, such as return-on-spend and budget constraints. To measure efficiency, the literature uses a standard metric known as the price of anarchy (PoA) which is the ratio of the welfare in the optimal allocation and the worst-case equilibrium welfare. Aggarwal et al. (2019) initiated this line of work, showing that the second-price auction (SPA) achieves a tight PoA of $2$ . Subsequent work by Liaw et al. (2023) proved that no deterministic mechanism—including the first-price auction (FPA)—can beat the PoA barrier of $2$ in the prior-free setting, even for two bidders. When randomization is combined with non-truthful payments, however, the barrier of $2$ can be broken for two bidders: Mehta (2022) achieved a two-bidder PoA of approximately $1.89$ via a randomized truthful auction, and Liaw et al. (2023) improved the two-bidder upper bound to $1.8$ using a randomized first-price auction, while proving an $n$ -bidder lower bound (for even $n$ ) showing that every anonymous mechanism has $\mathrm{PoA}\geq\frac{2n+4}{n+4}=2-\frac{4}{n+4}$ . At least two questions remain open: (i) what is the exact minimax optimal PoA for $n=2$ bidders, and (ii) for general $n\geq 3$ bidders, whether any mechanism can break the deterministic PoA barrier of $2$ and match the $2-\Theta(1/n)$ lower bound.
自动出价（autobidding）现已成为广告主参与高频广告拍卖时广泛采用的一种交互方式。在这种交互方式中，广告主指定高层次的约束，例如支出回报率约束和预算约束。为衡量效率，相关文献采用一种称为无政府代价（price of anarchy，PoA）的标准指标，即最优分配的福利与最坏情形下均衡福利的比值。Aggarwal et al. (2019) 开创了这一研究方向，证明了第二价格拍卖（SPA）的 PoA 为 $2$，且该界是紧的。Liaw et al. (2023) 的后续研究证明，在无先验设定下，任何确定性机制——包括第一价格拍卖（FPA）——都无法突破 PoA 为 $2$ 的障碍，即使只有两个竞价者也是如此。然而，当随机化与非真实支付相结合时，对于两个竞价者便可以突破 $2$ 的障碍：Mehta (2022) 通过一种随机化真实拍卖，实现了约为 $1.89$ 的双竞价者 PoA；Liaw et al. (2023) 则利用随机化第一价格拍卖将双竞价者情形的上界改进至 $1.8$，同时证明了一个适用于 $n$ 个竞价者的下界（其中 $n$ 为偶数），表明每一种匿名机制都满足 $\mathrm{PoA}\geq\frac{2n+4}{n+4}=2-\frac{4}{n+4}$。至少还有两个问题尚未解决：(i) 对于 $n=2$ 个竞价者，精确的极小极大最优 PoA 是多少；(ii) 对于一般的 $n\geq 3$ 个竞价者，是否存在某种机制能够突破确定性机制 PoA 为 $2$ 的障碍，并达到 $2-\Theta(1/n)$ 的下界。
