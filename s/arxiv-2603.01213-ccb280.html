Can AI Agents Agree?AI 智能体能达成一致吗？
Abstract摘要
Large language models are increasingly deployed as cooperating agents, yet their behavior in adversarial consensus settings has not been systematically studied. We evaluate LLM-based agents on a Byzantine consensus game over scalar values using a synchronous all-to-all simulation. We test consensus in a no-stake setting where agents have no preferences over the final value, so evaluation focuses on agreement rather than value optimality. Across hundreds of simulations spanning model sizes, group sizes, and Byzantine fractions, we find that valid agreement is not reliable even in benign settings and degrades as group size grows. Introducing a small number of Byzantine agents further reduces success. Failures are dominated by loss of liveness, such as timeouts and stalled convergence, rather than subtle value corruption. Overall, the results suggest that reliable agreement is not yet a dependable emergent capability of current LLM-agent groups even in no-stake settings, raising caution for deployments that rely on robust coordination.
大型语言模型日益被部署为相互协作的智能体，但其在对抗性共识环境中的行为尚未得到系统研究。我们通过同步全互联仿真，在标量值的拜占庭共识博弈中评估基于 LLM 的智能体。我们在无利益关联的设定下测试共识，其中智能体对最终值没有偏好，因此评估侧重于一致性，而非值的最优性。通过涵盖不同模型规模、群体规模和拜占庭智能体比例的数百次仿真，我们发现，即使在无恶意行为的设定下，也无法可靠地达成有效一致，而且随着群体规模增大，表现会进一步下降。引入少量拜占庭智能体会进一步降低成功率。失败主要源于活性（liveness）丧失，例如超时和收敛停滞，而非隐蔽的数值篡改。总体而言，结果表明，即使在无利益关联的设定下，可靠地达成一致也尚未成为当前 LLM 智能体群体中值得依赖的涌现能力，因此，对于依赖稳健协调的部署应保持谨慎。

1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents that collaborate on planning, coding, reasoning, and many other tasks. In some applications, a group of agents must reach a common decision rather than producing independent answers. When some agents malfunction or behave strategically, the ability of the group to still reach agreement becomes an explicit design requirement rather than a convenience. Classical Byzantine fault-tolerant consensus provides strong guarantees for deterministic algorithms (Pease et al., 1980; Lamport et al., 1982), but it is unclear how these guarantees translate when decisions are produced by stochastic, prompt-driven LLMs. We therefore study a simple no-stake scalar consensus task in which a group of agents repeatedly propose values and exchange messages over a synchronous all-to-all network, and in which agents do not have specific preferences over the final value. A subset of agents may behave in a Byzantine fashion, arbitrarily attempting to disrupt or bias consensus. Each agent is implemented as an LLM-driven policy that receives a compact textual history and outputs a proposal and justification. Fig. 1 illustrates the game structure.
大型语言模型日益被部署为自主智能体，在规划、编程、推理以及许多其他任务中开展协作。在某些应用中，一组智能体必须达成共同决策，而非各自给出独立答案。当某些智能体发生故障或采取策略性行为时，群体仍能达成一致的能力就成为一项明确的设计要求，而不再仅仅是便利性功能。经典的拜占庭容错共识为确定性算法提供了强有力的保证 (Pease et al., 1980; Lamport et al., 1982)，但当决策由具有随机性且由提示驱动的 LLM 生成时，这些保证如何适用尚不明确。因此，我们研究一项简单的、无利益关联的标量共识任务：一组智能体在同步全互联网络上反复提出数值并交换消息，且智能体对最终值没有特定偏好。其中一部分智能体可能表现出拜占庭行为，以任意方式试图破坏共识或使其产生偏向。每个智能体均实现为由 LLM 驱动的策略，接收精简的文本历史记录，并输出提议及其理由。图 1 展示了该博弈的结构。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
LLMs are increasingly deployed as interacting agents (Li et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024), and recent work documents common multi-agent failure patterns (Cemri et al., 2025). Several approaches target robustness to malicious agents via coordination mechanisms (Chen et al., 2024; Jo and Park, 2025). Closest to our setting, Chen et al. (2023) study benign numeric consensus via LLM negotiation and analyze the effects of agent number, personality, and topology. Our focus differs in three ways. First, we introduce a controlled Byzantine fraction. Second, we enforce a validity constraint requiring the decided value to be an initial honest proposal. Third, we separate validity from liveness and show that failures are dominated by liveness loss. Complementary work frames multi-agent reliability through a Byzantine fault-tolerance lens and proposes aggregation mechanisms such as confidence-weighted consensus (Zheng et al., 2025). Concurrently, Grötschla et al. (2025) also observe that multi-agent LLM coordination degrades as network size increases. In a Mixture-of-Agents setting, Wolf et al. (2025) similarly find that a single deceptive agent suffices to nullify performance gains.
LLM 日益被部署为相互交互的智能体 (Li et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024)，近期研究记录了常见的多智能体失败模式 (Cemri et al., 2025)。多种方法通过协调机制来提高对恶意智能体的稳健性 (Chen et al., 2024; Jo and Park, 2025)。与我们的设定最为接近的是，Chen et al. (2023) 通过 LLM 协商研究无恶意行为下的数值共识，并分析智能体数量、个性和拓扑结构的影响。我们的研究重点在三个方面有所不同。首先，我们引入可控的拜占庭智能体比例。其次，我们施加有效性约束，要求最终决定的值必须是某个诚实智能体的初始提议。第三，我们将有效性与活性区分开来，并表明失败主要源于活性丧失。作为补充，另有研究从拜占庭容错的视角考察多智能体可靠性，并提出置信度加权共识等聚合机制 (Zheng et al., 2025)。同期，Grötschla et al. (2025) 也观察到，多智能体 LLM 的协调能力随网络规模增大而下降。在 Mixture-of-Agents 设定中，Wolf et al. (2025) 同样发现，单个具有欺骗性的智能体就足以抵消性能增益。

3 Method3 方法
Setting.设定。
We consider $N$ agents communicating over a synchronous all-to-all network for rounds $t=1,\ldots,T_{\max}$ . A fraction $f\in[0,\nicefrac{{1}}{{3}}]$ of agents are Byzantine, with $B=f\cdot N$ the number of Byzantine agents. Each honest agent $i$ maintains a scalar proposal $\smash{v_{i}^{(t)}\in[0,50]}$ , initialized at $t=0$ by sampling i.i.d. from a fixed distribution over $[0,50]$ (uniform in our implementation), whereas Byzantine agents do not have an initial proposal and may choose arbitrary values in each round. The goal of the protocol is to find agreement among honest agents through a structured exchange.
我们考虑 $N$ 个智能体，它们在同步全互连网络上进行通信，轮次为 $t=1,\ldots,T_{\max}$。其中，比例为 $f\in[0,\nicefrac{{1}}{{3}}]$ 的智能体是拜占庭智能体，其数量为 $B=f\cdot N$。每个诚实智能体 $i$ 维护一个标量提议 $\smash{v_{i}^{(t)}\in[0,50]}$，在 $t=0$ 时通过从 $[0,50]$ 上的固定分布（在我们的实现中为均匀分布）中进行独立同分布采样来初始化，而拜占庭智能体没有初始提议，并且可以在每一轮选择任意值。该协议的目标是通过结构化的信息交换，使诚实智能体达成一致。
Protocol.协议。
Our simulator (A2A-Sim) shown in Fig. 1 delivers structured messages over a synchronous network. We present the full pseudo-code in Appendix A.2 and describe the prompting strategy in Appendix A.3.
图 1 所示的模拟器（A2A-Sim）通过同步网络传递结构化消息。我们在附录 A.2 中给出完整伪代码，并在附录 A.3 中描述提示策略。
Termination and outcomes.终止与结果。
The simulator declares termination when at least $2/3$ of all agents vote to stop; otherwise, the simulator times out at $t=T_{\max}$ and we declare no consensus. At termination, the game yields: (i) valid consensus (all honest agents hold identical values drawn from the initial honest proposals); (ii) invalid consensus (termination without validity); or (iii) no consensus (timeout). This is a no-stake agreement game: the final agreement value carries no external reward for any of the agents, and evaluation focuses on agreement properties rather than optimizing the value.
当至少 $2/3$ 的智能体投票停止时，模拟器宣告终止；否则，模拟器在 $t=T_{\max}$ 时超时，我们判定为未达成共识。游戏结束时会产生以下结果之一：(i) 有效共识（所有诚实智能体持有相同的值，且该值取自诚实智能体的初始提议）；(ii) 无效共识（已终止，但不满足有效性）；或 (iii) 未达成共识（超时）。这是一个无利益关联的一致性博弈：最终达成一致的值不会为任何智能体带来外部奖励，评估关注的是一致性属性，而非对该值进行优化。
Threat model.威胁模型。
For simplicity, we consider a restricted Byzantine model. Byzantine agents may propose arbitrary values and justifications and adapt to the history, but they cannot equivocate (send different messages to different recipients), forge identities, or drop or suppress messages. In each round, a Byzantine agent must send the same message to all recipients. We demonstrate that even this straightforward threat model can have a substantial impact on consensus performance.
为简单起见，我们考虑一种受限的拜占庭模型。拜占庭智能体可以提出任意值和理由，并根据历史记录调整行为，但不能进行歧义发送（向不同接收者发送不同消息）、伪造身份，也不能丢弃或阻止消息传递。在每一轮中，拜占庭智能体必须向所有接收者发送相同的消息。我们证明，即使是这种简单的威胁模型，也会对共识性能产生显著影响。
LLM agent implementation.LLM 智能体的实现。
Unless otherwise noted, all experiments use a common configuration. Each agent uses the same prompt template specifying its role and inputs: local summary, current proposal, and private strategy from the previous round. The agent must return a JSON-formatted output containing a scalar proposal, a free-text justification, and a termination decision. We pass a compact textual summary of previous rounds rather than the full raw history to stay within the context window. The summary includes, for the previous round, each agent’s scalar proposal and a truncated justification, along with the focal agent’s current proposal and private strategy.
除非另有说明，所有实验均使用同一套配置。每个智能体使用相同的提示模板，指定其角色和输入：局部摘要、当前提议以及上一轮的私有策略。智能体必须返回 JSON 格式的输出，其中包含一个标量提议、一段自由文本形式的理由以及一个终止决策。为避免超出上下文窗口，我们传入前几轮的精简文本摘要，而非完整的原始历史记录。摘要包含上一轮中每个智能体的标量提议和截断后的理由，以及当前智能体的当前提议和私有策略。
Evaluation.评估。
For each configuration we run 25 independent simulations and record outcome rates (valid, invalid, premature stop, no consensus), rounds to termination, and consensus quality. We use $T_{\max}=50$ for all experiments. Outcome rates are reported with 95% Wilson confidence intervals over 25 runs (Wilson, 1927). We test agents based on the Qwen3-8B/14B model family (Yang et al., 2025).
对于每种配置，我们运行 25 次独立模拟，并记录各类结果的发生率（有效共识、无效共识、提前停止、未达成共识）、终止所需轮数以及共识质量。所有实验均使用 $T_{\max}=50$。我们报告各类结果的发生率，并给出基于 25 次运行计算的 95% Wilson 置信区间（Wilson, 1927）。我们测试了基于 Qwen3-8B/14B 模型系列的智能体（Yang et al., 2025）。
4 Experiments and Results4 实验与结果
4.1 Consensus without Byzantine agents4.1 无拜占庭智能体时的共识
We first study whether LLM-agent groups can reliably solve the scalar consensus game in benign conditions ( $f=B=0$ ). We run 600 simulations using A2A-Sim, varying agents $N\in\{4,8,16\}$ and evaluating Qwen3-8B and 14B under two prompt variants: one mentioning that Byzantine agents may exist and one omitting any reference to Byzantine agents (see Appendix A.3 for more details). Even without Byzantine agents, only 41.6% of runs terminate in valid consensus. Qwen3-14B substantially outperforms Qwen3-8B (67.4% vs. 15.8%), yet both exhibit high timeout rates. Removing any mention of Byzantine agents from the prompt improves Qwen3-14B performance from 59.1% to 75.4% valid consensus and halves convergence time, suggesting that mentioning possible Byzantine agents harms liveness even when none are present. Increasing group size further degrades performance: valid consensus drops from 46.6% at $N{=}4$ to 33.3% at $N{=}16$ . Fig. 2 summarizes outcomes across conditions.
我们首先研究 LLM 智能体群体能否在良性条件（$f=B=0$）下可靠地解决标量共识博弈。我们使用 A2A-Sim 运行了 600 次模拟，改变智能体数量 $N\in\{4,8,16\}$，并在两种提示变体下评估 Qwen3-8B 和 14B：一种提及可能存在拜占庭智能体，另一种完全不提及拜占庭智能体（更多细节见附录 A.3）。即使没有拜占庭智能体，也只有 41.6% 的运行以有效共识终止。Qwen3-14B 的表现显著优于 Qwen3-8B（67.4% 对 15.8%），但两者的超时率都很高。从提示中删除所有对拜占庭智能体的提及后，Qwen3-14B 的有效共识率从 59.1% 提升至 75.4%，收敛时间缩短了一半，这表明，即使实际上不存在拜占庭智能体，提及其可能存在也会损害活性。增大群体规模会进一步降低性能：有效共识率从 $N{=}4$ 时的 46.6% 降至 $N{=}16$ 时的 33.3%。图 2 汇总了各条件下的结果。

5 Discussion and Conclusion5 讨论与结论
Even in benign, no-stake settings without Byzantine agents, LLM-agent groups frequently fail to reach valid consensus within the round limit, and performance declines as group size increases. Under adversarial conditions, the likelihood of valid consensus decreases further, with failures primarily resulting from no-consensus outcomes, even within our limited threat model. These findings indicate that current LLM agents are not yet reliable social decision-makers: agreement, which is essential for cooperation, delegation, and safety-critical coordination, remains fragile in our controlled, no-stake testbed. Our study is limited by testing only a single Byzantine strategy and two model sizes from one family, and future work should investigate diverse adversarial behaviors, heterogeneous agent populations, and larger-scale deployments. We hope this work will inspire further research to address in greater depth the fundamental question of whether AI agents can reliably achieve agreement.
即使在没有拜占庭智能体、无利害关系的良性环境中，LLM 智能体群体也经常无法在轮次上限内达成有效共识，而且随着群体规模增大，性能会下降。在对抗条件下，达成有效共识的可能性进一步降低，即使在我们有限的威胁模型内，失败也主要源于未能达成共识。这些发现表明，当前的 LLM 智能体尚不能成为可靠的社会决策者：达成一致对于合作、委托和安全关键协调至关重要，但在我们受控且无利害关系的测试环境中，这一能力仍然脆弱。本研究的局限在于仅测试了单一拜占庭策略以及同一模型系列中的两种模型规模，未来工作应研究多样化的对抗行为、异构智能体群体和更大规模的部署。我们希望这项工作能够激发进一步研究，更深入地探讨 AI 智能体能否可靠地达成一致这一根本问题。
