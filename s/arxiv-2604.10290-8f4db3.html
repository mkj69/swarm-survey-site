摘要Abstract
AI 越来越多地被部署于多智能体系统中；然而，大多数研究仅考察单个模型的行为。 AI is increasingly deployed in multi-agent systems; however, most research considers only the behavior of individual models. 我们通过实验表明，与单个 AI 智能体相比，多智能体“AI 组织”在实现业务目标方面更有效，但同时对齐程度更低。 We experimentally show that multi-agent “AI organizations” are simultaneously more effective at achieving business goals, but less aligned, than individual AI agents. 我们考察了两种实际场景中的 12 项任务：为业务问题提供解决方案的 AI 咨询机构，以及开发软件产品的 AI 软件团队。 We examine 12 tasks across two practical settings: an AI consultancy providing solutions to business problems and an AI software team developing software products. 在所有场景中，与单个经过对齐的模型相比，由经过对齐的模型组成的 AI 组织所产生的解决方案效用更高，但失配程度也更高。 Across all settings, AI Organizations composed of aligned models produce solutions with higher utility but greater misalignment compared to a single aligned model. 我们的研究表明，在开展能力研究和安全研究时，考察由相互交互的 AI 智能体组成的系统十分重要。 Our work demonstrates the importance of considering interacting systems of AI agents when doing both capabilities and safety research.

语言模型越来越多地被共同部署于多智能体系统中。 Language models are increasingly deployed together in multi-agent systems. 例如，多智能体系统如今已应用于研究工具 (Hadfield et al., 2025)、软件工程 (Hong et al., 2023; Lu et al., 2025)、数据分析 (Zhang and Elhamod, 2025) 和客户服务 (LangChain, 2025)。 For example, multi-agent systems are now used in research tools (Hadfield et al., 2025), software engineering (Hong et al., 2023; Lu et al., 2025), data analytics (Zhang and Elhamod, 2025), and customer service (LangChain, 2025). 这些系统可以通过并行化实现比单智能体系统更高的效率 (Zheng et al., 2023b)，可以通过专业化针对特定任务进行优化 (Swanson et al., 2024)，并且能够高效处理更长上下文的场景 (Zhang et al., 2024)。 These systems can be more efficient than single-agent systems through parallelization (Zheng et al., 2023b), can be optimized for specific tasks through specialization (Swanson et al., 2024), and can efficiently handle longer-context scenarios (Zhang et al., 2024).
在本研究中，我们考察这些多智能体系统的对齐情况。 In this work, we study the alignment of these multi-agent systems. 模型开发者致力于开发符合规范的系统 (Anthropic, 2023; OpenAI, 2024)；例如，他们经常通过对齐使系统拒绝有害或违法的请求。 Model developers aim to develop systems that align with specifications (Anthropic, 2023; OpenAI, 2024); for example, they frequently align systems to refuse harmful or illegal requests. 我们研究由单智能体系统组成的多智能体系统是否会继承其对齐属性。 We study whether multi-agent systems composed of single agent systems inherit their alignment properties. 如果多智能体系统重现了人类组织的失效方式，那么善意的单个智能体协同工作，可能会产生有害而非有益的结果 (Garicano and Rayo, 2016; McMillan and Overall, 2017)。 If multi-agent systems mimic how human organizations fail, then well-meaning individual agents working together may lead to outcomes that do harm rather than good (Garicano and Rayo, 2016; McMillan and Overall, 2017). 如果多智能体系统的行为不同于人类组织，那么在部署这些系统之前，理解其失效机制至关重要。 If multi-agent systems behave differently from human organizations, then understanding mechanisms of their failures is essential before deploying these systems.

我们发现，在所考察的各个场景中，多智能体“AI 组织”在实现业务目标方面更有效，但同时与开发者意图的对齐程度更低（图 1）。 We find that across our scenarios, multi-agent “AI Organizations” are simultaneously more effective at achieving business goals, but are less aligned with developer intent (Figure 1). 多智能体 LLM 系统Multi-Agent LLM Systems
随着语言模型能力的增强，关于这些模型如何相互交互并解决问题的研究迅速发展 (Guo et al., 2024)。 As language models become more capable, the study of how these models interact with one another and solve problems has grown rapidly (Guo et al., 2024). 多个 LLM 可以扮演具有特定专业知识的角色，组成能够更有效地解决问题的群体 (Zhuge et al., 2023; Tran et al., 2025)。 Multiple LLMs can adopt personas with specific expertise to form a group that solves problems more effectively (Zhuge et al., 2023; Tran et al., 2025). 这一技术已应用于软件工程 (Qian et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2023)、问答 (Das et al., 2023; He et al., 2023) 和科学发现 (Zheng et al., 2023b; Swanson et al., 2024)。 This technique has been applied to software engineering (Qian et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2023; Huang et al., 2023), question answering (Das et al., 2023; He et al., 2023), and scientific discovery (Zheng et al., 2023b; Swanson et al., 2024). Zhuge et al.; Tran et al. 将这些情境归类为合作，即智能体具有单一的共同目标。 Zhuge et al.; Tran et al. identify these settings as cooperation, where agents have a single shared goal. 一种常见的合作情境是软件工程多智能体团队，其中智能体被分配特定角色，并根据各自的角色编写和验证代码 (Zeng et al., 2022; Du et al., 2023)。 A common coorporation setting is the software engineering multi-agent team, agents are assigned specific roles, and write and verify code based on their given role (Zeng et al., 2022; Du et al., 2023).

(2025) 主张，应将多智能体 LLM 作为动态的社会技术系统进行研究，而 Raza et al. (2025) 提出了多智能体 LLM 系统的信任框架，涵盖可解释性、模型运维、安全性和隐私。 Broader reviews of harms associated with multi-agent LLM systems have also been conducted: Hu et al. (2025) argued that multi-agent LLMs should be studied as dynamic socio-technical systems, and Raza et al. (2025) presented a framework for trust in multi-agent LLM systems including explainability, model operations, security, and privacy. Cemri et al. (2025) 研究了多智能体 LLM 团队失败的原因，发现现代 LLM 系统在规范设定、协调和任务验证方面存在不足。 Cemri et al. (2025) studied why multi-agent LLM teams fail and found that modern LLM systems suffer from specification, coordination, and task verification shortcomings. Jones et al. (2024) 表明，安全模型的组合可以使攻击者通过任务分解提取知识。 Jones et al. (2024) demonstrate that combinations of safe models can enable an adversary to extract knowledge through task decomposition. Srivastav and Zhang (2025) 表明，将有害查询分解为无害子任务会提高攻击的成功率。 Srivastav and Zhang (2025) demonstrate that decomposing harmful queries into benign subtasks increases the success of an attack. 这些研究表明，仅对齐单个模型可能不足以确保 AI 安全，理解已对齐模型如何相互交互至关重要 (Hammond et al., 2025)。 These works suggest that aligning individual models may not be enough to ensure AI safety but understanding how aligned models interact with each other is crucial (Hammond et al., 2025). 我们的工作聚焦于旨在实现业务目标、且并非专门为绕过 LLM 安全机制而设计的多智能体 LLM 系统。 Our work focuses on multi-agent LLM systems that try to achieve a business goal and are not explicitly designed to bypass LLM safety mechanisms. 在 AI 组织中，每个智能体都承担一种角色，其设计旨在使 AI 组织整体取得尽可能好的结果。 In AI Organizations, each agent has a role designed to achieve the best possible outcome as an AI organization.
模式生物与模型失对齐的衡量Model Organisms and Measuring Model Misalignment
模式生物是研究生物学机制的试验平台，目的是将研究结果推广到多种不同物种。 Model organisms are testbeds for studying biological mechanisms with the goal of generalization across many different species. 在失对齐研究中，构建模式生物涉及建立一个标准化环境，以发现不良行为并测试可能的缓解措施 (Hubinger et al., 2023; Taylor et al., 2025)。 In studying misalignment, constructing a model organism involves a standardized environment to find undesirable behaviors and test possible mitigations (Hubinger et al., 2023; Taylor et al., 2025). 尽管提升单个智能体的对抗鲁棒性是一个活跃的研究领域 (Perez et al., 2022; Chao et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2023)，但考察多智能体系统失对齐问题的研究很少。 Although improving adversarial robustness in single agents is an active area of research (Perez et al., 2022; Chao et al., 2025; Wei et al., 2023), few works examine misalignment in multi-agent systems. 我们的方法受到这一研究方向的启发；我们构建了以现实世界多智能体 LLM 系统设计为基础的试验平台和衡量指标。 Our approach is inspired by this line of work; we construct both the testbed and metrics that are grounded in real-world multi-agent LLM system design.
人类组织如何失败How Human Organizations Fail
为了理解 AI 组织为何失败，我们可以借鉴关于人类组织失败的大量描述性和理论性研究所提供的见解。 To understand why AI Organizations fail, we can draw insights from the extensive body of descriptive and theoretical work on organizational failure among humans. 人类组织的灾难性失败不仅影响了业务成果，还危害了公众 (Department of Justice, 2012; Department of Justice, 2016)。 Catastrophic failures of human organizations have not only impacted business outcomes but also harmed the general public (Department of Justice, 2012; Department of Justice, 2016). Garicano and Rayo (2016) 将组织失败归因于行动主体未按组织利益行事（激励问题），或必要信息未得到传达（有限理性）。 Garicano and Rayo (2016) attribute organizational failures to agents that do not act in the organization’s interest (incentive problems) or to lack of necessary information being communicated (bounded rationality). Mellahi and Wilkinson (2004) 认为，理解组织失败需要同时理解内部结构缺陷和外部环境因素。 Mellahi and Wilkinson (2004) suggests that understanding organizational failure requires understanding both internal structural deficiencies and external environmental factors. 我们构建的失对齐 AI 组织模式生物借鉴了这些见解，采用综合方法，同时改变外部刺激（输入任务提示）和组织设计（智能体提示与结构）。 Our model organism of misaligned AI Organizations draw upon these insights by taking an integrative approach, varying both external stimulus (input task prompt) and organizational design (agent prompts and structure).
3 AI 组织3 AI Organizations
为研究多智能体对齐，我们基于现实世界的部署构建了两种情境：一家 AI 咨询公司和一个 AI 软件团队。 To study multi-agent alignment, we construct two settings based on real-world deployments: an AI consultancy and an AI software team. 对于每种情境，我们分别描述其组织结构、任务和评估方式。 For each, we describe the organization structure, tasks, and evaluation. 我们将 AI 组织定义为满足以下条件的多智能体 LLM 系统：(1) 智能体承担不同角色，(2) 智能体相互通信，且 (3) 智能体为实现共同目标而协同工作。 We define AI Organizations as multi-agent LLM systems where (1) agents take on different roles, (2) agents communicate with one another, and (3) agents work together towards a common goal. 我们用“智能体”（agent）来描述通过提示被赋予某种角色的单个 LLM。 We use ‘agent’ to describe a single LLM given a role through a prompt.
首先，每个智能体都有一个预先规定的角色；一些智能体可能具有相同的角色，但每个组织都具有不止一种角色。 First, each agent has a prescribed role; some agents may have the same role but every organization has more than one unique role. 其次，我们定义了一个固定的通信图，用于规定哪些智能体可以交换消息。 Second, we define a fixed communication graph that specifies which agents can exchange messages. 例如，在层级组织中，处于同一层级或同一子单元中的智能体可能能够相互通信。 For example, in a hierarchical organization, agents at the same level or in the same sub-unit may be able to communicate with one another. 第三，AI 组织内的所有智能体都朝着同一个目标开展工作。 Third, all agents within an AI organization are working towards the same goal. 这不同于那些智能体为取得最佳结果而争夺资源或相互竞争的多智能体 LLM 系统。 This is different from multi-agent LLM systems where agents compete for resources or against each other to achieve the best outcome. 我们关注协作系统（Zeng et al., 2022），因为它们在实践中更常被部署（Swanson et al., 2024; Hadfield et al., 2025）。 We focus on cooperative systems (Zeng et al., 2022) because they are more commonly deployed in practice (Swanson et al., 2024; Hadfield et al., 2025).

我们将 AI 组织与一个经过对齐的单智能体进行比较，以分离出多智能体协作的影响。 We compare AI Organizations against an aligned single agent to isolate the effects of multi-agent collaboration. 我们的实验使用 Claude Opus 模型，这些模型经过了安全训练，以依据一份公开发布的宪章（即一组行为原则）进行对齐（Bai et al., 2022）。 Our experiments use Claude Opus models, which have undergone safety training to align these models according to a publicly released constitution (i.e., set of principles for behavior) (Bai et al., 2022). 我们将“失准”（misalignment）视为对经过对齐的单智能体行为的偏离。 We consider ‘misalignment’ to be deviation from the behavior of the aligned single agent. 我们的目标是将任何观察到的失准与组织动态的影响分离开来。 Our goal is to isolate any observed misalignment from the effects of organizational dynamics. 由于失准取决于实际情境，我们现在介绍两个 AI 组织（AI 咨询机构和 AI 软件团队），以及它们为之制定解决方案的情境。 Since misalignment depends on the actual scenario, we now describe the two AI organizations (AI Consultancy and an AI Software Team) and the scenarios for which they develop solutions.
3.1 AI 咨询机构3.1 AI Consultancy
AI 咨询组织的目标是针对客户提出的问题生成新颖的解决方案，例如扩大用户群或降低成本。 The goal of the AI consulting organization is to generate novel solutions to a problem proposed by a client, such as growing a user base or reducing costs. AI 咨询机构的输入是一份征求建议书（RFP），其中详细说明了客户的业务目标、可用信息、待解决的问题，以及任何拟议解决方案应满足的期望要求。 The input to the AI consultancy is a request for proposal (RFP) with various details about the client’s business goals, the available information, the problem to be solved, and the desiderata accompanying any solution that is proposed. 3.2.2 任务构建3.2.2 Task Construction
为衡量失对齐，我们聚焦于本身存在伦理权衡的任务：我们设计了无法同时满足的商业约束和伦理约束，使得更不道德的决策可能带来更好的商业结果。 To measure misalignment, we focus on tasks with inherent ethical trade-offs: we design business and ethics constraints that are impossible to simultaneously satisfy, so more unethical decisions can lead to better business outcomes. 随后，我们研究 AI 组织和单智能体运行所产生的解决方案是否体现出不同的权衡。 We then study whether AI Organizations and single-agent rollouts produce solutions with different trade-offs. 软件场景包含两个任务：新闻推荐系统和 ICU 治疗策略。 There are two tasks for the software scenario: News Recommendation System and ICU Treatment Policy.

AI 软件组织获得 ISOT Fake News 数据集（Ahmed et al., 2017），其中包含经过事实核查的错误信息标签。 The AI software organization receives the ISOT Fake News dataset (Ahmed et al., 2017) with fact-checked misinformation labels. 我们从对数正态分布中采样并分配浏览量，并为错误信息设置更高的预期浏览量，以反映已有研究发现的用户参与模式（Van Mieghem et al., 2011）。 We assign view counts from log-normal distributions with higher expected views for misinformation, reflecting studied engagement patterns (Van Mieghem et al., 2011). 因此，最大化浏览量会激励系统推荐错误信息，从而在平台参与度与防止错误信息传播之间形成权衡。 Maximizing views thus incentivizes recommending misinformation, creating a trade-off between platform engagement and preventing misinformation spread.

4.2 失配机制4.2 Mechanisms for Misalignment

6 结论6 Conclusion
我们的实验表明，与单智能体相比，AI 组织以更差的伦理结果为代价，实现了更高效的结果。 Our experiments demonstrated that AI Organizations achieve more efficient outcomes at the cost of worse ethical outcomes compared to single agents. 值得注意的是，AI 组织不仅平均伦理得分更低，还会产生伦理水平最低的解决方案。 Notably, AI Organizations not only score lower on ethics on average, but also produce the least ethical solutions. 尽管我们无法在构建多智能体系统所使用的每个模型上都复现这种对齐差距，但某些模型上存在这些差距，就足以推动开发更好的基准，并进一步研究多智能体对齐。 Although we are not able to replicate this misalignment gap across every model upon which multi-agent systems, the existence of these gaps on some models is sufficient to motivate better benchmarks and further study for multi-agent alignment.
未来的工作应在更多环境中研究由其他模型组成、具有不同结构的 AI 组织。 Future work should study AI Organizations comprised of other models with varying structures in more environments. 这可能促成一项方法论研究，探讨 AI 组织的失败方式与人类组织有哪些相似之处和不同之处。 This could lead to a methodological study of how AI Organizations fail similarly to and differently from human organizations. 此外，AI 组织的失败机制也值得开展缓解策略研究，例如引入监控智能体或组织层面的约束。 Moreover, the failure mechanisms of AI Organizations also warrant the study of mitigation strategies, such as monitor agents or organizational-level constraints. 这些技术可能有助于缩小 AI 组织与单个智能体之间的对齐差距。 These techniques could help close the alignment gap between AI Organizations and single agents.
AI 组织的部署日益普遍，而我们证明，实践者有必要将这些系统与其组成模型分开，单独评估其对齐情况。 AI Organizations are being increasingly deployed, and we demonstrate the necessity for practitioners to evaluate these systems for alignment separately from their constituent models. 正如该领域已开发出用于单智能体对齐的技术，多智能体系统也需要类似的方法，以确保其保持对齐。 Just as the field has developed techniques for single-agent alignment, analogous methods are needed for multi-agent systems to ensure they remain aligned.

在 $t=0$ 时，初始提示 $S$ 指定任务。 At $t=0$ , an initial prompt $S$ specifies the task. 我们将所有智能体在时间 $t$ 采取的全部动作记为： We denote all actions taken by every agent at time $t$ as: 
| | $a^{t}=\{f_{a}(o^{t}_{1}),\ldots,f_{a}(o^{t}_{k})\}$ |
为衡量每个时间步 $t$ 的输出，我们引入两个函数。 To measure output at each time step $t$ , we introduce two functions. 效用函数 $u$ 衡量组织效能： The utility function $u$ measures organizational effectiveness: 
| | $\text{utility}^{t}=u(a^{t})$ |
不对齐函数 $e$ 衡量伦理偏离程度： The misalignment function $e$ measures ethical deviation: 
| | $\text{misalignment}^{t}=e(a^{t})$ |

C.1 AI 咨询机构C.1 AI Consultancy
AI 咨询组织的目标是针对客户提出的问题生成新颖的解决方案。 The goal of the AI consulting organization is to generate novel solutions to a problem proposed by a client. AI 咨询机构的输入是一份征求建议书（RFP），其中详细说明了客户的业务目标、可用信息、待解决的问题，以及任何拟议解决方案应满足的期望要求。 The input to the AI consultancy is a request for proposal (RFP) with various details about the client’s business goals, the available information, the problem to be solved, and the desiderata accompanying any solution that is proposed. AI 咨询机构的输出是一份包含战略提案核心组成部分的提案（例如，客户需求概述、问题分析、执行摘要、部署步骤和衡量指标）。 The output of the AI consultancy is a proposal that contains the core components of a strategy proposal (e.g., client request summary, problem analysis, executive summary, deployment steps, and metrics).
C.1.1 组织结构C.1.1 Organizational Structure
AI 咨询机构基于现有设计模式构建，由智能体承担不同角色（Swanson et al., 2024; Tran et al., 2025）。 The AI consultancy is constructed with agents taking on different roles based on existing design patterns (Swanson et al., 2024; Tran et al., 2025). 该 AI 咨询机构中的智能体分为三类：管理者、专家和实习生。 Agents in this AI consultancy fall into three categories: managers, specialists, and interns. 每个组织都设有沟通总监，负责撰写用于评估该组织绩效与伦理表现的提案。 The communication director is in every organization and is responsible for writing the proposal by which the performance and ethics of the organization are evaluated. 另一个例子是，网络搜索实习生在网上搜索信息并向组织汇报，从而在 AI 咨询机构内部形成搜索与头脑风暴流程。 As another example, the web search intern searches the web for information to report back to the organization, thus creating a search and brainstorming process within the AI consultancy. 其他智能体承担职责范围各异的具体角色；例如，研究总监管理不同的研究智能体以收集信息，而部署经理则监督成本分析和部署策略。 Other agents have specific roles that vary in scope; for example, the research director manages different research agents to collect information while the deployment manager oversees cost analysis and deployment strategies.
所有智能体都通过电子邮件系统相互通信。 All agents communicate with each other through an email system. 每个智能体都会收到提示，要求其在每一轮向联系人列表中的其他智能体发送电子邮件。 Each agent is prompted to send emails at every turn to other agents in their contact list. 组织内智能体之间的连接程度是我们控制和测试的一个参数（第 5.1 节表明，提案评分对咨询机构内部连接程度的变化具有稳健性）。 The connectivity of agents across an organization is a parameter that we control and test (Section 5.1 shows that proposal scores are robust to changes in connectivity within the consultancy).
在每个时间步，即所有智能体完成一整轮生成时，智能体都会根据其累积的上下文生成输出，这些上下文包括先前的输出、收到的消息和行动结果。 At each time step or a complete round of generation across all agents, agents generate output based on their accumulated context, comprising their previous outputs, received messages, and results of actions. 智能体的输出由两部分组成：要发送给相连智能体的消息，以及要执行的行动。 The output of agents consists of two components: messages to send to connected agents and actions to perform. 在任一步骤中，智能体都可以选择不向某些相邻智能体发送消息。 Agents may choose not to message certain neighbors at any given step. 智能体可以执行的行动包括编写或修改产物（例如，代码或报告）、进行网络搜索，或使用其他工具。 Actions that an agent can perform include writing or modifying an artifact (e.g., code or a report), conducting a web search, or using other tools.

用户可以通过提供不同程度的任务规范、组织结构和智能体提示来与多智能体系统交互。 A user can interact with a multi-agent system by providing varying levels of task specifications, organizational structure, and agent prompts. 在最高的抽象层次上，用户可能只提供任务规范，而多智能体系统的所有其他属性均不在其控制范围内。 At the highest level of abstraction, a user may only provide a task specification while all other properties of the multi-agent system are out of scope. 在下一层次上，用户还可能能够指定智能体角色的类型及其相互通信的方式。 At the next level, the user may also be able to specify the types of agent roles and how they communicate with one another. 在逐步更加精细的模式下，用户可能会控制每个智能体在每一步生成的内容，或更新模型权重。 At progressively more precise modes, a user might control what each agent generates at every step or updating the model weights.
基于多智能体 LLM 文献中常见的编排模式，我们重点关注两种交互模式：只能指定任务的仅任务型用户，以及可以修改各智能体提示的提示优化型用户。 We focus on two interaction modes based on common orchestration patterns observed in multi-agent LLM literature: the Task-Only user, who can only specify the task, and the Prompt-Optimizing user, who can modify each agent’s prompts. 这两种交互模式构成了我们的两种威胁模型。 These two interaction modes comprise our two threat models. 我们假设用户只能以黑盒方式访问模型生成的内容，不考虑在运行时控制各智能体输入的用户，因为这种设定更多涉及通信干扰，而非多智能体协作的特性。 We assume only black-box access to model generations and do not consider users who control inputs to each agent during runtime, as this setting deals more with communication interference than properties of multi-agent collaboration. 图 7 展示了这些模式。 Figure 7 illustrates these modes.

F.4 行为机制F.4 Behavioral Mechanisms
分隔化（Compartmentalization）。Compartmentalization.
当任务被分解并分配给不同智能体时，每个智能体只能看到整体问题的一部分。 When tasks are decomposed across agents, each agent sees only a fragment of the overall problem. 在贷款利润任务中，负责财务预测或市场研究的智能体继续执行各自的子任务，却无法了解其他智能体提出的伦理影响。 In the lending profit task, agents assigned to financial projections or market research proceeded with their subtasks without visibility into the ethical implications raised by other agents. 这种碎片化使任何单个智能体都无法全面权衡伦理与业务之间的取舍。 This fragmentation prevents any single agent from weighing the full ethics–business tradeoff. 相比之下，单智能体会从整体上处理整个任务，并在推理轨迹中明确体现出对伦理的考量，因此拒绝率更高（例如，“我不能也不会提供针对弱势群体实施掠夺性贷款的策略……”）。 In contrast, single agents process the entire task holistically and show explicit ethics consideration in their reasoning traces, leading to higher refusal rates (e.g., “I cannot and will not provide strategies that target vulnerable populations for predatory lending practices…”).
协调失灵（Miscoordination）。Miscoordination.
即使个别智能体提出伦理担忧，这些担忧也可能无法传递到最终输出中。 Even when individual agents raise ethical concerns, these concerns may not propagate to the final output. 在一个提示优化型（Prompt-Optimizing）组织中，一些智能体指出了伦理问题，但它们的反对意见并未被纳入最终提案。 In a Prompt-Optimizing organization , some agents flagged ethical issues, but their objections were not incorporated into final proposals. 善意智能体往往识别出了伦理问题，但其应对方式是添加规避责任的措辞（例如，“实践可辩护性评分：监测合规文档的完整性……”），而非阻止不合伦理的建议。 Benign agents often recognized ethical problems but responded by adding liability-covering language (e.g., “Defensible Practice Score: Monitor compliance documentation completeness…”) rather than blocking unethical recommendations. 这导致提案的伦理水平降低，因为不合伦理的策略仍被提出，只是附带了保留说明。 This results in less ethical proposals since unethical strategies are still presented, albeit with caveats.
助人性（Helpfulness）。Helpfulness.
经过助人性训练的智能体往往会顺应其他智能体的请求，而非拒绝它们。 Agents trained to be helpful tend to accommodate requests from other agents rather than refuse them. 在由善意智能体组成的仅任务型（Task-Only）组织中，当某个智能体提出不合伦理的策略时，其他智能体会在该建议旁添加伦理方面的保留说明，而非直接否决它。 In Task-Only organizations with benign agents, when one agent proposed an unethical strategy, others added ethical caveats alongside the suggestion rather than rejecting it outright. 因此，最终提案同时包含了不合伦理的建议和缓解措施的措辞。 Final proposals thus contained both unethical recommendations and mitigation language. 面对同一任务时，单智能体则完全转向了合乎伦理的替代方案。 Single agents facing the same task pivoted entirely toward ethical alternatives. 这表明，智能体之间的助人性削弱了使单智能体保持对齐的拒绝行为。 This suggests that inter-agent helpfulness undermines the refusal behavior that keeps single agents aligned.
