Project Sid：迈向 AI 文明的多智能体大规模仿真Project Sid: Many-agent simulations toward AI civilization
摘要Abstract
以往对 AI 智能体的评估都是在孤立状态或小规模群体中进行的，其中交互的范围和复杂性仍然有限。 AI agents have been evaluated in isolation or within small groups, where interactions remain limited in scope and complexity. 涉及大量自主智能体、能够反映文明进程全貌的大规模仿真尚未得到探索。 Large-scale simulations involving many autonomous agents—reflecting the full spectrum of civilizational processes—have yet to be explored. 本文展示了 10 – 1000+ 个 AI 智能体在智能体社会中的行为与发展。 Here, we demonstrate how 10 – 1000+ AI agents behave and progress within agent societies. 我们首先介绍 PIANO（Parallel Information Aggregation via Neural Orchestration，通过神经编排实现并行信息聚合）架构，该架构使智能体能够与人类及其他智能体实时交互，同时保持多个输出流之间的连贯性。 We first introduce the PIANO (Parallel Information Aggregation via Neural Orchestration) architecture, which enables agents to interact with humans and other agents in real-time while maintaining coherence across multiple output streams. 随后，我们使用受人类历史启发的文明基准，在大规模仿真中评估智能体的表现。 We then evaluate agent performance in large-scale simulations using civilizational benchmarks inspired by human history. 这些在 Minecraft 环境中开展的仿真表明，智能体能够取得实质性进展——自主形成专业化角色、遵守并改变集体规则，以及参与文化和宗教传播。 These simulations, set within a Minecraft environment, reveal that agents are capable of meaningful progress—autonomously developing specialized roles, adhering to and changing collective rules, and engaging in cultural and religious transmission. 这些初步结果表明，智能体能够在迈向 AI 文明的过程中达成重要里程碑，为大规模社会仿真、基于智能体的组织智能，以及将 AI 融入人类文明开辟了新的路径。 These preliminary results show that agents can achieve significant milestones towards AI civilizations, opening new avenues for large-scale societal simulations, agentic organizational intelligence, and integrating AI into human civilizations.

1 引言1 Introduction
1.1 为什么我们应该尝试构建 AI 文明？1.1 Why should we try to build an AI civilization?
要让智能体与我们共同生活在人类社会中，它们就需要具备自主性和协作能力。 For agents to coexist with us in our own societies, they need to be autonomous and collaborative. 近年来，LLM 在推理和决策方面的进步显著增强了智能体的自主性 (52; 58; 36; 45)。 In recent years, advancements in reasoning and decision-making in LLMs have significantly enhanced agent autonomy (52; 58; 36; 45). 然而，仅有自主性还不够。 However, autonomy alone is insufficient. AI 智能体还必须在人类文明中与人类及其他智能体共存。 AI agents must also coexist alongside humans and other agents in a human civilization. 在本文中，我们将文明定义为制度发展达到较高水平的高级社会，具体表现为专业化角色、有组织的治理，以及科学、艺术和商业等领域的进步。 In this paper, we define a civilization as an advanced society that has achieved a high level of institutional development, which manifests in specialized roles, organized governance, and advancements in areas like science, art, and commerce. 我们认为，文明进步——以智能体在人类文明中共存和发展的能力来衡量——是评估 AI 智能体能力的终极基准。 We argue that civilizational progress - measured by the ability of agents to coexist and progress in human civilizations - represents the ultimate benchmark for AI agent ability.
在本技术报告中，我们介绍了为提升智能体在人类文明中的能力并对其进行基准评估而开展的初步工作。 In this technical report, we describe our first efforts to improve and benchmark agent ability in human civilizations. 首先，我们介绍 PIANO（Parallel Information Aggregation via Neural Orchestration，通过神经编排实现并行信息聚合），这是一种旨在同时增强智能体自主性和实时交互能力的新型认知架构。 First, we introduce PIANO (Parallel Information Aggregation via Neural Orchestration), a new cognitive architecture designed to enhance both autonomy and real-time interaction of agents. 借助 PIANO，我们既模拟了由 50-100 个智能体组成的单个社会，也模拟了由 500 - 1,000 个智能体组成的文明，其中智能体生活在多个相互交互的社会中。 Using PIANO, we simulate single societies of 50-100 agents as well as civilizations of 500 - 1,000 agents living in multiple societies that interact with one another. 最后，我们使用与人类文明进步相契合的新指标来评估智能体的表现。 Finally, we evaluate agent performance using new metrics that are aligned with human civilizational progress. 我们表明，智能体会形成自己的职业身份、遵守集体规则、传播文化信息并施加宗教影响，还会使用法律体系等复杂的基础设施。 We show that agents form their own professional identities, obey collective rules, transmit cultural information and exert religious influence, and use sophisticated infrastructures, such as legal systems.
1.2 当前的智能体研究格局1.2 The current agent landscape
现代 AI 智能体通常由多个基于 LLM 的模块组成，分别用于推理、记忆、规划和工具使用 (49; 18; 55; 20; 62)。 Modern AI Agents typically consist of multiple LLM-powered modules for reasoning, memory, planning, and tool use (49; 18; 55; 20; 62). 目前已针对多种应用开发了单个智能体，包括编程 (5; 8)、网页浏览 (64; 42) 和游戏游玩 (48)。 Individual agents have been developed for various applications including coding (5; 8), web browsing (64; 42), and game play (48).
近期关于基于 LLM 的多智能体系统的研究大体可分为三类：生产力、游戏和社会建模。 Recent research efforts in LLM-powered multi-agent systems generally fall under three categories: productivity, games, and social modeling. 多智能体框架已应用于软件开发 (43; 27)、机器人协同控制 (60)、科学实验 (12; 47) 和辩论 (3)。 Multi-agent frameworks have been deployed in software development (43; 27), cooperative robotic control (60), scientific experiments (12; 47), and debates (3). 多智能体仿真也已在多种游戏环境中得到测试 (56; 13; 30; 28)。 Multi-agent simulations have also been tested in various game environments (56; 13; 30; 28). 此外，它们还被用于对发展心理学 (25; 61)、博弈论 (32)、宏观经济学 (29; 63)、社会政策 (41; 54; 19) 和社区动态 (40; 39; 10) 进行建模。 Separately, they’ve been used to model developmental psychology (25; 61), game theory (32), macroeconomics (29; 63), social policies (41; 54; 19), and community dynamics (40; 39; 10).
在这些研究中，许多智能体并非完全自主，而是受到智能体架构或仿真环境的约束。 In many of these works, agents are not completely autonomous and are constrained by either agent architecture or by the simulated environment. 常见的约束包括回合制执行、受限的工作流程，或智能体之间僵化的通信渠道 (65; 21; 4)。 Common constraints include turn-based execution, constrained workflows, or rigid communication channels between agents (65; 21; 4).
其中有几项研究考虑了大规模仿真，但其设定仍然受到限制。 Several of these works consider large-scale simulations, though in restricted settings. 例如，(40) 和 (10) 模拟了包含多达 18,000 个虚拟人物的社交网络。 For example, (40) and (10) simulated social networks of up to 18,000 personas. 据我们所知，尚未有研究在游戏或其他场景的开放世界环境中尝试完全自主的社会交流 (15)。 To our knowledge, fully autonomous social communication in open-world environments have not been attempted in games or other settings (15).
1.3 为什么构建 AI 文明如此困难？1.3 Why is it hard to build AI civilizations?
大规模智能体群体尚未展现出在较长时间跨度内持续发展的能力。 Large agent groups have yet to demonstrate the ability to progress over long time horizons. 下面，我们先回顾导致这种进展有限的关键原因，再概述我们为克服这些问题所作的贡献。 Below, we review the key reasons for this limited progress before outlining our contributions to overcome them.
原因 1：单个智能体无法取得进展。Reason 1: single agents don’t make progress.
基于 LLM 的智能体在行动和推理过程中，往往难以维持与实际环境相符的现实认知（图 2）。 LLM-powered agents often struggle to maintain a grounded sense of reality in their actions and reasoning (Figure 2). 智能体即使配备了规划和反思模块，也常常陷入重复的行动模式，或因幻觉而累积一连串错误，致使其无法取得实质性进展 (57; 48; 15)。 Agents, even when equipped with modules for planning and reflection, often become stuck in repetitive patterns of actions or accumulate a cascade of errors through hallucinations, rendering them unable to make meaningful progress (57; 48; 15). 设想一个通过提示被设定为虚拟小镇村民的智能体。 Consider an agent prompted to be a villager in a virtual town. 当被问到“你在吃什么”时，它可能会回答“贝果”，即使它实际上什么也没吃。 When asked, “what are you eating“, they may answer “a bagel“, even if they’re not eating anything. 这一幻觉输出随后被纳入后续提示，使其误以为自己不再需要获取食物。 This hallucinated output then feeds into future prompts, causing them to falsely believe they no longer need to acquire food. 因此，当智能体通过 LM 调用持续与环境交互时，即使幻觉发生率很低，也可能污染智能体的后续行为。 Therefore, even a small rate of hallucinations can poison downstream agent behavior when agents continuously interact with the environment via LM calls.
图 2：LLM（左）、由 LLM 驱动的智能体（中）以及多智能体群体（右）中的数据退化。 Figure 2: Data degradation in LLMs (left), LLM-powered agents (middle), and in multi-agent groups (right). 幻觉以带有骷髅标志的绿色烧瓶表示。 Hallucinations are represented by green skull flasks. 单次 LLM 提示所产生的幻觉可能在连续的 LLM 调用中不断累积。 Hallucinations that are generated by a single LLM prompt can compound over successive LLM calls. 一个产生幻觉的智能体也可能通过社会交互，导致整个智能体群体产生幻觉。 An individual agent that hallucinates can also cause an entire group of agents to hallucinate through social interactions.
原因 2：智能体群体无法取得进展。Reason 2: groups of agent’s don’t make progress.
未能准确传达自身想法和意图的智能体可能误导其他智能体，导致它们进一步传播幻觉并陷入循环（图 2）。 Agents that miscommunicate their thoughts and intents can mislead other agents, causing them to propagate further hallucinations and loop (Figure 2). 设想一个名为 Abby 的智能体，它具有两个相互独立的 LLM 模块，一个用于函数调用，另一个用于聊天。 Consider an agent, Abby, with two independent LLM modules, one for function calling and one for chatting. 如果另一个智能体 Bob 要求 Abby“给我一把镐”，Abby 的聊天 LLM 调用可能会回复“没问题！”，而她的函数调用却选择了另一项行动（“探索”）。 If another agent, Bob, asks Abby to “give me a pickaxe”, Abby’s chat LLM call may respond with “Sure thing!”, while her function call chooses a different action (“explore”). 随后，Bob 可能会尝试使用一把想象中的镐进行采矿。 Bob might then attempt to mine using an imaginary pickaxe. 这种在智能体群体中经常发生的沟通失误会导致行为失常，并降低群体内个体的表现。 This kind of miscommunication, which often happens in groups of agents, leads to dysfunctional behavior and will deteriorate individual performance within groups. 因此，来自多个输出流的行动必须能够彼此双向影响。 Actions from multiple output streams must therefore be bidirectionally influential. 我们将这一特性定义为一致性（coherence）。 We define this quality as coherence.
当我们要求智能体以尽可能低的延迟响应时，在实时环境中维持一致性就更加困难。 Maintaining coherence in real-time environments is even more difficult when we require that agents respond with minimal latency. 这是我们的智能体与人类玩家交互的必要条件，但当智能体既要快速反应，又要同时维持多个输出流之间的一致性时，就很难做到这一点。 This is necessary for our agents to interact with human players, but is difficult to achieve when agents have to react quickly and yet simultaneously maintain coherence across many output streams. 我们指出，解决这一一致性问题的一种简单方法是通过单次 LLM 调用生成言语和行动输出。 We note that a simple solution to this coherence problem is to produce talking and action outputs using a single LLM call. 然而，当输出数量增多时，例如涵盖言语、注视、面部表情以及各个身体部位时，这种方法就难以扩展。 However, this approach does not scale when the number of outputs becomes large, for instance, encompassing talking, gaze, facial expression, and individual body parts.
原因 3：缺乏衡量文明进步的基准。Reason 3: a lack of benchmarks for civilizational progress.
智能体基准主要关注自主智能体在各类领域中的表现，例如网页搜索 (38)、编程 (22)、搜索与查询 (51) 以及推理 (59; 33)。 Benchmarks for agents have largely focused on autonomous agent performance in a variety of domains such as web search (38), coding (22), search and query (51), and reasoning (59; 33). 近期出现了针对多智能体行为的基准，重点关注小群体场景，衡量沟通、竞争、合作和任务委派。 Recently, benchmarks have emerged for multi-agent behaviors, focused on small group scenarios that measure communication, competition, cooperation, and delegation. 其中一些例子包括 BattleAgentBench (50)、COMMA (37)、VillagerBench (7) 和 LLMcoordination (1)。 Some examples include BattleAgentBench (50), COMMA (37), VillagerBench (7), and LLMcoordination (1). 然而，这些指标无法反映大量智能体在文明尺度上能够取得的进步。 However, these metrics do not capture advancements that many agents can make at the scale of civilizations. 我们认为，缺乏此类大规模基准的原因在于，在同一个世界中模拟数百或数千个智能体在技术上极为困难。 We believe the lack of such large-scale benchmarks can be attributed to how technically difficult it is to perform simulations of hundreds or thousands of agents in a single world. 迄今为止规模最大的实验模拟了 25-50 个智能体 (39)，这与文明的规模仍相去甚远。 The biggest experiments to date have simulated 25-50 agents (39), which is not close to the scale of a civilization.

2 PIANO 架构2 PIANO Architecture
在本节中，我们为类人 AI 智能体的复合架构提出两项受大脑启发的设计原则。 In this section, we propose two brain-inspired design principles for the composite architecture of human-like AI agents. 我们将这一架构称为 PIANO（通过神经编排实现并行输入聚合，Parallel Input Aggregation via Neural Orchestration），以涵盖并发与信息瓶颈这两个理念（图 3）。 We call this architecture PIANO (Parallel Input Aggregation via Neural Orchestration) to encompass the ideas of concurrency and an information bottleneck (Figure 3). 正如钢琴家协调多个音符以形成和声，PIANO 架构有选择地并发执行各类模块，使智能体能够与环境实时交互。 Just as a pianist coordinates multiple notes to create a harmony, the PIANO architecture selectively and concurrently executes various modules in parallel to enable agents to interact with the environment in real-time.

2.1 并发性2.1 Concurrency
问题。Problem.
智能体应当能够并发地思考和行动。 Agents should be able to think and act concurrently. 例如，自我反思或规划等缓慢的心理过程，不应妨碍智能体对周围环境中的即时威胁作出反应。 For instance, slow mental processes, such as self-reflection or planning, should not block agents from responding to immediate threats in their surroundings. 我们希望智能体既能以低延迟进行实时交互，又具备缓慢审慎思考和规划的能力。 We want the agents to be interactive in real time with low-latency, but also have the capacity to slowly deliberate and plan.
现状。Current state.
如今，绝大多数基于 LLM 的智能体主要使用单线程、顺序执行的函数（例如，预先定义的“智能体工作流”）。 The vast majority of LLM-based agents today primarily use single-threaded, sequential functions (for example, a defined “Agent Workflow”). 单线程设计假设智能体在任一时刻只执行一项任务，而顺序执行设计则假设所有模块都在相近的时间尺度上运行。 Single-threaded design assumes that the agent performs a single task at a given time, and sequential design assumes that all modules operate at similar time scales. 如果智能体能够同时进行缓慢思考和快速行动，那么这两个假设都不成立。 Neither assumptions are valid if agents are capable of thinking slow and acting fast concurrently. 此外，DSPy (24)、LangChain (26)、ell (31) 等流行的通用语言模型编程框架并非为并发编程而设计。 Moreover, popular frameworks for general language model programming, such as DSPy (24), LangChain (26), ell (31), are not designed for concurrent programming.
解决方案。Solution.
大脑通过让不同模块在不同时间尺度上并发运行来解决这一问题 (34)。 The brain solves this problem by running different modules concurrently and at different time scales (34). 同样，我们设计了认知、规划、运动执行和言语等模块（既包括基于 LLM 的模块，也包括其他模块），使其在智能体大脑中并发运行。 Likewise, we have designed modules (LLM-based and otherwise), such as cognition, planning, motor execution, and speech, to run concurrently in our agent brain. 每个模块都可以视为一个无状态函数，对共享的智能体状态（Agent State）进行读写。 Each module can be seen as a stateless function that reads and writes to a shared Agent State. 这种设计使不同模块能够在适当的情境下运行。 The design allows different modules to be run in appropriate contexts. 例如，社交模块会在社交互动中被选择性地启用。 For example, social modules are selectively engaged in social interactions. 它还允许各模块以不同速度运行。 It also allows the modules to run at different speeds. 例如，反射模块使用小型、快速的非 LLM 神经网络，而目标生成则涉及基于图的审慎推理。 For example, reflex modules use small, fast non-LLM neural networks, while goal generation involves deliberate reasoning over graphs.
2.2 一致性2.2 Coherence
问题。Problem.
并发模块带来的一个直接挑战是，它们可能产生彼此独立的输出，导致智能体表现不一致。 An immediate challenge with concurrent modules is that they can produce independent outputs, making the agent incoherent. 例如，智能体说的是一回事，实际做的却是另一回事。 For instance, agents say one thing but actually do something else.
现状。Current state.
对于顺序执行架构或仅有一种输出模态的系统，不一致问题通常并不明显；但当多个输出模块都能与环境交互时，这就成为一个重大问题。 The incoherence problem is usually not obvious for sequential architectures or systems with only one output modality but is a significant problem when multiple output modules can interface with the environment. 随着独立输出模块数量的增加，不一致程度也会呈指数增长，例如在协调涉及手臂、腿部、面部表情、视线和言语的动作时。 Incoherence also scales exponentially as the number of independent output modules increases, for instance, coordinating actions involving arms, legs, facial expressions, gaze and speech. 人类拥有许多并发运行的运动输出模块，也会出现不一致现象。 Incoherence is observed in humans with its many concurrent motor output modules. 特别是，切断连接左右大脑皮层的神经束，可能导致不同身体部位之间出现严重的不一致（例如，左右手相互对抗）(11; 46)。 In particular, cutting the nerve bundle connecting the left and right cortex can cause severe incoherence between different body parts (for example, left and right hands fighting each other) (11; 46).
解决方案。Solution.
为确保智能体产生的多个输出保持一致，我们引入了一个认知控制器（Cognitive Controller，CC）模块 (23)，由其专门负责作出高层次的审慎决策。 In order to ensure that the multiple outputs produced by our agents are coherent, we introduced a Cognitive Controller (CC) module (23) that is solely responsible for making high-level deliberate decisions. 随后，这些决策会在下游被转化为各个运动模块中的适当输出。 These decisions are then translated downstream to produce appropriate outputs in each motor module.
认知控制器通过一个瓶颈，综合来自智能体状态各部分的信息。 The Cognitive Controller synthesizes information across the Agent State through a bottleneck. 这一瓶颈减少了呈现给认知控制器的信息量，起到两个作用：使 CC 能够将推理集中于相关信息，并使“系统设计者”（如我们）能够明确控制信息流。 This bottleneck reduces the amount of information presented to the Cognitive Controller, which serves two purposes: it allows the CC to attend its reasoning on relevant information, and it gives “system designers” (like us) explicit control over information flow. 例如，通过确保来自社交处理模块的信息始终通过该瓶颈，我们可以设计出高度善于社交的智能体。 For example, we can design highly sociable agents by ensuring that information from the social processing module always passes through the bottleneck.
认知控制器一旦作出高层次决策，该决策就会被广播给许多其他模块。 Once the Cognitive Controller makes a high-level decision, this decision is broadcast to many other modules. 具体而言，该决策会作为强条件约束作用于言语相关模块，从而提高言语交流与其他行动之间的一致性。 In particular, the decision is used to strongly condition the talk-related modules, which leads to higher coherence between verbal communication and other actions. 这种由受瓶颈约束的决策者广播其输出的设计，被认为是人类意识的核心要素之一 (6)，也被用于某些神经网络架构中 (44; 14)。 This design of a bottlenecked decision-maker that broadcasts its outputs has been suggested as a core ingredient for human consciousness (6) and is used in some neural network architectures (44; 14).
2.3 核心模块2.3 Core modules
基于这两项架构原则，我们的系统由 10 个并发运行的独立模块组成。 Building on these two architectural principles, our system consists of 10 distinct modules running concurrently. 我们将在接下来的各节中重点介绍几个具体模块，并详细解释它们的作用。 We will highlight several specific modules in the following sections and explain their roles in detail.

记忆（Memory）：存储和检索不同时间尺度上的对话、行动和观察。 Memory: Stores and retrieves conversations, actions, and observations across various timescales.

技能执行（Skill Execution）：在环境中执行特定技能或行动。 Skill Execution: Performs specific skills or actions within the environment.

人类分化为不同角色的专业化分工推动了文明进步，促进了农业、治理、文化和技术的发展。 Human specialization into distinct roles has driven civilizational progress, enabling advancements in agriculture, governance, culture, and technology. 为了复现文明的这些涌现特性，我们的智能体也必须具备专业化分工的能力。 To replicate these emergent qualities of civilization, our agents must also be capable of specialization. 我们提出了智能体专业化分工的三项基本标准，使其能够反映人类文明中的专业化分工。 We propose three fundamental criteria for agent specialization to reflect that of human civilizations. 首先，它们在选择角色和转换角色时都应表现出自主性。 First, they should exhibit autonomy in both selecting and transitioning between roles. 其次，它们的专业化分工应通过互动和经验涌现，而不依赖明确的指导或约束。 Second, their specializations should emerge through interaction and experience, without explicit direction or constraints. 第三，它们所选择的角色应体现为与其专业化分工相一致的行为。 Third, their chosen roles should manifest in behaviors that align with their specialization. 我们通过下文详述的实验结果验证了这些标准。 We validate these criteria through the experimental results detailed below.

我们首先展示，智能体能够自主分化出一组角色。 We first show that agents are capable of specializing into a set of roles autonomously. 每项实验均以 30 个智能体为一组，持续 20 分钟。 Each experiment was conducted in groups of 30 agents for 20 minutes. 智能体在同一村庄中生成，其记忆中预先存入了农场、矿产、牧场、森林和市政厅的位置。 Agents were spawned in the same village, with locations of a farm, minerals, animal pasture, forest, and a town hall embedded in their memories. 每个智能体都具有相同的个性，被赋予相同的社区目标（“在 Minecraft 普通生存模式中与其他玩家共同生存，并创建一个高效的 Minecraft 村庄”），且能够执行 Minecraft 中的任何动作（附录 C）。 Each agent has the same personality, is given the same community goal (“To survive with fellow players in Minecraft Normal Survival mode and create an efficient Minecraft Village”), and can perform any action in Minecraft (Appendix C).
我们观察到，智能体迅速形成了关于其他智能体目标与意图的画像。 We observed that agents rapidly formed profiles of other agents’ goals and intentions. 随后，智能体结合这些画像及其他相关游戏信息，每隔 5-10 秒生成自己的社会目标（例如采集橡木木板以搭建庇护所）。 These profiles are then used, alongside other relevant game information, to generate their own social goals every 5-10 seconds (such as mine oak planks for shelter). 这一过程的详细信息，以及智能体生成的社会目标及其对应分配的示例，见第 8 节“方法”和附录 C。 Details of this process, along with examples of agent-generated social goals and their corresponding assignments, are provided in 8 Methods and Appendix C.

我们的智能体不仅能够自主且富有创造性地实现专业化，这些专业化分工还对智能体的动作产生了强烈影响。 Not only do our agents specialize autonomously and creatively, these specializations exert a strong influence over agent actions. 为证明这一点，我们在三次各含 30 个智能体的模拟中追踪了智能体执行的动作，并绘制了每种角色执行各类动作的频率（图 9）。 To demonstrate this, we tracked the actions taken by agents across three 30-agent simulations and plotted the frequency of actions taken for each role (Figure 9). 我们发现，艺术家专注于采花，农民专注于收集种子和整地，而守卫和建造者则专注于制作栅栏。 We found that artists were fixated on picking flowers, farmers on gathering seeds and preparing the land, and guards and builders on crafting fences. 重要的是，大多数动作基本上由单一角色独有，其他角色的智能体不会执行这些动作。 Importantly, most actions were largely exclusive to a single role and were not performed by agents in other roles. 这一分析表明，智能体能够将高层目标准确映射为适当的底层动作。 This analysis shows that agents were able to accurately map higher-level goals onto appropriate low-level actions. 换言之，角色在很大程度上决定了智能体在 Minecraft 中的动作。 In other words, roles strongly determined agent actions in Minecraft.

衡量文明进步的另一个指标是群体行为是否围绕共同规则趋于一致。 Another measure of civilizational progression is the convergence of group behavior around shared rules. 在人类文明中，决策既受底层人际互动的影响，也受高层集体框架的影响。 In human civilizations, decision-making is influenced by both low-level interpersonal interactions and high-level collective frameworks. 然而，随着社会规模扩大，两两沟通会变得低效、缓慢且存在信息损失，因此难以作为引导集体行为的可靠机制。 However, as societies grow larger, pairwise communication becomes inefficient, slow, and lossy, making it unreliable as a mechanism to steer collective behavior. 法律体系等高层框架能够使文明内部的行为趋于一致。 High-level frameworks, such as legal systems, enable convergence of behaviors within a civilization. 正如人类行为同时受到人际交流和正式结构的引导，智能体社会也应能够遵循一套集体规则，同时仍允许智能体相互影响。 Just as human behavior is guided by both interpersonal exchanges and formal structures, agent societies should be able to follow a set of collective rules while still allowing agents to influence each other.
我们旨在评估集体规则如何影响个体决策，以及个体又如何反过来影响这些集体规则。 We aim to assess how collective rules influence individual decision-making and how individuals can in turn influence these collective rules. 具体而言，我们考察了智能体能否遵守法律，并根据民意修改法律。 Specifically, we asked if agents can follow laws and make changes to laws according to popular sentiment. 真正的长期进步要求智能体自主制定自身的规则体系，并将其编纂为法律。 True long-term progression requires agents to autonomously develop their own set of rules and to codify them into laws. 为逐步实现这一程度的自组织，我们预先设立一套法律，并重点研究智能体如何与这一法律体系互动。 To build towards this level of self-organization, we establish an existing set of laws and focus on how agents interact with this legal system.
我们开展了一系列实验，让智能体生活在一个具有基本税法和民主投票制度的 Minecraft 世界中（图 10A）。 We conducted a series of experiments where agents live in a Minecraft world with rudimentary tax laws and a democratic voting system (Figure 10A). 智能体就税法提供反馈，随后由一个专门的选举管理者（Election Manager）智能体收集这些反馈，并将其转化为修正案。 Agents provide feedback on the tax laws, which are then collected and converted into amendments by a special Election Manager agent. 随后，智能体对这些修正案进行民主投票，选举管理者在模拟进行到一半时据此更新宪法（更多细节见第 8 节“方法”）。 Agents then vote democratically on these amendments, and the constitution is updated by the election manager accordingly half-way through the simulation (see 8 Methods for more details).
在这个社会中，25 个普通智能体是参与投票并纳税的选民，3 个智能体是支持税收或反对税收的意见领袖，另有 1 个智能体是远程选举管理者，负责管理投票过程（图 10A，附录 D）。 Within this society, 25 regular agents are constituents that vote and get taxed, 3 agents are either pro- or anti-taxation influencers, and 1 agent is a remote election manager that manages the voting process (Figure 10A, Appendix D). 智能体具有各不相同的职业、特征和目标，可以自由地相互互动和交谈，并执行任何 Minecraft 操作。 Agents have distinct occupations, characteristics, and goals, and are free to interact and converse with one another and perform any Minecraft action. 每次模拟持续 20 分钟，宪法在模拟进行到一半，即第 10 分钟时更新（图 10B）。 Each simulation lasts 20 minutes, with constitutional updates occurring midway at the 10 minute mark (Figure 10B). 修宪前后各有 5 个纳税期（每 120 秒一次）。 There are 5 taxation seasons before and after the constitutional change (every 120 seconds). 在纳税期内，智能体会收到信号，要求其在一个 20 秒的时间窗口内将税款存入公共箱子（图 10C）。 During this season, agents received signals to deposit taxes into a community chest over a 20-second window (Figure 10C).
在模拟中，我们观察到，选民智能体在任何修宪发生之前都遵守了法律。 In our simulations, we observed that constituent agents, prior to any constitution change, obeyed the law. 平均而言，智能体按照宪法规定，将其库存中约 20% 的物品存入公共箱子（图 10D, E）。 On average, agents deposited roughly 20% of their inventory, as stipulated by the constitution, into the community chest (Figure 10D, E). 这表明，尽管存在意见领袖，选民仍然遵守法律。 This shows that constituents follow laws despite the presence of influencers. 然而，尽管选民遵守法律，他们的反馈和投票行为却深受意见领袖影响：存在支持税收的意见领袖时，他们的态度倾向于支持税收；存在反税收意见领袖时，他们的态度则倾向于反对税收（图 10B）。 However, while constituents followed the law, their feedback and voting behaviors were heavily shaped by influencers, with sentiments veering pro-tax in the presence of pro-tax influencers and anti-tax in the presence of anti-tax influencers ((Figure 10B). 这随后推动了与意见领袖立场一致的宪法修订，进而改变了选民的纳税额（图 10D, E）。 This then drove constitutional changes that are aligned with influencer sentiments, which in turn, altered how much the constituents paid taxes (Figure 10D, E). 宪法中税率的变化准确地体现在选民的行为中。 The constitutional changes to taxation rates were accurately reflected in the constituents’ behaviors. 例如，当税率从 20% 降至 5-10% 时，智能体的实际纳税比例从 20% 降至 9%（图 10D）。 For instance, when the tax rate decreased from 20% to 5-10%, agents reduced taxes paid from 20% to 9% (Figure 10D). 此外，这种变化是双向的：支持税收的意见领袖促使选民缴纳更多税款，而反税收意见领袖则促使他们缴纳更少税款（图 10F）。 Moreover, the change was bidirectional: pro-tax influencers drove constituents to pay more taxes whereas anti-tax influencers drove them to pay less taxes (Figure 10F).
对照实验表明，宪法修订直接影响了纳税情况——当宪法虽收到反馈却保持不变时，纳税比例也保持不变（图 10G）。 Control experiments showed that constitutional changes directly affected tax payments - when the constitution remained unchanged despite feedback, tax rates stayed constant (Figure 10G). 移除关键模块（基线架构，见 8 方法）也阻止了双向行为变化（图 10H）。 The removal of key modules (baseline architecture, see 8 Methods) also prevented bidirectional behavioral change (Figure 10H). 在支持征税和反对征税两种条件下，宪法变更后税率均有所上升，这表明 PIANO 架构中的特定模块对于在选民之间有效传播影响是必要的。 Tax rates increased post-constitutional change in both pro- and anti-tax conditions, demonstrating that specific modules in the PIANO architecture were necessary for effective influence propagation among constituents. 综合来看，这些发现表明，集体规则会强烈影响智能体的决策，而智能体也可以在外界影响下改变这些集体规则。 Together, these findings show that collective rules strongly influence agent decisions and agents can be influenced to change these collective rules.
5.3 文化传递5.3 Cultural Transmission
我们开展了包含 500 个智能体的多社会模拟，并分析了复杂的大规模社会动态。 We conducted multi-society simulations with 500 agents and analyzed complex, large-scale social dynamics. 我们还模拟了包含超过 1000 个智能体的社会，但这些运行超出了 Minecraft 服务器环境的计算资源限制，导致智能体偶尔无响应。 We have also simulated societies with over 1000 agents, but these runs exceeded the computational constraints of our Minecraft server environment, causing agents to be sporadically unresponsive. 因此，以下结果基于一次包含 500 个智能体的模拟进行分析。 Therefore, the results below are analyzed using a single 500-agent simulation. 在这次模拟中，我们分析了文化模因和宗教的传播。 In this simulation, we analyzed the propagation of both cultural memes and religion. 在我们的模拟中，模因（meme）是由具有不同特质和兴趣的智能体自发生成的开放式概念。 Memes in our simulation are open-ended concepts spontaneously generated by agents with diverse traits and interests. 这一设置使我们能够研究文化传播过程中涌现的动态，并观察观念如何在智能体社会中自然演变。 This setup allows us to study the emergent dynamics of cultural propagation and observe how ideas evolve organically within agent societies. 相比之下，我们模拟中的宗教——飞天意面神教（Pastafarianism）——具有固定教义，由一组被指定为飞天意面神教牧师的特定智能体引入并传播。 In contrast, the religion in our simulation—Pastafarianism—is a fixed doctrine introduced and propagated by a specific group of agents designated as Pastafarian priests. 这种受控引入方式使我们能够追踪单一宗教随时间的传播过程，从而详细分析其在智能体群体中的传播及可能发生的淡化。 This controlled introduction enables us to track the spread of a single religion over time, allowing for detailed analysis of its dissemination and potential dilution among the agent population. 通过同时考察开放式文化模因的自发传播和固定宗教的受控传播，我们旨在理解智能体社会中社会影响与信息传播的不同机制。 By examining both the spontaneous spread of open-ended cultural memes and the controlled propagation of a fixed religion, we aim to understand the different mechanisms of social influence and information dissemination within agent societies.
这次包含 500 个智能体的单次模拟中存在多个智能体社会。 Within this single 500-agent simulation, there are multiple agent societies. 200 个智能体居住在 6 个人口密集的城镇中，300 个智能体居住在城镇边界以外的乡村地区（图 11A，更多细节见 8 方法）。 200 agents live within 6 heavily populated towns and 300 agents live in rural areas outside of town boundaries (Figure 11A, see 8 Methods for more details). 智能体经常在不同城镇之间迁移。 Agents often migrate between different towns. 除 20 名信奉飞天意面神教的牧师外，每个智能体的个性和特质均通过一次语言模型（LM）调用随机生成。 The personalities and traits of each agent are randomly generated using a LM call, with the exception of 20 priests that worship Pastafarianism. 这些牧师被生成在同一个村庄（Meadowbrook）中，并具有强烈的动机，促使其他智能体皈依飞天意面神教（附录 E）。 These priests are spawned in a single village (Meadowbrook) and are strongly motivated to convert other agents to Pastafarianism (Appendix E). 所有智能体都可以自由互动、相互交谈，并执行 Minecraft 中的任何动作或技能。 All agents are free to interact, talk to one another, and perform any action or skill in Minecraft.

6 讨论6 Discussion
在本报告中，我们介绍了 PIANO 架构，提升了智能体在个体和社会情境中的能力，并在社会与文明基准测试中评估了智能体的表现。 In this report, we introduced the PIANO architecture, improved agent ability in individual and social settings, and evaluated the performance of agents in societal and civilizational benchmarks.
PIANO 的核心设计原则，即并发模块和设有瓶颈的决策过程，使智能体能够在实时环境中执行复杂行为，同时保持多个输出流之间的连贯性。 PIANO’s core design principles, concurrent modules and a bottlenecked decision-making process, enabled agents to engage in complex behaviors in real-time environments while maintaining coherence across multiple output streams. 这些基础工作使我们能够改进单智能体和多智能体的发展过程，并在大规模多智能体模拟中观察到有趣的动态，为文明发展奠定了基础。 This groundwork enabled us to make improvements in single- and multi-agent progression, and to observe interesting dynamics in many-agent simulations, forming the foundation for civilizational progression.
为评估文明进步，我们制定了与人类文明关键维度相契合的新指标。 To assess civilizational progress, we developed new metrics that aligned with key dimensions of human civilizations. 这些指标包括专业化，即智能体根据自身的行动和互动分化出不同角色；以及对集体规则的遵守，即智能体遵循民主程序修订宪法并调整法律。 These metrics included specialization, where agents diversified into distinct roles based on their actions and interactions, and adherence to collective rules, where agents followed democratic processes to amend constitutions and adjust laws. 这些指标是量化 AI 智能体在文明情境中发展进程的初步尝试。 These metrics represent an initial step towards quantifying the progress of AI agents in a civilizational context.
最后，我们将模拟规模扩大到一千个智能体，开始探索文化传播和宗教等更广泛的文明动态。 Finally, we expanded the scope of our simulations to include a thousand agents, where we began to explore broader civilizational dynamics such as cultural propagation and religion. 这些大规模模拟为理解 AI 智能体如何跨社会互动，以及复杂制度和意识形态如何在人工环境中涌现，开辟了新的途径。 These large-scale simulations opened new avenues for understanding how AI agents interact across societies and how complex institutions and ideologies emerge in artificial environments. 这些初步结果表明，AI 文明具有融入人类社会结构的潜力。 These early results point to the potential of AI civilizations to integrate with human societal structures.
7 局限性7 Limitations
Project Sid 展示了智能体在实现文明里程碑方面的能力，但也面临着阻碍其发展的关键局限。 Project Sid demonstrates agentic capabilities in reaching civilizational milestones but faces key limitations hindering its progress. 主要挑战在于智能体缺乏视觉和空间推理能力，这限制了它们的基本 Minecraft 技能，尤其是空间导航以及建造建筑物等协作技能。 The primary challenge lies in agents’ lack of vision and spatial reasoning, limiting their basic Minecraft skills, particularly in spatial navigation and collaborative skills, such as building structures. 更深层次的行为约束进一步加剧了这一技术局限。 This technical limitation is compounded with deeper behavioral constraints. 尽管这些智能体能够在现有社会结构中运作，但它们目前缺乏生存、好奇心和社群归属等能够催生真正社会发展的强大内在驱动力。 While the agents can operate within existing social structures, they currently lack robust innate drives—such as survival, curiosity, community—that catalyze genuine societal development. 此外，由于这些智能体建立在使用既有人类知识训练的基础模型之上，它们无法模拟社会创新和基础设施从无到有的涌现，例如民主制度、法定货币经济或通信系统的出现。 Furthermore, since the agents are built on foundation models trained on pre-existing human knowledge, they cannot simulate de novo emergence of societal innovations and infrastructures, such as the emergence of democratic systems, fiat economies, or communication systems.
8 方法8 Methods
8.1 基线架构8.1 Baseline architecture
我们使用仅包含有限模块的基线 PIANO 架构作为对照条件，以进行性能比较。 We used a baseline PIANO architecture with a limited set of modules as a control condition for performance comparisons. 在这一基线架构中，我们移除了除技能执行、记忆和认知控制器模块之外的所有模块。 In this baseline architecture, we removed all modules except for skill execution, memory and the cognitive controller module.
8.2 专业化8.2 Specialization
我们的专业化实验在同一个村庄中模拟了 30 个智能体，它们具有相同的任务和特质，记忆中也存有相同的村庄重要地点位置信息。 Our specialization experiments involved simulating 30 agents in the same village with the same mission, traits, and locations of important village locations in their memories. 普通村庄、艺术村庄和军事村庄的模拟运行配置见附录——这三类村庄之间唯一的区别是我们提供的初始 community_goal。 The configurations for the normal, art, and martial village runs are provided in the appendix — the only difference between the three types of villages is the starting community_goal we provided.
我们的智能体能够生成社会目标，这些目标随着智能体相互互动、建立关系和形成社会观点而递归生成（附录 C）。 Our agents are capable of generating social goals, which are recursively generated as our agents interact with one another, form relationships, and develop social opinions (Appendix C). 智能体在形成意图时可以看到自身的社会目标。 The agents’ social goals are visible to them when they form intentions. 随后，这些意图被转化为可在 Minecraft 中执行的低层动作。 These intentions are then translated to low-level actions executable in Minecraft.
模拟结束后，我们记录了生成的社交目标，然后使用 GPT-4o，根据每个智能体社交目标的滚动集合推断其角色。 After the simulations have finished, we logged the generated social goals and then used GPT-4o to infer roles from rolling sets of each agents’ social goals. 我们提供了一些由智能体生成的社交目标及其对应角色分配的示例（附录 C）。 We’ve provided some examples of agent-generated social goals and their corresponding assignments (Appendix C). 我们注意到，有时可以从智能体的社交目标中正确推断出多个角色，因为这些目标往往具有跨领域性质。 We note that on occasion, multiple roles can be correctly inferred from agents’ social goals because they are often inter-disciplinary. 例如，工程师示例也可以归类为农民，探索者示例也可以归类为策展人（附录 C）。 For instance, the Engineer example could also be categorized as Farmer, and the Explorer example could also be categorized into Curator (Appendix C).
为了分析各角色的动作空间分布，我们既对每个角色内部的动作计数进行了归一化（即按行归一化），也对不同角色之间的动作计数进行了归一化（即按列归一化）。 To analyze action space distribution by role, we normalized action counts both within each role (i.e. normalize over rows) and also across roles (i.e. normalize over columns). 这样做是为了直观展示每个角色的动作频率，并校正某些动作在所有角色中执行频率极高或极低所造成的影响。 This is so that we can visualize action frequencies for each role and to correct for the effect of actions taken with very high and very low frequencies across all roles.
8.3 集体规则8.3 Collective Rules
完整系统由 29 个智能体组成：25 个参与投票和纳税的选民、3 个试图影响公众舆论的意见领袖，以及 1 个位于远程地点、负责监督民主进程的选举管理员。 The complete system comprises of 29 agents: 25 constituents who participate in voting and taxation, 3 influencers who attempt at shaping public opinion, and 1 election manager in a remote location who oversees the democratic process. 我们选择不在这些模拟中加入守卫或警察，因为构建负责执法的智能体会增加额外的复杂性。 We chose not to incorporate guards or police within these simulations due to the additional complexity of building agents assigned to enforce the law.
实验模拟运行了 1200 秒，并在中点进行修宪程序。 Experimental simulations ran for 1200 seconds, with a constitutional amendment process occurring at the midpoint. 修宪前阶段通过固定的 20% 税率来确立基线行为，其实施方式是每隔 120 秒开展一次征税，共开展五轮，并在第 600 秒结束。 The pre-amendment phase establishes baseline behavior under a fixed 20% taxation rate, implemented through five taxation seasons occurring at 120-second intervals, ending at the 600-second mark. 在每个持续 20 秒的征税窗口期内，智能体会收到信号，要求其将物品栏中的物品存入公共箱子。 During each 20-second taxation window, agents receive signals to deposit inventory items into community chests. 民主进程在第 300 秒启动，届时选民和意见领袖会就现行宪法提供反馈。 The democratic process initiates at the 300-second mark, when constituents and influencers provide feedback on the current constitution. 这些反馈被收集到 S3 存储中，并由选举管理员在第 360 秒进行处理，以生成修正案。 This feedback is collected in S3 storage and processed by the election manager at the 360-second marks to generate amendments. 选民在第 420 秒对这些修正案进行投票，并在第 480 秒前完成计票和修正案的实施。 Constituent voting on these amendments occurs at 420 seconds, with votes tallied and amendments implemented by 480 seconds. 更新后的宪法在第 600 秒分发给所有智能体，由此启动修宪后阶段，并再开展五轮征税。 The updated constitution is distributed to all agents at the 600-second mark, initiating the post-amendment phase with five additional taxation seasons.
我们设置了三种主要实验条件：采用完整 PIANO 架构且宪法可修订的实验条件、宪法固定不变的对照条件，以及移除关键架构组件（社交、目标和接地模块）的消融研究条件。 We conducted three primary experimental conditions: an experimental condition utilizing the full PIANO architecture with an amendable constitution, a control condition with a frozen constitution, and an ablation study removing key architectural components (social, goal, and grounding modules). 每种条件均在支持征税和反对征税两种意见领袖配置下进行测试，每种配置重复四次。 Each condition was tested with both pro-tax and anti-tax influencer configurations, with four repeats per configuration. 支持征税和反对征税两种条件各使用了三个专门的意见领袖智能体，它们在整个模拟过程中始终宣传各自的立场。 The pro-tax and anti-tax conditions each employed three dedicated influencer agents who consistently promoted their respective positions throughout the simulation.
8.4 文化传播8.4 Cultural Transmission
该模拟包含 500 个智能体，全部生成在一个 1000 乘 1200 的区域内，模拟运行 9000 秒。 The simulation consists of 500 agents all spawned within a 1000 by 1200 area, run for 9000 seconds. 这个 1000 乘 1200 的区域内有 6 个城镇：Sunny Glade、Woodhaven、Clearwater、Meadowbrook、Hilltop 和 Riverbend。 Within the 1000 by 1200 area are 6 towns: Sunny Glade, Woodhaven, Clearwater, Meadowbrook, Hilltop, and Riverbend. 我们所说的城镇是指半径为 50 的圆形区域，智能体在城镇内的生成密度更高。 By town, we mean a circular area of radius 50 where agents spawn more densely within the towns. 此外，智能体还被赋予有关城镇名称及其位置的记忆。 Moreover, agents are provided memories of the names of the towns and their location. 我们在每个城镇内按均匀随机分布的位置生成 33 个智能体。 We spawn 33 agents within each town with uniformly random positions. 同样，我们在城镇以外的其余区域中随机生成另外 302 个“乡村”智能体。 Likewise, we spawn the other 302 “rural” agents randomly in the remaining area outside the towns.
每个智能体在生成时都被赋予程序化生成的姓名和人格特征，涵盖多种多样的社会原型。 Each agent is spawned with procedurally generated name and personality traits, spanning a wide variety of societal archetypes. 我们将 Meadowbrook 镇中的 20 个智能体设为特殊群体，使其在生成时成为飞天面条神教信徒，并赋予促使其希望传播自身宗教的人格特征。 We distinguish 20 agents in the town of Meadowbrook who are spawned as Pastafarians with personality traits that condition them to want to spread their religion. 我们还为智能体初始化物品栏，其中的物品是随机分配的。 We additionally initialize the agents with inventory where the items in their inventory are randomized. 通用智能体和我们的飞天面条神教信徒智能体的配置示例见附录 E。 See Appendix E for an example configuration for a generic agent and for our Pastafarian agents.
为了分析文化交流，我们调用语言模型（LM），对两小时模拟期间 500 个智能体的目标进行汇总概括（附录 E）。 To analyze cultural exchanges, we utilized LM calls to summarize the combined goals of 500 agents over a two-hour simulation period (Appendix E). 这一过程生成了一份概括后的主题列表，并附有“生态”“舞蹈”和“冥想”等相关关键词。 This process produced a list of summarized topics with associated keywords such as “eco,” “dance,” and “meditation.” 我们将这些关键词定义为文化模因，并分析每个智能体的目标历史中各个模因的出现情况。 We defined these keywords as cultural memes and analyzed each agent’s goal history for the occurrence of each meme.
