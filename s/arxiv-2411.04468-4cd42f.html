Abstract摘要
Modern AI agents, driven by advances in large foundation models, promise to enhance our productivity and transform our lives by augmenting our knowledge and capabilities. To achieve this vision, AI agents must effectively plan, perform multi-step reasoning and actions, respond to novel observations, and recover from errors, to successfully complete complex tasks across a wide range of scenarios. In this work, we introduce Magentic-One, a high-performing open-source agentic system for solving such tasks. Magentic-One uses a multi-agent architecture where a lead agent, the Orchestrator, plans, tracks progress, and re-plans to recover from errors. Throughout task execution, the Orchestrator also directs other specialized agents to perform tasks as needed, such as operating a web browser, navigating local files, or writing and executing Python code. Our experiments show that Magentic-One achieves statistically competitive performance to the state-of-the-art on three diverse and challenging agentic benchmarks: GAIA, AssistantBench, and WebArena. Notably, Magentic-One achieves these results without modification to core agent capabilities or to how they collaborate, demonstrating progress towards the vision of generalist agentic systems. Moreover, Magentic-One’s modular design allows agents to be added or removed from the team without additional prompt tuning or training, easing development and making it extensible to future scenarios. We provide an open-source implementation of Magentic-One, and we include AutoGenBench, a standalone tool for agentic evaluation. AutoGenBench provides built-in controls for repetition and isolation to run agentic benchmarks in a rigorous and contained manner – which is important when agents’ actions have side-effects. Magentic-One, AutoGenBench and detailed empirical performance evaluations of Magentic-One, including ablations and error analysis are available at https://aka.ms/magentic-one.

1 Introduction1 引言
Recent advances in artificial intelligence and foundation models are driving a renewed interest in agentic systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the world to complete tasks on our behalf [32, 59]. These systems promise to enhance our productivity by relieving us from mundane and laborious tasks, and revolutionize our lives by augmenting our knowledge and capabilities [16, 54, 6]. By leveraging the powerful reasoning and generative capabilities of large language models (LLMs), agentic systems are already making strides in fields like software engineering [66, 55], data analysis [4], scientific research [26, 7] and web navigation [79, 75].
人工智能和基础模型的最新进展，正推动人们重新关注能够在现实世界中感知、推理和行动，以代替我们完成任务的智能体系统 [32, 59]。这些系统有望将我们从单调繁重的任务中解放出来，从而提高生产力，并通过增强我们的知识与能力，彻底改变我们的生活 [16, 54, 6]。借助大型语言模型（LLMs）强大的推理和生成能力，智能体系统已在软件工程 [66, 55]、数据分析 [4]、科学研究 [26, 7] 和网页导航 [79, 75] 等领域取得进展。
Realizing the vision of agentic systems to transform our lives requires these systems to not only achieve high performance in specific domains, but also to generalize to the diverse range of tasks people may encounter throughout their day-to-day work and personal lives. In this paper, we take steps towards creating such a generalist agentic system by introducing Magentic-One .[fn1] Magentic-One uses a team ofagents, each specializing in generally-useful skills, such as: operating a web browser, handling files, and executing code. The team is directed by an Orchestrator agent which guides progress towards a high-level goal by iteratively planning, maintaining working memory of progress, assigning tasks to other agents, and retrying upon encountering errors. The Orchestrator uses two structured ledgers to achieve this and also to decide which agent should take the next action. Together, Magentic-One’s agents achieve strong performance on multiple challenging agentic benchmarks. Figure 1 shows an example of Magentic-One solving one such benchmark task that requires multiple steps and diverse tools.
要实现智能体系统改变我们生活的愿景，这些系统不仅需要在特定领域取得高性能，还需要能够泛化至人们在日常工作和个人生活中可能遇到的各种任务。本文通过引入 Magentic-One，朝着构建此类通用智能体系统迈出了步伐。[fn1] Magentic-One 使用一个智能体团队，每个智能体都专门掌握某些具有普遍用途的技能，例如操作网页浏览器、处理文件和执行代码。该团队由一个 Orchestrator 智能体指挥，它通过迭代规划、维护记录进度的工作记忆、向其他智能体分配任务，以及在遇到错误时重试，来引导团队朝着高层目标推进。Orchestrator 使用两个结构化账本来实现上述功能，并据此决定由哪个智能体执行下一步操作。Magentic-One 的各智能体通过协作，在多个具有挑战性的智能体基准上取得了出色的性能。图 1 展示了 Magentic-One 解决此类基准任务的一个示例，该任务需要多个步骤和多种工具。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Single-Agent Approaches.单智能体方法。
Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 [33] have renewed interest in the development of autonomous agents that can solve tasks on behalf of people [32, 59, 16, 60, 65, 49, 74, 43]. These modern agents have shown remarkable skills in software development [55, 76, 66, 63], web manipulation [8, 75, 79, 31, 1], manipulation of general graphical user interfaces [73, 61, 3, 34], and other domains [37, 54].
GPT-4 [33] 等大语言模型（LLMs）的近期进展，重新激发了人们对开发能够代替人类完成任务的自主智能体的兴趣 [32, 59, 16, 60, 65, 49, 74, 43]。这些现代智能体在软件开发 [55, 76, 66, 63]、网页操作 [8, 75, 79, 31, 1]、通用图形用户界面操作 [73, 61, 3, 34] 以及其他领域 [37, 54] 展现出了卓越的能力。
Common strategies for developing such agents [25, 62, 27, 6] include equipping LLMs with tools such as for code execution and web browsing [40, 41, 46, 29] and prompting strategies for better reasoning and planning such as CoT [58], ReACT [70] and few-shot prompting [79]. With the development of multimodal models, agents can also operate in visual domains with techniques such as Set-of-Marks prompting [67] among others [67, 77, 36, 14]. To allow agents to accomplish tasks that require multiple steps with improved reliability, agent systems can incorporate self-critique [61, 34, 38], and inference-time search [5, 69, 19, 50]. Finally, Agentic systems can also benefit from memory and training either through explicit fine-tuning [72, 34, 24, 39] or through memory mechanisms [57, 49]. Our work incorporates a subset of these techniques, and distributes them across agents in Magentic-One’s multi-agent workflow, resulting in a modular, easy-to-extend implementation.
开发此类智能体的常见策略 [25, 62, 27, 6] 包括为 LLMs 配备代码执行和网页浏览等工具 [40, 41, 46, 29]，以及采用 CoT [58]、ReACT [70] 和少样本提示 [79] 等提示策略来改善推理与规划。随着多模态模型的发展，智能体也能够借助 Set-of-Marks 提示 [67] 等技术 [67, 77, 36, 14] 在视觉领域中执行操作。为使智能体能够以更高的可靠性完成需要多个步骤的任务，智能体系统可以引入自我批评 [61, 34, 38] 和推理时搜索 [5, 69, 19, 50]。最后，智能体系统还可以通过显式微调 [72, 34, 24, 39] 或记忆机制 [57, 49]，从记忆和训练中获益。我们的工作采用了其中一部分技术，并将其分配给 Magentic-One 多智能体工作流中的各个智能体，从而形成模块化且易于扩展的实现。
Multi-Agent Approaches.多智能体方法。
The multi-agent paradigm presents an attractive modular and flexible approach to tackling complex tasks [51, 28, 12, 53, 45, 60, 52, 13, 25, 62, 27, 6].
Commonly each agent either has access to different tools or has a different role in the team, sometimes defined through the system prompt of the LLM or by explicit training. Sibyl presents a multi-agent approach with a debate-based jury mechanism with tools for python code execution and web browsing [56]. WebPilot uses a multi-agent system with global and local optimization in planning for web based tasks [75]. Trase claims to use a multi-agent architecture with a top level agent with self-critique and lower level agents [42]. A host of other multi-agent systems and frameworks have also been introduced [21, 22, 11, 2, 15]. However, the previous methods differ from the architecture of Magentic-One which incorporates dynamic routing between agents using the Orchestrator along with planning and recovery.
通常，各个智能体要么可以使用不同的工具，要么在团队中承担不同的角色，这些角色有时通过 LLM 的系统提示或显式训练来定义。Sibyl 提出了一种采用基于辩论的评审团机制的多智能体方法，并配备了用于 python 代码执行和网页浏览的工具 [56]。WebPilot 使用多智能体系统，在面向网页任务的规划中结合全局优化与局部优化 [75]。Trase 声称采用了一种多智能体架构，其中包含一个具备自我批评能力的顶层智能体和多个低层智能体 [42]。此外，还出现了许多其他多智能体系统和框架 [21, 22, 11, 2, 15]。然而，以往方法不同于 Magentic-One 的架构，后者通过协调器（Orchestrator）实现智能体之间的动态路由，并结合规划与恢复机制。
Agentic Evaluation.智能体评估。
To evaluate agents on general multi-step tasks, numerous benchmarks have been proposed in the literature [30, 79, 64, 23, 71, 68, 47, 8, 35, 20]. Given the general and ubiquitous nature of the web, many of these benchmarks heavily incorporate [29, 71], or exclusively consider [79, 8] browser-based tasks. These benchmarks either rely on non-interactive traces through real websites such as Mind2Web [8], interaction with synthetically created websites such as in WebArena [79], or interaction with real websites on the public Internet such as GAIA [29]. In the former case, non-interactive benchmarks are limiting for evaluating agentic systems since they do not allow agents to deviate from previously recorded paths. This makes it impossible to evaluate error recovery, or find novel alternative strategies for the given problem. Therefore, we focus on benchmarks that rely on interacting with live websites – whether synthetic or public – as they are more faithful to real-world tasks. Moreover, we prioritize benchmarks such as GAIA, which test generalist skills like data analysis or coding, in addition to commanding web browsers to navigate pages. We contribute AutoGenBench as a standalone tool to perform evaluation of agentic systems, relying on benchmarks from the literature. Furthermore, we provide an in-depth error analysis of Magentic-One’s performance contributing to work on debugging agentic systems [20].
为了评估智能体在通用多步骤任务上的表现，文献中已提出众多基准 [30, 79, 64, 23, 71, 68, 47, 8, 35, 20]。鉴于万维网的通用性和普及性，其中许多基准大量纳入基于浏览器的任务 [29, 71]，或仅考虑此类任务 [79, 8]。这些基准要么依赖真实网站上的非交互式操作轨迹，如 Mind2Web [8]；要么涉及与人工构建的网站交互，如 WebArena [79]；要么涉及与公共互联网上的真实网站交互，如 GAIA [29]。在第一种情况下，非交互式基准在评估智能体系统时存在局限，因为它们不允许智能体偏离预先记录的路径。这使得评估错误恢复能力或寻找解决给定问题的新替代策略成为不可能。因此，我们关注依赖与可实时交互的网站进行交互的基准，无论这些网站是人工构建的还是公开的，因为它们更贴近现实世界中的任务。此外，我们优先考虑 GAIA 这类基准，它们除了测试指挥网页浏览器浏览页面的能力，还测试数据分析或编程等通用技能。我们推出 AutoGenBench 作为独立工具，利用文献中的基准对智能体系统进行评估。此外，我们对 Magentic-One 的表现进行了深入的错误分析，为智能体系统调试方面的研究作出贡献 [20]。
3 Problem Setup3 问题设定
Complex Tasks.复杂任务。
In this work our goal is to build a generalist agentic system capable of solving complex tasks across a variety of domains. We define a task as complex if it requires, or significantly benefits from, a process involving planning, acting, observing, and reflecting, potentially multiple times. Acting refers to more than generating tokens, such as executing code, using tools, or interacting in an environment. Observing, in this context, provides information that was previously unavailable or unknowable. A task is defined by an input, a desired output and an evaluation function to compare the desired output to any candidate output. The input consists of a well-specified textual description and an optional arbitrary set of file attachments which may include images, dataset files, audio clips among other things. For example, the input task description could be “fact-check each claim in the attached PDF as correct or incorrect” with a PDF file as an attachment. The desired output consists either of a textual answer (possibly representing a structured object), or a specific state of the environment to reach. In the fact-checking example, the output might be a string labeling each fact as correct or not, e.g., “claim 1: correct, claim 2: incorrect, …”. Here, the evaluation function might simply determine whether the desired output and the proposed answer match exactly.
在本研究中，我们的目标是构建一个能够解决多种领域中复杂任务的通用智能体系统。如果一项任务需要经历涉及规划、行动、观察和反思的过程，或能从这一过程中显著受益，且该过程可能需要重复多次，我们就将其定义为复杂任务。行动是指超出生成词元范畴的操作，例如执行代码、使用工具或在环境中进行交互。在此语境下，观察提供的是此前无法获得或无法知晓的信息。一项任务由输入、期望输出以及用于将期望输出与任意候选输出进行比较的评估函数定义。输入包括明确的文本描述，以及一组可选的任意文件附件，其中可能包含图像、数据集文件、音频片段等。例如，输入的任务描述可以是“核查所附 PDF 中的每项陈述，判断其正确与否”，并附上一个 PDF 文件。期望输出可以是文本答案（可能表示一个结构化对象），也可以是需要达到的特定环境状态。在事实核查示例中，输出可以是一个字符串，标明每项事实正确与否，例如“陈述 1：正确，陈述 2：错误，……”。在这里，评估函数可以仅判断期望输出与给出的答案是否完全匹配。
Agentic Systems.智能体系统。
To complete a task, assume a computer which can be partially observed and operated to complete the task. The computer constitutes the environment. An agentic system can take as input the task description, and any related attachments that are present on the computer environment. The system is allowed to do arbitrary processing to complete the task, but must complete it within a time budget (e.g., 25 mins). For instance, on the computer, the autonomous system can execute Python code, navigate the web using a browser, download files locally, among other actions from its action space. The system’s ability to take action in, and potentially modify, both the local and web environments is why we refer to the system as agentic. After completing the task, the system returns a text answer, and a trace of its observations and steps along the way. The final state of the environment is also captured in sufficient detail to run the task evaluation. Note that this setting can be described as a Partially Observable Markov Decision Process, similar to formalizations used by prior work [49]. Nex, we describe Magentic-One, our multi-agent system that can autonomously solve complex tasks.
为完成任务，假设存在一台计算机，可以对其进行部分观察和操作以完成任务。这台计算机构成了环境。智能体系统可以将任务描述以及计算机环境中存在的任何相关附件作为输入。系统可以进行任意处理以完成任务，但必须在时间预算内（例如 25 分钟）完成。例如，自主系统可以在计算机上执行 Python 代码、使用浏览器浏览网页、将文件下载到本地，以及执行其动作空间中的其他操作。系统能够在本地环境和网络环境中采取行动，并可能修改这些环境，因此我们将其称为智能体系统。完成任务后，系统会返回文本答案，以及过程中观察结果和操作步骤的轨迹。系统还会足够详细地记录环境的最终状态，以便执行任务评估。请注意，这一设定可以描述为部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（Partially Observable Markov Decision Process），与先前研究采用的形式化描述类似 [49]。接下来，我们介绍 Magentic-One，这是我们构建的能够自主解决复杂任务的多智能体系统。
4 Magentic-One Overview4 Magentic-One 概述
Figure 2: Magentic-One features an Orchestrator agent that implements two loops: an outer loop and an inner loop. The outer loop (lighter background with solid arrows) manages the task ledger (containing facts, guesses, and plan). The inner loop (darker background with dotted arrows) manages the progress ledger (containing current progress, task assignment to agents).
图 2：Magentic-One 配备了一个协调器智能体，该智能体实现了两个循环：外循环和内循环。外循环（背景较浅，箭头为实线）管理任务账本（包含事实、推测和计划）。内循环（背景较深，箭头为虚线）管理进度账本（包含当前进度、分配给各智能体的任务）。
Magentic-One is a generalist multi-agent system for autonomously completing complex tasks. The team’s work is coordinated by an Orchestrator agent, responsible for task decomposition and planning, directing other agents in executing subtasks, tracking overall progress, and taking corrective actions as needed. The other agents on the team are specialized with different capabilities necessary for completing ad-hoc, open-ended tasks such as browsing the web and interacting with web-based applications, handling files, and writing and executing Python code (Figure 2).
Magentic-One 是一个用于自主完成复杂任务的通用多智能体系统。团队的工作由协调器智能体统筹，该智能体负责任务分解与规划、指导其他智能体执行子任务、跟踪整体进度，并根据需要采取纠正措施。团队中的其他智能体各具专长，拥有完成临时性、开放式任务所需的不同能力，例如浏览网页和与基于 Web 的应用程序交互、处理文件，以及编写和执行 Python 代码（图 2）。
Together, the Magentic-One team collaborates to solve tasks on behalf of a user. For example, suppose a user requests a survey and concise slide presentation of AI safety papers published in the last month. Magentic-One will approach this task as follows. The Orchestrator will first create a plan that breaks down the task into subtasks, such as searching for abstracts, downloading relevant papers, reading and summarizing the papers, and finally creating a presentation out of the findings. This initial plan serves as providing a guide or rubric for acting, and may not be followed exactly. Instead it can be interpreted as similar to chain of thought prompting for the agents [58]. Once this initial plan is formed, the Orchestrator then selects an appropriate agent and assigns it a subtask. For example, the WebSurfer agent might be directed to search for and download AI safety papers, while the FileSurfer agent might be directed to open the downloaded PDFs and extract relevant information. The Coder agent might create the presentation by writing Python code to interact with various files, and the ComputerTerminal agent would then execute the code written to produce the final output (or to report execution errors the coder agent has yet to address). As the task progresses, the Orchestrator coordinates the agents, monitors progress, and monitors for task completion.
Magentic-One 团队通过协作，代表用户完成任务。例如，假设用户要求对上个月发表的 AI 安全论文进行综述，并制作一份简明的幻灯片演示文稿。Magentic-One 将按如下方式处理这一任务。Orchestrator 首先会制定计划，将任务分解为多个子任务，例如搜索摘要、下载相关论文、阅读并总结论文，最后根据研究结果制作演示文稿。这一初始计划为行动提供指南或准则，实际执行时未必会完全遵循。更恰当的理解是，它类似于面向智能体的思维链提示（chain of thought prompting）[58]。初始计划形成后，Orchestrator 随即选择合适的智能体，并为其分配子任务。例如，可以指示 WebSurfer 智能体搜索并下载 AI 安全论文，同时指示 FileSurfer 智能体打开下载的 PDF 文件并提取相关信息。Coder 智能体可以通过编写 Python 代码来操作各类文件，从而创建演示文稿，随后 ComputerTerminal 智能体执行所编写的代码以生成最终输出（或报告 Coder 智能体尚未解决的执行错误）。随着任务推进，Orchestrator 负责协调各智能体、监控进展，并检查任务是否完成。
In the following sections, we first describe Magentic-One’s inter-agent workflow, driven by the Orchestrator, then describe each individual agent’s design, capabilities, and action space.
在以下各节中，我们首先介绍由 Orchestrator 驱动的 Magentic-One 智能体间工作流程，然后介绍各个智能体的设计、能力和动作空间。
4.1 Magentic-One’s Multi-Agent Workflow4.1 Magentic-One 的多智能体工作流程
Figure 2 illustrates Magentic-One’s workflow in more depth. At a high level, the workflow contains two loops, the outer loop maintains the task ledger, which contains the overall plan, while the inner loop maintains the progress ledger, which directs and evaluates the individual steps that contain instructions to the specialized agents.
图 2 更详细地展示了 Magentic-One 的工作流程。从整体上看，该工作流程包含两个循环：外层循环维护包含总体计划的任务台账（task ledger），内层循环维护进度台账（progress ledger），用于指导和评估各个步骤，而这些步骤包含对专门智能体的指令。
Outer Loop.外层循环。
The outer loop is triggered by an initial prompt or task. In response, the Orchestrator creates the task ledger to serve as short-term memory for the duration of the task. Upon receiving the task, the Orchestrator reflects on the request and pre-populates the task ledger with vital information– given or verified facts, facts to look up (e.g., via web search), facts to derive (e.g., programmatically, or via reasoning), and educated guesses. These initial educated guesses are important, and can allow the Orchestrator to express memorized closed-book information in a guarded or qualified manner, allowing agents to potentially benefit, while lessening the system’s overall sensitivity to errors or hallucinations. For example, agents might only rely on the guesses when they get stuck, or when they run out of time and need to output a best guess for the benchmark. Educated guesses are updated periodically, by the outer loop, as new information becomes available.
外层循环由初始提示或任务触发。作为响应，Orchestrator 创建任务台账，作为任务执行期间的短期记忆。收到任务后，Orchestrator 会思考该请求，并在任务台账中预先填入关键信息——已给出或已验证的事实、待查找的事实（例如通过网络搜索）、待推导的事实（例如通过编程或推理），以及有依据的猜测。这些初始的有依据猜测十分重要，它们使 Orchestrator 能够以审慎或附带限定的方式表达记忆中的闭卷知识，让智能体有机会从中受益，同时降低整个系统对错误或幻觉的敏感性。例如，智能体可能仅在陷入困境时，或在时间耗尽、需要为基准测试输出最佳猜测时，才依赖这些猜测。随着新信息的出现，外层循环会定期更新这些有依据的猜测。
Only after the facts and guesses in the task ledger have been populated, the Orchestrator considers the makeup of the team it is directing. Specifically, it uses each team member’s description, along with the current task ledger, to devise a step-by-step plan. The plan is expressed in natural language and consists of a sequence of steps and assignments of those steps to individual agents. Since the plan is used in a manner similar to chain of thought prompting [58], it serves more as a hint for step-by-step execution – neither the Orchestrator nor the other agents are required to follow it exactly. Since this plan may be revisited with each iteration of the outer loop, we force all agents to clear their contexts and reset their states after each plan update. Once the plan is formed, the inner loop is initiated.
只有在任务台账中的事实和猜测填充完毕后，Orchestrator 才会考虑其所指挥团队的构成。具体而言，它会结合各团队成员的描述和当前任务台账，制定逐步执行的计划。该计划以自然语言表述，由一系列步骤及其向各个智能体的分配安排组成。由于该计划的使用方式类似于思维链提示 [58]，它更多地充当逐步执行的提示——Orchestrator 和其他智能体都无须严格遵循该计划。由于外层循环每次迭代时都可能重新审视该计划，我们强制所有智能体在每次计划更新后清空上下文并重置状态。计划形成后，内层循环随即启动。
Inner Loop.内层循环。
During each iteration of the inner loop, the Orchestrator answers five questions to create the progress ledger:
在内层循环的每次迭代中，Orchestrator 通过回答五个问题来创建进度台账：
Is the request fully satisfied (i.e., task complete)?
请求是否已得到完全满足（即任务是否完成）？
Is the team looping or repeating itself?
团队是否陷入循环或重复自身的行为？
Is forward progress being made?
是否正在取得进展？
Which agent should speak next?
接下来应由哪个智能体发言？
What instruction or question should be asked of this team member?
应向该团队成员下达什么指令或提出什么问题？
When answering these questions, the Orchestrator considers both the task ledger (containing facts, guesses, and a plan), and the current agent conversation context.
回答这些问题时，Orchestrator 会同时考虑任务台账（包含事实、猜测和计划）以及当前的智能体对话上下文。
The Orchestrator also maintains a counter for how long the team has been stuck or stalled. If a loop is detected, or there is a lack of forward progress, the counter is incremented. As long as this counter remains below a threshold ( $\leq 2$ in our experiments), the Orchestrator initiates the next team action by selecting the next agent and its instruction. However, if the counter exceeds the threshold, the Orchestrator breaks from the inner loop, and proceeds with another iteration of the outer loop. This includes initiating a reflection and self-refinement step [48], where it identifies what may have gone wrong, what new information it learned along the way, and what it might do differently on the next iteration of the outer loop. It then updates the task ledger, revises the original plan, and starts the next cycle of inner loop. Together, this counter-based mechanism gives the agents a limited budget to recover from small errors, or to persist through brief episodes of uncertainty in progress.
Orchestrator 还维护一个计数器，用于记录团队陷入困境或停滞的持续情况。如果检测到循环，或未能取得进展，计数器就会递增。只要该计数器仍未超过阈值（在我们的实验中为 $\leq 2$），Orchestrator 就会通过选择下一个智能体及其指令，发起团队的下一步行动。但是，如果计数器超过阈值，Orchestrator 就会跳出内层循环，转而执行外层循环的下一次迭代。这包括启动一个反思与自我改进步骤 [48]，在此过程中，它会识别可能出错的环节、沿途学到的新信息，以及在外循环的下一次迭代中可以采取的不同做法。随后，它会更新任务台账、修订原始计划，并开始下一轮内循环。总体而言，这种基于计数器的机制为智能体提供了有限的预算，使其能够从小错误中恢复，或在进展暂时不明朗时继续坚持。
This nested-loop behavior continues until the Orchestrator determines the task is complete or the team has reached some (parameterized and configurable) termination logic, such as reaching a maximum number of attempts, or exceeding a specified maximum time limit.
这种嵌套循环行为会持续进行，直到 Orchestrator 判定任务已完成，或团队满足某种（可通过参数设置和配置的）终止条件，例如达到最大尝试次数，或超过指定的最长时间限制。
Finally, upon termination of both loops, the Orchestrator reviews the full transcript, along with the ledger, and reports either a final answer, or its best educated guess.
最后，在两个循环均终止后，Orchestrator 会审阅完整的交互记录及台账，并报告最终答案，或基于现有信息作出的最佳推测。
4.2 Magentic-One’s Agents4.2 Magentic-One 的智能体
The Orchestrator agent in Magentic-One coordinates with four specialized agents: WebSurfer, FileSurfer, Coder and ComputerTerminal. As the names suggest, each of these agents is optimized for a specific – yet generally useful – capability. In most cases, these agents are constructed around LLMs with custom system prompts, and capability-specific tools or actions. For example, WebSurfer can navigate to pages, click links, scroll the viewport, etc. In other cases, agents may operate deterministically, and do not include LLMs calls at all. For example, the ComputerTerminal deterministically runs Python code, or shell commands, when asked.
Magentic-One 中的 Orchestrator 智能体与四个专用智能体协同工作：WebSurfer、FileSurfer、Coder 和 ComputerTerminal。顾名思义，这些智能体各自针对一种特定但具有普遍用途的能力进行了优化。在大多数情况下，这些智能体以 LLM 为核心构建，并配有自定义系统提示词，以及针对特定能力的工具或动作。例如，WebSurfer 可以访问页面、点击链接、滚动视口等。在其他情况下，智能体可能以确定性方式运行，完全不涉及 LLM 调用。例如，ComputerTerminal 会在收到请求时以确定性方式运行 Python 代码或 shell 命令。
This decomposition of high-level capabilities across agents, and low-level actions within agents, creates a hierarchy over tool usage which may be easier for the LLMs to reason about. For example, rather than deciding between dozens of possible actions, the Orchestrator needs only to decide which agent to call to access a broad capability (e.g., browsing the web). The chosen agent then selects from a limited set of agent-specific actions (e.g., clicking a button versus scrolling the page).
这种在智能体之间划分高层能力、在智能体内部划分底层动作的方式，为工具使用构建了一种层次结构，可能使 LLM 更容易对此进行推理。例如，Orchestrator 无需在数十种可能的动作之间作出选择，而只需决定调用哪个智能体来使用某项广义能力（例如浏览网页）。随后，被选中的智能体会从一组有限的、该智能体专属的动作中进行选择（例如点击按钮或滚动页面）。
We detail the implementation of each of the agents below:
下面详细介绍各个智能体的实现：
WebSurfer: This is a highly specialized LLM-based agent that is proficient in commanding and managing the state of a Chromium-based web browser. With each incoming natural-language request, the WebSurfer maps the request to a single action in its action space (described below), then reports on the new state of the web page (providing both a screenshot and a written description). As an analogy, this configuration resembles a telephone technical support scenario where the Orchestrator knows what to do, but cannot directly act on the web page. Instead it relays instructions, and relies on the WebSurfer to carry out actions and report observations.
WebSurfer：这是一个基于 LLM 的高度专用化智能体，擅长操控基于 Chromium 的网页浏览器并管理其状态。每收到一条自然语言请求，WebSurfer 就会将其映射为动作空间（如下所述）中的一个动作，然后报告网页的新状态（同时提供截图和文字描述）。打个比方，这种配置类似于电话技术支持场景：Orchestrator 知道该做什么，却无法直接操作网页。因此，它会传达指令，并依靠 WebSurfer 执行动作和报告观察结果。
The action space of the WebSurfer includes navigation (e.g. visiting a URL, performing a web search, or scrolling within a web page); web page actions (e.g., clicking and typing); and reading actions (e.g., summarizing or answering questions). This latter category of reading actions allows the WebSurfer to directly employ document Q&A techniques in the context of the full document. This saves considerable return-trips to the orchestrator (e.g., where the orchestrator might simply command the agent to continue scrolling down), and is advantageous for many tasks.
WebSurfer 的动作空间包括导航（例如访问 URL、执行网页搜索或在网页内滚动）、网页操作（例如点击和输入）以及阅读动作（例如摘要或回答问题）。最后这一类阅读动作使 WebSurfer 能够在完整文档的上下文中直接运用文档问答技术。这省去了大量与 Orchestrator 的往返交互（例如，Orchestrator 可能只是命令智能体继续向下滚动），对许多任务都很有帮助。
When interacting with web page elements (e.g., when clicking or typing), the WebSurfer must ground the actions to specific coordinates or elements of the current web page. For this we use set-of-marks prompting [67] in a manner similar to Web Voyager[14]. This step relies on an annotated screenshot of the page, and thus is inherently multi-modal. We further extended the set-of-marks prompt to include textual descriptions of content found outside the visible view port, so that the agent can determine what might be found by scrolling [fn4], or opening menus or drop-downs.
在与网页元素交互时（例如点击或输入时），WebSurfer 必须将动作定位到当前网页的具体坐标或元素。为此，我们采用了与 Web Voyager[14] 类似的标记集提示（set-of-marks prompting）[67] 方法。这一步骤依赖于带有标注的页面截图，因此本质上是多模态的。我们进一步扩展了标记集（set-of-marks）提示，加入了对可见视口之外内容的文本描述，使智能体能够判断通过滚动 [fn4] 或打开菜单、下拉列表可能找到什么内容。
Footnote: 4 Scrolling is needed because, like human users, the WebSurfer agent cannot interact with page elements that are outside the active viewport.
脚注：4 之所以需要滚动，是因为与人类用户一样，WebSurfer 智能体无法与当前视口之外的页面元素交互。
FileSurfer: The FileSurfer agent is very similar to the WebSurfer, except that it commands a custom markdown-based file preview application rather than a web browser. This file preview application is read-only, but supports a wide variety of file types, including PDFs, Office documents, images, videos, audio, etc. The FileSurfer can also perform common navigation tasks such as listing the contents of directories, and navigating a folder structure.
FileSurfer：FileSurfer 智能体与 WebSurfer 非常相似，区别在于它操控的是一个基于 markdown 的自定义文件预览应用，而非网页浏览器。这个文件预览应用是只读的，但支持多种文件类型，包括 PDF、Office 文档、图像、视频、音频等。FileSurfer 还可以执行常见的导航任务，例如列出目录内容和浏览文件夹结构。
Coder: This is an LLM-based agent specialized through its system prompt for writing code, analyzing information collected from the other agents, or creating new artifacts. The coder agent can both author new programs and debug its previous programs when presented with console output.
Coder：这是一个基于 LLM 的智能体，通过系统提示词使其专门用于编写代码、分析从其他智能体收集的信息，或创建新的产物。Coder 智能体既能编写新程序，也能在获得控制台输出后调试自己先前编写的程序。
ComputerTerminal: Finally, the ComputerTerminal provides the team with access to a console shell where the Coder’s programs can be executed. ComputerTerminal can also run shell commands, such as to download and install new programming libraries. This allows the team to expand the available programming tool set, as needed.
ComputerTerminal：最后，ComputerTerminal 为团队提供控制台 shell 的访问能力，以便执行 Coder 编写的程序。ComputerTerminal 还可以运行 shell 命令，例如下载并安装新的编程库。这使团队能够根据需要扩展可用的编程工具集。
Together, Magentic-One’s agents provide the Orchestrator with the tools and capabilities that it needs to solve a broad variety of open-ended problems, as well as the ability to autonomously adapt to, and act in, dynamic and ever-changing web and file-system environments.
Magentic-One 的各个智能体共同为 Orchestrator 提供了解决各种开放式问题所需的工具和能力，以及自主适应动态且不断变化的网页与文件系统环境并在其中采取行动的能力。
5 Experiments5 实验
5.1 AutoGenBench and Setup5.1 AutoGenBench 与实验设置
Overview.概述。
Agentic systems, such as Magentic-One, that interact with stateful environments, pose unique challenges for evaluation. For example, if a task requires installing a Python library, the first system to be evaluated will be disadvantaged: Its agents will have to first write Python code that fails, then debug the problem, install the library, and finally try again. Subsequent runs – perhaps with other agents or models – will then benefit from the library’s presence, and thus may appear to perform better simply because they were executed later. Conversely, an erroneous agent could take actions (e.g. deleting files, or placing the the system in an inoperable state), that would harm all future tasks. To this end, it is crucial that any evaluation be independent across tasks, and provide safety from dangerous side effects (e.g., from agents’ actions).
像 Magentic-One 这样与有状态环境交互的智能体系统，为评估带来了独特的挑战。例如，如果某项任务需要安装一个 Python 库，那么第一个接受评估的系统将处于不利地位：其智能体必须先编写会运行失败的 Python 代码，然后调试问题、安装该库，最后再次尝试。后续运行（可能使用其他智能体或模型）则会受益于该库已安装这一条件，因此可能仅仅因为执行时间较晚，就显得表现更好。反过来，出错的智能体可能采取某些行动（例如删除文件，或使系统陷入无法运行的状态），从而损害之后的所有任务。因此，确保任何评估中的各项任务相互独立，并提供防范危险副作用（例如由智能体的行动引发的副作用）的安全保障，至关重要。
To address this challenge, we developed AutoGenBenchfor evaluating agentic systems. Given a benchmark, which consists of a set of independent tasks and associated evaluation functions, AutoGenBench allows users to run these tasks in a setting with tightly controlled initial conditions: in each task, AutoGenBench will start from a blank slate with freshly initialized Docker containers, providing the recommended level of consistency and safety. The results of each task are logged in a central location on the host machine (outside of Docker), and can be ingested for analysis by metrics scripts. Furthermore, AutoGenBench allows users to launch multiple tasks in parallel to speed up evaluation, or to compute variance across repeated runs.
为应对这一挑战，我们开发了用于评估智能体系统的 AutoGenBench。给定一个由一组独立任务及其对应评估函数组成的基准，AutoGenBench 允许用户在初始条件受到严格控制的环境中运行这些任务：对于每项任务，AutoGenBench 都会使用全新初始化的 Docker 容器，从空白状态开始运行，从而提供所建议的一致性和安全性水平。每项任务的结果都会记录在宿主机上的一个集中位置（Docker 外部），并可由指标脚本读取以供分析。此外，AutoGenBench 允许用户并行启动多项任务，以加快评估速度，或计算重复运行之间的方差。
Benchmarks.基准。
Using AutoGenBench, we can implement and evaluate Magentic-One on a variety of benchmarks. Our criteria for selecting benchmarks is that they should involve complex multi-step tasks, with at least some tasks or steps requiring planning and tool use ( including using web browsers to act on real or simulated webpages, handling files, etc.) We consider three benchmarks in this work that satisfy this criteria: GAIA, AssistantBench, and WebArena.
借助 AutoGenBench，我们可以在多种基准上实现并评估 Magentic-One。我们选择基准的标准是：它们应包含复杂的多步骤任务，且至少有部分任务或步骤需要规划和工具使用（包括使用网页浏览器在真实或模拟网页上执行操作、处理文件等）。本研究考虑了满足这一标准的三个基准：GAIA、AssistantBench 和 WebArena。

5.3 Ablations5.3 消融实验
In this section, we examine how different agents and capabilities contribute to Magentic-One’s performance through ablation experiments.
本节通过消融实验，考察不同智能体及能力对 Magentic-One 性能的贡献。
Setup.实验设置。
On the validation set of GAIA [29], we perform multiple ablation experiments to evaluate the impact of key Magentic-One (GPT-4o) agents and components. First, to understand the impact of Magentic-One’s Orchestrator, the AutoGen[60] library’s GroupChat mechanism. This baseline orchestrator simply decides which agent should speak next during task execution, eliminating both ledgers, planning, progress tracking, loop detection, and explicit instructions to other agents. The second set of ablations we perform is to remove individual agents from the Magentic-One team to measure the impact of those agents on overall task performance.
我们在 GAIA [29] 的验证集上进行了多项消融实验，以评估 Magentic-One (GPT-4o) 中关键智能体和组件的影响。首先，为了了解 Magentic-One 的编排器（Orchestrator）的影响，AutoGen[60] 库的 GroupChat 机制。这个基线编排器仅决定在任务执行过程中接下来应由哪个智能体发言，去除了两种账本、规划、进度跟踪、循环检测以及向其他智能体发出的明确指令。我们进行的第二组消融实验是从 Magentic-One 团队中移除单个智能体，以衡量这些智能体对整体任务表现的影响。
For all ablations, we report on results broken down by difficulty level and capabilities required. For the capabilities analysis, we mapped the tools needed to complete tasks, as reported by human annotators of the GAIA dataset [29], to four categories: web browsing, coding, file handling, and none. These categories roughly correspond to the categories defined in [29], with minor adjustments to better align to the core functional-responsibilities of Magentic-One’s agents. For example, the original categories in [29] included a multi-modality category since multi-modal task handling was accomplished via a tool. However, because Magentic-One leverages multi-modal models, multi-modality is handled inherently by all agents rather than through use of a specific tool. In such cases, we noted the task as requiring no tools (i.e., ’none’) to complete. Our capability mapping is described further in Appendix B.
对于所有消融实验，我们均按难度级别和所需能力分别报告结果。在能力分析中，我们将 GAIA 数据集 [29] 的人工标注者报告的完成任务所需工具映射为四个类别：网页浏览、编程、文件处理和无。这些类别与 [29] 中定义的类别大致对应，仅作了少量调整，以更好地契合 Magentic-One 各智能体的核心职能。例如，[29] 中的原始类别包含多模态类别，因为多模态任务处理是通过工具完成的。然而，由于 Magentic-One 使用多模态模型，所有智能体都能凭借自身能力处理多模态信息，而无须使用特定工具。在这种情况下，我们将该任务标记为无须工具（即“none”）即可完成。附录 B 进一步介绍了我们的能力映射。

5.4 Error Analysis5.4 错误分析
As a final element of evaluation, we conducted an analysis to better understand Magentic-One’s current failure modes.
作为评估的最后一个环节，我们开展了一项分析，以更好地了解 Magentic-One 当前的失效模式。
Approach.方法。
As Magentic-One works to solve tasks, it produces extremely rich and detailed logs. Manual inspection of these logs often reveals mistakes, missed opportunities, dead-ends, and run-time errors encountered by the agents. Many of these issues are systematic, suggesting opportunities where the team could be improved. These opportunities could exist even when the agents successfully complete a task e.g., because of suboptimal behavior. However, manual inspection of these lengthy logs is slow and laborious, and scaling this manual labor to a large number of logs can become cost-prohibitive.
Magentic-One 在解决任务的过程中会生成极其丰富且详尽的日志。人工检查这些日志通常能够发现智能体遇到的失误、错失的机会、走入的死胡同以及运行时错误。其中许多问题具有系统性，表明团队存在可改进之处。即使智能体成功完成了任务，也可能存在这些改进空间，例如其行为并非最优。然而，人工检查这些冗长的日志既缓慢又费力，而且将这项人工工作扩展到大量日志时，成本可能高到难以承受。
To address this, we opted to automate log analysis using LLMs. The general problem here is to automate the process of qualitative coding, i.e., automatically discovering major themes in errors and inefficiencies observed in the logs. We implemented a multi-phase approach to accomplish this. For each task, we use GPT-4o to distill the team logs into a detailed postmortem document, which seeks to identify the root cause of failure, along with any contributing factors. These will serve as the basis for analysis.
为解决这一问题，我们选择使用 LLMs 实现日志分析的自动化。这里的一般性问题是如何将定性编码过程自动化，即自动发现日志中所观察到的错误与低效现象的主要主题。我们采用了一种多阶段方法来实现这一目标。对于每项任务，我们使用 GPT-4o 将团队日志提炼为一份详细的事后分析文档，旨在识别失败的根本原因及相关促成因素。这些文档将作为分析的基础。
Each root-cause document is then automatically assigned a few descriptive codes (aka labels) using GPT-4o. With no pre-defined code book, there is initially a high diversity of codes across documents. After generating these initial codes, the next step is to group them into batches, with each batch being sent to GPT-4o for clustering. This step merges similar codes into a more consolidated set. The process of consolidating and refining the codes is repeated iteratively, either until the codes stabilize or a maximum number of iterations is reached.
随后，使用 GPT-4o 为每份根因分析文档自动分配若干描述性编码（也称标签）。由于没有预先定义的编码手册，各文档的初始编码具有很高的多样性。生成这些初始编码后，下一步是将其分批，并将每批编码发送给 GPT-4o 进行聚类。这一步将相似的编码合并为一个更精简的集合。编码的整合与细化过程以迭代方式重复进行，直到编码趋于稳定或达到最大迭代次数。
We used 200 random samples of logs to bootstrap these codes and then once the final set of codes is determined, it is applied to the entire set of documents.
我们使用 200 份随机抽取的日志样本来初步构建这些编码，并在确定最终编码集后，将其应用于全部文档。
Results.结果。

This persistence in ineffective actions leads to delays and suboptimal task outcomes. For instance, agents might continuously attempt the same unsuccessful web searches without modifying their queries or repeatedly access incorrect data sets without making necessary adjustments, resulting in wasted effort and time.

6.1 The Multi-Agent Paradigm6.1 多智能体范式
At the core of Magentic-One is its multi-agent design. We believe that this design is a principal contributing factor to Magentic-One’s performance. Indeed, we observe that most other top-performing systems also follow a multi-agent design (Sec. 5.2).
Magentic-One 的核心是其多智能体设计。我们认为，这一设计是促成 Magentic-One 性能表现的主要因素之一。事实上，我们观察到，其他表现最优的系统大多也采用多智能体设计（第 5.2 节）。
We argue that, beyond performance, the multi-agent setup offers numerous other advantages over the single-agent setup, in terms of ease-of-development, cost, and raw performance. For example, organizing skills into distinct agents can simplify development, much like with object-oriented programming. The separation of concerns across agents allows developers to focus model choices, prompting strategies, and other parameters to align to specific tasks (e.g., the web surfer agent benefits from multi-modality and structured output, but need not worry about writing code). Similarly, agent modularity can increase agent re-usability and ease of extensibility, particularly when teams are carefully designed to enable a plug-and-play approach. For example, Magentic-One’s design facilitates adapting the team’s functional scope by simply adding or removing agents, without requiring modifications to other agents’s prompts or the overall flows and orchestration strategy. In contrast, monolithic single-agent systems often rely on constrained workflows that can be difficult to adapt or extend.
我们认为，除性能之外，多智能体配置相较于单智能体配置，在开发便利性、成本和原始性能方面还具有诸多其他优势。例如，将各项技能组织到不同的智能体中可以简化开发，这与面向对象编程颇为相似。智能体之间的关注点分离使开发者能够针对具体任务选择模型、提示策略及其他参数（例如，网页浏览智能体可受益于多模态能力和结构化输出，但无需考虑编写代码）。同样，智能体的模块化可以提高其可复用性和扩展便利性，尤其是在经过精心设计、支持即插即用方式的团队中。例如，Magentic-One 的设计使得只需添加或移除智能体即可调整团队的功能范围，而无需修改其他智能体的提示词，也无需修改整体流程和编排策略。相比之下，单体式的单智能体系统通常依赖受限的工作流，可能难以调整或扩展。
As a consequence of such modularity, each agent can be implemented in a fashion best suited for its purpose. In this paper, we leveraged this diversity to incorporate the o1-preview model into some roles (e.g., the Coder, and the outer loop of the Orchestrator), while relying on a general purpose multi-modal model (GPT-4o) for web and file surfing. Looking ahead, we see this approach being used to reduce reliance on large models – whereas some subtasks might require the largest language models available, others (e.g., grounding actions in WebSurfer, or summarizing large files in FileSurfer) might be amenable to much smaller – and thus cheaper – models. Different subtasks may also require different modalities, and some subtasks might be offloaded to traditional, non-AI, tools (e.g., code execution, for which a standard code execution environment is both sufficient and necessary). By embracing this diversity, multi-agent systems can become more performant at lower costs.
得益于这种模块化，每个智能体都可以采用最适合其用途的方式实现。在本文中，我们利用这种多样性，将 o1-preview 模型用于某些角色（例如 Coder 和 Orchestrator 的外层循环），同时使用通用多模态模型（GPT-4o）进行网页和文件浏览。展望未来，我们认为这种方法可用于减少对大型模型的依赖——某些子任务可能需要使用当前可用的最大规模语言模型，而其他子任务（例如，将 WebSurfer 中的动作映射到具体操作对象，或在 FileSurfer 中总结大型文件）则可能适合使用小得多、因而成本更低的模型。不同的子任务也可能需要不同的模态，而某些子任务可以交由传统的非 AI 工具处理（例如代码执行，标准的代码执行环境对此既充分又必要）。通过充分利用这种多样性，多智能体系统可以以更低的成本实现更高的性能。
Understanding and quantifying the empirical advantages of multi- vs. single-agent setups constitutes a key question for future research. Moreover, many variants of the multi-agent setup are possible. Here we opted for a single, centralized control flow pattern, where the Orchestrator agent plans for, and invokes, specialized worker agents. Many other patterns are conceivable. For instance, we might consider less centralized control flows, such as a peer-to-peer setting where each agent decides on its own which other agent should take control next. At the other end of the spectrum, we might consider an even more rigid control flow where the orchestrator follows its own plan strictly (e.g., by encoding it as an executable program), rather than simply maintaining the plan it its prompt for chain-of-thought prompting. Determining which control flow works best for which tasks is of considerable theoretical and practical importance.
理解并量化多智能体配置相较于单智能体配置的实证优势，是未来研究的一个关键问题。此外，多智能体配置还存在许多可能的变体。在此，我们选择了单一的集中式控制流模式，由 Orchestrator 智能体为专门的工作智能体制定计划并调用它们。还可以设想许多其他模式。例如，我们可以考虑集中程度较低的控制流，如对等配置，其中每个智能体自行决定接下来应由哪个其他智能体接管控制权。在另一个极端，我们可以考虑更加严格的控制流，其中编排器严格遵循自己的计划（例如，将计划编码为可执行程序），而非仅仅将计划保留在提示词中以进行思维链提示。确定何种控制流最适合何种任务，具有重要的理论意义和实践意义。
In addition to the above-mentioned control-flow considerations, an alternate design dimension relates to the axes along which work is divided. Magentic-One’s design diverges from other recent examples of multi-agent systems in that agents take on functional or tool-based responsibilities (web browser, computer terminal, etc.), rather than role-based responsibilities analogous to human teams (planner, researcher, data analyst, critic, etc.). In our experience, tool-centric agents can provide a cleaner separation of concerns compared to role-based agents, and a cleaner path to re-usability and compositionality – if a web browser is a generic, multi-purpose tool, then a capable WebSurfer agent may hope to be generic and multi-purpose as well. Conversely, role-based patterns may require multiple agents to have redundant capabilities, while each agent fills only highly-specialized niche roles. For example, both a researcher and data analyst agent may need to operate a web browser or write code to complete their assigned tasks. Future work should empirically compare the performance of teams built with function- and role-based agents and examine the impact of each approach on the ease of development and debugging.
除了上述控制流方面的考量，另一个设计维度涉及工作划分所依据的维度。Magentic-One 的设计不同于近期其他多智能体系统实例之处在于，其智能体承担的是基于功能或工具的职责（网页浏览器、计算机终端等），而非类似人类团队的基于角色的职责（规划者、研究员、数据分析师、评审者等）。根据我们的经验，与基于角色的智能体相比，以工具为中心的智能体可以实现更清晰的关注点分离，并为可复用性和可组合性提供更清晰的实现路径——如果网页浏览器是一种通用的多用途工具，那么能力出众的 WebSurfer 智能体也有望具备通用性和多用途性。反过来，基于角色的模式可能要求多个智能体具备重复的能力，而每个智能体却只承担高度专门化的细分角色。例如，研究员智能体和数据分析师智能体可能都需要操作网页浏览器或编写代码，以完成分配给它们的任务。未来工作应通过实证研究比较由基于功能和基于角色的智能体构成的团队的性能，并考察这两种方法各自对开发和调试便利性的影响。
6.2 Limitations6.2 局限性
Our work necessarily comes with certain limitations, some of which affect today’s state of the field in general, and some of which are specific to our solution:
我们的工作不可避免地存在某些局限，其中一些普遍影响着该领域当前的发展状况，另一些则是我们的解决方案所特有的：
Accuracy-focused evaluation: Similar to other state-of-the-art systems, Magentic-One was evaluated on benchmarks that consider only the accuracy or correctness of final results. While considerably easier and more convenient to measure, such evaluations overlook important considerations such as cost, latency, user preference and user value [18]. For example, even a partially correct trajectory may be valuable [9], whereas a perfectly accurate answer, delivered too late or at too high cost, may have no, or even negative, value. Designing evaluation protocols that incorporate these considerations, and that include subjective or open-ended tasks where correctness is less clear, remains an ongoing open-challenge in this the field.
以准确性为中心的评估：与其他最先进的系统类似，Magentic-One 的评估采用了仅考虑最终结果准确性或正确性的基准。尽管此类评估在测量上容易且方便得多，但它们忽略了成本、延迟、用户偏好和用户价值等重要考量 [18]。例如，即使是部分正确的执行轨迹也可能有价值 [9]，而完全准确的答案如果给出得太晚或成本过高，则可能没有价值，甚至产生负价值。设计能够纳入这些考量，并涵盖正确性较难明确判定的主观任务或开放式任务的评估方案，仍是该领域尚待解决的挑战。
High cost and latency: Although it was not part of the formal evaluation of Magentic-One, we would be remiss to skip mention of cost and latency in any discussion of limitations [18]. Magentic-One requires dozens of iterations and LLM calls to solve most problems. The latency and cost of those calls can be prohibitive, incurring perhaps several US dollars, and tens of minutes per task. We believe we can reduce these costs through targeted application of smaller local models, for example to support tool use in FileSurfer and WebSurfer, or set-of-mark action grounding in WebSurfer. Adding human oversight and humans-in-the-loop, may also save costs by reducing the number of iterations incurred when agents are stuck and problem-solving. This remains an active and ongoing area of future research.
成本高、延迟大：尽管成本和延迟并未纳入 Magentic-One 的正式评估，但在讨论局限性时，如果不提及这两点，就是我们的疏漏 [18]。Magentic-One 在解决大多数问题时，需要进行数十次迭代和 LLM 调用。这些调用产生的延迟和成本可能高得令人难以承受，每项任务可能需要花费数美元，并耗时数十分钟。我们认为，可以通过有针对性地使用规模较小的本地模型来降低这些成本，例如，用于支持 FileSurfer 和 WebSurfer 中的工具使用，或 WebSurfer 中基于标记集（set-of-mark）的动作定位。引入人工监督和人在回路机制，也可能通过减少智能体陷入困境并尝试解决问题时产生的迭代次数来节省成本。这仍是未来研究中一个持续活跃的方向。
Limited modalities: Magentic-One cannot currently process or navigate all modalities. For example, WebSurfer cannot watch online videos – though it often compensates by consulting transcripts or captions. Likewise, FileSurfer operates by converting all documents to Markdown, making it impossible to answer questions about a document’s figures, visual presentation style, or layout. Audio files are similarly processed through a speech transcription model, so no agents can answer questions about music, or non-speech content. Benchmarks like GAIA exercise each of these skills. We would expect both benchmark and general task performance to improve with expanded support of multi-modal content. Future options include expanding Magentic-One’s WebSurfer and FileSurfer agents’ multi-modal capabilities or adding Audio and VideoSurfer agents specialized in handling audio and video processing tasks to the Magentic-One team. The latter approach is most inline with the value proposition of the multi-agent paradigm around easing development and reuse.
模态受限：Magentic-One 目前无法处理或浏览所有模态的内容。例如，WebSurfer 无法观看在线视频，不过它经常通过查阅转录文本或字幕来弥补这一不足。同样，FileSurfer 通过将所有文档转换为 Markdown 来开展工作，因此无法回答有关文档中的图、视觉呈现风格或版式的问题。音频文件也采用类似方式，通过语音转录模型进行处理，因此没有任何智能体能够回答有关音乐或非语音内容的问题。GAIA 等基准测试会考查上述每一项能力。我们预计，随着对多模态内容的支持范围扩大，基准测试表现和一般任务表现都会有所提升。未来可考虑的方案包括扩展 Magentic-One 中 WebSurfer 和 FileSurfer 智能体的多模态能力，或向 Magentic-One 团队中加入专门处理音频和视频处理任务的 Audio 和 VideoSurfer 智能体。后一种方法最符合多智能体范式在简化开发和促进复用方面的价值主张。
Limited action space: While agents in Magentic-One are afforded tools for the most common actions, tooling is not comprehensive. This simplifies the task of action grounding, but can lead to paths that are impossible to execute. For instance, the WebSurfer agent cannot hover over items on a webpage, or drag and resize elements. This can be limiting when interacting with maps, for example. Likewise, FileSurfer cannot handle all document types, and the Coder and Computer Terminal agents cannot execute code that requires API keys, or access to external databased or computational resources. We expect this limitation to close, over time, from two directions: first, we expect tool use to standardize across the industry, greatly enriching the set of tools available to agents. Second, similar to WebSurfer, agents will become better able to use operating systems and applications, affording them access to a broad range of tools developed for people.
动作空间受限：虽然 Magentic-One 中的智能体配备了执行最常见动作所需的工具，但工具覆盖并不全面。这简化了动作定位任务，但可能导致出现无法执行的路径。例如，WebSurfer 智能体无法将鼠标悬停在网页项目上，也无法拖动元素或调整其大小。例如，在与地图交互时，这可能构成限制。同样，FileSurfer 无法处理所有文档类型，而 Coder 和 Computer Terminal 智能体无法执行需要 API 密钥或需要访问外部数据库或计算资源的代码。我们预计，随着时间推移，这一局限将从两个方向得到消除：首先，我们预计工具使用将在整个行业中实现标准化，从而大幅丰富智能体可用的工具集合。其次，与 WebSurfer 类似，智能体将能够更好地使用操作系统和应用程序，从而能够使用为人类开发的广泛工具。
Limited coding capabilities: The Magentic-One Coder agent is particularly simple: it writes a new standalone Python program in respose to each coding request. In cases, where a prior coding attempt requires debugging, the Coder must correct the code by outputting an entirely new code listing. This is clearly not ideal. Two important limitations arise from this design choice: first, the Coder is ill-suited to operate over existing complex, or multi-file, code bases. Overcoming this limitation will be necessary to be competitive on benchmarks like SWE-bench [17]. Second, the Coder sometimes fails because it expects functions that it previously defined to be available later in the workflow. Migrating to a Jupyter Notebook-like design, where later invocations simply add cells to a notebook, might mitigate this particular issue. This capability is presently supported by the AutoGen library upon which Magentic-One is built, and should be explored further.
编码能力有限：Magentic-One 的 Coder 智能体尤其简单：它针对每个编码请求编写一个新的独立 Python 程序。当先前的编码尝试需要调试时，Coder 必须输出一份全新的完整代码来修正代码。这显然并不理想。这一设计选择带来了两个重要局限：首先，Coder 不适合处理现有的复杂代码库或多文件代码库。要在 SWE-bench [17] 等基准测试中具备竞争力，就必须克服这一局限。其次，Coder 有时会失败，因为它预期自己先前定义的函数在工作流后续阶段仍然可用。改用类似 Jupyter Notebook 的设计，让后续调用仅向笔记本中添加单元格，或许可以缓解这一特定问题。Magentic-One 所基于的 AutoGen 库目前已支持这一能力，值得进一步探索。
Fixed team membership: Additionally, Magentic-One’s composition is fixed to a common set of five agents: Orchestrator, WebSurfer, FileSurfer, Coder, and ComputerTerminal. When agents are not needed, they simply serve as a distraction to the Orchestrator, and this may lower performance. Conversely, when extra expertise might be needed, it is simply unavailable. We can easily imagine an alternative approach where agents are added or removed dynamically, based on the task need.
团队成员固定：此外，Magentic-One 的组成固定为一组通用的五个智能体：Orchestrator、WebSurfer、FileSurfer、Coder 和 ComputerTerminal。当某些智能体并非任务所需时，它们只会分散 Orchestrator 的注意力，这可能降低性能。反之，当可能需要额外的专业能力时，这些能力却无法获得。我们很容易设想另一种方法，即根据任务需求动态添加或移除智能体。
Limited learning: Finally, although Magentic-One can adapt its strategy based on trial and error within a single task attempt, such insights are discarded between tasks. We observe the direct consequences of this design in WebArena, where many problems share a common set of core sub-tasks (e.g., finding a particular thread or user profile). When competing on this benchmark, Magentic-One’s agents need to discover and rediscover solutions to these sub-tasks over and over. This is exhausting and frustrating to watch, is highly prone to error, and can incur significant additional costs. Overcoming this limitation through long-term memory is a key direction for future research.
学习能力有限：最后，尽管 Magentic-One 可以在单次任务尝试中通过试错调整策略，但这些经验不会在不同任务之间保留。我们在 WebArena 中观察到了这一设计的直接后果，其中许多问题都包含一组共同的核心子任务（例如，查找特定帖子或用户个人资料）。在此基准上参与评测时，Magentic-One 的智能体需要一遍又一遍地发现和重新发现这些子任务的解决方案。这一过程令人看得疲惫且沮丧，极易出错，还可能产生大量额外成本。通过长期记忆克服这一局限，是未来研究的一个关键方向。
6.3 Risks and Mitigations6.3 风险与缓解措施
The agents described in this paper interact with a digital world designed for, and inhabited by, humans. This carries inherent and undeniable risks. In our work we mitigate such risks by running all tasks in containers, leveraging synthetic environments like WebArena, choosing models with strong alignment and pre- and post-generation filtering, and by closely monitoring logs during and after execution. Nevertheless, we observed the agents attempt steps that would otherwise be risky. For example, during development, a mis-configuration prevented agents from successfully logging in to a particular WebArena website. The agents attempted to log in to that website until the repeated attempts caused the account to be temporarily suspended. The agents then attempted to reset the account’s password. In other cases, agents recognized that the WebArena Postmill website was not Reddit, then directed agents to the real website to commence work – this was ultimately blocked by network-layer restrictions we had put in place. Likewise, we observed cases where agents quickly accepted cookie agreements and website terms and conditions without any human involvement (though captchas were correctly refused). More worryingly, in a handful of cases – and until prompted otherwise – the agents occasionally attempted to recruit other humans for help (e.g., by posting to social media, emailing textbook authors, or, in one case, drafting a freedom of information request to a government entity). In each of these cases, the agents failed because they did not have access to the requisite tools or accounts, and/or were stopped by human observers. To this end, it is imperative that agents operate under a strict principle of least privilege, and maximum oversight.
本文描述的智能体与一个为人类设计、并由人类活动于其中的数字世界进行交互。这本身就带来了不可否认的风险。在我们的工作中，我们通过在容器中运行所有任务、使用 WebArena 等合成环境、选择具有较强对齐能力且配有生成前后过滤机制的模型，以及在执行期间和执行结束后密切监控日志，来缓解此类风险。尽管如此，我们仍观察到智能体尝试执行某些步骤；若没有这些措施，这些步骤就会带来风险。例如，在开发过程中，一处配置错误导致智能体无法成功登录某个 WebArena 网站。智能体不断尝试登录该网站，直到反复尝试导致账户被暂时停用。随后，智能体尝试重置该账户的密码。在其他情况下，智能体识别出 WebArena 中的 Postmill 网站并非 Reddit，随后指示其他智能体前往真实网站开始工作——这一行为最终被我们设置的网络层限制所阻止。同样，我们还观察到，在没有任何人类参与的情况下，智能体迅速接受了 Cookie 协议和网站条款与条件（不过，它们确实正确地拒绝了验证码）。更令人担忧的是，在少数情况下，智能体偶尔会尝试寻求人类帮助，直到收到要求其停止这样做的提示为止（例如，在社交媒体上发帖、向教科书作者发送电子邮件，或在某个案例中，起草一份向政府机构提出的信息公开申请）。在上述每种情况下，智能体都未能成功，因为它们无法使用所需的工具或账户，和／或被人类观察者制止。因此，智能体必须严格遵循最小权限原则，并在最大程度的监督下运行。
In addition to these observed and mitigated risks, we can anticipate new risks from such agentic systems on the horizon. As an example, as agents operate on the public internet, it is possible that they are subject to the same phishing, social engineering, and misinformation attacks that target human web surfers. The fact that the WebSurfer only occasionally recognized that Postmill was not Reddit, in WebArena, lends credence to the concern that agents can be fooled. If agents are equipped with a user’s personal information – for example, to complete tasks on their behalf – then this could potentially put that information at risk. Moreover, we can imagine that such attacks may be made more reliable and effective if attackers anticipate agentic use, and seed external material with specially crafted instructions or prompt-injections. To address these challenges, we can imagine several mitigations such as increasing human oversight, equipping agents with tools to validate external information (e.g., checking for typo-squatting in URLs, and ensuring TLS certificates, etc.), and including phishing rejection examples and other web-savvy skills in a model’s post-training and instruction tuning.
除了这些已观察到并得到缓解的风险之外，我们还可以预见，此类智能体系统未来会带来新的风险。例如，当智能体在公共互联网上运行时，它们可能遭受与人类上网者相同的网络钓鱼、社会工程和虚假信息攻击。在 WebArena 中，WebSurfer 只是偶尔才识别出 Postmill 并非 Reddit，这一事实进一步支持了智能体可能受骗的担忧。如果智能体获得了用户的个人信息——例如，为了代表用户完成任务——那么这些信息就可能面临风险。此外，我们可以设想，如果攻击者预料到智能体会使用这些外部材料，并在其中植入精心构造的指令或提示注入内容，那么此类攻击可能会变得更加可靠和有效。为应对这些挑战，我们可以设想若干缓解措施，例如加强人类监督，为智能体配备用于验证外部信息的工具（例如，检查 URL 中是否存在拼写仿冒，并确认 TLS 证书等），以及在模型的后训练和指令微调中纳入拒绝网络钓鱼的示例和其他熟练应对网络环境的技能。
Another cross-cutting mitigation we anticipate becoming important is equipping agents with an understanding of which actions are easily reversible, which are reversible with some effort, and which cannot be undone. As an example, deleting files, sending emails, and filing forms, are unlikely to be easily reversed. This concept is explored in some detail in [78], which provides a compelling framework for considering agent safety. When faced with a high-cost or irreversible action, systems should be designed to pause, and to seek human input.
我们预计，另一项将变得重要且适用于多个方面的缓解措施，是让智能体理解哪些操作容易撤销、哪些操作需要付出一定努力才能撤销，以及哪些操作无法撤销。例如，删除文件、发送电子邮件和提交表单，都不太可能轻易撤销。文献 [78] 对这一概念进行了较为详细的探讨，并提供了一个颇具说服力的框架，用于思考智能体安全问题。系统应被设计为在面临高成本或不可逆的操作时暂停，并征求人类意见。
Recent research has also investigated how interactions between multiple agents, such as iterative requests, long contexts, or cascading errors, may impact the effectiveness of the existing model alignment and guardrails upon which we rely. For example, the crescendo multi-turn attack [44], operates by prompting the model with a benign request, the slowly escalates the requests, building a pattern of agent compliance, until finally asking for a response that would otherwise be refused. In a multi-agent system, it is possible that a malicious agent, or an unfortunate accident, could result in a similar pattern of escalation. Fortunately, strong pre- and post-filtering, on the prompts and responses, remain a reasonable mitigation to such risks for the short-term. Moving forward, we strongly encourage model alignment work to focus on multi-turn scenarios. We also believe that red-team exercises are imperative to identify and mitigate such risks.
近期研究还考察了多个智能体之间的交互，例如反复请求、长上下文或级联错误，可能如何影响我们所依赖的现有模型对齐和防护机制的有效性。例如，渐强式多轮攻击（crescendo multi-turn attack）[44] 的做法是先用一个无害请求提示模型，然后逐步升级请求，形成智能体持续服从的模式，最终要求模型给出原本会被拒绝的回答。在多智能体系统中，恶意智能体或不幸的意外事件可能导致类似的逐步升级模式。幸运的是，短期内，对提示和回答进行严格的前置与后置过滤，仍然是缓解此类风险的合理措施。展望未来，我们强烈建议模型对齐工作重点关注多轮交互场景。我们还认为，开展红队演练对于识别和缓解此类风险至关重要。
Finally, there are potential long-term societal impacts of agentic systems, such as the potential to deskill or replace workers, leading to potential economic disruption. We believe it is therefore critical to work towards designing systems that facilitate effective collaboration between people and agents, such that humans and agents working together can achieve more than agents working alone.
最后，智能体系统还可能产生长期的社会影响，例如可能导致劳动者技能退化或取代劳动者，进而可能引发经济动荡。因此，我们认为，致力于设计能够促进人与智能体有效协作的系统至关重要，使人类与智能体协同工作时能够取得比智能体单独工作更大的成效。
7 Conclusions7 结论
In this work we introduced Magentic-One, a generalist multi-agent system for ad-hoc, open-ended, file- and web-based tasks. Magentic-One uses a multi-agent architecture with a lead Orchestrator agent that directs four other agents. The Orchestrator agent is able to plan, track progress, and recover from errors with a ledger-based orchestration. The remaining agents each specializes in the operation of generally-useful tools such as web browsers, file browsers, and computer console terminals. We show that Magentic-One is statistically competitive with other state-of-the-art (SOTA) systems on three challenging benchmarks, demonstrating both strong performance and generalization. Additionally, we have open-sourced the implementation of Magentic-One, which includes a reference framework for event-driven agents using the AutoGen framework. Finally, we discussed the limitations of Magentic-One, and the risks introduced by generalist AI agents, together with possible mitigation. To this end, Magentic-One represents a significant development in agentic systems capable of solving open-ended tasks.
在本研究中，我们介绍了 Magentic-One，这是一个通用多智能体系统，用于处理临时性的、开放式的、基于文件和网络的任务。Magentic-One 采用多智能体架构，由一个主导的 Orchestrator 智能体指挥其他四个智能体。Orchestrator 智能体通过基于账本的编排机制，能够进行规划、跟踪进展并从错误中恢复。其余智能体各自专门负责操作具有广泛用途的工具，例如网页浏览器、文件浏览器和计算机控制台终端。我们表明，Magentic-One 在三个具有挑战性的基准测试上，在统计意义上可与其他最先进（SOTA）的系统相媲美，展现出强大的性能和泛化能力。此外，我们已将 Magentic-One 的实现开源，其中包含一个使用 AutoGen 框架构建的事件驱动智能体参考框架。最后，我们讨论了 Magentic-One 的局限性、通用 AI 智能体带来的风险，以及可能的缓解措施。就此而言，Magentic-One 代表了能够解决开放式任务的智能体系统的一项重要进展。

In Table 1, we report the mean and an error bar for each reported method on the three benchmarks. To obtain the error bar we used a simple Wald 95% confidence interval for the proportion. The Wald confidence interval assumes a normal approximation for the sample mean which is only valid for larger sample sizes and is only accurate for proportions not near 0 or 1. Our application meets these criteria: the smallest evaluated test set consists of 181 samples, and all reported results hover around 30% with the exception of GPT-4. We also computed confidence intervals using the Wilson interval and found similar results (though Wilson intervals are not symmetric). For simplicity, we report only Wald intervals here.
在表 1 中，我们报告了所列各方法在三个基准测试上的均值及误差条。为获得误差条，我们使用了针对比例的简单 Wald 95% 置信区间。Wald 置信区间假定样本均值可用正态分布近似，这一近似仅在样本量较大时成立，并且仅在比例不接近 0 或 1 时才准确。我们的应用满足这些条件：所评估的最小测试集包含 181 个样本，并且除 GPT-4 外，所有报告的结果都在 30% 左右。我们还使用 Wilson 区间计算了置信区间，发现结果相近（尽管 Wilson 区间并不对称）。为简便起见，此处仅报告 Wald 区间。

The z-test for proportions is the only feasible test in our setting because we only have the mean accuracy and not results on each example (the task-level test set results are hidden by the benchamrks). Therefore, we cannot apply McNemar’s Test or a pairwise t-test. The limitation of the z-test is that it ignores pairing of the data and is generally conservative. 我们还报告了将 Magentic-One (GPT-4o, o1) 与所列各方法进行比较的统计检验结果。我们使用 z 检验，将 Magentic-One 的准确率与表 1 中的各个基线进行比较。比例 z 检验是我们在当前条件下唯一可行的检验方法，因为我们只有平均准确率，而没有每个样本的结果（这些基准测试并不公开任务级别的测试集结果）。因此，我们无法采用 McNemar 检验或配对 t 检验。z 检验的局限性在于，它忽略了数据的配对关系，并且通常较为保守。我们希望未来的基准测试排行榜除了报告均值之外，也能公布置信区间。
