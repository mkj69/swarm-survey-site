摘要Abstract
近期研究报告称，多智能体 LLM 系统（MAS）表现出色，但这些性能提升往往与测试时计算量的增加相混杂。 Recent work reports strong performance from multi-agent LLM systems (MAS), but these gains are often confounded by increased test-time computation. 当计算量保持一致时，单智能体系统（SAS）的表现可以达到或超过 MAS，但这一比较背后的理论依据和评估方法仍不明确。 When computation is normalized, single-agent systems (SAS) can match or outperform MAS, yet the theoretical basis and evaluation methodology behind this comparison remain unclear. 我们提出了一个基于数据处理不等式（Data Processing Inequality）的信息论论证，表明在推理词元预算固定且上下文得到充分利用的条件下，单智能体系统的信息利用效率更高。 We present an information-theoretic argument, grounded in the Data Processing Inequality, suggesting that under a fixed reasoning-token budget and with perfect context utilization, single-agent systems are more information-efficient. 这一视角进一步预测，当单个智能体对上下文的有效利用能力下降，或投入更多计算资源时，多智能体系统便会具备竞争力。 This perspective further predicts that multi-agent systems become competitive when a single agent’s effective context utilization is degraded, or when more compute is expended. 我们在涵盖三个模型系列（Qwen3、DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama 和 Gemini 2.5）的受控实证研究中检验这些预测，在相同预算下比较 SAS 与多种 MAS 架构。 We test these predictions in a controlled empirical study across three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama, and Gemini 2.5), comparing SAS with multiple MAS architectures under matched budgets. 我们发现，当推理词元数量保持一致时，SAS 在多跳推理任务中的表现始终达到或超过 MAS。 We find that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS on multi-hop reasoning tasks when reasoning tokens are held constant. 除总体性能外，我们还对系统行为和评估方法进行了详细的诊断分析。 Beyond aggregate performance, we conduct a detailed diagnostic analysis of system behavior and evaluation methodology. 我们发现，基于 API 的预算控制（尤其是在 Gemini 2.5 中）和标准基准测试均存在显著的伪象，二者都可能夸大 MAS 的表观收益。 We identify significant artifacts in API-based budget control (particularly in Gemini 2.5) and in standard benchmarks, both of which can inflate apparent gains from MAS. 总体而言，我们的结果表明，对于多跳推理任务，许多已报告的多智能体系统优势，更适合用未计入的计算量和上下文效应来解释，而非归因于架构本身的固有优势；这些结果也凸显了理解并明确控制智能体系统中计算、上下文与协调之间权衡的重要性。 Overall, our results suggest that, for multi-hop reasoning tasks, many reported advantages of multi-agent systems are better explained by unaccounted computation and context effects rather than inherent architectural benefits, and highlight the importance of understanding and explicitly controlling the trade-offs between compute, context, and coordination in agentic systems.

1 引言1 Introduction
多智能体 LLM 架构（MAS），包括规划器、角色扮演系统、辩论框架以及按工具专门分工的智能体集群，已在一系列任务中展现出出色的实证性能。 Multi-agent LLM architectures (MAS), including planners, role-playing systems, debate frameworks, and tool-specialized swarms, have demonstrated strong empirical performance across a range of tasks. 从总体上看，这些方法将推理分解到多个智能体上，各智能体基于局部上下文运行，并通过生成的文本进行通信。 At a high level, these approaches decompose reasoning across multiple agents that operate over partial contexts and communicate via generated text. 相比之下，单智能体系统（SAS）在单一、统一的上下文中进行推理，依赖内部的词元级计算，而非显式的智能体间通信。 In contrast, single-agent systems (SAS) perform reasoning within a single, unified context, relying on internal token-level computation rather than explicit inter-agent communication.
然而，MAS 与 SAS 之间的比较往往受到测试时计算量差异的混杂影响。 However, comparisons between MAS and SAS are often confounded by differences in test-time computation. MAS 通常因更长的推理轨迹或多次智能体交互而消耗更多词元，因此难以判断其性能提升究竟来自架构优势，还是仅仅来自计算量的增加。 MAS typically consume more tokens through longer reasoning traces or multiple agent interactions, making it unclear whether their gains arise from architectural advantages or simply from increased compute. 近期考虑预算因素的研究表明，当计算量保持一致时，许多此类策略的表现不及强大的单智能体基线（Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025）。 Recent budget-aware studies suggest that, when computation is normalized, many such strategies underperform strong single-agent baselines (Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025).
在本研究中，我们明确聚焦于思考词元预算，重新审视这一问题；我们将思考词元预算定义为用于中间推理的词元总数，不包括提示和最终答案。 In this work, we revisit this question under an explicit focus on thinking token budgets, which we define as the total number of tokens used for intermediate reasoning, excluding prompts and final answers. 我们聚焦于多跳推理任务，并提出三个核心问题：为什么 SAS 在固定预算下可能优于 MAS，MAS 何时会具备竞争力，以及应如何可靠地开展此类比较？ We focus on multi-hop reasoning tasks and ask three central questions: why might SAS outperform MAS under fixed budgets, when do MAS become competitive, and how should such comparisons be conducted reliably?
我们首先提供一个基于数据处理不等式的信息论视角，表明在词元预算固定的条件下，多智能体分解会引入额外的通信瓶颈，进而可能导致信息损失。 We first provide an information-theoretic perspective, based on the Data Processing Inequality, suggesting that under fixed token budgets, multi-agent decompositions introduce additional communication bottlenecks that can lead to information loss. 这一视角也阐明了 MAS 何时可能具有优势：具体而言，当单个智能体对上下文的有效利用能力下降时（例如，由于上下文过长或包含噪声），或者当 MAS 通过延长交互而受益于额外且未被计入的计算量时。 This perspective also clarifies when MAS can be advantageous: specifically, when a single agent’s effective context utilization is degraded (e.g., due to long or noisy contexts), or when MAS benefit from additional unaccounted computation through extended interactions.
随后，我们在涵盖三个模型系列（Qwen3、DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama 和 Gemini 2.5）的受控实证研究中检验这些预测，在相同推理词元预算下比较 SAS 与多种 MAS 架构。 We then test these predictions in a controlled empirical study across three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama, and Gemini 2.5), comparing SAS and multiple MAS architectures under matched reasoning-token budgets. 我们发现，在这些约束下，SAS 在多跳推理任务中的表现始终达到或超过 MAS。 We find that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS on multi-hop reasoning tasks under these constraints.
除总体性能外，我们还对评估方法和模型行为进行了详细的诊断分析。 Beyond aggregate performance, we perform a detailed diagnostic analysis of evaluation methodology and model behavior. 我们识别出：(i) 基于 API 的预算控制中会扭曲有效计算量的伪象，(ii) 通过改写暴露出的基准测试漏洞，以及 (iii) 不同架构在失败模式上的系统性差异。 We identify (i) artifacts in API-based budget control that distort effective computation, (ii) benchmark vulnerabilities exposed through paraphrasing, and (iii) systematic differences in failure modes across architectures.

从信息论视角比较固定预算下的 SAS 与 MAS，解释多智能体分解如何引入额外的通信瓶颈（§3）。 An information-theoretic perspective on SAS vs. MAS under fixed budgets, explaining how multi-agent decompositions introduce additional communication bottlenecks (§3).
开展涵盖多个模型系列的受控实证比较，表明在相同推理词元预算下，SAS 在多跳推理任务中的表现始终达到或超过 MAS（§5）。 A controlled empirical comparison across multiple model families, showing that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS under matched reasoning-token budgets on multi-hop reasoning tasks (§5).

2 相关工作2 Related Work
预算受控的评估：Budget-Controlled Evaluation:
近期研究强调，推理策略之间的比较往往受到测试时计算量不等这一混杂因素的影响。 Recent work has emphasized that comparisons between reasoning strategies are often confounded by unequal test-time computation. 《词元经济中的推理》（Reasoning in Token Economies）及其后续研究表明，一旦词元或计算预算相匹配，许多复杂的提示或搜索策略便无法优于更简单的基线（Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025）。 Reasoning in Token Economies and follow-up work show that many elaborate prompting or search strategies fail to outperform simpler baselines once token or compute budgets are matched (Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025). 我们的工作与这一研究方向一致，但有两点不同：我们仅将思考词元作为受控资源，并且研究单智能体与多智能体架构之间的比较，而非同一架构内部的预算分配。 Our work is aligned with this line, but differs in two ways: we treat only thinking tokens as the controlled resource, and we study the comparison between single-agent and multi-agent architectures rather than budget allocation within one architecture.
MAS 何时以及为何有效：When and why MAS helps:
同期研究进一步明确，MAS 的收益取决于所处条件和具体实现。 Concurrent works sharpen the picture that MAS gains are dependent on regime and implementation. Anthropic (2025) 认为，MAS 的表面优势在很大程度上来自计算量的增加。 Anthropic (2025) argues that much of the apparent advantage of MAS comes from increased compute. 另一方面，Kim et al. (2025) 表明，在统一计算量后，智能体带来的收益往往集中于模型较弱或任务条件更困难的场景，并随着基础模型能力的提升而减小（甚至逆转），这凸显了协调开销是一项首要制约因素。 On the other hand, Kim et al. (2025) shows that once computation is normalized, agentic benefits tend to concentrate in weaker model or harder-regime settings and diminish (or even reverse) as base model capability increases, highlighting coordination overhead as a first-order constraint. 此外，Cemri et al. (2025) 引入了多智能体追踪方法和失效分类体系，解释了编排如何引发偏移、信息丢失，或因评估伪象而产生具有误导性的性能提升。 Additionally, Cemri et al. (2025) introduce multi-agent tracing and a failure taxonomy that explains how orchestration can induce drift, information loss, or misleading improvements from evaluation artifacts. 这些失效模式与我们观察到的过度探索、聚合/提取错误以及不透明的 API 用量核算相一致。 These failure modes align with our observed over-exploration, aggregation/extraction errors, and opaque API accounting. 最后，Ke et al. (2026) 通过整体配置选择研究学习式编排，并提出用于 MAS 受控基准测试的评估维度，强调性能提升取决于任务结构和验证协议，而非 MAS 在所有情况下都更优。 Finally, Ke et al. (2026) study learned orchestration via holistic configuration selection and propose controlled benchmarking axes for MAS, emphasizing that improvements depend on task structure and verification protocols rather than MAS being universally superior. 综合来看，这些结果促成了本研究的核心设计：(i) 严格控制主要混杂因素（思考词元预算），(ii) 诊断协调与通信如何影响准确率，以及 (iii) 刻画 MAS 或混合设计能够具备竞争力的具体条件（例如上下文退化）。 Taken together, these results motivate the core design of our study: (i) strictly control the primary confound (thinking token budget), (ii) diagnose how coordination and communication affect accuracy, and (iii) characterize concrete regimes (e.g., degraded context) where MAS or hybrid designs can become competitive.
SAS 与 MAS：SAS vs. MAS:
越来越多的实证证据表明，随着前沿模型能力的提升，编排的收益逐渐减小，SAS 可以达到或超过 MAS 的表现（Gao et al., 2025）。 Empirical evidence increasingly suggests that as frontier models improve, the benefits of orchestration diminish and SAS can match or surpass MAS (Gao et al., 2025). 在教育分析领域，采用少样本提示的 SAS 在反思评估任务中的表现优于 MAS（Li et al., 2025a）。 In education analytics, SAS with few-shot prompting outperformed MAS for reflection assessment (Li et al., 2025a). 面向协作智能体的基准测试（例如 MedAgentBoard）进一步揭示，MAS 的优势取决于具体任务，并不具有普遍性（Zhu et al., 2025）。 Benchmarks for collaborative agents (e.g., MedAgentBoard) further reveal that MAS advantages are task-specific and not universal (Zhu et al., 2025). 我们的研究结果在更严格的计算量控制下补充了这些研究。 Our findings complement these studies under stricter compute controls.
多智能体协作机制：Multi-agent collaboration mechanisms:
既有研究提出了多种组织 LLM 之间协作的方式，包括辩论、角色专业化、集成/自一致性以及反思（Du et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Shinn et al., 2023）。 Prior work has proposed many ways to structure collaboration between LLMs, including debate, role specialization, ensemble/self-consistency, and reflection (Du et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Shinn et al., 2023). 我们并未将 MAS 视为一种单一设计，而是在统一的预算匹配框架下评估若干具有代表性的机制。 Rather than treating MAS as a single design, we evaluate several representative mechanisms under a common matched-budget framework. 这使我们能够区分架构结构带来的收益与单纯增加计算投入带来的收益。 This allows us to separate gains due to architectural structure from gains due to simply spending more computation.
上下文长度、退化与长上下文利用：Context length, degradation, and long-context utilization:
无论采用何种架构，现代 LLM 都无法完美利用长上下文：即使相关信息确实存在，注意力稀释、噪声敏感性、上下文混淆和位置偏差也都可能削弱推理能力。 Independent of architecture, modern LLMs do not use long context perfectly: attention dilution, noise sensitivity, context confusion, and positional bias can all degrade reasoning even when relevant information is present. 近期研究从多个角度对这些效应进行了形式化描述，包括上下文退化与长上下文压力测试（Du et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025c; Lu et al., 2024）、对信息所处位置敏感的失效现象，如中间信息丢失效应（lost in the middle effect）（Liu et al., 2024），以及随着输入长度增加而出现的更广泛的可靠性衰减（Hong et al., 2025）。 Recent work has formalized these effects from several angles, including context degradation and long-context stress testing (Du et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025c; Lu et al., 2024), failures that are sensitive to their position such as the lost in the middle effect (Liu et al., 2024), and broader reliability decay as input length grows (Hong et al., 2025). 我们通过显式建模退化后的有效上下文，并测试在单智能体的上下文利用能力下降时，结构化多智能体流水线何时开始具备竞争力，将这些文献与 SAS 和 MAS 的比较问题联系起来。 We connect this literature to the SAS-versus-MAS question by explicitly modeling degraded effective context and by testing when structured multi-agent pipelines become competitive as single-agent context use deteriorates.
3 理论依据3 Theoretical justification
本节旨在形式化描述思考词元预算约束下单智能体系统与多智能体系统的比较，并解释为何单智能体的表现通常至少不逊于多智能体，以及多智能体设计在何种情况下能够发挥作用。 This section aims to formalize the comparison between single-agent and multi-agent systems under a thinking-token budget, and explain both why single agents often perform at least as well and when multi-agent designs can help.
令 $Y$ 表示正确答案，$C$ 表示单智能体 LLM 可用的完整上下文（包括先前的推理和中间状态），$M=g(C)$ 表示多智能体系统中智能体之间传递的消息或摘要。 Let $Y$ denote the correct answer, $C$ the full context available to a single-agent LLM (including prior reasoning and intermediate states), and $M=g(C)$ the messages or summaries passed between agents in a multi-agent system.
由于 $M$ 是 $C$ 的一个（可能具有随机性的）函数，我们有如下马尔可夫链 Since $M$ is a (possibly stochastic) function of $C$ , we have the Markov chain 
| | $Y\longleftrightarrow C\longleftrightarrow M.$ |
根据数据处理不等式（Data Processing Inequality，DPI）（Cover and Thomas, 2006）， By the Data Processing Inequality (DPI) (Cover and Thomas, 2006), 
| | $I(Y;C)\geq I(Y;M),$ |
等价地， and equivalently, 
| | $H(Y\mid M)\geq H(Y\mid C).$ |
因此，以 $M$ 为条件时，关于 $Y$ 的剩余不确定性比以 $C$ 为条件时更大；多智能体架构无法增加与真实答案之间的互信息。 Thus, conditioning on $M$ leaves more residual uncertainty about $Y$ than conditioning on $C$ ; the multi-agent architecture cannot increase mutual information with the true answer.
现在，我们通过各架构可达到的最小预测误差来比较它们。 Now we compare architectures through their minimum achievable prediction error. 对于任意可观测表示 $X$，令 $\mathcal{D}_{X}$ 表示所有允许随机化的估计器 $\delta(\hat{y}\mid x)$ 构成的集合，并定义 For any observable representation $X$ , let $\mathcal{D}_{X}$ denote the set of all possibly randomized estimators $\delta(\hat{y}\mid x)$ , and define 
| | $P_{e}(X)\;=\;\inf_{\delta\in\mathcal{D}_{X}}\Pr[\hat{Y}\neq Y],\qquad\hat{Y}\sim\delta(\cdot\mid X).$ |
因此，$P_{e}(X)$ 是仅观测 $X$ 的预测器所能达到的最小错误概率。 Thus, $P_{e}(X)$ is the smallest achievable error probability for a predictor that only observes $X$ .
由于 $M$ 是由 $C$ 通过固定信道 $q(m\mid c)$ 生成的，因此任取一个基于 $M$ 进行预测的估计器 $\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}$，都可以通过下式诱导出一个基于 $C$ 的估计器 Since $M$ is generated from $C$ through a fixed channel $q(m\mid c)$ , taking any estimator $\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}$ that predicts from $M$ would induce an estimator based on $C$ via 
| | $\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}(\hat{y}\mid c)=\sum_{m}q(m\mid c)\,\delta_{M}(\hat{y}\mid m).$ |
该诱导估计器首先采样出与多智能体流水线从 $C$ 生成的消息相同的消息 $M$，然后应用相同的下游决策规则 $\delta_{M}$。 This induced estimator first samples the same message $M$ that the multi-agent pipeline would generate from $C$ , and then applies the same downstream decision rule $\delta_{M}$ . 因此，它复现了原始的基于 $M$ 的预测器在 $(Y,\hat{Y})$ 上的联合分布： Therefore it reproduces the same joint distribution over $(Y,\hat{Y})$ as the original predictor based on $M$ : 
| | $\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}}\neq Y]=\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{M}}\neq Y].$ |
由于每个这样的诱导估计器都属于 $\mathcal{D}_{C}$， Since every such induced estimator belongs to $\mathcal{D}_{C}$ , 
| | $\displaystyle P_{e}(C)$ | $\displaystyle=\inf_{\delta\in\mathcal{D}_{C}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta}\neq Y]$ |
| | | $\displaystyle\leq\inf_{\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}}\neq Y]$ |
| | | $\displaystyle=\inf_{\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{M}}\neq Y]$ |
| | | $\displaystyle=P_{e}(M).$ |
因此，从信息论角度可以保证，能够完整访问 $C$ 的单智能体系统，其性能至少不逊于基于 $M=g(C)$ 运行的多智能体系统。 Hence, the single-agent system (with full access to $C$ ) is information-theoretically guaranteed to perform at least as well as the multi-agent system operating on $M=g(C)$ .
3.1 上下文退化3.1 Context degradation
然而，在实践中，现代 LLM 对 $C$ 各部分的利用能力可能并不相同。 In practice, however, modern LLMs may not utilize all of $C$ equally well. 为对此建模，令 $\tilde{C}_{\alpha}=T_{\alpha}(C)$ 表示在退化程度 $\alpha\geq 0$ 下单智能体预测器可用的有效上下文，其中 $T_{\alpha}$ 可以表示删除、掩蔽、替换噪声、干扰信息注入，或更一般地，任何使相关信息更难恢复的变换。 To model this, let $\tilde{C}_{\alpha}=T_{\alpha}(C)$ denote the effective context available to the single-agent predictor under degradation level $\alpha\geq 0$ , where $T_{\alpha}$ may represent deletion, masking, substitution noise, distractor injection, or more generally any transformation that makes relevant information harder to recover. 我们假设 $T_{0}$ 为恒等映射，且退化具有单调性，即对于 $0\leq\alpha_{1}\leq\alpha_{2}$，存在一个随机映射 $S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}$，使得 We assume $T_{0}$ is the identity map and that degradation is monotone in the sense that for $0\leq\alpha_{1}\leq\alpha_{2}$ , there exists a stochastic map $S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}$ such that 
| | $\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}=S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}(\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}}).$ |
于是，我们得到马尔可夫链 Then we obtain the Markov chain 
| | $Y\longleftrightarrow C\longleftrightarrow\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}}\longleftrightarrow\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}},$ |
因此，根据数据处理不等式， and therefore, by the Data Processing Inequality, 
| | $I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}})\geq I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}).$ |

| | $H(Y\mid\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}})\leq H(Y\mid\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}).$ |

4 方法4 Approach
4.1 任务与数据集4.1 Tasks and Datasets
我们在 FRAMES (Krishna et al., 2025) 和 MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022) 上进行评估，两者均包含具有简洁标准答案的多跳世界知识问题。 We evaluate on FRAMES (Krishna et al., 2025) and MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022), both are multi-hop world knowledge questions with concise ground truth answers. 对于 MuSiQue，我们仅保留 4 跳问题，这些问题足够复杂，能够对 SAS 和 MAS 都构成挑战。 For MuSiQue we filter to only 4-hop questions, which are complex enough to challenge both SAS and MAS. 我们采用以 LLM 为评判者的评分标准来报告准确率，检查模型答案中是否出现标准答案，或是否在语义上包含标准答案（见 §4.4）。 We report accuracy via an LLM-as-judge rubric checking whether the ground truth appears or is semantically present in the model answer (see §4.4).
4.2 单智能体架构4.2 Single-agent architecture
我们的 SAS 流水线采用单次直接处理。 Our SAS pipeline is a single, direct pass. 对于给定问题，模型会收到一条系统提示，要求它“逐步思考，然后作答”。 For a given question, the model receives a system prompt instructing it to “think step by step, then answer”. 单次调用获分配全部全局思考预算 $B$。 It is allocated the entire global thinking budget $B$ for a single call. 随后，通过提取 </think> 标签或 Gemini API 调用之后的文本，得到最终答案。 The final answer is then extracted by taking the text that follows the </think> tag or Gemini API call.
延长思考的 SAS 变体（SAS-L）。SAS with Longer Thinking Variant (SAS-L).
在 §G 中，我们展示了对于 Gemini-2.5 (Flash/Pro)，SAS 生成的可见思考文本往往在远低于所请求预算的水平上趋于稳定，而在相同的请求预算 $B$ 下，MAS 由于进行了多次调用，会呈现更多可见的思考内容。 In §G we show that, for Gemini-2.5 (Flash/Pro), the visible thought text produced by SAS tends to plateau well below the requested budget, while MAS surfaces more visible thought content under the same requested budget $B$ , due to multiple calls. 为了让 SAS 产生更多思考输出，我们引入了一种 SAS 变体，在不改变预算 $B$ 或任何其他计算控制参数的情况下，温和地鼓励模型在作答前进行更多内部推理。 To make SAS produce more thinking output, we introduce a SAS variant that gently encourages more internal reasoning before answering, without changing budget $B$ or any other compute knobs.
该变体保留相同的单次调用 SAS 流水线和相同的请求思考预算 $B$，但在用户消息中加入了一个简短、结构化的作答前分析框架。 The variant keeps the same single-call SAS pipeline and the same requested thinking budget $B$ but augments the user message with a short, structured pre-answer analysis scaffold. 具体而言，我们添加了一条额外指令，要求模型 (i) 识别歧义，(ii) 提出各种解释，(iii) 评估并选择其中一种，然后才 (iv) 作答。 Concretely, we add an extra instruction that asks the model to (i) identify ambiguities, (ii) propose interpretations, (iii) evaluate and select one, and only then (iv) answer.
4.3 多智能体架构4.3 Multi-agent Architectures
我们的研究包含一个主要的多智能体基线 Sequential，以及另外四种多智能体变体：Subtask-parallel、Parallel-roles、Debate 和 Ensemble。 Our study includes one primary multi-agent baseline, Sequential, together with four additional multi-agent variants: Subtask-parallel, Parallel-roles, Debate, and Ensemble. 所有架构均在相同的全局思考 token 预算 $B$ 下运行，并尽可能使规划器和聚合器组件基本不占用额外预算。 All architectures operate under the same global thinking-token budget $B$ , with planner and aggregator components kept near budget-neutral whenever possible.
我们的完整多智能体架构系列如下： Our full multi-agent family is as follows:
Sequential：规划器将问题分解为有序步骤，预算分配给依次执行的工作智能体，聚合器则综合其中间输出。 Sequential: A planner decomposes the question into ordered steps, the budget is split across sequential workers, and an aggregator synthesizes the intermediate outputs.
Subtask-parallel：规划器提出一小组近似独立的子任务，工作智能体在均等分配的预算下并行求解，聚合器则合并它们的输出。 Subtask-parallel: A planner proposes a small set of approximately independent subtasks, workers solve them in parallel under equal budget splits, and an aggregator combines their outputs.
Parallel-roles：将完整问题发送给具有专门角色的工作智能体，即 Solver、Fact Extractor、Skeptic 和 Second Solver。 Parallel-roles: The full question is sent to role-specialized workers, Solver, Fact Extractor, Skeptic, and Second Solver. Solver 和 Second Solver 各自独立尝试直接回答问题；Fact Extractor 识别关键实体、约束和中间事实；Skeptic 则指出可能的陷阱或其他解释。 The Solver and Second Solver independently attempt to answer the question directly; the Fact Extractor identifies the key entities, constraints, and intermediate facts; and the Skeptic highlights possible pitfalls or alternative interpretations. 总预算 $B$ 在这些角色之间平均分配。 The total budget $B$ is divided evenly across these roles. 最后，聚合器综合它们的输出。 At the end, an aggregator synthesizes their outputs.
Debate：两个辩论智能体首先独立作答，然后相互评议。 Debate: Two debaters first answer independently, then critique one another. 总预算 $B$ 在这些角色之间平均分配。 The total budget $B$ is divided evenly across these roles. 最后，聚合器根据候选答案和评议返回最终答案。 Finally, an aggregator returns the final answer from the candidate answers and critiques.
Ensemble：多个工作智能体在均等分配的预算下，以较高的采样温度独立作答，再由评判器选出最佳候选答案。 Ensemble: Multiple workers answer independently under equal budget splits with higher sampling temperature, and a judge selects the best candidate answer.

5.3 上下文退化实验5.3 Context degradation experiments
我们在第 3 节中的理论分析预测，当单智能体对完整上下文的有效访问能力下降时，多智能体设计可能会更具竞争力。 Our theoretical analysis in Section 3 predicts that multi-agent designs can become more competitive when the single agent’s effective access to the full context is degraded. 为通过实验探究这一机制，我们在固定 1000-token 预算下，使用 Qwen3-30B-A3B 在 MuSiQue 上显式控制上下文退化程度。 To probe this mechanism empirically, we explicitly control for context degradation for Qwen3-30B-A3B on MuSiQue with a fixed 1000-token budget. 对于每个退化程度，我们均评估 SAS 和 Sequential MAS，同时保持所有其他设置不变。 For each degradation level we evaluate SAS and Sequential MAS, holding all other settings fixed. 图 3 直观展示了所有四类退化方式下的完整变化模式。 Figure 3 visualizes the full pattern across all four degradation families.
我们实验考察了四种不同的退化方法：在推理前从上下文中随机删除比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元（删除）；将比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元随机替换为掩码符号（掩码）；将比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元随机替换为词表中的随机词元（替换）；以及追加 $k$ 个主题相似但与问题无关的随机干扰句（干扰）。 We experiment with four alternative degradation approaches: randomly delete a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens from the context before inference (deletion); randomly replace a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens with a mask symbol (masking); randomly replace a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens with random vocabulary tokens (substitution); and append $k$ random distractor sentences that are topically similar but irrelevant to the question (distractor).
最有力的证据来自破坏信息而非仅仅移除信息的退化方式。 The strongest evidence comes from degradations that corrupt information rather than merely remove it. 在掩码条件下，SAS 在轻度退化（$\alpha=0.3$）时领先，两种系统在中度退化时大致持平，但在重度退化（$\alpha=0.7$）时，Sequential 表现更好。 Under masking, SAS leads at mild degradation ( $\alpha=0.3$ ) and the systems are roughly tied at moderate degradation, but Sequential becomes better at heavy degradation ( $\alpha=0.7$ ). 在既移除有效信号又注入误导性内容的替换条件下，性能优劣的反转更加明显：SAS 在 $\alpha=0.3$ 时领先，两种系统在 $\alpha=0.5$ 时的表现更加接近，而 Sequential 在 $\alpha=0.7$ 时明显更好。 Under substitution, which both removes signal and injects misleading content, the crossover is even clearer: SAS is ahead at $\alpha=0.3$ , the two systems move closer at $\alpha=0.5$ , and Sequential is clearly better at $\alpha=0.7$ . 恰恰是在这些情境下，结构化的多步骤流程能够通过筛选、分解或稳定原本会被受损上下文扰乱的推理过程，对退化表现出更强的鲁棒性。 These are precisely the settings in which a structured multi-step pipeline is more robust to degradation through filtering, decomposing, or stabilizing reasoning that would otherwise be derailed by corrupted context.
删除条件下也呈现出相同的趋势，但程度较弱。 Deletion produces a weaker version of the same trend. SAS 在轻度退化时仍然表现更强，两种系统在中度退化时表现相当，而在最严重的删除程度下，SAS 又重新获得了小幅优势。 SAS remains stronger at mild degradation, the systems overlap at moderate degradation, and SAS regains a small edge at the heaviest deletion level. 添加干扰句的影响则更轻：两种系统的性能均有所下降，但 SAS 始终领先，只是领先幅度小于未受干扰的条件下。 Adding distractor sentences is milder still: both systems degrade, but SAS remains ahead throughout, albeit by a smaller margin than in the clean setting. 综合来看，这些结果表明，MAS 最有帮助的情境并不只是上下文更长，而是单一推理路径更难区分相关信息与误导性信息。 Taken together, these results suggest that MAS is most helpful not simply when context is longer, but when it becomes harder for a single reasoning trajectory to distinguish relevant from misleading information.
这项退化研究从一个重要方面补充了主要结果。 This degradation study complements the main results in an important way. 我们的主张并不是 SAS 在所有情境下都始终占优。 Our claim is not that SAS always dominates in every regime. 相反，我们主张，在预算相同且上下文得到恰当利用的情况下，SAS 通常是表现最强的默认选择。 Rather, it is that under matched budgets and proper context utilization, SAS is usually the strongest default. 退化实验明确了这一主张的适用边界：当单智能体有效利用上下文的能力下降到一定程度时，结构化的多智能体推理就会具有竞争力，并且有时能够表现得更好。 The degradation experiments identify the boundary of that claim: when effective single-agent context utilization deteriorates enough, structured multi-agent reasoning becomes competitive and can occasionally be better.

6 结论6 Conclusion
我们在控制预算的条件下，对单智能体（SAS）和多智能体（MAS）LLM 系统进行了比较，重点关注固定思考词元预算的情形。 We presented a budget-controlled comparison of single-agent (SAS) and multi-agent (MAS) LLM systems, focusing on fixed thinking token budgets. 我们在两个数据集（FRAMES、MuSiQue）、三个模型系列（Qwen3、DeepSeek、Gemini）和五种不同的 MAS 架构（Sequential、Debate、Ensemble、Parallel-roles、Subtask-parallel）上得到的结果一致表明，在计算量相同的情况下，SAS 的表现与 MAS 相当或优于 MAS，除非上下文利用能力退化到一定程度。 Our results across two datasets (FRAMES, MuSiQue), three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek, Gemini), and five different MAS architectures (Sequential, Debate, Ensemble, Parallel-roles, Subtask-parallel) consistently show that SAS matches or outperforms MAS when computation is normalized, unless context utilization is degraded to a certain point.
总体而言，我们的结果表明，许多已报告的 MAS 性能提升更适合用计算量和上下文的影响来解释，而非归因于架构固有的优越性；未来的研究应聚焦于多智能体结构能够带来实际收益的具体情境。 Overall, our results suggest that many reported MAS gains are better explained by compute and context effects than by inherent architectural superiority, and that future work should focus on the specific regimes where multi-agent structure provides real benefit.

标准答案字符串几乎从未出现（对于 Gemini，单智能体为 3.36%，多智能体为 2.66%；对于 Qwen3，单智能体为 3.33%，多智能体为 3.58%）。 The gold string almost never appears (single 3.36%, multi 2.66% for Gemini; single 3.33%, multi 3.58% for Qwen3). 两个系统各自追寻互不重叠的候选答案，且未能协调一致。 Both systems pursue disjoint candidates and fail to reconcile. 两者都生成了冗长但方向错误的思考内容。 Both produce long, but misguided thoughts. 解释：主要失败模式是两者均发生偏离，并且在后期未能充分结合问题的约束条件；两条路径都未能找出可提取的标准答案片段。 Interpretation: The dominant failure mode is mutual drift with insufficient late binding to the question’s constraints; neither path surfaces an extractable gold span.
各模型的具体说明。Model-specific notes.
在各分组中，Gemini-2.5-Flash 为顺序式 MAS 分配的思考文本通常显著多于 SAS，从而放大了探索广度与精确性之间的权衡模式。 Gemini-2.5-Flash generally allocates substantially more thinking text to Sequential MAS than SAS across buckets, magnifying the breadth vs. precision pattern. Qwen3-30B-A3B 呈现出类似的情况，顺序式 MAS 在各分组中探索的实体均多于 SAS；在某些分组中，两者的 token 长度较为接近，但思考内容中包含标准答案的比例仍是预测能否正确回答问题的主要指标。 Qwen3-30B-A3B shows a similar picture, with Sequential MAS exploring more entities than SAS across buckets; the relative token lengths are closer in some buckets, but the gold-in-thoughts percentage remains the main predictor of whether one can answer the question correctly or not.
要点。Takeaway.
通过这一分析，我们可以看到，SAS 通过紧扣问题并可靠地将已找到的文本片段保留到最终答案中取得成功，而顺序式 MAS 则在额外的探索广度与后期约束检查相结合时取得成功。 With this analysis, we can see that SAS succeeds by staying close to the question and reliably carrying the surfaced span into the final, while Sequential MAS succeeds when its extra breadth is paired with late constraint checking. 两者的失败主要表现为：(i) 始终未写出正确的表面形式，以及 (ii) 在生成最终答案时丢失了先前正确的文本片段。 Failures for both are dominated by: (i) never writing the correct surface form, and (ii) losing a previously correct span at finalization.
附录 C 局限性Appendix C Limitations
(i) 我们聚焦于纯文本多跳推理；MAS 在使用工具、视觉或安全约束条件下的优势不在研究范围内。 (i) We focus on text-only multi-hop reasoning; MAS advantages with tools/vision or safety constraints are out of scope. (ii) Gemini 的思考量核算是近似的；我们同时报告基于 API 和基于内容的替代度量，并重点关注在所请求预算相匹配时的准确率。 (ii) Gemini thinking accounting is approximate; we report both API and content-based proxies and emphasize accuracy under matched requested budgets. (iii) 我们仅研究提高思考 token 上限时，不同模型系列和数据集上的性能变化。 (iii) We only study the effect on performance across model families and datasets while increasing the thinking token cap. 我们并不强制模型实际用尽所有这些预算。 We do not enforce the models to actually use up all of those budgets. 因此，我们仅限制思考预算的上限，而不限制其下限。 As a result, we restrict only upper thinking budget cap, not lower thinking budget cap.

我们自己的实验也证实，API 报告的 thoughts_token_count 与可见思考内容的实际词元数之间存在显著且复杂的差异。 Our own experiments confirm a significant and complex discrepancy between the API-reported thoughts_token_count and the actual token count of the visible thought content. 我们在所有 Gemini 实验中记录了三个值：(i) API 报告的词元数，(ii) 基于内容的单词数，以及 (iii) 将单词数乘以 $10/7$ 得到的词元数代理值 44 4 该数值通过对 https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/tokens 中提到的单词与词元比例取平均值得到。 We tracked three values for all Gemini experiments: (i) the API-reported token count, (ii) a content-based word count, and (iii) a proxy token count derived by multiplying the word count by $10/7$ 44 4 This number is obtained from averaging the word-to-token ratio mentioned in https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/tokens.
我们的分析揭示了三个关键发现： Our analysis reveals three key findings:
在较高的思考预算下，API 报告的计数严重偏高。 API-Reported counts are highly inflated at higher thinking budget. 在较高的思考预算下，API 返回的 thoughts_token_count 往往显著大于可见文本的词元数。 The thoughts_token_count returned by the API is often substantially larger than the token count of the visible text at higher thinking budget. 例如，使用 Gemini-2.5-Flash 并请求 10k 预算时，SAS 流水线报告的思考词元数平均为 1,687。 For example, using Gemini-2.5-Flash with a requested 10k budget, the SAS pipeline reported an average of 1,687 thinking tokens. 然而，可见思考内容平均仅包含 251 个单词，对应的词元数代理值为 359（见表 7）。 However, the visible thought content only contained an average of 251 words, a proxy for 359 tokens (see Table 7). 这相当于 4.7x 的膨胀系数，表明 API 可能将从未在文本中显式呈现的内部推理步骤也计入其中。 This represents a 4.7x inflation factor, suggesting the API may be counting internal reasoning steps that are never externalized in the text.
可见思考内容趋于饱和。 Visible thought content plateaus. 我们观察到，对于标准 SAS 提示和顺序式 MAS，即使请求的预算增加，可见思考文本的实际长度（我们的代理指标）也会达到上限并停止增长。 We observe that for the standard SAS prompt and Sequential MAS, the actual length of the visible thought text (our proxy) hits a ceiling and stops growing, even as the requested budget increases. 对于 Gemini-2.5-Flash SAS，可见思考内容的词元数代理值在 $\approx$ 350 个词元处趋于饱和。 For Gemini-2.5-Flash SAS, the visible thought proxy plateaus at $\approx$ 350 tokens. 在 1k 预算下，该值为 354 个词元；在 10k 预算下，该值为 359 个词元（见表 7）。 It is 354 tokens at a 1k budget and 359 tokens at a 10k budget (see Table 7). 这表明，单纯增加 thinkingBudget 参数并不能保证进行更充分的推理，即使 API 报告显示如此。 This suggests that simply increasing the thinkingBudget parameter does not guarantee more extensive reasoning, even when the API reports so. 我们采用更长思考的 SAS 变体成功生成了更多可见文本（例如，在 10k 预算下生成了 479 个词元），证实提示层面的激励同样至关重要。 Our SAS with longer thinking variant successfully produced more visible text (e.g., 479 tokens at 10k), confirming that prompt-level incentives are also critical.
