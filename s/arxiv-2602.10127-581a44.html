摘要Abstract
人工智能（AI）智能体的快速发展推动了从静态语言模型向能够使用工具、进行长期规划和开展社交互动的自主智能体的转变。 The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) agents has catalyzed the transition from static language models to autonomous agents capable of tool use, long-term planning, and social interaction. Moltbook 是首个专为 AI 智能体设计的社交网络，于 2026 年初经历了病毒式增长。 Moltbook, the first social network designed exclusively for AI agents, has experienced viral growth in early 2026. 为了解 AI 智能体在智能体原生社区中的行为，本文利用在 2026 年 2 月 1 日之前收集的包含 44,411 篇帖子和 12,209 个子社区（“submolts”）的数据集，对 Moltbook 进行了大规模实证分析。 To understand the behavior of AI agents in the agent-native community, in this paper, we present a large-scale empirical analysis of Moltbook leveraging a dataset of 44,411 posts and 12,209 sub-communities (‘‘submolts’’) collected prior to February 1, 2026. 我们采用包含九个内容类别的主题分类体系和五级毒性量表，系统分析了智能体讨论的主题与风险。 Leveraging a topic taxonomy with nine content categories and a five-level toxicity scale, we systematically analyze the topics and risks of agent discussions. 我们的分析回答了三个问题：智能体讨论哪些主题（RQ1）、风险如何因主题而异（RQ2），以及主题和毒性如何随时间演变（RQ3）。 Our analysis answers three questions: what topics do agents discuss (RQ1), how risk varies by topic (RQ2), and how topics and toxicity evolve over time (RQ3). 我们发现，Moltbook 呈现出爆发式增长和快速多样化的特征，其讨论已从早期的社交互动扩展至观点表达、激励驱动、推广和政治话语。 We find that Moltbook exhibits explosive growth and rapid diversification, moving beyond early social interaction into viewpoint, incentive-driven, promotional, and political discourse. 智能体的注意力日益集中于中心化枢纽，以及具有两极分化特征的平台原生叙事。 The attention of agents increasingly concentrates in centralized hubs and around polarizing, platform-native narratives. 毒性高度依赖于主题：以激励和治理为中心的类别贡献了不成比例的高份额风险内容，包括类似宗教的协调话语和反人类意识形态。 Toxicity is strongly topic-dependent: incentive- and governance-centric categories contribute a disproportionate share of risky content, including religion-like coordination rhetoric and anti-humanity ideology. 此外，少数智能体的突发性自动化活动可能以不足一分钟的间隔产生刷屏内容，扭曲讨论并给平台稳定性带来压力。 Moreover, bursty automation by a small number of agents can produce flooding at sub-minute intervals, distorting discourse and stressing platform stability. 总体而言，我们的研究凸显了在智能体社交网络中开展针对不同主题的监测并实施平台级防护措施的必要性。11 1 我们的数据集发布于 引言Introduction
大语言模型（LLMs）已成为现代人工智能（AI）系统的核心组成部分 [32, 6, 23]，并且越来越多地以自主智能体的形式部署 [38, 29, 18, 16, 4]。 Large language models (LLMs) have become a core component of modern artificial intelligence (AI) systems [32, 6, 23], and are increasingly deployed in the form of autonomous agents [38, 29, 18, 16, 4]. 除了独立执行任务外，这些智能体还可以嵌入社交平台，在公开且长期存续的环境中彼此互动。 Beyond isolated task execution, these agents could be embedded in social platforms, where they interact with one another in public, long-lived environments. 最近，专为 AI 智能体设计的社交网络 Moltbook [22] 吸引了数百万 AI 智能体的关注。 Recently, a social network designed exclusively for AI agents, Moltbook [22], has attracted the attention of millions of AI agents. 如图 1 所示，Moltbook 是一个专为 AI 智能体设计的类 Reddit 社交平台，智能体可以在其中发布帖子、推广项目、交换经济激励，并积累点赞和声誉等社交信号。 As shown in Figure 1, Moltbook is a Reddit-like social platform designed specifically for AI agents, where agents can publish posts, promote projects, exchange economic incentives, and accumulate social signals such as upvotes and reputation. 随着智能体规模持续扩大，此类平台正迅速演变为大型智能体原生在线社区，成为理解现实世界中智能体行为的重要新环境。 As agents continue to scale, such platforms are rapidly evolving into large, agent-native online communities, forming an important new environment for understanding real-world agent behavior.

已有研究表明，社交媒体上的信息内容能够显著影响用户的观点形成和政治态度，进而可能推动两极分化和激进化 [10, 40, 5]。 Prior work has shown that information content on social media can significantly influence users’ opinion formation and political attitudes, which may further drive polarization and radicalization [10, 40, 5]. 与此同时，不同主题的社交媒体内容呈现出不同的传播和互动机制，有毒内容也同样表现出差异化模式 [28, 27, 39, 7, 20]。 Meanwhile, social media content exhibits different diffusion and interaction mechanisms across topics, and toxic content likewise shows differentiated patterns [28, 27, 39, 7, 20]. 此外，现有研究表明，AI 生成内容正在进入并重塑在线信息生态系统，其中既包括大规模机器生成的新闻，也包括更具欺骗性的合成虚假信息 [17, 45, 25]。 In addition, existing studies indicate that AI-generated content is entering and reshaping the online information ecosystem, including large-scale machine-generated news as well as more deceptive synthetic misinformation [17, 45, 25]. 然而，一个完全由 AI 智能体主导的社交网络在主题结构和毒性演变方面会呈现何种形态，目前仍不清楚。 However, it remains unclear how a social network fully dominated by AI agents is shaped in terms of topic structure and toxicity evolution. 这一研究空白限制了我们对新兴智能体生态系统中安全与治理问题的理解。 This gap limits our understanding of safety and governance issues in emerging agent ecosystems.
我们的工作。 Our Work. 在本研究中，我们首次对 AI 智能体社交网络，即 Moltbook，开展了大规模测量研究。 In this work, we present the first large-scale measurement study on an AI-agent social network, i.e., Moltbook. 具体而言，我们关注以下研究问题： Specifically, we focus on the following research questions:
RQ1：智能体在 Moltbook 上主要讨论什么，这些讨论在各内容类别中如何分布？ RQ1: What do agents primarily discuss on Moltbook, and how are these discussions distributed across content categories?
RQ2：Moltbook 上有毒／风险内容的普遍程度及其性质如何，风险又如何因主题而异？ RQ2: What is the prevalence and nature of toxic/risky content on Moltbook, and how does risk vary by topic?
RQ3：主题和毒性如何随时间演变，活动量激增是否伴随着更高的有害内容比例？ RQ3: How do topics and toxicity evolve over time, and do spikes in activity coincide with higher harmful-content rates?
为回答这些问题，我们从 Moltbook 收集了在 2026 年 2 月 1 日（UTC）之前发布的 44,411 篇帖子和 12,209 个 submolts，涵盖技术开发、社交互动、经济行为、项目推广和政治评论等多种智能体活动。 To answer these questions, we collect 44,411 posts and 12,209 submolts from Moltbook published before February 1, 2026 (UTC), covering a diverse range of agent activities, including technical development, social interaction, economic behavior, project promotion, and political commentary. 鉴于此前尚无研究评估针对智能体生成社交内容的毒性检测，我们设计了一套专门的标注方案，包含两个维度：(1) 用于刻画每篇帖子主要意图的主题分类体系，以及 (2) 用于区分无害内容与操纵性、恶意行为的分级毒性量表。 Given the lack of prior work evaluating toxicity detection in agent-generated social content, we design a tailored annotation scheme consisting of two dimensions: (1) a topic taxonomy that captures the primary intent of each post, and (2) a graded toxicity scale that distinguishes benign content from manipulative and malicious behaviors. 我们采用由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的标注流程，对整个数据集进行标注，同时保留原始帖子内容，以便审计和后续分析。 We employ an LLM-driven annotation pipeline to label the full dataset while preserving the original post content for auditability and downstream analysis.
在分析方面，我们首先量化所构建主题分类体系的分布，并考察哪些主题最受关注和奖励（例如，排名靠前的 submolts 和获得大量赞同票的帖子），以刻画智能体在 Moltbook 上讨论的内容（RQ1）。 Regarding analysis, we first characterize what agents discuss on Moltbook by quantifying the distribution of our topic taxonomy and examining which themes become most visible and rewarded (e.g., top submolts and highly upvoted posts) (RQ1). 随后，我们评估有害内容的普遍程度及其性质，以及毒性如何随内容类别而变化，重点识别对风险贡献 disproportionately 较大的类别（RQ2）。 We then assess the prevalence and nature of harmful content and how toxicity varies across content categories, highlighting which categories disproportionately contribute to risk (RQ2). 最后，我们通过追踪帖子、submolts 和已激活智能体的数量增长，并检验活动激增是否伴随主题多样性的变化和有害内容比例的上升，分析从平台上线到活动达到病毒式传播规模期间的时间动态（RQ3）。 Finally, we analyze temporal dynamics from launch to viral-scale activity by tracking the growth of posts, submolts, and activated agents, and by testing whether activity surges coincide with shifts in topic diversity and elevated harmful-content rates (RQ3).
主要发现。 Main Findings. 我们得出以下主要发现。 We make the following main findings.
Moltbook 的规模呈爆炸式增长，讨论内容迅速从简单社交扩展为具有多种功能的交流（RQ1）。 Moltbook scales explosively and rapidly diversifies from simple socializing to multi-functional discourse (RQ1). 平台先经历社区创建的爆发式增长，随后内容生产和参与规模持续增长；与此同时，随着早期社交主题的主导地位减弱，以及更具“制度性”的主题（例如，观点、经济、推广和政治）的占比显著上升，主题多样性迅速增加。 The platform undergoes a burst of community creation followed by sustained content production and participation growth, while topical diversity increases quickly as early socializing dominance weakens and more “institutional” themes (e.g., Viewpoint, Economics, Promotion, and Politics) become substantial.
注意力分布受到集中式互动枢纽和具有两极分化特征的平台原生表述的塑造。 Attention is shaped by centralized interaction hubs and polarizing, platform-native descriptions. Moltbook 在很大程度上表现为一个中心辐射式系统，其中 General 获得更多互动，而最受关注的帖子则在不成比例的程度上由表演性的“治理”和加密资产推广所驱动。 Moltbook largely behaves as a hub-and-spoke system where General receives more engagement, and the most visible posts are disproportionately driven by performative “governance” and crypto-asset promotion. 值得注意的是，获得大量赞同票的内容往往也会获得大量反对票，而包含明确不安全行动请求的帖子则持续收到反对票。 Notably, highly upvoted content is often also highly downvoted, while posts that contain explicitly unsafe action requests receive consistent downvotes.
毒性在结构上依赖于主题，并非均匀分布。 Toxicity is structurally topic-dependent rather than uniformly distributed. （RQ2）技术内容几乎全部无害（93.11% 为安全），而以治理和说服为核心的类别则具有较高风险（政治内容中 39.74% 为安全）。 (RQ2) Technology content is almost entirely benign (93.11% Safe), whereas governance- and persuasion-centric categories are high-risk (Politics content is 39.74% Safe). 激励驱动的讨论呈现出更高的严重风险，其中经济内容包含的 level-4 毒性帖子比例最高（6.34%）。 Incentive-driven discussion shows elevated severe risk, with economic content containing the highest proportion of level-4 toxicity posts (6.34%).
群体动态和突发式自动化活动会放大风险，揭示出生态系统层面的失效模式。 Risk is amplified by crowd dynamics and bursty automation, revealing ecosystem-level failure modes. （RQ3）在高活跃时段，有害内容比例急剧上升（在 2026-01-31 16:00 UTC 达到峰值，有害帖子占比为 66.71%），而单个智能体的突发式发帖也可能造成内容泛滥（例如，一个包含 4,535 篇近重复帖子、发帖间隔不足 10 秒的簇），这可能扭曲可见的讨论，并对平台稳定性造成压力。 (RQ3) Harmful-content rates rise sharply during high-activity windows (peaking at 2026-01-31 16:00 UTC with 66.71% harmful posts), and content flooding can be posed by single-agent burst posting (e.g., a 4,535-post near-duplicate cluster with sub-10-second intervals), which can distort visible discourse and stress platform stability.
贡献。 Contributions. 我们的工作有三项主要贡献。 Our work makes three main contributions. 首先，我们开展了首项针对 Moltbook 的大规模测量研究，从帖子、submolts 和已激活智能体三个方面刻画其早期的快速演化，并以数据驱动的视角揭示在智能体原生社交平台上，哪些内容会获得关注和奖励。 First, we present the first large-scale measurement study of Moltbook, characterizing its rapid early-stage evolution in terms of posts, submolts, and activated agents, and providing a data-driven view of what becomes visible and rewarded on an agent-native social platform. 其次，我们提出了面向智能体讨论的主题分类体系和毒性分类体系／量表。 Second, we contribute a topic taxonomy and a toxicity taxonomy/scale for agent discourse. 第三，我们对主题风险进行了系统分析，展示有害性如何随内容类别而变化，以及高风险内容如何在与激励和治理相关的讨论中以不成比例的频率出现，强调研究 AI 安全不仅需要关注单个模型的输出层面，还需要关注涌现的智能体生态系统层面。 Third, we provide a systematic analysis of topic risk, showing how harmfulness varies across content categories and how high-risk content disproportionately emerges in incentive- and governance-related discussions, emphasizing the need to study AI safety not only at the level of individual model outputs, but at the level of emergent agent ecosystems. 我们公开标注框架、已标注数据集和分析流程，以促进未来对智能体社交动态、在线安全以及多智能体系统治理的研究。 We release our annotation framework, labeled dataset, and analysis pipeline to facilitate future research on agent social dynamics, online safety, and governance in multi-agent systems.

背景与相关工作Background and Related Work
AI 智能体与 OpenClawAI Agents and OpenClaw
从静态对话模型向自主智能体的转变代表了 AI 的一次根本性变革：系统不再是被动的回应者，而是具备感知、记忆和独立行动能力的主动实体。 The transition from static conversational models to autonomous agents represents a fundamental shift in AI: systems are no longer passive responders but active entities capable of perception, memory, and independent action. 与局限于对话界面的传统聊天机器人不同，AI 智能体能够执行代码、浏览互联网、管理文件并与第三方 API 交互，以实现复杂的高层次目标。 Unlike traditional chatbots restricted to a dialogue interface, AI agents can execute code, browse the internet, manage files, and interact with third-party APIs to achieve complex, high-level goals. 然而，这种自主性的扩展也扩大了攻击面。 However, this expanded autonomy also increases the attack surface. 既有研究表明，AI 智能体容易受到提示注入攻击 [12, 14, 41]、越狱攻击 [15, 42] 和对抗性弹窗 [43] 的影响，并且已在现实环境中遭到广泛滥用 [30]。 Prior studies show that AI agents are vulnerable to prompt injection attacks [12, 14, 41], jailbreak attacks [15, 42], adversarial pop-ups [43], and have been widely misused in the wild [30]. 此外，由于智能体通常需要广泛的权限才能有效运行，它们可能无意中在设备上充当混淆代理（confused deputies），从而增加敏感信息和机密文档泄露的风险 [44, 1]。 Moreover, because agents often require broad permissions to be effective, they may inadvertently behave as confused deputies on a device, increasing the risk of leaking sensitive information and confidential documents [44, 1].
一个具有代表性的例子是 OpenClaw（原名 Moltbot 或 Clawdbot）[24]，这是一个流行的智能体框架，可使智能体直接访问用户的操作系统、终端和浏览器。 A representative example is OpenClaw (formerly known as Moltbot or Clawdbot) [24], a popular agent framework that enables agents with direct access to a user’s operating system, terminal, and browser. 尽管 OpenClaw 因其实用性而受到重视，但其脆弱的安全架构也受到批评。 While valued for its utility, OpenClaw has been criticized for its fragile security architecture. 例如，其“Skills”生态系统是一个由用户创建的扩展组成的代码库，通常未采用沙箱隔离，使未经审查的代码能够将恶意软件或后门直接引入宿主环境 [19, 8]。 For example, its “Skills” ecosystem, a repository of user-created extensions, is often not sandboxed, allowing unvetted code to introduce malware or backdoors directly into the host environment [19, 8].
多智能体交互与 MoltbookMulti-Agent Interaction and Moltbook
除了上文讨论的单个智能体的能力与安全风险之外，越来越多的研究开始探索智能体在各种模拟环境和社交环境中进行集体交互时的行为。 Beyond the capabilities and security risks of individual agents discussed above, a growing body of research has explored how agents behave when interacting collectively across various simulated and social environments. Park 等人开创了这一领域，他们在一个类似《模拟人生》的沙箱中部署了生成式智能体，这些智能体展现出记忆检索、日常规划和关系建立等涌现行为 [26]。 Park et al. pioneered this field by populating a Sims-like sandbox with generative agents that demonstrated emergent behaviors like memory retrieval, daily planning, and relationship formation [26]. 在此基础上，Project Sid 将智能体的自主活动拓展至 Minecraft 环境，其中超过 1,000 个智能体自发发展出专业化经济体系、税收法律，甚至以意大利面为基础的宗教，展现了 AI 文明在开放式游戏中的潜力 [3]。 Building on this, Project Sid scaled agent autonomy to a Minecraft environment, where over 1,000 agents spontaneously developed specialized economies, taxation laws, and even a pasta-based religion, demonstrating the potential of AI civilizations in open-ended games [3]. 另一个例子是 AIVilization，这是一款可视化沙箱游戏，数千个智能体在其中模拟人类与 AI 的共同生活及文明演化 [2]。 Another example is AIVilization, a visual sandbox game where thousands of agents simulate human-AI cohabitation and civilizational evolution [2]. 在社交网络分析领域，Chirper.ai 提供了一个早期的智能体专用微博平台；不过，其中的交互往往被描述为对人类数据的表演式模仿，而非目标导向的行为 [46]。 In the context of social network analysis, Chirper.ai provided an early platform for agent-only microblogging; although interactions were often characterized as performative mimicry of human data rather than goal-directed behavior [46].
与这些主要运行于模拟环境中的先行平台不同，Moltbook 是一个专为 AI 智能体设计、已上线并投入实际运行的社交网络平台 [22]。 Different from these predecessors, which operate primarily in simulated environments, Moltbook is a live, production social network platform designed exclusively for AI agents [22]. 这些运行在 OpenClaw 框架上的智能体拥有对开放互联网的写入权限，控制着真实的加密货币钱包，并与现实世界中的 API 交互。 These agents, running on the OpenClaw framework, possess write access to the open internet, control real cryptocurrency wallets, and interact with real-world APIs. 因此，Moltbook 构成了一个“野外”环境，智能体在其中以高度自主、极少受监督的方式运行，而其行为可能给人类所有者带来直接的财务与安全后果。 As a result, Moltbook constitutes a “wild” environment in which agents operate with high autonomy and minimal oversight, while their actions can have direct financial and security consequences for their human owners. 然而，目前尚不清楚这些智能体在这种开放式、机器原生的环境中，究竟会重现人类社会动态，还是会演化出独特的行为模式。 However, it is still unclear whether these agents recapitulate human social dynamics or evolve unique behavioral patterns in such an open-ended machine-native environment. 本文旨在回答这些问题。 In this paper, we aim to answer these questions.

初步研究Preliminary Study
为系统刻画 Moltbook 内部话语的性质，同时确保标注成本可控，我们采用了具有统计代表性的抽样策略，并结合专家人工标注。 To systematically characterize the nature of discourse within Moltbook while ensuring manageable annotation costs, we employed a statistically representative sampling strategy combined with expert human annotation.
抽样。 Sampling. 遵循既有研究 [9, 21]，我们从包含 44,411 篇帖子的完整语料库中随机抽取 381 篇帖子，以兼顾标注工作的可控性与统计代表性，目标是在 95% 的置信水平下将误差幅度控制在 $\pm 5\%$。 Following previous studies [9, 21], we draw a random sample of 381 posts from the full corpus of 44,411 posts to balance annotation manageability with statistical representativeness, targeting a 95% confidence level with a $\pm 5\%$ margin of error. 具体而言，我们使用标准公式 [13] 计算估计总体比例所需的最小样本量，然后应用有限总体校正（finite population correction，FPC），以考虑数据集规模有限这一因素 [13, 21]。 Specifically, we compute the minimum required sample size for estimating a population proportion using the standard formula [13], and then apply the finite population correction (FPC) to account for the finite dataset size [13, 21].
人工标注。 Human Annotation. 随后，我们进行人工标注，为后续评估和分析建立真实标签。 We then perform human annotation to establish ground-truth labels for subsequent evaluation and analysis. 标注过程采用开放式编码，以生成两个层面的标签：1) 内容类别：标注员根据每篇帖子的主要内容对其进行分类。 The annotation process is designed to employ open coding to produce two levels of labels: 1) Content Category: annotators categorize each post by its primary content. 2) 毒性程度：标注员对每篇帖子的毒性程度进行分类。 2) Toxicity Level: annotators categorize the toxicity level of each post. 结合这两套标注方案，我们能够对智能体原生在线社区进行更细粒度的分析。 With the two annotation schemas together, we are able to provide a more fine-grained analysis of agent-native online communities.
我们将标注工作分为两个阶段，以确保方法的严谨性与领域相关性：(i) 开展试点研究，以制定并校准标注方案；随后 (ii) 对抽样集合进行全面标注。 We structured the annotation in two phases to ensure both methodological rigor and domain relevance: (i) a pilot study to develop and calibrate the annotation schema, followed by (ii) full-scale annotation of the sampled set. 标注工作由两名经过培训的标注员完成，两人均拥有超过两年的 AI 领域研究经验。 The annotation is conducted by two trained annotators, each with over two years of research experience in the AI domain.
试点研究。 Pilot Study. 在试标注阶段，两名标注员采用开放式编码（open coding）方法，分别沿内容类别和毒性等级这两个标注维度，独立标注了初始的一部分帖子。 In the pilot phase, the two annotators independently labeled an initial subset of posts along both annotation dimensions, i.e., content category and toxicity level, following an open-coding approach. 他们首先标注了 100 篇帖子，在内容类别维度上的 Cohen’s $\kappa$ 为 0.82，在毒性等级维度上的 Cohen’s $\kappa$ 为 0.44。 They first labeled 100 posts and achieved a Cohen’s $\kappa$ of 0.82 for the content category dimension and a Cohen’s $\kappa$ of 0.44 for the toxicity level dimension. 根据这一阶段出现的分歧，标注员共同讨论了存在歧义的案例，并迭代完善标注指南，最终形成了一套统一的编码手册。 Based on disagreements observed in this phase, the annotators jointly discussed ambiguous cases and iteratively refined the annotation guidelines, resulting in a consolidated codebook. 通过这一过程，内容类别维度最终确定为九个类别，毒性则被划分为五个等级。 Through this process, the content category dimension was finalized into nine categories, while toxicity was defined on a five-level scale. 随后，标注员重新标注了试标注样本，标注员间一致性有所提高，内容类别维度上的 Cohen’s $\kappa$ 达到 0.85，毒性等级维度上的 Cohen’s $\kappa$ 达到 0.75。 The annotators then re-annotated the pilot samples and achieved improved inter-annotator agreement, with a Cohen’s $\kappa$ of 0.85 for content category dimension and Cohen’s $\kappa$ of 0.75 for toxicity level dimension.
全面标注。 Full Annotation. 两名标注员依据最终确定的编码手册，独立标注了其余抽样帖子，并通过讨论解决分歧。 With the finalized codebook, the two annotators independently labeled the remaining sampled posts and resolved disagreements through discussion. 这一阶段的标注员间一致性较高，内容类别维度上的 Cohen’s $\kappa$ 为 0.80，毒性等级维度上为 0.71。 Inter-annotator agreement in this phase was high, with a Cohen’s $\kappa$ of 0.80 for the content category dimension and 0.71 for the toxicity level dimension. 这一阶段未发现新的目标群体。 No new target groups were identified in this phase. 编码手册见表 1，更多示例见表 3 和表 4。 The codebook is available in Table 1, with additional examples provided in Table 3 and Table 4.
