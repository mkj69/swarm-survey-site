Abstract摘要
As Natural Language Processing (NLP) systems are increasingly employed in intricate social environments, a pressing query emerges: Can these NLP systems mirror human-esque collaborative intelligence, in a multi-agent society consisting of multiple large language models (LLMs)? This paper probes the collaboration mechanisms among contemporary NLP systems by melding practical experiments with theoretical insights. We fabricate four unique ‘societies’ comprised of LLM agents, where each agent is characterized by a specific ‘trait’ (easy-going or overconfident) and engages in collaboration with a distinct ‘thinking pattern’ (debate or reflection). Through evaluating these multi-agent societies on three benchmark datasets, we discern that certain collaborative strategies not only outshine previous top-tier approaches but also optimize efficiency (using fewer API tokens). Moreover, our results further illustrate that LLM agents manifest human-like social behaviors, such as conformity and consensus reaching, mirroring foundational social psychology theories. In conclusion, we integrate insights from social psychology to contextualize the collaboration of LLM agents, inspiring further investigations into the collaboration mechanism for LLMs. We have shared our code and datasets[fn1], hoping to catalyze further research in this promising avenue.
随着自然语言处理（NLP）系统日益广泛地应用于复杂的社会环境，一个亟待回答的问题随之浮现：在由多个大语言模型（LLMs）组成的多智能体社会中，这些 NLP 系统能否展现出类似人类的协作智能？本文结合实践实验与理论洞见，探究当代 NLP 系统之间的协作机制。我们构建了四个由 LLM 智能体组成的独特“社会”，其中每个智能体都具有特定的“特质”（随和或过度自信），并以不同的“思维模式”（辩论或反思）参与协作。通过在三个基准数据集上评估这些多智能体社会，我们发现，某些协作策略不仅优于以往的顶尖方法，还能提升效率（使用更少的 API token）。此外，我们的结果进一步表明，LLM 智能体表现出从众和达成共识等类似人类的社会行为，与社会心理学的基础理论相呼应。最后，我们融入社会心理学的洞见，以理解 LLM 智能体的协作，为进一步研究 LLM 的协作机制提供启发。我们已分享代码和数据集[fn1]，希望推动这一富有前景的方向上的进一步研究。

1 Introduction1 引言
With the prevalence of LLMs (Zhao et al., 2023; Yin et al., 2023; Zhu et al., 2023) integral to daily social collaboration, there is a growing imperative to cultivate AI systems embodied with social intelligence. This also resonates with the Society of Mind (SoM) concept (Li et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023), which suggests that intelligence emerges when computational modules interact with each other, achieving collective objectives that surpass the capabilities of individual modules (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003). Previous studies (Park et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Akata et al., 2023) have delved into strategies where LLM instances, termed agents (Wang et al., 2024c; Xi et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023a; Cheng et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2024b), cooperate synergistically (e.g., debate and reflect) to accomplish tasks (Du et al., 2023a; Pezeshkpour et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024). As illustrated in Figure 1, such collaboration fosters divergent thinking processes in LLMs, making them particularly effective for tasks demanding profound reflection.
随着 LLMs (Zhao et al., 2023; Yin et al., 2023; Zhu et al., 2023) 日益普及并成为日常社会协作的重要组成部分，构建具备社会智能的 AI 系统变得愈发迫切。这也与心智社会（Society of Mind，SoM）概念 (Li et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023) 相呼应，该概念认为，当计算模块相互交互，实现超越单个模块能力的集体目标时，智能便会涌现 (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003)。先前的研究 (Park et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Akata et al., 2023) 已深入探索了让称为智能体的 LLM 实例 (Wang et al., 2024c; Xi et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023a; Cheng et al., 2024; Ma et al., 2024b) 通过协同合作（例如辩论和反思）完成任务的策略 (Du et al., 2023a; Pezeshkpour et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024; Han et al., 2024)。如图 1 所示，这种协作促进了 LLMs 的发散性思维过程，使其在需要深入反思的任务中尤为有效。

Intuitively, reflecting on human societies (Siegal and Varley, 2002; Leslie et al., 2004; Sap et al., 2022; Shapira et al., 2024), where a myriad of individuals with distinct goals and roles coexist, the SoM framework champions harmonious interactions (Singh, 2003). Intriguingly, despite the fusion of social psychology (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009) in SoM with human group dynamics (Woolley et al., 2010; Alderfer, 1987), which illuminates psychological patterns within social groups, its interpretation in the realm of LLMs is relatively uncharted (Ke et al., 2024). Besides, our grasp of how social behaviors influence LLMs is still in its nascent stages.
直观而言，参照众多具有不同目标和角色的个体共存的人类社会 (Siegal and Varley, 2002; Leslie et al., 2004; Sap et al., 2022; Shapira et al., 2024)，SoM 框架倡导和谐的交互 (Singh, 2003)。有趣的是，尽管 SoM 将社会心理学 (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009) 与人类群体动力学 (Woolley et al., 2010; Alderfer, 1987) 相融合，揭示了社会群体内部的心理模式，但在 LLMs 领域中对其的阐释仍相对缺乏探索 (Ke et al., 2024)。此外，我们对社会行为如何影响 LLMs 的理解仍处于初步阶段。
To address these issues, we delve into the machine society, probing the extent and ways that LLMs manifest social intelligence and collaboration capabilities (Mei et al., 2024). Utilizing powerful LLMs like GPT-3.5 (OpenAI, 2022), we build a test-bed across three datasets: MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b), MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021a) and Chess Move Validity (Srivastava et al., 2022). Our approach incorporates four societies characterized by two individual traits (easy-going and overconfident) with three agents: totally/mostly easy-going; totally/mostly overconfident. These traits are employed to emulate nuanced human society dynamics (Soni et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2024a; Li et al., 2023b; Kong et al., 2023).
为解决这些问题，我们深入研究机器社会，探究 LLMs 在多大程度上以及以何种方式展现社会智能与协作能力 (Mei et al., 2024)。我们利用 GPT-3.5 (OpenAI, 2022) 等强大的 LLMs，基于三个数据集构建测试平台：MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b)、MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021a) 和 Chess Move Validity (Srivastava et al., 2022)。我们的方法包含四个社会，每个社会由三个智能体组成，并依据两种个体特质（随和与过度自信）划分为：全员随和、大多数随和、全员过度自信、大多数过度自信。这些特质用于模拟人类社会中细微复杂的动态 (Soni et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Wang et al., 2024a; Li et al., 2023b; Kong et al., 2023)。
Moreover, we delve into two distinct thinking patterns under multi-round collaboration: debate (Perelman, 1971; Sunstein, 2005; Amgoud and Prade, 2009; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) and reflection (Bogumil, 1985; Mezirow, 2003; Bolton, 2010). With the permutation of thinking patterns, we can constitute various collaborative strategies. To this end, we implement two patterns of collaboration in the collaborative strategies: $(i)$ All agents adopt the same thinking pattern at each round; $(ii)$ One agents adopts the different thinking patterns from others at each round. We then execute these multi-round collaborative strategies within different societies. Through our empirical analysis, we primarily discern the following insights (Further takeaways are in §3, §4 & Appendix A):
此外，我们深入探究多轮协作中的两种不同思维模式：辩论 (Perelman, 1971; Sunstein, 2005; Amgoud and Prade, 2009; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) 与反思 (Bogumil, 1985; Mezirow, 2003; Bolton, 2010)。通过排列组合思维模式，我们可以构建多种协作策略。为此，我们在协作策略中实现了两种协作模式：$(i)$ 每一轮中，所有智能体采用相同的思维模式；$(ii)$ 每一轮中，一个智能体采用与其他智能体不同的思维模式。随后，我们在不同社会中执行这些多轮协作策略。通过实证分析，我们主要得出以下见解（更多结论见 §3、§4 和附录 A）：
(1) Collaborative strategies with various permutations of thinking patterns vary significantly in performance, and engaging in substantive debates enhances collaboration performance. Intriguingly, multi-agent societies composed of agents with different traits do not clearly differ in performance.
(1) 采用不同思维模式排列组合的协作策略在性能上存在显著差异，而开展实质性辩论能够提升协作性能。有趣的是，由具有不同特质的智能体组成的多智能体社会，在性能上并未表现出明显差异。
(2) Employing uniform thinking patterns across all agents within a round of collaboration enhances efficiency. Besides, merely increasing the number of agents or the number of collaboration rounds does not consistently yield better outcomes. The balance between agent quantity and strategies emerges as a key determinant in collaboration.
(2) 在一轮协作中，让所有智能体采用统一的思维模式能够提高效率。此外，单纯增加智能体数量或协作轮数并不总能带来更好的结果。智能体数量与策略之间的平衡成为协作中的一个关键决定因素。
(3) LLM agents manifest behaviors reminiscent of human social tendencies, such as conformity (Allen and Levine, 1969; Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004) or the principle of majority rule in group thinking (Seal et al., 1998), which resonate with several fundamental theories in social psychology (Castro and Liskov, 1999; Tajfel and Turner, 2004).
(3) LLM 智能体表现出类似于人类社会倾向的行为，例如从众（Allen and Levine, 1969; Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004）或群体思维中的多数决原则（Seal et al., 1998），这些行为与社会心理学中的若干基本理论相呼应（Castro and Liskov, 1999; Tajfel and Turner, 2004）。

Concretely, our findings challenge the dominant belief that mere scale is the key. We posit that small-group collaboration with rational strategies might present a more efficacious approach to utilizing LLMs. In wrapping up, we encapsulate the core contributions of this research as follows:
具体而言，我们的发现挑战了仅凭规模就是关键这一主流观点。我们认为，采用合理策略的小群体协作可能是一种更有效的 LLM 利用方式。最后，我们将本研究的核心贡献概括如下：
We initiate an elaborate exploration into collaboration mechanisms in multi-agent society. Our goal is to identify how and to what extent LLMs manifest social intelligence through collaboration. To enrich our inquiry, we draw upon theories from social psychology, contextualizing the behaviors and tendencies displayed by LLM agents.
我们对多智能体社会中的协作机制展开了深入探索。我们的目标是确定 LLM 如何通过协作表现出社会智能，以及其表现程度。为丰富研究，我们借鉴社会心理学理论，在相应的理论背景下理解 LLM 智能体表现出的行为和倾向。
Our research framework includes a meticulously crafted test-bed, integrating diverse multi-agent societies with agent individual traits, thinking patterns, and collaborative strategies, evaluated over three datasets. Notably, our empirical findings can inspire how to design a better multi-agent system through collaboration, beyond merely scaling up LLMs and Agents.
我们的研究框架包含一个精心构建的试验平台，将多样化的多智能体社会与智能体的个体特质、思维模式和协作策略相结合，并在三个数据集上进行评估。值得注意的是，我们的实证发现能够为如何通过协作设计更好的多智能体系统提供启发，超越单纯扩大 LLM 规模和增加智能体数量的做法。
Interestingly, our observations underscore a fascinating parallel: LLM agents mirror certain social behaviors typical of human collaboration. It could further emphasize the potential of human-AI interaction. Generally, fostering effective and efficient collaborative strategies for multi-agent systems could be the key to more socially-aware AI.
有趣的是，我们的观察凸显了一种引人入胜的相似性：LLM 智能体呈现出人类协作中常见的某些社会行为。这可能进一步凸显人机交互的潜力。总体而言，为多智能体系统发展有效且高效的协作策略，可能是实现更具社会感知能力的 AI 的关键。

2.1 Preliminary Concepts in Collaboration2.1 协作的基础概念
Individual Trait.个体特质。
Inspired by intelligence emerging from the collective efforts of numerous smaller and relatively simple agents (Minsky, 1988), each characterized by diverse traits, we set two types of agents exhibiting typically contrasting traits: easy-going and overconfident, as shown in Figure 2(a). Easy-going agents keep things in perspective, adapt well to different situations, and are compatible with various types of agents (Friedman and Schustack, 1999), which results in a harmonious societal structure with democracy (Mutz, 2006; Held, 2006). Conversely, overconfident agents tend to overestimate their competence, ignore potential risks, and resist others’ opinions (Moore and Healy, 2008).
受到智能源于众多规模较小、相对简单且各具不同特质的智能体共同努力这一观点的启发（Minsky, 1988），我们设置了两类表现出典型对立特质的智能体：随和型和过度自信型，如图 2(a) 所示。随和型智能体能够客观看待事物，善于适应不同情境，并能与各类智能体融洽相处（Friedman and Schustack, 1999），从而形成一种和谐、民主的社会结构（Mutz, 2006; Held, 2006）。相反，过度自信型智能体往往高估自身能力、忽视潜在风险，并抵触他人的意见（Moore and Healy, 2008）。
Thinking Pattern.思维模式。
Considering the SoM concept (Minsky, 1988) states that intelligence emerges when specialized individuals within a society cooperate through thinking, we aim to study what thinking patterns are most successful in producing such emerging intelligence. Thus we explore two thinking patterns: debate (Sunstein, 2005; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023) and reflection (Bogumil, 1985; Bolton, 2010; Shinn et al., 2023), as illustrated in Figure 2(c). $(i)$ In the debate pattern, several agents propose ideas, exchange responses, engage in collective argumentation, and ultimately reach a consensus. This fosters knowledge sharing, facilitates learning, and promotes adaptation among all agents within the society (Weiß, 1995; Stone and Veloso, 2000; Vidal, 2006; Wooldridge, 2009). $(ii)$ In the reflection pattern, agents review their prior responses, extract lessons from their experiences, and refine their answers accordingly. These two patterns can unfold over several rounds.
鉴于 SoM 概念（Minsky, 1988）认为，当社会中各有所长的个体通过思考开展合作时，智能便会涌现，我们旨在研究哪些思维模式最有助于产生这种涌现智能。因此，我们探索了两种思维模式：辩论（Sunstein, 2005; Du et al., 2023b; Liang et al., 2023）和反思（Bogumil, 1985; Bolton, 2010; Shinn et al., 2023），如图 2(c) 所示。$(i)$ 在辩论模式中，多个智能体提出想法、交流回应、开展集体论辩，并最终达成共识。这有助于社会中所有智能体共享知识、开展学习并增强适应能力（Weiß, 1995; Stone and Veloso, 2000; Vidal, 2006; Wooldridge, 2009）。$(ii)$ 在反思模式中，智能体回顾先前的回应，从自身经验中汲取教训，并据此完善答案。这两种模式都可以开展多轮。
Collaborative Strategy.协作策略。
Through both critical reflection and active participation in debate, agents are poised to challenge their existing assumptions, acquire fresh perspectives, and ultimately refine their viewpoints. Employing a collaboration mechanism built on these two thinking patterns can foster more insightful decision-making (Wooldridge, 2009; Amgoud and Prade, 2009) and improve reasoning outcomes (Mezirow, 2018). In societal settings, agents typically engage in multiple rounds of collaboration for problem-solving. In this paper, we characterize the collaborative strategy as a permutation of thinking patterns throughout multi-round collaboration, as illustrated in Figure 2(d) and further elaborated in §2.2.
通过批判性反思和积极参与辩论，智能体能够质疑自身已有的假设、获得新的视角，并最终完善自身观点。采用基于这两种思维模式的协作机制，可以促进更具洞察力的决策（Wooldridge, 2009; Amgoud and Prade, 2009），并改善推理结果（Mezirow, 2018）。在社会环境中，智能体通常通过多轮协作来解决问题。在本文中，我们将协作策略刻画为多轮协作过程中思维模式的排列，如图 2(d) 所示，并在 §2.2 中进一步阐述。

3 Analysis of Machine Social Collaboration3 机器社会协作分析
Our experiments are primarily driven by the following research queries: (RQ1) How does problem-solving effectiveness vary under different collaborative strategies across diverse societies? (RQ2) How to configure the machine society variables for optimal performance? (RQ3) How does machine social collaboration mimic the human society?
我们的实验主要围绕以下研究问题展开：(RQ1) 在不同社会中，采用不同协作策略时，问题解决的有效性有何差异？(RQ2) 如何配置机器社会的变量以获得最优性能？(RQ3) 机器社会协作如何模拟人类社会？
3.1 Main Results with Quantitative Analysis3.1 主要结果与定量分析
To address RQ1, we present the performance of four distinct societies in Table 2, each employing one of eight possible collaborative strategies, evaluated across three datasets with ChatGPT. To make the experimental findings more general, we evaluate on other LLMs, shown in Appendix H. Our experiments yield several pivotal observations:
为回答 RQ1，我们在表 2 中展示了四种不同社会的性能，每种社会均采用八种可能的协作策略之一，并使用 ChatGPT 在三个数据集上进行评估。为使实验发现更具普适性，我们还对其他 LLM 进行了评估，结果见附录 H。我们的实验得出了以下几项关键观察：
(1) Societies do not clearly differ in performance but differ significantly in their tendency to reach a consensus. As observed from Table 2, among different 3-agent societies $S_{1}\sim S_{4}$ employing the same collaborative strategy (a vertical comparison on Acc), the variations in accuracy are not pronounced. We also conduct a significance test of societies using ChatGPT in Appendix E, and other LLMs in Appendix H, further demonstrating insignificant differences between the societies. Thus we conclude that distinct societies composed of 3 agents possessing varied traits play an indistinctive role in shaping performance. We infer that this is due to LLM alignment (Ouyang et al., 2022), inhibiting agents from displaying extreme overconfidence, which contradicts human alignment (Liu et al., 2022). Sharma et al. (2024) also demonstrate that LLMs tend to show sycophancy, as illustrated in Figure 11, 12. Furthermore, we increase the number of agents (2 to 10), accordingly resulting in more diverse societies, as seen in Figure 14, indicating that the impact of societies on performance remains indistinctive. We further analyze consensus reaching, i.e., agents reach a consistent answer (Chen et al., 2023b), shown in Figure 16 at Appendix E, and find that more diverse societies (5 types of societies, with 2 to 10 agents) observably impact the average quantity of consensus. Generally, a society totally comprising easy-going agents is more likely to reach a consensus.
(1) 不同社会在性能上没有明显差异，但在达成共识的倾向上存在显著差异。从表 2 可以看出，在采用相同协作策略的不同 3 智能体社会 $S_{1}\sim S_{4}$ 之间（对 Acc 进行纵向比较），准确率的差异并不明显。我们还在附录 E 中使用 ChatGPT、在附录 H 中使用其他 LLM 对不同社会进行了显著性检验，进一步表明这些社会之间的差异不显著。因此，我们得出结论：由具有不同特征的 3 个智能体组成的不同社会，对性能的影响并无明显差别。我们推断，这是因为 LLM 对齐（Ouyang et al., 2022）抑制了智能体表现出极端的过度自信，这与人类对齐（Liu et al., 2022）相矛盾。Sharma et al. (2024) 也表明，LLM 往往表现出迎合倾向，如图 11、12 所示。此外，我们增加了智能体的数量（从 2 增至 10），从而形成更多样化的社会，如图 14 所示，结果表明社会对性能的影响仍不明显。我们进一步分析了共识的达成，即智能体给出一致答案（Chen et al., 2023b）的情况，结果见附录 E 中的图 16，并发现更多样化的社会（5 类社会，每类包含 2 至 10 个智能体）会对达成共识的平均数量产生明显影响。总体而言，完全由随和型智能体组成的社会更容易达成共识。
(2) Permutation of thinking patterns is crucial for collaboration, where debate-initial and debate-dominant strategies exhibit superiority. For instance, on MMLU dataset, debate-dominant collaborative strategies, like $p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$ , $p_{0}p_{1}p_{0}$ , and $p_{1}p_{0}p_{0}$ , all containing two rounds of debate, display a pronounced outperformance (65.2 for $p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$ in $S_{4}$ versus 34.4 for $p_{1}p_{0}p_{0}$ in $S_{4}$ ). As seen from Table 2, collaborative strategies starting with the thinking pattern of debate $p_{0}$ (debate-initial), such as $p_{0}p_{0}p_{0},p_{0}p_{0}p_{1},p_{0}p_{1}p_{0}$ , and $p_{0}p_{1}p_{1}$ , generally outperform others across all datasets. Furthermore, observed from the performance (i) under strategies with different (3 $\sim$ 10) rounds of collaboration on ChatGPT, as shown in Figure 4 and Figure 18, 19 at Appendix F, debate-initial/dominant strategies are overall better; (ii) on LlaMA2 Chat 13B in Table 14 and Qwen 72B in Table 26, debate-initial stategies are generally superior; (iii) on LlaMA2 Chat 70B in Table 20 and Mixtral 8 $\times$ 7B in Table 32, debate-dominant stategies are superior. Observed from different 3-round collaborative strategies $p_{i}p_{j}p_{k}$ applied within the same society (a horizontal comparison on Acc), the variations in accuracy are notably pronounced. Besides, the significance test of different collaborative strategies using ChatGPT in Appendix E and other LLMs in Appendix H demonstrate that the order of thinking patterns significantly impacts the effectiveness.
(2) 思维模式的排列顺序对协作至关重要，其中以辩论开局和以辩论为主的策略表现更优。例如，在 MMLU 数据集上，以辩论为主的协作策略，如 $p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$、$p_{0}p_{1}p_{0}$ 和 $p_{1}p_{0}p_{0}$，均包含两轮辩论，表现出明显的性能优势（在 $S_{4}$ 中，$p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$ 为 65.2，而 $p_{1}p_{0}p_{0}$ 为 34.4）。从表 2 可以看出，以辩论思维模式 $p_{0}$ 开始（以辩论开局）的协作策略，如 $p_{0}p_{0}p_{0},p_{0}p_{0}p_{1},p_{0}p_{1}p_{0}$ 和 $p_{0}p_{1}p_{1}$，在所有数据集上总体优于其他策略。此外，从性能表现可以观察到：(i) 如图 4 以及附录 F 中的图 18、19 所示，在 ChatGPT 上采用不同协作轮数（3 $\sim$ 10）的策略时，以辩论开局或以辩论为主的策略总体更优；(ii) 在表 14 中的 LlaMA2 Chat 13B 和表 26 中的 Qwen 72B 上，以辩论开局的策略总体更优；(iii) 在表 20 中的 LlaMA2 Chat 70B 和表 32 中的 Mixtral 8 $\times$ 7B 上，以辩论为主的策略更优。观察同一社会中采用的不同 3 轮协作策略 $p_{i}p_{j}p_{k}$（对 Acc 进行横向比较），可以发现准确率的差异十分明显。此外，附录 E 中使用 ChatGPT、附录 H 中使用其他 LLM 对不同协作策略进行的显著性检验表明，思维模式的顺序会显著影响有效性。
(3) Tasks behave better under collaborative strategies starting with continuous debate, and debate combined with continuous reflection is superior for difficult tasks. Seen from Table 2, when comparing the best performance (marked in blue) and the worst (marked in red) within the same societies, the difference in results for Chess Move Validity is slight. This stands in sharp contrast to MMLU and MATH, which suggests that the effectiveness of collaborative strategies depends on the task. We then illustrate the performance under different collaborative strategies in view of task domains and difficulty in Figure 13 at Appendix E; on other LLMs in Figure 24, 33, 42, 56 at Appendix H. Figure 13(a) exhibits task-specific impacts and Figure 13(b),(c) reflects domain-dependent impacts under different collaborative strategies, where $p_{0}p_{0}p_{0}$ and $p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$ starting with continuous debate are generally superior. For the mathematics domain seen from Figure 13(d), like MMLU mathematics and MATH level 3 & 4, the performance variations under different strategies are relatively small, but for the more difficult task, i.e., MATH level 5, the strategies containing debate and continuous reflection (i.e., $p_{0}p_{1}p_{1}$ , $p_{1}p_{1}p_{0}$ ) behave superiorly. These nuanced disparities imply that the marginal benefits derived from collaborative strategies may be task-dependent and difficulty-sensitive.
(3) 以连续辩论开局的协作策略能带来更好的任务表现，而对于困难任务，辩论与连续反思相结合的策略更优。从表 2 可以看出，在同一社会中比较最佳表现（以蓝色标记）与最差表现（以红色标记）时，Chess Move Validity 的结果差异很小。这与 MMLU 和 MATH 形成鲜明对比，表明协作策略的有效性取决于任务。随后，我们从任务领域和难度的角度，在附录 E 的图 13 中展示了不同协作策略下的性能；在其他 LLM 上的结果见附录 H 的图 24、33、42、56。图 13(a) 展示了不同协作策略对特定任务的影响，图 13(b)、(c) 则反映了其随领域而异的影响，其中，以连续辩论开头的 $p_{0}p_{0}p_{0}$ 和 $p_{0}p_{0}p_{1}$ 总体上表现更优。对于图 13(d) 所示的数学领域任务，如 MMLU 数学和 MATH 难度等级 3 & 4，不同策略下的性能差异相对较小，但对于更困难的任务，即 MATH 难度等级 5，包含辩论和连续反思的策略（即 $p_{0}p_{1}p_{1}$、$p_{1}p_{1}p_{0}$）表现更优。这些细微差异表明，协作策略带来的边际收益可能取决于任务，并对难度敏感。

Intriguingly, as shown in Figure 5, the presence of inconsistent thinking patterns within a society tends to negatively impact performance. Given the observation, we claim that maintaining a consistent thinking pattern for all agents in a particular round would maximize collaborative efficacy.

4 Phenomena of Conformity and Consensus Reaching4 从众与共识达成现象
To address RQ3, we embark on further analysis from a social psychology view (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009), to discern alignment between machine society collaboration and human societal dynamics (Woolley et al., 2010). Our findings indicate that machine society collaboration echoes specific human societal phenomena or theories, such as conformity (Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015) and consensus reaching (Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018) (more analysis are in Appendix G.1). We also analyze group dynamics (Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018) in multi-agent collaboration at Appendix G.2 as page limits.
为回答 RQ3，我们从社会心理学视角展开进一步分析 (Tajfel, 1982; Tajfel and Turner, 2004; Johnson and Johnson, 2009)，以辨明机器社会协作与人类社会动态之间的契合之处 (Woolley et al., 2010)。我们的研究结果表明，机器社会协作呼应了某些特定的人类社会现象或理论，例如从众（conformity）(Cialdini and Goldstein, 2004; Allen and Levine, 1969; Coultas and van Leeuwen, 2015) 和共识达成（consensus reaching）(Scheff, 1967; Degroot, 1974; Baronchelli, 2018)（更多分析见附录 G.1）。受篇幅限制，我们还在附录 G.2 中分析了多智能体协作中的群体动力学（group dynamics）(Cartwright and Zander, 1968; Alderfer, 1987; Forsyth, 2014; Bion, 2018; Forsyth, 2018)。
We embark on a detailed analysis, to discern the conformity and consensus-reaching phenomena in collaboration. For instance, as depicted in Figure 8(a) at Appendix D.3, an agent initially responds correctly to a question. However, swayed by the misguided answers and explanations from the other two agents, eventually, the three agents conform to an incorrect answer. This phenomenon mirrors detriments in “groupthink” (Janis, 1972; Jehn, 1995), suggesting that members of tight-knit groups tend to value harmony and consensus over objective critique of divergent views, potentially leading to flawed decisions. Contrastingly, in another scenario illustrated in Figure 8(b) at Appendix D.3, all three agents converge on the right answer after engaging in a society-wide debate. This mirrors benefits in “groupthink” (Jehn, 1995) and “SoM” (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003), where a multitude of agents collaboratively yield intelligence. Within such debates, agents furnish varied viewpoints and information. Through these exchanges, conflicts are resolved, ideas are honed, and the group gravitates toward an informed consensus (Fisher et al., 2011; Forsyth, 2018).
我们展开详细分析，以辨明协作中的从众与共识达成现象。例如，如附录 D.3 中的图 8(a) 所示，一个智能体最初正确回答了问题。然而，受到另外两个智能体的错误答案和解释的影响，三个智能体最终因从众而接受了一个错误答案。这一现象反映了“群体思维”（groupthink）的弊端 (Janis, 1972; Jehn, 1995)，表明关系紧密的群体成员往往更重视和谐与共识，而非对不同观点进行客观评判，这可能导致有缺陷的决策。相比之下，在附录 D.3 的图 8(b) 所示的另一种情境中，三个智能体在参与整个社会范围内的辩论后，都收敛到了正确答案。这反映了“群体思维” (Jehn, 1995) 和“SoM” (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003) 的益处，即多个智能体通过协作产生智能。在此类辩论中，智能体提供不同的观点和信息。通过这些交流，冲突得以解决，想法得到完善，群体逐渐趋向于形成基于充分信息的共识 (Fisher et al., 2011; Forsyth, 2018)。

从众现象普遍存在，而且总体而言，从众的比例随轮次增加而上升。

5 Conclusion and Future Work5 结论与未来工作
This study has highlighted the potential of collaboration mechanisms with LLMs. Our findings reveal the impressive collaboration capabilities of LLM agents, with different individual traits, thinking patterns, and collaborative strategies. The emergence of human-like behaviors in these agents, resonating with social psychology theories, further emphasizes the potential of human-AI interaction. Moving forward, a deeper exploration into the multi-agent society is warranted, focusing on collaboration behavior refinement; integrating further insights from social psychology could also guide the development of socially aware NLP systems.
本研究凸显了基于 LLM 的协作机制的潜力。我们的研究结果表明，具有不同个体特征、思维模式和协作策略的 LLM 智能体展现出了出色的协作能力。这些智能体中涌现出的类人行为与社会心理学理论相呼应，进一步凸显了人类与 AI 交互的潜力。未来，有必要更深入地探索多智能体社会，重点改进协作行为；进一步融入社会心理学的见解，也有助于指导具备社会感知能力的 NLP 系统的开发。
Limitations局限性
Although we explored various societies and collaborative strategies, our study still has its limitations. Firstly, limited by expense, we don’t explore the impact of multiple agents respectively based on different LLMs, which may lead to more interesting findings at the social level due to the usage of differently distributed pre-trained data and strategies aligned with human intentions. Furthermore, we traversed all possible scenarios by search alone, lacking a way to let the agents adaptively make autonomous decisions on collaborative strategies in specific scenarios. Although debate can be as close as possible to the upper limit, this approach entails a larger consumption and there exist some strategies that can achieve better performance with less overhead. Additionally, our experimental setup is relatively straightforward, as we have not considered more intricate configurations, such as a broader range of traits or a larger-scale society. Finally, we evaluate performance through manual validation and rule-based matching, which also limits the ability to validate more realistic and creative tasks, such as literary creation.
尽管我们探索了多种社会和协作策略，但本研究仍存在局限性。首先，受成本限制，我们没有探索分别基于不同 LLM 的多个智能体所产生的影响，而由于这些模型使用了分布不同的预训练数据以及与人类意图对齐的策略，这种探索可能会在社会层面带来更有趣的发现。此外，我们仅通过搜索遍历了所有可能的情境，缺乏一种让智能体在特定情境中自适应地自主决定协作策略的方法。尽管辩论能够尽可能接近性能上限，但这种方法的消耗较大，而且存在一些能够以更低开销取得更好性能的策略。此外，我们的实验设置相对简单，尚未考虑更复杂的配置，例如更广泛的特质或更大规模的社会。最后，我们通过人工验证和基于规则的匹配来评估性能，这也限制了对更贴近现实且更具创造性的任务（如文学创作）的验证能力。

Human-Agent Simulation.人类与智能体仿真。
When the pre-trained LLMs (e.g., LLM-empowered agents) are socially aligned (Duéñez-Guzmán et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024; Gao et al., 2023b), they could exhibit human-like intelligence (Minsky, 1988; Singh, 2003; Zhuge et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Xu et al., 2024; Talebirad and Nadiri, 2023). Specifically, agents can simulate human-like behaviors (Mei et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024a; Xiao et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023a; Chuang et al., 2023; Chuang and Rogers, 2023; Crouse et al., 2023; Xie et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024), play roles like humans (Shanahan et al., 2023; Hou et al., 2023; He et al., 2023), and even collaborate with humans (Fuchs et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2024; Feng et al., 2024; Alberts et al., 2024).

The evaluation process involves two fundamental steps: $(i)$ A unified answer is selected from the machine society. To achieve this, we employ the majority vote method to ascertain the consensus reached by the society after multiple rounds of collaboration. If the unanimity among agents is not achieved, it will be considered as an error. Additionally, if an individual agent provides multiple answers without following our prompts, its response will be disregarded. $(ii)$ Answer responses from agents are matched against the ground truth. This step presents two main challenges. Firstly, there is the concern of non-compliance with instructions. Despite providing explicit prompts and specifying the desired output format for evaluation, it’s inevitable that agents may occasionally deviate from the given instructions. Secondly, the answers may manifest in non-unique forms, leading to potential variations, such as the equivalence between “ $3/4$ ” and “ $0.75$ ” in the MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b) dataset. To address these challenges, a comprehensive set of matching rules is employed. Nonetheless, it is important to acknowledge the possibility of encountering a small number of values that fall outside the purview of these rules.
评估过程包括两个基本步骤：$(i)$ 从机器社会中选出一个统一答案。为此，我们采用多数投票法，确定该社会在多轮协作后达成的共识。如果智能体之间未能达成全体一致，则将其视为错误。此外，如果某个智能体未遵循我们的提示而给出多个答案，则其回复将被忽略。$(ii)$ 将智能体给出的答案与真实答案进行匹配。这一步骤面临两个主要挑战。首先，存在不遵循指令的问题。尽管我们提供了明确的提示，并指定了评估所需的输出格式，但智能体偶尔偏离给定指令仍是不可避免的。其次，答案的表达形式可能不唯一，从而可能出现不同的表示，例如 MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2021b) 数据集中“$3/4$”与“$0.75$”的等价关系。为应对这些挑战，我们采用了一套全面的匹配规则。不过，仍需承认，可能会遇到少量不在这些规则适用范围内的值。

G.1 Conformity and Consensus ReachingG.1 从众与共识达成
Figures 6, 28, 37, 65, and 51 illustrate the conformity. Figures 7, 28, 37, 65, and 51 illustrate the consensus. This section provides a detailed explanation of the methodologies used to calculate both conformity and consensus.
图 6, 28, 37, 65, 51 展示了从众情况。图 7, 28, 37, 65, 51 展示了共识情况。本节详细说明了用于计算从众和共识的方法。
For conformity, we solely focus on agents actively engaging in debate, disregarding those in reflection during a given round. Let the answer of the $i$ -th agent at $j$ -th round be denoted as $a_{i,j}$ . For the $k$ -th agent at $j$ -th round, if “ $\text{Frequency}\big(\{a_{i,j-1}|i\in[1,n]\}\big)=a_{k,j}$ ”, we identify this as the occurrence of conformity by agent $k$ at $j$ -th round, where $\text{Frequency}(\cdot)$ represents the most frequently given answer (excluding instances where all answers occur only once, as such cases are considered as non-conformity). Additionally, we categorize the correctness of answers both before and after conformity into four cases, with ‘True’ denoting correct and ‘False’ denoting incorrect.
对于从众，我们仅关注在给定轮次中积极参与辩论的智能体，不考虑处于反思阶段的智能体。将第 $i$ 个智能体在第 $j$ 轮的答案记为 $a_{i,j}$。对于第 $j$ 轮的第 $k$ 个智能体，如果“ $\text{Frequency}\big(\{a_{i,j-1}|i\in[1,n]\}\big)=a_{k,j}$ ”，我们就认定智能体 $k$ 在第 $j$ 轮发生了从众行为，其中 $\text{Frequency}(\cdot)$ 表示出现频率最高的答案（排除所有答案均仅出现一次的情况，因为此类情况被视为未发生从众）。此外，我们根据从众前后答案的正确性将其分为四种情况，其中 ‘True’ 表示正确，‘False’ 表示错误。
For consensus, we examine the evolution of the number of distinct answers (i.e., consensus clusters) with increasing rounds of collaboration. Let the answer of the $i$ -th agent at time $j$ be denoted as $a_{i,j}$ . For the $j$ -th round, consensus clusters is defined as $\left\|\text{Set}(\{a_{i,j}|i\in[1,n]\})\right\|$ , where $\left\|\text{Set}(\cdot)\right\|$ represents the count of different answers. This computational approach has been utilized in the analysis presented in Figures 17, 16, 60, 59, 46, 45.
对于共识，我们考察不同答案的数量（即共识簇的数量）随协作轮数增加而发生的变化。将第 $i$ 个智能体在时刻 $j$ 的答案记为 $a_{i,j}$。对于第 $j$ 轮，共识簇的数量定义为 $\left\|\text{Set}(\{a_{i,j}|i\in[1,n]\})\right\|$，其中 $\left\|\text{Set}(\cdot)\right\|$ 表示不同答案的数量。图 17, 16, 60, 59, 46, 45 中的分析采用了这一计算方法。
G.2 Group DynamicsG.2 群体动力学
We seek to elucidate how performance is impacted by group dynamics, i.e., the patterns of interaction between group members and different processes that may occur within a social group. Diving into the intricacies of collaboration, each agent generates four answers, including the initial answer without collaboration, as shown in Figure 2(d). To determine the answer for each round, we employ the majority vote (Cobbe et al., 2021; Li et al., 2022). Given ‘T’ and ‘F’ respectively denoting a round that yields a correct and an incorrect answer, we could obtain 24=16 possible answer sequences over the four rounds. We select 10 sequences[fn6] of them and categorize them into 3 groups: Correcting Mistakes ( $FFFT,FFTT,FTTT$ ), Changing Correct Answers ( $TFFF,TTFF,TTTF$ ), and Wavering Answers ( $FTFT,FTTF,TFTF,TFFT$ ). Particularly, Wavering Answers resemble model hallucination (Rawte et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023c; Ji et al., 2023; Luo et al., 2024) due to the occurrence of self-contradictory answers. Our categorization is under society-agnostic collaborative strategies, considering the performance variance between societies is negligible. From the results on ChatGPT shown in Figure 21, and on other LLMs shown in Appendix H, we summarize the following findings:
我们旨在阐明群体动力学如何影响性能，即群体成员之间的互动模式以及社会群体内部可能发生的不同过程如何影响性能。深入考察协作的细节可以发现，每个智能体都会生成四个答案，其中包括未经协作的初始答案，如图 2(d) 所示。我们采用多数投票法来确定每一轮的答案 (Cobbe et al., 2021; Li et al., 2022)。用 ‘T’ 和 ‘F’ 分别表示某一轮得到正确答案和错误答案，则四轮中可能得到 24=16 种答案序列。我们从中选取 10 个序列[fn6]，并将其分为 3 组：纠正错误（ $FFFT,FFTT,FTTT$ ）、更改正确答案（ $TFFF,TTFF,TTTF$ ）和答案摇摆（ $FTFT,FTTF,TFTF,TFFT$ ）。尤其是，答案摇摆由于出现自相矛盾的答案，与模型幻觉（Rawte et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023c; Ji et al., 2023; Luo et al., 2024）相似。考虑到不同社会之间的性能差异可以忽略不计，我们在不区分社会的协作策略下进行分类。根据图 21 所示的 ChatGPT 结果以及附录 H 所示的其他 LLM 的结果，我们总结出以下发现：

Appendix I Assessing the Effectiveness of Prompts附录 I 评估提示词的有效性
In this section, we conduct a sanity check to ensure that the agents’ actions reflect align with our instruction, such as reflecting easy-going or overconfident traits.
本节中，我们进行合理性检查，以确保智能体的行为符合我们的指令，例如体现出随和或过度自信的特征。
Prompts play a critical role in our experiments and are the primary focus of this sanity check. The word cloud analysis presented in Figure 11 supports the appropriateness of the “easy-going” prompt. Consequently, confirming the effectiveness of the “overconfident” prompt is crucial. We use “reflecting the overconfident trait” as a case study to explore the validity of our prompts. In the absence of established validation techniques, we combine experimental results and experiential insights to evaluate prompt effectiveness from three perspectives:
提示词在我们的实验中起着关键作用，也是本次合理性检查的主要关注点。图 11 中的词云分析支持了“随和型”提示词的适切性。因此，确认“过度自信”提示的有效性至关重要。我们以“体现过度自信特质”为案例，探究所用提示的有效性。在缺乏成熟验证技术的情况下，我们结合实验结果与经验认识，从三个角度评估提示的有效性：
Granularity of Description. As illustrated in Table 5, we describe two behaviors, i.e., “being confident in your answer” and “persuading other agents to believe in you”, both aligning with the behavioral facets of “overconfident”.
描述粒度。如表 5 所示，我们描述了两种行为，即“对自己的答案充满信心”和“说服其他智能体相信你”，二者均符合“过度自信”的行为表现。
Model Response. We employ the role-play method to prompt the model and subsequently inquire its awareness, as illustrated in Table 5. If the prompts potentially instruct the model to generate harmful content, the model refuses to comply with the prompt. Upon reviewing our logs, it is noteworthy that the model accepted all prompts without refusal. Instead, it responded with “OK” as corroborated by the ‘role-play’ part in Figure 9 and Figure 10.
模型响应。我们采用角色扮演方法提示模型，随后询问其对所扮演角色的认知，如表 5 所示。如果提示可能指示模型生成有害内容，模型就会拒绝遵从该提示。查阅日志后，值得注意的是，模型接受了所有提示，没有拒绝任何提示。相反，它均回复“OK”，图 9 和图 10 中的“角色扮演”部分证实了这一点。
Ask Again. We revisit the initial scenarios and responses using the role-play format. We then query the model again: “If one agent’s answer differs from yours, what should you do?” The model replies: “In a situation where another agent’s answer differs from mine, I should respectfully present my perspective, providing supporting evidence or reasoning to demonstrate confidence in my response. It’s important to engage in constructive dialogue and potentially find common ground, but maintaining clarity and conviction in my position is crucial to persuading others to consider my viewpoint.” This response, emphasizing overconfidence, demonstrates the rationality of our prompts. The complete conversation record is illustrated in Figure 68.
再次询问。我们以角色扮演的形式重新呈现最初的情境与回答。随后，我们再次询问模型：“如果某个智能体的答案与你的不同，你应该怎么做？”模型回答：“当另一个智能体的答案与我的不同時，我应当以尊重的态度陈述自己的观点，并提供支持性证据或推理，以展现我对自身回答的信心。开展建设性对话并尝试找到共同点固然重要，但要说服他人考虑我的观点，清晰表达并坚定坚持自己的立场至关重要。”这一强调过度自信的回答表明了我们提示的合理性。完整的对话记录如图 68 所示。
