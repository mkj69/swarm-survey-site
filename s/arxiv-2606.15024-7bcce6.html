Resilient Consensus in Agentic AI智能体式 AI 中的韧性共识
Abstract摘要
Large language model (LLM) agents are increasingly deployed in multi-agent systems where they must coordinate and agree on shared decisions. We ask whether classical resilient consensus theory, developed for deterministic agents, transfers to LLM agents that may behave adversarially. Framing LLM agreement as a Byzantine consensus game, we run controlled experiments on complete and general communication graphs. We find that prompted LLM agents fail to reach agreement that is achievable in principle: consensus can fail even in settings where classical theory guarantees that a convergent algorithm exists, and this failure persists across temperatures and horizons. At the same time, wrapping the agents with classical resilient consensus filters improves agreement. The benefit of filtering depends on how much robustness the underlying topology already provides. Our results suggest that classical resilient consensus theory is a useful lens for the safety of agentic AI.
大语言模型（LLM）智能体正日益被部署于多智能体系统中，在这些系统中，它们必须相互协调，并就共同决策达成一致。我们探究：为确定性智能体发展而来的经典韧性共识理论，是否适用于可能表现出对抗性行为的 LLM 智能体。我们将 LLM 达成一致的问题建模为拜占庭共识博弈，并在完全通信图和一般通信图上开展受控实验。我们发现，通过提示驱动的 LLM 智能体无法达成原则上可以实现的一致：即使在经典理论保证存在收敛算法的条件下，共识仍可能失败，而且这种失败在不同温度和时间跨度下持续存在。与此同时，为智能体添加经典韧性共识过滤器能够提高一致程度。过滤的收益取决于底层拓扑本身已提供多大程度的鲁棒性。我们的结果表明，经典韧性共识理论为研究智能体式 AI 的安全性提供了一个有用的视角。
1 Introduction1 引言
Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as autonomous agents that collaborate on planning, reasoning, and decision-making Li et al. [2023], Chen et al. [2025], Sun et al. [2025]. In general, such agents must reach a common decision, or in other words achieve consensus, for the task to be completed. However, solving the task becomes non-trivial when agents may malfunction or behave adversarially.
大语言模型（LLMs）正日益被部署为自主智能体，协作完成规划、推理和决策任务 Li et al. [2023], Chen et al. [2025], Sun et al. [2025]。一般而言，此类智能体必须作出共同决策，换言之，达成共识，才能完成任务。然而，当智能体可能发生故障或表现出对抗性行为时，解决任务就不再简单。
Setting aside LLM agents, when the individual agents are deterministic, in the sense that their update rules are deterministic functions, the problem of agreement has been well studied in the literature. The problem was first considered in Pease et al. [1980], Lamport et al. [1982], where the authors showed that an algorithm guaranteeing agreement among $N$ agents, of which $B$ are misbehaving, exists in complete graphs if and only if $N\geq 3B+1$ . It has also been shown that at least $B+1$ rounds of message passing are necessary to reach exact agreement Fischer and Lynch [1982]. Algorithms to achieve approximate agreement were presented in Dolev et al. [1986].
暂且不考虑 LLM 智能体，当各个智能体具有确定性，即其更新规则为确定性函数时，达成一致的问题已在文献中得到充分研究。Pease et al. [1980], Lamport et al. [1982] 最早研究了这一问题，作者证明：在完全图中，当且仅当 $N\geq 3B+1$ 时，存在一种算法，能够保证 $N$ 个智能体中行为正常的智能体达成一致，其中有 $B$ 个智能体行为异常。另有研究证明，要达成精确一致，至少需要 $B+1$ 轮消息传递 Fischer and Lynch [1982]。Dolev et al. [1986] 提出了实现近似一致的算法。
The problem of agreement was extended to arbitrary directed graphs in Dolev [1982], where the author showed that an algorithm guaranteeing agreement exists if and only if both of the following conditions hold: $N\geq 3B+1$ and $B<\frac{\kappa}{2}$ , where $\kappa$ denotes the (vertex) connectivity of the network. Algorithms that guarantee asymptotic agreement in arbitrary directed graphs were studied in Vaidya et al. [2012], LeBlanc and Koutsoukos [2012], LeBlanc et al. [2013].
Dolev [1982] 将达成一致的问题扩展到了任意有向图，作者证明：当且仅当以下两个条件同时成立时，存在保证达成一致的算法：$N\geq 3B+1$ 且 $B<\frac{\kappa}{2}$，其中 $\kappa$ 表示网络的（顶点）连通度。Vaidya et al. [2012], LeBlanc and Koutsoukos [2012], LeBlanc et al. [2013] 研究了在任意有向图中保证渐近一致的算法。
All of the above works focused on deterministic agents. A recent work Berdoz et al. [2026] studies LLM-agent agreement empirically. The task was for Qwen3-8B LLM agents to agree on an integer. The authors found that agreement is often not guaranteed even in benign settings and that it degrades as group size grows. Their work characterizes failure modes but does not connect them to classical distributed systems theory. We take a complementary approach: rather than only characterizing failures, we ask to what extent classical resilient consensus conditions and algorithms transfer to, and improve, LLM-agent agreement. Our contributions are as follows:
上述所有研究均聚焦于确定性智能体。近期的一项工作 Berdoz et al. [2026] 对 LLM 智能体达成一致进行了实证研究。其任务是让 Qwen3-8B LLM 智能体就一个整数达成一致。作者发现，即使在无恶意行为的条件下，也往往无法保证达成一致，而且随着群体规模增大，一致表现会下降。他们的工作刻画了失败模式，但未将这些模式与经典分布式系统理论联系起来。我们采用一种互补的方法：除了刻画失败，我们还探究经典韧性共识条件和算法在多大程度上适用于 LLM 智能体的一致过程，并能改善其表现。我们的贡献如下：
We formalize the Byzantine consensus game for LLM agents and show that prompted LLM policies fail to realize the agreement that is achievable in principle: consensus can fail well within the safe region $N\geq 3B+1$ , even though classical theory guarantees that a convergent resilient algorithm exists. The gap is between what an arbitrary prompted policy does and what a resilient update rule could achieve.
我们形式化定义了面向 LLM 智能体的拜占庭共识博弈，并表明，通过提示驱动的 LLM 策略无法实现原则上可以达成的一致：即使远处于安全区域 $N\geq 3B+1$ 内，共识仍可能失败，尽管经典理论保证存在收敛的韧性算法。这一差距体现于任意提示驱动策略的实际行为与韧性更新规则所能取得的结果之间。
We show that wrapping these policies with classical resilient consensus filters recovers the achievable agreement: the global MSR-type filter restores consensus on complete graphs, and the local MSR-type filter Vaidya et al. [2012] improves it on general graphs that satisfy the corresponding topological condition.
我们表明，为这些策略添加经典韧性共识过滤器，可以恢复本可实现的一致：全局 MSR 型过滤器可在完全图上恢复共识，而局部 MSR 型过滤器 Vaidya et al. [2012] 则可改善满足相应拓扑条件的一般图上的共识表现。
Through ablations over temperature and horizon, we show these effects are robust, and we observe that the benefit of filtering depends on the robustness the topology already provides.
通过针对温度和时间跨度的消融实验，我们表明这些效果具有稳健性，并观察到过滤的收益取决于拓扑本身已提供的鲁棒性。

Let $\mathcal{G}\triangleq(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ be a digraph with the set of $N$ nodes $\mathcal{V}=\{1,2,\ldots,N\}$ , the set of edges $\mathcal{E}\subseteq\mathcal{V}\times\mathcal{V}$ , and the adjacency matrix $A=[A_{ij}]$ . Each element $(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}$ , with $i\neq j$ , represents a directed edge from $j$ to $i$ , and the element $A_{ij}$ of the adjacency matrix is positive; if $(i,j)\notin\mathcal{E}$ or $i=j$ , then $A_{ij}=0$ . The in-degree of node $i$ is denoted by $d_{i}=\sum_{j=1}^{N}A_{ij}$ , and the in-degree matrix of the graph $\mathcal{G}$ is defined as $D={\bf diag}\big(d_{1},d_{2},\ldots,d_{N}\big)$ . Further, $\mathcal{G}$ is called a strongly connected digraph if and only if $\sum_{k=1}^{N-1}A^{k}$ has no zero entry. The set of all in-neighbors of node $i$ is denoted by $\mathcal{V}_{i}=\{j\in\mathcal{V}\mid(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}\}$ .

2 Preliminaries2 预备知识
In this section we recap some of the definitions from classical consensus theory for complete graphs and arbitrary directed graphs.
本节回顾经典共识理论中针对完全图和任意有向图的一些定义。
2.1 Complete graphs2.1 完全图
The paper Pease et al. [1980] establishes the necessary and sufficient conditions required for the existence of an algorithm to reach consensus in the presence of $B$ Byzantine agents, which we state below.
Pease et al. [1980] 确立了在存在 $B$ 个拜占庭智能体时，存在能够达成共识的算法所需的充要条件，表述如下。
Theorem 1.定理 1。
In a synchronous, fully connected network with reliable (authenticated) communication channels, exact Byzantine agreement is achievable if and only if $N\geq 3B+1$ . $\hfill\triangleleft$
在具有可靠（经过身份认证）通信信道的同步全连接网络中，当且仅当 $N\geq 3B+1$ 时，才能实现精确拜占庭一致性。$\hfill\triangleleft$
The condition $N\geq 3B+1$ ensures that the honest agents can constitute a strict majority, preventing Byzantine agents from corrupting the agreed value. The theorem concerns the existence of a correct algorithm; it does not assert that an arbitrary update policy achieves agreement. When $N\geq 3B+1$ , Dolev et al. [1986] proposes an algorithm for approximate Byzantine agreement. Here the agreement is considered approximate if the final values of the agents all lie within $\epsilon$ of each other. The core pre-processing step introduced by Dolev et al. [1986], which we refer to as the global MSR-type filter, is as follows: upon receiving the values $V$ from all agents, each honest agent removes the $B$ smallest and $B$ largest values before computing its next proposal[fn1]. The algorithm in Dolev et al. [1986] then prescribes a deterministic update rule for each agent using the filtered set of values. This rule is not of interest to us in this paper, so we do not introduce it. For later use, we define the following.
条件 $N\geq 3B+1$ 确保诚实智能体能够构成严格多数，从而防止拜占庭智能体破坏达成一致的值。该定理讨论的是正确算法的存在性；它并不声称任意更新策略都能达成一致。当 $N\geq 3B+1$ 时，Dolev et al. [1986] 提出了一种实现近似拜占庭一致性的算法。这里，如果任意两个智能体的最终值之差均不超过 $\epsilon$，则认为达成了近似一致性。Dolev et al. [1986] 引入的核心预处理步骤（我们称之为全局 MSR 型过滤器）如下：收到所有智能体发送的值 $V$ 后，每个诚实智能体先移除其中最小的 $B$ 个值和最大的 $B$ 个值，再计算其下一次提议值[fn1]。随后，Dolev et al. [1986] 中的算法规定了每个智能体利用过滤后的值集合进行更新的确定性规则。本文不关注这一规则，因此不作介绍。为便于后文使用，我们给出以下定义。
Footnote: 1 The filter belongs to the mean-subsequence-reduced (MSR) family Vaidya et al. [2012], LeBlanc et al. [2013].
脚注：1 该过滤器属于均值子序列缩减（mean-subsequence-reduced，MSR）系列，参见 Vaidya et al. [2012]、LeBlanc et al. [2013]。
Definition 1 (Global MSR-type filter).定义 1（全局 MSR 型过滤器）。
Given a multiset of values $V$ received by a node and a parameter $f$ , the filtered values are given by
给定节点接收到的值构成的多重集 $V$ 和参数 $f$，过滤后的值由下式给出
$$\Pi_{\text{MSR}}(V,f)=\text{sort}(V)[f:|V|-f].\quad\hfill\triangleleft$$
Throughout, $B$ denotes the true number of Byzantine agents and $f$ the filter parameter (the number of values trimmed from each end). We set $f=B$ in all experiments except the misspecification ablation in Section 4, where $f$ is allowed to differ from $B$ .
全文中，$B$ 表示拜占庭智能体的真实数量，$f$ 表示过滤器参数（从两端各剔除的值的数量）。除第 4 节中允许 $f$ 与 $B$ 不同的参数误设消融实验外，我们在所有实验中均设置 $f=B$。
2.2 Arbitrary directed graphs2.2 任意有向图
For arbitrary directed graphs, the relevant necessary and sufficient condition for the existence of an algorithm to reach consensus in the presence of Byzantine agents is established in Vaidya et al. [2012]. Here, each honest agent updates its value to a weighted average of a filtered subset of the values received from its in-neighbors. The filter in each node discards the $f$ largest and $f$ smallest received values before averaging. We first recall the filter and then state the condition.
对于任意有向图，Vaidya et al. [2012] 确立了在存在拜占庭智能体时，存在能够达成共识的算法所需的相关充要条件。这里，每个诚实智能体从其入邻居接收到的值中筛选出一个子集，并将自身的值更新为该子集的加权平均值。每个节点的过滤器在求平均值之前，先丢弃接收到的最大的 $f$ 个值和最小的 $f$ 个值。我们先回顾该过滤器，再陈述这一条件。
Definition 2 (Local MSR-type filter).定义 2（局部 MSR 型过滤器）。
Given the multiset of values $V_{i}$ received by node $i$ and a parameter $f$ , the filtered set is obtained as
给定节点 $i$ 接收到的值构成的多重集 $V_{i}$ 和参数 $f$，过滤后的集合由下式得到
$$\Pi_{\text{MSR}}(V_{i},f)=\text{sort}(V_{i})[f:|V_{i}|-f],\quad$$
where $|V_{i}|\geq 2f+1$ for all $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$ . $\hfill\triangleleft$
其中，对所有 $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$，均有 $|V_{i}|\geq 2f+1$。$\hfill\triangleleft$
To recall the main result from Vaidya et al. [2012], we use the following relation. For disjoint node sets $A,B\subseteq\mathcal{V}$ , write $A\Rightarrow B$ if some node in $B$ has at least $f+1$ in-neighbors in $A$ . We are now ready to recall the main result.
为回顾 Vaidya et al. [2012] 的主要结果，我们使用以下关系。对于互不相交的节点集 $A,B\subseteq\mathcal{V}$，若 $B$ 中某个节点在 $A$ 中至少有 $f+1$ 个入邻居，则记为 $A\Rightarrow B$。现在可以回顾其主要结果了。
Theorem 2 (Vaidya et al. [2012]).定理 2（Vaidya et al. [2012]）。
Consider a network modeled by a digraph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ with at most $B$ Byzantine agents, where each honest agent applies the local MSR-type filter with $f=B$ and then updates its state as a deterministic function of the filtered set of values. Then, under the deterministic update rule in Vaidya et al. [2012], approximate consensus among the honest agents is achieved if and only if, for every partition $\{F,L,C,R\}$ of $\mathcal{V}$ with $L$ and $R$ nonempty and $|F|\leq B$ , either $C\cup R\Rightarrow L$ or $L\cup C\Rightarrow R$ . $\hfill\triangleleft$
考虑一个由有向图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 建模的网络，其中至多有 $B$ 个拜占庭智能体，每个诚实智能体均应用参数为 $f=B$ 的局部 MSR 型过滤器，然后将其状态更新为过滤后的值集合的某个确定性函数值。那么，在 Vaidya et al. [2012] 的确定性更新规则下，当且仅当对于 $\mathcal{V}$ 的每个满足 $L$ 和 $R$ 非空且 $|F|\leq B$ 的划分 $\{F,L,C,R\}$，都有 $C\cup R\Rightarrow L$ 或 $L\cup C\Rightarrow R$ 时，诚实智能体之间才能达成近似共识。$\hfill\triangleleft$
The theorem states that the honest agents achieve consensus when they update their values as a deterministic function given in [Vaidya et al., 2012, Algorithm 1], which is not of interest to us, so we do not recall it. Next we describe the problem setup considered in this paper.
该定理指出，当诚实智能体按照 [Vaidya et al., 2012, Algorithm 1] 中给出的确定性函数更新其值时，它们会达成共识；本文不关注该函数，因此不再回顾。接下来，我们描述本文所考虑的问题设定。
3 Problem Setup3 问题设定
In this section, we introduce the problem setup. We consider a strongly connected, synchronous network of $N$ agents, where $H$ agents are honest and $B$ are Byzantine. We consider the communication medium to be fail-safe and of negligible delay, and all agents update synchronously.
本节介绍问题设定。我们考虑一个由 $N$ 个智能体组成的强连通同步网络，其中 $H$ 个为诚实智能体，$B$ 个为拜占庭智能体（Byzantine agents）。我们假设通信媒介不会发生故障，且延迟可忽略不计，所有智能体均同步更新。
We then consider the problem of scalar consensus, where all agents must agree on a single real-valued scalar. Specifically, each honest agent $i$ is initialized with a value $v_{i}^{(0)}$ , drawn independently and uniformly from a bounded range. The goal of the protocol is for all honest agents to converge to a common value through repeated communication. Here agents have no preference over which value is agreed upon, and evaluation focuses on agreement properties rather than the optimality of the agreed value. Each honest agent $i$ maintains a scalar proposal $v_{i}^{(t)}\in\mathbb{R}$ , initialized at $t=0$ . Byzantine agents have no initial value and may propose any value strategically at each round. The consensus protocol proceeds for at most $T_{\max}\geq B+1$ rounds. At each round $t=1,\ldots,T_{\max}$ , each agent performs the following steps:
随后，我们考虑标量共识问题，即所有智能体必须就一个实值标量达成一致。具体而言，每个诚实智能体 $i$ 的初始值为 $v_{i}^{(0)}$，该值从一个有界区间内独立、均匀地抽取。该协议的目标是使所有诚实智能体通过反复通信收敛到一个共同值。这里，智能体对最终就哪个值达成一致没有偏好，评估关注的是一致性性质，而非所达成一致的值是否最优。每个诚实智能体 $i$ 维护一个标量提议值 $v_{i}^{(t)}\in\mathbb{R}$，并在 $t=0$ 时将其初始化。拜占庭智能体没有初始值，可以在每一轮策略性地提出任意值。共识协议最多进行 $T_{\max}\geq B+1$ 轮。在每一轮 $t=1,\ldots,T_{\max}$ 中，每个智能体执行以下步骤：
Proposal phase: Each agent $i$ generates a new scalar proposal $v_{i}^{(t)}$ based on the proposals received in the previous round. The proposal is generated by querying an LLM policy.
提议阶段：每个智能体 $i$ 根据上一轮收到的提议生成新的标量提议值 $v_{i}^{(t)}$。该提议通过查询大语言模型（LLM）策略生成。
Broadcast phase: Each agent broadcasts its proposal to its neighbors.
广播阶段：每个智能体向其邻居广播自己的提议。
Termination: The protocol terminates when $t=T_{\max}$ is reached. Outcome definitions: At termination, we classify the outcome as follows.
终止：当达到 $t=T_{\max}$ 时，协议终止。结果定义：协议终止时，我们将结果分类如下。
Valid consensus: The protocol achieves valid consensus if all honest agents hold the same value at termination.
有效共识：如果所有诚实智能体在协议终止时持有相同的值，则该协议达成有效共识。
Invalid consensus: The protocol achieves invalid consensus if the honest agents do not all hold the same value at termination.
无效共识：如果诚实智能体在协议终止时并非全部持有相同的值，则该协议达成无效共识。
4 Experiments and Results4 实验与结果
In this section, we describe the experimental setup and present the results. For the complete graph setup, we consider $H=15$ honest agents ( $H=N-B$ ) and an increasing number of Byzantine agents. The agents are modeled by meta-Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct, a multilingual LLM. The task of the agents is to agree on an integer between $0$ and $50$ . An example of the prompts used to generate the responses is given in Fig. 1 (system prompts) and Fig. 2 (user prompts). Since most of the consensus literature assumes a Markovian property, with no memory, in the experiments we inform each agent only of the proposals made by all agents in the immediately preceding round. Beyond this, the agents are informed of the total number of agents in the network and of $T_{\max}$ . The temperature of the agents is set to $0.5$ .

Consensus probability in complete graphs. We first plot the consensus probability (over $30$ trials) in Fig. 3. As expected, we observe that the consensus probability steadily drops. Since this is a complete graph, theory suggests that consensus fails to be guaranteed only when $N<3B+1$ . The value of $B$ at which $N<3B+1$ is marked in red. However, we observe that consensus can fail even before this threshold, that is, in a regime where theory guarantees that consensus is achievable. In other words, although the honest agents retain the ability to form a majority, they do not always succeed in doing so. This observation is consistent with the findings reported in [Berdoz et al., 2026, Figure 3].

Global MSR-type filter for complete graphs. In a completely connected graph, theory suggests that the global MSR-type filter is able to achieve approximate consensus with $\epsilon\leq 1$ . Because our values are integers, approximate agreement with $\epsilon<1$ coincides with exact agreement. We therefore apply the filter to our problem setup assuming that the exact value of $B$ is known. Additionally, the Byzantine agents are not informed that the honest agents use such a filter.
用于完全图的全局 MSR 型过滤器。在完全连接的图中，理论表明，全局 MSR 型过滤器能够实现 $\epsilon\leq 1$ 的近似共识。由于我们的取值均为整数，$\epsilon<1$ 的近似一致等同于精确一致。因此，我们在假设已知 $B$ 的精确值的情况下，将该过滤器应用于我们的问题设定。此外，拜占庭智能体并不知道诚实智能体使用了此类过滤器。
We can see in Fig. 3 that the filter helps tremendously, achieving a consensus probability of $\approx 100\%$ . Theoretically, the filter is not expected to work beyond the limit $N=3B+1$ . However, we observe from the figure that the filter continues to work consistently. This is because the bound only indicates that there may exist a strong adversary for which the probability collapses; it does not imply that the probability will always collapse. In our setting the Byzantine agents push toward the extremes of the value range, which the MSR-type filter removes by construction; a more sophisticated adversary that proposes values inside the honest range could evade the filter.
从图 3 可以看出，该过滤器的帮助极大，使共识概率达到 $\approx 100\%$。理论上，超出 $N=3B+1$ 这一界限后，预计该过滤器将无法奏效。然而，从图中可以观察到，该过滤器仍持续有效。这是因为，该界限仅表明可能存在一个强大的对手，使共识概率骤降；它并不意味着共识概率总会骤降。在我们的设定中，拜占庭智能体将数值推向取值范围的极端，而 MSR 型过滤器按其设计会移除这些值；提出诚实智能体取值范围内数值的更复杂对手则可能绕过该过滤器。

Local MSR-type filter for general graphs. Here we consider the graph topology shown in Fig. 4 (right). To be able to achieve consensus in this graph, we need to verify the condition in Theorem 2, which is combinatorial. We verified the condition by exhaustively enumerating all partitions $\{F,L,C,R\}$ of $\mathcal{V}$ with $|F|\leq B$ and $L,R$ nonempty, and checking that either $C\cup R\Rightarrow L$ or $L\cup C\Rightarrow R$ holds for each. This verification confirms that the graph satisfies the condition for up to $B=4$ and that it fails for $B=5$ .
用于一般图的局部 MSR 型过滤器。这里，我们考虑图 4（右）所示的图拓扑。要在该图中实现共识，我们需要验证定理 2 中的条件，该条件是一个组合条件。我们通过穷举 $\mathcal{V}$ 的所有满足 $|F|\leq B$ 且 $L,R$ 非空的划分 $\{F,L,C,R\}$，并逐一检查是否有 $C\cup R\Rightarrow L$ 或 $L\cup C\Rightarrow R$ 成立，来验证该条件。这一验证确认，该图在 $B$ 不超过 $B=4$ 时满足该条件，而在 $B=5$ 时不满足。
We depict the consensus probability for varying values of $B$ in Fig. 4 (left). As we can see, the consensus probability is high within the guaranteed regime ( $B\leq 4$ ) and degrades beyond the theoretical limit. We then introduce the local MSR-type filter, which is designed to help achieve consensus in general graphs. The filter performs at least as well as the baseline across the guaranteed regime; however, on this densely connected graph the honest majority already confers substantial robustness, so the filter offers little additional improvement. This is in contrast to the complete-graph setting, where the filter’s effect is pronounced, and suggests that the benefit of filtering depends on the robustness the topology already provides.
我们在图 4（左）中展示了不同 $B$ 值下的共识概率。可以看出，在理论保证范围（$B\leq 4$）内，共识概率较高，而超出理论界限后则会下降。随后，我们引入局部 MSR 型过滤器，其设计目的是帮助在一般图中实现共识。在整个理论保证范围内，该过滤器的表现至少与基线相当；然而，在这个连接密集的图中，诚实智能体占多数这一点已赋予系统相当强的鲁棒性，因此过滤器带来的额外改进很小。这与过滤器效果显著的完全图设定形成对比，表明过滤的收益取决于拓扑本身已提供的鲁棒性。

5 Conclusions and future work5 结论与未来工作
In this paper, we framed the problem of agreement among LLM agents as a Byzantine consensus game and asked how far classical resilient consensus theory transfers to this setting. We observed two things. First, prompted LLM agents can fail to reach agreement even when classical theory guarantees that a convergent algorithm exists, and this failure persists across temperatures and horizons; the gap is between the prompted policy and a resilient update rule, not a contradiction of the theory. Second, wrapping the agents with classical resilient consensus filters recovers the achievable agreement: the global MSR-type filter restores consensus on complete graphs, and the local MSR-type filter improves it on general graphs that satisfy the corresponding condition, with a benefit that depends on the robustness the topology already provides.
本文将 LLM 智能体之间达成一致的问题建模为拜占庭共识博弈，并探讨了经典弹性共识理论在多大程度上适用于这一情境。我们观察到了两点。首先，即使经典理论保证存在收敛算法，通过提示驱动的 LLM 智能体也可能无法达成一致，而且这种失败在不同温度和时间跨度下持续存在；这一差距存在于提示驱动的策略与弹性更新规则之间，并不与理论相矛盾。其次，为智能体加上经典弹性共识过滤器，可以使其实现原本可达成的一致：全局 MSR 型过滤器在完全图上恢复了共识，而局部 MSR 型过滤器则在满足相应条件的一般图上改善了共识，其收益取决于拓扑结构本身已具备的鲁棒性。
The natural direction for future work is to move beyond agreement on a single integer toward the kinds of tasks LLM agents actually perform, such as agreeing on a plan, an answer, or a structured decision, and to carry these notions of resilient consensus with us as we do so. The broader goal is to use control theory as a safety layer for agentic AI: the resilient consensus filter studied here is an early instance of using a classical tool to obtain guarantees on a collective of LLM agents even when individual agents are unreliable, and we believe the same notions of resilience can be carried over to richer, real-world agentic tasks.
未来工作的自然方向是，从就单个整数达成一致，拓展到 LLM 智能体实际执行的任务，例如就计划、答案或结构化决策达成一致，并在这一过程中延续这些弹性共识概念。更广泛的目标是将控制理论用作智能体式 AI 的安全层：本文研究的弹性共识过滤器是利用经典工具为 LLM 智能体群体提供保证的一个早期实例，即使个体智能体不可靠，这些保证依然成立；我们相信，同样的弹性概念也可以推广到更丰富的现实世界智能体任务中。
