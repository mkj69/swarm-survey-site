Abstract摘要
Multi-agent systems can improve reliability, yet under a fixed test-time budget they often help, saturate, or even collapse. We develop a deliberately minimal and calibratable theory that predicts these regimes from three binding constraints of modern agent stacks: finite context windows, lossy inter-agent communication, and shared failures among similar agents. Each leaf agent is summarized by a compute–performance scaling exponent $\beta$ ; communication is captured by a message-length fidelity curve $\gamma(m)$ ; dependence is captured by an effective shared-error correlation $\rho$ ; and a context window $W$ imposes hard fan-in limits that make hierarchy necessary. For binary success/failure tasks with majority aggregation, we prove a sharp phase transition for deep $b$ -ary trees with correlated inputs and lossy communication: a single scalar $\alpha_{\rho}$ (combining $\gamma(m)$ , $\rho$ , and fan-in $b$ ) determines whether weak signal is amplified to a nontrivial fixed point or washed out to chance. In the amplifying regime, we derive an organization exponent $s$ and show that budgeted synergy, i.e., outperforming the best single agent under the same total budget, occurs exactly when $s>\beta$ , yielding closed-form compute allocation rules and explicit budget thresholds. We further characterize saturation via a mixing depth and provide a conservative clipped predictor that remains accurate across growth and saturation. A continuous-performance warm-up gives closed-form risks for star, chain, and tree organizations, making correlation- and communication-induced floors explicit and exposing the core design trade-offs in a smooth setting. Finally, we validate the predicted phase boundaries in controlled synthetic simulations and show how the same mechanisms explain the dominant bottlenecks reported in recent large-scale matched-budget studies of LLM agent-system scaling, including context saturation, subcritical error cascades, and diminishing returns at strong baselines.

1 Introduction1 引言
Multi-agent systems are often presented as a reliability primitive: if a single agent is fallible, run many agents, let them interact, and aggregate their outputs. Under a fixed computational budget per task, however, multi-agent coordination is not reliably beneficial. The same additional agents that sometimes improve performance can also lead to saturation or even outright degradation. This paper develops a predictive theory of this brittleness: when multi-agent organization produces budgeted synergy, i.e., outperforming the best single agent under the same total computational budget, and when scaling out must fail.
多智能体系统常被视为一种可靠性基础构件：如果单个智能体会出错，就运行多个智能体，让它们相互交互，并聚合其输出。然而，在每项任务的计算预算固定时，多智能体协调并不总能带来收益。那些有时能提升性能的新增智能体，也可能导致性能饱和，甚至直接退化。本文针对这种脆弱性构建了一套预测理论：多智能体组织何时能够产生预算约束下的协同增益，即在相同总计算预算下优于最佳单智能体，以及横向扩展何时必然失败。
The LLM era has made this phenomenon impossible to ignore. It is now routine to instantiate multiple LLM-based agents, assign roles, and coordinate them through discussion, critique, or voting in so-called “agent societies” and orchestration frameworks (Brown et al., 2020; Yao et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024). The underlying intuition is classical: ensembling can reduce error (Breiman, 1996; Freund and Schapire, 1997; Dietterich, 2000), and interaction protocols such as debate or amplification aim to turn many weak judgments into a stronger one (Irving et al., 2018; Christiano et al., 2018; Du et al., 2024). At the same time, controlled matched-budget evaluations increasingly reveal strong task- and topology-dependence, including regimes in which multi-agent variants degrade once coordination overhead and information loss dominate (Kim et al., 2025).
LLM 时代使这一现象变得不容忽视。如今，实例化多个基于 LLM 的智能体、为其分配角色，并在所谓的“智能体社会”和编排框架中通过讨论、批评或投票来协调它们，已成为常见做法 (Brown et al., 2020; Yao et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024)。其背后的直觉由来已久：集成能够减少错误 (Breiman, 1996; Freund and Schapire, 1997; Dietterich, 2000)，而辩论或放大等交互协议旨在将多个较弱的判断转化为一个更强的判断 (Irving et al., 2018; Christiano et al., 2018; Du et al., 2024)。与此同时，受控的等预算评估日益揭示出性能对任务和拓扑结构的强烈依赖，其中包括协调开销和信息损失占据主导后，多智能体变体性能退化的情形 (Kim et al., 2025)。
Our starting point is that these failures are not implementation accidents but structural consequences of a small number of constraints that repeatedly bind in modern agent stacks. First, agent errors are not independent: multiple instances derived from the same base model and prompt often share systematic failure modes, producing “groupthink” rather than error cancellation. Second, coordination is communication-limited: as systems scale, agents must compress reasoning into bounded-length messages, and the resulting information loss compounds across layers. Third, coordination is context-limited: a central aggregator cannot read arbitrarily many messages under a finite context window, so naive scale-out saturates structurally. Recent work on large-population collaboration and topology learning highlights the same bottleneck: token and context limits are frequently the binding resource (Qian et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2024).
我们的出发点是：这些失败并非实现过程中的偶然问题，而是少数几项在现代智能体技术栈中反复构成实际限制的约束所导致的结构性结果。首先，智能体的错误并不相互独立：基于相同基础模型和提示构建的多个实例往往具有共同的系统性失效模式，产生的是“群体思维”，而非错误抵消。其次，协调受通信限制：随着系统规模扩大，智能体必须将推理压缩为长度受限的消息，由此产生的信息损失会逐层累积。第三，协调受上下文限制：在有限的上下文窗口下，中央聚合器无法读取任意多条消息，因此简单的横向扩展会因结构性限制而达到饱和。近期关于大规模群体协作和拓扑学习的研究也强调了同一瓶颈：词元和上下文限制经常是实际起约束作用的资源限制 (Qian et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2024)。
Today, most agent-system design still proceeds by heuristics: choose a topology, message format, and number of agents, then iterate. In spirit, this resembles the hand-crafted feature engineering era of machine learning: powerful components exist, but system-level behavior depends on ad hoc choices and expensive trial-and-error. Our goal is to push multi-agent design toward an “agentic intelligence from first principles” approach: make the dominant constraints explicit, explain why scaling out works or fails under a fixed computational budget, and derive sharp, quantitative regime boundaries, most notably, a phase transition for budgeted multi-agent synergy.
如今，大多数智能体系统的设计仍依赖启发式方法：选择拓扑结构、消息格式和智能体数量，然后迭代调整。从理念上看，这类似于机器学习中手工特征工程的时代：虽然已有强大的组件，但系统层面的行为仍取决于临时性的选择和代价高昂的试错。我们的目标是推动多智能体设计采用一种“从第一性原理出发构建智能体智能”的方法：明确主要约束，解释在固定计算预算下横向扩展为何有效或失效，并推导出精确的定量区间边界，其中最重要的是预算约束下多智能体协同增益的相变。
A minimal, calibratable coordination theory.一种极简且可校准的协调理论。
We develop a deliberately small framework that isolates the coordination bottlenecks above while remaining analytically tractable. The term agent is used abstractly: a black-box solver that, given a task instance and an allocated compute budget, produces an output that another component can consume (a decision, a score, or an $m$ -token message). This deliberately includes LLM-based agents as a primary motivating instance, because (i) tokens provide a natural budget unit, (ii) finite context windows induce hard fan-in constraints, and (iii) single-agent capability is often well described by compute scaling laws (Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022). At the same time, the abstraction is broader than LLMs: none of the results relies on language as such, only on (a) how single-agent quality scales with compute, (b) how much usable information survives bounded-length communication, and (c) how strongly agents’ failures co-move.
我们构建了一个刻意保持精简的框架，在分离出上述协调瓶颈的同时，保持其在解析上的可处理性。这里以抽象意义使用智能体一词：它是一个黑箱求解器，在给定任务实例和分配的计算预算后，生成可供另一组件使用的输出（决策、分数或一条包含 $m$ 个词元的消息）。这一定义特意将基于 LLM 的智能体纳入其中，并将其作为主要的动机实例，因为 (i) 词元提供了自然的预算单位，(ii) 有限的上下文窗口带来了严格的扇入约束，且 (iii) 单智能体能力通常可以用计算规模律很好地描述 (Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022)。同时，这一抽象的适用范围比 LLM 更广：所有结果都不依赖语言本身，而仅依赖于 (a) 单智能体质量如何随计算量扩展，(b) 有限长度的通信能保留多少可用信息，以及 (c) 各智能体的失败之间存在多强的联动性。

By calibratable we do not mean a theory “about measurement.” We mean a coordination theory parameterized by a small set of environment-dependent effective quantities that can be estimated once (via targeted experiments) and then used to predict qualitative regimes. Concretely, the framework is driven by single-agent scaling (exponent $\beta$ ), communication fidelity as a function of message length (e.g., $\gamma(m)$ ), shared-failure correlation ( $\rho$ ), and the context window constraint ( $W$ ), together with a total budget $B$ that fixes the scale-up/scale-out trade-off. These parameters are not claimed to capture every detail of a real agent stack; they form a minimal interface for diagnosing which constraint is binding and for predicting when organization helps, saturates, or fails.
我们所说的可校准，并不是指一种“关于测量”的理论。我们指的是一种协调理论，其参数由少量依赖环境的有效量构成；这些量可以通过有针对性的实验一次性估计，随后用于预测定性区间。具体而言，该框架由单智能体扩展规律（指数 $\beta$）、作为消息长度函数的通信保真度（例如 $\gamma(m)$）、共同失败相关性（$\rho$）和上下文窗口约束（$W$）共同决定，并结合总预算 $B$，由后者确定纵向扩展与横向扩展之间的权衡。我们并不声称这些参数能够刻画真实智能体技术栈的所有细节；它们构成了一个最小接口，用于诊断哪项约束正在起制约作用，并预测组织何时带来帮助、达到饱和或失效。

Within this framework we analyze three canonical organizations: star (central aggregation), chain (sequential handoff), and hierarchical tree. Our main results characterize when hierarchy is viable and when it is provably counterproductive. For binary success/failure tasks with majority aggregation, we prove a sharp amplification–collapse transition for deep trees: a scalar $\alpha_{\rho}$ (combining communication fidelity, correlation, and fan-in) determines whether weak signal amplifies across layers or is washed out to chance, in the spirit of reconstruction thresholds in noisy broadcasting on trees (Evans et al., 2000; Mossel, 2001; Kesten and Stigum, 1966). In the amplifying regime, we derive an organization exponent $s$ governing growth with the number of leaves and show that budgeted synergy in the growth range occurs exactly when $s>\beta$ , yielding closed-form compute allocation and explicit budget thresholds. We also characterize finite-depth saturation via a nontrivial fixed point and mixing-depth guarantees. Finally, we make precise why topology becomes decisive only because constraints bind: absent context limits, central aggregation is information-dominant (data processing inequality) (Cover and Thomas, 2006), and hierarchy is valuable only insofar as it bypasses fan-in limits without crossing into the collapsing regime. Methodological stance: theory vs. benchmarking.方法论立场：理论与基准评测。
Our aim in this paper is to theoretically delineate regimes of behavior and identify the mechanisms that govern when multi-agent systems help, saturate, or collapse under realistic constraints. Large-scale matched-budget evaluations with real LLM agent stacks are expensive and can be confounded by prompt engineering and rapidly changing model/tool ecosystems. Accordingly, we validate the sharp regime boundaries via controlled synthetic simulations (Section 6.2) that probe the theory under its own assumptions, and we use external controlled evidence (Kim et al., 2025) to show that the bottlenecks isolated by the theory (context saturation, subcritical collapse, and diminishing returns) are salient in modern deployments.
本文旨在从理论上划定不同的行为区间，并识别在现实约束下决定多智能体系统何时能够带来增益、趋于饱和或发生崩溃的机制。使用真实 LLM 智能体技术栈开展预算匹配的大规模评估成本高昂，而且可能受到提示工程以及快速变化的模型与工具生态系统的混杂影响。因此，我们通过受控的合成模拟（第 6.2 节）来验证鲜明的区间边界，这些模拟在理论自身的假设下检验该理论；同时，我们利用外部受控证据（Kim et al., 2025），表明该理论所分离出的瓶颈（上下文饱和、次临界崩溃和收益递减）在现代部署中具有显著影响。

2 Framework and Measurable Parameters2 框架与可测量参数
This section formalizes the abstraction used throughout the paper. We keep only three constraints that repeatedly bind in practice: finite context windows, lossy communication, and shared failures, and summarize the environment by a small set of quantities. These quantities play distinct roles: $\beta$ captures how a single agent improves with compute, $\gamma(m)$ (or $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ ) captures what survives an $m$ -token message, and $\rho$ captures how correlated different agents’ errors are. Together with a total budget $B$ and a context window $W$ , they determine whether scaling out amplifies signal, saturates at a floor, or collapses.
本节对全文使用的抽象模型进行形式化描述。我们仅保留实践中反复成为制约因素的三项约束：有限的上下文窗口、有损通信和共同失效，并用少量变量概括环境。这些变量各有不同的作用：$\beta$ 刻画单个智能体如何随计算量增加而改进，$\gamma(m)$（或 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$）刻画经过一条含 $m$ 个 token 的消息后保留下来的信息，而 $\rho$ 刻画不同智能体的错误之间的相关程度。它们与总预算 $B$ 和上下文窗口 $W$ 共同决定横向扩展会放大信号、在某个下限处饱和，还是发生崩溃。
We model a multi-agent system as a directed acyclic computation graph. Leaf nodes (agents) produce signals about a latent task variable $Y$ ; internal nodes receive messages from their children (possibly through a lossy channel) and apply an aggregation rule; the root output is the system prediction. Throughout, an agent is a black-box solver with an adjustable compute budget $x$ that emits a bounded-length output (a decision, a score, or an $m$ -token message). LLM-based agents are the primary motivating instance, but the framework only assumes a single-agent scaling curve, a communication-fidelity curve, and a shared-failure correlation.
我们将多智能体系统建模为有向无环计算图。叶节点（智能体）生成关于潜在任务变量 $Y$ 的信号；内部节点从其子节点接收消息（可能经由有损信道），并应用聚合规则；根节点的输出即为系统预测。在全文中，智能体均指计算预算 $x$ 可调的黑箱求解器，其输出长度有界（输出可以是决策、分数或含 $m$ 个 token 的消息）。基于 LLM 的智能体是本研究的主要出发实例，但该框架仅假设存在单智能体扩展曲线、通信保真度曲线和共同失效相关性。
2.1 Tasks and performance metrics2.1 任务与性能指标
We study two task primitives that are deliberately simple but theoretically expressive. They are not meant to exhaust the diversity of real-world tasks; rather, they isolate the coordination bottlenecks that arise repeatedly inside more complex multi-agent pipelines. Binary success/failure captures decision-like outcomes (e.g., accept/reject, pass/fail, correctness of a proposed action), while continuous performance on $[0,1]$ captures graded judgments, scores, or calibrated estimates. Together, these two primitives cover a wide range of evaluation signals used in practice and allow clean analysis of aggregation, communication loss, and shared failures. From a theoretical standpoint, any richer task that can be decomposed into local scoring, communication, and aggregation steps must confront the same coordination constraints analyzed here.
我们研究两种刻意保持简单、但在理论上具有丰富表达能力的任务基本形式。它们并非旨在穷尽现实任务的多样性，而是用于分离出更复杂的多智能体流水线中反复出现的协调瓶颈。二元成功／失败刻画决策类结果（例如，接受／拒绝、通过／未通过、所提议行动的正确性），而 $[0,1]$ 上的连续性能则刻画分级判断、分数或经过校准的估计。这两种基本形式共同涵盖了实践中使用的广泛评估信号，并使我们能够清晰地分析聚合、通信损失和共同失效。从理论角度看，任何能够分解为局部评分、通信和聚合步骤的更丰富任务，都必须面对本文分析的相同协调约束。
The continuous setting serves as a smooth warm-up where many quantities admit closed forms and correlation and communication floors are explicit. The binary setting is not a special case of the continuous one: the nonlinearity of majority vote introduces qualitatively new behavior, including a sharp amplification–collapse phase transition that has no analogue in linear aggregation. Many realistic agent workflows combine both primitives. For example, agents may exchange graded scores or confidence estimates internally, but the final system decision is binary. So analyzing both is necessary to capture the full coordination picture.
连续情形提供了平滑的入门分析，其中许多量具有闭式表达式，且相关性与通信造成的下限均可显式给出。二元情形并不是连续情形的特例：多数投票的非线性引入了性质上全新的行为，包括线性聚合中不存在的鲜明放大—崩溃相变。许多现实中的智能体工作流结合了这两种基本形式。例如，智能体可能在内部交换分级分数或置信度估计，但系统的最终决策是二元的。因此，必须同时分析两者，才能完整刻画协调过程。
Binary tasks.二元任务。
Let $Y\in\{-1,+1\}$ denote the ground truth. A system outputs $\widehat{Y}\in\{-1,+1\}$ . We measure performance through the bias
令 $Y\in\{-1,+1\}$ 表示真实值。系统输出 $\widehat{Y}\in\{-1,+1\}$。我们通过偏置来衡量性能
$$\mu\;:=\;\mathbb{E}[\widehat{Y}Y]\in[-1,1],\qquad\Pr(\widehat{Y}=Y)=\frac{1+\mu}{2}.$$
The bias $\mu$ is a natural summary statistic for weak agents: it measures how far performance is above chance and composes cleanly under simple channel models and aggregation maps. This makes it particularly suitable for analyzing whether small, local improvements are amplified or destroyed by hierarchical organization.
偏置 $\mu$ 是弱智能体的一种自然汇总统计量：它衡量性能超出随机猜测水平的程度，并且在简单信道模型和聚合映射下易于组合。这使其特别适合分析微小的局部改进会被层级组织放大还是破坏。
Continuous tasks.连续任务。
Let $Y\in[0,1]$ and a system output $\widehat{Y}\in[0,1]$ . We use mean squared error (MSE)
设 $Y\in[0,1]$，系统输出为 $\widehat{Y}\in[0,1]$。我们使用均方误差（MSE）
$$v\;:=\;\mathbb{E}\big[(\widehat{Y}-Y)^{2}\big],$$
and occasionally report a bounded performance score such as $\mathrm{Perf}=1/(1+v)$ , which is monotone in $v$ . Continuous metrics capture graded information that is often exchanged within agent systems (scores, confidence levels, value estimates) and lead to linear aggregation dynamics. In this setting, the effect of correlation and communication loss appears as explicit error floors rather than phase transitions, providing intuition that complements the binary analysis.
并偶尔报告有界的性能分数，例如 $\mathrm{Perf}=1/(1+v)$，它是 $v$ 的单调函数。连续型指标能够刻画智能体系统内部经常交换的具有程度差异的信息（分数、置信水平、价值估计），并产生线性聚合动力学。在这一设定下，相关性和通信损失的影响表现为显式的误差下限，而非相变，从而提供了与二元分析互补的直观认识。
2.2 Single-agent capability and scaling2.2 单智能体能力与扩展
Our central comparison is fixed budget: the same total computational budget $B$ can be allocated either to scale up a single agent (making one agent stronger) or to scale out across many agents (running more agents in parallel). Understanding when scale out can outperform scale up under the same budget is the core question of this paper.
我们的核心比较以预算固定为前提：相同的总计算预算 $B$ 既可以用于纵向扩展单个智能体（使一个智能体更强），也可以用于横向扩展多个智能体（并行运行更多智能体）。理解在相同预算下横向扩展何时能够优于纵向扩展，是本文的核心问题。
We model per-leaf compute by a single nonnegative knob $x\geq 0$ , representing the share of the per-task computational budget allocated to an individual agent. In LLM-based agents, $x$ may correspond to inference tokens devoted to reasoning, the number of samples or self-consistency votes, structured test-time inference procedures (e.g., generate–select or tournament-style algorithms), the number of tool calls, or any other additive per-task resource that trades off directly against parallelism.
我们用单个非负调节参数 $x\geq 0$ 对每个叶节点的计算量建模，表示分配给单个智能体的每任务计算预算份额。在基于 LLM 的智能体中，$x$ 可以对应于用于推理的推理阶段 token 数、采样次数或自一致性投票数、结构化的测试时推理流程（例如，生成–选择或锦标赛式算法）、工具调用次数，或任何其他可加的每任务资源，这些资源都与并行度存在直接权衡。
We summarize the effect of per-agent compute via a single-agent scaling law. Classical scaling laws are typically reported for training loss as a function of training compute (Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022). Here, however, we use scaling laws in a different but related sense: as an effective description of how a fixed trained agent’s test-time performance improves when allocated additional per-task computational resources. Recent work on test-time or inference-time scaling shows that such improvements can follow systematic power-law or exponential trends in appropriate regimes and metrics, including accuracy, error probability, or calibrated scores (Chen et al., ; Levi, 2025; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2025). Motivated by these findings, we treat the scaling exponent as a per-task scaling exponent governing scale up, not as a statement about retraining the model.
我们通过单智能体扩展定律来概括每个智能体计算量的影响。经典扩展定律通常描述训练损失与训练计算量之间的函数关系（Kaplan et al., 2020; Hoffmann et al., 2022）。然而，这里我们在一种不同但相关的意义上使用扩展定律：它有效描述了一个训练完成且固定的智能体在获得额外的每任务计算资源后，其测试时性能如何提升。近期关于测试时或推理时扩展的研究表明，在适当的工作区间和指标下，这类提升可以遵循系统性的幂律或指数趋势，相关指标包括准确率、错误概率或经过校准的分数（Chen et al., ; Levi, 2025; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2025）。受这些发现启发，我们将扩展指数视为支配纵向扩展的每任务扩展指数，而非关于重新训练模型的论断。
For binary success/failure tasks, a leaf agent allocated compute $x$ produces a vote $\widehat{Y}$ with bias
对于二元成功/失败任务，分配了计算量 $x$ 的叶节点智能体产生投票 $\widehat{Y}$，其偏置为
$$\mu_{0}(x)=g(x),$$
where $g(\cdot)$ is increasing and exhibits diminishing returns. For continuous tasks, a leaf produces an estimate $X$ with conditional variance $v_{0}(x)$ , where $v_{0}(\cdot)$ decreases with $x$ .
其中，$g(\cdot)$ 单调递增，并表现出边际收益递减。对于连续型任务，叶节点产生估计值 $X$，其条件方差为 $v_{0}(x)$，其中 $v_{0}(\cdot)$ 随 $x$ 增大而减小。
In our main theorems, we work in a regime where these functions are well-approximated by power laws:
在主要定理中，我们考虑这些函数可以用幂律较好近似的区间：
$$\displaystyle g(x)\approx k\,x^{\beta}\quad\text{(small-signal regime)}, \\ \displaystyle v_{0}(x)\approx c\,x^{-\beta},$$
with constants $k,c>0$ and a single-agent scaling exponent $\beta>0$ . Here “small-signal regime” refers to the operating range of $x$ in which (i) the local power-law approximation is accurate enough for comparison, and (ii) the induced leaf performance is not already saturated. In the binary analysis, this typically corresponds to weak leaf votes (small bias $\mu_{0}(x)$ , i.e., accuracy only slightly above chance), which is precisely the regime where the majority update map is well-approximated by its derivative at the origin and where the organization exponent $s$ governs growth. In the continuous warm-up, it corresponds to the pre-saturation range where aggregation has not yet hit the communication/correlation floor. The exponent $\beta$ is not a universal constant: it depends on the model, prompting, tools, test-time procedure, and task family, and is intended to be measured in the operating regime of interest. Our theory does not require the power-law approximation to hold globally or asymptotically; it relies only on the existence of a local scaling exponent $\beta$ in the budget range where the scale-up versus scale-out comparison is made.
其中，常数 $k,c>0$，单智能体扩展指数 $\beta>0$。这里，“小信号区间”指 $x$ 的如下工作范围：(i) 局部幂律近似足够准确，可以用于比较；(ii) 由此产生的叶节点性能尚未饱和。在二元分析中，这通常对应于较弱的叶节点投票（偏置 $\mu_{0}(x)$ 较小，即准确率仅略高于随机猜测水平），而这恰好是多数表决更新映射可以用其在原点处的导数较好近似、且组织指数 $s$ 支配增长的区间。在连续型任务的预备分析中，它对应于聚合尚未触及通信/相关性所决定的误差下限的饱和前区间。指数 $\beta$ 并非普适常数：它取决于模型、提示方式、工具、测试时流程和任务族，应在所关注的工作区间内测量。我们的理论不要求幂律近似在全局或渐近意义上成立；它仅依赖于在比较纵向扩展与横向扩展的预算范围内存在局部扩展指数 $\beta$。
2.3 Communication as a controllable lossy channel2.3 作为可控有损信道的通信
Agents coordinate through messages. Our analysis uses a compact abstraction of how message length affects what downstream nodes can reliably use.
智能体通过消息进行协调。我们的分析采用一种简洁的抽象，描述消息长度如何影响下游节点能够可靠利用的信息。
We model each edge as a channel controlled by a message length parameter $m$ (in tokens). Longer messages generally preserve more usable information, but they also cost more budget and reduce feasible fan-in under a finite context window.
我们将每条边建模为由消息长度参数 $m$（以 token 为单位）控制的信道。较长的消息通常能够保留更多可用信息，但也会消耗更多预算，并在有限的上下文窗口下减少可实现的扇入数。
Binary: an effective bit channel.二元情形：等效比特信道。
For binary tasks and majority-style protocols, it is natural to treat each child as intending to transmit a single bit of information (its vote or decision), encoded into a message of length $m$ . We model the receiver’s decoded bit as passing through a binary symmetric channel (BSC) with reliability $\gamma(m)\in(0,1]$ :
对于二元任务和多数表决式协议，自然可以认为每个子节点都试图传输一个比特的信息（其投票或决策），并将其编码为长度为 $m$ 的消息。我们将接收方解码得到的比特建模为经过可靠度为 $\gamma(m)\in(0,1]$ 的二元对称信道（BSC）后的结果：
$$\mu_{\text{recv}}=\gamma(m)\,\mu.$$
Equivalently, $\gamma(m)=1-2\epsilon(m)$ , where $\epsilon(m)$ is an effective flip probability.
等价地，$\gamma(m)=1-2\epsilon(m)$，其中 $\epsilon(m)$ 是等效翻转概率。
Continuous: additive distortion.连续情形：加性失真。
For continuous tasks, we model transmission as additive zero-mean distortion,
对于连续型任务，我们将传输建模为加性零均值失真，
$$\widetilde{X}=X+\eta,\qquad\mathbb{E}[\eta]=0,\quad\mathrm{Var}(\eta)=\sigma_{c}^{2}(m),$$
where $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ decreases with $m$ . This captures compression loss, imperfect interpretation, and degradation introduced by summarization or constrained prompts.
其中，$\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ 随 $m$ 增大而减小。这涵盖了压缩损失、理解偏差，以及摘要或受限提示引入的性能退化。
The intent is not to claim that real messages are literally bits or Gaussian perturbations, but to represent an effective fidelity curve as a function of $m$ . In particular, $\gamma(m)$ or $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ can be estimated by simple encode–decode experiments at the same message length.
这里的意图并非声称真实消息在字面意义上就是比特或高斯扰动，而是将有效保真度曲线表示为 $m$ 的函数。具体而言，可以通过在相同消息长度下进行简单的编码–解码实验来估计 $\gamma(m)$ 或 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$。
2.4 Shared-failure correlation and the $\rho$ model2.4 共享失误的相关性与 $\rho$ 模型
A key departure from idealized ensemble analyses is that agent errors are not independent, even though many ensemble gains are easiest to analyze under weak dependence assumptions (Dietterich, 2000; Germain et al., 2015). Agents instantiated from the same base model and prompt often share systematic error modes, which can dominate any aggregation gain. We capture this effect with a single, measurable correlation parameter $\rho\in[0,1)$ .
与理想化集成分析的一个关键区别在于，智能体的错误并不独立，尽管许多集成增益在弱依赖假设下最容易分析 (Dietterich, 2000; Germain et al., 2015)。由相同基础模型和提示实例化的智能体往往具有共同的系统性错误模式，这些模式可能压倒任何聚合增益。我们用单个可测量的相关性参数 $\rho\in[0,1)$ 来刻画这一效应。
Binary: correlation of signed correctness.二元情形：带符号正确性的相关性。
For a leaf vote $\widehat{Y}_{i}$ , define the signed correctness variable
对于叶节点的投票 $\widehat{Y}_{i}$，定义带符号正确性变量
$$S_{i}:=\widehat{Y}_{i}Y\in\{-1,+1\}.$$
Then $\mathbb{E}[S_{i}]=\mu_{0}$ , and we define
则 $\mathbb{E}[S_{i}]=\mu_{0}$，并且我们定义
$$\rho\;:=\;\mathrm{Corr}(S_{i},S_{j}),\qquad i\neq j,$$
estimated empirically by averaging pairwise correlations across tasks and agent instances. Here $i$ and $j$ index two agent instances whose outputs are aggregated together: for a star this is any pair of leaves; for a tree it should be interpreted as the sibling correlation at an internal node. In practice the effective correlation can depend on depth, heterogeneity, or the message schema; for tractability we treat $\rho$ as a depth-independent effective parameter and return to extensions in Section 7. Intuitively, $\rho=0$ corresponds to independent errors, while $\rho$ close to 1 indicates near-perfect groupthink.
其经验估计通过对不同任务和智能体实例的两两相关性取平均得到。这里，$i$ 和 $j$ 表示输出被共同聚合的两个智能体实例的索引：对于星形结构，它们可以是任意一对叶节点；对于树形结构，应将其理解为内部节点处兄弟节点之间的相关性。在实践中，有效相关性可能取决于深度、异质性或消息模式；为便于分析，我们将 $\rho$ 视为与深度无关的有效参数，并在第 7 节重新讨论扩展情形。直观而言，$\rho=0$ 对应于相互独立的错误，而 $\rho$ 接近 1 则表示近乎完全的群体思维。
For analysis, we use a concrete generative model that matches this definition exactly. At each aggregation node, with probability $\rho$ the children share a common “mode” (their signed correctness variables are identical), and with probability $1-\rho$ they are conditionally independent given $Y$ . This model produces the same pairwise correlation $\rho$ while remaining analytically tractable and yields a closed-form correlated aggregation map used throughout the paper.
为进行分析，我们采用一个与这一定义完全一致的具体生成模型。在每个聚合节点处，子节点以概率 $\rho$ 共享同一种“模式”（它们的带符号正确性变量完全相同），并以概率 $1-\rho$ 在给定 $Y$ 的条件下相互独立。该模型产生相同的两两相关性 $\rho$，同时仍便于解析处理，并给出了全文使用的相关聚合映射的闭式表达式。
Continuous: correlated residuals.连续情形：相关残差。
For continuous outputs $X_{i}$ , define residuals $E_{i}:=X_{i}-Y$ . We use the standard equal-correlation model
对于连续输出 $X_{i}$，定义残差 $E_{i}:=X_{i}-Y$。我们采用标准的等相关模型
$$\mathrm{Var}(E_{i})=v,\qquad\mathrm{Cov}(E_{i},E_{j})=\rho v,\quad i\neq j,$$
which makes correlation floors explicit and leads to closed-form recursions in Section 3. The same $\rho$ can be estimated by pairwise residual correlations.
该模型明确体现了相关性导致的下限，并导出了第 3 节中的闭式递推关系。同一个 $\rho$ 可以通过残差的两两相关性来估计。
Why a single parameter is useful.单个参数为何有用。
Real systems may have richer dependence structure than a single coefficient. The intent here is not to model all dependence, but to isolate the dominant failure direction: shared errors. As we show later, this single parameter already shifts phase boundaries and imposes aggregation floors that are otherwise difficult to diagnose.
真实系统的依赖结构可能比单个系数所能描述的更为丰富。这里的意图并非对所有依赖关系建模，而是分离出主要的失效方向：共享错误。正如我们稍后将展示的，仅这一个参数就足以移动相边界，并为聚合设定下限，而这些下限在其他情况下很难诊断。
2.5 Budget and context constraints2.5 预算与上下文约束
Our central comparison is fixed-budget: a multi-agent system should be judged against the best single-agent baseline under the same total cost. We therefore make computation and communication costs explicit.
我们的核心比较基于固定预算：应在总成本相同的条件下，将多智能体系统与最佳单智能体基线进行比较。因此，我们明确计入计算和通信成本。
Budget.预算。
Let $B$ denote the total available budget. In token-based LLM systems, a natural accounting unit is tokens, including both generated tokens and tokens processed as input. Our theory is compatible with other budget units (time, API cost, calls), as long as they are additive and comparable across designs.
令 $B$ 表示可用总预算。在基于词元（token）的 LLM 系统中，一种自然的计量单位是词元，包括生成的词元和作为输入处理的词元。我们的理论也适用于其他预算单位（时间、API 成本、调用次数），只要这些单位具有可加性，且在不同设计之间具有可比性。
We separate two costs:
我们区分两类成本：
Leaf computation: each leaf agent is allocated compute $x$ .
叶节点计算：为每个叶节点智能体分配计算量 $x$。
Communication: each edge transmits a message of length $m$ tokens.
通信：每条边传输一条长度为 $m$ 个词元的消息。
To keep the model simple, we account a per-edge cost proportional to $m$ . A useful approximation in token-based systems is
为保持模型简单，我们将每条边的成本计为与 $m$ 成正比。在基于词元的系统中，一个实用的近似为
$$\text{edge cost}\approx 2m,$$
representing $m$ tokens produced by the sender and $m$ tokens read by the receiver.
表示发送方生成的 $m$ 个词元和接收方读取的 $m$ 个词元。
Context window as a fan-in constraint.将上下文窗口视为扇入约束。
Let $W$ denote the maximum number of tokens that an internal node can read and process. If each incoming message has length $m$ , then any aggregation node can read at most
令 $W$ 表示内部节点能够读取和处理的最大词元数。如果每条传入消息的长度为 $m$，那么任一聚合节点最多能够读取
$$\text{fan-in}\;\leq\;\Big\lfloor\frac{W}{m}\Big\rfloor.$$
This inequality is the reason depth matters: a star is capped by $N\leq\lfloor W/m\rfloor$ leaves, while a tree can scale to $N=b^{L}$ leaves by distributing the fan-in constraint across levels.
这一不等式说明了深度为何重要：星形结构的叶节点数上限为 $N\leq\lfloor W/m\rfloor$，而树形结构通过将扇入约束分散到各层，可扩展至 $N=b^{L}$ 个叶节点。
Topology-specific cost summaries.各类拓扑的成本汇总。

2.6 Topologies and protocols2.6 拓扑与协议
A topology specifies how information flows; a protocol specifies what each node does with the information it receives. We keep protocols deliberately simple to make the effect of constraints and scaling transparent. In particular, we focus on three canonical organizations that recur in practice and isolate the key trade-offs: star, chain, and hierarchical tree (Figure 1).
拓扑规定信息如何流动；协议规定各节点如何处理所接收的信息。我们有意保持协议简单，以清晰呈现约束与规模扩展的影响。具体而言，我们聚焦于实践中反复出现且能凸显关键权衡的三种典型组织形式：星形结构、链式结构和层次树形结构（图 1）。
Star.星形。
Leaves transmit their outputs directly to a central aggregator. Star avoids multi-hop loss, but it concentrates fan-in at one node and therefore saturates at $N\approx W/m$ leaves regardless of budget.
叶节点将其输出直接传送给中央聚合器。星形结构避免了多跳损失，但将扇入集中于单个节点，因此无论预算多少，其叶节点数都会在 $N\approx W/m$ 时达到上限。
Chain.链式。
Outputs are passed sequentially through $L$ steps. Chains avoid fan-in bottlenecks but repeatedly retransmit information; without new evidence injected along the path, each step can only preserve or degrade the signal.
输出依次经过 $L$ 个步骤传递。链式结构避免了扇入瓶颈，但会反复重传信息；如果没有沿路径注入新证据，每一步都只能保持或削弱信号。
Hierarchical tree.层次树结构。
A tree distributes aggregation across many small nodes. Each internal node aggregates $b$ child messages and forwards a summary upward, enforcing $bm\leq W$ locally and enabling many more leaves. The cost is multi-hop communication and repeated aggregation, so performance hinges on whether amplification dominates compounding loss and correlation.
树形结构将聚合工作分散到许多小型节点。每个内部节点聚合 $b$ 条子节点消息，并向上传递摘要，在局部满足 $bm\leq W$，从而支持更多叶节点。其代价是多跳通信和反复聚合，因此性能取决于放大作用能否压过逐步累积的损失与相关性。

These motifs capture the dominant structural trade-offs induced by finite context and imperfect coordination. Star minimizes depth but is capped by a global fan-in constraint $Nm\leq W$ . Chain removes fan-in limits but maximizes the number of lossy transmissions. Trees are the simplest family that bypasses the fan-in cap ( $bm\leq W$ locally) while still aggregating many signals; this is precisely the setting in which phase transitions and budget thresholds emerge.
这些基本结构涵盖了有限上下文与不完善协调所引发的主要结构性权衡。星形结构使深度最小，但受到全局扇入约束 $Nm\leq W$ 的限制。链式结构消除了扇入限制，但使有损传输次数达到最大。树形结构是既能绕过扇入上限（局部满足 $bm\leq W$）又能聚合大量信号的最简单结构族；相变和预算阈值恰恰会在这种情形下出现。

3 Continuous Warm-up: Averaging Under Correlation and Communication Noise3 连续情形预备分析：相关性与通信噪声下的平均聚合
We begin with a continuous-performance setting in which agents estimate a scalar target $Y\in[0,1]$ and performance is measured by mean squared error (MSE). This warm-up makes the basic organization trade-offs transparent in closed form: averaging reduces independent noise, shared errors impose floors, and communication loss introduces additional distortion. Throughout we adopt the framework of Section 2 and use averaging at internal nodes, which is the natural choice under squared loss and symmetric noise assumptions.
我们首先考虑一种性能连续的设置，其中智能体估计标量目标 $Y\in[0,1]$，并以均方误差（MSE）衡量性能。这一预备分析以闭式形式清晰呈现了组织结构的基本权衡：平均聚合减少独立噪声，共享误差形成误差下限，而通信损失引入额外失真。我们始终采用第 2 节的框架，并在内部节点处使用平均聚合；在平方损失和对称噪声假设下，这是自然的选择。
Although we phrase the warm-up as each leaf producing an estimate of the same latent $Y$ , it should be read as an abstraction of role-specialized pipelines. A child agent may solve a subtask and transmit an $m$ -token message; $X_{i}$ denotes the effective scalar contribution about the downstream target that the parent can decode from that message. Role heterogeneity then enters through the effective parameters ( $\rho$ and $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ or $\gamma(m)$ ); we return to richer semantics and extensions in Section 7.
尽管我们在预备分析中将每个叶节点描述为对同一个潜在目标 $Y$ 生成估计，但应将其理解为对角色专门化流水线的抽象。子智能体可以求解一个子任务并传输一条包含 $m$ 个词元的消息；$X_{i}$ 表示父节点能够从该消息中解码出的、关于下游目标的有效标量贡献。角色异质性由此通过有效参数（$\rho$ 以及 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ 或 $\gamma(m)$）体现；我们将在第 7 节重新探讨更丰富的语义及扩展。
3.1 Model assumptions for the continuous setting3.1 连续情形的模型假设
This warm-up instantiates the continuous-task primitive from Section 2 using the communication and shared-failure models already defined in Sections 2.3 and 2.4. The only additional assumptions are that leaf estimates are (approximately) unbiased and admit a compute-dependent MSE description.
这一预备分析利用第 2.3 节和第 2.4 节已定义的通信模型与共享失败模型，对第 2 节中的连续任务基本模型进行具体化。仅有的额外假设是：叶节点的估计（近似）无偏，并且可以用依赖于计算量的 MSE 来描述。
Let $Y\in[0,1]$ denote the latent target and let each leaf output an estimate $X\in[0,1]$ . To keep the warm-up focused on organization rather than estimator bias, we assume conditional unbiasedness,
令 $Y\in[0,1]$ 表示潜在目标，并令每个叶节点输出一个估计 $X\in[0,1]$。为使预备分析聚焦于组织结构而非估计器偏差，我们假设条件无偏性，
$$\mathbb{E}[X\mid Y]=Y.$$
Write the residual $E:=X-Y$ , so $\mathbb{E}[E\mid Y]=0$ , and summarize leaf quality by the MSE
将残差记为 $E:=X-Y$，因此 $\mathbb{E}[E\mid Y]=0$，并用 MSE 概括叶节点的质量
$$v_{0}(x):=\mathbb{E}\big[(X-Y)^{2}\big]=\mathbb{E}[E^{2}],$$
which depends on the per-leaf compute allocation $x$ . In the operating range where we compare scale up versus scale out, we use the scaling approximation
它取决于每个叶节点获得的计算量 $x$。在比较纵向扩展与横向扩展的运行区间内，我们采用如下标度近似
$$v_{0}(x)\approx c\,x^{-\beta},$$
with constants $c>0$ and $\beta>0$ (Section 2.2).
其中常数 $c>0$ 且 $\beta>0$（第 2.2 节）。
Communication follows the additive distortion model from Section 2.3, with message length $m$ ; we treat edge distortions as independent across edges and independent of $Y$ .
通信遵循第 2.3 节中的加性失真模型，消息长度为 $m$；我们将各条边上的失真视为彼此独立，且独立于 $Y$。
Within any aggregation group, residuals are modeled by the equal-correlation structure from Section 2.4: for children $i\neq j$ ,
在任意聚合组内，使用第 2.4 节中的等相关结构对残差建模：对于子节点 $i\neq j$，
$$\mathrm{Var}(E_{i})=v,\qquad\mathrm{Cov}(E_{i},E_{j})=\rho v,$$
where $\rho\in[0,1)$ is the shared-failure correlation parameter.
其中，$\rho\in[0,1)$ 是共享失败的相关性参数。
Finally, an internal node with fan-in $b$ outputs the average of its received inputs,
最后，入度为 $b$ 的内部节点输出其所接收输入的平均值，
$$\widehat{Y}=\frac{1}{b}\sum_{i=1}^{b}\widetilde{X}_{i}.$$
Under these symmetry assumptions, averaging is the MSE-optimal linear unbiased aggregator.
在这些对称性假设下，平均聚合是使 MSE 最小的线性无偏聚合器。
3.2 Closed-form risk for star, chain, and tree3.2 星形、链式和树形结构的闭式风险
We now derive exact MSE expressions (or recursions) for the three canonical topologies. These formulas already reveal three key messages: star saturates under context constraints, chains compound loss, and trees trade scale for multi-hop noise.
现在，我们为这三种典型拓扑推导精确的 MSE 表达式（或递推式）。这些公式已经揭示了三个关键结论：星形结构在上下文约束下趋于饱和，链式结构使损失累积，而树形结构在规模与多跳噪声之间进行权衡。
Star.星形。
Consider a star with $N$ leaves feeding a single aggregator. Each leaf sends a length- $m$ message, so the aggregator is feasible only if $Nm\leq W$ . Let $v_{0}$ denote the leaf MSE (for a given compute allocation $x$ ) and let $\rho$ denote the pairwise residual correlation within this aggregation group. After one hop, each received estimate has residual $\widetilde{E}_{i}=(X_{i}-Y)+\eta_{i}$ , with
考虑一种星形结构，其中 $N$ 个叶节点向单个聚合器提供输入。每个叶节点发送一条长度为 $m$ 的消息，因此仅当 $Nm\leq W$ 时，该聚合器才是可行的。令 $v_{0}$ 表示叶节点的均方误差（MSE）（对于给定的计算资源分配 $x$），并令 $\rho$ 表示该聚合组内残差的两两相关系数。经过一跳后，每个接收到的估计值的残差为 $\widetilde{E}_{i}=(X_{i}-Y)+\eta_{i}$，其中
$$\mathrm{Var}(\widetilde{E}_{i})=v_{0}+\sigma_{c}^{2}(m),\qquad\mathrm{Cov}(\widetilde{E}_{i},\widetilde{E}_{j})=\rho v_{0}.$$
The star output is the average of the received values, so the MSE is
星型结构的输出是接收值的平均值，因此其 MSE 为
$$v_{\text{star}}(N)=\mathbb{E}\big[(\widehat{Y}-Y)^{2}\big]=\frac{v_{0}\big(1+(N-1)\rho\big)}{N}+\frac{\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)}{N}.$$
If $N$ could grow arbitrarily, the channel noise term $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)/N$ would vanish. However, correlation produces an irreducible floor:
如果 $N$ 可以任意增大，信道噪声项 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)/N$ 就会趋于零。然而，相关性会产生一个不可消除的下限：
$$v_{\text{star}}(N)\ \to\ \rho v_{0}\qquad\text{as }N\to\infty\text{ (for fixed }v_{0}\text{)}.$$
Thus, even perfect communication cannot eliminate a globally shared residual component. In practice, the context constraint $Nm\leq W$ prevents $N$ from growing with budget once $m$ is fixed, which is why star organizations often stop improving beyond a modest scale.
因此，即使通信完全无损，也无法消除全局共享的残差分量。在实践中，一旦 $m$ 固定，上下文约束 $Nm\leq W$ 就会阻止 $N$ 随预算增加而增大，这就是星型组织通常在达到不大的规模后便不再改善的原因。
Chain.链式。
Consider a chain of length $L$ , where an estimate is handed off sequentially through $L$ communication steps, each of length $m$ , without introducing new independent evidence. Starting from a leaf estimate with MSE $v_{0}$ , each hop adds independent distortion variance $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ . Thus,
考虑一条长度为 $L$ 的链，其中一个估计值依次经过 $L$ 个通信步骤传递，每个步骤的消息长度均为 $m$，且不引入新的独立证据。从 MSE 为 $v_{0}$ 的叶节点估计值出发，每一跳都会增加独立的失真方差 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$。因此，
$$v_{\text{chain}}(L)=v_{0}+L\,\sigma_{c}^{2}(m).$$
Equation (14) isolates the unavoidable cost of multi-hop relay communication: if intermediate nodes primarily re-encode the same information (no new independent evidence), each hop injects distortion and MSE grows linearly in $L$ . Pipeline-style chains can still be beneficial when each stage performs additional computation or incorporates new information; in that case, the estimation error may decrease across stages, but the same $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ term captures a communication bottleneck that accumulates with depth and must be outweighed by per-stage gains.
公式 (14) 单独揭示了多跳中继通信不可避免的代价：如果中间节点主要对相同的信息重新编码（没有新的独立证据），那么每一跳都会引入失真，MSE 随 $L$ 线性增长。当每个阶段执行额外计算或引入新信息时，流水线式链结构仍然可能有益；在这种情况下，估计误差可能逐阶段减小，但同一个 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ 项刻画了随深度累积的通信瓶颈，各阶段的增益必须超过这一代价。
Hierarchical tree.层次树结构。
Consider a full $b$ -ary tree with depth $L$ , where each internal node averages its $b$ children. Let $v_{t}$ denote the MSE of a node output at level $t$ (leaves are $t=0$ ; the root is $t=L$ ). We assume that at each aggregation node, the $b$ child residuals satisfy the equal-correlation model (11) with coefficient $\rho$ , and that channel noise on incoming edges is independent.
考虑一棵深度为 $L$ 的满 $b$ 叉树，其中每个内部节点都对其 $b$ 个子节点的输出取平均。令 $v_{t}$ 表示第 $t$ 层节点输出的 MSE（叶节点位于 $t=0$；根节点位于 $t=L$）。我们假设，在每个聚合节点处，$b$ 个子节点的残差满足系数为 $\rho$ 的等相关模型 (11)，且各条入边上的信道噪声相互独立。
A parent receives $\widetilde{X}_{i}=X_{i}+\eta_{i}$ from each child, so each received residual has variance $v_{t}+\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ , while covariances remain $\rho v_{t}$ because the channel noises are independent. Averaging (12) yields the recursion
父节点从每个子节点接收到 $\widetilde{X}_{i}=X_{i}+\eta_{i}$，因此每个接收值的残差方差为 $v_{t}+\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$，而由于信道噪声相互独立，协方差仍为 $\rho v_{t}$。根据 (12) 取平均可得递推关系
$$v_{t+1}=a\,v_{t}+\frac{\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)}{b},\qquad a:=\frac{1+(b-1)\rho}{b}=\rho+\frac{1-\rho}{b}.$$
Unrolling gives the closed form
展开递推可得闭式表达式
$$v_{L}=a^{L}v_{0}+\frac{\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)}{b}\cdot\frac{1-a^{L}}{1-a}.$$
The coefficient $a\in(0,1)$ when $\rho<1$ , so the first term $a^{L}v_{0}$ shrinks exponentially in depth. This is the statistical benefit of hierarchy: averaging repeatedly reduces the variance of what is being passed upward. The second term is the cost: each level injects fresh communication distortion, which accumulates into an error floor described next.
当 $\rho<1$ 时，系数 $a\in(0,1)$，因此第一项 $a^{L}v_{0}$ 随深度呈指数衰减。这就是层次结构在统计上的益处：反复取平均会降低向上传递的量的方差。第二项则是代价：每一层都会引入新的通信失真，这些失真累积形成下文所述的误差下限。
3.3 Saturation floors and mixing depth3.3 饱和下限与混合深度
Equation (16) exposes a sharp and practical phenomenon: deeper trees do not improve indefinitely. Even if leaves become arbitrarily accurate, repeated communication loss produces a floor. Figure 4 illustrates the typical behavior: $v_{L}$ shrinks rapidly with depth at first (roughly at rate $a^{L}$ ) and then saturates at the floor $v^{\star}$ determined by $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ , $b$ , and $\rho$ .
公式 (16) 揭示了一个鲜明且具有实际意义的现象：树的深度增加并不会带来无限的改善。即使叶节点的估计可以任意精确，反复发生的通信损失仍会产生一个下限。图 4 展示了典型行为：$v_{L}$ 最初随深度迅速减小（大致按 $a^{L}$ 的速率衰减），随后在由 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$、$b$ 和 $\rho$ 决定的下限 $v^{\star}$ 处达到饱和。
Tree floor.树结构的误差下限。
For $\rho<1$ we have $a<1$ , and therefore $a^{L}\to 0$ as $L\to\infty$ . Taking limits in (16) yields
对于 $\rho<1$，有 $a<1$，因此当 $L\to\infty$ 时，$a^{L}\to 0$。对 (16) 取极限可得
$$v^{\star}(b,m,\rho):=\lim_{L\to\infty}v_{L}=\frac{\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)}{b(1-a)}=\frac{\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)}{(b-1)(1-\rho)}.$$
This expression cleanly separates the levers available to a designer:
该表达式清晰地区分了设计者可以调节的各个因素：
Increasing message length $m$ decreases $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ , lowering the floor.
增加消息长度 $m$ 会减小 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$，从而降低误差下限。
Increasing fan-in $b$ lowers the floor approximately as $1/(b-1)$ , but $b$ is limited by $bm\leq W$ .
增大扇入数 $b$ 会使误差下限大致按 $1/(b-1)$ 降低，但 $b$ 受到 $bm\leq W$ 的限制。
Reducing shared correlation $\rho$ lowers the floor and also accelerates convergence (since $a$ decreases).
降低共享相关系数 $\rho$ 会降低误差下限，并加快收敛（因为 $a$ 会减小）。
Mixing depth.混合深度。
The recursion (15) is linear, so convergence to the floor is explicit:
递推关系 (15) 是线性的，因此可以显式表示向误差下限的收敛过程：
$$v_{L}-v^{\star}=a^{L}\,(v_{0}-v^{\star}).$$
Thus, to achieve a relative tolerance $v_{L}\leq(1+\varepsilon)v^{\star}$ when $v_{0}>v^{\star}$ , it suffices to choose
因此，当 $v_{0}>v^{\star}$ 时，要满足相对容差要求 $v_{L}\leq(1+\varepsilon)v^{\star}$，只需选取
$$L\ \geq\ \left\lceil\frac{\log\big((v_{0}-v^{\star})/(\varepsilon v^{\star})\big)}{\log(1/a)}\right\rceil.$$
Equation (19) is the continuous analogue of the “mixing depth” results we prove for binary trees later. It is also a direct design rule: beyond a certain depth, additional leaves yield negligible improvement unless the designer reduces the floor by improving communication or reducing correlation.
公式 (19) 是我们稍后为二叉树证明的“混合深度”结果的连续形式。这也是一条直接的设计准则：超过某一深度后，增加叶节点带来的改善微乎其微，除非设计者通过改善通信或降低相关性来降低误差下限。
A note on when deeper is worse.关于何时更深反而更差的说明。
If leaves are already more accurate than the floor (i.e., $v_{0}<v^{\star}$ ), then (16) implies that increasing depth moves error upward toward $v^{\star}$ . In that regime, deeper hierarchies are counterproductive unless they come with reduced $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ or reduced $\rho$ . This observation will reappear in the binary setting as a saturation effect: once the system has reached its fixed point, additional scale should be spent on changing the communication or correlation regime rather than adding depth.
如果叶节点的准确度已经高于该误差下限所对应的水平（即 $v_{0}<v^{\star}$ ），那么 (16) 意味着，增加深度会使误差上升并趋近 $v^{\star}$ 。在这种情形下，更深的层级结构会适得其反，除非同时降低 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ 或 $\rho$ 。这一观察将在二元情形中以饱和效应的形式再次出现：一旦系统达到其不动点，进一步扩大规模时，就应将资源用于改变通信或相关性状况，而非增加深度。

Longer messages reduce $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ and therefore lower the attainable floor.

3.4 Scale-out versus scale-up under a fixed budget3.4 固定预算下的横向扩展与纵向扩展
The closed forms above become design tools once we connect them to budget. We use the budget model from Section 2.5, where each leaf receives compute $x$ and each edge uses message length $m$ . For a full $b$ -ary tree of depth $L$ with $N=b^{L}$ leaves, the total cost is approximately
一旦将上述闭式表达式与预算联系起来，它们就能成为设计工具。我们采用第 2.5 节的预算模型，其中每个叶节点分配到的计算资源为 $x$ ，每条边使用的消息长度为 $m$ 。对于一棵深度为 $L$ 、具有 $N=b^{L}$ 个叶节点的满 $b$ 叉树，总成本近似为
$$B\;\approx\;N\big(x+c_{0}(b,m)\big),\qquad c_{0}(b,m)=2m\frac{b}{b-1},$$
so the number of leaves is $N\approx B/(x+c_{0})$ , and depth is $L\approx\log_{b}N$ .
因此，叶节点数量为 $N\approx B/(x+c_{0})$ ，深度为 $L\approx\log_{b}N$ 。
A growth-regime approximation.增长阶段的近似。
When the tree has not yet hit the communication floor, the dominant term in (16) is the shrinking term $a^{L}v_{0}(x)$ . Using $N=b^{L}$ and $a^{L}=N^{-t}$ with
当树尚未达到由通信决定的误差下限时，(16) 中的主导项是衰减项 $a^{L}v_{0}(x)$ 。利用 $N=b^{L}$ 和 $a^{L}=N^{-t}$ ，其中
$$t(b,\rho):=-\frac{\log a}{\log b}=-\frac{\log\big(\rho+(1-\rho)/b\big)}{\log b},$$
we obtain the approximation
我们得到如下近似
$$v_{L}\;\approx\;v_{0}(x)\,N^{-t(b,\rho)}.$$
The exponent $t\in[0,1]$ quantifies how efficiently scaling out reduces error in the continuous setting. If $\rho=0$ , then $a=1/b$ and $t=1$ , recovering the familiar $1/N$ averaging benefit. As $\rho$ increases, $a$ approaches 1 and $t$ approaches 0, reflecting that shared residuals diminish the value of adding more agents.
指数 $t\in[0,1]$ 量化了在连续值情形下，横向扩展降低误差的效率。如果 $\rho=0$ ，则 $a=1/b$ 且 $t=1$ ，从而恢复了熟知的 $1/N$ 平均化收益。随着 $\rho$ 增大，$a$ 趋近于 1，$t$ 趋近于 0，这反映出共享残差会削弱增加智能体数量的价值。
Compute allocation and a simple threshold.计算资源分配与一个简单阈值。
Substituting $v_{0}(x)\approx cx^{-\beta}$ and $N\approx B/(x+c_{0})$ into (21) yields
将 $v_{0}(x)\approx cx^{-\beta}$ 和 $N\approx B/(x+c_{0})$ 代入 (21)，得到
$$v_{L}\;\approx\;c\,x^{-\beta}\left(\frac{B}{x+c_{0}}\right)^{-t}=c\,B^{-t}\,x^{-\beta}(x+c_{0})^{t}.$$
For a fixed budget $B$ , minimizing this expression over $x$ reduces to minimizing $x^{-\beta}(x+c_{0})^{t}$ . A short calculus argument shows a qualitative threshold:
对于固定预算 $B$ ，关于 $x$ 最小化这一表达式，可归结为最小化 $x^{-\beta}(x+c_{0})^{t}$ 。通过简短的微积分推导，可以得到一个定性阈值：
If $t>\beta$ , there is a unique interior optimum
如果 $t>\beta$ ，则存在唯一的内部最优解
$$x^{\star}\;=\;\frac{\beta}{t-\beta}\,c_{0}(b,m).$$
In this regime, scale-out and scale-up should be balanced: make agents strong enough that their improvement exponent $\beta$ is not wasted, but keep them weak enough that adding more of them leverages the organization exponent $t$ .
在这种情形下，应平衡横向扩展与纵向扩展：使智能体足够强，以充分利用其改进指数 $\beta$ ，同时将其能力控制在适当水平，使增加智能体数量能够发挥组织指数 $t$ 的作用。
If $t\leq\beta$ , the objective decreases as $x$ increases, suggesting that under the growth approximation it is better to spend budget on fewer stronger agents rather than more weaker ones. Correlation and context constraints tend to push systems into this regime by reducing $t$ (via higher $\rho$ ) or restricting $b$ , which increases communication overhead $c_{0}(b,m)$ and raises the floor $v^{\star}(b,m,\rho)$ , shortening the growth regime.
如果 $t\leq\beta$ ，则目标函数随 $x$ 增大而减小，这表明在增长近似下，将预算用于更少但更强的智能体，优于用于更多但更弱的智能体。相关性和上下文约束往往会使系统进入这种情形：前者通过更高的 $\rho$ 降低 $t$ ，后者则限制 $b$ ，从而增加通信开销 $c_{0}(b,m)$ 并抬高误差下限 $v^{\star}(b,m,\rho)$ ，缩短增长阶段。
This is the continuous analogue of our main binary condition $s>\beta$ developed later: synergy from scaling out appears only when the organization exponent exceeds the single-agent scaling exponent.
这是我们随后提出的二元情形主要条件 $s>\beta$ 在连续值情形下的对应形式：只有当组织指数超过单智能体扩展指数时，横向扩展才会产生协同效应。
How the floor changes the budget story.误差下限如何改变预算分配的逻辑。
The growth approximation (21) cannot hold indefinitely because of the communication floor $v^{\star}$ . Once the predicted $v_{L}$ approaches $v^{\star}(b,m,\rho)$ , additional budget spent on more leaves or more depth yields diminishing returns. At that point, the effective levers are no longer $N$ and $L$ , but $m$ and diversity (which affect $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ and $\rho$ ). This conclusion will reappear in the binary setting through fixed-point saturation and mixing depth.
由于存在由通信决定的误差下限 $v^{\star}$ ，增长近似 (21) 不可能一直成立。一旦预测的 $v_{L}$ 接近 $v^{\star}(b,m,\rho)$ ，将额外预算用于增加叶节点数量或深度所带来的收益就会递减。此时，有效的调节手段不再是 $N$ 和 $L$ ，而是 $m$ 和多样性（它们影响 $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ 和 $\rho$ ）。这一结论将在二元情形中通过不动点饱和与混合深度再次体现。
3.5 Design implications and a bridge to the binary analysis3.5 设计启示及与二元分析的衔接
The continuous warm-up already exposes the structural trade-offs we will reuse in the binary theory. Absent a binding context constraint, star aggregation is information-dominant because it avoids multi-hop loss; trees are useful primarily as a way to bypass the fan-in limit $Nm\leq W$ by enforcing $bm\leq W$ locally, at the cost of repeated communication. Both correlation and communication loss induce explicit performance floors, so scale-out can stall early when $\rho$ is large or messages are too short. In the growth regime under a fixed budget, the key comparison is exponent versus exponent: whether the organization exponent beats the single-agent scaling exponent.
作为铺垫的连续值分析已经揭示了我们将在二元理论中再次用到的结构性权衡。在上下文约束不构成实际限制时，星形聚合在信息保留方面占优，因为它避免了多跳损失；树形结构的主要用途是通过在局部满足 $bm\leq W$ ，绕过扇入限制 $Nm\leq W$ ，代价则是重复通信。相关性与通信损失都会导致明确的性能下限，因此，当 $\rho$ 较大或消息过短时，横向扩展可能过早停滞。在固定预算下的增长阶段，关键在于指数之间的比较：组织指数是否超过单智能体扩展指数。
The binary case is qualitatively different. Because majority vote is nonlinear, it can fundamentally change how errors propagate under repeated aggregation. Section 4 analyzes this effect and shows that hierarchical organization exhibits a sharp amplification–collapse transition on success/failure tasks.
二元情形在性质上有所不同。由于多数投票是非线性的，它能够从根本上改变重复聚合过程中误差的传播方式。第 4 节分析了这一效应，并表明层级组织在以成功或失败判定结果的任务上表现出突变式的放大—崩溃转变。
4 Binary Core: Majority Aggregation and Phase Transitions4 二元核心：多数聚合与相变
We begin by characterizing how one majority layer transforms weak bias, then incorporate correlated errors and lossy communication to obtain a phase transition and budget-relevant exponents for deep trees.
我们首先刻画单个多数表决层如何变换微弱偏置，然后纳入相关误差和有损通信，从而得到深层树的相变以及与预算相关的指数。
4.1 Majority maps and correlated aggregation4.1 多数映射与相关聚合
The central object in the binary analysis is the one-step aggregation map that describes how an internal node transforms the biases of its child votes into an output bias.
二元分析的核心对象是单步聚合映射，它描述内部节点如何将其子节点投票的偏置变换为输出偏置。
Majority aggregation map.多数聚合映射。
Let $b\geq 3$ be an odd integer and let $V_{1},\dots,V_{b}\in\{-1,+1\}$ be i.i.d. child votes with
设 $b\geq 3$ 为奇整数，且 $V_{1},\dots,V_{b}\in\{-1,+1\}$ 为独立同分布的子节点投票，满足
$$\mathbb{E}[V_{i}Y]=u\in[-1,1].$$
Equivalently, $V_{i}$ is correct with probability $p=(1+u)/2$ . Define the majority vote
等价地，$V_{i}$ 正确的概率为 $p=(1+u)/2$。定义多数表决
$$\mathrm{Maj}(V_{1},\dots,V_{b}):=\mathrm{sign}\!\Big(\sum_{i=1}^{b}V_{i}\Big)\in\{-1,+1\},$$
where ties are impossible because $b$ is odd; even $b$ can be handled by specifying a tie-breaking rule (e.g., random) with only minor notational changes. For the same reason, we will assume an odd $b$ in our future sections. The induced majority map is the bias at the output:
其中，由于 $b$ 为奇数，不会出现平票；对于偶数 $b$，只需指定一种平票处理规则（例如随机决定），并对记号稍作修改即可。出于同样的原因，后续各节均假设 $b$ 为奇数。所诱导的多数映射为输出端的偏置：
$$f_{b}(u)\;:=\;\mathbb{E}\big[\mathrm{Maj}(V_{1},\dots,V_{b})\,Y\big]=2\,\Pr\!\Big(\mathrm{Bin}(b,p)\geq\tfrac{b+1}{2}\Big)-1,\qquad p=\frac{1+u}{2}.$$
The function $f_{b}$ summarizes the effect of one noiseless majority aggregation step on the bias.
函数 $f_{b}$ 概括了单次无噪声多数聚合对偏置的影响。
Lemma 1 (Basic properties of the majority map).引理 1（多数映射的基本性质）。
For odd $b\geq 3$ , the map $f_{b}:[-1,1]\to[-1,1]$ satisfies:
对于奇数 $b\geq 3$，映射 $f_{b}:[-1,1]\to[-1,1]$ 满足：
$f_{b}$ is odd and increasing, with $f_{b}(0)=0$ and $f_{b}(1)=1$ .
$f_{b}$ 为奇函数且单调递增，并且 $f_{b}(0)=0$、$f_{b}(1)=1$。
$f_{b}$ is concave on $[0,1]$ .
$f_{b}$ 在 $[0,1]$ 上为凹函数。
The derivative at the origin is
原点处的导数为
$$f_{b}^{\prime}(0)=\frac{b\binom{b-1}{(b-1)/2}}{2^{b-1}}.$$
Lemma 1 is standard for majority maps; we provide a self-contained proof in Appendix A. The quantity $f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$ is especially important. It is the linear gain of the majority when the inputs are only slightly better than chance. As $b$ increases, $f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$ grows sublinearly (in fact on the order of $\sqrt{b}$ ), so increasing fan-in yields diminishing marginal amplification. Figure 5 visualizes $f_{b}(u)$ for several fan-ins, highlighting its concavity and the increasing (but sublinear) small-signal gain $f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$ .
引理 1 是关于多数映射的标准结论；我们在附录 A 中给出了自包含的证明，其中量 $f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$ 尤为重要。它表示输入的正确率仅略高于随机猜测时，多数表决的线性增益。随着 $b$ 增大，$f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$ 呈次线性增长（实际上为 $\sqrt{b}$ 量级），因此，增加扇入数所带来的边际放大效应逐渐减弱。图 5 展示了几种扇入数下的 $f_{b}(u)$，突出显示了其凹性以及随扇入数增大而增长（但呈次线性）的小信号增益 $f_{b}^{\prime}(0)$。

The diagonal $f(u)=u$ is shown for reference. A tractable shared-failure model.一种易于分析的共同失效模型。
To capture groupthink, we need a model in which child votes are positively correlated. We use the following local generative model, which matches a measurable correlation coefficient and yields a closed-form aggregation rule.
为刻画群体思维，我们需要一种子节点投票之间具有正相关性的模型。我们采用以下局部生成模型，该模型与可测量的相关系数相匹配，并可导出闭式聚合规则。
Definition 2 ( $\rho$ -shared correlation model).定义 2（$\rho$ 共享相关模型）。
Fix a target bias $u\in[-1,1]$ and correlation strength $\rho\in[0,1)$ . Conditional on $Y$ , the child votes $V_{1},\dots,V_{b}\in\{-1,+1\}$ are generated as follows: with probability $\rho$ , all children share a common vote $V_{1}=\cdots=V_{b}=:V$ where $\mathbb{E}[VY]=u$ ; with probability $1-\rho$ , the votes are i.i.d. with $\mathbb{E}[V_{i}Y]=u$ .
固定目标偏置 $u\in[-1,1]$ 和相关强度 $\rho\in[0,1)$。给定 $Y$，子节点投票 $V_{1},\dots,V_{b}\in\{-1,+1\}$ 按如下方式生成：以概率 $\rho$，所有子节点共享同一个投票 $V_{1}=\cdots=V_{b}=:V$，其中 $\mathbb{E}[VY]=u$；以概率 $1-\rho$，各投票独立同分布，且 $\mathbb{E}[V_{i}Y]=u$。
This model has two practical advantages. First, $\rho$ equals the pairwise correlation of signed correctness. Let $S_{i}:=V_{i}Y\in\{-1,+1\}$ . Then $\mathrm{Corr}(S_{i},S_{j})=\rho$ for any $i\neq j$ , so $\rho$ can be estimated from logs as described in Section 2.4. Second, the induced aggregation map is explicit. Figure 6 provides an intuition for the mixture: with probability $\rho$ the children act as a single shared voter (groupthink), and with probability $1-\rho$ they behave as independent voters.
该模型有两个实际优势。首先，$\rho$ 等于带符号正确性变量之间的两两相关系数。令 $S_{i}:=V_{i}Y\in\{-1,+1\}$。则对于任意 $i\neq j$，都有 $\mathrm{Corr}(S_{i},S_{j})=\rho$，因此可以按照第 2.4 节所述的方法，从日志中估计 $\rho$。其次，所诱导的聚合映射具有显式表达式。图 6 直观展示了这种混合机制：以概率 $\rho$，子节点如同一个共享的投票者那样行动（群体思维）；以概率 $1-\rho$，它们则表现为相互独立的投票者。
Lemma 3 (Correlated majority map under the $\rho$ -shared model).引理 3（$\rho$ 共享模型下的相关多数映射）。
Under Definition 2, the output bias of majority aggregation is
在定义 2 下，多数聚合的输出偏置为
$$f_{b,\rho}(u)\;:=\;\mathbb{E}\big[\mathrm{Maj}(V_{1},\dots,V_{b})\,Y\big]=\rho\,u+(1-\rho)\,f_{b}(u).$$
In particular, $f_{b,\rho}$ is increasing and concave on $[0,1]$ , and
特别地，$f_{b,\rho}$ 在 $[0,1]$ 上单调递增且为凹函数，并且
$$f_{b,\rho}^{\prime}(0)=\rho+(1-\rho)f_{b}^{\prime}(0).$$
Lemma 3 is immediate from the mixture construction: in the shared mode, majority returns the same vote (bias $u$ ); in the independent mode, it returns $f_{b}(u)$ . The key design message is already visible in (27): increasing $\rho$ moves $f_{b,\rho}$ closer to the identity map, weakening amplification and making deep organization harder.

4.2 Phase transition in deep trees: $\alpha_{\rho}\gtrless 1$4.2 深层树中的相变：$\alpha_{\rho}\gtrless 1$
We now incorporate communication and study deep trees. Consider a homogeneous $b$ -ary tree in which every internal node aggregates $b$ child votes by majority, and every edge transmits a message of length $m$ tokens. Communication is modeled as an effective bit channel with reliability $\gamma(m)\in(0,1]$ (Section 2.3): if a child vote has bias $\mu$ , then the parent receives a vote with bias $\gamma(m)\mu$ .
现在，我们将通信纳入考虑，并研究深层树。考虑一棵同质的 $b$ 叉树，其中每个内部节点通过多数表决聚合 $b$ 个子节点的投票，每条边传输一条长度为 $m$ 个词元的消息。通信被建模为可靠性为 $\gamma(m)\in(0,1]$ 的有效比特信道（第 2.3 节）：若子节点投票的偏差为 $\mu$，则父节点接收到的投票的偏差为 $\gamma(m)\mu$。

Let $\mu_{t}$ denote the bias of a node at depth $t$ from the leaves (so leaves are $t=0$ ). After one hop, each child vote’s marginal bias is attenuated to $\gamma(m)\mu_{t}$ . We model the $b$ received votes entering each majority gate by the $\rho$ -shared model (Definition 2) with this marginal bias and within-group correlation $\rho$ . (Any additional dependence induced by shared message templates or decoding is absorbed into the effective $\rho$ .) Under this abstraction, the bias evolves as

We interpret $\alpha_{\rho}$ as the effective per-layer gain on weak signal. If leaf votes are only slightly better than random, then one layer of aggregation and communication multiplies their bias by approximately $\alpha_{\rho}$ . This single scalar already suggests a threshold: if $\alpha_{\rho}<1$ , weak signal shrinks from layer to layer; if $\alpha_{\rho}>1$ , it grows.
我们将 $\alpha_{\rho}$ 解释为弱信号的每层有效增益。如果叶节点投票仅略优于随机猜测，那么一层聚合与通信会使其偏差约乘以 $\alpha_{\rho}$。仅这一标量就已表明存在一个阈值：若 $\alpha_{\rho}<1$，弱信号会逐层衰减；若 $\alpha_{\rho}>1$，弱信号则会增强。
The next theorem shows that this intuition is exact at the level of global dynamics: $\alpha_{\rho}$ determines whether deep trees amplify accuracy or collapse to chance.

Theorem 4 (Phase transition for deep majority trees).定理 4（深层多数表决树的相变）。

We emphasize that $\alpha_{\rho}$ is estimator-dependent (majority rather than Bayes-optimal) and explicitly incorporates shared failures through $\rho$ .

What the threshold means in practice.该阈值在实践中的含义。
Theorem 4 clarifies why adding hierarchical structure is risky. In a supercritical regime, a tree can turn many weak votes into a strong decision and is robust to depth until it saturates. In a subcritical regime, depth destroys information: the system becomes less reliable as it grows. Both correlation ( $\rho$ ) and short messages (small $\gamma(m)$ ) push $\alpha_{\rho}$ downward, making collapse more likely. This matches the practitioner experience that groupthink and aggressive compression are the two most common reasons multi-agent scaling fails.

Bias is bounded by 1, and communication loss and correlation prevent arbitrarily deep trees from achieving perfect accuracy unless the channel is lossless. Theorem 4 already tells us that in the supercritical regime the recursion converges to a fixed point $\mu^{\star}$ . The results above suggest a simple approximation that is both interpretable and safe: use the small-signal prediction $\mu_{0}\alpha_{\rho}^{L}$ when it is below the saturation point, and clip it at $\mu^{\star}$ otherwise:

$$\widehat{\mu}_{L}:=\min\big\{\mu^{\star},\ \mu_{0}\,\alpha_{\rho}^{L}\big\}.$$
The clipped objective captures the two dominant regimes with a single expression. In the common small-signal regime $\mu_{0}\leq\mu^{\star}$ , concavity yields the global upper bound $\mu_{L}\leq\widehat{\mu}_{L}$ , while Theorem 5 guarantees that $\mu_{L}$ tracks the growth term when $\widehat{\mu}_{L}=\mu_{0}\alpha_{\rho}^{L}$ . Design takeaway.设计启示。
Equation (35) makes a practical point precise. In the supercritical regime, adding depth and leaves helps only until the system approaches $\mu^{\star}$ . Beyond that point, additional budget should be spent on changing $\mu^{\star}$ itself, which requires improving communication fidelity (increasing $\gamma(m)$ by using longer messages) or reducing shared-error correlation $\rho$ (increasing diversity or adding verification). In the subcritical regime, adding depth is actively harmful; the only viable route to scale-out is to move the system across the threshold $\alpha_{\rho}>1$ by improving $m$ , reducing $\rho$ , or increasing fan-in $b$ within the context constraint.
式 (35) 精确阐明了一个实践要点。在超临界区域中，增加深度和叶节点只有在系统接近 $\mu^{\star}$ 之前才有帮助。超过这一点后，应将额外预算用于改变 $\mu^{\star}$ 本身，这需要提高通信保真度（通过使用更长的消息来增大 $\gamma(m)$），或降低共享误差相关性 $\rho$（增加多样性或加入验证）。在次临界区域中，增加深度反而有害；实现横向扩展的唯一可行途径，是通过改进 $m$、降低 $\rho$，或在上下文约束内增大扇入数 $b$，使系统跨越阈值 $\alpha_{\rho}>1$。

4.5.1 Star as an upper bound without constraints4.5.1 无约束条件下作为上界的星形结构
A recurring empirical pattern is that hierarchies sometimes outperform naive centralization. It is tempting to interpret this as “hierarchy is intrinsically better.” Without constraints, that interpretation is false. A star is not merely a particular topology; it is the topology that gives the decision rule access to all leaf information in a single place. Any hierarchy that compresses information on the way up can only discard information, not create it (Cover and Thomas, 2006).
一个反复出现的经验现象是，层级结构有时优于朴素的集中式结构。人们很容易将其解读为“层级结构在本质上更优”。在无约束条件下，这种解读是错误的。星形结构不仅仅是一种特定拓扑；它是一种使决策规则能够在同一处访问所有叶节点信息的拓扑。任何在信息向上传递过程中对其进行压缩的层级结构，都只能丢弃信息，而不能创造信息（Cover and Thomas, 2006）。
We formalize this point in an intentionally general way, independent of the details of the aggregation rule.
我们刻意采用一种不依赖聚合规则细节的一般性方式，将这一观点形式化。
Proposition 7 (Centralization dominates under unlimited context).命题 7（在上下文无限的条件下，集中式结构占优）。
Let $X=(X_{1},\dots,X_{N})$ denote the collection of all leaf messages (or leaf outputs) generated in response to a task with truth label $Y$ . Consider any multi-agent protocol on any directed acyclic topology such that the root ultimately observes a variable $Z$ that is a (possibly randomized) function of $X$ with randomness independent of $Y$ . Then for any loss $\ell(\widehat{Y},Y)$ ,
令 $X=(X_{1},\dots,X_{N})$ 表示针对真实标签为 $Y$ 的任务所生成的全部叶节点消息（或叶节点输出）的集合。考虑任意有向无环拓扑上的任意多智能体协议，其中根节点最终观测到变量 $Z$，它是 $X$ 的一个（可能随机化的）函数，且其随机性独立于 $Y$。那么，对于任意损失 $\ell(\widehat{Y},Y)$，
$$\inf_{\widehat{Y}=\phi(Z)}\mathbb{E}[\ell(\widehat{Y},Y)]\;\geq\;\inf_{\widehat{Y}=\psi(X)}\mathbb{E}[\ell(\widehat{Y},Y)].$$
In particular, a centralized decision rule $\psi(X)$ that has access to $X$ cannot perform worse than any rule $\phi(Z)$ that only sees $Z$ .
特别地，能够访问 $X$ 的集中式决策规则 $\psi(X)$，其性能不会差于任何仅能观测 $Z$ 的规则 $\phi(Z)$。
Proof.证明。
Let $U$ collect the protocol’s internal randomness, independent of $Y$ , so that $Z=g(X,U)$ . Any rule $\phi(Z)$ induces a (possibly randomized) rule $\psi(X,U):=\phi(g(X,U))$ ; hence predictors based on $Z$ are a subset of predictors based on $(X,U)$ (and thus no better than those based on $X$ ), proving the risk inequality. $\square$
令 $U$ 汇集协议内部独立于 $Y$ 的随机性，从而有 $Z=g(X,U)$。任意规则 $\phi(Z)$ 都会诱导出一个（可能随机化的）规则 $\psi(X,U):=\phi(g(X,U))$；因此，基于 $Z$ 的预测器是基于 $(X,U)$ 的预测器的子集（因而不会优于基于 $X$ 的预测器），由此证明了风险不等式。$\square$
Proposition 7 is not an endorsement of star in constrained settings. It is a sanity check: hierarchies are only needed because some constraint makes full centralization infeasible or too expensive. In this paper, the dominant such constraint is the context window $W$ , which caps the fan-in of any node (Section 2.5). Once $W$ binds, a star can only incorporate a bounded number of leaves regardless of budget, and that is precisely where hierarchy becomes a meaningful design choice.
命题 7 并非主张在受约束的情境下采用星形结构。它是一项合理性检验：只有当某种约束使完全集中化不可行或成本过高时，才需要层级结构。在本文中，此类约束中起主导作用的是上下文窗口 $W$，它限制了任意节点的扇入数（第 2.5 节）。一旦 $W$ 成为有效约束，无论预算多少，星形结构能够纳入的叶节点数量都有上限，而这恰恰是层级结构成为有意义的设计选择之处。
4.5.2 Why chains degrade without new evidence4.5.2 为何在没有新证据时链式结构会退化
Chains are appealing in practice because they resemble deliberation: one agent’s output becomes the next agent’s input. However, in our framework a chain does not introduce new independent evidence; it only retransmits and transforms what already exists. Under lossy communication, repeated retransmission compounds loss.
链式结构在实践中颇具吸引力，因为它类似于审议过程：一个智能体的输出成为下一个智能体的输入。然而，在我们的框架中，链式结构不会引入新的独立证据；它只是转发和变换已有信息。在有损通信下，反复转发会使损失累积。
This is explicit in both settings. In the continuous warm-up, (14) shows MSE increases linearly with chain length. In the binary setting, the effect is even simpler.
这一点在两种设定中都很明确。在作为铺垫的连续情形中，式 (14) 表明均方误差（MSE）随链长线性增加。在二元设定中，这种效应甚至更为简单。
Proposition 8 (Bias decays along a chain under lossy communication).命题 8（在有损通信下，偏差沿链衰减）。
Consider a chain of length $L$ in which a single leaf vote with bias $\mu_{0}$ is transmitted through $L$ independent communication steps, each with reliability $\gamma(m)$ , and no new independent evidence enters the chain. Then the final bias is
考虑一条长度为 $L$ 的链，其中偏差为 $\mu_{0}$ 的单个叶节点投票经过 $L$ 个相互独立的通信步骤传递，每一步的可靠性均为 $\gamma(m)$，且没有新的独立证据进入该链。则最终偏差为
$$\mu_{\text{chain}}(L)=\gamma(m)^{L}\,\mu_{0}.$$
Consequently, if $\gamma(m)<1$ and $L\geq 1$ , then $\mu_{\text{chain}}(L)<\mu_{0}$ .
因此，若 $\gamma(m)<1$ 且 $L\geq 1$，则 $\mu_{\text{chain}}(L)<\mu_{0}$。
Proof.证明。
Each hop multiplies bias by $\gamma(m)$ by the channel model in Section 2.3. $\square$
根据第 2.3 节的信道模型，每一跳都会将偏差乘以 $\gamma(m)$。$\square$
Design takeaway.设计启示。
A relay-only chain strictly attenuates signal (Proposition 8); it helps only when intermediate steps add new evidence (tools, retrieval, verification) rather than passing along the same information. We therefore focus on star versus tree as the two scalable evidence-aggregation strategies under context limits.
仅起中继作用的链会严格衰减信号（命题 8）；只有当中间步骤增加新证据（工具、检索、验证），而非仅仅传递相同信息时，它才有帮助。因此，我们将重点放在星形结构与树形结构的比较上，将二者视为上下文限制下两种可扩展的证据聚合策略。
4.5.3 Context-limited star versus hierarchical trees4.5.3 上下文受限的星形结构与分层树结构
The context window $W$ turns Proposition 7 from a theoretical upper bound into a practical obstacle. A star aggregator that receives $N$ messages of length $m$ must read roughly $Nm$ tokens, so feasibility requires
上下文窗口 $W$ 使命题 7 从理论上界变成了实际障碍。一个接收 $N$ 条长度为 $m$ 的消息的星形聚合器必须读取大约 $Nm$ 个词元，因此可行性要求
$$Nm\leq W\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad N\leq N_{\max}(m):=\Big\lfloor\frac{W}{m}\Big\rfloor.$$
For fixed $W$ , $N_{\max}(m)$ is a constant independent of budget. Thus, once a star uses as many leaves as it can fit in context, additional budget cannot increase the number of incorporated leaves. The only remaining levers are to strengthen each leaf (increase $x$ ) or to increase message length (increase $m$ ) at the cost of reducing $N_{\max}(m)$ .
对于固定的 $W$，$N_{\max}(m)$ 是一个与预算无关的常数。因此，一旦星形结构使用的叶节点数量达到上下文所能容纳的上限，增加预算就无法再增加纳入的叶节点数量。剩下的调节手段只有增强每个叶节点（增大 $x$），或者以降低 $N_{\max}(m)$ 为代价增加消息长度（增大 $m$）。
A hierarchical tree avoids the global cap (36) by enforcing the context constraint locally. If each internal node has fan-in $b$ and reads $b$ messages of length $m$ , feasibility requires
分层树通过在局部施加上下文约束，避免了全局上限 (36)。如果每个内部节点的扇入（fan-in）为 $b$，并读取 $b$ 条长度为 $m$ 的消息，则可行性要求
$$bm\leq W,$$
but the number of leaves can grow as $N=b^{L}$ with depth $L$ . In other words, a tree converts a global bottleneck ( $Nm\leq W$ at one node) into many local bottlenecks ( $bm\leq W$ at many nodes). 但叶节点数量可以随深度 $L$ 按 $N=b^{L}$ 增长。换言之，树将一个全局瓶颈（单个节点处的 $Nm\leq W$）转化为多个局部瓶颈（多个节点处的 $bm\leq W$）。图 8 展示了这一权衡：星形结构由于上下文限制而较早达到饱和，而树可以继续扩展，直到达到其不动点上限 $\mu^{\star}$。

$$\alpha_{\rho}=\gamma(m)\big(\rho+(1-\rho)f_{b}^{\prime}(0)\big)>1.$$
Even when $\alpha_{\rho}>1$ , deeper trees saturate at a fixed point $\mu^{\star}$ , so trees should be viewed as a tool for scaling up to a regime of strong performance, not for unlimited improvement.
即使 $\alpha_{\rho}>1$，更深的树也会在不动点 $\mu^{\star}$ 处饱和，因此应将树视为通过扩展来达到高性能区间的工具，而非实现无限改进的工具。

$$t^{\star}=\frac{q_{1}-q_{2}}{s_{2}-s_{1}}.$$

6 Empirical Touchpoints6 实证关联
This paper is primarily theoretical: our goal is to explain why budgeted multi-agent scaling amplifies in some regimes and collapses in others, and to make the relevant regime boundaries explicit. Accordingly, we keep the empirical component modest. We (i) connect our bottlenecks and regime predictions to recent large-scale matched-budget evaluations reported elsewhere, and (ii) provide a minimal synthetic sanity check that recovers the predicted amplification boundary under the assumed model.
本文主要是一项理论研究：我们的目标是解释为何预算约束下的多智能体扩展在某些区间产生放大效应，而在其他区间发生塌缩，并明确相关的区间边界。因此，我们将实证部分保持在较小规模。我们 (i) 将所识别的瓶颈和区间预测与其他文献近期报告的大规模等预算评估联系起来，并且 (ii) 提供一个最简合成合理性检验，在所假定的模型下复现预测的放大边界。

7 Discussion and Limitations7 讨论与局限性
We asked when scaling out a budgeted multi-agent system yields synergy and when it fails. The framework is intentionally minimal: it treats an agent as a black-box solver with an allocated compute budget, models inter-agent communication as a controllable lossy channel, and summarizes shared failures through an effective correlation parameter. This minimalism makes the regime boundaries transparent, but it also defines what the theory does and does not claim.
我们探讨了在预算约束下，横向扩展多智能体系统何时能够产生协同效应，以及何时会失效。该框架刻意采用极简设计：将智能体视为获得一定计算预算的黑箱求解器，将智能体间的通信建模为可控的有损信道，并通过一个有效相关性参数概括共同失效。这种极简设计使各状态区域的边界清晰可见，但也界定了该理论能够和不能够作出的论断。
While our results provide a rigorous asymptotic foundation for agent scaling, the practical utility of these bounds hinges on the empirical estimation of the shared-failure correlation $\rho$ and the organization exponent $s$ . In practice, $\rho$ serves as a ‘homogeneity metric’ for a given model ensemble; a high $\rho$ suggests that the agents are inheriting the same inductive biases or training data flaws, thereby collapsing the effective fan-in $b$ . Future work should focus on developing diagnostic probes—short, high-entropy test batteries—to calibrate these parameters for specific LLM families. By doing so, the Phase Transition $\alpha_{\rho}$ can be transformed from a theoretical threshold into a real-time ‘stopping rule’ for determining whether further tree depth will yield diminishing returns or catastrophic signal decay.
尽管我们的结果为智能体扩展提供了严格的渐近理论基础，但这些界限的实际效用取决于对共同失效相关性 $\rho$ 和组织指数 $s$ 的经验估计。在实践中，$\rho$ 可作为给定模型集成的“同质性指标”；较高的 $\rho$ 表明，各智能体继承了相同的归纳偏置或训练数据缺陷，从而使有效扇入数 $b$ 大幅缩减。未来的工作应着重开发诊断性探测工具，即简短的高熵测试组，以针对特定 LLM 家族校准这些参数。通过这种方式，相变参数 $\alpha_{\rho}$ 可以从理论阈值转化为实时“停止规则”，用于判断进一步增加树的深度会带来收益递减，还是灾难性的信号衰减。
Relationship to prior results与已有结果的关系
Our binary phase transition echoes reconstruction thresholds for broadcasting on trees (Kesten and Stigum, 1966; Evans et al., 2000; Mossel, 2001), where information can or cannot propagate from leaves to the root depending on a single scalar. Two differences are central for agent-system design. First, our “channel” is partly under design control: message length trades off fidelity $\gamma(m)$ against budget and context constraints, so the relevant threshold depends on an explicit communication choice. Second, we couple propagation to a fixed-budget allocation problem: an amplifiable hierarchy is synergistic only when its organization exponent outpaces single-agent scaling.

Recent work offers complementary perspectives on information flow in agent systems. He et al. Li (2026) study skill selection in single-agent systems and observe a phase transition: selection accuracy remains stable up to a critical library size then drops sharply, with semantic confusability among skills playing a central role. This echoes our amplification–collapse dichotomy, but at a different interface: where we model information loss across agents, they model selection confusion within a single agent’s skill routing. Together, these results suggest that sharp thresholds, whether in inter-agent communication, intra-agent skill selection, or hierarchical aggregation, may be a recurring motif in bounded-resource AI systems.

What the framework explains (and what it does not)该框架能够解释什么（以及不能解释什么）
The framework is designed to explain qualitative regime changes that are otherwise hard to diagnose: why stars saturate under context limits, why chains compound loss, why trees can either amplify or collapse depending on $\alpha_{\rho}$ , and why correlated failures impose floors that make large ensembles behave like “one agent with shared blind spots.” It also clarifies what additional sophistication must accomplish: any useful complexity must effectively increase communication fidelity, reduce shared failures, or relax the fan-in constraint.

At the same time, the model is not a faithful simulator of real agent stacks. Parameters such as $\rho$ and $\gamma(m)$ are effective summaries that can vary with depth, prompts, tools, and task difficulty; real communication is far richer than a one-bit abstraction; and practical budget accounting can differ across implementations. For these reasons, we view the theory as a coordination diagnostic, i.e., predicting which constraint is binding and which direction should help, rather than as a source of precise point predictions. Crucially, the alignment between our derived bottlenecks and independent large-scale observations (Kim et al., 2025) suggests that the effective parameters defined here capture the structural essence of the scaling problem, rendering the theory robust to changes in the underlying base models.
与此同时，该模型并不能忠实模拟真实的智能体技术栈。$\rho$ 和 $\gamma(m)$ 等参数是有效的概括性指标，可能随深度、提示词、工具和任务难度而变化；真实通信远比单比特抽象丰富；实际的预算核算方式也可能因实现而异。基于这些原因，我们将该理论视为一种协调诊断工具，即预测哪个约束起制约作用，以及朝哪个方向调整会有所帮助，而不是用来给出精确的点预测。关键在于，我们推导出的瓶颈与独立的大规模观测结果（Kim et al., 2025）相吻合，这表明此处定义的有效参数抓住了扩展问题的结构本质，使该理论对底层基础模型的变化具有稳健性。
How stronger protocols fit the framework更强的协议如何纳入该框架
Protocols such as debate, critique, verification, or self-consistency can be interpreted as mechanisms that change the effective parameters of the model (Irving et al., 2018; Christiano et al., 2018; Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023). For example, structured verification can reduce effective shared failures ( $\rho$ ), while richer message schemas can improve effective fidelity ( $\gamma(m)$ ); both, however, typically increase coordination cost. The theory suggests a concrete way to evaluate such protocols: measure how much they move the effective bottlenecks per unit budget, and check whether they move the system across the supercritical boundary $\alpha_{\rho}>1$ or the synergy boundary $s>\beta$ .
辩论、批评、验证或自一致性等协议可以被理解为改变模型有效参数的机制（Irving et al., 2018; Christiano et al., 2018; Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023）。例如，结构化验证可以减少有效共享失效（$\rho$），而更丰富的消息模式可以提高有效保真度（$\gamma(m)$）；然而，两者通常都会增加协调成本。该理论提出了一种评估此类协议的具体方法：衡量它们在单位预算下能使有效瓶颈改善多少，并检查它们是否使系统跨越超临界边界 $\alpha_{\rho}>1$ 或协同边界 $s>\beta$。
Extensions beyond our scope超出本文范围的扩展
Several extensions are natural. Allowing heterogeneity across leaves (different models, prompts, or tools) makes $\rho$ and scaling task- and agent-dependent; a tractable next step is to treat $\rho$ as depth-dependent or to model mixtures of agent types. More general graphs and multi-round interaction can be analyzed by unrolling protocols into computation graphs, but doing so requires accounting for how added edges change effective dependence as well as cost. Finally, our strongest binary bounds rely on one-bit-style messages and majority aggregation; allowing richer messages or confidence reports changes the amplification map and may relax universal exponent limits. In all cases, the guiding principle remains the same: additional structure helps only insofar as it relaxes the binding constraints (context, communication loss, or shared failures) under a fixed budget.
有几种自然的扩展方向。允许叶节点之间存在异质性（使用不同的模型、提示词或工具），会使 $\rho$ 和扩展行为取决于任务与智能体；一个易于处理的后续方向是将 $\rho$ 视为随深度变化的量，或对智能体类型的混合进行建模。可以通过将协议展开为计算图来分析更一般的图结构和多轮交互，但这需要考虑新增边如何改变有效依赖关系及成本。最后，我们最强的二元任务界限依赖于单比特式消息和多数聚合；允许更丰富的消息或置信度报告会改变放大映射，并可能放宽普适的指数限制。在所有情况下，指导原则始终相同：在固定预算下，额外结构只有在缓解起制约作用的约束（上下文、通信损失或共享失效）时才有帮助。
8 Conclusion8 结论
Multi-agent systems can exhibit synergy, but under a fixed budget scaling out is not monotone: it can help, saturate, or collapse. We argued that these outcomes become predictable once we make the binding constraints explicit: finite context windows, lossy communication, and shared failures, and summarize an environment by a small set of measurable effective parameters. In a continuous warm-up we derived closed-form risks for star/chain/tree organizations and exposed correlation and communication floors. For binary success/failure tasks, we proved a sharp amplification–collapse phase transition for deep majority trees and characterized the resulting fixed-point saturation and mixing depth. In the amplifying regime we introduced an organization exponent $s$ governing small-signal growth and showed that budgeted synergy in the growth range occurs exactly when $s>\beta$ , yielding closed-form compute allocation and explicit budget thresholds. We also translated these results into simple design diagnostics within the model. The broader message is that topology is not magic. Hierarchies are useful because context limits can make full centralization infeasible, and they succeed only when communication is sufficiently faithful and shared failures are sufficiently weak. We hope this work helps move agent-system design from ad hoc heuristics toward principled, testable coordination theories.
多智能体系统可以表现出协同效应，但在固定预算下，横向扩展的效果并非单调：它可能带来改善、达到饱和，或发生崩溃。我们认为，一旦明确起制约作用的约束——有限的上下文窗口、有损通信和共享失效——并用一小组可测量的有效参数概括环境，这些结果就变得可以预测。在作为铺垫的连续情形分析中，我们推导了星形、链式和树形组织的闭式风险表达式，并揭示了相关性和通信所造成的风险下限。对于二元成败任务，我们证明了深层多数树中存在明确的放大—崩溃相变，并刻画了由此产生的不动点饱和与混合深度。在放大区间中，我们引入了控制小信号增长的组织指数 $s$，并表明，在增长范围内，预算约束下的协同效应当且仅当 $s>\beta$ 时出现，由此得到了闭式计算资源分配方案和显式预算阈值。我们还将这些结果转化为模型内的简单设计诊断方法。更广泛的启示是，拓扑结构并无魔力。层级结构之所以有用，是因为上下文限制可能使完全集中化不可行，而它们只有在通信足够保真、共享失效足够弱时才能成功。我们希望这项工作有助于推动智能体系统设计从临时性的启发式方法走向有原则、可检验的协调理论。

这与 (59) 矛盾。因此，$m_{2}\geq m_{1}$，即 $m^{-}(t)$ 单调不减。由 $t=\log B$ 转回以 $B$ 为变量的表述，即证明了可以选取一个关于 $B$ 单调不减的最优消息长度。∎
I.2 Upper-envelope construction and correctness of Algorithm 1I.2 上包络的构造与算法 1 的正确性
We sketch a standard correctness argument for the stack-based envelope construction used in Algorithm 1. Consider a finite family of lines $\ell_{i}(t)=s_{i}t+q_{i}$ with strictly increasing slopes $s_{1}<s_{2}<\cdots<s_{M}$ . Define the upper envelope $E(t)=\max_{i}\ell_{i}(t)$ .
我们简述算法 1 中基于栈的包络构造方法的一个标准正确性论证。考虑有限个直线 $\ell_{i}(t)=s_{i}t+q_{i}$，其斜率严格递增，即 $s_{1}<s_{2}<\cdots<s_{M}$。定义上包络 $E(t)=\max_{i}\ell_{i}(t)$。
Lemma 19 (Envelope structure for increasing slopes).引理 19（斜率递增时的包络结构）。
If $s_{i}$ are strictly increasing, then there exist indices $1\leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{r}\leq M$ and breakpoints $-\infty=\tau_{0}<\tau_{1}<\cdots<\tau_{r}=\infty$ such that for every $j\in\{1,\dots,r\}$ ,
若 $s_{i}$ 严格递增，则存在索引 $1\leq i_{1}<i_{2}<\cdots<i_{r}\leq M$ 和断点 $-\infty=\tau_{0}<\tau_{1}<\cdots<\tau_{r}=\infty$，使得对每个 $j\in\{1,\dots,r\}$，
$$E(t)=\ell_{i_{j}}(t)\quad\text{for all }t\in[\tau_{j-1},\tau_{j}).$$
That is, the maximizing index is nondecreasing in $t$ and changes only at increasing intersection points.
也就是说，取得最大值的索引随 $t$ 单调不减，并且仅在依次递增的交点处发生变化。
Proof.证明。
For any pair $i<j$ , the difference $\ell_{j}(t)-\ell_{i}(t)$ is an increasing affine function of $t$ (because $s_{j}-s_{i}>0$ ). Therefore, the set where $\ell_{j}$ dominates $\ell_{i}$ is either empty or a half-line $[t^{\star},\infty)$ , where $t^{\star}$ is their intersection. This single-crossing property implies the argmax index over $\{\ell_{i}\}$ is nondecreasing in $t$ , and the envelope is piecewise linear with breakpoints at pairwise intersections. ∎
对于任意一对 $i<j$，差值 $\ell_{j}(t)-\ell_{i}(t)$ 是关于 $t$ 的递增仿射函数（因为 $s_{j}-s_{i}>0$）。因此，$\ell_{j}$ 不小于 $\ell_{i}$ 的点集要么为空，要么为半直线 $[t^{\star},\infty)$，其中 $t^{\star}$ 是二者的交点。这一单交叉性质意味着，$\{\ell_{i}\}$ 中取得最大值的索引随 $t$ 单调不减，且包络线是分段线性的，其断点位于两两交点处。∎
Algorithm 1 is a constructive implementation of Lemma 19. Processing lines in increasing slope order, it maintains a stack of candidate lines $(\ell_{i_{1}},\dots,\ell_{i_{r}})$ and the associated increasing breakpoints $(\tau_{1},\dots,\tau_{r-1})$ . When a new line $\ell$ is added, if its intersection with the current top occurs to the left of the top’s activation breakpoint, then the top line is never maximal and is removed. Because each line is pushed and popped at most once, the construction runs in linear time. The resulting stack and breakpoint list exactly encode the envelope $E(t)$ , and the associated maximizing message length is the piecewise-constant function $m_{b}^{\star}(B)$ in Theorem 12.
算法 1 是引理 19 的一种构造性实现。它按斜率递增的顺序处理直线，维护一个候选直线栈 $(\ell_{i_{1}},\dots,\ell_{i_{r}})$ 及相应的递增断点 $(\tau_{1},\dots,\tau_{r-1})$。加入一条新直线 $\ell$ 时，若它与当前栈顶直线的交点位于栈顶直线的激活断点左侧，则栈顶直线不可能取得最大值，因此将其移除。由于每条直线最多入栈和出栈各一次，该构造的运行时间为线性。最终得到的栈和断点列表精确地表示了包络线 $E(t)$，相应的最优消息长度就是定理 12 中的分段常数函数 $m_{b}^{\star}(B)$。

本小节将第 2.7 节关于“可测量参数”的承诺转化为可操作的流程。目标不是拟合复杂模型，而是获得足够准确且稳定的估计，从而将系统定位到相图中正确的定性区间。

Communication fidelity ( $\gamma(m)$ ) or distortion ( $\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$ ).通信保真度（$\gamma(m)$）或失真（$\sigma_{c}^{2}(m)$）。

J.2 LLM diagnostic template: context limits, correlation floors, and saturationJ.2 LLM 诊断模板：上下文限制、相关性下限与饱和
The synthetic setting checks internal consistency under the assumed generative models. The more important question is whether the framework remains useful as an engineering diagnostic when agents are real LLM instances with non-Gaussian errors and prompt effects. Our aim here is therefore qualitative: do calibrated parameters correctly identify which lever is binding and which intervention should help?
合成实验设置用于检验所假设的生成模型下的内部一致性。更重要的问题是，当智能体是真实的 LLM 实例，其错误呈非高斯分布且受到提示影响时，该框架是否仍能作为有效的工程诊断工具。因此，我们在此的目标是定性的：校准后的参数能否正确识别哪个调控因素构成约束，以及哪种干预应当有所帮助？
We recommend using at least one binary family with unambiguous correctness (e.g., multiple-choice, exact-answer math, pass/fail tests) and one continuous family with graded scores (e.g., rubric grading or scalar estimation with known targets), since the framework makes distinct predictions in each. Implement single-agent, star, tree (and optionally chain) pipelines under matched realized budgets, standardizing prompts and message schemas across topologies. For stars, feasibility is $Nm\leq W$ ; for trees, feasibility is $bm\leq W$ , with depth and leaf count chosen using the design rules in Section 5.
我们建议至少使用一类正确性明确的二元任务（例如选择题、具有精确答案的数学题、通过/不通过测试），以及一类具有分级评分的连续任务（例如按评分量规评分，或目标值已知的标量估计），因为该框架对这两类任务给出了不同的预测。在实际预算相匹配的条件下，实现单智能体、星形、树形（以及可选的链式）流水线，并在不同拓扑结构之间统一提示和消息模式。对于星形结构，可行性条件为 $Nm\leq W$；对于树形结构，可行性条件为 $bm\leq W$，其深度和叶节点数量依据第 5 节的设计规则选取。
In this setting, the most useful validations are diagnostic. First, under fixed $W$ , the best feasible star should saturate in effective parallelism once $N$ hits $\lfloor W/m\rfloor$ , so additional budget primarily benefits scale-up. Second, measured $\widehat{\rho}$ should help explain why naive voting sometimes fails: high $\widehat{\rho}$ predicts limited aggregation gains even with many agents. Third, the calibrated quantity $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}=\widehat{\gamma}(m)(\widehat{\rho}+(1-\widehat{\rho})f_{b}^{\prime}(0))$ should act as a feasibility check for trees: when $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}\leq 1$ , deeper hierarchies should become unstable or drift toward chance; when $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}>1$ , trees should amplify and then saturate. Finally, when performance plateaus with depth or budget, the framework predicts that further gains come primarily from improving communication fidelity (longer messages, better schemas) or reducing shared failures (diversity, verification), rather than adding depth.
在这一设置中，最有用的验证具有诊断性质。首先，在 $W$ 固定时，一旦 $N$ 达到 $\lfloor W/m\rfloor$，最优可行星形结构的有效并行度就应达到饱和，因此额外预算主要有利于纵向扩展。其次，测得的 $\widehat{\rho}$ 应有助于解释朴素投票为何有时失效：较高的 $\widehat{\rho}$ 预示着，即使有许多智能体，聚合收益也有限。第三，校准后的量 $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}=\widehat{\gamma}(m)(\widehat{\rho}+(1-\widehat{\rho})f_{b}^{\prime}(0))$ 应可用于检验树形结构的可行性：当 $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}\leq 1$ 时，更深的层级结构应变得不稳定，或逐渐趋近随机猜测水平；当 $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}>1$ 时，树形结构应产生放大效应，随后达到饱和。最后，当性能随深度或预算增加而进入平台期时，该框架预测，进一步的收益主要来自提高通信保真度（更长的消息、更好的消息模式）或减少共同失误（多样性、验证），而非增加深度。
Diagnostic interventions (compact ablations).诊断性干预（精简消融实验）。
Three inexpensive interventions connect the theory’s parameters to practice under matched budgets: varying message length $m$ (trading off $\gamma(m)$ against coordination cost), injecting diversity to reduce $\widehat{\rho}$ (e.g., prompt, model, temperature, tool-policy variation or explicit verification roles), and swapping topology (single, star, chain, tree) under fixed schemas. When these interventions move $\widehat{\gamma}(m)$ or $\widehat{\rho}$ in the expected direction, the phase-diagram predictions provide testable hypotheses about whether amplification becomes feasible and whether scale-out can outpace scale-up before saturation.
在预算相匹配的条件下，三种低成本干预将理论参数与实践联系起来：改变消息长度 $m$（在 $\gamma(m)$ 与协调成本之间进行权衡）、引入多样性以降低 $\widehat{\rho}$（例如改变提示、模型、温度、工具策略，或设置明确的验证角色），以及在消息模式固定的条件下切换拓扑结构（单智能体、星形、链式、树形）。当这些干预使 $\widehat{\gamma}(m)$ 或 $\widehat{\rho}$ 朝预期方向变化时，相图预测便提供了可检验的假设，用于判断放大是否变得可行，以及横向扩展能否在饱和之前优于纵向扩展。
Logging and reproducibility.日志记录与可复现性。
For each run, log realized tokens per node, realized message lengths, and outputs at each level (or hashes if privacy is needed). These logs enable post hoc estimation of $\rho$ , auditing of budget fairness, and direct diagnosis of failure modes such as repeated identical errors.
对于每次运行，记录每个节点实际使用的 token 数、实际消息长度，以及各层级的输出（如需保护隐私，则记录哈希值）。这些日志支持事后估计 $\rho$、审查预算分配的公平性，以及直接诊断反复出现相同错误等失效模式。
These templates are meant to support qualitative diagnostics: whether context is binding, whether measured correlation suggests limited gains, and whether the calibrated $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}$ places a hierarchy in the amplifying or collapsing regime. In that sense, the phase diagrams and design curves from Sections 4.5–5 provide concrete hypotheses about which organization should help under matched budgets and which lever (communication fidelity versus shared-failure reduction) is most likely to matter when it does not. Establishing quantitative predictiveness in realistic deployments, where prompts, tools, and dependence structure may drift with depth and task difficulty, is an important direction for future work.
这些模板旨在支持定性诊断：上下文是否构成约束、测得的相关性是否表明收益有限，以及校准后的 $\widehat{\alpha}_{\rho}$ 将层级结构置于放大区间还是崩塌区间。从这个意义上说，第 4.5–5 节的相图和设计曲线提供了具体假设，用于判断在预算相匹配的条件下，哪种组织结构应当有所帮助，以及当其未能奏效时，哪个调控因素（通信保真度或共同失误的减少）最可能发挥作用。在实际部署中，提示、工具和依赖结构可能随深度和任务难度而变化，因此，确立该框架在此类部署中的定量预测能力，是未来研究的一个重要方向。
