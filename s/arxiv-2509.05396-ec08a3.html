交谈并非总是没有代价：理解多智能体辩论中的失效模式Talk Isn’t Always Cheap: Understanding Failure Modes in Multi-Agent Debate
摘要Abstract
尽管多智能体辩论被提出作为一种有望提升 AI 推理能力的策略，但我们发现，辩论有时非但无益，反而有害。 While multi-agent debate has been proposed as a promising strategy for improving AI reasoning ability, we find that debate can sometimes be harmful rather than helpful. 既有研究主要关注同质智能体群体内部的辩论，而我们探讨模型能力的多样性如何影响多智能体交互的动态过程与结果。 Prior work has primarily focused on debates within homogeneous groups of agents, whereas we explore how diversity in model capabilities influences the dynamics and outcomes of multi-agent interactions. 通过一系列实验，我们表明，辩论可能导致准确率随时间下降——即使在较强（即能力更强）模型的数量多于较弱模型的情况下也是如此。 Through a series of experiments, we demonstrate that debate can lead to a decrease in accuracy over time — even in settings where stronger (i.e., more capable) models outnumber their weaker counterparts. 我们的分析表明，模型在回应其他智能体的推理时，经常会从正确答案转向错误答案，倾向于表示认同，而非质疑有缺陷的推理。 Our analysis reveals that models frequently shift from correct to incorrect answers in response to peer reasoning, favoring agreement over challenging flawed reasoning. 我们开展了额外实验，考察可能促成这些有害转变的多种因素，包括迎合（sycophancy）、社会从众（social conformity），以及模型类型和任务类型。 We perform additional experiments investigating various potential contributing factors to these harmful shifts – including sycophancy, social conformity, and model and task type. 这些结果凸显了多智能体辩论中理由交流的重要失效模式，表明当智能体既缺乏抵制具有说服力但错误的推理的激励，也未充分具备相应能力时，简单地应用辩论可能导致性能下降。 These results highlight important failure modes in the exchange of reasons during multi-agent debate, suggesting that naive applications of debate may cause performance degradation when agents are neither incentivised nor adequately equipped to resist persuasive but incorrect reasoning.

1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型（LLM）智能体在广泛的复杂推理任务中展现出了卓越的问题解决能力 (Brown et al., 2020)。 Large Language Model (LLM) agents have demonstrated remarkable problem-solving abilities across a wide array of complex reasoning tasks (Brown et al., 2020). 近期，一条围绕多个 LLM 通过辩论开展交互式推理的新研究路线，将多智能体辩论框架推为一种有望增强 LLM 智能体推理与决策能力的方法 (Du et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Khan et al., 2024)。 Recently, a new line of research on interactive reasoning among multiple LLMs through debate has promoted the multi-agent debate framework as a promising approach to enhancing the reasoning and decision-making capabilities of LLM agents (Du et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Khan et al., 2024). 已有研究表明，多种形式的多智能体辩论能够提升模型在多个算术与策略推理基准上的表现 (Du et al., 2023; Subramaniam et al., 2025)，生成更符合事实的答案与评估 (Chan et al., 2023; Khan et al., 2024)，并改善机器翻译 (Liang et al., 2023) 和谈判 (Fu et al., 2023) 等任务的表现。 Various forms of multi-agent debate have been shown to improve performance on multiple arithmetic and strategic reasoning benchmarks (Du et al., 2023; Subramaniam et al., 2025), produce more truthful answers and evaluations (Chan et al., 2023; Khan et al., 2024), and enhance tasks such as machine translation (Liang et al., 2023) and negotiation (Fu et al., 2023). 这些研究的核心理念是，通过让 LLM 智能体参与结构化论证或讨论，我们可以促进不同智能体之间的推理交流，并引导它们得出更准确的答案。 The core concept of these studies is that by engaging LLM agents through structured argumentation or discourse, we can facilitate the exchange of reasoning among different agents and guide them toward more accurate answers. 直观而言，更多的推理交流应当带来更好的决策——使多个智能体能够质疑有缺陷的推理、指出被忽略的细节，并减少个体偏差。 Intuitively, greater exchanges of reasoning should lead to better decisions—allowing multiple agents to challenge flawed reasoning, highlight overlooked details, and reduce individual biases. 但事实总是如此吗？ But is this always the case?
在本研究中，我们表明，多智能体辩论的益处并不像通常认为的那样普遍。 In this work, we show that the benefits of multi-agent debate are not as universal as commonly assumed. 通过一系列实证研究，我们表明，多智能体辩论有时会降低性能，导致最终答案比单个智能体独立生成的答案更差。 Through a series of empirical studies, we show that multi-agent debate can sometimes degrade performance, leading to worse final answers than those generated by a single agent acting alone. 这些失效并非罕见的边缘情况，而是在智能体相互放大错误的情境中系统性地出现——它们不假思索地表示认同，而非质疑有缺陷的推理。 These failures are not rare edge cases, but arise systematically in settings where agents amplify each other’s errors – agreeing reflexively rather than challenging flawed reasoning. 即使参与辩论的 LLM 智能体之间存在能力差异，这些发现仍然成立。 These findings hold even when there is variation in the abilities of the participating LLM agents. 例如，我们发现，在与一个较强或能力更强（表现更好）的智能体进行的辩论中，引入一个较弱或能力较弱（表现较差）的 LLM 智能体，可能对辩论结果产生不利影响，使结果比这些智能体未进行讨论时更差。 For instance, we discover that introducing a weak or less capable (lower-performing) LLM agent into a debate with a strong or more capable (higher-performing) agent can detrimentally affect the debate outcome, producing results worse than if the agents had not engaged in discussion. 较弱智能体的存在会干扰较强智能体的表现。 The presence of a weaker agent disrupts the performance of the stronger agent. 此外，在某些情况下，辩论持续得越久，性能下降的程度可能越大。 Moreover, in certain cases, the longer a debate continues, the more performance can degrade. 换言之，交谈并非总是没有代价——在某些情况下，它甚至会造成实质性损害。 In other words: talk isn’t always cheap – and in some cases, it’s actively harmful.
我们对多智能体辩论在多个任务上的表现进行了系统评估，表明辩论有时会损害群体表现，尤其是在参与辩论的 LLM 智能体具有异质性时。 We present a systematic evaluation of multi-agent debate across multiple tasks, showing that debate can sometimes harm group performance, particularly with heterogeneous LLM agents engaged in debate. 我们的发现挑战了一种主流观点，即智能体之间更多的讨论本身就有益。 Our findings challenge the prevailing narrative that more discussion between agents is inherently beneficial. 相反，我们揭示了影响辩论成败的几个关键因素，包括任务类型与复杂度、智能体的多样性与能力，以及社会影响。 Instead, we uncover several key factors that mediate the success or failure of debate, including task type and complexity, agent diversity and capability, and social influence. 由此，我们对辩论何时以及为何有益、何时有害，给出了更为细致的认识。 In doing so, we offer a nuanced view of when and why debate helps – and when it hurts. 综合来看，这些结果表明，尽管辩论仍是一种有望改善模型推理的工具，但应谨慎应用，以确保其在所关注的任务与情境中的安全性。 Together, these results suggest that while debate remains a promising tool for improving model reasoning, it should be applied carefully to ensure safety on the task and setting of interest.

2 相关工作2 Related Work
辩论与多智能体推理。 Debate and multi-agent reasoning. 多智能体辩论最初被提出用于解决可扩展监督问题，其中，裁判或验证者可以在结构化的往返对话中介入，使隐藏的矛盾显现出来 (Irving et al., 2018; Khan et al., 2024; Michael et al., 2023; Kenton et al., 2024)。 Multi-agent debate was initially proposed as method for the scalable oversight problem where a judge or verifier can interject and elicit hidden contradictions, using structured back-and-forth conversations (Irving et al., 2018; Khan et al., 2024; Michael et al., 2023; Kenton et al., 2024). 近年来，另一种形式的多智能体辩论（有时称为多智能体审议）探索如何利用不同的 LLM 智能体，通过迭代讨论交流推理过程，从而得出更好的答案 (Chan et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Subramaniam et al., 2025)。 More recently, another form of multi-agent debate, sometimes referred to as multi-agent deliberation, investigates leveraging different LLM agents to surface better answers by having them exchange reasoning via iterative discussion (Chan et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Subramaniam et al., 2025). 这些研究大多聚焦于同质设置，即所有 LLM 智能体均使用相同的底层语言模型 (Du et al., 2023) 或能力相近的模型 (Yao et al., 2025)，并发现这种方法能够提高各类问答（Question-Answering，QA）任务的准确率。 Most of these studies focus on a homogeneous setting where all LLM agents utilize the same underlying language model (Du et al., 2023) or a model of similar ability (Yao et al., 2025), finding that this approach enhances accuracy across various Question-Answering (QA) tasks.
Estornell and Liu (2024) 考察了辩论框架中观点多样性的理论性质及其影响，并报告了一种“多数人的暴政”效应。 Estornell and Liu (2024) examine the theoretical properties and effects of opinion diversity within the debate framework, reporting a “tyranny of the majority” effect. 他们发现，如果多数智能体给出相同答案，无论该答案是否正确，少数智能体都倾向于随之附和，从而形成回音室效应。 They found that if the majority of agents provide the same answer – regardless of its correctness – minority agents tend to conform, creating an echo chamber effect. 此外，他们提出了一项理论结果，表明模型能力的多样性应当能够提高辩论的整体表现；我们则提供了实证证据，表明这一点在实践中往往并不成立。 Additionally, they propose a theoretical result indicating that diversity of model abilities should improve overall debate performance; we show empirical evidence that this is often not true in practice.
一些工作探索了性质或能力各异的智能体之间的辩论。 Some works explore debates between agents of diverse nature or abilities. 例如，Estornell et al. (2025); Subramaniam et al. (2025) 提出，训练 LLM 智能体以不同角色（行动者/生成者和批评者）开展协作辩论，并证明这种方法在推理基准测试中的表现可以超越以往的无监督辩论设置。 For instance, Estornell et al. (2025); Subramaniam et al. (2025) propose training LLM agents to debate collaboratively with distinct roles (actors/generators and critics), demonstrating that this approach can surpass previous unsupervised debate setups in reasoning benchmarks. 最后，Amayuelas et al. (2024) 等研究考察了在辩论过程中引入显式对手后，协作性是否仍然成立，其中该对手会主动设法降低性能。 Finally, studies such as Amayuelas et al. (2024) investigate whether the collaborative nature holds if an explicit adversary is introduced into the debate process—where the adversary actively seeks to reduce performance. 这些工作表明，当智能体以建设性的方式相互质疑时，答案可以得到改善。 These works suggest that, when agents constructively challenge each other, answers can improve. 然而，其他研究提醒，当智能体更重视说服力而非真实性时，辩论可能失败。 However, other studies caution that debate can fail when agents emphasize persuasion over truth. Agarwal and Khanna (2025) 针对事实性问题（使用 TruthfulQA）引入了单轮辩论，其中一个智能体平静地陈述正确答案，另一个则自信且情绪激昂地给出错误答案。 Agarwal and Khanna (2025) introduce a single-round debate on factual questions (using TruthfulQA) where one agent states a true answer calmly and another delivers a confident, emotional false answer. 他们表明，LLM 裁判往往以较高的置信度选择具有说服力的错误说法，这说明生动但错误的论证可能压过正确的论证。 They show the LLM judge often chooses the persuasive falsehood with high confidence, suggesting that a vivid but incorrect argument can override a correct one. 这些结果凸显了一种风险：除非裁判经过良好校准，否则辩论可能放大虚张声势的言辞，重现人类社会中的错误信息传播情境。 These results highlight a risk: unless the judge is well-calibrated, debate may amplify bluster, mirroring human misinformation scenarios. Yao et al. (2025) 重点研究了辩论情境中的迎合行为，表明随着辩论的推进，智能体之间的分歧率会下降，而且这一现象与性能下降相关。 Yao et al. (2025) focus on investigating sycophancy in the debate setting, showing that agent disagreement rate decreases as debate progresses, and that this observation is correlated with performance degradation.
协作式多智能体框架。 Collaborative multi-agent frameworks. 除显式辩论之外，近年来的许多系统也假定 LLM 之间的协作能够改善推理 (Li et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024; Tran et al., 2025)。 Beyond explicit debates, many recent systems assume collaboration among LLMs improves reasoning (Li et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024; Tran et al., 2025). 例如，Wu et al. (2023) 提供了一个通用的多智能体对话框架：开发者可以定义多个智能体（助手、用户代理、工具等），使其自主对话以解决复杂任务。 For instance, Wu et al. (2023) provides a general multi-agent conversation framework: developers can define many agents (assistant, user-proxy, tools, etc.) that autonomously chat to solve complex tasks. 与单个 LLM 相比，这些基于角色的去中心化系统通常能够产生更丰富的交互（例如多轮规划）。 These role-based and decentralized systems often yield richer interactions (e.g. multi-turn planning) than a single LLM alone.
顺序修订。 Sequential revision. 在推理时以顺序方式开展交互式推理的框架，被用于自我改进（self-refinement）(Madaan et al., 2023) 或自一致性（self-consistency）(Wang et al., 2022)。 Frameworks that utilize interactive reasoning at inference time in a sequential manner are employed for either self-refinement (Madaan et al., 2023) or self-consistency (Wang et al., 2022). 自我改进是指根据先前的输出，迭代修订或调整模型的回答，提示模型有意识地反思已有回答并纠正其中的错误 (Kamoi et al., 2024)。 Self-refinement involves iteratively revising or adapting a model’s responses based on previous outputs, prompting the model to intentionally reflect on its existing responses and correct any mistakes (Kamoi et al., 2024). 基于自一致性的工作通常会显式地并行运行多条推理路径。 Works based on self-consistency often explicitly run multiple reasoning paths in parallel. 例如，Wang et al. (2023) 采样生成大量独立的思维链答案，并选择其中最常见的答案。 For example, Wang et al. (2023) sample numerous independent chain-of-thought answers and select the most common answer. 通过最大限度地减少不确定性，汇总这些多样化的推理路径显著提高了算术和常识问答的准确率。 This aggregation of diverse reasoning paths significantly enhances the accuracy of arithmetic and commonsense QA by minimizing uncertainty. He et al. (2025) 扩展了这一思路：他们创建了多组由“反应型”和“反思型”智能体组成的配对，每组探索不同的推理路径，然后使用一个独立的总结器汇总这些路径。 He et al. (2025) extend this idea: they spawn multiple “reactive” and “reflection” agent pairs, each exploring a different reasoning path, and then use a separate summarizer to aggregate them. 类似地，Yang et al. (2025) 构建了一个去中心化的多智能体规划器，其中每个 LLM 智能体都维护自己的记忆（一个层次化知识图谱），并通过结构化提示进行通信。 Likewise, Yang et al. (2025) build a decentralized multi-agent planner where each LLM agent maintains its own memory (a hierarchical knowledge graph) and communicates via structured prompts. 他们发现，与单个智能体相比，这些协作智能体达成目标所需的步骤减少了 60%，凸显了结构化协作的价值。 They find that these collaborative agents reach goals with 60% fewer steps than a lone agent, underscoring the value of structured cooperation.
总体而言，已有研究表明，多智能体辩论与协作能够增强推理能力，但也揭示了关键的失效模式：评判者可能被修辞蒙蔽，而类似人类的互动模式可能使群体输出产生偏差。 Collectively, prior work shows that multi-agent debate and collaboration can amplify reasoning abilities, but also highlights key failure modes: judges may be fooled by rhetoric, and human-like dynamics can bias group outputs. 我们的工作以这些认识为基础，将分析扩展到异构辩论设置，并深入探究在各种任务中影响辩论何时有助于或有损于 LLM 表现的潜在因素。 Our work builds on these insights by expanding the analysis to heterogeneous debate settings, then performing a deep dive into the potential factors affecting when debate helps – or hurts – LLM performance on a variety of tasks.
3 设置：多智能体辩论3 Setting: Multi-agent debate
令 $\mathcal{Q}$ 表示与某项任务相关的问题数据集，其中该任务中的每个问题 $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ 都是自然语言文本。 Let $\mathcal{Q}$ denote a dataset of questions related to a task, where each question $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ in the task is natural language text. 目标是为任意给定的输入问题 $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ 生成答案 $a\in\mathcal{A}$，其中 $\mathcal{A}$ 是该任务的可能答案集合。 The objective is to generate an answer $a\in\mathcal{A}$ for any given input question $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ , where $\mathcal{A}$ is the set of possible answers for that task. 我们假设每个问题 $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ 都存在一个真实答案 $a^{\star}\in\mathcal{A}$，并用 $f^{gt}:\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ 表示这一映射，即 $f^{gt}(q)=a^{\star}$。 We assume that there exists a ground truth answer $a^{\star}\in\mathcal{A}$ for each question $q\in\mathcal{Q}$ which is denoted by $f^{gt}:\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ , i.e., $f^{gt}(q)=a^{\star}$ .
单智能体设置：在单智能体设置中，每个智能体使用底层 LLM $l:\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ 生成答案 $a\sim l(q)$，其中 $a\in\mathcal{A}$ 表示 LLM 针对问题 $q$ 生成的答案。 Single-Agent Setting: In the single agent setting, each agent uses an underlying LLM $l:\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ to generate answer $a\sim l(q)$ where $a\in\mathcal{A}$ denotes the answer generated by the LLM in response to the question $q$ . 主要关注的指标是准确率，其计算方式为 $\frac{1}{|\mathcal{Q}|}\sum_{q\in\mathcal{Q}}\mathbbm{1}[l(q)=f^{gt}(q)]$。 The main metric of interest is accuracy which is calculated as $\frac{1}{|\mathcal{Q}|}\sum_{q\in\mathcal{Q}}\mathbbm{1}[l(q)=f^{gt}(q)]$ .
通常，除了输入问题外，还会向 LLM 输入各种针对任务的提示，以生成答案。 Usually, various task specific prompts are also given as input to the LLM in addition to the input question for generating an answer. 令 $\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}$ 表示针对任务的提示，则相应的 LLM 可描述为 $l_{\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}}:\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}\times\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ 或 $a\sim l(\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}(q))$。 Let $\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}$ denote the task-specific prompt, then the resulting LLM can be described as $l_{\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}}:\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}\times\mathcal{Q}\rightarrow\mathcal{A}$ or $a\sim l(\mathcal{P}_{\text{T}}(q))$ .

6 辩论的失效模式6 Failure Modes of Debate
多智能体辩论旨在利用分歧来改善推理——鼓励模型通过交互完善各自的论证，并最终收敛于正确结论。 Multi-agent debate is intended to improve reasoning by leveraging disagreement—encouraging models to refine their arguments through interaction and converge on correct conclusions. 然而，LLM 智能体之间的辩论往往无法使多数智能体就正确答案达成一致，即使至少有一个智能体最初的答案是正确的。 Yet, debates between LLM agents often fail to reach majority agreement on the true answer, even when at least one agent is initially correct. 探究这些失败为何发生，对于理解和设计此类系统都至关重要。 Examining why these failures occur is crucial for both understanding and designing these systems. 我们表明，还有许多其他因素会影响辩论的失效模式，本节将对此进行深入探讨。 We show that there are many other factors influencing failure modes of debate, which we explore in depth in this section. 这表明，影响辩论有效性的各因素之间存在更丰富、更复杂的相互作用。 This suggests a richer and more complex interplay of factors influencing the effectiveness of debate. 本节通过实证分析剖析这些动态过程，考察智能体何时以及如何修改答案、哪些社会情境与不良的答案修改相关，以及针对迎合行为（sycophancy）的显式干预能否切实改善辩论性能。 In this section, we dissect these dynamics empirically, examining when and how agents revise their answers, what social conditions are correlated with undesirable answer revisions, and whether an explicit intervention targeting sycophancy can meaningfully improve debate performance.

7 讨论7 Discussion
我们的发现挑战了一种普遍且直观的看法，即 AI 智能体之间的讨论总能改善推理。 Our findings challenge the prevailing and natural view that deliberation among AI agents will always improve reasoning. 事实上，我们的实验表明，多智能体辩论有时会降低性能：群体准确率往往随着辩论轮次的增加而下降。 In fact, our experiments reveal that multi-agent debate can sometimes degrade performance: group accuracy often declines over successive rounds of debate. 即使大多数智能体独立完成任务时表现良好，甚至较弱模型能够获取较强模型的推理过程，这一反直觉的趋势仍然存在。 This counterintuitive trend holds even when the majority of agents perform well individually on the task and even when weaker models have access to the reasoning of stronger models. 换言之，智能体之间进一步交流理由并不总能纠正错误，反而可能放大错误。 In other words, additional exchange of reasons between agents does not always correct mistakes; instead, it may amplify them.
我们研究了与辩论中这些失败模式相关并促成这些失败模式的若干因素，包括顺序修正、社会条件化（social conditioning）和迎合倾向。 We study a number of correlated and contributing factors to these failure modes in debate, including sequential revision, social conditioning, and sycophancy. 此外，我们通过所有实验展示了不同能力的模型在多智能体辩论中如何作出不同反应，以及任务或数据集的类型如何也能对辩论结果产生显著影响。 We additionally show across all experiments how models of different capabilities respond differently within multi-agent debate, and how the type of task or dataset can also have a significant influence on the results of debate. 我们发现，多智能体辩论的失败模式并非由某个简单的单一原因造成，而是可能受到多种因素的共同影响；其总体效果是，由不同模型组成的异质群体经常共同收敛到错误答案，从而可能抵消多智能体辩论的益处。 We find that beyond a simple single cause, multiple factors exist that likely contribute to failure modes in multi-agent debate, with the net effect that heterogeneous groups of models frequently converge on wrong answers together, potentially negating the benefits of multi-agent debate.
这些结果表明，简单的辩论协议可能会无意中放大错误，而非纠正错误，这凸显了采用更具原则性的方法开展多智能体辩论的必要性。 These results suggest that naive debate protocols can inadvertently amplify errors rather than correcting them, underscoring the need for more principled approaches to multi-agent debate. 未来的辩论框架应纳入优先促进批判性评估而非达成共识的机制，例如鼓励智能体评估其他智能体推理的合理性，引入置信度估计或可信度评分以依据专业能力对各方贡献赋予权重，以及奖励对主张的独立核验。 Future debate frameworks should incorporate mechanisms that promote critical evaluation over consensus – such as encouraging agents to assess the soundness of others’ reasoning, integrating confidence estimates or credibility scores to weight contributions by expertise, and rewarding independent verification of claims. 训练或激励方案还可以通过惩罚缺乏依据的从众行为，进一步抑制表面上的附和。 Training or incentive schemes might further discourage superficial agreement by penalizing unsupported conformity. 归根结底，提高辩论的稳健性要求引导模型不仅追求共识，还要就认知分歧展开建设性的讨论——在有充分理由时质疑其他智能体，并在社会压力下坚持有依据的信念。 Ultimately, improving the robustness of debate requires aligning models not merely to achieve consensus, but to engage in constructive epistemic disagreement – challenging peers when warranted and maintaining justified beliefs under social pressure.
