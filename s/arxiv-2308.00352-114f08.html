Abstract摘要
Remarkable progress has been made on automated problem solving through societies of agents based on large language models (LLMs). Existing LLM-based multi-agent systems can already solve simple dialogue tasks. Solutions to more complex tasks, however, are complicated through logic inconsistencies due to cascading hallucinations caused by naively chaining LLMs. Here we introduce MetaGPT, an innovative meta-programming framework incorporating efficient human workflows into LLM-based multi-agent collaborations. MetaGPT encodes Standardized Operating Procedures (SOPs) into prompt sequences for more streamlined workflows, thus allowing agents with human-like domain expertise to verify intermediate results and reduce errors. MetaGPT utilizes an assembly line paradigm to assign diverse roles to various agents, efficiently breaking down complex tasks into subtasks involving many agents working together. On collaborative software engineering benchmarks, MetaGPT generates more coherent solutions than previous chat-based multi-agent systems. Our project can be found at https://github.com/geekan/MetaGPT.
通过由基于大语言模型（LLMs）的智能体组成的群体，自动化问题求解已取得显著进展。现有的基于 LLM 的多智能体系统已经能够解决简单的对话任务。然而，简单地将 LLM 串联起来会引发级联幻觉，进而导致逻辑不一致，使更复杂任务的求解变得困难。本文提出 MetaGPT，这是一种创新的元编程框架，将高效的人类工作流程融入基于 LLM 的多智能体协作。MetaGPT 将标准化操作规程（SOPs）编码为提示序列，使工作流程更加精简顺畅，从而让具备类人领域专业知识的智能体能够验证中间结果并减少错误。MetaGPT 采用流水线范式，为不同智能体分配多样化的角色，高效地将复杂任务分解为由多个智能体共同完成的子任务。在协作式软件工程基准测试中，MetaGPT 生成的解决方案比以往基于聊天的多智能体系统更加连贯一致。我们的项目地址为 https://github.com/geekan/MetaGPT。

Autonomous agents utilizing Large Language Models (LLMs) offer promising opportunities to enhance and replicate human workflows. In real-world applications, however, existing systems (Park et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Cai et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023c; Li et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023b) tend to oversimplify the complexities. They struggle to achieve effective, coherent, and accurate problem-solving processes, particularly when there is a need for meaningful collaborative interaction (Chen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023a; Dong et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023a; Qian et al., 2023; Tang et al., 2023b; Hong et al., 2024).
利用大语言模型（LLMs）的自主智能体为改进和复现人类工作流程提供了充满前景的机会。然而，在实际应用中，现有系统 (Park et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Cai et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023c; Li et al., 2023; Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023b) 往往过度简化了其中的复杂性。这些系统难以实现有效、连贯且准确的问题求解过程，尤其是在需要实质性协作交互时 (Chen et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023a; Dong et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023a; Qian et al., 2023; Tang et al., 2023b; Hong et al., 2024)。
Through extensive collaborative practice, humans have developed widely accepted Standardized Operating Procedures (SOPs) across various domains (Belbin, 2012; Manifesto, 2001; DeMarco & Lister, 2013). These SOPs play a critical role in supporting task decomposition and effective coordination. Furthermore, SOPs outline the responsibilities of each team member, while establishing standards for intermediate outputs. Well-defined SOPs improve the consistent and accurate execution of tasks that align with defined roles and quality standards (Belbin, 2012; Manifesto, 2001; DeMarco & Lister, 2013; Wooldridge & Jennings, 1998). For instance, in a software company, Product Managers analyze competition and user needs to create Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) using a standardized structure, to guide the developmental process.
通过广泛的协作实践，人类已在各个领域形成了被普遍接受的标准化操作规程（SOPs）(Belbin, 2012; Manifesto, 2001; DeMarco & Lister, 2013)。这些 SOPs 在支持任务分解和有效协调方面发挥着关键作用。此外，SOPs 明确了每位团队成员的职责，同时为中间产出制定了标准。定义明确的 SOPs 能够提高任务执行的一致性和准确性，使其符合既定的角色分工和质量标准 (Belbin, 2012; Manifesto, 2001; DeMarco & Lister, 2013; Wooldridge & Jennings, 1998)。例如，在软件公司中，产品经理分析竞争情况和用户需求，按照标准化结构编写产品需求文档（PRDs），以指导开发过程。

Unlike other works (Li et al., 2023; Qian et al., 2023), MetaGPT requires agents to generate structured outputs, such as high-quality requirements documents, design artifacts, flowcharts, and interface specifications. The use of intermediate structured outputs significantly increases the success rate of target code generation. Because it helps maintain consistency in communication, minimizing ambiguities and errors during collaboration. 受这些理念启发，我们设计了一种名为 MetaGPT 的、基于 GPT 的元编程框架，该框架具有良好的发展前景，并显著受益于 SOPs。与其他工作 (Li et al., 2023; Qian et al., 2023) 不同，MetaGPT 要求智能体生成结构化输出，例如高质量的需求文档、设计产物、流程图和接口规范。使用结构化的中间输出能够显著提高目标代码生成的成功率。这是因为它有助于保持沟通的一致性，最大限度地减少协作过程中的歧义和错误。更形象地说，在 MetaGPT 模拟的公司中，所有员工都遵循严格且精简顺畅的工作流程，所有工作交接都必须符合特定的既定标准。这降低了 LLM 之间因闲聊而产生幻觉的风险，尤其是在角色扮演框架中，例如：“嗨，你好，最近怎么样？”——Alice（产品经理）；“很好！你吃过午饭了吗？”——Bob（架构师）。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Automatic Programming自动编程
The roots of automatic programming reach back deep into the previous century. In 1969, Waldinger & Lee (1969) introduced “PROW,” a system designed to accept program specifications written in predicate calculus, generate algorithms, and create LISP implementations (McCarthy, 1978). Balzer (1985) and Soloway (1986) made efforts to advance automatic programming and identified potential methods to achieve it. Recent approaches use natural language processing (NLP) techniques (Ni et al., 2023; Skreta et al., 2023; Feng et al., 2020; Li et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2018; Chen et al., 2021b; Zhang et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2023b; Tang et al., 2023a; Muennighoff et al., 2023). Automatic programming has grown into an industry delivering paid functions such as Microsoft Copilot. Lately, LLMs-based agents (Yao et al., 2022; Shinn et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023) have advanced automatic programming development. Among them, ReAct (Yao et al., 2022) and Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023) utilize a chain of thought prompts (Wei et al., 2022) to generate reasoning trajectories and action plans with LLMs. Both works demonstrate the effectiveness of the ReAct style loop of reasoning as a design paradigm for empowering automatic programming. Additionally, ToolFormer (Schick et al., 2023) and ToolLLM (Qin et al., 2023) can learn how to use external tools through simple APIs. The research most closely aligned with our work by Li et al. (2023) proposes a straightforward role-play framework for programming that involves communication between agents playing different roles. Qian et al. (2023) utilizes multiple agents for software development. Although existing papers (Li et al., 2023; Qian et al., 2023) have improved productivity, they have not fully tapped into effective workflows with structured output formats. This makes it harder to deal with complex software engineering issues.
自动编程的起源可追溯至上个世纪较早时期。1969 年，Waldinger & Lee (1969) 提出了“PROW”，该系统旨在接收用谓词演算编写的程序规约、生成算法，并创建 LISP 实现 (McCarthy, 1978)。Balzer (1985) 和 Soloway (1986) 致力于推动自动编程的发展，并确定了实现自动编程的潜在方法。近期的方法采用自然语言处理（NLP）技术 (Ni et al., 2023; Skreta et al., 2023; Feng et al., 2020; Li et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2018; Chen et al., 2021b; Zhang et al., 2023a; Liu et al., 2023b; Tang et al., 2023a; Muennighoff et al., 2023)。自动编程已发展成为一个产业，提供诸如 Microsoft Copilot 之类的付费功能。近来，基于 LLM 的智能体 (Yao et al., 2022; Shinn et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023) 推动了自动编程的发展。其中，ReAct (Yao et al., 2022) 和 Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023) 利用思维链提示 (Wei et al., 2022)，通过 LLM 生成推理轨迹和行动计划。这两项工作均证明，ReAct 风格的推理循环作为一种赋能自动编程的设计范式是有效的。此外，ToolFormer (Schick et al., 2023) 和 ToolLLM (Qin et al., 2023) 能够学习如何通过简单的 API 使用外部工具。与我们的工作最为接近的是 Li et al. (2023) 的研究，该研究提出了一个简单直接的编程角色扮演框架，其中扮演不同角色的智能体相互通信。Qian et al. (2023) 利用多个智能体进行软件开发。尽管现有论文 (Li et al., 2023; Qian et al., 2023) 提高了生产力，但它们尚未充分挖掘采用结构化输出格式的有效工作流程的潜力。这使得复杂的软件工程问题更难处理。
LLM-Based Multi-Agent Frameworks基于 LLM 的多智能体框架
Recently, LLM-based autonomous agents have gained tremendous interest in both industry and academia (Wang et al., 2023b; Zhou et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023b). Many works (Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023c; Du et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Akata et al., 2023; Tang et al., 2023b) have improved the problem-solving abilities of LLMs by integrating discussions among multiple agents. Stable-Alignment (Liu et al., 2023a) creates instruction datasets by deriving consensus on value judgments through interactions across a sandbox with LLM agents. Other works focus on sociological phenomena. For example, Generative Agents (Park et al., 2023) creates a “town” of 25 agents to study language interaction, social understanding, and collective memory. In the Natural Language-Based Society of Mind (NLSOM) (Zhuge et al., 2023), agents with different functions interact to solve complex tasks through multiple rounds of “mindstorms.” Cai et al. (2023) propose a model for cost reduction by combining large models as tool makers and small models as tool users.
近年来，基于 LLM 的自主智能体在工业界和学术界都引起了广泛关注 (Wang et al., 2023b; Zhou et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2023b)。许多工作 (Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2023c; Du et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Hao et al., 2023; Akata et al., 2023; Tang et al., 2023b) 通过引入多个智能体之间的讨论，提高了 LLM 解决问题的能力。Stable-Alignment (Liu et al., 2023a) 通过 LLM 智能体在沙盒中的交互，就价值判断达成共识，从而创建指令数据集。其他工作则关注社会学现象。例如，Generative Agents (Park et al., 2023) 创建了一个由 25 个智能体组成的“小镇”，以研究语言交互、社会理解和集体记忆。在基于自然语言的心智社会（NLSOM）(Zhuge et al., 2023) 中，具有不同功能的智能体通过多轮“心智风暴”相互交互，以解决复杂任务。Cai et al. (2023) 提出了一种降低成本的模型，将作为工具制造者的大模型与作为工具使用者的小模型相结合。
Some works emphasize cooperation and competition related to planning and strategy (Bakhtin et al., 2022); others propose LLM-based economies (Zhuge et al., 2023). These works focus on open-world human behavior simulation, while MetaGPT aims to introduce human practice into multi-agents frameworks. Besides, LLM-based agents face the challenges of “assistant repeated instruction” or “infinite loop of message” (Talebirad & Nadiri, 2023; Li et al., 2023). These challenges become more urgent in task-oriented collaborations, which require consistent and mutually beneficial interactions (Elazar et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2022; Jiang et al., 2023). This motivates our focus on applying advanced concepts such as Standard Operating Procedures in software development to multi-agent frameworks.
一些研究强调与规划和策略相关的合作与竞争（Bakhtin et al., 2022）；另一些研究则提出了基于 LLM 的经济体（Zhuge et al., 2023）。这些研究侧重于开放世界中的人类行为模拟，而 MetaGPT 旨在将人类实践引入多智能体框架。此外，基于 LLM 的智能体还面临“助手重复指令”或“消息无限循环”等挑战（Talebirad & Nadiri, 2023; Li et al., 2023）。在面向任务的协作中，这些挑战变得更加紧迫，因为此类协作要求一致且互利的交互（Elazar et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2022; Jiang et al., 2023）。这促使我们着重将软件开发中的标准操作程序（Standard Operating Procedures）等先进理念应用于多智能体框架。

3.1 Agents in Standard Operating Procedures3.1 标准操作程序中的智能体
Specialization of Roles角色专业化分工
Unambiguous role specialization enables the breakdown of complex work into smaller and more specific tasks. Solving complex tasks or problems often requires the collaboration of agents with diverse skills and expertise, each contributing specialized outputs tailored to specific issues.
明确的角色专业化分工能够将复杂工作分解为更小、更具体的任务。解决复杂任务或问题通常需要具有不同技能和专业知识的智能体相互协作，每个智能体都针对特定问题提供专业化的输出。
In a software company, a Product Manager typically conducts business-oriented analysis and derives insights, while a software engineer is responsible for programming. We define five roles in our software company: Product Manager, Architect, Project Manager, Engineer, and QA Engineer, as shown in Figure 1. In MetaGPT, we specify the agent’s profile, which includes their name, profile, goal, and constraints for each role. We also initialize the specific context and skills for each role. For instance, a Product Manager can use web search tools, while an Engineer can execute code, as shown in Figure 2. All agents adhere to the React-style behavior as described in Yao et al. (2022).
在软件公司中，产品经理通常负责面向业务的分析并从中提炼洞见，而软件工程师则负责编程。我们在软件公司中定义了五种角色：产品经理、架构师、项目经理、工程师和质量保证工程师，如图 1 所示。在 MetaGPT 中，我们为每种角色指定智能体的配置，其中包括名称、角色简介、目标和约束。我们还为每种角色初始化特定的上下文和技能。例如，产品经理可以使用网络搜索工具，而工程师可以执行代码，如图 2 所示。所有智能体都遵循 Yao et al. (2022) 所描述的 React 风格行为。

3.2 Communication Protocol3.2 通信协议
Structured Communication Interfaces结构化通信接口
Most current LLM-based multi-agent frameworks (Li et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023a; Park et al., 2023) utilize unconstrained natural language as a communication interface.
目前大多数基于 LLM 的多智能体框架（Li et al., 2023; Zhuge et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023a; Park et al., 2023）都使用不受约束的自然语言作为通信接口。
However, despite the versatility of natural language, a question arises: does pure natural language communication suffice for solving complex tasks? For example, in the telephone game (or Chinese whispers)[fn2], after several rounds of communication, the original information may be quite distorted. Inspired by human social structures, we propose using structured communication to formulate the communication of agents. We establish a schema and format for each role and request that individuals provide the necessary outputs based on their specific role and context.
然而，尽管自然语言用途广泛，一个问题随之而来：仅靠纯自然语言通信是否足以解决复杂任务？例如，在传话游戏（telephone game，也称 Chinese whispers）[fn2]中，经过几轮交流后，原始信息可能会发生相当严重的失真。受人类社会结构的启发，我们提出使用结构化通信来规范智能体之间的通信。我们为每个角色制定模式（schema）和格式，并要求各个智能体根据自身的具体角色和上下文提供必要的输出。

These contain system module design and interaction sequences, which serve as important deliverables for Engineers. Publish-Subscribe Mechanism发布-订阅机制
Sharing information is critical in collaboration. For instance, Architects and Engineers often need to reference PRDs. However, communicating this information each time in a one-to-one manner, as indicated by previous work (Li et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023a), can complicate the communication topology, resulting in inefficiencies.
信息共享在协作中至关重要。例如，架构师和工程师经常需要参考产品需求文档（PRDs）。然而，如先前工作（Li et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2023a）所示，每次都以一对一的方式传递这些信息，会使通信拓扑复杂化，导致效率低下。
To address this challenge, a viable approach is to store information in a global message pool. As shown in Figure 2 (left), we introduce a shared message pool that allows all agents to exchange messages directly. These agents not only publish their structured messages in the pool but also access messages from other entities transparently. Any agent can directly retrieve required information from the shared pool, eliminating the need to inquire about other agents and await their responses. This enhances communication efficiency.
为应对这一挑战，一种可行的方法是将信息存储在全局消息池中。如图 2（左）所示，我们引入了一个共享消息池，使所有智能体都能直接交换消息。这些智能体不仅在消息池中发布自己的结构化消息，还能透明地访问其他实体的消息。任何智能体都可以直接从共享池中检索所需信息，无须向其他智能体询问并等待其响应。这提高了通信效率。
Sharing all information with every agent can lead to information overload. During task execution, an agent typically prefers to receive only task-related information and avoid distractions through irrelevant details. Effective management and dissemination of this information play a crucial role. We offer a simple and effective solution-subscription mechanism (in Figure 2 (left)). Instead of relying on dialogue, agents utilize role-specific interests to extract relevant information. They can select information to follow based on their role profiles. In practical implementations, an agent activates its action only after receiving all its prerequisite dependencies. As illustrated in Figure 3, the Architect mainly focuses on PRDs provided by the Product Manager, while documents from roles such as the QA Engineer might be of lesser concern.
向每个智能体共享全部信息可能导致信息过载。在执行任务时，智能体通常倾向于只接收与任务相关的信息，并避免受到无关细节的干扰。有效地管理和传播这些信息至关重要。我们提供了一种简单而有效的解决方案——订阅机制（见图 2（左））。智能体根据各自角色所关注的内容提取相关信息，而不是依赖对话。它们可以根据自身的角色描述选择要关注的信息。在实际实现中，智能体只有在收到其所需的全部前置依赖项后，才会启动相应行动。如图 3 所示，架构师主要关注产品经理提供的 PRDs，而对质量保证工程师等角色提供的文档可能关注较少。
3.3 Iterative programming with executable feedback3.3 基于可执行反馈的迭代编程
In daily programming tasks, the processes of debugging and optimization play important roles. However, existing methods often lack a self-correction mechanism, which leads to unsuccessful code generation. Previous work introduced non-executable code review and self-reflection (Zhao et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2022; Shinn et al., 2023; Dong et al., 2023). However, they still face challenges in ensuring code executability and runtime correctness.
在日常编程任务中，调试和优化过程发挥着重要作用。然而，现有方法往往缺乏自我纠正机制，导致代码生成失败。先前的工作引入了不执行代码的代码审查和自我反思（Zhao et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2022; Shinn et al., 2023; Dong et al., 2023）。然而，这些方法在确保代码可执行性和运行时正确性方面仍面临挑战。
Our first MetaGPT implementations overlooked certain errors during the review process, due to LLM hallucinations (Manakul et al., 2023). To overcome this, after initial code generation, we introduce an executable feedback mechanism to improve the code iteratively. More specifically, as shown in Figure 2, the Engineer is asked to write code based on the original product requirements and design.
在 MetaGPT 的最初实现中，由于 LLM 幻觉（Manakul et al., 2023），我们在审查过程中遗漏了某些错误。为克服这一问题，我们在初步生成代码后引入了可执行反馈机制，以迭代改进代码。更具体地说，如图 2 所示，工程师被要求根据原始产品需求和设计编写代码。
This enables the Engineer to continuously improve code using its own historical execution and debugging memory. To obtain additional information, the Engineer writes and executes the corresponding unit test cases, and subsequently receives the test results. If satisfactory, additional development tasks are initiated. Otherwise the Engineer debugs the code before resuming programming. This iterative testing process continues until the test is passed or a maximum of 3 retries is reached.
这使工程师能够利用自身以往执行和调试的记忆，持续改进代码。为获取更多信息，工程师编写并执行相应的单元测试用例，随后接收测试结果。如果结果令人满意，则启动额外的开发任务。否则，工程师会先调试代码，然后再继续编程。这一迭代测试过程会持续进行，直到测试通过或达到最多 3 次重试的上限。

4.1 Experimental setting4.1 实验设置
Datasets数据集
We use two public benchmarks, HumanEval (Chen et al., 2021a) and MBPP (Austin et al., 2021), and a self-generated, more challenging software development benchmark named SoftwareDev: (1) HumanEval includes 164 handwritten programming tasks. These tasks encompass function specifications, descriptions, reference codes, and tests. (2) MBPP consists of 427 Python tasks. These tasks cover core concepts and standard library features and include descriptions, reference codes, and automated tests. (3) Our SoftwareDev dataset is a collection of 70 representative examples of software development tasks, each with its own task prompt (see Table 8). These tasks have diverse scopes (See Figure 5), such as mini-games, image processing algorithms, data visualization. They offer a robust testbed for authentic development tasks. Contrary to previous datasets (Chen et al., 2021a; Austin et al., 2021), SoftwareDev focuses on the engineering aspects. In the comparisons, we randomly select seven representative tasks for evaluation.
我们使用了两个公开基准 HumanEval (Chen et al., 2021a) 和 MBPP (Austin et al., 2021)，以及一个自行生成、更具挑战性的软件开发基准 SoftwareDev：(1) HumanEval 包含 164 个手工编写的编程任务。这些任务包含函数规范、描述、参考代码和测试。(2) MBPP 由 427 个 Python 任务组成。这些任务涵盖核心概念和标准库功能，并包含描述、参考代码和自动化测试。(3) 我们的 SoftwareDev 数据集收集了 70 个具有代表性的软件开发任务示例，每个示例都有各自的任务提示（见表 8）。这些任务涉及多种领域（见图 5），例如小游戏、图像处理算法和数据可视化。它们为真实开发任务提供了可靠的测试平台。与以往的数据集 (Chen et al., 2021a; Austin et al., 2021) 不同，SoftwareDev 侧重于工程方面。在比较中，我们随机选取了七个具有代表性的任务进行评估。
Evaluation Metrics评估指标
For HuamnEval and MBPP, we follow the unbiased version of $\operatorname{Pass}@k$ as presented by (Chen et al., 2021a; Dong et al., 2023), to evaluate the functional accuracy of the top-k generated codes: $\operatorname{Pass}@k={\mathbb{E}}_{\text{Problems}}\left[1-\frac{{\binom{n-c}{k}}}{{\binom{n}{k}}}\right]$ .
对于 HuamnEval 和 MBPP，我们采用 (Chen et al., 2021a; Dong et al., 2023) 提出的 $\operatorname{Pass}@k$ 无偏版本，评估排名前 k 的生成代码的功能正确性：$\operatorname{Pass}@k={\mathbb{E}}_{\text{Problems}}\left[1-\frac{{\binom{n-c}{k}}}{{\binom{n}{k}}}\right]$。
For SoftwareDev, we prioritize practical use and evaluate performance through human evaluations (A, E) or statistical analysis (B, C, D): (A) Executability: this metric rates code from 1 (failure/non-functional) to 4 (flawless). ‘1’ is for non-functional, ‘2’ for runnable but imperfect, ‘3’ for nearly perfect, and ‘4’ for flawless code. (B) Cost: the cost evaluations here include the (1) running time, (2) token usage, and (3) expenses. (C) Code Statistics: this includes (1) code files, (2) lines of code per file, and (3) total code lines. (D) Productivity: basically, it is defined as the number of token usage divided by the number of lines of code, which refers to the consumption of tokens per code line. (E) Human Revision Cost: refers to times of manual code corrections, which tackle problems like package import errors, incorrect class names, or incomplete reference paths. Typically, each correction involves up to 3 lines of code.
对于 SoftwareDev，我们优先考虑实际使用，并通过人工评估 (A, E) 或统计分析 (B, C, D) 来评估性能：(A) 可执行性：该指标对代码的评分范围为 1（失败／无法正常工作）至 4（完美无缺）。‘1’ 表示代码无法正常工作，‘2’ 表示可以运行但不完善，‘3’ 表示接近完美，‘4’ 表示完美无缺。(B) 成本：此处的成本评估包括 (1) 运行时间、(2) token 用量和 (3) 费用。(C) 代码统计：包括 (1) 代码文件数、(2) 每个文件的代码行数和 (3) 代码总行数。(D) 生产率：其基本定义为 token 用量除以代码行数，即每行代码消耗的 token 数。(E) 人工修改成本：指手动修正代码的次数，这些修正用于解决包导入错误、类名错误或引用路径不完整等问题。通常，每次修正涉及的代码不超过 3 行。

5 Conclusion5 结论
This work introduces MetaGPT, a novel meta-programming framework that leverages SOPs to enhance the problem-solving capabilities of multi-agent systems based on Large Language Models (LLMs). MetaGPT models a group of agents as a simulated software company, analogous to simulated towns (Park et al., 2023) and the Minecraft Sandbox in Voyager (Wang et al., 2023a). MetaGPT leverages role specialization, workflow management, and efficient sharing mechanisms such as message pools and subscriptions, rendering it a flexible and portable platform for autonomous agents and multi-agent frameworks. It uses an executable feedback mechanism to enhance code generation quality during runtime. In extensive experiments, MetaGPT achieves state-of-the-art performance on multiple benchmarks. The successful integration of human-like SOPs inspires future research on human-inspired techniques for artificial multi-agent systems. We also view our work as an early attempt to regulate LLM-based multi-agent frameworks. See also the outlook (Appendix A).
本研究提出了 MetaGPT，这是一种新颖的元编程框架，利用 SOPs 增强基于大语言模型（LLMs）的多智能体系统的问题求解能力。MetaGPT 将一组智能体建模为一家模拟软件公司，类似于模拟小镇（Park et al., 2023）和 Voyager 中的 Minecraft 沙盒（Wang et al., 2023a）。MetaGPT 利用角色专业化、工作流程管理以及消息池和订阅等高效共享机制，成为一个面向自主智能体和多智能体框架的灵活且可移植的平台。它使用执行反馈机制，在运行期间提高代码生成质量。在广泛的实验中，MetaGPT 在多个基准上达到了当前最先进的性能。成功融入类人的 SOPs，为未来研究受人类启发的人工多智能体系统技术提供了启示。我们也将这项工作视为规范基于 LLM 的多智能体框架的一次早期尝试。另请参见展望（附录 A）。

A.1 Self-Improvement MechanismsA.1 自我改进机制
One limitation of the MetaGPT version in the main text of this paper is that each software project is executed independently. However, through active teamwork, a software development team should learn from the experience gained by developing each project, thus becoming more compatible and successful over time.
本文正文中所述 MetaGPT 版本的一个局限在于，每个软件项目都是独立执行的。然而，通过积极的团队协作，软件开发团队应当从每个项目的开发经验中学习，从而随着时间的推移，配合更加默契，取得更大的成功。
This is somewhat related to the idea of recursive self-improvement, first informally proposed in 1965 (Good, 1965), with first concrete implementations since 1987 (Schmidhuber, 1987; Schmidhuber, 1993b; Schmidhuber et al., 1998), culminating in the concept of mathematically optimal self-referential self-improvers (Schmidhuber, 2003; Schmidhuber, 2009). Generally speaking, a system should learn from experience in the real world, and meta-learn better learning algorithms from experiences of learning, and meta-meta-learn better meta-learning algorithms from experiences of meta-learning, etc., without any limitations except those of computability and physics.
这与递归自我改进的思想有一定关联；该思想于 1965 年首次以非正式形式提出 (Good, 1965)，自 1987 年起出现了首批具体实现 (Schmidhuber, 1987; Schmidhuber, 1993b; Schmidhuber et al., 1998)，最终发展为在数学意义上最优的自指式自我改进系统这一概念 (Schmidhuber, 2003; Schmidhuber, 2009)。一般而言，系统应当从现实世界的经验中学习，从学习经验中通过元学习获得更好的学习算法，从元学习经验中通过元元学习获得更好的元学习算法，依此类推，除可计算性和物理规律的限制外，不受任何限制。
More recent, somewhat related work leverages the reasoning ability of Large Language Models (LLMs) and recursively improves prompts of LLMs, to improve performance on certain downstream tasks (Fernando et al., 2023; Zelikman et al., 2023), analogous to the adaptive prompt engineer of 2015 (Schmidhuber, 2015) where one neural network learns to generate sequence of queries or prompts for another pre-trained neural network whose answers may help the first network to learn new tasks more quickly.
较近期的一些相关工作利用大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）的推理能力，递归地改进 LLMs 的提示，以提升其在某些下游任务上的表现 (Fernando et al., 2023; Zelikman et al., 2023)；这类似于 2015 年提出的自适应提示工程师 (Schmidhuber, 2015)，其中一个神经网络学习为另一个预训练神经网络生成一系列查询或提示，后者的回答可能帮助前者更快地学习新任务。
In our present work, we also explore a self-referential mechanism that recursively modifies the constraint prompts of agents based on information they observe during software development. Our initial implementation works as follows. Prior to each project, every agent in the software company reviews previous feedback and makes necessary adjustments to their constraint prompts. This enables them to continuously learn from past project experiences and enhance the overall multi-agent system by improving each individual in the company. We first establish a handover feedback action for each agent. This action is responsible for critically summarizing the information received during the development of previous projects and integrating this information in an updated constraint prompt. The summarized information is stored in long-term memory such that it can be inherited by future constraint prompt updates. When initiating a new project, each agent starts with a react action. Each agent evaluates the received feedback and summarizes how they can improve in a constraint prompt.
在当前工作中，我们还探索了一种自指机制，根据智能体在软件开发过程中观察到的信息，递归地修改其约束提示。我们的初步实现如下。在每个项目开始之前，软件公司中的每个智能体都会回顾以往的反馈，并对自身的约束提示作出必要的调整。这使它们能够持续从以往的项目经验中学习，并通过改进公司中的每个个体来增强整个多智能体系统。我们首先为每个智能体设置一个交接反馈动作。该动作负责以批判性视角总结在以往项目开发过程中收到的信息，并将这些信息整合到更新后的约束提示中。总结后的信息存储在长期记忆中，以便未来更新约束提示时能够继承这些信息。启动新项目时，每个智能体都首先执行一个 react 动作。每个智能体都会评估收到的反馈，并在约束提示中总结自身可以如何改进。
One current limitation is that these summary-based optimizations only modify constraints in the specialization of roles (Sec. 3.1) rather than structured communication interfaces in communication protocols (Sec. 3.2). Future advancements are yet to be explored.
目前的一个局限是，这些基于总结的优化仅修改角色专门化中的约束（第 3.1 节），而不修改通信协议中的结构化通信接口（第 3.2 节）。未来的进一步改进仍有待探索。
A.2 Multi-Agent EconomiesA.2 多智能体经济体
In real-world teamwork, the interaction processes are often not hardcoded. For example, in a software company, the collaboration SOP may change dynamically.
在现实世界的团队协作中，交互过程往往并非硬编码的。例如，在软件公司中，协作 SOP 可能会动态变化。
One implementation of such self-organization is discussed in the paper on a “Natural Language-Based Society of Mind” (NLSOM) (Zhuge et al., 2023), which introduced the idea of an “Economy of Minds” (EOM), a Reinforcement Learning (RL) framework for societies of LLMs and other agents. Instead of using standard RL techniques to optimize the total reward of the system through modifications of neural network parameters, EOMs use the principles of supply and demand in free markets to assign credit (money) to those agents that contribute to economic success (reward).
关于“基于自然语言的心智社会”（Natural Language-Based Society of Mind，NLSOM）的论文 (Zhuge et al., 2023) 讨论了此类自组织的一种实现，并提出了“心智经济体”（Economy of Minds，EOM）的思想，这是一个面向由 LLMs 和其他智能体组成的社会的强化学习（Reinforcement Learning，RL）框架。EOMs 利用自由市场中的供需原则，将功劳（货币）分配给那些为经济成功（奖励）作出贡献的智能体，而非采用标准 RL 技术，通过修改神经网络参数来优化系统的总奖励。

E.1 Deep-seated challengesE.1 深层挑战
MetaGPT also alleviates or solves these challenges with its unique designs:
MetaGPT 还通过其独特的设计缓解或解决了以下挑战：
Use Context Efficiently高效利用上下文
Two sub-challenges are present. First, unfolding short natural language descriptions accurately to eliminate ambiguity. Second, maintaining information validity in lengthy contexts, enables LLMs to concentrate on relevant data without distraction.
这包含两个子挑战。首先，准确展开简短的自然语言描述，以消除歧义。其次，在冗长的上下文中保持信息的有效性，使 LLM 能够专注于相关数据而不受干扰。
Reduce Hallucinations减少幻觉
Using LLMs to generate entire software programs faces code hallucination problems—-including incomplete implementation of functions, missing dependencies, and potential undiscovered bugs, which may be more serious. LLMs often struggle with software generation due to vague task definitions. Focusing on granular tasks like requirement analysis and package selection offers guided thinking, which LLMs lack in broad task solving.
使用 LLM 生成完整的软件程序会面临代码幻觉问题，包括函数实现不完整、依赖项缺失，以及可能更为严重的、尚未发现的潜在缺陷。由于任务定义模糊，LLM 在软件生成方面往往面临困难。专注于需求分析和软件包选择等细粒度任务，可以为思考提供引导，而 LLM 在解决宽泛任务时缺乏这种引导。
E.2 Information OverloadE.2 信息过载
In MetaGPT, we use a global message pool and a subscription mechanism to address “information overload,” which refers to the problem of receiving excessive or irrelevant information. This issue is dependent on specific applications. MetaGPT employs a message pool to streamline communication, ensuring efficiency. Additionally, a subscription mechanism filters out irrelevant contexts, enhancing the relevance and utility of the information. This design is particularly crucial in software design scenarios and standard operating procedures (SOPs) where effective communication is essential.
在 MetaGPT 中，我们使用全局消息池和订阅机制来解决“信息过载”问题，即接收过量信息或无关信息的问题。这一问题取决于具体应用。MetaGPT 采用消息池来简化通信流程，确保效率。此外，订阅机制会过滤掉无关的上下文，从而提高信息的相关性和实用性。这种设计在有效沟通至关重要的软件设计场景和标准操作规程（SOPs）中尤为关键。
