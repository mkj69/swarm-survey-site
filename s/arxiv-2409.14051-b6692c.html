摘要。Abstract.
近年来，大型语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）在各类自然语言处理（NLP）任务中展现出卓越的能力，包括复杂逻辑推理、数学问题求解和多步决策。 In recent years, Large Language Models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities across diverse NLP tasks, including complex logical reasoning, mathematical problem-solving, and multi-step decision-making. 大量研究探索了如何增强逻辑推理能力，提出了思维链（Chain-of-Thought）、结合自一致性的思维链（Chain-of-Thought with Self-Consistency）、思维树（Tree-Of-Thoughts）以及多智能体辩论等方法。 Extensive research has explored how to enhance the logical reasoning abilities such as Chain-of-Thought, Chain-of-Thought with Self-Consistency, Tree-Of-Thoughts, and multi-agent debates. 在多智能体辩论中，增加智能体数量和辩论轮数可以显著提升性能。 In the context of multi-agent debates, significant performance improvements can be achieved with an increasing number of agents and debate rounds. 然而，智能体数量和辩论轮数的增加会大幅提高辩论的词元（token）开销，从而限制多智能体辩论技术的可扩展性。 However, the escalation in the number of agents and debate rounds can drastically raise the tokens cost of debates, thereby limiting the scalability of the multi-agent debate technique. 为了更好地发挥多智能体辩论在逻辑推理任务中的优势，本文提出了一种显著降低多智能体辩论词元开销的方法。 To better harness the advantages of multi-agent debates in logical reasoning tasks, this paper proposes a method to significantly reduce token cost in multi-agent debates. 该方法将所有智能体划分为多个辩论小组，让智能体在各自的小组内进行辩论，并在小组之间共享阶段性辩论结果。 This approach involves dividing all agents into multiple debate groups, with agents engaging in debates within their respective groups and sharing interim debate results between groups. 在多个数据集上的对比实验表明，该方法可在辩论过程中将词元总量最多减少46.9%，同时有可能将准确率最多提高21.9%。 Comparative experiments across multiple datasets have demonstrated that this method can reduce the total tokens by up to 46.9% during debates and while potentially enhancing accuracy by as much as 21.9%. 我们的方法显著提升了多智能体辩论中交互的性能与效率。 Our method significantly enhances the performance and efficiency of interactions in the multi-agent debate.

2.1. 多智能体辩论2.1. Multi-agent Debate
在多智能体辩论（MAD）中，通过整合多个大语言模型（LLM，每个模型均视为一个独立智能体）并采用各种协作策略，智能体可以在多轮辩论中提出观点、审查其他智能体的结果并作出回应 (Chan et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2023; Terekhov et al., 2023)。 In the context of multi-agent debates (MAD), by integrating multiple LLMs (each treated as an individual agent) and using various collaboration strategies, agents can propose viewpoints, review, and respond to the results of other agents in multiple rounds of debates (Chan et al., 2023; Sun et al., 2023; Terekhov et al., 2023). MAD 的过程可概括如下： The process of MAD can be summarized as follows:
(i) 开始时，向每个智能体提供一个问题，由其独立生成回答； (i) At the beginning, each agent is provided with a question and generates an individual response;
(ii) 随后，这些回答构成每个智能体新的输入上下文，智能体据此生成新的回答； (ii) These responses then form the new input context for each agent, and the agents generate new responses;
(iii) 这一辩论过程重复多轮，最终答案通过多数投票获得。 (iii) This debate procedure is repeated over multiple rounds and the final answer is obtained through majority voting.
在整个多智能体辩论过程中，所有智能体都可以根据其他智能体的回答持续改进自身的回答。 Throughout multi-agent debate procedure, all agents can consistently improve their own responses based on the responses of other agents. 为缩短输入上下文的长度，(Du et al., 2023) 提出，在收集其他智能体的回答后，应先将这些回答汇总为摘要，再将其用作每个智能体新的输入上下文。 In order to reduce input context length, (Du et al., 2023) proposes that after collecting the responses from other agents, the responses should first be summarized and then used as the new input context for each agent. 图 2 展示了三个智能体开展两轮辩论的示例。 Figure 2 shows an example of two-round debates among three agents. 在第一轮中，每个智能体独立响应输入，其输出随后被收集并汇总为摘要。 In the first round, each agent independently responds to the input and their outputs are collected and summarized. 在第二轮中，每个智能体的输入都包含上一轮的摘要，这些摘要与提示相结合，以引导输出。 In the second round, each agent’s input includes summaries from the previous round, which are combined with a prompt to guide the output. 最终，所有智能体达成一致结论。 Ultimately, all agents reach a consensus conclusion.

3.1. GroupDebate3.1. GroupDebate
GroupDebate 框架组织 M 个基于 LLM 的智能体进行协作推理，这些智能体记为 $A=\{A_{i}|i=1,2,\cdots,M\}$，可随机划分为 $N$ 个组 $G=\{G_{j}|j=1,2,\cdots,N\}$，每组平均有 K 个智能体。 The GroupDebate framework orchestrates collaborative reasoning among M LLM-based agents, denoted as $A=\{A_{i}|i=1,2,\cdots,M\}$ , which can be randomly divided into $N$ groups $G=\{G_{j}|j=1,2,\cdots,N\}$ , with average K agents in each group. GroupDebate 将全部辩论轮次划分为 $S$ 个阶段，每个阶段包含 $R$ 轮。 The GroupDebate splits the total debate rounds into $S$ stages, with each stage encompassing $R$ rounds. 因此，总轮数 $T$ 可计算为 $T=S\times R$。 Thus, the total number of rounds $T$ can be calculated as $T=S\times R$ . 这种分层设计通过交替进行局部改进（组内）与全局综合（组间），实现对解空间的高效探索。 This hierarchical design enables efficient exploration of solution spaces through alternating phases of localized refinement (intra-group) and global synthesis (inter-group). 对于第 $s$ 个阶段的第 $r$ 轮，GroupDebate 选择以下流程之一： For the $s$ -th stage’s $r$ -th round, GroupDebate selects one of the following processes:
初始思考。 Initial Thinking. 若 $s=1$ 且 $r=1$（即第一个阶段的第一轮），则将初始问题提示 $Q$ 输入每个智能体。 If $s=1$ and $r=1$ (i.e., the first stage’s first round), we input the initial question prompt $Q$ to each agent.
组内辩论。 Intra-group Debate. 若 $r>1$，则将同组内其他智能体的输出用作每个智能体的输入。 If $r>1$ , we utilize the outputs from other agents within the same group as the input for each agent.
组间辩论。 Inter-group Debate. 若 $s>1$ 且 $r=1$，则将每个组最后一轮的输出汇总为一份摘要，并将其他组的摘要输入每个智能体。 If $s>1$ and $r=1$ , we merge the outputs from the last round of each group into a summary and input the summaries from other groups to each agent.
同时，受 (Sun et al., 2023) 启发，我们对其他组的回复进行摘要，并在组间辩论中限制每个智能体仅接收上一阶段的最新摘要。 Meanwhile, inspired by (Sun et al., 2023), we summarize the responses from other groups and restrict each agent to receive the latest summary from the previous stage in the inter-group debate. 在第 $S$ 个阶段的第 $R$ 轮结束后，所有智能体进行投票，最终结果由多数票决定。 After the $S$ -th stage’s $R$ -th round, all agents vote, and the ultimate result is determined by the majority selection. 6. 结论6. Conclusion
在本研究中，我们着力解决多智能体辩论系统中词元（token）效率这一关键挑战，该问题已成为扩展协同推理框架规模的主要瓶颈。 In this work, we address the critical challenge of token efficiency in multi-agent debate systems, which has emerged as a key bottleneck in scaling collaborative reasoning frameworks. 我们提出了一种新颖的 GroupDebate 方法，利用小组讨论缓解这一问题，同时促进多样化观点的形成。 We propose a novel GroupDebate method, which leverages the group discussion to mitigate this issue while fostering a diverse range of viewpoints. 具体而言，我们将所有参与的智能体划分为若干辩论小组，每个智能体既可以参与组内辩论，也可以参与组间观点交流。 Specifically, we divide all participating agents into several debate groups, where each agent can engage in both intra-group debates and inter-group exchanges of ideas. 在四个逻辑推理数据集上的实验结果表明，GroupDebate 能够显著降低多智能体辩论的词元成本，并提高其准确率。 Experimental results across four logical reasoning datasets demonstrate GroupDebate can significantly reduce token cost as well as enhance accuracy in multi-agent debates. 未来，我们将进一步探索关于小组讨论如何提高准确率的定理，并从理论上确定 GroupDebate 的最优设置。 In the future, we will further explore the theorem of how group discussion can improve accuracy and theoretically determine the optimal settings in GroupDebate.
7. 局限性7. Limitations
尽管 GroupDebate 在词元效率和推理准确率方面取得了显著进展，但仍存在若干局限性以及未来研究方向：首个主要局限性在于，我们仅从理论上分析了实现最优词元成本复杂度所需的 $N$ 和 $S$ 的约束条件，尚未深入探究 $N$ 和 $S$ 的最优设置，以及 GroupDebate 可能提高 MAD 准确率的内在原因。 While GroupDebate demonstrates significant advancements in token efficiency and reasoning accuracy, several limitations and avenues for future research remain: The first key limitation is that we only theoretically analyze the constraints of $N$ and $S$ required to achieve optimal token cost complexity but we have not delved into the optimal settings of $N$ and $S$ and the underlying reasons why GroupDebate can potentially improve the accuracy of MAD. 然而，确定 N 和 S 的最优值还需要考虑准确率，以便在相同词元成本下使其最大化；这一问题非常复杂，需要开展更多评估和实验，以推导 GroupDebate 准确率提升及其最优设置的理论依据。 However, determining the optimal values of N and S also requires considering accuracy to maximize it under the same token cost, which is very complex and necessitates more evaluations and experiments to deduce the theoretical basis for the enhancement of accuracy and optimal settings in GroupDebate. 此外，尽管 GroupDebate 能够显著降低多智能体辩论的词元成本，但其词元成本仍高于 CoT 等单智能体方法。 Furthermore, although GroupDebate can significantly reduce token cost in muti-agent debates, its token cost is still higher than single-agent methods like CoT. 有必要探索更多方法，在确保高准确率的同时进一步降低词元成本。 It is necessary to explore more ways to further reduce token cost while ensuring high accuracy.
