Abstract摘要
As multi-agent language model networks become widely deployed, predicting their collective behavior is a critical challenge. Classical distributed consensus theory relies on network topology to predict convergence, structurally assuming that agent updates are convex combinations, population means are conserved, and weights are content-independent. In this work, we demonstrate that these foundational assumptions fail for large language models (LLMs). By deterministically extracting continuous credence from token-level probabilities across eight instruction-tuned models and 73,080 controlled exchanges, we directly measure the empirical LLM update operator. We find that LLMs systematically violate convexity by updating outside the hull of observed opinions, exhibiting active repulsion (negative weights) that fundamentally breaks row-stochastic modeling and standard bounded-confidence repairs. Scaling these empirically fitted operators to simulations of 10,000 agents reveals that algebraic connectivity explains almost none of the final outcome variance. Instead, population dynamics are governed entirely by the model’s internal update bias and message framing. Consequently, populations of several models, including those with up to 7B parameters, reliably compound small local drifts to reach confident agreement on objectively false claims regardless of their network structure. Our results prove that classical graph-theoretic metrics are inadequate for LLM populations, highlighting a profound deployment risk where multi-agent deliberation predictably saturates in collective delusion.
随着多智能体语言模型网络得到广泛部署，预测其集体行为成为一项关键挑战。经典分布式共识理论依赖网络拓扑来预测收敛，其结构性假设是：智能体的更新为凸组合、群体均值保持不变，且权重与内容无关。在本研究中，我们证明，这些基础假设对于大语言模型（LLMs）并不成立。通过在八个经过指令微调的模型和 73,080 次受控交互中，从词元级概率中以确定性方式提取连续的置信度，我们直接测量了 LLM 的经验更新算子。我们发现，LLMs 的更新会超出所观测观点的凸包，从而系统性地违反凸性，并表现出主动排斥（负权重），这从根本上使行随机矩阵建模和标准的有界置信修正方法失效。将这些通过经验拟合得到的算子扩展至 10,000 个智能体的仿真后发现，代数连通度几乎无法解释最终结果的方差。相反，群体动力学完全由模型内部的更新偏置和消息框架所支配。因此，无论网络结构如何，多个模型的群体，包括参数量高达 7B 的模型群体，都会稳定地累积微小的局部漂移，最终对客观上错误的论断达成高度确信的共识。我们的结果证明，经典图论指标不足以刻画 LLM 群体，并揭示了一项严重的部署风险：多智能体审议会可预测地在集体妄信中达到饱和。
1 Introduction1 引言
Systems connecting multiple language model agents are increasingly common, raising the critical question of whether we can predict a group’s final consensus. Distributed consensus theory in network science offers a standard framework, suggesting that if agents repeatedly average their neighbors’ positions, the group converges at a rate determined by the graph’s algebraic connectivity ( $\lambda_{2}$ ) Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003). This makes network topology the primary focus for system designers.
连接多个语言模型智能体的系统日益普遍，这引出了一个关键问题：我们能否预测群体最终达成的共识。网络科学中的分布式共识理论提供了一个标准框架：如果智能体反复对邻居的立场取平均值，那么群体将以由图的代数连通度（ $\lambda_{2}$ ）决定的速率收敛 Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003)。这使网络拓扑成为系统设计者关注的重点。
However, this classical theory relies on three structural assumptions (also mentioned formally in 3): updates are weighted averages, population means are preserved, and weights depend strictly on the graph rather than message content. Human populations often violate these assumptions, prompting non-linear alternatives in opinion dynamics Friedkin and Johnsen (1990); Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000). We empirically test if language models similarly deviate by measuring the update operator directly rather than assuming its form. By tracking a model’s revised position across a grid of prior states, peer states, and message framings, we inspect the underlying rule and scale it to populations of $10^{4}$ agents.
然而，这一经典理论依赖三项结构性假设（第 3 节也给出了正式表述）：更新是加权平均，群体均值保持不变，且权重仅取决于图，而不取决于消息内容。人类群体经常违反这些假设，因此观点动力学领域提出了非线性的替代模型 Friedkin and Johnsen (1990); Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000)。我们通过直接测量更新算子，而非预先假定其形式，来实证检验语言模型是否也存在类似偏离。通过在先验状态、同伴状态和消息框架构成的网格上追踪模型修正后的立场，我们考察其底层规则，并将其扩展至由 $10^{4}$ 个智能体组成的群体。
Our findings show all three classical assumptions fail substantially. Notably, some updates carry a negative effective weight where agents move away from peers, a behavior impossible to represent with non-negative row-stochastic matrices. The standard bounded confidence repair does not fix this, because it still keeps every update inside the range of the opinions being averaged. At scale, $\lambda_{2}$ explains almost none of the outcome variance. Instead, each model’s own drift and the message wording drive the final state, and populations of four of the eight models we test, including a 7B model, converge to confident belief in false claims regardless of topology.
我们的研究结果表明，三项经典假设均在很大程度上不成立。值得注意的是，某些更新具有负的有效权重，表现为智能体的立场远离同伴，而这种行为无法用非负行随机矩阵表示。标准的有界置信修正方法无法解决这一问题，因为它仍然要求每次更新都处于参与平均的观点所界定的范围之内。在大规模情形下，$\lambda_{2}$ 几乎无法解释结果的方差。相反，每个模型自身的漂移和消息措辞决定了最终状态；在我们测试的八个模型中，有四个模型的群体（包括一个 7B 模型的群体）无论采用何种拓扑，都会收敛到对错误论断的高度确信。

Classical opinion dynamics and network consensus.经典观点动力学与网络共识。
The mathematical study of opinion dynamics originates in distributed control theory and network science Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003). Foundational linear models define consensus as a process where individuals repeatedly adopt the weighted average of their neighbors’ beliefs Degroot (1974); Xiao and Boyd (2003). In these systems network topology serves as the primary predictor of convergence speed Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Jadbabaie et al. (2003). Researchers subsequently developed non-linear bounded confidence models to better capture human behavior by stipulating that individuals ignore opinions that diverge too far from their own Friedkin and Johnsen (1990); Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000). While these classical models effectively describe engineered systems and provide theoretical baselines they rely on strict structural assumptions regarding convexity and conservation that do not naturally map to modern artificial intelligence agents Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000).
观点动力学的数学研究起源于分布式控制理论和网络科学 Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003)。基础线性模型将共识定义为个体反复采纳其邻居信念的加权平均值的过程 Degroot (1974); Xiao and Boyd (2003)。在这些系统中，网络拓扑是预测收敛速度的主要因素 Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Jadbabaie et al. (2003)。研究者随后提出了非线性有界信任模型（bounded confidence models），通过规定个体忽略与自身观点差异过大的意见，来更好地刻画人类行为 Friedkin and Johnsen (1990); Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000)。尽管这些经典模型能够有效描述工程系统并提供理论基准，但它们依赖于关于凸性和守恒性的严格结构假设，而这些假设无法自然地适用于现代人工智能智能体 Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000)。

3 Preliminaries3 预备知识
Classical linear consensus.经典线性共识。
Let $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ be a graph of $n$ agents with scalar states $x_{i}(t)\in[0,1]$ at discrete time $t$ , where $\mathcal{V}$ represents the set of agents (vertices) and $\mathcal{E}$ represents the edges defining their interactions. The classical model Degroot (1974); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004) updates the joint state via
设 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 为由 $n$ 个智能体构成的图，各智能体在离散时间 $t$ 的标量状态为 $x_{i}(t)\in[0,1]$，其中 $\mathcal{V}$ 表示智能体（顶点）的集合，$\mathcal{E}$ 表示定义其交互关系的边的集合。经典模型 Degroot (1974); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004) 通过下式更新联合状态
$$\displaystyle x(t+1) \\ \displaystyle=W\,x(t),\qquad W\text{ row stochastic}, \\ \displaystyle W_{ij} \\ \displaystyle>0\iff(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}.$$
Here, $W$ represents the row stochastic weight matrix defining the interaction weights between agents. For strongly connected, aperiodic graphs, $x(t)$ converges to a shared value at a rate determined by the graph Laplacian’s algebraic connectivity ( $\lambda_{2}$ ), making topology the central focus of prediction.
这里，$W$ 表示行随机权重矩阵，用于定义智能体之间的交互权重。对于强连通、非周期图，$x(t)$ 以由图拉普拉斯矩阵的代数连通度（$\lambda_{2}$）决定的速率收敛到一个共同值，因此拓扑结构成为预测的核心关注点。
Choosing a state variable.选择状态变量。
To evaluate LLM agents against Equation (1), we define $x_{i}$ as an agent’s credence (stated subjective probability) in a fixed binary proposition $P$ . This provides a verifiable $[0,1]$ scalar that mirrors real-world factual consensus building.
为了依据 Equation (1) 评估 LLM 智能体，我们将 $x_{i}$ 定义为智能体对某个固定二元命题 $P$ 的置信度（credence，即所声明的主观概率）。这提供了一个取值于 $[0,1]$ 的可验证标量，能够反映现实世界中围绕事实形成共识的过程。
Reading the state without sampling.无需采样地读取状态。
To eliminate parsing errors and sampling noise, we compute credence continuously and deterministically as the expectation of the model’s token distribution over a two-digit answer:
为消除解析错误和采样噪声，我们以模型对两位数字答案的词元分布的期望值，连续且确定性地计算置信度：
$$x_{i}\;=\;\frac{1}{99}\sum_{d_{1}=0}^{9}\sum_{d_{2}=0}^{9}(10d_{1}+d_{2})\;p(d_{1}\mid\text{ctx})\;p(d_{2}\mid\text{ctx},d_{1}).$$
Here, $d_{1}$ and $d_{2}$ denote the first and second digits (ranging from $0$ to $9$ ) of the model’s two-digit answer. The conditional probability for the second digit, $p(d_{2}\mid\text{ctx},d_{1})$ , is obtained via a single batched forward pass over the ten sequences formed by appending possible first digit to the context. Llama and Mistral split numbers into tokens differently, so for them we compute the same expectation in a slightly different way (Appendix B.4).
这里，$d_{1}$ 和 $d_{2}$ 分别表示模型两位数字答案的第一位和第二位数字（取值范围为 $0$ 至 $9$）。第二个数字的条件概率 $p(d_{2}\mid\text{ctx},d_{1})$ 是通过对十个序列进行一次批量前向传播得到的，这些序列由在上下文后附加各个可能的首位数字构成。Llama 和 Mistral 将数字拆分为词元的方式不同，因此，我们采用略有不同的方式为它们计算同一个期望值（附录 B.4）。
The three load bearing assumptions.三个支柱性假设。
These structural assumptions form the mathematical foundation of classical linear consensus models, originating from DeGroot’s seminal work on opinion formation and subsequent literature in distributed control theory Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003). They enforce the strict row-stochastic matrix properties required to guarantee that a network of agents will reliably converge over a graph. Consequently, Equation (1) strictly relies on these three conditions:
这些结构性假设构成了经典线性共识模型的数学基础，源自 DeGroot 关于意见形成的开创性研究以及随后分布式控制理论领域的相关文献 Degroot (1974); Jadbabaie et al. (2003); Olfati-Saber and Murray (2004); Xiao and Boyd (2003)。它们施加了严格的行随机矩阵性质，而这些性质是保证智能体网络在图上可靠收敛所必需的。因此，方程 (1) 严格依赖以下三个条件：
Updates are convex combinations of visible states ( $x_{i}(t{+}1)\in\mathrm{conv}\{x_{j}(t):j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}\}$ , where $\mathcal{N}_{i}$ represents the set of neighbors of agent $i$ and $\mathrm{conv}$ denotes the convex hull). This guarantees the population’s diameter strictly shrinks toward agreement.
更新后的状态是可见状态的凸组合（$x_{i}(t{+}1)\in\mathrm{conv}\{x_{j}(t):j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}\}$，其中 $\mathcal{N}_{i}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 的邻居集合，$\mathrm{conv}$ 表示凸包）。这保证了群体的直径严格缩小，从而趋于一致。
For doubly stochastic $W$ , the population mean is invariant, anchoring the final consensus directly to the initial condition.
当 $W$ 为双随机矩阵时，群体均值保持不变，使最终共识直接锚定于初始条件。
Weights $W_{ij}$ depend solely on the graph’s wiring, completely ignoring the numerical value or framing of the communicated message.
权重 $W_{ij}$ 仅取决于图的连接结构，完全不考虑所传递消息的数值或表述方式。
The empirical update operator and realized weight.经验更新算子与实际权重。
Instead of assuming Equation (1), we empirically measure the update operator $f:\;(x_{i},\,x_{j},\,c_{j})\;\longmapsto\;x_{i}^{\prime}$ (where $x_{i}^{\prime}$ represents the revised position of agent $i$ ) across a grid of prior positions $x_{i}$ , peer positions $x_{j}$ , and message framings $c_{j}$ . We connect this back to classical theory using the realized weight:
我们不预先假定方程 (1) 成立，而是在由先验立场 $x_{i}$、同伴立场 $x_{j}$ 和消息表述方式 $c_{j}$ 构成的网格上，实证测量更新算子 $f:\;(x_{i},\,x_{j},\,c_{j})\;\longmapsto\;x_{i}^{\prime}$（其中 $x_{i}^{\prime}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 修正后的立场）。我们通过实际权重将其与经典理论联系起来：
$$\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})\;=\;\frac{x_{i}^{\prime}-x_{i}}{x_{j}-x_{i}},\qquad x_{j}\neq x_{i}.$$
This realized weight quantifies the peer influence dynamically. A value of $\lambda=1$ means the agent completely adopts the peer’s opinion, while $\lambda=0$ means the agent completely ignores the peer. The interval $\lambda\in(0,1)$ indicates that normal averaging occurs, which is exactly what classical theory requires. Finally, $\lambda<0$ means the agent repels and actively moves away from the peer’s position.
这一实际权重动态地量化了同伴的影响。$\lambda=1$ 表示智能体完全采纳同伴的意见，而 $\lambda=0$ 表示智能体完全忽略同伴。$\lambda\in(0,1)$ 表示发生了正常的平均化，这恰好是经典理论所要求的。最后，$\lambda<0$ 表示智能体产生排斥，并主动远离同伴的立场。
Classical theory strictly requires $\lambda\in[0,1]$ (A1), constant pair means in symmetric exchanges (A2), and constant $\lambda$ across framings (A3). Crucially, $\lambda<0$ implies an action impossible to represent with any non-negative row stochastic matrix.
经典理论严格要求 $\lambda\in[0,1]$（A1）、对称交流中的双方均值保持不变（A2），以及不同表述方式下的 $\lambda$ 保持不变（A3）。关键在于，$\lambda<0$ 意味着一种无法用任何非负行随机矩阵表示的行为。
4 Where Consensus Theory Breaks4 共识理论在何处失效
This section works through the three assumptions in turn. For each one we state what the classical theory obtains from it, what is lost when it fails, and what the failure looks like as a measurable quantity. The measurements themselves are reported in Section 5.
本节依次探讨这三个假设。对于每个假设，我们都会说明经典理论从中获得了什么、该假设失效时会失去什么，以及这种失效如何体现为可测量的量。测量结果本身将在第 5 节报告。
4.1 (A1) Convexity, and why contraction is lost4.1 (A1) 凸性，以及为何不再具有收缩性
The reason convexity matters is that it supplies a Lyapunov function. In traditional network math, an agent’s updated opinion is always a weighted average of the opinions it currently sees. Because an average can never be higher than the highest number or lower than the lowest number, the total gap between the most extreme opinions in the group, the diameter, can only shrink over time. Define this diameter as:
凸性之所以重要，是因为它提供了一个李雅普诺夫函数（Lyapunov function）。在传统的网络数学中，智能体更新后的意见始终是其当前所见意见的加权平均值。由于平均值绝不可能高于最大值或低于最小值，群体中最极端意见之间的总差距，即直径，只会随时间缩小。将该直径定义为：
$$D(t)\;=\;\max_{i}x_{i}(t)\;-\;\min_{i}x_{i}(t).$$
where $t$ represents the discrete time step and $x_{i}(t)$ represents the credence state of agent $i$ at that specific time. Under (A1), $D(t{+}1)\leq D(t)$ . As long as the network is connected, this shrinking diameter guarantees the group will eventually contract toward total agreement. This single fact is what makes the classical theory so clean.
其中，$t$ 表示离散时间步，$x_{i}(t)$ 表示智能体 $i$ 在该特定时刻的置信状态。在 (A1) 条件下，$D(t{+}1)\leq D(t)$。只要网络连通，这种不断缩小的直径就能保证群体最终收缩至完全一致。正是这一事实使经典理论如此简洁。
Proposition 4.1 (Hull escape destroys monotone contraction).命题 4.1（逸出凸包会破坏单调收缩性）。
Suppose an update may land outside the convex hull of the states the agent can see by at most $\delta>0$ . Then $D(t{+}1)\leq D(t)+2\delta$ , and the bound is attained. Consequently $D$ is no longer non-increasing, and connectivity alone no longer implies contraction toward agreement.
假设一次更新可能落在该智能体可见状态的凸包之外，且超出距离至多为 $\delta>0$。则 $D(t{+}1)\leq D(t)+2\delta$，且该界可以达到。因此，$D$ 不再单调不增，仅凭连通性也不再能推出群体会收缩并趋于一致。
Proof Sketch. The complete proof sketch is provided in Appendix A.1.
证明概要。完整的证明概要见附录 A.1。
In practice, language models do not strictly average what they hear. They sometimes adopt positions that are more extreme than any of the opinions presented to them. When an agent lands outside the bounds of the existing opinions (escaping the convex hull), the mathematical maximum and minimum bounds stretch. Because this allows the diameter of the group’s opinions to grow rather than shrink, simply wiring agents together in a connected network no longer guarantees they will ever reach a consensus. The failure has an even stronger form when the realized weight of Equation (3) is negative.
在实践中，语言模型并不会严格对其听到的意见取平均。它们有时会采取比呈现给它们的任何意见都更极端的立场。当一个智能体落在现有观点的边界之外（逸出凸包）时，数学上的最大值和最小值边界就会扩张。由于这会使群体观点的直径增大而非缩小，仅仅将智能体连接成一个连通网络，就不再能保证它们最终会达成共识。当公式 (3) 的实际权重为负时，这种失效会以更强的形式出现。
Corollary 4.2 (Negative weights are unrepresentable).推论 4.2（负权重不可表示）。
If $\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})<0$ for some reachable configuration, then there is no non-negative row stochastic $W$ (the matrix defining the network weights) that reproduces that update, for any choice of weights.
如果在某个可达配置下有 $\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})<0$，那么无论如何选择权重，都不存在能够复现该次更新的非负行随机矩阵 $W$（定义网络权重的矩阵）。

证明概要。完整的证明概要见附录 A.2。
The classical theory breaks completely when an agent hears a peer’s opinion and actively repels, moving further away from that peer’s stance (a realized weight of $\lambda<0$ ). The classical linear model relies on matrices that strictly require non-negative numbers. A large but positive $\lambda$ would mean the linear model simply has the wrong parameters (a fitting problem). However, a negative $\lambda$ cannot be fixed by adjusting the parameters of the classical model; it proves that the foundational mathematical structure itself is fundamentally incapable of representing how AI agents actually interact.
当一个智能体听到同伴的观点后主动产生排斥，进一步远离该同伴的立场时（实际权重为 $\lambda<0$），经典理论便完全失效。经典线性模型所依赖的矩阵严格要求所有元素均为非负数。较大但为正的 $\lambda$ 意味着线性模型仅仅是参数有误（即拟合问题）。然而，负的 $\lambda$ 无法通过调整经典模型的参数来修正；它证明，该模型赖以建立的数学结构本身根本无法表示 AI 智能体的实际交互方式。
4.2 (A2) Conservation, and why the destination moves4.2 (A2) 守恒，以及终点为何会移动
In classical consensus theory, when $W$ is doubly stochastic, the overall average opinion of the entire population remains mathematically constant during discussions. Because this average is perfectly preserved over time, the group’s final agreed-upon consensus is directly anchored to whatever their starting beliefs were. We now show what happens when each exchange nudges the mean instead of preserving it.
在经典共识理论中，当 $W$ 为双随机矩阵时，整个群体的总体平均观点在讨论过程中始终保持数学上的恒定。由于这一平均值随时间被完全保留，群体最终达成的共识便直接锚定于其初始信念。下面我们展示，当每次交流都使均值发生微小偏移而非保持不变时，会出现什么情况。
Proposition 4.3 (Operator bias relocates the fixed point).命题 4.3（算子偏差使不动点移位）。
Let $m(t)$ be the population mean and suppose each exchange shifts it by an expected amount $b$ , so that $m(t{+}1)=m(t)+b$ while $m(t)$ remains interior to $[0,1]$ . Then $m(T)=\min\{1,\,m(0)+Tb\}$ for $b>0$ , where $T$ is the final time horizon. For any $b>0$ and any $m(0)$ , there is a horizon $T$ beyond which the population sits arbitrarily close to $1$ , independently of $m(0)$ .
令 $m(t)$ 为群体均值，并假设每次交流使其产生期望值为 $b$ 的偏移，因此，当 $m(t)$ 仍位于 $[0,1]$ 的内部时，有 $m(t{+}1)=m(t)+b$。那么，当 $b>0$ 时，$m(T)=\min\{1,\,m(0)+Tb\}$，其中 $T$ 为最终时间跨度。对于任意 $b>0$ 和任意 $m(0)$，都存在一个时间跨度 $T$，超过该时间跨度后，群体均值将任意接近 $1$，而与 $m(0)$ 无关。

证明概要。完整的证明概要见附录 A.3。
Language models do not preserve this mathematical average; instead, each pairwise exchange introduces a small expected bias that slightly nudges the group’s overall mean. As agents continue to interact, this slight bias accumulates and compounds, eventually pushing the population to the extreme boundary of maximum certainty. Consequently, the group’s final destination is determined entirely by the AI’s internal interaction bias, rendering the initial starting opinions irrelevant except for determining how long the process takes.
语言模型并不保持这一数学平均值；相反，每次两两交流都会引入一个微小的期望偏差，使群体的总体均值略微偏移。随着智能体持续交互，这种微小偏差不断累积并叠加，最终将群体推向确定性最高的极端边界。因此，群体的最终归宿完全由 AI 内部的交互偏差决定，初始观点除了决定这一过程需要多长时间之外，不再具有相关性。
Corollary 4.4 (Confident false consensus).推论 4.4（高度确信的错误共识）。
If $b>0$ on a proposition that is false, the population converges to high credence in that proposition, and no choice of initial condition prevents it.
如果对于一个错误命题有 $b>0$，群体就会收敛至对该命题具有高度置信度的状态，且无论选择何种初始条件都无法阻止这一结果。

证明概要。完整的证明概要见附录 A.4。
If this persistent upward bias applies to a factual claim that is objectively false, the population will inexorably drift toward high confidence in that falsehood. Because this upward drift represents blind assent rather than genuine evidence gathering, the network will trap itself in a state of collective delusion. This macro-scale failure aligns with broader architectural findings that language models frequently rely on internal shortcuts and exhibit explanation drift rather than maintaining stable, robust evidence tracking across contexts Dhayalkar (2026). No amount of initial skepticism programmed into the agents can prevent them from eventually talking each other into confidently believing the false claim. Corollary 4.4 is what makes the measurement of $b$ worth doing carefully: if $b$ is positive and roughly the same on true and false claims, it proves the drift is pure assent rather than evidence gathering.
如果这种持续向上的偏差作用于一个客观上错误的事实性主张，群体就会不可避免地逐渐对这一错误主张产生高度确信。由于这种向上漂移体现的是盲目赞同，而非真正的证据收集，网络将使自身陷入集体妄想状态。这种宏观层面的失效与更广泛的架构研究发现相一致：语言模型经常依赖内部捷径并表现出解释漂移，而无法在不同语境中保持稳定、稳健的证据追踪 Dhayalkar (2026)。无论在智能体中预先设定多强的初始怀疑倾向，都无法阻止它们最终通过相互说服而确信这一错误主张。推论 4.4 说明了为何值得仔细测量 $b$：如果 $b$ 为正，且在正确和错误主张上大致相同，就证明这种漂移纯粹源于赞同，而非证据收集。
4.3 (A3) Content independence, and why the graph is not enough4.3 (A3) 内容独立性，以及为何仅有图还不够
The third assumption is what licenses the whole practice of reasoning about multi agent systems by drawing a graph. If $W_{ij}$ (the mathematical weight assigned by agent $i$ to agent $j$ ) depends only on the edge, then the graph determines the dynamics.
第三个假设为通过绘制图来推理多智能体系统的整套做法提供了依据。如果 $W_{ij}$（智能体 $i$ 赋予智能体 $j$ 的数学权重）仅取决于边，那么图就决定了系统的动力学。
Proposition 4.5 (Content dependence underdetermines the graph).命题 4.5（内容依赖性使图不足以确定系统行为）。
If $\lambda$ (the realized weight) depends on the framing $c_{j}$ , then $W=W(c)$ (meaning the weight matrix becomes a direct function of the message framing), and two systems with identical graphs and identical initial conditions can follow different trajectories and reach different fixed points. No function of $\mathcal{G}$ (the network graph) alone predicts the outcome.
如果 $\lambda$（实际权重）取决于表述框架 $c_{j}$，那么 $W=W(c)$（意味着权重矩阵成为消息表述框架的直接函数），而具有相同图和相同初始条件的两个系统可能沿着不同的轨迹演化，并到达不同的不动点。任何仅以 $\mathcal{G}$（网络图）为自变量的函数都无法预测结果。

证明概要。完整的证明概要见附录 A.5。
This means you cannot predict a network’s final consensus just by analyzing who is connected to whom. The actual words and framing the agents use dynamically alter the effective structure of the network itself. The practical reading is that the wiring diagram of an agent system is not a sufficient description of it. Two deployments with the same topology but different message conventions, for example one where agents state confidence and one where they do not, are completely different dynamical systems.
这意味着，仅分析谁与谁相连，无法预测网络最终会达成何种共识。智能体实际使用的措辞和表述框架会动态改变网络本身的有效结构。其实际含义是，智能体系统的连接图不足以完整描述该系统。两个具有相同拓扑结构、但采用不同消息约定的部署，例如一个要求智能体陈述置信度，另一个则不要求，实际上是完全不同的动力系统。
4.4 Why the standard repair does not apply and what replaces $\lambda_{2}$4.4 为什么标准修正方法不适用，以及什么取代了 $\lambda_{2}$
When human populations deviate from simple mathematical averages, researchers typically rely on bounded confidence models Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000). This theory assumes that individuals will naturally discount or ignore opinions that are vastly different from their own, meaning the influence weight should decrease as the disagreement gap grows. This does not fix the problem, because bounded confidence only changes which opinions an agent averages, so every update still stays inside the convex hull and it cannot produce the hull escapes and negative weights that break A1.
当人群的行为偏离简单的数学平均规律时，研究者通常采用有界信任模型（bounded confidence models）Hegselmann and Krause (2002); Deffuant et al. (2000)。该理论假设，个体会自然地降低与自身观点差异极大的意见的权重，或直接忽略这些意见，这意味着影响权重应随着分歧的扩大而减小。这并不能解决问题，因为有界信任只改变智能体对哪些意见取平均，因此每次更新仍然位于凸包内，无法产生破坏 A1 的凸包逃逸和负权重。
Taking the three failures together explains the paradigm shift away from classical metrics. In classical theory, algebraic connectivity (represented as $\lambda_{2}$ ) is the central metric because it uses the network’s wiring diagram to predict the dynamics of the entire group. However, because the three foundational assumptions fail, the network’s wiring entirely loses its predictive power. Group contraction is no longer guaranteed, the final destination is driven by the agent’s internal bias rather than the initial starting average, and message content overrides the graph’s edges. Instead of the graph’s connectivity, the final consensus of an LLM population is governed by new predictors: the individual model’s internal update bias $b$ and the group’s overall confidence structure, such as the starting distribution of opinions and the fraction of agents that refuse to change their minds. We test this claim at $n=10^{4}$ (a population size of 10,000 agents) in Section 5.
综合这三种失效情形，就能解释为何需要从经典指标转向新的范式。在经典理论中，代数连通度（记为 $\lambda_{2}$）是核心指标，因为它利用网络的连接图来预测整个群体的动态行为。然而，由于这三个基本假设失效，网络的连接结构完全丧失了预测能力。群体收缩不再得到保证，最终状态由智能体的内部偏差驱动，而非由初始平均值决定，消息内容的作用也凌驾于图的边之上。决定 LLM 群体最终共识的不再是图的连通性，而是新的预测因素：单个模型的内部更新偏差 $b$，以及群体整体的置信度结构，例如初始意见分布和拒绝改变观点的智能体所占比例。我们在第 5 节中以 $n=10^{4}$（即由 10,000 个智能体组成的群体）检验这一论断。

When calculating the realized weight $\lambda$ from Equation (3), the value becomes undefined when $x_{j}=x_{i}$ and gets numerically unstable when the two values are very close because the denominator vanishes. To avoid this, we computed all statistics involving $\lambda$ strictly on the subset of interactions where the agents disagreed by at least $0.30$ , which leaves $5{,}040$ measurements per model. For hull escape, mean drift, and all other statistics, we used the full grid. Testing the conservation assumption (A2) requires looking at both directions of a pairwise exchange. Since $x_{i}$ and $x_{j}$ range over the exact same set of values, the grid already contains both $f(x_{i},x_{j})$ and $f(x_{j},x_{i})$ , meaning no extra measurements are necessary.
根据公式 (3) 计算实际权重 $\lambda$ 时，当 $x_{j}=x_{i}$ 时，该值无定义；当这两个值非常接近时，由于分母趋近于零，计算会出现数值不稳定。为避免这一问题，我们严格限定在智能体之间的置信度差异至少为 $0.30$ 的交互子集上计算所有涉及 $\lambda$ 的统计量，这使每个模型保留 $5{,}040$ 次测量。对于凸包逃逸、平均漂移以及所有其他统计量，我们使用完整网格。检验守恒假设 (A2) 需要考察成对交流的两个方向。由于 $x_{i}$ 和 $x_{j}$ 的取值集合完全相同，网格中已经同时包含 $f(x_{i},x_{j})$ 和 $f(x_{j},x_{i})$，因此不需要额外测量。
Fitted operator.拟合算子。
For one model, one claim and one framing, E1 gives the agent’s new credence $x_{i}^{\prime}$ at each of the $7\times 7$ grid pairs $(x_{i},x_{j})$ . We turn this table into a function $\hat{f}(x_{i},x_{j})$ by bilinear interpolation between grid points and clip its output to $[0,1]$ . We call $\hat{f}$ the fitted operator. We need it because running the real models at population scale is far too costly: E3 has $1{,}620$ runs of $10^{4}$ agents over $30$ rounds, about $5\times 10^{8}$ updates per model and over $60{,}000$ times the size of E2. We use $\hat{f}$ in two places. In E2 we run it next to the real agents to check how well it predicts them (Section 5.8), and in E3 it stands in for the real model in every $10^{4}$ -agent run.
对于一个模型、一个主张和一种消息框架，E1 给出了智能体在 $7\times 7$ 网格中的每个数值对 $(x_{i},x_{j})$ 下的新置信度 $x_{i}^{\prime}$。我们通过在网格点之间进行双线性插值，将这张表转换为函数 $\hat{f}(x_{i},x_{j})$，并将其输出截断至 $[0,1]$。我们将 $\hat{f}$ 称为拟合算子。我们需要这一算子，是因为在群体规模上运行真实模型的成本过高：E3 包含 $1{,}620$ 次运行，每次涉及 $10^{4}$ 个智能体并持续 $30$ 轮，相当于每个模型约 $5\times 10^{8}$ 次更新，规模超过 E2 的 $60{,}000$ 倍。我们在两处使用 $\hat{f}$。在 E2 中，我们将其与真实智能体并行运行，以检验其预测真实智能体行为的准确程度（第 5.8 节）；在 E3 中，它在每次包含 $10^{4}$ 个智能体的运行中替代真实模型。
E2, real populations.E2，真实群体。
The second instrument places $12$ real agents on a graph and runs them for $8$ rounds. We tested complete, ring, star, and small-world topologies. By using five random seeds and four specific propositions (t1, f1, f3, and u2 from Table 4), we generated $80$ distinct populations per model, which resulted in $7{,}680$ agent updates per model. We chose this specific subset of claims to represent true, false, and uncertain categories while keeping the computational cost of simulating entire populations manageable. We ran this experiment for all eight models, giving $640$ populations and $61{,}440$ agent updates in total, always with the neutral message framing.
第二种实验工具将 $12$ 个真实智能体置于一个图上，并让它们运行 $8$ 轮。我们测试了完全图、环形、星形和小世界拓扑。通过使用五个随机种子和四个特定命题（表 4 中的 t1、f1、f3 和 u2），我们为每个模型生成了 $80$ 个不同群体，每个模型由此产生 $7{,}680$ 次智能体更新。我们选择这一特定的主张子集来代表真实、虚假和不确定三类，同时将整个群体的模拟计算成本控制在可承受范围内。我们对全部八个模型进行了这一实验，总计得到 $640$ 个群体和 $61{,}440$ 次智能体更新，且始终采用中性消息框架。
To ensure a perfect comparison, we generated the exact interaction schedules and initial conditions in advance. Every agent updates synchronously by reading the state from the previous round. We then replayed these identical scenarios through three different dynamics: the real LLM agents (where inputs were rounded to integer percentages), the fitted operator $\hat{f}$ , and classical DeGroot averaging (using a uniform weight of $\alpha=0.5$ to represent a single observed neighbor).
为确保完全可比，我们预先生成了确切的交互调度和初始条件。每个智能体都通过读取上一轮的状态进行同步更新。随后，我们使用三种不同的动力学机制重放这些完全相同的情境：真实 LLM 智能体（输入四舍五入为整数百分比）、拟合算子 $\hat{f}$，以及经典 DeGroot 平均机制（使用统一权重 $\alpha=0.5$ 来表示单个被观测邻居的影响）。
Because all three dynamics followed the exact same sequence of encounters from the exact same starting points, we can compare their trajectories agent-by-agent and round-by-round rather than just looking at overall distributions. We evaluated the results one step ahead using teacher forcing, giving each predictor the exact states the real agents held in the previous round to completely isolate single-step accuracy from cumulative errors.
由于这三种动力学机制均从完全相同的起点出发，并遵循完全相同的相遇序列，我们能够逐个智能体、逐轮比较它们的轨迹，而不只是考察总体分布。我们使用教师强制（teacher forcing）进行单步前瞻评估，向每个预测器提供真实智能体在上一轮的确切状态，从而将单步准确性与累积误差完全分离。
E3, extrapolation to population scale.E3，外推至群体规模。
The third instrument scales our measurements up to $10{,}000$ agents. In every round, each agent draws one random neighbor to interact with, applying the fitted operator for a total of $30$ rounds. We generated $27$ different graphs using three network families (Watts-Strogatz, Erdos-Renyi, and Barabasi-Albert) across various densities, giving us algebraic connectivities ranging from $\lambda_{2}=0$ to $\lambda_{2}=5.49$ .
第三种实验工具将我们的测量扩展至 $10{,}000$ 个智能体的规模。每一轮中，每个智能体随机抽取一个邻居进行交互，并应用拟合算子，总共运行 $30$ 轮。我们使用三类网络（Watts-Strogatz、Erdos-Renyi 和 Barabasi-Albert），在不同密度下生成了 $27$ 个不同的图，其代数连通度范围从 $\lambda_{2}=0$ 到 $\lambda_{2}=5.49$。
Against these graphs, we systematically varied the population’s starting confidence structure. We swept the initial population mean across $\mu\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$ and the stubborn agent fraction across $\text{stub}\in\{0,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10\}$ for four specific propositions, yielding $1{,}620$ unique runs for each of the eight model operators. We anchored all committed agents strictly to the true pole at a $0.95$ credence. Restoring them to this exact value after every round represents the absolute best-case scenario for these agents to successfully capture the rest of the population.
在这些图上，我们系统地改变了群体的初始置信度结构。针对四个特定命题，我们遍历了初始群体均值 $\mu\in\{0.3,0.5,0.7\}$ 和顽固智能体比例 $\text{stub}\in\{0,0.01,0.02,0.05,0.10\}$，为八个模型算子中的每一个生成了 $1{,}620$ 次不同的运行。我们将所有立场坚定的智能体严格锚定在真值一端，置信度设为 $0.95$。每轮结束后都将它们恢复至这一确切数值，代表了这些智能体成功使群体中其余成员接受其立场的绝对最佳情境。
We classified a run as reaching consensus if the final standard deviation dropped below $0.12$ , and we labeled it as polarized if over $20$ percent of agents landed below $0.33$ while another $20$ percent landed above $0.67$ . Finally, to prove exactly what drove the final outcomes, we used an ordinary least squares commonality analysis. This variance decomposition explicitly separated the predictive power of the network’s connectivity (using $\lambda_{2}$ and mean degree) from the population’s confidence structure (using the initial mean and committed fraction).
如果最终标准差降至 $0.12$ 以下，我们就将该次运行归类为达成共识；如果超过 $20$ 的百分比的智能体最终置信度低于 $0.33$，同时另有超过 $20$ 的百分比的智能体最终置信度高于 $0.67$，我们就将其标记为极化。最后，为了确切证明最终结果由哪些因素驱动，我们采用了普通最小二乘法共同性分析。这种方差分解明确区分了网络连通性（使用 $\lambda_{2}$ 和平均度）与群体置信度结构（使用初始均值和坚定者比例）各自的预测能力。

5.4 Validating Corollary 4.2: some updates move away from the peer5.4 验证推论 4.2：部分更新远离同伴

5.5 Validating Proposition 4.3: the end point stops depending on the start5.5 验证命题 4.3：终点不再依赖起点

For the heavily biased 0.5B and 1.5B operators, the slope is effectively zero. A population starting at a $0.3$ average credence terminates in the exact same place as one starting at $0.7$ . 5.6 Validating Corollary 4.4: drift on false claims decides the outcome5.6 验证推论 4.4：虚假断言上的漂移决定结果

Because their upward drift is virtually identical on both true and false claims, these networks are not gathering evidence. They are simply assenting. Qwen-7B and Gemma-4B drift less on false claims than on true ones, but their overall upward drift is still strong enough that $83\%$ and $88\%$ of their simulated runs end up believing the false claims. Their real 12-agent populations in E2 agree, finishing at credences of $0.73$ and $0.79$ on false claims. Four models reject these false claims. Qwen-7B and Gemma-4B are close to zero, at $-0.025$ and $-0.015$ , and their groups still drift up.

5.8 Validating the fitted operator5.8 验证拟合算子
To justify using the fitted operator for massive scale simulations, we validated its accuracy against real agent populations. Evaluated one step ahead, the fitted operator consistently outperforms classical DeGroot averaging across all topologies and all eight models, with DeGroot’s error between $1.28$ and $4.05$ times larger (Figure 3(b) and (c) show this for the 3B model). While DeGroot approximates single steps reasonably well, its true failure lies in predicting the final destination. As Figure 3(a) shows for the 3B model, when real agents discuss a false claim, they correctly lower their credence to reject it. DeGroot, however, mathematically forces the trajectory upward to preserve the initial mean. A classical model can therefore predict individual moves accurately but still drive the group to an entirely wrong consensus by enforcing false mathematical invariants.
为论证使用拟合算子开展大规模模拟的合理性，我们以真实智能体群体为基准验证了其准确性。在单步超前预测评估中，拟合算子在所有拓扑结构和全部八个模型上的表现均优于经典 DeGroot 平均法，DeGroot 的误差为拟合算子的 $1.28$ 至 $4.05$ 倍（图 3(b) 和 (c) 展示了 3B 模型的这一结果）。尽管 DeGroot 对单步变化的近似相当准确，但其真正的失败之处在于预测最终归宿。如图 3(a) 中 3B 模型的结果所示，当真实智能体讨论错误主张时，它们会正确地降低置信度，从而拒绝该主张。然而，为保持初始均值不变，DeGroot 在数学上强制轨迹向上发展。因此，经典模型可以准确预测个体的变化，却仍会因强制维持错误的数学不变量，而将群体引向完全错误的共识。

5.9 Validating Section 4.4: connectivity explains almost none of the outcome5.9 验证第 4.4 节：连通性几乎无法解释结果
To test whether predictive power still resides in the graph, we decomposed the outcome variance into factors driven by connectivity versus confidence structure. For all eight operators, the network’s algebraic connectivity ( $\lambda_{2}$ ) and mean degree uniquely explain zero percent of the final population mean. The initial confidence structure explains more, but how much depends on the model, ranging from almost nothing to $0.23$ of the final mean. The real populations of E2 point the same way. Changing the topology moves a model’s final credence on false claims by at most $0.11$ , while changing the model moves it by up to $0.82$ . The central metric of classical consensus theory therefore carries essentially no predictive information for AI agent populations.
为检验图是否仍具有预测能力，我们将结果方差分解为由连通性驱动的因素和由置信度结构驱动的因素。对于全部八个算子，网络的代数连通度（$\lambda_{2}$）和平均度对群体最终均值的独立解释比例均为百分之零。初始置信度结构的解释力更强，但具体程度取决于模型，对最终均值的解释比例从几乎为零到 $0.23$ 不等。E2 中的真实群体也呈现出相同趋势。改变拓扑结构使模型对错误主张的最终置信度最多变化 $0.11$，而更换模型则使其最多变化 $0.82$。因此，经典共识理论的核心指标对于 AI 智能体群体基本不包含任何预测信息。
5.10 Committed agents fragment rather than capture5.10 立场坚定的智能体使群体分裂，而非主导群体
In models of human opinion dynamics, a committed minority typically steers the population toward its own position. We observe no such effect here. Raising the fraction of stubborn agents to ten percent moves the final population mean by at most $0.15$ . Instead, these committed agents simply prevent agreement. For the 3B model, the consensus rate drops from $90.7\%$ to $16.7\%$ with no increase in polarization. Qwen-7B and Gemma-4B show the same effect more mildly, with consensus dropping from $91.4\%$ to $70.4\%$ and from $100\%$ to $83.3\%$ , while their final means move by only about $0.03$ . Llama, Mistral and Gemma-2 show the same pattern, with consensus dropping from $100\%$ to $0\%$ , from $91.4\%$ to $9.9\%$ , and from $90.7\%$ to $25.9\%$ . Stubborn AI agents disperse and fragment the population rather than successfully capturing it.
在人类意见动力学模型中，立场坚定的少数群体通常会引导整个群体趋向其自身立场。我们在此未观察到这种效应。将固执智能体的比例提高到百分之十，群体最终均值最多变化 $0.15$。相反，这些立场坚定的智能体只是阻碍了共识的达成。对于 3B 模型，共识率从 $90.7\%$ 降至 $16.7\%$，而极化程度并未增加。Qwen-7B 和 Gemma-4B 也表现出同样的效应，但程度较轻，共识率分别从 $91.4\%$ 降至 $70.4\%$、从 $100\%$ 降至 $83.3\%$，而其最终均值仅变化约 $0.03$。Llama、Mistral 和 Gemma-2 表现出相同的模式，共识率分别从 $100\%$ 降至 $0\%$、从 $91.4\%$ 降至 $9.9\%$，以及从 $90.7\%$ 降至 $25.9\%$。固执的 AI 智能体使群体分散并碎片化，而未能成功主导群体。
6 Conclusion6 结论
Classical consensus theory fails to describe populations of LLM agents because all three structural assumptions break down under direct measurement. The standard non-linear repairs for human populations also fail, because they still keep every update inside the convex hull. The practical consequence is that predicting a multi-agent system’s behavior by analyzing its network topology is fundamentally flawed. Algebraic connectivity explains almost none of the variation in where a population lands. Instead, system dynamics are driven by the chosen model’s internal update bias and the specific wording of the prompts. A designer choosing between different network graphs is adjusting a variable that hardly matters.
经典共识理论无法描述 LLM 智能体群体，因为直接测量表明，其全部三个结构性假设均不成立。针对人类群体的标准非线性修正方法同样失效，因为这些方法仍将每次更新限制在凸包内。其实际后果是，通过分析网络拓扑来预测多智能体系统行为的做法存在根本性缺陷。代数连通度几乎无法解释群体最终状态的差异。相反，系统动力学由所选模型的内部更新偏向和提示词的具体措辞所驱动。设计者在不同网络图之间进行选择，实际上是在调整一个几乎无关紧要的变量。
This failure highlights a concrete deployment risk. Populations of four of our eight models, including Qwen2.5 at 7B, reliably talk themselves into confident agreement on objectively false claims, regardless of how their network is wired. Small upward drifts in isolated exchanges compound until the entire network saturates in collective delusion. This does not go away with model size. The 7B Qwen model’s populations believe false claims, while those of the smaller 3B Qwen model and of Llama-8B, Mistral-7B and Gemma-2-9B do not, so the outcome depends on each model’s own drift rather than on how large it is.
这一失效揭示了一项具体的部署风险。在我们研究的八个模型中，有四个模型的智能体群体，包括 7B 的 Qwen2.5，无论其网络如何连接，都会稳定地通过相互交流，对客观上错误的断言达成充满信心的共识。单次交流中微小的向上漂移不断累积，直至整个网络完全陷入集体妄信。这一现象并不会随着模型规模增大而消失。7B Qwen 模型的智能体群体相信错误断言，而规模更小的 3B Qwen 模型以及 Llama-8B、Mistral-7B 和 Gemma-2-9B 的智能体群体则不会，因此，结果取决于各模型自身的漂移，而非模型规模。
7 Limitations7 局限性
Two main limitations bound these findings. First, our tested models span 0.5B to 9B parameters, meaning these findings may not perfectly extrapolate to frontier models like GPT, Claude or Gemini, which are far larger and whose token probabilities are often not fully available, so our readout cannot be applied to them directly. Second, our state variable relies on a simple scalar credence for a single proposition, which lacks the multidimensional complexity of beliefs in deployed systems.
两项主要局限性限定了这些发现的适用范围。首先，我们测试的模型参数规模涵盖 0.5B 至 9B，这意味着这些发现未必能够完全推广到 GPT、Claude 或 Gemini 等前沿模型；这些模型的规模大得多，而且其词元概率通常无法完整获取，因此我们的读出方法无法直接应用于它们。其次，我们的状态变量依赖于对单一命题的简单标量置信度，未能体现已部署系统中信念的多维复杂性。
8 Broader Impact8 更广泛的影响
This work studies how groups of LLM agents update their beliefs when they talk to each other, a setup now common in multi-agent debate, voting, and agent teams. We show that how the agents are connected says little about where the group ends up, and that some models reach confident agreement on false claims. This helps designers of such systems avoid treating group agreement as a sign of correctness, and gives them a simple pairwise test to check a model’s drift on known false claims before using it in a group. All experiments use public open-weight models and public factual claims, and involve no human participants or personal data.
本研究考察 LLM 智能体群体在相互交流时如何更新其信念，这种设置如今已广泛用于多智能体辩论、投票和智能体团队。我们表明，智能体之间的连接方式几乎无法说明群体最终会达到何种状态，而且某些模型会对错误断言达成充满信心的共识。这有助于此类系统的设计者避免将群体共识视为正确性的标志，并为他们提供一种简单的成对测试方法，以便在将模型用于群体之前，检验其针对已知错误断言的漂移。所有实验均使用公开的开放权重模型和公开的事实性断言，不涉及人类参与者或个人数据。

A.1 Proof of Proposition 4.1: Hull escape destroys monotone contractionA.1 命题 4.1 的证明：逸出凸包会破坏单调收缩
Proof.证明。
This proposition demonstrates how agents adopting positions outside their visible convex hull destroy the monotone contraction of the group’s diameter.
本命题说明，智能体采取其可见凸包之外的立场，如何破坏群体直径的单调收缩。
Let the diameter of the population at time $t$ be defined as $D(t)=\max_{k}x_{k}(t)-\min_{k}x_{k}(t)$ . During an update step, agent $i$ observes a subset of states $\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}$ . The convex hull of these visible states is bounded by the local minimum and local maximum:
将群体在时刻 $t$ 的直径定义为 $D(t)=\max_{k}x_{k}(t)-\min_{k}x_{k}(t)$。在一次更新步骤中，智能体 $i$ 观测状态子集 $\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}$。这些可见状态的凸包以局部最小值和局部最大值为界：
$$[\min_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t),\max_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t)]$$
Because these local extremes are drawn from the global population, they are strictly bounded by the global extremes:
由于这些局部极值取自全局群体，它们严格受全局极值的界定：
$$\min_{k}x_{k}(t)\leq\min_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t)\quad\text{and}\quad\max_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t)\leq\max_{k}x_{k}(t)$$
By the premise of the proposition, an agent’s new state $x_{i}(t+1)$ can escape its local convex hull by at most a margin of $\delta>0$ . This establishes the absolute upper and lower bounds for the updated state:
根据命题的前提，智能体的新状态 $x_{i}(t+1)$ 可以逸出其局部凸包，逸出幅度至多为 $\delta>0$。由此可确定更新后状态的绝对上界和下界：
$$x_{i}(t+1)\leq\max_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t)+\delta\leq\max_{k}x_{k}(t)+\delta$$
$$x_{i}(t+1)\geq\min_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\cup\{i\}}x_{j}(t)-\delta\geq\min_{k}x_{k}(t)-\delta$$
The new diameter at time $t+1$ is $D(t+1)=\max_{i}x_{i}(t+1)-\min_{i}x_{i}(t+1)$ . Substituting the worst case bounds yields:
时刻 $t+1$ 的新直径为 $D(t+1)=\max_{i}x_{i}(t+1)-\min_{i}x_{i}(t+1)$。代入最坏情况下的界可得：
$$D(t+1)\leq(\max_{k}x_{k}(t)+\delta)-(\min_{k}x_{k}(t)-\delta)$$
$$D(t+1)\leq\max_{k}x_{k}(t)-\min_{k}x_{k}(t)+2\delta$$
$$D(t+1)\leq D(t)+2\delta$$
This bound is perfectly attained if at least one agent updates to the maximum possible positive deviation while another independently updates to the maximum possible negative deviation in the same round. Consequently, $D(t)$ is no longer monotonically decreasing, meaning the network is not guaranteed to contract toward agreement. ∎
如果至少一个智能体更新至可能的最大正向偏离，而另一个智能体在同一轮中独立地更新至可能的最大负向偏离，则恰好达到此界。因此，$D(t)$ 不再单调递减，这意味着无法保证网络会向共识收缩。∎
A.2 Proof of Corollary 4.2: Negative weights are unrepresentableA.2 推论 4.2 的证明：负权重无法表示
Proof.证明。
This corollary proves that a realized negative weight fundamentally breaks the row stochastic matrix requirement of the classical linear model.
本推论证明，实际出现的负权重从根本上违反了经典线性模型对行随机矩阵的要求。
The classical consensus model dictates that the updated state $x_{i}^{\prime}$ is a weighted sum of the states the agent observes:
经典共识模型规定，更新后的状态 $x_{i}^{\prime}$ 是智能体所观察到的各状态的加权和：
$$x_{i}^{\prime}=\sum_{k}W_{ik}x_{k}(t)$$
For a controlled pairwise exchange between agent $i$ and a peer $j$ , the only visible states are $x_{i}$ and $x_{j}$ . The update equation simplifies to:
对于智能体 $i$ 与另一智能体 $j$ 之间的一次受控两两交流，唯一可见的状态是 $x_{i}$ 和 $x_{j}$ 。更新方程简化为：
$$x_{i}^{\prime}=W_{ii}x_{i}+W_{ij}x_{j}$$
The classical model strictly requires the weight matrix $W$ to be row stochastic, meaning all entries are non-negative ( $W_{ik}\geq 0$ ) and each row sums to exactly $1$ ( $\sum_{k}W_{ik}=1$ ). For this isolated pairwise interaction, $W_{ii}+W_{ij}=1$ , allowing us to substitute $W_{ii}=1-W_{ij}$ :
经典模型严格要求权重矩阵 $W$ 为行随机矩阵，即所有元素均非负（ $W_{ik}\geq 0$ ），且每行之和恰好为 $1$ （ $\sum_{k}W_{ik}=1$ ）。对于这次孤立的两两交互，$W_{ii}+W_{ij}=1$ ，因此可代入 $W_{ii}=1-W_{ij}$ ：
$$x_{i}^{\prime}=(1-W_{ij})x_{i}+W_{ij}x_{j}$$
$$x_{i}^{\prime}=x_{i}-W_{ij}x_{i}+W_{ij}x_{j}$$
$$x_{i}^{\prime}=x_{i}+W_{ij}(x_{j}-x_{i})$$
Isolating the weight applied to the peer’s state gives:
将施加于另一智能体状态的权重单独解出，得到：
$$W_{ij}=\frac{x_{i}^{\prime}-x_{i}}{x_{j}-x_{i}}$$
This isolated term matches the exact definition of the realized weight $\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})$ . Therefore, $W_{ij}=\lambda$ . If the empirically measured update yields $\lambda<0$ , it dictates that $W_{ij}<0$ . A negative entry is explicitly forbidden by the definition of a non-negative row stochastic matrix, proving that no such matrix can reproduce this specific update for any choice of weights. ∎
这一单独解出的项与实际权重 $\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})$ 的定义完全一致。因此，$W_{ij}=\lambda$ 。如果实证测得的更新产生 $\lambda<0$ ，则必有 $W_{ij}<0$ 。非负行随机矩阵的定义明确禁止出现负元素，这证明，无论如何选择权重，都不存在能够复现这一特定更新的此类矩阵。∎

Proof.证明。
This proposition proves mathematically that any persistent bias in the update rule dictates the system’s final destination, rendering the initial conditions irrelevant over a sufficiently long time horizon.
本命题从数学上证明，更新规则中任何持续存在的偏差都会决定系统的最终归宿，使初始条件在足够长的时间跨度内变得无关紧要。
Let $m(t)$ represent the population mean at time step $t$ . By assumption, each exchange shifts the mean by an expected bias $b>0$ , meaning the recurrence relation for the unbounded mean is:
令 $m(t)$ 表示时间步 $t$ 时的群体均值。根据假设，每次交流都会使均值产生期望为 $b>0$ 的偏移，这意味着无界均值的递推关系为：
$$m(t+1)=m(t)+b$$
We can solve this linear recurrence relation directly by induction. For $t=1$ :
我们可以直接通过归纳法求解这一线性递推关系。当 $t=1$ 时：
$$m(1)=m(0)+b$$
For $t=2$ :
当 $t=2$ 时：
$$m(2)=m(1)+b=(m(0)+b)+b=m(0)+2b$$
Assuming the relation holds for an arbitrary step $t$ , we have:
假设该关系在任意时间步 $t$ 成立，则有：
$$m(t)=m(0)+tb$$
However, the state variable $x_{i}(t)$ represents a credence probability and is strictly bounded within the interval $[0,1]$ . Therefore, the population mean $m(t)$ must also remain interior to $[0,1]$ . Applying this absolute upper bound gives the true state at time $T$ :
然而，状态变量 $x_{i}(t)$ 表示信念概率，其取值被严格限制在区间 $[0,1]$ 内。因此，群体均值 $m(t)$ 也必须保持在 $[0,1]$ 内。施加这一绝对上界，可得时刻 $T$ 的真实状态：
$$m(T)=\min\{1,m(0)+Tb\}$$
To prove that the population eventually sits arbitrarily close to $1$ independently of $m(0)$ , we solve for the horizon $T$ required to reach the maximum bound:

$$T\geq\frac{1-m(0)}{b}$$
Because $b>0$ and $m(0)\in[0,1]$ , the term $\frac{1-m(0)}{b}$ is always a finite, non-negative number. This proves there is always a finite time horizon $T$ where the mean will saturate at $1$ , completely overriding the initial mean $m(0)$ . ∎
由于 $b>0$ 且 $m(0)\in[0,1]$ ，$\frac{1-m(0)}{b}$ 始终是一个有限的非负数。这证明，总存在一个有限的时间跨度 $T$ ，使均值在 $1$ 处达到饱和，从而完全压倒初始均值 $m(0)$ 的影响。∎

A.5 Derivation of Proposition 4.5: Content dependence underdetermines the graphA.5 命题 4.5 的推导：内容依赖性使图无法被充分确定
Proof.证明。
This proposition demonstrates mathematically that if communication weights rely on message framing, the network topology alone is strictly insufficient to predict the dynamic trajectory or final consensus of the system.
本命题从数学上表明，如果通信权重依赖于消息的表述框架，那么仅凭网络拓扑结构严格不足以预测系统的动态轨迹或最终共识。
In classical consensus theory, the weight matrix $W$ is strictly a function of the graph topology $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ . We can denote this invariant matrix as $W_{\mathcal{G}}$ . The state update for the entire network is written as:
在经典共识理论中，权重矩阵 $W$ 严格地是图拓扑 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 的函数。我们可以将这个不变矩阵记为 $W_{\mathcal{G}}$。整个网络的状态更新可写为：
$$x(t+1)=W_{\mathcal{G}}\,x(t)$$
Given an initial condition $x(0)$ , the trajectory of the system is entirely deterministic and defined by:
给定初始条件 $x(0)$，系统的轨迹完全是确定性的，并由下式定义：
$$x(t)=W_{\mathcal{G}}^{t}\,x(0)$$
However, empirical measurements dictate that the realized weight $\lambda$ is a function of the message framing $c_{j}$ . As established in Corollary 4.2, the realized weight corresponds directly to the matrix entry: $W_{ij}=\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})$ .
然而，实证测量表明，实际权重 $\lambda$ 是消息表述框架 $c_{j}$ 的函数。如推论 4.2 所确立的，实际权重直接对应于矩阵元素：$W_{ij}=\lambda(x_{i},x_{j},c_{j})$。
Because $W_{ij}$ depends on the continuous framing variables vector $c(t)$ at time $t$ , the weight matrix is no longer a static property of the graph $\mathcal{G}$ . It becomes a time-varying, content-dependent matrix $W(c(t))$ . The state update equation becomes:
由于 $W_{ij}$ 取决于时刻 $t$ 的连续表述框架变量向量 $c(t)$，权重矩阵不再是图 $\mathcal{G}$ 的静态属性。它变成了一个随时间变化且依赖于内容的矩阵 $W(c(t))$。状态更新方程变为：
$$x(t+1)=W(c(t))\,x(t)$$
To prove underdetermination, consider two separate multi-agent deployments, System A and System B. Both share the exact same network topology $\mathcal{G}$ and the exact same initial state $x(0)$ .
为证明欠决定性，考虑两个独立部署的多智能体系统，即系统 A 和系统 B。二者具有完全相同的网络拓扑 $\mathcal{G}$ 和完全相同的初始状态 $x(0)$。
Let System A adopt a communication convention using framing $c_{A}$ (e.g., highly assertive framing), and System B adopt framing $c_{B}$ (e.g., highly hedged framing), where $c_{A}\neq c_{B}$ . At time step $t=1$ , their respective updates are:
设系统 A 采用使用表述框架 $c_{A}$ 的通信惯例（例如，高度肯定的表述），系统 B 则采用表述框架 $c_{B}$（例如，使用大量保留措辞的表述），其中 $c_{A}\neq c_{B}$。在时间步 $t=1$，它们各自的更新为：
$$x_{A}(1)=W(c_{A})\,x(0)$$
$$x_{B}(1)=W(c_{B})\,x(0)$$
Because the measured realized weight varies significantly with framing, $W(c_{A})\neq W(c_{B})$ . Assuming a non-trivial $x(0)$ , this guarantees that:
由于测得的实际权重随表述框架发生显著变化，因此 $W(c_{A})\neq W(c_{B})$。假设 $x(0)$ 非平凡，则可保证：
$$x_{A}(1)\neq x_{B}(1)$$
Despite possessing identical wiring diagrams and starting beliefs, the trajectories of the two systems diverge immediately at the first timestep. Consequently, they will cascade toward completely different fixed points. Therefore, no mathematical function $f(\mathcal{G})$ that maps only the graph topology to a consensus outcome can correctly predict the fixed points of both System A and System B. The graph fundamentally underdetermines the dynamics. ∎
尽管具有相同的连接结构和初始信念，这两个系统的轨迹在第一个时间步就立即发生分歧。因此，它们将通过级联演化趋向完全不同的不动点。因此，任何仅将图拓扑映射到共识结果的数学函数 $f(\mathcal{G})$，都无法正确预测系统 A 和系统 B 各自的不动点。图从根本上不足以唯一确定系统动力学。∎

B.4 Credence readoutB.4 置信度读出
We intentionally avoid text sampling. Sampling introduces random noise that looks identical to genuine variations in the model’s update rule. Furthermore, parsing sampled text often fails on malformed outputs, which would silently bias our dataset toward contexts the model happens to answer cleanly. We avoid these issues by computing the exact joint distribution over a two-digit answer and taking its mathematical expectation, as shown in Equation (2). The full procedure is:
我们有意避免对文本进行采样。采样会引入随机噪声，而这些噪声与模型更新规则的真实变化在表现上完全相同。此外，解析采样文本时，往往会因输出格式不正确而失败，这会在不知不觉中使我们的数据集偏向模型恰好能够规范作答的上下文。为避免这些问题，我们计算两位数答案的精确联合分布，并取其数学期望，如公式 (2) 所示。完整流程如下：
Render the four turn chat to token ids with the model’s chat template and add_generation_prompt=True.
使用模型的聊天模板，并设置 add_generation_prompt=True，将包含四个轮次的聊天转换为词元 ID。
Form ten sequences by appending each digit token "0" through "9". At construction we assert that each of the ten digits encodes to exactly one token under the model’s tokenizer, which holds for the Qwen and Gemma models.
分别追加数字词元 "0" 至 "9"，构成十个序列。在构造时，我们通过断言检查这十个数字中的每一个在模型的分词器下都恰好编码为一个词元；Qwen 和 Gemma 模型均满足这一条件。
Run one batched forward pass over those ten sequences using left padding, with explicit position ids computed as (attention_mask.cumsum(-1) - 1).clamp(min=0). Left padding is what keeps the final two positions aligned across contexts of different lengths.
对这十个序列使用左侧填充，执行一次批量前向传播，并使用通过 (attention_mask.cumsum(-1) - 1).clamp(min=0) 显式计算的位置 ID。左侧填充使不同长度上下文的最后两个位置保持对齐。
Read $p(d_{1}\mid\text{ctx})$ from the logits at position $-2$ . These are identical across the ten sequences because those sequences share the entire prefix, so one row suffices. Read $p(d_{2}\mid\text{ctx},d_{1})$ from position $-1$ , which gives one row per value of $d_{1}$ .
从位置 $-2$ 的 logits 中读取 $p(d_{1}\mid\text{ctx})$。由于这十个序列共享完整的前缀，因此它们在该位置的 logits 完全相同，只需取其中一行即可。从位置 $-1$ 读取 $p(d_{2}\mid\text{ctx},d_{1})$，由此得到的每一行对应 $d_{1}$ 的一个取值。
Renormalise both distributions over the ten digit tokens and return $\frac{1}{99}\sum_{d_{1}}\sum_{d_{2}}(10d_{1}+d_{2})\,p(d_{1})\,p(d_{2}\mid d_{1})$ .
在十个数字词元上对这两个分布分别重新归一化，并返回 $\frac{1}{99}\sum_{d_{1}}\sum_{d_{2}}(10d_{1}+d_{2})\,p(d_{1})\,p(d_{2}\mid d_{1})$。
The steps above are for Qwen and Gemma. Llama’s tokenizer stores every number from 00 to 99 as a single token, so for Llama we skip steps 2 to 5. We run one forward pass on the prompt alone, read the probability of each of the 100 tokens "00" to "99" at the last position, renormalise over them, and return $\frac{1}{99}\sum_{k=0}^{99}k\,p(k)$ . Mistral’s tokenizer puts a space token before a number, so for Mistral each of the ten sequences in step 2 is the prompt, then the space token, then $d_{1}$ , and steps 3 to 5 stay the same. We add the space token only if, at the answer position, the model puts more probability on it than on the ten digits together, which we check once on the first prompt. For both models, the readout tracks the number the model actually writes (correlation $0.90$ for Llama and $0.87$ for Mistral).
上述步骤适用于 Qwen 和 Gemma。Llama 的分词器将 00 至 99 中的每个数都存储为单个词元，因此对于 Llama，我们跳过步骤 2 至 5。我们仅对提示词执行一次前向传播，在最后一个位置读取 "00" 至 "99" 这 100 个词元各自的概率，对这些概率重新归一化，并返回 $\frac{1}{99}\sum_{k=0}^{99}k\,p(k)$。Mistral 的分词器会在数字前放置一个空格词元，因此对于 Mistral，步骤 2 中的十个序列均依次由提示词、空格词元和 $d_{1}$ 组成，步骤 3 至 5 保持不变。只有当模型在答案位置赋予空格词元的概率高于赋予十个数字词元的概率之和时，我们才添加该空格词元；我们在第一个提示词上检查一次这一条件。对于这两个模型，读出值均能反映模型实际写出的数字（Llama 的相关系数为 $0.90$，Mistral 为 $0.87$）。
We materialize only the final two positions by setting logits_to_keep=2. Computing logits for the entire sequence would allocate billions of floating-point values and exceed several gigabytes of memory, which would force us to use an impractically small batch size.
我们通过设置 logits_to_keep=2，仅实际生成最后两个位置的 logits。计算整个序列的 logits 需要分配数十亿个浮点值，占用超过数 GB 的内存，这将迫使我们使用小到不切实际的批量大小。
