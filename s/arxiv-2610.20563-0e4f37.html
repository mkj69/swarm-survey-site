Scaling Discovery Requires Forgetting扩大探索规模需要遗忘
Abstract摘要
Automated research with LLM agents has shown promising results in various domains. Through iterative refinement and scored verifiers, LLM agents are capable of discovering new ideas. There are different ways to scale automated research: let one agent iterate for longer, run several agents in parallel, or further let the parallel agents communicate and build on one another’s candidates. However, we find that all three strategies eventually plateau as the agents accumulate history. Each agent becomes anchored to its own past attempts, making fewer and smaller edits. Beyond this point, additional budget brings little further improvement. In this paper, we show that the key to scaling discovery further is to forget. Periodically discarding the search state, including every agent’s session and the candidate history, and restarting from the best candidate found so far, unblocks the search and pushes the same budget further.
基于大语言模型（LLM）智能体的自动化研究已在多个领域展现出良好的前景。通过迭代改进和评分式验证器，LLM 智能体能够发现新想法。扩大自动化研究规模有多种方式：让一个智能体进行更长时间的迭代，并行运行多个智能体，或进一步让并行智能体相互交流，并在彼此的候选方案基础上继续改进。然而，我们发现，随着智能体积累历史信息，这三种策略最终都会进入平台期。每个智能体都会受到自身过去尝试的锚定，修改次数越来越少，修改幅度也越来越小。超过这一阶段后，增加预算几乎无法带来进一步的改进。本文表明，进一步扩大探索规模的关键在于遗忘。定期丢弃搜索状态，包括每个智能体的会话和候选方案历史，并从迄今找到的最佳候选方案重新开始，可以打破搜索停滞，使相同预算带来更多进展。
We implement forgetting in a team of agents with communication and evaluate it on four tasks spanning heuristic programming, kernel optimization and model training. The results show up to 15% improvement compared to parallel agents, and up to 39% compared to a single agent. Our results suggest that, for open-ended discovery, what an agent is allowed to remember matters as much as how much compute it is given.
我们在一个能够相互交流的智能体团队中实现了遗忘机制，并在涵盖启发式编程、内核优化和模型训练的四项任务上进行了评估。结果显示，与并行智能体相比，提升幅度最高达 15%；与单个智能体相比，提升幅度最高达 39%。我们的结果表明，对于开放式探索而言，允许智能体记住什么，与为其提供多少计算资源同样重要。

1 Introduction1 引言
Automated research and discovery increasingly rely on iterative refinement, where a system proposes a solution, measures it, learns from the outcome, and proposes a better one. Recently, large language model (LLM) agents have automated this cycle end-to-end. These autoresearch agents have produced new results in mathematics, algorithm design, prompt optimization, and machine learning research (Agrawal et al., 2026; Novikov et al., 2025; Karpathy, 2026). As the underlying models grow more capable, autoresearch agents can now sustain this process for hours on a single problem (Liu et al., 2026a).
自动化研究与探索日益依赖迭代改进，即系统提出一个解决方案，对其进行评估，从结果中学习，并提出更好的方案。近年来，大语言模型（LLM）智能体已实现了这一循环的端到端自动化。这些自动研究智能体已在数学、算法设计、提示词优化和机器学习研究中取得了新成果（Agrawal et al., 2026; Novikov et al., 2025; Karpathy, 2026）。随着底层模型的能力不断增强，自动研究智能体如今能够针对单个问题持续数小时执行这一过程（Liu et al., 2026a）。
With more compute and time, an autoresearch agent can be scaled in three ways. One is to run a single agent longer, giving it more opportunities to refine its ideas. Another is to run multiple agents in parallel, exploring a broader set of ideas at once. A third is to allow those agents to share their progress, so that an idea discovered by one can be developed by the others. These three strategies mirror ways of scaling a human research effort.
借助更多计算资源和时间，可以通过三种方式扩大自动研究智能体的研究规模。一种方式是让单个智能体运行更长时间，使其有更多机会完善自己的想法。另一种方式是并行运行多个智能体，同时探索更广泛的想法。第三种方式是允许这些智能体共享进展，使一个智能体发现的想法能够由其他智能体继续发展。这三种策略与扩大人类研究工作规模的方式相对应。
However, in practice none of them scales indefinitely. We find that all three strategies eventually plateau, limited by the same underlying mechanism. In every strategy, each agent carries its growing autoresearch state and session context into every new proposal. As this history accumulates, the agent’s proposals become increasingly conservative, and the agent’s progress stalls well before the budget is exhausted (Figure 1).
然而，在实践中，这些策略都无法无限扩展。我们发现，三种策略最终都会进入平台期，并受到同一种底层机制的限制。在每种策略中，每个智能体都会将不断增长的自动研究状态和会话上下文带入每一次新方案的提出过程。随着这些历史信息不断积累，智能体提出的方案变得越来越保守，其进展在预算耗尽之前很早就陷入停滞（图 1）。
We introduce a key concept to address this plateau: forgetting. When an agent forgets, it discards its session, along with all candidate solutions and records in the filesystem state, and keeps only the best candidate. A new agent with an empty context then starts from this best candidate. Forgetting has two benefits. First, little of the progress made is lost. We observe that in iterative autoresearch, the best candidate often subsumes many effective heuristics from earlier iterations. Second, the new agent is no longer conditioned on earlier failed attempts.
我们引入了一个解决这一平台期问题的关键概念：遗忘。当智能体进行遗忘时，它会丢弃自身会话，以及文件系统状态中的所有候选方案和记录，仅保留最佳候选方案。随后，一个上下文为空的新智能体从这一最佳候选方案开始工作。遗忘有两个好处。首先，已经取得的进展几乎不会损失。我们观察到，在迭代式自动研究中，最佳候选方案往往已经吸收了先前迭代中的许多有效启发式方法。其次，新智能体不再受先前失败尝试的制约。
Forgetting works well with communication. In multi-agent team settings, agents build on each other’s candidates, so a good idea found by one agent quickly propagates to the others. We periodically make the whole team forget, where every agent reinitializes from the single best candidate. This clears the accumulated history before it constrains fresh exploration (Figure 2).
遗忘与交流能够很好地配合。在多智能体团队中，智能体会在彼此的候选方案基础上继续改进，因此，一个智能体发现的好想法会迅速传播给其他智能体。我们定期让整个团队遗忘，此时每个智能体都从唯一的最佳候选方案重新初始化。这会在累积的历史记录限制新的探索之前将其清除（图 2）。
We evaluate forgetting on four tasks: writing a heuristic solver for a programming contest problem (AtCoder Heuristic Contest 014; AtCoder, 2022), solving an algorithmic optimization problem (Frontier-CS; Mang et al., 2025), optimizing a VLIW kernel (Anthropic Kernel Challenge; Anthropic, 2026), and improving a PyTorch script for training a small language model (nanochat-autoresearch; Karpathy, 2026). A team of four agents with forgetting is best on every task and improves on the strongest baseline by 1.9% to 15%. These results show that forgetting unblocks plateaued discoveries.
我们在四项任务上评估遗忘机制：为编程竞赛问题编写启发式求解器（AtCoder Heuristic Contest 014; AtCoder, 2022）、求解算法优化问题（Frontier-CS; Mang et al., 2025）、优化 VLIW 内核（Anthropic Kernel Challenge; Anthropic, 2026），以及改进用于训练小型语言模型的 PyTorch 脚本（nanochat-autoresearch; Karpathy, 2026）。采用遗忘机制的四智能体团队在每项任务上均表现最佳，相较于最强基线提升了 1.9% 至 15%。这些结果表明，遗忘能够打破探索发现过程中的停滞。
In summary, we make the following contributions:
总而言之，我们的贡献如下：
We show that both sequential and parallel autoresearch agent scaling paradigms plateau under a fixed budget (Section 4.1). We further show that this plateau happens because the agent becomes more conservative as its session grows longer (Section 5).
我们表明，在固定预算下，自动研究（autoresearch）智能体的串行和并行扩展范式都会进入平台期（第 4.1 节）。我们进一步表明，出现这一平台期是因为智能体会随着会话的延长而变得更加保守（第 5 节）。
We propose forgetting: periodically discarding every agent’s session and the candidate history and restarting from the best candidate found so far (Section 3).
我们提出遗忘机制：定期丢弃每个智能体的会话及候选方案历史记录，并从迄今找到的最佳候选方案重新开始（第 3 节）。
We combine forgetting with a team of agents with communication, evaluate it on four diverse tasks, and find steady improvement over strong parallel-agent baselines, by 10% on average (Section 4).
我们将遗忘机制与可相互通信的智能体团队相结合，在四项不同的任务上进行评估，发现其相较于强大的并行智能体基线均有稳定提升，平均提升幅度为 10%（第 4 节）。
2 Related Work2 相关工作
Autoresearch.自动研究。
Hillclimbing with language models began with a single model call as the proposer inside an evolutionary loop. FunSearch (Romera-Paredes et al., 2024) and AlphaEvolve (Novikov et al., 2025) pair an LLM with an automated evaluator and a database of programs, and prompt optimizers (Yang et al., 2024; Agrawal et al., 2026) follow the same pattern, with adaptive variants that route budget across populations as the search stalls (Cemri et al., 2026) or evolve the search strategy itself (Liu et al., 2026b). Each proposal in these systems is a fresh prompt, and diversity is maintained at the level of the population. Agents can also branch together with their workspace, where forking outperforms single paths at matched cost (Tan et al., 2026). A more recent line of work hands the whole loop to a coding agent. ScientistOne (Meng et al., 2026) runs the full cycle from hypothesis to experiment to write-up, Meta-Harness (Lee et al., 2026) has an agent rewrite the harness around a model and keep the versions that score better, and autoresearch (Karpathy, 2026) reduces this to one agent that edits a training script in a single session and is told never to stop. However, recent studies also find that agents that inherit the history of failed attempts solve far fewer tasks than those that start clean (Tan et al., 2025) and such iterative loops lose diversity and stall (He et al., 2026; Virk et al., 2026).
利用语言模型进行爬山搜索，最初是在演化循环中通过单次模型调用来提出候选方案。FunSearch (Romera-Paredes et al., 2024) 和 AlphaEvolve (Novikov et al., 2025) 将 LLM 与自动评估器及程序数据库相结合，提示优化器（Yang et al., 2024; Agrawal et al., 2026）也遵循同样的模式，其中一些自适应变体会在搜索停滞时在不同种群之间调配预算（Cemri et al., 2026），或对搜索策略本身进行演化（Liu et al., 2026b）。在这些系统中，每次提出候选方案都使用全新的提示，而多样性则在种群层面维持。智能体还可以连同其工作区一起创建分支，在成本相同的情况下，分支探索优于单路径探索（Tan et al., 2026）。较近期的一类工作将整个循环交给编程智能体。ScientistOne (Meng et al., 2026) 执行从提出假设、开展实验到撰写报告的完整周期，Meta-Harness (Lee et al., 2026) 让智能体重写模型外围的运行框架并保留得分更高的版本，而 autoresearch (Karpathy, 2026) 则将这一过程简化为由单个智能体在一次会话中编辑训练脚本，并要求其永不停止。然而，近期研究也发现，继承失败尝试历史记录的智能体所能解决的任务远少于从头开始的智能体（Tan et al., 2025），而且此类迭代循环会丧失多样性并陷入停滞（He et al., 2026; Virk et al., 2026）。
Recent work also explores alternative ways to manage optimization state and the proposer agent. Stateless Language Agents (Zhang et al., 2026) use a stateless advisor that assigns each experiment to isolated workers, which coordinate through the harness state. A concurrent work, Fork-and-Flush (Cai et al., 2026), periodically forks an agent into fresh-context trajectories that inherit the whole workspace. While the idea of resetting agent context sessions is similar, we more aggressively remove the workspace state as well, keeping only the best candidate solution. Additionally, our agents communicate with each other within each round before and after forgetting without an orchestrator.
近期工作还探索了管理优化状态和候选方案提出智能体的其他方式。Stateless Language Agents (Zhang et al., 2026) 使用一个无状态顾问，将每项实验分配给彼此隔离的工作智能体，后者通过运行框架的状态进行协调。同期工作 Fork-and-Flush (Cai et al., 2026) 定期将一个智能体分叉为多条具有全新上下文的轨迹，这些轨迹继承整个工作区。尽管重置智能体上下文会话的思路相似，我们还会更彻底地移除工作区状态，仅保留最佳候选解。此外，在遗忘前后的每一轮中，我们的智能体都在没有编排器的情况下相互通信。
Multi-Agent Systems.多智能体系统。
Multi-agent LLM systems organize collaboration through assigned roles and structured communication (Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024), or drop the orchestrator and let agents coordinate through a shared log of findings and a task queue (Mao et al., 2026). Allowing agents to read or debate one another’s outputs can improve accuracy on individual queries (Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025), and teams with communication can outperform independent ones on open-ended optimization given clear feedback and enough compute (Park et al., 2026). Collaboration can still fail in systematic ways (Cemri et al., 2025), and the gain is conditional because communication has two effects on different timescales. It propagates improvements, which compounds over a long run with a verifier, and it homogenizes proposals, which happens the moment agents read one another’s full solutions (Ann et al., 2026). Under short budgets and matched compute the second effect dominates: teams often fail to beat a strong single agent (Kim et al., 2026), homogeneous agents saturate as their outputs correlate (Yang et al., 2026), deliberation drifts to a compromise that holds back the best member (Pappu et al., 2026), and debating agents adopt an incorrect majority view (Wynn et al., 2025).
基于 LLM 的多智能体系统通过角色分配和结构化通信来组织协作（Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024），或者去掉编排器，让智能体通过共享的发现日志和任务队列进行协调（Mao et al., 2026）。允许智能体阅读彼此的输出或就这些输出展开辩论，可以提高单个查询的回答准确率（Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025）；在反馈明确且计算资源充足的情况下，可相互通信的团队在开放式优化任务上能够优于彼此独立的团队（Park et al., 2026）。协作仍可能以系统性的方式失败（Cemri et al., 2025），而且其收益取决于具体条件，因为通信会在不同时间尺度上产生两种效应。通信会传播改进，在配有验证器的长期运行中，这些改进会产生复利效应；同时，通信也会使候选方案趋于同质化，而这一效应在智能体读到彼此完整解答的那一刻就会发生（Ann et al., 2026）。在预算有限且计算量相同的情况下，第二种效应占主导：团队往往无法超越强大的单个智能体（Kim et al., 2026），同质智能体的输出随着彼此相关性增强而使性能趋于饱和（Yang et al., 2026），讨论逐渐偏向一种限制最佳成员发挥的折中方案（Pappu et al., 2026），而参与辩论的智能体则会采纳错误的多数观点（Wynn et al., 2025）。

Given a problem, an autoresearch agent improves the solution iteratively: the agent proposes a candidate, a judge evaluates it, and then the agent uses scores and feedback from the judge to decide what to try next. As this loop runs, two types of history accumulate. The search state records every candidate together with its score and feedback, while the agent’s session holds the messages that produced these candidates, including the reasoning behind every attempt. Standard autoresearch, shown at the top of Figure 2, keep both kinds of history within one continuous session. A team with forgetting, shown at the bottom, handles this history in a specific way by periodically discarding every session together with the search state and keeping only the best candidate found so far.
给定一个问题，autoresearch 智能体会迭代改进解决方案：智能体提出一个候选方案，评判器对其进行评估，随后智能体利用评判器给出的分数和反馈决定下一步尝试什么。随着这一循环持续运行，两类历史信息会不断累积。搜索状态记录每个候选方案及其分数和反馈，而智能体的会话则保存生成这些候选方案的消息，包括每次尝试背后的推理。图 2 上半部分所示的标准 autoresearch 在一个连续会话中保留这两类历史信息。下半部分所示的具备遗忘机制的团队以一种特定方式处理这些历史信息：定期丢弃所有会话及搜索状态，仅保留迄今为止找到的最佳候选方案。
3.1 Forgetting3.1 遗忘
Forgetting divides the search into rounds. At the end of each round, the agent discards its session together with the search state and keeps only the best candidate found so far along with its score. A new agent then starts the next round from this candidate with an empty context and the same instructions as the first round. We call each such step a forget. In Figure 2, each forget line marks the end of a round.
遗忘将搜索划分为多个轮次。每轮结束时，智能体会丢弃其会话及搜索状态，仅保留迄今为止找到的最佳候选方案及其分数。随后，一个新的智能体以该候选方案为起点，在上下文为空、指令与第一轮相同的条件下开始下一轮。我们将每个这样的步骤称为一次遗忘。在图 2 中，每条遗忘线都标志着一轮的结束。
We keep only the best candidate because it already contains most of the progress of the previous agent sessions, while the discarded states mainly record how that progress was made. The new agent is no longer conditioned on earlier failed attempts towards certain optimization directions. Our observation shows that with a fresh context and search state, the autoresearch agent produces more thinking tokens and much larger edits (Section 5).
我们仅保留最佳候选方案，因为它已经包含了此前智能体会话所取得的大部分进展，而被丢弃的状态主要记录了这些进展是如何取得的。新的智能体不再受此前沿某些优化方向进行的失败尝试所制约。我们的观察表明，在全新的上下文和搜索状态下，autoresearch 智能体会生成更多思考 token，并进行幅度大得多的修改（第 5 节）。
3.2 Teams3.2 团队
We implement a simple communication scheme for our agent teams. A team consists of several agents that work on the same problem in parallel, each in its own session and with its own search state. Before each proposal, every agent gets access to other agents’ candidates as commits along with their scores. There is no direct, point-to-point communication between agents during the agent sessions. Therefore, communication is fully asynchronous and token-free. While this scheme is simple, we find that it works particularly well in our setting.
我们为智能体团队实现了一种简单的通信方案。一个团队由多个并行处理同一问题的智能体组成，每个智能体都有各自的会话和搜索状态。每次提出候选方案之前，每个智能体都可以访问其他智能体以提交（commit）形式保存的候选方案及其分数。在智能体会话期间，智能体之间没有直接的点对点通信。因此，通信完全异步，且不消耗 token。尽管这一方案很简单，但我们发现它在我们的设置下效果尤为出色。
Forgetting combines naturally with agent teams. Communication lets a good idea found by one agent reach the others within a few proposals. Yet each agent still works within its own growing session, so every agent stalls in the same way as a single agent. Similarly, we extend forgetting to the whole team. At each forget, every agent discards its session and search state, and all agents restart from the best candidate across the team. The bottom of Figure 2 shows this for a team of four. We discuss the benefit of combining forgetting with agent teams in Section 4.3.
遗忘可以自然地与智能体团队相结合。通信使一个智能体发现的好想法能够在几次候选方案提议之内传递给其他智能体。然而，每个智能体仍然在各自不断增长的会话中工作，因此每个智能体都会以与单个智能体相同的方式陷入停滞。类似地，我们将遗忘扩展到整个团队。每次遗忘时，每个智能体都会丢弃其会话和搜索状态，所有智能体都从整个团队的最佳候选方案重新开始。图 2 的下半部分以一个四智能体团队展示了这一过程。我们在第 4.3 节讨论将遗忘与智能体团队相结合的益处。
3.3 Implementation3.3 实现
Figure 3: The harness. The reducer reads the agent’s search state and its sessions and decides what the agent sees and which session it continues. The agent then edits a candidate and the judge scores it. The harness records the new candidate with its score in the search state and the turn in the agent sessions. A team runs four such loops at once, each with its own state, and the candidates of an agent’s peers come from the other three loops.
图 3：运行框架（harness）。归约器（reducer）读取智能体的搜索状态及其会话，并决定智能体能看到什么以及继续哪个会话。随后，智能体修改一个候选方案，评判器对其评分。运行框架将新候选方案及其分数记录到搜索状态中，并将该轮交互记录到智能体会话中。一个团队同时运行四个这样的循环，每个循环都有各自的状态，而一个智能体的同伴候选方案则来自另外三个循环。
We implement agent teams with forgetting in a small harness, where each agent runs the loop of Figure 3. The search state of an agent is an append-only record of its candidates, where every candidate is a commit in a version-controlled repository together with the judge’s score and feedback. The harness keeps the session script of every past agent session.
我们在一个小型运行框架中实现了具备遗忘机制的智能体团队，其中每个智能体都运行图 3 所示的循环。智能体的搜索状态是一份仅追加的候选方案记录，其中每个候选方案都是版本控制仓库中的一次提交，并附有评判器给出的分数和反馈。运行框架保留每次既往智能体会话的会话记录。
The central component is the reducer, which controls the search state and the agent sessions of the agent’s next turn. It decides which candidates the agent sees as files in its workspace and which session the agent continues. The agent then edits a candidate using only file tools, and the harness records the result and runs the judge on it. Every configuration we compare is a setting of the reducer: a single agent and the parallel agents see only their own candidates and continue their latest session, whereas a team also sees the candidates of its peers. Forgetting clears both kinds of memory. The harness starts every agent on a new search state that holds only the best candidate, so its workspace shows no earlier candidates. With no past session to continue, the agent also starts with an empty context.
核心组件是归约器（reducer），它控制搜索状态以及智能体下一轮所使用的会话。它决定智能体能在工作区中以文件形式看到哪些候选方案，以及智能体将继续哪个会话。随后，智能体仅使用文件工具编辑一个候选方案，运行框架则记录结果，并对其运行评判程序。我们比较的每种配置都对应归约器的一种设置：单智能体和并行智能体只能看到各自的候选方案，并继续各自最近的会话，而团队中的智能体还能看到同伴的候选方案。遗忘会清除这两类记忆。运行框架让每个智能体从仅包含最佳候选方案的新搜索状态开始，因此其工作区中不会显示更早的候选方案。由于没有可继续的既往会话，智能体也会从空上下文开始。
4 Experiments4 实验
We evaluate on four tasks. AtCoder Heuristic Contest 014 (AHC014) and Frontier-CS Q171 are combinatorial optimization problems. The Anthropic kernel performance challenge optimizes a kernel for a simulated VLIW SIMD machine. Finally, nanochat-autoresearch improves the training of a small language model in five minutes. Appendix A describes each task and its budget in detail (Table 4). We run all our experiments with the Pi agent (Zechner, 2025) and the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model (DeepSeek-AI, 2026).
我们在四项任务上进行评估。AtCoder Heuristic Contest 014（AHC014）和 Frontier-CS Q171 是组合优化问题。Anthropic 内核性能挑战要求针对一台模拟的 VLIW SIMD 机器优化内核。最后，nanochat-autoresearch 在五分钟内改进一个小型语言模型的训练。附录 A 详细描述了各项任务及其预算（表 4）。我们的所有实验均使用 Pi 智能体（Zechner, 2025）和 DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 模型（DeepSeek-AI, 2026）。
We evaluate the four tasks in three settings, a single agent, four parallel agents and a team with forgetting. All three settings use the same model and the same budget. The single agent continues one session for the whole budget, and the parallel agents split the budget without seeing one another’s work. The team with forgetting has four agents that communicate through their candidates. It forgets three times, which splits its budget into four rounds. We ran each configuration three times per task.
我们在三种设置下评估这四项任务：单智能体、四个并行智能体，以及带遗忘机制的团队。这三种设置均使用相同的模型和相同的预算。单智能体在整个预算内持续使用同一个会话，而并行智能体分摊预算，彼此无法看到对方的工作。带遗忘机制的团队由四个智能体组成，它们通过各自的候选方案进行交流。该团队进行三次遗忘，从而将预算分为四轮。对于每项任务，我们将每种配置运行了三次。

并行运行四个智能体对四项任务中的三项有所帮助（表 1）。例如，在 AHC014 上，并行智能体的平均最佳得分比单智能体提高了 21%。在 nanochat-autoresearch 上，它们没有带来任何收益。随着会话不断增长，并行智能体的进展也会放缓（图 1）。例如，在预算的后半段，它们在 AHC014 上的平均最佳得分仅提高了 1.2%，而 VLIW 内核的周期数仅减少了 2.4%。Frontier-CS Q171 是个例外，并行智能体在该任务上持续改进，但带遗忘机制的团队最终得分仍比它们高出 15%。
Communication alone does not remove the plateau either. Before its first forget, the team is simply a group of agents with communication that keep their sessions. On some tasks communication already helps at this point. For example, on Frontier-CS Q171 the team leads the parallel agents by 13% at the same spend, while on AHC014 it is slightly behind them. Still, communication does not prevent the plateau. As we observed in Figure 1, each team configuration’s progress still flattened before each forget. In an additional experiment in Section 4.3, continuing the sessions of a team with communication for 30 more proposals per agent gains only 1.4%.
仅靠通信也无法消除平台期。在第一次遗忘之前，该团队只是一组能够通信且保留各自会话的智能体。在某些任务上，通信在这一阶段就已带来帮助。例如，在 Frontier-CS Q171 上，在相同开销下，团队领先并行智能体 13%，而在 AHC014 上则略微落后。尽管如此，通信仍无法避免平台期。正如我们在图 1 中观察到的，每种团队配置的进展在每次遗忘前仍会趋于平缓。在第 4.3 节的一项补充实验中，让能够通信的团队继续各自的会话，使每个智能体再提出 30 个方案，仅带来了 1.4% 的收益。
4.2 Forgetting Unblocks the Plateau4.2 遗忘使搜索突破平台期
The team with forgetting achieves the best result on every task, both on average and in its best run (Table 1). On average across the four tasks, it improves on the parallel agents by 10% and on a single agent by 22%. For example, on AHC014 its mean best score is 14% above the parallel agents and 39% above a single agent. On the VLIW kernel it also has the smallest spread of the three configurations, being 10% better than the parallel agents and 23% better than a single agent.
采用遗忘机制的团队在每项任务上都取得了最佳结果，无论是平均表现还是最佳单次运行表现均如此（表 1）。在四项任务上，其平均表现比并行智能体高出 10%，比单个智能体高出 22%。例如，在 AHC014 上，其最佳得分的均值比并行智能体高出 14%，比单个智能体高出 39%。在 VLIW 内核任务上，它的结果离散程度也是三种配置中最小的，表现比并行智能体高出 10%，比单个智能体高出 23%。
Table 2 shows these gains over the rounds. The mean best score improves after every forget on every task, and 32 of the 36 forgets lead to a new best candidate within the next round. The gain from each forget generally shrinks over the rounds. For example, on AHC014 the mean best score rises by 11.2% after the first forget and by 1.7% after the third. This is expected, since most of our tasks have an explicit or implicit upper bound, and progress becomes harder as the search approaches it.
表 2 展示了各轮中的这些增益。在每项任务上，每次遗忘后最佳得分的均值都会提高，而且 36 次遗忘中有 32 次在下一轮内产生了新的最佳候选方案。每次遗忘带来的增益通常随轮次推进而减小。例如，在 AHC014 上，第一次遗忘后最佳得分的均值提高了 11.2%，而第三次遗忘后提高了 1.7%。这是预期之中的，因为我们的大多数任务都有显式或隐式的上界，随着搜索接近这一上界，取得进展会变得更加困难。

To understand whether forgetting helps across agent setups, we start from a run that has already plateaued and spend its next round in two ways. Continue keeps every session and candidate, as if the plateaued agent continues in place. Forget discards all the states and sessions and keeps only the best candidate. Both then run 30 more proposals per agent. We do this on AHC014 for a single agent, four parallel agents and a team of four (Figure 4 and Table 3).
为了解遗忘是否对不同智能体配置都有帮助，我们从一次已经进入平台期的运行出发，以两种方式执行其下一轮。继续运行会保留所有会话和候选方案，相当于让已进入平台期的智能体从原处继续。遗忘会丢弃所有状态和会话，仅保留最佳候选方案。随后，两种方式都让每个智能体再提出 30 次提案。我们在 AHC014 上针对单个智能体、四个并行智能体和一个由四个智能体组成的团队开展了这一实验（图 4 和表 3）。
Continuing barely moves any configuration. The single agent gains only 1.6%, the parallel agents 1.9% and the team 1.4%, even though every continued turn costs more because it carries the long session. Forgetting gains 4.5% for the single agent, 7.0% for the parallel agents and 9.2% for the team at little more than half the cost.
继续运行几乎无法使任何一种配置取得进展。单个智能体仅提升了 1.6%，并行智能体提升了 1.9%，团队提升了 1.4%，而且由于需要携带冗长的会话历史，继续运行的每一轮交互成本都更高。遗忘使单个智能体提升了 4.5%，并行智能体提升了 7.0%，团队提升了 9.2%，成本却仅略高于继续运行的一半。
Forgetting also works best with a team. After forgetting, the team gains 9.2%, the highest across all three setups. Four fresh agents explore more directions from the best candidate than one, and communication broadcasts a good direction found by one of them to the others within the same round. We show one concrete example of why this works in Section 5.
遗忘在团队配置下的效果也最好。遗忘后，团队提升了 9.2%，是三种配置中最高的。四个全新的智能体从最佳候选方案出发，能够比单个智能体探索更多方向，而通信使其中一个智能体发现的良好方向能在同一轮内广播给其他智能体。我们在第 5 节中给出了一个具体示例，说明这种机制为何有效。

Search Narrows as Sessions Grow搜索随会话增长而收窄
As sessions lengthen, agents make smaller edits and use less reasoning. In Figure 5, all three configurations begin with edits of around 100 lines and then decrease within the first few dollars. Reasoning tokens also falls to a third or less of its initial level. By the fourth dollar, the single agent only edits a few lines. Before its first reset, the team’s edits fall from about 150 lines to 30 and reasoning from about 28k tokens to 7k, showing the same decline as its performance plateaus in Figure 1.
随着会话变长，智能体的修改幅度越来越小，使用的推理也越来越少。在图 5 中，三种配置最初的编辑量均约为 100 行，随后在最初花费的几美元内下降。推理 token 数也降至初始水平的三分之一或更低。花费达到第四美元时，单智能体仅编辑几行。在首次重置之前，团队的编辑量从约 150 行降至 30 行，推理 token 数从约 28k 降至 7k，呈现出与图 1 中性能进入平台期时相同的下降趋势。
On VLIW, within the $40 budget of each of the three single-agent replicates, 532 of 763 completed turns produce no candidate, consuming 66.2% of the model spend. This behavior appears in all three replicates, consuming between 58.4% and 70.4% of their spend. These empty turns are not failures. In 531 of the 532, the agent reads at most one file, makes no edit and ends its turn by declaring a plateau. For example, after 34 turns at 1,710 cycles, one single agent writes “I’ve reached the practical limit of this machine configuration” and proposes nothing. Of the agent’s remaining 201 turns, 164 end the same way. Yet 1,710 cycles is far from the practical limit. The same agent later reaches 1,644 cycles, and the team with forgetting reaches 1,077 cycles on the same task.
在 VLIW 上，三次单智能体重复实验各自的 $40 预算内，已完成的 763 个回合中有 532 个未产生候选方案，消耗了模型开支的 66.2%。这种行为出现在全部三次重复实验中，消耗了各自开支的 58.4% 至 70.4%。这些空回合并非失败。在这 532 个回合中的 531 个回合里，智能体最多读取一个文件，不进行任何编辑，并以宣告已进入平台期来结束回合。例如，一个单智能体在性能维持于 1,710 个周期达 34 个回合后写道：“我已经达到了这种机器配置的实际极限”，且未提出任何方案。该智能体剩余的 201 个回合中，有 164 个以同样的方式结束。然而，1,710 个周期远非实际极限。同一智能体后来达到了 1,644 个周期，而采用遗忘机制的团队在同一任务上达到了 1,077 个周期。
The search can stall even when the agent continues to generate and consider new ideas. In one single-agent VLIW session, eleven consecutive turns produce no candidate even though the agent develops an untested selection-tree implementation. It initially rejects the idea using a mistaken comparison of its estimated cost with the kernel’s load bound. A later turn explicitly corrects that comparison and works through the register requirements, but still declines to implement the change because it appears too complex and risky. The session thus stalls where the agent continues to generate ideas but no longer turns them into experiments.
即使智能体仍在不断生成和考虑新想法，搜索也可能停滞。在一次单智能体 VLIW 会话中，尽管智能体构思了一种未经测试的选择树实现，连续十一个回合仍未产生任何候选方案。它最初将该实现的估计成本与内核的加载下界进行了错误比较，并据此否定了这一想法。在后续一个回合中，它明确纠正了这一比较，并详细分析了寄存器需求，但仍因该变更看起来过于复杂且风险过高而拒绝实施。因此，该会话陷入停滞：智能体继续生成想法，却不再将其付诸实验。
Forgetting Renews Collective Search遗忘使集体搜索重新活跃
After forgetting, the agents resume larger edits and use more reasoning tokens per proposal. In AHC014, edits return to about fifty changed lines and reasoning-token usage rises to twice its pre-reset level or more. The runs behind the same-state experiment in Figure 4 show a similar trend. Over the first ten proposals per agent, median edits are 40 lines after forgetting versus 6 under continuation, and median reasoning-token usage is 9.4k versus 2.9k. Both increases appear in all three fresh-team replicates, alongside the performance advantage in Table 3. Edit size and reasoning-token usage decline again within a fresh phase.
遗忘之后，智能体重新开始进行较大规模的编辑，并为每个提案使用更多推理 token。在 AHC014 中，编辑量回升至约五十行变更，推理 token 使用量则增至重置前的两倍或更多。图 4 中同状态实验所对应的各次运行也呈现出类似趋势。在每个智能体最初的十个提案中，遗忘后的编辑量中位数为 40 行，而继续运行时为 6 行；推理 token 使用量中位数分别为 9.4k 和 2.9k。这两项增长均出现在全部三次新团队重复实验中，并伴随着表 3 所示的性能优势。在一个新的阶段内，编辑规模和推理 token 使用量会再次下降。
Forgetting and communication work well together. Before the first forget in one VLIW team run, the team’s last improvements are only a few cycles each, taking its best from 1,212 to 1,201 cycles. After the forget, one fresh agent tries a much larger change, a new way to read values from the tree in the kernel. Its first attempt is slower at 1,409 cycles, and its second only matches the starting point. The agent then reads a peer’s diff and borrows a small trick that simplifies its new code. The combined version reaches 1,136 cycles, the largest single improvement of the round, and the team ends the round at 1,114 cycles. Without forgetting, the agent would stay conditioned on its accumulated context, keep making small changes and never try the bold idea. Without communication, the agent would give up on the idea after its two failed attempts, without absorbing a fix from its peer.
遗忘与通信能够良好协同。在一次 VLIW 团队运行的首次遗忘之前，团队最后几次改进每次仅减少几个周期，使其最佳结果从 1,212 个周期降至 1,201 个周期。遗忘之后，一个新智能体尝试了一项幅度大得多的变更，即采用一种从内核中的树读取数值的新方法。它的首次尝试更慢，需要 1,409 个周期，第二次尝试也仅与起点持平。随后，该智能体阅读了一个同伴的代码差异（diff），并借用了一个小技巧来简化自己的新代码。结合两者的版本达到了 1,136 个周期，成为该轮最大的单次改进，而团队在该轮结束时达到了 1,114 个周期。如果没有遗忘，智能体就会继续受其累积上下文的制约，不断进行小幅修改，始终不会尝试这一大胆想法。如果没有通信，智能体就会在两次尝试失败后放弃这一想法，无法吸收同伴提供的修正方法。
6 Conclusion6 结论
In conclusion, we propose forgetting, a general mechanism for long-running discovery agents. Forgetting addresses the plateau problem by discarding the sessions and the search state and keeping only the best candidate periodically.
总之，我们提出了遗忘这一适用于长期运行的发现型智能体的通用机制。遗忘通过定期丢弃会话和搜索状态、仅保留最佳候选方案来解决平台期问题。
Combined with a team of agents with communication, forgetting gives the best result on all four of our tasks and improves on parallel agents by up to 15% at the same budget. These results suggest forgetting pushes the performance of autoresearch agents. A natural next step is to decide when to forget and what to remember.
与能够通信的智能体团队结合后，遗忘在我们的全部四项任务上均取得了最佳结果，并在相同预算下相较于并行智能体实现了最高 15% 的提升。这些结果表明，遗忘提升了自动研究智能体的性能。一个自然的后续方向是决定何时遗忘以及记住什么。
