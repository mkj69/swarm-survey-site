摘要Abstract
社会中许多最紧迫的挑战——从疫情应对、供应链中断到气候适应——都源于数百万个体随时间推移不断作出决策所形成的集体行为。 Many of society’s most pressing challenges—from pandemic response to supply chain disruptions to climate adaptation—emerge from the collective behavior of millions of individuals making decisions over time. 大规模群体模型（Large Population Models，LPMs）通过以前所未有的规模模拟具有真实行为和交互的整个群体，为理解这些复杂系统提供了一种方法。 Large Population Models (LPMs) offer an approach to understand these complex systems by simulating entire populations with realistic behaviors and interactions at unprecedented scale.
LPMs 通过三项关键创新拓展了传统建模方法：能够同时高效模拟数百万个体的计算方法、能够从多样化现实世界数据流中学习的数学框架，以及连接模拟环境与物理环境的隐私保护协议。 LPMs extend traditional modeling approaches through three key innovations: computational methods that efficiently simulate millions of individuals simultaneously, mathematical frameworks that learn from diverse real-world data streams, and privacy-preserving protocols that bridge simulated and physical environments. 这使研究人员能够观察个体选择如何汇聚成系统层面的结果，并在现实世界实施干预措施之前对其进行测试。 This allows researchers to observe how individual choices aggregate into system-level outcomes and test interventions before real-world implementation.
当前的人工智能进展主要着眼于创建具有复杂个体能力的“数字人”，而 LPMs 则构建“数字社会”，通过丰富的交互揭示涌现现象。 While current AI advances primarily focus on creating "digital humans" with sophisticated individual capabilities, LPMs develop "digital societies" where the richness of interactions reveals emergent phenomena. 通过连接个体行为与群体尺度的动态，LPMs 为人工智能研究提供了一条互补路径——揭示集体智能，并为政策和社会创新在现实世界部署之前提供试验场。 By bridging individual behavior and population-scale dynamics, LPMs offer a complementary path in AI research—illuminating collective intelligence and providing testing grounds for policies and social innovations before real-world deployment. 我们在此讨论其技术基础以及一些开放问题。 We discuss the technical foundations and some open problems here. 1 引言1 Introduction
许多复杂的社会挑战——从疫情风险到供应链中断——都源于数百万个体在随时间推移不断作出决策的过程中产生的交互。 Many complex societal challenges - from pandemic risk to supply chain disruptions - emerge from the interactions of millions of individuals making decisions over time. 理解和应对这些挑战，需要具备以下能力的计算工具：(1) 大规模模拟具有真实性的群体，(2) 从异构数据流中学习，以及 (3) 与现实世界系统集成。 Understanding and addressing these challenges requires computational tools that can: (1) simulate realistic populations at scale, (2) learn from heterogeneous data streams, and (3) integrate with real-world systems.
基于智能体的模型（Agent-based models，ABMs）通过模拟在数字世界中行动并相互交互的一组自主实体（称为智能体），为捕捉这些动态提供了一种很有前景的解决方案。 Agent-based models (ABMs) offer a promising solution to capture these dynamics by simulating a collection of autonomous entities (called agents) that act and interact within a digital world. ABMs 已被证明在多个领域具有实用价值，包括流行病学 [5, 19, 17]、经济学 [3, 4, 8] 和灾害应对 [7, 16]。 ABMs have proven useful across various domains, including epidemiology [5, 19, 17], economics [3, 4, 8], and disaster response [7, 16]. 其自下而上的方法使涌现现象能够从简单规则中自然产生，从而帮助理解个体行为如何汇聚并形成系统层面的结果。 Their bottom-up approach allows emergent phenomena to arise naturally from simple rules, offering insights into how individual behaviors aggregate to create system-level outcomes. 然而，其实际效用一直受到三项根本性挑战的限制： However, their practical utility has been limited by three fundamental challenges:
规模：ABMs 难以在保持复杂智能体行为的同时，将模拟规模扩展至现实群体的大小。 Scale: ABMs struggle to scale to realistic population sizes while maintaining sophisticated agent behaviors. 近期将大语言模型（LLMs）用作智能体的研究展示了更接近人类的决策行为 [23, 27]，但仅限于由 25-1000 个智能体组成的小规模群体。 Recent work using large language models (LLMs) as agents has demonstrated more human-like decision-making [23, 27], but is restricted to small populations of 25-1000 agents. 然而，现实世界中的政策决策需要模拟数百万个兼具丰富行为和复杂交互的智能体。 Real-world policy decisions, however, require simulating millions of agents with both rich behaviors and complex interactions.
数据：ABMs 在大规模同化异构数据源时面临重大挑战。 Data: ABMs face significant challenges when assimilating heterogeneous data sources at scale. 当前的校准方法通常需要计算开销很大的采样方法，并且难以高效地纳入流式数据或处理高维参数空间。 Current calibration approaches typically require computationally intensive sampling methods and struggle to incorporate streaming data or handle high-dimensional parameter spaces efficiently. 无法实时、无缝地从多样化的现实世界数据流中学习，严重限制了 ABMs 的预测能力。 The inability to seamlessly learn from diverse real-world data streams in real-time significantly constrains ABMs’ predictive power.
反馈：ABMs 通常将智能体视为模仿现实世界对应实体的纯合成实体。 Feedback: ABMs typically treat agents as purely synthetic entities mimicking real-world counterparts. 然而，移动设备和物联网（IoT）设备的日益普及，为模拟智能体与物理智能体之间的双向反馈创造了机会。 However, the increasing ubiquity of mobile and IoT devices creates opportunities for bidirectional feedback between simulated and physical agents. 实现这一潜力需要能够同时处理集中式模拟和保护隐私的去中心化计算的框架——传统 ABM 实现缺乏这种能力。 Realizing this potential requires frameworks that can handle both centralized simulation and privacy-preserving decentralized computation - a capability lacking in traditional ABM implementations.

2 预备知识2 Preliminaries
基于智能体的模型（agent-based models，ABMs）通过表征个体实体（“智能体”）在计算环境中的行动与交互方式，为复杂系统仿真提供了一种自下而上的方法。 Agent-based models (ABMs) offer a bottom-up approach to simulating complex systems by representing how individual entities ("agents") act and interact within computational environments. 与描述总体行为的基于方程的模型不同，ABMs 能够刻画个体行动和局部交互的异质性，使涌现现象从简单规则中自然产生。 Unlike equation-based models that describe aggregate behaviors, ABMs capture the heterogeneity of individual actions and local interactions, allowing emergent phenomena to arise naturally from simple rules. 为明确术语：在 ABMs 中，“智能体”指的是代表具有自主行为的现实世界个体的计算实体，与代表用户执行任务的“AI 智能体”有所区别。 To clarify terminology: in ABMs, "agents" refer to computational entities that represent real-world individuals with autonomous behaviors, distinct from "AI agents" that execute tasks on behalf of users. ABM 智能体通过基于规则的决策过程来模拟现实世界中的行为，这些过程在离散时间步上执行，最终揭示个体决策如何汇聚为群体层面的结果。 ABM agents are designed to mimic real-world behaviors through rule-based decision processes, executed over discrete timesteps, ultimately revealing how individual decisions aggregate to population-level outcomes.
2.1 形式化表示2.1 Formal Representation
考虑一个由 $N$ 个个体组成的群体。 Consider a population of $N$ individuals. 我们用 $\bm{s}_{i}(t)$ 表示个体 $i$ 在仿真时刻 $t$ 的状态，其中包含个体的静态属性和随时间演变的属性。 We denote by $\bm{s}_{i}(t)$ the state of individual $i$ at simulation time $t$ which contains both static and time evolving properties of individuals. 例如，$\bm{s}$ 可以表示流行病学模型中人的年龄和疾病状态，或金融拍卖模型中企业的账户余额。 For instance, $\bm{s}$ may represent the age and disease status of humans in epidemiological models, or the account balance of firms in a financial auction model.
随着仿真的进行，个体 $i$ 通过与其邻居 $\mathcal{N}_{i}(t)$ 及其环境 $e(t)$ 交互来更新自身状态 $\bm{s}_{i}(t)$，而邻居和环境也都可以随时间演变。 As the simulation proceeds, an individual $i$ updates their state $\bm{s}_{i}(t)$ by interacting with their neighbors $\mathcal{N}_{i}(t)$ and their environment $e(t)$ , which can both also be time evolving. 个体的邻域可以通过图、基于邻近性的方法或其他方法来指定。 The neighborhood of an individual can be specified using a graph, a proximity method or other methods.
我们用 $m_{ij}(t)=M(\bm{s}_{j}(t),e_{ij}(t),\boldsymbol{\theta},t)$ 表示个体 $i$ 通过与邻居 $j$ 交互而获得的消息或信息。 We denote by $m_{ij}(t)=M(\bm{s}_{j}(t),e_{ij}(t),\boldsymbol{\theta},t)$ the message or information that individual $i$ obtains from their interaction with neighbor $j$ . 在流行病学模型中，这可以表示感染从个体 $j$ 传播至个体 $i$ 的过程，该过程取决于 $j$ 的传染性（$\bm{s}_{j}$）、病毒的属性（$\boldsymbol{\theta}$）以及交互的性质（$e_{ij}$）。 In an epidemiological model, this may represent the transmission of infection from individual $j$ to individual $i$ , dependent upon the infectivity of $j$ ( $\bm{s}_{j}$ ), the properties of the virus ( $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ ) and the nature of the interaction ( $e_{ij}$ ).
个体的结果还取决于其行为，该行为被建模为 $\ell(\cdot|\bm{s}_{i}(t))$。 The individual’s outcome also depends upon their behavior, modeled as $\ell(\cdot|\bm{s}_{i}(t))$ . 该行为函数表示个体根据自身状态和所处环境作出的决策，例如是否选择隔离、佩戴口罩或参加社交聚会。 This behavior function represents the decisions an individual makes given their state and environmental context—for example, whether they choose to isolate, wear masks, or participate in social gatherings. 因此，在时间步 $t$，每个智能体按如下方式更新其状态： Thus, at step $t$ , each agent updates its state as:

其中，$\oplus$ 表示对所有接收到的消息进行聚合。 where $\oplus$ denotes an aggregation over all received messages. 类似地，环境也可以具有自身的动态变化，这些变化取决于智能体的更新和行动， Similarly, the environment can also have it’s own dynamics that depend upon the agents’ updates and actions, 
| | $\bm{e}(t+1)=g\left(\bm{s}(t),e(t),\bm{\theta}\right).$ | | (2)
例如，在流行病学模型中，$e$ 刻画疾病传播、病毒演化、疫苗接种方案等的动态变化。 For instance, in an epidemiological model, $e$ captures the dynamics of disease transmission, viral evolution, vaccination protocols, etc.
$f$ 和 $g$ 的具体选择决定了仿真的动态变化，它们通常是随机函数，可以依据机理加以指定，也可以从数据中学习得到。 The specific choices of $f$ and $g$ define the dynamics of the simulation and they are typically stochastic functions which can be mechanistically specified or learned from data.
2.2 核心任务2.2 Core Tasks
ABMs 支持三项任务，它们构成了理解和分析复杂系统的基础： ABMs enable three tasks that form the basis for understanding and analyzing complex systems:
仿真包括初始化个体和环境的状态 $(\bm{s}(0),e(0))$，并在多个时间步上递归应用方程 1 和 2 中的更新规则。 Simulation involves initializing individual and environment states $(\bm{s}(0),e(0))$ and recursively applying the update rules in Equations 1 and 2 over multiple timesteps. 尽管仿真的状态空间十分庞大，我们通常关注的是以时间序列 $\bm{x}_{t}=h(\bm{s}(t))$ 表示的汇总结果。 While the state space of the simulation is vast, we typically focus on aggregate outcomes represented as time series $\bm{x}_{t}=h(\bm{s}(t))$ . 例如，流行病学仿真可以追踪不同人口群体中每日感染者的人数，而经济仿真则可以监测失业率和市场指标。 For example, epidemiological simulations might track the daily count of infected individuals across different demographic groups, while economic simulations might monitor unemployment rates and market indicators. 形式上，仿真可以表示为一个随机映射： Formally, the simulation can be expressed as a stochastic map: 
| | $\bm{x}=F(\bm{\theta},\bm{s}(0),\bm{e}(0)),$ | | (3)
其中，$F=(f,g)\circ\dots\circ(f,g)$ 表示智能体与环境更新函数在 $T$ 个时间步内重复进行的复合。 where $F=(f,g)\circ\dots\circ(f,g)$ represents the composition of agent and environment update functions repeated for $T$ timesteps. 这一过程的计算复杂度随种群规模和行为复杂程度的增加而增长，从而在传统 ABM 实现中形成了两者之间的权衡。 The computational complexity of this process grows with both population size and behavioral sophistication, creating a tension in traditional ABM implementations.
校准旨在确定使模拟输出与观测数据一致的参数 $\hat{\bm{\theta}}$（或参数的分布）。 Calibration aims to identify parameters $\hat{\bm{\theta}}$ (or a distribution over parameters) that make simulation outputs consistent with observed data. 形式上，校准是指调整结构参数 $\boldsymbol{\theta}$，使模拟输出 $\mathbf{x}$ 与给定观测数据 $\mathbf{y}$ 相容的过程。 Formally, calibration refers to the process of tuning structural parameters $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ so that simulation outputs $\mathbf{x}$ are compatible with given observational data $\mathbf{y}$ . 例如，在流行病学模型中，这意味着确定再生数和死亡率等参数的取值，使其与观测到的感染或死亡数据相符。 In epidemiological models, for instance, this entails determining values for parameters like the reproduction number and mortality rates to align with observed infection or mortality data.
由于 ABM 的随机性及其部分可观测性，多组参数值 $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ 都可能与观测数据 $\mathbf{y}$ 相容。 Due to the stochasticity of ABMs and their partial observability, multiple sets of parameter values $\boldsymbol{\theta}$ may be compatible with the observed data $\mathbf{y}$ . 因此，准确估计校准后参数的不确定性至关重要。 Consequently, accurate uncertainty estimation around calibrated parameters becomes essential. 纳入可能表明参数空间中某些区域更受偏好的专家知识（例如外部数据信号），对于稳健校准同样重要。 Incorporating expert knowledge (e.g. external data signals) that may indicate preferences for certain regions of the parameter space is also important for robust calibration.
这些要求可以通过贝叶斯框架来满足，其中参数推断对应于使用贝叶斯定理确定参数的后验分布 $\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta}\mid\mathbf{y})$：$\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta}\mid\mathbf{y})=\frac{p(\mathbf{y}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta});\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta})}{p(\mathbf{y})}$，其中 $\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ 是先验分布，$p(\mathbf{y}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta})$ 是似然函数，$p(\mathbf{y})$ 是边际似然。 These requirements can be addressed through a Bayesian framework, where parameter inference corresponds to determining the posterior distribution over parameters, $\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta}\mid\mathbf{y})$ using Bayes’ theorem: $\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta}\mid\mathbf{y})=\frac{p(\mathbf{y}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta});\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta})}{p(\mathbf{y})}$ , where $\pi(\boldsymbol{\theta})$ is the prior distribution, $p(\mathbf{y}\mid\boldsymbol{\theta})$ is the likelihood function, and $p(\mathbf{y})$ is the marginal likelihood. 对于 ABM，似然函数通常难以求解，因此需要采用无似然校准算法。 For ABMs, the likelihood function is typically intractable, necessitating likelihood-free calibration algorithms.
在实践中，校准涵盖一系列方法，从由领域专业知识指导的手动参数调整 [25]，到能够量化不确定性的复杂贝叶斯技术 [17, 6]。 In practice, calibration encompasses a spectrum of approaches, from manual parameter tuning guided by domain expertise [25] to sophisticated Bayesian techniques that quantify uncertainty [17, 6]. $F$ 的计算复杂性和随机性，以及 $\bm{\theta}$ 的高维性，使优化变得困难。 The optimization becomes challenging with computational complexity and stochasticity of $F$ and the high dimensionality of $\bm{\theta}$ .
分析利用经过校准的模型来理解系统动态、探索反事实情景，并为决策提供依据。 Analysis leverages calibrated models to understand system dynamics, explore counterfactuals, and inform decision-making. ABM 在分析任务中的独特之处在于，它们能够将宏观结果追溯到具体的智能体行为及其相互作用，从而提供纯统计模型无法提供的机制性洞见。 What distinguishes ABMs in analysis tasks is their ability to trace macroscopic outcomes to specific agent behaviors and interactions, providing mechanistic insights that purely statistical models cannot.
回溯性分析考察观测到的结果是如何由相互作用产生的。 Retrospective analysis examines how observed outcomes emerged from interactions. 例如，[22] 分析了社会人口因素如何导致 COVID-19 影响的差异，并识别了风险在不同人口群体之间的变化。 For example, [22] analyzed how sociodemographic factors contributed to COVID-19 disparities identifying how risk varies across demographics. 反事实分析通过改变条件或干预措施来探索“如果……会怎样”的情景。 Counterfactual analysis explores "what-if" scenarios by varying conditions or interventions. [25] 评估了推迟接种第二剂疫苗的做法，以确定这种推迟在何种条件下能够降低总体死亡率，并直接为多个国家的疫苗接种政策提供了依据。 [25] evaluated delaying second vaccine doses to identify conditions where such delays could reduce overall mortality—directly informing vaccination policies in multiple countries. 这些能力回应了卢卡斯批判（Lucas critique），即当智能体能够根据政策调整自身行为时，仅凭历史数据无法预测政策效果 [21]。 These capabilities address the Lucas critique—the observation that policy effects cannot be predicted solely from historical data when agents can adapt their behavior in response to policies [21]. 通过使用 $\ell(\cdot|\bm{s}_{i}(t))$ 显式建模行为适应，ABM 能够刻画干预措施如何重塑产生结果的决策规则，从而为政策设计提供更稳健的指导。 By explicitly modeling behavioral adaptation through $\ell(\cdot|\bm{s}_{i}(t))$ , ABMs capture how interventions reshape the decision rules that generate outcomes, providing more robust guidance for policy design.

4.1 贡献 1：可组合的领域专用语言4.1 Contribution 1: Composable Domain-specific Language
全面的模拟必须捕捉跨越不同时空尺度的多个相互作用的系统。 A comprehensive simulation must capture multiple interacting systems across different spatial and temporal scales. 例如，大流行动态环境 $e$ 必须同时表示接触网络中的疾病传播事件（离散时间模型）、个体内的病毒演化（使用微分方程的连续时间模型）、出行模式（通过神经网络学习）和经济刺激干预（离散事件逻辑）。 For instance, an environment of pandemic dynamics, $e$ , must simultaneously represent disease transmission events across contact networks (discrete-time models), individual viral evolution (continuous-time with differential equations), mobility patterns (learned via neural networks) and stimulus interventions (discrete-event logic). 在模拟的每一步，都必须在个体、家庭、社区和城市尺度上协调这些不同的动态过程。 These diverse dynamics must be coordinated across individual, household, neighborhood, and city-level scales, at each step of the simulation.
我们提出了 FLAME [12]，这是一种领域专用语言，可通过可组合的相互作用灵活地定义复杂的环境动态。 We introduce FLAME [12], a domain-specific language that enables flexible specification of complex environmental dynamics through composable interactions. 该框架扩展了第 2 节定义的环境更新函数 $e(t+1)=g(s(t),e(t),\theta)$，以支持多种建模范式和相互作用尺度。 This framework extends the environment update function $e(t+1)=g(s(t),e(t),\theta)$ defined in Section 2 to support multiple modeling paradigms and interaction scales. FLAME 通过三项关键能力应对这一挑战：模块化子步骤定义、张量化执行和基于梯度的组合。 FLAME addresses this challenge through three key capabilities: modular substep definition, tensorized execution, and gradient-based composition.

首先，函数式架构：FLAME 将环境动力学分解为模块化子步骤，每个子步骤均表示为一个变换仿真状态的函数映射 $\Phi_{i}:S\rightarrow S$。 First, functional architecture: FLAME decomposes environment dynamics into modular substeps, each represented as a functional mapping $\Phi_{i}:S\rightarrow S$ that transforms the simulation state. 每个子步骤都遵循观察—策略—转移模式，其中，观察函数 $o:S\rightarrow O$ 提取相关状态特征，策略函数 $\pi:H\rightarrow A$ 根据观察历史确定动作，转移函数 $t:S\times A\rightarrow S$ 则相应地更新状态。 Each substep follows an observation-policy-transition pattern, where observation functions $o:S\rightarrow O$ extract relevant state features, policy functions $\pi:H\rightarrow A$ determine actions based on observation histories, and transition functions $t:S\times A\rightarrow S$ update states accordingly. 这种分解能够精确建模异质动力学——一个子步骤可以通过基于机理的接触模型表示病毒传播事件，而另一个子步骤则可以通过训练得到的神经网络刻画移动模式。 This decomposition enables precise modeling of heterogeneous dynamics—a substep might represent virus transmission events through a mechanistic contact model, while another might capture mobility patterns through a learned neural network.
其次，张量化执行：FLAME 采用张量化执行，在数百万个智能体之间的数十亿次潜在交互中模拟这些子步骤。 Second, tensorized execution: FLAME implements tensorized execution to simulate these substeps across billions of potential interactions among millions of agents. 对于大规模群体，直接模拟所有两两交互很快就会变得难以计算。 For large populations, direct simulation of all pairwise interactions quickly becomes intractable. FLAME 将这些交互转化为稀疏张量运算，使其能够在普通 GPU 硬件上高效执行。 FLAME transforms these interactions into sparse tensor operations that can be efficiently executed on commodity GPU hardware. 这一优化依赖于两个原则：(1) 小世界交互网络，其中大多数智能体仅与群体中的有限子集交互，因此可以采用稀疏张量表示；(2) 子步骤内的置换不变性，即仿真步骤中运算的顺序不会影响结果。 This optimization relies on two principles: (1) small-world interaction networks, where most agents interact with a limited subset of the population, enabling sparse tensor representations; and (2) permutation invariance within substeps, where the order of operations in a simulation step doesn’t affect the outcome. 例如，在对某一天不同家庭中的疾病传播进行建模时，智能体的感染概率取决于其总暴露量，而非各次接触的先后顺序。 For instance, when modeling disease transmission across households on a given day, the likelihood of infection for an agent depends on their total exposure, not the sequence of individual contacts.
第三，可微设计：FLAME 通过可微的形式化定义确保子步骤的模块化。 Third, differentiable design: FLAME ensures modularity of substeps via differentiable specification. 形式上，对于定义在仿真状态上的光滑目标 $L=f(\Phi_{i}(S_{T}))$，若梯度 $\nabla_{\theta}L$ 存在且可以计算，则环境子步骤 $\Phi_{i}$ 是可微的。 Formally, each environment substep $\Phi_{i}$ is differentiable if, given a smooth objective $L=f(\Phi_{i}(S_{T}))$ defined on the simulation state, the gradient $\nabla_{\theta}L$ exists and can be computed. FLAME 通过对随机机制应用重参数化技术来保持可微性，而不是近似梯度或用代理模型替换随机组件。 Rather than approximating gradients or replacing stochastic components with surrogate models, FLAME maintains differentiability by applying reparameterization techniques to stochastic mechanisms. 例如，对离散接触事件进行重参数化，可以在保持符合现实的随机性的同时保留梯度流。每个子步骤的这种可微性支持通过链式法则进行基于梯度的组合，并允许对复杂仿真进行模块化设计。 For example, discrete contact events are reparameterized to preserve gradient flow while maintaining realistic stochasticity.This differentiability of each substep enables gradient-based composition, via the chain rule, and allows modular design of complex simulations. 正如下文所示，这种方法支持跨尺度的高效校准、敏感性分析和优化，使研究人员能够探究复杂的反馈回路，例如病毒特性可能如何响应城市层面的病例统计数据而发生适应性变化。 As we see later, this approach enables efficient calibration, sensitivity analysis, and optimization across scales—allowing researchers to investigate complex feedback loops like how viral properties might adapt in response to city-level case statistics.
这些能力极大地改变了我们对复杂环境进行建模的能力。 These capabilities dramatically transform our ability to model complex environments. 通过将复杂动力学分解为可组合、可微的子步骤，并以大规模方式高效执行这些子步骤，FLAME 在模拟 8.4 百万个智能体时，相较于传统实现取得了 200× 的加速 [24]。 By decomposing intricate dynamics into composable, differentiable substeps and executing them efficiently at scale, FLAME achieves a 200× speedup compared to traditional implementations when simulating 8.4 million agents [24]. 这使得此前难以计算的情景在计算上变得可行，从而能够更准确地表示智能体相互交互所处的环境。 This makes previously intractable scenarios computationally feasible, enabling more accurate representation of the environments within which agents interact.
