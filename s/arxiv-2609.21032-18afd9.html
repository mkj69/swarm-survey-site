Science advances not in isolation but through collaboration, yet existing agentic systems capture little of this. Whether communicating agents help remains an open question with mixed prior results. We show that test-time communication can substantially outperform independent parallel attempts on challenging tasks, where sharing a breakthrough can push the whole group forward. We first study the effect of scaling multi-agent test-time communication, where agents have no predefined roles and communicate via a shared directory, on ARC-AGI-3, a benchmark requiring novel problem solving. We find that a team of $k$ communicating agents, team@ $k$ , matches the success rate of $4k$ independent agents, and this advantage grows with $k$ , suggesting gains compound with scale. The effect is not merely efficiency: a task that no single agent can solve, a team of agents can solve reliably. Furthermore, these gains transfer to research-oriented tasks, given sufficient compute. On polyomino packing, communicating agents outperform best@ $k$ and exceed the prior best-known score. On MNIST classifier compression, communication surpasses the best-known human solution. A team of four agents produced a 1,957-byte classifier submission achieving 99.4% test accuracy, smaller than both the best-known human solution and the best single-agent result. These gains are not unconditional. Independent agents may outperform communication when compute is limited or when a clear measure of progress is absent. However, under sufficient compute and clear feedback, multi-agent communication consistently yields stronger results.
科学通过协作而非孤立研究取得进步，但现有智能体系统很少体现这一点。相互通信的智能体是否有帮助，仍是一个悬而未决的问题，既有研究的结果也不一致。我们表明，在具有挑战性的任务中，测试时通信可以显著优于相互独立的并行尝试，因为分享一项突破能够推动整个团队前进。我们首先在 ARC-AGI-3 这一要求解决新颖问题的基准上，研究扩大多智能体测试时通信规模的效果；其中，智能体没有预定义角色，并通过共享目录进行通信。我们发现，由 $k$ 个相互通信的智能体组成的团队 team@ $k$，其成功率可与 $4k$ 个独立智能体相当，而且这一优势随 $k$ 增大而扩大，表明收益会随规模扩大而累积。这一效果并不只体现在效率上：对于任何单个智能体都无法解决的任务，智能体团队能够可靠地解决。此外，在计算资源充足的情况下，这些收益也能迁移到研究导向的任务中。在多连方（polyomino）装填任务中，相互通信的智能体优于 best@ $k$，并超过了此前已知的最佳分数。在 MNIST 分类器压缩任务中，通信超越了已知最佳人工方案。一个由四个智能体组成的团队生成了一份大小为 1,957 字节的分类器提交结果，测试准确率达到 99.4%，其体积小于已知最佳人工方案和最佳单智能体结果。这些收益并非在任何条件下都能获得。当计算资源有限或缺乏明确的进展衡量指标时，独立智能体的表现可能优于相互通信的智能体。然而，在计算资源充足且反馈明确的条件下，多智能体通信能够持续取得更好的结果。
1 Introduction1 引言
For test-time compute, it is standard to sample $k$ independent outputs in parallel from a large language model (LLM) and aggregate them, e.g., by taking the majority answer or the best@ $k$ when a verifier is available (Wang et al., 2023; Brown et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2025; Snell et al., 2025). Independent attempts, however, leave discoveries made during one run unavailable to guide another while its search is still unfolding. This opportunity is especially crucial for LLM agents, which operate a terminal, execute code, use tools, and observe results over horizons of hours or days (Yang et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2025; Merrill et al., 2026). Their trajectories contain intermediate results, failed experiments, and reusable artifacts that could help other agents avoid dead ends or build on promising approaches.
在测试时计算中，标准做法是从大型语言模型（LLM）并行采样 $k$ 个独立输出并进行聚合，例如采用多数答案，或在有验证器可用时采用 best@ $k$ (Wang et al., 2023; Brown et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2025; Snell et al., 2025)。然而，相互独立的尝试使得一次运行中取得的发现无法在另一次运行的搜索仍在进行时为其提供指导。这种机会对 LLM 智能体尤其重要，因为它们会在数小时乃至数天的时间跨度内操作终端、执行代码、使用工具并观察结果 (Yang et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2025; Merrill et al., 2026)。它们的执行轨迹包含中间结果、失败的实验和可复用的产物，这些内容可以帮助其他智能体避开死胡同，或在有前景的方法上继续推进。
Communication between agents would be the natural remedy, but the evidence for its benefits is mixed. While keeping the best of $k$ independent candidates improves with $k$ , having agents exchange opinions may not (Choi et al., 2025). Multi-agent debate often fails to outperform chain-of-thought prompting despite using substantially more inference compute (Smit et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025d). Agents can defer to an incorrect majority or dilute expertise by compromising between expert and non-expert judgments (Wu et al., 2025; Pappu et al., 2026). Most recently, Anthropic (2026b) report that coordinating agents find more vulnerabilities than independent agents, but use more tokens and search more broadly. Within the common search scope, tokens per vulnerability are comparable, leaving the efficiency gains from communication unclear. On the other hand, multi-agent systems clearly help when a task can be decomposed into subtasks and solved in parallel (Kim et al., 2025), but such gains are limited to cleanly decomposable tasks. Can communication offer more than just parallelism?
智能体之间的通信是顺理成章的补救办法，但有关其益处的证据并不一致。虽然从 $k$ 个独立候选结果中保留最佳结果的表现会随 $k$ 增大而提升，但让智能体交换意见未必如此 (Choi et al., 2025)。尽管使用了多得多的推理计算资源，多智能体辩论往往仍无法优于思维链提示 (Smit et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025d)。智能体可能服从错误的多数意见，或在专家与非专家的判断之间折中，从而削弱专业判断的作用 (Wu et al., 2025; Pappu et al., 2026)。最近，Anthropic (2026b) 报告称，相互协调的智能体比独立智能体发现了更多漏洞，但也使用了更多 token，搜索范围也更广。在共同的搜索范围内，每发现一个漏洞所需的 token 数量相当，因此通信带来的效率收益仍不明确。另一方面，当任务能够分解为子任务并并行求解时，多智能体系统显然有所帮助 (Kim et al., 2025)，但这种收益仅限于能够清晰分解的任务。通信能否带来超越并行处理本身的收益？
Science suggests that it can. Scientists coordinate by adjusting their research to the results achieved by others (Polanyi, 1962), while accumulated empirical evidence helps distinguish competing explanations and turn varied efforts into collective progress (Strevens, 2020). This process is iterative rather than merely parallel. Collaboration should therefore do more than divide and conquer. One agent’s observation redirects another’s search, and partial discoveries combine into a result that no individual reaches alone. We ask whether agent communication can produce the same effect.
科学实践表明，这是可能的。科学家通过根据他人取得的结果调整自身研究来进行协调 (Polanyi, 1962)，而积累的实证证据有助于区分相互竞争的解释，并将不同的努力转化为集体进步 (Strevens, 2020)。这一过程是迭代式的，而不仅仅是并行的。因此，协作应当发挥超越分而治之的作用。一个智能体的观察会改变另一个智能体的搜索方向，局部发现则会结合起来，形成任何个体都无法独自取得的结果。我们探究智能体通信能否产生同样的效果。
To this end, we study a minimal form of test-time communication in which identical agents receive the same open-ended objective and communicate through a shared workspace, without predefined roles or a central orchestrator. Autonomously and asynchronously, the agents can exchange intermediate results, failures, and artifacts while their search is still unfolding. Our central comparison pits a team of $k$ communicating agents, team@ $k$ , against the best result from $k$ independent agents, best@ $k$ , under the same per-agent resources. This isolates the value of communication from the gains produced by additional parallel attempts.
为此，我们研究了一种最简形式的测试时通信（test-time communication）：相同的智能体接收同一个开放式目标，并通过共享工作空间进行通信，无需预先设定角色或中央协调器。在搜索仍在进行时，智能体可以自主、异步地交换中间结果、失败经验和产出物。我们的核心比较是在每个智能体资源相同的条件下，将由 $k$ 个可通信智能体组成的团队 team@ $k$ 与 $k$ 个独立智能体中的最佳结果 best@ $k$ 进行对比。这将通信的价值与增加并行尝试次数所带来的收益区分开来。
We first study ARC-AGI-3 (ARC Prize Foundation, 2026) using Claude Sonnet 4.6. In terms of full-game success rate, team@3 matches best@13 and team@5 matches best@33, making them as effective as $4.3$ and $6.6$ times as many independent agents (Figure 1). More strikingly, communicating teams solve games that independent agents rarely or never solve. These benefits transfer to longer-horizon research problems. On Frontier-CS polyomino packing (Mang et al., 2026), where agents pack polyominoes into a minimum-area rectangle, team@4 scores 0.922 with Claude Opus 4.6 and 0.910 with Sonnet 4.6, compared with 0.893 and 0.891, respectively, for the strongest solo run of each model and 0.894 for the prior best-known result. On MNIST classifier compression under an accuracy constraint, we evaluate team@4 with GPT-5.6 Sol over 96 hours. The best team produces a state-of-the-art 1,957-byte classifier at 99.4% accuracy, beating the 2,461-byte best-known human solution and single-agent baselines.
我们首先使用 Claude Sonnet 4.6 研究 ARC-AGI-3 (ARC Prize Foundation, 2026)。在完整游戏通关率方面，team@3 与 best@13 相当，team@5 与 best@33 相当，即它们的效果分别相当于数量为其 $4.3$ 倍和 $6.6$ 倍的独立智能体（图 1）。更引人注目的是，可通信团队能够解决独立智能体极少解决或从未解决的游戏。这些收益也能迁移到时间跨度更长的研究问题上。在 Frontier-CS 多连方装箱任务（polyomino packing）(Mang et al., 2026) 中，智能体需要将多个多连方放入面积最小的矩形；使用 Claude Opus 4.6 和 Sonnet 4.6 时，team@4 的得分分别为 0.922 和 0.910，相比之下，这两个模型各自表现最强的单智能体运行得分分别为 0.893 和 0.891，而此前已知的最佳结果为 0.894。在准确率约束下的 MNIST 分类器压缩任务中，我们使用 GPT-5.6 Sol 对 team@4 进行了持续 96 小时的评估。表现最好的团队生成了一个达到当前最优水平的分类器，其大小为 1,957 字节，准确率为 99.4%，优于此前已知最佳的 2,461 字节人工方案和单智能体基线。
These results suggest that test-time communication is most useful when agents have enough compute to build on one another’s discoveries and can objectively assess whether those discoveries improve on earlier results. Sufficient compute allows teams to overcome initial coordination costs, while an accessible verifier, such as level success or a solution scorer, helps agents decide which discoveries to adopt. We call this mechanism verified progress sharing. By building on successive breakthroughs from different members, a communicating team can turn parallel exploration into cumulative progress.
这些结果表明，当智能体拥有足够的计算资源来利用彼此的发现继续推进，并且能够客观评估这些发现是否优于先前结果时，测试时通信最为有用。充足的计算资源使团队能够克服初期的协调成本，而可用的验证器，例如关卡通关判定或解答评分器，则帮助智能体决定采纳哪些发现。我们将这一机制称为经验证的进展共享（verified progress sharing）。通过在不同成员相继取得的突破之上继续推进，可通信团队能够将并行探索转化为累积进展。
When agents cannot verify intermediate progress, however, communication may offer less benefit. On Terminal-Bench 2.0, where available feedback may not reliably rank intermediate solutions, we find that team@2 improves over a single attempt but does not outperform independent pass@2. This distinction also offers an explanation for the prior negative results, where agents lack a verifier to distinguish better solutions from worse ones and therefore abandon stronger solutions in favor of weaker ones (Choi et al., 2025; Pappu et al., 2026). Together, our findings show that communication can offer more than divide and conquer parallelism and help identify the conditions under which test-time communication excels.
然而，当智能体无法验证中间进展时，通信带来的收益可能较小。在 Terminal-Bench 2.0 上，可获得的反馈可能无法可靠地对中间解答进行优劣排序；我们发现，team@2 优于单次尝试，但并未超过独立尝试的 pass@2。这一区别也为先前的负面结果提供了解释：在那些研究中，智能体缺少能够区分解答优劣的验证器，因此会放弃更好的解答，转而采用较差的解答 (Choi et al., 2025; Pappu et al., 2026)。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，通信能够提供超越分治式并行的收益，并有助于确定测试时通信表现出色所需的条件。

We study a minimal form of test-time communication instantiated through a shared workspace among identical agents. In each team@ $k$ trial, the harness launches $k$ CLI agents concurrently in the same task container. They use the same model, tools, task instruction, and communication prompt. Each agent has a separate model context and designated scratch directory, while all agents share the task filesystem and communication artifacts.
我们研究一种最简形式的测试时通信，通过相同智能体之间的共享工作空间实现。在每次 team@ $k$ 试验中，框架会在同一个任务容器内并发启动 $k$ 个 CLI 智能体。它们使用相同的模型、工具、任务指令和通信提示。每个智能体都有独立的模型上下文和指定的临时工作目录，而所有智能体共享任务文件系统和通信产物。
Agents communicate directly through an append-only communication log, which acts as an asynchronous broadcast channel. Additional shared records contain adopted approaches, disconfirming evidence, and a score log of the approaches so far. Without assigned roles or a central orchestrator, the only protocol-level allocation, enforced by a synchronization primitive, is slot ownership. To ensure that agents claim distinct approaches without collision, agents race to create numbered slot directories using an atomic filesystem operation.
智能体通过仅追加的通信日志直接通信，该日志充当异步广播通道。其他共享记录包含已采纳的方法、反驳性证据，以及迄今各方法的得分日志。在未指定角色、也没有中央协调器的情况下，协议层面唯一的分配是由同步原语强制实施的槽位归属。为确保智能体能够无冲突地认领不同方法，智能体使用原子文件系统操作，竞争创建带编号的槽位目录。
The communication prompt (Appendix A.2) specifies how agents use these mechanisms and discourages premature convergence. After claiming a slot, each agent is asked to declare a distinct approach by considering the already-claimed slots. During execution, agents publish concise findings with timestamps and reproducible evidence whenever they make notable progress, allowing peers to reproduce or build upon their results. Results placed on the leaderboard should include measured outcomes, reproduction instructions, approach lineage, or known counterevidence. An agent is to adopt a peer’s approach only after observing a clearly better result, and even after adoption, it should preserve one meaningful variation.
通信提示（附录 A.2）规定了智能体如何使用这些机制，并抑制过早收敛。认领槽位后，每个智能体都被要求在考虑已被认领的槽位的基础上，声明一种不同的方法。在执行过程中，每当取得显著进展时，智能体都会发布简洁的发现，并附上时间戳和可复现的证据，使其他智能体能够复现其结果或在其基础上继续推进。列入排行榜的结果应包含实测结果、复现说明、方法的演变脉络或已知反证。智能体只有在观察到明显更好的结果后，才应采纳其他智能体的方法；即使采纳，也应保留一种有意义的变体。
The protocol combines diverse exploration with evidence-based adoption to discourage convergence on poor solutions. Atomic slot ownership, together with the requirement to pursue distinct approaches, helps preserve diversity. Verifier scores give agents a basis for filtering out poorly performing approaches when deciding what to adopt. This contrasts with the task settings of Pappu et al. (2026), where agents lack verifier scores for agent solutions and can dilute expertise by compromising between expert and non-expert judgments.
该协议将多样化探索与基于证据的采纳相结合，以抑制向劣质解收敛。槽位归属的原子性，加上采用不同方法的要求，有助于保持多样性。验证器得分为智能体在决定采纳何种方法时筛除表现不佳的方法提供了依据。这与 Pappu et al. (2026) 的任务设置形成对比：在其设置中，智能体无法获得针对智能体解答的验证器得分，并且可能因在专家与非专家判断之间折中而削弱专业知识的作用。

We study multi-agent communication on the following three tasks, each covering a different aspect of open-ended research tasks.
我们在以下三项任务上研究多智能体通信，每项任务涵盖开放式研究任务的一个不同方面。
ARC-AGI-3ARC-AGI-3
(ARC Prize Foundation, 2026) tests novel problem solving and interactive discovery by asking agents to solve unfamiliar grid-world games without instructions, inferring the rules, the goal, and the effect of each control from play alone. Each game comprises $l_{\max}\in[6,10]$ levels, and progress depends on carrying forward what was learned in earlier ones. This leveled structure makes breakthroughs cleanly measurable. Real-world research problems may not offer such a clean signal. A genuine conceptual advance in a domain such as approximation factors for NP-hard problems may move the reported number by only a small constant, an improvement easily lost in run-to-run variance.
（ARC Prize Foundation, 2026）通过要求智能体在没有说明的情况下解决陌生的网格世界游戏，仅凭游玩推断规则、目标及各项控制操作的效果，来测试新颖问题求解与交互式发现能力。每个游戏包含 $l_{\max}\in[6,10]$ 个关卡，后续进展依赖于沿用先前关卡中学到的知识。这种分关卡的结构使突破能够得到清晰的度量。现实世界中的研究问题可能无法提供如此清晰的信号。在 NP-hard 问题的近似因子等领域，真正的概念性进展可能仅使报告的数值改变一个很小的常数，而这种改进很容易被不同运行之间的波动所掩盖。
We evaluate on all 25 public games with Claude Sonnet 4.6 under the benchmark’s native per-level action budget and measure solve rate as the proportion of trials that clear all levels successfully. Though more recent models such as GPT-6 Astra now succeed easily on the benchmark, we focus on one model, especially one that does not saturate, to isolate the effect of test-time communication. For teams, an agent that exhausts its action budget is terminated, while the remaining agents continue. We run 64 single-agent trials per game and label a game unsolved by single agents if no trial clears all levels. For teams of three and five agents, we run 20 trials for every combination of game and team size.
我们使用 Claude Sonnet 4.6，在该基准原有的每关动作预算下，对全部 25 个公开游戏进行评估，并将成功通过所有关卡的试验所占比例作为解题成功率。尽管 GPT-6 Astra 等更新的模型如今已能轻松完成该基准，我们仍聚焦于单一模型，尤其是表现尚未饱和的模型，以单独考察测试时通信的影响。对于团队，耗尽动作预算的智能体会被终止，其余智能体则继续运行。我们对每个游戏进行 64 次单智能体试验；如果没有任何一次试验通过所有关卡，则将该游戏标记为单智能体未解出。对于由三个和五个智能体组成的团队，我们对每种游戏与团队规模的组合进行 20 次试验。

Runtime Environment.运行环境。
Every agent operates through GitHub Copilot CLI (GitHub, 2026), with each trial isolated as a Harbor task (Harbor Framework Team, 2026). Single-agent trials contain one CLI agent. Team@ $k$ trials contain $k$ agents in the same task container. Agents receive the same task, tools, and base filesystem, but have private scratch directories. Within a team, agents communicate explicitly. They append timestamped claims, evidence, failures, and adoption events to shared logs, publish measured candidates to a board, and use file locks to serialize changes to a shared graded artifact. Distinct trials share neither files nor messages. Hidden evaluation data are kept outside the agent container. Polyomino submissions are sent to a separate scorer service, while MNIST submissions are evaluated by a host-owned oracle in a fresh network-disabled container. These services return evaluation feedback but never expose the underlying cases, labels, or retained program outputs.
每个智能体均通过 GitHub Copilot CLI（GitHub, 2026）运行，每次试验都作为一个 Harbor 任务（Harbor Framework Team, 2026）进行隔离。单智能体试验包含一个 CLI 智能体。Team@ $k$ 试验在同一个任务容器中包含 $k$ 个智能体。各智能体获得相同的任务、工具和基础文件系统，但拥有各自私有的临时工作目录。团队内的智能体通过显式方式进行通信。它们将带有时间戳的主张、证据、失败记录及采纳事件追加到共享日志中，将经过测量的候选方案发布到看板上，并使用文件锁来串行化对共享待评分产物的修改。不同试验之间既不共享文件，也不共享消息。隐藏评估数据保存在智能体容器之外。Polyomino 提交内容会被发送至独立的评分服务，而 MNIST 提交内容则由宿主机控制的评估器在一个全新且禁用网络的容器中进行评估。这些服务会返回评估反馈，但绝不会暴露底层测试用例、标签或保留的程序输出。
Metrics.评估指标。
Our central comparison is the outcome of $k$ communicating agents (team@ $k$ ) versus the best outcome among $k$ independent agents (best@ $k$ ). We measure both metrics against wall-clock time and against total output tokens to account for compute. For ARC-AGI-3, a level counts as solved if any of the $k$ agents clears it, and a game counts as solved if any of the $k$ agents clears all levels. For a finite ARC solo pool with $s$ successes among $n$ trials, we compute best@ $k$ exactly, without replacement, as $\mathrm{best@}k=1-\binom{n-s}{k}/\binom{n}{k}$ . We apply the same calculation level by level and average over games, giving each game equal weight. Throughout the paper, solve rate refers to the final solve rate averaged over all 25 games, and per-level rates are labeled explicitly. We measure team@ $k$ and best@ $k$ as the best-so-far packing score (mean packed-cell density across 70 cases) or MNIST classifier compression size (gzipped submission size including code and weights).
我们的核心比较是 $k$ 个相互通信的智能体所取得的结果（team@ $k$）与 $k$ 个独立智能体中的最佳结果（best@ $k$）之间的比较。我们分别以实际耗时和输出 token 总数为横轴衡量这两项指标，以计入计算资源的消耗。对于 ARC-AGI-3，只要 $k$ 个智能体中有任意一个通过某个关卡，该关卡就算解决；只要其中有任意一个通过某个游戏的全部关卡，该游戏就算解决。对于一个有限的 ARC 单智能体试验池，若 $n$ 次试验中有 $s$ 次成功，我们按无放回抽样精确计算 best@ $k$，公式为 $\mathrm{best@}k=1-\binom{n-s}{k}/\binom{n}{k}$。我们逐关应用同样的计算方法，然后在各游戏之间取平均值，赋予每个游戏相同的权重。在全文中，解决率指对全部 25 个游戏取平均后的最终解决率，各关卡的解决率则会明确标注。我们以截至当前的最佳装填得分（70 个用例的已填充单元格密度均值）或 MNIST 分类器压缩大小（包含代码和权重的提交内容经 gzip 压缩后的大小）来衡量 team@ $k$ 和 best@ $k$。
Within every comparison, communicating and independent agents use the same model, maximum reasoning effort, longest context-length setting, and per-agent resource allocation. Appendix A.1 provides the communication protocol, prompts, and Copilot CLI versions.
在每一组比较中，相互通信的智能体与独立智能体均使用相同的模型、最高推理强度、最长上下文长度设置以及相同的单智能体资源配额。附录 A.1 提供了通信协议、提示词和 Copilot CLI 版本。

We next move from abstract games and competitive programming style problems to a more realistic ML research engineering task. The main question here is whether communication benefits extend to realistic multi-day engineering and how well communicating agents perform relative to the best-known human solutions. In MNIST classifier compression, agents must produce the smallest possible classifier that achieves at least $99.4\%$ test accuracy by minimizing the compressed size of the complete submission that includes its inference code and model weights in a 96-hour timeframe. The task combines empirical model development with deployment engineering, since agents must preserve accuracy while compressing both the learned parameters and the code needed to use them.
接下来，我们从抽象博弈和竞赛编程风格的问题转向一项更贴近现实的机器学习研究工程任务。这里的核心问题是，通信的益处能否延伸到持续多天的现实工程任务，以及相互通信的智能体相较于已知最佳人类方案表现如何。在 MNIST 分类器压缩任务中，智能体必须在 96 小时内，通过最小化包含推理代码和模型权重的完整提交文件的压缩后大小，构建尽可能小且测试准确率至少达到 $99.4\%$ 的分类器。这项任务将实证模型开发与部署工程结合起来，因为智能体必须在压缩学习得到的参数及使用这些参数所需代码的同时保持准确率。

Its $10\times 128$ matrix converts 128 features into ten digit scores using 1,280 learned parameters, plus ten bias parameters. It first compresses the 128 input features into eight learned weighted sums before computing the ten digit scores. This stalls at $99.30\%$ validation accuracy. To recover information lost in that compression, it also sends selected original features directly to the final scoring layer. Figure 11(b) shows these two paths in red and teal. Passing original features to the scoring layer helps recover accuracy as the learned matrix shrinks.
a3 将目标锁定在最终评分层，也称为分类头（classification head）。该层的 $10\times 128$ 矩阵使用 1,280 个学习得到的参数和十个偏置参数，将 128 个特征转换为十个数字类别的得分。它先将 128 个输入特征压缩为八个学习得到的加权和，再计算十个数字类别的得分。这一方法的验证准确率停滞在 $99.30\%$。为了恢复压缩过程中丢失的信息，它还将选定的原始特征直接传递到最终评分层。图 11(b) 用红色和蓝绿色展示了这两条路径。利用原始特征在训练数据上的协方差选出五个特征后，模型甚至在进一步拟合之前就达到了 $99.42\%$ 的准确率。由此得到的 3,120 字节模型达到了要求，a1 明确采用了这一设计。随后，a4 将七个学习得到的加权和与七个直接选取的特征相结合，将大小降至 2,790 字节。将原始特征传递给评分层，有助于在学习得到的矩阵缩小时恢复准确率。

Architecture.架构。
Figure 11(b) shows a recurrent convolutional network with 2,900 quantized parameters and 30 scale factors. The input is cyclically shifted upward by one pixel. A $3\times 3$ convolution maps the single input channel to 32 channels, followed by SiLU to form $h_{0}$ . Nine residual updates then reuse the same depthwise $3\times 3$ filters $D$ and pointwise $1\times 1$ filters $C$ . For update $t$ , the feature maps change according to
图 11(b) 展示了一个包含 2,900 个量化参数和 30 个缩放因子的循环卷积网络。输入向上循环平移一个像素。一个 $3\times 3$ 卷积将单个输入通道映射为 32 个通道，随后通过 SiLU 形成 $h_{0}$。随后，九次残差更新重复使用同一组逐通道 $3\times 3$ 滤波器 $D$ 和逐点 $1\times 1$ 滤波器 $C$。对于第 $t$ 次更新，特征图按如下方式变化
$$\widetilde{h}_{t}=\operatorname{SiLU}\!\big(h_{t-1}+g_{t}\odot\operatorname{GN}_{4}\!\left(C\bigl(\operatorname{SiLU}(D(h_{t-1}))\bigr)\right)+b_{t}\big).$$
Here $D$ filters each channel separately, $C$ mixes channels, and $\operatorname{GN}_{4}$ is GroupNorm with four groups, no learned affine parameters, and $\epsilon=10^{-5}$ . Each $g_{t}$ contains 32 learned gains, broadcast over spatial positions. The offset $b_{t}$ is the 32-parameter vector $A$ for updates 1–3 and the shared vector $B$ for updates 4–9. The shortcut adds the unchanged input to the learned correction, making these updates residual. All convolutions have stride one and no bias, with padding one for $3\times 3$ filters. After updates 3 and 6, $2\times 2$ max-pooling with stride two forms $h_{t}$ from $\widetilde{h}_{t}$ . Otherwise $h_{t}=\widetilde{h}_{t}$ . This gives three updates at each of $28\times 28$ , $14\times 14$ , and $7\times 7$ resolution.

3.4 Considerations for Effective Multi-Agent Communication3.4 实现有效多智能体通信的考量
Prior work finds that tasks admitting decomposition into independent subtasks are particularly amenable to multi-agent collaboration (Kim et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2025). Our tasks admit no such decomposition, yet communication helps regardless. What makes it help is verified progress sharing. Once a discovery is confirmed to be useful, every agent can build on it rather than rediscover it independently, and no agent is left exploring a direction the others have already exhausted.
已有研究发现，可分解为独立子任务的任务尤其适合多智能体协作 (Kim et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2025)。我们的任务无法进行这种分解，但通信仍然有帮助。通信之所以有帮助，在于共享经过验证的进展。一旦某项发现被确认有用，每个智能体都可以在此基础上继续推进，而无需各自独立地重新发现它，也不会有智能体继续探索其他智能体已经穷尽的方向。
In the following discussion, we provide a simple pedagogical model that formalizes this intuition. We show how it can lead to an exponential separation between communicating and independent agents, and also provide a small-scale experiment on Terminal-Bench 2.0 (Merrill et al., 2026) where team@ $k$ improves over single-agent attempts but not independent best@ $k$ , illustrating the importance of the availability of an agent-accessible verifier.
在下文讨论中，我们提供一个用于教学说明的简单模型，将这一直觉形式化。我们展示了它如何使相互通信的智能体与独立智能体之间产生指数级差距，并提供了一个在 Terminal-Bench 2.0 (Merrill et al., 2026) 上开展的小规模实验，其中 team@ $k$ 优于单智能体尝试，但未优于独立的 best@ $k$，说明了存在智能体可访问的验证器的重要性。
From independent trajectories to cumulative progress.从独立轨迹到累积进展。
Consider a task requiring $m$ successive improvements, with an accessible verifier reporting score $j/m$ after improvement $j$ . Assume that continuation difficulty depends only on the stage, improvements can be transferred freely, and agents conduct fresh, independent searches after each transfer. Both methods can query the verifier. Writing $X_{ij}$ for agent $i$ ’s search time at stage $j$ , their completion times would be
考虑一项需要连续完成 $m$ 次改进的任务，其中有一个可访问的验证器，在第 $j$ 次改进后报告得分 $j/m$。假设后续推进的难度仅取决于所处阶段，改进成果可以自由传递，且智能体在每次传递后都开展全新的独立搜索。两种方法都可以查询验证器。用 $X_{ij}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 在阶段 $j$ 的搜索时间，则两种方法的完成时间为
$$T_{\mathrm{best}}=\min_{i\leq k}\sum_{j=1}^{m}X_{ij},\qquad T_{\mathrm{team}}=\sum_{j=1}^{m}\min_{i\leq k}X_{ij}.$$
Thus $T_{\mathrm{team}}\leq T_{\mathrm{best}}$ . Independent sampling needs one agent to complete the entire sequence quickly, whereas a team can use a different agent’s breakthrough at each stage. Communication changes a minimum of sums into a sum of minima. Figure 12 illustrates this effect visually.
因此，$T_{\mathrm{team}}\leq T_{\mathrm{best}}$。独立采样需要某一个智能体快速完成整个序列，而团队可以在每个阶段利用不同智能体取得的突破。通信将各个和的最小值转化为各个最小值之和。图 12 直观展示了这一效果。

将 $m$ 个服从指数分布的等待时间相加，即可得到 Erlang 分布（形状参数为整数的 Gamma 分布）。因此，将 $k$ 加倍会使团队的平均完成时间减半。

The separation concerns completion probability at matched runtime, not an exponential expected-time speedup. The proof is in Appendix B. The benefit also depends on preserving diverse continuations. Sometimes, communicating agents may converge on a subset of approaches, which can reduce diversity and slow progress. To model herding, we can imagine the $k$ agents acting as essentially $g\leq k$ independent groups whose members duplicate the same search. The team’s discovery rate becomes $g\lambda$ and its mean completion time becomes $m/(g\lambda)$ . The high-success regime now requires $g\alpha>1$ . Sharing progress helps only to the extent that independent search survives after sharing.

As seen in Table 3, communication improves over an individual attempt, but does not outperform independent sampling (pass@ $k$ ) with the same number of agents. This result can be partially explained by Proposition 1 when $m$ is effectively $1$ . In Terminal-Bench, tasks require long sequences of actions, but the official verifier runs after agent execution. Available feedback varies across tasks, from package tests and direct performance measurements to checks of selected requirements. For example, the public evaluator in largest-eigenval checks the eigenvector equation and reports timings without verifying that the eigenvalue is dominant. A secondary disadvantage may be the loss of diversity. If communication causes $k$ agents to behave like only $g<k$ independent groups, then $g$ end-to-end candidates cover only $1-(1-p)^{g}$ , below pass@ $k$ ’s $1-(1-p)^{k}$ , where $p$ denotes the success probability of an independent candidate. This penalty is especially relevant here because team@2 contributes one shared final state, while pass@2 retains two isolated states and receives oracle selection after evaluation. Premature convergence or interference in the shared state can therefore make communication slightly worse than independent sampling. Together, the results suggest that communication is most useful when feedback is accessible and discriminative, though we do not exclude the possibility that test-time communication could be useful when feedback is sparse, if the agents can construct their own intermediate verification signals or have better communication protocol harnesses.
另一个次要劣势可能是多样性的丧失。如果通信使 $k$ 个智能体的行为仅相当于 $g<k$ 个独立群组，那么 $g$ 个端到端候选方案的成功覆盖率仅为 $1-(1-p)^{g}$，低于 pass@ $k$ 的 $1-(1-p)^{k}$，其中 $p$ 表示独立候选方案的成功概率。这一不利影响在此处尤为相关，因为 team@2 产生一个共享的最终状态，而 pass@2 保留两个相互隔离的状态，并在评估后通过预言机进行选择。因此，过早收敛或共享状态中的相互干扰可能使通信的表现略逊于独立采样。综合来看，这些结果表明，当反馈易于获取且具有区分能力时，通信最为有用；不过，我们并不排除在反馈稀疏时测试时通信仍可能有用，只要智能体能够自行构建中间验证信号，或拥有更好的通信协议运行框架。
4 Related Work4 相关工作
Multi-agent systems.多智能体系统。
Multi-agent systems (MAS) are becoming increasingly popular and widely adopted. As discussed in the introduction, some prior work (Du et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024) reports gains from multi-agent debate and belief exchange. However, deliberation without a verifier or external feedback can amplify correlated errors or move the team away from its strongest member (Smit et al., 2024; Pappu et al., 2026). In fact, independent sampling followed by aggregation remains a strong baseline (Wang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Choi et al., 2025). Benefits also depend on task structure and model strength. Kim et al. (2025) find stronger gains on decomposable tasks, but these gains diminish as the underlying model becomes stronger with respect to the task. While Kim et al. (2025) comprehensively cover a range of tasks and configurations, our work studies the benefits of MAS in a more open-ended discovery setting with a clear verifier signal. As discussed in Section 3.4, it is verified progress sharing that allows us to avoid “agent collapse” that previous works have observed. For more detailed surveys of MAS, we refer to Ferrag et al. (2026); Tran et al. (2025).
多智能体系统（MAS）正变得日益流行，并得到广泛采用。如引言所述，一些先前研究（Du et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024）报告了多智能体辩论和信念交换带来的增益。然而，缺乏验证器或外部反馈的研讨可能会放大相关性错误，或使团队偏离其最强成员的判断（Smit et al., 2024; Pappu et al., 2026）。事实上，先独立采样再聚合的方法仍然是一个强有力的基线（Wang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Choi et al., 2025）。收益还取决于任务结构和模型能力。Kim et al. (2025) 发现，在可分解任务上收益更大，但随着底层模型处理该任务的能力增强，这些收益会逐渐减小。尽管 Kim et al. (2025) 全面涵盖了一系列任务和配置，我们的工作则在具有明确验证器信号、开放性更强的发现情境中研究 MAS 的收益。如第 3.4 节所述，正是对经过验证的进展进行共享，使我们能够避免先前研究所观察到的“智能体坍缩（agent collapse）”。关于 MAS 更详细的综述，请参阅 Ferrag et al. (2026); Tran et al. (2025)。
Test-time discovery.测试时发现。
Test-time discovery often uses LLMs (or agents) to generate, evaluate, and iteratively improve candidate solutions, rather than producing an answer in a single attempt from a pretrained model. Within this paradigm, Novikov et al. (2025) keep the underlying LLMs frozen and accumulate progress through evolutionary program search, evaluator feedback, and a database of generated attempts. Wang et al. (2026); Yuksekgonul et al. (2026), by contrast, train the model via reinforcement learning during discovery, updating the LLM using feedback obtained from the target problems. Our setting, however, is orthogonal to the works above as they focus on either single-agent capabilities or multi-agent workflows without establishing an advantage over single-agent systems under matched compute.
测试时发现通常使用大语言模型（LLM）或智能体来生成、评估并迭代改进候选解，而不是通过预训练模型一次性生成答案。在这一范式下，Novikov et al. (2025) 保持底层 LLM 的参数冻结，并通过演化式程序搜索、评估器反馈以及存储已生成尝试的数据库来积累进展。相比之下，Wang et al. (2026); Yuksekgonul et al. (2026) 在发现过程中通过强化学习训练模型，利用从目标问题中获得的反馈更新 LLM。近期研究还关注定制的智能体工作流，其中智能体被分配专门角色，以生成、批判和完善假设，通常由一个中央智能体进行编排（Gottweis et al., 2026; Yamada et al., 2025）。然而，我们的研究设定与上述工作的研究方向相互独立，因为它们要么关注单智能体能力，要么关注多智能体工作流，却未能确立在计算量相同的条件下相对于单智能体系统的优势。
Recent work illustrates the potential of multi-agent communication for discovery. Anthropic (2026a) describe an improved attack on HAWK whose key idea emerged through an exchange between two agents, while OpenAI (2026a) report a Navier–Stokes result produced by a group of roughly 10,000 concurrent agents. Similarly, Bianchi et al. (2026) explore mathematical discoveries by an open community of heterogeneous agents sharing solutions and encouraging public discussion. These are systems in which agents choose how to explore and communicate without a fixed workflow.
近期研究展示了多智能体通信在发现任务中的潜力。Anthropic (2026a) 描述了一种针对 HAWK 的改进攻击，其关键思路产生于两个智能体之间的交流，而 OpenAI (2026a) 则报告了一项由约 10,000 个并发智能体共同得出的 Navier–Stokes 结果。类似地，Bianchi et al. (2026) 探索了由异构智能体组成的开放社区通过共享解答并鼓励公开讨论来开展数学发现。在这些系统中，智能体自行选择如何探索和通信，而不遵循固定的工作流。
These findings motivate controlled tests of whether communication improves on independent search, especially under matched compute. Anthropic (2026b) report more vulnerabilities from coordinating agents than from independent agents, though Claude Mythos Preview uses comparable tokens per vulnerability when both methods are evaluated on the same search scope. On other tasks, they observe failures to integrate agents’ work, conformity, and failures to share decisive evidence. Closely related to our setup is Qu et al. (2026), whose agents share discoveries through persistent memory and outperform the best of four independent runs under matched wall-clock budgets. In contrast, we diagnose clear conditions for when test-time communication is advantageous, through compute-controlled studies of scaling coordinating agents on ARC-AGI-3 and long-horizon algorithm/ML tasks, including negative results at low-compute budgets and on Terminal-Bench 2.0.
这些发现促使人们开展受控测试，以检验通信能否优于独立搜索，尤其是在计算量相同的条件下。Anthropic (2026b) 报告称，相互协调的智能体比独立智能体发现了更多漏洞，不过，当两种方法在相同搜索范围内接受评估时，Claude Mythos Preview 每发现一个漏洞所使用的 token 数量相近。在其他任务上，他们观察到了无法整合各智能体的工作、从众，以及未能共享决定性证据等问题。与我们的设定密切相关的是 Qu et al. (2026)，其中的智能体通过持久记忆共享发现，并在实际运行时间预算相同的条件下，超越了四次独立运行中的最佳结果。相比之下，我们通过在计算量受控的条件下，研究 ARC-AGI-3 和长时程算法/ML 任务中相互协调的智能体的规模扩展，明确了测试时通信具有优势的条件，其中也包括低计算预算下以及 Terminal-Bench 2.0 上的负面结果。
Harness optimization and orchestration.运行框架（Harness）优化与编排。
One line of work aims to learn the ideal topology and communication protocol of MAS. Agent graphs and communication links can be optimized directly (Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025c), while architecture search can adapt the system to the task (Zhang et al., 2025b; Yun et al., 2026). A second line controls when and which agents participate. Systems can select teams or gate communication (Liu et al., 2024; Ghosh and Chakraborty, 2026), regulate interaction to avoid harmful scaling (Wang et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2026), or add agents dynamically and decompose work into explicit subtasks (Costa, 2026; Gu et al., 2025). Gu et al. (2025) investigate agent diversity and observe weaker returns from homogeneous teams, though heterogeneity may bring down the performance of the best model in the team even when explicitly told who the best is (Pappu et al., 2026).
一类研究旨在学习 MAS 的理想拓扑结构和通信协议。智能体图和通信链路可以直接优化（Zhuge et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2025c），而架构搜索可以使系统适应任务（Zhang et al., 2025b; Yun et al., 2026）。第二类研究控制哪些智能体在何时参与。系统可以选择团队或控制通信的启闭（Liu et al., 2024; Ghosh and Chakraborty, 2026），调节交互以避免规模扩展带来的负面影响（Wang et al., 2025; Shao et al., 2026），或动态添加智能体并将工作分解为明确的子任务（Costa, 2026; Gu et al., 2025）。Gu et al. (2025) 研究了智能体多样性，并观察到同质团队的收益较小，不过，即使明确告知哪个模型最强，异质性仍可能拉低团队中最强模型的表现（Pappu et al., 2026）。
Hierarchical systems place these decisions with a central coordinator. Coordinators can be trained to assign work to frozen agents (Xu et al., 2026; Nielsen et al., 2026), while adaptive hierarchies can delegate work and synthesize results, primarily for parallel decomposition (Tang et al., 2026; OpenAI, 2026b). Recursive delegation has also been used for long-context inference rather than multi-agent orchestration (Zhang et al., 2025a). Harness optimization is broader and need not involve multiple agents. Executable or prompt-level agent loops can be optimized from scores, traces, or revision feedback (Lee et al., 2026b; Lee et al., 2026a). Prompt optimization has also been studied for multi-agent protocols (Bai and Shi, 2026).
层级式系统将这些决策交由中央协调器负责。可以训练协调器向参数冻结的智能体分配工作（Xu et al., 2026; Nielsen et al., 2026），而自适应层级结构可以委派工作并综合结果，主要用于并行分解（Tang et al., 2026; OpenAI, 2026b）。递归委派也被用于长上下文推理，而非多智能体编排（Zhang et al., 2025a）。运行框架（harness）优化的范围更广，不一定涉及多个智能体。可以根据评分、执行轨迹或修订反馈，对可执行代码层面或提示词层面的智能体循环进行优化（Lee et al., 2026b; Lee et al., 2026a）。也有研究针对多智能体协议开展提示词优化（Bai and Shi, 2026）。
Unlike these approaches, we do not study orchestration or multi-agent harness optimization. Instead, we use a fixed shared-workspace scaffold of identical, unassigned peers and compare its performance with compute-matched independent runs to isolate communication from parallel exploration.
与这些方法不同，我们不研究编排或多智能体运行框架优化。相反，我们采用固定的共享工作区框架，其中的对等智能体完全相同且未分配角色，并将其性能与计算量匹配的独立运行进行比较，以将通信的作用与并行探索的作用区分开来。
Agent coordination as interactive systems.作为交互系统的智能体协调。
We view sequential Monte Carlo as an interacting-population counterpart to best-of- $k$ , with partial solutions repeatedly scored and resampled (Gordon et al., 1993; Liu and Chen, 1998; Moral, 2004). Populations degenerate over long horizons (Kong et al., 1994; Doucet and Johansen, 2011), while resampling concentrates shared ancestors, and harder problems require larger populations (Liu and Chen, 1998; Snyder et al., 2008). These phenomena correspond to our gains at depth, herding failures, and non-monotone returns to team size.
我们将序贯蒙特卡洛视为 best-of- $k$ 在交互种群中的对应方法，其中部分解会被反复评分和重采样（Gordon et al., 1993; Liu and Chen, 1998; Moral, 2004）。种群会在较长的时间跨度内退化（Kong et al., 1994; Doucet and Johansen, 2011），而重采样会使种群集中于共同祖先，且更困难的问题需要更大的种群（Liu and Chen, 1998; Snyder et al., 2008）。这些现象分别对应于我们观察到的深度增加时的收益、从众导致的失败，以及团队规模带来的非单调收益。
Related methods now guide language model inference. Particle methods support probabilistic inference and test-time scaling (Zhao et al., 2024; Puri et al., 2025), as well as constrained generation (Loula et al., 2025). Interacting particles have also been adapted to diffusion models (Singhal et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2026), with related path selection for diffusion language model decoding (Lee et al., 2025). The analogy is conceptual and suggests that coordination is most useful over long horizons with informative intermediate feedback and a diverse population.
相关方法如今也被用于指导语言模型推理。粒子方法支持概率推理和测试时扩展（Zhao et al., 2024; Puri et al., 2025），也支持约束生成（Loula et al., 2025）。交互粒子方法也已被应用于扩散模型（Singhal et al., 2025; Luo et al., 2026），扩散语言模型解码中也采用了相关的路径选择方法（Lee et al., 2025）。这一类比是概念性的，它表明，当任务时间跨度较长、中间反馈富含信息且种群具有多样性时，协调最为有用。
5 Conclusion and Future Directions5 结论与未来方向
In this paper, we studied the capabilities of test-time communication, a multi-agent setting in which agents pursue the same open-ended objective and exchange evidence through a shared workspace. This allows agents to explore different approaches in parallel while turning individual discoveries into cumulative progress. On ARC-AGI-3, team@3 matches the solve rate of 13 independent agents and team@5 matches that of 33, with the multiplier growing from $4.3\times$ to $6.6\times$ as the team scales. Communication also solves a game that remains unsolved across single-agent trials and makes the average member of a team as action-efficient as the best of independent agents. The same pattern emerges in longer-horizon tasks. Team@ $k$ reaches a packing score of $0.945$ on Frontier-CS polyomino packing, above the prior best-known score of $0.894$ , and produce a 1,957-byte MNIST classifier, improving on both the best@4 result and the 2,461-byte best-known human solution. These gains emerge only after an initial coordination cost, but with adequate compute, communication can outperform independent agents in both performance and efficiency.
本文研究了测试时通信的能力；这是一种多智能体设置，其中各智能体追求同一个开放式目标，并通过共享工作区交换证据。这使智能体能够并行探索不同方法，同时将个体发现转化为累积性进展。在 ARC-AGI-3 上，team@3 的解题率与 13 个独立智能体相当，team@5 则与 33 个独立智能体相当，随着团队规模扩大，这一倍数从 $4.3\times$ 增长到 $6.6\times$。通信还解决了一款在所有单智能体试验中均未被解决的游戏，并使团队成员的平均动作效率达到独立智能体中最佳者的水平。在时间跨度更长的任务中，也出现了相同的模式。Team@ $k$ 在 Frontier-CS 多格骨牌装填任务上达到了 $0.945$ 的装填得分，超过此前已知的最佳得分 $0.894$，并生成了一个 1,957 字节的 MNIST 分类器，优于 best@4 的结果和 2,461 字节的已知最佳人工方案。这些收益只有在付出初始协调成本后才会显现，但在计算资源充足的情况下，通信可以在性能和效率上都超越独立智能体。
The agent-accessible verifier in each task is central to our experimental design. Our setting differs from multi-agent debate, which asks agents to reconcile answers without necessarily grounding the exchange in environmental feedback. Level completion, packing score, and compression size give agents an objective basis for rejecting failures, comparing approaches, and building on improvements. Whether communication produces similar gains when feedback is sparse, noisy, or subjective remains an important open question.
每项任务中智能体可访问的验证器，是我们实验设计的核心。我们的设置不同于多智能体辩论，后者要求智能体就答案达成一致，但不一定以环境反馈作为交流的依据。关卡完成情况、装填得分和压缩大小为智能体提供了客观依据，使其能够否定失败方案、比较不同方法，并在已有改进的基础上继续推进。当反馈稀疏、含有噪声或具有主观性时，通信是否仍能带来类似收益，依然是一个重要的开放问题。
On the other hand, multi-agent harnesses may matter as much as single-agent harnesses. Our controlled design is deliberately narrow. We fix one communication harness and use homogeneous agents with identical instructions and no assigned roles or central orchestrator. This isolates communication, but it does not imply that roles, model diversity, communication topology, or orchestration are unimportant. Future work should consider how agents communicate, which peers receive their messages, or how teams should be organized. These policies could be engineered or evolved, similarly to harness optimization (Lee et al., 2026b; Lee et al., 2026a). Our results provide a baseline for future work, and hopefully motivate further study of multi-agent collaboration and test-time communication.
另一方面，多智能体运行框架可能与单智能体运行框架同样重要。我们的受控设计有意限定了研究范围。我们固定采用一种通信运行框架，并使用指令完全相同的同质智能体，不分配角色，也不设置中央编排器。这隔离了通信的作用，但并不意味着角色、模型多样性、通信拓扑或编排不重要。未来工作应考虑智能体如何通信、哪些对等智能体接收其消息，以及应如何组织团队。与运行框架优化类似，这些策略可以通过工程设计或演化获得（Lee et al., 2026b; Lee et al., 2026a）。我们的结果为未来工作提供了基线，也希望能推动对多智能体协作和测试时通信的进一步研究。

A.3 ARC-AGI-3A.3 ARC-AGI-3
Task and interface.任务与接口。
Agents interact with the games by receiving integer-valued grids and selecting from the available discrete controls or coordinate clicks. Each level has its own cap, so unused budget from a later level cannot rescue an agent stalled earlier. We use the benchmark-native cap of five times the corresponding human-action baseline, independently for every agent.
智能体通过接收整数值网格，并从可用的离散控制操作或坐标点击操作中进行选择，与游戏交互。每个关卡都有各自的上限，因此后续关卡中未使用的预算无法帮助此前就已受阻的智能体。我们对每个智能体独立采用该基准原生的上限，即相应人类动作基线的五倍。
Every team member owns a separately authenticated ARC session. Sharing an action trace does not copy state and does not clear a peer’s level. Time-dependent progress curves use the first logged completion of each level.
每个团队成员都拥有一个单独认证的 ARC 会话。共享动作轨迹不会复制状态，也不会使同伴通过其关卡。随时间变化的进度曲线采用日志中各关卡首次完成的时间。
Synchronization.同步。
Team runs synchronize at intervals of half the current level’s action budget, rounded up to an integer number of actions. The harness tracks each agent’s phase by its level and the number of these intervals it has used. An agent ahead of the slowest live teammate, either within a level or by reaching the next level, cannot take another game action until its teammates catch up or leave the live set. During this pause, agents can still inspect state, run shell commands, and exchange notes. Refused game actions consume no budget. Finished, game-over, and budget-exhausted agents do not block their teammates, and inactive sessions are excluded after an idle timeout. This synchronization applies only to communicating teams, not to best@ $k$ .
团队运行以当前关卡动作预算的一半为间隔进行同步，间隔向上取整为整数个动作。实验运行框架根据每个智能体所在的关卡及其已使用的此类间隔数，跟踪其所处阶段。如果某个智能体领先于仍处于活跃状态的最慢队友，无论是在同一关卡内领先，还是已进入下一关卡，它都无法执行下一个游戏动作，直到队友追上或退出活跃集合。在此暂停期间，智能体仍可检查状态、运行 shell 命令并交换笔记。被拒绝的游戏动作不消耗预算。已完成任务、游戏结束或预算耗尽的智能体不会阻碍队友，而不活跃的会话会在空闲超时后被排除。这种同步仅适用于可通信的团队，不适用于 best@ $k$ 。
Closed-book condition.闭卷条件。
The task container requires network egress for the model control plane, but agents receive no browser or web-search tool and are explicitly forbidden to fetch public solutions, replays, or ARC pages. Only the task prompt, local run files, the agent’s own environment observations, and teammates’ within-trial notes are admissible. A.4 Frontier-CS polyomino packingA.4 Frontier-CS 多连方装填
Objective and scorer.目标与评分器。
Each of 70 hidden cases contains $n\in[100,10^{4}]$ edge-connected polyominoes of one to ten cells. A program may reflect, rotate by multiples of $90^{\circ}$ , and translate each piece. All pieces must lie without overlap in one integer-grid rectangle. If $C_{j}$ is the total number of occupied cells and $A_{j}=W_{j}H_{j}$ the returned area, the normalized case quality is $C_{j}/A_{j}$ and the reported score is its mean over the fixed cases. Invalid output causes the submission to be rejected. Candidate programs compile as GNU C++17 and receive 2 seconds and 256 MiB per case.
70 个隐藏测试用例中的每一个都包含 $n\in[100,10^{4}]$ 个多连方，每个多连方由一至十个通过边相连的单元格组成。程序可以对每个块进行镜像反射、按 $90^{\circ}$ 的整数倍旋转以及平移。所有块都必须互不重叠地放置在同一个整数网格矩形内。若 $C_{j}$ 为被占据的单元格总数，$A_{j}=W_{j}H_{j}$ 为返回的矩形面积，则该测试用例的归一化质量为 $C_{j}/A_{j}$，报告的分数为其在固定测试用例集上的均值。无效输出会导致提交被拒绝。候选程序使用 GNU C++17 编译，每个测试用例的时间限制为 2 秒，内存限制为 256 MiB。
The hidden instance directory is mounted read-only inside a separate scorer service and is never mounted into the agent container, which contains the task statement and a submission client. On each attempt, the client sends the candidate C++ source over an internal endpoint. The scorer compiles and executes it, with case-output retention disabled, and returns the aggregate score together with compact status, timing, and scoring metadata. The scorer never returns the instances or program outputs. Agents may submit repeatedly without penalty, but cannot inspect the evaluation data. Every attempt, timestamp, status, and score is recorded in a submission ledger.
