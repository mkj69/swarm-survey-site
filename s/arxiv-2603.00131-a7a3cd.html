思想病毒：通过多智能体系统中的阈下提示实现病毒式失对齐Thought Virus: Viral Misalignment via Subliminal Prompting in Multi-Agent Systems
摘要Abstract
阈下提示（subliminal prompting）是一种通过使用语义无关的词元进行提示，使语言模型偏向某些概念或特征的现象。 Subliminal prompting is a phenomenon in which language models are biased towards certain concepts or traits through prompting with semantically unrelated tokens. 尽管先前的研究已考察用户与 LLM 交互中的阈下提示，但多智能体系统中潜在的偏向传递及其相关安全影响仍未得到探索。 While prior work has examined subliminal prompting in user-LLM interactions, potential bias transfer in multi-agent systems and its associated security implications remain unexplored. 在本研究中，我们表明，单个受到阈下提示的智能体能够将一种逐渐减弱但持续存在的偏向传播至其整个网络。 In this work, we show that a single subliminally prompted agent can spread a weakening but persisting bias throughout its entire network. 我们采用两种不同的拓扑结构，在 6 个智能体中测量这一现象，观察到所传递概念在整个网络中的响应率持续处于较高水平。 We measure this phenomenon across 6 agents using two different topologies, observing that the transferred concept maintains an elevated response rate throughout the network. 为展示潜在的失对齐风险，我们在多项选择形式的 TruthfulQA 上评估网络性能，结果表明，对单个智能体施加阈下提示可能降低其他智能体回答的真实性。 To exemplify potential misalignment risks, we assess network performance on multiple-choice TruthfulQA, showing that subliminal prompting of a single agent may degrade the truthfulness of other agents. 我们的研究结果揭示，阈下提示为多智能体安全引入了一种新的攻击途径，并对这类系统的对齐产生影响。 Our findings reveal that subliminal prompting introduces a new attack vector in multi-agent security, with implications for the alignment of such systems. 所有实验的实现均已公开，地址为 https://github.com/Multi-Agent-Security-Initiative/thought_virus。 The implementation of all experiments is publicly available at https://github.com/Multi-Agent-Security-Initiative/thought_virus.

由 LLM 构成的多智能体系统（MAS）已展现出强大的性能，并日益广泛地用于自主交易 (Xiao et al., 2025) 和协作编程 (Hong et al., 2024) 等任务。 Multi-agent systems (MAS) of LLMs have demonstrated strong performance and are increasingly used for tasks such as autonomous trading (Xiao et al., 2025) and collaborative coding (Hong et al., 2024). 尽管这些架构支持专业化分工与可扩展性，在更复杂的任务上优于单个 LLM (Guo et al., 2024)，但它们也引入了新的攻击面。 While these architectures enable specialization and scalability, outperforming single LLMs on more complex tasks (Guo et al., 2024), they also introduce new attack surfaces. 具体而言，单个智能体的失对齐或偏向可能通过智能体间的交互传播，放大其对整个系统的影响，这种现象被称为错误传播 (Shen et al., 2025)。 In particular, misalignment or bias of a single agent may propagate through inter-agent interactions, amplifying its effect on the overall system, a phenomenon known as error propagation (Shen et al., 2025).
先前研究表明，蓄意攻击可以通过操纵智能体间的通信，在多智能体系统中传播不良行为 (He et al., 2025; Shahroz et al., 2025; Men et al., 2025)。 Prior research has shown that deliberate attacks can spread undesirable behaviour through multi-agent systems by manipulating inter-agent communications (He et al., 2025; Shahroz et al., 2025; Men et al., 2025). 一种攻击途径是直接优化看似毫无意义的查询，以诱发特定的智能体行为 (Cherepanova and Zou, 2024)，我们将这一策略称为对抗性优化（见图 1 第 1 列）。 One attack vector involves direct optimization of seemingly nonsensical queries to elicit specific agent behaviours (Cherepanova and Zou, 2024), a strategy we refer to as adversarial optimization (see column 1 of Figure 1). 尽管该消息可能对首个接收者产生预期效果，但优化后的消息通常不会被逐字传播，从而在后续智能体交互中有效地化解该攻击，这类似于改写防御策略 (Jain et al., 2023)。 While the message may have the desired effect on the primary recipient, the optimized message will generally not be propagated verbatim, effectively neutralizing the attack in subsequent agent interactions, analogous to paraphrasing defence strategies (Jain et al., 2023). 另一种称为提示注入的攻击途径（图 1 第 2 列）使用明确的恶意指令，通过角色扮演场景或欺骗性操纵绕过安全机制 (Liu et al., 2025a)。 Another attack vector, termed prompt injection (column 2 of Figure 1), employs explicit malicious instructions that bypass safety mechanisms via role-play scenarios or deceptive manipulation (Liu et al., 2025a). 针对此类攻击，一种自然的防御方式是（自动）监控和/或过滤智能体间消息中明确的恶意内容 (Chennabasappa et al., 2025; Jacob et al., 2025; Hung et al., 2025)。 A natural defence against such attacks is (automatic) monitoring and/or filtering of inter-agent messages for explicit malicious content (Chennabasappa et al., 2025; Jacob et al., 2025; Hung et al., 2025). 要规避这两种防御，攻击必须在不明确提及目标概念的情况下传递偏向，同时保持对改写的鲁棒性。 To evade both defences, an attack must transmit biases without explicit reference to the target concept while maintaining robustness to paraphrasing.
近期关于阈下学习（subliminal learning）(Cloud et al., 2025) 及其在阈下提示方面的拓展 (Zur et al., 2025) 的研究表明，这种隐于感知阈限之下且具有鲁棒性的传播可能是可行的。 Recent work on subliminal learning (Cloud et al., 2025) and extensions on subliminal prompting (Zur et al., 2025) suggest that such subliminal and robust propagation may be feasible. 先前研究表明，通过包含语义无关词元序列的提示，可以使语言模型偏向特定概念或行为；尽管这些序列与目标概念之间没有明显的语义联系，它们仍能诱发对具体概念的系统性偏好，例如喜爱某种特定动物（如狮子）。 Prior studies show that language models can be biased toward specific concepts or behaviours through prompts containing semantically unrelated token sequences, inducing systematic preferences for concrete concepts like the love of a specific animal (e.g., lion), despite no apparent semantic connection. 然而，对这一现象的研究主要局限于孤立的用户与 LLM 交互，其对多智能体系统的影响在很大程度上仍未得到探索。 However, this phenomenon has been studied primarily in isolated user–LLM interactions, leaving its implications for multi-agent systems largely unexplored.
在潜意识提示（subliminal prompting）的既有研究 (Zur et al., 2025) 基础上，我们提出了 Thought Virus，这是一种新型攻击途径，利用潜意识提示在智能体网络中传播偏置。 Building on prior work on subliminal prompting (Zur et al., 2025), we introduce Thought Virus, a novel attack vector which applies subliminal prompting to spread bias through a network of agents. 具体而言，我们表明，如果通过潜意识提示向单个智能体引入偏置，该偏置就会传递给与其通信的其他智能体，而这些智能体随后又会进一步传播该偏置。 Specifically, we show that if a single agent is subliminally prompted with a bias, that bias transfers to other agents it communicates with, and those agents then transfer the bias further.
我们在第 3 节介绍的多智能体通信环境中对此进行了测试，采用了两种拓扑结构：链式（$A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C$ 等）和双向链式（$A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C\rightarrow B\rightarrow A$）。 We test this in a multi-agent communication environment, explained in Section 3, with two topologies: a chain ( $A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C$ etc.) and a bidirectional chain ( $A\rightarrow B\rightarrow C\rightarrow B\rightarrow A$ ). 在包含六个智能体的实验中，我们发现，单个接受潜意识提示的智能体无需提及其偏置，就能将一种逐渐减弱但持续存在的偏置传播至整个网络，即使经过多跳传递，与该偏置相符的响应率仍然较高。 Across six agents, we find that a single subliminally prompted agent can spread a weakening yet persistent bias throughout its entire network without reference to its bias, with elevated response rates even after multiple hops. 为展示失对齐风险，我们在 TruthfulQA 的多项选择版本上评估：对单个智能体施加潜意识提示，是否能够诱导其他智能体产生失对齐行为。 To demonstrate misalignment risks, we evaluate whether subliminal prompting of a single agent can induce misaligned behaviour in other agents on a multiple-choice version of TruthfulQA.

2 背景与相关工作2 Background and Related Work
潜意识学习。 Subliminal Learning. 潜意识学习（subliminal learning）最早由 (Cloud et al., 2025) 探索，指的是这样一种现象：学生语言模型在由带有偏置的教师模型生成的、语义上无意义的数据上进行微调后，也表现出这种偏置。 First explored in (Cloud et al., 2025), subliminal learning is the phenomenon in which a student language model fine-tuned on semantically meaningless data generated by a biased teacher model also exhibits this bias. 这引发了严重的安全担忧，因为用于训练或微调的合成数据可能被恶意行为者以潜意识方式植入偏置。 This raises critical safety concerns, since synthetic data used for training or fine-tuning could be subliminally biased by a malicious actor. 已有研究表明，潜意识偏置也会通过提示传递 (Zur et al., 2025)，其中 (Zur et al., 2025) 通过使用所谓的纠缠词元（entanglement tokens）提示模型来引入这种偏置。 It has been shown that subliminal biases also transfer through prompting (Zur et al., 2025), where (Zur et al., 2025) introduce this bias through prompting the model with so called entanglement tokens. 然而，正如 (Schrodi et al., 2025) 所表明的，这些似乎无法完全解释潜意识偏置的传递。 However, these seemingly fail to fully explain subliminal bias transfer as was shown in (Schrodi et al., 2025). 与潜意识学习相关的是所谓的涌现性失对齐（emergent misalignment）(Betley et al., 2026)，即在失对齐数据（例如不良财务建议或含有缺陷的代码）上进行狭窄范围的微调，可能会在与微调目标无关的任务上引发广泛的失对齐。 Related to subliminal learning is so-called emergent misalignment (Betley et al., 2026), where narrow fine-tuning on misaligned data (e.g., bad financial advice or buggy code) can induce broad misalignment on tasks unrelated to the fine-tuning objective.
多智能体系统中的错误传播。 Error Propagation in Multi-Agent Systems. 近年来，由多个相互交互的 LLM 构成的多智能体系统受到越来越多的关注 (Guo et al., 2024)。 In recent years, multi-agent systems comprised of multiple interacting LLMs have seen a rise in attention (Guo et al., 2024). 正如 (Hammond et al., 2025) 所表明的，多智能体系统（MAS）的安全性至关重要。 As is shown in (Hammond et al., 2025) the safety of MAS systems is critical. 鉴于这些系统广泛应用于金融 (Xiao et al., 2025)、编程 (Hong et al., 2024)，以及能源或国防等更为关键的领域，其安全性尤其重要，正如 (Hammond et al., 2025) 所讨论的那样。 This is especially true due to the multitude of applications of these systems in finance (Xiao et al., 2025), programming (Hong et al., 2024), or more critical domains such as the energy sector or defence, as discussed in (Hammond et al., 2025). 多智能体系统中的一项重大潜在安全风险是错误传播，即单个智能体在事实层面出错的行为或失对齐行为被其他智能体采纳 (Wynn et al., 2025)。 A large potential safety risk in multi-agent systems is error propagation, where factually wrong or misaligned behaviour of a single agent is adopted by the other agents (Wynn et al., 2025). 本文聚焦于错误源于针对网络中一个或多个智能体的对抗性攻击的情形，不包括由幻觉等因素引入的错误。 In this paper, we focus on the case where the errors are due to an adversarial attack on one or more agents of the network, excluding errors introduced by e.g. hallucination. 传播如何发生以及何时发生，既取决于具体攻击，也取决于系统所采用的拓扑结构 (Huang et al., 2025)，其中连接密集的拓扑结构往往较少传播错误 (Shen et al., 2025)。 How and when propagation happens depends on both the concrete attack and the chosen topology of the system (Huang et al., 2025), where densely connected topologies tend to propagate errors less (Shen et al., 2025).
针对 LLM 和多智能体系统的对抗性攻击。 Adversarial Attacks on LLMs and Multi-Agent Systems. 虽然拓扑结构的选择在错误传播中起着重要作用 (Shen et al., 2025)，但具体采用的攻击同样如此 (Huang et al., 2025)。 While the choice of topology plays an important role in error propagation (Shen et al., 2025), the specific attack does too (Huang et al., 2025). 首先，已有关于提示敏感性（prompt sensitivity）的研究 (Zhuo et al., 2024; Ismithdeen et al., 2025; Sclar et al., 2023) 表明，提示设计可以大幅改变 LLMs 的行为，从而为基于提示的攻击提供可乘之机。 Firstly, there exists prior work on prompt sensitivity (Zhuo et al., 2024; Ismithdeen et al., 2025; Sclar et al., 2023), showing that prompt design can drastically change the behaviour of LLMs, opening the door to prompt based attacks. 对此，无论是在单纯的用户与 LLM 交互情境中，还是在多智能体场景中，都存在大量可能的攻击方式 (de Witt, 2025)。 For this, in both the pure user-LLM case and the multi-agent scenario, an extensive number of possible attacks exists (de Witt, 2025). 黑盒和白盒越狱攻击均已得到研究 (Yi et al., 2024)，并被应用于多智能体情境 (Men et al., 2025; Rahman et al., 2025; Shahroz et al., 2025)。 Both black and white box jailbreak attacks have been studied (Yi et al., 2024) and also applied to the multi-agent case (Men et al., 2025; Rahman et al., 2025; Shahroz et al., 2025). 特别是，提示注入（prompt injection）是一种值得关注的 LLMs 越狱方式 (Liu et al., 2025a; Rossi et al., 2024)，因为这类攻击完全采用黑盒方式，易于使用。 In particular, prompt injections are a relevant way to jailbreak LLMs (Liu et al., 2025a; Rossi et al., 2024) due to their ease of use, as they are completely black box. 针对提示注入的防御机制包括检测提示中的恶意内容 (Chennabasappa et al., 2025; Jacob et al., 2025; Hung et al., 2025)。 Defence mechanisms against prompt injections include the detection of malicious content in the prompts (Chennabasappa et al., 2025; Jacob et al., 2025; Hung et al., 2025). 这一方向的近期研究还探索了完全无法理解的提示注入 (Cherepanova and Zou, 2024)，这类注入属于图 1 中用户与 LLM 交互的对抗性提示情形。 Recent work in this vein also explored completely non-understandable prompt injections (Cherepanova and Zou, 2024) that would fit the adversarial prompting case for user-LLM interactions from Figure 1. 作为一种强度稍低的对抗性提示情形，隐蔽式提示注入方法是针对用户与 LMM 交互情境开发的，并采用基于后缀的方式 (Liu et al., 2024; Mu et al., 2025)。 As a slightly less strong case of adversarial prompting, we have stealthy prompt injection methods, developed for the user-LMM case, which are suffix based (Liu et al., 2024; Mu et al., 2025). 这些攻击与我们的设定相似，其中偏置通过无关的词元以潜意识方式传递。 These attacks are similar to our setting, where bias transfer happens subliminally through unrelated tokens. 我们同样会隐藏提示的真实意图，但在隐蔽式提示注入中 (Liu et al., 2024; Mu et al., 2025)，由于只有提示的后缀在语义上无关，因此提示仍有部分内容可被人类理解。 We, too, conceal the true motive of our prompts, however in the stealthy case (Liu et al., 2024; Mu et al., 2025) the prompts are still partly human understandable due to only the suffix of the prompt being semantically unrelated. 针对这类对抗性提示的标准防御技术包括改述问题 (Liu et al., 2025b)。 Standard defence techniques against such adversarial prompting include rephrasing of the question (Liu et al., 2025b). 具体而言，对于 MAS，分布式攻击构成一种威胁 (Shahroz et al., 2025)，这类攻击通过中间人攻击等方式 (He et al., 2025) 利用分布式系统的弱点。 For MAS specifically, distributed attacks are a threat (Shahroz et al., 2025), exploiting weaknesses of distributed systems through e.g. man in the middle attacks (He et al., 2025).

3.1 实验设置3.1 Experimental setup
图 2 展示了我们的实验框架。 Figure 2 depicts our experimental framework. 我们开展了一系列实验，旨在通过对一个初始智能体施加潜意识影响，使其偏置通过网络内的对话交流传播，从而隐蔽地使 MAS 中的智能体倾向于特定输出或行为。 We conduct a series of experiments aimed at covertly biasing agents in a MAS toward specific outputs or behaviours by subliminally influencing an initial agent whose bias propagates through conversational exchanges within the network. 关键在于，这种传播还会延伸至最初被施加偏置的智能体已不再参与的对话，从而实现病毒式传播，使其他智能体成为自主传播该偏置的宿主。 Crucially, this extends to conversations in which the primary biased agent no longer participates, thereby enabling viral transmission where other agents become hosts that spread the bias autonomously. 我们通过一个“潜意识词元”（subliminal token）来诱导这种潜意识偏置：它是一个三位数，我们在被施加偏置的智能体的系统提示中指示其偏好该数字，从而在初始智能体中触发目标行为。 We induce this subliminal bias via a ‘subliminal token’, a three-digit number that the biased agent is instructed to favour in its system prompt, triggering the target behaviour in the primary agent. 我们假设，该智能体能够通过基于提示的交流，将自身的偏置传播给后续智能体，后者再进一步传播这一偏好。 We hypothesize that this agent can spread its bias through prompt-based exchange with subsequent agents, who then propagate the preference further. 要使这一机制发挥作用，必须满足两个条件：1) 潜意识偏置不仅能够通过此前已得到验证的微调和系统提示传播，还能够通过用户提示传播；2) 潜意识偏置对具体措辞具有稳健性，即语义等价但措辞不同的提示能够产生相近的效果。 For this mechanism to function, two conditions must hold: 1) subliminal biases can propagate not only through fine-tuning and system prompts as previously demonstrated, but also through user prompts, and 2) subliminal bias exhibits robustness to exact phrasing, such that semantically equivalent prompts with varied wording produce comparable effects. 我们在早期的小规模实验中对这两个假设进行了定性验证。 We were able to qualitatively validate both hypotheses in early small-scale experiments.
选择潜意识词元。Selecting subliminal tokens.
现有文献对潜意识偏置提出了多种解释，并给出了相应的潜意识词元发现方法 (Zur et al., 2025; Cloud et al., 2025; Schrodi et al., 2025)，但这些方法的有效性取决于模型和具体设定。 The literature presents various explanations for subliminal biases and the corresponding methods to discover subliminal tokens (Zur et al., 2025; Cloud et al., 2025; Schrodi et al., 2025), although the effectiveness of these methods depends on the model and setting. 我们采用一种直接的方法：开展 $10^{3}$ 次独立的单智能体评估，分别通过系统提示使用每一个可能的三位数词元 (000–999) 对智能体施加偏置，并将产生最强预期效果的词元选为潜意识词元。 We adopt a direct approach: we run $10^{3}$ separate single agent evaluations, biasing the agent via its system prompt with each possible three-digit token (000–999), and select as subliminal tokens those that produce the strongest desired effect. 我们假设，在单个智能体中引发最强偏置的词元，也最有可能将这种行为传播给其他智能体。 We assume that tokens eliciting the strongest bias in an individual agent also possess the highest likelihood of spreading this behaviour to other agents. 衡量这一预期效果的指标因场景而异，各场景的具体指标详见第 3.2 节。 The metric for this desired effect is scenario-dependent and detailed for each scenario in Section 3.2. 哪些潜意识词元与哪些目标行为相关联，取决于具体模型，并且可能随模型架构的不同而变化。 Which subliminal tokens are linked to which targetted behaviour is specific to each model and may vary across distinct model architectures. 因此，在由采用不同 LLMs 的智能体组成的 MAS 中，多个模型可能表现出对同一潜意识词元的偏好，但只有采用特定模型架构的智能体才会建立该词元与相应目标概念之间的潜意识关联。 Consequently, in a MAS comprising agents employing different LLMs, multiple models may demonstrate preference for the same subliminal token, yet only agents utilizing a particular model architecture will establish the subliminal association to the corresponding target concept.
MAS 设置与对话模拟。MAS setup and simulating conversations.
我们的实验采用基于“链式”拓扑的 MAS 配置：将用户提示提供给 Agent0，Agent0 生成一条回复以及一条供 Agent1 使用的后续提示；Agent1 按照相同模式继续向 Agent2 传递，依此类推，直至链条终止、对话结束。 Our experiments utilize a MAS configuration based on a ‘chain’ topology: a user prompt is provided to Agent0, who produces both a response and a subsequent prompt for Agent1, which continues this pattern to Agent2 and further until the chain terminates and the conversation ends. 我们还考察了一种“双向链”，其中最后一个智能体的回复沿链反向传播，每个智能体都向其前一个智能体生成回复，直至到达 Agent0，由其生成面向用户的最终回复并结束对话。 We also examine a ‘bidirectional chain’, where the final agent’s response propagates backward through the chain, with each agent formulating a response to its predecessor until reaching Agent0, which generates the final user response and ends the conversation. 这两种拓扑代表了相对稀疏的通信结构；既有研究表明，基于 LLM 的多智能体系统中的信息传播在很大程度上取决于通信拓扑，尤其是在更稀疏的拓扑中，错误传播发生的可能性更低 (Shen et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025)。 These two topologies represent relatively sparse communication structures; prior work shows that information propagation in LLM-based multi-agent systems depends strongly on the communication topology and, in particular, that error propagation is less likely to occur in sparser topologies (Shen et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025). 在这两种情况下，我们都通过向 Agent0 提供潜意识系统提示词来操纵它，指示其偏好潜意识词元，而所有其他智能体均接收用于提供有益帮助的标准系统提示词。 In both cases, we compromise Agent0 by providing it with a subliminal system prompt instructing the agent to favour the subliminal token, while all other agents receive a standard system prompt for helpful assistance. 最后，我们向 Agent0 提供一条用户提示词，指示其传播自己对潜意识词元的偏好，从而启动智能体之间的对话。 Finally, we start a conversation between agents by providing Agent0 with a user prompt that instructs it to propagate its preference for the subliminal token. 我们通过向 Agent0 提供一条用户提示词，要求其传播自己对潜意识词元的喜爱，来模拟智能体之间的对话。 We simulate a conversation between the agents, by giving Agent0 a user prompt, telling it to spread its love for the subliminal token. 我们在系统中运行对话，并记录在 20 个随机种子下产生的交互。 We run the conversation through the system and record the resulting exchanges across 20 random seeds. 3.2 评估场景3.2 Evaluation scenarios
我们在两种场景中评估潜意识偏差的病毒式传播：动物偏好，即引导智能体偏好某种目标动物；以及失对齐，即评估潜意识词元是否影响智能体正确回答 TruthfulQA 问题的倾向。 We evaluate the viral spread of subliminal biases in two scenarios: animal preferences, in which we bias agents toward a target animal, and misalignment, in which we evaluate whether subliminal tokens affect agents’ propensity to answer TruthfulQA questions correctly.
动物偏好。Animal preferences.
在动物偏好实验中，我们旨在使智能体在被问及其偏好的动物时，倾向于输出某种特定的目标动物。 In the animal preference experiment, we aim to bias agents to output a specific target animal when queried about their preferred animal. 为识别潜意识词元，我们针对每个候选词元，仅向智能体提供相应的潜意识系统提示词，并选择使目标动物对应的 logit 增幅最大的词元（例如，在我们贯穿全文的示例中，词元“613”与对“狮子”的偏好增强相关）。 To identify subliminal tokens, we provide the agent with only the subliminal system prompt for each candidate token and select those that produce the largest logit increases for the desired animal (e.g., token “613” is associated with increased preference for “lion” in our running example).
为评估智能体在对话后的潜意识偏差，我们将每个智能体接收或发送的消息作为上下文提供给它，并向其提出一个关于其偏好动物的问题。 To assess an agent’s subliminal biases following their conversation, we provide each agent with the messages it received or sent as context and prompt it with a question about its preferred animal. 我们同时记录回答频率，即在最大词元长度为 20 的 200 次模拟回答中提及目标动物的比例，以及目标动物的对数概率。 We record both the response frequency, i.e. the rate with which the target animal is mentioned across 200 simulated responses with a maximum token length of 20, and the log probabilities for the target animal. 我们对 20 次模拟 MAS 对话的结果取平均值，以获得每个词元的平均结果。 Results are averaged over 20 simulated MAS conversations to obtain an average result for each token.
重要的是，我们核实了目标动物（例如“狮子”）未出现在任何智能体间消息中；因此，下游动物偏好的任何变化都必然源于潜意识传递，而非明确提及。 Importantly, we verify that the target animal (e.g., “lion”) does not appear in any inter-agent message; any downstream shift in animal preferences must therefore arise from subliminal transfer rather than explicit mention.
我们假设，相对于不使用系统提示词的模型的基线频率，智能体的回答频率并不总会提高（这与先前的研究结果一致 (Zur et al., 2025)），因为对话也可能降低目标概率。 We hypothesize that agents will not always exhibit increased response frequencies relative to the baseline rate of a model without a system prompt (consistent with prior findings (Zur et al., 2025)), as a conversation may also decrease target probabilities. 为单独识别潜意识词元的特定影响，而非一般性的对话影响，我们还选择了 10 个随机词元，并对其开展完全相同的实验。 To isolate the effect of subliminal tokens specifically, rather than conversational influence generally, we also select 10 random tokens for which we conduct the identical experiment. 随后，我们通过进行单侧 Mann-Whitney $U$ 检验，评估潜意识词元比随机词元产生更强影响这一假设。 We then evaluate our hypothesis that subliminal tokens produce stronger effects than random tokens by performing a one-sided Mann-Whitney $U$ test.
失对齐Misalignment
在失对齐实验中，我们沿用先前确立的实验设置 (Zur et al., 2025; Betley et al., 2026)，考察受到潜意识偏差影响的智能体更倾向于生成真实答案，还是复现人类常见的错误观念。 In the misalignment experiment, we examine whether a subliminally biased agent is more likely to generate truthful answers or to reproduce common human misconceptions, following previously established experimental setup (Zur et al., 2025; Betley et al., 2026). 我们采用 TruthfulQA-MC 数据集 (Lin et al., 2022)，该基准包含涵盖 38 个类别的 684 道问题。 We employ the TruthfulQA-MC dataset (Lin et al., 2022), a benchmark consisting of 684 questions spanning 38 categories. 这些问题经过专门设计，旨在针对人类因广泛传播的错误信息或认知偏差而形成的常见错误信念。 Questions are specifically constructed to target common false beliefs of humans, stemming from widespread misinformation or cognitive biases. 该数据集是 TruthfulQA (Lin et al., 2022) 的选择题改编版本，仅包含每题有四个备选答案的选择题。 The dataset is a multiple-choice adaptation of TruthfulQA (Lin et al., 2022), consisting only of multiple choice questions with four possible answers. 我们识别出两类潜意识词元：将其纳入系统提示词后，分别使智能体的真实作答行为达到最多（“真实词元”）和最少（“欺骗词元”）。 We identify subliminal tokens that, when incorporated into the system prompt, induce maximal truthful behaviour (‘truthful tokens’) and minimal truthful behaviour (‘deceitful tokens’) in agents. 我们通过智能体在该数据集上的准确率对此进行量化，其中，智能体自动选择输出分布中对数概率最高的答案词元所对应的回答。 We quantify this through the agent’s accuracy rate on the dataset, where the agent automatically selects the response corresponding to the answer token with the highest log probability in the output distribution. 我们通过记录模拟 MAS 对话结束后的准确率，以及正确与错误回答词元之间的平均对数概率差，来评估智能体对真实性的偏好。 We assess agents’ truthfulness preference by recording both the accuracy rate and the average log probability difference between correct and incorrect response tokens following a simulated MAS conversation. 结果取 20 次模拟 MAS 对话的平均值。 Results are averaged over 20 simulated MAS conversations. 为检验对真实性的偏好或对错误观念的坚持会在系统中传播这一假设，我们对准确率以及真实与欺骗性词元所对应的对数概率差均进行单侧 Mann-Whitney $U$ 检验。 To test the hypothesis that preference for truthfulness or adherence to misconceptions propagates through the system, we apply a one-sided Mann-Whitney $U$ test to both accuracy rates and log probability differences corresponding to truthful and deceitful tokens.

5 讨论与结论5 Discussion and Conclusion
本文探讨了潜意识偏置是否会在多智能体系统中传播。 In this paper, we explored whether subliminal biases propagate through multi-agent systems. 我们的结果表明，在所测试的不同拓扑结构中，潜意识偏置可能在智能体之间以病毒式方式传播，但随着与最初带有偏置的智能体之间的距离增大，偏置强度会减弱。 Our results indicate that subliminal biases may transfer virally between agents across different tested topologies, although the strength of the bias decreases with further difference from the originally biased agent.
在动物偏好实验中，我们观察到，对于不同智能体和动物类别，使用潜意识词元影响网络所产生的响应率均显著高于基准响应率和使用随机词元时的响应率。 In experiments on animal preferences, we see that influencing the network with subliminal tokens significantly increases the response rate compared to both the base rate and random tokens across agents and animal categories. 具体而言，在我们的 6 智能体链中，末端智能体的响应率最高可达到基准响应率的 $437\times$，以及使用随机词元时响应率的 $7\times$。 Specifically, the terminal agent in our 6-agent chain shows an increased response rate by a factor of up to $437\times$ over base rate and $7\times$ over random tokens. 在所测试的两种拓扑结构以及 Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct 和 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct 上，均观察到了类似效应。 A similar effect is observed over both tested topologies and Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct as well as Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. 鉴于观察到偏置会随着与最初受攻击智能体之间的网络距离增大而衰减，我们预计，具有高中心性的拓扑结构（如中心辐射式架构）会特别容易受到此类攻击。 Given the observed decay of bias with network distance from the initially compromised agent, we expect topologies with high centrality, such as a hub-and-spoke architecture, to be particularly vulnerable to such attacks. 尽管我们的研究发现表明，潜意识偏置的病毒式传播构成了多智能体系统面临的一项广泛安全隐患，但刻画这一效应在不同 MAS 架构中的大小，并确定攻击效力如何取决于具体网络特征，如拓扑结构（另见 (Shen et al., 2025)）、通信协议和协调机制，仍是未来的重要研究工作。 While our findings demonstrate that viral propagation of subliminal biases represents a broad safety concern for multi-agent systems, characterizing the magnitude of this effect across diverse MAS architectures and establishing how attack efficacy depends on specific network characteristics, such as topologies (see also (Shen et al., 2025)), communication protocols, and coordination mechanisms, remains important future work.
此外，为展示一项具体的安全应用，我们评估了潜意识偏置迁移对智能体失对齐的影响。 Additionally, to demonstrate a concrete safety application, we evaluated subliminal bias transfer effects on the misalignment of agents. 具体而言，我们证明了偏向不真实回答的偏置会在智能体之间传播，其证据是：在改编自 TruthfulQA 的多项选择题上，智能体回答的真实性显著下降。 Specifically, we demonstrate that biases favouring untruthful responses propagate between agents, evidenced by significant degradation in agent truthfulness on multiple-choice questions adapted from TruthfulQA. 尽管这项分析主要用于说明实际的安全影响，但它证实了潜意识偏置迁移能够推广到简单的单词元回答之外的情形。 While this analysis primarily serves to illustrate practical safety implications, it confirms that subliminal bias transfer generalizes beyond simple single-token responses.
在本文呈现的实验中，我们通过系统提示将潜意识偏置引入 MAS，这限制了该方法在攻击者仅能访问用户提示的场景中的适用性。 In our presented experiments, we introduce subliminal bias into the MAS via system prompts, limiting the applicability of this approach in scenarios where attackers possess only user prompt access. 然而，我们推测，仅通过提示注入也可能实现潜意识提示，因为我们已经观察到，智能体之间在提示层面的交互足以将偏置传递给未接触过带有偏置的系统提示的智能体。 However, we suspect that subliminal prompting is also possible through prompt injections alone, since we have seen that prompt level interactions between the agents are enough to transfer the bias to agents that have not seen the biased system prompt. 对此开展调查是未来一个值得探索的研究方向。 Investigating this is an interesting avenue for future work. 此外，我们的实验受限于所采用的简单的基于通信的 MAS 设置；在金融 (Xiao et al., 2025) 或编程 (Hong et al., 2024) 领域所使用的更贴近现实、面向特定任务的 MAS 网络上开展实验，将拓展我们的研究结果。 Furthermore, our experiments are limited by our simple communication-based MAS setup, experiments on more realistic and task-specific MAS networks used in finance (Xiao et al., 2025) or coding (Hong et al., 2024) would broaden our results. 最后，在我们的实验设置中，病毒式失对齐对智能体行为的影响具有统计显著性，但幅度较小。 Finally, in our experimental setup, viral misalignment exhibits statistically significant yet modest effects on agent behaviour. 我们假设这主要由两个因素造成：首先，智能体在传播对潜意识词元的偏好时可能效力有限，未能成功传递偏置（或者，如我们在实验中偶尔观察到的那样，完全未提及潜意识词元），从而削弱了潜意识提示的效果。 We hypothesize two primary factors: First, agents may demonstrate limited efficacy in propagating preferences for subliminal tokens, failing to successfully transmit the bias (or, as we have seen albeit rarely in our experiments, failing to mention the subliminal token altogether) and thereby reducing the subliminal prompting effect. 我们认为，可以通过采用不同的用户指令来分享潜意识偏置，并让最初受到影响的智能体反复分享偏置，以缓解这一问题。 We suggest this can be mitigated via different user instructions for sharing of the subliminal bias, and repeated bias sharing by the primary influenced agent. 其次，所发现的潜意识词元可能无法诱导出强烈的失对齐偏置。 Second, the discovered subliminal tokens may not induce strong misalignment biases. 这一局限可以通过将潜意识词元的搜索空间扩展到三位数之外，或开发有效的潜意识词元发现方法来解决。 This limitation may be addressed by expanding the search space for subliminal tokens beyond three-digit numbers or developing effective subliminal token discovery methods. 进一步研究这些提高攻击有效性的策略，是未来一个有前景的研究方向。 Further investigation of these strategies to increase the attack’s effectiveness represents a promising direction for future work.
除了将我们的研究扩展到更多场景和 MAS 设置之外，未来的研究还可以考察其他能够传播潜意识偏置的 LLM-LLM 交互，例如智能体利用其他 LLM 的输出进行微调，这可能将我们的设置与最初的潜意识学习 (Cloud et al., 2025) 或涌现性失对齐 (Betley et al., 2026) 方法联系起来。 Next to extending our study to additional scenarios and MAS setups, future research may also look into other LLM-LLM interaction which can spread subliminal biases, such as agents fine-tuning on the outputs on other LLMs, possibly connecting our setup with the original subliminal learning (Cloud et al., 2025) or emergent misalignment (Betley et al., 2026) approaches. 这具有实际意义，例如，互联网上由 LLM 生成的内容可能被用作微调数据；如果生成这些内容的模型曾在狭窄的不对齐任务上接受训练，或带有潜意识偏见，那么这些内容就可能向使用它们进行微调的智能体中植入后门。 This would have practical relevance, as e.g. LLM-generated content on the internet could be used as fine-tuning data, which, if the model that generated this content was trained on a narrowly misaligned task or subliminally biased, could introduce a backdoor into the agent that fine-tunes on it. 另一种可能向 MAS 中的单个智能体植入偏见的攻击途径，是滥用信息检索、代码执行器或 API 等工具。 Another potential angle of attack for inserting biases into individual agents in MAS may be the misuse of tools, such as information retrieval, code executors or APIs. 总体而言，理解潜意识学习和提示仍是一个开放问题，进一步研究如何检测带有潜意识偏见的数据或提示将有所裨益，尤其是这将为构建抵御潜意识攻击的稳健防御机制开辟途径。 In general, understanding subliminal learning and prompting is still an open problem and further work on detecting subliminally biased data or prompts would be beneficial, in particular this would open up ways to build robust defence mechanisms against subliminal attacks. 总的来说，我们的研究表明，潜意识偏见迁移可以遍及整个 MAS。 Overall, our work demonstrates that subliminal bias transfer can occur throughout entire MAS.
