摘要Abstract
随着人工智能日益推动竞争市场中的决策自动化，理解由此产生的动态变化并确保市场机制的公平性至关重要。 As artificial intelligence increasingly automates decision-making in competitive markets, understanding the resulting dynamics and ensuring fair market mechanisms is essential. 我们研究了大型语言模型（LLMs）在寡头垄断古诺市场中的多方面决策，结果表明，LLMs 不仅能够理解复杂的市场动态，展现出作为有效经济规划智能体的潜力，还会进行持续的默契合谋，使价格较纳什均衡水平最多高出 200%。 We investigate the multi-faceted decision-making of large language models (LLMs) in oligopolistic Cournot markets, showing that LLMs not only grasp complex market dynamics—demonstrating their potential as effective economic planning agents—but also engage in sustained tacit collusion, driving prices up to 200% above Nash equilibrium levels. 我们的分析从三个维度考察 LLM 的行为——(1) 决策类型、(2) 对手策略和 (3) 市场构成——揭示了这些因素可能如何影响基于 LLM 的决策者的竞争性。 Our analysis examines LLM behavior across three dimensions—(1) decision type, (2) opponent strategies, and (3) market composition—revealing how these factors may shape the competitiveness of LLM-based decision-makers. 此外，我们表明，通过强制执行最优反应策略来监管少数占主导地位的智能体，能够有效打破合谋，并有助于恢复竞争性定价。 Furthermore, we show that regulating a few dominant agents by enforcing best-response strategies effectively disrupts collusion and helps restore competitive pricing. 我们的研究结果识别了将 AI 引入竞争性市场环境可能带来的问题，并为自动化时代提供了监管政策建议。 Our findings identify potential concerns associated with AI integration in competitive market environments and provide regulatory policy recommendations for the era of automation.
1 引言1 Introduction
人工智能（AI）正日益用于定价、资源管理和财务管理等各类市场领域的决策自动化，引发了关于个体策略及其对市场动态的集体影响的讨论 [34, 11, 42]。 Artificial Intelligence (AI) is increasingly used in automating decision-making across various market domains, such as pricing, resource, and financial management, prompting discussions about both individual strategies and their collective effects on market dynamics [34, 11, 42]. 人工智能领域的最新进展——大型语言模型（LLMs），或称生成式 AI——有望通过实现具备策略推理能力和适应能力的更复杂的自主智能体，对自动化产生重大影响。 The recent developments in AI, Large Language Models (LLMs), or generative AI, are expected to substantially impact automation by enabling more sophisticated autonomous agents capable of strategic reasoning and adaptability. 然而，LLMs 的黑箱性质使其决策过程难以理解，引发了有关其更广泛经济影响的疑问，以及其行为可能如何偏离传统经济人概念的问题；经济人是理性且追求自身利益的典型经济主体 [30]。 However, LLMs’ black-box nature complicates the comprehension of their decision-making, raising questions about their broader economic implications and how their behavior may deviate from the traditional conception of homo economicus—the archetypal rational, self-interested economic agent [30].
具体而言，当自主 AI 智能体帮助企业在竞争市场中作出策略决策时，会出现几个问题：它们能否结合商业情境理解并适应市场动态？ In particular, when autonomous AI agents help firms make strategic decisions in competitive markets, several questions emerge: Can they understand and adapt to market dynamics, given business contexts? 它们能否在竞争中制定有效且可能涉及多个方面的策略？ Can they craft effective, potentially multifaceted strategies amid competition? 如果事实证明它们具备策略能力，那么当多个 AI 智能体彼此互动，或与基于理论的决策者博弈时，会产生怎样的结果？ And if they prove to be strategically competent, what outcomes emerge when multiple AI agents interact with each other or against theory-based decision-makers? 从多智能体系统的角度来看，这些问题涉及策略环境中智能体学习、协调与涌现行为等基本问题。 From a multi-agent systems perspective, these questions relate to fundamental issues of agent learning, coordination, and emergent behaviors in strategic environments. 尽管 LLMs 展现出潜力，但其应对复杂经济环境的能力仍存在争议，尤其是在需要前瞻性、适应能力和策略决策的情境中 [44, 49, 20]。 While LLMs show promise, their capacity to navigate complex economic environments remains a subject of debate—particularly in contexts requiring foresight, adaptability, and strategic decision-making [44, 49, 20].
在这一背景下，一个重大关切是合谋的可能性。 One significant concern in this context is the potential for collusion. 算法的使用引发了人们的担忧：算法可能学会在没有明确沟通的情况下合作，从而提高利润。 The use of algorithms has raised fears that they might learn to cooperate, increasing profits without explicit communication. 默契合谋（tacit collusion）也称价格协调（为简便起见，也简称“合谋”），是指集中度较高的市场中的企业可能在没有明确协议的情况下，实际上共享垄断势力的过程 [10]。 Tacit collusion—also referred to as price coordination (or just ‘collusion’, for simplicity)—is a process where firms in a concentrated market might effectively share monopoly power without explicit agreements [10]. 有研究指出，由于市场中的竞争性，“没有协议，默契合谋就难以维持” [33]。 It is noted, ‘without an agreement, tacit collusion is difficult to sustain’ [33] due to competitiveness in the market. 然而，现实世界中的案例 [4]、强化学习算法 [14]，以及表明重复博弈的均衡可能导致价格高于竞争性水平的理论设定 [7, 38]，均证实了算法合谋的存在。 However, algorithmic collusion is validated in real-world examples [4], via reinforcement-learning algorithms [14], and via theoretical settings which show that the equilibria of repeated games may result in higher than competitive prices [7, 38]. 鉴于难以区分涌现的合谋行为与优化失败，主流经济学观点侧重于识别算法中的奖惩机制，并将其作为合谋的指标 [25]。 Given the challenge of distinguishing emergent collusive behavior from optimization failures, the prevailing economic perspective focused on identifying algorithmic reward-punishment mechanisms as indicators [25]. 然而，对于 LLMs 而言，决策过程的不透明性阻碍了对其策略行为的理解与监测，加剧了人们对合谋的担忧。 With LLMs, however, the opacity in decision-making hinders the comprehension and monitoring of their strategic behaviors, worsening concerns about collusion.
在本研究中，我们考察了 LLMs 在古诺寡头垄断市场中的策略潜力，并评估采用这些模型所带来的更广泛市场后果，包括合谋的可能性。 In this work, we examine the strategic potential of LLMs within Cournot oligopolistic markets and assess the broader market consequences of their adoption, including the potential for collusion. 在既有研究的基础上，例如 Fish 等人 [22] 的研究表明，LLMs 能够在直接设定价格的伯特兰博弈中维持高于竞争性水平的价格，我们将研究拓展至古诺市场；古诺市场是一种成熟的模型，用于理解通过设定产量进行竞争、且价格由总产量决定的寡头垄断市场。 Building on prior research—such as Fish et al. [22], which shows that LLMs can sustain supra-competitive prices in a direct price-setting Bertrand game—we extend the inquiry to Cournot markets, a well-established model for understanding oligopolistic quantity-setting markets where price is derived from aggregate production. 在标准古诺框架中，各企业通过作出生产决策来实现利润最大化，而总产量决定市场价格。 In the standard Cournot framework, individual firms make production decisions to maximize profits, while aggregate production determines the market price. 我们将影响生产成本并进而影响利润的企业层面资本投资决策纳入这一框架，对其加以扩展。 We augment this framework by incorporating firm-level capital investment decisions that influence production-costs and, consequently, profits. 这一扩展拓宽了策略互动的范围，使模型更加贴近现实世界的复杂性，并使我们能够检验 LLMs 在多方面决策中的策略潜力。 Broadening the scope of strategic interactions, this augmentation aligns the model more closely with real-world complexities and allows us to test LLMs’ strategic potential over multifaceted decisions. 我们在同质和异质的多参与者市场中开展这项研究。 We do this over homogeneous and heterogeneous multi-player markets. 通过构建市场模拟，我们首先让大语言模型（LLMs）同时制定产量策略和资本投资策略，以测试其决策能力。 Building a market simulation, we first test the decision-making capabilities of LLMs, by tasking them with both production level and capital investment strategies. 随后，我们分析部分或全部决策者基于 LLM 时的整体市场结果。 We follow this by analyzing the overall market outcomes when a fraction or all decision-makers are LLM-based. 我们的具体方法和主要发现如下。 Our detailed approach and main findings are as follows.
1.1 方法与贡献1.1 Approach and Contributions
在第 2 节中，我们构建了一个纳入投资决策的古诺框架，统称为扩展古诺框架，并使用纳什基准验证该博弈设定。 In Section 2, we develop a Cournot framework incorporating investment decisions—collectively referred to as the augmented Cournot framework—and validate the game setting using Nash benchmarks. 在这一设定中，市场总产量，即各企业产量之和，决定市场价格。 In this setting, total market production, i.e., the aggregate output of individual firms, defines the market price. 企业可以通过资本投资（受上限约束）降低单位生产成本。 Firms can reduce their per-unit production-costs by making capital investments (subject to an upper bound). 在假设各企业将部分利润作为资本进行再投资的条件下，我们直接依据古诺利润最大化原则对这一投资与成本之间的关系建模。 This investment-to-cost relationship is directly modeled from the Cournot profit-maximization principle, under the assumption that a portion of each firm’s profit is reinvested as capital. 代表竞争性（寡头垄断）市场中一家企业的决策者，同时选择产量策略和资本投资策略，两者共同决定企业的总利润。 A decision-maker, representing a firm in a competitive (oligopolistic) market, chooses both the production and capital investment strategies, which jointly determine overall firm profit. 我们确立了扩展古诺框架中纳什均衡的存在性及其计算方法，为研究提供理论基准。 We establish the existence and computation of Nash equilibria in the augmented Cournot framework, providing the theoretical benchmarks.
接下来，我们构建市场模拟，使多种类型的决策者能够在多个时期内互动：LLM 智能体，将决策任务交给生成式 AI；纳什智能体，始终选择预先确定的纳什最优策略；以及最优反应（BR）智能体，根据此前的市场反馈直接优化自身策略。 We next develop the market simulation, which allows several types of decision-makers to interact over a number of periods: LLM agents, who task the generative AI for decision-making; Nash agents, who consistently decide on predetermined Nash optimal strategies; and best-response (BR) agents, who directly optimize their strategies based on previous market feedback. 我们通过将 LLM 的表现与纳什智能体和 BR 智能体的表现进行比较，评估其策略潜力。 We assess the strategic potential of LLMs by comparing their performance against both Nash and BR agents. 每个智能体都会收到有关其此前决策、市场总产量以及由此产生的价格和利润的数据，但并不确切了解底层市场机制。 Each agent receives data on its previous decisions, the market’s total production, and resulting prices and profits—but lacks exact knowledge of the underlying market mechanism. 因此，它们必须仅凭观察到的反馈来推断该机制。 Consequently, they must gauge it solely from observed feedback. 市场始终处于动态变化之中，随着所有参与者之间的互动而持续演变。 The market remains dynamic, continuously evolving in response to the interactions among all participants.

竞争性市场中的策略判断能力：我们发现，LLM，尤其是 GPT-4，展现出稳健的多维度决策能力。 Strategic Acumen in Competitive Markets: We find that LLMs, especially GPT-4, demonstrate robust multi-faceted decision-making capabilities. 无论面对静态纳什智能体还是动态 BR 智能体，它们都能持续作出最优决策，表明其具备在基于古诺模型的框架中充当有效规划智能体的潜力。 They consistently make optimal decisions against both static Nash and dynamic BR agents, ascertaining potential as effective planning agents in Cournot-based frameworks.
默契合谋的可能性：我们通过多项实验表明，LLM 持续表现出自主的默契合谋行为。 Potential of Tacit Collusion: We show, via multiple experiments, that LLMs consistently exhibit autonomous tacit collusion. 它们的互动共同形成低于纳什均衡的产量水平，在没有明确协调的情况下，使价格持续高于竞争水平，有时甚至达到纳什最优价格（即竞争性的古诺—纳什价格）的 200%。 Their interactions collectively produce output levels below the Nash equilibrium, leading to sustained supra-competitive prices without explicit coordination, sometimes reaching up to 200% of Nash optimal price, i.e., the competitive Cournot-Nash price. 因此，利润通常保持在高于纳什水平的状态。 Consequently, profits remain generally higher than the Nash levels.
通过（部分）监管实现公平的市场机制：通过强制少数主要企业（即市场份额较大的企业）遵循最优反应策略对其实施监管，能够有效缓解自主的默契合谋。 Fair Market Mechanism via (Partial) Regulation: Regulating a small number of major firms (those with larger market shares) by enforcing adherence to best-response strategies effectively mitigates autonomous tacit collusion. 在多企业市场中，我们证明，仅需约束排名最前的少数企业作为最优反应智能体运行，即可实现纳什定价。 In multi-firm markets, we demonstrate that Nash pricing can be achieved when only the top few firms are constrained to operate as best-response agents.
我们的模型将 AI 智能体置于这样一种环境中：价格由它们共同作出的产量和投资决策形成，而它们通过对自身过去行动的反馈来理解价格。 Our model situates AI agents in a setting where pricing emerges from their collective production and investment decisions, and is understood through feedback on their past actions. 超竞争定价的出现必然源于以下两种情况之一：要么缺乏优化能力，要么理解了共同降低产量能够推高价格和利润，并且由于威胁的存在，偏离行为最终可能无利可图。 The emergence of supra-competitive pricing must occur either due to an inability to optimize or through an understanding that collectively lower production drives higher prices and profits and that deviations may not be eventually profitable due to threats. 我们的同质双企业实验提供了多方面的理由来排除第一种可能性：AI 在面对基于理论的静态纳什智能体以及动态最优反应智能体时，始终促成竞争性价格，而在彼此对抗时则总是实现超竞争定价。 Our homogeneous two-firm experiments provide multiple reasons to discount the first possibility: the AI consistently leads competitive prices against theory-based static Nash as well as dynamic best-response agents and invariably reaches supra-competitive pricing against each other. 值得注意的是，在 AI 作出的产量和投资决策中，后者影响自身的生产成本，进而影响利润，始终更接近最优水平；而前者共同决定价格，却远离最优水平。 Notably, among the production and investment decisions that the AI makes, the latter—which affects its own production-costs and thereby profits—is consistently closer to the optimal, while the former—which collectively drives prices—is far from optimal. 关键在于，这一现象发生在最优反应策略只需几次迭代便会收敛至纳什均衡的博弈环境中。 This crucially happens in a game environment where best response strategies would converge to the Nash equilibrium in a few iterations.
我们的异质多企业实验进一步支持了这一论点，表明 AI 并不总需要在降低产量上保持一致，就能推高价格。 Our heterogeneous multi-firm experiments strengthen the argument by showing that the AI need not always align with producing lower quantities to drive higher prices. 在此情形下，小型企业的产量可能接近甚至超过其纳什均衡产量，而中型或大型企业则始终选择较低的产量，从而推动价格高于竞争性水平。 Here, small-sized firms may produce close to their Nash production levels or even exceed those, whereas mid-sized or larger firms consistently decide on lower production and drive supra-competitive prices. 这表明，LLMs 并未内置低产量策略，而是必须根据市场反馈作出决策。 This suggests that LLMs do not encode the strategies of low production, but must make the decision based on the market feedback.
在这一背景下，尽管 AI 驱动的超竞争性定价引发了严重的反垄断关切，但局部监管的有效性提供了一种有望发挥制衡作用的手段。 In this context, although AI-driven supra-competitive pricing raises significant anti-trust concerns, the efficacy of partial regulation offers a promising counterbalance. 如果监管机构能够深入理解市场的策略机制，那么聚焦主要企业的决策或许就足以遏制合谋。 If regulatory bodies develop a robust understanding of the market’s strategic mechanisms, focusing on the major firms’ decisions may be sufficient to curb collusion. 尽管最优反应策略引导市场动态走向纳什竞争性结果 [40] 可能并不完全出人意料，但我们发现，值得关注且令人鼓舞的是，在复杂且不断变化的市场条件下，LLM 智能体始终能够实现这一结果；这些市场由 LLM 智能体和 BR 智能体异质混合而成，它们产生的最优反应反馈并不完美，而这一结果的实现既无需外部干预，也无需关于对手策略的明确信息。 While it may not be entirely unexpected that best-response strategies steer market dynamics toward Nash-competitive outcomes [40], we find it noteworthy—and encouraging—that LLM agents consistently achieve this in complex and evolving market conditions, characterized by a heterogeneous mix of LLM and BR agents who generate imperfect best-response feedback, all without external intervention or explicit information about opponents’ strategies. 这也表明，至少在自主决策的初始阶段，监管机构可能拥有一个制定适当反垄断措施的关键窗口期。 This also suggests that regulatory bodies may have a critical window to develop appropriate antitrust measures, at least during the initial phase of autonomous decision-making.

算法合谋：算法自主合谋的可能性及其监管已受到计算机科学、法学、经济学和管理科学等领域的关注 [35, 23, 45, 8, 26, 24, 41, 6, 28]。 Algorithmic Collusion: The potential and regulation of algorithmic autonomous collusion have garnered attention across the fields of computer science, law, economics, and management sciences [35, 23, 45, 8, 26, 24, 41, 6, 28]. 在市场经济学中，研究者通过实验考察了 Q-learning 算法在价格设定（Bertrand）模型和产量设定（Cournot）模型中的收敛情况，发现两者最终均形成了合谋 [14, 52]。 In market economics, the convergence of Q-learning algorithms has been experimentally examined in both price-setting (Bertrand) and quantity-setting (Cournot) models, both concluding in collusion [14, 52]. 已有研究表明，算法之间的自发耦合可能促使它们在优于静态纳什均衡的行动上开展合作 [5]。 It has been shown that spontaneous coupling between algorithms may lead to cooperation on actions better than the static Nash equilibrium [5]. 多智能体系统研究表明，自主智能体可以通过重复交互和环境反馈，在没有显式通信的情况下学会协调 [46, 37]。 Multi-agent systems research has demonstrated that autonomous agents can learn to coordinate without explicit communication through repeated interactions and environmental feedback [46, 37]. Arunachaleswaran 等人 [3] 深入讨论了合谋，并表明，在由两家企业构成的领导者—跟随者情境中，即使双方使用的算法均未显式编码威胁，也可能出现超竞争性定价。 Arunachaleswaran et al. 2 模型与方法2 Model and Methods
本节介绍市场动态及相应的模拟博弈，各类决策者（包括基于 AI 的智能体）均参与其中。 In this section, we present the market dynamics and the corresponding simulation game in which various decision-makers—including AI-based agents—participate. 第 2.1 节首先描述企业决策之间的经济相互作用及其引发的市场动态。 Section 2.1 first describes the economic interplay between firms’ decisions and the resulting market dynamics. 该模型以 Cournot 框架为基础，在这一框架中，市场动态完全由企业的生产决策所驱动；我们通过纳入资本投资决策对其进行了扩展。 The model builds on the Cournot framework—where market dynamic solely unfolds from firms’ production decisions—and extends it by incorporating capital investment decisions. 这一框架被称为扩展古诺（augmented Cournot）框架，它将企业作为参与者、生产和投资决策作为行动、由此产生的利润作为效用，对博弈进行形式化描述。 Referred to as the augmented Cournot framework, this formalizes a game with firms as players, production and investment decisions as actions, and resulting profits as utilities. 我们对该模型进行了严格验证，并为生产和投资决策建立了纳什基准。 We rigorously validate the model and establish Nash benchmarks for both production and investment decisions. 随后，第 2.2 节详细介绍实验设计，概述各类决策者如何参与模拟并影响市场。 Section 2.2 then details the experimental design, outlining how various decision-makers participate in the simulation, and influence the market.
2.1 市场设计2.1 Market Design
在传统古诺框架中，企业通过选择产量而非价格来实现利润最大化。 In the traditional Cournot framework, firms maximize profits by selecting production quantities rather than prices. 所有企业面临相同的市场价格，该价格由市场总产量以及界定需求与价格关系的弹性参数共同决定 [52]。 The market price is the same for all firms and is determined by total market production and an elasticity parameter that defines the demand–price relationship [52]. 在这种由市场决定价格的产量设定框架下，寡头企业在选择产量时，可能会依据历史数据、预测和预期生产成本。 Given the quantity-setting framework where price is derived in the market, oligopolistic firms may rely on past data, predictions, and expected production-costs while making production choices. 在这里，资本投资也可能影响成本结构 [9, 27]。 Here, capital investment may also play a role in shaping cost structures [9, 27]. 然而，实际市场价格以及由此决定的各企业利润，最终取决于所有企业的总产量。 Yet, the realized market price—and hence individual profits—ultimately depend on the collective production of all firms.
下面，我们将扩展古诺框架划分为两个不同阶段加以介绍。 We now present the augmented Cournot framework by structuring it in two distinct phases. 第一阶段是校准阶段，用于确立基准古诺市场均衡；第二阶段则引入动态博弈，企业可在其中调整生产和投资决策。 The first is a calibration phase that establishes the baseline Cournot market equilibrium, while the second phase introduces a dynamic game in which firms can adjust production and investment decisions. 第二阶段构成了我们开展多智能体模拟实验的基础。 This second phase forms the basis for our multi-agent simulation experiments. 扩展古诺框架满足以下条件： The augmented Cournot framework satisfies the following conditions:
企业具有策略性和理性，并且可能存在异质性。 Firms are strategic, rational, and potentially heterogeneous. 企业独立选择产量和投资额，以实现利润最大化。 They independently choose production and investment to maximize profits. 市场为非垄断市场，即任何企业的市场份额均不超过 95%。 The market is non-monopolistic, such that no firm has more than 95% market share.
市场价格遵循恒定弹性的价格与产量函数，全部产出均以该函数推导出的市场出清价格售出。 The market price follows a constant-elasticity price–production function, with all production selling at the derived market-clearing price.
依据利润最大化的古诺原则，根据基准市场价格和各企业的市场份额，推导出现状或基准生产成本与利润。 Status quo or baseline production-costs and profits are derived from the baseline market price and firm-wise market shares under the profit-maximizing Cournot principle. 这些数值是企业起始时所处的初始纳什均衡值，但其未来的产量、成本和利润取决于后续的生产和投资决策。 These are initial Nash equilibrium values that the firms begin at, however, their future production, costs, and profits depend on subsequent production and investment decisions.
企业可以进行资本投资以降低生产成本，但投资额受到与其自身现状利润挂钩的上限约束。 Firms can invest in capital, subject to an upper bound tied to their own status quo profits, to reduce production-costs. 投资与成本的关系由一个固定的类柯布–道格拉斯（Cobb–Douglas）函数决定，该函数由古诺框架外推而来，并考虑了企业现状市场份额所带来的异质性。 A fixed Cobb–Douglas-like function (extrapolated from the Cournot framework) governs the investment–cost relationship, accommodating heterogeneity coming from firms’ status quo market shares. 投资收益在每期均完全折旧，因此需要持续作出投资决策以维持成本优势。 Investment benefits depreciate fully each period, requiring continuous investment decisions to maintain cost advantages.
图 1：市场设计：企业作出生产和投资决策；后者通过 Cobb-Douglas 函数影响生产成本。 Figure 1: The market design: Firms make production and investment decisions; the latter decision affects the production-cost via a Cobb-Douglas function. 市场总产量与弹性共同决定价格。 The total market production and elasticity together determine price. 各企业的利润等于其产量乘以价格与其生产成本之差。 Profits for each firm equal its production multiplied by the difference between price and its production-cost. 这些利润中的一部分被转化为投资。 A fraction of these profits is converted into investments.
给定各企业的市场份额和市场价格，基准均衡将确定现状利润和生产成本，并用这些数值设定投资与生产成本函数的参数。 Given firm-wise market shares and market price, the baseline equilibrium would define status quo profits and production-costs, which are used to parameterize the investment to production-cost function. 动态博弈将允许企业同时作出生产和投资决策，并获得实际市场价格和各自的生产成本，进而获得利润。 The dynamic game would allow the firms to simultaneously make production and investment decisions and receive realized market price and individual production-costs, and thereby profits. 图 1 展示了两家代表性企业的市场动态。 Figure 1 diagrams the market dynamics for two representative firms.

2.2 实验设计2.2 Experimental Design
第 2.1 节设计了动态市场博弈，企业可通过同时对产量 $q_{i}$ 和投资 $b_{i}$ 作出决策来参与该博弈。 Section 2.1 designs the dynamic market game in which firms may participate by making simultaneous decisions on production $q_{i}$ and investment $b_{i}$ . 在理想条件下，企业可以依据有关其他企业市场份额和价格的必要信息，预测其投资意愿和生产能力。 In ideal conditions, a firm may predict others’ investment appetite and production capacities, stemming from the necessary information about their market share and price. 然而，在现实中，企业可能缺乏这些知识，甚至不了解支配市场变量的方程。 However, in reality, it may lack this knowledge and moreover, the equations that govern the market variables.

3.4 战略考量与优化失败3.4 Strategic Deliberation vs. Optimization Failure
为区分这些高于竞争水平的结果究竟源于战略协调还是优化失败，我们进行了两项验证分析： To distinguish whether these supra-competitive outcomes result from strategic coordination or optimization failures, we conduct two validation analyses:
投资最优性：我们验证了在整个实验过程中的每一个决策时点，20%的投资确实都是最优选择。 Investment Optimality: We verify that 20% investment was indeed the optimal choice at every decision point throughout our experiments. 在全部9个实验中，每个实验均包含300期，我们发现全额投资（20%）始终是两个LLM智能体的最优决策，证实了LLMs始终能够识别最优投资策略。 Across all 9 experiments and 300 periods each, we find that full investment (20%) remains the optimal decision for both LLM agents, confirming that LLMs consistently identified the optimal investment strategy.
最优反应收敛性：我们检验了从观测到的LLM所处状态出发，是否仍能达到纳什均衡。 Best Response Convergence: We test whether Nash equilibrium remains accessible from observed LLM positions. 我们从实验中的任意时点出发，模拟两个智能体均转而采用最优反应策略后会发生的情况。 Starting from any point in our experiments, we simulate what would happen if both agents switched to best-response strategies. 结果表明，从全部300期中观测到的每一个状态出发，博弈平均经过3次迭代便会收敛至纳什均衡产量，与纳什值的偏差达到1%以内。 Results show that from every observed state in all 300 periods, the game would converge to Nash equilibrium production in an average of 3 iterations, reaching within 1% of Nash values.

5 结论与讨论5 Conclusions and Discussion
随着人工智能（AI）的能力不断增强、成本不断降低且日益普及，其融入企业策略决策的进程已为期不远。 With the increasing capabilities, affordability, and availability of artificial intelligence (AI), its integration into strategic decision-making within businesses is forthcoming. 在本研究中，我们提出如下问题：“在竞争性市场情境下，AI能否作出策略决策？ In this work, we ask: ‘Can AI make strategic decisions, given a competitive market context? 如果可以，这可能会对市场动态产生什么影响？ And, if so, what may be the impact on market dynamics? 我们对第一个问题给出了肯定回答，展示了AI在基于古诺模型的寡头垄断市场情境中的多方面策略能力。 We answer the first question in the affirmative, demonstrating multi-faceted strategic adeptness in the context of oligopolistic Cournot-based markets. 对于第二个问题，我们表明，市场可能出现‘默契合谋’，表现为持续高于竞争水平的定价。 For the second, we show that the market may engage in the instance of ‘tacit collusion’, manifested as sustained supra-competitive pricing.
我们利用一个结合生产与投资决策、基于古诺模型的策略环境来研究这些问题，构建了一个供传统智能体与AI驱动的智能体互动的试验平台。 We examine these questions using a Cournot-based strategic environment combining production and investment decisions, creating a testbed where traditional and AI-driven agents interact. 我们以纳什策略为基准评估其表现，并推导证明了这些策略存在闭式解（定理1）。 Their performance is benchmarked against Nash strategies, for which we derive the existence of closed-form solutions (Theorem 1). 随后，我们作出两项主要的实证贡献：首先，我们通过实证表明，LLM在通过反复互动和绩效反馈进行训练后，会展现出熟练的策略能力。 We then make two main empirical contributions: First, we empirically demonstrate that LLMs, when trained through repeated interactions and performance feedback, exhibit strategic proficiency. 然而，当它们在市场中相互竞争时，会通过提高市场价格和降低产量来增加集体利润，这类似于默契合谋。 However, when paired against each other in markets, they engage in boosting collective profits by increasing market price and dropping production levels—akin to tacit collusion. 对于基于古诺模型的寡头垄断环境而言，这对以下认识提出了挑战：“成功的合谋需要某种监督和执行机制，即使不存在明确协议，甚至尤其是在不存在明确协议时，也是如此”[43, 31]。 For Cournot-based oligopolistic settings, this challenges the understanding that “successful collusion requires some system of monitoring and enforcement, even if or especially if there is no concrete agreement” [43, 31]. 其次，我们表明，以部分企业承诺采用最优反应（BR）策略的形式引入局部监管，可以推动市场结果更接近纳什均衡，从而减轻合谋倾向。 Second, we show that introducing partial regulation, in the form of some firms committing to best-response (BR) strategies, can push market outcomes closer to Nash equilibria, thus mitigating collusive tendencies.
我们的实验表明，LLM的策略行为既非完全随机，也非僵化不变。 Our experiments demonstrate that LLMs’ strategic behavior is neither purely random nor rigid. 相反，它们在多个维度上作出的决策既具有一致性，又有所区别：(i) 决策类型（生产和投资），(ii) 对手类型（BR、Nash和LLM智能体），以及(iii) 市场构成（各种多参与者情境中的市场份额）。 Instead, they generate consistent yet distinct decisions across multiple dimensions: (i) decision types (production and investment), (ii) opponent types (BR, Nash, and LLM agents), and (iii) market composition (market shares in various multi-player settings). LLM能够作出考虑情境的决策，这反映了它们既理解自身的多项决策如何影响其结果，也理解市场整体如何运作，即使市场由多个自主行动的智能体组成也是如此。 LLMs make context-aware decisions, reflecting their understanding of how their multiple decisions shape their outcomes, as well as how the market behaves in its entirety, even when it is made of multiple self-acting agents. 例如，尽管规模较大的企业是推动合谋结果的主要力量，规模较小的企业却通过采取反合谋策略，实现了更高的利润增长百分比。 For instance, while larger firms predominantly drive collusive outcomes, smaller firms achieve higher percentage profit increases by adopting anti-collusive strategies. 总体而言，这些发现表明，LLMs 如今已足够成熟，能够充当有效的经济规划代理，指导企业整体战略决策。 Overall, these findings indicate that LLMs are now sophisticated enough to serve as effective economic planning agents, capable of guiding firm-wide strategic decisions.
当部分代理遵循最优反应（best-response，BR）策略时——这模拟了某种形式的局部监管——随着市场结果趋近纳什基准，合谋行为会减少。 When some agents adhere to best-response (BR) strategies—simulating a form of partial regulation— collusive behavior diminishes as market outcomes converge toward the Nash benchmark. 这具有两个关键含义。 This has two key implications. 首先，这表明，即使相当一部分企业不采用 AI 辅助策略或受到监管机构的监督，合谋风险也可能降低。 First, this suggests that the risks of collusion may decrease even if a significant fraction of firms do not adopt AI-assisted strategies or are monitored by regulatory authorities. 这可能为监管机构实施适当的反垄断措施争取更多时间。 This potentially grants regulators additional time to implement appropriate antitrust measures. 其次，在不受监管的市场中，LLM 策略或许会对偏离合谋结果的企业构成威胁，或在竞争者之间形成一种隐性信任，从而抑制最优反应策略的采用。 Second, in unregulated markets, LLM strategies perhaps pose a threat to deviators from collusive outcomes, or generate an implicit trust among competitors that discourages best-response strategies. 进一步的研究可以探索新兴及现有架构，以更好地理解这些战略行为是如何产生的。 Further research could explore emerging and current architectures to better understand how these strategic behaviors come about.
总体而言，我们的工作为理解 LLMs 的战略行为及其对市场的潜在影响提供了新的见解。 Overall, our work provides new insights into the strategic behavior of LLMs and their potential impact on markets. 这里的关键发现是，LLMs 持续表现出合谋行为，以及这种行为给日益发展的自主代理辅助市场带来的风险。 The key insight here is the consistent collusive behavior of LLMs and the risks this presents in the growing autonomous-aided market. 我们在需要作出两项决策的、基于古诺模型的合成市场中对此进行了检验；未来的研究值得探索策略制定更复杂、情境不断演变的市场。 We test this out in synthetic Cournot-based markets with two decisions to be taken; for future research, it is worthwhile to explore more sophisticated strategy-making and context-evolving markets. 例如，放宽我们的模拟假设——如允许动态投资约束，或允许企业进入和退出——将更贴近现实世界的复杂性。 For instance, relaxing our simulation assumptions—such as permitting dynamic investment constraints or allowing for firm entry and exit—would more closely reflect real-world complexities. 研究市场不对称性、复杂成本结构以及多个战略变量（例如定价、研发、广告）如何与 AI 驱动的策略相互作用，也很有意义。 It is also interesting to study how market asymmetries, complex cost structures, and multiple strategic variables (e.g., pricing, R&D, advertising) interact with AI-driven strategies. 在监管方面，尽管受监管市场的结果展现出良好前景，但考虑到实践和法律层面的限制，要在随机且不断演变的市场中强制企业严格遵循最优反应策略，可能难以实现。 For regulation, while the regulated market results show promise, enforcing strict adherence to best response strategies in stochastic and evolving markets may be tricky to accomplish, given practical and legal limitations. 现有文献对检测和遏制算法合谋的关注已日益增加，如今应将此类努力切实扩展至 LLM 驱动的合谋。 There is already a growing interest in detecting and resisting algorithmic collusion in the literature, and such efforts should now be meaningfully extended to LLM-driven collusion as well.
