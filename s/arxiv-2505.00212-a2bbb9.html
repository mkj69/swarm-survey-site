摘要Abstract
LLM 多智能体系统中的失败归因，即识别对任务失败负有责任的智能体和步骤，为系统调试提供了关键线索，但目前相关研究仍不充分，且这一过程需要耗费大量人力。 Failure attribution in LLM multi-agent systems—identifying the agent and step responsible for task failures—provides crucial clues for systems debugging but remains underexplored and labor-intensive. 本文提出并形式化定义了一个新的研究领域：LLM 多智能体系统的自动化失败归因。 In this paper, we propose and formulate a new research area: automated failure attribution for LLM multi-agent systems. 为支持这一研究方向，我们推出了 Who&When 数据集，其中包含来自 127 个 LLM 多智能体系统的大量失败日志，并提供了将失败关联到具体智能体和决定性错误步骤的细粒度标注。 To support this initiative, we introduce the Who&When dataset, comprising extensive failure logs from 127 LLM multi-agent systems with fine-grained annotations linking failures to specific agents and decisive error steps. 利用 Who&When，我们开发并评估了三种自动化失败归因方法，并总结了各自的优缺点。 Using the Who&When, we develop and evaluate three automated failure attribution methods, summarizing their corresponding pros and cons. 表现最佳的方法在识别导致失败的智能体方面达到了 53.5% 的准确率，但在精确定位失败步骤方面的准确率仅为 14.2%，且部分方法的表现低于随机猜测。 The best method achieves 53.5% accuracy in identifying failure-responsible agents but only 14.2% in pinpointing failure steps, with some methods performing below random. 即使是 OpenAI o1 和 DeepSeek R1 等最先进的（SOTA）推理模型，也未能达到实际可用的水平。 Even SOTA reasoning models, such as OpenAI o1 and DeepSeek R1, fail to achieve practical usability. 这些结果凸显了该任务的复杂性，以及在这一领域开展进一步研究的必要性。 These results highlight the task’s complexity and the need for further research in this area. 代码和数据集已在公开仓库中提供。 Code and dataset are available in the public repository.

1 引言1 Introduction
近年来，将大语言模型（LLMs）重新定位为智能体，并构建由交互式、LLM 驱动的智能体组成、通过协作实现共同目标的多智能体系统，受到了广泛关注 (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023)。 In recent years, the reframing Large Language Models (LLMs) as agents and built agentic multi-agent systems—composed of interactive, LLM-powered agents collaborating to achieve shared goals—has garnered significant attention (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023). 这些经过专门设计的智能体系统已在多个领域展现出显著潜力，包括编程 (Hong et al., 2023)、科学发现 (Ghafarollahi & Buehler, 2024)，以及应对复杂的现实世界挑战 (Fourney et al., 2024)。 These purposefully designed agentic systems have demonstrated remarkable potential across various domains, including coding (Hong et al., 2023), scientific discovery (Ghafarollahi & Buehler, 2024), and addressing complex real-world challenges (Fourney et al., 2024).
这些系统构建完成后，若在特定场景中出现失败，通常会通过迭代过程加以改进：先依据既定基准进行评估，再开展人工失败归因并改进系统。 Once constructed, these systems are typically refined through an iterative process when they fail in specific scenarios: evaluation against established benchmarks, followed by manual failure attribution and system refinement. 这一循环会持续进行，直至取得预期结果。 This cycle repeats until the desired outcomes are achieved. 失败归因，即识别系统中直接导致任务失败的组件，是一个关键步骤，为指导改进提供了基础。 Failure attribution—identifying the components of the system that directly lead to task failures—is a crucial step that serves as the foundation for guiding improvements. 尽管这一过程十分重要，但主流研究在很大程度上忽视了它，通常将其留作一项需要投入大量人力的手动任务，例如分析复杂的历史日志，以及梳理系统中错综复杂的技术细节。 Despite its importance, this process is largely overlooked in mainstream research, which typically leaves it as a manual task requiring significant labor, such as analyzing complex historical logs and navigating the technical intricacies of the system. 此外，将基准评估结果映射到导致失败的组件高度依赖领域专业知识，这对实践者提出了额外要求。 Moreover, mapping benchmark evaluation results to failure components is heavily dependent on domain expertise, imposing additional requirements on practitioners. 随着系统复杂性的增加，开展失败归因时必须考虑的组件数量不断增多，使这一挑战愈发严峻。 As systems grow in complexity, this challenge becomes increasingly difficult due to the growing number of components that must be considered when conducting failure attribution.

为推动该领域的研究，我们推出了 Who&When 基准，其中包含大量在处理现实世界任务时产生的失败日志，并附有细粒度的失败详情标注；这些日志采集自 127 个由 LLM 驱动的多智能体系统。 To advance the research in this area, we introduce the Who&When benchmark, comprising extensive failure logs annotated with fine-grained failure details for addressing real-world tasks, where these logs are collected from 127 LLMs-powered multi-agent systems. 该基准既包含通过算法生成的多智能体系统，也包含手工构建的多智能体系统，涵盖了广泛的现实场景。 This benchmark includes both algorithmically generated and hand-crafted multi-agent systems, encompassing a wide range of realistic scenarios. 每份失败日志均附有细致的标注，明确指出应对失败负责的智能体、相应的失败步骤，以及用自然语言描述的失败原因。 Each failure log is accompanied by meticulous annotations, specifying the failure-responsible agent, the corresponding failure step, and the reasons for failure in plain language. 主要任务是准确定位对失败负有最大责任的智能体，以及错误发生的确切步骤。 The primary task involves pinpointing the agent most accountable for the failure and the exact step where the error occurred. 鉴于智能体是多智能体系统的基本单元，预测应对失败负责的智能体是失败归因的一项基本要求。 Predicting the failure-responsible agent serves as a fundamental requirement for failure attribution, given that agents are the basic units of multi-agent systems. 将这一任务扩展至预测具体的失败步骤，则提出了更高的要求，能够实现更细粒度的失败归因，例如揭示失败背后的具体原因，从而进一步促进有针对性的系统改进。 Extending this to the specific failure step prediction imposes a higher level of requirement, enabling more fine-grained failure attribution, such as uncovering the specific reasons behind failures, which can further facilitate targeted system refinements. 我们认为，Who&When 可以作为推动自动化失败归因研究进展的基础资源。 We believe that Who&When can serve as a foundational resource for driving progress in automated failure attribution research.
此外，我们构建了多种自动化失败归因方法，并在 Who&When 上对其进行了评估。 Additionally, we construct and evaluate several automated failure attribution approaches on the Who&When. 我们的研究结果揭示了各方法的优势与局限，以及它们在不同条件下的表现，包括使用不同模型、不同历史上下文长度，以及提供或不提供查询标签的情况。 Our findings reveal the strengths and limitations of each method, as well as their performance across different conditions, including model variations, historical context lengths, and the presence or absence of query labels. 这些结果凸显了使用 LLM 对多智能体系统进行失败分析的复杂性。 The results underscore the complexity of using LLMs for failure analysis in multi-agent systems. 例如，在手工构建的智能体系统中，即使是表现最好的方法，识别决定性错误步骤的准确率也仅为 8.77%。 For example, the best-performing method achieved only 8.77% accuracy in identifying decisive error steps within the hand-crafted agentic system.
2 问题形式化：多智能体协作中的自动化失败归因2 Problem Formulation: Automated Failure Attribution in Multi-Agent Cooperation
本节介绍决定性错误，并对自动化失败归因问题进行形式化描述。 In this section, we introduce decisive errors and formulate the automated failure attribution problem. 我们采用广泛使用的基于轮次的 LLM 多智能体协议 (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023)。 We adopt the widely-adopted turn-based LLM multi-agent protocol (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023).
背景。Background.
考虑一个由 LLM 驱动的多智能体系统 $\mathcal{M}$，其中包含一组在离散时间步上运行的智能体，共有 $N$ 个，记为 $\mathcal{N}=\{1,2,..N\}$。 Considering a LLMs-powered multi-agent system $\mathcal{M}$ with a group of $N$ agents, denoted as $\mathcal{N}=\{1,2,..N\}$ , that operate at discrete time steps. 这些智能体按照基于轮次的协议采取动作，即每个时间步恰好有一个智能体执行一个动作。 These agents are taking actions in a turn-based protocol, meaning that exactly one agent performs an action at each time step. 形式上，该系统描述如下： Formally, the system is described as: 
| | $\displaystyle\mathcal{M}\;=\;\Bigl\langle\mathcal{N},\,S,\,A,\,P,\,\phi\Bigr\rangle.$ | | (1)
其中，$S$ 是所有可能状态的集合。 Here, $S$ is the set of possible states. $A$ 是全局动作集合；每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{N}$ 通常可以执行某个子集 $A_{i}\subseteq A$ 中的动作。 $A$ is the global action set; each agent $i\in\mathcal{N}$ can typically perform actions from some subset $A_{i}\subseteq A$ . $\phi(t)$ 是一个函数，用于指示哪个智能体在时刻 $t$ 处于活动状态，从而规定基于轮次的规则。 $\phi(t)$ is a function that indicates which agent is active at time $t$ , thus specifying the turn-based rule. $P\bigl(s_{t+1}\mid s_{t},\,a_{t},\,\phi(t)\bigr)$ 是在时刻 $t$ 仅有一个智能体 $\phi(t)$ 采取动作时的状态转移概率。 $P\bigl(s_{t+1}\mid s_{t},\,a_{t},\,\phi(t)\bigr)$ is the state-transition probability, given that only one agent $\phi(t)$ acts at time $t$ .
我们用 $\phi(t)$ 表示在时间步 $t$ 采取动作 $a_{t}$ 的智能体。 We employ $\phi(t)$ to denote the agent that takes an action $a_{t}$ at time step $t$ . 完整轨迹 $\tau$ 可写为：$\tau\;=\;\bigl(s_{0},\,a_{0},\,s_{1},\,a_{1},\,\dots,\,s_{T}\bigr),$ 其中，$T$ 为终止时间步，或系统进入终止状态时的时间步。 A full trajectory $\tau$ can be written as: $\tau\;=\;\bigl(s_{0},\,a_{0},\,s_{1},\,a_{1},\,\dots,\,s_{T}\bigr),$ where $T$ is a terminal time step or when the system enters a terminating state.
决定性错误与目标。Decisive Error and Objective.
我们用二元组 $(i,t)$ 表示轨迹中的一个错误，即智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 处于活动状态，且其动作 $a_{t}$ 被判定为错误（例如，推理错误等）。 We use a tuple $(i,t)$ to denote a mistake in a trajectory, which means agent $i$ is active at time $t$ , and its action $a_{t}$ is deemed a mistake (e.g., wrong reasoning etc.). 一条轨迹可能包含多个错误，但并非所有错误都会导致整体失败。 A trajectory may contain multiple mistakes, but not all of them result in overall failure. 我们用 $Z(\tau)$ 表示轨迹 $\tau$ 的结果。 We employ $Z(\tau)$ to denote the result of a trajectory $\tau$ . 
| | $\displaystyle Z(\tau)\;=\;\begin{cases}1,&\text{if the system ultimately fails},\\[6.0pt] 0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$ | | (2)
假设原始轨迹 $\tau$ 的结果为失败，即 $Z(\tau)=1$。 Suppose the original trajectory $\tau$ is a failure, i.e., $Z(\tau)=1$ . 考虑以下情形：若要纠正智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 所犯的错误，我们将 $a_{t}$ 替换为一个“正确”的动作 $\tilde{a}_{t}$。 Considering the following scenario, if correcting the mistake made by agent $i$ at time $t$ : we replace $a_{t}$ with a ”correct” action $\tilde{a}_{t}$ . 步骤 $t$ 之前的步骤保持不变，而 $t$ 之后的动作则相应调整，以确保正确性。 The steps prior to step $t$ remain unchanged, while the actions following $t$ are adjusted accordingly to ensure correctness. 这一过程生成一条修改后的轨迹： This process generates a modified trajectory: 
| | $\displaystyle\tau^{(i,t)}\;=\;\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}(\tau),$ | | (3)
其中，$\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}$ 表示该干预。 where $\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}$ denotes the intervention. 如果修改后的轨迹满足 $Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0$（成功），则称错误 $(i,t)$ 为决定性错误。 If in the modified trajectory we obtain $Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0$ (success), then the error $(i,t)$ is said to be a decisive error. 形式上，我们将决定性错误指示函数 $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)$ 定义为 Formally, we define the decisive error indicator $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)$ as 
| | $\displaystyle\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)\;=\;\begin{cases}1,&\text{if }Z(\tau)=1\text{ and }Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0,\\[6.0pt] 0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$ | | (4)
用文字表述，$\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad$ 纠正智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 所犯的错误，会使 $Z(\tau)$ 从 $1$（失败）变为 $0$（成功）。 In words, $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad$ Fixing agent $i$ ’s mistake at time $t$ changes $Z(\tau)$ from $1$ (fail) to $0$ (success). 形式上，决定性错误可定义为满足 $\Delta_{i^{*},t^{*}}(\tau)=1$ 的智能体与时间二元组 $(i^{*},t^{*})$，其中，$i^{*}$ 表示应对系统失败负责的智能体，$t^{*}$ 表示关键错误发生的确切时间步。 Formally, the decisive error could be defined as agent-time pairs $(i^{*},t^{*})$ such that $\Delta_{i^{*},t^{*}}(\tau)=1$ , where $i^{*}$ represents the agent responsible for the system failure, and $t^{*}$ represents the exact time step at which the critical mistake occurs. 在全文中，我们分别将其称为失败责任智能体和决定性错误步骤。 We refer to these as the failure-responsible agent and the decisive error step, respectively across the paper.
在实际中，一条轨迹中可能出现多个决定性错误。 In practice, multiple decisive errors may occur within a trajectory. 在本研究中，我们通过将时间上最早出现的错误确定为失败的主要原因来处理这种情况。 In our study, we address this situation by identifying the earliest error in time as the principal cause of failure. 具体而言，我们定义一个目标，以确定： Specifically, we define an objective to determine: 
| | $\displaystyle\mathcal{C}(\tau)$ | $\displaystyle=\;\bigl\{(i,t)\;\mid\;\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)\;=\;1\bigr\},$ | | (5)
| | | $\displaystyle(i^{*},\,t^{*})=\;\arg\min_{(i,t)\,\in\,\mathcal{C}(\tau)}\;t.$ |
该目标选择决定性错误指示值最高且时间步最早的配对 $(i^{*},t^{*})$。 which selects the pair $(i^{*},t^{*})$ yielding the highest decisive error indicator with earliest time step. 本研究聚焦于自动识别由大语言模型（LLMs）驱动的多智能体系统中的 $(i^{*},t^{*})$。 In this study, the research problem focuses on the automatic identification of the $(i^{*},t^{*})$ in LLMs-powered multiple agent systems.
3 Who&When 数据集3 The Who&When Dataset
为推动该领域的研究，我们提出了一个名为 Who&When 的数据集。 To advance research in this area, we introduce a dataset called Who&When. 该数据集包含来自 127 个 LLM 多智能体系统的大量失败日志，涵盖算法生成的系统和人工构建的系统。 This dataset comprises extensive failure logs from 127 LLM multi-agent systems including both algorithm-generated and human-crafted systems. 这些日志经过仔细标注，标签指出了在智能体协作中直接导致问题求解失败的失败责任智能体及决定性错误步骤。 These logs are carefully annotated with labels that identify the failure-reponsible agents and the decisive error steps in agent cooperation directly responsible for problem-solving failures. 此外，每条标注均附有自然语言解释，最终形成了 184 个不同的失败标注任务。 Additionally, each annotation is supplemented with natural language explanations, culminating in 184 distinct failure annotation tasks. 该数据集专门用于识别每条失败日志中的失败责任智能体（who）及其对应的步骤（when）。 The dataset is specifically designed to detect the failure-reponsible agents (who) and the corresponding steps (when) within each failure log.
具体而言，Who&When 中的每个实例包含以下条目：(1) 查询：来自 GAIA (Mialon et al., 2023) 或 AssistantBench (Yoran et al., 2024) 的查询，描述一个现实世界中的问题。 Specifically, each instance in Who&When includes the following entry: (1) Query: A query from GAIA (Mialon et al., 2023) or AssistantBench (Yoran et al., 2024), describing a real-world question. (2) 失败日志：某个特定系统未能解决该查询时的完整对话日志。 (2) Failure log: The full conversation log of a specific system as it fails to solve the query. (3) 智能体系统信息：对于算法生成的系统，包括针对该特定查询定制的系统提示词、工具和智能体名称。 (3) Agentic system information: For algorithm-generated systems, including system prompts, tools, and agent names, all tailored to this specific query. (4) 标注：标明对任务失败负有责任的智能体，指定发生失败的步骤，并用通俗语言解释失败原因。 (4) Annotations: An annotation of the agent responsible for task failure, specifying the step where the failure occurred, along with a plain-language explanation of why the failure took place. 附录 C 中提供了该基准的一个实例。 An example of the instance in this benchmark could be found in Appendix C.

3.2 决定性错误标注3.2 Decisive Error Annotation
获取各类智能体系统的失败日志后，我们引入一套标注流程，以识别导致失败的决定性错误及其发生步骤。 After obtaining the failure logs of various agentic systems, we introduce an annotation procedure to identify the decisive error failure and decisive error step. 为确保标注准确，我们由三位 AI 智能体领域的专家开展多轮标注（其身份匿名标记为 0dmfogp3、8n3d0wmg 和 204nd84n）。 To ensure precise annotation, we conduct multiple rounds of annotation performed by three human experts in AI agent (whose identities are anonymized as 0dmfogp3, 8n3d0wmg, and 204nd84n).
第 I 轮：在第一轮中，我们将每个查询对应的所有智能体系统失败日志平均分配给三位专家。 Round I: In the first round, we distribute the failure logs from all agentic systems for each query equally among three experts. 为确保一致性，我们向专家提供了附录 F 所示的标准化标注指南。每位专家需要标注三个要素：对任务失败负主要责任的单个出错智能体、错误发生的具体步骤，以及用自然语言描述的出错原因。 To ensure consistency, we provide the experts with a standardized annotation guideline as shown in Appendix F. Each expert is tasked with annotating three elements: the single erroneous agent primarily responsible for the task failure, the specific step at which the error occurred, and the reasoning behind the mistake in natural language. 此外，每位专家都需要将自己的标注分为两组：完全确信正确的标注，以及存在任何不确定性的标注。 Additionally, each expert is required to categorize their annotations into two groups: those they are undoubtedly confident are correct and those they have any uncertainty about. 第 II 轮：在第二轮中，要求标注人员就第 II 轮中所有不确定的标注达成一致。 Round II: In the second round, people are instructed to make an agreement on all the uncertain annotations from Round II. 对于这些不确定的标注，我们通过共同讨论达成共识。 For these uncertain annotations, we engage in a collaborative discussion to reach a consensus. 我们并非简单地遵循多数决原则，而是力求确保每个人都被说服，并最终达成共识。 We do not simply follow the principle of majority rule; instead, we aim to ensure that everyone is persuaded and that a consensus is ultimately reached. 第 III 轮：在最后一轮中，采用交叉验证流程。 Round III: In the final round, a cross-validation procedure is employed. 每位专家都需要审阅另一位专家的标注，以评估标注标准的一致性。 Each expert is asked to go through another expert’s annotations to assess the consistency of the annotation standards. 如果发现标注存在任何分歧或问题，专家们会进一步讨论，并在必要时根据既定指南重新标注数据，直至达成共识。 If any discrepancies or issues with the annotations are identified, the experts engage in further discussion and, if necessary, re-annotate the data according to the established guidelines until a consensus is reached. 正如以往研究 (Clemen, 1989; Zhuge et al., 2024) 所建议的那样，我们通过综合多位标注者的观点并确保他们达成共识，力求准确获得真实的标准答案。 Incorporating the viewpoints of multiple annotators and ensuring consensus among them, we aim to accurately reach the actual ground truth, as suggested by previous studies (Clemen, 1989; Zhuge et al., 2024).
3.3 分析3.3 Analysis
无论对于非专业人员还是领域专家，标注造成决定性错误的智能体并确定错误发生的具体步骤都具有挑战性。 Annotating the decisive error agent and identifying the specific step of the error is challenging for both non-expert people and domain experts. 标注者必须解析复杂的日志，追踪每个智能体的问题求解逻辑，并判断每个动作是否正确，或者是否会使整个问题求解过程偏离正确方向。 The annotators must parse complex logs, follow the problem-solving logic of each agent, and assess whether each action is correct or if it misleads the entire problem-solving process. 例如，如果某个智能体使用网页浏览器收集解决问题所需的关键信息，标注者就必须检查浏览历史并访问每个网站，以判断失败是由于网站上没有所需信息，还是由于智能体未能检索到这些信息。 For example, if an agent uses a web browser to gather essential information for problem-solving, annotators must check the browser history and visit each website to determine whether the failure is due to unavailable information on the website or because the agent failed to retrieve it. 如图 2(a) 所示，三位标注者分别花费了 30.9、30.2 和 23.2 个工时完成标注。 As shown in Figure 2(a), three annotators spent 30.9, 30.2, and 23.2 human hours, respectively, to complete the annotations. 这表明标注过程非常耗时，促使我们考虑开展自动化失败归因研究。 This demonstrates that the annotation process is very time-consuming, leading us to consider doing research on automated failure attribution.

4.1 利用 LLMs 对智能体系统进行失败归因4.1 LLMs for Failure Attribution in Agentic Systems
为回答上述问题，我们提出了三种用于智能体系统自动化失败归因的判定方法。 To answer the question mentioned above, we propose three judgement methods for automated failure attribution in agentic systems. 通过大量实验，我们表明每种方法都有各自的优势和局限，既可以独立使用，也可以组合使用。 Through extensive experiments, we demonstrate that each method has distinct advantages and limitations, and they can be applied either independently or in combination. 此外，我们分析了这些方法在各种场景和约束条件下的表现，重点说明了它们在不同情境中的适用性。 Furthermore, we analyze the performance of these methods across various scenarios and constraints, highlighting their applicability in different contexts.
(1) 一次性判定（All-at-once）：向 LLM 提供一个查询和完整的失败日志，要求其识别对失败负责的智能体，以及决定性错误发生的具体步骤。 (1) All-at-once: An LLM is provided with a query and the complete failure log, and it is tasked with identifying the failure-responsible agent as well as the specific step where the decisive error occurred. (2) 逐步判定（Step-by-step）：向 LLM 提供一个查询，并逐步呈现失败日志。 (2) Step-by-step: An LLM is provided with a query, and the failure log is presented step-by-step. 在每一步，要求 LLM 判断当前步骤是否发生了错误。 At each step, the LLM is tasked with determining whether a mistake has occurred in the current step. 如果发现错误，判定过程即终止，并返回责任智能体的名称和当前步骤编号作为输出。 If a mistake is identified, the judging process terminates, and the responsible agent’s name along with the current step number are returned as the output. 否则，该过程将继续，直至最后一步。 Otherwise, the process continues until the final step is reached. (3) 二分搜索（Binary search）：另一种方法采用了感受野范围介于前两种方法之间的方式。 (3) Binary search: Alternatively, this method uses a receptive field approach that lies between the previous two methods. 首先向 LLM 提供查询和完整的失败日志，要求其判断错误发生在失败日志的前半部分还是后半部分。 Starting with the query and the full failure log, the LLM is tasked with determining whether the mistake occurred in the upper half or lower half of the failure logs. 作出这一判断后，将选定的半部分日志提供给 LLM，并迭代重复该过程，直到确定某一个步骤。 Once this decision is made, the LLM is provided with the selected half of the log and the process is repeated iteratively until a single step is identified. 这三种算法及其对应的提示词详见附录 A 和附录 G。 The three algorithms and corresponding prompts are detailed in Appendix A and Appendix G.
