AlphaStar 是 DeepMind 开发的一款人工智能（AI）软件，用于玩电子游戏《星际争霸 II》。 AlphaStar is an artificial intelligence (AI) software developed by DeepMind for playing the video game StarCraft II. 它于 2019 年 1 月以 AlphaStar 之名首次向公众亮相。 It was unveiled to the public by name in January 2019. AlphaStar 于 2019 年 8 月达到“宗师”等级，这在当时被视为人工智能在电子游戏领域的一个里程碑。 AlphaStar attained "Grandmaster" status in August 2019, considered a milestone for AI in video games at the time.
背景Background
为人类设计的游戏被认为在用作衡量人工智能进展的基准时具有外部效度。 Games created for humans are considered to have external validity as benchmarks of progress in artificial intelligence. IBM 的国际象棋引擎 Deep Blue（1997）和 DeepMind 的 AlphaGo（2016）被视为重要的里程碑；有人认为，攻克《星际争霸》也将是一个重要的里程碑，因为该游戏具有“实时对局、部分可观测性、不存在单一占优策略、规则复杂而难以构建快速的前向模型，以及尤为庞大且多样的动作空间”等特点。 IBM's chess engine Deep Blue (1997) and DeepMind's AlphaGo (2016) were considered major milestones; some argue that StarCraft would also be a major milestone, due to the game's "real-time play, partial observability, no single dominant strategy, complex rules that make it hard to build a fast forward model, and a particularly large and varied action space." 尽管困难重重，现有技术仍可能足以应对《星际争霸》，因为“其规则是已知的，且游戏世界是离散的，其中只有少数几类对象”。 Though difficult, StarCraft may still be tractable with current technology because "its rules are known and the world is discrete with only a few types of objects".
《星际争霸 II》是暴雪娱乐开发的一款广受欢迎、节奏明快的在线即时战略游戏。 StarCraft II is a popular fast-paced online real-time strategy game developed by Blizzard Entertainment.
历史History
DeepMind Technologies 于 2010 年在英国成立。 DeepMind Technologies was founded in the UK in 2010. 早在 2011 年，创始人 Demis Hassabis 就将《星际争霸》称为继围棋等游戏之后“更上一层的挑战”。 As early as 2011, founder Demis Hassabis called StarCraft "the next step up" after games like Go. DeepMind 展示了能够自主学习、并在多种 Atari 2600 游戏中表现出超人水平的智能体后，于 2014 年成为 Google 的子公司。 DeepMind became a subsidiary of Google in 2014, after demonstrating self-learning bots with superhuman ability at a variety of Atari 2600 games. 2015 年 2 月，计算机科学家 Zachary Mason 预测，Deepmind 的研究“将在五到十年内推进到《星际争霸》”。 In February 2015, computer scientist Zachary Mason predicted Deepmind's research "leads to StarCraft in five or ten years". 2016 年 3 月，在 AlphaGo 击败围棋世界冠军李世石之后，Hassabis 公开表示正在考虑为《星际争霸》构建人工智能，并指出它是一款信息不完全的策略游戏，与围棋不同，其大部分“棋盘”是不可见的。 In March 2016, following AlphaGo's victory over Lee Sedol, a world champion Go player, Hassabis publicly mulled building an AI for StarCraft, citing it as a strategic game with incomplete information where, unlike Go, much of the "board" is invisible. 双方于 2016 年 11 月在暴雪嘉年华上宣布正式合作，同时公布了于 2017 年第一季度发布智能体开放开发环境的计划。 A formal collaboration was announced at BlizzCon in November 2016, alongside a plan to release an open development environment for bots in Q1 of 2017.
到 2017 年，DeepMind 已开始尝试将《星际争霸》的数据输入其软件。 By 2017, DeepMind was experimenting with feeding StarCraft data into its software. 2017 年 8 月，DeepMind 和暴雪发布了辅助开发智能体的开发工具，以及来自 65,000 场历史对局的数据。 In August 2017, DeepMind and Blizzard released development tools to assist in bot development, as well as data from 65,000 historical games. 当时，计算机科学家兼《星际争霸》赛事负责人 David Churchill 估计，智能体还需要五年才能击败人类，但他也补充指出，AlphaGo 的进展曾超出预期。 At the time, computer scientist and StarCraft tournament manager David Churchill estimated it would take five years for a bot to beat a human, but made the caveat that AlphaGo had beaten expectations. 科技记者 Tom Simonite 在《连线》中写道：“没有人预期这个机器人会在短期内获胜。 In Wired, tech journalist Tom Simonite stated "No one expects the robot to win anytime soon. 但当它获胜时，这将是一项远超 DeepMind 攻克围棋的成就。” But when it does, it will be a far greater achievement than DeepMind's conquest of Go."
2018 年 12 月，DeepMind 的智能体以 5-0 击败了职业选手 Grzegorz “MaNa” Komincz。 In December 2018, DeepMind's bot defeated professional player Grzegorz "MaNa" Komincz, 5-0. DeepMind 于 2019 年 1 月公布了这款名为“AlphaStar”的智能体。 DeepMind announced the bot, named "AlphaStar", in January 2019. Ars Technica 的一名记者及其他人士认为，AlphaStar 仍具有不公平的优势：“AlphaStar 能够通过 API 以手术般的精度执行点击，而人类玩家则受到计算机鼠标机械性能的限制。” A journalist at Ars Technica and others argued that AlphaStar still had unfair advantages: "AlphaStar has the ability to make its clicks with surgical precision using an API, whereas human players are constrained by the mechanical limits of computer mice". AlphaStar 还拥有全局视野，不受游戏内摄像机视野的限制。 AlphaStar also had a global view rather than being limited by the in-game camera. 此外，尽管每个五秒时间窗口内的操作次数设有上限，AlphaStar 仍可在该窗口内自由地、不均匀地分配其操作配额，从而在关键时刻以超人水平爆发式地执行操作。 Furthermore, while there was a cap on the number of actions over a five-second window, AlphaStar was free to allocate its action quota unevenly across the window in order to launch superhuman bursts of activity at critical moments. DeepMind 很快在更符合实际的约束条件下重新训练了 AlphaStar，但随后在与 Komincz 的复赛中落败。 DeepMind quickly retrained AlphaStar under more realistic constraints, and then lost a rematch with Komincz. 从 2019 年 7 月开始，受到新约束的 AlphaStar 版本在欧洲公开 1v1 多人天梯中，匿名与“自愿参与”的玩家对战。 Starting in July 2019, the new, constrained version of AlphaStar anonymously competed against players who "opted in" on the public 1v1 European multiplayer ladder. 截至 2019 年 8 月底，AlphaStar 已达到宗师水平，跻身人类玩家的前 0.2%。 By the end of August 2019, AlphaStar had attained Grandmaster level, ranking among the top 0.2% of human players.
算法Algorithms
与 AlphaZero 不同，AlphaStar 最初会学习模仿其人类对战数据库中顶尖玩家的操作；这一步对于解决 DeepMind 的 Dave Silver 所称的“探索问题”是必要的：否则，发现新策略就如同“大海捞针”。 Unlike AlphaZero, AlphaStar initially learns to imitate the moves of the best players in its database of human vs. human games; this step is necessary to solve what DeepMind's Dave Silver calls "the exploration problem": discovering new strategies would otherwise be like finding a "needle in a haystack". 随后，智能体彼此对战，并采用深度强化学习。 Agents then play each other and deploy deep reinforcement learning. 这些主智能体还通过与表现次优的“漏洞利用智能体”（exploiter agents）对战来学习，后者的目的是暴露主智能体的弱点。 These main agents also learn by playing against suboptimal "exploiter agents" whose purpose is to expose weaknesses in the main agents.
各方反应Reactions
在 2018 年 12 月以 5-0 落败后，Komincz 表示：“我没想到人工智能会这么强。” After his 5-0 defeat in December 2018, Komincz stated "I wasn't expecting the AI to be that good".
Stuart Russell 评估认为，AlphaStar 在 2018 年的胜利需要“相当多针对具体问题的工作”，而通用方法“尚未完全具备应对《星际争霸》的能力”。 Stuart Russell assessed that AlphaStar's 2018 victory required "a fair amount of problem-specific effort" and that general-purpose methods were "not quite ready for StarCraft".
《连线》英国版的一篇文章认为，AlphaStar 为 2019 年 7 月的比赛采用的新约束条件，这一次是“公平的”。 An article in Wired UK judged AlphaStar's new constraints, adopted for the July 2019 matches, to be "fair" this time around. 《星际争霸》职业选手 Raza “RazerBlader” Sekha 表示，AlphaStar“令人印象深刻”，但也存在一些怪异之处，曾在一场比赛中败给一支仅由空中单位组成的非传统部队。 StarCraft professional Raza "RazerBlader" Sekha stated AlphaStar was "impressive" but had its quirks, succumbing in one game to an unorthodox army composition made up of only air units. 英国顶尖选手 Joshua “RiSky” Hayward 表达了些许失望，称 AlphaStar“经常未能作出最高效、最具战略性的决策”。 The UK's top player, Joshua "RiSky" Hayward, expressed some disappointment, saying AlphaStar "often didn't make the most efficient, strategic decisions". 职业选手 Diego "Kelazhur" Schwimer 称 AlphaStar 的打法“异乎寻常，令人难以想象；这确实让人质疑，职业选手究竟探索了 StarCraft 丰富多样的可能性中的多少”。 Professional Diego "Kelazhur" Schwimer called AlphaStar's play "unimaginably unusual; it really makes you question how much of StarCraft's diverse possibilities pro players have really explored". AlphaStar 的对手往往没有意识到自己正在与一个机器人程序对战。 AlphaStar's opponents often did not realize they were playing a bot.
《卫报》的 Ian Sample 称 AlphaStar 是 AI 领域的一项“里程碑式成就”。 Ian Sample, of The Guardian, called AlphaStar a "landmark achievement" for the field of AI. Churchill 表示，他此前见过能够掌握 StarCraft 中一两个要素的机器人程序，但 AlphaStar 是首个能够全面应对这款游戏的机器人程序。 Churchill stated that he had previously seen bots that master one or two elements of StarCraft, but that AlphaStar was the first that can handle the game in its entirety. Gary Marcus 表达了他对深度学习一贯持有的怀疑态度，称：“迄今为止，该领域一直难以将此类技术从实验室和游戏环境应用到现实世界中，而我目前并不认为这一成果代表了朝这个方向取得的进展。” Gary Marcus expressed his continuing skepticism about deep learning, stating: "So far the field has struggled to take techniques like this out of the laboratory and game environments and into the real world, and I don't immediately see this result as progress in that direction". AI 研究者 Jon Dodge 对 AlphaStar 感到惊讶，表示他原本预计“还要再过几年”才会出现如此“超越人类”的表现；相比之下，Churchill 表示：“StarCraft 远未被‘解决’，而 AlphaStar 的竞技水平甚至还远未接近世界冠军的水准。” AI researcher Jon Dodge was surprised by AlphaStar, stating that he did not expect such a "superhuman" performance for "another couple of years"; in contrast, Churchill states "StarCraft is nowhere near being 'solved', and AlphaStar is not yet even close to playing at a world champion level".
后续影响Legacy
DeepMind 认为，从 AlphaStar 中获得的见解可能有益于机器人、自动驾驶汽车和虚拟助手，因为它们需要在“信息观测不完全”的情况下运行。 DeepMind argues that insights from AlphaStar might benefit robots, self-driving cars, and virtual assistants, which need to operate with "imperfectly observed information". Silver 表示，他的实验室“可能就此暂歇”，而不会尝试大幅改进 AlphaStar。 Silver has indicated his lab "may rest at this point", rather than try to substantially improve AlphaStar. Silver 本人认为：“AlphaStar 已成为首个在职业认可的条件下，在完整且不受限制的游戏中，达到职业电子竞技项目人类顶尖水平的 AI 系统…… Silver himself argues that "AlphaStar has become the first AI system to reach the top tier of human performance in any professionally played e-sport on the full unrestricted game under professionally approved conditions... 自从计算机攻克围棋、国际象棋和扑克以来，StarCraft 基本上已被相关社区一致视为 AI 面临的下一个重大挑战。” Ever since computers cracked Go, chess, and poker, the game of StarCraft has emerged, essentially by consensus from the community, as the next grand challenge for AI."
计算机科学家 Noel Sharkey 不无反对地指出：“军事分析人士肯定会关注 AlphaStar 成功的实时策略，将其视为 AI 在战场规划方面具有优势的一个鲜明例证。” Computer scientist Noel Sharkey argues, disapprovingly, that "military analysts will certainly be eyeing the successful AlphaStar real-time strategies as a clear example of the advantages of AI for battlefield planning". 相比之下，Silver 认为：“声称这具有某种军事用途，无非就相当于说，国际象棋 AI 可能被用于推动军事应用。” In contrast, Silver argues: "To say that this has any kind of military use is saying no more than to say an AI for chess could be used to lead to military applications".
