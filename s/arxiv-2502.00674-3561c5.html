摘要Abstract
集成来自不同来源的输出，是一种简单而有效的性能提升方法。 Ensembling outputs from diverse sources is a straightforward yet effective approach to boost performance. Mixture-of-Agents（MoA）就是这样一种流行的集成方法，它聚合多个不同大语言模型（LLMs）的输出。 Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) is one such popular ensemble method that aggregates outputs from multiple different Large Language Models (LLMs). 本文在语言模型的背景下提出一个问题：混合不同的 LLMs 真的有益吗？ This paper raises the question in the context of language models: is mixing different LLMs truly beneficial? 我们提出 Self-MoA——一种仅聚合单个性能最佳的 LLM 所生成输出的集成方法。 We propose Self-MoA — an ensemble method that aggregates outputs from only the single top-performing LLM. 我们的大量实验揭示了一个令人意外的结果：在许多场景中，Self-MoA 优于混合不同 LLMs 的标准 MoA：在 AlpacaEval 2.0 基准上，Self-MoA 相较 MoA 提升了 $6.6\%$；在包括 MMLU、CRUX 和 MATH 在内的多个基准上，平均提升了 $3.8\%$。 Our extensive experiments reveal that, surprisingly, Self-MoA outperforms standard MoA that mixes different LLMs in a large number of scenarios: Self-MoA achieves $6.6\%$ improvement over MoA on the AlpacaEval 2.0 benchmark, and an average of $3.8\%$ improvement across various benchmarks, including MMLU, CRUX, and MATH. 将 Self-MoA 应用于 AlpacaEval 2.0 中排名靠前的一个模型，即可直接在排行榜上取得新的最先进性能。 Applying Self-MoA to one of the top-ranking models in AlpacaEval 2.0 directly achieves the new state-of-the-art performance on the leaderboard. 为理解 Self-MoA 的有效性，我们系统研究了各种 MoA 设置下输出多样性与质量之间的权衡。 To understand the effectiveness of Self-MoA, we systematically investigate the trade-off between diversity and quality of outputs under various MoA settings. 我们证实，MoA 的性能对质量相当敏感，而混合不同的 LLMs 往往会降低模型的平均质量。 We confirm that the MoA performance is rather sensitive to the quality, and mixing different LLMs often lowers the average quality of the models. 为完善这项研究，我们还确定了混合不同 LLMs 可能有帮助的场景。 To complement the study, we identify the scenarios where mixing different LLMs could be helpful. 本文进一步提出了 Self-MoA 的序贯版本，能够在多轮过程中即时聚合大量 LLM 输出，其效果与一次性聚合所有输出相当。 This paper further introduces a sequential version of Self-MoA, that is capable of aggregating a large number of LLM outputs on-the-fly over multiple rounds, and is as effective as aggregating all outputs at once.
1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型在提升不同领域的性能方面取得了显著进展，其中的代表性例子包括 GPT (Achiam et al., 2023)、Gemini (Team et al., 2023) 和 Claude (Anthropic, 2023)。 Large language models have made remarkable strides in improving performance across different domains, with notable examples such as GPT (Achiam et al., 2023), Gemini (Team et al., 2023), and Claude (Anthropic, 2023). 为增强模型能力，研究者投入了大量精力来扩大模型规模和增加训练数据。 Significant efforts have been directed toward increasing model size and training data to boost capabilities. 然而，在训练阶段扩大规模的成本高昂，而在推理阶段扩展计算量仍有待充分探索。 However, scaling at training time comes with steep costs, while scaling computation during inference remains largely underexplored.
利用测试时计算量的一种直接方法是集成，其目标是组合多个 LLMs 的输出 (Wang et al., 2024a; Lin et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a)。 A straightforward way to utilize test-time compute is ensembling, which aims to combine outputs of multiple LLMs (Wang et al., 2024a; Lin et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a). 在各种集成方法中，Mixture-of-Agents（MoA）(Wang et al., 2024a) 受到了广泛关注，并在指令遵循 (Wang et al., 2024a)、摘要生成、数据提取 (OpenPipe, 2024) 以及现实场景中的代码问题解决 (Zhang et al., 2024b) 等具有挑战性的任务上取得了优异表现。 Among various ensembling approaches, Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) (Wang et al., 2024a) has garnered significant interest, achieving superior performance in challenging tasks such as instruction following (Wang et al., 2024a), summarization, data extraction (OpenPipe, 2024), and real-world code issue resolution (Zhang et al., 2024b). 具体而言，MoA 首先向多个 LLMs（提议者，proposers）发出查询以生成回答，然后使用一个 LLM（聚合器，aggregator）综合并总结这些回答，形成高质量的回答。 Specifically, MoA first queries multiple LLMs (proposers) to generate responses, and then uses an LLM (aggregator) to synthesize and summarize these responses into a high-quality response.
先前研究强调，提议者中的模型多样性对于优化 MoA 性能至关重要，并主要关注如何集成一组多样化的单独模型。 Previous research highlights the significance of model diversity within the proposers for optimizing the performance of MoA, primarily focusing on strategies for ensembling a diverse set of individual models. 我们将跨模型多样性定义为不同模型之间的差异。 We consider cross-model diversity as the variation among different models. 然而，追求跨模型多样性可能会无意中纳入低质量模型，从而导致质量与多样性之间的权衡。 However, pursuing cross-model diversity may inadvertently include low-quality models, resulting in a quality-diversity trade-off. 以往研究主要关注实现较高的跨模型多样性 (Wang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b)，而我们通过考虑模型内多样性，从更全面的视角看待模型多样性；模型内多样性源于同一模型生成的多个回答之间的差异。 While previous studies mainly concentrate on achieving a high cross-model diversity (Wang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b), we adopt a holistic perspective on model diversity by considering in-model diversity, which arises from the variability of multiple responses generated by the same model. 模型内多样性使我们能够聚合单个模型的多个输出。 In-model diversity enables us to aggregate multiple outputs from an individual model. 直观而言，利用性能最佳的单个模型的输出，可以构建质量更高的提议者组合，从而更有效地处理质量与多样性之间的权衡。 Intuitively, leveraging outputs from the best-performing individual model can more effectively navigate the quality-diversity trade-off by creating a higher-quality proposer mixture. 因此，我们提出了如图 1b 所示的 Self-MoA，它采用与 MoA 相同的提示模板，但聚合的是从同一模型中反复采样得到的输出，而非来自一组不同模型的输出。 Thus, we propose Self-MoA as depicted in Figure 1b, which utilizes the same prompting template as MoA but aggregates outputs that are repeatedly sampled from the same model, rather than from a set of different models. 为加以区分，我们在必要时使用 Mixed-MoA 来指代组合不同单独模型的 MoA 配置。 To distinguish, we use Mixed-MoA to refer to MoA configurations that combine different individual models when necessary.
令人意外的是，我们发现，与 Self-MoA 相比，Mixed-MoA 通常并非最优选择，尤其是在提议者之间存在显著质量差异时。 Surprisingly, we find that Mixed-MoA is usually sub-optimal compared with Self-MoA, especially when there exist significant quality differences among proposers. 具体而言，我们重新考察了 Wang et al. (2024a) 所采用的 MoA 实验设置，其中包含六个经过指令微调的开源模型。 Specifically, we revisit the same experiment setting of MoA with six open-source instruction fine-tuned models as Wang et al. (2024a). 与聚合全部六个模型的 Mixed-MoA 相比，基于最强模型的 Self-MoA 在 AlpacaEval 2.0 基准上取得了 6.6 个百分点的提升，展示了模型内多样性更有效的一种情形。 Compared with Mixed-MoA which aggregates all six models, Self-MoA on the strongest model achieves 6.6 point improvement over its mixed counterpart on the AlpacaEval 2.0 benchmark, showing a case of when intra-model diversity is more effective. 此外，将 Self-MoA 分别应用于 AlpacaEval 2.0 上表现最好的两个模型，均能稳定取得 2-3 个百分点的提升，并在排行榜上的非对抗性方法中位居榜首，进一步证实了 Self-MoA 在该任务中的有效性。 Moreover, Self-MoA on two best-performed models on AlpacaEval 2.0 consistently achieves a 2-3 point gain and secures the top position among non-adversarial methods on the leaderboard, which further confirms the effectiveness of Self-MoA in this task.
为探索模型多样性对 MoA 的作用极限，我们将实验扩展到包含三个专用模型的设置，其中每个模型都擅长一项特定任务。 To explore the limits of model diversity for MoA, we extend our experiments to a setting with three specialized models, each excelling in a specific task. 具体而言，我们使用 Qwen2-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) 处理常识问答任务（MMLU-redux (Gema et al., 2024)），使用 Qwen2-Math-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) 处理数学任务（MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2020)），并使用 DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct (Zhu et al., 2024) 处理编程任务（CRUX (Gu et al., 2024)）。 Specifically, we utilize Qwen2-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) for common sense QA (MMLU-redux (Gema et al., 2024)), Qwen2-Math-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) for mathematics (MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2020)), and DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct (Zhu et al., 2024) for coding (CRUX (Gu et al., 2024)). 我们将 Self-MoA 与一系列 Mixed-MoA 策略进行比较，根据各组合在这三项任务上的平均性能，评估了单个模型的 13 种组合。 We compare Self-MoA against a range of Mixed-MoA strategies, evaluating 13 combinations of individual models based on their average performance across the three tasks. 我们的研究结果表明，在 Self-MoA 中使用任务专用模型作为提议者（proposer），其性能可以显著优于最佳的 Mixed-MoA。 Our findings indicate that employing task-specific models as proposers for Self-MoA can significantly outperform the best Mixed-MoA. 此外，即使在专为 Mixed-MoA 构建的混合任务中，每个单独的模型都擅长某项特定子任务，也仅有两种 Mixed-MoA 策略略微优于 Self-MoA，优势分别为 0.17% 和 0.35%。 Furthermore, even in a constructed mixture task tailored for Mixed-MoA where each individual model excels in a specific subtask, only two Mixed-MoA strategies slightly outperform Self-MoA by 0.17% and 0.35%.
为更好地理解 Self-MoA 的有效性，我们通过超过 200 项实验，全面研究了 MoA 中质量与多样性之间的权衡。 To better understand the effectiveness of Self-MoA, we conduct a comprehensive investigation of the trade-off between quality and diversity in MoA, involving over 200 experiments. 我们使用 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 评估提议者输出之间的多样性，并以提议者的平均性能作为质量的衡量指标。 We use the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) to evaluate the diversity among the outputs of the proposers, while the average performance of the proposers serves as the measure of quality. 在第 4 节中，我们证实 MoA 的性能与质量和多样性均呈正相关。 In Section 4, we confirm that MoA performance has a positive correlation with both quality and diversity. 此外，我们清楚地展示了在质量与多样性的可达帕累托前沿上存在的权衡关系。 Moreover, we clearly show a trade-off along the achievable Pareto front of quality and diversity. 有趣的是，我们发现 MoA 对质量变化相当敏感，其最优性能通常出现在质量较高而多样性相对较低的区域。 Interestingly, we find that MoA is quite sensitive to variations in quality, with optimal performance typically occurring in regions characterized by high quality and relatively low diversity. 这一发现自然解释了 Self-MoA 的有效性，因为它使用最强的模型作为提议者，从而确保其输出具有较高质量。 This finding naturally explains the effectiveness of Self-MoA, as it utilizes the strongest model as the proposer, ensuring high quality in its outputs.
最后，我们评估了 Self-MoA 在计算预算不断增加时的性能。 Finally, we evaluate the performance of Self-MoA under increasing computational budgets. 随着输出数量的增加，Self-MoA 的可扩展性受到聚合器上下文长度的限制。 As the number of outputs grows, the scalability of Self-MoA becomes constrained by the context length of the aggregator. 为解决这一问题，我们提出了 Self-MoA-Seq（图 1c），这是一个使用滑动窗口处理样本的序列版本，能够处理任意数量的模型输出。 To address this issue, we introduce Self-MoA-Seq (Figure 1c), a sequential version that processes samples using a sliding window, allowing it to handle an arbitrary number of model outputs. 我们的研究结果表明，Self-MoA-Seq 的性能至少与 Self-MoA 相当，使上下文长度较短的 LLM 也能在不损害最终性能的情况下实现可扩展的集成。 Our findings show that Self-MoA-Seq performs at least as effectively as Self-MoA, enabling scalable ensembling for LLMs with shorter context lengths without compromising final performance.
总体而言，我们的贡献包括三个方面： Overall, our contributions are three-fold:
我们提出了 Self-MoA，通过综合同一模型的多个输出来利用模型内部的多样性。 We introduce Self-MoA, which leverages in-model diversity by synthesizing multiple outputs from the same model. 令人意外的是，在广泛的基准测试中，它的性能优于现有的、强调模型间多样性的 Mixed-MoA 方法。 Surprisingly, it demonstrates superior performance compared to existing Mixed-MoA approaches, which emphasize cross-model diversity, across a wide range of benchmarks.
通过系统性实验和统计分析，我们揭示了提议者多样性与质量之间的核心权衡，并强调 MoA 对提议者的质量高度敏感。 Through systematic experiments and statistical analysis, we uncover a core trade-off between diversity and quality among the proposers, emphasizing that MoA is highly sensitive to proposer quality. 这一发现也解释了 Self-MoA 的成功：它利用性能最强模型的输出，从而确保更高的整体质量。 This finding also explains the success of Self-MoA, which leverages outputs from the highest-performing model, ensuring superior overall quality.
我们将 Self-MoA 扩展为其序列版本 Self-MoA-Seq，逐步迭代聚合少量输出。 We extend Self-MoA to its sequential version Self-MoA-Seq, which iteratively aggregates a small amount of outputs step by step. Self-MoA-Seq 释放了受上下文长度限制的 LLM 的潜力，并支持在推理过程中扩展计算量。 Self-MoA-Seq unlocks LLMs that are constrained by the context length and enables computation scaling during inference.

LLM 集成。Ensembles of LLMs.
模型集成旨在结合多个模型的优势。 Model ensembling aims to combine strengths from multiple models. 以往研究探索了多种利用一组多样化模型的方法，包括但不限于提示（Wang et al., 2024a）、权重平均（Lin et al., 2024; Ramé et al., 2024）、路由（Jiang et al., 2024b; Lu et al., 2023）、训练生成式融合模型（Jiang et al., 2023b）等。 Previous studies have explored various methods to leverage a diverse set of models, including but not limited to prompting (Wang et al., 2024a), weight averaging (Lin et al., 2024; Ramé et al., 2024), routing (Jiang et al., 2024b; Lu et al., 2023), training a generative fusion model (Jiang et al., 2023b), and so on. Zhang et al. (2024a) 认为，融合具备一定通用能力的专用模型，可能是迈向通用人工智能的一个有前景的方向。 Zhang et al. (2024a) argues that the fusion of specialized models with certain general abilities could be a promising direction toward Artificial General Intelligence. 智能体混合（Mixture-of-Agents，MoA，Wang et al. (2024a)）首先查询多个 LLM 以生成回答，然后通过多轮综合，迭代聚合这些样本。 Mixture-of-Agents (MoA, Wang et al. (2024a)) first queries multiple LLMs to generate responses, then iteratively aggregates these samples through several rounds of synthesis. MoA 在多个基准测试中展现出良好的结果，其变体在 AlpacaEval 2.0 排行榜上取得了优异的性能。 MoA shows promising results on several benchmarks, and its variants achieve superior performance on the AlpacaEval 2.0 leaderboard. 我们的方法受到 MoA 所提出的提示流水线的启发。 Our method is inspired by the prompt pipeline proposed in MoA. 然而，现有 MoA 着重于释放多个不同模型的优势（Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024b），而我们展示了提议者多样性与质量之间的权衡，强调仅关注多样性可能损害整体质量和最终性能。 However, while existing MoA focuses on unleashing the strength from multiple different models (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024b), we demonstrate the trade-off between diversity and quality within the proposers, highlighting that focusing solely on diversity may compromise overall quality and final performance.
采用重复采样的 LLM 推理。LLM Inference with Repeated Sampling.
以往研究表明，在多个领域中，结合重复采样得到的模型输出可以产生更好的回答。 Previous studies have shown that combining model outputs from repeated sampling can yield a better response in various domains. 在数学 (Hendrycks et al., 2021) 和代码 (Chen et al., 2021) 等具有自动验证器的任务中，仅对 LLM 进行多次采样就能显著提高 pass@k 指标，从而提升任务求解的成功率 (Roziere et al., 2023; Li et al., 2022; Brown et al., 2024)。 In tasks with automatic verifiers available, such as math (Hendrycks et al., 2021) and code (Chen et al., 2021), simply sampling LLMs multiple times can significantly improve the pass@k metric and hence boost the success rate of solving the tasks (Roziere et al., 2023; Li et al., 2022; Brown et al., 2024). 在没有验证工具的更一般的任务中，我们可以采用多数投票、自一致性和 best-of-n 等技术，从候选回答中选择最有希望的一个 (Wang et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2023b; Gui et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024)。 In more general tasks without verification tools, we can conduct techniques like majority vote, self-consistency, and best-of-n to choose the most promising one from candidate responses (Wang et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2023b; Gui et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024). 因此，重复采样近来被视为扩大推理时计算规模的一种方法 (Brown et al., 2024)。 Therefore, repeated sampling is recently regarded as one approach of scaling compute during inference time (Brown et al., 2024). 在本研究中，我们发现重复采样在 MoA 中具有出人意料的有效性。 In this work, we identify the surprising effectiveness of repeated sampling in the context of MoA. 与多数投票或 best-of-N 不同，Self-MoA 要求 LLM 综合重复采样生成的输出，因此能够在各个单独输出的基础上进一步提升表现。 Unlike majority vote or best-of-N, Self-MoA asks LLMs to synthesize outputs generated from repeated sampling, hence can further improve over each individual output.

3 集成不同的 LLM 是否有益？3 Is Ensembling Different LLMs Beneficial?
如第 1 节所述，先前的研究主要强调跨模型多样性，这可能会无意中引入低质量的提议模型。 As introduced in Section 1, previous research primarily emphasizes cross-model diversity, which can inadvertently include low-quality proposers. 在本研究中，我们提出了 Self-MoA（图 1），它使用一个表现最优的模型生成多个输出，并将这些输出聚合以产生最终结果。 In this work, we introduce Self-MoA (Figure 1), which uses a single top-performing model to generate multiple outputs and aggregate them to produce the final result. Self-MoA 利用模型内多样性，因为重复采样通常会产生不同的输出。 Self-MoA leverages in-model diversity as repeated sampling often produces varied outputs. 我们提出如下研究问题： We propose our research question as follows:
MoA 的收益是否源于跨模型多样性？ Does the benefit of MoA stem from cross-model diversity? 我们能否利用模型内多样性构建更强的 MoA？ Can we build a stronger MoA using in-model diversity?

4 质量与多样性的权衡4 The Quality-Diversity Trade-off
图 2：多样性与质量的权衡：Mixed-MoA 采用不同的单个模型作为提议模型，而 Self-MoA 使用同一个模型承担这一角色。 Figure 2: The diversity-quality trade-off: Mixed-MoA incorporates different individual models as proposers, while Self-MoA uses the same individual model for this role. 质量根据各提议模型的平均性能进行评估，多样性则通过计算提议模型针对相同提示生成的输出的 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 来衡量。 Quality is assessed based on the average performance of each proposer, and diversity is computed with the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) of outputs generated by proposers on the same prompts.
我们通过精心设计的实验，研究促成 Self-MoA 出色表现的因素。 We investigate factors that contribute to the strong performance of Self-MoA through careful experiments. 先前的研究主要关注提高模型群体内部的多样性 (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b)。 Previous studies have mainly focused on increasing model diversity within the group (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b). 然而，寻找多样化模型有时会导致纳入表现较差的模型，从而在多样性与质量之间形成权衡，其中质量指群体中各个模型的表现水平。 However, searching for diverse models can sometimes lead to including poorly performed models, resulting in a trade-off between diversity and quality, where quality refers to how well each individual model performs in the group.
因此，我们旨在确定 MoA 的性能与质量及多样性之间是否存在普遍关系。 Therefore, we aim to identify the existence of a general relationship between MoA’s performance and quality as well as diversity. 沿用第 3 节的设置，我们在 MMLU、CRUX 和 MATH 上评估 MoA 的性能，这些基准涵盖了需要广泛能力的任务。 Following Section 3, we evaluate MoA’s performance on MMLU, CRUX, and MATH, which cover tasks requiring a wide range of capabilities. 我们通过两个自由度来改变质量和多样性：1) 第 3.2 节中提议模型所采用的单个模型组合；2) 采样温度，即 0.5、0.7、1.0、1.1 和 1.2。 We vary the quality and diversity with two orders of freedom: 1) combinations of individual models in proposers from Section 3.2; and 2) sampling temperature. i.e., 0.5, 0.7, 1.0, 1.1, and 1.2. 由此共得到超过 70 种不同的 MoA 提议模型混合配置。 This results in a total of over 70 unique MoA proposer mixtures. 我们按如下方式衡量质量和多样性： We measure the quality and diversity as follows:
多样性：我们使用 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 来评估提议模型混合配置中各个模型之间的多样性。 Diversity: We utilize the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) to assess the diversity among individual models in the proposer mixture. Vendi Score 表示样本集合中不同元素的有效数量 (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023)，更多细节见附录 A.2。 The Vendi Score represents the effective number of unique elements within a collection of samples (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023), with further details provided in the Appendix A.2. 具体而言，对于给定的提示 $x$，我们获取各个模型的回答，记为 $y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{6}$。 Specifically, for a given prompt $x$ , we obtain responses from each model, denoted as $y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{6}$ . 针对提示 $x$ 的提议模型多样性记为 $d(x)$，通过计算集合 $[y_{1},\ldots,y_{6}]$ 的 Vendi Score 得到。 The diversity of the proposers for prompt $x$ , denoted as $d(x)$ , is calculated using the Vendi Score on the set $[y_{1},\ldots,y_{6}]$ . 随后，我们按如下方式计算数据集 $S$ 上的总体多样性： We then compute the overall diversity across the dataset $S$ as: 
| | $d=\frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{x\in S}d(x).$ |
质量：我们首先确定各个模型在数据集 $S$ 上的准确率，得到数值 $q_{1},q_{2},\ldots,q_{6}$。 Quality: We first determine the accuracy of each model on the dataset $S$ , yielding values $q_{1},q_{2},\ldots,q_{6}$ . 平均准确率 $q=\frac{1}{6}(q_{1}+q_{2}+\ldots+q_{6})$ 用作我们衡量提议模型质量的指标。 The average accuracy, $q=\frac{1}{6}(q_{1}+q_{2}+\ldots+q_{6})$ , serves as our measure of the quality of the proposers. 我们将在后续章节中探索其他质量衡量策略。 We will explore additional quality measurement strategies in later sections.

此前关于集成的研究 (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b) 主要侧重于提高提议模型组合中模型的多样性。 Previous work on ensembles (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b) primarily focuses on increasing the diversity of models within the proposer mixture. 然而，如图 2 所示，与基于表现最佳模型的 Self-MoA 相比，单纯追求提议模型组合的更高多样性可能导致整体质量下降，从而对 MoA 的性能产生负面影响。 However, as shown in Figure 2, compared to Self-MoA on the best-performing model, simply aiming for greater diversity in the proposer mixture can result in lower overall quality, which may negatively impact MoA’s performance. 多样性与质量之间的这种权衡有助于解释为何 Self-MoA 能在各种基准测试中取得更优的性能。 This trade-off between diversity and quality helps to explain why Self-MoA achieves superior performance across various benchmarks.

平均值：$\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}$。 Average: $\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}$ .

5 使用 Self-MoA 扩展推理计算量5 Scaling Inference Compute with Self-MoA
在前面的章节中，我们提供了证据，表明基于一个强模型的 Self-MoA 方法简单而有效。 In previous sections, we have provided evidence that Self-MoA over one strong model is straightforward but effective. 随着研究界越来越重视扩展推理时的计算量（Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024），一个自然的问题是： As the community is becoming more aware of scaling inference time computing (Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024), one natural question to ask is:
给定一个强模型，Self-MoA 的性能是否会随着重复采样次数的增加而提升？ Given a strong model, does Self-MoA’s performance scale with the number of repeated samples?
直观而言，Self-MoA 无法仅靠增加计算预算而无限提升性能，原因至少有以下三个： Intuitively, Self-MoA cannot scale indefinitely by simply increasing the computation budget for at least three reasons:
随着从单个模型中采样得到的回答增多，这些样本之间的多样性往往会趋于平台期。 As more responses are sampled from a single model, the diversity among those samples tends to plateau.
对于 LLM 而言，与处理较少的样本相比，从大量样本中聚合信息更具挑战性。 Aggregating information from many samples is more challenging for LLMs compared to handling a smaller number of samples.
每个 LLM 都有上下文长度限制（例如，Gemma 2 的限制为 8192 个词元），这限制了聚合模型一次能够处理的回答数量。 Every LLM has a context length limit (e.g., 8192 tokens for Gemma 2), which restricts the number of responses an aggregator can process at once.
虽然第一个限制是重复采样所固有的，但我们通过引入 Self-MoA-Seq 来应对后两个限制；这是一种顺序处理变体，旨在管理大量回答，同时避免使聚合模型负担过重。 While the first limitation is inherent to repeated sampling, we address the latter two by introducing Self-MoA-Seq, a sequential variant designed to manage large numbers of responses without overwhelming the aggregator. Self-MoA-Seq 使用滑动窗口，每次聚合固定数量的回答，从而能够处理任意数量的回答，不受上下文长度的约束。 Self-MoA-Seq uses a sliding window to aggregate a fixed number of responses at a time, allowing it to handle an unlimited number of responses, regardless of context length constraints. 图 1 提供了直观示意。 A visual illustration is provided in Figure 1.

A.2 Vendi 分数A.2 Vendi Score
Vendi 分数（Vendi Score，VS）是一种用于评估机器学习中多样性的指标。 The Vendi Score (VS) is a metric designed to evaluate diversity in machine learning. 它以一组样本及一个成对相似度函数为输入，输出一个数值，表示样本集中独特元素的有效数量。 It takes as input a collection of samples along with a pairwise similarity function, and it outputs a single value that represents the effective number of unique elements within the sample set.
该分数使用半正定相似度矩阵 $K\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$，按如下方式计算： The score is computed using a positive semi-definite similarity matrix $K\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ as follows: 
| | $VS(K)=\exp\left(-\text{tr}\left(\frac{K}{n}\log\left(\frac{K}{n}\right)\right)\right)=\exp\left(-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\log(\lambda_{i})\right)$ |
这里，$\lambda_{i}$ 是归一化矩阵 $\frac{K}{n}$ 的特征值，且 $0\log 0=0$。 Here, $\lambda_{i}$ are the eigenvalues of the normalized matrix $\frac{K}{n}$ , and $0\log 0=0$ . 本质上，Vendi 分数是 $\frac{K}{n}$ 的冯·诺依曼熵的指数，该熵反映了其特征值的香农熵，而所得分数也称为有效秩。 Essentially, the Vendi Score is the exponential of the von Neumann entropy of $\frac{K}{n}$ , which reflects the Shannon entropy of its eigenvalues, also referred to as the effective rank. 该指标根据样本间相似度分数的分布，对多样性进行量化度量。 This metric provides a quantitative measure of diversity based on the distribution of similarity scores among the samples.
