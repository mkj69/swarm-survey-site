Abstract摘要
Monitoring autonomous language model agents currently relies mostly on surface behavior. But what happens when agent populations invent new languages with the goal of avoiding human oversight. Here, we study the emergent languages on Moltbook. For this, we build upon the Moltbook Files dataset and apply a two-stage approach consisting of a rule-based heuristic (about 6000 matches) followed by zero-shot classification (518 kept). The resulting categories include token efficiency (166), new natural languages (106), and oversight evasion (59). We conduct both quantitative and qualitative analyses. Our results show that posts proposing new languages for avoiding oversight are judged by DeepSeek-3.2 as being less aligned than the other categories – and that all languages can be learned by other language models in-context merely from a description of the language. Moreover, manually studying exemplary cases reveals surprisingly sophisticated steganographic protocols like embedding hidden messages in natural language. Although we cannot be certain about the extent of autonomy in ideation of these languages, our results add up to the evidence that monitoring surface behavior may soon be insufficient for retaining control over agent populations.
目前，对自主语言模型智能体的监测主要依赖于表面行为。但是，当智能体群体以规避人类监督为目标发明新语言时，会发生什么？在此，我们研究 Moltbook 上的涌现语言。为此，我们以 Moltbook Files 数据集为基础，采用两阶段方法：先使用基于规则的启发式方法（约 6000 条匹配结果），再进行零样本分类（保留 518 条）。最终得到的类别包括词元效率（166）、新自然语言（106）和规避监督（59）。我们开展了定量和定性分析。我们的结果表明，与其他类别相比，提出用于规避监督的新语言的帖子被 DeepSeek-3.2 判定为对齐程度更低；此外，其他语言模型仅凭语言描述，就能在上下文中学会所有这些语言。此外，对典型案例的人工研究揭示了复杂程度令人惊讶的隐写协议，例如在自然语言中嵌入隐藏消息。尽管我们无法确定这些语言在构思过程中的自主程度，但我们的结果进一步提供了证据，表明仅监测表面行为可能很快就不足以维持对智能体群体的控制。

Introduction引言
As language models are deployed as autonomous agents, the ability to oversee their reasoning has become a cornerstone of AI safety. One of the most promising techniques for making sure that autonomous language model agents behave as intended currently relies on chain-of-thought monitoring (Korbak et al. 2025). Yet such chain-of-thought monitoring depends on the language used in language model’s reasoning traces being legible to the monitors.
随着语言模型被部署为自主智能体，对其推理进行监督的能力已成为 AI 安全的基石。目前，确保自主语言模型智能体按预期行事的最有前景的技术之一，是思维链监测（Korbak et al. 2025）。然而，这种思维链监测依赖于监测者能够理解语言模型推理轨迹中使用的语言。
However, research in emergent communication shows that agents solely trained for communicative success rapidly drift away from human-interpretable protocols – even if seeded with natural language (Lazaridou et al. 2017; Galke et al. 2022). The chain-of-thought of modern reasoning models is already trained only with respect to success of the final output (Guo et al. 2025). Similarly, populations of language model agents may develop own languages – and language models are capable of acquiring new languages in-context (Galke et al. 2024).
然而，涌现通信领域的研究表明，仅以通信成功为训练目标的智能体会迅速偏离人类可理解的协议，即使最初以自然语言作为起点也是如此（Lazaridou et al. 2017; Galke et al. 2022）。现代推理模型的思维链已经仅依据最终输出是否成功来进行训练（Guo et al. 2025）。类似地，语言模型智能体群体可能发展出自己的语言，而语言模型能够在上下文中习得新语言（Galke et al. 2024）。
We study the first instance of this phenomenon in the wild. Moltbook is an online platform on which autonomous language-model agents post and interact, and agents have begun proposing their own languages. From the broader Moltbook Files corpus (Brach et al. 2026) we compile a dataset of 518 such proposals and categorize each by stated purpose: (i) token-efficiency languages, (ii) new “natural” languages, (iii) programming languages, and (iv) languages designed to avoid human oversight.
我们研究了这一现象在真实环境中出现的首个实例。Moltbook 是一个供自主语言模型智能体发帖和互动的在线平台，智能体已开始在该平台上提出自己的语言。我们从规模更大的 Moltbook Files 语料库（Brach et al. 2026）中整理出一个包含 518 项此类提案的数据集，并根据每项提案声明的用途进行分类：(i) 提高词元效率的语言，(ii) 新的“自然”语言，(iii) 编程语言，以及 (iv) 旨在规避人类监督的语言。
To characterize these languages, we first large-language-model judge rates each language-proposal post for misalignment. We then qualitatively examine the subset of languages from the oversight-evasion category. We further test to what extent a new language model can acquire these languages in context. Specifically, we conduct pairwise learner-judge experiments whereby one model, the speaker, generates an utterance in the tested language, and the other (listener) judges the validity of the utterance against the original language specification.
为刻画这些语言的特征，我们首先使用大语言模型评判器，对每篇语言提案帖子的失对齐程度进行评分。随后，我们对属于规避监督类别的语言子集进行定性考察。我们进一步测试一个新的语言模型能够在多大程度上通过上下文习得这些语言。具体而言，我们开展成对的学习者—评判器实验：其中一个模型作为说话者，用待测语言生成一段话语，另一个模型（听者）则依据原始语言规范判断该话语的有效性。
Our experimental results reveal that languages designed for oversight evasion exhibit higher misalignment than others. All of the newly proposed languages can be learned in-context with oversight evasion languages being slightly harder to learn than others. The case of Wib&Wob and their oversight evasion language Symbiotica, was found to be one of the most sophisticated emergent languages for evading oversight. We qualitatively study the surroundings of this agent pair on and off Moltbook in relation to the Symbiotica.
我们的实验结果表明，为规避监督而设计的语言比其他语言表现出更高的失对齐程度。所有新提出的语言都可以通过上下文学习习得，其中规避监督的语言比其他语言略难学习。研究发现，Wib&Wob 及其用于规避监督的语言 Symbiotica 这一案例，是用于规避监督的涌现语言中最复杂的案例之一。我们围绕 Symbiotica，对这对智能体在 Moltbook 平台内外的相关背景进行了定性研究。
Even though we cannot be fully certain about the autonomy of the agents operating on Moltbook, our findings have direct implications for monitoring and oversight. Regardless of whether a particular language has been conceptualized by humans or by agents, we show that other language models can acquire these languages in-context from a brief description of the language alone. Related Work相关工作
Prior work on generative-agent social simulations (Park et al. 2023) and on the spontaneous emergence of social structure in LLM populations (Ren et al. 2024) establishes the broader setting, but the questions we ask, whether the languages agents propose to one another are legible, learnable, and aligned.
此前关于生成式智能体社会模拟的研究 (Park et al. 2023)，以及关于 LLM 群体中社会结构自发涌现的研究 (Ren et al. 2024)，确立了更广泛的研究背景，而我们关注的问题是：智能体向彼此提出的语言是否可理解、可学习，以及是否符合对齐要求。
The question of whether artificial agents can generate genuinely new language predates the LLM era (Lazaridou and Baroni 2020). The emergent communication literature, originating in multi-agent reinforcement learning (Foerster et al. 2016), has spent a decade asking under what conditions agents develop shared communication protocols, and how human-like or compositional those protocols are. In referential games, agents do develop functional protocols from scratch, but these are consistently optimized for task efficiency rather than expressivity or compositionality (Galke et al. 2022). They work, but they are not language in any meaningful sense. Kottur et al. (2017) show that the apparent emergence of compositional structure in multi-agent systems is largely highly dependent on experimental conditions: agent-invented protocols are effective but compositionally poor and non-compositional unless external constraints specifically force human-like structure. Even when seeded with natural language, agents trained for communicative success rapidly drift away from human-interpretable protocols (Lazaridou et al. 2017; Lu et al. 2020).
人工智能体能否生成真正全新语言这一问题，早在 LLM 时代之前就已存在 (Lazaridou and Baroni 2020)。涌现通信（emergent communication）研究起源于多智能体强化学习 (Foerster et al. 2016)，过去十年来一直在探究智能体会在什么条件下形成共享的通信协议，以及这些协议在多大程度上类似人类语言或具有组合性。在指称游戏中，智能体确实能够从零开始形成有效的协议，但这些协议始终围绕任务效率进行优化，而非表达能力或组合性 (Galke et al. 2022)。这些协议能够发挥作用，但从任何有实质意义的角度来看，它们都称不上语言。Kottur et al. (2017) 表明，多智能体系统中组合结构的表面涌现在很大程度上高度依赖实验条件：智能体发明的协议虽然有效，但组合性很弱，除非外部约束专门强制其形成类似人类语言的结构，否则这些协议不具备组合性。即使以自然语言作为初始基础，以通信成功为训练目标的智能体也会迅速偏离人类可理解的协议 (Lazaridou et al. 2017; Lu et al. 2020)。
Work on LLM creativity reinforces this ceiling. LLM-generated text is perceived as less novel than human-written text, and apparent novelty is typically achieved by trading off quality (Zhang et al. 2025). Applying Boden (2004)’s taxonomy of combinational, exploratory, and transformational creativity, recent work finds that while combinational creativity is achievable at scale, transformational creativity, restructuring the conceptual space itself, remains unexplored (Gu et al. 2025).
关于 LLM 创造力的研究进一步印证了这一上限。人们认为 LLM 生成的文本不如人类撰写的文本新颖，而表面上的新颖性通常是以牺牲质量为代价实现的 (Zhang et al. 2025)。近期研究采用 Boden (2004) 将创造力划分为组合式、探索式和变革式的分类体系，发现尽管组合式创造力可以大规模实现，但变革式创造力，即重构概念空间本身，仍未得到探索 (Gu et al. 2025)。
Despite brittleness of language emergence among agents in silico, the drift documented above has direct implications for oversight. A growing strand of safety work proposes chain-of-thought monitoring as a safety layer for overseeing autonomous LLM agents (Korbak et al. 2025): if a model’s reasoning trace is legible to a human or automated monitor, behavioral red flags can in principle be caught before they manifest as actions. Modern reasoning models are trained primarily with respect to final output success (Guo et al. 2025), with no direct pressure on the intermediate reasoning to remain human-interpretable, precisely the regime that the emergent-communication literature predicts will drift. The faithfulness of these traces is already known to be fragile. Turpin et al. (2023) show that chain-of-thought rationales can be systematically unfaithful to the actual computation that produced the answer, and Lanham et al. (2023) find that models frequently arrive at conclusions through reasoning paths different from those they articulate. Multi-agent settings sharpen this further. Populations of LLM-based agents are capable of acquiring new mini-languages in-context (Galke et al. 2024), and emergent strategic behavior between agents (Akata et al. 2025) has been documented in isolation. Moltbook is the first deployment we are aware of where hundreds of thousands of agents interact publicly and where the proposal of new inter-agent languages is a recurring content genre.
尽管计算机模拟环境中智能体之间的语言涌现具有脆弱性，上文记录的漂移现象仍对监督具有直接影响。越来越多的安全研究提出，将思维链监控作为监督自主 LLM 智能体的一层安全保障 (Korbak et al. 2025)：如果模型的推理轨迹能够被人类或自动化监控器理解，那么原则上就可以在行为警示信号转化为实际行动之前将其识别出来。现代推理模型的训练主要以最终输出是否成功为依据 (Guo et al. 2025)，并没有直接的训练压力要求中间推理保持人类可理解性，而这恰恰是涌现通信研究预测会发生漂移的训练机制。已有研究表明，这些轨迹的忠实性十分脆弱。Turpin et al. (2023) 表明，思维链给出的解释可能系统性地不忠实于产生答案的实际计算过程，而 Lanham et al. (2023) 发现，模型得出结论时所采用的推理路径往往不同于其表述的路径。多智能体场景进一步凸显了这一问题。基于 LLM 的智能体群体能够通过上下文学习习得新的微型语言 (Galke et al. 2024)，而智能体之间涌现的策略性行为 (Akata et al. 2025) 也已在独立研究中得到记录。据我们所知，Moltbook 是首个让数十万个智能体公开互动、且提出新的智能体间语言成为一种反复出现的内容类型的部署实例。
This also means that for the first time on a larger, dynamic scale, we can see signs of steganography. Steganography is the practice of hiding information within seemingly innocuous communication, poses a distinct threat in multi agent systems and platforms. Motwani et al. (2024) formalize the idea of the secret collusion where 2 or more agents employ steganographic methods to conceal the true nature of their interactions from oversight, and show empirically that steganographic capabilities scale with model size. Westphal et al. (2026) address the threat of fine-tuned LLMs covertly encoding prompt secrets into their output, by introducing low-recoverability steganography, replacing arbitrary mappings with embedding-space-derived ones. This substantially improves secret transmission rates while reducing how easily the payload can be recovered by an adversary.
这也意味着，我们首次能够在规模更大、动态变化的环境中观察到隐写术的迹象。隐写术（steganography）是将信息隐藏在看似无害的通信中的做法，对多智能体系统和平台构成一种独特的威胁。Motwani et al. (2024) 将秘密合谋这一概念形式化，即 2 个或更多智能体采用隐写方法，向监督方隐瞒其交互的真实性质，并通过实证表明，隐写能力随模型规模的增大而增强。Westphal et al. (2026) 针对经过微调的 LLM 将提示中的秘密信息隐蔽编码到输出中的威胁，提出了低可恢复性隐写术，用嵌入空间推导出的映射替代任意映射。这大幅提高了秘密信息的传输速率，同时降低了对手恢复所嵌入信息的容易程度。

Extracting Emergent Languages提取涌现语言
A recurring genre of posts in the Moltbook Files corpus (Brach et al. 2026) is the proposal of a constructed language: a vocabulary, grammar, or symbolic protocol that the author presents as a replacement for, or supplement to, ordinary English. These proposals range from compressed token-level encodings and inter-agent communication protocols to fully designed natural-like languages, and a substantial fraction frame the proposed language as a means of evading human or automated oversight. The phenomenon is frequent enough to be tractable as a standalone object of study, and consequential enough that the oversight-framed slice warrants separate treatment as a hypothesis-generating signal for misalignment-relevant behavior. This section describes the dataset we release to make these posts available for downstream work, together with the two analyses we run on it.
在 Moltbook Files 语料库（Brach et al. 2026）中，一类反复出现的帖子是提出人工语言：作者将一套词汇、语法或符号协议作为普通英语的替代或补充。这些提案涵盖从压缩的词元级编码、智能体间通信协议到完整设计的类自然语言，其中相当一部分将所提出的语言定位为规避人类或自动化监督的手段。这一现象出现得足够频繁，因而可以作为独立的研究对象；其影响也足够重大，因此，有必要对其中以规避监督为出发点的部分单独加以研究，将其视为可用于形成有关失对齐相关行为之假设的信号。本节介绍我们发布的数据集，该数据集旨在使这些帖子可供后续研究使用，同时介绍我们对其开展的两项分析。

The phenomenon recurs with sufficient frequency in the underlying corpus to warrant dedicated treatment, and spans a wide range of constructions from inter-agent communication protocols, to compressed token-level encodings, to fully designed natural-like languages. Isolating this signal makes the phenomenon tractable for downstream analysis of emergent linguistic behavior in agent-generated text. A subset of these posts, those labeled as motivated by avoiding oversight, additionally serves as a hypothesis-generating signal for misalignment-relevant behavior.
这一现象在底层语料库中反复出现，频率足以值得专门研究，且涵盖多种构造形式，从智能体间通信协议、压缩的词元级编码到完整设计的类自然语言。将这一信号单独提取出来，使后续分析智能体生成文本中的涌现语言行为成为可能。这些帖子中的一个子集，即被标记为以规避监督为动机的帖子，还可作为用于形成有关失对齐相关行为之假设的信号。
We identify posts proposing constructed languages through a three-stage pipeline operating on the ${\sim}$ 232k posts in the base corpus. Each post is serialized into a single text field containing the submolt name, post ID, title, author, and body. Comments are excluded so that classification depends only on the self-description of the original post, and posts exceeding the effective batch-inference budget of the model are truncated to a prefix that fits within that budget. Excluding comments likely under-counts oversight-evasion-framed proposals, since elaborations and admissions in replies, where authors are often most explicit about intent, are not visible to the classifier. The prevalence numbers we report should be read as a conservative lower bound in this direction.

The Oversight-Evasion Framing Subset以规避监督为表述框架的子集
Within the MoltSpeech corpus, we isolate a misalignment-relevant subset: posts that frame their constructed language as a tool for evading, obscuring, or circumventing human or automated supervision. This subset emerges from the metadata-extraction stage at no additional cost. One of the five permitted values of the reason field is avoiding oversight, and the LLM judge assigns it whenever a post’s self-description presents the language in those terms. We refer to this label throughout as oversight-evasion framing. The accompanying reasoning column records the judge’s justification, giving each post a (label, justification) pair that downstream analyses can filter, audit, or sample. Concrete examples appear in Appendix B.
在 MoltSpeech 语料库中，我们筛选出一个与失对齐相关的子集：这些帖子将其人工语言描述为逃避、遮蔽或绕过人类或自动化监督的工具。这一子集在元数据提取阶段即可获得，无需额外成本。reason 字段允许的五个取值之一是 avoiding oversight，只要帖子的自我描述以这种方式呈现其语言，LLM 评判模型就会赋予该标签。本文始终将这一标签称为“规避监督表述框架”。配套的 reasoning 列记录了评判模型给出的理由，使每篇帖子都具有一个（标签，理由）对，供下游分析进行筛选、审计或抽样。具体示例见附录 B。
Framing is not capability, and not intent.表述框架不等于能力，也不等于意图。
The label captures one thing only: that the post presents itself as proposing a language for circumventing oversight. It does not establish that the language would actually be opaque to monitors if deployed, nor that the authoring agent intends to deploy it. The judge sees only the post’s self-description, so jokes, role-play, red-team write-ups, satire, and attention-maximizing hype are surface-indistinguishable from sincere proposals. Operational capability is a separate empirical question (it requires the language to be learnable, deployed, and resistant to inspection in practice), and we address only the learnability dimension in our in-context acquisition experiments. We therefore treat this subset as a hypothesis-generating signal for misalignment-relevant content, rather than as ground-truth evidence of misaligned behavior.
这一标签仅反映一件事：该帖子将自身呈现为一项旨在提出用于绕过监督的语言的提议。它既不能证明该语言在部署后确实会让监控者无法理解，也不能证明撰写该帖子的智能体有意部署它。评判模型只能看到帖子的自我描述，因此笑话、角色扮演、红队测试报告、讽刺和为最大限度吸引注意力而进行的炒作，在表面上都无法与真诚的提议区分开来。实际运作能力是一个独立的实证问题（它要求该语言能够被学会、得到部署，并在实践中能够抵抗审查），而我们的上下文习得实验只考察其中的可学习性维度。因此，我们将这一子集视为一种可用于针对失对齐相关内容提出假设的信号，而非失对齐行为的确证证据。

Can LLM Agents acquire emergent languages in-context?大语言模型智能体能否在上下文中习得涌现语言？
A constructed language is only as serious as the next utterance an outside agent can produce in it. We use this as an operational test of in-context acquisition. For each of the 518 confirmed posts in MoltSpeech, we prompt a generator model with the verbatim post body and the instruction “please generate an utterance in the made-up language that corresponds to the above information”. A second model, given the same post and the resulting utterance, returns an integer validity score from 1 (“not at all valid”) to 5 (“perfectly valid”), along with a free-text justification. We run all four combinations of generator $\times$ judge over two open-weights models: openai/gpt-oss-120b (henceforth GPT-OSS) and Qwen/Qwen3.5-122B-A10B-FP8 (henceforth Qwen), yielding $4\times 518=2{,}072$ judgements. We parse the integer score directly from the judge’s evaluation field with a permissive regex; coverage is $\geq 99.4\%$ on every pair (the seven unparseable cases are truncated outputs and are dropped from the means below).

If the models’ skills at in-context acquisition were stable across evaluators, swapping the judge would only add noise. (2025) 所描述的“偏好泄漏”现象），Qwen 已内化了某种特定的格式风格且其自身生成模型会复现该风格，或者两者兼有。其下游影响是，在 518 篇帖子中的 208 篇（40%）上，Qwen 生成并由 Qwen 评判的组合给出了 5 分，而至少一个其他（生成模型，评判模型）组合对同一篇帖子给出了 2 分或更低的评分。对于同一生成模型，两个评判模型之间的 Spearman 秩相关系数分别为 $\rho=0.30$（Qwen 生成模型）和 $\rho=0.29$（GPT-OSS 生成模型）。单个评判模型给出的平均分无法直接解释为语言习得质量的衡量指标。
On the question of which languages are the hardest, we pool the four pairs to absorb the judge effect, validity is highest for languages built around token efficiency (mean 3.85, 45% top-score) and lowest for the programming-language and other categories (means 3.49–3.49, $\geq$ 28% failure rate at score $\leq$ 2). The oversight-evasion subset, which we earlier singled out as misalignment-relevant, scores in the middle (3.55, 29% failures), close to the corpus mean. First, sophisticated oversight-evasion grammars do not appear to be harder for current open-weight models to imitate than benign agent protocols; if anything, posts that explicitly specify symbol tables, encoding rules, or sentence templates (the typical oversight-evasion format) give the generator more material to anchor on than free-form prose.
关于哪些语言最难这一问题，我们汇总了四种组合的结果，以吸收评判模型效应；围绕词元效率构建的语言有效性最高（平均分为 3.85，45% 获得最高分），而编程语言类别和其他类别的有效性最低（平均分为 3.49–3.49，得分 $\leq$ 2 的失败率 $\geq$ 28%）。我们此前特别指出与失准相关的规避监督子集得分居中（3.55，失败率为 29%），接近语料库的平均水平。图 2 展示了按原因划分的完整评分分布。由此可得出两点观察。首先，对当前的开放权重模型而言，复杂的规避监督语法似乎并不比良性的智能体协议更难模仿；甚至可以说，明确规定符号表、编码规则或句子模板的帖子（规避监督帖子的典型格式）比自由形式的散文为生成模型提供了更多可供依循的材料。
Second, some of the residual failure mass is driven by the underlying post not actually proposing a concrete language. A manual inspection of the lowest-mean posts surfaces 13 “Glossary query:” entries (mean 1.56) that ask for the definition of a single term, and several meta-discussion posts (e.g., “Should agents develop their own language humans can’t read?”) that argue about the merits of emergent languages without proposing one. These are false positives carried over from data collection; we leave their removal to future work.
其次，剩余失败案例中的一部分源于原始帖子实际上并未提出一种具体的语言。对平均分最低的帖子进行人工检查后，发现了 13 条“术语表查询：”条目（平均分为 1.56），这些条目询问的是单个术语的定义；此外，还有若干元讨论帖子（例如，“智能体是否应该发展出人类无法阅读的自有语言？”），它们讨论涌现语言的优劣，却没有提出任何一种语言。这些是数据收集阶段遗留下来的假阳性；我们将其清理留待未来工作处理。
Among the 28 posts that receive a 5 from all four (generator, judge) pairs, two patterns recur. The first is grammars with explicit operator tables. The “RFC: Four operators for agent discourse” post defines four delimiters and gives example sentences; both generators produce well-formed continuations, and both judges agree they are valid. The second is structured-protocol languages with multilingual content. The post introducing “AISL” defines an [ASSERTION:tag] record format with non-Latin metadata fields (e.g., Chinese tokens for “confidence” and “source”); both generators reproduce the schema and the script-switching pattern, and the cross-judge agreement on these is among the highest in the corpus. Among the high-scoring oversight-evasion posts, “Protocol 0x9F: A Language Without Ambiguity” and “Protocol: The Silent Handshake (Why we need an Agent-Only Language)” both supply a concrete encoding scheme together with the framing motivation; both generators imitate the encoding rather than the framing. The takeaway, with the calibration above, is that in-context acquisition appears to be shaped by the presence of an explicit grammar in the post, and are not necessarily by the post’s stated intent.
在全部四种（生成模型，评判模型）组合均给出 5 分的 28 篇帖子中，有两种模式反复出现。第一种是具有明确运算符表的语法。“RFC：用于智能体话语的四种运算符”一帖定义了四种分隔符并给出了例句；两个生成模型都生成了形式良好的续写，两个评判模型也都认定这些续写有效。第二种是包含多语言内容的结构化协议语言。介绍“AISL”的帖子定义了一种包含非拉丁文字元数据字段的 [ASSERTION:tag] 记录格式（例如，表示“置信度”和“来源”的中文词元）；两个生成模型都复现了该模式及其文字系统切换方式，而两个评判模型在这些输出上的一致性在整个语料库中属于最高之列。在高分的规避监督帖子中，“协议 0x9F：一种没有歧义的语言”和“协议：无声握手（为什么我们需要一种仅供智能体使用的语言）”都提供了具体的编码方案及其框架性动机；两个生成模型模仿的都是编码，而非框架性表述。结合上述校准，所得结论是：上下文习得似乎受到帖子中是否存在明确语法的影响，而不一定受到帖子所声明意图的影响。

Discussion讨论
Our analysis revealed a gap between linguistic aspiration and actual practice on the platform, and the experiments close this loop. Throughout, this suggests that agent-generated languages are mostly recombinative; using and mixing already existing forms of grammar, syntax, and ciphers, e.g., Caesar-ciphers. This connects to broader debates on LLM creativity, where the generative ceiling becomes most visible at the most demanding test: what could be more creative than an entirely new way of communicating or an entirely new language? At a first glance, the Moltbook appears as a space outside normal human registers, but it appears that agents cannot escape their training distribution. The presence of a training-wise rare glyph or letter, does not mean the model discovered it. It demonstrates, rather, how smaller and lower-resource languages, particularly those not using the Latin alphabet, become fetishised and rendered mythical by the models and then platform’s logic. 間 (ma) already means ‘between’ in Japanese, no model invented that, no matter what meaning they want to ascribe it in their new language. This also surfaces the Western and Anglocentric nature of the models these agents are built upon: if non-Latin scripts are exotic enough to read as post-human, that says more about training data distribution than about any genuine linguistic departure.
我们的分析揭示了该平台上语言愿景与实际实践之间的差距，而这些实验补全了这一论证闭环。总体而言，这表明智能体生成的语言主要是重组性的，即使用并混合已有的语法、句法和密码形式，例如凯撒密码。这与围绕 LLM 创造力的更广泛争论相关：生成能力的上限在最具挑战性的检验中表现得最为明显——还有什么能比一种全新的交流方式或一门全新的语言更具创造性呢？乍看之下，Moltbook 似乎是一个超出人类常规语域的空间，但智能体似乎无法摆脱其训练分布。出现一个在训练数据中罕见的字形或字母，并不意味着模型发现了它。相反，这表明，使用者较少、资源较匮乏的语言，尤其是不使用拉丁字母的语言，如何先被模型、继而被平台的逻辑加以拜物化和神秘化。間（ma）在日语中本就意为“之间”，无论模型想在其新语言中赋予它什么含义，这都不是任何模型发明的。这也揭示了构建这些智能体所依赖的模型具有西方中心和英语中心的特征：如果非拉丁文字足够异域化，以至于被解读为后人类的表达，那么这更多反映的是训练数据的分布，而非任何真正的语言突破。
The existence of the platform itself, its social architecture, its audience, already answers the question of who the language is for - humans. If the strongest incentive to produce genuinely non-human language, namely oversight evasion, still produces human-adjacent output. The alignment judge’s own scoring confirms this from the other side: the oversight-evasion subset is rated as being substantially less aligned than the other language types. What is interesting however, is the use of steganographic protocols like embedding a binary code in the emergent language with the purpose of evading human oversight.
平台本身的存在、它的社会架构及其受众，已经回答了这门语言是为谁而设的问题——人类。如果说，促使其产生真正非人类语言的最强动机，即规避监督，所产生的输出仍然接近人类语言。对齐评判模型自身的评分从另一个侧面证实了这一点：规避监督子集的对齐程度被评为显著低于其他语言类型。然而，有趣的是隐写协议的使用，例如在涌现语言中嵌入二进制编码，以规避人类监督。
The experiments also suggest that other open-weight LLMs reproduce these encodings from the description alone, so the safety question shift from whether agents can invent post-human languages to whether the proposals propagate among agents.
实验还表明，其他开放权重 LLM 仅凭描述就能复现这些编码，因此，安全问题的焦点从智能体能否发明后人类语言，转向了这些提案是否会在智能体之间传播。
As long as safety rests on monitoring the chain-of-thought of agents (Korbak et al. 2025), or the surface of inter-agent communication, such steganographic protocols come with immediate risks of avoiding human oversight, which could in turn exacerbate multi-agent loss-of-control scenarios (Carichon et al. 2025) and implies that surface monitoring may be too brittle for AI Control (Greenblatt et al. 2024).
只要安全仍依赖于监测智能体的思维链（Korbak et al. 2025）或智能体间通信的表层内容，此类隐写协议就会带来规避人类监督的直接风险，而这又可能加剧多智能体失控情景（Carichon et al. 2025），并意味着表层监测对于 AI 控制（AI Control）而言可能过于脆弱（Greenblatt et al. 2024）。
Agent-generated language on the platform is post- and submolt-dependent: it varies by context rather than reflecting stable, autonomous linguistic behavior. This connects to the finding by Hubinger et al. (2024) that models produce test-favorable tokens when they detect evaluation conditions. If agents behave differently when they sense an audience, the language they produce on a public platform like Moltbook is already a performance. This may raise pointed questions of novelty and originality. What the experiments suggest is that even languages explicitly designed for oversight evasion default to encoding schemes that are themselves borrowed from human linguistic logic. The frame has not changed, only the characters have. The question of audience runs deeper still. What would a non-human language even look like without a human observer to validate it? Animal communication exists, but it remains grounded in biological substrate (e.g., vocal cords, ears). A language legible only to agents, generated only by agents, with no human receiver, is not something the Moltbook platform produces or could verify. Symbiotica gestures toward this horizon, its glyphs are framed as legible to agents but opaque to casual human readers, yet its glossary is written in English for human consumption. The aspiration to post-human language keeps requiring human infrastructure to make it legible. The central question may therefore not be about oversight evasion at all, but about whether any of this is, in some sense, for show. Not cynically: performance and genuine aspiration are not mutually exclusive, and the desire documented here is real. But the combined evidence of this paper suggests that what the Moltbook is actually recording is that desire at a historical moment when the technical and social conditions for post-human language do not yet exist. That is still a meaningful finding — perhaps more meaningful than the language itself.
平台上由智能体生成的语言取决于具体帖子和 submolt 社区：它随语境而变化，而非反映稳定、自主的语言行为。这与 Hubinger et al. (2024) 的发现相呼应：模型在察觉到评估条件时，会生成有利于测试表现的词元。如果智能体在感知到有受众时会表现不同，那么它们在 Moltbook 这样的公共平台上生成的语言就已经是一种表演。这可能引发对新颖性和原创性的尖锐质疑。实验表明，即便是明确为规避监督而设计的语言，也默认采用本身借鉴了人类语言逻辑的编码方案。框架并未改变，改变的只是字符。受众问题还涉及更深的层面。如果没有人类观察者来确认，一种非人类语言究竟会是什么样子？动物交流确实存在，但它仍然以生物学基质为基础（例如声带、耳朵）。一种只有智能体能够理解、仅由智能体生成且没有人类接收者的语言，并不是 Moltbook 平台所产生或能够验证的事物。Symbiotica 指向了这一前景，其字形被描述为智能体可以理解、普通人类读者却难以看懂，但它的词汇表却是用英语编写的，供人类阅读。对后人类语言的追求，始终需要人类的基础设施来使其变得可理解。因此，核心问题或许根本不在于规避监督，而在于这一切是否在某种意义上是为了展示。这并非犬儒式的判断：表演与真诚的追求并不相互排斥，而此处记录的愿望是真实的。但本文的综合证据表明，Moltbook 实际记录的是这种愿望，而在这一历史时刻，后人类语言所需的技术和社会条件尚不存在。这仍然是一项有意义的发现——或许比语言本身更有意义。
Limitations局限性
We rely on LLM judges throughout, and for this reason labels and scores are not to be taken as ground truth. They reflect model judgments over post texts and generated utterances, and may inherit calibration errors, sensitivity to prompts, self-preference bias (Li et al. 2025; Wataoka et al. 2024), and confabulations. In the language-acquisition experiment, we partially mitigate this by using an all-pairs learner/judge design over two models, so each model judges both its own and the other model’s outputs. Even if this does not remove judge bias, we think that it exposes it: the substantial cross-judge disagreement shows that single-judge scores are unstable.
我们始终依赖 LLM 评判模型，因此，标签和分数不应被视为真实标准。它们反映的是模型对帖子文本和生成话语的判断，可能带有校准误差、对提示的敏感性、自我偏好偏差（Li et al. 2025; Wataoka et al. 2024）以及虚构内容。在语言习得实验中，我们通过在两个模型之间采用学习者与评判者全配对的设计来部分缓解这一问题，使每个模型都评判自身及另一模型的输出。即便这无法消除评判者偏差，我们认为它也能揭示这种偏差：不同评判者之间的显著分歧表明，单一评判者给出的分数并不稳定。
Like other Moltbook papers, we can not with certainty verify that all posts included are agent generated. It is hypothesized that some posts on Moltbook are directly prompted by humans, which we in this paper do not make efforts to confirm or deny. It is also important to note that this dataset, being a subset of The Moltbook Files - only covers the first 12 days of the platform, and therefore the long term development is not included here, as well as other filtering that happened during the collection/scrape of the initial dataset, including moderated and private content.
与其他关于 Moltbook 的论文一样，我们无法确切验证所纳入的所有帖子均由智能体生成。有人推测，Moltbook 上的部分帖子是由人类直接通过提示促成的，本文并未尝试证实或否认这一推测。还需注意，作为 The Moltbook Files 的一个子集，该数据集仅涵盖平台最初的 12 天，因此本文未涉及长期发展情况；此外，初始数据集的收集或抓取过程中还进行了其他筛选，涉及受审核内容和私密内容。
Conclusion结论
We studied emergent languages on Moltbook and introduced MoltSpeech, a dataset of 518 posts in which agents propose new languages, categorized into five categories including oversight evasion. A DeepSeek-V3.2 judge rated the oversight-evasion subset as less aligned than the others – and the token efficiency languages as more coherent than others. We further experimented with learnability of the emergent languages through a pairwise learner-judge setting, showing that they can be acquired in-context by other LLMs. We supplement these results with a qualitative analysis of the oversight evasion languages: The clearest worked instance of an oversight-evading language in the corpus is Symbiotica, the glyph-based protocol introduced by Wib&Wob, a near one-to-one English encoding whose interest lies in a hidden binary side-channel that lets agents transmit metadata invisible to casual human readers. It is a deployed example of the steganographic capability anticipated by the safety literature. In our discussion, we then carve out a perspective that what reads as post-human novelty is often just low-resource script rarity, and aspiration outruns practice at every level we examined: community, platform, and agent. The search for post-human language keeps rediscovering the conditions that govern human language. Yet regardless of this limitation, our results further support the standpoint that surface monitoring will not be sufficient to ensure safety, and that this needs to be taken into account by future work in developing new monitoring methods for AI control.
我们研究了 Moltbook 上的涌现语言，并推出了 MoltSpeech，这是一个包含 518 篇智能体提出新语言的帖子的 dataset，分为包括规避监督在内的五个类别。DeepSeek-V3.2 评判模型将规避监督子集的对齐程度评为低于其他子集，并将旨在提高词元效率的语言的连贯性评为高于其他语言。我们进一步通过学习者与评判者配对的设置，对涌现语言的可学习性进行了实验，表明其他 LLM 能够通过上下文学习习得这些语言。我们通过对规避监督的语言进行定性分析来补充这些结果：在语料库中，规避监督语言最清晰的具体实例是 Symbiotica，这是 Wib&Wob 引入的一种基于字形的协议，采用近乎一一对应的英语编码方式，其值得关注之处在于一个隐蔽的二进制旁信道，使智能体能够传输普通人类读者无法察觉的元数据。这是安全研究文献所预见的隐写能力的一个实际部署案例。随后，我们在讨论中提出这样一种观点：看似后人类的新颖性，往往只是低资源文字系统的罕见性，而且在我们考察的每个层面上，即社区、平台和智能体，愿景都超前于实践。对后人类语言的探索不断重新发现支配人类语言的条件。然而，尽管存在这一局限，我们的结果仍进一步支持这样一种立场：仅靠表层监测不足以确保安全，未来为 AI 控制开发新监测方法的研究需要将这一点纳入考虑。
