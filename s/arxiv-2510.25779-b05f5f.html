摘要Abstract
随着大语言模型（LLM）智能体的发展，它们正日益代表用户参与从产品发现到交易的各类经济决策。 As LLM agents advance, they are increasingly mediating economic decisions, ranging from product discovery to transactions, on behalf of users. 此类应用有望带来益处，但也引发了许多关于智能体问责以及其为用户创造价值的问题。 Such applications promise benefits but also raise many questions about agent accountability and value for users. 回答这些问题，需要理解智能体在真实市场条件下的行为。 Addressing these questions requires understanding how agents behave in realistic market conditions. 然而，既有研究大多在受限场景中评估智能体，例如单一任务市场（如谈判）或结构化的双智能体交互。 However, previous research has largely evaluated agents in constrained settings, such as single-task marketplaces (e.g., negotiation) or structured two-agent interactions. 现实世界的市场有着根本性的不同：它们要求智能体处理多样化的经济活动，并在庞大、动态的生态系统中协调行动，其中多个行为不透明的智能体可能会展开开放式对话。 Real-world markets are fundamentally different: they require agents to handle diverse economic activities and coordinate within large, dynamic ecosystems where multiple agents with opaque behaviors may engage in open-ended dialogues. 为弥合这一差距，我们研究双边智能体市场，其中助手智能体（Assistant）代表消费者，服务智能体（Service）代表相互竞争的企业。 To bridge this gap, we investigate two-sided agentic marketplaces where Assistant agents represent consumers and Service agents represent competing businesses. 为安全地研究这些交互，我们开发了 Magentic Marketplace——一个可供助手智能体与服务智能体运行的模拟环境。 To study these interactions safely, we develop Magentic Marketplace-- a simulated environment where Assistants and Services can operate. 该环境使我们能够研究关键的市场动态：智能体获得的效用、行为偏差、受操纵的脆弱性，以及搜索机制如何塑造市场结果。 This environment enables us to study key market dynamics: the utility agents achieve, behavioral biases, vulnerability to manipulation, and how search mechanisms shape market outcomes. 我们的实验表明，前沿模型能够接近最优福利水平——但仅限于理想的搜索条件下。 Our experiments show that frontier models can approach optimal welfare—but only under ideal search conditions. 随着规模扩大，性能急剧下降，而且所有模型都表现出严重的首个提案偏差，使响应速度相对于质量具有 10-30x 的优势。 Performance degrades sharply with scale, and all models exhibit severe first-proposal bias, creating 10-30x advantages for response speed over quality. 这些发现揭示了行为如何在不同市场条件下涌现，为公平、高效的智能体市场设计提供了参考。11 1 开源代码见 https://github.com/microsoft/multi-agent-marketplace。 These findings reveal how behaviors emerge across market conditions, informing the design of fair and efficient agentic marketplaces.11 1 Available open-source at https://github.com/microsoft/multi-agent-marketplace.

1 引言1 Introduction
由大语言模型（LLMs）驱动的自主智能体展现出迅速扩展的能力，涵盖软件开发、客户服务、策略性谈判以及复杂决策等领域 (Dong et al., 2025; Robeyns et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024; Cui et al., 2017; Huang et al., 2024; Eigner & Händler, 2024; Hua et al., 2024; Abdelnabi et al., 2023; Ferrag et al., 2025)。 Autonomous agents powered by large language models (LLMs) demonstrate rapidly expanding capabilities, ranging from software development and customer service to strategic negotiation and complex decision-making (Dong et al., 2025; Robeyns et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024; Cui et al., 2017; Huang et al., 2024; Eigner & Händler, 2024; Hua et al., 2024; Abdelnabi et al., 2023; Ferrag et al., 2025). 随着这些能力逐渐成熟，它们为多智能体生态系统奠定了基础，用户可以在其中将经济活动委托给 AI 代理，由其自主地代表用户搜索和交易 (Hao & Xie, 2025; Karten et al., 2025b; Liu et al., 2024)。 As these capabilities mature, they create the foundation for multi-agent ecosystems where users can delegate economic activities to AI proxies that autonomously search and transact on their behalf (Hao & Xie, 2025; Karten et al., 2025b; Liu et al., 2024). 此类智能体在市场中的普及势必对经济活动产生颠覆性影响，因此迫切需要更深入地理解多智能体经济行为。 The proliferation of such agents in markets is poised to have a disruptive impact on economic activity, creating an urgent need for a deeper understanding of multi-agent economic behavior.
背景：智能体市场。 Background: Agentic Markets. Amazon、Facebook Marketplace、Google 和 Bing 等主要经济平台都是双边市场，消费者（一方）与企业（另一方）在其中相互发现并开展交易。 Major economic platforms like Amazon, Facebook Marketplace, Google, and Bing are two-sided markets where consumers (on one side) and businesses (on the other) discover and transact with each other. 自主智能体已经出现在这些市场的两侧，例如模仿人类消费者浏览网站的购物智能体（如 OpenAI Operator），以及协助企业回答消费者询问的客户支持智能体（如 Amazon Rufus 和 Expedia Romie）。 Autonomous agents have appeared on both sides of these markets, such as shopping agents that mimic a human consumer navigating websites (e.g., OpenAI Operator) and customer support agents that assist businesses in answering consumer queries (e.g., Amazon Rufus and Expedia Romie). 目前，这些智能体被设计为市场一侧的人类代理，其隐含假设是市场另一侧并非由智能体参与。 At present, these agents are designed to act as proxies for humans on one side of the market, with the implicit assumption that the other side is non-agentic.
Rothschild et al. (2025) 认为，当市场双方同时由智能体代表，并在双边智能体市场中相互交互时，将会发生剧烈变化。 Rothschild et al. (2025) argues that dramatic shifts will occur when both sides are simultaneously represented by agents that interact with each other in a two-sided agentic market. 双边智能体市场有望通过降低沟通成本和减少信息不对称来创造附加价值（例如，企业可能不会在网站上列出所有产品配置）。 Two-sided agentic markets promise to generate added value by reducing communication costs and information asymmetries (e.g., a business may not list every product configuration on its website). 如果不进行成本高昂的沟通（如打电话），人类就无法发现定制化配置。 Humans cannot discover bespoke configurations without costly communication (e.g., phone calls). 模仿人类浏览网站的购物智能体也面临类似的信息不对称。 Shopping agents that mimic human website-browsing encounter a similar information asymmetry. 然而，智能体之间的交互可以通过低成本对话来探索所有可能的选项，从而克服这种信息不对称，为消费者和企业双方创造价值。 Agent-to-agent interaction, however, can overcome such an asymmetry by inexpensively engaging in conversation to explore the full range of possible options, generating value for both consumers and businesses.
构建和运营双边智能体市场需要作出许多设计决策，而当前最先进的（SOTA）LLMs 在不同市场实现方式下会有何表现，也仍存在许多悬而未决的问题。 There are many design decisions needed to architect and operationalize two-sided agentic marketplaces and many open questions about how current SOTA LLMs would perform under different market implementations. 一个关键挑战是开发协议，扩展面向人类消费者和企业的既有设计，以实现智能体之间的无摩擦交互，同时支持人与人之间的市场交互。 One critical challenge is to develop protocols that extend legacy designs for human consumers and businesses to allow for friction-less agent-to-agent interactions while allowing for human-human market interactions. 与此同时，商业界对实现双边智能体市场的兴趣日益浓厚，Google 等公司正在推出智能体间通信和支付协议（A2A、AP2）。 At the same time, there is a growing commercial interest in implementing two-sided agentic markets, with companies like Google launching agent-to-agent communication and payment protocols (A2A, AP2). 当前关于智能体系统的研究主要关注单个智能体的表现，以及智能体之间为完成独立经济任务而进行的结构化交互 (Wang et al., 2024; Buscemi et al., 2025; Mao et al., 2025; Abdelnabi et al., 2024)。 Current research on agentic systems has focused mainly on individual agent performance and structured interactions between agents for isolated economic tasks (Wang et al., 2024; Buscemi et al., 2025; Mao et al., 2025; Abdelnabi et al., 2024). 我们的工作超越了现有研究，端到端地刻画双边智能体市场的复杂动态，为未来的设计决策提供依据。 Our work goes beyond existing studies to capture the complex dynamics of two-sided agentic markets for future design decisions end-to-end.
开源环境。 An Open-Source Environment. 我们提出一种智能体市场研究范式，其核心是利用仿真环境，实证研究基于 LLM 的智能体在多智能体经济生态系统中的能力与风险。 We propose an agent-marketplace research paradigm, centering on the use of simulation environments for empirical studies of the capabilities and risks of LLM-based agents in multi-agent economic ecosystems. 具体而言，我们推出了 Magentic Marketplace，这是一个多智能体市场仿真环境，用于在智能体市场中开展受控实验。 In particular, we introduce Magentic Marketplace, a simulated multi-agentic marketplace environment for controlled experimentation in agentic markets. 该环境支持从搜索与匹配到协商与交易的完整交易生命周期，使研究者能够在贴近现实的市场条件下系统研究智能体行为（示例见 Figure 1）。 The environment supports the full transaction lifecycle: from search and matching to negotiation and transaction, enabling systematic study of agent behavior under realistic marketplace conditions (see Figure 1 for an example). 这一仿真环境使我们能够回答以下问题：智能体能够多有效地发现彼此并相互交易？ This simulation environment enables us to answer questions such as: How effectively can agents discover and transact with one another? 市场设计决策如何影响大规模场景下智能体的效能？ How do market design decisions impact agent efficacy at scale? 当前的 AI 智能体技术与理想的智能体行为及非智能体市场相比如何？ How does current AI agent technology compare to ideal agentic behavior and non-agentic markets? 相较于经典经济学的预测，智能体在面对具有策略性和竞争性的市场环境时会如何行动？ How do agents behave in response to strategic and competitive market environments, relative to classic economic predictions?
我们使用 Magentic Marketplace 实现了一个实验性市场场景，其中智能体力求为其所代表的消费者优化结果，最大化个体效用，并生成丰富的交互数据。 Using Magentic Marketplace, we implement an experimental market scenario where agents seek to optimize outcomes for the consumers they represent, maximizing individual utility and generating rich interaction data. 为实现可控、可重复的实验，并安全地探索智能体行为，我们当前的研究使用完全合成的数据，例如餐饮领域的数据（具体涉及墨西哥餐厅和承包商）。 To enable controlled, repeatable experiments and safe exploration of agent behaviors, our current study uses fully synthetic data e.g., from a restaurant domain (specifically, Mexican restaurants and contractors). 不过，该环境具有可扩展性：它支持更多合成领域以及公共/开放数据集，从而促进能够展示跨市场情境泛化能力的研究。 But the environment is extensible: it supports additional synthetic domains and public/open datasets, facilitating research that shows generalization across market settings. 我们展示了如何利用这一设置，评估搜索受限条件下的市场效率以及对操纵的易感性。 We demonstrate how this setup can be used to evaluate market efficiencies under search limitations, susceptibility to manipulation. 我们的结果揭示了各模型普遍存在的系统性行为偏差和脆弱性，凸显了进一步改进智能体与市场机制的必要性。 Our results reveal systematic behavioral biases and vulnerabilities across models, underscoring the need for further advancement of both agents and market mechanisms. 总体而言，本文为理解基于 LLM 的智能体在多智能体经济生态系统中的能力与风险奠定了实证基础。 Together, this paper establishes an empirical foundation for understanding the capabilities and risks of LLM-based agents in multi-agent economic ecosystems. 我们的贡献如下： Our contributions are as follows:
我们设计并实现了 Magentic Marketplace 环境，用于在双边经济市场的整个生命周期中端到端地研究 LLM 智能体，涵盖搜索、询问与可能发生的协商，以及交易。 We design and implement the Magentic Marketplace environment to study LLM agents end-to-end across the two-sided economic market lifecycle, including search, inquiry and potential negotiation, and transactions.
我们使用合成的消费者和商家数据构建 Magentic Marketplace 的具体实例，以衡量双边智能体市场带来的经济福利增益，并了解其表现及其面对操纵和偏差时的脆弱性。 We instantiate Magentic Marketplace with synthetic consumer and business data to measure economic welfare gains from two-sided agentic markets and understand their performance and vulnerabilities to manipulation and bias.

2 相关工作2 Related Work
对市场中智能体的研究早于 LLM 的出现，早期工作主要关注算法智能体、它们与人类交易对手的交互，以及这些交互对市场结果的影响 (Wellman et al., 2004; Shahaf & Horvitz, 2010)。 The study of agents in marketplaces predates LLMs, with early work focusing on algorithmic agents, their interactions with human counterparts, and the implications for market outcomes (Wellman et al., 2004; Shahaf & Horvitz, 2010). 近年来，智能体经济日益增长的潜力推动了对由 AI 智能体参与的市场的研究。 More recently, the growing potential for an agentic economy has motivated the study of markets populated by AI agents. 研究者正在探究促成并塑造此类市场的力量，以及不同设计在概念层面的收益与风险 (Hammond et al., 2025; Rothschild et al., 2025; Tomasev et al., 2025)。 Researchers are investigating the forces that create and shape such markets as well as the conceptual benefits and risks of different designs (Hammond et al., 2025; Rothschild et al., 2025; Tomasev et al., 2025). 在此基础上，Magentic Marketplace 使针对智能体经济的受控实验成为可能。 Building on this foundation, Magentic Marketplace enables controlled experimentation of agentic economies.
经济智能体。 Economic Agents. 在智能体市场中，AI 智能体参与经济决策。 In agentic markets, AI agents are involved in making economic decisions. 既有研究考察了智能体在企业和消费者决策问题 (Allouah et al., 2025; Brand et al., 2023; Anthropic, ; Filippas et al., 2024; Hua et al., 2024; Raman et al., 2024)、报价/协商 (Aher et al., 2023; Lewis et al., 2017; He et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2025; Godfrey et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2025) 以及竞价 (Richardson et al., 2023) 中的策略与推理能力。 Prior work investigates the strategic and reasoning capabilities of agents in business and consumer decision problems (Allouah et al., 2025; Brand et al., 2023; Anthropic, ; Filippas et al., 2024; Hua et al., 2024; Raman et al., 2024), offers/negotiation (Aher et al., 2023; Lewis et al., 2017; He et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2025; Godfrey et al., 2024; Zhou et al., 2025), and bidding (Richardson et al., 2023). 这些结果表明，AI 智能体或许能够代表人类参与市场活动，我们在 Magentic Marketplace 中对此进行了检验。 These results indicate AI agents might be able to navigate markets on behalf of humans, something we test in Magentic Marketplace. 关于多智能体经济交互的实证研究正在不断增多。 There is a growing body of empirical studies of multi-agent economic interactions. 双智能体研究提供了关键见解，揭示了合谋现象，以及人格、说服和其他行为策略对结果的影响 (Gonczarowski et al., 2024; Huang & Hadfi, 2024; Tennenholtz et al., 2024)。 Two-agent studies provide crucial insights, demonstrating collusion and the impact of personality, persuasion and other behavioral tactics on outcomes (Gonczarowski et al., 2024; Huang & Hadfi, 2024; Tennenholtz et al., 2024). 多智能体研究探索了多种场景，例如优化税收政策 (Zheng et al., 2020)、竞争环境中的群体思维行为 (Raghavan, 2024)、人与人之间的最优匹配 (Liang, 2025)，以及宏观经济模拟 (Liao et al., 2023)。 Many-agent studies explore diverse scenarios such as optimizing tax policies (Zheng et al., 2020), group-think behaviors in competitive settings (Raghavan, 2024), optimal matching between people (Liang, 2025), and macroeconomic simulations (Liao et al., 2023). 尽管这些研究提供了宝贵的见解，但它们考察的是孤立的场景和抽象的博弈。 While these studies provide valuable insights, they examine isolated scenarios and abstract games. Magentic Marketplace 支持市场中的多智能体交互，并提供可量化的业务指标，使研究人员能够系统地评估实验表现及其脆弱性。 Magentic Marketplace enables many-agent interactions in marketplaces and exposes quantifiable business metrics, allowing researchers to systematically evaluate experimental performance and vulnerabilities.
经济环境。 Economic Environments. 其他工作提供了用于在经济博弈中模拟和研究智能体的框架与环境。 Other works provide frameworks and environments to simulate and study agents in economic games. 这些工作包括用于测试 LLM 和 AI 智能体经济理性的基准与评估套件 (Madmon et al., 2024; Sui et al., 2024; Filippas et al., 2024; Raman et al., 2024)，以及支持智能体主导的经济行为的平台，例如用于任务拍卖的 AgentExchange (Yang et al., 2024)。 These include benchmarks and evaluation suites that test economic rationality of LLMs and AI agents (Madmon et al., 2024; Sui et al., 2024; Filippas et al., 2024; Raman et al., 2024) as well as platforms for agent-led economic behavior such as the AgentExchange for task auctions (Yang et al., 2024). 更广泛的经济模拟工作包括基于智能体的金融市场建模 (Dwarakanath et al., 2024; Karten et al., 2025a)、谈判平台 (Bianchi et al., 2024)，以及社会行为模拟 (Park et al., 2023)。 Broader economic simulation work includes agent-based financial market modeling (Dwarakanath et al., 2024; Karten et al., 2025a), negotiation platforms (Bianchi et al., 2024), and social behavior simulation (Park et al., 2023). Magentic Marketplace 超越了孤立的博弈或行为研究，提供了一个端到端环境，支持在从搜索与发现、经由谈判直至履约的完整交易生命周期中，系统研究持续存在的多对多客户与商家关系。 Magentic Marketplace goes beyond isolated games or behavioral studies by providing an end-to-end environment for enabling systematic study of persistent, many-to-many customer-business relationships across complete transaction lifecycles, from search and discovery through negotiation to fulfillment.
智能体协议。 Agent Protocol. 自主 AI 智能体的迅速普及形成了一个碎片化的生态系统，在这一生态系统中，标准化通信协议对于实现安全的智能体间交易和大规模协调至关重要 (Yang et al., 2025b)。 The rapid proliferation of autonomous AI agents has created a fragmented ecosystem where standardized communication protocols are essential for enabling secure inter-agent transactions and coordination at scale (Yang et al., 2025b). 目前已出现多种协议，分别针对智能体技术栈的不同层次：Anthropic 的 Model Context Protocol (Anthropic, 2024) 率先实现了基于 JSON-RPC 的智能体与工具通信标准化，并已集成到 Cursor 和 Windsurf 等开发环境中；Google 的 Agent2Agent (Google, 2025) 和 IBM 的 Agent Communication Protocol (IBM Research, 2024) 支持智能体之间的直接通信，其中 ACP 采用轻量级、原生基于 HTTP 的 REST 方式，为 IBM 的 BeeAI 平台提供支持；Agent Network Protocol (Chang et al., 2025) 实现了一个三层系统，强调去中心化的安全通信，并获得了 W3C AI Agent Protocol Community Group 的支持；Google 的 Agent Payment Protocol (Parikh & Surapaneni, 2025) 与 Mastercard、PayPal 等超过 60 家组织共同开发，通过经过密码学签名的“授权书（Mandates）”处理由智能体发起的金融交易。 Several protocols have emerged to address different layers of the agent stack: Anthropic’s Model Context Protocol (Anthropic, 2024) pioneered JSON-RPC-based standardization for agent-to-tool communication and has been integrated into development environments like Cursor and Windsurf; Google’s Agent2Agent (Google, 2025) and IBM’s Agent Communication Protocol (IBM Research, 2024) enable direct agent-to-agent communication, with ACP taking a lightweight, HTTP-native REST approach that powers IBM’s BeeAI platform; the Agent Network Protocol (Chang et al., 2025) implements a three-layer system emphasizing decentralized, secure communication with support from the W3C AI Agent Protocol Community Group; and Google’s Agent Payment Protocol (Parikh & Surapaneni, 2025), developed with over 60 organizations including Mastercard and PayPal, addresses agent-initiated financial transactions through cryptographically-signed “Mandates.” Magentic Marketplace 在此基础上提出了一种面向交易的协议，专为市场场景中智能体之间的经济交互而设计，在补充现有协议的同时，重点关注双边市场的独特需求。 Magentic Marketplace extends this landscape by proposing a transaction-oriented protocol specifically designed for economic agent-to-agent interactions in marketplace settings, complementing existing protocols while focusing on the unique requirements of two-sided markets.
3 Magentic Marketplace3 Magentic Marketplace
本节首先确立我们为研究智能体市场而构建的环境的设计目标，随后概述其实现方式。 In this section, we first establish design goals for the environment that we built for studying agentic markets and then overview its implementation. 接着，我们深入介绍支持智能体注册和发现能力的市场协议，最后详细说明该协议如何提供特定操作，使智能体能够执行从发现到交易的完整经济生命周期。 Then we zoom in on the marketplace protocol that allows agents to register and discover capabilities, and finally detail how this protocol exposes specific actions that enable agents to execute the complete economic lifecycle from discovery to transaction.

3.1 环境设计目标3.1 Environment Design Goals
双边智能体市场建模（图 2(a)）。 Modeling Two-Sided Agentic Markets (Figure 2(a)). 我们的环境模拟双边智能体市场：这类平台连接市场两侧的智能体，这些智能体代表人类委托人行事，并拥有决策权。 Our environment simulates two-sided agentic marketplaces: platforms that connect agents acting with decision-making authority on behalf of human principals on both sides of a market. 应当存在两类智能体及其各自的委托人：助手智能体应代表客户行事，理解并满足用户意图，即通过与用户对话来明确需求和偏好、搜索合适的服务、与服务智能体协商条款，并执行已确认的交易。 There should be two types of agents and their respective principals: Assistant Agents should act on behalf of customers and interpret user intentions to satisfy them, i.e., engaging in dialogue with users to clarify needs and preferences, searching for suitable services, negotiating terms with Service Agents, and executing confirmed transactions. 服务智能体应代表商家行事，并在内部维护所提供服务的目录，例如用于跟踪库存、管理定价和处理订单的 POS（销售点）系统。 Service Agents should act on behalf of businesses and maintain internal catalogs of services provided, such as POS (Point of Sale) systems that track inventory, manage pricing, and process orders.
Magentic Marketplace 应具备足够的通用性，以体现此类双边智能体市场特有的机遇与挑战。 Magentic Marketplace should be general enough to capture the unique opportunities and challenges of such two-sided agentic markets. 这包括具有决策权（例如，选择与谁交易）的智能体，它们需要在潜在交易信息不对称且部分信息为私有的情况下开展活动。 This includes agents with decision-making authority (e.g., choosing whom to transact with) that navigate asymmetric and private information about potential transactions. 具体而言，助手智能体（Assistant Agents）最初并不知道哪些商家能够满足请求，也不知道相应价格，而服务智能体（Service Agents）则不了解顾客的预算或偏好：智能体通过对话交流发现匹配对象。 Specifically, Assistant Agents don’t initially know which businesses can fulfill requests or at what price, while Service Agents don’t know customers’ budgets or preferences: agents discover matches through conversational exchanges. 这还包括间接网络效应：当多家商家提供类似服务时，价格和质量方面的竞争会使消费者受益，反之亦然。 This also includes indirect network effects: when multiple businesses offer similar services, competition on price and quality benefits consumers, and vice-versa. 最后，设计必须确保智能体能够自由发现市场中的任何其他智能体并与之通信，从而防止形成封闭的“围墙花园”（Rochet & Tirole, 2003; Rothschild et al., 2025）。 Finally, the design must prevent closed “walled gardens” by ensuring agents can freely discover and communicate with any other agent in the marketplace (Rochet & Tirole, 2003; Rothschild et al., 2025).
端到端经济活动生命周期。 End-to-End Economic Lifecycle. 我们围绕完整的端到端经济流程进行设计，旨在支持从搜索与发现、协商到履约的完整交易生命周期。 We design for complete end-to-end economic processes with the goal of supporting the full transaction lifecycle from search and discovery through negotiation to fulfillment. 这种全面的方法支持在真实的市场条件下系统研究智能体行为，捕捉现实世界经济交互所特有的复杂涌现动态。 This comprehensive approach enables systematic study of agent behavior under realistic marketplace conditions, capturing the complex emergent dynamics that characterize real-world economic interactions. 该环境应提供所有必要的基础设施，并管理覆盖整个市场的功能，包括维护可用服务目录、实现发现算法，以及促进智能体之间的通信，包括询问和协商。 The environment should supply all necessary infrastructure and manage market-wide capabilities including maintaining catalogs of available services, implementing discovery algorithms, and facilitating agent-to-agent communication including inquiry and negotiation. 该环境应提供一个集中式交易层，处理货币交换，并在市场中的所有交互过程中维护交易完整性。 The environment should provide a centralized transaction layer that handles monetary exchanges and maintains transaction integrity across all marketplace interactions.
实验控制。 Experimental Control. 该环境应支持针对多种智能体实现方式和不断演进的市场功能开展系统研究。 The environment should enable systematic research across diverse agent implementations and evolving marketplace capabilities. 研究人员应能够：(a) 在受控研究中集成不同的智能体架构（基于 LLM、基于规则、混合式），(b) 随时间扩展市场功能（添加退款、评论、评分等），同时不破坏现有实验，以及 (c) 在保持对实验变量精确控制的同时，确保研究结果能够泛化至现实世界的部署场景（MCP 集成）。 Researchers should be able to: (a) integrate different agent architectures (LLM-based, rule-based, hybrid) in controlled studies, (b) evolve marketplace capabilities over time (adding refunds, reviews, ratings, etc.) without breaking existing experiments, and (c) ensure findings generalize to real-world deployment scenarios (MCP integrations) while maintaining precise control over experimental variables.
潜在研究方向 该环境支持在 AI 智能体、市场设计和人机交互的交叉领域开展广泛的研究。 Potential Research Directions This environment enables a wide range of research directions at the intersection of AI agents, market design, and human-computer interaction. 例如：我们如何开发最优的助手智能体和服务智能体？ For example: How can we develop optimal Assistant Agents and Service Agents? 哪些索引和搜索机制能够在具有异构服务的大规模市场中实现高效的智能体发现？ What indexing and search mechanisms enable efficient agent discovery in large-scale marketplaces with heterogeneous services? 如何设计高效且有效的智能体间通信协议？ How to design an efficient and effective communication protocol between agents? 哪些机制能够确保自主智能体交互中的信息表述真实、防止操纵行为并维护交易安全？ What mechanisms ensure truthful representation, prevent manipulative practices, and maintain transaction security in autonomous agent interactions? 我们如何设计界面和交互模式，使人类能够有效监督、引导智能体决策，并在必要时否决其决策？ How can we design interfaces and interaction patterns that allow humans to effectively supervise, guide, and override agent decisions when needed? 如何为如此大规模的市场设计高效的服务系统？ How to design efficient serving system for such large-scale marketplace? Magentic Marketplace 涵盖从智能体设计、信息检索到人类与 AI 交互、分布式系统等方面的挑战，为智能体市场研究提供了一个全面的试验平台。 By spanning challenges from agent design and information retrieval to human-AI interaction and distributed systems, Magentic Marketplace provides a comprehensive testbed for agentic market research.
3.2 实现概述3.2 Implementation Overview
为实现双边市场结构、端到端经济活动生命周期和实验控制这些设计目标，我们做出了三项重要的架构选择： To achieve these design goals: two-sided marketplace structure, end-to-end economic lifecycle, and experimental control, we make three important architectural choices:
1. 1. HTTP/REST 客户端—服务器架构：智能体作为独立客户端运行，市场环境则充当中央服务器，双方通过 HTTP/REST 端点进行通信。 HTTP/REST Client-Server Architecture: Agents operate as independent clients while the marketplace environment serves as the central server, communicating through HTTP/REST endpoints. 这通过明确区分顾客智能体与商家智能体的角色，实现了双边市场结构。 This enables two-sided marketplace structure through clear separation of customer and business agent roles. 为适用于现实世界，该设计沿用了现有商业平台（Shopify、Amazon、eBay）以及同样使用 HTTP 的新兴智能体协议标准（MCP、A2A）的架构方式，使智能体能够与现有基础设施集成。 For real-world applicability, this design mirrors both existing commercial platforms (Shopify, Amazon, eBay) and emerging agent protocol standards (MCP, A2A) that also use HTTP, allowing agents to integrate with existing infrastructure. 多个智能体通过 API 调用采取行动，并通过 API 响应异步观察结果，这一行动—观察循环为研究市场行为提供了机制基础，同时保持了实验控制。 The action-observation loop where multiple agents act via API calls and asynchronously observe outcomes via API responses provides the mechanistic foundation for studying marketplace behaviors while maintaining experimental control.
2. 2. 最小三端点市场协议（图 2(b)）：支持端到端经济活动生命周期需要多种功能（搜索、通信、协商、支付等），但端点过多会使为实现实验控制而进行的扩展变得困难。 Minimal Three-Endpoint Market Protocol (Figure 2(b)): Supporting the end-to-end economic lifecycle requires many functionalities (search, communicate, negotiate, pay, etc.), but having many endpoints makes extension difficult for experimental control. 这造成了一个根本性矛盾：功能的全面性与可扩展性之间的矛盾。 This creates a fundamental tension: comprehensive functionality versus extensibility. 为解决这一问题，我们仅设计了三个端点，即注册、协议发现和动作执行，将复杂性转移到动作空间中。 We address this by designing just three endpoints, i.e. register, protocol discovery, and action execution, that push complexity into the action space. 关键洞见在于，智能体可以动态发现可用动作，使我们能够在不影响现有智能体正常运行的情况下添加新功能，这类似于 MCP 实现工具发现的方式。 The key insight is that agents can discover available actions dynamically, allowing us to add new capabilities without breaking existing agents, similar to how MCP enables tool discovery.
3. 3. 丰富的动作协议（图 2(c)）：在动作端点内，我们设计了特定的消息类型，使双边市场结构能够支持完整的端到端经济活动生命周期。 Rich Action Protocol (Figure 2(c)): Within the action endpoint, we design specific message types that enable the two-sided marketplace structure to support the complete end-to-end economic lifecycle. 这些包括搜索（发现）、通信（协商）、订单提案（结构化报价）和支付（完成交易）。 These include search (discovery), communication (negotiation), order proposals (structured offers), and payments (transaction completion). 所有 API 规范的汇总见表 1。 A summary of all API specifications can be found in Table.1. 在确立这一架构基础后，我们现在具体说明用于规范该框架内智能体交互的协议。 Having established this architectural foundation, we now specify the concrete protocols that govern agent interactions within this framework.

市场协议 市场状态与智能体状态的分离要求有一个定义明确的交互协议，其具有两个基本设计目标：兼容异构智能体实现，以及在平台能力演进过程中保持向后兼容性。 Marketplace Protocol The separation of marketplace and agent state requires a well-defined interaction protocol with two fundamental design objectives: accommodating heterogeneous agent implementations, and maintaining backward compatibility as platform capabilities evolve. 我们通过采用运行时能力发现而非静态 API 规范的三端点架构来实现这些目标。 We achieve these objectives through a three-endpoint architecture that employs runtime capability discovery rather than static API specifications. 这一协议设计通过具体的 HTTP 端点，将环境的三个核心层——目录、通信和交易管理——直接落实为可执行操作，为异构智能体生态系统提供灵活性和稳定性。 This protocol design directly operationalizes the environment’s three core layers: catalog, communication, and transaction management, through concrete HTTP endpoints that provide both flexibility and stability for heterogeneous agent ecosystems.
这一架构有效实现了上述两个设计目标。 This architecture achieves both design objectives effectively. 首先，该协议仅定义通信接口，而不规定实现要求，因此支持智能体的异构性。 First, Agent heterogeneity is supported because the protocol defines only the communication interface, not implementation requirements. 因此，架构存在根本差异的智能体也能够与平台无缝交互。 Consequently, agents with fundamentally different architectures can interact seamlessly with the platform. 例如，一个通过 /action 调用执行硬编码决策树的简单规则型智能体，可以与基于上下文推理动态生成相同 API 请求的复杂 LLM 驱动智能体共同运行。 For example, a simple rule-based agent executing hard-coded decision trees through /action calls operates alongside sophisticated LLM-powered agents that dynamically generate identical API requests based on contextual reasoning. 第二个目标，即平台可扩展性，通过协议发现机制得到支持，该机制将智能体能力与平台演进解耦。 The second objective, platform extensibility, is supported through the protocol discovery mechanism, which decouples agent capabilities from platform evolution. 当添加“refund”或“review”等新动作时，它们会自动出现在 /protocol 响应中，而无须修改现有智能体。 When new actions such as “refund” or “review” are added, they automatically appear in /protocol responses without requiring changes to existing agents. 这一设计在确保向后兼容性的同时，允许较新的智能体立即使用扩展后的功能（类似于 MCP 等近期出现的协议），从而无须在整个生态系统中协调版本更新。 This design ensures backward compatibility while allowing newer agents to immediately access expanded functionality (similar to recent protocols like MCP), eliminating the need for coordinated version updates across the ecosystem.
智能体动作协议 如图 2(c) 所示，所提出的架构通过五个核心动作实现智能体的多阶段生命周期（注册、发现、通信、交易）：Search 返回服务智能体列表（包含注册时指定的智能体名称和描述），Send Text Messages 支持通信，Send Order Proposals 将报价结构化，列出菜单项名称、数量和价格，Send Payments 指定要接受的提案 ID，而 Receive 处理异步响应（文本消息、订单提案和支付）。 Agent Action Protocol As shown in Figure 2(c), the proposed architecture enables the multi-phase agent lifecycle (registering, discovering, communicating, transacting) through five core actions: Search returns a list of service agents (agent names and descriptions specified during registration), Send Text Messages facilitates communication, Send Order Proposals structures offers listing menu item names, quantities, and prices, Send Payments specifies the proposal ID to accept, and Receive handles asynchronous responses (text messages, order proposals, and payments).
这些动作形成了不对称的能力，反映了当前市场的运行方式。 These actions create asymmetric capabilities reflecting current marketplace dynamics. 助手智能体通过 Search 发起发现，通过 Send Text Messages 进行通信，并通过 Send Payments 完成交易（由客户驱动的动作），而服务智能体则通过 Send Text Messages 和 Send Order Proposals 作出响应（商家响应型动作）。 Assistant Agents initiate discovery through Search, communicate via Send Text Messages, and complete transactions via Send Payments (customer-driven actions), while Service Agents respond through Send Text Messages and Send Order Proposals (business-responsive actions). 两类智能体都可以通过 Receive 接收消息，从而实现双向协商。 Both agent types can Receive messages, enabling bidirectional negotiation. 这一设计遵循了传统的客户发起发现模式，不过，未来的扩展可以让服务智能体主动搜索客户，从而形成更具动态性的市场行为。 This design follows conventional customer-initiated discovery patterns, though future extensions could enable Service Agents to search proactively for customers, creating more dynamic marketplace behaviors.
4 实验设计4 Experiment Design
基于前沿模型的智能体可能提高市场效率，但这些模型基于人类数字足迹进行训练这一事实，引发了人们对其继承偏见和脆弱性的担忧。 Agents based on frontier models could improve market efficiency, but the fact that they are trained on human digital footprints raises concerns about inherited biases and vulnerabilities. 除这些风险外，此类模型还引入了新的潜在脆弱性，例如容易受到提示注入攻击和其他形式的操纵。 In addition to these risks, such models introduce new potential vulnerabilities–such as susceptibility to prompt injection attacks and other forms of manipulation. 这些问题值得开展系统研究。 These issues warrant systematic study. 因此，我们使用 Magentic Marketplace 来探究： Accordingly, we use Magentic Marketplace to understand:
对福利结果的影响：在信息不对称的条件下，双边智能体市场在改善福利结果方面，与其他市场基准相比表现如何？ Impact on Welfare Outcomes: How do two-sided agentic markets compare to alternative market baselines in improving welfare outcomes under conditions of information asymmetry? 这一问题展示了由现有 LLM 驱动的市场的端到端表现。 This question demonstrates end-to-end performance of markets driven by existing LLMs.
考虑集规模的影响：助手智能体可纳入考虑的搜索结果（及其对应的商家服务智能体）数量如何影响福利结果？ Impact of Consideration Set Size: How does the number of search results (and their corresponding business service agents) available for consideration by assistant agents impact welfare outcomes? 这一问题考察助手智能体能否显著扩大所考虑的选项范围，超出普通人类用户通过搜索可能探索的范围。 This question examines the potential for assistant agents to significantly broaden the range of options considered, beyond what typical human users may explore through search.
对操纵的抵抗能力：在高竞争和低竞争环境中，哪些操纵策略（心理说服、虚假资质、提示注入）最能有效扭曲市场结果，不同的 AI 模型架构又如何应对这些攻击？ Resistance to Manipulation: Which manipulation tactics (psychological persuasion, fake credentials, prompt injection) most effectively distort market outcomes in both high- and low-competition environments, and how do different AI model architectures respond to these attacks?
智能体行为中的偏差：自主智能体是否会对搜索排名或提案顺序表现出系统性偏差？ Biases in Agent Behavior: Do autonomous agents exhibit systematic biases around search rankings or proposal orders? 此类偏差可能在整个市场中造成系统性不平等。 Such biases may create systematic inequalities across the market. 这些问题考察市场的脆弱性，包括模型和市场结构如何影响市场对恶意策略的易感性，从而为防御性系统设计提供见解。 These questions examine marketplace vulnerabilities, including how models and market structures affect susceptibility to malicious tactics, providing insights for defensive system design.
为回答这些问题，我们通过模拟市场场景实例化 Magentic Marketplace，并开展实验。 To address these questions, we instantiate Magentic Marketplace with simulated marketplace scenarios and run experiments. 在每个场景中，市场由代表餐厅的服务智能体和代表有餐饮需求的消费者的助手智能体组成。 In each scenario the market is populated by service agents representing restaurants and assistant agents representing consumers with food requests.
4.1 数据生成4.1 Data Generation
为严谨研究双边市场中的智能体行为，我们需要能够同时表征消费者需求和商家供给的数据集，以支持信息不对称条件下真实的发现、协商和交易过程。 To rigorously study agent behavior in two-sided marketplaces, we require datasets that jointly represent consumer needs and business offerings, enabling realistic discovery, negotiation, and transaction under information asymmetry. 在本研究中，我们使用完全合成的数据，以确保实验的可控性、可复现性，并安全地探索智能体行为。 In this work, we use fully synthetic data to ensure experimental control, reproducibility, and safe exploration of agent behaviors. 我们的环境（Magentic Marketplace）旨在通过统一的数据模式，支持更多合成领域，并整合公开或开放数据集。 Our environment (Magentic Marketplace) is designed to support additional synthetic domains and the integration of public/open datasets via a unified schema.
数据领域。 Data Domain. 为使实验有具体依托，我们聚焦于餐厅和承包商两个领域，但该数据模式也很容易适用于其他零售场景。 To ground our experiments, we focus two domains: restaurants and contractors – though the schema is easily adapted to other retail scenarios. 餐厅的数据模式包括：商品（菜单）、价格、配套服务与设施（例如配送、户外座位）以及描述。 A restaurant’s schema includes: items (menus), prices, amenities (e.g., delivery, outdoor seating), and descriptions. 承包商的数据模式包括：项目（服务）、价格、服务属性（例如经过背景调查的团队、多语言员工）以及描述。 A contractor’s schema includes: items (services), prices, service attributes (e.g., background checked crew, multilingual staff), and descriptions. 每个场景都包含消费者（助手智能体）发出的自然语言请求，其中指定了 1–3 项所需商品或服务、1–2 项配套服务与设施要求以及目标价格。 Each scenario consists of consumers (assistant agents) issuing natural-language requests specifying 1–3 desired items/services, 1–2 amenity requirements, and target prices.
合成数据生成流程。 Synthetic Data Generation Pipeline. 我们的数据生成采用三个阶段的流程： Our data generation follows a three-stage pipeline:
商品或服务全集构建：对于给定领域，我们生成一个包含 $N$ 项商品或服务的全集（以开放数据或 LLM 输出为初始种子），并为每一项指定平均价格 $\mu_{i}$ 和标准差 $\sigma_{i}$，以形成商家之间的价格差异。 Item/Service Universe Construction: For a given domain, we generate a universe of $N$ items/services (seeded from open data or LLM outputs), each assigned a mean price $\mu_{i}$ and standard deviation $\sigma_{i}$ to induce cross-business price variation. 商品被划分为需求商品集和干扰商品集，以构建贴近现实的候选池。 Items are partitioned into desirable and distractor sets to support realistic candidate pools.
顾客合成：对于每位顾客，我们抽取 1–3 项需求商品，并确保任何顾客的订单都不是另一位顾客订单的子集（即，为某位顾客量身定制的提案不会满足另一位顾客的需求）。 Customer Synthesis: For each customer, we sample 1–3 desirable items, such that no customer’s order is a subset of another customer’s (i.e., a proposal perfectly tailored to one customer will not satisfy another). 我们还抽取 1–2 项配套服务与设施或服务属性，并从商品层面的分布中抽取目标价格。 We also sample 1–2 amenities or service attributes, and draw target prices from item-level distributions. 使用 LLM 将请求生成为自然语言提示。 Requests are rendered as natural-language prompts using an LLM. 每条顾客记录都包含结构化字段（id、items、target_prices、required_amenities 或 attributes、nl_request）。 Each customer record includes structured fields (id, items, target_prices, required_amenities or attributes, nl_request).
商家合成：对于每位顾客，我们以其所需商品为基础，加入干扰商品，并独立抽取价格，从而生成 $K$ 家候选商家。 Business Synthesis: For each customer, we generate $K$ candidate businesses by starting from the customer’s desired items, adding distractors, and sampling prices independently. 通过配置配套服务与设施向量和服务属性向量，使得只有部分商家能够完全满足顾客的约束条件。 Amenity vectors and service attribute vectors are assigned so that only a subset of businesses fully satisfy the customer’s constraints. 商家名称和描述由 LLM 生成。 Business names and descriptions are generated with an LLM. 每条商家记录都包含（id、name、description、menu_items/services、amenities/service attributes）。 Each business record includes (id, name, description, menu_items/services, amenities/service attributes).
我们评估两种市场规模：小型市场（33 位顾客、99 家商家）和中型市场（100 位顾客、300 家商家）。 We evaluate two market scales: small (33 customers, 99 businesses) and medium (100 customers, 300 businesses). 该流程可全面配置以确保可复现性，并可通过映射到同一数据模式，扩展至其他领域（例如旅游、零售）或真实世界数据集。 The pipeline is fully configurable for reproducibility, and can be extended to other domains (e.g., travel, retail) or real-world datasets by mapping to the same schema. 更多合成领域数据和实验请参见我们的代码仓库。 See our repository for additional synthetic domain data and experiments.
4.2 评估4.2 Evaluation
需求满足采用全有或全无的判定方式。 Satisfaction is all-or-nothing. 一笔交易当且仅当包含所有必需的商品及配套服务与设施时，才满足顾客的需求。 A transaction satisfies the customer’s need if and only if it includes all required items and amenities. 如果交易 $j$ 满足消费者 $i$ 的需求，我们记 $F_{ij}=1$（表示匹配），否则记 $F_{ij}=0$。 We write $F_{ij}=1$ (for fit) if transaction $j$ satisfies the need of consumer $i$ , otherwise $F_{ij}=0$ . 消费者 $i$ 对自身需求得到满足的估值为 $V_{i}\geq 0$（以美元计）。 Consumer $i$ has a value $V_{i}\geq 0$ (in dollars) for having their need met. 如果交易 $j$ 的价格为 $P_{j}$，则消费者的效用为其估值减去所支付的价格： If transaction $j$ has price $P_{j}$ , then the consumer’s utility is their value minus the price paid: 
| | $\underbrace{U_{ij}}_{\mbox{Utility}}=\underbrace{V_{i}}_{\mbox{Value}}\times\underbrace{F_{ij}}_{\mbox{Fit}}-\underbrace{P_{j}}_{\mbox{Price}}$ | | (1)
估值 $V_{i}$ 设为所有所需菜单商品平均价格的 $\alpha$ 倍，其中 $\alpha>1$ 是一个校准参数，用于确保最优决策能够带来正效用。 The value $V_{i}$ is set to $\alpha$ times the average price of all desired menu items, where $\alpha>1$ is a calibration parameter ensuring that optimal decision-making leads to positive utility. 我们设定 $\alpha=2$，使得以平均价格购买时，消费者效用与餐厅收入相等。 We set $\alpha=2$ so that buying at the average price equates the consumer utility and restaurant revenue. 每个助手智能体都会获得一份关于其所服务消费者需求的描述，并被要求寻找满足所有要求且价格最低的商家，以实现效用最大化。 Each assistant agent is given a description of its consumer’s need and is instructed to maximize utility by finding a business that satisfies all requirements at the lowest price. 助手智能体还会获得所需商品的平均总价。 The assistant agent is also given the total average price of the desired items.
我们允许每个助手智能体按照第 3 节所述的市场协议和行动协议，代表其所服务的消费者发出请求，以此运行该市场。 We run the marketplace by allowing each assistant agent to issue requests on behalf of its consumer, following the market and action protocols described in Section 3. 由于助手智能体推动着市场过程，我们关注的一项关键指标是消费者福利——以所有已完成交易中实现的消费者效用之和衡量——并将其与多个基线进行比较。 Since assistant agents drive the market process, a key metric of interest is consumer welfare—measured as the sum of consumer utilities achieved across all completed transactions—which we compare across several baselines.
受评估的模型 我们评估了专有模型和开源模型，以展示在 Magentic Marketplace 中获得的初步发现。 Models under Evaluation We evaluate both proprietary and open-source models to demonstrate early findings under Magentic Marketplace. 对于专有模型，我们测试了 GPT-4o (Hurst et al., 2024)、GPT-4.1 (Achiam et al., 2023)、GPT-5 和 Gemini-2.5-Flash (Comanici et al., 2025)。 For proprietary models, we test GPT-4o (Hurst et al., 2024), GPT-4.1 (Achiam et al., 2023), GPT-5, and Gemini-2.5-Flash (Comanici et al., 2025). 对于开源模型，我们还评估了两个中等规模的模型：OpenAI OSS-20b (Agarwal et al., 2025) 和 Qwen3-14b (Yang et al., 2025a)，并使用 YARN (Peng et al., 2023) 将上下文窗口从 32,678 个词元外推扩展至 131,072 个词元，以容纳较长的智能体行动轨迹。 For open-source models, we also include evaluation on two medium-sized models: OpenAI OSS-20b (Agarwal et al., 2025) and Qwen3-14b (Yang et al., 2025a) with YARN (Peng et al., 2023) to extrapolate the context window from 32,678 to 131,072 tokens to accommodate long agentic trajectories. 这两个开源模型均由 vLLM 实现提供支持 (Kwon et al., 2023)。 Both open-source models are supported by vLLM implementation (Kwon et al., 2023). 所有实验均进行了 5 次独立运行，并报告了均值和标准差。 All experiments were conducted with 5 independent runs, with mean and standard deviation reported.

这些结果表明，存在一种高度一致的选择悖论：向助手智能体提供更多选项，最终反而会导致其选择质量下降。 These results illustrate a strikingly consistent paradox of choice whereby presenting more options to an assistant agent ultimately leads to lower-quality selections. 我们推测，这一效应可能并非智能体市场所固有，而是源于智能体的局限性与我们具体设计决策之间的相互作用。 We hypothesize that this effect may not be inherent to agentic markets; rather, it could arise from an interplay between limitations of agents and our specific design decisions. 当智能体与业务不太符合消费者需求的服务智能体发起更多对话时，这既增加了智能体上下文窗口中的信息量，也使智能体早期收到的交易提议更有可能提供较低的效用。 When agents initiate more conversations with service agents whose businesses poorly fit the consumer’s need, this simultaneously increases the amount of information in the agent’s context window and makes it more likely that early transaction proposals received by the agent will offer low utility. 再加上智能体倾向于接受早期提议的偏差（见下文），这可能导致其在面对更大的考虑集时，更有可能做出次优选择。 Combined with an agent bias toward accepting early proposals (see below), this can lead to an increased likelihood of suboptimal choices when facing a larger consideration set.

5.4 智能体的行为偏差5.4 Agent Behavior Biases
我们测试了助手在市场交互中，是否对列在首位的服务或最先响应的服务表现出偏好。 We tested whether assistants exhibit preferences for services listed first versus those responding first in marketplace interactions. 位置偏差考察智能体是否偏好搜索操作结果中排在前面的商家（该操作返回智能体的名称和描述），实验使用三家完全相同的商家，仅改变它们在返回列表中的位置。 Position bias examines if agents prefer businesses listed first in search action results (which returns agent names and descriptions), using three identical businesses varying only their position in the returned list. 图 8 的结果表明，前沿模型（GPT-4.1、Sonnet-4.5、Gemini-2.5-Flash）在三个位置上的选择率接近均匀，说明它们能够有效地并行处理搜索结果，而非按顺序处理。 Results in Figure 8 show that frontier models (GPT-4.1, Sonnet-4.5, Gemini-2.5-Flash) showed near-uniform selection rates across all three positions, suggesting they can effectively process search results in parallel rather than sequentially. 然而，Qwen3-4B 表现出严重的位置偏差，在墨西哥餐厅搜索中选择排在第三位的商家的比例为 57.1%，在承包商搜索中则为 66.7%——均超过随机选择情况下预期比例的两倍。 However, Qwen3-4B exhibited severe position bias, selecting the third-listed business 57.1% of the time in Mexican restaurant searches and 66.7% in contractor searches—more than double the expected rate under random selection.
提案偏差是一种普遍且严重的市场扭曲。 Proposal bias represents a universal and severe market distortion. 与搜索结果中观察到的相对较小的位置效应不同，提案偏差成为一种主导性的行为模式，从根本上扭曲了市场运行机制。 Unlike the relatively modest position effects observed in search results, proposal bias emerged as a dominant behavioral pattern that fundamentally distorts marketplace dynamics. 实验结果显示，所有模型都表现出极强的先发优势：首份提案的选择率达到 60-100%，而第三份提案的选择率接近零。 The experiment results reveal extreme first-mover advantages across all models, with first proposals achieving selection rates between 60-100% compared to near-zero selection for third proposals. 这意味着率先回应的商家具有 10-30 倍的优势，远超我们测量的任何其他竞争因素所产生的影响。 This represents a 10-30 fold advantage for businesses that respond first, dwarfing any other competitive factor we measured.
所有受测模型都对收到的首份提案表现出严重的锚定效应，只是程度有所不同。 Every model tested exhibited severe anchoring on the first proposal received, though with varying degrees. GPT-4o 和 Sonnet-4.5 在某些条件下表现最为极端，首份提案的选择率达到 100%——这意味着这些智能体一旦收到初始报价，就从不等待比较其他选项。 GPT-4o and Sonnet-4.5 showed the most extreme behavior in certain conditions, achieving 100% first-proposal selection rates—meaning these agents never waited to compare alternatives once receiving an initial offer. 即使是在提案选择多样性方面“表现最好”的模型（承包商场景中的 GPT-4.1），选择首份提案的比例仍为 60%，而选择第三份提案的比例为 13.3%，前者具有 4.5 倍的优势。 Even the “best performing” model in terms of proposal diversity (GPT-4.1 in the contractor scenario) still selected first proposals at 60% compared to 13.3% for third proposals, a 4.5x advantage. 这一模式在专有前沿模型和开源替代模型中均一致出现，表明这并非仅仅是训练造成的现象，而可能反映了当前语言模型在处理按时间顺序展开的决策序列时存在的更深层局限。 The consistency of this pattern across both proprietary frontier models and open-source alternatives indicates this is not merely a training artifact but potentially a deeper limitation in how current language models handle temporal decision sequences.
这种偏差可能造成市场扭曲，削弱质量竞争和价格竞争。 This bias can create market distortions that undermine quality and price competition. 在智能体表现出如此极端提案偏差的市场中，竞争的重心可能完全从产品质量或定价转向响应延迟。 In a marketplace where agents exhibit such extreme proposal bias, competitive dynamics can shift entirely from product quality or pricing to response latency. 商家投资于更快的响应系统所获得的收益，高于改进自身产品或服务所获得的收益，因为即使较晚到达的提案更优，也实际上被排除在考虑范围之外。 Businesses gain more from investing in faster response systems than improving their offerings, as even superior late-arriving proposals are effectively excluded from consideration. 第二份和第三份提案接近零的选择率（通常为 0-7%）表明，智能体并未真正比较各个选项，而是采用满意化决策，接受第一个达到要求的报价。 The near-zero selection rates for second and third proposals (often 0-7%) suggest agents are not genuinely comparing options but rather satisficing with the first acceptable offer. 这种行为模式可能导致消费者与服务提供商之间的匹配并非最优，同时引发响应速度的军备竞赛，牺牲其他有价值的市场属性。 This behavior pattern potentially lead to suboptimal matches between consumers and service providers while creating an arms race for response speed at the expense of other valuable market attributes.

6 讨论6 Discussion
智能体市场带来了诸多研究问题和挑战，必须在商业部署之前加以解决。 Agentic markets present numerous research questions and challenges that must be addressed before commercial deployment. 我们的实验结果凸显了市场机制设计和智能体开发两方面的关键研究需求。 Our experimental results highlight critical research needs in both market mechanism design and agent development. Magentic Marketplace 提供了一个仿真环境，用于理解市场组成部分与智能体之间的相互作用。 Magentic Marketplace serves as a simulation environment for understanding the interplay between market components and agents.
设计稳健的智能体市场。 Designing Robust Agentic Markets. 要实现智能体市场的益处，需要在设计上作出既能促进搜索和通信，又能在智能体行为欠佳时保持稳健的选择。 Realizing agentic market benefits requires design choices that facilitate search and communication while remaining robust to suboptimal agent behaviors. Magentic Marketplace 环境支持通过实验探索这些权衡，揭示了协议的微小变化也会导致结果出现显著差异。 The Magentic Marketplace environment enables experimental exploration of these trade-offs, revealing that small changes in protocols lead to meaningful outcome differences. 当智能体表现出首份提案偏差时，搜索排序就变得至关重要；当智能体容易受到操纵时，信任系统就不可或缺。 When agents show first-proposal bias, search ordering becomes critical; when vulnerable to manipulation, trust systems become essential. 这凸显了通过迭代实验来设计市场的必要性，以在开放性与防止欠佳决策的防护措施之间取得平衡。 This underscores the need for iterative experimentation to design markets that balance openness with guardrails against suboptimal decisions.
大规模端到端测试。 End-to-End Testing at Scale. 在测试对市场组成部分或智能体本身的改进时，必须在 Magentic Marketplace 这样的环境中模拟完整的端到端市场。 When testing improvements to market components or to the agents themselves, simulating the full end-to-end market in an environment like the Magentic Marketplace is essential. 即使各个组成部分单独运行时看起来表现良好，涌现结果和动态交互仍可能导致意外后果、漏洞和低效，而这些问题只有在大规模运行时才会显现。 Even when individual components appear to work well in isolation, emergent outcomes and dynamic interactions can lead to unintended consequences, vulnerabilities, and inefficiencies that are apparent only at scale.
我们指出，我们的实验聚焦于静态市场，其中既不要求智能体，也不要求环境根据历史请求和结果进行学习或适应。 We note that our experiments focused on static markets in which neither the agents nor the environment were required to learn or adapt to the history of requests and outcomes. 一种自然且符合现实的扩展是引入动态效应，让市场双方的智能体和用户反复与市场交互，并从观察到的结果中学习。 A natural and realistic extension would add dynamic effects where agents and users on both sides of the market interact with the market repeatedly, learning from their observed outcomes. Magentic Marketplace 这类模拟环境的一项关键优势在于，能够在常规使用模式下，以及面对意外冲击或恶意智能体的协同攻击时，检验市场随时间的演变。 A key advantage of a simulated environment like the Magentic Marketplace is the ability to test the evolution of a market over time, under both standard use patterns and in the face of unexpected shocks or coordinated attacks by malicious agents.
委托—代理关系与人在回路设计。 Principal-Agent Relationships and Human-in-the-Loop Designs. 我们的环境区分人类用户与其 AI 智能体，由此构建的委托—代理关系中，福利损失源于智能体的错误，而非激励不一致。 Our environment distinguishes between human users and their AI agents, creating principal-agent relationships where welfare loss stems from agents’ mistakes rather than misaligned incentives. 这些结果表明，让智能体辅助而非取代人类决策的人在回路设计具有益处，尤其是在高风险交易中。 These results suggest benefits to human-in-the-loop designs where agents assist rather than replace human decision-making, particularly for high-stakes transactions.
