群体机器人学（swarm robotics）研究如何设计无需集中控制的自主机器人系统。 Swarm robotics is the study of how to design independent systems of robots without centralized control. 机器人群体所涌现的集群行为，是通过机器人个体之间以及机器人与环境之间的相互作用形成的。 The emerging swarming behavior of robotic swarms is created through the interactions between individual robots and the environment. 这一理念源于人工群体智能领域，以及对昆虫、蚂蚁和自然界中其他存在集群行为的领域的研究。 This idea emerged on the field of artificial swarm intelligence, as well as the studies of insects, ants and other fields in nature, where swarm behavior occurs.
相对简单的个体规则可以产生大量复杂的集群行为。 Relatively simple individual rules can produce a large set of complex swarm behaviors. 一个关键要素是群体成员之间的通信，它构成了一个持续反馈的系统。 A key component is the communication between the members of the group that build a system of constant feedback. 集群行为既涉及个体在与其他个体协作过程中的持续变化，也涉及整个群体的行为。 The swarm behavior involves constant change of individuals in cooperation with others, as well as the behavior of the whole group.
机器人群体的关键特征Key Attributes of Robotic Swarms
群体机器人系统的设计以群体智能原则为指导，这些原则有助于提高容错性、可扩展性和灵活性。 The design of swarm robotics systems is guided by swarm intelligence principles, which promote fault tolerance, scalability, and flexibility. 与一般的分布式机器人系统不同，群体机器人学强调使用大量机器人。 Unlike distributed robotic systems in general, swarm robotics emphasizes a large number of robots. 尽管群体智能原则存在多种表述，但其中一套得到广泛认可的原则包括： While various formulations of swarm intelligence principles exist, one widely recognized set includes:
机器人具有自主性。 Robots are autonomous.
机器人能够与周围环境交互，并通过反馈改变环境。 Robots can interact with the surroundings and give feedback to modify the environment.
机器人具备局部感知和通信能力，例如使用射频或红外等无线传输系统。 Robots possess local perceiving and communicating capabilities, such as Wireless transmission systems, like radio frequency or infrared.
机器人不依赖集中式群体控制或全局知识。 Robots do not exploit centralized swarm control or global knowledge.
机器人彼此协作，以完成给定任务。 Robots cooperate with each other to accomplish the given task.
微型化也是群体机器人学中的一个关键因素，因为数千个小型机器人能够通过增加个体层面的交互次数，最大限度地发挥群体智能方法的效能，从而在群体层面实现有意义的行为。 Miniaturization is also key factor in swarm robotics, as the effect of thousands of small robots can maximize the effect of the swarm-intelligent approach to achieve meaningful behavior at swarm-level through a greater number of interactions on an individual level.
与单个机器人相比，机器人群体通常能够将给定任务分解为子任务；群体对局部故障具有更强的鲁棒性，在应对不同任务时也更灵活。 Compared with individual robots, a swarm can commonly decompose its given missions to their subtasks; a swarm is more robust to partial failure and is more flexible with regard to different missions.
历史History
据 Google Scholar 记载，“群体机器人学”一词最早出现于 1991 年，但相关研究在 2000 年代初才开始增多。 The phrase "swarm robotics" was reported to have first appeared in 1991, according to Google Scholar, but research regarding swarm robotics began to grow in the early 2000s. 研究群体机器人学的最初目标，是检验能否利用痕迹协同（stigmergy）这一概念，使机器人间接地进行相互通信与协调。 The initial goal of studying swarm robotics was to test whether the concept of stigmergy could be used as a method for robots to indirectly communicate and coordinate with each other.
群体机器人学最早的国际项目之一是欧洲委员会于 2001 至 2005 年资助的 SWARM-BOTS 项目，该项目使用由最多 20 个机器人组成的群体来研究集体搬运、区域覆盖和物体搜索等集群行为，这些机器人能够自主地相互进行物理连接，形成协作系统。 One of the first international projects regarding swarm robotics was the SWARM-BOTS project funded by the European Commission between 2001 and 2005, in which a swarm of up to 20 of robots capable of independently physically connect to each other to form a cooperating system were used to study swarm behaviors such as collective transport, area coverage, and searching for objects. 研究结果展示了自组织机器人团队通过协作解决复杂任务的能力，其中群体内的机器人会随时间推移承担不同角色。 The result was demonstration of self-organized teams of robots that cooperate to solve a complex task, with the robots in the swarm taking different roles over time. 随后，Swarmanoid 项目（2006–2010）进一步拓展了这项工作，将 Swarm-bots 中提出的理念和算法扩展到由飞行、攀爬和地面移动三类机器人组成的异构机器人群体，这些机器人通过协作执行搜索与取回任务。 This work was then expanded upon through the Swarmanoid project (2006–2010), which extended the ideas and algorithms developed in Swarm-bots to heterogeneous robot swarms composed of three types of robots—flying, climbing, and ground-based—that collaborated to carry out a search and retrieval task.
应用Applications
群体机器人技术具有许多潜在应用。 There are many potential applications for swarm robotics. 这些应用包括需要微型化的任务（纳米机器人技术、微型机器人技术），例如在微型机械或人体内执行分布式感知任务。 They include tasks that demand miniaturization (nanorobotics, microbotics), like distributed sensing tasks in micromachinery or the human body. 群体机器人技术的一项前景广阔的用途是执行搜救任务。 A promising use of swarm robotics is in search and rescue missions. 可以将由不同尺寸机器人组成的群体派往救援人员无法安全到达的地方，通过机载传感器探索未知环境并找到穿越复杂迷宫的路径。 Swarms of robots of different sizes could be sent to places that rescue-workers cannot reach safely, to explore the unknown environment and solve complex mazes via onboard sensors. 群体机器人技术也适用于要求低成本设计的任务，例如采矿或农业放牧任务。 Swarm robotics can also be suited to tasks that demand cheap designs, for instance mining or agricultural shepherding tasks.
无人机群Drone swarms
无人机群用于目标搜索、无人机表演和配送。 Drone swarms are used in target search, drone displays, and delivery. 无人机表演通常在夜间使用多架配有灯光的无人机，进行艺术展示或广告宣传。 A drone display commonly uses multiple, lighted drones at night for an artistic display or advertising. 配送无人机群能够一次将多个包裹运送至同一目的地，并克服单架无人机在载荷和电池续航方面的限制。 A delivery drone swarm can carry multiple packages to a single destination at a time and overcome a single drone's payload and battery limitations. 无人机群可以采用不同的飞行编队，以减少阻力造成的总体能量消耗。 A drone swarm may undertake different flight formations to reduce overall energy consumption due to drag forces.
无人机集群运行也可能带来与人为因素和集群操作员有关的额外控制问题。 Drone swarming can also introduce additional control issues connected to human factors and the swarm operator. 例如，在与多架无人机交互时，由于需要在不同无人机个体之间切换注意力，操作员会面临较高的认知负荷和操作复杂性。 Examples of this include high cognitive demand and complexity when interacting with multiple drones due to changing attention between different individual drones. 操作员与集群之间的通信也是一个核心方面。 Communication between operator and swarm is also a central aspect.
军事机器人群Military swarms
更具争议的是，军用机器人群可以组成一支自主军队。 More controversially, swarms of military robots can form an autonomous army. 美国海军已测试过一种自主艇群，这些艇能够自行操舵并采取进攻行动。 U.S. Naval forces have tested a swarm of autonomous boats that can steer and take offensive actions by themselves. 这些艇均为无人艇，可配备各种装备，以威慑和摧毁敌方舰艇。 The boats are unmanned and can be fitted with any kind of kit to deter and destroy enemy vessels.
叙利亚内战期间，驻扎在该地区的俄罗斯军队报告称，其在叙利亚的主要空军基地遭到装载炸药的固定翼无人机集群袭击。 During the Syrian Civil War, Russian forces in the region reported attacks on their main air force base in the country by swarms of fixed-wing drones loaded with explosives.
微型集群Miniature swarms
另一大类应用问题可通过微型飞行器集群来解决，这也是当前受到广泛研究的方向。 Another large set of applications may be solved using swarms of micro air vehicles, which are also broadly investigated nowadays. 早期的飞行机器人集群研究在实验室条件下使用精确的运动捕捉系统，而如今，Shooting Star 等系统可以利用 GNSS 系统（如 GPS），在室外环境中控制由数百架微型飞行器组成的编队，甚至可以在 GPS 不可用时，利用机载定位系统使其保持稳定。 In comparison with the pioneering studies of swarms of flying robots using precise motion capture systems in laboratory conditions, current systems such as Shooting Star can control teams of hundreds of micro aerial vehicles in outdoor environment using GNSS systems (such as GPS) or even stabilize them using onboard localization systems where GPS is unavailable. 微型飞行器集群已在自主监视、羽流追踪以及以紧密方阵执行侦察等任务中得到测试。 Swarms of micro aerial vehicles have been already tested in tasks of autonomous surveillance, plume tracking, and reconnaissance in a compact phalanx. 围绕无人地面车辆与无人飞行器协同集群，已有大量研究开展，其目标应用包括协同环境监测、同时定位与地图构建、车队防护，以及运动目标定位与跟踪。 Numerous works on cooperative swarms of unmanned ground and aerial vehicles have been conducted with target applications of cooperative environment monitoring, simultaneous localization and mapping, convoy protection, and moving target localization and tracking.
声学集群Acoustic swarms
2023 年，华盛顿大学和 Microsoft 的研究人员展示了由微型机器人组成的声学集群，可构成形状可变的智能扬声器。 In 2023, University of Washington and Microsoft researchers demonstrated acoustic swarms of tiny robots that create shape-changing smart speakers. 这些集群可用于调控声学场景，以突出或消除来自房间内特定区域的声音。 These can be used for manipulating acoustic scenes to focus on or mute sounds from a specific region in a room. 在这里，微型机器人通过声音信号相互协作，无需任何摄像头，即可实现厘米级精度的协同导航。 Here, tiny robots cooperate with each other using sound signals, without any cameras, to navigate cooperatively with centimeter-level accuracy. 这些集群设备在一个表面上分散开来，形成分布式、可重构的无线麦克风阵列。 These swarm devices spread out across a surface to create a distributed and reconfigurable wireless microphone array. 它们还会自主导航返回充电站，在那里自动充电。 They also navigate back to the charging station where they can be automatically recharged.
KilobotKilobot
大多数研究工作都集中于规模相对较小的机器群体。 Most efforts have focused on relatively small groups of machines. 然而，哈佛大学在 2014 年展示了一个由 1,024 个独立机器人组成的 Kilobot 集群，是截至当时规模最大的机器人集群。 However, a Kilobot swarm consisting of 1,024 individual robots was demonstrated by Harvard in 2014, the largest to date.

微型化的另一个例子是 LIBOT 机器人系统，其中采用了为室外集群机器人研究而构建的低成本机器人。 Another example of miniaturization is the LIBOT Robotic System that involves a low cost robot built for outdoor swarm robotics. 这些机器人在设计时也考虑了通过 Wi-Fi 在室内使用，因为 GPS 传感器在建筑物内部的通信效果较差。 The robots are also made with provisions for indoor use via Wi-Fi, since the GPS sensors provide poor communication inside buildings.

另一项类似尝试是英国林肯大学计算智能实验室研制的微型机器人（Colias）。 Another such attempt is the micro robot (Colias), built in the Computer Intelligence Lab at the University of Lincoln, UK. 该微型机器人采用 4 cm 的圆形底盘，是一个低成本的开放平台，可用于多种集群机器人应用。 This micro robot is built on a 4 cm circular chassis and is a low-cost and open platform for use in a variety of swarm robotics applications.
制造集群Manufacturing swarms
此外，自主集群在制造领域的应用也取得了进展，这种应用被称为集群 3D 打印。 Additionally, progress has been made in the application of autonomous swarms in the field of manufacturing, known as swarm 3D printing. 这对于大型结构和部件的生产尤为有用，因为受硬件尺寸限制，传统 3D 打印无法用于此类生产。 This is particularly useful for the production of large structures and components, where traditional 3D printing is not able to be utilized due to hardware size constraints. 微型化与大规模协同运作使制造系统能够实现尺度不变性，不受有效成形体积的限制。 Miniaturization and mass mobilization allows the manufacturing system to achieve scale invariance, not limited in effective build volume. 尽管集群 3D 打印仍处于发展初期，但目前已有初创公司正在推动其商业化。 While in its early stage of development, swarm 3D printing is currently being commercialized by startup companies.
