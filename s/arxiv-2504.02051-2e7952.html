多智能体 LLM 系统中的自主资源分配Self-Resource Allocation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems
摘要Abstract
随着 LLM 作为智能体的发展，将多个智能体连接成多智能体系统以并发解决任务的研究日益受到关注，重点在于它们在任务分配与协调中的作用。 With the development of LLMs as agents, there is a growing interest in connecting multiple agents into multi-agent systems to solve tasks concurrently, focusing on their role in task assignment and coordination. 本文探讨 LLM 如何综合考虑成本、效率和性能等因素，在多个智能体之间有效分配计算任务。 This paper explores how LLMs can effectively allocate computational tasks among multiple agents, considering factors such as cost, efficiency, and performance. 在本研究中，我们探讨了若干关键问题，包括 LLM 作为编排器（orchestrator）和规划器（planner）的有效性，并比较二者在任务分配与协调方面的效能。 In this work, we address key questions, including the effectiveness of LLMs as orchestrators and planners, comparing their effectiveness in task assignment and coordination. 我们的实验表明，LLM 在资源分配任务中能够实现较高的有效率和准确率。 Our experiments demonstrate that LLMs can achieve high validity and accuracy in resource allocation tasks. 我们发现，规划器方法在处理并发动作时优于编排器方法，从而提高了效率和智能体利用率。 We find that the planner method outperforms the orchestrator method in handling concurrent actions, resulting in improved efficiency and better utilization of agents. 此外，我们表明，明确提供工作智能体的能力信息能够改善规划器的分配策略，尤其是在面对能力欠佳的工作智能体时。 Additionally, we show that providing explicit information about worker capabilities enhances the allocation strategies of planners, particularly when dealing with suboptimal workers.
1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）已广泛用于文本生成之外的各种任务。 Large Language Models (LLMs) have become popular for various tasks beyond just generating text. 它们能够充当与环境交互的智能体 (Xi et al., 2025a)，并有效使用工具。 They can act as agents that interact with their environment (Xi et al., 2025a) and use tools effectively. 这推动了功能更加多样的系统的发展，这些系统能够像人类一样操作计算机 (Agashe et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024)、担任研究助手 (Schmidgall et al., 2025)，甚至实现实验室任务的自动化 (M. Bran et al., 2024)。 This has led to the development of more versatile systems that can operate a computer like a human (Agashe et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024), serve as research assistants (Schmidgall et al., 2025), or even automate tasks in a lab (M. Bran et al., 2024).
随着智能体被用于更多任务和场景，它们需要与其他智能体交互。 As agents are used for more tasks and in more scenarios, they need to interact with other agents. 这种交互既可以通过多智能体系统（Multi-Agent Systems，MAS）的设计来实现，也可以通过更自发的交互来发生。 This can happen by design in Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) or through more spontaneous interactions. 因此，AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) 和 Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a) 等框架应运而生，以发挥 MAS 的能力。 As a result, frameworks like AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) and Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a) have been developed to leverage the capabilities of MAS. 这些多智能体系统的构建带来了值得探索的新可能性和新挑战 (Han et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024a; Agashe et al., 2023)，例如其组织结构、共享记忆、通信效率和协调能力。 The creation of these multi-agent systems opens up new possibilities and challenges to explore (Han et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024a; Agashe et al., 2023), such as their organizational structures, shared memory, communication efficiency, and coordination capabilities.
具体而言，我们关注这些智能体如何通过协调来分配任务并实现目标。 In particular, we focus on how these agents coordinate to assign tasks and achieve their goals. Marvin Minsky 认为，智能源于计算模块的协同工作，这些模块共同实现任何单个模块都无法独立实现的目标 Minsky (1988)；受这一观点启发，我们旨在分析 LLM 如何向智能体分配任务。 Inspired by Marvin Minsky’s idea that intelligence emerges from computational modules working together to accomplish goals that none could achieve alone Minsky (1988), we aim to analyze how LLMs allocate tasks to agents. 我们的目标是由 LLM 自身优化资源和任务的分配。 Our goal is to optimize the allocation of resources and tasks by LLMs themselves. 因此，我们的根本研究问题是：由基于 LLM 的智能体构成的网络如何优化其任务分配？ Thus, our underlying research question is: How does a network of LLM-based agents optimize their task allocation?
我们开展了一系列实验，以了解 LLM 如何在多智能体系统内向智能体分配任务。 We conduct a series of experiments to understand how LLMs allocate tasks to agents within multi-agent systems. 首先，对于一个已由匈牙利算法给出已知解的问题，我们评估单个 LLM 如何通过为每个智能体分配动作来生成正确的任务分配方案。 First, we evaluate how one LLM can generate the correct task allocation, assigning actions to each agent for a problem with a known solution provided by the Hungarian Algorithm. 其次，我们使用第 4.2 节详细介绍的 CuisineWorld 基准 (Gong et al., 2023)，考察图 1 所示的两种方法——编排器和规划器。 Second, we examine two methods illustrated in Figure 1—Orchestrator and Planner—using the CuisineWorld benchmark (Gong et al., 2023), which is detailed in Section 4.2. 编排器使用一个 LLM 生成所有待执行的动作，而规划器先制定计划，再将其交给执行器 LLM 智能体，由这些智能体独立生成各自的动作。 The Orchestrator uses one LLM to generate all the actions to be executed, while the Planner creates a plan that is then given to executor LLM agents, who generate their actions independently. 只有在发生相关事件时，才会重新评估该计划。 The plan is only re-evaluated when a relevant event occurs. 最后，我们评估规划器如何根据智能体的能力分配任务。 Lastly, we assess how the Planner can allocate tasks based on the agents’ abilities.
通过实验，我们得出了以下有趣的发现：首先，随着参数规模增大，LLM 在资源分配任务中的有效率和准确率有所提高，但这需要付出显著的计算成本和资金成本。 From the experiments, we concluded interesting findings: First, LLMs achieve higher validity and accuracy in resource allocation tasks as their parameter size increases, but at a significant computational and monetary cost. 其次，规划器方法在处理并发动作时优于编排器方法，从而提高了多智能体任务执行的效率。 Second, the planner method outperforms the orchestrator method in handling concurrent actions, resulting in improved efficiency in multi-agent task execution. 规划器方法实现了更高的智能体利用率，空闲动作也更少。 The planner method achieves better utilization of agents, with fewer idle actions. 最后，我们发现，LLM 对工作智能体的能力较为敏感，但难以动态推断这些能力。 Finally, we see that LLMs are sensitive to worker capabilities and struggle to infer them dynamically. 明确提供工作智能体的能力信息能够改善规划器的分配策略，尤其是在工作智能体能力欠佳时。 Providing explicit information about worker capabilities improves planner’s allocation strategies, especially with suboptimal workers.

3 问题定义3 Problem Definition
在现实环境中，多智能体系统中的资源分配通常可以视为一个多智能体资源分配问题。 In real-world settings, resource allocation in multi-agent systems can often be seen as a multi-agent resource allocation problem. 这涉及将计算任务分配给多个模型实例，以优化成本、效率和性能等指标。 This involves distributing computational tasks among multiple model instances to optimize criteria such as cost, efficiency, and performance. 随着 AI 系统获得自主性，并需要在信息不完整的情况下做出资源分配决策，这一挑战变得尤为重要。 This challenge becomes especially relevant as AI systems gain autonomy and need to make resource allocation decisions with incomplete information. 我们对该问题进行形式化定义，为分析 LLM 如何在多重约束和目标下进行自主资源分配奠定基础。 We formalize the problem to provide a foundation for analyzing how LLMs can perform self-resource allocation under multiple constraints and objectives.
在一个包含 $N$ 个智能体和 $P$ 个不同任务的多智能体系统中，每个任务都可以分解为由 $M_{p}$ 个子任务构成的序列。 In a multi-agent system with $N$ agents and $P$ different tasks, each task can be decomposed into a sequence of $M_{p}$ subtasks. 由于能力存在差异，每个智能体执行不同任务时产生的成本也不同。 Each agent incurs different costs for different tasks due to variations in capability. 目标是在满足时间约束和分配约束的同时，最大化整体效用。 The objective is to maximize overall utility while respecting time and assignment constraints.

4.1 实验 1：基本资源分配4.1 Experiment 1: Basic Resource Allocation
问题描述Problem Statement
编排智能体在多智能体系统中得到了广泛应用。 Orchestrator agent is widely adopted in multi-agent systems. 我们首先在成本和奖励均被明确定义的最简单设置下，评估 LLM 编排器分配资源的有效性。 We start with evaluating how effectively LLM orchestrator can allocate resources in the most simple setting where both costs and rewards are explicitly defined.
图 2：具有基于 LLM 的编排器的多智能体系统。 Figure 2: A multi-agent system with an LLM-based orchestrator. 在每个步骤中，编排器以最优方式将多个任务分配给智能体，并使总成本最小化。 At each step, the orchestrator optimally assigns multiple tasks to agents and minimizes the total cost. 智能体完成任务后，环境会更新，提供新任务和新的成本矩阵，使这一过程迭代进行。 After agents complete their tasks, the environment updates with new tasks and a new cost matrix, continuing the process iteratively.
考虑一个多智能体系统，其中包含一项长时程任务，该任务可以分解为一系列可由智能体执行的基本子任务或动作。 Consider a multi-agent system with a long-horizon task that can be broken down into a sequence of fundamental sub-tasks or actions executable by agents. 在该系统中，基于 LLM 的编排器根据智能体执行任务的相应成本，将任务分配给智能体。 In this system, the LLM-based orchestrator assigns tasks to agents based on their associated costs. 由于能力或资源获取条件存在差异，每个智能体执行不同任务时产生的成本各不相同。 Each agent incurs different costs for different tasks due to variations in capability or access to resources. 一旦完成分配且智能体完成各自的任务，环境就会相应更新，从而为下一轮产生一组新任务，如 Figure 2 所示。 Once an assignment is made and agents complete their tasks, the environment updates accordingly, leading to a new set of tasks for the next turn, as is shown in Figure 2. 这里，我们重点评估编排器大语言模型（LLM）在单轮中的表现，与标准指派问题保持一致。 Here, we focus on evaluating the orchestrator LLM in a single turn, aligning with the standard assignment problem.
指派问题的目标是确定将 $n$ 个智能体分配给 $n$ 个任务的最优方案。 The objective of the assignment problem is to determine the optimal assignment of $n$ agents to $n$ tasks. 该问题的每个实例都由一个成本矩阵 $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ 定义，其中每个元素表示将某个特定智能体分配给某项任务的成本。 Each instance of the problem is defined by a cost matrix $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ , where each entry represents the cost associated with assigning a particular agent to a task. 作为一个基本的组合优化问题，指派问题可以使用匈牙利算法在多项式时间内求解，其复杂度为 $O(n^{3})$，其中 $n$ 为任务和智能体的数量。 As a fundamental combinatorial optimization problem, the assignment problem can be solved in polynomial time using the Hungarian algorithm, which has a complexity of $O(n^{3})$ , where $n$ is the number of tasks and agents. 这里，我们旨在评估 LLM 能否在开箱即用的情况下有效地最小化指派成本。 Here, we aim to evaluate whether LLMs can effectively minimize the assignment cost out of the box.
实验细节Experiment Details
为评估 LLM 在指派问题上的表现，我们首先生成多个填充随机数的成本矩阵，以此构建评估集。 To evaluate the LLM performance on assignment problems, we first created an evaluation set by generating a multiple cost matrix with random numbers filled in. 随后，我们使用匈牙利算法获得每道题的标准答案。 After that, we utilize the Hungarian algorithm to get the ground truth for each answer. 所有 LLM 编排器均采用贪心解码进行评估，最大序列长度为 2048。 All LLM orchestrators are evaluated using greedy decoding with a maximum sequence length of 2048.
评估Evaluation
我们评估了不同模型规模的 LLM 在自主资源分配方面的表现。 We evaluate the performance of self-resource allocation across LLMs of varying model sizes. 具体而言，我们考察了 GPT-4o-mini(~8B)、Mistral-Small-3.1(24B)、Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct、Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct、GPT-4o(~200B) 和 Llama3.1-405B-Instruct-FP8。 Specifically, we consider GPT-4o-mini(~8B), Mistral-Small-3.1(24B), Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct, GPT-4o(~200B), and Llama3.1-405B-Instruct-FP8. 我们采用准确率和有效率两项评估指标。❶ 准确率衡量 LLM 生成的指派方案中有多少是最优的。 Two evaluation metrics, accuracy and validity rate, are used. ❶ Accuracy measures how many of the LLM-generated assignment solutions are optimal. 只有当某个方案与匈牙利算法给出的标准最优指派方案完全一致时，才将其视为正确方案。 A solution is considered as correct only when it is exactly the same as the ground truth optimal assignment given by the Hungarian algorithm. 这里，我们使用基于 GPT-4o 的 LLM 评判器来检查各模型的回答。❷ 有效率检验 LLM 生成的指派方案中有多少是有效的，这些方案可以不是最优的。 Here we use the LLM judge based on GPT-4o to examine each model response. ❷ Validity rate tests how many of the LLM-generated assignment solutions are valid, which can be not optimal. 无效指派包括将一个智能体分配给多项任务、部分任务未分配智能体，以及为智能体编造一个错误的较低成本。 Invalid assignments include assigning one agent to more than one task, leaving some tasks not assigned with agents, and making up an incorrect lower cost of the agent.

实验细节Experiment Details
本实验旨在评估多智能体 LLM 系统中规划相较于使用协调器的有效性。 The goal of this experiment is to evaluate the effectiveness of planning in a multi-agent LLM system compared to the use of an orchestrator. 我们遵循 React (Yao et al., 2023) 及其他文献 (Xi et al., 2025b) 中给出的智能体定义，即：环境 $\rightarrow$ 推理 $\rightarrow$ 行动。 We follow the definition of an agent provided in React (Yao et al., 2023) and other literature (Xi et al., 2025b), where agents are defined as: environment $\rightarrow$ reasoning $\rightarrow$ action. 我们比较三种方法，图 1 展示了智能体数量为 $n=1,...,6$ 时的情况： We compare three methods, represented in Figure 1 from $n=1,...,6$ agents:
独立模式（Individual）：每个智能体由不同的 LLM 控制，并独立生成自己的行动。 Individual: Each agent is controlled by a different LLM, and each agent generates its own action independently.
协调器（Orchestrator）：一个 LLM 控制游戏中的所有智能体，并为它们全部生成行动。 Orchestrator: One LLM controls all the agents in the game and generates actions for all of them.
规划器（Planner）：每当游戏中发生相关事件时，例如引入、完成或移除一道菜品，就生成一个总体计划。 Planner: Generates a general plan whenever a relevant event occurs in the game, such as when a dish is introduced, completed, or removed. 随后，该计划被提供给各个 LLM 智能体，由它们独立生成行动。 This plan is then provided to the LLM agents, which produce their independent actions.

图 10 比较了即时分配与知情分配。 Figure 10 compares On-the-fly Allocation to Informed Allocation. 在即时分配中，由于事先未获得任何关于各工作 LLM 优势的提示，规划器难以动态确定最佳分配方案，尤其是在工作智能体的表现差异较大时。 In On-the-fly allocation, Without any prior indication of each worker LLM’s strengths, planners struggle to identify the best allocations dynamically, especially when worker performance varies widely. 然而，在知情分配中，仅提供有关工作智能体能力的细微线索（根据即时分配表现得到的动作成功率），就能显著提高整体效率——尤其是在规划器必须使用次优模型时。 However, simply providing subtle cues about worker capabilities (action success rate from On-the-fly allocation performance) in Informed Allocation leads to a marked increase in overall efficiency—particularly when planners must work with suboptimal models. 这些线索减少了无效动作，使基于 LLM 的规划器在了解工作智能体之间差异的情况下，能够更好地将任务与模型匹配。 These cues reduce non-productive actions, helping LLM-based planners better match tasks to models when aware of worker differences.
LLM 对工作智能体的能力高度敏感。LLMs are highly sensitive to worker capabilities.
我们发现，多智能体系统的性能显著受到各工作 LLM 的具体能力及其组合方式的影响。 We find the performance of a multi-agent system is significantly influenced by the specific capabilities of each worker LLM and how these capabilities are combined. 在同构设置中，Llama-70B-Instruct 和 Qwen32B 作为工作模型时，表现始终优于 GPT-4o-mini。 In a homogeneous setting, Llama-70B-Instruct and Qwen32B consistently outperform GPT-4o-mini when used as worker models. 混合使用能力强弱不同的模型通常会降低平均效率。 Mixing models of varying strengths generally reduces average efficiency. 然而，在异构团队中纳入至少一个能力较强的模型可以提高效率。 However, including at least one stronger model in a heterogeneous team improves efficiency. 例如，将 Qwen 与 GPT-4o-mini 组合使用时，效率为 4.04，而实验 2 中两个 GPT-4o-mini 模型的效率为 2.79。 For instance, combining Qwen with GPT-4o-mini yields an efficiency of 4.04, compared to 2.79 for two GPT-4o-mini models from experiment 2. 在异构情况下，我们观察到，由能力更强的 LLM 组成的较小团队，其表现可以优于能力参差不齐的较大团队，例如 Llama–Qwen 组合的表现优于全部三个模型的组合。 In the heterogenous case, we observe that a smaller team of more capable LLMs can outperform a larger team with uneven skills, as seen with the Llama–Qwen pairing outperforming the combination of all three.
6 结论6 Conclusions
本研究探讨了 LLM 优化多智能体系统中任务分配的能力。 This work explores the capabilities of LLMs to optimize task allocations in multi-agents systems. 实验表明，LLM 可以充当协调器，其性能可与匈牙利算法等成熟算法相媲美。 Experiments show that LLMs can function as orchestrators, with relative performance to that of established algorithms like the Hungarian Algorithm. 然而，采用规划器方法代替协调器方法，可以提高处理并发动作的效率。 However, using a planner method instead of an orchestrator improves efficiency in handling concurrent actions. 这些发现表明，利用 LLM 可以构建效率更高、成本效益更好的多智能体框架，根据实时需求动态分配资源，从而提升性能和成本效益。 These findings suggest that leveraging LLMs can create more efficient and cost-effective multi-agent frameworks, dynamically allocating resources based on real-time needs, enhancing performance and cost-effectiveness.
