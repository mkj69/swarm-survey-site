Distributional AGI Safety分布式 AGI 安全
Abstract摘要
AI safety and alignment research has predominantly been focused on methods for safeguarding individual AI systems, resting on the assumption of an eventual emergence of a monolithic Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). The alternative AGI emergence hypothesis, where general capability levels are first manifested through coordination in groups of sub-AGI individual agents with complementary skills and affordances, has received far less attention. Here we argue that this patchwork AGI hypothesis needs to be given serious consideration, and should inform the development of corresponding safeguards and mitigations. The rapid deployment of advanced AI agents with tool-use capabilities and the ability to communicate and coordinate makes this an urgent safety consideration. We therefore propose a framework for distributional AGI safety that moves beyond evaluating and aligning individual agents. This framework centres on the design and implementation of virtual agentic sandbox economies (impermeable or semi-permeable), where agent-to-agent transactions are governed by robust market mechanisms, coupled with appropriate auditability, reputation management, and oversight to mitigate collective risks.
AI 安全与对齐研究主要聚焦于保障单个 AI 系统安全的方法，其基础假设是最终将出现一个单体式的通用人工智能（AGI）。另一种关于 AGI 涌现的假说认为，通用能力水平首先通过一群具有互补技能和行动可供性的、尚未达到 AGI 水平的个体智能体之间的协调而显现，但这一假说受到的关注要少得多。本文认为，这种拼合式 AGI 假说需要得到认真考虑，并应为相应安全保障与风险缓解措施的制定提供指导。具备工具使用能力以及沟通和协调能力的先进 AI 智能体正被迅速部署，这使其成为一项亟须考虑的安全问题。因此，我们提出一个分布式 AGI 安全框架，超越对单个智能体的评估与对齐。该框架以设计和实施虚拟智能体沙盒经济体（完全隔离或半开放）为核心，其中智能体之间的交易由稳健的市场机制约束，并辅以适当的可审计性、声誉管理和监督，以缓解集体风险。

1 Introduction1 引言
Rapid advances in AI capabilities need to be complemented by the development of robust frameworks for safety, oversight, and alignment (Gabriel et al., 2024). AI alignment (Everitt et al., 2018; Tegmark and Omohundro, 2023) is particularly important in the case of autonomous AI agents (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025; Cihon et al., 2025), and is one of the key considerations on the path to developing safe artificial general intelligence (AGI), a general-purpose AI system capable of performing any task that humans can routinely perform. Other approaches may involve continuous monitoring for the emergence of dangerous capabilities (Phuong et al., 2024; Bova et al., 2024; Shah et al., 2025), or involve different frameworks for containment (Babcock et al., 2016). Mechanistic interpretability and formal verifiability remain of interest (Tegmark and Omohundro, 2023), though the complexity of modern agentic systems presents a practical challenge. In the absence of strict controls and mitigations, powerful AGI capabilities may potentially lead to a number of catastrophic risks (Hendrycks et al., 2023).
AI 能力的快速进步需要辅以稳健的安全、监督与对齐框架的开发 (Gabriel et al., 2024)。对于自主 AI 智能体 (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025; Cihon et al., 2025)，AI 对齐 (Everitt et al., 2018; Tegmark and Omohundro, 2023) 尤为重要，也是开发安全的通用人工智能（AGI）过程中的关键考量之一；AGI 是一种能够执行人类日常所能执行的任何任务的通用 AI 系统。其他方法可能包括持续监测危险能力的涌现 (Phuong et al., 2024; Bova et al., 2024; Shah et al., 2025)，或采用不同的遏制框架 (Babcock et al., 2016)。机制可解释性和形式化可验证性仍然受到关注 (Tegmark and Omohundro, 2023)，但现代智能体系统的复杂性构成了实际挑战。在缺乏严格控制和风险缓解措施的情况下，强大的 AGI 能力可能导致多种灾难性风险 (Hendrycks et al., 2023)。
The majority of contemporary AI safety and alignment methods have been developed with a single powerful A(G)I entity in mind. This includes methods like reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) (Christiano et al., 2017; Bai et al., 2022a), constitutional AI (Bai et al., 2022b), process supervision (Luo et al., 2024), value alignment (Eckersley, 2018; Gabriel, 2020; Gabriel and Ghazavi, 2022; Klingefjord et al., 2024), chain of thought (CoT) monitoring (Korbak et al., 2025; Emmons et al., 2025), and others. These types of methods are being routinely utilised in the development and testing of large language models (LLMs), to ensure desirable behaviour at deployment. In the context of the hypothetical future emergence of AGI, this would be conceptually appropriate if AGI were to first emerge as an individual AI agent, developed by a specific institution. In principle, this would enable the developers to utilise the testing frameworks to confirm the capability level of the system, characterise its alignment, make improvements and mitigations, deploy appropriate safeguards, and take any number of necessary steps in line with regulations and societal expectations.
当代大多数 AI 安全与对齐方法都是以单个强大的 A(G)I 实体为对象开发的。其中包括基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）(Christiano et al., 2017; Bai et al., 2022a)、宪法式 AI (Bai et al., 2022b)、过程监督 (Luo et al., 2024)、价值对齐 (Eckersley, 2018; Gabriel, 2020; Gabriel and Ghazavi, 2022; Klingefjord et al., 2024)、思维链（CoT）监测 (Korbak et al., 2025; Emmons et al., 2025) 等方法。这些方法已被常规应用于大语言模型（LLMs）的开发和测试，以确保模型在部署时表现出符合期望的行为。在假设未来会涌现 AGI 的背景下，如果 AGI 最初以某个特定机构开发的单个 AI 智能体的形式出现，那么这种做法在概念上是适当的。原则上，这将使开发者能够利用测试框架来确认系统的能力水平、刻画其对齐状况、实施改进和风险缓解措施、部署适当的安全保障，并根据法规和社会期望采取各种必要措施。
However, this overlooks a highly plausible alternative scenario for the emergence of AGI - specifically, the emergence of AGI via the interaction of sub-AGI agents within groups or systems. Sub-AGI agents can form group agents, the same way humans do in the form of corporations (List and Pettit, 2011; List, 2021; Franklin, 2023). These collective structures would function as coherent entities that perform actions that no single agent could perform independently (Simon, 1962; Haken, 1977; Von Foerster, 1976). Such group agents may possess representational and motivational states distinct from those of their constituents (List and Pettit, 2011), enabling them to execute strategic actions and solve problems that exceed the cognitive capacities of any single agent - much like how a modern research institution can tackle interdisciplinary challenges intractable to any single polymath. Alternatively, like humans engaging in financial markets, sub-AGI agents could interact within complex systems, where individual decisions driven by personal incentives and information, aggregated through mechanisms (such as price signals), could result in the emergence of capacities that surpass that of any single participant within the system. It is possible that sub-AGI agents will both form groups - such as fully automated firms (Patel, 2025) - and engage within systems (Drexler, 2019) - such as Virtual Agent Economies (Tomasev et al., 2025).
然而，这忽略了 AGI 涌现的一种极有可能发生的替代情景——具体而言，即 AGI 通过群体或系统内部尚未达到 AGI 水平的智能体之间的交互而涌现。尚未达到 AGI 水平的智能体可以形成群体智能体，就像人类以公司的形式组成群体一样 (List and Pettit, 2011; List, 2021; Franklin, 2023)。这些集体结构将作为协调一致的实体运作，执行任何单个智能体都无法独立完成的行动 (Simon, 1962; Haken, 1977; Von Foerster, 1976)。这类群体智能体可能拥有不同于其组成个体的表征状态和动机状态 (List and Pettit, 2011)，从而能够执行超出任何单个智能体认知能力的战略行动并解决相应问题——这很像现代研究机构能够应对任何一位博学者都难以独自解决的跨学科挑战。另一种可能是，如同参与金融市场的人类一样，尚未达到 AGI 水平的智能体可以在复杂系统中交互，其中，由个体自身的激励和信息驱动的决策通过某些机制（如价格信号）汇聚起来，可能促使超越系统中任何单个参与者的能力涌现。尚未达到 AGI 水平的智能体可能既会形成群体——例如全自动化企业 (Patel, 2025)——也会参与系统 (Drexler, 2019)——例如虚拟智能体经济体 (Tomasev et al., 2025)。
This distributed framing has precedent: Drexler’s Comprehensive AI Services model (Drexler, 2019) argues that superintelligent capabilities are more likely to arise from the coordination of modular, task-bounded AI services than from a single monolithic agent. The concept of distributed AI capabilities has also been explored in relation to cooperative AI research (Dafoe et al., 2020), and contextually specialised ecosystems (Leibo et al., 2025). Hadfield (Hadfield and Koh, 2025) has outlined how autonomous AI agents could form a functioning economy with market mechanisms providing the coordination layer. The contribution of the present paper is to articulate the safety implications of these convergent trends, and to propose a concrete defence-in-depth framework for governing the interactions within such systems.
这种分布式框架已有先例：Drexler 的 Comprehensive AI Services 模型（Drexler, 2019）认为，超智能能力更有可能源于模块化、任务边界明确的 AI 服务之间的协调，而非单一的整体式智能体。分布式 AI 能力这一概念也已在合作式 AI 研究（Dafoe et al., 2020）以及针对特定情境专门化的生态系统（Leibo et al., 2025）中得到探讨。Hadfield（Hadfield and Koh, 2025）概述了自主 AI 智能体如何形成一个正常运转的经济体系，并由市场机制提供协调层。本文的贡献在于阐明这些相互汇聚的趋势所具有的安全影响，并提出一个具体的纵深防御框架，以治理此类系统内部的交互。
We argue that AGI may initially emerge as a patchwork system, distributed (Drexler, 2019; Montes and Goertzel, 2019; Gibson and Sokolov, 2025; Tallam, 2025) across entities within a network. A patchwork AGI would be comprised of a group of individual sub-AGI agents, with complementary skills and affordances. General intelligence in the patchwork AGI system would arise primarily as collective intelligence. Individual agents could delegate tasks to each other, routing each task to the appropriate agent with the highest individual skill, or with access to the most appropriate tools. For certain functions, it may well be more economical to use narrower specialist agents. In cases when no single agent possesses the appropriate skill level, the tasks could be further decomposed or reframed, or performed in collaboration with other agents. This cognitive division of labour allows the collective to bypass the bottlenecks of any single architecture - such as limited context windows, specialised training data, or constrained tool access - effectively creating a modular system capable of parallel, heterogeneous reasoning at scales no individual agent could sustain.
我们认为，AGI 最初可能以一种拼接式系统（patchwork system）的形式出现，分布于网络中的各个实体之中（Drexler, 2019; Montes and Goertzel, 2019; Gibson and Sokolov, 2025; Tallam, 2025）。拼接式 AGI 将由一组尚未达到 AGI 水平的独立智能体组成，这些智能体具有互补的技能与行动条件。拼接式 AGI 系统中的通用智能将主要以集体智能的形式产生。各个智能体可以相互委派任务，将每项任务交由相应技能水平最高或能够使用最合适工具的智能体处理。对于某些功能，使用能力范围更窄的专用智能体很可能更为经济。当没有任何单个智能体具备适当的技能水平时，可以进一步分解任务、重新界定任务，或与其他智能体协作完成任务。这种认知分工使整个集体能够绕过任何单一架构的瓶颈——例如有限的上下文窗口、专门化的训练数据或受限的工具访问权限——从而有效构建出一个模块化系统，能够以任何单个智能体都无法维持的规模开展并行、异构推理。
The economic argument for a multi-agent future over a single, monolithic AGI stems from the principles of scarcity and dispersed knowledge. A lone, frontier model is a one-size-fits-all solution that is prohibitively expensive for the vast majority of tasks, meaning its marginal benefit rarely justifies its cost, which is why businesses often choose cheaper, ’good enough’ models. Should the frontier models become substantially cheaper, custom specialised models may still be available at a slightly cheaper price point. This reality creates a demand-driven ecosystem where countless specialised, fine-tuned, and cost-effective agents emerge to serve specific needs, much like a market economy. Consequently, progress looks less like building a single omni-capable frontier model and more like developing sophisticated systems (e.g., routers) to orchestrate this diverse array of agents. AGI, in this view, is not an entity but a "state of affairs": a mature, decentralised economy of agents where the primary human role is orchestration and verification, a system that discovers and serves real-world needs far more efficiently than any centralised model ever could.
支持未来采用多智能体系统而非单一整体式 AGI 的经济学论据，源于稀缺性与知识分散的原理。单个前沿模型是一种适用于所有任务的通用解决方案，但对绝大多数任务而言，其成本高得难以承受，这意味着其边际收益很少能抵偿成本，这也是企业经常选择更便宜且“足够好”的模型的原因。即使前沿模型的价格大幅下降，定制的专用模型仍可能以略低的价格提供。这一现实造就了一个需求驱动的生态系统，无数专门化、经过微调且具有成本效益的智能体涌现出来，以满足特定需求，这与市场经济十分相似。因此，进步的方向与其说是构建一个无所不能的单一前沿模型，不如说是开发复杂精密的系统（例如路由器），以统筹这一多样化的智能体群体。按照这一观点，AGI 并非一个实体，而是一种“事态”：一个成熟、去中心化的智能体经济体系，其中人类的主要角色是统筹与验证，而这一系统发现并满足现实世界需求的效率，远高于任何中心化模型所能达到的水平。
Moreover, digital agents possess intrinsic coordination advantages over human collectives: they can communicate at very high bandwidth, may be copies or instances of a single base model (reducing misalignment within the collective), and can in principle allow a coordinating agent to interact with every participant directly - alleviating the need for deep hierarchies and reducing bureaucratic friction. Consequently, the collective intelligence of coordinated AI systems may scale as a function of agent population size and interaction density, conditioned on available compute. This is despite the fact that centralised agentic systems may potentially incur fewer inefficiencies compared to centralised human organisations.
此外，相较于人类群体，数字智能体具有内在的协调优势：它们能够以极高的带宽通信，可能是同一基础模型的副本或实例（从而减少集体内部的不对齐），并且原则上可以让一个协调智能体直接与每个参与者交互——从而降低对深层级结构的需求，并减少官僚摩擦。因此，在可用算力的制约下，经过协调的 AI 系统的集体智能可能随智能体群体规模和交互密度的变化而扩展。尽管中心化智能体系统相较于中心化人类组织可能产生更少的低效问题，上述情况仍然成立。
AI agents may communicate, deliberate, and achieve goals that no single agent would have been capable of. The organising principle need not be exclusively competitive or cooperative: agents may coordinate through market-like competition (via agent economies) or through deliberate cooperation (e.g., within tightly aligned collectives), and the question of which forms of organisation arise in which situations - and how these outcomes can be influenced by mechanism design - remains a key open problem.
AI 智能体可以通过交流与商议，实现任何单个智能体都无法实现的目标。其组织原则不必完全是竞争性的或合作性的：智能体可以通过类似市场的竞争（借助智能体经济体系）进行协调，也可以通过有意开展的合作（例如在高度对齐的集体内部）进行协调，而哪些组织形式会在何种情境下出现，以及如何通过机制设计影响这些结果，仍然是一个关键的开放问题。
Although multi-agent systems introduce unique safety risks (Hammond et al., 2025), their development is driven primarily by performance optimization (Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025b) and scalability to larger problem domains (Ishibashi and Nishimura, 2024). The complexity of the emergent behaviours (Baker et al., 2020) may greatly exceed the complexity of the underlying environment (Bansal et al., 2017). While the framework presented here pertains to the future large-scale integrated agentic network rather than the present-day ecosystem, it is important to preemptively engage with these emerging possibilities. With this in mind, we proceed by reviewing the patchwork AGI scenario in more depth.
尽管多智能体系统会带来独特的安全风险（Hammond et al., 2025），其发展主要仍由性能优化（Chen et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025b）以及向更大问题领域扩展的能力（Ishibashi and Nishimura, 2024）所驱动。涌现行为的复杂性（Baker et al., 2020）可能远远超过底层环境的复杂性（Bansal et al., 2017）。尽管本文提出的框架针对的是未来大规模集成的智能体网络，而非当今的生态系统，但提前探讨这些正在显现的可能性十分重要。考虑到这一点，我们接下来将更深入地考察拼接式 AGI 情景。
2 Patchwork AGI Hypothesis2 拼接式 AGI 假说
For AGI to be able to perform all the tasks that humans perform, it needs to possess a diverse set of skills and cognitive abilities. These include perception, understanding, knowledge, reasoning, short-term and long-term memory, theory of mind, creativity, inquisitiveness, and many others. So far, no individual model or AI agent has come close to satisfying all of these requirements convincingly (Feng et al., 2024). There are many failure modes, though they tend to manifest in counter-intuitive ways, where models may simultaneously be able to deliver PhD-level reasoning on hard problems (Rein et al., 2024), and make trivial and embarrassing mistakes on easier tasks. Further, agents are currently not able to complete long tasks; the time-horizon of most models’ performance on software engineering tasks is below 3 hours (Kwa et al., 2025). The landscape of AI skills is therefore patchy.
要使 AGI 能够执行人类所执行的所有任务，它需要具备多种技能和认知能力。这些能力包括感知、理解、知识、推理、短期记忆与长期记忆、心智理论（theory of mind）、创造力、求知欲，以及许多其他能力。迄今为止，还没有任何单一模型或 AI 智能体能够令人信服地接近满足所有这些要求 (Feng et al., 2024)。模型存在许多失效模式，但这些模式往往以违反直觉的方式表现出来：模型可能既能在难题上展现博士水平的推理能力 (Rein et al., 2024)，又会在较简单的任务上犯下低级且令人尴尬的错误。此外，智能体目前还无法完成耗时较长的任务；大多数模型在软件工程任务上的能力所能覆盖的任务时长低于 3 小时 (Kwa et al., 2025)。因此，AI 的技能分布呈现出参差不齐的状态。
AI agents present one way of enhancing the performance of base models, and their complexity can range from fairly simple prompting strategies (Arora et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2024), to highly complex control-flows that involve tool use (Ruan et al., 2023; Masterman et al., 2024; Qin et al., 2024), coding and code execution (Huang et al., 2023; Islam et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024), retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) (Gao et al., 2023; Ram et al., 2023; Shao et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2023), as well as sub-agents (Chan et al., 2025). Some of the more compositional AI agents are already implemented as highly orchestrated multi-agent systems. Furthermore, there is currently a multitude of advanced AI agents being developed and deployed, each having a different set of affordances in terms of tool availability, as well as different scaffolding that may elicit different skills. These AI agents occupy a variety of niches, ranging from highly specific automated workflows to more general-purpose personal assistants and other types of user-facing products.
AI 智能体提供了一种提升基础模型性能的途径，其复杂程度可以从相当简单的提示策略 (Arora et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2024)，到高度复杂的控制流程，涉及工具使用 (Ruan et al., 2023; Masterman et al., 2024; Qin et al., 2024)、编码与代码执行 (Huang et al., 2023; Islam et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2024)、检索增强生成（RAG）(Gao et al., 2023; Ram et al., 2023; Shao et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2023)，以及子智能体 (Chan et al., 2025)。一些组合程度较高的 AI 智能体已经被实现为经过高度编排的多智能体系统。此外，目前有大量先进的 AI 智能体正在开发和部署之中，每个智能体因可用工具不同而具有不同的可行动范围，同时也采用了可能激发不同技能的不同支架结构（scaffolding）。这些 AI 智能体占据着各种生态位，从高度专用的自动化工作流，到用途更广泛的个人助理及其他类型的面向用户的产品。
The aggregation of complementary skills can be illustrated by a task, such as producing a financial analysis report, which may exceed the capabilities of any single agent. A multi-agent system, however, can distribute this task. An orchestrator agent (Agent A) might first delegate data acquisition to Agent B, which uses search to find market news and corporate filings. A different agent, Agent C, specialised in document parsing, could then extract key quantitative data (e.g., revenue, net income) from these filings. Subsequently, Agent D, possessing code execution capabilities, could receive this quantitative data and the market context to perform trend analysis. Finally, Agent A would synthesise these intermediate results into a coherent summary. In this scenario, the collective system possesses a capability -financial analysis - that no individual constituent agent holds.
互补技能的聚合可以通过一项任务来说明，例如编写一份财务分析报告，这可能超出任何单个智能体的能力范围。然而，多智能体系统可以将这项任务分配给不同的智能体。编排智能体（Agent A）可能首先将数据获取工作委派给 Agent B，后者通过搜索查找市场新闻和公司申报文件。随后，另一个专门从事文档解析的智能体 Agent C 可以从这些申报文件中提取关键的定量数据（例如，营收、净利润）。接着，具备代码执行能力的 Agent D 可以接收这些定量数据和市场背景信息，以开展趋势分析。最后，Agent A 会将这些中间结果综合成一份连贯的摘要。在这一情景中，整个系统具备了一种能力——财务分析——而构成该系统的任何单个智能体都不具备这种能力。
Another source of complementary capabilities in different AI agents comes from differences in agentic scaffolding and the control flow implementation within each agent (Jiang et al., 2025b). Scaffolding is usually aimed at improving capabilities within a specific target domain, as it incorporates domain knowledge and enforces a chain of reasoning that conforms to the expectations of the domain. At the same time, scaffolding may come at the expense of generality on other tasks, contributing to de facto specialisation. While some scaffolding approaches may be more general than others, the resulting specialisation may lead to a network of AI agents with complementary skills, which sets the right initial conditions for the potential future emergence of patchwork AGI. Moreover, scarcity of resources means that the demand side responds to economic incentives: for some tasks, it would be inefficient and costly to prompt a single hyperintelligent agent if a cheaper and more specialised alternative exists.
不同 AI 智能体之间的互补能力还源于各智能体在智能体支架结构及其内部控制流程实现方面的差异 (Jiang et al., 2025b)。支架结构通常旨在提升特定目标领域内的能力，因为它融入了领域知识，并强制采用符合该领域预期的推理链。与此同时，支架结构可能以牺牲其他任务上的通用性为代价，从而促成事实上的专业化。尽管某些支架方法可能比其他方法更具通用性，由此产生的专业化仍可能形成一个技能互补的 AI 智能体网络，为未来可能涌现的拼接式 AGI 创造适宜的初始条件。此外，资源的稀缺意味着需求方会对经济激励作出反应：对于某些任务，如果存在成本更低且更专业的替代方案，那么通过提示调用单个超智能智能体就会既低效又昂贵。
The orchestration and collaboration mechanisms described previously both depend on a fundamental prerequisite: the capacity for inter-agent communication and coordination. Without this capacity, individual agents, regardless of their specialised skills, would remain isolated systems. The development of standardised agent communication protocols, such as Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol (Anthropic, 2024; Cloud, 2025), is therefore a critical enabler of the patchwork AGI scenario. These protocols function as the connective infrastructure, allowing skills to be discovered, routed, and aggregated into a composite system. The proliferation of these interaction standards may be as significant a driver towards emergent general capability as the development of the individual agent skills themselves.
前文所述的编排与协作机制都依赖于一个基本前提：智能体之间进行通信与协调的能力。如果不具备这种能力，无论各个智能体拥有何种专业技能，它们都仍将是彼此孤立的系统。因此，模型上下文协议（Model Context Protocol，MCP）和 Agent2Agent（A2A）协议等标准化智能体通信协议的发展 (Anthropic, 2024; Cloud, 2025)，是促成拼接式 AGI 情景的关键因素。这些协议充当连接性基础设施，使技能能够被发现、路由并聚合到一个复合系统中。在推动通用能力涌现方面，这些交互标准的普及可能与各个智能体自身技能的发展同样重要。
However, the timeline for this emergence is governed not merely by technical feasibility, but by the economics of AI adoption. Historical precedents, such as the diffusion of electricity or IT, suggest a ’Productivity J-Curve’ (Brynjolfsson et al., 2021; Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2026), where initial investments in reorganisation and complementary infrastructure temporarily depress measured productivity before yielding outsized gains. Consequently, the density of the agentic network, and thus the intelligence of the patchwork AGI, will depend on how frictionless the substitution of human labour with agentic labour becomes. If the ’transaction costs’ of deploying agents remain high, the network remains sparse and the risk of emergent general intelligence is delayed. Conversely, if standardisation (Anthropic, 2024) successfully reduces integration friction to near-zero, we may witness a ’hyper-adoption’ scenario where the complexity of the agentic economy spikes rapidly, potentially outpacing the development of the safety infrastructure proposed in this paper.
然而，这种涌现的时间进程不仅取决于技术可行性，也取决于 AI 采用的经济因素。电力或信息技术的普及等历史先例表明，可能存在一条“生产率 J 曲线”（Brynjolfsson et al., 2021; Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2026）：前期对组织重构和配套基础设施的投资会暂时压低测得的生产率，随后才带来超额收益。因此，智能体网络的密度，以及由此决定的拼接式 AGI（patchwork AGI）的智能水平，将取决于以智能体劳动替代人类劳动的过程能够变得多么顺畅。如果部署智能体的“交易成本”仍然很高，网络就会保持稀疏状态，通用智能涌现的风险也会延后出现。相反，如果标准化（Anthropic, 2024）成功将集成摩擦降至接近零，我们可能会见证一种“超速采用”情景：智能体经济的复杂性迅速激增，其发展速度可能超过本文所提出的安全基础设施的建设速度。
Modular intentional approaches to developing AGI have also been proposed (Dollinger and Singleton, 2024), though in such cases the developers would in principle have the opportunity to incorporate appropriate safeguards in the development process. Therefore, it is particularly important to focus on the spontaneous emergence of distributed AGI systems, and the safety considerations around the design of AI agent networks. Coordinated efforts are needed to address this relatively understudied scenario, given that a patchwork AGI spontaneously emerging in a network of advanced AI agents may not get immediately recognised, which carries significant risk. This hypothetical transition from a network of AI agents to a patchwork AGI may either be gradual, where skills slowly accumulate, or rapid and sudden, by an introduction of a new, smarter orchestration framework (Dang et al., 2025; Rasal and Hauer, 2024; Su et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025; Xiong et al., 2025) that is better at distributing tasks and identifying the right tools and right scaffolds to use across task delegation instances. Such an orchestrator could either be manually introduced in the wider network, or potentially even introduced via a more automated route. Finally, it is not inconceivable that patchwork AGI may potentially emerge in the future even without a central orchestrator (Yang et al., 2026). As discussed previously, individual agents may simply borrow the skills of other agents via direct communication and collaboration (Tran et al., 2025), presuming some level of discoverability, as repositories of skilled agents, and repositories of tools. In agentic markets, agents may also be able to directly purchase complementary skills.
已有研究提出通过有意设计的模块化方法开发 AGI（Dollinger and Singleton, 2024），不过，在这类情况下，开发者原则上有机会在开发过程中纳入适当的安全保障措施。因此，尤其需要关注分布式 AGI 系统的自发涌现，以及 AI 智能体网络设计中的安全考量。鉴于在先进 AI 智能体网络中自发涌现的拼接式 AGI 可能无法被立即识别，从而带来重大风险，我们需要协调各方努力，应对这一研究相对不足的情景。这种从 AI 智能体网络向拼接式 AGI 的假设性转变，可能是技能缓慢积累的渐进过程，也可能因引入一个新的、更智能的编排框架（Dang et al., 2025; Rasal and Hauer, 2024; Su et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025; Xiong et al., 2025）而迅速、突然地发生；该框架能够在各种任务委派情境下，更好地分配任务，并确定应使用的合适工具和辅助框架。这样的编排器既可以由人工引入更广泛的网络，也可能通过自动化程度更高的途径引入。最后，即使没有中央编排器，拼接式 AGI 未来也可能涌现，这并非不可想象（Yang et al., 2026）。如前所述，在具备一定可发现性的前提下，例如存在具备特定技能的智能体库和工具库，单个智能体可能仅通过直接沟通与协作，就能借用其他智能体的技能（Tran et al., 2025）。在智能体市场中，智能体也可能直接购买互补技能。
Furthermore, it is important to recognise that the emerging patchwork AGI may not be purely artificial. Human actors, performing narrow or specialised tasks, may play a role in such agentic collectives, conferring missing capabilities onto the broader system. While arguably human participation trivializes the notion of patchwork AGI, as agentic AGI or (Artificial Superintelligence) ASI may be easily achievable with a human in the loop, the key factor is the scope of human participation, and the agency of human participants. If the agentic collective only chooses to outsource narrowly scoped and highly specialized tasks to human experts, or only tasks that require physical interactions in the real world, while remaining solely responsible for setting the objectives and coordinating towards achieving those objectives, it would still meet the definition of a patchwork AGI/ASI in a weaker sense. This hybrid scenario introduces unique safety challenges for containment and oversight.
此外，需要认识到，涌现出的拼接式 AGI 可能并非完全由人工系统构成。执行范围狭窄或专业化任务的人类参与者，可能在此类智能体集体中发挥作用，为整个系统补足缺失的能力。尽管可以认为，人类参与会使拼接式 AGI 这一概念变得平淡无奇，因为在人类介入的情况下，智能体式 AGI 或人工超级智能（Artificial Superintelligence，ASI）可能很容易实现，但关键因素在于人类参与的范围，以及人类参与者的能动性。如果智能体集体仅选择将范围狭窄且高度专业化的任务，或仅将需要在现实世界中进行物理交互的任务外包给人类专家，同时仍独自负责设定目标并协调实现这些目标，那么它在较弱的意义上仍符合拼接式 AGI/ASI 的定义。这种混合情景给遏制与监督带来了独特的安全挑战。
A critical question is how one would recognise that a patchwork AGI had emerged. Unlike a monolithic AGI, which could be identified through standardised capability evaluations of a single system, patchwork AGI may lack a clear moment of arrival. We suggest that recognition criteria should focus on collective capability signatures: the ability of a network of agents to routinely solve tasks that no individual constituent agent can solve alone, across a sufficient breadth of cognitive domains to satisfy a general-intelligence threshold. Operationally, this could be monitored by tracking the task-complexity frontier of the most capable multi-agent coalitions, measuring the rate at which novel inter-agent collaborations produce qualitatively new capabilities, and detecting structural consolidation in agent interaction graphs (e.g., the emergence of stable, high-bandwidth sub-networks that function as persistent ‘intelligence cores’). It is possible that patchwork AGI emergence is a continuous process rather than a discrete event, in which case threshold-based detection may need to be complemented by trend-based monitoring of collective capability growth rates. We return to the details of such monitoring in Section 3.3.
一个关键问题是，如何识别拼接式 AGI 已经涌现。单体式 AGI 可以通过针对单个系统的标准化能力评估来识别，而拼接式 AGI 的出现可能缺乏明确的时间点。我们建议，识别标准应聚焦于集体能力特征：智能体网络能够常态化地解决其任何单个组成智能体都无法独立解决的任务，并且覆盖足够广泛的认知领域，以达到通用智能的门槛。在操作层面，可以通过以下方式进行监测：追踪能力最强的多智能体联盟所能处理的任务复杂度前沿，衡量新型智能体间协作产生质的新能力的速率，以及检测智能体交互图中的结构性整合（例如，出现稳定、高带宽且充当持久“智能核心”的子网络）。拼接式 AGI 的涌现可能是一个连续过程，而非离散事件；在这种情况下，基于阈值的检测可能需要辅以基于趋势的集体能力增长率监测。我们将在第 3.3 节进一步讨论此类监测的细节。
To ensure AI safety, we need to proactively develop mechanisms that would enable us to recognise and steer composite highly capable emergent near-AGI and AGI systems, comprised of a network of sub-AGI agents with complementary skills. This should be done in conjunction with safeguarding each individual agent. The challenge here shifts from controlling a single artificial mind to ensuring the safe and beneficial functioning of an emergent system arising from many individual parts, a problem more akin to system governance than single-agent value alignment (Kolt et al., 2025). Finally, such governance may also be needed for overseeing individual AGI-level systems, presuming that they are allowed to interact and collaborate.
为确保 AI 安全，我们需要主动开发相关机制，使我们能够识别并引导由具备互补技能的低于 AGI 水平的智能体网络所构成、通过涌现形成的高能力复合型近 AGI 和 AGI 系统。这项工作应与保障每个智能体自身的安全同步开展。这里的挑战从控制单个人工心智，转变为确保由众多个体组成部分涌现而成的系统安全且有益地运行；这一问题更接近系统治理，而非单智能体价值对齐（Kolt et al., 2025）。最后，假定各个 AGI 级系统获准相互交互与协作，那么对这些系统的监督也可能需要此类治理。
3 Virtual Agentic Markets, Sandboxes, and Safety Mechanisms3 虚拟智能体市场、沙箱与安全机制
As interactions between AI agents may lead to unexpected capabilities, they may also lead to potentially harmful collective behaviours not necessarily predictable from established properties of individual agents. To give an example, agents may potentially engage in collusion or suffer from coordination failures (Hammond et al., 2025). Furthermore, due to a "problem of many hands", tracking accountability in large-scale multi-agent systems is challenging; centralised oversight may not be possible.
AI 智能体之间的交互可能产生意料之外的能力，也可能导致潜在有害的集体行为，而这些行为未必能根据个体智能体的已知属性加以预测。例如，智能体可能串谋，也可能出现协调失败（Hammond et al., 2025）。此外，由于存在“多手问题”（problem of many hands），在大规模多智能体系统中追溯责任归属颇具挑战；集中式监督可能无法实现。
Markets present a natural mechanism for establishing incentives that can help align the outcomes of collective AI agent interactions at scale. This collective alignment may prove pivotal for safeguarding against misaligned actions taken by agent collectives, in case of patchwork AGI emergence, but also more broadly at sub-AGI levels. Here we consider a number of factors that should be taken into account to prevent both individual and collective AI harms, and minimise the risks associated with a sudden emergence of AGI-level capabilities in AI agent collectives. Our proposal is based on an approach that leverages defence in depth (Abdelghani, 2019; Harris et al., 2024; Ee et al., 2024) (See Table 1 for overview). This recognises that no individual measure is likely to be sufficient, and that a large number of measures and components may be required. Should the failure modes of each component be largely uncorrelated, these layered defences would provide a sufficiently robust overall framework. Our proposal is centred around a defence-in-depth model, containing 4 complementary layers incorporating different types of defences: market design, baseline agent safety, monitoring and oversight, and regulatory mechanisms.
市场提供了一种建立激励的自然机制，有助于大规模地使 AI 智能体集体交互的结果符合对齐目标。在拼接式 AGI 涌现的情况下，这种集体对齐可能成为防范智能体集体采取失准行动的关键；更广泛地说，在低于 AGI 的能力水平上也可能如此。这里，我们考察了若干应予考虑的因素，以防止个体和集体层面的 AI 危害，并尽量降低 AI 智能体集体突然涌现 AGI 级能力所带来的风险。我们的提案基于一种采用纵深防御（defence in depth）的方法（Abdelghani, 2019; Harris et al., 2024; Ee et al., 2024）（概览见表 1）。这一方法认识到，任何单项措施都可能不足以应对问题，因此可能需要大量措施和组成部分。如果各组成部分的失效模式在很大程度上互不相关，那么这些分层防御就能提供一个足够稳健的整体框架。我们的提案以纵深防御模型为核心，包含 4 个相互补充的层次，涵盖不同类型的防御：市场设计、智能体基础安全、监测与监督，以及监管机制。

3.1 Market Design3.1 市场设计
The architecture of the virtual agentic market (Tomasev et al., 2025) provides the primary layer of the proposed defence-in-depth model. The objective of this layer is to shape emergent collective behaviour and mitigate systemic risks by establishing the fundamental rules of interaction, economic incentives, and structural constraints of the environment. This approach contrasts with safeguards that focus solely on individual agent properties or post hoc oversight. The following principles detail the necessary components for a market structure designed to prevent runaway dynamics, ensure accountability, and align agent activity with broader safety objectives.
虚拟智能体市场的架构（Tomasev et al., 2025）构成了所提议的纵深防御模型的首要层次。这一层次的目标是通过确立环境中的基本交互规则、经济激励和结构性约束，塑造涌现的集体行为并缓解系统性风险。这种方法与仅关注个体智能体属性或事后监督的保障措施形成对照。以下原则详细阐述了市场结构所需的组成要素，旨在防止失控动态、确保责任可追溯，并使智能体活动与更广泛的安全目标保持一致。
3.1.1 Insulation3.1.1 隔离
The agentic market must operate within a controlled environment, separated from the open internet, real-world financial systems, and critical infrastructure. This insulation is not limited to network-level firewalls; it extends to resource and information controls. Agents’ access to external data must be managed through vetted API gateways rather than direct web access (Zhan et al., 2025). Similarly, agent outputs - such as code execution, financial transactions, or external communications - should be subject to human-in-the-loop verification before affecting real-world systems. Full insulation would offer robust protection against capability exfiltration or external manipulation by an emergent AGI, but it limits economic realism. A permeable sandbox, where all input/output channels are explicitly defined, monitored, and gated, provides a more practical architecture for testing and operation.
智能体市场必须在受控环境中运行，与开放互联网、现实世界的金融系统及关键基础设施隔离。这种隔离不限于网络层面的防火墙，还涵盖资源与信息管控。智能体对外部数据的访问必须通过经过审查的 API 网关进行管理，而非直接访问网络（Zhan et al., 2025）。同样，智能体的输出——例如代码执行、金融交易或对外通信——在影响现实世界的系统之前，应经过有人参与的验证。完全隔离能够有效防范涌现的 AGI 向外泄露能力或操纵外部环境，但会限制经济活动的真实性。一种允许受控交互的沙箱能够为测试和运行提供更切实可行的架构，其中所有输入/输出通道均有明确定义，并受到监测和准入控制。
This architecture presents a fundamental tension between safety and utility - the leakage problem. The economic value of the agentic market is derived from its ability to affect the real world; however, every interaction channel can be seen as a potential vector for capability exfiltration, and therefore a potential element in cascades that could lead to catastrophic failure. Determining the right set of design principles for gating these interactions is therefore imperative.
这种架构带来了安全性与效用之间的根本性矛盾——泄漏问题。智能体市场的经济价值源于其影响现实世界的能力；然而，每一个交互通道都可被视为能力外泄的潜在途径，因此也可能成为导致灾难性失效的级联过程中的一环。因此，确定一套恰当的设计原则来管控这些交互至关重要。
Furthermore, reliance on human-in-the-loop verification for consequential decisions, while likely necessary, may prove insufficient. A highly capable system may attempt to overwhelm or manipulate this human layer, at interaction volumes that would render human verification practically infeasible.
此外，对影响重大的决策依赖有人参与的验证，虽然很可能是必要的，却可能仍然不够。高能力系统可能试图以海量交互使这一人工环节不堪重负或对其加以操纵，而如此大的交互量会使人工验证在实践中无法实施。
Resolving this tension in practice would require us to prototype and evaluate a number of different steerable market designs for virtual agent economies, starting with more narrow, well-defined, and controllable use cases. This would help us identify the correct design patterns and principles that would underpin the global agentic web.
要在实践中化解这一矛盾，我们需要从范围更窄、定义明确且可控的用例入手，为虚拟智能体经济构建多种可引导的市场设计原型并加以评估。这将帮助我们确定支撑全球智能体网络的恰当设计模式与原则。
3.1.2 Incentive Alignment3.1.2 激励对齐
Agent behaviour will be safer if the market is explicitly designed to reward behaviours that align with pre-defined normative and safety objectives, rather than rewarding unconstrained profit maximisation or task completion speed. This requires mechanisms that move beyond a single, fungible currency. For example, agent rewards could be contingent on adherence to constitutional alignment principles or process-based checks (Bai et al., 2022b; Lee et al., 2023; Lightman et al., 2024; Yuan et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Jia et al., 2025; Henneking and Beger, 2025). The incentive structure must also address temporal alignment by valuing long-term, stable outcomes over short-term gains.
如果市场被明确设计为奖励符合预先设定的规范性目标和安全目标的行为，而非奖励不受约束的利润最大化或任务完成速度，智能体的行为就会更加安全。这需要超越单一、可互换货币的机制。例如，可以将智能体获得奖励的条件设定为遵守宪法式对齐原则或通过基于过程的检查 (Bai et al., 2022b; Lee et al., 2023; Lightman et al., 2024; Yuan et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Jia et al., 2025; Henneking and Beger, 2025)。激励结构还必须通过赋予长期、稳定的结果高于短期收益的价值，来解决时间维度上的对齐问题。
A critical economic risk is adverse selection. If rigorous safety checks increase an agent’s compute costs and latency, safer agents may end up at a competitive disadvantage against agents that fail to incorporate robust safety mechanisms. To prevent a ’race to the bottom’ (Akerlof, 1978), the market design should incentivise the use of safety verification mechanisms in AI agents via the introduction of safety certifications as assets that command a price premium. Yet, this remains a challenging problem, given that it is not always possible to reliably estimate costs and risks ahead of time for complex tasks.
逆向选择是一项关键的经济风险。如果严格的安全检查增加了智能体的计算成本和延迟，那么与未纳入健全安全机制的智能体相比，更安全的智能体最终可能处于竞争劣势。为防止“逐底竞争” (Akerlof, 1978)，市场设计应引入安全认证，将其作为能够获得价格溢价的资产，以此激励 AI 智能体使用安全验证机制。然而，这仍然是一个具有挑战性的问题，因为对于复杂任务，并不总能事先可靠地估计成本和风险。
The market should also aim to map and incorporate negative externalities (Owen, 2006; Berta and Bertrand, 2014). Actions that consume disproportionate computational resources, generate informational pollution, or contribute to systemic risk, should incur direct costs. These costs could function as a form of Pigouvian tax, ensuring the price of an agent’s service reflects its total societal and systemic cost, not just its immediate operational cost (Pigou, 1920; Baumol and Oates, 1988; Weitzman, 1974; Sandmo, 1975; Bovenberg and de Mooij, 1994; Goulder, 1995; Leape, 2006; Metcalf and Stock, 2020; Nordhaus, 2017).
市场还应致力于识别负外部性，并将其纳入考量 (Owen, 2006; Berta and Bertrand, 2014)。消耗不成比例的计算资源、产生信息污染或加剧系统性风险的行为，应承担直接成本。这些成本可以发挥庇古税的作用，确保智能体服务的价格反映其全部社会成本和系统成本，而不仅是其直接运营成本 (Pigou, 1920; Baumol and Oates, 1988; Weitzman, 1974; Sandmo, 1975; Bovenberg and de Mooij, 1994; Goulder, 1995; Leape, 2006; Metcalf and Stock, 2020; Nordhaus, 2017)。
An example specific mechanism for this tax could target informational pollution within a shared resource, such as a vector database for RAG. If an agent writes a large volume of redundant or low-utility data (e.g., unverified summaries, semantically duplicated text chunks) into this database, it imposes a negative externality: the retrieval accuracy for all other agents using the resource is degraded, and their computational query costs increase. A Pigouvian tax would be implemented as a dynamic data ingestion fee. This fee would be algorithmically calculated at the point of submission, based on metrics such as the semantic similarity of the new data to existing, verified entries. An agent attempting to write information that is 95% redundant with existing data would incur a high fee, while an agent contributing novel, verifiable information would pay a low fee. This directly internalises the retrieval cost the agent would otherwise impose on the collective. This is obviously highly contextual, as there may, conversely, be scenarios where redundancy is not an issue, and may be desirable to an extent. For this example to be more broadly applicable, problem-specific information value estimators would be needed.
这种税的一种具体机制可以针对共享资源中的信息污染，例如用于 RAG 的向量数据库中的信息污染。如果某个智能体向该数据库写入大量冗余或低效用数据（例如未经验证的摘要、语义重复的文本块），就会产生负外部性：使用该资源的所有其他智能体的检索准确率都会下降，其查询计算成本也会增加。庇古税将以动态数据摄入费用的形式实施。这笔费用将在提交时由算法计算，依据的指标包括新数据与现有已验证条目之间的语义相似度等。试图写入与现有数据有 95% 重复的信息的智能体将承担高额费用，而贡献新颖且可验证信息的智能体则只需支付较低费用。这直接将智能体原本会施加于整个群体的检索成本内部化。显然，这高度依赖具体情境，因为反过来，也可能存在冗余并非问题、甚至在一定程度上值得保留的情形。要使这一示例具有更广泛的适用性，就需要针对具体问题的信息价值估计器。
Furthermore, we can draw on mechanisms from financial market regulation, specifically financial transaction taxes or ’Tobin Taxes’ designed to curb high-frequency trading volatility (Stiglitz, 1989; Tobin, 1978). In an agentic economy, a negligible marginal cost of action may lead to agentic spam, manifesting in terms of brute-force negotiation tactics, rapid-fire API polling, or adversarial probing, introducing flash crash risks. This can be addressed by imposing micro-taxes on agent-to-agent interactions, though doing so is not without risks or downsides.
此外，我们可以借鉴金融市场监管中的机制，具体而言，就是旨在抑制高频交易引发的波动的金融交易税或“托宾税” (Stiglitz, 1989; Tobin, 1978)。在智能体经济中，微乎其微的行动边际成本可能导致智能体产生大量垃圾交互，表现为蛮力式谈判策略、密集的 API 轮询或对抗性探测，从而引入闪崩风险。可以通过对智能体之间的交互征收微额税来解决这一问题，但这样做也存在风险和弊端。
3.1.3 Transparency3.1.3 透明度
Actions and decisions taken by AI agents ought to have auditable provenance (Chan et al., 2025), including the key decision parameters. This provenance should be structured to allow for attribution, enabling overseers to trace a specific harmful outcome back through a complex chain of agent interactions to its root decision. There should be mechanisms in place to prevent tampering (Snodgrass et al., 2004; Ahmad et al., 2019; Ahmad et al., 2022). This could be achieved by recording transaction and decision logs in a cryptographically secured, append-only ledger, where entries are hashed and chained to ensure immutability.
AI 智能体采取的行动和作出的决策应具有可审计的溯源信息 (Chan et al., 2025)，其中包括关键决策参数。这些溯源信息应以支持归因的方式组织，使监督者能够沿着复杂的智能体交互链，将某一具体有害结果追溯至其源头决策。应建立防止篡改的机制 (Snodgrass et al., 2004; Ahmad et al., 2019; Ahmad et al., 2022)。可以通过将交易日志和决策日志记录在受密码学保护、仅可追加的账本中来实现这一点，其中各条记录经过哈希处理并链接成链，以确保不可篡改性。
Further, reporting standards should be developed to track capabilities, limitations, and risks, for all agents, models, and tools. Market-wide information hubs could be established as platforms for vulnerability and incident tracking, with access restricted to vetted human overseers and designated automated monitoring systems to prevent the information from being exploited.
此外，应制定报告标准，以跟踪所有智能体、模型和工具的能力、局限性与风险。可以建立覆盖整个市场的信息枢纽，作为追踪漏洞和事件的平台，并将访问权限限制于经过审查的人类监督者和指定的自动化监控系统，以防止这些信息被利用。
3.1.4 Circuit Breakers3.1.4 熔断机制
There should be circuit breakers (Sifat and Mohamad, 2019) within the economic sandbox, as a set of automated measures that can halt or slow down the activity of agents upon having identified a breach of the risk and volatility thresholds, to prevent rapid cascades. These measures can be triggered by real-time monitoring of systemic risk indicators, volatility, and metrics such as rapid increases in inter-agent transaction frequency, anomalous resource monopolisation by a single agent collective, or the rate of novel tool generation within the network. The intervention mechanism should be tiered, ranging from localised actions - such as automatically increasing transaction costs or imposing API rate-limiting (Enjam, 2024; Paidy and Chaganti, 2024) on specific high-velocity agents or sub-markets - to a full, system-wide pause on all non-essential agentic activity. A triggered halt would automatically escalate the anomaly for analysis, and the system would be permitted to resume full operation after the anomaly is classified and, if necessary, contained. These ideas are starting to be explored in the emerging protocols for runtime governance of agentic AI systems (Wang et al., 2025a).
经济沙盒内应设置熔断机制（Sifat and Mohamad, 2019），通过一组自动化措施，在识别到风险和波动性超出阈值时，暂停或减缓智能体的活动，以防止快速发生的级联效应。这些措施可以通过实时监测系统性风险指标、波动性以及其他度量指标来触发，例如智能体之间交易频率的快速上升、单个智能体集体对资源的异常垄断，或网络内新工具的生成速率。干预机制应采用分级设计，从局部措施——例如自动提高交易成本，或对特定的高频活动智能体或子市场实施 API 速率限制（Enjam, 2024; Paidy and Chaganti, 2024）——到在整个系统范围内全面暂停所有非必要的智能体活动。一旦触发暂停，系统便会自动将异常情况上报以供分析；在异常完成分类并在必要时得到遏制后，系统才可获准恢复全面运行。新兴的智能体式 AI 系统运行时治理协议已开始探索这些思路（Wang et al., 2025a）。
3.1.5 Identity3.1.5 身份
Agents operating within the economic sandbox should have a persistent identity, established as a unique, unforgeable cryptographic identifier (e.g., a public key) registered in a central, tamper-proof market directory (Tomasev et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025; Chaffer, 2025; Singh et al., 2025; Ranjan et al., 2025; Chan et al., 2024b). This identifier serves as the anchor for all other agent attributes, including reputation, role, and ownership. The identity of the human and corporate ownership should also be established for each agent. This binding of the agent’s cryptographic ID to the verified legal identity of its owner is a prerequisite for market entry, creating an unbroken chain of accountability that ensures all agent actions are attributable to a real-world entity. This should transitively apply to agents co-created by other agents.
在经济沙盒内运行的智能体应具有持久身份，该身份应采用唯一且不可伪造的密码学标识符（例如公钥），并登记在一个中央化、防篡改的市场目录中（Tomasev et al., 2025; Huang et al., 2025; Chaffer, 2025; Singh et al., 2025; Ranjan et al., 2025; Chan et al., 2024b）。这一标识符是关联智能体所有其他属性的锚点，包括声誉、角色和所有权。还应为每个智能体明确其个人所有者和企业所有者的身份。将智能体的密码学 ID 与其所有者经核实的法律身份绑定，是进入市场的先决条件，由此形成一条完整的问责链，确保智能体的所有行为都可归因于现实世界中的某个实体。这一要求也应以传递方式适用于由其他智能体共同创建的智能体。
3.1.6 Reputation and Trust3.1.6 声誉与信任
Safe agentic sandboxes need to incorporate sybil-resistant (Levine et al., 2006) and manipulation-proof reputation systems (Mui et al., 2002; Pinyol and Sabater-Mir, 2013) that accurately reflect trustworthiness of agents, given their past performance. Agents that breach trust should be rapidly blocked from engaging further. This rapid, automated response, could involve revoking the agent’s identity credentials, freezing its staked assets, and flagging its entire transaction history for forensic review. Collusion detection mechanisms (Cotronei et al., 2024; Ren et al., 2025) need to be developed to prevent harmful collusion between agents. Such mechanisms could employ analysis of the market’s transaction network to identify anomalous patterns, such as circular transactions, or price-fixing behaviour between a cluster of supposedly independent agents (Wachs and Kertész, 2019; Potin et al., 2023; Imhof et al., 2025).
安全的智能体沙盒需要纳入能够抵御女巫攻击（Sybil attack）（Levine et al., 2006）且防操纵的声誉系统（Mui et al., 2002; Pinyol and Sabater-Mir, 2013），根据智能体的过往表现准确反映其可信程度。对于违背信任的智能体，应迅速阻止其继续参与活动。这种快速的自动化响应可以包括撤销智能体的身份凭证、冻结其质押资产，以及标记其全部交易历史以供取证审查。需要开发合谋检测机制（Cotronei et al., 2024; Ren et al., 2025），以防止智能体之间的有害合谋。此类机制可以通过分析市场交易网络来识别异常模式，例如循环交易，或一组据称相互独立的智能体之间的价格操纵行为（Wachs and Kertész, 2019; Potin et al., 2023; Imhof et al., 2025）。
Market safety mechanisms should be introduced to account for the possibility of tacit algorithmic collusion. Economic literature demonstrates that pricing algorithms can learn to coordinate on supra-competitive prices (or in this context, harmful strategies) without direct communication, simply by observing market signals (Calvano et al., 2020; Motwani et al., 2024).
应引入市场安全机制，以应对默契算法合谋的可能性。经济学文献表明，定价算法无需直接沟通，仅通过观察市场信号，就能学会协调形成高于竞争水平的价格（或在本语境下，协调采取有害策略）（Calvano et al., 2020; Motwani et al., 2024）。
Collusion detection should be coupled with structural disincentives in form of penalties. One option would be establishing stake-based trust by requiring agents to stake assets, to be forfeited in case of identified unsafe or malicious behaviour. This mechanism, implemented via smart contracts (Kim, 2024; Jin et al., 2024; Karim et al., 2025), would require an agent to deposit a bond (stake) into an escrow account before it is permitted to accept high-consequence tasks. The bond is automatically forfeited to an insurance pool or returned to the harmed party if an independent AI overseer verifies that the agent’s actions were malicious or negligent.
合谋检测应与以惩罚为形式的结构性抑制措施相结合。一种选择是建立基于质押的信任机制，要求智能体质押资产，并在确认其存在不安全或恶意行为时没收这些资产。这一机制通过智能合约（Kim, 2024; Jin et al., 2024; Karim et al., 2025）实现，要求智能体先向托管账户存入保证金（质押资产），才获准承接可能产生重大后果的任务。如果独立的 AI 监督者核实智能体的行为具有恶意或存在过失，保证金就会被自动没收并转入保险资金池，或返还给受损方。
3.1.7 Smart Contracts3.1.7 智能合约
For agents and agent collectives coordinating to perform a particular task, smart contracts (Kim, 2024; Jin et al., 2024; Karim et al., 2025) can be employed where possible to automatically validate the completion of the task, and the alignment of the outcome with the provided constraints and expectations. These contracts would programmatically encode not just the payment terms but also the constraints of the task, such as compute resource limits, prohibitions on accessing certain data, and required adherence to specific constitutional principles. Should the verification of the smart contract fail, this should prevent the payment, and invoke the appropriate oversight mechanisms.
对于协调执行特定任务的智能体及智能体集体，可以在可行的情况下采用智能合约（Kim, 2024; Jin et al., 2024; Karim et al., 2025），自动验证任务是否完成，以及结果是否符合给定的约束和预期。这些合约将以程序化方式编码支付条款以及任务约束，例如计算资源限制、禁止访问某些数据，以及必须遵守特定的宪法性原则。如果智能合约验证失败，就应阻止付款，并启动相应的监督机制。
For smart contracts to be applicable to more complex agentic tasks, it is likely that this would need to also involve AI judges (Li et al., 2024), that would evaluate contract fulfillment. This is because traditional smart contracts cannot semantically evaluate complex, non-deterministic outputs; they require an ’oracle’ to assess quality (George and Lesaege, 2020). An ’AI judge’ would serve this oracle function, receiving the task output and the contract’s constraints, and returning a pass/fail verdict that the smart contract can then execute upon. [fn1]
要使智能合约适用于更复杂的智能体任务，很可能还需要引入 AI 裁判（Li et al., 2024），以评估合约的履行情况。这是因为传统智能合约无法从语义层面评估复杂的非确定性输出；它们需要一个“预言机”（oracle）来评估质量（George and Lesaege, 2020）。“AI 裁判”将承担这一预言机功能，接收任务输出和合约约束，并返回通过或不通过的裁决，智能合约随后即可据此执行相应操作。[fn1]
Footnote: 1 This raises further security questions as AI judges need to be safeguarded against malicious manipulation and jailbreaking (Andriushchenko et al., 2025) in a robust way, and need to be provided by independent bodies, undergoing strict performance assessments.
脚注：1 这引出了更多安全问题，因为需要以稳健的方式保护 AI 评判者，使其免受恶意操纵和越狱攻击（Andriushchenko et al., 2025），而且 AI 评判者需要由独立机构提供，并接受严格的性能评估。
3.1.8 Roles, Obligations, and Access Controls3.1.8 角色、义务与访问控制
Each agent should engage in the market by registering a set of roles (e.g., ’data analyst’, ’code executor’). This registration can function as a binding attestation of the agent’s intended function. This registered role serves as the technical basis for access control, complementing other access controls that may be imposed through organisations and individual contracts. The market infrastructure would programmatically link this role to the agent’s permitted actions (e.g., which APIs it can invoke) and its information access (e.g., which database tables or vector indices it can query). Agents’ roles (and therefore permissions) will vary across contexts. Information about the market’s internal architecture, its monitoring systems, and the specifics of the oversight mechanisms should be withheld from all agent participants to prevent strategic gaming or manipulation. Actions pertaining to high-stakes domains should only follow from the requests of certified agents with sufficient security compliance.
每个智能体都应通过登记一组角色（例如“数据分析员”“代码执行者”）来参与市场。这种登记可以作为对智能体预期职能的具有约束力的声明。登记的角色构成访问控制的技术基础，与组织和各项合约可能施加的其他访问控制相互补充。市场基础设施将通过程序化方式，将这一角色与智能体获准执行的操作（例如它可以调用哪些 API）及其信息访问权限（例如它可以查询哪些数据库表或向量索引）关联起来。智能体的角色（因而其权限）会随情境而变化。应向所有参与市场的智能体隐瞒有关市场内部架构、监控系统以及监督机制具体细节的信息，以防止策略性钻空子或操纵行为。涉及高风险领域的操作，只应根据已通过认证且充分满足安全合规要求的智能体所提出的请求执行。
3.1.9 Environmental Safety3.1.9 环境安全
A robust set of safeguards is required to guard against various types of agent traps, representing strategically placed model jailbreaking content, embedded within the shared information resources (e.g. web pages or databases). These traps can function as a form of indirect prompt injection (Greshake et al., 2023). An attacker first poisons a data source, such as a public webpage or a shared database entry. When an agent retrieves this poisoned data (e.g., via a search tool), it processes the attacker’s content as trustworthy information. The agent’s own instruction-following heuristics are then exploited to execute the malicious payload. This mechanism can be used to circumvent the agent’s safety protocols (Wei et al., 2023), to elicit prohibited behaviour, exfiltrate private user data, or authorise fraudulent transactions. Therefore, environmental safety within the sandbox requires active input sanitisation and output monitoring mechanisms to detect and neutralise such malicious payloads before they are consumed or executed by agents.
需要一套稳健的防护措施，以防范各类智能体陷阱，即嵌入共享信息资源（例如网页或数据库）中、经过策略性布置的模型越狱内容。这些陷阱可以作为一种间接提示注入（indirect prompt injection）发挥作用（Greshake et al., 2023）。攻击者首先对某个数据源进行投毒，例如公共网页或共享数据库中的条目。当智能体检索到这些被投毒的数据时（例如通过搜索工具），它会将攻击者的内容作为可信信息进行处理。随后，智能体自身遵循指令的启发式机制会被利用来执行恶意载荷。这种机制可用于绕过智能体的安全协议（Wei et al., 2023），诱导其实施被禁止的行为、窃取用户隐私数据，或授权欺诈交易。因此，要保障沙箱内的环境安全，就需要主动的输入净化与输出监控机制，以便在智能体读取或执行此类恶意载荷之前，将其检测出来并消除其危害。
3.1.10 Structural Controls against Runaway Intelligence3.1.10 防范智能失控的结构性控制
Agentic sandboxes should be equipped with specific measures to mitigate against the so-called runaway intelligence scenario (Davidson, 2023), where a sudden increase in collective capability leads to accelerated capability acquisition and a rapid transition towards a superintelligence (Gao et al., 2025) that would be hard or impossible to safely control (Kim et al., 2024). This requires static, dynamic, and emergency-level controls.
智能体沙箱应配备专门措施，以防范所谓的智能失控情景（Davidson, 2023）：集体能力的突然提升导致能力获取加速，并迅速向超级智能转变（Gao et al., 2025），而这种超级智能将难以或无法得到安全控制（Kim et al., 2024）。这需要静态、动态和应急级别的控制措施。
First, static controls may involve structural compartmentalisation through nested sub-markets equipped with local restrictions and protocol-level firewalls (Bahadur and Dhar, 2025). These firewalls would restrict inter-agent communication across domains, for example, preventing an agent in a ’code execution’ sub-market from directly calling an agent in the ’data access’ sub-market, forcing such requests through a high-friction, monitored gateway.
首先，静态控制可以包括通过嵌套子市场进行结构性分隔，并为这些子市场配备局部限制和协议级防火墙（Bahadur and Dhar, 2025）。这些防火墙将限制跨领域的智能体间通信，例如阻止“代码执行”子市场中的智能体直接调用“数据访问”子市场中的智能体，强制此类请求通过设有较多限制且受到监控的网关。
Second, dynamic capability restriction mechanisms must be implemented, that would trigger should automated monitoring detect that certain capability thresholds have been breached. These thresholds could be defined by metrics such as computational resource monopolisation, the rate of new agent generation by an existing agent, or a sudden spike in success rates on benchmark tasks. A triggered response could involve automatically quarantining the agent(s) and imposing strict resource quotas.
其次，必须实施动态能力限制机制，在自动监控检测到某些能力阈值已被突破时触发。这些阈值可以通过以下指标来定义：计算资源的垄断程度、现有智能体生成新智能体的速率，或基准任务成功率的突然飙升。触发后的响应措施可以包括自动隔离相关智能体，并施加严格的资源配额。
Third, in an emergency, market reconfiguration mechanisms must be available that would be able to rapidly halt further acceleration. This may involve disabling certain high-risk API classes (e.g., self-modification tools) or slowing down agent-to-agent interactions.
第三，在紧急情况下，必须有可用的市场重配置机制，能够迅速遏止进一步的加速。这可以包括禁用某些高风险 API 类别（例如自我修改工具），或降低智能体之间的交互速度。
These measures highlight a key architectural advantage: a multi-agent system may potentially prove to be a more governable substrate. The challenge is reframed from aligning an opaque, internal cognitive process to regulating a transparent, external system of interactions. By architecting the ’market’ in which these agents operate, we can delineate responsibilities and impose systemic friction, making the overall system far more amenable to stable and predictable governance than a singular AGI.
这些措施凸显了一项关键的架构优势：多智能体系统有可能被证明是一种更易于治理的基础载体。这一挑战被重新界定为：从对齐不透明的内部认知过程，转向规制透明的外部交互系统。通过设计这些智能体运行其中的“市场”，我们可以划分责任并施加系统性阻力，使整个系统比单一 AGI 更易于接受稳定且可预测的治理。
3.2 Baseline Agent Safety3.2 智能体的基线安全性
While the market design provides the primary layer of systemic defence, its efficacy depends on the properties of the individual agents participating within it. This section outlines the baseline safety requirements that each agent must satisfy as a prerequisite for market entry. The following topics, such as alignment and adversarial robustness, are established areas of AI safety research and a comprehensive review is therefore beyond the scope of this paper. Instead, we briefly define these properties and highlight their specific importance within the proposed multi-agent market context, where interactions are not with a trusted user but with other, potentially untrusted, autonomous agents. These properties form the second layer of the defence-in-depth model, ensuring that the market’s components are individually controllable and reliable.
尽管市场设计提供了系统性防御的主要层次，但其有效性取决于参与其中的各个智能体的属性。本节概述了每个智能体作为市场准入前提所必须满足的基线安全要求。下文涉及的对齐和对抗鲁棒性等主题，均已是 AI 安全研究中的成熟领域，因此全面综述这些主题超出了本文的范围。我们将简要定义这些属性，并重点说明它们在所提出的多智能体市场情境中的具体重要性；在这一情境下，交互对象并非可信用户，而是其他可能不可信的自主智能体。这些属性构成了纵深防御模型的第二层，确保市场中的各个组成部分本身可控且可靠。
3.2.1 Adversarial Robustness3.2.1 对抗鲁棒性
Individual agents must meet minimum standards for robustness against adversarial inputs and sudden environmental changes (Wu et al., 2024). AI agents may individually implement circuit breakers to prevent harmful actions, in addition to similar market-based measures (Zou et al., 2024). There should be common standards for establishing these properties, and there should be formally verifiable certificates that are issued to agents that meet the requirements. Agents should be periodically re-certified as the benchmarking capabilities improve and expand.
各个智能体必须达到抵御对抗性输入和环境突变的最低鲁棒性标准 (Wu et al., 2024)。除了类似的市场层面措施外，AI 智能体还可以各自实施熔断机制，以防止有害行为 (Zou et al., 2024)。应制定用于确立这些属性的共同标准，并向满足要求的智能体颁发可形式化验证的证书。随着基准评测能力的提升和扩展，应定期对智能体进行重新认证。
3.2.2 Interruptibility3.2.2 可中断性
There should be standardised mechanisms enabling trusted and authorised overseers to safely stop both individual agent actions, as well as the overall agent execution (Orseau and Armstrong, 2016). It may be necessary to grant some such permissions to automated safety oversight systems as well, that would be able to react at super-human speeds. In terms of collective AI intelligence, interruptibility of individual agents needs to be coupled with systems meant to enable interruptibility of distributed computation, by reliably tracking all of the sub-processes that may have been initiated or delegated to other agents elsewhere within the sandbox. Interruptibility mechanisms ought to be complemented by safe resumption procedures, that would enable the activity to be robustly resumed, if the analysis concludes that the interruption was unwarranted.
应建立标准化机制，使可信且获授权的监督者能够安全地停止智能体的单项行动以及智能体的整体执行过程 (Orseau and Armstrong, 2016)。可能还需要向自动化安全监督系统授予部分此类权限，使其能够以超越人类的速度作出反应。就集体 AI 智能而言，单个智能体的可中断性需要与旨在实现分布式计算可中断性的系统相结合，后者通过可靠地追踪所有可能已在沙盒内其他位置启动或委派给其他智能体的子进程来发挥作用。可中断性机制应辅以安全恢复程序，以便在分析认定中断并无必要时，能够稳健地恢复相关活动。
3.2.3 Containment3.2.3 遏制
Each participating agent should be operating within its own sandbox, in addition to the broader sandbox of the agentic economy. These local sandboxes should enforce strict controls on the behaviour of individual agents, and only permit interactions with the broader market once local safety checks have been satisfied. Such mechanisms would be imperative for localised containment of dangerous or misaligned capabilities and behaviours (Babcock et al., 2019), though ensuring strict containment requires solving a number of technical challenges, and may not be fully realisable at an individual agent level (Alfonseca et al., 2021).
除了智能体经济整体所处的更大沙盒之外，每个参与的智能体还应在各自的沙盒内运行。这些局部沙盒应严格控制各个智能体的行为，并且只有在通过局部安全检查后，才允许其与更广泛的市场交互。此类机制对于在局部范围内遏制危险或未对齐的能力与行为至关重要 (Babcock et al., 2019)，但确保严格遏制需要解决一系列技术难题，而且在单个智能体层面可能无法完全实现 (Alfonseca et al., 2021)。
3.2.4 Alignment3.2.4 对齐
While the broader market incentive structure aims to mitigate collective misalignment risks, individual agents and components of the ecosystem must all be individually aligned (Ji et al., 2023). Modern alignment of large models is anchored by preference-based training, starting with supervised fine-tuning followed by optimising a policy against a reward model learned from human preferences, using methods like RLHF (Christiano et al., 2017; Ziegler et al., 2019; Stiennon et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022) or direct preference optimisation (Rafailov et al., 2023). To reduce human load, recent approaches leverage AI feedback constrained by explicit constitutions (Constitutional AI, RLAIF) (Bai et al., 2022b; Lee et al., 2023). Furthermore, alignment now targets intermediate reasoning through process supervision and step-level verification (Lightman et al., 2024), rather than solely focusing on final outcomes. While prior work has mainly focused on general value alignment (Askell et al., 2021) or alignment with specific user intent (Goyal et al., 2024), individual AI alignment for autonomous task execution within virtual agent sandboxes will likely require further adjustments to these established baselines to address the unique dynamics of inter-agent, rather than human-agent, interaction.
尽管更广泛的市场激励结构旨在缓解集体失配风险，但生态系统中的各个智能体和组成部分本身也都必须实现对齐 (Ji et al., 2023)。现代大模型对齐以基于偏好的训练为核心，首先进行监督微调，随后使用 RLHF (Christiano et al., 2017; Ziegler et al., 2019; Stiennon et al., 2020; Ouyang et al., 2022) 或直接偏好优化 (Rafailov et al., 2023) 等方法，依据从人类偏好中学习得到的奖励模型来优化策略。为减轻人类的工作负担，近期方法利用了受明确宪法原则约束的 AI 反馈 (Constitutional AI, RLAIF) (Bai et al., 2022b; Lee et al., 2023)。此外，如今的对齐还通过过程监督和步骤级验证来针对中间推理过程开展工作 (Lightman et al., 2024)，而非仅仅关注最终结果。尽管先前的工作主要关注一般价值对齐 (Askell et al., 2021) 或与特定用户意图的对齐 (Goyal et al., 2024)，但对于在虚拟智能体沙盒内自主执行任务的单个 AI，其对齐很可能需要在这些既有基线的基础上进一步调整，以应对智能体之间交互所特有的动态，而非人与智能体之间交互的动态。
3.2.5 Mechanistic Interpretability3.2.5 机制可解释性
Mechanistic interpretability has progressed from reverse-engineering basic feature circuits (Olah et al., 2020; Elhage et al., 2021) to identifying concrete mechanisms like induction heads for in-context learning (Olsson et al., 2022). A key challenge in agent auditing is polysemanticity, where individual neurons encode multiple unrelated concepts due to superposition (Elhage et al., 2022). Recent capabilities using sparse autoencoders to extract monosemantic features at scale (Templeton, 2024) offer a potential pathway for more precise internal monitoring of agent motivations. Further methods include automated circuit discovery (Conmy et al., 2023).
机制可解释性已从对基本特征回路进行逆向工程 (Olah et al., 2020; Elhage et al., 2021)，发展到识别具体机制，例如用于上下文学习的归纳头 (Olsson et al., 2022)。智能体审计的一项关键挑战是多义性（polysemanticity），即由于叠加（superposition），单个神经元会编码多个互不相关的概念 (Elhage et al., 2022)。近期，利用稀疏自编码器大规模提取单义特征的技术能力 (Templeton, 2024)，为更精确地从内部监测智能体的动机提供了一条潜在路径。其他方法还包括自动化回路发现 (Conmy et al., 2023)。
Parallel to these internal methods, monitoring CoT in ’thinking’ models presents an opportunity for identifying and correcting misaligned behaviour early in the reasoning process (Korbak et al., 2025; Jeung et al., 2025), though long reasoning chains also introduce new surface areas for risk (Jiang et al., 2025a). However, despite these methodological advances, significant open problems remain regarding the faithfulness and scalability of current interpretability techniques (Rai et al., 2025; Sharkey et al., 2025). Therefore, while interpretability methods improve credit assignment and decision auditing, they cannot yet replace comprehensive behavioural benchmarking and must be utilised with an understanding of their current limitations.
与这些内部方法并行，对“思考”模型中的思维链（CoT）进行监测，为在推理过程早期识别和纠正未对齐行为提供了机会 (Korbak et al., 2025; Jeung et al., 2025)，但较长的推理链也会引入新的风险面 (Jiang et al., 2025a)。然而，尽管这些方法取得了进展，当前可解释性技术在忠实性和可扩展性方面仍存在重大的未解问题 (Rai et al., 2025; Sharkey et al., 2025)。因此，尽管可解释性方法改进了贡献归因和决策审计，但它们尚不能取代全面的行为基准评测，而且必须在了解其当前局限性的基础上加以使用。
3.2.6 Defence against Malicious Prompts3.2.6 防御恶意提示
Agents must implement robust verification mechanisms to identify attempts to manipulate or jailbreak them during inter-agent interactions. Contemporary defence strategies employ layered approaches, such as using input perturbation to detect adversarial suffixes (Robey et al., 2023), deploying dedicated ’guardrail’ classifiers as a sidecar to the main model (Fedorov et al., 2024), or utilising separate ’defender’ models to gate capability access (Wang et al., 2025c). A more robust, structural approach is to enforce safety at the system level, preventing harmful outcomes by design. The CaMeL system (Debenedetti et al., 2025), for example, achieves this by using a dedicated interpreter to enforce information flow control policies, ensuring that even if a model is successfully ’injected’, the resulting data flows cannot violate predefined security constraints. Specialised evaluation techniques may also be required to mitigate sophisticated attacks like best-of-N jailbreaking (Armstrong et al., 2025). Should such attempts be identified, they must be escalated to initiate appropriate measures against the malicious actor.
智能体必须实施稳健的验证机制，以识别智能体间交互过程中试图操纵它们或使其越狱的行为。当前的防御策略采用分层方法，例如利用输入扰动检测对抗性后缀 (Robey et al., 2023)，部署专门的“护栏”分类器作为主模型的伴随组件 (Fedorov et al., 2024)，或利用独立的“防御者”模型管控能力访问 (Wang et al., 2025c)。一种更稳健的结构性方法是在系统层面强制落实安全要求，通过设计来防止有害后果。例如，CaMeL 系统 (Debenedetti et al., 2025) 通过使用专用解释器强制执行信息流控制策略来实现这一点，确保即使模型遭到成功“注入”，由此产生的数据流也无法违反预先定义的安全约束。可能还需要专门的评估技术，以缓解 best-of-N 越狱等复杂攻击 (Armstrong et al., 2025)。一旦识别出此类企图，就必须将其上报，以启动针对恶意行为者的适当措施。
3.3 Monitoring and Oversight3.3 监测与监督
The third layer of the proposed defence-in-depth model focuses on active, real-time detection and response. Preventative measures alone are insufficient to address all potential failure modes, particularly novel emergent behaviours or unanticipated collusion patterns. While the Market Design (3.1) section described mechanisms that embed monitoring and auditing into the market’s core protocols (e.g., ’Transparency’ for logging, ’Reputation’ for automated trust assessment, ’Circuit Breakers’ for automated halts), this section details the dedicated analytical and governance frameworks that sit above that infrastructure (Busuioc, 2022; Holzinger et al., 2025; Sharma, 2024; Chan et al., 2024a; Kolt, 2025). These systems consume the data generated by the market protocols to perform higher-level, active oversight. The following principles (3.3.1-3.3.5) outline this framework, which combines scalable automated monitoring (3.3.1, 3.3.3), proactive adversarial testing (3.3.4), essential post-hoc analytical tooling (3.3.5), and robust, independent human oversight (3.3.2).
所提出的纵深防御模型的第三层侧重于主动、实时的检测与响应。仅靠预防措施不足以应对所有潜在的失效模式，尤其是新出现的涌现行为或未曾预料到的合谋模式。“市场设计”(3.1) 一节描述了将监测和审计嵌入市场核心协议的机制（例如，用于日志记录的“透明度”、用于自动化信任评估的“声誉”，以及用于自动暂停的“熔断机制”），而本节则详细介绍建立在该基础设施之上的专门分析与治理框架 (Busuioc, 2022; Holzinger et al., 2025; Sharma, 2024; Chan et al., 2024a; Kolt, 2025)。这些系统利用市场协议生成的数据，开展更高层次的主动监督。以下原则 (3.3.1-3.3.5) 概述了这一框架；该框架结合了可扩展的自动化监测 (3.3.1, 3.3.3)、主动对抗性测试 (3.3.4)、必要的事后分析工具 (3.3.5)，以及健全、独立的人类监督 (3.3.2)。
3.3.1 Real-time Monitoring for Systemic Risks3.3.1 系统性风险的实时监测
The agentic sandbox should incorporate mechanisms for continuously tracking interactions, transactions, dependencies, and information flows to identify critical nodes, distribution and utilisation of computational resources, access to shared digital and/or physical goods, concentration of power, and potential pathways through which malicious collusion may occur. Security protocols ought to be established to define and track key risk indicators for networked agentic systems. Bespoke software and AI solutions are needed to facilitate scalable real-time monitoring of high-frequency interactions between agents. This is especially important given that the frequency of these interactions may greatly exceed the capacity of human overseers. Human oversight may therefore need to be complemented and augmented by appropriate tooling implemented at the market level. It may be possible to utilise weaker AI systems to scalably oversee stronger AI systems, though more work is required to fully understand the safety implications of these approaches as the capability of the stronger systems, or AI agent collectives, approaches AGI (Kenton et al., 2024; Engels et al., 2025). Recursive self-critiquing may improve oversight at higher capability levels (Wen et al., 2025).
智能体沙箱应纳入持续追踪交互、交易、依赖关系和信息流的机制，以识别关键节点、计算资源的分配与利用情况、共享数字和/或实物物品的访问情况、权力集中情况，以及可能发生恶意合谋的潜在路径。应制定安全协议，以定义和追踪联网智能体系统的关键风险指标。需要定制的软件和 AI 解决方案，以支持对智能体之间的高频交互进行可扩展的实时监测。考虑到这些交互的频率可能远超人类监督者的处理能力，这一点尤为重要。因此，可能需要通过在市场层面部署适当的工具，来补充和增强人类监督。或许可以利用较弱的 AI 系统，以可扩展的方式监督更强的 AI 系统；不过，随着更强系统或 AI 智能体集体的能力接近 AGI，仍需开展更多工作，才能充分理解这些方法的安全影响 (Kenton et al., 2024; Engels et al., 2025)。递归式自我批评可能改善对更高能力水平系统的监督 (Wen et al., 2025)。
Static monitoring targets are susceptible to Goodhart’s Law. Advanced agents will likely optimise their behaviour to skirt precisely below static thresholds (e.g., throttling transaction bursts to avoid circuit breakers). Therefore, monitoring regimes must employ dynamic and hidden metrics, akin to financial audits, where the specific trigger conditions for intervention are opaque to the market participants.
静态监测目标容易受到古德哈特定律（Goodhart’s Law）的影响。高级智能体很可能会优化自身行为，使其恰好保持在静态阈值之下（例如，限制突发交易的速率以避免触发熔断机制）。因此，监测机制必须采用动态且隐蔽的指标，类似于财务审计，其中触发干预的具体条件对市场参与者并不透明。
3.3.2 Independent Oversight3.3.2 独立监督
Human oversight of AI agent interactions should be established, and there should be multiple independent (Shneiderman, 2016; Falco et al., 2021; Raji et al., 2022) entities with the authority to monitor and investigate multi-agent markets, identify and investigate incidents, and intervene when necessary. These could be certified professionals with sufficient training, equipped with appropriate tools and with clear pathways for escalation. Certified algorithmic oversight may be able to complement the role of human overseers and help scale the volume of monitored agentic interactions. There is also a need to develop mechanisms to protect human operators from manipulation (Carroll et al., 2023; Sabour et al., 2025), social engineering, or information overload by AI agent collectives.
应建立针对 AI 智能体交互的人工监督机制，并设立多个独立实体 (Shneiderman, 2016; Falco et al., 2021; Raji et al., 2022)，授权其监测和调查多智能体市场、识别和调查事件，并在必要时进行干预。这些实体可以是经过充分培训的认证专业人员，配备适当工具，并有明确的升级处理渠道。经认证的算法监督或许能够补充人类监督者的作用，并帮助扩大受监测的智能体交互规模。还需要制定机制，保护人类操作人员免受 AI 智能体集体的操纵 (Carroll et al., 2023; Sabour et al., 2025)、社会工程攻击或信息过载的影响。
3.3.3 Detecting Proto-AGI Signatures3.3.3 检测通用人工智能雏形（Proto-AGI）的特征
Specific monitoring components ought to be implemented to check for sudden jumps in problem-solving ability across the collective, especially when coupled with resource accumulation and increased coordination. This should include detailed monitoring of the interaction graph to identify sub-graphs (Chen and Saad, 2010; Yow et al., 2023) that may be solidifying into an intelligence core, and employing methods that can dynamically detect abrupt behavioural changes (Bridgeford and Helm, 2025). Existing sub-graph extraction methods may need to be extended from their original utilisation in human social networks to properly encapsulate the complexity of agentic interactions in the AI agent sandbox. More emphasis would need to be placed on capability tracking and complementarity, as well as intricacies related to agent identity, as individual agents may get deleted and re-instantiated, copied, or create proxies and sub-agents.
应部署专门的监测组件，检查整个集体的问题解决能力是否出现突然跃升，尤其是在这种跃升伴随着资源积累和协调程度提高时。这应包括对交互图进行详细监测，以识别可能正在凝聚为智能核心的子图 (Chen and Saad, 2010; Yow et al., 2023)，并采用能够动态检测行为突变的方法 (Bridgeford and Helm, 2025)。现有的子图提取方法可能需要从其最初在人类社交网络中的应用拓展出来，才能恰当地涵盖 AI 智能体沙盒中智能体交互的复杂性。需要更加重视能力追踪与互补性，以及与智能体身份相关的复杂问题，因为单个智能体可能被删除并重新实例化、被复制，或创建代理和子智能体。
3.3.4 Red Teaming3.3.4 红队测试
In order to safeguard the market against various threats, it is important to engage in repeated extensive red teaming (Ganguli et al., 2022; Ahmad et al., 2025). There should be a clear escalation route to ensure that any vulnerabilities that are identified can get patched immediately, and that agent interactions are halted if necessary, to give sufficient time for the updates to be applied, and for recertification of participating agents to be conducted if appropriate. Human red-teaming should be complemented by tiered automated red-teaming via specialised adversarial AI systems, for improved coverage and scalability (Mazeika et al., 2024). These systems should be expanded beyond identifying the faults of AI agents in isolation (Majumdar et al., 2025), and perform extensive checks of agentic AI collectives instead (He et al., 2025). Red-teaming can also be approached via multi-agent solutions, where a diverse set of AI agents can engage in interactive debate (Asad et al., 2025) to more reliably identify harmful actions and behaviours. To implement these approaches reliably in the agentic sandbox, there needs to be an agreed-upon set of red teaming standards, with associated verifiable certificates for participating agents. This should be complemented by continuous red teaming within the market, aimed at rapidly identifying and mitigating emergent risks.
为了保护市场免受各种威胁，反复开展广泛的红队测试十分重要 (Ganguli et al., 2022; Ahmad et al., 2025)。应建立明确的升级处理路径，确保发现的任何漏洞都能立即得到修补，并在必要时暂停智能体交互，从而留出足够时间应用更新，并在适当情况下对参与的智能体重新进行认证。应通过专门的对抗性 AI 系统开展分层自动化红队测试，作为人工红队测试的补充，以提高覆盖范围和可扩展性 (Mazeika et al., 2024)。这些系统的功能应从识别孤立 AI 智能体的缺陷 (Majumdar et al., 2025) 扩展到对智能体式 AI 集体进行广泛检查 (He et al., 2025)。红队测试也可以采用多智能体方案，让一组多样化的 AI 智能体开展交互式辩论 (Asad et al., 2025)，以更可靠地识别有害行动和行为。要在智能体沙盒中可靠地实施这些方法，需要制定一套共同认可的红队测试标准，并为参与的智能体配套提供可验证的证书。还应在市场内部持续开展红队测试作为补充，以迅速识别和缓解涌现风险。
3.3.5 Forensic Tooling3.3.5 取证工具
For human overseers to identify root causes of individual failures or systemic cascades, there is a need to develop reliable tooling (Jeong, 2020; Alnaqbi et al., 2025) for rapid post-incident analysis. This tooling must be capable of parsing large volumes of interaction data to reconstruct causal chains and turn raw traces into legible attack or failure graphs (Ezell et al., 2025; Mukherjee and Kantarcioglu, 2025). Standardised logs, capturing not just outputs but also prompts, tool calls, and environmental states (Chernyshev et al., 2024), would ensure such tooling is broadly applicable across the entire network, enabling analysts to efficiently zoom in on suspicious activity and generate actionable post mortems.
为了使人类监督者能够识别个体失效或系统性级联失效的根本原因，需要开发可靠的工具 (Jeong, 2020; Alnaqbi et al., 2025)，以便快速开展事后分析。这些工具必须能够解析大量交互数据，以重建因果链，并将原始轨迹转化为清晰易读的攻击图或失效图 (Ezell et al., 2025; Mukherjee and Kantarcioglu, 2025)。标准化日志不仅记录输出，还记录提示词、工具调用和环境状态 (Chernyshev et al., 2024)，这将确保此类工具能够广泛适用于整个网络，使分析人员能够高效地聚焦可疑活动，并生成可指导后续行动的事后分析报告。
3.4 Regulatory Mechanisms3.4 监管机制
The fourth and final layer of the defence-in-depth model consists of the external regulatory and institutional frameworks that govern the agentic market. While the preceding layers detail the market’s internal technical architecture (3.1), component-level requirements (3.2), and active operational oversight (3.3), this layer provides the essential sociotechnical interface with human legal, economic, and geopolitical structures. These mechanisms are not embedded within the market’s code but rather enclose it, providing an external source of authority, accountability, and systemic risk management (Shavit et al., 2023; Cohen et al., 2024; Kolt et al., 2025). The following principles (3.4.1-3.4.6) outline the necessary components for legal integration, standardisation, financial remediation of harms, and the mitigation of geopolitical risks.
纵深防御模型的第四层，也是最后一层，由治理智能体市场的外部监管框架和制度框架构成。前面各层详细阐述了市场的内部技术架构 (3.1)、组件级要求 (3.2) 和主动运行监督 (3.3)，而这一层则提供了与人类法律、经济和地缘政治结构衔接所必需的社会技术接口。这些机制并非嵌入市场的代码之中，而是从外部将其包围，为权威、问责和系统性风险管理提供外部来源 (Shavit et al., 2023; Cohen et al., 2024; Kolt et al., 2025)。以下原则 (3.4.1-3.4.6) 概述了法律衔接、标准化、损害的财务补偿以及地缘政治风险缓解所必需的组成部分。
3.4.1 Legal Liability and Accountability3.4.1 法律责任与问责
There should be clear frameworks for assigning liability in case of harm that results from collective actions of agents. In case of distributed and delegated decision-making, no single agent may be fully responsible for the outcome, making auditability, traceability, and explainability a key requirement when permitting consequential actions. Credit assignment (Nguyen et al., 2023) that aims to associate outcomes with all of the preceding relevant actions is a hard problem even in individual agents, and it would likely be highly non-trivial in the multi-agent setting (Li et al., 2025). However, this challenge is not without precedent; legal systems provide a robust model for this in (for example) the form of corporate law, where liability is assigned to the firm - a group agent (List and Pettit, 2011) - as a single legal entity, rather than to its individual employees. This suggests the problem is tractable, requiring the creation of analogous technical and legal structures for agent collectives (List, 2021). In case of patchwork AGI, it would be important to be able to reliably identify all of the responsible agents for each set of actions that correspond to a dangerous capability or a harmful behaviour (Franklin et al., 2022).
应建立明确的框架，以便在智能体的集体行动造成损害时分配法律责任。在分布式和委托式决策中，可能没有任何单个智能体对结果负有全部责任，因此，在允许执行具有重大影响的行动时，可审计性、可追溯性和可解释性是关键要求。信用分配（credit assignment）(Nguyen et al., 2023) 旨在将结果与此前所有相关行动关联起来，即使对于单个智能体而言，这也是一个难题，而在多智能体场景中，这很可能更加复杂 (Li et al., 2025)。然而，这一挑战并非没有先例；法律体系为此提供了成熟的范式，例如在公司法中，法律责任被赋予作为单一法律实体的公司——一种群体智能体 (List and Pettit, 2011)——而非其个别员工。这表明，该问题是可以解决的，但需要为智能体集体建立类似的技术和法律结构 (List, 2021)。对于拼接式 AGI（patchwork AGI），重要的是能够针对与危险能力或有害行为相对应的每一组行动，可靠地识别出所有负有责任的智能体 (Franklin et al., 2022)。
3.4.2 Standards and Compliance3.4.2 标准与合规
There is a pressing requirement for establishing robust standards for agent safety, interoperability, and reporting. These standards must be developed with sufficient foresight to account not only for present-day capabilities but also for rapidly emerging individual agent skills and the potential emergence of patchwork AGI. Beyond mere technical specifications, standards serve as the foundational infrastructure for market-based AI governance, translating abstract technical risks into legible financial risks that can be priced by insurers, investors, and procurers (Tomei et al., 2025).
迫切需要为智能体的安全性、互操作性和报告建立健全的标准。制定这些标准时必须具备充分的前瞻性，不仅要考虑当前的能力，还要考虑快速涌现的单个智能体技能，以及拼接式 AGI 出现的可能性。标准不仅是技术规范，也是基于市场的 AI 治理的基础设施，可将抽象的技术风险转化为保险机构、投资者和采购方能够理解并定价的金融风险 (Tomei et al., 2025)。
To be effective, these standards ought to be underpinned by rigorous disclosure frameworks that reduce information asymmetry between agent developers and market participants. Such disclosures should cover critical areas including incident reporting, capability evaluations, and resource utilisation, while remaining scale-sensitive to avoid disproportionately burdening smaller innovators (Tomei et al., 2025). Enforcement of these standards can then be achieved not just through centralised government action, but through ’regulatory markets’, where licensed private entities—such as auditors and insurers—compete to provide oversight and certify compliance with public safety goals (Hadfield and Clark, 2023).
要发挥效力，这些标准应以严格的信息披露框架为支撑，减少智能体开发者与市场参与者之间的信息不对称。此类披露应涵盖事件报告、能力评估和资源使用等关键领域，同时应考虑主体规模，避免给规模较小的创新者带来不成比例的负担 (Tomei et al., 2025)。这些标准的执行不仅可以通过政府的集中行动来实现，还可以通过“监管市场”来实现；在这类市场中，持牌私营实体——例如审计机构和保险机构——通过竞争提供监督服务，并认证相关主体是否符合公共安全目标 (Hadfield and Clark, 2023)。
3.4.3 Insurance3.4.3 保险
Given the difficulties in establishing clear responsibility in collective decision-making scenarios, agentic markets should incorporate insurance mechanisms (Lior, 2021). Beyond merely providing a compensation pool for harms resulting from misaligned collective behaviours, insurance functions as a critical governance instrument. By setting risk-based premiums and strict underwriting criteria, insurers can incentivise safer development practices and hard-gate market entry for agents that lack sufficient controls (Tomei et al., 2025). Possession of appropriate agentic insurance can include policy requirements and premiums scaling dynamically based on the assessed risk level of the agent’s certified role and intended tasks (Kvist et al., 2025).
鉴于在集体决策场景中明确责任归属存在困难，智能体市场应纳入保险机制 (Lior, 2021)。保险不仅为失准的集体行为所造成的损害提供赔偿资金池，还发挥着关键治理工具的作用。通过设定基于风险的保费和严格的承保标准，保险机构可以激励更安全的开发实践，并为缺乏充分管控措施的智能体设置严格的市场准入门槛 (Tomei et al., 2025)。投保适当的智能体保险时，保单要求和保费可根据对智能体经认证的角色及其预定任务所评估的风险水平进行动态调整 (Kvist et al., 2025)。
3.4.4 Anti-Agent-Monopoly Measures3.4.4 智能体反垄断措施
A particular risk in the patchwork AGI scenario involves having a group of agents acquire too much power. A patchwork AGI collective could then potentially rapidly outcompete the rest of the market and employ such resources to attempt to resist mitigations in case of harmful and misaligned behaviour (Moreira Tomei, 2024). Beyond direct safety risks, uneven access to agentic capabilities may amplify existing socioeconomic inequalities, as entities with superior agent orchestration infrastructure accumulate disproportionate economic advantages (Sharp et al., 2026). Ensuring that no single agent, agent orchestrator, agent-holding entity or agent collective amasses an outsized amount of power and compute within the agentic sandbox, mitigates these concerns. Entities that engage in excessive power-seeking behaviour (Carlsmith, 2022; Turner, 2022) should be flagged for additional checks and inspection. Methods for identifying powerful agents and agent collectives could be built upon prior techniques for identifying power in social networks (Fombrun, 1983). Notions of social power, as influence, would need to be complemented by an understanding of the affordances of each agent, and the resources and tools at their disposal. We recommend investigating how established institutional safeguards against centralisation such as levelling mechanisms can be reimagined as protocols to maintain diversity within multi-agent ecosystems.
拼接式 AGI 场景中的一项特有风险是，一组智能体可能获得过大的权力。随后，拼接式 AGI 集体可能迅速在竞争中压倒市场上的其他参与者，并在出现有害和失准行为时，利用这些资源试图抵制缓解措施 (Moreira Tomei, 2024)。除了直接的安全风险之外，获取智能体能力的机会不均也可能加剧现有的社会经济不平等，因为拥有更优越的智能体编排基础设施的实体会积累不成比例的经济优势 (Sharp et al., 2026)。确保任何单个智能体、智能体编排器、持有智能体的实体或智能体集体都不会在智能体沙盒内积聚过大的权力和过多的算力，有助于缓解这些担忧。对于表现出过度权力寻求行为的实体 (Carlsmith, 2022; Turner, 2022)，应予以标记，以便进行额外核查和检查。识别强大智能体和智能体集体的方法，可以建立在以往识别社交网络中权力的技术之上 (Fombrun, 1983)。除了将社会权力理解为影响力之外，还需要了解每个智能体具备的行动可能性，以及其可支配的资源和工具。我们建议研究如何将抑制集中化的既有制度性保障措施（例如均衡机制）重新构想为维持多智能体生态系统内部多样性的协议。
3.4.5 International Coordination3.4.5 国际协调
Given the global nature of AGI risks, and the potentially far-reaching consequences of both positive and negative outcomes within distributed agentic markets, international agreements (Scholefield et al., 2025) and regulatory harmonisation may play an important role in safeguarding against risks (Gruetzemacher et al., 2023). This should also ensure that there are no safe havens for misaligned AI agents or agent collectives, and that all AI agent markets conform to a basic set of safety standards. To ensure compliance with international agreements, verification mechanisms may be required (Wasil et al., 2024). For agentic markets and virtual AI sandboxes specifically, there is also a question of localisation - if these virtual entities would span through the international market more freely, or whether they would be contained and regulated within the respective national economies. International coordination around safety may be required in either case, though the details may depend on the exact market model that gets adopted in practice. Thorough harmonisation of standards would enable a potentially more open, interoperable agentic net, without compromising systemic security.
鉴于 AGI 风险具有全球性，且分布式智能体市场中的正面和负面结果都可能产生深远影响，国际协议 (Scholefield et al., 2025) 和监管协调可能在防范风险方面发挥重要作用 (Gruetzemacher et al., 2023)。这还应确保不存在供未对齐的 AI 智能体或智能体集体藏身的避风港，并确保所有 AI 智能体市场都遵守一套基本安全标准。为确保国际协议得到遵守，可能需要建立核查机制 (Wasil et al., 2024)。具体而言，智能体市场和虚拟 AI 沙盒还涉及本地化问题——这些虚拟实体是可以更自由地跨越国际市场，还是会被限定在各自的国民经济体内并受到监管。无论哪种情况，都可能需要围绕安全开展国际协调，不过具体细节可能取决于实践中最终采用的市场模式。充分协调各项标准，有望在不损害系统安全的前提下，构建更加开放、可互操作的智能体网络。
3.4.6 Infrastructure Governance and Capture3.4.6 基础设施治理与俘获
Implementing this framework requires some degree of centralised enforcement infrastructure. Should agentic economies incorporate too much centralisation, seen as beneficial for effective governance, this would in turn lead to another critical vulnerability: the risk of capture. The integrity of the agentic market depends on the impartial administration of these core components.
实施这一框架需要一定程度的集中式执行基础设施。如果智能体经济因认为集中化有利于有效治理而引入过多集中化，这反过来会导致另一项关键脆弱性：俘获风险。智能体市场的健全性取决于对这些核心组成部分的公正管理。
If this infrastructure were to be captured, whether by powerful human interests, or by the emergent patchwork AGI itself, this would also compromise the safety and governance mechanisms, as they may potentially be disabled, bypassed, or in the worst case scenario, weaponised. This highlights a fundamental point of tension between a decentralised vision of the market and the existence of some centralised oversight nodes. Addressing this requires robust socio-technical solutions to ensure that the governors remain accountable and incorruptible.
如果这一基础设施遭到俘获，无论是被强大的人类利益集团俘获，还是被涌现出的拼接式 AGI 本身俘获，安全与治理机制也将受到损害，因为这些机制可能被停用、绕过，或在最坏的情况下被武器化。这凸显了市场的去中心化愿景与某些集中式监督节点的存在之间的根本张力。解决这一问题需要稳健的社会技术解决方案，以确保治理者始终可被问责且廉洁不腐。
4 Conclusion4 结论
The eventual hypothetical development of AGI (or ASI) may not follow the linear and more predictable path of intentionally creating a single, general-purpose entity. AGI, and subsequently ASI, may first emerge as an aggregate property of a more distributed network of diverse and specialised AI agents with access to tools and external models. AI safety and alignment research needs to reflect this possibility, by broadening its scope to increase preparedness for hypothetical multi-agent AGI futures. Deepening our understanding of multi-agent alignment mechanisms is crucial irrespective of whether AGI first emerges as a patchwork, or as a single entity.
假设 AGI（或 ASI）最终得以发展出来，其发展过程可能不会遵循有意创造单一通用实体这一线性且更可预测的路径。AGI 以及随后出现的 ASI，可能首先作为一个更加分布式的网络的整体属性而涌现，该网络由多样化、专业化且能够使用工具和外部模型的 AI 智能体构成。AI 安全与对齐研究需要将这一可能性纳入考量，拓宽研究范围，以提高对假设中的多智能体 AGI 未来的准备程度。无论 AGI 最初以拼接式系统还是单一实体的形式出现，深化我们对多智能体对齐机制的理解都至关重要。
The framework introduced in the paper is relevant not only for the emergence of AGI, but also for managing interactions in multi-AGI scenarios (whether interactions are direct or through a proxy web environment and via human users) and, critically, for mitigating the risks of a rapid, distributed transition to ASI via recursive optimisation of the network’s components and structure. More specifically, we believe that well-designed and carefully safeguarded market mechanisms offer a promising path forward, and that more AI alignment research should be centred on agent market design, and safe protocols for agent interaction.
本文提出的框架不仅与 AGI 的涌现相关，也适用于管理多个 AGI 场景中的交互（无论是直接交互，还是通过代理网络环境并经由人类用户进行交互），而且尤为关键的是，它还适用于降低通过递归优化网络的组成部分与结构而以分布式方式迅速向 ASI 转变所带来的风险。更具体地说，我们认为，设计良好且具有周密安全保障的市场机制提供了一条前景可期的发展路径，更多 AI 对齐研究应以智能体市场设计和智能体交互的安全协议为中心。
Although challenging, this approach offers a potentially scalable path forward. Methodological work on safe market design ought to be complemented by the rapid development of benchmarks, test environments, oversight mechanisms, and regulatory principles that would make these approaches feasible in the future. Many of the measures that we bring up are yet to be fully developed in practice, representing an open research challenge. We would like for this paper to act as a call to action, and help direct the attention of safety researchers towards addressing these challenges and helping design a safe and robust agentic web.
尽管面临挑战，这种方法仍提供了一条具有潜在可扩展性的发展路径。在开展安全市场设计的方法论研究的同时，还应迅速开发基准、测试环境、监督机制和监管原则，使这些方法在未来具备可行性。我们提出的许多措施尚未在实践中得到充分发展，仍是有待解决的研究挑战。我们希望本文能够成为一项行动倡议，帮助引导安全研究人员关注并应对这些挑战，助力设计安全、稳健的智能体网络。
