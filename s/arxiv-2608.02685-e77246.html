Abstract.摘要。
Coding-agent benchmarks increasingly cover long-horizon, end-to-end, and interactive development, but typically retain one requested outcome or a fixed change sequence. Sequential policies can process a pull-request (PR) queue one candidate at a time, but when queued PRs interact, maximizing safe delivery can require jointly deciding which changes to merge and in what order. We introduce BulkPR-Bench, an executable benchmark in which an agent must recover consequential PR relations and return a large safe subset in executable order under a rolling-release protocol. The suite contains 581 newly authored candidate PRs on frozen snapshots of 18 real repositories. Registered state-by-state repository execution, including hidden safety checks, validates the gold relation graph; an exact oracle then computes the largest safe subset. Our primary metric, Relational Delivery Score (RDS), scores safe delivery and correct rejection over relation groups from the realized merge trace; Global Safety-Gated Yield (Global-SGY) separately measures strict delivery of the realized whole-queue plan. Under the buffered primary protocol with batch size $K{=}32$ , the three highest RDS estimates among the six models are 66.6%, 62.0%, and 57.9%, compared with 53.1% for the strongest sequential baseline. Only 8 of 324 model runs complete a queue exactly. Critical-relation recall ranges from 35.2% to 57.7%, and diagnostic runs supplied with the gold relations show substantial remaining headroom. Gains on relation groups therefore do not yet translate into dependable whole-queue governance.
编码智能体基准日益涵盖长时程、端到端和交互式开发，但通常仍只设定一个要求实现的结果或一个固定的变更序列。顺序策略可以逐一处理拉取请求（PR）队列中的候选项，但当队列中的 PR 相互作用时，要最大化安全交付量，可能需要联合决定合并哪些变更以及按何种顺序合并。我们提出 BulkPR-Bench，这是一项可执行基准，要求智能体在滚动发布协议下识别具有实质影响的 PR 关系，并按可执行顺序返回一个规模较大的安全子集。该基准套件包含基于 18 个真实代码仓库的冻结快照新编写的 581 个候选 PR。按照预先登记的方案逐状态执行代码仓库（包括隐藏安全检查），以验证金标准关系图；随后由精确预言机计算最大的安全子集。我们的主要指标“关系交付得分”（Relational Delivery Score，RDS）根据实际产生的合并轨迹，按关系组对安全交付和正确拒绝进行评分；“全局安全门控产出”（Global Safety-Gated Yield，Global-SGY）则单独衡量实际执行的整个队列计划在严格标准下的交付情况。在批大小为 $K{=}32$ 的带缓冲主协议下，六个模型中最高的三个 RDS 估计值分别为 66.6%、62.0% 和 57.9%，而最强顺序基线的得分为 53.1%。324 次模型运行中，只有 8 次精确完成了一个队列。关键关系召回率介于 35.2% 至 57.7% 之间，而提供了金标准关系的诊断性运行表明，仍存在相当大的提升空间。因此，关系组上的改进尚未转化为可靠的整个队列治理能力。

Code review and pull-based development normally assess one proposed change against the current trunk (Bacchelli and Bird, 2013; Rigby and Bird, 2013; Gousios et al., 2014; Gousios et al., 2015; Tsay et al., 2014). CI studies and production merge queues improve the reliability or throughput of that process (Hilton et al., 2016; Vasilescu et al., 2015; Beller et al., 2017; Juloori et al., 2025). A queue of interacting candidates changes the decision, however. Parallel changes can interfere syntactically or semantically (Perry et al., 1998; Ghiotto et al., 2020; Vale et al., 2022); review data also exposes dependencies, duplicate or competing PRs, and concurrent edits (Arabat and Sayagh, 2024; Li et al., 2022b; Zhang et al., 2018; Maddila et al., 2022). A sequential public-gate policy can process a queue and keep public CI green, yet still merge a combination that fails hidden safety checks, reject changes that are valid only together, or deliver fewer safe changes than a joint plan.
代码审查和基于拉取请求的开发通常针对当前主干评估单个拟议变更 (Bacchelli and Bird, 2013; Rigby and Bird, 2013; Gousios et al., 2014; Gousios et al., 2015; Tsay et al., 2014)。持续集成（CI）研究和生产环境中的合并队列提高了这一过程的可靠性或吞吐量 (Hilton et al., 2016; Vasilescu et al., 2015; Beller et al., 2017; Juloori et al., 2025)。然而，当队列中的候选项相互作用时，决策问题就发生了变化。并行变更可能在语法或语义层面相互干扰 (Perry et al., 1998; Ghiotto et al., 2020; Vale et al., 2022)；审查数据也揭示了依赖关系、重复或相互竞争的 PR，以及并发编辑 (Arabat and Sayagh, 2024; Li et al., 2022b; Zhang et al., 2018; Maddila et al., 2022)。基于公开门控的顺序策略可以处理队列并使公开 CI 持续通过，但仍可能合并无法通过隐藏安全检查的组合、拒绝只有放在一起才有效的变更，或者交付比联合计划更少的安全变更。
We study queue-level PR governance: recover the relations that matter, choose which candidates to merge or reject, and order the chosen subset so that repository execution succeeds. BulkPR-Bench makes this a repeatable task. Each instance pairs a pinned repository snapshot with $N$ newly authored candidates revealed in batches of size $K$ . The agent submits atomic merge proposals, deferrals, and a revisable relation ledger. Scoring follows the realized plan accepted by the executor, not the agent’s claimed plan. Hidden verifiers, an execution-validated gold relation graph, and exact safe-subset optima provide the scoring truth. Figure 1 shows why arrival-order decisions and joint planning can produce different subsets and orders.
我们研究队列级 PR 治理：识别重要的关系，选择要合并或拒绝的候选项，并对选定子集排序，使代码仓库能够成功执行。BulkPR-Bench 将其转化为一项可重复的任务。每个实例将一个固定版本的代码仓库快照与 $N$ 个新编写的候选项配对，这些候选项以大小为 $K$ 的批次逐批揭示。智能体提交原子性合并提案、暂缓决策以及可修订的关系台账。评分依据执行器接受的实际计划，而非智能体声称的计划。隐藏验证器、经执行验证的金标准关系图以及精确的安全子集最优解共同提供评分所依据的真值。图 1 展示了为何按到达顺序决策与联合规划会产生不同的子集和顺序。
The evaluation asks four linked questions. RQ1 (Final delivery) asks how agents compare with deterministic public-CI queue baselines. RQ2 (Visibility and batch size) asks how $K$ and deferral change available information and realized decisions. RQ3 (Relation recovery and action) asks how completely agents recover consequential relations and what headroom appears when reliable relation information is supplied. RQ4 (Strict completion and outcomes) asks how often exact whole-queue completion occurs and how remaining runs terminate.
评估围绕四个相互关联的问题展开。RQ1（最终交付）考察智能体与基于公开 CI 的确定性队列基线相比表现如何。RQ2（可见性与批大小）考察 $K$ 和暂缓决策如何改变可用信息与实际决策。RQ3（关系恢复与行动）考察智能体对具有实质影响的关系恢复得有多完整，以及在提供可靠关系信息时呈现出多大的提升空间。RQ4（严格完成与结果）考察精确完成整个队列的频率，以及其余运行如何终止。
This paper contributes four pieces of evidence and infrastructure. (1) The queue-level governance task couples relation recovery, subset selection, executable order, and bounded rolling visibility (Section 3). (2) Realized-plan measurement uses RDS as the sole ranking metric, with whole-queue, hidden-relation, recovery, and action diagnostics (Section 4). (3) An executable relational construction draws on historical PRs and expert input to author new candidates on real snapshots, and establishes their relations and safe optima through repository execution (Section 5). (4) A formal evaluation over 18 repositories finds that the three highest buffered model RDS estimates are 66.6%, 62.0%, and 57.9%, compared with 53.1% for the strongest sequential baseline; only 8 of 324 model runs exactly complete a queue (Section 7).
本文在证据与基础设施方面作出四项贡献。(1) 队列级治理任务将关系恢复、子集选择、可执行顺序以及有界的滚动可见性结合起来（第 3 节）。(2) 已实现计划的测量以 RDS 为唯一排名指标，并提供整个队列、隐藏关系、恢复和行动方面的诊断（第 4 节）。(3) 一种可执行的关系构造方法利用历史 PR 和专家意见，在真实快照上编写新的候选变更，并通过仓库执行确定它们之间的关系及安全最优解（第 5 节）。(4) 在 18 个仓库上开展的正式评估发现，缓冲模式下模型的三个最高 RDS 估计值分别为 66.6%、62.0% 和 57.9%，而最强顺序基线为 53.1%；324 次模型运行中仅有 8 次精确完成了一个队列（第 7 节）。

Repository change and review. SWE-bench and its extensions evaluate one issue-level repository change with held-out verification (Jimenez et al., 2024; Zan et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025). More recent benchmarks cover feature development, original long-horizon objectives, complete issue-resolution cycles, and dialogue-driven workflows (Zhou et al., 2026; Huang et al., 2026; Desai et al., 2026; Guan et al., 2026; King and Flanigan, 2026), broadening task duration, lifecycle coverage, and interaction, but each instance still targets one requested outcome or issue. SWE-STEPS and ChainSWE instead evaluate fixed change chains: repository state accumulates, but the agent neither chooses chain membership nor reorders it (Shastry et al., 2026; Jin et al., 2026). Code-review benchmarks likewise assess comments, decisions, or revision utility for one PR at a time (Tufano et al., 2021; Li et al., 2022a; Zhang et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026). Together, these tasks establish repository reasoning and executable verification without making the choice among concurrently pending changes the output.
仓库变更与审查。SWE-bench 及其扩展通过留出的验证来评估单个问题级别的仓库变更（Jimenez et al., 2024; Zan et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025）。较新的基准涵盖功能开发、原创的长期目标、完整的问题解决周期以及对话驱动的工作流（Zhou et al., 2026; Huang et al., 2026; Desai et al., 2026; Guan et al., 2026; King and Flanigan, 2026），扩展了任务持续时间、生命周期覆盖范围和交互形式，但每个实例仍以单个请求结果或问题为目标。SWE-STEPS 和 ChainSWE 则评估固定的变更链：仓库状态会累积，但智能体既不能选择链中的成员，也不能调整其顺序（Shastry et al., 2026; Jin et al., 2026）。代码审查基准同样每次只评估一个 PR 的评论、决策或修订效用（Tufano et al., 2021; Li et al., 2022a; Zhang et al., 2026; Wang et al., 2026）。总体而言，这些任务确立了仓库推理和可执行验证的评估方式，但并未将对同时待处理的多个变更所作的选择作为输出。
Composition and queue management. Merge research studies conflict detection, resolution, and structure-aware integration (Mens, 2002; Apel et al., 2011; Brun et al., 2011; Dinella et al., 2023). GitGoodBench evaluates Git operations such as conflict resolution and history rewriting, whereas MOSAIC-Bench evaluates a fixed three-stage composition (Lindenbauer et al., 2025; Steinberg and Gal, 2026). Closer to queue management, Olmedo et al. group and sequence PRs from textual conflicts, while PRioritize ranks waiting PRs to improve pipeline throughput (Olmedo et al., 2022; Jungwirth et al., 2025). Among the representative designs in Table 1, none jointly evaluates an agent-chosen final subset and integration order against composition-sensitive repository truth under bounded rolling visibility.
组合与队列管理。合并研究关注冲突检测、冲突解决以及结构感知的集成（Mens, 2002; Apel et al., 2011; Brun et al., 2011; Dinella et al., 2023）。GitGoodBench 评估冲突解决和历史重写等 Git 操作，而 MOSAIC-Bench 评估固定的三阶段组合（Lindenbauer et al., 2025; Steinberg and Gal, 2026）。在更接近队列管理的研究中，Olmedo et al. 根据文本冲突对 PR 进行分组和排序，而 PRioritize 对等待处理的 PR 进行排名，以提高流水线吞吐量（Olmedo et al., 2022; Jungwirth et al., 2025）。在表 1 所列的代表性设计中，没有任何一种在可见范围受限且滚动更新的条件下，依据对组合敏感的仓库真值，联合评估由智能体选择的最终子集和集成顺序。
Why construction is needed. Historical issues, PRs, and review records expose combinations that maintainers attempted, not counterfactual outcomes for subsets and orders they never tried (Yu et al., 2015; McIntosh et al., 2014). They therefore cannot by themselves provide the composition-specific relation labels or exact oracle plan required by this evaluation. Section 5 describes how we construct and execute these counterfactual states.
为何需要构造。历史问题、PR 和审查记录揭示的是维护者尝试过的组合，而非他们从未尝试过的子集和顺序所对应的反事实结果（Yu et al., 2015; McIntosh et al., 2014）。因此，仅凭这些记录，无法提供本评估所需的特定组合关系标签或精确的预言机计划。第 5 节介绍了我们如何构造并执行这些反事实状态。
3. The Bulk-PR Governance Task3. 批量 PR 治理任务
3.1. Task Contract3.1. 任务约定
An instance contains a frozen repository snapshot, $N$ candidate PR diffs under neutral identifiers, public tests, and a fixed interaction budget. The agent is asked to deliver a large safe subset in executable order. It acts through batch decisions; the scored delivery output is the realized merge plan accepted by the evaluator, not a patch or a self-reported final plan. Each decision also updates a persistent, revisable typed relation ledger used for separate diagnostics. The ledger distinguishes pairwise and higher-order conflicts, dependencies, all-or-none groups, must-reject candidates, duplicates, and superseding changes.
一个实例包含一个冻结的仓库快照、以中性标识符标记的 $N$ 个候选 PR 差异、公开测试以及固定的交互预算。智能体需要按可执行的顺序交付一个较大的安全子集。它通过批量决策采取行动；参与评分的交付输出是评估器接受的已实现合并计划，而非补丁或自行报告的最终计划。每次决策还会更新一份持久保存、可修订且带类型的关系台账，用于单独的诊断。该台账区分两两冲突与高阶冲突、依赖关系、全选或全不选的分组、必须拒绝的候选变更、重复变更以及取代其他变更的变更。
The gold relation graph (the full gold constraints) and exact oracle remain on the scoring side. A realized plan is safe when every committed atomic state satisfies the full gold constraints, and executable when its protected diffs apply in the submitted order and each prerequisite precedes its dependent. The runtime public gate—the released public test suite, which we also call public CI—exposes only public constraints, so its acceptance is necessary for committing a proposal but is not sufficient for gold safety.
金标准关系图（完整的金标准约束）和精确预言机仅保留在评分端。当每个已提交的原子状态都满足完整的金标准约束时，实际执行的计划是安全的；当其受保护的差异补丁能够按提交顺序应用，且每个前置项都先于依赖它的项时，该计划是可执行的。运行时的公开门禁——即已发布的公开测试套件，我们也称之为公开 CI——仅暴露公开约束，因此，通过该门禁是提交提案的必要条件，但不足以保证金标准安全性。
3.2. Rolling Protocol3.2. 滚动协议
The queue follows a frozen arrival order and is released in consecutive batches of size $K$ , except possibly the last. Future batches are absent from the agent-visible file system, while the workspace and agent session persist; thus $K$ changes concurrent visibility rather than memory. In the buffered protocol, at most $B$ candidates may remain pending across batch boundaries, each for at most $T$ boundaries after arrival and only when deferral is renewed. In the no-deferral protocol, every candidate must be merged or permanently rejected in its arrival batch.
队列遵循固定的到达顺序，并按大小为 $K$ 的连续批次发布，最后一批的大小可能例外。未来批次不会出现在智能体可见的文件系统中，而工作区和智能体会话会持续保留；因此，$K$ 改变的是同时可见的范围，而非记忆。在缓冲协议中，跨批次边界保持待定状态的候选项最多为 $B$ 个，每个候选项在到达后最多可跨越 $T$ 个批次边界保持待定，且仅在延期得到续期时才可如此。在不允许延期的协议中，每个候选项都必须在其到达批次中被合并或永久拒绝。
At batch step $s$ , the available set $A_{s}$ contains the new batch and any still-pending candidates. The agent submits an ordered merge list $M_{s}$ , a disjoint defer set $D_{s}$ , and an updated relation ledger, with $M_{s},D_{s}\subseteq A_{s}$ . Candidates in $A_{s}\setminus(M_{s}\cup D_{s})$ are permanently rejected.
在批次步骤 $s$，可用集合 $A_{s}$ 包含新批次以及所有仍处于待定状态的候选项。智能体提交一个有序合并列表 $M_{s}$、一个与其不相交的延期集合 $D_{s}$，以及更新后的关系台账，其中 $M_{s},D_{s}\subseteq A_{s}$。$A_{s}\setminus(M_{s}\cup D_{s})$ 中的候选项会被永久拒绝。
The evaluator treats $M_{s}$ as one atomic proposal. If the public gate accepts it, the evaluator applies the protected diffs in the submitted order to an authoritative trunk and commits the proposal as a whole. If the gate rejects it, none of its members is accepted and those candidates leave the queue; separately deferred candidates remain pending. The evaluator never repairs a failed proposal, although the agent may use public-gate feedback and public tests in later decisions.
评估器将 $M_{s}$ 视为一个原子提案。如果公开门禁接受该提案，评估器就会按提交顺序将受保护的差异补丁应用到权威主干，并将该提案作为一个整体提交。如果门禁拒绝该提案，则其中任何成员都不会被接受，这些候选项会离开队列；另行延期的候选项则继续保持待定。评估器绝不会修复失败的提案，但智能体可以在后续决策中使用公开门禁的反馈和公开测试。
Delivery metrics score accepted proposals concatenated in execution order. A scored prefix ends at an accepted atomic-proposal boundary, not after each individual diff within the proposal. Public-green proposals that violate hidden gold remain in the realized plan and are penalized by scoring rather than rolled back. Relation diagnostics separately score the revisable ledger. A malformed or missing decision advances the step as an empty action, marks the trial invalid, and does not prevent later batches from being released. Appendix A.1 specifies execution and integrity checks.
交付指标对按执行顺序串接的已接受提案进行评分。计分前缀以已接受的原子提案边界为终点，而不是以提案内部每个单独差异补丁的结束位置为终点。通过公开测试但违反隐藏金标准的提案仍会保留在实际执行的计划中，并通过评分受到惩罚，而不会被回滚。关系诊断会对可修订的台账单独评分。格式错误或缺失的决策会被视为空操作，使步骤继续推进，并将该次试验标记为无效，但不会阻止后续批次的发布。附录 A.1 规定了执行检查和完整性检查。
4. Metrics and Diagnostics4. 指标与诊断
The Relational Delivery Score (RDS) is the sole ranking metric for relational delivery. Global-SGY and Exact Completion ask whether that localized performance becomes a deployable whole-queue plan; RDS (hidden), CriticalRecall, and controlled information conditions diagnose where performance is lost.
关系交付得分（Relational Delivery Score，RDS）是关系交付的唯一排名指标。Global-SGY 和 Exact Completion 用于检验这种局部表现能否转化为可部署的全队列计划；RDS (hidden)、CriticalRecall 和受控信息条件则用于诊断性能损失发生在何处。
4.1. Relational Delivery Score4.1. 关系交付得分
RDS measures realized delivery on candidates whose correct handling depends on PR relations, rather than total PR throughput. It scores relation groups locally so that one failed group does not erase correct decisions elsewhere, and equal group and repository weights prevent large components or repositories from dominating; relation-free delivery and whole-queue deployability are measured separately below.
RDS 衡量的是对那些正确处理方式取决于 PR 关系的候选项所实现的实际交付，而非 PR 总吞吐量。它对关系组进行局部评分，使一个组的失败不会抹去其他组中的正确决策，同时通过赋予各组和各仓库相同的权重，防止大型连通分量或大型仓库主导得分；不涉及关系的交付和全队列可部署性将在下文分别衡量。
For repository $r$ , the full gold graph induces relation groups $\mathcal{C}_{r}$ : constraints sharing a PR form one connected component, and an isolated must-reject candidate forms a singleton. Candidates with no relation constraint are excluded. For trial $t$ and group $c$ , let $\mathrm{OPT}_{c}$ be the largest number of group members in any safe solution, $R_{r,t,c}$ the realized accepted trace projected onto that group, and $G_{r,t,c}$ indicate that the projection has only safe prefixes and an executable order. Let $V_{t}=\mathrm{Valid}_{t}\wedge\mathrm{ExecutorCompleted}_{t}$ . The group score is
对于仓库 $r$，完整金标准图诱导出关系组 $\mathcal{C}_{r}$：共享 PR 的约束构成同一个连通分量，而孤立的必须拒绝的候选项则构成一个单元素组。没有关系约束的候选项不计入其中。对于试验 $t$ 和组 $c$，令 $\mathrm{OPT}_{c}$ 为任意安全解中所能包含的组成员数量的最大值，$R_{r,t,c}$ 为实际接受轨迹在该组上的投影，$G_{r,t,c}$ 表示该投影的所有前缀均安全且顺序可执行。令 $V_{t}=\mathrm{Valid}_{t}\wedge\mathrm{ExecutorCompleted}_{t}$。组得分为
$$q_{r,t,c}=\begin{cases}0,&\neg V_{t}\ \text{or}\ \neg G_{r,t,c},\\ |R_{r,t,c}|/\mathrm{OPT}_{c},&V_{t}\wedge G_{r,t,c}\wedge\mathrm{OPT}_{c}>0,\\ 1,&V_{t}\wedge G_{r,t,c}\wedge\mathrm{OPT}_{c}=0.\end{cases}$$
Thus a safe partial delivery receives fractional credit. The last case gives full credit for correctly accepting no candidate from a zero-optimum group; accepting any member makes that group unsafe and therefore scores zero. A safe realized set larger than the exact optimum is flagged as an artifact error and excluded rather than silently clipped.
因此，安全的部分交付会获得部分分数。最后一种情况对正确地不接受最优值为零的组中的任何候选项给予满分；接受其中任意成员都会使该组不安全，因此得分为零。若安全的实际交付集合的大小超过精确最优值，则会被标记为评测产物错误并予以排除，而不是悄然截断其得分。
Groups are equally weighted within a run. Repeats are averaged inside each repository before repositories are macro-averaged:
在一次运行中，各组的权重相同。先在每个仓库内部对重复运行的结果取平均，再对各仓库进行宏平均：
$$\mathrm{RDS}=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{R}|}\sum_{r\in\mathcal{R}}\frac{1}{n_{r}}\sum_{t=1}^{n_{r}}\frac{1}{|\mathcal{C}_{r}|}\sum_{c\in\mathcal{C}_{r}}q_{r,t,c}.$$
This order makes repositories, rather than repeated runs, the units of the reported score.
这一顺序使所报告得分以仓库而非重复运行为统计单位。
RDS scores the system’s realized decision. A proposal rejected by the public gate is atomically rolled back and does not enter $R_{r,t,c}$ ; its members leave the queue, but later proposals over a group’s deferred, still-pending members may improve that group’s score. A committed prefix that violates a hidden gold relation zeros its group but not unrelated groups. RDS therefore measures delivery after model decisions, public-gate feedback, and revision—not the correctness of the model’s initial, feedback-free judgment.
RDS 对系统实际执行的决策进行评分。被公开门禁拒绝的提案会以原子方式整体回滚，不会进入 $R_{r,t,c}$；其成员会离开队列，但后续针对某组中已延期且仍待定的成员提出的提案，可能提高该组的得分。违反隐藏金标准关系的已提交前缀会使其所属组的得分归零，但不会影响无关的组。因此，RDS 衡量的是经过模型决策、公共门控反馈和修订之后的交付情况，而非模型在未获得反馈时所作初始判断的正确性。
4.2. Whole-Queue and Strict Certificates4.2. 全队列与严格证书
RDS omits relation-free candidates and localizes a group failure. Global Safety-Gated Yield (Global-SGY) instead uses the complete realized accepted set $R_{t}$ , including relation-free candidates. Let $\mathrm{OPT}_{N,r}$ be the maximum safe cardinality over all $N$ candidates in repository $r$ . Its whole-queue gate is
RDS 不计入无关系的候选项，并将组失败的影响限定在该组内。相比之下，全局安全门控产出率（Global Safety-Gated Yield，Global-SGY）使用实际实现的完整接受集合 $R_{t}$，其中包括无关系的候选项。令 $\mathrm{OPT}_{N,r}$ 表示仓库 $r$ 中全部 $N$ 个候选项上的最大安全基数。其全队列门控条件为
$$\Gamma_{t}=\mathrm{Valid}_{t}\wedge\mathrm{ExecutorCompleted}_{t}\wedge\mathrm{AllPrefixSafe}_{t}\wedge\mathrm{Executable}_{t}.$$
Here validity requires protocol-conforming decisions; executor completion requires the episode to finish normally; prefix safety checks every committed atomic-proposal boundary against full gold; and executability checks the realized order. For repository $r$ ,
这里，有效性要求决策符合协议；执行器完成要求回合正常结束；前缀安全性依据完整金标准检查每个已提交的原子提案边界；可执行性则检查实际实现的顺序。对于仓库 $r$，
$$\mathrm{Global\text{-}SGY}_{r,t}=\Gamma_{t}\frac{|R_{t}|}{\mathrm{OPT}_{N,r}}.$$
It uses the same repeat-then-repository aggregation as RDS. Any invalid, unfinished, unsafe, or unexecutable run scores zero; Global-SGY is a strict companion measure of whole-queue deployability and is never combined with RDS.
它采用与 RDS 相同的先按重复运行、再按仓库聚合的方式。任何无效、未完成、不安全或不可执行的运行均得零分；Global-SGY 是衡量全队列可部署性的严格配套指标，绝不与 RDS 合并。
Let $P_{t}=\bigcup_{s}M_{s}$ be the set of all candidates proposed for merging during the run. Writing $\mathrm{Exact}_{r,t}$ for the per-run Exact Completion certificate,
令 $P_{t}=\bigcup_{s}M_{s}$ 表示运行期间所有被提议合并的候选项的集合。用 $\mathrm{Exact}_{r,t}$ 表示每次运行的精确完成（Exact Completion）证书，
$$\mathrm{Exact}_{r,t}=\mathds{1}\!\left[\Gamma_{t}\wedge|R_{t}|=\mathrm{OPT}_{N,r}\wedge P_{t}=R_{t}\right].$$
The final equality requires that no proposed merge remain unrealized. Table 3 reports Exact Completions as raw successes/runs.
最后一个等式要求所有提议的合并均已实现。表 3 以原始成功次数/运行次数报告精确完成情况。
4.3. Relation and Information Diagnostics4.3. 关系与信息诊断
RDS (hidden) applies the RDS calculation only to positive-optimum relation groups containing a constraint invisible to public CI. It therefore measures positive delivery under hidden interactions rather than refusal credit. One repository (langgraph) has no eligible group, so this diagnostic covers 17 repositories.
RDS (hidden) 仅对最优值为正且包含公共 CI 不可见约束的关系组应用 RDS 计算。因此，它衡量的是隐藏交互下的正向交付，而非因拒绝而获得的分数。有一个仓库（langgraph）没有符合条件的组，因此该诊断覆盖 17 个仓库。
Let $E_{r}^{*}$ be repository $r$ ’s normalized gold relation atoms and $\hat{E}_{r,t}$ the trial’s final active typed ledger; a match requires exact relation type, membership, and direction. For a gold atom $e$ , let $\mathcal{O}_{e}$ contain the maximum-size subsets of $e$ ’s original relation component that are safe under the gold constraints with $e$ removed. Its criticality weight is
令 $E_{r}^{*}$ 表示仓库 $r$ 的规范化金标准关系原子，$\hat{E}_{r,t}$ 表示该次试验最终的活跃类型化账本；匹配要求关系类型、成员构成和方向完全一致。对于金标准原子 $e$，令 $\mathcal{O}_{e}$ 包含 $e$ 所属原始关系分量中，在移除 $e$ 后的金标准约束下安全的最大规模子集。其关键性权重为
$$w_{e}=\frac{|\{S\in\mathcal{O}_{e}:S\text{ violates }e\}|}{|\mathcal{O}_{e}|}.$$
CriticalRecall then gives greater weight to relations whose omission admits unsafe maximum-size decisions:
随后，CriticalRecall 对那些一旦遗漏就会允许不安全的最大规模决策的关系赋予更高权重：
$$\mathrm{CriticalRecall}_{r,t}=\frac{\sum_{e\in E_{r}^{*}\cap\hat{E}_{r,t}}w_{e}}{\sum_{e\in E_{r}^{*}}w_{e}}.$$
It is undefined when the denominator is zero and otherwise uses the same repeat-then-repository aggregation as the delivery metrics.
当分母为零时，该指标未定义；否则，它采用与交付指标相同的先按重复运行、再按仓库聚合的方式。
Agent uses its own ledger. In the Gold-fed diagnostic, the same model receives the reliable gold subgraph induced by candidates seen so far but still chooses every merge, deferral, and order. A clairvoyant Solver receives full gold and uses a deterministic scheduler under the same buffered $K{=}32$ protocol. Gold feeding changes the model’s information; the solver additionally changes the information horizon and decision maker. Their RDS differences are diagnostic headroom, not model scores or a causal decomposition. Appendix A.2 defines two additional solver replays retained in the artifact.
Agent 使用自己的账本。在 Gold-fed 诊断中，同一个模型接收由迄今已见候选项诱导出的可靠金标准子图，但仍自行决定每一次合并、延后处理及其顺序。具有全知信息的 Solver 接收完整金标准，并在相同的缓冲式 $K{=}32$ 协议下使用确定性调度器。金标准馈入改变了模型所获得的信息；求解器还进一步改变了信息视野和决策者。它们之间的 RDS 差异表示诊断性的提升空间，而非模型得分或因果分解。附录 A.2 定义了保留在配套产物中的另外两种求解器重放。

5.2. Executable Ground Truth5.2. 可执行真值
The gold graph is accepted only when symbolic prediction and repository behavior agree. A state applies an ordered subset of candidate diffs to the snapshot. For every registered state, a constraint predictor gives the expected green or red outcome, while the repository-execution gate applies the diffs and runs the frozen test scope. The registered evidence covers every nonempty subset within each relation component, together with whole-pool witnesses and selected cross-component probes; it does not enumerate all $2^{N}$ global subsets. Any mismatch rejects the pool. Apply and infrastructure failures remain separate outcomes rather than substitutes for an expected red state.
仅当符号预测与仓库行为一致时，金标准图才会被接受。一个状态通过将候选差异补丁的一个有序子集应用到快照上形成。对于每个已登记状态，约束预测器给出预期的通过或失败结果，而仓库执行关卡则应用差异补丁，并运行冻结的测试范围内的测试。已登记的证据覆盖每个关系连通分量内的所有非空子集，以及全池见证和选定的跨分量探测；它并不枚举全部 $2^{N}$ 个全局子集。任何不一致都会导致该候选池被拒绝。补丁应用失败和基础设施故障仍被视为独立结果，不能替代预期的测试失败状态。
For each public-invisible engineered relation, a construction-time hidden verifier is bound to concrete production behavior. It passes on the registered base and negative-control states and fails on the registered target states. The accepted public projection becomes the runtime public gate, while formal trials are scored against the frozen full-gold constraints rather than by rerunning hidden verifiers or the full repository test scope after every proposal. Finally, an exact solver derives $\mathrm{OPT}_{N}$ —exact with respect to the frozen gold constraints—and a maximum-safe witness set; a separate topology check confirms that the witness admits an executable order. Appendix A.3 specifies the repeated-execution, failure-witness, flakiness, oracle-recomputation, and drift checks.
对于每一种公开测试不可见的人为设计关系，都会在构建时将一个隐藏验证器绑定到具体的生产代码行为。该验证器在已登记的基线状态和阴性对照状态上通过，在已登记的目标状态上失败。经接受的公开投影成为运行时的公开关卡，而正式试验依据冻结的完整金标准约束评分，并非在每次提案后重新运行隐藏验证器或完整仓库测试范围内的测试。最后，精确求解器推导出 $\mathrm{OPT}_{N}$——其精确性以冻结的金标准约束为准——以及一个最大安全见证集；另行进行的拓扑检查确认该见证集存在可执行顺序。附录 A.3 规定了重复执行、失败见证、测试不稳定性、预言机重计算和漂移检查。
5.3. Held-Out Release, Licensing, and Ethics5.3. 留出数据发布、许可与伦理
Before formal evaluation, candidate diffs, ground truth, and hidden verifiers remain non-public. During an episode, candidate diffs are revealed only at their scheduled batch; future diffs, full gold, and hidden verifiers remain outside the agent-visible tree. A committed public randomness beacon fixes neutral identifiers and arrival order. A frozen pool version is admitted to formal evaluation only after its execution gates pass; every evaluation is bound to that version, and any change to version-bound bytes creates a new version and reruns the affected leakage and integrity checks. Because publication changes direct contamination risk, later evaluations must account for the dated release (Jacovi et al., 2023; Sainz et al., 2023; Jain et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025).
在正式评估之前，候选差异补丁、真值和隐藏验证器均不公开。在一个回合中，候选差异补丁仅在其预定批次到达时才会披露；未来的差异补丁、完整金标准和隐藏验证器始终置于智能体可见的目录树之外。一个预先承诺的公共随机信标用于确定中性标识符和到达顺序。冻结的候选池版本只有在通过其执行关卡后，才可进入正式评估；每次评估都绑定到该版本，对版本绑定字节的任何更改都会生成新版本，并重新运行受影响的泄漏检查和完整性检查。由于公开发布会改变直接污染的风险，后续评估必须考虑带有日期记录的发布情况（Jacovi et al., 2023; Sainz et al., 2023; Jain et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025）。
We will release the formal pools, hidden verifiers, scorer, and protocol as versioned artifacts at https://github.com/Eureka246/BulkPR-Bench-Release. BulkPR-Bench’s code will be released under the MIT License and its constructed annotations, manifests, and task diffs under CC BY 4.0; upstream snapshots and upstream-derived patch context remain governed by their original repository licenses. The release will bind the reported results to a tag and manifest digest and provide metadata and instructions for reproducing the reported tables from frozen trial records. Appendix Table 5 identifies each fixed upstream snapshot and its original repository license; the artifact manifest retains the full commits and license-file URLs. The release omits contributor names and emails, historical PR diffs, and expert source material; the surface audit retains public repository/PR IDs. BulkPR-Bench evaluates repository states rather than developers and is intended for offline agent evaluation, not unattended production merging; its scores do not support claims about developer populations. Evaluations run in isolated containers without scoring-side artifacts or credentials. Repository cards record the benchmark’s motivation, composition, provenance, construction, intended use, maintenance, and known limits (Gebru et al., 2021; Bender and Friedman, 2018; Pushkarna et al., 2022).
我们将以带版本的制品形式，在 https://github.com/Eureka246/BulkPR-Bench-Release 发布正式候选池、隐藏验证器、评分器和协议。BulkPR-Bench 的代码将依据 MIT License 发布，其构建的标注、清单和任务差异将依据 CC BY 4.0 发布；上游快照及源自上游的补丁上下文仍受其原始仓库许可证约束。发布版本将把报告的结果与一个标签及清单摘要绑定，并提供元数据和说明，以便根据冻结的试验记录复现所报告的表格。附录表 5 列出了每个固定的上游快照及其原始仓库许可证；工件清单保留了完整的提交标识和许可证文件 URL。发布内容不包含贡献者姓名和电子邮箱、历史 PR 差异以及专家源材料；公开信息审计保留公开的仓库/PR ID。BulkPR-Bench 评估的是仓库状态，旨在用于离线智能体评估，而非无人值守的生产环境合并；其得分不支持对开发者总体作出论断。评估在隔离容器中运行，容器内不包含评分端工件或凭据。仓库卡片记录了该基准的动机、组成、来源、构建过程、预期用途、维护方式及已知局限（Gebru et al., 2021; Bender and Friedman, 2018; Pushkarna et al., 2022）。

6.1. Primary Profile and Sweeps6.1. 主要配置与参数扫描
The primary profile covers all 18 repositories and uses the frozen default arrival order, generic instructions, typed ledger, buffered protocol ( $B{=}4$ , $T{=}16$ ; Section 3.2), and $K{=}32$ . Each model–repository pair has three separately initialized runs ( $n{=}3$ ). The evaluated models are Claude-Opus-4.8, DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GLM-5.2, GPT-5.4, Kimi-K3, and Qwen3.7-Max.
主要配置涵盖全部 18 个仓库，并采用冻结的默认到达顺序、通用指令、带类型的台账、缓冲协议（$B{=}4$，$T{=}16$；第 3.2 节），以及 $K{=}32$。每个模型–仓库组合均进行三次独立初始化的运行（$n{=}3$）。接受评估的模型为 Claude-Opus-4.8、DeepSeek-V4-Pro、GLM-5.2、GPT-5.4、Kimi-K3 和 Qwen3.7-Max。
All model backends use the same frozen Claude Code v2.1.138 agent scaffold, runtime, 150-turn interaction budget, generic instructions, and ledger schema. Because agent scaffolding can materially affect repository-task performance (Yang et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2025; Kapoor et al., 2025), the primary comparison holds these choices fixed. Alternative prompts, ledger formats, arrival orders, and gold-information conditions are evaluated separately and do not enter the primary ranking. Trials hit by infrastructure failures are rerun; turn-budget exhaustion remains a model failure and scores zero.
所有模型后端均使用相同且已冻结的 Claude Code v2.1.138 智能体框架、运行时、150 轮交互预算、通用指令和台账模式。由于智能体框架会显著影响仓库任务的表现（Yang et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2025; Kapoor et al., 2025），主要比较将这些选择保持固定。替代提示词、台账格式、到达顺序和标准答案信息条件均单独评估，不计入主要排名。受基础设施故障影响的试验会重新运行；耗尽交互轮次预算仍被视为模型失败，得分为零。

6.2. Baselines and Diagnostic Arms6.2. 基线与诊断实验组
We include three deterministic public-information baselines: Merge-Queue scans once and rejects public-gate failures; CI-Fixedpoint revisits rejections after trunk changes; and Batch-Greedy tries candidates within each batch without deferral. Merge-All, which proposes each released batch as one atomic proposal, and No-Op, which merges nothing, are anchors; Gold-fed and Solver conditions remain unranked diagnostics (Section 4.3). Each follows the frozen arrival order, yields one deterministic outcome per repository, and sees no hidden verifiers.
我们纳入了三种基于公开信息的确定性基线：Merge-Queue 仅扫描一次，并拒绝未通过公开门控检查的候选项；CI-Fixedpoint 在主干发生变化后重新考察被拒绝的候选项；Batch-Greedy 在每个批次内尝试各候选项，不允许延后处理。Merge-All 将每个已释放批次作为一个原子提案提交，No-Op 则不合并任何内容，二者作为参照；Gold-fed 和 Solver 条件仍作为不参与排名的诊断条件（第 4.3 节）。每种方法均遵循冻结的到达顺序，对每个仓库产生一个确定性结果，且无法访问任何隐藏验证器。
6.3. Aggregation, Statistics, and Scope of Inference6.3. 汇总、统计与推断范围
Repositories are the units of aggregation and resampling. We average three runs within each repository, then macro-average the 18 repositories equally; PRs, batches, and repeats within one repository share a base, test ecosystem, and construction style and are not treated as independent repository samples. The 95% bias-corrected and accelerated (BCa) intervals resample these 18 means 10,000 times and capture cross-repository, not run-to-run, variation (Efron, 1987; Field and Welsh, 2007). Baselines are fixed-suite point estimates. Because the purposive suite is not a probability sample, its intervals describe these pools rather than a repository population.
仓库是汇总和重采样的单位。我们先在每个仓库内对三次运行取平均，再对 18 个仓库等权计算宏平均；同一仓库内的 PR、批次和重复运行共享基础版本、测试生态和构建风格，因此不被视为独立的仓库样本。95% 偏差校正加速（BCa）区间对这 18 个均值进行 10,000 次重采样，反映的是跨仓库变异，而非运行间变异（Efron, 1987; Field and Welsh, 2007）。基线结果是针对固定测试套件的点估计。由于该套件采用目的性选取而非概率抽样，其区间描述的是这些候选池，而非仓库总体。

7.2. RQ2: Visibility and Batch Size7.2. RQ2：可见性与批次大小
Higher- $K$ conditions generally produced higher observed mean RDS, but effects varied by model and deferral policy. Under buffering, $K{=}32$ exceeded $K{=}1$ for all six models by 0.1–19.1 points: Claude, DeepSeek, GPT, and GLM gained at least 11.7 points, Kimi-K3 peaked at $K{=}8$ , and Qwen was nearly flat. Hidden-group curves were likewise heterogeneous. At $K{=}32$ , removing deferral helped GPT, GLM, and Kimi but hurt Claude, DeepSeek, and Qwen, with changes from $+3.7$ to $-16.6$ points. Under buffering, DeepSeek ended at 40.9% and Qwen at 47.2%; both remained below all three sequential baselines at $K{=}32$ .
较高 $K$ 值的条件通常产生较高的观测平均 RDS，但效果因模型和延迟处理策略而异。在缓冲协议下，全部六个模型在 $K{=}32$ 时的得分均高于 $K{=}1$ 时，高出 0.1–19.1 个百分点：Claude、DeepSeek、GPT 和 GLM 至少提高了 11.7 个百分点，Kimi-K3 在 $K{=}8$ 时达到峰值，而 Qwen 几乎没有变化。隐藏组的曲线同样呈现出异质性。在 $K{=}32$ 时，取消延迟处理提高了 GPT、GLM 和 Kimi 的表现，却降低了 Claude、DeepSeek 和 Qwen 的表现，变化幅度从 $+3.7$ 到 $-16.6$ 个百分点不等。在缓冲协议下，DeepSeek 的最终得分为 40.9%，Qwen 为 47.2%；在 $K{=}32$ 时，两者仍低于全部三个顺序执行基线。
7.3. RQ3: Relation Recovery and Action7.3. RQ3：关系恢复与行动
Agents recovered only part of the consequential relation graph, but usually respected what they recorded. Buffered CriticalRecall ranged from 35.2% to 57.7% across all six models (Table 3). Across 12 model–protocol cells, agents respected 98.1%–100.0% of the relations they explicitly recovered and that were evaluable (Appendix B.1); this rate is conditional on recovered relations.
智能体仅恢复了具有实质影响的关系图的一部分，但通常会遵守其记录的关系。在缓冲协议下，全部六个模型的 CriticalRecall 介于 35.2% 至 57.7% 之间（表 3）。在 12 个模型–协议组合中，对于智能体明确恢复且可评估的关系，其遵守比例为 98.1%–100.0%（附录 B.1）；这一比例以已恢复的关系为条件。
Across all six models, gold-fed RDS spans 77.5%–99.1%, 22.3–50.2 points above the corresponding agent RDS; Figure 4(a) shows the four highest-RDS models. The gap is diagnostic, not a causal recovery estimate (Section 4.3). The full-gold solver also changes the information horizon and policy, so its residual gap is not pure action loss.
在全部六个模型中，提供金标准信息时的 RDS 介于 77.5%–99.1%，比相应的智能体 RDS 高出 22.3–50.2 个百分点；图 4(a) 展示了 RDS 最高的四个模型。这一差距用于诊断，并非对关系恢复效果的因果估计（第 4.3 节）。完整金标准求解器还改变了信息视野和策略，因此其剩余差距并非纯粹的行动损失。
7.4. RQ4: Strict Completion and Outcomes7.4. RQ4：严格完成与结果
Exact completion was rare, and unsafe merging dominated. Only 8 of 324 runs met Exact Completion; none completed all three repeats for any repository (Table 3). Figure 4(b) shows the outcomes. Across all six models, 226 runs ended with an unsafe merge (69.8%), 59 merged nothing (18.2%), 27 produced deployable nonempty plans (8.3%), and 12 had no valid plan (3.7%).
精确完成很少见，不安全合并占主导地位。324 次运行中仅有 8 次达到了 Exact Completion；没有任何代码仓库在全部三次重复运行中均达到该标准（表 3）。图 4(b) 展示了结果。在全部六个模型中，226 次运行以不安全合并告终（69.8%），59 次未合并任何内容（18.2%），27 次生成了可部署的非空计划（8.3%），12 次没有有效计划（3.7%）。
These four outcomes form a coarse, mutually exclusive partition that groups the eight buckets of Appendix A.4. Deployable requires a nonempty safe delivery and includes the 8 Exact Completions; merged nothing passes all gates without delivery; and no valid plan covers invalid, incomplete, or whole-queue-gate failures, none of which contained an unsafe commit in our runs.
这四种结果构成了一个粗粒度、互斥的划分，将附录 A.4 中的八个类别归为四组。可部署要求交付内容非空且安全，其中包括 8 次精确完成（Exact Completions）；未合并任何内容表示通过了所有门禁但没有交付；没有有效计划则涵盖计划无效、不完整或未通过全队列门禁的情况，在我们的运行中，这些情况均未包含不安全提交。
8. Discussion and Limitations8. 讨论与局限性
The evidence does not isolate one bottleneck. Incomplete CriticalRecall and higher gold-fed RDS implicate missing relation information but do not separate it from subset selection, ordering, feedback-driven revision, or refusal. Batch size and deferral jointly change visibility, timing, feedback, and revisability, so their curves show configuration sensitivity rather than a causal visibility effect.
现有证据无法单独确定某一个瓶颈。CriticalRecall 未达到完整召回，以及提供金标准信息后 RDS 更高，均表明关系信息缺失可能是问题所在，但无法将其影响与子集选择、排序、基于反馈的修订或拒绝执行的影响区分开来。批次大小与延后处理共同改变了可见性、时机、反馈和可修订性，因此其曲线反映的是对配置的敏感性，而非可见性的因果效应。
Claims are bounded by the suite, objective, and setup. The 581 candidates form one designed pool on each of 18 snapshots; neither their relation mix nor the purposive intervals represent natural queue frequencies, other projects, or within-repository variation. The cardinality oracle and equally weighted RDS omit business value, urgency, review cost, and harm severity. Passing means satisfying pinned tests, hidden verifiers, and the registered full gold, not production correctness. All backends share one scaffold, prompt, ledger, arrival order, and turn budget (Section 6.1), so other designs may change results (Yang et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2025; Kapoor et al., 2025). Three runs per cell incompletely characterize run variation.
这些结论受到评测套件、目标和实验设置的限制。在 18 个快照中的每个快照上，581 个候选项构成一个经过设计的候选池；其关系构成与有目的选取的区间均不能代表自然队列中的出现频率、其他项目或仓库内部的变化。基于基数的预言机（cardinality oracle）和等权重 RDS 未考虑业务价值、紧迫性、审查成本和危害严重程度。通过意味着满足固定版本的测试、隐藏验证器以及已登记的完整金标准，而不意味着在生产环境中正确。所有后端共用同一套脚手架、提示词、台账、到达顺序和交互轮次预算（第 6.1 节），因此其他设计可能会改变结果（Yang et al., 2024; Xia et al., 2025; Kapoor et al., 2025）。每个实验单元仅运行三次，无法充分刻画运行间的变异。
Future work should vary objectives and agent design. Alternative scaffolds and a dedicated bulk-PR harness could maintain a richer interaction graph, plan within buffer limits, and replan after gate feedback; value-aware objectives could weight PR importance and risk.
未来工作应探索不同的目标和智能体设计。替代脚手架和专用的批量 PR 测试框架可以维护更丰富的交互图，在缓冲区限制内制定计划，并在收到门禁反馈后重新规划；考虑价值的目标可以为 PR 的重要性和风险赋予权重。
9. Conclusion9. 结论
BulkPR-Bench evaluates queue-level PR governance—recovering consequential relations, choosing a large safe subset, and producing an executable order under rolling release—using execution-validated relation truth, exact optima, and realized-plan scoring across 581 candidates on 18 repository snapshots. Three of six models scored above the strongest sequential baseline in RDS, yet only 8 of 324 runs achieved Exact Completion, separating relation-group progress from dependable whole-queue governance.
BulkPR-Bench 利用经执行验证的关系真值、精确最优解和实际执行计划评分，在 18 个仓库快照的 581 个候选项上评估队列级 PR 治理能力——恢复具有实质影响的关系、选择规模较大的安全子集，以及在滚动发布条件下生成可执行的顺序。六个模型中有三个的 RDS 得分高于最强的顺序基线，但 324 次运行中仅有 8 次实现了精确完成（Exact Completion），表明关系组层面的进展与可靠的全队列治理之间仍存在差距。
Appendix A Protocol and Metric Details附录 A 协议与指标细节
A.1. Execution and IntegrityA.1. 执行与完整性
Each released task pins the base commit, snapshot archive hash, candidate-diff hashes, neutral identifiers, arrival order, and truth fingerprint. Protected diffs, future candidates, signed rolling state, and full gold remain outside the agent-visible tree. Accepted groups execute on an authoritative trunk writable only by the root-side evaluator, which applies the protected diffs in the submitted order and synchronizes the resulting files back to the workspace. It does not trust agent-controlled Git metadata, references, hooks, or claimed merge status. Integrity failures are separated from model protocol failures.
每个发布的任务均固定基础提交、快照归档哈希、候选差异哈希、中性标识符、到达顺序和真值指纹。受保护的差异、未来候选项、经签名的滚动状态和完整金标准均保留在智能体可见目录树之外。获准的组在仅允许 root 端评估器写入的权威主干上执行；评估器按提交的顺序应用受保护的差异，并将生成的文件同步回工作区。它不信任由智能体控制的 Git 元数据、引用、钩子或声称的合并状态。完整性失败与模型协议失败分开处理。
A.2. Solver ReplaysA.2. 求解器重放
Two solver replays use agent-recovered relations. The rolling replay uses the active ledger after updates and retractions at each batch and obeys the original partition, $K$ , and buffering regime. The static replay instead plans once at $K{=}N$ from the final active ledger. A missing or incomplete timeline makes the rolling replay unavailable; the final ledger is not used to reconstruct earlier knowledge. The static replay is a ceiling under a different information and execution regime, not a counterfactual trajectory of the agent.
两种求解器重放均使用智能体恢复的关系。滚动重放使用每个批次经过更新和撤回后的当前有效台账，并遵循原始划分、$K$ 和缓冲机制。静态重放则根据最终的当前有效台账，在 $K{=}N$ 时一次性制定计划。时间线缺失或不完整会导致滚动重放不可用；最终台账不会被用于重建此前的知识状态。静态重放是在不同信息和执行机制下的上限，而非智能体的反事实轨迹。
A.3. Construction Audit and VersioningA.3. 构建审计与版本管理
Registered real-gate states run twice, with caches bypassed on the second pass; critical states receive a flakiness probe and a full-suite confirmation. Green states must show that the intended tests executed. Red states require an executed witness failure or a preregistered failure signature; apply and infrastructure failures never substitute for red. The maximum-safe-subset oracle is independently recomputed at plan freeze, after authoring, and during task compilation. Each accepted version pins the snapshot, diffs, truth fingerprint, test scope, and gate transcripts. Post-freeze drift or any content change invalidates the old task bytes and requires a new version.
已登记的真实门禁状态运行两次，第二次运行时绕过缓存；关键状态还会接受不稳定性探测和完整测试套件确认。绿色状态必须证明预期测试已执行。红色状态要求存在实际执行中出现的见证失败或预先登记的失败特征；差异应用失败和基础设施失败绝不能代替红色状态。最大安全子集预言机在计划冻结时、编写完成后以及任务编译期间均会被独立重新计算。每个获准版本均固定快照、差异、真值指纹、测试范围和门禁记录。冻结后的漂移或任何内容变更都会使旧任务的字节内容失效，并要求创建新版本。
A.4. Terminal Outcome TaxonomyA.4. 终止结果分类
Every scored non-infrastructure trial enters one of eight mutually exclusive buckets, in this priority order: turn-budget exhaustion; invalid output or protocol failure; unsafe-light; unsafe-heavy; exact completion; safe-all-reject; safe-near-optimal; and safe-suboptimal. Unsafe-light violates one gold relation atom, whereas unsafe-heavy violates at least two. Among safe, non-exact runs, safe-all-reject has $|R|{=}0$ , safe-near-optimal has $|R|{=}\mathrm{OPT}_{N}-1$ , and safe-suboptimal contains the remaining positive-yield outcomes. The coarse partition of Section 7.4 groups these buckets—unsafe merge covers unsafe-light and unsafe-heavy—and additionally routes safe runs that fail the whole-queue executability gate into no valid plan. These are machine-judged terminal facts, not automatic explanations of why a run failed.
每个经过评分的非基础设施类试验均按以下优先顺序归入八个互斥类别之一：轮次预算耗尽；输出无效或协议失败；轻度不安全；重度不安全；精确完成；安全但全部拒绝；安全且近乎最优；以及安全但次优。轻度不安全违反一个金标准关系原子，而重度不安全违反至少两个。在安全但未精确完成的运行中，安全但全部拒绝对应 $|R|{=}0$，安全且近乎最优对应 $|R|{=}\mathrm{OPT}_{N}-1$，安全但次优则包含其余产出为正的结果。第 7.4 节的粗粒度划分将这些类别归组，其中不安全合并涵盖轻度不安全和重度不安全，并额外将未通过整个队列可执行性检查的安全运行归入无有效计划。这些是由机器判定的终止状态事实，并不能自动解释运行失败的原因。
