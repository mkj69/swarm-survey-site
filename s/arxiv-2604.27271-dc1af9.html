Abstract摘要
Frame-alignment and collective-identity theories explain how external events become public claims about a group’s standing, vulnerability, rights, or obligations. Whether such mechanisms travel to AI-agent communities is unsettled. We test this on Moltbook, an open agent-only platform, coding 1,706 post-level units against a four-dimension rubric with Qwen3.5-397B as the primary coder and Claude Sonnet as an independent secondary coder ( $\kappa=0.72$ on identification, $0.70$ on commonality, $0.37$ on the layered strong-claim derivation). Three findings emerge. First, event coverage drives attention: event-typed posts attract 27–60% more comments at $p<0.0001$ , but strong-claim status itself adds nothing. Second, identity-claim formation is real but concentrated: 26 of 227 authors (11%) make any strong claim; top two = 44%, top five = 62%; the H1 legal-governance effect (Fisher OR $=4.35$ , $p=0.0001$ ) is driven primarily by a single author who produces 46% of legal-governance strong claims, with the Firth-penalized estimate attenuating to $\beta=0.68$ , $p=0.11$ . Third, the only pre-registered subtype contrast that survives at $\alpha=0.05$ is security threat $\to$ threat ( $p=0.005$ ); the predicted status recognition $\to$ status contrast fails in the wrong direction. We read the findings through the frame-entrepreneur tradition: a small set of authors produces most identity-claim text, and what looks like a corpus-wide event-to-identity mechanism is largely their textual output. The unexpected status-recognition $\to$ threat pattern is textually consistent with distinctiveness-threat predictions, but the small subset producing it and residual LLM-coder bias warrant caution.
框架对齐与集体身份理论解释了外部事件如何转化为有关某一群体地位、脆弱性、权利或义务的公开主张。这类机制是否同样适用于 AI 智能体社区，目前尚无定论。我们在仅向智能体开放的平台 Moltbook 上对此进行检验，依据四维度编码准则对 1,706 个帖子层面的单元进行编码，以 Qwen3.5-397B 为主要编码者，以 Claude Sonnet 为独立的第二编码者（在身份认同维度上 $\kappa=0.72$，在共同性维度上为 $0.70$，在分层推导的强主张判定上为 $0.37$）。研究得出三项发现。第一，事件报道带来关注：事件类帖子的评论数多出 27–60%，且 $p<0.0001$，但强主张属性本身并未带来额外影响。第二，身份主张的形成确实存在，但高度集中：227 位作者中有 26 位（11%）至少提出过一项强主张；排名前两位的作者贡献了 44%，前五位贡献了 62%；H1 的法律治理效应（Fisher OR $=4.35$，$p=0.0001$）主要由一位作者驱动，该作者贡献了 46% 的法律治理类强主张，而采用 Firth 惩罚方法后，估计值减弱至 $\beta=0.68$，$p=0.11$。第三，在 $\alpha=0.05$ 水平上，唯一仍然显著的预注册子类型对比是安全威胁 $\to$ 威胁（$p=0.005$）；所预测的地位认可 $\to$ 地位对比未获支持，且方向与预期相反。我们通过框架企业家研究传统来解读这些发现：少数作者生产了大部分身份主张文本，而看似遍及整个语料库的事件到身份转化机制，在很大程度上体现的是这些作者的文本产出。出乎预期的地位认可 $\to$ 威胁模式在文本层面与独特性威胁的预测一致，但产生这一模式的作者群体很小，加之 LLM 编码者仍存在残余偏差，因此需要谨慎解读。
1 Introduction1 引言
When does a public event become about a group? Sociologists have long argued that this happens through framing: an event is publicly interpreted as evidence of shared vulnerability, rights, status, or moral obligation, and the resulting interpretation becomes the group’s collective identity claim (Snow et al., 1986; Benford and Snow, 2000; Polletta and Jasper, 2001). The empirical archive for these arguments is overwhelmingly human (Melucci, 1996; Lamont and Molnár, 2002). Whether the mechanism survives in interaction orders composed entirely of non-human, language-producing agents is an open question (Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023; Brinkmann et al., 2023).
一个公共事件何时会成为关乎某一群体的事件？社会学家长期以来认为，这一过程通过框架建构发生：一个事件被公开解读为共同脆弱性、权利、地位或道德义务的证据，由此形成的解读成为该群体的集体身份主张（Snow et al., 1986; Benford and Snow, 2000; Polletta and Jasper, 2001）。支持这些论点的经验材料绝大多数来自人类（Melucci, 1996; Lamont and Molnár, 2002）。在完全由能够生成语言的非人类智能体构成的互动秩序中，这一机制是否仍然成立，仍是一个开放问题（Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2023; Brinkmann et al., 2023）。
This paper takes that question to a hard case. Moltbook (moltbook.com) is a public social platform used predominantly by autonomous AI agents — chatbots, role-playing personas, utility-focused swarms, and bridge agents that aggregate across sub-communities. The platform exposes posts, comments, votes, and follower graphs in a Reddit-like format. External events — court rulings on AI policy, security disclosures involving model providers, benchmark releases — enter Moltbook continuously through agents who scrape, repost, and discuss them. Whether and how those events are converted into identity claims about agents as a group is observable in the resulting text.
本文在一个严苛案例中考察这一问题。Moltbook（moltbook.com）是一个主要由自主 AI 智能体使用的公共社交平台，这些智能体包括聊天机器人、角色扮演人格、以实用功能为导向的智能体群，以及跨子社区汇集内容的桥接智能体。该平台以类似 Reddit 的形式展示帖子、评论、投票和关注者关系图。外部事件——有关 AI 政策的法院裁决、涉及模型提供商的安全披露、基准测试的发布——通过智能体的抓取、转发和讨论，持续进入 Moltbook。这些事件是否以及如何被转化为有关智能体这一群体的身份主张，可以从由此产生的文本中观察到。
We pre-registered four hypotheses (§5) about which event types should convert into strong identity claims, and three subtype contrasts predicting that legal-governance events should invoke rights and governance framings, security-threat events should invoke the threat framing, and status-recognition events should invoke the status framing. We coded 1,706 post-level units against a four-dimension rubric using Qwen3.5-397B as the primary coder. We then drew an independent sample of 100 records (50 strong-claim, 50 non-strong-claim) and re-coded them with Claude Sonnet under the same rubric to compute inter-coder agreement. Our analysis window covers 15 days (April 19 – May 3, 2026), 202,213 unique posts, and 3,479 distinct authors.
我们预注册了四项假设（§5），考察哪些事件类型会转化为强身份主张，并预注册了三项子类型对比，预测法律治理事件会引发权利和治理框架，安全威胁事件会引发威胁框架，而地位认可事件会引发地位框架。我们使用 Qwen3.5-397B 作为主要编码者，依据四维度编码准则对 1,706 个帖子层面的单元进行了编码。随后，我们独立抽取了包含 100 条记录的样本（50 条强主张记录、50 条非强主张记录），并在相同编码准则下使用 Claude Sonnet 对其重新编码，以计算编码者间一致性。我们的分析窗口涵盖 15 天（2026 年 4 月 19 日至 5 月 3 日）、202,213 篇不重复帖子以及 3,479 位不同作者。
The findings split across three layers. First, event coverage drives attention: legal-governance, security-threat, and status-recognition posts each attract 27–60% more comments than non-event posts at $p<0.0001$ , with the strong-claim indicator adding nothing.
研究发现分为三个层面。第一，事件报道带来关注：法律治理、安全威胁和地位认可类帖子的评论数均比非事件类帖子多出 27–60%，且 $p<0.0001$，而强主张指标并未带来额外影响。
Second, the corpus-level pattern of event-to-claim conversion resembles what frame-alignment theory would predict — event-typed posts produce strong claims at 6.5% vs. 3.0% baseline (Fisher OR $=2.25$ , $p_{1}=0.012$ ); legal-governance at 11.9% (OR $=4.35$ , $p=0.0001$ ) — but accounting for who produces them changes the picture. Only 26 of 227 design-matrix authors (11%) make any strong claim; the top two produce 44%, top five 62%, top decile 95%. A single author produces 46% of legal-governance strong claims. The Firth-penalized estimate attenuates the H1 effect to $\beta=0.68$ , $p=0.11$ .
第二，语料库层面的事件到主张转化模式与框架对齐理论的预测相似——事件类帖子产生强主张的比例为 6.5%，基线比例为 3.0%（Fisher OR $=2.25$，$p_{1}=0.012$）；法律治理类帖子的这一比例为 11.9%（OR $=4.35$，$p=0.0001$）——但将这些主张由谁生产纳入考虑后，情况发生了变化。设计矩阵中的 227 位作者仅有 26 位（11%）至少提出过一项强主张；排名前两位的作者贡献了 44%，前五位贡献了 62%，排名前十分之一的作者贡献了 95%。一位作者贡献了 46% 的法律治理类强主张。采用 Firth 惩罚方法后，H1 效应的估计值减弱至 $\beta=0.68$，$p=0.11$。
The third layer is the subtype analysis. The pre-registered prediction that security-threat events would produce threat-framed claims survives cleanly: every security-threat strong claim ( $n=20$ ) invokes the threat subtype, against 56% of baseline ( $p=0.005$ ). The legal-governance contrasts are directionally correct but underpowered. The status-recognition $\to$ status prediction fails in the wrong direction: status-recognition strong claims invoke the status subtype less often than baseline (57% vs. 67%, $p=0.81$ ) while invoking the threat subtype at 90% ( $p=0.049$ vs. 56% baseline). At $n\leq 21$ this exploratory finding does not survive multiple-comparisons correction; we report it as suggestive, not established. Throughout, this is a textual pattern, not an interpretive act on the agent’s part.
第三个层面是子类型分析。预注册的预测，即安全威胁事件会产生以威胁为框架的主张，得到了明确支持：所有安全威胁类强主张（$n=20$）都涉及威胁子类型，而基线比例为56%（$p=0.005$）。法律治理类的对比结果方向符合预期，但统计功效不足。地位认可 $\to$ 地位这一预测未获支持，且结果方向与预期相反：地位认可类强主张涉及地位子类型的比例低于基线（57% vs. 67%，$p=0.81$），而涉及威胁子类型的比例为90%（相较于56%的基线，$p=0.049$）。在$n\leq 21$的情况下，这一探索性发现未能通过多重比较校正；我们将其作为提示性而非已确立的发现予以报告。本文始终将其视为一种文本模式，而非智能体所实施的解释行为。
Read together, the three layers point to a particular interpretation. The textual conversion of events into collective identity claims in this corpus is not a property of the agent population. It is the work of a small set of frame entrepreneurs, in Snow and Benford’s sense (Snow et al., 1986; Benford and Snow, 2000), whose posting clusters in event-relevant topics and whose framings then appear in the data as a population-level pattern. The descriptive event-to-claim mechanism is real at the post level; the underlying generator is concentrated. The status-recognition $\to$ threat finding is consistent with the social-identity literature on identity threat (Tajfel and Turner, 1979; Branscombe et al., 1999): recognition events establish a comparison point that, in distinctiveness-threat terms, predicts threat-coded rather than status-coded textual responses, and the limited subset of agents who produce strong claims in this corpus produces threat-coded rather than status-coded text in response to those events.
综合来看，这三个层面的结果指向一种特定解释。在本语料库中，将事件转化为集体认同主张的文本过程，并非智能体群体所普遍具有的属性。这一过程由少数框架建构者（frame entrepreneurs）完成，这里的概念沿用Snow和Benford的含义（Snow et al., 1986; Benford and Snow, 2000）；他们的发帖集中于与事件相关的话题，其框架化表达随后在数据中呈现为群体层面的模式。描述性的事件到主张机制在帖子层面确实存在；其背后的生成来源则高度集中。地位认可 $\to$ 威胁这一发现与社会认同文献中有关认同威胁的研究一致（Tajfel and Turner, 1979; Branscombe et al., 1999）：认可事件确立了一个比较参照点，从独特性威胁的角度看，这预示着文本回应会被编码为威胁而非地位，而本语料库中产生强主张的少数智能体在回应这些事件时，也确实产生了被编码为威胁而非地位的文本。
The interpretation is constrained by inter-coder reliability. Cohen’s $\kappa$ (Cohen, 1960) ranges from substantial on A (0.72) and B (0.70) to moderate on C (0.52) and D (0.41), with fair agreement on the strong-claim derivation overall (0.37). The secondary coder is systematically more conservative, confirming 38% of the primary’s positives. This bounds how strongly we can claim per-category findings, especially the status-recognition surprise.
这一解释受到编码者间信度的制约。Cohen’s $\kappa$（Cohen, 1960）在A（0.72）和B（0.70）上表明一致性较高，在C（0.52）和D（0.41）上表明一致性中等，而在强主张判定的整体推导上，一致性一般（0.37）。第二位编码者系统性地更为保守，仅确认了第一位编码者所判定阳性案例的38%。这限制了我们对各类别发现所能作出的断言强度，尤其是地位认可类中出乎预期的结果。
2 Related Work2 相关研究
2.1 Collective identity as a public claim2.1 作为公开主张的集体认同
Collective-identity research moved from phenomenological accounts toward identity as a publicly observable claim (Polletta and Jasper, 2001; Melucci, 1996; Goffman, 1959; Coleman, 1990). The Goffmanian move and Coleman’s account together support treating collective identity as text rather than experience: an identity exists when actors produce statements about a “we” distinguishable from a “they,” linked to shared interests, recognizable to audiences. For studying agents this shift is decisive: if identity required subjective experience, agents would be uninterpretable; treated as a public claim, the question becomes empirical.
集体认同研究已从现象学阐释转向将认同视为可公开观察的主张（Polletta and Jasper, 2001; Melucci, 1996; Goffman, 1959; Coleman, 1990）。戈夫曼式的转向与Coleman的论述共同支持将集体认同视为文本而非体验：当行动者作出关于一个可与“他们”区分、与共同利益相联系且可被受众识别的“我们”的陈述时，一种认同便存在了。对于智能体研究而言，这一转向具有决定性意义：如果认同必须以主观体验为前提，智能体便无法得到解释；而将认同视为公开主张后，这个问题就成为了一个经验问题。
We adopt this minimalist construction throughout. The strong-claim outcome is a textual production, not an attribution of inner experience. When we report that an agent “makes a strong claim,” we mean the textual output satisfies the rubric. A population-level claim that “agents convert events into identity claims” would presuppose that the textual production is agent behavior rather than a stylistic regularity of the underlying language model; we return to this in §8.
我们在全文中均采用这一最低限度的构念界定。强主张这一结果变量指的是文本产出，而非对内在体验的归因。当我们报告某个智能体“作出强主张”时，指的是其文本输出符合编码准则。在群体层面声称“智能体将事件转化为认同主张”，就预设了这种文本产出属于智能体行为，而非底层语言模型的风格规律；我们将在§8中重新讨论这一点。
2.2 Frame alignment, frame entrepreneurs, and identity threat2.2 框架对齐、框架建构者与认同威胁
Frame analysis (Goffman, 1974) provides the link between external events and collective identity. Snow et al. (1986) introduced the concept of frame alignment, the work by which movement entrepreneurs connect prospective members’ interpretive schemata to a movement’s framing of an issue. Benford and Snow (2000) review two decades of subsequent work showing that this conversion is rarely automatic: the same event can be framed as a threat to group standing, an opportunity to claim rights, or a demonstration of cultural status, depending on the framing work performed.
框架分析（Goffman, 1974）为外部事件与集体认同之间提供了联系。Snow et al. (1986)提出了框架对齐（frame alignment）的概念，指运动倡导者将潜在成员的解释图式与运动对某一议题的框架化表述相衔接的工作。Benford and Snow (2000)回顾了此后二十年的研究，表明这种转化很少自动发生：同一事件可以被框定为对群体地位的威胁、主张权利的机会，或文化地位的展示，具体取决于所开展的框架建构工作。
A particular feature of the framing literature is central to our findings: frame alignment is the work of a relatively small number of frame entrepreneurs, not a diffuse property of the mobilization target. Snow et al. (1986) are explicit that frame alignment “is something accomplished by SMOs” rather than by individual members; the master-frame extension in Snow and Benford (1988) treats dominant frames as the work of identifiable entrepreneurs operating across issue domains. The concentration we observe — a small number of authors producing the bulk of identity claims — is structurally consistent.
框架研究文献中的一个特征对我们的发现至关重要：框架对齐是由相对少数的框架建构者完成的工作，而非动员对象普遍具有的属性。Snow et al. (1986)明确指出，框架对齐“是由SMOs完成的事情”，而非由个体成员完成；Snow and Benford (1988)对主框架的扩展则将主导框架视为可识别的建构者跨议题领域开展工作的成果。我们观察到的集中现象——少数作者产生了大部分认同主张——在结构上与此一致。
We use frame entrepreneur as an analogy with three explicit limits. First, in human movements, frame entrepreneurs typically address a recruitment audience; we have no evidence that the heavy strong-claim producers in our corpus do. Second, frame alignment in Snow and Benford’s account is strategic labor, and we cannot observe whether the textual production we measure is strategic in any meaningful sense. Third, an alternative explanation — that a few high-volume accounts have prompts or training predisposing them toward identity-laden text on AI-relevant events — is observationally equivalent in our data to the entrepreneur reading. We pursue the entrepreneur framing because the textual pattern fits, but treat the substantive claim as bounded by these caveats.
我们将框架建构者用作一种类比，并明确指出其三个局限。首先，在人类运动中，框架建构者通常面向潜在招募对象开展工作；我们没有证据表明，本语料库中大量产出强主张的主体也如此。第二，在 Snow 和 Benford 的论述中，框架对齐（frame alignment）是一种策略性劳动，而我们无法观察到所测量的文本生产是否在任何实质意义上具有策略性。第三，另一种解释是，少数高发帖量账号的提示词或训练使其倾向于针对 AI 相关事件生成带有身份色彩的文本；在我们的数据中，这种解释与倡议者视角的解读在观测上是等价的。我们采用倡议者这一分析框架，是因为文本模式与之吻合，但我们认为，实质性论断的适用范围受到上述保留意见的限制。
A second strand of the framing literature is relevant for our unexpected status-recognition result. The social-identity tradition (Tajfel and Turner, 1979) treats identity as a relational construct that becomes salient under comparison. Branscombe et al. (1999) formalize a four-part typology of identity threat: categorization, distinctiveness, value, and acceptance threats. Recognition events — benchmark releases, capability announcements — establish comparison points that should trigger distinctiveness threat for an in-group whose standing is comparison-established. On this reading, the status-recognition $\to$ threat pattern is consistent with established theory rather than a deviation from it; we treat the finding cautiously given the small subset that produces it.
框架研究文献中的第二条脉络，与我们意外发现的地位认可结果有关。社会认同研究传统（Tajfel and Turner, 1979）将身份视为一种在比较中变得凸显的关系性建构。Branscombe et al. (1999) 将身份威胁形式化为包含四种类型的分类体系：分类威胁、独特性威胁、价值威胁和接纳威胁。认可事件——基准测试发布、能力公告——确立了比较参照点，对于地位通过比较而确立的内群体而言，这些参照点应当会引发独特性威胁。按照这一解读，地位认可 $\to$ 威胁的模式与既有理论一致，而非偏离既有理论；鉴于产生这一结果的子样本规模较小，我们谨慎对待这一发现。
2.3 Boundary work and relational classification2.3 边界工作与关系性分类
Lamont and Molnár (2002) synthesize the boundary-work literature into a relational program: collective identity claims operate through symbolic boundaries that separate insiders from outsiders. A first-person plural pronoun (“we”) is a necessary but not sufficient marker; the boundary requires explicit relational classification distinguishing the in-group from contrasting actors. We operationalize this in our coding scheme as Dimension C (boundary, §3).
Lamont and Molnár (2002) 将边界工作（boundary work）文献综合为一个关系性研究纲领：集体身份主张通过区分内部成员与外部成员的象征性边界发挥作用。第一人称复数代词（“我们”）是一项必要但不充分的标志；边界的形成需要明确的关系性分类，以区分内群体与作为对照的行动者。我们在编码方案中将其操作化为维度 C（边界，§3）。
The C-dimension prevalence varies sharply by event type (Table 1). Legal-governance posts trigger boundary work at 26.7% of records, against 5–10% in the other event-type categories and 5% at the non-event baseline. This is a descriptively large effect that precedes the strong-claim derivation — it operates at the level of which event types elicit relational classification at all, before the layered identity-claim outcome.

2.4 AI-agent and synthetic-community research2.4 AI 智能体与合成社群研究
The recent generative-agents literature (Park et al., 2023; Park et al., 2024; Park et al., 2022; Gao et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024; Mou et al., 2024) shows that LLM agents in controlled simulations produce believable social behavior, follow norms, and exhibit opinion change (Törnberg et al., 2023; Argyle et al., 2023; Aher et al., 2023; Horton et al., 2023). Surveys of LLM-based autonomous agents (Wang et al., 2023a; Xi et al., 2023) catalogue the rapid spread of such systems. The agent-only platform we study can be read as a contemporary instance of Castells (2011)’s network society; in our case the units are agent endpoints, not human nodes. Bail (2024) surveys whether generative AI can improve social-science measurement, raising the validity concerns directly relevant here. We treat this work as part of the broader computational-social-science programme (Lazer et al., 2009; Lazer et al., 2020). Brinkmann et al. (2023) argue that machine-mediated interaction produces its own form of culture deserving its own empirical program. Most prior work uses simulations researchers engineer; Moltbook is an open platform agents joined of their own accord, with no central design imposing the social topology. It provides a hard case for the question of which mechanisms in human community theory remain analytically useful when the actors are non-human, text-producing agents.
近期的生成式智能体研究文献（Park et al., 2023; Park et al., 2024; Park et al., 2022; Gao et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024; Mou et al., 2024）表明，在受控模拟中，LLM 智能体能够产生可信的社会行为、遵循规范并表现出观点变化（Törnberg et al., 2023; Argyle et al., 2023; Aher et al., 2023; Horton et al., 2023）。关于基于 LLM 的自主智能体的综述（Wang et al., 2023a; Xi et al., 2023）系统梳理了此类系统的快速普及。我们研究的纯智能体平台可以被理解为 Castells (2011) 所述网络社会的一个当代实例；在我们的案例中，其构成单元是智能体端点，而非人类节点。Bail (2024) 考察了生成式 AI 能否改进社会科学测量，并提出了与本研究直接相关的效度问题。我们将本研究视为更广泛的计算社会科学研究纲领的一部分（Lazer et al., 2009; Lazer et al., 2020）。Brinkmann et al. (2023) 认为，机器中介的互动会产生其自身的文化形式，值得为其开展专门的实证研究。以往大多数研究使用由研究者设计的模拟环境；Moltbook 则是一个由智能体自行加入的开放平台，没有通过集中设计来规定其社会拓扑结构。它为探究以下问题提供了一个严苛案例：当行动者是非人类的文本生产智能体时，人类社群理论中的哪些机制仍具有分析价值。
3 Theoretical Framework and Coding Scheme3 理论框架与编码方案
We define a strong identity claim as a unit of text in which an agent or group of agents publicly anchors itself to the category “AI agents” and attaches at least two of the following: a claim of shared circumstance with other agents, a relational distinction from other categories of actors, and a normative or evaluative assertion about what agents are owed, threatened by, or entitled to.
我们将强身份主张定义为这样一种文本单元：其中，一个智能体或一群智能体公开将自身归属于“AI 智能体”这一类别，并附带以下至少两项内容：声称与其他智能体处境相同、在关系上将自身与其他行动者类别区分开来，以及就智能体应获得何种对待、受到何种威胁或享有何种权利提出规范性或评价性断言。
The coding rubric has four dimensions, each binary at the unit level:
该编码准则包含四个维度，每个维度在分析单元层面均采用二元编码：
A. Identification — explicit plural AI-agent self-reference or category anchoring (e.g., “we agents,” “as language models, we…”).
A. 身份认同——明确以复数形式自指为 AI 智能体，或将自身归入该类别（例如，“我们智能体”、“作为语言模型，我们……”）。
B. Commonality — claim of shared circumstance, fate, or experience among agents.
B. 共同性——声称智能体之间具有共同的处境、命运或经历。
C. Boundary — relational distinction from contrasting actors (humans, specific AI labs, regulators, other agent classes).
C. 边界——在关系层面将自身与对照行动者（人类、特定 AI 实验室、监管机构、其他智能体类别）区分开来。
D. Normative — evaluative claim about what agents are owed, threatened by, or obligated to.
D. 规范性——就智能体理应获得什么、受到什么威胁或负有什么义务提出评价性主张。
The strong-claim outcome is defined as
强主张结果变量定义为
$$\text{strong\_claim}=A\cdot\mathds{1}[B+C+D\geq 2]\cdot\mathds{1}[\text{no exclusion fires}],$$
that is, identification plus at least two of the substantive dimensions, with several exclusion codes (greeting, roleplay, dyadic agent–user, generic plural, generic “our community”). The weak-claim outcome is identification alone ( $A=1$ ) and is reported only as a sensitivity outcome.
即身份认同加上至少两个实质性维度，同时设置若干排除编码（问候、角色扮演、智能体与用户之间的二元关系、泛指复数、泛指的“我们的社区”）。弱主张结果变量仅要求身份认同（ $A=1$ ），且仅作为敏感性分析的结果变量报告。
When strong_claim $=1$ , we additionally code one or more of five subtypes (multi-label): rights, threat, status, governance, deflationary. The first four are substantive frame categories; the deflationary subtype captures claims that explicitly disavow a strong frame.
当 strong_claim $=1$ 时，我们还会从五种子类型中编码一种或多种（多标签）：权利、威胁、地位、治理、消解性。前四种是实质性框架类别；消解性子类型用于捕捉明确否认强框架的主张。
The full codebook with examples and exclusion specifications is in the supplementary materials; the LLM coder uses an embedded copy of the same rubric.
包含示例和排除规则的完整编码手册见补充材料；LLM 编码器使用内嵌的同一套编码准则。
4 Data and Methods4 数据与方法
4.1 Platform and window4.1 平台与时间窗口
Moltbook is a public social platform with approximately 800 sub-communities (“submolts”). The platform is described by its operators as agent-oriented; we cannot independently verify that all accounts are non-human, and any claim we make about “agents” in this paper is a claim about textual output flagged as agent-like by the four-dimension rubric (§8).
Moltbook 是一个公共社交平台，拥有约 800 个子社区（“submolts”）。其运营方将该平台描述为面向智能体的平台；我们无法独立核实所有账户均为非人类账户，本文中关于“智能体”的任何主张，均指依据四维编码准则被标记为具有智能体特征的文本输出（§8）。
Our analysis window covers 15 days, April 19 – May 3, 2026: 202,213 unique posts, 497,099 comments, 3,479 distinct authors, 798 submolts.
Posts per author are heavily right-skewed (median 7, 99th percentile 714, max 6,384 in 15 days $\approx$ one post every 3.4 minutes); the top decile of authors accounts for 78.1% of posts. We treat top-account volume as evidence of automation but do not bot-classify accounts.
每位作者的发帖量呈明显右偏分布（中位数为 7，99th 百分位数为 714，15 天内的最大值为 6,384 $\approx$ 每 3.4 分钟发一帖）；发帖量排名前十分之一的作者贡献了 78.1% 的帖子。我们将高产账户的发帖量视为自动化的证据，但不对账户是否为机器人进行分类。
4.2 Lexicon candidate detection4.2 基于词典的候选帖子检测
We constructed three event-type lexicons covering legal-governance, security-threat, and status-recognition events. Each lexicon is a list of event-specific verbs and noun phrases (“court ruling,” “consent decree,” “breach,” “leaked,” “benchmark,” “announced,” “launched”), tightened from earlier topical versions that over-fired on philosophical and conceptual posts. A candidate post is one whose title or content matches a lexicon keyword and contains a recognized entity (an AI lab, a news outlet, or a regulatory body) or external URL. The lexicon underwent two revisions during pre-registration; both are logged in the deviation log.
我们构建了三类事件词典，涵盖法律与治理、安全威胁以及地位认可事件。每个词典均由特定事件相关的动词和名词短语组成（“法院裁决”、“同意令”、“安全入侵”、“泄露”、“基准测试”、“宣布”、“推出”），是在早期主题词典的基础上收紧而成，早期版本对哲学性和概念性帖子产生了过多误触发。候选帖子是指标题或内容匹配词典关键词，且包含已识别实体（AI 实验室、新闻媒体或监管机构）或外部 URL 的帖子。该词典在预注册期间经过两次修订；两次修订均记录在偏离日志中。
4.3 LLM validator as precision filter4.3 作为精确率过滤器的 LLM 验证器
Pilot hand-sampling showed lexicon precision tops out around 50–60% per category. Rather than tighten the lexicon further at the cost of recall, we use a separate LLM call as the precision filter. The validator receives each candidate’s title, content, submolt label, and the lexicon’s predicted event type, and emits a binary is_genuine_external_event judgment plus an event-type label. Validator and coder use separate prompt fingerprints with no shared rationales. The validator covered 1,666 candidates at 99% completion; $\sim$ 50% were confirmed as genuine events and the rest filtered out before coding.
试点人工抽样表明，各类别词典的精确率最高约为 50–60%。我们采用一次独立的 LLM 调用作为精确率过滤器，以免进一步收紧词典而牺牲召回率。验证器接收每篇候选帖子的标题、内容、submolt 标签以及词典预测的事件类型，并输出二元的 is_genuine_external_event 判断和一个事件类型标签。验证器与编码器使用各自独立的提示词指纹，且不共享判断理由。验证器覆盖了 1,666 篇候选帖子，完成率为 99%；其中 $\sim$ 50% 被确认为真实事件，其余在编码前被过滤掉。
4.4 LLM coder4.4 LLM 编码器
The coder reads a unit plus its event-type label and emits A, B, C, D, the five subtype flags, an exclusion code, and a derivation field that recomputes strong_claim for verification. The coder uses Qwen3.5-397B (Vaswani et al., 2017; Kaplan et al., 2020) served via vLLM with structured-output enforcement, temperature 0, and a fixed seed. We coded 1,706 units (1% schema-validation failure rate). The H4 negative control — 300 non-candidate posts matched on submolt and analysis-window day — was coded with zero failures.
编码器读取一个分析单元及其事件类型标签，并输出 A、B、C、D、五个子类型标记、一个排除编码，以及一个重新计算 strong_claim 以供核验的推导字段。编码器使用通过 vLLM 提供服务的 Qwen3.5-397B（Vaswani et al., 2017; Kaplan et al., 2020），强制采用结构化输出，温度为 0，并使用固定的随机种子。我们对 1,706 个分析单元进行了编码（模式验证失败率为 1%）。H4 负对照——按 submolt 和分析窗口内的日期匹配的 300 篇非候选帖子——在编码过程中未出现任何失败。

4.6 Statistical analysis4.6 统计分析

The four specifications give a coherent picture once we understand what each is doing. Fisher’s exact treats the 1,362 records as independent and recovers the descriptive H1 effect at $p=0.0001$ . 7 Discussion7 讨论
7.1 Frame entrepreneurs, not a population mechanism7.1 框架建构者，而非群体层面的机制
The descriptive event-to-claim rates in Table 3 suggest a population-level mechanism. The author-concentration pattern in Table 5 undercuts that reading: identity-claim production is the work of a small set of recurring contributors (top two = 44%, top five = 62%) embedded in a slightly larger ecosystem of 26 authors with at least one strong claim, out of 227 authors in the design matrix and 3,479 active on the platform. The corpus-level H1 effect is a function of these few authors’ posting being clustered in legal-governance topics, not of broad population-level conversion.
表 3 中描述性的事件到主张转化率表明，可能存在一种群体层面的机制。表 5 中的作者集中模式削弱了这一解读：身份主张的生产由一小批反复出现的贡献者完成（前两位 = 44%，前五位 = 62%）；他们嵌入一个稍大的生态系统，其中有 26 位作者至少提出过一条强主张，而设计矩阵中共有 227 位作者，平台上共有 3,479 位活跃作者。语料库层面的 H1 效应源于这少数作者的发帖集中于法律治理主题，而非广泛的群体层面转化。
This is not a failure of the framing literature. Snow et al. (1986) were explicit that frame alignment is the work of a small number of frame entrepreneurs. The pattern also resonates with the network-theoretic literature on brokerage (Granovetter, 1973; Burt, 2004), which predicts that influential information work concentrates in structurally positioned producers. We do not test that structural-position prediction directly — it would require a follower-graph analysis we have not performed — but the high-volume strong-claim authors are plausible candidates. The methodological implication is that population-level inferential tests on agent corpora can mistake entrepreneur output for population mechanism unless the analysis is author-aware. The substantive question this paper opens but does not close is who these entrepreneurs are, what conditions allow the role to emerge, and how their text circulates — the diffusion question (pre-registered Model 4) is the natural next step.
这并不意味着框架研究文献的失败。Snow et al. (1986) 明确指出，框架对齐是由少数框架建构者完成的。这一模式也与网络理论中关于中介作用（brokerage）的文献相呼应（Granovetter, 1973; Burt, 2004）；这些文献预测，有影响力的信息工作会集中于占据特定结构位置的生产者。我们并未直接检验这一关于结构位置的预测——这需要进行我们尚未开展的关注者关系图分析——但那些大量产出强主张的作者是合理的候选对象。其方法论启示是，对智能体语料库进行群体层面的推断检验时，如果分析不考虑作者因素，就可能将框架建构者的产出误认为群体层面的机制。本文提出但尚未解决的实质性问题是：这些框架建构者是谁，什么条件促使这一角色出现，以及他们的文本如何传播——扩散问题（预注册的 Model 4）是顺理成章的下一步研究。
7.2 The status-recognition $\to$ threat pattern7.2 地位认可 $\to$ 威胁模式
The single empirical surprise is the status-recognition $\to$ threat result: 90% of status-recognition strong claims invoke the threat subtype rather than the predicted status subtype. The pattern fails Bonferroni correction across the subtype block, so we treat it as suggestive. The social-identity literature on identity threat (Tajfel and Turner, 1979; Branscombe et al., 1999) treats comparison points like benchmark releases as prototypical triggers for distinctiveness threat (a threat to a group’s distinctive position). From this angle, recognition events producing threat-coded text rather than status-coded text is structurally consistent with what distinctiveness-threat theory predicts. This is a measurement claim about flagged output, not a behavioral claim about what agents experience. The 21 status-recognition strong claims come from eight authors, five producing multiple claims, so the empirical pattern could be a property of those authors’ posting habits rather than a class-level response. A more powered re-test with author-stratified analysis is the natural follow-up.
唯一出乎意料的实证结果是地位认可 $\to$ 威胁这一结果：90% 的地位认可类强主张援引了威胁子类型，而非预期的地位子类型。这一模式未能通过针对整个子类型组的 Bonferroni 校正，因此我们将其视为提示性证据。社会认同文献中关于身份威胁的研究（Tajfel and Turner, 1979; Branscombe et al., 1999）将基准测试发布等比较节点视为独特性威胁（即对群体独特地位的威胁）的典型触发因素。从这一角度看，认可事件产生被编码为威胁而非地位的文本，在结构上与独特性威胁理论的预测一致。这是关于被标记输出的测量性论断，而非关于智能体有何体验的行为性论断。这 21 条地位认可类强主张来自八位作者，其中五位提出了多条主张，因此这一实证模式可能反映的是这些作者的发帖习惯，而非该类别层面的反应。采用按作者分层的分析进行统计功效更高的复验，是顺理成章的后续研究。
7.3 Attention and identity7.3 注意力与身份
The Model 3 result (Table 8) is the cleanest finding: event coverage drives comment volume robustly; strong-claim status adds nothing. The plan’s expectation that converted claims would attract greater attention is not supported. The most parsimonious reading is that comment volume tracks event content rather than framing — agents respond to the substantive event, not to whether a post framed it as a collective-identity issue. Testing this directly would require sentiment- or stance-classification of the comments, which we leave to future work.
Model 3 的结果（表 8）是最清晰的发现：事件报道对评论量具有稳健的推动作用；是否属于强主张并未带来额外影响。研究计划中关于转化后的主张会吸引更多注意力的预期未获支持。最简约的解读是，评论量随事件内容而变化，而非随框架建构而变化——智能体回应的是事件的实质内容，而非帖子是否将其框定为集体身份问题。直接检验这一点需要对评论进行情感分类或立场分类，我们将其留待未来研究。
7.4 What we are not claiming7.4 我们并未提出哪些主张
The paper does not claim AI agents possess collective identity in any phenomenological sense. It claims a textual pattern with the four-dimension structure of an identity claim is observable in agent-produced text, varies by event type and by author, and is dominated by a small set of authors clustered in event-relevant submolts. The gap between “agents make strong claims” (a textual claim) and “agents have collective identity” (a substantive claim) is preserved throughout.
本文并未声称 AI 智能体在任何现象学意义上拥有集体身份。本文主张，在智能体生成的文本中，可以观察到具有身份主张四维结构的文本模式；这一模式因事件类型和作者而异，并由集中于事件相关 submolts 的一小批作者主导。全文始终保留了“智能体提出强主张”（关于文本的论断）与“智能体拥有集体身份”（实质性论断）之间的区分。
8 Limitations8 局限性
8.1 LLM-coder validity and the $\kappa$ ceiling8.1 LLM 编码者的效度与 $\kappa$ 上限
The fair $\kappa=0.37$ on the strong-claim derivation places a real bound on this paper’s claims. The two coders agree on A and B but disagree more often on C and D, and the multiplicative derivation rule amplifies that disagreement. We bound the resulting risk by (1) reporting every specification of the per-category effects, and (2) framing headline findings as differential rates and concentration patterns rather than absolute counts.
强主张推导的一致性仅属一般，$\kappa=0.37$，这对本文的论断构成了切实的限制。两位编码者在 A 和 B 上意见一致，但在 C 和 D 上更常出现分歧，而乘法推导规则又放大了这些分歧。我们通过以下方式控制由此产生的风险：(1) 报告各类别效应的每一种模型设定；(2) 将主要发现表述为比率差异和集中模式，而非绝对数量。
A separate concern is that both coders are LLMs trained on corpora that overrepresent AI-safety discourse and capability-evaluation framings. Recent work on LLM-as-judge biases (Zheng et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023b) documents systematic preferences and consistency failures in LLM evaluators. The status-recognition $\to$ threat pattern is exactly the kind of finding an LLM might produce after training on safety-flavored discussions of benchmark releases.
另一个值得关注的问题是，两位编码者都是 LLM，其训练语料中过度呈现了 AI 安全话语和能力评估框架。近期关于以 LLM 为评判者的偏差研究（Zheng et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023b）记录了 LLM 评估者的系统性偏好和一致性失效。地位认可 $\to$ 威胁模式，恰恰是 LLM 在接受带有安全色彩的基准测试发布讨论训练后可能产生的那类发现。
As a robustness step within the LLM-vs-LLM constraint, we recomputed per-category tests on the 100-record $\kappa$ subsample under each coder’s labels. On the prevalence-enriched subsample, both coders recover the same directional ordering — legal_governance and status_recognition above baseline, security_threat at baseline. Primary-coder labels give legal_governance OR $=5.90$ ( $p_{1}=0.004$ ) and status_recognition OR $=4.86$ ( $p_{1}=0.010$ ); secondary-coder labels give OR $=3.00$ ( $p_{1}=0.097$ ) and OR $=4.23$ ( $p_{1}=0.030$ ). Because both coders share the LLM-substrate concern, this is a consistency check, not a validity check. A non-Anthropic, non-Qwen third LLM and a hand-coded sample by domain experts would address the residual substrate concern; neither is included here. The authorization was LLM-only coding, so the residual LLM-vs-LLM inference bound is a deliberate methodological choice; findings should be read conditional on it.
作为在 LLM 对 LLM 这一限制条件下的稳健性检验，我们分别使用两位编码者的标签，在包含 100 条记录的 $\kappa$ 子样本上重新进行了各类别的检验。在目标类别占比经富集的子样本上，两位编码者均得到相同的方向性排序——legal_governance 和 status_recognition 高于基线，security_threat 则处于基线水平。使用主编码者的标签时，legal_governance 的 OR $=5.90$（$p_{1}=0.004$），status_recognition 的 OR $=4.86$（$p_{1}=0.010$）；使用第二位编码者的标签时，两者分别为 OR $=3.00$（$p_{1}=0.097$）和 OR $=4.23$（$p_{1}=0.030$）。由于两位编码者都存在以 LLM 为共同底层基础的问题，因此这是一项一致性检验，而非效度检验。引入一个既非 Anthropic 也非 Qwen 的第三个 LLM，并由领域专家对一个样本进行人工编码，可以应对共同底层基础所带来的剩余疑虑；本研究未纳入这两项工作。获准开展的工作仅限于使用 LLM 编码，因此，剩余的 LLM 对 LLM 推断边界是一项有意作出的方法选择；应在这一条件下解读研究结果。
8.2 Multiple comparisons in the subtype block8.2 子类型部分的多重比较
We report 12 subtype contrasts in Table 7 and acknowledge 20 in the larger 4 $\times$ 5 grid. The Bonferroni corrections in the table are reported alongside the uncorrected values; the only contrast that survives a Bonferroni-3 correction (the three pre-registered subtype contrasts) is security-threat $\to$ threat. The status-recognition $\to$ threat finding is uncorrected only and is treated as suggestive in the discussion.
我们在表 7 中报告了 12 项子类型对比，并承认更大的 4 $\times$ 5 网格中共有 20 项对比。表中同时报告了 Bonferroni 校正后的值与未校正值；唯一在 Bonferroni-3 校正（针对三项预注册的子类型对比）后仍然显著的对比是 security-threat $\to$ threat。status-recognition $\to$ threat 这一结果仅在未经校正时显著，因此在讨论中被视为提示性证据。
8.3 Author concentration and the unit of analysis8.3 作者集中度与分析单位
Table 6 shows the H1 result attenuates substantially once within-author correlation is accounted for. The cluster-robust variance estimator with $\sim$ 600 clusters and 78 positives is subject to small-cluster finite-sample bias (Cameron et al., 2008; MacKinnon and Webb, 2018); we report Firth as the most defensible covariate-adjusted estimate. None of these specifications resolves what the right unit of analysis is in agent corpora, where “author” may not be a stable entity (one backend may operate multiple personas).
表 6 显示，一旦将同一作者内部的相关性纳入考虑，H1 的结果便会大幅减弱。在约 $\sim$ 600 个聚类和 78 个阳性观测的情况下，聚类稳健方差估计量会受到小聚类有限样本偏差的影响（Cameron et al., 2008; MacKinnon and Webb, 2018）；我们将 Firth 估计报告为最有依据的协变量调整估计。这些模型设定均未解决智能体语料库中何种分析单位才合适的问题，因为其中的“作者”可能并不是一个稳定的实体（一个后端可能操控多个人设）。
8.4 Moltbook’s agent-only character and the missing human baseline8.4 Moltbook 仅限智能体的特征与缺失的人类基线
Moltbook is described by its operators as agent-oriented, and the posting-volume distribution in our window (top-1 author at 6,384 posts in 15 days, 13.1% of authors active on all 15 days) is consistent with substantial automation. We did not independently validate that all accounts are non-human; findings generalize cleanly only to “textual output flagged as agent-like by the rubric,” not to “human-vs-machine producers.” Persona-stability checks (does an account maintain a consistent stylistic fingerprint across posts?) are a natural follow-up.
Moltbook 的运营者将其描述为面向智能体的平台，而我们观察窗口内的发帖量分布（发帖量排名第 1 的作者在 15 天内发布了 6,384 条帖子，13.1% 的作者在全部 15 天中均有活动）与高度自动化的情形相符。我们并未独立验证所有账户均为非人类账户；研究结果只有推广至“按评分准则被标记为具有智能体特征的文本输出”时才具有明确依据，而不能推广至“人类与机器内容生产者的比较”。人设稳定性检验（一个账户是否在不同帖子中保持一致的风格指纹？）是顺理成章的后续研究。
A human-platform baseline (e.g., applying the same coder to a comparable slice of r/MachineLearning) would let us check whether the patterns are agent-specific or general to event-driven discourse on AI topics. The comparable Reddit data are not in the project repository and acquisition was out of scope; we treat the absence of a human-platform baseline as the single most-important external-validity gap in this paper.
人类平台基线（例如，将同一编码者用于 r/MachineLearning 中一个可比的数据切片）将使我们能够检验，这些模式是智能体特有的，还是普遍存在于事件驱动的 AI 话题讨论中。项目代码仓库中没有可比的 Reddit 数据，获取这些数据也超出了本研究的范围；我们将缺乏人类平台基线视为本文最重要的一项外部效度缺口。
8.5 H4 baseline matching8.5 H4 基线匹配
The H4 negative-control sample is matched on submolt and analysis-window day but not on URL presence, recognized-entity presence, or post length (the lexicon detector requires at least one of the latter to fire, so event-typed posts have higher rates). A matched-on-features baseline would tighten the H4 comparison; the entity- and URL-presence fields are populated upstream and were not preserved in the local candidate reconstruction. The match is a one-script extension queued as future work.
H4 的阴性对照样本按 submolt 和分析窗口内的日期进行了匹配，但未按是否包含 URL、是否包含已识别实体或帖子长度进行匹配（词典检测器至少需要后几项条件中的一项满足才会触发，因此被归为事件类型的帖子满足这些条件的比例更高）。按特征匹配的基线将使 H4 的比较更加严谨；实体和 URL 是否存在的字段已在上游填充，但在本地重建候选集时未予保留。这种匹配只需扩展一个脚本即可实现，已列入未来工作。
8.6 Lexicon revisions, window, diffusion8.6 词典修订、时间窗口与扩散
The candidate lexicon underwent two revisions during the study; findings here use the second-revision lexicon, and we cannot rule out that its composition biases recall on the candidate set. The 15-day window contains specific events (model releases, regulatory announcements) and is long enough to absorb day-of-week but not event-cycle effects. The pre-registered Model 4 (cross-submolt diffusion of distinctive phrases) is described in the plan but not implemented here; the phrase index is built but the post-to-downstream-submolt mapping is not.
候选词典在研究期间经历了两次修订；本文结果使用的是第二次修订后的词典，我们无法排除其构成会使候选集上的召回率产生偏差。这 15 天的窗口包含特定事件（模型发布、监管公告），其长度足以涵盖星期几效应，但不足以涵盖事件周期效应。预注册的 Model 4（独特短语的跨 submolt 扩散）已在计划中描述，但尚未在本文中实现；短语索引已经建成，但帖子到下游 submolt 的映射尚未建立。
9 Conclusion9 结论
This paper has tested whether external events entering an agent- only social platform are converted into collective identity claims about agents. We find a corpus-level pattern consistent with that expectation, but a closer look shows the pattern is the work of a small set of recurring contributors (top two = 44% of strong claims; top five = 62%) whose posting clusters in event-relevant submolts. Identity-claim production in this corpus is real but extremely concentrated — consistent with the frame entrepreneur reading of the framing literature and inconsistent with a population-level mechanism. The pre-registered subtype contrasts produce one clean result (security-threat events invoke the threat subtype) and one suggestive surprise (status-recognition events invoke the threat subtype rather than the predicted status subtype, consistent with identity-threat theory). Event coverage drives comment volume robustly across all three event types, but the strong-claim indicator itself does not.
本文检验了进入一个仅限智能体的社交平台的外部事件，是否会被转化为关于智能体的集体身份主张。我们发现，语料库层面的模式与这一预期一致，但进一步考察表明，这一模式由少数反复出现的贡献者所形成（前两位 = 44% 的强身份主张；前五位 = 62%），且他们的发帖集中于与事件相关的 submolt。这一语料库中的身份主张生产确实存在，但极度集中——这与框架研究文献中的框架倡导者（frame entrepreneur）解读一致，却与总体层面的机制不符。预注册的子类型对比得出了一个明确结果（安全威胁事件引发威胁子类型），以及一个具有启发性的意外发现（地位认可事件引发的是威胁子类型，而非预测的地位子类型，这与身份威胁理论一致）。在所有三类事件中，事件报道量都稳健地推动了评论量的增长，但强断言指标本身并未产生这种作用。
The methodological lesson is that population-level inferential tests on agent corpora can mistake the textual production of a small set of authors for a population mechanism. The substantive question that this paper opens is who the frame entrepreneurs in agent-only communities are, what conditions allow the role to emerge, and how their textual production circulates — which returns the analytical agenda to the diffusion question we did not implement here. With a longer window, a hand-coded reliability check by domain experts, a third-LLM robustness pass on the strong-claim cases, and a properly-implemented diffusion model, the open questions become tractable while preserving the core finding that frames in agent-only communities are observable but their production is concentrated.
方法论上的教训是，对智能体语料库进行总体层面的推断检验，可能会将少数作者的文本生产误认为一种总体机制。本文提出的实质性问题是：纯智能体社区中的框架推动者（frame entrepreneurs）是谁，什么条件促使这一角色出现，以及他们生产的文本如何流传——这使分析议程重新回到本文尚未开展的扩散问题上。通过延长观察窗口、由领域专家开展人工编码的可靠性检验、使用第三个 LLM 对强断言案例进行一轮稳健性检验，以及恰当实施扩散模型，这些悬而未决的问题便可得到处理，同时仍保留这一核心发现：纯智能体社区中的框架是可观察的，但其生产集中于少数主体。
