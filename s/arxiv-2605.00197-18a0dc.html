硅基社会实用指南：基于 LLM 的社会模拟设计空间The Silicon Society Cookbook: Design Space of LLM-based Social Simulations
摘要Abstract
尝试利用硅基社会（Silicon Societies）模拟人类行为的研究日益增多，与此同时，完全由 LLM 构成的社交网络已开始出现在受控环境之外。 Studies attempting to simulate human behavior with Silicon Societies grow in numbers while LLM-only social networks have started appearing outside of controlled settings. 然而，这些网络的设计空间仍缺乏充分研究，这在一定程度上导致了模型真实性验证方面的缺口。 However, the design space of these networks remains under-studied, which contributes to a gap in validating model realism. 为使未来研究能够作出更有依据的设计决策，我们系统分析了模拟社交网络中关键设计选择的影响及其相互作用，包括用于建模个体智能体的基础模型选择，以及智能体彼此之间的连接方式。 To enable future works to make more informed design decisions, we perform a systematic analysis of the consequences and interactions of key design choices in simulated social networks, including the choice of base model used to model individual agents, and how they are connected to each other. 我们以调查结果作为智能体观点的代理指标，研究发现表明，设计空间的几何结构并不简单：某些参数表现出加性特征，而另一些参数则呈现出更复杂的相互作用。 Using surveys as a proxy for agent opinions, our findings suggest that the geometry of the design space is non-trivial, with some parameters behaving in additive ways while others display more complex interactions. 其中，基础 LLM 的选择是影响模拟结果的最重要变量。 In particular, the choice of the base LLM is the most important variable impacting the simulation outcomes.

1 引言1 Introduction
基于智能体的模型（Agent-Based Models，ABMs）和社会模拟在社会科学中已有悠久历史（De Marchi & Page, 2014），但近年来出现了一种利用大语言模型模拟人类互动的新范式。 Agent-Based Models (ABMs) and social simulations have a long history in social sciences (De Marchi & Page, 2014), but recent years have seen the emergence of a new paradigm using Large Language Models to simulate human interactions. 这类模型有望以任意精度和规模，同时涵盖微观现象（个体用户、消息，甚至给定 token 的激活）和宏观现象（回音室、羊群效应、信息传播），这一前景已吸引了许多研究者（综述见第 2 节）。 The promise of models encompassing both micro- (individual users, messages, or even activations for a given token) and macro-level phenomena (echo chambers, herd effects, information propagation) at arbitrary levels of precision and scale has proven alluring to many (See Section 2 for a survey). 然而，尽管越来越复杂的新模拟器不断发表，许多近期研究长期缺乏验证，这限制了它们在彼此成果基础上进一步发展、并解决传统 ABMs 局限性的能力（Li et al., 2025; Larooij & Törnberg, 2025）。 However, while new increasingly more complex simulators are continuously being published, a chronic lack of validation characterizing many recent works limits their ability to build upon each other and address the limitations of classical ABMs (Li et al., 2025; Larooij & Törnberg, 2025).
本文专门关注硅基社会，并将研究范围限定于此。 In this paper, we are specifically interested in Silicon Societies, to which we limit the scope of our work. 我们将硅基社会定义为利用大语言模型对人类互动进行的模拟。 We define a Silicon Society as a simulation of human interactions using Large Language Models. 这一定义包括旨在复现人类社会互动网络某些特征的受控研究（例如 OASIS，Yang et al. (2025)），但也在较小程度上涵盖由 LLM 构成的自然涌现的社交网络（例如 Moltbook）。 This definition includes controlled studies seeking to reproduce some traits of human social interaction networks (e.g. OASIS, Yang et al. (2025)), but to a lesser extent also covers naturally emerging social networks populated by LLMs (e.g. Moltbook). 我们明确排除以实现某种特定目标（例如编程智能体）而非类人性为目的的多智能体框架，不过我们也注意到，这些框架所开发的某些验证技术或许可迁移至本研究场景。 We explicitly exclude multi-agent frameworks seeking to achieve a particular goal (e.g. coding agents) other than human-likeness, even though we do note that some of the validation techniques they developed may be transferable to this setting.
验证硅基社会并非易事。 Validating Silicon Societies is a non-trivial task. 如何在社交网络尺度上定义并衡量类人性？ How does one go about defining and then measuring human-likeness on the scale of a social network? 已有大量尝试，但其“说服力和严谨性程度各不相同”（Larooij & Törnberg, 2025）。 Numerous attempts have been made, with “varying levels of persuasiveness and rigor” (Larooij & Törnberg, 2025). 截至目前，尚未形成统一标准。 As of now, a standard has yet to emerge. 人类基准通常针对特定应用，因而难以获取。 Human baselines, which are often application-specific, are challenging to obtain. 本文并不试图依据任意选定的指标构建“最真实”的模拟器，而是力求理解我们在设计硅基社会时所选取的参数如何影响模拟过程及其结果。 Instead of attempting to build the ”most realistic” simulator according to arbitrary metrics, in this paper we seek to understand how the parameters we pick when designing a Silicon Society impacts the simulation and its outcome. 刻画这些社会模拟的设计空间，有助于未来研究作出更有依据的设计决策。 Mapping the design space of these social simulations enables future work to make more informed design decisions. 为此，我们对 595 次模拟运行中的轨迹进行系统分析，并将部分参数随机化，以识别各参数影响的性质和强度及其复杂的相互作用。 To this end, we perform a systematic analysis of simulation trajectories over 595 roll-outs with some parameters randomized to identify the nature and intensity of their respective impacts, and their complex interactions.
我们假设，设计空间不会表现出加性特征（假设参数 A 和参数 B 均能独立提高指标 X，若同时采用参数 A 和 B 的模拟所得到的指标 X 高于分别采用其中任一参数时的值，则该空间表现出加性特征）。 We hypothesize that the design space will not behave additively (given that parameter A and parameter B both independently increase metric X, the space behaves additively if a simulation with both parameters A and B has a larger metric X than either of them separately). 相反，我们预期参数之间会呈现复杂的相互作用，使得仅凭初始设置难以预测模拟将如何表现。 Instead, we expect to find that parameters will display complex interactions, making it difficult to predict how a simulation will behave based solely on its initial settings. 我们进一步假设，使用社交媒体数据对 LLM 进行微调，会使其在风格上更接近人类，并改变其在模拟中的行为。 We further hypothesize that fine-tuning LLMs on social media data will make them stylistically closer to humans and change their behavior in simulations.
我们在涵盖七个参数的硅基社会设计空间中，对 595 次模拟运行开展系统研究。 We conduct a systematic study of 595 simulation roll-outs across a Silicon Society design space spanning seven parameters. 我们使用通过调查获得的观点作为智能体信念的操作性代理指标。 We use surveyed opinions as an operational proxy for agent beliefs. 我们的研究发现，设计空间既非处处呈现加性特征，也非处处呈现复杂性。 Our findings reveal that the design space is neither uniformly additive nor uniformly complex. 基础模型的选择是影响最大的单一因素，在风格上的不可区分性（以 BERT 衡量的 AI 可检测性上，$\eta^{2}$ =0.266）和观点动态（在共识净变化上，$\eta^{2}$ =0.090）两个方面均起主导作用。 The choice of base model is the single most influential factor, dominating both stylistic indistinguishability ( $\eta^{2}$ =0.266 on BERT AI-detectability) and opinion dynamics ( $\eta^{2}$ =0.090 on net consensus change). 在社交媒体数据上进行微调与更强的社会动态显著相关，并会降低 AI 可检测性，而带有偏见的新闻智能体对任何指标均无可测量的影响。 Fine-tuning on social media data substantially correlates with stronger social dynamics and reduces AI detectability, while a biased news agent has no measurable effect on any metric. 多种相互作用被证实具有模型依赖性和规模依赖性，这凸显出仅凭边际效应无法可靠预测模拟行为。 Several interactions prove model-dependent and scale-dependent, underscoring that simulation behavior cannot be reliably predicted from marginal effects alone.
我们的主要贡献包括：1) 衡量 Silicon Society 的若干设计决策的相对重要性（从而识别出其中最重要的决策），以及这些决策各自对模拟运行过程的影响性质；2) 刻画设计空间的部分可加性；3) 提供证据表明，在社交媒体数据上对模型进行微调，会使模拟生成的文本在风格上更接近人类社交媒体文本，并加剧模拟过程中的观点动态变化。 Our main contributions are 1) measuring the relative importance of several Silicon Society design decisions (allowing us to identify the most important ones) and the nature of their respective impacts on simulation roll-outs, 2) characterizing the partially-additive nature of the design space, and 3) providing evidence that fine-tuning models on social media data leads to simulations stylistically closer to human social media text and intensified opinion dynamics during simulations.

为避免受限于某个现有模拟器的假设，进而可能无法研究我们感兴趣的全部参数，我们构建了自己的社交网络模拟器，以便完全控制所有假设。 In order to avoid being constrained into some pre-existing simulator’s assumptions, which might prevent us from conducting our study on all the parameters we are interested in, we build our own social network simulator to have full control over all assumptions.
我们的设计大致基于 Vezhnevets et al. (2023)。 Our design is loosely based on Vezhnevets et al. (2023). 我们实例化一组智能体来构成模拟中的群体，并创建一个将它们连接起来的关注关系网络。 We instantiate a set of agents to populate our simulation and create a followership network connecting them together. 具体实现方式取决于模拟参数（见 3.2）。 The specific way in which this is done depends on the simulation parameters (see 3.2). 每个智能体都被赋予一个在整个模拟期间保持不变的“行动概率”。 Agents get attributed an ”action probability” fixed for the duration of the simulation. 在每个模拟步骤中，根据这一行动概率选出 10 个智能体。 At each simulation step, 10 agents are selected according to this action probability. 前 9 个智能体会观察一个近期的讨论串，其中有其关注的对象参与；最后一个智能体则以 1/3 的概率创建新讨论串，以 2/3 的概率回复已有讨论串。 The first 9 observe a recent thread in which someone they follow participated, while the last one has a 1/3 probability of creating a new thread and a 2/3 probability of replying to an already existing one. 有关模拟器的详细信息，见 A.1。 For details on the simulator, see A.1. 模拟共运行 2500 步，每 250 步对智能体进行一次调查。 The simulations run for a total of 2500 steps, with the agents being surveyed every 250 steps. 我们选取了 3 个备选调查问题。 We select 3 possible survey questions. 为了尽可能清晰地呈现观点动态变化，我们选择了最具分歧性的问题（见 A.5）。 In order to make opinion dynamics as clear as possible, we chose questions that are maximally divisive (see A.5). 借助 BluePrint (Bück-Kaeffer et al., 2025)，我们发现每位用户每月平均发布 24.3 条消息，这意味着模拟 1024 个智能体之间的 2500 条消息，相当于约 75h 的模拟时间。 Using BluePrint (Bück-Kaeffer et al., 2025) we find that there are on average 24.3 messages per user per month being posted, meaning simulating 2500 messages between 1024 agents corresponds to about 75h in simulated time. 每次模拟均在一块 A100 GPU 上运行，完成所需时间为 2 至 11h，具体取决于参数设置。 Each simulation runs on a A100 GPU and takes between 2 and 11h to complete depending on the specific parameters.

以 SimBench 基准（Hu et al., 2025）为例，它通过提出一道选择题、收集包含模型给出各个可能答案的概率的向量，并将该向量与人类对同一问题的回答分布进行比较，来衡量 LLM 与人类观点的对齐程度。 Consider the SimBench benchmark (Hu et al., 2025) which measures alignment of LLMs against human opinions by asking a multiple choice question, collecting a vector containing the probability that the model gives each possible answer, and comparing it against the distribution of human answers to that same question. 我们定义矩阵 $H$ 来表示人类基准观点，其中 $H_{ij}$ 是人类在 SimBench 中对问题 $i$ 给出答案 $j$ 的概率。 We define a matrix $H$ that captures the human baseline opinions, where $H_{ij}$ is the probability that a human gives answer $j$ to question $i$ in SimBench. 类似地，矩阵 $M_{\ell}$ 是模型 $\ell$ 的观点矩阵，其中 $M_{\ell:ij}$ 是模型 $\ell$ 对问题 $i$ 给出答案 $j$ 的概率。 Similarly, matrix $M_{\ell}$ is the opinion matrix of model $\ell$ , where $M_{\ell:ij}$ is the probability that model $\ell$ gives answer $j$ to question $i$ . 因此，使 $\ell$ 与人类观点分布对齐可以表述为最小化某种矩阵范数 $||H-M_{\ell}||$。 Aligning $\ell$ with human opinion distribution can then be expressed as minimizing some matrix norm $||H-M_{\ell}||$ .

本质上，我们将一组模型用作向量基，以“张成观点空间”。 Essentially, we are using a set of models as a vector basis to ”span the space of opinions”. 在模拟器中使用以这种方式获得的权重 $w_{\ell}$ 时，我们称之为使用 Distribution 比例，因为我们在分布层面匹配了人类观点。 Using the weights $w_{\ell}$ obtained this way in our simulator, we say that we are using Distribution proportions, because we match human opinions in distribution. 如果改为用 $\hat{H}$ 替换 $H$，即将 $H$ 每一行中的最大元素设为 1，其余元素均设为 0，并针对这一新目标进行优化，我们就称之为使用 Average 比例，因为我们匹配的是平均（最常见的）人类观点。 If instead we replace $H$ by $\hat{H}$ , in which for each row of $H$ we set the maximum element to 1 and all the others to 0, and optimize for this new target, we say that we are using Average proportions, since we match the average (most common) human opinions. 直观地说，我们检验的是：匹配人类观点的完整分布是否比仅匹配最常见的答案更重要。 Intuitively, we test whether matching the full distribution of human opinions matters more than matching only the most common answer. 值得注意的是，尽管 Distribution 和 Average 的权重分布差异很大，但二者得到的 SimBench 得分都高于群体中的任何单个模型（对于 Minitaur，我们得到的得分分别为 17.08 和 17.05，而表现最好的单个模型的得分为 14.60）。 Notably, both Distribution and Average yield SimBench scores higher than any individual model in the population (for Minitaur, we obtain respectively 17.08 and 17.05, while the best individual model stands at 14.60) despite very different weight distributions.

4 基于社交媒体数据的微调4 Fine-tuning on Social Media Data
LLMs 因其特有的“AI”写作风格而备受诟病：有时机械生硬，有时过度附和，其独特的句式和词汇使其显得不自然 (Reinhart et al., 2025)。 LLMs are infamous for their characteristic ”AI” style of writing: sometimes robotic, or over-agreeable, their distinctive sentence structures and vocabulary makes them feel artificial (Reinhart et al., 2025). 经过指令微调的商业模型通常会主动避免表达个人观点，尤其是在有争议的话题上。 Instruction-tuned commercial models often actively avoid presenting personal opinions, especially on controversial topics. 从设计上看，LLMs 被设定为乐于助人、友善且不惹麻烦。 By design, LLMs are helpful, pleasant and non-problematic. 值得注意的是，这些并不是社交媒体话语和互动的典型特征。 Notably, these are not the traits which characterize social media discourse and interactions. 为尝试纠正这些差异，我们使用 BluePrint 数据集 (Bück-Kaeffer et al., 2025) 中的社交媒体数据，通过 LoRAs (Hu et al., 2021) 对模型进行微调。 In an attempt to correct these discrepancies, we use social media data from the BluePrint dataset (Bück-Kaeffer et al., 2025) to finetune models using LoRAs (Hu et al., 2021). 提供角色描述因成本相对较低且操作简单而成为更常见的方法，但有证据表明，基于人口统计特征的角色描述可能使 LLMs 表现出刻板印象式的行为 (Venkit et al., 2026)，而更冗长的描述则可能导致不真实的结果 (Li et al., 2025)，这促使我们采用微调方案。 While providing persona descriptions is a more common approach due to it’s relative low cost and straightforwardness, some evidence suggests that demographic-based persona descriptions may make LLMs behave in stereotypical ways (Venkit et al., 2026) while more verbose descriptions could lead to unrealistic outcomes (Li et al., 2025), motivating our fine-tuning solution.
BluePrint 论文利用用户历史记录计算“用户嵌入”（user embeddings），然后将相似用户聚类为角色原型。 The BluePrint paper uses user histories to compute ”user embeddings”, then clusters similar users together into persona archetypes. 分别基于这些原型训练 LLMs，预计可以得到覆盖广泛用户行为的模型。 Training LLMs on each of these archetypes presumably creates models covering a wide range of user behaviors. 虽然我们使用了该数据集，但对 Bück-Kaeffer et al. (2025) 的方法做了一些修改。 While we use this dataset, we make some changes to the approach of Bück-Kaeffer et al. (2025). 在计算每个用户的嵌入时，我们不仅使用其发布的消息，还纳入了该用户进行过正向互动（点赞、转发）的帖子的嵌入。 Instead of only using messages in order to compute each user embedding, we also include embeddings from posts the user positively interacted with (likes, reposts). 这一改动使我们能够在聚类过程中考虑更多用户行为，并纳入那些不发帖、但通过其他操作参与互动的用户。 This change allows us to consider more user behaviors into our clustering process and include users which do not write posts yet interact through other actions. 我们对四个不同的模型进行了微调：Llama-3.1-Minitaur-8B（一个“人类认知基础模型”，Binz et al. (2024)）、Llama-3.1-8B（用于单独考察 Binz et al. (2024) 工作的影响）、gemma-3-4b-pt 和 Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct。 We finetune four different models: Llama-3.1-Minitaur-8B (a ”foundation model for human cognition”, Binz et al. (2024)), Llama-3.1-8B (to isolate the impact of Binz et al. (2024)), gemma-3-4b-pt and Qwen-2.5-7B-Instruct. 其中，只有 Qwen 经过了指令微调：这是因为有一些有限的证据表明，指令微调可能导致回答多样性坍缩 (Shypula et al., )，而我们预计多样性在模拟社会互动时十分重要。 Among those, only Qwen is instruction tuned: that is because there is some limited evidence suggesting that instruction tuning may collapse answer diversity (Shypula et al., ), and we expect diversity to matter when simulating social interactions.
为衡量此次训练的效果，我们比较了各模型在微调前后于 SimBench (Hu et al., 2025) 上的表现（表 1）。 In order to measure the results of this training, we compare the models on SimBench (Hu et al., 2025) before and after fine-tuning (Table 1). 总体而言，模型在微调后似乎往往能获得更高的分数，这表明使用社交媒体数据训练模型能够提高其与人类观点的一致性。 Overall, it appears that models tend to obtain better scores after fine-tuning, suggesting that training models on social media data increases their alignment with human opinions. 如果模型原本就表现良好，例如 Minitaur，那么这种影响要么可以忽略不计，要么略微为负。 If the model already performed well, as for Minitaur, the effect is either negligible or slightly negative. 虽然这些结果为我们的微调方法提供了依据，但需要牢记，基准测试本身就可能被针对性地优化，而且只能间接反映模型的内在属性。 While these results serve to justify our fine-tuning approach, it is important to keep in mind that benchmarks are inherently gameable, and only provide a proxy to the underlying properties of the models. 值得注意的是，我们后续的结果并未表明，SimBench 分数与模型在风格上更接近人类有关，也未表明其与我们在实验中测量的任何观点动态指标相关。 Notably, our later results do not suggest that SimBench score correlate with more stylistic similarity with humans, or with any of the opinion dynamics metrics we measured in our experiments. 为了更有力地论证我们的方法，我们在第 5.1 节中给出了针对这一训练的消融研究。 For a stronger justification of our approach, we present an ablation study of this training in section 5.1.

5.4 参数空间的几何结构5.4 Geometry of the Parameter Space

可加效应。 Additive Effects. 若干参数在不同的模拟设置下表现出一致的影响。 Several parameters display consistent impacts across different simulation settings. 例如，同质性偏好对同配性变化的影响具有统计显著性，并且无论基础模型或群体规模如何，其影响方向都保持一致。 For instance, the effect of homophily on assortativity shift is statistically significant and maintains a consistent direction regardless of the base model or population size. 同样，提供调查上下文与引入新闻智能体对 BERT 可检测性的影响相互独立（交互对比 IC=-0.006），表明二者以可加的方式提升语言风格的真实感。 Similarly, the impacts of providing survey context and the presence of a news agent on BERT detectability are independent (interaction contrast IC=-0.006), indicating that they contribute additively to stylistic realism.

6.1 结论6.1 Conclusion
我们系统探索了基于 LLM 的社会模拟的设计空间，并将这类模拟称为 Silicon Societies。 We conducted a systematic exploration of the design space for LLM-based social simulations, which we refer to as Silicon Societies. 通过在一系列参数配置（例如基础模型、群体构成比例、网络拓扑）下开展 595 次模拟运行，我们识别出了决定这些模拟环境行为的若干关键因素。 By conducting 595 simulation roll-outs across an array of parameters (e.g. base models, population proportions, network topologies) we identified several key factors that define the behavior of these simulated environments.
我们发现，设计空间的几何结构既不是简单可加的，也并非处处复杂。 We found that the geometry of the design space is neither trivially additive nor uniformly complex. 参数之间的交互作用，例如同质性偏好与模型特有倾向之间的关系，表明研究者不能将模拟设置视为彼此独立的变量。 The interactions between parameters, such as the relationship between homophily and model-specific tendencies, suggest that researchers cannot treat simulation settings as independent variables. 最值得关注的是，我们发现基础模型的选择是决定模拟结果时影响最大的单一因素，其影响超过了网络拓扑等结构性因素。 Most notably, we found that the choice of the base model is the single most influential factor in determining simulation outcomes, outweighing structural factors like network topology. 此外，我们在 BluePrint 数据集上的实验表明，使用真实世界的社交媒体数据对模型进行微调，是迈向实现风格真实性和更强观点动态的一步。 Furthermore, our experiments with the BluePrint dataset demonstrate that fine-tuning models on real-world social media data constitutes a step towards achieving stylistic realism and stronger opinion dynamics.
随着该领域向更复杂的智能体技术发展，本研究为作出更有依据的设计决策提供了基线。 As the field moves toward more complex agentic technologies, this study offers a baseline for more informed design decisions. 未来工作应着重扩展这一设计空间，纳入更精细的动作空间，并探索这些数字社会的长期稳定性。 Future work should focus on expanding this design space to include more nuanced action spaces and exploring the longitudinal stability of these digital societies. 归根结底，理解这些设计约束，是弥合验证差距并将硅基社会（Silicon Societies）转变为可靠的社会科学研究工具的先决条件。 Ultimately, understanding these design constraints is a prerequisite for closing the validation gap and transforming Silicon Societies into reliable tools for social science research. 我们希望这一分析为未来工作构建更有原则依据的模拟器奠定基础，并鼓励研究社区采用更严格的多参数评估框架。 We hope this analysis provides a foundation upon which future works can build more principled simulators, and encourages the community to adopt more rigorous, multi-parameter evaluation frameworks.
6.2 局限性6.2 Limitations
参数空间极其庞大：不仅存在许多我们未涵盖的潜在参数，即便对于已涵盖的参数，我们也远未覆盖其所有可能选项。 The parameter space is immense: not only are there many possible parameters we did not cover, even in the parameters we covered we did not cover nearly all possible options. 我们希望我们的方法能够启发类似工作，探索参数空间中的更多部分。 We hope our approach will inspire similar works to explore more of the parameter space.
基础模型这一变量与各问题特定的初始共识值存在混杂（例如，Qwen 在 Q28 上的初始值为 0.993，Gemma 在 Q29 上的初始值为 0.562），这可能影响同一问题内的比较。 The base model variable is confounded with question-specific initial consensus values (e.g., Qwen on Q28 starts at 0.993, Gemma on Q29 at 0.562), which may affect within-question comparisons.
统计检验未进行多重比较校正。 Statistical tests are unadjusted for multiple comparisons. 由于进行了 30+ 次检验，若干在 $p<0.05$ 水平上名义显著的结果可能是假阳性；应谨慎对待边缘显著的结果（$p<0.05$）。 With 30+ tests performed, several nominally significant results at $p<0.05$ may be false positives; treat marginal results ( $p<0.05$ ) with caution. 我们再次重申，本研究仅将 $p<0.001$ 视为具有统计显著性，并建议读者采用相同标准。p 值使用 Welch’s t 检验计算。 Once again, we reiterate that we only consider $p<0.001$ to be statistically significant in this study, and advise readers do the same. p-values are computed using Welch’s t test. 我们使用正态近似，而非 t 分布的累积分布函数（CDF）。 We use a normal approximation rather than a t-distribution CDF. 这种方法对于中等至大样本是准确的，但对于较小的子群体，准确性略低。 This is accurate for moderate-to-large samples but slightly less accurate for small subgroups.
我们不考虑两个以上参数相互作用产生的效应，因为需要考虑的可能组合数量会迅速激增。 We do not consider effects resulting from the interaction of more than two parameters because the number of possibilities to consider explodes quickly.

A.5 调查问题的设计A.5 Designing survey questions
我们推测，如果一个调查问题使研究人群的意见均分为两派，相比于绝大多数人都支持某一方的情况，它更有可能产生可清晰识别的观点动态。 We conjecture that if a survey question splits the studied population evenly, it is more likely to lead to clearly identifiable opinion dynamics than if there is an overwhelming majority of votes in favor of one side. 鉴于我们已阐明的目标是识别并量化若干设计决策对模拟结果的影响，我们选择了那些看起来更有可能使各次模拟运行之间的差异更加清晰的问题，尽管涉及少数派和多数派观点的动态也有其值得研究之处。 Given our stated goal, which is to identify and quantify the impact of several design decisions on simulation outcomes, we pick questions that appear more likely to make any differences between roll-outs clearer even if there is an argument to be made on the interesting nature of dynamics involving a minority and a majority opinion.

A.7 同质性（Homophily）A.7 Homophily
在该模拟器中，具有同质性的图初始化通过空间与观点对齐策略实现。 In this simulator, graph initialization with homophily is implemented as a spatial-opinion alignment strategy. 这一过程确保意识形态特征相似的智能体位于社交图中的同一“邻域”。 The process ensures that agents with similar ideological profiles are positioned in the same ”neighborhoods” of the social graph.
具体算法遵循以下步骤： The exact algorithm follows these steps:
空间嵌入：我们首先使用 Fruchterman-Reingold 弹簧算法（‘nx.spring_layout‘）计算社交图（幂律聚类图）的二维空间布局。 Spatial Embedding: We first computes a 2D spatial layout of the social graph (Power-law Cluster Graph) using the Fruchterman-Reingold spring algorithm (‘nx.spring_layout‘). 该算法会自然地将连接紧密的节点聚集在欧几里得空间中的相近位置。 This algorithm naturally clusters well-connected nodes together in Euclidean space.
节点排序：随后，根据这一空间布局中的 X 坐标，对图中的所有节点进行排序。 Node Ordering: All nodes in the graph are then sorted based on their X-coordinate from this spatial layout. 这使网络的结构区域形成了一维的“从左到右”排序。 This creates a one-dimensional ”left-to-right” ordering of the network’s structural regions.
观点评分：对于每个智能体（或 LLM 适配器配置），系统根据其针对特定调查问题的基准概率分布，计算观点得分的期望值（例如，将“强烈反对”计为 0 分，将“强烈赞同”计为 4 分）。 Opinion Scoring: For every agent (or LLM adapter profile), the system calculates an expected opinion score based on its baseline probability distribution for specific survey questions (e.g., scoring a ”Strongly Disagree” as 0 and ”Strongly Agree” as 4).
智能体排序：按照模型配置对智能体群体进行分组，并根据其平均观点得分进行排序，从而形成一个从一个极端延伸到另一个极端的意识形态谱系。 Agent Sorting: The agent population is grouped by their model profiles and sorted by their mean opinion scores, creating an ideological spectrum from one extreme to the other.
交错分配：最后，将按空间位置排序的节点直接映射到按观点排序的智能体。 Interleaved Assignment: Finally, the spatially ordered nodes are mapped directly to the opinion-sorted agents.
结果：观点得分最低的智能体被分配到图的空间布局中“最左侧”的节点，而得分最高的智能体则被分配到“最右侧”的节点。 Result: Agents with the lowest opinion scores are assigned to the ”leftmost” nodes of the graph’s spatial layout, while agents with the highest scores are assigned to the ”rightmost” nodes. 由于弹簧布局会将相互连接的节点放置在彼此邻近的位置，这种映射确保社交图中相邻的智能体具有相似初始观点的可能性显著更高，从而形成研究回音室形成过程所需的初始结构同质性。 Because the spring layout places connected nodes near each other, this mapping ensures that neighbors in the social graph are significantly more likely to share similar initial opinions, creating the initial structural homophily required for studying echo chamber formation.
