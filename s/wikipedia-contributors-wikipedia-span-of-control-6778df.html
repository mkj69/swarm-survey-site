管理幅度（span of control），也称管辖幅度（span of management），是企业管理，尤其是人力资源管理中的一个术语。 Span of control, also called span of management, is a term used in business management, particularly human resource management. 该术语指一名主管所负责的直接下属人数（即该主管所支持的人数）。 The term refers to the number of direct reports a supervisor is responsible for (the number of people the supervisor supports).
概述Overview
简单来说，管理幅度是指一名上级能够有效管理的下属人数。 In simple words, the span of control means the manageable number of subordinates of a superior. 一名管理者所管理的下属人数越多，其管理幅度就越大。 The bigger the number of the subordinates a manager controls, the broader is her/his span of control.
过去某个时期，在层级制企业组织中，平均管理幅度为1-to-4甚至更小的情况并不少见，即一名管理者平均监督四名员工。 In a hierarchical business organization of some time in the past it was not uncommon to see average spans of 1-to-4 or even less, i.e., one manager supervised four employees on average. 在1980年代，企业领导者将许多组织结构扁平化，使平均管理幅度趋近于1-to-10。 In the 1980s, corporate leaders flattened many organizational structures, causing average spans to move closer to 1-to-10. 这主要得益于低成本信息技术的发展。 That was made possible primarily by the development of inexpensive information technology. 随着信息技术的发展，许多中层管理者的任务——例如收集、处理和呈现运营信息——变得更加轻松，高层管理者发现，他们可以雇用更少的中层管理者，以更低的成本完成更多工作，并管理更多下属。 As information technology was developed capable of easing many middle manager tasks – tasks like collecting, manipulating, and presenting operational information – upper managers found they could hire fewer middle managers to do more work managing more subordinates for less money.
当前，组织正转向自主型跨职能团队及其他形式的非层级结构，这使管理幅度这一概念的重要性有所降低。 The current shift to self-directed cross-functional teams and other forms of non-hierarchical structures have made the concept of span of control less important.
关于最优管理幅度的理论可以追溯到V. A. Graicunas。 Theories about the optimum span of control go back to V. A. Graicunas. 1933年，他基于有关心智能力和注意力持续时间的假设，提出了一套实用的启发式规则。 In 1933, he used assumptions about mental capacity and attention span to develop a set of practical heuristics. Lyndall Urwick（1956）基于地理分散程度和面对面会议的需要，提出了一种理论。 Lyndall Urwick (1956) developed a theory based on geographical dispersion and the need for face-to-face meetings. 尽管此后进行了多次尝试，但仍未提出令人信服的理论。 In spite of numerous attempts since then, no convincing theories have been presented. 这是因为最优管理幅度取决于众多变量，包括组织结构、可用技术、所履行的职能，以及管理者和员工的胜任能力。 This is because the optimum span of control depends on numerous variables, including organizational structure, available technology, the functions being performed, and the competencies of the manager as well as staff. Elliott Jaques提出了另一种观点：管理者所能拥有的直接下属人数上限，取决于其能够亲自了解多少人，而这种了解应达到能够评估其个人工作效能的程度。 An alternative view is proposed by Elliott Jaques that a manager may have up to as many immediate subordinates that they can know personally in the sense that they can assess personal effectiveness.
确定管理幅度时应考虑的因素Factors considerable deciding span of control
影响管理幅度的因素如下： These are the factors affecting span of control:
地理分散程度：如果企业的各个分支机构分布广泛，管理者就会难以监督每个分支机构，因此管理幅度会较小。 Geographical dispersion, if the branches of a business are widely dispersed, then the manager will find it difficult to supervise each of them, as such the span of control will be smaller.
员工能力：如果员工具备很强的能力，只需少量监督即可独立工作，例如Y理论所描述的那类人，那么由于他们具有工作动力并会主动开展工作，就无须对其进行密切监督；因此，管理幅度可以更大。 Capability of employees: if employees are highly capable, need little supervision, and can be left on their own, e.g., Theory Y type of people, they need not be supervised closely as they are motivated and take initiative to work; as such, the span of control may be broader.
管理者能力：经验丰富、充分理解工作任务、深入了解员工且与员工关系良好的管理者，能够监督更多员工。 Capability of managers, an experienced manager with good understanding of the tasks, good knowledge of the workers and good relationships with the workers, will be able to supervise more workers
管理者的增值作用：通过培训员工并培养其新技能来创造价值的管理者，相比仅专注于绩效管理的管理者，需要更小的管理幅度（这一点与上述员工能力因素的作用方向相反）。 Value-add of the manager: a manager that is adding value by training and developing new skills in the workers will need a more narrow span of control than one who is focused only on performance management (this is the reverse of the capability of workers point above)
任务相似性：如果下属执行的任务相似，管理幅度就可以更大，因为管理者能够同时监督所有下属。 Similarity of task: if the tasks that the subordinates are performing are similar, then the span of control can be wider, as the manager can supervise them all at the same time.
其他任务的工作量：如果管理者还承担其他职责，例如担任委员会成员、参与其他项目或与利益相关者联络，其直接下属人数就需要更少。 Volume of other tasks: if the manager has other responsibilities, such as membership of committees, involvement in other projects, liaising with stakeholders, the number of direct reports will need to be smaller
必须完成的行政事务：如果管理者需要定期举行面对面会议、完成考核与发展计划、讨论薪酬福利、编写职位说明书和劳动合同、解释用工政策变化，以及处理其他行政事务，管理幅度可能会缩小。 Required administrative tasks: if the manager is required to have regular face-to-face meetings, complete appraisal and development plans, discuss remuneration benefits, write job descriptions and employment contracts, explain employment policy changes, and other administrative task:, span of control may be reduced.
业务流程的精简、有效性和效率可以缩小管理幅度。 Business process streamlining, effectiveness, and efficiency can reduce the span of control.
理论考量Theoretical considerations
最早提出较为一般性的管理理论的是Henry Fayol，他在担任煤钢企业Commentary-Fourchambault Company总经理期间积累了实践经验。 The first to develop a more general theory of management was Henry Fayol, who had gathered empirical experience during his time as general manager of a coal and steel company, the Commentary-Fourchambault Company. 他率先将管理者视角引入组织治理问题。 He was the first to add a managerial perspective to the problem of organizational governance. 对沟通渠道规定严格层级的理由，在于各项活动需要纵向整合，而这种需要源于管理层对控制和信息的需求。 The rationale for defining a strict hierarchy of communication channels is found in the need for vertical integration of activities, imposed by management's need for control and information.
然而，对下属开展的活动实施控制并监控其沟通，会使较高层级的节点承受信息过载，因为所有通往组织结构其他分支的沟通都要经由这些节点传递。 However, exercising control over activities performed by subordinates and monitoring their communication would inflict information overload to the nodes at the upper hierarchical levels, since all communication to other branches of the organizational structure would be routed through them. 此外，下属人数越多，主管就越需要监控其所在层级以下的大量互动；信息过载与管理幅度呈正相关。 In addition, a larger number of subordinates also requires supervisors to monitor a high number of interactions below their own level; information overload and span of control are positively correlated.

Graicunas 通过这些公式表明，每增加一名下属，潜在互动的数量都会显著增加。 Graicunas showed with these formulas that each additional subordinate increases the number of potential interactions significantly. 显然，任何组织都无法承担维持如此庞大的控制结构所需的代价，而在统一指挥条件下实施等级链恰恰需要这种规模的控制结构。 It appears natural that no organization can afford to maintain a control structure of a dimension being required for implementing a scalar chain under the unity of command condition. 因此，必须寻找其他机制来解决这一两难问题：既要维持管理控制，又要将成本和时间保持在合理水平，这也使管理幅度成为组织的一项关键指标。 Therefore, other mechanisms had to be found for dealing with the dilemma of maintaining managerial control, while keeping cost and time at a reasonable level, thus making the span of control a critical figure for the organization. 因此，长期以来，确定最优管理幅度一直是组织设计面临的一项重大挑战。 Consequently, for a long time, finding the optimum span of control has been a major challenge to organization design. 正如 Mackenzie (1978, p 121) 所描述的： As Mackenzie (1978, p 121) describes it:
“可以认为，管理幅度越大，监督成本就越有可能降低，因为主管在组织成员中所占的比例更小。 ”One could argue that with larger spans, the costs of supervision would tend to be reduced, because a smaller percentage of the members of the organization are supervisors. 另一方面，如果管理幅度过大，主管可能没有能力有效监督如此众多的直接下属。 On the other hand, if the span of control is too large, the supervisor may not have the capacity to supervise effectively such large numbers of immediate subordinates. 因此，在力求平衡这些可能相互对立的趋势时，可能需要作出权衡。” Thus, there is a possible trade-off to be made in an attempt to balance these possibly opposing tendencies.”
Fayol 提出，只要下属员工的上级同意这一程序，就应允许他们彼此直接沟通。 Fayol proposed that subordinate employees should be allowed to communicate directly with each other, given that their superiors had agreed upon this procedure. 这一原则后来被称为法约尔桥。 This principle became known as Fayol's bridge.
法约尔桥的运用使得其他一些方面也需要纳入考虑。 The use of Fayol’s bridge resulted in a number of other aspects needing to be taken into consideration. 为了使这一制度发挥作用，必须放弃 Taylor 的职能工长制，并确立统一指挥原则。 In order to put this system to work, Taylor’s functional foremanship has to be abandoned, and unity of command needs to be established. 与此同时，决策权被分配给组织较低层级的个人，只有超出员工预先规定的决策权限范围的决策才会向上提交。 At the same time, decision power is distributed to individuals on lower levels in the organization, and only decisions that exceed the pre-defined decision scope of an employee are referred upwards. 这反过来又强化了权力与责任的对等性。 This, in turn, strengthens the co-equality of authority and responsibility. 由于法约尔桥（Fayol's bridge）并不局限于组织内的某个职能领域，而是可以跨越职能边界，例如从采购延伸到制造，因此可以将其视为在一定程度的自主管理下，对相关活动进行横向整合的初步尝试，即早期的业务流程。 Since a Fayol's bridge is not limited to a certain functional area within the organization, but can span over functional boundaries, e.g. from purchasing to manufacturing, it can be considered as a first attempt to create a horizontal integration of related activities under a certain level of self-management, an early business process.
麦肯齐及其他学者（Massie 1965, Pugh et al., 1972）也指出，并不存在普遍适用的最优管理幅度（span of control）。 Mackenzie and others (Massie 1965, Pugh et al., 1972) also noted that there is no generally applicable optimum span of control. 相反，期望的控制程度与组织的可管理性之间的平衡受到多种因素的影响。 There are instead several factors influencing the balance between the desired level of control and the manageability of the organization.
首先，这取决于组织成员、管理者和员工的能力。 Firstly, it depends on the capabilities of the organizational members, managers and workers. 当时人们认为，任何管理者都无法监督超过 5-6 名直接下属。 It was assumed, that no manager would be capable of supervising more than 5-6 direct subordinates. 然而，这一结论建立在如下假设之上：上级必须主动监督所有下属的工作。 However, this conclusion built on the assumption that the superior must actively monitor the work of all subordinates. 后来，戴维斯（1951）将管理工作分为两类，一类需要关注体力劳动，另一类需要开展脑力活动，从而对这一论断作出了更细致的区分。 Later on, this statement was diversified when Davis (1951) divided managerial work into two categories, one requiring the attention to physical work, the other one requiring mental activity. 根据监督类型的不同，高层管理者以管理 3-8 名下属为宜，而基层主管，即监督车间人员的主管，则可以管理多达 30 名下属。 Depending on the type of supervision, a span of 3-8 subordinates for managers at higher levels was considered adequate, while first level supervisors, i.e. those supervising shop floor personnel could have up to 30 subordinates.
新古典理论家提出了另一种解决方案。 The neoclassical theorists have developed a different solution. 他们认为，可以将相当一部分决策权下放给组织较低层级的成员。 They assumed that a considerable amount of decisions could be delegated to organizational members at lower organizational levels. 这一方案相当于将法约尔桥与他提出的员工主动性原则结合起来加以应用。 This solution would be equivalent to the application of Fayol's bridge combined with the principle of employee initiative that he proposed. 因此，监督需求将从直接控制减少为例外处理。 As a result, the need for supervision would be reduced from direct control to exception handling. 基于这一假设，他们认为，在常规情形下，只要能够联系到负责监督的管理者，就足以满足控制的需要。 According to this assumption, they considered the opportunity of having access to a supervising manager would be sufficient to satisfy the need for control in standard situations. 彼得·德鲁克将这一原则称为管理责任幅度。 Peter Drucker refers to this principle as the span of managerial responsibility.
