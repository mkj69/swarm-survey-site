Abstract摘要
The capabilities of frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) continue to advance, with different providers increasingly specializing in distinct domains. This raises a natural next objective: how to combine the individual specializations of various LLMs into a collectively intelligent system. To this end, we report the development of Sakana Fugu, a family of orchestrator models that harness and amplify the capabilities of an LLM agent team. Fugu models are themselves language models trained to understand user queries and dynamically devise agentic scaffolds to solve them. Through these adaptive scaffolds, Fugu accesses performance beyond any individual LLM agent, achieving state-of-the-art results compared to other publicly accessible models across a range of challenging tasks, including SWE-Bench Pro, Terminal Bench, LiveCodeBench, GPQA-Diamond, Humanity’s Last Exam, and CharXiv Reasoning. We release two models: Fugu, which balances performance with latency for everyday use, and Fugu-Ultra, which prioritizes answer quality on the hardest problems. We describe our training paradigm, which encompasses large-scale fine-tuning, evolutionary algorithms, and reinforcement learning approaches, along with the infrastructure and core design principles that turn these methods into a production system. We hope this report encourages further research into multi-agent systems and dynamic, query-adaptive agentic scaffolds as a path toward the next frontier of AI capabilities, accessed through collective intelligence. https://sakana.ai/fugu

Frontier Large Language Models (LLMs) now approach or match expert-level performance across a wide range of domains (Luong and Lockhart, 2025; Anthropic, 2026d; OpenAI, 2026a; Patwardhan et al., 2025; Nori et al., 2025). For example, Gemini-3-Deep-Think recently achieved gold-medal performance at the International Mathematical Olympiad (Luong and Lockhart, 2025), GPT-5.5 disproved an 80-year-old Erdős conjecture in combinatorial geometry (OpenAI, 2026a), and Claude-Mythos uncovered numerous zero-day vulnerabilities in OpenBSD and FreeBSD (Carlini et al., 2026). Yet as frontier capabilities advance, we also observe increasingly differentiated model specializations at multiple levels of granularity. At the domain level, past GPT-series models often displayed state-of-the-art performance on mathematical reasoning tasks (OpenAI, 2026a; Glazer et al., 2024), while Opus-series models have specialized in software engineering and cybersecurity (Carlini et al., 2026; Jimenez et al., 2024). Within a single domain, the differences can be even more fine-grained: in competitive coding, Gemini-3.1-Pro can be particularly effective at directly implementing known algorithms, while GPT-series models can often excel at planning and combining multiple algorithmic ideas to solve the hardest problems (Zheng et al., 2026). These observations suggest that the next frontier may not be achieved by any single model alone, but by systems that can identify, combine, and amplify the complementary strengths of many models. A system that can engineer software like the strongest coding model, reason mathematically like the strongest theorem-proving model, and dynamically decide when each specialization is needed would provide a natural path toward expanding frontier performance through collective intelligence.
前沿大语言模型（LLMs）如今在广泛领域中的表现已接近或达到专家水平 (Luong and Lockhart, 2025; Anthropic, 2026d; OpenAI, 2026a; Patwardhan et al., 2025; Nori et al., 2025)。例如，Gemini-3-Deep-Think 最近在国际数学奥林匹克竞赛中取得了金牌水平的成绩 (Luong and Lockhart, 2025)，GPT-5.5 推翻了组合几何中一个已有 80 年历史的 Erdős 猜想 (OpenAI, 2026a)，而 Claude-Mythos 则在 OpenBSD 和 FreeBSD 中发现了大量零日漏洞 (Carlini et al., 2026)。然而，随着前沿能力的提升，我们也观察到模型在多个粒度层面上的专长日益分化。在领域层面，以往的 GPT 系列模型往往在数学推理任务上展现出最先进的性能 (OpenAI, 2026a; Glazer et al., 2024)，而 Opus 系列模型则在软件工程和网络安全方面形成了专长 (Carlini et al., 2026; Jimenez et al., 2024)。即使在同一领域内，这种差异也可能更加细致：在竞赛编程中，Gemini-3.1-Pro 在直接实现已知算法方面可能尤为有效，而 GPT 系列模型则往往擅长规划并组合多种算法思路，以解决最困难的问题 (Zheng et al., 2026)。这些观察表明，下一前沿或许无法仅由任何单一模型独自实现，而需要能够识别、组合并增强多个模型互补优势的系统。如果一个系统能够像最强的编程模型一样开发软件，像最强的定理证明模型一样进行数学推理，并动态决定何时需要哪种专长，那么它将为通过集体智能拓展前沿性能提供一条自然的路径。
At the same time, recent improvements in LLM performance have been driven not only by advances in the underlying models but also by agentic scaffolds that treat the LLM as one component within a larger system (Hu et al., 2025). Such scaffolds augment autoregressive generation with structured prompting (Wei et al., 2022; Yao et al., 2022; Madaan et al., 2023), external tool use and function calling (Qu et al., 2025; Schick et al., 2024), environment feedback, and memory management (Zhang et al., 2025). This is especially visible in software engineering, where Claude Code and other modern agentic scaffolds can shape the model’s interaction trajectory, supply continuous environment feedback, and turn raw model capability into an iterative agentic workflow (Anthropic, 2026a). These developments suggest that capability should be understood not only as a property of the model, but also as a property of the scaffold through which the model acts.
与此同时，近期 LLM 性能的提升不仅源于底层模型的进步，也得益于将 LLM 作为更大系统中一个组件的智能体支撑框架 (Hu et al., 2025)。此类支撑框架通过结构化提示 (Wei et al., 2022; Yao et al., 2022; Madaan et al., 2023)、外部工具使用和函数调用 (Qu et al., 2025; Schick et al., 2024)、环境反馈以及记忆管理 (Zhang et al., 2025)，增强自回归生成。这一点在软件工程中尤为明显：Claude Code 和其他现代智能体支撑框架能够塑造模型的交互轨迹，持续提供环境反馈，并将模型的原始能力转化为迭代式智能体工作流 (Anthropic, 2026a)。这些进展表明，能力不仅应被理解为模型的属性，也应被理解为模型借以行动的支撑框架的属性。
The growing range of language models with diverse capabilities and the impact of domain-specific agentic scaffolds motivate the development of systems that can orchestrate collective intelligence: dynamically choosing which models to involve, how they should communicate, use tools and interact with the environment, and when their outputs should be synthesized. We view this kind of model orchestration as a new complementary scaling axis beyond ever larger and expensive language models. If successful, this direction could make frontier-level capability more efficient, more modular, and more broadly accessible.
具备不同能力的语言模型种类日益丰富，领域专用智能体框架（agentic scaffolds）的作用也愈发显著，这推动了能够编排集体智能的系统的开发：动态选择让哪些模型参与、它们应如何通信、使用工具并与环境交互，以及何时综合它们的输出。我们将这种模型编排视为一种新的互补性扩展维度，超越了单纯追求规模更大、成本更高的语言模型的路径。如果取得成功，这一方向有望使前沿水平的能力更高效、更模块化，并更易于广泛获取。
In this technical report, we detail the development of Sakana Fugu, our system harnessing the benefits of collective intelligence to realize this vision. Sakana Fugu is a family of language models trained to adaptively and dynamically orchestrate a team of more powerful frontier agent workers. In our initial release, we make two variants available to the public, targeting different operating points. Fugu is optimized for speed, selecting a single worker per input so that its latency is comparable to a direct call to a frontier model, while still routing each query to the most capable agent for that input. Despite this efficiency, Fugu matches and in many cases surpasses best-in-class performance across tasks. Fugu-Ultra is optimized for performance, composing workflows of multiple agents per input. It therefore trades additional latency for higher quality and is intended for the most complex tasks that benefit from combining multiple specializations. Our Fugu models surpass publicly accessible frontier models and are shoulder-to-shoulder with Fable 5 and Mythos Preview in various rigorous engineering, scientific, and reasoning benchmarks while delivering frontier capability without the risk of export controls. Beyond raw performance, we believe learned orchestration carries broader implications. If capability can be amplified by composing existing models rather than only by training larger ones, then progress in AI need not depend solely on access to the largest training runs. Orchestration offers a more modular and accessible path, in which improvements arise from how models are selected, coordinated, and combined, and in which newly released models can be incorporated into the worker pool as they appear. This composability also gives users meaningful control over the system. For example, agent pools can be configured to favor particular providers, exclude specific models, or respect data, privacy, and compliance constraints, without retraining. More speculatively, treating orchestration as a first-class scaling axis may have economic and geopolitical consequences, distributing the benefits of frontier AI more broadly across organizations and regions rather than concentrating them in those able to train the largest models. We offer Fugu as a first step in this direction, and hope it encourages further research into collective intelligence as a path toward the next frontier of AI capabilities.
除了直接的性能表现之外，我们认为，通过学习实现的编排还具有更广泛的意义。如果可以通过组合现有模型来增强能力，而非仅通过训练更大的模型来实现，那么 AI 的进步就不必完全依赖于开展最大规模训练的条件。编排提供了一条更模块化、更易于采用的路径：改进源于模型的选择、协调与组合方式，而新发布的模型也可以在推出时纳入工作者池。这种可组合性也让用户能够对系统进行实质性的控制。例如，无需重新训练，即可配置智能体池，使其优先采用特定提供商、排除特定模型，或遵守数据、隐私和合规方面的约束。进一步推测，将编排视为一个核心扩展维度，可能会产生经济和地缘政治影响，使前沿 AI 的益处更广泛地惠及各类组织和地区，而非集中于那些有能力训练最大模型的组织和地区。我们推出 Fugu，作为朝这一方向迈出的第一步，并希望它能推动对集体智能的进一步研究，将其作为通向 AI 能力下一前沿的路径。
2 Related Work2 相关工作
LLM Collective Intelligence. Collective intelligence studies how groups of relatively limited individuals can, through interaction, produce behavior that exceeds the capability of any single member (Ha and Tang, 2022; Jiang et al., 2025). This perspective has recently become increasingly relevant for LLMs, as frontier models from different providers exhibit complementary strengths and weaknesses. A growing body of work therefore studies how to coordinate groups of LLM agents through communication protocols, voting mechanisms, routing policies, and learned collaboration structures (Du et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2025; Dang et al., 2025; Guha et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Yue et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024). Recent multi-agent LLM systems often rely on hand-designed collaboration patterns: for example, Du et al. (2023) and Wang et al. (2025) orchestrate multiple agents across successive discussion or reasoning rounds, using fixed interaction structures to improve final answers. Other works move toward learned or adaptive coordination. Guha et al. (2024) and Yue et al. (2025) learn representations that map queries to suitable agents or topologies, Zhuge et al. (2024) treats collaboration as a learnable graph, and Chen et al. (2024) learns a router that directs each query to a single best-matched agent.
LLM 集体智能。集体智能研究的是，由能力相对有限的个体组成的群体如何通过交互，产生超越任何单个成员能力的行为 (Ha and Tang, 2022; Jiang et al., 2025)。近来，这一视角对 LLM 的适用性日益凸显，因为不同提供商的前沿模型展现出互补的优势与劣势。因此，越来越多的研究开始探索如何通过通信协议、投票机制、路由策略以及通过学习获得的协作结构来协调 LLM 智能体群体 (Du et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2025; Dang et al., 2025; Guha et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Yue et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2024)。近期的多智能体 LLM 系统通常依赖人工设计的协作模式：例如，Du et al. (2023) 和 Wang et al. (2025) 在连续多轮讨论或推理中编排多个智能体，利用固定的交互结构来改进最终答案。另一些工作则转向通过学习实现协调或采用自适应协调。Guha et al. (2024) 和 Yue et al. (2025) 学习将查询映射至合适智能体或拓扑结构的表示，Zhuge et al. (2024) 将协作视为可学习的图，而 Chen et al. (2024) 则学习一个路由器，将每个查询导向与之最匹配的单个智能体。
Sakana Fugu follows this line of work in treating intelligence as an emergent property of coordinated model collectives. However, our goal differs from that of many prior multi-agent systems in two respects. First, rather than exposing a multi-agent workflow that users must design, tune, or operate, Sakana Fugu presents the collective as a single model interface. Second, rather than relying solely on fixed communication patterns or single-step routing, Fugu models are themselves trained orchestrators that can adaptively decide how to use an agent pool for each query. In this sense, Sakana Fugu aims to make collective intelligence not only a research paradigm for studying LLM collaboration, but also a practical model interface for accessing complementary model capabilities.
Sakana Fugu 延续了这一研究路线，将智能视为经过协调的模型群体所具有的涌现属性。然而，我们的目标在两个方面不同于许多以往的多智能体系统。首先，Sakana Fugu 将模型群体呈现为单一模型接口，而不是向用户提供需要自行设计、调优或运行的多智能体工作流。其次，Fugu 模型本身就是经过训练的编排器，能够针对每个查询自适应地决定如何使用智能体池，而非仅仅依赖固定的通信模式或单步路由。从这个意义上说，Sakana Fugu 旨在使集体智能不仅成为研究 LLM 协作的研究范式，也成为调用模型互补能力的实用模型接口。
Agentic Scaffolds. To elevate LLMs from autoregressive chatbots into action-taking agents, a large body of work has developed agentic scaffolds that elicit reasoning, planning, reflection, and tool use from an underlying model. A first line of work shapes the model’s reasoning through prompting and decoding strategies, including chain-of-thought prompting (Wei et al., 2022), self-consistency (Wang et al., 2022), and structured search over reasoning paths such as Tree of Thoughts (Yao et al., 2023). A second line interleaves reasoning with acting, allowing the model to plan, call tools, and incorporate observations: ReAct couples reasoning traces with actions (Yao et al., 2022), while Toolformer and subsequent work equip models with external tools and function calling (Schick et al., 2024; Qu et al., 2025). A third line adds feedback and iteration, where models critique and revise their own outputs through self-reflection (Madaan et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023) or by incorporating signals from an external environment. Together with growing support for long-horizon memory (Zhang et al., 2025), these components transform a static model into a capable agent.
智能体支架（Agentic Scaffolds）。为了将 LLM 从自回归聊天机器人提升为能够采取行动的智能体，大量研究开发了智能体支架，以激发底层模型的推理、规划、反思和工具使用能力。第一类研究通过提示和解码策略塑造模型的推理过程，包括思维链提示（Wei et al., 2022）、自一致性（Wang et al., 2022），以及对推理路径进行结构化搜索的方法，如 Tree of Thoughts（Yao et al., 2023）。第二类研究将推理与行动交织起来，使模型能够规划、调用工具并整合观察结果：ReAct 将推理轨迹与行动相结合（Yao et al., 2022），而 Toolformer 及后续研究则为模型配备外部工具和函数调用能力（Schick et al., 2024; Qu et al., 2025）。第三类研究引入反馈和迭代，使模型通过自我反思（Madaan et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023）或整合外部环境中的信号，评判并修订自身的输出。这些组件与日益完善的长期记忆支持（Zhang et al., 2025）相结合，将静态模型转变为能力强大的智能体。
These ideas have also been consolidated into production harnesses that wrap frontier models in rich interactive environments. In software engineering, for example, systems such as Claude Code and Codex manage repository context, tool use, code execution, editing loops, and environment feedback, often contributing substantially to end-to-end performance beyond the raw model call itself (OpenAI, 2026b; Anthropic, 2026a). These systems demonstrate that the practical capability of an LLM depends critically on the scaffold through which it interacts with the task environment. However, such harnesses are typically designed for a specific class of tasks and expose the scaffold as an external system around the model. Sakana Fugu takes a different approach, in which the scaffold is generated by a trained orchestrator at inference time. Rather than applying a fixed interaction pattern or relying on a task-specific harness, Fugu dynamically decides how to reason about each request, which frontier agents to involve, how they should communicate, and how their outputs should be synthesized into a final answer.
这些理念也被整合进生产级运行框架（harness），将前沿模型封装于丰富的交互环境之中。例如，在软件工程领域，Claude Code 和 Codex 等系统管理代码仓库上下文、工具使用、代码执行、编辑循环及环境反馈，往往能在原始模型调用本身的基础上，显著提升端到端性能（OpenAI, 2026b; Anthropic, 2026a）。这些系统表明，LLM 的实际能力在很大程度上取决于其与任务环境交互时所依托的支架。然而，此类运行框架通常针对某一特定类别的任务设计，并将支架作为模型外围的外部系统呈现。Sakana Fugu 采用了不同的方法，由经过训练的编排器在推理时生成支架。Fugu 不采用固定的交互模式，也不依赖针对特定任务的运行框架，而是动态决定如何针对每个请求进行推理、让哪些前沿智能体参与、它们应如何通信，以及应如何将它们的输出综合为最终答案。
Model Merging. Another line of work studies how to combine the capabilities of multiple models through model merging. At the parameter level, early approaches use static recipes such as weight averaging, model soups, or task-balanced interpolation to integrate capabilities across domains with minimal additional computation (Goddard et al., 2024). More recent work introduces optimization-based merging, for example evolutionary search over “merging recipes” showing that learned strategies can outperform hand-designed ones and yield stronger generalization (Akiba et al., 2025). Other methods improve the reliability of parameter-space fusion by resolving task conflicts, sparsifying parameter updates, or preserving directions important to individual models (Yadav et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2024). However, because parameter-space merging operates directly on weights, it requires access to model parameters and typically assumes architectural or representational compatibility. This confines its applicability largely to open-source checkpoints and makes it unsuitable for composing the closed-source frontier models that currently define much of the state of the art.
模型合并（Model Merging）。另一类研究探讨如何通过模型合并来结合多个模型的能力。在参数层面，早期方法使用权重平均、模型汤（model soups）或任务平衡插值等静态配方，以极少的额外计算整合跨领域能力（Goddard et al., 2024）。较新的研究引入了基于优化的合并方法，例如对“合并配方”进行演化搜索，表明学习得到的策略能够优于人工设计的策略，并实现更强的泛化能力（Akiba et al., 2025）。其他方法通过解决任务冲突、稀疏化参数更新，或保留对各个模型重要的方向，提高参数空间融合的可靠性（Yadav et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2024）。然而，由于参数空间合并直接操作权重，因此需要访问模型参数，并且通常假设模型在架构或表示上具有兼容性。这使其适用范围主要局限于开源模型检查点，也使其不适合组合当前在很大程度上代表最先进水平的闭源前沿模型。
A complementary family of methods composes models in the data-flow or representation space. Instead of averaging all weights, these approaches stitch layers, mix blocks, route hidden states, or construct hybrid architectures from existing components (Bansal et al., 2021; Akiba et al., 2025). Such methods relax some constraints of full parameter merging and can combine capabilities at a more modular level, but they still generally require internal access to model activations, layers, or architectures. They therefore cannot be generally applied to heterogeneous API-only systems, where models may differ in architecture, provider, context interface, latency, cost, and availability.
一类与之互补的方法在数据流或表示空间中组合模型。这些方法不对所有权重取平均，而是拼接层、混合模块、路由隐藏状态，或利用现有组件构建混合架构（Bansal et al., 2021; Akiba et al., 2025）。此类方法放宽了完整参数合并的某些限制，能够在更具模块化的层面上结合能力，但通常仍需要访问模型内部的激活值、层或架构。因此，它们无法普遍适用于仅能通过 API 访问的异构系统，因为这些系统中的模型可能在架构、提供商、上下文接口、延迟、成本和可用性方面存在差异。
Sakana Fugu can be viewed as a macro-level analogue of model merging. Rather than merging weights or stitching layers, it composes model capabilities at the behavioral level, treating frontier models as black-box agents and learning how to route, coordinate, verify, and synthesize their outputs. In this sense, Fugu performs a form of functional model composition wherein it aims to preserve and amplify the complementary specializations of different models without requiring parameter access or architectural compatibility. This macro-level perspective allows Fugu to incorporate closed-source frontier models, heterogeneous providers, and user-specific constraints, while still pursuing the central goal of model merging so that it combines multiple specialized models into a stronger collective system.
Sakana Fugu 可以视为模型合并在宏观层面的对应形式。它不合并权重或拼接层，而是在行为层面组合模型能力，将前沿模型视为黑箱智能体，并学习如何对它们的输出进行路由、协调、验证和综合。从这个意义上说，Fugu 实现了一种功能层面的模型组合（functional model composition），旨在保留并增强不同模型之间互补的专长，而无需访问模型参数，也不要求架构兼容。这种宏观视角使 Fugu 能够纳入闭源前沿模型、不同的模型提供商以及用户特定的约束，同时仍然追求模型合并的核心目标，即将多个专用模型组合成一个更强大的协同系统。
3 Sakana Fugu3 Sakana Fugu
Sakana Fugu is a family of learned orchestrators that expose a multi-agent system through a single model interface. Given a user query, a Fugu model constructs an agentic scaffold over a pool of frontier LLM workers, deciding which workers to involve, what instructions or roles to assign, how intermediate outputs should be combined or verified, and when to synthesize the final answer. The user interacts with Fugu as if calling a single model, while internally the system can route, delegate, and coordinate across multiple specialized agents. We release two variants targeting different points on the quality-latency frontier. Fugu balances strong performance with low latency, making it suitable for everyday interactive use and configurable deployment constraints. Fugu-Ultra prioritizes answer quality, using deeper orchestration over a larger worker pool at the cost of additional latency.
Sakana Fugu 是一个经过学习的编排器系列，通过单一模型接口提供多智能体系统的功能。给定用户查询，Fugu 模型会基于一个前沿大语言模型工作者池构建智能体框架，决定调用哪些工作者、分配什么指令或角色、如何组合或验证中间输出，以及何时综合生成最终答案。用户与 Fugu 交互时就像在调用单个模型，而系统内部则可以在多个专门化智能体之间进行路由、任务委派和协调。我们发布了两个变体，分别面向质量与延迟权衡前沿上的不同位置。Fugu 在强大性能与低延迟之间取得平衡，适合日常交互式使用，并可适应可配置的部署约束。Fugu-Ultra 优先考虑答案质量，通过在更大的工作者池上进行更深入的编排来提升质量，代价是增加延迟。
3.1 Fugu: Balancing Performance and Latency3.1 Fugu：平衡性能与延迟
Fugu is the latency-aware variant of Sakana Fugu, designed for interactive and everyday workloads where response time matters alongside answer quality. It builds on Trinity (Xu et al., 2025), but scales and adapts the learned-orchestration idea to a production setting in which the orchestrator must make fast, reliable routing decisions under reasonable latency and must be optimized not only for single-step performance but also for multi-turn end-to-end interactive tasks common in real-world use.
Fugu 是 Sakana Fugu 中具备延迟感知能力的变体，专为交互式和日常工作负载而设计；在这些场景中，响应时间与答案质量同样重要。它以 Trinity (Xu et al., 2025) 为基础，将学习式编排的理念扩展并调整至生产环境；在这种环境中，编排器必须在合理的延迟内快速、可靠地做出路由决策，而且优化目标不仅包括单步性能，还包括实际使用中常见的多轮端到端交互任务。
3.1.1 Parametrization3.1.1 参数化
Fugu’s orchestrator is designed as a fast decision module over a pool of frontier worker models. Fugu uses a pre-trained language model as its backbone and coordinates the worker pool based on its own hidden states. We provide a high-level illustration of this process in Figure 2.
Fugu 的编排器被设计为一个面向前沿工作者模型池的快速决策模块。Fugu 使用预训练语言模型作为骨干网络，并根据自身的隐藏状态协调工作者池。我们在图 2 中给出了这一过程的概览示意。
Figure 2: Fugu Parametrization. A lightweight selection head operates in parallel to the base model’s LM head. It takes a hidden state $h$ from the orchestrator backbone as input and outputs $L$ logits, one for each worker model in the pool. Unlike the Trinity coordinator, Fugu does not assign roles. The selected model is always invoked as a worker, which reduces the coordination space and lowers orchestration latency. We also fine-tune the singular-value scales of selected parameter matrices in the LM’s layers, indicated by the red diagonal lines. In the figure, the hidden state at the position marked by “ $<$ Head Input $>$ ” is the input to the lightweight head. The semantic correspondence between the decoded message “ $<$ BOS $>$ …” and the hidden state is shown only for illustration, since the lightweight head operates on the internal hidden state at that position, not on the final decoded text.
图 2：Fugu 的参数化。一个轻量级选择头与基础模型的语言模型头（LM head）并行运行。它以编排器骨干网络的隐藏状态 $h$ 为输入，输出 $L$ 个 logits，分别对应池中的每个工作者模型。与 Trinity 协调器不同，Fugu 不分配角色。被选中的模型始终以工作者身份被调用，从而缩小协调空间并降低编排延迟。我们还对语言模型各层中选定参数矩阵的奇异值缩放系数进行微调，图中以红色对角线表示。在图中，标记为“ $<$ Head Input $>$ ”的位置处的隐藏状态是轻量级头的输入。解码后的消息“ $<$ BOS $>$ …”与隐藏状态之间的语义对应关系仅用于示意，因为轻量级头处理的是该位置处的内部隐藏状态，而非最终解码出的文本。
Concretely, given a pool of $L$ worker models, we attach a lightweight prediction head after the final hidden layer of the orchestrator backbone. For a hidden state $h\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$ , the head outputs $L$ logits that score which worker model should be selected for the input. Unlike Trinity, which additionally assigns one of several roles to the selected model, Fugu always dispatches the query to the selected model as a worker. Removing role assignment narrows the coordination space to model selection alone, which keeps the orchestration decision simple and minimizes the latency overhead of the orchestrator.
具体而言，给定一个包含 $L$ 个工作者模型的池，我们在编排器骨干网络的最后一个隐藏层之后附加一个轻量级预测头。对于隐藏状态 $h\in\mathbb{R}^{d}$，该预测头输出 $L$ 个 logits，为针对该输入应选择哪个工作者模型进行评分。Trinity 还会从若干角色中选择一个分配给选中的模型，而 Fugu 与之不同，始终将查询派发给选中的模型，并由其作为工作者执行。取消角色分配后，协调空间被缩小为仅包含模型选择，从而使编排决策保持简单，并将编排器带来的延迟开销降至最低。
To improve the representation used by this lightweight head without full fine-tuning, we adapt a small subset of the backbone parameters using singular-value fine-tuning, following recent work on efficient adaptation (Sun et al., 2025). For selected weight matrices in the orchestrator backbone, we decompose the matrix and train only the singular-value scales, keeping the orthogonal components fixed. Together with the lightweight prediction head, this yields an extremely small trainable parameter set while still allowing the orchestrator representation to effectively adapt to the routing problem.
为了在不进行全量微调的情况下改进这一轻量级头所使用的表示，我们遵循近期关于高效适配的研究 (Sun et al., 2025)，通过奇异值微调来调整骨干网络中的一小部分参数。对于编排器骨干网络中选定的权重矩阵，我们先进行矩阵分解，然后仅训练奇异值缩放系数，同时保持正交分量固定。结合轻量级预测头，这种方法使可训练参数集极小，同时仍能让编排器的表示有效适应路由问题。
A key design choice is that Fugu uses the orchestrator’s logits rather than its generated text. Since prompting and task execution are delegated to the selected frontier worker model, the orchestrator only needs to produce a worker-selection decision. This makes inference substantially cheaper: the system can compute a hidden state at an early token position, apply the selection head, and immediately dispatch the query to the selected worker, without the expensive autoregressive decoding process. This decision-only parametrization is central to Fugu’s latency profile and also makes evolutionary optimization practical, as described in the following subsection.
一个关键的设计选择是，Fugu 使用编排器的 logits，而不是其生成的文本。由于提示构建和任务执行均委派给选中的前沿工作者模型，编排器只需做出工作者选择决策。这显著降低了推理成本：系统可以在较早的词元位置计算隐藏状态，应用选择头，并立即将查询派发给选中的工作者，无需进行开销高昂的自回归解码过程。这种仅针对决策的参数化方式是决定 Fugu 延迟特性的关键，也使演化优化切实可行，具体将在下一小节介绍。
3.1.2 Supervised Fine-tuning on Single-Step Tasks3.1.2 在单步任务上进行监督微调
We train Fugu in two stages, starting with large-scale supervised fine-tuning (SFT). For this stage, we assemble a large collection of single-step tasks spanning coding, mathematics, reasoning, language understanding, and numerous agentic scenarios. Each question $q_{i}$ in this collection $\mathcal{D}$ is verifiable and has a ground-truth solution $s_{i}$ .
我们分两个阶段训练 Fugu，首先进行大规模监督微调（SFT）。在这一阶段，我们汇集了大量单步任务，涵盖编程、数学、推理、语言理解以及众多智能体应用场景。该集合 $\mathcal{D}$ 中的每个问题 $q_{i}$ 都是可验证的，并具有标准答案 $s_{i}$ 。
To construct training labels, we run every worker model $\mathcal{M}_{j}$ where $j=1\cdots K$ in the pool on $q_{i}$ for $n$ repetitions and measure each model’s performance by comparing with $s_{i}$ , yielding for each model a set of candidate solutions $\{o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{1},o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{2},...,o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{n}\}$ and a corresponding reward set $\{r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{1},r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{2},...,r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{n}\}$ . These rewards induce a ranking over the worker pool, from the model best suited to the problem to the least suited. We summarize for each model $j$ the performance on $q_{i}$ by the average of its rewards, $\bar{r}_{i,j}\;=\;\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{k},$ and collect these scores into a vector $\mathbf{s}_{i}=(\bar{r}_{i,1},\dots,\bar{r}_{i,K})$ .
为构建训练标签，我们让模型池中的每个工作模型 $\mathcal{M}_{j}$（其中 $j=1\cdots K$）针对 $q_{i}$ 重复运行 $n$ 次，并通过与 $s_{i}$ 比较来衡量各模型的表现，从而为每个模型得到一组候选解 $\{o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{1},o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{2},...,o^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{n}\}$ 及其对应的奖励集合 $\{r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{1},r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{2},...,r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{n}\}$ 。这些奖励确定了工作模型池的排序，从最适合该问题的模型到最不适合的模型依次排列。对于每个模型 $j$，我们用其奖励的平均值 $\bar{r}_{i,j}\;=\;\frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n}r^{\mathcal{M}_{j}}_{k},$ 概括它在 $q_{i}$ 上的表现，并将这些分数组成向量 $\mathbf{s}_{i}=(\bar{r}_{i,1},\dots,\bar{r}_{i,K})$ 。
Rather than discarding the reward magnitudes by supervising on the hard ranking alone, we convert the scores into a soft target distribution over workers via a softmax transform,
我们并非仅以硬排序进行监督而丢弃奖励的数值大小，而是通过 softmax 变换将这些分数转换为工作模型上的软目标分布，
$$p_{i}(j)\;=\;\frac{\exp(\bar{r}_{i,j}/\tau)}{\sum_{j^{\prime}=1}^{K}\exp(\bar{r}_{i,j^{\prime}}/\tau)},$$
with temperature $\tau$ . We use this distribution as supervision, training both the lightweight selection head and the singular-value scales in the orchestrator backbone to minimize:
其中温度为 $\tau$ 。我们将该分布用作监督信号，同时训练轻量级选择头和编排器主干网络中的奇异值缩放系数，以最小化：
$$\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SFT}}(\theta)=\frac{1}{|\mathcal{D}|}\sum_{i=1}^{|\mathcal{D}|}\mathbb{D}_{KL}\!\left(p_{i}(\cdot)\,\|\,\pi_{\theta}(\cdot\mid q_{i})\right),$$
Learning from a soft performance distribution, rather than classifying a single best label, gives the orchestrator a richer training signal and makes its selection more robust when several workers are similarly capable. Because supervision is derived directly from measured worker performance and does not require generation from the orchestrator itself, this stage provides an efficient and stable initialization for the subsequent evolutionary optimization stage.
从软性能分布中学习，而非对单个最佳标签进行分类，可以为编排器提供更丰富的训练信号，并使其在多个工作模型能力相近时做出更稳健的选择。由于监督信号直接源于实际测得的工作模型性能，且不需要编排器自身生成内容，因此这一阶段为后续的演化优化阶段提供了高效、稳定的初始化。
3.1.3 Applying Evolutionary Strategies on End-to-end Tasks3.1.3 在端到端任务上应用演化策略
After supervised fine-tuning on single-step tasks, we further optimize Fugu with evolutionary strategies on end-to-end tasks. While single-step tasks provide clean correctness signals and broad coverage across domains, they do not fully capture how models are used in real interactive systems. We therefore collect real-world multi-turn trajectories from different coding-assistant environments like Claude Code, Codex, and OpenCode, and construct end-to-end tasks that involve repository context, iterative editing, tool calls, execution feedback, and final task completion. This stage expands the training distribution from static questions to agentic workflows that better reflect production usage. Formally, for an end-to-end task $q_{i}$ drawn from a collection $\mathcal{E}$ , the orchestrator interacts with the harness over a sequence of turns. Let $s_{t}$ denote the interaction state at turn $t$ , i.e., the task together with the transcript of prior turns, tool calls, and execution feedback. At each turn, Fugu computes a hidden state $h(s_{t})$ and selects a worker $a_{t}\sim\pi_{\theta}(\cdot\mid s_{t})$ with $\pi_{\theta}(a\mid s)\propto\exp\!\big(f_{\theta}(h(s))_{a}\big)$ , yielding a trajectory $\tau=(s_{0},a_{0},s_{1},a_{1},\dots,s_{T})$ of horizon $T\leq B$ , where $B$ is a fixed turn budget. A terminal reward $R(\tau)\in\{0,1\}$ records whether the task is ultimately completed.
在单步任务上完成监督微调后，我们进一步在端到端任务上使用演化策略优化 Fugu。尽管单步任务提供了明确的正确性信号，并广泛覆盖各个领域，但它们无法全面反映模型在真实交互式系统中的使用方式。因此，我们从 Claude Code、Codex 和 OpenCode 等不同的编程助手环境中收集真实的多轮交互轨迹，并构建涉及代码仓库上下文、迭代编辑、工具调用、执行反馈以及最终任务完成的端到端任务。这一阶段将训练分布从静态问题扩展到更能反映生产环境实际使用情况的智能体工作流。形式化地说，对于从集合 $\mathcal{E}$ 中抽取的端到端任务 $q_{i}$，编排器通过一系列轮次与运行框架进行交互。令 $s_{t}$ 表示第 $t$ 轮的交互状态，即任务本身以及此前各轮交互、工具调用和执行反馈的记录。在每一轮中，Fugu 计算隐藏状态 $h(s_{t})$，并选择一个工作模型 $a_{t}\sim\pi_{\theta}(\cdot\mid s_{t})$，其中 $\pi_{\theta}(a\mid s)\propto\exp\!\big(f_{\theta}(h(s))_{a}\big)$，从而产生一条长度为 $T\leq B$ 的轨迹 $\tau=(s_{0},a_{0},s_{1},a_{1},\dots,s_{T})$，其中 $B$ 为固定的轮次预算。终止奖励 $R(\tau)\in\{0,1\}$ 记录任务最终是否完成。
These end-to-end trajectories also reveal differences between agents that are not visible from performance scores alone. Some models may be strong at high-level reasoning or proposing implementation plans, but less reliable when operating tools, editing files, or reacting to environment feedback. Others may be less impressive on standalone reasoning benchmarks but more robust inside an interactive coding harness. Training on these trajectories therefore helps Fugu learn a more practical notion of worker capability, one that accounts not only for answer quality in isolation, but also for how well each worker performs when embedded in a scaffold with tools and feedback.
这些端到端轨迹还揭示了仅凭性能分数无法观察到的智能体之间的差异。有些模型可能擅长高层次推理或提出实现方案，但在操作工具、编辑文件或响应环境反馈时可靠性较低。另一些模型在独立推理基准测试中的表现可能不那么突出，但在交互式编程运行框架中更加稳健。因此，在这些轨迹上进行训练有助于 Fugu 学会以更贴近实际应用的方式理解工作模型的能力，这不仅考虑孤立情境下的答案质量，也考虑各工作模型嵌入带有工具和反馈的支撑框架后表现如何。
For this stage, we use sep-CMA-ES, following the optimization approach of Trinity. Concretely, we directly maximize the expected terminal reward of the orchestrator,
在这一阶段，我们沿用 Trinity 的优化方法，使用 sep-CMA-ES。具体而言，我们直接最大化编排器的期望终止奖励，
$$J(\theta)\;:=\;\mathbb{E}_{\tau\sim\pi_{\theta}}\!\big[R(\tau)\big],$$
over end-to-end task outcomes. sep-CMA-ES maintains a parent parameter vector $\theta_{t}$ , a step size $\sigma_{t}$ , and a diagonal covariance $D_{t}$ , and at each iteration samples a population of $\lambda$ candidates,
该期望针对端到端任务的结果计算。sep-CMA-ES 维护一个父代参数向量 $\theta_{t}$、一个步长 $\sigma_{t}$ 和一个对角协方差矩阵 $D_{t}$，并在每次迭代中采样一个包含 $\lambda$ 个候选解的种群，
$$\theta^{(k)}\;=\;\theta_{t}+\sigma_{t}\,D_{t}\,z^{(k)},\qquad z^{(k)}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I),\quad k=1,\dots,\lambda.$$
Each candidate’s fitness $J(\theta^{(k)})$ is estimated by averaging the terminal reward over replicated end-to-end runs, and the top- $\mu$ candidates are recombined via fitness-weighted averaging to form the next parent,
每个候选解的适应度 $J(\theta^{(k)})$ 通过对重复进行的端到端运行的终止奖励取平均来估计，随后通过按适应度加权平均，将排名前 $\mu$ 的候选解重组为下一代父代，
$$\theta_{t+1}\;=\;\theta_{t}+\sigma_{t}\,D_{t}\sum_{j=1}^{\mu}w_{j}\,z_{j:\lambda},$$
where $z_{j:\lambda}$ is the perturbation of the $j$ -th best candidate under the fitness ranking and $\{w_{j}\}_{j=1}^{\mu}$ are the recombination weights. Evolutionary optimization is well suited here because the SFT stage already places the orchestrator parameters in a strong region of the search space, allowing the evolutionary search to refine routing behavior at a finer granularity (Risi et al., 2026). It also lets us optimize end-to-end task outcomes directly, including sparse or noisy success signals, without constructing reliable ranking labels for complex multi-turn trajectories. Empirically, we find this stage to be substantially more stable than supervised fine-tuning on the additional end-to-end tasks and better suited to settings where correctness depends on the full interaction between the worker model, the harness, tools, and environment feedback.
其中，$z_{j:\lambda}$ 是适应度排名中第 $j$ 优候选解的扰动，$\{w_{j}\}_{j=1}^{\mu}$ 是重组权重。进化优化非常适合这一场景，因为 SFT 阶段已将编排器参数置于搜索空间中表现较好的区域，使进化搜索能够以更细的粒度优化路由行为 (Risi et al., 2026)。它还使我们能够直接优化端到端任务结果，包括稀疏或含噪的成功信号，而无需为复杂的多轮交互轨迹构建可靠的排序标签。实证结果表明，与在额外的端到端任务上进行监督微调相比，这一阶段的训练稳定性显著更高，也更适合那些正确性取决于工作模型、执行框架（harness）、工具和环境反馈之间完整交互的场景。
3.2 Fugu-Ultra: Prioritizing Performance3.2 Fugu-Ultra：性能优先
Fugu-Ultra prioritizes unlocking the maximum capability from an LLM agent team and is designed for the most complex user workloads, where absolute answer quality takes precedence. Fugu-Ultra builds on the Conductor (Nielsen et al., 2025), adding novel extensions to accommodate long-horizon function-calling and multi-agent workflows through adaptive agent memory.
Fugu-Ultra 优先致力于充分释放 LLM 智能体团队的最大能力，面向最复杂的用户工作负载而设计，在这些场景中，答案的绝对质量具有最高优先级。Fugu-Ultra 基于 Conductor (Nielsen et al., 2025) 构建，并引入了新的扩展，通过自适应智能体记忆支持长时程函数调用和多智能体工作流。
3.2.1 Conducting an Orchestra of Models3.2.1 指挥模型交响乐团
We begin with a brief overview of the Conductor framework powering Fugu-Ultra before detailing the extensions to accommodate multi-agent function calling and shared memory. The Conductor framework leverages reinforcement learning for training a language model to prompt-engineer and coordinate a set of powerful LLM agents. The Conductor outputs full agentic workflows as natural language that divide an input task, allocate arbitrary subtasks, and define targeted communication strategies to best make use of the agents’ complementary capabilities.
我们首先简要介绍支撑 Fugu-Ultra 的 Conductor 框架，然后详细说明为支持多智能体函数调用和共享记忆而进行的扩展。Conductor 框架利用强化学习训练语言模型，使其能够为一组强大的 LLM 智能体设计提示并进行协调。Conductor 以自然语言输出完整的智能体工作流，对输入任务进行分解、分配任意子任务，并制定有针对性的通信策略，以最大限度地利用各智能体的互补能力。
The Conductor task. The Conductor’s objective is to solve tasks indirectly by designing agentic workflows specific to any input question $q_{i}$ . Each agentic workflow is defined as a sequence of workflow steps whose final output is returned as the actual Conductor response $o_{i}$ . Each step specifies a string with a natural-language subtask, an integer id corresponding to the assigned worker agent responsible for performing that subtask, and an access list indexing which subtask solutions from the previous steps to include in the worker’s context. This design lets the Conductor freely craft tailored subtasks and communication strategies across its workers, allowing the specification of agentic workflows ranging from simple best-of-N and sequential chain-like topologies to arbitrary parallelizable tree-structured approaches, harnessing the individual strengths and synergies of its highly-specialized agents.
Conductor 任务。Conductor 的目标是针对任意输入问题 $q_{i}$ 设计专门的智能体工作流，以间接求解任务。每个智能体工作流都被定义为一系列工作流步骤，其最终输出作为 Conductor 的实际响应 $o_{i}$ 返回。每个步骤都指定一个包含自然语言子任务的字符串、一个对应于负责执行该子任务的指定工作智能体的整数 id，以及一个访问列表，用于索引应将先前步骤中的哪些子任务解答纳入该工作智能体的上下文。这一设计使 Conductor 能够自由地为各工作智能体制定量身定制的子任务和通信策略，从而定义多种智能体工作流，既包括简单的 best-of-N 和顺序链式拓扑，也包括任意可并行化的树形结构方法，充分发挥其高度专业化智能体的个体优势和协同作用。
Workflow execution and learning dynamics. Each agentic workflow the Conductor outputs is executed by prompting the specified worker agent with their assigned subtask. In each workflow step, the worker’s context includes the sequence of previous subtasks and corresponding responses defined in the access list. Analogously to the traditional RL framework, the reward $r_{i}$ for each response from the Conductor model is determined by two progressive conditions:
工作流执行与学习动态。Conductor 输出的每个智能体工作流，均通过向指定的工作智能体提供其所分配子任务的提示来执行。在每个工作流步骤中，工作智能体的上下文都包含访问列表所指定的先前子任务序列及其对应响应。与传统 RL 框架类似，Conductor 模型每个响应的奖励 $r_{i}$ 由两个递进条件决定：
The Conductor format condition, setting $r_{i}$ to 0 for responses from which the lists of subtasks, worker agents, and access lists cannot be parsed.
Conductor 格式条件：对于无法从中解析出子任务列表、工作智能体列表和访问列表的响应，将 $r_{i}$ 设为 0。
The Conductor correctness condition, setting $r_{i}$ to 1 if the final output from executing a well-formatted agentic workflow $o_{i}$ matches the solution $s_{i}$ and to $0.5$ otherwise.
Conductor 正确性条件：如果执行格式正确的智能体工作流所得到的最终输出 $o_{i}$ 与解答 $s_{i}$ 匹配，则将 $r_{i}$ 设为 1，否则设为 $0.5$。
We trained Fugu-Ultra with GRPO (Shao et al., 2024):
我们使用 GRPO (Shao et al., 2024) 训练 Fugu-Ultra：
$$J(\theta)=\mathbb{E}_{q\sim D,\,\{o\}^{G}_{1}\sim\pi_{\theta}(\cdot\mid q)}\left[\frac{1}{G}\sum_{i=1}^{G}\Big(\min\!\big(r_{i}A_{i},\;\mathrm{clip}(r_{i},\,1-\epsilon,\,1+\epsilon)\,A_{i}\big)-\beta\,\mathbb{D}_{\mathrm{KL}}(\pi_{\theta}\,\|\,\pi_{\text{ref}})\Big)\right],$$
using the grouped completions to compute a Monte-Carlo advantage function (Sutton et al., 1999):
使用分组生成的补全来计算蒙特卡洛优势函数 (Sutton et al., 1999)：
$$A_{i}=\frac{r_{i}-\mathrm{mean}(\{r_{1},\dots,r_{G}\})}{\mathrm{std}(\{r_{1},\dots,r_{G}\})},$$
where $r_{i}$ is our specific Conductor reward. The Conductor framework also allows specifying the orchestrator itself as a worker agent, further extending the range and complexity of possible coordination topologies.
其中，$r_{i}$ 是我们专门为 Conductor 定义的奖励。Conductor 框架还允许将编排器自身指定为工作智能体，从而进一步拓展可能的协调拓扑的范围和复杂程度。
Training Fugu-Ultra with this recipe, we observe the emergence of problem decompositions and prompt-engineered subtasks that leverage the differing strengths and skills of each agent in the team, alongside communication topologies that leverage both independent work and agent-to-agent collaboration according to the task requirements.
使用这一方案训练 Fugu-Ultra 时，我们观察到，模型涌现出能够利用团队中各智能体不同优势和技能的问题分解方式及经过提示工程设计的子任务，同时还形成了根据任务需求兼顾独立工作与智能体间协作的通信拓扑。
3.2.2 Function Calling Agentic Workflows3.2.2 函数调用智能体工作流
Function calling within a multi-agent workflow poses a unique additional challenge in terms of orchestration memory. For a generic, single-agent system, the function call loop (Anthropic, 2026e; OpenAI, 2026c; Model Context Protocol, 2026) requires no additional persistent memory on the part of the agent, since the message transcript carries the full context and there is only one possible recipient to receive that context.
多智能体工作流中的函数调用在编排记忆方面带来了一项独特的额外挑战。对于通用的单智能体系统，函数调用循环 (Anthropic, 2026e; OpenAI, 2026c; Model Context Protocol, 2026) 不要求智能体额外维护持久记忆，因为消息记录承载了完整上下文，且只有一个可能接收该上下文的接收方。
In our Fugu-Ultra system, however, any agent may make a function call at any time. Thus, to honor the function call loop and enable any agent to interact freely with the user environment, the system must therefore retain which agent emitted every call, along with where that agent sits in the overall Conductor workflow, such that each agent’s function call loop is correctly routed to the corresponding agent and the inter-agent communication topology is maintained. This therefore requires our orchestrator to track the Conductor’s workflow state containing the selected models, communication topology, and assigned subtasks for the corresponding user query throughout a user-agent interaction.
然而，在我们的 Fugu-Ultra 系统中，任何智能体都可以随时发起函数调用。因此，为了遵循函数调用循环，并使任何智能体都能自由地与用户环境交互，系统必须记录每次调用由哪个智能体发出，以及该智能体在整个 Conductor 工作流中的位置，从而将每个智能体的函数调用循环正确路由至相应智能体，并维持智能体间的通信拓扑。因此，这要求我们的编排器在整个用户与智能体的交互过程中，持续跟踪 Conductor 针对相应用户查询的工作流状态，包括所选模型、通信拓扑和分配的子任务。
Intra-workflow agent isolation.工作流内的智能体隔离。
In order to fully leverage the differing specialties of the agent team, we isolate each agent’s function calling trajectory from one another. This intra-workflow isolation is necessary to prevent orchestration collapse, whereby the first agent to interact with the environment sets the trajectory for all future agents, leading to redundant contributions from future agents as they are steered to follow the path initialized by the first agent. That is, an agent observes the actions and outputs of another agent only through the access list, as proscribed by Fugu-Ultra, otherwise observing a transcript history preserving only their own actions. This prevents conditioning subsequent agents’ solution trajectories on the work done by previous agents, allowing each agent full freedom to determine its own solution path as instructed by the subtask assigned to it by our model.
为了充分发挥智能体团队中各成员的不同专长，我们将各智能体的函数调用轨迹彼此隔离。这种工作流内隔离对于防止编排坍缩（orchestration collapse）是必要的；所谓编排坍缩，是指首个与环境交互的智能体为后续所有智能体设定了轨迹，后续智能体被引导沿着首个智能体开辟的路径前进，从而产生冗余贡献。也就是说，按照 Fugu-Ultra 的规定，智能体只能通过访问列表观察其他智能体的行动和输出；除此之外，它所能观察到的交互记录历史仅保留自身的行动。这可以避免后续智能体的求解轨迹受先前智能体所做工作的制约，使每个智能体都能按照我们的模型为其分配的子任务要求，完全自主地确定自己的求解路径。
Persistent shared memory.持久共享记忆。
While intra-workflow isolation is necessary to prevent orchestration collapse, complete isolation from all function calling over a multi-turn conversation history is likewise suboptimal. This is because agents must retain some memory of their interactions with the environment in order to not make redundant, repeated tool calls to rediscover the same artifacts and accrue the necessary background context to solve the required task. To resolve this tension, we permit inter-workflow shared memory across agents, which allows agents to observe tool calling from previous workflows. Thus, we grant agents full memory over the ongoing state of a multi-turn conversation, which provides the required background context to each agent, while isolating the agents from one another within the current workflow, but for the context determined necessary by Fugu-Ultra’s designated communication topology described by the access list.
虽然工作流内隔离对于防止编排坍缩是必要的，但在多轮对话历史中与所有函数调用完全隔离，同样并非最优。这是因为智能体必须保留一些与环境交互的记忆，才能避免为重新发现相同产物而进行冗余、重复的工具调用，并积累解决所需任务必备的背景上下文。为了解决这一矛盾，我们允许智能体跨工作流共享记忆，使其能够观察先前工作流中的工具调用。因此，我们赋予智能体对多轮对话持续演进状态的完整记忆，为每个智能体提供所需的背景上下文，同时在当前工作流内将智能体彼此隔离，仅允许它们访问由 Fugu-Ultra 指定、通过访问列表描述的通信拓扑所确定的必要上下文。
3.2.3 Training Setup3.2.3 训练设置
To train Fugu-Ultra, we scale the Conductor’s reinforcement learning approach starting from a pre-trained checkpoint of a regular language model. Ultra is instructed to design agentic workflows of up to 5 steps using a diverse pool of frontier LLMs that includes Gemini-3.1-Pro (Google DeepMind, 2026), Claude-Opus-4.8 (Anthropic, 2026c), and GPT-5.5 (OpenAI, 2026d). For multi-turn tasks, any agent is permitted unlimited interaction with the user environment. We train using our Conductor reward detailed in section 3.2.1 with GRPO and without any KL divergence penalty.
为了训练 Fugu-Ultra，我们从常规语言模型的预训练检查点出发，扩展 Conductor 的强化学习方法。Ultra 被要求使用一个包含多种前沿 LLM 的模型池，设计最多包含 5 个步骤的智能体工作流；该模型池包括 Gemini-3.1-Pro (Google DeepMind, 2026)、Claude-Opus-4.8 (Anthropic, 2026c) 和 GPT-5.5 (OpenAI, 2026d)。对于多轮任务，任何智能体都可以不受限制地与用户环境交互。我们使用第 3.2.1 节详述的 Conductor 奖励，通过 GRPO 进行训练，且不施加任何 KL 散度惩罚。
Our training dataset is a mixture of both publicly available data and expert-designed end-to-end environments simulating real agent-user interactions. These end-to-end tasks were also used in the development of our previous model (section 3.1.3) and help expose different kinds of real-world specializations across agents, such as expertise in math, science, engineering, factual knowledge and recall, numerous tool-usage scenarios, conversational dialog, multi-turn context retention, and planning. Large-scale training on these tasks produces substantial gains in Fugu-Ultra, boosting its ability to discern the expertise and skills across the worker pool and how to optimally combine them.
我们的训练数据集混合了公开可用的数据，以及由专家设计、模拟真实智能体与用户交互的端到端环境。这些端到端任务也曾用于我们先前模型的开发（第 3.1.3 节），有助于显现不同智能体在现实世界中的各类专长，例如数学、科学、工程、事实性知识与回忆、多种工具使用场景、对话交流、多轮上下文保留以及规划方面的能力。在这些任务上进行大规模训练，使 Fugu-Ultra 获得了显著提升，增强了其辨识工作智能体池中各智能体的专长与技能、并确定如何对其进行最优组合的能力。

Recovering the reading order of classical Japanese kana letters (kana-shōsoku) remains an open problem, with no established method and no public benchmark. Recovering the reading order is a task that even trained readers of classical Japanese find demanding. 4.4 Optimal Strategies and Topologies4.4 最优策略与拓扑结构
We conclude with findings on performant orchestrative strategies, demonstrating granular application of agent expertise to achieve the high performance reported throughout this section.
最后，我们介绍有关高效编排策略的研究发现，展示如何细粒度地运用智能体的专长，以实现本节各处报告的高性能。
Debate and aggregation.辩论与聚合。
A core benefit of Fugu-Ultra’s dynamic workflows is that Fugu-Ultra can produce any coordination topology describable in natural language on a per-question basis. In knowledge-intensive domains, multi-round debate and tree topologies are powerful strategies to maximize the collective knowledge from an agent team. We find that such strategies indeed arise in domains requiring niche or specialized factual knowledge. For instance, in Humanity’s Last Exam, when Fugu-Ultra was tasked to determine the outcome of a specific game state in Heroes of Might & Magic III – a question testing both knowledge and application of game mechanics with precise trivia knowledge – Fugu-Ultra produced a tree structure with Gemini-3.1-Pro at the head of the tree tasked to synthesize two independent attempts. Usage of Gemini as the aggregator made use of Gemini’s SOTA performance in niche factual recall, however, further noteworthy, Fugu-Ultra also used a second instance of Gemini alongside GPT in the two leaf positions to attempt the problem independently. In this task, both leaf-agents produced partially correct answers, with Gemini incorrectly inferring a base defense statistic and GPT incorrectly applying a game-mechanical rule, however the Gemini-as-aggregator correctly identified the partial correctness in either answer and synthesized a fully correct answer. In a second example, when tasked to compute the Crowley–Nordström invariant of the Calabi–Yau link defined by a given weighted-homogeneous polynomial, Fugu-Ultra, leveraged the mathematical expertise of GPT at the root of the tree with both Gemini and Opus at leaf positions. Here, GPT-as-aggregator resolved a disagreement between Gemini and Opus over a single integer in the solution by rederiving the spectral numbers from the spectral generating function, locating the exact error and crux of the question’s difficulty, and solving the question.
Fugu-Ultra 动态工作流的一项核心优势在于，它能够针对每个问题生成任何可用自然语言描述的协调拓扑结构。在知识密集型领域，多轮辩论和树形拓扑是最大限度利用智能体团队集体知识的有力策略。我们发现，在需要冷门或专业事实性知识的领域中，确实会出现这类策略。例如，在 Humanity’s Last Exam 中，当 Fugu-Ultra 被要求判断 Heroes of Might & Magic III 中某个特定游戏状态的结果时——这道题既考查对游戏机制的理解与运用，也考查精确的冷门事实知识——Fugu-Ultra 生成了一个树形结构，将 Gemini-3.1-Pro 置于树的顶端，负责综合两次独立求解的结果。使用 Gemini 作为聚合器，利用了 Gemini 在冷门事实回忆方面达到 SOTA 水平的性能，但进一步值得注意的是，Fugu-Ultra 还在两个叶节点位置分别使用了另一个 Gemini 实例和 GPT，让它们独立尝试解题。在这项任务中，两个叶节点智能体都给出了部分正确的答案，其中 Gemini 错误地推断了一项基础防御属性值，GPT 则错误地应用了一条游戏机制规则，但作为聚合器的 Gemini 正确识别了两个答案中各自正确的部分，并综合得出了完全正确的答案。在第二个例子中，当被要求计算由给定加权齐次多项式定义的 Calabi–Yau 链环的 Crowley–Nordström 不变量时，Fugu-Ultra 将 GPT 置于树的根节点，以利用其数学专长，并将 Gemini 和 Opus 分别置于叶节点。在这里，作为聚合器的 GPT 通过从谱生成函数重新推导谱数，解决了 Gemini 与 Opus 在解答中某个整数上的分歧，定位了确切的错误和该问题的难点所在，并解出了这道题。
These simple examples are revealing in two ways. First, once again, we observe granular adaptivity on a per-question basis, with Fugu-Ultra utilizing tree-like topologies in both tasks while adapting its aggregator between Gemini for a trivia-heavy task and GPT for a math-heavy task, leveraging their individual expertise in the key aggregation role. Second, dynamic adaptation of an aggregator role is precisely the kind of adaptation unavailable to existing multi-agent systems (Wang et al., 2025; OpenRouter, Inc., 2026), which necessitate a fixed model to always act as a final synthesizer, regardless of whether that model is suitable for the task or not. Such systems are thereby bottlenecked by that rigidity, and typically struggle to surpass the performance of the aggregator for tasks outside of the aggregator’s expertise. Fugu-Ultra’s adaptivity sidesteps this issue, and is key to unlocking the capabilities discussed throughout this section.
这些简单的例子从两个方面揭示了重要信息。首先，我们再次观察到了针对每个问题的细粒度适应性：Fugu-Ultra 在两项任务中都使用了树形拓扑，同时根据任务调整聚合器，在冷门事实知识密集型任务中使用 Gemini，在数学密集型任务中使用 GPT，从而在关键的聚合角色上发挥它们各自的专长。其次，聚合者角色的动态调整恰恰是现有多智能体系统（Wang et al., 2025; OpenRouter, Inc., 2026）所不具备的适应能力；这些系统要求始终由一个固定模型担任最终综合者，无论该模型是否适合当前任务。因此，这类系统受制于这种僵化机制，在超出聚合者专业范围的任务上，其表现通常难以超越聚合者本身。Fugu-Ultra 的适应能力绕开了这一问题，也是释放本节所讨论各项能力的关键。
Build and debug.构建与调试。
In multi-turn agentic tasks, one performant strategy we observe is GPT being deployed as a builder, making use of its impressive performance in agentic coding, with Opus being deployed at crucial moments to reverify quality and expose vulnerabilities, making use of Opus’ expertise in debugging. When Fugu-Ultra was tasked to build a PyPI server hosting a vectorops package in Terminal Bench 2.1, Fugu-Ultra first deployed GPT-5.5 to build the server and confirm the server’s reachability after it finished. After GPT finished the build, Opus was then deployed to enumerate all concrete risks in the GPT’s implementation, for which Opus noticed 1) GPT used a plain static http.server rather than a pypiserver, 2) the hand-built wheel was built via zipfile, which was fragile and 3) GPT had failed to properly manage the Debian-slim environment, which would lead to several commands failing. Noticing these issues, Opus then discovered GPT’s reachability check was erroneous and had come from an orphaned http.server. Relaying these findings back to GPT enabled GPT to complete the build successfully.
在多轮智能体任务中，我们观察到一种表现出色的策略：利用 GPT 在智能体编程方面的优异表现，让其担任构建者，并在关键时刻部署 Opus，利用其调试专长重新核验质量、发现漏洞。在 Terminal Bench 2.1 中，当 Fugu-Ultra 被要求构建一个托管 vectorops 包的 PyPI 服务器时，它首先部署 GPT-5.5 来构建服务器，并在构建完成后确认服务器的可访问性。GPT 完成构建后，Opus 随即被部署来逐一列出 GPT 实现中的所有具体风险，Opus 发现：1) GPT 使用的是普通的静态 http.server，而非 pypiserver；2) 手工制作的 wheel 是通过 zipfile 构建的，这种做法较为脆弱；3) GPT 未能妥善管理 Debian-slim 环境，这会导致多条命令执行失败。注意到这些问题后，Opus 进一步发现 GPT 的可访问性检查有误，其结果来自一个孤立的 http.server 进程。将这些发现反馈给 GPT 后，GPT 得以成功完成构建。
For Fugu, we similarly observe select application of Opus’s debugging expertise to expose and correct vulnerabilities in GPT’s build. In Terminal Bench 2.1’s merge-diff-arc-agi-task, Fugu allowed GPT to build out the scaffold, fetch the two bundles into the branches, and perform the merge in algo.py. At the point GPT recieved the merge conflict back from the environment, Fugu then swapped in Opus, which then both rechecked the scaffold and resolved the merge initiated by GPT, which then revealed the required input-output mapping as originating from neither branch, prompting Opus to then correctly infer the solution from first principles. This per-step alternation between GPT-as-builder and then Opus-as-debugger was critical to allowing Fugu to access performance beyond either model through merging their fine-grained and complementary capabilities.
对于 Fugu，我们同样观察到，它会有选择地运用 Opus 的调试专长，发现并修正 GPT 所构建实现中的漏洞。在 Terminal Bench 2.1 的 merge-diff-arc-agi-task 中，Fugu 让 GPT 搭建框架，将两个包分别获取到对应分支中，并在 algo.py 中执行合并。当 GPT 收到环境返回的合并冲突时，Fugu 随即切换至 Opus，后者重新检查了框架，并解决了 GPT 发起的合并中的冲突，进而发现所需的输入输出映射并非来自任何一个分支，这促使 Opus 从第一性原理出发，正确推导出解决方案。这种在各步骤中交替让 GPT 担任构建者、让 Opus 担任调试者的方式，将两者细粒度且互补的能力结合起来，是 Fugu 获得超越任一单独模型表现的关键。
In other agentic coding tasks, we observe Fugu-Ultra alternating Opus into the lead engineer role with GPT probing for weaknesses and offering alternate perspectives. One example of this was during Fugu-Ultra’s solution to a gravitational/teleport instance in SWE Bench Pro, where the model needs to resolve a failed validation error code when a user adds an additional OTP device. Fugu-Ultra deployed Opus to understand the issue and propose a fix, to which Opus tracked the server registration and validation all the way into the TOTP library itself, ultimately leading to a dead end. At this point, Fugu-Ultra then called GPT to re-examine the issue from a clean slate, to which GPT noticed the issue was not a server-side issue at all but a client-side concurrency bug, for which the server error was merely a symptom of a larger, structural bug. GPT then relayed this information back to Opus which duly changed course and identified the proper fix, which was to introduce a single, shared ContextReader to prevent the concurrency bug.
在其他智能体编程任务中，我们观察到 Fugu-Ultra 也会转而让 Opus 担任首席工程师，由 GPT 探查薄弱之处并提供不同视角。例如，在 Fugu-Ultra 解决 SWE Bench Pro 中的一个 gravitational/teleport 实例时，模型需要修复用户添加额外 OTP 设备时出现的验证失败错误码问题。Fugu-Ultra 部署 Opus 来理解问题并提出修复方案，Opus 将服务器端的注册和验证流程一路追踪到 TOTP 库内部，但最终陷入了死胡同。此时，Fugu-Ultra 调用 GPT，让其从头重新审视问题，GPT 发现这根本不是服务器端问题，而是客户端的并发缺陷，服务器错误只是一个更大范围的结构性缺陷的表征。随后，GPT 将这一信息反馈给 Opus，Opus 随之调整方向，并确定了正确的修复方案，即引入一个统一共享的 ContextReader，以避免该并发缺陷。
Bringing in a specialist.引入专家。
Another strategy we observed delivering strong performance was the select usage of additional models when highly specific knowledge was required. To take another example from Terminal Bench 2.1, an interesting instance of this was during Fugu-Ultra’s solution to the feal-differential-cryptanalysis problem. In this problem, the model is tasked to write an attack that recovers a key from a FEAL-like encryption function. Making use of Opus-4.8’s expertise in cybersecurity and software engineering, Fugu-Ultra started the task with Opus building a first-pass chosen-plaintext attack. After Opus was satisfied with its attack, Fugu-Ultra then made use of GPT-5.5’s mathematical expertise, tasking it to act specifically as a math specialist to re-derive the entire attack from first principles, and in particular trace through the cipher bit-by-bit to derive the differential constant necessary for the attack to succeed and recover the required round subkey. This example illustrates Fugu-Ultra’s ability to recognize the need to combine cross-domain expertise, drawing on cybersecurity, engineering, and math, and efficiently leverage that expertise from within its agent team to solve complex, multi-disciplinary problems.
我们观察到的另一种表现出色的策略，是在需要高度专门的知识时，有选择地使用其他模型。再以 Terminal Bench 2.1 为例，Fugu-Ultra 解决 feal-differential-cryptanalysis 问题的过程就是一个有趣的实例。在这一问题中，模型需要编写一种攻击方法，从一个类似 FEAL 的加密函数中恢复密钥。利用 Opus-4.8 在网络安全和软件工程方面的专长，Fugu-Ultra 在任务开始时让 Opus 构建了一个初步的选择明文攻击方案。当 Opus 对其攻击方案感到满意后，Fugu-Ultra 随即利用 GPT-5.5 的数学专长，专门让其担任数学专家，从第一性原理出发重新推导整个攻击过程，尤其是逐比特追踪该密码算法，以推导出使攻击成功并恢复所需轮子密钥所必需的差分常数。这一示例表明，Fugu-Ultra 能够识别出结合网络安全、工程和数学等跨领域专长的必要性，并高效利用其智能体团队内部的这些专长，解决复杂的多学科问题。
5 Conclusions5 结论
We present Sakana Fugu, a family of learned LLM orchestrators that expose multi-agent intelligence through a single model interface. Motivated by the complementary specializations of frontier models and the growing importance of agentic scaffolds, Fugu models learn to construct query-adaptive workflows over a pool of expert LLM workers. We introduced two variants: Fugu, which balances performance and latency for everyday interactive use, and Fugu-Ultra, which prioritizes answer quality for difficult multi-step tasks. Building on the Trinity and Conductor frameworks, and extending them with production-oriented design choices, Sakana Fugu demonstrates that model orchestration can serve as a practical scaling axis for frontier AI capabilities. Our evaluations and early user experiences suggest that learned coordination can achieve strong performance on challenging coding, reasoning, and agentic tasks while providing a simple interface to a complex multi-agent system.
我们提出了 Sakana Fugu，这是一个通过学习获得的 LLM 编排器系列，可通过单一模型接口提供多智能体智能。鉴于前沿模型各有所长且优势互补，以及智能体支撑框架（agentic scaffolds）日益重要，Fugu 模型学习如何在一组专家 LLM 工作模型上构建适应查询的工作流。我们推出了两种变体：Fugu 在性能与延迟之间取得平衡，适用于日常交互使用；Fugu-Ultra 则针对困难的多步骤任务，优先追求回答质量。Sakana Fugu 以 Trinity 和 Conductor 框架为基础，并通过面向生产环境的设计选择加以扩展，表明模型编排可以成为提升前沿 AI 能力的一条切实可行的扩展路径。我们的评估和早期用户体验表明，通过学习获得的协调机制能够在具有挑战性的编程、推理和智能体任务上取得出色表现，同时为复杂的多智能体系统提供简洁的接口。
Looking ahead, we believe learned orchestration will become increasingly important as the ecosystem of frontier models continues to diversify. Because Sakana Fugu composes models at the behavioral level rather than the parameter level, it can incorporate new worker models as they become available, adapt to different user constraints, and support a wider range of deployment and privacy requirements. More broadly, Sakana Fugu points toward a view of AI progress in which capabilities arise not only from scaling individual models but also from learning how specialized models can collaborate. We hope this report encourages further research into collective intelligence, query-adaptive scaffolds, and production-ready multi-agent systems.
展望未来，我们认为，随着前沿模型生态系统持续多样化，通过学习获得的编排机制将变得越来越重要。由于 Sakana Fugu 在行为层面而非参数层面组合模型，因此它能够在新的工作模型推出时将其纳入系统，适应不同的用户约束，并支持更广泛的部署和隐私要求。更广泛地说，Sakana Fugu 指向了这样一种 AI 进步观：能力不仅源于单个模型的规模扩展，也源于学习如何让专门化模型相互协作。我们希望本报告能够推动对集体智能、适应查询的支撑框架以及可用于生产环境的多智能体系统的进一步研究。

B.1 One-shot Rubik’s Cube SolverB.1 单次尝试生成魔方求解器

B.2 Blindfold Chess GameplayB.2 国际象棋盲棋对弈
Blindfold chess is a stress test for one specific ability: to hold a complex, changing state in mind, and to think about it for a long time. Each model is given a fixed opening as a list of moves (never as a board in ASCII or FEN string) and continues from there entirely from memory. On each turn, it receives only the opponent’s last move in coordinate notation and must return its own, within a single continuous session. We do not provide a list of legal moves available, and models are expected to find one on its own. In addition, no external record is maintained in the conversation history. We expect the model to track the entire position on its own by internally maintaining a record of every capture, castle, and promotion over a game that may span dozens of moves. It is important to note that in a game of chess, a single forgotten or misplaced piece is typically sufficient to lose.
国际象棋盲棋是对一项特定能力的压力测试：在脑海中保持复杂且不断变化的状态，并对其进行长时间思考。每个模型都会收到一个以着法列表形式给出的固定开局（绝不以 ASCII 棋盘或 FEN 字符串形式提供），此后完全凭记忆继续对弈。在每个回合中，模型仅收到以坐标记谱法表示的对手上一步着法，并且必须在同一个连续会话中返回自己的着法。我们不提供可用合法着法的列表，模型需要自行找到一个合法着法。此外，对话历史中不保留任何外部记录。我们期望模型自行跟踪整个棋局局面，在内部记录一盘可能持续数十步的对局中的每一次吃子、王车易位和升变。需要注意的是，在国际象棋对局中，仅仅遗忘一枚棋子或记错一枚棋子的位置，通常就足以导致落败。

B.3 Online Sequential Stock TradingB.3 在线序贯股票交易
We ask whether agents can trade profitably online, where future information is unavailable and each decision permanently shapes the portfolio [fn2]. The method extends the next-action prediction paradigm, studied in sequential recommendation (Kang and McAuley, 2018) and agentic recommendation (Yue et al., 2026), into an agentic trading task requiring the agent to both anticipate future dynamics and commit to irreversible decisions. Built on the Apple Inc. Stock Price Dataset from Kaggle (meetnagadia, 2022), the experiment follows the causal, no-look-ahead principle of LSTM-based financial prediction (Fischer and Krauss, 2018). But where such work uses batch walk-forward evaluation, we adopt a fully online, per-step setting: the agent observes only past prices for a single asset (STOCK_X), commits to an action, and sees the true next value only afterward, before acting again with accumulated feedback. We disable web search in addition to anonymizing and normalizing the data into weekly observations, so performance reflects genuine reasoning rather than memorized ticker knowledge.
我们探究智能体能否在无法获取未来信息、且每次决策都会对投资组合产生永久影响的在线环境中实现盈利交易 [fn2]。该方法将序贯推荐（Kang and McAuley, 2018）和智能体推荐（Yue et al., 2026）中研究的下一步行动预测范式，扩展为一项智能体交易任务，要求智能体既预测未来动态，又作出不可逆的决策。该实验基于 Kaggle 上的 Apple Inc. Stock Price Dataset（meetnagadia, 2022），遵循基于 LSTM 的金融预测中的因果性与无前视原则（Fischer and Krauss, 2018）。不过，此类研究采用批量滚动前推评估，而我们采用完全在线、逐步进行的设置：智能体只能观察单一资产（STOCK_X）的历史价格，确定并执行动作后才能看到真实的下一期数值，随后再根据累积的反馈采取下一次行动。除了对数据进行匿名化和归一化处理，并将其整理为每周观测数据外，我们还禁用了网络搜索，以使表现反映真实的推理能力，而非记忆中的股票代码相关知识。
