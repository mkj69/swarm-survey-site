1 Introduction1 引言
The rapid advancement of large language models (LLMs) has fundamentally reshaped the landscape of autonomous systems. As foundation models grow increasingly capable of handling long-horizon, complex tasks, the accompanying agent harnesses, such as Claude Code (Anthropic, 2025), Codex (OpenAI, 2025a), and DeepSeek Harness (DeepSeek, 2026a), have matured into highly sophisticated infrastructures. These single-agent systems now deliver robust, end-to-end execution in real-world scenarios, setting a formidable baseline for autonomy. In stark contrast, generic multi-agent collaboration frameworks often remain toy-level in practice, lingering on simplistic benchmarks (Paech, 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Peng et al., 2024) and marginal performance gains (Yun et al., 2026; Alzu’bi et al., 2026). This widening gap compels a critical reexamination of when and why multi-agent collaboration offers genuine value over a well-equipped single-agent harness.
大语言模型（LLMs）的快速发展从根本上重塑了自主系统的格局。随着基础模型处理长时程复杂任务的能力不断增强，配套的智能体执行框架（agent harness），如 Claude Code (Anthropic, 2025)、Codex (OpenAI, 2025a) 和 DeepSeek Harness (DeepSeek, 2026a)，也已发展为高度成熟的基础设施。这些单智能体系统如今已能在真实场景中实现稳健的端到端执行，为自主能力树立了强有力的基准。与之形成鲜明对比的是，通用多智能体协作框架在实践中往往仍停留在玩具级水平，局限于简单的基准测试 (Paech, 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Peng et al., 2024)，性能提升也十分有限 (Yun et al., 2026; Alzu’bi et al., 2026)。这一日益扩大的差距迫使我们审慎地重新审视：相较于配备完善的单智能体执行框架，多智能体协作何时以及为何能够提供真正的价值。
Existing work often attributes the primary advantage of multi-agent systems to context isolation, which partitions a task to prevent context pollution and context rot (Huang et al., 2026; Hong et al., 2025). While intuitively appealing, this explanation remains largely qualitative and lacks a rigorous theoretical foundation. In this work, we aim to delineate the capability boundaries of multi-agent collaboration through graph theory. We formalize multi-agent collaboration by modeling task trajectories as dependency graphs, where subtasks are partitioned by bridge edges. This formalism reveals that multi-agent collaboration is not a universal panacea. Instead, it confers systematic benefits specifically in long-horizon tasks with sparse dependencies, where context isolation can be exploited without incurring prohibitive coordination overhead. Conversely, in tightly coupled, sequential workflows, single-agent harnesses remain superior.
现有研究常将多智能体系统的主要优势归因于上下文隔离，即通过划分任务来防止上下文污染和上下文退化（context rot）(Huang et al., 2026; Hong et al., 2025)。尽管这一解释在直觉上颇具吸引力，但它仍主要停留在定性层面，缺乏严谨的理论基础。在本研究中，我们旨在通过图论刻画多智能体协作的能力边界。我们将任务轨迹建模为依赖图，并通过桥边划分子任务，从而对多智能体协作进行形式化描述。这一形式化描述揭示，多智能体协作并非万能的解决方案。具体而言，它能在依赖关系稀疏的长时程任务中带来系统性收益，因为在这类任务中，可以利用上下文隔离，而不必承担高昂到难以承受的协调开销。相反，在紧密耦合的顺序工作流中，单智能体执行框架仍然更具优势。
Building on these theoretical insights, we propose SAIGE (Semantic-Aware Incremental Graph Evolution), an elegant and general multi-agent collaboration mechanism. SAIGE models collaboration as a dynamically evolving graph, where nodes are agent instances spawned on demand and edges encode semantic dependencies established through content-based information retrieval. Rather than committing to a static topology upfront, SAIGE grows the subtask tree incrementally as execution unfolds, grounding orchestration decisions in actual execution feedback. We empirically validate our framework on long-horizon, complex task benchmarks, showing that SAIGE achieves a favorable trade-off between context efficiency and task performance compared to existing generic multi-agent methods. Notably, our ablation study indicates that neither a larger agent pool nor a deeper recursion hierarchy consistently improves performance, suggesting that more agents do not necessarily make a system more intelligent. Our findings offer a principled understanding of when collaboration provides meaningful advantage over single-agent execution, and suggest that indiscriminate multi-agent scaling warrants reconsideration.
基于这些理论洞见，我们提出了 SAIGE（Semantic-Aware Incremental Graph Evolution，语义感知的增量图演化），一种简洁而通用的多智能体协作机制。SAIGE 将协作建模为一个动态演化的图，其中节点是按需创建的智能体实例，边则编码了通过基于内容的信息检索所建立的语义依赖关系。SAIGE 随着执行过程的推进逐步扩展子任务树，使编排决策以实际执行反馈为依据，而非预先确定静态拓扑。我们在长时程复杂任务基准上对该框架进行了实证验证，结果表明，与现有通用多智能体方法相比，SAIGE 在上下文效率与任务性能之间实现了良好的权衡。值得注意的是，我们的消融研究表明，无论是扩大智能体池，还是加深递归层级，都无法持续改善性能，这说明更多的智能体并不一定能使系统更智能。我们的研究结果为理解协作何时能比单智能体执行带来实质性优势提供了有理论依据的解释，并表明不加区分地扩展多智能体规模值得重新考虑。
2 Related Work2 相关工作
2.1 Multi-Agent Collaboration2.1 多智能体协作
Multi-agent collaboration governs how agents coordinate, communicate, and align behaviors toward shared objectives, evolving from rigid predefined structures to adaptive, context-aware interactions. Role allocation ranges from static, pre-assigned responsibilities (Chen et al., 2025; Fei et al., 2026) to dynamic selection or runtime instantiation based on task demands (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Alzu’bi et al., 2026). Communication likewise spans explicit message passing via structured documents or natural language (Hong et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025c; Mou et al., 2025) and implicit inference from environmental cues (Park et al., 2023; Zou et al., 2026). Information flow further structures these interactions as sequential pipelines or parallel exploration (Shen et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a), while interaction patterns ultimately manifest as cooperative or competitive dynamics (Yang et al., 2026; Fu et al., 2023).
多智能体协作规定了智能体如何围绕共同目标进行协调、沟通并使行为保持一致，其形式已从僵化的预定义结构演变为自适应、上下文感知的交互。角色分配的方式涵盖静态的预先职责分配 (Chen et al., 2025; Fei et al., 2026)，以及根据任务需求进行动态选择或在运行时实例化 (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Alzu’bi et al., 2026)。同样，通信方式既包括通过结构化文档或自然语言进行显式消息传递 (Hong et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025c; Mou et al., 2025)，也包括根据环境线索进行隐式推断 (Park et al., 2023; Zou et al., 2026)。信息流进一步将这些交互组织为顺序流水线或并行探索 (Shen et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024a)，而交互模式最终表现为合作或竞争的动态关系 (Yang et al., 2026; Fu et al., 2023)。
2.2 Multi-Agent Topology2.2 多智能体拓扑
Multi-agent topology serves as a critical structural framework for governing agent execution, communication, and interactions. While early attempts often embedded structural organization implicitly (Chan et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Khattab et al., 2023), recent practices have explicitly represented multi-agent organizations as graphs (Liu et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025). These topologies generally fall into three representative paradigms: centralized, distributed, and hybrid. Centralized topologies leverage a global coordinator for information aggregation and routing (Zhang et al., 2026a; Li et al., 2025); distributed topologies employ peer-to-peer communication and localized routing (Yang et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025a) to enhance autonomy and scalability; and hybrid topologies combine centralized strategic planning with decentralized execution (Qian et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2026b). Concretely, these orchestration strategies manifest as distinct structural morphologies, including chain (Qian et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Holt et al., 2025), star (Wu et al., 2023; Yan et al., 2024), tree (Ishibashi and Nishimura, 2024), and general graph topologies (Qian et al., 2025; Yun et al., 2026).
多智能体拓扑是管理智能体执行、通信和交互的关键结构框架。早期尝试通常以隐式方式嵌入组织结构 (Chan et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2023; Khattab et al., 2023)，而近期实践则将多智能体组织显式表示为图 (Liu et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025)。这些拓扑通常可归为三种代表性范式：集中式、分布式和混合式。集中式拓扑利用全局协调器进行信息聚合与路由 (Zhang et al., 2026a; Li et al., 2025)；分布式拓扑采用点对点通信和局部路由 (Yang et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025a)，以增强自主性和可扩展性；混合式拓扑则将集中式战略规划与分散式执行相结合 (Qian et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2026b)。具体而言，这些编排策略表现为不同的结构形态，包括链式拓扑 (Qian et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Holt et al., 2025)、星形拓扑 (Wu et al., 2023; Yan et al., 2024)、树形拓扑 (Ishibashi and Nishimura, 2024) 和一般图拓扑 (Qian et al., 2025; Yun et al., 2026)。
3 Formalizing Multi-Agent Collaboration3 多智能体协作的形式化
What is the core of multi-agent collaboration, and under what idealized conditions does it genuinely help? We take a deliberately idealized route: we ask what multi-agent collaboration is fundamentally doing to a task, and derive its benefit from that answer, leaving the gap between the idealized and the practical as a source of empirically testable predictions rather than a hidden assumption.
多智能体协作的核心是什么，它在什么理想化条件下才能真正发挥作用？我们有意采用一条理想化的研究路径：探究多智能体协作究竟从根本上对任务做了什么，并据此推导其收益，同时将理想与实践之间的差距作为可通过实证检验的预测的来源，而非隐含假设。
3.1 Preliminaries3.1 预备知识
3.1.1 Trajectory as a Dependency Directed Acyclic Graph3.1.1 将轨迹表示为依赖关系有向无环图
Consider a task trajectory of length $T$ : $\tau=\{x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{T}\}$ , where each $x_{t}=(o_{t},a_{t})$ denotes the interaction tuple at step $t$ , consisting of the new observation $o_{t}$ received and the action $a_{t}$ taken by the agent. Let $\mathcal{H}_{t}=\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{t-1}\}$ denote the historical context available before generating $x_{t}$ . This history grows monotonically with $t$ , and the agent’s decisions may depend on arbitrary subsets of this history.
考虑一条长度为 $T$ 的任务轨迹：$\tau=\{x_{1},x_{2},\ldots,x_{T}\}$，其中每个 $x_{t}=(o_{t},a_{t})$ 表示第 $t$ 步的交互元组，由智能体接收到的新观测 $o_{t}$ 和采取的动作 $a_{t}$ 组成。令 $\mathcal{H}_{t}=\{x_{1},\ldots,x_{t-1}\}$ 表示生成 $x_{t}$ 之前可用的历史上下文。该历史随 $t$ 单调增长，而智能体的决策可能依赖于该历史的任意子集。
To characterize these dependencies formally, we define a value function $Q_{t}(a_{t}\mid o_{t},\mathcal{H}_{t})$ that represents the expected return of taking action $a_{t}$ given historical context $\mathcal{H}_{t}$ . For any pair of nodes $x_{i},x_{t}\in\tau$ with $i<t$ , we say that $x_{t}$ has a direct dependency on $x_{i}$ if removing the interaction tuple $x_{i}$ from the historical context causes a significant drop in the action value at step $t$ :
为形式化刻画这些依赖关系，我们定义价值函数 $Q_{t}(a_{t}\mid o_{t},\mathcal{H}_{t})$，表示在给定历史上下文 $\mathcal{H}_{t}$ 时采取动作 $a_{t}$ 的期望回报。对于满足 $i<t$ 的任意一对节点 $x_{i},x_{t}\in\tau$，如果从历史上下文中移除交互元组 $x_{i}$ 会导致第 $t$ 步的动作价值显著下降，则称 $x_{t}$ 直接依赖于 $x_{i}$：
$$(x_{i},x_{t})\in\mathcal{E}\iff Q_{t}(a_{t}\mid o_{t},\mathcal{H}_{t}\setminus\{x_{i}\})<Q_{t}(a_{t}\mid o_{t},\mathcal{H}_{t})-\delta,$$
where $\mathcal{H}_{t}\setminus\{x_{i}\}$ denotes the historical context with the contribution of $x_{i}$ removed, and $\delta>0$ is a predefined threshold. Since all edges point forward in time, the trajectory naturally induces a directed acyclic graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ , with node set $\mathcal{V}=\tau$ . Each directed edge $(x_{i},x_{t})\in\mathcal{E}\subseteq\mathcal{V}\times\mathcal{V}$ indicates that node $x_{t}$ directly depends on node $x_{i}$ .
其中，$\mathcal{H}_{t}\setminus\{x_{i}\}$ 表示移除了 $x_{i}$ 的贡献后的历史上下文，$\delta>0$ 是预先设定的阈值。由于所有边都沿时间向前指向，该轨迹自然诱导出一个有向无环图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$，其节点集为 $\mathcal{V}=\tau$。每条有向边 $(x_{i},x_{t})\in\mathcal{E}\subseteq\mathcal{V}\times\mathcal{V}$ 都表示节点 $x_{t}$ 直接依赖于节点 $x_{i}$。
We further assume that a task trajectory contains no redundant interactions. Specifically, every node in the trajectory contributes to at least one subsequent decision, implying that each node has out-degree at least one in the dependency graph, with the terminal node serving as the unique sink. Under this assumption, the induced DAG $\mathcal{G}$ is weakly connected. This assumption is mild in practice, as interactions that do not influence any future step can be discarded from the trajectory without affecting the task outcome.
我们进一步假设，任务轨迹中不存在冗余交互。具体而言，轨迹中的每个节点都至少对一个后续决策有所贡献，这意味着依赖图中每个节点的出度至少为一，终止节点则作为唯一的汇点。在此假设下，所诱导的有向无环图 $\mathcal{G}$ 是弱连通的。这一假设在实践中并不苛刻，因为不影响任何未来步骤的交互可以从轨迹中删除，而不影响任务结果。

3.1.2 Decomposing Trajectories into Subtasks via Bridge Edges3.1.2 通过桥边将轨迹分解为子任务
Within the DAG $\mathcal{G}$ , certain edges serve as critical information channels connecting distinct regions of the graph. An edge $(x_{i},x_{t})\in\mathcal{E}$ is a bridge edge if its removal increases the number of weakly connected components of $\mathcal{G}$ .
在有向无环图 $\mathcal{G}$ 中，某些边充当连接图中不同区域的关键信息通道。如果移除边 $(x_{i},x_{t})\in\mathcal{E}$ 会增加 $\mathcal{G}$ 的弱连通分量数，则该边为桥边。
Let $\mathcal{E}^{*}\subseteq\mathcal{E}$ denote the set of all bridge edges. Removing all bridge edges from $\mathcal{G}$ decomposes the graph into $m$ weakly connected components $\mathcal{G}\setminus\mathcal{E}^{*}=\mathcal{C}_{1}\cup\cdots\cup\mathcal{C}_{m}$ . The corresponding subtask trajectory is defined as the ordered sequence of nodes in $\mathcal{C}_{k}$ : $\tau_{(k)}=\{x_{n_{1}},x_{n_{2}},\ldots,x_{n_{T_{k}}}\}\subseteq\tau$ , where $T_{k}=|\mathcal{C}_{k}|$ , and the subtask trajectories collectively partition the original trajectory: $\tau=\tau_{(1)}\cup\tau_{(2)}\cup\cdots\cup\tau_{(m)}$ .
令 $\mathcal{E}^{*}\subseteq\mathcal{E}$ 表示所有桥边的集合。从 $\mathcal{G}$ 中移除所有桥边，会将该图分解为 $m$ 个弱连通分量 $\mathcal{G}\setminus\mathcal{E}^{*}=\mathcal{C}_{1}\cup\cdots\cup\mathcal{C}_{m}$。相应的子任务轨迹定义为 $\mathcal{C}_{k}$ 中节点的有序序列：$\tau_{(k)}=\{x_{n_{1}},x_{n_{2}},\ldots,x_{n_{T_{k}}}\}\subseteq\tau$，其中 $T_{k}=|\mathcal{C}_{k}|$，且这些子任务轨迹共同构成原始轨迹的一个划分：$\tau=\tau_{(1)}\cup\tau_{(2)}\cup\cdots\cup\tau_{(m)}$。
In multi-agent systems, this decomposition has a natural interpretation: each subtask $\tau_{(k)}$ is assigned to an individual agent responsible for executing that segment of the trajectory. The bridge edges between components correspond to communication channels between the respective agents, enabling information flow across subtask boundaries when necessary.
在多智能体系统中，这种分解具有一种自然的解释：每个子任务 $\tau_{(k)}$ 被分配给一个单独的智能体，由其负责执行轨迹的相应片段。分量之间的桥边对应于相应智能体之间的通信通道，使信息能够在必要时跨越子任务边界流动。
Let $\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{S}}=\{\tau_{(1)},\tau_{(2)},\ldots,\tau_{(m)}\}$ denote the set of subtask trajectories. The directed edges between subtasks are induced by the bridge edges $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ . Since each bridge edge connects nodes from different components, this yields a well-defined relation:
令 $\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{S}}=\{\tau_{(1)},\tau_{(2)},\ldots,\tau_{(m)}\}$ 表示子任务轨迹的集合。子任务之间的有向边由桥边 $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ 诱导产生。由于每条桥边连接的节点属于不同分量，因此可以得到如下良定义的关系：
$$(\tau_{(p)},\tau_{(q)})\in\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{S}}\iff\exists(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{E}^{*}\text{ such that }x_{p}\in\tau_{(p)}\text{ and }x_{q}\in\tau_{(q)}.$$
The pair $\mathcal{T}=(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{S}})$ forms a subtask tree that captures information flow at the granularity of subtasks.
二元组 $\mathcal{T}=(\mathcal{V}_{\mathcal{S}},\mathcal{E}_{\mathcal{S}})$ 构成一棵子任务树，用于刻画子任务粒度上的信息流。
3.1.3 Context Cost Reduction3.1.3 上下文成本降低
Let $c(x)$ denote the non-negative computational overhead of including interaction tuple $x$ in the historical context. For a subtask trajectory $\tau_{(k)}=\{x_{n_{1}},\ldots,x_{n_{T_{k}}}\}$ , let $\mathcal{B}_{k}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}$ denote the set of bridge edges that enter $\tau_{(k)}$ , i.e., $\mathcal{B}_{k}=\{(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid x_{q}\in\tau_{(k)}\}.$ Let $\operatorname{idx}(x,\tau)$ denote the index of node $x$ within trajectory $\tau$ . The total context cost of executing subtask $\tau_{(k)}$ is decomposed as
令 $c(x)$ 表示将交互元组 $x$ 纳入历史上下文所产生的非负计算开销。对于子任务轨迹 $\tau_{(k)}=\{x_{n_{1}},\ldots,x_{n_{T_{k}}}\}$，令 $\mathcal{B}_{k}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}$ 表示进入 $\tau_{(k)}$ 的桥接边集合，即 $\mathcal{B}_{k}=\{(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid x_{q}\in\tau_{(k)}\}.$ 令 $\operatorname{idx}(x,\tau)$ 表示节点 $x$ 在轨迹 $\tau$ 中的索引。执行子任务 $\tau_{(k)}$ 的总上下文成本可分解为
$$C(\tau_{(k)})=C_{I}(\tau_{(k)})+C_{E}(\tau_{(k)})=\sum_{t=1}^{T_{k}}\sum_{i=1}^{t-1}c(x_{n_{i}})+\sum_{(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{B}_{k}}\sum_{i=\operatorname{idx}(x_{q},\tau_{(k)})}^{T_{k}}c(x_{p}),$$
where $C_{I}(\tau_{(k)})$ accounts for the cost of accumulating historical context from nodes within the same subtask, and $C_{E}(\tau_{(k)})$ accounts for the cost of pulling context $x_{p}$ from predecessor subtasks via incoming bridge edges, starting from the position of the target node $x_{q}$ through all subsequent steps.
其中，$C_{I}(\tau_{(k)})$ 表示从同一子任务内的节点累积历史上下文的成本，$C_{E}(\tau_{(k)})$ 表示通过入向桥接边从前序子任务引入上下文 $x_{p}$，并从目标节点 $x_{q}$ 所在位置起在所有后续步骤中保留该上下文的成本。
For the original monolithic trajectory $\tau$ , there are no incoming bridge edges, so $\mathcal{B}=\varnothing$ , and the cost is simply:
对于原始的整体轨迹 $\tau$，不存在入向桥接边，因此 $\mathcal{B}=\varnothing$，其成本仅为：
$$C(\tau)=\sum_{t=1}^{T}\sum_{i=1}^{t-1}c(x_{i}).$$
Our key claim is that, under idealized conditions where the complete task trajectory is known in advance, decomposing the trajectory into subtasks reduces the total context cost. The core of the proof is the following lemma, which shows that partitioning a trajectory along a single bridge edge does not increase the total context cost. The detailed proof of Lemma 1 is deferred to Appendix A.
我们的核心论断是，在预先已知完整任务轨迹的理想化条件下，将轨迹分解为子任务能够降低总上下文成本。证明的核心是以下引理，该引理表明，沿单条桥接边划分轨迹不会增加总上下文成本。引理 1 的详细证明见附录 A。
Lemma 1.引理 1。
For any trajectory $\tau_{(S)}=\tau_{(L)}\cup\tau_{(R)}$ partitioned into two subtask trajectories by a single bridge edge $(x_{L},x_{R})$ with $x_{L}\in\tau_{(L)}$ and $x_{R}\in\tau_{(R)}$ , the sum of the context costs of the two subtask trajectories is no greater than the cost of the original trajectory:
对于任意由单条桥边 $(x_{L},x_{R})$ 划分为两条子任务轨迹的轨迹 $\tau_{(S)}=\tau_{(L)}\cup\tau_{(R)}$，其中 $x_{L}\in\tau_{(L)}$ 且 $x_{R}\in\tau_{(R)}$，这两条子任务轨迹的上下文成本之和不大于原轨迹的成本：
$$C(\tau_{(L)})+C(\tau_{(R)})\leq C(\tau_{(S)}).$$
Recursively applying Lemma 1 to $\tau_{(L)}$ and $\tau_{(R)}$ yields:
对 $\tau_{(L)}$ 和 $\tau_{(R)}$ 递归应用引理 1，可得：
$$C(\tau)\geq C(\tau_{(L)})+C(\tau_{(R)})\geq\cdots\geq\sum_{k=1}^{m}C(\tau_{(k)}).$$
This establishes that, when the task trajectory is known and the decomposition is performed correctly, multi-agent decomposition reduces context cost. It should be noted that this analysis does not account for context compression or other mitigation strategies that may alter the cost structure.
这表明，在任务轨迹已知且分解正确的情况下，多智能体分解能够降低上下文成本。需要注意的是，该分析未考虑上下文压缩或其他可能改变成本结构的缓解策略。
3.1.4 Orchestration as Bridge Edge Prediction3.1.4 将编排视为桥接边预测
The decomposition above is post-hoc: it presumes complete knowledge of the entire trajectory $\tau$ and its dependency structure. In practice, the trajectory unfolds sequentially, and the DAG $\mathcal{G}$ is not known in advance. The objective of multi-agent orchestration is therefore to predict the bridge edges $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ before or during execution, without access to the complete trajectory.
上述分解是一种事后分解：它假定已完全掌握整条轨迹 $\tau$ 及其依赖结构。在实践中，轨迹按顺序逐步展开，有向无环图（DAG）$\mathcal{G}$ 并非预先已知。因此，多智能体编排的目标是在无法获取完整轨迹的情况下，于执行前或执行过程中预测桥接边 $\mathcal{E}^{*}$。
Let $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ denote the true set of bridge edges induced by the full trajectory, and let $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ be the set of bridge edges predicted by the orchestration strategy at time $t$ based on the observed prefix $\tau_{<t}$ . Since the true bridge edges $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ are only revealed after the entire trajectory is complete, the prediction $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ concerns edges that lie in the future, i.e., edges $e_{i\to j}$ with $j>t$ . The orchestration problem is to minimize the expected discrepancy between the true and predicted bridge edge sets:
令 $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ 表示由完整轨迹诱导的真实桥接边集合，令 $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ 表示编排策略在时刻 $t$ 基于已观测前缀 $\tau_{<t}$ 预测的桥接边集合。由于真实桥接边 $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ 只有在整条轨迹完成后才会揭示，因此预测 $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ 针对的是未来的边，即满足 $j>t$ 的边 $e_{i\to j}$。编排问题旨在最小化真实桥接边集合与预测桥接边集合之间的期望差异：
$$\min_{\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}}\;\mathbb{E}\left[\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{E}^{*},\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t})\mid\tau_{<t}\right],$$
where $\mathcal{L}$ is a loss function that measures the dissimilarity between two sets of directed edges over $\mathcal{V}$ . A correct prediction partitions the trajectory into the true subtasks; an incorrect prediction results in suboptimal decomposition and diminished context cost reduction.
其中，$\mathcal{L}$ 是一个损失函数，用于衡量定义在 $\mathcal{V}$ 上的两个有向边集合之间的差异。正确的预测会将轨迹划分为真实的子任务；错误的预测则会导致次优分解，并削弱上下文成本的降低效果。
3.2 Discussion3.2 讨论
Our theoretical and empirical results jointly characterize when multi-agent collaboration helps and why its benefits are bounded.
我们的理论与实证结果共同刻画了多智能体协作何时有益，以及其收益为何存在上限。
When collaboration pays off. Multi-agent collaboration is effective precisely when a task admits relatively independent subtasks, so that each agent maintains a focused context window and excludes task-irrelevant information. A single-agent system, by contrast, must accumulate observations indiscriminately, degrading the signal-to-noise ratio and increasing the risk of erroneous execution. This benefit is contingent on the accuracy of bridge-edge prediction: a mispredicted edge can trap an agent in a local deadlock while awaiting a dependency that never materializes, or inflate communication overhead as agents recover from an erroneous partition.
协作何时有效。当任务能够分解为相对独立的子任务，使每个智能体都能维持聚焦的上下文窗口并排除与任务无关的信息时，多智能体协作才能发挥作用。相比之下，单智能体系统必须不加区分地累积观测信息，从而降低信噪比，并增加执行出错的风险。这一收益取决于桥接边预测的准确性：错误预测的边可能使智能体在等待一个始终未能满足的依赖条件时陷入局部死锁，也可能在智能体从错误划分中恢复的过程中增加通信开销。
The cost of isolation. Context isolation localizes each agent’s working memory to its subtask, containing failures within the corresponding scope rather than letting them contaminate the global context. This mitigates the incomplete workflows and premature termination that plague single-agent execution on long-horizon tasks, at the cost of higher aggregate token consumption, since decomposition extends the overall workflow.
隔离的代价。上下文隔离将每个智能体的工作记忆限制在其子任务范围内，使故障局限于相应范围，而不会污染全局上下文。这缓解了单智能体执行长时程任务时常见的工作流不完整和过早终止问题，但由于分解延长了整体工作流，其代价是更高的总 token 消耗。
Why scaling does not help. Because each bridge edge is predicted from an observed prefix, prediction errors accumulate as the number of subtasks grows, while additional agents incur higher coordination costs in communication, synchronization, and conflict resolution. The marginal benefit of adding agents therefore diminishes, and may eventually be offset by accumulated errors and rising coordination overhead. This explains why more agents do not necessarily make a system more intelligent, and motivates a mechanism that grows its collaboration graph only where the task structure permits.
为何扩大规模无济于事。由于每条桥边都是根据已观测到的前缀预测的，预测误差会随着子任务数量的增加而累积，同时，增加智能体会带来更高的通信、同步和冲突解决等协调成本。因此，增加智能体的边际收益会递减，并可能最终被累积误差和不断上升的协调开销所抵消。这解释了为什么更多的智能体并不一定会使系统更智能，也促使我们设计一种仅在任务结构允许时才扩展协作图的机制。
4 Methodology4 方法
We propose Semantic-Aware Incremental Graph Evolution (SAIGE), a lightweight mechanism for decomposing tasks under trajectory uncertainty. Rather than predicting all bridge edges in advance, SAIGE evolves the subtask tree incrementally during execution, growing the structure only when semantic dependencies are revealed by execution feedback.
我们提出语义感知增量图演化（Semantic-Aware Incremental Graph Evolution，SAIGE），这是一种在轨迹不确定性下进行任务分解的轻量级机制。SAIGE 并不预先预测所有桥边，而是在执行过程中以增量方式演化子任务树，仅当执行反馈揭示语义依赖关系时才扩展其结构。

图 2：SAIGE：通过渐进式子任务分解实现增量节点生成，并通过按需获取消息实现渐进式边解析。
4.1 Incremental Node Generation4.1 增量节点生成
Bridge edges are inherently difficult to predict reliably at the outset, and premature commitment to a static topology risks compounding early mispredictions. Let $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}$ and $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ denote the predicted bridge-edge sets at consecutive steps, with $\mathcal{E}^{*}$ the ground truth. Since SAIGE predicts incrementally, we have $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}\subseteq\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ , and
桥边本就难以在一开始得到可靠预测，过早确定静态拓扑可能使早期误判的影响不断累积。令 $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}$ 和 $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$ 表示连续两个步骤中预测的桥边集合，$\mathcal{E}^{*}$ 表示真实桥边集合。由于 SAIGE 以增量方式进行预测，因此有 $\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}\subseteq\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}$，并且
$$P(\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*},\tau_{<t})=\frac{P(\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid\tau_{<t})}{P(\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t-1}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid\tau_{<t})}\geq P(\hat{\mathcal{E}}_{t}\subseteq\mathcal{E}^{*}\mid\tau_{<t}),$$
which indicates that deferring edge prediction until execution evidence accumulates is more reliable than committing to a topology upfront.
这表明，将边预测推迟至执行证据积累之后，比预先确定拓扑更可靠。
Accordingly, SAIGE spawns subtask nodes on demand rather than pre-allocating them. A running agent delegates a child agent only when it detects a dependency boundary that satisfies the bridge-edge property, that is, a single-step dependency across which the subproblem can be executed independently given a bounded context summary. When such a boundary is present, the subproblem is delegated and the child agent executes autonomously with a self-contained instruction. When no such boundary is present, no child is spawned and the parent agent continues to execute the task monolithically. This conditional delegation is what allows SAIGE to avoid the decomposition penalty on densely coupled tasks.
因此，SAIGE 按需生成子任务节点，而非预先分配这些节点。运行中的智能体只有在检测到满足桥边性质的依赖边界时，才会将子任务委派给子智能体；这种边界即单步依赖，在给定大小受限的上下文摘要后，边界另一侧的子问题即可独立执行。当存在这样的边界时，子问题会被委派出去，子智能体则依据一条自包含的指令自主执行。当不存在这样的边界时，不会生成子智能体，父智能体会继续以单体方式执行任务。正是这种有条件的委派，使 SAIGE 能够避免在高度耦合的任务上产生分解代价。
4.2 Progressive Edge Resolution4.2 渐进式边解析
Edges between subtask nodes are established through progressive message fetching. Each agent writes its result upon completion and retrieves dependencies only when needed. Let $\mathcal{B}_{k}$ denote the set of bridge edges entering subtask $\tau_{(k)}$ , and let $\operatorname{idx}(x_{q},\tau_{(k)})$ denote the step at which dependency on $x_{p}$ becomes active. Progressive fetching incurs cost
子任务节点之间的边通过渐进式消息获取来建立。每个智能体在完成任务后写入其结果，并仅在需要时检索依赖信息。令 $\mathcal{B}_{k}$ 表示指向子任务 $\tau_{(k)}$ 的桥边集合，并令 $\operatorname{idx}(x_{q},\tau_{(k)})$ 表示对 $x_{p}$ 的依赖开始生效的步骤。渐进式获取产生的成本为
$$\sum_{(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{B}_{k}}\sum_{i=\operatorname{idx}(x_{q},\tau_{(k)})}^{T_{k}}c(x_{p})\leq\sum_{(x_{p},x_{q})\in\mathcal{B}_{k}}\sum_{i=1}^{T_{k}}c(x_{p}),$$
where the right-hand side loads all dependencies at the start. Sparse on-demand retrieval thus dominates push-based communication in context cost. More importantly, progressive fetching establishes communication channels dynamically rather than fixing them at decomposition time, allowing the collaboration graph to adapt to dependencies not fully determined in advance. This converts global topology prediction into localized retrieval decisions grounded in execution feedback.
其中，右侧对应于在开始时加载所有依赖信息的情形。因此，稀疏的按需检索在上下文成本方面优于基于推送的通信。更重要的是，渐进式获取会动态建立通信通道，而非在任务分解时将其固定下来，从而使协作图能够适应事先未完全确定的依赖关系。这将全局拓扑预测转化为以执行反馈为依据的局部检索决策。

5.1 Experimental Setup5.1 实验设置
5.1.1 Benchmarks5.1.1 基准
We evaluate on four benchmarks designed to assess agent capabilities on complex, long-horizon, realistic tasks. Terminal Bench 2.1 (Merrill et al., 2026) tests agents on hard, realistic command-line interface tasks, with resolution rate as the primary metric. NL2Repo Bench (Ding et al., 2026) evaluates long-horizon repository generation from a single natural-language requirements document, using the average test pass rate across all tasks as the primary metric. Deep Research Bench II (Li et al., 2026) diagnoses deep research agents through rubrics derived from expert reports, scored as the fraction of rubrics passed. AgentIF-OneDay (Chen et al., 2026) is a task-level instruction-following benchmark for general AI agents in daily scenarios, employing instance-level, rubric-based scoring with binary evaluation, separated bonus/penalty items, and file-content alignment. Detailed descriptions of all benchmarks are provided in Appendix B.1.
我们在四个基准上进行评估，这些基准旨在考察智能体处理复杂、长程、现实任务的能力。Terminal Bench 2.1 (Merrill et al., 2026) 使用高难度的现实命令行界面任务测试智能体，并以解决率作为主要指标。NL2Repo Bench (Ding et al., 2026) 评估根据单份自然语言需求文档进行长程代码仓库生成的能力，并以所有任务的平均测试通过率作为主要指标。Deep Research Bench II (Li et al., 2026) 通过从专家报告中提炼的评分标准，对深度研究智能体进行诊断，并以通过的评分标准占比计分。AgentIF-OneDay (Chen et al., 2026) 是面向日常场景中通用 AI 智能体的任务级指令遵循基准，采用基于评分标准的实例级评分方式，实施二元评估、区分加分项与扣分项，并对齐文件内容。所有基准的详细说明见附录 B.1。
5.1.2 Baselines5.1.2 基线
Single-Agent executes the full task within a single agent context and serves as the monolithic baseline. It runs on the native Codex CLI harness (OpenAI, 2025b). Task Decomposition and Agent Generation (TDAG) (Wang et al., 2025b) is a multi-agent framework that decomposes complex tasks into smaller subtasks, assigns each subtask to a specifically generated subagent, and continuously summarizes successful execution patterns into reusable skills for future reference. DynTaskMAS (Yu et al., 2025) is a dynamic task graph-driven framework for asynchronous and parallel LLM-based multi-agent systems. Graph-of-Agents (GoA) (Yun et al., 2026) models multi-agent LLM communication through a graph-based framework, sampling relevant agents, constructing edges by evaluating response relevance, and aggregating responses via graph-based pooling. Further details on baseline implementations are provided in Appendix B.2.
Single-Agent 在单个智能体上下文中执行完整任务，作为单体基线。它在原生 Codex CLI 运行框架 (OpenAI, 2025b) 上运行。Task Decomposition and Agent Generation (TDAG) (Wang et al., 2025b) 是一种多智能体框架，它将复杂任务分解为较小的子任务，将每个子任务分配给为其专门生成的子智能体，并持续将成功的执行模式总结为可复用的技能，以供后续参考。DynTaskMAS (Yu et al., 2025) 是一种由动态任务图驱动的框架，面向基于 LLM 的异步并行多智能体系统。Graph-of-Agents (GoA) (Yun et al., 2026) 通过基于图的框架对多智能体 LLM 通信进行建模，采样相关智能体，通过评估响应相关性构建边，并通过基于图的池化聚合响应。基线实现的更多细节见附录 B.2。
5.1.3 Implementation5.1.3 实现
To ensure harness consistency, all methods are instantiated on the Codex CLI harness. For multi-agent methods, token usage is aggregated across all agents. All baselines and our approach are built upon DeepSeek-V4-Pro (DeepSeek, 2026c) as the foundation LLM. For all multi-agent methods evaluated in this work, we impose a uniform constraint on agent hierarchy: the maximum recursion depth is set to 1, meaning that spawned sub-agents are not permitted to recursively create further sub-agents. Additionally, the maximum number of agents is capped at 4. These constraints are imposed uniformly across all multi-agent methods to ensure system stability and prevent unbounded agent proliferation during execution. We examine the effect of relaxing these constraints in the ablation study (Section 5.4) and further verify it on the multi-agent baselines in Appendix B.3.
为确保运行框架的一致性，所有方法均在 Codex CLI 运行框架上实例化。对于多智能体方法，token 用量按所有智能体的用量汇总计算。所有基线和我们的方法均以 DeepSeek-V4-Pro (DeepSeek, 2026c) 作为基础 LLM 构建。对于本研究评估的所有多智能体方法，我们对智能体层级施加统一约束：最大递归深度设为 1，即不允许生成的子智能体递归创建更多子智能体。此外，智能体数量上限设为 4。这些约束统一施加于所有多智能体方法，以确保系统稳定性，并防止执行过程中智能体数量无限增长。我们在消融研究（第 5.4 节）中考察放宽这些约束的影响，并在附录 B.3 中针对多智能体基线进一步验证这一影响。

SAIGE does not exhibit the same degradation: it remains comparable to the single-agent baseline on Terminal Bench 2.1 and on NL2Repo Bench, while using fewer tokens than the other multi-agent methods. On the sparsely dependent benchmarks, decomposition appears more beneficial, and SAIGE performs competitively on both, with the most visible margin on AgentIF-OneDay, without the token inflation observed in the other multi-agent variants. GoA, by contrast, employs a more elaborate orchestration design yet yields weaker results on these benchmarks, which suggests that added coordination complexity does not by itself translate into gains when it is not matched to the task’s dependency structure.
在密集耦合的基准上，通用多智能体方法的表现低于单智能体基线，这表明任务分解会割裂依赖链，而协调开销可能超过上下文隔离带来的收益。SAIGE 未出现同样的性能下降：它在 Terminal Bench 2.1 和 NL2Repo Bench 上均保持了与单智能体基线相当的表现，同时使用的 token 少于其他多智能体方法。在依赖稀疏的基准上，任务分解似乎更有益；SAIGE 在这两个基准上均表现出竞争力，在 AgentIF-OneDay 上的优势最为明显，同时未出现其他多智能体变体中观察到的 token 用量膨胀。相比之下，GoA 采用了更复杂的编排设计，却在这些基准上取得了较差的结果，这表明，当增加的协调复杂性与任务的依赖结构不匹配时，其本身并不能转化为收益。
Taken together, these results are consistent with our central claim that the benefit of multi-agent collaboration is bounded by task structure rather than universal. SAIGE does not dominate the single-agent baseline on every benchmark, and its advantage is most evident on sparsely dependent tasks. Across both regimes, however, it appears to attain a favorable trade-off: it avoids the decomposition penalty on tightly coupled tasks, exploits context isolation on decomposable ones, and grows its collaboration graph in response to execution feedback rather than a pre-committed topology.
综合来看，这些结果与我们的核心论点一致：多智能体协作的收益受任务结构制约，而非普遍存在。SAIGE 并非在每个基准上都优于单智能体基线，其优势在依赖稀疏的任务上最为明显。不过，在这两种模式下，它似乎都实现了较好的权衡：在紧密耦合的任务上避免任务分解带来的性能损失，在可分解的任务上利用上下文隔离，并根据执行反馈扩展其协作图，而非采用预先确定的拓扑结构。

6 Conclusion6 结论
In this work, we reexamine the value of multi-agent collaboration in the era of increasingly capable single-agent harnesses. Through a graph-theoretic formalization of task trajectories, we show that multi-agent collaboration is not universally beneficial, but confers systematic advantages specifically on long-horizon tasks with sparse dependencies, where context isolation can be exploited without incurring prohibitive coordination overhead. Building on this insight, we propose SAIGE, a multi-agent collaboration mechanism that models collaboration as a dynamically evolving graph and establishes semantic dependencies through content-based information retrieval. Experiments on long-horizon benchmarks show that SAIGE achieves a favorable trade-off between context efficiency and task performance. These findings suggest that multi-agent superiority is bounded by task structure rather than universal, and that indiscriminate scaling of agents warrants reconsideration.
在本研究中，我们重新审视了单智能体运行框架（harness）能力日益增强的时代中多智能体协作的价值。通过对任务轨迹进行图论形式化，我们表明，多智能体协作并非普遍有益，而是在依赖关系稀疏的长程任务上具有系统性优势，因为在此类任务中，可以利用上下文隔离，而不会产生高得难以承受的协调开销。基于这一认识，我们提出了 SAIGE，这是一种多智能体协作机制，将协作建模为动态演化的图，并通过基于内容的信息检索建立语义依赖关系。在长程任务基准上的实验表明，SAIGE 在上下文效率与任务性能之间实现了良好的权衡。这些发现表明，多智能体的优势受到任务结构的制约，并非普遍成立，因此有必要重新考虑不加区分地扩增智能体的做法。

Terminal Bench 2.1 [Merrill et al., 2026] is a curated hard benchmark composed of tasks in computer terminal environments inspired by real-world workflows. Each task features a unique environment, a human-written solution, and comprehensive tests for verification. Terminal Bench 2.1 [Merrill et al., 2026] 是一个经过精心筛选的高难度基准，由计算机终端环境中的任务组成，这些任务的灵感来源于真实世界的工作流程。每个任务均配有独特的环境、人工编写的解决方案，以及用于验证的全面测试。由于该框架具有交互性，智能体与模型的性能难以解耦；许多智能体框架经过专门设计，以适应特定模型的行为倾向，而且并未明确要求智能体仅使用终端作为工具。为忠实于该基准的设定，我们采用仅配备一个无头终端的中立测试环境，并仅使用 Bash 命令完成任务。主要指标是解决率。在我们的评估中，每个任务均尝试三次，解决率的计算方式为：将每轮运行中完成的任务数除以任务总数，再对三轮运行的结果取平均值。

Graph-of-Agents (GoA) [Yun et al., 2026] models multi-agent LLM communication through a graph-based framework. It first samples relevant agents from a candidate pool, then constructs edges between them by evaluating the relevance of their responses to one another, and finally aggregates the refined responses via graph-based pooling. To ensure consistency of the base model across all evaluated methods, we do not adopt GoA’s original multi-source LLM design, in which different agents are backed by different foundation models. To obtain a ground-truth decomposition for AgentIF-OneDay, we first collect the successful trajectories produced by the Single-Agent baseline on this benchmark. For each such trajectory, we construct a semantic dependency matrix with DeepSeek-V4-Pro: for every step $t$ and each earlier step $i<t$ , the model judges whether step $t$ semantically conditions on the output of step $i$ , i.e., whether the action or observation at step $t$ would change if $x_{i}$ were removed from the history. This yields a binary dependency matrix. Bridge edges are then identified as the single-step dependencies whose removal partitions the trajectory into weakly connected components, and the resulting components define the ground-truth decomposition points at which the task can be split into independently executable subtasks.
为获得 AgentIF-OneDay 的真实分解，我们首先收集 Single-Agent 基线在该基准上生成的成功轨迹。对于每条这样的轨迹，我们使用 DeepSeek-V4-Pro 构建语义依赖矩阵：对于每个步骤 $t$ 及其之前的每个步骤 $i<t$，模型判断步骤 $t$ 是否在语义上以步骤 $i$ 的输出为条件，即如果从历史中移除 $x_{i}$，步骤 $t$ 的动作或观测是否会发生变化。由此得到一个二元依赖矩阵。随后，将移除后会使轨迹分裂为多个弱连通分量的单步依赖识别为桥边，由此得到的分量确定了真实分解点，在这些位置，任务可以拆分为可独立执行的子任务。
We then evaluate the decomposition produced by each multi-agent method against this ground truth. For every sub-agent trajectory generated by a method, we semantically match its instruction and executed steps to the ground-truth subtask components using DeepSeek-V4-Pro, and count a predicted bridge edge as correct if it connects the same pair of subtask components as a ground-truth bridge edge. Precision, recall, and F1 are computed over the matched bridge edges across all tasks.
随后，我们以这一真实分解为参照，评估各多智能体方法产生的分解。对于一种方法生成的每条子智能体轨迹，我们使用 DeepSeek-V4-Pro 将其指令和已执行步骤与真实子任务分量进行语义匹配；如果一条预测桥边连接的子任务分量对与某条真实桥边相同，则将该预测桥边计为正确。基于所有任务中匹配的桥边，计算精确率、召回率和 F1。

C.2 Bridge Edge Mis-Prediction: A Local Deadlock CaseC.2 桥接边误预测：一个局部死锁案例
We illustrate the effect of bridge edge mis-prediction on a representative task from Terminal Bench 2.1, make-doom-for-mips, in which the agent must build a MIPS ELF that runs under a supplied JavaScript MIPS VM. The task contains a long upstream-downstream chain: the VM defines the syscall ABI and the memory layout, the build must target a compatible MIPS ABI, and the resulting ELF must be validated by running it inside the VM. A correct decomposition must preserve the bridge edges that connect these stages.
我们以 Terminal Bench 2.1 中的代表性任务 make-doom-for-mips 为例，说明桥接边误预测的影响；在该任务中，智能体必须构建一个能够在所提供的 JavaScript MIPS 虚拟机（VM）中运行的 MIPS ELF。该任务包含一条较长的上下游链：VM 定义系统调用 ABI 和内存布局，构建过程必须面向兼容的 MIPS ABI，而生成的 ELF 必须通过在 VM 内运行来进行验证。正确的分解必须保留连接这些阶段的桥接边。
Figure 8: Ground-truth and mis-predicted dependency structures on make-doom-for-mips.
图 8：make-doom-for-mips 上的真实依赖结构与误预测的依赖结构。
The error is one omitted bridge edge: the dependency from VM memory layout (step 3) to build configuration (step 6) in the ground-truth dependency structure. In the ground-truth structure, this edge carries the constraint that determines the correct ABI and memory model. In the predicted structure, the edge is absent.
错误在于遗漏了一条桥接边：即真实依赖结构中从 VM 内存布局（步骤 3）到构建配置（步骤 6）的依赖关系。在真实结构中，这条边承载着决定正确 ABI 和内存模型的约束。在预测结构中，这条边缺失了。
This omission produces a local deadlock. The build agent configures the MIPS toolchain without the VM memory layout, while the validation agent repeatedly rebuilds and waits for a build that satisfies the omitted constraint. Neither agent can break the cycle: the build agent adjusts flags that are not the actual bottleneck, and the validation agent keeps issuing rebuild-and-run cycles.
这一遗漏导致了局部死锁。构建智能体在缺少 VM 内存布局信息的情况下配置 MIPS 工具链，而验证智能体则反复重新构建，并等待一个满足被遗漏约束的构建结果。两个智能体都无法打破这一循环：构建智能体不断调整并非真正瓶颈所在的标志参数，而验证智能体则持续发起重新构建并运行的循环。
Two costs follow. Communication overhead rises because repeated build-and-run messages do not converge, and redundant execution accumulates because each cycle reruns the same build and the same VM validation.
由此产生了两项成本。通信开销上升，因为反复传递的构建与运行消息无法促使流程收敛；冗余执行不断累积，因为每轮循环都会重新执行相同的构建过程和相同的 VM 验证。
If the bridge edge is predicted correctly, the build configuration is conditioned on the VM memory layout and syscall ABI, and the correct ABI and soft-float configuration are selected before the first build. The validation stage then observes a consistent ELF, and the trajectory terminates after a small number of build-and-run cycles. This contrast shows why orchestration is naturally formulated as bridge edge prediction: an omitted bridge edge does not merely reduce context cost reduction, but can turn a convergent workflow into a local deadlock.
如果桥接边预测正确，构建配置就会以 VM 内存布局和系统调用 ABI 为依据，并在首次构建之前选定正确的 ABI 和软浮点配置。随后，验证阶段便会得到一个符合要求的 ELF，执行轨迹在少量构建与运行循环后即可终止。这一对比说明了为何可以自然地将编排表述为桥接边预测问题：遗漏一条桥接边不仅会削弱上下文成本的降低效果，还可能使一个能够收敛的工作流陷入局部死锁。
Appendix D Impact Statement附录 D 影响声明
This work studies when multi-agent collaboration is beneficial for long-horizon agentic tasks and when it is not. Its primary impact is conceptual and practical: it clarifies that multi-agent superiority is bounded by task structure rather than universal, and that blindly scaling the number of agents can increase coordination overhead without improving task performance. This message is relevant to practitioners who build agentic systems, since it suggests that collaboration should be introduced selectively, based on the dependency structure of the target task, rather than adopted as a default design choice.
本研究探讨多智能体协作在何种情况下有益于长时程智能体任务，以及在何种情况下无益。其主要影响体现在概念和实践两个层面：它阐明了多智能体的优势受任务结构制约，并非普遍成立；盲目增加智能体数量可能会增加协调开销，却无法改善任务表现。这一结论与构建智能体系统的实践者密切相关，因为它表明，应根据目标任务的依赖结构，有选择地引入协作，而非将其作为默认的设计选择。
On the positive side, the proposed SAIGE mechanism grows its collaboration graph in response to execution feedback and establishes semantic dependencies through content-based information retrieval. This may help reduce unnecessary context accumulation and improve the reliability of long-horizon execution, which is relevant to applications such as software engineering agents, repository-level code generation, and deep research assistants. By making the cost-performance trade-off of multi-agent decomposition explicit, our analysis may also encourage more disciplined evaluation and reporting practices in this area.
在积极影响方面，所提出的 SAIGE 机制根据执行反馈扩展其协作图，并通过基于内容的信息检索建立语义依赖关系。这可能有助于减少不必要的上下文累积，并提高长时程执行的可靠性，对软件工程智能体、代码仓库级代码生成和深度研究助手等应用具有意义。通过明确多智能体分解在成本与性能之间的权衡，我们的分析还可能推动该领域采用更加严谨的评估和报告实践。
On the risk side, more capable and autonomous agentic systems can be misused for harmful purposes, including automated exploitation of software vulnerabilities, unauthorized access to computing resources, or large-scale generation of misleading content. The benchmarks used in this work are designed for research evaluation and operate in controlled environments, but the underlying techniques are not inherently restricted to benign settings. Multi-agent decomposition may also introduce failure modes that are harder to audit, such as local deadlocks, mispredicted dependencies, and opaque coordination among agents, which can complicate debugging, accountability, and oversight.
在风险方面，能力更强、自主性更高的智能体系统可能被滥用于有害目的，包括自动利用软件漏洞、未经授权访问计算资源，或大规模生成误导性内容。本研究所使用的基准测试专为研究评估而设计，并在受控环境中运行，但其底层技术本身并不限于良性应用场景。多智能体分解还可能引入更难审计的失效模式，例如局部死锁、依赖关系误预测以及智能体之间不透明的协调，这可能使调试、问责和监督变得更加复杂。
