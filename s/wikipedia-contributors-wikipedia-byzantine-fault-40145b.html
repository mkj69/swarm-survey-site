A Byzantine fault is a condition of a system, particularly a distributed computing system, where a fault occurs such that different symptoms are presented to different observers, including imperfect information on whether a system component has failed. The term takes its name from an allegory, the "Byzantine generals problem", developed to describe a situation in which, to avoid catastrophic failure of a system, the system's actors must agree on a strategy, but some of these actors are unreliable in a way which causes other (good) actors to disagree on the strategy and they may be unaware of the disagreement.
拜占庭故障（Byzantine fault）是系统，尤其是分布式计算系统的一种状态：发生的故障会向不同观察者呈现不同的症状，包括使观察者无法获得关于某个系统组件是否已失效的完整信息。该术语得名于一个名为“拜占庭将军问题”的寓言，该寓言旨在描述这样一种情形：为了避免系统发生灾难性失效，系统中的参与者必须就某项策略达成一致，但其中一些参与者并不可靠，其行为会导致其他（正常的）参与者在策略上产生分歧，而后者可能并未意识到这种分歧。
A Byzantine fault is also known as a Byzantine generals problem, a Byzantine agreement problem, or a Byzantine failure.
拜占庭故障也称为拜占庭将军问题、拜占庭一致性问题或拜占庭失效。
Byzantine fault tolerance (BFT) is the resilience of a fault-tolerant computer system or similar system to such conditions.
拜占庭容错（Byzantine fault tolerance，BFT）是指容错计算机系统或类似系统抵御此类状况的能力。
Definition定义
A Byzantine fault is any fault presenting different symptoms to different observers. A Byzantine failure is the loss of a system service due to a Byzantine fault in systems that require consensus among multiple components.
拜占庭故障是指向不同观察者呈现不同症状的任何故障。拜占庭失效是指在需要多个组件达成共识的系统中，由于拜占庭故障而导致系统服务丧失。
The Byzantine allegory considers a number of generals who are attacking a fortress. The generals must decide as a group whether to attack or retreat; some may prefer to attack, while others prefer to retreat. The important thing is that all generals agree on a common decision, for a half-hearted attack by a few generals would become a rout, and would be worse than either a coordinated attack or a coordinated retreat.
拜占庭寓言设想了若干名正在进攻一座堡垒的将军。将军们必须集体决定是进攻还是撤退；有些将军可能倾向于进攻，另一些则倾向于撤退。关键在于所有将军都要就同一个决定达成一致，因为少数将军发起的零散进攻会演变成溃败，其结果比协同进攻或协同撤退都要糟糕。
The problem is complicated by the presence of treacherous generals who may not only cast a vote for a suboptimal strategy; they may do so selectively. For instance, if nine generals are voting, four of whom support attacking while four others are in favor of retreat, the ninth general may send a vote of retreat to those generals in favor of retreat, and a vote of attack to the rest. Those who received a retreat vote from the ninth general will retreat, while the rest will attack (which may not go well for the attackers). The problem is complicated further by the generals being physically separated and having to send their votes via messengers who may fail to deliver votes or may forge false votes.
叛变将军的存在使问题变得复杂：他们不仅可能投票支持并非最优的策略，还可能有选择地向不同将军投出不同的票。例如，如果有九名将军投票，其中四名支持进攻，另四名支持撤退，那么第九名将军可能向支持撤退的将军发送撤退票，并向其余将军发送进攻票。收到第九名将军撤退票的将军会撤退，其余将军则会进攻（进攻方的结果可能并不理想）。将军们身处不同地点，必须通过信使传递选票，而信使可能未能送达选票，也可能伪造选票，这使问题更加复杂。
Without message signing, Byzantine fault tolerance can only be achieved if the total number of generals is greater than three times the number of disloyal (faulty) generals. There can be a default vote value given to missing messages. For example, missing messages can be given a "null" value. Further, if the agreement is that the null votes are in the majority, a pre-assigned default strategy can be used (e.g., retreat).
在不对消息进行签名的情况下，只有当将军总数大于不忠诚（有故障）将军数量的三倍时，才能实现拜占庭容错。可以为缺失的消息指定一个默认投票值。例如，可以为缺失的消息赋予“null”值。此外，如果一致认定 null 票占多数，则可以采用预先指定的默认策略（例如撤退）。
The typical mapping of this allegory onto computer systems is that the computers are the generals and their digital communication system links are the messengers. Although the problem is formulated in the allegory as a decision-making and security problem, in electronics, it cannot be solved by cryptographic digital signatures alone, because failures such as incorrect voltages can propagate through the encryption process. Thus, a faulty message could be sent such that some recipients detect the message as faulty (bad signature), others see it as having a good signature, and a third group also sees a good signature but with different message contents than the second group.
将这一寓言映射到计算机系统时，通常由计算机对应将军，由其数字通信系统的链路对应信使。尽管该寓言将问题表述为一个决策与安全问题，但在电子系统中，仅靠密码学数字签名无法解决这一问题，因为电压不正确等故障可能在加密过程中传播。因此，一条有故障的消息可能以这样的方式发送：一些接收者检测出消息有故障（签名无效），另一些接收者认为其签名有效，而第三组接收者也认为签名有效，但看到的消息内容与第二组不同。
History历史
The problem of obtaining Byzantine consensus was conceived and formalized by Robert Shostak, who dubbed it the interactive consistency problem. This work was done in 1978 in the context of the NASA-sponsored SIFT project in the Computer Science Lab at SRI International. SIFT (for Software Implemented Fault Tolerance) was the brainchild of John Wensley, and was based on the idea of using multiple general-purpose computers that would communicate through pairwise messaging in order to reach a consensus, even if some of the computers were faulty.
达成拜占庭共识的问题由 Robert Shostak 提出并形式化，他将其命名为交互一致性问题（interactive consistency problem）。这项工作于 1978 年在 SRI International 的计算机科学实验室开展，属于 NASA 资助的 SIFT 项目。SIFT（Software Implemented Fault Tolerance，即软件实现的容错）由 John Wensley 构想，其基本思路是使用多台通用计算机，通过两两交换消息进行通信，从而即使部分计算机发生故障也能达成共识。
At the beginning of the project, it was not clear how many computers in total were needed to guarantee that a conspiracy of n faulty computers could not "thwart" the efforts of the correctly-operating ones to reach consensus. Shostak showed that a minimum of 3n+1 are needed, and devised a two-round 3n+1 messaging protocol that would work for n=1. His colleague Marshall Pease generalized the algorithm for any n > 0, proving that 3n+1 is both necessary and sufficient. These results, together with a later proof by Leslie Lamport of the sufficiency of 3n using digital signatures, were published in the seminal paper, Reaching Agreement in the Presence of Faults. The authors were awarded the 2005 Edsger W. Dijkstra Prize for this paper.
项目开始时，尚不清楚总共需要多少台计算机，才能保证 n 台故障计算机的合谋无法“阻挠”正常运行的计算机达成共识。Shostak 证明至少需要 3n+1 台计算机，并设计了一种适用于 n=1 的两轮 3n+1 消息传递协议。他的同事 Marshall Pease 将该算法推广到了任意 n > 0 的情况，并证明 3n+1 既是必要条件，也是充分条件。这些结果与 Leslie Lamport 后来关于使用数字签名时 3n 即已足够的证明，一同发表于奠基性论文《存在故障时达成一致》（Reaching Agreement in the Presence of Faults）。作者们因这篇论文获得了 2005 年 Edsger W. Dijkstra 奖。
To make the interactive consistency problem easier to understand, Lamport devised a colorful allegory in which a group of army generals formulate a plan for attacking a city. In its original version, the story cast the generals as commanders of the Albanian army. The name was changed, eventually settling on "Byzantine", at the suggestion of Jack Goldberg to future-proof any potential offense-giving. This formulation of the problem, together with some additional results, were presented by the same authors in their 1982 paper, "The Byzantine Generals Problem".
为使交互一致性问题更易于理解，Lamport 构思了一个生动的寓言，讲述一群将军制定进攻一座城市的计划。在最初的版本中，故事将这些将军设定为阿尔巴尼亚军队的指挥官。根据 Jack Goldberg 的建议，为避免这一称呼今后可能冒犯他人，该名称经过修改，最终定为“拜占庭”。同一批作者在其 1982 年的论文《拜占庭将军问题》中提出了这一问题表述以及一些其他结果。
Mitigation缓解措施
The objective of Byzantine fault tolerance is to be able to defend against failures of system components with or without symptoms that prevent other components of the system from reaching an agreement among themselves, where such an agreement is needed for the correct operation of the system.
拜占庭容错（Byzantine fault tolerance）的目标是抵御系统组件的失效，无论这些失效是否表现出症状；这些失效会妨碍系统中的其他组件彼此达成一致，而这种一致是系统正确运行所必需的。
The remaining operationally correct components of a Byzantine fault tolerant system will be able to continue providing the system's service as originally intended, assuming there are a sufficient number of accurately-operating components to maintain the service.
只要有足够数量的组件准确运行以维持服务，拜占庭容错系统中其余运行正常的组件就能继续按照最初的预期提供系统服务。
When considering failure propagation only via errors, Byzantine failures are considered the most general and most difficult class of failures among the failure modes. The so-called fail-stop failure mode occupies the simplest end of the spectrum. Whereas the fail-stop failure mode simply means that the only way to fail is a node crash, detected by other nodes, Byzantine failures imply no restrictions on what errors can be created, which means that a failed node can generate arbitrary data, including data that makes it appear like a functioning node to a subset of other nodes. Thus, Byzantine failures can confuse failure detection systems, which makes fault tolerance difficult. Despite the allegory, a Byzantine failure is not necessarily a security problem involving hostile human interference: it can arise purely from physical or software faults.
当仅考虑通过错误传播的失效时，拜占庭失效被认为是各类失效模式中最一般、也最难处理的一类。所谓的故障即停（fail-stop）失效模式处于这一谱系中最简单的一端。故障即停失效模式仅意味着，唯一的失效方式是节点崩溃，且这一崩溃会被其他节点检测到；而拜占庭失效对可能产生的错误没有任何限制，这意味着失效节点可以生成任意数据，包括使其在部分其他节点看来仍正常运行的数据。因此，拜占庭失效可能迷惑失效检测系统，从而使容错变得困难。尽管采用了这一寓言，拜占庭失效并不一定是涉及人为恶意干扰的安全问题：它也可能完全由物理故障或软件故障引起。
The terms fault and failure are used here according to the standard definitions originally created by a joint committee on "Fundamental Concepts and Terminology" formed by the IEEE Computer Society's Technical Committee on Dependable Computing and Fault-Tolerance and IFIP Working Group 10.4 on Dependable Computing and Fault Tolerance. See also dependability.
这里使用的故障（fault）和失效（failure）两个术语，遵循最初由“基本概念与术语”联合委员会制定的标准定义；该委员会由 IEEE 计算机学会的可信计算与容错技术委员会和 IFIP 可信计算与容错第 10.4 工作组共同组建。另请参见可信性（dependability）。
Byzantine fault tolerance is only concerned with broadcast consistency, that is, the property that when a component broadcasts a value to all the other components, they all receive exactly this same value, or in the case that the broadcaster is not consistent, the other components agree on a common value themselves. This kind of fault tolerance does not encompass the correctness of the value itself; for example, an adversarial component that deliberately sends an incorrect value, but sends that same value consistently to all components, will not be caught in the Byzantine fault tolerance scheme.
拜占庭容错仅关注广播一致性，即以下性质：当一个组件向所有其他组件广播某个值时，其他组件接收到的都是完全相同的这个值；或者，当广播方的行为不一致时，其他组件自行就某个共同的值达成一致。这种容错并不涵盖值本身的正确性；例如，一个对抗性组件故意发送错误的值，但始终向所有组件发送相同的这个值，拜占庭容错方案就无法将其识别出来。
Solutions解决方案
Several early solutions were described by Lamport, Shostak, and Pease in 1982. They began by noting that the Generals' Problem can be reduced to solving a "Commander and Lieutenants" problem where loyal Lieutenants must all act in unison and that their action must correspond to what the Commander ordered in the case that the Commander is loyal:
Lamport、Shostak 和 Pease 在 1982 年描述了几种早期解决方案。他们首先指出，将军问题可以归约为一个“指挥官与副官”问题：所有忠诚的副官必须采取一致行动，而且在指挥官忠诚的情况下，他们的行动必须符合指挥官的命令：
One solution considers scenarios in which messages may be forged, but which will be Byzantine-fault-tolerant as long as the number of disloyal generals is less than one third of the generals. The impossibility of dealing with one-third or more traitors ultimately reduces to proving that the one Commander and two Lieutenants problem cannot be solved, if the Commander is traitorous. To see this, suppose we have a traitorous Commander A, and two Lieutenants, B and C: when A tells B to attack and C to retreat, and B and C send messages to each other, forwarding A's message, neither B nor C can figure out who is the traitor, since it is not necessarily A—the other Lieutenant could have forged the message purportedly from A. It can be shown that if n is the number of generals in total, and t is the number of traitors in that n, then there are solutions to the problem only when n > 3t and the communication is synchronous (bounded delay). The full set of BFT requirements are: For F number of Byzantine failures, there needs to be at least 3F+1 players (fault containment zones), 2F+1 independent communication paths, and F+1 rounds of communication. There can be hybrid fault models in which benign (non-Byzantine) faults as well as Byzantine faults may exist simultaneously. For each additional benign fault that must be tolerated, the above numbers need to be incremented by one. If the BFT rounds of communication do not exist, Byzantine failures can occur even with no faulty hardware.
一种解决方案考虑了消息可能被伪造的情形，只要不忠诚的将军人数少于将军总数的三分之一，该方案就能实现拜占庭容错。无法应对占总数三分之一或更多的叛徒，这一不可能性最终可归结为证明：如果指挥官是叛徒，那么由一名指挥官和两名副官组成的问题就无法解决。要理解这一点，假设有一名叛变的指挥官 A 和两名副官 B、C：当 A 命令 B 进攻、命令 C 撤退，而 B 和 C 相互发送消息、转发 A 的命令时，B 和 C 都无法判断谁是叛徒，因为叛徒未必是 A——另一名副官可能伪造了那条声称来自 A 的消息。可以证明，如果 n 是将军总数，t 是这 n 名将军中的叛徒人数，那么只有当 n > 3t 且通信是同步的（延迟有界）时，该问题才有解。BFT 的完整要求是：对于 F 个拜占庭失效，至少需要 3F+1 个参与方（故障遏制区）、2F+1 条独立通信路径以及 F+1 轮通信。也可以存在混合故障模型，其中良性（非拜占庭）故障与拜占庭故障可能同时存在。每多需要容忍一个良性故障，上述各个数量都需要增加一。如果缺少 BFT 所需的通信轮次，即使硬件没有故障，也可能发生拜占庭失效。
A second solution requires unforgeable message signatures. Digital signatures can provide Byzantine fault tolerance in the presence of an arbitrary number of traitorous generals provided that n > F+1 (the problem becomes vacuous otherwise). In particular, one Commander and two Lieutenants can reach consensus in the presence of a single traitor since the traitor cannot impersonate others. Solutions based on other cryptographic primitives such as Message Authentication Codes (MACs) were developed later.
第二种解决方案要求使用不可伪造的消息签名。只要 n > F+1，数字签名就能在存在任意数量叛变将军的情况下提供拜占庭容错（否则，该问题便失去了实际意义）。特别是，在存在一名叛徒的情况下，一名指挥官和两名副官也能达成共识，因为叛徒无法冒充其他人。后来又发展出了基于其他密码学原语的解决方案，例如基于消息认证码（Message Authentication Codes，MACs）的方案。
Also presented is a variation on the first two solutions allowing Byzantine-fault-tolerant behavior in some situations where not all generals can communicate directly with each other.
文中还提出了前两种解决方案的一种变体，使得在并非所有将军都能彼此直接通信的某些情况下，也能实现拜占庭容错。
There are many systems that claim BFT without meeting the above minimum requirements (e.g., blockchain). Given that there is mathematical proof that this is impossible, these claims need to include a caveat that their definition of BFT strays from the original. That is, systems such as blockchain don't guarantee agreement. They use resource-intensive mechanisms that make disagreements impractical to maintain.
许多系统（例如区块链）声称具备拜占庭容错（BFT）能力，却不满足上述最低要求。鉴于已有数学证明表明这是不可能的，这些声明需要附加说明，指出其对 BFT 的定义偏离了原始定义。也就是说，区块链等系统并不保证达成一致。它们采用资源密集型机制，使持续维持分歧在实践中不可行。
Several system architectures were designed c. 1980 that implemented Byzantine fault tolerance. These include: Draper's FTMP, Honeywell's MMFCS, and SRI's SIFT.
若干系统架构设计于约1980 年，实现了拜占庭容错。其中包括：Draper 的 FTMP、Honeywell 的 MMFCS 和 SRI 的 SIFT。
In 1999, Miguel Castro and Barbara Liskov introduced the "Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance" (PBFT) algorithm, which provides high-performance Byzantine state machine replication, processing thousands of requests per second with sub-millisecond increases in latency.
1999 年，Miguel Castro 和 Barbara Liskov 提出了“实用拜占庭容错”（PBFT）算法，该算法提供高性能的拜占庭容错状态机复制，每秒可处理数千个请求，而延迟增幅低于一毫秒。
After PBFT, several BFT protocols were introduced to improve its robustness and performance. For instance, Q/U, HQ, Zyzzyva, and ABsTRACTs, addressed the performance and cost issues; whereas other protocols, like Aardvark and RBFT, addressed its robustness issues. Furthermore, Adapt tried to make use of existing BFT protocols, through switching between them in an adaptive way, to improve system robustness and performance as the underlying conditions change. Furthermore, BFT protocols were introduced that leverage trusted components to reduce the number of replicas, e.g., A2M-PBFT-EA and MinBFT.
在 PBFT 之后，人们提出了若干 BFT 协议，以提高其鲁棒性和性能。例如，Q/U、HQ、Zyzzyva 和 ABsTRACTs 解决了性能和成本问题；而 Aardvark 和 RBFT 等其他协议则解决了其鲁棒性问题。此外，Adapt 尝试利用现有的 BFT 协议，通过在这些协议之间进行自适应切换，在底层条件变化时提高系统的鲁棒性和性能。此外，人们还提出了利用可信组件来减少副本数量的 BFT 协议，例如 A2M-PBFT-EA 和 MinBFT。

Applications应用
Several examples of Byzantine failures that have occurred are given in two equivalent journal papers. These and other examples are described on the NASA DASHlink web pages.
两篇内容等价的期刊论文给出了若干实际发生过的拜占庭故障实例。NASA DASHlink 网页描述了这些实例及其他实例。
Applications in computing计算领域的应用
Byzantine fault tolerance mechanisms use components that repeat an incoming message (or just its signature, which can be reduced to just a single bit of information if self-checking pairs are used for nodes) to other recipients of that incoming message. All these mechanisms make the assumption that the act of repeating a message blocks the propagation of Byzantine symptoms. For systems that have a high degree of safety or security criticality, these assumptions must be proven to be true to an acceptable level of fault coverage. When providing proof through testing, one difficulty is creating a sufficiently wide range of signals with Byzantine symptoms. Such testing will likely require specialized fault injectors.
拜占庭容错机制使用一些组件，将收到的消息（或仅将其签名；如果节点采用自检对，则可将其缩减为仅一个比特的信息）转发给该消息的其他接收者。所有这些机制都假设，转发消息这一行为能够阻断拜占庭故障症状的传播。对于安全性或安保性至关重要的系统，必须在可接受的故障覆盖水平上证明这些假设成立。通过测试提供证明时，一个难点在于生成足够广泛的、带有拜占庭故障症状的信号。此类测试很可能需要专用的故障注入器。
Military applications军事应用
Byzantine errors were observed infrequently and at irregular points during endurance testing for the newly constructed Virginia-class submarine, at least through 2005 (when the issues were publicly reported).

Cryptocurrency applications加密货币应用
The Bitcoin network works in parallel to generate a blockchain with proof of work allowing the system to overcome Byzantine failures and reach a coherent global view of the system's state. Some proof of stake blockchains also use BFT algorithms.
比特币网络以并行方式运行，通过工作量证明生成区块链，使系统能够克服拜占庭故障，并对系统状态形成一致的全局视图。一些采用权益证明的区块链也使用 BFT 算法。
Blockchain区块链
Byzantine fault tolerance is a crucial concept in blockchain technology, ensuring that a network can continue to function even when some nodes (participants) fail or act maliciously. This tolerance is necessary because blockchains are decentralized systems with no central authority, making it essential to achieve consensus among nodes, even if some try to disrupt the process.
拜占庭容错是区块链技术中的一个关键概念，它确保网络即使在部分节点（参与者）发生故障或存在恶意行为时，也能继续运行。这种容错能力是必要的，因为区块链是没有中央权威的去中心化系统，因此即使某些节点试图干扰共识过程，也必须在节点之间达成共识。
Artificial Intelligence人工智能
Ensuring that an AI system behaves reliably and as intended, especially in the presence of unexpected faults or adversarial conditions, is a complex challenge. By accepting that components may fail, and further that frontier AI models may deceive, Byzantine fault tolerance has been proposed as an approach towards AI safety. Structuring AI systems as ensembles of AI artifacts or modules that check and balance each other leads to strong assurances that no single errant or deceptive component can easily steer the system into an unsafe state.
确保 AI 系统可靠地按预期运行，尤其是在出现意外故障或对抗性条件时，是一项复杂的挑战。在承认组件可能发生故障，并进一步承认前沿 AI 模型可能实施欺骗的前提下，拜占庭容错（Byzantine fault tolerance）已被提出作为实现 AI 安全的一种方法。将 AI 系统构建为由相互制衡的 AI 构件或模块组成的集成体，可以提供有力保证，使任何单个出错或具有欺骗性的组件都难以轻易将系统引向不安全状态。
Applications and examples应用与示例
Safety mechanisms
安全机制
Different blockchains use various BFT-based consensus mechanisms like Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance (PBFT), Tendermint, and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) to handle Byzantine faults. These protocols ensure that the majority of honest nodes can agree on the next block in the chain, securing the network against attacks and preventing double-spending and other types of fraud. Practical examples of networks include Hyperledger Fabric, Cosmos and Klever in this sequence.
不同的区块链采用各种基于 BFT 的共识机制来处理拜占庭故障，例如实用拜占庭容错（Practical Byzantine Fault Tolerance，PBFT）、Tendermint 和委托权益证明（Delegated Proof of Stake，DPoS）。这些协议确保占多数的诚实节点能够就链中的下一个区块达成一致，从而保护网络免受攻击，并防止双重支付及其他类型的欺诈。依次对应的实际网络示例包括 Hyperledger Fabric、Cosmos 和 Klever。
51% attack mitigation
51% 攻击的缓解
While traditional blockchains like Bitcoin use proof of work (PoW), which is susceptible to a 51% attack, BFT-based systems are designed to tolerate up to one-third of faulty or malicious nodes without compromising the network's integrity.
Bitcoin 等传统区块链采用容易受到 51% 攻击的工作量证明（proof of work，PoW），而基于 BFT 的系统则旨在容忍至多三分之一的节点发生故障或具有恶意，同时不损害网络的完整性。
Decentralized trust
去中心化信任
Byzantine fault tolerance underpins the trust model in decentralized networks. Instead of relying on a central authority, the network's security depends on the ability of honest nodes to outnumber and outmaneuver malicious ones.
拜占庭容错是去中心化网络中信任模型的基础。网络的安全性不依赖于中央权威机构，而取决于诚实节点在数量上超过恶意节点并在应对策略上胜过它们的能力。
Private and permissioned blockchains
私有区块链与许可型区块链
BFT is especially important in private or permissioned blockchains, where a limited number of known participants need to reach a consensus quickly and securely. These networks often use BFT protocols to enhance performance and security.
BFT 在私有区块链或许可型区块链中尤为重要，因为这类区块链中数量有限、身份已知的参与者需要快速且安全地达成共识。这些网络通常采用 BFT 协议来提升性能和安全性。
In aviation在航空领域
Some aircraft systems, such as the Boeing 777 Aircraft Information Management System (via its ARINC 659 SAFEbus network), the Boeing 777 flight control system, and the Boeing 787 flight control systems, use Byzantine fault tolerance; because these are real-time systems, their Byzantine fault tolerance solutions must have very low latency. For example, SAFEbus can achieve Byzantine fault tolerance within the order of a microsecond of added latency.
某些飞机系统采用拜占庭容错，例如 Boeing 777 飞机信息管理系统（通过其 ARINC 659 SAFEbus 网络）、Boeing 777 飞行控制系统以及 Boeing 787 飞行控制系统；由于这些系统属于实时系统，其拜占庭容错解决方案必须具有极低的延迟。例如，SAFEbus 能够以微秒量级的附加延迟实现拜占庭容错。
In space systems在航天系统中
The SpaceX Dragon considers Byzantine fault tolerance in its design. The Ares rocket avionics, now the Space Launch System (SLS), utilizes a triplex with an interstage of accomplish Byzantine resilience. The Orion spacecraft utilizes self-checking pairs to detect and mask Byzantine faults.
SpaceX Dragon 在设计中考虑了拜占庭容错。Ares 火箭的航空电子系统，即如今的 Space Launch System（SLS）所用系统，采用带有级间结构的三重冗余配置来实现拜占庭故障韧性。Orion 航天器利用自检配对来检测并屏蔽拜占庭故障。
In democracy and civil societies在民主制度与公民社会中
In legislatures, civil societies, polls, statistical surveys, and so forth, systems that require a minimum supermajority threshold agreement greater than two-thirds are similar to consensus reflective of Byzantine Fault Tolerant -like characteristics. By contrast, systems that operate on the sub-supermajority basis of simple majorities or a minority government could be vulnerable to Byzantine fault -like behavior.
在立法机构、公民社会、民意测验、统计调查等领域，要求达到超过三分之二这一最低特定多数门槛才能达成一致的系统，类似于体现拜占庭容错特征的共识机制。相比之下，基于低于特定多数门槛的简单多数或少数派政府运行的系统，可能容易受到类似拜占庭故障的行为的影响。
