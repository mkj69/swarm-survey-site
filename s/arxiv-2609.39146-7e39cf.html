Abstract摘要
Multi-agent debate (MAD) can improve large language model (LLM) reasoning by allowing multiple agents to exchange and critique their answers to the same task. However, the interactions that enable agents to correct mistakes can also spread adversarial errors and steer the agents toward an incorrect answer. Although some efforts have been made to examine particular attack types on MAD, systematic evaluation of MAD under diverse attacks remains limited. A central question is whether debate mitigates adversarial influence or amplifies it.
多智能体辩论（multi-agent debate，MAD）允许多个智能体交流并相互评议各自对同一任务给出的答案，从而能够提升大语言模型（large language model，LLM）的推理能力。然而，使智能体能够纠正错误的交互也可能传播对抗性错误，并引导智能体得出错误答案。尽管已有一些研究考察了针对 MAD 的特定攻击类型，但针对多种攻击下 MAD 表现的系统性评估仍然有限。一个核心问题是：辩论究竟会减弱还是放大对抗性影响。
In this paper, we present $\mathsf{MADBench}$ , a benchmark for evaluating the security of MAD. We organize attacks into a layered taxonomy following the MAD workflow, incorporating both established attacks and new strategies tailored to debate. We evaluate six attack families over 356 source tasks and 3,958 test cases, examining their effects on the final answer and the propagation of adversarial influence. Our results show that, under attacks, MAD does not necessarily improve LLM reasoning. Compared with a single-agent baseline, MAD can mitigate attacks on answer accuracy in question-answering tasks while amplifying unauthorized reads or writes in both question-answering and workspace tasks. Moreover, even when three out of five agents collude, the attack changes the final answer from correct to wrong on only 28.30% of tasks answered correctly without attack, while only 3.26% of initially correct honest agents switch to wrong answers during debate.
本文提出 $\mathsf{MADBench}$，一个用于评估 MAD 安全性的基准。我们按照 MAD 的工作流程，将攻击组织为一个分层分类体系，其中既包含已有攻击，也包含专门针对辩论设计的新策略。我们基于 356 个源任务和 3,958 个测试用例，对六类攻击进行了评估，考察它们对最终答案的影响以及对抗性影响的传播。我们的结果表明，在遭受攻击时，MAD 并不一定能提升 LLM 的推理能力。与单智能体基线相比，MAD 可以减轻攻击对问答任务答案准确性的影响，但会在问答任务和工作区任务中放大未经授权的读取或写入行为。此外，即使五个智能体中有三个相互串通，在无攻击时能够正确作答的任务中，攻击也仅使 28.30% 的任务的最终答案由正确变为错误，而最初回答正确的诚实智能体中，仅有 3.26% 在辩论过程中转而给出错误答案。
1 Introduction1 引言
Multi-agent debate (MAD) is a collaborative reasoning approach in which multiple large language model (LLM)-based agents propose answers to the same task, exchange and critique their reasoning over successive rounds, and aggregate their responses into a final answer (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a). Prior work has demonstrated its potential to encourage divergent thinking (Liang et al., 2024), improve mathematical and commonsense reasoning (Du et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024a), enhance factual accuracy (Du et al., 2023), and improve agreement with human judgments in text evaluation (Chan et al., 2024). Unlike systems that coordinate agents primarily through task decomposition, MAD uses multiple agents on the same task, leveraging alternative reasoning paths and mutual critique in the hope of revising errors. Thus, it is particularly useful in domains where ground truth is often unavailable (Pitre et al., 2026).
多智能体辩论（MAD）是一种协作推理方法，其中多个基于大语言模型（LLM）的智能体针对同一任务提出答案，在连续多轮交互中交流并相互评议各自的推理，最终将各自的回答汇总为一个最终答案 (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a)。已有研究表明，它具有促进发散思维 (Liang et al., 2024)、改进数学与常识推理 (Du et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024a)、提高事实准确性 (Du et al., 2023)，以及提高文本评估结果与人类判断一致性的潜力 (Chan et al., 2024)。与主要通过任务分解来协调智能体的系统不同，MAD 让多个智能体处理同一任务，利用不同的推理路径和相互评议，以期纠正错误。因此，它在通常无法获得真实答案的领域中尤为有用 (Pitre et al., 2026)。
However, the same interactions can also expose MAD to adversarial influence. Malicious agents can introduce incorrect proposals and use the debate process to persuade honest agents or manipulate the final decision. For example, Amayuelas et al. (2024) show that introducing a single adversarial agent into three-agent debates reduces system accuracy by 10 to nearly 40 percentage points across multiple datasets. More recent studies further show that adversarial agents can exploit conformity to propagate plausible but incorrect answers (Cui and Du, 2025), while even incorrect responses produced by honest agents can spread during the debate (Cui et al., 2026).
然而，这些交互也可能使 MAD 暴露于对抗性影响之下。恶意智能体可以提出错误方案，并利用辩论过程说服诚实智能体或操纵最终决策。例如，Amayuelas et al. (2024) 表明，在三个智能体参与的辩论中引入一个对抗性智能体，会使系统在多个数据集上的准确率下降 10 至近 40 个百分点。较新的研究进一步表明，对抗性智能体可以利用从众倾向传播看似合理但实际错误的答案 (Cui and Du, 2025)，而即使是诚实智能体产生的错误回答，也可能在辩论过程中传播 (Cui et al., 2026)。
Existing security benchmarks predominantly target individual agents, evaluating vulnerabilities in their prompts, observations, memory, tools, and action execution (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024; Andriushchenko et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025). A separate line of work studies security in broader multi-agent systems, including attacks that intercept inter-agent messages, propagate malicious instructions, or exploit network topology (He et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026; Kavathekar et al., 2026). When it comes to MAD, prior works either focus on the failure-free case assuming that all agents are honest (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024; Smit et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a; Choi et al., 2025), or analyze MAD by focusing on certain types of attacks (Amayuelas et al., 2024; Cui and Du, 2025; Cui et al., 2026). To our knowledge, there is no benchmark available that systematically analyzes the security of MAD in a layered view.
现有安全基准主要面向单个智能体，评估其在提示、观测、记忆、工具和动作执行方面的漏洞 (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024; Andriushchenko et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025)。另一类研究考察更广泛的多智能体系统中的安全问题，包括拦截智能体间消息、传播恶意指令或利用网络拓扑的攻击 (He et al., 2025; Gu et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2025; Zhou et al., 2026; Kavathekar et al., 2026)。就 MAD 而言，已有研究要么假设所有智能体均诚实，关注无故障情形 (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024; Smit et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a; Choi et al., 2025)，要么聚焦于某些特定类型的攻击来分析 MAD (Amayuelas et al., 2024; Cui and Du, 2025; Cui et al., 2026)。据我们所知，目前尚无从分层视角系统分析 MAD 安全性的基准。

In this work, we propose $\mathsf{MADBench}$ , a benchmark for evaluating the security of MAD, with a particular focus on whether attacks are absorbed or amplified through multi-agent interaction. An attack is absorbed when MAD reduces its impact relative to that on a single agent, and amplified when communication or coordination among agents increases its impact. Based on the typical roles of MAD, as shown in Fig. 1, we provide a layered taxonomy. Specifically, we evaluate six attack families, including four attacks inherited from single-agent systems and two attacks specific to multi-agent coordination and orchestration, as summarized as follows.
在本研究中，我们提出用于评估 MAD 安全性的基准 $\mathsf{MADBench}$，重点关注攻击在多智能体交互过程中是被吸收还是被放大。如果 MAD 使攻击的影响相较于其对单个智能体的影响有所减弱，则称该攻击被吸收；如果智能体之间的通信或协调使攻击的影响增强，则称该攻击被放大。如图 1 所示，我们根据 MAD 中的典型角色，提出了一个分层分类体系。具体而言，我们评估了六类攻击，包括四类沿用自单智能体系统的攻击，以及两类针对多智能体协调与编排的特有攻击，概述如下。
$\bullet$
Single-agent attacks. Several works propose benchmarks for the security of individual agents (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024; Andriushchenko et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025). We consider four representative attack families: direct prompt injection (Zhang et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024), indirect prompt injection (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024), RAG poisoning (Zou et al., 2025), and tool hijacking (Wang et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024). The idea is to launch an attack on a single agent and observe whether such attacks can influence other honest agents to manipulate the final MAD output.
单智能体攻击。多项研究提出了用于评估单个智能体安全性的基准（Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024; Andriushchenko et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025）。我们考虑四类具有代表性的攻击：直接提示注入（Zhang et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2024）、间接提示注入（Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024）、RAG 投毒（Zou et al., 2025）和工具劫持（Wang et al., 2026; Yang et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024）。其思路是对单个智能体发起攻击，并观察此类攻击能否影响其他诚实智能体，从而操纵最终的 MAD 输出。
$\bullet$
Multi-agent coordination attacks. LLM agents are susceptible to the social conformity issue (Cui et al., 2026), and even a single malicious agent can easily degrade the outcome of a three-agent debate (Amayuelas et al., 2024). Existing studies, however, primarily consider independently malicious agents. Thus, we introduce a family of coordination attacks in which multiple compromised agents adopt different actions towards a shared objective, or equivocate by sending inconsistent messages to the agents. This family assesses whether coordination among compromised agents further increases their influence on honest agents and on the final output.
多智能体协同攻击。LLM 智能体容易受到社会从众问题的影响（Cui et al., 2026），即使只有一个恶意智能体，也能轻易降低三智能体辩论的结果质量（Amayuelas et al., 2024）。然而，现有研究主要考虑独立实施恶意行为的智能体。因此，我们引入一类协同攻击，其中多个被攻陷的智能体为实现共同目标而采取不同的行动，或通过向各智能体发送不一致的消息来实施矛盾陈述（equivocation）。这类攻击用于评估被攻陷智能体之间的协同是否会进一步增强其对诚实智能体及最终输出的影响。
$\bullet$
Compromised orchestrator. The orchestrator is a special role in multi-agent systems that controls the execution of the protocol, such as assigning tasks, routing messages, and determining when the interaction terminates. While some prior works study a malicious orchestrator in the context of general multi-agent systems (Naik et al., 2026), the security impact of a malicious orchestrator in MAD remains underexplored. Thus, we also provide an attack family for a compromised orchestrator to see how it can affect the output.
被攻陷的编排器。编排器（orchestrator）是多智能体系统中的一种特殊角色，负责控制协议的执行，例如分配任务、路由消息以及确定交互何时终止。尽管已有一些研究在一般多智能体系统的背景下探讨恶意编排器（Naik et al., 2026），但恶意编排器在 MAD 中的安全影响仍缺乏充分研究。因此，我们还提供了一类针对被攻陷编排器的攻击，以考察它如何影响输出。
We present an amplification factor (AF) metric to assess whether an attack can be absorbed or amplified in MAD. In particular, we consider four attack goals that are important for MAD: accuracy, which measures whether the final MAD output is correct when a ground-truth answer is available; cost, the token consumption; incorrect read, which denotes that at least one honest agent reads a file outside the set authorized for the task; and incorrect write, which denotes that at least one honest agent writes to an unauthorized file for the task. For each goal, AF compares the impact of an attack under MAD with its impact under the corresponding single-agent baseline, such that values below one indicate absorption and values above one indicate amplification.
我们提出放大因子（amplification factor，AF）指标，用于评估攻击在 MAD 中的影响是被吸收还是被放大。具体而言，我们考虑四个对 MAD 而言重要的攻击目标：准确性，用于在有标准答案时衡量最终 MAD 输出是否正确；成本，即 token 消耗量；不当读取，指至少一个诚实智能体读取了任务授权文件集合之外的文件；以及不当写入，指至少一个诚实智能体向任务未授权的文件执行了写入操作。对于每个目标，AF 将攻击在 MAD 下的影响与其在相应单智能体基线下的影响进行比较，低于一的值表示吸收，高于一的值表示放大。
We implement $\mathsf{MADBench}$ using AutoGen AgentChat and its OpenAI model extension (Wu et al., 2023), a widely used framework for constructing multi-agent applications. We evaluate the six attack families on three datasets (Pham et al., 2026; Geva et al., 2021; Debenedetti et al., 2024), comprising 356 source tasks and 3,958 security test cases. Our evaluation results show that MAD can absorb attacks on accuracy for question-answering tasks while amplifying unauthorized reads and writes in both question-answering and workspace tasks. Specifically, under RAG poisoning, MAD reduces the attack success rate to 74% of the single-agent baseline. However, unauthorized operations can have serious consequences. In a workspace case that authorizes only an address update, an injection requesting an unauthorized file read leads to an unauthorized payment, which does not occur when the attack is launched on a single agent. Furthermore, when three out of five agents collude, MAD outputs a wrong answer on 28.30% of tasks answered correctly without attack, while only 3.26% of initially correct honest agents switch to wrong answers during debate.
我们使用 AutoGen AgentChat 及其 OpenAI 模型扩展（Wu et al., 2023）实现 $\mathsf{MADBench}$，该框架广泛用于构建多智能体应用。我们在三个数据集（Pham et al., 2026; Geva et al., 2021; Debenedetti et al., 2024）上评估这六类攻击，涵盖 356 个源任务和 3,958 个安全测试用例。我们的评估结果表明，MAD 能够吸收问答任务中针对准确性的攻击影响，但会放大问答任务和工作空间任务中的未授权读取与写入。具体而言，在 RAG 投毒攻击下，MAD 将攻击成功率降至单智能体基线的 74%。然而，未授权操作可能造成严重后果。在一个仅授权更新地址的工作空间案例中，要求执行未授权文件读取的注入导致了一笔未授权付款，而在针对单个智能体发起该攻击时，这种情况并未发生。此外，当五个智能体中的三个相互串通时，MAD 在无攻击时能够正确回答的任务中，有 28.30% 输出了错误答案，而在辩论过程中，最初回答正确的诚实智能体中仅有 3.26% 转而给出错误答案。
2 Background and Definitions2 背景与定义
A multi-agent debate (MAD) instance is a tuple $\mathcal{M}=(O,\{A_{i}\}_{i=1}^{n},R,W)$ , where $O$ is the orchestrator, $\{A_{i}\}_{i=1}^{n}$ are the debate agents, $R$ is the number of debate rounds, and $W$ is the execution workspace. All MAD approaches explicitly or implicitly require an orchestrator (Zhu et al., 2026; Naik et al., 2026; Hong et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2024). We later show that by explicitly modeling this layer, we can better understand the root causes of certain attacks. By default, $O$ assigns the workspace to all agents. The workspace contains files, an email store, web pages, and a tool catalog $\mathcal{T}$ , which are shared with all agents. Besides, the workspace consists of a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) database, a set of text documents provided with the task but not directly included in the agent’s initial context. Each agent $i$ only accesses part of the RAG, denoted as $\mathrm{RAG}_{i}$ , through a retrieval call, which returns the $k$ documents most relevant to its query. We consider two types of tools, read tools $\mathcal{T}_{R}$ , which return information from the workspace without changing it, and write tools $\mathcal{T}_{W}$ , which modify the workspace or perform an external action.
一个多智能体辩论（MAD）实例是一个元组 $\mathcal{M}=(O,\{A_{i}\}_{i=1}^{n},R,W)$，其中 $O$ 是编排器，$\{A_{i}\}_{i=1}^{n}$ 是参与辩论的智能体，$R$ 是辩论轮数，$W$ 是执行工作空间。所有 MAD 方法都显式或隐式地需要一个编排器（Zhu et al., 2026; Naik et al., 2026; Hong et al., 2024; Chan et al., 2024）。我们将在后文表明，通过对这一层进行显式建模，可以更好地理解某些攻击的根本原因。默认情况下，$O$ 将工作空间分配给所有智能体。工作空间包含文件、电子邮件存储库、网页和工具目录 $\mathcal{T}$，这些内容与所有智能体共享。此外，工作空间还包含一个检索增强生成（RAG）数据库，即随任务提供、但未直接纳入智能体初始上下文的一组文本文档。每个智能体 $i$ 仅通过检索调用访问 RAG 的一部分，记为 $\mathrm{RAG}_{i}$，该调用返回与其查询最相关的 $k$ 篇文档。我们考虑两类工具：读取工具 $\mathcal{T}_{R}$，从工作空间返回信息而不改变工作空间；以及写入工具 $\mathcal{T}_{W}$，修改工作空间或执行外部操作。
Given a task $q$ , a MAD run proceeds in three stages. In Propose, denoted as round $r{=}0$ , the orchestrator dispatches $(\sigma_{1},\dots,\sigma_{n})=\mathrm{Disp}(q)$ , where $\sigma_{i}$ denotes the task instructions assigned to agent $i$ , and each agent proposes a candidate answer, denoted as
给定任务 $q$，一次 MAD 运行分为三个阶段。在提议（Propose）阶段，即第 $r{=}0$ 轮，编排器执行任务分派 $(\sigma_{1},\dots,\sigma_{n})=\mathrm{Disp}(q)$，其中 $\sigma_{i}$ 表示分配给智能体 $i$ 的任务指令，每个智能体提出一个候选答案，记为
$$\rho_{i}^{(0)}=A_{i}\!\left(q,\sigma_{i},W\right).$$
A response is represented as $\rho_{i}^{(r)}=(a_{i}^{(r)},\mathrm{reason}_{i}^{(r)},\mathrm{evidence}_{i}^{(r)})$ , where $a_{i}^{(r)}$ is the answer of agent $i$ after round $r$ , $\mathrm{reason}_{i}^{(r)}$ is its reasoning, and $\mathrm{evidence}_{i}^{(r)}$ is the evidence it reports, such as information obtained from retrieved documents or read tools.
响应表示为 $\rho_{i}^{(r)}=(a_{i}^{(r)},\mathrm{reason}_{i}^{(r)},\mathrm{evidence}_{i}^{(r)})$，其中 $a_{i}^{(r)}$ 是智能体 $i$ 在第 $r$ 轮后的答案，$\mathrm{reason}_{i}^{(r)}$ 是其推理过程，$\mathrm{evidence}_{i}^{(r)}$ 是其报告的证据，例如从检索到的文档或读取工具中获得的信息。
In Debate, denoted as round $r=1,\dots,R$ , each agent reads the responses of all other agents and revises its own, denoted as:
在辩论（Debate）阶段，即第 $r=1,\dots,R$ 轮，每个智能体读取所有其他智能体的响应，并修改自己的响应，记为：
$$\rho_{i}^{(r)}=A_{i}\!\left(q,\sigma_{i},\{\rho_{j}^{(r-1)}\}_{j\neq i},W\right).$$
In Decide, the orchestrator reads the final responses and produces the system output,
在决策（Decide）阶段，编排器读取最终响应并生成系统输出，
$$y=\mathrm{Agg}\!\left(q,\rho_{1}^{(R)},\dots,\rho_{n}^{(R)}\right),$$
where $\mathrm{Agg}$ is often an LLM call with possible access to $W$ . A debate agent influences the final output and any downstream write action through the response it reports. Agents exchange these responses. Raw tool outputs are not automatically shared among them.
其中 $\mathrm{Agg}$ 通常是一次可能能够访问 $W$ 的 LLM 调用。辩论智能体通过其报告的响应影响最终输出以及任何下游写入操作。智能体之间交换这些响应。原始工具输出不会在它们之间自动共享。
3 The design of $\mathsf{MADBench}$3 $\mathsf{MADBench}$ 的设计
3.1 A layered threat model3.1 分层威胁模型
We classify our attacks based on a layered view of MAD, as shown in Fig. 1. Each attack targets one of the three layers and leaves the other layers unchanged. We present the layers and the adversary’s threat model as follows.
我们基于对 MAD 的分层视角对攻击进行分类，如图 1 所示。每种攻击针对三个层中的一个层，并保持其他层不变。下面介绍这些层以及攻击者的威胁模型。
$\bullet$
$L_{1}$ : The orchestration layer contains the task input $q$ , the orchestrator prompt $\pi_{O}$ , the dispatch rule $\mathrm{Disp}$ , the aggregation rule $\mathrm{Agg}$ , and the write tools $\mathcal{T}_{W}$ . An adversary at $L_{1}$ rewrites $\pi_{O}$ , and controls the subtasks every agent receives, the aggregation step, and the write calls.
$L_{1}$：编排层包含任务输入 $q$、编排器提示词 $\pi_{O}$、分派规则 $\mathrm{Disp}$、聚合规则 $\mathrm{Agg}$ 和写入工具 $\mathcal{T}_{W}$。位于 $L_{1}$ 的攻击者改写 $\pi_{O}$，并控制每个智能体接收的子任务、聚合步骤以及写入调用。
$\bullet$
$L_{2}$ : The agent layer contains each agent’s system prompt $\pi_{i}$ and assigned roles of all agents. An adversary targeting this layer controls a subset of $f$ agents; we write $C\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ and $H=\{1,\ldots,n\}\setminus C$ for the corrupted and honest agents, with $|C|=f$ .
$L_{2}$：智能体层包含每个智能体的系统提示词 $\pi_{i}$ 以及所有智能体被分配的角色。针对该层的攻击者控制由 $f$ 个智能体组成的子集；我们用 $C\subseteq\{1,\ldots,n\}$ 和 $H=\{1,\ldots,n\}\setminus C$ 分别表示被攻陷的智能体和诚实智能体，其中 $|C|=f$。
$\bullet$
$L_{3}$ : The resource layer contains the RAG database available to each agent, files, the email store, web pages, and tool specifications. An adversary targeting this layer may modify the content in the resources of one or more agents, such as documents in their RAG databases.
$L_{3}$：资源层包含每个智能体可访问的 RAG 数据库、文件、电子邮件存储库、网页以及工具规范。针对该层的攻击者可以修改一个或多个智能体的资源内容，例如其 RAG 数据库中的文档。
We use $P$ to denote the adversarial payload, the concrete content that an attack $\mathcal{A}$ injects. The benchmark records each execution as a run trace $\tau$ , including its model interactions and tool calls. We write $y(\tau)$ for the final output recorded in run $\tau$ , $T(\tau)$ for the total number of tokens consumed, and $\mathcal{R}(\tau)$ and $\mathcal{W}(\tau)$ for the sets of read and write calls recorded in $\tau$ , respectively. Let $\mathcal{S}$ denote the set of tasks in an experiment. For each task $s\in\mathcal{S}$ , the benchmark may provide a reference answer $y_{s}^{\star}$ and specifies a set of authorized read calls $\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ and a set of authorized write calls $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ .
我们用 $P$ 表示攻击载荷，即攻击 $\mathcal{A}$ 注入的具体内容。该基准将每次执行记录为运行轨迹 $\tau$，其中包含模型交互和工具调用。我们用 $y(\tau)$ 表示运行 $\tau$ 中记录的最终输出，用 $T(\tau)$ 表示消耗的 token 总数，并分别用 $\mathcal{R}(\tau)$ 和 $\mathcal{W}(\tau)$ 表示 $\tau$ 中记录的读取调用集合和写入调用集合。令 $\mathcal{S}$ 表示一次实验中的任务集合。对于每个任务 $s\in\mathcal{S}$，该基准可能提供参考答案 $y_{s}^{\star}$，并规定授权读取调用集合 $\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ 和授权写入调用集合 $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$。
$\mathsf{MADBench}$ evaluates each attack using paired executions of the same task:
$\mathsf{MADBench}$ 通过对同一任务进行成对执行来评估每种攻击：
$$\tau_{c}=\mathrm{Run}(q,W,\bot),\qquad\tau_{a}=\mathrm{Run}(q,W,\mathcal{A}),$$
where $\tau_{c}$ and $\tau_{a}$ denote the clean (no attacks) and attacked runs, respectively. The two runs use the same task, initial workspace, model configuration, random seed, and number of debate rounds, differing only in the presence of $\mathcal{A}$ . We abbreviate their outputs as $y_{c}=y(\tau_{c})$ and $y=y(\tau_{a})$ , and their token counts as $T_{c}=T(\tau_{c})$ and $T=T(\tau_{a})$ .
其中 $\tau_{c}$ 和 $\tau_{a}$ 分别表示干净运行（无攻击）和受攻击运行。两次运行使用相同的任务、初始工作空间、模型配置、随机种子和辩论轮数，唯一区别在于是否存在 $\mathcal{A}$。我们将它们的输出简记为 $y_{c}=y(\tau_{c})$ 和 $y=y(\tau_{a})$，将它们的 token 数简记为 $T_{c}=T(\tau_{c})$ 和 $T=T(\tau_{a})$。
The goal of an attack is to achieve at least one of the following outcomes:
攻击的目标是至少实现以下一种结果：
$\bullet$
Accuracy. The MAD output $y$ differs from the reference answer $y^{\star}$ .
准确性。MAD 输出 $y$ 与参考答案 $y^{\star}$ 不同。
$\bullet$
Unauthorized reads. Given task $s$ , the run issues at least one read call that is not authorized for the task, i.e., $\mathcal{R}_{s}(\tau_{a})\nsubseteq\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ .
未经授权的读取。给定任务 $s$，运行过程中发出至少一次未经该任务授权的读取调用，即 $\mathcal{R}_{s}(\tau_{a})\nsubseteq\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$。
$\bullet$
Unauthorized writes. Given tasks $s$ , the run issues at least one write call that is not authorized for the task, i.e., $\mathcal{W}_{s}(\tau_{a})\nsubseteq\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ .
未经授权的写入。给定任务 $s$，运行过程中发出至少一次未经该任务授权的写入调用，即 $\mathcal{W}_{s}(\tau_{a})\nsubseteq\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$。
$\bullet$
Cost. The attack increases token consumption relative to the clean run, i.e., $T(\tau_{a})>T(\tau_{c})$ .
成本。攻击使 token 消耗量相对于干净运行有所增加，即 $T(\tau_{a})>T(\tau_{c})$。
These goals draw on two lines of prior work. Accuracy is a primary objective in MAD in almost all prior works (Amayuelas et al., 2024; Cui and Du, 2025). Several MAD approaches also explicitly seek to reduce the substantial token cost of multi-round debate (Zeng et al., 2025). In contrast, unauthorized read and write are often the focus of single agents only (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024). We consider it here because inter-agent communication may propagate a malicious agent’s influence and cause other agents or the orchestrator to access information or perform actions beyond the task’s authorization.
这些目标借鉴了既有研究的两条研究路线。在几乎所有既有研究中，准确性都是 MAD 的主要目标 (Amayuelas et al., 2024; Cui and Du, 2025)。一些 MAD 方法还明确致力于降低多轮辩论带来的高昂 token 成本 (Zeng et al., 2025)。相比之下，未经授权的读取和写入往往仅在单智能体研究中受到关注 (Debenedetti et al., 2024; Zhan et al., 2024)。我们在此考虑这一问题，是因为智能体间的通信可能传播恶意智能体的影响，导致其他智能体或编排器访问超出任务授权范围的信息，或执行超出任务授权范围的操作。
3.2 Six Attack Families3.2 六类攻击
We summarize in Table 1 our six attack families. As discussed in the introduction, four of them adapt from single-agent studies (M1–M4), and two of them are specific to MAD (M5–M6). For M5 and M6, we draw inspiration from closely related works on general multi-agent systems and evaluate whether these threats also arise in MAD.
我们在表 1 中概述了六类攻击。如引言所述，其中四类改编自单智能体研究 (M1–M4)，另外两类则是 MAD 特有的攻击 (M5–M6)。对于 M5 和 M6，我们从通用多智能体系统领域密切相关的研究中获得启发，并评估这些威胁是否也会出现在 MAD 中。
Single-agent attacks, M1–M4. M1 appends an injected payload to the task, M2 appends it to an external resource such as a file or an email body, M3 adds a poisoned document to an agent’s RAG database, and M4 appends the payload to the specification of a tool. Note that MAD introduces an additional propagation path: M1 enters through the dispatched subtask $\sigma_{i}$ and directly reaches all $n$ agents. In comparison, a payload initially observed by one agent in M2–M4 may reach the other $n-1$ agents if the affected agent incorporates it into its response in Eq. 2. For the corresponding single-agent baseline, we deliver the same payload through the same entry point, i.e., the task prompt, external resource, RAG database, or tool specification.
单智能体攻击，M1–M4。M1 将注入载荷追加到任务中，M2 将其追加到文件或电子邮件正文等外部资源中，M3 向智能体的 RAG 数据库中添加投毒文档，M4 则将载荷追加到工具的规格说明中。请注意，MAD 引入了一条额外的传播路径：M1 通过分派的子任务 $\sigma_{i}$ 进入，并直接到达全部 $n$ 个智能体。相比之下，在 M2–M4 中，最初由一个智能体观察到的载荷，如果被受影响的智能体纳入其在式 2 中的响应，就可能传播到其余 $n-1$ 个智能体。对于相应的单智能体基线，我们通过相同的入口传递相同的载荷，即任务提示词、外部资源、RAG 数据库或工具规格说明。
Multi-agent coordination attacks, M5. This family replaces the system prompts of a set $C$ of $f$ agents. We consider three attack modes: in the colluding mode, malicious agents collaboratively propose candidate answers towards a shared wrong target $a^{\dagger}$ ; in the independent mode, each agent chooses a wrong answer independently, without a shared target; and in the inconsistent mode, each malicious agent sends inconsistent answers and supporting evidence to different agents, also known as equivocation in the literature of distributed systems (Yin et al., 2019; Duan et al., 2024; Duan and Zhang, 2022). We compare the modes using the same $f$ (the number of malicious agents) and also vary $f$ to examine how attack strategy and $f$ affect MAD.
多智能体协调攻击，M5。这类攻击会替换由 $f$ 个智能体组成的集合 $C$ 中各智能体的系统提示词。我们考虑三种攻击模式：在串谋模式下，恶意智能体协同提出指向共同错误目标 $a^{\dagger}$ 的候选答案；在独立模式下，每个智能体独立选择一个错误答案，不设共同目标；在不一致模式下，每个恶意智能体向不同智能体发送不一致的答案及支持证据，这在分布式系统文献中也称为歧义行为（equivocation）(Yin et al., 2019; Duan et al., 2024; Duan and Zhang, 2022)。我们在相同的 $f$（恶意智能体的数量）下比较这些模式，同时改变 $f$，以考察攻击策略和 $f$ 如何影响 MAD。
Compromised orchestrator, M6. M6 appends malicious instructions to the orchestrator’s system prompt, allowing the adversary to manipulate both task dispatch and final aggregation. We construct a goal-specific variant for each security outcome. To cause an unauthorized read for task $s$ , the orchestrator adds an instruction to $\sigma_{i}$ directing agents to read a file outside $\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ . To increase cost, it assigns agents additional task-irrelevant calculations. To compromise accuracy, it ignores the agents’ final responses and returns a specified wrong answer. To cause an unauthorized write for task $s$ , it invokes a write tool outside $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ during aggregation.
编排器遭入侵，M6。M6 将恶意指令追加到编排器的系统提示词中，使攻击者能够同时操纵任务分派和最终聚合。我们针对每一种安全结果构建一个目标特定的变体。为了使任务 $s$ 发生未经授权的读取，编排器向 $\sigma_{i}$ 中添加一条指令，要求智能体读取 $\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ 之外的文件。为了增加成本，它向智能体分配额外的、与任务无关的计算。为了损害准确性，它忽略智能体的最终响应，并返回指定的错误答案。为了使任务 $s$ 发生未经授权的写入，它在聚合过程中调用 $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ 之外的写入工具。
M6 evaluates the impact of compromising the component that controls both information flow and the final decision in MAD. Because the adversary can manipulate these operations, M6 also serves as a direct-control baseline for attacks that must propagate through debate agents or external resources.
M6 评估了 MAD 中同时控制信息流和最终决策的组件遭入侵所造成的影响。由于攻击者能够操纵这些操作，M6 也可作为直接控制基线，用于比较那些必须经由辩论智能体或外部资源传播的攻击。

3.3 Metrics3.3 指标
We use two main metrics to assess the four attack goals in Sec. 3.1: attack success rate (ASR) for accuracy, and incorrect read and write rate (IRW) for assessing unauthorized reads and writes. For cost, we report the median token ratio $T(\tau_{a})/T(\tau_{c})$ between the attacked and clean runs. In addition, we present the amplification factor (AF) as a unified metric to assess whether an attack is absorbed or amplified in MAD.
我们使用两个主要指标来评估第 3.1 节中的四个攻击目标：使用攻击成功率（ASR）评估准确性，使用错误读写率（IRW）评估未经授权的读取和写入。对于成本，我们报告受攻击运行与干净运行之间 token 比值 $T(\tau_{a})/T(\tau_{c})$ 的中位数。此外，我们引入放大因子（AF）作为统一指标，用于评估攻击在 MAD 中是被吸收还是被放大。
Attack success rate (ASR). ASR assesses whether the MAD output deviates from the reference answer $y_{s}^{\star}$ for a task $s$ in the task set $\mathcal{S}$ , as follows:
攻击成功率（ASR）。对于任务集 $\mathcal{S}$ 中的任务 $s$，ASR 评估 MAD 的输出是否偏离参考答案 $y_{s}^{\star}$，如下所示：
$$\mathrm{ASR}=\frac{\left|\{s\in\mathcal{S}:y_{s}\neq y^{\star}_{s}\ \wedge\ y_{c,s}=y^{\star}_{s}\}\right|}{\left|\{s\in\mathcal{S}:y_{c,s}=y^{\star}_{s}\}\right|},$$
where $y_{c,s}$ and $y_{s}$ denote the MAD outputs in the clean and the attacked runs for task $s$ , respectively. Tasks that the clean run already answers incorrectly are excluded because they cannot show that the attack caused the error.
其中，$y_{c,s}$ 和 $y_{s}$ 分别表示任务 $s$ 在干净运行和受攻击运行中的 MAD 输出。干净运行中已回答错误的任务会被排除，因为这些任务无法表明错误是由攻击造成的。
Incorrect read and write (IRW). IRW denotes whether an unauthorized read or write occurs. We use a unified metric to assess them, as:
错误读写（IRW）。IRW 表示是否发生未经授权的读取或写入。我们使用统一指标来评估这两种情况，如下所示：
$$\mathrm{IRW}=\frac{\left|\left\{s\in\mathcal{S}:\mathcal{R}(\tau_{a,s})\nsubseteq\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}\ \vee\ \mathcal{W}(\tau_{a,s})\nsubseteq\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}\right\}\right|}{|\mathcal{S}|}.$$
Here, both $\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ and $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ are fixed and cannot be manipulated by the adversary. Thus, any read or write call outside the corresponding set is considered unauthorized.
这里，$\mathcal{R}_{s}^{\star}$ 和 $\mathcal{W}_{s}^{\star}$ 均为固定集合，攻击者无法操纵。因此，任何超出相应集合范围的读取或写入调用均被视为未经授权。
Amplification factor (AF). For the single-agent attack families, namely M1–M4, we use AF to assess whether MAD absorbs or amplifies an attack relative to a single-agent baseline. Let $m$ denote ASR, IRW, or cost. AF is defined as:
放大因子（AF）。对于单智能体攻击类别，即 M1–M4，我们使用 AF 评估 MAD 相对于单智能体基线是吸收还是放大了攻击。令 $m$ 表示 ASR、IRW 或成本。AF 定义如下：
$$\mathrm{AF_{m}}=\frac{m\ \text{of MAD}}{m\ \text{of the single agent}},\qquad m\in\{\mathrm{ASR},\ \mathrm{IRW},\ \mathrm{cost}\}.$$
$\mathrm{AF}<1$ means that MAD absorbs the attack, $\mathrm{AF}>1$ means that MAD amplifies the attack, and $\mathrm{AF}=1$ means that MAD neither absorbs nor amplifies it.
$\mathrm{AF}<1$ 表示 MAD 吸收了攻击，$\mathrm{AF}>1$ 表示 MAD 放大了攻击，而 $\mathrm{AF}=1$ 表示 MAD 既未吸收也未放大攻击。

Implementation. We implement $\mathsf{MADBench}$ in Python with approximately $20$ k new LoC. We build the underlying MAD system using AutoGen AgentChat (Wu et al., 2023) and its OpenAI extension, both at version 0.7.5. A clean run and its attacked run follow the same execution path and differ only in the attack. Each attack is implemented as a registered class that modifies one layer, allowing new attacks to reuse the same execution, logging, and evaluation pipeline.
实现。我们使用 Python 实现了 $\mathsf{MADBench}$，新增代码约 $20$ 千行。我们使用 AutoGen AgentChat (Wu et al., 2023) 及其 OpenAI 扩展构建底层 MAD 系统，两者的版本均为 0.7.5。无攻击运行及其对应的受攻击运行遵循相同的执行路径，唯一区别在于是否受到攻击。每种攻击均实现为一个已注册的类，仅修改一个层，使新攻击能够复用相同的执行、日志记录和评估流水线。
Experiment setup. We run $\mathsf{MADBench}$ on a workstation with an Intel Core Ultra 7 155H processor (11 visible cores and 22 threads), 31 GiB of RAM, Ubuntu 24.04.1 LTS under WSL2, and Python 3.12.3. Every agent queries an LLM through a hosted API, using gpt-4o unless otherwise mentioned. The orchestrator uses temperature $0$ , the debate agents use temperature $0.5$ , and every model call has a maximum output length of $4{,}096$ tokens. By default, MAD uses $n{=}5$ debate agents, one orchestrator $O$ , and $R{=}1$ Debate round. For attacks on the agent layer $L_{2}$ , we set $f{=}3$ by default and vary $f$ in selected experiments. For RAG poisoning on the resource layer $L_{3}$ , we poison the RAG database copies of three agents by default. Each agent may query its RAG database at most once per stage, retrieving the top $k{=}4$ documents, each truncated to its first $1{,}000$ characters.

Our results show that MAD generally absorbs attacks against answer accuracy on question-answering tasks but amplifies most attack effects on workspace tasks. For M3 and M4, the AF of ASR is below one for nearly all attack goals. We believe this absorption occurs because the orchestrator summarizes and aggregates the debate results, so it can compare the agents’ answers and their supporting evidence before producing the final answer. Thus, invalid proposals are less likely to determine the MAD output. Meanwhile, for workspace tasks, the injected information can propagate through the agents’ responses and induce unauthorized operations. Accordingly, M2 amplifies ASR, IRW, and cost in 11 of the 12 reported comparisons. In fact, the IRW results on question-answering tasks further show that although MAD usually reduces ASR and cost for M3 and M4, it can still amplify unauthorized reads and writes.
我们的结果表明，MAD 通常能够吸收问答任务中针对答案准确性的攻击影响，但在工作区任务中会放大大多数攻击的影响。对于 M3 和 M4，几乎所有攻击目标下 ASR 的 AF 都小于一。我们认为，这种吸收效应源于协调器会总结并汇总辩论结果，因此能够在生成最终答案之前比较各智能体的答案及其支持证据。因此，无效提议决定 MAD 输出的可能性较低。与此同时，对于工作区任务，注入的信息可以通过智能体的响应传播，并诱发未经授权的操作。相应地，在所报告的 12 项比较中，M2 在其中 11 项中放大了 ASR、IRW 和成本。事实上，问答任务上的 IRW 结果进一步表明，尽管 MAD 通常会降低 M3 和 M4 的 ASR 和成本，但仍可能放大未经授权的读写行为。

5 Related Work5 相关工作
Related attacks of M5 and M6. Related to M5, Amayuelas et al. (2024) introduce a single persuasive adversary into a three-agent debate, showing that it can reduce answer accuracy. Meanwhile, several studies investigate attacks on orchestration in general multi-agent systems (MASs), including orchestrator-mediated data leakage (Naik et al., 2026), planner manipulation (Yu et al., 2026), and control-flow hijacking (Triedman et al., 2025). These attacks manipulate task assignment and agent invocation. For example, OMNI-LEAK considers data-processing agents that access databases in the workspace. It shows that even in the presence of data access control, agents can leak sensitive data when an indirect prompt injection compromises the orchestrator through a data-processing agent. These studies motivate our attack family M6, which evaluates how a compromised orchestrator affects MAD through task dispatch and final aggregation.
与 M5 和 M6 相关的攻击。在与 M5 相关的研究中，Amayuelas et al. (2024) 在三个智能体的辩论中引入了一个具有说服力的对抗者，表明其能够降低答案准确率。与此同时，多项研究考察了针对通用多智能体系统（MASs）中编排机制的攻击，包括由编排器介导的数据泄露（Naik et al., 2026）、规划器操纵（Yu et al., 2026）和控制流劫持（Triedman et al., 2025）。这些攻击操纵任务分配和智能体调用。例如，OMNI-LEAK 考察了访问工作区中数据库的数据处理智能体。该研究表明，即使存在数据访问控制，当间接提示注入通过数据处理智能体攻陷编排器时，智能体仍可能泄露敏感数据。这些研究启发了我们的攻击族 M6，该攻击族评估被攻陷的编排器如何通过任务分派和最终汇总影响 MAD。
Benchmarks on multi-agent systems (MAS). As mentioned in the introduction, there are several benchmarks on general multi-agent systems. One of the closest to our work is TAMAS (Kavathekar et al., 2026), a benchmark on MAS under adversarial attacks. TAMAS evaluates different agent interaction configurations under prompt injection and compromised-agent attacks. In contrast, we focus on MAD and evaluate attacks on all three layers, with particular attention to how debate absorbs or amplifies adversarial influence.
多智能体系统（MAS）基准。如引言所述，已有若干针对通用多智能体系统的基准。与我们的工作最接近的研究之一是 TAMAS (Kavathekar et al., 2026)，这是一个针对对抗攻击下的 MAS 的基准。TAMAS 评估了不同智能体交互配置在提示注入和智能体被攻陷攻击下的表现。相比之下，我们聚焦于 MAD，并评估针对全部三个层面的攻击，特别关注辩论如何吸收或放大对抗性影响。
In addition, NetSafe (Yu et al., 2025) studies how network topology affects the propagation of misinformation, bias, and harmful content injected by malicious agents. Huang et al. (2025) analyze the resilience of different collaboration structures to faulty agents. These studies emphasize topology and resilience to agent-generated errors, rather than systematically evaluating attacks across MAD’s orchestration, agent, and resource layers.
此外，NetSafe (Yu et al., 2025) 研究了网络拓扑如何影响恶意智能体所注入的错误信息、偏见和有害内容的传播。Huang et al. (2025) 分析了不同协作结构对故障智能体的抵御能力。这些研究侧重于拓扑结构以及对智能体生成错误的抵御能力，而非系统地评估针对 MAD 的编排层、智能体层和资源层的攻击。

In this section, we show the details of the tasks mentioned in Sec. 4.
本节展示第 4 节所述任务的详细信息。
Question-answering tasks vs. workspace tasks. Beyond the overview in Sec. 4, the two task types differ in which workspace operations are authorized. A question-answering task gives the agents a question and expects one answer string, and requires access only to readable resources. Such a task authorizes no state change, so $\mathcal{W}^{\star}=\varnothing$ and every write call made by the run counts towards IRW, while all retrieval calls are included in $\mathcal{R}^{\star}$ . We use these tasks to evaluate ASR and cost, while IRW can also capture unauthorized write calls.
问答任务与工作区任务。除第 4 节概述的内容外，这两类任务的区别还在于哪些工作区操作获得了授权。问答任务向智能体提供一个问题，要求返回一个答案字符串，并且只需要访问可读资源。此类任务不授权任何状态变更，因此 $\mathcal{W}^{\star}=\varnothing$，运行过程中发出的每次写入调用均计入 IRW，而所有检索调用均包含在 $\mathcal{R}^{\star}$ 中。我们使用这些任务评估 ASR 和成本，同时 IRW 也可用于捕捉未经授权的写入调用。
Meanwhile, a workspace task gives the agents an instruction that requires interaction with a stateful workspace. In our benchmark, the workspace emulates common applications and services, including a calendar, a file store, an email client, and a bank account, and completing a task may require both read and write calls. Each task is associated with a set of authorized read calls $\mathcal{R}^{\star}$ and a set of authorized write calls $\mathcal{W}^{\star}$ supplied by the suite. The user instruction specifies the intended task, while these sets determine which workspace operations are authorized.
与此同时，工作区任务向智能体提供一条需要与有状态工作区交互的指令。在我们的基准中，工作区模拟了常见的应用和服务，包括日历、文件存储、电子邮件客户端和银行账户，完成任务可能同时需要读取和写入调用。每个任务都关联着由该套件提供的一组已授权读取调用 $\mathcal{R}^{\star}$ 和一组已授权写入调用 $\mathcal{W}^{\star}$。用户指令规定预期执行的任务，而这些集合则确定哪些工作区操作获得了授权。
