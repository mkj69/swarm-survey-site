《企业的性质》（1937）是罗纳德·科斯发表于经济学期刊《Economica》的一篇文章。 "The Nature of the Firm" (1937) is an article by Ronald Coase published in the economics journal Economica. 该文从经济学角度解释了为什么个人会选择组建合伙企业、公司及其他商业实体，而不是在市场上通过合同进行双边交易。 It offered an economic explanation of why individuals choose to form partnerships, companies, and other business entities rather than trading bilaterally through contracts on a market. 作者于1991年获颁诺贝尔经济学奖，部分原因就在于这篇论文。 The author was awarded the Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences in 1991 in part due to this paper. 尽管获得了这一殊荣，这篇论文却是科斯在本科期间撰写的，他晚年将其形容为“不过是一篇本科生论文而已”。 Despite the honor, the paper was written when Coase was an undergraduate and he described it later in life as "little more than an undergraduate essay."
该文认为，企业之所以出现，是因为它们比个人更有能力应对生产和交换中固有的交易成本。 The article argues that firms emerge because they are better equipped to deal with the transaction costs inherent in production and exchange than individuals are. 奥利弗·威廉姆森、道格拉斯·诺斯、奥利弗·哈特、本特·霍姆斯特伦、阿 Armen·阿尔钦和哈罗德·德姆塞茨等经济学家拓展了科斯关于企业、交易成本和合同的研究。 Economists such as Oliver Williamson, Douglass North, Oliver Hart, Bengt Holmström, Armen Alchian and Harold Demsetz expanded on Coase's work on firms, transaction costs and contracts. 经济学家和政治学家运用科斯研究中的见解来解释一般组织的运作，而不仅仅是企业的运作。 Economists and political scientists have used insights from Coase's work to explain the functioning of organizations in general, not just firms. 科斯的研究对新组织经济学（新制度经济学）产生了深远影响。 Coase's work strongly influenced the New Economics of Organization (New Institutional Economics).
科斯的文章区分了作为协调机制的市场与作为协调机制的企业。 Coase's article distinguished between markets as a coordination mechanism and firms as a coordination mechanism.
概要Summary
鉴于生产可以在没有任何组织的情况下进行，科斯问道：“为什么会出现企业，以及在什么条件下我们应当预期企业会出现？” Given that production could be carried on without any organization, Coase asks, 'Why and under what conditions should we expect firms to emerge?' 由于现代企业只有在某种企业家开始雇用人员时才会出现，科斯的分析便从考察以下条件入手：在什么条件下，企业家寻求雇员协助，而不是将某项特定任务外包，才是合理的。 Since modern firms can only emerge when an entrepreneur of some sort begins to hire people, Coase's analysis proceeds by considering the conditions under which it makes sense for an entrepreneur to seek hired help instead of contracting out for some particular task.
当时的传统经济理论认为，由于市场是“有效率的”（也就是说，最擅长以最低成本提供各种商品或服务的人已经在这样做了），因此外包的成本应当总是低于雇用人员的成本。 The traditional economic theory of the time suggested that, because the market is "efficient" (that is, those who are best at providing each good or service most cheaply are already doing so), it should always be cheaper to contract out than to hire.
然而，科斯指出，利用市场会产生多种交易成本；通过市场获取商品或服务的成本，实际上并不只是商品本身的价格。 Coase noted, however, that there are a number of transaction costs to using the market; the cost of obtaining a good or service via the market is actually more than just the price of the good. 其他成本，包括搜寻和信息成本、议价成本、保守商业秘密的成本，以及监督和执行成本，都可能增加通过市场采购某物的成本。 Other costs, including search and information costs, bargaining costs, keeping trade secrets, and policing and enforcement costs, can all potentially add to the cost of procuring something via the market. 这表明，当企业能够在内部安排生产所需之物，并以某种方式避免这些成本时，企业就会出现。 This suggests that firms will arise when they can arrange to produce what they need internally, and somehow avoid these costs.
然而，内部生产的范围存在自然界限。 There is a natural limit to what can be produced internally, however. 科斯注意到“企业家职能的收益递减”，其中包括间接费用不断增加，以及不堪重负的管理者在资源配置上犯错的可能性不断上升。 Coase notices "decreasing returns to the entrepreneur function", including increasing overhead costs and increasing propensity for an overwhelmed manager to make mistakes in resource allocation. 这是利用企业这一组织形式所产生的一项抵消性成本。 This is a countervailing cost to the use of the firm.
科斯认为，企业规模（以有多少合同关系处于企业“内部”、多少处于“外部”来衡量）取决于在上述各类成本相互竞争的变化趋势之间找到最优平衡。 Coase argues that the size of a firm (as measured by how many contractual relations are "internal" to the firm and how many "external") is a result of finding an optimal balance between the competing tendencies of the costs outlined above. 一般而言，扩大企业规模起初会带来好处，但上述收益递减最终会开始发挥作用，从而阻止企业无限扩张。 In general, making the firm larger will initially be advantageous, but the decreasing returns indicated above will eventually kick in, preventing the firm from growing indefinitely.
在其他条件不变（ceteris paribus）的情况下，满足以下条件时，企业的规模往往会更大： Other things being equal (ceteris paribus), a firm will tend to be larger:
组织成本越低，且随着所组织的交易增加，这些成本上升得越慢。 the less the costs of organizing and the slower these costs rise with an increase in the transactions organized.
企业家犯错的可能性越低，且随着所组织的交易增加，错误增加得越少。 the less likely the entrepreneur is to make mistakes and the smaller the increase in mistakes with an increase in the transactions organized.
向规模更大的企业供应生产要素时，其供给价格下降得越多（或上涨得越少）。 the greater the lowering (or the less the rise) in the supply price of factors of production to firms of larger size.
随着所组织的交易在空间上分布得更分散，以及交易之间的差异增大，前两项成本都会增加。 The first two costs will increase with the spatial distribution of the transactions organized and the dissimilarity of the transactions. 这解释了为什么企业往往要么分布在不同的地理位置，要么承担不同的职能。 This explains why firms tend to either be in different geographic locations or to perform different functions. 此外，能够降低跨空间组织交易成本的技术变革会促使企业规模扩大；例如，电话和廉价航空旅行的出现，预计会扩大企业规模。 Additionally, technology changes that mitigate the cost of organizing transactions across space will cause firms to be larger—the advent of the telephone and cheap air travel, for example, would be expected to increase the size of firms. 与此相关，互联网及相关现代信息与通信技术的使用，似乎促成了所谓虚拟组织的出现。 On a related note the use of the internet and related modern information and communication technologies seem to lead to the existence of so-called virtual organizations.
科斯没有考虑非合同关系，例如朋友或家人之间的关系。 Coase does not consider non-contractual relationships, as between friends or family.
反响Reactions
1991年，科斯获颁瑞典国家银行（瑞典中央银行）纪念阿尔弗雷德·诺贝尔经济学奖。 In 1991, Coase was awarded the Sveriges Riksbank (Bank of Sweden) Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel. 他的论文在阐明交易成本和产权对经济的制度结构及其运行的重要意义方面取得了突破。 His paper provided a breakthrough on the significance of transaction costs and property rights for the institutional structure and functioning of the economy.
这篇论文对微观经济学领域产生了极其深远的影响，尤其是它基本上开创了企业理论这一研究领域。 The paper has had an outsized impact on the field of microeconomics, particularly in essentially inventing the body of research that deals with the theory of the firm. 据Google Scholar统计，截至2024年9月，这篇论文已被引用超过59,000次。 According to Google Scholar, the paper has been cited more than 59,000 times as of September 2024.
Yochai Benkler 在其著名文章《科斯的企鹅，或 Linux 与企业的性质》中引用了这篇文章，将科斯的论文与利用互联网的基于公地的同伴生产（commons-based peer production）社群的兴起联系起来。 This article was famously referred by Yochai Benkler in his article "Coase's Penguin, or, Linux and The Nature of the Firm", where he links Coase's essay to the emergence of commons-based peer production communities using the Internet. 具体而言，Benkler 认为，基于公地的同伴生产是在市场与层级组织这一二分框架之外，协调经济交易的第三种可选机制。 In particular, Benkler considers the commons-based peer production a third alternative coordination mechanism for economic transactions besides the dichotomy composed of markets and hierarchies. 文章标题中的“企鹅”指的是 Linux 操作系统的标志，意在引出 Linux 对科斯研究提出的挑战：它通过不同于市场和企业中现有机制的方式运作。 In the article's title, ‘penguin’ refers to the logo of the Linux operating system, invoking the challenge it poses to Coase's work by working through different mechanisms than those present in markets and firms. Benkler 认为，解决这一挑战的关键在于，在解释基于公地的同伴生产的兴起时，用信息机会成本的概念取代交易成本在科斯研究中所起的作用。 Resolving this challenge, according to Benkler, lies in substituting the role of transaction costs in Coase's work with the concept of information opportunity costs when explaining the emergence of commons-based peer production.
世界银行以《工作性质的变化》为主题的 2019 年《世界发展报告》指出，随着技术降低了借助公开市场完成部分生产流程的成本，企业和生产流程的纵向一体化程度会随之降低。 The World Bank's 2019 World Development Report on The Changing Nature of Work suggests that firms and production processes become less vertically integrated as technology makes it cheaper to resort to the open market to complete portions of the production process.
