Abstract摘要
Multi-agent systems (MAS) decompose complex tasks and delegate subtasks to different large language model (LLM) agents and tools. Prior studies have reported the superior accuracy performance of MAS across diverse domains, enabled by long-horizon context tracking and error correction through role-specific agents. However, the design and deployment of MAS incur higher complexity and runtime cost compared to single-agent systems (SAS). Meanwhile, frontier LLMs, such as OpenAI-o3 and Gemini-2.5-Pro, have rapidly advanced in long-context reasoning, memory retention, and tool usage, mitigating many limitations that originally motivated MAS designs. In this paper, we conduct an extensive empirical study comparing MAS and SAS across various popular agentic applications. We find that the benefits of MAS over SAS diminish as LLM capabilities improve, and we propose efficient mechanisms to pinpoint the error-prone agent in MAS. Furthermore, the performance discrepancy between MAS and SAS motivates our design of a hybrid agentic paradigm, request cascading between MAS and SAS, to improve both efficiency and capability. Our design improves accuracy by 1.1-12% while reducing deployment costs by up to 20% across various agentic applications.
多智能体系统（MAS）将复杂任务分解，并将子任务委派给不同的大语言模型（LLM）智能体和工具。已有研究表明，通过由承担特定角色的智能体进行长程上下文跟踪和错误纠正，MAS 在多个领域展现出更高的准确率。然而，与单智能体系统（SAS）相比，MAS 的设计与部署具有更高的复杂度和运行成本。与此同时，OpenAI-o3 和 Gemini-2.5-Pro 等前沿 LLM 在长上下文推理、记忆保持和工具使用方面取得了快速进展，缓解了最初促使人们设计 MAS 的诸多局限。本文开展了一项广泛的实证研究，比较 MAS 与 SAS 在多种主流智能体应用中的表现。我们发现，随着 LLM 能力的提升，MAS 相较于 SAS 的优势逐渐减弱；我们还提出了高效机制，用于精准定位 MAS 中容易出错的智能体。此外，MAS 与 SAS 之间的性能差异促使我们设计了一种混合智能体范式，即在 MAS 与 SAS 之间进行请求级联，以同时提升效率与能力。在多种智能体应用中，我们的设计将准确率提高了 1.1-12%，同时将部署成本最多降低了 20%。
1 Introduction1 引言
In the face of growing complexity and interactivity in LLM-based applications, multi-agent systems (MAS) are gaining traction across a wide range of domains. In MAS architectures, complex tasks are decomposed and delegated to specialized agents—each instantiated as an LLM with a defined role—who coordinate through structured natural language exchanges [37, 4]. This paradigm is increasingly adopted in areas such as software engineering, where agent roles like product manager, architect, developer, and tester collaborate to transform high-level requirements into production-quality code [19, 32]; mathematical reasoning, where agents debate and refine proofs [13, 27]; and scientific discovery, including hypothesis generation, peer critique, and experiment design [15, 23].
面对基于 LLM 的应用日益增长的复杂性与交互性，多智能体系统（MAS）正受到众多领域的广泛关注。在 MAS 架构中，复杂任务被分解并委派给专门的智能体，每个智能体均由具有明确角色的 LLM 实例化，并通过结构化的自然语言交流进行协调 [37, 4]。这一范式正日益广泛地应用于多个领域，例如软件工程，其中产品经理、架构师、开发者和测试人员等智能体角色协同工作，将高层次需求转化为达到生产质量标准的代码 [19, 32]；数学推理，其中智能体通过辩论完善证明 [13, 27]；以及科学发现，包括假设生成、同行评议和实验设计 [15, 23]。
Prior studies have shown the superior accuracy performance of MAS over single-agent systems (SAS) in collaborative reasoning and human-aligned workflows [28]. By modularizing and distributing responsibilities across agents with dedicated working memory, external tool access, and the ability to critique peer outputs, MAS paradigms support long-horizon context and planning, iterative refinement, and error correction within and across agents.
已有研究表明，在协作推理和与人类需求对齐的工作流程中，MAS 的准确率优于单智能体系统（SAS）[28]。通过将职责模块化并分配给具有专用工作记忆、外部工具访问能力以及评判其他智能体输出能力的智能体，MAS 范式支持长程上下文处理与规划、迭代改进，以及智能体内部和智能体之间的错误纠正。
However, the design and deployment of MAS introduce significant complexity. Defining agent roles, capabilities, and coordination logic demands substantial engineering effort upfront, while runtime execution incurs overheads such as increased latency and token costs. Diagnosing failure modes in MAS becomes even more challenging, with recent work showing that MAS can severely degrade due to coordination breakdowns or misaligned agent behaviors [3]. Meanwhile, frontier LLMs—such as OpenAI-o3 and Gemini 2.5 Pro—are rapidly improving in long-context reasoning, memory, and tool usage, challenging many limitations that originally motivated MAS designs. This evolving landscape raises two pressing questions:
然而，MAS 的设计与部署引入了显著的复杂性。定义智能体的角色、能力和协调逻辑需要在前期投入大量工程工作，而运行时执行则会产生延迟增加和 token 成本上升等开销。诊断 MAS 的失效模式更加困难，近期研究表明，协调失灵或智能体行为不一致可能导致 MAS 的性能严重下降 [3]。与此同时，OpenAI-o3 和 Gemini 2.5 Pro 等前沿 LLM 在长上下文推理、记忆和工具使用方面正迅速进步，使最初促使人们设计 MAS 的诸多局限不再像以往那样成立。这一不断演变的形势提出了两个亟待回答的问题：
How does the MAS paradigm compare to the increasingly capable SAS paradigm? Moreover, how can we navigate the tradeoffs between output quality and cost-efficiency for the practical deployment of agentic systems?
与能力日益增强的 SAS 范式相比，MAS 范式表现如何？此外，在实际部署智能体系统时，我们应如何权衡输出质量与成本效益？

Node-Level Defect: The performance of both MAS and SAS are bottlenecked by a critical agent responsible for the most difficult subtask, capping MAS performance.
节点层面缺陷：MAS 与 SAS 的性能均受制于负责最困难子任务的关键智能体，这限制了 MAS 的性能上限。

路径级缺陷：不确定性错误可能沿智能体交互链传播，最终导致错误的最终输出。
However, identifying and mitigating these defects is challenging due to the variety of agentic applications. To address this, we propose a confidence-guided tracing method that considers both the confidence and output quality of agents to pinpoint critical agents—those that bottleneck overall MAS performance, whereby we prioritize augmenting the critical agents for better cost-effectiveness. In light of the performance discrepancy between SAS and MAS, we further introduce agent routing and agent cascade paradigms to selectively offload requests between SAS and MAS, thereby unlocking new accuracy-efficiency tradeoffs in agentic system deployments. Our evaluations show that our hybrid paradigm improves accuracy by 1.1-12% while reducing costs by up to 88.1%.
然而，由于智能体应用的多样性，识别和缓解这些缺陷颇具挑战。为此，我们提出一种置信度引导的追踪方法，同时考虑智能体的置信度和输出质量，以定位制约 MAS 整体性能的关键智能体，并优先增强这些关键智能体，从而提高成本效益。鉴于 SAS 与 MAS 之间的性能差异，我们进一步引入智能体路由和智能体级联范式，在 SAS 与 MAS 之间选择性地分配请求，从而为智能体系统部署提供新的准确率与效率权衡方式。评估结果表明，我们的混合范式将准确率提高了 1.1-12%，同时将成本最多降低了 88.1%。

2 Background and Related Work2 背景与相关工作
Multi-agent Applications.多智能体应用。
Multi-agent systems (MAS) are gaining traction in various domain-specific tasks, typically featuring specialized agents with role-specific prompts and behaviors. For instance, MetaGPT [19] introduces a meta-programming framework that formalizes human workflows into structured multi-agent collaborations to improve code generation quality. ChatDev [32] employs agents with distinct business roles (CEO, CTO, programmer, reviewer, tester), using structured chat chains to guide their interactions and employing communicative dehallucination to reduce coding errors. In scientific reasoning, [13] shows that collaborative agent debates can improve factual consistency. Other systems have demonstrated the utility of MAS in solving complex tasks across domains including mathematical reasoning [27], scientific discovery [15, 23], healthcare [7], travel planning [37], game development [4, 41], and financial trading [40].
多智能体系统（MAS）在各种领域特定任务中日益受到关注，通常包含具有角色专属提示和行为的专业化智能体。例如，MetaGPT [19] 引入了一种元编程框架，将人类工作流程形式化为结构化的多智能体协作，以提高代码生成质量。ChatDev [32] 采用具有不同业务角色（CEO、CTO、程序员、审查员、测试员）的智能体，使用结构化聊天链引导其交互，并通过交流式去幻觉（communicative dehallucination）减少编码错误。在科学推理领域，[13] 表明，智能体之间的协作辩论可以提高事实一致性。其他系统已证明，MAS 有助于解决多个领域的复杂任务，包括数学推理 [27]、科学发现 [15, 23]、医疗保健 [7]、旅行规划 [37]、游戏开发 [4, 41] 和金融交易 [40]。
Optimizing Agentic Systems.智能体系统优化。
Despite its promising performance, MAS often involves large design complexity and operational overhead. To support long-horizon context tracking, Agent Workflow Memory [38] introduces a memory module tailored for web navigation tasks. Similarly, StateFlow [39] improves task-solving performance by controlling and reasoning over internal agent states during execution. To automate the optimization of generative AI workflows, Cognify [16] proposes a hierarchical search strategy that adaptively tunes parameters to enhance output quality and efficiency. Ayo [34] optimizes end-to-end MAS performance by modeling agent workflows as primitive-level dataflow graphs for efficient resource scheduling. To diagnose failure modes in agentic workflows, [26] shows that embedding structured error-type relationships into chain-of-thought (CoT) and tree-of-thought (ToT) prompts can improve error classification in logic programming. Other studies [43, 3] explore the use of LLMs as judges to identify failure points and trace error attribution.
尽管 MAS 展现出良好的性能，但通常也伴随着较高的设计复杂度和运行开销。为支持长程上下文追踪，Agent Workflow Memory [38] 引入了一个专为网页导航任务设计的记忆模块。类似地，StateFlow [39] 通过在执行过程中控制智能体的内部状态并对其进行推理，提高任务求解性能。为实现生成式 AI 工作流优化的自动化，Cognify [16] 提出一种分层搜索策略，通过自适应调整参数来提高输出质量和效率。Ayo [34] 将智能体工作流建模为原语级数据流图，以实现高效的资源调度，从而优化 MAS 的端到端性能。为诊断智能体工作流中的失效模式，[26] 表明，将结构化的错误类型关系嵌入思维链（CoT）和思维树（ToT）提示，可以改善逻辑编程中的错误分类。其他研究 [43, 3] 探索了使用 LLM 作为评判者来识别失效点并追踪错误归因。
3 Multi-agent or Single-agent Systems?3 多智能体系统还是单智能体系统？
With the skyrocketing interest in MAS and the rapid advancement of LLM capabilities, it is pressing to reassess agentic system designs. We begin by formalizing the quality and efficiency requirements of agentic applications (§3.1). Next, we present a comprehensive performance study comparing MAS and SAS across a range of tasks (§3.2), followed by an analysis of the MAS defects (§3.3).
随着人们对 MAS 的兴趣急剧增长，以及 LLM 能力的快速提升，重新审视智能体系统设计已迫在眉睫。我们首先对智能体应用的质量和效率要求进行形式化描述（§3.1）。接着，我们开展全面的性能研究，比较 MAS 与 SAS 在一系列任务中的表现（§3.2），随后分析 MAS 的缺陷（§3.3）。
3.1 Problem Formulation.3.1 问题形式化。
We model the agentic execution flow as a directed graph, $G=(V,E)$ . Each node $v_{i}\in V$ , where $V=V_{llm}+V_{tool}$ , represents an entity such as an LLM agent or an external tool. A directed edge, $e_{i,j}=(v_{i},v_{j},w_{i,j})$ , denotes a dependency between nodes, where a message $w_{i,j}$ is generated by $v_{i}$ and consumed by $v_{j}$ with possible post processing. Then $e_{i,i}$ can represent self-reflection. Under this formulation, Single-Agent System (SAS) corresponds to $\{G(V,E):|V_{llm}|=1\}$ , and Multi-Agent System (MAS) corresponds to $\{G(V,E):|V_{llm}|>1\}$ .
我们将智能体执行流程建模为有向图 $G=(V,E)$。每个节点 $v_{i}\in V$ 表示一个实体，例如 LLM 智能体或外部工具，其中 $V=V_{llm}+V_{tool}$。有向边 $e_{i,j}=(v_{i},v_{j},w_{i,j})$ 表示节点之间的依赖关系，其中消息 $w_{i,j}$ 由 $v_{i}$ 生成，并由 $v_{j}$ 使用，期间可能经过后处理。因此，$e_{i,i}$ 可以表示自我反思。在这一形式化描述下，单智能体系统（SAS）对应于 $\{G(V,E):|V_{llm}|=1\}$，多智能体系统（MAS）对应于 $\{G(V,E):|V_{llm}|>1\}$。
Objectives.目标。
Practical agentic systems aim for high accuracy while also maintaining low deployment cost (e.g., monetary cost for API calls). Specifically, let a user request $r\in R$ be processed through the agentic execution graph $G$ , producing a final output $G(r)$ . We denote the task-specific evaluation function as $f$ , where $f(G(r))$ quantifies the output quality, which can be a binary $\{0,1\}$ (e.g., task success) or a continuous value (e.g. , accuracy score).
实际应用中的智能体系统力求在保持较低部署成本（例如 API 调用的货币成本）的同时实现高准确率。具体而言，设用户请求 $r\in R$ 通过智能体执行图 $G$ 进行处理，生成最终输出 $G(r)$。我们将任务特定的评估函数记为 $f$，其中 $f(G(r))$ 用于量化输出质量，其取值可以是二元值 $\{0,1\}$（例如任务是否成功），也可以是连续值（例如准确率得分）。
Similarly, we can define the cost of executing request $r$ as the cumulative cost of all messages:
类似地，我们可以将执行请求 $r$ 的成本定义为所有消息的累计成本：
$$C(r)=\sum_{e_{i,j}\in E}|w_{i,j}|\times(c_{out}(i)+c_{in}(j))$$
Here, $c_{out}$ and $c_{in}$ are the node-specific cost functions, such as the token cost in the input (prefilling) and output (decoding) stage of LLM agents, or a customized function for tools.
这里，$c_{out}$ 和 $c_{in}$ 是节点特定的成本函数，例如 LLM 智能体在输入（预填充）和输出（解码）阶段的 token 成本，或为工具定制的函数。
Then, the deployment objective of agentic systems is mostly to maximize $f(G(r))$ and/or minimize $C(r)$ for many requests, highlighting the need for cost-effective deployment [17].
因此，智能体系统的部署目标通常是针对大量请求最大化 $f(G(r))$ 和/或最小化 $C(r)$，这凸显了高性价比部署的必要性 [17]。

接下来，我们探究为何 MAS 的性能增益逐渐减小，甚至在某些情况下落后于 SAS。为此，我们开展了数据点级别的分析：对于数据集中的每个样本，我们比较 MAS 和 SAS 两种范式输出的正确性。具体而言，Both Pass 表示 MAS 和 SAS 均给出正确答案；Both Fail 表示两者均回答错误；MAS Win 表示 MAS 回答正确而 SAS 回答错误；SAS Win 的含义依此类推。

Node-Level Defect:节点级缺陷：
Our conclusion stems from Observation 1, where the majority of cases fall under Both Pass or Both Fail, suggesting that task decomposition in MAS often has limited impact: if the task is simple, SAS can solve it; if it is too difficult, MAS still fails. Observation 3 reinforces our conclusion, as the increase in Both Pass cases with stronger base models does not correspond to a notable rise in MAS Wins. This indicates that stronger models benefit both systems similarly, and MAS fails to yield a higher win rate than SAS under more capable LLMs.
我们的结论源于观察 1，其中大多数案例属于双方均通过（Both Pass）或双方均失败（Both Fail），这表明 MAS 中的任务分解往往作用有限：如果任务简单，SAS 就能解决；如果任务过于困难，MAS 仍然会失败。观察 3 进一步支持了我们的结论，因为随着基础模型能力增强，双方均通过的案例有所增加，但 MAS 获胜（MAS Wins）的案例并未相应显著增加。这表明，更强的模型给两种系统带来的收益相近，在使用能力更强的 LLMs 时，MAS 未能取得比 SAS 更高的胜率。
There are exceptions like AIME, which is considered the most difficult math dataset. As such, MAS is able to consistently outperform SAS on this dataset, illustrating its ability to solve extremely difficult tasks.
也存在例外，例如 AIME，它被认为是最困难的数学数据集。因此，MAS 能够在该数据集上持续优于 SAS，体现了其解决极其困难任务的能力。
Edge-Level Defect:边级缺陷：
In MAS, agents often engage in multi-way conversations or prolonged iterative refinements. It is common for certain nodes in the MAS execution graph to exhibit high in-degree, such as the summarizer in math debates or the response synthesizer in RAG-based documentation systems [25, 13]. However, the influx of information from upstream agents can overwhelm the receiving agent, leading to overthinking on edge cases and ultimately producing incorrect results.
在 MAS 中，智能体经常进行多方对话或长时间的迭代改进。MAS 执行图中的某些节点通常具有较高的入度，例如数学辩论中的总结智能体，或基于 RAG 的文档系统中的回答综合智能体 [25, 13]。然而，上游智能体涌入的信息可能使接收信息的智能体不堪重负，导致其对边界情况过度思考，最终产生错误结果。
We hypothesize that this contributes to Observation 2, where SAS consistently outperforms MAS on a non-trivial subset of examples. 我们推测，这促成了观察 2 中的现象，即 SAS 在相当一部分样例上始终优于 MAS。为了验证这一假设，我们手动开展了广泛的案例研究。例如，在使用 SelfCol. 的代码生成任务中。在 [6] 框架中，问题分析智能体和测试智能体引入了过多不必要的极端情况。这使编码智能体负担过重，导致任务失败。我们在附录中提供了更多示例。
Such a phenomenon is analogous to the overthinking of the reasoning model [8], but rather than being "lost" in thinking, the agent becomes overwhelmed by inputs from upstream agents. Though we might mitigate this defect by better prompt engineering or extended context lengths, the root cause lies in hallucination [33] and overthinking, which are intrinsic challenges of LLMs themselves. MAS aggravates the problem as agents process much more data.
这种现象类似于推理模型的过度思考 [8]，但智能体并非在思考中“迷失”，而是被来自上游智能体的输入压得不堪重负。尽管我们可以通过更好的提示工程或更长的上下文长度来缓解这一缺陷，但其根本原因在于幻觉 [33] 和过度思考，而这两者都是 LLMs 自身固有的挑战。由于智能体需要处理多得多的数据，MAS 加剧了这一问题。
Path-Level Defect:路径级缺陷：
As information flows between agents, crucial context can be lost or diluted [44], especially when intermediate outputs are summarized or filtered before being passed along (e.g., from multi-hops ago). Even a small piece of lost information can cause irreversible errors in downstream reasoning due to a snowball effect. In contrast, SAS has full access to its history, though still limited by context length. Emerging models with extended long-context capabilities [10, 29] help alleviate this issue.
随着信息在智能体之间流动，关键上下文可能丢失或被淡化 [44]，尤其是当中间输出在继续传递之前经过总结或筛选时（例如，来自多跳之前的信息）。由于滚雪球效应，即使丢失一小部分信息，也可能在下游推理中造成不可逆的错误。相比之下，SAS 可以完整访问其历史记录，尽管仍然受到上下文长度的限制。具备更长上下文处理能力的新兴模型 [10, 29] 有助于缓解这一问题。

我们推测，这一缺陷促成了观察 2 中的现象，即 SAS 在部分案例中始终优于 MAS，并开展了广泛的案例研究以验证这一假设。下面我们给出一个数学推理任务中的示例，表明先前某轮提出的正确解法，在传递给下一个智能体之前的总结过程中丢失了。这种丢失无法恢复，因为下游智能体已无法访问此前的完整结果。
Unlike decisive errors studied in previous literature [43], these propagation-induced errors are difficult to detect and backtrace, as it requires global knowledge to determine which information can be safely diluted and which must be retained. However, the design principle of MAS—the division of tasks—runs counter to this requirement.
与既有文献 [43] 研究的决定性错误不同，这些由传播引起的错误难以检测和追溯，因为判断哪些信息可以安全地淡化、哪些信息必须保留，需要掌握全局信息。然而，MAS 的设计原则——任务分工——与这一要求背道而驰。

While MAS still offers advantages such as better privacy [45], improved parallelism, and democratization [35], our extensive real-world evaluations reveal that MAS is not a one-size-fits-all solution for diverse tasks. In fact, it often suffers from cost-ineffectiveness. Consequently, naively converting a SAS solution into an MAS one may yield less accuracy improvement than expected, while incurring substantially higher costs.
尽管 MAS 仍具有更好的隐私保护 [45]、更高的并行性以及民主化 [35] 等优势，但我们广泛的真实场景评估表明，MAS 并非适用于各种任务的通用解决方案。事实上，它往往存在成本效益不佳的问题。因此，直接将 SAS 方案转换为 MAS 方案，所带来的准确率提升可能低于预期，同时却会产生高得多的成本。
4 Towards Cost-Effective Agentic Deployments4 迈向高成本效益的智能体部署
The intricate accuracy-cost tradeoff in MAS and SAS has led to substantial deployment challenges. We next explore the design space for automatically improving agentic systems. Specifically, we (i) identify and augment the critical execution path in MAS to enhance accuracy (§4.1), and (ii) introduce a hybrid SAS-MAS deployment paradigm for better accuracy and cost efficiency (§4.2).
MAS 和 SAS 中复杂的准确率与成本权衡带来了重大的部署挑战。接下来，我们探索自动改进智能体系统的设计空间。具体而言，我们 (i) 识别并增强 MAS 中的关键执行路径，以提高准确率（§4.1）；(ii) 引入 SAS-MAS 混合部署范式，以提高准确率和成本效率（§4.2）。
4.1 Augmenting MAS Critical Path.4.1 增强 MAS 关键路径。
Our studies (§3.3) reveal that MAS can suffer from node-, edge-, and path-level defects that limit accuracy. However, identifying these defects is challenging due to the diversity of tasks and the intricate interactions among agents. Verifying every intermediate message introduces exponential complexity and overhead as workflow depth increases. Moreover, most existing MAS frameworks invoke LLM agents through public APIs (e.g., OpenAI or Gemini) [20, 31, 13], which offer limited control over internal mechanisms such as KV cache management or fine-tuning, further constraining optimization opportunities. A strawman solution is to use the most advanced model for every agent, but this incurs excessive cost, as these models are significantly more expensive to run.
我们的研究（§3.3）表明，MAS 可能存在节点级、边级和路径级缺陷，从而限制准确率。然而，由于任务的多样性以及智能体之间复杂的交互，识别这些缺陷具有挑战性。随着工作流深度增加，验证每条中间消息会带来指数级增长的复杂度和开销。此外，大多数现有 MAS 框架通过公共 API（例如 OpenAI 或 Gemini）调用 LLM 智能体 [20, 31, 13]，而这些 API 对 KV 缓存管理或微调等内部机制的控制有限，进一步限制了优化空间。一种简单的初步方案是为每个智能体使用最先进的模型，但这会产生过高的成本，因为这些模型的运行费用要高得多。
As discussed in Section 3.3, one promising strategy is to selectively upgrade critical agents rather than uniformly upgrading all agents. However, this is nontrivial, as it requires systematically identifying error-prone nodes and edges in the execution graph—a challenge that remains open-ended. To address this, we propose a lightweight, automated probing mechanism to detect critical agents.
如第 3.3 节所述，一种有前景的策略是选择性地升级关键智能体，而非统一升级所有智能体。然而，这并非易事，因为它需要系统地识别执行图中容易出错的节点和边，而这一挑战仍有待解决。为此，我们提出了一种轻量级自动探测机制，用于检测关键智能体。
Figure 3: We propose a lightweight, confidence-guided probing method to identify critical agents for improvement (left), and further improve cost-effectiveness by integrating SAS and MAS paradigms.
图 3：我们提出了一种轻量级、置信度引导的探测方法，用于识别需要改进的关键智能体（左），并通过整合 SAS 和 MAS 范式进一步提高成本效益。
Confidence-Guided Critical Path Tracing.置信度引导的关键路径追踪。
Our key insight is to assign differentiated rewards to agents based on both their confidence and the quality of the final output. This enables us to prioritize the augmentation of agents that bottleneck successful outcomes. Consider the MATH-DEBATE framework [13], where agents propose solutions and a majority vote determines the final answer. If the final output is correct (or incorrect), each agent contributing to it receives a reward (or penalty).
我们的核心思路是根据智能体的置信度和最终输出的质量，为其分配差异化奖励。这使我们能够优先增强那些制约任务成功完成的智能体。以 MATH-DEBATE 框架 [13] 为例，智能体各自提出解答，再通过多数投票确定最终答案。如果最终输出正确（或错误），每个对该输出作出贡献的智能体都会获得奖励（或惩罚）。
However, this naive scheme treats all contributing agents equally, failing to account for their differing contribution levels. To address this, we incorporate confidence scores into the reward function. Each agent is prompted to self-report its confidence level on a scale from 1 (least confident) to 10 (most confident), formatted as “Agent Response [Confidence Level]”. Understandably, an agent that is both confident and correct should be rewarded more than one that is unsure or incorrect.
然而，这种朴素方案平等地对待所有作出贡献的智能体，未能考虑它们贡献程度的差异。为此，我们将置信度分数纳入奖励函数。我们通过提示要求每个智能体自行报告其置信度，评分范围为 1（置信度最低）到 10（置信度最高），格式为“智能体回复 [置信度等级]”。显然，一个既自信又正确的智能体，应当比一个不确定或错误的智能体获得更多奖励。
As shown in Figure 3, after completing a request $r\in R$ , we propagate the quality of its final output $Q_{r}$ , which is defined by a task-specific evaluation metric (e.g., $Q_{r}=1$ if the output is correct, and 0 otherwise). Together with the response confidence $c_{i}$ of agent $i$ , its reward score, $I_{i}$ , is updated as:
如图 3 所示，在完成请求 $r\in R$ 后，我们传播其最终输出的质量 $Q_{r}$，该质量由任务特定的评估指标定义（例如，若输出正确，则 $Q_{r}=1$，否则为 0）。结合智能体 $i$ 的回复置信度 $c_{i}$，其奖励分数 $I_{i}$ 更新如下：
$$I_{i}=\sum_{r\in R}Q_{r}\cdot c_{i}+(1-c_{i})(1-Q_{r}),\quad\text{where }Q_{r}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if final output correct}\\ 0&\text{if final output wrong}\end{cases}$$
Our confidence-based critical path tracing identifies the agent that presents the greatest reward bottleneck. To show the effectiveness of our design, we use node augmentation to boost the MAS performance in a cost-efficient way. Specifically, we identify the agent with the lowest reward score and upgrade its model to a more capable version (e.g., replacing Gemini-1.5-Flash with Gemini-2.0-Flash). This targeted enhancement reduces the likelihood of localized errors and strengthens the agent’s ability to solve previously unresolvable subtasks.
我们基于置信度的关键路径追踪方法能够识别在奖励方面构成最大瓶颈的智能体。为展示我们设计的有效性，我们采用节点增强，以具有成本效益的方式提升 MAS 的性能。具体而言，我们识别奖励分数最低的智能体，并将其模型升级为能力更强的版本（例如，将 Gemini-1.5-Flash 替换为 Gemini-2.0-Flash）。这种有针对性的增强降低了局部错误发生的可能性，并提升了该智能体解决此前无法解决的子任务的能力。
We use a math reasoning task (AIME) to demonstrate the effectiveness of our design. Here we assign three agents (solvers) with different model series, Llama series (Llama3.1-8b, Llama3.1-70b) for agent A, GPT series (GPT-3.5, GPT-4) for agent B, and Gemini series (Gemini-1.5-Flash, Gemini-2.0-Flash) for agent C. We performed two experiment setup: (i) Agent Upgrading, where the original agent pipeline consists of (Llama3.1-8b, GPT-3.5, Gemini-1.5-Flash) and we hope to identify which agent to upgrade to its premium model to maximize accuracy performance; and (ii) Agent Downgrading, where the original agent pipeline consists of (Llama3.1-70b, GPT-4, Gemini-2.0-Flash) and we hope to identify which agent to downgrade to its basic model to minimize accuracy drop. Our decision is optimal for both cases, bringing the most (least) performance gain (drop).
我们使用一项数学推理任务（AIME）来展示我们设计的有效性。这里，我们为三个智能体（求解器）分别配置不同系列的模型：智能体 A 使用 Llama 系列（Llama3.1-8b、Llama3.1-70b），智能体 B 使用 GPT 系列（GPT-3.5、GPT-4），智能体 C 使用 Gemini 系列（Gemini-1.5-Flash、Gemini-2.0-Flash）。我们采用了两种实验设置：(i) 智能体升级，原始智能体流水线由（Llama3.1-8b、GPT-3.5、Gemini-1.5-Flash）组成，我们希望确定应将哪个智能体升级到其高级模型，以最大化准确率；(ii) 智能体降级，原始智能体流水线由（Llama3.1-70b、GPT-4、Gemini-2.0-Flash）组成，我们希望确定应将哪个智能体降级到其基础模型，以尽可能减少准确率下降。图 4(a) 展示了逐一切换各模型后的准确率差异，结果表明，当预算仅允许切换一个智能体的模型时，我们的引导式追踪方法能够识别出最值得切换的智能体。我们的决策在这两种情况下均为最优，分别带来了最大的性能提升和最小的性能下降。

4.2 Integrating SAS-MAS Paradigms.4.2 整合 SAS-MAS 范式。
Augmenting the critical path makes MAS more suitable for complex problems that benefit from specialized roles. However, this still comes at deployment cost. In contrast, SAS tends to be more efficient and effective for simpler, well-scoped tasks. More subtly, investing more resources in MAS does not necessarily yield better performance than SAS (§3.2). As such, one would expect a hybrid paradigm that strategically combines MAS and SAS to leverage their respective design sweet spots.
增强关键路径使 MAS 更适合处理能够从专门化角色中获益的复杂问题。然而，这仍然会带来部署成本。相比之下，对于较简单且范围明确的任务，SAS 往往效率更高、效果更好。更微妙的是，向 MAS 投入更多资源，并不一定能使其性能优于 SAS（§3.2）。因此，可以预期，通过有策略地结合 MAS 和 SAS，混合范式能够发挥二者各自在设计上的优势。
However, integrating MAS and SAS paradigms is non-trivial due to the wide variability in task complexity and application domains. Moreover, a practical solution must be request-dependent and cost-efficient. To this end, we introduce two primary strategies: Agent Routing and Agent Cascade for different scenarios.
然而，由于任务复杂度和应用领域存在广泛差异，整合 MAS 和 SAS 范式并非易事。此外，实用的解决方案必须能够根据请求进行调整，并具备成本效益。为此，我们针对不同场景引入两种主要策略：智能体路由（Agent Routing）和智能体级联（Agent Cascade）。
Agent Routing.智能体路由。
The key idea behind agent routing is to use an LLM-based rater to assess the complexity of each request and decide whether to route it to an SAS or MAS system. This strategy is broadly applicable across diverse workloads. Here, the rater assigns a difficulty score to each incoming request. Based on a user-defined threshold, requests with scores above the threshold are routed to MAS, while those below are handled by SAS.
智能体路由的核心思想是使用基于 LLM 的评分器评估每个请求的复杂度，并决定将其路由至 SAS 系统还是 MAS 系统。这一策略广泛适用于各种工作负载。在此，评分器为每个传入请求分配一个难度分数。根据用户定义的阈值，分数高于阈值的请求被路由至 MAS，而分数低于阈值的请求则由 SAS 处理。
The routing design offers users flexibility to balance accuracy and cost. Suppose a proportion $p$ of requests are routed to MAS, and the remaining ( $1-p$ ) to SAS. Then, the total cost of agent routing can be defined as:
这种路由设计使用户能够灵活地权衡准确率与成本。假设有比例为 $p$ 的请求被路由至 MAS，其余（$1-p$）被路由至 SAS。那么，智能体路由的总成本可定义为：
$$C_{\text{Rater}}(r)+p\cdot C_{\text{MAS}}(r)+(1-p)\cdot C_{\text{SAS}}(r),$$
where $C(r)$ denotes the cost of processing request $r$ . The rater adds only minimal overhead because it relies on a system prompt that can be effectively cached and produces only a small, constant number of tokens representing the difficulty score. The accuracy of this routing approach depends on the rater’s capability and the choice of the threshold value.
其中，$C(r)$ 表示处理请求 $r$ 的成本。评分器仅引入极少的额外开销，因为它依赖于可被有效缓存的系统提示词，并且仅生成少量、数量固定的词元来表示难度分数。这种路由方法的准确率取决于评分器的能力以及阈值的选择。
Figure 4(b) shows the results on the math reasoning task (GSM8K + AIME dataset) with Gemini-2.0-Flash as the rater, achieving 2% better accuracy at 50% cost compared with MAS baseline. Note that we did not fine-tune a difficulty rating model to demonstrate the generalizability of our solution, so the reported performance can be viewed as a lower bound. Nonetheless, we observe that agent routing generally achieves higher accuracy than SAS and, with a properly selected threshold value (routing easy requests to SAS and hard requests to MAS), it can even outperform MAS (§3.3). Figure 4(c) also shows that the overhead introduced by the LLM rater is negligible.
图 4(b) 展示了以 Gemini-2.0-Flash 作为评分器时在数学推理任务（GSM8K + AIME 数据集）上的结果：与 MAS 基线相比，准确率提高了 2%，而成本仅为其 50%。请注意，为了展示我们方案的泛化能力，我们并未对难度评分模型进行微调，因此所报告的性能可视为一个下界。尽管如此，我们观察到，智能体路由通常能够达到比 SAS 更高的准确率，而且在恰当选择阈值的情况下（将简单请求路由至 SAS，将困难请求路由至 MAS），其表现甚至可以超过 MAS（§3.3）。图 4(c) 还表明，LLM 评分器引入的额外开销可以忽略不计。

In many tasks (e.g., exact math problems and code generation), the final output of agentic systems can be efficiently verified. This enables an agent cascade design that first routes the request to SAS to obtain an initial output. The output is then evaluated for its quality (e.g., correctness). If the result from SAS is satisfactory, it is returned directly; otherwise, the request is escalated to MAS.
在许多任务中（例如精确数学问题和代码生成），智能体系统的最终输出可以得到高效验证。这使得智能体级联设计成为可能：首先将请求路由至 SAS，以获得初始输出。随后，对输出的质量（例如正确性）进行评估。如果 SAS 的结果令人满意，则直接返回；否则，将请求升级至 MAS 处理。
Suppose a proportion $p$ of requests are passed through SAS, while the remaining ( $1-p$ ) requests have to be escalated to MAS, the cost of agent cascade can be defined as:
假设有比例为 $p$ 的请求经 SAS 处理后通过验证，而其余（$1-p$）请求必须升级至 MAS 处理，则智能体级联的成本可定义为：
$$(1-p)\cdot C_{\text{MAS}}(r)+C_{\text{SAS}}(r)$$
As shown in the formula, this cascade paradigm is justified as long as the cost of SAS is significantly lower than that of MAS, which has been confirmed in Table 3. This makes the overhead of a failed SAS attempt negligible. Conversely, a successful SAS attempt—the “short path”—yields substantial savings by avoiding the costly MAS.
如公式所示，只要 SAS 的成本显著低于 MAS，这种级联范式就是合理的，而表 3 已证实了这一点。这使得一次失败的 SAS 尝试所产生的额外开销可以忽略不计。相反，一次成功的 SAS 尝试，即“短路径”，能够通过避免使用成本高昂的 MAS 来大幅节省成本。
We evaluate our cascade paradigm across a diverse set of agentic applications. As shown in Table 4, agent cascade achieves consistent accuracy improvement, up to 12% over MAS and SAS, while lowering cost by 20%.
我们在多种智能体应用上评估了所提出的级联范式。如表 4 所示，智能体级联实现了稳定的准确率提升，相较于 MAS 和 SAS，提升幅度最高达 12%，同时将成本降低了 20%。
5 Conclusion5 结论
We present a comprehensive study comparing MAS and SAS across 15 tasks, finding that while MAS excels on complex tasks, SAS is often more efficient and equally effective on simpler ones. We attribute MAS underperformance to node-, edge-, and path-level defects, and introduce a confidence-guided tracing method to identify critical agents. To optimize cost-effectiveness, we propose a hybrid agentic paradigm that selectively offloads requests between MAS and SAS, achieving up to 12% better accuracy and 88.1% cost savings. Our findings offer actionable insights for deploying adaptive, cost-efficient agentic systems.
我们开展了一项全面研究，在 15 项任务上比较了 MAS 和 SAS，发现虽然 MAS 在复杂任务上表现出色，但在较简单的任务上，SAS 往往效率更高，且效果同样出色。我们将 MAS 表现不佳归因于节点、边和路径层面的缺陷，并引入了一种置信度引导的追踪方法来识别关键智能体。为优化成本效益，我们提出了一种混合智能体范式，在 MAS 和 SAS 之间选择性地分派请求，实现了最高 12% 的准确率提升和 88.1% 的成本节省。我们的研究结果为部署自适应且具备成本效益的智能体系统提供了可操作的见解。
Limitation: Although in this paper, we did extensive analysis across tasks, frameworks, and models, our experiments are mostly focused on agentic systems built upon general-purpose models, rather than fine-tuned models. In addition, this paper focuses on agentic systems for task solving, despite they are the most popular ones. We left those unexplored areas as future work.
局限性：尽管本文针对不同任务、框架和模型进行了广泛分析，但我们的实验主要关注基于通用模型构建的智能体系统，而非基于微调模型构建的系统。此外，本文聚焦于用于任务求解的智能体系统，尽管这类系统是最为流行的智能体系统。我们将这些尚未探索的领域留作未来工作。
Broader Impact: This paper challenges the prevailing assumption that MAS outperforms SAS in terms of accuracy. We identify that this misconception arises from fundamental limitations inherent to agentic frameworks. We further advocate for future research in multi-agent systems to place greater emphasis on deployment efficiency rather than accuracy alone.
更广泛的影响：本文对 MAS 在准确率方面优于 SAS 这一普遍假设提出了质疑。我们发现，这一误解源于智能体框架固有的根本性局限。我们进一步倡导，未来的多智能体系统研究应更加重视部署效率，而非仅关注准确率。
