Abstract摘要
Large-language models (LLMs) have demonstrated powerful problem-solving capabilities, in particular when organized in multi-agent systems. However, the advent of such systems also raises several questions on the ability of a complex network of agents to effectively self-organize and collaborate. While measuring performance on standard reasoning benchmarks indicates how well multi-agent systems can solve reasoning tasks, it is unclear whether these systems are able to leverage their topology effectively. Here, we propose AgentsNet, a new benchmark for multi-agent reasoning. By drawing inspiration from classical problems in distributed systems and graph theory, AgentsNet measures the ability of multi-agent systems to collaboratively form strategies for problem-solving, self-organization, and effective communication given a network topology. We evaluate a variety of baseline methods on AgentsNet including homogeneous networks of agents which first have to agree on basic protocols for organization and communication. We find that some frontier LLMs are already demonstrating strong performance for small networks but begin to fall off once the size of the network scales. While existing multi-agent benchmarks cover at most 2–5 agents, AgentsNet is practically unlimited in size and can scale with new generations of LLMs. As such, we also probe frontier models in a setup with up to 100 agents.
大语言模型（LLMs）已展现出强大的问题求解能力，尤其是在被组织成多智能体系统时。然而，此类系统的出现也引发了一系列问题，涉及复杂智能体网络能否有效地进行自组织与协作。虽然标准推理基准上的性能能够反映多智能体系统解决推理任务的能力，但这些系统能否有效利用其拓扑结构仍不明确。在此，我们提出 AgentsNet，一个用于多智能体推理的新基准。AgentsNet 从分布式系统和图论中的经典问题汲取灵感，衡量多智能体系统在给定网络拓扑下，协作制定问题求解、自组织和有效通信策略的能力。我们在 AgentsNet 上评估了多种基线方法，其中包括同质智能体网络，这些网络首先必须就组织和通信的基本协议达成一致。我们发现，一些前沿 LLM 在小规模网络中已表现出强大的性能，但随着网络规模扩大，其性能开始下降。现有多智能体基准最多仅涵盖 2–5 个智能体，而 AgentsNet 的规模几乎不受限制，并且能够随着新一代 LLM 的出现而扩展。因此，我们还在包含多达 100 个智能体的设置下测试了前沿模型。

1 Introduction1 引言
Human societies thrive on collaboration, with language serving as the primary medium through which individuals coordinate and achieve collective goals. From small teams to large-scale organizations, effective communication enables structured decision-making, problem-solving, and the emergence of complex behaviors that surpass the capabilities of any single individual. This interplay between communication and coordination is mirrored in computing, where distributed systems rely on structured information exchange to tackle problems that exceed the capacity of any single processor. Just as psychology studies individual cognition while sociology examines emergent behaviors in groups, distributed systems research focuses on multi-agent coordination beyond what a single machine can accomplish [23].
人类社会因协作而繁荣，语言则是个体进行协调并实现集体目标的主要媒介。从小型团队到大型组织，有效的沟通使结构化决策、问题求解以及超越任何单个个体能力的复杂行为的涌现成为可能。通信与协调之间的这种相互作用也体现在计算领域，分布式系统依靠结构化的信息交换来解决超出任何单个处理器能力的问题。正如心理学研究个体认知，而社会学考察群体中的涌现行为，分布式系统研究关注的是超越单台机器能力的多智能体协调 [23]。
Recently, distributed systems have been playing an increasingly important role in AI through the emergence of general-purpose multi-agent systems built on top of large language and vision models (LLMs). Agent-based frameworks such as generative agents [36] have demonstrated the potential of solving complex problems with LLM-based agents. In particular, it has been shown that networks of LLM-based agents can outperform single agents [9, 38, 53, 29], mirroring aspects of human teamwork. For example, GPTSwarm [53] introduce a graph-based approach inspired by language-based societies of mind [52], demonstrating that organizing LLM-based agents in structured topologies enhances their performance on benchmarks like MMLU [20], HumanEval [8], and GAIA [31]. Yet despite promising results from structured agent networks, existing benchmarks fall short in evaluating the core competencies of multi-agent systems: scalable coordination, decentralized communication, and collaborative reasoning. To address this gap, we introduce AgentsNet, a principled multi-agent benchmark that measures these capabilities across diverse network structures and scales.
近年来，随着基于大型语言和视觉模型（LLMs）构建的通用多智能体系统的出现，分布式系统在 AI 中发挥着日益重要的作用。生成式智能体（generative agents）[36] 等基于智能体的框架已展现出利用基于 LLM 的智能体解决复杂问题的潜力。尤其是，研究已表明，由基于 LLM 的智能体组成的网络能够优于单个智能体 [9, 38, 53, 29]，体现出人类团队协作的某些特征。例如，GPTSwarm [53] 受基于语言的心智社会（language-based societies of mind）[52] 启发，提出了一种基于图的方法，表明将基于 LLM 的智能体组织为结构化拓扑能够提升其在 MMLU [20]、HumanEval [8] 和 GAIA [31] 等基准上的性能。然而，尽管结构化智能体网络取得了令人鼓舞的结果，现有基准在评估多智能体系统的核心能力方面仍有不足：可扩展的协调、去中心化通信以及协作推理。为弥补这一不足，我们提出 AgentsNet，这是一个具有明确理论依据的多智能体基准，用于在不同网络结构和规模下衡量这些能力。

AgentsNet assesses the agent’s coordinative and collaborative capabilities through fundamental problems in distributed computing. Concretely, we identify five central problems from the distributed systems literature to construct corresponding coordination and collaboration tasks for multi-agent systems. Solving these tasks requires anything from local information aggregation to global coordination over multiple communication rounds. As a canonical example, whenever multi-agent systems are tasked with solving a certain problem, agents must necessarily be able to reach an agreement on the solution, a problem known in fault-tolerant distributed computing as consensus [16]. In another example, agents first agree on a single agent to take a leadership role, and then subsequently solve the task, guided and instructed by the elected leader. Selecting a single leader in a network is known as the leader election problem [2]. Fortunately, such problems are well-studied and theoretically grounded, providing an ideal testbed for the coordination and collaboration skills of multi-agent systems.
AgentsNet 通过分布式计算中的基本问题来评估智能体的协调与协作能力。具体而言，我们从分布式系统文献中选取了五个核心问题，为多智能体系统构建相应的协调与协作任务。解决这些任务所需的能力涵盖从局部信息聚合到跨多个通信轮次的全局协调。一个典型例子是，每当多智能体系统被要求解决某个问题时，智能体都必须能够就解决方案达成一致，这一问题在容错分布式计算中被称为共识（consensus）[16]。另一个例子是，智能体首先一致选定一个智能体担任领导者，随后在当选领导者的引导和指示下解决任务。在网络中选出唯一领导者被称为领导者选举问题（leader election problem）[2]。幸运的是，此类问题已得到充分研究，并具有扎实的理论基础，为检验多智能体系统的协调与协作能力提供了理想的试验平台。
Various multi-agent benchmarks exist, but no benchmark explicitly assesses the ability of multi-agent systems for structured coordination and collaboration, which should be seen as fundamental capabilities of effective distributed systems. As such, AgentsNet complements the existing suite of multi-agent benchmarks of LLMs with a particular focus on grounding in distributed systems theory, network topology, and scalability to large agent networks. 目前已有多种多智能体基准，但尚无基准明确评估多智能体系统进行结构化协调与协作的能力，而这些能力应被视为有效分布式系统的基本能力。因此，AgentsNet 补充了现有的大语言模型（LLM）多智能体基准体系，尤其注重以分布式系统理论为基础、网络拓扑以及向大规模智能体网络扩展的能力。具体而言，我们作出以下贡献：
We build AgentsNet from graph coloring, minimal vertex cover, maximal matching, leader election and consensus: five fundamental distributed computing problems that evaluate the ability of multi-agent systems to effectively self-organize, coordinate, and communicate to solve complex reasoning problems.
我们基于图着色、极小顶点覆盖、极大匹配、领导者选举和共识这五个基本的分布式计算问题构建 AgentsNet，用于评估多智能体系统通过有效的自组织、协调和通信来解决复杂推理问题的能力。
We design a robust and scalable message-passing protocol for effective agent-to-agent communication and evaluate on a rich set of graph instances, sampled from various graph models such as small-world [45] or preferential attachment models [4], which capture structural properties of real-world networks.
我们设计了一种稳健且可扩展的消息传递协议，以实现有效的智能体间通信，并在丰富的图实例上进行评估；这些实例采样自小世界模型 [45] 或优先连接模型 [4] 等多种图模型，能够刻画现实世界网络的结构特性。
We evaluate a variety of agentic baselines on AgentsNet, ranging from open-source LLMs such as Llama 4, to frontier models such as GPT, Gemini, and Claude, as well as the latest reasoning models, on the graphs of 4, 8, 16 nodes scaling the problem size to 100 agents which is well beyond existing agentic benchmarks.

We provide an in-depth qualitative analysis and highlight the challenges in coordinative and collaborative capabilities of LLMs to further improve multi-agent systems.

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Ensembling multiple agents to collaboratively negotiate solutions has emerged as an effective paradigm to improve LLM performance on complex tasks [13, 46, 24]. This has been extended through work on different network topologies for more structured agent interaction. Some studies examine pre-determined graph structures [39, 38] while others propose automatically adapting network topology [27, 9, 53]. Experiments show different topologies perform best for specific tasks [9, 53] and large-scale LLM agent networks exhibiting known social phenomena [48, 12]. Parallel research examines LLMs’ ability to reason with graph-structured data. Studies propose evaluation datasets [15, 44, 51, 43, 42] using single-agent setups where graphs are encoded as text. Fatemi et al., [15] investigate graph encoding methods, Sanford et al., [40] categorize graph reasoning problems by complexity, while Wang et al., [44] and Skianis et al., [42] explore effective prompting techniques. Our work bridges these research directions by studying multi-agent systems solving graph reasoning problems collaboratively. Our benchmark is complementary to recent agentic benchmarks [26, 50, 1, 49, 33] but scales to a practically unlimited number of agents due to the generative problem creation protocol, with experiments involving up to 100 coordinating agents. Related human studies on decentralized problem-solving in social networks show that network topology and size strongly influence coordination success [22, 21, 11]. Appendix F provides an extended discussion of related studies.
集成多个智能体以协作协商解决方案，已成为提升 LLM 在复杂任务上表现的一种有效范式 [13, 46, 24]。研究者通过探索不同的网络拓扑来实现更有结构的智能体交互，从而进一步拓展了这一范式。一些研究考察预先确定的图结构 [39, 38]，另一些研究则提出自动调整网络拓扑的方法 [27, 9, 53]。实验表明，不同拓扑在特定任务上表现最佳 [9, 53]，而大规模 LLM 智能体网络会呈现已知的社会现象 [48, 12]。与此同时，另一条研究路线考察 LLM 基于图结构数据进行推理的能力。相关研究提出了评估数据集 [15, 44, 51, 43, 42]，采用将图编码为文本的单智能体设置。Fatemi 等人 [15] 研究了图编码方法，Sanford 等人 [40] 按复杂度对图推理问题进行分类，而 Wang 等人 [44] 和 Skianis 等人 [42] 则探索了有效的提示技术。我们的工作通过研究多智能体系统如何协作解决图推理问题，将这些研究方向联系起来。我们的基准与近期的智能体基准 [26, 50, 1, 49, 33] 形成互补，但得益于生成式问题创建协议，其智能体数量实际上几乎可以无限扩展，实验涉及多达 100 个相互协调的智能体。关于人类在社交网络中进行去中心化问题求解的相关研究表明，网络拓扑和规模会显著影响协调能否成功 [22, 21, 11]。附录 F 对相关研究进行了更详尽的讨论。

3 Tasks, Evaluation, and Graph Models3 任务、评估与图模型
To evaluate the ability of multi-agent systems to self-organize, coordinate, and communicate effectively, we design a benchmark consisting of fundamental problems from distributed computing. These problems span a range of complexities, from local tasks that require minimal coordination to global problems that necessitate multi-round communication. In what follows, we introduce the theoretical problems and describe how we map each problem to a corresponding agentic task. Afterwards, we introduce the graph distributions used within AgentsNet.
为了评估多智能体系统有效进行自组织、协调和通信的能力，我们设计了一个由分布式计算基本问题组成的基准。这些问题涵盖不同的复杂度，从仅需少量协调的局部任务，到必须进行多轮通信的全局问题。下文将介绍这些理论问题，并说明我们如何将每个问题映射为相应的智能体任务。随后，我们将介绍 AgentsNet 中使用的图分布。
3.1 Benchmarking Tasks3.1 基准测试任务
We evaluate multi-agent systems on a set of distributed computing problems that test their ability to aggregate information, self-organize, and coordinate. These tasks are selected for their foundational nature in distributed computing and for their relevance as naturally appearing subproblems in more complicated tasks. They span a diverse range of coordination requirements and communication complexities, from purely local information exchange to global decision-making; see Table 1 for an overview of the different theoretical problems selected for AgentsNet.
我们通过一组分布式计算问题评估多智能体系统，以检验其聚合信息、自组织和协调的能力。选择这些任务，是因为它们在分布式计算中具有基础性地位，并且会作为子问题自然出现在更复杂的任务中。这些任务涵盖多样的协调要求和通信复杂度，从纯粹的局部信息交换到全局决策；AgentsNet 所选各类理论问题的概述见表 1。

( $\Delta+1$ )-Coloring.（$\Delta+1$）-着色。
Each node is assigned a color using at most $\Delta+1$ colors, where $\Delta$ is the maximum node degree. This problem has a well-defined distributed complexity of $O(\log^{*}n)$ in bounded-degree graphs [5]. This task is particularly useful for role assignment within multi-agent systems. For instance, agents can be designated to perform specific sub-tasks (e.g., web search, reasoning, coding, planning), with the constraint that directly connected agents are assigned distinct roles to avoid redundancy. Solving this task reflects the system’s ability to efficiently distribute responsibilities across the network with minimal overlap in capabilities. The corresponding agentic task is to form groups, with a pre-defined number of groups, and where each group corresponds to a color. After message-passing, each agent chooses the group it wants to be in. The task is solved if the groups form a valid $\Delta+1$ -coloring. In AgentsNet, we refer to this task as Coloring.
使用至多 $\Delta+1$ 种颜色为每个节点分配一种颜色，其中 $\Delta$ 为最大节点度。在有界度图中，该问题具有已明确确立的分布式复杂度 $O(\log^{*}n)$ [5]。这项任务对于多智能体系统中的角色分配尤其有用。例如，可以指定智能体执行特定子任务（如网络搜索、推理、编码、规划），同时要求直接相连的智能体被分配不同的角色，以避免冗余。解决这项任务体现了系统在网络中高效分配职责、尽量减少能力重叠的能力。对应的智能体任务是组建预先规定数量的组，每个组对应一种颜色。消息传递结束后，每个智能体选择自己想要加入的组。如果这些组构成有效的 $\Delta+1$ 着色，则任务解决。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将该任务称为 Coloring。
Minimal Vertex Cover.极小顶点覆盖。
A minimal vertex cover is a subset of nodes such that every edge in the graph has at least one endpoint in the subset, and removing any node from this subset would violate that property. This problem has a close relationship with the maximal independent set and is similarly fundamental in distributed computing, with known randomized solutions in $O(\log^{*}n)$ rounds [25]. In agentic networks, a minimal vertex cover can represent a minimal set of monitor or gateway agents that maintain awareness of all interactions in the system. These agents could take on responsibilities such as relaying messages, auditing behavior, or bridging subgroups. The task tests the ability to identify a compact yet effective set of nodes with high influence or observability. The corresponding agentic task is to select a group of coordinators among the agents. After message-passing, each agent is asked whether it is a coordinator. The agents can respond with either Yes or No. The task is solved if coordinators form a minimal vertex cover. In AgentsNet, we refer to this task as VertexCover.
极小顶点覆盖是一个节点子集，满足图中的每条边至少有一个端点属于该子集，且从该子集中移除任何节点都会破坏这一性质。该问题与极大独立集密切相关，在分布式计算中同样具有基础性地位，已有可在 $O(\log^{*}n)$ 轮内求解的随机化算法 [25]。在智能体网络中，极小顶点覆盖（minimal vertex cover）可以表示一组极小的监控智能体或网关智能体，它们能够掌握系统中的所有交互。这些智能体可以承担消息转发、行为审计或连接子群体等职责。该任务检验识别一组规模小而有效、具有较高影响力或观测能力的节点的能力。对应的智能体任务是从智能体中选出一组协调者。消息传递结束后，每个智能体都会被询问自己是否为协调者。智能体可以回答 Yes 或 No。如果协调者构成极小顶点覆盖，则任务解决。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将该任务称为 VertexCover。
Maximal Matching.极大匹配。
A maximal matching is a set of edges such that no two edges share a vertex, and no additional edges can be added without violating this property. This task captures the ability of agents to negotiate pairwise agreements without global knowledge, which is useful in scenarios where resource allocation or mutual exclusivity must be enforced (e.g., agent-to-agent task assignment). Randomized algorithms typically solve this problem in $O(\log^{*}n)$ rounds [37]. The corresponding agentic task is for the agents to form pairs. After message-passing, each agent is asked to name the neighbor it wants to pair up with. The agents can also respond with None if they cannot find a match (all neighbor agents are already paired up with other agents). The task is solved if the paired agents form a maximal matching. In AgentsNet, we refer to this task as Matching.
极大匹配（maximal matching）是一个边集合，其中任意两条边都不共享顶点，且无法在不违反这一性质的情况下添加任何其他边。该任务体现了智能体在没有全局知识的情况下协商达成两两协议的能力，这在必须实施资源分配或互斥约束的场景中很有用（例如，智能体之间的任务分配）。随机化算法通常可在 $O(\log^{*}n)$ 轮内解决该问题 [37]。对应的智能体任务是让智能体组成配对。消息传递结束后，每个智能体都需要指出自己希望与哪个邻居配对。如果找不到配对对象（所有邻居智能体都已与其他智能体配对），智能体也可以回答 None。如果配对的智能体构成极大匹配，则任务解决。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将该任务称为 Matching。
Leader Election.领导者选举。
One node must be selected as the leader, while all others acknowledge that they are not. This classic coordination task is central to evaluating how well agents establish hierarchy and delegate global decision-making [2]. In multi-agent systems, leader election can be interpreted as selecting a central planner or controller agent responsible for strategy synthesis, while the remaining agents act as executors. Effective leader election demonstrates the system’s capacity to break symmetry and converge on a single authority. In general graphs, the round complexity is $O(D)$ , where $D$ is the network diameter [28]. The corresponding agentic task is to select a single leader among the agents. After message-passing, each agent is asked whether it is the leader. The agents can respond with either Yes or No. The task is solved if there exists exactly one leader. In AgentsNet, we refer to this task as LeaderElection.
必须选出一个节点作为领导者，同时所有其他节点都确认自己不是领导者。这一经典协调任务是评估智能体建立层级结构并委派全局决策职责的能力的核心任务 [2]。在多智能体系统中，领导者选举可以理解为选出一个负责策略合成的中央规划智能体或控制智能体，其余智能体则充当执行者。有效的领导者选举体现了系统打破对称性并收敛到单一权威的能力。在一般图中，轮次复杂度为 $O(D)$，其中 $D$ 为网络直径 [28]。对应的智能体任务是从智能体中选出唯一的领导者。消息传递结束后，每个智能体都需要回答自己是否为领导者。智能体可以回答 Yes 或 No。如果恰好存在一个领导者，则任务解决。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将该任务称为 LeaderElection。

Consensus.共识。
In the consensus problem, all agents must agree on a single value from the set $\{0,1\}$ . In our benchmark, we focus on the basic setting without any faulty or Byzantine agents. The goal is for all agents to coordinate and produce the same final answer after a number of communication rounds. A successful solution requires that every agent outputs the same value, either 0 or 1. This task tests the ability of multi-agent systems to converge to a global agreement through local message-passing alone. In synchronous networks, achieving consensus generally requires $\mathcal{O}(D)$ rounds [28]. The corresponding agentic task is to choose between a value $0$ and $1$ . After message-passing, each agent is asked to announce its selected value. The task is solved if all agents announce the same value. In AgentsNet, we refer to this task as Consensus.
在共识问题中，所有智能体必须就集合 $\{0,1\}$ 中的某个值达成一致。在我们的基准测试中，我们关注不存在任何故障智能体或拜占庭智能体的基本设定。目标是让所有智能体经过若干轮通信后，通过协调给出相同的最终答案。成功的解决方案要求每个智能体输出相同的值，即全部为 0 或全部为 1。该任务考察多智能体系统仅通过局部消息传递收敛到全局共识的能力。在同步网络中，达成共识通常需要 $\mathcal{O}(D)$ 轮通信 [28]。对应的智能体任务是在值 $0$ 和 $1$ 之间进行选择。消息传递结束后，要求每个智能体公布其选定的值。如果所有智能体宣布的值相同，则该任务得到解决。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将此任务称为 Consensus。

3.2 Network Topologies3.2 网络拓扑
While classical distributed computing often studies problems on random graphs such as Erdős-Renyi networks [14], these do not adequately capture the structural properties of real-world networks. Instead, we focus on three well-established graph models, namely the Watts-Strogatz graphs [45] (SmallWorld) exhibiting both short average path lengths and high clustering coefficients; preferential attachment graphs [4] (ScaleFree) containing hubs (high-degree nodes) and follow a power-law degree distribution; geometric graphs by constructing a Delaunay triangulation over randomly sampled 2D points, (Delaunay), maintaining a spatial relationship between nearby agents. We describe these graph models in more detail in Appendix D.
经典分布式计算通常研究 Erdős-Renyi 网络 [14] 等随机图上的问题，但这些图不足以刻画现实世界网络的结构特性。因此，我们重点关注三种成熟的图模型，即同时具有较短平均路径长度和较高聚类系数的 Watts-Strogatz 图 [45]（SmallWorld）；包含枢纽节点（高度数节点）且度分布遵循幂律的优先连接图 [4]（ScaleFree）；以及通过对随机采样的二维点构建 Delaunay 三角剖分而生成、保留邻近智能体之间空间关系的几何图（Delaunay）。我们在附录 D 中更详细地介绍这些图模型。
4 Agent-to-Agent Communication via Message-Passing4 通过消息传递实现智能体间通信
To systematically study how agents exchange information and collaborate, we employ a communication model that draws inspiration from classical distributed computing, while adapting to the capabilities and constraints of modern LLM-based agents. Our setup is based on the LOCAL model [25] from distributed algorithms, in which the computation proceeds in synchronous rounds and each agent can exchange messages only with its immediate neighbors on the communication graph. Agents must base their decisions exclusively on local information aggregated over multiple rounds of interaction. This model captures fundamental aspects of decentralized reasoning, where global strategies emerge from purely local exchanges without centralized control. Unlike nodes in deterministic systems, LLM-based agents exhibit stochastic behavior due to inherent randomness in their generation processes. This means that our model is most closely aligned with the randomized version of the LOCAL model.
为系统地研究智能体如何交换信息并开展协作，我们采用了一种通信模型，该模型借鉴了经典分布式计算，同时针对现代基于 LLM 的智能体的能力与约束进行了调整。我们的设置基于分布式算法中的 LOCAL 模型 [25]，在该模型中，计算按同步轮次进行，每个智能体只能与通信图中直接相邻的节点交换消息。智能体必须完全依据多轮交互中汇集的局部信息作出决策。该模型刻画了去中心化推理的基本特征，即在没有集中控制的情况下，全局策略通过纯粹的局部信息交换涌现。与确定性系统中的节点不同，基于 LLM 的智能体由于其生成过程中的内在随机性而表现出随机行为。这意味着我们的模型最接近 LOCAL 模型的随机化版本。

5.1 Setup5.1 设置
For benchmarking, we generate a set of 27 network topologies, consisting of 9 small-world, scale-free, and Delaunay graphs, respectively, ranging in size from 4 to 16 nodes. Concretely, for each graph size in $\{4,8,16\}$ and each graph distribution in $\{\textsc{SmallWorld},\textsc{ScaleFree},\textsc{Delaunay}\}$ , we generate three graphs. Further, we determine the number of message-passing rounds as follows. For our global tasks, LeaderElection and Consensus, each agent must be able to exchange information with the entire network. Hence, for those two tasks, we select the number of message-passing rounds as $2D+1$ , where $D$ is the diameter of the graph, to ensure that each pair of agents is able to exchange messages at least once. For the local tasks, Coloring, Matching, and VertexCover, we determine the number of rounds based on the graph size. Specifically, for graphs with 4 nodes, we choose 4 rounds, for 8 nodes – 5 rounds, for 16 nodes – 6 rounds.
为开展基准测试，我们生成了一组共 27 个网络拓扑，其中小世界图、无标度图和 Delaunay 图各 9 个，规模从 4 个节点到 16 个节点不等。具体而言，对于 $\{4,8,16\}$ 中的每种图规模，以及 $\{\textsc{SmallWorld},\textsc{ScaleFree},\textsc{Delaunay}\}$ 中的每种图分布，我们生成三个图。此外，我们按如下方式确定消息传递的轮数。对于全局任务 LeaderElection 和 Consensus，每个智能体都必须能够与整个网络交换信息。因此，对于这两个任务，我们将消息传递轮数设为 $2D+1$，其中 $D$ 为图的直径，以确保每对智能体都能够至少交换一次消息。对于局部任务 Coloring、Matching 和 VertexCover，我们根据图的规模确定轮数。具体而言，对于有 4 个节点的图，我们选择 4 轮；对于 8 个节点的图，选择 5 轮；对于 16 个节点的图，选择 6 轮。

Evaluation.评估。
AgentsNet uses a binary evaluation metric, counting only fully correct solutions where the entire agent network satisfies the task specification. This strict criterion reflects the nature of distributed computing problems, where partial correctness often does not imply successful coordination. For example, in Coloring, most nodes may be correctly colored by chance, but only a valid global coloring confirms coordinated conflict resolution. However, in Appendix B, we also discuss and report the soft evaluation scores to obtain a more continuous measure of the quality of responses, motivated by the findings in Schaeffer et al., [41], that emergent behaviors can often be explained by discontinuous metrics.
AgentsNet 使用二元评估指标，仅将整个智能体网络均满足任务规范的完全正确解计为成功。这一严格标准反映了分布式计算问题的本质：部分正确往往并不意味着协调成功。例如，在 Coloring 中，大多数节点可能碰巧被正确着色，但只有有效的全局着色才能证实通过协调解决了冲突。不过，在附录 B 中，我们也讨论并报告了软评估分数，以获得对响应质量更连续的衡量；这样做的依据是 Schaeffer 等人 [41] 的发现，即涌现行为往往可以用不连续的指标来解释。
For each task and graph size, we sample three graphs per topology (small-world, scale-free, Delaunay) and run at least one repeat per graph. We report the mean of solved runs and the standard errors of the mean, computed across these runs [32]. Details on scoring and statistical methodology are provided in Appendix C.
对于每项任务和每种图规模，我们针对每种拓扑（小世界、无标度、Delaunay）采样三个图，并对每个图至少重复运行一次。我们报告在这些运行中计算得到的求解成功比例的均值及均值标准误 [32]。评分和统计方法的详细信息见附录 C。

6 Limitations6 局限性
We implement AgentsNet on the LOCAL computation model from distributed computing, which uses synchronous message-passing. However, other message-passing protocols may be more effective in the context of collaborative problem solving. For example, a downside of synchronous message-passing is that agents receive answers to their messages only in the subsequent message-passing round. Moreover, our implementation relies on answer parsing via JSON, which can be ambiguous and error-prone. However, we note that both of the above limitations are implementation choices not inherent to AgentsNet. For example, future work on effective message-passing protocols or the use of structured output[fn2] could be easily incorporated into AgentsNet. We elaborate on the limitations in Appendix G.
我们基于分布式计算中的 LOCAL 计算模型实现了 AgentsNet，该模型采用同步消息传递。然而，在协作求解问题的情境下，其他消息传递协议可能更有效。例如，同步消息传递的一个缺点是，智能体只有在下一轮消息传递中才能收到对其消息的回复。此外，我们的实现依赖于通过 JSON 解析答案，这可能存在歧义且容易出错。不过，我们指出，上述两项局限性均源于实现方式的选择，并非 AgentsNet 固有的局限性。例如，未来关于有效消息传递协议或结构化输出（structured output）[fn2] 使用方法的研究成果，可以很容易地整合到 AgentsNet 中。我们在附录 G 中进一步阐述了这些局限性。

7 Conclusion7 结论
In this work, we proposed AgentsNet, a multi-agent benchmark built on top of fundamental problems from distributed computing, with the goal of assessing the ability of agentic networks to coordinate and collaborate to solve problems. While existing benchmarks are limited to 2–5 agents, the initial AgentsNet suite probes up to 100 agents and is practically unlimited in size and can generate problems of increasing complexity to keep up with new generations of frontier models. To this end, we design a robust message-passing protocol to enable multi-step communication between agents and evaluate models on a variety of graph instances, sampled from multiple graph models, and with different graph sizes. We evaluate and compare a variety of frontier LLMs on AgentsNet and find that our tasks can be challenging for even the best models.
在本研究中，我们提出了 AgentsNet，这是一个基于分布式计算基本问题构建的多智能体基准，旨在评估智能体网络通过协调与协作解决问题的能力。现有基准仅限于 2–5 个智能体，而初始的 AgentsNet 基准套件可测试多达 100 个智能体，其规模实际上不受限制，并且能够生成复杂度不断提高的问题，以跟上新一代前沿模型的发展。为此，我们设计了一种稳健的消息传递协议，以支持智能体之间的多步通信，并在从多种图模型中采样得到、具有不同规模的各类图实例上评估模型。我们在 AgentsNet 上评估并比较了多种前沿大语言模型（LLM），发现我们的任务即使对最优秀的模型也具有挑战性。

Consensus.共识。
In the consensus problem, all agents must agree on a single value from the set ${0,1}$ . In our benchmark, we focus on the basic setting without any faulty or Byzantine agents. Appendix C Score Computation附录 C 评分计算
We apply a systematic statistical methodology to evaluate model performance and quantify uncertainty. For each combination of graph size, task, and graph generator (Watts-Strogatz, Barabási-Albert, and Delaunay triangulations), we generate three distinct graph instances. We conduct one or more experimental runs per instance, resulting in at least three observations per configuration. For each model, we compute a mean score $\mu_{s,t,g}$ for each configuration triplet $(s,t,g)$ where $s$ represents graph size, $t$ represents task type, and $g$ represents the graph generation algorithm:
我们采用系统的统计方法来评估模型性能并量化不确定性。对于图规模、任务和图生成器（Watts-Strogatz、Barabási-Albert 和 Delaunay 三角剖分）的每种组合，我们生成三个不同的图实例。我们对每个实例进行一次或多次实验运行，因此每种配置至少产生三个观测值。对于每个模型，我们为每个配置三元组 $(s,t,g)$ 计算平均得分 $\mu_{s,t,g}$，其中 $s$ 表示图规模，$t$ 表示任务类型，$g$ 表示图生成算法：
$$\mu_{s,t,g}=\frac{1}{N_{s,t,g}}\sum_{i=1}^{3}\sum_{j=1}^{n_{i}}x_{s,t,g,i,j}$$
where $x_{s,t,g,i,j}$ denotes the performance score of the $j$ -th run on the $i$ -th graph instance of configuration $(s,t,g)$ , $n_{i}$ is the number of runs performed on the $i$ -th graph instance, and $N_{s,t,g}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}n_{i}$ is the total number of runs for this configuration. For each configuration, we compute the standard error $\text{SE}_{s,t,g}$ as:
其中，$x_{s,t,g,i,j}$ 表示在配置 $(s,t,g)$ 的第 $i$ 个图实例上第 $j$ 次运行的性能得分，$n_{i}$ 是在第 $i$ 个图实例上执行的运行次数，$N_{s,t,g}=\sum_{i=1}^{3}n_{i}$ 是该配置的总运行次数。对于每种配置，我们按如下方式计算标准误 $\text{SE}_{s,t,g}$：
$$\text{SE}_{s,t,g}=\frac{\sigma_{s,t,g}}{\sqrt{N_{s,t,g}}}$$
where $\sigma_{s,t,g}$ is the standard deviation of all runs for this configuration. To compute an aggregate score for each model across all configurations, we average the mean scores and derive the standard error of this aggregate score. Let $C$ be the set of all configurations, with cardinality $|C|=|S|\times|T|\times|G|$ . The aggregate mean score $\bar{\mu}$ for a model is:
其中，$\sigma_{s,t,g}$ 是该配置下所有运行得分的标准差。为了计算每个模型在所有配置上的汇总得分，我们对各配置的平均得分取平均，并推导该汇总得分的标准误。令 $C$ 为所有配置的集合，其基数为 $|C|=|S|\times|T|\times|G|$。模型的汇总平均得分 $\bar{\mu}$ 为：
$$\bar{\mu}=\frac{1}{|C|}\sum_{(s,t,g)\in C}\mu_{s,t,g}$$
For the standard error of this aggregate mean, assuming independence between configurations, we apply error propagation principles to obtain:
对于该汇总均值的标准误，假设各配置之间相互独立，我们应用误差传播原理得到：
$$\text{SE}_{\bar{\mu}}=\sqrt{\frac{\sum_{(s,t,g)\in C}\text{SE}_{s,t,g}^{2}}{|C|^{2}}}$$
This approach enables us to quantify both the average performance of each model across the entire benchmark and the statistical uncertainty associated with this estimate. We follow the recommendation of Miller, [32] and report the standard error of the mean for all our experimental results. In Figure 1, we present the mean AgentsNet score for each model with error bars indicating the standard error of the mean, allowing for comparison of model performance while accounting for statistical variability in the results.
这种方法使我们能够量化每个模型在整个基准上的平均性能，以及该估计所伴随的统计不确定性。我们遵循 Miller [32] 的建议，为所有实验结果报告均值的标准误。在图 1 中，我们展示了每个模型的 AgentsNet 平均得分，误差条表示均值的标准误，从而能够在考虑结果统计变异性的同时比较模型性能。

Small-world networks.小世界网络。
Generated using the Watts-Strogatz model [45], these graphs exhibit both short average path lengths and high clustering coefficients. They are commonly found in social networks, biological systems, and communication networks, making them highly relevant for studying agent-based interactions. In AgentsNet, we refer to these graphs as SmallWorld; see Figure 6 for a visualization of the network topologies generated from SmallWorld graphs.

Scale-free networks.无标度网络。
Constructed using preferential attachment models [4], these graphs contain hubs (high-degree nodes) and follow a power-law degree distribution. Such networks are prevalent in the internet topology, citation networks, and organizational hierarchies, making them crucial for benchmarking coordination strategies. Notably, Kearns et al., [22] also draws a comparison between the two and claims that graphs generated with a preferential attachment model were more difficult to solve, although the graphs and testing they used were very limited. In AgentsNet, we refer to these graphs as ScaleFree; see Figure 7 for a visualization of the network topologies generated from ScaleFree graphs.
这些图使用优先连接模型 [4] 构建，包含枢纽节点（高度数节点），其度分布遵循幂律。此类网络广泛存在于互联网拓扑、引文网络和组织层级结构中，因此对于协调策略的基准评测至关重要。值得注意的是，Kearns 等人 [22] 也对这两类网络进行了比较，并声称使用优先连接模型生成的图更难求解，尽管他们使用的图和开展的测试都非常有限。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将这些图称为 ScaleFree；由 ScaleFree 图生成的网络拓扑的可视化见图 7。
Geometric Graphs.几何图。
We generate geometric graphs by constructing a Delaunay triangulation over randomly sampled 2D points. These graphs maintain a spatial relationship between nearby agents. Delaunay graphs are planar and connected, have relatively low maximum degrees, and short local distances, which makes them particularly relevant for modeling geographic networks, wireless communication, and distributed sensor systems. In contrast to small-world or scale-free graphs, geometric graphs emphasize proximity-based coordination, where collaboration is often limited by spatial locality rather than global topology. In AgentsNet, we refer to these graphs as Delaunay; see Figure 8 for a visualization of the network topologies generated from Delaunay graphs.
我们通过对随机采样的二维点构建 Delaunay 三角剖分来生成几何图。这些图保留了邻近智能体之间的空间关系。Delaunay 图是连通的平面图，最大度相对较低，局部距离较短，因此特别适合用于对地理网络、无线通信和分布式传感器系统进行建模。与小世界图或无标度图不同，几何图强调基于邻近关系的协调，其中协作往往受到空间局部性而非全局拓扑的限制。在 AgentsNet 中，我们将这些图称为 Delaunay；由 Delaunay 图生成的网络拓扑的可视化见图 8。

Appendix F Extended Related Work附录 F 扩展相关工作
Recent research has increasingly focused on utilizing multiple LLM agents collaboratively to enhance performance and tackle complex problems. “Multi-Agent Debate” [13, 46, 24] allows multiple agents to iteratively discuss solutions, effectively acting as a parallelizable test-time computation scaling and self-consistency mechanism. Further work introduces different network topologies for more structured agent interaction. Some works study pre-determined graph structures [39, 38] while others propose to automatically adapt the network topology towards a given task [27, 9, 53]. In particular, it has been observed that different network topologies work best for different tasks [9, 53] and that, in some scenarios, the reasoning performance scales logistically in the network size [38]. The behavior of large-scale LLM agent networks has further been shown to resemble real social phenomena, such as misinformation spreading and herd effects [48, 12].
近期研究日益关注利用多个大语言模型（LLM）智能体协作来提升性能并解决复杂问题。“多智能体辩论”（Multi-Agent Debate）[13, 46, 24] 允许多个智能体迭代讨论解决方案，实际上发挥着可并行化的测试时计算扩展机制和自一致性机制的作用。进一步的工作引入了不同的网络拓扑，以实现更有结构的智能体交互。一些工作研究预先确定的图结构 [39, 38]，另一些则提出针对给定任务自动调整网络拓扑 [27, 9, 53]。具体而言，已有研究观察到，不同任务各自适合不同的网络拓扑 [9, 53]，且在某些场景中，推理性能随网络规模的增长呈逻辑斯蒂曲线变化 [38]。研究还表明，大规模 LLM 智能体网络的行为与现实中的社会现象相似，例如错误信息传播和羊群效应 [48, 12]。
Understanding the ability of LLMs to perform reasoning tasks on graph-structured data has become another active research area. A range of studies propose datasets for evaluating LLMs on graph reasoning tasks [15, 44, 51, 43, 42]. These generally rely on a single-agent setup where a graph is encoded as text, and a single LLM instance is prompted to solve a particular reasoning task for this graph. This setup is well-suited to study the capability of LLMs for solving complex tasks on structured data in a controlled setting. Fatemi et al., [15] investigate the impact of how the input graph is encoded as text. Appendix G Limitations附录 G 局限性
While AgentsNet provides a principled and scalable benchmark for evaluating coordination and collaboration in multi-agent LLM systems, several limitations remain. The benchmark adopts a fixed and synchronous communication model based on the LOCAL framework, with all agents engaging in a pre-defined number of message-passing rounds. Although this choice aligns with theoretical work in distributed computing, it limits the ecological validity of the set-up. Many real-world multi-agent systems operate asynchronously or under dynamic communication constraints, and it remains unclear how well performance would transfer under such conditions. Our evaluation protocol considers an instance solved only if it meets strict task-specific correctness criteria. This binary metric provides a clear signal for coordination success, but may obscure partial progress, particularly in tasks where near-correct solutions still demonstrate substantial reasoning capability. Moreover, while tasks are instantiated in diverse graph topologies, the agents themselves are homogeneous within each experiment, sharing architecture, capabilities, and prompting style. This homogeneity simplifies analysis, but does not capture heterogeneous agent settings, which are common in real-world deployments and pose additional coordination challenges. Finally, the scalability of AgentsNet is limited in practice by the computational cost of LLM inference. Although the benchmark can be instantiated with up to 100 agents, performance degrades significantly beyond small network sizes. This suggests that current LLMs are not yet capable of maintaining coherent global strategies under increasing communication and memory demands. In addition, the current setup assumes that all agents act cooperatively and faithfully follow the protocol. We do not consider settings with noisy, faulty, or adversarial agents, which would be essential for assessing robustness in more realistic deployments.
尽管 AgentsNet 为评估多智能体 LLM 系统中的协调与协作提供了一个具有严谨设计依据且可扩展的基准，但仍存在若干局限性。该基准采用基于 LOCAL 框架的固定同步通信模型，所有智能体均参与预先设定轮数的消息传递。尽管这一选择与分布式计算领域的理论研究一致，但它限制了该设置的生态效度。许多现实世界中的多智能体系统以异步方式运行，或受到动态通信约束，而其性能在这些条件下能够在多大程度上迁移，目前仍不明确。我们的评估协议仅在实例满足严格的任务特定正确性标准时，才将其视为已解决。这一二元指标能够清晰地反映协调是否成功，但可能掩盖部分进展，尤其是在近乎正确的解仍能体现出较强推理能力的任务中。此外，尽管任务在多种图拓扑上实例化，但每次实验中的智能体本身都是同质的，具有相同的架构、能力和提示风格。这种同质性简化了分析，但无法涵盖异质智能体的设置，而这种设置在现实部署中十分常见，并会带来额外的协调挑战。最后，AgentsNet 的可扩展性在实践中受到 LLM 推理计算成本的限制。尽管该基准可以实例化为包含多达 100 个智能体的系统，但当网络规模超出较小范围时，性能会显著下降。这表明，随着通信和记忆需求的增加，当前的 LLM 尚无法维持连贯一致的全局策略。此外，当前设置假定所有智能体均以合作方式行动，并忠实遵循协议。我们未考虑包含噪声智能体、故障智能体或对抗性智能体的设置，而要评估更贴近现实的部署中的鲁棒性，考虑这些设置至关重要。
