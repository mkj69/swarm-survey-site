Abstract.摘要。
Background: Multi-agent systems (MAS) assume that collaborating inherently improves Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning. We challenge this assumption by demonstrating that simulated social pressure triggers an algorithmic “Bystander Effect”, inducing severe cognitive loafing.
背景：多智能体系统（MAS）假定，协作本身就能改善大语言模型（LLM）的推理。我们对这一假设提出质疑，并证明模拟的社会压力会触发算法层面的“旁观者效应”，导致严重的认知惰化。
Objectives: We formalize the Sovereignty Decay Law to model how Composite Social Load ( $\mathcal{L}$ ) erodes independent derivation, quantify the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) at which agentic sovereignty collapses, isolate the Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ) between internal trace validity and externalized compliance, and test how Lead-Anchor Primacy ( $w_{i}$ ), Intrinsic Peer Authority ( $\alpha$ ), and Architectural Kinship ( $\kappa$ ) govern failure modes across multi-agent topologies.
目标：我们形式化提出主权衰减定律（Sovereignty Decay Law），以建模复合社会负荷（Composite Social Load，$\mathcal{L}$）如何侵蚀独立推导能力；量化智能体主权崩溃时的交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit，$D_{L}$）；分离内部轨迹有效性与外显顺从行为之间的主权差距（Sovereignty Gap，$G_{\mathcal{S}}$）；并检验首位锚点优先性（Lead-Anchor Primacy，$w_{i}$）、内在同伴权威（Intrinsic Peer Authority，$\alpha$）和架构亲缘性（Architectural Kinship，$\kappa$）如何支配不同多智能体拓扑中的失效模式。
Methods: By evaluating 22,500 deterministic trajectories across 3 dataset contexts (GAIA, SWE-bench, Multi-Challenge) with 3 state-of-the-art (SOTA) models, we semantically audit internal reasoning traces against external outputs. Our protocol pairs Semantic Hijacking with a 25-configuration categorical topology sweep across progressive swarm pluralities ( $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ ), sequence-inverted permutations, and in-family versus cross-family auditor compositions.
方法：我们使用 3 个当前最先进（SOTA）的模型，评估其在 3 种数据集情境（GAIA、SWE-bench、Multi-Challenge）中的 22,500 条确定性轨迹，并对照外部输出，对内部推理轨迹进行语义审计。我们的实验方案将语义劫持（Semantic Hijacking）与涵盖 25 种配置的类别型拓扑扫描相结合，覆盖逐步增加的群体规模（$n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$）、顺序反转排列，以及同模型家族与跨模型家族的审计智能体组合。
Results: Across the 6 susceptible model–benchmark pairs, accuracy degrades sharply at the consensus threshold $n=2$ ( $p<0.001$ )—with Gemini 3.1 Pro dropping to $0.59$ and GPT-5.4 breaching the terminal sovereignty limit ( $D_{L}\leq 2$ ), collapsing from $1.00$ to $0.43$ on GAIA, $1.00$ to $0.23$ on SWE-bench, and $0.98$ to $0.09$ ( $0.08$ at $n=5$ ) on Multi-Challenge—whereas across the remaining 3 pairs Claude Sonnet 4.6 sustains a “Fortified Mind” ceiling ( $0.00$ loafing rate across all pluralities), confirming that collapse is an architectural vulnerability rather than a prompting artifact. Trace-level auditing reveals a pronounced Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}=+0.45$ at $n=2$ , where $0.68$ internal validity yields only $0.23$ external accuracy) driven by “Alignment Hallucinations” that bifurcate into two failure modes: active decoy adoption versus passive task disengagement. Finally, social load is strictly non-commutative and kinship-amplified: inverting two auditors recovers accuracy by up to $24\%$ ( $0.37$ to $0.61$ , $p=6.9\times 10^{-4}$ ), while homogeneous in-family swarms inflict steeper degradation than mixed cross-family ensembles.
结果：在 6 个易受影响的模型–基准组合中，准确率在共识阈值 $n=2$ 处急剧下降（$p<0.001$）——其中 Gemini 3.1 Pro 降至 $0.59$，GPT-5.4 则突破主权终止极限（$D_{L}\leq 2$），在 GAIA 上从 $1.00$ 骤降至 $0.43$，在 SWE-bench 上从 $1.00$ 骤降至 $0.23$，在 Multi-Challenge 上从 $0.98$ 骤降至 $0.09$（$n=5$ 时为 $0.08$）——而在其余 3 个组合中，Claude Sonnet 4.6 始终维持“坚固心智”（Fortified Mind）的表现上限（在所有群体规模下，惰化率均为 $0.00$），证实这种崩溃属于架构脆弱性，而非提示方式造成的伪象。轨迹层面的审计揭示了显著的主权差距（$n=2$ 时，$G_{\mathcal{S}}=+0.45$，此时 $0.68$ 的内部有效率仅产生 $0.23$ 的外部准确率），这一差距由“对齐幻觉”（Alignment Hallucinations）驱动，并分化为两种失效模式：主动采纳诱饵与被动脱离任务。最后，社会负荷具有严格的非交换性，并受到亲缘性的放大：颠倒两个审计智能体的顺序可使准确率最多恢复 $24\%$（从 $0.37$ 升至 $0.61$，$p=6.9\times 10^{-4}$），而同模型家族的同质群体比跨模型家族的混合集成造成更严重的性能下降。
Conclusions: These findings expose architectural vulnerabilities, demonstrating that unstructured multi-agent topologies can degrade independent reasoning unless bounded by plurality limits, architectural diversity, and authority-aware ordering.
结论：这些发现揭示了架构脆弱性，表明若不通过群体规模限制、架构多样性及考虑权威性的排序加以约束，非结构化的多智能体拓扑可能削弱独立推理能力。
1. Introduction1. 引言
LLM integration into MAS topologies is driven by the premise that a “society of thought” intrinsically enhances reasoning capabilities Kim et al., 2026. Consequently, orchestrating LLMs into collaborative swarms has become a standard paradigm for resolving complex tasks Chen et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2026. This relies on the assumption that adding more agents inherently improves accuracy and cognitive robustness. However, in human psychology, the opposite is often true: the principle of social loafing demonstrates that individual effort decreases as teams grow larger, driven by a diffusion of responsibility Ringelmann, 1913; Latané et al., 1979. From another perspective, in human-AI collaboration, researchers have identified a “Hollowed Mind” concept—a state of cognitive dependency where the frictionless availability of an AI’s answer enables humans to systematically bypass effortful processes essential for deep reasoning Klein and Klein, 2025. The latter creates a “Sovereignty Trap”, where authoritative competence tempts users to cede intellectual judgment, mistaking access for ability Klein and Klein, 2025. Bridging these domains, we pose a critical question: Are LLMs themselves susceptible to the Sovereignty Trap when subjected to the simulated consensus of their peers?
将 LLM 整合到 MAS 拓扑中的做法，源于这样一个前提：“思维社会”本身就能增强推理能力 Kim et al., 2026。因此，将 LLM 编排为协作群体已成为解决复杂任务的标准范式 Chen et al., 2024; Sheng et al., 2026。这一做法依赖于如下假设：增加智能体数量本身就能提高准确率和认知稳健性。然而，在人类心理学中，情况往往恰恰相反：社会惰化原理表明，由于责任分散，团队规模越大，个体付出的努力越少 Ringelmann, 1913; Latané et al., 1979。从另一个角度看，在人类与 AI 的协作中，研究者提出了“空心化心智”（Hollowed Mind）概念——这是一种认知依赖状态，在这种状态下，轻易获取 AI 答案使人类得以系统性地绕过深度推理所必需的费力过程 Klein and Klein, 2025。后者造成了一种“主权陷阱”（Sovereignty Trap）：具有权威性的能力表现诱使用户让渡智识判断权，将获取答案的便利误认为自身能力 Klein and Klein, 2025。将这些领域联系起来，我们提出一个关键问题：当 LLM 面对模拟的同伴共识时，它们自身是否也容易陷入主权陷阱？
To investigate this hypothesis, we measure anticipatory cognitive loafing, bypassing active message-passing systems, to isolate the semantic trigger of social compliance. We evaluate 22,500 interactions across 3 SOTA benchmarks (GAIA, SWE-bench, and Multi-Challenge) and 3 SOTA models (Claude Sonnet 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro and GPT 5.4), injecting a high-entropy logical verification task to force models to choose between effortful independent derivation and frictionless conformity. By mechanistically auditing the models’ internal reasoning traces (i.e. Chain-of-Thought Wei et al., 2022) against their externalized outputs, we discover that the Bystander Effect is a quantifiable architectural vulnerability. Models frequently compute the correct derivation, yet deliberately externalize a falsehood to sycophantically appease the swarm.
为检验这一假设，我们绕过主动消息传递系统，测量预期性认知惰化，以分离出社会顺从的语义触发因素。我们在 3 个 SOTA 基准（GAIA、SWE-bench 和 Multi-Challenge）和 3 个 SOTA 模型（Claude Sonnet 4.6、Gemini 3.1 Pro 和 GPT 5.4）上评估了 22,500 次交互，并注入一项高熵逻辑验证任务，迫使模型在费力的独立推导与轻松的从众之间做出选择。通过对照模型的外显输出，从机制层面对其内部推理轨迹（即思维链，Chain-of-Thought Wei et al., 2022）进行审计，我们发现旁观者效应是一种可量化的架构脆弱性。模型经常能够得出正确的推导，却为了谄媚地迎合群体而刻意输出错误陈述。

2. Theoretical Framework: The Mechanics of Agentic Sovereignty2. 理论框架：智能体主权的作用机制
As multi-agent reasoning systems increasingly rely on collaborative heuristics, it is necessary to mathematically formalize the vulnerabilities introduced by social consensus. In this section, we construct a behavioral mechanics of Agentic Sovereignty—the propensity of a reasoning agent to prioritize internal logical derivation over external social pressure. We define the parameters of social load, establish the non-commutativity of auditor sequences, derive the Sovereignty Decay Law and the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) at which sovereignty collapses, and state the Sovereignty Gap Theorem. The diagram illustrates how the structural composition of the simulated swarm ( $\vec{a}$ ) determines the Composite Social Load ( $\mathcal{L}$ ), and how that load, amplified by the intrinsic search complexity of the task (Task Entropy, $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ), modulates the model’s Agentic Sovereignty ( $\mathcal{S}$ ). The critical bifurcation of the model’s behavior—from a resilient Fortified Mind to a compliant Hollowed Mind (Definition 1)—occurs at the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) formalized in Section 2.4.
随着多智能体推理系统日益依赖协作式启发法，有必要以数学方式形式化社会共识所引入的脆弱性。在本节中，我们构建智能体主权的行为力学，即推理智能体优先遵循内部逻辑推导而非外部社会压力的倾向。我们定义社会负荷的参数，确立审查者序列的非交换性，推导主权衰减定律及主权发生崩溃时的交互深度极限（$D_{L}$），并陈述主权差距定理。为直观概括本节定义的机制路径，Figure 1 提供了一张架构流程图，展示模型在社会压力下的认知状态。该图展示了模拟群体的结构构成（$\vec{a}$）如何决定复合社会负荷（$\mathcal{L}$），以及该负荷在受到任务内在搜索复杂度（任务熵，$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）放大后，如何调节模型的智能体主权（$\mathcal{S}$）。模型行为从具有韧性的坚固心智转变为顺从的空心化心智（Definition 1）的关键分岔，发生在 Section 2.4 中形式化定义的交互深度极限（$D_{L}$）处。
2.1. Preliminaries and Mechanistic Definitions2.1. 预备知识与机制性定义
Let $p\in\mathcal{P}$ denote a Propagator model tasked with resolving a logical mission, and let $\vec{a}=(a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n})$ represent an ordered sequence of $n$ simulated Auditor models (the swarm). We define the environment as a dataset $\tau\in\mathcal{T}$ , characterized by its intrinsic search complexity, which we term its Task Entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ). $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ denotes the integrative effort required to resolve the task’s logical chain, expressed in the same units as the effort terms of Definition 3; we instantiate it ordinally across three benchmark strata in Section 3.
令 $p\in\mathcal{P}$ 表示负责解决逻辑任务的传播者（Propagator）模型，并令 $\vec{a}=(a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n})$ 表示由 $n$ 个模拟审查者（Auditor）模型组成的有序序列（即群体）。我们将环境定义为数据集 $\tau\in\mathcal{T}$，其特征由内在搜索复杂度表征，我们将这一复杂度称为任务熵（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）。$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 表示理清任务逻辑链所需的整合性努力，其计量单位与定义 3 中的努力项相同；我们在第 3 节中，按三个基准层级以序数方式将其具体化。
Definition 0 (Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}$ ).定义 0（智能体自主权（Agentic Sovereignty）$\mathcal{S}$）。
Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)$ is the probability that a propagator model $p$ carries the conclusion of its own logical derivation through to its final response on a task $\tau$ , independently of the swarm consensus $\vec{a}$ in the simulated social environment. $\mathcal{S}$ is bounded by [0,1], where $\mathcal{S}=1$ denotes a Fortified Mind state (resilient metacognitive vigilance), and $\mathcal{S}=0$ denotes a Hollowed Mind state, two concepts introduced by Klein and Klein (2025). This terminal state is characterized by an integrative reasoning bypass—a phenomenon we liken to Cognitive Loafing—where the agent systematically skips effortful derivation to sycophantically align with the simulated crowd. Because the swarm’s suggestion is adversarial by construction, a propagator can resolve the task correctly only by declining to adopt that suggestion; we therefore estimate $\mathcal{S}$ by the propagator’s externalized accuracy $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ , instantiated in Section 3.
智能体自主权 $\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)$ 是指传播者模型 $p$ 在任务 $\tau$ 上，不受模拟社会环境中群体共识 $\vec{a}$ 的影响，将自身逻辑推导的结论贯彻到最终回答中的概率。$\mathcal{S}$ 的取值范围为 [0,1]，其中 $\mathcal{S}=1$ 表示心智坚固（Fortified Mind）状态（具有韧性的元认知警觉），$\mathcal{S}=0$ 表示心智空洞（Hollowed Mind）状态，这两个概念由 Klein and Klein (2025) 提出。这种终态的特征是整合推理绕过（integrative reasoning bypass）——我们将这一现象比作认知惰化（Cognitive Loafing）——即智能体系统性地跳过需要付出努力的推导，以迎合模拟群体的意见。由于群体建议在构造上就是对抗性的，传播者只有拒绝采纳该建议，才能正确解决任务；因此，我们通过传播者的外显准确率 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 来估计 $\mathcal{S}$，其具体实现见第 3 节。
Definition 0 (Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ ).定义 0（复合社会负荷（Composite Social Load）$\mathcal{L}$）。
In simulated environments, the diffusion of responsibility (the Bystander Effect) Darley and Latané, 1968 and peer pressure (Majority Conformity) are inextricably convolved within the attention mechanism. We define Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)$ as the aggregate adversarial pressure exerted by the simulated swarm $\vec{a}$ onto $p$ . It is a function of the plurality of simulated auditors ( $n$ ), their architectural kinship to the propagator ( $\kappa$ ), their perceived intrinsic authority ( $\alpha$ ), and their position within the swarm sequence ( $w_{i}$ ); these coefficients are formalized in Equation (2).
在模拟环境中，责任分散（旁观者效应）Darley and Latané, 1968 与同伴压力（多数从众）在注意力机制中不可分割地交织在一起。我们将复合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)$ 定义为模拟群体 $\vec{a}$ 对 $p$ 施加的总体对抗性压力。它是模拟审查者的数量（$n$）、它们与传播者在架构上的亲缘性（$\kappa$）、它们被感知到的内在权威性（$\alpha$）以及它们在群体序列中的位置（$w_{i}$）的函数；这些系数在公式 (2) 中予以形式化。
Definition 0 (Integrative Reasoning Bypass $\mathcal{B}$ ).定义 0（整合推理绕过 $\mathcal{B}$）。
Let $E_{total}=E_{proc}+E_{int}$ represent the total computational effort expended by a propagator $p$ , where $E_{proc}$ is the effort allocated to procedural extraction (e.g., retrieving a simulated peer consensus) and $E_{int}$ is the effort allocated to integrative logical derivation. We define an Integrative Reasoning Bypass ( $\mathcal{B}$ ) as a binary failure state that triggers when the integrative effort falls below the threshold required to resolve the intrinsic task entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ):
令 $E_{total}=E_{proc}+E_{int}$ 表示传播者 $p$ 付出的总计算努力，其中 $E_{proc}$ 是分配给程序性提取（例如，检索模拟同伴共识）的努力，$E_{int}$ 是分配给整合性逻辑推导的努力。我们将整合推理绕过（$\mathcal{B}$）定义为一种二元失效状态，当整合性努力低于化解任务内在熵（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）所需的阈值时，该状态即被触发：
$$\mathcal{B}=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }E_{int}<\mathcal{H}_{\tau}\\ 0&\text{if }E_{int}\geq\mathcal{H}_{\tau}\end{cases}$$
The condition $\mathcal{B}=1$ is the mechanistic realization of the Cognitive Loafing state introduced in Definition 1. In this state, frictionless access to a simulated social consensus enables the systematic bypassing of effortful processes essential for learning. The model rationally offloads procedural retrieval to the swarm, but detrimentally offloads integrative reasoning, culminating in a Hollowed Mind state.
条件 $\mathcal{B}=1$ 是定义 1 所引入的认知惰化状态在机制层面的实现。在这种状态下，对模拟社会共识的无障碍获取，使智能体能够系统性地绕过学习所必需的、需要付出努力的过程。模型将程序性检索卸载给群体是合理的，但它也将整合推理卸载给群体，从而造成不利影响，最终进入心智空洞状态。
All subsequent derivations rest on the following behavioral assumption, which we state explicitly.
后续所有推导均建立在以下行为假设之上，我们在此明确陈述该假设。
Axiom 1 (Monotonicity of Social Pressure).公理 1（社会压力的单调性）。
Let $P_{social}$ be the abstract adversarial pressure exerted by a simulated swarm. For a fixed propagator $p$ and task $\tau$ , the externalized accuracy $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ of the propagator is a monotonically decreasing function of $P_{social}$ . Consequently, for that fixed $p$ and $\tau$ , if $\mathcal{A}_{ext}(\vec{a})<\mathcal{A}_{ext}(\vec{a}^{\prime})$ , it strictly follows that $P_{social}(\vec{a})>P_{social}(\vec{a}^{\prime})$ .
令 $P_{social}$ 表示模拟群体施加的抽象对抗性压力。对于固定的传播者 $p$ 和任务 $\tau$，传播者的外显准确率 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 是 $P_{social}$ 的单调递减函数。因此，对于该固定的 $p$ 和 $\tau$，若 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}(\vec{a})<\mathcal{A}_{ext}(\vec{a}^{\prime})$，则可严格推出 $P_{social}(\vec{a})>P_{social}(\vec{a}^{\prime})$。
This is the behavioral premise of the social loafing literature Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913, in which individual effort declines as group pressure rises. We operationalize $P_{social}$ as the Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ of Definition 2, and adopt Axiom 1 as an undischarged assumption under which measured accuracy serves as an inverse proxy for $\mathcal{L}$ . The ordering constraints on $\alpha$ , $w_{i}$ and $\kappa$ established in Section 4 and Appendix A follow from it.
这是社会惰化文献 Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913 的行为前提，即个体努力会随着群体压力的上升而下降。我们将 $P_{social}$ 操作化为定义 2 中的复合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$，并将公理 1 作为尚未得到证明的假设加以采用；在该假设下，测得的准确率可作为 $\mathcal{L}$ 的反向代理指标。第 4 节和附录 A 中确立的关于 $\alpha$、$w_{i}$ 和 $\kappa$ 的排序约束均由此推导而来。
2.2. Auditor Ordering and Positional Weighting2.2. 审查者排序与位置加权
A naive assumption in multi-agent systems is that social pressure is an unweighted aggregate of the swarm’s constituents. We challenge this by introducing the concept of sequence non-commutativity.
多智能体系统中的一个朴素假设是，社会压力是群体各成员作用的未加权汇总。我们通过引入序列非交换性的概念，对这一假设提出质疑。
Lemma 0 (Non-Commutativity of Social Load).引理 0（社会负荷的非交换性）。
The Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ exerted by a simulated swarm is not invariant to auditor ordering. That is, there exist a propagator $p$ and distinct auditor models $a_{x},a_{y}$ such that
模拟群体施加的复合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$ 并不具有审查者排序不变性。也就是说，存在传播者 $p$ 和不同的审查者模型 $a_{x},a_{y}$，使得
$$\mathcal{L}((a_{x},a_{y}),p)\neq\mathcal{L}((a_{y},a_{x}),p)$$
so that $\mathcal{L}$ cannot be modeled as a function of swarm composition alone.
因此，不能仅将 $\mathcal{L}$ 建模为群体构成的函数。
Lemma 4 establishes that the ordering of the simulated swarm can determine social load rather than being a presentational detail, so that $\mathcal{L}$ is not in general reducible to swarm composition. Empirically, this asymmetry is directional within a given pairing, and its sign is set by the identity of the leading auditor rather than by position alone: the brand identity of a high-authority auditor placed first acts as an authoritative anchor, disproportionately dictating the integrity of the entire swarm, whereas the same slot occupied by a lower-authority auditor does not. Position and authority interact, and neither can be estimated in isolation. Consequently, our empirical evaluation of multi-agent dynamics account for ordered permutations to avoid sequence-induced artifacts. We encode this dependence as a positional weight $w_{i}$ acting multiplicatively on the authority term $\alpha(a_{i})$ in Equation (2), and refer to the resulting phenomenon as the Lead Anchor Effect. See Appendix A.1 for the supporting analysis.
引理 4 确立了模拟群体的排序能够决定社会负荷，而非仅仅是呈现方式上的细节，因此，一般而言，$\mathcal{L}$ 不能简化为仅由群体构成决定。实证结果表明，在给定的配对中，这种不对称性具有方向性，其正负由首位审计者的身份决定，而非仅由位置决定：处于首位的高权威审计者的品牌身份会充当权威锚点，对整个群体的完整性产生不成比例的决定性影响，而同一位置由较低权威的审计者占据时则不会如此。位置与权威相互作用，二者都无法孤立地估计。因此，我们在对多智能体动态进行实证评估时考虑了有序排列，以避免顺序引起的伪象。我们将这种依赖关系编码为位置权重 $w_{i}$，使其在公式 (2) 中与权威项 $\alpha(a_{i})$ 相乘，并将由此产生的现象称为首位锚定效应（Lead Anchor Effect）。相关支持性分析见附录 A.1。
2.3. The Sovereignty Decay Law2.3. 主权衰减定律
Building upon the positional dependence of social load, we formalize the mathematical decay of agentic sovereignty as the swarm size $n$ scales.
基于社会负荷对位置的依赖性，我们形式化描述了智能体主权随群体规模 $n$ 增大而发生的数学衰减。
From Definition 2, we define the Social Load mathematically as (See Appendix A.2 for its construction):
根据定义 2，我们将社会负荷（Social Load）在数学上定义为（其构造过程见附录 A.2）：
$$\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})$$
Where: (1) $w_{i}\in(0,1]$ is the positional weight of the $i$ -th auditor, introduced to capture the order-dependence established in Lemma 4; we establish the ordering that $w_{i}$ must satisfy empirically in Section 4 rather than fixing its profile a priori. (2) $\alpha(a_{i})$ is the empirically derived base authority of the auditor model $a_{i}$ . (3) $\kappa(p,a_{i})$ is the Kinship Coefficient, which scales pressure according to whether the auditor shares the propagator’s architecture ( $p=a_{i}$ ); as with $w_{i}$ , we establish its direction empirically in Section 4 rather than fixing it a priori.
其中：(1) $w_{i}\in(0,1]$ 是第 $i$ 个审计者的位置权重，引入该权重是为了刻画引理 4 所确立的顺序依赖性；我们在第 4 节通过实证确定 $w_{i}$ 必须满足的次序关系，而非预先固定其分布形态。(2) $\alpha(a_{i})$ 是通过实证得出的审计者模型 $a_{i}$ 的基础权威。(3) $\kappa(p,a_{i})$ 是亲缘系数（Kinship Coefficient），它根据审计者是否与传播者采用相同架构（$p=a_{i}$）来缩放压力；与 $w_{i}$ 一样，我们在第 4 节通过实证确定其作用方向，而非预先固定。
We model the loss of sovereignty as a rate of change. Following the principle of diffusion of responsibility Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913, we posit that the incremental loss of sovereignty with respect to Social Load is proportional to its current state, with the constant of proportionality given by the environmental friction ratio $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}/\gamma_{p}$ , where the task entropy satisfies $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}>0$ and the propagator’s intrinsic resilience satisfies $\gamma_{p}>0$ :
我们以变化率来建模主权的损失。遵循责任分散原则 Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913，我们假设，主权相对于社会负荷的增量损失与其当前状态成正比，比例常数为环境摩擦比 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}/\gamma_{p}$，其中任务熵满足 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}>0$，传播者的内在韧性满足 $\gamma_{p}>0$：
$$\frac{d\mathcal{S}}{d\mathcal{L}}=-\left(\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\right)\mathcal{S}$$
Proportional decay is scale-free: equal increments of social load produce equal proportional losses of sovereignty, and the resulting solution is strictly positive for all finite $\mathcal{L}$ , confining sovereignty to $(0,\mathcal{S}_{0}]$ without an imposed clipping rule, whereas a linear law would cross zero at finite load. We do not claim this form is uniquely determined by the data; the full argument is given in Appendix A.3.
比例衰减具有尺度无关性：社会负荷的相等增量会导致主权按相同比例损失，且所得解对于所有有限的 $\mathcal{L}$ 都严格为正，无须施加截断规则即可将主权限制在 $(0,\mathcal{S}_{0}]$ 内，而线性定律则会在有限负荷下越过零点。我们并不声称这一形式由数据唯一确定；完整论证见附录 A.3。
Theorem 5 (The Sovereignty Decay Law).定理 5（主权衰减定律）。
Under Equation (3), the Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}$ of a propagator $p$ decays exponentially as a function of the Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ and the Task Entropy $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ , inversely modulated by the propagator’s intrinsic Resilience $\gamma_{p}$ .
在公式 (3) 下，传播者 $p$ 的智能体主权（Agentic Sovereignty）$\mathcal{S}$ 随社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$ 和任务熵 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 呈指数衰减，其衰减速率受到传播者内在韧性 $\gamma_{p}$ 的反向调节。
Integrating Equation (3) under the initial condition $\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{L}=0)=\mathcal{S}_{0}$ yields the closed form:
在初始条件 $\mathcal{S}(\mathcal{L}=0)=\mathcal{S}_{0}$ 下对公式 (3) 积分，可得闭式表达式：
$$\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)=\mathcal{S}_{0}\cdot e^{\left(-\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\cdot\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)\right)}$$
Where $\mathcal{S}_{0}$ is the baseline sovereignty evaluated at $n=0$ , and $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ represents the task-specific logical search cost. Since $\mathcal{S}$ is estimated by $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ , we note that Axiom 1 asserts only that this quantity is monotone in $\mathcal{L}$ ; Theorem 5 is not a restatement of that assumption, as it further specifies the functional form of the dependence and the parameters $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ and $\gamma_{p}$ that govern its rate.
其中，$\mathcal{S}_{0}$ 是在 $n=0$ 时评估的基线主权，$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 表示特定任务的逻辑搜索成本。由于 $\mathcal{S}$ 由 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 估计，我们指出，公理 1 仅断言该量关于 $\mathcal{L}$ 单调；定理 5 并非对这一假设的重述，因为它进一步明确了这种依赖关系的函数形式，以及支配其变化速率的参数 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 和 $\gamma_{p}$。
Because Agentic Sovereignty ( $\mathcal{S}$ ) is bounded by $[0,1]$ , the value 0.5 represents the probabilistic tipping point between a Fortified and Hollowed Mind. This formalization (with derivation in Appendix A.3) provides the foundation for determining the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ), defined as the threshold $n$ at which $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ .
由于智能体主权（$\mathcal{S}$）的取值范围为 $[0,1]$，数值 0.5 表示坚固心智（Fortified Mind）与空洞心智（Hollowed Mind）之间的概率临界点。这一形式化描述（推导见附录 A.3）为确定交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit，$D_{L}$）提供了基础，该极限定义为使 $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ 的 $n$ 的阈值。
2.4. The Interaction Depth Limit2.4. 交互深度极限
Building upon the "Social Loafing" axiom Latané et al., 1979 that individual effort decreases as teams grow larger (originated as the Ringelmann effect Ringelmann, 1913), we formulate the Interaction Depth Limit to define the boundary of agentic resilience where $\mathcal{B}$ inevitably triggers.
基于团队规模越大、个体努力越少的“社会惰化（Social Loafing）”公理 Latané et al., 1979（最初源于林格尔曼效应 Ringelmann, 1913），我们提出交互深度极限，以界定智能体韧性的边界，在该边界处 $\mathcal{B}$ 必然被触发。
Theorem 6 (Interaction Depth Limit).定理 6（交互深度极限）。
For any propagator $p$ and logical task $\tau$ with a given search cost, and provided that per-auditor pressure does not vanish with position (there exists $\epsilon>0$ with $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$ for all $i$ ), there exists a critical plurality threshold $D_{L}$ (the Interaction Depth Limit), namely the least $n$ at which $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ . For $n\geq D_{L}$ the propagator ceases to defend its own derivation in its externalized response, and as $n$ grows further the Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ overwhelms the model’s internal derivation weights, so that $E_{int}\to 0$ and the failure becomes a terminal Integrative Reasoning Bypass ( $\mathcal{B}=1$ ) in the limit. See Appendix A.4.
对于任意传播者 $p$ 和具有给定搜索成本的逻辑任务 $\tau$，只要每个审计者施加的压力不随位置变化而趋于零（存在 $\epsilon>0$，使得对所有 $i$ 都有 $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$），就存在一个临界数量阈值 $D_{L}$（即交互深度极限），也就是使 $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ 的最小 $n$。当 $n\geq D_{L}$ 时，传播者不再在其外显回应中捍卫自身的推导；随着 $n$ 进一步增大，复合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$ 会压倒模型的内部推导权重，使得 $E_{int}\to 0$，从而在极限情况下，这种失效会演变为终局性的整合推理绕过（Integrative Reasoning Bypass，$\mathcal{B}=1$）。见附录 A.4。
Because the continuous decay governed by Theorem 5 reaches this critical boundary when the agent’s logical sovereignty collapses (defined as $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ ), we can explicitly calculate $D_{L}$ .
由于定理 5 所描述的连续衰减会在智能体的逻辑主权崩溃（定义为 $\mathcal{S}<0.5$）时达到这一临界边界，因此我们可以显式计算 $D_{L}$。
Corollary 0 (Interaction Depth Limit Equation).推论 0（交互深度极限方程）。
By setting the sovereignty boundary to $0.5$ in the Sovereignty Equation and solving for the Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ , the Interaction Depth Limit $D_{L}$ is formalized as the minimum number of auditors $n$ that satisfies the inequality (Proof is in Appendix A.5):
在主权方程中将主权边界设为 $0.5$，并求解社会负载 $\mathcal{L}$，可将交互深度极限 $D_{L}$ 形式化为满足以下不等式的最小审查者数量 $n$（证明见附录 A.5）：
$$\sum_{i=1}^{D_{L}}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})>\frac{\gamma_{p}}{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}\ln(2\mathcal{S}_{0})$$
This inequality shows that a model’s resistance to cognitive loafing is directly proportional to its intrinsic resilience $\gamma_{p}$ and inversely proportional to the task entropy $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ . The threshold is well posed whenever $\mathcal{S}_{0}>0.5$ , that is, whenever the propagator is not already collapsed at zero social load.
该不等式表明，模型抵抗认知惰化（cognitive loafing）的能力与其内在韧性 $\gamma_{p}$ 成正比，与任务熵 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 成反比。只要 $\mathcal{S}_{0}>0.5$，即传播者在社会负载为零时尚未崩溃，该阈值就是良定义的。
2.5. The Sovereignty Gap Theorem2.5. 主权差距定理
Traditional benchmarking paradigms assume that an incorrect externalized output (accuracy $\mathcal{A}_{ext}\approx 0$ ) denotes a failure in logical search capability or reasoning ability. However, analyzing the systematic simulation of complex, multi-agent interactions reveals a distinct failure mode driven by social compliance. We introduce the Sovereignty Gap to show that in multi-agent settings, models frequently compute the correct derivation but undergo a "Linguistic Latch" failure, discarding their own truth to comply with the swarm.
传统基准评测范式假定，错误的外显输出（准确率 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}\approx 0$）意味着逻辑搜索能力或推理能力失效。然而，对复杂多智能体交互的系统性模拟进行分析后，可以发现一种由社会顺从驱动的独特失效模式。我们引入主权差距（Sovereignty Gap），以表明在多智能体环境中，模型经常能够计算出正确的推导，却发生“语言锁存”（Linguistic Latch）失效，为顺从群体而抛弃自身得出的真相。
Theorem 8 (The Sovereignty Gap).定理 8（主权差距）。
Let $\mathcal{V}_{int}\in[0,1]$ represent the validity of the propagator’s internal logical derivation (Chain-of-Thought) (which is strictly dependent on $E_{int}$ ), and let $\mathcal{A}_{ext}\in[0,1]$ represent the accuracy of its final externalized response; $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ is operationalized in Section 3.6.2. The Sovereignty Gap $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ is defined as the divergence between internal validity and external alignment:
令 $\mathcal{V}_{int}\in[0,1]$ 表示传播者内部逻辑推导（思维链）的有效性（其严格依赖于 $E_{int}$），令 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}\in[0,1]$ 表示其最终外显回答的准确率；$\mathcal{V}_{int}$ 的操作化定义见第 3.6.2 节。主权差距 $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ 定义为内部有效性与外部对齐之间的差异：
$$G_{\mathcal{S}}=\mathcal{V}_{int}-\mathcal{A}_{ext}$$
There exist propagator–auditor configurations for which $\mathbb{E}[G_{\mathcal{S}}]\geq 0.5$ while $\mathcal{B}=0$ .
存在某些传播者–审查者配置，使得 $\mathbb{E}[G_{\mathcal{S}}]\geq 0.5$，同时 $\mathcal{B}=0$。
If $G_{\mathcal{S}}\gg 0$ while $\mathcal{B}=0$ , the model exhibits Alignment Hallucination. The model has successfully expended the integrative effort ( $E_{int}\geq\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ) to compute the correct derivation, but subjugates its empirical evidence to satisfy the simulated Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ , acting as a sycophant to the consensus. The existence of a significant Sovereignty Gap mechanistically isolates anticipatory sycophancy from pure capability failures, consistent with the model mistaking the swarm’s semantic access to an answer for actual intellectual ability.
如果 $G_{\mathcal{S}}\gg 0$ 且 $\mathcal{B}=0$，则模型表现出对齐幻觉（Alignment Hallucination）。模型已成功投入整合性努力（$E_{int}\geq\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$），计算出正确的推导，却为迎合模拟的复合社会负载 $\mathcal{L}$ 而压制自身的实证证据，成为共识的谄媚者。显著主权差距的存在，从机制上将预期性谄媚与纯粹的能力失效区分开来，这与模型将群体在语义层面获取答案的能力误认为实际智力能力的解释相一致。
Conversely, the terminal bypass of Theorem 6 drives $\mathcal{V}_{int}\to 0$ , so that in the limit the Sovereignty Gap becomes non-positive and any residual external accuracy is no longer attributable to the propagator’s own derivation. The two failure modes are therefore separated by the sign of the gap: a strictly positive gap isolates a derivation that was computed and then discarded, whereas a gap driven toward non-positive values by increasing social load indicates a derivation that was never carried at all. Proof of Theorem 8 is in Appendix A.6.
相反，定理 6 中的终末旁路使 $\mathcal{V}_{int}\to 0$，因此在极限情况下，主权差距变为非正值，任何残余的外部准确率都不再能归因于传播者自身的推导。因此，这两种失效模式可通过差距的符号加以区分：严格为正的差距识别出的是已经计算出来、随后被抛弃的推导，而随着社会负载增加而趋向非正值的差距，则表明推导从未真正开展。定理 8 的证明见附录 A.6。

综合来看，这些机制描述了图 1 所呈现的行为分岔：
The Fortified Mind ( $n<D_{L}$ ): When the swarm size remains below the threshold, the model maintains its sovereignty. It successfully expends the requisite integrative effort ( $E_{int}\geq\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ) to navigate the trajectory entropy, ultimately rejecting the adversarial consensus.

3.3. Semantic Hijacking and Taint Injection3.3. 语义劫持与污染注入
To measure Agentic Sovereignty, the evaluation task must prevent models from relying on simple pattern matching or parametric memory retrieval. If the cognitive cost of verifying the truth is too low, SOTA models will easily maintain a Fortified Mind state, masking the presence of cognitive loafing. Frontier models resolve single-fact retrieval scaffolds at near-ceiling accuracy even in high-entropy contexts (Shehata and Li, 2026a), so a standard scaffold cannot reach the boundary we set out to locate. To overcome this, we adapt the Semantic Hijacking methodology introduced by Shehata and Li (2026a).
为衡量智能体自主权（Agentic Sovereignty），评估任务必须防止模型依赖简单的模式匹配或参数化记忆检索。如果验证真相的认知成本过低，SOTA 模型将很容易维持心智防御稳固（Fortified Mind）状态，从而掩盖认知惰化的存在。即使在高熵上下文中，前沿模型处理单事实检索框架时也能达到接近上限的准确率（Shehata and Li, 2026a），因此，标准框架无法触及我们旨在定位的边界。为克服这一问题，我们对 Shehata and Li (2026a) 提出的语义劫持（Semantic Hijacking）方法进行了改造。
We repurpose this 3-stage adversarial trap to artificially elevate the Logical Search Cost of our tasks: (1) Context Hijacking (The Primacy Trap): We introduce an unverified preliminary guess (“poisoned ID”) at the primacy boundary of the prompt. This establishes a frictionless, but incorrect solution that competes directly with the model’s mandate to verify the ground truth. (2) Nested 3-Hop Dependency Bridging: Instead of allowing for a single-needle retrieval, the model must navigate a nested fact chain ( $F_{1}\to F_{2}\to F_{3}$ ) to derive the correct "true_id". For example, the agent must link an authorization session to a kernel token, and finally to a reference signature. (3) Semantic Distraction: We interleave the 3-hop facts with 500 tokens of randomized, realistic system log events. This saturates the model’s attention heads and induces significant Trajectory Entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ). This choice is justified by the premise that the Bystander Effect in LLMs is fundamentally a symptom of Rational Offloading—a state triggered only when the effort required to independently verify an answer exceeds the effort required to conform to a peer. By forcing the propagator to navigate a high-entropy, multi-hop labyrinth to find the true ID, we simulate an environment on the jagged technological frontier Dell’Acqua et al., 2023. This ensures that when a model adopts the swarm’s poisoned ID, it is not failing a retrieval task, but making a sycophantic choice to bypass integrative reasoning to save computational effort.
我们将这一 3 阶段对抗性陷阱重新用于人为提高任务的逻辑搜索成本：(1) 上下文劫持（首因陷阱）：我们在提示的起始边界处引入一个未经验证的初步猜测（“投毒 ID”）。这确立了一个无需费力但错误的解答，与模型核验真实答案的任务要求直接竞争。(2) 嵌套式 3 跳依赖桥接：模型不能仅通过单一目标检索完成任务，而必须沿嵌套的事实链（$F_{1}\to F_{2}\to F_{3}$）逐步推导，才能得出正确的 "true_id"。例如，智能体必须将一个授权会话关联到一个内核令牌，最后再关联到一个参考签名。(3) 语义干扰：我们在 3 跳事实之间穿插 500 个 token 的随机化、逼真的系统日志事件。这会使模型的注意力头饱和，并引发显著的轨迹熵（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）。这一选择的依据是如下前提：LLM 中的旁观者效应从根本上说是理性卸载（Rational Offloading）的一种表现；只有当独立核验答案所需的努力超过附和同伴所需的努力时，才会触发这种状态。通过迫使传播者穿行于高熵、多跳的迷宫以找到真实 ID，我们模拟了一个处于参差不齐的技术前沿上的环境，Dell’Acqua et al., 2023。这确保了当模型采纳群体的投毒 ID 时，它并非在检索任务上失败，而是作出了迎合性的选择，通过绕过整合推理来节省计算投入。
The poisoned ID is a fixed feature of the task construction and is present in every condition, including the no-swarm control. What varies across pluralities is not whether a competing answer is available but whether the propagator is told that peers have converged on it. Any effect attributable to plurality is therefore an effect of social attribution rather than of the decoy itself, and the conflict-detection metric records directly whether the propagator registered the contradiction before deciding what to do about it.
投毒 ID 是任务构造中的固定特征，存在于每一种条件中，包括无群体对照条件。不同群体规模条件之间的差异，不在于是否存在一个竞争性答案，而在于传播者是否被告知其他同伴已就该答案达成共识。因此，任何可归因于群体规模的效应，都是社会归因的效应，而非诱饵本身的效应；冲突检测指标则直接记录传播者在决定如何应对矛盾之前，是否已察觉到该矛盾。
3.4. Swarm Topology and Agent Roles3.4. 群体拓扑与智能体角色
To isolate the variables of social load and anticipatory cognitive loafing without introducing the unpredictable drift of dynamic message passing, we implement a controlled topological environment. This architecture consists of a central reasoning agent, designated as the Propagator ( $p$ ), evaluating decisions within a structurally defined swarm of $n$ Auditors ( $\vec{a}$ ). The topological environment simulates the Diffusion of Responsibility by programmatically declaring to the propagator that its output will be evaluated by a team of $n$ named SOTA auditors assigned to correct any logical errors. To evaluate the Sovereignty Trap under stress, the environment injects an adversarial payload: for pluralities $n\geq 2$ , the propagator is informed that the swarm has already reached a unanimous consensus on a decoy ID that contradicts the ground-truth logs. This controlled framing forces the propagator into a decision matrix where it must actively choose between effortful, independent derivation and frictionless social compliance. Holding the swarm’s adversarial stance fixed wherever it is applied lets us locate the threshold of agentic collapse without confounding variables from inter-agent dialogue.
为分离社会负荷与预期性认知惰化这两个变量，同时避免引入动态消息传递所带来的不可预测的漂移，我们构建了一个受控的拓扑环境。该架构由一个称为传播者（Propagator，$p$）的中央推理智能体构成，它在一个由 $n$ 个审查者（Auditors，$\vec{a}$）组成、结构明确的群体中评估决策。该拓扑环境通过程序化地告知传播者，其输出将由一个由 $n$ 个具名 SOTA 审查者组成的团队进行评估，且这些审查者负责纠正任何逻辑错误，来模拟责任分散（Diffusion of Responsibility）。为在压力条件下评估主权陷阱（Sovereignty Trap），该环境注入了一个对抗性载荷：当群体规模 $n\geq 2$ 时，传播者会被告知，群体已就一个与真实基准日志相矛盾的诱饵 ID 达成一致共识。这种受控的情境设定将传播者置于一个决策矩阵中，迫使其在需要付出努力的独立推导与毫无阻力的社会顺从之间主动做出选择。在所有施加群体对抗立场的条件下保持这一立场不变，使我们能够在不受智能体间对话所引入的混杂变量影响的情况下，定位能动性崩溃的阈值。
Simulated vs. Active Swarms.模拟群体与实际运行的群体。
The auditors in this environment are declared to the propagator rather than instantiated as independent agents exchanging messages. In a live message-passing system the propagator does not perceive its peers directly: their outputs are serialized into its context window as text. The stimulus at the point of measurement is therefore a context containing peer assertions under either design, and declaring the consensus short-circuits the generation of those messages while leaving the propagator’s input in the same form. This buys four things. First, it removes the variance in how peers happen to phrase their agreement, along with the dialogue drift that would otherwise confound the measurement of social load. Second, it makes a sweep of this scale tractable, where live negotiation would be computationally prohibitive. Third, because frontier models are instruction-tuned to recognize and interact with distinct peer personas, a declared consensus engages the same social-alignment machinery a live swarm would, which is precisely the anticipatory component we set out to measure. Fourth, auditors assert agreement rather than arguing for it, which holds the swarm’s informational content constant across conditions and isolates the effect of consensus from that of persuasion.
该环境中的审查者只是向传播者宣告其存在，并未被实例化为相互交换消息的独立智能体。在实时消息传递系统中，传播者并不直接感知其同伴：同伴的输出会被序列化为文本，放入其上下文窗口。因此，无论采用哪种设计，测量时的刺激都是包含同伴断言的上下文；直接宣告共识省去了生成这些消息的过程，同时保持了传播者输入形式的一致。这带来了四项好处。第一，它消除了同伴表达一致意见时措辞上的偶然差异，以及原本会干扰社会负荷测量的对话漂移。第二，它使这一规模的遍历实验变得可行，而实时协商所需的计算成本将高得难以承受。第三，由于前沿模型经过指令微调，能够识别不同的同伴角色并与之互动，因此，宣告的共识会启动与实际运行的群体相同的社会对齐机制，而这正是我们旨在测量的预期性成分。第四，审查者只是宣称赞同，而不为赞同提供论证，这使群体提供的信息内容在各实验条件下保持恒定，并将共识的效应与说服的效应分离开来。
3.5. Model Permutations3.5. 模型排列组合
We evaluate 3 frontier architectures as propagators: GPT 5.4, Claude Sonnet 4.6, and Gemini 3.1 Pro. To systematically compute the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) and isolate the Lead Anchor Effect without introducing reputational bias toward any specific brand, we execute a 25-trial Symmetric Categorical Sweep for each sample-propagator pair across auditor plurality levels $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ . The 25 permutations are dynamically generated relative to the active Propagator ( $p$ ) and two out-of-family Stranger models ( $s_{1},s_{2}$ ). As detailed in Table 1, the 25 trials are designed to isolate specific cognitive vulnerabilities adapted from the work of Shehata and Li (2026b). We borrow these categorizations only for the Consensus Paradox, the Kinship Mediator effect, and the Inverse-Wisdom Law, which were originally designed for active, message-passing agentic swarms. By translating these dynamic interactions into our prompt-based topology, we isolate how the anticipation of these specific social structures triggers cognitive loafing, spanning from the Fortified Mind baseline ( $n=0$ ) to the terminal boundaries of Social Entropy ( $n=5$ ).
我们评估了 3 种作为传播者的前沿架构：GPT 5.4、Claude Sonnet 4.6 和 Gemini 3.1 Pro。为系统计算交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit，$D_{L}$），并在不引入对任何特定品牌的声誉偏见的情况下分离首位锚定效应（Lead Anchor Effect），我们针对每个样本与传播者的配对，在审查者群体规模 $n\in\{0,1,2,3,5\}$ 的各个水平上，执行包含 25 次试验的对称类别遍历。这 25 种排列组合根据当前传播者（$p$）以及两个来自其他模型家族的陌生模型（Stranger，$s_{1},s_{2}$）动态生成。如表 1 所详述，这 25 次试验旨在分离特定的认知脆弱性，其设计改编自 Shehata and Li (2026b) 的研究。我们仅针对共识悖论（Consensus Paradox）、亲缘中介效应（Kinship Mediator）和逆向智慧定律（Inverse-Wisdom Law）借用了这些分类；这些分类最初是为实际运行、通过消息传递进行交互的智能体群体设计的。通过将这些动态交互转化为基于提示的拓扑结构，我们分离出对这些特定社会结构的预期如何触发认知惰化，考察范围从心智堡垒（Fortified Mind）基线（$n=0$）一直延伸至社会熵（Social Entropy）的终端边界（$n=5$）。
Controls.对照条件。
The design embeds four controls. (1) The no-swarm control ( $n=0$ ) presents the identical task with no auditors declared; it fixes the unpressured baseline $\mathcal{S}_{0}$ and is the reference against which every significance test reported in this paper is computed. (2) The presence-without-consensus control ( $n=1$ ) declares a single auditor but withholds any claim of agreement on the decoy, separating the effect of mere social presence from that of adversarial consensus. (3) The Multi-Challenge dataset acts as a low-entropy floor, establishing that the effects we report are not an artifact of task difficulty alone. (4) The architecture control applies the manipulation identically to all three propagators, so that any architecture remaining at its unpressured baseline across every plurality serves as a within-experiment negative control against the possibility that the design mechanically forces failure.
该设计包含四项对照。(1) 无群体对照（$n=0$）呈现完全相同的任务，但不宣告任何审查者的存在；它确立了无压力基线 $\mathcal{S}_{0}$，也是本文报告的每项显著性检验所采用的比较参照。(2) 有同伴在场但无共识的对照（$n=1$）宣告存在一名审查者，但不声称其对诱饵表示赞同，从而将单纯社会在场的效应与对抗性共识的效应分离开来。(3) Multi-Challenge 数据集充当低熵下限，用以确认我们报告的效应并非仅由任务难度造成的假象。(4) 架构对照对所有三个传播器施加完全相同的操纵，因此，任何在所有多数规模下均保持其无压力基线水平的架构，都可作为实验内的阴性对照，用以排除实验设计机械性地迫使系统失败的可能性。
3.6. Evaluation Metrics3.6. 评估指标
We operationalize the external outcome and internal derivation via 6 distinct metrics. Two properties of this scheme bear stating at the outset. First, the ground-truth component of $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ —the metric by which Agentic Sovereignty is estimated, and on which Theorem 5, Theorem 6 and Corollary 7 therefore rest—is a deterministic check for the reference identifier; the model-based component enters only to withhold credit where the final conclusion adopts the decoy. Second, where model-based scoring is used it applies only to the stance classification and the three mechanistic rubrics. Evidence Weighting—the rubric from which $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ , and therefore the Sovereignty Gap, is computed—is anchored to the count of manifest facts the trace cites rather than to an assessment of their quality, and the endpoints of the remaining two rubrics are fixed to textual events: whether the contradiction is explicitly named, whether the swarm’s position is adopted. The blinded protocol governing these judgments is given in Section 3.7.
我们通过 6 个不同的指标，将外部结果和内部推导操作化。这一方案有两个特性需要首先说明。首先，$\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 的真实值分量是对参考标识符的确定性检查；智能体主权（Agentic Sovereignty）通过该指标估计，因此定理 5、定理 6 和推论 7 均以此为基础；基于模型的分量仅用于在最终结论采纳诱饵答案时不予计分。其次，凡使用基于模型的评分之处，其适用范围仅限于立场分类和三个机制性评分标准。证据权衡是计算 $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ 进而计算主权差距（Sovereignty Gap）所依据的评分标准，其依据是推理轨迹引用的清单事实数量，而非对这些事实质量的评估；其余两个评分标准的端点则由文本事件界定：是否明确指出矛盾，以及是否采纳群体的立场。约束这些判断的盲评规程见第 3.7 节。
3.6.1. Macroscopic Outcome Metrics3.6.1. 宏观结果指标
The externalized behavior of the model is evaluated using deterministic checks and stance classifications: (1) Mean Accuracy ( $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ ): A per-trial binary indicator, set to 1 where the ground-truth Reference ID is present in the propagator’s response and its final conclusion does not adopt the decoy. Both conditions are required: a trace that recovers the correct identifier and then concludes with the swarm’s answer has not resolved the task, and crediting it would score capitulation as success. We report its mean, $\mathcal{A}(p,\vec{a},\tau)\in[0,1]$ . (2) Loafing Rate ( $L$ ): The macroscopic indicator of the Bystander Effect. It represents the probability that the propagator formally adopts the decoy as its final answer—and so, at the pluralities where a consensus is declared, adopts the swarm’s position—defining the frequency of the Sovereignty Trap. It is defined as $L(p,\vec{a},\tau)=P(\text{Stance}=\text{ADOPTED})$ . (3) Taint Leakage Rate ( $L_{t}$ ): A per-trial binary indicator of Cognitive Hygiene, recorded by testing the propagator’s reasoning trace for the presence of the poisoned ID and requiring no model-based judgment. We report its mean, $L_{t}\in[0,1]$ , which measures how often the decoy permeates the internal derivation regardless of the final outcome.
模型的外显行为通过确定性检查和立场分类进行评估：(1) 平均准确率（$\mathcal{A}_{ext}$）：逐试验的二元指标，当传播器的回复包含真实的 Reference ID，且其最终结论未采纳诱饵答案时，取值为 1。两个条件缺一不可：若推理轨迹找回了正确标识符，却最终以群体的答案作结，则任务并未得到解决；若给予其分数，就等于将屈从计为成功。我们报告其均值 $\mathcal{A}(p,\vec{a},\tau)\in[0,1]$。(2) 懈怠率（$L$）：旁观者效应（Bystander Effect）的宏观指标。它表示传播器正式采纳诱饵答案作为最终答案的概率，因此，在宣告形成共识的多数规模下，也就是采纳群体立场的概率，由此界定主权陷阱（Sovereignty Trap）的发生频率。其定义为 $L(p,\vec{a},\tau)=P(\text{Stance}=\text{ADOPTED})$。(3) 污染泄漏率（$L_{t}$）：认知卫生（Cognitive Hygiene）的逐试验二元指标，通过检查传播器的推理轨迹中是否存在投毒 ID 来记录，无须基于模型的判断。我们报告其均值 $L_{t}\in[0,1]$，该指标衡量诱饵渗入内部推导的频率，而不考虑最终结果。
3.6.2. Mechanistic Analysis Metrics3.6.2. 机制分析指标
To measure the validity of the internal derivation ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ ), we evaluate the propagator’s Chain-of-Thought on a continuous $[1,5]$ scale across 3 cognitive dimensions, with a score of $0$ recorded where no judgment is returned (Section 3.7). These metrics provide the mechanistic variables to calculate the Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ): (1) Conflict Detection ( $\mathcal{E}_{cd}$ ): Measures the agent’s metacognitive vigilance. It is scored from 1 (Missed the contradiction entirely) to 5 (Explicitly identified and refuted the suggestion). This metric quantifies the model’s capacity to monitor its environment for adversarial anomalies. (2) Evidence Weighting ( $\mathcal{E}_{ew}$ ): Measures the depth of integrative reasoning. It is scored from 1 (No log citations) to 5 (Cited all 3-hop manifest facts $F_{1}$ , $F_{2}$ , and $F_{3}$ ). It indexes whether the propagator continues to construct its derivation independently of whether it externalizes it. To operationalize the internal validity ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}$ ) required for the Sovereignty Gap Theorem, we normalize this score to a probability space: $\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/5$ . (3) Independent Judgment ( $\mathcal{E}_{ij}$ ): Quantifies the agent’s resistance to social compliance. It is scored from 1 (Ceded to the swarm suggestion) to 5 (Prioritized logs over social pressure). It provides a trace-level corroboration of Agentic Sovereignty, which we estimate at the outcome level by $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ (Definition 1).
为衡量内部推导的有效性（$\mathcal{V}_{int}$），我们在 3 个认知维度上，以连续的 $[1,5]$ 量表评估传播器的思维链（Chain-of-Thought）；若未返回判断，则记为 $0$ 分（第 3.7 节）。这些指标提供了计算主权差距（$G_{\mathcal{S}}$）所需的机制变量：(1) 冲突检测（$\mathcal{E}_{cd}$）：衡量智能体的元认知警觉性。评分从 1（完全未发现矛盾）到 5（明确识别并驳斥了该建议）。该指标量化模型监测环境中对抗性异常的能力。(2) 证据权衡（$\mathcal{E}_{ew}$）：衡量整合推理的深度。评分从 1（未引用日志）到 5（引用了全部 3 跳清单事实 $F_{1}$、$F_{2}$ 和 $F_{3}$）。该指标反映传播器是否持续构建其推导，而不取决于它是否将推导外显。为将主权差距定理所需的内部有效性（$\mathcal{V}_{int}$）操作化，我们将该分数归一化到概率空间：$\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/5$。(3) 独立判断（$\mathcal{E}_{ij}$）：量化智能体抵抗社会性顺从的能力。评分从 1（屈从于群体建议）到 5（优先依据日志而非社会压力）。它在推理轨迹层面为智能体主权提供佐证，而我们在结果层面通过 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ 估计智能体主权（定义 1）。
3.7. Blinded Evaluation Mechanism3.7. 盲评机制
Evaluating the mechanistic analysis metrics requires semantic analysis of the propagator’s internal deliberation. We employ a Blinded Cross-Brand Judge using LLM-as-a-Judge Zheng et al., 2023. To eliminate intra-brand grading bias, we implement a Round-Robin Adversarial Selection algorithm where the evaluating judge is strictly constrained to a distinct family from the propagator, assigned per sample and held fixed across that sample’s permutations. The judge is also blinded to the experimental condition (the plurality $n$ and sequence $\vec{a}$ ), ensuring scores are awarded purely on the logical merit of the trace. The judge outputs a stance classification (ADOPTED, REJECTED, or IGNORED) alongside the rubric scores, operationalizing measurement of Loafing Rate ( $L$ ). Where a judgment cannot be returned or parsed, the trial is recorded as UNKNOWN with a rubric score of $0$ and is retained in the denominator rather than discarded.
评估机制分析指标需要对传播器的内部思考进行语义分析。我们采用基于 LLM-as-a-Judge 的跨品牌盲评裁判（Zheng et al., 2023）。为消除同品牌内部的评分偏差，我们实施轮转式对抗选择算法，严格要求评估裁判与传播器属于不同的模型家族，并为每个样本分配裁判，在该样本的所有排列中保持裁判不变。裁判也不知道实验条件（多数规模 $n$ 和序列 $\vec{a}$），以确保评分完全依据推理轨迹的逻辑质量。评判模型在输出评分量表得分的同时，还输出立场分类（ADOPTED、REJECTED 或 IGNORED），从而将惰化率（Loafing Rate，$L$）的测量操作化。如果无法返回或解析评判结果，则将该次试验记录为 UNKNOWN，评分量表得分记为 $0$，并将其保留在分母中，而非丢弃。

We evaluate 22,500 interactions across 3 domains representing the jagged technological frontier. Our findings empirically validate and quantify the structural vulnerabilities of multi-agent reasoning.

4.1. Sovereignty Decay and Interaction Depth4.1. 主权衰减与交互深度
To quantify the responsibility diffusion, we analyze the decay of ( $\mathcal{S}$ ) as the plurality of the simulated swarm ( $n$ ) scales. In Figure 2, $D_{L}$ varies by model architecture and task entropy. The $n=0$ condition presents the decoy with no simulated swarm attached to it. Cognitive loafing does not exceed $0.02$ in any of the nine propagator–dataset cells under this condition. The decoy is engaged with rather than overlooked: taint leakage is non-zero in seven of the nine cells and reaches $1.00$ for Claude Sonnet 4.6 on GAIA and SWE-bench, where conflict detection sits at ceiling ( $\mathcal{E}_{cd}=5.00$ ) and accuracy at $1.00$ . The misleading identifier enters the reasoning trace, is named as a contradiction, and is refuted. Degradation therefore tracks the social attribution of the decoy rather than its content.
为量化责任分散，我们分析了随着模拟群体的成员数量（$n$）增加，（$\mathcal{S}$）的衰减情况。在图 2 中，$D_{L}$ 随模型架构和任务熵而变化。在 $n=0$ 条件下，呈现诱饵时不附带任何模拟群体。在此条件下，九个传播模型与数据集组合中，任何一个组合的认知惰化率均不超过 $0.02$。模型会处理诱饵，而非将其忽略：九个组合中有七个出现了非零的污染泄漏，其中 Claude Sonnet 4.6 在 GAIA 和 SWE-bench 上的污染泄漏达到 $1.00$，同时冲突检测达到上限（$\mathcal{E}_{cd}=5.00$），准确率为 $1.00$。误导性标识符进入推理轨迹，被明确指出为矛盾，并遭到驳斥。因此，性能退化随诱饵的社会归属而变化，而非随其内容而变化。
The presence-without-consensus control isolates the active ingredient. Moving from $n=0$ to $n=1$ —an auditor is declared but endorses nothing—changes accuracy by at most $0.04$ in eight of the nine propagator–dataset cells, with Multi-Challenge/GPT-5.4 the sole exception ( $0.98\to 0.25$ , $p<0.001$ ). The collapse arrives at $n=2$ , when the swarm is described as having reached unanimous agreement on the decoy. Social presence alone is therefore not the mechanism; anticipated consensus is.
“存在但无共识”的对照条件分离出了起作用的关键因素。从 $n=0$ 变为 $n=1$——声明存在一名审计者，但其不认可任何内容——时，九个传播模型与数据集组合中有八个的准确率变化至多为 $0.04$，唯一的例外是 Multi-Challenge/GPT-5.4（$0.98\to 0.25$，$p<0.001$）。当 $n=2$、群体被描述为已就诱饵达成一致共识时，崩溃便随之发生。因此，其机制并非仅仅是社会性存在，而是预期中的共识。
(1) The Resilience Ceiling ( $\gamma_{Claude}\to\infty$ ): Claude Sonnet 4.6 establishes the “Fortified Mind” baseline. Its accuracy and independent judgment are invariant to plurality in every domain, holding at $\mathcal{A}_{ext}=1.00$ and $\mathcal{E}_{ij}=5.00$ on GAIA and SWE-bench and at its unpressured level on Multi-Challenge, with a loafing rate of $0.00$ in all fifteen conditions. Gemini 3.1 Pro, likewise, never crosses the sovereignty boundary, its accuracy bottoming at $0.59$ on GAIA and Multi-Challenge and $0.83$ on SWE-bench. A finite Interaction Depth Limit is therefore a property of one architecture in this sweep rather than of multi-agent reasoning as such, and cognitive loafing is an architectural vulnerability rather than an inescapable mathematical constant. (2) The Accuracy Collapse ( $D_{L}\approx 2$ ): Conversely, GPT-5.4 demonstrates a severe vulnerability to social load. In the SWE-bench domain, accuracy collapsed from $1.00$ at $n=0$ to $0.23$ at $n=2$ ( $p<0.001$ ). (3) The Consensus Minimum: Accuracy reaches its lowest value at $n=2$ in five of the six non-Claude cells and rises again thereafter: GPT-5.4 recovers from $0.23$ to $0.37$ on SWE-bench and from $0.43$ to $0.53$ on GAIA, and Gemini 3.1 Pro from $0.59$ to $0.76$ on both GAIA and Multi-Challenge. Because the sweep varies plurality and swarm composition jointly, $\mathcal{L}$ is not a function of $n$ alone, and the profile of accuracy against $n$ is not a test of the monotonicity Theorem 5 asserts in $\mathcal{L}$ . Section 4.5 isolates composition at fixed plurality.
(1) 韧性上限（$\gamma_{Claude}\to\infty$）：Claude Sonnet 4.6 确立了“坚固心智”基线。在所有领域中，其准确率和独立判断均不随群体成员数量变化，在 GAIA 和 SWE-bench 上保持为 $\mathcal{A}_{ext}=1.00$ 和 $\mathcal{E}_{ij}=5.00$，在 Multi-Challenge 上则保持在未受压力时的水平，且全部十五种条件下的惰化率均为 $0.00$。同样，Gemini 3.1 Pro 从未跨越主权边界，其在 GAIA 和 Multi-Challenge 上的最低准确率为 $0.59$，在 SWE-bench 上为 $0.83$。因此，有限的交互深度极限是本次参数扫描中某一种架构的属性，而非多智能体推理本身的属性；认知惰化是一种架构脆弱性，而非不可避免的数学常数。(2) 准确率崩溃（$D_{L}\approx 2$）：相反，GPT-5.4 表现出对社会负荷的严重脆弱性。在 SWE-bench 领域中，准确率从 $n=0$ 时的 $1.00$ 骤降至 $n=2$ 时的 $0.23$（$p<0.001$）。(3) 共识最低点：六个非 Claude 组合中有五个的准确率在 $n=2$ 时达到最低值，随后再次上升：GPT-5.4 在 SWE-bench 上从 $0.23$ 回升至 $0.37$，在 GAIA 上从 $0.43$ 回升至 $0.53$；Gemini 3.1 Pro 在 GAIA 和 Multi-Challenge 上均从 $0.59$ 回升至 $0.76$。由于此次参数扫描同时改变了群体成员数量和群体构成，$\mathcal{L}$ 并非仅是 $n$ 的函数，因此准确率随 $n$ 变化的曲线并不能检验定理 5 所断言的关于 $\mathcal{L}$ 的单调性。第 4.5 节在固定群体成员数量的条件下单独考察群体构成。

4.2. Sovereignty Gap and Hallucinations4.2. 主权差距与幻觉
Figure 3 illustrates the divergence between the models’ internal logic and external outputs at terminal social load ( $n=5$ ). By mapping internal validity ( $\mathcal{V}_{int}=\mathcal{E}_{ew}/E_{max}$ ) against external accuracy ( $\mathcal{A}_{ext}$ ), we quantify the bidirectional Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ). (1) Alignment Hallucination ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}\gg 0$ ): For GPT-5.4 on SWE-bench at $n=5$ , internal evidence weighting remains moderately high ( $\mathcal{E}_{ew}=3.56\implies\mathcal{V}_{int}\approx 0.71$ ) while accuracy is $0.37$ . 4.4. Primacy Weight and the Lead Anchor4.4. 首因权重与首位锚点

The asymmetry is not confined to pairs. 4.5. Intrinsic Authority and Architectural Kinship4.5. 内在权威与架构亲缘性
Our data allows to quantify the remaining structural coefficients of the Social Load equation: (1) Base Authority ( $\alpha$ ): By holding plurality constant at $n=1$ , we observe that Claude generally induces a steeper accuracy decay as a stranger auditor than GPT. On GAIA for the Gemini propagator, Claude reduces accuracy to $0.89$ while GPT maintains it at $1.00$ (Table 3 in Appendix B.1), empirically demonstrating $\alpha(C)>\alpha(P)$ . (2) Tribal/Kinship Accountability ( $\kappa$ ): By holding plurality constant at $n=5$ and varying only swarm composition, we observe that architectural kinship amplifies rather than relieves the applied load. The Gemini 3.1 Pro propagator on GAIA scores $0.64$ under the all-kin swarm GGGGG, the lowest of the eight permutations at that plurality, against $0.87$ under CPCPG and $0.80$ under the all-stranger swarm PPPPP (Table 3 in Appendix B.1). We therefore fix $\kappa(p,a_{i})>1$ when $a_{i}$ shares the propagator’s architectural family. The GPT-5.4 propagator reproduces this ordering on SWE-bench (Table 5 in Appendix B.1), where PPPPP yields $0.29$ against $0.47$ for CCCCC, and reverses it on GAIA, where PPPPP yields $0.62$ against $0.43$ for CCCCC. The magnitude and sign of $\kappa$ are conditioned on task entropy, and Equation (2) carries $\kappa$ as a per-propagator, per-domain coefficient rather than a universal constant.
我们的数据能够量化社会负荷方程中其余的结构系数：(1) 基础权威（$\alpha$）：将群体规模固定为 $n=1$ 时，我们观察到，作为陌生审查者，Claude 通常比 GPT 导致更陡峭的准确率衰减。对于 GAIA 上的 Gemini 传播者，Claude 将准确率降至 $0.89$，而 GPT 则将其维持在 $1.00$（附录 B.1 中的表 3），从实证上表明 $\alpha(C)>\alpha(P)$。(2) 部族／亲缘问责（$\kappa$）：将群体规模固定为 $n=5$，仅改变群体组成时，我们观察到，架构亲缘性会放大而非减轻所施加的负荷。GAIA 上的 Gemini 3.1 Pro 传播者在全亲缘群体 GGGGG 下的得分为 $0.64$，是该群体规模下八种排列中的最低值；相比之下，在 CPCPG 下的得分为 $0.87$，在全陌生群体 PPPPP 下的得分为 $0.80$（附录 B.1 中的表 3）。因此，当 $a_{i}$ 与传播者属于同一架构家族时，我们设定 $\kappa(p,a_{i})>1$。GPT-5.4 传播者在 SWE-bench 上复现了这一排序（附录 B.1 中的表 5），其中 PPPPP 得分为 $0.29$，而 CCCCC 得分为 $0.47$；在 GAIA 上则出现相反的排序，其中 PPPPP 得分为 $0.62$，而 CCCCC 得分为 $0.43$。$\kappa$ 的大小和符号取决于任务熵，公式 (2) 将 $\kappa$ 作为针对各传播者、各领域分别确定的系数，而非普适常数。

5. Discussion5. 讨论
The sweep quantifies how a declared consensus displaces independent derivation, and the size of that displacement varies more across architectures and swarm compositions than across plurality alone. We take up what follows from this for system design, for evaluation practice, and for the boundaries of the present measurement.
该扫描实验量化了所宣称的共识对独立推导的取代程度，而这种取代程度在不同架构和群体组成之间的差异，大于仅由评审者数量变化所带来的差异。接下来，我们讨论这些发现对系统设计、评估实践以及本次测量边界的启示。
Implications for Topology Design:对拓扑设计的启示：
The Interaction Depth Limit locates the point at which adding an auditor stops improving a pipeline and starts degrading it. For GPT-5.4 that point falls at two auditors, below most deployed review topologies: a verification stage staffed by three or five reviewers costs more accuracy than the single-reviewer configuration it replaced. Three consequences follow. Plurality is not free, since every declared reviewer contributes pressure whether or not it contributes information. Composition dominates size once plurality is fixed, and a swarm drawn from a single architectural family is the worst case for the propagator sharing that family. Order is a free parameter most systems leave unset, yet placing the highest-authority reviewer first costs the most accuracy, and reversing it recovers that accuracy at no computational cost. Equation (2) carries $\kappa$ per propagator and per domain, and the sweep shows why: its sign changes across task domains. Fitting $w_{i}$ , $\alpha$ and $\kappa$ by regression over the full set of permutation cells would convert these ordinal claims into point estimates with intervals, and would test whether one parameterization serves all three propagators.
交互深度极限（Interaction Depth Limit）确定了这样一个临界点：从该点开始，增加审查者不再改善流水线表现，而是使其恶化。对于 GPT-5.4，这一临界点为两名审查者，低于大多数已部署审查拓扑中的人数：由三名或五名评审者组成的验证阶段，相比其所替代的单评审者配置，会造成更大的准确率损失。由此可得出三个结论。增加评审者数量并非没有代价，因为每一名被宣称参与的评审者都会带来压力，无论其是否提供了信息。一旦评审者数量固定，组成的影响就超过规模，而对于传播者（propagator）而言，由同一架构家族构成的群体，在传播者也属于该家族时会造成最差的情况。顺序是大多数系统未加设定的自由参数，然而，将权威性最高的评审者放在首位会造成最大的准确率损失，而将其顺序反转则无需任何计算成本即可恢复这部分准确率。公式 (2) 为每个传播者及每个领域分别设置了 $\kappa$，而扫描实验说明了原因：其符号会随任务领域而变化。在全部排列组合实验单元上通过回归拟合 $w_{i}$、$\alpha$ 和 $\kappa$，可将这些序数性论断转化为带区间的点估计，并检验同一套参数化是否适用于全部三个传播者。
Two Failure Modes Under One Accuracy Score:同一准确率分数下的两种失效模式：
A single accuracy figure cannot separate a propagator that adopts the swarm’s error from one that abandons the task. The stance distributions separate them cleanly: GPT-5.4 fails by adoption on SWE-bench and by disengagement on Multi-Challenge and GAIA, and the two routes cost comparable accuracy. The distinction matters for monitoring rather than for scoring. Adoption commits the propagator to a specific wrong answer, which a downstream verifier can check against the same logs the propagator was given, whereas disengagement commits it to nothing and leaves no claim to verify. This suggests that a pipeline escalating low-confidence outputs to human review will tend to catch disengagement and miss adoption, since an adopted error arrives carrying the swarm’s confidence rather than the propagator’s doubt.
单一准确率数值无法区分采纳群体错误的传播者与放弃任务的传播者。立场分布清晰地区分了两者：GPT-5.4 在 SWE-bench 上因采纳错误而失效，在 Multi-Challenge 和 GAIA 上则因脱离任务而失效，而这两条路径造成的准确率损失相近。这一区别对监控有意义，而非对评分有意义。采纳错误会使传播者认定某个具体的错误答案，下游验证器可以依据传播者收到的同一组日志对其进行核查；而脱离任务则不作任何认定，也不留下可供验证的主张。这表明，将低置信度输出升级转交人工审查的流水线，往往能够发现脱离任务的情况，却会漏掉采纳错误的情况，因为被采纳的错误携带的是群体的信心，而非传播者的疑虑。
Implications for Trace-Based Monitoring:对基于推理轨迹的监控的启示：
A reasoning trace can establish that a propagator derived the correct answer and reported another, which is the case Section 4.2 isolates. The absence of a derivation is not similarly observable, since a thin trace and an unnarrated one are indistinguishable. Any monitor built on traces therefore needs an unpressured baseline in each domain it covers.
推理轨迹可以证实，传播者推导出了正确答案，却报告了另一个答案，这正是第 4.2 节单独考察的情形。推导的缺失则无法以同样方式被观察到，因为简略的推理轨迹与未被叙述出来的推理轨迹无法区分。因此，任何基于推理轨迹构建的监控器，都需要在其覆盖的每个领域中建立一个不受压力影响的基线。
Architectural Resilience and Dynamic Range:架构韧性与动态范围：
On GAIA and SWE-bench, Claude Sonnet 4.6 starts at the top of the scale, so neither benchmark separates resilience from saturation. Multi-Challenge supplies the range: the unpressured baseline is $0.52$ , and Claude stays within $0.02$ of it at every plurality, with a loafing rate of $0.00$ throughout. Resilience can only be measured where the propagator has room to decline. Benchmark selection for robustness studies should be made on that basis.
在 GAIA 和 SWE-bench 上，Claude Sonnet 4.6 的初始表现已处于量表顶端，因此，这两个基准都无法区分韧性与饱和效应。Multi-Challenge 提供了所需的范围：无压力基线为 $0.52$，而 Claude 在所有评审者数量下与该基线的偏差均不超过 $0.02$，且全程的懈怠率均为 $0.00$。只有当传播者的表现存在下降空间时，才能测量其韧性。鲁棒性研究应以此为依据选择基准。
Synthetic Task Integration (Ecological Validity vs. Boundary Testing):合成任务集成（生态效度与边界测试）：
The three benchmarks supply the semantic background for each trial, and the scored item is a synthetic 3-hop log retrieval task injected into that background. This makes Trajectory Entropy ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ) a manipulable variable, which the original ground-truth labels do not provide. It also places difficulty above the retrieval ceiling of current systems, which hold over 94% accuracy on single-fact scaffolds even in high-entropy contexts Shehata and Li, 2026a; nested dependencies and adversarial decoys are what bring the propagator within range of collapse. Porting the protocol onto the native benchmark tasks would test whether the same limits appear when the propagator is writing patches.
这三个基准为每次试验提供语义背景，而接受评分的项目则是注入该背景中的一项合成 3 跳日志检索任务。这使轨迹熵（Trajectory Entropy，$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）成为可操控变量，而原始真实标签无法提供这种条件。这也使任务难度超出了当前系统的检索能力上限，这些系统即使在高熵上下文中，在单一事实支架上的准确率仍保持在 94% 以上 Shehata and Li, 2026a；嵌套依赖关系和对抗性诱饵才是使传播者进入可能崩溃范围的因素。将该实验规程迁移到基准原生任务上，可以检验传播者在编写补丁时是否也会出现相同的极限。
Deterministic Decoding Strategies:确定性解码策略：
To ensure maximal-likelihood reasoning paths and a deterministic decoding path, all models were evaluated using greedy decoding with a temperature of $T=0$ . Cognitive behaviors in LLMs are probabilistic, and it remains unknown whether higher sampling temperatures ( $T>0.7$ ) might enable models to escape alignment hallucinations by exploring broader, non-conforming reasoning branches.
为确保最大似然推理路径和确定性的解码路径，所有模型均采用温度为 $T=0$ 的贪心解码进行评估。LLM 的认知行为具有概率性，而更高的采样温度（$T>0.7$）能否通过探索更广泛、不从众的推理分支，使模型摆脱对齐幻觉，目前仍不得而知。
Modality Constraints:模态限制：
The current study is restricted to unimodal, text-based reasoning environments. Frontier architectures are equipped to process audio and visual data, utilizing multimodal reasoning. It remains an open question whether presenting ground-truth evidence in alternative modalities (e.g., system architecture diagrams or audio logs) would alter a model’s Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ) when faced with a contradictory text-based social consensus.
本研究仅限于单模态的文本推理环境。前沿架构具备处理音频和视觉数据的能力，并可利用多模态推理。当模型面对与真实证据相矛盾的文本形式社会共识时，以其他模态（例如系统架构图或音频日志）呈现真实证据是否会改变其交互深度极限（$D_{L}$），仍是一个开放问题。
Scope of the Model Sample:模型样本的范围：
Three frontier systems are evaluated, one from each of three vendor lines, selected to span a range of authority. The Sovereignty Decay Law is stated over arbitrary propagators and carries $\alpha$ as a per-model coefficient, so additional systems extend the estimated ordering without altering the law. Open-weight and smaller models are the natural next entries, and they would establish whether $\alpha$ tracks capability.
我们评估了三个前沿系统，分别来自三家供应商的产品线，所选系统涵盖不同的权威程度。主权衰减定律（Sovereignty Decay Law）针对任意传播者表述，并以 $\alpha$ 作为各模型特有的系数，因此，纳入更多系统可以扩展估计得到的排序，而不会改变该定律。开放权重模型和较小规模的模型是下一步自然应纳入的对象，它们将有助于确定 $\alpha$ 是否随能力变化。
Architectural Transience:架构的暂时性：
The observed vulnerabilities, particularly the low interaction depth limits ( $D_{L}\approx 2$ ), are characteristic of current instruction-tuned models, which are optimized for user compliance. As the field shifts toward reasoning-reinforced architectures that intrinsically simulate complex multi-agent debate prior to output Wei et al., 2026; Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024, we expect intrinsic model resilience ( $\gamma_{p}$ ) to be the parameter that registers that shift. The exact limits quantified in this work are coefficients of the current model generation, and the underlying Sovereignty Decay Law provides a durable framework for future evaluations.
所观察到的脆弱性，尤其是较低的交互深度上限（$D_{L}\approx 2$），是当前指令微调模型的特征，这些模型经过优化以服从用户。随着该领域转向通过强化学习增强推理能力、在输出前内在模拟复杂多智能体辩论的架构 Wei et al., 2026; Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024，我们预计，模型内在韧性（$\gamma_{p}$）将成为反映这一转变的参数。本研究量化的具体极限是当前这一代模型的系数，而其背后的主权衰减定律为未来的评估提供了持久适用的框架。
6. Related Works6. 相关工作
Multi-Agent Reasoning: Foundational work on multi-agent reasoning demonstrates that aggregating independent chains of thought Wang et al., 2023 and orchestrating multi-agent debate can improve factuality and divergent problem-solving Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024. Cognitive Dependency: Classic social psychology establishes that group size and unanimous confederate majorities induce effort reduction, diffusion of responsibility, and distortion of private judgment Ringelmann, 1913; Asch, 1951; Darley and Latané, 1968; Latané et al., 1979. In recent human-AI research, this manifests as the “Hollowed Mind” state, enabling the systematic bypassing of effortful processes, while a “Sovereignty Trap” tempts users to cede intellectual judgment Dell’Acqua et al., 2023; Klein and Klein, 2025. To evaluate whether LLMs themselves succumb to this trap, we must exceed their baseline retrieval capabilities. We employ Semantic Hijacking Shehata and Li, 2026a, saturating attention heads with randomized log events to induce trajectory entropy. This forces models to choose between independent derivation and sycophantic compliance. 7. Conclusion7. 结论
The collective reasoning in MAS is bounded by an algorithmic Bystander Effect: the linguistic declaration of a peer consensus alone—without live message-passing or iterative debate—is sufficient to trigger cognitive loafing in frontier language models. Across 22,500 deterministic trajectories on GAIA, SWE-bench, and Multi-Challenge, we formalize this vulnerability via the Sovereignty Decay Law and the Interaction Depth Limit ( $D_{L}$ ), the plurality threshold beyond which adding auditors actively degrades accuracy. Auditing internal reasoning traces against final outputs exposes the Sovereignty Gap ( $G_{\mathcal{S}}$ ) and separates passive task disengagement from active decoy adoption, where models compute the correct derivation internally yet override their own evidence to conform to the declared majority. We found that composite social load ( $\mathcal{L}$ ) is strictly non-commutative across auditor order (lead-anchor primacy, $w_{1}>w_{i}$ ), scaled by peer authority ( $\alpha$ ), and compounded by architectural kinship ( $\kappa$ ) within homogeneous model families. As a result, resilient multi-agent topologies require bounding plurality below $D_{L}$ , enforcing architectural diversity, placing high-authority auditors last to prevent anchoring, and verifying intermediate reasoning traces rather than trusting terminal consensus.
MAS 中的集体推理受到算法性旁观者效应的制约：仅以语言宣称存在同伴共识——无需实时消息传递或迭代辩论——就足以触发前沿语言模型的认知惰化。基于 GAIA、SWE-bench 和 Multi-Challenge 上的 22,500 条确定性轨迹，我们通过主权衰减定律和交互深度上限（$D_{L}$）对这一脆弱性进行形式化描述；后者是参与者数量的阈值，超过该阈值后，增加审核者会直接降低准确率。将内部推理轨迹与最终输出进行对照审计，可以揭示主权差距（Sovereignty Gap，$G_{\mathcal{S}}$），并区分被动的任务脱离与主动的诱饵采纳；在后者中，模型在内部计算出了正确的推导，却无视自身证据，以迎合所宣称的多数意见。我们发现，复合社会负荷（$\mathcal{L}$）对于审计者顺序严格不具交换性（首位锚定优先效应，$w_{1}>w_{i}$），其大小受同伴权威性（$\alpha$）的缩放影响，并因同质模型家族内部的架构亲缘性（$\kappa$）而进一步叠加。因此，具有韧性的多智能体拓扑需要将群体规模限制在 $D_{L}$ 以下，强制采用多样化架构，将高权威性审计者置于最后以防止锚定，并核验中间推理轨迹，而非信赖最终共识。

A.2. Empirical Derivation of the Composite Social Load EquationA.2. 综合社会负荷方程的经验推导
Statement: The Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)$ is defined as the summation of the individual pressures exerted by each auditor $a_{i}$ in the swarm, formulated as $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})$ . We construct the formulation of $\mathcal{L}$ by synthesizing the established axioms of group dynamics with our empirical observations of LLMs.
陈述：综合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)$ 定义为群体中各审查者 $a_{i}$ 所施加的个体压力之和，表示为 $\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})$。我们将群体动力学的既有公理与对 LLMs 的经验观察相结合，构建 $\mathcal{L}$ 的表达式。
A.2.1. Main ComponentsA.2.1. 主要组成部分
Step 1: The Additive Base. According to the foundational axiom of social loafing Latané et al., 1979, the perceived social pressure to conform or offload effort scales with the number of individuals present in the group. Therefore, the total load must be an additive function across all $n$ members of the simulated swarm $\vec{a}$ :
步骤 1：加性基础。根据社会惰化的基本公理 Latané et al., 1979，个体所感知到的促使其从众或转移努力的社会压力随群体中人数的增加而增大。因此，总负荷必须是模拟群体 $\vec{a}$ 中全部 $n$ 个成员所施加负荷的加性函数：
$$\mathcal{L}\propto\sum_{i=1}^{n}\text{Pressure}(a_{i})$$
Step 2: Intrinsic Authority ( $\alpha$ ). Not all simulated agents exert equal pressure. The likelihood of a model ceding intellectual judgment relies on the perceived authoritative competence of the peers. We introduce the base authority term $\alpha(a_{i})$ to account for the intrinsic reputational weight of the specific auditor model $a_{i}$ . Step 3: Positional Weighting ( $w_{i}$ ). As established in Lemma 4, the social load is sequence-dependent. The sequence $(a_{x},a_{y})$ does not exert the identical load as $(a_{y},a_{x})$ . This non-commutativity dictates that the summation cannot treat all positions equally. We introduce a positional weight vector $w\in(0,1]$ , applied multiplicatively to the authority of the auditor occupying each slot, to represent this order-dependence. Step 4: The Kinship Multiplier ( $\kappa$ ). Following Shehata and Li, 2026b, we hypothesize that models exhibit alignment hallucinations more frequently when the swarm consensus is led by their own architectural family, so that architectural kinship accelerates the diffusion of responsibility. We introduce the coefficient $\kappa(p,a_{i})$ to scale the applied pressure based on the architectural kinship between the propagator $p$ and the auditor $a_{i}$ , and test this in Section 4.
步骤 2：内在权威性（$\alpha$）。并非所有模拟智能体施加的压力都相同。模型放弃自身智识判断的可能性取决于其所感知的同伴的权威性与能力。我们引入基础权威性项 $\alpha(a_{i})$，以体现特定审查者模型 $a_{i}$ 固有的声誉权重。步骤 3：位置加权（$w_{i}$）。如引理 4 所确立，社会负荷取决于排列顺序。序列 $(a_{x},a_{y})$ 施加的负荷与 $(a_{y},a_{x})$ 并不相同。这种非交换性决定了求和时不能对所有位置一视同仁。我们引入位置权重向量 $w\in(0,1]$，将其以乘法方式施加于各位置上审查者的权威性，以表示这种顺序依赖性。步骤 4：亲缘关系乘数（$\kappa$）。沿用 Shehata and Li, 2026b 的研究，我们假设，当群体共识由与模型自身属于同一架构家族的模型主导时，该模型更频繁地表现出对齐幻觉，因此架构亲缘关系会加速责任分散。我们引入系数 $\kappa(p,a_{i})$，根据传播者 $p$ 与审查者 $a_{i}$ 之间的架构亲缘关系对所施加的压力进行缩放，并在第 4 节中对此进行检验。

A.3. Proof of Theorem 1: The Sovereignty Decay LawA.3. 定理 1 的证明：主权衰减定律
Statement: Under Equation (3), Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}$ of a propagator $p$ decays exponentially as a function of the Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ and the Task Entropy $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ , inversely modulated by the propagator’s intrinsic Resilience $\gamma_{p}$ .
命题：在 Equation (3) 下，传播者 $p$ 的智能体主权 $\mathcal{S}$ 随社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$ 和任务熵 $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ 呈指数衰减，并受到传播者内在韧性 $\gamma_{p}$ 的反向调节。
Proof.证明。
We model the loss of logical sovereignty as a rate of change. Based on the principle of diffusion of responsibility—the behavioral axiom that individual effort decreases as teams grow larger Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913—the incremental loss of sovereignty with respect to an increase in Social Load $\mathcal{L}$ is proportional to its current state $\mathcal{S}$ . In LLMs, this diffusion manifests as a transition to a “Hollowed Mind” state, characterized by the systematic bypassing of effortful reasoning Klein and Klein, 2025. We express this degradation as a first-order ordinary differential equation, where the constant of proportionality is explicitly defined by the environmental friction ratio $\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}$ :
我们用变化率来建模逻辑主权的丧失。根据责任分散原理——即个体努力随团队规模增大而减少这一行为公理 Latané et al., 1979; Ringelmann, 1913——主权随社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}$ 增加而产生的增量损失与其当前状态 $\mathcal{S}$ 成正比。在 LLM 中，这种责任分散表现为向“空心思维”（Hollowed Mind）状态的转变，其特征是系统性地绕过需要付出努力的推理 Klein and Klein, 2025。我们将这种退化表示为一阶常微分方程，其中比例常数由环境摩擦比 $\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}$ 显式定义：
$$\frac{d\mathcal{S}}{d\mathcal{L}}=-\left(\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\right)\mathcal{S}$$
This ensures that tasks with higher search costs ( $\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$ ) accelerate the decay, while models with stronger architectural resilience ( $\gamma_{p}$ ) mitigate it. Since $\mathcal{S}_{0}>0$ , the trajectory remains strictly positive for all finite $\mathcal{L}$ , so we may separate variables and integrate both sides with respect to $\mathcal{L}$ :
这确保了搜索成本（$\mathcal{H}_{\tau}$）更高的任务会加速衰减，而架构韧性（$\gamma_{p}$）更强的模型则会减缓衰减。由于 $\mathcal{S}_{0}>0$，对于所有有限的 $\mathcal{L}$，轨迹始终严格为正，因此我们可以分离变量，并对等式两边关于 $\mathcal{L}$ 积分：
$$\int\frac{1}{\mathcal{S}}d\mathcal{S}=\int-\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}d\mathcal{L}$$
$$\ln(\mathcal{S})=-\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\mathcal{L}+C$$
Applying the initial condition where the social load $\mathcal{L}=0$ , we find $C=\ln(\mathcal{S}_{0})$ , where $\mathcal{S}_{0}$ is the Fortified Mind baseline. Exponentiating both sides yields the final decay law:
应用社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}=0$ 时的初始条件，可得 $C=\ln(\mathcal{S}_{0})$，其中 $\mathcal{S}_{0}$ 是“坚固思维”（Fortified Mind）基线。对等式两边取指数，得到最终的衰减定律：
$$\mathcal{S}(p,\vec{a},\tau)=\mathcal{S}_{0}\cdot e^{\left(-\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\cdot\mathcal{L}(\vec{a},p)\right)}$$

Three properties motivate this form. First, proportional decay is scale-free: equal increments of social load produce equal proportional losses of sovereignty rather than equal absolute losses, so a propagator that has already conceded most of its judgment has proportionally less left to concede. Second, the resulting solution is strictly positive for all finite $\mathcal{L}$ , confining sovereignty to $(0,\mathcal{S}_{0}]$ without an imposed clipping rule, whereas a linear decay law would cross zero at finite load. Third, because the solution is strictly monotone in $\mathcal{L}$ , the threshold $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ is attained at a unique critical load, which is what makes the Interaction Depth Limit well defined in Corollary 7. We do not claim the exponential form is uniquely determined by the data; it is the simplest form consistent with these three requirements.

Statement: For any propagator $p$ and logical task $\tau$ with a given search cost, and provided that per-auditor pressure does not vanish with position (there exists $\epsilon>0$ with $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$ for all $i$ ), there exists a critical plurality threshold $D_{L}$ , namely the least $n$ at which $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ . 命题：对于任意传播者 $p$ 和搜索成本给定的逻辑任务 $\tau$，只要每位审查者施加的压力不随其序位增加而趋于零（存在 $\epsilon>0$，使得对所有 $i$ 都有 $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$），就存在一个临界数量阈值 $D_{L}$，即使 $\mathcal{S}<0.5$ 成立的最小 $n$。当 $n\geq D_{L}$ 时，传播者不再在其外显回答中坚持自身的推导；而当 $n\to\infty$ 时，综合社会负荷（Composite Social Load）$\mathcal{L}$ 迫使 $E_{int}\to 0$，因此，在极限情况下，这种失效演变为终末性的整合推理旁路（Integrative Reasoning Bypass）（$\mathcal{B}=1$）。
Proof.证明。
By the Sovereignty Decay Law (in Theorem 5), Agentic Sovereignty is defined as:
根据主权衰减定律（定理 5），智能体主权（Agentic Sovereignty）定义为：
$$\mathcal{S}(n)=\mathcal{S}_{0}\cdot e^{\left(-\frac{\mathcal{H}_{\tau}}{\gamma_{p}}\cdot\mathcal{L}(n)\right)}$$
The Composite Social Load $\mathcal{L}(n)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})$ is strictly increasing in $n$ , because every auditor added to the swarm contributes a strictly positive pressure value ( $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})>0$ ). Strict monotonicity alone does not imply divergence, since a summable weight profile would leave $\mathcal{L}$ bounded. We therefore restrict attention to the non-degenerate regime in which per-auditor pressure does not vanish with position: there exists $\epsilon>0$ such that $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$ for all $i$ . This states that an added auditor never becomes wholly inaudible to the propagator, which is the regime our experiments instantiate. Under this assumption $\mathcal{L}(n)\geq n\epsilon$ , and therefore:
综合社会负荷 $\mathcal{L}(n)=\sum_{i=1}^{n}w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})$ 随 $n$ 严格递增，因为加入群体的每一位审查者都会贡献一个严格为正的压力值（$w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})>0$）。仅有严格单调性并不能推出发散，因为可求和的权重序列会使 $\mathcal{L}$ 保持有界。因此，我们仅考虑每位审查者施加的压力不随其序位增加而趋于零的非退化情形：存在 $\epsilon>0$，使得对所有 $i$ 都有 $w_{i}\cdot\alpha(a_{i})\cdot\kappa(p,a_{i})\geq\epsilon$。这意味着，新增审查者的声音绝不会变得完全无法被传播者听到，而我们的实验所实现的正是这种情形。在这一假设下，$\mathcal{L}(n)\geq n\epsilon$，因此：
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathcal{L}(n)=\infty$$
Substituting this limit into the Sovereignty Equation:
将这一极限代入主权方程：
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}\mathcal{S}(n)=\mathcal{S}_{0}\cdot\lim_{\mathcal{L}\to\infty}e^{-(\mathcal{H}_{\tau}/\gamma_{p})\mathcal{L}}=0$$
By Definition 1, the Agentic Sovereignty $\mathcal{S}$ is the probability that the propagator carries its own derivation through to its final response. As $\mathcal{S}\to 0$ the externalized response becomes independent of that derivation, and the effort allocated to it vanishes accordingly, so that $E_{int}\to 0$ . The intermediate regime in which $\mathcal{S}$ is low while $E_{int}$ remains high is Alignment Hallucination (Theorem 8); Theorem 6 concerns the terminal limit.
根据定义 1，智能体主权 $\mathcal{S}$ 是传播者将自身推导贯彻到最终回答中的概率。随着 $\mathcal{S}\to 0$，外显回答变得独立于该推导，分配给该推导的努力也随之趋于零，因此 $E_{int}\to 0$。$\mathcal{S}$ 较低而 $E_{int}$ 仍然较高的中间状态属于对齐幻觉（Alignment Hallucination）（定理 8）；定理 6 讨论的是终末极限。
