‘Dunbar's number’ deconstructed解构“邓巴数”（Dunbar's number）
Abstract摘要
A widespread and popular belief posits that humans possess a cognitive capacity that is limited to keeping track of and maintaining stable relationships with approximately 150 people. This influential number, ‘Dunbar's number’, originates from an extrapolation of a regression line describing the relationship between relative neocortex size and group size in primates. Here, we test if there is statistical support for this idea. Our analyses on complementary datasets using different methods yield wildly different numbers. Bayesian and generalized least-squares phylogenetic methods generate approximations of average group sizes between 69–109 and 16–42, respectively. However, enormous 95% confidence intervals (4–520 and 2–336, respectively) imply that specifying any one number is futile. A cognitive limit on human group size cannot be derived in this manner.
一种广为流传且颇受欢迎的观点认为，人类的认知能力有限，只能掌握约 150 人的关系状况并与之维持稳定关系。这个颇具影响力的数字，即“邓巴数”，源于对一条描述灵长类动物相对新皮质大小与群体规模之间关系的回归线进行外推。在此，我们检验这一观点是否得到统计学支持。我们使用不同方法对互为补充的数据集进行分析，得出的数字差异极大。贝叶斯和广义最小二乘系统发育方法得到的平均群体规模估计值分别为 69–109 和 16–42。然而，极为宽泛的 95% 置信区间（分别为 4–520 和 2–336）意味着，指定任何一个数字都是徒劳的。无法以这种方式推导出人类群体规模的认知上限。
Introduction引言
‘Dunbar's number’ is the notion that there exists a cognitive limit on human groups of about 150 individuals. [1,2] This because ‘[t]o maintain group cohesion, individuals must be able to meet their own requirements, as well as coordinate their behaviour with other individuals in the group. They must also be able to defuse the direct and indirect conflicts that are generated by foraging in the same space’. [3] According to the hypothesis, since the neocortex is commonly believed to play a crucial role in handling social relationships [4], its size should set an upper limit on the number of stable social relationships that primate brains can keep track of and maintain.
“邓巴数”指的是这样一种观点：人类群体存在一个由认知能力决定的规模上限，约为 150 人。[1,2] 这是因为，“为了维持群体凝聚力，个体必须既能满足自身需求，又能协调自身行为，使之与群体中其他个体的行为相配合。它们还必须能够化解在同一空间内觅食所产生的直接和间接冲突”。[3] 根据这一假说，由于新皮质通常被认为在处理社会关系方面发挥着关键作用 [4]，其大小应当决定灵长类动物大脑能够掌握并维持的稳定社会关系数量的上限。
The number 150 was established by extrapolating a regression line describing the relationship between group size and relative neocortex size in primates, to humans. [1,2,5,6] That there exists a correlation between group size and relative neocortex size has been replicated in several studies (e. g. [7–14]), though in some cases only for female primates [15,16], but often not finding a significant relationship (making an estimate of Dunbar's number unachievable) [11,14,17,18]. However, the replication studies are of somewhat limited value as most studies have used the same brain data. [14] Additional studies have been made using similar reasoning, but analysing relative brain size instead of relative neocortex size (e. g. [9,19,20]).
150 这一数字，是通过将描述灵长类动物群体规模与相对新皮质大小之间关系的回归线外推至人类而确定的。[1,2,5,6] 群体规模与相对新皮质大小之间存在相关性，这一结果已在多项研究中得到重复验证（例如[7–14]），尽管在某些情况下，这一相关性仅见于雌性灵长类动物 [15,16]，但研究也常常未能发现显著关系（因而无法估计邓巴数）[11,14,17,18]。然而，由于大多数研究使用了相同的脑部数据，这些重复验证研究的价值有一定局限。[14] 另有一些研究采用了类似的推理，但分析的是相对脑大小，而非相对新皮质大小（例如[9,19,20]）。
The expected human group size of 150 has been substantiated by observations of human communities with group sizes ranging between 100 and 200, including hunter–gatherer communities, military units, businesses, 18th-century and Neolithic villages, information from the Domesday Book [2] and Christmas card networks [6].
对规模在 100 至 200 人之间的人类群体的观察，为人类群体规模预期为 150 人提供了佐证；这些观察涵盖狩猎采集群体、军事单位、企业、18 世纪及新石器时代的村落、《末日审判书》（Domesday Book）中的信息 [2]，以及圣诞贺卡往来网络 [6]。
‘Dunbar's number’ is often cited1, has had great impact in popular culture (e.g. it featured prominently in Malcolm Gladwell's book Tipping point [21]) and has had consequences such as the Swedish Tax Authority restructuring their offices to stay within the 150-person limit [22], with the implicit but hopefully unintended assumption that their employees have neither family nor friends outside work.
“邓巴数”经常被引用1，对大众文化产生了重大影响（例如，它在 Malcolm Gladwell 的《引爆点》（Tipping point）一书中占有突出地位 [21]），并带来了一些实际后果，例如瑞典税务局为将人数控制在 150 人的上限之内而重组其办公机构 [22]；此举隐含着一个假设，即其员工在工作之外既无家人也无朋友，希望这并非其本意。
Attempting to make a decisive deconstruction of the empirical basis of Dunbar's number, we here perform Bayesian and generalized least-squares (GLS) phylogenetic comparative analyses on larger datasets of the relationship between group size and both relative brain and relative neocortex sizes, and then extrapolate from these relationships to arrive at an updated estimate of the cognitive limit on human group size, including confidence intervals.
为尝试对邓巴数的实证基础作出决定性的解构，我们在此使用更大的数据集，对群体规模与相对脑大小及相对新皮质大小之间的关系进行贝叶斯和广义最小二乘（GLS）系统发育比较分析，然后根据这些关系进行外推，得出人类群体规模认知上限的更新估计值及其置信区间。
Methods方法
Volumes on brain components were averaged from three sources [18,23,24]. Only measurements of individuals where both brain volume and neocortex volume were available were included in analyses of neocortex size. However, one of the datasets contains brain region measurements that differ significantly from the others [25]. For this reason, we replicated our analyses using data from only the most carefully collected dataset [18]. These results are presented in the electronic supplementary material, appendix S1 (electronic supplementary material, tables S2 and S3). The results from these analyses do not differ in any meaningful way from those provided in the main paper, however. We also present results from measurements of endocranial volumes with corresponding estimates of body mass [26] and replicate all analyses also on anthropoid primates only (electronic supplementary material, tables S1 and S3). Average brain weights, body weights and group size data were taken from one recent study [20], complemented with additional group size data where available [26]. Measures on the human brain [27] and body mass [28] were collected separately. Consensus phylogenies for each dataset were obtained from the 10kTrees website [29]. All brain measures were log-transformed prior to analysis, except when using ratios, that instead were arcsine-square root transformed.
脑各组成部分的体积数据取自三个来源 [18,23,24]，并取其平均值。在新皮质大小的分析中，仅纳入同时具有脑体积和新皮质体积测量值的个体。然而，其中一个数据集所包含的脑区测量值与其他数据集存在显著差异 [25]。因此，我们仅使用采集最为严谨的数据集 [18] 中的数据，重复进行了分析。这些结果见电子补充材料附录 S1（电子补充材料，表 S2 和 S3）。不过，这些分析的结果与正文中提供的结果并无实质性差异。我们还报告了颅腔容积测量值及相应体重估计值 [26] 的分析结果，并仅针对类人猿类灵长类动物（anthropoid primates）重复了全部分析（电子补充材料，表 S1 和 S3）。平均脑重量、体重和群体规模数据取自近期的一项研究 [20]，并在有可用数据时补充了其他群体规模数据 [26]。人类的脑部测量数据 [27] 和体重数据 [28] 则另行收集。各数据集的共识系统发育树均从 10kTrees 网站 [29] 获取。分析前，除比值采用反正弦平方根变换外，所有脑部测量值均进行了对数变换。
All analyses were executed in R using the packages NLME [30], APE [31], MASS [32] and BRMS [33]. We used Bayesian multilevel models with varying intercepts over species specified by a covariance matrix and phylogenetic generalized least-squares (PGLS) regressions throughout. In all Bayesian analyses, group size was modelled as a continuous gamma distribution and not discrete Poisson because available data are averages calculated from different sources. Results for the Bayesian analyses will differ some for each time running the analyses and are only fully reproducible using the same machine. For the Bayesian analyses, uncertainty associated with phylogeny was added by taking random samples from the posterior distribution of the existing random effects, centred around zero and used for calculating confidence intervals. Ninety-five per cent of confidence intervals for GLS were calculated without phylogenetic effects. As electronic supplementary material, appendix S2, we include the R-code and resulting output.
所有分析均在 R 中使用 NLME [30]、APE [31]、MASS [32] 和 BRMS [33] 软件包完成。我们在整个研究中使用了贝叶斯多层模型，其物种间变截距由协方差矩阵指定，并使用了系统发育广义最小二乘（PGLS）回归。在所有贝叶斯分析中，群体规模均采用连续的伽马分布而非离散的泊松分布建模，因为现有数据是根据不同来源计算得出的平均值。贝叶斯分析的结果在每次运行分析时都会略有不同，只有使用同一台计算机才能完全复现。在贝叶斯分析中，我们从现有随机效应的后验分布中随机抽样，将样本以零为中心进行中心化，并用于计算置信区间，以此纳入与系统发育相关的不确定性。GLS 的 95% 置信区间在计算时未纳入系统发育效应。我们在电子补充材料附录 S2 中提供了 R 代码及其运行输出。
Results结果
Our results (table 1) reveal that estimates of expected human group sizes vary depending on method and variable choice (Bayesian approximations between 69.2 and 108.6 and GLE approximations between 16.4 and 42.0). Note that these estimates (as was true for Dunbar's original estimated group size) are averages, not estimates of upper bounds. If an upper constraint from this type of statistical reasoning was to be determined, a better approach would be to specify the upper boundary of the 95% confidence interval. As is shown in table 1, however, 95% confidence intervals yield enormous variation in their estimates, 3.8–520.0 and 2.1–336.3, respectively, and thus indicate upper limits far exceeding 150 in almost all cases.
我们的结果（表 1）表明，人类预期群体规模的估计值因方法和变量选择而异（贝叶斯近似估计值介于 69.2 和 108.6 之间，GLE 近似估计值介于 16.4 和 42.0 之间）。请注意，这些估计值（与 Dunbar 最初估计的群体规模一样）是平均值，而非上限的估计值。如果要通过这类统计推理确定一个上限，更好的方法是采用 95% 置信区间的上界。然而，如表 1 所示，95% 置信区间的估计范围极大，分别为 3.8–520.0 和 2.1–336.3，因此几乎所有情况下所指示的上限都远超 150。
The best model to test the hypothesis, given Dunbar's original formulation (a cognitive limit on group size deduced from relative neocortex size) and the current state-of-the-art of comparative phylogenetic studies, is the Bayesian analyses of group size using the volume of the neocortex, with the volume of the rest of the brain included as a covariate. The estimate from this analysis indicates a human group size average of 69.2 individuals, with a 95% confidence interval ranging from 3.8 to 292.0 individuals. This is not very informative. We also provide results from other methods and variable choices, for comparison, both in table 1 and in electronic supplementary material, appendix S1. These do not provide estimates with more enlightening estimated confidence intervals, though one of our estimates actually came close to 150 (electronic supplementary material, appendix S1; table 1, top row: 134.0), but again, the confidence intervals were enormous (7.0 to 583.2).
考虑到 Dunbar 最初的表述（根据相对新皮层大小推断群体规模的认知上限），以及当前比较系统发育研究的最先进方法，检验这一假说的最佳模型是使用新皮层体积对群体规模进行贝叶斯分析，并将大脑其余部分的体积纳入协变量。该分析的估计结果表明，人类群体规模平均为 69.2 人，95% 置信区间为 3.8 至 292.0 人。这一结果所能提供的信息十分有限。我们还在表 1 和电子补充材料附录 S1 中提供了采用其他方法和变量选择所得的结果，以供比较。这些结果的估计置信区间也未能提供更多有启发性的信息；尽管我们的一项估计值实际上接近 150（电子补充材料附录 S1；表 1，首行：134.0），但其置信区间同样极大（7.0 至 583.2）。
Discussion讨论
Most research on primate social evolution has not concerned cognitive limitations but instead generally focused on the so-called ‘socio-ecological model of primate social evolution’ where primate group size mainly is determined by socio-ecological factors having to do with foraging and predation, infanticide and sexual selection—not on brain or neocortex size. According to this model of social evolution, females go where it is safe and where there are resources, while males go where the females are [35–38].
大多数关于灵长类动物社会演化的研究并不涉及认知限制，而是通常聚焦于所谓的“灵长类动物社会演化的社会生态模型”；该模型认为，灵长类动物的群体规模主要由与觅食和捕食、杀婴及性选择有关的社会生态因素决定，而非由大脑或新皮层的大小决定。根据这一社会演化模型，雌性前往安全且有资源的地方，而雄性则前往雌性所在的地方 [35–38]。
Further, it is easily observed that human brains function differently from those of other primates [38–42], as is evidenced by the existence of cumulative cultural evolution resulting in marvels such as Stockholm, symphonies and science [43–45]. This was concisely summarized by de Ruiter et al. [22] in their examination of Dunbar's number: ‘Dunbar's assumption that the evolution of human brain physiology corresponds with a limit in our capacity to maintain relationships ignores the cultural mechanisms, practices, and social structures that humans develop to counter potential deficiencies’.
此外，很容易观察到，人脑的运作方式与其他灵长类动物的大脑不同 [38–42]，累积性文化演化的存在便是明证，它造就了斯德哥尔摩、交响乐和科学等非凡成就 [43–45]。de Ruiter 等人 [22] 在考察 Dunbar 数时对此作了简明概括：“Dunbar 假定人脑生理特征的演化对应着我们维持关系能力的上限，这忽视了人类为弥补潜在不足而发展出的文化机制、实践和社会结构。”
Also, researchers have disputed the empirical observation of mean human group sizes approximately averaging around 150 persons, presenting empirical observations of group sizes indicating a wide variety of other numbers [46–53]. Thus, ecological research on primate sociality, the uniqueness of human thinking and empirical observations all indicate that there is no hard cognitive limit on human sociality. Our reanalysis provides the last piece of evidence needed to disregard Dunbar's number.
此外，研究者也对人类群体规模平均约为 150 人这一经验观察提出了质疑，并提供了群体规模的实证观察结果，显示出各种不同的数值 [46–53]。因此，关于灵长类动物社会性的生态学研究、人类思维的独特性以及实证观察均表明，人类社会性并不存在严格的认知上限。我们的重新分析提供了摒弃 Dunbar 数所需的最后一项证据。
In summary, extrapolating human cognitive limits from regressions on non-human primate data is of limited value for both theoretical and empirical reasons. It is our hope, though perhaps futile, that this study will put an end to the use of ‘Dunbar's number’ within science and in popular media. ‘Dunbar's number’ is a concept with limited theoretical foundation lacking empirical support.
总之，无论从理论还是实证角度来看，通过对非人灵长类动物数据进行回归来外推人类认知上限，其价值都十分有限。我们希望，尽管这一希望或许徒劳，本研究能终结科学界和大众媒体对“Dunbar 数”的使用。“Dunbar 数”是一个理论基础有限且缺乏实证支持的概念。
