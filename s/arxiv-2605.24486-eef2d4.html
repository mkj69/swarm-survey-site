摘要Abstract
近期，长程智能体任务的进展主要得益于通过更强的模型、更好的工具和更有效的支撑框架来纵向扩展单个智能体。 Recent progress on long-horizon agentic tasks has been driven largely by scaling up individual agents through stronger models, better tools, and more effective scaffolding. 相比之下，人们对横向扩展的理解要少得多：多个面向同一任务的对等智能体，能否在不依赖显式角色分工或工作流编排的情况下，成为能力提升的额外来源。 In contrast, much less is understood about scaling out: whether multiple peer agents, all targeting the same task, can become an additional source of capability without relying on explicit role specialization or workflow orchestration. 我们研究了这一问题，并提出 AgentFugue，这是一种围绕共享推理中枢构建的集体推理框架。 We study this question and propose AgentFugue, a collective reasoning framework built around a shared reasoning hub. 当对等智能体并行探索同一任务时，该中枢会以简明笔记记录每个智能体已确立、尝试或排除的内容，并使每个智能体能够以有助于其当前搜索的形式，选择性地获取其他智能体的发现。 As peer agents explore the same task in parallel, the hub records concise notes on what each agent has established, attempted, or ruled out, and enables each agent to selectively access what other agents have discovered in a form useful for its current search. 这一设计无需集中式规划，即可将原本相互孤立的轨迹转变为彼此关联、可复用中间推理的生态系统。 This design turns otherwise isolated trajectories into a connected ecology of reusable intermediate reasoning without requiring centralized planning. 我们将该中枢实现为一个插件式通信层，并通过监督微调和端到端强化学习对其进行训练。 We instantiate the hub as a plug-in communication layer, trained with supervised fine-tuning and end-to-end reinforcement learning. 在我们研究的各类具有挑战性的长程任务场景中，AgentFugue 的表现均优于强基线。 Across the challenging long-horizon settings we study, AgentFugue improves over strong baselines. 我们的结果表明，集体推理能够使对等智能体系统的横向扩展成为一种独立的能力提升来源，而不仅仅是消耗更多计算资源的方式。 Our results suggest that collective reasoning can turn scaling out peer agent systems into a distinct source of capability gains, rather than merely a way of spending more compute. 我们的代码可在 https://github.com/qhjqhj00/cabeza 获取 Our code is available at https://github.com/qhjqhj00/cabeza
1 引言1 Introduction
近期进展表明，基于大语言模型（LLM）的智能体能够在复杂的长程任务上取得出色表现 (Nakano et al., 2021; Qiao et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Zheng et al., 2025; Jin et al., 2025a)。 Recent progress has shown that LLM-based agents can perform remarkably well on complex long-horizon tasks (Nakano et al., 2021; Qiao et al., 2025; Chen et al., 2026; Ye et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025a; Zheng et al., 2025; Jin et al., 2025a). 推动这一进展的一个关键因素是在多个维度上持续进行纵向扩展，包括更强的基础模型 (OpenAI, 2023; Team, 2024; DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Team, 2025)、更好的工具使用能力 (Schick et al., 2023; Patil et al., 2024; Qin et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Li et al., 2025d; Li et al., 2025e; Jin et al., 2025a)，以及更有效的智能体支撑框架 (Yao et al., 2023b; Shinn et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a; Jin et al., 2025b)。 A key driver of this progress is sustained scaling up along several dimensions, including stronger foundation models (OpenAI, 2023; Team, 2024; DeepSeek-AI, 2025; Team, 2025), better tool use (Schick et al., 2023; Patil et al., 2024; Qin et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2024b; Li et al., 2025d; Li et al., 2025e; Jin et al., 2025a), and more effective agent scaffolding (Yao et al., 2023b; Shinn et al., 2023; Yao et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a; Jin et al., 2025b). 这种纵向扩展范式显著拓展了单个智能体的能力范围。 This scaling-up paradigm has substantially expanded what a single agent can do. 然而，与此同时，它增强的是单条轨迹的能力，而非探索的广度，因此，复杂任务能否也从超越单个智能体的扩展中获益，仍是一个有待解答的问题。 At the same time, however, it improves the strength of one trajectory rather than the breadth of exploration, leaving open the question of whether complex tasks may also benefit from scaling beyond a single agent.
既有研究也表明，多智能体系统能够有效处理复杂任务 (Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023)，但其主要侧重点一直是编排：为不同智能体分配不同角色 (Qian et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024b)、将任务分解为独立子任务 (Shen et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a; Team, 2026)，或设计显式的交互工作流 (Zhuge et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025)。 Prior work has also shown that multi-agent systems can be effective for complex tasks (Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023), but the dominant emphasis has been on orchestration: assigning different roles to different agents (Qian et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024b), decomposing tasks into separate subtasks (Shen et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a; Team, 2026), or designing explicit interaction workflows (Zhuge et al., 2024; Liu et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2025; Zhang et al., 2025). 另一条互补的研究路线通过审议来协调多个智能体 (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a)。 A complementary line of work coordinates multiple agents through deliberation (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a). 此类方法通过结构化协调来提升能力，不同智能体以不同方式作出贡献。 Such approaches improve capability through structured coordination, with different agents contributing in different ways. 人们尚不甚了解的是，在一种更简单的设定下，即多个智能体以对等身份处理同一任务，而非按预先定义的职责进行划分，是否也能获得能力提升。 What remains less understood is whether gains can also arise in a simpler setting, where multiple agents act as peers on the same task rather than being separated by pre-defined responsibilities.
这种对等设定为能力增长创造了另一种机会。 This peer setting creates a different opportunity for capability growth. 当多个智能体并行探索同一任务时 (Wang et al., 2023b; Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024)，它们可能发现不同的局部推理路径、中间证据或失败分支。 When multiple agents explore the same task in parallel (Wang et al., 2023b; Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2025; Li et al., 2024), they may uncover different partial reasoning paths, intermediate evidence, or failed branches. 我们研究这种并行探索本身能否成为额外能力的来源，而不仅仅意味着额外的计算投入。 We study whether such parallel exploration can itself become a source of additional capability, rather than merely additional compute. 这就是我们使用“横向扩展”（scaling out）一词时的含义：增加处理同一任务的对等智能体的数量或多样性，使它们的轨迹能够相互提供信息，并引导彼此调整方向。 This is the sense in which we use the term scaling out: increasing the number or diversity of peer agents working on the same task so that their trajectories can inform and redirect one another. 然而，实现这一收益并非易事。 Realizing this benefit, however, is non-trivial. 在没有通信的情况下，多个智能体基本上退化为彼此孤立的搜索，其结果必须在事后合并 (Wang et al., 2023b; Brown et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026)；而在通信不受限制的情况下，有用信号可能被原始轨迹中的噪声淹没 (Li et al., 2025b)，探索的多样性也可能迅速丧失。 Without communication, multiple agents largely reduce to isolated searches whose results must be merged after the fact (Wang et al., 2023b; Brown et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Lee et al., 2026); with unrestricted communication, useful signals can be overwhelmed by raw trajectory noise (Li et al., 2025b), and the diversity of exploration may quickly collapse. 因此，我们认为，有效的横向扩展需要一种集体推理机制，使对等智能体能够有选择地交流中间进展，同时继续从不同方向探索同一任务。 We therefore argue that effective scaling out requires a mechanism for collective reasoning, through which peer agents can selectively exchange intermediate progress while continuing to explore the same task from different directions. 从这个意义上说，集体推理最适合被理解为一种赋格式的并行搜索结构，而非共享对话 (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a)：如同巴洛克赋格曲，多条轨迹保持各自独立，同时吸收并发展彼此取得的局部进展 (Mann, 1987)。 In this sense, collective reasoning is best understood not as a shared conversation (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024; Chen et al., 2024a), but as a fugue-like structure of parallel search: in the spirit of a Baroque fugue, multiple trajectories remain distinct while still picking up and developing one another’s partial progress (Mann, 1987).
为实现这种形式的集体推理，我们提出 AgentFugue，这是一个围绕共享推理枢纽构建的框架。 To realize this form of collective reasoning, we propose AgentFugue, a framework built around a shared reasoning hub. 该枢纽充当外部通信层，而非集中式规划器：当一个智能体完成一段连贯的交互过程时，枢纽会记录一则简短笔记，概述该智能体已确立的结论、尝试过的方法或排除的可能性，随后允许其他智能体有选择地访问这些进展中对自身搜索有用的部分。 The hub serves as an external communication layer rather than a centralized planner: when an agent completes a coherent episode of interaction, the hub records a compact note about what that agent established, attempted, or ruled out, and later allows other agents to selectively access the parts of that progress that are useful for their own search. 由于该枢纽附加于核心策略之外，其理念类似于单智能体场景中研究的外置记忆模块 (Chhikara et al., 2025; Fang et al., 2025; Tan et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2026)，因此 AgentFugue 能够适配不同的推理智能体，同时保留其局部轨迹的独立性。 Because the hub is attached outside the core policy, similar in spirit to externalized memory modules studied for single-agent settings (Chhikara et al., 2025; Fang et al., 2025; Tan et al., 2026; Xu et al., 2025; Hu et al., 2026), AgentFugue is adaptive to different reasoning agents while preserving the independence of their local trajectories.
这一设计使我们能够研究两种互补的横向扩展形式。 This design lets us study two complementary forms of scaling out. 在同质团队中，多个智能体采用相同的骨干模型和配置，因此任何增益都必须来自并行轨迹之间的交互，而非预设的角色差异。 In homogeneous teams, multiple agents share the same backbone and configuration, so any gain must come from interaction among parallel trajectories rather than from built-in role differences. 在异质团队中，智能体采用不同的模型或设置，使不同的推理偏向能够在同一任务上相互补充 (Wang et al., 2025)。 In heterogeneous teams, agents differ in model or setup, making it possible for distinct reasoning biases to complement one another on the same task (Wang et al., 2025). 在这两种设置下，核心实证问题都是：集体推理能否不仅提高团队层面的成功率，还能提升构成团队的各条个体轨迹的质量和效率。 Across both settings, the central empirical question is whether collective reasoning can improve not only team-level success, but also the quality and efficiency of the individual trajectories that make up the team. 在我们的实现中，共享推理枢纽与任务智能体本身分别进行优化。 In our implementation, the shared reasoning hub is optimized separately from the task agents themselves. 我们使用一个中等规模的语言模型实现其写入和读取功能，然后先通过监督微调、再通过端到端强化学习加以改进，使枢纽不仅学会总结中间进展，还学会返回在完整智能体循环中有用的指导。 We instantiate its write and read functions with a moderate-sized language model, then improve them through supervised fine-tuning followed by end-to-end reinforcement learning so that the hub learns not only to summarize intermediate progress, but also to return guidance that is useful inside the full agent loop.
我们在具有挑战性的长程基准上评估 AgentFugue，涵盖信息搜寻、开放式问题求解和多步网页推理。 We evaluate AgentFugue on challenging long-horizon benchmarks spanning information seeking, open-ended problem solving, and multi-step web reasoning. 在所研究的各类设置中，我们观察到同质团队和异质团队均取得了增益，这支持了以下观点：除增强单个智能体之外，同级智能体之间的通信也能提供一种稳健的能力来源。 Across the settings we study, we observe gains in both homogeneous and heterogeneous teams, supporting the view that peer-agent communication can provide a robust source of capability beyond stronger individual agents alone.
我们的贡献有三个方面：(1) 我们将同级智能体扩展确定为长程推理中的一种独特设置，其中多个智能体处理同一任务，能力提升必须来自跨轨迹复用，而非角色专门化。 Our contributions are threefold: (1) we identify peer-agent scaling as a distinct setting for long-horizon reasoning, in which multiple agents work on the same task and capability must arise from cross-trajectory reuse rather than role specialization. (2) 我们提出 AgentFugue，这是一个基于写入、检索和读取共享推理片段的通信框架，无需集中式规划，即可将并行轨迹转化为有选择地共享信息的推理生态。 (2) we propose AgentFugue, a communication framework based on writing, retrieving, and reading shared reasoning episodes, which turns parallel trajectories into a selectively shared reasoning ecology without centralized planning. (3) 我们在同质团队和异质团队中研究这一框架，并通过分析检验横向扩展何时能提高单个智能体的效率、何时能带来更大的团队层面增益，以及通信机制在哪些情况下会失效。 (3) we study this framework in both homogeneous and heterogeneous teams, with analyses designed to test when scaling out improves per-agent efficiency, when it yields larger team-level gains, and where the communication mechanism breaks down.

2.1 问题设置2.1 Problem Setting
目标知识空间。Target knowledge space.
考虑一个长程任务实例 $x$，其求解需要汇集一整套证据与推理，我们将其称为目标知识空间 $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$。 Consider a long-horizon task instance $x$ whose solution requires assembling a body of evidence and reasoning that we call the target knowledge space $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ . 对于困难任务，$\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ 规模庞大且结构复杂：它可能涵盖多种证据类型、推理链和验证步骤。 For hard tasks, $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ is large and structurally complex: it may span multiple evidence types, reasoning chains, and verification steps. 在单智能体运行中，智能体沿轨迹 $\tau$ 进行探索，并逐步积累已发现的子空间 $\mathcal{K}(\tau)\subseteq\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$。 In a single-agent run, the agent explores a trajectory $\tau$ and accumulates a discovered subspace $\mathcal{K}(\tau)\subseteq\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ . 任何单条轨迹都不太可能完全覆盖 $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$，因为每次运行触及的都是不同的、局部的且往往并非最优的片段，这部分取决于能力，部分取决于恰好探索了哪些分支的运气。 Any single trajectory is unlikely to cover $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ fully, since each run touches a different, partial, and often sub-optimal fragment, partly by skill and partly by the luck of which branches happen to be explored. 通过让多个同级智能体处理同一任务来进行横向扩展，可以为它们发现的片段创造相互补充的机会，但前提是这些片段能够被共享。 Scaling out, by running multiple peer agents on the same task, creates the opportunity for their discovered fragments to complement one another, but only if the fragments can be shared.
任务、团队与轨迹。Task, team, and trajectories.
我们将这一设置形式化如下。 We formalize this setting as follows. 一个由 $N$ 个智能体组成的团队共同面向同一个任务实例 $x$。 A team of $N$ agents all target the same task instance $x$ . 智能体 $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$ 通过推理步骤、工具调用和观测与环境交互，产生一条局部轨迹，其截至步骤 $t$ 的前缀为 Agent $i\in\{1,\ldots,N\}$ interacts with the environment through reasoning steps, tool calls, and observations, producing a local trajectory whose prefix up to step $t$ is 
| | $\tau_{i,t}=\big[(a_{i,1},o_{i,1}),\ldots,(a_{i,t},o_{i,t})\big],$ | | (1)
其中，$a_{i,t}$ 为动作，$o_{i,t}$ 为该动作产生的观测。 where $a_{i,t}$ is an action and $o_{i,t}$ the resulting observation. 每条轨迹代表穿越 $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ 的一条不同探索路径，并具有其自身发现的子空间 $\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i})$。 Each trajectory represents a different exploratory path through $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ , with its own discovered subspace $\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i})$ .
共享推理枢纽。Shared reasoning hub.
为连接这些分散的片段，我们为团队增设了一个共享推理枢纽 $\mathcal{H}$。 To connect these scattered fragments, the team is augmented with a shared reasoning hub $\mathcal{H}$ . 如图 1 所示，该枢纽与各对等智能体并列，充当团队层面的通信接口：它将各智能体推理历史中已完成的部分压缩为可复用的笔记，并允许智能体在搜索过程中查阅彼此的进展。 As shown in Figure 1, the hub sits alongside the peer agents as a team-level communication interface: it compresses completed portions of each agent’s reasoning history into reusable notes and allows agents to consult one another’s progress during search. 它的作用并非取代局部推理或集中协调团队，而是使一条轨迹产生的中间发现能够有选择地供其他智能体使用，从而将每个智能体的有效知识空间扩展至其自身轨迹所覆盖的范围之外。 Its role is not to replace local reasoning or to centrally orchestrate the team, but to make intermediate discoveries produced by one trajectory selectively available to others, thereby expanding each agent’s effective knowledge space beyond what its own trajectory covers.
交互片段（episode）。Episodes.
为使阶段性进展能够共享，我们将每条局部轨迹划分为若干已完成的交互片段。 To make partial progress shareable, we divide each local trajectory into completed episodes. 交互片段 $\epsilon_{i,e}$ 是由固定的局部上下文预算界定的一段连续交互历史： An episode $\epsilon_{i,e}$ is a contiguous chunk of interaction history determined by a fixed local context budget: 
| | $\epsilon_{i,e}=\big[(a_{i,t_{s}},o_{i,t_{s}}),\ldots,(a_{i,t_{e}},o_{i,t_{e}})\big].$ | | (2)
一旦当前上下文达到预算上限，便对累积的片段进行摘要并写入枢纽。 Once the active context reaches the budget, the accumulated segment is summarized and written to the hub. 因此，交互片段是智能体通过 $\mathcal{H}$ 向团队其他成员呈现其阶段性进展的基本单位。 Episodes are therefore the units through which an agent’s partial progress becomes visible to the rest of the team through $\mathcal{H}$ . 在搜索过程中的任意时刻，智能体既可以沿自身的局部轨迹继续探索，也可以查阅 $\mathcal{H}$，以获取其他团队成员产生的相关进展。 At any point during search, an agent may either continue along its own local trajectory or consult $\mathcal{H}$ to access relevant progress produced by other team members.
这一形式化表述涵盖了几种有用的极限情形。 This formulation subsumes several useful limiting cases. 当 $N{=}1$ 时，它退化为一个与外部类记忆模块耦合的单一推理智能体。 When $N{=}1$ , it reduces to a single reasoning agent coupled with an external memory-like module. 当 $N{>}1$ 但没有智能体查阅枢纽时，它退化为多条彼此隔离的轨迹，这些轨迹共享计算资源，但不共享信息。 When $N{>}1$ but no agent consults the hub, it reduces to multiple isolated trajectories that share compute but not information. 我们主要关注介于这两个极端之间的情形：由对等智能体组成的团队，其中多个智能体执行同一任务，同时有选择地复用彼此的中间推理。 Our main interest lies between these extremes: peer-agent teams in which multiple agents pursue the same task while selectively reusing one another’s intermediate reasoning.
横向扩展的两种形式。Two forms of scaling out.
我们研究这一设定的两种形式。 We study this setting in two forms. 在同构团队中，所有智能体使用相同的模型和配置，因此任何收益都必须源于跨轨迹交互，而非智能体固有的差异。 In homogeneous teams, all agents share the same model and configuration, so any gains must arise from cross-trajectory interaction rather than built-in agent differences. 在异构团队中，智能体采用不同的骨干模型或提示配置，从而引入超出随机变化的系统性多样性：不同模型具有不同的推理偏向、知识分布和失效模式，因此枢纽还能够促进团队成员之间的优势互补。 In heterogeneous teams, agents differ in model backbone or prompting configuration, introducing systematic diversity beyond stochastic variation: different models carry different reasoning biases, knowledge distributions, and failure modes, so the hub can additionally mediate complementary strengths across the team.
从孤立片段到相互连接的知识。From isolated fragments to connected knowledge.
在没有通信的情况下，团队的集体知识 $\bigcup_{i}\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i})$ 仅存在于汇总意义上：任何单个智能体都无法获取其他智能体的发现，因此每个智能体仍受限于自身的知识片段。 Without communication, the team’s collective knowledge $\bigcup_{i}\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i})$ exists only in aggregate: no individual agent can access another’s discoveries, so each remains limited to its own fragment. $\mathcal{H}$ 的作用是使一条轨迹中的有用部分能够有选择地供另一条轨迹使用，以此连接这些分散的片段，将每个智能体的有效知识空间扩展至仅由 $\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i,t})$ 所界定的范围之外。 The role of $\mathcal{H}$ is to connect these scattered fragments by making useful portions of one trajectory selectively available to another, expanding each agent’s effective knowledge space beyond $\mathcal{K}(\tau_{i,t})$ alone. 这一视角阐明了横向扩展的潜力及其局限：增加智能体会提高所发现片段的多样性，但边际收益取决于新轨迹是否触及解决 $x$ 所需的任务相关知识中真正全新的区域（这些知识在概念上记为 $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$），以及枢纽能否在需要时呈现这些区域。 This perspective clarifies both the promise and the limit of scaling out: adding agents increases the diversity of discovered fragments, but the marginal gain depends on whether new trajectories reach genuinely new regions of the task-relevant knowledge needed to solve $x$ , denoted conceptually by $\mathcal{K}^{*}(x)$ , and whether the hub can surface those regions when they are needed. 本节其余部分将介绍实现这一思路的枢纽机制（§2.2），以及我们如何对其进行优化（§2.3）。 The rest of this section describes the hub mechanism that operationalizes this view (§2.2) and how we optimize it (§2.3).
2.2 共享推理枢纽2.2 Shared Reasoning Hub
AgentFugue 通过两种操作实现共享推理枢纽：交互片段写入，将已完成的轨迹片段压缩为可复用的笔记；意图驱动的读取，使智能体能够按需查看并综合队友的相关交互片段。 AgentFugue operationalizes the shared reasoning hub through two operations: episode writing, which compresses completed trajectory segments into reusable notes, and intent-driven reading, which lets agents inspect and synthesize relevant teammate episodes on demand.
交互片段写入与上下文逐出。Episode writing and context eviction.
如图 1 右上面板所示，智能体 $i$ 的局部上下文窗口不断累积推理步骤、工具调用和观测，直至达到固定的写入预算，此时将当前片段结束并形成一个交互片段 $\epsilon_{i,e}$。 As illustrated in the top-right panel of Figure 1, agent $i$ ’s local context window accumulates reasoning steps, tool calls, and observations until it reaches a fixed write budget, at which point the current segment is closed as an episode $\epsilon_{i,e}$ . 随后，枢纽模型将该交互片段压缩为一则交互片段笔记： The hub model then compresses the episode into an episode note: 
| | $z_{i,e}=M_{\mathrm{write}}(\epsilon_{i,e}),$ | | (3)
该笔记记录了这一交互片段中与团队相关的内容：得出了哪些结论、收集了哪些证据、进行了哪些尝试，以及排除了哪些分支。 which captures the team-relevant content of that episode: what was established, what evidence was collected, what was attempted, and which branches were ruled out. 笔记写入后，智能体工作上下文中的原始交互片段内容会被逐出，并由对应的交互片段笔记 $z_{i,e}$ 替代。 Once the note is written, the raw episode content in the agent’s working context is evicted and replaced by its episode note $z_{i,e}$ . 这有双重作用：一是压缩智能体自身的历史，为持续探索释放上下文容量；二是生成适合与其他智能体共享的表示。 This serves a dual purpose: it compresses the agent’s own history to free context capacity for continued exploration, and it produces a representation suitable for sharing with other agents. 完整的交互片段内容 $\epsilon_{i,e}$ 保留在枢纽的存储中，以供后续深入阅读。 The full episode content $\epsilon_{i,e}$ is retained in the hub’s storage for later deep reading.
因此，在搜索过程中的任意时刻，智能体 $i$ 的工作上下文具有如下形式： At any point during search, agent $i$ ’s working context therefore takes the form: 
| | $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}=\big[\,\underbrace{z_{i,1},\ldots,z_{i,e-1}}_{\text{own episode notes}},\;\;\underbrace{z_{j_{1},e^{\prime}},\ldots,z_{j_{k},e^{\prime\prime}}}_{\text{teammates' notes}},\;\;\underbrace{\tau_{i,t}^{\mathrm{active}}}_{\text{current interaction}}\,\big],$ | | (4)
其中，第一组包含概述智能体 $i$ 自身已完成回合的回合笔记，第二组包含通过先前与中枢的交互而变得可见的其他智能体的回合笔记，$\tau_{i,t}^{\mathrm{active}}$ 则是当前尚未完成的交互片段。 where the first group contains episode notes summarizing agent $i$ ’s own completed episodes, the second group contains episode notes from other agents that have been made visible through prior hub interactions, and $\tau_{i,t}^{\mathrm{active}}$ is the current unfinished interaction segment. 这种设计使工作上下文即使在总推理投入不断增加时也能保持在有限范围内，同时以结构化方式呈现团队的整体进展。 This design keeps the working context bounded even as total reasoning effort grows, while exposing a structured view of the team’s collective progress.
意图驱动的读取。Intent-driven reading.
如图 1 右下方的面板所示，智能体不会被动接收所有队友的回合笔记。 As shown in the bottom-right panel of Figure 1, agents do not passively receive all teammate episode notes. 相反，当智能体 $i$ 根据其当前上下文 $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ 判断，更深入地查阅某位队友的工作会有所帮助时，它会向中枢发出包含两个组成部分的结构化请求：描述所需信息类型的意图 $q_{i,t}$，以及表明它希望完整查看哪些队友回合的回合引用集合 $\mathcal{E}_{i,t}\subseteq\{\epsilon_{j,e}\mid j\neq i\}$。 Instead, when agent $i$ judges, based on its current context $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ , that consulting a teammate’s work in greater depth would be useful, it issues a structured request to the hub with two components: an intent $q_{i,t}$ describing what kind of information is needed, and a set of episode references $\mathcal{E}_{i,t}\subseteq\{\epsilon_{j,e}\mid j\neq i\}$ indicating which teammates’ episodes it wants to inspect in full. 智能体根据 $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ 中已经可见的回合笔记来选择这些引用：例如，某条回合笔记可能表明另一智能体发现了与当前搜索方向相关的证据，从而促使该智能体请求查看原始回合。 The agent selects these references based on the episode notes already visible in $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ : for example, an episode note may indicate that another agent found evidence related to the current search direction, prompting a request for the original episode.
收到该请求后，中枢会从存储中检索所引用回合的完整原始内容，并根据该意图对这些内容进行综合： Given this request, the hub retrieves the full raw content of the referenced episodes from its storage and synthesizes them in light of the intent: 
| | $r_{i,t}=M_{\mathrm{read}}\!\big(q_{i,t},\;\;\{\epsilon_{j,e}:\epsilon_{j,e}\in\mathcal{E}_{i,t}\}\big).$ | | (5)
由此得到的读取结果 $r_{i,t}$ 是一段针对请求智能体当前需求定制的、有针对性的证据或指导，并被追加到 $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ 中。 The resulting readout $r_{i,t}$ is a focused piece of evidence or guidance tailored to the requesting agent’s current need, which is appended to $\mathcal{C}_{i,t}$ . 在这种设计中，回合笔记提供粗粒度的态势感知，帮助智能体识别哪些回合值得查看，而中枢则对所引用的原始内容执行实际的综合处理。 In this design, episode notes provide coarse awareness and help the agent identify which episodes are worth inspecting, while the hub performs the actual synthesis over the raw referenced content. 这种双层设计通过回合笔记提供整体态势感知，通过意图驱动的读取实现有选择的深入了解，从而避免了完全不通信和全面广播这两个极端。 This two-level design, with episode notes for broad awareness and intent-driven reading for selective depth, avoids both extremes of no communication and full broadcast. 智能体通过回合笔记以较低开销掌握团队进展概况，并可在需要更深入的信息时详细查看特定回合。 Agents maintain a lightweight overview of team progress through episode notes and can drill into specific episodes when deeper information is needed.
与相近范式的区别。Distinction from nearby paradigms.
该写入/读取机制在若干重要方面与一些相近的设置有所不同。 The write/read mechanism differs from several adjacent settings in important ways. 与单智能体记忆不同，笔记的撰写旨在支持跨智能体复用，而不仅仅服务于产生这些笔记的轨迹。 Unlike single-agent memory, notes are written to support cross-agent reuse, not just the originating trajectory. 与多智能体辩论或群聊不同，智能体无需被迫同步轮流发言，也无需共享同一个对话上下文。 Unlike multi-agent debate or group chat, agents are not forced into synchronized turn-taking or a shared conversational context. 与 best-of- $N$ 采样不同，各条轨迹会在完成之前通过可复用的中间进展相互影响。 Unlike best-of- $N$ sampling, trajectories influence one another before completion through reusable intermediate progress. 此外，与针对静态语料库的 RAG 式检索不同，读取路径由意图驱动，按需综合原始回合内容，而不是返回预先形成的文本段落。 And unlike RAG-style retrieval over a static corpus, the read path is intent-driven and synthesizes raw episode content on demand rather than returning pre-formed passages.
2.3 中枢优化2.3 Hub Optimization
中枢以 Qwen3.5-9B 为骨干模型进行初始化，$M_{\mathrm{write}}$ / $M_{\mathrm{read}}$ 是从同一检查点初始化的独立实例，并分两个阶段进行优化。 The hub is initialized from a Qwen3.5-9B backbone, with separate $M_{\mathrm{write}}$ / $M_{\mathrm{read}}$ instances from the same checkpoint, and optimized in two stages.
监督微调。Supervised fine-tuning.
教师模型为每个已完成的回合生成参考笔记 $z^{*}$，并为每个读取请求生成参考读取结果 $r^{*}$，由此得到 $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{write}}$ 和 $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{read}}$。 A teacher model produces reference notes $z^{*}$ for each completed episode and reference readouts $r^{*}$ for each read request, yielding $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{write}}$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{read}}$ . 使用标准语言模型（LM）损失对两个头进行联合训练： Both heads are trained jointly with the standard LM loss: 
| | $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SFT}}=-\mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{write}}}\!\big[\log\pi_{\theta}(z^{*}\!\mid\!\epsilon)\big]-\mathbb{E}_{\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{read}}}\!\big[\log\pi_{\theta}(r^{*}\!\mid\!q,\mathcal{E})\big].$ | | (6)
组相对策略优化。Group relative policy optimization.
随后，我们在完整的多智能体循环中通过 GRPO 使中枢与下游任务成功目标对齐，同时保持任务智能体的参数冻结。 We then align the hub with downstream task success via GRPO in the full multi-agent loop, keeping task agents frozen. 对于实例 $x$，采样 $G$ 个候选中枢输出 $\{y_{g}\}$，分别使用每个输出运行该循环，观测奖励 $\{R_{g}\}$，构造组相对优势 $\hat{A}_{g}=(R_{g}-\mathrm{mean})/(\mathrm{std}+\varepsilon)$，并进行优化： For instance $x$ , sample $G$ candidate hub outputs $\{y_{g}\}$ , run the loop with each, observe rewards $\{R_{g}\}$ , form group-relative advantages $\hat{A}_{g}=(R_{g}-\mathrm{mean})/(\mathrm{std}+\varepsilon)$ , and optimize: 
| | $\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{GRPO}}=\mathbb{E}\!\Big[\tfrac{1}{G}\!\sum_{g}\min\!\big(\rho_{g}\hat{A}_{g},\,\mathrm{clip}(\rho_{g},1{-}\delta,1{+}\delta)\hat{A}_{g}\big)\Big]-\beta\,D_{\mathrm{KL}}\!\big[\pi_{\theta}\,\|\,\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}\big],$ | | (7)
其中，$\rho_{g}=\pi_{\theta}(y_{g})/\pi_{\mathrm{old}}(y_{g})$，$\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ 为 SFT 检查点，$R_{g}$ 则将任务成功奖励与简洁性奖励相结合，后者偏好能够带来更短有效搜索路径的中枢输出。 with $\rho_{g}=\pi_{\theta}(y_{g})/\pi_{\mathrm{old}}(y_{g})$ , $\pi_{\mathrm{ref}}$ the SFT checkpoint, and $R_{g}$ combining task success with a brevity bonus that favors hub outputs leading to shorter effective search paths. 由于任务智能体的参数被冻结，GRPO 的优化压力作用于通信层本身。 Because task agents are frozen, GRPO pressure lands on the communication layer itself.

5 结论5 Conclusion
本文研究将横向扩展作为长程智能体推理的一个互补维度：我们关注的并非如何增强单条轨迹，而是多条对等轨迹能否在解决同一任务时相互促进。 This paper studies scaling out as a complementary axis for long-horizon agentic reasoning: instead of making a single trajectory stronger, we ask whether multiple peer trajectories can improve one another while solving the same task. AgentFugue 通过一个共享推理中枢将这一想法付诸实现，该中枢根据已完成的片段撰写简洁笔记，并支持以意图为导向读取队友轨迹。 AgentFugue operationalizes this idea with a shared reasoning hub that writes compact notes from completed episodes and supports intent-driven reading over teammate trajectories. 在同质和异质团队中，结果均表明，这一通信层既能提升团队整体表现，也能提高单条轨迹的质量，这说明对等智能体扩展可以超越独立采样加最终聚合的模式。 Across homogeneous and heterogeneous teams, the results show that this communication layer improves both team-level performance and individual trajectory quality, suggesting that peer-agent scaling can be more than independent sampling plus final aggregation. 尚待解决的挑战是，使这种通信具有足够的选择性以保持多样性，同时具备足够的可靠性，以防止具有误导性的中间假设在团队中传播。 The remaining challenge is to make such communication selective enough to preserve diversity while reliable enough to prevent misleading intermediate hypotheses from spreading through the team.

附录 G 局限性与更广泛的影响Appendix G Limitations and Broader Impact
局限性。Limitations.
本研究探讨，在同一任务上横向扩展对等智能体的数量，能否通过集体推理带来能力提升。 This work studies whether scaling out peer agents on the same task can yield capability gains through collective reasoning. 尽管目前的结果令人鼓舞，但仍存在若干局限性。 While the current results are promising, several limitations remain.
首先，我们当前的实现采用中等规模的语言模型构建共享推理中心，且仅研究了有限的智能体骨干模型和配置。 First, our current implementation instantiates the shared reasoning hub with a moderate-sized language model and studies a limited set of agent backbones and configurations. 我们尚未全面评估更强的基础模型、其他模型系列或更大的推理中心容量，因此，观察到的增益能够在多大程度上迁移至不同规模，仍是一个悬而未决的问题。 We do not yet evaluate the full space of stronger base models, alternative model families, or larger hub capacities, so the degree to which the observed gains transfer across scales remains an open question.
其次，实证研究聚焦于具有挑战性的长程推理基准，但尚未覆盖更广泛的场景，例如开放式报告撰写、持续的软件工程工作，或具有更丰富工具生态的现实交互式工作流。 Second, the empirical study focuses on challenging long-horizon reasoning benchmarks, but does not yet cover broader settings such as open-ended report writing, sustained software engineering, or real-world interactive workflows with richer tool ecosystems. 我们认为 AgentFugue 框架可以扩展至这些场景，但其在这些场景中的表现仍有待验证。 We believe the AgentFugue framework is extensible to such settings, but its behavior there remains to be validated.
第三，集体推理也引入了其自身的失效模式。 Third, collective reasoning introduces its own failure modes. 如果过程笔记质量低下、不完整或过于自信，共享中心就可能在整个团队中传播具有误导性的中间结论。 If episode notes are low quality, incomplete, or overconfident, the shared hub may propagate misleading intermediate conclusions across the team. 同样，如果许多智能体反复阅读相似且显著性较高的笔记，通信就可能降低轨迹的多样性，并导致过早收敛。 Likewise, if many agents repeatedly read similar high-salience notes, communication can reduce trajectory diversity and lead to premature convergence. 更好的置信度校准、考虑多样性的阅读策略，以及更具适应性的笔记选择，仍是未来工作的重要方向。 Better confidence calibration, diversity-aware reading policies, and more adaptive note selection remain important directions for future work.
更广泛的影响。Broader Impact.
本文的核心贡献在于提出这样一种理念：智能体能力的扩展不仅可以通过增强单个智能体来实现，还可以通过使对等智能体团队在搜索过程中共享中间推理来实现。 The central contribution of this paper is the idea that agent capability can scale not only by making a single agent stronger, but also by enabling teams of peer agents to share intermediate reasoning during search. 这一视角可能有助于为知识密集型任务构建更有效的系统，例如科学辅助、开放域研究、调查分析，以及其他不同探索路径能够相互提供有益信息的场景。 This perspective may be useful for building more effective systems for knowledge-intensive tasks such as scientific assistance, open-domain research, investigative analysis, and other settings where different exploratory paths can productively inform one another.
与此同时，更强的多智能体推理系统也可能加剧滥用。 At the same time, stronger multi-agent reasoning systems may also amplify misuse. 能够协调证据收集、综合局部发现并通过增加智能体数量进行横向扩展的系统，可能被用于大规模监视、策略性操纵，或更高效地生成欺骗性或误导性内容。 Systems that can coordinate evidence gathering, synthesize partial discoveries, and scale out across many agents could be applied to high-volume surveillance, strategic manipulation, or more efficient generation of deceptive or misleading content. 此外，如果共享推理中心传播了错误的中间结论，这些错误就可能扩散至整个团队，而非局限于单条轨迹。 In addition, if a shared reasoning hub propagates erroneous intermediate conclusions, those errors may spread across the whole team rather than remaining isolated to a single trajectory. 这些风险表明，未来部署时应考虑采取访问控制、使用监测、考虑置信度的中心输出，以及保留多样性、避免智能体行为过度同步的机制等保障措施。 These risks suggest that future deployments should consider safeguards such as access control, usage monitoring, confidence-aware hub outputs, and mechanisms that preserve diversity rather than over-synchronizing agent behavior.

论文应指出所有较强的假设，并说明当这些假设被违反时，结果具有何种程度的稳健性（例如，独立性假设、无噪声设定、模型设定正确，以及仅在局部成立的渐近近似）。 The paper should point out any strong assumptions and how robust the results are to violations of these assumptions (e.g., independence assumptions, noiseless settings, model well-specification, asymptotic approximations only holding locally). 作者应思考这些假设在实践中可能如何被违反，以及这会产生什么影响。 The authors should reflect on how these assumptions might be violated in practice and what the implications would be.

作者应讨论所提算法的计算效率，以及其随数据集规模增长时的扩展性。 The authors should discuss the computational efficiency of the proposed algorithms and how they scale with dataset size.
如适用，作者应讨论其方法在解决隐私和公平性问题方面可能存在的局限性。 If applicable, the authors should discuss possible limitations of their approach to address problems of privacy and fairness.
尽管作者可能担心，如实披露所有局限性会被审稿人用作拒稿的理由，但更糟糕的结果可能是，审稿人发现了论文中未予承认的局限性。 While the authors might fear that complete honesty about limitations might be used by reviewers as grounds for rejection, a worse outcome might be that reviewers discover limitations that aren’t acknowledged in the paper. 作者应尽其所能作出判断，并认识到，个人为提高透明度而采取的行动，对于形成维护学术共同体诚信的规范具有重要作用。 The authors should use their best judgment and recognize that individual actions in favor of transparency play an important role in developing norms that preserve the integrity of the community. 审稿人将被明确告知，不得因作者如实披露局限性而给予负面评价。 Reviewers will be specifically instructed to not penalize honesty concerning limitations.
