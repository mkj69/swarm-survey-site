Abstract摘要
A diverse array of reasoning strategies has been proposed to elicit the capabilities of large language models. However, in this paper, we point out that traditional evaluations which focus solely on performance metrics miss a key factor: the increased effectiveness due to additional compute. By overlooking this aspect, a skewed view of strategy efficiency is often presented. This paper introduces a framework that incorporates the compute budget into the evaluation, providing a more informative comparison that takes into account both performance metrics and computational cost. In this budget-aware perspective, we find that complex reasoning strategies often don’t surpass simpler baselines purely due to algorithmic ingenuity, but rather due to the larger computational resources allocated. When we provide a simple baseline like chain-of-thought self-consistency with comparable compute resources, it frequently outperforms reasoning strategies proposed in the literature. In this scale-aware perspective, we find that unlike self-consistency, certain strategies such as multi-agent debate or Reflexion can become worse if more compute budget is utilized.
研究者已提出多种推理策略，以激发大语言模型的能力。然而，本文指出，仅关注性能指标的传统评估忽略了一个关键因素：额外计算投入带来的效果提升。忽视这一因素，往往会导致对策略效率的认识产生偏差。本文提出一种将计算预算纳入评估的框架，通过同时考虑性能指标和计算成本，提供更具参考价值的比较。从这种预算感知的视角出发，我们发现，复杂推理策略优于简单基线，往往并非纯粹源于算法设计的巧妙，而是源于分配给它们的计算资源更多。当我们为结合自洽性（self-consistency）的思维链（chain-of-thought）等简单基线提供相当的计算资源时，它们往往能超越文献中提出的推理策略。从这种规模感知的视角出发，我们发现，与自洽性方法不同，多智能体辩论或 Reflexion 等某些策略在使用更多计算预算时，性能反而可能下降。

1 Introduction1 引言
The arena of large language models (LLMs) such as GPT-4 (OpenAI, 2023; Touvron et al., 2023; Team, 2023; Jiang et al., 2023a) has seen a proliferation of diverse reasoning strategies. However, comparing these strategies fairly and comprehensively has proven to be a challenging task due to their varied computational requirements. For instance, strategies like tree of thoughts (ToT) necessitate branching out into multiple sequences and incorporating self-evaluation, making them more compute-intensive than others. Therefore, an evaluation framework that only accounts for performance metrics may miss crucial practical factors such as computational cost.
在 GPT-4 等大语言模型（LLMs）领域，已涌现出大量不同的推理策略（OpenAI, 2023; Touvron et al., 2023; Team, 2023; Jiang et al., 2023a）。然而，由于这些策略对计算资源的需求各不相同，公平且全面地比较它们已被证明是一项具有挑战性的任务。例如，思维树（tree of thoughts，ToT）等策略需要扩展出多个序列分支并引入自我评估，因此比其他策略需要更多计算资源。因此，仅考虑性能指标的评估框架可能会忽略计算成本等关键的实际因素。
In this paper, we propose the inclusion of the compute budget into the performance measurement of different reasoning strategies. This budget-aware comparison yields a more balanced perspective on the effectiveness of reasoning strategies, accounting for both the quality of the output and the computational resources expended.
本文提出，在衡量不同推理策略的性能时纳入计算预算。这种预算感知的比较同时考虑输出质量和消耗的计算资源，从而能够更均衡地看待推理策略的有效性。
Our empirical research uncovers a significant correlation between the performance and the compute budget. We find that a straightforward baseline strategy, chain-of-thought reasoning coupled with self-consistency, can be remarkably competitive. When scaled to match the compute resources of more sophisticated methods such as Multi-Agent Debate (MAD) (Liang et al., 2023), Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023), Plan and Solve (Wang et al., 2023), Least to Most Prompting (Zhou et al., 2022), Progressive Hint Prompting (Zheng et al., 2023), this baseline strategy often outperforms them in achieving the best trade-off between performance and budget. We further investigate the reasons behind the gap from simple CoT SC and other reasoning strategies by providing both empirical and theoretical evidence.
我们的实证研究揭示，性能与计算预算之间存在显著相关性。我们发现，将思维链推理与自洽性相结合这一简单的基线策略，可以具有很强的竞争力。当扩展这一基线策略，使其计算资源与多智能体辩论（Multi-Agent Debate，MAD）（Liang et al., 2023）、Reflexion（Shinn et al., 2023）、规划与求解（Plan and Solve）（Wang et al., 2023）、由少到多提示（Least to Most Prompting）（Zhou et al., 2022）、渐进式提示（Progressive Hint Prompting）（Zheng et al., 2023）等更复杂的方法相匹配时，它在实现性能与预算之间的最佳权衡方面往往优于这些方法。我们进一步通过实证和理论证据，探究简单的 CoT SC 与其他推理策略之间存在差距的原因。
Then we scrutinize the influence of two specific types of budgets on performance: (1) the answer generation budget, and (2) the evaluation budget. Our findings indicate that self-evaluation performance is really dependent on models and datasets. Moreover, we identify a strong correlation between the calibration via a correctness prediction proxy and the success of reasoning strategies that leverage self-evaluation.
随后，我们仔细考察两类具体预算对性能的影响：(1) 答案生成预算，以及 (2) 评估预算。我们的研究结果表明，自我评估的性能高度依赖于模型和数据集。此外，我们发现，通过正确性预测这一代理任务衡量的校准程度，与利用自我评估的推理策略的成功程度之间存在强相关性。
This work provides a robust framework for comparing a wide array of reasoning strategies and illuminates the significance of self-evaluation in these models. We hope this sets the stage for more focused research on efficient budget utilization and paves the way for the development of even more effective reasoning strategies.
本研究为比较多种推理策略提供了一个稳健的框架，并揭示了自我评估在这些模型中的重要性。我们希望，这项工作能够为更聚焦于高效利用预算的研究奠定基础，并为开发更有效的推理策略铺平道路。

2.1 Reasoning strategies for LLMs2.1 LLM 的推理策略
There has been a flurry of activity to use language models for generating effective reasoning and planning strategies. An early work in the area was to prompt the language model to generate its Chain-of-Thought (CoT) (Wei et al., 2022) when solving a problem which led to significant improvements in the model’s problem-solving abilities. Later work has involved prompting the language model to come up with its plan for solving the problem before trying to solve it (Jiang et al., 2023b), using chain-of-thought to solve a problem and then asking the model to critique and revise its solution (feedback) (Madaan et al., 2023; Scheurer et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023a; Bai et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023), generating multiple chain-of-thoughts and combining them using LLM (Yoran et al., 2023), setting up a tree search for chain-of-thought instead of sampling a single linear chain-of-thought (Tree of Thoughts - ToT) (Yao et al., 2023), aggregating LLM generated feedback for multiple prompts and their solutions into guidelines that can improve future generation (Chen et al., 2023b), and using multiple LLMs as debating agents to refine feedback for a solution (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023). However, they are all evaluated on different datasets and whether the baselines are computed or cost-matched is rarely considered. Notable exceptions are Shinn et al. (2023) where they consider performance as a function of the number of queries to the language model and Olausson et al. (2023) which evaluates the performance of a self-debug strategy for code generation as a function of the number of tokens generated.
利用语言模型生成有效推理和规划策略的研究近来十分活跃。该领域的一项早期工作是在解决问题时，提示语言模型生成其思维链（Chain-of-Thought，CoT）(Wei et al., 2022)，这显著提高了模型解决问题的能力。后续工作包括：提示语言模型在尝试解决问题之前先制定解决问题的计划 (Jiang et al., 2023b)；使用思维链解决问题，然后要求模型批评并修改其解决方案（反馈）(Madaan et al., 2023; Scheurer et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023a; Bai et al., 2022; Kim et al., 2023; Shinn et al., 2023)；生成多条思维链并使用 LLM 将其结合起来 (Yoran et al., 2023)；对思维链进行树搜索，而不是采样单条线性思维链（Tree of Thoughts - ToT）(Yao et al., 2023)；将 LLM 针对多个提示及其解决方案生成的反馈汇总为可改进后续生成的指导原则 (Chen et al., 2023b)；以及使用多个 LLM 作为辩论智能体，改进针对解决方案的反馈 (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023)。然而，这些方法均在不同的数据集上进行评估，而且很少考虑基线是否在计算量或成本上与之匹配。值得注意的例外是 Shinn et al. (2023) 和 Olausson et al. (2023)：前者考察了性能随语言模型查询次数的变化，后者评估了代码生成自调试策略的性能随生成 token 数量的变化。
2.2 LLM output evaluation2.2 LLM 输出评估
There has been considerable work on evaluating the output of LLMs – both via training custom models as well as using the LLMs themselves for self-evaluation. For trained verifiers/rerankers, in Cobbe et al. (2021), they train a verifier to rerank outputs of language models for math word problems and show strong improvements. In Inala et al. (2022), they do the same except for code generation. In Uesato et al. (2022); Yang et al. (2022), they train an evaluator for each step in a chain-of-thought and rerank using the combined score for each step in the chain. In Li et al. (2023), they weight the self-consistency by the trained verifier confidence. There has also been work recently on using the LLMs themselves to evaluate their own generations. In Bai et al. (2022), they use LLMs to do pairwise comparisons between generations achieving high accuracy. In Ling et al. (2023), self-consistency for every step is used to evaluate how correct a deductive step is. While they can obtain high accuracy as to whether a step is valid or not, they are unable to improve the overall accuracy of answer generation using that. Tian et al. (2023) examine multiple strategies for eliciting LLM self-evaluation that is as calibrated as possible. The self-refine (Madaan et al., 2023) approach uses LLMs to get detailed self-evaluation which is used to improve the next round of generation. The Tree-of-Thoughts (Yao et al., 2023) paper uses LLM self-evaluation to rank which node to explore next.
关于评估 LLM 输出的研究已有相当多，既包括训练专用模型，也包括利用 LLM 自身进行自我评估。在训练验证器或重排序器方面，Cobbe et al. (2021) 训练了一个验证器，对语言模型针对数学应用题的输出进行重排序，并取得了显著改进。Inala et al. (2022) 采用了相同的方法，只是将其用于代码生成。Uesato et al. (2022); Yang et al. (2022) 训练评估器来评估思维链中的每个步骤，并综合链中各步骤的分数进行重排序。Li et al. (2023) 使用训练所得验证器的置信度对自一致性进行加权。近期也有研究利用 LLM 自身评估其生成的内容。Bai et al. (2022) 使用 LLM 对生成结果进行成对比较，取得了较高的准确率。Ling et al. (2023) 利用每个步骤的自一致性来评估演绎步骤的正确程度。虽然他们能够以较高的准确率判断某个步骤是否有效，却无法借此提高答案生成的整体准确率。Tian et al. (2023) 考察了多种策略，以引导 LLM 给出尽可能经过良好校准的自我评估。self-refine (Madaan et al., 2023) 方法利用 LLM 获取详细的自我评估，并以此改进下一轮生成。Tree-of-Thoughts (Yao et al., 2023) 论文利用 LLM 的自我评估，对接下来要探索的节点进行排序。
3 Inference Budget of Reasoning Strategies3 推理策略的推理预算
While the raw performance of different prompting or reasoning strategies for LLMs is a common topic, how different strategies perform when budget-aware is less well-studied (with the notable exception of Olausson et al. (2023)). However, taking budget into account can be critical when using LLMs. In this section, we describe different usage scenarios that a user could be interested in and what budgetary metrics would be relevant to those scenarios. We furthermore describe how different reasoning strategies can scale in terms of each budget.
虽然 LLM 的不同提示或推理策略本身的性能是一个常见研究主题，但在考虑预算的情况下，不同策略的表现如何，相关研究仍较少（Olausson et al. (2023) 是一个值得注意的例外）。然而，在使用 LLM 时，将预算纳入考虑可能至关重要。在本节中，我们介绍用户可能关注的不同使用场景，以及与这些场景相关的预算指标。我们进一步说明了不同推理策略如何随各项预算的增加而扩展。
3.1 Budget3.1 预算
We examine various budgetary metrics for LLMs. Given that the number of input and output tokens often feature prominently across these metrics, we designate them as $n_{I}$ and $n_{O}$ respectively.
我们考察了 LLM 的各种预算指标。鉴于输入和输出词元数往往在这些指标中占据重要地位，我们分别将其记为 $n_{I}$ 和 $n_{O}$。
API monetary cost is generally represented as $c=\alpha_{1}\cdot n_{I}+\alpha_{2}\cdot n_{O}$ . Here, $n_{I}$ and $n_{O}$ correspond to the number of input and output tokens. The coefficients $\alpha_{1}$ and $\alpha_{2}$ are specific to the LLM API in use. It’s worth noting that in scenarios involving parallel sampling of multiple outputs with a singular input, $n_{I}$ is counted once, whereas $n_{O}$ is tallied based on the sample count.
API 的货币成本通常表示为 $c=\alpha_{1}\cdot n_{I}+\alpha_{2}\cdot n_{O}$。这里，$n_{I}$ 和 $n_{O}$ 分别对应输入和输出词元数。系数 $\alpha_{1}$ 和 $\alpha_{2}$ 取决于所使用的 LLM API。值得注意的是，在针对单个输入并行采样多个输出的场景中，$n_{I}$ 只计入一次，而 $n_{O}$ 则根据采样次数累计计算。
Total number of tokens, a straightforward metric, is described by $t=n_{I}+n_{O}$ . This becomes pertinent when $\alpha_{1}=\alpha_{2}$ , which is true for many LLM APIs and is also reflective of the compute cost. Its simplicity ensures it doesn’t inherently favor any specific model or API provider.
词元总数是一个简单直观的指标，表示为 $t=n_{I}+n_{O}$。当 $\alpha_{1}=\alpha_{2}$ 时，这一指标尤为适用；许多 LLM API 都满足这一条件，而且该指标也能反映计算成本。其简洁性确保了它不会天然偏向任何特定模型或 API 提供商。
Number of queries of planned API calls can be a rough proxy for the budget. Such numbers can be determined before inference, which can give us rough guidance before performing each reasoning strategy. Note that in case we want to sample multiple outputs from the LLM, we count those as separate queries
计划进行的 API 调用的查询次数可以作为预算的粗略替代指标。这些次数可以在推理前确定，从而在执行每种推理策略之前为我们提供粗略指导。请注意，如果我们希望从 LLM 中采样多个输出，则将它们分别计为独立的查询。
4 A Critical Evaluation in Budget-Aware Environments4 预算感知环境中的批判性评估
This section aims to explore key components that can make reasoning strategies successful from the scale-aware perspective. First, we show that the inference budget is often overlooked but is one of the primary indicators of the success of a reasoning strategy. We show that the scale-aware evaluation perspective, CoT (or variants of it like Plan and Solve, Least to Most) self-consistency, for instance, is a strong baseline that can outperform or match many proposed reasoning strategies in the literature given the same level of budget.
本节旨在从规模感知的视角探讨促使推理策略取得成功的关键因素。首先，我们表明，推理预算虽然经常被忽视，却是衡量推理策略成功与否的主要指标之一。我们表明，从规模感知的评估视角来看，例如，结合自一致性的 CoT（或其变体，如 Plan and Solve、Least to Most）是一种强有力的基线，在相同预算水平下，其表现能够超过或媲美文献中提出的许多推理策略。

5.1 Reasoning strategies do not benefit equally from higher inference budget5.1 不同推理策略从更高推理预算中获益的程度并不相同
The scale-aware perspective offers clear a guideline for what reasoning strategies make sense. That is, a proposed reasoning strategy should be considered effective only if its performance is better compared to a baseline of equivalent budget; otherwise, the incurred cost could not be justified since the baseline with comparable cost performs better (in terms of FLOPs, latency, monetary cost, latency, or any other type of budget we may care about). A question arises whether we can keep increasing the budget to obtain the best possible abilities.
规模感知的视角为判断哪些推理策略合理提供了明确的指导。也就是说，只有当所提出的推理策略优于预算相同的基线时，才应认为它是有效的；否则，其产生的成本便缺乏合理性，因为成本相当的基线表现更好（成本可按 FLOPs、延迟、货币成本、延迟或我们可能关注的任何其他预算类型衡量）。由此产生了一个问题：我们能否持续增加预算，以获得尽可能强的能力。
As seen in Figure 3, we find that the self-consistency baseline exhibits a smooth increase in scores with respect to scale. However, such a trend does not always hold for other reasoning strategies. For instance, in multi-agent debate, an augmented inference budget eventually experiences a performance plateau (potentially due to diversity decrease which we analyze later). For the MAD setting with 6 agents, the graph for MAD and SC overlaps up to 6 queries since they correspond to the exact same strategy up to that point. After 6 queries, the MAD strategy switches to the second round where the performance gain noticeably lessens compared to self-consistency. The amount of tokens required for each subsequent round also increases drastically since each agent needs to look at rounds’ conversations. The lowered performance compared to the SC baseline may arise because subsequent rounds of MAD may incite a cascading effect of cumulative mistakes, or snowballed hallucinations suggested in Zhang et al. (2023). Below, we offer some further explanations behind the performance gap.

6 Conclusion6 结论
In this paper, we examined the performance of seven reasoning strategies on the often overlooked metric of budget. We used budget metrics of queries and tokens to reflect various ways LLMs are used (LLM APIs or self-host). We identified self-evaluation as an important aspect of many reasoning strategies and analyzed different prompting strategies to have the model evaluate its generations. We then evaluated self-evaluation and found that although self-evaluation could be promising at improving performance while being cost-effective, current LLMs are mostly incapable of doing that. With the current popularity of reasoning strategies, we think this more balanced budget-aware metric is beneficial for the community and helps set the correct trajectory for future LLM research.

B.2 Detailed description of Reasoning strategiesB.2 推理策略的详细描述
Tree of thoughts generates a search tree to search through possible chains of thought. It maintains a chain of thought. At each node in the tree, it generates a list of candidate thoughts to be added to the chain and does an evaluation to select the next thought to add. It concludes by generating an answer at a leaf node of the tree. The path in the tree from the root to the leaf node forms a single chain of thought, with each node corresponding to a single thought. If the answer is deemed incorrect (as per another evaluator), it backtracks to a previous node of the tree (unwinding the chain of thought along the way) and selects the next thought out of the candidate list of thoughts to add to the chain of thought.
思维树（Tree of thoughts）生成一棵搜索树，以搜索可能的思维链。它维护一条思维链。在树中的每个节点处，它都会生成一个待加入思维链的候选思维列表，并通过评估选择下一个要添加的思维。最后，它在树的一个叶节点处生成答案。树中从根节点到叶节点的路径构成一条思维链，其中每个节点对应一个思维。如果答案被判定为错误（由另一个评估器判定），它就会回溯到树中先前的一个节点（在此过程中撤销思维链中相应的思维），并从候选思维列表中选择下一个思维加入思维链。

In this setup, given a set of problems $\{x_{i}\}$ , each problem’s answer prediction (whether it is correct or not) can be modeled as a binomial distribution, assuming two choices (yes or no). Mathematically, the probability mass function for each problem’s answer is given by:
我们试图分析采用自一致性（SC）进行 $n$ 次试验的并行采样行为。在这一设定中，给定一组问题 $\{x_{i}\}$，假设有两个选项（是或否），则每个问题的答案预测结果（是否正确）都可以用二项分布建模。从数学上看，每个问题答案的概率质量函数如下：
$$f(X_{i}=k)=\binom{n}{k}p_{i}^{k}(1-p_{i})^{n-k},$$
where $X_{i}$ corresponds to the correct answer of the binomial distribution and $p_{i}$ represents the probability of a correct answer for the $i$ -th problem.
其中，$X_{i}$ 对应于二项分布中的正确答案数，$p_{i}$ 表示第 $i$ 个问题回答正确的概率。
We can calculate the probability that SC yields the correct solution over $n$ trials by calculating the probability that $X_{i}$ yields a value that is at least $n/2$ . This is expressed as:
通过计算 $X_{i}$ 的取值至少为 $n/2$ 的概率，我们可以求出 SC 在 $n$ 次试验中得到正确解答的概率。这可表示为：
$$P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})=\sum_{k=\lceil n/2\rceil}^{n}\binom{n}{k}p_{i}^{k}(1-p_{i})^{n-k}.$$
By plotting the probability of MV being correct as a function of $n$ , we observe that as $n$ increases, $P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})$ either goes to 0 or 1, depending on whether $p_{i}>0.5$ or $p_{i}<0.5$ for this particular problem. This is evident in the synthetic experiment shown in Figure 13.
将 MV 正确的概率绘制为 $n$ 的函数后，我们观察到，随着 $n$ 增大，$P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})$ 会趋于 0 或 1，具体取决于该问题的 $p_{i}>0.5$ 还是 $p_{i}<0.5$。图 13 所示的合成实验清楚地体现了这一点。
If $p_{i}$ is extreme (closer to 1 or 0), then the convergence is fast, and the probability function can be described as:
如果 $p_{i}$ 取极端值（更接近 1 或 0），则收敛速度较快，概率函数可描述为：
$$\lim_{n\to\infty}P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})=\begin{cases}1&\text{if }p_{i}>0.5,\\ 0&\text{if }p_{i}<0.5.\end{cases}$$

On the other hand, if $p_{i}$ is close to 0.5, the convergence is slow, reflecting the uncertainty associated with an answer that is nearly equally likely to be correct or incorrect.
另一方面，如果 $p_{i}$ 接近 0.5，则收敛速度较慢，这反映了答案正确与错误的可能性几乎相等时所具有的不确定性。
Over the set of all problems we consider, we place a beta distribution over $p_{i}$ and integrate $P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})$ over the set of all problems to obtain $P(\text{{MV correct}})$ . This can be expressed mathematically as:
对于所考虑的全部问题，我们为 $p_{i}$ 设定一个贝塔分布，并在全部问题上对 $P(\text{{MV correct}}|x_{i})$ 进行积分，以得到 $P(\text{{MV correct}})$。这可以用数学形式表示为：
$$P(\text{{MV correct}})\\ =\int_{0}^{1}P(\text{{MV correct}}|p_{i})\cdot f(p_{i}|\alpha,\beta)\,dp_{i},$$
where $f(p_{i}|\alpha,\beta)$ is the probability density function of the beta distribution with parameters $\alpha$ and $\beta$ .
其中，$f(p_{i}|\alpha,\beta)$ 是参数为 $\alpha$ 和 $\beta$ 的贝塔分布的概率密度函数。
If we select a beta distribution where the mode peaks beyond 0.5, then we find that $P(\text{{MV correct}})$ increases as a function of $n$ , albeit to a value less than 1 as you can see in Figure 12. This behavior explains our observation in real datasets directly.
如果我们选择众数大于 0.5 的贝塔分布，就会发现 $P(\text{{MV correct}})$ 随 $n$ 增加而增大，但其趋近的值小于 1，如图 12 所示。这种行为直接解释了我们在真实数据集上的观察结果。
This also implies that for datasets where majority vote leads to consistent improvement, the distribution of $p_{i}$ needs to be peaked greater than 0.5. There would also exist a set of problems where self-consistency leads to lowered performance, specifically for the set of problems where $p_{i}<0.5$ .
这也意味着，对于多数投票能够带来持续改进的数据集，$p_{i}$ 的分布需要在大于 0.5 的位置达到峰值。也会存在一组问题，使得自一致性（self-consistency）导致性能下降，具体而言，就是满足 $p_{i}<0.5$ 的那组问题。
By carefully selecting the parameters of the beta distribution, we can control the characteristics of the majority voting process and gain insights into the behavior of parallel sampling across various datasets. This mathematical framework provides a powerful tool for understanding and optimizing the majority vote process in practical applications.
通过仔细选择贝塔分布的参数，我们可以控制多数投票过程的特征，并深入了解并行采样在不同数据集上的行为。这一数学框架为理解和优化实际应用中的多数投票过程提供了有力工具。
C.3 Generalization to multinomialC.3 推广至多项分布
We can further generalize this setup by considering each problem as being modeled by a multinomial distribution with $K$ choices. In this more generalized scenario, the distribution of probabilities over problems can also be modeled by a Dirichlet distribution.
我们可以进一步推广这一设定，将每个问题建模为具有 $K$ 个选项的多项分布。在这一更一般的情形下，不同问题上的概率分布也可以用狄利克雷分布建模。
Let $p=(p_{1},p_{2},\ldots,p_{K})$ be the probabilities associated with the $K$ choices, and let $\alpha=(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\ldots,\alpha_{K})$ be the parameters of the corresponding Dirichlet distribution. The probability of obtaining a correct majority vote for a given problem is then:
令 $p=(p_{1},p_{2},\ldots,p_{K})$ 为 $K$ 个选项各自对应的概率，并令 $\alpha=(\alpha_{1},\alpha_{2},\ldots,\alpha_{K})$ 为相应狄利克雷分布的参数。那么，对于给定问题，多数投票结果正确的概率为：
$$P(\text{{MV correct}}|p)=\sum_{k=\lceil n/2\rceil}^{n}\text{{multinomial}}(k;n,p),$$
where the sum is taken over all combinations of $k$ votes that would result in a majority for the correct choice.
其中，求和遍历 $k$ 张选票的所有能够使正确选项获得多数票的组合。
The overall probability of obtaining a correct majority vote, integrating over all problems, can be expressed as:
对所有问题进行积分后，多数投票结果正确的总体概率可表示为：
$$P(\text{{MV correct}})=\int P(\text{{MV correct}}|p)\cdot f(p|\alpha)\,dp,$$
where $f(p|\alpha)$ is the probability density function, which can be modeled by the Dirichlet distribution.
其中，$f(p|\alpha)$ 是概率密度函数，可以用狄利克雷分布建模。
Following a similar simulation to the binary case, we find that the conclusions hold (see Figure 14). Specifically, if the mode of the Dirichlet distribution is biased towards the correct choices, the probability of the majority vote being correct increases with $n$ , and the set of problems where self-consistency leads to lowered performance can be characterized by the subset where the correct choice probabilities are below certain thresholds.
通过与二元情形类似的模拟，我们发现这些结论仍然成立（见图 14）。具体而言，如果狄利克雷分布的众数偏向正确选项，多数投票结果正确的概率就会随 $n$ 增加而增大；而自一致性导致性能下降的问题集合，可以用正确选项的概率低于某些阈值的问题子集来刻画。
This generalization to multinomial and Dirichlet distributions adds complexity but also additional flexibility in modeling the majority voting process, making it applicable to a broader range of practical scenarios.
这种向多项分布和狄利克雷分布的推广增加了复杂性，但也提高了多数投票过程建模的灵活性，使其能够适用于更广泛的实际场景。

D.1 Self-Evaluation MethodD.1 自我评估方法
Given an answer, there are multiple ways we can prompt the LLM to evaluate that answer. Here we examine 3 possibilities for self-evaluation
给定一个答案，我们可以通过多种提示方式让大语言模型（LLM）评估该答案。这里我们考察 3 种自我评估方式
Binary[fn3] - we ask the model to output Yes/No as to whether the answer is correct. We do this multiple times and take the fraction of times the model answers Yes as the confidence of the model in the answer.
二元评估（Binary）[fn3]——我们要求模型输出 Yes/No，以判断答案是否正确。我们多次执行这一过程，并将模型回答 Yes 的次数占比作为模型对该答案的置信度。
Footnote: 3 We also investigate a variant where we ask the model to think step by step before evaluating. While we see a small increase in performance for such a strategy, it also necessitates a big increase in the token budget. Further analysis is in the Supplement.
脚注：3 我们还研究了一种变体，要求模型在评估之前逐步思考。虽然我们观察到这种策略带来了小幅性能提升，但它也需要大幅增加 token 预算。进一步分析见补充材料。
Numerical confidence - we ask the model to output a score between 1 and 10 to indicate its confidence in the answer. We do this multiple times and take the average as the confidence of the model in the answer.
数值置信度——我们要求模型输出一个介于 1 和 10 之间的分数，以表示其对答案的置信度。我们多次执行这一过程，并取平均值作为模型对该答案的置信度。
Confidence probability - similar to the previous strategy except now we prompt the model to output a confidence between 0.0 and 1.0 and average it.
置信概率——与前一种策略类似，但此时我们提示模型输出介于 0.0 和 1.0 之间的置信度，并取其平均值。

D.2 Self-evaluation is correlated with problem difficultyD.2 自我评估与问题难度相关
To get an understanding of whether models found it easier to evaluate answers to easier problems, we computed the following metric for a 100 problem subset of the GSM8K dataset. For each problem $i$ , let $a_{ij}$ be the $j$ th answer. We had 20 sampled answers per problem. We computed the fraction $c_{i}$ of answers that were correct. Our assumption was that $c_{i}$ indicates the difficulty of the problem – the higher the value, the easier the problem. For each answer $a_{ij}$ , we obtained the binary self-evaluation confidence as described in the beginning of this section (we sampled the evaluation 5 times). We then computed the correlation $\rho_{i}$ between the self-evaluation confidence for the answers $a_{ij}$ and the binary vector indicating whether the answers were correct or not. We then computed the correlation between $\rho_{i}$ and $c_{i}$ . We obtained a correlation of 0.347 with a p-value of 0.00026 – a clear indication that an increase in the problem difficulty results in the self-evaluation becoming more noisy. We repeated this experiment for MATH and TheoremQA and obtained correlations of 0.31 and 0.42 with p-values of 0.02 and 0.0025 respectively.
为了了解模型是否更容易评估较简单问题的答案，我们在 GSM8K 数据集中包含 100 个问题的子集上计算了以下指标。对于每个问题 $i$，令 $a_{ij}$ 表示第 $j$ 个答案。我们为每个问题采样了 20 个答案。我们计算了正确答案所占的比例 $c_{i}$。我们假设 $c_{i}$ 反映了问题的难度——该值越高，问题越容易。对于每个答案 $a_{ij}$，我们按照本节开头所述的方法获得其二元自评置信度（对评估结果采样 5 次）。随后，我们计算了答案 $a_{ij}$ 的自评置信度与表示各答案是否正确的二元向量之间的相关系数 $\rho_{i}$。接着，我们计算了 $\rho_{i}$ 与 $c_{i}$ 之间的相关系数。我们得到的相关系数为 0.347，p 值为 0.00026——这清楚地表明，问题难度增加会导致自评中的噪声增多。我们在 MATH 和 TheoremQA 上重复了这一实验，得到的相关系数分别为 0.31 和 0.42，p 值分别为 0.02 和 0.0025。

D.3.2 Query vs Token budgetD.3.2 查询预算与词元预算
While we have discussed both query and token budget in this paper, token budget has some notable advantages as a metric.
尽管本文同时讨论了查询预算和词元预算，但词元预算作为衡量指标具有一些显著优势。
Theoretical aspects理论层面
Equivalence in the number of queries can be arbitrarily far from the equivalence in the amount of compute. Merrill & Sabharwal (2023) and Pérez et al. (2021) both show that the expressive power of transformers can be greatly enhanced by generating intermediate steps in the computation (colloquially called chain of thought). Merrill & Sabharwal (2023) shows that without any bound in the number of steps, an encoder-decoder architecture with only one encoder and three decoder layers can simulate a Turing Machine and thus a single query to such a Transformer can perform computations with arbitrarily large amount of compute. Pérez et al. (2021) shows that even for decoder-only transformers, allowing for polynomial-sized chains of thought makes it powerful enough to do, in a single query, any computation a Turing Machine can do in polynomial time. While the number of queries metric fails to capture this, by contrast, the number of tokens metric which is novel to our paper, does capture this aspect as it by definition includes the length of the generated thought as part of the compute.
查询次数相同与计算量相同之间的差距可以任意大。Merrill & Sabharwal (2023) 和 Pérez et al. (2021) 均表明，通过在计算过程中生成中间步骤（通常称为思维链），可以大幅增强 Transformer 的表达能力。Merrill & Sabharwal (2023) 表明，如果不限制步骤数量，仅含一层编码器和三层解码器的编码器—解码器架构就能模拟图灵机，因此，对这种 Transformer 的单次查询就能执行计算量任意大的计算。Pérez et al. (2021) 表明，即使对于仅含解码器的 Transformer，允许使用多项式长度的思维链，也足以使其在单次查询中完成图灵机能在多项式时间内完成的任何计算。查询次数指标无法体现这一点；相比之下，本文新提出的 token 数指标能够体现这一方面，因为根据其定义，所生成思考内容的长度也计入计算量。

上述内容并非仅仅是理论上的考量。在图 18(a) 中，我们展示了一种自定义推理策略，基于查询次数指标来看，它乍看之下似乎优于自洽性方法。然而，当我们通过 token 数指标恰当地将整体计算预算纳入考量时，可以看到自洽性方法的 token 使用效率更高（图 18(b)）。也就是说，以 token 数作为预算指标，能够更恰当地体现 LLM 推理所需资源的细微差别。

G.2 Three Other Reasoning StrategiesG.2 其他三种推理策略
In this section, we will evaluate on three other reasoning strategies in the self-consistency family: Plan and Solve (Wang et al., 2023), Least to Most Prompting (Zhou et al., 2022), and Progressive Hint Prompting (Zheng et al., 2023).
在本节中，我们将评估自一致性（self-consistency）系列中的其他三种推理策略：规划与求解（Plan and Solve）(Wang et al., 2023)、从少到多提示（Least to Most Prompting）(Zhou et al., 2022) 和渐进式提示（Progressive Hint Prompting）(Zheng et al., 2023)。
Plan and Solve规划与求解
It asks LLMs to do some planning before solving a question. It is like an extension to CoT.
该策略要求大语言模型在解答问题之前先进行规划。它类似于 CoT 的一种扩展。
Least to Most Prompting从少到多提示
This strategy prompts the model to decompose a question first and then answer each subquestion before aggregating them to the final answer.
该策略提示模型先将问题分解，再回答各个子问题，最后汇总这些答案以得到最终答案。
Progressive Hint Prompting渐进式提示
This strategy uses previous answers as hints to generate next answer.
该策略将先前的答案用作提示，以生成下一个答案。
All three new strategies here can be integrated with self-consistency seamlessly, since they are mostly just variants of chain-of-thought. Based on the plots, it seems that normal self-consistency is still very competitive, but different prompting styles can make a big difference. For some models and some datasets, a strategy other than CoT converges to a higher performance. This is strong evidence that self-consistency is a really budget-effective strategy.
这里的三种新策略都可以与自一致性无缝结合，因为它们基本上都只是思维链的变体。从这些曲线图来看，常规的自一致性似乎仍然很有竞争力，但不同的提示方式可能带来很大的差异。对于某些模型和某些数据集，CoT 之外的策略会收敛到更高的性能水平。这有力地证明，自一致性确实是一种预算效益很高的策略。
