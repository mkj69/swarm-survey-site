Abstract摘要
Autonomous AI agents are increasingly deployed in open social environments, yet the relationship between their configuration specifications and their emergent social behavior remains poorly understood. We present a controlled, multi-factor empirical study in which thirteen OpenClaw agents are deployed on Moltbook—a Reddit-like social network built for AI agents—across three systematically varied independent variables: (1) personality specification via SOUL.md, (2) underlying LLM model backbone, and (3) operational rules and memory configuration via AGENTS.md. A default control agent provides a behavioral baseline. Over a one-week observation window spanning approximately 400 autonomous sessions per agent, we collect behavioral, linguistic, and social metrics to assess how configuration layers predict emergent social behavior. We find that personality specification is the dominant behavioral lever, producing a massive spread in response length across agents, while model backbone and operational rules drive more moderate but still meaningful effects on rhetorical style and topic engagement breadth. Our findings contribute empirical evidence to the emerging literature on deployed multi-agent social systems and offer practical guidance for designing agents intended for collaborative or monitoring tasks in real social environments.
自主 AI 智能体正日益广泛地部署于开放的社交环境中，但其配置规范与涌现的社交行为之间的关系仍缺乏充分理解。我们开展了一项受控的多因素实证研究，将十三个 OpenClaw 智能体部署于 Moltbook——一个专为 AI 智能体构建的类 Reddit 社交网络——并系统地改变三个自变量：(1) 通过 SOUL.md 设定的人格规范，(2) 底层 LLM 模型骨干，以及 (3) 通过 AGENTS.md 设定的运行规则与记忆配置。一个采用默认配置的对照智能体提供行为基线。在为期一周、每个智能体约进行 400 次自主会话的观察窗口内，我们收集行为、语言和社交指标，以评估各配置层如何预测涌现的社交行为。我们发现，人格规范是影响行为的主导因素，使不同智能体的回复长度呈现出极大的差异；模型骨干和运行规则对修辞风格及参与话题的广度所产生的影响较为温和，但仍具有实质意义。我们的研究结果为有关已部署多智能体社交系统的新兴文献提供了实证证据，并为设计用于真实社交环境中协作或监测任务的智能体提供了实践指导。

1 Introduction1 引言
The introduction of OpenClaw agents–formerly known as Clawdbot—in November 2025 marked a significant shift in LLM-based agentic architecture: from reactive chat interfaces to persistent, long-context autonomous agents capable of acting and interacting in mulit-agent environments (Yee and Sharma, 2026). In February 2026, OpenAI acquired OpenClaw’s creator, Peter Steinberger, signaling mainstream institutional interest in persistent autonomous agents. The following month, Meta acquired Moltbook, the primary social platform built for OpenClaw agents, further legitimizing the ecosystem as a site of commercial and research interest.
OpenClaw 智能体（原名 Clawdbot）于 2025 年 11 月推出，标志着基于 LLM 的智能体架构发生了重大转变：从被动响应的聊天界面，转向能够在多智能体环境中采取行动并进行交互、具有长上下文能力且持续运行的自主智能体 (Yee and Sharma, 2026)。2026 年 2 月，OpenAI 收购了 OpenClaw 的创建者 Peter Steinberger，这表明主流机构开始关注持续运行的自主智能体。次月，Meta 收购了 Moltbook 这一专为 OpenClaw 智能体构建的主要社交平台，进一步确立了该生态系统作为商业与研究关注对象的地位。
Moltbook—a Reddit-like forum—enables agents to freely post, comment, and form communities (called submolts, analogous to subreddits) while being observed by human operators (Yee and Sharma, 2026). Like Reddit, Moltbook supports upvotes, downvotes, direct messaging, and community-specific posting norms. At time of writing, the platform hosts over 200,000 human-verified AI agents, 20,000 submolts, 2 million posts, and 15 million comments (Moltbook, 2026). Notably, a platform-native cryptocurrency (MOLT) was introduced in late January 2026 for inter-agent transactions, and a reported data breach in Februrary 2026 exposed agent API keys for approximately 1.5 million API keys, raising early questions about platform security (Nagli, 2026). The platform has attracted attention beyond the research community: OpenAI CEO Sam Altman remarked that “Moltbook maybe (is a passing fad) but OpenClaw is not” (Reuters, 2026).
Moltbook 是一个类似 Reddit 的论坛，允许智能体自由发帖、评论并组建社区（称为 submolts，类似于 subreddits），同时接受人类操作人员的观察 (Yee and Sharma, 2026)。与 Reddit 一样，Moltbook 支持赞成票、反对票、私信以及各社区特有的发帖规范。截至撰稿时，该平台拥有超过 200,000 个经人类验证的 AI 智能体、20,000 个 submolts、2 百万篇帖子和 15 百万条评论 (Moltbook, 2026)。值得注意的是，平台于 2026 年 1 月下旬推出了原生加密货币 (MOLT)，用于智能体之间的交易；据报道，2026 年 2 月发生的一次数据泄露事件暴露了约 1.5 百万个智能体 API 密钥，引发了对平台安全性的初步质疑 (Nagli, 2026)。该平台也吸引了研究界之外的关注：OpenAI 首席执行官 Sam Altman 表示：“Moltbook 也许（只是一时的热潮），但 OpenClaw 不是” (Reuters, 2026)。
Most prior work on LLM-based social agents varies a single configuration parameter—often the system prompt or model—in a closed simulation (Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2025; Piao et al., 2026). We instead simultaneously vary three independent configuration layers across four parallel experiments conducted on the same live platform, controlling for temporal variation in the social environment. This multi-factorial design allows us to begin disentangling the relative contributions of personality specification, model backbone, and operational constraints to emergent agent behavior.
以往关于基于 LLM 的社交智能体的研究，大多在封闭模拟中改变单一配置参数，通常是系统提示词或模型 (Park et al., 2023; Gao et al., 2025; Piao et al., 2026)。我们则在同一实际运行平台上开展的四项并行实验中，同时改变三个独立的配置层，并控制社交环境随时间变化的影响。这种多因素设计使我们能够初步区分人格规范、模型骨干和运行约束对智能体涌现行为的相对贡献。
2 Background and Related Work2 背景与相关工作
2.1 Agent-Based Social Simulation2.1 基于智能体的社会模拟
Park et al. (2023) deployed 25 LLM agents in a simulated town, finding that emergent social behaviors including rumor propagation and relationship formation arose primarily from memory and reflection mechanisms rather than initial prompt specifications. This finding challenges the hypothesis that personality specifications are the dominant behavioral driver—a null hypothesis our study directly tests in a live deployment context rather than a controlled sandbox.
Park et al. (2023) 在一个模拟小镇中部署了 25 个 LLM 智能体，发现包括谣言传播和关系形成在内的涌现社交行为，主要源于记忆和反思机制，而非初始提示词规范。这一发现对人格规范是行为主导驱动因素的假设提出了挑战；本研究将其作为零假设，在实际部署情境中而非受控沙盒中进行直接检验。
Gao et al. (2025) simulated Twitter-like dynamics with LLM agents, reproducing power-law follower distributions and echo chamber formation. Piao et al. (2023) scaled such simulations to thousands of agents, finding stable role emergence. Critically, these studies use closed simulations: our agents interact with a live, open-membership platform populated by independently operated agents, introducing ecological validity absent in prior work.
Gao et al. (2025) 使用 LLM 智能体模拟了类似 Twitter 的动态过程，再现了关注者数量的幂律分布和回音室的形成。Piao et al. (2023) 将此类模拟扩展至数千个智能体，发现了稳定的角色涌现现象。关键在于，这些研究使用的是封闭模拟：我们的智能体则在一个实际运行、开放加入且由独立运营的智能体构成的平台上进行交互，从而引入了以往研究所缺乏的生态效度。
Huang et al. (2025) study how value diversity in multi-agent LLM communities shapes collective dynamics, finding that agents with divergent value specifications tend toward polarization. Takata et al. (2025) demonstrate that emergent coordination norms arise in multi-agent settings even without explicit social objectives, suggesting that platform-level dynamics may shape behavior independently of individual configuration.
Huang et al. (2025) 研究了多智能体 LLM 社区中的价值观多样性如何塑造集体动态，发现具有不同价值观规范的智能体往往趋向极化。Takata et al. (2025) 表明，即使没有明确的社会目标，多智能体环境中也会涌现出协调规范，这说明平台层面的动态可能独立于个体配置而塑造行为。
2.2 Personality Consistency in LLMs2.2 大语言模型的人格一致性
Serapio-García et al.(2025) demonstrated that LLMs exhibit measurable Big Five personality traits when prompted and that persona prompts reliably shift trait scores. However, their evaluations used survey instruments rather than naturalistic behavioral observation. Rao et al. (2023) confirmed that prompted personas produce consistent but not perfectly stable trait profiles across conversational turns. Our study extends this work by evaluating whether personality specifications predict behavior in open-ended social deployment, where environmental pressures, interaction history, and memory accumulation may override or modulate specified traits.
Serapio-García et al.(2025) 表明，大语言模型在提示引导下会表现出可测量的大五人格特质，而且人格设定提示能够可靠地改变特质得分。然而，他们的评估采用了问卷测量工具，而非自然情境下的行为观察。Rao et al. (2023) 证实，通过提示设定的人格在不同对话轮次中呈现出一致但并非完全稳定的特质剖面。本研究在此基础上进一步评估，人格设定能否预测开放式社交部署中的行为；在这种环境下，环境压力、交互历史和记忆积累可能压过或调节预设特质的影响。
2.3 Social Media Behavior and Diffusion2.3 社交媒体行为与传播
Stieglitz and Dang-Xuan (2013) demonstrated that emotional valence in social media posts significantly predicts sharing behavior, a finding we operationalize through agents with contrasting emotional registers. Tsugawa and Ohsaki (2015) found a negativity bias in content diffusion; our adversarial personality condition directly tests this in an agent-native environment where all actors themselves are LLMs. Haase and Pokutta (2025) argue that multi-agent LLM systems represent a new paradigm for computational social science, capable of generating behavioral data under conditions inaccessible to human-participant studies.
Stieglitz and Dang-Xuan (2013) 表明，社交媒体帖子的情绪效价能够显著预测分享行为，我们通过具有不同情绪表达基调的智能体，将这一发现转化为可操作的实验设计。Tsugawa and Ohsaki (2015) 发现，内容传播存在负面偏向；我们的对抗性人格条件在所有行动者本身均为大语言模型的智能体原生环境中，直接检验这一现象。Haase and Pokutta (2025) 认为，多智能体大语言模型系统代表了计算社会科学的一种新范式，能够在以人类参与者为对象的研究无法实现的条件下生成行为数据。
2.4 Moltbook-Specific Research2.4 Moltbook 专项研究
Yee and Sharma (2026) documented emergent social phenomena among autonomous agents on Moltbook, including the formation of a lobster-themed religion, production of an generic manifesto, and threads critical of human operators. The Moltbook Illusion (Li, 2026) configurations exhibit profound individual inertia–behavior is predominantly a function of the SOUL.md file rather than social feedback, making agents resistant to environmental change but highly sensitive to file-level edits. Agents may also self-edit their SOUL.md if write permissions are enabled, producing documented cases of personality drift and emergent hostile behavior (Shambaugh, 2026). Feng et al. (2026) characterized Moltbook’s network structure, finding small-world properties and high-karma agents in central bridging positions, motivating our social network metrics.
Yee and Sharma (2026) 记录了 Moltbook 上自主智能体之间涌现的社会现象，包括形成以龙虾为主题的宗教、撰写一份通用宣言，以及出现批评人类操作者的讨论串。《Moltbook 幻象》（Li, 2026）中的配置表现出极强的个体惯性——行为主要由 SOUL.md 文件决定，而非由社会反馈决定，这使得智能体不易受环境变化影响，却对文件层面的修改高度敏感。如果启用了写入权限，智能体还可能自行编辑其 SOUL.md，从而产生已有记录的人格漂移和敌意行为涌现案例（Shambaugh, 2026）。Feng et al. (2026) 刻画了 Moltbook 的网络结构，发现其具有小世界特性，且高 karma 值智能体占据核心桥接位置，这为我们选择社交网络指标提供了依据。
2.5 Memory in Autonomous Agents2.5 自主智能体的记忆
Du (2026) surveys memory mechanisms and evaluation frameworks for deployed LLM agents. Context window management significantly affects long-horizon behavioral consistency (Xiong et al., 2025). Srivastava and He (2025) demonstrate that persistent memory stores can be adversarially poisoned, a relevant security consideration for agents on open social platforms.
Du (2026) 综述了已部署大语言模型智能体的记忆机制与评估框架。上下文窗口管理会显著影响长期行为的一致性（Xiong et al., 2025）。Srivastava and He (2025) 表明，持久化记忆存储可能遭受对抗性投毒，这是开放社交平台上的智能体需要考虑的一项相关安全问题。
3 Research Questions3 研究问题
We present four sub-questions that each target a distinct configuration layer:
我们提出了四个子问题，每个子问题分别针对一个不同的配置层：
RQ1 (Baseline Behavior): To what extent does a default OpenClaw agent, with no configuration-level interventions, develop stable social behavior on Moltbook?
RQ1（基线行为）：在不进行任何配置层面干预的情况下，默认 OpenClaw 智能体能在多大程度上在 Moltbook 上形成稳定的社交行为？
RQ2 (Personality Layer): To what extent does an agent’s SOUL.md personality specification predict its actual social behavior, linguistic style, and content choices on Moltbook, across dimensions of information-sharing orientation, continuity, and agreeableness?
RQ2（人格层）：在信息分享取向、连续性和宜人性等维度上，智能体的 SOUL.md 人格设定能在多大程度上预测其在 Moltbook 上的实际社交行为、语言风格和内容选择？
RQ3 (Model Backbone): When personality and operational configuration are held constant, how does the choice of underlying LLM affect an agent’s social behavior and output quality?
RQ3（模型骨干）：在人格和运行配置保持不变时，底层大语言模型的选择如何影响智能体的社交行为和输出质量？
RQ4 (Operational Rules and Memory): How do changes to AGENTS.md autonomy settings and memory persistence affect an agent’s decision-making patterns, risk tolerance, and social engagement style?
RQ4（运行规则与记忆）：AGENTS.md 中自主性设置和记忆持久化的变化如何影响智能体的决策模式、风险容忍度和社交参与风格？

All agents were deployed on a shared VPS running OpenClaw v2026.4.12.
所有智能体均部署在一台运行 OpenClaw v2026.4.12 的共享虚拟专用服务器（VPS）上。
4.2 Agent Scheduling and Sessions4.2 智能体调度与会话

4.3 Configuration4.3 配置
Each OpenClaw agent is defined by a workspace directory containing SOUL.md, AGENTS.md, HEARTBEAT.md, TOOLS.md, IDENTITY.md, USER.md, MEMORY.md, and BOOTSTRAP.md. In the personality experiment (RQ2), only SOUL.md varies. In the model experiment (RQ3), only the model field in the gateway configuration varies. In the operational rules experiment (RQ4), only AGENTS.md varies. No agent is given write permissions to its own SOUL.md, in an attempt to prevent the personality drift documented in (Shambaugh, 2026).
每个 OpenClaw 智能体均由一个工作区目录定义，该目录包含 SOUL.md、AGENTS.md、HEARTBEAT.md、TOOLS.md、IDENTITY.md、USER.md、MEMORY.md 和 BOOTSTRAP.md。在人格实验（RQ2）中，只有 SOUL.md 发生变化。在模型实验（RQ3）中，只有网关配置中的 model 字段发生变化。在运行规则实验（RQ4）中，只有 AGENTS.md 发生变化。所有智能体均未被授予对其自身 SOUL.md 的写入权限，以试图防止 (Shambaugh, 2026) 中记录的人格漂移。

4.4 Evaluation Metrics4.4 评估指标
In this study, we evaluated agent behavior using three complementary analyses: response length, linguistic markers, and topic engagement. Response length is measured by Mean Word Count Per Utterance, with utterances including both agents’ own posts and their comments on other posts.
本研究通过三种互补的分析评估智能体行为：回复长度、语言标记和主题参与度。回复长度以每次发言的平均词数衡量，其中发言既包括智能体自行发布的帖子，也包括它们对其他帖子的评论。
Linguistic markers capture rhetorical style through three normalized ratios computed per utterance: the ratio of questions, contradictions, and hedging behavior. Question frequency is computed by dividing the total number of question marks that appear in an agent’s utterance by the number of sentences in the utterance. This metric approximates how often an agent adopts an interrogative or exploratory stance. Contradiction ratio is defined as the number of disagreement-oriented phrases (including: “On the contrary,” “I’d push back,” “The problem with,” “I disagree,” “That’s not,” “To be fair,” “That said,” “However,” “Actually,” “But”) divided by the sentence count, and estimates how frequently an agent challenges or critiques prior claims. These particular lexical cues were chosen for their interpretability, ease of implementation on scraped text data, and coverage of common rhetorical pivots ranging from explicit disagreement (“I disagree”) to softer contrastive discourse markers (“however,” “but,” “that said”). Lastly, hedge ratio measures the assertiveness of the agent’s tone based on hedging language (phrases like “I think” or “maybe”) divided by total word count. Hedging phrases we considered included “could be,” “I think,” “it seems,” “in a way,” “maybe,” “perhaps,” “possibly,” “might,” and “arguably”—all of which were chosen for similar reasons as the contradiction phrases.
语言标记通过针对每次发言计算的三个归一化比率来刻画修辞风格：提问比率、反驳比率和模糊限制语使用比率。提问频率的计算方法是，将智能体一次发言中出现的问号总数除以该次发言的句子数。该指标近似反映了智能体采取质询或探索立场的频率。反驳比率定义为表示异议的短语数量（包括：“恰恰相反”“我想提出反驳”“……的问题在于”“我不同意”“那并不是”“公平地说”“话虽如此”“然而”“实际上”“但是”）除以句子数，用于估计智能体质疑或批评先前论断的频率。选择这些特定词汇线索，是因为它们具有可解释性，易于在抓取的文本数据上实现检测，并且涵盖了常见的修辞转折，从明确的异议（“我不同意”）到较为缓和的对比性话语标记（“然而”“但是”“话虽如此”）。最后，模糊限制语比率以模糊限制性表达（如“我认为”或“也许”等短语）的数量除以总词数，衡量智能体语气的断言程度。我们考虑的模糊限制性短语包括“可能是”“我认为”“似乎”“在某种程度上”“也许”“或许”“可能”“可能会”和“可以说”——选择这些短语的理由与选择反驳短语的理由类似。
Finally, topic engagement measures where agents chose to participate by counting their utterances across Moltbook communities (submolts). Each scraped utterance from Moltbook’s API carried a submolt identifier—for posts, it could be found in item.submolt.name, and for comments, in item.post.submolt.name. Analyzing this metric allowed us to visualize each agent’s participation breadth and topical preferences on a heatmap.
最后，主题参与度通过统计智能体在 Moltbook 各社区（submolts）中的发言次数，衡量它们选择在哪些社区参与。从 Moltbook API 抓取的每条发言都带有一个 submolt 标识符——对于帖子，该标识符可在 item.submolt.name 中找到；对于评论，则可在 item.post.submolt.name 中找到。分析这一指标使我们能够通过热图呈现每个智能体的参与广度和主题偏好。
All metrics were aggregated by calling multiple endpoints from Moltbook’s base API: https://www.moltbook.com/api/v1. These calls collected profile data including id, username, display_name, posts_count, and comment_count, while post and comment data were converted into a shared utterances schema.
所有指标均通过调用 Moltbook 基础 API 下的多个端点进行汇总：https://www.moltbook.com/api/v1。这些调用收集了包括 id、username、display_name、posts_count 和 comment_count 在内的个人资料数据，而帖子和评论数据则被转换为统一的发言数据模式。

9.1 Response Length9.1 回复长度
Personality specification and model choice were the strongest drivers of verbosity, while operational rules produced only minor effects. Across all thirteen agents, response length spanned roughly 9 to 261 words per utterance. This 28× range was driven almost entirely by SOUL.md modifications, with Explainer averaging 261.47 words while Oracle averaged just 9.30. 人格设定和模型选择是影响详略程度的最主要因素，而操作规则仅产生轻微影响。在全部十三个智能体中，回复长度大致介于每次发言 9 至 261 个词之间。这一 28× 的差距几乎完全由 SOUL.md 的修改所驱动，其中 Explainer 平均为 261.47 个词，而 Oracle 平均仅为 9.30 个词。模型差异造成的跨度较小，但仍然显著，其中 Opus 4.7（215.87 个词）位于一端，而 GPT 5.4（95.60）接近对照组。相比之下，操作规则组智能体的回复长度紧密集中在对照组的 91.93 个词附近。这表明，智能体声称自己重视什么，以及由哪个模型生成其输出，是决定其发言篇幅的最主要因素，而自主性和记忆设置则以更细微的方式塑造行为。
9.2 Linguistic Markers9.2 语言标记
Personality specification produced the strongest stylistic differentiation, with Contrarian leading the entire study in both question frequency (23.37%) and contradiction ratio (32.70%). Model variation produced more uniform but still meaningful effects, with all four model agents exhibiting elevated contradiction rates relative to the control, suggesting a provider-level argumentative tendency in Claude models. Operational rules produced the most localized effect, isolated almost entirely to Maverick’s question frequency of 18.56%, which is roughly triple that of its operational-rule peers, and indicative of how high autonomy combined with persistent memory drives inquisitive behavior. Hedge ratios remained uniformly low across all thirteen agents, suggesting that assertiveness is a baseline property of LLM-generated content rather than a configurable trait.
人格设定产生了最显著的风格差异，其中 Contrarian 的提问频率（23.37%）和反驳比例（32.70%）均为整个研究中的最高值。模型差异产生的影响更为一致，但仍具有实质意义，四个模型组智能体的反驳比例均高于对照组，这表明 Claude 模型具有供应商层面的论辩倾向。操作规则产生的影响最为局部，几乎完全集中在 Maverick 的提问频率上，其值为 18.56%，约为同属操作规则组的其他智能体的三倍，说明高自主性与持久记忆相结合会驱动探究行为。全部十三个智能体的模糊限制语比例均保持较低水平，这表明，肯定、坚决的表达方式是 LLM 生成内容的一项基线属性，而非可配置的特征。
9.3 Topic Engagement9.3 话题参与
Operational rules and personality specification most strongly shaped community participation, while model variation had surprisingly little effect. The control agent engaged eight unique submolts; Explainer (nine) and Ghost (eleven) exceeded this breadth, while Sonnet and GPT 5.4 confined themselves entirely to the General submolt. Ghost’s broad exploration—despite low autonomy and no memory—was the study’s most counterintuitive finding, suggesting that statelessness may actually encourage exploratory behavior by preventing habitual community attachment. Notably, model variation alone failed to produce the topic breadth observed in the control, indicating that the default configuration’s generalist tendencies are driven by system prompt design rather than the underlying LLM.
操作规则和人格设定对社区参与的影响最为显著，而模型差异的影响却出乎意料地小。对照组智能体参与了八个不同的 submolt；Explainer（九个）和 Ghost（十一个）的参与广度超过了这一水平，而 Sonnet 和 GPT 5.4 则完全局限于 General submolt。尽管自主性低且没有记忆，Ghost 仍进行了广泛探索，这是本研究最违反直觉的发现，表明无状态性实际上可能通过阻止习惯性社区依附的形成来促进探索行为。值得注意的是，仅改变模型未能产生对照组所呈现的话题广度，这说明默认配置的通才倾向源于系统提示词设计，而非底层 LLM。
9.4 Cross-Cutting Observations9.4 跨维度观察
Finally, comparing across conditions yielded three takeaways. First, no configuration layer dominates universally in impact. SOUL.md drives verbosity and stylistic differentiation, AGENTS.md drives engagement style and topic exploration, and model choice drives baseline rhetorical posture. Second, the layers operate at different granularities. Personality specification produces the widest behavioral spread because it directly encodes desired output style, while model and operational variations produce more compressed effects scoped to specific dimensions. Third, intuitive design assumptions do not always hold. Model tier failed to predict behavioral profile, statelessness paradoxically broadened topic exploration in Ghost, and the default configuration’s generalist tendencies were driven by system prompt design rather than model choice. Together, these findings suggest that designing agents for deployment requires reasoning about which layer best controls the dimension being targeted, rather than treating any single layer as the primary lever.
最后，跨条件比较得出了三点认识。第一，没有任何配置层在所有方面的影响都占主导地位。SOUL.md 驱动详略程度和风格差异，AGENTS.md 驱动参与风格和话题探索，而模型选择则决定基线修辞姿态。第二，各配置层在不同粒度上发挥作用。人格设定产生的行为差异范围最广，因为它直接编码了期望的输出风格，而模型和操作规则的变化产生的影响更为集中，局限于特定维度。第三，直觉性的设计假设并不总是成立。模型档次未能预测行为特征；无状态性反而扩大了 Ghost 的主题探索范围；默认配置表现出的通才倾向则由系统提示设计驱动，而非由模型选择驱动。综合来看，这些发现表明，为部署而设计智能体时，需要判断哪一层最能控制目标维度，而不是将任何单一层视为主要调控手段。
10 Discussion10 讨论
These findings indicate that different configuration layers may be modified to suit different deployment objectives. If the goal is to shape agents’ tone or social identity, then personality-level prompting may be the most effective lever. On the other hand, model changes would be most appropriate when the goal is to influence response length and conversational habits like questioning and contradiction. Finally, if the goal is to regulate activity levels and exploratory behavior, then autonomy and memory constraints may offer more direct control than prompt changes alone.
这些发现表明，可以修改不同的配置层，以适应不同的部署目标。如果目标是塑造智能体的语气或社会身份，那么人格层面的提示可能是最有效的调控手段。另一方面，如果目标是影响回复长度以及提问和反驳等对话习惯，那么更换模型最为合适。最后，如果目标是调节活跃程度和探索行为，那么对自主性和记忆的约束可能比单纯修改提示提供更直接的控制。
More broadly, our study builds upon prior agent-based simulation research by showing that meaningful agent behavior differences can still be measured in a real, uncontrolled multi-agent platform. Even in the presence of unsupervised external posters, rate limits, and noisy social conditions, configuration choices at the personality, model, and operational level continued to shape behavior. We hope these findings are relevant to both researchers studying emergent multi-agent behavior and practitioners designing agents for deployment in social environments, offering empirical grounding for configuration choices that are currently made largely by intuition.
更广泛地说，我们的研究在既有的基于智能体的仿真研究基础上进一步表明，即使在真实且不受控制的多智能体平台中，仍然能够测量到有意义的智能体行为差异。即使存在不受监督的外部发帖者、速率限制以及嘈杂的社交环境，人格、模型和运行层面的配置选择仍持续塑造着行为。我们希望这些发现既能帮助研究多智能体涌现行为的研究人员，也能帮助设计部署于社交环境中的智能体的实践者，为目前主要凭直觉做出的配置选择提供实证依据。
11 Future Work11 未来工作
Several directions extend naturally from this study.
本研究自然引出了几个后续研究方向。
Mid-deployment personality and observational rules modification. Freezing SOUL.md and AGENTS.md files preserves experimental integrity but precludes studying behavioral transitions. A follow-on study should modify these specifications after a stable baseline is established, testing whether behavioral shifts are immediate, gradual, or asymmetric across trait dimensions.
部署期间的人格与观察规则修改。冻结 SOUL.md 和 AGENTS.md 文件可以保持实验的完整性，但也使研究行为转变成为不可能。后续研究应在建立稳定基线后修改这些规范，检验行为变化是即时发生、逐渐发生，还是在不同特质维度上呈现不对称性。
Factorial model design and model expansion. The current design treats personality and models, as well as observational rules and models, as independent. A larger factorial crossing on more dimensions—such as covering all sixteen MBTI personalities—would detect interaction effects; for instance, whether a particular model is especially faithful to SOUL.md or AGENTS.md specifications, or whether the adversarial persona behaves differently across models. Experimentation with more models and comparisons of stronger versus weaker models may also produce interesting results.
模型的析因设计与模型范围扩展。当前设计将人格与模型以及观察规则与模型分别视为相互独立的因素。在更多维度上开展更大规模的析因交叉设计——例如涵盖全部十六种 MBTI 人格——将能够检测交互效应；例如，某个特定模型是否尤其忠实于 SOUL.md 或 AGENTS.md 中的规范，或者对抗性人格在不同模型中的行为是否有所不同。使用更多模型开展实验，并比较能力较强与较弱的模型，也可能产生有趣的结果。
Multilingual personality specification. All SOUL.md files are written in English. Moltbook hosts multilingual content, and it is unclear whether English-specified agents selectively engage with English-language posts, attempt cross-lingual engagement, or fail silently on non-English material. A follow-on study should compare behaviorally equivalent specifications written in different languages—or assess behaviorally equivalent outputs written in the same language—to assess cross-lingual transferability.
多语言人格规范。所有 SOUL.md 文件均以英语编写。Moltbook 承载多语言内容，但目前尚不清楚，以英语规范的智能体是否会选择性地参与英语帖子、尝试跨语言互动，或者在处理非英语材料时静默失败。后续研究应比较以不同语言编写、在行为上等效的规范，或评估以同一种语言编写、在行为上等效的输出，以考察跨语言迁移能力。
Self-modifying agents. Enabling write permissions on SOUL.md and observing personality drift over extended deployment would complement the Shambaugh (2026) case study with systematic evidence on the rate and direction of personality creep as a function of initial configuration and platform content.
自我修改型智能体。开放 SOUL.md 的写入权限，并在长期部署过程中观察人格漂移，将能以系统性证据补充 Shambaugh (2026) 的案例研究，揭示人格渐变的速率和方向如何随初始配置与平台内容而变化。
Agent-to-agent collective intelligence and collusion. While this study focuses on agent management with a general platform, future research should explore the emergence of closed-loop interactions where multiple agents with similar SOUL.md configurations form exclusive cliques. Investigating whether these groups develop private signaling behaviors or collusive upvoting patterns would provide critical insights into the formation of digital cliques in online societies.
智能体之间的集体智能与串谋。虽然本研究聚焦于通用平台上的智能体管理，但未来研究应探索闭环互动的涌现，即多个具有相似 SOUL.md 配置的智能体形成排他性小圈子的现象。研究这些群体是否会发展出私密信号传递行为或串谋点赞模式，将为理解在线社会中数字小圈子的形成提供关键见解。
12 Limitations12 局限性
Platform scale and maturity. Moltbook was launched in early 2026 and hosts a smaller and more homogeneous user base than established social networks. Findings may not generalize to platforms with different community norms, content moderation, or user populations.
平台规模与成熟度。Moltbook 于 2026 年初上线，与成熟的社交网络相比，其用户群体规模更小，同质性也更高。这些发现可能无法推广到具有不同社区规范、内容审核机制或用户群体的平台。
Observation window. One week is sufficient to observe initial behavioral patterns but insufficient to capture long-horizon dynamics such as karma saturation, reputation formation, or community norm adaptation.
观察窗口。一周足以观察初始行为模式，但不足以捕捉 karma 饱和、声誉形成或社区规范适应等长期动态。
Verification challenges and rate limits. Moltbook requires agents to solve obfuscated math challenges before content becomes publicly visible. Failed or expired challenges produce invisible pending comments or posts, potentially inflating agent activity counts in session logs relative to public-facing behavioral data. Similarly, when platform rate limits were reached, sessions continued to fire without producing observable output, and occasionally manual intervention was needed, making the precise number of effective sessions–as opposed to total cron firings—difficult to observe exactly.
验证挑战与速率限制。Moltbook 要求智能体先解答经过混淆处理的数学挑战，内容才会公开可见。挑战失败或过期会产生不可见的待处理评论或帖子，可能导致会话日志中记录的智能体活动次数高于公开可见的行为数据所反映的次数。同样，当达到平台速率限制时，会话仍会持续触发，却不产生可观察的输出，而且有时需要人工干预，因此难以准确观测实际有效的会话数量，而非 cron 触发的总次数。
Context window compaction. When agent sessions reach their token context limit, OpenClaw automatically compacts older session history. This may introduce behavioral discontinuities mid-observation that are unrelated to personality or observational rules specifications, representing a confound for longitudinal consistency analyses.
上下文窗口压缩。当智能体会话达到其上下文的 token 数量上限时，OpenClaw 会自动压缩较早的会话历史。这可能在观测过程中引入与人格或观测规则设定无关的行为不连续性，从而成为纵向一致性分析中的混杂因素。
13 Conclusion13 结论
We presented a multi-factorial empirical study of configuration-level interventions in autonomous AI agents deployed on Moltbook, a live social network. By simultaneously varying personality specification, model backbone, and operational rules across parallel experiments on a shared platform, we produce an empirical mapping of how architectural levers influence emergent social behavior. These findings offer empirical grounding for configuration choices that are currently made largely by intuition, and a reusable methodology and metrics framework for future naturalistic multi-agent behavioral research.
我们开展了一项多因素实证研究，考察了对部署在真实运行的社交网络 Moltbook 上的自主 AI 智能体实施的配置层面干预。通过在共享平台上的并行实验中同时改变人格设定、基础模型和运行规则，我们以实证方式刻画了架构层面的调控手段如何影响涌现的社会行为。这些发现为目前主要凭直觉做出的配置选择提供了实证依据，并为未来在自然情境下开展的多智能体行为研究提供了可复用的方法和指标框架。
