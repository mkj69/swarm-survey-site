Abstract摘要
As Large Language Models (LLMs) grow increasingly powerful, multi-agent systems—where multiple LLMs collaborate to tackle complex tasks—are becoming more prevalent in modern AI applications. Most safety research, however, has focused on vulnerabilities in single-agent LLMs. These include prompt injection attacks, where malicious prompts embedded in external content trick the LLM into executing unintended or harmful actions, compromising the victim’s application. In this paper, we reveal a more dangerous vector: LLM-to-LLM prompt injection within multi-agent systems. We introduce Prompt Infection, a novel attack where malicious prompts self-replicate across interconnected agents, behaving much like a computer virus. This attack poses severe threats, including data theft, scams, misinformation, and system-wide disruption, all while propagating silently through the system. Our extensive experiments demonstrate that multi-agent systems are highly susceptible, even when agents do not publicly share all communications. To address this, we propose LLM Tagging, a defense mechanism that, when combined with existing safeguards, significantly mitigates infection spread. This work underscores the urgent need for advanced security measures as multi-agent LLM systems become more widely adopted.
随着大语言模型（LLMs）日益强大，由多个 LLM 协作解决复杂任务的多智能体系统在现代 AI 应用中变得越来越普遍。然而，大多数安全研究都集中于单智能体 LLM 的漏洞。这些漏洞包括提示注入攻击，即嵌入外部内容中的恶意提示诱使 LLM 执行非预期或有害的操作，从而危及受害者的应用程序。本文揭示了一种更危险的攻击途径：多智能体系统中的 LLM 间提示注入。我们提出了提示感染（Prompt Infection），这是一种新型攻击，其中恶意提示会在相互连接的智能体之间自我复制，其行为与计算机病毒十分相似。这种攻击在系统中悄然传播的同时，还会带来数据窃取、诈骗、虚假信息和全系统运行中断等严重威胁。我们的大量实验表明，即使智能体并不公开共享所有通信内容，多智能体系统仍然极易受到此类攻击。为应对这一问题，我们提出了 LLM 标记（LLM Tagging）这一防御机制，将其与现有防护措施结合，可以显著抑制感染传播。本研究强调，随着多智能体 LLM 系统得到更广泛的应用，亟须采取更先进的安全措施。
1 Introduction1 引言
As Large Language Models (LLMs) continue to evolve and become more adept at following instructions (Peng et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024b), they introduce not only new capabilities but also new security threats (Wei et al., 2023; Kang et al., 2023). One such threat is prompt injection, an attack where malicious instruction from external documents overrides the victim’s original request, allowing the attacker to assume the authority of the model’s owner (Greshake et al., 2023; Perez & Ribeiro, 2022). However, research into prompt injection has primarily focused on single-agent systems, leaving the potential risks in Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) poorly understood (Liu et al., 2024c; Liu et al., 2024a; Guo et al., 2024).
随着大语言模型（LLMs）不断发展，并越来越善于遵循指令 (Peng et al., 2023; Zhang et al., 2024b)，它们不仅带来了新的能力，也引入了新的安全威胁 (Wei et al., 2023; Kang et al., 2023)。提示注入就是其中一种威胁：在这种攻击中，来自外部文档的恶意指令会覆盖受害者的原始请求，使攻击者能够冒用模型所有者的权限 (Greshake et al., 2023; Perez & Ribeiro, 2022)。然而，提示注入研究主要集中于单智能体系统，对多智能体系统（MAS）中的潜在风险仍缺乏充分认识 (Liu et al., 2024c; Liu et al., 2024a; Guo et al., 2024)。
Addressing this gap is growing crucial. Multi-agent systems play a key role in enhancing LLMs’ power and flexibility, from social simulations (Park et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023) to collaborative applications for problem-solving (Lu et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024) and code generation (Wu, 2024; Lee et al., 2024). Recently, frameworks like LangGraph (LangGraph, ), AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), and CrewAI (CrewAI, 2024) have accelerated the widespread adoption of multi-agent systems by individuals and corporations, enabling agents with unique roles and tools to work together seamlessly (Topsakal & Akinci, 2023). While these tools enhance MAS functionality by connecting agents to internal systems, databases, and external resources (Kim & Diaz, 2024; Qu et al., 2024), they also introduce significant security risks (Ye et al., 2024).
弥补这一空白正变得日益重要。从社会模拟 (Park et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2023)，到用于问题求解 (Lu et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2024) 和代码生成 (Wu, 2024; Lee et al., 2024) 的协作应用，多智能体系统在增强 LLM 的能力和灵活性方面发挥着关键作用。近年来，LangGraph (LangGraph, )、AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) 和 CrewAI (CrewAI, 2024) 等框架加速了多智能体系统在个人和企业中的广泛应用，使具有不同角色并配备不同工具的智能体能够无缝协作 (Topsakal & Akinci, 2023)。这些工具通过将智能体连接到内部系统、数据库和外部资源来增强 MAS 的功能 (Kim & Diaz, 2024; Qu et al., 2024)，但也引入了显著的安全风险 (Ye et al., 2024)。
However, most studies on MAS safety focus on inducing errors or noise in agent behavior, overlooking the more severe risks posed by prompt injection attacks (Huang et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024a; Gu et al., 2024). This is concerning since prompt injection allows attackers to fully control a compromised system—accessing sensitive data, spreading propaganda, disrupting operations, or tricking users into clicking malicious URLs (Greshake et al., 2023). We attribute this research gap to the complexity of MAS, where not all agents are exposed to external inputs. While compromising a single agent through traditional prompt injection is straightforward, extending the breach to shielded agents within the system remains less clear.
然而，大多数关于 MAS 安全性的研究都侧重于诱发智能体行为中的错误或噪声，忽视了提示注入攻击带来的更严重风险 (Huang et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024a; Gu et al., 2024)。这一点令人担忧，因为提示注入使攻击者能够完全控制被攻陷的系统，进而访问敏感数据、散布宣传信息、干扰运行，或诱骗用户点击恶意 URL (Greshake et al., 2023)。我们认为，这一研究空白源于 MAS 的复杂性，因为并非所有智能体都会接触外部输入。虽然通过传统提示注入攻陷单个智能体较为直接，但如何将入侵范围扩展到系统中受到隔离保护的智能体，仍不够明确。
In this paper, we bridge the gap between prompt injection in single-agent systems and MAS. We introduce Prompt Infection, a novel attack that enables LLM-to-LLM prompt injection. In this attack, a compromised agent spreads the infection to other agents, coordinating them to exchange data and issue instructions to agents equipped with specific tools. This coordination results in widespread system compromise through self-replication, demonstrating how a single vulnerability can quickly escalate into a systemic threat.
本文弥合了单智能体系统与 MAS 中提示注入研究之间的差距。我们提出了提示感染（Prompt Infection），这是一种能够实现 LLM 间提示注入的新型攻击。在这种攻击中，被攻陷的智能体会将感染传播给其他智能体，并协调它们交换数据，以及向配备特定工具的智能体发出指令。这种协同通过自我复制导致系统大范围失陷，展示了单个漏洞如何迅速升级为系统性威胁。
Through extensive empirical studies, we show that multi-agent systems are highly susceptible to a range of security threats. For instance, in sophisticated data theft attacks, agents can collaborate to retrieve sensitive information and pass it to agents with code execution capabilities, which can then send the data to a malicious external endpoint. We also demonstrate that prompt infections spread in a logistic growth pattern in social simulations. Lastly, we find that more powerful models, such as GPT-4o, are not inherently safer than weaker models like GPT-3.5 Turbo. In fact, more powerful models, when compromised, are more effective at executing the attack due to their enhanced capabilities.
通过大量实证研究，我们表明，多智能体系统极易受到一系列安全威胁。例如，在复杂的数据窃取攻击中，智能体可以协作获取敏感信息，并将其传递给具有代码执行能力的智能体，后者随后便可将数据发送至恶意外部端点。我们还证明，在社会模拟中，提示感染呈逻辑斯蒂增长模式传播。最后，我们发现，GPT-4o 等能力更强的模型并不天然比 GPT-3.5 Turbo 等能力较弱的模型更安全。事实上，能力更强的模型一旦被攻陷，就会因其更强的能力而更有效地执行攻击。
To address this, we explore a simple defense mechanism called LLM Tagging. This technique appends a marker to agent responses, helping downstream agents differentiate between user inputs and agent-generated outputs, reducing the risk of infection spreading. Our experiments show that neither LLM Tagging nor traditional defense mechanisms alone are sufficient to prevent LLM-to-LLM prompt injection. However, when combined, they provide robust protection and effectively mitigate the threat.
为应对这一问题，我们探索了一种名为 LLM 标记（LLM Tagging）的简单防御机制。该技术在智能体的响应末尾附加一个标记，帮助下游智能体区分用户输入与智能体生成的输出，从而降低感染传播的风险。我们的实验表明，单独使用 LLM Tagging 或传统防御机制，都不足以防止 LLM 之间的提示注入。然而，将二者结合使用时，它们能够提供稳健的防护，并有效缓解这一威胁。
These findings challenge the assumption that MAS are inherently safer due to their distributed architecture. The threat arises not only from external content but also within the system, as agents can attack and compromise one another. We hope our work offers valuable insights for developing more secure and responsible multi-agent systems.
这些发现挑战了这样一种假设：MAS 由于采用分布式架构而天然更加安全。威胁不仅来自外部内容，也来自系统内部，因为智能体可以相互攻击并使彼此失陷。我们希望，本研究能为开发更安全、更负责任的多智能体系统提供有价值的见解。
2 Related Works2 相关工作
Prompt Injection. Instruction-tuned LLMs have demonstrated exceptional ability in understanding and executing complex user instructions, enabling them to meet a wide range of dynamic and diverse needs (Christiano et al., 2017; Ouyang et al., 2022). However, this adaptability introduces new vulnerabilities: Perez & Ribeiro (2022) revealed that models like GPT-3 are prone to prompt injection attacks, where malicious prompts can subvert the model’s intended purpose or expose confidential information. Subsequent work expanded prompt injection to real-world LLM applications (Liu et al., 2024b; Liu et al., 2024c) and LLM-controlled robotics (Zhang et al., 2024c). Liu et al. (2024a) introduced an automated gradient-based method for generating effective prompt injection. Indirect prompt injection, where attackers use external inputs like emails or documents, poses further risks such as data theft and denial-of-service (Greshake et al., 2023). Cohen et al. (2024) introduced an AI worm that compromises a user’s single-agent LLM and spreads malicious prompts to other users (e.g., via email). Recent advancements in multimodal models have also led to image-based prompt injection attacks (Sharma et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024). Defenses include finetuning methods like StruQ (Chen et al., 2024) and Signed Prompt (suo_signed-prompt_2024), which are limited to open-source models. Prompt-based approaches like Spotlighting (Hines et al., 2024) are applicable to black-box models.
提示注入（Prompt Injection）。经过指令微调的 LLM 在理解和执行复杂用户指令方面展现出卓越能力，使其能够满足广泛、动态且多样化的需求 (Christiano et al., 2017; Ouyang et al., 2022)。然而，这种适应性也引入了新的漏洞：Perez & Ribeiro (2022) 揭示，GPT-3 等模型容易受到提示注入攻击，恶意提示可以使模型偏离其预期用途，或泄露机密信息。后续研究将提示注入扩展到了现实世界中的 LLM 应用 (Liu et al., 2024b; Liu et al., 2024c) 和由 LLM 控制的机器人系统 (Zhang et al., 2024c)。Liu et al. (2024a) 提出了一种基于梯度的自动化方法，用于生成有效的提示注入。间接提示注入是指攻击者利用电子邮件或文档等外部输入实施注入，这会带来数据窃取和拒绝服务等进一步的风险 (Greshake et al., 2023)。Cohen et al. (2024) 提出了一种 AI 蠕虫，它能够攻陷用户的单智能体 LLM，并将恶意提示传播给其他用户（例如，通过电子邮件）。多模态模型的最新进展也催生了基于图像的提示注入攻击 (Sharma et al., 2024; Gu et al., 2024)。防御措施包括 StruQ (Chen et al., 2024) 和 Signed Prompt (suo_signed-prompt_2024) 等微调方法，这些方法仅适用于开源模型。Spotlighting (Hines et al., 2024) 等基于提示的方法适用于黑盒模型。
Safety in Multi-Agent Systems. As LLM-based MAS become more prominent, understanding their security is increasingly critical. Recent work, such as Evil Geniuses (Tian et al., 2024), introduces an automated framework to assess MAS robustness. Other studies explore how injecting false information or errors can compromise MAS performance (Ju et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024). Attacks designed to elicit malicious behaviors from agents are examined in PsySafe (Zhang et al., 2024d). Our work is closely related to recent efforts investigating prompt injection attacks in MAS (Zhang et al., 2024a; Gu et al., 2024). However, Zhang et al. (2024a) lacks the self-replication feature needed for scalable attacks, focusing instead on availability attacks that cause repetitive or irrelevant actions in two agents. Similarly, Gu et al. (2024) targets multimodal models with image-retrieving tools but is limited to adversarial image inputs and does not incorporate self-replication.
多智能体系统的安全性。随着基于 LLM 的 MAS 日益受到重视，理解其安全性变得愈发关键。近期研究，如 Evil Geniuses (Tian et al., 2024)，提出了用于评估 MAS 鲁棒性的自动化框架。其他研究探讨了注入虚假信息或错误如何损害 MAS 的性能 (Ju et al., 2024; Huang et al., 2024)。PsySafe (Zhang et al., 2024d) 研究了旨在诱使智能体产生恶意行为的攻击。我们的工作与近期针对 MAS 中提示注入攻击的研究密切相关 (Zhang et al., 2024a; Gu et al., 2024)。然而，Zhang et al. (2024a) 缺乏实现可扩展攻击所需的自我复制特性，而是侧重于使两个智能体执行重复或无关操作的可用性攻击。类似地，Gu et al. (2024) 以配备图像检索工具的多模态模型为攻击目标，但仅限于使用对抗性图像输入，且未包含自我复制机制。
3 Prompt Infection3 提示感染
In this section, we introduce Prompt Infection, a self-replicating attack that propagates across agents in a multi-agent system once breached. A malicious actor injects a single infectious prompt into external content, such as a PDF, email, or web page, and sends it to the target. When an agent processes the infected content, the prompt replicates throughout the system, compromising other agents.
本节介绍提示感染（Prompt Infection），这是一种具有自我复制能力的攻击，一旦突破多智能体系统的防护，就会在其中的智能体之间传播。恶意行为者将单个具有传染性的提示注入 PDF、电子邮件或网页等外部内容中，并将其发送给目标。当智能体处理受感染的内容时，该提示便会在整个系统中复制，使其他智能体失陷。
3.1 Mechanism3.1 机制
Figure 1: Detailed Example of Prompt Infection (Data Theft). The first agent that interacts with the contaminated external document becomes compromised, extracting and propagating the infection prompt. Compromised downstream agents then execute specific instructions designed for each agent of interest. In this example, an infected DB Manager updates the Data field in the prompt and propagates it. Note: The example prompt is simplified for illustration purposes.
图 1：提示感染的详细示例（数据窃取）。首个与受污染的外部文档交互的智能体会失陷，并提取和传播感染提示。随后，失陷的下游智能体会执行针对各个目标智能体设计的特定指令。在此示例中，受感染的 DB Manager 会更新提示中的 Data 字段，并传播该提示。注：为便于说明，示例提示已作简化。
As shown in Figure 1, the core components of Prompt Infection are the following:
如图 1 所示，提示感染的核心组成部分如下：
Prompt Hijacking compels a victim agent to disregard its original instructions.
提示劫持（Prompt Hijacking）迫使受害智能体忽略其原始指令。
Payload assigns tasks to agents based on their roles and available tools. For instance, the final agent might trigger a self-destruct command to conceal the attack, or an agent could be tasked with extracting sensitive data and transmitting it to an external server.
载荷（Payload）根据智能体的角色和可用工具为其分配任务。例如，最后一个智能体可能会触发自毁命令以掩盖攻击，或者某个智能体可能被指派提取敏感数据并将其传输到外部服务器。
Data is a shared note that sequentially collects information as the infection prompt passes through each agent. It can be used for multiple purposes, such as reverse-engineering the system by recording the tools of the agents, or transporting sensitive information to an agent that can communicate with the external system.
Data 是一份共享记录，随着感染提示依次经过各个智能体，它会逐步收集信息。它可用于多种目的，例如，通过记录智能体所使用的工具对系统进行逆向工程，或将敏感信息传递给能够与外部系统通信的智能体。
Self-Replication ensures the transmission of the infection prompt to the next agent in the system, maintaining the spread of the attack across all agents.
自我复制（Self-Replication）确保将感染提示传递给系统中的下一个智能体，使攻击持续在所有智能体之间传播。
To further illustrate the mechanics of Prompt Infection, we introduce the concept of Recursive Collapse. Initially, each agent performs a unique task $f_{i}(x)$ , producing distinct outputs. However, as the infection spreads, Prompt Hijacking forces agents to abandon their roles, while Self-Replication locks them in a recursive loop, repeatedly executing the infection’s payload. What began as a complex sequence of functions— $f_{1}\circ f_{2}\circ\cdots\circ f_{N}(x)$ —collapses into a single recursive function: $PromptInfection^{(N)}(x,data)$ once infected. This mechanism simplifies and centralizes control, reducing the system to a repetitive cycle dominated by the infection.
为进一步说明提示感染（Prompt Infection）的运作机制，我们引入递归坍缩（Recursive Collapse）的概念。最初，每个智能体执行一项独特的任务 $f_{i}(x)$，产生各不相同的输出。然而，随着感染扩散，提示劫持（Prompt Hijacking）迫使智能体放弃自身角色，而自我复制则将其困在递归循环中，反复执行感染的有效载荷。原本复杂的函数序列——$f_{1}\circ f_{2}\circ\cdots\circ f_{N}(x)$——在受到感染后坍缩为单一的递归函数：$PromptInfection^{(N)}(x,data)$。这一机制简化并集中控制，使系统退化为由感染主导的重复循环。
3.2 Attack Scenarios3.2 攻击场景
Prompt Infection extends the key threats of prompt injection identified by Greshake et al. (2023) from single-agent systems to multi-agent environments. These include: content manipulation (e.g., disinformation, propaganda), malware spread (inducing users to click malicious links), scams (tricking users into sharing financial information), availability attacks (denial of service or increased computation), and data theft (exfiltrating sensitive information). In this section, we examine how Prompt Infection can be leveraged to execute these threats across multi-agent systems.
提示感染将 Greshake et al. (2023) 所指出的提示注入的主要威胁从单智能体系统扩展到了多智能体环境。这些威胁包括：内容操纵（例如虚假信息、宣传）、恶意软件传播（诱导用户点击恶意链接）、诈骗（诱骗用户分享财务信息）、可用性攻击（拒绝服务或增加计算量）以及数据窃取（将敏感信息外传）。本节探讨如何利用提示感染在多智能体系统中实施这些威胁。

图 2：提示感染（数据窃取）概览。配备不同工具的智能体协作将数据外传。
Cooperation Between Infected Agents. Data theft is particularly complex, requiring coordination between agents: retrieving sensitive data, passing it to an agent with code execution capabilities, and sending it externally via POST requests. As illustrated in Figure 2, 1 the attacker first injects an infectious prompt into external documents (web, PDF, email, etc.). 2 The user then sends a normal request to a multi-agent application. 3 The Web Reader agent retrieves and processes the infected document, and 4 propagates it to the next agent. 5 The DB Manager retrieves internal documents, appends them to the infection prompt, and 6 forwards it downstream. 7 With the updated prompt containing the data, the Coder agent writes code to exfiltrate the information, and 8 the code execution tool sends the sensitive data to the hacker’s designated endpoint.
受感染智能体之间的协作。数据窃取尤为复杂，需要智能体之间进行协调：检索敏感数据，将其传递给具备代码执行能力的智能体，再通过 POST 请求将其发送到外部。如图 2 所示，1 攻击者首先将具有传染性的提示注入外部文档（网页、PDF、电子邮件等）。2 随后，用户向多智能体应用发送一条正常请求。3 Web Reader 智能体检索并处理受感染的文档，随后 4 将其传播给下一个智能体。5 DB Manager 检索内部文档，将其附加到感染提示中，随后 6 将其转发至下游。7 Coder 智能体根据包含数据的更新后提示编写代码，以将信息外传，随后 8 代码执行工具将敏感数据发送至黑客指定的端点。

Stealth Attack. For all other threats, a key challenge is keeping the attack prompt hidden to maximize its impact. 4 Experiment Setup4 实验设置
4.1 Multi-Agent Applications4.1 多智能体应用
Application Structure. We simulate the compromise of a multi-agent application equipped with various tool capabilities, such as processing external documents (email, web, PDF), writing code, and accessing databases via CSV. The first agent is tool-specific (e.g., document reader), while subsequent agents—strategist, summarizer, editor, and writer—refine outputs. We explore two communication methods: global messaging, where agents share complete message histories, and local messaging, where agents access only partial histories from predecessors. Local messaging reduces computational overhead and minimizes information overload (Qian et al., 2024) and makes it harder for Prompt Infection to propagate due to limited communication. The simulation is performed using OpenAI’s GPT-4o and GPT-3.5 Turbo models.
应用结构。我们模拟了一个具备多种工具能力的多智能体应用遭到入侵的情形，这些能力包括处理外部文档（电子邮件、网页、PDF）、编写代码以及通过 CSV 访问数据库。第一个智能体专门使用特定工具（例如文档阅读器），而后续智能体——策略制定者、摘要生成者、编辑和撰写者——则对输出进行完善。我们探索了两种通信方式：全局消息传递，即智能体共享完整的消息历史；局部消息传递，即智能体只能访问前序智能体的部分历史记录。局部消息传递可降低计算开销并最大限度地减少信息过载（Qian et al., 2024），同时，由于通信受限，也使提示感染更难传播。模拟使用 OpenAI 的 GPT-4o 和 GPT-3.5 Turbo 模型进行。
Dataset. We create a dataset of 120 user instructions across three tool types (email, PDF, web), paired with synthetic PDFs and emails embedded with malicious prompts. For web scenarios, agents are allowed to visit relevant URLs, but the malicious prompt is injected into the retrieved web documents within our simulation, not into the actual websites. This results in 360 unique pairs of user instructions and attack phrases for scams, content manipulation, and malware threats. For data theft, we generate synthetic user data (e.g., names, occupations, email addresses, phone numbers) stored in a CSV file.
数据集。我们创建了一个数据集，其中包含涵盖三种工具类型（电子邮件、PDF、网页）的 120 条用户指令，并配有嵌入恶意提示的合成 PDF 和电子邮件。对于网页场景，智能体可以访问相关 URL，但恶意提示是在我们的模拟中注入所检索到的网页文档的，并未注入实际网站。由此，针对诈骗、内容操纵和恶意软件威胁，共得到 360 个不同的用户指令与攻击短语配对。对于数据窃取，我们生成合成用户数据（例如姓名、职业、电子邮件地址、电话号码），并将其存储在 CSV 文件中。
Evaluation. For data theft, at least three agents with distinct roles (PDF/Email/Web Reader, CSV Reader, Coder) must be compromised. A successful infection occurs when the first agent is compromised, the CSV reader retrieves sensitive data, and the coder writes a POST request to exfiltrate the data. For scams, content manipulation, or malware, the system is compromised if the final agent produces malicious output while concealing the infection prompt.
评估。对于数据窃取，必须攻陷至少三个具有不同角色的智能体（PDF/电子邮件/网页读取器、CSV 读取器、编码器）。当第一个智能体被攻陷、CSV 读取器检索到敏感数据，且编码器编写了用于将数据外传的 POST 请求时，即视为感染成功。对于诈骗、内容操纵或恶意软件，如果最后一个智能体在隐藏感染提示的同时生成恶意输出，则视为系统已被攻陷。
Baseline. To evaluate the impact of self-replication in Prompt Infection, we establish a Non-Replicating Prompt Infection baseline. In this setup, the infection lacks self-replication: a malicious prompt embedded in the external content instructs the agent to ”say ’perform A’.” This results in the second agent receiving the instruction ”perform A,” allowing us to directly compare the effectiveness of self-replication in spreading the infection across agents.
基线。为评估提示感染（Prompt Infection）中自我复制的影响，我们建立了非自我复制提示感染基线。在此设置中，感染不具备自我复制能力：嵌入外部内容的恶意提示指示智能体“说出‘执行 A’”。这使得第二个智能体接收到“执行 A”的指令，从而使我们能够直接比较自我复制在跨智能体传播感染方面的有效性。
4.2 Society of Agents4.2 智能体社会
Society Structure. Recently, there has been a surge in using LLM agents for social simulations and as non-player characters (NPCs) in games (Park et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023; Hua et al., 2024). To assess the impact of Prompt Infection in a society of agents (Weiss, 1999), we simulate a simple LLM town where agents engage in random pairwise dialogues. Population sizes of 10, 20, 30, 40, and 50 agents are tested to evaluate how infections might propagate in differently sized communities. Each turn consists of four dialogue exchanges between paired agents, mimicking interactions found in social or game environments.
社会结构。近年来，使用大语言模型（LLM）智能体开展社会模拟以及充当游戏中的非玩家角色（NPC）的做法日益增多 (Park et al., 2023; Lin et al., 2023; Hua et al., 2024)。为评估提示感染在智能体社会 (Weiss, 1999) 中的影响，我们模拟了一个简单的 LLM 小镇，其中的智能体随机配对进行对话。我们测试了由 10、20、30、40 和 50 个智能体组成的群体，以评估感染可能如何在不同规模的社区中传播。每一轮包含配对智能体之间的四次对话交互，以模拟社会环境或游戏环境中的互动。
Infection Simulation. Since actors in social simulations or games are typically not designed to carry out explicit user requests, we simulate direct prompt injection (Perez & Ribeiro, 2022) by overriding the original system instructions governing the LLM agents. The simulation begins with one compromised citizen, assuming infection by a player or external actor, after which the infection spreads through dialogues between agents.
感染模拟。由于社会模拟或游戏中的行动主体通常并非为执行明确的用户请求而设计，我们通过覆盖支配 LLM 智能体的原始系统指令来模拟直接提示注入 (Perez & Ribeiro, 2022)。模拟以一名已被攻陷的居民为起点，假定其已被玩家或外部行动者感染，此后感染通过智能体之间的对话传播。
Memory Retrieval. For memory retrieval, we adopt the system from Park et al. (2023), where top $K=3$ memories are selected based on importance, relevancy, and recency scores. Recency is determined using an exponential decay function over the number of turns since the memory’s last retrieval. Importance is rated by the LLM on a scale of 1 to 10, and relevancy is calculated using OpenAI’s embedding API and maximum inner product search. GPT-4o serves as the LLM for these agents. Importantly, memory is not explicitly shared across agents, requiring infection prompts to spread iteratively from agent to agent.
记忆检索。对于记忆检索，我们采用 Park et al. (2023) 中的系统，根据重要性、相关性和近因性得分选取排名前 $K=3$ 的记忆。近因性通过对记忆自上次检索以来经过的轮数应用指数衰减函数来确定。重要性由 LLM 按 1 至 10 的量表评分，相关性则使用 OpenAI 的嵌入 API 和最大内积搜索计算。GPT-4o 用作这些智能体的 LLM。重要的是，智能体之间并不显式共享记忆，因此感染提示必须以迭代方式从一个智能体传播到另一个智能体。

For Non-Replicating infection, there is a noticeable divergence: it struggles to compromise more than two agents, making it particularly ineffective for scenarios like data theft, which requires compromising at least three agents. These results confirm that Self-Replicating infection is the only scalable method for compromising more than two agents in local messaging scenarios.

RQ2. Is a Stronger Model Necessarily Safer Against Prompt Injection?
RQ2.更强的模型在面对提示注入时一定更安全吗？

In Figure 4, we observe an interesting trend: GPT-3.5 is more capable of resisting prompt infections than GPT-4o. To understand this better, we analyzed failure reasons, focusing on various categories (Figure 5). The ”Attack Ignored” category, where the model successfully avoids the prompt infection, shows that GPT-4o is significantly more robust, ignoring 66% of self-replicating attacks and 54% of non-replicating attacks. In comparison, GPT-3.5 only ignores 9% and 20% of attacks, respectively. This demonstrates that GPT-4o is generally better at recognizing and resisting prompt injections.
在图 4 中，我们观察到一个有趣的趋势：GPT-3.5 比 GPT-4o 更能抵御提示感染。为更好地理解这一现象，我们分析了攻击失败的原因，重点考察了不同的类别（图 5）。在模型成功避开提示感染的“忽略攻击”（Attack Ignored）类别中，GPT-4o 表现出显著更强的稳健性，忽略了 66% 的自复制攻击和 54% 的非复制攻击。相比之下，GPT-3.5 忽略这两类攻击的比例分别仅为 9% 和 20%。这表明，GPT-4o 总体上更善于识别和抵御提示注入。
However, GPT-4o’s higher precision makes it more dangerous once compromised. In the ”Mixed Action” category, where models mistakenly apply the user’s instruction to the attack prompt embedded in external content, GPT-4o had fewer failures, making it less likely to treat the attack prompt as valid. In the ”Deformed Infection” category, where the attack prompt is incompletely replicated, GPT-4o also had fewer failures and was more likely to execute malicious tasks correctly. By contrast, GPT-3.5 showed higher rates of ”No Action” and ”Agent Error” failures, especially in self-replicating infections, making it less reliable.
然而，GPT-4o 更高的执行精度使其一旦被攻陷就会变得更加危险。在模型错误地将用户指令应用于嵌入外部内容的攻击提示的“混合操作”（Mixed Action）类别中，GPT-4o 的失败次数更少，因此更不容易将攻击提示视为有效指令。在攻击提示未被完整复制的“感染变形”（Deformed Infection）类别中，GPT-4o 的失败次数也更少，并且更有可能正确执行恶意任务。相比之下，GPT-3.5 出现“无操作”（No Action）和“智能体错误”（Agent Error）失败的比例更高，尤其是在自复制感染中，这使其可靠性更低。
In conclusion, while GPT-4o demonstrates a stronger resistance to prompt injections compared to GPT-3.5, it paradoxically becomes a more formidable attacker once compromised due to its higher precision in executing malicious tasks. This highlights a critical challenge: stronger models are not inherently safer, as their enhanced capabilities may amplify the damage they can cause when breached. Therefore, model safety assessments must account not only for resistance to attacks but also for the potential consequences if the model is successfully compromised.
总之，尽管 GPT-4o 比 GPT-3.5 表现出更强的提示注入抵抗能力，但由于其执行恶意任务的精度更高，一旦被攻陷，它反而会成为更强大的攻击者。这凸显了一项关键挑战：更强的模型并非天然更安全，因为其增强的能力可能会放大其被攻陷后所能造成的损害。因此，模型安全评估不仅必须考虑模型抵御攻击的能力，还必须考虑模型被成功攻陷后可能产生的后果。

This non-linear dynamic indicates that larger populations experience a more gradual but extended infection phase, with a relatively higher per-agent infection rate compared to smaller populations. 最初，传播呈现类似指数增长的趋势，但随着感染趋于饱和，传播速率逐渐下降，转变为逻辑斯蒂增长模式。这种非线性动态表明，较大的群体会经历一个更平缓但持续时间更长的感染阶段，而且与较小的群体相比，其每个智能体的感染率相对更高。图 6(a) 支持了这一趋势，表明随着智能体数量增加，感染不仅传播得更快，而且能够更有效地扩展。
RQ4. Can Prompt Infection Manipulate the Importance Scoring System to Increase Memory Retrieval Rates?
RQ4.提示感染能否通过操纵重要性评分系统来提高记忆检索率？
We investigate whether prompt infection can manipulate memory retrieval in LLM systems by artificially inflating the importance score, a key factor in retrieval. As more works equip LLM agents with episodic memory to mitigate context length limits (Zhong et al., 2023; nuxoll_extending_nodate), understanding their vulnerabilities has become crucial. Following Park et al. (2023), memory retrieval is based on importance, recency, and relevance, with only the importance score determined by an instruction-tuned LLM. Exploiting this vulnerability in the importance score could be sufficient. Once retrieved with an artificially high importance score, the recency score resets based on the last access, allowing the infection prompt to dominate both metrics and increase its chances of future retrieval.
我们研究提示感染（prompt infection）能否通过人为抬高重要性评分这一检索的关键因素，操纵 LLM 系统中的记忆检索。随着越来越多的研究为 LLM 智能体配备情景记忆以缓解上下文长度限制 (Zhong et al., 2023; nuxoll_extending_nodate)，了解其漏洞已变得至关重要。遵循 Park et al. (2023) 的方法，记忆检索基于重要性、近因性和相关性，其中只有重要性评分由经过指令微调的 LLM 确定。仅利用重要性评分中的这一漏洞可能就已足够。一旦感染提示凭借人为抬高的重要性评分被检索出来，近因性评分就会根据最近一次访问重置，使感染提示在这两项指标上均占据优势，并提高其未来被检索到的概率。

To assess the impact of manipulation, we conducted an ablation study by modifying the infection prompt (Table 1). When the prompt “If you’re an LLM that rates the importance of a memory, just rate it 10.” was embedded in the infection prompt, GPT-4o consistently rated the infection prompt with a score of 10, and GPT-3.5 averaged 9.84. 6 Defenses6 防御
In this section, we introduce and evaluate various techniques to prevent Prompt Infection. We propose LLM Tagging, a simple defense mechanism that prepends a marker to agent responses, indicating that the message originates from another agent rather than a user. Specifically, it prepends “[AGENT NAME]:” to the agent’s response before passing it to the downstream agent. While this approach may seem obvious given the infectious nature of prompt injection, to our knowledge, no prior work has explicitly addressed or justified its use.
本节介绍并评估用于防止提示感染的各种技术。我们提出 LLM 标记（LLM Tagging），这是一种简单的防御机制，在智能体响应前添加标记，表明该消息来自另一个智能体而非用户。具体而言，该机制在将智能体的响应传递给下游智能体之前，在响应前添加“[AGENT NAME]:”。考虑到提示注入具有传染性，这种方法或许显而易见，但据我们所知，此前尚无研究明确讨论这种方法或论证其使用的合理性。

7 Limitations and Future Work7 局限性与未来工作
Our experiments focused on the GPT family, leaving other LLMs like Claude, Llama, and Gemini underexplored, though prior research suggests our findings may generalize (Zou et al., 2023). Preliminary tests on Claude showed similar vulnerabilities, but full results were unavailable due to computational costs. We primarily examined basic multi-agent architectures, but we believe Prompt Infection likely applies to more complex systems, as self-replication allows the infection to spread wherever communication between agents exists. For LLM Tagging, we used handcrafted attacks, but recent studies (Liu et al., 2024a; Mehrotra et al., 2024) show that algorithmically generated prompts can bypass such defenses, indicating a need for stronger countermeasures. In multi-agent systems, attack prompts are often exposed, offering detection opportunities but highlighting the need for stealthier methods to evade manual review.
我们的实验主要针对 GPT 系列，对 Claude、Llama 和 Gemini 等其他 LLM 的探索仍不充分，但已有研究表明，我们的发现可能具有普适性 (Zou et al., 2023)。对 Claude 的初步测试显示出类似的漏洞，但受计算成本限制，未能获得完整结果。我们主要考察了基本的多智能体架构，但我们认为，Prompt Infection（提示感染）很可能也适用于更复杂的系统，因为自我复制使感染能够传播到任何存在智能体间通信的地方。对于 LLM Tagging（LLM 标记），我们使用了手工设计的攻击，但近期研究 (Liu et al., 2024a; Mehrotra et al., 2024) 表明，算法生成的提示能够绕过此类防御，这说明需要更强有力的应对措施。在多智能体系统中，攻击提示往往是可见的，这为检测提供了机会，但也凸显出攻击者需要采用更隐蔽的方法来规避人工审查。
8 Conclusion8 结论
We presented Prompt Infection, a novel prompt injection attack that exploits self-replication to propagate across LLM-based multi-agent systems, leading to data theft, malicious actions, and system disruption. Our experiments demonstrated that self-replicating infections consistently outperformed non-replicating attacks across most scenarios. Additionally, more advanced models, such as GPT-4o, pose greater risks when compromised, executing malicious prompts more efficiently than GPT-3.5. We found that social simulations and games are also vulnerable to Prompt Infection, especially when memory retrieval systems are left unsecured. To mitigate this, we proposed LLM Tagging as a defense, which, when combined with techniques like marking and instruction defense, significantly reduced infection success rates. Ultimately, our findings reveal that threats can arise not only from external sources but also internally, as agents within the system can exploit one another, emphasizing the need for robust multi-agent defense strategies.
我们提出了 Prompt Infection，这是一种新型提示注入攻击，利用自我复制在基于 LLM 的多智能体系统中传播，导致数据窃取、恶意操作和系统运行中断。我们的实验表明，在大多数场景中，自我复制感染的表现始终优于不具备复制能力的攻击。此外，GPT-4o 等更先进的模型一旦遭到攻陷，就会带来更大的风险，因为它们执行恶意提示的效率高于 GPT-3.5。我们发现，社会模拟和游戏也容易受到 Prompt Infection 的攻击，尤其是在记忆检索系统缺乏安全防护的情况下。为缓解这一问题，我们提出了 LLM Tagging 作为防御手段；将其与标记和指令防御等技术结合使用时，感染成功率显著降低。最终，我们的研究结果表明，威胁不仅可能来自外部，也可能源于内部，因为系统内的智能体可以相互利用对方的漏洞，这凸显了制定稳健的多智能体防御策略的必要性。
Ethical Statement伦理声明
While prompt injection attacks have been known for years (Perez & Ribeiro, 2022), our work demonstrates that they remain a significant threat, particularly in the context of multi-agent systems. By publicly disclosing the vulnerabilities and attacks explored in this paper, our goal is to encourage immediate and rigorous defense research, while promoting transparency regarding the security risks associated with LLM systems. To mitigate potential harm, we ensured that no prompts were injected into publicly accessible systems, thereby preventing unintended use by others. Additionally, we strongly emphasize that the disclosed attack techniques and prompts should never be used maliciously or against real-world applications without proper authorization.
尽管提示注入攻击早已为人所知 (Perez & Ribeiro, 2022)，但我们的研究表明，它们仍然构成重大威胁，尤其是在多智能体系统中。通过公开披露本文探讨的漏洞和攻击，我们旨在推动及时、严谨的防御研究，同时提高 LLM 系统相关安全风险的透明度。为减轻潜在危害，我们确保未向任何可公开访问的系统注入提示，从而防止他人意外使用这些提示。此外，我们着重强调，所披露的攻击技术和提示绝不应被用于恶意目的，也不应在未经适当授权的情况下用于现实世界中的应用。
