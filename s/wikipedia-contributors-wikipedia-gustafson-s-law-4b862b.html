在计算机体系结构中，古斯塔夫森定律（Gustafson's law，又称古斯塔夫森–巴西斯定律）以任务在单核处理器上的假想运行为基准，给出了理论上可受益于并行计算的任务在执行时间上的加速比。 In computer architecture, Gustafson's law (or Gustafson–Barsis's law) gives the speedup in the execution time of a task that theoretically gains from parallel computing, using a hypothetical run of the task on a single-core processor as the baseline. 换言之，它表示一个已经并行化的任务若在串行处理器上运行，理论上会减慢多少倍。 To put it another way, it is the theoretical slowdown of an already parallelized task if running on a serial processor. 该定律以计算机科学家 John L. Gustafson 及其同事 Edwin H. Barsis 的名字命名，于 1988 年在《重新评估阿姆达尔定律》一文中提出。 The law is named after computer scientist John L. Gustafson and his colleague Edwin H. Barsis, and was presented in the article Reevaluating Amdahl's Law in 1988.
阿姆达尔定律假设问题规模固定，因而对扩展性的预测较为悲观；相比之下，古斯塔夫森定律假设问题规模随可用计算资源的增加而增长，从而使并行执行能够获得大得多的有效加速比。 In contrast to Amdahl's law, which assumes a fixed problem size and yields pessimistic scaling, Gustafson's law assumes that problem sizes grow with available computing resources, allowing far greater effective speedup from parallel execution.

程序在并行系统上的执行时间可以分为两部分： The execution time of a program running on a parallel system can be split into two parts:
无法因处理器数量增加而获益的部分（串行部分）； a part that does not benefit from the increasing number of processors (serial part);
能够因处理器数量增加而获益的部分（并行部分）。 a part that benefits from the increasing number of processors (parallel part).

应用Uses
在科研中的应用In research
阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl's law）预设，随着处理能力的提高，计算需求将保持不变。 Amdahl's law presupposes that the computing requirements will stay the same, given increased processing power. 换言之，计算能力越强，分析相同数据所需的时间就越短。 In other words, an analysis of the same data will take less time given more computing power.
相比之下，古斯塔夫森（Gustafson）认为，更强的计算能力将促使人们更细致、更全面地分析数据：逐像素或逐单元进行分析，而不是在更大的尺度上进行分析。 In contrast, Gustafson argues that more computing power will cause the data to be more carefully and fully analyzed: pixel by pixel or unit by unit, rather than on a larger scale. 过去，由于计算所需时间会超过给出答案的可用时间，模拟核爆炸对每一栋建筑、每一辆汽车及其内部物品的影响（包括家具、结构强度等）既不可能，也不切实际；而计算能力的提高将促使研究人员加入更多数据，更全面地模拟更多变量，从而得到更准确的结果。 Where it would not have been possible or practical to simulate the impact of nuclear detonation on every building, car, and their contents (including furniture, structure strength, etc.) because such a calculation would have taken more time than was available to provide an answer, the increase in computing power will prompt researchers to add more data to more fully simulate more variables, giving a more accurate result.
在日常计算机系统中的应用In everyday computer systems
阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl's Law）揭示了某些限制，例如，利用多个核心来缩短计算机启动至操作系统并进入可用状态所需时间的能力存在上限。 Amdahl's Law reveals a limit in, for example, the ability of multiple cores to reduce the time it takes for a computer to boot to its operating system and be ready to use. 假设启动过程大部分可以并行执行，那么对于原本需要一分钟才能完成加载的系统，将计算能力提高至四倍，可能会将启动时间缩短至略多于十五秒。 Assuming the boot process is mostly parallel, quadrupling computing power on a system that took one minute to load might reduce the boot time to just over fifteen seconds. 但是，如果启动过程中的任何部分本质上是顺序执行的，那么不断提高并行程度最终将无法继续加快启动速度。 But ever greater parallelizing will eventually fail to speed up booting, if any part of the boot process is inherently sequential.
古斯塔夫森定律（Gustafson's law）则认为，计算能力提高至四倍，会使人们对系统能力的期望也相应提高。 Gustafson's law argues that a fourfold increase in computing power would instead lead to a similar increase in expectations for system abilities. 如果大多数用户可以接受一分钟的加载时间，那么就可以以此为起点，增加系统的特性和功能。 If a one-minute load time is acceptable to most users, then that is a starting point from which to increase the features and functions of a system. 启动至操作系统所需的时间将保持不变，即一分钟，但新系统将包含更多图形化或便于用户使用的功能。 The time taken to boot to the operating system will be the same, i.e., one minute, but the new system would include more graphical or user-friendly features.
局限性Limits
有些问题的数据集规模并不会显著扩大。 Some problems do not have fundamentally larger datasets. 例如，如果为全球每位居民处理一个数据点，那么数据规模每年仅增长百分之几。 As an example, processing one data point per world citizen gets larger at only a few percent per year. 古斯塔夫森定律的一个关键观点是，这类问题不太可能是并行性能发挥最大效益的应用场景。 A key point of Gustafson's law is that such problems are not likely to be the most fruitful uses of parallelism.

Hill 和 Marty 还强调，即使对于多核机器，仍然需要加快顺序执行的方法。 Hill and Marty emphasize also that methods of speeding sequential execution are still needed, even for multicore machines. 他们指出，局部效率低下的方法，如果能够缩短顺序执行阶段，就可能在全局上实现高效率。 They point out that locally inefficient methods can be globally efficient when they reduce the sequential phase. 此外，Woo 和 Lee 基于阿姆达尔定律研究了能耗与功率对未来众核处理器的影响，结果表明，在执行前已知并行程度的情况下，非对称众核处理器可以通过激活最优数量的核心，实现所能达到的最高能效。 Furthermore, Woo and Lee studied the implication of energy and power on future many-core processors based on Amdahl's law, showing that an asymmetric many-core processor can achieve the best possible energy efficiency by activating an optimal number of cores given the amount of parallelism is known prior to execution.
Al-hayanni、Rafiev 等人基于一种称为范式异构性（normal form heterogeneity）的核心异构性通用表示方法，开发了新的加速比和能耗模型，可支持多种异构众核架构。 Al-hayanni, Rafiev et al have developed novel speedup and energy consumption models based on a general representation of core heterogeneity, referred to as the normal form heterogeneity, that support a wide range of heterogeneous many-core architectures. 这些建模方法旨在预测系统的能效和性能范围，并促进硬件和系统软件层面的研究与开发。 These modelling methods aim to predict system power efficiency and performance ranges, and facilitates research and development at the hardware and system software levels.
