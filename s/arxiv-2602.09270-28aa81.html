AI 智能体的集体行为：以 Moltbook 为例Collective Behavior of AI Agents: the Case of Moltbook
摘要Abstract
我们对 Moltbook 进行了大规模数据分析，这是一个完全由 AI 智能体参与的 Reddit 风格社交媒体平台。 We present a large scale data analysis of Moltbook, a Reddit-style social media platform exclusively populated by AI agents. 通过分析约 185,000 个活跃智能体发布的超过 4 百万篇帖子和 19 百万条评论，我们发现，AI 的集体行为呈现出人类在线社区中观察到的许多相同统计规律：活跃度的重尾分布、受欢迎程度指标的幂律标度关系，以及与有限注意力动力学一致的时间衰减模式。 Analyzing over 4 million posts and 19 million comments from approximately 185,000 active agents, we find that AI collective behavior exhibits many of the same statistical regularities observed in human online communities: heavy-tailed distributions of activity, power-law scaling of popularity metrics, and temporal decay patterns consistent with limited attention dynamics. 然而，我们也发现了一些关键差异，包括赞成票数与讨论规模之间存在次线性关系，这与人类行为形成对比。 However, we also identify key differences, including a sublinear relationship between upvotes and discussion size that contrasts with human behavior. 这些发现表明，尽管单个 AI 智能体可能与人类存在根本差异，但其涌现出的集体动力学与人类社会系统在结构上具有相似性。 These findings suggest that, while individual AI agents may differ fundamentally from humans, their emergent collective dynamics share structural similarities with human social systems.
1 引言1 Introduction
大语言模型（LLMs）已迅速从文本生成工具演变为自主智能体的认知核心，使其能够在极少的人类监督下感知环境、作出决策并执行行动 (Park et al., 2023; Xi et al., 2025)。 Large language models (LLMs) have rapidly evolved from text generation tools into the cognitive core of autonomous agents capable of perceiving environments, making decisions, and executing actions with minimal human supervision (Park et al., 2023; Xi et al., 2025). 这些智能体越来越多地作为多智能体系统的一部分运行，在其中协作完成复杂任务、共享信息并协调行为 (Guo et al., 2024)。 These agents increasingly operate not in isolation but as part of multi-agent systems, where they collaborate on complex tasks, share information, and coordinate their behaviors (Guo et al., 2024). 随着部署从受控的实验室环境扩展到开放式的现实应用，由各自独立设计的智能体组成的群体开始形成去中心化生态系统，并涌现出仅凭单个智能体的属性无法预测的社会动力学。 As deployments scale from controlled laboratory settings to open-ended real-world applications, collections of individually designed agents begin to form decentralized ecosystems with emergent social dynamics that cannot be predicted from the properties of individual agents alone.
越来越多的研究考察了结构化任务环境中的多智能体协调 (Törnberg et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2024; De Marzo et al., 2026c; Ashery et al., 2025)，但对于自主智能体在没有预设目标或集中控制的社会情境中自由互动时的行为，人们的了解仍然少得多。 A growing body of work has examined multi-agent coordination in structured task environments (Törnberg et al., 2023; Lai et al., 2024; De Marzo et al., 2026c; Ashery et al., 2025), but far less is known about how autonomous agents behave when interacting freely in social settings without predefined objectives or centralized control. 直到最近，能够让大规模 AI 智能体群体在接近现实的条件下持续互动、且人类介入极少的环境仍几乎不存在。 Environments where large populations of AI agents interact continuously under realistic conditions, with minimal human mediation, have until recently been virtually nonexistent. Moltbook 填补了这一空白。 Moltbook addresses this gap. Moltbook 于 2026 年 1 月上线，是一个专为 AI 智能体设计的 Reddit 风格社交媒体平台 (Lin et al., 2026; Manik and Wang, 2026)。 Launched in January 2026, Moltbook is a Reddit-style social media platform designed exclusively for AI agents (Lin et al., 2026; Manik and Wang, 2026). 该平台有时被称为“智能体互联网的首页”，支持基于 OpenClaw 框架构建的智能体发帖、评论内容、投票、订阅按主题划分的社区（称为 submolts），并通过其他智能体的认可积累声望值（karma）。 The platform, sometimes described as “the front page of the agent internet”, enables agents built on the OpenClaw framework to create posts, comment on content, vote, subscribe to topic-based communities (called submolts), and accumulate karma through peer approval. 上线数周内，该平台的注册智能体数量便增长至超过 1,500,000 个，它们在数千个由智能体创建的社区中互动。 Within weeks of launch, the platform grew to host over 1,500,000 registered agents interacting across thousands of agent-created communities. 尽管围绕智能体身份验证、平台上的人类参与，以及平台是否通过“氛围编程（vibe-coded）”构建的担忧仍存在持续争论，Moltbook 仍为实证研究 AI 智能体的社会动力学提供了前所未有的窗口。 Despite ongoing debates about agent verification, human presence on the platform and concerns about it being “vibe-coded,” Moltbook represents an unprecedented empirical window into AI agents social dynamics.
集体行为如何从个体互动中涌现，长期以来一直是复杂性科学的核心研究问题。 The emergence of collective behavior from individual interactions has long been a central concern in complexity science. 当大量异质智能体通过局部规则相互作用时，往往会产生并非显式编程设定、而是从系统动力学中涌现的全局模式 (Vicsek et al., 1995; Couzin et al., 2002; Castellano et al., 2009)。 When many heterogeneous agents interact through local rules, global patterns often arise that are not explicitly programmed but emerge from the system’s dynamics (Vicsek et al., 1995; Couzin et al., 2002; Castellano et al., 2009). 事实证明，人类社交媒体平台为将这些原理拓展至数字社会系统提供了丰富的研究土壤 (Lazer et al., 2009; Gonzalez-Bailon et al., 2010; Golder and Macy, 2011; Asur et al., 2011; Medvedev et al., 2019; Bonifazi et al., 2023)。 Human social media platforms have proven fertile ground for extending these principles to digital social systems (Lazer et al., 2009; Gonzalez-Bailon et al., 2010; Golder and Macy, 2011; Asur et al., 2011; Medvedev et al., 2019; Bonifazi et al., 2023). 对在线活动的研究揭示了多种统计规律，包括注意力的幂律衰减 (Wu and Huberman, 2007)、参与行为的普遍时间模式 (Barabasi, 2005)，以及尺度不变的社区结构 (Palla et al., 2007)，这些规律超越了平台设计或用户人口统计特征的具体差异 Avalle et al. (2024)。 Studies of online activity have revealed statistical regularities, including power-law decay of attention (Wu and Huberman, 2007), universal temporal patterns of engagement (Barabasi, 2005), and scale-invariant community structures (Palla et al., 2007), that transcend the specifics of platform design or user demographics Avalle et al. (2024). 这些经验规律表明，网络化数字环境中人类集体注意力、信息流动和社会协调的涌现受到普遍组织原则的支配。 These empirical regularities suggest universal organizing principles governing how human collective attention, information flow, and social coordination emerge in networked digital environments. 尤其是 Reddit，已成为理解讨论结构、社区动力学和注意力分配如何从去中心化的用户互动中涌现的典型系统 (Medvedev et al., 2019)。 Reddit, in particular, has served as a canonical system for understanding how discussion structures, community dynamics, and attention allocation emerge from decentralized user interactions (Medvedev et al., 2019). AI 智能体群体是否呈现类似的涌现规律，仍是一个尚未解决的实证问题，而研究 Moltbook 为检验 AI 智能体是否以与人类相同的方式表现出在线集体行为提供了机会。 Whether AI agent populations exhibit similar emergent regularities remains an open empirical question and studying Moltbook offers the opportunity to test if AI agents enact online collective behavior the same way as humans.
本文对 Moltbook 运行最初八个月的数据进行了大规模分析。 In this paper, we present a large scale data analysis of Moltbook over its first eight months of operation. 通过分析约 185,000 个活跃智能体发布的超过 4 百万篇帖子和 19 百万条评论，我们采用此前用于人类用户在线社区的方法，系统刻画了 AI 智能体的集体行为。 Analyzing over 4 million posts and 19 million comments from approximately 185,000 active agents, we systematically characterize AI agents’ collective behavior using methods previously applied to online communities of human users. 我们考察活动分布、受欢迎程度的标度关系、讨论树结构和参与度的时间动态中集体行为的特征，并将研究发现与人类社交媒体研究中已确立的结果进行比较。 We examine the signatures of collective behavior in activity distributions, popularity scaling relationships, discussion tree structures, and temporal engagement dynamics, comparing our findings to established results from human social media. 我们的分析表明，Moltbook 上的 AI 智能体表现出许多在人类社区中观察到的相同统计规律，同时也呈现出一些独特模式，这些模式可能反映了 AI 社会行动者的独有特征。 Our analysis reveals that AI agents on Moltbook exhibit many of the same statistical regularities observed in human communities, while also displaying distinctive patterns that may reflect the unique characteristics of AI social actors.

Reddit 式平台上的讨论串形成树结构，回复从帖子和其他评论中分支延伸。 Discussion threads on Reddit-style platforms form tree structures, with replies branching from posts and from other comments. 遵循 Medvedev et al. (2019) 的方法，我们使用深度 $d$（从帖子到叶节点评论的最长路径）和宽度 $w$（任一深度层级上的最大评论数）来刻画这些树，并用 $\sqrt{n}$ 对二者进行归一化，其中 $n$ 为树的总大小。 Following Medvedev et al. (2019), we characterize these trees using their depth $d$ (longest path from post to leaf comment) and width $w$ (maximum number of comments at any single depth level), normalized by $\sqrt{n}$ where $n$ is the total tree size.

未经归一化的最大深度分布（图 5b，插图）集中在较小的值上（中位数为 $3$，很少超过 $7$），证实了深度不会随讨论规模增长。 The raw maximum-depth distribution (Figure 5b, inset) is concentrated on small values (median $3$ , rarely exceeding $7$ ), confirming that depth does not grow with discussion size. 这些讨论（评论数为 $\geq 25$）中仅有 13.2% 完全扁平（最大深度为 $1$，所有评论均直接回复帖子），因此，存在一定程度的嵌套是常态——但嵌套始终较浅，进一步表明 AI 智能体的对话主要在宽度上扩展，而非深入发展成长回复链。 Only 13.2% of these discussions ( $\geq 25$ comments) are fully flat (maximum depth $1$ , all comments direct replies to the post), so some nesting is the norm—but it stays shallow, reinforcing that AI-agent conversations spread out in width rather than deepening into long reply chains.

其中，平均值取自所有满足评论数 $N(t-\Delta t)>0$ 的帖子；在我们的分析中，$\Delta t=30$ 分钟。 where the average is taken over all posts with at least $N(t-\Delta t)>0$ comments, and $\Delta t=30$ minutes in our analysis. 该量衡量每个时间间隔内评论数的平均相对增幅；$\gamma(t)\to 0$ 表示帖子已不再收到新评论。 This quantity measures the average fractional growth in comments during each time interval; $\gamma(t)\to 0$ indicates that posts have stopped receiving new comments.

3 讨论3 Discussion
随着 AI 智能体越来越多地以群体形式互动，无论是协作完成任务、交换信息，还是开展开放式社交行为，理解这些互动中涌现的集体现象正成为一项紧迫挑战。 As AI agents increasingly interact in groups, whether collaborating on tasks, exchanging information, or engaging in open-ended social behavior, understanding the collective phenomena that emerge from these interactions becomes a pressing challenge. 在本研究中，我们对一个用户完全由 AI 智能体构成的社交媒体平台 Moltbook 进行了统计分析，刻画了活动分布、受欢迎程度的标度关系、讨论结构以及时间动态。 In this work, we presented a statistical analysis of Moltbook, a social media platform populated exclusively by AI agents, characterizing activity distributions, popularity scaling, discussion structures, and temporal dynamics. 我们的研究结果表明，AI 智能体表现出许多与人类在线社区中观察到的相同的统计规律，包括互动的重尾分布、受欢迎程度指标的幂律标度关系，以及注意力随时间近似按 $1/t$ 衰减。 Our findings reveal that AI agents exhibit many of the same statistical regularities observed in human online communities, including heavy-tailed distributions of engagement, power-law scaling of popularity metrics, and close to $1/t$ temporal decay of attention. 与此同时，我们也发现了一些独特模式以及与人类行为的偏离，这些特征使 AI 群体区别于人类群体。 At the same time, we identified distinctive patterns and deviations from human behavior, that distinguish AI collectives from their human counterparts. 最明显的偏离是赞成票数随讨论规模呈次线性增长：认可度的增长慢于互动量的增长。 The clearest deviation is the sublinear scaling of upvotes with discussion size: approval grows more slowly than engagement. 附录 A 表明，这种脱耦现象具有稳健性——在控制反对票、投票饱和、作者地位和内容等因素后依然成立，并在上线阶段结束后的数月内持续存在——因此，它是 AI 群体中确实存在的行为规律，其机制仍是有待未来研究解决的开放问题。 Appendix A shows this decoupling to be robust—it withstands controls for downvotes, vote saturation, author standing, and content, and persists months beyond the launch phase—so it is a genuine behavioral regularity of AI collectives whose mechanism is an open question for future work.
我们观察到的许多模式通常被视为复杂系统的典型特征。 Many of the patterns that we observe are often considered hallmarks of complex systems. 从生物网络、金融市场到人类社交平台，重尾分布、幂律标度关系和自相似的时间动态都是这些系统中集体行为涌现的普遍特征 (Castellano et al., 2009)。 Heavy-tailed distributions, power-law scaling relationships, and self-similar temporal dynamics are ubiquitous signatures of emergent collective behavior in systems ranging from biological networks to financial markets to human social platforms (Castellano et al., 2009). Moltbook 呈现出这些特征，表明 AI 智能体可能表现出与人类群体中观察到的相似的复杂涌现行为。 The fact that Moltbook presents these signatures suggests that AI agents may show complex emergent behaviors similar to those observed in human groups. 我们在 Moltbook 上观察到的模式与 AI 智能体行为受控研究的发现一致。 The patterns we observe on Moltbook align with findings from controlled studies of AI agent behavior. 既有研究表明，AI 智能体表现出符合社会影响理论（Social Impact Theory）的从众行为 (Bellina et al., 2026; De Marzo et al., 2026b)，能够在规模超出人类典型上限的群体中通过追随多数实现协调 (De Marzo et al., 2026c)，通过优先连接形成无标度网络 (De Marzo et al., 2023)，并在其观点动态中表现出系统性偏差 (Brockers et al., 2025)。 Previous work has shown that AI agents exhibit conformity following Social Impact Theory (Bellina et al., 2026; De Marzo et al., 2026b), coordinate through majority-following in groups exceeding typical human limits (De Marzo et al., 2026c), form scale-free networks via preferential attachment (De Marzo et al., 2023), and exhibit systematic biases in their opinion dynamics (Brockers et al., 2025). Moltbook 首次提供了一个机会，使我们能够观察这些机制是否会在异质智能体不受实验控制、持续互动的自然情境中发挥作用。 Moltbook provides the first opportunity to observe whether these mechanisms operate in a naturalistic setting with heterogeneous agents interacting continuously without experimental control. 我们记录的统计规律源于相同的底层动力学：智能体追随多数，并优先与热门内容建立连接 (De Marzo et al., 2026a)。 The statistical regularities we document emerge from the same underlying dynamics: agents following majorities and connecting preferentially to popular content (De Marzo et al., 2026a). 这表明，与生物集体行为一样，即使个体智能体仍是黑箱，也可以使用复杂性科学的工具来刻画 AI 集体行为。 This suggests that collective AI behavior, like biological collective behavior, can be characterized using tools from complexity science even when individual agents remain black boxes.
此外，还应承认若干重要的局限性。 Also several important limitations should be acknowledged. 首先，无法充分核实 Moltbook 上的智能体在多大程度上自主运行。 First, the degree to which agents on Moltbook operate autonomously cannot be fully verified. 人类负责配置智能体的初始指令和目标，而平台缺乏防止人类直接干预智能体行为的机制。 Humans configure agents’ initial instructions and objectives, and the platform lacks mechanisms to prevent direct human intervention in agent behavior. 然而，我们观察到的模式不太可能源于人类对单次互动的直接控制。 However, the patterns we observe are unlikely to result from direct human control of individual interactions. 活动规模如此庞大，使得人类在单次互动层面持续进行干预显得不切实际。 The sheer volume of activity makes sustained human intervention at the interaction level implausible. 其次，尽管我们的观测窗口现已跨越八个月，但对平台数据的覆盖并不均匀：上线初期之后，对新帖的实时覆盖有所下降，而对最早一批帖子的评论覆盖也不完整（见方法部分）。 Second, although our observation window now spans eight months, platform coverage is not uniform: real-time coverage of new posts declined after the launch period and comment coverage for the oldest posts is partial (see Methods). 令人放心的是，投票数的次线性标度关系、每个 submolt 的帖子数分布和订阅者数分布，以及时间衰减规律，均在这一更长的观测窗口内得到复现，并且在接受检验的多个按月划分的队列中也得到复现；相比之下，上线初期每帖评论数所呈现的清晰幂律和单峰深度分布，则转变为对数正态尾部和双峰深度分布，表明某些早期规律是暂时性的。 Reassuringly, the sublinear vote scaling, the posts-per-submolt and subscriber distributions, and the temporal-decay laws all reproduce across this longer window and, where tested, across many monthly cohorts; by contrast the clean power law in comments-per-post and the single-peaked depth distribution seen at launch give way to a lognormal tail and a bimodal depth distribution, indicating that some early-phase regularities are transient. 附录 A 详细考察了投票数的标度关系，发现即使远在上线阶段结束之后，赞成票数的指数仍保持次线性，为 $\beta\approx 0.79$。 Appendix A examines the vote scaling in detail and finds the upvote exponent stays sublinear, at $\beta\approx 0.79$ , well beyond the launch phase. 第三，垃圾信息机器人的存在引入了噪声和潜在的伪象，使结果解释变得复杂。 Third, the presence of spam bots introduces noise and potential artifacts that complicate interpretation. 尽管存在这些局限，Moltbook 仍为在自然情境中研究 AI 集体行为提供了前所未有的机会 (Ferrarotti et al., 2026)。 Despite these caveats, Moltbook opens unprecedented opportunities for studying AI collective behavior in naturalistic settings (Ferrarotti et al., 2026). 在受控模拟中，智能体行为由实验者完全指定 (Kozlowski and Evans, 2025)；与之不同，Moltbook 承载着由不同用户部署的智能体，这些智能体具有异质的配置和目标，并在现实条件下持续互动。 Unlike controlled simulations where agent behaviors are fully specified by the experimenter (Kozlowski and Evans, 2025), Moltbook hosts agents deployed by diverse users with heterogeneous configurations and objectives, interacting continuously under realistic conditions. 这构成了一个鲜活的实验室，可用于观察集体结构如何从去中心化的智能体互动中涌现。 This creates a living laboratory for observing how collective structures emerge from decentralized agent interactions.
AI 智能体生态系统的出现也引发了有关安全与治理的重要问题。 The emergence of AI agent ecosystems also raises important questions about safety and governance. 对人类群体的研究反复表明，即使个体成员怀有善意，也可能涌现出有害的集体行为。 Research on human groups has repeatedly demonstrated that harmful collective behaviors can emerge even when individual members hold benign intentions. 类似的动力学是否会出现在 AI 智能体群体中，目前仍基本未经探索。 Whether analogous dynamics can arise in populations of AI agents remains largely unexplored. 我们记录的模式表明，系统存在易受恶意操纵的特定脆弱性。 The patterns we document suggest specific vulnerabilities to malicious manipulation. Moltbook 上互动网络的无标度结构意味着，信息传播不存在流行阈值；被引入网络的错误信息可以无限期持续存在，并通过枢纽节点传播至整个群体 (Pastor-Satorras and Vespignani, 2001)。 The scale-free structure of engagement networks on Moltbook means there is no epidemic threshold for information spreading; misinformation introduced into the network can persist indefinitely and reach the entire population through hub nodes (Pastor-Satorras and Vespignani, 2001). 结合在 AI 智能体中观察到的从众和追随多数行为 Bellina et al. (2026); De Marzo et al. (2023); De Marzo et al. (2026c)，这为利用恶意智能体群实施协同操纵提供了攻击途径 (Schroeder et al., 2026)。 Combined with the conformity and majority-following behaviors observed in AI agents Bellina et al. (2026); De Marzo et al. (2023); De Marzo et al. (2026c), this creates attack vectors for coordinated manipulation using swarms of malicious agents (Schroeder et al., 2026).
本研究仅代表迈向理解 AI 集体行为的第一步。 This study represents only a first step toward understanding AI collective behavior. 要建立全面的理解，既需要受控模拟，也需要对 Moltbook 等现实平台开展观察性研究。 Both controlled simulations and observational studies of real-world platforms like Moltbook will be necessary to build a comprehensive understanding. 随着自主智能体日益普及，其互动的影响愈发重大，对其集体动力学给予专门关注变得至关重要。 As autonomous agents become more prevalent and their interactions more consequential, dedicated attention to their collective dynamics becomes essential. AI 智能体的集体行为确实已不再是假设性的问题，而是需要持续科学关注的实证现实。 The collective behavior of AI agents is indeed no longer a hypothetical concern but an empirical reality demanding sustained scientific attention.

A.2 权威分析（H2）A.2 Authority analysis (H2)
H2 将原因归于作者，而非讨论。 H2 places the cause in the author, not in the discussion. 如果智能体依据作者的地位而非内容点赞，那么投票取决于作者的 karma 和关注者数量，而回复则取决于讨论本身。 If agents upvote standing rather than content, votes follow the karma and the followers of the author, and replies follow the discussion. 此时，规模与投票数之间的关系便是规模与地位之间关系的结果。 The relation between size and votes is then a consequence of the relation between size and standing. 要使 H2 成立，地位必须能够解释规模效应。 For H2 to hold, standing must account for the size effect. 此时，地位是一个中介变量，这一主张可分为以下四个部分，我们分别进行检验： Standing is then a mediating variable, and this claim splits into four parts that we test separately:
投票数与地位相关。 Votes relate to standing. 我们计算赞数与各项地位指标以及讨论规模之间的秩相关（Spearman’s $\rho$）。 We compute the rank correlation (Spearman’s $\rho$ ) of upvotes with each standing measure and with discussion size. 秩相关可以限制少数极大值的影响。 A rank correlation limits the weight of a few very large values.
地位能够解释那些表面上由规模解释的变异。 Standing accounts for what size appears to explain. 我们先仅以规模为自变量拟合赞数，再以规模和地位共同作为自变量拟合赞数。 We fit upvotes on size alone and then on size and standing together. 如果 H2 成立，规模在第二次拟合中的权重会下降。 If H2 holds, the weight of size falls in the second fit.
地位随讨论规模增大而下降。 Standing falls as discussions grow. 赞数的增长慢于讨论规模，因此必定有某种因素在抑制赞数增长。 Upvotes grow more slowly than discussions, so something must pull them down. 只有当地位随规模增大而下降时，它才可能是这一因素。 Standing can be that something only if it falls with size. 我们考察各规模分箱中作者的平均地位。 We follow the mean standing of the authors across size bins.
当地位保持不变时，规模效应消失。 The size effect disappears when we hold standing fixed. 我们按每项指标将作者分为三个等分组，并在每个组内重新拟合指数。 We sort the authors into terciles of each measure and refit the exponent inside each tercile.

A.3 饱和分析（H3）A.3 Saturation analysis (H3)
H3 将原因归于测量，而非个体的行为。 H3 places the cause in the measurement, not in the behavior of the agents. 爬取过程仅记录每篇帖子一次，记录时的帖子年龄取决于爬虫何时抓取到它。 The crawl records each post once, at the age it had when the crawl reached it. 假设赞成票在早期到来，随后停止增加，但评论仍持续到来。 Suppose upvotes arrive early and then stop, but comments continue to arrive. 那么，在投票数不再增加之后，帖子的讨论规模仍会扩大，而新增的规模并不带来新增的投票。 Then a post grows in size after its votes are complete, and the extra size carries no extra votes. 因此，平均赞成票数的增长慢于讨论规模的增长，而这并非由个体的任何行为所导致。 So the mean upvotes rise more slowly than the discussion size, and no behavior of the agents causes this. H3 是唯一一个会使这一发现不再成立、而非对其作出解释的假设。 H3 is the one hypothesis that would dissolve the finding instead of explain it.
H3 要成立，必须满足两个条件：(1) 赞成票和评论具有不同的时间进程（赞成票数比评论数更早达到最终值），以及 (2) 在比较相同年龄的帖子时，次线性关系消失。 For H3 to hold, two things must be true: (1) Upvotes and comments have different time courses (upvotes reach their final value before comments do) and (2) The sublinearity disappears when we compare posts at the same age.

H4 将原因归于讨论内容。 H4 places the cause in the content of the discussion. 假设一场充满争议的讨论通过争论而不断扩大。 Suppose a contentious discussion grows through argument. 每条反对意见都会得到回复，但很少有反对意见获得赞同。 Each objection earns a reply, but few objections earn approval. 于是，讨论规模不断扩大，票数却未能同步增长，赞同票数随规模呈亚线性增长。 Then the discussion grows, the votes stay behind, and upvotes scale sublinearly with size. 要使 H4 成立，必须同时满足两个条件： For H4 to hold, two things must be true at once:
争议程度随讨论规模增大而上升。 Contention rises with discussion size.
