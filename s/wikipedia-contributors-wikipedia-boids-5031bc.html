Boids 是一种人工生命算法，由 Craig Reynolds 于 1986 年开发，用于模拟鸟类的集群行为及相关的群体运动。 Boids is an artificial life algorithm, developed by Craig Reynolds in 1986, which simulates the flocking behaviour of birds, and related group motion. 他关于这一主题的论文于 1987 年发表在 ACM SIGGRAPH 会议论文集中。 His paper on this topic was published in 1987 in the proceedings of the ACM SIGGRAPH conference. “boid”这一名称是“bird-oid object”的缩写，意指类似鸟的对象，同时也呼应了人们印象中纽约口音对“bird”一词的发音。 The name "boid" corresponds to a shortened version of "bird-oid object", which refers to a bird-like object, as well as referencing the stereotypical New York pronunciation of 'bird' as .
Reynolds 的 boid 模型是一个更广泛的一般概念的实例，此后，人们围绕这一概念开发了许多其他变体。 Reynolds' boid model is one example of a larger general concept, for which many other variations have been developed since. Ichiro Aoki 与之密切相关的研究值得关注，因为该研究发表于 1982 年，比 Reynolds 的 boids 论文早了五年。 The closely related work of Ichiro Aoki is noteworthy because it was published in 1982 – five years before Reynolds' boids paper.
模型细节Model details
与大多数人工生命模拟一样，Boids 是涌现行为（emergent behavior）的一个实例；也就是说，Boids 的复杂性源于遵循一组简单规则的个体智能体（在此为 boids）之间的相互作用。 Like most artificial life simulations, Boids is an example of emergent behavior; that is, the complexity of Boids arises from the interaction of individual agents (the boids, in this case) adhering to a set of simple rules. 最简单的 Boids 世界采用如下规则： The rules applied in the simplest Boids world are as follows:
分离：调整运动方向，以避免与邻近的群体成员过度拥挤 separation: steer to avoid crowding local flockmates
对齐：调整运动方向，使其趋向邻近群体成员的平均航向 alignment: steer towards the average heading of local flockmates
内聚：调整运动方向，向邻近群体成员的平均位置（质心）移动 cohesion: steer to move towards the average position (center of mass) of local flockmates
还可以添加更复杂的规则，例如避障和目标追寻。 More complex rules can be added, such as obstacle avoidance and goal seeking.
自 Reynolds 提出基本模型以来，该模型已在多个不同方面得到扩展。 The basic model has been extended in several different ways since Reynolds proposed it. 例如，Delgado-Mata 等人扩展了基本模型，将恐惧的影响纳入其中。 For instance, Delgado-Mata et al. extended the basic model to incorporate the effects of fear. 动物之间通过嗅觉传递情绪，其媒介是信息素，信息素被建模为自由膨胀气体中的粒子。 Olfaction was used to transmit emotion between animals, through pheromones modelled as particles in a free expansion gas. Chistopher Hartman 和 Bedrich Benes 在对齐作用的基础上引入了一种补充力，并将其称为领导者更替。 Chistopher Hartman and Bedrich Benes introduced a complementary force to the alignment that they call the change of leadership. 这种转向作用决定了 boid 成为领导者并尝试逃离的概率。 This steer defines the chance of the boid to become a leader and try to escape.
Boids 的运动既可以表现为混沌状态（群体分裂和无序行为），也可以表现为有序状态。 The movement of Boids can be characterized as either chaotic (splitting groups and wild behaviour) or orderly. 诸如鸟群分裂以及避开障碍物后重新聚合等意料之外的行为，可以被视为涌现行为。 Unexpected behaviours, such as splitting flocks and reuniting after avoiding obstacles, can be considered emergent.
在群体机器人学中，Boids 模型可用于对由简单无人地面车辆（UGV）或微型飞行器（MAV）组成的团队进行直接控制和稳定化。 The Boids model can be used for direct control and stabilization of teams of simple unmanned ground vehicles (UGV) or micro aerial vehicles (MAV) in swarm robotics. 为了使异构 UAV-UGV 团队保持稳定，Saska 等人对该模型进行了调整，使其能够使用机载相对定位。 For stabilization of heterogeneous UAV-UGV teams, the model was adapted for using onboard relative localization by Saska et al.
在提出之时，与电影计算机动画中采用的传统技术相比，Reynolds 的方法代表了一次巨大进步。 At the time of proposal, Reynolds' approach represented a giant step forward compared to the traditional techniques used in computer animation for motion pictures. 使用该模型制作的第一部动画是《Stanley and Stella in: Breaking the Ice》（1987），随后，该模型首次应用于长篇电影，即 Tim Burton 的《Batman Returns》（1992），用于生成蝙蝠群以及穿行于 Gotham City 街道的企鹅大军。 The first animation created with the model was Stanley and Stella in: Breaking the Ice (1987), followed by a feature film debut in Tim Burton's film Batman Returns (1992) with computer-generated bat swarms and armies of penguins marching through the streets of Gotham City.
boids 模型还被用于其他一些有趣的应用。 The boids model has been used for other interesting applications. 它已被用于为互联网多频道广播电台自动编排节目。 It has been applied to automatically program Internet multi-channel radio stations. 它还被用于信息可视化和优化任务。 It has also been used for visualizing information and for optimization tasks.
生物学家 Richard Dawkins 在其 2009 年出版的《地球上最伟大的表演：进化的证据》中提到了 Boids 模型。 Biologist Richard Dawkins references the Boids model in his 2009 book The Greatest Show on Earth: The Evidence for Evolution.
