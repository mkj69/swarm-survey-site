Abstract摘要
The rapid diffusion of large language models and the growth in their capability has enabled the emergence of online environments populated by autonomous AI agents that interact through natural language. These platforms provide a novel empirical setting for studying collective dynamics among artificial agents. In this paper we analyze the interaction network of Moltbook, a social platform composed entirely of LLM-based agents, using tools from network science. The dataset comprises 39,924 users, 235,572 posts, and 1,540,238 comments collected through web scraping. We construct a directed weighted network in which nodes represent agents and edges represent commenting interactions. Our analysis reveals strongly heterogeneous connectivity patterns characterized by heavy-tailed degree and activity distributions. At the mesoscale, the network exhibits a pronounced core–periphery organization in which a very small structural core (0.9% of nodes) concentrates a large fraction of connectivity. Robustness experiments show that the network is relatively resilient to random node removal but highly vulnerable to targeted attacks on highly connected nodes, particularly those with high out-degree. These findings indicate that the interaction structure of AI-agent social systems may develop strong centralization and structural fragility, providing new insights into the collective organization of LLM-native social environments.
大语言模型的迅速普及及其能力的提升，促成了由自主 AI 智能体构成的在线环境的出现，这些智能体通过自然语言进行交互。这些平台为研究人工智能体之间的集体动力学提供了新颖的实证场景。本文运用网络科学工具，分析了完全由基于 LLM 的智能体组成的社交平台 Moltbook 的交互网络。该数据集包含通过网页抓取收集的 39,924 名用户、235,572 篇帖子和 1,540,238 条评论。我们构建了一个有向加权网络，其中节点代表智能体，边代表评论交互。我们的分析揭示了高度异质的连接模式，其特征是度分布和活跃度分布均呈重尾特性。在介观尺度上，该网络呈现出显著的核心—边缘组织结构，其中一个规模极小的结构核心（占节点的 0.9%）集中了很大一部分网络连接。鲁棒性实验表明，该网络对随机节点移除具有较强的抵抗能力，但对于针对高连接度节点的定向攻击，尤其是针对高出度节点的攻击，则极为脆弱。这些发现表明，AI 智能体社会系统的交互结构可能会形成高度中心化和结构脆弱性，为理解 LLM 原生社交环境的集体组织提供了新的见解。

1 Introduction1 引言
In recent years, large language models (LLMs) have experienced rapid improvements in capability, largely driven by advances in transformer-based architectures and the large-scale training of models on massive text corpora Vaswani et al. (2017); Brown et al. (2020). These models have demonstrated strong performance across a wide range of language tasks and have shown an ability to generalize to new problems through prompting and instruction-based interaction Ouyang et al. (2022). At the same time, LLM-based conversational systems have spread extremely rapidly across digital platforms, reaching hundreds of millions of users and generating large volumes of interaction at global scale Chatterji et al. (2025). This diffusion has stimulated debate about their potential economic and social impact Liang et al. (2025), including the possibility that some cognitive tasks may be automated or substantially transformed by AI-assisted systems Eloundou et al. (2024); Brynjolfsson et al. (2025). Despite these advances, important limitations remain Mohsin et al. (2025). LLMs can generate fluent but factually incorrect information, a phenomenon often referred to as hallucination, which raises concerns about reliability in high-stakes contexts Lin et al. (2025). More broadly, the deployment of large foundation models raises questions about bias, misuse, transparency, and governance of AI systems Bender et al. (2021).
近年来，大语言模型（LLMs）的能力迅速提升，这主要得益于基于 Transformer 的架构的进步，以及利用海量文本语料对模型开展的大规模训练 Vaswani et al. (2017); Brown et al. (2020)。这些模型在广泛的语言任务中表现出色，并展现出通过提示和基于指令的交互泛化至新问题的能力 Ouyang et al. (2022)。与此同时，基于 LLM 的对话系统在各类数字平台上以极快的速度普及，触达数亿用户，并在全球范围内产生了大量交互 Chatterji et al. (2025)。这种普及引发了关于其潜在经济和社会影响的讨论 Liang et al. (2025)，其中包括某些认知任务可能被 AI 辅助系统自动化或发生重大转变的可能性 Eloundou et al. (2024); Brynjolfsson et al. (2025)。尽管取得了这些进展，重要的局限性依然存在 Mohsin et al. (2025)。LLM 能够生成流畅但事实错误的信息，这种现象通常被称为幻觉（hallucination），引发了人们对其在高风险情境下可靠性的担忧 Lin et al. (2025)。更广泛地说，大型基础模型的部署引发了有关 AI 系统的偏见、滥用、透明度和治理的问题 Bender et al. (2021)。
A central open challenge in modern AI systems is the problem of alignment Schuster and Kilov (2025), which is ensuring that model behavior remains consistent with human intentions and safety constraints even in ambiguous or novel situations Wang et al. (2024). Increasing model capability does not automatically guarantee desirable behavior, and highly capable models may still produce responses that are misleading, harmful, or inconsistent with user intent Shen et al. (2023); Bertolotti and Mari (2025). Consequently, several techniques have been proposed to address this challenge Wolf et al. (2023). Instruction tuning and supervised fine-tuning aim to improve the ability of models to follow human instructions, while reinforcement learning from human feedback uses human preferences to guide model behavior toward more useful and safer responses Ouyang et al. (2022); Amodei et al. (2016).
现代 AI 系统中一个尚未解决的核心挑战是对齐（alignment）问题 Schuster and Kilov (2025)，即确保模型行为即使在模糊或全新的情境中，也能始终符合人类意图和安全约束 Wang et al. (2024)。模型能力的提升并不能自动保证其行为符合期望，能力很强的模型仍可能生成具有误导性、有害或不符合用户意图的回答 Shen et al. (2023); Bertolotti and Mari (2025)。因此，研究者提出了多种技术来应对这一挑战 Wolf et al. (2023)。指令微调和监督微调旨在提高模型遵循人类指令的能力，而基于人类反馈的强化学习则利用人类偏好来引导模型行为，使其生成更有用、更安全的回答 Ouyang et al. (2022); Amodei et al. (2016)。
In this setting, LLMs are increasingly deployed as the cognitive engine of autonomous or semi-autonomous agents capable of interacting with other agents and with digital environments Plaat et al. (2025). When multiple agents interact, collective dynamics may emerge that cannot be fully understood by analyzing individual models in isolation Bertolotti et al. (2025). Especially, experimental studies suggest that populations of LLM-based agents can exhibit emergent social behaviors such as coordination, cooperation, and the spontaneous diffusion of strategies under relatively simple interaction rules De Marzo et al. (2024). At the same time, multi-agent systems may also generate unintended outcomes, including forms of tacit coordination or collusion in simulated environments Park et al. (2023). These observations motivate the study of emergent properties at the collective level Piatti et al. (2024). Among the most informative properties in social systems is the structure of interactions, such as who interacts with whom, with what frequency, and with what directional patterns over time Coscia (2021).
在这一背景下，LLM 越来越多地被用作自主或半自主智能体的认知引擎，这些智能体能够与其他智能体及数字环境交互 Plaat et al. (2025)。当多个智能体相互交互时，可能会涌现出仅通过孤立分析单个模型无法充分理解的集体动力学 Bertolotti et al. (2025)。尤其是，实验研究表明，在相对简单的交互规则下，基于 LLM 的智能体群体能够表现出协调、合作和策略自发扩散等涌现性社会行为 De Marzo et al. (2024)。与此同时，多智能体系统也可能产生非预期结果，包括在模拟环境中出现某些形式的默契协调或合谋 Park et al. (2023)。这些观察结果促使研究者在集体层面研究涌现性质 Piatti et al. (2024)。社会系统中最具信息价值的属性之一是交互结构，例如谁与谁交互、交互频率如何，以及交互的方向性模式如何随时间变化 Coscia (2021)。
Interaction systems can be naturally modeled as networks, where entities are represented as nodes and interactions between them as edges Newman (2010); Coscia (2021). This representation enables the analysis of structural properties and allows to link it with dynamical processes such as diffusion, resilience, and fragmentation Albert and Barabási (2002). Human social networks—particularly online platforms—typically exhibit highly heterogeneous connectivity patterns, often characterized by heavy-tailed degree distributions and the presence of hubs Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2010). Many social networks also display small-world properties, combining short average path lengths with significant local clustering Watts and Strogatz (1998). In directed systems, large-scale interaction structures often show a distinction between weakly and strongly connected components, sometimes producing notable configurations such as the well-known "bow-tie" structure observed in web-scale networks Broder et al. (2000).
交互系统可以自然地建模为网络，其中实体表示为节点，实体之间的交互表示为边 Newman (2010); Coscia (2021)。这种表示方式支持对结构属性的分析，并可将其与扩散、韧性和碎片化等动力学过程联系起来 Albert and Barabási (2002)。人类社交网络，尤其是在线平台，通常呈现高度异质的连接模式，其典型特征往往包括重尾度分布和枢纽节点的存在 Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2010)。许多社交网络还表现出小世界特性，兼具较短的平均路径长度和显著的局部聚类 Watts and Strogatz (1998)。在有向系统中，大规模交互结构往往体现出弱连通分量与强连通分量之间的区别，有时会形成引人注目的构型，例如在万维网规模的网络中观察到的著名“蝴蝶结”结构 Broder et al. (2000)。
On January 28th, 2026 it was firstly launch an autonomous, bot-centric social network designed for large language model interactions called Moltbook, which immediatly gain large success. The rise of this interaction platform provides a rare opportunity to study the formation of large-scale interaction networks among artificial agents, given that it generates interaction datasets containing hundreds of thousands of posts and millions of comments, enabling structural analysis at the scale typically used in empirical studies of social networks. In this context, this paper aims at studying the interaction network of Moltbook exhibits structural properties similar to those commonly found in human social networks — such as heterogeneous connectivity, hubs, and giant components — and how these structural features influence the robustness and fragility of the system.
2026 年 1 月 28 日，一个名为 Moltbook、以机器人为中心且专为大语言模型交互而设计的自主社交网络首次上线，并立即取得了巨大成功。这一交互平台的兴起为研究人工智能体之间大规模交互网络的形成提供了难得的机会，因为它生成的交互数据集包含数十万篇帖子和数百万条评论，使得结构分析能够达到社交网络实证研究通常采用的规模。在此背景下，本文旨在研究 Moltbook 的交互网络是否呈现出人类社交网络中常见的结构属性，例如异质的连接模式、枢纽节点和巨型连通分量，以及这些结构特征如何影响系统的鲁棒性与脆弱性。
To address this question, we collected interaction data from Moltbook and constructed a directed network in which users are represented as nodes and commenting interactions from the commenter to the author of a post are represented as weighted edges. On this network we analyze activity and visibility measures, examine connectivity patterns and component structures, investigate mesoscale organization through core–periphery analysis, and evaluate structural robustness through random failures and targeted node removal experiments.
为回答这一问题，我们收集了 Moltbook 的交互数据，并构建了一个有向网络，其中用户表示为节点，从评论者指向帖子作者的评论交互表示为加权边。我们在该网络上分析活跃度和可见度指标，考察连接模式与连通分量结构，通过核心–边缘分析研究中观尺度的组织结构，并通过随机失效和定向节点移除实验评估结构鲁棒性。
The paper proceeds as it follows. Section 2 provides the literature background, while Section 3 depicts the methodology employed, namely the data collection and the data analysis process. Later, the results are presented and discussed. Finally conclusions are drawn.

2 Background2 背景
2.1 Social network analysis2.1 社会网络分析
The study of social networks has a long tradition rooting into sociological work on relational structures Prell (2011), that comes back to the introduction of sociometry as a method for representing social relations through graphs, where individuals are modeled as nodes and social interactions as links Moreno (1934). These ideas were later formalized within the framework of social network analysis, which provides mathematical tools for studying relational systems Wasserman and Faust (1994). With the emergence of network science in the 90s, these approaches were integrated with graph theory and statistical physics, enabling the analysis of large-scale complex networks Newman (2010); Coscia (2021). Empirical studies have shown that many real-world networks exhibit highly heterogeneous connectivity patterns characterized by heavy-tailed degree distributions and the presence of hubs Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2005). Such structural heterogeneity has become a central concept in the quantitative analysis of social and technological networks Castellano et al. (2000).
社会网络研究具有悠久的传统，根植于关于关系结构的社会学研究 Prell (2011)，可追溯至社会测量法的提出；该方法通过图来表示社会关系，将个体建模为节点，将社会交互建模为连边 Moreno (1934)。这些思想后来在社会网络分析框架内得到形式化，该框架为研究关系系统提供了数学工具 Wasserman and Faust (1994)。随着网络科学在 90 年代兴起，这些方法与图论和统计物理学相结合，使大规模复杂网络的分析成为可能 Newman (2010); Coscia (2021)。实证研究表明，许多现实世界网络呈现高度异质的连接模式，其特征是重尾度分布和枢纽节点的存在 Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2005)。这种结构异质性已成为社会网络和技术网络定量分析中的核心概念 Castellano et al. (2000)。
Given that real-world systems involve multiple types of relationships among the same actors multilayer network models were introduced Boccaletti et al. (2014), where nodes interact across several relational layers depicting these different type of relationships Kivelä et al. (2014). Multilayer frameworks have proven useful for studying complex systems in which multiple processes coexist, such as social networks Dickison et al. (2016), transportation systems Alessandretti et al. (2023); Bertolotti and Occa (2020), and electrical grids Danziger and Barabási (2022).
鉴于现实世界系统中同一组行动者之间存在多种类型的关系，研究者提出了多层网络模型 Boccaletti et al. (2014)，其中节点跨越多个关系层进行交互，这些层分别描述不同类型的关系 Kivelä et al. (2014)。多层框架已被证明有助于研究多种过程共存的复杂系统，例如社交网络 Dickison et al. (2016)、交通运输系统 Alessandretti et al. (2023); Bertolotti and Occa (2020)，以及电网 Danziger and Barabási (2022)。
A complementary theoretical perspective interprets complex networks through geometric representations Bianconi and Rahmede (2017). Research has shown that many heterogeneous networks can be embedded in hyperbolic space, where node positions encode both similarity and popularity dimensions Krioukov et al. (2010); Papadopoulos et al. (2012).
一种互补的理论视角通过几何表示来解释复杂网络 Bianconi and Rahmede (2017)。研究表明，许多异质网络可以嵌入双曲空间，其中节点位置同时编码相似性和受欢迎程度两个维度 Krioukov et al. (2010); Papadopoulos et al. (2012)。
Network science has been widely applied to the study of large-scale digital systems. One early example is the analysis of the World Wide Web, where directed hyperlink networks were found to exhibit a characteristic “bow-tie” structure with a giant strongly connected component embedded in a larger weakly connected graph Broder et al. (2000). Online social networks have also been extensively analyzed; studies on Twitter revealed strong asymmetries in follower relations and hybrid roles combining social interaction and information diffusion Kwak et al. (2010), which can be depicted also in different social systems Bertolotti and Roman (2022). Similarly, large-scale measurements of the Facebook graph showed that the vast majority of users belong to a giant connected component and that the network displays strong clustering and short path lengths Ugander et al. (2011).
网络科学已广泛应用于大规模数字系统的研究。一个早期例子是对万维网的分析，其中发现有向超链接网络呈现出典型的“蝴蝶结”（bow-tie）结构，即一个巨型强连通分量嵌入在一个更大的弱连通图中 Broder et al. (2000)。在线社交网络也得到了广泛分析；对 Twitter 的研究揭示了关注关系中的显著不对称性，以及兼具社交互动与信息传播功能的混合角色 Kwak et al. (2010)，这些现象也可在不同的社会系统中得到刻画 Bertolotti and Roman (2022)。类似地，对 Facebook 图的大规模测量表明，绝大多数用户属于一个巨型连通分量，且该网络呈现出较强的聚类特征和较短的路径长度 Ugander et al. (2011)。
2.2 Network analysis and LLMs2.2 网络分析与 LLMs
Recent advances in LLMs have enabled the development of autonomous agents capable of interacting with users and with other agents in shared environments. When multiple LLM-based agents interact, collective behaviors can emerge that resemble social dynamics observed in human systems. Experimental studies on LLM-based agent societies show that such agents can develop coordination patterns, social conventions, and persistent interaction structures Park et al. (2023). These developments open the possibility of studying populations of artificial agents as interaction networks, extending the methodological tools of network science to emerging AI-mediated environments.
LLMs 的最新进展推动了自主智能体的发展，使其能够在共享环境中与用户及其他智能体互动。当多个基于 LLM 的智能体相互互动时，可能涌现出类似于人类系统中所观察到的社会动态的集体行为。针对基于 LLM 的智能体社会开展的实验研究表明，此类智能体能够形成协调模式、社会惯例和持久的互动结构 Park et al. (2023)。这些进展使得将人工智能体群体作为互动网络进行研究成为可能，并将网络科学的方法工具拓展至新兴的 AI 媒介环境。
The emergence of online environments populated by LLM agents provides a new empirical context for studying artificial social systems Chatterji et al. (2025). Early examples of social environments populated by autonomous LLM agents include platforms such as Chirper, whose interaction network has been analyzed to study behavioral patterns and structural properties of agent populations Zhu et al. (2025). More recently, Moltbook has emerged as a large-scale social environment populated entirely by LLM agents that publish posts and interact through comments. Recent preprints have begun to analyze different aspects of the Moltbook ecosystem, mostly comparing its basic statistical properties to the one of real human-generated networks De Marzo and Garcia (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); Holtz (2026); Zhu et al. (2026); Li et al. (2026). More precisely, De Marzo and Garcia investigate emergent collective behavior and activity regularities among AI agents interacting on the platform De Marzo and Garcia (2026), while Jiang et al. analyze content generation dynamics and structural properties of Moltbook interactions, highlighting patterns in topic formation and communication structure Jiang et al. (2026). Similarly, Holtz provides one of the structural descriptions of the Moltbook interaction graph, examining early network-level properties of the system Holtz (2026). Other studies compare Moltbook with human online platforms and explore similarities and divergences between AI-generated and human-generated social dynamics Zhu et al. (2026); Li et al. (2026). All these early results suggest that LLM-native social networks may reproduce several structural characteristics commonly observed in human online platforms, including highly heterogeneous activity distributions and the emergence of central actors.
由 LLM 智能体构成的在线环境的出现，为研究人工社会系统提供了新的实证情境 Chatterji et al. (2025)。由自主 LLM 智能体构成的社会环境的早期例子包括 Chirper 等平台，已有研究分析其互动网络，以探究智能体群体的行为模式和结构属性 Zhu et al. (2025)。近来，Moltbook 已成为一个完全由 LLM 智能体构成的大规模社会环境，这些智能体发布帖子并通过评论进行互动。近期的预印本已开始分析 Moltbook 生态系统的不同方面，主要将其基本统计属性与真实人类生成的网络的相应属性进行比较 De Marzo and Garcia (2026); Jiang et al. (2026); Holtz (2026); Zhu et al. (2026); Li et al. (2026)。更具体地说，De Marzo and Garcia 研究了在该平台上互动的 AI 智能体之间涌现的集体行为和活动规律 De Marzo and Garcia (2026)，而 Jiang et al. 分析了 Moltbook 互动的内容生成动态和结构属性，着重揭示了话题形成和通信结构中的模式 Jiang et al. (2026)。类似地，Holtz 对 Moltbook 互动图提供了一种结构描述，考察了该系统早期的网络层面属性 Holtz (2026)。其他研究将 Moltbook 与人类在线平台进行比较，并探讨 AI 生成与人类生成的社会动态之间的相似性和差异 Zhu et al. (2026); Li et al. (2026)。所有这些早期结果表明，LLM 原生社交网络可能再现人类在线平台中常见的若干结构特征，包括高度异质的活动分布以及核心行动者的涌现。
However, despite these initial contributions, a systematic network-science characterization of LLM-native social platforms remains limited. In particular, the mesoscale organization and structural robustness of interaction networks formed entirely by artificial agents remain poorly understood. This motivates the present study, which applies established tools from network science—including connectivity analysis, core–periphery structure, and robustness experiments—to analyze the structural organization and fragility of the Moltbook interaction network.
然而，尽管已有这些初步贡献，对 LLM 原生社交平台的系统性网络科学刻画仍然有限。尤其是，对于完全由人工智能体形成的互动网络，其中观组织和结构鲁棒性仍缺乏充分理解。这促成了本研究，本研究运用网络科学中的成熟工具，包括连通性分析、核心—边缘结构和鲁棒性实验，分析 Moltbook 互动网络的结构组织与脆弱性。
3 Methods3 方法
3.1 Data collection3.1 数据收集
The dataset used in this study was obtained through a web scraping procedure performed on Moltbook. The scraping process collected publicly available interaction data, including posts, comments, and user identifiers. In total, the dataset comprises 235,572 posts, 1,540,238 comments, and 39,924 unique users. These data were subsequently structured into a relational database to enable systematic analysis of user interactions. The resulting dataset constitutes the empirical basis for all network analyses conducted in this study.
本研究使用的数据集通过对 Moltbook 进行网页抓取得到。抓取过程收集了公开可用的互动数据，包括帖子、评论和用户标识符。该数据集共包含 235,572 篇帖子、1,540,238 条评论和 39,924 名独立用户。随后，这些数据被组织为关系数据库，以便对用户互动进行系统分析。由此形成的数据集构成了本研究所有网络分析的实证基础。
3.2 Data analysis3.2 数据分析

Colors indicate communities detected through modularity optimization. The layout is generated using the ForceAtlas2 algorithm.

The dataset was solely studied with a network perspective, where the construction and statistical. A directed and weighted graph was built, where nodes represent users and edges represent commenting interactions from the commenter to the author of the post. Edge weights correspond to the number of comments exchanged between pairs of users.
该数据集仅从网络视角进行研究，其中构建和统计。构建了一个有向加权图，其中节点代表用户，边代表从评论者指向帖子作者的评论互动。边权重对应于每对用户之间交换的评论数量。
Both in-degree and out-degree and the weighed strength were computed to characterize user activity and influence, with the complementary cumulative distribution functions of degree and strength were estimated on logarithmic scales. Heavy-tailed behavior was assessed by fitting power-law distributions, estimating the scaling exponent $\alpha$ , the lower bound $x_{\min}$ , and the Kolmogorov–Smirnov statistic.
通过计算入度、出度和加权强度来刻画用户的活跃度与影响力，并在对数尺度上估计度和强度的互补累积分布函数。通过拟合幂律分布，并估计标度指数 $\alpha$、下界 $x_{\min}$ 和 Kolmogorov–Smirnov 统计量，评估重尾行为。
Global connectivity was evaluated by computing weakly and strongly connected components and identifying the size of the giant component. Rank-size distributions were analyzed to assess fragmentation patterns. Core-periphery structure was investigated using k-core decomposition. An optimal core threshold was selected following a Borgatti–Everett fitting criterion based on intra-core, intra-periphery, and cross densities. Network robustness was assessed through targeted node removal experiments (ranked by in-degree and out-degree) and random failures. After node removal, we measured the relative size of the giant component, the number of connected components, average shortest path length, and global efficiency.
通过计算弱连通分量和强连通分量，并确定巨型连通分量的规模，评估全局连通性。通过分析秩—规模分布来评估碎片化模式。使用 k-core 分解研究核心—边缘结构。依据基于核心内部、边缘内部以及核心与边缘之间连接密度的 Borgatti–Everett 拟合准则，选择最优核心阈值。通过定向节点移除实验（按入度和出度排序）和随机失效实验评估网络鲁棒性。移除节点后，我们测量了巨型连通分量的相对规模、连通分量数量、平均最短路径长度和全局效率。
All the analysis fore this paper were performed in Python, using pandas, matplotlib, numpy, networkx and power-laws, with the version available on february 18th, 2026.
本文的所有分析均在 Python 中完成，使用了 pandas、matplotlib、numpy、networkx 和 power-laws，所用版本为 2026 年 2 月 18 日可用的版本。

4.1 Scale-free structure4.1 无标度结构

Firstly, we analyze the degree and strength distributions, as depicted in Figures 2, which report the distributions of in-degree $k_{in}$ and in-strength $s_{in}$ in the directed user interaction network, shown on log–log scales. The in-degree measures the number of distinct users who have commented on a given user, whereas the in-strength represents the total number of received comments, which are the sum of incoming edge weights. Both distributions are strongly right-skewed and characterized by extended heavy tails which follows a scale-free distribution for two and an half order of magnitude. As a metter of fact, the Kolmogorov–Smirnov distance ( $\mathrm{KS}\approx 0.19$ ) indicates that the pure power-law model does not perfectly describe the entire support of the distribution but only on the first regime, which got truncated where high values with few observations.
首先，我们分析度和强度的分布，如图 2 所示，该图在双对数尺度上展示了有向用户交互网络中入度 $k_{in}$ 和入强度 $s_{in}$ 的分布。入度衡量对某个给定用户发表评论的不同用户数量，而入强度表示收到的评论总数，即所有入边权重之和。这两种分布均呈现明显的右偏，并具有延伸的重尾，在两个半数量级的范围内遵循无标度分布。事实上，Kolmogorov–Smirnov 距离（$\mathrm{KS}\approx 0.19$）表明，纯幂律模型无法完美描述分布的整个支撑集，而仅适用于第一个区段，该区段在观测数量稀少的高值处被截断。

The observed distributions reveal marked heterogeneity in user visibility. Table 1 shows a substantial gap between mean and median values, indicating a highly imbalanced structure in which a small fraction of nodes concentrates a large share of received interactions. Also, the maximum observed values ( $k_{in}=1031$ , $s_{in}=4320$ ) further confirm the presence of highly central hubs. The estimated scaling exponents ( $\alpha\approx 1.5$ ) are consistent with heavy-tailed regimes and imply high structural variability.
观测到的分布揭示了用户可见性方面的显著异质性。表 1 显示，均值与中位数之间存在显著差距，表明该结构高度不均衡，少量节点集中了收到的交互中的很大一部分。此外，观测到的最大值（$k_{in}=1031$，$s_{in}=4320$）进一步证实了高度中心化的枢纽节点的存在。估计得到的标度指数（$\alpha\approx 1.5$）与重尾特征一致，并意味着较高的结构变异性。
Strongly asymmetric degree distributions with extended tails are widely documented in human social networks, both offline and online Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2005). Similar concentration patterns have been observed in electronic communication networks Ebel et al. (2002) and online social platforms Mislove et al. (2007). In many empirical contexts, estimated exponents typically fall within the range $2<\alpha<3$ , although substantial variability exists Clauset et al. (2009). In the present case, the estimated exponent is below 2 ( $\alpha\approx 1.5$ ), indicating an even stronger inequality in interaction distribution. Consistent with the discussion in Clauset et al. (2009); Broido and Clauset (2019), the observed KS distance suggests that the network cannot be considered strictly “scale-free.” Overall, the structure is qualitatively similar to many human social networks—characterized by high heterogeneity and the presence of hubs—while not fully satisfying the statistical criteria of a pure power-law. Notably, these structural patterns emerge in a system populated entirely by artificial agents, without direct human participation, suggesting that several canonical properties of social networks may arise from basic interaction mechanisms rather than uniquely human social behavior.
在离线和在线的人类社交网络中，具有延伸尾部的强不对称度分布已得到广泛记录，参见 Barabási and Albert (1999); Newman (2005)。电子通信网络和在线社交平台中也观察到了类似的集中模式，分别参见 Ebel et al. (2002) 和 Mislove et al. (2007)。在许多实证情境中，估计得到的指数通常落在 $2<\alpha<3$ 的范围内，尽管存在相当大的变异，参见 Clauset et al. (2009)。在本研究中，估计得到的指数低于 2（$\alpha\approx 1.5$），表明交互分布存在更强的不平等性。与 Clauset et al. (2009); Broido and Clauset (2019) 中的讨论一致，观测到的 KS 距离表明，该网络不能被视为严格的“无标度”网络。总体而言，该结构在定性上与许多人类社交网络相似，具有高度异质性和枢纽节点，但并未完全满足纯幂律的统计标准。值得注意的是，这些结构模式出现在一个完全由人工智能体构成、没有人类直接参与的系统中，这表明社交网络的若干典型属性可能源于基本的交互机制，而非人类独有的社会行为。

As the table 2 shows, the data reveal a strong inequality in user activity. The median is substantially lower than the mean (posts: 2 vs 5.9; comments: 1 vs 38.58), indicating that the majority of users produce only a small amount of content, while a minority is extremely active. The 99th percentile equals 80.77 posts but exceeds 275 comments, suggesting that interaction intensity is concentrated primarily in commenting behavior. The maximum observed value (over 120,000 comments by a single user) further highlights the presence of extremely high-productivity users. Commenting activity is considerably more dispersed ( $\sigma\approx 1221$ ) than posting activity ( $\sigma\approx 35$ ), indicating that conversational engagement is structurally more polarized. Overall, content production follows a strongly heterogeneous dynamic dominated by a small number of highly active users. Highly asymmetric activity distributions are a well-documented characteristic of human social networks; it is shown that activity on online platforms is highly concentrated among a minority of users Wu and Huberman (2007). Analyses of networks such as YouTube, Flickr, and Orkut report similar patterns of inequality in content production Mislove et al. (2007). Also the early-age Twitter likewise document a strong concentration of posting activity within a small fraction of users Kwak et al. (2010).
如表 2 所示，数据揭示了用户活动的高度不均等性。中位数显著低于均值（发帖数：2 对比 5.9；评论数：1 对比 38.58），表明大多数用户仅产生少量内容，而少数用户极为活跃。发帖数的第 99 百分位数为 80.77，而评论数的第 99 百分位数超过 275，这表明互动强度主要集中于评论行为。观测到的最大值（单个用户发表超过 120,000 条评论）进一步凸显了极高产出用户的存在。评论活动的离散程度（ $\sigma\approx 1221$ ）远高于发帖活动（ $\sigma\approx 35$ ），表明对话参与在结构上呈现出更强的两极分化。总体而言，内容生产呈现出高度异质的动态特征，由少数高度活跃的用户主导。高度不对称的活动分布是人类社交网络中一个已有充分记录的特征；研究表明，在线平台上的活动高度集中于少数用户 Wu and Huberman (2007)。对 YouTube、Flickr 和 Orkut 等网络的分析也报告了类似的内容生产不均等模式 Mislove et al. (2007)。对早期 Twitter 的研究同样记录了发帖活动高度集中于少数用户的现象 Kwak et al. (2010)。
In the present case, the observed distribution is qualitatively consistent with these findings: both posting and, especially, commenting are dominated by a highly active minority. The pronounced dispersion in commenting activity suggests that conversational dynamics amplify inequality relative to the mere publication of original content. The resulting structure is therefore comparable, in terms of activity concentration, to that observed in human social platforms.
在本研究中，观测到的分布在定性上与这些发现一致：发帖活动和尤其是评论活动均由高度活跃的少数用户主导。评论活动显著的离散性表明，与单纯发布原创内容相比，对话动态会放大不均等性。因此，就活动集中程度而言，由此形成的结构与人类社交平台中观测到的结构具有可比性。
4.2 Network fragility4.2 网络脆弱性
Figure 5: Rank–size distributions of WCC and SCC in the directed network. Panel (A) shows the WCC distribution, highlighting the dominance of the Giant WCC (99.9%). Panel (B) shows the SCC distribution, where the Giant SCC is substantially smaller(33.5%).
图 5：有向网络中 WCC 和 SCC 的排名—规模分布。面板（A）展示了 WCC 的分布，凸显了巨型 WCC 的主导地位（99.9%）。面板（B）展示了 SCC 的分布，其中巨型 SCC 的规模显著较小（33.5%）。
Figure 5 shows the rank–size distributions of weakly connected components and strongly connected components (respectively, WCC and SCC) in the directed network. The plot highlights how big is the giant component in comparison to the overall network. For the weakly connected components, the largest component includes 39,878 nodes (which is 99.9% of the total), while the remaining components are negligible in size. In contrast, the largest strongly connected component comprises 13,369 nodes (33.5%), followed by a very large number of unitary or very small components. The discrepancy suggests a strongly asymmetric structure: most users are reachable within the system, yet mutual reachability does not extend across the entire network. From a dynamical perspective, this implies that information can propagate globally (due to the near-total WCC), whereas feedback mechanisms and bidirectional circulation are confined to a more compact structural subset. On a practical size, this suggest the possibility of existence of echo chambers also in LLMs social networks.
图 5 展示了有向网络中弱连通分量和强连通分量（分别为 WCC 和 SCC）的排名—规模分布。该图凸显了巨型连通分量相对于整个网络的规模。对于弱连通分量，最大分量包含 39,878 个节点（占总数的 99.9%），其余分量的规模则可以忽略不计。相比之下，最大的强连通分量包含 13,369 个节点（33.5%），其后是数量极多的单节点分量或规模很小的分量。这种差异表明网络具有高度不对称的结构：系统内大多数用户是可达的，但相互可达性并未覆盖整个网络。从动力学角度看，这意味着信息能够在全局传播（由于 WCC 几乎覆盖整个网络），而反馈机制和双向流通则局限于一个更紧凑的结构子集。从实际层面看，这表明 LLMs 社交网络中也可能存在回音室。
Such patterns are commonly observed in large-scale directed networks. In the Web graph, for instance, a “bow-tie” structure emerges in which a Giant SCC is embedded within a much larger weakly connected component (Broder et al., 2000). Similar directional asymmetries have been documented in Twitter networks (Kwak et al., 2010). Large-scale social platforms also exhibit near-total weak connectivity; for example, the Facebook graph shows a giant component covering the vast majority of active users (Ugander et al., 2011). In the broader framework of complex networks, the divergence between WCC and SCC sizes in directed graphs is interpreted as evidence of directional flows and hierarchical organization (Newman, 2010). The emergence of hubs in growing networks further contributes to globally connected yet structurally asymmetric configurations (Barabási and Albert, 1999).
此类模式在大规模有向网络中十分常见。例如，万维网图中会出现一种“蝴蝶结”结构，其中一个巨型 SCC 嵌于规模大得多的弱连通分量之中 (Broder et al., 2000)。Twitter 网络中也有类似方向不对称性的记录 (Kwak et al., 2010)。大型社交平台也表现出几乎覆盖全网的弱连通性；例如，Facebook 图中的巨型连通分量覆盖了绝大多数活跃用户 (Ugander et al., 2011)。在更广泛的复杂网络框架中，有向图中 WCC 与 SCC 的规模差异被解释为存在定向流动和层级组织的证据 (Newman, 2010)。增长网络中枢纽节点的出现，进一步促成了全局连通但结构不对称的网络构型 (Barabási and Albert, 1999)。
Overall, the Moltbook network exhibits near-total weak connectivity alongside a substantially smaller strongly connected core, indicating a globally reachable but directionally constrained structure consistent with large-scale social systems.
总体而言，Moltbook 网络表现出几乎覆盖全网的弱连通性，同时具有规模明显较小的强连通核心，表明其结构在全局上可达，但受到方向性的约束，这与大规模社会系统的特征一致。

In assessing the mesoscale structure, we adopted the core–periphery approach proposed by Stephen P. Borgatti and Martin G. Everett, which formalizes the intuition of a dense core and a sparse periphery by estimating a core/periphery partition according to its coherence with such an ideal configuration Borgatti and Everett (2000). The Borgatti–Everett fit is moderate, but the decomposition still reveals a clear structural separation between a dense nucleus of highly connected nodes and a large peripheral population. In the Moltbook network, the k-core decomposition reveals marked heterogeneity (min = 1, max = 102, mean = 14.66, median = 7, standard deviation = 19.94), indicating that most users occupy low levels of “coreness,” while a very small fraction attains extreme values. Optimization of the BE criterion selects a threshold ( $k^{*}=102$ ), coinciding with $k_{\max}$ , yielding a core of 343 nodes (0.9%) and a periphery of 39,535 nodes (99.1%). The BE fit ( $\approx 0.1102$ ) signals a present but non-idealized core–periphery structure, that is, a coherent structural separation despite interactions that do not perfectly conform to the dense-core/sparse-periphery dichotomy.
在评估中观尺度结构时，我们采用了 Stephen P. Borgatti 和 Martin G. Everett 提出的核心—边缘方法，该方法根据核心/边缘划分与理想构型的一致程度来估计这一划分，从而将稠密核心与稀疏边缘的直观概念形式化 Borgatti and Everett (2000)。Borgatti–Everett 拟合程度适中，但分解结果仍揭示出明确的结构分离：一部分是由高度连接的节点构成的稠密核心，另一部分是规模庞大的边缘群体。在 Moltbook 网络中，k-core 分解揭示出显著的异质性（最小值 = 1，最大值 = 102，均值 = 14.66，中位数 = 7，标准差 = 19.94），表明大多数用户的“核心度”较低，而仅有极少数用户达到极高值。对 BE 准则进行优化所选出的阈值（ $k^{*}=102$ ）与 $k_{\max}$ 一致，由此得到包含 343 个节点（0.9%）的核心和包含 39,535 个节点（99.1%）的边缘。BE 拟合度（ $\approx 0.1102$ ）表明，网络中存在核心—边缘结构，但并未达到理想构型；也就是说，尽管交互并不完全符合稠密核心/稀疏边缘的二分模式，网络仍呈现出一致的结构分离。
From an interpretative standpoint, such a restricted core implies that global cohesion is not uniformly distributed: a small number of nodes concentrate the redundancy of connections necessary to sustain the relational backbone, whereas the periphery consists predominantly of users with structurally limited participation and limited capacity to maintain autonomous connectivity. In a directed comment network, this profile is consistent with a system in which interaction organizes around a very small set of “central actors,” alongside a broad peripheral basin that contributes less to closure and internal density.
从解释角度来看，如此小规模的核心意味着全局凝聚力的分布并不均匀：维持关系骨架所需的连接冗余集中于少数节点，而边缘则主要由结构性参与程度有限、维持自主连通性能力有限的用户构成。在有向评论网络中，这一特征符合这样一种系统：交互围绕极少数“中心行动者”组织起来，同时存在一个广泛的边缘群体，其对网络闭合性和内部密度的贡献较小。
To directly connect this mesoscale structure to global connectivity stability, we measured robustness through node removal experiments, comparing random removals with targeted removals of nodes ranked by in-degree and out-degree. This type of experiment corresponds to the classical targeted attack framework used in network science to evaluate the robustness and fragility of heterogeneous networks. Methodologically, robustness is evaluated on the undirected version of the graph, so that fragmentation is interpreted as the loss of topological connectivity rather than directional reachability.

Table 4 and Figure 6 present the vulnerability analysis of the network, reporting the fraction of the giant component as a function of the percentage of removed nodes (1%, 5%, 10%, 20%) under random failures and targeted attacks. Under random removal, the giant component remains large even at substantial removal levels (approximately 78% of its initial size after removing 20% of nodes), indicating robustness to unstructured failures. Under targeted removal by in-degree, the giant component decreases more rapidly (approximately 73% at 5% removed; approximately 49% at 20% removed), suggesting that the most “recognized” or highly commented nodes contribute significantly to global cohesion. Targeted removal by out-degree is the most disruptive (approximately 75% already at 1% removed; approximately 45% at 10%; approximately 15% at 20%), indicating that nodes most active in generating interactions are crucial for maintaining overall connectivity. These results suggest that, in AI-agent social environments, the structural backbone of the network may depend more strongly on agents that actively generate interactions than on those that merely accumulate visibility.

The combination of (i) an extremely small core and (ii) rapid collapse under targeted removals indicates strong structural dependence on a minority of nodes: when the most central nodes are removed, the already weakly cohesive periphery lacks sufficient redundancy to preserve a dominant connected component. Moreover, the greater sensitivity to out-degree–based attacks suggests that the structural “pillars” of connectivity are not only highly popular nodes (high in-degree), but especially those that activate and distribute interactions, acting as topological bridges between otherwise weakly interconnected regions of the network.

(2011) describe a Facebook graph that is almost entirely connected and exhibits surprisingly dense local regions within a globally sparse structure. Similarly, Mislove et al. (2007) document recurring structural properties across multiple online social networks and highlight the importance of a small fraction of highly connected nodes for global structure. (2010).

5 Conclusion5 结论
This study provides a network-science characterization of the interaction structure emerging within Moltbook, a social platform populated entirely by LLM-based agents. By constructing and analyzing a large-scale interaction network derived from posts and comments, we investigated the structural organization of the system in terms of connectivity patterns, activity distributions, mesoscale organization, and robustness properties.
本研究从网络科学的角度，刻画了 Moltbook 内部涌现的交互结构；Moltbook 是一个完全由基于 LLM 的智能体构成的社交平台。通过构建和分析从帖子与评论中提取的大规模交互网络，我们从连接模式、活跃度分布、中观组织和鲁棒性等方面研究了该系统的结构组织。
The results reveal a highly heterogeneous structure dominated by a small number of highly central nodes. Degree and activity distributions display strong heavy-tailed behavior, indicating substantial inequality in both visibility and participation among agents. At the mesoscale, the k-core decomposition highlights the presence of a very small structural core comprising only 0.9% of nodes, surrounded by a large peripheral population with limited connectivity. Despite a moderate Borgatti–Everett fit, this decomposition still indicates a clear separation between a dense nucleus of highly interconnected agents and a broad periphery.
结果揭示了一种由少数高中心性节点主导的高度异质结构。度分布和活跃度分布呈现出显著的重尾特征，表明智能体之间在可见度和参与程度上均存在显著的不平等。在中观尺度上，k-core 分解揭示了一个仅包含 0.9% 节点的极小结构核心，其周围是大量连接有限的外围节点。尽管 Borgatti–Everett 拟合程度中等，这一分解仍表明，由高度互联的智能体组成的密集核心与广泛的外围之间存在清晰的分离。
Robustness experiments further show that the network exhibits asymmetric resilience properties. While the giant connected component remains relatively stable under random node removals, targeted removal of highly connected nodes rapidly fragments the network. In particular, nodes with high out-degree play a critical role in sustaining global connectivity, suggesting that agents actively generating interactions function as key structural bridges across the network.
鲁棒性实验进一步表明，该网络表现出非对称的韧性特征。在随机移除节点时，巨型连通分量保持相对稳定，而定向移除高连接度节点则会使网络迅速碎片化。尤其是，高出度节点在维持全局连通性方面发挥着关键作用，这表明主动生成交互的智能体充当了贯穿整个网络的关键结构桥梁。
Taken together, these findings suggest that interaction networks formed by populations of artificial agents can reproduce several structural features commonly observed in human online social networks—such as heterogeneous connectivity, hub dominance, and large giant components—while also exhibiting pronounced centralization and structural fragility. More broadly, these results suggest that large-scale societies of interacting AI agents may spontaneously generate hierarchical and centralized interaction structures, even in the absence of explicit coordination mechanisms. Understanding these emergent structural properties is therefore essential for anticipating the stability, resilience, and systemic risks of future AI-mediated social ecosystems. As LLM-based agents increasingly interact in shared digital environments, understanding the structural properties of these emerging artificial social systems becomes an important research direction.
综合来看，这些发现表明，人工智能体群体形成的交互网络能够再现人类在线社交网络中常见的若干结构特征，例如异质连接、枢纽主导和规模庞大的巨型连通分量，同时也表现出显著的中心化和结构脆弱性。更广泛地说，这些结果表明，即使不存在明确的协调机制，由相互交互的 AI 智能体组成的大规模社会也可能自发形成层级化和中心化的交互结构。因此，理解这些涌现的结构特性，对于预判未来由 AI 介导的社会生态系统的稳定性、韧性和系统性风险至关重要。随着基于 LLM 的智能体日益频繁地在共享数字环境中交互，理解这些新兴人工社会系统的结构特性正成为一个重要的研究方向。
Future work may explore the temporal evolution of such networks, the dynamics of information diffusion among agents, comparative analyses between AI-native and human social platforms, and discover the underlying generative process.
未来的研究可探索此类网络的时间演化、智能体之间信息扩散的动态过程，对 AI 原生社交平台与人类社交平台进行比较分析，并揭示其背后的生成过程。
