Abstract摘要
Recent work reports strong performance from multi-agent LLM systems (MAS), but these gains are often confounded by increased test-time computation. When computation is normalized, single-agent systems (SAS) can match or outperform MAS, yet the theoretical basis and evaluation methodology behind this comparison remain unclear. We present an information-theoretic argument, grounded in the Data Processing Inequality, suggesting that under a fixed reasoning-token budget and with perfect context utilization, single-agent systems are more information-efficient. This perspective further predicts that multi-agent systems become competitive when a single agent’s effective context utilization is degraded, or when more compute is expended. We test these predictions in a controlled empirical study across three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama, and Gemini 2.5), comparing SAS with multiple MAS architectures under matched budgets. We find that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS on multi-hop reasoning tasks when reasoning tokens are held constant. Beyond aggregate performance, we conduct a detailed diagnostic analysis of system behavior and evaluation methodology. We identify significant artifacts in API-based budget control (particularly in Gemini 2.5) and in standard benchmarks, both of which can inflate apparent gains from MAS. Overall, our results suggest that, for multi-hop reasoning tasks, many reported advantages of multi-agent systems are better explained by unaccounted computation and context effects rather than inherent architectural benefits, and highlight the importance of understanding and explicitly controlling the trade-offs between compute, context, and coordination in agentic systems.

1 Introduction1 引言
Multi-agent LLM architectures (MAS), including planners, role-playing systems, debate frameworks, and tool-specialized swarms, have demonstrated strong empirical performance across a range of tasks. At a high level, these approaches decompose reasoning across multiple agents that operate over partial contexts and communicate via generated text. In contrast, single-agent systems (SAS) perform reasoning within a single, unified context, relying on internal token-level computation rather than explicit inter-agent communication.
多智能体 LLM 架构（MAS），包括规划器、角色扮演系统、辩论框架以及按工具专门分工的智能体集群，已在一系列任务中展现出出色的实证性能。从总体上看，这些方法将推理分解到多个智能体上，各智能体基于局部上下文运行，并通过生成的文本进行通信。相比之下，单智能体系统（SAS）在单一、统一的上下文中进行推理，依赖内部的词元级计算，而非显式的智能体间通信。
However, comparisons between MAS and SAS are often confounded by differences in test-time computation. MAS typically consume more tokens through longer reasoning traces or multiple agent interactions, making it unclear whether their gains arise from architectural advantages or simply from increased compute. Recent budget-aware studies suggest that, when computation is normalized, many such strategies underperform strong single-agent baselines (Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025).
然而，MAS 与 SAS 之间的比较往往受到测试时计算量差异的混杂影响。MAS 通常因更长的推理轨迹或多次智能体交互而消耗更多词元，因此难以判断其性能提升究竟来自架构优势，还是仅仅来自计算量的增加。近期考虑预算因素的研究表明，当计算量保持一致时，许多此类策略的表现不及强大的单智能体基线（Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025）。
In this work, we revisit this question under an explicit focus on thinking token budgets, which we define as the total number of tokens used for intermediate reasoning, excluding prompts and final answers. We focus on multi-hop reasoning tasks and ask three central questions: why might SAS outperform MAS under fixed budgets, when do MAS become competitive, and how should such comparisons be conducted reliably?
在本研究中，我们明确聚焦于思考词元预算，重新审视这一问题；我们将思考词元预算定义为用于中间推理的词元总数，不包括提示和最终答案。我们聚焦于多跳推理任务，并提出三个核心问题：为什么 SAS 在固定预算下可能优于 MAS，MAS 何时会具备竞争力，以及应如何可靠地开展此类比较？
We first provide an information-theoretic perspective, based on the Data Processing Inequality, suggesting that under fixed token budgets, multi-agent decompositions introduce additional communication bottlenecks that can lead to information loss. This perspective also clarifies when MAS can be advantageous: specifically, when a single agent’s effective context utilization is degraded (e.g., due to long or noisy contexts), or when MAS benefit from additional unaccounted computation through extended interactions.
我们首先提供一个基于数据处理不等式的信息论视角，表明在词元预算固定的条件下，多智能体分解会引入额外的通信瓶颈，进而可能导致信息损失。这一视角也阐明了 MAS 何时可能具有优势：具体而言，当单个智能体对上下文的有效利用能力下降时（例如，由于上下文过长或包含噪声），或者当 MAS 通过延长交互而受益于额外且未被计入的计算量时。
We then test these predictions in a controlled empirical study across three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama, and Gemini 2.5), comparing SAS and multiple MAS architectures under matched reasoning-token budgets. We find that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS on multi-hop reasoning tasks under these constraints.
随后，我们在涵盖三个模型系列（Qwen3、DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Llama 和 Gemini 2.5）的受控实证研究中检验这些预测，在相同推理词元预算下比较 SAS 与多种 MAS 架构。我们发现，在这些约束下，SAS 在多跳推理任务中的表现始终达到或超过 MAS。
Beyond aggregate performance, we perform a detailed diagnostic analysis of evaluation methodology and model behavior. We identify (i) artifacts in API-based budget control that distort effective computation, (ii) benchmark vulnerabilities exposed through paraphrasing, and (iii) systematic differences in failure modes across architectures.
除总体性能外，我们还对评估方法和模型行为进行了详细的诊断分析。我们识别出：(i) 基于 API 的预算控制中会扭曲有效计算量的伪象，(ii) 通过改写暴露出的基准测试漏洞，以及 (iii) 不同架构在失败模式上的系统性差异。
We summarize our contributions as follows:
我们的贡献概括如下：
An information-theoretic perspective on SAS vs. MAS under fixed budgets, explaining how multi-agent decompositions introduce additional communication bottlenecks (§3).
从信息论视角比较固定预算下的 SAS 与 MAS，解释多智能体分解如何引入额外的通信瓶颈（§3）。
A controlled empirical comparison across multiple model families, showing that SAS consistently match or outperform MAS under matched reasoning-token budgets on multi-hop reasoning tasks (§5).
开展涵盖多个模型系列的受控实证比较，表明在相同推理词元预算下，SAS 在多跳推理任务中的表现始终达到或超过 MAS（§5）。
A diagnostic analysis of evaluation methodology, uncovering artifacts in API-based budgeting, benchmark vulnerabilities, and failure modes (§G, §A, §B).
对评估方法进行诊断性分析，揭示基于 API 的预算控制中的伪象、基准测试的脆弱性以及失效模式（§G、§A、§B）。
2 Related Work2 相关工作
Budget-Controlled Evaluation:预算受控的评估：
Recent work has emphasized that comparisons between reasoning strategies are often confounded by unequal test-time computation. Reasoning in Token Economies and follow-up work show that many elaborate prompting or search strategies fail to outperform simpler baselines once token or compute budgets are matched (Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025). Our work is aligned with this line, but differs in two ways: we treat only thinking tokens as the controlled resource, and we study the comparison between single-agent and multi-agent architectures rather than budget allocation within one architecture.
近期研究强调，推理策略之间的比较往往受到测试时计算量不等这一混杂因素的影响。《词元经济中的推理》（Reasoning in Token Economies）及其后续研究表明，一旦词元或计算预算相匹配，许多复杂的提示或搜索策略便无法优于更简单的基线（Wang et al., 2024; Han et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025b; Wen et al., 2025）。我们的工作与这一研究方向一致，但有两点不同：我们仅将思考词元作为受控资源，并且研究单智能体与多智能体架构之间的比较，而非同一架构内部的预算分配。
When and why MAS helps:MAS 何时以及为何有效：
Concurrent works sharpen the picture that MAS gains are dependent on regime and implementation. Anthropic (2025) argues that much of the apparent advantage of MAS comes from increased compute. On the other hand, Kim et al. (2025) shows that once computation is normalized, agentic benefits tend to concentrate in weaker model or harder-regime settings and diminish (or even reverse) as base model capability increases, highlighting coordination overhead as a first-order constraint. Additionally, Cemri et al. (2025) introduce multi-agent tracing and a failure taxonomy that explains how orchestration can induce drift, information loss, or misleading improvements from evaluation artifacts. These failure modes align with our observed over-exploration, aggregation/extraction errors, and opaque API accounting. Finally, Ke et al. (2026) study learned orchestration via holistic configuration selection and propose controlled benchmarking axes for MAS, emphasizing that improvements depend on task structure and verification protocols rather than MAS being universally superior. Taken together, these results motivate the core design of our study: (i) strictly control the primary confound (thinking token budget), (ii) diagnose how coordination and communication affect accuracy, and (iii) characterize concrete regimes (e.g., degraded context) where MAS or hybrid designs can become competitive.
同期研究进一步明确，MAS 的收益取决于所处条件和具体实现。Anthropic (2025) 认为，MAS 的表面优势在很大程度上来自计算量的增加。另一方面，Kim et al. (2025) 表明，在统一计算量后，智能体带来的收益往往集中于模型较弱或任务条件更困难的场景，并随着基础模型能力的提升而减小（甚至逆转），这凸显了协调开销是一项首要制约因素。此外，Cemri et al. (2025) 引入了多智能体追踪方法和失效分类体系，解释了编排如何引发偏移、信息丢失，或因评估伪象而产生具有误导性的性能提升。这些失效模式与我们观察到的过度探索、聚合/提取错误以及不透明的 API 用量核算相一致。最后，Ke et al. (2026) 通过整体配置选择研究学习式编排，并提出用于 MAS 受控基准测试的评估维度，强调性能提升取决于任务结构和验证协议，而非 MAS 在所有情况下都更优。综合来看，这些结果促成了本研究的核心设计：(i) 严格控制主要混杂因素（思考词元预算），(ii) 诊断协调与通信如何影响准确率，以及 (iii) 刻画 MAS 或混合设计能够具备竞争力的具体条件（例如上下文退化）。
SAS vs. MAS:SAS 与 MAS：
Empirical evidence increasingly suggests that as frontier models improve, the benefits of orchestration diminish and SAS can match or surpass MAS (Gao et al., 2025). In education analytics, SAS with few-shot prompting outperformed MAS for reflection assessment (Li et al., 2025a). Benchmarks for collaborative agents (e.g., MedAgentBoard) further reveal that MAS advantages are task-specific and not universal (Zhu et al., 2025). Our findings complement these studies under stricter compute controls.
越来越多的实证证据表明，随着前沿模型能力的提升，编排的收益逐渐减小，SAS 可以达到或超过 MAS 的表现（Gao et al., 2025）。在教育分析领域，采用少样本提示的 SAS 在反思评估任务中的表现优于 MAS（Li et al., 2025a）。面向协作智能体的基准测试（例如 MedAgentBoard）进一步揭示，MAS 的优势取决于具体任务，并不具有普遍性（Zhu et al., 2025）。我们的研究结果在更严格的计算量控制下补充了这些研究。
Multi-agent collaboration mechanisms:多智能体协作机制：
Prior work has proposed many ways to structure collaboration between LLMs, including debate, role specialization, ensemble/self-consistency, and reflection (Du et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Shinn et al., 2023). Rather than treating MAS as a single design, we evaluate several representative mechanisms under a common matched-budget framework. This allows us to separate gains due to architectural structure from gains due to simply spending more computation.
既有研究提出了多种组织 LLM 之间协作的方式，包括辩论、角色专业化、集成/自一致性以及反思（Du et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024; Shinn et al., 2023）。我们并未将 MAS 视为一种单一设计，而是在统一的预算匹配框架下评估若干具有代表性的机制。这使我们能够区分架构结构带来的收益与单纯增加计算投入带来的收益。
Context length, degradation, and long-context utilization:上下文长度、退化与长上下文利用：
Independent of architecture, modern LLMs do not use long context perfectly: attention dilution, noise sensitivity, context confusion, and positional bias can all degrade reasoning even when relevant information is present. Recent work has formalized these effects from several angles, including context degradation and long-context stress testing (Du et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025c; Lu et al., 2024), failures that are sensitive to their position such as the lost in the middle effect (Liu et al., 2024), and broader reliability decay as input length grows (Hong et al., 2025). We connect this literature to the SAS-versus-MAS question by explicitly modeling degraded effective context and by testing when structured multi-agent pipelines become competitive as single-agent context use deteriorates.
无论采用何种架构，现代 LLM 都无法完美利用长上下文：即使相关信息确实存在，注意力稀释、噪声敏感性、上下文混淆和位置偏差也都可能削弱推理能力。近期研究从多个角度对这些效应进行了形式化描述，包括上下文退化与长上下文压力测试（Du et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025c; Lu et al., 2024）、对信息所处位置敏感的失效现象，如中间信息丢失效应（lost in the middle effect）（Liu et al., 2024），以及随着输入长度增加而出现的更广泛的可靠性衰减（Hong et al., 2025）。我们通过显式建模退化后的有效上下文，并测试在单智能体的上下文利用能力下降时，结构化多智能体流水线何时开始具备竞争力，将这些文献与 SAS 和 MAS 的比较问题联系起来。
3 Theoretical justification3 理论依据
This section aims to formalize the comparison between single-agent and multi-agent systems under a thinking-token budget, and explain both why single agents often perform at least as well and when multi-agent designs can help.
本节旨在形式化描述思考词元预算约束下单智能体系统与多智能体系统的比较，并解释为何单智能体的表现通常至少不逊于多智能体，以及多智能体设计在何种情况下能够发挥作用。
Let $Y$ denote the correct answer, $C$ the full context available to a single-agent LLM (including prior reasoning and intermediate states), and $M=g(C)$ the messages or summaries passed between agents in a multi-agent system.
令 $Y$ 表示正确答案，$C$ 表示单智能体 LLM 可用的完整上下文（包括先前的推理和中间状态），$M=g(C)$ 表示多智能体系统中智能体之间传递的消息或摘要。
Since $M$ is a (possibly stochastic) function of $C$ , we have the Markov chain
由于 $M$ 是 $C$ 的一个（可能具有随机性的）函数，我们有如下马尔可夫链
$$Y\longleftrightarrow C\longleftrightarrow M.$$
By the Data Processing Inequality (DPI) (Cover and Thomas, 2006),
根据数据处理不等式（Data Processing Inequality，DPI）（Cover and Thomas, 2006），
$$I(Y;C)\geq I(Y;M),$$
and equivalently,
等价地，
$$H(Y\mid M)\geq H(Y\mid C).$$
Thus, conditioning on $M$ leaves more residual uncertainty about $Y$ than conditioning on $C$ ; the multi-agent architecture cannot increase mutual information with the true answer.
因此，以 $M$ 为条件时，关于 $Y$ 的剩余不确定性比以 $C$ 为条件时更大；多智能体架构无法增加与真实答案之间的互信息。
Now we compare architectures through their minimum achievable prediction error. For any observable representation $X$ , let $\mathcal{D}_{X}$ denote the set of all possibly randomized estimators $\delta(\hat{y}\mid x)$ , and define
现在，我们通过各架构可达到的最小预测误差来比较它们。对于任意可观测表示 $X$，令 $\mathcal{D}_{X}$ 表示所有允许随机化的估计器 $\delta(\hat{y}\mid x)$ 构成的集合，并定义
$$P_{e}(X)\;=\;\inf_{\delta\in\mathcal{D}_{X}}\Pr[\hat{Y}\neq Y],\qquad\hat{Y}\sim\delta(\cdot\mid X).$$
Thus, $P_{e}(X)$ is the smallest achievable error probability for a predictor that only observes $X$ .
因此，$P_{e}(X)$ 是仅观测 $X$ 的预测器所能达到的最小错误概率。
Since $M$ is generated from $C$ through a fixed channel $q(m\mid c)$ , taking any estimator $\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}$ that predicts from $M$ would induce an estimator based on $C$ via
由于 $M$ 是由 $C$ 通过固定信道 $q(m\mid c)$ 生成的，因此任取一个基于 $M$ 进行预测的估计器 $\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}$，都可以通过下式诱导出一个基于 $C$ 的估计器
$$\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}(\hat{y}\mid c)=\sum_{m}q(m\mid c)\,\delta_{M}(\hat{y}\mid m).$$
This induced estimator first samples the same message $M$ that the multi-agent pipeline would generate from $C$ , and then applies the same downstream decision rule $\delta_{M}$ . Therefore it reproduces the same joint distribution over $(Y,\hat{Y})$ as the original predictor based on $M$ :
该诱导估计器首先采样出与多智能体流水线从 $C$ 生成的消息相同的消息 $M$，然后应用相同的下游决策规则 $\delta_{M}$。因此，它复现了原始的基于 $M$ 的预测器在 $(Y,\hat{Y})$ 上的联合分布：
$$\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}}\neq Y]=\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{M}}\neq Y].$$
Since every such induced estimator belongs to $\mathcal{D}_{C}$ ,
由于每个这样的诱导估计器都属于 $\mathcal{D}_{C}$，
$$\displaystyle P_{e}(C) \\ \displaystyle=\inf_{\delta\in\mathcal{D}_{C}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta}\neq Y] \\ \displaystyle\leq\inf_{\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{C}^{\delta_{M}}}\neq Y] \\ \displaystyle=\inf_{\delta_{M}\in\mathcal{D}_{M}}\Pr[\hat{Y}_{\delta_{M}}\neq Y] \\ \displaystyle=P_{e}(M).$$
Hence, the single-agent system (with full access to $C$ ) is information-theoretically guaranteed to perform at least as well as the multi-agent system operating on $M=g(C)$ .
因此，从信息论角度可以保证，能够完整访问 $C$ 的单智能体系统，其性能至少不逊于基于 $M=g(C)$ 运行的多智能体系统。
3.1 Context degradation3.1 上下文退化
In practice, however, modern LLMs may not utilize all of $C$ equally well. To model this, let $\tilde{C}_{\alpha}=T_{\alpha}(C)$ denote the effective context available to the single-agent predictor under degradation level $\alpha\geq 0$ , where $T_{\alpha}$ may represent deletion, masking, substitution noise, distractor injection, or more generally any transformation that makes relevant information harder to recover. We assume $T_{0}$ is the identity map and that degradation is monotone in the sense that for $0\leq\alpha_{1}\leq\alpha_{2}$ , there exists a stochastic map $S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}$ such that
然而，在实践中，现代 LLM 对 $C$ 各部分的利用能力可能并不相同。为对此建模，令 $\tilde{C}_{\alpha}=T_{\alpha}(C)$ 表示在退化程度 $\alpha\geq 0$ 下单智能体预测器可用的有效上下文，其中 $T_{\alpha}$ 可以表示删除、掩蔽、替换噪声、干扰信息注入，或更一般地，任何使相关信息更难恢复的变换。我们假设 $T_{0}$ 为恒等映射，且退化具有单调性，即对于 $0\leq\alpha_{1}\leq\alpha_{2}$，存在一个随机映射 $S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}$，使得
$$\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}=S_{\alpha_{1}\to\alpha_{2}}(\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}}).$$
Then we obtain the Markov chain
于是，我们得到马尔可夫链
$$Y\longleftrightarrow C\longleftrightarrow\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}}\longleftrightarrow\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}},$$
and therefore, by the Data Processing Inequality,
因此，根据数据处理不等式，
$$I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}})\geq I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}).$$
Equivalently, residual uncertainty is non-decreasing with degradation:
等价地，剩余不确定性随退化程度的增加而非递减：
$$H(Y\mid\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}})\leq H(Y\mid\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}).$$
The same argument as above implies that heavier degradation cannot improve the best achievable prediction error. Hence
与上文相同的论证表明，更严重的退化无法改善可达到的最小预测误差。因此
$$P_{e}(\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{1}})\leq P_{e}(\tilde{C}_{\alpha_{2}}).$$
Suppose a multi-agent architecture constructs a collection of intermediate messages
假设一个多智能体架构构造了一组中间消息
$$M_{\alpha}=g_{\alpha}(C),$$
where $g_{\alpha}$ may correspond to a structured transformation of the original context. The original DPI comparison only guarantees $I(Y;C)\geq I(Y;M_{\alpha})$ , not $I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha})\geq I(Y;M_{\alpha})$ . In other words, once effective single-agent context utilization deteriorates sufficiently, a well-designed MAS may recover task-relevant information more reliably than a degraded single pass, even though it still cannot exceed the ideal information available in $C$ .
其中，$g_{\alpha}$ 可以对应于对原始上下文的结构化变换。原始的数据处理不等式（DPI）比较仅保证 $I(Y;C)\geq I(Y;M_{\alpha})$，而不保证 $I(Y;\tilde{C}_{\alpha})\geq I(Y;M_{\alpha})$。换言之，一旦单智能体对有效上下文的利用能力下降到足够低的程度，设计良好的 MAS 就可能比退化的单次处理更可靠地恢复与任务相关的信息，尽管它仍然无法超越 $C$ 中理想情况下可用的信息。
This yields a concrete prediction for our experiments. Under low degradation, SAS should dominate because it retains access to the richest available representation of the task. As degradation increases, the SAS advantage may shrink, since the practical single-agent predictor now operates on an increasingly lossy effective context. In sufficiently degraded regimes, MAS and SAS may become comparable, and carefully structured MAS pipelines may occasionally surpass SAS by imposing useful factorization, filtering, or verification structure on the reasoning process.
这为我们的实验提供了一个具体预测。在退化程度较低时，SAS 应当占据优势，因为它仍能访问可用的最丰富的任务表示。随着退化程度增加，SAS 的优势可能缩小，因为此时实际的单智能体预测器所使用的有效上下文存在越来越多的信息损失。在退化足够严重的情况下，MAS 与 SAS 的表现可能趋于相当，而经过精心结构化设计的 MAS 流水线可能通过为推理过程引入有益的分解、过滤或验证结构，偶尔超越 SAS。
4 Approach4 方法
4.1 Tasks and Datasets4.1 任务与数据集
We evaluate on FRAMES (Krishna et al., 2025) and MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022), both are multi-hop world knowledge questions with concise ground truth answers. For MuSiQue we filter to only 4-hop questions, which are complex enough to challenge both SAS and MAS. We report accuracy via an LLM-as-judge rubric checking whether the ground truth appears or is semantically present in the model answer (see §4.4).
我们在 FRAMES (Krishna et al., 2025) 和 MuSiQue (Trivedi et al., 2022) 上进行评估，两者均包含具有简洁标准答案的多跳世界知识问题。对于 MuSiQue，我们仅保留 4 跳问题，这些问题足够复杂，能够对 SAS 和 MAS 都构成挑战。我们采用以 LLM 为评判者的评分标准来报告准确率，检查模型答案中是否出现标准答案，或是否在语义上包含标准答案（见 §4.4）。
4.2 Single-agent architecture4.2 单智能体架构
Our SAS pipeline is a single, direct pass. For a given question, the model receives a system prompt instructing it to “think step by step, then answer”. It is allocated the entire global thinking budget $B$ for a single call. The final answer is then extracted by taking the text that follows the </think> tag or Gemini API call.
我们的 SAS 流水线采用单次直接处理。对于给定问题，模型会收到一条系统提示，要求它“逐步思考，然后作答”。单次调用获分配全部全局思考预算 $B$。随后，通过提取 </think> 标签或 Gemini API 调用之后的文本，得到最终答案。
SAS with Longer Thinking Variant (SAS-L).延长思考的 SAS 变体（SAS-L）。
In §G we show that, for Gemini-2.5 (Flash/Pro), the visible thought text produced by SAS tends to plateau well below the requested budget, while MAS surfaces more visible thought content under the same requested budget $B$ , due to multiple calls. To make SAS produce more thinking output, we introduce a SAS variant that gently encourages more internal reasoning before answering, without changing budget $B$ or any other compute knobs.
在 §G 中，我们展示了对于 Gemini-2.5 (Flash/Pro)，SAS 生成的可见思考文本往往在远低于所请求预算的水平上趋于稳定，而在相同的请求预算 $B$ 下，MAS 由于进行了多次调用，会呈现更多可见的思考内容。为了让 SAS 产生更多思考输出，我们引入了一种 SAS 变体，在不改变预算 $B$ 或任何其他计算控制参数的情况下，温和地鼓励模型在作答前进行更多内部推理。
The variant keeps the same single-call SAS pipeline and the same requested thinking budget $B$ but augments the user message with a short, structured pre-answer analysis scaffold. Concretely, we add an extra instruction that asks the model to (i) identify ambiguities, (ii) propose interpretations, (iii) evaluate and select one, and only then (iv) answer.
该变体保留相同的单次调用 SAS 流水线和相同的请求思考预算 $B$，但在用户消息中加入了一个简短、结构化的作答前分析框架。具体而言，我们添加了一条额外指令，要求模型 (i) 识别歧义，(ii) 提出各种解释，(iii) 评估并选择其中一种，然后才 (iv) 作答。
4.3 Multi-agent Architectures4.3 多智能体架构
Our study includes one primary multi-agent baseline, Sequential, together with four additional multi-agent variants: Subtask-parallel, Parallel-roles, Debate, and Ensemble. All architectures operate under the same global thinking-token budget $B$ , with planner and aggregator components kept near budget-neutral whenever possible.
我们的研究包含一个主要的多智能体基线 Sequential，以及另外四种多智能体变体：Subtask-parallel、Parallel-roles、Debate 和 Ensemble。所有架构均在相同的全局思考 token 预算 $B$ 下运行，并尽可能使规划器和聚合器组件基本不占用额外预算。
Our full multi-agent family is as follows:
我们的完整多智能体架构系列如下：
Sequential: A planner decomposes the question into ordered steps, the budget is split across sequential workers, and an aggregator synthesizes the intermediate outputs.
Sequential：规划器将问题分解为有序步骤，预算分配给依次执行的工作智能体，聚合器则综合其中间输出。
Subtask-parallel: A planner proposes a small set of approximately independent subtasks, workers solve them in parallel under equal budget splits, and an aggregator combines their outputs.
Subtask-parallel：规划器提出一小组近似独立的子任务，工作智能体在均等分配的预算下并行求解，聚合器则合并它们的输出。
Parallel-roles: The full question is sent to role-specialized workers, Solver, Fact Extractor, Skeptic, and Second Solver. The Solver and Second Solver independently attempt to answer the question directly; the Fact Extractor identifies the key entities, constraints, and intermediate facts; and the Skeptic highlights possible pitfalls or alternative interpretations. The total budget $B$ is divided evenly across these roles. At the end, an aggregator synthesizes their outputs.
Parallel-roles：将完整问题发送给具有专门角色的工作智能体，即 Solver、Fact Extractor、Skeptic 和 Second Solver。Solver 和 Second Solver 各自独立尝试直接回答问题；Fact Extractor 识别关键实体、约束和中间事实；Skeptic 则指出可能的陷阱或其他解释。总预算 $B$ 在这些角色之间平均分配。最后，聚合器综合它们的输出。
Debate: Two debaters first answer independently, then critique one another. The total budget $B$ is divided evenly across these roles. Finally, an aggregator returns the final answer from the candidate answers and critiques.
Debate：两个辩论智能体首先独立作答，然后相互评议。总预算 $B$ 在这些角色之间平均分配。最后，聚合器根据候选答案和评议返回最终答案。
Ensemble: Multiple workers answer independently under equal budget splits with higher sampling temperature, and a judge selects the best candidate answer.
Ensemble：多个工作智能体在均等分配的预算下，以较高的采样温度独立作答，再由评判器选出最佳候选答案。

4.4 Evaluation Metric4.4 评估指标
We follow the standard LLM-as-a-judge paradigm, in which a separate evaluation model scores candidate answers according to a fixed natural-language rubric. Concretely, for each example we provide the judge with the question, the model’s prediction, and the ground-truth answer, together with a short decision rubric that specifies what counts as a correct answer (semantic equivalence, allowance for paraphrases, handling of minor formatting differences, etc.). The judge’s rubric and prompt are used for all datasets and for both SAS and MAS configurations, so any differences in accuracy arise from the systems being evaluated, not from the judge.[fn1]
我们遵循标准的大语言模型评判（LLM-as-a-judge）范式，由一个独立的评估模型根据固定的自然语言评分准则对候选答案进行评分。具体而言，对于每个样例，我们向评判模型提供问题、模型的预测答案和标准答案，以及一份简短的判定准则，其中规定了何种答案可视为正确答案（语义等价、允许改述、如何处理细微的格式差异等）。所有数据集以及 SAS 和 MAS 两种配置均使用相同的评判准则和提示词，因此，准确率的任何差异都源于被评估的系统，而非评判者。[fn1]

5.1 SAS vs. Multi-Agent Results Across Models and Datasets5.1 跨模型和数据集的 SAS 与多智能体结果比较
Our main finding is that, under matched thinking token budgets (except for very small budgets, which essentially do not produce any reasoning), SAS is the strongest default architecture for multi-hop reasoning. Across model families and datasets, SAS is the best-performing system or statistically indistinguishable from the best for all budgets except the lowest one (100 tokens), in which case the model does not produce a useful reasoning trace at all (for either approach). SAS also consumes much less thinking token than any MAS variants while achieving the same or better results (refer to Appendix F for full results with thinking token spent).
我们的主要发现是，在思考 token 预算相同的条件下（极小预算除外，因为此时基本不会产生任何推理），SAS 是多跳推理最强的默认架构。在不同模型系列和数据集上，除最低预算（100 tokens）外，SAS 在所有预算下都是表现最好的系统，或与最佳系统在统计上无显著差异；而在最低预算下，两种方法的模型都完全无法生成有用的推理轨迹。SAS 消耗的思考 token 也远少于任何 MAS 变体，同时取得相同或更好的结果（包含思考 token 消耗量的完整结果见附录 F）。
SAS-L improves accuracy mostly for Gemini models.SAS-L 主要提升了 Gemini 模型的准确率。
On Gemini-2.5-Flash, SAS-L is the strongest single-agent configuration in every MuSiQue panel, and its block averages remain above standard SAS throughout. On Gemini-2.5-Pro MuSiQue, SAS-L is also generally stronger than standard SAS, especially in higher thinking token budgets. By contrast, SAS-L is not a universal gain in the open-source setting. For Qwen3-30B-A3B, SAS-L is sometimes competitive in the low-budget panel, but standard SAS is usually stronger in the middle and high-budget ranges. In conclusion, SAS-L is mainly a Gemini effect: it helps when the Gemini models’ thinking channel appears underutilized, but it does not produce the same consistent benefit for the open-source runs.
对于 Gemini-2.5-Flash，SAS-L 在 MuSiQue 的每个子图中都是最强的单智能体配置，且其各预算区间的平均准确率始终高于标准 SAS。在 Gemini-2.5-Pro 的 MuSiQue 实验中，SAS-L 通常也强于标准 SAS，尤其是在较高的思考 token 预算下。相比之下，SAS-L 在开源模型设置下并不能普遍带来提升。对于 Qwen3-30B-A3B，SAS-L 在低预算子图中有时具有竞争力，但标准 SAS 在中、高预算范围内通常更强。总之，SAS-L 的效果主要体现在 Gemini 上：当 Gemini 模型的思考通道似乎未得到充分利用时，它会有所帮助，但在开源模型的实验中并未带来同样一致的收益。
Debate is the most consistently strong MAS variant. It repeatedly appears in the highest-confidence range, and on Gemini-2.5-Pro MuSiQue it is often the strongest overall MAS architecture. Parallel-roles is frequently the next strongest and is especially competitive on FRAMES and on Gemini-Pro settings. Ensemble is more dependent on thinking token budgets. It is usually weaker than Debate and often weaker than Parallel-roles on MuSiQue, especially at low and medium budgets. However, on Gemini-2.5-Pro FRAMES at high budgets, Ensemble becomes strong and even attains the best accuracies in the 5000 and 10000 thinking token budgets.
Debate 是表现最为稳定且强劲的 MAS 变体。它反复出现在置信度最高的范围内，并且在 Gemini-2.5-Pro 的 MuSiQue 实验中，往往是整体表现最强的 MAS 架构。Parallel-roles 通常是次强的架构，在 FRAMES 上以及 Gemini-Pro 设置下尤其具有竞争力。Ensemble 更依赖思考 token 预算。在 MuSiQue 上，它通常弱于 Debate，也往往弱于 Parallel-roles，尤其是在低、中预算下。然而，在 Gemini-2.5-Pro 的 FRAMES 实验中，Ensemble 在高预算下表现强劲，甚至在 5000 和 10000 的思考 token 预算下取得了最高准确率。
Performance improves as the thinking token budget increases and then flattens out. For many models/architectures, the 1000/2000 thinking token average is already close to the 5000/10000 thinking token average, even when the accuracies continue to fluctuate slightly. Although the trend is not perfectly monotonic, the results suggest diminishing returns from simply increasing the thinking budget beyond a certain point. At higher thinking token budgets, the models begin to saturate, and in some cases may over-explore or overthink rather than convert extra budget into better final answers.

5.3 Context degradation experiments5.3 上下文退化实验
Our theoretical analysis in Section 3 predicts that multi-agent designs can become more competitive when the single agent’s effective access to the full context is degraded. To probe this mechanism empirically, we explicitly control for context degradation for Qwen3-30B-A3B on MuSiQue with a fixed 1000-token budget. For each degradation level we evaluate SAS and Sequential MAS, holding all other settings fixed. Figure 3 visualizes the full pattern across all four degradation families.
我们在第 3 节中的理论分析预测，当单智能体对完整上下文的有效访问能力下降时，多智能体设计可能会更具竞争力。为通过实验探究这一机制，我们在固定 1000-token 预算下，使用 Qwen3-30B-A3B 在 MuSiQue 上显式控制上下文退化程度。对于每个退化程度，我们均评估 SAS 和 Sequential MAS，同时保持所有其他设置不变。图 3 直观展示了所有四类退化方式下的完整变化模式。
We experiment with four alternative degradation approaches: randomly delete a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens from the context before inference (deletion); randomly replace a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens with a mask symbol (masking); randomly replace a fraction $\alpha$ of tokens with random vocabulary tokens (substitution); and append $k$ random distractor sentences that are topically similar but irrelevant to the question (distractor).
我们实验考察了四种不同的退化方法：在推理前从上下文中随机删除比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元（删除）；将比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元随机替换为掩码符号（掩码）；将比例为 $\alpha$ 的词元随机替换为词表中的随机词元（替换）；以及追加 $k$ 个主题相似但与问题无关的随机干扰句（干扰）。
The strongest evidence comes from degradations that corrupt information rather than merely remove it. Under masking, SAS leads at mild degradation ( $\alpha=0.3$ ) and the systems are roughly tied at moderate degradation, but Sequential becomes better at heavy degradation ( $\alpha=0.7$ ). Under substitution, which both removes signal and injects misleading content, the crossover is even clearer: SAS is ahead at $\alpha=0.3$ , the two systems move closer at $\alpha=0.5$ , and Sequential is clearly better at $\alpha=0.7$ . These are precisely the settings in which a structured multi-step pipeline is more robust to degradation through filtering, decomposing, or stabilizing reasoning that would otherwise be derailed by corrupted context.
最有力的证据来自破坏信息而非仅仅移除信息的退化方式。在掩码条件下，SAS 在轻度退化（$\alpha=0.3$）时领先，两种系统在中度退化时大致持平，但在重度退化（$\alpha=0.7$）时，Sequential 表现更好。在既移除有效信号又注入误导性内容的替换条件下，性能优劣的反转更加明显：SAS 在 $\alpha=0.3$ 时领先，两种系统在 $\alpha=0.5$ 时的表现更加接近，而 Sequential 在 $\alpha=0.7$ 时明显更好。恰恰是在这些情境下，结构化的多步骤流程能够通过筛选、分解或稳定原本会被受损上下文扰乱的推理过程，对退化表现出更强的鲁棒性。
Deletion produces a weaker version of the same trend. SAS remains stronger at mild degradation, the systems overlap at moderate degradation, and SAS regains a small edge at the heaviest deletion level. Adding distractor sentences is milder still: both systems degrade, but SAS remains ahead throughout, albeit by a smaller margin than in the clean setting. Taken together, these results suggest that MAS is most helpful not simply when context is longer, but when it becomes harder for a single reasoning trajectory to distinguish relevant from misleading information.
删除条件下也呈现出相同的趋势，但程度较弱。SAS 在轻度退化时仍然表现更强，两种系统在中度退化时表现相当，而在最严重的删除程度下，SAS 又重新获得了小幅优势。添加干扰句的影响则更轻：两种系统的性能均有所下降，但 SAS 始终领先，只是领先幅度小于未受干扰的条件下。综合来看，这些结果表明，MAS 最有帮助的情境并不只是上下文更长，而是单一推理路径更难区分相关信息与误导性信息。
This degradation study complements the main results in an important way. Our claim is not that SAS always dominates in every regime. Rather, it is that under matched budgets and proper context utilization, SAS is usually the strongest default. The degradation experiments identify the boundary of that claim: when effective single-agent context utilization deteriorates enough, structured multi-agent reasoning becomes competitive and can occasionally be better.
这项退化研究从一个重要方面补充了主要结果。我们的主张并不是 SAS 在所有情境下都始终占优。相反，我们主张，在预算相同且上下文得到恰当利用的情况下，SAS 通常是表现最强的默认选择。退化实验明确了这一主张的适用边界：当单智能体有效利用上下文的能力下降到一定程度时，结构化的多智能体推理就会具有竞争力，并且有时能够表现得更好。

6 Conclusion6 结论
We presented a budget-controlled comparison of single-agent (SAS) and multi-agent (MAS) LLM systems, focusing on fixed thinking token budgets. Our results across two datasets (FRAMES, MuSiQue), three model families (Qwen3, DeepSeek, Gemini), and five different MAS architectures (Sequential, Debate, Ensemble, Parallel-roles, Subtask-parallel) consistently show that SAS matches or outperforms MAS when computation is normalized, unless context utilization is degraded to a certain point.
我们在控制预算的条件下，对单智能体（SAS）和多智能体（MAS）LLM 系统进行了比较，重点关注固定思考词元预算的情形。我们在两个数据集（FRAMES、MuSiQue）、三个模型系列（Qwen3、DeepSeek、Gemini）和五种不同的 MAS 架构（Sequential、Debate、Ensemble、Parallel-roles、Subtask-parallel）上得到的结果一致表明，在计算量相同的情况下，SAS 的表现与 MAS 相当或优于 MAS，除非上下文利用能力退化到一定程度。
Overall, our results suggest that many reported MAS gains are better explained by compute and context effects than by inherent architectural superiority, and that future work should focus on the specific regimes where multi-agent structure provides real benefit.
总体而言，我们的结果表明，许多已报告的 MAS 性能提升更适合用计算量和上下文的影响来解释，而非归因于架构固有的优越性；未来的研究应聚焦于多智能体结构能够带来实际收益的具体情境。

With this analysis, we can see that SAS succeeds by staying close to the question and reliably carrying the surfaced span into the final, while Sequential MAS succeeds when its extra breadth is paired with late constraint checking. Failures for both are dominated by: (i) never writing the correct surface form, and (ii) losing a previously correct span at finalization.
通过这一分析，我们可以看到，SAS 通过紧扣问题并可靠地将已找到的文本片段保留到最终答案中取得成功，而顺序式 MAS 则在额外的探索广度与后期约束检查相结合时取得成功。两者的失败主要表现为：(i) 始终未写出正确的表面形式，以及 (ii) 在生成最终答案时丢失了先前正确的文本片段。
Appendix C Limitations附录 C 局限性
(i) We focus on text-only multi-hop reasoning; MAS advantages with tools/vision or safety constraints are out of scope. (ii) Gemini thinking accounting is approximate; we report both API and content-based proxies and emphasize accuracy under matched requested budgets. (iii) We only study the effect on performance across model families and datasets while increasing the thinking token cap. We do not enforce the models to actually use up all of those budgets. As a result, we restrict only upper thinking budget cap, not lower thinking budget cap.
(i) 我们聚焦于纯文本多跳推理；MAS 在使用工具、视觉或安全约束条件下的优势不在研究范围内。(ii) Gemini 的思考量核算是近似的；我们同时报告基于 API 和基于内容的替代度量，并重点关注在所请求预算相匹配时的准确率。(iii) 我们仅研究提高思考 token 上限时，不同模型系列和数据集上的性能变化。我们并不强制模型实际用尽所有这些预算。因此，我们仅限制思考预算的上限，而不限制其下限。

As introduced in the paper, we use the official thinkingBudget parameter for Gemini models. However, official documentation [fn2] defines this as a guide rather than a hard cap and discloses model-specific min/max ranges and possible over/underflow. This aligns with community reports of mismatches between requested budget and observed output [fn3].
如正文所述，我们对 Gemini 模型使用官方的 thinkingBudget 参数。然而，官方文档 [fn2] 将其定义为指导性参考，而非硬性上限，并披露了各模型特定的最小值和最大值范围，以及实际用量可能高于或低于设定值的情况。这与社区关于请求的预算与实际观测到的输出不一致的报告相吻合 [fn3]。

Our own experiments confirm a significant and complex discrepancy between the API-reported thoughts_token_count and the actual token count of the visible thought content. We tracked three values for all Gemini experiments: (i) the API-reported token count, (ii) a content-based word count, and (iii) a proxy token count derived by multiplying the word count by $10/7$ [fn4].

Our analysis reveals three key findings:
我们的分析揭示了三个关键发现：
API-Reported counts are highly inflated at higher thinking budget. The thoughts_token_count returned by the API is often substantially larger than the token count of the visible text at higher thinking budget. For example, using Gemini-2.5-Flash with a requested 10k budget, the SAS pipeline reported an average of 1,687 thinking tokens. However, the visible thought content only contained an average of 251 words, a proxy for 359 tokens (see Table 7). This represents a 4.7x inflation factor, suggesting the API may be counting internal reasoning steps that are never externalized in the text.
在较高的思考预算下，API 报告的计数严重偏高。在较高的思考预算下，API 返回的 thoughts_token_count 往往显著大于可见文本的词元数。例如，使用 Gemini-2.5-Flash 并请求 10k 预算时，SAS 流水线报告的思考词元数平均为 1,687。然而，可见思考内容平均仅包含 251 个单词，对应的词元数代理值为 359（见表 7）。这相当于 4.7x 的膨胀系数，表明 API 可能将从未在文本中显式呈现的内部推理步骤也计入其中。
Visible thought content plateaus. We observe that for the standard SAS prompt and Sequential MAS, the actual length of the visible thought text (our proxy) hits a ceiling and stops growing, even as the requested budget increases. For Gemini-2.5-Flash SAS, the visible thought proxy plateaus at $\approx$ 350 tokens. It is 354 tokens at a 1k budget and 359 tokens at a 10k budget (see Table 7). This suggests that simply increasing the thinkingBudget parameter does not guarantee more extensive reasoning, even when the API reports so. Our SAS with longer thinking variant successfully produced more visible text (e.g., 479 tokens at 10k), confirming that prompt-level incentives are also critical.
可见思考内容趋于饱和。我们观察到，对于标准 SAS 提示和顺序式 MAS，即使请求的预算增加，可见思考文本的实际长度（我们的代理指标）也会达到上限并停止增长。对于 Gemini-2.5-Flash SAS，可见思考内容的词元数代理值在 $\approx$ 350 个词元处趋于饱和。在 1k 预算下，该值为 354 个词元；在 10k 预算下，该值为 359 个词元（见表 7）。这表明，单纯增加 thinkingBudget 参数并不能保证进行更充分的推理，即使 API 报告显示如此。我们采用更长思考的 SAS 变体成功生成了更多可见文本（例如，在 10k 预算下生成了 479 个词元），证实提示层面的激励同样至关重要。
Sequential MAS produces more visible thought text than SAS. At a matched requested budget, the Sequential MAS pipeline consistently produced more total visible thought text than the SAS pipeline. At a 1k budget on Pro, MAS produced 693 proxy tokens vs. SAS’s 390 (see Table 8). This is likely because the Sequential MAS pipeline executes $k$ separate agent calls, and the final thought text is a concatenation of $k$ distinct (and potentially truncated) thought blocks, leading to more total text output.
顺序式 MAS 生成的可见思考文本多于 SAS。在请求预算相同的情况下，顺序式 MAS 流水线生成的可见思考文本总量始终多于 SAS 流水线。在 Pro 上采用 1k 预算时，MAS 生成的词元数代理值为 693，而 SAS 为 390（见表 8）。这很可能是因为顺序式 MAS 流水线执行了 $k$ 次独立的智能体调用，而最终的思考文本由 $k$ 个不同的（且可能被截断的）思考块拼接而成，因此文本输出总量更多。
These accounting artifacts make a direct “apples-to-apples” compute comparison based on observed tokens (either API-reported or content-based) intractable. The API-reported count is unreliable, and the proxy visible token is non-linear with the requested budget.
这些计数机制带来的偏差，使得基于观测词元数（无论是 API 报告的计数还是基于内容的计数）直接进行同等条件下的计算量比较难以实现。API 报告的计数不可靠，而可见词元数代理值与请求预算之间呈非线性关系。
Therefore, our primary analysis intentionally and necessarily compares configurations based on their matched requested budget $B$ . This is the only variable that can be directly controlled by the researcher. We argue this remains the most fair and reproducible method for evaluation, even if the underlying models utilize that budget in different and opaque ways. We strongly recommend researchers to acknowledge this discrepancy and call on API providers to clarify how thoughts_token_count is calculated.
因此，我们的主要分析有意且有必要基于相同的请求预算 $B$ 来比较不同配置。这是研究者唯一能够直接控制的变量。我们认为，即使底层模型以不同且不透明的方式使用该预算，这仍然是最公平且最具可复现性的评估方法。我们强烈建议研究者正视这一差异，并呼吁 API 提供商澄清 thoughts_token_count 的计算方式。
