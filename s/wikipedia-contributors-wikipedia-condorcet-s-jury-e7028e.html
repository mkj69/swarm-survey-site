孔多塞陪审团定理（Condorcet's jury theorem）是一条统计学定理，研究给定的一群人作出正确决策的概率。 Condorcet's jury theorem is a statistical theorem about the probability of a given group of individuals arriving at a correct decision. 该定理由孔多塞侯爵于1785年在其著作《论将分析应用于多数决策概率》中首次提出。 The theorem was first expressed by the Marquis de Condorcet in his 1785 work Essay on the Application of Analysis to the Probability of Majority Decisions.
该定理假设，一个群体希望通过多数投票作出决策。 The assumptions of the theorem are that a group wishes to reach a decision by majority vote. 投票的两种结果中有一种是正确的，每位投票者以概率 p 投出支持正确决策的一票，且彼此独立。 One of the two outcomes of the vote is correct, and each voter has an independent probability p of voting for the correct decision. 该定理探讨的是，我们应当在群体中纳入多少位投票者。 The theorem asks how many voters we should include in the group. 结果取决于 p 是大于还是小于 1/2： The result depends on whether p is greater than or less than 1/2:
如果 p 大于 1/2（每位投票者更有可能投出正确的一票），那么增加投票者人数会提高多数决策正确的概率。 If p is greater than 1/2 (each voter is more likely to vote correctly), then adding more voters increases the probability that the majority decision is correct. 在极限情况下，随着投票者人数增加，多数票正确的概率趋近于 1。 In the limit, the probability that the majority votes correctly approaches 1 as the number of voters increases.
另一方面，如果 p 小于 1/2（每位投票者更有可能投出错误的一票），那么增加投票者人数会使情况变得更糟：最优的陪审团仅由一位投票者组成。 On the other hand, if p is less than 1/2 (each voter is more likely to vote incorrectly), then adding more voters makes things worse: the optimal jury consists of a single voter.
自孔多塞以来，许多其他研究者通过放宽孔多塞的部分或全部假设，证明了各种其他陪审团定理。 Since Condorcet, many other researchers have proved various other jury theorems, relaxing some or all of Condorcet's assumptions.

为避免需要采用平票裁决规则，我们假设 n 为奇数。 To avoid the need for a tie-breaking rule, we assume n is odd. 如果通过增加一位投票者来打破平票，那么基本相同的论证也适用于 n 为偶数的情况。 Essentially the same argument works for even n if ties are broken by adding a single voter.

m 原本距离在 n 票中占据多数还差一票，但新增的两位投票者都投出了正确的一票。 m was one vote too small to get a majority of the n votes, but both new voters voted correctly.
m 原本恰好达到 n 票中的最小多数票数，但新增的两位投票者都投出了错误的一票。 m was just equal to a majority of the n votes, but both new voters voted incorrectly.
其余情况下，新增的选票要么相互抵消，要么仅扩大票数差距，要么不足以改变结果。 The rest of the time, either the new votes cancel out, only increase the gap, or do not make enough of a difference. 因此，我们只需关注最初 n 票中正确与错误的多数结果仅由一票之差决定的情况。 So we only care what happens when a single vote (among the first n) separates a correct from an incorrect majority.
将注意力限定在这种情况下，我们可以设想前 n−1 票相互抵消，而决定性的一票由第 n 位投票者投出。 Restricting our attention to this case, we can imagine that the first n−1 votes cancel out and that the deciding vote is cast by the nth voter. 在这种情况下，获得正确多数结果的概率就是 p。 In this case the probability of getting a correct majority is just p. 现在假设我们让新增的两位投票者参与投票。 Now suppose we send in the two extra voters. 他们将错误的多数结果变为正确的多数结果的概率为 (1−p)p2，而将正确的多数结果变为错误的多数结果的概率为 p(1−p)2。 The probability that they change an incorrect majority to a correct majority is (1−p)p2, while the probability that they change a correct majority to an incorrect majority is p(1−p)2. 当且仅当 p > 1/2 时，第一个概率大于第二个概率，由此证明了该定理。 The first of these probabilities is greater than the second if and only if p > 1/2, proving the theorem.

该定理在其他学科中的应用The theorem in other disciplines
近年来，孔多塞陪审团定理（Condorcet jury theorem）已被用于构建评分整合的概念框架，适用于多名医师阅片者（放射科医师、内镜医师等）独立评估影像以判断疾病活动度的情形。 The Condorcet jury theorem has recently been used to conceptualize score integration when several physician readers (radiologists, endoscopists, etc.) independently evaluate images for disease activity. 这项任务出现在临床试验期间开展的集中阅片中，与投票具有相似之处。 This task arises in central reading performed during clinical trials and has similarities to voting. 作者指出，应用该定理可以将各阅片者的评分转化为最终评分，其方式既在数学上合理（避免对有序数据取平均值），又便于开展进一步的数学分析，同时也符合当前评分任务的性质（基于对某些特征存在与否的判断，属于主观分类任务）。 According to the authors, the application of the theorem can translate individual reader scores into a final score in a fashion that is both mathematically sound (by avoiding averaging of ordinal data), mathematically tractable for further analysis, and in a manner that is consistent with the scoring task at hand (based on decisions about the presence or absence of features, a subjective classification task).
孔多塞陪审团定理也被用于机器学习领域的集成学习。 The Condorcet jury theorem is also used in ensemble learning in the field of machine learning. 集成方法通过多数投票，将多个独立分类器的预测结果结合起来。 An ensemble method combines the predictions of many individual classifiers by majority voting. 假设每个单独分类器的预测准确率都略高于 50%，且它们的预测相互独立，那么将它们的预测集成后，预测表现将远远优于各个分类器单独的预测表现。 Assuming that each of the individual classifiers predict with slightly greater than 50% accuracy and their predictions are independent, then the ensemble of their predictions will be far greater than their individual predictive scores.
