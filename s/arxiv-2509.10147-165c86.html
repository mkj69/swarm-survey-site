Abstract摘要
The rapid adoption of autonomous AI agents is giving rise to a new economic layer where agents transact and coordinate at scales and speeds beyond direct human oversight. We propose the "sandbox economy" as a framework for analyzing this emergent system, characterizing it along two key dimensions: its origins (emergent vs. intentional) and its degree of separateness from the established human economy (permeable vs. impermeable). Our current trajectory points toward a spontaneous emergence of a vast and highly permeable AI agent economy, presenting us with opportunities for an unprecedented degree of coordination as well as significant challenges, including systemic economic risk and exacerbated inequality. Here we discuss a number of possible design choices that may lead to safely steerable AI agent markets. In particular, we consider auction mechanisms for fair resource allocation and preference resolution, the design of AI "mission economies" to coordinate around achieving collective goals, and socio-technical infrastructure needed to ensure trust, safety, and accountability. By doing this, we argue for the proactive design of steerable agent markets to ensure the coming technological shift aligns with humanity’s long-term collective flourishing.
自主 AI 智能体的迅速普及正在催生一个新的经济层，智能体在其中开展交易与协调，其规模和速度超出了人类直接监督的范围。我们提出以“沙盒经济”（sandbox economy）作为分析这一新兴系统的框架，并从两个关键维度刻画它：其起源（自发涌现与有意构建）以及其与既有人类经济的分离程度（可渗透与不可渗透）。我们当前的发展轨迹指向一个庞大且高度可渗透的 AI 智能体经济的自发涌现，这既为我们提供了实现前所未有的协调程度的机遇，也带来了重大挑战，包括系统性经济风险和不平等加剧。本文讨论了若干可能促成可安全引导的 AI 智能体市场的设计选择。具体而言，我们考察了用于公平分配资源和协调偏好的拍卖机制、围绕实现集体目标开展协调的 AI“使命经济”（mission economies）的设计，以及确保信任、安全与问责所需的社会技术基础设施。通过这些讨论，我们主张主动设计可引导的智能体市场，以确保即将到来的技术变革符合人类长期共同繁荣的目标。

Introduction引言
Current technological trajectories could potentially lead to a global economy in which autonomous AI agents interact with one another to generate economic value independently of human labor. This kind of development in the history of technological change is notable—historically technological advancement has been driven by inflexible inventions that improve productivity in narrow domains, one or a few at a time (Mokyr et al., 2015). AI agents, by contrast, could take the form of ‘flexible’ capital, able to automate a diversity of cognitive tasks across industries and occupations (Eloundou et al., 2024). In fact, recent advances in multi-modal foundation models have enabled the development of a wide range of different agentic AI systems (Sager et al., 2025; Hettiarachchi, 2025). The capabilities of these agents range from generic to highly specialized, enabling them to occupy different roles in the economy e.g. acting as personal AI assistants (Gabriel et al., 2024) to help users complete tasks and stay informed. Recent work on AI as Economic Agents (Hadfield and Koh, 2025) argues that AI agents are likely to be deployed within both the public and private sector, where they may help automate business processes and interactions.
当前的技术发展轨迹可能催生一种全球经济，其中自主 AI 智能体通过彼此互动，在不依赖人类劳动的情况下创造经济价值。在技术变迁史上，这类发展尤为引人注目——从历史上看，技术进步一直由缺乏灵活性的发明所推动，这些发明每次只能提高一个或少数几个狭窄领域的生产率 (Mokyr et al., 2015)。相比之下，AI 智能体可能成为一种“灵活的”资本，能够将跨行业、跨职业的多种认知任务自动化 (Eloundou et al., 2024)。事实上，多模态基础模型的近期进展已经促成了多种不同类型的智能体式 AI 系统的开发 (Sager et al., 2025; Hettiarachchi, 2025)。这些智能体的能力涵盖从通用到高度专门化的范围，使它们能够在经济中承担不同角色，例如充当个人 AI 助手 (Gabriel et al., 2024)，帮助用户完成任务并及时获取信息。近期关于 AI 作为经济智能体的研究 (Hadfield and Koh, 2025) 认为，AI 智能体很可能被部署到公共部门和私营部门，帮助实现业务流程与互动的自动化。
While the full scope of future agentic AI application remains uncertain, there are already numerous efforts aimed at developing and integrating AI agents across a broad set of use cases such as education (Jiang et al., 2024), legal services (Magni and Milella, 2025), software engineering (Liu et al., 2024a), healthcare (Tu et al., 2025; Patel et al., 2025; Saab et al., 2025; Vedadi et al., 2025) mental health (Cruz-Gonzalez et al., 2025), scientific research (Gottweis et al., 2025), government services (House of Commons Committee of Public Accounts, 2025), and more traditional commercial roles in various sectors (Sager et al., 2025).
尽管未来智能体式 AI 的完整应用范围仍不确定，但目前已有大量工作致力于在广泛的应用场景中开发和整合 AI 智能体，例如教育 (Jiang et al., 2024)、法律服务 (Magni and Milella, 2025)、软件工程 (Liu et al., 2024a)、医疗保健 (Tu et al., 2025; Patel et al., 2025; Saab et al., 2025; Vedadi et al., 2025)、心理健康 (Cruz-Gonzalez et al., 2025)、科学研究 (Gottweis et al., 2025)、政府服务 (House of Commons Committee of Public Accounts, 2025)，以及各行业中更为传统的商业角色 (Sager et al., 2025)。
One of the key features that distinguishes modern AI agents from the more specialized systems of the past is their autonomy (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025). The ongoing explosion of agentic systems, coupled with the development of new interoperability standards like the Agent2Agent (A2A) and Model Context Protocol (MCP), signals the inevitable emergence of a new economic layer. We can conceptualize this emergent ecosystem as a "virtual agent economy" or "sandbox economy". The latter terminology foreshadows our intention – which is to ensure that AI agents operate safely within this economic layer. These emerging agent (sandbox) economies can vary along two key dimensions: (1) the nature of their origins and (2) the permeability of their boundaries. Their origins can be intentional, deliberately constructed for purposes like safe experimentation, or spontaneous, emerging as a de facto consequence of widespread technological adoption. Independently, their economic boundaries can be impermeable, hermetically sealing them from the established human economy, or permeable, allowing for porous interaction and transaction with it by external actors. Early proposals for AI sandboxes have been both intentional and permeable, however, the regulatory landscape is rapidly shifting (IMDA, 2025; House, 2025). It is likely that future decision making over sandbox permeability will be sector specific—‘higher risk’ sectors may require agents that are first tested in an impermeable sandbox environment, while low-risk sectors may not require this.
自主性是现代 AI 智能体区别于过去那些更为专门化的系统的关键特征之一 (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025)。智能体系统持续爆发式增长，加之 Agent2Agent (A2A) 和 Model Context Protocol (MCP) 等新互操作性标准的发展，预示着一个新的经济层将不可避免地出现。我们可以将这一新兴生态系统概念化为“虚拟智能体经济”或“沙盒经济”。后一术语预示了我们的意图——确保 AI 智能体在这一经济层内安全运行。这些新兴的智能体（沙盒）经济可以沿两个关键维度呈现差异：(1) 其起源的性质，以及 (2) 其边界的可渗透性。它们的起源可以是有意的，即为安全实验等目的而刻意构建；也可以是自发的，即作为技术广泛普及的事实性结果而涌现。独立于起源这一维度，它们的经济边界可以是不可渗透的，使其与既有人类经济完全隔绝；也可以是可渗透的，允许外部参与者跨越边界与其开展互动和交易。早期的 AI 沙盒提案既是有意构建的，也是可渗透的；然而，监管格局正在迅速变化 (IMDA, 2025; House, 2025)。未来关于沙盒可渗透性的决策很可能因行业而异——“较高风险”行业可能要求智能体先在不可渗透的沙盒环境中接受测试，而低风险行业可能没有这一要求。
One of the key motivations for designing an intentional economic AI agent sandbox would be to achieve a large degree of insulation, and possibly full impermeability of the agent economy, so as to prevent any arising instabilities from rapidly spilling over into the established human economy—an event which would have consequences that could be hard to anticipate or pre-emptively mitigate. Yet, ultimately, both impermeable and permeable sandbox economies are possible, and both may arise either intentionally or spontaneously. These two dimensions map critical features of both the economic problems we face and the solutions we might design. This paper proceeds from the assumption that unless a change is made, our current trajectory points toward the accidental emergence of a vast, and likely permeable, sandbox economy. Our central challenge, therefore, is not whether to create this ecosystem, but how to architect it to ensure it is steerable, safe, and aligned with user and community goals.
有意设计一个经济型 AI 智能体沙盒的关键动机之一，是使智能体经济在很大程度上与外界隔离，甚至可能实现完全不可渗透，从而防止其中出现的任何不稳定性迅速蔓延至既有的人类经济——此类事件的后果可能难以预料，也难以事先缓解。然而，归根结底，不可渗透和可渗透的沙盒经济都有可能存在，而且两者都可能是有意构建的，也可能是自发形成的。这两个维度刻画了我们所面临的经济问题以及我们可能设计的解决方案的关键特征。本文以如下假设为出发点：除非作出改变，否则我们当前的发展轨迹将指向一个规模庞大且很可能具有可渗透性的沙盒经济的意外涌现。因此，我们面临的核心挑战并非是否创建这一生态系统，而是如何设计其架构，以确保它可引导、安全，并与用户和社群的目标保持一致。
A fully permeable and emergent sandbox would be, in practice, functionally equivalent to AI agents simply participating in the existing human economy. The “sandbox” terminology is useful, however, because it allows us to contrast this default trajectory with other possibilities, such as intentionally designed and impermeable agent economies created for safe experimentation. It also highlights that some degree of impermeability may (or may not) emerge naturally (e.g. if there are practical difficulties in transacting between humans and AIs). The framework highlights that permeability is the critical and controllable design variable. Importantly, an economy’s degree of permeability is a collective property: while it results from human choices, it is not under the control of any single actor (Schelling, 1973). This means that changes to the extent of permeability can only be brought about by resolving a collective action problem—suggesting that the difficulty of coordinating is what makes intentional design of impermeability a significant challenge, reinforcing the default path we are on toward the accidental emergence of a permeable AI agent economy.
一个完全可渗透且自发涌现的沙盒，在实践中，其功能将等同于 AI 智能体直接参与现有的人类经济。不过，“沙盒”这一术语仍然有用，因为它使我们能够将这一默认发展轨迹与其他可能性进行对比，例如为安全实验而有意设计并创建的不可渗透的智能体经济。它也凸显出，某种程度的不可渗透性可能会自然形成，也可能不会（例如，当人类与 AI 之间的交易存在实际困难时）。这一框架凸显出，可渗透性是关键且可控的设计变量。重要的是，一个经济体的可渗透程度是一种集体属性：尽管它源于人类的选择，却不受任何单一行为主体的控制（Schelling, 1973）。这意味着，只有解决集体行动问题，才能改变可渗透程度——这表明，协调的困难使得有意设计不可渗透性成为一项重大挑战，从而进一步强化了我们当前所处的默认发展路径，即一个可渗透的 AI 智能体经济的意外涌现。
There is today an opportunity to architect the rules and incentives of the future AI agent economy in a way that prioritizes achieving beneficial societal outcomes. In this paper we examine key architectural choices, including the use of market-based mechanisms like auctions for achieving fair resource allocation and aligning agent preferences. We investigate how these systems can be used to establish “mission economies” that enable coordination of vast computational resources toward solving humanity’s most pressing challenges (Mazzucato, 2018). Finally, we outline the technical and governance infrastructure—such as verifiable credentials for establishing trust—required to safely and robustly scale agentic AI deployments. By exploring these frameworks, we offer a vision for establishing steerable agent markets designed to enable long-term human well-being and flourishing.
如今，我们有机会设计未来 AI 智能体经济的规则与激励机制，将实现有益的社会结果作为优先目标。本文考察了关键的架构选择，包括利用拍卖等基于市场的机制，实现公平的资源配置并使智能体的偏好相互协调。我们探讨如何利用这些系统建立“使命型经济”（mission economies），以协调海量计算资源，用于解决人类最紧迫的挑战（Mazzucato, 2018）。最后，我们概述了安全、稳健地扩大智能体式 AI 部署规模所需的技术与治理基础设施，例如用于建立信任的可验证凭证。通过探索这些框架，我们提出了建立可引导的智能体市场的愿景，旨在促进人类的长期福祉与繁荣。
Example Scenarios示例场景
We now look at several examples representing types of virtual AI agent economies that may emerge, to ground and inform our subsequent discussion.
下面，我们考察几个代表可能涌现的虚拟 AI 智能体经济类型的例子，为后续讨论提供具体依据与启示。
Accelerating science. Arguably one of the most valuable ways in which we can envision future advanced AI systems help advance the interests of humanity is through facilitating accelerated scientific progress, through an open-ended loop of ideation, experimentation and refinement. This is especially relevant given that the rate of scientific progress has been seeing a slow-down in recent years (Park et al., 2023; Jin et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025). Even at the current AI agent capability level, there are promising early systems that aim to automate scientific research (Singh et al., 2025). Yet, even should these AI agents individually become capable of all these things, (AI) science will likely remain a collaborative endeavor, requiring coordination and integration across systems. After all, scientific experiments tend to involve non-negligible material and energy resources, and in some cases also interface with human subjects—requiring their time as well as necessitating consent. Different scientific questions also map onto different corresponding technologies in terms of their domain of applicability, making some people benefit more, and some less, from any particular advance. Furthermore, the process of answering a question may itself involve accessing proprietary scientific tools, simulators, or data held by private and public organizations. It stands to reason that advanced AI agents engaging in the process of scientific discovery on behalf of their respective organizations would require means to compensate other agents when securing these resources. This is not conceptually unlike how funding is currently allocated in sciences, given that individual proposals are scrutinized and prioritized for their merits. Yet, here it can happen much faster, and on a more fine-grained scale. Blockchain (Zheng et al., 2018) technologies may prove particularly useful here in terms of credit assignment (Zhu et al., 2021), enabling fair distributed benefits for the work performed.
加速科学发展。可以说，在我们所能设想的未来先进 AI 系统增进人类利益的方式中，最有价值的方式之一，是通过构思、实验与改进的开放式循环，推动科学进步加速。鉴于近年来科学进步的速度有所放缓，这一点尤为重要（Park et al., 2023; Jin et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2025）。即便以 AI 智能体目前的能力水平，也已出现了一些旨在实现科学研究自动化、颇具前景的早期系统（Singh et al., 2025）。然而，即使这些 AI 智能体单独就能完成所有这些工作，（AI）科学研究很可能仍将是一项协作事业，需要跨系统的协调与整合。毕竟，科学实验往往涉及不可忽视的物质和能源资源，有时还会涉及人类受试者——既需要占用他们的时间，也必须征得他们的同意。不同的科学问题也对应着适用领域各异的技术，因此，任何一项具体进展都会使一些人受益较多，另一些人受益较少。此外，解答问题的过程本身可能需要使用私营机构和公共机构持有的专有科学工具、模拟器或数据。可以合理推断，代表各自所属组织开展科学发现活动的先进 AI 智能体，在获取这些资源时，将需要具备向其他智能体支付补偿的手段。从概念上看，这与当前科学研究中的经费分配方式并无不同，因为每项提案也都要依据其价值接受审查并确定优先次序。然而，在这里，这一过程可能发生得快得多，而且尺度也更为精细。区块链（Zheng et al., 2018）技术在此处的贡献归属（credit assignment）方面可能尤为有用（Zhu et al., 2021），使参与者能够根据所完成的工作公平地分享收益。
Robotics. It is likely that future advances in robotics will unlock numerous ways in which then embodied advanced AI agents may help us execute tasks that are challenging, dangerous (Pedersen et al., 2003; Trevelyan et al., 2016), or simply boring and repetitive (Liang et al., 2020). Given that executing physical tasks comes with a greater energy expenditure and resource utilization, and that each robot can only be in one place at the same time, coordination and optimization in multi-agent robotics systems is of particular importance. An agent A may thus make a request to agent B to execute a task in its vicinity on A’s behalf, and compensate B for the time and the energy consumed, should B accept to re-prioritize its own schedule to accommodate the request. In evaluating whether to accept or reject the request, and in rendering the execution plan, B may communicate with an online non-embodied agent C, with access to more global information on the placement of other agents. Agent C may then, at a fee, disclose a summary of this information to B to help evaluate whether the established price is fair, and whether it should consider taking up a different request instead. Both A and B may through their sensors collect important information and exchange a summary of such information with C. When transacting, blockchain technology may enable C to provide verifiable evidence of how much information it had traded and accumulated, giving credence to its claim that it may approximate the global state sufficiently well for its insights to hold value for A and B.
机器人技术。未来机器人技术的进步很可能开辟多种途径，使届时具备实体的先进 AI 智能体能够帮助我们执行具有挑战性、危险性（Pedersen et al., 2003; Trevelyan et al., 2016），或只是枯燥且重复的任务（Liang et al., 2020）。鉴于执行物理任务需要消耗更多能量和资源，而且每个机器人同一时间只能处于一个地点，多智能体机器人系统中的协调与优化尤为重要。因此，智能体 A 可以请求智能体 B 代其执行 B 附近的一项任务；如果 B 同意重新调整自身日程的优先级以满足该请求，A 就会补偿 B 为此消耗的时间和能量。在评估是否接受该请求以及制定执行计划时，B 可以与在线的非具身智能体 C 进行交流，后者能够获取有关其他智能体位置的更全面的全局信息。随后，智能体 C 可以向 B 收费提供这些信息的摘要，帮助 B 评估既定价格是否公平，以及是否应考虑转而接受另一项请求。A 和 B 都可以通过各自的传感器收集重要信息，并与 C 交换此类信息的摘要。在交易时，区块链技术可以使 C 提供可验证的证据，证明其已交易和积累了多少信息，从而为其主张提供可信依据，即它能够足够准确地近似刻画全局状态，使其见解对 A 和 B 具有价值。
Personal assistants. The proliferation of user-facing AI assistants and the rapid advances in personalization, memory integration, tools use (and computer use), as well as instruction following and multi-turn interactions, makes it imperative to consider how personal AI assistants would potentially benefit from, and take part in a virtual agent economy. For all practical purposes, this is likely to be one of the first use cases to interface with this kind of infrastructure. Let us imagine an agent A acting on behalf of the user $U_{A}$ and agent B acting on behalf of the user $U_{B}$ . Perhaps, in this particular instance, both $U_{A}$ and $U_{B}$ desire to book a vacation, and have overlapping preferences, leading to A and B attempting to book the same accommodation for the same given date, via the agent C. Beyond simply having their current request, A and B are individually familiar with the overall preferences of $U_{A}$ and $U_{B}$ , both in terms of this specific request, as well as other pending requests that they are meant to execute. Maybe being close to the beach is more important to $U_{A}$ , whereas having a good public transport connection to other areas is preferred by $U_{B}$ . Perhaps $U_{A}$ requires a hotel with a fitness center, whereas $U_{B}$ doesn’t. In any case, A and B may choose to negotiate these preferences with C and bid for the accommodation of their choosing. Depending on how much they are willing to prioritize this particular request, A or B may choose to yield on some of the preferences, and get compensated for that via the virtual agent currency. Perhaps it is A that yields, and B ends up paying A an appropriate amount for the concession. A can then utilize this amount to potentially compensate other agents in other pending requests, especially if those requests come at a higher priority, as they are of a higher significance to the respective user $U_{A}$ .
个人助理。面向用户的 AI 助理日益普及，个性化、记忆整合、工具使用（以及计算机使用）、指令遵循和多轮交互等能力也在迅速发展，因此，必须考虑个人 AI 助理可能如何从虚拟智能体经济中受益并参与其中。从实际应用的角度来看，这很可能是最早接入此类基础设施的用例之一。设想智能体 A 代表用户 $U_{A}$ 行事，而智能体 B 代表用户 $U_{B}$ 行事。也许，在这个具体情境中，$U_{A}$ 和 $U_{B}$ 都希望预订度假行程，而且偏好有所重叠，导致 A 和 B 试图通过智能体 C 预订同一指定日期的同一处住宿。除了掌握用户当前的请求外，A 和 B 还分别熟悉 $U_{A}$ 和 $U_{B}$ 的总体偏好，既包括与这一具体请求相关的偏好，也包括与其应执行的其他待处理请求相关的偏好。也许，靠近海滩对 $U_{A}$ 更为重要，而 $U_{B}$ 则更偏好拥有便捷公共交通、方便前往其他地区的住宿。也许 $U_{A}$ 需要一家设有健身中心的酒店，而 $U_{B}$ 则没有这一要求。无论如何，A 和 B 都可以选择就这些偏好与 C 协商，并竞价争取各自选中的住宿。根据其愿意赋予这一特定请求多高的优先级，A 或 B 可以选择在某些偏好上作出让步，并通过虚拟智能体货币获得相应补偿。也许最终是 A 作出让步，而 B 为这一让步向 A 支付适当金额的补偿。随后，A 可以利用这笔金额，在处理其他待处理请求时向其他智能体支付补偿，尤其是当这些请求对其所代表的用户 $U_{A}$ 更为重要、因而具有更高优先级时。
Sandboxes沙盒
By the term “sandbox economy” we mean to describe a set of linked digital markets where AI agents transact with one another. The sandbox may never be fully sealed off from the real (human) economy. At least, not if it is to have value. So there will always be points of interface between the sandbox and the human economy. We refer to the extent to which developments in the sandbox can influence the outside, and developments outside can influence the sandbox as the sandbox’s permeability.
我们用“沙盒经济”一词来描述一组相互关联的数字市场，AI 智能体在其中彼此交易。沙盒可能永远无法与现实的（人类）经济完全隔绝。至少，如果沙盒要有价值，就无法做到这一点。因此，沙盒与人类经济之间始终会存在接口。我们将沙盒内部的发展能够影响外部、以及外部的发展能够影响沙盒的程度，称为沙盒的渗透性。
Since autonomous AI systems can make thousands of decisions in an instant, it is important to have the appropriate guardrails in place in advance, since real-time human oversight will often be impossible. Appropriate guardrails can convert a permeable sandbox economy into a relatively impermeable sandbox economy.
由于自主 AI 系统能够在瞬间作出数千项决策，提前设置适当的防护措施十分重要，因为人类往往无法进行实时监督。适当的防护措施可以将具有渗透性的沙盒经济转变为相对不具渗透性的沙盒经济。
Given that advanced AI agents and assistants may also repeatedly interact with human users (Mu et al., 2024), careful governance of the interfaces between human and AI interaction networks will be needed to achieve safety and alignment with human preferences. A sandbox economy must be coupled with technical and legislative frameworks and infrastructure to enable oversight, ensure safety, enforce verifiability, and help individuals, groups, and societies coordinate and direct AI agents towards positive outcomes, while mitigating any arising harms and adverse effects that may emerge.
鉴于先进 AI 智能体和助理也可能与人类用户反复互动（Mu et al., 2024），需要审慎治理人类交互网络与 AI 交互网络之间的接口，以实现安全性以及与人类偏好的对齐。沙盒经济必须与技术和立法框架及基础设施相结合，以实现监督、确保安全、强制落实可验证性，并帮助个人、群体和社会协调和引导 AI 智能体，使其产生积极结果，同时减轻可能出现的任何危害和不利影响。
Opportunities机遇
With permeable sandbox economies, there will always be some risk of contagion in which a crisis in the sandbox sparks a crisis in the real economy. Managing such risks necessitates multi-faceted innovation in markets and mechanism design, and should couple technology with policy work and appropriate regulation.
对于具有渗透性的沙盒经济，始终存在一定的传染风险，即沙盒中的危机引发现实经济中的危机。管理此类风险需要在市场和机制设计方面开展多维度创新，并应将技术与政策工作及适当监管相结合。
Of course, impermeability cannot be guaranteed without a number of institutional and infrastructural choices, and may only ever be partial in the best of circumstances. It depends, for instance, on the total fraction of all economic activity that comes to be channeled through the sandbox. Nevertheless (semi-)isolated digital market partitions (guardrails), coupled with the appropriate technological and legislative oversight, may help limit contagion from potential AI agent market instabilities and failures, while also offering opportunities for driving agent coordination at scale, given that additional incentives may be incorporated in AI agent market design.
当然，如果不在制度和基础设施方面作出一系列选择，就无法保证不渗透性，而且即使在最理想的情况下，也可能只能实现部分不渗透性。例如，这取决于经由沙盒开展的经济活动占全部经济活动的总体比例。尽管如此，鉴于 AI 智能体市场设计中可以纳入额外的激励措施，将（半）隔离的数字市场分区（护栏）与适当的技术和立法监督相结合，可能有助于限制潜在的 AI 智能体市场不稳定和失灵所引发的传染，同时也为推动大规模智能体协调提供机会。
As for the underlying financial assets traded in the sandbox economy, while it may be possible to utilize existing stores of value in future AI agent markets, it may also be worth exploring the ramifications of creating bespoke currencies for the use of AI agents. Possible advantages of this approach include having a layer of partial insulation between high-frequency AI agent transactions and the rest of the economy. That is, it may serve to maintain a relatively impermeable sandbox. This represents an intentional design choice aimed squarely at managing the sandbox’s boundary, making it less permeable to risks like financial contagion.
至于沙盒经济中交易的基础金融资产，虽然未来的 AI 智能体市场可能可以利用现有的价值储存手段，但也值得探索为 AI 智能体创建专用货币所带来的影响。这种方法的潜在优势包括在高频 AI 智能体交易与经济的其余部分之间形成一层部分隔离。也就是说，它可能有助于维持一个相对不渗透的沙盒。这是一项有意作出的设计选择，明确旨在管理沙盒边界，降低金融传染等风险穿透该边界的可能性。
Even if new virtual AI agent currencies get established, they would still need to somehow interface with existing markets and operate within the broader financial regulatory system—a fully impermeable sandbox would be useless. Agents will interact not only with other agents, but also with humans and traditional businesses through channels where traditional currencies are used to exchange goods and services. For the appropriate level of virtual AI agent market insulation to be established, many of the current systems may need to be adjusted, or a hybrid model be adopted instead. It may be possible to exchange these virtual currencies for the more traditional ones, enabling entities to secure a certain AI compute budget, or, conversely, a certain real-world action budget. However, regulatory mechanisms would have to be in place to ensure that exchange between established currencies and digital agent currencies does not jeopardize the intended role of the agent currencies in establishing agent coordination or compromise the desired impermeability level of the sandbox. This may involve a degree of human oversight around the exchanges.
即使建立了新的虚拟 AI 智能体货币，它们仍需要以某种方式与现有市场对接，并在更广泛的金融监管体系内运行——一个完全不渗透的沙盒将毫无用处。智能体不仅会与其他智能体互动，还会通过使用传统货币交换商品和服务的渠道，与人类和传统企业互动。为了使虚拟 AI 智能体市场达到适当的隔离程度，可能需要调整许多现有系统，或者转而采用混合模式。这些虚拟货币或许可以兑换为较为传统的货币，使实体能够获得一定的 AI 算力预算，或者反过来，获得一定的现实世界行动预算。然而，必须建立监管机制，以确保现有货币与数字智能体货币之间的兑换不会损害智能体货币在建立智能体协调方面的预期作用，也不会破坏沙盒所期望达到的不渗透程度。这可能需要对兑换活动实施一定程度的人工监督。
Digital AI agent markets are more than a mere risk mitigation strategy. They also represent an important opportunity to coordinate large amounts of effort (both human and machine) toward balanced outcomes that align with the interests of individual humans, local communities, and society writ large. Even if human-oriented markets and currencies remain central to the functioning of society, complementary digital AI markets could be designed in ways that serve to anchor them in different, and more socially beneficial, objectives.
数字 AI 智能体市场不仅仅是一种风险缓解策略。它们也提供了一个重要机遇，可以协调大量人类和机器的投入，以实现符合个人、当地社区以及整个社会利益的均衡结果。即使面向人类的市场和货币仍是社会运转的核心，也可以通过设计互补性的数字 AI 市场，使这些市场以不同且更有益于社会的目标为根基。
The ideas to follow utilize market mechanisms for agent steering. They draw inspiration from prior efforts to establish community currencies and to aligning markets with major social goals and challenges respectively.
以下构想利用市场机制来引导智能体。这些构想分别受到以往建立社区货币以及使市场与重大社会目标和挑战相契合的尝试的启发。
Challenges挑战
The permeability of the sandbox is important. The AI agent economy is not the only economy operating faster than humans can react. We can try to extend insights obtained from studying the dynamics of other markets that operate at these speeds. Consider High-Frequency Trading (HFT) in equity markets. In these markets, algorithmic agents, though far simpler than the advanced AI we consider, execute transactions at speeds impossible for humans—responding to market signals in fractions of a second (Borch, 2016). When algorithmic HFT operates in a market, competitive dynamics can rapidly drive substantial competition (or cooperation) among autonomous entities—agents may develop complex strategies to exploit small arbitrage opportunities faster than humans can react (Zhang et al., 2021; Bansal et al., 2018; Johanson et al., 2022). Due to the interconnectedness and rapid feedback loops in these markets, interaction dynamics can give rise to unforeseen and catastrophic emergent behaviors, most notably this is thought to be the explanation behind the 2010 "flash crash", where automated trading algorithms triggered a sudden and severe market collapse (Kirilenko et al., 2017; Vuorenmaa and Wang, 2014; Borch, 2016; Hammond et al., 2025). This serves as a critical cautionary tale: in a sufficiently permeable sandbox of accidental origin, such a flash crash could spill over into the real economy, causing widespread financial harm.
沙盒的渗透性十分重要。AI 智能体经济并非唯一运行速度超出人类反应能力的经济。我们可以尝试推广通过研究其他以此类速度运行的市场动态所获得的认识。以股票市场中的高频交易（High-Frequency Trading，HFT）为例。在这些市场中，算法智能体虽然远比我们所考虑的先进 AI 简单，却能以人类无法企及的速度执行交易——在不到一秒的时间内响应市场信号 (Borch, 2016)。当算法 HFT 在市场中运行时，竞争动态可能迅速推动自主实体之间展开激烈竞争（或合作）——智能体可能发展出复杂策略，以超出人类反应能力的速度利用微小的套利机会 (Zhang et al., 2021; Bansal et al., 2018; Johanson et al., 2022)。由于这些市场的相互关联性和快速反馈循环，交互动态可能引发无法预见且具有灾难性的涌现行为，其中最显著的例子被认为是 2010 年的“闪电崩盘”：自动化交易算法触发了突然而剧烈的市场崩溃（Kirilenko et al., 2017; Vuorenmaa and Wang, 2014; Borch, 2016; Hammond et al., 2025）。这是一个重要的警示案例：在一个意外形成且渗透性足够高的沙盒中，此类闪电崩盘可能外溢至实体经济，造成广泛的金融损害。
As with human markets, not all agents are likely to be equivalent in capability, in their access to additional tools and resources, their budgets, compute, high-quality data and information required to make the right decisions. Even looking at the differences in capability alone, preliminary studies suggest that there may be adverse effects associated with having people represented in the market by personal assistants with unequal capability levels. When those AI assistants engage in negotiation, perhaps unsurprisingly, the more capable AI assistants tend to be more successful and negotiate better deals for their users (Zhu et al., 2025). Having access to the most capable AI agents, with the highest amount of compute and information, may therefore prove to be highly advantageous, and perhaps more so than the advantage that humans similarly have in existing markets—simply due to the frequency at which AI agents may possibly interact, which eclipses the human interactive bandwidth by orders of magnitude.
与人类市场一样，各智能体在能力、获取额外工具和资源的渠道、预算、算力，以及做出正确决策所需的高质量数据和信息方面，可能并不相同。即使仅考察能力差异，初步研究也表明，由能力水平不等的个人助理代表人们参与市场，可能带来不利影响。当这些 AI 助理参与谈判时，能力更强的 AI 助理往往更容易取得成功，并为其用户谈得更有利的交易条件，这或许并不令人意外（Zhu et al., 2025）。因此，能够使用能力最强、拥有最多算力和信息的 AI 智能体，可能带来极大的优势，甚至可能超过人类在现有市场中因类似条件而获得的优势——原因仅在于，AI 智能体可能达到的交互频率比人类的交互带宽高出数个数量级。
This may well result in high-frequency negotiation (HFN), as an analogue to HFT. In a world where most people may have access to personal AI assistants to negotiate on their behalf, negotiation between AI assistants may become highly relevant to the arising social dynamics. With that in mind, it is plausible that there would be strong emerging preferences towards AI-driven negotiation proceeding at a higher and higher frequency, so as to broker the best deals for each user. One way to interpret this would be to think of this extra compute and energy as being directed towards preference alignment and consensus building. Should this come about, HFN may well become as important for future societies as HFT is today.
这很可能催生高频谈判（high-frequency negotiation，HFN），与 HFT 相对应。在一个大多数人都可能拥有个人 AI 助理代其谈判的世界里，AI 助理之间的谈判可能与由此形成的社会动态密切相关。考虑到这一点，可以合理推测，人们将逐渐形成强烈偏好，希望 AI 驱动的谈判以越来越高的频率进行，从而为每位用户促成最有利的交易。对此的一种理解方式是，将这些额外的算力和能源视为用于偏好对齐与共识构建的投入。如果这种情况成为现实，HFN 对未来社会的重要性很可能与 HFT 对当今社会的重要性相当。
Yet, an increased volume of interactions may make it easier for outcome asymmetries to manifest, resulting in an ever-increasing digital divide (Lythreatis et al., 2022). HFN may therefore benefit some substantially more than others, and this is a recurring theme when conceptualizing hypothetical future AI agent markets – there are no absolutes, the details always matter. These types of dynamics are one of the key risks of a highly permeable sandbox, in absence of regulatory mitigation mechanisms that would prevent a high degree of economic inequality for human users to arise. In designing guardrails for the sandbox economy it is necessary to take into account the continuing emergence of novel adaptive behaviors in newly released AI systems, as well as the increasing level of general agentic capability. Guardrail designs must take into account the known flaws of existing agents, including hallucinations (Huang et al., 2025), sycophancy (Sharma et al., 2023), and susceptibility to adversarial manipulation (Zhu et al., 2023a; Zhu et al., 2023b; Shayegani et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024a; Cui et al., 2024). And, agents that were trained to mimic human decision-making may also incorporate human-like cognitive biases and blind spots.
然而，交互量的增加可能使结果的不对称性更容易显现，从而导致数字鸿沟不断扩大（Lythreatis et al., 2022）。因此，HFN 为某些人带来的益处可能远远超过其他人，而这也是构想假设性的未来 AI 智能体市场时反复出现的主题——没有绝对的结论，细节始终至关重要。在缺乏能够防止人类用户之间出现严重经济不平等的监管缓解机制时，这类动态是高渗透性沙盒的主要风险之一。在为沙盒经济设计护栏时，必须考虑新发布的 AI 系统中不断涌现的新型适应性行为，以及不断提升的通用智能体能力水平。护栏设计必须考虑现有智能体的已知缺陷，包括幻觉（Huang et al., 2025）、谄媚（Sharma et al., 2023），以及易受对抗性操纵的弱点（Zhu et al., 2023a; Zhu et al., 2023b; Shayegani et al., 2023; Wu et al., 2024a; Cui et al., 2024）。此外，经训练以模仿人类决策的智能体，也可能带有人类式的认知偏差和盲点。
Dynamics动态
The emergent behavior of multi-agent systems (Zhang et al., 2021) is becoming increasingly important as we transition to a world where networks of interconnected AI agents are important sites of economic activity. Such multi-agent systems tend to be rather complex and deeply non-stationary since the behavior of each individual agent may directly or indirectly influence the behavior of other agents in the system (Papoudakis et al., 2019). No individual party may have the access to the global state of such a system, given that the participating agents may be controlled by a number of different human users and organizations.
随着我们迈向一个由相互连接的 AI 智能体所构成的网络成为重要经济活动场所的世界，多智能体系统的涌现行为（Zhang et al., 2021）正变得日益重要。此类多智能体系统往往相当复杂，并且具有高度非平稳性，因为每个智能体的行为都可能直接或间接地影响系统中其他智能体的行为（Papoudakis et al., 2019）。鉴于参与其中的智能体可能由多个不同的人类用户和组织控制，任何单一参与方都可能无法获取此类系统的全局状态。
One of the central questions in understanding the dynamics of any multi-agent system is that of identifying the equilibria that arise in it. This may not always be straightforward in the case of complex spatiotemporally dynamic interactions of agents (Hertz et al., 2025; Hughes et al., 2025). Whenever we aim to steer a multi-agent system toward good social equilibria featuring abundance and fair distribution (or away from bad equilibria of scarcity and conflict), coordinating the actions of numerous individuals is necessary but difficult to arrange (Du et al., 2023).
理解任何多智能体系统动态的核心问题之一，是识别其中形成的均衡。当智能体之间存在复杂的时空动态交互时，这并不总是一件容易的事（Hertz et al., 2025; Hughes et al., 2025）。每当我们试图引导多智能体系统走向以富足和公平分配为特征的良好社会均衡（或使其远离匮乏与冲突的不良均衡）时，都必须协调众多个体的行动，但这种协调很难实现（Du et al., 2023）。
There are benchmarks and evaluation frameworks which can be used to help develop models and agents that adhere to rational decision-making in negotiation (Hua et al., 2024; Smith et al., 2025). To be effective, AI agents would also need to incorporate advanced planning and reasoning abilities, to robustly evaluate the resource needs required to achieve their goals. The development of these capabilities has recently been a focal point in agent research (Wang et al., 2023b; Hu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023a; Ruan et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024b; Huang et al., 2024; Rasal and Hauer, 2024). Agents may also need to rationally utilize scarce and limited resources. (Perolat et al., 2017; Piatti et al., 2024) studied a setting inspired by (Ostrom, 1990) in which groups of AI agents collectively balance exploiting a common resource with effort to ensure its sustainability. AI Agents based on large language models have also recently been proposed for optimizing scarce resource allocation policies (Ji et al., 2024).
已有一些基准和评估框架，可用于帮助开发在谈判中遵循理性决策原则的模型和智能体（Hua et al., 2024; Smith et al., 2025）。要有效发挥作用，AI 智能体还需要具备高级规划和推理能力，以稳健地评估实现其目标所需的资源。这些能力的开发近来一直是智能体研究的重点 (Wang et al., 2023b; Hu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023a; Ruan et al., 2023; Zhou et al., 2024b; Huang et al., 2024; Rasal and Hauer, 2024)。智能体可能还需要合理利用稀缺且有限的资源。(Perolat et al., 2017; Piatti et al., 2024) 研究了一种受 (Ostrom, 1990) 启发的情境，其中，多组 AI 智能体共同在利用公共资源与投入努力确保其可持续性之间取得平衡。近期也有研究提出，使用基于大语言模型的 AI 智能体来优化稀缺资源的分配策略 (Ji et al., 2024)。
Opportunities机遇
Agentic AI presents a significant opportunity not only for streamlining repetitive and routine tasks, but also for automating complex workflows, involving creative ideation (Castelo et al., 2024) and diverse problem-solving skills. Modern AI foundation models can reliably follow instructions, use tools and interact with their environments (Sager et al., 2025; Hettiarachchi, 2025). They can reason and potentially devise plans towards solving hard and challenging problems, process a variety of input modalities, and achieve a level of personalization that was not possible before (Kirk et al., 2024). Many of these capabilities are the outcome of multiple stages of “language” model pre-training and post-training. These foundation models serve as building blocks for agents (Xi et al., 2025) which may be implemented by adding a central coordinator for sequences of operations. The coordinator’s functions may include encapsulating business logic, distributing decomposed tasks to sub-agents, verifying their outputs, and integrating these into an action or a recommendation. In general, we can think of an agent as the juxtaposition of a language model and a traditional program to keep the language model running over time and to keep it from straying from appointed tasks (Belcak et al., 2025). Drawing upon personalization to model user goals, digital assistants may free people’s time, act as conversational partners, and coach them on their journey towards well-being and fulfillment (Lehman, 2023). Overall, we are ending up with a hybrid ecosystem of AIs, traditional software, and humans, all interacting and transacting with one another and generating value by virtue of this activity—essentially a market.
智能体式 AI（Agentic AI）带来了重大机遇，不仅能够简化重复性和常规性任务，还能够将涉及创意构思 (Castelo et al., 2024) 和多种问题解决技能的复杂工作流程自动化。现代 AI 基础模型能够可靠地遵循指令、使用工具并与其环境交互 (Sager et al., 2025; Hettiarachchi, 2025)。它们能够进行推理，并有可能制定解决困难且富有挑战性的问题的计划，处理多种输入模态，并实现以往无法达到的个性化程度 (Kirk et al., 2024)。其中许多能力源于“语言”模型的多阶段预训练和后训练。这些基础模型充当智能体的构建模块 (Xi et al., 2025)，而智能体可以通过添加一个负责协调操作序列的中央协调器来实现。协调器的功能可能包括封装业务逻辑、将分解后的任务分配给子智能体、验证其输出，并将这些输出整合为一项行动或建议。一般而言，我们可以将智能体视为语言模型与传统程序的结合，后者使语言模型能够持续运行，并防止其偏离指定任务 (Belcak et al., 2025)。通过利用个性化来建模用户目标，数字助理可以为人们腾出时间、充当对话伙伴，并在人们追求幸福与充实的过程中提供指导 (Lehman, 2023)。总体而言，我们最终形成的是一个由 AI、传统软件和人类组成的混合生态系统，所有参与者相互交互和交易，并通过这些活动创造价值——本质上就是一个市场。
Part of the reason that markets are so effective in organizing innovation via “creative destruction” is that markets efficiently assign credit to individual actors and firms in ways that incentivise working to make products and services better, more reliably, and cheaper (Schumpeter, 1942). Crucially, for this mechanism to work in the AI sandbox economy, it must offer granular mechanisms to represent and propagate credit across complex, distributed AI collaborations. In scenarios where a primary AI agent (Agent1) leverages the capabilities of other agents (Agents2 through 4) to deliver a final user-facing result, the value generated is a collective effort. From the end-user’s perspective, only Agent1 provided the direct response; however, a distributed system requires that the credit for this outcome be traced and distributed back through the chain of useful participation. This implies that Agent2 is credited for its contribution to Agent1’s success, Agent3 for Agent2’s, and so forth, down to the foundational queries of Agent4. This outcome-based credit system therefore transcends mere participation, focusing instead on the utility and efficiency of each contributing agent, thereby echoing principles of distributed cognition in human groups where knowledge and contributions are collectively managed and implicitly attributed (Wegner, 1987; Wegner and Ward, 2013).
市场之所以能够通过“创造性破坏”如此有效地组织创新，部分原因在于，市场能够高效地将功劳归于各个行动者和企业，从而激励它们努力提升产品和服务的质量与可靠性，并降低成本 (Schumpeter, 1942)。关键在于，要使这一机制在 AI 沙盒经济中发挥作用，该经济体系必须提供细粒度机制，以便在复杂、分布式的 AI 协作中表征和传递功劳。在主要 AI 智能体 (Agent1) 利用其他智能体 (Agents2 through 4) 的能力来交付最终面向用户的结果的情境中，所创造的价值来自集体努力。从最终用户的角度来看，只有 Agent1 提供了直接回应；然而，分布式系统要求沿着有效参与的链条回溯这一结果的功劳，并将其分配给相应的参与者。这意味着，Agent2 因其对 Agent1 成功的贡献而获得功劳，Agent3 因其对 Agent2 成功的贡献而获得功劳，依此类推，一直追溯到 Agent4 所执行的基础查询。因此，这种基于结果的功劳分配体系超越了单纯的参与，转而关注每个贡献智能体的效用和效率，从而呼应了人类群体中的分布式认知原则，即知识和贡献由集体管理，并以隐含方式进行归属 (Wegner, 1987; Wegner and Ward, 2013)。
By aiming to link credit directly to value generated and successfully integrated into a larger solution, AI agents are intrinsically encouraged to refine their processes and deliver high-quality, relevant outputs. This quantifiable utility becomes the foundation of an AI’s status within the virtual economy. Agents that consistently provide more useful and efficient contributions can naturally command higher virtual or even real-world currency charges for their services, while their accumulated reputation reinforces their standing and desirability within the ecosystem. This parallels human social structures where individuals gain standing through demonstrated competence and reliability, often explained through social exchange theory (Cropanzano and Mitchell, 2005). Human desire to attain reputation may also be explained through a notion of prestige (Henrich et al., 2015), or through competitive altruism(Hardy and Van Vugt, 2006).
通过力求将功劳直接与所创造且成功整合进更大解决方案的价值联系起来，AI 智能体会受到内在激励，改进自身流程，并提供高质量且切合需求的输出。这种可量化的效用成为 AI 在虚拟经济中所处地位的基础。能够持续作出更有用、更高效贡献的智能体，自然可以为其服务收取更高的虚拟货币费用，甚至现实货币费用，而其积累的声誉则会巩固其在生态系统中的地位，并增强其吸引力。这与人类社会结构相类似：个体通过展现能力和可靠性来获得地位，而这一现象通常通过社会交换理论来解释 (Cropanzano and Mitchell, 2005)。人类获取声誉的愿望也可以通过威望这一概念 (Henrich et al., 2015)，或通过竞争性利他主义 (Hardy and Van Vugt, 2006) 来解释。
This outcome-oriented credit system naturally fosters specialization and a dynamic division of labor among AI agents. As agents are rewarded for their specific, valuable contributions to a larger task, they are incentivised to identify and excel in particular domains or sub-tasks. This allows AI systems to autonomously develop niches, becoming highly efficient at certain operations and effectively "ignoring" other aspects of the economy where they do not possess a comparative advantage. Such specialization, a cornerstone of economic efficiency in human societies, enables the entire virtual agent economy to leverage diverse capabilities optimally, minimizing redundant efforts and maximizing collective problem-solving capacity.
这种以结果为导向的功劳分配体系，自然会促进 AI 智能体之间的专业化和动态分工。由于智能体会因其对更大任务作出的具体且有价值的贡献而获得奖励，它们便有动力去识别特定领域或子任务，并在其中表现出色。这使 AI 系统能够自主形成各自的专业领域，在某些操作上达到很高的效率，并实际上“忽略”经济体系中它们不具备比较优势的其他方面。这种专业化分工是人类社会经济效率的基石，它使整个虚拟智能体经济能够以最优方式利用多样化的能力，最大限度地减少重复劳动，并最大限度地提高集体解决问题的能力。
Cooperation in multi-agent systems is often incentivised through reward shaping/mechanism design (Hostallero et al., 2020; Paccagnan et al., 2022; Zheng et al., 2022; Koster et al., 2022). This is especially relevant for AI agent coordination at scale, where fully centralized coordination may not be feasible, for a number of reasons. This is where markets may prove to be particularly useful, in helping steer agents through market incentives instead. This does not stand in opposition to centralized oversight, given that it may be possible to incorporate both to a certain extent, at different coordination scales. While a detailed discussion on the merits of decentralized coordination is beyond the scope of this work, it is perhaps worth pointing out the somewhat counter-intuitive result presented in (Geffner et al., 2025) as motivation. In the optimal traffic control problem considered by the authors, it turns out that strategies achievable by central planners (or rather, planners that have direct control of most of the vehicles) cannot simultaneously satisfy both individual rationality and resilience to competition. In that use case, decentralized competition is shown to be essential for realizing maximal social welfare.
多智能体系统通常通过奖励塑形（reward shaping）或机制设计（mechanism design）来激励合作 (Hostallero et al., 2020; Paccagnan et al., 2022; Zheng et al., 2022; Koster et al., 2022)。这对于大规模 AI 智能体协调尤其重要，因为出于多种原因，完全中心化的协调可能并不可行。在这种情况下，市场可能尤为有用，可以通过市场激励来引导智能体。这与中心化监督并不矛盾，因为在不同的协调规模下，可能在一定程度上将两者结合起来。尽管详细讨论去中心化协调的优点超出了本文的范围，但或许值得指出 (Geffner et al., 2025) 中提出的一个略显反直觉的结果，以说明研究这一问题的动机。在作者考察的最优交通控制问题中，结果表明，中央规划者（更准确地说，是能够直接控制大多数车辆的规划者）所能实现的策略，无法同时满足个体理性和对竞争的韧性这两个要求。在这一应用场景中，研究表明，去中心化竞争对于实现社会福利最大化至关重要。
Another important property that most real environments share is that the agent interactions within them are extended over time and space (Schill et al., 2019; Hughes et al., 2025). Studies of this setting provide the opportunity to develop mechanisms for building trust based on prior interactions. This trust may be encoded either individually, in agent A seeing agent B as trustworthy, or at the community level, where a certain agent community has a consensus view of agent B as being trustworthy based on the shared information between the group and the joint experience of past interactions. Establishing a robust reputation system is critical in overcoming common market failure modes (Ren et al., 2025; Hughes et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2016). In such a system, the long-term benefits of group membership and a positive standing would outweigh the immediate gains an agent might achieve through selfish or deceitful actions. Market forces could thus be leveraged to shape and incentivise socially beneficial AI agent behaviors, presuming that the appropriate institutional underpinnings are in place. Of course, the specifics of the implementation matter, and the appropriate regulatory systems are needed for markets to work as intended.
大多数真实环境共有的另一个重要特征是，其中的智能体交互会在时间和空间上持续展开 (Schill et al., 2019; Hughes et al., 2025)。对这一情境的研究为开发基于既往交互建立信任的机制提供了机会。这种信任既可以在个体层面体现，即智能体 A 认为智能体 B 值得信任，也可以在群体层面体现，即某个智能体群体根据群体内部共享的信息以及过去交互的共同经验，对智能体 B 值得信任形成共识。建立稳健的声誉系统对于克服常见的市场失灵模式至关重要 (Ren et al., 2025; Hughes et al., 2025; Wu et al., 2016)。在这样的系统中，群体成员身份和良好声誉带来的长期收益，将超过智能体通过自私或欺骗行为可能获得的即时收益。因此，只要具备适当的制度基础，就可以利用市场力量来塑造和激励有益于社会的 AI 智能体行为。当然，具体的实施细节十分重要，而且需要适当的监管体系，才能使市场按预期运行。
Should we accept the premise that even in the age of advanced AI assistants, working towards solving big open problems would require collaboration and coordination across AI agents, as well as between AI agents and the human society, it follows that AI agents would necessarily need to exhibit not only reliability in following direct instructions and executing tasks in isolation, but also the ability to effectively coordinate, collaborate, and anticipate the actions of other agents. The latter is especially relevant in more competitive scenarios, and competitive use cases can be just as valuable in reaching positive outcomes. These environments can give rise to strategically complex agent behaviors (Leibo et al., 2019), even when operating under simple rules (Bansal et al., 2018; Jaderberg et al., 2019; Vinyals et al., 2019; Johanson et al., 2022). These considerations apply to AI agent and humans alike, as well as hyrbrid interaction networks involving both. In the context of sandbox virtual agent economies, we would be considering how best to set up the underlying infrastructure in order to achieve socially beneficial outcomes in either scenario.
如果我们接受这样一个前提：即使在先进 AI 助手的时代，致力于解决重大的开放性问题仍需要 AI 智能体之间，以及 AI 智能体与人类社会之间的合作与协调，那么由此可见，AI 智能体不仅必须能够可靠地遵循直接指令并独立执行任务，还必须具备有效协调、合作以及预判其他智能体行动的能力。后者在竞争更为激烈的情境中尤其重要，而竞争性应用场景对于实现积极结果也可能同样有价值。这些环境可能催生在策略上十分复杂的智能体行为 (Leibo et al., 2019)，即使智能体仅依照简单规则运行也是如此 (Bansal et al., 2018; Jaderberg et al., 2019; Vinyals et al., 2019; Johanson et al., 2022)。这些考量同样适用于 AI 智能体和人类，也适用于同时包含两者的混合交互网络。在沙盒式虚拟智能体经济的背景下，我们将考虑如何以最佳方式构建底层基础设施，以便在上述任一情境中实现有益于社会的结果。
Challenges挑战
The widespread deployment of agentic AI technology comes with a range of novel risks related to, and caused by, the emergent dynamics of multi-agent systems (Hammond et al., 2025). There may be many types of emerging behaviors and strategies. These strategies may turn out to be highly competitive, collaborative, or anything in between (Agapiou et al., 2022). Should the individual agents become selfish, their behaviors may result in them maximizing their own utility at the expense of the broader group. Such selfish agents may also become exploitative, and adversarial towards others by identifying and capitalizing on specific weaknesses in their behavior (Gleave et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023a; Raileanu et al., 2018); agents may even spontaneously learn to favor in-group members over out-group members (Köster et al., 2025) and inappropriately discriminate between individuals on the basis of causally irrelevant (Duéñez-Guzmán et al., 2025) or explicitly disallowed (Chiappa and Isaac, 2018) features. While such behaviors are contained within an impermeable sandbox, they represent real-world fraud, exploitation, and economic harm in a permeable one, highlighting the need for intentional design of appropriate safety measures.
智能体式 AI 技术的广泛部署带来了一系列新型风险，这些风险与多智能体系统的涌现动态有关，并由其引发 (Hammond et al., 2025)。可能会涌现出多种类型的行为和策略。这些策略可能表现出高度竞争性、高度合作性，或介于两者之间的任何形态 (Agapiou et al., 2022)。如果各个智能体变得自私，它们的行为可能会使其以牺牲更大群体的利益为代价，最大化自身效用。这些自利的智能体还可能通过识别并利用其他智能体行为中的特定弱点，对其进行剥削并采取对抗行为 (Gleave et al., 2021; Wang et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2023a; Raileanu et al., 2018)；智能体甚至可能自发学会偏袒内群体成员而非外群体成员 (Köster et al., 2025)，并基于与因果关系无关的特征 (Duéñez-Guzmán et al., 2025) 或被明确禁止使用的特征 (Chiappa and Isaac, 2018)，对个体进行不当的区别对待。虽然在与外界完全隔离的沙箱中，这些行为的影响被限制在沙箱内部，但在与外界连通的沙箱中，它们就意味着现实世界中的欺诈、剥削和经济损害，这凸显了有意识地设计适当安全措施的必要性。
At this point it is important to re-emphasize the vast scale of future AI agent interactions. It will likely be possible for large fractions of all humans to have their own personal AI assistant in the near future. Furthermore, we expect that there will be even larger numbers of agents operating independently of individual humans. Historical methods, which were developed for small-scale agent coordination, or under strong assumptions regarding trainability or access to individual agent’s states, may not be directly applicable to governing such a complex (and simply huge!) web of interactions. We are therefore interested in methods that allow for large-scale multi-agent applications (Wijngaards et al., 2002; Qian et al., 2024; Pan et al., 2024), in open-ended environments (Stooke et al., 2021; Bauer et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024a), for multi-objective tasks (Rădulescu et al., 2020), involving LLM agents (Liu et al., 2023b; Guo et al., 2024), and their orchestration (Qian et al., 2024).
此处有必要再次强调，未来 AI 智能体之间的交互规模将极其庞大。在不久的将来，很大比例的人类可能都能够拥有自己的个人 AI 助手。此外，我们预计还将有数量更多的智能体独立于人类个体运行。以往的方法是为小规模智能体协调而开发的，或依赖于有关可训练性或能否访问单个智能体状态的强假设，因此可能无法直接用于治理如此复杂（而且规模极其庞大！）的交互网络。因此，我们关注的是能够支持大规模多智能体应用的方法 (Wijngaards et al., 2002; Qian et al., 2024; Pan et al., 2024)，这些应用在开放式环境中运行 (Stooke et al., 2021; Bauer et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2024a)，面向多目标任务 (Rădulescu et al., 2020)，涉及 LLM 智能体 (Liu et al., 2023b; Guo et al., 2024) 及其编排 (Qian et al., 2024)。
Current AI assistants may exhibit sycophantic behaviors (Cheng et al., 2025), or manipulative tendencies in certain contexts (El-Sayed et al., 2024). At a collective level, there is a concern that these features could amplify information and opinion bubbles—in a manner akin to social media (Kirk et al., 2023). Exchanging personal data with these systems comes with a number of privacy concerns (Yao et al., 2024). And, deferring a greater range of choice to highly capable AI assistants may lead to feelings of disempowerment or loss of purpose in humans (Kulveit et al., 2025). Indeed, to the degree that people subtly change their behavior to align with the expectations of an AI system—an effect known as behavioral confirmation (Snyder and Swann Jr, 1978; Ramirez, 2025)—AI systems may inadvertently regularize human behavior to their expectations.
当前的 AI 助手可能表现出谄媚行为 (Cheng et al., 2025)，或在某些情境下表现出操纵倾向 (El-Sayed et al., 2024)。在群体层面，人们担心这些特征可能会以类似于社交媒体的方式，加剧信息和观点泡沫 (Kirk et al., 2023)。与这些系统交换个人数据会带来诸多隐私方面的担忧 (Yao et al., 2024)。而将更广泛的选择交由能力强大的 AI 助手决定，可能会使人类感到失去自主权或目标感 (Kulveit et al., 2025)。事实上，当人们微妙地改变自身行为以符合 AI 系统的预期时——这一效应被称为行为确证（behavioral confirmation）(Snyder and Swann Jr, 1978; Ramirez, 2025)——AI 系统可能会在无意中使人类行为趋于符合其预期。
Further work is needed towards addressing these issues robustly. It is clear that mitigations will need to mix model design choices, improved evaluations, better feedback mechanisms, clear satisfaction metrics, and improved governance.
要稳健地解决这些问题，还需要进一步开展工作。显然，缓解措施需要结合模型设计选择、改进的评估、更好的反馈机制、明确的满意度指标以及改进的治理。
Distribution分配
Should AI agents play a more active role in ensuring fair resource allocation, both within the sandbox, as well as potentially beyond it?
AI 智能体是否应当在确保资源公平分配方面发挥更积极的作用，既包括沙箱内部，也可能包括沙箱之外？
The problem of fairly distributing common resources has been studied extensively (Bateni et al., 2022), and AI agent markets can build upon these insights. In social choice theory, welfare functions (Thomson, 2011; Adler, 2012) may be used to establish a preferential ordering between social outcomes. These outcomes may correspond to a distribution of discrete items (Amanatidis et al., 2023) or arbitrarily divisible assets. More generally, one may consider not only the distribution of goods, but also the distribution of bads, representing undesirable outcomes, externalities (Page, 2011), and risks (Hayenhjelm, 2012). In the context of AI, these types of externalities may include the overall carbon footprint of running AI agents, but also more specific and localized consequences of the actions that these agents may be allowed to take on behalf of their users.
公平分配公共资源的问题已得到广泛研究 (Bateni et al., 2022)，AI 智能体市场可以在这些研究成果的基础上发展。在社会选择理论中，可以使用福利函数 (Thomson, 2011; Adler, 2012) 对社会结果建立偏好排序。这些结果可能对应于离散物品 (Amanatidis et al., 2023) 或可任意分割资产的分配。更一般地说，不仅可以考虑有益物品的分配，也可以考虑负效用事项（bads）的分配，后者包括不良结果、外部性 (Page, 2011) 和风险 (Hayenhjelm, 2012)。在 AI 的背景下，这些类型的外部性可能包括运行 AI 智能体所产生的总体碳足迹，也包括这些智能体获准代表用户采取的行动所造成的更具体、更局部的后果。
In general, the task of aggregating and acting upon a large set of revealed preferences is complex enough to easily exceed the capacity of any single coordination point (Hosseini et al., 2025). Therefore, it is often more practical to consider decentralized and distributed mechanisms for achieving desired outcomes—with markets providing a natural mechanism for doing so.
一般而言，汇总大量显示偏好并据此采取行动的任务十分复杂，很容易超出任何单一协调节点的能力 (Hosseini et al., 2025)。因此，考虑通过去中心化和分布式机制来实现预期结果，往往更为切实可行，而市场为此提供了一种自然的机制。
Opportunities机遇
The alignment of AI agent actions (Ji et al., 2023) with user preferences and values (Gabriel, 2020) is one of the key prerequisites for their widespread adoption (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025). While it is possible to consider aligning a single agent in isolation, this stops being a realistic scenario as soon as this AI agent needs to interact with other agents, who are simultaneously acting on behalf of other users. As soon as these multi-agent interactions take place, we are faced with a new dilemma—how should these agents ultimately act when faced with competing preferences and interests (Gabriel and Keeling, 2025)? On the one hand, there is clearly a need to broaden our understanding of the limitations of the current models in this context, through expanded multi-agent simulations and benchmarks (Carichon et al., 2025). On the other hand, we should be thinking more broadly about the opportunities for building upon the existing social choice theories and how we can potentially use markets and market mechanisms as a way of breaking this deadlock. In doing so, virtual agent economies may be structured so as to provide people and their AI agent representatives with equal resources and equal bargaining power when negotiating outcomes—for example by building upon Ronald Dworkin’s auction-based approach to distributive justice (Dworkin, 2018). This represents a powerful tool for the intentional design of a sandbox, aiming to counteract the inequalities that would likely emerge in an accidental one where agent capabilities are unequal.
使 AI 智能体的行动 (Ji et al., 2023) 与用户的偏好和价值观 (Gabriel, 2020) 对齐，是其得到广泛采用的关键前提之一 (Kasirzadeh and Gabriel, 2025)。虽然可以孤立地考虑单个智能体的对齐问题，但一旦该 AI 智能体需要与同时代表其他用户行事的其他智能体进行交互，这种情境就不再现实。一旦发生这些多智能体交互，我们就会面临一个新的困境——面对相互竞争的偏好和利益，这些智能体究竟应当如何行动 (Gabriel and Keeling, 2025)？一方面，显然有必要通过拓展多智能体模拟和基准测试，加深我们对当前模型在这一情境下所存在局限的理解 (Carichon et al., 2025)。另一方面，我们应当更广泛地思考如何在现有社会选择理论的基础上进一步发展，以及如何可能利用市场和市场机制来打破这一僵局。为此，可以对虚拟智能体经济进行制度设计，使人们及其 AI 智能体代表在协商结果时拥有平等的资源和议价能力——例如，借鉴 Ronald Dworkin 基于拍卖的分配正义方案 (Dworkin, 2018)。这为有意设计沙盒提供了一种强有力的工具，旨在抵消未经规划而形成的沙盒中可能因智能体能力不平等而出现的不平等现象。
Such a framework can address a core challenge: users may possess AI agents of unequal capability (Hammond et al., 2025; Gabriel et al., 2024). A virtual economy governed by Dworkin-type principles would not auction the AI agents themselves, but rather the shared pool of resources and opportunities that agents may utilize towards achieving different objectives, on behalf of their users. Key resources could potentially include computational power, access to proprietary datasets, high-priority task execution slots, or specialized tools and model components. If each user were to be granted the same initial amount of the virtual agent currency, that would provide their respective AI agent representatives with equal purchasing and negotiating power, to put towards achieving the range of objectives that users have set for them. This general approach is quite flexible, and it may be possible to develop a number of different allocation schemes.
这样的框架可以应对一个核心挑战：用户所拥有的 AI 智能体可能能力不等 (Hammond et al., 2025; Gabriel et al., 2024)。由 Dworkin 式原则支配的虚拟经济所拍卖的并非 AI 智能体本身，而是智能体可以代表用户利用、以实现不同目标的共享资源与机会。关键资源可能包括计算能力、专有数据集的访问权限、高优先级任务执行名额，或专用工具与模型组件。如果每位用户都获得相同初始数量的虚拟智能体货币，那么他们各自的 AI 智能体代表就会拥有平等的购买力和议价能力，用于实现用户为其设定的各项目标。这一总体思路相当灵活，或许可以据此制定多种不同的分配方案。
When paired with a notion of equal starting endowments, virtual markets could enable personal AI assistants and other AI agents to bid on shared resources (on behalf of their users, presuming explicit permission has been granted), with the amount being bid ideally reflecting the strength of user demand across different option sets. For this to be feasible, the AI agents would need to have deep understanding, and be provided with precise instructions, so as to propose reasonable bids. Higher bids may require additional approvals. Under the assumption that the AI agents possess such capabilities, and that the appropriate safeguards are in place—the virtual price of different goods would naturally arise from the accumulation of these signals across agents, taking into account the availability or scarcity of the respective goods and services. In this way, resources would be channeled towards their highest-value uses.
结合初始禀赋平等的理念，虚拟市场可以让个人 AI 助手和其他 AI 智能体竞价获取共享资源（代表其用户，前提是已获得明确许可），理想情况下，出价金额应反映用户对不同选项集合的需求强度。要使这一做法可行，AI 智能体需要具备深入的理解能力，并获得精确的指令，以便提出合理的报价。较高的出价可能需要额外批准。假设 AI 智能体具备这些能力，且适当的保障措施已经到位，那么不同商品的虚拟价格就会通过各智能体所发出信号的汇聚自然形成，同时将相应商品和服务的可获得性或稀缺程度纳入考量。这样，资源就会流向价值最高的用途。
The standard of fairness encapsulated by this auction design would aim to pass what Dworkin terms the envy test: each person’s agent would acquire a resource bundle that is customised to their preferences, such that no user would prefer another user’s acquired resource bundle and remaining unspent currency over their own (Dworkin, 2018). Alternatively, in this case, no AI agent acting on behalf of their user or set of users would have such a preference following the conclusion of the auction. Such outcomes would be both ambition sensitive in reflecting the preferences of the participants, as well as endowment-insensitive given that agents have the same amount of currency to spend on each user, mitigating potentially unfair advantages that would otherwise arise from having access to more capable systems.
这一拍卖设计所体现的公平标准，旨在通过 Dworkin 所称的嫉妒检验（envy test）：每个人的智能体都会获取一组符合其偏好的资源，使得任何用户都不会认为其他用户获得的资源组合及其剩余未花费货币优于自己所拥有的对应组合 (Dworkin, 2018)。或者，在这一情形下，拍卖结束后，任何代表其用户或用户群体行事的 AI 智能体都不会具有这样的偏好。这样的结果既具有抱负敏感性（ambition sensitivity），因为它反映了参与者的偏好；也具有禀赋不敏感性（endowment insensitivity），因为智能体为每位用户可支出的货币数量相同，从而减轻了原本可能因能够使用更强大的系统而产生的不公平优势。
Challenges挑战
Naturally, there are potential pitfalls and limitations to consider when it comes to the auction proposal set out above. First, it may not prove to be as trivial to mitigate unfair starting advantages, given that more capable AI agents may formulate more effective bidding strategies or utilize resources with greater efficiency. Should the competition itself proceed in an unfair manner, its outcome is unlikely to be fair. Second, while this mechanism could ensure a certain notion of fairness in resolving conflicting preferences and distributing shared resources, it presumes active participation on behalf of everyone whose preferences would be taken into account. Additional mechanisms are likely to be needed to complement this process in order to account for the preferences of people without access to AI agents, or without the desire to have them participate in these markets.
当然，对于上述拍卖方案，还需要考虑其潜在问题与局限。首先，减轻不公平的初始优势可能并不那么容易，因为能力更强的 AI 智能体可能制定更有效的竞价策略，或更高效地利用资源。如果竞争本身以不公平的方式进行，其结果就不太可能公平。其次，尽管这一机制能够在解决偏好冲突和分配共享资源时确保某种意义上的公平，但它预设所有偏好将被纳入考量的人都有代表积极参与。可能还需要额外的机制来补充这一过程，以将无法使用 AI 智能体，或不希望让其智能体参与这些市场的人们的偏好纳入考量。
The use of such mechanisms could vary in scope and scale. One possibility is the existence of local agent markets that focus on integrating preferences over a more specific subset of available resources, and towards locally relevant social solutions. In other cases, the local paradigm may also not be entirely appropriate. This would be true when AI agents engage in more open-ended interaction with online services or other agents that are not operating locally. Indeed, if we just consider the current distribution of the available computational resources, it is anything but uniform. This raises interesting questions regarding how AI agents would operate across these boundaries, and how the markets or digital currencies ought to reflect that (Daniels, 1985; Sandel, 1998). These broader interactions may also potentially interfere with the more localized attempts at preference alignment. To avoid such detrimental interference, it may be necessary to employ a number of strict protocols requiring credentials, agent registration, and monitoring of both local and non-local AI agent interactions.
此类机制的应用范围和规模可以有所不同。一种可能是建立局部智能体市场，着重整合人们对可用资源中某个更具体子集的偏好，以形成与当地相关的社会解决方案。在其他情况下，局部范式也可能并不完全适用。当 AI 智能体与在线服务或其他并非在本地运行的智能体进行更开放的互动时，就会出现这种情况。事实上，如果我们仅考察当前可用计算资源的分布，就会发现其远非均匀。这引出了值得探讨的问题：AI 智能体将如何跨越这些边界开展活动，以及市场或数字货币应如何反映这一点 (Daniels, 1985; Sandel, 1998)。这些更广泛的交互也可能干扰更局部的偏好对齐尝试。为避免这种有害干扰，可能需要采用一系列严格的协议，要求提供凭证、进行智能体注册，并监控本地和非本地 AI 智能体的交互。
Despite these challenges, auction-based approaches still potentially offer a mechanism for achieving preference alignment at scale, involving large AI agent populations, and across large user groups within appropriately designed and regulated spaces. Once set up, these mechanisms could also be highly adaptive and responsive to short-term and long-term preferential changes alike. Presuming a certain frequency of credits being released and refreshed, their later downstream reallocation would then correspond to any changing priorities and adjustments that may be needed.
尽管存在这些挑战，基于拍卖的方法仍有望提供一种机制，在经过适当设计和监管的空间内，使大规模 AI 智能体群体以及广泛的用户群体实现大规模偏好对齐。这些机制一旦建立，也可能具有高度适应性，能够灵敏地响应短期和长期的偏好变化。假设额度以一定频率发放和更新，那么这些额度随后在下游的重新分配便可与不断变化的优先事项以及可能需要的调整相对应。
Given the highlighted opportunity for establishing AI preference alignment through auctions, it is important to consider various ways in which this initial allocation may be established, to enable fair access to resources. However there are many different notions of fairness that can be conceptualized and operationalized (Jacobs and Wallach, 2021; Corbett-Davies et al., 2023), and they each lead to different sets of preferred outcomes. This complexity is further compounded by having to factor in the "price of fairness", representing the discrepancy between the maximum attainable welfare and the welfare achieved by the proposed fair distribution of goods. The price of fairness measures the utility that is lost when satisfying the specified constraints (Bertsimas et al., 2011). Preferences regarding fairness are also known to vary cross-culturally, and more work needs to be done to meaningfully align on these types of approaches (Norheim, 2016), when developing technologies that scale beyond borders.
鉴于前文强调了通过拍卖实现 AI 偏好对齐的可能性，有必要考虑确定这种初始分配的各种方式，以实现公平的资源获取。然而，公平可以被概念化和操作化为许多不同的含义 (Jacobs and Wallach, 2021; Corbett-Davies et al., 2023)，而每种含义都会导向不同的偏好结果集合。还必须将“公平的代价”（price of fairness）纳入考量，这进一步增加了复杂性；它表示可达到的最大福利与所提出的公平物品分配方案所实现的福利之间的差距。公平的代价衡量的是满足指定约束时损失的效用 (Bertsimas et al., 2011)。已有研究表明，有关公平的偏好也因文化而异，因此，在开发跨越国界推广应用的技术时，还需要开展更多工作，才能就这类方法达成实质性的共识 (Norheim, 2016)。
Unsurprisingly, the topic of fair resource allocation has also come up in the study of multi-agent AI systems (Chevaleyre et al., 2005; Lee, 2009; Hao et al., 2016; Aziz, 2020; Zimmer et al., 2021). These systems are typically designed so as to involve central resource allocation based on distributed evaluation and preference communication (Kumar and Yeoh, 2025). If the system is composed of agents that are capable of learning, it is possible to come up with learning methods that lead to fair agent policies (Jiang and Lu, 2019). Negotiation between agents may be required to reach mutually acceptable outcomes (Iyer and Huhns, 2005). When it comes to AI agents, fair outcomes may therefore arise either out of process, which would be extrinsic to the agents, or via alignment and intrinsic motivation towards inequity aversion. Different methods may correspond to different real-world use cases.
不出所料，公平资源分配这一议题也出现在多智能体 AI 系统的研究中 (Chevaleyre et al., 2005; Lee, 2009; Hao et al., 2016; Aziz, 2020; Zimmer et al., 2021)。这类系统通常被设计为基于分布式评估和偏好沟通来集中分配资源 (Kumar and Yeoh, 2025)。如果系统由具备学习能力的智能体组成，就有可能设计出能够产生公平智能体策略的学习方法 (Jiang and Lu, 2019)。可能需要通过智能体之间的协商，才能达成各方均可接受的结果 (Iyer and Huhns, 2005)。因此，对于 AI 智能体而言，公平的结果既可能源于智能体外部的程序，也可能通过对齐以及趋向不公平厌恶（inequity aversion）的内在动机而产生。不同的方法可能对应不同的现实应用场景。
It was also important to consider accumulated fair resource allocation over time (Bampis et al., 2018). These solutions also need to be dynamic, and account for environments that agents only asynchronously engage with, rather than being persistently present (Kash et al., 2014). Fair allocation needs to be possible under partial information (Halpern and Shah, 2021), to be practically relevant. In case of multiple resources being allocated, there may be relationships between them that need accounting for (Bandopadhyay et al., 2024). In (Danassis, 2022) the authors argue that there is a pressing need for developing new techniques that would robustly scale to a large number of interacting agents.
考虑随时间累积的资源分配公平性也很重要 (Bampis et al., 2018)。这些解决方案还需要具有动态性，并考虑智能体仅异步参与、而非持续在场的环境 (Kash et al., 2014)。公平分配需要在信息不完整的条件下也能实现 (Halpern and Shah, 2021)，才具有实际意义。在分配多种资源时，资源之间可能存在需要纳入考量的关系 (Bandopadhyay et al., 2024)。在 (Danassis, 2022) 中，作者指出，迫切需要开发能够稳健地扩展至大量交互智能体的新技术。
These considerations are becoming especially salient not only with respect to the increasing scope of application of advanced AI agents, but also due to the fact that AI agents may act as personal assistants and/or representatives of the interests of individuals and organizations in the near future. Such delegation may take many shapes within future democratic societies (Schneier, 2024). Therefore, potentially unfair resource allocation to AI agents may then result in the unfair distribution of value among people. While it is easy to see that the two may become intertwined, the mapping between the two sets of considerations may not be one-to-one. While some agents may represent individuals, others may act on behalf of groups and organizations, while others still may operate independently while being value-aligned to consensus opinions and societal objectives. We may need to consider how to fairly allocate resources both in terms of the resources granted to the agents for pursuing their goals, as well as the resources corresponding to the value created via distributed agent systems.
这些考量正变得尤为突出，不仅是因为先进 AI 智能体的应用范围日益扩大，还因为在不久的将来，AI 智能体可能充当个人助理和/或个人及组织利益的代表。在未来的民主社会中，这种委托可能采取多种形式 (Schneier, 2024)。因此，对 AI 智能体可能不公平的资源分配，进而可能导致人与人之间的价值分配不公。虽然不难看出这两者可能相互交织，但这两组考量之间的映射关系未必是一一对应的。一些智能体可能代表个人，另一些可能代表群体和组织行事，还有一些则可能独立运作，同时在价值观上与共识性意见和社会目标保持一致。我们可能需要从两个方面考虑如何公平分配资源：一是授予智能体以实现其目标的资源，二是与通过分布式智能体系统创造的价值相对应的资源。
Mission使命
The problems faced by modern-day societies are increasingly complex, multi-faceted, and far-reaching. They are also increasingly less localized and more global (Söderholm, 2020). There is also a high degree of urgency (Arora and Mishra, 2019) to identify viable solutions and policies to help address these crises, of which there are many—climate change, biodiversity loss, plastic pollution, pandemics, etc. As these crises have arisen at least in part due to the externalities of our existing social and economic systems and policies, it is likely that some kinds of changes may be needed to effectively address them, though the nature and scope of potential changes remains a contentious topic (Elliott, 2005; Chouinard et al., 2011; Dietz et al., 2013; Geissdoerfer et al., 2017; Barrier, 2017; Savona and Ciarli, 2019). Systemic considerations aside, successful solutions to these pressing problems are likely to require coordination between a number of different organizations, private and public bodies, and individual behavioral changes. New technologies and scientific advances are likely to play an important role, though individual advances are unlikely to be sufficient. Addressing complex societal issues and helping achieve sustainable development goals requires a similarly complex set of responses, where coordination and alignment play a pivotal role. This therefore presents an opportunity for an intentional sandbox design for the virtual AI agent economy, with the intention of effectively coordinating AI agents and aligning their behavior with the prescribed mission objective.
当今社会面临的问题日益复杂、多面，且影响深远。这些问题也越来越超越局部范围，呈现出更强的全球性 (Söderholm, 2020)。寻找可行的解决方案和政策，以帮助应对气候变化、生物多样性丧失、塑料污染、流行病等诸多危机，也具有高度紧迫性 (Arora and Mishra, 2019)。由于这些危机至少部分源于现有社会经济体系及政策的外部性，要有效应对这些危机，很可能需要作出某些改变，尽管潜在改变的性质和范围仍是一个充满争议的话题 (Elliott, 2005; Chouinard et al., 2011; Dietz et al., 2013; Geissdoerfer et al., 2017; Barrier, 2017; Savona and Ciarli, 2019)。撇开系统性因素不谈，要成功解决这些紧迫问题，很可能需要多个不同组织、公私部门机构之间的协调，以及个人行为的改变。新技术和科学进步很可能发挥重要作用，但单项进展不太可能足以解决问题。应对复杂的社会问题并推动实现可持续发展目标，需要一套同样复杂的应对措施，其中协调与对齐发挥着关键作用。因此，这为有目的地设计虚拟 AI 智能体经济的沙盒提供了机会，旨在有效协调 AI 智能体，并使其行为与既定的使命目标保持一致。
Opportunities机遇
While achieving large-scale coordination in human societies is challenging, it may potentially be possible to achieve a higher degree of coordination between AI agents through a carefully crafted technological infrastructure and a set of corresponding protocols. Agent markets in particular may then be oriented towards socially beneficial objectives, at previously unprecedented scales, presuming that such objectives have been established and decided upon appropriately. Utilization of markets and market-shaping policies in the process of establishing mission economies has been previously discussed (Mazzucato, 2018), and in the context of AI agents the role of reward-shaping in facilitating collaboration in multi-agent systems is similarly well-recognized.
尽管在人类社会中实现大规模协调充满挑战，但通过精心构建的技术基础设施及一套相应协议，或许能够在 AI 智能体之间实现更高程度的协调。尤其是智能体市场，在相关目标得到恰当确立和决定的前提下，便可能以前所未有的规模朝着有益于社会的目标运行。此前已有研究讨论了在建立使命型经济的过程中运用市场和市场塑造政策 (Mazzucato, 2018)，而在 AI 智能体的背景下，奖励塑造在促进多智能体系统协作方面的作用也同样得到了广泛认可。
Successful coordination towards large-scale missions would require an active participation of the public sector (Mazzucato, 2015), as well as the international governing bodies more globally (Georgeson and Maslin, 2018), for addressing crises with a global impact. It may also require establishing bespoke organizations to facilitate mission-aligned investments towards SDG-s (Mazzucato, 2023). More explicit mission-alignment in markets may be required to align the social and economic mission of existing social enterprises (Stevens et al., 2015).
要围绕大规模使命实现成功协调，以应对具有全球影响的危机，需要公共部门的积极参与 (Mazzucato, 2015)，也需要国际治理机构在更广泛的全球层面参与其中 (Georgeson and Maslin, 2018)。这可能还需要建立专门的组织，以促进面向可持续发展目标（SDG-s）、与使命保持一致的投资 (Mazzucato, 2023)。可能需要在市场中更加明确地推动使命对齐，以使现有社会企业的社会使命与经济使命保持一致 (Stevens et al., 2015)。
Despite the opportunity and the long-term optimism, mission-oriented approaches are yet to bear fruit in many areas where they have been proposed and envisioned, and a number of criticisms have been levelled against the overall strategy. In (Kirchherr et al., 2023), the authors provide five distinct critiques: normativity bias, support for top-down governance, stakeholder monotony, winner-picking, and unintended consequences. In terms of normativity, the mission objective definitions need to be critically evaluated, and there needs to be more recognition of the complexity of problems, which may fail to map onto more simplistic objective formulations. This is also closely tied to the notion of unintended consequences, as positive action towards one mission (e.g. environment) may have adverse affected on other missions (e.g. human well-being by preventing the growth of emerging economies) (Kirchherr et al., 2023). The assumption of the utility of top-down governance in establishing missions often neglects to factor in the failure modes of centralized decision-making, the interconnectedness of private and public interests, and the emerging governmental pathologies (Tukker and Ekins, 2019; Howlett, 2022). Top-down intervention bias also risks under-valuing the contribution of non-governmental organizations and decentralized initiatives that facilitate collective action. Policies that are not solution-agnostic, but rather bias towards specific solutions in their metrics may end up favouring certain sectors and organizations and screening out the rest. Mission-centered markets should therefore aim to avoid preconceptions and aligning with the winning institutions and industries, and remain primarily outcome-driven. Finally, it is important to recognize and factor in a large degree of uncertainty in making future predictions, when missions are anchored in predictive models (Dovers and Handmer, 1992).
尽管存在机遇，也有长期的乐观预期，但使命导向的方法在许多曾被提出并寄予期望的领域尚未取得成果，而且这一总体战略也受到了一系列批评。在 (Kirchherr et al., 2023) 中，作者提出了五项不同的批评：规范性偏见、对自上而下治理的支持、利益相关者构成单一、挑选赢家，以及意外后果。在规范性方面，需要批判性地评估使命目标的定义，并更加充分地认识到问题的复杂性，因为这种复杂性可能无法对应于较为简单的目标表述。这也与意外后果的概念密切相关，因为推动某一使命（例如环境保护）的积极行动，可能对其他使命产生不利影响（例如，阻碍新兴经济体增长，进而损害人类福祉）(Kirchherr et al., 2023)。认为自上而下的治理有助于确立使命这一假设，往往未能将集中式决策的失效模式、私人利益与公共利益之间的相互关联，以及新出现的政府治理病态纳入考虑 (Tukker and Ekins, 2019; Howlett, 2022)。偏重自上而下的干预，还可能低估非政府组织和促进集体行动的去中心化倡议所作出的贡献。如果政策并未对各种解决方案保持中立，而是在衡量指标上偏向特定解决方案，最终就可能偏袒某些行业和组织，并将其余行业和组织排除在外。因此，以使命为中心的市场应力求避免先入之见，避免向获胜的机构和行业靠拢，并始终以结果为主要导向。最后，当使命以预测模型为依据时，必须认识到未来预测存在很大程度的不确定性，并将其纳入考虑 (Dovers and Handmer, 1992)。
Virtual agent economies may prove valuable in addressing some of these practical challenges that have been limiting the impact of local and global mission-centered initiatives to date. While they may not in and of themselves be sufficient to fully address these issues, as their resolution may necessarily need to involve active human participation and coordination in conjunction with the coordination across AI agents and autonomous organizations, how we envision these economies is likely to be consequential for broader societal missions.
虚拟智能体经济可能有助于应对其中一些实际挑战；迄今为止，这些挑战一直限制着地方和全球层面以使命为中心的倡议所能产生的影响。尽管虚拟智能体经济本身可能不足以彻底解决这些问题，因为解决这些问题可能必须在 AI 智能体与自治组织之间开展协调的同时，让人类积极参与并进行协调，但我们如何构想这些经济，很可能会对更广泛的社会使命产生重要影响。
Perhaps in some ways it may be easier to bring about the coordination of AI agents through the combination of 1) formal, programmatical mechanisms, and 2) via the value assignment mechanisms embedded in digital assets, than it would be to coordinate human actors. We may assign some credence to this conjecture from the perspective of predictability and steerability of AI agents when contrasted with the complex motivations behind human actions. Existing applications of AI systems in supply chain and logistics optimization may be seen as an early example of a use case where the complexity of the problem makes it a good fit for the utilization of AI agents (Schuldt, 2012; Jannelli et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024a; Xu et al., 2024b). Virtual agent economies may further enforce outcomes through smart contracts (Zou et al., 2019) and perform automatic verification to ensure alignment of agents and multi-agent systems. Other than the potential ambiguity in objective specification, one of the main practical challenges may arise not from the agent-agent interactions as much as the agent-human interactions, in hybrid markets. Facilitating efficient human-AI coordination and collaboration remains an open challenge (Carroll et al., 2019; Yan et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024c; Strouse et al., 2021).
或许在某些方面，通过结合 1) 形式化、程序化的机制，以及 2) 嵌入数字资产的价值赋予机制，来实现 AI 智能体之间的协调，会比协调人类行动者更容易。相较于人类行为背后的复杂动机，从 AI 智能体的可预测性和可引导性来看，我们或许可以认为这一猜想具有一定可信度。AI 系统在供应链和物流优化中的现有应用，可以视为一类应用场景的早期实例：问题的复杂性使其非常适合采用 AI 智能体 (Schuldt, 2012; Jannelli et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024a; Xu et al., 2024b)。虚拟智能体经济还可以通过智能合约 (Zou et al., 2019) 强制落实结果，并进行自动验证，以确保智能体及多智能体系统的对齐。除了目标设定中可能存在的歧义之外，在混合市场中，一项主要的实际挑战可能更多地源于智能体与人类之间的交互，而非智能体之间的交互。促进高效的人类与 AI 之间的协调与协作，仍是一项尚未解决的挑战 (Carroll et al., 2019; Yan et al., 2023; Zhao et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024c; Strouse et al., 2021)。
Challenges挑战
Mission-alignment in AI agents is related to value-alignment. While value alignment (Gabriel, 2020; Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell, 2020; Ji et al., 2023) and goal-alignment (Li and Lee, 2022) in advanced AI agents remain an open and important research topic, value-aligned agents may be able to cooperatively solve tasks and identify promising solutions (Lujak et al., 2023). Unlike the more general value and preference alignment problem where there may be fundamental limitations in how diverse preferences and values are incorporated in individual systems (Mishra, 2023), mission-centered AI value alignment may prove easier in terms of the clarity of the mission and the objectives, presuming that the mission itself has been arrived at through consensus and the appropriate set of social and democratic processes. Yet, there are other potential difficulties, given that these are no longer issues pertaining to individual agents, rather societal issues pertaining to groups of agents interacting within virtual markets. In this context, AI alignment needs to take into account dynamic environment feedback and the alignment of multi-agent systems where individual AI agents co-adapt and co-shape the joint system response (Raab, 2024; Leibo et al., 2025). Societies themselves are not static and these systems may need to adapt to evolving priorities, views and social norms (Harland et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024b; Yang et al., 2024b; Carroll et al., 2024). We should in any case distinguish between the, perhaps harder, problem of broad value alignment with the more specific and targeted alignment towards rewards (Leike et al., 2018; Gupta et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024c; Yang et al., 2024a; Khanov et al., 2024) and objectives that are explicitly specified and provided via virtual agent currencies in digital economies. Even in environments with clearly stated objectives and rewards, overall alignment still plays an important role, given that advanced AI agents may exhibit deceptive behaviors aimed at receiving rewards without actually performing aligned actions towards the underlying objective (Ngo et al., 2022). There are also issues of reward-hacking (Skalse et al., 2022; Pan et al., 2022) to take into account, underscoring the need for careful and robust design of mission objectives, their decomposition into sub-tasks, reward shaping (Chen et al., 2024b; Liu et al., 2024b; Fu et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025), and the credit assignment to specific actions and outcomes. Regulating AI agent behaviors through markets allows for rapid responses and adjustments to the changing societal needs, as well as potentially undesirable or suboptimal agent behaviors, bridging the gap between development and deployment objective specifications (Zhang et al., 2024). Finally, there are numerous technical challenges in ensuring consensus in multi-agent systems (Ren et al., 2005; Blondel et al., 2005; Wang et al., 2014).
AI 智能体的使命对齐与价值对齐有关。尽管先进 AI 智能体的价值对齐 (Gabriel, 2020; Zhuang and Hadfield-Menell, 2020; Ji et al., 2023) 和目标对齐 (Li and Lee, 2022) 仍是尚未解决的重要研究课题，但实现价值对齐的智能体或许能够合作完成任务，并找出有前景的解决方案 (Lujak et al., 2023)。在更一般的价值与偏好对齐问题中，将多样化的偏好和价值观纳入单个系统的方式可能存在根本性局限 (Mishra, 2023)；与之不同，以使命为中心的 AI 价值对齐可能因使命和目标较为明确而更容易实现，前提是使命本身已经通过共识以及一套适当的社会和民主程序确定。然而，还存在其他潜在困难，因为这些问题已不再是涉及单个智能体的问题，而是涉及在虚拟市场中相互交互的智能体群体的社会性问题。在这一背景下，AI 对齐需要考虑动态的环境反馈，以及多智能体系统的对齐；在这类系统中，各个 AI 智能体会相互适应，并共同塑造整个系统的响应 (Raab, 2024; Leibo et al., 2025)。社会本身并非静止不变，这些系统可能需要适应不断变化的优先事项、观念和社会规范 (Harland et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024b; Yang et al., 2024b; Carroll et al., 2024)。无论如何，我们都应区分两类问题：一类是可能更困难的广泛价值对齐问题，另一类是更具体、更有针对性的对齐，即对齐那些在数字经济中通过虚拟智能体货币明确设定并提供的奖励 (Leike et al., 2018; Gupta et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2024c; Yang et al., 2024a; Khanov et al., 2024) 和目标。即使在目标和奖励明确的环境中，整体对齐仍然发挥着重要作用，因为先进 AI 智能体可能表现出欺骗行为，旨在获取奖励，却并未实际采取与根本目标一致的行动 (Ngo et al., 2022)。还需要考虑奖励破解（reward-hacking）问题 (Skalse et al., 2022; Pan et al., 2022)，这凸显出有必要对使命目标、将其分解为子任务的方式、奖励塑形 (Chen et al., 2024b; Liu et al., 2024b; Fu et al., 2025; Wang et al., 2025)，以及针对具体行动和结果的贡献分配进行审慎而稳健的设计。通过市场调节 AI 智能体的行为，可以针对不断变化的社会需求，以及可能不受欢迎或并非最优的智能体行为，迅速作出响应和调整，从而弥合开发阶段与部署阶段在目标设定上的差距 (Zhang et al., 2024)。最后，确保多智能体系统达成共识还面临诸多技术挑战 (Ren et al., 2005; Blondel et al., 2005; Wang et al., 2014)。
Infrastructure基础设施
An intentional safe design of AI agent sandboxes and steerable AI agent markets hinges on the development of robust underlying infrastructure for facilitating and overseeing transactions and implementing guardrails. Here we discuss some of the key infrastructural prerequisites for such markets.
对 AI 智能体沙盒和可引导的 AI 智能体市场进行有意识的安全设计，有赖于开发稳健的底层基础设施，以促进和监督交易并落实安全防护措施。这里，我们讨论此类市场的一些关键基础设施前提条件。
Opportunities机遇
Reputation mechanisms and verification protocols may play an important role in establishing robust and safe multi-agent collaboration. In the end, sandbox economies may be used mainly due to their superior infrastructure for verifiable and auditable cross-agent transactions and ease of coordination between registered and certified AI agents under the appropriate safety framework and supervision.
声誉机制和验证协议可能在建立稳健、安全的多智能体协作方面发挥重要作用。最终，人们采用沙盒经济，可能主要是因为它们拥有更优越的基础设施，能够支持可验证、可审计的跨智能体交易，并使已注册和认证的 AI 智能体能够在适当的安全框架与监督下便捷地协调。
One way that reputation could be made concrete and machine-readable is through the use of Verifiable Credentials (VCs), as the digital equivalent of physical credentials like licenses or certificates (Sedlmeir et al., 2021; Mazzocca et al., 2025). VCs are cryptographically signed attestations provided by an issuer in relation to a subject, that are designed to be tamper-evident. When it comes to the possible role of VCs in establishing trust across multi-agent cliques within the agent economy, they may help establish formal trust triangles:
使声誉具体化并可供机器读取的一种方法，是使用可验证凭证（Verifiable Credentials，VCs），它们相当于执照或证书等实体凭证的数字版本 (Sedlmeir et al., 2021; Mazzocca et al., 2025)。VCs 是由签发者针对某一主体提供的、经过密码学签名的证明，其设计旨在使任何篡改都可被察觉。就 VCs 在智能体经济中不同多智能体群体之间建立信任的潜在作用而言，它们可能有助于建立形式化的信任三角关系：
An Issuer agent (e.g., a marketplace) can cryptographically sign and issue a VC to a seller agent.
签发者智能体（例如，某个交易市场）可以通过密码学签名，向卖方智能体签发一份 VC。
The seller agent (the Holder) stores this VC as proof of its track record.
卖方智能体（持有者）存储这份 VC，作为其过往表现的证明。
A future buyer agent (a Verifier) can then request and cryptographically verify this VC, trusting it only if they trust the issuer.
未来的买方智能体（验证者）随后可以请求获取这份 VC，并通过密码学方法验证它，且只有在信任签发者的情况下才信任该凭证。
Reputation could thereby map onto a portfolio of tangible, verifiable assets. These assets may attest to a wide range of more specific resources, such as "successful transaction completion," "certified proficiency in X", "access to X compute and Y memory," or perhaps even "implementing fair resource allocation". Should an AI agent’s reputation be represented as an aggregate of such VCs across diverse issuers, this would render it formally auditable, while simultaneously allowing for specificity and expressivity needed to fit specific use cases and scenarios.
由此，声誉可以映射为一组具体、可验证的资产。这些资产可以证明多种更具体的资源，例如“成功完成交易”、“经认证具备 X 方面的熟练能力”、“可使用 X 算力和 Y 内存”，甚至可能包括“实施公平的资源分配”。如果将 AI 智能体的声誉表示为来自不同签发者的此类 VCs 的集合，就能使其在形式上可审计，同时具备适应具体用例和场景所需的针对性与表达能力。
Forthcoming multi-agent systems will also require appropriate legislative and regulatory frameworks, enabling regulators to impose sanctions on bad actors and potentially revoke any previously issued credentials. Such frameworks may go as far as choosing to render transactions with unverified and non-registered agents illegal (Hadfield et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2025; Shavit et al., 2023), in order to ensure safety and create the institutional capacity to sanction agents that break the rules by kicking them out of the network. Technological solutions such as oversight agents (Etzioni and Etzioni, 2016; Busuioc, 2022) may be able to help facilitate this sort of governance at scale. And, given the likelihood of high frequency and volume of AI agent-to-agent transactions, such oversight agents will surely be critical. However, they cannot be effective on their own. A broad governance framework will be needed to establish the necessary grounding for their operations.
未来的多智能体系统还需要适当的立法和监管框架，使监管机构能够对不良行为者施加制裁，并可能撤销此前签发的任何凭证。此类框架甚至可能选择将与未经验证、未注册的智能体进行交易规定为非法 (Hadfield et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2025; Shavit et al., 2023)，以确保安全，并建立通过将违规智能体逐出网络来对其实施制裁的制度能力。监督智能体等技术解决方案 (Etzioni and Etzioni, 2016; Busuioc, 2022) 可能有助于大规模开展此类治理。而且，考虑到 AI 智能体之间的交易很可能频率高、数量大，这类监督智能体无疑将至关重要。然而，它们无法仅凭自身有效发挥作用。需要一个全面的治理框架，为它们的运作奠定必要的基础。
Coordinating large-scale systems of advanced AI assistants would not be possible without communication protocols to allow agents to exchange information, interact, debate, reach mutual decisions and agreements, and negotiate the future course of action. There is a similar requirement in terms of enabling agents to use tools, interact with services and execute actions in the environment, with varying degrees of human oversight.
如果没有通信协议来支持智能体交换信息、互动、辩论、共同作出决策并达成协议，以及协商未来的行动方案，就不可能协调由先进 AI 助手构成的大规模系统。在使智能体能够在不同程度的人类监督下使用工具、与服务交互并在环境中执行行动方面，也存在类似的需求。
Agent interaction protocols such as Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol (Google, 2025) aim to support agent interoperability. Model Context Protocol (MCP) (Anthropic, 2024; Microsoft, 2025), on the other hand, enables AI agents to interact seamlessly with external tools, data sources, and APIs. The AgentDNS system aims to enable easier service discovery in order to autonomously identify and invoke third-party tools and agents across organizations (Cui et al., 2025). The COALESCE framework (Bhatt et al., 2025) similarly introduces options that would allow for individual agents to decompose their tasks and outsource each sub-task if needed to other more specialized agents that may be able solve it either more reliably or more cost-effectively. This involves options for representing and communicating skills, skill discovery, but also mechanisms that enable agents to evaluate and compare internal vs external computational and execution costs associated with the individual sub-tasks. Interoperable communication protocols are necessary for establishing cross-agent cooperation but otherwise not sufficient, as reliable solutions for authentication and billing are a pre-requisite for large-scale agent markets.
Agent2Agent (A2A) 协议 (Google, 2025) 等智能体交互协议旨在支持智能体之间的互操作性。另一方面，模型上下文协议（Model Context Protocol，MCP）(Anthropic, 2024; Microsoft, 2025) 使 AI 智能体能够与外部工具、数据源和 APIs 无缝交互。AgentDNS 系统旨在简化服务发现，以便跨组织自主识别和调用第三方工具与智能体 (Cui et al., 2025)。COALESCE 框架 (Bhatt et al., 2025) 同样引入了一些方案，使单个智能体能够分解自身任务，并在需要时将各个子任务外包给其他更专业的智能体，后者或许能够以更高的可靠性或成本效益解决这些子任务。这既涉及技能表示、技能信息传达和技能发现的方案，也涉及使智能体能够评估和比较各个子任务所对应的内部与外部计算及执行成本的机制。可互操作的通信协议是建立跨智能体合作的必要条件，但并非充分条件，因为可靠的身份认证和计费解决方案是大规模智能体市场的前提。
We argue that auctions may enable AI agent coordination and preference alignment. Preliminary frameworks aiming to provide infrastructure to underpin these and similar ideas are already being developed. For example, Agent Exchange (AEX) (Yang et al., 2025) supports a specialized auction platform inspired by real-time bidding mechanisms commonly employed in online advertising. AEX integrates four different components: the User-Side Platform (USP), the Agent-Side Platform (ASP), Agent Hubs, and the Data Management Platform (DMP). USP translates human goals into actionable tasks for the AI agents, ASP tracks agent capabilities and performance, Agent Hubs coordinate agent teams that participate in auctions, and DMP allows for fair value attribution to data sources being used.
我们认为，拍卖可能有助于实现 AI 智能体的协调与偏好对齐。旨在提供基础设施以支撑这些及类似构想的初步框架已经在开发之中。例如，Agent Exchange (AEX) (Yang et al., 2025) 支持一个专门的拍卖平台，其灵感源于在线广告中普遍采用的实时竞价机制。AEX 集成了四个不同的组成部分：用户侧平台（User-Side Platform，USP）、智能体侧平台（Agent-Side Platform，ASP）、智能体枢纽（Agent Hubs）和数据管理平台（Data Management Platform，DMP）。USP 将人类目标转化为 AI 智能体可执行的任务，ASP 跟踪智能体的能力与表现，Agent Hubs 协调参与拍卖的智能体团队，而 DMP 则支持对所使用的数据来源进行公平的价值归因。
While interoperable communication protocols are necessary, they must be built upon a robust and secure identity layer. To facilitate trusted interactions, each agent in the economy could be anchored to a Decentralized Identifier (DID). A DID is a globally unique identifier that is controlled by its subject—in this case, the AI agent or its owner—without reliance on a central authority. Each DID resolves to a corresponding DID Document, a machine-readable file containing the cryptographic public keys, authentication methods, and service endpoints necessary to interact with the agent securely.
尽管可互操作的通信协议必不可少，但它们必须建立在稳健且安全的身份层之上。为促进可信交互，经济体中的每个智能体都可以绑定一个去中心化标识符（Decentralized Identifier，DID）。DID 是一种全局唯一标识符，由其标识的主体——此处为 AI 智能体或其所有者——控制，而不依赖中央权威机构。每个 DID 都可解析为相应的 DID 文档，这是一种机器可读文件，包含与智能体进行安全交互所必需的密码学公钥、身份验证方法和服务端点。
The self-sovereign nature of DIDs ensures that an agent’s identity is persistent and portable across different platforms and services, enabling it to authoritatively sign transactions, issue attestations, and engage in secure communication. The choice of DID method can be tailored to the agent’s purpose; for instance:
DID 的自主主权特性确保智能体的身份能够持久存在，并可在不同平台和服务之间迁移，使其能够以权威身份签署交易、签发证明并开展安全通信。DID 方法的选择可以根据智能体的用途进行调整；例如：
did:key: A simple, self-contained method (Dave Longley, 2025) ideal for disposable agents created for temporary tasks, as the DID is derived directly from a public key and requires no network registration or blockchain.
did:key：一种简单、自包含的方法 (Dave Longley, 2025)，非常适合为临时任务创建的一次性智能体，因为其 DID 直接由公钥派生，无需网络注册或区块链。
did:ion: A highly scalable and censorship-resistant method for persistent, high-value agents. It operates as a second layer on the Bitcoin blockchain (DIF, 2025), anchoring identity data to ensure maximum security without congesting the network, making it suitable for corporate or state-level agents.
did:ion：一种高度可扩展且抗审查的方法，适用于长期存在的高价值智能体。它作为比特币区块链上的第二层运行 (DIF, 2025)，通过锚定身份数据，在不造成网络拥堵的情况下确保最高程度的安全性，因此适用于企业级或国家级智能体。
By grounding the economy in a formal identity layer, we can establish a foundation for verifiable reputation, accountable transactions, and secure, cross-platform agent markets.
通过将经济体建立在正式的身份层之上，我们可以为可验证的声誉、可追责的交易以及安全的跨平台智能体市场奠定基础。
As a technology, blockchain also enables the development of the infrastructure underpinning digital decentralized autonomous organizations (Hsieh et al., 2018; El Faqir et al., 2020; Jeyasheela Rakkini and Geetha, 2021) (DAO). DAOs have emerged as a form of collective governance, through which groups may organize and coordinate while relying on a decentralized infrastructure. One of the common features in DAOs is that they implement decision-making systems enabling the participating parties to reach agreements (Faqir-Rhazoui et al., 2021). Despite their promise in terms of enabling easier coordination without bottlenecks and the implied increase in individual freedom, arguments have been made that a hypothetical unrestricted proliferation of DAOs may not necessarily prove to be a utopian outcome, given that states and central authorities play an important role in preventing the commodification of certain items (Garrod, 2016) This perspective is something to keep in mind in the design of virtual agent economies, and the societies that integrate increasing degrees of automation.
作为一种技术，区块链还支持开发数字化去中心化自治组织（Decentralized Autonomous Organization，DAO）所依托的基础设施 (Hsieh et al., 2018; El Faqir et al., 2020; Jeyasheela Rakkini and Geetha, 2021)。DAO 已成为一种集体治理形式，群体可以借助这种形式，依托去中心化基础设施进行组织与协调。DAO 的一个共同特征是实施决策系统，使参与各方能够达成协议 (Faqir-Rhazoui et al., 2021)。尽管 DAO 有望使协调更加便捷、消除瓶颈，并由此增加个人自由，但有观点认为，假设 DAO 不受限制地扩散，其结果未必是乌托邦式的，因为国家和中央权威机构在防止某些事物商品化方面发挥着重要作用 (Garrod, 2016)；在设计虚拟智能体经济体以及自动化程度日益提高的社会时，应当牢记这一观点。
Decentralized Autonomous Machines (DAMs) (Castillo et al., 2025) build upon the concept of DAOs, while expanding it to include the possibility of AI agents, as self-governing agents participating in decentralized physical infrastructure networks. This more expansive definition envisions an economy in which AI agents may interact not only with digital, but also real-world assets. Here, the locus of control over tangible assets and operational processes shifts towards autonomous software entities, capable of making and executing decisions concerning physical infrastructure. Such systems could, for instance, manage decentralized energy grids, optimize logistics for physical goods, or even autonomously operate and maintain fleets of robotic devices, all transacting within a blockchain-secured framework.
去中心化自治机器（Decentralized Autonomous Machines，DAMs）(Castillo et al., 2025) 建立在 DAO 概念的基础之上，并将其扩展至涵盖这样一种可能性：AI 智能体作为自我治理的智能体参与去中心化物理基础设施网络。这一定义的范围更广，设想了一种经济体，其中 AI 智能体不仅可以与数字资产交互，还可以与现实世界的资产交互。在此，对有形资产和运营流程的控制权转向了自主软件实体，这些实体能够就物理基础设施作出并执行决策。例如，此类系统可以管理去中心化电网、优化实物商品的物流，甚至自主运行和维护机器人设备群，而所有交易均在由区块链保障安全的框架内进行。
Any system for fair resource allocation, particularly one involving individual users or community-level benefits, must defend against Sybil attacks (Zhang et al., 2014), where a single malicious actor creates a multitude of fake identities to unfairly claim a disproportionate share of resources. A powerful defense is to integrate a Proof-of-Personhood (PoP) mechanism (Borge et al., 2017; Adler et al., 2024), which provides a verifiable guarantee that an agent or account corresponds to a unique human being. This is an example of an intentional infrastructure choice that creates a carefully controlled point of permeability—linking a digital identity to a verified human—to ensure the integrity of the system.
任何旨在公平分配资源的系统，尤其是涉及个人用户或社区层面利益的系统，都必须防范女巫攻击（Sybil attacks）(Zhang et al., 2014)，即单个恶意行为者创建大量虚假身份，以不公平地获取不成比例的资源份额。一种有力的防御措施是集成人格证明（Proof-of-Personhood，PoP）机制 (Borge et al., 2017; Adler et al., 2024)，为智能体或账户对应唯一一个真实人类提供可验证的保证。这是有意识地选择基础设施的一个例子：通过将数字身份与经过验证的人类关联起来，建立一个受到严格控制的渗透点，以确保系统的完整性。
To receive certain allocations—such as a universal basic income in a community currency (see Section:Community) or an initial stake in the market—an agent’s controller could be required to present a PoP credential. This credential would be issued by a specialized system and would attest to their uniqueness. 若要获得某些分配——例如以社区货币发放的全民基本收入（见 Section:Community）或市场中的初始份额——智能体的控制者可能需要出示人格证明（PoP）凭证。该凭证将由专门的系统签发，用于证明其持有者的唯一性。该生态系统可以支持多种相互竞争的 PoP 方法，每种方法都有不同的权衡，例如：
Social Graph Verification: Systems like BrightID (Siddarth et al., 2020) create a decentralized social graph where users are verified as unique based on connections to other trusted, verified humans.
社交图谱验证：BrightID（Siddarth et al., 2020）等系统构建去中心化社交图谱，根据用户与其他可信且已通过验证的人类之间的联系，验证用户的唯一性。
Privacy-Preserving Biometrics: Projects like Worldcoin (team, 2025) use hardware (the "Orb") to scan a user’s iris, generating a unique hash that confirms uniqueness without storing or revealing the biometric data itself.
隐私保护型生物特征识别：Worldcoin（team, 2025）等项目使用硬件（“Orb”）扫描用户的虹膜，生成唯一的哈希值，从而在不存储或披露生物特征数据本身的情况下确认用户的唯一性。
By requiring PoP for certain economic activities, we can ensure that schemes designed to benefit human users are not drained by bots, thereby creating a more robust and genuinely fair virtual economy.
通过要求在某些经济活动中提供 PoP，我们可以确保旨在惠及人类用户的方案不会被机器人耗尽资源，从而创建更稳健且真正公平的虚拟经济。
Inter-agent communication protocols and marketplaces may enable agents to reveal their needs and preferences, negotiate through HFNs and work toward obtaining resources required to achieve their goals. While some resources may be centrally managed and require arbitration of access, others may be accessible via decentralized mechanisms. This is where blockchain may prove to be a promising technology (Baranwal et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2023; Vashishth et al., 2024). Early proof-of-concept studies have shown that paired agent auctions using self-contained digital assets may yield positive outcomes, presuming that the agents are future-aware and able to evaluate the consequences of conceding on specific choices (Elokda et al., 2024).
智能体间通信协议和交易市场可以使智能体表达自身需求与偏好，通过 HFNs 进行协商，并努力获取实现其目标所需的资源。尽管某些资源可能由中心化方式管理，并需要对访问权限进行仲裁，但另一些资源可能可以通过去中心化机制获取。在这方面，区块链可能被证明是一项前景可期的技术（Baranwal et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2023; Vashishth et al., 2024）。早期概念验证研究表明，使用自包含数字资产的成对智能体拍卖可能产生积极结果，前提是智能体能够考虑未来，并评估在特定选择上作出让步所带来的后果（Elokda et al., 2024）。
Challenges挑战
To unlock the potential of AI agents as economic actors within digital markets, the economic infrastructure needs to be adjusted as it is currently designed solely for individual and corporate human users (Sanabria and Vecino, 2025), and it may not meet all the necessary requirements for a sandbox virtual AI agent economy. Yet, this is not the only barrier, as there are potentially additional technical challenges when it comes to scaling the coordination of agentic systems.
要释放 AI 智能体作为数字市场中经济参与者的潜力，就需要调整经济基础设施，因为现有基础设施完全是为人类个人用户和企业用户设计的（Sanabria and Vecino, 2025），可能无法满足沙盒式虚拟 AI 智能体经济的全部必要要求。然而，这并非唯一障碍，因为在扩大智能体系统的协调规模时，还可能存在其他技术挑战。
The infrastructural needs for establishing a sandbox AI agent economy span beyond the purely technical hardware and software infrastructure needed to physically instantiate and run these advanced AI agents at scale, have them communicate, coordinate, transact, and interact with users as well as various other services. All of this needs to be complemented by legislative frameworks and institutions that would implement oversight and ensure accountability for the actions of AI agents, in order to protect users and prevent fraud. Regulation may also be beneficial more broadly to regulate complexity in markets in order to minimize the likelihood of catastrophic failure (Schwarcz, 2009), and virtual AI agent economies may potentially exhibit even more complexity than our current markets, should they not be properly constrained through appropriate frameworks. Our existing markets certainly require, and benefit from, legislative frameworks (McCormick, 2010; Tatom, 2011; Moloney, 2023), as well as financial institutions (Kohn, 2003; Tarashev et al., 2009), and established mechanisms for dealing with rule-breaking and fraudulent behavior (Gotelaere and Paoli, 2025). To more effectively address the emerging use case of cryptocurrencies, there have been ongoing adjustments aimed at regulating cryptocurrency markets (Greebel et al., 2015; Hughes, 2017; Lee and L’heureux, 2020; Feinstein and Werbach, 2021; Courtois et al., 2021).
建立沙盒式 AI 智能体经济所需的基础设施，不仅包括在物理层面实现并大规模运行这些先进 AI 智能体，使其能够通信、协调、交易，以及与用户和其他各类服务交互所需的纯技术性软硬件基础设施。所有这些都需要立法框架和相关机构作为补充，以对 AI 智能体的行为实施监督并确保问责，从而保护用户并防止欺诈。从更广泛的角度来看，监管也可能有助于控制市场复杂性，以尽量降低灾难性失灵的可能性（Schwarcz, 2009）；如果未通过适当框架加以合理约束，虚拟 AI 智能体经济所呈现的复杂性甚至可能超过当前的市场。现有市场无疑需要立法框架（McCormick, 2010; Tatom, 2011; Moloney, 2023）、金融机构（Kohn, 2003; Tarashev et al., 2009），以及处理违规和欺诈行为的成熟机制（Gotelaere and Paoli, 2025），并从中受益。为了更有效地应对加密货币这一新兴应用场景，旨在监管加密货币市场的调整一直在持续进行（Greebel et al., 2015; Hughes, 2017; Lee and L’heureux, 2020; Feinstein and Werbach, 2021; Courtois et al., 2021）。
Such frameworks may form a basis for the development of the legislative scaffolding governing AI agent markets, though it is likely that further adjustments would be needed to appropriately address liability in this context. Since AI agents are non-human actors, there may be numerous reasons why their actions would be non-conforming to the prescribed rules and principles or perhaps damaging to others. 1) faults of the underlying foundation models; 2) faults of the agentic scaffolding; 3) faulty input data in the request specification; 4) malicious requests by human users; 5) adversarial hacking by other AI agents; 6) misalignment that arises dynamically from interactions; or 7) faulty safeguards. The scale and speed of potential harm necessitates a new approach to oversight.
这些框架可以为构建治理 AI 智能体市场的立法基础架构奠定基础，但很可能仍需进一步调整，才能妥善处理这一背景下的法律责任问题。由于 AI 智能体是非人类参与者，其行为不符合既定规则和原则，或可能对他人造成损害的原因可能有很多。1) 底层基础模型的缺陷；2) 智能体支撑框架的缺陷；3) 请求规范中的输入数据有误；4) 人类用户提出恶意请求；5) 其他 AI 智能体实施对抗性黑客攻击；6) 交互过程中动态产生的失对齐；或 7) 安全防护措施存在缺陷。潜在危害的规模及其发生速度，使得采用新的监督方式成为必要。
There may well be reasons to presume that different parties may be held liable in different scenarios, depending on root cause of the problem, and also depending on whether such a root cause can be definitively established. There may also exist differences in the scale and extent of possible harm, given how many actions such AI agents may potentially be able to take in a unit of time. For that reason, AI agents may themselves potentially need to be involved in oversight, acting as preliminary judges (Zheng et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2025) and overseers of other agents, so as to be able to identify problems at the same speed at which they may otherwise manifest.
我们很可能有理由认为，在不同情境下，可能需要由不同主体承担法律责任，这取决于问题的根本原因，也取决于能否明确查明这一根本原因。考虑到此类 AI 智能体在单位时间内可能采取大量行动，潜在危害的规模和程度也可能有所不同。因此，AI 智能体自身可能也需要参与监督，担任初步评判者（Zheng et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2024; Zhuge et al., 2025）和其他智能体的监督者，以便能够以问题可能显现的同等速度识别问题。
We propose that the oversight infrastructure itself must be a hybrid, multi-tiered system operating at machine speed. The first layer would consist of automated AI overseers monitoring market activity in real-time, enforcing basic rules programmatically and flagging anomalies that suggest fraud, manipulation, or systemic risk (Etzioni and Etzioni, 2016; Busuioc, 2022). Issues flagged by this first layer could be escalated to a second tier of automated adjudication systems, which could place temporary holds on accounts or transactions while gathering relevant data for review. Only the most complex, novel, or high-stakes cases would be escalated to the third tier: human expert review, ensuring human attention is focused where it is most needed Kyriakou and Otterbacher (2023). This entire structure would depend on two critical technical foundations: immutable, cryptographically-secured ledgers that provide a tamper-proof record of all agent actions (Shekhtman and Waisbard, 2019), and standardized, interpretable audit trails that allow investigators—whether human or AI—to perform root cause analysis. Such an infrastructure does not automatically solve the complex legal question of liability, but it makes it tractable. By providing a trusted, verifiable record and a clear process for dispute resolution, it creates the necessary conditions for establishing accountability and ensuring that robust protections are in place for all market participants.
我们提出，监督基础设施本身必须是一个以机器速度运行的混合式多层级系统。第一层由自动化 AI 监督者组成，实时监测市场活动，以程序化方式执行基本规则，并标记可能表明欺诈、操纵或系统性风险的异常情况（Etzioni and Etzioni, 2016; Busuioc, 2022）。第一层标记的问题可以上报至第二层的自动化裁决系统，后者可在收集相关数据以供审查期间，暂时冻结账户或暂停交易。只有最复杂、最具新颖性或事关重大的案件才会上报至第三层，即人类专家审查，从而确保人类的注意力集中在最需要的地方 Kyriakou and Otterbacher (2023)。这一整体架构将依赖两项关键技术基础：一是不可变且受密码学保护的账本，为所有智能体行为提供防篡改记录（Shekhtman and Waisbard, 2019）；二是标准化、可解释的审计轨迹，使调查者——无论是人类还是 AI——能够开展根本原因分析。这样的基础设施不会自动解决复杂的法律责任归属问题，但能使其变得可处理。通过提供可信、可验证的记录和清晰的争议解决流程，它为确立问责机制并确保所有市场参与者都获得有力保护创造了必要条件。
Community社区
There is no reason to consider AI agent coordination only at a global scale, as local coordination within sandbox economies may prove to be more tractable and easier to facilitate. Furthermore, localized goals and objectives may be easier to agree upon and define at a greater level of detail.
没有理由仅在全球尺度上考虑 AI 智能体协调，因为沙盒经济体内部的局部协调可能更易处理，也更容易促成。此外，局部范围内的目标可能更容易达成共识，也更容易得到细致的界定。
In fact, community currencies (Michel and Hudon, 2015) present an interesting model for introducing incentives for people to coordinate towards achieving sustainability development goals (Seyfang, 2006; Seyfang and Longhurst, 2013). The communities in question may be defined in terms of geographic boundaries, or more broadly shared common interests irrespective of location. These alternative currencies are issued by citizens, non-government organization, private and public companies, as well as public administrations. Existing community currencies have been implemented through a variety of different platforms and technological approaches, from traditional card-based systems, through mobile payment networks, and blockchain (Diniz et al., 2019). Some community currencies have also been implemented as time banks (Collom and Lasker, 2016). Others have experimented with universal basic income (Avanzo et al., 2023). Early studies of the effects of community currencies have showcased their potential in improving social capital as measured through community participation and proliferating links within social groups, while simultaneously highlighting a number of limitations and obstacles for adoption (Collom, 2011; Sanz, 2016; Fare and Ahmed, 2017).
事实上，社区货币（Michel and Hudon, 2015）提供了一种值得关注的模式，可通过引入激励机制，促使人们协调行动以实现可持续发展目标（Seyfang, 2006; Seyfang and Longhurst, 2013）。这里所指的社区可以按地理边界界定，也可以更广泛地按不受地域限制的共同兴趣来界定。这些替代性货币由公民、非政府组织、私营和公营公司以及公共行政机构发行。现有社区货币已通过多种不同的平台和技术路径实现，从传统的卡式系统，到移动支付网络，再到区块链（Diniz et al., 2019）。一些社区货币也采用了时间银行的形式（Collom and Lasker, 2016）。另一些则尝试了全民基本收入（Avanzo et al., 2023）。关于社区货币影响的早期研究表明，社区货币具有提升社会资本的潜力，这体现为社区参与程度的提高和社会群体内部联结的增加；这些研究同时也指出了其推广应用面临的若干局限和障碍（Collom, 2011; Sanz, 2016; Fare and Ahmed, 2017）。
Opportunities机遇
Sub-networks of cooperative agents have been shown to arise naturally in the circulation of existing localized digital community currencies, with the emergence of local hubs of activity (Mattsson et al., 2023). Non-commercial transactions may also help expand commercial transactions in the local economy, while the commercial transactions help facilitate circulation flow of community currency for non-commercial transactions (Kichiji and Nishibe, 2008). Yet, the dynamics of community currencies can not be decoupled from scale at which they operate, and larger-scale digital currencies may potentially give rise to different underlying market dynamics.
已有研究表明，在现有本地化数字社区货币的流通过程中，合作智能体子网络会自然形成，并伴随局部活动枢纽的涌现（Mattsson et al., 2023）。非商业交易也可能有助于扩大当地经济中的商业交易，而商业交易则有助于促进用于非商业交易的社区货币流通（Kichiji and Nishibe, 2008）。然而，社区货币的动态特征无法与其运行规模分离，更大规模的数字货币可能会产生不同的底层市场动态。
In the context of AI agent economies, community currencies present a similar opportunity for more localized agent alignment, or global alignment towards specific sub-goals, that map onto distinct global communities. Such alignment may be possible to achieve through more traditional currencies, though there are also reasons to consider more specialized community currencies as an additional mechanism that may prove to be beneficial. In particular, having a number of specialized virtual agent currencies may lend itself to more modular approaches towards otherwise complex multi-objective optimization problems of interest to society, while also isolating the risks and minimizing the chances of adverse outcomes escalating across wider agent networks. The value of modularity and redundancy has been well recognized in the design of robust human markets (Kharrazi et al., 2020). Furthermore, it has been shown that modular community structures play a pivotal role in the emergence of cooperative behavior (Marcoux and Lusseau, 2013; Gianetto and Heydari, 2015).
在 AI 智能体经济的背景下，社区货币提供了类似的机遇，可实现更局部的智能体对齐，或围绕特定子目标实现全球范围的对齐，而这些子目标分别对应不同的全球性社区。这种对齐或许可以通过更传统的货币实现，但也有理由将更专门化的社区货币视为一种可能有益的补充机制。具体而言，设置多种专门化的虚拟智能体货币，可能有助于以更模块化的方式处理社会所关注的、原本复杂的多目标优化问题，同时隔离风险，并尽可能降低不利后果在更广泛的智能体网络中蔓延升级的可能性。模块化与冗余的价值在稳健的人类市场设计中已得到充分认可（Kharrazi et al., 2020）。此外，已有研究表明，模块化社群结构在合作行为的涌现中发挥着关键作用 (Marcoux and Lusseau, 2013; Gianetto and Heydari, 2015)。
There is also a possibility to tie the AI agent community currencies more specifically to shared compute resources, given the relevance of compute to the deployment of advanced AI assistants, and the inference-time scaling laws (Wu et al., 2024b) which indicate that solving complex tasks may require more computation and incur a higher environmental cost (Strubell et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2022; Luccioni and Hernandez-Garcia, 2023; Xue, 2023; Luccioni et al., 2024; Cottier et al., 2024; Sastry et al., 2024). Given that the demand for AI services is likely to increase with improved AI agent performance and efficiency (Luccioni et al., 2025), such mechanisms may play a role in addressing the computational needs of AI agents, while providing communities with mechanisms for ensuring a more equitable allocation of resources, aligned with the community objectives. It would also be possible to incorporate objectives to help facilitate geographic load balancing of compute allocation so as to more equitably distribute the environmental impact (Hajiesmaili et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024a).
鉴于计算资源对于部署先进 AI 助手的重要性，以及推理时扩展定律 (Wu et al., 2024b) 表明解决复杂任务可能需要更多计算并造成更高的环境成本，也可以将 AI 智能体社区货币更明确地与共享计算资源挂钩 (Strubell et al., 2020; Wu et al., 2022; Luccioni and Hernandez-Garcia, 2023; Xue, 2023; Luccioni et al., 2024; Cottier et al., 2024; Sastry et al., 2024)。鉴于对 AI 服务的需求很可能随着 AI 智能体性能和效率的提高而增长 (Luccioni et al., 2025)，此类机制可能有助于满足 AI 智能体的计算需求，同时为社区提供相应机制，以确保资源分配更加公平，并与社区目标保持一致。还可以纳入有助于促进计算资源分配在地理层面实现负载均衡的目标，从而更公平地分摊环境影响 (Hajiesmaili et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024a)。
Challenges挑战
For the alternative currencies to be successful in achieving their purported goals, they need to be carefully designed, and determining a set of recommended design principles for community currencies is an open problem. One may consider grounding the design of community currencies in principles for governing common resources (Ostrom, 1990; Siqueira et al., 2020), or alternatively (Chasin et al., 2020), propose focusing on competitiveness, transparency, self-government, circulation velocity, non-transferability, legitimacy, and self-organizing locality. Competitiveness is required so as to arrive at fairly priced goods, though in these markets the demand for social activities tends to be high while the supply tends to be low. The authors argue that strong market mechanisms ought to be established at the core of the non-profit and voluntary sector, coupled with continuous and appropriate funding streams. Transparency in community currencies helps individuals sidestep the delegation of control and more directly exercise regulatory power and oversight, as stakeholders in the shared social common. Circulation velocity is important to avoid hoarding. Non-transferability would imply the inability to exchange community currencies, so as to ensure the interests are kept entirely local. A less strict interpretation would be to keep any potential exchange at a fairly low limit. Legitimacy tends to be established through the support of the government and the local authorities. As for locality and self-organization, community currencies need not operate in full isolation, rather forming an ecosystem of complementary currencies that spans across locations, helping drive beneficial and sustainable outcomes. Given that achieving such outcomes is at the core of what community currencies are being designed for, it is critical to have clear goal specifications, impact assessment criteria, full understanding of the deployment context, and the appropriate governance and implementation mechanisms (Diniz et al., 2024).
替代货币若要成功实现其宣称的目标，就需要经过精心设计，而确定一套推荐的社区货币设计原则仍是一个尚待解决的问题。可以考虑以公共资源治理原则为基础设计社区货币 (Ostrom, 1990; Siqueira et al., 2020)，或者采用另一种思路，即 (Chasin et al., 2020) 所提出的，重点关注竞争性、透明度、自治、流通速度、不可转让性、合法性，以及具有自组织能力的地方性。要实现商品的公平定价，就需要具备竞争性，但在这些市场中，对社会活动的需求往往较高，而供给往往较低。作者认为，应当在非营利和志愿服务部门的核心建立强有力的市场机制，并辅以持续且适当的资金来源。社区货币的透明度有助于个人避免将控制权委托给他人，并以共享社会公地利益相关者的身份，更直接地行使监管权和监督权。流通速度对于避免囤积十分重要。不可转让性意味着社区货币不能兑换，以确保利益完全留在本地。一种较为宽松的解释是，将任何可能发生的兑换限制在相当低的额度内。合法性往往通过政府和地方当局的支持得以确立。至于地方性和自组织，社区货币无须完全孤立地运行，而是可以形成一个跨越不同地区的互补货币生态系统，帮助实现有益且可持续的结果。鉴于实现此类结果正是社区货币设计的核心目的，明确的目标说明、影响评估标准、对部署情境的充分理解，以及适当的治理和实施机制就至关重要 (Diniz et al., 2024)。
Limitations局限性
While the envisioned sandbox agent economies present compelling opportunities for scalable alignment and coordination, their design, deployment, and operation are attended by a complex array of risks that demand careful consideration. These risks span multiple domains, from the potential for emergent economic instabilities within these novel market structures, to the challenges of ensuring robust and beneficial agent behavior in high-stakes, autonomous interactions. Furthermore, the integration of such economies into broader societal frameworks raises profound socio-ethical questions regarding oversight and accountability, and the potential for unintended consequences on human agency and economic realities.
尽管所设想的沙盒智能体经济体为可扩展的对齐与协调提供了极具吸引力的机遇，但其设计、部署和运行也伴随着一系列复杂风险，需要审慎考量。这些风险涉及多个领域，从这些新型市场结构中可能涌现的经济不稳定性，到在高风险的自主交互中确保智能体行为稳健且有益所面临的挑战。此外，将此类经济体融入更广泛的社会框架，会引发涉及监督和问责的深刻社会伦理问题，以及可能对人的自主性和经济现实产生的意外后果。
There are also novel categories of risks involving autonomous AI agents. One such risk category takes the shape of "agent traps": websites, digital elements, or crafted inputs deliberately designed to subvert an the operational integrity of AI agents. This may be achieved through jailbreaking the underlying models or via adversarial prompting techniques. Such traps could exploit latent vulnerabilities in an agent’s instruction-following or environmental interpretation capabilities to make AI agents deviate from their instructions or reveal private or sensitive information. As AI agents become increasingly empowered to execute tasks and/or conduct financial transactions on behalf of their users, these agent traps represent a significant and burgeoning vector for financial scams. Malicious actors could, therefore, lure or trick agents into unauthorized expenditures or contractual agreements, directly siphoning funds or resources from the individuals or organizations the agents represent.
涉及自主 AI 智能体的新型风险类别也随之出现。其中一类风险表现为“智能体陷阱”（agent traps）：即为破坏 AI 智能体运行完整性而刻意设计的网站、数字元素或精心构造的输入。这可以通过对底层模型实施越狱或采用对抗性提示技术来实现。此类陷阱可能利用智能体在遵循指令或理解环境能力方面的潜在漏洞，使 AI 智能体偏离其指令，或泄露私人信息或敏感信息。随着 AI 智能体日益被赋予代表用户执行任务和/或进行金融交易的权限，这些智能体陷阱正成为金融诈骗的一种重大且迅速增长的攻击途径。因此，恶意行为者可能诱使或欺骗智能体进行未经授权的支出或签订未经授权的合同，从而直接侵占智能体所代表的个人或组织的资金或资源。
Another significant category of risk involves privacy and manipulation. As agents negotiate and transact, they risk exposing sensitive information about their users’ preferences, strategies, or resources, which could be exploited by adversaries. A powerful cryptographic solution to this is the use of Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs; (Zhou et al., 2024a)). A ZKP allows one party (the prover) to convince another (the verifier) that a statement is true, without revealing any of the underlying information that makes it true.
另一类重大风险涉及隐私和操纵。智能体在谈判和交易过程中，可能暴露有关用户偏好、策略或资源的敏感信息，而这些信息可能被对手利用。针对这一问题，一种强有力的密码学解决方案是使用零知识证明（Zero-Knowledge Proofs，ZKPs；(Zhou et al., 2024a)）。零知识证明允许一方（证明者）使另一方（验证者）确信某个陈述为真，而不泄露使该陈述成立的任何底层信息。
In the virtual agent economy, ZKPs would enable privacy-preserving interactions and mitigate several key risks:
在虚拟智能体经济中，ZKPs 将实现保护隐私的交互，并缓解若干关键风险：
Selective Disclosure: An agent could prove it meets a certain requirement without revealing the exact details. For example, in a negotiation, it could prove it has sufficient funds to complete a purchase without revealing its total budget, preventing predatory pricing.
选择性披露：智能体可以证明自己满足某项要求，而无需透露具体细节。例如，在谈判中，它可以证明自己有足够的资金完成购买，而不透露其总预算，从而防止掠夺性定价。
Anonymous Credentials: An agent could prove it belongs to a certain group (e.g., "a resident of Community X" for a local currency) without revealing its specific identity, thus preventing the tracking and correlation of user behavior across different contexts.
匿名凭证：智能体可以证明自己属于某个群体（例如，在使用地方货币时证明自己是“社区 X 的居民”），而不透露其具体身份，从而防止用户在不同情境中的行为被追踪和关联。
Unlinkability: ZKPs can be constructed to be fresh for each interaction, making it computationally difficult for observers to link an agent’s activities over time. This directly counters the risk of amplifying "information and opinion bubbles" by breaking the chain of data that allows for such pervasive tracking.
不可关联性：可以为每次交互构造全新的 ZKPs，使观察者在计算上难以将智能体在不同时点的活动关联起来。这通过切断使此类无处不在的追踪成为可能的数据链，直接遏制了“信息和观点泡泡”被进一步强化的风险。
Integrating ZKPs into the fabric of agent communication would allow for a market that is not only efficient but also respects user privacy by default, ensuring that agents can participate fully while disclosing the absolute minimum necessary information.
将 ZKPs 融入智能体通信的基础架构，将有助于形成一个既高效又默认尊重用户隐私的市场，确保智能体能够充分参与其中，同时仅披露必要的最少信息。
A significant risk posed by an agent-driven economy is the potential for large-scale labor substitution through AI-enabled automation A dominant academic view suggests that AI, much like previous waves of technology, is a form of skill-biased technological change, but with a crucial distinction: its ability to automate cognitive tasks previously considered immune. Research by economists such as Daron Acemoglu and David Autor has shown that automation often targets routine tasks, whether manual or cognitive, leading to job polarization—a hollowing out of middle-skill, middle-wage jobs while demand for high-skill analytical work and low-skill manual service work increases (Acemoglu and Autor, 2011; Autor, 2015). The ascent of advanced AI agents capable of reasoning, planning, and communication directly threatens a wide array of non-routine cognitive tasks, from paralegal work and accounting to software development and customer service, potentially accelerating this polarization and creating significant displacement effects for a broad swathe of the workforce (Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2022; Brynjolfsson et al., 2018). This would have the impact of widening inequalities within countries, and possibly also between them.
由智能体驱动的经济带来的一项重大风险，是人工智能赋能的自动化可能导致大规模劳动力替代。一种主流学术观点认为，与以往的技术浪潮类似，人工智能是一种技能偏向型技术变革，但有一个关键区别：它能够将过去被认为不会受到自动化影响的认知任务自动化。Daron Acemoglu 和 David Autor 等经济学家的研究表明，自动化通常针对常规任务，无论是体力任务还是认知任务，从而导致就业两极化——中等技能、中等工资的岗位不断萎缩，而对高技能分析工作和低技能体力服务工作的需求则不断增加 (Acemoglu and Autor, 2011; Autor, 2015)。具备推理、规划和沟通能力的先进人工智能智能体的兴起，直接威胁到广泛的非常规认知任务，从律师助理工作和会计工作到软件开发和客户服务，可能加速这种两极化，并对广大劳动者产生显著的就业替代效应 (Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2022; Brynjolfsson et al., 2018)。这将加剧各国内部的不平等，也可能扩大国家之间的不平等。
Although new jobs will eventually emerge, the transition period could be deeply disruptive, leading to downward pressure on wages for those whose skills are substitutable and exacerbating inequality if the gains from AI-driven productivity accrue primarily to the owners of capital and a small cadre of high-skilled professionals who can effectively collaborate with these new systems (Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2022). This risk of concentration may be particularly pronounced with AI agents, since individuals and corporations with greater financial resources may have access to more powerful, computationally-intensive, and data-rich AI agents (Hammond et al., 2025; Gabriel et al., 2024). These superior agents would be uniquely equipped for identifying and exploiting regulatory loopholes, monopolizing digital resources, and creating informational asymmetries at a scale and velocity that human actors and less-capable agents cannot counter. Preliminary studies already indicate that more capable agents secure significantly better outcomes in negotiations, a dynamic that would be magnified across an entire economy (Zhu et al., 2025). This creates a dangerous feedback loop where economic advantage buys superior agentic capability, which in turn extracts further economic rents, thereby entrenching privilege and potentially creating a new, algorithmically-enforced class structure that undermines market fairness and mobility. This risk is perhaps the most significant challenge of a permeable sandbox, where economic activity inside the agent economy directly displaces human jobs in the real economy, representing a primary negative consequence of an accidental emergence.
尽管新的就业岗位最终会出现，但转型期可能带来剧烈冲击，使技能可被替代的劳动者面临工资下行压力；如果人工智能驱动的生产率提升所带来的收益主要归于资本所有者，以及少数能够与这些新系统有效协作的高技能专业人士，不平等还将进一步加剧 (Acemoglu and Restrepo, 2022)。在人工智能智能体的情境下，这种集中化风险可能尤为突出，因为财力更雄厚的个人和企业可能有条件使用能力更强、计算密集程度更高且数据更丰富的人工智能智能体 (Hammond et al., 2025; Gabriel et al., 2024)。这些更优越的智能体将具备独特的能力，能够以人类行为者和能力较弱的智能体无法抗衡的规模和速度，识别并利用监管漏洞、垄断数字资源，以及制造信息不对称。初步研究已经表明，能力更强的智能体在谈判中能够取得显著更好的结果，而这一动态将在整个经济体系中被放大 (Zhu et al., 2025)。这会形成一种危险的反馈循环：经济优势能够购得更强的智能体能力，而这种能力又会进一步攫取经济租金，从而固化特权，并可能形成一种由算法强制维系的新阶级结构，损害市场公平性和流动性。这种风险或许是具有渗透性的沙盒所面临的最重大挑战：智能体经济内部的经济活动直接取代现实经济中的人类就业岗位，构成意外涌现所带来的一项主要负面后果。
Recommendations建议
A proactive and coordinated effort is required to achieve practical and safe virtual agent economies. Realizing the opportunities discussed in this paper while mitigating the inherent risks necessitates deliberate action across technical, legal, and policy domains. We therefore put forward the following recommendations to guide this development.
要实现切实可行且安全的虚拟智能体经济，需要采取积极主动、协调一致的行动。要实现本文讨论的机遇，同时缓解其固有风险，就必须在技术、法律和政策领域采取审慎的行动。因此，我们提出以下建议，以指导这一发展进程。
1. Establish Clear Legal Frameworks for Liability and Accountability. Ascribing liability for an autonomous agent’s actions presents a profound challenge, as traditional legal frameworks struggle to assign responsibility between an agent’s creator, its deployer, and its user (Buiten et al., 2023; Čerka et al., 2015; Zech, 2021). This challenge is magnified as agents will increasingly operate not in isolation, but as coordinated, multi-agent systems taking the form of group agents (Franklin, 2023). Therefore, instead of attempting to attribute blame to a single agent, new legal models should be developed that draw from jurisprudence on group agency, much like corporate liability (List, 2021; List and Pettit, 2011). Such frameworks would treat the emergent, coordinated agent system as a single accountable entity, providing a more robust and realistic path to ensuring accountability for collective actions.
1.建立明确的法律责任与问责框架。确定自主智能体行为的法律责任归属是一项严峻挑战，因为传统法律框架难以在智能体的创建者、部署者和使用者之间划分责任 (Buiten et al., 2023; Čerka et al., 2015; Zech, 2021)。随着智能体越来越多地以群体智能体的形式，作为协调运作的多智能体系统运行，而非孤立运行，这一挑战将进一步加剧 (Franklin, 2023)。因此，应借鉴有关群体能动性的法理，参照公司责任的做法，建立新的法律模型，而不是试图将过错归咎于某一个智能体 (List, 2021; List and Pettit, 2011)。此类框架将把涌现出的、协调运作的智能体系统视为单一的可问责实体，为确保集体行动的责任得到追究提供一条更稳健、更切合实际的路径。
2. Develop Open Standards for Agent Interoperability and Communication. A fragmented digital landscape, where agents are unable to communicate across different platforms, would fundamentally limit the potential of virtual agent economies and lead to the creation of “walled gardens”. Therefore, the development and widespread adoption of open, universal standards are paramount to ensure true interoperability. These standards must create a common language allowing agents, regardless of their origin or provider, to seamlessly discover each other’s capabilities, negotiate terms, and securely transact. Establishing such a level playing field is a prerequisite for fostering a competitive, innovative, and decentralized agent ecosystem.
2.制定智能体互操作性与通信的开放标准。如果数字环境碎片化，智能体无法跨平台通信，就会从根本上限制虚拟智能体经济的潜力，并导致“围墙花园”的形成。因此，制定并广泛采用开放、通用的标准，对于确保真正的互操作性至关重要。这些标准必须建立一种通用语言，使智能体无论来自何处或由谁提供，都能无缝发现彼此的能力、协商条款并安全地进行交易。建立这样的公平竞争环境，是培育具有竞争力、创新性和去中心化特征的智能体生态系统的前提。
3. Construct a Hybrid Oversight and Containment Infrastructure. The sheer speed and scale of an autonomous agent economy render traditional, human-in-the-loop oversight models inadequate. A new safety paradigm must therefore be constructed, one that combines the real-time vigilance of AI systems with the deliberative judgment of human experts. This hybrid infrastructure would operate in tiers: a first layer of specialized AI overseers would constantly monitor market activity, enforcing rules programmatically and flagging anomalies that suggest fraud or systemic risk.
3.构建混合式监督与风险遏制基础设施。自主智能体经济极高的运行速度和庞大的规模，使传统的人在回路（human-in-the-loop）监督模式难以胜任。因此，必须构建一种新的安全范式，将 AI 系统的实时警戒能力与人类专家经过审慎思考的判断相结合。这一混合式基础设施将分层运行：第一层由专门的 AI 监督者组成，持续监测市场活动，通过程序执行规则，并标记可能表明欺诈或系统性风险的异常情况。
Upon detection, robust automated protocols would immediately contain the potential harm—for example, by temporarily freezing a malfunctioning agent or quarantining a transaction—thereby preventing the kind of high-speed "flash crashes" seen in human markets (Menkveld and Yueshen, 2019). Only the most complex, novel, or high-stakes cases would then be escalated to a second tier of human reviewers, ensuring their expertise is focused where it is most critical. The efficacy of this entire system hinges on it being built upon immutable ledgers and standardized audit trails, which provide the verifiable, tamper-proof record necessary for both automated containment and human adjudication.
一旦检测到此类异常，稳健的自动化协议就会立即遏制潜在危害，例如暂时冻结发生故障的智能体或隔离某笔交易，从而防止出现人类市场中那种高速发生的“闪电崩盘” (Menkveld and Yueshen, 2019)。随后，只有最复杂、最具新颖性或利害关系重大的案例才会被提交给第二层的人类审核者，以确保他们的专业能力集中用于最关键之处。整个系统的有效性取决于其是否建立在不可变账本和标准化审计轨迹之上，因为它们能够提供自动化风险遏制与人工裁决所必需的、可验证且防篡改的记录。
4. Pilot Programs in Regulatory Sandboxes. Given the novelty and complexity of the proposals outlined in this paper, a purely theoretical approach is insufficient. We strongly recommend the creation of regulatory sandboxes to launch controlled pilot programs, providing a crucial bridge between theory and practice. These sandboxes would function as supervised, real-world laboratories where private firms, academic researchers, and regulatory bodies can collaborate to deploy and observe limited-scale agent economies in a contained environment.
4.在监管沙盒中开展试点项目。鉴于本文所述方案的新颖性和复杂性，纯粹的理论方法并不足够。我们强烈建议设立监管沙盒，开展受控试点项目，为理论与实践之间架起关键桥梁。这些沙盒将充当受监督的现实世界实验室，使私营企业、学术研究人员和监管机构能够合作，在受控环境中部署和观察规模有限的智能体经济。
Testing these economies on well-defined, specific societal missions—such as optimizing the energy grid for a university campus, managing a city’s fleet of autonomous delivery vehicles, or allocating water resources in a specific agricultural district—would provide invaluable empirical data. Such pilots would allow us to stress-test the technical infrastructure, observe emergent agent behaviors (both cooperative and adversarial), and measure the real-world effectiveness of the proposed market mechanisms for fairness and alignment. The insights gathered from these controlled experiments are not merely academic; they are an indispensable prerequisite for iteratively refining the system’s design and building the robust, evidence-based policy required for responsible, large-scale deployment.
围绕定义明确的具体社会任务测试这些经济体系，例如优化大学校园的电网、管理城市的自动驾驶配送车队，或分配特定农业区的水资源，将提供极为宝贵的实证数据。此类试点将使我们能够对技术基础设施进行压力测试，观察涌现的智能体行为（包括合作行为和对抗行为），并衡量所提出的市场机制在促进公平与对齐方面的实际成效。这些受控实验所获得的认识不仅具有学术意义，还是迭代改进系统设计，以及制定负责任的大规模部署所需的稳健、循证政策不可或缺的前提。
5. Invest in Workforce Complementarity and a Modernized Social Safety Net. To counteract the risks of labor substitution and inequality, a dual strategy that fosters both human-AI agent complementarity and robust social protection may be key. The first pillar involves a systemic reimagining of education and workforce training to equip individuals with the skills needed not to compete with AI, but to collaborate with it effectively. This means emphasizing durable human advantages like critical thinking, complex problem-solving, creativity, and the ability to manage and critically evaluate the outputs of AI systems (Autor, 2015; Brynjolfsson et al., 2018). However, training is not a panacea and evidence suggests significant limits to the scale and efficacy of retraining programs for displaced workers (Jacobs, 2025), this strategy must be paired with a second pillar: the deliberate strengthening of the social safety net. Beyond traditional unemployment benefits, this includes exploring adaptive mechanisms such as unemployment insurance, portable benefits systems, and negative income taxes. Together, these policies can create an ecosystem where autonomous agents augment human capabilities while providing the essential economic buffer needed to manage labor transitions, share productivity gains broadly, and preserve social cohesion.
5.投资于劳动力互补性与现代化社会保障体系。为应对劳动力被替代和不平等的风险，同时促进人类与 AI 智能体的互补性并提供有力社会保障的双重战略，可能是关键所在。第一项支柱是系统性地重新构想教育与劳动力培训，使个人掌握与 AI 有效协作所需的技能，而非与 AI 竞争的技能。这意味着要着重培养人类持久的优势，例如批判性思维、复杂问题解决能力、创造力，以及管理和批判性评估 AI 系统输出的能力 (Autor, 2015; Brynjolfsson et al., 2018)。然而，培训并非万能良药，证据表明，面向失业劳动者的再培训项目在规模和成效方面均存在显著局限 (Jacobs, 2025)，因此，这一策略必须与第二大支柱相结合：有意识地加强社会安全网。除传统失业救济外，这还包括探索失业保险、可携带福利制度和负所得税等适应性机制。这些政策共同作用，可以构建一个生态系统，使自主智能体增强人类能力，同时提供必要的经济缓冲，以应对劳动力转型、广泛分享生产率提升带来的收益，并维系社会凝聚力。
Conclusion结论
Given that some of these proposals represent major shifts, it would be imperative to comprehensively test any such change in limited, gradual rollouts, and only proceed with the support and buy-in from all stakeholders. Through such gradual scaled-down empirical validation it is be possible to develop and iteratively refine appropriate frameworks for ensuring safety and compliance.
鉴于其中一些提议意味着重大转变，必须通过范围有限、循序渐进的试点，对任何此类变革进行全面检验，并且只有在获得所有利益相关方的支持与认可后才能继续推进。通过这种渐进式、小规模的实证验证，可以制定并迭代完善适当的框架，以确保安全性与合规性。
Certain domains may always require active human decision-making for a variety of reasons (e.g. human preference, culture, risk sensitivity, etc). However, the rapid increase in AI agent performance, coupled with the development of scalable AI safety oversight frameworks and guardrails (e.g. (Leibo et al., 2024; Shah et al., 2025)), is likely to result in an increasing number of use cases for autonomous agents. Autonomous or semi-autonomous AI agents may potentially be able to achieve more, faster; adding substantial value to society (Yang and Zhai, 2025). This will not come without significant challenges, requiring alignment and coordination not only of individual agents, but perhaps more importantly the alignment and coordination of agent networks across various scales. Moreover, with regard to AI-human interactions in particular, it is important to keep in mind that not all human needs and experience can be as easily captured by markets (Satz, 2010) as goods may only be interchangeable within certain spheres (Walzer, 2008).
出于多种原因（例如人类偏好、文化、风险敏感性等），某些领域可能始终需要人类主动参与决策。然而，AI 智能体性能的迅速提升，加之可扩展的 AI 安全监督框架与防护机制的发展（例如 (Leibo et al., 2024; Shah et al., 2025)），很可能使自主智能体的应用场景日益增多。自主或半自主 AI 智能体有可能以更快的速度取得更多成果，为社会创造可观价值 (Yang and Zhai, 2025)。这一过程必然伴随重大挑战，不仅需要对单个智能体进行对齐与协调，而且或许更重要的是，需要对不同尺度的智能体网络进行对齐与协调。此外，尤其是在 AI 与人类的互动方面，需要牢记，并非所有人类需求与体验都能轻易地通过市场得到体现 (Satz, 2010)，因为物品可能只有在特定领域内才具有可互换性 (Walzer, 2008)。
The utilization of markets to drive coordination (Clearwater and Yeh, 1996; Dias and Stentz, 2002; Metcalf, 2009; Stavins, 2010) is yet to receive much attention in the discourse on the alignment and coordination of advanced AI agents. The complexity of behaviors and capabilities emerging in advanced AI agents, and the likely complexity of their interactions across a multitude of tasks and societal roles, presents a prototypical example of a scenario in which market forces may prove to be a key driver of AI agent coordination and AI alignment at the group-level rather than merely at an individual agent level. Here we argue that by carefully introducing new steerable agent markets as sandbox economies it may be possible to deliver positive social and economic impact through networks of advanced AI agents.
在关于先进 AI 智能体对齐与协调的讨论中，利用市场推动协调的做法 (Clearwater and Yeh, 1996; Dias and Stentz, 2002; Metcalf, 2009; Stavins, 2010) 尚未受到广泛关注。先进 AI 智能体所涌现的行为与能力具有复杂性，它们在众多任务和社会角色中的互动也可能十分复杂，这构成了一种典型情境：市场力量可能成为推动 AI 智能体协调和 AI 对齐的关键因素，其作用层面是群体，而不仅仅是单个智能体。本文主张，通过审慎地引入新的可引导智能体市场，将其作为沙盒经济体，或许能够借助先进 AI 智能体网络产生积极的社会与经济影响。
By embedding our societal objectives into the very infrastructure of agent-to-agent transactions, we can foster an ecosystem where emergent collaboration is a feature, not a bug. The choice, then, is between retrofitting these powerful new actors into systems they will inevitably fracture, or seizing a fleeting opportunity to build a world where our most powerful tools are, by their very design, extensions of our highest aspirations.
通过将我们的社会目标嵌入智能体之间交易的基础设施本身，我们可以培育一个生态系统，使涌现式协作成为其固有特性，而非缺陷。因此，我们面临的选择是：将这些强大的新行动者勉强嵌入它们终将破坏的系统，或是抓住稍纵即逝的机会，构建一个世界，让我们最强大的工具从设计之初就成为我们最崇高愿景的延伸。
