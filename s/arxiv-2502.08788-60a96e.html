停止高估多智能体辩论——我们必须重新思考评估并拥抱模型异质性Stop Overvaluing Multi-Agent Debate—We Must Rethink Evaluation and Embrace Model Heterogeneity
摘要Abstract
多智能体辩论（Multi-agent debate，MAD）作为提升大语言模型（LLMs）事实准确性与推理能力的一个有前景的研究方向，已受到广泛关注。 Multi-agent debate (MAD) has gained significant attention as a promising line of research to improve the factual accuracy and reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs). 尽管 MAD 在概念上颇具吸引力，当前相关研究的评估实践仍存在关键局限，包括基准测试覆盖范围有限、基线对比不充分以及实验设置不一致。 Despite its conceptual appeal, current MAD research suffers from critical limitations in evaluation practices, including limited benchmark coverage, weak baseline comparisons, and inconsistent setups. 本文使用 4 个基础模型，在 9 个基准测试上对 5 种具有代表性的 MAD 方法进行了系统评估。 This paper presents a systematic evaluation of 5 representative MAD methods across 9 benchmarks using 4 foundational models. 令人意外的是，我们的研究结果表明，即使消耗了显著更多的推理时计算资源，MAD 的表现也往往无法超越思维链（Chain-of-Thought）和自一致性（Self-Consistency）等简单的单智能体基线方法。 Surprisingly, our findings reveal that MAD often fail to outperform simple single-agent baselines such as Chain-of-Thought and Self-Consistency, even when consuming significantly more inference-time computation. 为推动 MAD 研究进一步发展，我们还探索了模型异质性的作用，并发现它是一种能够持续改善现有 MAD 框架表现的通用良方。 To advance MAD research, we further explore the role of model heterogeneity and find it as a universal antidote to consistently improve current MAD frameworks. 基于上述发现，我们认为，该领域必须停止高估当前形式的 MAD；要取得真正的进展，我们必须批判性地重新思考评估范式，并积极将模型异质性作为核心设计原则。 Based on our findings, we argue that the field must stop overvaluing MAD in its current form; for true advancement, we must critically rethink evaluation paradigms and actively embrace model heterogeneity as a core design principle.
