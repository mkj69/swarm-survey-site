集体智能（collective intelligence，CI），又称群体智能（group intelligence，GI），是群体通过合作，或通过汇聚多样化的信息、视角和行为而涌现出的能力，使其能够比单独的个体更有效地解决问题、作出决策或生成知识；这些群体可以完全由人类组成，也可以由动物组成，或由人类与人工智能体构成网络。 Collective intelligence (CI) or group intelligence (GI) is the emergent ability of groups, whether composed of humans alone, animals, or networks of humans and artificial agents, to solve problems, make decisions, or generate knowledge more effectively than individuals alone, through either cooperation or by aggregation of diverse information, perspectives, and behaviors. 群集智能（swarm intelligence，SI）一词有时与集体智能互换使用，但它实际上只是集体智能的一种具体形式。 The term swarm intelligence (SI) is sometimes used interchangeably with collective intelligence but is simply one instance of it.
集体智能不仅涵盖在动态环境中自组织并适应环境的复杂适应系统，还涵盖社会群体中观察到的创造与认知过程，后者通常被称为群体智慧。 Collective intelligence encompasses not only complex adaptive systems, which self-organize and adapt in dynamic environments, but also creative and cognitive processes observed in social groups, which are often referred to as the wisdom of crowds. 在这一背景下，集体判断有时来自非专家，却往往比专家预测更准确，Francis Galton 关于估算一头牛体重的著名实验便说明了这一点。 In this context, collective judgments, sometimes from non-experts, often exceed the accuracy of expert predictions, as illustrated by Francis Galton's famous experiment on estimating the weight of an ox. 当代理论家提出，智能可以被理解为一种涌现的集体过程，体现在神经、个体生物和群体等不同的生物与社会尺度上。 Contemporary theorists have posited that intelligence can be interpreted as an emergent collective process that manifests across various biological and social scales, including neural, organismal, and group levels.
这一术语见于社会生物学、政治学，以及大规模同行评审和众包应用的语境中。 The term appears in sociobiology, political science and in the context of mass peer review and crowdsourcing applications. 它可能涉及共识决策、社会资本，以及投票制度、社交媒体和其他量化群体活动的手段等形式化机制。 It may involve consensus decision-making, social capital and formalisms such as voting systems, social media and other means of quantifying mass activity. 不应将集体智能与泛心论的形而上学理论或有关群体意识的主张混为一谈。 Collective intelligence should not be conflated with metaphysical theories of panpsychism or with claims about group consciousness. 这些理论和主张关注心灵的根本性质，以及意识是否可能是一种普遍存在或涌现的属性。 These concern the fundamental nature of the mind, and the possibility that consciousness is a ubiquitous or emergent property. 然而，集体智能的实证研究关注的是群体协调信息、任务或问题解决过程的可观察机制。 The empirical study of collective intelligence, however, focuses on observable mechanisms by which groups coordinate information, tasks or problem-solving.
群体智能一词有时与集体智能一词互换使用。 The term group intelligence is sometimes used interchangeably with the term collective intelligence. Anita Woolley 将集体智能视为衡量群体智能与群体创造力的指标。 Anita Woolley presents collective intelligence as a measure of group intelligence and group creativity. 其基本观点是，集体智能的测量涵盖群体的广泛特征，主要包括群体构成和群体互动。 The idea is that a measure of collective intelligence covers a broad range of features of the group, mainly group composition and group interaction. 能够提高群体集体智能水平的构成特征包括群体中女性人数较多、群体多样性较高等。 The features of composition that lead to increased levels of collective intelligence in groups include criteria such as higher numbers of women in the group as well as increased diversity of the group.

Norman Lee Johnson 将这种集体智能观念称为“共生智能”。 This notion of collective intelligence is referred to as "symbiotic intelligence" by Norman Lee Johnson. 这一概念应用于社会学、商业、计算机科学和大众传播领域，也出现在科幻作品中。 The concept is used in sociology, business, computer science and mass communications: it also appears in science fiction.
Pierre Lévy 将集体智能定义为“一种普遍分布、不断得到增强、实时协调，并能有效调动各种技能的智能形式。 Pierre Lévy defines collective intelligence as "a form of universally distributed intelligence, constantly enhanced, coordinated in real time, and resulting in the effective mobilization of skills. (...) (...) 集体智能的基础和目标是个体之间的相互认可与相互充实，而不是对被拜物化或实体化的共同体的崇拜。” The basis and goal of collective intelligence is mutual recognition and enrichment of individuals rather than the cult of fetishized or hypostatized communities."
根据 Lévy 和 Derrick de Kerckhove 的观点，它指的是联网的信息与通信技术（ICTs）通过同步扩大人类互动的范围，增强社会共享知识库的能力。 According to Lévy and Derrick de Kerckhove, it refers to capacity of networked ICTs (Information communication technologies) to enhance the collective pool of social knowledge by simultaneously expanding the extent of human interactions.
Geoff Mulgan 在自 2006 年起的一系列讲座和报告以及《Big Mind》一书中提出了一个更广泛的定义，并提出一个框架，从功能要素（观察、预测、创造力、判断等）、学习循环和组织形式等方面分析任何思维系统，包括人类智能和机器智能。 A broader definition was provided by Geoff Mulgan in a series of lectures and reports from 2006 onwards and in the book Big Mind, which proposed a framework for analysing any thinking system, including both human and machine intelligence, in terms of functional elements (observation, prediction, creativity, judgement etc.), learning loops and forms of organisation. 其目的是提供一种方法，用于诊断并改善城市、企业、非政府组织或议会的集体智能。 The aim was to provide a way to diagnose, and improve, the collective intelligence of a city, business, NGO or parliament.
集体智能有力地推动了知识和权力从个体向集体的转移。 Collective intelligence strongly contributes to the shift of knowledge and power from the individual to the collective. 根据 Eric S. Raymond 在 1998 年和 JC Herz 在 2005 年提出的观点，开源智能最终将产生比企业内部开发的专有软件所生成的知识更优的成果。 According to Eric S. Raymond in 1998 and JC Herz in 2005, open-source intelligence will eventually generate superior outcomes to knowledge generated by proprietary software developed within corporations. 媒体理论家 Henry Jenkins 将集体智能视为一种与融合文化相关的“媒体权力的替代来源”。 Media theorist Henry Jenkins sees collective intelligence as an 'alternative source of media power', related to convergence culture. 他关注教育，以及人们如何在正式学习环境之外学习参与知识文化。 He draws attention to education and the way people are learning to participate in knowledge cultures outside formal learning settings. 亨利·詹金斯批评那些倡导培养“自主的问题解决者和自足的学习者”，却对通过集体智能进行学习持排斥态度的学校。 Henry Jenkins criticizes schools which promote 'autonomous problem solvers and self-contained learners' while remaining hostile to learning through the means of collective intelligence. 皮埃尔·列维和亨利·詹金斯都支持这样一种主张：集体智能对民主化具有重要意义，因为它与以知识为基础的文化相互关联，并通过集体分享想法得以维系，从而有助于更好地理解多元社会。 Both Pierre Lévy and Henry Jenkins support the claim that collective intelligence is important for democratization, as it is interlinked with knowledge-based culture and sustained by collective idea sharing, and thus contributes to a better understanding of diverse society.
类似于衡量个体一般智力的 g 因子（g），对集体智能的一种新科学认识旨在提取群体的一般集体智能因子，即 c 因子，以表征群体执行广泛任务的能力，尽管这一得分本身并不是一个商数。 Similar to the g factor (g) for general individual intelligence, a new scientific understanding of collective intelligence aims to extract a general collective intelligence factor c factor for groups indicating a group's ability to perform a wide range of tasks, although the score is not a quotient per se.
数字通信平台和协作技术往往有助于促进集体智能。 Collective intelligence is often facilitated by digital communication platforms and collaborative technologies.
历史History
许多理论家将亚里士多德在《政治学》中的论述“众人共同筹办的宴席胜过由一人出资提供的晚餐”解释为：正如众人可以各自带来不同的菜肴，在商议中，众人也可以各自提供不同的信息，从而形成更好的决策。 Many theorists have interpreted Aristotle's statement in the Politics that "a feast to which many contribute is better than a dinner provided out of a single purse" to mean that just as many may bring different dishes to the table, so in a deliberation many may contribute different pieces of information to generate a better decision. 然而，近期研究表明，这很可能并非亚里士多德的本意，而是基于我们如今对团队智能的认识所作的现代诠释。 Recent scholarship, however, suggests that this was probably not what Aristotle meant but is a modern interpretation based on what we now know about team intelligence. 梅利莎·施瓦茨伯格（2016）重新诠释了亚里士多德的论述，认为《政治学》III.11 应被理解为以公民具有平等的政治判断能力为基础、对民主统治作出的辩护，而非关于集体判断在质量上更为优越的主张。 Melissa Schwartzberg (2016) reinterprets Aristotle's discussion, arguing that Politics III.11 should be read not as a claim that collective judgement is superior in quality, but as a defence of democratic rule grounded in an equal capacity for political judgment among citizens. 她的“平等判断”解读将重点从有关群体智慧的认识论论证，转向亚里士多德对平等参与之政治价值的关注，并提醒人们，不应直接援引亚里士多德来支持当代关于集体判断具有更高准确性的主张。 Her "equal judgment" reading shifts emphasis from epistemic arguments about the wisdom of crowds toward Aristotle's concern with the political value of equal participation, and cautions against using Aristotle as straightforward support for contemporary claims of superior collective accuracy.
现代集体智能理论（尽管当时尚未使用这一名称）始于1785年的孔多塞侯爵，其“陪审团定理”指出，如果投票群体中的每个成员作出正确决策的概率都高于作出错误决策的概率，那么，随着群体成员数量的增加，群体中获得最多票数的决策为正确决策的概率也会增加。 Modern collective intelligence theory (although not so named at the time) began in 1785 with the Marquis de Condorcet, whose "jury theorem" states that if each member of a voting group is more likely than not to make a correct decision, the probability that the highest vote of the group is the correct decision increases with the number of members of the group.
这一概念也见于昆虫学家威廉·莫顿·惠勒在1910年提出的观察：看似独立的个体能够如此紧密地合作，以至于与单一生物体无从区分。 The concept is also found in entomologist William Morton Wheeler's observation in 1910 that seemingly independent individuals can cooperate so closely as to become indistinguishable from a single organism. 惠勒在蚂蚁身上观察到了这一协作过程：它们如同单一动物体内的细胞一样行动，他将这样的整体称为超个体（superorganism）。 Wheeler saw this collaborative process at work in ants that acted like the cells of a single beast he called a superorganism.
1912年，埃米尔·涂尔干认定，社会是人类逻辑思维的唯一来源。 In 1912 Émile Durkheim identified society as the sole source of human logical thought. 他在《宗教生活的基本形式》中提出，社会构成了一种更高层次的智能，因为它在空间和时间上都超越了个体。 He argued in "The Elementary Forms of Religious Life" that society constitutes a higher intelligence because it transcends the individual over space and time. 其他思想先驱包括弗拉基米尔·维尔纳茨基和皮埃尔·泰亚尔·德·夏尔丹提出的“智圈”（noosphere）概念，以及 H. G. 威尔斯提出的“世界大脑”（world brain）概念。 Other antecedents are Vladimir Vernadsky and Pierre Teilhard de Chardin's concept of "noosphere" and H. G. Wells's concept of "world brain".
彼得·罗素、伊丽莎白·萨图里斯和芭芭拉·马克思·哈伯德（“有意识进化”一词的首创者）都受到智圈愿景的启发：智圈是一种超越个体、快速演化的集体智能，是地球的信息皮层。 Peter Russell, Elisabet Sahtouris, and Barbara Marx Hubbard (originator of the term "conscious evolution") are inspired by the visions of a noosphere – a transcendent, rapidly evolving collective intelligence – an informational cortex of the planet.
在1962年的一份研究报告中，道格拉斯·恩格尔巴特将集体智能与组织效能联系起来，并预测，主动“增强人类智力”将在群体解决问题时产生倍增效应：“三个人以这种增强模式共同工作，在解决复杂问题时，其效能似乎[会]超过一个经过增强的人独自工作时的三倍。” In a 1962 research report, Douglas Engelbart linked collective intelligence to organizational effectiveness, and predicted that pro-actively 'augmenting human intellect' would yield a multiplier effect in group problem solving: "Three people working together in this augmented mode [would] seem to be more than three times as effective in solving a complex problem as is one augmented person working alone". 1994年，他创造了“集体智商”（collective IQ）一词，将其作为集体智能的衡量指标，以促使人们关注在商业和社会中大幅提升集体智商的机会。 In 1994, he coined the term 'collective IQ' as a measure of collective intelligence, to focus attention on the opportunity to significantly raise collective IQ in business and society.
1996年，丹尼尔·戈尔曼在其如今已成为经典的情绪智力著作中指出，在他所称的“群体智能”中，最重要的单一因素是“社会和谐”，而非群体成员的平均 IQ：“在其他条件相同的情况下，正是这种协调一致的能力，使一个群体格外有才干、富有成效且取得成功，而另一个群体——其成员在其他方面具有同等的才干和技能——却表现不佳。” In 1996, in his now classic book on emotional intelligence, Daniel Goleman stated that ‘social harmony’ was the single most important factor in what he termed ‘group intelligence,’ rather than the average IQ of the group’s individuals: “it is this ability to harmonize that, all other things being equal, will make one group especially talented, productive, and successful, and another - with members whose talent and skills are equal in other regards - do poorly.”
布朗和劳德（2000, 2001）将集体智能界定为一种社会资本：一种植根于制度、合作和共同解决问题的社会能力，而非仅仅属于个体的属性。 Brown and Lauder (2000, 2001) framed collective intelligence as a form of social capital: a social capacity rooted in institutions, cooperation, and shared problem-solving, rather than as an attribute of individuals alone. 他们将其视为制衡“市场个人主义”的力量。 They presented it as a counterweight to ‘market individualism.’ 布朗和劳德借鉴了早期对以 IQ 为基础的智力观念的批评，强调智力是在社会条件塑造下取得的成就，而且即使认知潜能广泛分布，结构性不平等仍可能限制群体发展和调动集体智能的能力。 Drawing on earlier critiques of IQ-based conceptions of intelligence, Brown and Lauder emphasised that intelligence is an achievement shaped by social conditions, and that structural inequalities can restrict groups’ ability to develop and mobilise collective intelligence, even when cognitive potential is widely distributed. 他们建议有意识地将其嵌入社会：“争取集体智能（collective intelligence），不仅涉及改变我们对自身能力以及自身与社会关系的看法，还包括将集体智能的潜力织入社会本身的肌理之中。” They recommended deliberately embedding it: “The struggle for collective intelligence involves more than changing the way we think about our own capacities and our relationship to society, it also includes weaving the potential of collective intelligence into the very fabric of our society.”
2000 年代初期的研究考察了集体智能在数字网络化群体中涌现所需的信息条件。 Work in the early 2000s examined the informational conditions required for collective intelligence to emerge in digitally networked groups. 这一思路与其他理论阐释相呼应，后者强调共同认知、透明反馈和共享认知工具是促成集体智能的机制。 This line of thinking parallels other theoretical accounts that emphasise common awareness, transparent feedback and shared cognitive artefacts as enabling mechanisms for collective intelligence.
Pierre Lévy 是一位颇具影响力的早期理论家，他将集体智能重新界定为一种由网络化媒体和共享符号工具促成的文明层面的现象。 Pierre Lévy was an influential early theorist who reframed collective intelligence as a civilisation-level phenomenon enabled by networked media and shared symbolic tools. 他在著作中指出，新兴的信息环境使新形式的分布式认知和集体意义建构成为可能，并提出了一个名为“积极诠释”（positive interpretation）的方法论纲领，以研究如何在网络空间中培育和治理集体生成的意义与问题解决能力。 In his work he argued that the emerging information environment makes possible new forms of distributed cognition and collective sense-making, and he proposed a methodological program of "positive interpretation" to study how collectively produced meanings and problem-solving capacities can be cultivated and governed in cyberspace. Lévy 的论述强调文化与语义基础设施，即共同语言、本体和中介工具，它们使分散的个体能够协调行动、积累知识，并参与大规模的协作智能活动。 Lévy's account emphasises cultural and semantic infrastructure, i.e. common languages, ontologies and mediation tools, that allow dispersed individuals to coordinate, accumulate knowledge and engage in large-scale cooperative intelligence. 他的思想最初通过其 1990 年代的著述得到广泛传播，随后在后来的论文和综述中得到重申，对有关集体智能的数字基础，以及如何设计制度与技术以支持集体反思和决策的讨论产生了重要影响。 His ideas, first widely circulated in his 1990s writings and reiterated in later essays and reviews, have been influential in shaping debates about the digital foundations of collective intelligence and about how institutions and technologies can be designed to support collective reflection and decision-making.
Howard Bloom (1995) 讨论了大众行为——集体行为—— Howard Bloom (1995) discussed mass behavior – collective behavior –
其范围从夸克层面延伸至细菌、植物、动物和人类社会层面。 from the level of quarks to the level of bacterial, plant, animal, and human societies. 他强调，生物适应使地球上大多数生物成为他所谓的“一台学习机器”的组成部分。 He stressed the biological adaptations that have turned most of this earth's living beings into components of what he calls "a learning machine". Bloom 将细胞凋亡、并行分布式处理、群体选择和超有机体等概念结合起来，提出了一套解释集体智能如何运作的理论。 Bloom combined the concepts of apoptosis, parallel distributed processing, group selection, and the superorganism to produce a theory of how collective intelligence works. 后来 (2000)，他展示了如何用计算机生成的“复杂适应系统”和“遗传算法”来解释相互竞争的细菌菌落与人类社会的集体智能，这些概念由 John Holland 开创。 Later (2000), he showed how the collective intelligences of competing bacterial colonies and human societies can be explained in terms of computer-generated "complex adaptive systems" and the "genetic algorithms", concepts pioneered by John Holland.
Bloom 将集体智能的演化追溯至 10 亿年前我们的细菌祖先，并展示了多物种智能自生命诞生以来是如何运作的。 Bloom traced the evolution of collective intelligence to our bacterial ancestors 1 billion years ago and demonstrated how a multi-species intelligence has worked since the beginning of life. 从技术角度看，蚂蚁社会展现出的智能超过了除人类以外的任何其他动物，而且它们会合作饲养牲畜，例如饲养蚜虫以供“挤奶”。 Ant societies exhibit more intelligence, in terms of technology, than any other animal except for humans and co-operate in keeping livestock, for example aphids for "milking". 切叶蚁照料真菌，并搬运树叶来喂养真菌。 Leaf cutters care for fungi and carry leaves to feed the fungi.
Tom Atlee 主要关注人类，以及提升 Howard Bloom 所谓的“群体智商”的工作。 Tom Atlee focused primarily on humans and on work to upgrade what Howard Bloom calls "the group IQ". Atlee 认为，可以培育集体智能，“以克服‘群体思维’和个体认知偏差，使一个集体能够围绕同一过程开展合作，同时提升智力表现。” Atlee felt that collective intelligence can be encouraged "to overcome 'groupthink' and individual cognitive bias in order to allow a collective to cooperate on one process – while achieving enhanced intellectual performance." George Pór 将集体智能现象定义为“人类共同体通过分化与整合、竞争与协作等创新机制，向更高层次的复杂性与和谐演进的能力。” George Pór defined the collective intelligence phenomenon as "the capacity of human communities to evolve towards higher order complexity and harmony, through such innovation mechanisms as differentiation and integration, competition and collaboration." Atlee 和 Pór 指出，“集体智能还涉及形成统一的注意焦点和衡量标准，从而为采取行动提供适当的阈值”。 Atlee and Pór stated that "collective intelligence also involves achieving a single focus of attention and standard of metrics which provide an appropriate threshold of action". 他们的方法植根于科学共同体这一隐喻。 Their approach is rooted in scientific community metaphor.
Atlee 和 Pór 提出，集体智能领域首先应被视为一项人类事业，其中，思维方式、分享意愿，以及对分布式智能服务于共同利益的价值持开放态度，都是至关重要的，尽管群体理论和人工智能也能有所贡献。 Atlee and Pór suggested that the field of collective intelligence should primarily be seen as a human enterprise in which mind-sets, a willingness to share and an openness to the value of distributed intelligence for the common good are paramount, though group theory and artificial intelligence have something to offer. 尊重集体智能的个体对自身能力充满信心，并认识到整体确实大于各个部分之和。 Individuals who respect collective intelligence are confident of their own abilities and recognize that the whole is indeed greater than the sum of any individual parts. 最大限度地发挥集体智能，有赖于组织接纳并完善“黄金建议”的能力；所谓“黄金建议”，就是任何成员提出的任何可能有用的意见。 Maximizing collective intelligence relies on the ability of an organization to accept and develop "The Golden Suggestion", which is any potentially useful input from any member. 群体思维往往会阻碍集体智能，因为它将意见来源限制在少数特定个体之内，或在尚未将潜在的黄金建议充分完善并付诸实施之前，就将其过滤掉。 Groupthink often hampers collective intelligence by limiting input to a select few individuals or filtering potential Golden Suggestions without fully developing them to implementation.
2008 年，Don Tapscott 和 Anthony D. Williams 提出，集体智能就是大规模协作，而实现这种协作需要四个条件： In 2008, Don Tapscott and Anthony D. Williams, proposed that collective intelligence is mass collaboration, and four conditions are needed to enable it:
开放——分享想法和知识产权：尽管这些资源能够带来相对于竞争对手的优势，但允许他人分享想法，并通过协作实现显著改进和接受审视，能够带来更大的收益。 Openness – Sharing ideas and intellectual property: though these resources provide the edge over competitors more benefits accrue from allowing others to share ideas and gain significant improvement and scrutiny through collaboration.
对等协作（Peering）——一种横向组织形式，例如 Linux 程序的“开放”：用户可以自由修改和开发该程序，前提是将其提供给他人使用。 Peering – Horizontal organization as with the 'opening up' of the Linux program where users are free to modify and develop it provided that they make it available for others.

群体的智慧The Wisdom of Crowds
James Surowiecki 在其著作《群体的智慧》（2005）中对集体智能理论作出了重要贡献，书中探讨了如何通过聚合一群个体的信息，作出比任何单个成员或专家所作决策更好的决策。 A major contribution to the theory of collective intelligence was made by James Surowiecki in his book The Wisdom of Crowds (2005), which explores how the aggregation of information from groups of individuals can lead to better decisions than those made by any single member or expert. Surowiecki 指出了群体展现“群体智慧”的三个关键条件：多样性、思考的独立性以及去中心化。 Surowiecki identifies three key conditions for a group to display "crowd wisdom": diversity, independence of thought, and decentralization. 在这些条件下，集体判断可能比专家判断更准确、更具创新性，也更具韧性。 Under these conditions, collective judgments can be more accurate, innovative, and resilient than those of experts.
群体智慧的实证研究Empirical studies of Wisdom of Crowds
多项关于群体智慧的实证研究支持这样一种观点：当成员提供独立且多样的视角时，群体在估计、决策和协调任务中的表现优于个人。 Several empirical studies on the wisdom of the crowd support the notion that groups outperform individuals in estimation, decision-making, and coordination tasks when members contribute independent and diverse perspectives. 近期研究进一步证实并拓展了 Surowiecki 的发现：Simoiu et al. (2020) 开展了大规模实验，验证了在多个领域中，聚合群体答案所得的结果优于个人判断，并凸显了群体表现的一致性以及随领域而异的差异。 Recent research reinforces and expands upon Surowiecki's findings: Simoiu et al. (2020) conducted large-scale experiments validating that crowd aggregated answers across many domains outperform individual judgments, highlighting crowd consistency and domain-dependent variation.

聚合与群体智慧Aggregation and Wisdom of Crowds
在集体智能研究中，聚合是指将多个个体的判断、信号或行为组合成单一群体层面输出的过程，例如形成决策、预测或解决方案。 In the study of collective intelligence, aggregation refers to the process of combining multiple individual judgments, signals, or behaviors into a single group-level output, such as a decision, forecast, or solution. 聚合机制是“群体智慧”现象的核心：只要群体具有多样性、意见独立性以及汇总这些意见的方法（例如对数值估计取平均值，或对备选方案进行投票），许多个体估计值的平均值或以其他方式组合得到的估计值，就可能比大多数个人甚至专家的估计更准确。P 可以将分散的信息聚合为价格，使其近似于对未来事件的共识性概率预测，其表现往往优于许多个人预测者。 Aggregation mechanisms are central to “the wisdom of crowds” phenomena, where the average or otherwise combined estimate of many individuals can be more accurate than that of most individuals or even experts, provided the group has diversity, independence of opinions, and a way to aggregate them (for example, by averaging numerical estimates or voting over alternatives).P can aggregate dispersed information into prices that approximate consensus probabilistic forecasts of future events, often outperforming many individual forecasters. 问题解决群体的模型进一步表明，成员在认知和启发式方法上的多样性有利于聚合，因为多样化群体能够以集体方式探索解空间，其效率高于同质化群体，甚至高于单独行动的高能力个体。 Models of problem‑solving groups further indicate that aggregation benefits from cognitive and heuristic diversity among members, as diverse groups can collectively explore solution spaces more effectively than homogeneous groups or even high‑ability individuals alone.

Kameda, Toyokawa and Tindale (2022) 综述了对经典群体智慧模型加以拓展的信息聚合机制。 Kameda, Toyokawa and Tindale (2022) review information aggregation mechanisms that extend the classical wisdom of crowds model. 作者区分了共识决策与组合决策：前者旨在使群体达成共同结果，后者则指个体在受到影响的同时作出独立选择。 The authors distinguish between consensus decision making, where groups aim to reach a common outcome, and combined decision making, where individuals make influenced but independent choices. 他们指出了一系列能够使集体准确性超越简单平均的方法，包括按专业知识和准确性加权、按置信度加权汇总、自愿参与、“不确定时模仿”等社会学习规则，以及认知分工。 They identify a range of procedures that can improve collective accuracy beyond simple averaging, including expertise and accuracy weighting, confidence-weighted aggregation, opt-in participation, social-learning rules such as copy-when-uncertain, and cognitive division of labour. 该综述广泛借鉴了预测竞赛的证据；在这些竞赛中，大量参与者作出概率预测，并根据预测准确性获得评分。 The review draws extensively on forecasting tournament evidence, in which large numbers of participants make probabilistic predictions that are scored for accuracy. 这些竞赛的结果表明，多样化团队、校准训练以及加权或极端化汇总算法能够取得高于未加权平均值的准确性。 Results from these tournaments show that diverse teams, calibration training and weighted or extremised aggregation algorithms can achieve higher accuracy than unweighted means. 作者认为，这些机制说明，当个体异质性得到保留，且汇总规则被设计为减少相关性错误和信息级联时，集体智能便能够涌现。 The authors argue that these mechanisms illustrate how collective intelligence can emerge when individual heterogeneity is retained and aggregation rules are designed to reduce correlated errors and information cascades.
群体智慧的局限性Limitations of the wisdom of crowds
Lorenz et al. (2011) 的一项值得警惕的研究表明，即使群体成员之间仅存在轻微的社会影响，也可能通过降低意见多样性并促使估计值趋同于不准确的数值，削弱群体智慧效应。 A notable cautionary study by Lorenz et al. (2011) showed that even mild social influence among group members can undermine the wisdom of crowds effect by reducing diversity of opinions and causing convergence on inaccurate estimates. 他们识别出了三种主要现象：社会影响效应降低了多样性，却未提高准确性；范围缩小效应使真实值在估计值范围中的位置趋向边缘，从而降低群体判断的可靠性；置信效应则提高了个体的信心，却未带来群体准确性的相应改善。 They identified three main phenomena: the social influence effect reduces diversity without improving accuracy; the range reduction effect shifts the true value toward the periphery of estimate ranges, making the crowd less reliable; and the confidence effect increases individuals' confidence with no corresponding improvement in group accuracy.
在 Orzechowski et al. 的研究中，作者使用决策树和支持向量机的集成重现了这一效应，并得出结论：输入之间的依赖性和较低的多样性可能抵消更大规模群体通常具有的优势。 In Orzechowski et al. The authors reproduce this effect using ensembles of decision trees and support vector machines and conclude that dependence and low diversity of inputs can negate the usual benefits of larger groups. 这一结果强调了在汇总多个判断时保持独立性和多样性的重要性。 This result emphasises the importance of preserving independence and diversity when aggregating many judgments.
集体智能因子 cCollective intelligence factor c
最初，尝试为集体智能建立度量指标的研究者将其称为“集体智商”。 Initially, researchers attempting to create a metric for collective intelligence called it ‘collective IQ.’ 他们将其定义为“衡量人们在应对重要挑战时协同工作能力的指标，即他们能够多么迅速而明智地预判或应对某种情境，并将集体的感知、记忆、洞察力、愿景、规划、推理、预见能力和经验转化为可应用的知识”。 They defined it as ‘a measure of how well people can work together on important challenges – how quickly and intelligently they can anticipate or respond to a situation, leveraging their collective perception, memory, insight, vision, planning, reasoning, foresight, and experience into applicable knowledge.’ 如今，对集体智能的新科学认识将其定义为群体执行广泛任务的一般能力。 Now, new scientific understanding of collective intelligence defines it as a group's general ability to perform a wide range of tasks.
其定义、操作化和统计方法与研究个体一般智力的心理测量学方法相似。 The definition, operationalization and statistical methods are similar to the psychometric approach of general individual intelligence. 在这一方法中，个体在一组给定认知任务上的表现被用来测量一般认知能力；这种能力以英国心理学家 Charles Spearman 提出的一般智力因子 g 表示，并通过因子分析提取。 Hereby, an individual's performance on a given set of cognitive tasks is used to measure general cognitive ability indicated by the general intelligence factor g proposed by English psychologist Charles Spearman and extracted via factor analysis. 正如 g 用于反映个体之间在认知任务上的表现差异，集体智能研究旨在寻找一个与之对应的群体智力因子，即“c 因子”（也称为“集体智能因子”（CI）），以反映群体之间在任务表现上的差异。 In the same vein as g serves to display between-individual performance differences on cognitive tasks, collective intelligence research aims to find a parallel intelligence factor for groups 'c factor' (also called 'collective intelligence factor' (CI)) displaying between-group differences on task performance. 不过，这里的任务是指小群体执行的心理或智力任务，尽管研究者希望这一概念能够推广至其他类型的表现，以及从家庭、公司乃至整座城市的各种群体或人群。 Yet tasks, hereby, refer to mental or intellectual tasks performed by small groups even though the concept is hoped to be transferable to other performances and any groups or crowds reaching from families to companies and even whole cities. 由于个体的 g 因子得分与全量表 IQ 得分高度相关，而后者又被视为对 g 的良好估计，因此，这种集体智能测量也可被视为群体的智力指标，或与个体智商（IQ）相对应的群体智商（Group-IQ），尽管该得分本身并不是一个商值。 Since individuals' g factor scores are highly correlated with full-scale IQ scores, which are in turn regarded as good estimates of g, this measurement of collective intelligence can also be seen as an intelligence indicator or quotient respectively for a group (Group-IQ) parallel to an individual's intelligence quotient (IQ) even though the score is not a quotient per se.
从数学上看，c 和 g 都是概括不同任务之间正相关关系的变量，其前提是某一任务上的表现可以与其他类似任务上的表现进行比较。因此，c 是群体间变异的一个来源，只能被理解为某个群体相对于给定相关总体中其他群体在 c 因子上的相对位置。 Mathematically, c and g are both variables summarizing positive correlations among different tasks supposing that performance on one task is comparable with performance on other similar tasks. c thus is a source of variance among groups and can only be considered as a group's standing on the c factor compared to other groups in a given relevant population. 这一概念与其他竞争性假说形成对照；这些假说采用其他相关结构来解释群体智能，例如认为群体智能由若干同等重要但彼此独立的因子构成，类似于个体人格研究中的发现。 The concept is in contrast to competing hypotheses including other correlational structures to explain group intelligence, such as a composition out of several equally important but independent factors as found in individual personality research.
此外，这一科学构想还旨在探索影响集体智能的原因，例如群体规模、协作工具或群体成员的人际交往技能。 Besides, this scientific idea also aims to explore the causes affecting collective intelligence, such as group size, collaboration tools or group members' interpersonal skills. 例如，MIT Center for Collective Intelligence 宣布，将识别集体智能的基因组作为其主要目标之一，旨在建立一套“组织构成要素或基因的分类体系，这些要素或基因可以组合和重组，以利用群体的智慧”。 The MIT Center for Collective Intelligence, for instance, announced the detection of The Genome of Collective Intelligence as one of its main goals aiming to develop a "taxonomy of organizational building blocks, or genes, that can be combined and recombined to harness the intelligence of crowds".

研究表明，个体智力受到遗传和环境因素的影响。 Individual intelligence is shown to be genetically and environmentally influenced. 类似地，鉴于 c 与群体成员个体智力的相关性仅为中等程度，集体智能研究旨在探究某些群体为何比其他群体表现得更有智慧。 Analogously, collective intelligence research aims to explore reasons why certain groups perform more intelligently than other groups given that c is just moderately correlated with the intelligence of individual group members. 根据 Woolley 等人的研究结果，团队凝聚力、动机和满意度均与 c 不相关。 According to Woolley et al.'s results, neither team cohesion nor motivation or satisfaction is correlated with c. 然而，他们声称发现了三个与 c 显著相关的因素：发言轮次数的方差、群体成员的平均社会敏感性以及女性比例。 However, they claim that three factors were found as significant correlates: the variance in the number of speaking turns, group members' average social sensitivity and the proportion of females. 这三个因素对 c 的预测能力相近，但只有社会敏感性在统计上显著（b=0.33, P=0.05）。 All three had similar predictive power for c, but only social sensitivity was statistically significant (b=0.33, P=0.05).
发言轮次数表明，“由少数人主导对话的群体，其集体智能低于对话轮次分配更均等的群体”。 The number of speaking turns indicates that "groups where a few people dominated the conversation were less collectively intelligent than those with a more equal distribution of conversational turn-taking". 因此，让多名团队成员有机会发言，可以提高群体的智能水平。 Providing multiple team members with the chance to speak up therefore makes a group more intelligent.
群体成员的社会敏感性通过眼神读心测试（Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test，RME）测量，其与 c 的相关系数为 .26。 Group members' social sensitivity was measured via the Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test (RME) and correlated .26 with c. 该测试要求参与者识别图片中他人眼神所表达的想法或感受，并采用选择题形式进行评估。 This test asks participants to detect thinking or feeling expressed in other peoples' eyes presented on pictures and is assessed in multiple choice format. 该测试旨在测量人们的心智理论（theory of mind，ToM），也称为“心智化”或“读心”，指将信念、欲望或意图等心理状态归于他人的能力，以及理解他人拥有不同于自身的信念、欲望、意图或视角的程度。 The test aims to measure peoples' theory of mind (ToM), also called 'mentalizing' or 'mind reading', which refers to the ability to attribute mental states, such as beliefs, desires or intents, to other people and in how far people understand that others have beliefs, desires, intentions or perspectives different from their own ones. RME 是一种面向成年人的 ToM 测试，具有足够的重测信度，并能稳定地区分对照组与高功能自闭症或阿斯伯格综合征个体。 RME is a ToM test for adults that shows sufficient test-retest reliability and constantly differentiates control groups from individuals with functional autism or Asperger Syndrome. 它是成人 ToM 测试中接受度最高、经过充分验证的测试之一。 It is one of the most widely accepted and well-validated tests for ToM within adults. ToM 可被视为情绪智力这一更广泛概念中一组相互关联的技能与能力。 ToM can be regarded as an associated subset of skills and abilities within the broader concept of emotional intelligence.
女性比例对 c 的预测作用在很大程度上由社会敏感性所中介（Sobel z = 1.93, P= 0.03），这与先前研究所发现的女性在社会敏感性测试中得分更高的结果一致。 The proportion of females as a predictor of c was largely mediated by social sensitivity (Sobel z = 1.93, P= 0.03) which is in line with previous research showing that women score higher on social sensitivity tests. 从统计学角度而言，中介作用阐明了因变量与自变量之间关系的内在机制，而 Woolley 在接受《哈佛商业评论》采访时也认同，这些发现意味着女性群体比男性群体更聪明。 While a mediation, statistically speaking, clarifies the mechanism underlying the relationship between a dependent and an independent variable, Woolley agreed in an interview with the Harvard Business Review that these findings are saying that groups of women are smarter than groups of men. 然而，她也对这一说法作了限定，指出真正重要的是群体成员具有较高的社会敏感性。 However, she relativizes this stating that the actual important thing is the high social sensitivity of group members.
当前理论认为，集体智能因子 c 是自下而上与自上而下的过程共同作用所产生的涌现属性。 Current theory holds that the collective intelligence factor c is an emergent property resulting from bottom-up as well as top-down processes. 自下而上的过程涵盖群体成员特征的聚合。 The bottom-up processes cover aggregated group-member characteristics. 自上而下的过程涵盖影响群体协作与协调方式的群体结构和规范。 The top-down processes cover group structures and norms that influence a group's way of collaborating and coordinating.
过程Processes
自上而下的过程Top-down processes
自上而下的过程涵盖群体互动，包括结构、流程和规范等。 Top-down processes cover group interaction, such as structures, processes, and norms. 对话中的轮流发言就是此类自上而下过程的一个例子。 An example of such top-down processes is conversational turn-taking. 研究进一步表明，集体智能较高的群体总体上沟通更多，沟通分配也更均等；参与情况同样如此，而且这一现象既见于面对面交流的群体，也见于仅通过文字交流的在线群体。 Research further suggest that collectively intelligent groups communicate more in general as well as more equally; same applies for participation and is shown for face-to-face as well as online groups communicating only via writing.
自下而上的过程Bottom-up processes
自下而上的过程包括群体构成，即聚合到团队层面的群体成员特征。 Bottom-up processes include group composition, namely the characteristics of group members which are aggregated to the team level. 此类自下而上过程的例子包括群体成员的平均社会敏感性，以及平均智力得分和最高智力得分。 An example of such bottom-up processes is the average social sensitivity or the average and maximum intelligence scores of group members. 此外，研究发现，集体智能与群体的认知多样性有关，其中包括思维方式和视角的多样性。 Furthermore, collective intelligence was found to be related to a group's cognitive diversity including thinking styles and perspectives. 认知风格具有适度多样性的群体，其集体智能高于认知风格非常相似或差异很大的群体。 Groups that are moderately diverse in cognitive style have higher collective intelligence than those who are very similar in cognitive style or very different. 因此，成员彼此过于相似的群体缺乏取得良好表现所需的多样化视角和技能。 Consequently, groups where members are too similar to each other lack the variety of perspectives and skills needed to perform well.
串行过程与并行过程Serial vs Parallel processes
在人类历史的大部分时期，集体智能局限于小型部落群体，成员通过实时的并行互动汇集意见。 For most of human history, collective intelligence was confined to small tribal groups in which opinions were aggregated through real-time parallel interactions among members. 在现代，大众传播、大众媒体和网络技术使集体智能能够扩展至跨越各大洲和时区分布的大规模群体。 In modern times, mass communication, mass media, and networking technologies have enabled collective intelligence to span massive groups, distributed across continents and time-zones. 为适应这种规模变化，大规模群体中的集体智能主要采用串行投票过程，例如随时间推移汇总赞成票、点赞和评分。 To accommodate this shift in scale, collective intelligence in large-scale groups been dominated by serialized polling processes such as aggregating up-votes, likes, and ratings over time. 一项关于串行集体智能的重要研究发现，串行投票系统收到的第一票可使最终结果产生 34% 的偏差。 In one significant study of serialized collective intelligence, it was found that the first vote contributed to a serialized voting system can distort the final result by 34%.
为解决大规模群体中输入信息的串行汇总问题，集体智能领域的近期进展致力于以并行系统取代串行的投票、民意调查和市场机制，例如仿照自然界中同步集群建立的“人类集群”。 To address the problems of serialized aggregation of input among large-scale groups, recent advancements collective intelligence have worked to replace serialized votes, polls, and markets, with parallel systems such as "human swarms" modeled after synchronous swarms in nature. 这些由联网的人类组成的人工集群以群体智能（Swarm Intelligence）的自然过程为基础，使参与者能够以并行方式协同工作，通过涌现的集体智能回答问题并作出预测。 Based on natural process of Swarm Intelligence, these artificial swarms of networked humans enable participants to work together in parallel to answer questions and make predictions as an emergent collective intelligence. 在一个备受瞩目的例子中，CBS Interactive 向一个人类集群发起挑战，要求其预测肯塔基德比赛马的结果。 In one high-profile example, a human swarm challenge by CBS Interactive to predict the Kentucky Derby. 该集群正确预测了前四匹赛马及其名次，克服了 542–1 的赔率，将 $20 的赌注变成了 $10,800。 The swarm correctly predicted the first four horses, in order, defying 542–1 odds and turning a $20 bet into $10,800.
斯坦福大学医学院和 Unanimous AI 的研究人员在一系列已发表的研究中展示了并行集体智能在医疗应用中的价值；在这些研究中，多组医生通过实时集群算法连接起来，受命判读胸部 X 光片以诊断是否存在肺炎。 The value of parallel collective intelligence was demonstrated in medical applications by researchers at Stanford University School of Medicine and Unanimous AI in a set of published studies wherein groups of human doctors were connected by real-time swarming algorithms and tasked with diagnosing chest x-rays for the presence of pneumonia. 当以“人类集群”的形式协同工作时，由经验丰富的放射科医生组成的小组与采用传统方法时相比，诊断错误减少了 33%。 When working together as "human swarms", the groups of experienced radiologists demonstrated a 33% reduction in diagnostic errors as compared to traditional methods.
证据Evidence
Woolley, Chabris, Pentland, Hashmi, & Malone (2010) 是这一集体智能科学认识的提出者，他们从公众中随机招募参与者组成 192 个小组，并在研究中发现了一个表征集体智能的单一统计因子。 Woolley, Chabris, Pentland, Hashmi, & Malone (2010), the originators of this scientific understanding of collective intelligence, found a single statistical factor for collective intelligence in their research across 192 groups with people randomly recruited from the public. 在 Woolley 等人的最初两项研究中，各小组共同完成了来自 McGrath 任务环形模型（McGrath Task Circumplex）的不同任务，该模型是一种成熟的群体任务分类体系。 In Woolley et al.'s two initial studies, groups worked together on different tasks from the McGrath Task Circumplex, a well-established taxonomy of group tasks. 所选任务涵盖该环形模型的全部四个象限，包括视觉谜题、头脑风暴、作出集体道德判断，以及围绕有限资源进行谈判。 Tasks were chosen from all four quadrants of the circumplex and included visual puzzles, brainstorming, making collective moral judgments, and negotiating over limited resources. 研究者利用这些任务的结果进行了因子分析。 The results in these tasks were taken to conduct a factor analysis. 两项研究均支持存在一个一般集体智能因子 c，它是群体表现差异的潜在基础；其初始特征值对应的方差解释率为 43%（研究 2 中为 44%），而下一个因子仅解释了 18%（20%）的方差。 Both studies showed support for a general collective intelligence factor c underlying differences in group performance with an initial eigenvalue accounting for 43% (44% in study 2) of the variance, whereas the next factor accounted for only 18% (20%). 这与一般个体智力因子 g 的研究中通常发现的范围相符，该因子通常可解释认知测验中个体间表现差异的 40% 至 50%。 That fits the range normally found in research regarding a general individual intelligence factor g typically accounting for 40% to 50% percent of between-individual performance differences on cognitive tests.
随后，每个小组都完成了一项更复杂的任务，以确定 c 因子得分能否预测原始测验之外的任务表现。 Afterwards, a more complex task was solved by each group to determine whether c factor scores predict performance on tasks beyond the original test. 第一项研究的效标任务是与标准化的计算机程序对弈西洋跳棋，第二项研究的效标任务则是一项复杂的建筑设计任务。 Criterion tasks were playing checkers (draughts) against a standardized computer in the first and a complex architectural design task in the second study. 在同时使用小组成员的个体智力和 c 来预测效标任务表现的回归分析中，c 的影响显著，而个体智力的平均值和最大值的影响均不显著。 In a regression analysis using both individual intelligence of group members and c to predict performance on the criterion tasks, c had a significant effect, but average and maximum individual intelligence had not. 虽然小组成员个体智力的平均值（r=0.15, P=0.04）和最大值（r=0.19, P=0.008）与 c 呈中等程度的相关，但 c 对效标任务表现的预测能力仍强得多。 While average (r=0.15, P=0.04) and maximum intelligence (r=0.19, P=0.008) of individual group members were moderately correlated with c, c was still a much better predictor of the criterion tasks. Woolley 等人认为，这支持集体智能因子 c 的存在，因为它表明，c 的作用超出了小组成员个体智力的作用，因此 c 并非只是个体 IQ 的汇总，也并非只是 IQ 最高的小组成员所产生的影响。 According to Woolley et al., this supports the existence of a collective intelligence factor c, because it demonstrates an effect over and beyond group members' individual intelligence and thus that c is more than just the aggregation of the individual IQs or the influence of the group member with the highest IQ.
Engel 等人 (2014) 使用一套耗时更短的任务测验组合，重复验证了 Woolley 等人的发现：因子分析中的第一个因子解释了群体间表现方差的 49%，而后续各因子解释的方差均不足这一数值的一半。 Engel et al. (2014) replicated Woolley et al.'s findings applying an accelerated battery of tasks with a first factor in the factor analysis explaining 49% of the between-group variance in performance with the following factors explaining less than half of this amount. 此外，他们在仅通过文字交流、在线协作的小组中也发现了类似结果，并证实女性比例和社会敏感性在这两种情况下均对集体智能的形成起作用。 Moreover, they found a similar result for groups working together online communicating only via text and confirmed the role of female proportion and social sensitivity in causing collective intelligence in both cases. 与 Woolley 等人类似，他们也使用 RME 测量社会敏感性，而该测验实际上旨在测量人们通过他人的眼神识别其心理状态的能力。 Similarly to Woolley et al., they also measured social sensitivity with the RME which is actually meant to measure people's ability to detect mental states in other peoples' eyes. 然而，这些在线协作的参与者彼此既不认识，也完全看不到对方。 The online collaborating participants, however, did neither know nor see each other at all. 作者据此得出结论：RME 得分必然与更广泛的一组社会推理能力有关，而不仅仅涉及根据他人的眼神作出推断的能力。 The authors conclude that scores on the RME must be related to a broader set of abilities of social reasoning than only drawing inferences from other people's eye expressions.
此后，在整个学期中共同协作的 MBA 学生小组、在线游戏小组，以及来自不同文化的群体和处于短期或长期等不同情境中的群体中，也发现了 Woolley 等人所指的集体智能因子 c。 A collective intelligence factor c in the sense of Woolley et al. was further found in groups of MBA students working together over the course of a semester, in online gaming groups as well as in groups from different cultures and groups in different contexts in terms of short-term versus long-term groups. 这些研究均未将团队成员的个体智力得分作为控制变量。 None of these investigations considered team members' individual intelligence scores as control variables.
预测效度Predictive validity
除了原始实验所显示的能够预测群体在更复杂的效标任务上的表现外，研究还发现，集体智力因子 c 能够预测群体在持续数月的 MBA 课程中完成各类任务时的表现。 Next to predicting a group's performance on more complex criterion tasks as shown in the original experiments, the collective intelligence factor c was also found to predict group performance in diverse tasks in MBA classes lasting over several months. 其中，集体智力较高的群体在小组作业中获得的分数显著更高，尽管其成员在其他独立完成的作业中并未表现得更好。 Thereby, highly collectively intelligent groups earned significantly higher scores on their group assignments although their members did not do any better on other individually performed assignments. 此外，集体智力较高的团队，其表现会随时间推移而改善，这表明集体智力较高的团队学习效果更好。 Moreover, highly collective intelligent teams improved performance over time suggesting that more collectively intelligent teams learn better. 这可能是集体智力与个体智力的又一相似之处，因为研究发现，智力较高的人掌握新材料的速度更快。 This is another potential parallel to individual intelligence where more intelligent people are found to acquire new material quicker.
个体智力可用于预测多种人生结果，从学业成就、职业成功到健康状况，甚至死亡率。 Individual intelligence can be used to predict plenty of life outcomes from school attainment and career success to health outcomes and even mortality. 集体智力能否预测群体在认知任务上的表现之外的其他结果，仍有待研究。 Whether collective intelligence is able to predict other outcomes besides group performance on mental tasks has still to be investigated.
与个体智力的潜在联系Potential connections to individual intelligence
Gladwell (2008) 表明，个体 IQ 与成功之间的关系只在一定范围内成立；当 IQ 超过估计为 120 的阈值后，额外增加的 IQ 分数并不会转化为现实生活中的优势。 Gladwell (2008) showed that the relationship between individual IQ and success works only to a certain point and that additional IQ points over an estimate of IQ 120 do not translate into real life advantages. Group-IQ 是否也存在类似的界限，或者其带来的优势是否呈线性增长且没有上限，仍有待探索。 If a similar border exists for Group-IQ or if advantages are linear and infinite, has still to be explored. 同样，个体智力研究中还有许多可能适用于集体智力的思路，例如智力随时间的发展或智力能否提高等问题；在这些方面，也需要进一步研究个体智力与集体智力之间可能存在的联系。 Similarly, demand for further research on possible connections of individual and collective intelligence exists within plenty of other potentially transferable logics of individual intelligence, such as, for instance, the development over time or the question of improving intelligence. 尽管人类智力能否通过训练得到增强仍存在争议，但群体的集体智力可能更容易通过更换团队成员，或引入组织结构和技术来提高。 Whereas it is controversial whether human intelligence can be enhanced via training, a group's collective intelligence potentially offers simpler opportunities for improvement by exchanging team members or implementing structures and technologies. 此外，研究发现，阅读文学小说和观看剧情电影能够提高社会敏感性，至少这种提高在短期内是可实现的。 Moreover, social sensitivity was found to be, at least temporarily, improvable by reading literary fiction as well as watching drama movies. 此类训练最终能在多大程度上通过提高社会敏感性来提升集体智力，仍是一个悬而未决的问题。 In how far such training ultimately improves collective intelligence through social sensitivity remains an open question.
还有一些更为成熟的概念和因子模型试图解释个体认知能力，包括将智力划分为流体智力和晶体智力，以及智力差异的层级模型。 There are further more advanced concepts and factor models attempting to explain individual cognitive ability including the categorization of intelligence in fluid and crystallized intelligence or the hierarchical model of intelligence differences. 然而，除了一般性的“c 因子”之外，目前尚缺乏对集体智力基因组（Genomes of collective intelligence）因子结构的进一步补充解释和概念化。 Further supplementing explanations and conceptualizations for the factor structure of the Genomes of collective intelligence besides a general 'c factor', though, are missing yet.
关于集体智力的争议与反证Controversies and Counter-Evidence for Collective Intelligence
其他学者通过将团队成员的一般智力汇总到团队层面来解释团队表现，而不是另行构建一个总体集体智力指标。 Other scholars explain team performance by aggregating team members' general intelligence to the team level instead of building an own overall collective intelligence measure. Devine and Philips (2001) 在一项元分析中表明，平均认知能力能够预测实验室情境（0.37）和实地情境（0.14）中的团队表现——请注意，这只是一个较小的效应。 Devine and Philips (2001) showed in a meta-analysis that mean cognitive ability predicts team performance in laboratory settings (0.37) as well as field settings (0.14) – note that this is only a small effect. 其他学者发现，需要高度沟通与合作的任务受认知能力最低的团队成员影响最大，这表明这种影响在很大程度上取决于具体任务。 Suggesting a strong dependence on the relevant tasks, other scholars showed that tasks requiring a high degree of communication and cooperation are found to be most influenced by the team member with the lowest cognitive ability. 研究表明，对于那些以选出最佳团队成员作为最有效策略的任务，认知能力最高的成员对任务表现的影响最大。 Tasks in which selecting the best team member is the most successful strategy, are shown to be most influenced by the member with the highest cognitive ability.
由于 Woolley et al. 的结果并未显示群体满意度、群体凝聚力或动机有任何影响，因此，这些结果至少隐含地质疑了这些概念对群体整体表现的重要性，从而与元分析已证实的群体凝聚力、动机和满意度对群体表现具有积极影响的证据相矛盾。 Since Woolley et al.'s results do not show any influence of group satisfaction, group cohesiveness, or motivation, they, at least implicitly, challenge these concepts regarding the importance for group performance in general and thus contrast meta-analytically proven evidence concerning the positive effects of group cohesion, motivation and satisfaction on group performance.
Credé and Howardson (2017) 声称，Woolley et al. 的系列研究中支持集体智力的证据较弱，并且可能存在错误或对数据的误解。 Credé and Howardson (2017) claimed that the evidence for collective intelligence in the body of work by Woolley et al. was weak and may contain errors or misunderstandings of the data. 例如，Woolley et al. 在研究结果中指出，Wonderlic Personnel Test（WPT；其研究中使用的一项个体智力测验）的个体最高分为 39，但同时又指出，同一测验中团队平均分的最高值也是 39。 For example, Woolley et al. stated in their findings that the maximum individual score on the Wonderlic Personnel Test (WPT; an individual intelligence test used in their research) was 39, but also that the maximum averaged team score on the same test was also a 39. 这意味着，他们的样本中似乎存在这样一个团队：所有成员各自在 WPT 上都获得了完全相同的分数，而且恰好都达到了 Woolley et al. 研究中观察到的 WPT 最高分；Credé and Howardson 声称，这种情况不太可能发生。 This indicates that their sample seemingly had a team composed entirely of people who, individually, got exactly the same score on the WPT, and also all happened to all have achieved the highest scores on the WPT found in Woolley et al. Credé and Howardson claimed this was unlikely to occur. 数据中发现的其他异常表明，研究结果可能部分受到低投入作答的影响。 Other anomalies found in the data indicate that results may be driven in part by low-effort responding. 例如，Woolley et al. 的数据表明，在一项要求团队用 10 分钟尽可能多地想出砖块用途的任务中，至少有一个团队得分为 0。 For instance, Woolley et al.'s data indicates that at least one team scored a 0 on a task in which they were given 10 minutes to come up with as many uses for a brick as possible. 同样，Woolley et al. 的数据表明，至少有一个团队在满分为 50 的 WPT 上的平均得分为 8。 Similarly, Woolley et al.'s data show that at least one team had an average score of 8 out of 50 on the WPT. Credé 和 Howardson 声称，对于认真投入的研究参与者而言，出现这种情况的概率几乎为零。 Credé and Howardson claimed that the probability of this occurring with study participants who are putting forth effort is nearly zero. 这或许可以解释为什么 Woolley 等人发现，群体成员的个体智力得分无法预测群体表现。 This may explain why Woolley et al. found that the group's individual intelligence scores were not predictive of performance. 此外，在以人为被试的研究中，任务投入程度较低可能会夸大基于跨任务表现相似性而提出的所谓集体智能因子的证据，因为团队在某项研究任务上的低投入可能会泛化为在多项任务上的低投入。 In addition, low effort on tasks in human subjects research may inflate evidence for a supposed collective intelligence factor based on similarity of performance across tasks, because a team's low effort on one research task may generalize to low effort across many tasks. 值得注意的是，这种现象的存在仅仅是因为实验室研究对研究参与者而言利害关系较小，而非因为它反映了组织中团队的运作方式。 It is notable that such a phenomenon is present merely because of the low stakes setting of laboratory research for research participants and not because it reflects how teams operate in organizations.
Woolley、Kim 和 Malone (2018) 回应了 Credé 和 Howardson 针对集体智能因子 (c) 提出的方法论批评，报告了对其早期工作的重新分析和扩展，以澄清测量程序及其稳健性。 Woolley, Kim and Malone (2018) responded to the methodological critiques of the collective intelligence factor (c) raised by Credé and Howardson, reporting re-analyses and extensions of their earlier work to clarify measurement procedures and robustness. 通过使用规模更大且经过重新审查的数据集、额外的任务控制措施以及替代评分方法，他们表明，在处理批评者提出的具体问题后，最初与 c 相关的群体层面预测变量，如社会敏感性、对话中的轮流发言以及群体中的女性比例，仍能解释群体在不同任务上表现的方差。 Using larger and re-examined datasets, additional task controls and alternative scoring approaches, they showed that group-level predictors originally associated with c, such as social sensitivity, conversational turn-taking and proportion of women in the group, continue to account for variance in group performance across diverse tasks after addressing the specific concerns raised by critics. 作者强调，c 并非个体智力的简单对应物，而是一个具有多个侧面、依赖于任务且需要谨慎进行操作化的构念。 The authors emphasised that c is not a simple analogue of individual intelligence but a multi-faceted, task-dependent construct that requires careful operationalisation. 他们建议采用标准化测验组合、提高任务评分的透明度，并在不同的群体构成和现实世界任务中开展进一步的重复研究。 They recommend standardized batteries, transparency in task scoring, and further replication with varied group compositions and real-world tasks.
2022 年 5 月，《量化人类群体中的集体智能》(Riedl et al., 2021) 的作者发表了正式更正，指出某个软件包中的错误导致其验证性因子分析中的平均方差提取量（average variance extracted，AVE）数值报告有误；该更正将所报告的单因子模型先前的 AVE 值 44% 替换为更正后的 AVE 值 19.6%。 In May 2022 the authors of "Quantifying collective intelligence in human groups" (Riedl et al., 2021) published a formal correction noting that an error in a software package had led to incorrectly reported average variance extracted (AVE) values in their confirmatory factor analyses; the correction replaces an earlier AVE of 44% with a corrected AVE of 19.6% for the reported one-factor models.
平均方差提取量（AVE）是验证性因子分析中使用的一项标准度量，用于量化潜在因子能够解释观测指标方差的多少。 The average variance extracted (AVE) is a standard measure used in confirmatory factor analysis that quantifies how much of the variance in the observed indicators is explained by the latent factor. 一项被广泛引用的经验法则 (Fornell and Larcker, 1981) 认为，AVE ≥ 0.50 表明收敛效度可接受。 A commonly cited rule of thumb (Fornell and Larcker, 1981) considers AVE ≥ 0.50 indicative of acceptable convergent validity. 显著低于这一阈值的数值表明，该因子只能解释少量的指标方差，因此引发了对收敛效度的担忧。 Values substantially below this threshold suggest that the factor explains little of the indicator variance, and therefore raise concerns about convergent validity.
因此，更正后的 AVE 值使人有理由质疑所报告的具体验证性因子模型的收敛效度。 The corrected AVE therefore raises legitimate questions about the convergent validity of the specific confirmatory factor models reported. 与此同时，Riedl 等人在更正中指出，这些变化并不改变论文的主要结论，而且该文为一般集体智能因子的存在提供了多方面的证据（包括所报告的因子载荷、替代模型的其他模型拟合指标，以及来自 22 项研究的元分析数据集）。 At the same time, Riedl et al. note in their correction that these changes do not alter the paper's major conclusions, and the article presents multiple lines of evidence for a general collective intelligence factor (including reported factor loadings, other model fit indices for alternative models, and a meta-analytic dataset drawn from 22 studies). 这一问题是持续进行的方法论争论的一部分：群体表现究竟最适合用单一的一般集体智能（CI）因子来刻画，还是用多个依赖于任务的维度来刻画。 The issue is part of an ongoing methodological debate about whether group performance is best characterised by a single general CI factor or by multiple, task-dependent dimensions.
集体智能与复杂适应系统Collective intelligence and complex adaptive systems
研究越来越多地将集体智能系统视为复杂适应系统（complex adaptive systems，CAS）的一个子集。 Research increasingly conceptualizes collective intelligence systems as a subset of complex adaptive systems (CAS). 这些系统表现出三项核心属性：适应不确定且不断变化的环境、自主的自组织，以及通过局部相互作用涌现出全局行为。 These systems display three core properties: adaptation to uncertain and changing environments, autonomous self-organization, and emergence of global behaviors from local interactions.
集体智能依赖于反馈动力学、分布式控制、冗余、稳健性和非线性交互模式等促成机制：这些特征同样也是 CAS 的核心特征。 Collective intelligence draws on enabling mechanisms such as feedback dynamics, distributed control, redundancy, robustness, and nonlinear interaction patterns: features also central to CAS.
在社交媒体平台和协作式 AI 环境等人机系统中，集体智能通过社会主体与技术主体之间大规模的多对多交互而涌现。 In human-machine systems, like social media platforms and collaborative AI environments, collective intelligence emerges from large-scale, many-to-many interactions across social and technological agents. 这些系统通过适应性反馈过程不断调整自身的结构和策略，有时会产生无法通过孤立分析参与者来理解的“涌现”集体行为。 These systems continuously adjust their structure and strategies via adaptive feedback processes, sometimes resulting in "emergent" collective behaviors that cannot be understood by analyzing participants in isolation.
在科学与工程实践中，集体智能研究既包括分析，即理解生物或社会集体系统中的自然现象，也包括设计，即创建用于分布式问题求解的人工系统、计算系统或混合系统。 Within scientific and engineering practice, the study of collective intelligence includes both analysis—understanding natural phenomena in biological or social collective systems, and design, creating artificial, computational, or hybrid systems for distributed problem-solving. 随着研究的推进，集体智能与复杂适应系统日益融合，为在大规模社会技术环境中对韧性、可扩展性和涌现智能进行建模、仿真及工程实现提供了方法。 As research advances, collective intelligence and complex adaptive systems increasingly converge, offering methodologies for modeling, simulating, and engineering resilience, scalability, and emergent intelligence in large-scale socio-technical environments.
Ha 和 Tang (2022) 综述了集体智能与复杂系统的思想如何应用于机器学习。 Ha and Tang (2022) review how ideas from collective intelligence and complex systems are used in machine learning. 他们指出，集成方法、基于种群的训练、演化搜索、多智能体强化学习、群体启发式优化、联邦学习和混合专家模型都依赖于多样性、并行搜索与聚合。 They note that ensemble methods, population-based training, evolutionary search, multi-agent reinforcement learning, swarm-inspired optimisation, federated learning and mixture-of-experts models rely on diversity, parallel search and aggregation. 作者将这些技术作为人工系统中集体问题求解的实例，并概述了复杂系统理论可能为未来研究提供启示的领域。 The authors present these techniques as examples of collective problem solving in artificial systems and outline areas where complex systems theory may inform future work.
近期的一种理论视角将各个尺度上的智能都视为复杂适应系统中的一种集体行为。 A recent theoretical perspective treats intelligence at all scales as a form of collective behaviour in complex adaptive systems. Falandays et al. (2023) 认为，许多被描述为个体智能的过程，都可以理解为组成部分之间的涌现性交互，无论这些交互发生在神经系统、多细胞生物、动物群体，还是人类社会与文化网络之中。 Falandays et al. (2023) argue that many processes described as individual intelligence can be understood as emergent interactions among components, whether these occur within neural systems, multicellular organisms, animal groups or human social and cultural networks. 作者列举了生物、认知和社会系统中的实例，以支持这样一种观点：个体智能与集体智能之间的区分，往往反映的是分析层次的差异，而非本质上的类别差异。 The authors present examples from biological, cognitive and social systems to support the view that the distinction between individual and collective intelligence often reflects the level of analysis rather than a fundamental difference in kind.
集体智能、未来研究与战略前瞻Collective intelligence, futures studies and strategic foresight
未来研究与战略前瞻运用集体智能方法来探索和塑造可能的未来，通常通过参与式过程，将不同利益相关者的见解汇聚为共同的情景、愿景和预警分析。 Futures studies and strategic foresight use collective intelligence methods to explore and shape possible futures, often through participatory processes that aggregate insights from diverse stakeholders into shared scenarios, visions, and early‑warning analyses. 一些作者将其称为“集体前瞻智能”（collective forward intelligence），强调围绕公共政策、城市发展和可持续性转型等领域的长期变化，开展动态、网络化的意义建构。 Some authors describe this as "collective forward intelligence," emphasizing dynamic, networked sense‑making about long‑term change in areas such as public policy, urban development, and sustainability transitions.
实际应用包括利用群体预测来预测大流行病、经济趋势和选举结果，例如瑞典 FOI 在支持乌克兰开展预期性治理时的实践，以及 Futures4Europe 等欧洲倡议对面向未来的集体智能在研究与创新政策中的应用所开展的探索。 Practical applications include crowd forecasting for predicting pandemics, economic trends, and election outcomes, as demonstrated by Sweden's FOI in supporting Ukrainian anticipatory governance, and European initiatives like Futures4Europe that explore future-oriented collective intelligence for research and innovation policy. 在全球未来研究中，千年项目的全球未来智能系统等系统整合数据、专家判断和公众意见，以支持科技前瞻与政策制定。 In global futures research, systems such as the Millennium Project's Global Futures Intelligence System integrate data, expert judgment, and public input to support science–technology foresight and policy-making.
集体智能与协商民主Collective intelligence and deliberative democracy
协商民主是参与式民主的一种形式，强调公民之间在充分知情的基础上开展包容性对话，通过理性辩论和共识构建，就公共议题作出集体决策。 Deliberative democracy is a form of participatory democracy which emphasizes informed and inclusive dialogue among citizens to reach collective decisions on public issues through reasoned debate and consensus-building. 它通过汇聚多元视角和知识来发挥集体智能的作用，从而提高决策的正当性与质量。 It harnesses collective intelligence by aggregating diverse perspectives and knowledge to improve the legitimacy and quality of decisions.
协商民主的一项关键制度机制是公民大会，也称为微型公众（mini-public）。 A key institutional mechanism of deliberative democracy is the citizens' assembly, also called a mini-public. 公民大会中的协商以广泛的信息交流、由主持人引导的讨论，以及对专家证言的听取和讨论为特征。 Deliberation in citizens' assemblies is characterized by extensive information exchange, facilitated discussions, and engagement with expert testimony. 这一过程使参与者能够在提出建议之前，审慎地思考价值观和社会层面的权衡取舍。 This process enables participants to reflect on values and societal trade-offs thoughtfully before formulating recommendations.
公民大会的参与者通过抽签随机选出，以形成具有代表性的人口横截面。 The participants of a citizens' assembly are randomly selected via sortition to provide a representative cross-section of the population. 这一遴选过程通常先进行广泛的随机抽样，再进行分层，以确保在性别、年龄、社会经济地位、族裔，以及可能涉及的对待讨论议题的看法等类别上，具有人口构成的多样性。 This selection process typically involves an initial broad random sampling followed by stratification to ensure demographic diversity across categories such as gender, age, socioeconomic status, ethnicity, and possibly opinion on the topic to be discussed. 通过以这种方式呈现社会的缩影，公民大会能够汇集通常比民选立法机构更为多样的经历和观点，从而增强集体智能。 By capturing a microcosm of society in this manner, citizens' assemblies enhance collective intelligence by enabling a diversity of experiences and viewpoints greater than that typically found in elected legislatures.
对于在传统政治环境中常被回避或难以解决的高度争议性和敏感性议题，公民大会尤其具有价值。 Citizens' assemblies are particularly valuable in tackling highly controversial and sensitive issues often avoided or intractable in traditional political settings. 例如，爱尔兰公民大会在为爱尔兰堕胎问题的公共辩论与立法改革（正式称为关于宪法第八修正案的辩论）提供参考方面发挥了关键作用，表明此类大会能够针对分歧严重的议题，提出具有正当性、基于充分信息且经过深思熟虑的政策建议。 For example, the Citizens' Assembly (Ireland) played a pivotal role in informing the public debate and legislative reform on abortion in Ireland (formally referred to as the debate on the Eighth Amendment of the Constitution), demonstrating how such assemblies can generate legitimate, informed, and well-considered policy recommendations on divisive topics.
研究表明，公民大会通过增强参与者和更广泛公众的政治效能感、增进社会信任、抵制政治极化，以及通过集体协商增强社会凝聚力，为民主革新作出了重要贡献。 Research shows that citizens' assemblies significantly contribute to democratic renewal by enhancing political efficacy among participants and the broader public, fostering social trust, countering political polarization, and reinforcing societal cohesion through collective deliberation. 它们让原本不参与政治的人参与其中，从而促进包容性治理，使决策更具代表性，并提高民主正当性。 They facilitate inclusive governance by involving people who are otherwise politically disengaged, resulting in more representative decision-making and increased democratic legitimacy. 公民大会形成经过深思熟虑的公共判断，帮助政策制定者作出艰难决策，从而提升治理质量，这也是世界各国政府日益采用公民大会来共同制定政策的原因。 Assemblies produce well-considered public judgments that help policymakers make difficult decisions, thereby elevating the quality of governance, which is why governments around the world are increasingly using them to cocreate policy.
Rafał Olszowski 的《开放政策制定中的集体智能》（Collective Intelligence in Open Policymaking，2024）聚焦于开放政策制定举措，为理解集体智能如何超越个体层面演化提供了一个全面的框架。 Rafał Olszowski's Collective Intelligence in Open Policymaking (2024) provides a comprehensive framework for understanding the evolution of collective intelligence beyond the individual level, focusing on open policymaking initiatives. 该书将理论见解与 Decide Madrid 和 Better Reykjavik 等实践案例研究相结合，探讨集体智能如何塑造民主创新以及公民对政策设计的参与。 The book combines theoretical insights with practical case studies, such as Decide Madrid and Better Reykjavik, exploring how collective intelligence shapes democratic innovation and citizen participation in policy design.
《数字时代的公共审议：平台、参与与合法性》（Public Deliberation in the Digital Age: Platforms, Participation, and Legitimacy，2025）全面考察了数字平台如何变革审议式民主进程与公民参与。 Public Deliberation in the Digital Age: Platforms, Participation, and Legitimacy (2025) offers a comprehensive examination of how digital platforms are transforming deliberative democratic processes and citizen engagement. 该书将理论见解与实证案例研究相结合，分析数字工具如何影响参与、审议质量以及民主决策的合法性。 The book combines theoretical insights with empirical case studies to analyze how digital tools influence participation, deliberation quality, and the legitimacy of democratic decision-making. 该书讨论了从气候治理到青年参与和 AI 介导对话的多种应用，探讨了数字鸿沟和多语言审议等挑战。 It discusses diverse applications ranging from climate governance to youth engagement and AI-mediated dialogue, exploring challenges such as the digital divide and multilingual deliberation. 该书采用投入—过程—产出合法性框架，系统评估数字平台如何塑造参与者的构成、参与的方式，以及这些过程如何与更广泛的公共成果相联系。 Using a framework of input-throughput-output legitimacy, it systematically evaluates how digital platforms shape who participates, how engagement occurs, and how these processes connect to broader public outcomes.
Lewis 等人（2025）讨论了数字赋能的公民基础设施的设计原则，此类基础设施旨在支持大规模审议以及公民与公共机构之间的持续反馈。 Lewis et al. (2025) discuss design principles for digitally enabled civic infrastructure intended to support large scale deliberation and ongoing feedback between citizens and public institutions. 该论文描述了一种模块化架构，将频繁的数字参与、对参与者贡献的算法综合以及有针对性的小组审议相结合，使局部审议成果能够被汇总并反馈至政策过程。 The paper describes a modular architecture that combines frequent digital engagement, algorithmic synthesis of contributions, and targeted small-group deliberation so that local deliberative outputs can be aggregated and fed back into policy processes. 该论文建议使用对意见数据进行聚类和桥接的工具、贝叶斯自适应评估，以及用于测试平台设计的迭代式沙盒试验。 It recommends tools for clustering and bridging opinion data, Bayesian adaptive evaluation, and iterative sandboxing to test platform designs. 作者们（包括 UCL 集体智能、公共政策与社会创新教授 Geoff Mulgan）强调了汇总方法的透明度、社区对数据的控制权，以及防范操纵和排斥的保障措施等治理要求。 The authors (including Geoff Mulgan, Professor of Collective Intelligence, Public Policy and Social Innovation at UCL) emphasise governance requirements such as transparency of aggregation methods, community control of data, and safeguards against manipulation and exclusion.
尽管该论文没有明确使用“集体智能”一词，但其提议与既有的集体智能概念和机制直接对应。 Although the paper does not explicitly use the term "collective intelligence", its proposals map directly onto established collective-intelligence concepts and mechanisms. 从集体智能的视角来看，所描述的架构明确回应了产生可靠群体判断所需的三项已得到充分研究的要求：通过广泛且多样化的参与扩大信息基础；通过维持或恢复各项贡献独立性的机制避免相关性错误；以及通过有明确原则依据的汇总程序，将大量低成本输入转化为质量更高的集体产出。 From a collective-intelligence perspective, the described architecture explicitly addresses three well-studied requirements for producing reliable group judgements: broad and diverse participation to increase the information base; mechanisms for preserving or restoring independence of contributions to avoid correlated errors; and principled aggregation procedures that transform many low-cost inputs into higher-quality collective outputs. 公民议会等规模较小、资源充足的审议模块充当高质量信号的生成器；算法综合与定向抽样将这些局部信号转化为系统层面的输入；自适应评估则提供学习和改进所必需的反馈回路。 Small, well resourced deliberative modules such as citizens' assemblies operate as high-quality signal generators; algorithmic synthesis and targeted sampling convert those local signals into system-level inputs; and adaptive evaluation provides the feedback loops necessary for learning and improvement. 因此，该论文提供了一套应用性设计方案，用于将审议机构与计算汇总方法及治理实践相衔接，而这些方法和实践正是当代集体智能研究的核心内容。 The paper therefore provides an applied design programme for connecting deliberative institutions with computational aggregation and governance practices that are central to contemporary collective-intelligence research.
互动管理（Interactive Management）是一种由引导者协助开展的群体方法，起源于 John N. Warfield 的研究，并在包括结构化民主对话（Structured Democratic Dialogue）在内的多个研究传统中得到发展；该方法让参与者判断其想法之间的影响关系，并以有向图而非排序列表呈现结果。 Interactive Management, a facilitated group method originating in the work of John N. Warfield and developed in several research traditions including Structured Democratic Dialogue, has participants judge influence relations between their ideas and presents the result as a directed graph rather than a ranked list. 将其作为集体智能方法加以应用的研究者描述了该方法所依赖的引导技能，而且已有研究评估了其在线应用；在线环境不再受到单一会议室的限制。 Researchers who apply it as a collective-intelligence method have described the facilitation skills it depends on, and it has been assessed for use online, where the constraints of a single meeting room no longer apply.
计算集体智能Computational collective intelligence
2001 年，波兰 AGH University 的 Tadeusz (Tad) Szuba 提出了一个关于集体智能现象的形式化模型。 In 2001, Tadeusz (Tad) Szuba from the AGH University in Poland proposed a formal model for the phenomenon of collective intelligence. 该模型假定，集体智能是一种无意识、随机、并行且分布式的计算过程，由社会结构以数理逻辑的方式运行。 It is assumed to be an unconscious, random, parallel, and distributed computational process, run in mathematical logic by the social structure.
在该模型中，生物个体和信息被建模为携带数理逻辑表达式的抽象信息分子。 In this model, beings and information are modeled as abstract information molecules carrying expressions of mathematical logic. 它们在进行预期位移的同时，因与环境相互作用而发生准随机位移。 They are quasi-randomly displacing due to their interaction with their environments with their intended displacements. 它们在抽象计算空间中的相互作用产生了多线程推理过程，而这一过程被我们感知为集体智能。 Their interaction in abstract computational space creates multi-thread inference process which we perceive as collective intelligence. 因此，这里采用了一种非图灵计算模型。 Thus, a non-Turing model of computation is used. 这一理论使我们能够将集体智能作为社会结构的一种属性，给出简明的形式化定义，而且似乎能够很好地适用于从细菌菌落到人类社会结构的广泛生物群体。 This theory allows simple formal definition of collective intelligence as the property of social structure and seems to be working well for a wide spectrum of beings, from bacterial colonies up to human social structures. 将集体智能视为一种特定的计算过程，可以为若干社会现象提供直观的解释。 Collective intelligence considered as a specific computational process is providing a straightforward explanation of several social phenomena. 针对这一集体智能模型，研究者提出了 IQS（IQ Social，社会智商）的形式化定义，将其定义为“反映社会结构推理活动的 N 元素推理在时间和领域上的概率函数”。 For this model of collective intelligence, the formal definition of IQS (IQ Social) was proposed and was defined as "the probability function over the time and domain of N-element inferences which are reflecting inference activity of the social structure". 尽管 IQS 似乎在计算上难以求解，但如上所述，将社会结构建模为计算过程，为近似求解提供了可能。 While IQS seems to be computationally hard, modeling of social structure in terms of a computational process as described above gives a chance for approximation. 潜在应用包括通过最大化企业的 IQS 来优化企业，以及分析细菌菌落的集体智能所涉及的耐药性。 Prospective applications are optimization of companies through the maximization of their IQS, and the analysis of drug resistance against collective intelligence of bacterial colonies.
集体智能与领导力Collective intelligence and leadership
组织团队中的集体智能依赖于能够整合分布式知识、协调专业化贡献并实现适应性决策的领导过程。 Collective intelligence in organisational teams depends on leadership processes that enable the integration of distributed knowledge, coordination of specialised contributions, and adaptive decision-making. 这些特征表明，它与复杂性领导理论，即对复杂适应系统中领导力的研究，存在广泛的交集。 These features indicate a broad overlap with complexity leadership theory, i.e the study of leadership in complex adaptive systems.
研究确定了支持集体智能的两大类领导形式：团队内部的共享式或分布式领导，以及协调跨组织、跨部门行动的系统领导。 Research has identified two broad leadership forms that support collective intelligence: shared or distributed leadership within teams, and systems leadership that coordinates action across organisations and sectors.
共享式领导是指由多名团队成员行使领导职能，而非由一名指定的领导者行使。 Shared leadership is where leadership functions are exercised by multiple team members rather than a single designated leader. 它也被定义为一种涌现现象，即当条件适宜时，在团队成员之间自然产生的现象。 It is also defined as an emergent phenomenon that arises naturally among team members when the conditions are conducive to it. 此类共享式领导始终与更高的团队绩效和更强的集体效能感相关。 Such shared leadership is consistently associated with higher team performance and greater collective efficacy. 对共享式领导的网络和密度测量，往往比单一来源的测量更能预测绩效。 Network and density measures of shared leadership often predict performance better than single-source measures. 其他决定因素包括任务相互依赖性、心理安全感和成员的专业知识。 Other determining factors are task interdependence, psychological safety, and member expertise.
系统领导是指跨越机构边界开展的领导活动，旨在动员多个组织和利益相关方，应对任何单一行动主体都无法解决的问题。 Systems leadership refers to leadership enacted across institutional boundaries to mobilise multiple organisations and stakeholders for problems that no single actor can solve. 通过网络分布、并以一系列职能或过程的形式开展的领导活动，可以增强群体整合多样化信息和适应复杂任务的能力。 Leadership dispersed through networks and practised as a set of functions or processes can increase a group's capacity to integrate diverse information and adapt to complex tasks. 一项对实证案例研究的综述发现，有效的系统领导始于明确的行动号召和坚定投入的联盟的形成。 A review of empirical case studies found that effective systems leadership begins with a clear call to action and the formation of a committed coalition. 持续保持推进势头取决于系统结构、支持性文化和人际因素，包括维持共同目标、牢固的关系、好奇心，以及对系统动态和韧性的理解。 Sustaining momentum depends on system structures, supportive cultures and interpersonal factors, including maintaining a shared purpose, strong relationships, curiosity, and an understanding of system dynamics and resilience. 成功的指标包括工作中的愉悦感、系统资源的增加，以及群体层面数据中可观察到的改善。 Indicators of success include enjoyment in the work, gains in system resources and observable improvements in population-level data. 此外，还应将领导视为一种分布式的资源供给职能，而非由单一个人承担的角色。 Also, seeing leadership as a distributed, resourcing function rather than a single-person role. 当需要大规模组织集体智能时，系统领导尤为重要，例如在应对大流行等危机的紧急行动中。 Systems leadership is especially relevant where collective intelligence must be organised at scale, for example in the emergency response to a crisis such as a pandemic.
为充分释放大规模集体智能的潜力，Cairney and Toomey (2025) 主张采用多层级、系统导向的领导方式来应对复杂的社会问题。 To fully unlock collective intelligence at scale, Cairney and Toomey (2025) advocate for multi-level and systems-oriented leadership for addressing complex societal problems. 这一方法印证了早期研究的结论，即成功的领导是一种分布式能力，必须在各组织和网络中加以培育。 This approach confirms the conclusions of earlier research that successful leadership is a distributed capability that must be cultivated across organisations and networks. 他们确定了具有集体智能的系统领导的若干关键特征：“摒弃英雄式的自上而下领导和集中控制，转而支持跨越边界的协作；培养在复杂系统中行动所需的特质（例如谦逊）、思维方式（例如全局观）和技能（例如引导协调）（尽管对于何为复杂系统尚无共同认识）；并为这一方法寻求组织和政治支持。” They identified several key features of collectively intelligent systems leadership: "reject heroic top-down leadership and central control in favour of collaboration across boundaries; develop attributes (e.g. humility), mindsets (e.g. big picture), and skills (e.g. facilitation) to act in complex systems (albeit without a common view on what a complex system is); and, seek organisational and political support for this approach."
Pentland (2012) 报告的应用社会计量学研究表明，团队绩效与三个可测量的互动参数相关：交流量、参与的均衡程度以及外部联系。 Pentland (2012) reports applied sociometric studies showing that team performance correlates with three measurable interaction parameters: amount of exchanges, evenness of participation, and external connections. 他发现，团队内部可测量的互动与沟通模式，如均衡的轮流发言、广泛参与和高度投入，比个体智力指标更能可靠地预测团队绩效。 He found that measurable patterns of interaction and communication within teams, such as balanced conversational turn-taking, broad participation, and high engagement, predict team performance more reliably than individual intelligence measures. 该研究将领导界定为一种塑造互动模式的角色，其中领导者和“富有魅力的联结者”促进分布式参与和信息流动，而非仅仅行使层级权威。 The study frames leadership as a pattern-shaping role in which leaders and “charismatic connectors” facilitate distributed participation and information flow rather than solely exercising hierarchical authority. 从集体智能的角度看，该论文与 CI 的核心机制相契合：它将可靠的绩效视为互动模式（而非个体能力）的涌现属性，识别了支持共享情境意识和信息整合的可测量信号，并展示了反馈循环（测量 → 可视化 → 训练 → 改善互动）如何增强集体解决问题的能力。 From a collective intelligence perspective the paper maps onto core CI mechanisms: it treats reliable performance as an emergent property of interaction patterns (not of individual ability), it identifies measurable signals that support shared situational awareness and information integration, and it demonstrates how feedback loops (measurement → visualization → training → improved interaction) can increase collective problem solving. 这项应用研究影响了管理实践，但应结合 Woolley et al. (2010) 等经过同行评审的研究一同阅读。 This applied work has influenced managerial practice but should be read alongside peer-reviewed studies such as Woolley et al. (2010).

作为集体智能实例的维基媒体项目Wikimedia projects as examples of collective intelligence
在维基百科及其他维基媒体项目中，大量志愿者在没有集中式编辑控制的情况下，协作创建和整理内容。 In Wikipedia and other Wikimedia projects, large numbers of volunteers collaborate to create and curate content without centralised editorial control. 在这一参与式知识构建过程中，维基百科编辑者采用共识决策方法，甚至具体到每一次编辑，尽量减少对权威的依从，转而采用征询所有利益相关方和参与方意见的流程。 In this participatory knowledge-building process, Wikipedia editors rely on consensus decision-making methods down to the edit level, minimizing deference to authority in favour of processes that consult all interested and involved parties.
因此，学术文献将这些项目列为大规模集体智能的突出实例。 These projects are therefore cited in the academic literature as prominent examples of large-scale collective intelligence. 例如，Livingstone (2016) 指出，维基百科“通过依托由人类智能、程序化机器人、社会官僚体系和软件协议构成的社会技术组合，在一个将信息同时体现为产品和过程的虚拟知识空间中，实现了 Lévy 所提出的更具人文性的集体智能（CI）（...）。” Livingstone (2016), for example, states that Wikipedia, “by relying on sociotechnical ensembles of human intelligence, programmed bots, social bureaucracy, and software protocols, a more humanistic CI, as proposed by Lévy, is realized in a virtual knowledge space that embodies information as both product and process (...).”
早期研究将维基百科的知识生产模式概念化为：以共识为导向的参与式机制取代传统的专家把关机制，汇集非专业贡献者的多样化贡献。 Early research conceptualised Wikipedia’s model of knowledge production as replacing traditional expert gatekeeping with consensus-oriented, participatory mechanisms that harness diverse contributions from lay contributors. 一项针对学术研究的元综述指出，维基百科展现了集体智能的关键特征，例如间接协调（痕迹协同，stigmergy）、通过机器人和工具实现的分布式认知，以及从大量个体编辑和讨论中涌现的结构。 A meta-review of academic work notes that Wikipedia exhibits key collective intelligence features such as indirect coordination (stigmergy), distributed cognition through bots and tools, and emergent structures arising from many individual edits and discussions. 近期关于维基百科自动化内容生成的系统综述也将人类贡献者与自动化智能体（机器人）之间的互动描述为更广泛的集体智能生态系统的一部分，这对互联网规模的协作式内容创建和质量管理具有启示意义。 Recent systematic reviews of automated content generation for Wikipedia also describe the interaction between human contributors and automated agents (bots) as part of a broader collective intelligence ecosystem, with implications for collaborative content creation and quality management at Internet scale.
维基数据是一个与维基媒体项目相关联、由协作编辑构建的结构化数据知识图谱，也被描述为一个大规模集体智能平台。 Wikidata, a collaboratively edited structured data knowledge graph associated with Wikimedia projects, has also been described as a large-scale collective intelligence platform. 它支持多语言知识表示，由全球志愿者社区维护，使分布广泛的参与者能够共同贡献于一个共享数据资源，为各种维基媒体应用和外部应用提供支撑。 It supports multilingual knowledge representation and is maintained by a global community of volunteers, allowing widely distributed contributions to a shared data resource that underpins various Wikimedia and external applications.

政府与公共政策Government and public policy
Lowry 等人 (2023) 提出了一项经过同行评审的案例研究，展示了如何运用结构化的集体智能方法重新设计公共部门的数据基础设施。 Lowry et al. (2023) present a peer-reviewed case study illustrating how structured collective intelligence methods can be used to redesign public sector data infrastructures. 作者与研究人员、数据保管者及政策利益相关方合作，采用基于情景的设计、交互式管理和书面创意法（ideawriting），揭示了北爱尔兰纵向研究中尚未满足的用户需求及阻碍环节。 Working with researchers, data custodians and policy stakeholders, the authors applied scenario-based design, interactive management and ideawriting to surface unmet user needs and points of friction in the Northern Ireland Longitudinal Study. 这一过程促成了具体的制度变革，包括重新设计的研究界面、合成训练数据集和新的用户论坛，旨在扩大对复杂行政数据的访问，并提升其政策价值。 The process produced concrete institutional changes, including a redesigned research interface, synthetic training datasets and new user forums intended to widen access to complex administrative data and strengthen its policy value. 该研究被引用为证据，表明集体智能技术能够支持政府数据系统的切实改进，同时也凸显了利益相关方的代表性，以及维护可直接用于研究的数据集所需的人力投入等挑战。 The study is cited as evidence that collective intelligence techniques can support practical improvements in government data systems while highlighting challenges such as stakeholder representativeness and the labour required to maintain research-ready datasets.
自动化软件构建Automated software construction
通过汇集分布式开发者知识并将其转化为可执行机制的框架，集体智能已被应用于自动化软件工程。 Collective intelligence has been applied to automated software engineering through frameworks that aggregate and operationalise distributed developer knowledge. Liu 等人 (2025) 提出了 CIPAC（基于集体智能的程序自动化构建，Collective Intelligence–based Program Automated Construction），该框架同时利用开源代码仓库中的历史集体智能和开发者社区的实时集体输入，自动构建软件级程序。 Liu et al. (2025) propose CIPAC (Collective Intelligence–based Program Automated Construction), a framework that uses both historical collective intelligence from open-source repositories and real-time collective input from developer communities to automate the construction of software-level programs. 该系统将集体式规格细化、架构生成、程序合成和质量优化整合到单一工作流中，从而能够自动组装完整且通过测试验证的软件项目，而非孤立的代码片段。 The system integrates collective specification refinement, architecture generation, program synthesis and quality optimisation within a single workflow, enabling the automatic assembly of complete and test-validated software projects rather than isolated code fragments. CIPAC 通过对大规模开发者群体的贡献进行加权和汇总，将其与源自群体智能的算法协调机制相结合，并在整个软件开发生命周期中嵌入反馈回路，从而将集体智能转化为可执行机制。 CIPAC operationalises collective intelligence by weighting and aggregating contributions from large developer populations, combining them with algorithmic coordination mechanisms derived from swarm intelligence, and embedding feedback loops across the software development lifecycle. 包括矩阵计算和航空航天领域应用在内的实证案例研究表明，可以系统地利用集体智能，将程序合成的规模从函数级代码扩展到完全集成的软件系统。 Empirical case studies, including applications in matrix computation and aerospace domains, indicate that collective intelligence can be systematically harnessed to scale program synthesis beyond function-level code toward fully integrated software systems.

市场判断Market judgment
由于互联网能够在全球范围内快速传播大量信息，利用集体智能预测股票价格及其走势已变得越来越可行。 Because of the Internet's ability to rapidly convey large amounts of information throughout the world, the use of collective intelligence to predict stock prices and stock price direction has become increasingly viable. 网站汇集尽可能及时的股市信息，使专业或业余股票分析师能够发表观点，也使业余投资者能够提交自己的财务见解，并汇聚形成总体意见。 Websites aggregate stock market information that is as current as possible so professional or amateur stock analysts can publish their viewpoints, enabling amateur investors to submit their financial opinions and create an aggregate opinion. 可以赋予所有投资者的意见相同的权重，从而落实有效应用集体智能的一项关键前提：利用具有不同程度股市专业知识的广大群体，更准确地预测金融市场的行为。 The opinion of all investor can be weighed equally so that a pivotal premise of the effective application of collective intelligence can be applied: the masses, including a broad spectrum of stock market expertise, can be utilized to more accurately predict the behavior of financial markets.
集体智能是 Eugene Fama 的有效市场假说（efficient-market hypothesis）的基础，尽管他的论文并未明确使用集体智能这一术语。 Collective intelligence underpins the efficient-market hypothesis of Eugene Fama – although the term collective intelligence is not used explicitly in his paper. Fama 引用了 Michael Jensen 的研究，其中选取的 115 只基金有 89 只在 1955 至 1964 年期间的表现逊于指数。 Fama cites research conducted by Michael Jensen in which 89 out of 115 selected funds underperformed relative to the index during the period from 1955 to 1964. 但扣除申购费（前端费用）的影响后，仅有 72 只表现逊于指数；扣除经纪交易费用的影响后，则仅有 58 只表现逊于指数。 But after removing the loading charge (up-front fee) only 72 underperformed while after removing brokerage costs only 58 underperformed. 基于此类证据，指数基金成为广受欢迎的投资工具，其投资策略是利用市场的集体智能，而非专业基金经理的判断。 On the basis of such evidence index funds became popular investment vehicles using the collective intelligence of the market, rather than the judgement of professional fund managers, as an investment strategy.
政治与技术领域的预测Predictions in politics and technology
政党动员大量人员制定政策、遴选候选人，以及为竞选活动筹集资金并组织实施。 Political parties mobilize large numbers of people to form policy, select candidates and finance and run election campaigns. 通过各种投票方式汇聚知识，可以使不同观点趋于一致，其依据的假设是：缺乏充分信息的投票在一定程度上具有随机性，可以从决策过程中被过滤掉，从而只留下基于充分信息的共识。 Knowledge focusing through various voting methods allows perspectives to converge through the assumption that uninformed voting is to some degree random and can be filtered from the decision process leaving only a residue of informed consensus. 批评者指出，糟糕的想法、误解和错误观念往往广泛存在，因此，决策过程的设计必须更侧重专家，因为在特定情境下，专家被认为较少会随机投票或基于错误信息投票。 Critics point out that often bad ideas, misunderstandings, and misconceptions are widely held, and that structuring of the decision process must favor experts who are presumably less prone to random or misinformed voting in a given context.

2012 年，The Millennium Project 创建了全球未来集体智能系统（Global Futures Collective Intelligence System，GFIS），该系统集中体现了集体智能：数据／信息／知识、软件／硬件以及专业知识／洞见之间的协同交汇，并通过递归学习过程，作出比各参与者单独决策更好的决策。 In 2012, the Global Futures Collective Intelligence System (GFIS) was created by The Millennium Project, which epitomizes collective intelligence as the synergistic intersection among data/information/knowledge, software/hardware, and expertise/insights that has a recursive learning process for better decision-making than the individual players alone.
新媒体常被认为能够促进和增强集体智能。 New media are often associated with the promotion and enhancement of collective intelligence. 新媒体主要通过数据库和互联网便捷地存储与检索信息的能力，使信息能够轻松共享。 The ability of new media to easily store and retrieve information, predominantly through databases and the Internet, allows for it to be shared without difficulty. 因此，通过与新媒体互动，知识能够在不同来源之间便捷流动，从而形成一种集体智能。 Thus, through interaction with new media, knowledge easily passes between sources resulting in a form of collective intelligence. 交互式新媒体，尤其是互联网的使用，促进了在线互动以及用户之间的这种知识传播。 The use of interactive new media, particularly the internet, promotes online interaction and this distribution of knowledge between users.
Francis Heylighen、Valentin Turchin 和 Gottfried Mayer-Kress 等人从计算机科学和控制论的视角看待集体智能。 Francis Heylighen, Valentin Turchin, and Gottfried Mayer-Kress are among those who view collective intelligence through the lens of computer science and cybernetics. 在他们看来，互联网使集体智能能够在最广泛的全球尺度上发挥作用，从而促进全球大脑的涌现。 In their view, the Internet enables collective intelligence at the widest, planetary scale, thus facilitating the emergence of a global brain.
万维网的开发者 Tim Berners-Lee 致力于推动全球范围内的信息共享与发布。 The developer of the World Wide Web, Tim Berners-Lee, aimed to promote sharing and publishing of information globally. 后来，他的雇主开放了这项技术，供人们免费使用。 Later his employer opened up the technology for free use. 在 '90 年代初期，互联网的潜力仍未得到开发，直到 1990 年代中期，美国高级研究计划局（ARPA）负责人 J.C.R. Licklider 博士所称的“临界规模”要求互联网具备更高的可访问性和实用性。 In the early '90s, the Internet's potential was still untapped, until the mid-1990s when 'critical mass', as termed by the head of the Advanced Research Project Agency (ARPA), Dr. J.C.R. 挑战与未来方向Challenges and future directions
集体智能系统面临着威胁其有效性的重大挑战，尤其是在这些系统变得更加复杂并得到人工智能增强的情况下。 Collective intelligence systems face significant challenges that threaten their effectiveness, particularly as these systems become more complex and augmented by artificial intelligence.
一个核心弱点是社会影响导致的独立思考丧失；Lorenz 等人（2011）表明，这会降低意见的多样性，并导致群体判断趋同于不准确的结论。 A core vulnerability is the loss of independent thought due to social influence, which Lorenz et al. (2011) showed can reduce opinion diversity and lead to convergence on inaccurate group judgments. 他们识别出了一些现象，例如社会影响降低了多样性却未提高准确性、范围缩小使真实值偏离意见分布的中心，以及参与者产生缺乏依据的自信。 They identified phenomena such as social influence reducing diversity without improving accuracy, range reduction shifting true values, and unwarranted confidence among participants.
新兴的集体智能平台日益将 AI 融入其中，以增强人类协作。 Emerging collective intelligence platforms increasingly integrate AI to enhance human collaboration. 根据 Riedl 等人（2025）的研究，AI 可以通过改善分布式知识聚合、协调注意力以及促进集体推理来增强集体智能。 According to Riedl et al. (2025), AI can augment collective intelligence by improving distributed knowledge aggregation, coordinating attention, and facilitating collective reasoning. 然而，大规模部署 AI 也带来了诸多挑战，包括维护用户信任、确保公平的代表性、提升易用性以及保护隐私。 However, deploying AI at scale introduces challenges including preserving user trust, ensuring equitable representation, facilitating usability, and protecting privacy. 社会动态的复杂性以及社群结构的持续变化，要求采用完善的以人为中心的设计原则和治理框架，以避免放大偏见或形成算法单一化生态。 The complexity of social dynamics and constant changes in community structure require sophisticated human-centered design principles and governance frameworks to avoid amplifying biases or creating algorithmic monocultures.
近期研究提出从“人机社会系统”的角度看待集体智能；在这类系统中，人类与异质人工智能体（如算法、机器人程序和机器人）共同构成复杂网络，其涌现结果无法仅凭人类行为或机器行为推导出来。 Recent work has proposed viewing collective intelligence in terms of "human–machine social systems," in which humans and heterogeneous artificial agents (such as algorithms, bots, and robots) jointly form complex networks whose emergent outcomes cannot be deduced from human or machine behavior alone. 在这一视角下，研究人员考察人与人、人与机器以及机器与机器之间不同的交互配置如何影响竞争、协调、合作、传播和集体决策，以及如何通过设计和监管这些社会技术生态系统来提高稳健性、韧性和群体绩效。 Under this perspective, researchers study how different configurations of human–human, human–machine, and machine–machine interactions affect competition, coordination, cooperation, contagion, and collective decision-making, and how design and regulation of these socio-technical ecologies can improve robustness, resilience, and group performance.
将集体智能扩展到国家或全球层面，会进一步增加这些问题的复杂性，因此需要健全的治理结构以及可持续、透明的 AI 系统。 Scaling collective intelligence to national or global levels further complicates these issues, demanding robust governance structures and sustainable, transparent AI systems. 为了在应对气候变化或可持续发展等复杂全球性挑战时保持问题求解的稳健性，未来的平台必须审慎地平衡 AI 增强与人类自主性、多样性及开放性之间的关系。 To maintain robustness in problem-solving around complex global challenges such as climate change or sustainable development, future platforms must carefully balance AI augmentation with human autonomy, diversity, and openness.

分布式集体智能研讨会（DISCOLI）DIStributed COllective Intelligence Workshop (DISCOLI)
DISCOLI（DIStributed COLlective Intelligence，分布式集体智能）系列研讨会是一个年度学术论坛，首次举办于 2022 年，汇集了研究分布式及工程化集体智能的研究人员和实践者。 The DISCOLI (DIStributed COLlective Intelligence) workshop series is an annual academic forum, first held in 2022, that brings together researchers and practitioners working on distributed and engineered forms of collective intelligence. DISCOLI 通常与国际分布式计算系统会议（ICDCS）、智能系统中的分布式计算会议（DCOSS）等会议同期同地举办，重点关注自组织系统和多智能体系统的算法与架构、人类与 AI 的社会技术协作、物联网部署中的集体知识提取、涌现行为的编程与验证，以及相关工程挑战。 Typically co-located with conferences such as the International Conference on Distributed Computing Systems (ICDCS) and Distributed Computing in Smart Systems (DCOSS), DISCOLI focuses on algorithms and architectures for self-organising and multi-agent systems, human-AI socio-technical collaborations, extraction of collective knowledge in Internet of Things deployments, programming and verification of emergent behaviour, and related engineering challenges. 历届研讨会分别在博洛尼亚（2022）、帕福斯（2023）、阿布扎比（2024）和托斯卡纳（2025）举办，第五届计划于 2026 年举行。 Editions have been held in Bologna (2022), Pafos (2023), Abu Dhabi (2024) and Tuscany (2025), with a fifth edition planned for 2026.

国际计算集体智能会议（ICCCI）是一项成熟的年度会议，重点关注集体智能的计算与应用层面。 The International Conference on Computational Collective Intelligence (ICCCI) is an established annual conference focusing on computational and applied aspects of collective intelligence. 自 2000 年代后期以来，该系列会议持续出版论文集（Springer），涵盖计算集体智能、语义网、社交网络、多智能体系统及应用等主题。 Since the late 2000s the series has published proceedings (Springer) under themes such as computational collective intelligence, semantic web, social networks, multi-agent systems and applications. 近期的 ICCCI 会议举办地包括哈马马特（2022）、罗得岛（2021）、布达佩斯（2023）、莱比锡（2024）和胡志明市（2025）。 Recent ICCCI editions have included conferences in Hammamet (2022), Rhodes (2021), Budapest (2023), Leipzig (2024) and Ho Chi Minh City (2025). ICCCI 是开展计算、算法及应用导向的集体智能（CI）研究的主要交流平台，着重关注大规模 CI 系统的方法、评估与部署。 ICCCI serves as a principal venue for computational, algorithmic and application-oriented CI research with an emphasis on methods, evaluation and deployment of large-scale CI systems.
