Kilobot 是一种高 3.3 cm 的低成本集群机器人，由哈佛大学的 Radhika Nagpal 和 Michael Rubenstein 开发。 The Kilobot is a 3.3 cm tall low-cost swarm robot developed by Radhika Nagpal and Michael Rubenstein at Harvard University. 它们可以集群行动（数量超过一千台），执行由用户编程设定、单个机器人无法执行的指令。 They can act in groups (over a thousand), to execute commands programmed by users that could not be executed by individual robots. 机器人群体研究面临的一个问题是单个机器人的成本较高。 A problem with research on robot collectives is that the cost of individual units is high. Kilobot 的零部件总成本低于 $15。 The Kilobot's total cost of parts is under $15. 除成本低廉外，它还可用于协同搬运、人机群交互以及形状自组装等任务。 In addition to low cost, it has applications such as collective transport, human-swarm interaction, and shape self-assembly.
开发Development
2010 年 11 月，Michael Rubenstein 和 Radhika Nagpal 在维斯生物启发工程研究所和美国国家科学基金会的支持下，开始开发 Kilobot。 During November 2010, Michael Rubenstein and Radhika Nagpal began the development of the Kilobot with the support of the Wyss Institute for Biologically Inspired Engineering and the National Science Foundation. Kilobot 设计的两个主要目标是保持低成本，同时具备足够的功能以执行一系列集体任务。 The two primary objectives of the Kilobot's design were to keep the cost low while having enough functionality to perform an array of collective tasks. 开发者的目标之一是使 Kilobot 能够运行 S-DASH 程序： One of the developer's goals was to allow the Kilobot to run the program S-DASH:

设计Design
其电源是一块可充电锂离子电池，根据机器人的活动程度，可为其供电 3–12 小时。 The power source is a rechargeable lithium-ion battery that can power the robot for up to 3–12 hours depending upon how active the robot is. 它们配备了一个三色（红、绿、蓝）LED，用于向用户显示信息。 They are equipped with a three color (red, green, and blue) LED which displays information to the user. 虽然轮子能够有效实现移动，但价格昂贵，会显著增加每台机器人的成本。 While wheels are effective at movement, they are expensive, which substantially increases the cost of each unit. 降低成本的一种方式是使用两个振动器来实现移动。 One way the cost was lowered was using two vibrators for movement. 当其中任意一个振动器启动时，Kilobot 会以约每秒 45° 的速度转动。 When either is activated, the Kilobot turns at about 45° per second. 当两个振动器同时启动时，机器人会以约 1 cm/s 的速度向前移动。 When both are activated, the robot moves forward at about 1 cm/s. 机器人由 3 条刚性支腿支撑，使其机身距表面 2 cm。 The robot stands on 3 rigid legs, which elevates the robot 2 cm above the surface. 每台机器人都配备了红外发射器和接收器，以便彼此通信。 The individual robots are equipped with an infrared transmitter and receiver so that they can communicate with each other. 一台机器人的发射器向表面发出光，光经反射后向上传至附近另一台机器人的接收器，后者随后根据程序执行指令。 The transmitter of a robot sends light toward the surface which reflects up to the receiver of another nearby robot, which then executes a command based on the program. 这些通信和移动方式存在一些缺点：Kilobot 只能在平坦表面上工作，而且无法在长距离移动或长时间运行中保持精确移动。 Some drawbacks of these methods of communication and movement are: the area on which the Kilobot works is limited to flat surfaces and the inability to move precisely over long distances or over an extended period of time.
应用Applications
理论上，Kilobot 旨在模拟昆虫群体，即每台 Kilobot 都与整个群体协作，执行单个机器人无法完成的任务。 In theory, the Kilobot is meant to simulate swarms of insects, in that each Kilobot works with the whole to perform tasks that would not work on an individual level. Kilobot 能够进行协同搬运，即通过共同协作来移动大型物体。 The Kilobots are capable of collective transport, which is the movement of a large object by working together. Kilobot 群体还可以利用 S-DASH 形成不同的形状，并在形状发生变形时进行修复。 Kilobot collectives can also form different shapes using S-DASH and repair them should it be distorted. 根据形状的不同，它们还可能能够改变其尺寸。 Depending on the shape, they may also be able to change its scale. 在一个程序中，它们模拟昆虫的行为，从一个充当“巢穴”的固定不动的 Kilobot 所在的位置出发，分散到周围区域寻找“食物”，而“食物”则是另一台固定不动的 Kilobot。 With one program, they simulated insects by starting from a "home" location, which was a certain stationary Kilobot, and scatter around the area in search of "food", which was another stationary Kilobot. 当一台正在搜索的 Kilobot 找到“食物”后，它会返回“巢穴”所在的位置，将其放下。 When a searching Kilobot found the "food", it traveled back to the "home" location to drop it off. 另一个程序使一组机器人跟随领头机器人，排成一列行进。 Another program caused a group of robots to travel in a line while following a leader robot. 这些机器人会确保自己不会行进得过于靠前，以免后面的机器人掉队。 The robots made sure that they wouldn't travel too far ahead so that the following robots would not fall behind. 借助传感器，它们还能够使自身行为保持同步，例如同步闪烁灯光。 Using their sensors, they also have the ability to synchronize their behavior, such as blinking their lights. 通过使用上方的红外控制器和红外接收器，用户可以进行可扩展的操作。 By using an overhead infrared controller and the infrared receivers, a user can do scalable operations. 这意味着，用户无需逐一接近每个机器人，即可完成充电、编程和启动等简单任务。 This means they don't have to go to each individual robot to do simple tasks such as charging, programming, and start-up.
反响Reception
Kilobot 在 2012 年非洲机器人网络（African Robotics Network）举办的 $10 机器人设计挑战赛的漫游类别中获得第一名；该挑战赛要求工程师设计用于发展中国家儿童教育的低成本机器人。 The Kilobot placed first in the roaming category of the 2012 African Robotics Network $10 Robot Design Challenge, which asked engineers to create low-cost robots for educating children in developing countries.
研发 Kilobot 的目的是让普通公众更容易负担得起廉价的集群机器人（swarm-bot）。 The Kilobot was created for the purpose of making a cheap swarm-bot more affordable to the general public. 秉承这一理念，Kilobot 的设计已开源，供非商业用途使用。 To continue with this mindset, the Kilobots' design has been made open-source for non-commercial use. 此外，开发者还与瑞士制造商 K-team 合作，使公众能够购买这些机器人用于教育或研究。 Also, the developers cooperated with the K-team, a Swiss manufacturer, so that they can be purchased publicly for educational or research purposes.
