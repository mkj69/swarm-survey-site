Abstract摘要
Multi-agent LLM systems are often expected to improve as team size increases, yet the scaling behavior may depend on task structure. Our central contribution is to introduce Steiner’s taxonomy of group tasks as a framework for analyzing multi-agent LLM scaling and focusing the analysis on disjunctive and compensatory tasks. We model independently sampled agents as conditionally independent given the item, which yields their large-team limits: plurality voting converges to the model’s modal answer, and averaging converges to the model’s item-level bias. Across selected representative benchmarks, 13 open-weight models, and teams of up to 30 agents, we find qualitatively different scaling behavior. On disjunctive tasks, the probability that at least one agent is correct grows by 5–20 points with team size, but plurality voting over agents that answer directly realises almost none of this potential, as the model predicts to within 0.5 points on average. Multi-round revision raises accuracy considerably, yet the gain is nearly the same with one peer as with 29. In contrast, scaling provides little benefit on Fermi estimation, despite its natural suitability for aggregation: item-level biases shared across the samples of a model account for about 87% of the squared error, so averaging reduces error by only about 6%. Combining model families helps on Fermi estimation but does not surpass the strongest member on disjunctive tasks. These results show that task structure, together with the mechanism combining member outputs, is a fundamental determinant of team scaling. [Open code placeholder]

1 Introduction1 引言
Motivation. With the rapid emergence of Large Language Model (LLM) multi-agent frameworks (Wu et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023; Du et al., 2024), researchers have deployed LLM ensembles and multi-agent debates across a multitude of reasoning and decision-making problems under the implicit assumption that scaling team size $N$ universally enhances system performance. As LLMs are trained on human generated knowledge largely encoded through language, that inherently also captures cultural and behavioral traits, we turn to observing multi-agent behavior in a similar way human group behavior was observed and studied. The ability of a group to outperform its individual members is among the most studied phenomenon in cognitive science, economics, and social psychology. In his classic 1907 experiment, Francis Galton observed that while individual fairgoers made highly scattered guesses regarding the weight of an ox, the median of their estimates fell within $0.8\%$ of the true weight (Galton, 1907; Surowiecki, 2004). Conversely, the foundational Ringelmann effect (Ingham et al., 1974) reveals the opposing tension in collective dynamics: as group size increases, teams frequently suffer from diminishing per-member returns and performance saturation.
研究动机。随着大语言模型（LLM）多智能体框架的迅速涌现（Wu et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023; Du et al., 2024），研究人员已将 LLM 集成和多智能体辩论应用于众多推理与决策问题，其隐含假设是扩大团队规模 $N$ 能够普遍提升系统性能。由于 LLM 的训练所依据的是主要通过语言编码的人类知识，而这些知识本身也承载着文化和行为特征，因此我们转而借鉴观察和研究人类群体行为的方式来观察多智能体行为。群体能够超越其个体成员的表现，是认知科学、经济学和社会心理学中研究最广泛的现象之一。在其经典的 1907 年实验中，Francis Galton 观察到，尽管集市参与者对一头牛体重的个人猜测高度分散，但他们估计值的中位数与真实体重的偏差不超过 $0.8\%$（Galton, 1907; Surowiecki, 2004）。相反，具有奠基意义的 Ringelmann 效应（Ingham et al., 1974）揭示了集体动力学中的反向张力：随着群体规模增加，团队往往出现人均收益递减和性能饱和。
In Ivan Steiner’s seminal taxonomy of group processes (Steiner, 1972), resolving whether a collective achieves Galtonian crowd wisdom or succumbs to Ringelmann-style process loss depends on the underlying task structure. The tasks observed by Galton are the so-called compensatory tasks Steiner (1972) where group performance is determined by statistical aggregation of continuous estimates. In human crowds, because individual participants possess idiosyncratic cognitive biases and independent heuristic noise, their random errors compensate for one another (Larrick and Soll, 2006; Davis-Stober et al., 2014).
在 Ivan Steiner 关于群体过程的开创性分类体系（Steiner, 1972）中，一个集体究竟能实现 Galton 式的群体智慧，还是会陷入 Ringelmann 式的过程损失，取决于其底层任务结构。Galton 所观察的任务属于 Steiner（1972）所称的补偿型任务，此类任务中的群体表现由连续估计值的统计聚合决定。在人类群体中，由于各参与者具有各自特有的认知偏差和独立的启发式噪声，他们的随机误差会相互抵消（Larrick and Soll, 2006; Davis-Stober et al., 2014）。
Ringelmann originally observed this performance drop on additive tasks, where per-member productivity decayed as group size increased due to coordination losses and social loafing (Latané et al., 1979; Ingham et al., 1974). It was later shown by Kerr and Bruun (1983) that group performance losses are fundamentally mediated by Steiner’s task typology and the perceived dispensability of individual effort. While additive and conjunctive tasks frequently trigger free-rider effects or coordination bottlenecks, compensatory tasks can circumvent these losses when members provide independent, unbiased estimates that aggregate symmetrically. This established that collective human team scaling laws are fundamentally task-dependent. The same observation has been recently made by Kim et al. (2026): LLM agent team performance is task dependent. At the same time, inspired by Ringlemann’s findings, Bertalanič and Fortuna (2026) developed a scaling law for effective team size.
Ringelmann 最初是在加和型（additive）任务中观察到这种表现下降的：由于协调损失和社会惰化，人均生产率随群体规模增加而下降（Latané et al., 1979; Ingham et al., 1974）。Kerr and Bruun（1983）随后表明，群体表现损失从根本上受到 Steiner 任务类型以及个体对自身努力是否可有可无的感知的调节。虽然加和型和合取型（conjunctive）任务经常引发搭便车效应或协调瓶颈，但当成员提供独立、无偏且以对称方式聚合的估计时，补偿型任务可以规避这些损失。这确立了人类团队的集体规模扩展规律从根本上依赖于任务这一结论。Kim et al.（2026）最近也得出了同样的观察结果：LLM 智能体团队的表现取决于任务。与此同时，受 Ringlemann 研究发现的启发，Bertalanič and Fortuna（2026）提出了有效团队规模的扩展定律。
Prior art. Without classifying them into the Steiner taxonomy nor recognizing them explicitly as disjunctive or compensatory tasks, the community has already investigated related multi-agent performance. The rich state of the art on disjunctive tasks represented by benchmarks such as MMLU, GSM8K, MATH500, ARC reports gains from sampling and voting (Wang et al., 2023; junyou li et al., 2024), and from debate (Du et al., 2024) although these gains can saturate or reverse as the number of calls grows (Chen et al., 2024; Bertalanič and Fortuna, 2026). Debate induces conformity (Weng et al., 2025), and correlated errors limit ensembles (Kim et al., 2025). Improvements have also been reported for compensatory tasks represented by Fermi Kalyan et al. (2021); Liu et al. (2025) and guesstimate Chuang et al. (2025) problems. The closest related works are Chen et al. (2024), who explain non-monotone voting by a mixture of easy and hard queries, the basis of our plurality limit, and Bertalanič and Fortuna (2026), who fit a law for the effective team size $N_{\text{eff}}/N$ across 44 conditions and find that only heterogeneous teams escape hard ceilings; homogeneous teams also saturate in Yang et al. (2026), and LLM teams fall short of their best member in Pappu et al. (2026).
已有研究。尽管尚未将这些任务纳入 Steiner 分类体系，也未明确将其识别为析取型或补偿型任务，研究界已经对相关的多智能体表现展开了研究。以 MMLU、GSM8K、MATH500、ARC 等基准为代表的析取型任务（disjunctive tasks）已有丰富的前沿研究，这些研究报告了采样与投票（Wang et al., 2023; junyou li et al., 2024）以及辩论（Du et al., 2024）带来的性能提升，但随着调用次数增加，这些增益可能趋于饱和甚至逆转（Chen et al., 2024; Bertalanič and Fortuna, 2026）。辩论会诱发从众行为（Weng et al., 2025），而误差之间的相关性会限制集成方法的效果（Kim et al., 2025）。对于以费米问题（Kalyan et al., 2021; Liu et al., 2025）和粗略估算问题（Chuang et al., 2025）为代表的补偿型任务（compensatory tasks），也有研究报告了性能提升。与本研究最密切相关的工作是 Chen et al. (2024) 和 Bertalanič and Fortuna (2026)：前者通过简单查询与困难查询的混合来解释投票表现的非单调性，这构成了我们推导相对多数投票极限的基础；后者基于 44 种条件拟合了有效团队规模 $N_{\text{eff}}/N$ 的规律，并发现只有异质团队才能突破硬性上限；Yang et al. (2026) 同样发现同质团队的表现会趋于饱和，而 Pappu et al. (2026) 发现 LLM 团队的表现不及其最优秀成员。

2.2 Modeling Disjunctive Tasks Through Voting2.2 通过投票对析取型任务建模
2.2.1 The Pure Disjunctive Case2.2.1 纯析取情形
In Steiner’s classical disjunctive framework (Steiner, 1972), a task is disjunctive if group success requires only that at least one team member generates the correct solution $y^{*}\in\mathcal{Y}$ and that the group recognises it. Let $p\in(0,1)$ denote the probability that an individual agent independently produces the correct answer. For independent agents and an ideal verifier, the probability of collective success is $P_{\text{disj}}(N)=1-(1-p)^{N}$ , so the collective failure probability decays exponentially with team size $N$ : $P(\text{failure})=(1-p)^{N}\to 0$ when $N\to\infty$ . We refer to this, estimated empirically, as the proportion of items on which at least one agent is correct, as pass@ $N$ .
在 Steiner 的经典析取框架（Steiner, 1972）中，如果群体成功仅要求至少一名团队成员生成正确解 $y^{*}\in\mathcal{Y}$，且群体能够识别该解的正确性，那么该任务就是析取型任务。令 $p\in(0,1)$ 表示单个智能体独立生成正确答案的概率。对于相互独立的智能体和理想验证器，集体成功的概率为 $P_{\text{disj}}(N)=1-(1-p)^{N}$，因此集体失败的概率随团队规模 $N$ 呈指数衰减：当 $N\to\infty$ 时，$P(\text{failure})=(1-p)^{N}\to 0$。我们将通过经验估计得到的、至少有一个智能体回答正确的题目比例称为 pass@ $N$。

在没有预言机验证器的情况下，多智能体框架通过多数投票或相对多数投票来聚合离散的候选输出 (Wang et al., 2023; Du et al., 2024)。对于备选解之间的二元决策，设每个智能体独立投票，其支持正确解 $y^{*}$ 的投票为 $V_{i}\sim\text{Bernoulli}(p)$，其中 $p>\frac{1}{2}$。多数投票决策 $\hat{y}_{\text{maj}}=\sum_{i=1}^{n}V_{i}>\frac{n}{2}$ 成功的概率为 $\sum_{k>N/2}\binom{N}{k}p^{k}(1-p)^{N-k}$。根据 Hoeffding 不等式，对于满足 $a_{i}=0,b_{i}=1$ 且偏差为 $t=p-\frac{1}{2}$ 的伯努利随机变量，多数投票出错的概率具有如下严格上界：

2.3 Modeling Compensatory Tasks through Geometric Aggregation2.3 通过几何聚合对补偿型任务建模
Next, we contrast the disjunctive voting paradigm with the classic statistical model of group judgment on compensatory tasks (Galton, 1907; Surowiecki, 2004; Steiner, 1972). 2.3.1 The Statistical Principle of Team Superiority2.3.1 团队优越性的统计学原理
To show why an averaged team judgment is expected to be more accurate than a typical individual (Gigone and Hastie, 1997; Armstrong, 2001), we analyse the expected value and the variance of the collective error $\bar{e}_{N}$ . In modelling human team performance, the theory relies on i) unbiased estimates where on average, individual human errors balance out to zero across the crowd: $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}]=0\quad\text{for all }i\in\{1,\dots,N\}$ and ii) independence where team members do not influence one another, meaning individual errors are statistically uncorrelated: $\text{Cov}(e_{i},e_{j})=0\quad\text{for all }i\neq j$ . If each individual’s judgment error has a variance of $\text{Var}(e_{i})=\sigma^{2}$ , the variance of the team’s collective estimate is calculated as: $\text{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=\frac{1}{N^{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\text{Var}(e_{i})=\sigma^{2}/N$ , so that the standard error of the team’s judgment shrinks at the rate $\text{SE}(\bar{e}_{N})=\sigma/\sqrt{N}$ . As team size $N$ grows, the variance approaches zero ( $\lim_{N\to\infty}\text{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=0$ ). This shows that the averaged team judgment will systematically be more accurate and consistent than any single individual judgment (Clemen, 1989; Page, 2007).
为说明为何团队判断的平均值预期比典型个体的判断更准确 (Gigone and Hastie, 1997; Armstrong, 2001)，我们分析集体误差 $\bar{e}_{N}$ 的期望值和方差。在对人类团队表现建模时，该理论依赖于以下条件：i) 估计无偏，即从平均意义上看，群体中各个个体的误差相互抵消为零：$\mathbb{E}[e_{i}]=0\quad\text{for all }i\in\{1,\dots,N\}$；ii) 相互独立，即团队成员之间互不影响，这意味着个体误差在统计上不相关：$\text{Cov}(e_{i},e_{j})=0\quad\text{for all }i\neq j$。如果每个个体的判断误差方差为 $\text{Var}(e_{i})=\sigma^{2}$，则团队集体估计的方差计算为：$\text{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=\frac{1}{N^{2}}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\text{Var}(e_{i})=\sigma^{2}/N$，因此团队判断的标准误按 $\text{SE}(\bar{e}_{N})=\sigma/\sqrt{N}$ 的速率缩小。随着团队规模 $N$ 增大，方差趋近于零（$\lim_{N\to\infty}\text{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=0$）。这表明，团队判断的平均值将系统性地比任何单个个体的判断更准确、更稳定 (Clemen, 1989; Page, 2007)。
2.4 Effective Team Capacity and Scaling Limits Across Task Types2.4 不同任务类型的有效团队容量与规模扩展极限
Comparing the mathematical foundations of the ideal disjunctive model (Section 2.2) and the ideal compensatory model (Section 2.3) reveals the theoretical origin of the scaling dichotomy: the failure probability of independent agents decreases exponentially in disjunctive tasks while it drops polynomially in compensatory tasks. More details of what behavior is expected from correlated and biased agents on disjunctive and compensatory task types are available in Appendix B.1. For correlated draws (i.e. answers sampled from a language model), the Kish design effect (Kish, 1965) provides the effective team capacity $N_{\text{eff}}$ defined as the size of an ideal, independent ensemble ( $\rho=0$ ) that achieves the equivalent variance reduction as a correlated ensemble of size $N$ : $N_{\text{eff}}(N)=N/(1+(N-1)\rho)$ . For any positive pairwise error correlation $\rho>0$ : $\lim_{n\to\infty}N_{\text{eff}}(N)=1/\rho$ .
比较理想析取模型（第 2.2 节）与理想补偿模型（第 2.3 节）的数学基础，可以揭示规模扩展二分性的理论根源：在析取型任务中，独立智能体的失败概率呈指数下降，而在补偿型任务中则呈多项式下降。关于存在相关性和偏差的智能体在析取型与补偿型任务中预期表现的更多细节，见附录 B.1。对于相关抽样（即从语言模型中采样得到的答案），Kish 设计效应 (Kish, 1965) 给出了有效团队容量 $N_{\text{eff}}$，其定义为：与规模为 $N$ 的相关集成实现相同方差缩减的理想独立集成（$\rho=0$）的规模，即 $N_{\text{eff}}(N)=N/(1+(N-1)\rho)$。对于任意正的成对误差相关性 $\rho>0$，有：$\lim_{n\to\infty}N_{\text{eff}}(N)=1/\rho$。
An important consequence arises when agents are independent samples from the same model, as in our experiments. Conditional on the item $k$ , the outputs of such agents are independent and identically distributed with an item-specific distribution $P_{k}$ . The pairwise correlation of their correctness across items then equals $\rho=\mathrm{Var}_{k}(p_{k})/(\bar{p}(1-\bar{p}))$ , where $p_{k}$ is the single-agent success probability on item $k$ and $\bar{p}$ its mean (Boland, 1989; Ladha, 1992); that is, $\rho$ measures heterogeneity of item difficulty rather than interaction between agents. Such a team can be seen as a “crowd within” a single model, whose repeated judgments are more correlated than those of different individuals (Vul and Pashler, 2008; Herzog and Hertwig, 2009), and correlated judges add little information (Hogarth, 1978).
当智能体是来自同一模型的独立样本时，就像我们的实验中那样，会产生一个重要结果。给定题目 $k$，这些智能体的输出相互独立，并且服从该题目特有的同一分布 $P_{k}$。此时，它们在不同题目上作答正确性的成对相关性等于 $\rho=\mathrm{Var}_{k}(p_{k})/(\bar{p}(1-\bar{p}))$，其中 $p_{k}$ 是单个智能体在题目 $k$ 上的成功概率，$\bar{p}$ 是其均值 (Boland, 1989; Ladha, 1992)；也就是说，$\rho$ 衡量的是题目难度的异质性，而非智能体之间的交互。这样的团队可以被视为单个模型“内部的群体”，其重复判断之间的相关性高于不同个体的判断之间的相关性（Vul and Pashler, 2008; Herzog and Hertwig, 2009），而判断者之间存在相关性时，所增加的信息很少（Hogarth, 1978）。

On the remaining 385 items (open markers in Fig. 1; fermi-clean in Table 4), all levels are higher, but the pattern is unchanged: Round-1 accuracy rises only from $40.9\%$ for a single agent to $43.6\%$ at $N=30$ and the clipped log-error decreases from $1.52$ to $1.39$ at $N=5$ and $1.37$ at $N=30$ , whereas pass@30 reaches $70.8\%$ . 对于以费米问题为代表的补偿型（compensatory）问题，图 1 和表 4 表明，增大团队规模 $N$ 在任一轮中带来的改善都很小。在第 1 轮采用平均法时，误差在一个数量级以内的准确率从单智能体的 $31.6\%$ 上升至 $N=7$ 时的 $33.1\%$ 和 $N=30$ 时的 $33.4\%$，截断对数误差则从 $1.84$ 降至 $N=5$ 时的 $1.73$ 和 $N=30$ 时的 $1.71$。修订并无帮助：在不同团队规模下，第 3 轮准确率与第 1 轮准确率之差介于 $-0.6$ 至 $+0.7$ 个百分点之间。尽管如此，费米问题团队的潜力仍在增长：至少有一个智能体的估计误差在一个数量级以内的题目比例，从单智能体的 $31.6\%$ 上升至 $N=30$ 时的 $57.3\%$（第 2.3 节；附录表 4），但平均法无法选出这一估计值。连续值估计缺乏可验证的中间步骤，因此修订很少能纠正数量级错误。RealFP 包含不合理的参考值，例如将一个人的祖先数量设为 $2\times 10^{90}$；在 529 道题目中的 144 道（27.2%）上，十个模型的估计中位数与参考值相差超过三个数量级。在剩余的 385 道题目上（图 1 中的空心标记；表 4 中的 fermi-clean），各项表现水平都有所提高，但整体模式不变：第 1 轮准确率仅从单智能体的 $40.9\%$ 上升至 $N=30$ 时的 $43.6\%$，截断对数误差从 $1.52$ 降至 $N=5$ 时的 $1.39$ 和 $N=30$ 时的 $1.37$，而 pass@30 则达到 $70.8\%$。因此，费米问题团队的潜在表现与实际表现之间的差距并非源于错误标签。
3.3 The Anatomy of the Disjunctive Ceiling3.3 析取型任务性能上限的剖析
While Section 3.2 showed that disjunctive tasks benefit substantially from multi-agent deliberation compared to compensatory tasks, a critical question remains: does disjunctive performance scale monotonically as team size grows? Figure 1 shows that it does not: rather than approaching the pass@ $N$ curve, realised disjunctive accuracy exhibits its own ceiling. In this section, we analyse the empirical evidence and the mechanisms governing this ceiling.
虽然第 3.2 节表明，与补偿型任务相比，析取型任务从多智能体讨论中获益显著，但一个关键问题仍然存在：析取型任务的表现是否会随着团队规模增大而单调提升？图 1 表明并非如此：实际的析取型任务准确率并未趋近 pass@ $N$ 曲线，而是呈现出自身的上限。在本节中，我们分析相关实证证据，以及决定这一上限的机制。
The Early Saturation of Independent Ensembles (Round 1) Round-1 plurality voting yields almost no improvement with team size: from $N=2$ to $N=30$ , accuracy changes by $+0.3$ points on GSM8K (95% CI $[0.0,0.5]$ ), $+1.0$ on GSM-Hard, $+1.3$ on MATH-500, $+0.8$ on MMLU-Hard, and $+0.8$ on ARC-Challenge. As Proposition 2.1 predicts, plurality voting converges to the model’s modal answer on each item. We estimated the item-level answer distributions from the Round-1 answers of the $N=30$ teams (up to 90 samples per item) and predicted plurality accuracy at every team size by resampling. Across 650 model–task–team-size configurations, predicted and observed accuracies differ by 0.48 points on average (median 0.18; Pearson $r=0.999$ ; Appendix Figure 2). The intra-class correlation of correctness ranges from 0.53 to 0.97 (mean 0.78), and the predicted large-team limit exceeds single-agent accuracy by only 0.4–1.2 points per task. Consistent with Eq. 3, the gain remains within the bound $2\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})(1-\rho)$ for all 65 model–task pairs and uses on average only 11–21% of it (Appendix Figure 3). The two models that reason before answering have a lower $\rho$ ( $0.66$ against $0.81$ ), a larger predicted voting gain ( $+3.4$ against $+0.2$ points), and observed gains of $+7.2$ and $+2.8$ points from one to 30 agents, against $-1.0$ to $+1.0$ for the others, consistent with Eq. 3 and with the success of self-consistency under chain-of-thought prompting (Wang et al., 2023); the model predicts both regimes (mean error 0.19 and 0.43 points, excluding MATH-500). A higher temperature is no substitute: for qwen2.5-7b, raising $T$ from 0.2 to 1.0 lowers $\rho$ and raises pass@30 but leaves plurality accuracy unchanged, because the extra samples spread over incorrect answers (Appendix Table 5). Additional queries cannot produce a correct plurality on items whose modal answer is wrong; the difference between pass@30 and plurality accuracy at $N=30$ (4.8–20.1 points) is the process loss of plurality voting.
独立集成的早期饱和（第 1 轮） 第 1 轮的相对多数投票几乎未随团队规模增大而带来改善：从 $N=2$ 到 $N=30$，GSM8K 上的准确率变化为 $+0.3$ 个百分点（95% 置信区间为 $[0.0,0.5]$），GSM-Hard 上为 $+1.0$ 个百分点，MATH-500 上为 $+1.3$ 个百分点，MMLU-Hard 上为 $+0.8$ 个百分点，ARC-Challenge 上为 $+0.8$ 个百分点。正如命题 2.1 所预测的，相对多数投票会收敛于模型在每道题目上的众数答案。我们利用 $N=30$ 的团队在第 1 轮给出的答案（每道题目最多 90 个样本）估计题目层面的答案分布，并通过重采样预测各个团队规模下的相对多数投票准确率。在 650 种模型–任务–团队规模配置中，预测准确率与观测准确率平均相差 0.48 个百分点（中位数为 0.18；Pearson $r=0.999$；附录图 2）。正确性的组内相关系数介于 0.53 至 0.97 之间（均值为 0.78），且预测的大团队极限准确率在每项任务上仅比单智能体准确率高 0.4–1.2 个百分点。与公式 3 一致，全部 65 个模型–任务配对的增益均未超过界限 $2\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})(1-\rho)$，平均仅达到该界限的 11–21%（附录图 3）。两个先推理后回答的模型具有更低的 $\rho$（$0.66$，其他模型为 $0.81$）、更大的预测投票增益（$+3.4$ 个百分点，其他模型为 $+0.2$ 个百分点），而且从一个智能体增加到 30 个智能体时，观测到的增益分别为 $+7.2$ 和 $+2.8$ 个百分点，其他模型则为 $-1.0$ 至 $+1.0$ 个百分点，这与公式 3 以及思维链提示下自一致性方法的成功相符（Wang et al., 2023）；该模型能够预测这两种情形（排除 MATH-500 后，平均误差分别为 0.19 和 0.43 个百分点）。提高温度也无法替代这一点：对于 qwen2.5-7b，将 $T$ 从 0.2 提高到 1.0 会降低 $\rho$ 并提高 pass@30，但相对多数投票准确率保持不变，因为额外样本分散到了错误答案上（附录表 5）。对于众数答案错误的题目，增加查询次数无法使正确答案获得相对多数；在 $N=30$ 时，pass@30 与相对多数投票准确率之间的差距（4.8–20.1 个百分点）就是相对多数投票的过程损失。
The Peak-and-Decay Regime in Deliberated Teams (Round 3) Multi-turn communication nearly doubles accuracy on gsm8k ( $34.1\%\to 62.0\%$ at the peak) and gsmhard ( $18.0\%\to 33.5\%$ ). Deliberated teams do not, however, continue to improve with size (Appendix Table 6): accuracy peaks at an intermediate team size and declines by $0.4$ – $1.1$ points towards $N=30$ on the mathematical and knowledge tasks, most clearly on gsm8k ( $-1.0$ points relative to $N=5$ , 95% CI $[-1.4,-0.7]$ ), and is flat on arc. The largest declines occur on multi-step symbolic tasks, consistent with an incorrect intermediate result shared by several peers acting as an attractor during revision.

Section 3.2 showed that compensatory tasks benefit little from multi-agent scaling or deliberation. Table 3 provides a per-model breakdown. For homogeneous models, the error reduction from a single agent to a team of five ranges from $0.9\%$ to $8.1\%$ , with one exception (marin-8b, $15.4\%$ ), and increasing the team to $N=30$ yields little further reduction. The intra-class correlation of signed log-errors lies between 0.70 and 0.95, and effective team capacity $N_{\text{eff}}(5)$ lies between $1.04$ and $1.32$ : five agents provide the variance reduction of little more than one. Averaged over models, item-level bias accounts for $\beta=0.87$ of the mean squared log-error (between-item standard deviation of $b_{k}$ : 1.96 orders of magnitude; within-item standard deviation: 0.75), so that even an infinite homogeneous team could remove at most about 13% of it. Eq. 4 predicts the observed error of the geometric mean at every team size to within 0.6% on average for all ten models (Pearson $r=0.997$ ; Appendix Figure 4). Only the model with the most dispersed samples (marin-8b) and heterogeneous multi-family teams depart noticeably from the ceiling: the 7B–8B pool reduces the error of its average member by $25.8\%$ (95% CI [23.1, 28.7]) and, with $1.45$ , is also more accurate than its best member alone ( $1.80$ ). Empirical Analysis of Unbiasedness The classical model in Section 2.3 assumes that errors balance out to zero on average ( $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}]=0$ ). To test this condition, we measure the distribution of signed log-errors $e_{i}=\log_{10}(x_{i}/g)$ in Appendix Table 9. Models such as mistral-7b ( $b=+0.71$ ), qwen2.5-3b ( $b=+0.65$ ), and llama3.1-8b ( $b=+0.62$ ) overestimate by more than half an order of magnitude on average, and 61–64% of their estimates exceed the ground truth. Conversely, marin-8b ( $b=-0.20$ , 95% CI $[-0.38,-0.03]$ ) significantly underestimates. Even for the models whose global mean is not significantly different from zero (qwen2.5-7b, $b=+0.11$ , $[-0.05,0.28]$ , and qwen3-8b, $b=-0.04$ , $[-0.20,0.13]$ ), the unbiasedness condition fails at the level of individual items. For a given prompt $k$ , independent samples from the same model cluster around the model’s item-specific estimate, so that $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}\mid\text{item }k]=b_{k}\neq 0$ . The root-mean-square item-level bias, averaged across models, is $2.01$ orders of magnitude (with clipping at $\pm 3$ ). Intra-model averaging reduces sampling variation around $b_{k}$ but leaves the systematic error $b_{k}$ untouched (Appendix C.1 shows an example).
无偏性的实证分析 第 2.3 节中的经典模型假设误差平均而言相互抵消为零（$\mathbb{E}[e_{i}]=0$）。为检验这一条件，我们在附录表 9 中测量了带符号对数误差 $e_{i}=\log_{10}(x_{i}/g)$ 的分布。mistral-7b（$b=+0.71$）、qwen2.5-3b（$b=+0.65$）和 llama3.1-8b（$b=+0.62$）等模型平均高估超过半个数量级，且其估计值中有 61–64% 超过真实值。相反，marin-8b（$b=-0.20$，95% CI $[-0.38,-0.03]$）存在显著低估。即使对于总体均值与零无显著差异的模型（qwen2.5-7b，$b=+0.11$，$[-0.05,0.28]$，以及 qwen3-8b，$b=-0.04$，$[-0.20,0.13]$），无偏性条件在单个题目层面也不成立。对于给定提示 $k$，同一模型的独立样本聚集在该模型针对该题目的估计值周围，因此 $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}\mid\text{item }k]=b_{k}\neq 0$。各模型的题目级偏差均方根的平均值为 $2.01$ 个数量级（截断于 $\pm 3$）。模型内平均能够减少围绕 $b_{k}$ 的采样波动，但不会改变系统误差 $b_{k}$（附录 C.1 给出了一个例子）。
Cross-Model Error Correlation and Shared Directional Misconceptions. If individual models have non-zero item-level biases, can a multi-model ensemble cancel these errors? That is, does $\sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathbb{E}[e^{(m)}\mid\text{item }k]\approx 0$ ? To answer this question, we compute the pairwise correlation of item-level biases across models (Appendix Table 10). The mean pairwise correlation is $r=0.63$ (95% CI [0.60, 0.67]; standard deviation across model pairs 0.06), ranging from $r=0.46$ between mistral-7b and qwen3-8b to $r=0.77$ between qwen3-8b and qwen3-14b. On $31.5\%$ of all items, every model errs in the same direction. Because these correlations are well below one, averaging across model families can cancel part of the item-level bias, which is consistent with the gains of the heterogeneous pool in Table 3. Part of this alignment is due to erroneous reference labels: on the 385 label-clean items (Section 3.2), the mean correlation falls to $r=0.41$ and all models err in the same direction on $19.1\%$ of items, whereas the bias share remains high ( $\beta=0.82$ ).
跨模型误差相关性与共同的方向性误解。如果各个模型都存在非零的题目级偏差，多模型集成能否抵消这些误差？也就是说，是否有 $\sum_{m=1}^{M}\mathbb{E}[e^{(m)}\mid\text{item }k]\approx 0$？为回答这一问题，我们计算了不同模型之间题目级偏差的两两相关系数（附录表 10）。平均两两相关系数为 $r=0.63$（95% CI [0.60, 0.67]；各模型对之间的标准差为 0.06），最低为 mistral-7b 与 qwen3-8b 之间的 $r=0.46$，最高为 qwen3-8b 与 qwen3-14b 之间的 $r=0.77$。在全部题目的 $31.5\%$ 上，所有模型都朝同一方向出错。由于这些相关系数远低于一，跨模型家族取平均能够抵消部分题目级偏差，这与表 3 中异质模型池的性能提升一致。这种一致性部分源于错误的参考标签：在 385 道标签无误的题目上（第 3.2 节），平均相关系数降至 $r=0.41$，所有模型朝同一方向出错的题目占 $19.1\%$，而偏差占比仍然很高（$\beta=0.82$）。
4 Discussion4 讨论
Implications. On disjunctive tasks (Section 3.3), the difference between pass@ $N$ and plurality accuracy identifies the process loss that verifiers, reward models, or structured deliberation must recover; reasoning before answering or combining model families can raise the voting ceiling, by an amount that Eq. 3 bounds. On compensatory tasks (Section 3.4), reducing the item-level bias requires changing the estimates themselves, for instance by combining models whose biases are not aligned, rather than adding samples; prompting interventions and a fine-tuned model are evaluated in Appendix F.
启示。在析取型任务中（第 3.3 节），pass@ $N$ 与相对多数投票准确率之差揭示了验证器、奖励模型或结构化审议必须弥补的过程损失；先推理再作答或组合不同模型家族可以提高投票上限，其提升幅度受式 3 约束。在补偿型任务中（第 3.4 节），降低题目级偏差需要改变估计值本身，例如组合偏差方向不一致的模型，而非增加样本；附录 F 评估了提示干预和一个经过微调的模型。
Limitations. First, the prompts request the answer before the rationale, which lowers absolute accuracy and shapes the deliberation gain; chain-of-thought prompting was not evaluated for the answer-first models, so the size of the voting ceiling is specific to this regime, whereas its mechanism is not. Second, there is no revision condition without peers. Third, the Fermi results rest on one benchmark with substantial label noise, generated with two prompt families (Appendix C). Fourth, all models are open-weight with at most 20B parameters, and additive, conjunctive, and discretionary tasks remain to be studied.
局限性。首先，提示要求先给出答案再给出理由，这降低了绝对准确率，并影响了审议带来的增益；我们未对先作答的模型评估思维链提示，因此投票上限的大小仅适用于这一设置，但其机制并不局限于此。其次，未设置没有同伴参与的修订条件。第三，费米估计结果基于一个存在大量标签噪声的基准，且使用两类提示生成（附录 C）。第四，所有模型均为开放权重模型，参数量最多为 20B，而加和型、合取型和裁量型任务仍有待研究。

Disjunctive Tasks析取型任务
in which the outcome is established through $Y=\max_{i}\{y_{i}\}$ or plurality/majority/verifier selection and/or debate. The tasks are unitary and optimizing and group success requires that at least one agent discovers a valid solution, and the group then recognizes it as such. In recent literature, example benchmarks falling under this task type are: i) multi-step mathematical reasoning such as MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021b; Du et al., 2024) and GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021), ii) challenging multiple-choice knowledge suites such as MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024) and GPQA Diamond (Rein et al., 2024), iii) school science answering in ARC-Challenge (Clark et al., 2018), and iv) functional code generation in BigCodeBench (Zhuo et al., 2025). Multi-agent LLM systems operationalize this task structure via plurality voting (Wang et al., 2023), verifier re-ranking, and multi-agent debate (Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025), in some studies reporting success while in others showing diminishing benefits with scale.
此类任务通过 $Y=\max_{i}\{y_{i}\}$，或通过相对多数投票／绝对多数投票／验证器选择和／或辩论来确定结果。这些任务具有不可分割性和择优性，群体成功要求至少有一个智能体找到有效解，并且群体随后能够识别出该解有效。在近期文献中，属于这一任务类型的基准示例包括：i) 多步数学推理，如 MATH-500 (Hendrycks et al., 2021b; Du et al., 2024) 和 GSM8K (Cobbe et al., 2021)；ii) 具有挑战性的多项选择知识测试集，如 MMLU-Pro (Wang et al., 2024) 和 GPQA Diamond (Rein et al., 2024)；iii) ARC-Challenge (Clark et al., 2018) 中的学校科学问答；以及 iv) BigCodeBench (Zhuo et al., 2025) 中的功能性代码生成。多智能体 LLM 系统通过相对多数投票 (Wang et al., 2023)、验证器重排序和多智能体辩论 (Du et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025) 来实现这一任务结构，一些研究报告了成功，而另一些研究则显示收益随规模扩大而递减。
Compensatory Tasks补偿型任务
in which the outcome is determined by $Y=\text{Aggr}(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})$ via statistical aggregation (geometric mean, arithmetic mean, or median) of continuous numerical estimates ( $x_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{+}$ ) or calibrated probabilities ( $p_{i}\in[0,1]$ ). In recent AI research, this type is exemplified by post-cutoff future event forecasting in ForecastBench (Karger et al., 2025), numerical guesstimation of world-model properties in Guesstimate (Chuang et al., 2025), multi-scale physical and dimensional estimation in RealFP and SynthFP (Kalyan et al., 2021), and competitive Science Olympiad estimation in SciOly. LLMs typically perform worse on such tasks types compared to disjunctive types Liu et al. (2025); Epstein et al. (2025).
此类任务通过对连续数值估计（$x_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{+}$）或经过校准的概率（$p_{i}\in[0,1]$）进行统计聚合（几何平均、算术平均或中位数），按 $Y=\text{Aggr}(x_{1},\dots,x_{n})$ 确定结果。在近期 AI 研究中，此类任务的示例包括 ForecastBench (Karger et al., 2025) 中对知识截止日期之后的未来事件进行预测、Guesstimate (Chuang et al., 2025) 中对世界模型属性进行数值概估、RealFP 和 SynthFP (Kalyan et al., 2021) 中的多尺度物理与量纲估算，以及 SciOly 中的科学奥林匹克竞赛估算。与析取型任务相比，LLM 在此类任务上的表现通常更差 Liu et al. (2025); Epstein et al. (2025)。
Additive Tasks加和型任务
in which the outcome is established through $Y=\sum_{i}y_{i}$ or $Y=\bigcup_{i}S_{i}$ via cumulative sum, total coverage, or deduplicated set union of independent member efforts. These tasks are divisible and maximizing by nature. This type represents the classical study by Ingham et al. (1974) and Latané et al. (1979), where social loafing and coordination decay originally manifested. And this is where the strongest member determines the overall performance of the team. Existing benchmarks that may fall under this category include open-ended creative brainstorming and hypothesis generation (Olson et al., 2021), divergent semantic exploration in the Divergent Association Task (DAT), and parallel synthetic instruction generation.
此类任务通过对成员独立努力的成果进行累加、计算总覆盖范围或求取去重后的集合并集，按 $Y=\sum_{i}y_{i}$ 或 $Y=\bigcup_{i}S_{i}$ 确定结果。这些任务本质上具有可分割性，并以最大化产出为目标。这一类型对应于 Ingham et al. (1974) 和 Latané et al. (1979) 的经典研究，社会惰化与协调衰退现象最初便在这些研究中显现。而在此类任务中，能力最强的成员决定团队的整体表现。可能属于这一类别的现有基准包括开放式创意头脑风暴与假设生成 (Olson et al., 2021)、发散联想任务（Divergent Association Task，DAT）中的发散性语义探索，以及并行合成指令生成。
Conjunctive Tasks合取型任务
in which the outcome is established through $Y=\min_{i}\{y_{i}\}$ via weakest-link coupling). Group success requires that all agents or subtasks succeed without failure; an error at any intermediate stage can invalidate the entire collective output. Looking at real-world repository-level software engineering in SWE-bench and SWE-bench Verified (Jimenez et al., 2024), end-to-end multi-agent enterprise workflows in TheAgentCompany (Xu et al., 2026), interactive multi-turn operating system manipulation in OSWorld (Xie et al., 2024), and sequential tool-calling environments in WorkBench and BrowseComp-Plus (Kim et al., 2026), we notice that they can be solved as a sequence of sub-tasks where a failure in any intermediate step causes zero task completion.
此类任务通过最弱环节耦合，按 $Y=\min_{i}\{y_{i}\}$ 确定结果）。群体成功要求所有智能体或子任务均成功且不发生失败；任何中间阶段的错误都可能使整个集体输出失效。考察 SWE-bench 和 SWE-bench Verified (Jimenez et al., 2024) 中真实世界的代码仓库级软件工程、TheAgentCompany (Xu et al., 2026) 中端到端的多智能体企业工作流、OSWorld (Xie et al., 2024) 中交互式多轮操作系统操控，以及 WorkBench 和 BrowseComp-Plus (Kim et al., 2026) 中的序贯工具调用环境，我们注意到，这些任务都可以通过一系列子任务来解决，而任何中间步骤的失败都会导致任务完成度为零。
Discretionary Tasks自主裁量型任务（Discretionary Tasks）
in which the outcome is established through $Y=f_{\text{group}}(\{y_{i}\})$ via group discretion. In Steiner’s taxonomy, discretionary tasks grant the group complete autonomy to select its own combination rule; the task constraints themselves do not mandate how member contributions combine. Modern multi-agent meta-orchestration frameworks (e.g., router-judge architectures, Mixture-of-Agents (Wang et al., 2025), ChatEval (Chan et al., 2024), and AgentBench (Liu et al., 2024)) where a central orchestrator dynamically decides whether to average, vote, deliberate, or delegate to specialized tool-calling agents may correspond to this category.
在这类任务中，结果由群体自主裁量，通过 $Y=f_{\text{group}}(\{y_{i}\})$ 确定。在 Steiner 的分类体系中，自主裁量型任务赋予群体完全的自主权，使其能够自行选择组合规则；任务约束本身并不规定成员贡献应如何组合。现代多智能体元编排框架（例如，路由器—评判器架构、Mixture-of-Agents (Wang et al., 2025)、ChatEval (Chan et al., 2024) 和 AgentBench (Liu et al., 2024)）可能属于这一类别，其中，中央编排器动态决定是进行平均、投票、审议，还是将任务委派给专门的工具调用智能体。

$$P(\text{Disjunctive Error})\leq\exp\left(-2N\left(p-\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}\right)=\mathcal{O}(e^{-\alpha N}).$$
Compensatory Tasks (Statistical Aggregation): Estimates $x_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{+}$ and signed errors $e_{i}=\log_{10}(x_{i}/g)\in\mathbb{R}$ are continuous, and the collective relies on statistical cancellation. The standard error decays as
补偿型任务（Compensatory Tasks，统计聚合）：估计值 $x_{i}\in\mathbb{R}^{+}$ 和有符号误差 $e_{i}=\log_{10}(x_{i}/g)\in\mathbb{R}$ 是连续的，集体依赖于误差在统计上的相互抵消。标准误按如下形式衰减
$$\text{SE}(\bar{e}_{N})=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{N}}=\mathcal{O}(N^{-1/2}),$$
and, for sub-Gaussian errors, the probability that $|\bar{e}_{N}|$ exceeds a fixed tolerance $\epsilon$ also decays exponentially, as $\exp(-N\epsilon^{2}/(2\sigma^{2}))$ .
而且，对于次高斯误差，$|\bar{e}_{N}|$ 超过固定容差 $\epsilon$ 的概率也呈指数衰减，其形式为 $\exp(-N\epsilon^{2}/(2\sigma^{2}))$。
Under ideal independence and unbiasedness, both task types therefore enjoy exponential suppression of the probability of a large error. The difference between them lies in how they respond to correlation and bias, which we analyse next.
因此，在理想的独立性和无偏性条件下，这两类任务中出现大误差的概率均受到指数级抑制。两者的差异在于其对相关性和偏差的响应方式，我们将在下文对此进行分析。
B.2 Correlated and Biased LLM Ensembles: Breakdown Across Task TypesB.2 存在相关性和偏差的 LLM 集成：不同任务类型中的失效
The classical models in Sections 2.2 and 2.3 assume independent and unbiased members. Ensembles of samples from the same LLM violate both assumptions at the level of individual items, and the consequences differ across Steiner’s task classes.
第 2.2 节和第 2.3 节中的经典模型假设成员相互独立且无偏。由同一 LLM 的采样结果构成的集成在单个题目层面上违背了这两项假设，而其后果因 Steiner 的任务类别而异。
B.2.1 Disjunctive Tasks Under Correlation and BiasB.2.1 存在相关性和偏差时的析取型任务
In discrete reasoning tasks (e.g., GSM8K, MATH-500, MMLU), agents exhibit positive pairwise correlation $\rho>0$ :
在离散推理任务（例如 GSM8K、MATH-500、MMLU）中，智能体之间表现出正的两两相关性 $\rho>0$：
Correlated Condorcet Voting: When individual votes are exchangeable with pairwise correlation $\rho>0$ , the variance of the collective vote count is inflated by the factor $1+(N-1)\rho$ (Boland, 1989; Ladha, 1992), which corresponds to the effective size $N_{\text{eff}}(N)=N/(1+(N-1)\rho)$ . For independent samples from one model, this correlation arises from heterogeneous item difficulty, and plurality accuracy converges to the fraction of items on which the correct answer is modal (Proposition 2.1).
相关的 Condorcet 投票：当个体投票具有可交换性且两两相关性为 $\rho>0$ 时，集体票数的方差会膨胀至原来的 $1+(N-1)\rho$ 倍 (Boland, 1989; Ladha, 1992)，这对应于有效规模 $N_{\text{eff}}(N)=N/(1+(N-1)\rho)$。对于来自同一模型的独立样本，这种相关性源于题目难度的异质性，而相对多数投票的准确率收敛于正确答案为众数的题目所占比例（命题 2.1）。
Error Dispersion in Generative Search: In open-ended reasoning, the space of incorrect derivations is large. When incorrect answers are dispersed across many values while correct derivations converge on $y^{*}$ , the correct answer can remain the plurality even for $p_{k}<\frac{1}{2}$ , which allows voting to succeed despite substantial correlation. When a model concentrates its probability on a single incorrect answer, however, additional samples reinforce that answer; our measurements show that this concentration is common, with intra-class correlations between 0.53 and 0.97.
生成式搜索中的错误分散：在开放式推理中，错误推导的空间很大。当错误答案分散在许多取值上，而正确推导收敛于 $y^{*}$ 时，即使 $p_{k}<\frac{1}{2}$，正确答案仍可能获得相对多数，这使得投票即便在存在显著相关性的情况下也能成功。然而，当模型将概率集中于某个错误答案时，增加样本会强化该答案；我们的测量表明，这种集中现象很常见，组内相关系数介于 0.53 和 0.97 之间。
Verifiable Truth Propagation in Deliberation: Mathematical reasoning steps can be checked, that is, correct answers are demonstrable (Laughlin and Ellis, 1986). When agents exchange rationales, an agent with a correct step can present an argument that its peers are able to verify.
审议中可验证真理的传播：数学推理步骤可以被检验，也就是说，正确答案是可以论证的 (Laughlin and Ellis, 1986)。当智能体交换推理依据时，掌握正确步骤的智能体可以提出其他智能体能够验证的论证。
B.2.2 Compensatory Tasks Under Correlation and BiasB.2.2 存在相关性和偏差时的补偿型任务
Continuous numerical estimation (e.g., Fermi estimation in RealFP) offers fewer structural defences against correlated errors:
连续数值估计（例如 RealFP 中的费米估计）抵御相关误差的结构性机制较少：
Violation of Unbiasedness ( $b\neq 0$ ): LLMs internalise miscalibrated priors about physical, demographic, and geographical quantities, producing item-level biases $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}\mid k]=b_{k}\neq 0$ . Because geometric averaging is linear in log space, the expected team error is invariant to ensemble size:
无偏性的违反（$b\neq 0$）：LLM 内化了关于物理、人口和地理量的校准不当的先验，从而产生题目层面的偏差 $\mathbb{E}[e_{i}\mid k]=b_{k}\neq 0$。由于几何平均在对数空间中是线性的，团队的期望误差不随集成规模变化：
$$\mathbb{E}[\bar{e}_{N}\mid k]=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{E}[e_{i}\mid k]=b_{k}\implies\text{Bias}^{2}(\bar{e}_{N}\mid k)=b_{k}^{2}\quad\text{for all }N.$$
Averaging samples of one model cannot reduce its item-level bias.
对同一模型的样本取平均无法减小其在题目层面的偏差。
Violation of Independence ( $\rho>0$ ): With equicorrelated errors, $\text{Cov}(e_{i},e_{j})=\rho\sigma^{2}>0$ , and the variance of the average approaches a non-zero floor:
独立性假设的违反（$\rho>0$）：当误差等相关时，$\text{Cov}(e_{i},e_{j})=\rho\sigma^{2}>0$，均值的方差趋近于一个非零下限：
$$\text{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=\frac{\sigma^{2}}{N}+\frac{N-1}{N}\rho\sigma^{2}\xrightarrow[N\to\infty]{}\rho\sigma^{2}.$$
Empirically, the intra-class correlation of signed log-errors lies between 0.70 and 0.95 (Table 3).
实证结果表明，带符号对数误差的组内相关系数介于 0.70 与 0.95 之间（表 3）。
Limited Verifiability in Deliberation: Order-of-magnitude estimates lack verifiable intermediate steps in zero-shot dialogue. Consistent with this, revision leaves Fermi accuracy essentially unchanged (between $-0.6$ and $+0.7$ points).
推敲过程中的可验证性有限：在零样本对话中，数量级估算缺乏可验证的中间步骤。与此一致，修订后费米估算的准确率基本不变（变化介于 $-0.6$ 与 $+0.7$ 个百分点之间）。
B.3 ProofsB.3 证明
Oracle accuracy.预言机准确率。
Given item $k$ , the $N$ outputs are independent, so the probability that none is correct is $(1-p_{k})^{N}$ ; averaging over items and applying monotone convergence gives the limit $\Pr_{k}(p_{k}>0)$ .
给定题目 $k$，$N$ 个输出相互独立，因此没有一个输出正确的概率为 $(1-p_{k})^{N}$；对题目取平均并应用单调收敛定理，可得极限 $\Pr_{k}(p_{k}>0)$。
Plurality voting.相对多数投票。
This argument generalises Lemma 1 of Chen et al. (2024), whose difficulty indicator $d_{V}(x)$ equals $-\delta_{k}$ , to arbitrary answer sets. Fix item $k$ and a competing answer $y\neq y_{k}^{\ast}$ . The variables $D_{i}=\mathbb{1}[Y_{k,i}=y_{k}^{\ast}]-\mathbb{1}[Y_{k,i}=y]\in[-1,1]$ are independent with mean $\delta_{k,y}=p_{k}-P_{k}(y)$ . If $\delta_{k,y}>0$ , Hoeffding’s inequality gives $\Pr(\sum_{i}D_{i}\leq 0)\leq\exp(-N\delta_{k,y}^{2}/2)$ , and a union bound over competitors yields exponential convergence to success; if $\delta_{k,y}<0$ for some $y$ , the same argument shows that $y_{k}^{\ast}$ loses with probability tending to one. Averaging over items yields the limit $\pi$ .
这一论证将 Chen et al. (2024) 的引理 1 推广至任意答案集合，其中的难度指标 $d_{V}(x)$ 等于 $-\delta_{k}$。固定题目 $k$ 和一个竞争答案 $y\neq y_{k}^{\ast}$。变量 $D_{i}=\mathbb{1}[Y_{k,i}=y_{k}^{\ast}]-\mathbb{1}[Y_{k,i}=y]\in[-1,1]$ 相互独立，其均值为 $\delta_{k,y}=p_{k}-P_{k}(y)$。若 $\delta_{k,y}>0$，则由 Hoeffding 不等式可得 $\Pr(\sum_{i}D_{i}\leq 0)\leq\exp(-N\delta_{k,y}^{2}/2)$，再对所有竞争答案应用联合界，即可得到成功概率以指数速度收敛于 1；若存在某个 $y$ 使得 $\delta_{k,y}<0$，则同样的论证表明，$y_{k}^{\ast}$ 落败的概率趋近于 1。对题目取平均，可得极限 $\pi$。
Attainable voting gain.可实现的投票增益。
For binary outcomes, item $k$ is decided correctly in the limit if and only if $p_{k}>\frac{1}{2}$ , so $|\pi-\bar{p}|\leq\mathbb{E}_{k}[\min(p_{k},1-p_{k})]\leq 2\,\mathbb{E}_{k}[p_{k}(1-p_{k})]$ , since $\min(p,1-p)\leq 2p(1-p)$ on $[0,1]$ . Finally, $\mathbb{E}_{k}[p_{k}(1-p_{k})]=\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})-\mathrm{Var}_{k}(p_{k})=\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})(1-\rho)$ .
对于二元结果，当且仅当 $p_{k}>\frac{1}{2}$ 时，题目 $k$ 在极限下被正确判定，因此 $|\pi-\bar{p}|\leq\mathbb{E}_{k}[\min(p_{k},1-p_{k})]\leq 2\,\mathbb{E}_{k}[p_{k}(1-p_{k})]$，因为在 $[0,1]$ 上有 $\min(p,1-p)\leq 2p(1-p)$。最后，$\mathbb{E}_{k}[p_{k}(1-p_{k})]=\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})-\mathrm{Var}_{k}(p_{k})=\bar{p}(1-\bar{p})(1-\rho)$。
Compensatory decomposition.补偿性分解。
With $e_{k,i}=b_{k}+\varepsilon_{k,i}$ and conditionally independent $\varepsilon_{k,i}$ of variance $\sigma_{k}^{2}$ , $\mathbb{E}[\bar{e}_{k,N}^{2}\mid k]=b_{k}^{2}+\sigma_{k}^{2}/N$ . For equicorrelated errors with common bias $b$ , $\mathrm{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=\sigma^{2}(1+(N-1)\rho)/N=\sigma^{2}/N_{\text{eff}}(N)$ , which gives the stated MSE.
当 $e_{k,i}=b_{k}+\varepsilon_{k,i}$，且 $\varepsilon_{k,i}$ 条件独立、方差为 $\sigma_{k}^{2}$ 时，有 $\mathbb{E}[\bar{e}_{k,N}^{2}\mid k]=b_{k}^{2}+\sigma_{k}^{2}/N$。对于具有共同偏差 $b$ 的等相关误差，$\mathrm{Var}(\bar{e}_{N})=\sigma^{2}(1+(N-1)\rho)/N=\sigma^{2}/N_{\text{eff}}(N)$，由此可得上述均方误差（MSE）。

Evaluation protocol.评估方案。
Models are served with vLLM with nucleus parameter $0.95$ and at most 1,024 generated tokens; explicit thinking modes are disabled, and gpt-oss-20b uses low reasoning effort. Each configuration is run with three random seeds (two for qwen2.5-3b, one or two for r1-distill-qwen-14b) on the benchmark test sets: 1,319 items for GSM8K, 1,017 for GSM-Hard, 500 for MATH-500, 724 for MMLU-Hard, 1,165 for ARC-Challenge, and the 529 of 557 RealFP test items with a positive numerical answer. In Rounds 2 and 3, each agent receives its own previous response and the previous responses of all $N-1$ teammates, each truncated to 120 tokens (answer, confidence, and the beginning of the rationale). Plurality ties are broken at random. On disjunctive tasks, $\rho$ is the intra-class correlation of binary correctness indicators across items, macro-averaged over the five benchmarks, and $N_{\text{eff}}(N)=N/(1+(N-1)\rho)$ . On RealFP, the item-level bias $b_{k}$ is the mean signed log-error of a model on item $k$ , $\rho$ is the intra-class correlation of signed log-errors, and an estimate is counted as accurate if $|\bar{e}_{N}|\leq 1$ . Confidence intervals are percentile intervals from 1,000–2,000 bootstrap resamples of items, with items resampled jointly across models and differences computed on paired items.

Prompts.提示词。

Appendix F De-Saturating Compensatory LLMs: Interventions and Specialist Models附录 F 打破补偿型 LLM 的饱和：干预措施与专用模型
Having established that homogeneous LLM teams do not overcome the compensatory ceiling on their own, we explore structured interventions intended to decorrelate errors ( $\rho\to 0$ ) or to reduce systematic bias ( $b^{2}\to 0$ ).
在确认同质 LLM 团队无法自行突破补偿上限之后，我们探索了旨在降低误差相关性（$\rho\to 0$）或减少系统性偏差（$b^{2}\to 0$）的结构化干预措施。
F.1 The Heterogeneous Intervention BatteryF.1 异质团队干预方案组
We evaluate interventions on a heterogeneous team of five 7B–8B models (qwen2.5-7b, llama3.1-8b, mistral-7b, olmo2-7b, and marin-8b; one run, $T=0.4$ , 529 items):

Heterogeneous Baseline: the five models without intervention.
异质基线：不施加干预的五个模型。
Prefix Anchoring: each agent receives a different decomposition strategy (e.g., top-down, bottom-up, rate $\times$ time) as a prompt prefix.

结构化支架：每个智能体遵循不同的完整估计模板。

对数计算器卸载（logcalc）：智能体列出估计的各个因子，再在对数空间中根据这些因子计算答案。

单位制换基：智能体使用不同的单位制进行推理，并结合 logcalc。

常数参照：在提示中插入参考常数表，并结合 logcalc。

F.2 Evaluation of a Specialist ModelF.2 专用模型的评估
We also compared Ornith-1.5-9B, a publicly available model fine-tuned from Qwen3.5-9B with reinforcement learning for agentic coding, with its base model Qwen3.5-9B on RealFP and on a Science Olympiad estimation set (SciOly, 500 items), using Program-of-Thought (PoT) and decomposition-scaffold prompting. The control runs with direct and chain-of-thought prompting used a smaller token budget and were truncated on about half of the items, so we report only the paired comparison on items that both models answered with the same 16k-token budget.
