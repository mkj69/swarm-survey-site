摘要Abstract
本文探讨孔多塞陪审团定理在情感分析领域的应用，具体考察各种大语言模型（LLMs）相较于较简单的自然语言处理（NLP）模型的表现。 This paper explores the application of the Condorcet Jury theorem to the domain of sentiment analysis, specifically examining the performance of various large language models (LLMs) compared to simpler natural language processing (NLP) models. 该定理认为，只要各个分类器的决策相互独立，多数投票分类器就应能够提高预测准确率。 The theorem posits that a majority vote classifier should enhance predictive accuracy, provided that individual classifiers’ decisions are independent. 我们的实证研究通过在不同模型之间实施多数投票机制来检验这一理论框架，其中包括 ChatGPT 4 等先进的 LLMs。 Our empirical study tests this theoretical framework by implementing a majority vote mechanism across different models, including advanced LLMs such as ChatGPT 4. 与预期相反，结果表明，纳入更大的模型仅带来了微小的性能提升，这说明这些模型之间缺乏独立性。 Contrary to expectations, the results reveal only marginal improvements in performance when incorporating larger models, suggesting a lack of independence among them. 这一发现与以下假设一致：尽管 LLMs 结构复杂，但在情感分析中的推理任务上，其表现并未显著优于较简单的模型，这揭示了在高级 NLP 任务背景下模型独立性的实际局限。 This finding aligns with the hypothesis that despite their complexity, LLMs do not significantly outperform simpler models in reasoning tasks within sentiment analysis, showing the practical limits of model independence in the context of advanced NLP tasks.
1 引言1 Introduction
将自然语言处理（NLP）引入金融领域，对于从文本数据中提取洞见发挥了关键作用；随着大语言模型和生成式模型的发布，这一融合取得了显著发展（Arner et al., 2015; Fatouros et al., 2023）。 The integration of Natural Language Processing (NLP) into the financial sector has been pivotal in providing insights from textual data, evolving significantly with the release of Large Language models and generative models (Arner et al., 2015; Fatouros et al., 2023). 具体而言，NLP 使情感分析成为可能。 In particular, NLP has allowed to do sentiment analysis. “情感分析”这一术语强调，该方法旨在提取市场情绪或语气，进而通过分析提供具有预测价值的洞见（Tetlock, 2007; Schumaker and Chen, 2009）。 The terminology sentiments analysis emphasizes that the method aims at extracting market sentiments or tones and then at offering predictive insights thanks to its analysis (Tetlock, 2007; Schumaker and Chen, 2009).
与其他 NLP 应用相比，金融情感分析面临独特的挑战（Wankhade et al., 2022）。 Compared to other NLP applications, financial sentiment analysis faces unique challenges (Wankhade et al., 2022). 金融叙事往往涉及复杂的领域专用术语，并呈现出与不同实体相关的多种情感，这可能使通用情感分析工具失效（Loughran and McDonald, 2011; Poria et al., 2017; Sensoy et al., 2018）。 Financial narratives often involve complex, domain-specific terminologies and exhibit a multiplicity of sentiments tied to different entities, which can render general sentiment analysis tools ineffective (Loughran and McDonald, 2011; Poria et al., 2017; Sensoy et al., 2018). 金融新闻中的细微差别，以及将情感得分融入实际投资策略的困难，进一步加剧了这种复杂性；金融新闻可能只是对既有事件的反应，而非对未来的预测（Yuan et al., 2020; Iordache et al., 2022）。 The complexity is further exacerbated by the nuances of financial news, which may be reactive rather than predictive, and the difficulty of integrating sentiment scores into practical investment strategies (Yuan et al., 2020; Iordache et al., 2022).
以往，情感分析依赖简单的机器学习技术和预先定义的词表（Mejova, 2009）。 Historically, sentiments analysis relied on simple machine learning techniques and predefined word lists (Mejova, 2009). 后来，随着更复杂的 NLP 技术被引入，尤其是 BERT 和 FinBERT 等模型的应用，情感分析的准确率得到了显著提升（Devlin et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2021）。 Later on, with the introduction of more sophisticated NLP techniques, and in particular the deployment of models such as BERT and FinBERT, sentiment analysis has significantly refined its accuracy (Devlin et al., 2018; Liu et al., 2021). 随着 GPT 等大语言模型（LLMs）的出现，尤其是 ChatGPT 等专为对话应用设计的变体的出现，人工智能领域形成了一种新范式（Brown et al., 2020; Roumeliotis and Tselikas, 2023; OpenAI, 2023）。 With the emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT, and particularly its variants tailored for conversational applications such as ChatGPT, a new paradigm in AI has been set forth (Brown et al., 2020; Roumeliotis and Tselikas, 2023; OpenAI, 2023). 这些模型在理解和生成类人文本方面具有更强的能力，为改进金融情境下的情感分析带来了新的机遇。 These models offer enhanced capabilities in understanding and generating human-like text, presenting new opportunities to improve sentiment analysis within financial contexts.
然而，这些新模型与以往模型有何区别尚不明确，因此有必要在坚实的理论基础上对二者进行详细比较。 However, it is not clear how these new models differ from previous ones, motivating for a detailed and theoretically sound comparison with previous ones. 本文着重考察利用 LLMs 进行集成情感分析时的独立性，并运用孔多塞定理研究这些模型在金融应用中的局限与能力。 In this paper, we focus on examining the independence of ensemble sentiment analysis conducted through LLMs, utilizing the Condorcet Theorem to investigate the limits and capabilities of these models in financial applications. 研究旨在确定这些模型能够在多大程度上独立运作，以及在复杂情感任务背景下，其分析在多大程度上可以被认为是可靠且独特的。 The goal is to determine the extent to which these models can operate independently and to what degree their analysis can be considered reliable and unique in the context of complex sentiment tasks. 理解这种独立性至关重要，因为它直接影响模型在现实金融决策场景中的有效性。 Understanding this independence is critical, as it directly impacts the efficacy of models in real-world financial decision-making scenarios.

Srivastava et al. (2022) 利用孔多塞陪审团定理解释了为什么他们用于从放射影像中筛查 Covid-19 和肺炎的集成深度神经网络能够改善诊断结果。 Srivastava et al. 孔多塞陪审团定理（de Condorcet, 1785）最初是针对需要就一个是非问题进行投票的陪审团证明的。 The Condorcet Jury theorem (de Condorcet, 1785), was initially proven for a jury that should vote for a yes or no question. 该定理指出，在集体决策过程中，如果多数人倾向于某个选项，那么该选项就应被选为最终胜出者，前提是每位陪审员的判断能力优于盲目猜测，且各陪审员相互独立（List and Goodin, 2001; Morreau, 2021）。 It states that if there is a majority preference for one option, then that option should be chosen as the overall winner in a collective decision-making process, provided that individual jury members are better at deciding than a blind guess and that there are independent (List and Goodin, 2001; Morreau, 2021). 从数学角度看，如果将每位陪审员替换为机器学习分类器，并假设各分类器都遵循相同的分布规律，那么采用多数投票的集成袋装分类器应当比任何单个分类器表现更好。 Mathematically, if we change individual jury members into machine learning classifiers and assume that each classifier follows the same law, an ensemble majority voting bagging classifier should perform better than any individual classifiers. 本节将探讨如何将这些结果扩展至多类别分类。 In this section, we will see how to extend these results to a multi class classification. 我们需要假设分类器在某种程度上是相似的。 We will need to assume that classifiers are somehow similar. 这是我们的 LLMs 实验将要质疑的核心假设。 This is the core assumption that will be challenged in our LLMs experiment.
定义 1。Definition 1.
IWTUB 集合：对于由 $n$ 个分类器 $\{C_{1},C_{2},\ldots,C_{n}\}$ 构成的集合，如果所有分类器均满足以下四个条件，我们就称其为 IWTUB（相互独立、训练充分且一致偏向正确选项，Independent, Well-Trained and Uniformly Biased towards the correct Alternative）集合： IWTUB Set: We say that a set of $n$ classifiers $\{C_{1},C_{2},\ldots,C_{n}\}$ is an IWTUB (Independent, Well-Trained and Uniformly Biased towards the correct Alternative) Set if all the classifiers satisfy the following four conditions:
同分布：各分类器具有相同的分布。 Identical Distribution: Classifiers have the same distribution.
优于随机猜测：每个分类器 $C_{i}$ 的准确率均高于在 $k$ 个可能的预测标签中随机猜测的准确率，即对于每个 $C_{i}$ 和真实标签 $t$，概率 $P(C_{i}(x)=t)$ 均大于 $\frac{1}{k}$。 Better Than Random: Each classifier $C_{i}$ has an accuracy better than random guessing over the $k$ possible predicted labels, i.e., for each $C_{i}$ and for the true label $t$ , the probability $P(C_{i}(x)=t)$ is more than $\frac{1}{k}$ . 在二分类情形下，该阈值为 0.5。 For binary classification scenarios, this threshold stands at 0.5. 在我们的具体情境中，共有三个可能的类别（正面、未定或负面），因此该阈值调整为三分之一。 In our specific context, where there are three possible classes (positive, indecisive or negative), the threshold adjusts to one-third.
错误选项之间的均匀分布：每个分类器 $C_{i}$ 选择各个错误选项的概率相同，且低于随机猜测的概率，即对于每个 $C_{i}$ 和任意错误标签 $y\neq t$，概率 $P(C_{i}(x)=y)$ 均小于 $\frac{1}{k}$， Uniform Distribution Among Incorrect Alternatives: Each classifier $C_{i}$ has the same probability which is lower than random guessing for choosing the incorrect alternatives, i.e., for each $C_{i}$ and for any incorrect label $y\neq t$ , the probability $P(C_{i}(x)=y)$ is less than $\frac{1}{k}$ ,
独立性：各分类器相互独立地进行预测。 Independence: Classifiers make their predictions independently of each other.
在所有假设中，最后一项（模型的独立性）通常相当难以满足，因为模型处理数据的方式往往存在相关性。 Among all the assumptions, the last one (independence of models) is usually quite problematic as there are often correlation in the way models handle data. 在分类器构成 IWTUB 集合的假设下，我们可以将孔多塞定理扩展至多类别分类问题，具体如下。 Under the assumption of IWTUB Set of classifiers, we can extend the Condorcet theorem to multi class classifications problems as follows.
定理 1。Theorem 1.
分类器的孔多塞陪审团定理：对于一个 IWTUB 分类器集合 ${C_{1},C_{2},\ldots,C_{n}}$，多数投票分类器 $C_{\text{bag}}$ 的准确率高于该集成中的任何单个分类器。 Condorcet Jury Theorem for Classifiers: For an IWTUB Set of classifiers ${C_{1},C_{2},\ldots,C_{n}}$ , the majority vote classifier $C_{\text{bag}}$ exhibits higher accuracy than any individual classifier within the ensemble. 当分类器数量趋于无穷大时，多数投票分类器依概率收敛于真实标签。 When the number of classifiers tends to infinity, the majority classifier converges in probability to the true label. 反之，如果基分类器的表现差于随机猜测，那么当分类器数量趋于无穷大时，多数投票分类器将依概率偏离真实标签，因此表现差于随机分类器。 Conversely, if base classifiers perform worse than random guessing, as the number of classifiers tends to infinity, majority classifier will diverge in probability from the true label and hence be worse than the random classifier.

到目前为止，我们的分析一直假设所有分类器均服从均匀分布，且准确率相同。 Up to this point, our analysis has assumed that all classifiers are uniformly distributed and equally accurate. 现在，我们将放宽这些假设，考察性能各异但仍优于随机猜测的分类器。 We will now relax these assumptions to consider classifiers that vary in performance but are still more effective than random guessing. 为了探究这些非均匀分类器与随机猜测相比的表现，我们进一步细化分类器优势（Classifier Advantage）这一概念，用以衡量分类器优于随机猜测的程度。 To explore how these non-uniform classifiers compare to random chance, we refine the concept of Classifier Advantage, which measures the extent to which a classifier outperforms random guessing.

由于这三个假设均成立且已得到实验验证，IWTUB 条件集中的最后一个条件便不可能成立，即我们在 bagging 实验中使用的 LLM 模型之间相互独立的假设。 Since the three hypothesis hold and are confirmed by the experiments, the last condition of IWTUB set can not hold, namely the independence hypothesis of the LLMs models used in our bagging experiment. 具体而言，这表明，不能将 GPT 模型视为独立于或不同于 FinBERT 和 DistilRoBERTa 等较简单模型的模型。 In particular, this indicates that GPT models cannot be considered as independent or different models from simpler models like FinBERT and DistilRoBERTa. 具体而言，这意味着 GPT 模型的推理能力可能与较小模型并无太大差异，而且它们缺乏针对金融情感分析等复杂金融任务进行推理的能力。 In particular, this suggests that the reasoning capacity of GPT models may not be very different form the smaller models and that they lack the capacity to reason on complex financial tasks like financial sentiment analysis.
