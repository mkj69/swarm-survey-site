Abstract摘要
Large language model (LLM) agents increasingly collaborate over peer-to-peer networks to improve their reliability. However, these same interactions can also introduce vulnerability to unreliable or Byzantine agents that can propagate incorrect information and degrade overall system performance. To address this, we propose Self-Anchored Consensus (SAC), a fully decentralized filter-and-refine protocol in which agents iteratively exchange responses, locally evaluate and filter unreliable messages, and refine their own outputs. We present $(F{+}1)$ -robustness conditions on the communication graph that ensure honest agents preserve and propagate reliable information despite Byzantine influence. Experiments across diverse open- and closed-weight LLMs on mathematical and commonsense reasoning benchmarks show that SAC effectively suppresses Byzantine influence and consistently improves performance across diverse communication topologies, whereas prior methods degrade significantly under Byzantine attacks. 大语言模型（LLM）智能体日益通过点对点网络开展协作，以提高其可靠性。然而，这些交互也可能使系统易受不可靠智能体或拜占庭智能体的影响，这些智能体能够传播错误信息并降低系统的整体性能。为此，我们提出自锚定共识（Self-Anchored Consensus，SAC），这是一种完全去中心化的过滤与精炼协议，其中智能体迭代交换回答，在本地评估并过滤不可靠消息，并精炼自身的输出。我们给出通信图的 $(F{+}1)$-鲁棒性条件，以确保诚实智能体在受到拜占庭影响的情况下仍能保留和传播可靠信息。在数学和常识推理基准上对多种开放权重和闭源权重 LLMs 进行的实验表明，SAC 能够有效抑制拜占庭影响，并在多种通信拓扑下持续提升性能，而先前方法在拜占庭攻击下的性能则显著下降。[fn†]

1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly deployed as communicating agents in multi-agent systems (MAS), where multiple agents exchange responses, critique one another, and converge on collective answers (Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024). We consider a peer-to-peer setting in which LLM agents collaboratively solve tasks with objectively verifiable answers through consensus.
大语言模型（LLMs）越来越多地作为能够相互通信的智能体部署于多智能体系统（MAS）中，其中多个智能体交换回答、相互评议，并就共同答案达成一致（Guo et al., 2024; Du et al., 2024）。我们考虑一种点对点场景，其中 LLM 智能体通过共识协作求解答案可被客观验证的任务。
As these systems move toward real-world deployment, a central challenge is robustness to faulty and adversarial agents. Individual agents may hallucinate, miscalculate, or strategically inject misleading responses. Since agents cannot locally distinguish trustworthy messages from Byzantine ones (Fig. 1), unreliable responses can contaminate otherwise reliable agents. The key question is therefore whether Byzantine influence can be contained so that honest agents preserve their base capability.
随着这些系统走向现实部署，如何对故障智能体和对抗性智能体保持鲁棒性成为一项核心挑战。单个智能体可能产生幻觉、计算错误，或有策略地注入误导性回答。由于智能体无法在本地区分可信消息与拜占庭消息（图 1），不可靠的回答可能污染原本可靠的智能体。因此，关键问题在于能否遏制拜占庭影响，使诚实智能体保持其基础能力。

This setting naturally connects to classical Byzantine-resilient consensus, where some agents behave arbitrarily while honest nodes must still reach correct agreement (Lamport et al., 1982; LeBlanc et al., 2013; Su and Vaidya, 2021). Viewing LLM-MAS through this lens raises two questions: (i) how should agents decide which neighbors to trust, and (ii) how should the communication graph be designed to limit Byzantine influence?
这一场景自然与经典的拜占庭容错共识相关联：部分智能体可以任意行事，而诚实节点仍须达成正确的一致意见（Lamport et al., 1982; LeBlanc et al., 2013; Su and Vaidya, 2021）。从这一视角审视 LLM-MAS 会引出两个问题：(i) 智能体应如何决定信任哪些邻居；(ii) 应如何设计通信图以限制拜占庭影响？
Recent methods such as CP-WBFT (Zheng et al., 2026) aggregate neighbor responses using self-reported confidence scores. However, this implicitly assumes Byzantine agents report honest reliability signals, departing from the standard Byzantine setting where adversaries may falsify confidence values. Moreover, communication graphs are typically selected heuristically from generic topologies without principled fault-tolerance guarantees, resulting in unstable performance across different network structures (Zheng et al., 2026).
近期的 CP-WBFT（Zheng et al., 2026）等方法使用邻居自行报告的置信度分数来聚合其回答。然而，这隐含地假设拜占庭智能体会如实报告可靠性信号，偏离了允许对手伪造置信度值的标准拜占庭设定。此外，通信图通常是从通用拓扑中以启发式方式选取的，缺乏具有理论依据的容错保证，导致其在不同网络结构下的性能不稳定（Zheng et al., 2026）。
We address these limitations by drawing on classical Byzantine-resilient consensus. Specifically, we adapt Mean-Subsequence-Reduced (MSR) filtering (Dolev et al., 1986; Kieckhafer and Azadmanesh, 1994) and $r$ -robustness (LeBlanc et al., 2013) to LLM-MAS by (i) replacing sender-reported confidence with receiver-side evaluations computed independently by each agent, and (ii) introducing an MSR-inspired filter-and-refine mechanism for iterative response refinement. We further design communication graphs under which honest agents remain robust to Byzantine influence.
我们借鉴经典的拜占庭容错共识来解决这些局限。具体而言，我们通过以下方式将均值子序列削减（Mean-Subsequence-Reduced，MSR）过滤（Dolev et al., 1986; Kieckhafer and Azadmanesh, 1994）和 $r$-鲁棒性（LeBlanc et al., 2013）引入 LLM-MAS：(i) 用各智能体独立计算的接收端评估取代发送方报告的置信度；(ii) 引入一种受 MSR 启发的过滤与精炼机制，以迭代精炼回答。我们进一步设计通信图，使诚实智能体能够在这些图上对拜占庭影响保持鲁棒性。
Contributions.贡献。
Our contributions are twofold. First, we introduce sac (sac), a decentralized MSR-inspired algorithm that enables robust LLM collaboration through receiver-side confidence evaluation and iterative filter-and-refine updates tailored to natural-language interactions. Second, we establish graph-theoretic robustness conditions under which honest agents contain Byzantine influence under sac. Experiments show that our method consistently preserves honest-agent capability across diverse communication topologies, whereas prior approaches collapse catastrophically under Byzantine faults.
我们的贡献有两个方面。首先，我们提出 sac (sac)，这是一种受 MSR 启发的去中心化算法，通过接收端置信度评估以及针对自然语言交互设计的迭代过滤与精炼更新，实现鲁棒的 LLM 协作。其次，我们建立了图论层面的鲁棒性条件，在这些条件下，诚实智能体能够通过 sac 遏制拜占庭影响。实验表明，我们的方法能够在多种通信拓扑下持续保持诚实智能体的能力，而已有方法在拜占庭故障下则出现灾难性的性能崩溃。
2 Related Works2 相关工作
LLM multi-agent systems.LLM 多智能体系统。
Multiple LLM instances coordinated as agents have been shown to outperform single-agent inference through debate (Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024) and role-based frameworks (Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024). A growing body of work studies the reliability cost when such agents behave incorrectly (Tian et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024). Our work takes this failure mode as its starting point and asks what aggregation rule and graph structure are needed to contain it.
已有研究表明，将多个 LLM 实例作为智能体进行协调，可通过辩论（Du et al., 2024; Liang et al., 2024）和基于角色的框架（Wu et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024）取得优于单智能体推理的表现。越来越多的工作研究此类智能体行为出错时造成的可靠性损失（Tian et al., 2023; Yu et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024）。我们的工作以这一失效模式为出发点，探究需要何种聚合规则和图结构才能遏制它。
Byzantine-robust consensus in LLM-MAS.LLM-MAS 中的拜占庭鲁棒共识。
Three recent methods are closest to ours. CP-WBFT (Zheng et al., 2026) relies on self-reported confidence to select high-confidence responses, implicitly assuming Byzantine agents would honestly report their confidence. It also evaluates their method across the six hand-picked graphs, with wildly varying performance. Trusted MultiLLMN (Luo et al., 2025) uses a leader-based BFT protocol in which consecutive Byzantine leaders force expensive re-elections. DecentLLMs (Jo and Park, 2025) removes the leader via a geometric-mean filter over evaluator scores, which is brittle under high-variance scoring and requires a fully-connected evaluator-worker graph. Our method differs on two axes at once: confidence is computed on the receiver side, so Byzantine agents cannot manipulate how others evaluate their output; and our communication topology is grounded on mathematically-driven robustness properties that limit Byzantine influence on general graphs.
近期有三种方法与我们的方法最为接近。CP-WBFT (Zheng et al., 2026) 依赖智能体自行报告的置信度来选择高置信度响应，隐含地假设拜占庭智能体会如实报告其置信度。该研究还在六个精心挑选的图上评估了其方法，性能差异极大。Trusted MultiLLMN (Luo et al., 2025) 使用基于领导者的拜占庭容错（BFT）协议，其中连续出现的拜占庭领导者会迫使系统进行代价高昂的重新选举。DecentLLMs (Jo and Park, 2025) 通过对评估者评分应用几何平均滤波器来取消领导者，但该方法在评分方差较高时较为脆弱，并且要求评估者与工作者之间的图完全连通。我们的方法同时在两个方面有所不同：置信度在接收端计算，因此拜占庭智能体无法操纵其他智能体对其输出的评估；我们的通信拓扑以数学推导的鲁棒性性质为基础，这些性质能够限制拜占庭智能体在一般图上的影响。
Classical Byzantine-resilient algorithms.经典的抗拜占庭算法。
The Byzantine agreement problem (Lamport et al., 1982; Dolev et al., 1986) has been extensively studied to ensure reliability under arbitrary node failures, inspiring a broad line of work on fault-tolerant distributed computation. In particular, the Weighted Mean-Subsequence-Reduced (W-MSR) algorithm, together with $r$ - and $(r,s)$ -robustness conditions (LeBlanc et al., 2013), provides consensus guarantees despite Byzantine agents. These notions have been extended beyond consensus to distributed optimization (Sundaram and Gharesifard, 2019; Yuan and Ishii, 2025) and distributed learning (Xie et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2024), with applications in robotics and smart grids (Lee and Panagou, 2025; Yuan and Ishii, 2025). To our knowledge, this work is the first to bring MSR-type algorithms and robustness conditions to design Byzantine-resilient LLM-MAS.
拜占庭一致性问题 (Lamport et al., 1982; Dolev et al., 1986) 已得到广泛研究，旨在任意节点故障下确保可靠性，并由此推动了容错分布式计算领域的大量研究。具体而言，加权均值子序列削减（Weighted Mean-Subsequence-Reduced，W-MSR）算法结合 $r$-鲁棒性和 $(r,s)$-鲁棒性条件 (LeBlanc et al., 2013)，能够在存在拜占庭智能体的情况下提供共识保证。这些概念已从共识问题扩展到分布式优化 (Sundaram and Gharesifard, 2019; Yuan and Ishii, 2025) 和分布式学习 (Xie et al., 2023; Ye et al., 2024)，并应用于机器人技术和智能电网 (Lee and Panagou, 2025; Yuan and Ishii, 2025)。据我们所知，本研究首次将 MSR 类算法和鲁棒性条件引入抗拜占庭 LLM-MAS 的设计。

3.1 LLM Multi-Agent System3.1 LLM 多智能体系统
We consider a multi-agent system of $n$ LLM agents, indexed by $\mathcal{V}=\{1,\dots,n\}$ , collaboratively answering a query $x$ drawn from a task distribution $\mathcal{D}$ . Agents exchange messages over an undirected, time-invariant communication graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ , where an edge $(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}$ indicates bidirectional exchange between agents $i$ and $j$ , and $\mathcal{N}_{i}=\{j\in\mathcal{V}\mid(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}\}$ denotes the neighbor set of agent $i$ . We also define a directed graph $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$ , where a directed edge $(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}^{\prime}$ indicates that $j$ receives messages from $i$ . A directed graph contains a rooted out-branching if there exists $p\in\mathcal{V}$ that can reach all other nodes. Let $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ denote the non-negative integers.
我们考虑一个由 $n$ 个 LLM 智能体组成的多智能体系统，智能体的索引集合为 $\mathcal{V}=\{1,\dots,n\}$，它们协作回答从任务分布 $\mathcal{D}$ 中抽取的查询 $x$。智能体通过一个无向、时不变的通信图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 交换消息，其中边 $(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 与 $j$ 之间的双向交换，$\mathcal{N}_{i}=\{j\in\mathcal{V}\mid(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}\}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 的邻居集合。我们还定义了一个有向图 $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$，其中有向边 $(i,j)\in\mathcal{E}^{\prime}$ 表示 $j$ 接收来自 $i$ 的消息。如果存在一个能够到达所有其他节点的节点 $p\in\mathcal{V}$，则该有向图包含一棵有根出树。令 $\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ 表示非负整数集合。
At each time step $t\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ , agents communicate with their neighbors. Each agent $i$ is associated with an underlying LLM $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ and produces a response $r_{i}^{(t)}\in\mathcal{Y}$ to a query $x$ , where $\mathcal{Y}$ is the space of admissible outputs (e.g., numerical answers or binary safety labels). We assume that each query $x$ admits a well-defined ground-truth answer $y^{*}\in\mathcal{Y}$ , allowing responses to be classified as correct ( $r_{i}^{(t)}=y^{*}$ ) or incorrect ( $r_{i}^{(t)}\neq y^{*}$ ) at each round $t$ .
在每个时间步 $t\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$，智能体与其邻居进行通信。每个智能体 $i$ 都关联一个底层 LLM $\mathcal{M}_{i}$，并针对查询 $x$ 生成响应 $r_{i}^{(t)}\in\mathcal{Y}$，其中 $\mathcal{Y}$ 是允许输出的空间（例如，数值答案或二元安全标签）。我们假设每个查询 $x$ 都有一个明确定义的真实答案 $y^{*}\in\mathcal{Y}$，因此可以在每轮 $t$ 将响应归类为正确（$r_{i}^{(t)}=y^{*}$）或错误（$r_{i}^{(t)}\neq y^{*}$）。

Definition 3.1 (Byzantine agent).定义 3.1（拜占庭智能体）。
An agent $i\in\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$ is Byzantine if, in some round $t$ , it deviates from the ideal behavior and transmits unreliable information to neighbors. This may occur as follows:
如果智能体 $i\in\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$ 在某一轮 $t$ 偏离理想行为，并向邻居传输不可靠信息，则称其为拜占庭智能体。这种情况可能以下列方式发生：
Faulty Byzantine: the agent $b\in\mathcal{F}\subset\mathcal{B}$ follows the prescribed protocol, but produces incorrect or noisy responses due to internal errors, limited capability, or uncertainty.
故障型拜占庭智能体：智能体 $b\in\mathcal{F}\subset\mathcal{B}$ 遵循规定的协议，但由于内部错误、能力有限或不确定性而生成错误或含噪声的响应。
Adversarial Byzantine: the agent $b\in\mathcal{A}\subset\mathcal{B}$ does not follow the prescribed protocol by acting arbitrarily, e.g., producing incorrect responses ( $r_{i}^{(t)}\neq y^{*}$ ) or sending inconsistent information to different neighbors.
对抗型拜占庭智能体：智能体 $b\in\mathcal{A}\subset\mathcal{B}$ 不遵循规定的协议，而是任意行动，例如生成错误响应（$r_{i}^{(t)}\neq y^{*}$）或向不同邻居发送不一致的信息。
Byzantine agents consist of both faulty agents $\mathcal{F}$ and adversarial agents $\mathcal{A}$ such that $\mathcal{A}\cap\mathcal{F}=\emptyset$ and $\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{B}$ . Their behaviors may arise from systematic errors (e.g. hallucinations, stochastic errors) as well as adversarial manipulation, which are indistinguishable to non-Byzantine agents.
拜占庭智能体由故障智能体 $\mathcal{F}$ 和对抗性智能体 $\mathcal{A}$ 共同组成，满足 $\mathcal{A}\cap\mathcal{F}=\emptyset$ 且 $\mathcal{A}\cup\mathcal{F}=\mathcal{B}$。它们的行为可能源于系统性错误（例如幻觉、随机错误），也可能源于对抗性操纵，而非拜占庭智能体无法区分这些成因。
The remaining agents $\mathcal{P}:=\mathcal{V}\setminus\mathcal{B}$ are referred to as reliable agents. We assume identities of Byzantine agents are unknown to reliable agents. Furthermore, we define the set of honest agents as $\mathcal{H}:=\mathcal{V}\setminus\mathcal{A}$ , i.e., non-adversarial agents. 若集合 $\mathcal{S}\subset\mathcal{V}$ 之外的每个节点在 $\mathcal{S}$ 中至多有 $F$ 个邻居，则称 $\mathcal{S}$ 为 $F$ -局部集合，即对所有 $i\in\mathcal{V}\setminus\mathcal{S}$，均有 $|\mathcal{N}_{i}\cap\mathcal{S}|\leq F$。
3.3 Graph Robustness3.3 图鲁棒性
We now introduce the notion of $r$ -robustness (LeBlanc et al., 2013):
下面引入 $r$ -鲁棒性的概念（LeBlanc et al., 2013）：
Definition 3.3 ( $r$ -robust LeBlanc et al. (2013)).定义 3.3（$r$ -鲁棒，LeBlanc et al. (2013)）。
A graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ is $r$ -robust if for every pair of nonempty, disjoint subsets $\mathcal{S}_{1},\mathcal{S}_{2}\subset\mathcal{V}$ , at least one of the subsets contains a node with at least $r$ neighbors outside the subset. That is, there exists a node $i\in\mathcal{S}_{k}$ such that $|\mathcal{N}_{i}\setminus\mathcal{S}_{k}|\geq r$ for some $k\in\{1,2\}$ .
对于图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$，若对任意一对非空且互不相交的子集 $\mathcal{S}_{1},\mathcal{S}_{2}\subset\mathcal{V}$，至少有一个子集包含一个在该子集之外拥有至少 $r$ 个邻居的节点，则称该图为 $r$ -鲁棒图。也就是说，存在某个 $k\in\{1,2\}$ 以及节点 $i\in\mathcal{S}_{k}$，使得 $|\mathcal{N}_{i}\setminus\mathcal{S}_{k}|\geq r$。
Higher robustness levels limit the influence of more Byzantine agents on reliable ones but require denser communication graphs. We now present a useful property of $r$ -robustness:
更高的鲁棒性水平能够限制更多拜占庭智能体对可靠智能体的影响，但需要更稠密的通信图。下面给出 $r$ -鲁棒性的一个有用性质：
Lemma 3.4.引理 3.4。
Given an $r$ -robust graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ , let $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$ be a directed graph produced by removing up to $k$ incoming edges of each agent in $\mathcal{G}$ . Then, $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}$ is $(r-k)$ -robust.
给定一个 $r$ -鲁棒图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$，令 $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$ 为从 $\mathcal{G}$ 中删除每个智能体至多 $k$ 条入边后得到的有向图。则 $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}$ 是 $(r-k)$ -鲁棒的。
The notion of $r$ -robustness is a preferable graph-theoretic condition against Byzantine agents, as it can achieve the same level of resilience as classical metrics such as connectivity or minimum degree with sparser connectivity (LeBlanc et al., 2013; Pirani et al., 2023). This sparsity is particularly valuable in LLM-MAS, where each edge in $\mathcal{E}$ corresponds to an LLM-to-LLM message exchange and thus directly contributes to per-round inference cost. We later leverage this notion of robustness to design communication networks that systematically limit Byzantine influence in LLM-MAS.
$r$ -鲁棒性是抵御拜占庭智能体的一种更理想的图论条件，因为它能在连接更稀疏的情况下，达到与连通度或最小度等经典指标相同的容错水平（LeBlanc et al., 2013; Pirani et al., 2023）。这种稀疏性在 LLM-MAS 中尤为重要，因为 $\mathcal{E}$ 中的每条边都对应一次 LLM 之间的消息交换，因此会直接增加每轮的推理成本。后文将利用这一鲁棒性概念来设计通信网络，以系统性地限制 LLM-MAS 中的拜占庭影响。

The procedure is iterated for $T$ rounds.

3.4 Problem Statement3.4 问题陈述
Given $n$ LLM agents, a query $x$ , and an $F$ -local Byzantine set $\mathcal{B}=\mathcal{F}\cup\mathcal{A}$ , our objective is twofold: (i) ensure that reliable agents $\mathcal{P}$ remain robust to Byzantine messages, and (ii) enable collaborative refinement that helps all honest agents $\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{P}\cup\mathcal{F}$ move toward the correct answer in a fully decentralized peer-to-peer network.
给定 $n$ 个 LLM 智能体、一个查询 $x$ 和一个 $F$ -局部拜占庭集合 $\mathcal{B}=\mathcal{F}\cup\mathcal{A}$，我们的目标包括两个方面：(i) 确保可靠智能体 $\mathcal{P}$ 对拜占庭消息保持鲁棒性；(ii) 在完全去中心化的点对点网络中实现协作改进，帮助所有诚实智能体 $\mathcal{H}=\mathcal{P}\cup\mathcal{F}$ 逐步趋近正确答案。
We address this in two steps: first, we develop a decentralized protocol where agents iteratively refine responses using neighbor messages while filtering Byzantine influence; second, we use $r$ -robustness to characterize communication graphs under which honest agents can reliably refine their responses.
我们分两步解决这一问题：首先，开发一种去中心化协议，使智能体在过滤拜占庭影响的同时，利用邻居消息迭代改进回答；其次，利用 $r$ -鲁棒性刻画能够使诚实智能体可靠地改进回答的通信图。
Remark 3.5.注 3.5。
Adversarial agents $\mathcal{A}$ may arbitrarily deviate from the protocol, whereas faulty agents $\mathcal{F}$ follow the protocol but may produce unreliable outputs. Although both are Byzantine, faulty agents can be viewed as temporary Byzantine agents, since iterative refinement may eventually correct their responses and restore reliable behavior.
对抗性智能体 $\mathcal{A}$ 可以任意偏离协议，而故障智能体 $\mathcal{F}$ 遵循协议，但可能产生不可靠的输出。尽管两者都属于拜占庭智能体，但故障智能体可以视为暂时性的拜占庭智能体，因为迭代改进最终可能纠正其回答，并使其恢复可靠行为。
4 Method4 方法
4.1 sac (sac)4.1 sac (sac)
Here we describe sac (sac) (visualized in Fig. 3), with which each agent $i\in\mathcal{V}$ iteratively refines its response by incorporating trusted neighbor responses while filtering potential Byzantine information through four steps. Given query $x$ , every agent $i\in\mathcal{V}$ first produces an initial response and evaluates its own response:
这里介绍 sac (sac)（如图 3 所示），其中每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{V}$ 通过以下四个步骤，在过滤潜在拜占庭信息的同时，融合可信邻居的回答来迭代改进自己的回答。给定查询 $x$，每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{V}$ 首先生成初始回答，并评估自己的回答：
$$r_{i}^{(0)}\;=\;\mathcal{M}_{i}(x),\quad s_{i\to i}^{(0)}=\phi_{i}(x,r_{i}^{(0)})\in[0,1],$$
where $\phi_{i}$ is a scoring function implemented by agent $i$ ’s own LLM. In our implementation, $\phi_{i}$ is realized via a verification prompt that asks $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ to judge the correctness of a given response with respect to $x$ , returning a calibrated score in $[0,1]$ ; the full template is given in Appendix B.
其中，$\phi_{i}$ 是由智能体 $i$ 自身的 LLM 实现的评分函数。在我们的实现中，$\phi_{i}$ 通过验证提示实现，该提示要求 $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ 判断给定回答针对 $x$ 的正确性，并返回一个位于 $[0,1]$ 范围内的校准分数；完整模板见附录 B。
At each subsequent round $t\geq 0$ , every agent $i\in\mathcal{V}$ performs the following steps.
在随后每一轮 $t\geq 0$ 中，每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{V}$ 执行以下步骤。
Broadcast responses. Every agent $i$ broadcasts its current response $r_{i}^{(t)}$ to its neighbors.
广播回答。每个智能体 $i$ 向其邻居广播当前回答 $r_{i}^{(t)}$。
Score neighbors. Upon receiving neighbor responses $\{r_{j}^{(t)}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}}$ , agent $i$ computes, for each neighbor $j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}$ , an evaluation score
为邻居评分。收到邻居回答 $\{r_{j}^{(t)}\}_{j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}}$ 后，智能体 $i$ 为每个邻居 $j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}$ 计算一个评估分数
$$s_{i\to j}^{(t)}\;=\;\phi_{i}\bigl(x,\,r_{j}^{(t)}\bigr)\,\in\,[0,1].$$
The key distinction from prior work Zheng et al. (2026) is that $s_{i\to j}^{(t)}$ depends only on agent $i$ ’s own computation applied to the content of $r_{j}^{(t)}$ , and in particular does not rely on any score reported by agent $j$ . This removes the possibility for a Byzantine agent to manipulate its own reliability by reporting an inflated self-confidence.
与先前工作 Zheng et al. (2026) 的关键区别在于，$s_{i\to j}^{(t)}$ 仅取决于智能体 $i$ 自身对 $r_{j}^{(t)}$ 内容的计算，尤其不依赖于智能体 $j$ 报告的任何分数。这消除了拜占庭智能体通过报告虚高的自信度来操纵自身可靠性评估的可能性。
Filter. Let $\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}\subseteq\mathcal{N}_{i}$ be the set of neighbors whose score is strictly below agent $i$ ’s self-score,
过滤。令 $\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}\subseteq\mathcal{N}_{i}$ 表示评分严格低于智能体 $i$ 自评分数的邻居集合，
$$\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}\;=\;\bigl\{\,j\in\mathcal{N}_{i}\,\bigm|\,s_{i\to j}^{(t)}<s_{i\to i}^{(t)}\,\bigr\}.$$
Agent $i$ then discards the $\min(F,|\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}|)$ lowest-scoring elements of $\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}$ , yielding the retained set
随后，智能体 $i$ 丢弃 $\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}$ 中评分最低的 $\min(F,|\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}|)$ 个元素，得到保留集合
$$\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}\;=\;\mathcal{N}_{i}\setminus\mathrm{bottom}_{F}(\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}),$$
where $\mathrm{bottom}_{F}(\cdot)$ returns the $F$ lowest-scoring elements of its argument, or the entire argument when $|\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}|\leq F$ .
其中，$\mathrm{bottom}_{F}(\cdot)$ 返回其输入中评分最低的 $F$ 个元素；当 $|\mathcal{L}_{i}^{(t)}|\leq F$ 时，则返回整个输入。
Refine. Agent $i$ refines its response by conditioning $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ on its current response and the retained neighbor responses, weighted by the corresponding receiver-side scores:
改进。智能体 $i$ 将其当前回答和保留的邻居回答作为 $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ 的条件输入，并依据相应的接收端评分进行加权，以改进其回答：
$$r_{i}^{(t+1)}\;=\;\mathcal{M}_{i}\Bigl(x,\;r_{i}^{(t)},\;\bigl\{(r_{j}^{(t)},\,s_{i\to j}^{(t)})\bigr\}_{j\in\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}}\Bigr).$$
After refinement, agent $i$ re-evaluates its updated response using $\phi_{i}$ to obtain an updated self-evaluation score. The prompt template for Eq. (4) instructs $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ to prefer high-scored responses while retaining the option to keep its own response if none of the retained neighbor responses improves upon it; see Appendix B for the full template.
改进后，智能体 $i$ 使用 $\phi_{i}$ 重新评估其更新后的回答，以获得更新后的自评得分。式 (4) 的提示模板指示 $\mathcal{M}_{i}$ 优先采用高分回答，同时保留如下选项：如果保留的邻居回答均不优于自身回答，则保留自身回答；完整模板见附录 B。
After $T$ rounds (a user-specified parameter), the final response of agent $i\in\mathcal{V}$ is $r_{i}^{(T)}$ . The overall procedure of SAC is summarized in Algorithm 1.
经过 $T$ 轮（由用户指定的参数）后，智能体 $i\in\mathcal{V}$ 的最终回答为 $r_{i}^{(T)}$。SAC 的整体流程概述于算法 1。

Remark 4.1.注 4.1。
Note that Algorithm 1 is fully decentralized; each LLM agent operates using only local information from its neighbors and does not require knowledge of the global network topology $\mathcal{G}$ or the identities of Byzantine agents.
注意，算法 1 是完全去中心化的；每个 LLM 智能体仅使用来自其邻居的局部信息运行，无需了解全局网络拓扑 $\mathcal{G}$ 或拜占庭智能体的身份。
Remark 4.2.注 4.2。
On top of generating its own answer to the given problem, sac incurs additional inference cost by independently evaluating neighbors’ responses before refinement. With $n$ agents and $T$ communication rounds, sac requires $n(1+2T)$ LLM calls: one initialization call and two calls per round for batched scoring and refinement. Since the scoring stage evaluates all received responses in a single prompt, the number of LLM invocations does not directly scale with the neighborhood size, although token usage and inference latency do increase with it.
除针对给定问题生成自身答案外，sac 还会在改进前独立评估邻居的回答，因此产生额外的推理成本。对于 $n$ 个智能体和 $T$ 轮通信，sac 需要 $n(1+2T)$ 次 LLM 调用：每个智能体进行一次初始化调用，并在每轮进行两次调用，分别用于批量评分和改进。由于评分阶段在单个提示中评估所有收到的回答，LLM 调用次数不会随邻域规模直接增长，但词元用量和推理延迟确实会随之增加。
4.2 Robust Topological Conditions4.2 鲁棒拓扑条件
While Algorithm 1 is fully decentralized, its effectiveness depends on the connectivity of the underlying communication graph. Since the filtering step in line 6 of sac removes up to $F$ neighbors per round, an agent with $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\leq F$ may have no incoming information after filtering, reducing the algorithm to repeated self-updates. We therefore design the topology to satisfy $(F+1)$ -robustness, which ensures two key properties.
尽管算法 1 是完全去中心化的，其有效性仍取决于底层通信图的连通性。由于 sac 第 6 行的过滤步骤每轮最多移除 $F$ 个邻居，满足 $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\leq F$ 的智能体在过滤后可能没有任何传入信息，从而使算法退化为反复进行自我更新。因此，我们将拓扑设计为满足 $(F+1)$-鲁棒性，以确保以下两个关键性质。
Property 4.3.性质 4.3。
Consider an LLM-MAS connected through a communication graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ with an $F$ -local Byzantine set $\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$ . Let $\mathcal{S}_{i}^{(t)}=\{\phi_{i}(x,r_{j}^{(t)})\mid j\in\mathcal{P}\}$ denote the set of evaluation scores assigned by agent $i$ at time $t$ to responses from reliable agents. If $\mathcal{G}$ is $(F+1)$ -robust, then for every $k\in\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ and time $t\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$ , the evaluation score $s_{i\to k}^{(t)}$ is at least as high as the minimum evaluation score assigned to any reliable agent’s response, i.e., $s_{i\to k}^{(t)}\geq\min\mathcal{S}_{i}^{(t)},\ \forall k\in\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ .
考虑一个通过通信图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 连接的 LLM-MAS，其中存在一个 $F$-局部拜占庭集合 $\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$。令 $\mathcal{S}_{i}^{(t)}=\{\phi_{i}(x,r_{j}^{(t)})\mid j\in\mathcal{P}\}$ 表示智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 对可靠智能体的回答所赋予的评估得分集合。若 $\mathcal{G}$ 具有 $(F+1)$-鲁棒性，则对于每个 $k\in\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ 和时刻 $t\in\mathbb{Z}_{\geq 0}$，评估得分 $s_{i\to k}^{(t)}$ 至少与赋予可靠智能体回答的最低评估得分一样高，即 $s_{i\to k}^{(t)}\geq\min\mathcal{S}_{i}^{(t)},\ \forall k\in\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$。
Proof.证明。
By Lemma 5 of LeBlanc et al. (2013), since $\mathcal{G}$ is $(F+1)$ -robust, we have $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\geq F+1$ for all $i\in\mathcal{V}$ . Since the filtering step of sac removes at most $F$ neighbor indices corresponding to the lowest evaluation scores, and $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\geq F+1$ , at least one neighbor remains in $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ , i.e., $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}\neq\emptyset$ . Furthermore, under the $F$ -local model, and since at most $F$ indices are removed from the lowest end of the ordered scores, $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ contains only agent indices whose responses have scores no lower than the minimum score among reliable agents. ∎
根据 LeBlanc et al. (2013) 的引理 5，由于 $\mathcal{G}$ 具有 $(F+1)$-鲁棒性，对于所有 $i\in\mathcal{V}$，均有 $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\geq F+1$。由于 sac 的过滤步骤至多移除与最低评估得分对应的 $F$ 个邻居索引，且 $|\mathcal{N}_{i}|\geq F+1$，因此 $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ 中至少保留一个邻居，即 $\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}\neq\emptyset$。此外，在 $F$-局部模型下，由于从排序后得分的最低端至多移除 $F$ 个索引，$\mathcal{R}_{i}^{(t)}$ 仅包含回答得分不低于可靠智能体中最低得分的智能体索引。∎
Property 4.3 guarantees that, after filtering, each agent retains at least one response whose quality is comparable to that of a reliable agent, even if the response does not originate from a reliable agent. This allows honest agents to safely refine their outputs using information of comparable quality to that produced by reliable agents, despite the presence of Byzantine agents.
性质 4.3 保证，过滤后，每个智能体至少保留一个质量与可靠智能体的回答相当的回答，即使该回答并非来自可靠智能体。这使得诚实智能体即使在存在拜占庭智能体的情况下，也能安全地利用质量与可靠智能体所生成信息相当的信息来改进其输出。
Property 4.4.性质 4.4。
Consider an LLM-MAS connected through a communication graph $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ with an $F$ -local Byzantine set $\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$ . Let $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$ be a directed graph obtained by removing up to any $F$ incoming edges of each agent in $\mathcal{G}$ . If $\mathcal{G}$ is $(F+1)$ -robust, then $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}$ contains a rooted-out branching.
考虑一个通过通信图 $\mathcal{G}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E})$ 连接的 LLM-MAS，其中存在一个 $F$-局部拜占庭集合 $\mathcal{B}\subset\mathcal{V}$。令 $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}=(\mathcal{V},\mathcal{E}^{\prime})$ 为从 $\mathcal{G}$ 中移除每个智能体至多任意 $F$ 条入边后得到的有向图。若 $\mathcal{G}$ 具有 $(F+1)$-鲁棒性，则 $\mathcal{G}^{\prime}$ 包含一棵有根外向生成树（rooted-out branching）。
Property 4.4 is a direct consequence of Lemma 3.4 and Lemma 7 of LeBlanc et al. (2013). It guarantees that $F$ -filtering does not disconnect the network, thereby preventing fragmentation into isolated subgroups that may converge to different or incorrect solutions. Therefore, under $(F+1)$ -robustness, the subgraph induced by reliable agents remains connected even after filtering out potentially malicious messages.
性质 4.4 可由 LeBlanc et al. (2013) 的引理 3.4 和引理 7 直接推出。它保证 $F$-过滤不会使网络断开，从而防止网络分裂为可能收敛到不同解或错误解的孤立子群。因此，在满足 $(F+1)$-鲁棒性的条件下，即使过滤掉潜在的恶意消息，可靠智能体所诱导的子图仍保持连通。

5.1 Evaluation Metrics5.1 评估指标
Let $y^{\star}$ denote the ground-truth answer to query $x\sim\mathcal{D}$ . We partition honest agents $\mathcal{H}$ into strong and weak groups, where strong and weak agents are treated as reliable and faulty (non-adversarial Byzantine) agents, respectively. Following Zheng et al. (2026), we report the following metrics (higher is better). We set communication round to be $T=6$ for all experiments.
令 $y^{\star}$ 表示查询 $x\sim\mathcal{D}$ 的真实答案。我们将诚实智能体 $\mathcal{H}$ 划分为强、弱两组，其中强智能体和弱智能体分别被视为可靠智能体和故障智能体（非对抗性拜占庭智能体）。遵循 Zheng et al. (2026)，我们报告以下指标（越高越好）。所有实验的通信轮数均设为 $T=6$。
Initial / Final Agent Accuracy (IAA / FAA). Average per-agent accuracy before communication ( $t=0$ ) and after $T$ consensus rounds ( $t=T$ ).
初始 / 最终智能体准确率（IAA / FAA）。通信前（$t=0$）和经过 $T$ 轮共识后（$t=T$）各智能体准确率的平均值。
Byzantine Fault Tolerance Improvement (BFTI). $\mathrm{BFTI}=\mathrm{FAA}-\mathrm{IAA}$ measures the gain or degradation introduced by the consensus protocol.
拜占庭容错改进量（BFTI）。$\mathrm{BFTI}=\mathrm{FAA}-\mathrm{IAA}$ 衡量共识协议带来的性能提升或下降。
Round-level Accuracy (RA). The fraction of queries for which the majority vote across all agents in $\mathcal{V}$ at round $T$ matches $y^{\star}$ .
轮次级准确率（RA）。在第 $T$ 轮，$\mathcal{V}$ 中所有智能体的多数投票结果与 $y^{\star}$ 一致的查询所占的比例。
Group-wise Accuracy (W_IAA->FAA, S_IAA->FAA). Accuracy change of the weak and strong honest groups before and after consensus.
分组准确率（W_IAA->FAA、S_IAA->FAA）。弱、强诚实智能体组在共识前后的准确率变化。
Honest Majority (H-Majority). The fraction of queries for which the majority vote among honest agents $\mathcal{H}$ at round $T$ matches $y^{\star}$ .
诚实智能体多数投票准确率（H-Majority）。在第 $T$ 轮，诚实智能体 $\mathcal{H}$ 的多数投票结果与 $y^{\star}$ 一致的查询所占的比例。

5.3.1 Closed-Weight LLMs5.3.1 权重不公开的 LLM
We first test our method with closed-weight LLMs on 100 Level 4 problems sampled from the Hendrycks MATH test set and 30 sampled instances from Commonsense170k, and the results are shown in Tables 1 and 2.
我们首先使用权重不公开的 LLM，在从 Hendrycks MATH 测试集中抽样的 100 道 Level 4 题目和从 Commonsense170k 中抽样的 30 个样本上测试我们的方法，结果如表 1 和表 2 所示。
MATH. We use gpt-3.5-turbo and gpt-4o-mini as the weak and strong models, respectively. CP-WBFT consistently yields negative BFTI across all topologies ( $-8.7\%$ to $-17.5\%$ ), with strong-agent accuracy decreasing after the first communication round while weak-agent accuracy increases. In contrast, sac achieves non-negative BFTI ( $+0.4\%$ to $+3.0\%$ ), preserves strong-agent capability, and maintains stable per-round behavior. H-Majority reaches $78$ – $79\%$ across all topologies.
MATH。我们分别使用 gpt-3.5-turbo 和 gpt-4o-mini 作为弱模型和强模型。CP-WBFT 在所有拓扑下的 BFTI 均为负值（$-8.7\%$ 至 $-17.5\%$），且在第一轮通信后，强智能体的准确率下降，而弱智能体的准确率上升。相比之下，sac 实现了非负的 BFTI（$+0.4\%$ 至 $+3.0\%$），保留了强智能体的能力，并在各轮中保持稳定表现。在所有拓扑下，H-Majority 均达到 $78$ 至 $79\%$。
Commonsense reasoning. A similar trend can be observed on Commonsense170k, where we use gpt-4o and gpt-5 as the weak and strong models. CP-WBFT suffers severe degradation under Byzantine influence ( $-54.7\%$ to $-32.4\%$ BFTI), with both weak and strong agents collapsing immediately after Rnd 1. In contrast, sac maintains near-zero degradation ( $-2.8\%$ to $+0.0\%$ BFTI), preserving the performance of strong and weak agents.
常识推理。在 Commonsense170k 上也可以观察到类似趋势，其中我们使用 gpt-4o 和 gpt-5 分别作为弱模型和强模型。CP-WBFT 在拜占庭智能体的影响下出现严重的性能退化（BFTI 为 $-54.7\%$ 至 $-32.4\%$），弱智能体和强智能体的性能均在第 1 轮后立即崩溃。相比之下，sac 的性能退化接近于零（BFTI 为 $-2.8\%$ 至 $+0.0\%$），保留了强智能体和弱智能体的性能。

The results in Tables 3 and 4 show a similar trend as that of the closed-weight LLMs. CP-WBFT consistently yields negative BFTI across all topologies ( $-2.8\%$ to $-12.0\%$ ), with both weak and strong groups suffering from accuracy loss after the first communication round. In contrast, SAC consistently achieves positive BFTI ( $+1.1\%$ to $+6.5\%$ ) with stable or improving per-round behavior. SAC achieves H-Majority of $55.4$ - $74.6\%$ , compared to $38.2$ - $50.0\%$ for CP-WBFT. Across all topologies, SAC preserves strong-agent capability while preventing collapse of weaker agents.
现在，我们使用权重开放的 LLM 评估我们的方法，分别使用 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 和 Qwen3-4B 作为弱模型和强模型。表 3 和表 4 中的结果呈现出与权重不公开的 LLM 类似的趋势。CP-WBFT 在所有拓扑下的 BFTI 均为负值（$-2.8\%$ 至 $-12.0\%$），且弱智能体组和强智能体组的准确率均在第一轮通信后下降。相比之下，SAC 始终实现正的 BFTI（$+1.1\%$ 至 $+6.5\%$），且各轮表现保持稳定或有所改善。SAC 的 H-Majority 达到 $55.4$ 至 $74.6\%$，而 CP-WBFT 为 $38.2$ 至 $50.0\%$。在所有拓扑下，SAC 均保留了强智能体的能力，同时防止较弱智能体的性能崩溃。

Self-reported confidence creates a point of vulnerability. Across all evaluated topologies and benchmarks, CP-WBFT with adversarial agents with falsified self-confidence consistently exhibits rapid and significant performance degradation. Receiver-side evaluation contains Byzantine influence. By construction, sac contains the impact of Byzantine agents through receiver-side evaluation. This allows sac to achieve non-negative BFTI in nearly all settings while achieving substantially higher H-Majority than CP-WBFT.
接收端评估可遏制拜占庭影响。根据其设计，sac 通过接收端评估来遏制拜占庭智能体的影响。这使 sac 在几乎所有设置下都能实现非负的 BFTI，同时获得显著高于 CP-WBFT 的 H-Majority。
Strong agents are preserved while weak agents are lifted. CP-WBFT may improve weak-agent accuracy, but by dragging strong agents toward incorrect responses, degrading overall reliability. In contrast, SAC preserves strong-agent capability with only minor degradation while improving weak agents across all topologies in general. These results suggest that SAC acts as a protective filtering mechanism against Byzantine faults.
强智能体的能力得以保持，弱智能体的能力得到提升。CP-WBFT 可能会提高弱智能体的准确率，但代价是将强智能体引向错误回答，从而降低整体可靠性。相比之下，SAC 能保持强智能体的能力，仅造成轻微的性能下降，同时总体上在所有拓扑中都能提升弱智能体的表现。这些结果表明，SAC 起到了抵御拜占庭故障的保护性过滤机制的作用。
7 Conclusion7 结论
We studied robust decision-making in decentralized LLM-MAS under Byzantine faults. We proposed sac (sac), a fully decentralized filter-and-refine protocol with receiver-side evaluation, and established $(F{+}1)$ -robustness conditions under which honest agents reliably refine their responses despite Byzantine influence. Experiments on mathematical and commonsense reasoning benchmarks show that sac consistently maintains robustness across diverse topologies, whereas prior methods suffer a collapse in performance under Byzantine attacks.
我们研究了拜占庭故障下去中心化 LLM-MAS 中的鲁棒决策问题。我们提出了 sac（sac），这是一种采用接收端评估的完全去中心化过滤与改进协议，并确立了 $(F{+}1)$-鲁棒性条件，在这些条件下，诚实智能体即使受到拜占庭影响，也能可靠地改进其回答。数学和常识推理基准上的实验表明，sac 在多种拓扑中始终保持鲁棒性，而已有方法在拜占庭攻击下则出现性能崩溃。
Limitations局限性
One main limitation of sac is that its practical robustness relies on the agents’ base capabilities, as effective filtering and refinement require both (i) a reliable scoring function $\phi_{i}$ for identifying correct responses and (ii) the ability to effectively integrate neighbor information. Thus, a key failure mode could arise when a faulty agent is overconfident and assigns low scores to correct neighbor responses, thereby discarding useful information and remaining trapped in error despite reliable neighbors. A complete characterization of how model capabilities affect performance remains future work.
sac 的一个主要局限性在于，其实际鲁棒性依赖于智能体的基础能力，因为有效的过滤和改进同时需要 (i) 用于识别正确回答的可靠评分函数 $\phi_{i}$，以及 (ii) 有效整合邻居信息的能力。因此，当某个故障智能体过度自信，并对邻居的正确回答赋予低分时，就可能出现一种关键的失效模式：该智能体会丢弃有用信息，即使邻居可靠，也仍然陷于错误之中。完整刻画模型能力如何影响性能，仍有待未来研究。
In addition, our evaluation is limited to networks of $n\in\{7,9\}$ , a single adversarial behavior (cached weak-model replay with falsified confidence), and single-turn responses. Larger and dynamic networks, multi-turn reasoning, and tool use are outside the present scope.
此外，我们的评估仅限于规模为 $n\in\{7,9\}$ 的网络、单一的对抗行为（重放缓存的弱模型回答并伪造置信度），以及单轮回答。更大规模的网络、动态网络、多轮推理和工具使用均不在本研究的范围之内。
Lastly, we note that stronger robustness can improve resilience but this requires denser network, thereby increasing computational and communication costs, as discussed in Remark 4.2. This highlights a resilience-efficiency trade-off, which we plan to investigate in future work.
最后，我们指出，更强的鲁棒性可以提升韧性，但这需要更稠密的网络，从而增加计算和通信成本，如注 4.2 所述。这凸显了韧性与效率之间的权衡，我们计划在未来工作中对此进行研究。

Appendix D Additional Experimental Results附录 D 补充实验结果
In this section, we report benchmark-level and per-round results for the open-weight LLM experiments summarized in Section 5.3.2. Tables 11 and 12 expand the averaged results reported in Tables 3 and 4.
本节报告第 5.3.2 节所概述的开放权重大语言模型实验在各基准上的结果及逐轮结果。表 11 和表 12 对表 3 和表 4 所报告的平均结果进行了展开。
Per-benchmark robustness.各基准上的鲁棒性。
Across all five commonsense reasoning benchmarks and topologies, SAC achieves positive BFTI on ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, and OpenBookQA, with improvements of up to $+7.5\%$ on ARC-Challenge. On BoolQ, SAC remains within $0.6\%$ of its initial accuracy across all topologies, while on RTE it limits degradation to between $-3.4\%$ and $-0.1\%$ . By contrast, CP-WBFT exhibits substantial negative BFTI on ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, OpenBookQA, and RTE, while remaining near zero on BoolQ. The largest gap appears on RTE under the Complete topology, where CP-WBFT yields $-24.2\%$ BFTI while SAC yields only $-0.1\%$ . Overall, these results suggest that SAC’s receiver-side evaluation generalizes robustly across benchmarks with substantially different reasoning formats and difficulty levels.
在全部五个常识推理基准和所有拓扑结构的评估中，SAC 在 ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag 和 OpenBookQA 上均取得正的 BFTI，其中在 ARC-Challenge 上的提升最高达 $+7.5\%$。在 BoolQ 上，SAC 在所有拓扑结构下的准确率与初始准确率的差距均保持在 $0.6\%$ 以内；而在 RTE 上，其将性能下降幅度限制在 $-3.4\%$ 至 $-0.1\%$ 之间。相比之下，CP-WBFT 在 ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag、OpenBookQA 和 RTE 上的 BFTI 均为较大的负值，而在 BoolQ 上则保持在零附近。最大的差距出现在 Complete 拓扑下的 RTE 上，其中 CP-WBFT 的 BFTI 为 $-24.2\%$，而 SAC 仅为 $-0.1\%$。总体而言，这些结果表明，SAC 的接收端评估能够稳健地泛化到推理形式和难度水平差异显著的各类基准上。
Per-group behavior.各组表现。
The per-group accuracies reported in Table 11 show that SAC preserves or improves strong-agent accuracy on ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, BoolQ, and OpenBookQA while limiting degradation on RTE. Representative examples include $51\to 64\%$ on ARC-Challenge with Complete, $30\to 34\%$ on HellaSwag with Complete, and $42\to 44\%$ on OpenBookQA with Complete. Under CP-WBFT, strong-agent accuracy degrades substantially on ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, OpenBookQA, and RTE. Weak-agent accuracy under SAC generally changes more modestly, although some degradation remains on BoolQ and RTE. H-Majority is higher under SAC across all benchmark-topology pairs, with particularly large improvements on ARC-Challenge, OpenBookQA, and RTE.
Table 11 报告的各组准确率表明，SAC 在 ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag、BoolQ 和 OpenBookQA 上保持或提高了强智能体的准确率，同时限制了其在 RTE 上的性能下降。代表性例子包括：在 Complete 拓扑下，ARC-Challenge 上的准确率为 $51\to 64\%$，HellaSwag 上为 $30\to 34\%$，OpenBookQA 上为 $42\to 44\%$。在 CP-WBFT 下，强智能体在 ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag、OpenBookQA 和 RTE 上的准确率显著下降。在 SAC 下，弱智能体准确率的变化通常较为温和，但在 BoolQ 和 RTE 上仍存在一定程度的下降。在所有基准与拓扑的组合中，SAC 下的 H-Majority 均更高，其中在 ARC-Challenge、OpenBookQA 和 RTE 上的提升尤为显著。
Per-round dynamics.逐轮动态。
Table 12 reports per-round weak/strong accuracies. Under SAC, strong-agent accuracy improves quickly and remains relatively stable on ARC-Challenge and HellaSwag. For example, strong-agent accuracy increases from $52.1\%$ at initialization to $63.7\%$ at round 6 on ARC-Challenge with MERG, and from $29.7\%$ to $33.6\%$ on HellaSwag with Complete. BoolQ and OpenBookQA show smaller changes across rounds, while RTE exhibits larger round-to-round fluctuations, with strong-agent accuracy recovering to $75.0$ – $79.4\%$ by round 6 across the three topologies. In contrast, the aggregated CP-WBFT results over rounds 1–6 show substantial strong-agent degradation on ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, OpenBookQA, and RTE.
Table 12 报告了各轮的弱智能体和强智能体准确率。在 SAC 下，强智能体在 ARC-Challenge 和 HellaSwag 上的准确率迅速提高，并保持相对稳定。例如，在 MERG 拓扑下的 ARC-Challenge 上，强智能体准确率从初始化时的 $52.1\%$ 提高至第 6 轮的 $63.7\%$；在 Complete 拓扑下的 HellaSwag 上，则从 $29.7\%$ 提高至 $33.6\%$。BoolQ 和 OpenBookQA 在各轮之间的变化较小，而 RTE 的轮间波动较大，在三种拓扑下，强智能体准确率到第 6 轮时恢复至 $75.0$ – $79.4\%$。相比之下，CP-WBFT 在第 1–6 轮的汇总结果显示，强智能体在 ARC-Challenge、HellaSwag、OpenBookQA 和 RTE 上的性能显著下降。
Effect of topology.拓扑的影响。
SAC exhibits relatively small performance variation across topologies within the same benchmark. FAA differs by only a few percentage points across all five commonsense reasoning benchmarks. In contrast, CP-WBFT shows larger instability depending on the graph structure, particularly on ARC-Challenge, OpenBookQA, and RTE, where FAA varies substantially across topologies while remaining consistently lower than SAC. This relatively small across-topology variance of SAC is consistent with our theoretical analysis, where the qualitative robustness of SAC primarily depends on satisfying the $(F{+}1)$ -robustness condition rather than the specific topology construction.
在同一基准内，SAC 在不同拓扑之间的性能差异相对较小。在全部五个常识推理基准上，FAA 在不同拓扑之间的差异均仅为几个百分点。相比之下，CP-WBFT 随图结构的不同表现出更大的不稳定性，尤其是在 ARC-Challenge、OpenBookQA 和 RTE 上，其 FAA 在不同拓扑之间变化显著，且始终低于 SAC。SAC 在不同拓扑之间的这种相对较小的方差与我们的理论分析一致：SAC 的定性鲁棒性主要取决于是否满足 $(F{+}1)$-鲁棒性条件，而非具体的拓扑构造。
