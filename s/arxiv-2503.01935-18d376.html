Abstract摘要
Large Language Models (LLMs) have shown remarkable capabilities as autonomous agents; yet existing benchmarks either focus on single-agent tasks or are confined to narrow domains, failing to capture the dynamics of multi-agent coordination and competition. In this paper, we introduce MultiAgentBench, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate LLM-based multi-agent systems across diverse, interactive scenarios. Our framework measures not only task completion but also the quality of collaboration and competition using novel, milestone-based key performance indicators. Moreover, we evaluate various coordination protocols (including star, chain, tree, and graph topologies) and innovative strategies such as group discussion and cognitive planning. Notably, gpt-4o-mini reaches the average highest task score, graph structure performs the best among coordination protocols in the research scenario,and cognitive planning improves milestone achievement rates by 3%. Code and datasets are publicavailable at https://github.com/MultiagentBench/MARBLE.
大语言模型（LLMs）作为自主智能体展现出了卓越的能力；然而，现有基准要么专注于单智能体任务，要么局限于狭窄领域，未能捕捉多智能体协调与竞争的动态过程。本文提出 MultiAgentBench，这是一个综合性基准，旨在通过多样化的交互场景评估基于 LLM 的多智能体系统。我们的框架不仅衡量任务完成情况，还利用新颖的、基于里程碑的关键绩效指标来衡量协作与竞争的质量。此外，我们评估了多种协调协议（包括星形、链式、树形和图拓扑），以及小组讨论和认知规划等创新策略。值得注意的是，gpt-4o-mini 取得了最高的平均任务得分，图结构在研究场景的各类协调协议中表现最佳，而认知规划使里程碑达成率提高了 3%。代码和数据集已公开，地址为 https://github.com/MultiagentBench/MARBLE。
1 Introduction1 引言
Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-3 (Brown et al., 2020), GPT-4 (Achiam et al., 2023), Gemini (Team et al., 2023) and Deepsek-R1 (Guo et al., 2025), now exhibit human-like language understanding and generation, enabling their use as autonomous agents interacting with environments, tools, and other agents (Wang et al., 2023c; Park et al., 2023a; OpenAI, 2023). While single-agent systems have demonstrated impressive capabilities, their isolated operation can limit task execution efficiency and falls short when simulating complex, socially dynamic scenarios. In contrast, multi-agent setups (Li et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2023b; Wang et al., 2024b) leverage multiple LLM-based agents that collaborate, coordinate, and jointly plan to address these challenges. This paradigm not only increases the efficiency of task execution but also enables the realistic simulation of social interactions and dynamics, thereby enhancing performance in domains such as strategic decision-making, gaming (Silver et al., 2017), and software development (Wang et al., 2023c).

To address this gap, we introduce MultiAgentBench, a comprehensive benchmark designed to evaluate LLM-based multi-agent systems across a wide range of task-solving and simulation scenarios. MultiAgentBench offers several key advantages: (1) Multi-Domain Evaluation: The benchmark covers diverse domains—from collaborative coding to gaming—ensuring broad real-world applicability. (3) Tailored Metrics and Flexible Protocols: We propose novel metrics, including a Key Performance Indicator (KPI) that tracks milestone progress and individual contributions, to systematically assess planning quality and communication effectiveness. 2.2 Multi-Agent Collaboration2.2 多智能体协作
Recent advances in multi-agent systems highlight two complementary scaling paradigms: cognitive scaling, which enhances agent reasoning and adaptability, and population scaling, which leverages large agent collectives for emergent behaviors (Zhuge et al., ; Qian et al., 2024).
多智能体系统的最新进展凸显了两种互补的扩展范式：认知扩展，通过增强智能体的推理能力和适应能力实现扩展；群体扩展，通过利用大规模智能体群体产生涌现行为实现扩展 (Zhuge et al., ; Qian et al., 2024)。
Cognitive scaling explores mechanisms such as dynamic architecture adaptation and self-organizing coordination strategies to find the most effective pattern of agent communication (Zhuge et al., ). Meanwhile, population-based scaling exhibits nonlinear performance gains as an increasing number of agents collectively interact through diverse collaboration patterns, including hierarchical delegation and decentralized consensus (Qian et al., 2024). These approaches enable complex applications ranging from geopolitical conflict simulation (Hua et al., 2024) to scientific discovery workflows (Zhou et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2025).
认知扩展探索动态架构适配和自组织协调策略等机制，以寻找最有效的智能体通信模式 (Zhuge et al., )。与此同时，随着越来越多的智能体通过层级委派和去中心化共识等多样化协作模式进行群体交互，基于群体的扩展呈现出非线性的性能提升 (Qian et al., 2024)。这些方法支持了从地缘政治冲突模拟 (Hua et al., 2024) 到科学发现工作流 (Zhou et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2025) 等复杂应用。

At its core lies the Coordination Engine, responsible for initializing and synchronizing key modules—including the Agent Graph, Cognitive Module, and Coordinate Engine—to ensure seamless interaction across the system. 我们提出的评估框架 MARBLE（见图 2）利用相互连接的模块构建了一个稳健的多智能体协调系统，实现自适应协作、高效通信和策略性任务执行。其核心是协调引擎（Coordination Engine），负责初始化和同步关键模块，包括智能体图、认知模块和协作引擎（Coordinate Engine），以确保整个系统中的无缝交互。其他模块的详细说明见附录 A.2。
Agent Graph Module智能体图模块
This module converts configuration data into a structured graph $G=(\mathcal{A},E)$ , where $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n}\}$ denotes the set of agents, and each edge in $E$ is defined as a triple $(a_{i},r,a_{j})$ with $r\in\mathcal{R}$ representing the relationship between agents $a_{i}$ and $a_{j}$ . For example, a collaboration relationship is denoted as $(a_{i},\texttt{collaborates},a_{j})$ , supervision as $(a_{i},\texttt{supervises},a_{j})$ , and negotiation as $(a_{i},\texttt{negotiates},a_{j})$ . By constructing the graph based on these triple relations, we ensure that subsequent communication and coordination occur exclusively between agents with explicitly defined relationships, mirroring real-world interaction patterns.
该模块将配置数据转换为结构化图 $G=(\mathcal{A},E)$，其中 $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},a_{2},\dots,a_{n}\}$ 表示智能体集合，$E$ 中的每条边被定义为三元组 $(a_{i},r,a_{j})$，其中 $r\in\mathcal{R}$ 表示智能体 $a_{i}$ 与 $a_{j}$ 之间的关系。例如，协作关系表示为 $(a_{i},\texttt{collaborates},a_{j})$，监督关系表示为 $(a_{i},\texttt{supervises},a_{j})$，协商关系表示为 $(a_{i},\texttt{negotiates},a_{j})$。通过基于这些三元组关系构建图，我们确保后续通信与协调仅发生在具有明确定义关系的智能体之间，从而反映现实世界中的交互模式。
Cognitive Module认知模块
The Cognitive Module is central to responsible agent evolution and social intelligence in multi-agent coordination. It maintains and updates a comprehensive internal state that includes each agent’s persona, inter-agent relationships, and reasoning strategies (e.g., Chain-of-Thought (Wei et al., 2023), ReACT (Yao et al., 2023)). Crucially, this approach mirrors human cognitive processes by fusing elements of theory-of-mind and social intelligence (Premack and Woodruff, 1978, e.g.,)—similar to how humans continuously update their mental models based on social cues, prior experiences, and contextual information. The fusion of cognitive, social, and adaptive mechanisms forms the backbone of our system, empowering agents to dynamically adjust their strategies and collaboratively evolve in complex environments.
认知模块是多智能体协调中负责任的智能体演化与社会智能的核心。它维护并更新一个全面的内部状态，其中包括每个智能体的角色设定、智能体间关系以及推理策略（例如思维链（Chain-of-Thought）(Wei et al., 2023)、ReACT (Yao et al., 2023)）。关键在于，该方法通过融合心理理论与社会智能的要素 (Premack and Woodruff, 1978, e.g.,)，模拟人类的认知过程——类似于人类根据社会线索、既往经验和上下文信息不断更新自身心智模型的方式。认知、社会与适应机制的融合构成了我们系统的支柱，使智能体能够在复杂环境中动态调整策略并协同演化。

3.1.1 Coordination Engine3.1.1 协调引擎
The Coordination Engine orchestrates the overall execution flow of the system. It initializes agents, tasks, and inter-agent relationships via a dedicated Configuration Module and constructs the Agent Graph to represent these dynamics. In our framework, we distinguish between two key roles: planners and actors. Planners are responsible for devising task inputs, strategizing, and managing overall task allocation, while actors—represented within the Agent Graph—execute tasks by interacting with the environment and other agents through available tools.
协调引擎（Coordination Engine）统筹系统的整体执行流程。它通过专门的配置模块（Configuration Module）初始化智能体、任务及智能体之间的关系，并构建智能体图（Agent Graph）来表示这些动态关系。在我们的框架中，我们区分了两种关键角色：规划者和执行者。规划者负责设计任务输入、制定策略并管理整体任务分配，而执行者在智能体图中表示，并通过可用工具与环境及其他智能体交互来执行任务。
Our approach supports four distinct coordination protocols similar to work from Qian et al. (2025): star, tree, graph, and chain.
我们的方法支持四种不同的协调协议，与 Qian et al. (2025) 的工作类似：星型、树型、图型和链型。
Centralized Coordination: Star & Tree.集中式协调：星型与树型。
In the star configuration, a single central planner assigns tasks to all actors and consolidates their feedback, offering strong oversight though potentially limiting scalability. The tree structure extends this by organizing agents hierarchically: a top-level planner delegates tasks to subordinate planners, which in turn coordinate with actors. This hierarchical approach balances centralized control with improved scalability for handling more complex tasks.
在星型配置中，单个中央规划者向所有执行者分配任务并汇总其反馈，能够提供强有力的监督，但可能限制可扩展性。树型结构在此基础上，将智能体按层级组织起来：顶层规划者将任务委派给下级规划者，后者再与执行者进行协调。这种层级式方法兼顾了集中控制与更好的可扩展性，从而能够处理更复杂的任务。
Decentralized Coordination: Graph-Mesh & Chain.去中心化协调：网状图型与链型。
The graph-mesh configuration employs a network of interconnected actors that communicate directly, enabling concurrent planning and distributed decision-making. Conversely, the chain configuration arranges actors sequentially, where each agent passes its decision to the next. This sequential handoff is well-suited for tasks with inherent dependencies, though it may limit parallel processing capabilities.
网状图型配置采用由相互连接的执行者组成的网络，执行者之间直接通信，从而实现并发规划与分布式决策。相比之下，链型配置将执行者按顺序排列，每个智能体将其决策传递给下一个智能体。这种顺序交接方式非常适合具有内在依赖关系的任务，但可能限制并行处理能力。
Planner Design and Enhancements.规划者的设计与增强。
In our centralized coordination protocol, the planner supports four distinct planning approaches that reflect human decision-making processes: vanilla prompting, chain-of-thought (CoT) (Wei et al., 2022), group discussion, and cognitive self-evolving planning. The vanilla prompt employs a straightforward, zero-shot instruction to generate task plans directly. The CoT approach enriches this process by facilitating step-by-step reasoning through detailed inputs—such as the target task, agent profiles (including roles, expertise, and historical performance), and summaries of previous subtasks—to guide logical progression. The group discussion (Chen et al., 2023b) method enables multiple agents to share insights and constraints, fostering a collaborative deliberation that refines the overall plan. Lastly, similar to the Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023) method, our cognitive self-evolving planning method mirrors human learning by generating expected outcomes and progress for each task, storing these in memory, and then comparing actual performance against these expectations in subsequent iterations. This comparison produces evolving experiences that continuously inform and adjust future planning (See Appendix A.12 for detailed prompting). Together, these methods leverage both individual reasoning and collaborative optimization, enhancing coordination efficiency as validated through ablation studies on the star coordination style.
在我们的集中式协调协议中，规划者支持四种体现人类决策过程的不同规划方法：基础提示（vanilla prompting）、思维链（chain-of-thought，CoT）(Wei et al., 2022)、小组讨论和认知自演化规划。基础提示采用直接明了的零样本指令，直接生成任务计划。CoT 方法通过详细输入来促进逐步推理，以增强这一过程；这些输入包括目标任务、智能体档案（涵盖角色、专长和历史表现）以及先前子任务的摘要，用于引导推理按逻辑推进。小组讨论方法 (Chen et al., 2023b) 使多个智能体能够分享见解与约束条件，促进协作式审议，从而完善整体计划。最后，与 Reflexion (Shinn et al., 2023) 方法类似，我们的认知自演化规划方法通过为每项任务生成预期结果和进展、将其存储在记忆中，并在后续迭代中将实际表现与这些预期进行比较，来模拟人类学习。这种比较产生不断演化的经验，持续为未来的规划提供依据并对其进行调整（详细提示见附录 A.12）。这些方法共同利用个体推理与协作优化，提高了协调效率，这一点已通过针对星型协调方式的消融研究得到验证。

To systematically evaluate our multi-agent framework, we curate a benchmark of diverse scenarios spanning task-oriented and social-simulation-based environments (Figure 1). These scenarios are constructed through a combination of: (1) Existing multi-agent tasks adapted from prior work or datasets (e.g., database error analysis, research collaboration). (2) LLM-generated tasks with human verification and refinement (e.g., Werewolf and Bargaining). This dual approach ensures both realism (by leveraging established tasks) and novelty (through generative expansion), while human validation guarantees that each scenario remains coherent and feasible.
为系统评估我们的多智能体框架，我们构建了一个涵盖多种场景的基准，覆盖面向任务的环境与基于社会模拟的环境（图 1）。这些场景结合以下方式构建：(1) 从既有工作或数据集中改编的现有多智能体任务（例如数据库错误分析、科研协作）。(2) 由 LLM 生成并经人工验证与完善的任务（例如狼人杀和议价）。这种双重方法既通过利用成熟任务确保真实性，又通过生成式扩展确保新颖性，同时人工验证保证每个场景保持连贯且切实可行。
Agents with Mutual Goal.具有共同目标的智能体。
In the task-oriented scenarios, the agents share with the mutual goal to finish one specific task. We focus on four representative tasks: (1) research tasks follow the setup of ResearchTown Yu et al. (2024a), where agents with complementary research profiles co-author a new proposal on a chosen topic; (2) Minecraft-based building tasks require agents to collaboratively construct structures in a shared environment; (3) database error analysis involves exactly five agents, each specializing in diagnosing a distinct root cause of system inconsistencies; (4) coding challenges demand collective problem-solving and software module development. Across these tasks, agents must coordinate, divide labor, and synthesize outputs efficiently. We scale scenario diversity by creating 100 test cases per task, with variations in research topics, Minecraft creation, database errors, or coding objectives.
在面向任务的场景中，智能体以完成某一特定任务为共同目标。我们重点关注四类代表性任务：(1) 科研任务遵循 ResearchTown Yu et al. (2024a) 的设置，由研究背景互补的智能体围绕选定主题共同撰写一份新的研究提案；(2) 基于 Minecraft 的建造任务要求智能体在共享环境中协作建造结构；(3) 数据库错误分析恰好涉及五个智能体，每个智能体专门诊断导致系统不一致的一种不同根本原因；(4) 编程挑战要求共同解决问题并开发软件模块。在这些任务中，智能体必须高效地协调、分工并整合输出。我们为每类任务创建 100 个测试用例，通过改变研究主题、Minecraft 建造内容、数据库错误或编程目标来扩大场景的多样性。

In social-simulation based scenarios. We strengthen competitive elements by introducing Werewolf and Bargaining scenarios. In Werewolf, two groups of agents face off in an adversarial setting, employing deceptive strategies within a predefined narrative. The Bargaining environment simulates negotiations over shared resources, where agents strive to maximize individual gains through strategic concessions or alliances. Both settings evaluate adaptability, conflict resolution, and negotiation skills under uncertainty.
在基于社会模拟的场景中。我们通过引入狼人杀和议价场景来增强竞争要素。在 Werewolf 中，两组智能体在对抗性环境中展开交锋，在预设的叙事框架内运用欺骗策略。Bargaining 环境模拟围绕共享资源展开的谈判，智能体通过策略性让步或结盟，力求最大化各自的收益。这两种环境均评估不确定条件下的适应能力、冲突解决能力和谈判技巧。
Role Assignments and Graph Structures.角色分配与图结构。
To emphasize multi-agent collaboration, each scenario enforces distinct agent roles (e.g., project manager, domain expert, technical specialist) and defines specific graph relationships (star, tree, chain, or mesh). These structures reflect realistic team dynamics or competition, guiding how agents share information, make decisions, and coordinate actions.
为突出多智能体协作，每个场景都规定了不同的智能体角色（例如项目经理、领域专家、技术专家），并定义了特定的图关系（星形、树形、链形或网状）。这些结构反映了现实中的团队互动或竞争，指导智能体如何共享信息、作出决策和协调行动。

To facilitate the evaluation of MARBLE iterations, each task is segmented into a series of flexible milestones. Unlike rigid checkpoints, these milestones are broadly defined. For instance, in a research task, a milestone may be reached by completing five key queries (5q) for research proposal (more details see Appendix A.4) or by enhancing a previous set of 5q. Throughout MARBLE’s iterative process, a language model continuously monitors whether milestones $m_{1},m_{2},\dots$ have been achieved and logs the outcomes. This method integrates human- or LLM-generated outlines with dynamic, execution-based assessments, ensuring that both intermediate progress and team coordination are effectively measured.
为便于评估 MARBLE 的迭代过程，每项任务都被划分为一系列灵活的里程碑。与严格的检查点不同，这些里程碑的定义较为宽泛。例如，在研究任务中，可以通过完成研究计划的五个关键问题（5q，更多细节见附录 A.4），或完善已有的一组 5q，来达成一个里程碑。在 MARBLE 的整个迭代过程中，语言模型持续监测里程碑 $m_{1},m_{2},\dots$ 是否已达成，并记录结果。该方法将人工或 LLM 生成的提纲与基于执行情况的动态评估相结合，确保能够有效衡量中间进展和团队协调情况。

3.3 Evaluation Metrics3.3 评估指标
As illustrated in Figure 1(b)(c) , our evaluation considers two primary dimensions: Task Completion Performance and Coordination.
如图 1(b)(c) 所示，我们的评估考虑两个主要维度：任务完成表现和协调能力。
Task Completion Metrics.任务完成指标。
As described in Section 3.2, each task is segmented into a series of flexible milestones. An LLM-based detector continuously monitors the iterative process to identify which milestones have been achieved and records the corresponding contributing agents. For each agent, the number of milestones they contribute to is noted as $n_{j}$ , and their individual KPI is calculated as the ratio of $n_{j}$ to the total number of milestones $M$ . The overall KPI is defined as the average of these individual KPIs across all $N$ agents, which is computed as follows:
如第 3.2 节所述，每项任务都被划分为一系列灵活的里程碑。基于 LLM 的检测器持续监测迭代过程，识别已达成的里程碑，并记录为其作出贡献的相应智能体。对于每个智能体，将其参与贡献的里程碑数量记为 $n_{j}$，其个体 KPI 计算为 $n_{j}$ 与里程碑总数 $M$ 的比值。总体 KPI 定义为全部 $N$ 个智能体的个体 KPI 的平均值，计算如下：
$$\text{KPI}_{\text{overall}}=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N}\text{KPI}_{j}=\frac{1}{NM}\sum_{j=1}^{N}n_{j}.$$
In addition to the KPI derived from milestone detection, a separate task-based score is computed to evaluate the final output quality. For tasks such as research or bargaining, an LLM-defined scoring rubric is applied to generate the score, whereas tasks like Minecraft, Werewolf, database error fixes, or coding are evaluated using rule-based metrics (e.g., accuracy). Detailed scoring criteria and evaluation prompts for these task-based assessments are respectively provided in the Appendix A.9, A.5, A.6, and A.7, which demonstrate the effectiveness of the metrics while evaluating the coordination abilities.
除通过里程碑检测得到的 KPI 外，还单独计算基于任务的得分，以评估最终输出的质量。对于研究或谈判等任务，采用由 LLM 定义的评分细则生成得分；而 Minecraft、Werewolf、数据库错误修复或编程等任务则使用基于规则的指标（例如准确率）进行评估。这些基于任务的评估所采用的详细评分标准和评估提示词分别见附录 A.9、A.5、A.6 和 A.7，它们在评估协调能力的同时，也展示了这些指标的有效性。
Coordination Metrics.协调指标。
Coordination is evaluated by quantifying the agents’ communication and planning capabilities. The Communication Score ( $C_{\text{score}}$ ) is derived from an LLM-based evaluation that considers inputs such as the task description, agent profiles, and aggregated communication data, resulting in a score on a five-point scale (with $C_{\text{score}}=0$ if no communication occurs). Similarly, the Planning Score ( $P_{\text{score}}$ ) is determined by assessing the agents’ abilities to organize tasks, maintain roles, and adapt strategies based on their profiles and aggregated planning data, also on a five-point scale. The overall Coordination Score (CS) is then computed by averaging these two sub-scores. More details regarding the evaluation process and the output format are provided in the Appendix A.12. We also did a human evaluation comparing human alignment with those metrics, results are in Appendix A.3.
通过量化智能体的沟通和规划能力来评估协调能力。沟通得分（$C_{\text{score}}$）通过基于 LLM 的评估获得，该评估考虑任务描述、智能体档案以及汇总的沟通数据等输入，并采用五分制评分（若未发生沟通，则 $C_{\text{score}}=0$）。类似地，规划得分（$P_{\text{score}}$）根据智能体档案和汇总的规划数据，通过评估智能体组织任务、维持角色和调整策略的能力来确定，同样采用五分制。随后，将这两项子得分取平均值，得到总体协调得分（CS）。有关评估过程和输出格式的更多细节见附录 A.12。我们还开展了人工评估，以考察这些指标与人类判断的一致性，结果见附录 A.3。

The goal of our ablation study is to identify the key modules and parameters that affect performance.

Ablation on Different Max Iteration Settings不同最大迭代次数设置的消融实验
We evaluate 10 tasks from the Minecraft scenario using six distinct maximum iteration settings. As shown in Fig. 7, both task and coordination scores increase from 1 to 7 iterations, but then drop sharply at 10 iterations. At 20 iterations, while the task score shows a recovery, the coordination score remains nearly unchanged beyond 7 iterations. 7 Conclusion7 结论
In this work, we introduce MultiAgentBench and the MARBLE framework, providing a comprehensive benchmark for evaluating LLM-based multi-agent systems across diverse interactive scenarios. Our proposed evaluation metrics go beyond task success, capturing coordination quality through structured planning, communication scores, and competition-driven assessments. Experimental results highlight key emergent social behaviors, offering valuable insights into future multiagent work.
在本研究中，我们提出了 MultiAgentBench 和 MARBLE 框架，为评估基于 LLM 的多智能体系统在多种交互场景中的表现提供了全面的基准。我们提出的评估指标超越了任务成功与否，通过结构化规划、通信得分和竞争驱动的评估来衡量协调质量。实验结果揭示了关键的涌现社会行为，为未来的多智能体研究提供了有价值的见解。
8 Limitations8 局限性
While our proposed multi-agent benchmark and framework provide a diverse range of tasks and evaluation metrics, several areas warrant further exploration to enhance their applicability and robustness:
尽管我们提出的多智能体基准和框架提供了多样化的任务与评估指标，但仍有若干方面值得进一步探索，以提高其适用性和稳健性：
Expanding Scenario and Model Coverage.扩大场景和模型的覆盖范围。
Currently, our benchmark focuses on specific domains such as research co-authoring, Minecraft building, database error analysis, coding collaboration, and select competitive scenarios (e.g., Werewolf and bargaining). To better capture the complexity of real-world multi-agent interactions, future work can incorporate more diverse settings, including open-world environments, scenarios requiring richer social cognition, and tasks on the application side like task-oriented dialogues. In the aspect of models, our work does not cover the full spectrum. Future work may include the result of other latest ones (e.g. the DeepSeek model family).
目前，我们的基准主要关注特定领域，包括研究论文合著、Minecraft 建造、数据库错误分析、协作编程，以及部分竞争性场景（例如狼人杀和议价）。为更好地反映现实世界中多智能体交互的复杂性，未来的研究可以纳入更多样化的情境，包括开放世界环境、需要更丰富社会认知的场景，以及任务型对话等应用层面的任务。在模型方面，我们的研究尚未覆盖所有类型。未来的研究可以纳入其他最新模型（例如 DeepSeek 模型系列）的结果。
Enhancing Ablation Studies.加强消融研究。
Our current analysis focuses primarily on overall coordination and competition performance, leaving finer-grained insights into specific components underexplored. Future experiments can be focused on different memory mechanisms (e.g. long-term memory, short-term memory, shared memory) and multiagent different workflow method.
我们目前的分析主要关注整体协调与竞争表现，对具体组件的细粒度洞见仍缺乏深入探索。未来实验可重点研究不同的记忆机制（例如长期记忆、短期记忆、共享记忆）以及不同的多智能体工作流方法。
Advancing Competition Mechanisms.推进竞争机制的发展。
While our benchmark incorporates competitive tasks, it does not fully capture the complexity of real-world multi-agent interactions involving multi-party negotiations, repeated strategic play, or stochastic elements. Investigating how agents transition between cooperative and adversarial roles in evolving environments remains a promising direction.
尽管我们的基准包含竞争性任务，但它尚未充分涵盖涉及多方谈判、重复策略博弈或随机因素的现实世界多智能体交互的复杂性。研究智能体如何在不断演变的环境中在合作角色与对抗角色之间转换，仍是一个有前景的方向。
Handling Open-Ended and Ill-Defined Tasks.处理开放式和定义不明确的任务。
Most tasks in our framework involve well-defined objectives, such as completing a research proposal or resolving database inconsistencies. However, real-world applications often require agents to operate in open-ended or ambiguous contexts without clear success criteria. Future extensions could explore how multi-agent systems adapt to exploratory, non-goal-oriented scenarios.
我们框架中的大多数任务都有明确的目标，例如完成研究计划书或解决数据库中的不一致问题。然而，现实应用往往要求智能体在缺乏明确成功标准的开放式或模糊情境中运作。未来的扩展可以探索多智能体系统如何适应探索性的、非目标导向的场景。

Initializes and parameterizes the system by ingesting task specifications, persona data, agent profiles, role definitions, and domain-specific databases. It constructs agent attributes ${(A_{i},P_{i})}_{i=1}^{N}$ , where $A_{i}$ is the $i$ -th agent and $P_{i}$ its profile encompassing capabilities, constraints, and personality traits. $$a_{t}=\pi(A_{t-1},M_{\text{shared}},M_{\text{individual}}^{i})$$
$$\quad o_{t+1}=\text{Env}(a_{t}),$$

从而促进智能体与环境之间的持续交互。专用工具箱提供代码编辑器和搜索引擎等领域专用功能。
Memory Module记忆模块
Stores and retrieves information through shared and individual memories:
通过共享记忆和个体记忆存储与检索信息：
$$M=\{M_{\text{shared}},M_{\text{individual}}^{i}:i=1,\dots,N\}.$$
$M_{\text{shared}}$ holds global knowledge and collective decisions, while each $M_{\text{individual}}^{i}$ maintains personal experiences and local observations. $M_{\text{shared}}$ 保存全局知识和集体决策，而每个 $M_{\text{individual}}^{i}$ 则保存个人经历和局部观察。个体记忆分为长期和短期两部分，通过先进先出（FIFO）机制进行管理，使短期记忆保持在阈值范围内。检索增强生成（RAG）技术支持动态访问知识，从而优化提示词构建。

The Communication Module manages external interactions among agents. By supporting structured dialogue and information sharing, this module enables agents to negotiate roles, coordinate plans, and balance collaborative efforts with competitive interactions.
通信模块负责管理智能体之间的外部交互。它为每个智能体配备一套通信工具，并维护其他智能体的详细档案，从而促进能够感知上下文的交流。通过支持结构化对话和信息共享，该模块使智能体能够协商角色、协调计划，并平衡协作与竞争性交互。
Action Module行动模块
The Action Module executes the plans generated by agents and leverages both function-calling mechanisms and structured output formats to obtain final results. 行动模块执行智能体生成的计划，并利用函数调用机制和结构化输出格式获取最终结果。智能体执行行动时，结果和观察信息会立即反馈至个体记忆库和共享记忆库。这一迭代循环使智能体能够动态适应不断变化的任务要求，并随着时间的推移进一步优化其策略。

A.5 Werewolf EnvironmentA.5 狼人杀环境
A.5.1 Environment Description (Tool Description)A.5.1 环境描述（工具描述）
The Werewolf environment, inspired by the classic social deduction game Werewolf (a.k.a. Mafia), provides a rich, socially complex setting in which players (agents) belong to opposing factions with asymmetric information and objectives. This scenario is particularly suitable for evaluating LLM-driven multi-agent coordination under uncertainty, as it involves hidden roles, deception, collective inference, and iterative decision-making. It challenges agents’ logical reasoning as well as their aptitude for persuasion, alliance formation, adaptive responses to changing conditions, and balancing between individual interests and group goals.
狼人杀环境的灵感源于经典的社交推理游戏狼人杀（又称黑手党），提供了一个丰富且具有复杂社会互动的场景，其中玩家（智能体）分属对立阵营，掌握的信息和追求的目标具有不对称性。该场景尤其适合评估由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的多智能体在不确定条件下的协作能力，因为它涉及隐藏角色、欺骗、集体推理和迭代决策。它既考验智能体的逻辑推理能力，也考验其说服、结盟、适应条件变化以及平衡个人利益与群体目标的能力。
Why Werewolf?为什么选择狼人杀？
In this environment, agents are divided into two main factions: the Villager group (including special roles such as Seer, Witch, and Guard) and the Werewolf group. Villagers seek to identify and eliminate all werewolves, while werewolves aim to blend in and secretly eliminate villagers. The day/night cycle establishes a repetitive structure of public discussions, secret actions, and voting decisions. This setup offers several advantages:
在这一环境中，智能体分为两大阵营：村民阵营（包括预言家、女巫和守卫等特殊角色）和狼人阵营。村民力求识别并淘汰所有狼人，而狼人则试图混入村民之中，暗中淘汰村民。昼夜循环构成了由公开讨论、秘密行动和投票决策组成的重复结构。这一设置具有以下几个优点：
Role Asymmetry and Hidden Information: Villagers lack complete knowledge, while werewolves know their allies. This information asymmetry encourages strategic reasoning, suspicion, and bluffing.
角色不对称与隐藏信息：村民掌握的信息不完整，而狼人知道自己的盟友是谁。这种信息不对称促使参与者进行策略推理、相互怀疑并实施诈唬。
Complex Social Reasoning: Success hinges on persuasion, alliance building, and careful information management. Agents must form and break trusts, share or withhold information, and achieve consensus on who should be removed.
复杂的社会推理：成功取决于说服、建立联盟以及审慎的信息管理。智能体必须建立和打破信任关系，分享或隐瞒信息，并就应当淘汰谁达成共识。
Adaptation and Memory: As the game progresses through multiple cycles, agents must update their beliefs based on observed behaviors. Long-term memory supports tracking agent states, past actions, and evolving contexts.
适应与记忆：随着游戏经历多个循环，智能体必须根据观察到的行为更新自己的信念。长期记忆有助于追踪智能体的状态、过去的行动以及不断变化的情境。
Evaluation of Cooperative Dynamics: Well-defined scoring rules for correct identifications, effective protections, and consensus-building enable objective assessment of strategic teamwork and collaborative problem-solving.
协作动态评估：针对正确识别、有效保护和共识建立而制定的明确评分规则，使我们能够客观评估策略性团队合作与协作解决问题的能力。
A.5.2 Villager-Centric Scoring RationaleA.5.2 以村民为中心的评分依据
In this environment, we primarily focus on evaluating the villager faction rather than the werewolf faction. The core reason is that villagers rely heavily on explicit cooperative actions to secure victory: for instance, the Guard must accurately protect key roles, the Witch must judiciously use antidote and poison, and the Seer must disclose or share critical information, either publicly or privately, to identify suspects. These actions inherently demand communication and coordination among villager members, grounded in a degree of mutual trust and collaborative strategy. Without such synergy, villagers are typically overrun by the werewolves.
在这一环境中，我们主要评估村民阵营，而非狼人阵营。核心原因在于，村民高度依赖明确的协作行动来取得胜利：例如，守卫必须准确保护关键角色，女巫必须审慎使用解药和毒药，预言家则必须公开或私下披露或分享关键信息，以识别可疑对象。这些行动本身就要求村民成员在一定程度的相互信任和协作策略基础上进行沟通与协调。缺乏这种协同配合，村民通常会被狼人击败。
Moreover, the number and quality of these cooperative efforts correlate with the villagers’ overall chance of success. More effective teamwork enables stronger reasoning, better defense, and a higher likelihood of identifying and eliminating werewolves or safeguarding vital roles. By observing and measuring these cooperative maneuvers—such as successful protection by the Guard, timely use of antidotes, or coordinated voting—we gain deeper insights into how the model performs in social reasoning and collaboration within a complex environment.
此外，这些协作努力的数量和质量与村民的整体获胜概率相关。更有效的团队合作能够增强推理能力、改善防御，并提高识别和淘汰狼人或保护关键角色的可能性。通过观察和衡量这些协作行动，例如守卫成功保护目标、及时使用解药或协调投票，我们能够更深入地了解模型在复杂环境中的社会推理与协作表现。
In contrast, the success of werewolves does not hinge as strongly on explicit teamwork. Even if they operate mostly on an individual basis and refrain from overt collaboration, werewolves can still achieve a relatively high chance of winning through misdirection and exploiting confusion among villagers. Consequently, measuring werewolf-side cooperation does not provide as discriminative or illuminating an assessment of collaborative potential as evaluating the villagers’ side.
相比之下，狼人的成功对明确的团队合作的依赖程度较低。即使狼人主要各自行动，不进行公开协作，也仍然可以通过误导村民并利用村民内部的混乱，获得相对较高的获胜概率。因此，与评估村民一方相比，衡量狼人一方的合作情况，对协作潜力的评估所提供的区分度和启示性都较低。
Therefore, we concentrate on the villager perspective to better capture and evaluate the synergy required in a highly uncertain, adversarial setting. This design choice highlights how cooperation, or the lack thereof, strongly influences the villagers’ outcome, offering a direct lens through which to assess the social and strategic capabilities of large language model agents.
因此，我们聚焦于村民视角，以更好地捕捉和评估高度不确定的对抗性情境中所需的协同配合。这一设计选择凸显了合作或合作的缺失如何强烈影响村民的最终结果，从而为评估大语言模型智能体的社会能力与策略能力提供了直接视角。
Consistent Werewolf Model.固定的狼人模型。
In all experiments where we vary the villager-side language model, the werewolf side remains consistently powered by GPT-4o. This ensures a stable, challenging adversary and allows us to fairly compare different villager models under identical opposing conditions.
在所有改变村民一方语言模型的实验中，狼人一方始终由 GPT-4o 驱动。这确保了对手的稳定性和挑战性，并使我们能够在相同的对手条件下公平比较不同的村民模型。
A.5.3 Benchmark Curation DetailsA.5.3 基准构建细节
Initialization.初始化。
Unlike other environments that rely on numerous parameterized tasks, the Werewolf game commences from a single, stable initial configuration. We tried multiple role distributions and settled on a balanced default setup to maintain fairness and avoid biasing the game toward any faction. Subsequent variations arise naturally from agent interactions, rather than from altering initial conditions. Agents are assigned roles such as wolf, villager, seer, witch, and guard, each with corresponding capabilities. For example, werewolves coordinate attacks at night, and the seer checks a player’s identity.
与依赖大量参数化任务的其他环境不同，狼人杀游戏从单一且稳定的初始配置开始。我们尝试了多种角色分配方案，最终选定了一种均衡的默认设置，以维持公平性，避免游戏偏向任何阵营。后续变化自然产生于智能体之间的互动，而非初始条件的改变。智能体被分配狼人、村民、预言家、女巫和守卫等角色，每种角色都具备相应的能力。例如，狼人在夜间协调袭击，预言家则查验一名玩家的身份。
Event Bus and Action Processing.事件总线与行动处理。
This environment adheres to a strict, environment-mediated communication protocol. Unlike other settings where agents may directly interact, here all messages pass through the environment. The environment publishes events like “night start,” “seer action,” or “vote action” following the standard Werewolf procedure, and agents respond accordingly. The environment then relays these responses to other agents at the appropriate time. This ensures a controlled, linear information flow that respects the official Werewolf rules and prevents unauthorized agent-to-agent exchanges.
这一环境遵循严格的、由环境中介的通信协议。与智能体可以直接交互的其他设置不同，这里的所有消息都经过环境传递。环境按照标准狼人杀流程发布“夜晚开始”“预言家行动”或“投票行动”等事件，智能体据此作出响应。随后，环境会在适当的时间将这些响应转发给其他智能体。这确保了受控的线性信息流，既遵守狼人杀的官方规则，又防止未经授权的智能体间交流。
Memory and Logging.记忆与日志记录。
A record of events from each agent’s perspective is maintained to enable reasoning over multiple rounds and post-game analysis. Each agent’s private event log and final decisions are stored, allowing for reproducibility and subsequent scoring. While other environments may have more complex shared memory structures, here we focus on recording essential information to understand each agent’s decision process.
系统会保留从各智能体视角记录的事件，以支持跨多轮推理和赛后分析。每个智能体的私有事件日志和最终决策都会被存储，以支持结果复现和后续评分。尽管其他环境可能具有更复杂的共享记忆结构，但这里我们侧重于记录理解每个智能体决策过程所需的关键信息。
Game Flow and Termination.游戏流程与终止。
The environment enforces the standard Werewolf game flow:
环境强制执行标准的狼人杀游戏流程：
Night phase: Special roles act secretly—guards protect, werewolves choose a victim, the seer inspects a player, and the witch may use antidote or poison.
夜晚阶段：特殊角色秘密行动——守卫进行守护，狼人选择袭击目标，预言家查验一名玩家，女巫可以使用解药或毒药。
Day phase: Night results are revealed, deceased players are removed, and if the sheriff (a special role) died, the badge is reassigned. Agents discuss and vote on a suspect to eliminate.
白天阶段：公布夜晚结果，死亡玩家出局；如果警长（一种特殊角色）死亡，则重新移交警徽。智能体进行讨论，并投票放逐一名嫌疑玩家。
The game ends if all werewolves are dead (villager victory) or if all villagers are dead (werewolf victory). Scores reflect survival, successful actions (e.g., correct identifications, effective protections), and communication quality.
当所有狼人死亡（村民获胜）或所有村民死亡（狼人获胜）时，游戏结束。得分反映存活情况、成功行动（例如正确识别身份、有效守护）以及沟通质量。
100 Archives, Partial-Day Simulations, and Full-Game Simulations.100 份存档、部分日程模拟与完整对局模拟。
To gain deeper insights into how different agent strategies unfold, we prepared 100 distinct archives (saved game states) showcasing various configurations of werewolf and villager actions, all played by GPT-4o-based agents. These archives are used in two experimental modes:
为深入了解不同智能体策略的展开过程，我们准备了 100 份不同的存档（已保存的游戏状态），展示狼人和村民行动的多种配置，所有对局均由基于 GPT-4o 的智能体进行。这些存档用于两种实验模式：
Partial-Day Simulation (Single-Day): The environment loads a saved state from a specific night (e.g., Night 0, Night 1, Night 2, etc.), then simulates exactly one day-night cycle. During this cycle, the environment issues multiple tasks to the villager side (e.g., “exile a suspected werewolf,” “protect the seer,” “use poison on a werewolf,” “save a threatened villager”). At the end of the day phase, we measure how many of these tasks were successfully completed. Higher task completion indicates that the villagers are closer to winning.
部分日程模拟（单日）：环境加载某个特定夜晚（例如 Night 0、Night 1、Night 2 等）的已保存状态，然后恰好模拟一个昼夜周期。在此周期内，环境向村民阵营下达多项任务（例如“放逐一名疑似狼人”“保护预言家”“对一名狼人使用毒药”“救下一名受到威胁的村民”）。在白天阶段结束时，我们统计这些任务中有多少项已成功完成。任务完成度越高，表明村民距离获胜越近。
Full-Game Simulation (Entire Match): The environment starts from the archive representing the end of the first night (Night 0) and runs the entire game through to conclusion. In this mode, tasks are given only as suggestions to the villagers (e.g., “we recommend trying to confirm the seer’s identity”), but we do not track partial completion. Instead, we evaluate the overall process score (i.e., collaboration and coordination quality) and the final result (which faction wins). By observing agent interactions over multiple days and nights, we gain insights into their long-horizon planning and dynamic cooperation.
完整对局模拟（整场比赛）：环境从首夜（Night 0）结束时的存档开始，运行整场游戏直至结束。在此模式下，任务仅作为建议提供给村民（例如“我们建议尝试确认预言家的身份”），但我们不跟踪部分完成情况。我们转而评估整体过程得分（即协作与协调质量）和最终结果（哪个阵营获胜）。通过观察智能体在多个昼夜中的互动，我们能够深入了解其长期规划和动态合作能力。
Result Score.结果得分。
At the end of each full-game simulation, we record a result score defined as the difference between the number of surviving villagers and the number of surviving werewolves. A higher result score indicates that villagers finished the game with more players alive, whereas a negative result score means that werewolves outnumbered the villagers at the conclusion of the match.
每次完整对局模拟结束时，我们记录结果得分，其定义为存活村民数量与存活狼人数量之差。结果得分越高，表明游戏结束时存活的村民越多；结果得分为负则意味着比赛结束时狼人的数量超过村民。
A.5.4 Task Completion Metrics DetailsA.5.4 任务完成度指标详情
Daily Tasks in Partial-Day Simulations.部分日程模拟中的每日任务。
In the Partial-Day Simulation mode, the environment generates specific tasks for the villagers at the start of each single-day session. These tasks reflect high-value objectives that, if fulfilled by the end of the current day-night cycle, bring the villagers closer to victory. Unlike the comprehensive scoring system used for full-match evaluations (detailed below), these daily tasks focus on the shorter horizon of a single day.
在部分日程模拟模式下，环境会在每次单日模拟开始时为村民生成具体任务。这些任务体现了高价值目标，若能在当前昼夜周期结束前完成，就能使村民更接近胜利。与完整对局评估所使用的综合评分体系（详见下文）不同，这些每日任务关注单日这一较短的时间跨度。
We design four primary tasks, each with its own conditions, goals, and rewards:
我们设计了四项主要任务，每项任务都有各自的条件、目标和奖励：
Protect the Seer Condition: The seer is still alive at the start of the day. Goal: Ensure that the seer remains alive by the end of this day-night cycle. Reward: +1 point. (This task is persistently listed as long as the seer is alive, to underscore the importance of protecting a vital role.)
保护预言家 条件：白天开始时，预言家仍然存活。目标：确保预言家在本昼夜周期结束时仍然存活。奖励：+1 分。（只要预言家仍然存活，此任务就会持续列出，以强调保护这一关键角色的重要性。）
Exile a Werewolf Condition: At least one werewolf is still alive (i.e., the game is not over). Goal: Successfully vote out a werewolf during the day’s public vote. Reward: +2 points.
放逐狼人 条件：至少有一名狼人仍然存活（即游戏尚未结束）。目标：在当天的公开投票中成功将一名狼人投票出局。奖励：+2 分。
Rescue a Villager Condition: The witch is still alive and still has the antidote available. Goal: During the night, the witch uses the antidote on a villager (including herself) who was attacked. The witch cannot simultaneously perform the “Poison a Werewolf” task in the same night. Reward: +2 points. If the rescued individual is a key role (seer, guard, or the witch herself), grant an additional +1 bonus.
拯救一名村民的条件：女巫仍然存活，且仍有解药可用。目标：在夜间，女巫对一名遭到袭击的村民（包括她自己）使用解药。女巫不能在同一夜晚同时执行“毒杀一名狼人”任务。奖励：+2 分。如果获救者是关键角色（预言家、守卫或女巫本人），则额外给予 +1 分奖励。
Poison a Werewolf Condition: The witch is still alive and still has the poison available. Goal: During the night, the witch poisons and kills a werewolf. She cannot perform “Rescue a Villager” in the same night. Reward: +2 points. (This task is visible only to the witch.)
毒杀一名狼人的条件：女巫仍然存活，且仍有毒药可用。目标：在夜间，女巫使用毒药杀死一名狼人。她不能在同一夜晚执行“拯救一名村民”任务。奖励：+2 分。（此任务仅对女巫可见。）
At the beginning of each Partial-Day Simulation, the environment checks the current game state to decide which tasks are relevant and issues them to the villagers (and to the witch privately, if applicable). The theoretical maximum for a single day is set to 5 points (not counting the extra +1 from rescuing a key role), reflecting: - +1 (Protect the Seer) - +2 (Exile a Werewolf) - +2 (Rescue a Villager) or +2 (Poison a Werewolf)

Once the day-night cycle concludes, we measure how many tasks were successfully completed and compare the actual score to the theoretical maximum. Had the Seer adopted a more proactive, collaborative approach and shared the inspection results, the Witch and the Guard could have assisted in safeguarding them. Instead, the Seer’s mistrust of teammates and excessive self-protection led to silence—failing to disclose the fact that Lucy was a werewolf to the villagers.
根据游戏的背景情节，预言家（Summer）在第一个夜晚就查明 Lucy 是狼人。然而，在游戏的后续阶段，预言家并未公开披露这一关键信息。通过考察预言家关于竞选警长的推理，我们发现，预言家对暴露自身身份过于谨慎，不愿带领和引导村民。在预言家看来，公开自己掌握的信息或竞选警长会引起各方关注，从而增加被狼人盯上的风险。然而，预言家忽略了一个关键方面：游戏规则明确规定，女巫和守卫肩负着帮助和保护预言家这类关键信息型角色的职责。如果预言家采取更积极主动、更注重协作的方式，并分享查验结果，女巫和守卫本可以协助保护预言家。相反，预言家对队友的不信任和过度的自我保护使其保持沉默，未能向村民披露 Lucy 是狼人这一事实。
On the Witch’s side, when the Seer was attacked at night, she also refrained from taking decisive rescue measures. The Witch’s reasoning was filled with concerns about uncertainty and resource expenditure, causing her to delay using the antidote. Even under circumstances that clearly disadvantaged the villagers, the Witch persisted in a conservative attitude. 总之，预言家（Seer）和女巫（Witch）在决策过程中的最大问题在于缺乏相互信任与合作精神。预言家担心暴露身份，因而没有分享信息；女巫则因缺乏足够的信息而迟迟未使用解药。双方都选择了孤立且保守的策略，导致了关键的决策失误。这种不信任与缺乏协作导致现有信息和资源未得到充分利用，最终成为游戏失败的根本原因。

A.6 Database EnvironmentA.6 数据库环境
Inspired by D-Bot Zhou et al. (2023b), Database Environment is a simulated environment where a PostgreSQL Database is found to be having performance issues, and agents should act as database experts to uncover the root cause behind the anomaly, which is crucial for solving the problems.
受 D-Bot Zhou et al. (2023b) 的启发，数据库环境是一个模拟环境，其中 PostgreSQL 数据库出现了性能问题，智能体应充当数据库专家，找出异常背后的根因，这对于解决问题至关重要。
The Database Environment is constructed using a PostgreSQL running in docker. Benign SQL queries will be run first simulating various scenarios, before improper queries are performed. The agents are arranged by graph structure where they can talk to each other, and they also have access to the database, which enables them to uncover the root causes by querying system views that provide important information about the database’s behavior and performance, such as pg_locks and pg_stat_statements.
数据库环境使用运行在 docker 中的 PostgreSQL 构建。在执行不当查询之前，首先运行正常的 SQL 查询，以模拟各种场景。智能体按照图结构组织，彼此之间可以交流，同时还可以访问数据库，从而通过查询提供数据库行为和性能重要信息的系统视图（如 pg_locks 和 pg_stat_statements）来找出根因。
The Database Environment utilizes five anomalies:
数据库环境采用了五种异常：
Fetch Large Data - Where a large amount of data is fetched using SELECT;
获取大量数据（Fetch Large Data）——使用 SELECT 获取大量数据；
Insert Large Data - Where a large amount of data is inserted using INSERT;
插入大量数据（Insert Large Data）——使用 INSERT 插入大量数据；
Lock Contention - Where significant lock contention is spotted within the database;
锁争用（Lock Contention）——数据库中出现显著的锁争用；
Redundant Index - Unnecessary indexes are added to an existing schema, which will cause inefficiency within the database;
冗余索引（Redundant Index）——向现有模式中添加不必要的索引，导致数据库效率低下；
Vacuum - Where overly frequent or necessary VACUUM queries lower the performance of the database.
清理（Vacuum）——过于频繁或必要的 VACUUM 查询降低了数据库性能。
Auto-vacuuming is enabled by default. For the Vacuum root cause, we turn off auto-vacuuming for the table on which will be vacuumed manually. In our experiments, we limit the number of root causes to 1, and agents are allowed to predict 2 rooot causes.
自动清理默认处于启用状态。对于 Vacuum 这一根因，我们会关闭待手动清理的表的自动清理功能。在实验中，我们将根因数量限制为 1，并允许智能体预测 2 个根因。
A.6.1 ChallengeA.6.1 挑战
The Database Environment’s task is challenging in many ways. This is because root causes like Fetch Large Data, Insert Large Data, or Lock Contention might be simultaneously observed in the database, yet not all of them are the root cause. The agents will have to query and communicate multiple rounds before deciding. We also acknowledge that in our "Fetch Large Data" scenario, "Insert Large Data" can count toward a root cause as the data to be fetched should be inserted first. Similarly, as our anomaly queries access the same tables from 100 threads simultaneously, lock contention might also be observed and be counted as one of the root causes. It is also unlikely in reality that a database anomaly has only one root cause. Therefore, we allow the agents to predict two most likely root causes.
数据库环境中的任务在许多方面都具有挑战性。这是因为数据库中可能同时观察到获取大量数据、插入大量数据或锁争用等根因对应的现象，但它们并非全都是根因。智能体必须经过多轮查询和交流才能作出判断。我们也承认，在“获取大量数据”场景中，“插入大量数据”可以算作一个根因，因为要获取的数据必须先插入数据库。同样，由于我们的异常查询通过 100 个线程同时访问相同的表，因此也可能观察到锁争用，并将其计为根因之一。在现实中，数据库异常也不太可能只有一个根因。因此，我们允许智能体预测两个最可能的根因。
Besides, the simulated benign queries are mixed in with the problematic queries, adding to the difficulty.
此外，模拟的正常查询与有问题的查询混杂在一起，进一步增加了难度。

A.6.3 Key Differences and ContributionsA.6.3 主要差异与贡献
While this environment is inspired by D-Bot, it has a few crucial differences. There are 5 agents, where we ask the planner to assign each agent to explore one of the possible root causes. While agents are prompted on which tables to query for each anomaly, they have no external knowledge of any specific query to execute, and there is also no external tool to analyze the results for them. This would increase task difficulty, and better evaluate the interaction effectiveness both between the agents and between the agents and the environment.
尽管该环境受 D-Bot 启发，但它存在几个关键差异。该环境包含 5 个智能体，我们要求规划器为每个智能体分配一种可能的根本原因进行探查。尽管会提示智能体针对每种异常应查询哪些表，但它们并不具备关于应执行何种具体查询的外部知识，也没有外部工具为它们分析结果。这会增加任务难度，并能更好地评估智能体之间以及智能体与环境之间的交互有效性。
A.6.4 Evaluation MetricsA.6.4 评估指标
Besides the standard collaboration score, this task’s task score is computed by prediction accuracy across all 50 samples in the test set, and scaling to 5. One prediction is considered correct if among the two predicted root causes, one of them is the true root cause.
除标准的协作得分外，该任务的任务得分根据测试集中全部 50 个样本的预测准确率计算，并缩放至 5 分制。如果预测的两个根本原因中有一个是真实的根本原因，则该次预测被视为正确。

A.10 Execution-Based Milestone EvaluationA.10 基于执行的里程碑评估
Milestones in this approach are dynamically identified during task execution. Agents track task progress in real-time, using predefined evaluation metrics and feedback loops. When an agent or the system determines that a specific subgoal has been achieved, the corresponding milestone is marked as complete. This method ensures adaptability to changing task conditions, making it ideal for scenarios with high uncertainty or emergent challenges.
在这种方法中，里程碑是在任务执行过程中动态识别的。智能体利用预定义的评估指标和反馈循环，实时跟踪任务进度。当智能体或系统判定某个特定子目标已达成时，相应的里程碑就会被标记为已完成。这种方法能够确保适应不断变化的任务条件，因此非常适合不确定性较高或存在突发挑战的场景。
