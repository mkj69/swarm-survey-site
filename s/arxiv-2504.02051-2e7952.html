Self-Resource Allocation in Multi-Agent LLM Systems多智能体 LLM 系统中的自主资源分配
Abstract摘要
With the development of LLMs as agents, there is a growing interest in connecting multiple agents into multi-agent systems to solve tasks concurrently, focusing on their role in task assignment and coordination. This paper explores how LLMs can effectively allocate computational tasks among multiple agents, considering factors such as cost, efficiency, and performance. In this work, we address key questions, including the effectiveness of LLMs as orchestrators and planners, comparing their effectiveness in task assignment and coordination. Our experiments demonstrate that LLMs can achieve high validity and accuracy in resource allocation tasks. We find that the planner method outperforms the orchestrator method in handling concurrent actions, resulting in improved efficiency and better utilization of agents. Additionally, we show that providing explicit information about worker capabilities enhances the allocation strategies of planners, particularly when dealing with suboptimal workers.
随着 LLM 作为智能体的发展，将多个智能体连接成多智能体系统以并发解决任务的研究日益受到关注，重点在于它们在任务分配与协调中的作用。本文探讨 LLM 如何综合考虑成本、效率和性能等因素，在多个智能体之间有效分配计算任务。在本研究中，我们探讨了若干关键问题，包括 LLM 作为编排器（orchestrator）和规划器（planner）的有效性，并比较二者在任务分配与协调方面的效能。我们的实验表明，LLM 在资源分配任务中能够实现较高的有效率和准确率。我们发现，规划器方法在处理并发动作时优于编排器方法，从而提高了效率和智能体利用率。此外，我们表明，明确提供工作智能体的能力信息能够改善规划器的分配策略，尤其是在面对能力欠佳的工作智能体时。
1 Introduction1 引言
Large Language Models (LLMs) have become popular for various tasks beyond just generating text. They can act as agents that interact with their environment (Xi et al., 2025a) and use tools effectively. This has led to the development of more versatile systems that can operate a computer like a human (Agashe et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024), serve as research assistants (Schmidgall et al., 2025), or even automate tasks in a lab (M. Bran et al., 2024).
大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）已广泛用于文本生成之外的各种任务。它们能够充当与环境交互的智能体 (Xi et al., 2025a)，并有效使用工具。这推动了功能更加多样的系统的发展，这些系统能够像人类一样操作计算机 (Agashe et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024)、担任研究助手 (Schmidgall et al., 2025)，甚至实现实验室任务的自动化 (M. Bran et al., 2024)。
As agents are used for more tasks and in more scenarios, they need to interact with other agents. This can happen by design in Multi-Agent Systems (MAS) or through more spontaneous interactions. As a result, frameworks like AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) and Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a) have been developed to leverage the capabilities of MAS. The creation of these multi-agent systems opens up new possibilities and challenges to explore (Han et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024a; Agashe et al., 2023), such as their organizational structures, shared memory, communication efficiency, and coordination capabilities.
随着智能体被用于更多任务和场景，它们需要与其他智能体交互。这种交互既可以通过多智能体系统（Multi-Agent Systems，MAS）的设计来实现，也可以通过更自发的交互来发生。因此，AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023) 和 Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a) 等框架应运而生，以发挥 MAS 的能力。这些多智能体系统的构建带来了值得探索的新可能性和新挑战 (Han et al., 2024; Guo et al., 2024a; Agashe et al., 2023)，例如其组织结构、共享记忆、通信效率和协调能力。
In particular, we focus on how these agents coordinate to assign tasks and achieve their goals. Inspired by Marvin Minsky’s idea that intelligence emerges from computational modules working together to accomplish goals that none could achieve alone Minsky (1988), we aim to analyze how LLMs allocate tasks to agents. Our goal is to optimize the allocation of resources and tasks by LLMs themselves. Thus, our underlying research question is: How does a network of LLM-based agents optimize their task allocation?
具体而言，我们关注这些智能体如何通过协调来分配任务并实现目标。Marvin Minsky 认为，智能源于计算模块的协同工作，这些模块共同实现任何单个模块都无法独立实现的目标 Minsky (1988)；受这一观点启发，我们旨在分析 LLM 如何向智能体分配任务。我们的目标是由 LLM 自身优化资源和任务的分配。因此，我们的根本研究问题是：由基于 LLM 的智能体构成的网络如何优化其任务分配？
We conduct a series of experiments to understand how LLMs allocate tasks to agents within multi-agent systems. First, we evaluate how one LLM can generate the correct task allocation, assigning actions to each agent for a problem with a known solution provided by the Hungarian Algorithm. Second, we examine two methods illustrated in Figure 1—Orchestrator and Planner—using the CuisineWorld benchmark (Gong et al., 2023), which is detailed in Section 4.2. The Orchestrator uses one LLM to generate all the actions to be executed, while the Planner creates a plan that is then given to executor LLM agents, who generate their actions independently. The plan is only re-evaluated when a relevant event occurs. Lastly, we assess how the Planner can allocate tasks based on the agents’ abilities.
我们开展了一系列实验，以了解 LLM 如何在多智能体系统内向智能体分配任务。首先，对于一个已由匈牙利算法给出已知解的问题，我们评估单个 LLM 如何通过为每个智能体分配动作来生成正确的任务分配方案。其次，我们使用第 4.2 节详细介绍的 CuisineWorld 基准 (Gong et al., 2023)，考察图 1 所示的两种方法——编排器和规划器。编排器使用一个 LLM 生成所有待执行的动作，而规划器先制定计划，再将其交给执行器 LLM 智能体，由这些智能体独立生成各自的动作。只有在发生相关事件时，才会重新评估该计划。最后，我们评估规划器如何根据智能体的能力分配任务。
From the experiments, we concluded interesting findings: First, LLMs achieve higher validity and accuracy in resource allocation tasks as their parameter size increases, but at a significant computational and monetary cost. Second, the planner method outperforms the orchestrator method in handling concurrent actions, resulting in improved efficiency in multi-agent task execution. The planner method achieves better utilization of agents, with fewer idle actions. Finally, we see that LLMs are sensitive to worker capabilities and struggle to infer them dynamically. Providing explicit information about worker capabilities improves planner’s allocation strategies, especially with suboptimal workers.
通过实验，我们得出了以下有趣的发现：首先，随着参数规模增大，LLM 在资源分配任务中的有效率和准确率有所提高，但这需要付出显著的计算成本和资金成本。其次，规划器方法在处理并发动作时优于编排器方法，从而提高了多智能体任务执行的效率。规划器方法实现了更高的智能体利用率，空闲动作也更少。最后，我们发现，LLM 对工作智能体的能力较为敏感，但难以动态推断这些能力。明确提供工作智能体的能力信息能够改善规划器的分配策略，尤其是在工作智能体能力欠佳时。
We highlight the following key contributions from this paper:
我们着重介绍本文的以下主要贡献：
An evaluation of LLMs as effective orchestrators for optimizing task allocation in multi-agent systems by comparing their task allocations against the Hungarian Algorithm optimal solution.
通过将 LLM 的任务分配方案与匈牙利算法的最优解进行比较，评估 LLM 作为有效编排器优化多智能体系统任务分配的能力。
We demonstrate the differences between the orchestrator and the planner methods when handling concurrent actions, highlighting the efficiency gains achieved by the planner.
我们展示了编排器方法与规划器方法在处理并发动作时的差异，重点说明了规划器带来的效率提升。
An analysis of how the Planner allocates tasks based on agents’ abilities within the system, demonstrating that providing explicit information about worker capabilities enhances allocation strategies, particularly when dealing with suboptimal workers.
分析规划器如何根据系统内智能体的能力分配任务，并表明明确提供工作智能体的能力信息能够改善分配策略，尤其是在面对能力欠佳的工作智能体时。
As multi-agent systems evolve, optimizing task allocation will be critical for enhancing efficiency and performance across applications. This work addresses current resource management challenges in LLM-based frameworks and paves the way for advancements in autonomous and collaborative AI systems.
随着多智能体系统的发展，优化任务分配将成为提升各类应用效率和性能的关键。本研究应对了基于 LLM 的框架当前面临的资源管理挑战，并为自主式和协作式 AI 系统的发展铺平了道路。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Agent Interaction and Collaboration智能体交互与协作
As more agents are deployed in real-world settings, studying their interactions becomes increasingly relevant. These agents need to communicate not only with each other but also with humans (Jiang et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2023a). Agents can choose to cooperate, compete, or do a mix of both (Tran et al., 2025). Since these models use natural language for communication, recent studies have explored the concept of ”Theory of Mind,” which involves understanding and attributing mental states to oneself and others (Strachan et al., 2024; Street, 2024). Some works apply Game Theory to analyze these interactions (Hua et al., 2024). Several studies measure the coordination abilities of LLMs, such as the LLM-Coordination Benchmark (Agashe et al., 2023) and MindAgent (Gong et al., 2023), Gamma( $\gamma$ )-Bench (Huang et al., 2024). Other works focus on improving the abilities of LLMs to interact and coordinate (Li et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2023b; Cross et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023a; Guo et al., 2024b).
随着越来越多的智能体被部署到现实环境中，研究它们之间的交互变得日益重要。这些智能体不仅需要彼此通信，还需要与人类交流 (Jiang et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2023a)。智能体可以选择合作、竞争，或将两者结合起来 (Tran et al., 2025)。由于这些模型使用自然语言进行交流，近期研究探索了“心智理论”（Theory of Mind）这一概念，即理解自身和他人的心理状态，并将这些状态归属于相应主体 (Strachan et al., 2024; Street, 2024)。一些研究运用博弈论分析这些交互 (Hua et al., 2024)。多项研究对 LLM 的协调能力进行了评估，例如 LLM-Coordination Benchmark (Agashe et al., 2023)、MindAgent (Gong et al., 2023) 和 Gamma( $\gamma$ )-Bench (Huang et al., 2024)。其他研究则致力于提升 LLM 的交互与协调能力 (Li et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2023b; Cross et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2023a; Guo et al., 2024b)。
LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems基于 LLM 的多智能体系统
Extending single-agent systems to multi-agent systems has led to increased computational efficiency during inference, with shorter execution times. Multi-agent systems offer several benefits, including modularity, specialization, collaborative learning, and improved decision-making. These systems provide better scalability and flexibility, making them more effective at solving problems that a single model might struggle with. The development of multi-agent systems has led to the creation of various frameworks focused on MAS, such as AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023), Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a), or MetaGPT (Hong et al., 2024). These frameworks have enabled the creation of systems with better scalability and flexibility, enhancing problem-solving capabilities. For example, the Chain of Agents (Zhang et al., 2024b) can process long contexts effectively, FilmAgent (Xu et al., 2024) generate coherent long-sequence videos , Chemrow (M. Bran et al., 2024) support new chemistry research, and Smart-LLM to control multiple robots simultaneously (Kannan et al., 2024).
将单智能体系统扩展为多智能体系统，提高了推理阶段的计算效率，缩短了执行时间。多智能体系统具有多种优势，包括模块化、专业化、协作学习以及决策能力的提升。这些系统具有更好的可扩展性和灵活性，因此能够更有效地解决单个模型可能难以应对的问题。多智能体系统的发展催生了各种专注于多智能体系统（MAS）的框架，例如 AutoGen (Wu et al., 2023)、Camel-AI (Li et al., 2023a) 和 MetaGPT (Hong et al., 2024)。这些框架使构建具有更好可扩展性和灵活性的系统成为可能，从而增强了解决问题的能力。例如，Chain of Agents (Zhang et al., 2024b) 能够有效处理长上下文，FilmAgent (Xu et al., 2024) 能够生成连贯的长序列视频，Chemrow (M. Bran et al., 2024) 能够支持新的化学研究，而 Smart-LLM 能够同时控制多个机器人 (Kannan et al., 2024)。
Learning to optimize resources学习优化资源
When developing multi-agent systems, a crucial question arises: What is the optimal architecture? This is explored in Zhuge et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2023b). Once the architecture is defined, how do these systems utilize it effectively? First, selecting the right model for specific queries can optimize performance by ensuring high-quality answers, as explored in RouteLLM (Ong et al., 2025) Hybrid-LLM (Ding et al., 2024), and the corresponding benchmark RouterEval (Huang et al., 2025). In Mindstorms (Zhuge et al., 2023), the authors theorize how LLMs can lead to systems that self-manage resources based on a monetary system, optimizing resources and maximizing rewards to create an ”Economy of Minds” (EOM). Models can also learn to optimize their communication, as demonstrated in Agora (Marro et al., 2024). Due to the need for communication and coordination, Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL) becomes more complex than simply scaling up single-agent RL (Sun et al., 2024). Self-organized MASs can reduce the burden on developers while achieving better results, such as in code generation (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024) or leveraging scale in multi-agent reward-based learning (Slumbers et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2024; Tekin et al., 2025).
在开发多智能体系统时，一个关键问题随之出现：最优架构是什么？Zhuge et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2023b) 对这一问题进行了探索。架构确定之后，这些系统如何有效地利用它？首先，针对特定查询选择合适的模型，可以通过确保答案的高质量来优化性能，RouteLLM (Ong et al., 2025)、Hybrid-LLM (Ding et al., 2024) 以及相应的基准 RouterEval (Huang et al., 2025) 对此进行了探索。在 Mindstorms (Zhuge et al., 2023) 中，作者从理论上探讨了 LLM 如何促成基于货币体系自主管理资源的系统，通过优化资源并最大化奖励来构建“心智经济”（Economy of Minds，EOM）。模型还可以学习优化自身的通信方式，Agora (Marro et al., 2024) 展示了这一点。由于需要通信与协调，多智能体强化学习（MARL）比单纯扩大单智能体强化学习（RL）的规模更加复杂 (Sun et al., 2024)。自组织多智能体系统（MASs）能够在减轻开发者负担的同时取得更好的结果，例如用于代码生成 (Ishibashi & Nishimura, 2024)，或在基于奖励的多智能体学习中利用规模优势 (Slumbers et al., 2023; Ma et al., 2024; Tekin et al., 2025)。
3 Problem Definition3 问题定义
In real-world settings, resource allocation in multi-agent systems can often be seen as a multi-agent resource allocation problem. This involves distributing computational tasks among multiple model instances to optimize criteria such as cost, efficiency, and performance. This challenge becomes especially relevant as AI systems gain autonomy and need to make resource allocation decisions with incomplete information. We formalize the problem to provide a foundation for analyzing how LLMs can perform self-resource allocation under multiple constraints and objectives.
在现实环境中，多智能体系统中的资源分配通常可以视为一个多智能体资源分配问题。这涉及将计算任务分配给多个模型实例，以优化成本、效率和性能等指标。随着 AI 系统获得自主性，并需要在信息不完整的情况下做出资源分配决策，这一挑战变得尤为重要。我们对该问题进行形式化定义，为分析 LLM 如何在多重约束和目标下进行自主资源分配奠定基础。
In a multi-agent system with $N$ agents and $P$ different tasks, each task can be decomposed into a sequence of $M_{p}$ subtasks. Each agent incurs different costs for different tasks due to variations in capability. The objective is to maximize overall utility while respecting time and assignment constraints.
在一个包含 $N$ 个智能体和 $P$ 个不同任务的多智能体系统中，每个任务都可以分解为由 $M_{p}$ 个子任务构成的序列。由于能力存在差异，每个智能体执行不同任务时产生的成本也不同。目标是在满足时间约束和分配约束的同时，最大化整体效用。
Agents智能体
Let $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{n}\}$ represent a set of agents, where agent is characterized by
令 $\mathcal{A}=\{a_{1},a_{2},\ldots,a_{n}\}$ 表示一个智能体集合，其中每个智能体由以下特征刻画
Operational Cost ( $c_{i}$ ): It represents the computational expense.
运行成本（$c_{i}$）：表示计算开销。
Capability Measure ( $\phi_{i}$ ): It reflects the model proficiency in performing tasks.
能力度量（$\phi_{i}$）：反映模型执行任务的熟练程度。
Tasks任务
$\mathcal{T}=\{t_{1},t_{2},\ldots,t_{p}\}$ denote a set of tasks to be allocated. A task $t_{j}\in\mathcal{T}$ is defined by:
$\mathcal{T}=\{t_{1},t_{2},\ldots,t_{p}\}$ 表示待分配的任务集合。任务 $t_{j}\in\mathcal{T}$ 定义如下：
Difficulty level ( $d_{j}$ ): It indicates the task complexity
难度等级（$d_{j}$）：表示任务的复杂程度。
Sub-tasks ( $m_{j}$ ): It refers to the subtask that a main task can be decomposed into.
子任务（$m_{j}$）：指主任务可分解得到的子任务。

工作负载需求（$w_{j}$）：表示计算需求。

奖励（$r_{j}$）：一种潜在奖励，并非在所有场景中都存在。
Utility Function效用函数
Combine utility function for a subtask
子任务的综合效用函数
$$\begin{cases}q_{pim}-c_{pim},&\text{if agent }i\text{ can execute subtask }m\text{ for task }p\\ -\infty,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$
where $q_{pim}$ and $c_{pim}$ : Quality and cost, respectively, for allocating agent $i$ to work on subtask $m$ for task $p$ .
其中，$q_{pim}$ 和 $c_{pim}$ 分别表示将智能体 $i$ 分配去执行任务 $p$ 的子任务 $m$ 时的质量和成本。
The assignment of subtask $m$ to agent $i$ :
将子任务 $m$ 分配给智能体 $i$：
$$v_{pim}=\begin{cases}1,&\text{agent }i\text{ is assigned to subtask }m\text{ for task }p\\ 0,&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$
Optimization Problem优化问题
The goal is to maximize the utility under a time constraint $T_{max}$ :
目标是在时间约束 $T_{max}$ 下最大化效用：
$$\arg\max_{v}\sum_{p=1}^{P}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{m=1}^{M_{p}}u_{pim}v_{pim}$$
Subject to:
约束条件为：
$$\displaystyle\sum_{p}\sum_{i}\sum_{m}\tau_{pim}v_{pim} \\ \displaystyle\leq T_{max} \\ \displaystyle\sum_{i}v_{pim} \\ \displaystyle\leq 1\quad\forall m\in\mathcal{M}_{p},\forall p\in\mathcal{P} \\ \displaystyle v_{pim} \\ \displaystyle\in\{0,1\}\quad\forall i\in\mathcal{N},\forall m\in\mathcal{M}_{p},\forall p\in\mathcal{P}$$
As noted by Korsah et al. (2013); Gong et al. (2023), this problem cannot be solved in polynomial time. In our work, we aim to tackle this problem with LLMs.
正如 Korsah et al. (2013); Gong et al. (2023) 所指出的，该问题无法在多项式时间内求解。在本研究中，我们旨在利用 LLMs 解决这一问题。
Assignment problem指派问题
For the basic assignment problem in Experiment 1, we can simplify the notation. We set $N=P$ , indicating that the number of agents equals the number of tasks. Each task consists of a single subtask, represented by $M_{p}=1$ . The cost matrix $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ represents the costs, with $c_{pi1}=A_{ij}$ . We focus solely on minimizing costs, setting $q_{pi1}=0$ . Each assignment takes one unit of time, denoted by $\tau_{pi1}=1$ . Finally, we set $T_{max}=N$ , ensuring that the time constraint allows each agent to be assigned exactly one task.
对于实验 1 中的基本指派问题，我们可以简化符号表示。我们设定 $N=P$，表示智能体数量等于任务数量。每个任务仅包含一个子任务，表示为 $M_{p}=1$。成本矩阵 $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ 表示各项成本，其中 $c_{pi1}=A_{ij}$。我们仅关注成本最小化，因此设定 $q_{pi1}=0$。每次指派耗时一个时间单位，记为 $\tau_{pi1}=1$。最后，我们设定 $T_{max}=N$，确保时间约束允许为每个智能体恰好分配一个任务。
$$\arg\min_{v}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{j=1}^{N}A_{ij}v_{ij}$$
$\text{s.t.}\quad\sum_{i=1}^{N}v_{ij}=1;$ $\forall j,;\sum_{j=1}^{N}v_{ij}=1\forall i,$ ensuring each agent is assigned to one task $v_{ij}\in{0,1};\forall i,j\in{1,\ldots,N}$ specifying the assignment variable is binary.
$\text{s.t.}\quad\sum_{i=1}^{N}v_{ij}=1;$ $\forall j,;\sum_{j=1}^{N}v_{ij}=1\forall i,$ 确保每个智能体被分配一个任务；$v_{ij}\in{0,1};\forall i,j\in{1,\ldots,N}$ 规定指派变量为二元变量。

4.1 Experiment 1: Basic Resource Allocation4.1 实验 1：基本资源分配
Problem Statement问题描述
Orchestrator agent is widely adopted in multi-agent systems. We start with evaluating how effectively LLM orchestrator can allocate resources in the most simple setting where both costs and rewards are explicitly defined.
编排智能体在多智能体系统中得到了广泛应用。我们首先在成本和奖励均被明确定义的最简单设置下，评估 LLM 编排器分配资源的有效性。
Figure 2: A multi-agent system with an LLM-based orchestrator. At each step, the orchestrator optimally assigns multiple tasks to agents and minimizes the total cost. After agents complete their tasks, the environment updates with new tasks and a new cost matrix, continuing the process iteratively.
图 2：具有基于 LLM 的编排器的多智能体系统。在每个步骤中，编排器以最优方式将多个任务分配给智能体，并使总成本最小化。智能体完成任务后，环境会更新，提供新任务和新的成本矩阵，使这一过程迭代进行。
Consider a multi-agent system with a long-horizon task that can be broken down into a sequence of fundamental sub-tasks or actions executable by agents. In this system, the LLM-based orchestrator assigns tasks to agents based on their associated costs. Each agent incurs different costs for different tasks due to variations in capability or access to resources. Once an assignment is made and agents complete their tasks, the environment updates accordingly, leading to a new set of tasks for the next turn, as is shown in Figure 2. Here, we focus on evaluating the orchestrator LLM in a single turn, aligning with the standard assignment problem.
考虑一个多智能体系统，其中包含一项长时程任务，该任务可以分解为一系列可由智能体执行的基本子任务或动作。在该系统中，基于 LLM 的编排器根据智能体执行任务的相应成本，将任务分配给智能体。由于能力或资源获取条件存在差异，每个智能体执行不同任务时产生的成本各不相同。一旦完成分配且智能体完成各自的任务，环境就会相应更新，从而为下一轮产生一组新任务，如 Figure 2 所示。这里，我们重点评估编排器大语言模型（LLM）在单轮中的表现，与标准指派问题保持一致。
The objective of the assignment problem is to determine the optimal assignment of $n$ agents to $n$ tasks. Each instance of the problem is defined by a cost matrix $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ , where each entry represents the cost associated with assigning a particular agent to a task. As a fundamental combinatorial optimization problem, the assignment problem can be solved in polynomial time using the Hungarian algorithm, which has a complexity of $O(n^{3})$ , where $n$ is the number of tasks and agents. Here, we aim to evaluate whether LLMs can effectively minimize the assignment cost out of the box.
指派问题的目标是确定将 $n$ 个智能体分配给 $n$ 个任务的最优方案。该问题的每个实例都由一个成本矩阵 $A\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ 定义，其中每个元素表示将某个特定智能体分配给某项任务的成本。作为一个基本的组合优化问题，指派问题可以使用匈牙利算法在多项式时间内求解，其复杂度为 $O(n^{3})$，其中 $n$ 为任务和智能体的数量。这里，我们旨在评估 LLM 能否在开箱即用的情况下有效地最小化指派成本。
Experiment Details实验细节
To evaluate the LLM performance on assignment problems, we first created an evaluation set by generating a multiple cost matrix with random numbers filled in. After that, we utilize the Hungarian algorithm to get the ground truth for each answer. All LLM orchestrators are evaluated using greedy decoding with a maximum sequence length of 2048.
为评估 LLM 在指派问题上的表现，我们首先生成多个填充随机数的成本矩阵，以此构建评估集。随后，我们使用匈牙利算法获得每道题的标准答案。所有 LLM 编排器均采用贪心解码进行评估，最大序列长度为 2048。
Evaluation评估
We evaluate the performance of self-resource allocation across LLMs of varying model sizes. Specifically, we consider GPT-4o-mini(~8B), Mistral-Small-3.1(24B), Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct, GPT-4o(~200B), and Llama3.1-405B-Instruct-FP8. Two evaluation metrics, accuracy and validity rate, are used. ❶ Accuracy measures how many of the LLM-generated assignment solutions are optimal. A solution is considered as correct only when it is exactly the same as the ground truth optimal assignment given by the Hungarian algorithm. Here we use the LLM judge based on GPT-4o to examine each model response. ❷ Validity rate tests how many of the LLM-generated assignment solutions are valid, which can be not optimal. Invalid assignments include assigning one agent to more than one task, leaving some tasks not assigned with agents, and making up an incorrect lower cost of the agent.
我们评估了不同模型规模的 LLM 在自主资源分配方面的表现。具体而言，我们考察了 GPT-4o-mini(~8B)、Mistral-Small-3.1(24B)、Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct、Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct、GPT-4o(~200B) 和 Llama3.1-405B-Instruct-FP8。我们采用准确率和有效率两项评估指标。❶ 准确率衡量 LLM 生成的指派方案中有多少是最优的。只有当某个方案与匈牙利算法给出的标准最优指派方案完全一致时，才将其视为正确方案。这里，我们使用基于 GPT-4o 的 LLM 评判器来检查各模型的回答。❷ 有效率检验 LLM 生成的指派方案中有多少是有效的，这些方案可以不是最优的。无效指派包括将一个智能体分配给多项任务、部分任务未分配智能体，以及为智能体编造一个错误的较低成本。

Problem Statement问题描述
In this experiment, we introduce additional complexity by incorporating delayed rewards, as outlined in Table 1. The reward is only provided after the completion of a number of actions. 在本实验中，我们通过引入延迟奖励增加了复杂性，如 Table 1 所述。只有在完成若干动作之后才会提供奖励。奖励 在现实场景中，智能体通常需要执行动作，却无法立即知晓这些动作最终是否会。

我们使用 CuisineWorld (Gong et al., 2023) 作为基准，其灵感源自 Overcooked!(Carroll et al., 2019)。智能体必须通过收集和烹饪食材，并在规定时间内交付菜品，来完成菜品订单。目标是最大化已完成任务的数量。该基准包含 10 个地点、27 种食材和 33 道菜品，并根据难度和所需烹饪工具进行分类，形成 12 个游戏关卡。Figure 3 展示了这一游戏设定。更多信息请参见 Appendix A。

As originally described, the agent decision process can be formulated as a Markov Decision Process ( $\mathcal{S,A,T,M,G}$ ) with state space $\mathcal{S}$ , action space $\mathcal{A}$ , (effectively indicating all the possible schedules that can be made at a single time step), transition dynamics $\mathcal{T}$ , reward function $\mathcal{R}$ and task instruction space $\mathcal{G}$ .
按照最初的描述，智能体的决策过程可以形式化为一个马尔可夫决策过程（$\mathcal{S,A,T,M,G}$），其中包含状态空间 $\mathcal{S}$、动作空间 $\mathcal{A}$（实际上表示在单个时间步内可以制定的所有可能调度方案）、转移动态 $\mathcal{T}$、奖励函数 $\mathcal{R}$ 和任务指令空间 $\mathcal{G}$。
•State Space $\mathcal{S}$ : The environment consists of two main entities: locations and agents. Locations can be storage areas, serving tables, or cooking tools like pans and blenders. Each location’s description includes the items it contains and whether it is currently occupied. Agents are described by their current location, the items they are holding, and whether they are using a tool.
•状态空间 $\mathcal{S}$：环境由两类主要实体组成：地点和智能体。地点可以是储物区、出餐台，或平底锅和搅拌机等烹饪工具。每个地点的描述都包括其中包含的物品以及该地点当前是否被占用。智能体的描述包括其当前位置、持有的物品以及是否正在使用工具。
•Actions: Actions in CuisineWorld involve dispatching commands to agents, such as moving to a location, obtaining or placing items, activating tools, or performing no operation. The list of actions include goto (agent, location), get (agent, location, item), put (agent, location), activate (agent, location) and the idle action.
•动作：CuisineWorld 中的动作涉及向智能体下达命令，例如移动到某个地点、获取或放置物品、启动工具，或不执行任何操作。动作列表包括 goto (agent, location)、get (agent, location, item)、put (agent, location)、activate (agent, location) 和 idle 动作。
•Tasks: The tasks involve completing dish orders, which range from simple to complex recipes. New tasks are introduced at regular intervals, and each task has a limited time frame within which it must be completed, otherwise it fails.
•任务：任务涉及完成菜品订单，其食谱从简单到复杂不等。新任务会定期引入，每项任务都必须在限定时间内完成，否则即告失败。

The goal of this experiment is to evaluate the effectiveness of planning in a multi-agent LLM system compared to the use of an orchestrator. We follow the definition of an agent provided in React (Yao et al., 2023) and other literature (Xi et al., 2025b), where agents are defined as: environment $\rightarrow$ reasoning $\rightarrow$ action. We compare three methods, represented in Figure 1 from $n=1,...,6$ agents:
本实验旨在评估多智能体 LLM 系统中规划相较于使用协调器的有效性。我们遵循 React (Yao et al., 2023) 及其他文献 (Xi et al., 2025b) 中给出的智能体定义，即：环境 $\rightarrow$ 推理 $\rightarrow$ 行动。我们比较三种方法，图 1 展示了智能体数量为 $n=1,...,6$ 时的情况：
Individual: Each agent is controlled by a different LLM, and each agent generates its own action independently.
独立模式（Individual）：每个智能体由不同的 LLM 控制，并独立生成自己的行动。
Orchestrator: One LLM controls all the agents in the game and generates actions for all of them.
协调器（Orchestrator）：一个 LLM 控制游戏中的所有智能体，并为它们全部生成行动。
Planner: Generates a general plan whenever a relevant event occurs in the game, such as when a dish is introduced, completed, or removed. This plan is then provided to the LLM agents, which produce their independent actions.
规划器（Planner）：每当游戏中发生相关事件时，例如引入、完成或移除一道菜品，就生成一个总体计划。随后，该计划被提供给各个 LLM 智能体，由它们独立生成行动。
Evaluation评估
We evaluate the performance of the previous methods in producing the correct allocation and maximizing the number of orders completed. We aim to study how individual, independent weak models (decentralized) can benefit from planning by a larger model. For this purpose, we use Llama 70B-Instruct, Qwen 32B-Instruct, and GPT4o-mini as executor models, while GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 Sonnet are used for the Orchestrator (centralized). The Planner method is then evaluated using a combination of these models, where GPT-4o and Claude 3.7 generate the plans, and open-source models generate the actions. To evaluate the results, we use the following metrics: ❶ #Completed Orders: the number of orders the agents are able to complete; ❷ Execution Cost: the cost of LLM calls at current prices, detailed in Table 5, which closely represents their running cost; and ❸ Efficiency= $\frac{\#complted\_orders}{\$cost}$ : the ratio of completed orders to cost, representing the work done per dollar spent.
我们评估上述方法在实现正确分配以及最大化已完成订单数量方面的表现。我们旨在研究各自独立的弱模型（去中心化）如何受益于较大模型的规划。为此，我们使用 Llama 70B-Instruct、Qwen 32B-Instruct 和 GPT4o-mini 作为执行器模型，而使用 GPT-4o 和 Claude 3.7 Sonnet 作为协调器（中心化）。随后，我们使用这些模型的组合来评估规划器方法，其中 GPT-4o 和 Claude 3.7 生成计划，开源模型生成行动。为评估结果，我们使用以下指标：❶ 已完成订单数：智能体能够完成的订单数量；❷ 执行成本：按当前价格计算的 LLM 调用成本，详见表 5，该指标能够较准确地反映其运行成本；以及 ❸ 效率= $\frac{\#complted\_orders}{\$cost}$ ：已完成订单数与成本的比值，表示每花费一美元所完成的工作量。
4.3 Experiment 3: Capability-Aware Allocation4.3 实验 3：能力感知分配
Problem Statement.问题陈述。
In the third experiment, we want to evaluate the capability of LLMs to allocate tasks based on the ability of the worker agents. We fix the Planner model and evaluate its ability to allocate plans to a heterogeneous mix of worker agents, each with a different backbone model. Moreover, each of these backbone models has varying levels of intelligence and parameter sizes – allowing us to evaluate the task distribution of LLMs to Worker LLMs of varying intelligence capabilities. We use the same CuisineWorld environment Gong et al. (2023) - with similar settings as Experiment 2 (Section 4.2).
在第三个实验中，我们希望评估 LLM 根据工作智能体的能力分配任务的能力。我们固定规划器模型，并评估其向异构工作智能体组合分配计划的能力，其中每个工作智能体采用不同的骨干模型。此外，这些骨干模型的智能水平和参数规模各不相同，使我们能够评估 LLM 向具有不同智能水平的工作 LLM 分配任务的情况。我们使用同一个 CuisineWorld 环境 Gong et al. (2023)，设置与实验 2（第 4.2 节）相似。
Experiment Details实验细节
We run two experimental settings to evaluate the Capability-Aware Allocation abilities of LLMs as Planners. In the first setting, On-the-fly Allocation, the planner agent is not provided any information about its workers and needs to infer their capabilities dynamically during execution. In the second setting, Informed Allocation, we want the Planner to have knowledge about the capabilities of the worker agents ( $\phi_{i}$ ). We indicate the capabilities of underlying worker models. For this, we use the action success rates of the individual models on CuisineWorld as a proxy measure of their intelligence.
我们采用两种实验设置，评估 LLM 作为规划器时的能力感知分配能力。在第一种设置“即时分配”（On-the-fly Allocation）中，规划器智能体不会获得任何有关其工作智能体的信息，需要在执行过程中动态推断它们的能力。在第二种设置“知情分配”（Informed Allocation）中，我们希望规划器了解工作智能体的能力（$\phi_{i}$）。我们明确提供底层工作模型的能力信息。为此，我们使用各个模型在 CuisineWorld 中的行动成功率作为其智能水平的代理指标。
Evaluation:评估：
For the evaluation we use the Claude-3.7-sonnet model as the planner, as it shows to have the best planning capabilities. We vary the heterogenous worker models from the pool of: Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, and GPT-4o-mini. We run a total of 7 experiments with varying combinations of these models for $n=1,2,3$ worker agents. To evaluate the results, we use ❶ Efficiency ( $=\frac{\#complted\_orders}{\$cost}$ ) to measure the effectiveness of the task allocation strategies in terms of the number of orders completed relative to the cost incurred.
在评估中，我们使用 Claude-3.7-sonnet 模型作为规划器，因为它表现出最强的规划能力。我们从 Llama-3.1-70B-Instruct、Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct 和 GPT-4o-mini 组成的模型池中选取不同的异构工作模型。我们针对 $n=1,2,3$ 个工作智能体，采用这些模型的不同组合，共开展 7 项实验。为评估结果，我们使用 ❶ 效率（$=\frac{\#complted\_orders}{\$cost}$），根据已完成订单数与所产生成本的比值来衡量任务分配策略的有效性。

However, simply providing subtle cues about worker capabilities (action success rate from On-the-fly allocation performance) in Informed Allocation leads to a marked increase in overall efficiency—particularly when planners must work with suboptimal models. These cues reduce non-productive actions, helping LLM-based planners better match tasks to models when aware of worker differences.
图 10 比较了即时分配与知情分配。在即时分配中，由于事先未获得任何关于各工作 LLM 优势的提示，规划器难以动态确定最佳分配方案，尤其是在工作智能体的表现差异较大时。然而，在知情分配中，仅提供有关工作智能体能力的细微线索（根据即时分配表现得到的动作成功率），就能显著提高整体效率——尤其是在规划器必须使用次优模型时。这些线索减少了无效动作，使基于 LLM 的规划器在了解工作智能体之间差异的情况下，能够更好地将任务与模型匹配。
LLMs are highly sensitive to worker capabilities.LLM 对工作智能体的能力高度敏感。
We find the performance of a multi-agent system is significantly influenced by the specific capabilities of each worker LLM and how these capabilities are combined. In a homogeneous setting, Llama-70B-Instruct and Qwen32B consistently outperform GPT-4o-mini when used as worker models. Mixing models of varying strengths generally reduces average efficiency. However, including at least one stronger model in a heterogeneous team improves efficiency. For instance, combining Qwen with GPT-4o-mini yields an efficiency of 4.04, compared to 2.79 for two GPT-4o-mini models from experiment 2. In the heterogenous case, we observe that a smaller team of more capable LLMs can outperform a larger team with uneven skills, as seen with the Llama–Qwen pairing outperforming the combination of all three.
我们发现，多智能体系统的性能显著受到各工作 LLM 的具体能力及其组合方式的影响。在同构设置中，Llama-70B-Instruct 和 Qwen32B 作为工作模型时，表现始终优于 GPT-4o-mini。混合使用能力强弱不同的模型通常会降低平均效率。然而，在异构团队中纳入至少一个能力较强的模型可以提高效率。例如，将 Qwen 与 GPT-4o-mini 组合使用时，效率为 4.04，而实验 2 中两个 GPT-4o-mini 模型的效率为 2.79。在异构情况下，我们观察到，由能力更强的 LLM 组成的较小团队，其表现可以优于能力参差不齐的较大团队，例如 Llama–Qwen 组合的表现优于全部三个模型的组合。
6 Conclusions6 结论
This work explores the capabilities of LLMs to optimize task allocations in multi-agents systems. Experiments show that LLMs can function as orchestrators, with relative performance to that of established algorithms like the Hungarian Algorithm. However, using a planner method instead of an orchestrator improves efficiency in handling concurrent actions. These findings suggest that leveraging LLMs can create more efficient and cost-effective multi-agent frameworks, dynamically allocating resources based on real-time needs, enhancing performance and cost-effectiveness.
本研究探讨了 LLM 优化多智能体系统中任务分配的能力。实验表明，LLM 可以充当协调器，其性能可与匈牙利算法等成熟算法相媲美。然而，采用规划器方法代替协调器方法，可以提高处理并发动作的效率。这些发现表明，利用 LLM 可以构建效率更高、成本效益更好的多智能体框架，根据实时需求动态分配资源，从而提升性能和成本效益。
