Abstract摘要
Ensembling outputs from diverse sources is a straightforward yet effective approach to boost performance. Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) is one such popular ensemble method that aggregates outputs from multiple different Large Language Models (LLMs). This paper raises the question in the context of language models: is mixing different LLMs truly beneficial? We propose Self-MoA — an ensemble method that aggregates outputs from only the single top-performing LLM. Our extensive experiments reveal that, surprisingly, Self-MoA outperforms standard MoA that mixes different LLMs in a large number of scenarios: Self-MoA achieves $6.6\%$ improvement over MoA on the AlpacaEval 2.0 benchmark, and an average of $3.8\%$ improvement across various benchmarks, including MMLU, CRUX, and MATH. Applying Self-MoA to one of the top-ranking models in AlpacaEval 2.0 directly achieves the new state-of-the-art performance on the leaderboard. To understand the effectiveness of Self-MoA, we systematically investigate the trade-off between diversity and quality of outputs under various MoA settings. We confirm that the MoA performance is rather sensitive to the quality, and mixing different LLMs often lowers the average quality of the models. To complement the study, we identify the scenarios where mixing different LLMs could be helpful. This paper further introduces a sequential version of Self-MoA, that is capable of aggregating a large number of LLM outputs on-the-fly over multiple rounds, and is as effective as aggregating all outputs at once.
集成来自不同来源的输出，是一种简单而有效的性能提升方法。Mixture-of-Agents（MoA）就是这样一种流行的集成方法，它聚合多个不同大语言模型（LLMs）的输出。本文在语言模型的背景下提出一个问题：混合不同的 LLMs 真的有益吗？我们提出 Self-MoA——一种仅聚合单个性能最佳的 LLM 所生成输出的集成方法。我们的大量实验揭示了一个令人意外的结果：在许多场景中，Self-MoA 优于混合不同 LLMs 的标准 MoA：在 AlpacaEval 2.0 基准上，Self-MoA 相较 MoA 提升了 $6.6\%$；在包括 MMLU、CRUX 和 MATH 在内的多个基准上，平均提升了 $3.8\%$。将 Self-MoA 应用于 AlpacaEval 2.0 中排名靠前的一个模型，即可直接在排行榜上取得新的最先进性能。为理解 Self-MoA 的有效性，我们系统研究了各种 MoA 设置下输出多样性与质量之间的权衡。我们证实，MoA 的性能对质量相当敏感，而混合不同的 LLMs 往往会降低模型的平均质量。为完善这项研究，我们还确定了混合不同 LLMs 可能有帮助的场景。本文进一步提出了 Self-MoA 的序贯版本，能够在多轮过程中即时聚合大量 LLM 输出，其效果与一次性聚合所有输出相当。
1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models have made remarkable strides in improving performance across different domains, with notable examples such as GPT (Achiam et al., 2023), Gemini (Team et al., 2023), and Claude (Anthropic, 2023). Significant efforts have been directed toward increasing model size and training data to boost capabilities. However, scaling at training time comes with steep costs, while scaling computation during inference remains largely underexplored.
大语言模型在提升不同领域的性能方面取得了显著进展，其中的代表性例子包括 GPT (Achiam et al., 2023)、Gemini (Team et al., 2023) 和 Claude (Anthropic, 2023)。为增强模型能力，研究者投入了大量精力来扩大模型规模和增加训练数据。然而，在训练阶段扩大规模的成本高昂，而在推理阶段扩展计算量仍有待充分探索。
A straightforward way to utilize test-time compute is ensembling, which aims to combine outputs of multiple LLMs (Wang et al., 2024a; Lin et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a). Among various ensembling approaches, Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) (Wang et al., 2024a) has garnered significant interest, achieving superior performance in challenging tasks such as instruction following (Wang et al., 2024a), summarization, data extraction (OpenPipe, 2024), and real-world code issue resolution (Zhang et al., 2024b). Specifically, MoA first queries multiple LLMs (proposers) to generate responses, and then uses an LLM (aggregator) to synthesize and summarize these responses into a high-quality response.
利用测试时计算量的一种直接方法是集成，其目标是组合多个 LLMs 的输出 (Wang et al., 2024a; Lin et al., 2024; Jiang et al., 2023a; Wang et al., 2024a)。在各种集成方法中，Mixture-of-Agents（MoA）(Wang et al., 2024a) 受到了广泛关注，并在指令遵循 (Wang et al., 2024a)、摘要生成、数据提取 (OpenPipe, 2024) 以及现实场景中的代码问题解决 (Zhang et al., 2024b) 等具有挑战性的任务上取得了优异表现。具体而言，MoA 首先向多个 LLMs（提议者，proposers）发出查询以生成回答，然后使用一个 LLM（聚合器，aggregator）综合并总结这些回答，形成高质量的回答。
Previous research highlights the significance of model diversity within the proposers for optimizing the performance of MoA, primarily focusing on strategies for ensembling a diverse set of individual models. We consider cross-model diversity as the variation among different models. However, pursuing cross-model diversity may inadvertently include low-quality models, resulting in a quality-diversity trade-off. While previous studies mainly concentrate on achieving a high cross-model diversity (Wang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b), we adopt a holistic perspective on model diversity by considering in-model diversity, which arises from the variability of multiple responses generated by the same model. In-model diversity enables us to aggregate multiple outputs from an individual model. Intuitively, leveraging outputs from the best-performing individual model can more effectively navigate the quality-diversity trade-off by creating a higher-quality proposer mixture. Thus, we propose Self-MoA as depicted in Figure 1b, which utilizes the same prompting template as MoA but aggregates outputs that are repeatedly sampled from the same model, rather than from a set of different models. To distinguish, we use Mixed-MoA to refer to MoA configurations that combine different individual models when necessary.
先前研究强调，提议者中的模型多样性对于优化 MoA 性能至关重要，并主要关注如何集成一组多样化的单独模型。我们将跨模型多样性定义为不同模型之间的差异。然而，追求跨模型多样性可能会无意中纳入低质量模型，从而导致质量与多样性之间的权衡。以往研究主要关注实现较高的跨模型多样性 (Wang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2024b)，而我们通过考虑模型内多样性，从更全面的视角看待模型多样性；模型内多样性源于同一模型生成的多个回答之间的差异。模型内多样性使我们能够聚合单个模型的多个输出。直观而言，利用性能最佳的单个模型的输出，可以构建质量更高的提议者组合，从而更有效地处理质量与多样性之间的权衡。因此，我们提出了如图 1b 所示的 Self-MoA，它采用与 MoA 相同的提示模板，但聚合的是从同一模型中反复采样得到的输出，而非来自一组不同模型的输出。为加以区分，我们在必要时使用 Mixed-MoA 来指代组合不同单独模型的 MoA 配置。
Surprisingly, we find that Mixed-MoA is usually sub-optimal compared with Self-MoA, especially when there exist significant quality differences among proposers. Specifically, we revisit the same experiment setting of MoA with six open-source instruction fine-tuned models as Wang et al. (2024a). Compared with Mixed-MoA which aggregates all six models, Self-MoA on the strongest model achieves 6.6 point improvement over its mixed counterpart on the AlpacaEval 2.0 benchmark, showing a case of when intra-model diversity is more effective. Moreover, Self-MoA on two best-performed models on AlpacaEval 2.0 consistently achieves a 2-3 point gain and secures the top position among non-adversarial methods on the leaderboard, which further confirms the effectiveness of Self-MoA in this task.
令人意外的是，我们发现，与 Self-MoA 相比，Mixed-MoA 通常并非最优选择，尤其是在提议者之间存在显著质量差异时。具体而言，我们重新考察了 Wang et al. (2024a) 所采用的 MoA 实验设置，其中包含六个经过指令微调的开源模型。与聚合全部六个模型的 Mixed-MoA 相比，基于最强模型的 Self-MoA 在 AlpacaEval 2.0 基准上取得了 6.6 个百分点的提升，展示了模型内多样性更有效的一种情形。此外，将 Self-MoA 分别应用于 AlpacaEval 2.0 上表现最好的两个模型，均能稳定取得 2-3 个百分点的提升，并在排行榜上的非对抗性方法中位居榜首，进一步证实了 Self-MoA 在该任务中的有效性。
To explore the limits of model diversity for MoA, we extend our experiments to a setting with three specialized models, each excelling in a specific task. Specifically, we utilize Qwen2-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) for common sense QA (MMLU-redux (Gema et al., 2024)), Qwen2-Math-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) for mathematics (MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2020)), and DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct (Zhu et al., 2024) for coding (CRUX (Gu et al., 2024)). We compare Self-MoA against a range of Mixed-MoA strategies, evaluating 13 combinations of individual models based on their average performance across the three tasks. Our findings indicate that employing task-specific models as proposers for Self-MoA can significantly outperform the best Mixed-MoA. Furthermore, even in a constructed mixture task tailored for Mixed-MoA where each individual model excels in a specific subtask, only two Mixed-MoA strategies slightly outperform Self-MoA by 0.17% and 0.35%.
为探索模型多样性对 MoA 的作用极限，我们将实验扩展到包含三个专用模型的设置，其中每个模型都擅长一项特定任务。具体而言，我们使用 Qwen2-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) 处理常识问答任务（MMLU-redux (Gema et al., 2024)），使用 Qwen2-Math-7B-Instruct (Bai et al., 2023) 处理数学任务（MATH (Hendrycks et al., 2020)），并使用 DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct (Zhu et al., 2024) 处理编程任务（CRUX (Gu et al., 2024)）。我们将 Self-MoA 与一系列 Mixed-MoA 策略进行比较，根据各组合在这三项任务上的平均性能，评估了单个模型的 13 种组合。我们的研究结果表明，在 Self-MoA 中使用任务专用模型作为提议者（proposer），其性能可以显著优于最佳的 Mixed-MoA。此外，即使在专为 Mixed-MoA 构建的混合任务中，每个单独的模型都擅长某项特定子任务，也仅有两种 Mixed-MoA 策略略微优于 Self-MoA，优势分别为 0.17% 和 0.35%。
To better understand the effectiveness of Self-MoA, we conduct a comprehensive investigation of the trade-off between quality and diversity in MoA, involving over 200 experiments. We use the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) to evaluate the diversity among the outputs of the proposers, while the average performance of the proposers serves as the measure of quality. In Section 4, we confirm that MoA performance has a positive correlation with both quality and diversity. Moreover, we clearly show a trade-off along the achievable Pareto front of quality and diversity. Interestingly, we find that MoA is quite sensitive to variations in quality, with optimal performance typically occurring in regions characterized by high quality and relatively low diversity. This finding naturally explains the effectiveness of Self-MoA, as it utilizes the strongest model as the proposer, ensuring high quality in its outputs.
为更好地理解 Self-MoA 的有效性，我们通过超过 200 项实验，全面研究了 MoA 中质量与多样性之间的权衡。我们使用 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 评估提议者输出之间的多样性，并以提议者的平均性能作为质量的衡量指标。在第 4 节中，我们证实 MoA 的性能与质量和多样性均呈正相关。此外，我们清楚地展示了在质量与多样性的可达帕累托前沿上存在的权衡关系。有趣的是，我们发现 MoA 对质量变化相当敏感，其最优性能通常出现在质量较高而多样性相对较低的区域。这一发现自然解释了 Self-MoA 的有效性，因为它使用最强的模型作为提议者，从而确保其输出具有较高质量。
Finally, we evaluate the performance of Self-MoA under increasing computational budgets. As the number of outputs grows, the scalability of Self-MoA becomes constrained by the context length of the aggregator. To address this issue, we introduce Self-MoA-Seq (Figure 1c), a sequential version that processes samples using a sliding window, allowing it to handle an arbitrary number of model outputs. Our findings show that Self-MoA-Seq performs at least as effectively as Self-MoA, enabling scalable ensembling for LLMs with shorter context lengths without compromising final performance.
最后，我们评估了 Self-MoA 在计算预算不断增加时的性能。随着输出数量的增加，Self-MoA 的可扩展性受到聚合器上下文长度的限制。为解决这一问题，我们提出了 Self-MoA-Seq（图 1c），这是一个使用滑动窗口处理样本的序列版本，能够处理任意数量的模型输出。我们的研究结果表明，Self-MoA-Seq 的性能至少与 Self-MoA 相当，使上下文长度较短的 LLM 也能在不损害最终性能的情况下实现可扩展的集成。
Overall, our contributions are three-fold:
总体而言，我们的贡献包括三个方面：
We introduce Self-MoA, which leverages in-model diversity by synthesizing multiple outputs from the same model. Surprisingly, it demonstrates superior performance compared to existing Mixed-MoA approaches, which emphasize cross-model diversity, across a wide range of benchmarks.
我们提出了 Self-MoA，通过综合同一模型的多个输出来利用模型内部的多样性。令人意外的是，在广泛的基准测试中，它的性能优于现有的、强调模型间多样性的 Mixed-MoA 方法。
Through systematic experiments and statistical analysis, we uncover a core trade-off between diversity and quality among the proposers, emphasizing that MoA is highly sensitive to proposer quality. This finding also explains the success of Self-MoA, which leverages outputs from the highest-performing model, ensuring superior overall quality.
通过系统性实验和统计分析，我们揭示了提议者多样性与质量之间的核心权衡，并强调 MoA 对提议者的质量高度敏感。这一发现也解释了 Self-MoA 的成功：它利用性能最强模型的输出，从而确保更高的整体质量。
We extend Self-MoA to its sequential version Self-MoA-Seq, which iteratively aggregates a small amount of outputs step by step. Self-MoA-Seq unlocks LLMs that are constrained by the context length and enables computation scaling during inference.
我们将 Self-MoA 扩展为其序列版本 Self-MoA-Seq，逐步迭代聚合少量输出。Self-MoA-Seq 释放了受上下文长度限制的 LLM 的潜力，并支持在推理过程中扩展计算量。
2 Related Work2 相关工作
Ensembles of LLMs.LLM 集成。
Model ensembling aims to combine strengths from multiple models. Previous studies have explored various methods to leverage a diverse set of models, including but not limited to prompting (Wang et al., 2024a), weight averaging (Lin et al., 2024; Ramé et al., 2024), routing (Jiang et al., 2024b; Lu et al., 2023), training a generative fusion model (Jiang et al., 2023b), and so on. Zhang et al. (2024a) argues that the fusion of specialized models with certain general abilities could be a promising direction toward Artificial General Intelligence. Mixture-of-Agents (MoA, Wang et al. (2024a)) first queries multiple LLMs to generate responses, then iteratively aggregates these samples through several rounds of synthesis. MoA shows promising results on several benchmarks, and its variants achieve superior performance on the AlpacaEval 2.0 leaderboard. Our method is inspired by the prompt pipeline proposed in MoA. However, while existing MoA focuses on unleashing the strength from multiple different models (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024b), we demonstrate the trade-off between diversity and quality within the proposers, highlighting that focusing solely on diversity may compromise overall quality and final performance.
模型集成旨在结合多个模型的优势。以往研究探索了多种利用一组多样化模型的方法，包括但不限于提示（Wang et al., 2024a）、权重平均（Lin et al., 2024; Ramé et al., 2024）、路由（Jiang et al., 2024b; Lu et al., 2023）、训练生成式融合模型（Jiang et al., 2023b）等。Zhang et al. (2024a) 认为，融合具备一定通用能力的专用模型，可能是迈向通用人工智能的一个有前景的方向。智能体混合（Mixture-of-Agents，MoA，Wang et al. (2024a)）首先查询多个 LLM 以生成回答，然后通过多轮综合，迭代聚合这些样本。MoA 在多个基准测试中展现出良好的结果，其变体在 AlpacaEval 2.0 排行榜上取得了优异的性能。我们的方法受到 MoA 所提出的提示流水线的启发。然而，现有 MoA 着重于释放多个不同模型的优势（Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023b; Zhang et al., 2024b），而我们展示了提议者多样性与质量之间的权衡，强调仅关注多样性可能损害整体质量和最终性能。
LLM Inference with Repeated Sampling.采用重复采样的 LLM 推理。
Previous studies have shown that combining model outputs from repeated sampling can yield a better response in various domains. In tasks with automatic verifiers available, such as math (Hendrycks et al., 2021) and code (Chen et al., 2021), simply sampling LLMs multiple times can significantly improve the pass@k metric and hence boost the success rate of solving the tasks (Roziere et al., 2023; Li et al., 2022; Brown et al., 2024). In more general tasks without verification tools, we can conduct techniques like majority vote, self-consistency, and best-of-n to choose the most promising one from candidate responses (Wang et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2023b; Gui et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024). Therefore, repeated sampling is recently regarded as one approach of scaling compute during inference time (Brown et al., 2024). In this work, we identify the surprising effectiveness of repeated sampling in the context of MoA. Unlike majority vote or best-of-N, Self-MoA asks LLMs to synthesize outputs generated from repeated sampling, hence can further improve over each individual output.
以往研究表明，在多个领域中，结合重复采样得到的模型输出可以产生更好的回答。在数学 (Hendrycks et al., 2021) 和代码 (Chen et al., 2021) 等具有自动验证器的任务中，仅对 LLM 进行多次采样就能显著提高 pass@k 指标，从而提升任务求解的成功率 (Roziere et al., 2023; Li et al., 2022; Brown et al., 2024)。在没有验证工具的更一般的任务中，我们可以采用多数投票、自一致性和 best-of-n 等技术，从候选回答中选择最有希望的一个 (Wang et al., 2022; Chen et al., 2023b; Gui et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024)。因此，重复采样近来被视为扩大推理时计算规模的一种方法 (Brown et al., 2024)。在本研究中，我们发现重复采样在 MoA 中具有出人意料的有效性。与多数投票或 best-of-N 不同，Self-MoA 要求 LLM 综合重复采样生成的输出，因此能够在各个单独输出的基础上进一步提升表现。
Collaborative Agents协作智能体
There is a surge of interest in building agent systems based on verification, critique, discussion, and refinement. For example, Stechly et al. (2023), Valmeekam et al. (2023), and Madaan et al. (2024) use self-critique to iteratively refine outputs through a chain structure. Madaan et al. (2024), Chen et al. (2024), and Wang et al. (2024a) explore the incorporation of multiple models to create a stronger agent that outperforms each individual model. Du et al. (2023) incorporates multiple LLMs that propose and debate their individual responses over several rounds to reach a common final answer. Liang et al. (2023) proposes Multi-Agent Debate, which encourages divergent thinking during LLM debates to arrive at more informative conclusions and avoid rushing to incorrect answers. Chen et al. (2024b) shows that a single model with carefully designed prompts can sometimes match the performance of agent discussions. Moreover, agent discussions mainly outperform a single LLM when the prompts are insufficient.
人们对构建基于验证、批评、讨论和改进的智能体系统的兴趣正在迅速增长。例如，Stechly et al. (2023)、Valmeekam et al. (2023) 和 Madaan et al. (2024) 使用自我批评，通过链式结构迭代改进输出。Madaan et al. (2024)、Chen et al. (2024) 和 Wang et al. (2024a) 探索了整合多个模型，以创建一个比各个单独模型表现更好的强大智能体。Du et al. (2023) 整合了多个 LLM，让它们提出各自的回答，并围绕这些回答进行多轮辩论，以达成共同的最终答案。Liang et al. (2023) 提出了多智能体辩论（Multi-Agent Debate），鼓励 LLM 在辩论过程中进行发散思维，以得出信息更丰富的结论，并避免仓促得出错误答案。Chen et al. (2023a) 引入了 RECONCILE，该方法采用置信度加权投票机制，以便在 LLM 讨论中更好地达成共识。有趣的是，Wang et al. (2024b) 表明，使用精心设计的提示词的单个模型，有时能够达到与智能体讨论相当的表现。此外，智能体讨论主要在提示词不充分时优于单个 LLM。
3 Is Ensembling Different LLMs Beneficial?3 集成不同的 LLM 是否有益？
As introduced in Section 1, previous research primarily emphasizes cross-model diversity, which can inadvertently include low-quality proposers. In this work, we introduce Self-MoA (Figure 1), which uses a single top-performing model to generate multiple outputs and aggregate them to produce the final result. Self-MoA leverages in-model diversity as repeated sampling often produces varied outputs. We propose our research question as follows:
如第 1 节所述，先前的研究主要强调跨模型多样性，这可能会无意中引入低质量的提议模型。在本研究中，我们提出了 Self-MoA（图 1），它使用一个表现最优的模型生成多个输出，并将这些输出聚合以产生最终结果。Self-MoA 利用模型内多样性，因为重复采样通常会产生不同的输出。我们提出如下研究问题：
Does the benefit of MoA stem from cross-model diversity? Can we build a stronger MoA using in-model diversity?
MoA 的收益是否源于跨模型多样性？我们能否利用模型内多样性构建更强的 MoA？
Figure 1: Comparison of MoA, Self-MoA, and Self-MoA-Seq. (a) In MoA, multiple models respond to a query, followed by an aggregator synthesizing their outputs. (b) Self-MoA simplifies this by repeatedly sampling from a single model. (c) Self-MoA-Seq extends Self-MoA by applying a sliding window to combine the best output so far with candidate outputs. At each timestep, the synthesized output is repeated to bias the aggregator towards it, reducing the context length requirements and expanding the method’s applicability. Note that MoA can extend to multiple rounds of aggregation (Appendix A.1), while Self-MoA and Self-MoA-Seq can extend to more outputs, but we omit them here for clarity.

4 The Quality-Diversity Trade-off4 质量与多样性的权衡
Figure 2: The diversity-quality trade-off: Mixed-MoA incorporates different individual models as proposers, while Self-MoA uses the same individual model for this role. Quality is assessed based on the average performance of each proposer, and diversity is computed with the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) of outputs generated by proposers on the same prompts.
图 2：多样性与质量的权衡：Mixed-MoA 采用不同的单个模型作为提议模型，而 Self-MoA 使用同一个模型承担这一角色。质量根据各提议模型的平均性能进行评估，多样性则通过计算提议模型针对相同提示生成的输出的 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 来衡量。
We investigate factors that contribute to the strong performance of Self-MoA through careful experiments. Previous studies have mainly focused on increasing model diversity within the group (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b). However, searching for diverse models can sometimes lead to including poorly performed models, resulting in a trade-off between diversity and quality, where quality refers to how well each individual model performs in the group.
我们通过精心设计的实验，研究促成 Self-MoA 出色表现的因素。先前的研究主要关注提高模型群体内部的多样性 (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b)。然而，寻找多样化模型有时会导致纳入表现较差的模型，从而在多样性与质量之间形成权衡，其中质量指群体中各个模型的表现水平。
Therefore, we aim to identify the existence of a general relationship between MoA’s performance and quality as well as diversity. Following Section 3, we evaluate MoA’s performance on MMLU, CRUX, and MATH, which cover tasks requiring a wide range of capabilities. We vary the quality and diversity with two orders of freedom: 1) combinations of individual models in proposers from Section 3.2; and 2) sampling temperature. i.e., 0.5, 0.7, 1.0, 1.1, and 1.2. This results in a total of over 70 unique MoA proposer mixtures. We measure the quality and diversity as follows:
因此，我们旨在确定 MoA 的性能与质量及多样性之间是否存在普遍关系。沿用第 3 节的设置，我们在 MMLU、CRUX 和 MATH 上评估 MoA 的性能，这些基准涵盖了需要广泛能力的任务。我们通过两个自由度来改变质量和多样性：1) 第 3.2 节中提议模型所采用的单个模型组合；2) 采样温度，即 0.5、0.7、1.0、1.1 和 1.2。由此共得到超过 70 种不同的 MoA 提议模型混合配置。我们按如下方式衡量质量和多样性：
Diversity: We utilize the Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) to assess the diversity among individual models in the proposer mixture. The Vendi Score represents the effective number of unique elements within a collection of samples (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023), with further details provided in the Appendix A.2. Specifically, for a given prompt $x$ , we obtain responses from each model, denoted as $y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{6}$ . The diversity of the proposers for prompt $x$ , denoted as $d(x)$ , is calculated using the Vendi Score on the set $[y_{1},\ldots,y_{6}]$ . We then compute the overall diversity across the dataset $S$ as:
多样性：我们使用 Vendi Score (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023) 来评估提议模型混合配置中各个模型之间的多样性。Vendi Score 表示样本集合中不同元素的有效数量 (Dan Friedman and Dieng, 2023)，更多细节见附录 A.2。具体而言，对于给定的提示 $x$，我们获取各个模型的回答，记为 $y_{1},y_{2},\ldots,y_{6}$。针对提示 $x$ 的提议模型多样性记为 $d(x)$，通过计算集合 $[y_{1},\ldots,y_{6}]$ 的 Vendi Score 得到。随后，我们按如下方式计算数据集 $S$ 上的总体多样性：
$$d=\frac{1}{|S|}\sum_{x\in S}d(x).$$
Quality: We first determine the accuracy of each model on the dataset $S$ , yielding values $q_{1},q_{2},\ldots,q_{6}$ . The average accuracy, $q=\frac{1}{6}(q_{1}+q_{2}+\ldots+q_{6})$ , serves as our measure of the quality of the proposers. We will explore additional quality measurement strategies in later sections.
质量：我们首先确定各个模型在数据集 $S$ 上的准确率，得到数值 $q_{1},q_{2},\ldots,q_{6}$。平均准确率 $q=\frac{1}{6}(q_{1}+q_{2}+\ldots+q_{6})$ 用作我们衡量提议模型质量的指标。我们将在后续章节中探索其他质量衡量策略。

However, as shown in Figure 2, compared to Self-MoA on the best-performing model, simply aiming for greater diversity in the proposer mixture can result in lower overall quality, which may negatively impact MoA’s performance. This trade-off between diversity and quality helps to explain why Self-MoA achieves superior performance across various benchmarks.
此前关于集成的研究 (Wang et al., 2024a; Jiang et al., 2023a; Zhang et al., 2024b) 主要侧重于提高提议模型组合中模型的多样性。然而，如图 2 所示，与基于表现最佳模型的 Self-MoA 相比，单纯追求提议模型组合的更高多样性可能导致整体质量下降，从而对 MoA 的性能产生负面影响。多样性与质量之间的这种权衡有助于解释为何 Self-MoA 能在各种基准测试中取得更优的性能。

Alternative quality measurements.其他质量度量方式。
We use the averaged accuracy of each individual model to measure quality in the previous analysis. In this section, we explore alternative methods for assessing the quality of proposers. Recall that $q_{1},\ldots,q_{6}$ denote the accuracy of each individual model among proposers, and without loss of generality, we assume $q_{1}\geq q_{2}\geq\ldots\geq q_{6}$ . It is reasonable to assume that the aggregator can select the correct answer from the proposers, particularly when the responses of individual models are inconsistent. In such cases, the aggregator would rely more heavily on models with better individual performance, meaning the weight of $q_{1}$ would be greater than that of $q_{6}$ .
在前面的分析中，我们使用各个模型准确率的平均值来衡量质量。在本节中，我们探索评估提议模型质量的其他方法。回顾前文，$q_{1},\ldots,q_{6}$ 表示各个提议模型的准确率，不失一般性，我们假设 $q_{1}\geq q_{2}\geq\ldots\geq q_{6}$。可以合理地假设，聚合模型能够从提议模型的回答中选出正确答案，尤其是在各个模型的回答不一致时。在这种情况下，聚合模型会更加依赖单独表现更好的模型，这意味着 $q_{1}$ 的权重会大于 $q_{6}$ 的权重。
Therefore, we compare the following methods to calculate quality:
因此，我们比较以下质量计算方法：
Average: $\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}$ .
平均值：$\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}$。
K-Norm: $\left(\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}^{K}\right)^{1/K}$ , where a larger $K$ places more emphasis on stronger individual models.
K-范数（K-Norm）：$\left(\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}q_{i}^{K}\right)^{1/K}$，其中，较大的 $K$ 会使表现更强的单个模型得到更多重视。
Centered-1/K-Norm: $q_{1}-\left(\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}(q_{1}-q_{i})^{1/K}\right)^{K}$ . In this formulation, we first compute the difference between $q_{i}$ and the best model’s $q_{1}$ . The $1/K$ norm emphasizes the weights of models whose performance is closer to $q_{1}$ .
中心化 1/K-范数（Centered-1/K-Norm）：$q_{1}-\left(\frac{1}{6}\sum_{i=1}^{6}(q_{1}-q_{i})^{1/K}\right)^{K}$。在这一公式中，我们首先计算 $q_{i}$ 与最佳模型的 $q_{1}$ 之间的差值。$1/K$ 范数会突出性能更接近 $q_{1}$ 的模型的权重。

5 Scaling Inference Compute with Self-MoA5 使用 Self-MoA 扩展推理计算量
In previous sections, we have provided evidence that Self-MoA over one strong model is straightforward but effective. As the community is becoming more aware of scaling inference time computing (Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024), one natural question to ask is:
在前面的章节中，我们提供了证据，表明基于一个强模型的 Self-MoA 方法简单而有效。随着研究界越来越重视扩展推理时的计算量（Brown et al., 2024; Snell et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2024），一个自然的问题是：
Given a strong model, does Self-MoA’s performance scale with the number of repeated samples?
给定一个强模型，Self-MoA 的性能是否会随着重复采样次数的增加而提升？
Intuitively, Self-MoA cannot scale indefinitely by simply increasing the computation budget for at least three reasons:
直观而言，Self-MoA 无法仅靠增加计算预算而无限提升性能，原因至少有以下三个：
As more responses are sampled from a single model, the diversity among those samples tends to plateau.
随着从单个模型中采样得到的回答增多，这些样本之间的多样性往往会趋于平台期。
Aggregating information from many samples is more challenging for LLMs compared to handling a smaller number of samples.
对于 LLM 而言，与处理较少的样本相比，从大量样本中聚合信息更具挑战性。
Every LLM has a context length limit (e.g., 8192 tokens for Gemma 2), which restricts the number of responses an aggregator can process at once.
每个 LLM 都有上下文长度限制（例如，Gemma 2 的限制为 8192 个词元），这限制了聚合模型一次能够处理的回答数量。
While the first limitation is inherent to repeated sampling, we address the latter two by introducing Self-MoA-Seq, a sequential variant designed to manage large numbers of responses without overwhelming the aggregator. Self-MoA-Seq uses a sliding window to aggregate a fixed number of responses at a time, allowing it to handle an unlimited number of responses, regardless of context length constraints. A visual illustration is provided in Figure 1.
虽然第一个限制是重复采样所固有的，但我们通过引入 Self-MoA-Seq 来应对后两个限制；这是一种顺序处理变体，旨在管理大量回答，同时避免使聚合模型负担过重。Self-MoA-Seq 使用滑动窗口，每次聚合固定数量的回答，从而能够处理任意数量的回答，不受上下文长度的约束。图 1 提供了直观示意。
We evaluate the performance of Self-MoA and Self-MoA-Seq with increasing sample sizes on the MMLU and CRUX benchmarks to study their scaling behavior. For each benchmark, we use the best-performing model as both the proposer and aggregator (Qwen2-7B-Instruct for MMLU and DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct for CRUX), with a sampling temperature of 0.7. In Self-MoA-Seq, the window size is set to six, with the first three slots reserved for the current synthesized output. 我们在 MMLU 和 CRUX 基准上评估了 Self-MoA 和 Self-MoA-Seq 在采样数量不断增加时的性能，以研究其扩展特性。对于每个基准测试，我们均使用表现最佳的模型同时作为提议模型和聚合模型（MMLU 使用 Qwen2-7B-Instruct，CRUX 使用 DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite-Instruct），采样温度设为 0.7。在 Self-MoA-Seq 中，窗口大小设为六，前三个位置预留给当前的综合输出。我们将样本数量从 6 增加到 30，并在图 3 中绘制了使用不同随机种子进行三次运行所得的准确率曲线。我们的主要观察结果如下：

与单个基础模型相比，Self-MoA 和 Self-MoA-Seq 均显著提升了性能。
Adding more samples can have both positive and negative effects, meaning there is no universal compute-optimal solution.
增加样本数量既可能产生正面影响，也可能产生负面影响，这意味着不存在普遍适用的计算最优方案。

6 Conclusion6 结论
In this paper, we introduce Self-MoA, an innovative approach that utilizes in-model diversity to enhance the performance of large language models during inference. Our experiments demonstrate that Self-MoA outperforms traditional Mixed-MoA strategies in many popular benchmarks, particularly when the proposer model quality varies. By aggregating outputs from a single high-performing model, Self-MoA effectively addresses the quality-diversity trade-off. We further identify the scenarios where mixing LLM can be potentially beneficial and extend Self-MoA to the constrained context length setting. These findings highlight the potential of in-model diversity in optimizing LLM performance and pave the way for further advancements in ensemble methods.

A.2 Vendi ScoreA.2 Vendi 分数
The Vendi Score (VS) is a metric designed to evaluate diversity in machine learning. It takes as input a collection of samples along with a pairwise similarity function, and it outputs a single value that represents the effective number of unique elements within the sample set.
Vendi 分数（Vendi Score，VS）是一种用于评估机器学习中多样性的指标。它以一组样本及一个成对相似度函数为输入，输出一个数值，表示样本集中独特元素的有效数量。
The score is computed using a positive semi-definite similarity matrix $K\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$ as follows:
该分数使用半正定相似度矩阵 $K\in\mathbb{R}^{n\times n}$，按如下方式计算：
$$VS(K)=\exp\left(-\text{tr}\left(\frac{K}{n}\log\left(\frac{K}{n}\right)\right)\right)=\exp\left(-\sum_{i=1}^{n}\lambda_{i}\log(\lambda_{i})\right)$$
Here, $\lambda_{i}$ are the eigenvalues of the normalized matrix $\frac{K}{n}$ , and $0\log 0=0$ . Essentially, the Vendi Score is the exponential of the von Neumann entropy of $\frac{K}{n}$ , which reflects the Shannon entropy of its eigenvalues, also referred to as the effective rank. This metric provides a quantitative measure of diversity based on the distribution of similarity scores among the samples.
这里，$\lambda_{i}$ 是归一化矩阵 $\frac{K}{n}$ 的特征值，且 $0\log 0=0$。本质上，Vendi 分数是 $\frac{K}{n}$ 的冯·诺依曼熵的指数，该熵反映了其特征值的香农熵，而所得分数也称为有效秩。该指标根据样本间相似度分数的分布，对多样性进行量化度量。
