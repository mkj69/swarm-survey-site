智能体通信语言（Agent Communication Language，ACL）由旨在供 AI 智能体相互通信的计算机通信协议组成。 Agent Communication Language (ACL) consists of computer communication protocols that are intended for AI agents to communicate with each other.
2007 年提出的此类协议包括： In 2007, protocols of this nature were proposed which include:
FIPA-ACL（由标准化联盟智能物理智能体基金会制定） FIPA-ACL (by the Foundation for Intelligent Physical Agents, a standardization consortium)
KQML（知识查询与操作语言） KQML (Knowledge Query and Manipulation Language)
随着基于大语言模型的生成式 AI 迅速发展，以及智能体式 AI 的出现，这一领域涌现出自然语言交互协议（Natural Language Interaction Protocol，NLIP）和 Agent2Agent 开放协议等新协议。 After the surge in generative AI based on the use of Large language models and the advent of Agentic AI, new protocols emerge in this area as the Natural Language Interaction Protocol (NLIP) or the Agent2Agent open protocol. NLIP 是一种为 AI 智能体之间或人类与 AI 智能体之间的通信而定义的应用层通信协议。 NLIP is an application-level communication protocol defined between AI Agents or between a human and an AI agent. Ecma International 是一个为信息与通信行业制定和发布国际标准的标准化机构，于 2025 年 12 月 10 日发布了五项新标准和一份技术报告，对自然语言交互协议（NLIP）进行了定义。 Ecma International; a standards body which develops and publishes international standards for the information and communication industry; published on 10 December 2025 five new standards and one technical report defining the Natural Language Interaction Protocol (NLIP).
因此，我们可以将智能体通信协议分为两类：基于本体的智能体通信协议和基于生成式 AI 的智能体通信协议。 As a result, we can define agent communication protocols into two categories: ontology based agent communication protocols and generative AI based agent communication protocols.
基于本体的智能体通信协议使用智能体之间共用的本体（ontology）。 Ontology based agent communication protocols use a common ontology to be used between agents. 本体是智能体知识库的一部分，用于描述智能体能够处理哪些类型的事物，以及这些事物之间如何相互关联。 An ontology is a part of the agent's knowledge base that describes what kind of things an agent can deal with and how they are related to each other. FIPA-ACL 和 KQML 是此类协议的例子。 FIPA-ACL and KQML are examples of such protocols. 这些协议以 Searle 在 1960 年代提出、并由 Winograd 和 Flores 在 1970 年代进一步完善的言语行为理论为基础。 These protocols rely on speech act theory developed by Searle in the 1960s and enhanced by Winograd and Flores in the 1970s. 它们定义了一组施为式（performatives），也称为交际行为（Communicative Acts），及其含义（例如 ask-one）。 They define a set of performatives, also called Communicative Acts, and their meaning (e.g. ask-one). 施为式的内容并未标准化，而是因系统而异。 The content of the performative is not standardized, but varies from system to system. NLIP 等基于生成式 AI 的智能体通信协议不要求通信的智能体之间共享本体。 Generative AI based agent communication protocols such as NLIP do not require a shared ontology among communicating agents. 取而代之的是，它们使用生成式 AI 模型，将自然语言文本、图像、视频或其他模态的数据转换为本地本体中的表示。 In its stead, they use generative AI models to translate natural language text, images, videos or other modalities of data into a local ontology. 这使得协议能够在运行时扩展，同一协议可用于多种通信需求；同时，由于不同智能体可以使用共享本体的不同版本，版本控制也得以简化。 This provides for hot-extensibility where the same protocol can be used for multiple communication needs, and simplifies version control since different agents can use different versions of a shared ontology.
NLIP 在设计时已考虑安全性。 NLIP has been designed with security considerations in mind. 构成 NLIP 的规范和标准由 Ecma 第 56 技术委员会制定和维护。 The specification and standards comprising NLIP are developed and maintained by Ecma Technical Community 56.
