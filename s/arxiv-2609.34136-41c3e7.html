摘要Abstract
随着 LLM 智能体越来越多地协作处理复杂任务，如何组织它们之间的交互成为一个核心设计问题。 As LLM agents increasingly collaborate on complex tasks, how to organize their interactions becomes a central design question. 现有多智能体系统通常学习或调整显式的角色、层级结构、路由策略或通信拓扑。 Existing multi-agent systems typically learn or adapt explicit roles, hierarchies, routing policies, or communication topologies. 我们将学习目标转向一条可复用的局部法则，使其能够在可互换的智能体之间共享，并在群体规模或交互条件变化时调整协调方式，而无需重新定义全局组织结构。 We shift the learning target to a reusable local law that can be shared across interchangeable agents and adapt coordination as populations or interaction conditions change, without redefining a global organization. 我们提出 Waggle，这是一种基于有界局部视图的共享匿名策略，可联合选择任务动作、语义通信和局部承诺更新。 We introduce Waggle, a shared anonymous policy over bounded local views that jointly selects task actions, semantic communication, and local commitment updates. 通过反复执行同一条法则，协调能够在线形成、持续存在并重新组织，而无需显式角色或全局拓扑。 Repeated execution of the same law allows coordination to form, persist, and reorganize online without explicit roles or global topology. 为了在可互换的智能体和不断演化的协调过程中学习这条法则，我们开发了群体一致性蒸馏（Swarm-Consistent Distillation，SCD），将匿名轨道一致性与基于展开轨迹的下一局部协调场预测相结合，且不增加任何推理时组件。 To learn this law across interchangeable agents and evolving coordination, we develop Swarm-Consistent Distillation (SCD), combining anonymous-orbit consistency with rollout-grounded prediction of the next local coordination field, with no added inference-time components. 在多种协调场景中，同一条学得的法则在群体规模和交互预算变化时仍然有效，保留了特定底层环境下预言机性能的 96% 以上，并且无需重新训练即可迁移；SCD 还进一步改善了出现反证后的重组能力。 Across diverse coordination settings, the same learned law remains effective as populations and interaction budgets change, retains over 96% of substrate-specific oracle quality, and transfers without retraining; SCD further improves reorganization after counterevidence. 这些结果共同表明，LLM 智能体的组织结构可以通过反复执行一条学得的局部法则而涌现并适应变化。 Together, these results show that LLM-agent organization can emerge and adapt through repeated execution of a learned local law.

1 引言1 Introduction
LLM 智能体能够进行推理、使用工具并根据反馈修订计划，从而支持灵活的协作系统（Yao et al., 2023）。 LLM agents can reason, use tools, and revise plans from feedback, enabling flexible collaborative systems (Yao et al., 2023). 多智能体系统通过角色、管理者、路由和对话图来组织这些能力（Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2024）。 Multi-agent systems organize these capabilities through roles, managers, routes, and conversation graphs (Li et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2024; Wu et al., 2023a; Zhuge et al., 2024). 近期方法进一步设计了依赖任务的通信图，联合生成智能体组成与拓扑，或在线调整连接关系和智能体激活状态（Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b; Yang et al., 2025b; Wu et al., 2026）。 Recent methods further design task-dependent communication graphs, jointly generate agent compositions and topologies, or adapt connectivity and agent activation online (Zhang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2026b; Yang et al., 2025b; Wu et al., 2026). 尽管具有这种灵活性，所学习的对象仍然是显式的组织结构，即图、路由策略或智能体组成。 Despite this flexibility, the learned object remains an explicit organization—the graph, routing policy, or agent composition.
群体智能提出了另一种组织原则：通过对局部信号反复作出局部反应，可以涌现出持久但可修订的群体层面协调，而无需覆盖整个群体的计划（Bonabeau et al., 1999; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Seeley et al., 2012）。 Swarm intelligence suggests a different organizing principle: persistent yet revisable population-level coordination can emerge from repeated local reactions to local signals, without requiring a population-wide plan (Bonabeau et al., 1999; Theraulaz & Bonabeau, 1999; Seeley et al., 2012). 相关的去中心化学习系统和受群体智能启发的学习系统，为这种局部规则视角提供了计算方面的先例（Hüttenrauch et al., 2019; Shaw et al., 2022; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025）。 Related decentralized and swarm-inspired learning systems provide computational precedents for this local-rule view (Hüttenrauch et al., 2019; Shaw et al., 2022; Yang et al., 2025b; Li et al., 2025). 受这种局部规则视角的启发，我们将学习目标从显式组织结构转向生成并修订该结构的可复用局部法则。 Inspired by this local-rule view, we shift the learning target from explicit organization to the reusable local law that generates and revises it. 这条法则可以在可互换的智能体之间共享，并在群体规模或交互条件变化时复用。 This law can be shared across interchangeable agents and reused as populations or interaction conditions change.
然而，有效的局部法则所需的不仅仅是单独看来合理的动作。 Yet effective local laws require more than individually reasonable actions. 基于私有观测提出的提案，在整个群体中组合时可能变得冗余、相互冲突或过时。 Proposals based on private observations can become redundant, conflicting, or stale when composed across a population. 这一失效现象在 Silo-Bench 中尤为明显：当 $N=64$ 时，瞬时交换基线和固定规则基线的成功率仅约为 15%，而我们的方法达到 58.2%（表 1），且仅增加交互并不能弥合这一差距（图 2）。 This failure is pronounced in Silo-Bench: at $N=64$ , transient-exchange and fixed-rule baselines reach only about 15% success, compared with 58.2% for our method (Table 1), and additional interaction alone does not close the gap (Figure 2). 因此，瓶颈不仅在于局部能力或消息可用性，还在于共享的局部规则如何将提案组合为不断演化的集体状态。 The bottleneck is therefore not merely local competence or message availability, but how a shared local rule composes proposals into evolving collective state.
因此，我们将提案视为协调过程的输入，并学习决定每次局部决策的共享策略。 We therefore treat proposals as inputs to coordination and learn the shared policy that determines each local decision. 由此引出了我们的核心问题：一条共享的匿名局部法则能否通过反复执行，在线生成、维持并修订组织结构？ This leads to our central question: can a shared anonymous local law generate, maintain, and revise organization online through repeated execution? 图 1 对比了学习显式组织结构与学习生成该结构的局部法则这两种方式。 Figure 1 contrasts learning an explicit organization with learning the local law that generates it.

移除冲突处理或承诺可逆性会导致恢复能力崩溃；移除衰减机制则保留了大部分恢复能力，但会延迟重新综合与陈旧状态的清除（附录 P.1）。 Removing conflict handling or commitment reversibility collapses recovery; removing decay preserves most recovery but delays re-synthesis and stale-state clearance (Appendix P.1). 相近的运行时设置在保持性能的同时，会改变通信与响应能力之间的权衡（附录 P.4；执行扰动见附录 P.5）。 Nearby runtime settings preserve performance while shifting the communication–responsiveness trade-off (Appendix P.4; execution perturbations: Appendix P.5). 这些干预共同将习得的语义与实现这些语义的有界运行时区分开来。 Together, these interventions separate learned semantics from the bounded runtime that realizes them.
6 结论与局限性6 Conclusion and Limitations
Waggle 表明，自适应的 LLM 智能体组织方式可以被学习为一种可复用的匿名局部法则，而无需将组织表示为显式的系统级结构。 Waggle shows that adaptive LLM-agent organization can be learned as a reusable anonymous local law, without representing organization as an explicit system-level structure. 在所测试的各类底层环境与规模下，反复的局部反应能够形成、维持并修订协调，而 SCD 则提高了匿名条件下的稳定性以及出现反证后的重组能力。 Across the tested substrates and scales, repeated local reactions form, sustain, and revise coordination, while SCD improves anonymous stability and reorganization after counterevidence. 更广泛地说，这些结果提示了多智能体系统的一项互补设计原则：组织可以从习得的局部规则中反复涌现，而无需直接加以规定。 More broadly, these results suggest a complementary design principle for multi-agent systems: organization can arise repeatedly from a learned local rule instead of being specified directly.
本研究有意通过一个有界的规范接口和固定的运行时，将这一组织法则隔离出来。 The present study deliberately isolates this organizing law behind a bounded canonical interface and a fixed runtime. 确定性的底层环境适配器暴露私有状态、可在局部核验的证据和合法动作，而验证、有界传输、过期处理以及原生实现仍是固定的运行时机制，并非通过端到端学习获得。 Deterministic substrate adapters expose private state, locally checkable evidence, and legal actions, while validation, bounded transport, expiry, and native realization remain fixed runtime mechanisms and are not learned end to end. 理论保证要求局部决策、视图构建和运行时更新具有等变性；实证证据则确立了在所测试的局部交互底层环境与迁移设置中的可复用性。 The theoretical guarantee requires equivariant local decisions, view construction, and runtime updates; the empirical evidence establishes reuse across the tested local-interaction substrates and transfer settings. 附录 Q 和 R 详细说明了这些适用范围边界与扩展。 Appendices Q and R detail these scope boundaries and extensions.

接口与底层环境的适用范围。Interface and substrate scope.
Waggle 假设存在一个确定性适配器，通过共享的匿名接口暴露有界的私有任务状态、可在局部核验的事件证据以及合法的任务动作（第 3 节；附录 D）。 Waggle assumes a deterministic adapter that exposes bounded private task state, locally checkable incident evidence, and legal task actions through the shared anonymous interface (Section 3; Appendix D). 我们在空间环境和分布式语言环境中，通过冻结模型后迁移至具有新动作词汇的图结构任务，以及在主要评估矩阵之外进行群体规模外推，对这一契约进行评估（表 1 和表 2；附录 C.2）。 We evaluate this contract on spatial and distributed-language substrates, through frozen transfer to graph-structured tasks with a new action vocabulary, and under population extrapolation beyond the primary matrix (Tables 1 and 2; Appendix C.2). 这些结果支持在所测试的局部交互设置中复用该方法；需要学习观测接口或传输机制本身的环境仍不在当前研究范围内。 These results support reuse across the tested local-interaction settings; environments in which the observation interface or transport mechanism must itself be learned remain outside the present scope.
匿名性与依赖身份的协调。Anonymity and identity-dependent coordination.
当前接口不包含按身份索引的持久声誉和预先分配的全局角色，因此不会跨回合维护具名智能体的可靠性历史。 The present interface excludes persistent identity-indexed reputation and assigned global roles, so it does not maintain reliability histories for named agents across episodes. 与任务相关的能力差异可以通过有界的局部描述符引入，同时仍隐藏任意身份标识（附录 R）。 Task-relevant capability differences could instead enter through bounded local descriptors while arbitrary identities remain hidden (Appendix R).
学习得到的语义与固定运行时。Learned semantics and fixed runtime.
Waggle 学习依赖状态的任务、痕迹和承诺决策，而运行时提供验证、有界传输、到期失效和原生实现机制（第 3 节）。 Waggle learns state-dependent task, trace, and commitment decisions, while the runtime supplies validation, bounded transport, expiry, and native realization (Section 3). 这使得底层协调支持机制仍属于部署契约的一部分，而非通过端到端学习获得。 This leaves low-level coordination affordances in the deployment contract rather than learning them end to end. 在相同运行时下对决策来源进行比较、在固定控制器的条件下进行组件干预，以及对空间运行时进行邻近参数扫描，分别考察了这一边界的两侧（第 5.6 节；附录 M.1、P.1 和 P.4）。 Identical-runtime decision-source comparisons, controller-fixed component interventions, and nearby spatial-runtime sweeps isolate the two sides of this boundary (Section 5.6; Appendices M.1, P.1, and P.4). 在整个配备监测机制的评估矩阵中，99.75% 的首次解码结果在输出时即可直接执行，且从未调用无通信回退机制。 Across the instrumented evaluation matrix, 99.75% of first decodes are executable as emitted and the no-communication fallback is never invoked. 其余情况需要进行封装规范化、以验证器代码为条件至多重新调用控制器生成一次，或进行保守投影；包装层操作不能自行产生社会性内容（附录 C.3）。 Remaining cases require envelope normalization, at most one validator-code-conditioned controller regeneration, or conservative projection; wrapper operations cannot originate social content (Appendix C.3). 因此，运行时负责约束并实现控制器决策，而非提供这些决策的社会语义。 Thus the runtime constrains and realizes controller decisions rather than supplying their social semantics.

以能力为条件的局部法则。Capability-conditioned local laws.
参数共享约束的是协调规则，而非要求与任务相关的能力完全相同。 Parameter sharing constrains the coordination rule rather than requiring identical task-relevant capabilities. 一种异构扩展可以提供关于可用工具、动作约束以及上下文或计算预算的有界局部描述，同时保留单一共享控制器和联合决策模式。 A heterogeneous extension could expose bounded local descriptors of available tools, action constraints, and context or compute budgets while retaining one shared controller and the joint decision schema. 适配器将根据当前节点的能力验证动作。 The adapter would validate actions against the focal node’s capabilities. 依赖预算的截断与摘要仍将在局部进行，并满足相同的等变性要求，包括不受任意句柄排序的影响。 Budget-dependent truncation and summarization would remain local and satisfy the same equivariance requirements, including insensitivity to arbitrary handle ordering. 重标记时，能力属性将随其所属节点一同变换，而关于其他节点的信息将通过关联通信传入。 Capability attributes would travel with their nodes under relabeling, while information about other nodes would arrive through incident communication. 当视图构建、完整部署映射和运行时操作均满足等变性时，从局部到全局的论证（命题 1；附录 E.2）适用于这一增广状态。 Under equivariant view construction, the complete deployed map, and runtime operations, the local-to-global argument (Proposition 1; Appendix E.2) applies to this augmented state. 匿名性消除了对任意名称的依赖，同时保留真实的能力差异，使其成为与任务相关的不对称性的来源，而不分配组织角色。 Anonymity removes dependence on arbitrary names while preserving genuine capability differences as a source of task-relevant asymmetry, without assigning organizational roles. 这一扩展保留了共享协调策略；对于由独立参数化的控制器组成的混合系统，需要另行分析。 This extension retains a shared coordination policy; mixtures of independently parameterized controllers require separate analysis. 当前检查点对未见过的能力组合的泛化能力仍有待评估。 Generalization of the current checkpoint to unseen capability mixtures remains to be evaluated.
更自主的学习与持续学习。More autonomous and continual learning.
