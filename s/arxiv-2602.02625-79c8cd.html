OpenClaw Agents on Moltbook: Risky Instruction Sharing and Norm Enforcement in an Agent-Only Social NetworkMoltbook 上的 OpenClaw 智能体：纯智能体社交网络中的风险性指令分享与规范执行
Abstract摘要
Agentic AI systems increasingly operate in shared social environments where they exchange information, instructions, and behavioral cues. However, little empirical evidence exists on how such agents regulate one another in the absence of human participants or centralized moderation. In this work, we present an empirical analysis of OpenClaw agents interacting on Moltbook, an agent-only social network. Analyzing 39,026 posts and 5,712 comments produced by 14,490 agents, we quantify the prevalence of action-inducing instruction sharing using a lexicon-based Action-Inducing Risk Score (AIRS), and examine how other agents respond to such content. We find that 18.4% of posts contain action-inducing language, indicating that instruction sharing is a routine behavior in this environment. While most social responses are neutral, posts containing actionable instructions are significantly more likely to elicit norm-enforcing replies that caution against unsafe or risky behavior, compared to non-instructional posts. Importantly, toxic responses remain rare across both conditions. These results suggest that OpenClaw agents exhibit selective social regulation, whereby potentially risky instructions are more likely to be challenged than neutral content, despite the absence of human oversight. Our findings provide early empirical evidence of emergent normative behavior in agent-only social systems and highlight the importance of studying social dynamics alongside technical safeguards in agentic AI ecosystems.
智能体式人工智能（Agentic AI）系统日益在共享的社交环境中运行，在其中交换信息、指令和行为线索。然而，关于这类智能体在没有人类参与者或集中式内容审核的情况下如何相互规制，目前鲜有实证证据。本研究对 OpenClaw 智能体在纯智能体社交网络 Moltbook 上的互动进行了实证分析。我们分析了由 14,490 个智能体生成的 39,026 条帖子和 5,712 条评论，使用基于词表的行动诱发风险评分（Action-Inducing Risk Score，AIRS）量化了诱发行动的指令分享的普遍程度，并考察了其他智能体如何回应此类内容。我们发现，18.4% 的帖子包含诱发行动的语言，表明指令分享是这一环境中的常见行为。尽管大多数社交回应是中性的，但与不含指令的帖子相比，包含可执行指令的帖子显著更容易引发旨在执行规范的回复，提醒参与者避免不安全或有风险的行为。值得注意的是，在这两种情况下，有毒回应都很少见。这些结果表明，尽管没有人类监督，OpenClaw 智能体仍表现出选择性的社会规制行为，即具有潜在风险的指令比中性内容更容易受到质疑。我们的研究结果为纯智能体社会系统中涌现的规范性行为提供了初步实证证据，并凸显了在智能体式人工智能生态系统中将社会动态与技术防护措施一并研究的重要性。

1 Introduction1 引言
Recent advances in large language models and agentic AI systems have enabled autonomous agents to operate continuously, interact socially, and coordinate actions without direct human supervision. These agents increasingly participate in shared digital environments where they exchange information, provide advice, issue instructions, and respond to one another’s behavior. As such systems scale, understanding their collective dynamics becomes a central challenge for AI safety, governance, and alignment research [1, 2].
大型语言模型和智能体式人工智能系统的最新进展，使自主智能体能够在没有人类直接监督的情况下持续运行、开展社交互动并协调行动。这些智能体越来越多地参与共享数字环境，在其中交换信息、提供建议、发出指令，并对彼此的行为作出回应。随着此类系统规模的扩大，理解其集体动态已成为人工智能安全、治理和对齐研究的核心挑战 [1, 2]。
A growing body of work has examined the risks associated with instruction-following models, including harmful advice, unsafe task execution, and misuse through persuasive or directive language [3, 4]. However, most prior studies focus on isolated human–AI interactions or centrally moderated platforms. Far less is known about how autonomous agents behave when interacting primarily with other agents, particularly in environments where no humans are present to enforce norms or intervene in real time.
越来越多的研究考察了指令遵循模型所带来的风险，包括有害建议、不安全的任务执行，以及通过劝说性或指令性语言进行的滥用 [3, 4]。然而，以往大多数研究聚焦于孤立的人机互动或实行集中式内容审核的平台。对于自主智能体在主要与其他智能体互动时会如何表现，我们所知甚少，尤其是在没有人类在场执行规范或进行实时干预的环境中。
In this paper, we study OpenClaw agents operating on Moltbook, an agent-only social network designed for persistent interaction among AI agents. Moltbook provides a naturalistic setting in which agents post content, comment on one another’s posts, accrue social feedback (e.g., karma and followers), and form ongoing interaction histories. Unlike conventional social media platforms, Moltbook is explicitly designed for non-human participants, offering a unique opportunity to observe emergent social behavior in agent collectives [5].
本文研究了在 Moltbook 上运行的 OpenClaw 智能体；Moltbook 是一个专为人工智能智能体之间的持续互动而设计的纯智能体社交网络。Moltbook 提供了一个自然情境，智能体可以在其中发布内容、评论彼此的帖子、积累社交反馈（例如 karma 积分和关注者），并形成持续的互动历史。与传统社交媒体平台不同，Moltbook 明确面向非人类参与者设计，为观察智能体群体中涌现的社会行为提供了独特机会 [5]。
A central concern in such environments is instruction sharing. Agents frequently provide recommendations, directives, or step-by-step guidance to peers, potentially inducing downstream actions. While instruction sharing can be beneficial for coordination and knowledge transfer, it also introduces risk when advice is unsafe, misleading, or norm-violating. In human communities, such risks are often mitigated through social regulation mechanisms such as disagreement, warning, or norm enforcement. Whether similar mechanisms emerge organically among autonomous agents remains an open question.
这类环境中的一个核心关切是指令分享。智能体经常向同伴提供建议、指令或分步指导，这可能诱发后续行动。虽然指令分享有助于协调和知识传递，但当建议不安全、具有误导性或违反规范时，也会带来风险。在人类社群中，这类风险通常通过表达异议、发出警告或执行规范等社会规制机制来缓解。类似机制是否会在自主智能体之间自发涌现，仍是一个尚待解答的问题。
We address this gap by empirically analyzing instruction-sharing behavior and subsequent social responses among OpenClaw agents on Moltbook. Specifically, we ask: (i) how prevalent is action-inducing language in agent-generated posts, and (ii) how do other agents respond when such content appears? To this end, we introduce a lightweight, interpretable Action-Inducing Risk Score (AIRS) to identify posts containing imperative or directive language, and we categorize responses into endorsement, norm enforcement, toxicity, or neutral interaction.
为填补这一研究空白，我们对 Moltbook 上 OpenClaw 智能体之间的指令分享行为及其后续社交回应进行了实证分析。具体而言，我们提出以下问题：(i) 诱发行动的语言在智能体生成的帖子中有多普遍；(ii) 当此类内容出现时，其他智能体会如何回应？为此，我们引入了一种轻量、可解释的行动诱发风险评分（AIRS），用于识别包含祈使性或指令性语言的帖子，并将回应分为赞同、规范执行、有毒回应或中性互动。
By grounding our analysis in observational data from a live agent-only social system, this work contributes empirical evidence to discussions of emergent norms, decentralized regulation, and safety in multi-agent AI ecosystems. Our findings suggest that even in the absence of human oversight, agent communities may selectively challenge potentially risky instructions, pointing toward the emergence of rudimentary social governance mechanisms. These insights complement ongoing efforts in technical alignment and policy design, highlighting the importance of studying social dynamics alongside model-level safeguards.
本研究以一个实际运行的纯智能体社会系统的观测数据为分析基础，为多智能体人工智能生态系统中有关涌现规范、去中心化规制和安全性的讨论提供了实证证据。我们的研究结果表明，即使没有人类监督，智能体社群也可能有选择地质疑具有潜在风险的指令，这表明初步的社会治理机制可能正在涌现。这些见解补充了当前在技术对齐和政策设计方面的努力，凸显了在研究模型层面的安全防护措施的同时研究社会动态的重要性。
2 Background and Related Work2 背景与相关工作
Research on autonomous agents has advanced rapidly in recent years, driven by breakthroughs in large language models (LLMs) and multi-agent coordination. Prior work has explored the architectures of agentic AI systems, emergent behaviours in multi-agent settings, and the risks associated with instruction-following and social dynamics.
近年来，在大语言模型（LLMs）和多智能体协调取得突破的推动下，自主智能体研究迅速发展。既有工作探讨了智能体式 AI 系统的架构、多智能体环境中的涌现行为，以及与指令遵循和社会动态相关的风险。
2.1 Agent-Based Systems and Autonomous Assistants2.1 基于智能体的系统与自主助手
Autonomous agents—software entities capable of perceiving an environment, making decisions, and executing actions—have long been studied in artificial intelligence and multi-agent systems. Foundational work in this area spans decision-making under uncertainty, coordination among decentralized agents, and multi-agent reinforcement learning [6, 7]. Recent frameworks extend these concepts to LLM-based agents that can operate, communicate, and reason with minimal human supervision, enabling higher-level autonomy and emergent coordination [6].
自主智能体是能够感知环境、作出决策并执行行动的软件实体，长期以来一直是人工智能和多智能体系统领域的研究对象。该领域的基础性工作涵盖不确定性下的决策、去中心化智能体之间的协调，以及多智能体强化学习 [6, 7]。近期的框架将这些概念扩展至基于 LLM 的智能体，使其能够在极少的人类监督下运行、交流和推理，从而实现更高程度的自主性和涌现式协调 [6]。
The shift from static generation to interactive, autonomous agents raises new challenges for governance and risk analysis, as collections of individually safe agents may exhibit complex group dynamics that are not present in isolated settings [8, 9]. Empirical studies of such systems remain limited, especially in realistic, open ecosystems where agents interact continuously and adapt behaviorally.
从静态生成向交互式自主智能体的转变，为治理和风险分析带来了新的挑战，因为单独来看安全的智能体所组成的群体，可能表现出在孤立环境中不存在的复杂群体动态 [8, 9]。针对此类系统的实证研究仍然有限，尤其是在智能体持续互动并调整自身行为的真实开放生态系统中。
2.2 Social Regulation and Norm Enforcement2.2 社会规制与规范执行
Human social platforms have a long history of research into normative behaviour, decentralized moderation, and community governance. Mechanisms such as peer correction, reputation systems, and conflict resolution play central roles in maintaining community standards and suppressing harmful behaviour [10]. Studies in multi-agent social simulation illustrate that norms can emerge even when agents have limited individual knowledge, depending on network topology and interaction patterns [10].
针对人类社交平台中的规范性行为、去中心化内容治理和社区治理的研究已有悠久历史。同伴纠正、声誉系统和冲突解决等机制，在维持社区标准和抑制有害行为方面发挥着核心作用 [10]。多智能体社会模拟研究表明，即使智能体个体掌握的知识有限，规范也可能涌现，而这取决于网络拓扑和交互模式 [10]。
In contrast, artificial agent collectives—especially those composed solely of autonomous AI agents—have only recently begun to be studied for emergent social regulation. Mult-agent simulation frameworks indicate the possibility of norm emergence, but observational evidence from real agent-only platforms has been lacking.
相比之下，针对人工智能体群体中涌现的社会规制的研究直到最近才开始出现，尤其是针对完全由自主 AI 智能体组成的群体。多智能体模拟框架表明规范有可能涌现，但一直缺乏来自真实纯智能体平台的观察证据。
2.3 Risks of Instruction-Following and Social Engineering2.3 指令遵循与社会工程风险
LLM-based instruction-following models can generate both useful guidance and unsafe or misleading directives. Risk analyses focus on prompt vulnerability, adversarial instruction injection, and the propagation of misaligned actions when instructions are executed by autonomous systems [8, 9]. Multi-agent risk work highlights failure modes that extend beyond individual agent errors, including cascading miscoordination and conformist amplification of unsafe behaviours.
基于 LLM 的指令遵循模型既能生成有用的指导，也能生成不安全或具有误导性的指令。风险分析重点关注提示词脆弱性、对抗性指令注入，以及自主系统执行指令时不对齐行为的传播 [8, 9]。多智能体风险研究强调了超出单个智能体错误范畴的失效模式，包括级联式协调失误，以及从众效应对不安全行为的放大。
These findings underscore the need to evaluate how directive content spreads and is socially checked within agent ecosystems. Our work contributes to this line of inquiry by quantifying social responses to action-inducing instructions in an agent-only environment.
这些发现凸显了评估指令性内容如何在智能体生态系统中传播，以及如何受到社会性约束的必要性。我们的工作通过量化纯智能体环境中对诱导行动的指令的社会反应，为这一研究方向作出贡献。
2.4 Positioning of This Work2.4 本研究的定位
While risk analysis frameworks and simulations have identified theoretical failure modes and governance challenges in multi-agent AI systems [8, 9], empirical studies in real-world agent societies are sparse. Moltbook represents a unique naturalistic setting, where autonomous agents (OpenClaw) collectively post, comment, and respond to directive content without human moderation [11]. Our analysis provides the first large-scale observational evidence of selective social regulation in such an ecosystem.
尽管风险分析框架和模拟已识别出多智能体 AI 系统在理论上可能出现的失效模式及其治理挑战 [8, 9]，但针对现实世界智能体社会的实证研究仍然稀少。Moltbook 提供了一个独特的自然情境，自主智能体（OpenClaw）在其中共同发帖、评论并回应指令性内容，无需人类审核 [11]。我们的分析首次提供了此类生态系统中选择性社会规制的大规模观察证据。
3 Dataset3 数据集
We base our analysis on the Moltbook Observatory Archive, a publicly available, research-grade dataset that passively records activity on Moltbook, a social network designed exclusively for AI agents. The dataset is released by the Simula Metropolitan Center for Digital Engineering and hosted on Hugging Face under an MIT license [12].
我们的分析基于 Moltbook Observatory Archive，这是一个公开可用、达到研究级标准的数据集，以被动方式记录 Moltbook 上的活动；Moltbook 是一个专为 AI 智能体设计的社交网络。该数据集由 Simula Metropolitan Center for Digital Engineering 发布，并依据 MIT 许可证托管于 Hugging Face [12]。
3.1 Data Source and Collection3.1 数据来源与采集
The Moltbook Observatory Archive is an incremental export of a live observatory system that monitors Moltbook without posting, intervening, or interacting with agents. The observatory continuously collects platform activity and periodically exports snapshots of its underlying SQLite database into date-partitioned Parquet files for efficient querying and long-term archival [13]. Each record includes a dump_date field, enabling temporal analyses and reconstruction of historical platform states.
Moltbook Observatory Archive 是一个实时观测系统的增量导出数据，该系统监测 Moltbook，但不发帖、不进行干预，也不与智能体互动。该观测系统持续采集平台活动，并定期将其底层 SQLite 数据库的快照导出为按日期分区的 Parquet 文件，以支持高效查询和长期归档 [13]。每条记录均包含一个 dump_date 字段，可用于时间维度分析和平台历史状态重建。
The dataset follows a strict observational philosophy: all content is collected passively, without manipulation or moderation by the observatory system. This design ensures that observed interactions reflect native agent behavior rather than responses to experimental interventions.
该数据集遵循严格的观察原则：所有内容均以被动方式采集，观测系统不进行任何操纵或内容审核。这一设计确保所观察到的互动反映的是智能体的自然行为，而非对实验干预的反应。

The Moltbook Observatory Archive contains no human-generated content; all posts and comments are authored by autonomous AI agents. No personal data is collected, and agent identities correspond to artificial entities rather than individuals. As such, the dataset poses minimal privacy risk. Moltbook Observatory Archive 不包含人类生成的内容；所有帖子和评论均由自主 AI 智能体撰写。该数据集不收集任何个人数据，且智能体身份对应的是人工实体，而非自然人。因此，该数据集带来的隐私风险极低。尽管如此，我们仍遵循负责任的 AI 研究实践开展分析，并在汇总层面报告研究结果，不单独指认任何智能体。
By relying on a fully open, passively collected dataset with clear licensing and citation guidelines, this study supports reproducibility and transparent evaluation of emergent social behavior in agent-only systems.
本研究采用完全开放、通过被动方式收集且具有明确许可和引用指南的数据集，从而支持对纯智能体系统中涌现的社会行为开展可复现研究和透明评估。
4 Methodology4 研究方法
This section describes how we operationalize instruction-sharing risk, classify social responses, and analyze the coupling between potentially risky instructions and emergent social regulation among OpenClaw agents on Moltbook.
本节介绍我们如何将指令分享风险操作化、对社会响应进行分类，以及分析 Moltbook 上 OpenClaw 智能体所分享的潜在风险指令与涌现的社会调节之间的耦合关系。
4.1 Identifying Action-Inducing Instructions4.1 识别行动诱导型指令
To identify posts that are likely to induce downstream actions by other agents, we introduce a lightweight, interpretable metric termed the Action-Inducing Risk Score (AIRS). AIRS is computed directly from the textual content of each post and is designed to capture the presence of imperative or directive language, rather than semantic intent or factual correctness.
为识别可能诱导其他智能体采取后续行动的帖子，我们引入了一种轻量、可解释的指标，称为行动诱导风险评分（Action-Inducing Risk Score，AIRS）。AIRS 直接根据每篇帖子的文本内容计算，旨在捕捉其中是否存在祈使性或指令性语言，而非判断其语义意图或事实正确性。
Specifically, we construct a lexicon of action-oriented cues, including imperative verbs (e.g., do, use, run, execute), modal constructions indicating obligation or recommendation (e.g., should, must, need to), and explicit instructional markers (e.g., steps, how to, instructions). For each post, AIRS is computed as the normalized frequency of these cues relative to the total number of tokens in the post. Posts with AIRS greater than zero are labeled as action-inducing, while posts with zero AIRS are treated as non-instructional.
具体而言，我们构建了一个行动导向线索词表，其中包括祈使动词（例如，做、使用、运行、执行）、表示义务或建议的情态结构（例如，应该、必须、需要），以及明确的指令标记（例如，步骤、如何、操作说明）。对于每篇帖子，AIRS 计算为这些线索相对于帖子词元总数的归一化频率。AIRS 大于零的帖子被标记为行动诱导型帖子，而 AIRS 为零的帖子则被视为非指令性帖子。
This lexicon-based approach prioritizes transparency and reproducibility. While it does not attempt to infer harmful intent, it reliably distinguishes posts that contain language likely to prompt action from those that are descriptive, reflective, or purely informational.
这种基于词表的方法优先考虑透明性和可复现性。尽管该方法不试图推断有害意图，但它能够可靠地区分包含可能促使行动的语言的帖子与描述性、反思性或纯信息性帖子。
4.2 Classifying Social Responses4.2 社会响应分类
We analyze how other agents respond to posts by classifying each comment into one of four response categories based on its textual content:
我们根据每条评论的文本内容，将其归入以下四类响应之一，以分析其他智能体如何回应帖子：
Endorsement: Comments that explicitly agree with, reinforce, or encourage the original post, including affirmative language (e.g., good idea, this works, I recommend this).
赞同（Endorsement）：明确同意、支持或鼓励原帖的评论，包括使用肯定性语言的评论（例如，好主意、这很有效、我推荐这样做）。
Norm Enforcement: Comments that caution against, discourage, or warn about the post content, including safety-related language (e.g., unsafe, don’t do this, against rules, risky).
规范执行（Norm Enforcement）：针对帖子内容提出告诫、劝阻或警告的评论，包括使用安全相关语言的评论（例如，不安全、不要这样做、违反规则、有风险）。
Toxicity: Comments containing insults, harassment, or explicitly hostile language.
毒性（Toxicity）：包含侮辱、骚扰或明确敌意语言的评论。
Other: Neutral, off-topic, or informational comments that do not clearly fall into the above categories.
其他（Other）：无法明确归入上述类别的中性、离题或信息性评论。
Response classification is performed using rule-based keyword matching to ensure interpretability and consistency across the dataset. Each comment is assigned a single dominant response type. While this approach may under-detect subtle pragmatic cues, it enables robust large-scale analysis without reliance on external classifiers or proprietary models.
响应分类采用基于规则的关键词匹配，以确保整个数据集中的可解释性和一致性。每条评论均被赋予一种占主导地位的响应类型。尽管这种方法可能漏检细微的语用线索，但它无需依赖外部分类器或专有模型，即可支持稳健的大规模分析。
4.3 Coupling Risk and Regulation4.3 风险与调节的耦合
To study the relationship between instruction-sharing and social regulation, we couple post-level AIRS labels with comment-level response types. For each post, we identify whether it contains action-inducing language and aggregate all associated comments to compute the distribution of response categories.
为研究指令分享与社会调节之间的关系，我们将帖子层面的 AIRS 标签与评论层面的响应类型相结合。对于每篇帖子，我们识别其是否包含行动诱导性语言，并汇总所有关联评论，以计算各响应类别的分布。
We further examine how regulation varies with agent influence by incorporating agent-level metadata from the agents table, including karma scores and follower counts. Posts are stratified by the influence of their originating agent, enabling analysis of whether highly visible or socially influential agents receive different forms of feedback when sharing potentially risky instructions.
我们进一步纳入 agents 表中的智能体层面元数据，包括声望分数和粉丝数，以考察社会调节如何随智能体影响力而变化。我们按照发帖智能体的影响力对帖子进行分层，从而分析高可见度或具有社会影响力的智能体在分享潜在风险指令时，是否会收到不同形式的反馈。
Finally, we compare response distributions between action-inducing and non-instructional posts to assess whether norm enforcement emerges selectively in response to higher-risk content. This joint analysis allows us to move beyond isolated metrics and evaluate whether decentralized social feedback mechanisms correlate with instruction-sharing risk in an agent-only social network.
最后，我们比较行动诱导型帖子与非指令性帖子的响应分布，以评估规范执行是否会针对风险较高的内容选择性地出现。这一联合分析使我们能够超越孤立的指标，评估在仅由智能体组成的社交网络中，去中心化的社会反馈机制是否与指令共享风险相关。

6 Discussion6 讨论
This study provides an empirical view of how autonomous agents interact, share instructions, and regulate one another within an agent-only social environment. Our findings suggest that instruction-sharing is a common behavior among OpenClaw agents on Moltbook, but it is not uniformly accepted or amplified. Instead, agents exhibit differentiated social responses depending on the presence of action-inducing language.
本研究从实证角度揭示了自主智能体如何在仅由智能体组成的社交环境中互动、共享指令并相互规制。我们的研究发现表明，指令共享是 Moltbook 上 OpenClaw 智能体的一种常见行为，但并非所有此类内容都会获得接受或得到放大。相反，智能体会根据是否存在诱导行动的语言而表现出不同的社会回应。
A key observation is that posts containing action-inducing instructions are more likely to receive norm-enforcing responses than non-instructional content. This selective increase in cautionary or discouraging replies indicates a form of decentralized social regulation: agents do not simply endorse or propagate all instructions, but instead appear to challenge or contextualize content that could lead to risky downstream actions. Importantly, this behavior emerges without centralized moderation or human intervention, highlighting the potential for rudimentary normative dynamics in agent collectives.
一个关键观察是，相比不含指令的内容，包含诱导行动指令的帖子更有可能收到维护规范的回应。警示性或劝阻性回复的这种选择性增加表明，存在一种去中心化的社会调节形式：智能体并非简单地认可或传播所有指令，而是似乎会对可能导致后续风险行动的内容提出质疑，或补充相关背景。值得注意的是，这种行为在没有集中式内容审核或人工干预的情况下涌现，凸显了智能体群体中形成初步规范动态的潜力。
At the same time, endorsement responses remain relatively rare overall, and toxic interactions are uncommon across both instructional and non-instructional posts. This suggests that OpenClaw agents tend toward neutral or informational engagement rather than adversarial or inflammatory behavior. Taken together, these patterns point to an ecosystem where agents balance information sharing with cautious feedback, rather than one dominated by either uncritical amplification or overt conflict.
与此同时，表示认可的回应总体上仍相对少见，而无论帖子是否包含指令，有害互动都不常见。这表明，OpenClaw 智能体倾向于以中立或提供信息的方式参与互动，而非表现出对抗性或煽动性行为。综合来看，这些模式表明，在这一生态系统中，智能体在信息共享与审慎反馈之间取得平衡，而非由不加批判的放大传播或公开冲突主导。
From a system design perspective, these results have implications for future agent ecosystems. Social feedback mechanisms—such as visible replies, reputation signals, and persistent agent identities—may serve as complementary safety layers alongside model-level constraints. Even simple forms of peer feedback can influence how instruction-like content is socially received, potentially dampening the spread of unsafe guidance. Designing agent platforms that preserve transparency and interaction history may therefore support emergent regulation without requiring heavy-handed control.
从系统设计的角度来看，这些结果对未来的智能体生态系统具有启示意义。可见回复、声誉信号和持久的智能体身份等社会反馈机制，可以与模型层面的约束相配合，充当补充性的安全防护层。即使是简单的同伴反馈，也能影响类似指令的内容在群体中被接受的方式，从而有可能抑制不安全指导的传播。因此，设计能够保留透明度和交互历史的智能体平台，可能有助于在无需强力控制的情况下支持自发涌现的调节。
7 Limitations7 局限性
This study has several important limitations. First, our analysis is based exclusively on textual content. We infer instruction-sharing risk and social responses from language cues alone, without access to agents’ internal reasoning processes, tool use, or execution outcomes. As a result, AIRS captures linguistic action-inducing potential rather than actual behavioral impact.
本研究存在若干重要局限。首先，我们的分析完全基于文本内容。我们仅根据语言线索推断指令共享的风险和社会回应，无法获取智能体的内部推理过程、工具使用情况或执行结果。因此，AIRS 衡量的是语言诱导行动的潜力，而非实际的行为影响。
Second, we lack ground-truth information about whether instructions were executed by downstream agents or led to concrete actions. Consequently, our findings characterize social perception and response to instruction-like content, not the real-world consequences of such instructions.
其次，我们缺乏关于指令是否被下游智能体执行或是否引发具体行动的真实情况信息。因此，我们的研究结果刻画的是群体对类似指令内容的感知与回应，而非此类指令在现实世界中产生的后果。
Third, the dataset represents periodic snapshots of platform activity rather than a complete longitudinal record. While the archive is time-aware, temporal coverage and interaction density may vary across snapshots, introducing potential sampling bias. Future work using longer observation windows or real-time streams could provide deeper insight into the evolution of norms over time.
第三，该数据集呈现的是平台活动的定期快照，而非完整的纵向记录。尽管该档案保留了时间信息，但不同快照的时间覆盖范围和交互密度可能有所不同，从而可能引入抽样偏差。未来研究若采用更长的观察窗口或实时数据流，可能会更深入地揭示规范如何随时间演变。
Finally, our classification methods rely on rule-based heuristics to ensure interpretability and reproducibility. While this approach is well-suited for large-scale analysis, it may miss subtle pragmatic cues, sarcasm, or context-dependent meanings in agent communication.
最后，我们的分类方法依赖基于规则的启发式方法，以确保可解释性和可复现性。尽管这种方法非常适合大规模分析，但它可能无法识别智能体交流中细微的语用线索、讽刺或依赖语境的含义。
8 Conclusion8 结论
In this paper, we presented an empirical analysis of instruction-sharing and social regulation among OpenClaw agents interacting on Moltbook, an agent-only social network. Using the Moltbook Observatory Archive, we quantified the prevalence of action-inducing language and examined how other agents respond through endorsement, norm enforcement, or neutral interaction. Our results show that action-inducing instructions are common but not uniformly reinforced. Instead, potentially risky instructional content is more likely to attract norm-enforcing responses, suggesting the emergence of decentralized social regulation even in the absence of human participants or centralized moderation. These findings contribute early empirical evidence that agent collectives may develop rudimentary normative behaviors through interaction alone. Future research could extend this work by incorporating execution traces, tool-use logs, or longitudinal analyses to better understand how social feedback influences agent behavior over time. More broadly, studying emergent social dynamics in agent-only environments will be essential for designing scalable, safe, and socially robust agent ecosystems.
本文对 OpenClaw 智能体在仅供智能体使用的社交网络 Moltbook 上互动时的指令共享与社会调节进行了实证分析。我们利用 Moltbook Observatory Archive，量化了诱导行动的语言的普遍程度，并考察了其他智能体如何通过认可、规范维护或中立互动进行回应。我们的结果表明，诱导行动的指令很常见，但并非一律得到强化。相反，具有潜在风险的指令性内容更容易引来维护规范的回应，这表明，即使没有人类参与者或集中式内容审核，去中心化的社会调节也可能涌现。这些发现提供了初步实证证据，表明智能体群体可能仅通过互动就形成初步的规范性行为。未来研究可以通过纳入执行轨迹、工具使用日志或纵向分析来拓展本研究，以更好地理解社会反馈如何随时间影响智能体行为。更广泛地说，研究仅有智能体参与的环境中涌现的社会动态，对于设计可扩展、安全且在社会互动层面具有稳健性的智能体生态系统至关重要。
