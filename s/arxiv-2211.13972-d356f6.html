总是针对同一个人：算法单一化（Algorithmic Monoculture）是否会导致结果同质化（Outcome Homogenization）？Picking on the Same Person: Does Algorithmic Monoculture lead to Outcome Homogenization?
摘要Abstract
随着机器学习的应用范围不断扩大，我们观察到一种反复出现的现象，即算法单一化：多个决策者部署相同的系统，或共享组件（例如训练数据）的系统。 As the scope of machine learning broadens, we observe a recurring theme of algorithmic monoculture: the same systems, or systems that share components (e.g. training data), are deployed by multiple decision-makers. 尽管共享具有明显优势（例如分摊成本），但它是否也伴随着风险？ While sharing offers clear advantages (e.g. amortizing costs), does it bear risks? 我们提出并形式化了其中一种风险，即结果同质化：特定个人或群体从所有决策者那里得到负面结果的程度。 We introduce and formalize one such risk, outcome homogenization: the extent to which particular individuals or groups experience negative outcomes from all decision-makers. 如果总是只有相同的个人或群体遭遇不利结果，这可能会使系统性排斥制度化，并强化既有的社会等级结构。 If the same individuals or groups exclusively experience undesirable outcomes, this may institutionalize systemic exclusion and reinscribe social hierarchy. 为建立算法单一化与结果同质化之间的联系，我们提出组件共享假说（component-sharing hypothesis）：如果决策者共享训练数据或特定模型等组件，那么他们产生的结果就会更加同质化。 To relate algorithmic monoculture and outcome homogenization, we propose the component-sharing hypothesis: if decision-makers share components like training data or specific models, then they will produce more homogeneous outcomes. 我们在算法公平性基准上检验了这一假说，结果表明，共享训练数据会稳定地加剧同质化，而且个体层面的影响通常大于群体层面的影响。 We test this hypothesis on algorithmic fairness benchmarks, demonstrating that sharing training data reliably exacerbates homogenization, with individual-level effects generally exceeding group-level effects. 此外，鉴于基础模型（foundation models），即可适配多种下游任务的模型，已成为人工智能领域的主导范式，我们检验了模型共享是否会使不同任务的结果同质化。 Further, given the dominant paradigm in AI of foundation models, i.e. models that can be adapted for myriad downstream tasks, we test whether model sharing homogenizes outcomes across tasks. 我们观察到的结果并不一致：在视觉和语言两类场景中，适配基础模型所采用的具体方法都会显著影响结果同质化的程度。 We observe mixed results: we find that for both vision and language settings, the specific methods for adapting a foundation model significantly influence the degree of outcome homogenization. 最后，我们对结果同质化进行哲学分析，并探讨其带来的社会挑战，同时关注这些讨论对已部署机器学习系统的启示。 We conclude with philosophical analyses of and societal challenges for outcome homogenization, with an eye towards implications for deployed machine learning systems.
1 引言1 Introduction
机器学习建立在深厚的共享传统之上：我们共享数据集（例如 ImageNet）、模型（例如 BERT）、库（例如 PyTorch）、优化器（例如 Adam）、评测（例如 SuperGLUE），以及更多资源。 Machine learning is built on strong traditions of sharing: we share datasets (e.g. ImageNet), models (e.g. BERT), libraries (e.g. PyTorch), optimizers (e.g. Adam), evaluations (e.g. SuperGLUE) and much more. 这种共享精神使该领域受益良多：我们能够反复利用构建高质量资源所付出的努力（例如，ImageNet 已为数千名计算机视觉研究人员提供支持），而对这些资源的改进也能带来广泛的益处（例如，BERT 推动了 NLP 领域的整体进步）。 This ethos of sharing serves the field well: we are able to repeatedly capitalize on the effort required to build high-quality assets (e.g. ImageNet has supported thousands of researchers in computer vision), and improvements to these assets have sweeping benefits (e.g. BERT raised all boats in NLP). 然而，共享是否也存在风险？ Yet does sharing also have risks? 该领域的这一核心制度是否可能导致不利结果？ Could this core institution of the field lead to undesirable outcomes?
我们观察到，某些共享形式可以被重新理解为单一化：Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 将算法单一化定义为“许多决策者都依赖于[完全]相同的算法”的状态。 We observe that certain forms of sharing can be reinterpreted as monoculture: Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) define algorithmic monoculture as the state "in which many decision-makers all rely on the [exact] same algorithm." 在算法系统无处不在的某些社会领域，我们看到了走向这种单一化的趋势 Moore and Tambini (2018); Engler (2021)。 In parts of society where algorithmic systems are ubiquitous, we see trends towards such monoculture Moore and Tambini (2018); Engler (2021). 单一化往往带来严重风险：Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 表明，当决策者的决策相互关联时，例如在竞争招聘求职者时，单一化对决策者而言并非最优。 Monocultures often pose serious risks: Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) show monoculture is suboptimal for decision-makers when their decisions are interconnected, as when they compete to hire job candidates. 在 ML 中，我们的共享实践往往比共享整个算法系统更加复杂：我们是否应该将 ML 中共享资源的实践视为单一化？如果是，我们应该担心哪些危害？ In ML, our sharing practices often are more complex than sharing the entire algorithmic system: should we think of our practices of sharing assets in ML as monoculture and, if so, what harms should we worry about?
我们通过提出结果同质化这一风险来研究这个问题，即个人（或群体）从其接触的所有决策者那里得到的均为负面结果的现象。 We investigate this question by introducing the risk of outcome homogenization, i.e. the phenomenon of individuals (or groups) exclusively receiving negative outcomes from all decision-makers they interact with. 例如，由于所有公司都使用类似的算法简历筛选系统，一名求职者可能在申请的每一个岗位上都遭到拒绝。 For example, a job applicant may be rejected from every job they apply to due to the use of similar algorithmic resume screening systems at all companies. 同质化的结果会对个人造成严重伤害，尤其是在某些高风险场景中：一个在所有就业机会中都遭到拒绝，或被所有学校拒绝录取的人，可能会陷入严重困境（例如，无法养家糊口，无法获得教育机会）。 Homogeneous outcomes, especially in certain high-stakes settings, constitute serious harms to individuals: someone who is rejected from every employment opportunity, or denied admission at every school, may be severely compromised (e.g. unable to provide for their family, unable to secure an education). 我们将结果同质化视为研究社会系统时所揭示的一类重要的系统性危害，即需要通过观察多个决策者如何对待个人才能发现的危害。11 1 事实上，即使不存在算法单一化，也可能出现结果同质化这种系统性危害，不过本研究仅限于存在单一化的场景。 We view outcome homogenization as an important class of systemic harms that arise when we study social systems, i.e. harms that require observing how individuals are treated by many decision-makers.11 1 In fact, outcome homogenization is a systemic harm that may arise even in the absence of algorithmic monoculture, though this work is restricted to settings where monoculture is present. 在 §2 中，我们以（算法）招聘为背景，从概念层面阐明研究结果同质化的动机。 In §2, we conceptually motivate outcome homogenization in the context of (algorithmic) hiring. 在 §3 中，我们首次提出结果同质化的数学形式化描述：我们以观察到的系统性失败概率除以基准率，作为同质化的度量。 In §3, we introduce the first mathematical formalism for outcome homogenization: we measure homogenization as the observed probability of systemic failure normalized by a base rate.
为将 ML 中共享这一社会实践与结果同质化这一社会危害联系起来，我们提出并检验了组件共享假说。 To link the social practice of sharing in ML with the social harm of outcome homogenization, we pose and test the component-sharing hypothesis. 如果决策者共享相同的底层组件，例如训练数据和机器学习模型，那么他们往往会系统性地使相同的个人或群体遭遇不利结果。 If decision-makers share the same underlying components, such as training data and machine learning models, then they will tend to systematically fail the same individuals or groups. 在思考组件共享时，我们扩展了 Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 对算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）的初始定义：单一化不仅指决策者部署完全相同的系统，也包括他们部署相似系统的情况（此处的相似是指构建方式相似）。 In reasoning about the sharing of components, we broaden the initial definition of algorithmic monoculture from Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021): monoculture is not just when decision-makers deploy the exact same system, but also when they deploy similar systems (here, meaning similar in how they were constructed). 我们研究两类共享组件——训练数据和基础模型（foundation models）——如何促成结果同质化。 We investigate how two types of shared components — training data and foundation models — contribute to homogeneous outcomes.
在 §4 中，我们通过 3 个算法公平性数据集表明，数据共享往往会使个人和种族群体层面的结果同质化。 In §4, we demonstrate that data sharing often homogenizes outcomes for individuals and for racial groups across 3 algorithmic fairness datasets. 在 §5 中，我们讨论基础模型（Bommasani et al., 2021），即能够适配多种下游任务的模型，其兴起如何可能导致前所未有的同质化。22 2 美国证券交易委员会（SEC）现任主席 Gary Gensler 同期也提出了这一担忧：https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/secs-gary-gensler-how-artificial-intelligence-changing-finance。 In §5, we discuss how the rise of foundation models (Bommasani et al., 2021), i.e. models that can be adapted to myriad downstream tasks, could yield unprecedented homogenization.22 2 Gary Gensler, the current chair of the US Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) concurrently has raised this concern: https://mitsloan.mit.edu/ideas-made-to-matter/secs-gary-gensler-how-artificial-intelligence-changing-finance. 基于对视觉基础模型（CLIP）和语言基础模型（RoBERTa）的实验，我们惊讶地发现，使用基础模型并不总会加剧结果同质化。 Based on experiments with foundation models for vision (CLIP) and language (RoBERTa), to our surprise, we find the use of foundation models does not always exacerbate outcome homogenization. 相反，我们发现，将基础模型适配于下游任务的具体机制会显著影响同质化：例如，对于这两种模态，线性探测（linear probing）始终比微调（finetuning）产生更同质化的结果。 Instead, we find the specific mechanism for modalities. 这些实验清楚地表明，我们的假设并不能完全解释共享与同质化之间的关系，但确实存在一些证据表明，共享会使结果同质化。 Through these experiments, it is clear that the relationship between sharing and homogenization is not fully explained by our hypothesis, but that there is some evidence that sharing homogenizes outcomes. 为推进对同质化的研究，我们在结尾从安德森式关系平等主义（Andersonian relational egalitarian lens (§6.1), practical challenges for its diagnosis, measurement, and rectification (§6.2) and future directions for the research community (§7).
2 招聘中的结果同质化2 Outcome Homogenization in Hiring
为说明结果同质化及其潜在成因（包括算法单一化），我们以招聘为引例，具体聚焦于简历筛选阶段。 To illustrate outcome homogenization and its potential causes (including algorithmic monoculture), we consider the motivating example of hiring, specifically the resume screening phase. 公司利用简历筛选求职者，决定邀请哪些候选人参加面试，以及拒绝哪些候选人。 Companies use resumes to screen job applicants, choosing which candidates to interview and which to reject. 当每家公司对每位候选人都作出相同决定时，同质化达到最大程度，使得每位幸运的候选人都获得所有公司的面试机会，而每位不幸的候选人都无法获得任何公司的面试机会。 Maximum homogenization occurs when every company makes the same decision about each candidate, such that each lucky candidate is interviewed by all companies and each unlucky candidate by no companies. 我们将那些未获得任何面试机会的不幸候选人的遭遇称为系统性失效（systemic failure）。33 3 一个根本性的考量是，招聘中何为公正的结果存在争议：与将图像分类为狗或猫等任务相比，才干和真实标签（ground truth）的概念要主观得多（Kasy and Abebe, 2021）。 We say that the unlucky candidates who receive no interviews experience a systemic failure.33 3 A fundamental consideration is that just outcomes in hiring are contested: the notions of merit and ground truth are much more subjective than, say, classifying images as dogs or cats (Kasy and Abebe, 2021). 对于这个说明性例子，我们不深入探讨这一问题，不过我们承认，拒绝某些人获得任何机会可能是合理的（例如，未通过律师资格考试却试图成为律师的人）。 For this illustrative example, we do not delve into this, though we acknowledge that some individuals can be justifiably rejected from all opportunities (e.g. those attempting to become lawyers without passing the bar exam). 因此，对同质化结果的解读需要结合具体情境，而且在招聘、教育、信贷和医疗等资源分配情境中，很可能带有价值判断。 Hence, the interpretation of homogeneous outcomes will need to be contextual, and is likely to be value-laden in allocative contexts such as hiring, education, lending, and health.
哪些因素可能使人类决策的结果同质化？ What factors might homogenize outcomes in human decision-making? 即便没有算法，我们也能在许多情境中观察到同质化的结果。 Even in the absence of algorithms, we observe homogeneous outcomes in many settings. 在招聘中，以往由各公司的招聘经理决定邀请谁参加面试，而他们的决定往往一致。 In hiring, historically, hiring managers at each company decided who to interview and often agreed in their decisions. 这种一致性可以归因于多种因素：首先，如果各公司的需求完全相同，那么不同公司的经理可能会有动机邀请相同的候选人参加面试，从而使结果同质化。 This agreement can be attributed to multiple sources: first, if the needs of each company were identical, then managers at different companies may be incentivized to interview the same candidates, thereby homogenizing outcomes. 其次，如果招聘经理的选择受到相同社会偏见的影响，他们就会错误地拒绝相似的人（例如属于边缘化群体的人），从而促成同质化。 Second, if hiring managers’ choices are influenced by the same social biases, they will mistakenly reject similar people (e.g. those belonging to marginalized groups), thereby contributing to homogenization. 简历筛选中的偏见已有充分记录，而且仍然十分显著（Jowell and Prescott-Clarke, 1970; Bertrand and Mullainathan, 2004; Kline et al., 2021，等）。 Bias in resume screening is well-documented and remains significant (Jowell and Prescott-Clarke, 1970; Bertrand and Mullainathan, 2004; Kline et al., 2021, inter alia).
然而，这两种解释都不意味着系统性失效不可避免。 However, neither explanation implies that systemic failures are inevitable. 各公司的需求不同，而简历并不能完美预测一个人在岗位上能否取得成功，因此，不同公司眼中的“最佳”候选人很可能不同。 Companies have different needs, and resumes are imperfect predictors of success in role, so the “best" candidates will likely differ across companies. 此外，各公司的偏见程度并不相同：Kline et al. (2021) 发现，21% 的公司造成了面试决策中 46% 的种族偏见。 Further, bias is not uniform across companies: Kline et al. (2021) find that 21% of firms were responsible for 46% of the racial bias in interview decisions. 即使决策受到相同的群体层面偏见的影响，不同公司也可能从优势群体和劣势群体中选择不同的个体成员。 Even if decisions are influenced by the same group-level biases, different companies may choose different individual members of the advantaged and disadvantaged groups. 公司需求、偏见的普遍程度以及招聘经理个人偏好的差异，都会使不同的简历更有可能在不同公司通过筛选阶段，从而确保简历筛选结果具有一定的异质性/多样性。 Variance in company needs, in prevalence of bias, and in individual hiring manager preferences all make it more likely that different resumes survive the screening stage at different companies, ensuring some heterogeneity/diversity in resume screening outcomes.
引入算法决策后，这些动态关系会如何变化？ How do these dynamics change with the introduction of algorithmic decision-making? 算法简历筛选已十分普遍：许多大型公司部署简历筛选算法来解析简历，并为哪些申请人进入下一阶段提供决策依据或直接作出决定 (Sonderling et al., 2022)。 Algorithmic resume screening is ubiquitous: many large companies deploy resume screening algorithms to parse resumes and inform/decide which applicants advance (Sonderling et al., 2022). 举一个简化的例子，如果每家公司都部署同一个确定性系统，并采用相同的招聘标准，那么结果必然是同质化的：个人要么获得所有公司的面试机会，要么被所有公司拒绝（即系统性失败）。 As a stylized example, if every company deploys the same deterministic system and has the same hiring criteria, then outcomes will be necessarily homogeneous: individuals will either receive interviews at every company or be rejected by all of them (i.e. systemic failure). 虽然这种情况似乎不太可能发生（即不同公司，尤其是竞争对手，依赖同一个算法系统），但我们观察到，许多公司依赖第三方供应商提供这些算法招聘工具。 While this may seem unlikely (i.e. different companies, especially competitors, relying on the same algorithmic system), we observe that many companies rely on third-party vendors to provide these algorithmic hiring tools. 因此，尽管现状可能比这个简化的例子更复杂（例如，供应商可能针对每个客户定制其使用的算法，每个客户也可能将供应商的建议与其他信息来源相结合来作出决定），但我们确实观察到，少数几家主要供应商主导了算法简历筛选市场（例如，700 家公司依赖 Hirevue，其中包括超过 30% 的《财富》100 强公司 Hirevue (2021)）。 Hence, while the status quo is likely more complex than this stylized example (e.g. vendors could customize the algorithms it uses for each client, each client may integrate a vendor’s recommendation with other information sources to make a decision), we do observe that a few major vendors dominate the marketplace for algorithmic resume screening (e.g. 700 companies, including over 30% of Fortune 100 companies, rely on Hirevue Hirevue (2021)). Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 将不同决策者部署同一系统的这种做法定义为算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）。 This practice of different decision-makers deploying the same system is defined as algorithmic monoculture by Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021).
更一般地，不同公司也可能部署相似但不完全相同的系统。 More generally, different companies may instead deploy similar, but non-identical, systems. 我们扩展了算法单一化的定义，以涵盖这一更广泛的情境，Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 也曾提及这一情境。 We expand the definition of algorithmic monoculture to encapsulate this broader setting, which is also alluded to in Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021). Engler (2021) 将其描述为高校招生管理算法的现实情况，写道：“招生管理算法市场中知名供应商的数量相对较少（介于 5 和 10 之间），……它们的流程和分析方法显著相似。 Engler (2021) describes this as the reality for college enrollment management algorithms, writing "there are a relatively small number (between 5 and 10) of prominent vendors in the enrollment management algorithm market, …their process and analytics are markedly similar. 由于它们的流程看起来相对一致，其结果也可能如此——这可能使符合高校以往预期的学生持续获得良好结果，而不符合这些预期的学生则持续遭遇不良结果”。 Since their processes seem relatively consistent, the outcomes might be as well — potentially leading to consistently good results for students who match the historical expectations of colleges, and consistently poor results for students who don’t".
组件共享假说。 Component-sharing hypothesis. 在本研究中，我们研究在构建方式上存在关联的系统，类似于 Engler (2021) 所描述的情况。 In this work, we study systems that are related in how they are constructed, akin to what is described by Engler (2021). 我们提出组件共享假说，将这种算法单一化与结果同质化联系起来：如果部署的算法系统共享组件，结果同质化就会加剧（即会出现更多系统性失败）。 We pose the component-sharing hypothesis that relates such algorithmic monoculture with outcome homogenization: If deployed algorithmic systems share components, outcome homogenization will increase (i.e. there will be more systemic failures). 在本研究中，我们针对两种主要的组件共享形式对这一假说进行实证检验：(i) 在训练所有部署系统时共享训练数据 (§4)，以及 (ii) 在构建所有部署系统时共享同一个基础模型 (§5)。 In this work, we empirically test this hypothesis for two prominent forms of component sharing: (i) the sharing of training data in training all deployed systems (§4) and (ii) the sharing of the same foundation model for building all deployed systems (§5).
3 结果同质化的形式化3 Formalizing Outcome Homogenization

3.3 理解我们的指标3.3 Understanding Our Metrics
作为概率的比值，我们的指标取值范围为 $[0,\infty)$，其中 $0$ 表示不存在系统性失败，$1$ 表示观测到的比率与预期比率一致，大于 $1$ 的值则表示结果的同质化程度超出了根据基础失败率所能预期的水平。 As a ratio of probabilities, our metrics take values in $[0,\infty)$ where $0$ indicates no systemic failures, $1$ indicates the observed rate matches the expected rate, and values greater than $1$ indicate homogeneous outcomes beyond what can be expected from the underlying failure rates . 在个体层面的设定中，我们假设每个个体与每个决策者恰好交互一次，但这一假设在实践中可能不成立（例如，人们可能多次向某家公司申请，也可能完全不向该公司申请）。 In the individual setting, we assume each individual interacts exactly once with each decision-maker, which may not hold in practice (e.g. people may apply multiple times or not apply at all to a given company). 我们在附录 A 中对个体层面的指标进行了适当推广，以处理这一问题。此外，在群体情境下，如果将每个个体的输入视为属于该个体自己的群体，那么使用均匀加权（或平均加权）即可还原个体层面的指标。 We appropriately generalize our individual-level metric to address this in Appendix A. Further, in the group setting, we recover the individual-level metric using the uniform weighting (or the average weighting) if each individual’s inputs are treated as belonging to their own group.
3.4 与其他指标的关系3.4 Relationship with Other Metrics
由于我们引入了（多个）指标，因此我们考察它们与相关构念（例如准确性、公平性、鲁棒性）的指标之间的关系。 Since we introduce (several) metrics, we consider how they relate to metrics for related constructs (e.g. accuracy, fairness, robustness). 这涉及我们所提出指标的聚合效度与区分效度（Campbell and Fiske, 1959; Messick, 1987; Jacobs and Wallach, 2021），即它们是否与相似构念的指标具有足够高的相关性，并与不同构念的指标具有足够低的相关性。 This speaks to the convergent and divergent validity of our metrics (Campbell and Fiske, 1959; Messick, 1987; Jacobs and Wallach, 2021), i.e. whether they are adequately correlated with metrics of similar constructs and adequately uncorrelated with metrics of dissimilar constructs. 此处，我们讨论理论上的关系，而在 §5.2 中，我们考察实证相关性。 Here, we discuss theoretical relationships, whereas in §5.2 we look at the empirical correlations.
准确性。 Accuracy. 当失败表现为错误时，我们在设计指标时力求尽量降低其与准确性的（正或负）相关性。 When failures are errors, we design our metrics to minimize (anti-)correlation with accuracy. 尽管这一点在理论上没有保证，但我们在表 1 中通过实证加以展示。 While not theoretically guaranteed, we empirically demonstrate this in Table 1. 尽管如此，我们预计仍会存在两者相关的情境：我们的目标不是强行（即机械地）确保两者在技术意义上不存在相关性，而是确保我们不会忽视高准确性系统中的结果同质化（即不会忽视那些即使整体情况看似良好，仍系统性地遭遇失败的个体）。 With that said, we expect there will be settings where the two are correlated: our goal is not to forcibly (i.e. mechanically) ensure no correlation in the technical sense, but to ensure that we do not neglect homogeneous outcomes in highly accurate systems (i.e. neglect the individuals who are systemically failed even when the overall picture may seem favorable).
公平性与鲁棒性。 Fairness and robustness. 除准确性之外，群体情境下的结果同质化还与公平性和鲁棒性密切相关。 Beyond accuracy, outcome homogenization in the group setting is closely related to fairness and robustness. 然而，我们强调，结果同质化从根本上关注的是社会系统中相互关联的结果，而几乎所有鲁棒性或公平性指标都是针对单个模型定义的。 However, we emphasize that outcome homogenization is fundamentally about correlated outcomes for social systems, whereas almost all robustness or fairness metrics are defined for a single model. 此外，几乎所有公平性和鲁棒性的形式化表述都需要群体（即使这些群体不必预先指定），而我们的同质化形式化表述的吸引力在于，它可以针对单个个体作出有意义的定义。 Further, almost all formalisms of fairness and robustness necessitate groups (even if these groups need not be specified in advance), whereas our formalism of homogenization is attractive in that it can be meaningfully defined for singular individuals. 近期研究（Zhao and Chen, 2019; D’Amour et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2021）已开始探讨多任务学习中的公平性，但这些研究关注的是跨任务的有利整体权衡，而非系统性失败模式。 Recent work (Zhao and Chen, 2019; D’Amour et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2021) has initiated the study of fairness in multi-task learning, however these works focus on favorable overall trade-offs across tasks as opposed to systemic modes of failure. 反过来，当只有一个决策者时，由于系统性失败退化为单模型失败，我们的指标便不再具有研究价值（例如，$H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 始终为 $1$）。 Conversely, our metrics cease to be interesting (e.g. $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ is always $1$ ) when there is a single decision-maker as systemic failures degenerate to single-model failures.
在更细粒度的层面上，算法公平性指标（例如 Dwork et al., 2012; Hardt et al., 2016）强调个体或群体之间的差异。 At a more fine-grained level, algorithmic fairness metrics (Dwork et al., 2012; Hardt et al., 2016, e.g. ) emphasize discrepancies between individuals/groups. 相比之下，我们的指标并未（显式地）以这些差异为中心：我们关注的是观测到的系统性失败率（以及它是否超过预期失败率）。 In contrast, our metrics do not (explicitly) center these differences: we are interested in the observed rate of systemic failures (and whether this exceeds the expected rate). 性能差异并不足以导致结果同质化：如果不同决策者的性能差异并不一致，那么结果可能并不同质。 Performance disparities are not sufficient for outcome homogenization: if the performance disparities across decision-makers do not align, then outcomes may not be homogeneous. 就鲁棒性指标而言，我们在最坏情形下的指标 $H_{\textbf{worst}}$ 与最差群体鲁棒性研究中考察的指标非常相似（例如 Sagawa* et al., 2020）。 For robustness metrics, our metric $H_{\textbf{worst}}$ in the worst-case setting closely resembles the metrics studied in work on worst-group robustness (Sagawa* et al., 2020, e.g. ). 具体而言，当只有一个决策者时，我们的指标还原为以总体错误率归一化的标准最差群体错误率。 In particular, when there is only one decision-maker, our metric recovers the standard worst-group error normalized by the overall error.

实验设计。 Experimental design. 为了检验数据共享是否以及如何影响结果同质化，直观地说，我们希望设定共享数据量不同的情境，以检验这会如何影响结果。 To test if and how data sharing influences outcome homogenization, informally, we would like to specify settings with different amounts of shared data to test how this affects outcomes. 然而，一般而言，要准确阐明“共享数据”的含义，并由此令人信服地确保一种情境比另一种情境涉及更多共享，是一项挑战。 However, in general, it is challenging to precisely articulate what it means to "share data" and, therefore, convincingly ensure that one setting involves more sharing than the other. 为应对这一挑战，我们提出了一种严格控制的比较，为训练数据规定了两种采样方案：固定采样（fixed）和不相交采样（disjoint）。 To address this challenge, we present a highly controlled comparison specifying two sampling protocols for the training data: fixed and disjoint. 在固定采样情境中，我们从整个训练数据集中无放回地抽取 $n$ 个数据点，并用它们训练全部 $k$ 个任务专用模型（对于 GC，$k=2$；对于 ACS PUMS，$k=3$）。 In the fixed setting, we sample $n$ points without replacement from the entire training dataset, which we use to train all of the $k$ task-specific models ( $k=2$ for GC and $k=3$ for ACS PUMS). 在不相交采样情境中，我们无放回地抽取 $kn$ 个数据点，并将它们随机划分给 $k$ 个任务专用模型。 In the disjoint setting, we sample $kn$ points without replacement that we randomly partition across the $k$ task-specific models. 换言之，在固定采样情境中，各任务专用模型共享完全相同的训练数据输入；而在不相交采样情境中，各任务专用模型共享相同的训练分布，但不共享具体的数据。 In other words, in the fixed setting, the task-specific models share the exact same training data inputs, whereas in the disjoint setting, the task-specific models share the same training distribution, but not the exact data. 我们强调，这两种情境之间的差异很微妙，但它意味着固定采样情境中的共享程度高于不相交采样情境。 We emphasize that this is a subtle difference between the settings, but it implies fixed shares more than disjoint.
确定训练数据后，我们采用逻辑回归、SVMs、梯度提升树和小型神经网络这几类模型，为 $k$ 个任务中的每个任务训练模型（更多细节见附录 C）。 Having specified the training data, we train models for each of the $k$ tasks across the model families of logistic regression, SVMs, gradient-boosted trees, and small neural networks (further details in Appendix C). 为考虑随机性的影响，我们报告了 25 次实验试验的平均结果（即，对于所考虑的每个 $n$ 值，抽取 5 份训练数据样本，并对每份样本进行 5 次训练）。 To account for randomness, we report results averaged over 25 trials of the experiment (i.e. 5 samples of training data and 5 training runs per sample for every value of $n$ we consider).

结果与分析。 Results and analysis. 我们在图 1 和图 2 中报告结果，以展示特定现象，其余结果见附录 C。在图 1（左）中，我们看到了支持我们假设的明确证据：按设计涉及更多共享的固定采样情境，始终比不相交采样情境表现出更高的同质性。 We report results in Figure 1 and Figure 2 to demonstrate specific phenomena, deferring the remainder of the results to Appendix C. In Figure 1 (left), we see clear evidence for our hypothesis: the fixed setting, which by construction involves more sharing, reliably shows more homogeneity than the disjoint setting. 总体而言，在我们考虑的全部 3 个数据集和 4 类模型中，我们发现固定采样情境通常比不相交采样情境产生更加同质化的结果，这为我们的假设提供了证据：与使用不同但同分布的训练数据相比，使用相同的训练数据会导致更高程度的结果同质化。 Overall, across all 3 datasets and 4 model families we consider, we find that the fixed setting generally leads to more homogeneous outcomes than disjoint, which provides evidence towards our hypothesis: the use of the same training data leads to greater outcome homogenization than the use of different (but identically distributed) training data.
然而，这一关系并非总是成立：我们确实观察到若干情形，其中不相交采样情境的同质性程度与固定采样情境相近，有时甚至更高（例如，图 2 左侧子图中的某些区域）。 However, this relationship is not perfect: we do see several instances where the degree of homogeneity is similar or even sometimes greater for the disjoint setting (e.g. regions of the left subplot of Figure 2). 因此，仅凭数据共享无法完整刻画同质性（例如，训练中的随机性和观测到的系统性失败数量的不稳定性，都是需要考虑的重要因素）。 Therefore, data sharing alone does not fully characterize homogeneity (e.g. randomness in training and instability in the number of observed systemic failures are important to consider). 从概念上讲，我们强调，这一点对于理解系统性失败的性质至关重要：所有模型均在其上失败的数据点对变化非常敏感，因此，即使在我们这里所考虑的这类共享程度受到严格控制的非常纯粹的情境中，趋势也可能相当不稳定。 Conceptually, we highlight that this is critical to the character of systemic failures: data points that are failed by all models are very sensitive and, as a result, the trends can be quite fickle even in very pure settings with very controlled sharing like the ones we consider here.
此外，固定采样和不相交采样的错误率变化趋势几乎完全相同（图 1 右侧子图），考虑到两种采样方案之间的关系，这符合我们的预期。 Further, the trends in the error rates for fixed and disjoint are near-identical (right subplot of Figure 1), as we would expect given the relationship between the sampling protocols. 也就是说，这明确证明了一个重要的定性结论：仅看准确率，无法区分这两种情境，但它们在观测到的系统性失败数量方面存在显著差异。 That is, this is clear evidence of an important qualitative consequence: by looking at accuracy alone, these settings are indistinguishable, whereas they differ considerably in terms of the number of observed systemic failures. 尽管底层错误率相同，我们的度量仍能正确识别这些情境之间的差异。 Our measures correctly identify discrepancies in these settings, even though the underlying error rates are the same. 最后，就随数据集大小的变化而言，我们没有观察到数据集规模与结果同质化之间关系的一致趋势，不过，正如预期的那样，我们确实看到不相交采样和固定采样情境在数据集较大时趋于一致（因为依测度收敛使得采样差异变得可以忽略）。 Finally, as a function of dataset size, we do not see consistent trends on the relationship between dataset scale and outcome homogenization, though we unsurprisingly see the disjoint and fixed settings converge for larger dataset sizes (since the discrepancies in the sampling become negligible due to convergence in measure).
针对同一个人，而不仅仅是同一个群体。 Picking on the same person, not just the same group. 对于图 2 中的 ACS PUMS，我们对比了个体层面和群体层面的同质化。 For ACS PUMS in Figure 2, we contrast the individual-level and group-level homogenization. （回顾一下，平均度量和均匀度量是我们个体层面度量在群体层面的对应形式。） (Recall the average and uniform metrics are the group-level analogues of our individual-level metric.) 个体层面的结果始终比种族群体层面的结果更加同质。 Outcomes are consistently more homogeneous at the individual-level than for racial groups. 事实上，群体层面的分析显示，同质化程度很低（数值接近 $1$），且随数据集规模的变化很小；而个体层面的度量则揭示了更高的同质性和更大的变化（这并不令人意外，因为群体层面的量经过了更大程度的聚合）。 In fact, group-level analysis shows little homogenization (values near $1$ ) and little change as a function of dataset size, whereas individual-level measurement exposes greater homogeneity and more variation (which is unsurprising since group-level quantities are more extensively aggregated). 这对许多仅考虑社会群体（例如种族）的算法公平性研究具有重大影响：这些研究可能遗漏针对特定个体的系统性失效，因为这些失效在群体层面被掩盖了（Kearns et al., 2018; Hashimoto et al., 2018, cf.）。 This has significant ramifications for many works on algorithmic fairness, which only consider social groups (e.g. race): these works may miss systemic failures for particular individuals that are obscured at the group-level (Kearns et al., 2018; Hashimoto et al., 2018, cf.). 即使采用交叉性方法（Crenshaw, 1989），也可能不足以揭示这些系统性失效，除非每个交叉性“群体”都缩减为单个个体。 Even intersectional approaches (Crenshaw, 1989) may not suffice to surface these systemic failures, unless each intersectional "group" reduces to a single individual. 因此，为了全面、整体地描述算法危害，我们建议将以个体为中心的视角作为广泛采用的以群体为中心的分析的必要补充。 As a results, to build comprehensive and holistic accounts of algorithmic harm, we recommend an individual-centric lens as essential complements to widespread group-centric analyses.
5 模型共享实验5 Model-sharing Experiments
在发现数据共享似乎会加剧结果同质化之后，我们现在转向模型共享。 Having found that data sharing appears to exacerbate outcome homogenization, we now turn to model sharing. 具体而言，我们检验共享基础模型（foundation models，FMs）如何影响结果同质化。 Specifically, we test how sharing foundation models (FMs) affects outcome homogenization. Bommasani et al. (2021) 将基础模型定义为“在广泛的数据上训练（通常采用大规模自监督学习）、能够适配多种下游任务的模型”。 Bommasani et al. (2021) define foundation models as "models trained on broad data (generally using self-supervision at scale) that can be adapted to a wide range of downstream tasks". FMs 带来了广泛的范式转变，改变了许多子领域（尤其是 NLP）的 AI 研究，并加快了从 AI 研究到 AI 部署的产品化进程。 FMs have ushered in a sweeping paradigm shift, transforming AI research in many subareas (most notably NLP) and accelerating the productionization process from AI research to AI deployment. 从其具体社会影响来看，无论是在初创企业（例如 Hugging Face、Cohere、AI21 Labs）还是成熟科技公司（例如 Google、Microsoft、OpenAI），基础模型在 ML 部署中的核心地位都日益凸显。88 8 据 https://www.scalevp.com/blog/introducing-the-scale-generative-ai-index 记录，不断壮大的、以 FMs 为中心的初创企业生态系统已筹集了数十亿资金。 Focusing on their concrete societal impact, foundation models are increasingly central to deploying ML at both startups (e.g. Hugging Face, Cohere, AI21 Labs) and established technology companies (e.g. Google, Microsoft, OpenAI).88 8 The growing startup ecosystem centered on FMs has raised billions in capital as documented by https://www.scalevp.com/blog/introducing-the-scale-generative-ai-index.
共享是 FM 范式的固有特征：为了使其庞大的资源需求具有合理性，模型必须被反复使用，才能有效摊薄成本。 Sharing is endemic to the FM paradigm: to justify their immense resource requirements, models must be used repeatedly for costs to amortize favorably. 在极端情况下，如果像 NLP 这样的整个领域几乎都基于一个或少数几个 FMs 构建下游系统，那么这些模型中广泛体现于下游的任何偏差或特异性，都可能导致前所未有的系统性失效和结果同质化（Bommasani et al., 2021; Fishman and Hancox-Li, 2022）。 In the extreme, if an entire domain like NLP comes to build almost all downstream systems on one or a few FMs, then any biases or idiosyncrasies of these models that pervasively manifest downstream could potentially yield unprecedented systemic failures and outcome homogenization (Bommasani et al., 2021; Fishman and Hancox-Li, 2022). 我们已经看到了这种算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）的初步证据：仅在过去一个月，BERT 就被下载了 24 million 次99 9 https://huggingface.co/bert-base-uncased，截至 November 2022。而 GPT-3 则支撑着数百个已部署的应用。1010 10 https://openai.com/blog/gpt-3-apps/ 因此，我们认为，在这些模型逐渐成为根深蒂固的基础设施之际，理解结果是否会同质化以及同质化的程度，是一个尤为及时的议题。 We see initial evidence for such algorithmic monoculture: BERT was downloaded 24 million times in the past month99 9 https://huggingface.co/bert-base-uncased as of November 2022. alone and GPT-3 enables hundreds of deployed applications.1010 10 https://openai.com/blog/gpt-3-apps/ Consequently, we believe it is especially timely to understand if, and to what extent, outcomes get homogenized as these models become entrenched as infrastructure.
5.1 实验5.1 Experiments
数据。 Data. 为了检验基础模型如何影响同质化，我们分别针对视觉数据和语言数据开展实验。1111 11 视觉实验的完整可复现性细节见 §B.2；语言实验的相关细节见 §B.3。 To test how foundation models influence homogenization, we conduct experiments for both vision and language data.1111 11 Full reproducibility details for vision are in §B.2; for language are in §B.3. 在视觉方面，我们使用 CelebA 数据集（Liu et al., 2015），其中包含名人人脸图像及其面部属性标注。 On the vision side, we work with the CelebA dataset (Liu et al., 2015) of celebrity faces paired with annotations for facial attributes. 对于每张人脸图像，我们根据其关联属性定义两个任务（Earrings、Necklace），用于预测图中人物是否佩戴特定饰品。 For each face image, given the associated attributes, we define two tasks (Earrings, Necklace) that involve predicting whether the individual is wearing the specific apparel item. 此前关于公平性和鲁棒性的研究已探讨过 CelebA 中的属性预测问题（Sagawa* et al., 2020; Khani and Liang, 2021; Wang et al., 2021）。 Attribute prediction in CelebA has been studied previously in work on fairness and robustness (Sagawa* et al., 2020; Khani and Liang, 2021; Wang et al., 2021). 在语言方面，我们遵循 Gururangan et al. (2019)，使用四个标准英语文本分类数据集：IMDB（Maas et al., 2011）、AGNews（Zhang et al., 2015）、Yahoo（Chang et al., 2008）和 HateSpeech18（de Gibert et al., 2018）。 On the language side, we use four standard English text classification datasets following Gururangan et al. (2019): IMDB (Maas et al., 2011), AGNews (Zhang et al., 2015), Yahoo (Chang et al., 2008), and HateSpeech18 (de Gibert et al., 2018).
个体与群体。 Individuals and groups. 由于视觉任务均基于 CelebA，我们拥有个体层面的信息。 Since the vision tasks are all based on CelebA, we have individual-level information. 然而，由于语言任务涉及完全不同的数据（例如电影评论与新闻文章），因此不存在（共享的）个体层面信息。 However, since the language tasks involve entirely different data (e.g. movie reviews vs. news articles), there is no (shared) individual-level information. 在群体层面，对于视觉任务，我们使用发色以及个体是否留有胡须的标注；对于语言任务，我们则按二元性别对输入进行自动分组。 At the group-level, for vision we use annotations for hair color and for whether the individual has a beard, whereas for language we automatically group inputs by binary gender. 我们有意不使用 CelebA 中的性别或种族信息，因为我们认为，从人脸图像推断此类信息存在诸多问题，而且已受到广泛批评。 We deliberately do not use gender or racial information in CelebA because we believe inference of such information from face images is fraught, and has been the subject of extensive critique.
实验设计。 Experimental design. 为了检验模型共享是否以及如何影响结果同质化，与数据共享的情况类似，我们必须精确定义共享模型的含义。 To test if and how model sharing influences outcome homogenization, akin to data sharing, we must precisely specify what it means to share models. 在视觉实验中，我们通过以下方式之一为每项任务生成任务特定模型：(i) 在 CelebA 数据上从头训练，(ii) 在 CLIP 基础模型（Radford et al., 2021）提取的特征上拟合线性分类器，进行线性探测（linear probing），或 (iii) 对 CLIP 进行微调。 In the vision experiments, we produce task-specific models for each task by either (i) training from scratch on CelebA data, (ii) linearly probing by fitting a linear classifier on features from the CLIP foundation model (Radford et al., 2021), or (iii) finetuning CLIP. 为确保比较具有意义，从头训练的模型采用与 CLIP 相同的 ViT 架构（Dosovitskiy et al., 2021），但权重随机初始化。 To ensure meaningful comparisons, the models trained from scratch shared the same ViT architecture (Dosovitskiy et al., 2021) used in CLIP but with weights initialized randomly.
在语言实验中，我们进一步聚焦于将基础模型（具体为 RoBERTa-base（Liu et al., 2019））适配到各项任务时所采用的具体适配方法。 In the language experiments, we further hone in on the specific adaptation method used to adapt the foundation model (specifically RoBERTa-base (Liu et al., 2019)) to each task. 我们考虑 (i) 线性探测、(ii) 微调和 (iii) BitFit（Ben Zaken et al., 2022）；BitFit 是近期提出的一种轻量级微调方法，它冻结基础模型（FM）中除偏置参数之外的所有权重，并像微调一样更新偏置参数。 We consider (i) linear probing, (ii) finetuning, and (iii) BitFit (Ben Zaken et al., 2022), which is a recent lightweight finetuning method that involves freezing all the FM weights except the bias parameters which are updated as in finetuning. 因此，BitFit 是介于探测与微调之间的一种方法，已有研究表明，它仅更新极少量的 FM 参数，就能达到与微调相近的准确率（Ben Zaken et al., 2022）。 Consequently, BitFit is an intermediary between probing and finetuning, which has been shown to achieve similar accuracy as finetuning while updating very few FM parameters (Ben Zaken et al., 2022). 对于视觉和语言实验，所有模型均训练相同的轮数，并且对每种训练方法，我们使用 5 个随机种子重复每项实验。 For both vision and for language, all models are trained for the same number of epochs and we repeat each experiment for 5 random seeds per training approach.
假设。 Hypotheses. 与数据共享非常相似，模型共享存在程度之分，并非二元状态：不同的下游系统可以在不同程度上共享底层模型。 Much like data sharing, model sharing is graded and is not binary: different downstream systems can share varying degrees of underlying models. 我们的实验设计体现了共享程度的连续变化：首先，下游系统可以共享一个基础模型，也可以不共享（从头训练）。 By design, our experimental design suggests a continuum in sharing: first, downstream system either can share a foundation model or not (scratch). 其次，在使用基础模型的方法中，所有方法均使用 FM 权重来初始化权重，但在适配完成后哪些参数保持不变这一点上有所不同：微调会改变所有参数，BitFit 只改变偏置参数，而探测不改变任何参数。 Second, among methods that involve foundation models, all methods initialize the weights using the FM weights but differ in which parameters remain the same after adaptation is completed: finetuning changes all the parameters, BitFit only changes the bias parameters, and probing changes none of the parameters. 因此，总体而言，我们可以按共享程度从高到低将这些方法排序为 (i) 探测、(ii) BitFit、(iii) 微调、(iv) 从头训练；根据我们的组件共享假设，我们据此预测，同质化程度也将遵循这一排序。 As a result, overall, we can rank methods from most to least sharing as (i) probing, (ii) BitFit, (iii) finetuning, (iv) scratch, which leads us to predict the amount of homogenization will also follow this ranking under our component-sharing hypothesis.

5.3 讨论5.3 Discussion
通过各项实验，我们提供了大量证据，表明共享会导致结果同质化，但共享并不能完整解释同质性。 Across our experiments, we provide considerable evidence that sharing leads to homogeneous outcomes, but that it is incomplete explanation of homogeneity. 在对比 §4 和 §5 的发现时，这一点尤为重要，因为在基础模型范式下，模型共享间接意味着通过训练数据进行大规模的数据共享（以 FM 权重初始化为中介）。 This is particularly relevant when the findings in §4 and §5 are contrasted, given model sharing in the foundation model regime indirectly implies immense data sharing via the training data (as mediated by the FM weight initialization). 我们强调，这些发现所处的实验条件差异很大：数据共享实验采用低维表格数据和简单模型族，而模型共享实验采用高维图像/文本和大型神经网络，因此，研究发现之间的差异可能归因于这些不同。 We emphasize that the regimes for these findings are quite different: low-dimensional tabular data with simple model families in our data-sharing experiments vs. high-dimensional images/text with large neural networks in our model-sharing experiments, so discrepancies in the findings may be attributable to these differences. 更广泛地说，我们认为，更完整的解释需要考虑其中涉及的数据分布及相关的分布偏移（例如训练与适配之间的分布偏移）。 More broadly, we believe a more complete explanation requires accounting for the data distributions and the associated distribution shifts (e.g. between training and adaptation) at play. 然而，我们认为明确的是，我们的研究发现为进一步发展我们的概念性论证提供了实证基础，即机器学习中的共享可能加剧同质化。 What we believe is clear, however, is that our findings provide an empirical basis to build on our conceptual arguments that sharing in machine learning can increase homogenization. 这促使我们研究机器学习的实际部署：例如，共享/单一化是否会导致算法招聘中的同质化？ This motivates investigation into real deployments of machine learning: for example, does sharing/monoculture lead to homogenization in algorithmic hiring?
6 社会层面的考量6 Societal Considerations
为了将我们的工作置于更广泛的社会背景中，我们阐述了我们如何思考结果同质化的危害，并指出在实际部署的系统中诊断、测量和纠正结果同质化所面临的核心挑战。 To situate our work in a broader social context, we articulate how we reason about the harms of outcome homogenization as well as identify core challenges to diagnosing, measuring, and rectifying outcome homogenization in real deployed systems.
6.1 为什么同质化的结果有害？6.1 Why are Homogeneous Outcomes Harmful?
到目前为止，我们一直主张，同质化的结果是一类系统性危害；尤其是，鉴于共享和单一化实践的普遍存在，我们应当更加关注这类危害。 To this point, we have argued homogeneous outcomes are a class of systemic harms and, in particular, a class of harms we should be paying greater attention to given pervasive practices of sharing and monoculture. 在某些情境中，同质化结果的危害似乎显而易见：当分类器至少有可能正确分类某个个体时，我们当然不希望该个体在与之交互的每一个分类器上都遭遇分类失败。 In some settings, it may feel clear that homogeneous outcomes are harmful: we surely do not want individuals to be failed by every classifier they interact with and it is at least possible for the classifiers to correctly classify the individual. 在其他情境中，例如我们用来引出研究问题的招聘示例，这一点可能就不那么明确。 In others, such as our motivating example of hiring, it may feel less clear. 如果一个人被所有雇主排除在就业机会之外，尤其是这种排斥可以追溯到各雇主部署的某个共享算法，这难道不是一种危害吗？ Surely there is harm if someone is locked out from employment across all employers, especially if this lockout can be traced to a shared algorithm being deployed by each employer? 另一方面，如果求职者总数超过空缺职位的数量，那么（在没有更广泛的结构性变革的情况下）必然会有一些人被所有雇主拒绝；而且，即使仍有职位空缺，也可能存在有正当理由拒绝某人担任所有职位的情形。 On the other hand, some people must be rejected from every employer (in the absence of broader structural change) if the total number of candidates exceeds the number of open positions, and there may be justifiable circumstances to reject someone from all positions even when there are unfilled vacancies.
总体而言，判断同质化结果何时构成社会伤害，需要结合具体情境：特定情境决定了应如何理解同质化是否值得在道德上关注，以及值得关注的程度。 Overall, understanding when homogeneous outcomes constitute a social harm is contextual: the particular circumstances determine how to interpret whether, and to what extent, homogenization is of moral concern. 为了提出一种思考这些伤害的方式，我们沿用 Elizabeth Anderson 的关系平等主义进路（Anderson, 1999; Anderson, 2016），评估同质化带来的社会伤害。 To provide one account of how to reason about these harms, we follow Elizabeth Anderson in taking a relational egalitarian approach (Anderson, 1999; Anderson, 2016) to evaluate the social harm of homogenization. 关系平等主义（relational egalitarianism）作为一种正义理论，主张在正义的社会中，个体必须以平等者的身份彼此交往。 Relational egalitarianism, as a theory of justice, argues that individuals must relate to each other as equals in a just society. 近年来，关系进路已被用于 AI 相关研究：Birhane (2021) 提出了关于算法不正义的关系性解释，数据治理、决策和公平性研究也采用了类似进路（Viljoen, 2021; Kasy and Abebe, 2021; Fish and Stark, 2022）。 Relational approaches have seen recent adoption in relation to AI: Birhane (2021) presents a relational account of algorithmic injustice with similar approaches taken to data governance, decision-making, and fairness (Viljoen, 2021; Kasy and Abebe, 2021; Fish and Stark, 2022).
为了使关系分析有具体依托，我们回到招聘情境。 To ground a relational analysis, we return to the context of hiring. 尽管单个组织可能会对候选人进行排序，而且我们确实可以预期，同一市场部门内的公司往往会对某种等级排序达成一致，但同一套等级排序不应持续主导整个部门或地区，以致某些人被完全排除在就业之外（Anderson, 1999, 74）。 While individual organizations may establish rankings of candidates, and indeed we would expect that companies within a market sector will often agree on a hierarchy, the same hierarchy should not consistently dominate an entire sector or territory such that some people are entirely excluded from work (Anderson, 1999, 74). Anderson 主张，“要能够作为平等参与者在合作生产体系中发挥作用，就需要……获得发展自身才能所需的教育、职业选择自由、订立合同并与他人达成合作协议的权利、因自身劳动而获得公平报酬的权利，以及他人对自身生产贡献的认可”（Anderson, 2016, 318）。 Anderson argues that “to be capable of functioning as an equal participant in a system of cooperative production requires …access to the education needed to develop one’s talents, freedom of occupational choice, the right to make contracts and enter into cooperative agreements with others, the right to receive fair value for one’s labor, and recognition by others of one’s productive contributions” (Anderson, 2016, 318). 如果某些人持续被排除在求职面试之外，进而无法就业，他们就无法享有职业选择自由；如果他们被排除在高等教育之外，就难以发展自身才能。 If some people are consistently excluded from job interviews and, therefore, employment, they will not enjoy freedom of occupational choice; if they are excluded from higher education, they will struggle to develop their talents. 被排斥者不仅因等级体系的建立而亲身遭受伤害，也无法作为平等参与者在社会中发挥作用。 Not only do those excluded personally suffer from the establishment of the hierarchy, they also are unable to function as equal participants in society. 由于就业、教育和信贷是基础性的社会善，持续将某些人排除在这些社会善之外，可能会形成基于社会尊重或支配关系的等级体系。 Because employment, education, and credit are foundational social goods, consistent exclusion from them risks establishing a social hierarchy of esteem or domination. 这种社会尊重的等级体系又会损害被排斥者以平等民主公民的身份与他人交往的能力（Anderson, 1999）。 The hierarchy of esteem in turn damages the ability of the excluded to relate to others as equal democratic citizens (Anderson, 1999).
根据这一解释，我们强调，这种伤害在道德上的重要性取决于排斥的范围。 Under this account, we emphasize how the moral importance of the harm depends on the scale of exclusion. 也就是说，在其最纯粹的形式下，存在强烈的阈值效应：当个体被所有就业（或教育、信贷等）来源拒之门外时，同质化结果最为严重。 That is, in its purest form, there is a strong threshold effect: homogeneous outcomes are most severe when the individual is denied from all sources of employment (or education or credit or so on). 如果自主性意味着能够获得足够广泛且足够多样的机会，那么被拒绝某一个机会，例如一份工作或一笔贷款，并不构成伤害（Raz, 1988）。 If autonomy is access to a sufficient range of sufficiently varied opportunities, then it is not a harm to be denied one opportunity, such as a job or a loan (Raz, 1988). 然而，被排除在所有机会之外则构成伤害。 It is a harm, however, to be shut out of all opportunities. 因此，在本研究中，我们考察个体被排除在所有机会之外的极端情形。1313 13 我们确实注意到，尽管被排除在所有机会之外可能是构成伤害的充分条件，但在某些情境下，被剥夺相当大比例的机会就可能足以构成伤害。 For this reason, in this work, we study the extreme case in which individuals are shut out of all opportunities.1313 13 We do note that while all might be a sufficient condition for harm, it may suffice in some contexts to be denied access to a significant fraction of opportunities. 因此，尽管我们研究的是最强形式的排斥，我们仍鼓励未来的研究针对更一般的情境，即个体被剥夺大多数机会而非仅限于所有机会的情境，对同质化结果开展技术、实验和道德分析。 For this reason, while we study the strongest form of exclusion, we encourage future work to put forth technical, experimental, and moral analyses of homogeneous outcomes in the more general setting where individuals are denied most opportunities, not just all opportunities.
6.2 结果同质化的实践挑战6.2 Practical Challenges for Outcome Homogenization
诊断。 Diagnosis. 在本研究中，我们假定单一化（monoculture）会导致同质化：采用这一进路，需要知道哪些部署依赖同一家供应商、同一数据集或同一基础模型（即知道何处存在单一化）。 In our work, we posit monoculture yields homogenization: to follow this approach would require knowing which deployments rely on the same vendor, dataset, or foundation model (i.e. knowing where there is monoculture). 遗憾的是，算法系统的构建方式往往极不透明，以至于几乎不可能识别其共享组件。 Unfortunately, how algorithmic systems are constructed is often so opaque that identifying shared components is nigh impossible. 然而，如果能够证实存在高度同质化，那么这一测量结果本身就可以为提高透明度的相关规定提供依据，以识别潜在的单一化（即沿逆因果方向进行推断）。 However, if high homogenization were demonstrated, the measurement itself could justify provisions for increased transparency to identify the latent monoculture (i.e. the anti-causal direction). 这提供了一种可行机制，使审计人员能够获准有条件地访问原本无法访问的专有系统。 This provides a plausible mechanism for empowering auditors to be granted conditional access to otherwise inaccessible proprietary systems.
测量。 Measurement. 测量同质化只需要黑箱访问权限，而这在实践中通常可以实现（参见 Buolamwini and Gebru, 2018; Raji and Buolamwini, 2019; Metaxa et al., 2021）。 Measuring homogenization only requires black box access, which is often achievable in practice (Buolamwini and Gebru, 2018; Raji and Buolamwini, 2019; Metaxa et al., 2021, see). 然而，识别个体层面的影响，需要将同一个体在不同部署中的结果关联起来。 However, identifying individual-level effects requires linking individual outcomes across deployments. 由于隐私方面的限制，在不同部署之间关联同一个体可能很困难，甚至无法实现，因此，群体层面的同质化成为一种更普遍可行的研究对象（见 §3.2）。 Due to privacy constraints, linking individuals across different deployments may be challenging or impossible, which motivates group-level homogenization as more generally accessible (see §3.2).
纠正。 Rectification. 即便已经识别出结果同质化，组织也可能缺乏减少同质化的动力。 Even once outcome homogenization is identified, organizations may not be incentivized to reduce it. 事实上，同质化既不能归因于任何单一实体，也并非总能通过单个组织的单方面行动加以解决。 In fact, homogenization neither is attributable to any single entity nor can it always be addressed by unilateral action from a single organization. 面对激励不一致和集体行动问题，可能需要监管、政策或其他合规机制。 In the face of misaligned incentives and collective action problems, regulation, policy, or other compliance mechanisms may be required. 如果同质化结果的危害需要一段时间才能显现或累积，而准确率最大化等做法的收益却能立即获得，那么组织激励与同质化之间潜在的权衡就会变得更加复杂。 Potential trade-offs between organization incentives and homogenization are further complicated if the harms of homogeneous outcomes take time to observe/accrue, but the benefits of, say, maximizing accuracy are immediate. 更乐观的是，Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) 表明，在特定条件下，准确率最大化策略与为社会福祉而促进结果多样化之间不存在权衡。 More optimistically, Kleinberg and Raghavan (2021) show (under specific conditions) no trade-off exists between accuracy-maximizing policies and diversifying outcomes for societal benefit.
7 局限性与结论7 Limitations and Conclusion
我们提出了结果同质化的概念，对其进行了形式化描述和度量，并将其视为机器学习（ML）中的共享实践可能引发的一种系统性危害。 We have introduced, formalized, and measured outcome homogenization as a systemic harm that may arise from practices of sharing in ML. 结果同质化是一个新颖、研究不足且在概念上引人深思的主题：尽管开展了本研究，人们对其定义、解释、统计估计、缓解方法以及与单一化（monoculture）的联系仍缺乏充分理解。 Outcome homogenization is a new, understudied, and conceptually compelling topic: its definition, interpretation, statistical estimation, mitigation, and connections to monoculture remain poorly understood in spite of this work. 我们鼓励未来的研究在所有这些方向上继续推进。 We encourage future work to push in all of these directions. 就我们提出的度量而言，直接对其进行优化未必会带来理想的结果，甚至可能助长伦理洗白（ethics-washing）：必须结合具体情境来解释这一度量，因为同质化结果的影响在很大程度上取决于更广泛的社会背景。 As to our measure, direct optimization may not lead to desirable outcomes, potentially even contributing to ethics-washing: its interpretation must be contextual since the implications of homogeneous outcomes heavily depend on broader societal context.
我们认为，同质化对于全面刻画算法危害至关重要，尤其是考虑到单一化现象日益加剧（例如，通过基础模型的使用）。 We believe homogenization is essential to holistically characterizing algorithmic harm, especially given growing monoculture (e.g. via foundation models). 如果缺乏审视，其危害可能会在不知不觉中变得根深蒂固。 Without scrutiny, its harms may insidiously entrench. 因此，我们认为有必要及早干预，以防止此类危害在社会中发生。 Consequently, we believe early intervention is necessary to prevent such harms in society.

A.2 个体同质化与群体同质化的关系A.2 Relating Individual and Group Homogenization
在 §4 中，我们通过实证表明，个体层面的结果可能比种族群体层面的结果更加同质化。 In §4, we demonstrate empirically that outcomes can be more homogeneous for individuals than for racial groups. 人们可能因此认为，采用更细粒度的分组时，同质化程度必然更高，因为个体（视为单元素群体时）是群体的子集（即按个体分组时，不同种族的数据点不会出现在同一群体中，因为每个群体都只有一个成员）。 One may be led to believe that homogenization is necessarily greater when considering finer-grained groupings since individuals (when viewed as singleton groups) are subsets of groups (i.e. when grouping by individuals, no data points from different races appear in the same group as each group is a singleton). 然而，事实并非如此。 However, this is not true.
为阐明这一点，我们给出两个简化情景，分别展示个体层面的结果同质化高于和低于群体层面的结果同质化的情况。 To elucidate this, we provide two toy scenarios that demonstrate circumstances where individual-level outcome homogenization is greater than, and is less than, group-level outcome homogenization. （当然，二者也可能相等。） (Of course, they can also be equal.) 在这两种设定中，都有两个模型（即两个决策者）和两个群体，每个群体由两个个体组成。 In both settings, we will have two models (i.e. two decision-makers) and two groups, where each group is comprised of two individuals. 因此，$H_{\textbf{avg}}$ = $H_{\textbf{unif}}$，所以我们可以将其中任意一个与 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 进行比较。 As a result, $H_{\textbf{avg}}$ = $H_{\textbf{unif}}$ , so we can compare either to $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ .
在这两种设定中，我们都假定 Alice 和 Angelique 是群体 1 的成员，而 Bob 和 Bernardo 是群体 2 的成员。 In both settings, we will say Alice and Angelique are members of Group 1 and Bob and Bernardo are members of Group 2.
情景 1。 Scenario 1. 假设 Alice 和 Bob 被 $h^{1}$ 错误分类，但被 $h^{2}$ 正确分类。 Let Alice and Bob be misclassified by $h^{1}$ but correctly classified by $h^{2}$ . 假设 Angelique 和 Bernardo 被 $h^{2}$ 错误分类，但被 $h^{1}$ 正确分类。 Let Angelique and Bernardo be misclassified by $h^{2}$ but correctly classified by $h^{1}$ . 没有人同时被两个模型错误分类，因此在个体层面观测到的系统性失败次数为 0，故 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ = 0。 No one is misclassified by both models, hence the number of observed systemic failures is 0 at the individual level, hence $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ = 0.
然而，由于两个模型在群体 1 内均出现了失败（群体 2 也同样如此），群体层面的系统性失败次数非零，因此 $\text{$H_{\textbf{avg}}$}~=~\text{$H_{\textbf{unif}}$}~>0$。 However, since there is a failure within Group 1 for both models (and for Group 2 as well), the number of systemic failures is nonzero at the group level, hence $\text{$H_{\textbf{avg}}$}~=~\text{$H_{\textbf{unif}}$}~>0$ . 因此，在这一情景中，我们看到个体层面的结果同质化可能低于群体层面的结果同质化。 Thus, in this scenario, we have seen that individual-level outcome homogenization can be less than group-level outcome homogenization.
情景 2。 Scenario 2. 假设 Alice 和 Bob 均被 $h^{1}$ 和 $h^{2}$ 错误分类。 Let Alice and Bob be misclassified by both $h^{1}$ and $h^{2}$ . 假设 Angelique 和 Bernardo 均被 $h^{1}$ 和 $h^{2}$ 正确分类。 Let Angelique and Bernardo be correctly classified by both $h^{1}$ and $h^{2}$ . 在个体层面，观察到 2 次系统性失败，因此系统性失败的观测发生率为 $\frac{2}{4}=0.5$。 At the individual level, there are 2 observed systemic failures, so the observed rate of systemic failure is $\frac{2}{4}=0.5$ . 每个应用的总体错误率均为 0.5，因此系统性失败的预期发生率为 $0.5\times 0.5=0.25$。 The overall error rate for each application is 0.5, so the expected rate of systemic failure is $0.5\times 0.5=0.25$ . 因此，$H^{\textbf{individual}}$ = $\frac{0.5}{0.25}=2$。 Therefore, $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ = $\frac{0.5}{0.25}=2$ .
在群体层面，两个群体的系统性失败发生率均为 $0.25$。 At the group level, the rate of systemic failures is $0.25$ for both groups. 每个应用的总体错误率仍为 0.5，因此分母也为 $0.5\times 0.5=0.25$。 The overall error rate for each application is still 0.5, so the denominator is also $0.5\times 0.5=0.25$ . 因此，$\text{$H_{\textbf{avg}}$}=\text{$H_{\textbf{unif}}$}=\text{$H_{\textbf{worst}}$}=1$。 Therefore, $\text{$H_{\textbf{avg}}$}=\text{$H_{\textbf{unif}}$}=\text{$H_{\textbf{worst}}$}=1$ . 由此可见，在这一情境中，个体层面的结果同质化程度可以高于群体层面的结果同质化程度。 Thus, in this scenario, we have seen that individual-level outcome homogenization can be greater than group-level outcome homogenization.
A.3 个体层面度量的推广A.3 Generalizing Individual-Level Metric
在 §3.3 中，我们指出，个体层面的表述框架假设每个个体 $j$ 都会为每个决策者 $i$ 生成输入 $x_{j}^{i}$。 In §3.3, we note that our individual-level framing assumes that every individual $j$ produces inputs $x_{j}^{i}$ for every decision-maker $i$ . 正文中的形式化框架已经允许同一个体面向不同决策者提供不同的输入（例如，Bob 在向 Microsoft 和 Google 求职时可能提交不同的简历）。 The formalism in the main paper already permits these inputs to be different across decision-makers for the same individual (e.g. Bob may submit different resumes when applying to Microsoft and Google). 然而，该形式化框架不支持另外两种更一般的情形：(i) 向每个决策者提供多个输入，以及 (ii) 不向某些决策者提供输入（例如，Bob 不向 Amazon 求职）。 However, the formalism does not support two further general concepts: (i) multiple inputs per decision-maker and (ii) no inputs for some decision-maker (e.g. Bob does not apply to Amazon). 为涵盖前一种情形，我们指出，可以根据应如何汇总多个输入对应的结果来修改失败的定义（例如，可以根据有一定比例的搜索查询向用户返回了较差的结果，来判定搜索引擎发生失败）。 To accommodate the former, we note that the notion of failure can be modified depending on how the outcomes for the multiple inputs should be aggregated (e.g. a failure of a search engine may be determined by some fraction of search queries producing poor results for the user).

A.4 替代度量A.4 Alternative Metrics
在 §3 中，我们介绍了用于量化结果同质化的度量。 In §3, we introduce the metrics we use to quantify outcome homogenization. 当然，正如用于衡量偏见和公平性的数学表达式多种多样，也有许多合理衡量同质化的方法。 Of course, much like the many mathematical expressions that have been used to measure bias and fairness, there are many ways to reasonably measure homogenization. 从根本上说，鉴于结果同质化这一底层构念在很大程度上还是新事物，我们首先承认，对这一概念的理解尚不完整，而且很可能需要在真实系统中开展研究，才能真正明确一个度量应满足的确切要求。 Fundamentally, given the underlying construct of outcome homogenization is largely new, we begin by recognizing our understanding of the concept is incomplete and likely will require study in real systems to truly identify the precise desiderata for a measure.
在此期间，很难评估该度量是否具有结构忠实性（structural fidelity）[Loevinger, 1957]，即该度量的结构是否忠实地刻画了结果同质化？ In the interim, it is difficult to assess if the metric has structural fidelity [Loevinger, 1957], i.e. does the metric’s structure faithfully captures outcome homogenization? 此外，尚不清楚该度量是否具有足够的预测效度，能够预测长期结果（例如，结果同质化带来的长期累积危害），也不清楚它对于检验具体的科学假设和社会假设有多大用处 [Jacobs and Wallach, 2021]。 Further, it is unclear if the metric has sufficient predictive validity to predict long-term outcomes (e.g. longitudinal harms arising from outcome homogenization) or how useful it is for testing specific scientific and social hypotheses [Jacobs and Wallach, 2021]. 最终，我们认为，对该度量的关键检验将是其后果效度（consequential validity）[Messick, 1987]：当该度量“既反映世界中的结构，又向世界施加结构”[Hand, 2016] 时，它是否会产生积极的社会影响。 Ultimately, we believe the key test for the metric will be its consequential validity [Messick, 1987]: will the metric yield positive social impact as it "both reflects structure in the world and imposes structure upon the world" [Hand, 2016].
为便于理解，我们公开讨论所考虑过的其他度量，以及它们为何在某些情况下可能更为合适。 To facilitate understanding, we transparently discuss other metrics we considered and why they may be preferable in some circumstances. 最终，我们采用了论文中描述的度量，因为我们发现它们最为简单，而且更倾向于它们的概率解释；不过，在介绍替代度量时，我们也说明了可能更倾向于采用它们的理由。 Ultimately, we worked with the metrics we describe in the paper as we found them to be the simplest and preferred their probabilistic interpretations, but we include reasons to prefer alternatives as we describe them.
A.4.1 二元情形下的替代度量A.4.1 Alternative Metrics in the Binary Setting
协方差与逐点互信息。 Covariance and Pointwise Mutual Information. 当 $k=2$，即有两个决策者时，我们注意到，我们的度量与（指示）随机变量 $F^{1}$ 和 $F^{2}$ 之间的协方差非常相似。 When $k=2$ , i.e. there are two decision-makers, we note that our metric bears a very close resemblance to the covariance between the (indicator) random variables $F^{1}$ and $F^{2}$ . 具体而言，协方差是构成我们同质化度量中比值的两个量之差。 In particular, the covariance is the difference of the quantities that define the ratio for our homogenization metric. 类似地，除去 $\log$ 运算，我们的度量就是在 $(1,1)$ 处计算的逐点互信息（PMI）。 Similarly, our metric is the pointwise mutual information (PMI) evaluated at $(1,1)$ up to the $\log$ . 
| | $\displaystyle\text{$H^{\textbf{individual}}$}(h^{1},h^{2})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]}{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{2}\right]}$ | | (11)
| | $\displaystyle\text{Cov}(F^{1},F^{2})$ | $\displaystyle=\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]-\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{2}\right]$ | | (12)
| | $\displaystyle\text{PMI}(F^{1}=1,F^{2}=1)$ | $\displaystyle=\log\left(\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]}{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[F^{2}\right]}\right)=\log\left(\text{$H^{\textbf{individual}}$}(h^{1},h^{2})\right)$ | | (13)
与协方差相比，我们更倾向于采用自己的度量，因为在社会系统失败率不同的情境之间，它更便于进行比较，而协方差则与失败率的绝对尺度联系更直接。 With respect to the covariance, we prefer that our metric is more naturally comparable across settings where the failure rates of social systems vary, whereas the covariance is more directly tied to the absolute scale of the failure rates. 对于逐点互信息，我们指出，我们只关注所有模型均失败这一特殊情况下社会系统的行为（这是个体总体上可能获得的 $2^{k}$ 种结果之一），而整体 PMI 则考虑所有这些结果，并且在某些对称变换下保持不变，而这些对称变换在我们的情境中具有重要意义。 With respect to the pointwise mutual information, we note that we are simply looking at the behavior of the social system in a special case where all models fail (which is one of the $2^{k}$ possible outcomes an individual could receive overall), whereas the overall PMI considers all of them and is invariant to symmetries that are significant in our setting. 此外，传统上，这两者都在二元情形下进行研究，而我们研究的是 $k>2$ 情形下的行为。 Further, both are traditionally studied in the binary setting, whereas we study behavior in settings where $k>2$ .
皮尔逊相关系数。 Pearson Correlation. 基于与协方差的关系，我们指出，我们的度量因此也与皮尔逊相关系数相似。 Building on the relationship with the covariance, we note that our metric therefore also resembles the Pearson correlation. 
| | $\displaystyle\text{$H^{\textbf{individual}}$}(h^{1},h^{2})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]}{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right]}$ | | (14)
| | $\displaystyle\text{Corr}(F^{1},F^{2})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]-\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right]}{\sqrt{\left(\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right](1-\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right])\right)\left(\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right](1-\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right])\right)}}$ | | (15)
具体而言，对于准确且同质的模型（即 $\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]>>\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right]$），皮尔逊相关系数与我们的指标近似相同，区别仅在于分母中的平方根。 In particular, when dealing with accurate models that are homogeneous (i.e. $\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\big[F^{1}F^{2}\big]>>\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{1}\right]\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\left[F^{2}\right]$ ), the Pearson correlation coefficient approximates our metric up to the square root in the denominator. 对于这个平方根（更一般地，对于 $k>2$ 时的 $k$ 次方根），支持和反对的理由都存在；为简单起见，我们倾向于采用不引入此类归一化的指标，但也承认，随着该指标接受进一步的压力测试，这种归一化可能会被证明更为有利。 Arguments can be made in favor and against this square root (and more generally a $k$ -th root for $k>2$ ); for simplicity we favor our metric that does not introduce such normalization but acknowledge this normalization may prove to be more favorable as the metric is further stress-tested.
A.4.2 二元设定之外的替代指标A.4.2 Alternative Metrics beyond the Binary Setting
正如我们在脚注 2 中指出的，我们的形式化框架和指标旨在具有通用性，也就是说，它们能够适用于模型 $h^{i}$ 对应不同任务或场景的设定。 As we note in Footnote 2, our formalism and metrics are designed to be general, meaning that they can accommodate settings where the models $h^{i}$ correspond to different tasks or scenarios. （我们在视觉实验，尤其是语言实验中利用了这种通用性（§5）。） (We make use of this generality in our experiments (§5) for vision and, especially, language.) 为了实现这种通用性，我们在使用指示函数 $F^{i}$ 时，将结果（以简化的方式）二值化为失败或非失败。 To permit this generality, we (reductively) binarize outcomes as either failures or not in our use of the indicator functions $F^{i}$ . 具体而言，对于任意不同的任务，其结果空间及其对个体的影响可能无法（轻易地）关联起来。 In particular, for arbitrarily different tasks, the outcome spaces and their consequences on individuals may not be (easily) related.
在某些设定中，特别是当构成社会系统的任务足够相似时，我们可能更倾向于用具有更细致程度区分的损失来替代二元的失败概念。 In some settings, specifically those where the tasks that constitute the social system are sufficiently similar, we may instead prefer a more graded loss in the place of the binary notion of failures. 对于决策者 $i$ 的每次部署，将相应的损失函数记为 $\mathcal{L}^{i}$，使得 $\mathcal{L}^{i}(h^{i}(x_{j}^{i}),y_{j}^{i})=\ell_{j}^{i}$ 表示个体 $j$ 与模型 $h^{i}$ 交互时承受的损失。 For each deployment by decision-maker $i$ , denote the associated loss function as $\mathcal{L}^{i}$ such that $\mathcal{L}^{i}(h^{i}(x_{j}^{i}),y_{j}^{i})=\ell_{j}^{i}$ is the loss experienced by individual $j$ when interacting with model $h^{i}$ . 在这些不同应用中的损失具有可比性的设定下，我们可以考虑以这种损失为基础的其他同质化度量。 In these settings, where the loss achieved across different applications is comparable, we can consider additional measures for homogenization in terms of this loss. 
| | $\displaystyle\text{$H^{\textbf{individual}}$}(h^{1},\dots,h^{k})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[\prod\limits_{i}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}{\prod\limits_{i}\left[\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}$ | | (16)
| | $\displaystyle\text{MinExp}(h^{1},\dots,h^{k})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[\min\limits_{i}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}{\min\limits_{i}\left[\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}$ | | (17)
| | $\displaystyle\text{ExpExp}(h^{1},\dots,h^{k})$ | $\displaystyle=\frac{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}\left[\min\limits_{i}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}{\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{i}\left[\mathop{\mathbb{E}}\limits_{j}~\ell_{j}^{i}\right]}$ | | (18)
用文字表述，MinExp 定义为个体在最佳情况下的平均损失与最佳模型 $h^{\text{best}}$ 的损失之比；ExpExp 的定义与之相同，只是分母采用各模型的平均损失，而非最佳损失。 In words, the MinExp definition is the ratio of the average best-case loss for individuals with the loss of the best model $h^{\text{best}}$ and the ExpExp definition is the same but the denominator is the average loss of the models rather than the best loss. 这些定义与公平性文献中研究的 MaxMin 和字典序（leximax 和 leximin）公平性定义十分相似 [Dubins and Spanier, 1961, Henzinger et al., 2022]；采用最不利群体权重的群体加权版本，其分母可还原为 MaxMin 定义。 These definitions bear close resemblance to the MaxMin and lexicographic (leximax and leximin) fairness definitions studied in the fairness literature [Dubins and Spanier, 1961, Henzinger et al., 2022]; the group-weighted analogue that use the worst group weighting recovers the MaxMin definition in the denominator. （请注意，这里的命名惯例是反过来的，因为我们以损失来定义指标，而公平性与公平分配领域的研究通常以效用来定义指标。） (Note that the naming conventions are reversed since we define metrics in terms of loss whereas work in fairness and equitable allocations generally defines metrics in terms of utility.)
当损失为 0-1 分类损失时，MinExp 定义与 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 非常相似：两者的分子相同（因为指示变量的乘积等于它们的最小值），而分母恰好相差一个 $z=\frac{\prod_{i=1}^{k}\textsc{fail}(h^{i})}{\textsc{fail}(h^{\text{best}})}$ 因子（即除最佳模型外所有模型的失败率之积）。 When the loss is the 0-1 classification loss, the MinExp definition and $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ are very similar: the numerators are the same (as the product of indicator variables is the same as their minimum) and the denominators are precisely $z=\frac{\prod_{i=1}^{k}\textsc{fail}(h^{i})}{\textsc{fail}(h^{\text{best}})}$ factors of each other (i.e. the failure rate of all of the models except the best one). 因此，MinExp 的取值范围为 $[0,1]$，而 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 的取值范围为 $[0,\infty)$。 Consequently, the MinExp yields values in the range $[0,1]$ whereas $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ is in $[0,\infty)$ . 模型间行为相互独立时，$H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 保证为 1，而系统性失败达到最大程度时，MinExp 保证为 1；相应地，行为相互独立时，MinExp 为 $z$（并非常数），而系统性失败达到最大程度时，$H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 为 $\frac{1}{z}$。 Independent behavior across models in $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ is guaranteed to be 1 and maximal systemic failure in MinExp is guaranteed to be 1; symmetrically, independent behavior in MinExp is $z$ (which is non-constant) and maximal systemic failure in $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ is $\frac{1}{z}$ .
更一般地，正文中使用的指示变量可视为采用 0-1 分类损失时的特例，而任意损失函数都可以通过对损失设定阈值来映射为指示变量（这不要求损失具有可比性，因为不同部署可以采用不同的阈值）。 More broadly, the indicator variables we use in the main paper can be seen as a special case when using the 0-1 classification loss whereas arbitrary loss functions can be mapped to indicators by thresholding the loss (which does not require the loss to comparable, as different thresholds can be applied for different deployments). 因此，$H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 和 MinExp 都有明显的优点；我们认为，当个体可以从 $k$ 个可能的模型中选择与哪个模型 $h^{i}$ 交互时，MinExp 尤其可能是一种更自然的定义。 As a result, both $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ and MinExp have clear merits; we believe MinExp may especially be a more natural definition where individuals have choices on which model $h^{i}$ to interact with of the $k$ possible models. 在这些设定中（例如选择使用哪个语音助手，或更一般地选择消费产品），损失自然具有可比性，而且对个体而言，有一个好的选项可能就足够了；相比于指示变量的乘积，损失的最小值能够更平滑地表达这一点。 In these settings (e.g. picking which voice assistant to use, or more generally consumer products), the losses will naturally be comparable and it may suffice for the individual to have one good option, which is more smoothly encoded by the minimum of the loss rather than the product of the indicators.
我们鼓励未来的研究探索 MinExp 与 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 哪个更可取：我们预计，在许多设定中，两者将高度相关，因为它们互为缩放形式，且缩放关系取决于总体错误率，而非错误的相关性；但在某些设定中，两者可能出现分歧，其中一个会明显优于另一个。 We encourage future work to explore whether the MinExp or $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ is preferable: in many settings we expect they will be strongly correlated given they are scalings of each other that depend not on the correlated nature of errors but the overall error rates, but they may be some settings where they diverge and one is clearly preferable to the other. 目前，我们建议在损失具有可比性时也考虑 MinExp，但默认采用 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$，因为它不要求损失具有可比性，且 $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ 具有简单的概率解释。 Currently, we recommend work to also consider MinExp when the losses are comparable, but to default to $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ since this is not required and $H^{\textbf{individual}}$ has a simple probabilistic interpretation.

我们在图 4–6 中报告了 ACS PUMS 的补充结果，在图 7–9 中报告了 LSAC 的补充结果，并在图 10–1 中报告了 GC 的补充结果。 We report additional results for ACS PUMS in Figures 4–6, for LSAC in Figures 7–9, and for GC in Figures 10–1. 通过这些图，我们首先确认了核心发现依然成立：(i) 在不同数据集和模型族中，同质化程度较高（固定）的设置所产生的同质化程度始终高于同质化程度较低（不相交）的设置；(ii) 将个体层面的度量与相应的种族群体层面的度量进行对比时，个体层面的同质化程度始终更高。2626 26 ACS PUMS 是唯一包含种族元数据的数据集。 Across these figures, we first establish that our core findings are upheld: (i) homogenization is reliably greater when there is more homogenized (fixed) than less (disjoint) across datasets and model families and (ii) homogenization is reliably greater when contrasting individual-level measures with the appropriate racial group-level measures.2626 26 ACS PUMS is the only dataset where we have race metadata. 不过，我们确实注意到，不同数据集的同质化程度在绝对量级上存在很大差异：考虑到这些数据集本身差异很大，各数据集内部预测任务之间的关系也很不相同，这并不令人意外。 However, we do note the absolute scale of homogenization is quite different across the datasets: we note that this is not surprising given the datasets are quite different, as are the relationship between the prediction tasks within a dataset. 因此，我们强调，我们的假设预测的是相对变化（即共享会提高同质化程度），但基础同质化程度及其绝对数值也会在很大程度上取决于数据结构和预测任务之间的关系。 Therefore, we highlight that our hypotheses make predictions about relative change (i.e. sharing increases homogenization), but the underlying homogenization and absolute quantities will also depend significantly on the structure of the data and relationship between prediction tasks.
