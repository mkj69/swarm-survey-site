在计算机体系结构中，阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl's law，或阿姆达尔论断）是一个公式，用于限定向执行某项任务的系统增加资源时，该任务所能获得的加速比。 In computer architecture, Amdahl's law (or Amdahl's argument) is a formula limiting the speedup of a task as resources are added to the system executing that task.
该定律可表述为： The law can be stated as:
通过优化系统的某一部分所获得的整体性能提升，受限于被改进部分实际使用时间所占的比例。 the overall performance improvement gained by optimizing a single part of a system is limited by the fraction of time that the improved part is actually used.
该定律以计算机科学家 Gene Amdahl 的名字命名，并于 1967 年在美国信息处理学会联合会（AFIPS）春季联合计算机会议上提出。 It is named after computer scientist Gene Amdahl, and was presented at the American Federation of Information Processing Societies (AFIPS) Spring Joint Computer Conference in 1967.
阿姆达尔定律常用于并行计算，以预测使用多个处理器时的理论加速比。 Amdahl's law is often used in parallel computing to predict the theoretical speedup when using multiple processors.

阿姆达尔定律（Amdahl's law）经常与收益递减规律混为一谈，但只有应用阿姆达尔定律的一种特殊情形才体现了收益递减规律。 Amdahl's law is often conflated with the law of diminishing returns, whereas only a special case of applying Amdahl's law demonstrates the law of diminishing returns. 如果以最优方式（就所实现的加速比而言）选择要改进的部分，那么随着改进的进行，每次改进带来的收益将单调递减。 If one picks optimally (in terms of the achieved speedup) what is to be improved, then one will see monotonically decreasing improvements as one improves. 然而，如果选择并非最优，那么在改进了一个非最优部件之后，转而改进一个更接近最优选择的部件时，就可能看到收益增加。 If, however, one picks non-optimally, after improving a sub-optimal component and moving on to improve a more optimal component, one can see an increase in the return. 请注意，鉴于某些改进比其他改进更困难，或需要更长的开发时间，按照在此意义上“非最优”的顺序改进系统，往往是合理的。 Note that it is often rational to improve a system in an order that is "non-optimal" in this sense, given that some improvements are more difficult or require larger development time than others.

阿姆达尔定律的一个推论是，要加速同时包含串行部分和并行部分的实际应用，就需要采用异构计算技术。 An implication of Amdahl's law is that to speed up real applications which have both serial and parallel portions, heterogeneous computing techniques are required. 已有新型加速比模型和能耗模型，它们基于一种更一般的异构性表示，称为范式异构性（normal form heterogeneity），可支持多种异构众核架构。 There are novel speedup and energy consumption models based on a more general representation of heterogeneity, referred to as the normal form heterogeneity, that support a wide range of heterogeneous many-core architectures. 这些建模方法旨在预测系统的能效和性能范围，并促进硬件和系统软件层面的研究与开发。 These modelling methods aim to predict system power efficiency and performance ranges, and facilitates research and development at the hardware and system software levels.
