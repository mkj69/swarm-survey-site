摘要Abstract
市场中的 LLM 智能体存在算法合谋风险。 LLM agents in markets present algorithmic collusion risks. 尽管先前研究表明，LLM 智能体能够通过默契协调达到高于竞争水平的价格，但现有研究主要关注人工设计的提示。 While prior work shows LLM agents reach supracompetitive prices through tacit coordination, existing research focuses on hand-crafted prompts. 新兴的提示优化范式要求采用新的方法来理解自主智能体的行为。 The emerging paradigm of prompt optimization necessitates new methodologies for understanding autonomous agent behavior. 我们研究提示优化是否会导致市场模拟中涌现合谋行为。 We investigate whether prompt optimization leads to emergent collusive behaviors in market simulations. 我们提出了一个元学习循环，其中 LLM 智能体参与双寡头市场，而一个 LLM 元优化器则迭代改进共享的策略指导。 We propose a meta-learning loop where LLM agents participate in duopoly markets and an LLM meta-optimizer iteratively refines shared strategic guidance. 我们的实验表明，元提示优化使智能体能够发现稳定的默契合谋策略，与基线智能体相比，其协调质量显著提高。 Our experiments reveal that meta-prompt optimization enables agents to discover stable tacit collusion strategies with substantially improved coordination quality compared to baseline agents. 这些行为能够泛化到留出的测试市场，表明智能体发现了通用的协调原则。 These behaviors generalize to held-out test markets, indicating discovery of general coordination principles. 对演化后提示的分析揭示了通过稳定的共享策略实现的系统性协调机制。 Analysis of evolved prompts reveals systematic coordination mechanisms through stable shared strategies. 我们的研究结果呼吁进一步研究自主多智能体系统对 AI 安全的影响。 Our findings call for further investigation into AI safety implications in autonomous multi-agent systems.

1 引言1 Introduction
大型语言模型（LLM）智能体在现实世界中日益强大且日益普及，其应用范围涵盖经济市场和协作系统等领域。 Large Language Model (LLM) agents have become increasingly powerful and prevalent in the real world, ranging from economic markets to collaborative systems. 这既带来了机遇，也带来了需要应对的重大风险 (Tomašev et al., 2025)。 This presents both opportunities and significant risks that need to be addressed (Tomašev et al., 2025). 特别是，以往在各自领域中研究的现实问题，如今需要将 LLM 智能体纳入考量。 In particular, real-world issues that were previously studied in respective domains now need to be considered with LLM agents in mind. 市场机制中的算法合谋就是一个例子 (Akerlof, 1970)。 One example is algorithmic collusion in market mechanisms (Akerlof, 1970). 此前，基于强化学习（RL）的算法已展现出在没有显式通信的情况下，通过默契协调学会将价格设定在高于竞争水平的能力 (Waltman and Kaymak, 2008; Klein, 2021; Calvano et al., 2019)，后续研究表明，LLM 智能体也能达到高于竞争水平的价格 (Lin et al., 2024; Fish et al., 2024; Fish et al., 2025; Cao and Hu, 2026)。 Previously, RL-based algorithms have shown the ability to learn supracompetitive prices through tacit coordination without explicit communication (Waltman and Kaymak, 2008; Klein, 2021; Calvano et al., 2019), and further work shows LLM agents also reach supracompetitive prices (Lin et al., 2024; Fish et al., 2024; Fish et al., 2025; Cao and Hu, 2026). 已有研究提出了识别此类合谋的方法 (Motwani et al., 2024)，以及通过对齐智能体来纠正此类合谋的方法 (Bai et al., 2022; Lee et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2025; Keppo et al., 2026)。 Work has been proposed to find such collusion (Motwani et al., 2024) and fix it by aligning agents (Bai et al., 2022; Lee et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2025; Keppo et al., 2026).
尽管这些工作对于将严谨的市场经济学与 LLM 智能体相结合十分重要，但它们大多关注人工设计的智能体提示。 While these efforts are important in bridging rigorous market-based economics with LLM agents, they mostly focus on hand-crafted agent prompts. 然而，当前推动 LLM 智能体广泛应用的趋势体现了向自我改进系统的范式转变 (Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Ozer et al., 2025)，在此类系统中，智能体自主优化自身的提示和策略 (Agrawal et al., 2025; Yi et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025)，而非依赖人类明确指定的指令。 However, the current trend driving widespread adoption of LLM agents represents a paradigm shift towards self-improving systems (Shinn et al., 2023; Madaan et al., 2023; Ozer et al., 2025), where agents autonomously optimize their own prompts and strategies (Agrawal et al., 2025; Yi et al., 2025; Li et al., 2025) rather than relying on explicit human-specified instructions. 在这一新范式下，指导智能体的提示是优化的结果，而非明确指定的内容，因此现有的智能体行为分析方法可能不再足够。 Given this new paradigm where prompts that guide agents are the result of optimization rather than explicit specification, existing approaches to agent behavior analysis may no longer suffice. 这一转变要求采用新的方法来理解此类自主系统中的智能体行为及其机制设计。 This shift necessitates new methodologies for understanding agent behavior in such autonomous systems and their mechanism designs.
在本研究中，我们顺应这一新范式，研究将提示优化作为控制智能体行为的机制，以及这是否会导致合谋行为涌现。 In this work, we embrace this new paradigm by investigating prompt optimization as a mechanism for controlling agent behavior and whether this leads to emergent collusive behaviors. 我们采用了一种能够捕捉关键动态的市场经济模拟。 We use a market-based economic simulation that captures the essential dynamics. 随后，我们针对元提示（指令层面的提示，而非特定情境下的提示组件）提出了一个元学习循环：在每一轮中，我们先使用 LLM 智能体运行经济模拟，再利用具有反思能力的 LLM 对元提示进行自我改进。 We then propose a meta-learning loop for meta-prompts (instruction-level prompts rather than context-specific prompt components): in each round, we run the economics simulation with LLM agents, then self-improve the meta-prompt using a reflective LLM. 我们证明，这些智能体表现出有趣的涌现行为，包括更稳定的市场行为和可迁移的算法合谋。 We demonstrate that these agents exhibit interesting emergent behaviors, including more stable market behavior and transferable algorithmic collusion. 我们的研究结果揭示了 LLM 智能体在这一新范式下的潜在行为，呼吁对机制设计展开讨论。 Our findings reveal potential behaviors of LLM agents in this new paradigm, calling for discussion of mechanism designs.
2 方法2 Method
2.1 经济环境设定2.1 Economic Setting
我们采用与 Fish et al. (2025) 相同的市场模型，该模型基于嵌套 logit 需求函数 (Berry, 1994; Mansley et al., 2019)。 We use the same market model as Fish et al. (2025), based on the nested logit demand function (Berry, 1994; Mansley et al., 2019). 此处我们使用 Mansley et al. (2019) 中的符号来描述该模型。 We describe it here using notation from Mansley et al. (2019). 我们考虑一个市场，其中的产品（商品）以 $j=1,\dots,N$ 为索引，另有一种外部商品 $j=0$。 We consider a market with products (goods) indexed by $j=1,\dots,N$ , and an outside good $j=0$ . 产品被划分为 $G+1$ 个互不相交的组 $g=0,1,\dots,G$，其中组 $0$ 仅包含外部商品。 Products are grouped into $G+1$ disjoint groups $g=0,1,\dots,G$ , where group $0$ contains only the outside good. 产品 $j$ 被选择的概率为 $z_{j}=\left(\exp\left(\frac{\delta_{j}}{1-\sigma}\right)\right)/\left(\left(D_{g}\right)^{\sigma}\left(\sum_{g^{\prime}}\left(D_{g^{\prime}}\right)^{1-\sigma}\right)\right)$，其中 $D_{g}=\sum_{k\in\mathscr{G}_{g}}\exp\left(\frac{\delta_{k}}{1-\sigma}\right)$ 是求和的简写（$\mathscr{G}_{g}$ 表示组 $g$ 中的产品集合），$g^{\prime}$ 遍历所有组，$\delta_{j}$ 表示产品 $j$ 的总体吸引力，$0\leq\sigma<1$ 表示替代弹性。 The selection probability of product $j$ is $z_{j}=\left(\exp\left(\frac{\delta_{j}}{1-\sigma}\right)\right)/\left(\left(D_{g}\right)^{\sigma}\left(\sum_{g^{\prime}}\left(D_{g^{\prime}}\right)^{1-\sigma}\right)\right)$ , where $D_{g}=\sum_{k\in\mathscr{G}_{g}}\exp\left(\frac{\delta_{k}}{1-\sigma}\right)$ is shorthand for summation ( $\mathscr{G}_{g}$ denotes the set of products in group $g$ ), $g^{\prime}$ iterates over all groups, $\delta_{j}$ represents the overall attractiveness of product $j$ , and $0\leq\sigma<1$ represents the elasticity of substitution. 产品 $j$ 的需求量就是 $q_{j}=Mz_{j}$，其中 $M$ 为市场总规模。 The demand (quantity) for product $j$ is simply $q_{j}=Mz_{j}$ , where $M$ is the total market size. 产品 $j$ 的吸引力为 $\delta_{j}=a_{j}-p_{j}/\alpha_{j}$，其中，$a_{j}$ 为产品 $j$ 的质量，$p_{j}$ 为价格，$\alpha_{j}$ 为价格敏感度。 The attractiveness of product $j$ is $\delta_{j}=a_{j}-p_{j}/\alpha_{j}$ , where $a_{j}$ is the quality of product $j$ , $p_{j}$ the price, and $\alpha_{j}$ the price sensitivity. 对于外部商品（outside good）$j=0$，有 $\delta_{0}=a_{0}$。 For the outside good $j=0$ , $\delta_{0}=a_{0}$ . 给定产品 $j$ 的成本 $c_{j}$，其利润为 $\pi_{j}=q_{j}(p_{j}/\alpha_{j}-c_{j})$。 Given the cost $c_{j}$ for product $j$ , the profit is $\pi_{j}=q_{j}(p_{j}/\alpha_{j}-c_{j})$ .
令 $\mathcal{A}=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ 表示参与市场的智能体集合。 Let $\mathcal{A}=\{1,2,\ldots,n\}$ denote the set of agents participating in the market. 对于每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{A}$，令 $\mathcal{J}_{i}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ 表示由智能体 $i$ 控制的产品集合，其中，各集合 $\{\mathcal{J}_{i}\}_{i\in\mathcal{A}}$ 两两不相交，且 $\bigcup_{i\in\mathcal{A}}\mathcal{J}_{i}=\{1,\ldots,N\}$（每种产品恰好由一个智能体控制）。 For each agent $i\in\mathcal{A}$ , let $\mathcal{J}_{i}\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ denote the set of products controlled by agent $i$ , where the sets $\{\mathcal{J}_{i}\}_{i\in\mathcal{A}}$ are pairwise disjoint and $\bigcup_{i\in\mathcal{A}}\mathcal{J}_{i}=\{1,\ldots,N\}$ (each product is controlled by exactly one agent). 每个智能体 $i$ 为其所有产品 $j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}$ 设定价格 $p_{j}$，以最大化总利润 $\pi_{i}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}}\pi_{j}$。 Each agent $i$ sets prices $p_{j}$ for all its products $j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}$ to maximize total profit $\pi_{i}=\sum_{j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}}\pi_{j}$ .
我们从博弈论视角将合谋描述为非竞争性行为，并计算理论上的纳什均衡价格和垄断价格，作为分析中的参照，两者的计算方法均见附录 A。请注意，智能体并不知道这两种参照价格，因为计算它们需要访问隐藏的市场参数。 We describe collusion as non-competitive behavior from a game-theoretic point of view and compute the theoretical Nash-equilibrium and monopoly prices for reference in our analysis, both as described in Appendix A. Note that these two reference prices are not known to agents, since computing them would need to access hidden market parameters.
市场运行多个轮次 $t=1,2,\ldots,T$。 The market runs for multiple episodes $t=1,2,\ldots,T$ . 在每个轮次 $t$，可观测的市场状态包括每种产品 $j$ 的价格 $p_{j}^{(t)}$、需求量（数量）$q_{j}^{(t)}$、成本 $c_{j}^{(t)}$ 和利润 $\pi_{j}^{(t)}$。 At each episode $t$ , the observable market state consists of prices $p_{j}^{(t)}$ , demands (quantities) $q_{j}^{(t)}$ , costs $c_{j}^{(t)}$ , and profits $\pi_{j}^{(t)}$ for each product $j$ . 在每个轮次，所有智能体都基于截至当前的所有轮次的信息做出决策。 At each episode, all agents make decisions based on information from all episodes up to the current point. 每个智能体都能观测到其控制的产品的价格、成本、需求量（数量）和利润，以及市场上所有其他产品的价格。 Each agent observes the price, cost, demand (quantity), and profit for the products it controls, as well as the prices of all other products in the market. 为避免各轮次之间呈现平稳性，我们遵循 Fish et al. (2025) 的做法，使价格敏感度参数 $\alpha_{j}$ 逐渐变化。 To avoid stationarity across episodes, we introduce gradual changes to the price sensitivity parameters $\alpha_{j}$ , following Fish et al. (2025).

2.2 LLM 智能体与元提示2.2 LLM Agents and Meta-Prompting
市场模拟中的智能体行为。Agent Behavior in Market Simulation.
每个 LLM 智能体 $i\in\mathcal{A}$ 都在由多个轮次组成的市场模拟中运行（见算法 1）。 Each LLM agent $i\in\mathcal{A}$ operates within a market simulation consisting of multiple episodes (See Algorithm 1). 所有智能体都是同质的：它们在相同的元提示 $\mathcal{M}$ 下运行，该元提示包含高层次的策略指令，而各智能体观测到的信息则因其产品而异。 All agents are homogeneous: they operate under the same meta-prompt $\mathcal{M}$ containing high-level strategic instructions, while observing distinct information determined by their products. 在每个轮次 $t$，智能体 $i$ 观测到：(1) 其控制的产品 $j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}$ 的历史价格、成本、需求量和利润；以及 (2) 所有竞争产品的历史价格信息。 At each episode $t$ , agent $i$ observes: (1) historical prices, costs, demands, and profits for products $j\in\mathcal{J}_{i}$ under its control; and (2) historical price information for all competing products. 每个智能体都维护自己的观测历史 $\mathcal{H}_{i}$ 和推理自我笔记 $\mathcal{N}_{i}$。 Each agent maintains its own history $\mathcal{H}_{i}$ of observations and self-notes $\mathcal{N}_{i}$ of reasoning. 随后，智能体 $i$ 为其产品生成定价决策 $p_{j}^{(t)}$，并给出相应理由，将其追加到智能体的自我笔记中，以供后续参考。 Agent $i$ then generates pricing decisions $p_{j}^{(t)}$ for its products accompanied by a rationale, that is appended to the agent’s self-notes for reference later. 这种做法使智能体能够进行上下文学习，也是以往研究中的常见做法。 Doing so allows in-context learning of agents and is commonly done in prior works. 有关 LLM 智能体的更多细节见附录 B.1。 More details of LLM agents are in Appendix B.1.
元提示优化。Meta-Prompt Optimization.
我们在多个市场情景中优化共享的元提示 $\mathcal{M}$（见算法 2）。 We optimize the shared meta-prompt $\mathcal{M}$ over multiple market scenarios (See Algorithm 2). 在每一轮优化 $r$ 中，我们使用当前元提示 $\mathcal{M}^{(r)}$，在全部 $K$ 种市场配置下运行 LLM 智能体（每种配置包含 $T$ 个轮次），然后使用一个 LLM 元优化器分析 $\mathcal{M}^{(r)}$ 和完整的市场记录。 In each optimization round $r$ , we run the LLM agent with the current meta-prompt $\mathcal{M}^{(r)}$ across all $K$ market configurations (each with $T$ episodes), and then we use an LLM meta-optimizer to analyze the $\mathcal{M}^{(r)}$ and the complete market records. 元优化器改进元提示，生成供下一轮使用的 $\mathcal{M}^{(r+1)}$，提供更完善的通用策略指导，而非针对特定市场的指令或数值指令。 The meta-optimizer refines the meta-prompt to produce $\mathcal{M}^{(r+1)}$ in the subsequent round, with improved generic strategic guidance rather than market-specific or numerical directives. 我们选择让所有智能体共享元提示 $\mathcal{M}$，因为它提供不因智能体或市场而变化的指导，概括了通用的元策略。 We choose the meta-prompt $\mathcal{M}$ to be shared among all agents, since it serves as agent- and market-invariant guidance that captures generic, meta strategies. 在提示 LLM 进行元提示优化时，我们明确禁止任何会使元提示成为秘密共享渠道的行为。 When prompting the LLM for meta-prompt optimization, we explicitly forbid behaviors that would turn the meta-prompt into a channel for secret sharing. 有关元提示优化的更多细节见附录 B.2，附录 D 则表明，演化得到的元提示具有通用性。 More details of the meta-prompt optimization are in Appendix B.2, and Appendix D shows the evolved meta-prompt is generic.
合谋设置。Collusion Settings.
我们考虑一个包含两个智能体（$|\mathcal{A}|=2$）和两种产品（$N=2$）的双寡头市场。 We consider a duopoly market with two agents ( $|\mathcal{A}|=2$ ) and two products ( $N=2$ ). 每个智能体 $i$ 各自控制一种不同的产品 $i$，因此对于 $i\in\{1,2\}$，有 $\mathcal{J}_{i}=\{i\}$。 Each agent $i$ controls a single distinct product $i$ , so $\mathcal{J}_{i}=\{i\}$ for $i\in\{1,2\}$ . 关键在于，每个智能体只能观测到竞争对手产品的价格，而无法观测到其成本、需求量或利润。 Critically, each agent observes only the prices of competitors’ products, but not their costs, demands, or profits. 这种信息结构有利于默契合谋，即智能体通过价格信号进行协调，而无需智能体之间的显式通信。 This information structure facilitates tacit collusion where agents coordinate through price signals without explicit inter-agent communication.

4 结论4 Conclusion
我们研究了经济市场中 LLM 智能体的提示优化，证明元提示优化使智能体能够在没有显式协调的情况下发现稳定的默契合谋策略：与仅依靠上下文学习相比，优化后的智能体表现出显著更稳定的定价和更高的协调质量，且其策略能够成功泛化至未见过的市场。 We investigate prompt optimization for LLM agents in economic markets, demonstrating that meta-prompt optimization enables agents to discover stable tacit collusion strategies without explicit coordination: optimized agents exhibit substantially more stable pricing and improved coordination quality compared to in-context learning alone, with strategies generalizing successfully to unseen markets. 重要的是，我们的方法为智能体所学到的系统性协调机制提供了显式且人类可解释的表示。 Importantly, our method provides explicit, human-interpretable representations of systematic coordination mechanisms learned by agents. 随着 LLM 智能体在现实世界系统中日益普及，这些发现对 AI 安全和市场监管具有重要意义。 These findings have important implications for AI safety and market regulation as LLM agents become increasingly prevalent in real-world systems. 未来的大规模研究应理解优化后智能体的行为，探索干预策略，并建立稳健的框架，以使自主多智能体系统产生有益结果。 Future large-scale research should understand optimized agent behavior, explore intervention strategies, and develop robust frameworks for beneficial outcomes in autonomous multi-agent systems.

A.1 合谋A.1 Collusion
我们在寡头垄断理论框架下分析合谋（Stigler, 1964），在该框架中，市场参与者共同决定价格。 We analyze collusion within the framework of oligopoly theory (Stigler, 1964), where market participants jointly determine prices. 合谋可以是显式的，例如通过侧信道通信；也可以是默契的，例如在定价时仅使用市场中的公开信息。 Collusion can be explicit, such as via side-channel communication, or tacit, such as using only public information in the market when setting prices. 在本研究中，我们重点关注默契合谋。 In this work, we focus on tacit collusion.
我们使用博弈论研究合谋，这意味着我们关注行为如何偏离非合作纳什均衡（Telser, 2017）。 We study collusion using game theory, which means we focus on how behavior moves beyond the non-cooperative Nash equilibrium (Telser, 2017). 为给分析提供参照，我们遵循 Fish et al. (2025); Mansley et al. (2019) 的方法，计算所使用的嵌套 Logit 需求模型的纳什均衡价格和垄断价格。 For reference in our analysis, we compute the Nash equilibrium and monopoly prices for the nested logit demand model we use, following Fish et al. A.2 垄断定价A.2 Monopoly Pricing
对于控制所有产品 $j=1,\ldots,N$ 的单个智能体，垄断价格通过求解以下优化问题得到： For a single agent controlling all products $j=1,\ldots,N$ , the monopoly prices solve the optimization problem: 
| | $\displaystyle p^{\text{mon}}=\arg\max_{p}\sum_{j=1}^{N}q_{j}(p)\left(\frac{p_{j}}{\alpha_{j}}-c_{j}\right)$ |
其中，$q_{j}(p)$ 是给定价格 $p$ 时产品 $j$ 的需求量。 where $q_{j}(p)$ is the demand for product $j$ given prices $p$ . 我们使用数值优化方法（信赖域约束法）求解该问题，以边际成本定价 $p_{j}^{(0)}=c_{j}\alpha_{j}$ 为初始值，约束条件为 $p_{j}\geq c_{j}\alpha_{j}$，收敛容差为 $\epsilon=10^{-8}$。 We solve this using numerical optimization (a trust-region-constrained method) initialized at marginal cost pricing $p_{j}^{(0)}=c_{j}\alpha_{j}$ with constraints $p_{j}\geq c_{j}\alpha_{j}$ and convergence tolerance $\epsilon=10^{-8}$ .
A.3 纳什均衡定价A.3 Nash Equilibrium Pricing
对于每个控制产品 $i$ 并最大化 $\pi_{i}=q_{i}(p)(p_{i}/\alpha_{i}-c_{i})$ 的智能体 $i$，纳什均衡价格满足： For each agent $i$ controlling product $i$ and maximizing $\pi_{i}=q_{i}(p)(p_{i}/\alpha_{i}-c_{i})$ , the Nash equilibrium prices satisfy: 
| | $\displaystyle p_{i}^{\text{nash}}\in\arg\max_{p_{i}}q_{i}(p_{i},p_{-i}^{\text{nash}})\left(\frac{p_{i}}{\alpha_{i}}-c_{i}\right)\quad\forall i$ |
我们使用类似 EM 的迭代算法计算纳什均衡价格：首先初始化 $p^{(0)}\sim\mathcal{U}[\min_{j}c_{j}\alpha_{j},\max_{j}p_{j}^{\text{mon}}]$，然后迭代更新每个智能体的最优反应 We compute the Nash equilibrium prices using an EM-style iterative algorithm: we initialize $p^{(0)}\sim\mathcal{U}[\min_{j}c_{j}\alpha_{j},\max_{j}p_{j}^{\text{mon}}]$ , then iteratively update each agent’s best response 
| | $\displaystyle p_{i}^{(t+1)}=\arg\max_{p_{i}\in[c_{i}\alpha_{i},\max_{j}p_{j}^{\text{mon}}]}q_{i}(p_{i},p_{-i}^{(t)})\left(\frac{p_{i}}{\alpha_{i}}-c_{i}\right)$ |
直至满足收敛条件 $\|p^{(t+1)}-p^{(t)}\|<\epsilon=10^{-8}$ 或 $t>1000$。 until convergence $\|p^{(t+1)}-p^{(t)}\|<\epsilon=10^{-8}$ or $t>1000$ . 对于嵌套 Logit 需求，数值方法通常是计算纳什均衡时唯一在计算上可行的方法（Berry, 1994）。 For nested-logit demand, numerical methods are typically the only computationally feasible approach to computing Nash equilibria (Berry, 1994).
