Abstract摘要
When are multi-agent LLM systems merely a collection of individual agents versus an integrated collective with higher-order structure? We introduce an information-theoretic framework to test—in a purely data-driven way—whether multi-agent systems show signs of higher-order structure. This information decomposition lets us measure whether dynamical emergence is present in multi-agent LLM systems, localize it, and distinguish spurious temporal coupling from performance-relevant cross-agent synergy. We implement a practical criterion and an emergence capacity criterion operationalized as partial information decomposition of time-delayed mutual information (TDMI). We apply our framework to experiments using a simple guessing game without direct agent communication and minimal group-level feedback with three randomized interventions. Groups in the control condition exhibit strong temporal synergy but little coordinated alignment across agents. Assigning a persona to each agent introduces stable identity-linked differentiation. Combining personas with an instruction to “think about what other agents might do” shows identity-linked differentiation and goal-directed complementarity across agents. Taken together, our framework establishes that multi-agent LLM systems can be steered with prompt design from mere aggregates to higher-order collectives. Our results are robust across emergence measures and entropy estimators, and not explained by coordination-free baselines or temporal dynamics alone. Without attributing human-like cognition to the agents, the patterns of interaction we observe mirror well-established principles of collective intelligence in human groups: effective performance requires both alignment on shared objectives and complementary contributions across members.
多智能体 LLM 系统何时仅仅是独立智能体的集合，何时又是具有高阶结构的一体化集体？我们提出了一个信息论框架，以纯粹由数据驱动的方式检验多智能体系统是否表现出高阶结构的迹象。这种信息分解使我们能够衡量多智能体 LLM 系统中是否存在动力学涌现，定位其所在，并区分虚假的时间耦合与对性能有实际影响的跨智能体协同。我们实现了一项实用判据和一项涌现能力判据，并通过对时间延迟互信息（TDMI）进行部分信息分解来将其操作化。我们将该框架应用于一项简单猜测游戏的实验；该实验不允许智能体直接交流，仅提供极少的群体层面反馈，并设置了三种随机化干预。对照条件下的群体表现出很强的时间协同，但智能体之间几乎没有协调一致性。为每个智能体指定一个角色设定，会引入与身份相关的稳定差异化。将角色设定与“思考其他智能体可能会怎么做”的指令相结合，会使智能体之间表现出与身份相关的差异化，以及以目标为导向的互补性。综合来看，我们的框架证实，可以通过提示设计，引导多智能体 LLM 系统从单纯的聚合体转变为高阶集体。我们的结果在不同涌现度量和熵估计方法下均具有稳健性，且无法仅用无协调基线或时间动态来解释。在不将类人认知归于智能体的前提下，我们观察到的交互模式体现了人类群体集体智能中已得到充分确立的原则：有效的表现既需要围绕共同目标保持一致，也需要成员之间作出互补的贡献。
1 Introduction1 引言
Recent advances in generative AI (specifically LLMs) have led to tremendous advances in multi-agent systems (Qu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024a; Subramaniam et al., 2025). Multi-agent systems often show impressive performance increases over single-agent solutions (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024b; Tao et al., 2024). A key argument behind such performance gains are claims to “greater-than-the-sum-of-its-parts” effects from differentiated agents (Chen et al., 2023, p.1). Connecting multiple differentiated agents has the potential benefit to leverage unique contributions that would not be available if the task was assigned to a single agent (Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Tollefsen et al., 2013; Luppi et al., 2024; Lix et al., 2022). Conceptually, any group gains depend on synergy and emergence (Theiner, 2018; Riedl et al., 2021; Page, 2008; Hayek, 1945).[fn1] Despite impressive performance of many multi-agent systems we do not yet have a principled understanding when and how such synergy arises, what role agent differentiation plays, and how to steer it systematically. This all points to a crucial need for deeper understanding of collective intelligence in multi-agent systems, specifically whether multi-agent LLMs exhibit any synergy or complementarity at all—the necessary prerequisite for any absolute team-over-solo gains.
生成式 AI（特别是 LLMs）的近期进展推动了多智能体系统的巨大进步 (Qu et al., 2024; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024a; Subramaniam et al., 2025)。与单智能体解决方案相比，多智能体系统往往表现出显著的性能提升 (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024b; Tao et al., 2024)。解释此类性能提升的一个核心论点是，具有差异的智能体能够产生“整体大于部分之和”的效应 (Chen et al., 2023, p.1)。将多个具有差异的智能体连接起来，具有利用其独特贡献的潜在优势；如果将任务交给单个智能体，这些贡献便无法获得 (Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Tollefsen et al., 2013; Luppi et al., 2024; Lix et al., 2022)。从概念上讲，任何群体增益都依赖于协同（synergy）与涌现（emergence）(Theiner, 2018; Riedl et al., 2021; Page, 2008; Hayek, 1945)。[fn1] 尽管许多多智能体系统的表现令人瞩目，我们仍未从原理上理解这种协同何时以及如何产生、智能体分化在其中发挥什么作用，以及如何系统地引导这种协同。这一切都表明，我们迫切需要更深入地理解多智能体系统中的集体智能，尤其是多智能体 LLM 是否确实表现出任何协同或互补性——这是团队相对于单个智能体获得任何绝对增益的必要前提。

Determining whether multi-agent systems function as genuine collectives—rather than merely aggregating agents—requires a principled measure of synergy. We follow a purely data-driven approach to assess whether these systems exhibit higher-order synergy, characterized by structural coupling and joint information about future states and task outcomes, as evidence of emergent collective behavior. Intuitively, synergy refers to information about a target that a collection of variables provide only jointly but not individually (Rosas et al., 2020; Humphreys, 1997).[fn2] We develop a principled framework (Figure 1a) building on new insights in information theory (Rosas et al., 2020; Mediano et al., 2022b). Our goal is to develop methods to measure emergent role specialization, determine whether multi-agent systems show such signs of emergent synergy, explore whether synergy enables increased performance, and explore whether emergent behavior can be systematically steered with prompting.[fn3]

Our findings provide evidence for multi-agent LLM system capacity for emergence and that it underpins performance. Through a variety of complementary analyses and robustness tests, we show that the coordination style across interventions is very different. While emergence is present in all, only the ToM-prompt condition leads to groups with identity-linked differentiation and goal-directed complementarity across agents: they operate as a dynamically stable, integrated, goal-directed unit. 2 Method2 方法
Group Task.群体任务。
We study a group guessing game (developed by Goldstone et al. (2024) who called it “group binary search”) without communication: agents propose integers whose sum needs to match a randomly generated hidden target number. Agents are unaware of each others’ guesses and the size of their group, and receive only group-level feedback “too high” or “too low.” The task is challenging because identical strategies induce oscillation and only complementary strategies yield success. This setting naturally pits redundancy (alignment) against synergy (useful diversity), and admits clear nulls via row‑wise (break identities) and column‑wise (break alignment) surrogates. The binary search setting is a minimalist testbed to isolate emergence of cross-agent complementarity and dynamic coordination, not to establish team-over-solo superiority. The implementation of a textbook-like XOR dynamic allows us to study the benefits of emergence capacity conditional on a multi-agent setup (and not to establish team-over-solo superiority). Research on humans has shown that groups can reliably solve this task through the emergence of specialized, complementary roles (Goldstone et al., 2024).
我们研究一项无通信的群体猜数游戏（由 Goldstone et al. (2024) 开发，并称为“群体二分搜索”）：智能体各自提出整数，这些整数之和需要与随机生成的隐藏目标数相匹配。智能体不知道彼此的猜测值，也不知道所在群体的规模，只会收到“过高”或“过低”的群体层面反馈。这项任务具有挑战性，因为相同的策略会引发振荡，只有互补策略才能取得成功。这一设置自然地使冗余性（对齐）与协同性（有用的多样性）形成对立，并可通过逐行（破坏身份）和逐列（破坏对齐）构造替代数据，建立明确的零假设。二分搜索设置是一个极简测试平台，用于单独考察跨智能体互补性和动态协调的涌现，而非证明团队优于个体。通过实现一种教科书式的 XOR 动态机制，我们能够研究在多智能体设置下涌现能力带来的益处（而非证明团队优于个体）。针对人类的研究表明，群体可以通过涌现出专业化、互补的角色，可靠地解决这项任务（Goldstone et al., 2024）。
Interventions.干预。
Plain: The control condition uses only instructions for the guessing game, similar to those used in human subject experiments (Goldstone et al., 2024). Persona: For the persona condition, we follow recent insights into the use of personas within the LLM community (Chen et al., 2024) and ensure personas have relevant attributes: name, gender, age, occupation or skill background, pronouns, personality traits (Goldberg, 1992), and personal values (Cieciuch and Davidov, 2012). ToM: The ToM condition instructs agents to think about what other agents might do, and how their actions might affect the group outcome. This is akin to introducing social skills as defined in the human capital literature (Deming, 2022). See Appendix A.1 for all prompts, full persona generation recipes, and example persona.
Plain：对照条件仅使用猜数游戏的指令，这些指令与人类被试实验中使用的指令相似（Goldstone et al., 2024）。角色设定（Persona）：对于角色设定条件，我们遵循 LLM 研究社区近期关于角色设定使用的见解 (Chen et al., 2024)，确保角色具备相关属性：姓名、性别、年龄、职业或技能背景、人称代词、人格特质 (Goldberg, 1992) 和个人价值观 (Cieciuch and Davidov, 2012)。心智理论（ToM）：ToM 条件要求智能体思考其他智能体可能采取什么行动，以及自身行动可能如何影响群体结果。这类似于引入人力资本文献中所定义的社交技能 (Deming, 2022)。所有提示词、完整的角色生成流程以及角色示例均见附录 A.1。
Analytical Framework.分析框架。
How would we know if a multi-agent system shows emergent properties that could suggest that the sum is more than its parts? We develop a principled test based on a recent formal theory of dynamic emergence based on information decomposition (Rosas et al., 2020; Mediano et al., 2022b; Bedau and Humphreys, 2008). Our framework connects emergence with information about a system’s temporal evolution—future states of the whole—with information that cannot be traced to the current state of its parts. This framework is based on partial information decomposition (Williams and Beer, 2010, PID;) and time-delayed mutual information (Luppi et al., 2022, TDMI;), is data-driven, quantifiable, and amenable to empirical testing with falsifiable conjectures against specific null hypotheses. Combining this with permutation tests, focused either on breaking connections between agents or connections across time allow us not only to quantity emergent properties but also to localize them in the system. This is crucial for multi-agent systems because we care about distinguishing synergy among specialized, differentiated agents from mere dynamic alignment.
我们如何判断一个多智能体系统是否表现出涌现特性，从而表明整体可能大于各部分之和？我们基于近期一种以信息分解为基础的动态涌现形式理论，提出了一项具有理论依据的检验 (Rosas et al., 2020; Mediano et al., 2022b; Bedau and Humphreys, 2008)。我们的框架将涌现与有关系统时间演化（即整体的未来状态）的信息联系起来，而这些信息无法追溯至各部分的当前状态。该框架以部分信息分解（partial information decomposition，PID）(Williams and Beer, 2010, PID;) 和时延互信息（time-delayed mutual information，TDMI）(Luppi et al., 2022, TDMI;) 为基础，由数据驱动、可量化，并且能够通过针对特定零假设提出可证伪的猜想来开展实证检验。将这一框架与旨在打破智能体之间联系或跨时间联系的置换检验相结合，不仅使我们能够量化涌现特性，还能确定这些特性在系统中的位置。这对多智能体系统至关重要，因为我们关注的是区分具有专业分工和差异化特征的智能体之间的协同作用与单纯的动态一致性。
We implement three tests. The first, termed emergence capacity, captures the ability of the multi-agent system to host any synergy. It measures the predictive synergy from two agents’ current states to their joint future state, quantified using mutual information $I(\cdot;\cdot)$ . For each pair of agents $(i,j)$ and time $t$ , let the sources be $X_{i,t}$ and $X_{j,t}$ , and define the bivariate target as the joint future state $t+\ell$ , $T_{ij,t+\ell}\equiv(X_{i,t+\ell},X_{j,t+\ell})$ , where $\ell$ is the integration timescale (Mediano et al., 2022a). From the joint contingency table over $(X_{i,t},X_{j,t},T_{ij,t+\ell})$ we compute a two-source PID of the predictive information
我们实施三项检验。第一项称为涌现能力，用于刻画多智能体系统产生任何协同作用的能力。它衡量两个智能体的当前状态对其联合未来状态的预测协同作用，并使用互信息 $I(\cdot;\cdot)$ 进行量化。对于每一对智能体 $(i,j)$ 和时间 $t$，令信息源为 $X_{i,t}$ 和 $X_{j,t}$，并将二元目标定义为 $t+\ell$ 时刻的联合未来状态，即 $T_{ij,t+\ell}\equiv(X_{i,t+\ell},X_{j,t+\ell})$，其中 $\ell$ 为整合时间尺度 (Mediano et al., 2022a)。根据 $(X_{i,t},X_{j,t},T_{ij,t+\ell})$ 的联合列联表，我们对预测信息进行双源 PID 分解
$$I(\{X_{i,t},X_{j,t}\};\,T_{ij,t+\ell})=\mathrm{UI}_{i}+\mathrm{UI}_{j}+\mathrm{Red}_{ij}+\mathrm{Syn}_{ij}$$
and take $\mathrm{Syn}_{ij}$ as the pairwise dynamical synergy. A positive score $\mathrm{Syn}_{ij}>0$ indicates that the predictive information about the joint future is not recoverable from any single component. We compute this for all unordered pairs $(i,j)$ and take the median as group-level synergy capacity. This test is limited to detect synergy of order $k=2$ but has the advantage that it does not rely on the definition of a suitable whole-system macro signal.
并将 $\mathrm{Syn}_{ij}$ 作为成对动态协同作用的度量。正值 $\mathrm{Syn}_{ij}>0$ 表明，有关联合未来状态的预测信息无法从任何单一组成部分中恢复。我们对所有无序对 $(i,j)$ 计算该值，并取其中位数作为群体层面的协同能力。该检验仅能检测阶数为 $k=2$ 的协同作用，但其优点在于不依赖于对合适的全系统宏观信号的定义。
The second test, termed the practical criterion, concerns predicting a macro signal $V$ of the system. It asks whether the macro contains predictive information beyond any individual part. Given microstate $X_{t}=(X_{1,t},\dots,X_{n,t})$ , macro $V_{t}=f(X_{t})$ . We align samples $(t,t+\ell)$ and compute the score as
第二项检验称为实用判据，涉及对系统宏观信号 $V$ 的预测。它考察宏观信号是否包含超出任一单独部分所含内容的预测信息。给定微观状态 $X_{t}=(X_{1,t},\dots,X_{n,t})$，宏观变量为 $V_{t}=f(X_{t})$。我们对齐样本 $(t,t+\ell)$，并按下式计算得分
$$S_{\mathrm{macro}}(\ell)=I(V_{t};V_{t+\ell})-\sum_{k=1}^{n}I(X_{k,t};V_{t+\ell}).$$
A positive value indicates that the macro’s self-predictability exceeds what the sum of its parts can explain, thus indicating emergent dynamical synergy. We refer to “dynamical” synergy because targets are time‑lagged and do not claim causal directionality beyond the temporal ordering (see Rosas et al., 2020, for a nuanced discussion). It is a coarse, order-agnostic screen sensitive to multi-part synergy (i.e., synergy of any order $\geq 2$ ). However, it is also penalized by redundancy across parts which can make the score negative even when higher-order synergy exists.
正值表明，宏观变量的自我可预测性超出了其各部分之和所能解释的程度，因此表明存在涌现的动态协同作用。我们称之为“动态”协同作用，是因为目标具有时间滞后，而我们并不主张存在超出时间先后顺序的因果方向性（详细讨论见 Rosas et al., 2020）。它是一种不区分阶数的粗略筛查方法，能够检测涉及多个部分的协同作用（即任意阶数 $\geq 2$ 的协同作用）。然而，各部分之间的冗余也会使其得分受到惩罚，因此即使存在高阶协同作用，得分也可能为负。
To explore system dynamics, we implement a coalition test, an extension of the practical criterion to triplets. Let $I_{3}$ be the mutual information between a triplet’s current state and the future macro signal: $I_{3}=I((X_{i,t},X_{j,t},X_{k,t});V_{t+\ell})$ . I.e., how much the three agents jointly predict the macro’s future serves as a measure of the system’s information-processing capacity (Mediano et al., 2022a). High $I_{3}$ indicates that agents’ behaviors are tightly coupled and contain rich information about the emerging collective outcome, a pattern frequently associated with collective intelligence (De Vincenzo et al., 2017; Wicks et al., 2007; Mora and Bialek, 2011; Barnett et al., 2013). Low $I_{3}$ signals weak alignment, resulting in uncoordinated or chaotic collective behavior. Then
为探索系统动态，我们实施一项联盟检验，将实用判据扩展至三元组。令 $I_{3}$ 为三元组当前状态与未来宏观信号之间的互信息：$I_{3}=I((X_{i,t},X_{j,t},X_{k,t});V_{t+\ell})$。也就是说，三个智能体共同预测宏观变量未来状态的程度，可作为系统信息处理能力的度量 (Mediano et al., 2022a)。较高的 $I_{3}$ 表明，智能体的行为紧密耦合，并包含有关正在涌现的集体结果的丰富信息，这种模式往往与集体智能相关 (De Vincenzo et al., 2017; Wicks et al., 2007; Mora and Bialek, 2011; Barnett et al., 2013)。较低的 $I_{3}$ 表明一致性较弱，导致集体行为缺乏协调或呈现混沌。于是
$$G_{3}=I_{3}-\textit{max}(I_{2\{1,2\}},I_{2\{1,3\}},I_{2\{2,3\}})$$
measures the additional predictive information the full triplet provides over the most predictive pair $I_{2}$ (Barrett and Seth, 2011; Mediano et al., 2025, a form of whole-minus-parts metric;). If we find $G_{3}>0$ this means no pair is sufficient to capture information that the triplet contains about the macro signal at $t+\ell$ . We again compute $I_{3}$ and $G_{3}$ for all possible triplets and take the median as group measure. This measure complements the other two by offering a coalition-level test geared towards functional relevance: it helps answers whether the joint information provided by coalitions of agents is actually about the goal. It allows us to localize where macro predictability depends on beyond-pair structure and to rule out “explained by best pair” explanations.
衡量完整三元组相较于预测能力最强的二元组 $I_{2}$ 所提供的额外预测信息 (Barrett and Seth, 2011; Mediano et al., 2025，一种整体减去部分的度量形式;)。如果我们发现 $G_{3}>0$，这意味着任何二元组都不足以捕捉该三元组所包含的、有关 $t+\ell$ 时刻宏观信号的信息。我们再次为所有可能的三元组计算 $I_{3}$ 和 $G_{3}$，并取中位数作为群体度量。该度量通过提供面向功能相关性的联盟层面检验，对另外两项度量形成补充：它有助于回答，智能体联盟所提供的联合信息是否确实与目标有关。它使我们能够定位宏观可预测性在何处依赖于超越成对关系的结构，并排除“由最佳配对解释”的解释。
Estimation Details.估计细节。
As microstates $X_{t}=(X_{1,t},\dots,X_{n,t})$ we use each agent $i$ ’s guess at time $t$ and transform it into the deviation from equal-share contribution to hit the target number: $\textit{devs}_{i,t}=\textit{raw}_{i,t}-\textit{target}/N$ . This minimal transformation removes the trivial level differences imposed by the target and aligns agents on a comparable scale, so that remaining variation reflects coordination (who compensates for whom). Over-/under-contributions are interpretable as the sign of deviations. As macro signal $V_{t}$ we take the group error $\sum{\textit{raw}_{i,t}}-\textit{target}$ (equivalently $\sum{\textit{devs}_{i,t}}$ ).[fn4]
我们将每个智能体 $i$ 在时间 $t$ 的猜测值用作微观状态 $X_{t}=(X_{1,t},\dots,X_{n,t})$，并将其转换为相对于达成目标数值所需的均摊贡献的偏差：$\textit{devs}_{i,t}=\textit{raw}_{i,t}-\textit{target}/N$。这一最小变换消除了目标所造成的平凡水平差异，并将各智能体置于可比较的尺度上，使剩余变异反映协调关系（谁在补偿谁）。贡献过多或不足可通过偏差的符号来解释。我们将群体误差 $\sum{\textit{raw}_{i,t}}-\textit{target}$（等价于 $\sum{\textit{devs}_{i,t}}$）作为宏观信号 $V_{t}$。[fn4]

Falsification Tests.证伪检验。
We perform two different falsification tests of each of the three criteria introduced above. Our primary test assesses significance by comparing the observed value against a null distribution computed on permuted (shuffled) instances of the devs data. We use two surrogates: row‑wise shuffles (to break identities) and column‑wise time‑shift surrogates (preserve individual dynamics while disrupting cross‑agent alignment). Full details in Appendix. We combine $p$ -values across independently simulated groups (independent seeds, independently sampled targets, and no shared state) using Fisher’s method. As a robustness test, we also compute bias-corrected (BC) estimates using time-demeaned data against a block-shuffled null with $\ell=2$ to mitigate autocorrelation confounds. We use both (a) linear regression demeaning and (b) a functional baseline without between-agent synergy (see Appendix for details). These tests provide additional robustness to the $\ell=2$ block size in the shuffled null and auto-correlation concerns.
我们对上述三项准则分别进行两种不同的证伪检验。我们的主要检验通过将观测值与基于 devs 数据的置换（打乱）实例计算出的零分布进行比较，来评估显著性。我们使用两种替代数据：逐行打乱（破坏身份对应关系），以及逐列时间平移替代数据（在保留个体动态的同时，破坏智能体之间的对齐关系）。完整细节见附录。我们使用 Fisher 方法合并各独立模拟群体（随机种子独立、目标独立抽样，且无共享状态）的 $p$ 值。作为稳健性检验，我们还使用去除时间均值后的数据，以 $\ell=2$ 的分块打乱零分布为参照，计算偏差校正（BC）估计值，以减轻自相关造成的混杂影响。我们同时使用（a）线性回归去均值法和（b）不含智能体间协同作用的功能基线（详见附录）。这些检验为打乱零分布中 $\ell=2$ 的分块大小选择以及自相关问题提供了额外的稳健性保障。
Entropy Estimation.熵估计。
Small-sample entropy estimation is often challenging because the true distribution is only partially observed and many outcome categories receive few or even zero counts (“empty bins”). Such finite-sample estimation of information measures is biased upward and can yield spurious positive findings. Bias grows with more bins, dimensionality (e.g., using triplets instead of pairs), and small $N$ (few timesteps). The empirical plug-in estimator replaces true probabilities with sample frequencies and unseen (or under-sampled) outcomes often are assigned zero or very low probabilities (Hausser and Strimmer, 2009). To account for this issue, we follow best practices and take several steps. First, we use the Williams–Beer two‑source PID (Williams and Beer, 2010) with the $I_{min}$ (minimum specific information) redundancy, estimated via plug-in probabilities. Second, we compute the emergence capacity and the coalition tests as order $k=2$ instead of the entire system of $n$ agents. Second, we report $\ell=1$ which is suitable to detect next-step oscillating behavior (see task description). Because trials end at success, episode lengths vary (see plot of rounds to success in Appendix). We report additional sensitivity tests using “early synergy” truncated to a fixed horizon of $H\in\{10,15\}$ rounds to ensure comparability (results in Appendix A.2 and A.12; main analyses use full trajectories). Third, we apply quantile binning (with two bins) to reduce the dimensionality of our data (sensitivity analyses with three bins reported in A.8). Data are encoded as factors with fixed levels ${1,2}$ (and for the joint target as the Cartesian product levels) to avoid dropping empty categories. Fourth, we use bias-corrected entropy estimator with Jeffreys’ prior, which smooths estimates and avoids empty bins by adding $\alpha=\frac{1}{2}$ pseudo-counts in the Dirichlet estimator (Jeffreys, 1946). This reduces systematic inflation and cures zero‑count pathologies. Finally, as a robustness check, we also report results using the Miller–Madow bias-corrected estimator (Miller, 1955), and the MMI redundancy measure (Mediano et al., 2025, MMI typically overestimates redundancy, making synergy estimates more conservative;).
小样本熵估计通常具有挑战性，因为真实分布只能被部分观测到，而且许多结果类别的计数很少，甚至为零（“空箱”）。此类基于有限样本的信息度量估计存在向上偏差，可能产生虚假的阳性结果。分箱数量越多、维度越高（例如使用三元组而非配对）、$N$ 越小（时间步越少），偏差就越大。经验代入估计量以样本频率替代真实概率，而未观测到（或抽样不足）的结果往往被赋予零概率或极低概率（Hausser and Strimmer, 2009）。为应对这一问题，我们遵循最佳实践并采取了若干措施。首先，我们使用 Williams–Beer 双源 PID（Williams and Beer, 2010），采用 $I_{min}$（最小特定信息）冗余度，并通过代入概率进行估计。其次，我们以 $k=2$ 阶计算涌现能力并进行联盟检验，而非针对由 $n$ 个智能体构成的整个系统。其次，我们报告 $\ell=1$ 的结果，这适合检测下一时间步的振荡行为（见任务描述）。由于试验在成功时结束，各回合序列的长度有所不同（见附录中成功所需轮数的图）。我们还报告了额外的敏感性检验，使用截断至固定时域 $H\in\{10,15\}$ 轮的“早期协同作用”，以确保可比性（结果见附录 A.2 和 A.12；主要分析使用完整轨迹）。第三，我们采用分位数分箱（分为两个箱）来降低数据维度（使用三个箱的敏感性分析见 A.8）。数据被编码为具有固定水平 ${1,2}$ 的因子（联合目标则采用笛卡尔积水平），以避免丢弃空类别。第四，我们使用带 Jeffreys 先验的偏差校正熵估计量，通过在 Dirichlet 估计量中加入 $\alpha=\frac{1}{2}$ 的伪计数，平滑估计并避免空箱（Jeffreys, 1946）。这减少了系统性高估，并消除了零计数引发的病态问题。最后，作为稳健性检查，我们还报告了使用 Miller–Madow 偏差校正估计量（Miller, 1955）和 MMI 冗余度量的结果（Mediano et al., 2025，MMI 通常会高估冗余，从而使协同作用的估计更为保守；）。
Test of Agent Differentiation.智能体分化检验。
Our final pillar evaluates agent differentiation via hierarchical (mixed) modeling (Gelman and Hill, 2007), asking whether agents adopt consistent, person-specific patterns. We estimate a sequence of three models for each multi-agent experiment:
我们的最后一项支柱通过层级（混合）建模（Gelman and Hill, 2007）评估智能体分化，考察智能体是否形成一致且具有个体特异性的模式。我们为每项多智能体实验依次估计三个模型：
$$\displaystyle m_{0}\colon \\ \displaystyle\quad y_{i}=\beta_{0}+u_{\text{time}[i]}+\epsilon_{i} \\ \displaystyle m_{1}\colon \\ \displaystyle\quad y_{i}=\beta_{0}+u_{\text{time}[i]}+u_{\text{agent}[i]}+\epsilon_{i} \\ \displaystyle m_{2}\colon \\ \displaystyle\quad y_{i}=\beta_{0}+u_{\text{time}[i]}+u_{\text{agent}[i],0}+u_{\text{agent}[i],\text{time}[i]}+\epsilon_{i}$$
where $y_{i}$ are $\textit{devs}_{i,t}$ , $u_{\text{time}[i]}$ are random intercepts for (continuous) time, $u_{\text{agent}[i]}$ are agent-level random intercepts, and $u_{\text{agent}[i],\text{time}[i]}$ are agent-level random slopes varying by time. Using equal‑share deviations removes target‑level drift and makes agents directly comparable round‑by‑round. The random intercepts for time captures round-to-round shifts due to group‑wide feedback and oscillation. Hence, agent effects reflect relative, identity‑linked behavior rather than shared dynamics. The partial pooling of hierarchical models regularizes per‑agent estimates, improving stability in short time series. We then use likelihood ratio tests to compare the nested hierarchical models. Comparing $m_{0}\rightarrow m_{1}$ asks whether agents differ in their group contribution (some agents guess higher/lower than others), while $m_{1}\rightarrow m_{2}$ tests whether agents vary in how much their contribution changes across rounds (learning rates). A significant $p$ -values (e.g., below conventional 0.05 levels) indicates that the more complex model explains the data significantly better, suggesting the added random effects (e.g., agent-level differences or varying slopes) are meaningful in this group. This offers an interpretable, non-information theoretic test of monotonic learning‑rate heterogeneity. Furthermore, this test does not require discretization, giving a complementary failure mode to the information‑theoretic analyses.
其中，$y_{i}$ 为 $\textit{devs}_{i,t}$，$u_{\text{time}[i]}$ 为（连续）时间的随机截距，$u_{\text{agent}[i]}$ 为智能体层面的随机截距，而 $u_{\text{agent}[i],\text{time}[i]}$ 为随时间变化的智能体层面的随机斜率。使用相对于均等份额的偏差可以消除目标层面的漂移，使各智能体在每一轮中都能直接比较。时间的随机截距捕捉由整个群体的反馈和振荡引起的轮次间变化。因此，智能体效应反映的是与身份相关的相对行为，而非共同的动态变化。分层模型的部分汇聚（partial pooling）对各智能体的估计进行正则化，提高了短时间序列中估计的稳定性。随后，我们使用似然比检验来比较这些嵌套的分层模型。比较 $m_{0}\rightarrow m_{1}$ 旨在检验智能体对群体的贡献是否存在差异（某些智能体的猜测值高于或低于其他智能体），而 $m_{1}\rightarrow m_{2}$ 则检验各智能体的贡献在不同轮次间的变化幅度（学习速率）是否存在差异。显著的 $p$ 值（例如，低于常规的 0.05 水平）表明，更复杂的模型能够显著更好地解释数据，说明新增的随机效应（例如，智能体层面的差异或变化的斜率）在该群体中具有意义。这为单调学习速率的异质性提供了一种可解释的、非信息论的检验方法。此外，该检验不需要离散化，因此其失效模式与信息论分析形成互补。
Together, these four tests of our framework allow us to (a) detect higher‑order structure in multi-agent systems, (b) assess whether it is driven by redundancy or synergy, (c) localize whether it is identity‑locked vs. dynamic alignment, and (d) show whether the higher-order structure is functionally useful for the task. No single measure does all four. Combined with the prompt-level interventions, the framework allow us to test whether interventions (Plain, Persona, ToM) causally increases dynamic synergy, how it affects identity‑locked differentiation, and goal alignment (i.e., increase in $I_{3}$ ).
综合而言，我们框架中的这四项检验使我们能够：(a) 检测多智能体系统中的高阶结构，(b) 评估其由冗余还是协同驱动，(c) 判定其属于身份锁定还是动态对齐，以及 (d) 展示这种高阶结构是否在功能上有助于完成任务。没有任何单一度量能够完成上述全部四项任务。结合提示层面的干预，该框架使我们能够检验干预（Plain、Persona、ToM）是否在因果上增强了动态协同，以及干预如何影响身份锁定的分化和目标对齐（即 $I_{3}$ 的增加）。

4.2 Dynamical Mechanisms4.2 动力学机制
How is this emergence dynamically maintained and where is it located within the groups? We find time-trend demeaned bias-corrected $I_{3}$ around $0$ in the Plain (Wilcoxon $p$ -value 0.974) and Persona condition ( $p=0.846$ ) indicating groups that fail to escape chaotic, uncoordinated states. In contrast, the ToM shows significant positive mutual information ( $p=3.5\times 10^{-14}$ ), collapsing the group’s behavior into a predictable macro-state (Figure A2). The number of groups with significantly positive $I_{3}$ is substantially higher in the ToM condition (Figure 3a), consistent with enhanced collective intelligence. To formalize this transition dynamic, we compute the Total Stability of the system (computed as $I_{3}$ normalized by the entropy of the macro-signal) which serves as a proxy for the Lyapunov stability of the collective state (Haddad and Chellaboina, 2008). We find Total Stability indistinguishable form zero in the Plain (Wilcoxon $p$ -value $0.976$ ) and Persona ( $p$ -value = 0.858) indicating a “gaseous” state where groups fail to settle into a stable attractor. However, the ToM intervention induces a sharp increase in Total Stability ( $p$ -value = $2.9\times 10^{-14}$ ). Theoretically, this suggests the ToM prompt acts as a control parameter, steering the multi-agent system from a chaotic regime into a deep basin of attraction where collective behavior stabilizes and information processing capacity spikes: turning the system from a collection of individuals into a collective group.
这种涌现是如何在动态过程中得以维持的，又位于群体内部的何处？我们发现，在 Plain 条件（Wilcoxon $p$ 值为 0.974）和 Persona 条件（$p=0.846$）下，去除时间趋势均值并经偏差校正后的 $I_{3}$ 均在 $0$ 附近，表明这些群体未能摆脱混沌、无协调的状态。相比之下，ToM 条件呈现出显著的正互信息（$p=3.5\times 10^{-14}$），使群体行为收敛到一个可预测的宏观状态（图 A2）。ToM 条件下，$I_{3}$ 显著为正的群体数量明显更多（图 3a），这与集体智能的增强相一致。为形式化描述这一转变的动力学过程，我们计算系统的总稳定性（Total Stability，计算方式为用宏观信号的熵对 $I_{3}$ 进行归一化），以此作为集体状态的李雅普诺夫稳定性的代理指标（Haddad and Chellaboina, 2008）。我们发现，Plain 条件（Wilcoxon $p$ 值为 $0.976$）和 Persona 条件（$p$ 值 = 0.858）下的总稳定性与零无显著差异，表明群体处于一种无法收敛到稳定吸引子的“气态”。然而，ToM 干预使总稳定性急剧上升（$p$ 值 = $2.9\times 10^{-14}$）。从理论上看，这表明 ToM 提示充当了控制参数，将多智能体系统从混沌状态引导至一个深吸引域，使集体行为趋于稳定，信息处理能力骤增：从而将系统从个体的集合转变为一个集体群体。
Decomposing this stability reveals the specific mechanism of coordination. While the system exhibits emergence capacity and practical emergence, we find the stability in group behavior is supported by pairwise alignment rather than irreducible triplet complexity. Specifically, triadic information gain ( $G_{3}$ ) is around 0 in Persona and ToM (Figure 3c). The Plain conditions shows small positive $G_{3}>0$ (Wilcoxon $p$ -value = $0.026$ ) but given the near-zero Total Stability, this likely reflects transient stochastic correlations and oscillation rather than sustained coordination. In dynamical systems terms, the ToM prompt creates a deep basin of attraction where agents converge on a shared schematic (or Schelling point), making the collective state robust but informationally redundant. This mirrors “Mean Field” coupling in physics: because agents receive only global feedback and cannot observe individual peers, they couple to the aggregate signal rather than forming distinct local bonds. In such an environment, attempting complex higher-order synergy would be fragile; instead, the system converges on the efficient solution of dense pairwise alignment to the Mean Field.
对这种稳定性进行分解，可以揭示协调的具体机制。尽管系统表现出涌现能力和实际涌现，我们发现，群体行为的稳定性由两两对齐所支撑，而非不可约的三元组复杂性。具体而言，Persona 和 ToM 条件下的三元信息增益（$G_{3}$）均在 0 附近（图 3c）。Plain 条件呈现出较小的正值 $G_{3}>0$（Wilcoxon $p$ 值 = $0.026$），但鉴于总稳定性接近零，这很可能反映的是短暂的随机相关和振荡，而非持续的协调。用动力系统的术语来说，ToM 提示创建了一个深吸引域，使智能体收敛于共同的图式（或谢林点），从而使集体状态具有稳健性，但在信息上存在冗余。这与物理学中的“平均场”（Mean Field）耦合相呼应：由于智能体仅接收全局反馈，无法观察其他个体，因此它们与聚合信号耦合，而非形成各异的局部联结。在这样的环境中，尝试复杂的高阶协同会十分脆弱；系统转而收敛于一种高效解，即通过密集的两两对齐与平均场保持一致。

Next, we explore whether agents evolve specialized roles and identities. Are there detectable between-agent differences in either the level of their contribution to the group guess or their temporal evolution (i.e., learning rate)? Using the hierarchical model comparison test we find that agents in many groups have differentiated identities (Figure 3a; a Fisher test for joint $p$ -value evidence is again highly significant). There is substantially more differentiation among agents in the Persona condition, and even more in the ToM condition. The reasoning traces of agents often contain references to the “personal experience” of their assigned persona such as “In my experience (whether it’s corraling cattle or wrangling numbers), starting toward the middle gives the group the best shot to zero in after the first feedback” (see Appendix for additional sample quotes). In Appendix A.10 we provide additional robustness tests ruling out heterogeneous learning rates as the sole source of differentiation and provide additional evidence for stable agent identities that persist over time. That is, in the Plain condition, differentiation results only from idiosyncratic noise and transient drift introduced by probabilistic LLM responses. With Persona, agents gain stable, identity linked behavioral preferences that refine their differentiation. The addition, ToM sharpens role differentiation through feedback loops from mutual predictive modeling by conditioning on the public history (Lewis, 1969; Skyrms, 2010, which functions as a common ground and coordination device;). This converts small persona-induced asymmetries into stable, self-reinforcing roles, creating the basis for strategic complementarity and stable alignment (answering RQ2 and RQ3)
接下来，我们探究智能体是否会演化出专门化的角色和身份。在智能体对群体猜测的贡献水平或其随时间的演化（即学习速率）方面，是否存在可检测的智能体间差异？通过层级模型比较检验，我们发现许多群体中的智能体具有分化的身份（图 3a；用于检验联合 $p$ 值证据的 Fisher 检验同样高度显著）。Persona 条件下智能体之间的分化显著更多，而 ToM 条件下的分化程度更高。智能体的推理轨迹经常提及其被赋予角色的“个人经历”，例如：“根据我的经验（无论是圈赶牛群还是处理数字），从中间开始最有利于群体在获得首次反馈后逐步逼近目标”（更多引文示例见附录）。在附录 A.10 中，我们提供了额外的稳健性检验，排除了学习率异质性是分化唯一来源的可能性，并为智能体具有随时间持续存在的稳定身份提供了更多证据。也就是说，在 Plain 条件下，分化仅源于 LLM 概率性响应所引入的特异性噪声和短暂漂移。在 Persona 条件下，智能体获得了与身份相关联的稳定行为偏好，从而进一步细化了它们之间的分化。此外，ToM 以公开历史为条件，通过相互预测建模产生的反馈循环强化角色分化（Lewis, 1969; Skyrms, 2010；公开历史发挥着共同基础和协调机制的作用）。这将角色设定引发的微小不对称转化为稳定且自我强化的角色，为策略互补性和稳定对齐奠定基础（回答了 RQ2 和 RQ3）。
4.3 Functional Role of Emergence in Group Performance4.3 涌现在群体绩效中的功能作用
What functional differences—if any—does emergence allow? We use regression analysis to explore the joint effect of synergy and redundancy (based on emergence capacity formulation) while carefully controlling for endogeneity of variables measuring emergent properties and performance and controlling for sample selection bias with stabilized inverse probability weighting (see Appendix A.11 for method details). On their own, higher levels of either synergy or redundancy do not predict success. However, when both are present, performance improves significantly (significant interaction with $\beta=0.24$ ; $p=0.014$ ). In marginal-effect terms, redundancy amplifies the benefit of synergy on the log-odds scale by 27%; and vice versa synergy amplifies benefits of redundancy by 27%. This pattern implies that systems benefit when redundant pathways create goal alignment, while synergistic interactions extract novel, non-overlapping information—together enabling higher overall performance. We complement the regression analysis with causal mediation analysis (Imai et al., 2010). While reaching only marginal significant levels the effect is consistent: the ToM treatment causally increases performance indirectly by increasing synergy (ACME $=0.034$ [95%CI: $-0.000-0.07$ ], $p=0.053$ ). This aligns with the interpretation that performance benefits emerge when systems achieve both redundancy (aligned toward a common goal) and synergistic integration (differentiated, complementary roles)—a form of functional and collective complexity (Varley et al., 2023; Luppi et al., 2024; Sterelny, 2007).
涌现能够带来哪些功能差异——如果确实存在这些差异的话？我们使用回归分析来探究协同与冗余的联合效应（基于涌现容量的形式化表述），同时谨慎控制衡量涌现属性与绩效的变量的内生性，并通过稳定化逆概率加权控制样本选择偏差（方法详情见附录 A.11）。单独来看，较高水平的协同或冗余都不能预测成功。然而，当两者同时存在时，绩效会显著提升（交互效应显著，$\beta=0.24$；$p=0.014$）。从边际效应来看，在对数优势尺度上，冗余将协同的益处放大了 27%；反过来，协同也将冗余的益处放大了 27%。这一模式表明，当冗余路径促成目标对齐，而协同交互提取出新颖且互不重叠的信息时，系统会从中受益——两者共同促成更高的整体绩效。我们以因果中介分析补充回归分析（Imai et al., 2010）。尽管仅达到边缘显著水平，该效应仍与上述结果一致：ToM 处理通过提高协同，间接地对绩效产生因果性的提升作用（ACME $=0.034$ [95%CI: $-0.000-0.07$ ]，$p=0.053$）。这与以下解释一致：当系统同时实现冗余（朝向共同目标对齐）和协同整合（分化且互补的角色）时，绩效收益便会涌现——这是一种功能性与集体性复杂性的表现形式（Varley et al., 2023; Luppi et al., 2024; Sterelny, 2007）。
4.4 Experiments using Other Models4.4 使用其他模型的实验
To assess generalizability of our findings, we repeat experiments with four other models: Llama 3.1 8B and 70B, Gemini 2.0 flash, and the reasoning model Qwen3 (see Appendix A.13 for full details). We find that the capacity for emergent synergy is robust across high-capability models: Llama 70B, Gemini, and Qwen3 achieved success rates on par with GPT 4.1 and exhibited strong evidence of emergence using both the emergence capacity and practical criteria. Consistent with our main results, these high-capability models achieved higher success rates, more differentiation, and stronger evidence of emergence in the Persona and ToM conditions compared to the Plain baseline. Conversely, the smaller Llama 8B largely failed to break oscillatory cycles and develop goal-directed cross-agent synergy due to its lower ToM reasoning reasoning capacity (Xiao et al., 2025). Crucially, our analysis of the reasoning model Qwen3 uncovered a distinct structural challenge we term paralysis under coordination ambiguity. Qwen3 agents enter infinite chain-of-thought loops when attempting to reconcile local binary search strategies with noisy group feedback. We provide a detailed taxonomy of these reasoning failure modes—including mutual mental modeling traps—in Appendix A.13, highlighting a critical frontier for research of reasoning models in multi-agent systems (Piedrahita et al., 2025).
为评估研究发现的可推广性，我们使用另外四个模型重复了实验：Llama 3.1 8B 和 70B、Gemini 2.0 flash，以及推理模型 Qwen3（完整详情见附录 A.13）。我们发现，涌现协同的能力在高能力模型中具有稳健性：Llama 70B、Gemini 和 Qwen3 的成功率与 GPT 4.1 相当，而且按照涌现容量和实践判据进行评估时，均表现出强有力的涌现证据。与我们的主要结果一致，相较于 Plain 基线，这些高能力模型在 Persona 和 ToM 条件下取得了更高的成功率、更明显的分化，以及更强的涌现证据。相反，规模较小的 Llama 8B 由于 ToM 推理能力较弱，基本未能打破振荡循环，也未能形成目标导向的跨智能体协同（Xiao et al., 2025）。关键的是，我们对推理模型 Qwen3 的分析揭示了一种独特的结构性挑战，我们将其称为协调歧义下的瘫痪（paralysis under coordination ambiguity）。Qwen3 智能体在尝试协调局部二分搜索策略与带噪声的群体反馈时，会陷入无限的思维链循环。我们在附录 A.13 中详细分类了这些推理失败模式——包括相互心智建模陷阱——凸显了多智能体系统中推理模型研究的一个关键前沿（Piedrahita et al., 2025）。
5 Related Work5 相关工作
Shortly after the release of ChatGPT, the community began exploring multi-agent LLM systems, where multiple LLM agents interact with each other. One early example is Park et al. (2023), who simulated a small population of “individuals” in a The Sims-style environment with friends, houses, and jobs, showing the emergence of rich social behaviors such as coordinating a Valentine’s Day party. This was followed by multi-agent systems for complex tasks such as software development (Chen et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2023), healthcare (Li et al., 2024a), and other domains (Subramaniam et al., 2025; Ashery et al., 2025). Federated systems or “society of models” also gained attention (Juneja et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024b). These systems often achieve significant performance gains over single-agent baselines (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024b; Tao et al., 2024).
ChatGPT 发布后不久，研究界便开始探索多智能体 LLM 系统，其中多个 LLM 智能体彼此交互。一个早期例子是 Park et al. (2023) 的研究，他们在一个具有朋友、住房和工作等要素的《模拟人生》风格环境中，模拟了一个由“个体”组成的小型群体，展示了丰富社会行为的涌现，例如协调举办情人节派对。此后，出现了用于软件开发 (Chen et al., 2023; Hong et al., 2024; Qian et al., 2023)、医疗保健 (Li et al., 2024a) 及其他领域 (Subramaniam et al., 2025; Ashery et al., 2025) 等复杂任务的多智能体系统。联邦式系统或“模型社会”也受到了关注 (Juneja et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024b)。与单智能体基线相比，这些系统通常能够显著提升性能 (Wu et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2023; Li et al., 2024b; Tao et al., 2024)。
A recurring explanation for such gains invokes “greater-than-the-sum-of-its-parts” effects (Chen et al., 2023, p.1), often attributed to division of labor among differentiated agents (Subramaniam et al., 2025). Yet while this work is “inspired by human group dynamics” (Chen et al., 2023, p.1), it rarely evaluates whether the same principles that underlie effective (and ineffective) human groups also emerge in multi-agent systems. Two gaps stand out. First, human groups often coordinate by developing role specialization (Goldstone et al., 2024). Developers of multi-agent systems often intuitively induce such role specialization (Qian et al., 2023, such as “programmer”, “tester”, and “CEO”;) yet have not systematically evaluated the impact on emergent shared cognition beyond win-rate comparisons. Second, groups only outperform individuals if their members contribute different and complementary information to the group task (Theiner, 2013; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010; Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Nickerson and Zenger, 2004). Effective groups balance complementarity—members contributing distinct information—with redundancy, the alignment on shared goals (Theiner, 2013; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010; Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Luppi et al., 2024; Tollefsen et al., 2013). Too much of either undermines performance. Unlike win-rate–centric evaluations—and tests of whether agents in multi-agent systems can sustain cooperation per se (Piedrahita et al., 2025; Piatti et al., 2024)—we propose falsification tests and null modeling, thus extending multi-agent frameworks with formal theory. While these principles likely apply to LLM-based collectives given the universality of the integration-segregation tradeoff (Tononi et al., 1994), it remains unclear whether agents develop differentiated roles, whether such roles complement each other, how to steer it with prompts, and what role ToM capacity of models plays for collaboration (Westby and Riedl, 2023; Kleiman-Weiner et al., 2025; Riedl and Weidmann, 2025).
对这些性能提升的一种常见解释是“整体大于部分之和”的效应 (Chen et al., 2023, p.1)，这种效应通常被归因于具有差异化角色的智能体之间的分工 (Subramaniam et al., 2025)。然而，尽管这类研究“受到人类群体动力学的启发” (Chen et al., 2023, p.1)，却很少评估支撑人类群体有效（及无效）运作的相同原则是否也会在多智能体系统中涌现。其中有两个突出的研究空白。首先，人类群体往往通过形成角色专业化来实现协调 (Goldstone et al., 2024)。多智能体系统的开发者往往凭直觉引入这种角色专业化 (Qian et al., 2023, 如“程序员”“测试员”和“CEO”；)，但除了比较胜率之外，尚未系统评估其对涌现的共享认知的影响。其次，只有当成员为群体任务提供不同且互补的信息时，群体的表现才会优于个体 (Theiner, 2013; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010; Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Nickerson and Zenger, 2004)。有效的群体会在互补性——即成员贡献不同的信息——与冗余性——即围绕共同目标保持一致——之间取得平衡 (Theiner, 2013; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010; Fazelpour and De-Arteaga, 2022; Luppi et al., 2024; Tollefsen et al., 2013)。任何一方过多都会损害表现。不同于以胜率为中心的评估，以及检验多智能体系统中的智能体本身能否持续合作的测试 (Piedrahita et al., 2025; Piatti et al., 2024)，我们提出了证伪检验与零模型建模，从而以形式化理论拓展多智能体框架。鉴于整合与分离之间的权衡具有普遍性 (Tononi et al., 1994)，这些原则很可能也适用于基于 LLM 的集体，但智能体是否会形成差异化角色、这些角色是否相互补充、如何通过提示加以引导，以及模型的心智理论（ToM）能力在协作中发挥什么作用，仍不清楚 (Westby and Riedl, 2023; Kleiman-Weiner et al., 2025; Riedl and Weidmann, 2025)。
6 Conclusion6 结论
Collectives of LLM agents are emerging as a powerful new paradigm, capable of tackling tasks that exceed the reach of any single model. Yet raw capability alone may not determine their effectiveness. Effective multi-agent systems depend on acting as integrated, cohesive units as opposed to loose aggregations of individual agents. In collective intelligence, this distinction often separates groups that merely average their parts from those that achieve true synergy (Riedl et al., 2021). This paper asks whether such coordination dynamics also matter for LLM collectives, what functional benefits they have, and whether we can design prompts, roles, or reasoning structures that foster such integrated yet synergistic behavior. The framework we developed connects human group cognition theory to LLM multi-agent systems, offering a novel conceptual bridge. Methodologically, we demonstrate how to operationalize group-level synergy in AI collectives using quantitative information-theoretic measures. We also provide specific design principles for creating productive LLM collectives that are aligned on “shared goals,” which can inform multi-agent orchestration tools, cooperative AI, and mixed human–AI teamwork. We further ground our information-theoretic framework in dynamical systems and game theory, showing that theory-of-mind interventions act as a control parameter that shifts multi-agent interactions from disordered to stable regimes with goal alignment. This work extends ToM research beyond standard false-belief tests to realistic collaborative tasks (Shapira et al., 2024; Shapira et al., 2023) and points to ToM-related failure modes in reasoning models. Those insights will help us understand when multiple LLMs working together will be beneficial, why that is, and inform their design.
LLM 智能体集体正成为一种强大的新范式，能够处理超出任何单一模型能力范围的任务。然而，仅凭原始能力或许无法决定其有效性。有效的多智能体系统需要作为整合且凝聚的单元行动，而非个体智能体的松散集合。在集体智能中，这一区别往往将仅仅达到各组成部分平均水平的群体与实现真正协同的群体区分开来 (Riedl et al., 2021)。本文探讨这种协调动态是否也对 LLM 集体具有重要意义、它们带来哪些功能性益处，以及我们能否设计提示、角色或推理结构，以促进这种既整合又具有协同效应的行为。我们开发的框架将人类群体认知理论与 LLM 多智能体系统联系起来，提供了一座新的概念桥梁。在方法上，我们展示了如何使用定量的信息论度量，将 AI 集体中群体层面的协同效应操作化。我们还提出了具体的设计原则，用于构建围绕“共同目标”保持一致且富有成效的 LLM 集体，这些原则可为多智能体编排工具、合作型 AI 以及人类与 AI 混合团队协作提供指导。我们进一步将信息论框架奠基于动力系统与博弈论，表明心智理论干预可作为一个控制参数，使多智能体交互从无序状态转变为目标一致的稳定状态。这项工作将 ToM 研究从标准的错误信念测试拓展到现实的协作任务 (Shapira et al., 2024; Shapira et al., 2023)，并指出了推理模型中与 ToM 相关的失效模式。这些见解将帮助我们理解多个 LLM 在何种情况下协同工作会带来益处、其原因是什么，并为其设计提供指导。
We note that evidence of higher-order synergy should not be interpreted as implying sophisticated cognition or consciousness. As conceptualized here, synergy is a structural property of part-whole relationships within multi-agent interactions. Such higher-order structures are known to arise in simple systems, including agents governed by reinforcement learning (Fulker et al., 2024) or via basic nonlinearities in contagion processes (Iacopini et al., 2019; Lee et al., 2025). Without attributing human-like cognition to the agents, the patterns of interaction we observe mirror well-established principles of collective intelligence in human groups (Riedl et al., 2021; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010): effective multi-agent performance requires both alignment on shared objectives and complementary contributions across members.
我们指出，不应将高阶协同效应的证据解读为意味着存在复杂认知或意识。按照本文的概念界定，协同效应是多智能体交互中部分与整体关系的一种结构属性。已有研究表明，这类高阶结构会出现在简单系统中，包括由强化学习支配的智能体 (Fulker et al., 2024)，也可由传播过程中的基本非线性机制产生 (Iacopini et al., 2019; Lee et al., 2025)。在不将类似人类的认知能力归于智能体的前提下，我们观察到的交互模式体现了人类群体集体智能中已得到充分确立的原则（Riedl et al., 2021; DeChurch and Mesmer-Magnus, 2010）：多智能体要实现有效表现，既需要围绕共同目标达成一致，也需要成员之间作出互补性贡献。
Limitations and Future Work.局限性与未来工作。
This work focuses on developing a framework, characterizing emergence, and localizing it. While we explore the degree of goal alignment via a performance-related macro signal and performance effects of “early synergy”, the analysis linking synergy and redundancy to performance is challenging because synergy and redundancy are often co-dependent and emerge alongside performance. Future work should aim to more directly connect measures of synergy with performance. Despite a significant number of robustness tests, estimating entropy and establishing synergy is difficult. Furthermore, this work established results only using a single task—albeit one that is uniquely suited to studying synergy given how closely it mirrors ideal case of complementary behavior in a minimalist setting. More work will be necessary to convincingly establish when and how multi-agent LLM systems develop emergent synergy across tasks and measures. Given the small-data setting some of our information theoretic measures are limited to computing dynamic emergence on order $k=2$ which is bound to miss synergy of a higher order. Finally, this work points to the importance of ToM. LLMs capacity to act in a ToM-like manner appears crucial to achieve functional alignment in multi-agent systems. This should provide additional motivation for research on ToM in LLMs.
本研究着重于开发一个框架、刻画涌现并确定其发生位置。尽管我们通过一个与性能相关的宏观信号探索了目标对齐的程度，并考察了“早期协同”的性能效应，但分析协同与冗余同性能之间的联系仍然具有挑战性，因为协同与冗余往往相互依赖，并与性能表现一同涌现。未来工作应致力于更直接地将协同度量与性能联系起来。尽管进行了大量稳健性检验，估计熵并确证协同的存在仍然很困难。此外，本研究仅使用单一任务得出了结果，尽管该任务在极简情境下与互补行为的理想情形高度吻合，因此特别适合用于研究协同。要令人信服地确定多智能体 LLM 系统何时以及如何在不同任务和度量下产生涌现协同，还需要开展更多研究。鉴于数据量较小，我们的部分信息论度量只能计算 $k=2$ 阶的动态涌现，这必然会遗漏更高阶的协同。最后，本研究表明了心智理论（ToM）的重要性。LLM 以类似 ToM 的方式行事的能力，似乎对于在多智能体系统中实现功能性对齐至关重要。这应能为 LLM 中的 ToM 研究提供进一步的动力。

A.4 Falsification Tests: Additional Method DetailsA.4 证伪检验：补充方法细节
We perform a falsification test of each of the three criteria introduced above in two different ways. In our robustness test, we remove time trends from the equal-share deviations via agent-level linear regression residualization to mitigate autocorrelation confounds. We then generate a null distribution of our statistics computed on column-wise block shuffled data (we use $B=200$ random shuffles for main analyses and confirm results using $B=1,000$ on the emergence criteria tests). We permute time indices jointly across agents in blocks of length $\ell$ , preserving within-block identity alignment while disrupting cross-agent temporal coupling beyond short time frames. Finally, we compute the bias-corrected estimate as the observed value minus the median of the null. We then perform a Wilcoxon signed‑rank tests to test whether the bias‑corrected values exceed zero ( $p(H1>0)$ . In some of our sensitivity tests we also perform a full within-row shuffle completely permuting guesses within each trial across agents (breaking agent identities), while preserving row constraints. This role differentiation or identity-specific coordination, including time-trend effects tests identity-locked differentiation beyond task induced temporal dynamics. Comparison between results using the full row-shuffle vs. the column-block-shuffle separates signs of identity‑locked (specialized roles) from dynamic alignment (mutual adaptation, turn‑taking, oscillation). In addition to regression residualizing we also implement a functional baseline (see Appendix).
我们以两种不同的方式，对上述三个判据分别进行证伪检验。在稳健性检验中，我们通过智能体层面的线性回归残差化，去除相对于均等份额的偏差中的时间趋势，以减轻自相关造成的混杂影响。随后，我们基于按列分块打乱的数据计算统计量，并生成这些统计量的零分布（主要分析采用 $B=200$ 次随机打乱，并在涌现判据检验中使用 $B=1,000$ 次打乱来确认结果）。我们以长度为 $\ell$ 的块为单位，对所有智能体的时间索引进行联合置换，在保留块内身份对齐的同时，破坏超出短时间范围的智能体间时间耦合。最后，我们以观测值减去零分布的中位数，得到偏差校正后的估计值。随后，我们进行 Wilcoxon 符号秩检验，以检验偏差校正后的值是否大于零（$p(H1>0)$。在部分敏感性检验中，我们还进行了完整的行内打乱，即在保留行约束的同时，将每次试验中各智能体的猜测完全置换（破坏智能体身份）。这种角色分化或身份特异性协调检验涵盖时间趋势效应，用于检验超出任务所诱发的时间动态的、与身份绑定的分化。比较完整行打乱与按列分块打乱所得的结果，可以区分与身份绑定的迹象（专门化角色）和动态对齐的迹象（相互适应、轮流行动、振荡）。除回归残差化外，我们还实现了一种功能性基线（见附录）。

A.10 Are Identities Persistent?A.10 身份是否具有持续性？
If some agents learn faster than others, even without persistent “roles,” synergy can emerge simply because differentiated trajectories create complementarity across time. As indeed the mixed model test shows that 32% of groups have significant slopes provides strong evidence for the presence of heterogeneous learning rates. That is, heterogeneous learning rates could be a confound explaining some of our observed synergy effects. To rule out this alternative explanation we explore if agents have persistent “identities.” We find the block-shuffle nulls show more significant $G_{3}$ than the full row shuffle. This suggests that synergy depends on temporal continuity of identities (not just on different learning rates at each timepoint). Furthermore, the hierarchical model test shows significant variance even without varying slopes, indicating stable role-like differences beyond learning rates. Finally, our analyses removing time trends and the functional null-model also remove learning rate slopes (Table A1). The functional null-model in particular does this in a non-linear way that is directly tied to our task. In summary, we find both heterogeneous learning rates and stable identity-linked differentiation. Heterogeneous learning rates alone cannot explain the observed emergent synergy that persist across time (providing additional insights regarding RQ2).
如果某些智能体比其他智能体学习得更快，那么即使不存在持续的“角色”，差异化的演化轨迹也能仅凭其在时间上形成的互补性而产生协同。事实上，混合模型检验表明，32% 的群组具有显著的斜率，这为学习速率存在异质性提供了有力证据。也就是说，学习速率的异质性可能是一个混杂因素，能够解释我们观察到的部分协同效应。为排除这一替代解释，我们探究智能体是否具有持续的“身份”。我们发现，与完整行打乱相比，分块打乱零模型显示出更显著的 $G_{3}$。这表明，协同依赖于身份在时间上的连续性（而不仅仅是每个时间点上学习速率的差异）。此外，层次模型检验表明，即使不允许斜率变化，方差仍然显著，说明除了学习速率之外，还存在稳定的、类似角色的差异。最后，我们去除时间趋势的分析以及功能性零模型也消除了学习速率斜率（表 A1）。尤其是，功能性零模型以一种与我们的任务直接相关的非线性方式实现了这一点。总之，我们既发现了学习速率的异质性，也发现了与身份相关的稳定分化。仅凭学习速率的异质性，无法解释所观察到的随时间持续存在的涌现协同（这为 RQ2 提供了进一步的见解）。
Together, the results suggest a nuanced picture of emergence in multi-agent systems. Persona and ToM produce agents with distinct, stable identities—moving agents on the differentiated-undifferentiated axis in Figure 1a. This differentiation shapes the space in which other agents can adapt, and specialization by one agent constrains the (useful) actions of the others (Tollefsen et al., 2013, agents mutually constrain each other; cf.)—shifts on the interdependent-complementary axis. When combined with ToM, mutual adaptation both strengthens agent differentiation (creating the foundation for possible synergistic complementarity) while also increasing mutual alignment on shared goals (the target signal)—shifts on the integration axis. Differentiation enables synergy, but without redundancy and integration, synergy alone does not translate into higher collective performance. Agents in the ToM condition develop specialized, complementary roles while achieving stronger shared goal alignment: they are simultaneously more differentiated yet also more integrated along highly overlapping task-relevant information which the capacity for synergy can now exploit in productive ways.
综合来看，这些结果表明，多智能体系统中的涌现呈现出复杂而细致的图景。Persona 和 ToM 使智能体形成独特且稳定的身份，从而使智能体沿图 1a 中的分化—未分化轴移动。这种分化塑造了其他智能体能够适应的空间，而一个智能体的专业化会约束其他智能体的（有用）行动（参见 Tollefsen et al., 2013，智能体相互约束），从而使其沿相互依赖—互补轴移动。与 ToM 结合时，相互适应既会强化智能体的分化（为可能产生的协同互补性奠定基础），也会增强它们在共同目标（目标信号）上的相互对齐，从而使其沿整合轴移动。分化使协同成为可能，但如果缺乏冗余和整合，单凭协同并不能转化为更高的集体绩效。ToM 条件下的智能体在形成专业化、互补角色的同时，也实现了更强的共同目标对齐：它们既更加分化，又围绕高度重叠的任务相关信息实现了更高程度的整合，而协同能力如今可以有效利用这些信息。
A.11 Additional Details for Performance AnalysisA.11 绩效分析的补充细节
What does synergy enable? In particular, does synergy affect performance? Connecting emergent properties of synergy and redundancy to performance is a challenging task for two main reasons. First, redundancy and synergy sit at opposite ends of the informational spectrum of correlations. Systems constrained by limited resources (such as energy or bandwidth) cannot maximize both simultaneously. The “informational budget” of correlations can be spent on shared overlap (redundancy) or on higher-order joint structures (synergy), but not both simultaneously (Williams and Beer, 2010; Varley et al., 2023; Luppi et al., 2024). Second, emergent properties are dichromatic—they require time to evolve (Bedau, 2002)—making them endogenous with run-length of the simulation. Namely, simulations in which the multi-agent system performs worse and takes longer to solve the task (or fails to solve the task altogether) provide them with more time to evolve sophisticated group coordination patterns; while successful runs terminate early, giving collectives less time to evolve emergent properties. This complicates meaningful analyses as it can make it appear that higher synergy is correlated with failure. In technical terms: time is an endogenous variable and confounds both performance and observed values of synergy and redundancy.
协同能够带来什么？具体而言，协同是否影响绩效？将协同和冗余的涌现属性与绩效联系起来是一项具有挑战性的任务，主要有两个原因。首先，冗余和协同位于相关性的信息谱的两端。受有限资源（如能量或带宽）约束的系统无法同时将二者最大化。相关性的“信息预算”可以用于共享的重叠信息（冗余），也可以用于高阶联合结构（协同），但不能同时用于两者（Williams and Beer, 2010; Varley et al., 2023; Luppi et al., 2024）。其次，涌现属性具有双色性——它们需要时间才能演化（Bedau, 2002）——这使它们与模拟的运行时长存在内生关联。也就是说，在多智能体系统表现较差、需要更长时间才能解决任务（或完全无法解决任务）的模拟中，智能体有更多时间演化出复杂的群体协调模式；而成功的运行会提前终止，使集体演化涌现属性的时间更少。这增加了开展有意义分析的难度，因为它可能造成更高的协同与失败相关的表象。用技术术语来说，时间是一个内生变量，会同时混杂绩效以及协同和冗余的观测值。
We address these issues as follows. First, we focus our analysis on the interaction of synergy and redundancy, rather than each variable individually to capture the tradeoff between the two. Second, we compute both emergent properties (synergy and redundancy) only on early rounds (in our case, 10 time steps). We chose 10 rounds as a good tradeoff between giving the multi-agent systems enough time to evolve, while limiting the amount of runs that have already completed. While this reduces our ability to detect the full emergent potential of the multi-agent system (since agents have less time to evolve) it creates a clean apples-to-apples comparison between measured values (they are all based on exactly 10 time steps). In that sense, our analyses likely underestimate the full effect of synergy because we only capture the early onset of synergy. Focusing on only the first 10 time steps, leads to additional complications, however. About 23% of experiments finish early and do not make it till round ten (roughly balanced across treatments with 22% in Plain, 25% in Persona, and 20% in ToM). Simply removing these observations would bias our results since this censoring occurs post-treatment assignment: removing those observations would introduce sampling bias. Instead, we take these observations as censored (i.e., we cannot compute the early synergy measure) and control for this censoring using stabilized inverse probability weighting (Wooldridge, 2002, IPW;). That is, in a separate modeling step we predict (via logistic regression and only task difficulty as exogenous predictor variables that is independent of the treatment) the likelihood of observing (early) synergy and redundancy.[fn5] We then take the inverse of the predicted likelihood as weight in the causal mediation analysis. This adjust observations in the primary analysis with the inverse of the probability of making such observations.
我们采用以下方式处理这些问题。首先，我们将分析重点放在协同与冗余的交互作用上，而非单独分析每个变量，以捕捉两者之间的权衡。其次，我们仅使用早期轮次（在我们的研究中为 10 个时间步）来计算这两种涌现属性（协同和冗余）。我们选择 10 轮，是为了在给予多智能体系统足够的演化时间与限制已经结束的运行数量之间取得良好平衡。虽然这降低了我们检测多智能体系统全部涌现潜力的能力（因为智能体的演化时间更少），但它使测量值之间能够进行清晰、同等条件下的比较（这些值均基于恰好 10 个时间步）。从这个意义上说，我们的分析很可能低估了协同作用的全部效应，因为我们仅捕捉到了协同作用的早期形成阶段。然而，仅关注前 10 个时间步也带来了额外的复杂性。约 23% 的实验提前结束，未能持续到第十轮（各处理条件下的比例大致均衡，Plain 为 22%，Persona 为 25%，ToM 为 20%）。直接删除这些观测值会使结果产生偏差，因为这种删失发生在处理分配之后：删除这些观测值会引入抽样偏差。因此，我们将这些观测值视为删失数据（即无法计算早期协同作用指标），并使用稳定化逆概率加权（stabilized inverse probability weighting）来控制这种删失（Wooldridge, 2002, IPW;）。也就是说，在一个单独的建模步骤中，我们预测观察到（早期）协同与冗余的概率（通过逻辑回归，且仅将独立于处理的任务难度作为外生预测变量）。[fn5] 随后，我们将预测概率的倒数作为因果中介分析中的权重。这相当于使用获得相应观测值的概率的倒数，对主要分析中的观测值进行调整。
Footnote: 5 As measures for task difficulty we use (a) how far groups target number was from the mid point (smaller numbers requiring more steps of the binary search process); and (b) the modulo remainder of the target number and the group size (target numbers that are closer to divisibility by the group size are easier to guess by pure chance).

As a reasoning model, Qwen3 exhibited persistent looping behavior during its chain-of-thought process—a known failure mode of reasoning models (Pipis et al., 2025; Cemri et al., 2025; Shapira et al., 2026). In our setting, we identify three specific patterns of oscillatory reasoning that arise reliably as soon as group feedback becomes inconsistent with the individual binary search strategy.
作为推理模型，Qwen3 在思维链过程中表现出持续的循环行为，这是推理模型的一种已知失效模式（Pipis et al., 2025; Cemri et al., 2025; Shapira et al., 2026）。这些模型尤其容易陷入自我反思和演绎推理陷阱（Baek and Tegmark, 2025），以及循环推理（Duan et al., 2026）。在我们的实验设置中，我们识别出三种具体的振荡式推理模式：只要群体反馈与个体的二分搜索策略不一致，这些模式就会稳定出现。
Adjacent Integers.相邻整数。
When group feedback places an agent between two adjacent integers, the agent fails to recognize that the ambiguity of that feedback is driven by the unobservability of other agents’ guesses. A typical reasoning pattern was: “8 was too low but 9 was too high, but guesses need to be integers, let me re-read the instructions …”). The agent interprets the goal literally and realizes they are mutually incompatible, not realizing that changes in the guesses of other agents explain the seeming inconsistent feedback.
当群体反馈将某个智能体的搜索范围限定在两个相邻整数之间时，该智能体无法认识到，这种反馈的歧义源于其无法观测其他智能体的猜测。一种典型的推理模式是：“8 太小了，但 9 又太大了，可猜测必须是整数，让我重新读一下说明……”）。智能体按字面理解目标，并意识到这些目标彼此不相容，却没有意识到，其他智能体猜测值的变化可以解释看似不一致的反馈。
Dual Strategies.双重策略。
Agents fail to reconcile the strategy inconsistency of both reasoning about the task (applying binary search strategy) while also coordinating with other agents. A typical reasoning pattern was: “I should guess the midpoint 5 … but I also need to adjust to others … so maybe I should guess higher … but that’s not the midpoint … let me re-read the instructions …”.
智能体既要对任务进行推理（应用二分搜索策略），又要与其他智能体协调，却无法调和这两者之间的策略不一致。一种典型的推理模式是：“我应该猜中点 5……但我也需要根据其他人的情况调整……所以也许我应该猜得更高一些……但那就不是中点了……让我再读一遍指令……”
Mutual Mental Modeling.相互心智建模。
We observe four different categories of mutual mental modeling traps, each representing a distinct cognitive stance.
我们观察到四类不同的相互心智建模陷阱，每一类都代表一种独特的认知立场。
Uncertainty Acknowledgment: “Perhaps the other players guess is changing. But no information.” Agents recognize that other agents may be changing their behavior and admit epistemic helplessness. This shows awareness of others, without actual modeling.
承认不确定性：“也许其他玩家的猜测正在变化。但没有相关信息。”智能体认识到其他智能体可能正在改变行为，并承认自己在认知上无能为力。这表明它们意识到了其他智能体的存在，却没有进行实际建模。
Causal Attribution Under Ambiguity: “Perhaps the mystery number is for the sum, and when you guess 10, the sum was too high, but maybe the other guesses changed.” Here, the agent is using mental modeling to interpret the group feedback. This shows mutual modeling as a diagnostic tool to explain the inconsistency.
模糊情境下的因果归因：“也许神秘数字指的是总和，而当你猜 10 时，总和过高了，但也可能是其他人的猜测发生了变化。”在这里，智能体正利用心智建模来解释群体反馈。这表明，相互建模可以作为解释这种不一致的诊断工具。
Strategic Simplification: “Given the instruction to use binary search, I think we must assume others are constant.” This is a meta-level decision to collapse the modeling problem by deliberately treating other agents as static to make the task tractable. In some sense, this is the most sophisticated move as it recognizes that mutual modeling creates infinite regress and chooses a pragmatic simplification.
策略性简化：“既然指令要求使用二分搜索，我认为我们必须假定其他人的猜测保持不变。”这是一种元层面的决策：通过有意将其他智能体视为静态不变，来简化建模问题，使任务变得可处理。从某种意义上说，这是最为高明的做法，因为它认识到相互建模会导致无穷倒退，并选择了一种务实的简化方式。
Concrete Joint-State Estimation: “I should guess 9, and others 8 — sum 17. So guess 9. Or if others are at 8, I should guess 9. But I don’t know.” This is an actual attempt to simulate the joint outcome by assigning specific values to other agents’ guesses yet it dissolves into uncertainty.

A.14 Specific Persona EffectsA.14 特定角色设定的影响
Both the Persona and ToM conditions rely on personas. Do the specific personas—or combinations of personas—present in a multi-agent system affect performance or the emergence of higher-order structures? We explore these questions with several test. Recall that our experiments are based on 20 different personas that are generated once globally, and then reused throughout the experiments by sampling 10 personas from the library (without replacement). First, we perform joint F-tests (based on linear regression) of the null hypothesis that all persona dummies have no effect on the outcome (i.e., no specific persona substantially affects group outcomes and all coefficients equal zero). Rejecting the null would indicate that at least one of the persona indicators is significantly associated with the outcome variable. We find no evidence that the presence of specific personas effect performance ( $F=1.217$ ; $p=0.239$ ), number of rounds to success ( $F=1.356$ ; $p=0.144$ ), PID synergy ( $F=0.839$ ; $p=0.66$ ), or PID redundancy ( $F=0.596$ ; $p=0.910$ ). These results suggest that what matters is the presence of personas overall—markers that allow the LLM agents to center their differentiation on—rather than who those personas are specifically.
Persona 和 ToM 条件都依赖角色设定。多智能体系统中的特定角色设定或角色设定组合，是否会影响表现或高阶结构的涌现？我们通过若干检验来探究这些问题。回顾一下，我们的实验基于 20 种不同的角色设定，这些设定在整个实验开始前统一生成一次，随后通过从角色设定库中抽取 10 种角色设定（不放回抽样），在各项实验中重复使用。首先，我们进行联合 F 检验（基于线性回归），检验所有角色设定虚拟变量均不影响结果这一原假设（即没有任何特定角色设定会显著影响群体结果，且所有系数均等于零）。拒绝原假设将表明，至少有一个角色设定指示变量与结果变量显著相关。我们未发现证据表明，特定角色设定的存在会影响表现（$F=1.217$；$p=0.239$）、成功所需的轮数（$F=1.356$；$p=0.144$）、PID 协同信息（$F=0.839$；$p=0.66$）或 PID 冗余信息（$F=0.596$；$p=0.910$）。这些结果表明，重要的是角色设定整体上的存在，即为 LLM 智能体提供据以形成差异的标记，而非这些角色设定具体是什么。
Next, we used an LMRA (Eloundou et al., 2024; Riedl and Weidmann, 2025) to judge the pairwise similarity of the personas in terms of their personality and background (we used gpt-oss-120b to assess similarity on a 0-10 scale). Even though judgments are subjective, the input they judge is standardized, controlled, information-rich, and deliberately designed to reveal deep-level characteristics in line with personas used in vignette studies (Aguinis and Bradley, 2014). This allows the LMRA to integrate many deep-level characteristics in a controlled and comparable format. From these, we compute group-level diversity as one minus the mean pairwise similarity rating, following the dispersion-based operationalization of separation diversity in Harrison and Klein (2007) (mean diversity: 5.457; SD = 0.385). In the last step, we explore whether group diversity is systematically related with key group-level outcomes using linear regression. We find no effect of diversity on performance ( $\beta=0.067$ ; $p=0.403$ ), number of rounds to success ( $\beta=-0.195$ ; $p=0.877$ ), PID synergy ( $\beta=-0.000$ ; $p=0.856$ ), or PID redundancy ( $\beta=0.003$ ; $p=0.113$ ). These results again suggest that there are no strong effects for specific combinations of personas.
接下来，我们使用 LMRA（Eloundou et al., 2024; Riedl and Weidmann, 2025）评判角色设定在个性和背景方面的两两相似度（我们使用 gpt-oss-120b 在 0-10 的量表上评估相似度）。尽管评判具有主观性，但其评判的输入经过标准化和控制，信息丰富，并且经过专门设计，以揭示深层特征，这与情境短文研究中使用的角色设定一致（Aguinis and Bradley, 2014）。这使 LMRA 能够以受控且可比较的形式整合多种深层特征。基于这些评分，我们遵循 Harrison and Klein (2007) 中以离散程度衡量分离型多样性的操作化方法，将群体层面的多样性计算为一减去两两相似度评分的均值（平均多样性：5.457；SD = 0.385）。最后，我们使用线性回归，探究群体多样性是否与关键的群体层面结果存在系统性关联。我们未发现多样性对表现（$\beta=0.067$；$p=0.403$）、成功所需的轮数（$\beta=-0.195$；$p=0.877$）、PID 协同信息（$\beta=-0.000$；$p=0.856$）或 PID 冗余信息（$\beta=0.003$；$p=0.113$）有影响。这些结果再次表明，特定的角色设定组合并不存在很强的影响。
