去中心化部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（Dec-POMDP）是一种用于多智能体协调与决策的模型。 The decentralized partially observable Markov decision process (Dec-POMDP) is a model for coordination and decision-making among multiple agents. 它是一种概率模型，能够考虑结果、传感器和通信中的不确定性（即通信成本高、有延迟、有噪声或不存在通信的情况）。 It is a probabilistic model that can consider uncertainty in outcomes, sensors and communication (i.e., costly, delayed, noisy or nonexistent communication).
它是马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）和部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（POMDP）在多个去中心化智能体情形下的推广。 It is a generalization of a Markov decision process (MDP) and a partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP) to consider multiple decentralized agents.
