大语言模型中的相关性错误Correlated Errors in Large Language Models
摘要Abstract
人们通常认为，训练数据、架构和提供商的多样性能够缓解大语言模型（LLM）的同质化。 Diversity in training data, architecture, and providers is assumed to mitigate homogeneity in LLMs. 然而，对于不同 LLM 是否存在实质性差异，我们缺乏实证证据。 However, we lack empirical evidence on whether different LLMs differ meaningfully. 我们利用两个热门排行榜和一项简历筛选任务，对总计超过 350 个 LLM 开展了大规模实证评估。 We conduct a large-scale empirical evaluation on over 350 LLMs overall, using two popular leaderboards and a resume-screening task. 我们发现，模型错误之间存在显著的相关性——在其中一个排行榜数据集上，当两个模型都出错时，它们有 60% 的情况会给出相同答案。 We find substantial correlation in model errors—on one leaderboard dataset, models agree 60% of the time when both models err. 我们识别出了导致模型相关性的因素，包括采用相同架构和来自相同提供商。 We identify factors driving model correlation, including shared architectures and providers. 然而，关键的是，规模更大、准确率更高的模型即使采用不同架构且来自不同提供商，其错误也具有高度相关性。 Crucially, however, larger and more accurate models have highly correlated errors, even with distinct architectures and providers. 最后，我们展示了相关性在两项下游任务中的影响：以 LLM 为评判者的评估和招聘——后者印证了有关算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）的理论预测。 Finally, we show the effects of correlation in two downstream tasks: LLM-as-judge evaluation and hiring—the latter reflecting theoretical predictions regarding algorithmic monoculture.

(1) LLM 错误的相关性有多强？(1) How correlated are LLM errors?
为了评估选择题上的相关性，我们在两个模型均回答错误的条件下，评估模型对的答案一致率。 To assess correlation on multiple choice questions, we evaluate the agreement rate of model pairs—conditional on both models being wrong. 对于简历评估，我们以人工标签为参照，评估残差之间的相关性。 On the resume evaluations, we evaluate the correlation in residuals in comparison to human labels. 在全部三个数据集中，我们都发现不同 LLM 的错误具有高度相关性。 In all three datasets, we find that errors correlate highly across LLMs. 例如，在 Helm 上，当两个模型均回答错误时，模型对平均约有 60% 的情况会给出相同答案（若在错误答案中进行均匀随机选择，答案一致率将为 $1/3$）。 For example, on Helm, pairs of models agree on average about 60% of the time when both models are incorrect (choosing between incorrect answers uniformly at random would lead to an agreement rate of $1/3$ ).
(2) 哪些因素能够解释模型相关性？(2) What explains model correlation?
通过对上述数据集进行回归分析，我们发现，来自相同提供商（公司）、采用相同基础架构或规模相近的模型，其错误具有更强的相关性。 Through a regression analysis on the datasets above, we find that models with the same provider (company), with the same base architecture, or with similar sizes have more correlated errors. 重要的是，即使控制这些因素，各自准确率更高的模型所组成的模型对，其错误也具有更强的相关性。 Importantly, even after conditioning on these factors, pairs of models that are more accurate individually also have more correlated errors. 这意味着，表面上有所不同的较新模型，其输出可能正趋于一致。 This means that newer models that differ on the surface may be converging in their outputs.
(3) 模型相关性会产生哪些下游影响？(3) What are the downstream effects of model correlation?
随后，我们研究这些相关性模式如何在两种场景中转化为下游结果：以 LLM 为评判者的评估 (Zheng et al., 2023) 和招聘应用。 We then study how these patterns in correlation translate to downstream outcomes in two settings: LLM-as-judge evaluation (Zheng et al., 2023) and hiring applications. 在以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）的设置中，我们表明，当使用另一个模型作为真实标签的替代时，模型间的相关性会显著影响对 LLM 模型的评估：评判模型会过度高估准确率低于自身的模型的准确率，尤其是来自同一提供商或采用相同架构的模型。 In the LLM-as-judge setting, we show that model correlations substantially affect LLM model evaluations when another model is used to proxy ground truth labels: judges overinflate the accuracy of models that are less accurate than it—especially for models of the same provider or architecture. 此外，出于对招聘市场中算法单一化（algorithmic monoculture）和系统性排斥（systemic exclusion）的担忧（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a），我们量化了企业间相关性对招聘决策的影响，例如，模型相关性如何影响哪些人被录用（以简历与岗位匹配程度的人工标签为参照），以及劳动者福利（他们是否与自己最心仪的企业达成匹配）。 Furthermore, motivated by concerns regarding algorithmic monoculture and systemic exclusion in hiring markets (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a), we quantify the implications of correlations across firms when making hiring decisions—for example, how model correlation affects who is hired (compared to hand labels of resume-job fit) and worker welfare (do they match with their most desired firms).

多智能体与多模型的使用Multi-agent and multi-model use
大量文献或隐含或明确地假设，使用不同的 LLM 比使用同一个模型更有益。 A large literature, either implicitly or explicitly, assumes that using different LLMs provides benefits over using the same model. 例如，为避免自我偏好（self-preferencing）（Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024），许多论文使用一个独立的 LLM 模型来评估一个或多个模型的输出（Zhou et al., 2024; Verga et al., 2024）。 For example, to avoid self-preferencing (Panickssery et al., 2024; Wataoka et al., 2024), many papers use a separate LLM model to evaluate outputs from one or more models (Zhou et al., 2024; Verga et al., 2024). Raghavan (2024) 构建了一个博弈模型，其中，保持准确以及与其他参与者有所不同都能带来收益；他发现，增加噪声（例如，通过提高温度参数）并选择与竞争对手不同的 LLM，可能比使用单独来看准确率最高的智能体更可取。 Raghavan (2024) models a game where it is beneficial to both be accurate and be different than other players; he finds that increasing noise (e.g., via increasing temperature) and choosing a different LLM than competitors may be preferable over using the most individually accurate agent. 我们的工作为这类文献提供了实证基础：不同模型之间的相关性有多强，又有哪些因素能够解释这种相关性？ Our work provides an empirical foundation for this literature: how correlated are different models, and what explains correlation? 因此，我们的结果有助于选择不相关的模型，并分析是否存在足够的多样性来获得其益处。 Our results thus aid in choosing uncorrelated models and analyzing whether there exists sufficient diversity to receive its benefits.
算法单一化与系统性排斥Algorithmic monoculture and systemic exclusion
越来越多的文献关注算法单一化的影响，即许多决策者使用同一个模型时所产生的影响（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Toups et al., 2023; Baek et al., 2025）。 A burgeoning literature is concerned with the effects of algorithmic monoculture, when many decision-makers use the same model (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Bommasani et al., 2022; Jain et al., 2024a; Jain et al., 2024b; Creel & Hellman, 2022; Peng & Garg, 2024a; Peng & Garg, 2024b; Toups et al., 2023; Baek et al., 2025). 例如，在招聘中，算法单一化可能对招聘决策相互关联的企业产生负面影响（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a）。 For example, in hiring, monoculture can negatively affect correlated hiring firms (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a). 另一个令人担忧的问题是系统性排斥，即由于所有岗位都使用同一种算法，一名劳动者被所有岗位拒绝（Bommasani et al., 2022; Creel & Hellman, 2022）。 There are further concerns about systemic exclusion, when one worker is rejected from all jobs because they all use the same algorithm (Bommasani et al., 2022; Creel & Hellman, 2022). 然而，Peng & Garg (2024a) 认为，算法单一化平均而言可能有利于求职者，因为获得录用邀请的人在选择工作单位时拥有更大的自主权，而且在均衡状态下，被录用的劳动者人数相近。 However, Peng & Garg (2024a) argue that monoculture can benefit applicants on average because those given offers have more power to choose where to work and, in equilibrium, a similar number of workers will be hired. 受这类文献启发，我们在第 5 节中模拟了一个劳动力市场，其中企业使用相同或不同的大语言模型来筛选求职者简历。 Motivated by this literature, in Section 5 we simulate a labor market in which firms use either the same or different large language models to screen applicant resumes.

大语言模型中的生成多样性与单一化Generative diversity and monoculture in large language models
生成多样性和模式坍塌（mode collapse）是围绕大语言模型的普遍担忧（Zhang et al., 2025; Senthilkumar et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024; Alvero et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025）。 Generative diversity and mode collapse is a common worry regarding large language models (Zhang et al., 2025; Senthilkumar et al., 2024; Xu et al., 2024; Alvero et al., 2024; Wang et al., 2025). Wu et al. (2024) 发现，同一个 LLM 在处理某项任务的不同实例时存在严重的单一化现象；例如，他们发现，LLM 编写的代码的分布方差小于其训练集中代码的分布方差。 Wu et al. (2024) find severe monoculture within an LLM in instances of a task; for example, they find that the distribution of code written by an LLM has less variance than the distribution of code in its training set. 他们进一步发现，改变推理参数（如温度参数、top-p 和提示词）并不能缓解这种单一化现象，而且经过 RLHF 微调的模型中，这种现象更为严重。 They further find that changing inference parameters (such as temperature, top-p, and prompts) does not mitigate this monoculture, and that it is worse for models with RLHF fine-tuning. 我们的工作通过衡量不同 LLM 之间的单一化程度，补充了这一分析：不同 LLM 相互之间的相关性是否高于它们与真实标签之间的相关性？ Our work complements this analysis by measuring monoculture across LLMs: are different LLMs more correlated with each other than they are with ground truth? 我们发现，答案是肯定的，而且这种现象在同一家模型公司的模型之间以及规模更大的模型之间更为严重；虽然模型内部的生成多样性令人担忧，但使用多个不同的模型也并非万能之策。 We find that they are, and that this is worse among models within the same model company and larger models—while within-model generative diversity is a concern, using multiple different models is not a panacea.
在同期开展且与本研究互补的工作中，Wenger & Kenett (2025) 和 Goel et al. (2025) 也研究了模型相关性。 In complementary and concurrent work, Wenger & Kenett (2025) and Goel et al. (2025) also study model correlation. Wenger & Kenett (2025) 表明，在创造性任务中，“LLM 回答之间的相似程度远高于人类回答之间的相似程度。” Wenger & Kenett (2025) show that, on creative tasks, “LLM responses are much more similar to other LLM responses than human responses are to each other.” Goel et al. (2025) 的研究基于以下观察：错误一致性指标（Geirhos et al., 2020）无法反映模型出错时所作预测之间的差异。 Goel et al. (2025) build on the observation that error consistency metrics (Geirhos et al., 2020) do not capture differing predictions on errors. 他们提出了一种衡量相似性的新指标，该指标对模型准确率进行校正，将不同的错误预测视为不一致，并利用答案选项上的概率分布；我们同样使用了一种指标（两个模型都出错时，它们是否给出相同答案），满足前两项标准，但不纳入模型概率。 They develop a novel metric for measuring similarity that adjusts for model accuracy, considers different wrong predictions as a disagreement, and uses the probability distribution over answer choices; we likewise use a metric (do models agree on an answer when both err) that satisfies the first two criteria but does not incorporate model probabilities. 随后，Goel et al. (2025) 研究了模型相关性的影响，例如以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）时的亲和偏差以及从弱到强的训练，并发现能力越强的模型所犯的错误越相似。 Goel et al. (2025) then study implications of model correlation, such as affinity bias in LLM-as-judge and weak-to-strong training, and find that more capable models make more similar mistakes. 我们同样在三个数据集上发现，准确率越高的模型所犯的错误越相似，而且这会影响以 LLM 为评判者的设置。 We similarly find—across three datasets—that more accurate models make similar mistakes, and that this affects LLM-as-judge setups. 不过，我们还广泛量化了与相似性相关的其他因素（相同的开发者、基础架构、模型规模）。 However, we also broadly quantify other correlates of similarity (shared developer, base architecture, model size). 随后，我们重点研究不同的下游结果，尤其是相关性如何影响招聘等场景中的多智能体系统。 We then focus on different downstream outcomes, especially how correlation affects multi-agent systems like in hiring.
最后，近期研究探索了语言模型表征中潜在的同质性，例如证明了不同模型之间的嵌入可以相互转换（Jha et al., 2025），以及多个网络在相近深度处的激活具有一致性（Wolfram & Schein, 2025）。 Finally, recent work explores underlying homogeneity in language model representations, e.g., demonstrating that embeddings across models can be translated (Jha et al., 2025) and that activations are consistent at similar depths across multiple networks (Wolfram & Schein, 2025). 这些发现为我们的结果提供了一种可能的机制解释。 These findings suggest a mechanistic explanation for our results.
3 相关性错误3 Correlated Errors
我们首先证实，LLM 普遍存在错误相关性，而且这种相关性既相当显著，又在单个模型准确率越高时越强。 We start by establishing that LLMs broadly have correlated errors, and that this correlation is both substantial and is higher for more individually accurate models.

首先，我们研究在不同企业偏好形成方法下，求职者的真实评分如何影响匹配概率。 First, we study how an applicant’s true rating affects match probability across different firm preference methods. 理论文献表明，单一化会使企业结果恶化，即真实评分最高的求职者与企业匹配的可能性更低（Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a）；相比之下，更多样化的模型可能产生“群体智慧”效应，使企业整体上与评分最高的求职者匹配（Peng & Garg, 2024b）。 The theoretical literature establishes that monoculture worsens firm outcomes, i.e., applicants with the highest true ratings are less likely to match with firms (Kleinberg & Raghavan, 2021; Peng & Garg, 2024a); in contrast, more diverse models can result in a “wisdom of crowds” effect, with firms collectively matching with the highest rated applicants (Peng & Garg, 2024b).

6 结论6 Conclusion
我们表明，LLM 的错误具有相关性，而且对于单个模型准确率较高的模型，以及由同一开发者开发或采用相同基础架构的模型，这种相关性显著更高。 We show that LLMs have correlated errors, and that this correlation is substantially higher for individually accurate models and those by the same developer or using the same base architecture. 这些发现表明，随着模型性能提升，模型所犯的错误也在趋同。 These findings suggest that as model performance increases, models are also converging in the errors that they make. 这种错误相关性会影响以 LLM 为评判者（LLM-as-judge）范式的有效性、LLM 在招聘中的应用，以及更广泛的多智能体系统应用。 This error correlation has implications for the effectiveness of the LLM-as-judge paradigm, the use of LLMs in hiring, and of multi-agent systems broadly.

(2025), such a metric is designed to correct for model accuracy: two models will not be deemed to be more correlated simply because they both get a question correct. Goel et al. (2025) 的指标还利用了正确答案上的一致性和模型输出概率，因此，在能够获得这些概率时，该指标可能更为合适。 The metric of Goel et al. (2025)) is that they treat incorrect answers identically; in practice, some incorrect answers may be “closer” to correct, and so preferred by more accurate models; some questions may also be harder than others. 未来研究应考虑开发一种对这些特征具有稳健性的指标。 Future work should consider developing a metric that is robust to these characteristics.

对生态系统监测的启示。Implications for ecosystem monitoring.
尽管单独评估模型的研究十分普遍，但对模型间相关性的分析却出人意料地少见。 While work evaluating models individually is prevalent, analysis of correlations across models has been surprisingly rare. 即便此类分析可以使用单独评估模型时所用的相同数据来开展，情况仍是如此；这是因为基准测试通常会对不同模型重复使用相同的问题，而我们正是利用了这一点。 This is true even though, as we leverage, such analyses can be done using the same data as is used to evaluate models individually, since benchmarks often repeat questions across models. 因此，我们鼓励排行榜开发者持续跟踪模型之间的相关性——正如我们所展示的，这样做能够揭示模型两两之间令人意外的模式，并帮助判断哪些模型搭配使用可能最为有效。 We thus encourage leaderboard developers to continuously track model correlation—as we show, doing so reveals surprising patterns between individual pairs of models and provides insights on which models might be most effective to use together. 同样，在我们将研究应用于劳动力市场中的 LLM 时，已有法律（如 NYC Local Law 144）要求企业审计招聘算法（Groves et al., 2024; Wright et al., 2024; Terzis et al., 2024）；然而，对模型间相关性或多家企业共用算法的实证分析仍相对缺乏。 Similarly, in our application to study LLMs in labor markets, there exist laws (such as NYC Local Law 144) mandating that companies audit hiring algorithms (Groves et al., 2024; Wright et al., 2024; Terzis et al., 2024); however, empirical analyses of correlations across models or of multiple firms sharing algorithms are relatively unexplored.

我们证明，LLM 表现出很强的相关性，甚至连出错方式也存在相关性。 We demonstrated that LLMs exhibit large amounts of correlation, even in how they err. 这对招聘等高风险场景具有影响，因为求职者可能面临被系统性地排除在机会之外的风险；这也对以 LLM 作为评判者的场景具有影响，因为模型可能会高估其他模型的表现。 This has implications for high-stakes settings such as hiring, where there is risk that applicants are systematically screened out of opportunities, and in LLM-as-judge settings, where models may inflate the estimated performance of other models. 尽管我们的分析仅限于同质性可能引发担忧的一部分任务，但它为进一步评估和监测 LLM 生态系统奠定了基础。 While our analysis is limited to a subset of tasks on which homogeneity may be a concern, it serves as a foundation for further evaluation and monitoring of the LLM ecosystem.
