Abstract摘要
Failure attribution in LLM multi-agent systems—identifying the agent and step responsible for task failures—provides crucial clues for systems debugging but remains underexplored and labor-intensive. In this paper, we propose and formulate a new research area: automated failure attribution for LLM multi-agent systems. To support this initiative, we introduce the Who&When dataset, comprising extensive failure logs from 127 LLM multi-agent systems with fine-grained annotations linking failures to specific agents and decisive error steps. Using the Who&When, we develop and evaluate three automated failure attribution methods, summarizing their corresponding pros and cons. The best method achieves 53.5% accuracy in identifying failure-responsible agents but only 14.2% in pinpointing failure steps, with some methods performing below random. Even SOTA reasoning models, such as OpenAI o1 and DeepSeek R1, fail to achieve practical usability. These results highlight the task’s complexity and the need for further research in this area. Code and dataset are available in the public repository.

1 Introduction1 引言
In recent years, the reframing Large Language Models (LLMs) as agents and built agentic multi-agent systems—composed of interactive, LLM-powered agents collaborating to achieve shared goals—has garnered significant attention (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023). These purposefully designed agentic systems have demonstrated remarkable potential across various domains, including coding (Hong et al., 2023), scientific discovery (Ghafarollahi & Buehler, 2024), and addressing complex real-world challenges (Fourney et al., 2024).
近年来，将大语言模型（LLMs）重新定位为智能体，并构建由交互式、LLM 驱动的智能体组成、通过协作实现共同目标的多智能体系统，受到了广泛关注 (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023)。这些经过专门设计的智能体系统已在多个领域展现出显著潜力，包括编程 (Hong et al., 2023)、科学发现 (Ghafarollahi & Buehler, 2024)，以及应对复杂的现实世界挑战 (Fourney et al., 2024)。
Once constructed, these systems are typically refined through an iterative process when they fail in specific scenarios: evaluation against established benchmarks, followed by manual failure attribution and system refinement. This cycle repeats until the desired outcomes are achieved. Failure attribution—identifying the components of the system that directly lead to task failures—is a crucial step that serves as the foundation for guiding improvements. Despite its importance, this process is largely overlooked in mainstream research, which typically leaves it as a manual task requiring significant labor, such as analyzing complex historical logs and navigating the technical intricacies of the system. Moreover, mapping benchmark evaluation results to failure components is heavily dependent on domain expertise, imposing additional requirements on practitioners. As systems grow in complexity, this challenge becomes increasingly difficult due to the growing number of components that must be considered when conducting failure attribution.
这些系统构建完成后，若在特定场景中出现失败，通常会通过迭代过程加以改进：先依据既定基准进行评估，再开展人工失败归因并改进系统。这一循环会持续进行，直至取得预期结果。失败归因，即识别系统中直接导致任务失败的组件，是一个关键步骤，为指导改进提供了基础。尽管这一过程十分重要，但主流研究在很大程度上忽视了它，通常将其留作一项需要投入大量人力的手动任务，例如分析复杂的历史日志，以及梳理系统中错综复杂的技术细节。此外，将基准评估结果映射到导致失败的组件高度依赖领域专业知识，这对实践者提出了额外要求。随着系统复杂性的增加，开展失败归因时必须考虑的组件数量不断增多，使这一挑战愈发严峻。

Previous manual efforts involve a non-straightforward way to facilitate failure attribution in multi-agent systems: developing increasingly fine-grained benchmarks, with the hope that more metrics will enable quicker failure attribution (Zhuge et al., 2024). For example, DevAI (Zhuge et al., 2024) introduces a coding benchmark that incorporates diverse delivery requirements, offering a more nuanced evaluation compared to the widely used SWE-Bench (Jimenez et al., 2023), which focuses exclusively on final resolution rates. Despite these advancements, the process of failure attribution based on benchmark results remains a manual process, merely providing more metrics as reference points without fundamentally addressing the underlying challenges. With increasingly comprehensive benchmarks, a fundamental question remains unanswered: which components of the agentic system require improvement?
以往的人工研究工作采用了一种间接方式来促进多智能体系统的失败归因：开发越来越细粒度的基准，期望通过增加指标来加快失败归因 (Zhuge et al., 2024)。例如，DevAI (Zhuge et al., 2024) 提出了一项纳入多样化交付要求的编程基准，相较于广泛使用且仅关注最终解决率的 SWE-Bench (Jimenez et al., 2023)，能够提供更细致的评估。尽管取得了这些进展，基于基准结果的失败归因仍然是一个人工过程，只是提供了更多指标作为参考，并未从根本上解决其深层挑战。随着基准日益全面，一个根本问题仍未得到回答：智能体系统中的哪些组件需要改进？
We argue that evaluation and failure attribution should be tightly integrated, adhering to the principle that “evaluation is not an end in itself, but a means to improvement.” (Scriven, 1991) More research efforts should focus on bridging the substantial gap between evaluation results and failure attribution, which currently relies heavily on manual labor. Drawing inspiration from the LLM-as-a-judge paradigm (Zheng et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2024), which leverages LLMs to replace human effort in evaluation, we propose to bridge the gap between evaluation and failure attribution by harnessing the comprehensive judgment capabilities of LLMs. Specifically, we propose and formulate a new research problem: automated failure attributions in LLM multi-agent systems. When failures occur under specific scenarios during evaluation, the goal is to automatically identify the components responsible for these failures without human intervention. We believe this research could serve as a substitute for manual failure attribution, enabling human resources to focus on improving system functionality rather than performing time-intensive diagnostics as shown in Figure 1
我们认为，评估与失败归因应当紧密结合，遵循“评估本身并非目的，而是改进的手段”这一原则。(Scriven, 1991) 更多研究应着力弥合评估结果与失败归因之间的巨大鸿沟，而目前失败归因仍高度依赖人工劳动。受利用 LLM 替代人工进行评估的 LLM 即裁判（LLM-as-a-judge）范式 (Zheng et al., 2023; Gu et al., 2024) 启发，我们提出利用 LLM 的综合判断能力来弥合评估与失败归因之间的鸿沟。具体而言，我们提出并形式化定义了一个新的研究问题：LLM 多智能体系统中的自动化失败归因。当评估过程中在特定场景下发生失败时，目标是在无需人工干预的情况下，自动识别导致这些失败的组件。我们认为，这项研究可以替代人工失败归因，使人力资源能够专注于改进系统功能，而非执行耗时的诊断工作，如图 1 所示。
To advance the research in this area, we introduce the Who&When benchmark, comprising extensive failure logs annotated with fine-grained failure details for addressing real-world tasks, where these logs are collected from 127 LLMs-powered multi-agent systems. This benchmark includes both algorithmically generated and hand-crafted multi-agent systems, encompassing a wide range of realistic scenarios. Each failure log is accompanied by meticulous annotations, specifying the failure-responsible agent, the corresponding failure step, and the reasons for failure in plain language. The primary task involves pinpointing the agent most accountable for the failure and the exact step where the error occurred. Predicting the failure-responsible agent serves as a fundamental requirement for failure attribution, given that agents are the basic units of multi-agent systems. Extending this to the specific failure step prediction imposes a higher level of requirement, enabling more fine-grained failure attribution, such as uncovering the specific reasons behind failures, which can further facilitate targeted system refinements. We believe that Who&When can serve as a foundational resource for driving progress in automated failure attribution research.
为推动该领域的研究，我们推出了 Who&When 基准，其中包含大量在处理现实世界任务时产生的失败日志，并附有细粒度的失败详情标注；这些日志采集自 127 个由 LLM 驱动的多智能体系统。该基准既包含通过算法生成的多智能体系统，也包含手工构建的多智能体系统，涵盖了广泛的现实场景。每份失败日志均附有细致的标注，明确指出应对失败负责的智能体、相应的失败步骤，以及用自然语言描述的失败原因。主要任务是准确定位对失败负有最大责任的智能体，以及错误发生的确切步骤。鉴于智能体是多智能体系统的基本单元，预测应对失败负责的智能体是失败归因的一项基本要求。将这一任务扩展至预测具体的失败步骤，则提出了更高的要求，能够实现更细粒度的失败归因，例如揭示失败背后的具体原因，从而进一步促进有针对性的系统改进。我们认为，Who&When 可以作为推动自动化失败归因研究进展的基础资源。
Additionally, we construct and evaluate several automated failure attribution approaches on the Who&When. Our findings reveal the strengths and limitations of each method, as well as their performance across different conditions, including model variations, historical context lengths, and the presence or absence of query labels. The results underscore the complexity of using LLMs for failure analysis in multi-agent systems. For example, the best-performing method achieved only 8.77% accuracy in identifying decisive error steps within the hand-crafted agentic system.
此外，我们构建了多种自动化失败归因方法，并在 Who&When 上对其进行了评估。我们的研究结果揭示了各方法的优势与局限，以及它们在不同条件下的表现，包括使用不同模型、不同历史上下文长度，以及提供或不提供查询标签的情况。这些结果凸显了使用 LLM 对多智能体系统进行失败分析的复杂性。例如，在手工构建的智能体系统中，即使是表现最好的方法，识别决定性错误步骤的准确率也仅为 8.77%。
2 Problem Formulation: Automated Failure Attribution in Multi-Agent Cooperation2 问题形式化：多智能体协作中的自动化失败归因
In this section, we introduce decisive errors and formulate the automated failure attribution problem. We adopt the widely-adopted turn-based LLM multi-agent protocol (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023).
本节介绍决定性错误，并对自动化失败归因问题进行形式化描述。我们采用广泛使用的基于轮次的 LLM 多智能体协议 (Wu et al., 2023; Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023)。
Background.背景。
Considering a LLMs-powered multi-agent system $\mathcal{M}$ with a group of $N$ agents, denoted as $\mathcal{N}=\{1,2,..N\}$ , that operate at discrete time steps. These agents are taking actions in a turn-based protocol, meaning that exactly one agent performs an action at each time step. Formally, the system is described as:
考虑一个由 LLM 驱动的多智能体系统 $\mathcal{M}$，其中包含一组在离散时间步上运行的智能体，共有 $N$ 个，记为 $\mathcal{N}=\{1,2,..N\}$。这些智能体按照基于轮次的协议采取动作，即每个时间步恰好有一个智能体执行一个动作。形式上，该系统描述如下：
$$\displaystyle\mathcal{M}\;=\;\Bigl\langle\mathcal{N},\,S,\,A,\,P,\,\phi\Bigr\rangle.$$
Here, $S$ is the set of possible states. $A$ is the global action set; each agent $i\in\mathcal{N}$ can typically perform actions from some subset $A_{i}\subseteq A$ . $\phi(t)$ is a function that indicates which agent is active at time $t$ , thus specifying the turn-based rule. $P\bigl(s_{t+1}\mid s_{t},\,a_{t},\,\phi(t)\bigr)$ is the state-transition probability, given that only one agent $\phi(t)$ acts at time $t$ .
其中，$S$ 是所有可能状态的集合。$A$ 是全局动作集合；每个智能体 $i\in\mathcal{N}$ 通常可以执行某个子集 $A_{i}\subseteq A$ 中的动作。$\phi(t)$ 是一个函数，用于指示哪个智能体在时刻 $t$ 处于活动状态，从而规定基于轮次的规则。$P\bigl(s_{t+1}\mid s_{t},\,a_{t},\,\phi(t)\bigr)$ 是在时刻 $t$ 仅有一个智能体 $\phi(t)$ 采取动作时的状态转移概率。
We employ $\phi(t)$ to denote the agent that takes an action $a_{t}$ at time step $t$ . A full trajectory $\tau$ can be written as: $\tau\;=\;\bigl(s_{0},\,a_{0},\,s_{1},\,a_{1},\,\dots,\,s_{T}\bigr),$ where $T$ is a terminal time step or when the system enters a terminating state.
我们用 $\phi(t)$ 表示在时间步 $t$ 采取动作 $a_{t}$ 的智能体。完整轨迹 $\tau$ 可写为：$\tau\;=\;\bigl(s_{0},\,a_{0},\,s_{1},\,a_{1},\,\dots,\,s_{T}\bigr),$ 其中，$T$ 为终止时间步，或系统进入终止状态时的时间步。
Decisive Error and Objective.决定性错误与目标。
We use a tuple $(i,t)$ to denote a mistake in a trajectory, which means agent $i$ is active at time $t$ , and its action $a_{t}$ is deemed a mistake (e.g., wrong reasoning etc.). A trajectory may contain multiple mistakes, but not all of them result in overall failure. We employ $Z(\tau)$ to denote the result of a trajectory $\tau$ .
我们用二元组 $(i,t)$ 表示轨迹中的一个错误，即智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 处于活动状态，且其动作 $a_{t}$ 被判定为错误（例如，推理错误等）。一条轨迹可能包含多个错误，但并非所有错误都会导致整体失败。我们用 $Z(\tau)$ 表示轨迹 $\tau$ 的结果。
$$\displaystyle Z(\tau)\;=\;\begin{cases}1,&\text{if the system ultimately fails},\\[6.0pt] 0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$
Suppose the original trajectory $\tau$ is a failure, i.e., $Z(\tau)=1$ . Considering the following scenario, if correcting the mistake made by agent $i$ at time $t$ : we replace $a_{t}$ with a ”correct” action $\tilde{a}_{t}$ . The steps prior to step $t$ remain unchanged, while the actions following $t$ are adjusted accordingly to ensure correctness. This process generates a modified trajectory:
假设原始轨迹 $\tau$ 的结果为失败，即 $Z(\tau)=1$。考虑以下情形：若要纠正智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 所犯的错误，我们将 $a_{t}$ 替换为一个“正确”的动作 $\tilde{a}_{t}$。步骤 $t$ 之前的步骤保持不变，而 $t$ 之后的动作则相应调整，以确保正确性。这一过程生成一条修改后的轨迹：
$$\displaystyle\tau^{(i,t)}\;=\;\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}(\tau),$$
where $\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}$ denotes the intervention. If in the modified trajectory we obtain $Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0$ (success), then the error $(i,t)$ is said to be a decisive error. Formally, we define the decisive error indicator $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)$ as
其中，$\mathcal{I}_{(i,t)}$ 表示该干预。如果修改后的轨迹满足 $Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0$（成功），则称错误 $(i,t)$ 为决定性错误。形式上，我们将决定性错误指示函数 $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)$ 定义为
$$\displaystyle\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)\;=\;\begin{cases}1,&\text{if }Z(\tau)=1\text{ and }Z\bigl(\tau^{(i,t)}\bigr)=0,\\[6.0pt] 0,&\text{otherwise}.\end{cases}$$
In words, $\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad$ Fixing agent $i$ ’s mistake at time $t$ changes $Z(\tau)$ from $1$ (fail) to $0$ (success). Formally, the decisive error could be defined as agent-time pairs $(i^{*},t^{*})$ such that $\Delta_{i^{*},t^{*}}(\tau)=1$ , where $i^{*}$ represents the agent responsible for the system failure, and $t^{*}$ represents the exact time step at which the critical mistake occurs. We refer to these as the failure-responsible agent and the decisive error step, respectively across the paper.
用文字表述，$\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)=1\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad$ 纠正智能体 $i$ 在时刻 $t$ 所犯的错误，会使 $Z(\tau)$ 从 $1$（失败）变为 $0$（成功）。形式上，决定性错误可定义为满足 $\Delta_{i^{*},t^{*}}(\tau)=1$ 的智能体与时间二元组 $(i^{*},t^{*})$，其中，$i^{*}$ 表示应对系统失败负责的智能体，$t^{*}$ 表示关键错误发生的确切时间步。在全文中，我们分别将其称为失败责任智能体和决定性错误步骤。
In practice, multiple decisive errors may occur within a trajectory. In our study, we address this situation by identifying the earliest error in time as the principal cause of failure. Specifically, we define an objective to determine:
在实际中，一条轨迹中可能出现多个决定性错误。在本研究中，我们通过将时间上最早出现的错误确定为失败的主要原因来处理这种情况。具体而言，我们定义一个目标，以确定：
$$\displaystyle\mathcal{C}(\tau) \\ \displaystyle=\;\bigl\{(i,t)\;\mid\;\Delta_{i,t}(\tau)\;=\;1\bigr\}, \\ \displaystyle(i^{*},\,t^{*})=\;\arg\min_{(i,t)\,\in\,\mathcal{C}(\tau)}\;t.$$
which selects the pair $(i^{*},t^{*})$ yielding the highest decisive error indicator with earliest time step. In this study, the research problem focuses on the automatic identification of the $(i^{*},t^{*})$ in LLMs-powered multiple agent systems.
该目标选择决定性错误指示值最高且时间步最早的配对 $(i^{*},t^{*})$。本研究聚焦于自动识别由大语言模型（LLMs）驱动的多智能体系统中的 $(i^{*},t^{*})$。
3 The Who&When Dataset3 Who&When 数据集
To advance research in this area, we introduce a dataset called Who&When. This dataset comprises extensive failure logs from 127 LLM multi-agent systems including both algorithm-generated and human-crafted systems. These logs are carefully annotated with labels that identify the failure-reponsible agents and the decisive error steps in agent cooperation directly responsible for problem-solving failures. Additionally, each annotation is supplemented with natural language explanations, culminating in 184 distinct failure annotation tasks. The dataset is specifically designed to detect the failure-reponsible agents (who) and the corresponding steps (when) within each failure log.
为推动该领域的研究，我们提出了一个名为 Who&When 的数据集。该数据集包含来自 127 个 LLM 多智能体系统的大量失败日志，涵盖算法生成的系统和人工构建的系统。这些日志经过仔细标注，标签指出了在智能体协作中直接导致问题求解失败的失败责任智能体及决定性错误步骤。此外，每条标注均附有自然语言解释，最终形成了 184 个不同的失败标注任务。该数据集专门用于识别每条失败日志中的失败责任智能体（who）及其对应的步骤（when）。
Specifically, each instance in Who&When includes the following entry: (1) Query: A query from GAIA (Mialon et al., 2023) or AssistantBench (Yoran et al., 2024), describing a real-world question. (2) Failure log: The full conversation log of a specific system as it fails to solve the query. (3) Agentic system information: For algorithm-generated systems, including system prompts, tools, and agent names, all tailored to this specific query. (4) Annotations: An annotation of the agent responsible for task failure, specifying the step where the failure occurred, along with a plain-language explanation of why the failure took place. An example of the instance in this benchmark could be found in Appendix C.
具体而言，Who&When 中的每个实例包含以下条目：(1) 查询：来自 GAIA (Mialon et al., 2023) 或 AssistantBench (Yoran et al., 2024) 的查询，描述一个现实世界中的问题。(2) 失败日志：某个特定系统未能解决该查询时的完整对话日志。(3) 智能体系统信息：对于算法生成的系统，包括针对该特定查询定制的系统提示词、工具和智能体名称。(4) 标注：标明对任务失败负有责任的智能体，指定发生失败的步骤，并用通俗语言解释失败原因。附录 C 中提供了该基准的一个实例。
To better reflect our definition of decisive error in Section 2, we design three metrics to evaluate the performance of various failure attribution methods: (1) Agent-Level Accuracy: This metric measures the percentage of correctly predicted failure-responsible agents by failure attribution algorithms. (2) Step-Level Accuracy: This metric quantifies the percentage of correctly identified decisive error steps. It imposes higher requirements on the algorithms compared to the first metric. (3) Step-Level Accuracy with Tolerance: To account for slight deviations, this metric allows a tolerance range for mistake step predictions. If the predicted step falls within the specified tolerance range of the actual mistake step, the prediction is considered correct.

3.2 Decisive Error Annotation3.2 决定性错误标注
After obtaining the failure logs of various agentic systems, we introduce an annotation procedure to identify the decisive error failure and decisive error step. To ensure precise annotation, we conduct multiple rounds of annotation performed by three human experts in AI agent (whose identities are anonymized as 0dmfogp3, 8n3d0wmg, and 204nd84n).
获取各类智能体系统的失败日志后，我们引入一套标注流程，以识别导致失败的决定性错误及其发生步骤。为确保标注准确，我们由三位 AI 智能体领域的专家开展多轮标注（其身份匿名标记为 0dmfogp3、8n3d0wmg 和 204nd84n）。
Round I: In the first round, we distribute the failure logs from all agentic systems for each query equally among three experts. To ensure consistency, we provide the experts with a standardized annotation guideline as shown in Appendix F. Each expert is tasked with annotating three elements: the single erroneous agent primarily responsible for the task failure, the specific step at which the error occurred, and the reasoning behind the mistake in natural language. Additionally, each expert is required to categorize their annotations into two groups: those they are undoubtedly confident are correct and those they have any uncertainty about. Round II: In the second round, people are instructed to make an agreement on all the uncertain annotations from Round II. For these uncertain annotations, we engage in a collaborative discussion to reach a consensus. We do not simply follow the principle of majority rule; instead, we aim to ensure that everyone is persuaded and that a consensus is ultimately reached. Round III: In the final round, a cross-validation procedure is employed. Each expert is asked to go through another expert’s annotations to assess the consistency of the annotation standards. If any discrepancies or issues with the annotations are identified, the experts engage in further discussion and, if necessary, re-annotate the data according to the established guidelines until a consensus is reached. Incorporating the viewpoints of multiple annotators and ensuring consensus among them, we aim to accurately reach the actual ground truth, as suggested by previous studies (Clemen, 1989; Zhuge et al., 2024).
第 I 轮：在第一轮中，我们将每个查询对应的所有智能体系统失败日志平均分配给三位专家。为确保一致性，我们向专家提供了附录 F 所示的标准化标注指南。每位专家需要标注三个要素：对任务失败负主要责任的单个出错智能体、错误发生的具体步骤，以及用自然语言描述的出错原因。此外，每位专家都需要将自己的标注分为两组：完全确信正确的标注，以及存在任何不确定性的标注。第 II 轮：在第二轮中，要求标注人员就第 II 轮中所有不确定的标注达成一致。对于这些不确定的标注，我们通过共同讨论达成共识。我们并非简单地遵循多数决原则，而是力求确保每个人都被说服，并最终达成共识。第 III 轮：在最后一轮中，采用交叉验证流程。每位专家都需要审阅另一位专家的标注，以评估标注标准的一致性。如果发现标注存在任何分歧或问题，专家们会进一步讨论，并在必要时根据既定指南重新标注数据，直至达成共识。正如以往研究 (Clemen, 1989; Zhuge et al., 2024) 所建议的那样，我们通过综合多位标注者的观点并确保他们达成共识，力求准确获得真实的标准答案。
3.3 Analysis3.3 分析
Annotating the decisive error agent and identifying the specific step of the error is challenging for both non-expert people and domain experts. The annotators must parse complex logs, follow the problem-solving logic of each agent, and assess whether each action is correct or if it misleads the entire problem-solving process. For example, if an agent uses a web browser to gather essential information for problem-solving, annotators must check the browser history and visit each website to determine whether the failure is due to unavailable information on the website or because the agent failed to retrieve it. As shown in Figure 2(a), three annotators spent 30.9, 30.2, and 23.2 human hours, respectively, to complete the annotations. This demonstrates that the annotation process is very time-consuming, leading us to consider doing research on automated failure attribution.
无论对于非专业人员还是领域专家，标注造成决定性错误的智能体并确定错误发生的具体步骤都具有挑战性。标注者必须解析复杂的日志，追踪每个智能体的问题求解逻辑，并判断每个动作是否正确，或者是否会使整个问题求解过程偏离正确方向。例如，如果某个智能体使用网页浏览器收集解决问题所需的关键信息，标注者就必须检查浏览历史并访问每个网站，以判断失败是由于网站上没有所需信息，还是由于智能体未能检索到这些信息。如图 2(a) 所示，三位标注者分别花费了 30.9、30.2 和 23.2 个工时完成标注。这表明标注过程非常耗时，促使我们考虑开展自动化失败归因研究。
Additionally, in many data instances, it’s not just one agent that makes mistakes, but several agents. People need to identify these mistakes and select the most severe ones, which can directly lead to problem-solving failures as formulated in Section 2. Since the severity of mistakes may be subtle and even subjective at times, the process becomes even more difficult. As shown in Figure 2(b), we present the uncertain annotation percentages for three individuals. The uncertain percentages across different annotators range from 15% to 30%. We also visualize the disagreement rates between different individuals when voting on each other’s uncertain data in Figure 2(c). We can see some disagreement remains before discussing to make the agreement, further highlighting the difficulties involved in the annotation process.
此外，在许多数据实例中，出错的并非只有一个智能体，而是多个智能体。标注人员需要识别这些错误，并选出其中最严重、能够直接导致问题求解失败的错误，如第 2 节所形式化描述的那样。由于错误的严重程度可能难以分辨，有时甚至带有主观性，这一过程变得更加困难。如图 2(b) 所示，我们展示了三位标注者各自不确定的标注所占的比例。不同标注者的不确定标注比例介于 15% 至 30% 之间。我们还在图 2(c) 中可视化展示了不同标注者对彼此不确定的数据进行投票时的分歧率。可以看到，在通过讨论达成一致之前，仍存在一些分歧，这进一步凸显了标注过程的困难。

如第 3.3 节所揭示的，在智能体系统中识别对失败负责的智能体及相应的失败步骤，往往需要进行细致辨别，并投入大量人力。鉴于这些挑战，我们考虑进行自动化失败归因，利用 LLMs 自身检测这些错误，并为人类开展必要的改进提供信号。在本节中，我们设计实验来回答一个基本问题：LLMs 能否帮助识别多智能体系统中哪个智能体在何时导致了任务失败？
4.1 LLMs for Failure Attribution in Agentic Systems4.1 利用 LLMs 对智能体系统进行失败归因
To answer the question mentioned above, we propose three judgement methods for automated failure attribution in agentic systems. Through extensive experiments, we demonstrate that each method has distinct advantages and limitations, and they can be applied either independently or in combination. Furthermore, we analyze the performance of these methods across various scenarios and constraints, highlighting their applicability in different contexts.
为回答上述问题，我们提出了三种用于智能体系统自动化失败归因的判定方法。通过大量实验，我们表明每种方法都有各自的优势和局限，既可以独立使用，也可以组合使用。此外，我们分析了这些方法在各种场景和约束条件下的表现，重点说明了它们在不同情境中的适用性。
(1) All-at-once: An LLM is provided with a query and the complete failure log, and it is tasked with identifying the failure-responsible agent as well as the specific step where the decisive error occurred. (2) Step-by-step: An LLM is provided with a query, and the failure log is presented step-by-step. At each step, the LLM is tasked with determining whether a mistake has occurred in the current step. If a mistake is identified, the judging process terminates, and the responsible agent’s name along with the current step number are returned as the output. Otherwise, the process continues until the final step is reached. (3) Binary search: Alternatively, this method uses a receptive field approach that lies between the previous two methods. Starting with the query and the full failure log, the LLM is tasked with determining whether the mistake occurred in the upper half or lower half of the failure logs. Once this decision is made, the LLM is provided with the selected half of the log and the process is repeated iteratively until a single step is identified. The three algorithms and corresponding prompts are detailed in Appendix A and Appendix G.
(1) 一次性判定（All-at-once）：向 LLM 提供一个查询和完整的失败日志，要求其识别对失败负责的智能体，以及决定性错误发生的具体步骤。(2) 逐步判定（Step-by-step）：向 LLM 提供一个查询，并逐步呈现失败日志。在每一步，要求 LLM 判断当前步骤是否发生了错误。如果发现错误，判定过程即终止，并返回责任智能体的名称和当前步骤编号作为输出。否则，该过程将继续，直至最后一步。(3) 二分搜索（Binary search）：另一种方法采用了感受野范围介于前两种方法之间的方式。首先向 LLM 提供查询和完整的失败日志，要求其判断错误发生在失败日志的前半部分还是后半部分。作出这一判断后，将选定的半部分日志提供给 LLM，并迭代重复该过程，直到确定某一个步骤。这三种算法及其对应的提示词详见附录 A 和附录 G。

Scenario.场景。
We conduct experiments under two distinct settings to simulate various realistic scenarios. (1) With Ground Truth: In this setting, the final ground truth of the query that the agentic system is attempting to resolve is available to the LLMs. Our focus here is on the typical AI system development cycle, where it is common practice to use a development dataset with ground truth to identify and debug potential errors in experimental systems. (2) Without Ground Truth: In the second setting, the final ground truth of the query is unavailable. In this scenario, LLMs are employed to perform failure attribution in agentic systems based on their running logs. These two results can be attributed to the ’space-in-the-needle’ problem, where LLMs often struggle to retrieve specific information from long contexts (Nelson et al., 2024). The all-at-once method has access to the largest context when making decisions about the decisive error step, but this can lead to difficulties in pinpointing the exact failure step within a long history. In contrast, the step-by-step method processes the context incrementally, allowing for more focused decision-making. The binary search method performs at a level between these two approaches.
这两个结果可归因于“针中空间”（space-in-the-needle）问题，即 LLM 往往难以从长上下文中检索特定信息（Nelson et al., 2024）。一次性方法在判断决定性错误步骤时能够访问最多的上下文，但这可能导致其难以在冗长的历史记录中精确定位导致失败的步骤。相比之下，逐步方法以增量方式处理上下文，使决策更具针对性。二分搜索方法的表现介于这两种方法之间。

5 Related Works5 相关工作
LLM Multi-Agent Systems.LLM 多智能体系统。
An emerging research focus examines using LLMs (Achiam et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024) as central controllers to develop LLM agents that interact with the external world beyond text domains (Xie et al., 2024; Deng et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024b; Zhang et al., 2025). While single-agent systems (Yao et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2023a) excel in specific tasks, they struggle with challenges requiring collaboration and collective intelligence. To address this, studies have explored LLM-powered multi-agent systems, where multiple interactive agents work concurrently (Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023). These systems leverage the specialized skills and roles of individual agents, enabling collaborative problem-solving for complex tasks by simulating real-world cooperation patterns.
一个新兴的研究方向是探索将 LLM (Achiam et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2024) 用作中央控制器，以开发能够与文本领域之外的外部世界交互的 LLM 智能体 (Xie et al., 2024; Deng et al., 2024; Zhang et al., 2024b; Zhang et al., 2025)。尽管单智能体系统 (Yao et al., 2022; Zhang et al., 2024a; Zhang et al., 2023a) 在特定任务中表现出色，但它们难以应对需要协作和集体智能的挑战。为解决这一问题，研究者探索了由 LLM 驱动的多智能体系统，其中多个相互交互的智能体并行工作 (Li et al., 2023a; Hong et al., 2023)。这些系统利用各个智能体的专门技能和角色，通过模拟现实世界中的合作模式，实现对复杂任务的协同求解。
LLM for Judging.用于评判的 LLM。
Numerous studies have explored the use of large language models (LLMs) as evaluators to assess various tasks based on pre-defined standards (Gu et al., 2024; Fu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Thakur et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023b; Hu et al., 2024). For instance, Zheng et al. (2023); Chan et al. (2023) utilize LLMs to evaluate the performance of LLMs in chat conversation scenarios, which would otherwise incur significant labor costs if performed by humans. Another notable example is van Schaik & Pugh (2024); Miao et al. (2023), who employ LLMs as evaluators in the context of text summarization which also heavily relies on human efforts. In the field of agentic systems, related research includes Shinn et al. (2024), who adopt the concept of LLMs-as-judges to analyze task feedback signals and guide corrective actions. Similarly, Zhuge et al. (2024) demonstrate the use of LLMs to provide detailed evaluations of agentic systems within their proposed DevAI dataset. Despite these advancements, failure attribution remains a manual process, with evaluation results serving only as a reference for such attributions
大量研究探索了使用大语言模型（LLM）作为评估者，依据预先定义的标准评估各类任务 (Gu et al., 2024; Fu et al., 2023; Liu et al., 2023; Thakur et al., 2024; Li et al., 2023b; Hu et al., 2024)。例如，Zheng et al. (2023); Chan et al. (2023) 利用 LLM 评估 LLM 在聊天对话场景中的表现，而如果由人工完成这项工作，则会产生高昂的人力成本。另一个值得关注的例子是 van Schaik & Pugh (2024); Miao et al. (2023)，他们在同样高度依赖人力的文本摘要场景中使用 LLM 作为评估者。在智能体系统领域，相关研究包括 Shinn et al. (2024)，他们采用 LLM 作为评判者（LLMs-as-judges）的概念，分析任务反馈信号并指导纠正行动。类似地，Zhuge et al. (2024) 展示了如何在其提出的 DevAI 数据集中使用 LLM 对智能体系统进行详细评估。尽管取得了这些进展，失败归因仍然是一个人工过程，评估结果仅作为此类归因的参考。
Reward Models奖励模型
Most reward models (RMs) are designed either to predict human preference rankings for outputs generated by large language models (Zhong et al., 2025) or to evaluate the reasoning process step by step, rather than assessing only the final answer (Wang et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2024; Lightman et al., 2023; Cui et al., 2025). A number of studies have proposed training process-level reward models that evaluate the correctness of intermediate reasoning steps produced by a single LLM (Lightman et al., 2023; Cui et al., 2025). For instance, Math-Shepherd (Wang et al., 2023) employs automatically generated supervision data to assign reward scores to each step in solving mathematical problems. Similarly, ProcessBench introduces a benchmark of step-by-step solutions annotated by human experts, identifying the location of errors within mathematical problem-solving processes. In this setting, models are tasked with detecting the earliest erroneous step or confirming that the entire solution is correct. However, these works focus primarily on constructing reward models for evaluating the outputs of individual LLMs, rather than identifying the errors in complex agentic systems.
大多数奖励模型（RM）的设计目的，要么是预测人类对大语言模型生成输出的偏好排序 (Zhong et al., 2025)，要么是逐步评估推理过程，而非仅评估最终答案 (Wang et al., 2023; Zheng et al., 2024; Lightman et al., 2023; Cui et al., 2025)。多项研究提出训练过程级奖励模型，以评估单个 LLM 生成的中间推理步骤的正确性 (Lightman et al., 2023; Cui et al., 2025)。例如，Math-Shepherd (Wang et al., 2023) 使用自动生成的监督数据，为数学问题求解中的每个步骤分配奖励分数。类似地，ProcessBench 提出了一个由人类专家标注的逐步解答基准，用于识别数学问题求解过程中的错误位置。在这一设定下，模型的任务是检测最早出现错误的步骤，或确认整个解答均正确。然而，这些工作主要关注构建用于评估单个 LLM 输出的奖励模型，而非识别复杂智能体系统中的错误。
6 Conclusion6 结论
In this study, we propose and formulate a new research area: automated failure attribution in LLM multi-agent systems, an area that has been largely overlooked in current research. To advance this field, we introduce the Who&When dataset, which consists of 127 multi-agent systems with extensive failure logs meticulously annotated with failure details. Furthermore, we develop and evaluate three automated failure attribution methods, highlighting the challenges and complexities of this task. Our findings underscore the significant difficulty of automated failure attribution and emphasize the urgent need for further research in this emerging area.
在本研究中，我们提出并形式化定义了一个新的研究领域：LLM 多智能体系统中的自动化失败归因，这一领域在当前研究中基本上被忽视了。为推动该领域的发展，我们引入了 Who&When 数据集，其中包含 127 个多智能体系统及其大量失败日志，并对失败详情进行了细致标注。此外，我们开发并评估了三种自动化失败归因方法，揭示了这项任务的挑战与复杂性。我们的研究结果凸显了自动化失败归因的巨大难度，并强调了在这一新兴领域开展进一步研究的迫切需求。

Appendix D Brief Cost Analysis附录 D 简要成本分析
We then present a brief analysis of the computational costs associated with three failure attribution methods. We focus solely on input tokens, as the contribution of output tokens such as the agent name and error step number is small. We also ignore the mirror token difference between one-time instruction from different methods. We let $C$ to denote the cost of query $Q$ and corresponding instructions of methods. We employ $L=\{l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n}\}$ , where each entry $l_{i}$ has an average token of $T_{l}$ .
接下来，我们简要分析三种失败归因方法的计算成本。我们仅关注输入 token，因为智能体名称和出错步骤编号等输出 token 对成本的贡献较小。我们还忽略了不同方法的一次性指令之间微小的 token 数量差异。我们用 $C$ 表示查询 $Q$ 及各方法相应指令的成本。我们使用 $L=\{l_{1},l_{2},...,l_{n}\}$，其中每个条目 $l_{i}$ 的平均 token 数为 $T_{l}$。
D.1 All-at-OnceD.1 一次性处理（All-at-Once）
In the all-at-once method, the LLM receives the full context in a single input. The total input token cost is:
在一次性处理方法中，LLM 通过单次输入接收完整上下文。输入 token 的总成本为：
$$\displaystyle Cost_{all-at-once}=C+n\cdot T_{l}$$
This method incurs the lowest cost as it requires only a single inference step.
该方法仅需一次推理，因此成本最低。
D.2 Step-by-StepD.2 逐步处理（Step-by-Step）
In the Step-by-Step method, the LLM processes the failure log incrementally. At each step $i$ , it receives query, instructions and the log segment $\{l_{1},...,l_{i}\}$ . The process terminates when the decisive error step $i^{*}$ is found.
在逐步处理方法中，LLM 以增量方式处理失败日志。在每一步 $i$，它都会接收查询、指令以及日志片段 $\{l_{1},...,l_{i}\}$。找到决定性出错步骤 $i^{*}$ 时，该过程终止。
$$\displaystyle Cost_{step-by-step}=\sum_{i=1}^{i^{*}}(C+i\cdot T_{l})=i^{*}\cdot C+T_{l}\cdot\frac{i^{*}\cdot(i^{*}+1)}{2}$$
In the worst case, $i^{*}=n$ , either when no error is detected or the decisive error occurs in the final step.
在最坏情况下，$i^{*}=n$，即未检测到错误，或决定性错误发生在最后一步。
D.3 Binary SearchD.3 二分搜索（Binary Search）
In the Binary Search method, the LLM operates in a logarithmic fashion by iteratively splitting the failure log into halves. At each step $i$ , the segment of the failure log processed by the LLM has a size of approximately $\frac{n}{2^{i-1}}$ , where $n$ is the total number of log entries. Therefore the total cost at interaction $i$ is $C+\frac{n\cdot T_{l}}{2^{i-1}}$ . The Binary Search continues until the search space is narrowed down to a single step, requiring $\lceil\log_{2}(n)\rceil$ iterations. Therefore the cost of binary search is:
在二分搜索方法中，LLM 通过反复将失败日志分成两半，以对数级的迭代次数执行搜索。在每一步 $i$，LLM 处理的失败日志片段大小约为 $\frac{n}{2^{i-1}}$，其中 $n$ 为日志条目总数。因此，第 $i$ 次交互的总成本为 $C+\frac{n\cdot T_{l}}{2^{i-1}}$。二分搜索持续进行，直到搜索空间缩小至单个步骤，需要 $\lceil\log_{2}(n)\rceil$ 次迭代。因此，二分搜索的成本为：
$$\displaystyle Cost_{BinarySearch}=\sum_{i=1}^{\lceil\log_{2}(n)\rceil}(C+\frac{n\cdot T_{l}}{2^{i-1}})=\lceil\log_{2}(n)\rceil\cdot C+\sum_{i=1}^{\lceil\log_{2}(n)\rceil}(\frac{n\cdot T_{l}}{2^{i-1}})$$
D.4 Cost SummaryD.4 成本汇总
In summary, the costs associated with the three methods are influenced by three key factors: the size of the failure log ( $n$ ), the average token count per log entry ( $T_{l}$ ), and the decisive error step ( $i^{*}$ ). The choice of method should align with the user’s budget and specific use case requirements. Among the methods, the all-at-once approach incurs the lowest cost as it requires only a single inference step. In contrast, the costs of the binary search and step-by-step methods are highly dependent on the specific scenario, particularly the distribution of decisive error locations and the total length of the failure log.
总之，这三种方法的成本受三个关键因素影响：失败日志的大小（$n$）、每条日志条目的平均词元数（$T_{l}$），以及决定性错误步骤（$i^{*}$）。方法的选择应与用户的预算和具体使用场景的需求相匹配。在这些方法中，一次性处理方法的成本最低，因为它只需要一次推理。相比之下，二分搜索方法和逐步处理方法的成本高度依赖于具体场景，尤其是决定性错误位置的分布和失败日志的总长度。
