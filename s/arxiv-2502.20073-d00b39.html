Abstract摘要
Large Language Models (LLMs) based agent systems have made great strides in real-world applications beyond traditional NLP tasks. This paper proposes a new LLM-based Multi-Agent System (LLM-MAS) benchmark, Collab-Overcooked, built on the popular Overcooked-AI game with more applicable and challenging tasks in interactive environments. Collab-Overcooked extends existing benchmarks in two novel ways. First, it provides a multi-agent framework supporting diverse tasks and objectives and encourages collaboration through natural language communication. Second, it introduces a spectrum of process-oriented evaluation metrics to assess the fine-grained collaboration capabilities of different LLM agents, a dimension often overlooked in prior work. We conduct extensive experiments with 13 popular LLMs and show that, while the LLMs exhibit a strong ability in goal interpretation, there are significant shortcomings in active collaboration and continuous adaptation, which are critical for efficiently fulfilling complex tasks. Notably, we highlight the strengths and weaknesses of LLM-MAS and provide insights for improving and evaluating LLM-MAS on a unified and open-source benchmark. The environments, 30 open-ended tasks, and the evaluation package are publicly available at https://github.com/YusaeMeow/Collab-Overcooked.
基于大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）的智能体系统在传统 NLP 任务之外的现实应用中取得了长足进展。本文提出了一项新的基于 LLM 的多智能体系统（LLM-MAS）基准 Collab-Overcooked，该基准基于流行的 Overcooked-AI 游戏构建，在交互式环境中提供了更贴近实际且更具挑战性的任务。Collab-Overcooked 从两个创新方面扩展了现有基准。首先，它提供了一个支持多样化任务和目标的多智能体框架，并鼓励通过自然语言交流开展协作。其次，它引入了一系列面向过程的评估指标，以细粒度评估不同 LLM 智能体的协作能力，而这一维度在以往工作中往往被忽视。我们对 13 个流行的 LLM 开展了广泛实验，结果表明，尽管这些 LLM 表现出较强的目标理解能力，但在主动协作和持续适应方面仍存在显著不足，而这两方面对于高效完成复杂任务至关重要。值得关注的是，我们揭示了 LLM-MAS 的优势与不足，并依托一个统一的开源基准，为改进和评估 LLM-MAS 提供了见解。环境、30 项开放式任务以及评估工具包已公开发布于 https://github.com/YusaeMeow/Collab-Overcooked。
1 Introduction1 引言
Leveraging the remarkable zero-shot and few-shot learning abilities of Large Language Models (LLMs), LLM-based agents are demonstrating their potential in complex task decomposition and planning Wang et al. (2023a); Wang et al. (2023c); Li et al. (2024b). Inspired by human collaborative behaviors in social activities, recent research demonstrates that multi-agent systems can significantly enhance task efficiency and tackle challenges surpassing single-agent capabilities Li et al. (2023a); Hong et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2023). To effectively address complex real-world tasks, LLM-based Multi-Agent Systems (LLM-MAS) require three essential collaboration capabilities beyond goal interpretation, which include: (a) Competence boundary awareness: the ability to analyze task flows and environmental states to determine feasible actions, recognize limitations, and identify when external assistance is needed; (b) Communication: proficiency in utilizing standardized protocols for transmitting task-critical information and resource requests; and (c) Dynamic adaptation: responsiveness to collaboration requests and dynamically adjusting their action sequences accordingly and efficiently.
凭借大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）出色的零样本和少样本学习能力，基于 LLM 的智能体正展现出其在复杂任务分解与规划方面的潜力 Wang et al. (2023a); Wang et al. (2023c); Li et al. (2024b)。受人类在社会活动中的协作行为启发，近期研究表明，多智能体系统能够显著提升任务执行效率，并应对超出单智能体能力范围的挑战 Li et al. (2023a); Hong et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2023)。为了有效处理现实世界中的复杂任务，基于 LLM 的多智能体系统（LLM-MAS）除目标理解能力外，还需要三项关键协作能力，包括：(a) 能力边界意识：分析任务流程和环境状态，以确定可行行动、识别自身局限并判断何时需要外部协助的能力；(b) 沟通：熟练运用标准化协议传递任务关键信息和资源请求的能力；以及 (c) 动态适应：响应协作请求，并据此高效、动态地调整自身行动序列的能力。

Despite recent progress in evaluating LLM-MAS collaboration capability, existing approaches exhibit three critical limitations. First, they prioritize task completion efficiency without imposing strict collaboration requirements, allowing individual agents to accomplish tasks that are nominally “collaborative” independently. This design flaw introduces assessment biases by obscuring the role of collaboration in performance gains, which contrasts with real-world applications where collaboration is often essential for task success. Second, existing benchmarks conflate collaboration capability with end-to-end metrics, such as task completion rates, which are frequently used as proxies for collaboration effectiveness in platforms like CuisineWorld Gong et al. (2023) and VillagerBench Dong et al. (2024). However, this approach overlooks two critical issues: divergent definitions of “success” across environments undermine comparability, and the absence of process-oriented metrics obscures actionable insights for optimizing collaborative strategies. Third, the lack of a fine-grained evaluation prevents a comprehensive, multi-perspective analysis of LLM agents’ capabilities, making it difficult to interpret their strengths and limitations effectively, thus falling short of insightful research suggestions.
尽管近期在评估 LLM-MAS 协作能力方面取得了进展，现有方法仍存在三项关键局限。首先，它们优先关注任务完成效率，却没有施加严格的协作要求，使得单个智能体能够独立完成名义上需要“协作”的任务。这一设计缺陷掩盖了协作在性能提升中的作用，从而引入了评估偏差，而在现实应用中，协作往往是成功完成任务的必要条件。其次，现有基准将协作能力与任务完成率等端到端指标混为一谈，CuisineWorld Gong et al. (2023) 和 VillagerBench Dong et al. (2024) 等平台经常使用这些指标作为协作有效性的替代指标。然而，这种方法忽略了两个关键问题：不同环境对“成功”的定义存在差异，削弱了可比性；而面向过程的指标的缺失，则掩盖了可用于优化协作策略的可操作性见解。第三，缺乏细粒度评估妨碍了对 LLM 智能体能力进行全面、多视角的分析，使研究者难以有效解读其优势与局限，因此无法提出富有洞见的研究建议。
To address the limitations of existing LLM-MAS benchmarks, we propose the Collab-Overcooked Benchmark, designed to provide a fine-grained analysis of collaborative interactions. Unlike prior benchmarks that focus primarily on task completion, our benchmark evaluates the capability of initiating and responding to collaboration during the collaboration process. Specifically, the Collab-Overcooked extends Overcooked-AI Carroll et al. (2019) to a chef-and-assistant collaborating environment and introduces 30 sequential process-specific tasks across 6 complexity levels. Each agent operates in an isolated environment with distinct action spaces, so task completion depends on effective communication and resource exchange, therefore collaboration is strictly required. Furthermore, we propose the Trajectory Efficiency Score (TES) and Incremental Trajectory Efficiency Score (ITES) to assess the collaboration capabilities from both coarse and fine perspectives. Through comprehensive experiments on 13 LLMs of varying sizes, including both open-source and closed-source LLMs, we reveal significant performance gaps in collaboration capabilities across different LLMs. We identify attention misalignment as a key factor affecting collaboration performance. Our results show that, in collaborative tasks, correcting attention alone can improve outcomes, revealing core limitations of current LLM-MAS and pointing to future directions such as collaborative memory and attention-guided fine-tuning.
为解决现有 LLM-MAS 基准的局限，我们提出了 Collab-Overcooked 基准，旨在对协作交互进行细粒度分析。与以往主要关注任务完成情况的基准不同，我们的基准评估协作过程中发起协作和响应协作的能力。具体而言，Collab-Overcooked 将 Overcooked-AI Carroll et al. (2019) 扩展为厨师与助手协作的环境，并引入了涵盖 6 个复杂度等级的 30 项针对特定过程的序列任务。每个智能体都在独立隔离的环境中运行，并拥有不同的动作空间，因此，任务完成依赖于有效的沟通和资源交换，协作也就成为必不可少的条件。此外，我们提出了轨迹效率得分（Trajectory Efficiency Score，TES）和增量轨迹效率得分（Incremental Trajectory Efficiency Score，ITES），从粗粒度和细粒度两个视角评估协作能力。通过对 13 个不同规模的 LLM 开展全面实验，涵盖开源和闭源 LLM，我们揭示了不同 LLM 在协作能力方面存在显著的性能差距。我们发现，注意力错位是影响协作表现的一个关键因素。我们的结果表明，在协作任务中，仅纠正注意力就能改善结果，这揭示了当前 LLM-MAS 的核心局限，并指明了协作记忆和注意力引导的微调等未来方向。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
LLM-Based Multi-Agent System基于 LLM 的多智能体系统
LLM-MAS enables agents to collaboratively engage in planning, discussing, and decision-making. Collaboration is a pivotal capability in task-oriented LLM-MAS, as it not only enhances task completion efficiency Zhang et al. (2024b); Tao et al. (2024) but also enables the pursuit of complex goals beyond the reach of a single agent Park et al. (2023); Hong et al. (2023). Recent methods for improving collaboration can be broadly categorized into (a) Structural optimization (e.g., DyLAN’s Liu et al. (2023) dynamic framework), (b) Role specialization (e.g., AutoGen’s Wu et al. (2023) personas and AgentVerse’s Chen et al. (2023) role assignments), and (c) Communication paradigm (e.g., MetaGPT’s Hong et al. (2023) message pool). Despite these advancements, the inherent complexity and diversity of multi-agent tasks make it difficult to compare methods directly, driving the emergence of standardized benchmarks that enable quantitative evaluations under unified conditions.
LLM-MAS 使智能体能够协同开展规划、讨论和决策。协作是面向任务的 LLM-MAS 的一项核心能力，因为它不仅能提高任务完成效率 Zhang et al. (2024b); Tao et al. (2024)，还能使系统追求单个智能体无法实现的复杂目标 Park et al. (2023); Hong et al. (2023)。近期提升协作能力的方法大致可分为：(a) 结构优化（例如，DyLAN 的动态框架 Liu et al. (2023)），(b) 角色专门化（例如，AutoGen 的角色设定 Wu et al. (2023) 和 AgentVerse 的角色分配 Chen et al. (2023)），以及 (c) 通信范式（例如，MetaGPT 的消息池 Hong et al. (2023)）。尽管取得了这些进展，多智能体任务固有的复杂性与多样性仍使不同方法难以直接比较，推动了标准化基准的出现，以便在统一条件下进行定量评估。
LLM-MAS Benchmark and EvaluationLLM-MAS 基准与评估
Benchmark testing in virtual environments is the primary method for evaluating multi-agent collaboration capability. As shown in Table 1, existing studies establish diverse tasks and commonly use End-to-End (E2E) metrics to assess LLM-MAS collaboration capability, with some benchmarks offering environmental scalability. However, several limitations persist. A key issue is the lack of a formal collaboration definition in most benchmarks, leading to ambiguous assessments and inconsistent comparisons across different benchmarks. Furthermore, the absence of enforced collaboration mechanisms allows agents to achieve objectives independently (e.g., in CuisineWorld, where many tasks can be completed by a single agent), undermining the true assessment of collaboration. Finally, the predominant focus on outcome-based metrics such as E2E performance overlooks the critical role of process-driven dynamics. Approaches like Song et al. (2024), LTC Wang et al. (2023b), and EvoMAC Hu et al. (2024) suggest refining LLMs through process behaviors to enhance adaptation and collaboration, indicating that incorporating process-oriented metrics could offer more comprehensive insights.
在虚拟环境中进行基准测试是评估多智能体协作能力的主要方法。如表 1 所示，现有研究设置了多样化的任务，并普遍使用端到端（End-to-End，E2E）指标来评估 LLM-MAS 的协作能力，其中一些基准还支持环境扩展。然而，仍存在若干局限。一个关键问题是，大多数基准缺乏对协作的形式化定义，导致评估含糊不清，不同基准之间的比较也缺乏一致性。此外，缺乏强制协作机制使智能体能够独立实现目标（例如，在 CuisineWorld 中，许多任务可由单个智能体完成），从而妨碍了对协作能力的真实评估。最后，主要关注 E2E 性能等基于结果的指标，忽视了过程驱动的动态变化所发挥的关键作用。Song et al. (2024)、LTC Wang et al. (2023b) 和 EvoMAC Hu et al. (2024) 等方法提出，通过过程行为改进 LLMs，以增强适应能力和协作能力，这表明纳入面向过程的指标可以提供更全面的洞见。

3 Task-Oriented Collaboration3 面向任务的协作
3.1 Collaboration Capability3.1 协作能力
A task in LLM-MAS can be formulated as a 4-tuple: $T=(G,E,\mathcal{P},\mathcal{R})$ , where $G$ is a natural language description of the task goal, such as “make a dish of tomato soup”; $E$ is a description of the environment, which can be either the layout of a simulated scenario or the visual input of real-world surroundings; $\mathcal{P}$ is optional natural language guidance, providing recipes, helpful hints, or task constraints; and $\mathcal{R}$ is a Referential Action Trajectory (RAT) that leads to the successful completion of the task and is used to assess the agents’ performance. It is worth noting that there are often multiple RATs for a task, especially in dynamic environments.
LLM-MAS 中的任务可形式化为一个四元组：$T=(G,E,\mathcal{P},\mathcal{R})$，其中，$G$ 是对任务目标的自然语言描述，例如“做一份番茄汤”；$E$ 是对环境的描述，可以是模拟场景的布局，也可以是现实周围环境的视觉输入；$\mathcal{P}$ 是可选的自然语言指导，提供食谱、有用的提示或任务约束；$\mathcal{R}$ 是能够引导任务成功完成的参考动作轨迹（Referential Action Trajectory，RAT），用于评估智能体的表现。值得注意的是，一个任务通常存在多条 RAT，在动态环境中尤其如此。
Collaboration often involves agents relying on each other to solve tasks. As shown in Figure 1 Part I, we define collaboration capability as comprising two essential components: the capability to initiate collaboration, where agents, upon realizing that their boundary prevents them from completing the task according to $G$ and $\mathcal{P}$ at environmental state $s_{t}\in E$ at time $t$ , generate a request for collaborative actions $\overline{a}_{req}$ to solicit assistance from other agents; and the capability to respond to collaboration, where agents, upon receiving $\overline{a}_{req}$ from another agent, adjust their action sequence based on $s_{t}$ and generate collaborative actions $\overline{a}_{resp}$ .
协作通常涉及智能体相互依赖以解决任务。如图 1 第 I 部分所示，我们将协作能力定义为包含两个基本组成部分：发起协作的能力，即智能体在意识到自身能力边界使其无法在时间 $t$ 的环境状态 $s_{t}\in E$ 下按照 $G$ 和 $\mathcal{P}$ 完成任务时，生成协作动作请求 $\overline{a}_{req}$，以寻求其他智能体的帮助；以及响应协作的能力，即智能体在收到另一智能体的 $\overline{a}_{req}$ 后，根据 $s_{t}$ 调整自身的动作序列，并生成协作动作 $\overline{a}_{resp}$。
3.2 TES and ITES3.2 TES 和 ITES
3.2.1 TES3.2.1 TES
Trajectory Efficiency Score (TES) is designed to compare the difference between two trajectories and is defined as:
轨迹效率得分（Trajectory Efficiency Score，TES）旨在比较两条轨迹之间的差异，定义如下：
$$\text{TES}(\overline{h}_{k})=\max_{j}\left\{\frac{(1+\beta^{2})D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})}{m_{k}+\beta^{2}n_{k}}\right\}$$
where $\overline{h}_{k}=\{a_{k}^{1},a_{k}^{2},\ldots,a_{k}^{T}\}$ is the historical action sequence up to timestep $T$ of agent $k$ , $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}=\{g_{i}\}_{i=1}^{m_{k}}\in\mathcal{R}$ is $j$ -th RAT of agent $k$ , $\beta$ is the hyperparameter balancing the weight of task progress and redundancy, and $D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})$ computes the length of the longest order-preserving subsequence in $\overline{h}_{k}$ that matches $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}$ :
其中，$\overline{h}_{k}=\{a_{k}^{1},a_{k}^{2},\ldots,a_{k}^{T}\}$ 是智能体 $k$ 截至时间步 $T$ 的历史动作序列，$\overline{g}_{k}^{j}=\{g_{i}\}_{i=1}^{m_{k}}\in\mathcal{R}$ 是智能体 $k$ 的第 $j$ 个 RAT，$\beta$ 是用于平衡任务进展与冗余权重的超参数，而 $D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})$ 计算 $\overline{h}_{k}$ 中与 $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}$ 匹配的最长保序子序列的长度：
$$D_{max}^{j}=\max_{d}\{d\mid\forall\,1\leq i_{1}<\dots<i_{d}\leq n_{k},\,\\ \text{s.t.}\ a_{i_{1}}=g_{1},a_{i_{2}}=g_{2},\dots,a_{i_{d}}=g_{d}\}$$
Unlike other sequence alignment scores (such as ROUGE-L Lin (2004)), TES takes into account sequence order and a redundancy penalty simultaneously, therefore suitable for assessing a planned action sequence (detailed in Appendix B.1).
与其他序列对齐得分（如 ROUGE-L Lin (2004)）不同，TES 同时考虑序列顺序和冗余惩罚，因此适合评估规划的动作序列（详见附录 B.1）。
3.2.2 ITES3.2.2 ITES
Incremental Trajectory Efficiency Score (ITES) introduces an incremental assessment to quantify the task-progress contribution of an individual collaborative action. The ITES is computed as:
增量轨迹效率得分（Incremental Trajectory Efficiency Score，ITES）引入增量评估，以量化单个协作动作对任务进展的贡献。ITES 的计算方式如下：
$$\text{ITES}(\overline{a},\overline{h}_{k})=\text{TES}(\overline{h}_{k}\cup\overline{a})-\text{TES}(\overline{h}_{k})$$
where $\overline{h}_{k}$ denotes the historical action sequence of agent $k$ , and $\overline{a}$ represents the newly executed actions, either a collaboration request ( $\overline{a}_{req}$ ) or response ( $\overline{a}_{resp}$ ).
其中，$\overline{h}_{k}$ 表示智能体 $k$ 的历史动作序列，$\overline{a}$ 表示新执行的动作，可以是协作请求（$\overline{a}_{req}$），也可以是协作响应（$\overline{a}_{resp}$）。
This differential formulation measures the marginal utility of action $\overline{a}$ by evaluating its impact on trajectory alignment with the RATs. It can be established that: $\text{ITES}(\overline{a},\overline{h}_{k})>0$ indicates $\overline{a}$ advances task progress, $\text{ITES}(\overline{a},\overline{h}_{k})\leq 0$ suggests $\overline{a}$ fails to advance task progress (i.e., $\overline{a}$ is redundant / premature action or incorrect response).
这一差分形式通过评估动作 $\overline{a}$ 对轨迹与 RAT 对齐程度的影响，衡量该动作的边际效用。可以确定：$\text{ITES}(\overline{a},\overline{h}_{k})>0$ 表示 $\overline{a}$ 推动了任务进展，$\text{ITES}(\overline{a},\overline{h}_{k})\leq 0$ 则表明 $\overline{a}$ 未能推动任务进展（即 $\overline{a}$ 是冗余动作、过早执行的动作或错误响应）。
3.3 Evaluation Metrics3.3 评估指标
Progress Completeness (PC)进度完成度（Progress Completeness，PC）
Built on the TES, which quantifies a piece of trajectory, PC measures the task progress of all involved agents while penalizing redundancy as a whole, and is defined as:
TES 用于量化一段轨迹，在此基础上，PC 衡量所有参与智能体的任务进展，同时对整体冗余进行惩罚，其定义如下：
$$PC=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=1}^{K}\text{TES}(\overline{h}_{k})$$
where $K$ is the number of agents, $\overline{h}_{k}=\bigcup_{t=0}^{T_{max}}a_{k}^{t}$ denotes the historical action sequence of agent $k$ at time $T_{max}$ , which occurs upon task completion or when the maximum time limit is reached. The PC offers a finer-grained assessment of task completion efficiency compared to boolean success rate.
其中，$K$ 是智能体的数量，$\overline{h}_{k}=\bigcup_{t=0}^{T_{max}}a_{k}^{t}$ 表示智能体 $k$ 在时间 $T_{max}$ 的历史动作序列，$T_{max}$ 为任务完成或达到最大时间限制时的时刻。与基于布尔值的成功率相比，PC 能够更细致地评估任务完成效率。
Initiating Capability (IC)发起能力（Initiating Capability，IC）
IC evaluates the correctness of the LLM agent’s collaboration initiation. IC is defined as:
IC 评估 LLM 智能体发起协作的正确性。IC 定义如下：
$$IC=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}\left(\text{ITES}(\overline{a}_{req}^{(i)},\overline{h}_{j})>0\right)$$
where $N$ is the number of required collaborations, $\mathbb{I}()$ is the indicator function. $\mathbb{I}\left(\text{ITES}(\overline{a}_{req}^{(i)},\overline{h}_{j})>0\right)$ determines whether the $i$ -th initiating collaboration request $\overline{a}_{req}^{(i)}$ advances the task progress, thereby indicating whether the initiation is correct.
其中，$N$ 是所需协作的次数，$\mathbb{I}()$ 是指示函数。$\mathbb{I}\left(\text{ITES}(\overline{a}_{req}^{(i)},\overline{h}_{j})>0\right)$ 判断第 $i$ 次发起的协作请求 $\overline{a}_{req}^{(i)}$ 是否推动了任务进展，从而表明此次协作发起是否正确。
Responding Capability (RC)响应能力（Responding Capability，RC）
Similarly, RC assesses the correctness of the LLM agent’s response to a collaboration request:
类似地，RC 评估 LLM 智能体对协作请求作出响应的正确性：
$$RC=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\mathbb{I}\left(\text{ITES}(\overline{a}_{resp}^{(i)},\overline{h}_{j})>0\right).$$
4 Benchmark4 基准
4.1 Collab-Overcooked Benchmark4.1 Collab-Overcooked 基准
The proposed Collab-Overcooked benchmark builds upon the open-source Overcooked-AI Carroll et al. (2019) and ProAgent Zhang et al. (2024a), introducing two key upgrades: (1) The environment is divided into two parts, featuring resource isolation and asymmetric task knowledge for Agent Bob and Agent Alice, respectively. This contrasts with Overcooked-AI, where agents mostly operate in a shared environment with identical items; (2) The benchmark encourages collaboration through natural language interactions, with some cases enforcing collaboration as a requirement for task success. Additionally, Collab-Overcooked provides APIs to configure new tasks and environmental settings, enabling the enhancement of LLM-MAS through scenario adaptation.
所提出的 Collab-Overcooked 基准基于开源的 Overcooked-AI Carroll et al. (2019) 和 ProAgent Zhang et al. (2024a) 构建，引入了两项关键改进：(1) 将环境划分为两个部分，使智能体 Bob 和智能体 Alice 之间存在资源隔离和任务知识不对称。这与 Overcooked-AI 不同，后者的智能体大多在共享环境中操作，且可用物品相同；(2) 该基准鼓励通过自然语言交互开展协作，并在某些情形下将协作设为任务成功的必要条件。此外，Collab-Overcooked 提供了用于配置新任务和环境设置的 API，从而能够通过场景适配来增强 LLM-MAS。
4.1.1 Environment4.1.1 环境
Our simulation environment is a grid-based kitchen simulation designed as a comprehensive testbed for analyzing collaboration behaviors in LLM-MAS. The environment comprises agents and configurable interactive elements. The interactive elements are dispensers, utensils, counters, and delivery location. Agents can freely retrieve raw materials from dispensers, place them into utensils for processing, and finally transfer the processed materials to other agents via counters or submit the required order through the delivery location. Notably, utensils process materials according to customizable synthesis tables, with each utensil having its own distinct synthesis table. Agents can interact with these elements through predefined action primitives formatted as “func(args)”. For example, “pickup(apple, ingredient_dispenser)” clarifies action type, target material, and interactive element. Details are provided in Appendix A.1.
我们的仿真环境是一个基于网格的厨房模拟环境，旨在为分析 LLM-MAS 中的协作行为提供全面的测试平台。该环境由智能体和可配置的交互元素组成。交互元素包括原料供应器、厨具、操作台和交付点。智能体可以自由地从原料供应器中获取原材料，将其放入厨具中进行加工，最后通过操作台将加工后的材料传递给其他智能体，或通过交付点提交所要求的订单。值得注意的是，厨具根据可自定义的合成表加工材料，每种厨具都有各自不同的合成表。智能体可以通过格式为“func(args)”的预定义动作原语与这些元素交互。例如，“pickup(apple, ingredient_dispenser)”明确指定了动作类型、目标材料和交互元素。详细信息见附录 A.1。
The environment executes agents’ actions sequentially and broadcasts the global state at each timestep, encompassing agents’ positions and the status of interactive elements. We developed a comprehensive rule-based action validator that identifies invalid actions, including environment-action mismatches and incorrect parameters. Upon rule violations, the validator issues error messages, prompting the agent to identify the error and regenerate the action accordingly.
环境按顺序执行智能体的动作，并在每个时间步广播全局状态，包括智能体的位置和交互元素的状态。我们开发了一个全面的基于规则的动作验证器，用于识别无效动作，包括与环境不匹配的动作和参数错误。当违反规则时，验证器会发出错误消息，提示智能体识别错误并相应地重新生成动作。
4.1.2 Tasks Construction4.1.2 任务构建
Sequential process-specific tasks are common in real-world scenarios Wang et al. (2023c); Zhang et al. (2023); Song et al. (2024), where interdependent actions must be completed in a specific order to achieve a goal. We curate 30 such tasks stratified into 6 complexity levels, requiring two agents to complete collaboratively. The task complexity level is determined by the minimum number of collaborative actions, increasing linearly with difficulty. To reduce LLM bias toward specific ingredients, tasks at the same level share workflows but differ in ingredients. Each task has a time constraint, set as the optimal completion time scaled by a time limit factor $\gamma$ .
具有特定顺序流程的任务在现实场景中很常见 Wang et al. (2023c); Zhang et al. (2023); Song et al. (2024)，在这类任务中，必须按照特定顺序完成相互依赖的动作才能实现目标。我们精心选取了 30 个此类任务，将其划分为 6 个复杂度等级，要求两个智能体协作完成。任务的复杂度等级由所需的最少协作动作数决定，该数量随难度线性增加。为减少 LLM 对特定食材的偏差，同一等级的任务采用相同的工作流程，但使用不同的食材。每个任务都有时间限制，设定为最优完成时间乘以时间限制因子 $\gamma$。
Each task is accompanied by a natural language structured process description and RATs for evaluation. As the tasks are process-specific with clear success criteria, their RATs are fully definable and easily traversable, making them suitable for evaluation. We manually annotated RATs for all 30 tasks. Detailed task list, task descriptions, and RAT examples are provided in the Appendix A.2.
每个任务都附有以自然语言编写的结构化流程描述，以及用于评估的 RATs。由于这些任务具有特定流程和明确的成功标准，其 RATs 可以得到完整定义且易于遍历，因此适合用于评估。我们为全部 30 个任务手动标注了 RATs。详细的任务列表、任务描述和 RAT 示例见附录 A.2。
4.1.3 Collaboration Designs4.1.3 协作设计
Collab-Overcooked benchmark imposes strict collaboration among agents. For this, we have two special designs: (a) Resource Isolation: agents operate in resource-isolated sub-environments, necessitating resource exchange via a shared “counter”. This enforces collaborative dependency. (b) Asymmetric Task Knowledge: Only one agent knows how to complete the task. Agents must communicate to synchronize task information. This setup makes collaboration indispensable, offering a stronger test of collaborative capabilities and better exposing potential deficiencies in collaboration.
Collab-Overcooked 基准严格要求智能体之间进行协作。为此，我们进行了两项特殊设计：(a) 资源隔离：智能体在资源相互隔离的子环境中操作，必须通过共享的“操作台”交换资源。这使智能体必须依赖协作。(b) 任务知识不对称：只有一个智能体知道如何完成任务。智能体必须通过通信同步任务信息。这种设置使协作不可或缺，能够更有力地检验协作能力，并更充分地暴露协作中潜在的不足。
While our current setup uses two agents to clearly expose and evaluate collaboration initiation and response, scaling to multiple agents primarily introduces complexity in collaboration rather than fundamentally altering these core collaborative capabilities. Thus, the two-agent design is optimal for isolating and analyzing LLM-specific collaborative behaviors in depth.
虽然我们当前采用两个智能体的设置，以清晰地呈现和评估协作的发起与响应，但扩展到多个智能体主要会增加协作的复杂性，而不会从根本上改变这些核心协作能力。因此，双智能体设计最适合用于单独考察并深入分析 LLM 特有的协作行为。

5.3 Results and Analysis5.3 结果与分析
5.3.1 Task Completion Efficiency5.3.1 任务完成效率
Table 2 presents the Success Rate (SR) and PC scores of 13 LLMs across six levels. Claude Sonnet 4 demonstrates the strongest overall performance, consistently outperforming other models on higher-complexity tasks. Among the open-source models, DeepSeek-R1 excels, especially on tasks of low to medium complexity. However, its token usage is 18.6 times that of GPT-4o, indicating a significant computational trade-off. From these results, we derive three key insights: (1) Smaller LLMs (8B parameters or fewer) struggle with simple tasks, whereas increasing model size significantly enhances performance. This suggests the existence of a clear emergent scaling threshold for low-level tasks. (2) Scaling up LLMs effectively improves task completion efficiency for lower-level tasks but fails to enhance performance on high-complexity tasks. This suggests that current performance gains primarily stem from pattern memorization rather than cognitive reasoning. (3) Beyond Level 4, model performance diverges sharply. Most models collapse, while top-tier agents like Claude Sonnet 4 and o4-mini only postpone this failure to higher complexity levels. This trend indicates that even the most capable models eventually falter under increasing collaborative demands, highlighting our benchmark’s challenging nature and the persistent difficulty of long-horizon reasoning.
表 2 展示了 13 个 LLM 在六个级别上的成功率（SR）和 PC 得分。Claude Sonnet 4 的整体表现最强，在较高复杂度的任务上始终优于其他模型。在开源模型中，DeepSeek-R1 表现出色，尤其是在低至中等复杂度的任务上。然而，其 token 使用量是 GPT-4o 的 18.6 倍，表明这种表现需要付出显著的计算代价。根据这些结果，我们得出三点关键认识：(1) 较小的 LLM（参数量为 8B 或更少）难以应对简单任务，而增大模型规模可显著提升性能。这表明，对于低级别任务，存在一个明确的、随规模增长而出现能力涌现的阈值。(2) 扩大 LLM 的规模能够有效提高低级别任务的完成效率，但无法提升其在高复杂度任务上的表现。这表明，当前的性能提升主要源于对模式的记忆，而非认知推理。(3) 超过 Level 4 后，模型性能出现显著分化。大多数模型的性能崩溃，而 Claude Sonnet 4 和 o4-mini 等顶尖智能体也只是将这种失败推迟到更高的复杂度级别。这一趋势表明，即使是能力最强的模型，最终也会在不断提高的协作要求下受挫，凸显了我们基准的挑战性，以及长程推理持续存在的困难。
5.3.2 Process-Oriented Evaluation5.3.2 面向过程的评估
Figure 3 presents the process-oriented evaluation of LLM-MAS, from which we derive three key insights. First, most models (14B+) exhibit higher RC than IC, indicating that LLMs are better at responding to collaboration than initiating collaboration. This is a result of their strong instruction-following capabilities, which make initiating collaboration the primary bottleneck for most LLMs. Second, the collaboration capability of all LLMs declines with increasing task complexity. Moreover, the decline rate is similar across all models, indicating that their ability to maintain collaboration performance is similar. Despite the scale-up of the models, there is no corresponding improvement in their ability to sustain collaboration capability. Third, the reasoning model outperforms others on simpler tasks. While its performance drops with complexity and it consumes more tokens, its consistent gains show the potential of the CoT-training paradigm for improving collaboration capabilities.

To establish a robust performance ceiling, we conducted experiments with 10 human participants performing tasks spanning all six levels. As shown in Figure 16, 17, we designed a human-computer interaction interface to enable participants to simulate agent behaviors within the environment. To ensure a fair comparison with LLMs in time-sensitive scenarios, we imposed time constraints on both communication and decision-making during each timestep for participants. We further evaluated human performance under various time limits, and detailed descriptions of the experimental design and rules are provided in Appendix C.2.
为确立可靠的性能上限，我们开展了实验，让 10 名人类参与者执行涵盖全部六个级别的任务。如图 16、17 所示，我们设计了一个人机交互界面，使参与者能够在环境中模拟智能体的行为。为确保在时间敏感场景下与 LLM 进行公平比较，我们对参与者在每个时间步内的通信和决策均施加了时间限制。我们进一步评估了不同时间限制下的人类表现，实验设计与规则的详细说明见附录 C.2。
As illustrated in Figure 4, human participants consistently achieved high and stable performance across all levels of task complexity, even under time constraints. In contrast, DeepSeek-R1, the strongest open-source model evaluated, exhibited a marked decline in performance as task complexity increased. These results highlight two key limitations of current LLM-MAS: a lack of performance consistency under increasing complexity, and the insufficiency of model parameter scaling alone to overcome this gap. This advantage in human performance stems from the participants’ ability to form high-level task abstractions and procedural understanding during interaction, allowing them to flexibly adapt to novel situations and maintain stable outcomes. In comparison, current LLM-MAS rely on shallow memory mechanisms that log past trajectories without abstracting them into reusable strategies. Consequently, they fail to generalize from simpler tasks to more complex ones, leading to cumulative errors and performance degradation as complexity rises.

5.3.4 Analysis of Collaboration Failures5.3.4 协作失败分析
Collaboration challenges are likely to arise across diverse multi-agent contexts. Recent work has begun to systematically categorize the failure modes of LLM-MAS. For example, Cemri et al. (2025) proposed MAST, a comprehensive taxonomy of MAS failures. While such taxonomies provide a valuable framework for understanding what failures occur, our benchmark’s design, with its strict resource isolation and asymmetric task knowledge, allows a deeper, quantitative analysis into why and how these failures manifest dynamically.
各种多智能体情境中都可能出现协作难题。近期研究已开始系统地归类 LLM-MAS 的失败模式。例如，Cemri et al. (2025) 提出了 MAST，这是一套全面的 MAS 失败分类体系。尽管此类分类体系为理解发生了哪些失败提供了有价值的框架，但我们基准所采用的严格资源隔离和任务知识不对称设计，使我们能够更深入地定量分析这些失败为何发生，以及它们如何动态显现。
Collaboration Capabilities Degradation协作能力退化
To better understand collaboration capability degradation, we conducted a series of controlled experiments detailed in Appendix C.4. We first identify a consistent performance decay across sequential collaborative steps. As tasks progress, agents become increasingly prone to specific failure modes such as premature or repetitive initiation, establishing the capability of initiating collaboration as the primary bottleneck. This trend of performance degradation in long-horizon tasks is consistent with findings from Li et al. (2024a) and Li et al. (2024c). Crucially, our analysis reveals this degradation persists even when we mitigate planning ambiguity by providing recipes with explicit step-to-action mappings. This experimental control allowed us to isolate the root cause to a strong positional dependence: identical collaborative actions that fail in later stages are executed with significantly higher success rates when their position is simply moved to the beginning of the workflow. This finding strongly suggests that the issue is not a failure in high-level reasoning, but stems from more fundamental limitations: inherent pretraining biases that favor sequence initiation over continuation, and an architectural inability to maintain coherent context over extended interactions, which are critical for sustained collaboration.
为更好地理解协作能力退化，我们开展了一系列受控实验，详见附录 C.4。我们首先发现，在连续的协作步骤中，性能持续衰减。随着任务推进，智能体越来越容易出现过早发起或重复发起协作等特定失败模式，表明发起协作的能力是主要瓶颈。这种在长程任务中出现的性能退化趋势，与 Li et al. (2024a) 和 Li et al. (2024c) 的研究发现一致。关键的是，我们的分析表明，即使通过提供具有明确步骤到动作映射的配方来减少规划歧义，这种退化仍然存在。这一实验控制使我们得以将根本原因定位为强烈的位置依赖性：在后期阶段失败的相同协作动作，只需将其位置移至工作流程的开头，执行成功率就会显著提高。这一发现有力地表明，问题并非高层次推理失效，而是源于更基础的局限：预训练中固有的偏差使模型更擅长启动序列而非延续序列，以及模型架构无法在长时间交互中保持上下文连贯性，而这些能力对于持续协作至关重要。
Attention Bias注意力偏差
By segmenting input prompts into 5 or 6 distinct parts and analyzing attention weight distributions (see Figure 10), we identified distinct attention patterns differentiating successful and failed collaborations, highlighting critical biases. During initiation, increased attention to collaboration rules correlates with success, whereas excessive focus on recipe information predicts failure. This suggests a fundamental attention bias where LLM-MAS agents overemphasize task execution details while undervaluing essential collaboration-specific information, causing errors in determining the appropriate collaboration approach at a given state (see Appendix C.3). In the responding phase, successful outcomes feature heightened attention to environmental observations and collaboration rules. In contrast, excessive deference to partner instructions without integrating environmental observation and collaboration rules causes failed responding. These attention biases directly contribute to redundant actions and degraded performance metrics (PC, IC, and RC), with their effects becoming more pronounced under increased task complexity due to error propagation.
通过将输入提示划分为 5 或 6 个不同部分，并分析注意力权重分布（见图 10），我们识别出了成功协作与失败协作之间不同的注意力模式，揭示了关键偏差。在发起阶段，对协作规则投入更多注意力与成功相关，而过度关注食谱信息则预示着失败。这表明存在一种根本性的注意力偏差：LLM-MAS 智能体过度强调任务执行细节，却低估了关键的协作专属信息，从而在判断给定状态下适当的协作方式时出错（见附录 C.3）。在响应阶段，成功结果的特点是对环境观测和协作规则投入更多注意力。相比之下，过度遵从伙伴的指令，而未结合环境观测和协作规则，会导致响应失败。这些注意力偏差直接导致冗余动作和性能指标（PC、IC 和 RC）下降；随着任务复杂度增加，错误传播会使其影响更加显著。
Attention Intervention注意力干预
To establish the causal relationship between attention distribution and collaboration outcomes, we conducted attention intervention experiments by manually adjusting the attention allocation to align with patterns observed in successful cases. Using the same random seeds and model parameters, we then regenerated the outputs. As shown in Figure 5, we observed performance improvements of 35% to 64% in previously failed instances, while originally successful outputs remained largely unaffected. These results confirm that attention bias is a key causal factor in collaboration failure, likely rooted in pretraining on single-agent execution tasks rather than on collaborative scenarios requiring joint decision-making.
为确立注意力分布与协作结果之间的因果关系，我们开展了注意力干预实验，手动调整注意力分配，使其与成功案例中观察到的模式一致。随后，我们使用相同的随机种子和模型参数重新生成了输出。如图 5 所示，我们观察到，此前失败的实例性能提升了 35% 至 64%，而原本成功的输出基本不受影响。这些结果证实，注意力偏差是导致协作失败的关键因果因素，其根源很可能在于预训练针对的是单智能体执行任务，而非需要联合决策的协作场景。
To the best of our knowledge, we are the first to reveal and analyze attention-driven failure modes in information and resource isolation environments, highlighting persistent biases toward task execution that are less evident in existing LLM-MAS collaboration benchmarks.
据我们所知，我们首次揭示并分析了信息与资源隔离环境中由注意力驱动的失败模式，凸显了持续存在的任务执行偏向，而这种偏向在现有 LLM-MAS 协作基准中并不明显。
5.4 Future Challenges5.4 未来挑战
Collaborative Memory and Experience Abstraction协作记忆与经验抽象
Future work should develop specialized memory mechanisms for multi-agent collaboration that go beyond single-agent approaches. LLM-MAS requires systems that can retain and generalize collaborative patterns across diverse contexts and complexity levels, enabling agents to progressively develop more sophisticated collaboration capabilities through accumulated experience.
未来工作应开发超越单智能体方法的、专门面向多智能体协作的记忆机制。LLM-MAS 需要能够在不同情境和复杂度水平下保留并泛化协作模式的系统，使智能体能够通过积累经验，逐步发展出更成熟的协作能力。
Attention-Guided Fine-tuning注意力引导的微调
Our attention intervention experiments demonstrate that targeted attention modification alone can dramatically improve collaborative outcomes. Future approaches should incorporate mechanisms that guide models to attend to critical collaboration-relevant information through fine-tuning regimens or soft attention constraints. These techniques could help overcome the inherent single-agent execution biases currently limiting LLM collaborative performance.
我们的注意力干预实验表明，仅有针对性地修改注意力，就能大幅改善协作结果。未来方法应纳入相应机制，通过微调方案或软性注意力约束，引导模型关注与协作相关的关键信息。这些技术有助于克服目前限制 LLM 协作性能的固有单智能体执行偏差。
6 Conclusion6 结论
We introduce the Collab-Overcooked Benchmark, a framework evaluating LLM-MAS collaboration from end-to-end and process-oriented perspectives. Experiments across 13 LLMs reveal significant performance gaps, with attention misalignment to collaboration-relevant instructions emerging as a key bottleneck. These findings underscore the difficulty of achieving high performance in collaborative tasks under training-free, zero-shot settings, highlighting the need to improve attention mechanisms for better adaptability and collaboration.
我们提出了 Collab-Overcooked Benchmark，这是一个从端到端和面向过程的视角评估 LLM-MAS 协作的框架。针对 13 个 LLM 的实验揭示了显著的性能差距，其中，注意力与协作相关指令之间的失配成为一个关键瓶颈。这些发现凸显了在无需训练的零样本设置下，协作任务难以实现高性能，也强调了改进注意力机制以提高适应性和协作能力的必要性。
Limitations局限性
The Collab-Overcooked Benchmark is introduced in our paper and we explore methods for evaluating the collaboration capabilities of LLM-MAS using both end-to-end and process-oriented approaches. However, there are three limitations to our work. First, all of our tasks are sequential and process-specific. While we assume that RATs can be exhaustively enumerated, making it possible to use exhaustive RATs as labeled data for evaluating the collaboration capabilities of LLM-MAS. However, in environments with highly complex state and action spaces, RATs are difficult to exhaustively enumerate. In such cases, only representative RATs can be listed as evaluation data, which introduces potential bias into our evaluation methodology. Second, due to the complex mechanisms of LLM-MAS, such as communication, memory, and reflection, the prompts are relatively long (approximately 2,000 tokens, with variation depending on the tokenizer used by the LLM). Additionally, process-oriented evaluation requires substantial interaction data, which leads to both low evaluation efficiency and significant token consumption, which is the common challenge across current methods for evaluating LLM-MAS capabilities. Third, the baseline used to evaluate LLM-MAS is composed of relatively simple structures, with the agent possessing only basic memory and reflection mechanisms, leaving substantial room for optimization.
本文提出了 Collab-Overcooked Benchmark，并探索了采用端到端和面向过程这两种方法来评估 LLM-MAS 协作能力的方法。然而，我们的工作存在三点局限。首先，我们的所有任务都是顺序性的，并且针对特定流程。尽管我们假设 RATs 可以被穷举，从而能够将穷举得到的 RATs 用作标注数据，以评估 LLM-MAS 的协作能力。然而，在状态空间和动作空间高度复杂的环境中，RATs 难以穷举。在这种情况下，只能将具有代表性的 RATs 列为评估数据，这会给我们的评估方法引入潜在偏差。其次，由于 LLM-MAS 具有通信、记忆和反思等复杂机制，提示词相对较长（约为 2,000 个 token，具体数量因 LLM 使用的分词器而异）。此外，面向过程的评估需要大量交互数据，导致评估效率低下且 token 消耗量大，这是当前评估 LLM-MAS 能力的各类方法面临的共同挑战。第三，用于评估 LLM-MAS 的基线结构相对简单，其中的智能体仅具备基本的记忆和反思机制，因此仍有很大的优化空间。

A.1.3 Layout Definition MethodA.1.3 布局定义方法
We follow the environment design principles of Overcooked-AI Carroll et al. (2019) and ProAgent Zhang et al. (2024a), enabling customization through external layout files. A.3.1 Baseline ConstructionA.3.1 基线构建
Figure 7 illustrates the structure of the baseline and provides an example of agents interacting and collaborating to complete a task within our benchmark. The baseline architecture consists of an Instruction-Builder, Planner, Communication, Error-Handling, Memory, and Reflection modules. The structure remains identical across different agents, with variations arising only in the environment descriptions, action spaces, and task-specific knowledge provided within the prompts.
图 7 展示了基线的结构，并提供了智能体在我们的基准测试中通过交互与协作完成任务的示例。基线架构由指令构建器（Instruction-Builder）、规划器（Planner）、通信（Communication）、错误处理（Error-Handling）、记忆（Memory）和反思（Reflection）模块组成。不同智能体的结构保持一致，差异仅在于提示词中提供的环境描述、动作空间和任务相关知识。
Instruction-builder指令构建器
The Instruction-builder is a rule-based module responsible for managing and integrating the prompts for each agent. It reads the state dictionary from the environment and fills in a prompt template. The prompt template includes both fixed prompts and slot-based prompts. Fixed prompts contain: (1) game rules, such as objectives, scoring workflows, functions of each kitchen utensils, and methods for preparing dishes; (2) communication rules and output format specifications; and (3) a definition of the agent’s action space, along with a brief description of actions available to teammates. Slot-based prompts include: (1) the current recipe for the task (if the agent has access to the recipe); (2) the current environment observations, such as kitchen layout and teammate status; (3) communication records with other agents up to the current time step; and (4) memory and reflection from previous time steps.
指令构建器是一个基于规则的模块，负责管理和整合每个智能体的提示词。它从环境中读取状态字典，并填充提示词模板。提示词模板包括固定提示词和基于槽位的提示词。固定提示词包含：(1) 游戏规则，例如目标、计分流程、各类厨房器具的功能以及菜肴的制作方法；(2) 通信规则和输出格式规范；(3) 智能体动作空间的定义，以及对队友可用动作的简要描述。基于槽位的提示词包括：(1) 当前任务的食谱（如果该智能体可以访问食谱）；(2) 当前的环境观测，例如厨房布局和队友状态；(3) 截至当前时间步与其他智能体的通信记录；(4) 先前时间步的记忆与反思。
Planner规划器
The planner is the core decision-making component for the agent. It generates three fields: “Analysis”, “Say”, and “Plan”. The “Analysis” field represents the agent’s assessment of the current environment state, task, and memories, assisting the planner in making informed decisions. The “Say” field determines whether collaboration is required; if the planner identifies a need for collaboration, it generates communication content directly in this field. The “Plan” field contains the action sequence that the planner has devised for the agent.
规划器是智能体的核心决策组件。它生成三个字段：“Analysis”、“Say”和“Plan”。“Analysis”字段表示智能体对当前环境状态、任务和记忆的评估，帮助规划器作出有充分依据的决策。“Say”字段用于确定是否需要协作；如果规划器判断需要协作，就会直接在该字段中生成通信内容。“Plan”字段包含规划器为智能体制定的动作序列。
Communication通信
Communication between agents enables the transmission of collaborative intentions or requests for assistance. When communication content is detected in the “Say” field, all agents enter the communication channel. Within this channel, each agent speaks in sequence until a special token “[END]” is generated or the maximum number of interaction rounds is reached. Once communication is complete, agents formulate their plans based on the information exchanged.
智能体之间的通信能够传递协作意图或求助请求。当检测到“Say”字段中存在通信内容时，所有智能体都会进入通信通道。在该通道内，各智能体依次发言，直到生成特殊词元“[END]”或达到最大交互轮数。通信完成后，智能体根据所交换的信息制定各自的计划。
Error-handling错误处理
The error-handling process manages situations in which the generated actions are deemed invalid by the environment. When an agent receives an error message from the environment, the error information is incorporated into the prompt and re-entered into the planner. This cycle continues until the generated actions are considered valid by the environment or the maximum number of attempts is reached.
错误处理流程用于处理生成的动作被环境判定为无效的情况。当智能体收到环境返回的错误消息时，错误信息会被纳入提示词，并重新输入规划器。这一循环持续进行，直到生成的动作被环境判定为有效，或达到最大尝试次数。
Memory and Reflection记忆与反思
Memory and reflection represent the accumulation of an agent’s past experiences, enabling it to engage in long-term planning. We implement memory and reflection using a straightforward approach. The memory logs the action sequences that the agent has completed in the past, while the reflection records the previous agent’s reflections on invalid actions.
记忆与反思体现了智能体对过往经验的积累，使其能够进行长期规划。我们采用一种简单直接的方法来实现记忆与反思。记忆记录智能体过去已完成的动作序列，而反思则记录智能体此前对无效动作的反思。

B.1 Details in TESB.1 TES 的细节
The TES is formally expressed as:
TES 的形式化表达如下：
$$\text{TES}(\overline{h}_{k})=\max_{j}\left\{\frac{(1+\beta^{2})D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})}{m_{k}+\beta^{2}n_{k}}\right\}$$
where $\overline{h}_{k}=\{a_{k}^{1},a_{k}^{2},\ldots,a_{k}^{T}\}$ is the historical action sequence up to timestep $T$ of agent $k$ , $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}=\{g_{i}\}_{i=1}^{m_{k}}\in\mathcal{R}$ is $j$ -th RAT of agent $k$ , $\beta$ is the hyperparameter balancing the weight of task progress and redundancy, and $D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})$ computes the length of the longest order-preserving subsequence in $\overline{h}_{k}$ that matches $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}$ :
其中，$\overline{h}_{k}=\{a_{k}^{1},a_{k}^{2},\ldots,a_{k}^{T}\}$ 是智能体 $k$ 截至时间步 $T$ 的历史动作序列，$\overline{g}_{k}^{j}=\{g_{i}\}_{i=1}^{m_{k}}\in\mathcal{R}$ 是智能体 $k$ 的第 $j$ 个 RAT，$\beta$ 是用于平衡任务进展与冗余权重的超参数，而 $D_{\text{max}}^{j}(\overline{h}_{k},\overline{g}_{k}^{j})$ 计算 $\overline{h}_{k}$ 中与 $\overline{g}_{k}^{j}$ 匹配的最长保序子序列的长度：
$$D_{max}^{j}=\max_{d}\{d\mid\forall\,1\leq i_{1}<\dots<i_{d}\leq n_{k},\,\\ \text{s.t.}\ a_{i_{1}}=g_{1},a_{i_{2}}=g_{2},\dots,a_{i_{d}}=g_{d}\}$$
It is important to note that the TES function introduces modifications to the Longest Common Subsequence (LCS) calculation in ROUGE-L Lin (2004). These modifications are driven by one main reason: Improved identification of redundant actions. Listing 4 illustrates a very common scenario where, due to the agent’s incorrect choice in step four, the fifth step fails to advance the task. Specifically, the agent places an irrelevant item, “egg”, onto the counter, which does not contribute to the task’s progress. In this case, the standard ROUGE-L, based on LCS, would mistakenly consider the agent’s fifth action as matching the RAT, leading to an inflated evaluation score.

B.2 Details in IC and RCB.2 IC 和 RC 的细节
Initiating Capability (IC) and Responding Capability (RC) are proposed to evaluate the LLM agent’s capabilities to initiate and respond to collaboration, respectively. Physically, these metrics represent the success rate of an LLM agent in initiating or responding to collaborative behaviors within a given task. The determination of success is based on the change in ITES induced by the newly proposed action compared to historical actions. Taking collaboration initiation as an example, a newly initiated collaborative action $a$ is considered successful if it results in an increase in ITES, i.e., $\text{ITES}>0$ . This reflects whether the proposed action $a$ contributes to the advancement of the task; if so, it is deemed a successful collaboration attempt. This evaluation paradigm has been widely adopted in prior research Gong et al. (2023); Hong et al. (2023); Mandi et al. (2024), and thus, both IC and RC are not only grounded in meaningful physical interpretations but also serve as effective indicators of real-world collaborative performance.
发起能力（Initiating Capability，IC）和响应能力（Responding Capability，RC）分别用于评估 LLM 智能体发起协作和响应协作的能力。从实际含义来看，这些指标表示 LLM 智能体在给定任务中发起或响应协作行为的成功率。成功与否的判定依据是新提出的动作相较于历史动作所引起的 ITES 变化。以发起协作为例，如果新发起的协作动作 $a$ 使 ITES 增加，即 $\text{ITES}>0$，则认为该动作成功。这反映了所提出的动作 $a$ 是否有助于推进任务；如果有，则将其视为一次成功的协作尝试。这一评估范式已在以往研究 Gong et al. (2023); Hong et al. (2023); Mandi et al. (2024) 中得到广泛采用，因此，IC 和 RC 不仅具有有意义的实际解释，也能有效衡量现实世界中的协作表现。

C.1 Impact of Varying $\gamma$ on Task Success RateC.1 改变 $\gamma$ 对任务成功率的影响
The hyperparameter $\gamma$ controls the task failure threshold. Specifically, it determines a time constraint on the task, which is calculated by multiplying the optimal completion time by the value of $\gamma$ . As $\gamma$ increases, the task success rate (SR) of the LLM-MAS will improve, as the system is allowed more time to complete the task. However, $\gamma$ cannot be increased indefinitely, as doing so would lead to inefficiencies in the evaluation process. An excessively high value of $\gamma$ might artificially inflate the success rate, as the extended time window may not reflect the true capabilities of the model in real-world scenarios, and it wastes computing resources. On the other hand, setting $\gamma$ too low could result in an overly strict evaluation, where the system is unable to complete tasks even when it could have more time. Therefore, it is essential to select an optimal value for $\gamma$ that balances both task success and evaluation efficiency.
超参数 $\gamma$ 控制任务失败的判定阈值。具体而言，它决定了任务的时间限制，该限制通过将最优完成时间乘以 $\gamma$ 的值来计算。随着 $\gamma$ 增大，LLM-MAS 的任务成功率（SR）会提高，因为系统获准使用更多时间来完成任务。然而，$\gamma$ 不能无限增大，因为这会降低评估过程的效率。过高的 $\gamma$ 值可能会人为抬高成功率，因为延长的时间窗口可能无法反映模型在现实场景中的真实能力，而且会浪费计算资源。另一方面，将 $\gamma$ 设置得过低可能会使评估过于严格，导致系统无法完成那些在给予更多时间后本可以完成的任务。因此，必须为 $\gamma$ 选择一个最优值，以兼顾任务成功率和评估效率。
