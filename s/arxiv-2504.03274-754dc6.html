Abstract摘要
Recent advancements in AI have reinvigorated Agent-Based Models (ABMs), as the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) has led to the emergence of “generative ABMs” as a novel approach to simulating social systems. While ABMs offer means to bridge micro-level interactions with macro-level patterns, they have long faced criticisms from social scientists, pointing to e.g., lack of realism, computational complexity, and challenges of calibrating and validating against empirical data. This paper reviews the generative ABM literature to assess how this new approach adequately addresses these long-standing criticisms. Our findings show that studies show limited awareness of historical debates. Validation remains poorly addressed, with many studies relying solely on subjective assessments of model ‘believability’, and even the most rigorous validation failing to adequately evidence operational validity. We argue that there are reasons to believe that LLMs will exacerbate rather than resolve the long-standing challenges of ABMs. The black-box nature of LLMs moreover limit their usefulness for disentangling complex emergent causal mechanisms. While generative ABMs are still in a stage of early experimentation, these findings question of whether and how the field can transition to the type of rigorous modeling needed to contribute to social scientific theory.
人工智能的近期进展使基于智能体的模型（Agent-Based Models，ABMs）重新焕发生机，因为大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）的融入催生了“生成式 ABMs”，成为模拟社会系统的一种新方法。尽管 ABMs 提供了将微观层面的互动与宏观层面的模式联系起来的手段，但它们长期以来一直受到社会科学家的批评，例如缺乏现实性、计算复杂，以及难以依据经验数据进行校准和验证。本文回顾生成式 ABM 文献，以评估这种新方法在多大程度上充分回应了这些长期存在的批评。我们的研究发现，现有研究对历史争论的认识有限。验证问题仍未得到妥善处理，许多研究仅依赖对模型“可信性”的主观评估，即使最严格的验证也未能为运行有效性（operational validity）提供充分证据。我们认为，有理由相信 LLMs 会加剧而非解决 ABMs 长期面临的挑战。此外，LLMs 的黑箱性质限制了它们在厘清复杂的涌现因果机制方面的作用。尽管生成式 ABMs 仍处于早期试验阶段，这些发现仍引出了一个问题：该领域能否以及如何转向能够为社会科学理论作出贡献的严格建模。

1 Introduction1 引言
Human societies emerge from countless interactions of individuals – yet, social scientific methods tend to focus on either aggregate patterns or individual decision-making, while neglecting the central question of the relationship between the two Byrne and Callaghan (2022). Agent-Based Models (ABMs) promise a means of changing this, by enabling researchers to simulate how macro-level patterns emerge from micro-level interactions. By modeling individuals as autonomous agents with heterogeneous characteristics and adaptive behaviors, ABMs offer a way to capture the decentralized, dynamic, and non-linear nature of social systems. This approach is particularly appealing for studying phenomena such as collective action, the diffusion of innovations, political polarization, segregation dynamics, and financial market fluctuations – domains where the emergent dynamics for which traditional analytical methods struggle to account play a central role. ABMs moreover enable exploring how the world could be different, testing how different policy interventions or structural changes might shape societal outcomes.
人类社会涌现于个体之间无数次的互动，然而，社会科学方法往往侧重于总体模式或个体决策，忽视了两者之间关系这一核心问题 Byrne and Callaghan (2022)。基于智能体的模型（ABMs）有望改变这一状况，使研究者能够模拟宏观层面的模式如何从微观层面的互动中涌现。通过将个体建模为具有异质性特征和适应性行为的自主智能体，ABMs 提供了一种刻画社会系统去中心化、动态和非线性特征的方法。这种方法对于研究集体行动、创新扩散、政治极化、隔离动态和金融市场波动等现象尤其具有吸引力，因为在这些领域中，传统分析方法难以解释的涌现动态发挥着核心作用。此外，ABMs 还使研究者能够探索世界可能呈现的不同样貌，检验不同的政策干预或结构性变化可能如何影响社会结果。
Despite their theoretical advantages, however, ABMs have historically struggled with widespread adoption within the social sciences. Critics have in particular raised two key objections.
然而，尽管 ABMs 在理论上具有优势，它们在社会科学领域的广泛应用历来面临困难。批评者尤其提出了两项关键异议。
First, the models have been criticized for oversimplifying human behavior by representing individuals as simple rule-followers or optimizers Xi et al. (2023); Epstein (2012). While this approach has been necessary to make computational implementation feasible, it often fails to capture the complexity of human decision-making, which is characterized by more complex reasoning, story-telling, as well as learning, emotions, social norms, and cognitive biases Törnberg and Uitermark (2025). As each model is essentially distinct and a tailor-made representation of a specific social phenomenon Heath et al. (2009), comparisons across models tend to be challenging, limiting the possibility for cumulativity of research findings Fagiolo et al. (2007); Windrum et al. (2007); Ormerod and Rosewell (2006); Epstein and Axtell (1996).
首先，这些模型被批评为将个体表示为简单的规则遵循者或优化者，从而过度简化了人类行为 Xi et al. (2023); Epstein (2012)。尽管这种方法对于使计算实现成为可能是必要的，但它往往无法捕捉人类决策的复杂性，后者涉及更复杂的推理、叙事，以及学习、情绪、社会规范和认知偏差 Törnberg and Uitermark (2025)。由于每个模型本质上都各不相同，都是针对某一特定社会现象量身定制的表征 Heath et al. (2009)，模型之间的比较往往十分困难，从而限制了研究成果累积的可能性 Fagiolo et al. (2007); Windrum et al. (2007); Ormerod and Rosewell (2006); Epstein and Axtell (1996)。
Second, the models have been criticized for lacking clear connections to the empirical world, often reflecting the assumptions of the modeler more than the realities of human behavior Windrum et al. (2007). Rigorously calibrating and validating the models against real-world data has proven challenging due to the models’ complexity and lack of standardization Heath et al. (2009); Windrum et al. (2007). Unlike traditional models with clear parameter estimation techniques, ABMs often involve a large number of assumptions about agent behavior, making empirical validation difficult. Scholars have argued that simulation models must be subjected to rigorous validation if they are to contribute to our understanding of the simulated system Naylor and Finger (1967). The lack of standardized methods for calibrating these models with real-world data has moreover led to concerns about their reliability and reproducibility Helbing (2012). Because ABMs can generate highly detailed and complex outputs, distinguishing between meaningful patterns and overfitting to specific assumptions became a major challenge, as the models can be fit to match nearly any data – in von Neumann’s famous phrasing, “with four parameters I can fit an elephant, with five I can make him wiggle his trunk”. Without rigorous empirical grounding, many ABM studies struggled to move beyond illustrative toy-models, limiting their influence in policy and empirical social science research.
其次，这些模型被批评为与经验世界缺乏明确联系，往往更多地反映建模者的假设，而非人类行为的实际情况 Windrum et al. (2007)。由于这些模型的复杂性和标准化的缺失，依据现实世界数据对其进行严格校准和验证已被证明十分困难 Heath et al. (2009); Windrum et al. (2007)。与具有明确参数估计技术的传统模型不同，ABMs 往往包含大量关于智能体行为的假设，使经验验证变得困难。学者们认为，模拟模型若要有助于我们理解所模拟的系统，就必须接受严格验证 Naylor and Finger (1967)。此外，缺乏利用现实世界数据校准这些模型的标准化方法，也引发了人们对其可靠性和可复现性的担忧 Helbing (2012)。由于 ABMs 能够生成高度详细且复杂的输出，区分有意义的模式与对特定假设的过拟合成为一项重大挑战，因为这些模型经过拟合几乎可以匹配任何数据——用 von Neumann 的名言来说，“用 four 个参数我就能拟合出一头大象，用 five 个参数我就能让它摆动鼻子”。由于缺乏严谨的实证基础，许多 ABM 研究难以超越用于示例说明的玩具模型，从而限制了其在政策研究和实证社会科学研究中的影响力。
The models have hence existed in a fundamental tension between the contradictory aims of realism and explainability, and between their inherent versatility and the demands of validation, calibration, and comparability. As a result of these challenges, the data-driven approach of computational social science came to overshadow ABMs from the 2010s onward, following advancement in machine learning and growing availability of digital social data Conte and Paolucci (2014).
因此，这些模型始终处于一种根本性的张力之中：既要兼顾真实性与可解释性这两个相互矛盾的目标，又要协调其固有的多用性与验证、校准及可比性的要求。由于这些挑战，随着机器学习的进步和数字社会数据日益丰富，从 2010 年代起，计算社会科学的数据驱动方法逐渐盖过了 ABM 的风头 Conte and Paolucci (2014)。
However, recently, ABMs have made an unexpected comeback. Large Language Models (LLMs) have gained significant traction in recent years, enabling computers to mimic human creativity, reasoning, and language-generation. The rise of LLMs quickly led to the idea that LLMs’ capacity to mimic human behavior could be leveraged to simulate social systems, by integrating LLMs with ABMs Törnberg et al. (2023); Park et al. (2023). This promises to solve the challenge of ABMs’ lacking realism, by drawing on LLMs’ human-like reasoning, language generation, and behavior Guo et al. (2024). Such “Generative ABMs” offer several advantages, allowing agents to be equipped with distinct personalities and human-like capabilities. Generative ABMs have already been harnessed in both cooperative – achieving a shared goal - and adverserial – debate and competition – settings Xi et al. (2023), with applications including debating Chan et al. (2023), policy making Xiao et al. (2023); Hua et al. (2023), economy Horton (2023); Li et al. (2023), epidemic modeling Williams et al. (2023), (online) society simulation Park et al. (2022); Park et al. (2023); Gao et al. (2023), psychology Aher et al. (2023); Kovač et al. (2023), gaming (FAIR)† et al. (2022); Xu et al. (2023), software development Qian et al. (2023) and embodied agents Mandi et al. (2024).
然而，最近 ABM 却出人意料地重新兴起。近年来，大语言模型（Large Language Models，LLMs）受到广泛关注，使计算机能够模仿人类的创造力、推理能力和语言生成能力。LLMs 的兴起很快催生了这样一种想法：通过将 LLMs 与 ABM 相结合，可以利用 LLMs 模仿人类行为的能力来模拟社会系统 Törnberg et al. (2023); Park et al. (2023)。通过利用 LLMs 类似人类的推理、语言生成和行为能力，这种方法有望解决 ABM 缺乏真实性的问题 Guo et al. (2024)。这种“生成式 ABM”具有多项优势，能够赋予智能体鲜明的个性和类似人类的能力。生成式 ABM 已被用于合作情境——实现共同目标——和对抗情境——辩论与竞争——Xi et al. (2023)，其应用包括辩论 Chan et al. (2023)、政策制定 Xiao et al. (2023); Hua et al. (2023)、经济 Horton (2023); Li et al. (2023)、流行病建模 Williams et al. (2023)、（在线）社会模拟 Park et al. (2022); Park et al. (2023); Gao et al. (2023)、心理学 Aher et al. (2023); Kovač et al. (2023)、游戏 (FAIR)† et al. (2022); Xu et al. (2023)、软件开发 Qian et al. (2023) 以及具身智能体 Mandi et al. (2024)。
However, while LLMs promise to address the first key challenge of ABMs by making agents more realistic, their implications for the second – rigorous validation and calibration – remain an open question that is central to the future potential of generative ABMs. To address the question of whether generative ABMs enable us to move past the problems that have historically limited the adoption of ABMs, this paper carries out a systematic review of the rapidly growing field of generative ABMs to examine whether and how modelers have dealt with the central challenge of validation. The paper situates generative ABMs in the longer history of ABMs, and the long-standing challenges of validation and calibration. We carry out a systematic review of current research in the field and their validation practices. We find that the issue of validation remains insufficiently addressed in the field, and that it represents a key weakness of the generative ABM literature. Discussing the challenge, we argue that there are reasons to believe that integrating LLMs in ABMs may, in fact, aggravate rather than resolve these challenges, as LLMs are highly complex ‘black-box’ models that intensify the challenges of interpretability, standardization, and replicability Zhao et al. (2023); Luo and Specia (2024).
然而，尽管 LLMs 有望通过使智能体更加逼真来应对 ABM 的第一个关键挑战，但它们对第二个挑战——严谨的验证与校准——有何影响，仍是一个悬而未决的问题，而这一问题对于生成式 ABM 的未来潜力至关重要。为回答生成式 ABM 是否能使我们克服那些在历史上限制 ABM 应用的问题，本文对快速发展的生成式 ABM 领域开展系统综述，以考察建模者是否以及如何应对验证这一核心挑战。本文将生成式 ABM 置于 ABM 更长的历史脉络以及长期存在的验证与校准挑战之中加以考察。我们对该领域的现有研究及其验证实践开展系统综述。我们发现，该领域对验证问题的处理仍不充分，这构成了生成式 ABM 文献的一项关键弱点。在讨论这一挑战时，我们认为，有理由相信，将 LLMs 整合到 ABM 中，实际上可能会加剧而非解决这些挑战，因为 LLMs 是高度复杂的“黑箱”模型，会进一步加大可解释性、标准化和可复现性方面的挑战 Zhao et al. (2023); Luo and Specia (2024)。
2 Agent-Based Modeling in the Social Sciences2 社会科学中的基于智能体的建模
Imagine a murmuration of starlings. Collectively, they form a fluid cloud, moving as a single organism. Yet, there is no “group mind” or leader orchestrating the flight. Each bird simply responds to its neighbors, who in turn respond to it – producing a fluid, nonlinear pattern that appears both choreographed and organic. Modeling this movement from a global perspective – as, say, a set of interacting variables – would be impossible. But, as early computer modelers discovered in the 1980s, if we model the individual birds, then the dynamics of the murmuration will simply emerge from the bottom-up through the aggregation of local interactions Reynolds (1987).
想象一下椋鸟群飞的景象。它们聚在一起，形成一片流动的云，如同一个有机体般移动。然而，并不存在指挥飞行的“群体心智”或领头者。每只鸟只是对邻近的鸟作出反应，而邻近的鸟又反过来对它作出反应——由此形成一种流动的非线性模式，看起来既像经过精心编排，又浑然天成。从全局视角对这种运动建模——例如，将其表示为一组相互作用的变量——是不可能的。但是，正如早期计算机建模者在 1980 年代所发现的，如果我们对单只鸟进行建模，那么群飞的动态便会通过局部互动的汇聚，自下而上地自然涌现 Reynolds (1987)。
ABMs take the same approach to studying human social dynamics, treating social systems as consisting of ‘agents’ that perceive their environment, interact and take actions – and casting society as a form of murmuration: a global pattern that emerges through individual interaction Russell and Norvig (2016). Traditional sociological methods view society as a hierarchical system where institutions and norms shape individual behavior from the top down, captured through a set of variables. ABMs, by contrast, explore how complex social patterns emerge from decentralized interactions among many agents. Instead of imposing top-down explanations, ABMS view social structures, norms, and institutions as emerging from the interactions of individuals. This shift views human groups as being similar to ant-hills or bird flocks: they are complex systems; nonlinear, path-dependent, and self-organizing. and enables studying how these dynamics shape social phenomena – which is often impossible to achieve using traditional methods Törnberg and Uitermark (2025). ABMs allow examining how macro-patterns may unexpectedly emerge from underlying micro-level mechanisms. At the same time, they only provide sufficient explanations, not necessary ones, as the same macro-phenomenon can be explained by several different micro-level mechanisms.
ABM 采用同样的方法研究人类社会动态，将社会系统视为由能够感知环境、相互互动并采取行动的“智能体”构成，并将社会描绘为一种群飞现象：通过个体互动而涌现的全局模式 Russell and Norvig (2016)。传统社会学方法将社会视为一个层级系统，其中制度与规范自上而下地塑造个体行为，并通过一组变量来刻画这一过程。相比之下，ABM 探索复杂的社会模式如何从众多智能体之间的分散互动中涌现。ABMS 不强加自上而下的解释，而是将社会结构、规范和制度视为个体互动的涌现结果。这一转向将人类群体视为类似于蚁群或鸟群的存在：它们是具有非线性、路径依赖和自组织特征的复杂系统；这一转向也使人们能够研究这些动态如何塑造社会现象，而使用传统方法往往无法做到这一点 Törnberg and Uitermark (2025)。ABMs 使研究者能够考察宏观模式如何从底层的微观机制中意外涌现。与此同时，它们只能提供充分解释，而非必要解释，因为同一宏观现象可以由多种不同的微观机制来解释。
ABM as a social scientific method traces back to the early 1970s, with Thomas Schelling’s influential models of social segregation Schelling (1971). The method however saw rapid growth as the rising availability of computers made modeling accessible to a broad range of researchers. In the 1990s and early 2000s, ABMs grew substantially, together with the broader field of complexity science. The method developed its own journals (e.g., the Journal of Artificial Societies and Social Simulation, JASSS) and scientific association (The European Social Simulation Association, ESSA).
ABM 作为一种社会科学方法，可以追溯到 1970 年代初 Thomas Schelling 提出的具有深远影响的社会隔离模型 Schelling (1971)。然而，随着计算机日益普及，广大研究者都能够开展建模，这一方法也随之迅速发展。在 1990 年代和 2000 年代初，ABMs 与更广泛的复杂性科学领域共同取得了长足发展。这一方法发展出了自己的期刊（例如《人工社会与社会模拟杂志》，JASSS）和学术协会（欧洲社会模拟协会，ESSA）。
From 2010 onward, however, ABM and complexity science stalled, challenged by the growth of Computational Social Science and the broader emphasis on data analysis over generative explanation Conte and Paolucci (2014).
然而，自 2010 年起，ABM 和复杂性科学的发展陷入停滞，受到计算社会科学兴起以及更广泛的重数据分析、轻生成性解释取向的挑战 Conte and Paolucci (2014)。
ABMs faced growing criticism for being empirically untethered to the systems being represented, reflecting the ad hoc intuitions of the modeler rather than the dynamics of the real world. The underlying rules governing the actions of the agents would often be simply assumed by the modeler based on what seemed to them to make sense, often without a clear link to neither empirical data nor existing social scientific theory Windrum et al. (2007). While such an approach may have been acceptable in a situation of data scarcity, the growing availability of digital data in the 2010s intensified demands that ABMs should be calibrated and validated against the real world Naylor and Finger (1967); Ormerod and Rosewell (2006); Ziems et al. (2024); Macy and Willer (2002). Critics revived long-standing arguments that models that have not been subjected to proper validation are “void of meaning” and contribute nothing to the understanding of the simulated system Naylor and Finger (1967). Without rigorous empirical grounding, ABMs remain mere toy-models or illustrations, limiting their usefulness for contributing to social science research, and their capacity to influence policy.
ABMs 受到越来越多的批评，被认为在经验层面脱离了其所表征的系统，反映的是建模者的临时性直觉，而非现实世界的动态。支配智能体行动的底层规则往往只是建模者根据自己认为合理的情况作出的假设，通常既未与经验数据建立明确联系，也未与现有社会科学理论建立明确联系 Windrum et al. (2007)。尽管这种做法在数据匮乏的情况下或许可以接受，但 2010 年代数字数据的日益丰富，使人们愈发要求依据现实世界对 ABMs 进行校准和验证 Naylor and Finger (1967); Ormerod and Rosewell (2006); Ziems et al. (2024); Macy and Willer (2002)。批评者重申了长期以来的一种观点，即未经适当验证的模型“毫无意义”，对理解所模拟的系统没有任何贡献 Naylor and Finger (1967)。如果缺乏严谨的经验基础，ABMs 就仍然只是玩具模型或示意性模型，这限制了它们对社会科学研究的贡献，也限制了它们影响政策的能力。
As journals increasingly began requiring ABMs to be calibrated and validated against real-world data, this often turned out to be a tall order. As ABMs seek to capture emergent outcomes from large numbers of interacting agents, they are themselves complex systems Silverman et al. (2009). While this property is necessary for the models to capture social complexity, it also means that the models display many of the pernicious properties that make complex systems challenging to study: the models are often mathematically chaotic, and hence sensitive to initial conditions Bertolotti et al. (2020), making them challenging to reproduce. They are moreover often high-dimensional, with their many degrees of freedom resulting in the so-called ‘curse of dimensionality’ De Marchi (2005). This dimensionality furthermore makes means that ABMs are computationally costly: the agent interactions tend to scale quadratically with the number of agents, whereas sensitivity analyses scale exponentially with the number of parameters.
随着越来越多的期刊开始要求依据现实世界的数据对 ABMs 进行校准和验证，这一要求往往被证明很难满足。由于 ABMs 试图捕捉大量相互作用的智能体所产生的涌现结果，它们本身也是复杂系统 Silverman et al. (2009)。尽管模型必须具备这一属性才能捕捉社会复杂性，但这也意味着模型表现出许多使复杂系统难以研究的棘手特性：模型往往具有数学意义上的混沌性，因而对初始条件敏感 Bertolotti et al. (2020)，这使其难以复现。此外，它们往往具有高维性，其众多自由度导致了所谓的“维度灾难” De Marchi (2005)。这种高维性还意味着 ABMs 的计算成本高昂：智能体之间的交互通常随智能体数量呈平方增长，而敏感性分析的计算量则随参数数量呈指数增长。
The flexibility and lack of standardization means that few design constraints are imposed by ABMs. As a result, every model is specifically tailored for its intended purpose, making models difficult to compare Fagiolo et al. (2007). The lack of comparability represents an impediment to the type of universal methodologies and benchmarks that are crucial for achieving cumulativity of research findings, as it is otherwise unclear how the findings of one model speak to those of another model, let alone to dynamics in the real world. As a result, the field of ABMs faced a “replication crisis”, as studies found that the models could almost never be replicated – and that some of the findings in the field turned out to be the results of software bugs Wilensky and Rand (2007).
灵活性和标准化的缺乏意味着 ABMs 对模型设计施加的约束很少。因此，每个模型都是为其预定用途专门定制的，这使模型之间难以比较 Fagiolo et al. (2007)。缺乏可比性阻碍了通用方法和基准的建立，而这些方法和基准对于实现研究发现的累积至关重要，因为否则就无法明确一个模型的发现与另一个模型的发现有何关联，更不用说它们与现实世界动态的关联了。因此，ABMs 领域面临了一场“复现危机”，因为研究发现，这些模型几乎从未能够被复现，而且该领域的一些发现最终被证明是软件缺陷造成的结果 Wilensky and Rand (2007)。
The lack of empirical grounding implies the need for the models to be highly convincing representations of the systems they simulate. However, social scientists have remained skeptical of the capacity of the often simple rule-based models to represent human behavior. The theoretical descriptions of ABMs as consisting of intelligent, adaptive, and goal-seeking agents that dynamically shape and respond to their environment while taking into account past events Epstein and Axtell (1997) stood in often stark contrast to their realities as simple rule-followers based on a series of “if-then” statements or simple optimization. Using such simple models, it is hard to convincingly argue that the models encompass the full complexity of human decision-making, in particular as the role of beliefs, memories, story-telling, and past interactions are rarely accounted for Sargent (2010). While ABMs do represent improvements over the atomized perfect optimizers of neoclassical theory, they remain part of the same broader methodologically individualist paradigm Törnberg (2018); Byrne and Callaghan (2022).
经验基础的缺乏意味着，模型需要能够极具说服力地表征其所模拟的系统。然而，社会科学家始终怀疑这些通常较为简单的基于规则的模型是否有能力表征人类行为。理论上，ABMs 被描述为由具有智能、适应能力和目标追求能力的智能体构成，这些智能体在考虑过去事件的同时，动态地塑造并响应其环境 Epstein and Axtell (1997)；但在现实中，它们只是依据一系列“if-then”语句或简单优化来遵循规则的执行者，两者往往形成鲜明对比。使用如此简单的模型，很难令人信服地论证这些模型涵盖了人类决策的全部复杂性，尤其是因为它们很少考虑信念、记忆、讲故事以及过往互动的作用 Sargent (2010)。尽管基于主体的模型（ABMs）相较于新古典理论中原子化的完全最优化者有所改进，但它们仍属于同一个更广泛的方法论个人主义范式 Törnberg (2018); Byrne and Callaghan (2022)。
Many social scientists have furthermore found it challenging to integrate ABMs with existing social scientific theory. In part due to their simplicity, the explanations offered by ABMs often seemed to be fundamentally at odds with existing social scientific explanations, as they cast any social phenomenon as simply ‘emerging’ from bottom-up interactions Macy and Willer (2002). Such reductive explanations tend to come with a hint of the same political biases as neoclassical models, through the lens of which the market appears as ‘natural’, while the top-down role of institutions are viewed as imposing unnatural constraints Baker (2022). The widely lauded Schelling Schelling (1971) segregation model, for instance, seemed to suggest that segregation naturally “emerged” from an innate mutual dislike of the opposing group, while disregarding dominant explanations like structural racism, white flight, and red-lining – thereby also seeming to eradicate the possibility of collective solutions to address the problem.
此外，许多社会科学家发现，将 ABMs 与现有社会科学理论相结合具有挑战性。部分由于模型本身较为简单，ABMs 提供的解释往往似乎与现有社会科学解释存在根本冲突，因为它们将任何社会现象都描述为仅仅从自下而上的互动中“涌现”出来 Macy and Willer (2002)。这类还原式解释往往带有与新古典模型相似的政治偏见色彩：从这一视角来看，市场似乎是“自然的”，而制度自上而下的作用则被视为施加了非自然的约束 Baker (2022)。例如，广受赞誉的 Schelling Schelling (1971) 隔离模型似乎表明，隔离是由群体之间对彼此与生俱来的反感自然“涌现”而来的，却忽视了结构性种族主义、白人外迁和划定红线等主流解释，因此似乎也抹除了通过集体解决方案应对这一问题的可能性。
As a result of these challenges, together with the growing capacities of machine learning and AI to produce insights from the increasingly abundant digital social data, the social data analytics and computational social science came to overshadow ABMs from the 2010s onward Conte and Paolucci (2014).
由于这些挑战，加之机器学习和 AI 从日益丰富的数字社会数据中获得洞见的能力不断增强，自 2010s 起，社会数据分析和计算社会科学逐渐盖过了 ABMs 的风头 Conte and Paolucci (2014)。
2.1 Generative ABMs2.1 生成式 ABMs
The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) has recently marked an unexpected shift in the fortunes of ABMs. While ABM research had already experimented with using deep learning-based approaches to modeling human behavior Xi et al. (2023), LLMs offer an entirely new approach to represent human behavior. As LLMs are trained to mimic human language and reasoning, they appear in many ways to be out-of-the-box models of humans. In contrast to traditional ABMs Zhao et al. (2023), such “generative agents” are trained on vast amounts of information, giving them an internal world-model that allows them to generalize to new problems and mimic human reasoning. Generative agents can furthermore understand and generate natural language in ways indistinguishable from humans Jones and Bergen (2024).
近期，大语言模型（LLMs）的出现标志着 ABMs 的发展迎来了意想不到的转折。尽管 ABM 研究此前已经尝试使用基于深度学习的方法对人类行为进行建模 Xi et al. (2023)，但 LLMs 提供了一种表征人类行为的全新方法。由于 LLMs 经过训练以模仿人类的语言和推理，它们在许多方面似乎是开箱即用的人类模型。与传统 ABMs 不同 Zhao et al. (2023)，这类“生成式主体”使用海量信息进行训练，从而具备内部世界模型，使其能够泛化至新问题并模仿人类推理。此外，生成式主体能够以与人类无法区分的方式理解和生成自然语言 Jones and Bergen (2024)。
These capacities were first explored in a 2023 paper using LLMs to simulate human behavior in an The Sims-like artificial world, finding that the generative agents would begin to reproduce human-like behavior and social dynamics Park et al. (2022). Following this, the field of generative ABMs exploded in growth, making it subject to several reviews seeking to survey the emerging field Guo et al. (2024); Xi et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2024); Cheng et al. (2024); Mou et al. (2024).
这些能力首次在一篇 2023 年的论文中得到探索，该论文使用 LLMs 在一个类似 The Sims 的人工世界中模拟人类行为，发现生成式主体会开始再现类似人类的行为和社会动态 Park et al. (2022)。此后，生成式 ABMs 领域呈爆发式增长，已有多篇综述试图梳理这一新兴领域 Guo et al. (2024); Xi et al. (2023); Wang et al. (2024); Cheng et al. (2024); Mou et al. (2024)。
The agents used in the ABMs are often provided with distinct personas, consisting of, for example, demographic, personal and social information, that is either hand-crafted, generated by AI, or based on real-world datasets. The agents are often given memory modules in which meaningful information can be stored and retrieved. Planning modules allow agents to reason and act according to self-generated plans. Based on these modules, agents are able to take actions to change the state of the environment (e.g. changing the state of a toilet to ‘occupied’), alter their internal states, or communicate with other agents.
ABMs 中使用的主体通常被赋予各不相同的角色设定，其中包括人口统计信息、个人信息和社会信息等；这些设定或由人工编写，或由 AI 生成，或基于现实世界的数据集构建。主体通常配有记忆模块，用于存储和检索有意义的信息。规划模块使主体能够依据自行生成的计划进行推理和行动。借助这些模块，主体能够采取行动改变环境状态（例如，将厕所的状态改为“占用”）、改变自身内部状态，或与其他主体交流。
Generative agents thus seem to promise to resolve the problem of ABMs’ lacking realism, by making it trivial to create simulations with agents that can remember, reason, argue, engage in realistic conversations, and achieve “believable human behavior” Park et al. (2022). The substantial excitement surrounding generative ABMs is hence understandable.
因此，生成式主体似乎有望解决 ABMs 缺乏现实性的问题，因为它们使创建模拟变得轻而易举，其中的主体能够记忆、推理、争论、开展逼真的对话，并实现“可信的人类行为” Park et al. (2022)。因此，围绕生成式 ABMs 的强烈热情是可以理解的。
At the same time, it would seem that the question of calibration and validation that has long haunted ABMs remains potentially exacerbated by the introduction of LLMs. We can point to three interrelated ways in which LLMs may aggravate the existing challenges of ABM validation.
与此同时，长期困扰 ABMs 的校准与验证问题似乎仍然存在，而且可能因 LLMs 的引入而加剧。我们可以指出，LLMs 可能从三个相互关联的方面加剧 ABM 验证面临的现有挑战。
First, LLMs are ‘black-box’ models: their capacities are emergent, and it is virtually impossible to understand why a particular model gives a particular output. Language models rely on large-scale neural networks, and their output is the result of billions of parameters Zini and Awad (2022). The more advanced the network gets, the less we know exactly what is going on in its decision-making process. LLMs are furthermore stochastic – the same input can lead to different outputs – making validating and reproducing results potentially challenging.
首先，LLMs 是“黑箱”模型：它们的能力是涌现的，而且几乎不可能理解某个特定模型为何给出某个特定输出。语言模型依赖大规模神经网络，其输出是数十亿个参数共同作用的结果 Zini and Awad (2022)。网络越先进，我们就越难确切了解其决策过程中究竟发生了什么。此外，LLMs 具有随机性——相同的输入可能产生不同的输出——这可能使结果的验证和复现面临挑战。
Second, the models cannot be trusted to accurately represent social groups or impersonate human behavior. While models are given descriptions of individuals to impersonate, studies have found that the models are poor at representing groups and their attributes, often engaging in exaggerated stereotypes rather than accurate representations. This is intimately associated with the hotly debated issue of LLM bias Ferrara (2023). Specifically, we can point to two types of bias that pose challenges to validation of generative ABMs: social bias and selection bias Navigli et al. (2023). Social bias refers to the discrimination, stereotypes, or prejudices against certain groups of people, which may mean that models do not accurately represent external groups, but rather engage in problematic stereotypes. Selection bias refers to the choice of texts that make up a training corpus, which means that LLMs may reproduce historical events or complete social network interactions, basing simulations on prior knowledge instead of dynamically producing the dynamics as intended by the modelers.
其次，不能相信这些模型能够准确表征社会群体或模仿人类行为。尽管模型会获得其所需扮演的个体的描述，但研究发现，模型并不擅长表征群体及其属性，往往会呈现夸大的刻板印象，而非准确的表征。这与备受争议的 LLM 偏见问题密切相关 Ferrara (2023)。具体而言，我们可以指出两类对生成式 ABMs 的验证构成挑战的偏差：社会偏见和选择偏差 Navigli et al. (2023)。社会偏见是指针对某些人群的歧视、刻板印象或成见，这可能意味着模型无法准确表征外部群体，而是呈现有问题的刻板印象。选择偏差是指构成训练语料库的文本在选择上存在的偏差，这意味着 LLMs 可能会重现历史事件或完整的社交网络互动，依据先验知识开展模拟，而非按照建模者的意图动态生成相应的演化过程。
Third, as LLMs are probabilistic next-word predictors, they possess no internal mechanism to validate the correctness of their outputs. They may hence generate outputs that are factually incorrect or nonsensical – a phenomenon referred to as ‘hallucination’ Huang et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2023). This could potentially lead to unexpected effects, or hard-to-solve problems in the internal workings of models, as the outputs may not be coherent with agents’ memories or personas (e.g. an agent ‘recalling’ events that it never experienced), leading to unexpected outcomes.
第三，由于 LLMs 是基于概率的下一词预测器，它们并不具备验证自身输出正确性的内部机制。因此，它们可能生成事实错误或毫无意义的输出，这种现象被称为“幻觉”（hallucination）Huang et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2023)。这可能产生意料之外的影响，或在模型的内部运行中造成难以解决的问题，因为输出可能与智能体的记忆或角色设定不一致（例如，智能体“回忆起”自己从未经历过的事件），从而导致意料之外的结果。
2.2 Validation in ABMs2.2 ABMs 中的验证
Before turning to reviewing how validation has been handled in the field of Generative ABMs, we will first draw on the literature on validation in ABMs to provide a classification of types of validation that have been discussed and applied. Validation is the process of assessing the degree to which a model is an accurate representation of the real world Ormerod and Rosewell (2006). Sargent Sargent (2010) argues that the aim of validation should be ‘operational validity’: “determining whether the simulation model’s output behavior has the accuracy required for the model’s intended purpose over the domain of the model’s intended applicability”. Models are always abstractions, and it is necessary to bracket part of the system being modeled. ‘Realism’ is, in other words, neither achievable nor desirable.
在回顾生成式 ABMs 领域如何开展验证之前，我们将首先借鉴有关 ABMs 验证的文献，对其中讨论和应用过的验证类型进行分类。验证是评估模型在多大程度上准确表征现实世界的过程 Ormerod and Rosewell (2006)。Sargent Sargent (2010) 认为，验证的目标应当是“操作有效性”（operational validity）：“确定仿真模型在其预期适用范围内的输出行为，是否具备模型预期用途所要求的准确性”。模型始终是抽象的，因此有必要将所建模系统的一部分暂时排除在考虑范围之外。换言之，“逼真性”既无法实现，也并非理想目标。
First, the literature distinguishes between internal and external validation methods. External validation makes use of external resources for validation, such as real-world data, well-studied behavior, or humans for judgment. Internal validation is carried out to validate the internal consistency or to do internal observations of the model, for example by monitoring the system and reasoning about the observations or performing a sensitivity analysis Christopher Frey and Patil (2002).
首先，文献区分了内部验证方法和外部验证方法。外部验证利用外部资源进行验证，例如现实世界的数据、经过充分研究的行为，或由人类作出判断。内部验证旨在验证模型的内部一致性，或对模型进行内部观察，例如通过监测系统并对观察结果进行推理，或开展敏感性分析 Christopher Frey and Patil (2002)。
Second, we can distinguish between ‘subjective’ and ‘objective’ validation. The former includes techniques like human judgment or basic observations of the system in the form of traces, graphics and performance indicators. One influential example of subjective validation is the Turing Test, in which individuals are asked if they can discriminate between human and model outputs Turing (2009). Objective validation encompasses techniques such as comparing with real-world data or similar models with the use of statistical tests. One of the earliest papers on model validation proposes a multi-stage validation approach of first developing the model’s assumptions, then validating where possible by empirically testing them, and lastly comparing the input-output relations of the model to the real system Naylor and Finger (1967).
其次，我们可以区分“主观”验证和“客观”验证。前者包括人工判断，以及通过运行轨迹、图形和性能指标对系统进行基本观察等技术。主观验证的一个有影响力的例子是图灵测试，其中受试者被问及能否区分人类输出与模型输出 Turing (2009)。客观验证涵盖了运用统计检验将模型与现实世界的数据或类似模型进行比较等技术。最早讨论模型验证的论文之一提出了一种多阶段验证方法：首先形成模型的假设，然后在可能的情况下通过实证检验验证这些假设，最后将模型的输入—输出关系与真实系统的输入—输出关系进行比较 Naylor and Finger (1967)。
These two dimensions allow us to divide validation techniques into four categories. For example, using human judges would be subjective and external, whereas a sensitivity analysis would be objective and internal.
这两个维度使我们能够将验证技术划分为四类。例如，使用人工评判属于主观且外部的验证，而敏感性分析则属于客观且内部的验证。

3.1 The target system3.1 目标系统
To answer RQ1, we distinguish between simulating individual and group behavior. Papers that seek to reproduce individual behavior focus on the actions of a single agent, for example if decisions are congruent with a given profile. Other papers search for group behavior at the macro-level of the simulation, like the formation of the network through connections, or the propagation of information through the network. For individual behavior, we further distinguish between the following aspects of the simulated phenomenon, which were identified inductively through an initial analysis:
为回答 RQ1，我们区分了对个体行为的模拟和对群体行为的模拟。试图复现个体行为的论文关注单个智能体的行动，例如其决策是否与给定的个体特征相符。其他论文则探究模拟中宏观层面的群体行为，例如通过建立连接形成网络，或信息在网络中的传播。对于个体行为，我们进一步区分了模拟现象的以下几个方面，这些方面是通过初步分析归纳得出的：
Alignment with profile: the alignment of the agent’s actions with their personality, memories and profile, as well as the specific situation.
与个体特征的一致性：智能体的行动与其人格、记忆和个体特征以及具体情境的一致程度。
Emotion: the emotions expressed by the agent given a specific situation or action.
情绪：智能体在特定情境或行动下表达的情绪。
Conversations and content generation: the communication and dialogue between agents.
对话与内容生成：智能体之间的交流与对话。
Social awareness: the awareness of others and of social norms.
社会意识：对他人和社会规范的认知。
Decision making and reasoning: the decision making and reasoning capabilities.
决策与推理：决策和推理能力。
Opinion and attitude: the opinion or attitude towards some event, and possibly the shift in opinion or attitude.
观点与态度：对某一事件的观点或态度，以及可能发生的观点或态度转变。
For group behavior, we used the following three categories:
对于群体行为，我们采用了以下三个类别：
Network propagation: the propagation of something through the network, such as (mis)information, emotion, attitudes, or cultural elements.
网络传播：某种事物在网络中的传播，例如信息（或错误信息）、情绪、态度或文化要素。
Network structure: the structure of the network, for example the network degree distribution, density or some other network structure attribute.
网络结构：网络的结构，例如网络的度分布、密度或其他网络结构属性。
Social dynamics: phenomena that emerge from individuals actions, for example a ’herd effect’
社会动态：由个体行动涌现的现象，例如“羊群效应”
Some papers focus on multiple phenomena are thus given multiple categorizations. A paper is classified as focusing on a certain type of behavior only if the paper sets out to model that behavior, thus excluding observed phenomena that are noted only as an aside. For example, Kaiya et al Kaiya et al. (2023) set out to measure social reasoning, but note some human-like emotions and reactions.
一些论文关注多种现象，因此被归入多个类别。只有当论文明确以某类行为的建模为目标时，才将其归为关注该类行为的论文，因此，仅被顺带提及的观察现象不在分类依据之列。例如，Kaiya 等人 Kaiya et al. (2023) 旨在测量社会推理能力，但也提到了一些类似人类的情绪和反应。
3.2 Validation Techniques3.2 验证技术
For RQ2, we draw on existing categorizations in the literature on ABM validation. We hence separate between internal and external validation, as well as between subjective and objective validation. We expand on the resulting four categories based on the initial analysis, and arrive at the following five categories of validation:
针对 RQ2，我们借鉴了 ABM 验证相关文献中已有的分类。因此，我们区分内部验证与外部验证，以及主观验证与客观验证。我们基于初步分析，对由此得到的四个类别加以扩展，最终形成以下五类验证：
Validation based on human(-like) judgment [External / Subjective]: using input from researchers, experts, crowd-workers or even LLMs to judge the alignment of the model with human behavior
基于人类（或类人）判断的验证 [外部 / 主观]：利用研究人员、专家、众包工作者甚至 LLMs 提供的意见，判断模型与人类行为的一致程度
Validation against well-known social patterns [External / Subjective]: comparing observations of the model to well-established phenomena in human behavior, although not quantitatively
参照已知社会模式的验证 [外部 / 主观]：将模型的观测结果与人类行为中已得到充分证实的现象进行比较，但不进行定量比较
Validation against similar models [External / Subjective or Objective]: comparing subjectively or objectively with the results and behavior of earlier models, such as traditional ABMs.
参照相似模型的验证 [外部 / 主观或客观]：与先前模型（如传统 ABMs）的结果和行为进行主观或客观比较。
Validation against human-generated data [External / Objective]: comparing to collected human behavioral data, such as social media interactions, human annotations or earlier experiments
参照人类生成数据的验证 [外部 / 客观]：与收集到的人类行为数据进行比较，如社交媒体互动、人工标注或先前的实验数据
Validation based on internal consistency [Internal / Objective]: evaluating the internal coherence and consistency of the system, for example through sensitivity analysis.
基于内部一致性的验证 [内部 / 客观]：评估系统内部的连贯性与一致性，例如通过敏感性分析。
We distinguish between primary and secondary validation techniques. Primary techniques are the most important in the validation process of the paper, while secondary techniques are used merely to lend supportive evidence.
我们区分主要验证技术与次要验证技术。主要技术在论文的验证过程中最为重要，而次要技术仅用于提供辅助证据。

An important aspect of human behavior which modelers seek to reproduce is the way human actions are shaped by their memories, personality traits, and experiences. We may refer to this as profile alignment. Systematically validating profile alignment however represents a challenge, as it not clear what constitutes the ‘correct’ agent action based on their profiles and specific situations. Ground truth is often challenging or nearly impossible to access, as it would require comparing the model agent’s behavior against the specific person being modelled. Park et al Park et al. (2023) assess the validity by interviewing agents on their self-knowledge, memory, plans, reactions, and reflections, and judging the answers ’believability’ based on the alignment with the personality and memories of the agents as well as with the environment. (2025) also search for believability of their agents’ behavior and functioning of the memory system. Hua et al Hua et al. (2023), which use LLM agents to simulate the interactions between conflicting countries, evaluate the congruence, stability, and rationality of actions with respect to the country profile in a war situation using expert assessment. Altera.ai Altera.al et al. (2024) deployed LLM agents in a Minecraft session, and sought to validate profile alignment by monitoring the specialization into distinct roles that should align with the profile of the agent, which in turn should align with the agent’s actions. Jarrett et al Jarrett et al. (2023) create ‘digital representatives’ of individuals, which are validated based on their alignment with the preferences of the individuals they substitute in collective decision-making scenarios. Validations of this category can be focused on replicating the change of attitude or opinion in reaction to a certain event. Gao et al Gao et al. (2023) validate their agents’ attitudes (positive/negative) towards posts on social media against real-world social media data. Wang et al Wang et al. (2023) tested whether they are able to predict if certain activities satisfy basic needs of fullness, social, fun, health, and energy, thus measuring the attitude towards events and the effect on the individual agents.
两篇论文重点研究了如何使用生成式智能体模拟人类的态度或观点。这类验证可以侧重于复现针对某一事件的态度或观点变化。Gao et al Gao et al. (2023) 将其智能体对社交媒体帖子的态度（正面/负面）与真实世界的社交媒体数据进行对照验证。Wang et al Wang et al. (2023) 测试了其是否能够预测某些活动能否满足饱腹、社交、娱乐、健康和精力方面的基本需求，从而衡量对事件的态度以及事件对各个智能体的影响。
Network propagation网络传播
Network propagation was a key focus of study, with 8 out of 29 papers carrying out some form of validation of network propagation. The validation of these systems are often concerned with measuring the spread of something, like information or attitudes, and comparing this with real-world human behavioral patterns.
网络传播是研究的一个重点，29 篇论文中有 8 篇对网络传播进行了某种形式的验证。对这些系统的验证通常关注衡量信息或态度等事物的传播，并将其与真实世界中的人类行为模式进行比较。
Park et al Park et al. (2023) focused on the diffusion of information through the network. Gao et al Gao et al. (2023) measure three forms of propagation on social media: information, emotion, and attitudes. Li et al. Li et al. (2024) and Liu et al Liu et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2024) simulate the spread of misinformation in a social media network structure. Yang et al. (2024) reported on the spread of all forms of information. Altera.ai Altera.al et al. (2024) track the spread of cultural memes and religion in a network, and Ren et al Ren et al. (2024) seek to model the spread of social norms through a social network.
Park et al Park et al. (2023) 关注信息在网络中的扩散。Gao et al Gao et al. (2023) 衡量了社交媒体上的三种传播形式：信息传播、情绪传播和态度传播。Li et al. Li et al. (2024) 和 Liu et al Liu et al. (2024); Liu et al. Network structure网络结构
The structure of the network is mainly determined by the formation of relationships between agents. Here, the goal of the validation is to make sure that the resulting network characteristics match real-world network properties. De Marzo et al De Marzo et al. (2023), for instance, evaluate whether the resulting network structure from their simulation was scale-free, which is a near-universal property of networks emerging on social media. He et al He et al. (2023) examine whether the networks produced on their synthetic social media network, Chirper.ai, show the property of homophily, i.e., the tendency of agents to be more connected with others that are similar to themselves. Park et al Park et al. (2023) and Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) both examine the evolution of social networks in their sandbox town simulation, using metrics such as the degree of nodes, and the density of the network. Hua et al. Hua et al. (2023) assess the historical correctness of the formed alliances and war declarations between countries in a war situation. Gu et al. Gu et al. (2025) try to measure the formation of echo chambers with measures like the modularity, network density, average path length and the clustering coefficient of the network.
网络结构主要由智能体之间关系的形成所决定。这里，验证的目标是确保所形成网络的特征与真实世界网络的属性相匹配。例如，De Marzo et al De Marzo et al. (2023) 评估了其模拟所形成的网络结构是否具有无标度性（scale-free），这是社交媒体上涌现的网络几乎普遍具有的一种属性。He et al He et al. (2023) 考察了其合成社交媒体网络 Chirper.ai 上形成的网络是否表现出同质性（homophily），即智能体倾向于与和自身相似的其他智能体建立更多连接。Park et al Park et al. (2023) 和 Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) 均使用节点度和网络密度等指标，考察了其沙盒小镇模拟中社交网络的演化。Hua et al. Hua et al. Social dynamics社会动态
This final category focuses on papers that seek to reproduce social dynamics, like a flock of starlings collectively forming a fluid cloud. This category encompasses group behavior that emerges from individual actions.
最后这一类别关注试图复现社会动态的论文，例如一群椋鸟集体形成流动云团般的形态。这一类别涵盖由个体行动涌现出的群体行为。
Zhang et al Zhang et al. (2023) claim to find conformity, consensus reaching, and group dynamics behavior in their agent society. The agents in Chen et al Chen et al. (2023) exhibit volunteering, conformity, and destructive behavior when trying to survive in Minecraft. Wang et al. Wang et al. (2025) also find conformity behavior, next to signs of an ’information cocoon’. A similar phenomenon, the formation of echo chambers, was also validated in various papers Gu et al. (2025); Ferraro et al. (2025); Zheng and Tang (2025). Chuang et al. Chuang et al. (2024) simulate the phenomenon of the Wisdom of Partisan Crowds, where the group average moves closer to the ground truth when exposed to the average belief of the partisan group, despite differences in initial belief. Mou et al Mou et al. Orlando et al. Orlando et al. (2025) measure the replication of the ’friendship paradox’, the phenomenon that individuals on average have fewer friends than their friends.
Zhang et al Zhang et al. (2023) 声称在其智能体社会中发现了从众、共识达成和群体动力学行为。Chen et al Chen et al. (2023) 中的智能体在尝试于 Minecraft 中生存时，表现出志愿行为、从众行为和破坏性行为。Wang et al. Wang et al. (2025) 除发现“信息茧房”的迹象外，也发现了从众行为。多篇论文也验证了一种类似的现象，即回音室的形成 Gu et al. (2025); Ferraro et al. (2025); Zheng and Tang (2025)。Chuang et al. Chuang et al. (2024) 模拟了党派群体智慧（Wisdom of Partisan Crowds）现象，即尽管初始信念存在差异，但在接触到党派群体的平均信念后，群体的平均判断会更接近真实值。Mou et al Mou et al. (2024) 使用相对于均值的偏差（偏倚）、相对于均值的标准差（多样性），以及通过动态时间规整（Dynamic Time Warping）和 Pearson 相关性衡量的相似度，来衡量模拟 Twitter 平台 HiSim 上用户的态度分布和平均态度。Yang et al Yang et al. (2025) 衡量了对“友谊悖论”的复现程度，该现象指个体的朋友数量平均而言少于其朋友的朋友数量。

Validation based on human or human-like judgment基于人类或类人判断的验证
One of the most widely used techniques for validation of generative agents is to draw on external or internal judges. The judges can be 1) experts (often in the form of the authors themselves), 2) crowd-workers, or 3) LLMs. For the first category, Hua et al Hua et al. (2023) use field experts to evaluate the congruence and consistency of actions taken by countries with respect to their profile. Li et al Li et al. (2023) use the authors’ own judgment to assess the performance of their MetaAgents to identify suited job-seekers, create a workflow and align with the given role. Wang et al. Wang et al. (2025) make use of humans to asses the believability of the chatting behavior and memory module of the agents.
验证生成式智能体最广泛使用的技术之一，是借助外部或内部评判者。评判者可以是 1) 专家（通常由作者本人担任）、2) 众包工作者，或 3) LLMs。对于第一类，Hua et al Hua et al. (2023) 使用领域专家来评估各国采取的行动与其国家概况的契合程度和一致性。Li et al Li et al. (2023) 依据作者自身的判断，评估其 MetaAgents 在识别合适的求职者、创建工作流程以及符合指定角色要求方面的表现。Wang et al. Wang et al. (2025) 通过人工评估智能体聊天行为和记忆模块的可信程度。
While the use of crowd-workers has been widely criticized both on ethical grounds, for low performance, and for their now wide-spread use of LLMs, crowd-workers remain widely used for validation, often hired through Amazon MTurk. Park et al Park et al. (2023) recruited crowd-workers to score the agents on ‘believability’. Crowd-workers were also used in Park et al Park et al. (2022), to distinguish between synthetic conversations and real conversations for repopulated subreddits. Human annotators were used to evaluate whether generative agents answer Theory of Mind questions correctly in Li et al Li et al. (2023). Several other papers also employ crowd-workers to evalaute whether humans can distinguish human and generative agents on various tasks Ren et al. (2024); Zhou et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2024).
尽管使用众包工作者的做法因伦理问题、表现不佳以及他们如今普遍使用 LLMs 而受到广泛批评，但众包工作者仍被广泛用于验证，且通常通过 Amazon MTurk 招募。Park et al Park et al. (2023) 招募众包工作者，对智能体的“可信程度”进行评分。Park et al Park et al. (2022) 也使用了众包工作者，以区分重新填充内容的 subreddit 中的合成对话与真实对话。Li et al Li et al. (2023) 使用人工标注者来评估生成式智能体是否正确回答了心智理论（Theory of Mind）问题。其他几篇论文也雇用了众包工作者，评估人类能否在各类任务中区分人类与生成式智能体 Ren et al. (2024); Zhou et al. (2023); Zhang et al. (2024)。
While using LLMs to validate the realism of LLMs appears self-evidently problematic, the practice has become increasingly widespread as a means of validation. Zhou et al Zhou et al. (2023) use GPT-4 to validate their model realism. Zhang et al Zhang et al. (2024) use ChatGPT to assess the consistency and coherence of the conversational content. Jarrett et al Jarrett et al. (2023) use an LLM to compare generated critique to a ground-truth. Liu et al. Liu et al. (2024) use both humans and GPT-4 to evaluate the behavior of agents on among other believability, social influence and the naturalness and expressiveness of the content. Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) use GPT-4 to discern whether responses originated from AI or a human, despite previous research suggesting that LLMs may be inappropriate for such tasks Bhattacharjee and Liu (2024).
尽管使用 LLMs 来验证 LLMs 的真实性似乎显然存在问题，但这种做法作为一种验证手段已日益普遍。Zhou et al Zhou et al. (2023) 使用 GPT-4 来验证其模型的真实性。Zhang et al Zhang et al. (2024) 使用 ChatGPT 来评估对话内容的一致性和连贯性。Jarrett et al Jarrett et al. (2023) 使用一个 LLM 将生成的评议与真实基准进行比较。Liu et al. Liu et al. (2024) 同时使用人类和 GPT-4，从可信程度、社会影响力以及内容的自然度和表现力等方面评估智能体的行为。Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) 使用 GPT-4 来辨别回答来自 AI 还是人类，尽管先前研究表明 LLMs 可能不适合此类任务 Bhattacharjee and Liu (2024)。
Validation against well-known social patterns以已知社会模式为参照的验证
Validation can also be carried out by matching the dynamics of the model with well-established real-world patterns. This means that no specific empirical data need to be collected for comparison, as the comparison is made against known social patterns.
也可以通过将模型的动态与现实世界中已得到充分确立的模式进行匹配来开展验证。这意味着无需收集特定的实证数据进行比较，因为比较所依据的是已知的社会模式。
De Marzo et al. De Marzo et al. (2023) examine whether the networks generated show the property of having scale-free degree distributions, which is a well-known and easily measurable property of social media networks. Li et al Li et al. (2024) show that the effect of different personalities and agent attributes on agents’ propensity to share fake news matches earlier studies. Liu et al Liu et al. (2024) conclude that their finding that fake political news propagates faster than other fake news topics is consistent with previous research. In addition, they compare the relationship between the Big Five personalities on agents’ propensity to spread misinformation with prior empirical research on the spread of misinformation. Orlando et al. Orlando et al. (2025) use the degrees of agents to validate the known phenomenon of the ’friendship paradox’.
De Marzo et al. De Marzo et al. (2023) 考察生成的网络是否具有无标度度分布这一特性；这是社交媒体网络中一种广为人知且易于测量的特性。Li et al Li et al. (2024) 表明，不同人格和智能体属性对智能体分享虚假新闻倾向的影响与早期研究一致。Liu et al Liu et al. (2024) 得出结论，他们关于政治类虚假新闻比其他主题的虚假新闻传播更快的发现，与先前研究一致。此外，他们还将大五人格与智能体传播错误信息倾向之间的关系，与先前有关错误信息传播的实证研究进行比较。Orlando et al. Orlando et al. (2025) 使用智能体的度来验证“友谊悖论”这一已知现象。
While some social patterns, such as degree distributions, are easily quantified, many known social patterns are qualitative rather than quantitative, meaning that authors often need to argue for the plausibility of the model in a more narrative form – with varying levels of persuasiveness and rigor. For instance, Zhang et al. Zhang et al. (2023) observe dynamics in their model that they argue corresponds to well-studied behavior in social psychology, such as conformity, consensus-reaching, and group dynamics. Chen et al Chen et al. (2023) similarly observe social dynamics that they argue match established theories within social psychology. Williams et al Williams et al. (2023) argue that the actions taken by agents in response to disease outbreak – such as staying at home and quarantining – share some similarities to the behavior observed during the COVID-19 pandemic. Yang et al Yang et al. (2024) observe the emergence of group polarization in their model, which they argue matches behavior observed in humans.
尽管某些社会模式（如度分布）易于量化，但许多已知的社会模式是定性的而非定量的，这意味着作者往往需要以更具叙述性的形式论证模型的合理性，而这些论证的说服力和严谨程度各不相同。例如，Zhang et al. Zhang et al. (2023) 在其模型中观察到一些动态过程，他们认为这些过程对应于社会心理学中已得到充分研究的行为，如从众、共识达成和群体动力学。Chen et al Chen et al. (2023) 同样观察到一些社会动态过程，并认为这些过程与社会心理学中的既有理论相符。Williams et al Williams et al. (2023) 认为，智能体为应对疾病暴发而采取的行动（如居家和隔离），与 COVID-19 大流行期间观察到的行为具有一定相似性。Yang et al Yang et al. (2024) 在其模型中观察到群体极化的涌现，并认为这与在人类中观察到的行为相符。
Kaiya et al Kaiya et al. (2023) and Altera.ai Altera.al et al. (2024) take an even more loose approach, merely observing the simulated system and claiming that it matches human behavior. Ren et al Ren et al. (2024) take a case study approach, observing their system and interpreting the dynamics as representing the adoption of norms and formation of social conflicts, claiming that it thereby matches real-world social dynamics. Wu et al Wu et al. (2024) find that agents agree to cooperate without explicit directions and report on performance metrics, which were compared against a study on humans in only one of the three experiments. Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) and Park et al Park et al. (2023) argue that the formation of relationships and the spread of information through the network matches expected social dynamics. Mou et al Mou et al. (2024), Zheng et al Zheng and Tang (2025) and Ferraro et al Ferraro et al. (2025) remark that their systems reproduce the known social pattern of echo chambers. Wang et al. Wang et al. (2025) similarly find signs of an ’information cocoon’ and conformity behavior.
Kaiya 等人 Kaiya et al. (2023) 和 Altera.ai Altera.al et al. (2024) 采用了更为宽松的方法，仅观察模拟系统，便声称其与人类行为相符。Ren 等人 Ren et al. (2024) 采用案例研究方法，观察其系统，并将其中的动态解读为规范的采纳和社会冲突的形成，进而声称该系统与现实世界的社会动态相符。Wu 等人 Wu et al. (2024) 发现，智能体在没有明确指示的情况下同意合作，并报告了性能指标，但仅在三个实验中的一个实验中将这些指标与一项人类研究进行了比较。Yu 等人 Yu et al. (2024) 和 Park 等人 Park et al. (2023) 认为，关系的形成和信息在网络中的传播符合预期的社会动态。Mou 等人 Mou et al. (2024)、Zheng 等人 Zheng and Tang (2025) 和 Ferraro 等人 Ferraro et al. (2025) 指出，其系统再现了回音室这一已知的社会模式。Wang 等人 Wang et al. (2025) 同样发现了“信息茧房”和从众行为的迹象。
Validation against similar models通过与类似模型比较进行验证
Generative ABMs can also be validated through comparison with previous models. The catch here is that it is implied that the previous models are already rigorously validated, which circles back to the validation problem that this paper addresses. As a consequence, authors can conclude on the performance compared to the other model, but the alignment with human behavior depends heavily on the quality of the other model. These comparisons again vary in whether it is carried out in a rigorous quantitative way or in a more subjective way that is argued for in narrative form.
生成式基于智能体的模型（ABMs）也可以通过与以往模型比较来进行验证。这里的问题在于，这种做法隐含地假定以往模型已经得到严格验证，从而又回到了本文所探讨的验证问题。因此，作者可以就相对于另一个模型的性能得出结论，但其与人类行为的契合程度在很大程度上取决于另一个模型的质量。这些比较同样存在差异：有些以严格的定量方式进行，有些则采用更为主观的方式，并以叙述形式加以论证。
Zhang et al. (2024) compare the generated conversations of their SpeechAgents with a previous single-agent model. Mou et al Mou et al. (2024) and Gu et al Gu et al. (2025) systematically compare their social media simulations with conventional non-generative ABMs. Liu et al Liu et al. (2024) compare the purchasing behavior of their agents to different filtering algorithms and two other agent based approaches. This system is in turn used by Wang et al Wang et al. (2025) in a comparison with their approach to recommender agents.
Zhang et al. (2024) 将其 SpeechAgents 生成的对话与以往的一个单智能体模型生成的对话进行比较。Mou 等人 Mou et al. (2024) 和 Gu 等人 Gu et al. (2025) 将其社交媒体模拟与传统的非生成式 ABMs 进行了系统比较。Liu 等人 Liu et al. (2024) 将其智能体的购买行为与不同的过滤算法和另外两种基于智能体的方法进行了比较。Wang 等人 Wang et al. (2025) 又使用这一系统，与其推荐智能体方法进行了比较。
Yu et al Yu et al. (2024) offers an example of a more subjective comparison, comparing their model with Park et al’s Park et al. (2023) Generative Agents. The main mode of validation in both papers was the ‘believability’ of the agents’ responses to a set of interview questions. Yu et al Yu et al. (2024), however, offers no quantitative comparison between the two models, but merely includes the responses of agents, concluding that their version did not obviously impair the believability of the agents compared to the original.
Yu 等人 Yu et al. (2024) 提供了一个更为主观的比较示例，将其模型与 Park 等人的 Park et al. (2023) Generative Agents 进行了比较。这两篇论文的主要验证方式都是评估智能体对一组访谈问题的回答是否具有“可信性”。然而，Yu 等人 Yu et al. (2024) 并未对这两个模型进行定量比较，而只是列出了智能体的回答，并据此得出结论：与原版相比，其版本并未明显损害智能体的可信性。
Validation against human-generated data通过与人类生成的数据比较进行验证
One common technique for validation is to quantitatively compare the synthetic data produced by the model with ‘real-world’ data, for instance collected from digital media or from human annotation. This technique is particularly common within social media simulations. Gao et al Gao et al. (2023), Mou et al Mou et al. (2024), Yang et al Yang et al. (2024), Gu et al Gu et al. (2025) and Ferraro et al Ferraro et al. (2025) all based their validation on social media datasets on comparison with data collected from platforms such as Twitter or Reddit. Used metrics include the speed and scale of the spread of information through the network, the stance and attitude of users towards certain events and the similarity of the generated content. In a non-social media system, Hua et al Hua et al. (2023) use historical data to qualitatively assess the accuracy of their war simulation, such as war declarations or alliance formation. Liu et al Liu et al. (2024) use a dataset of Amazon products and reviews, use a part of the purchase history for the initialization of agents and keep the rest as a ground truth for comparison to see to what degree agents make the same purchases.
一种常见的验证技术是，将模型产生的合成数据与“现实世界”数据进行定量比较，例如从数字媒体或人工标注中收集的数据。这种技术在社交媒体模拟中尤为常见。Gao 等人 Gao et al. (2023)、Mou 等人 Mou et al. (2024)、Yang 等人 Yang et al. (2024)、Gu 等人 Gu et al. (2025) 和 Ferraro 等人 Ferraro et al. (2025) 均以社交媒体数据集为验证依据，将模拟数据与从 Twitter 或 Reddit 等平台收集的数据进行比较。所使用的指标包括信息在网络中传播的速度和规模、用户对特定事件的立场和态度，以及生成内容的相似度。在一个非社交媒体系统中，Hua 等人 Hua et al. (2023) 使用宣战或结盟等历史数据，对其战争模拟的准确性进行定性评估。Liu 等人 Liu et al. (2024) 使用了一个包含 Amazon 商品和评论的数据集，将部分购买历史用于智能体的初始化，并将其余部分保留为用于比较的真实基准，以考察智能体在多大程度上会做出相同的购买行为。
Another common approach is to use simulations to reproduce experiments that have previously been carried out with human participants. Papers have been found to compare the results of generative agents on trust games Xie et al. (2024), the Ultimatum Game Sreedhar and Chilton (2024) and tests to measure the effect of the Wisdom of Partisan Crowds Chuang et al. (2024) to previous studies with human subjects.
另一种常见方法是通过模拟来复现此前由人类参与者完成的实验。已有论文将生成式智能体在信任博弈 Xie et al. (2024)、最后通牒博弈 Sreedhar and Chilton (2024) 以及衡量党派群体智慧（Wisdom of Partisan Crowds）效应的测试 Chuang et al. (2024) 中的结果，与此前以人类为受试者的研究进行了比较。
Finally, Wang et al Wang et al. (2023) compare classifications of human annotators with classifications made by the generative ABM on emotions. Zhang et al Zhang et al. (2024) use human annotations as a ground truth to validate generative agent choices of dialogue strategies. Jarrett et al Jarrett et al. (2023) validate the behavior of agents as digital representatives against an annotated dataset of human opinions and critiques in combination with demographic information.
最后，Wang 等人 Wang et al. (2023) 将人工标注者的情绪分类与生成式 ABM 做出的情绪分类进行了比较。Zhang 等人 Zhang et al. (2024) 使用人工标注作为真实基准，验证生成式智能体对对话策略的选择。Jarrett 等人 Jarrett et al. (2023) 将包含人类意见和批评的标注数据集与人口统计信息相结合，用于验证作为数字代表的智能体的行为。
Validation based on internal consistency基于内部一致性的验证
Some researchers seek to validate their models based on their internal consistency, coherence, and stability, for example, through sensitivity analysis. The goal may be to show that the agents’ behavior is stable to changes in system parameters and perturbations. For example, Hua et al Hua et al. (2023) experiment with injecting counterfactual information and de-anonymization of countries to compare with the base approach to see if the results were as expected. De Marzo et al De Marzo et al. (2023) use different prompts for their language model to see if the network structure instead converged to an expected structure, such as a random graph. He et al He et al. (2023) use statistical tests on network metrics to show that the network data can be divided into distinct communities. Sreedhar and Chilton Sreedhar and Chilton (2024) use simulations of agents with different personality traits to evaluate whether the differences between the models align with expectations. While these form of internal tests are relatively common, the extent to which they can claim to represent a plausible validation of the model, rather than just a verification of its basic functioning, is however debatable.
一些研究者试图根据模型的内部一致性、连贯性和稳定性来验证模型，例如通过敏感性分析。其目标可能是表明，智能体的行为在系统参数变化和扰动下保持稳定。例如，Hua et al Hua et al. (2023) 尝试注入反事实信息并对国家进行去匿名化，将结果与基础方法进行比较，以检验结果是否符合预期。De Marzo et al De Marzo et al. (2023) 为其语言模型使用不同的提示，以考察网络结构是否会转而收敛到某种预期结构，例如随机图。He et al He et al. (2023) 对网络指标进行统计检验，以表明网络数据可以划分为不同的社群。Sreedhar and Chilton Sreedhar and Chilton (2024) 对具有不同人格特质的智能体进行仿真，以评估模型之间的差异是否符合预期。尽管这类内部测试相对常见，但它们在多大程度上能够被视为对模型的合理有效性验证，而不仅仅是对其基本功能的核验，仍存在争议。
5 Discussion5 讨论
Based on our review of the way generative ABMs have been employed and validated in the literature, we now turn now to our central question of how and whether the field is sufficiently addressing the long-standing challenges of validation in ABM, as well as the new challenges that emerge from the use of LLMs.
基于对文献中生成式 ABM 的应用与验证方式的回顾，我们现在转向核心问题：该领域是否充分应对了 ABM 验证中长期存在的挑战，以及使用 LLM 所带来的新挑战，又是如何应对这些挑战的。
As we have seen, the most common validation technique is to simply employ on-the-face ‘believability’: 15 out of 35 papers are validated solely through subjective validation, and 22 out of 35 use subjective validation as their only primary technique. 14 papers use the judgment of human ‘experts’, crowd-workers, or LLMs as evaluators. At its best, this consists of the researchers themselves looking at the simulated system and arguing – with varying rigour – that it shares some similarity with human behavior or a social system. This form of validation can also involve using MTurk workers, despite the fact that the low quality of such validation data at this point has been well established Karpinska et al. (2021). At its worst, this form of validation consists of simply asking an LLM to evaluate its own believability. Various papers have doubted the qualities of LLMs for evaluation purposes Wang et al. (2023); Panickssery et al. (2024); Poláková et al. (2024). On the other hand, some have argued that LLMs as evaluators are as good as humans Chan et al. (2023); Zhou et al. (2023), this, however, should be taken as an argument against using human judgment in validation, rather than as an argument for using LLMs. Humans – in particular those without training – are famously poor at judging whether a material is produced by AI, and it is arguably relatively simple to set up an experiment in such a way as to generate a positive outcome with even low-quality results – for instance by asking whether a given text is generated by AI or by humans, rather than by allowing side-by-side comparison between the synthetic and real-world data.
如前所述，最常见的验证技术只是采用表面上的“可信性”：35 篇论文中有 15 篇仅通过主观验证进行验证，35 篇中有 22 篇将主观验证作为唯一的主要技术。14 篇论文使用人类“专家”、众包工作者或 LLM 作为评估者，依赖其判断。在最好的情况下，这种做法是由研究者自己观察仿真系统，并以不同程度的严谨性论证该系统与人类行为或某种社会系统具有一定的相似性。这种验证形式也可能涉及使用 MTurk 工作者，尽管此类验证数据质量低下这一事实如今已得到充分证实 Karpinska et al. (2021)。在最差的情况下，这种验证形式只是让 LLM 评估其自身的可信性。多篇论文对 LLM 用于评估的能力提出了质疑 Wang et al. (2023); Panickssery et al. (2024); Poláková et al. (2024)。另一方面，有人认为 LLM 作为评估者的表现与人类同样出色 Chan et al. (2023); Zhou et al. (2023)，然而，这应被视为反对在验证中使用人类判断的论据，而非支持使用 LLM 的论据。众所周知，人类，尤其是未经训练的人，并不擅长判断材料是否由 AI 生成；而且可以认为，通过实验设计使即便质量低下的结果也能获得正面评价，是相对容易的——例如，询问某段文本是由 AI 还是人类生成的，而不允许将合成数据与现实世界数据并列比较。
When studies do carry out more rigorous quantitative comparison between human-generated and synthetic data, it often quickly becomes clear that the two are substantially less similar than the on-the-face believability may suggest. The style of writing, which tends to be the focus of such comparisons, between zero-shot LLMs and human conversations tends to be quite different: LLM responses are longer, more polite, articulate, and respectful Zhang et al. (2024); Mou et al. (2024). While humans struggle to tell the difference, there are generally substantial syntactical differences between human-generated and synthetic text.
当研究确实对人类生成的数据与合成数据进行更严格的定量比较时，往往很快就会发现，两者的相似程度远低于表面可信性所暗示的程度。这类比较通常侧重于行文风格，而零样本 LLM 与人类对话在这方面往往差异很大：LLM 的回复更长、更有礼貌、表达更清晰，也更尊重对方 Zhang et al. (2024); Mou et al. (2024)。尽管人类难以辨别两者，人类生成的文本与合成文本之间通常存在显著的句法差异。
Yet, from the perspective of the literature on ABM validation, even the few studies that carry out more rigorous quantitative validation against real-world data show some fundamental issues. As mentioned, the notion of operational validity highlights that the purpose of validation is for the model to match the underlying mechanisms of the object over the domain of the model’s intended applicability Sargent (2010). In the case of generative ABMs, it is rarely clear that the model aspects being validated are at all relevant for the model’s mechanisms. Take, for instance, the models seeking to simulate social media platforms. The purpose of such simulation tends to be to test the effect of affordances or algorithms on social dynamics on social media. Yet, the validation tends to focus on whether the synthetic text produced shares some superficial syntactical features with real-world social media text. Arguably, unless the algorithms being tested happen to leverage syntactical features of the text, such aspects are irrelevant for operational validity, which would rather depend on how and when users react to one another, and how these actions in turn interact with the affordances and algorithms of the platform. The sufficiency of the aspects that are being validated for the dynamics of the model must be clearly evidenced.
然而，从 ABM 验证文献的视角来看，即使是少数使用现实世界数据开展更严格定量验证的研究，也存在一些根本性问题。如前所述，运行有效性（operational validity）这一概念强调，验证的目的是使模型在其预期适用范围内与研究对象的底层机制相吻合 Sargent (2010)。对于生成式 ABM，被验证的模型方面究竟是否与模型机制相关，通常并不明确。例如，以那些旨在模拟社交媒体平台的模型为例。此类仿真的目的通常是检验可供性（affordances）或算法对社交媒体上社会动态的影响。然而，验证往往侧重于生成的合成文本是否与现实世界中的社交媒体文本具有某些表层句法特征上的共同点。可以认为，除非被测试的算法恰好利用了文本的句法特征，否则这些方面与运行有效性无关；运行有效性更多取决于用户如何以及何时对彼此作出反应，以及这些行为又如何与平台的可供性和算法相互作用。必须提供明确证据，证明正在验证的各个方面足以支撑模型的动态过程。
Operational validity would moreover often require validating profile alignment for the individuals being simulated. In other words, it would be necessary not only to test that the agent is acting consistently with itself through the simulation, but that it is acting in the same way as the person being simulated – generally, in the included studies, a person described through a prompt persona. How to carry out such validation is however an open question, as rigorous persona alignment would require calibrating and validating against a specific individual and their actions within situation being captured by the model. Doing so would in most cases imply either collecting specific data on individuals through linked surveys or human-participant experiments, or seeking to create what we may call ‘digital human twins’ by calibrating on digital trace data – in turn implying challenging ethical questions.
此外，运行有效性通常还要求验证所模拟个体的画像对齐程度。换言之，不仅需要检验智能体在整个模拟过程中是否保持行为上的自洽，还需要检验其行为方式是否与被模拟者一致——在纳入的研究中，被模拟者通常是通过提示词中的人物设定来描述的。然而，如何开展这种验证仍是一个悬而未决的问题，因为严格的人物设定对齐需要依据某个特定个体及其在模型所刻画情境中的行为进行校准和验证。在大多数情况下，这意味着要么通过相互关联的调查或人类被试实验收集个体的特定数据，要么利用数字踪迹数据进行校准，尝试创建我们可以称为“数字人类孪生体”的对象——而这又会引出棘手的伦理问题。
In existing studies, however, nearly all studies use zero-shot models without finetuning. ‘Calibration’ hence consists of using prompt engineering to achieve on-the-face believability. As a result, it is simply assumed that LLMs will accurately model individuals – despite that it has been shown that the models are poor at representing social groups, not least due to often problematic social bias Boelaert et al. (2024). While some papers do mention the problems of social bias in LLMs, for instance finding that the agents display gender biases in the simulation Xie et al. (2024), the more fundamental question of how this impacts calibration of the model against human behavior is not raised.
然而，在现有研究中，几乎所有研究都使用未经微调的零样本模型。因此，“校准”就是利用提示工程来实现表面上的可信性。结果是，研究直接假定 LLMs 能够准确地模拟个体——尽管已有研究表明，这些模型并不擅长表征社会群体，其中一个重要原因是其往往存在成问题的社会偏见 Boelaert et al. (2024)。虽然一些论文确实提到了 LLMs 中的社会偏见问题，例如发现智能体在模拟中表现出性别偏见 Xie et al. (2024)，但并未提出更根本的问题，即这会如何影响以人类行为为依据的模型校准。
The central feature of ABMs – their inherent flexibility that simultaneously affords their great versatility but also makes them challenging to calibrate, validate, reproduce, and compare, and that make it nearly impossible to achieve cumulativity of findings – does not appear to have in any way been alleviated by the introduction of LLMs, but – if anything – exacerbated. While some of the papers do recognize the challenge implied by the lack of a shared validation framework or benchmarks De Marzo et al. (2023); Kaiya et al. (2023); Chen et al. (2023); Wu et al. (2024); Yu et al. (2024), and some even propose new frameworks Altera.al et al. (2024); Li et al. (2023); Chuang et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2024), such suggestions have been long-standing features of the ABM literature, and the challenge of negotiating the tradeoffs between versatility and standardization is not made easier by the introduction of LLMs. Historically, such frameworks have either seen limited re-use, or do not constraint ABMs enough to achieve standardization.
ABMs 的核心特征是其内在的灵活性：这种灵活性既赋予它们广泛的适用性，也使其难以校准、验证、复现和比较，并使研究发现几乎无法实现累积；引入 LLMs 似乎并未在任何方面缓解这些问题，反而可能加剧了这些问题。虽然一些论文确实认识到缺乏共同验证框架或基准所带来的挑战 De Marzo et al. (2023); Kaiya et al. (2023); Chen et al. (2023); Wu et al. (2024); Yu et al. (2024)，有些甚至提出了新的框架 Altera.al et al. (2024); Li et al. (2023); Chuang et al. (2024); Liu et al. (2024)，但此类建议长期以来就存在于 ABM 文献中，而引入 LLMs 并未使权衡通用性与标准化之间的取舍变得更容易。从历史上看，这类框架要么很少被重复使用，要么对 ABMs 的约束不足以实现标准化。
The introduction of LLMs also supercharges the long-standing challenge of the high computational costs associated to ABMs. While large simulations were always costly, the costs of conventional models appear modest compared to those of LLM-based social simulation. Some studies attempt to mitigate these costs by optimizing agent implementations Kaiya et al. (2023); Yu et al. (2024), leveraging distributed processing Yang et al. (2024) or replacing less critical agents with traditional ABMs Mou et al. (2024). However, these strategies do not change the fundamental reality: generative ABMs are orders of magnitude more computationally demanding than conventional ABMs—already criticized for their high costs. As a result, none of the existing generative ABM studies conduct the necessary sensitivity analysis to assess model reliability and identify key parameters. Even more concerning, most rely on a single model run. Given the inherent stochasticity of generative ABMs, this approach is highly problematic – akin to drawing conclusions from a single-case study.
引入 LLMs 还大幅加剧了 ABMs 长期面临的计算成本高昂这一挑战。虽然大规模模拟的成本一直很高，但与基于 LLMs 的社会模拟相比，传统模型的成本显得相对低廉。一些研究试图通过优化智能体的实现方式 Kaiya et al. (2023); Yu et al. (2024)、利用分布式处理 Yang et al. (2024)，或用传统 ABMs 替代较不关键的智能体 Mou et al. (2024)，来降低这些成本。然而，这些策略并未改变一个根本事实：生成式 ABMs 的计算需求比传统 ABMs 高出数个数量级，而后者本就因成本高昂而受到批评。因此，现有的生成式 ABM 研究均未开展必要的敏感性分析，以评估模型可靠性并识别关键参数。更令人担忧的是，大多数研究仅依赖模型的一次运行。鉴于生成式 ABMs 固有的随机性，这种做法存在严重问题——类似于根据单个案例研究得出结论。
6 Conclusion6 结论
Generative ABM is an exciting and quickly growing field, making use of the capacity of LLMs to mimic human behavior. This paper has situated generative ABMs in the longer history of, and debate surrounding, agent-based modeling. The paper has reviewed the field to assess whether and how the now-rejuvenated field deals with the long-standing challenges that have historically limited the use of ABMs in the social sciences.
生成式 ABM 是一个令人振奋且迅速发展的领域，它利用 LLMs 模仿人类行为的能力。本文将生成式 ABMs 置于基于智能体的建模的长期发展历史及相关争论中加以考察。本文回顾了该领域的研究，以评估如今重获活力的这一领域是否以及如何应对那些长期以来限制 ABMs 在社会科学中应用的挑战。
Our review has shown that the rapidly growing field is drawing on LLMs to model a wide range of human behavior, ranging from network dynamics to social media simulations. LLMs enable models to appear much more ‘realistic’, and open up for exploring aspects of human behavior that have previously been nearly impossible to model.
我们的综述表明，这一迅速发展的领域正在利用 LLMs 对广泛的人类行为进行建模，涵盖从网络动态到社交媒体模拟的各种情境。LLMs 使模型显得更加“逼真”，并为探索此前几乎无法建模的人类行为的某些方面开辟了可能。
However, based on the review of the recent literature, we have also found that the addition of LLMs to ABMs does little to address the challenges with which the field has struggled, often exacerbating rather than resolving the long-standing issues limiting the use of ABMs.
然而，通过回顾近期文献，我们也发现，将 LLMs 引入 ABMs 对解决该领域长期面临的挑战作用甚微，而且往往会加剧而非解决那些长期限制 ABMs 应用的问题。
The lack of rigor in relation to issues such as validation is partly excused by the fact that the field of generative ABMs is still in a stage of early experimentation, exploring the potential of using LLMs to reproduce aspects of human behavior through proof-of-concept models. This begs the question of whether field will be able to transition from such toy models to the type of rigorous modeling needed to contribute productively to social scientific theory.
在验证等问题上缺乏严谨性，在一定程度上情有可原，因为生成式 ABMs 领域仍处于早期实验阶段，正通过概念验证模型探索利用 LLMs 再现人类行为某些方面的潜力。这引出了一个问题：该领域能否从此类玩具模型过渡到能够为社会科学理论作出切实贡献的严谨建模。
To do so, it would be necessary to evidence operational validity Sargent (2010): the model must be convincingly shown to capture some existing social mechanism. For conventional ABMs, there have been two chief ways of achieving operational validity.
要做到这一点，就必须提供运行有效性（operational validity）的证据 Sargent (2010)：必须令人信服地证明，模型捕捉到了某种现实中存在的社会机制。对于传统 ABMs，实现运行有效性主要有两种途径。
The first way is to employ carefully calibration and validation to achieve model realism Edmonds and Moss (2004). LLMs appear to naturally afford this route, as they enable seemingly more realistic representations of human reasoning and decision-making. However, the introduction of LLMs do not appear to resolve the issues that made such validation and calibration challenging for ABMs, such as their inherent flexibility and lack of standardization and comparability. LLMs furthermore add to this the challenging task of developing calibration and validation procedures to culturally align the agents with real-world individuals, for instance showing that the model is not representing minorities by reproducing problematic stereotypes.

The second way to achieve operational validity is to focus on capturing highly simple – and therefore robust and generalizable – emergent phenomena. Many of the most insightful ABMs are highly simplistic thought-experiments that throw light on a minimal representation of a phenomenon. As such, these models do not necessarily “aim to provide an accurate representation of a particular empirical application. Instead, the goal of agent-based modeling is to enrich our understanding of fundamental processes that may appear in a variety of applications” (Axelrod, 1997, 25). Scholars who take this approach to ABMs are “much more concerned with theoretical development and explanation than with prediction” (Gilbert, 1997, 21); ABMs offer a form of computational thought-experiments that enable exploring whether particular micro-mechanisms can produce an observed pattern. Such models are believable not because of their realism but because the emergent phenomenon that they capture is so simple and general as to be applicable to a wide range of systems. The famous Schelling segregation model Schelling (1971), for example, is insightful not because the model accurately represents cities, but because the dynamics that it describes can offer insights into phenomena ranging from residential segregation to why oil separates from water.

However, it is not clear how generative ABMs would be useful for either of these two approaches. For the former, generative ABMs are, as we have seen, exceedingly challenging to calibrate and validate against real-world data. It has yet to be shown that the models can be made to reproduce human actions in such a way as to make them social scientifically productive. For the latter, generative ABMs rely on highly complicated black-box models whose behaviors are poorly understood even on their own, and it is hence not clear how these models may be used to reveal simple emergent mechanisms. While LLMs are exceptionally simple to use, as one can instruct the agents through natural language rather than employing the often complex mathematical and highly parameterized work of conventional ABMs, they can yet be highly challenging to interpret. As Axelrod (Axelrod, 1997, 27) puts it, “If the goal is to deepen our understanding of some fundamental process, then simplicity of the assumptions is important and realistic representation of all the details of a particular setting is not”. The introduction of LLMs into these models inevitably adds complexity that may undermine their usefulness as tools for theoretical research, as it makes it more challenging to figure out why and how the model produces a given result Macy and Willer (2002). Thought-experiments are productive because they are so simple as to constitute explanations, and it is not clear whether generative ABMs will afford such simplicity.

The issues raised by this paper beg the more fundamental question of whether there is an added value to introducing LLMs in ABMs, beyond the undeniable excitement of novelty. While generative ABMs may exacerbate rather than resolve the long-standing challenges of ABMs, there are still reasons to be optimistic about their possibilities. A range of scholars have long criticized ABMs on epistemic grounds, arguing that complexity in the social world is fundamentally different from type of complexity that ABMs enable us to study Byrne and Callaghan (2022); Bhaskar (2014); Bateson (2000); Törnberg and Uitermark (2025). Human society is not akin to a murmuration of starlings: it is characterized not by emergent patterns that stem from the mass-interaction of simple agents, but from agents that recognize and interact with the social context within which they are operating. In the social world, emergent patterns are not just self-organizing structures; they become named, institutionalized, and capable of exerting downward causation, shaping individual and collective behavior. A potential answer to the question of the novelty brought by generative ABMs is hence that they open the door to studying yet unexplored aspects of social emergence, such as the central role of narrative Polkinghorne (1988) and social construction Berger and Luckmann (1966) in the human world. However, doing so requires addressing the challenges raised in this paper, to avoid the door opening merely to a new replication crisis.
