我们应该采用 MAD 吗？探究面向 LLMs 的多智能体辩论策略Should we be going MAD? A Look at Multi-Agent Debate Strategies for LLMs
摘要Abstract
大型语言模型（LLMs）的近期进展凸显了其回答各领域问题的潜力。 Recent advancements in large language models (LLMs) underscore their potential for responding to inquiries in various domains. 然而，确保生成式智能体提供准确、可靠的答案仍是一项持续存在的挑战。 However, ensuring that generative agents provide accurate and reliable answers remains an ongoing challenge. 在此背景下，多智能体辩论（multi-agent debate，MAD）已成为一种有望提升 LLMs 回答真实性的策略。 In this context, multi-agent debate (MAD) has emerged as a promising strategy for enhancing the truthfulness of LLMs. 我们对一系列辩论策略和提示策略进行基准评测，以探索成本、时间与准确率之间的权衡。 We benchmark a range of debating and prompting strategies to explore the trade-offs between cost, time, and accuracy. 重要的是，我们发现，目前形式的多智能体辩论系统并不能稳定地优于其他已提出的提示策略，例如自一致性以及利用多条推理路径进行集成的策略。 Importantly, we find that multi-agent debating systems, in their current form, do not reliably outperform other proposed prompting strategies, such as self-consistency and ensembling using multiple reasoning paths. 然而，在进行超参数调优后，Multi-Persona 等若干 MAD 系统表现更好。 However, when performing hyperparameter tuning, several MAD systems, such as Multi-Persona, perform better. 这表明，MAD 协议可能并非本质上劣于其他方法，而是对不同的超参数设置更为敏感，且难以优化。 This suggests that MAD protocols might not be inherently worse than other approaches, but that they are more sensitive to different hyperparameter settings and difficult to optimize. 基于这些结果，我们提出了改进辩论策略的见解，例如调整智能体之间的意见一致程度，这能够显著提升性能，甚至超越我们评估的所有其他非辩论协议。 We build on these results to offer insights into improving debating strategies, such as adjusting agent agreement levels, which can significantly enhance performance and even surpass all other non-debate protocols we evaluated. 我们为社区提供了一个开源代码仓库，其中包含若干当前最先进的协议及评估脚本，可用于在常用研究数据集上开展基准评测。 We provide an open-source repository to the community with several state-of-the-art protocols together with evaluation scripts to benchmark across popular research datasets.

1 引言1 Introduction
能够如实作答的问答助手需要具备多种能力，而直到不久前，这些能力仍被认为超出了生成式语言模型的能力范围。 Truthful question-answering assistants require a plethora of skills that until recently were considered out-of-reach of generative language models. 这类智能体需要具备高级自然语言阅读理解能力，以及准确回忆和运用专业或技术知识的能力。 Such agents require advanced natural language reading comprehension, along with accurate recall and manipulation of expert or technical knowledge. 随着大型语言模型（LLMs）的性能提升和日益普及 (Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023)，人们越来越期待这些模型能够在医学 (Liévin et al., 2022; Han et al., 2023; Nori et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a; Singhal et al., 2023)、教育 (Pardos & Bhandari, 2023; Kumar et al., 2024) 和法律 (Lai et al., 2023) 等领域为人类提供帮助。 Following the increase in performance and popularity of large language models (LLMs) (Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023), there is a growing hope that these models could assist humans in various domains like medicine (Liévin et al., 2022; Han et al., 2023; Nori et al., 2023; Wang et al., 2023a; Singhal et al., 2023), education (Pardos & Bhandari, 2023; Kumar et al., 2024), and law (Lai et al., 2023). 作为提升这些系统推理能力的一种方式，提示策略受到了广泛关注，最初的代表包括少样本提示（few-shot）(Brown et al., 2020) 和思维链（chain-of-thought，CoT）(Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022)。 As a way to improve the reasoning abilities of these systems, prompting strategies have received significant attention, starting with few-shot (Brown et al., 2020), and chain-of-thought (CoT) (Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022). 为了进一步提升性能，研究者提出了多种策略，利用多个 LLMs 之间的交互式推理，或通过并行生成答案来保持某种形式的自一致性 (Wang et al., 2023b)，或促使模型模拟辩论。 To further improve performance, a wide variety of strategies have been proposed to use interactive reasoning between multiple LLMs, by either generating answers in parallel to maintain a form of self-consistency (Wang et al., 2023b), or promoting models to simulate debate. 这些多智能体方法近期在应用中日益普及，例如语言生成 (Chan et al., 2023)、机器翻译与算术推理 (Liang et al., 2023)、数学与策略推理 (Du et al., 2023)、谈判与议价 (Fu et al., 2023)，尤其是医学问答 (Anil et al., 2023; Singhal et al., 2023; Nori et al., 2023)。 These multi-agent approaches have recently seen an uptake in applications, e.g. language generation (Chan et al., 2023), machine translation and arithmetic reasoning (Liang et al., 2023), mathematical and strategic reasoning (Du et al., 2023), negotiation and bargaining (Fu et al., 2023), and notably, medical Q&A (Anil et al., 2023; Singhal et al., 2023; Nori et al., 2023). 如何以最佳方式利用多个智能体开展有效的交互式推理，是一个具有前瞻性的研究问题。 How to best utilize multiple agents for effective interactive reasoning is a prescient research question. 然而，据我们所知，目前尚无研究对这些策略进行比较，对于如何在不同策略之间作出选择也尚未形成共识。 However, to the best of our knowledge, there is no work comparing strategies, and there is no consensus for selecting one strategy over another.
本文对包括多智能体辩论（MAD）在内的多种提示策略进行基准评测，考察其回答广泛领域中多项选择题的表现。 In this paper, we benchmark various prompting strategies, which include multi-agent debate (MAD), for answering multiple-choice questions across a wide range of domains. 我们探究这些策略对事实准确率、时间和成本等关键因素的影响，以及这些因素之间的权衡。 We explore the impact on, and trade-offs between, critical factors such as factual accuracy, time and cost. 我们提供了一套开源的单智能体与 MAD 实现，供研究社区在此基础上进一步开发，并提供统一的 API，以便轻松构建和评估 MAD 系统。 We provide an open-source suite of single-agent and MAD implementations for the research community to build upon, with a unified API to easily build and evaluate MAD systems. 最后，我们证明，采用特定的提示策略能够提升 LLMs 的推理能力。 Finally, we demonstrate that by utilizing specific prompting strategies, LLMs exhibit improved reasoning abilities. 具体而言，我们提出了一种新颖的辩论提示策略，能够调节辩论过程中智能体之间的意见一致程度，并在给定模型类别下超越当前最先进的问答性能。 Concretely, we provide a novel debate prompting strategy able to modulate the level of agreement between agents during a debate and improve upon the state-of-the-art for Q&A for a given model class.
2 多智能体辩论2 Multi-Agent Debate
目前，最先进的问答模型以生成式 LLMs 为主。 Current state-of-the-art models for Q&A are dominated by generative LLMs. 为了使其回答更真实、可靠，通常会针对特定使用场景对这些模型进行微调。 To make them more truthful and reliable, they are often fine-tuned for specific use cases. 例如，在医学领域，这类模型包括 Med-PaLM (Singhal et al., 2022)、Med-PaLM2 (Singhal et al., 2023)、MedAlpaca (Han et al., 2023)、Galactica (Taylor et al., 2022)、ClinicalGPT (Wang et al., 2023a) 和 Medprompt (Nori et al., 2023)。 In medicine, for example, such models include Med-PaLM (Singhal et al., 2022), Med-PaLM2 (Singhal et al., 2023), MedAlpaca (Han et al., 2023), Galactica (Taylor et al., 2022), ClinicalGPT (Wang et al., 2023a) and Medprompt (Nori et al., 2023). 此外，许多单智能体提示策略已在医学问答场景中得到研究。例如，Liévin et al. (2022) 在 Instruct GPT-3 (Ouyang et al., 2022) 的基础上应用 CoT 推理，并取得了显著的性能提升。 Furthermore, many single-agent prompting strategies have been investigated in the context of medical Q&A. For example, Liévin et al. (2022) applied CoT reasoning on top of Instruct GPT-3 (Ouyang et al., 2022), and achieves noticeable performance improvements.
近年来，研究者提出了多种 MAD 策略，旨在单智能体提示方法所增强的推理能力基础上进一步改进，从而提升在具有挑战性的自然语言任务上的表现 (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2023)。 Recently, several MAD strategies have been proposed to improve upon the enhanced reasoning capabilities of single-agent prompting methods leading to improved performance on challenging natural language tasks (Du et al., 2023; Liang et al., 2023; Chan et al., 2023). 同样，Generative agents (Park et al., 2023)、multi-persona (Wang et al., 2023c) 和 CAMEL (Li et al., 2023) 研究了在多智能体交互中扮演不同角色或人格的智能体的行为。 Likewise, Generative agents (Park et al., 2023), multi-persona (Wang et al., 2023c), and CAMEL (Li et al., 2023) study the behaviour of agents taking on different roles or personas within multi-agent interactions. 辩论策略能够成为一种有效工具的一个主要原因，是 LLM 能够适应上下文中提供的额外信息 (Zhang et al., 2023)。 One major reason why debate strategies can be an effective tool is the ability of LLMs to adapt to additional information given in-context (Zhang et al., 2023). 这使得多个 LLM 能够完全通过上下文提示参与多智能体和/或多轮辩论。 This facilitates multiple LLMs to participate in multi-agent and/or multi-round debates entirely using in-context prompting. 也就是说，智能体在推理时根据其他智能体提供的信息调整自身行为，而无须基于梯度更新参数。 That is, the agents adapt their behaviour based on information provided by other agents at inference time, with no gradient-based parameter updates being required.
在本研究中，我们考察了几种用于问答的提示策略。尽管我们注意到，其中一些策略是专门针对医学问答领域提出的，但每种策略都为如何利用多个协作智能体提供了新的视角。 In our study, we investigate several prompting strategies for Q&A. Whilst we note that some of these strategies were introduced specifically for the medical Q&A domain, each provides novel perspectives on how to utilize multiple collaborative agents. 我们在此简要介绍每种策略。 We briefly introduce each strategy here.
心智社会（Society of Minds，SoM）：Du et al. (2023) 提出了一种 MAD 方法，其中多个智能体各自向彼此提供答案，以实现有效协作。 Society of Minds (SoM) Du et al. (2023) propose a MAD approach where multiple agents each provide their answers to each other in order to effectively collaborate. 也可以先对答案进行汇总，再将其加入后续轮次中智能体可访问的历史记录。 Optionally, answers are summarized before being added to the history that is available to the agents in future rounds.
Multi-Persona (Liang et al., 2023) 提出了一种 MAD 策略，通过提示智能体采用不同人格来鼓励其产生不同结果，即肯定型智能体（天使）和否定型智能体（魔鬼）各自向裁判智能体提供答案，由裁判智能体管理整个过程并得出最终解答。 Multi-Persona (Liang et al., 2023) propose a MAD strategy to encourage divergent agent outcomes via prompting different personalities, i.e. an affirmative agent (angel) and a negative agent (devil) each provide an answer to a judge agent who manages the process and obtains a final solution. 裁判还拥有额外的自主权：如果对所提供的答案感到满意，可以提前结束辩论。 The judge has additional agency to end the debate early if it is satisfied with the answers provided.
ChatEval (Chan et al., 2023) 提出了三种 MAD 模式：(1) 一对一（one-on-one），各智能体依次回答给定问题，并获得此前所有智能体答案的历史记录；(2) 同时发言（simultaneous-talk），智能体在每轮中异步生成回答，以消除智能体顺序的影响；(3) 带汇总器的同时发言（simultaneous-talk-with-summarizer），在此基础上还会汇总每轮提供的答案，并覆盖后续轮次中所有智能体可访问的历史记录。 ChatEval (Chan et al., 2023) propose three MAD modes: (1) one-on-one, where each agent answers the provided question in turn, and each agent is provided with the history of all previous agents’ answers; (2) simultaneous-talk, where agents asynchronously generate responses in each round to nullify the effects of agent order; and (3) simultaneous-talk-with-summarizer, which additionally summarizes answers provided in each round and overwrites the history available to all agents in future rounds.
自一致性（Self-consistency）(Wang et al., 2023b) 在给定固定提示的情况下采样多条推理路径，并选择出现频率最高的答案。 Self-consistency (Wang et al., 2023b) samples multiple reasoning paths given a fixed prompt and selects the most frequent answer. 尽管由于各样本是独立生成的，这本身并不属于辩论，但它依赖多次 API 调用，因此我们将其与仅使用一次 API 调用的单智能体情形区分开来。 Whilst this is not a debate per se, as samples are rolled out independently, it relies on multiple API calls so we distinguish it from the single agent case that uses a single API call.
集成精炼（Ensemble Refinement，ER）(Singhal et al., 2023) 扩展了自一致性方法。 Ensemble Refinement (ER) (Singhal et al., 2023) extends self-consistency. 在采样多条推理路径之后，第二阶段将这些路径拼接为一个学生推理列表，随后以该列表为条件执行多轮聚合。 After multiple reasoning paths are sampled, a second stage concatenates them into a list of student reasonings, after which multiple rounds of aggregation are performed conditioned on this list.
Medprompt (Nori et al., 2023) 将少样本示例选择与一个两阶段过程相结合，以增强 AI 驱动的医学问答：首先对 GPT-4 针对训练集生成的正确回答进行预处理，将其用作少样本示例；随后在推理步骤中采用 k 近邻（k-Nearest Neighbours，kNN）检索和集成精炼来生成答案。 Medprompt (Nori et al., 2023) enhances AI-driven medical question answering by combining few-shot exemplar selection with a two-stage process: preprocessing correct GPT-4 training set responses as few-shot examples, and an inference step employing a k-Nearest Neighbours (kNN) lookup and ensemble refinement for answer generation. 该方法仅使用最相关且经过验证的示例，从而确保较高的准确性和可靠性。 This approach ensures high accuracy and reliability by using only the most relevant and validated examples. Medprompt 在 MedQA 基准上的优异表现证明了其有效性，展现了其在医学诊断方面的潜力。 Medprompt’s effectiveness is demonstrated in its superior performance on the MedQA benchmark, showcasing its potential for medical diagnostics. 在本研究中，我们未采用 kNN 方法，因为它需要划分训练集和测试集。 In this work, we do not employ the kNN approach as it requires a training and test set split. 我们认为，由于其他协议不使用训练集，这可能会使 Medprompt 在本次评估中获得不公平的优势。 We believe this might provide Medprompt an unfair advantage in this evaluation as other protocols do not use a training set. 因此，我们仅实现完整 Medprompt 的部分组件，具体而言，是结合少样本思维链集成的问题随机化 (Nori et al., 2023)。 Therefore, we only implement subcomponents of the full Medprompt, specifically, question randomization with few-shot chain-of-thought ensembling (Nori et al., 2023).

我们使用采用 3.5-turbo 引擎的 GPT-3 (Brown et al., 2020) 作为 MAD 实现中的基础智能体。 As base agents for the MAD implementations, we utilize GPT-3 (Brown et al., 2020) with the 3.5-turbo engine. GPT-3 是一种基于 Transformer 的大规模生成式大语言模型（LLM）(Vaswani et al., 2017; Kaplan et al., 2020)，可通过 API 调用使用。 GPT-3 is a large-scale transformer-based generative LLM (Vaswani et al., 2017; Kaplan et al., 2020) available via API calls. 尽管 GPT-3.5 并非目前通过 API 可用的最强大模型，但我们选择它，是因为它在性能与成本效益之间实现了最佳平衡，这对于开展广泛的实验至关重要，而使用更强大的模型则会导致成本高昂到难以承受。 Although not the current most powerful model available via API, we selected GPT-3.5 for its optimal balance between performance and cost efficiency, crucial for conducting a broad range of experiments, whereas more powerful models would’ve been prohibitively expensive. 不过，我们非正式地指出，使用 GPT-4 等更先进模型开展的一些初步实验也得出了类似发现，尽管这些测试仅限于较小的数据子集。 However, we informally note that some of our preliminary experiments with more advanced models like GPT-4 suggested similar findings, although these tests were limited to a smaller subset of data.

通过调节一致程度改进 MADImproving MAD via agreement modulation
此前的实验使我们提出假设：智能体在辩论中彼此赞同的程度可能显著影响辩论性能。 Our previous experiments led us to hypothesise that the degree to which agents agree with one another during a debate may significantly affect debating performance. 在此，我们通过开发一种新的 MAD 提示策略来检验这一假设，该策略通过提示调节参与辩论的智能体在开始时彼此赞同的程度。 Here we test this hypothesis by developing a new MAD prompting strategy that modulates (via prompts) the degree to which agents within a debate agree with each other at the outset. 我们的提示采用以下简单形式：“你应该在 X% 的情况下赞同其他智能体”。 Our prompt takes the following simple form: “you should agree with the other agents X% of the time”. 我们将该提示中的 X 称为智能体的一致强度。 We call X in this prompt the agent’s agreement intensity.
Multi-Persona 是检验这种方法的理想平台，因为它仅使用两个具有不同系统提示的智能体。 Multi-Persona is an ideal testbed for this method, as it uses only two agents with different system prompts. 具体而言，我们向“天使”智能体提供问题，并使用“魔鬼”的系统提示来调节分歧程度。 Specifically, we provide the “angel” agent with the question, and we modulate the disagreement using the “devil”’s system prompt. 图 5 展示了调节智能体的一致程度对 6 个数据集的影响。 Figure 5 shows the effect of modulating the agent’s agreement on the 6 datasets. 可以看出，这一参数对性能有显著影响，而且在提示中调节一致程度会改变第一轮辩论中的一致程度，进而影响辩论结果和最终准确率。 We can see that this parameter has a significant effect on the performance and that modulating the agreement in the prompt has the effect of modifying the agreement on the first debate round, which in turn affects the outcome and the final accuracy. 值得注意的是，各数据集呈现的趋势并不总是相同：MedQA 和 PubMedQA 直接受益于较高的一致程度，而 CIAR 则呈现相反的趋势。 Strikingly, the tendency is not always the same for each dataset: while MedQA and PubMedQA directly benefit from a high agreement, CIAR follows a reverse pattern. 由于 CIAR 的设计旨在违背直觉，因此，提示第二个智能体强烈反对，很可能会提高在辩论过程中得出正确答案的概率。 As CIAR was created to be counter-intuitive, it is therefore probable that prompting the second agent to strongly disagree raises the chance of arriving at the correct answer during the debate.

4 结论4 Conclusions
本研究所详述的调查表明，在采用各自原始实现的情况下，MAD 方法目前并未优于 Medprompt 和自一致性（self-consistency）等其他集成方法。 The investigations detailed in this work have demonstrated that MAD approaches currently do not outperform other ensembling methods such as Medprompt and self-consistency using their original implementations. MAD 通常需要更多的 API 调用，因而增加了需要生成和处理的 token 数量，并最终提高了系统的总运行成本。 MAD typically requires a higher number of API calls, increasing the number of tokens to produce and process and ultimately, the total running cost of the system. 有趣的是，我们发现，这可能主要是因为 MAD 协议对超参数更加敏感，而不一定是因为辩论本身的表现逊于单智能体方法。 Interestingly, we found that this might primarily be due to MAD protocols being more sensitive to hyperparameters and not necessarily that debate inherently performs worse than single-agent methods. 为进一步支持这一论断，我们证明，仅通过提示词来简单调节智能体之间相互赞同的程度，即所谓的一致强度（agreement intensity），就能显著提升性能。 To strengthen this claim, we demonstrated that a simple prompt-based manipulation of the degree to which agents agree with each other, referred to as the agreement intensity, could provide significant performance gains. 通过为 Multi-Persona 调整这一一致强度，该协议从表现最差转变为表现最佳，超越了 Medprompt 和 Self-Consistency 等方法。 By tuning this agreement intensity for Multi-Persona, it went from being the worst-performing protocol to the best-performing, beating out methods such as Medprompt and Self-Consistency. 因此，尽管 MAD 系统目前落后于其他提示策略，我们仍认为它们具有改变提示策略格局的巨大潜力。 Therefore, while MAD systems currently trail behind other prompting strategies, we believe they hold significant potential to transform the prompting landscape. 为促进进一步探索，我们已将代码仓库开源，以便他人轻松复现我们的全部实验，并进一步探索值得研究的相关方向。 To foster further exploration, we have made our code repository open-source, for others to easily reproduce all our experiments and further pursue interesting related research directions.
局限性Limitations
我们通过 API 调用公开可用的大语言模型（LLM）(Brown et al., 2020)，这种方式虽然足以满足本研究的需要，但也使我们面临调用推理耗时不稳定以及模型意外更新的问题。 We utilize API calls to a publicly available LLM (Brown et al., 2020) which, whilst sufficient in the context of our investigation, exposes us to variable inference time calls and unforeseen model updates. 此外，基于 API 的大规模基准评测会产生高昂的资金和时间成本，这既限制了实验规模，也为复现和扩展研究设置了门槛。 Moreover, large-scale API-based benchmarking incurs substantial financial and time costs, which both limit the experiment scales and provide a barrier of entry to replication and extension efforts. 基于这些原因，未来的研究可以利用开源模型和自有基础设施来拓展这一研究方向。 For these reasons, future works could extend this line of work using open-source models and in-house infrastructure.
