摘要Abstract
大语言模型（LLM）多智能体系统正日益以相互交互的智能体社会形式部署，然而，扩大这些系统的规模往往带来递减或不稳定的收益，其原因仍未得到充分理解。 Large Language Model (LLM) multi-agent systems are increasingly deployed as interacting agent societies, yet scaling these systems often yields diminishing or unstable returns, the causes of which remain poorly understood. 我们开展了首项针对基于 LLM 的多智能体系统协调动力学的大规模实证研究，提出一种原子事件层面的形式化方法，将推理重构为协调级联。 We present the first large-scale empirical study of coordination dynamics in LLM-based multi-agent systems, introducing an atomic event-level formulation that reconstructs reasoning as cascades of coordination. 通过分析跨任务、拓扑结构和规模的超过 1.5 百万次交互，我们揭示了三条相互耦合的规律：协调呈现重尾级联，通过优先连接机制集中于智识精英，并随着系统规模扩大而产生愈发频繁的极端事件。 Analyzing over 1.5 Million interactions across tasks, topologies, and scales, we uncover three coupled laws: coordination follows heavy-tailed cascades, concentrates via preferential attachment into intellectual elites, and produces increasingly frequent extreme events as system size grows. 我们表明，这些效应通过同一个结构性机制相互耦合：整合瓶颈，即协调的扩展随系统规模增长，而协调的归并却未同步增长，从而产生规模庞大但整合程度较低的推理过程。 We show that these effects are coupled through a single structural mechanism: an integration bottleneck, in which coordination expansion scales with system size while consolidation does not, producing large but weakly integrated reasoning processes. 为检验这一机制，我们提出了亏缺触发整合（Deficit-Triggered Integration，DTI），在失衡情况下选择性地加强整合。 To test this mechanism, we introduce Deficit-Triggered Integration (DTI), which selectively increases integration under imbalance. DTI 恰能在协调失效之处提升性能，同时不抑制大规模推理。 DTI improves performance precisely where coordination fails, without suppressing large-scale reasoning. 总体而言，我们的结果确立了集体认知的定量规律，并指出，协调结构是理解和改进可扩展多智能体智能的一个根本性维度，而这一维度此前尚未得到测量。 Together, our results establish quantitative laws of collective cognition and identify coordination structure as a fundamental, previously unmeasured axis for understanding and improving scalable multi-agent intelligence.

我们超越该规律本身，进一步研究其影响。 We move beyond the law itself to further investigate its implications. 我们表明，观测到的重尾结构与协调中的强化机制相一致：在早期积累了较多参与的论断往往会吸引不成比例的更多后续活动，而且这一效应随系统规模增大而增强，类似于复杂系统中的优先连接机制 Barabási and Albert (1999)。 We show that the observed heavy-tailed structure is consistent with reinforcement in coordination: claims that accumulate early engagement tend to attract disproportionately more downstream activity, and this effect strengthens with system size, analogous to preferential attachment in complex systems Barabási and Albert (1999). 这使得认知努力集中于少数智能体，形成一个涌现出的智识精英阶层，其影响力随规模增大而增强。 This leads to a concentration of cognitive effort in a small subset of agents, forming an emergent tier of intellectual elites whose influence increases with scale. 更重要的是，大规模级联的内部构成揭示了一种结构性失衡。 More importantly, the internal composition of large cascades reveals a structural imbalance. 随着系统规模增大，由委派和反驳驱动的级联生成持续增长，而通过合并实现的整合并未按相同比例增长。 As system size increases, cascade generation driven by delegation and contradiction continues to grow, while integration through merge does not scale proportionally. 这表明，尽管系统在拓展推理方面变得越来越有效，但其整合推理的能力并未获得相应比例的提升。 This indicates that although the system becomes increasingly effective at expanding reasoning, it does not become proportionally better at consolidating it. 这一整合瓶颈为既有研究中观测到的非单调规模扩展行为提供了机制层面的解释 Chen et al. (2024b); Cemri et al. (2025)。 This integration bottleneck provides a mechanistic explanation for the non-monotonic scaling behavior observed in prior work Chen et al. (2024b); Cemri et al. (2025). 更大规模的协调级联本身并非有害。 Larger coordination cascades are not inherently detrimental. 相反，它们对于通过分支推理和任务分解来探索复杂解空间至关重要。 On the contrary, they are essential for exploring complex solution spaces through branching reasoning and task decomposition. 问题在于分配失当：随着规模增大，越来越多的大规模级联体现为冗余探索或未解决的冲突，而非富有成效的综合。 The problem is misallocation: as scale increases, a growing fraction of large cascades reflects redundant exploration or unresolved conflict rather than productive synthesis.
这一发现表明，改进 LLM MAS 不仅需要增强推理能力，还需要调控协调结构。 This finding suggests that improving LLM MAS requires not only increasing reasoning capacity, but also regulating coordination structure. 为考察这一点，我们引入了缺口触发整合（Deficit-Triggered Integration，DTI），这是一种干预方法，用于监测每个活跃级联内部级联扩展与合并活动之间的失衡，并在失衡程度超过阈值时触发整合。 To examine this, we introduce Deficit-Triggered Integration (DTI), an intervention that monitors the imbalance between cascade expansion and merge activity within each active cascade and triggers integration when this imbalance exceeds a threshold. DTI 在减少低效的后期扩展的同时，仍保留了支持大规模推理的重尾形态。 DTI still preserves the heavy-tailed regime that supports large-scale reasoning while reducing inefficient late-stage expansion. 在不同拓扑与任务的组合条件下，DTI 均能稳定提高任务成功率，其中最大的增益出现在扩展与整合失衡最为明显的情形中。 Across topology-task conditions, DTI consistently improves task success, with the largest gains observed in regimes where the expansion-integration imbalance is most pronounced. 这些结果支持了 LLM MAS 中的协调失效属于结构性问题这一观点，并表明通过有针对性地调控协调动力学可以缓解这一问题。 These results support the view that coordination failure in LLM MAS is a structural problem and demonstrate that it can be mitigated through targeted regulation of coordination dynamics. 更广泛地说，这些结果指向一种新的设计范式，其中可扩展性不仅取决于模型能力，还取决于集体推理的组织方式。 More broadly, they point toward a new design paradigm in which scalability depends not only on model capability, but also on how collective reasoning is organized.

4 强化路由理论4 Theory of Reinforced Routing
4.1 概述：递归协调与事件传播4.1 Overview: Recursive Coordination and Event Propagation
LLM 多智能体系统中的协调以围绕主张展开的递归过程进行。 Coordination in LLM multi-agent systems unfolds as a recursive process over claims. 在每一步中，智能体通过分解、细化、批判和综合等操作处理已有主张，以产生新的主张。 At each step, agents operate on existing claims to produce new ones through actions such as decomposition, refinement, critique, and synthesis. 这形成了一种依赖结构，使推理沿着由相互关联的主张构成且不断扩展的网络传播，而非独立生成。 This creates a dependency structure in which reasoning propagates through a growing network of interrelated claims rather than being generated independently. 一旦引入根主张，它就可能触发下游活动的级联。 Once a root claim is introduced, it can trigger a cascade of downstream activity. 委派扩展推理结构，修订完善中间输出，矛盾产生分支性的备选方案，而合并操作则整合多条推理路径。 Delegation expands the reasoning structure, revision refines intermediate outputs, contradiction creates branching alternatives, and merge operations integrate multiple reasoning paths. 这些交互形成了主张递归生成更多主张的级联。 These interactions give rise to cascades in which claims recursively generate further claims. 这一过程具有内在的路径依赖性：积累了更多交互的主张更有可能被重新审视和扩展，而其他主张则保持不活跃。 This process is inherently path-dependent: claims that accumulate more interaction become more likely to be revisited and expanded, while others remain inactive. 因此，协调集中于一部分不断演化的推理轨迹。 As a result, coordination concentrates around a subset of evolving reasoning trajectories. 为了理解这种集中现象如何出现，我们提出一种描述智能体如何选择已有主张并在其基础上继续构建的机制。 Understanding how this concentration emerges motivates a mechanism for how agents select and build upon existing claims.
4.2 强化路由机制4.2 Reinforced Routing Mechanism
我们将 LLM 多智能体系统中的协调建模为围绕论断的路由过程。 We model coordination in LLM multi-agent systems as a routing process over claims. 令 $c_{i}$ 表示在时间 $t$ 处于活跃状态的一个论断，令 $x_{i}(t)$ 表示其累积协调活动量，定义为截至时间 $t$ 引用、修订、反驳 $c_{i}$，或以 $c_{i}$ 为来源进行合并或委派的下游协调事件的数量。 Let $c_{i}$ denote a claim active at time $t$ , and let $x_{i}(t)$ denote its accumulated coordination activity, defined as the number of downstream coordination events that reference, revise, contradict, merge from, or delegate from $c_{i}$ up to time $t$ . 这一量刻画了一个论断参与持续推理过程的程度。 This quantity captures the extent to which a claim has been involved in the ongoing reasoning process.
智能体反复选择已有论断作为进一步推理的基础。 Agents repeatedly select existing claims as the basis for further reasoning. 这种选择并非均匀进行：累积活动量更多的论断更有可能被重新审视和扩展，这反映了它们在共享推理状态中的显著地位，以及它们与任务进展的相关性。 This selection is not uniform: claims that have accumulated more activity are more likely to be revisited and expanded, reflecting their prominence in the shared reasoning state and their relevance to task progress. 我们通过一条强化路由规则将这种行为形式化： We formalize this behavior through a reinforced routing rule: 
| | $\mathbb{P}(c_{i}\mid\mathcal{F}_{t})=\frac{x_{i}(t)^{\beta}}{\sum_{j}x_{j}(t)^{\beta}},$ | | (4)
其中，$\mathcal{F}_{t}$ 表示交互历史，$\beta>0$ 控制强化的强度。 where $\mathcal{F}_{t}$ denotes the interaction history and $\beta>0$ controls the strength of reinforcement. 当 $\beta=0$ 时，各论断被路由选择的概率相同；当 $\beta>0$ 时，已经活跃的论断会被优先选择，$\beta$ 越大，对活动的放大作用越强。 When $\beta=0$ , routing is uniform over claims; when $\beta>0$ , already-active claims are preferentially selected, with larger $\beta$ corresponding to stronger amplification of activity.

4.3 级联增长模型4.3 Cascade Growth Model
在强化路由机制下，协调通过以论断为根的级联传播。 Under reinforced routing, coordination propagates through claim-rooted cascades. 每个被选中的论断都可以通过委派、修订、反驳或综合生成新论断，从而在论断图上形成一个分支过程。 Each selected claim can generate new claims via delegation, revision, contradiction, or synthesis, inducing a branching process over the claim graph. 令 $\lambda$ 表示每个被选中的论断所生成的新论断数量的期望值。 Let $\lambda$ denote the expected number of new claims generated per selected claim. 当 $\lambda<1$ 时，级联保持较小规模；当 $\lambda>1$ 时，级联迅速增长；当 $\lambda\approx 1$ 时，级联规模在不同尺度上表现出高度变异性。 When $\lambda<1$ , cascades remain small; when $\lambda>1$ , they grow rapidly; and when $\lambda\approx 1$ , cascade sizes exhibit high variability across scales. 强化机制通过提高已经活跃的论断继续产生后续活动的可能性，放大了这一过程。 Reinforcement amplifies this process by increasing the likelihood that already-active claims continue to generate further activity. 因此，早期获得增长势头的级联更有可能持续并扩展，而其他级联则迅速终止，从而使不同级联呈现异质性的增长。 Cascades that gain early momentum are therefore more likely to persist and expand, while others terminate quickly, producing heterogeneous growth across cascades. 然而，级联扩展受到系统有限性的约束，包括智能体数量、上下文、通信容量和任务深度的限制。 However, cascade expansion is bounded by finite system constraints, including limited agents, context, communication capacity, and task depth. 这些约束为实际的分支过程设定了上限，阻止了无界增长。 These constraints cap the effective branching process and prevent unbounded growth. 因此，协调在接近临界状态的条件下运行，但仍保持有限规模，从而产生分布宽广但存在截断的级联规模分布。 As a result, coordination operates near criticality but remains finite, yielding broad but truncated cascade size distributions.

(7) 所述。➁ 智识精英的涌现。 Under bounded resources such as limited context, token budgets, and coordination overhead, this yields heavy-tailed but truncated distributions, as described by Eq. (7). ➁ Emergence of intellectual elites. 由于智能体优先对已经活跃的论断进行操作，一部分推理轨迹吸引了不成比例的大量协调活动。 Because agents preferentially operate on already-active claims, a subset of reasoning trajectories attracts disproportionate coordination. 对这些轨迹作出贡献的智能体会参与更大的级联并积累更大的影响力，从而使智识精英得以涌现，即使各智能体的初始能力相同。➂ 极值的标度关系。 Agents contributing to these trajectories participate in larger cascades and accumulate greater influence, leading to the emergence of intellectual elites despite symmetric initial capabilities. ➂ Scaling of extremes. 随着智能体数量增加，并发推理交互的数量也随之增长，从而提高了出现大规模级联的可能性。 As the number of agents increases, the volume of concurrent reasoning interactions grows, increasing the likelihood of large cascades. 这使得最大协调事件的规模随 $N$ 系统性增长。➃ 扩展与整合的不平衡。 This results in systematic growth of the largest coordination events with $N$ . ➃ Expansion–integration imbalance. 委派、修订和批评等协调原语推动推理分支迅速扩展，而通过综合实现的整合则相对受限。 Coordination primitives such as delegation, revision, and critique drive rapid expansion of reasoning branches, while integration through synthesis remains comparatively constrained. 随着级联增长，这种不平衡会产生愈加复杂但整合程度较低的推理结构。 As cascades grow, this imbalance produces increasingly complex but weakly integrated reasoning structures. 这些理论启示共同表明，重尾协调、精英形成和标度行为会从 LLM 多智能体系统中具有强化机制且受边界约束的协调动力学中自然产生。 Together, these implications show that heavy-tailed coordination, elite formation, and scaling behavior arise naturally from reinforced and bounded coordination dynamics in LLM multi-agent systems.

7 讨论与启示7 Discussion and Implications
本研究的核心发现是，LLM 多智能体系统中的协调会自组织形成一种既富有成效又具有自我限制性的模式。 The central finding of this work is that coordination in LLM multi-agent systems self-organizes into a pattern that is simultaneously productive and self-limiting. 重尾级联结构、精英集中以及极端事件的系统性增长并非彼此独立的失效模式；它们源于同一种强化动力学，而正是这种动力学使复杂的集体推理成为可能。 The heavy-tailed cascade structure, elite concentration, and systematic growth of extreme events are not independent failure modes; they arise from the same reinforcement dynamics that enable complex collective reasoning. 这些动力学过程在放大协调的同时逐步削弱巩固，导致收益呈非单调变化，而仅靠改进单个智能体无法解决这一问题。 These dynamics amplify coordination while progressively weakening consolidation, leading to non-monotonic returns that cannot be resolved by improving individual agents alone.
这对 LLM MAS 的设计与评估方式具有直接启示。 This has direct implications for how LLM MAS are designed and evaluated. 当前实践将规模扩展中的失效视为能力缺口，认为可以通过更好的模型、提示词或基准来弥补。 Current practice treats scaling failures as capability gaps, addressable through better models, prompts, or benchmarks. 我们的结果提出了另一个维度：协调结构。 Our results suggest an additional axis: coordination structure. 本文识别出的尾部指数、依附斜率和合并转化率，为诊断协调健康状况提供了一套独立于任务表现的定量表述体系。 The tail exponents, attachment slopes, and merge conversion ratios identified here provide a quantitative vocabulary for diagnosing coordination health that is orthogonal to task performance. 一个系统可能在结构脆弱的配置下取得高准确率，也可能在能力充足的情况下因协调配置不当而失败。 A system may achieve high accuracy while operating in a structurally fragile configuration, or fail despite sufficient capability if coordination is misallocated. 仅衡量结果的评估框架会遗漏这一维度。 Evaluation frameworks that measure only outcomes miss this dimension.
DTI 表明协调结构是可调控的，但它只是原理验证，而非完整的解决方案。 DTI demonstrates that the coordination structure is regulable, but it is a proof of principle rather than a complete solution. 它通过阈值触发的整合，针对一种特定的失衡：扩展过度而巩固不足。 It targets a single imbalance: expansion over consolidation through threshold-triggered integration. 要更全面地阐明协调调控，就需要动态拓扑适应、对强化动力学的控制，以及在更长时间跨度上将精英形成与有效整合相耦合的机制，从而随着系统规模增大，在智能体之间更好地分配效用。 A fuller account of coordination regulation would require dynamic topology adaptation, control of reinforcement dynamics, and mechanisms that couple elite formation with effective integration over longer horizons to better distribute utility across agents as the system size increases. 本文建立的规律为这一更广泛的设计空间提供了定量基础。 The laws developed here provide a quantitative foundation for this broader design space.
8 局限性8 Limitations
我们的分析聚焦于一组受控的协调原语和拓扑—任务配置；尽管观察到的模式在这些设置中保持一致，但其他协调机制和更长时间跨度的交互可能会引入本文未能涵盖的动力学。 Our analysis focuses on a controlled set of coordination primitives and topology-task configurations; while the observed patterns are consistent across these settings, additional coordination mechanisms and longer-horizon interactions may introduce dynamics not captured here. 我们的测量基于对协调的离散事件抽象，这种抽象虽然具有可解释性，并且可以跨条件进行比较，但可能无法充分捕捉推理质量中更细粒度的语义层面。 Our measurements are derived from discrete event abstractions of coordination, which, while interpretable and comparable across conditions, may not fully capture finer-grained semantic aspects of reasoning quality. DTI 被有意设计得尽可能精简，仅针对一种形式的失衡；它既未涵盖影响协调结果的所有因素，也不保证在所有运行状态下都能实现最优性能。 DTI is intentionally minimal and targets a single form of imbalance; it does not address all factors influencing coordination outcomes, nor does it guarantee optimal performance across all regimes. 最后，尽管规模扩展行为在不同模型和设置中表现一致，但我们的结论本质上仍是基于有限样本的实证结论，进一步的理论研究和大规模验证将增强其普适性。 Finally, while scaling behavior is consistent across models and settings, our conclusions are empirical and finite-sample in nature, and further theoretical and large-scale validation would strengthen their generality.
9 结论9 Conclusion
我们表明，LLM MAS 中的协调遵循一致的结构模式：重尾级联、强化驱动的集中，以及极端事件的系统性增长。 We show that coordination in LLM MAS follows a consistent structural pattern: heavy-tailed cascades, reinforcement-driven concentration, and systematic growth of extreme events. 这些是协调传播与累积方式所产生的耦合效应，随着系统规模扩大，会导致扩展与整合之间的失衡日益加剧，并产生规模庞大但巩固程度较弱的推理轨迹。 These are coupled effects of how coordination propagates and accumulates, leading, as systems scale, to an increasing imbalance between expansion and integration and to large but weakly consolidated reasoning trajectories. 这一视角将关注点从智能体能力转向协调结构。 This perspective shifts the focus from agent capability to coordination structure. 规模扩展中的失效并非仅由推理能力不足造成，还取决于协调如何分配和强化。 Scaling failures are not solely due to insufficient reasoning ability, but to how coordination is distributed and reinforced. 通过识别可测量的规律——尾部行为、依附动力学和整合效率，我们提供了一个用于诊断大规模协调的框架。 By identifying measurable laws-tail behavior, attachment dynamics, and integration efficiency, we provide a framework for diagnosing coordination at scale. 我们的干预表明，这些动力学过程是可调控的：有选择地重新平衡协调能够改善结果，而不会抑制大规模推理。 Our intervention demonstrates that these dynamics are regulable: selectively rebalancing coordination improves outcomes without suppressing large-scale reasoning. 因此，LLM MAS 未来的进步不仅取决于更强大的智能体，还取决于能够塑造集体推理展开方式的机制。 Future progress in LLM MAS will therefore depend not only on stronger agents, but on mechanisms that shape how collective reasoning unfolds.

我们将主张和事件形式化为协调动态的基本要素。 We formalize claims and events as the fundamental elements of coordination dynamics.

修订：一条主张恰好有一个父节点，形成链式结构。 Revision: a claim has exactly one parent, producing a chain.
矛盾：多条子主张可能引用同一条父主张，形成分支。 Contradiction: multiple child claims may reference the same parent, producing branching.

由于合并操作会引入多条入边，所形成的结构通常是有向无环图（DAG），而非树。 Because merge operations introduce multiple incoming edges, the resulting structure is in general a directed acyclic graph (DAG), rather than a tree.

给定一个根主张 $c_{\mathrm{root}}$，我们将其协调级联定义为由与该根主张相关的所有下游主张实例和事件实例构成的可达子图。 Given a root claim $c_{\mathrm{root}}$ , we define its coordination cascade as the reachable subgraph of all downstream claim and event instances associated with that root. 在具体操作中，级联提取从 root_claim_id 开始，遍历通过修订、矛盾、合并以及与委派相关的活动相连的所有后代主张。 Operationally, cascade extraction begins from root_claim_id and traverses all descendant claims connected through revision, contradiction, merge, and delegation-linked activity. 总认知投入等聚合可观测量均在这一可达子图上定义。 This reachable subgraph is the object on which aggregate observables, such as total cognitive effort, are defined.
子任务树与主张有向无环图（DAG）。Subtask Tree vs. Claim DAG.
子任务树和主张 DAG 用途不同，不应混为一谈。 The subtask tree and claim DAG serve different purposes and should not be conflated. 子任务树记录由委派产生的任务分解，而主张 DAG 记录推理在交互过程中涌现的传播与整合。 The subtask tree records task decomposition induced by delegation, while the claim DAG records the emergent propagation and integration of reasoning. 具体而言，子任务树规定工作结构，而主张 DAG 刻画协调结构。 In particular, the subtask tree prescribes work structure, whereas the claim DAG captures coordination structure. 我们的事件级可观测量主要在主张 DAG 及其相关级联上定义，而委派级联则从子任务树中获得。 Our event-level observables are defined primarily on the claim DAG and its associated cascades, with delegation cascades obtained from the subtask tree.
可复现性。Reproducibility.
这一构建过程确保论文中报告的所有协调可观测量均由结构化轨迹计算得出，而非由人工标注或通过启发式方法强行设定的交互图计算得出。 This construction procedure ensures that all coordination observables reported in the paper are computed from structured traces rather than manually annotated or heuristically imposed interaction graphs. 根据第 E.3 节中记录的字段，可以完整重建子任务树、主张 DAG 以及级联归属。 Given the logged fields in Section E.3, the subtask tree, claim DAG, and cascade assignments are fully reconstructable.
