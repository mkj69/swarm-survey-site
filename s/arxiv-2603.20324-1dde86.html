Abstract摘要
Multi-agent LLM pipelines produce contradictory evidence on whether team diversity improves output quality: heterogeneous Mixture-of-Agents teams outperform single models, yet homogeneous Self-MoA teams consistently win under synthesis-based aggregation. We propose a resolution by identifying the selection bottleneck—a crossover threshold in aggregation quality that determines whether diversity helps or hurts. Under this model, we obtain a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ (Proposition 3.3) that separates the regimes where diversity helps and hurts. In a targeted experiment spanning 42 tasks across 7 categories ( $N=210$ ), a diverse team with judge-based selection achieves a win rate of 0.810 against a single-model baseline, while a homogeneous team scores 0.512—near chance (Glass’s $\Delta=2.07$ ). Judge-based selection outperforms MoA-style synthesis by $\Delta_{\mathrm{WR}}=+0.631$ —the synthesis approach is preferred over the baseline in zero of 42 tasks by the judge panel. A decoupled evaluation with fully independent judges confirms all directional findings (Spearman $\rho=0.90$ ); win rates attenuate by 53–67% under independent evaluation relative to the primary estimates, consistent with partial measurement circularity in the judge-based cells. Exploratory evidence suggests that including a weaker model improves performance while reducing cost ( $p<10^{-4}$ , not pre-registered). Our results suggest that selector quality may be a more impactful design lever than generator diversity in single-round generate-then-select pipelines, with a specific Opus-only singleton as the baseline.
关于团队多样性是否能提高输出质量，多智能体 LLM 流水线给出了相互矛盾的证据：异构 Mixture-of-Agents 团队优于单个模型，但在基于综合的聚合方式下，同构 Self-MoA 团队始终更胜一筹。我们通过识别选择瓶颈来解释这一矛盾：它是聚合质量的一个交叉阈值，决定了多样性究竟有益还是有害。在该模型下，我们推导出交叉阈值 $s^{*}$ 的闭式表达式（命题 3.3），该阈值划分了多样性有益与有害的两种情形。在一项涵盖 7 个类别、42 项任务的针对性实验中（$N=210$），采用基于评判模型的选择方式时，多样化团队相对于单模型基线的胜率达到 0.810，而同构团队的胜率为 0.512，接近随机水平（Glass’s $\Delta=2.07$）。基于评判模型的选择方式优于 MoA 式综合，其差值为 $\Delta_{\mathrm{WR}}=+0.631$——在 42 项任务中，评判小组没有在任何一项任务上认为综合方法优于基线。采用完全独立评判模型的解耦评估证实了所有方向性发现（Spearman $\rho=0.90$）；相较于主要估计结果，独立评估下的胜率降低了 53–67%，这与采用评判模型选择的实验单元中存在部分测量循环性的情况一致。探索性证据表明，纳入一个较弱的模型能够在降低成本的同时提高性能（$p<10^{-4}$，未预注册）。我们的结果表明，在以某个特定的仅含 Opus 的单模型配置为基线的单轮先生成后选择流水线中，选择器质量可能比生成器多样性更能影响设计效果。

1 Introduction1 引言
“A group of diverse problem solvers can outperform a group of high-ability problem solvers”—so concluded Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) in one of the most cited results in collective intelligence. Two decades later, the multi-agent LLM community is reliably reproducing both sides of this claim. Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) demonstrated that mixing models from different families into a Mixture-of-Agents architecture improves response quality over any single model, declaring their approach “achieve[s] state-of-the-art on AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench, and FLASK.” Li et al. Li et al. (2025) tested the same premise under controlled conditions and reached the opposite conclusion: Self-MoA—a homogeneous team of identical models—“consistently outperforms Mixed-MoA across all benchmarks.” Both papers are methodologically sound. Both claim generality, yet their conclusions appear contradictory.
“一群多样化的问题解决者可以胜过一群能力出众的问题解决者”——Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) 在集体智能领域被引用最多的研究成果之一中得出了这一结论。二十年后，多智能体 LLM 研究界正在稳定地复现支持和反对这一论断的两类结果。Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) 表明，将不同系列的模型混合到 Mixture-of-Agents 架构中，可以获得优于任何单个模型的回答质量，并宣称其方法“在 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench 和 FLASK 上达到了最先进水平”。Li et al. Li et al. (2025) 在受控条件下检验了同一前提，却得出了相反的结论：Self-MoA——由相同模型组成的同构团队——“在所有基准测试中始终优于 Mixed-MoA”。两篇论文在方法上都严谨可靠。两者都声称其结论具有普遍性，但这些结论看起来相互矛盾。
We identify a mechanism that reconciles this tension: the selection bottleneck. Whether a diverse team’s high-variance candidate pool is an asset or a liability depends entirely on how those candidates are aggregated. A synthesis-based aggregator—the method both prior works employed—compresses all candidates into one blended response, forfeiting the advantage of having generated a standout candidate. A selection-based aggregator evaluates candidates individually and picks the best, exploiting exactly the variance that synthesis wastes. The distinction is not a nuance; it is the dominant factor determining whether diversity helps.
我们识别出了一种能够调和这一矛盾的机制：选择瓶颈。多样化团队所产生的高方差候选集合究竟是优势还是负担，完全取决于如何聚合这些候选。基于综合的聚合器——此前两项研究均采用的方法——将所有候选压缩为一个融合后的回答，从而丧失了生成出一个出色候选所带来的优势。基于选择的聚合器逐一评估候选并选出最佳者，恰好利用了综合方式所浪费的方差。这种区别并非细枝末节；它是决定多样性是否有益的主导因素。
In a targeted experiment crossing team composition with aggregation mechanism across 42 tasks spanning seven categories, we observe a crossover. A diverse team dramatically outperforms a homogeneous team under judge-based selection, but the same diverse team provides no advantage under majority voting. The largest effect in our data is the comparison practitioners most need: judge-based selection versus MoA-style synthesis. Selection wins in every single one of the 42 tasks across all 7 categories. The synthesis approach, far from combining the best of multiple agents, produces outputs that lose to a single-model baseline over 80% of the time. Win rates reported throughout this paper are Bradley–Terry-corrected (BT-WR; formally defined in Section 3.11). The apparent paradox may dissolve: Li et al. and Wang et al. each describe one side of a crossover governed by aggregator quality.
在一项涵盖七个类别、42 项任务，并将团队构成与聚合机制交叉组合的针对性实验中，我们观察到了一种交叉现象。在基于评判模型的选择方式下，多样化团队显著优于同构团队，但同一个多样化团队在多数投票方式下并未表现出优势。我们数据中最大的效应来自实践者最需要的比较：基于评判模型的选择与 MoA 式综合之间的比较。在全部 7 个类别的 42 项任务中，选择方式在每一项任务上都胜出。综合方法远未能汇集多个智能体各自的最佳表现，其输出反而在超过 80% 的情况下输给了单模型基线。本文通篇报告的胜率均经过 Bradley–Terry 校正（BT-WR；正式定义见第 3.11 节）。这一表面上的悖论或许可以化解：Li et al. 和 Wang et al. 分别描述了由聚合器质量决定的交叉现象的两侧。
A simple linear model makes this precise. We define a selector quality parameter $s\in[0,1]$ and obtain a crossover threshold $s^{*}$ below which diversity hurts and above which it helps (Proposition 3.3). The model predicts that selector quality should have no effect for homogeneous teams, consistent with the observed near-chance performance of the homogeneous cell in our data. When all candidates are drawn from the same distribution, there is nothing for even a perfect selector to exploit. Figure 1 illustrates the crossover geometry.
一个简单的线性模型使这一点得到了精确表述。我们定义了选择器质量参数 $s\in[0,1]$，并推导出交叉阈值 $s^{*}$：低于该阈值时，多样性有害；高于该阈值时，多样性有益（命题 3.3）。该模型预测，选择器质量应当对同构团队没有影响，这与我们数据中同构实验单元的表现接近随机水平这一观察结果一致。当所有候选输出均来自同一分布时，即使是完美的选择器也无从利用任何差异。图 1 展示了交叉的几何关系。
One finding warrants particular attention, though we label it exploratory because it was not pre-registered. Adding a substantially weaker model (Claude Haiku) to a strong diverse team is associated with a significantly higher win rate and lower cost. The mechanism is plausible: a model from a different capability tier introduces orthogonal error patterns, raising the oracle ceiling even as it lowers the team mean. A strong selector captures the upside and ignores the downside.
有一项发现尤其值得关注，不过由于它未经预注册，我们将其标记为探索性发现。在一个实力强且具有多样性的团队中加入一个明显较弱的模型（Claude Haiku），与显著更高的胜率和更低的成本相关。这一机制是合理的：来自不同能力层级的模型引入了正交的错误模式，在降低团队平均水平的同时，提高了理想选择器所能达到的性能上限。强大的选择器能够获取其收益，并忽略其不利影响。
This paper makes three contributions. First, we introduce the selection bottleneck model—an analytical explanatory lens that yields a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ unifying pro-diversity and anti-diversity findings (Proposition 3.3). The threshold offers one operationalization of Hong and Page’s abstract notion of an “effective aggregation mechanism.” Second, we present empirical evidence that selection dramatically outperforms synthesis—the aggregation mechanism used by both Wang et al. and Li et al.—with the largest effect size in our study, demonstrating that the MoA paradigm of blending candidates is substantially outperformed by selection in our setting. Third, we report exploratory evidence of a weak-model paradox: including a cheaper, weaker model may simultaneously improve quality and reduce cost, a finding that, if replicated, inverts conventional wisdom about team composition.
本文作出了三项贡献。首先，我们提出选择瓶颈模型——一种分析性的解释视角，它给出了闭式交叉阈值 $s^{*}$，将支持多样性与反对多样性的研究发现统一起来（命题 3.3）。这一阈值为 Hong 和 Page 提出的“有效聚合机制”这一抽象概念提供了一种操作化方式。其次，我们提供了实证证据，表明选择显著优于合成——Wang 等人与 Li 等人均采用的聚合机制——且这一比较的效应量在本研究中最大，说明在我们的研究设置下，选择的表现显著优于融合候选输出的 MoA 范式。第三，我们报告了弱模型悖论的探索性证据：加入一个更便宜、更弱的模型，可能同时提高质量并降低成本；如果这一发现得到重复验证，它将颠覆关于团队构成的传统认识。

2 Related Work2 相关工作
Multi-agent architectures for large language models have moved from curiosity to engineering pattern in under two years, yet the field lacks a unifying account of when combining models helps and when it does not. We organize prior work into five threads and identify the gap our framework addresses.
面向大语言模型的多智能体架构在不到两年的时间里，已从新奇的研究尝试发展为一种工程模式，但该领域仍缺乏统一的解释，来说明组合模型何时有益、何时无益。我们将既有研究梳理为五条脉络，并指出我们的框架所填补的空白。
2.1 Multi-Agent Debate and Frameworks2.1 多智能体辩论与框架
Multi-agent frameworks such as AutoGen Wu et al. (2023), CAMEL Li et al. (2023), and MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023) enable flexible orchestration topologies where agents converse and collaborate. Du et al. Du et al. (2023) showed that multi-agent debate improves factual accuracy by forcing agents to defend claims against challenges. Smit et al. Smit et al. (2023) found substantial variation across task types, and Choi et al. Choi et al. (2025) uncovered identity bias—LLMs preferentially agree with their own prior outputs—undermining the independence assumption. Identity bias is directly relevant to our framework: it manifests as elevated inter-agent correlation $\rho$ , reducing the effective sample size of any aggregation mechanism that relies on independence.
AutoGen Wu et al. (2023)、CAMEL Li et al. (2023) 和 MetaGPT Hong et al. (2023) 等多智能体框架支持灵活的编排拓扑，使智能体能够相互对话与协作。Du 等人 Du et al. (2023) 表明，多智能体辩论通过迫使智能体在受到质疑时为其主张辩护，提高了事实准确性。Smit 等人 Smit et al. (2023) 发现，不同任务类型之间存在显著差异；Choi 等人 Choi et al. (2025) 则揭示了身份偏差——LLM 更倾向于赞同自身先前的输出——这削弱了独立性假设。身份偏差与我们的框架直接相关：它表现为智能体间相关性 $\rho$ 升高，从而减小任何依赖独立性的聚合机制的有效样本量。
2.2 Mixture-of-Agents and Self-MoA2.2 Mixture-of-Agents 与 Self-MoA
Wang et al. Wang et al. (2024) proposed Mixture-of-Agents (MoA), a layered architecture in which diverse “proposer” models generate candidates that an “aggregator” synthesizes into a final output. MoA with heterogeneous proposers outperformed any single model. Li et al. Li et al. (2025) challenged this with Self-MoA, reporting that homogeneous teams “consistently outperform Mixed-MoA.” Both analyses share a critical blind spot: neither varied the aggregation mechanism. Both used synthesis-based aggregation, operating at a fixed selector quality $s$ that our framework identifies as the key moderator. The contradiction is not about diversity per se but about whether the aggregator operates above or below the crossover threshold $s^{*}$ .
Wang 等人 Wang et al. (2024) 提出了 Mixture-of-Agents（MoA），这是一种分层架构，其中多样化的“提议者”模型生成候选输出，再由一个“聚合器”将其合成为最终输出。采用异质提议者的 MoA 优于任何单一模型。Li 等人 Li et al. (2025) 通过 Self-MoA 对此提出挑战，报告称同质化团队“始终优于 Mixed-MoA”。这两项分析存在一个共同的关键盲点：二者均未改变聚合机制。二者都采用了基于合成的聚合方式，在固定的选择器质量 $s$ 下运行，而我们的框架将其识别为关键调节变量。这一矛盾并不在于多样性本身，而在于聚合器的运行水平是高于还是低于交叉阈值 $s^{*}$。
2.3 Selection and Routing2.3 选择与路由
Jiang et al. Jiang et al. (2023) proposed LLM-Blender, a two-stage pipeline that ranks candidate outputs using a learned scoring model then fuses the top candidates—the ranking stage is precisely a selection mechanism in our sense. Chen et al. Chen et al. (2025) extended this to per-query routing with LLMSelector. Their approach is complementary: they select which model to call, while we select which output to keep. Post-hoc selection over a candidate pool can exploit instance-level variance that pre-hoc routing cannot observe. Our judge-based selection is related to best-of- $N$ sampling with reward models Stiennon et al. (2020); Snell et al. (2024), a well-studied approach in the inference-time compute scaling literature. The key difference is that best-of- $N$ generates multiple samples from a single model, while our framework generates from diverse models. Self-consistency Wang et al. (2023) similarly samples multiple reasoning paths and aggregates via majority vote; our selection mechanism generalizes this by using a judge to evaluate open-ended outputs where voting has no natural aggregation target.
Jiang 等人 Jiang et al. (2023) 提出了 LLM-Blender，这是一种两阶段流程，先使用训练得到的评分模型对候选输出进行排序，再融合排名靠前的候选输出——其中的排序阶段恰好就是我们所指的选择机制。Chen 等人 Chen et al. (2025) 通过 LLMSelector 将这一方法扩展至针对每条查询的路由。他们的方法与我们的方法互为补充：他们选择调用哪个模型，而我们选择保留哪个输出。对候选池进行事后选择，可以利用事前路由无法观测到的实例层面的变异。我们基于评判器的选择与使用奖励模型的 best-of- $N$ 采样相关 Stiennon et al. (2020); Snell et al. (2024)，后者是推理时计算扩展研究中一种得到充分研究的方法。关键区别在于，best-of- $N$ 从单一模型生成多个样本，而我们的框架则使用多样化的模型生成样本。自一致性（Self-consistency）方法 Wang et al. (2023) 同样采样多条推理路径，并通过多数投票进行聚合；我们的选择机制对此进行了推广，使用评判者评估开放式输出，而在这类输出中，投票并没有自然的聚合目标。
2.4 Diversity Theory2.4 多样性理论
Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) proved that under certain conditions, a diverse group outperforms a group of individually superior agents—but their theorem requires an “effective aggregation mechanism” without specifying what makes it effective. Tang et al. Tang et al. (2026) provided complementary empirical evidence but did not isolate the aggregation mechanism’s role. Our crossover threshold $s^{*}$ provides exactly this quantification. The classical Condorcet Jury Theorem de Condorcet (1785) shows that majority vote among independent jurors converges to correctness, but Ladha Ladha (1992) showed that positive inter-voter correlation weakens the theorem—a prescient result for LLM agents. Li et al. Li et al. (2024) show that majority voting improves with agent count; our results suggest this scaling depends critically on task type and may not hold for open-ended generation where voting has no natural aggregation target. We provide empirical evidence from Chatbot Arena and MT-Bench that inter-model correlation is substantially higher within families than across them (Section 3.5).
Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) 证明，在某些条件下，多样化群体的表现优于由个体能力更强的智能体组成的群体——但他们的定理要求存在一种“有效的聚合机制”，却未说明何种因素使其有效。Tang et al. Tang et al. (2026) 提供了互补的实证证据，但并未单独考察聚合机制的作用。我们的交叉阈值 $s^{*}$ 恰好提供了这种量化。经典的孔多塞陪审团定理 de Condorcet (1785) 表明，独立陪审员的多数投票结果会收敛于正确答案，但 Ladha Ladha (1992) 表明，投票者之间的正相关会削弱该定理的结论——这一结果对 LLM 智能体而言颇具先见之明。Li et al. Li et al. (2024) 表明，多数投票的效果随智能体数量增加而改善；我们的结果表明，这种规模效应在很大程度上取决于任务类型，并且可能不适用于投票没有自然聚合目标的开放式生成任务。我们提供了来自 Chatbot Arena 和 MT-Bench 的实证证据，表明同一模型家族内的模型间相关性显著高于不同家族之间的相关性（第 3.5 节）。
2.5 LLM-as-Judge2.5 LLM 作为评判者
Zheng et al. Zheng et al. (2023) established that strong LLMs achieve high agreement with human raters, making LLM-as-judge a practical evaluation tool. Stureborg et al. Stureborg et al. (2024) documented systematic biases (verbosity, position, self-enhancement), and Panickssery et al. Panickssery et al. (2024) showed evaluators are susceptible to sycophancy. Verga et al. Verga et al. (2024) proposed using panels of diverse judges to mitigate biases—an approach we adopt. Our evaluation protocol randomizes candidate order, enforces agent–judge separation, and uses a multi-judge panel.
Zheng et al. Zheng et al. (2023) 证实，能力较强的 LLM 与人类评分者具有高度一致性，使 LLM 作为评判者成为一种实用的评估工具。Stureborg et al. Stureborg et al. (2024) 记录了系统性偏差（冗长偏差、位置偏差、自我偏好偏差），而 Panickssery et al. Panickssery et al. 3 Materials and Methods3 材料与方法
We model multi-agent LLM pipelines as a two-stage process—generation of a candidate set, followed by aggregation of a final output—and derive the conditions under which team diversity helps or hurts. We then describe the experimental protocol used to test these predictions. The core intuition is simple: a scout choosing the best performer from a diverse talent pool will do better than one choosing from a homogeneous group—but only if the scout is skilled enough to identify the best performer. The model formalizes this threshold, and the experiment tests whether judge-based selection clears it.
我们将多智能体 LLM 流程建模为一个两阶段过程——先生成候选集合，再聚合得到最终输出——并推导团队多样性产生有利或不利影响的条件。随后，我们介绍用于检验这些预测的实验方案。核心直觉很简单：从多样化人才库中选出最佳人选的星探，会比从同质群体中挑选的星探做得更好——但前提是该星探有足够的能力识别出最佳人选。该模型将这一阈值形式化，而实验则检验基于评判者的选择是否能够超过这一阈值。
3.1 Setup and Notation3.1 设定与符号
A team $T=\{m_{1},\ldots,m_{n}\}$ consists of $n$ LLM agents, each producing one candidate response to a given input. Each candidate carries a latent quality score $q_{i}$ drawn from a model-specific distribution $F_{i}$ ; we write $\mu_{i}=\mathbb{E}[q_{i}]$ for agent $i$ ’s expected quality. A homogeneous team has $F_{i}=F_{j}$ for all $i,j$ ; a diverse team has $F_{i}\neq F_{j}$ for at least one pair.
团队 $T=\{m_{1},\ldots,m_{n}\}$ 由 $n$ 个 LLM 智能体组成，每个智能体针对给定输入生成一个候选回答。每个候选回答都有一个从模型特定分布 $F_{i}$ 中抽取的潜在质量分数 $q_{i}$；我们用 $\mu_{i}=\mathbb{E}[q_{i}]$ 表示智能体 $i$ 的期望质量。对于同质团队，所有 $i,j$ 均满足 $F_{i}=F_{j}$；对于多样化团队，至少有一对满足 $F_{i}\neq F_{j}$。
Two statistics summarize the candidate pool:
以下两个统计量概括了候选池的特征：
Team mean: $M(T)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mu_{i}$ , the expected quality of a randomly chosen candidate.
团队均值：$M(T)=\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mu_{i}$，即随机选取的候选回答的期望质量。
Team oracle: $O(T)=\mathbb{E}[\max_{i}\,q_{i}]$ , the expected quality of the best candidate.
团队预言机值（team oracle）：$O(T)=\mathbb{E}[\max_{i}\,q_{i}]$，即最佳候选回答的期望质量。
The gap $\Delta(T)=O(T)-M(T)\geq 0$ measures the team’s exploitable diversity: the gain available to a perfect selector over a random one. A selector $\mathcal{S}$ is a (possibly randomized) function that maps the candidate pool $(q_{1},\ldots,q_{n})$ to a single index $\mathcal{S}(q_{1},\ldots,q_{n})\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$ . We define the selector quality as:
差值 $\Delta(T)=O(T)-M(T)\geq 0$ 衡量团队可利用的多样性：即完美选择器相较于随机选择器所能获得的增益。选择器 $\mathcal{S}$ 是一个（可能随机化的）函数，将候选池 $(q_{1},\ldots,q_{n})$ 映射为单个索引 $\mathcal{S}(q_{1},\ldots,q_{n})\in\{1,\ldots,n\}$。我们将选择器质量定义为：
$$s(\mathcal{S},T)\;=\;\frac{\mathbb{E}[q_{\mathcal{S}}]-M(T)}{O(T)-M(T)},$$
provided $O(T)>M(T)$ . A random selector has $s=0$ ; a perfect selector (always choosing $\arg\max_{i}q_{i}$ ) has $s=1$ .
前提是 $O(T)>M(T)$。随机选择器满足 $s=0$；完美选择器（始终选择 $\arg\max_{i}q_{i}$）满足 $s=1$。
3.2 The Selection Quality Model3.2 选择质量模型
We model the expected output quality as a linear interpolation between the team mean and the team oracle, governed by selector quality.
我们将期望输出质量建模为团队均值与团队预言机值之间的线性插值，由选择器质量控制。
Assumption 3.1 (Linear Selection Model).假设 3.1（线性选择模型）。
For a team $T$ with selector quality $s\in[0,1]$ , the expected quality of the selected output is:
对于选择器质量为 $s\in[0,1]$ 的团队 $T$，所选输出的期望质量为：
$$Q(T,s)\;=\;s\cdot O(T)\;+\;(1-s)\cdot M(T).$$
We adopt $[0,1]$ as the natural range for proper selection mechanisms. Destructive aggregators (e.g., synthesis-based blending) may effectively operate at $s<0$ , as discussed in Assumption 3.6 and the subsequent Remark.
我们采用 $[0,1]$ 作为合理选择机制的自然取值范围。破坏性聚合器（例如基于合成的混合）实际上可能在 $s<0$ 的范围内运作，如假设 3.6 及其后的评注所述。
This is a modeling choice, not a derived result. We adopt it for two reasons. First, Eq. (2) is exact when $n=2$ and the selector operates as a noisy argmax with Gaussian noise. Second, its predictions match the observed data: the model’s calibrated $s^{*}$ places the empirical crossover between diversity-helps and diversity-hurts conditions within a narrow confidence band (Section 4.4).
这是一种建模选择，而非推导所得的结果。我们采用这一选择有两个原因。首先，当 $n=2$ 且选择器以带有高斯噪声的含噪 argmax 方式运作时，式 (2) 精确成立。其次，其预测与观测数据相符：模型校准得到的 $s^{*}$ 将多样性有益与多样性有害两种条件之间的实证交叉点定位在一个狭窄的置信区间内（第 4.4 节）。
We are candid about what this model is and is not. It is a descriptive parameterization—a lens for organizing empirical findings and generating testable predictions. Its value lies in predictive power, not mathematical depth. The assumption trades generality for interpretability: a single parameter $s$ compresses the complex behavior of real selectors into a quantity that can be estimated, compared, and communicated to practitioners.
我们坦率说明这一模型是什么，以及它不是什么。它是一种描述性参数化表述——为组织实证发现和生成可检验的预测提供了一个视角。它的价值在于预测能力，而非数学深度。这一假设以牺牲一般性来换取可解释性：单个参数 $s$ 将真实选择器的复杂行为压缩为一个可以估计、比较并向实践者传达的量。
Assumption 3.2 (Homogeneous Team Invariance).假设 3.2（同质团队不变性）。
For a homogeneous team $T_{h}$ (all agents drawn from the same distribution $F$ ), within-model sampling variation provides negligible material for selection: $O(T_{h})\approx M(T_{h})=\mu_{F}$ , so that $Q(T_{h},s)\approx\mu_{F}$ for all $s\in[0,1]$ .
对于同质团队 $T_{h}$（所有智能体均来自同一分布 $F$），同一模型内部的采样变异几乎无法提供可供选择利用的差异：$O(T_{h})\approx M(T_{h})=\mu_{F}$，因此对于所有 $s\in[0,1]$，均有 $Q(T_{h},s)\approx\mu_{F}$。
This assumption is motivated by the observation that same-model copies at moderate temperature produce functionally identical outputs—our decoupled evaluation confirms this: all 756 pairwise verdicts from independent judges were ties for the homogeneous cell (Section 3.9). We acknowledge that complete tie rates can in principle reflect judge insensitivity to minor stylistic variation rather than true indistinguishability. We interpret the result as a validation rather than an artifact: the independent judges, who never participated in any selection decision, still cannot distinguish between the three Opus outputs, which supports the practical force of Assumption 3.2. The distinction matters little operationally—whether outputs are “identical” or merely “indistinguishable to judges,” the selector cannot exploit what the evaluator cannot differentiate.
这一假设源于如下观察：同一模型的多个副本在适中温度下会产生功能上相同的输出——我们的解耦评估证实了这一点：对于同质实验单元，独立评判者给出的全部 756 次成对比较判定均为平局（第 3.9 节）。我们承认，原则上，全部判为平局可能反映的是评判者对细微风格差异不敏感，而非输出确实无法区分。我们将这一结果解释为对假设的验证，而非伪象：从未参与任何选择决策的独立评判者仍然无法区分三个 Opus 输出，这支持了假设 3.2 的实际效力。这一区别在实际操作中影响甚微——无论输出是“完全相同”，还是仅仅“评判者无法区分”，选择器都无法利用评估者无法辨别的差异。

3.4 Optimal Team Size3.4 最优团队规模
Remark 3.8 (Team Expansion).注 3.8（团队扩展）。
Under Assumption 3.1, adding agent $m_{n+1}$ with mean quality $\mu_{n+1}$ to team $T_{n}$ changes expected output quality by

$$\delta(n,s)=s\cdot\bigl[O(T_{n+1})-O(T_{n})\bigr]+(1-s)\cdot\bigl[M(T_{n+1})-M(T_{n})\bigr].$$
When $\mu_{n+1}<M(T_{n})$ —i.e., the new agent is below the team average—the second term is negative, creating a trade-off between oracle gain and mean dilution governed by $s$ . 3.5 Connection to Classical Results3.5 与经典结果的联系
Condorcet Jury Theorem. The classical CJT de Condorcet (1785) shows that majority vote among $n$ independent jurors with accuracy $p>0.5$ converges to correctness as $n\to\infty$ . Ladha Ladha (1992) extended this to correlated voters: positive correlation $\rho$ reduces the effective number of independent votes.
孔多塞陪审团定理。经典的孔多塞陪审团定理（CJT），de Condorcet (1785)，表明：当 $n$ 名相互独立且准确率为 $p>0.5$ 的陪审员进行多数投票时，随着 $n\to\infty$，多数投票得出正确结果的概率趋于 1。Ladha，Ladha (1992)，将这一结果扩展到存在相关性的投票者：正相关 $\rho$ 会减少独立投票的有效数量。
LLM agents are not independent. In Chatbot Arena Chiang et al. (2024) (57,477 battles), the tie rate between same-family model pairs is 34.9%, compared to 28.8% for cross-family pairs ( $\chi^{2}=117.61$ , $p<10^{-27}$ ; rate difference 6.1 pp [95% CI: 5.0, 7.2]). In MT-Bench (3,355 human judgments), the same-tier tie rate is 31.9% versus 20.9% cross-tier ( $\chi^{2}=37.46$ , $p<10^{-6}$ ; difference 11.0 pp [95% CI: 7.5, 14.5]). These are not marginal differences—they reflect structural correlations from shared training. Diverse teams reduce $\rho$ by mixing families, which in principle should improve voting accuracy. However, our data show that majority vote achieves only chance-level performance even with a diverse team (WR = 0.496), suggesting that reduced correlation alone is insufficient—the CJT’s requirement that individual juror accuracy exceeds 0.5 may not hold for open-ended generation tasks where “correctness” is ill-defined. The benefit of reduced correlation may instead manifest through improved selection: candidates are less redundant, giving the judge more distinct options.
LLM 智能体并非相互独立。在 Chatbot Arena，Chiang et al. (2024)（57,477 场对战），同一家族模型配对的平局率为 34.9%，而跨家族配对的平局率为 28.8%（$\chi^{2}=117.61$，$p<10^{-27}$；比率差为 6.1 个百分点 [95% CI: 5.0, 7.2]）。在 MT-Bench（3,355 次人工评判）中，同一能力层级模型配对的平局率为 31.9%，而跨能力层级配对的平局率为 20.9%（$\chi^{2}=37.46$，$p<10^{-6}$；差值为 11.0 个百分点 [95% CI: 7.5, 14.5]）。这些差异并非微不足道，而是反映了共同训练所产生的结构性相关性。多样化团队通过混合不同家族的模型来降低 $\rho$，原则上这应当提高投票准确率。然而，我们的数据表明，即使采用多样化团队，多数投票也只能达到随机水平的表现（WR = 0.496），这说明仅降低相关性还不够；对于“正确性”缺乏明确定义的开放式生成任务，CJT 所要求的单个陪审员准确率超过 0.5 这一条件可能并不成立。降低相关性的益处可能转而通过改善选择体现出来：候选答案之间的冗余更少，从而为评判者提供更多不同的选项。
Hong and Page. Hong and Page Hong and Page (2004) proved that diverse groups can outperform ability-selected groups under specific conditions on a discrete optimization landscape—a setting that differs formally from LLM candidate selection. Their result provides conceptual motivation: they showed that an “effective aggregation mechanism” is required but did not specify what makes it effective. Our threshold $s^{*}$ offers one operationalization: diversity wins when selector quality exceeds $s^{*}$ , which can be estimated from pilot data. The model also makes explicit three failure regimes: (1) when $s<s^{*}$ , (2) when the mean-quality gap is too large, and (3) when the team is too small for the oracle advantage to accumulate.
Hong 和 Page。Hong 和 Page，Hong and Page (2004)，证明，在离散优化景观上的特定条件下，多样化群体可以优于按能力筛选组成的群体；这一情境在形式上不同于 LLM 候选答案选择。他们的结果提供了概念上的启发：他们表明需要一种“有效的聚合机制”，但并未明确说明什么使其有效。我们的阈值 $s^{*}$ 提供了一种操作化方式：当选择器质量超过 $s^{*}$ 时，多样性便能带来优势，而 $s^{*}$ 可以根据预实验数据估计。该模型还明确揭示了三种失效情形：(1) 当 $s<s^{*}$ 时，(2) 当平均质量差距过大时，以及 (3) 当团队规模过小，无法累积预言机优势时。

We employ a targeted cell design testing five specific composition $\times$ selector combinations across 42 tasks (7 categories $\times$ 6 tasks each), yielding $5\times 42=210$ experimental runs. 3.8 Selector Mechanisms3.8 选择器机制
Each team’s individual outputs are aggregated via one of three mechanisms:
每个团队的个体输出通过以下三种机制之一进行聚合：
Judge-based selection: An external judge panel—Claude Sonnet, GPT-5-mini, and DeepSeek-V3p2—reads all candidate outputs and selects the best via pairwise comparison with Bradley–Terry scoring (Section 3.11). Candidate order was randomized for each pairwise judge comparison to control for position bias Stureborg et al. (2024).
基于评审的选择：由 Claude Sonnet、GPT-5-mini 和 DeepSeek-V3p2 组成的外部评审小组阅读所有候选输出，并通过采用 Bradley–Terry 评分的成对比较选出最佳输出（第 3.11 节）。每次评审成对比较时，候选输出的顺序均随机排列，以控制位置偏差 Stureborg et al. (2024)。
Majority vote: Each agent independently selects the best candidate from the pool. The candidate receiving the most votes is chosen; ties are broken randomly.
多数投票：每个智能体独立地从候选池中选出最佳候选输出。得票最多的候选输出被选中；若出现平票，则随机决定。
MoA synthesis: Claude Sonnet reads all candidate outputs and produces a single synthesized response that blends elements from each candidate, following the Mixture-of-Agents protocol Wang et al. (2024).
MoA 综合：Claude Sonnet 阅读所有候选输出，并遵循 Mixture-of-Agents 协议，生成一条融合各候选输出要素的综合回答 Wang et al. (2024)。

智能体模型（生成候选输出）：Claude Opus、GPT-5.4、Gemini 2.5 Pro、Claude Haiku。

No model appears in both pools within the same cell. While Sonnet (judge/synthesizer) and Opus (agent) belong to the Anthropic family—a residual same-family concern we address in Section 3.13—they are distinct model checkpoints with different training runs and capability profiles. We report per-judge breakdowns (Section 4.1) confirming that DeepSeek-V3p2, which shares no lineage with any agent, reproduces the same qualitative patterns.
在同一实验单元中，没有任何模型同时出现在这两个池中。尽管 Sonnet（评审／综合器）与 Opus（智能体）同属 Anthropic 家族——这是我们在第 3.13 节讨论的一项残留的同家族疑虑——但它们是不同的模型检查点，来自不同的训练运行，且具有不同的能力特征。我们报告了按评审模型细分的结果（第 4.1 节），证实与任何智能体均无共同谱系的 DeepSeek-V3p2 也呈现出相同的定性模式。
Selection–Evaluation Overlap. We note an important limitation: in judge-based cells, the same three judges that participate in selecting the winning output also provide the pairwise preferences from which BT-WR is computed. This creates a potential self-consistency bias. We mitigate this concern with three observations: (1) the judges also evaluate the vote and synthesis cells, where they did not participate in selection, yet produce consistent relative rankings; (2) the homo_opus+judge cell achieves only WR = 0.512 despite using the same selection-evaluation pipeline, indicating that judges do not systematically inflate selected outputs; and (3) a fully decoupled evaluation pass using three independent judges—GPT-4o-mini, Gemini 2.0 Flash, and GLM-5, none of which participated in any selection decision—confirms that all four directional contrasts survive independent evaluation (Table 2). One independent judge (GPT-4o-mini) proved degenerate, returning ties on 99.6% of 1,260 pairwise comparisons; we therefore report a 2-judge sub-panel (Gemini Flash + GLM-5) as the primary decoupled estimate. Under this panel, win rates attenuate by 53–67% relative to the original estimates—consistent with partial circularity in the original design—but the rank ordering is preserved (Spearman $\rho=0.90$ ). Notably, the homo_opus+judge cell yields $\mathrm{WR}=0.500$ under independent evaluation, with all 756 pairwise verdicts across three judges returning ties, confirming that homogeneous outputs are genuinely indistinguishable rather than an artifact of shared-judge bias. The decoupled pass substantially mitigates the circularity concern; we discuss remaining limitations in Section 5.5.

3.11 Evaluation Metric3.11 评估指标
Our primary dependent variable is the Bradley–Terry-corrected consensus win rate (BT-WR). For each cell, the consensus output is compared against a single-agent baseline—a single Claude Opus call per task at $T=0.7$ with no multi-agent pipeline ( $k=1$ , not best-of- $k$ )—by all three judge models. Each judge provides a pairwise preference; ties are coded as 0.5. Raw preferences are corrected for judge-specific bias using a Bradley–Terry model Bradley and Terry (1952), yielding calibrated win probabilities. BT-WR of 0.500 indicates parity with the baseline.
我们的主要因变量是经 Bradley–Terry 校正的共识胜率（BT-WR）。对于每个实验单元，三个评判模型都会将共识输出与单智能体基线进行比较；该基线对每个任务仅以 $T=0.7$ 调用一次 Claude Opus，不使用多智能体流程（$k=1$，而非 best-of-$k$）。每个评判模型给出成对比较的偏好；平局编码为 0.5。使用 Bradley–Terry 模型 Bradley and Terry (1952) 对原始偏好进行校正，以消除各评判模型特有的偏差，得到校准后的获胜概率。BT-WR 为 0.500 表示与基线表现持平。

3.13 Threats to Validity3.13 效度威胁
Same-family bias. Claude Sonnet (judge) and Claude Opus (agent) share the Anthropic family. If Sonnet harbors a latent preference for Opus-generated text, this could inflate win rates for Opus-containing teams. We mitigate this by reporting per-judge results and confirming that DeepSeek-V3p2, which shares no family with any agent, shows the same qualitative pattern. Synthesis–judge overlap. In the synthesis cell, Claude Sonnet synthesizes candidates and also serves as one of three judges evaluating the synthesis output against the baseline. This creates a potential self-evaluation concern, though the extremely low synthesis win rate (0.179) argues against self-enhancement bias dominating. Selection–evaluation overlap. In judge-based cells, the same judges that select the winning output also provide evaluation preferences. A decoupled evaluation pass with fully independent judges confirms all directional contrasts with attenuated effect sizes (Table 2; see Section 3.9 for details). Task representativeness. Forty-two tasks across seven categories provide broad coverage but cannot represent all use cases. Our findings demonstrate the existence of a composition $\times$ selector interaction, not its precise magnitude across all possible tasks. API variability. We use $T=0.7$ for generation to balance output diversity with coherence, and $T=0.1$ for judge evaluation to promote consistency and reduce noise in pairwise preferences. All runs were completed within a 72-hour window.
同系列偏差。Claude Sonnet（评判模型）和 Claude Opus（智能体）同属 Anthropic 系列。如果 Sonnet 对 Opus 生成的文本存在潜在偏好，就可能抬高包含 Opus 的团队的胜率。我们通过报告各评判模型的结果，并确认与所有智能体均不同系列的 DeepSeek-V3p2 也呈现相同的定性模式，来减轻这一问题。综合与评判的角色重叠。在综合实验单元中，Claude Sonnet 负责综合候选输出，同时还担任三个评判模型之一，评估综合输出相对于基线的表现。这带来了潜在的自我评估问题，但极低的综合胜率（0.179）表明，自我美化偏差并未占据主导地位。选择与评估的角色重叠。在基于评判的实验单元中，选择获胜输出的评判模型也提供评估偏好。采用完全独立的评判模型进行的一轮解耦评估确认了所有对比的方向，但效应量有所减小（表 2；详情见第 3.9 节）。任务代表性。涵盖七个类别的四十二个任务提供了广泛的覆盖，但无法代表所有使用场景。我们的研究结果证明了组成 $\times$ 选择器交互作用的存在，而非其在所有可能任务中的确切大小。API 变异性。我们在生成时使用 $T=0.7$，以平衡输出的多样性与连贯性；在评判模型评估时使用 $T=0.1$，以提高一致性并减少成对比较偏好中的噪声。所有运行均在 72 小时的时间窗口内完成。

Contrast 2: Judge vs. vote. Under majority voting, the same diverse team produces $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.496$ [0.425, 0.563]—statistically indistinguishable from the homogeneous baseline. The judge advantage is $\Delta=+0.313$ [0.232, 0.397], $g=1.61$ [1.12, 2.10], $p=1.06\times 10^{-10}$ ( $p_{\mathrm{adj}}<2\times 10^{-10}$ ). Voting makes diversity inert: without a selector that can identify the best candidate, the diverse team’s oracle advantage goes to waste.
对比 2：评判与投票。采用多数投票时，同一个多样化团队的 $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.496$ [0.425, 0.563]，在统计上与同质基线无显著差异。评判模型带来的优势为 $\Delta=+0.313$ [0.232, 0.397]，$g=1.61$ [1.12, 2.10]，$p=1.06\times 10^{-10}$（$p_{\mathrm{adj}}<2\times 10^{-10}$）。投票使多样性无法发挥作用：如果没有能够识别最佳候选答案的选择器，多样化团队在理想选择下的优势就会被浪费。

The 0.500 phenomenon. Why does homo_opus+judge land near 0.500? When three copies of the same model receive the same prompt at $T=0.7$ , they generate functionally identical outputs—our decoupled evaluation confirms this: all 756 pairwise verdicts across three independent judges returned ties (Section 3.9). This cell is functionally equivalent to best-of- $N$ sampling from a single model with judge-based selection. The near-chance result (WR = 0.512) indicates that, under the fixed-temperature generation protocol used here ( $T=0.7$ ), within-model sampling variation provides negligible material for selection to exploit, confirming that cross-model diversity—not merely multiple candidates—drives the diversity advantage in this setting.

4.4 Calibrating the Crossover Threshold4.4 校准交叉阈值
The five V4 cells confirm the qualitative predictions of Proposition 3.3. Judge-based selection ( $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.810$ ) operates well above the crossover threshold; majority vote ( $0.496$ ) sits at or below it; and synthesis ( $0.179$ ) falls far below. The synthesis result is particularly informative: Assumption 3.6 posits that synthesis, with $s_{\mathrm{synth}}\approx 0$ , should collapse diverse-team quality to the team mean or below. The observed win rate of 0.179—well below the 0.500 baseline—suggests synthesis is not merely non-selective but actively destructive, consistent with the prediction that blending dilutes the best candidate.
V4 的五个实验条件证实了命题 3.3 的定性预测。基于评判者的选择（$\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.810$）的表现远高于交叉阈值；多数投票（$0.496$）处于或低于该阈值；而综合（$0.179$）则远低于该阈值。综合的结果尤其具有启示性：假设 3.6 提出，当 $s_{\mathrm{synth}}\approx 0$ 时，综合应使多样化团队的输出质量降至团队平均水平或更低。观测到的胜率为 0.179，远低于 0.500 的基线，这表明综合不仅不具备选择性，而且会主动造成损害，与混合会削弱最佳候选输出的预测一致。
Monte Carlo calibration on a pilot study (8 tasks, $N=136$ ) yielded $s^{*}\approx 0.567$ [bootstrap 95% CI: 0.48, 0.65; $B=10{,}000$ ]. The V4 scaled experiment is qualitatively consistent: the estimated selector qualities place vote below and judge above this threshold.

The V4 scaled experiment ( $N=210$ , 42 tasks) replicates the pilot ( $N=136$ , 8 tasks) with high fidelity. 5 Discussion5 讨论
5.1 Reconciling Conflicting Prior Work5.1 调和既有研究中的冲突
We stress that the reconciliation of Li et al. and Wang et al. offered by the selection bottleneck model is a hypothesis, not a proven explanation. Our experiment was not designed to replicate either study’s exact protocol. But the pattern is consistent: the field’s conflicting results may reflect incomplete factorial coverage rather than genuine inconsistency. Li et al.’s synthesis-based aggregation operates below $s^{*}$ ; Wang et al.’s multi-round refinement implicitly raises effective selector quality above it. Future work that systematically varies both factors should find the same interaction structure.
我们强调，选择瓶颈模型对 Li 等人与 Wang 等人研究结果的调和是一项假说，而非已获证实的解释。我们的实验并非为复现这两项研究中任何一项的具体实验方案而设计。但结果模式是一致的：该领域相互冲突的结果可能反映了因子组合覆盖不完整，而非真正的不一致。Li 等人基于综合的聚合机制运行于 $s^{*}$ 以下；Wang 等人的多轮改进则隐式地将有效选择器质量提升至该阈值以上。未来系统性地改变这两个因素的研究应当会发现相同的交互作用结构。
5.2 Why Synthesis Fails5.2 综合为何失败
The selection-vs-synthesis comparison ( $g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]) is the largest effect in our data and, we believe, the most practically important finding. The mechanism is straightforward: synthesis averages, selection picks.
选择与综合的比较（$g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]）呈现出我们数据中最大的效应，我们认为，这也是最具实践意义的发现。其机制很直接：综合进行平均，选择进行挑选。
A diverse team’s value lies in the variance of its candidate pool—specifically, the probability that at least one candidate is excellent. A selection-based aggregator captures this value by identifying and preserving the best candidate. A synthesis-based aggregator destroys it by blending all candidates into a single “compromise” output. The resulting synthesis is not the average of the candidates’ qualities but something potentially worse, because blending can introduce incoherence, conflicting perspectives, and diluted arguments that no individual candidate exhibited.
多样化团队的价值在于其候选输出池的方差，具体而言，在于至少一个候选输出表现出色的概率。基于选择的聚合器通过识别并保留最佳候选输出来获取这一价值。基于综合的聚合器则将所有候选输出混合成单一的“折中”输出，从而破坏这一价值。由此产生的综合输出，其质量并非候选输出质量的平均值，而可能更差，因为混合可能引入任何单个候选输出都未曾出现的连贯性不足、观点冲突以及论证被削弱等问题。
The synthesis cell’s $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.179$ means the synthesized output loses to a single model’s output 82% of the time. In the selection bottleneck model, this is exactly what Assumption 3.6 predicts: when $s\approx 0$ (synthesis has no selection capacity), diversity has no mechanism to help. The practical implication is stark: practitioners using MoA-style synthesis should consider switching to judge-based selection, which requires no additional training or infrastructure—only a structured evaluation prompt.
综合实验条件下的 $\mathrm{BT\text{-}WR}=0.179$ 意味着，综合输出在 82% 的情况下会输给单个模型的输出。在选择瓶颈模型中，这正是假设 3.6 所预测的：当 $s\approx 0$（综合不具备选择能力）时，多样性没有发挥益处的机制。其实践启示十分明确：采用 MoA 式综合的实践者应考虑改用基于评判者的选择，这不需要额外的训练或基础设施，只需一个结构化的评估提示词。
We note that our synthesis implementation uses single-round aggregation by Claude Sonnet. Multi-round iterative synthesis, as proposed in the original MoA architecture Wang et al. (2024), may yield different results. Our finding applies specifically to single-round synthesis, which is the more commonly deployed variant due to latency and cost constraints.
我们指出，我们的综合实现采用 Claude Sonnet 进行单轮聚合。Wang 等人（2024）在原始 MoA 架构中提出的多轮迭代综合可能产生不同的结果。我们的发现专门适用于单轮综合；由于延迟和成本限制，这是部署更为普遍的变体。
5.3 Why Weak Models May Help5.3 弱模型为何可能有帮助
The diverse_mixed advantage ( $\Delta=+0.119$ , $g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]) is statistically significant but exploratory (not pre-registered), so we interpret it cautiously. We propose a diversity-distinguishability-selection account. In a pool of three strong-model outputs, all candidates tend to be good but similar. Adding a weaker model increases the variance of the candidate pool, making the best candidate easier to identify by contrast. The key precondition is that the selector be good enough to avoid choosing the weakest candidate.
diverse_mixed 的优势（$\Delta=+0.119$，$g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]）具有统计显著性，但属于探索性发现（未预注册），因此我们对此持谨慎解释。我们提出一种多样性—可区分性—选择的解释框架。在由三个强模型输出构成的候选池中，所有候选输出往往都表现良好，但彼此相似。加入一个较弱的模型会增大候选输出池的方差，使最佳候选输出更容易通过对比被识别出来。关键前提是，选择器必须足够出色，能够避免选中最弱的候选输出。
Supporting evidence comes from the Chatbot Arena dataset, where pairwise model distinguishability correlates with judge agreement rates Chiang et al. (2024). Our diverse_mixed team includes a larger capability gap than diverse_strong, potentially making the judge’s task easier. We emphasize that this evidence is correlational and drawn from a different context.
支持性证据来自 Chatbot Arena 数据集，其中模型两两之间的可区分性与评判者的一致率相关，见 Chiang 等人（2024）。我们的 diverse_mixed 团队内部的能力差距比 diverse_strong 更大，这可能使评判者的任务更容易。我们强调，这一证据仅表明相关性，且来自不同的情境。
5.4 Practical Decision Framework5.4 实用决策框架
Our findings yield three rules for multi-agent pipeline design:
我们的研究结果为多智能体流水线设计提供了三条规则：
Rule 1: Diversify the team. Use models from different families. Replicating the same model provides no benefit regardless of which frontier model is chosen.
规则 1：使团队多样化。使用来自不同系列的模型。无论选择哪种前沿模型，重复使用同一模型都不会带来收益。
Rule 2: Select, don’t synthesize. Judge-based selection outperforms MoA synthesis in every task and category. Even majority voting—which negates diversity—is preferable to synthesis, which actively degrades quality. A judge-based selector requires only a structured evaluation prompt, no additional training.
规则 2：选择，而非综合。在每项任务和每个类别中，基于评判者的选择均优于 MoA 综合。即使是抵消了多样性作用的多数投票，也优于会主动降低质量的综合。基于评判者的选择器只需要结构化的评估提示，无需额外训练。
Rule 3: Consider weaker models. If the weak-model advantage replicates, the optimal team includes a cheaper model from a different capability tier. Table 5 shows that diverse_mixed+judge is simultaneously the highest-performing and cheapest configuration.

5.5 Limitations5.5 局限性
Targeted design. Our five-cell design maximizes power for specific contrasts but does not estimate all possible interactions (e.g., homogeneous+vote, diverse_mixed+synthesis). A full factorial would enable richer interaction analyses.
针对性设计。我们的五实验单元设计最大限度地提高了特定对比的统计功效，但并未估计所有可能的交互作用（例如 homogeneous+vote、diverse_mixed+synthesis）。全因子设计将支持更丰富的交互作用分析。
Model specificity and baseline scope. Results are demonstrated for a specific set of frontier models using an Opus-only homogeneous run as the single-model baseline. Comparing against the strongest individual model across the full diverse candidate pool might yield different quantitative estimates; whether the selector advantage survives that stricter comparison is left to future work. Whether the same patterns hold for other model families or future generations is also an open question.
模型特异性与基线范围。这些结果是在一组特定的前沿模型上得到的，并将仅使用 Opus 的同质运行作为单模型基线。若与整个多样化候选池中最强的单个模型进行比较，可能会得到不同的定量估计；选择器的优势能否在这一更严格的比较中依然成立，有待未来研究。相同模式是否适用于其他模型系列或未来几代模型，也仍是一个开放问题。
Fixed generation temperature. All generation runs use $T=0.7$ . Varying temperature could alter within-model output variance and potentially affect the relative performance of homogeneous versus diverse teams. The conclusion that homogeneous sampling variation is negligible is therefore specific to moderate fixed-temperature settings.
固定的生成温度。所有生成运行均使用 $T=0.7$。改变温度可能会改变同一模型的输出方差，并可能影响同质团队与多样化团队的相对表现。因此，同质采样变异可忽略不计这一结论，仅适用于固定为中等温度的设置。
LLM-as-judge and subjective tasks. LLM-based evaluation remains a proxy for human judgment. For open-ended subjective categories—creative writing and ethics/policy in our task battery—LLM judges are known to exhibit stylistic preferences and may agree less with human raters than on analytical tasks Zheng et al. (2023). We do not have human evaluation data for these categories, and the extent to which our results generalize to human preferences in subjective domains is unknown. Human evaluation on a representative subset of tasks, particularly creative and policy tasks, is a priority for future work. Our decoupled evaluation pass (Table 2) partially addresses selection–evaluation circularity, confirming all directional contrasts under independent judges, but the independent panel itself exhibited limitations: one of three judges (GPT-4o-mini) proved degenerate (99.6% tie rate), reducing the effective independent panel to two judges. Per-judge tie rates varied substantially (GPT-4o-mini: 99.6%, Gemini Flash: 76.7%, GLM-5: 50.3%), suggesting that weaker models may lack the discriminative capacity for reliable pairwise evaluation. The synthesis–judge overlap (Claude Sonnet serving as both synthesizer and one of three judges) remains a specific concern, though the very low synthesis win rate argues against self-enhancement bias as a primary driver.
以 LLM 为评判者与主观任务。基于 LLM 的评估仍然是人类判断的替代指标。对于开放式主观类别——在我们的任务集中即创意写作和伦理/政策——已有研究表明，LLM 评判者存在文体偏好，且其与人类评分者的一致性可能低于分析性任务中的一致性 Zheng et al. (2023)。我们没有这些类别的人类评估数据，因此尚不清楚我们的结果在多大程度上能够推广至主观领域中的人类偏好。未来研究的一项优先任务是对具有代表性的任务子集进行人类评估，尤其是创意和政策任务。我们的解耦评估轮次（表 2）部分解决了选择与评估之间的循环性问题，在独立评判者的评估下确认了所有对比的方向，但独立评审组本身也表现出局限性：三名评判者中的一名（GPT-4o-mini）出现了退化表现（平局率为 99.6%），使有效的独立评审组缩减为两名评判者。各评判者的平局率差异显著（GPT-4o-mini：99.6%，Gemini Flash：76.7%，GLM-5：50.3%），这表明较弱的模型可能缺乏进行可靠成对评估所需的区分能力。综合者与评判者的重叠（Claude Sonnet 同时担任综合者和三名评判者之一）仍是一个具体的隐忧，不过，极低的综合胜率表明，自我增强偏差不太可能是主要驱动因素。
Bounded dependent variable. Win rates are bounded in $[0,1]$ , yet we model them linearly. Our observed values (0.13–0.96) avoid extreme floor/ceiling effects, and we verified that logit-transformed results are qualitatively identical.
有界因变量。胜率的取值范围为 $[0,1]$，但我们仍采用线性模型对其建模。我们的观测值（0.13–0.96）未出现极端的地板效应或天花板效应，并且我们验证了，经 logit 变换后的结果在定性上完全一致。
Static topology. All experiments use a single-round generate-then-select pipeline. Iterative topologies (multi-round debate, recursive refinement) may exhibit different dynamics.
静态拓扑。所有实验均采用单轮的先生成后选择流程。迭代式拓扑（多轮辩论、递归改进）可能呈现不同的动态特征。
Distinguishability not measured. Our framework invokes output distinguishability ( $s$ ) as the key mediator, but we do not measure it directly. Future work should operationalize distinguishability via embedding-space distances.
未测量可区分性。我们的框架将输出可区分性（$s$）作为关键中介变量，但我们并未直接测量它。未来工作应通过嵌入空间中的距离将可区分性操作化。
Diverse_mixed confound. The diverse_mixed cell simultaneously changes capability (replacing GPT-5.4 with Claude Haiku) and family diversity (two Anthropic models instead of one). We cannot isolate these effects and flag this as a design limitation of the exploratory comparison.
Diverse_mixed 的混杂问题。diverse_mixed 实验条件同时改变了能力（以 Claude Haiku 替换 GPT-5.4）和模型家族多样性（使用两个 Anthropic 模型，而非一个）。我们无法分离这些效应，并将其明确列为这项探索性比较的设计局限。
5.6 Future Work5.6 未来工作
Three directions follow. First, replicating the weak-model finding with a pre-registered design would move it from exploratory to confirmatory. Second, human evaluation at scale would validate the LLM-as-judge proxy and quantify its bias structure. Third, varying selector quality continuously (e.g., by progressively weakening the judge model) would map the capability threshold below which judge-based selection fails, testing whether the “architecture $>$ capability” finding observed in our pilot has a lower bound.
未来工作有以下三个方向。第一，采用预注册设计复现有关弱模型的发现，可使其从探索性发现转变为验证性发现。第二，大规模人工评估可验证以大语言模型作为评判者（LLM-as-judge）这一代理评估方式的有效性，并量化其偏差结构。第三，连续改变选择器的质量（例如，逐步削弱评判模型）可确定基于评判者的选择失效时所对应的能力阈值，从而检验我们在先导研究中观察到的“架构 $>$ 能力”这一发现是否存在下限。
6 Conclusions6 结论
This paper makes three contributions. First, the selection bottleneck model—an analytical explanatory lens—provides a closed-form crossover threshold $s^{*}$ (Proposition 3.3) unifying pro-diversity and anti-diversity findings: when aggregation operates below $s^{*}$ , diversity hurts; above it, diversity helps. Second, our targeted experiment demonstrates that selection-based aggregation dramatically outperforms MoA-style synthesis ( $g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]), with the synthesis approach losing to a single-model baseline in all 42 tasks—a finding that challenges the dominant pipeline paradigm. Third, we report exploratory evidence that including a weaker, cheaper model paradoxically improves performance ( $g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]), suggesting that optimal team composition may not require all-frontier models.
本文作出了三项贡献。第一，选择瓶颈模型作为一种分析性的解释视角，给出了闭式的交叉阈值 $s^{*}$（命题 3.3），统一了支持多样性与反对多样性的研究发现：当聚合机制的运行水平低于 $s^{*}$ 时，多样性有害；高于该阈值时，多样性有益。第二，我们的针对性实验表明，基于选择的聚合显著优于 MoA 式综合（$g=3.86$ [3.14, 4.58]），且综合方法在全部 42 项任务中均不及单模型基线，这一发现对主流流程范式提出了挑战。第三，我们报告了探索性证据，表明纳入一个能力更弱、成本更低的模型反而能提升性能（$g=0.87$ [0.42, 1.32]），这意味着最优团队组成可能无需全部采用前沿模型。
The selection bottleneck is, we conjecture, a structural property of generate-then-select pipelines more broadly: the value of variance in the candidate pool depends entirely on the mechanism that exploits it. The field has spent considerable effort building better individual generators. Our results suggest that substantial gains are available by building better selectors. A decoupled evaluation pass using fully independent judges confirms all directional findings with attenuated effect sizes (Table 2; Spearman $\rho=0.90$ ), indicating that the core results survive even when selection–evaluation circularity is removed.
我们推测，选择瓶颈是更广泛的先生成后选择流程所具有的一种结构性属性：候选池中方差的价值完全取决于利用它的机制。该领域已投入大量精力来构建更好的单个生成器。我们的结果表明，构建更好的选择器可带来显著收益。采用完全独立的评判者进行的一轮解耦评估确认了所有发现的方向，但效应量有所减小（表 2；Spearman $\rho=0.90$），这表明即使消除选择与评估之间的循环依赖，核心结果依然成立。
Our results suggest that selector design may offer larger quality gains than generator improvement in multi-agent pipelines, a hypothesis we encourage future work to test across broader settings.
我们的结果表明，在多智能体流程中，选择器设计可能比生成器改进带来更大的质量提升；我们鼓励未来工作在更广泛的场景中检验这一假设。
