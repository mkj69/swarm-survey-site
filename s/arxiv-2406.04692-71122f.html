Abstract摘要
Recent advances in large language models (LLMs) demonstrate substantial capabilities in natural language understanding and generation tasks. With the growing number of LLMs, how to harness the collective expertise of multiple LLMs is an exciting open direction. Toward this goal, we propose a new approach that leverages the collective strengths of multiple LLMs through a Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) methodology. In our approach, we construct a layered MoA architecture wherein each layer comprises multiple LLM agents. Each agent takes all the outputs from agents in the previous layer as auxiliary information in generating its response. MoA models achieves state-of-art performance on AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench and FLASK, surpassing GPT-4 Omni. For example, our MoA using only open-source LLMs is the leader of AlpacaEval 2.0 by a substantial gap, achieving a score of 65.1% compared to 57.5% by GPT-4 Omni.[fn1]
大语言模型（LLMs）的最新进展表明，它们在自然语言理解和生成任务中具备强大的能力。随着 LLMs 数量的不断增长，如何利用多个 LLMs 的集体专长，是一个令人振奋且有待探索的方向。为实现这一目标，我们提出了一种新方法，通过混合智能体（Mixture-of-Agents，MoA）方法利用多个 LLMs 的集体优势。在我们的方法中，我们构建了一种分层 MoA 架构，其中每一层都包含多个 LLM 智能体。每个智能体在生成回答时，都会将上一层所有智能体的输出作为辅助信息。MoA 模型在 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench 和 FLASK 上取得了当前最先进的性能，超越了 GPT-4 Omni。例如，我们仅使用开源 LLMs 的 MoA 在 AlpacaEval 2.0 上以显著优势位居榜首，得分为 65.1%，而 GPT-4 Omni 的得分为 57.5%。[fn1]

1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models (LLMs) (Zhang et al., 2022a; Chowdhery et al., 2022; Touvron et al., 2023a; Team et al., 2023; Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023) have significantly advanced the field of natural language understanding and generation in recent years. These models are pretrained on vast amounts of data and subsequently aligned with human preferences to generate helpful and coherent outputs (Ouyang et al., 2022). However, despite the plethora of LLMs and their impressive achievements, they still face inherent constraints on model size and training data. Further scaling up these models is exceptionally costly, often requiring extensive retraining on several trillion tokens.
近年来，大语言模型（LLMs）(Zhang et al., 2022a; Chowdhery et al., 2022; Touvron et al., 2023a; Team et al., 2023; Brown et al., 2020; OpenAI, 2023) 显著推动了自然语言理解和生成领域的发展。这些模型在海量数据上进行预训练，随后与人类偏好对齐，以生成有用且连贯的输出 (Ouyang et al., 2022)。然而，尽管 LLMs 种类繁多且取得了令人瞩目的成就，它们仍然面临模型规模和训练数据方面的固有限制。进一步扩大这些模型的规模成本极其高昂，通常需要在数万亿个词元上进行大规模重新训练。
At the same time, different LLMs possess unique strengths and specialize in various tasks aspects. For instance, some models excel at complex instruction following (Xu et al., 2023a) while others may be better suited for code generation (Roziere et al., 2023; Guo et al., 2024). This diversity in skill sets among different LLMs presents an intriguing question: Can we harness the collective expertise of multiple LLMs to create a more capable and robust model?
与此同时，不同的 LLMs 各具独特优势，并擅长任务的不同方面。例如，一些模型擅长遵循复杂指令 (Xu et al., 2023a)，而另一些模型可能更适合代码生成 (Roziere et al., 2023; Guo et al., 2024)。不同 LLMs 在技能组合上的这种多样性引出了一个值得探究的问题：我们能否利用多个 LLMs 的集体专长，创建一个能力更强、更稳健的模型？
Our answer to this question is Yes. We identify an inherent phenomenon we term the collaborativeness of LLMs — wherein an LLM tends to generate better responses when presented with outputs from other models, even if these other models are less capable by itself. Figure 1 showcases the LC win rate on the AlpacaEval 2.0 benchmark (Dubois et al., 2024) for 6 popular LLMs.
我们对这个问题的回答是肯定的。我们发现了一种固有现象，并将其称为 LLMs 的协作性（collaborativeness）——当向一个 LLM 提供其他模型的输出时，它往往会生成更好的回答，即使这些其他模型本身的能力较弱。图 1 展示了 6 个主流 LLMs 在 AlpacaEval 2.0 基准 (Dubois et al., 2024) 上的 LC 胜率。
Figure 1: AlpacaEval 2.0 LC win rates improve when provided with responses from other models.
图 1：提供其他模型的回答后，模型在 AlpacaEval 2.0 上的 LC 胜率有所提升。
When these models are provided with answers generated independently by these models, their LC win rates significantly improve. This indicates that the collaborativeness phenomenon is widespread among LLMs. Remarkably, this improvement occurs even when the auxiliary responses provided by the other models are of lower quality than what an individual LLM could generate independently.
当向这些模型提供由这些模型独立生成的答案时，它们的 LC 胜率显著提高。这表明，协作性现象在 LLMs 中普遍存在。值得注意的是，即使其他模型提供的辅助回答质量低于单个 LLM 独立生成的回答，这种提升仍然会出现。
Based on this finding, this paper introduces a Mixture-of-Agents (MoA) methodology that leverages multiple LLMs to iteratively enhance the generation quality. The structure of MoA is illustrated in Figure 2. Initially, LLMs in the first layer, denoted as agents $A_{1,1},...A_{1,n}$ independently generate responses to a given prompt. These responses are then presented to agents in the next layer $A_{2,1},...A_{2,n}$ (which may reuse a model from the first layer) for further refinement. This iterative refinement process continues for several cycles until obtaining a more robust and comprehensive response.
基于这一发现，本文提出了一种混合智能体（Mixture-of-Agents，MoA）方法，利用多个 LLMs 迭代提升生成质量。MoA 的结构如图 2 所示。首先，第一层中的 LLMs，即智能体 $A_{1,1},...A_{1,n}$，针对给定提示独立生成回答。随后，这些回答被提供给下一层的智能体 $A_{2,1},...A_{2,n}$（其中可以复用第一层中的模型），以进行进一步完善。这一迭代完善过程会持续若干轮，直到获得更稳健、更全面的回答。
Figure 2: Illustration of the Mixture-of-Agents Structure. This example showcases 4 MoA layers with 3 agents in each layer. The agents here can share the same model.
图 2：混合智能体结构示意图。该示例展示了 4 个 MoA 层，每层包含 3 个智能体。这里的智能体可以共享同一个模型。
To ensure effective collaboration among models and improve overall response quality, careful selection of LLMs for each MoA layer is crucial. This selection process is guided by two primary criteria: (a) Performance Metrics: The average win rate of models in layer $i$ plays a significant role in determining their suitability for inclusion in layer $i+1$ . Therefore, selecting models based on their demonstrated performance metrics ensures higher-quality outputs. (b) Diversity Considerations: The diversity of model outputs is also crucial. Responses generated by heterogeneous models contribute significantly more than those produced by the same model as we show later in section 3.3. By leveraging these criteria — performance and diversity — MoA aims to mitigate individual model deficiencies and enhance overall response quality through collaborative synthesis.
为确保模型之间有效协作并提高整体回答质量，为每个 MoA 层精心选择 LLMs 至关重要。这一选择过程主要依据两个标准：(a) 性能指标：模型在第 $i$ 层中的平均胜率，是判断其是否适合纳入第 $i+1$ 层的重要依据。因此，根据模型实际表现出的性能指标进行选择，可以确保更高质量的输出。(b) 多样性考量：模型输出的多样性也至关重要。正如我们将在第 3.3 节中展示的，异构模型生成的回答所带来的贡献显著大于同一模型生成的回答。通过利用性能和多样性这两个标准，MoA 旨在通过协作综合来弥补单个模型的不足，并提升整体回答质量。
We conduct comprehensive evaluations using AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench (Zheng et al., 2023), FLASK (Ye et al., 2023) benchmarks for assessing the response quality across various dimensions. The results demonstrate substantial improvements with our proposed method, achieving a new SOTA win rate of 65.8% on AlpacaEval 2.0 compared to the previous best of 57.5% achieved by GPT-4 Omni.
我们使用 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench (Zheng et al., 2023) 和 FLASK (Ye et al., 2023) 基准进行全面评估，以衡量各个维度上的回答质量。结果表明，我们提出的方法带来了显著改进，在 AlpacaEval 2.0 上取得了 65.8% 的胜率，刷新了最先进水平（SOTA），而此前由 GPT-4 Omni 创下的最佳胜率为 57.5%。
The contributions of this work are summarized as follows: (1) Novel framework: we propose a Mixture-of-Agents framework designed to leverage the strengths of multiple LLMs, thereby improving their reasoning and language generation capabilities. (2) Finding of collaborativeness of language models: we highlight the inherit collaborativeness among LLMs, where models tend to generate better quality responses when they have access to outputs from other models, even if those outputs are of lower quality. (3) State-of-the-art LLM performance: we conducted extensive experiments using multiple highly-competitive benchmarks such as AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench, and FLASK; our MoA framework achieves state-of-the-art performance on these benchmarks.
本工作的贡献概括如下：(1) 新颖框架：我们提出了一个混合智能体（Mixture-of-Agents）框架，旨在利用多个大语言模型（LLM）的优势，从而提升其推理和语言生成能力。(2) 发现语言模型的协作性：我们强调了 LLM 固有的协作性，即当模型能够获取其他模型的输出时，即使这些输出的质量较低，它们也往往能生成质量更高的回答。(3) 最先进的 LLM 性能：我们使用 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench 和 FLASK 等多个竞争激烈的基准开展了广泛实验；我们的 MoA 框架在这些基准上均达到了最先进水平。

In this section, we present our proposed methodology for leveraging multiple models to achieve boosted performance. We begin by demonstrating that LLMs possess collaborativeness and thus can improve their responses based on the outputs of other models. Following this, we introduce the Mixture-of-Agents methodology and discuss its design implications.
本节介绍我们提出的利用多个模型提升性能的方法。我们首先展示 LLM 具备协作性，因此能够基于其他模型的输出改进自身的回答。随后，我们介绍混合智能体方法，并讨论其对设计的启示。
2.1 Collaborativeness of LLMs2.1 LLM 的协作性
We begin by demonstrating the collaborativeness of LLMs, specifically their ability to generate higher quality responses when they can reference outputs from other models. As we have shown in the introduction and Figure 1, many of today’s available LLMs exhibit this collaborative capability.
我们首先展示 LLM 的协作性，具体而言，即它们在能够参考其他模型的输出时生成更高质量回答的能力。正如我们在引言和图 1 中所展示的，目前可用的许多 LLM 都表现出这种协作能力。
An important pathway to extract maximum benefits from collaboration of multiple LLMs is to characterize how different models are good at in various aspects of collaboration. During the collaboration process, we can categorize LLMs into two distinct roles:
要从多个 LLM 的协作中获得最大收益，一条重要途径是明确不同模型在协作的各个方面分别擅长什么。在协作过程中，我们可以将 LLM 分为两种不同的角色：
Proposers excel at generating useful reference responses for use by other models. While a good proposer may not necessarily produce responses with high scores by itself, it should offer more context and diverse perspectives, ultimately contributing to better final responses when used by an aggregator.
提议者（Proposers）擅长生成有用的参考回答，供其他模型使用。优秀的提议者自身生成的回答未必能获得高分，但它应当提供更多上下文和多样化视角，使聚合者在利用这些回答时最终能够生成更好的回答。
Aggregators are models proficient in synthesizing responses from other models into a single, high-quality output. An effective aggregator should maintain or enhance output quality even when integrating inputs that are of lesser quality than its own.
聚合者（Aggregators）是擅长将其他模型的回答综合为单个高质量输出的模型。有效的聚合者即使在整合质量低于自身输出的输入时，也应能够维持或提升输出质量。
Section 3.3 empirically validate the roles of aggregators and proposers. Specifically, we show that many LLMs possess capabilities both as aggregators and proposers, while certain models displayed specialized proficiencies in distinct roles. GPT-4o, Qwen1.5, LLaMA-3 emerged as a versatile model effective in both assisting and aggregating tasks. In contrast, WizardLM demonstrated excellent performance as an proposer model but struggled to maintain its effectiveness in aggregating responses from other models.
第 3.3 节通过实证验证了聚合者和提议者的作用。具体而言，我们表明，许多 LLM 同时具备担任聚合者和提议者的能力，而某些模型则在特定角色上表现出专长。GPT-4o、Qwen1.5、LLaMA-3 表现出多面性，在辅助和聚合任务中均能有效发挥作用。相比之下，WizardLM 作为提议模型表现出色，但在聚合其他模型的回答时难以保持其有效性。
Given that an aggregator can generate higher-quality responses by building upon outputs from other models, we propose further enhancing this collaborative potential by introducing additional aggregators. One intuitive idea is to replicate the exercise with multiple aggregators — initially using several to aggregate better answers and then re-aggregating these aggregated answers. By incorporating more aggregators into the process, we can iteratively synthesize and refine the responses, leveraging the strengths of multiple models to produce superior outcomes. This leads to the design of our proposed Mixture-of-Agents.
鉴于聚合者能够在其他模型输出的基础上生成更高质量的回答，我们提出引入更多聚合者，以进一步增强这种协作潜力。一个直观的想法是使用多个聚合者重复这一过程——先用多个聚合者聚合出更好的回答，再对这些聚合后的回答进行再次聚合。通过在这一过程中引入更多聚合者，我们可以迭代地综合和完善回答，利用多个模型的优势产出更优结果。由此形成了我们提出的混合智能体设计。
2.2 Mixture-of-Agents2.2 混合智能体
The structure of MoA is illustrated in Figure 2. It has $l$ layers and each layer- $i$ consists of $n$ LLMs, denoted by $A_{i,1}$ , $A_{i,2}$ , …, $A_{i,n}$ . It is important to note that LLMs can be reused either within the same layer or across different layers. When many LLMs in a layer are identical, this configuration leads to a special structure that corresponds to a model generating multiple possibly different outputs (due to the stochasticity of temperature sampling). We refer to this setting as single-proposer, where only a sparse subset of models are activated.
MoA 的结构如图 2 所示。它具有 $l$ 层，每一层 $i$ 都由 $n$ 个 LLM 组成，分别记为 $A_{i,1}$、$A_{i,2}$、…、$A_{i,n}$。需要注意的是，LLM 可以在同一层内复用，也可以跨不同层复用。当某一层中的许多 LLM 相同时，这种配置会形成一种特殊结构，相当于由一个模型生成多个可能不同的输出（这是由温度采样的随机性造成的）。我们将这种设置称为单提议者（single-proposer）设置，其中仅激活模型中的一个稀疏子集。
Here, each LLM $A_{i,j}$ processes an input text and generates its continuation. Our method does not require any fine-tuning and only utilizes the interface of prompting and generation of LLMs. Formally, given an input prompt $x_{1}$ , the output of $i$ -th MoA layer $y_{i}$ can be expressed as follows:
在这里，每个 LLM $A_{i,j}$ 都处理一段输入文本，并生成其续写内容。我们的方法不需要任何微调，仅使用 LLM 的提示输入和生成接口。形式化地，给定输入提示 $x_{1}$，第 $i$ 个 MoA 层的输出 $y_{i}$ 可表示如下：
$$\displaystyle y_{i} \\ \displaystyle=\oplus_{j=1}^{n}[A_{i,j}(x_{i})]\,\,+x_{1},x_{i+1}=y_{i}$$
where $+$ here means concatenation of texts; $\oplus$ means application of the Aggregate-and-Synthesize prompt shown in Table 1 to these model outputs.
其中，$+$ 表示文本拼接；$\oplus$ 表示将表 1 所示的聚合与综合（Aggregate-and-Synthesize）提示应用于这些模型输出。
Table 1: Aggregate-and-Synthesize Prompt to integrate responses from other models.
表 1：用于整合其他模型回答的聚合与综合提示。

It is crucial to critically evaluate the information provided in these responses, recognizing that some of it may be biased or incorrect. Your response should not simply replicate the given answers but should offer a refined, accurate, and comprehensive reply to the instruction. Ensure your response is well-structured, coherent, and adheres to the highest standards of accuracy and reliability.

2. [Model Response from $A_{i,2}$ ]
…
$n$ . [Model Response from $A_{i,n}$ ]
In practice, we do not need to concatenate prompt and all model responses so only one LLM is needed to be used in the last layer. Therefore, we use the output of an LLM from the $l$ -th layer ( $A_{l,1}(x_{l})$ ) as the final output and evaluate the metrics based on it.
在实践中，我们不需要拼接提示和所有模型的回答，因此最后一层只需使用一个 LLM。因此，我们使用第 $l$ 层中一个 LLM 的输出（$A_{l,1}(x_{l})$）作为最终输出，并据此评估各项指标。
2.3 Analogy to Mixture-of-Experts2.3 与混合专家（Mixture-of-Experts）的类比
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) (Shazeer et al., 2017) is a prominent and well-established technique in machine learning where multiple expert networks specialize in different skill sets. The MoE approach has shown significant success across various applications due to its ability to leverage diverse model capabilities for complex problem-solving tasks. Our MoA method draws inspiration from this methodology.
混合专家（Mixture-of-Experts，MoE）(Shazeer et al., 2017) 是机器学习中一种重要且成熟的技术，其中多个专家网络分别专精于不同的技能。MoE 方法能够利用不同模型的多样化能力来完成复杂的问题求解任务，因此在各种应用中取得了显著成功。我们的 MoA 方法受到这一方法的启发。
A typical MoE design consists of a stack of layers known as MoE layers. Each layer comprises a set of $n$ expert networks alongside a gating network and includes residual connections for improved gradient flow. Formally, for layer $i$ , this design can be expressed as follows:
典型的 MoE 设计由一系列堆叠的层构成，这些层称为 MoE 层。每一层包含一组 $n$ 个专家网络和一个门控网络，并采用残差连接以改善梯度流动。形式上，对于第 $i$ 层，这一设计可表示如下：
$$\displaystyle y_{i} \\ \displaystyle=\sum_{j=1}^{n}{G_{i,j}(x_{i})E_{i,j}(x_{i})}+x_{i}$$
where $G_{i,j}$ represents the output from the gating network corresponding to expert $j$ , and $E_{i,j}$ denotes the function computed by expert network $j$ . The leverage of multiple experts allows the model to learn different skill sets and focus on various aspects of the task at hand.
其中，$G_{i,j}$ 表示门控网络中对应于专家 $j$ 的输出，$E_{i,j}$ 表示专家网络 $j$ 所计算的函数。利用多个专家使模型能够学习不同的技能，并关注当前任务的不同方面。
From a high-level perspective, our proposed MoA framework extends the MoE concept to the model level by operating at the model level rather than at the activation level. Specifically, our MoA approach leverages LLMs and operates entirely through the prompt interface rather than requiring modifications to internal activations or weights. This means that instead of having specialized sub-networks within a single model like in MoE, we utilize multiple full-fledged LLMs across different layers. Note that in our approach, we consolidate the roles of the gating network and expert networks using a LLM, as the intrinsic capacity of LLMs allows them to effectively regularize inputs by interpreting prompts and generating coherent outputs without needing external mechanisms for coordination.
从宏观角度来看，我们提出的 MoA 框架通过在模型层面而非激活值层面进行操作，将 MoE 的概念扩展到了模型层面。具体而言，我们的 MoA 方法利用大语言模型（LLM），完全通过提示接口运行，无需修改内部激活值或权重。这意味着，我们在不同层中使用多个完整的 LLM，而不是像 MoE 那样在单个模型内部设置专门的子网络。请注意，在我们的方法中，我们使用一个 LLM 来整合门控网络与专家网络的角色，因为 LLM 的内在能力使其能够通过理解提示并生成连贯的输出来有效规整输入，而无需外部协调机制。
Moreover, since this method relies solely on prompting capabilities inherent within off-the-shelf models: (1) It eliminates computational overhead associated with fine-tuning; (2) It provides flexibility and scalability: our method can be applied to the latest LLMs regardless of their size or architecture.
此外，由于该方法仅依赖现成模型自身具备的提示能力：(1) 它消除了微调带来的计算开销；(2) 它具有灵活性和可扩展性：无论最新 LLM 的规模或架构如何，我们的方法均可应用于这些模型。

Benchmarks基准
We mainly evaluate models on AlpacaEval 2.0 (Dubois et al., 2024), a leading benchmark for assessing the alignment of LLMs with human preferences. It contains 805 instructions representative of real use cases. Each model’s response is directly compared against that of the GPT-4 (gpt-4-1106-preview), with a GPT-4-based evaluator determining the likelihood of preferring the evaluated model’s response. To ensure fairness, the evaluation employs length-controlled (LC) win rates, effectively neutralizing length bias.[fn2]
我们主要在 AlpacaEval 2.0 (Dubois et al., 2024) 上评估模型，这是评估 LLM 与人类偏好对齐程度的一项领先基准。该基准包含 805 条能够代表真实使用场景的指令。每个模型的回答都直接与 GPT-4 (gpt-4-1106-preview) 的回答进行比较，并由基于 GPT-4 的评估器判断被评估模型的回答更受偏好的概率。为确保公平性，评估采用长度控制（length-controlled，LC）胜率，有效消除长度偏差。[fn2]
Footnote: 2 This metric tracks closely with human preferences, achieving a Spearman correlation of 0.98 with actual human evaluations (Dubois et al., 2024).

Additionally, we also evaluate on MT-Bench (Zheng et al., 2023) and FLASK (Ye et al., 2023). MT-Bench uses GPT-4 to grade and give a score to model’s answer. FLASK, on the other hand, offers a more granular evaluation with 12 skill-specific scores.
此外，我们还在 MT-Bench (Zheng et al., 2023) 和 FLASK (Ye et al., 2023) 上进行了评估。MT-Bench 使用 GPT-4 对模型的回答进行评判并打分。FLASK 则通过 12 项针对特定技能的评分提供更细粒度的评估。
Models模型
In our study, we constructed our default MoA by using only open-source models to achieve competitive performance. The models included are: Qwen1.5-110B-Chat (Bai et al., 2023), Qwen1.5-72B-Chat, WizardLM-8x22B (Xu et al., 2023a), LLaMA-3-70B-Instruct (Touvron et al., 2023b), Mixtral-8x22B-v0.1 (Jiang et al., 2024), dbrx-instruct (The Mosaic Research Team, 2024). We construct 3 MoA layers and use the same set of models in each MoA layer. We use Qwen1.5-110B-Chat as the aggregator in the last layer. We also developed a variant called MoA w/ GPT-4o, which prioritizes high-quality outputs by using GPT-4o as the aggregator in the final MoA layer. Another variant, MoA-Lite, emphasizes cost-effectiveness. It uses the same set of models as proposers but includes only 2 MoA layers and employs Qwen1.5-72B-Chat as the aggregator. This makes it more cost-effective than GPT-4o while achieving a $1.8\%$ improvement in quality on AlpacaEval 2.0. We ensure strict adherence to the licensing terms of all models utilized in this research. For open-source models, all inferences were ran through Together Inference Endpoint.[fn3]

3.3 What Makes Mixture-of-Agents Work Well?3.3 是什么使 Mixture-of-Agents 表现出色？
In this subsection, we conduct experiments that provide us better understandings of the internal mechanism of Mixture-of-Agents. We summarize key insights below.
在本小节中，我们通过实验进一步理解 Mixture-of-Agents 的内部机制。我们在下文总结了关键发现。
Mixture-of-Agents significantly outperforms LLM rankers.Mixture-of-Agents 显著优于 LLM 排序器。
First, we compare Mixture-of-Agents with an LLM-based ranker which uses the aggregator model to select one of the answers that are generated by the proposers, instead of generating a new output. The results are shown in Figure 4, where we can observe that the MoA approach significantly outperforms an LLM-ranker baseline. The fact that MoA outperforms the ranking approach suggests that the aggregator does not simply select one of the generated answers by the proposers, but potentially performs sophisticated aggregation over all proposed generations.
首先，我们将 Mixture-of-Agents 与一种基于 LLM 的排序器进行比较，该排序器使用聚合器模型从提议者生成的答案中选择一个，而不是生成新的输出。结果如图 4 所示，可以观察到，MoA 方法显著优于 LLM 排序器基线。MoA 优于排序方法这一事实表明，聚合器并非简单地选择提议者生成的某个答案，而是可能对所有候选生成结果进行了复杂的聚合。
MoA tends to incorporate the best proposed answers.MoA 倾向于吸纳最佳候选答案。
We also compare the aggregator’s response with the proposers’ responses via similarity scores such as BLEU (Papineni et al., 2002) which reflects n-gram overlaps. Within each sample, given $n$ proposed answers by the proposers, we calculate the the Spearman’s rank correlation coefficient between $n$ similar scores and $n$ preference scores determined by the GPT-4 based evaluator. The results in Figure 4 indeed confirms a positive correlation between the win rate and the BLEU score. We also provide results with Levenshtein similarity (RapidFuzz, 2023) or TF-IDF as opposed to BLEU scores in Appendix A. where both alternative approaches for textual similarities also yield positive correlation with the preference scores.
我们还通过 BLEU (Papineni et al., 2002) 等反映 n-gram 重叠程度的相似度分数，比较聚合器与提议者的回答。对于每个样本，给定提议者提供的 $n$ 个候选答案，我们计算 $n$ 个相似度分数与基于 GPT-4 的评估器确定的 $n$ 个偏好分数之间的斯皮尔曼秩相关系数。图 4 中的结果确实证实，胜率与 BLEU 分数之间存在正相关关系。我们还在附录 A 中提供了使用 Levenshtein 相似度 (RapidFuzz, 2023) 或 TF-IDF 替代 BLEU 分数的结果，这两种文本相似度替代方法也都与偏好分数呈正相关。

We analyze how the number of proposals affect the final output quality by varying $n$ , the number of proposers in each layer. For each $n$ , we compare two settings: “single-proposer” where the $n$ responses are generated by the same LLM with a temperature of 0.7; and “multiple-proposer” where each response is generated by a different LLMs. Overall, using multiple different LLMs consistently yielded better results. 我们通过改变每层提议者的数量 $n$，分析提议数量如何影响最终输出的质量。我们在表 4 中展示了结果，发现分数随 $n$ 单调增加，这反映了拥有更多辅助信息的益处。此外，我们还量化了使用一组多样化的 LLM 作为提议者的影响。对于每个 $n$，我们比较两种设置：“单一提议者”，即由同一个 LLM 在温度为 0.7 时生成 $n$ 个回答；以及“多个提议者”，即每个回答由不同的 LLM 生成。总体而言，使用多个不同的 LLM 始终能获得更好的结果。这两项结果均表明，在每个 MoA 层中使用数量更多且更加多样化的 LLM 智能体可以提高性能。进一步扩展 MoA 的宽度是未来研究中一个很有前景的方向。

4 Related Work4 相关工作
4.1 LLM Reasoning4.1 LLM 推理
In order to improve generation quality of LLMs, recent researches have experienced great progresses in optimizing LLMs to various downstream tasks through prompt engineering. Chain of Thought (CoT) (Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022) prompting techniques represent a linear problem-solving approach where each step builds upon the previous one. Fu et al. (2022) applied CoT to multi-step reasoning tasks. To automate CoT prompting, Auto-CoT (Zhang et al., 2022b) constructs demonstrations by sampling diverse questions and generating reasoning chains. Active-Prompt (Diao et al., 2023) focuses on selecting the most uncertain questions for task-specific annotations. PS Prompt (Wang et al., 2023) decomposes tasks into subtasks. Tree-of-Thought (ToT) (Yao et al., 2023a) expands on the reasoning process by considering multiple paths of reasoning and self-evaluating choices. Effective Graph-of-Thought (Yao et al., 2023b) frames thoughts as graphs. Natural Program prompting (Ling et al., 2023) is proposed for better solving deductive reasoning tasks. And re-reading prompt (Xu et al., 2023b) revisits question information embedded within input prompts.
为了提高 LLM 的生成质量，近期研究在通过提示工程优化 LLM 以适应各种下游任务方面取得了重大进展。思维链（Chain of Thought，CoT）(Wei et al., 2022; Kojima et al., 2022) 提示技术采用一种线性的问题求解方式，其中每一步都建立在前一步的基础上。Fu et al. (2022) 将 CoT 应用于多步推理任务。为了实现 CoT 提示的自动化，Auto-CoT (Zhang et al., 2022b) 通过采样多样化的问题并生成推理链来构建示例。Active-Prompt (Diao et al., 2023) 侧重于选择不确定性最高的问题，以进行针对特定任务的标注。PS Prompt (Wang et al., 2023) 将任务分解为子任务。思维树（Tree-of-Thought，ToT）(Yao et al., 2023a) 通过考虑多条推理路径并对选择进行自我评估，扩展了推理过程。Effective Graph-of-Thought (Yao et al., 2023b) 将思维表示为图。自然程序（Natural Program）提示 (Ling et al., 2023) 被提出用于更好地解决演绎推理任务。而重读提示（re-reading prompt）(Xu et al., 2023b) 则重新审视输入提示中包含的问题信息。
4.2 Model Ensemble4.2 模型集成
A straightforward solution to leverage the strengths of multiple models is reranking outputs from different models. For instance, Jiang et al. (2023) introduce PairRanker, which performs pairwise comparisons on candidate outputs to select the best one, showing improvements on a self-constructed instruction dataset. To address the substantial computational costs associated with multi-LLM inference, other studies have explored training a router that predicts the best-performing model from a fixed set of LLMs for a given input (Wang et al., 2024a; Shnitzer et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2023). Additionally, FrugalGPT (Chen et al., 2023b) proposed reducing the cost of using LLMs by employing different models in a cascading manner. In order to better leverage the responses of multiple models, Jiang et al. (2023) trained a GenFuser, a model that was trained to generate an improved response to capitalize on the strengths of multiple candidates. Huang et al. (2024) proposed to fuse the outputs of different models by averaging their output probability distributions.
利用多个模型优势的一种直接方法，是对不同模型的输出进行重新排序。例如，Jiang et al. (2023) 提出了 PairRanker，它通过对候选输出进行两两比较来选出最佳输出，并在自行构建的指令数据集上取得了性能提升。为了解决多 LLM 推理带来的高昂计算成本，其他研究探索了训练路由器的方法，以针对给定输入，从一组固定的 LLM 中预测表现最佳的模型 (Wang et al., 2024a; Shnitzer et al., 2024; Lu et al., 2023)。此外，FrugalGPT (Chen et al., 2023b) 提出以级联方式使用不同模型，从而降低使用 LLM 的成本。为了更好地利用多个模型的回答，Jiang et al. (2023) 训练了 GenFuser，该模型通过训练来综合多个候选回答的优势，生成改进后的回答。Huang et al. (2024) 提出，通过对不同模型的输出概率分布取平均来融合其输出。
Another line of work is multi-agent collaboration. Several studies explore using multiple large language models as agents that collectively discuss and reason through given problems interactively. Du et al. (2023) establishes a mechanism for symmetric discussions among agents. Around the same time, MAD (Liang et al., 2023) introduces an asymmetric mechanism design, with different roles, i.e., debater and judge. Other similar works include (Chan et al., 2023). Moreover, ReConcile (Chen et al., 2023a) exemplifies an asymmetric discussion involving weighted voting. To understand discussion more deeply, Zhang et al. (2023) aim to explain such collaboration mechanism in a social psychology view. Wang et al. (2024b) systematically compared multi-agent approaches and found a single agent with a strong prompt including detailed demonstrations can achieve comparable response quality to multi-agent approaches.
另一个研究方向是多智能体协作。多项研究探索了将多个大语言模型用作智能体，通过交互共同讨论给定问题并进行推理。Du et al. (2023) 建立了一种智能体之间的对称讨论机制。几乎与此同时，MAD (Liang et al., 2023) 引入了一种非对称机制设计，设置了不同的角色，即辩论者和裁判。其他类似工作包括 (Chan et al., 2023)。此外，ReConcile (Chen et al., 2023a) 展示了一种涉及加权投票的非对称讨论方式。为了更深入地理解讨论，Zhang et al. (2023) 旨在从社会心理学视角解释这种协作机制。Wang et al. (2024b) 系统地比较了多智能体方法，发现使用包含详细示例的高质量提示的单个智能体，可以实现与多智能体方法相当的回答质量。
5 Conclusion5 结论
This paper introduces a Mixture-of-Agents approach aimed at leveraging the capabilities of multiple LLMs via successive stages for iterative collaboration. Our method harnesses the collective strengths of agents in the Mixture-of-Agents family, and can significantly improve upon the output quality of each individual model. Empirical evaluations conducted on AlpacaEval 2.0, MT-Bench, and FLASK demonstrated substantial improvements in response quality, with our approach achieving the LC win rate up to $65\%$ . These findings validate our hypothesis that integrating diverse perspectives from various models can lead to superior performance compared to relying on a single model alone. In addition, we provide insights into improving the design of MoA; systematic optimization of MoA architecture is an interesting direction for future work.
本文提出了一种 Mixture-of-Agents 方法，旨在通过连续多个阶段的迭代协作，利用多个 LLM 的能力。我们的方法利用了混合智能体（Mixture-of-Agents）家族中各智能体的共同优势，能够显著提升各个单独模型的输出质量。在 AlpacaEval 2.0、MT-Bench 和 FLASK 上开展的实证评估表明，回答质量得到了显著提升，我们的方法实现了高达 $65\%$ 的长度控制（LC）胜率。这些发现验证了我们的假设：与仅依赖单个模型相比，整合不同模型的多样化视角可以带来更优的性能。此外，我们还为改进 MoA 的设计提供了见解；对 MoA 架构进行系统性优化是未来一个值得研究的方向。
Limitations.局限性。
Our proposed method requires iterative aggregation of model responses, which means the model cannot decide the first token until the last MoA layer is reached. This potentially results in a high Time to First Token (TTFT), which can negatively impact user experience. To mitigate this issue, we can limit the number of MoA layers, as the first response aggregation has the most significant boost on generation quality. Future work could explore chunk-wise aggregation instead of aggregating entire responses at once, which can reduce TTFT while maintaining response quality.
我们提出的方法需要对模型回答进行迭代聚合，这意味着模型在到达最后一个 MoA 层之前无法确定首个词元。这可能导致较长的首词元延迟（Time to First Token，TTFT），从而对用户体验产生负面影响。为缓解这一问题，我们可以限制 MoA 的层数，因为首次回答聚合对生成质量的提升最为显著。未来的工作可以探索分块聚合，而非一次性聚合完整回答，这可以在保持回答质量的同时降低 TTFT。
Broader Impact.更广泛的影响。
This study holds the potential to enhance the effectiveness of LLM-driven chat assistants, thereby making AI more accessible. Moreover, since the intermediate outputs that are expressed in natural language, MoA presented improves the interpretability of models. This enhanced interpretability facilitates better alignment with human reasoning.
本研究有望提升由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的聊天助手的效能，从而使 AI 更易于普及和使用。此外，由于中间输出以自然语言表达，本文提出的 MoA 提高了模型的可解释性。这种可解释性的增强有助于模型更好地与人类推理方式对齐。
