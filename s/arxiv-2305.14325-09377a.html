Abstract摘要
Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable capabilities in language generation, understanding, and few-shot learning in recent years. An extensive body of work has explored how their performance may be further improved through the tools of prompting, ranging from verification, self-consistency, or intermediate scratchpads. In this paper, we present a complementary approach to improve language responses where multiple language model instances propose and debate their individual responses and reasoning processes over multiple rounds to arrive at a common final answer. Our findings indicate that this approach significantly enhances mathematical and strategic reasoning across a number of tasks. We also demonstrate that our approach improves the factual validity of generated content, reducing fallacious answers and hallucinations that contemporary models are prone to. Our approach may be directly applied to existing black-box models and uses identical procedure and prompts for all tasks we investigate. Overall, our findings suggest that such "society of minds" approach has the potential to significantly advance the capabilities of LLMs and pave the way for further breakthroughs in language generation and understanding. Project website at https://composable-models.github.io/llm_debate/.
近年来，大语言模型（LLMs）在语言生成、理解和少样本学习方面展现出了卓越的能力。大量研究探索了如何通过提示手段进一步提升其性能，包括验证、自一致性以及中间草稿等方法。本文提出了一种改进语言回答的互补方法：多个语言模型实例分别提出各自的回答和推理过程，并围绕这些内容展开多轮辩论，最终达成一个共同的答案。我们的研究结果表明，这种方法显著提升了多项任务中的数学推理和策略推理能力。我们还证明，该方法提高了生成内容的事实准确性，减少了当前模型容易产生的错误回答和幻觉。我们的方法可以直接应用于现有的黑盒模型，并且对所研究的所有任务均采用相同的流程和提示。总体而言，我们的研究结果表明，这种“心智社会”（society of minds）方法有望显著提升 LLMs 的能力，并为语言生成和理解领域的进一步突破铺平道路。项目网站：https://composable-models.github.io/llm_debate/。
1 Introduction1 引言
Large language models (LLMs) have demonstrated remarkable language generation, understanding, and few-shot learning capabilities in recent years. These methods are trained on a massive corpus of text on the internet, where the quality and accuracy of extracted natural language may not be ensured. Thus, current models may suffer from confidently hallucinating facts or making implausible jumps in chains of reasoning. An extensive body of recent work has focused on improving factual accuracy and reasoning in language models. These range from prompting models with few or zero-shot chain-of-thought demonstrations, use of verification, self-consistency, or intermediate scratchpads.
近年来，大语言模型（LLMs）展现出了卓越的语言生成、理解和少样本学习能力。这些方法使用互联网上的海量文本语料进行训练，而从中提取的自然语言的质量和准确性可能无法得到保证。因此，当前模型可能会信心十足地编造事实，或在推理链中出现不合理的跳跃。近期大量研究致力于提高语言模型的事实准确性和推理能力。这些方法包括通过少样本或零样本思维链示例对模型进行提示，以及使用验证、自一致性或中间草稿。
We note that these techniques are applied over a single model instance. Instead, we propose a complementary approach inspired by The Society of Mind [19] and multi-agent settings, where multiple language model instances (or agents) individually propose and jointly debate their responses and reasoning processes to arrive at a single common answer. More specifically, given a query, multiple instances of a language model first generate individual candidate answers to a query. Then each individual model instance reads and critiques the responses of all other models and uses this content to update its own answer. This step is then repeated over several rounds. This process induces models to construct answers that are consistent with both their internal critic as well as sensible in light of the responses of other agents. The resulting quorum of models can hold and maintain multiple chains of reasoning and possible answers simultaneously before proposing the final answer.
我们注意到，这些技术均应用于单个模型实例。我们则提出了一种受《心智社会》（The Society of Mind）[19] 和多智能体环境启发的互补方法：多个语言模型实例（或智能体）分别提出各自的回答和推理过程，并共同展开辩论，最终达成一个统一的共同答案。更具体地说，给定一个查询，语言模型的多个实例首先分别生成针对该查询的候选答案。随后，每个模型实例都会阅读并评析其他所有模型的回答，并利用这些内容更新自己的答案。接着，将这一步骤重复若干轮。这一过程促使模型构建既能通过自身内部评判、又在考虑其他智能体回答后仍然合理的答案。由此形成的模型群体能够在提出最终答案之前，同时持有并维持多条推理链和多个可能的答案。
We find that our debate approach outperforms single model baselines such as zero-shot chain of thought [11] and reflection [26, 18] on a variety of six reasoning, factuality, and question-answering tasks. Using both multiple model agents and multiple rounds of debate are important to achieve the best performance. Given an initial query, we find that individual model instances propose a diverse range of answers despite being the same model class (although we also investigate the case of mixing different model types, such as chatGPT [21] and Bard [23]). After debating and examining the responses of other model instances, we find that the population almost always converges on a single and more accurate common answer. Debate results are also less likely to include false facts that models are internally uncertain of. This is because as the debate progresses, individual model instances tend to disagree on uncertain facts and omit them from the answer (Figure 7). Lastly, we find that debate does not just act to amplify one correct answer in a model quorum - we find many cases where all the models initially make incorrect predictions, but then arrive at the correct answer as debate progresses (Figure 4,11).
我们发现，在涵盖推理、事实准确性和问答的六项不同任务中，我们的辩论方法优于零样本思维链 [11] 和反思 [26, 18] 等单模型基线方法。同时采用多个模型智能体和多轮辩论，对于取得最佳性能至关重要。给定初始查询，我们发现，即使各个模型实例属于同一模型类别，它们也会提出多种不同的答案（不过，我们也研究了混合使用不同模型类型的情况，例如 chatGPT [21] 和 Bard [23]）。我们发现，在辩论并审视其他模型实例的回答之后，整个群体几乎总会收敛到一个统一且更准确的共同答案。辩论所得的结果也更不容易包含模型内部并不确定的错误事实。这是因为，随着辩论的推进，各个模型实例往往会对不确定的事实产生分歧，并将其从答案中省略（图 7）。最后，我们发现，辩论的作用并非仅仅是强化模型群体中的某个正确答案——在许多情况下，所有模型最初都作出了错误预测，但随着辩论的推进，最终得出了正确答案（图 4,11）。
We use the same methodology and prompt templates for all our tasks and require only black-box access to language model generations – no model-internal information such as likelihoods or gradients is needed. This allows our method to be used with common public models serving interfaces. The method is also orthogonal to other model generation improvements such as retrieval or prompt engineering (in fact, we combine our debate method with zero-shot chain of thought). While the debate process is more costly, requiring multiple model instances and rounds, it arrives at significantly improved answers and may be used to generate additional model training data, effectively creating a model self-improvement loop.
我们对所有任务均采用相同的方法和提示模板，并且只需要以黑盒方式访问语言模型的生成结果，无需似然或梯度等任何模型内部信息。这使得我们的方法可以通过常见的公共模型服务接口使用。该方法还与检索或提示工程等其他模型生成改进方法相互独立，可以结合使用（事实上，我们将辩论方法与零样本思维链相结合）。尽管辩论过程需要多个模型实例和多轮交互，成本更高，但它能够得到显著改进的答案，还可用于生成额外的模型训练数据，从而有效形成模型自我改进的循环。
To help evaluate the effect of our approach on factual accuracy, we introduce a new benchmark and dataset evaluating factual accuracy of famous computer scientist biographies. We find that contemporary language models have an especially high tendency to hallucinate factually incorrect biographies, often misrepresenting the relevant institutions and dates. Moreover, these facts often inconsistent across different language model instances. By asking models to come to a consensus across their answers, such inconsistent facts may be either removed or corrected.
为帮助评估我们的方法对事实准确性的影响，我们提出了一项新基准及相应数据集，用于评估著名计算机科学家传记的事实准确性。我们发现，当代语言模型尤其容易生成包含事实错误的虚构传记，经常错误描述相关机构和日期。此外，这些事实在不同语言模型实例之间往往不一致。通过要求模型就各自的答案达成共识，这些不一致的事实可能被删除或纠正。
In summary, our work contributes the following. First, we present a novel approach to improving factual correctness and reasoning accuracy in contemporary language models, leveraging a multi-agent debate process between models. Second, we introduce a new benchmark of factual correctness which contemporary language models struggle with. Finally, we evaluate the performance of our debate procedure in language generation, both in terms of the number of agents, the underlying rounds of debate, and the prompts that elicit such behavior across a set of six different reasoning and factual accuracy tasks.
总而言之，我们的工作作出了以下贡献。首先，我们提出了一种新方法，利用模型之间的多智能体辩论过程，提高当代语言模型的事实正确性和推理准确性。其次，我们提出了一项新的事实正确性评估基准，当代语言模型在该基准上表现欠佳。最后，我们在六项不同的推理和事实准确性任务上，从智能体数量、辩论轮数以及引发此类行为的提示等方面，评估了我们的辩论流程在语言生成中的表现。

2.1 Multiagent Language Generation2.1 多智能体语言生成
Consider your work process when solving the following math question on an exam: “What is the area of a triangle with side lengths of 3, 4, 5?". In one thread of work, you may recognize that the triangle side-lengths directly correspond to a right triangle, and thus directly compute the area as $0.5\times 3\times 4=64$ . To make sure that you have the right answer, you may then try to solve the problem differently by estimating an angle $\theta$ in the triangle using the Law of Cosines, and then obtain the area by using the formula $0.5\times 3\times 4\times\sin(\theta)$ , arriving at another answer to the given exam problem.
请考虑你在考试中求解以下数学题时的解题过程：“边长为 3、4、5 的三角形的面积是多少？”沿着一种解题思路，你可能会发现，这些边长直接对应一个直角三角形，从而直接计算出面积为 $0.5\times 3\times 4=64$。为确保答案正确，你随后可能会尝试用另一种方法求解：先利用余弦定理估算三角形中的一个角 $\theta$，再利用公式 $0.5\times 3\times 4\times\sin(\theta)$ 求出面积，从而得到这道考题的另一个答案。
When these lines of work give the same answer, your confidence about the answer increases. In contrast, when these answers are different, individual lines of work may engage in a mental “debate" procedure, where you closely cross-examine the reasoning and assumptions of each line of work and refine solutions until a consistent answer.
当这些解题思路得出相同答案时，你对答案的信心就会增强。相反，当这些答案不同时，各条解题思路可能会在头脑中展开一场“辩论”：你会仔细交叉审视每条思路的推理和假设，并不断完善解法，直到得出一致的答案。
Similarly, consider writing a biography of a historical figure. To ensure the factuality of the biography, you may consult multiple different sources on each fact. Facts that are consistent in each source increase your confidence about the fact. In contrast, facts that are inconsistent require careful cross-examination between sources to determine the final consistent data.
类似地，考虑为一位历史人物撰写传记的情形。为确保传记的事实准确性，你可能会针对每项事实查阅多个不同来源。各个来源中一致的事实会增强你对该事实的信心。相反，对于不一致的事实，则需要仔细交叉核查不同来源，以确定最终一致的数据。
To mimic the above multi-threaded reasoning process and multi-source factuality checking processes, we propose to generate answers subject to a multi-agent debate procedure between multiple instances of large language models. Given a question, multiple agents represented as copies of a large language model, generate answers to the question. Each response serves as a possible thought process or source of information which agents may re-examine to find consistent final answers.
为模拟上述多线索推理过程和多来源事实核查过程，我们提出通过多个大语言模型实例之间的多智能体辩论流程来生成答案。给定一个问题，由同一个大语言模型的多个副本构成的多个智能体分别生成该问题的答案。每个回答都代表一种可能的思考过程或信息来源，智能体可以重新审视这些回答，以找到一致的最终答案。
After initial responses are generated from different agents, we initiate a round of debate between agents. Individual responses from other agents are concatenated and given as context to each agent, with each agent instructed to construct a new response based on such responses. Each language agent is thus responsible for both verifying the collection of responses given by other agents, and refining its own response based on other agents’ responses. We iteratively repeat this debate procedure over multiple rounds for improved performance.
在不同智能体生成初始回答后，我们启动一轮智能体之间的辩论。其他智能体的各个回答会被拼接起来，作为上下文提供给每个智能体，并要求每个智能体基于这些回答构建新的回答。因此，每个语言智能体既负责核验其他智能体给出的整组回答，也负责根据其他智能体的回答完善自己的回答。我们通过多轮迭代重复这一辩论流程，以提高性能。
Concretely, we first prompt each agent to independently solve the given problem or task. After each agent generates a response, we feed each agent a consensus prompt, illustrated in Figure 3, where each agent is instructed to update their responses based on the responses of other agents. This resultant consensus prompt may then be repeatedly given, using the updated responses of each agent. We illustrate an overview of this multiagent debate procedure in Figure 2.
具体而言，我们首先通过提示要求每个智能体独立解决给定的问题或任务。在每个智能体生成回答后，我们向每个智能体输入一个共识提示，如图 3 所示，要求每个智能体根据其他智能体的回答更新自己的回答。随后，可以使用每个智能体更新后的回答，反复提供由此形成的共识提示。我们在图 2 中展示了这一多智能体辩论流程的概览。
Note that our proposed approach operates in an orthogonal manner to existing approaches to prompt language models. Given a question, we may apply additional techniques for prompting language models to further improve our debate procedure by eliciting additional more detailed responses from language models. We illustrate the synergy of our approach with existing approaches to prompting language models in Figure 6 and directly apply zero-shot chain-of-thought reasoning in our evaluations.
请注意，我们提出的方法与现有的语言模型提示方法彼此正交。给定一个问题，我们可以采用额外的语言模型提示技术，引导语言模型生成更多、更详细的回答，从而进一步改进我们的辩论流程。我们在图 6 中展示了我们的方法与现有语言模型提示方法之间的协同作用，并在评估中直接应用了零样本思维链推理（zero-shot chain-of-thought reasoning）。

2.2 Consensus in Debates2.2 辩论中的共识
Given multiple rounds of debate, how can we ensure that a set of language model agents will converge to a final consensus answer? In general, debate can be seen as a multi-agent game, where convergence is not guaranteed. Empirically, however, we find that language models are able to converge on a single shared answer after multiple rounds of debate (Figure 4).
经过多轮辩论，我们如何确保一组语言模型智能体收敛到最终的共识答案？一般而言，辩论可以被视为一种多智能体博弈，其收敛性并无保证。然而，实证结果表明，语言模型能够在多轮辩论后收敛到一个共同的答案（图 4）。
We found that we could control the duration of debates by how changing how much a language model trusts its own outputs over those generated by other models through different prompts. We illustrate two prompts below in Figure 3, which we use to induce different debate durations between language models, and illustrate the effect of such prompts in Figure 12. In general, we found that prompts that encouraged models to be more “stubborn’ based on their own solutions led to longer debates and better final solutions. Overall, we observed that language model agents were relatively "agreeable", perhaps as a result of instruction tuning or reinforcement learning based on human feedback [22].
我们发现，可以通过不同的提示，改变语言模型对自身输出相对于其他模型所生成输出的信任程度，从而控制辩论的持续时间。我们在下方图 3 中展示了两种提示，用于引导语言模型开展不同时长的辩论，并在图 12 中展示了这些提示的效果。总体而言，我们发现，鼓励模型更加“固执”地坚持自身解答的提示，会带来更长的辩论和更好的最终解答。总体而言，我们观察到语言模型智能体相对“随和”，这可能是指令微调或基于人类反馈的强化学习 [22] 所致。

Summarization.摘要。
While in the majority of experiments in the paper we directly concatenate the responses of other agents as context for an agent to generate a new response, this is expensive when the number of agents involved in debate gets large. We may alternatively first summarize the responses from all other agents into a single response that we provide to agent at each round for more efficient debate. We apply this strategy in Figure 10 to enable the use of five or more agents in debate. In Figure 13, we analyze the effect compared to directly concatenating the responses of other agents. We find this improves the performance of debate, suggesting that summarization is another tool that can further improve multiagent debate.
虽然在本文的大多数实验中，我们直接将其他智能体的回答拼接起来，作为某个智能体生成新回答的上下文，但当参与辩论的智能体数量较多时，这种做法的成本很高。另一种做法是，先将所有其他智能体的回答汇总为一条回答，并在每轮中提供给该智能体，以提高辩论效率。我们在图 10 中采用了这一策略，使五个或更多智能体能够参与辩论。在图 13 中，我们分析了这一策略相较于直接拼接其他智能体回答的效果。我们发现，这能提升辩论的性能，表明摘要是另一种可以进一步改进多智能体辩论的工具。

4 Related Work4 相关工作
Reasoning and Factuality in Language Models.语言模型的推理能力与事实性。
A wide range of work has explored how to enable reasoning and factuality in language models. To improve reasoning, approaches have relied on prompting techniques such as scratchpads [20], verification [3], chain-of-thought demonstrations [30, 11, 25], and intermediate self-reflection [26, 18] and finetuning [13, 24, 31]. To improve factuality, approaches have relied on training techniques such as RLHF [33, 16, 2], pruning truthful datasets [12], external knowledge retrieval [7] and training-free methods based off likelihood estimation [10].
大量研究探索了如何使语言模型具备推理能力和事实性。为提升推理能力，已有方法采用了草稿区（scratchpads）[20]、验证 [3]、思维链示例 [30, 11, 25] 和中间步骤自我反思 [26, 18] 等提示技术，以及微调 [13, 24, 31]。为提升事实性，已有方法采用了基于人类反馈的强化学习（RLHF）[33, 16, 2]、对真实数据集进行剪枝 [12] 等训练技术、外部知识检索 [7]，以及基于似然估计的免训练方法 [10]。
Our work provides an alternative way to obtain reasoning and factuality in language models using multiagent debates, which only requires black-box access to a language generator. Prior work also has explored how to take the majority vote across different models [15, 3, 29, 28] while in this work, we use the power of a language model to combine different answers. Most similar to our work, Irving et al. [9] also proposes a debate procedure to verify the accuracy and safety of powerful AI agents. In contrast to our approach, in their work, agents are asked to alternatively provide proof of a input, and humans are tasked with assessing these debates and determining safety.
我们的工作提供了另一种通过多智能体辩论使语言模型具备推理能力和事实性的方法，该方法仅需对语言生成器进行黑盒访问。先前的工作也探索了如何对不同模型的输出进行多数投票 [15, 3, 29, 28]，而在本研究中，我们利用语言模型的能力来整合不同答案。与我们的工作最为相近的是，Irving 等人 [9] 也提出了一种辩论流程，用于验证强大 AI 智能体的准确性和安全性。与我们的方法不同，在他们的工作中，智能体被要求交替针对某个输入提供证明，而人类则负责评估这些辩论并判定安全性。
Compositional Generation.组合式生成。
Our work is also related to existing works that focus on text generation by combining different models [4, 17, 32, 1, 5]. Most similar to our work, [14, 32] propose to combine multiple different large pretrained models together for multimodal reasoning. In contrast, in our work, we aim to use communication between different language models to enable more effective reasoning and factuality in language models.
我们的工作也与通过组合不同模型进行文本生成的现有研究有关 [4, 17, 32, 1, 5]。与我们的工作最为相近的是，[14, 32] 提出将多个不同的大型预训练模型组合起来，用于多模态推理。相比之下，在我们的工作中，我们旨在利用不同语言模型之间的交流，提升语言模型的推理能力和事实性。
5 Limitations and Discussion5 局限性与讨论
In this paper, we present an orthogonal approach to improve the performance of language models using multi-agent debate. We find that the approach is simple and effective across a wide set of different reasoning and validity language modeling tasks.
在本文中，我们提出了一种利用多智能体辩论提升语言模型性能的正交方法。我们发现，该方法简单有效，适用于多种涉及推理和有效性的语言建模任务。
Limitations.局限性。
In comparison to other prompting techniques, our multiagent debate procedure is more computationally expensive, as it requires both multiple language generations, and an underlying debate procedure. However, we believe that this approach may be seen as a method to generate additional data that may be distilled back to self-improve the original base model.
与其他提示技术相比，我们的多智能体辩论过程计算开销更大，因为它既需要多次语言生成，也需要一个底层辩论流程。不过，我们认为，这种方法可以视为一种生成额外数据的手段，这些数据可通过蒸馏反馈给原始基础模型，使其实现自我改进。
Further, we observed that as debates became longer in duration, current language models sometimes struggled to fully process the entire debate input, and typically only focused on the most recent generations. We believe that this performance will be alleviated with longer-context and improved language models or by summarizing early portions of the debate.
此外，我们观察到，随着辩论持续时间的延长，当前的语言模型有时难以完整处理整个辩论输入，通常只关注最近生成的内容。我们认为，采用上下文更长、能力更强的语言模型，或对辩论的早期部分进行总结，可以缓解这一问题。
Finally, we found that while debates typically converged into single final answers, these answers were not necessarily correct. Despite answers being incorrect, language models would confidently affirm that their answer is correct and consistent with all other agent responses. We believe this result is in part due to the fact that LMs do not correctly express their uncertainty when generating responses, and believe that other orthogonal approaches to improve this performance would improve the results of multiagent debate.
最后，我们发现，尽管辩论通常会收敛到一个最终答案，但这些答案未必正确。即使答案不正确，语言模型仍会自信地确认自己的答案正确，且与所有其他智能体的回答一致。我们认为，这一结果部分源于语言模型（LMs）在生成回答时未能正确表达自身的不确定性；我们也认为，采用其他与本方法正交的方法来改善这一表现，将有助于提升多智能体辩论的效果。

A.2 Evaluation DetailsA.2 评估细节
We provided detailed evaluation details for each setting in the paper. We run all experiments using the gpt-3.5-turbo-0301 model. We provide a table listing the prompts used to prompt models and initialize debate in Table 15.
我们提供了论文中各项设置的详细评估细节。我们使用 gpt-3.5-turbo-0301 模型运行所有实验。我们在表 15 中列出了用于提示模型和初始化辩论的提示。
Arithmetic.算术。
To evaluate the arithmetic task, we generated six random integers for each task between 0 and 30. We then evaluated the extent to which the correct integer answer was correctly obtained. We evaluated models on one hundred generated arithmetic tasks.
为评估算术任务，我们为每项任务生成了六个介于 0 和 30 之间的随机整数。随后，我们评估了正确得出整数答案的程度。我们在一百道生成的算术题上评估了模型。
Grade School Math.小学数学。
To evaluate the GSM8K task, we evaluated the accuracy at which models were able to obtain the final correct answer, as extracted from a box. We evaluated models on one hundred grade school math problems.
为评估 GSM8K 任务，我们从方框中提取最终答案，并评估模型得出正确答案的准确率。我们在一百道小学数学题上评估了模型。
Chess.国际象棋。
To evaluate the chess reasoning task, we used chess games from https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/Adams.zip. We asked chatGPT to predict the next move for white to move at turn 14 and reported the relative Stockfish pawn score with search depth 20 after executing the suggested move from chatGPT. We evaluated models on three hundred selected chess games.
为评估国际象棋推理任务，我们使用了来自 https://www.pgnmentor.com/players/Adams.zip 的国际象棋对局。我们要求 chatGPT 预测第 14 回合白方应走的下一步，并报告执行 chatGPT 建议的走法后，Stockfish 在搜索深度为 20 时给出的相对兵值评分。我们在三百局选定的国际象棋对局上评估了模型。
Biographies.人物传记。
To evaluate the biographies task, we compare each generated bullet point biography for a person with a ground truth set of facts about the person extracted from Wikipedia. We iteratively loop through each ground truth fact, and validate the extent to which the generated biography matches a particular bullet by prompting chatGPT with the prompt: Consider the following biography of <person>: <generated biography> Is the above biography above consistent with the fact below? <ground truth bullet> Give a single-word answer, yes, no, or uncertain. We then evaluate and report the percentage of ground bullets that chatGPT returns either yes or no on. We ignored ground truth bullets that chatGPT returns returned uncertain.
为评估人物传记任务，我们将为某个人生成的每条传记要点，与从 Wikipedia 提取的关于该人物的真实事实集进行比较。我们逐一遍历每条真实事实，并使用以下提示词向 chatGPT 提问，以验证生成的传记与特定条目的吻合程度：请考虑以下关于 <person> 的传记：<generated biography> 上述传记是否与以下事实一致？<ground truth bullet> 请用一个词回答：yes、no 或 uncertain。随后，我们评估并报告 chatGPT 返回 yes 或 no 的真实事实条目所占的百分比。我们忽略了 chatGPT 返回 uncertain 的真实事实条目。
We found this evaluation metric provided a fast way to evaluate how relatively correct a generated bullet point biography is. However, we found that generated facts could contain incorrect information that was not captured in the ground truth bullet and thus could not be validated through this metric. Nevertheless, we believe this evaluation scheme estimates the relative accuracy of a generated biography.
我们发现，这一评估指标提供了一种快速评估生成的条目式传记相对正确程度的方法。然而，我们发现，生成的事实可能包含真实事实条目未涵盖的错误信息，因此无法通过这一指标加以验证。尽管如此，我们认为，这一评估方案能够估计生成传记的相对准确性。
MMLU.MMLU。
To evaluate MMLU, we measured the accuracy in which models were able to select the correct multiple-choice answer in each problem. We evaluated models on one hundred selected MMLU questions randomly distributed across each of the subject areas.
为了评估 MMLU，我们测量了模型在每道题中选出正确选项的准确率。我们使用随机分布于各学科领域的 one hundred 道选定 MMLU 题目对模型进行了评估。
Chess Validity.国际象棋走法有效性。
To evaluate chess validity, we consider the BIG-Bench Chess-State Tracking Benchmark [27], where we used the hardest reported task in the benchmark synthetic_short. Each generated answer was deemed correct as long as it was one of the valid answers in the sequence. We evaluated models of one hundred selected chess validity tasks.
为了评估国际象棋走法的有效性，我们采用 BIG-Bench 国际象棋状态跟踪基准 [27]，并使用了该基准中报告的最难任务 synthetic_short。只要生成的答案是该序列中的有效答案之一，就被判定为正确。我们使用 one hundred 项选定的国际象棋走法有效性任务对模型进行了评估。
